On 2026-11-20 the assistant suggested that Schelling's focal points and
Tomasello's obligation account (THEORY-495) "may not be rivals at all".
The owner agreed: "the 'we' motivates the need for a change, and the 50-50
split is the 'conspicuous' solution", conspicuity being what the owner
would call an affordance. The assistant then analysed a shared affordance
as needing each to expect the other to perceive it. The owner asked:
"we're talking about three year olds, right? do they even have theory of
mind as developed as that by that age? Maybe we need to read Piaget?", and
then: "we can probably find more recent commentary that integrates those
ideas of his that have stood the test of time while reporting on more
reliably validated child development psychology." Four readers searched
and read; the filer read two more.

## What the readings settle

Recursive mindreading ("I expect you to expect") arrives at five to seven,
and recursive coordination is shown at six; three-year-olds do not
coordinate tacitly on salience. So at three the equal split is not a
Schelling focal point reached by mutual expectation. Three-year-olds
already state equality as the norm but keep more from windfalls, and split
equally after collaborating. The owner's claim is filed in that narrowed
form: the joint commitment as motive, a known equal default as content.
