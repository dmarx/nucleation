## The list

The open questions that only readings bear on: no claim, argument or case
answers, bears on or refines them. There were 99 of 410 open questions
(160 more are borne on by nothing; they are left for another run). Four
readers took them by first line: will-organization,
organizational-normativity and organizational-affect (25);
distributed-agency, readiness, record-as-apparatus and nucleating-argument
(24); learned-structure, evidence-for-experience and formal-apparatus
(29); transient-support, pragmatic-transport and realizability (21). Each
worked in its own worktree; the filer combined the four.

## The moved conditions

The readers found that a question holding a reading's former
`promote_when` (ADR-065) usually had no relation from that reading: the
move wrote the question and not the tie. Of 129 questions that name their
parent reading, 95 lacked it. The readers wrote some; the filer wrote the
remaining 73, each `bears_on` from the reading, with a history note quoting
the question's own words. The skill now says the move writes it.
