# ADR-028: ecology joins the topics

## Context

The owner's coral batch (four readings) had no topic for its subject:

- the holobiont paradigm;
- bleaching as dysbiosis;
- moonlight-cued mass spawning;
- herbivory and reef resilience.

The filing agent had to tag every one `natural-sciences` plus
`complex-systems`. That says neither what the works are about nor what
someone browsing for them would look under. Two subjects were missing:

- **Community and ecosystem ecology.** Hughes et al. is a field
  experiment on how herbivory governs reef recovery, and its theory is a
  claim about community interactions.
- **Symbiosis.** The holobiont and bleaching reviews are about a
  partnership between host and microbes. `individuation` and
  `philosophy-of-biology` cover only the question of which entity is the
  individual.

## Decision

Add **`ecology`**: how organisms interact with each other and with their
environment. That includes:

- symbiosis and host–microbe partnerships;
- herbivory and trophic interactions;
- community structure;
- resilience and regime shifts in ecosystems.

Tags in the batch:

- Hughes et al. and its theory take `ecology` first.
- The two holobiont reviews and the bleaching theory take it second, after
  `natural-sciences`.
- The spawning paper keeps `natural-sciences` first, because its subject
  is timing. It does not take `ecology`.

Existing documents that are about ecology are retagged as they are next
touched. About twenty LITs use ecological vocabulary, and a sweep is a
separate piece of work.

## Alternatives considered

- **`natural-sciences` plus `complex-systems`.** That is what the filing
  agent was forced into. It gives the subject no word.
- **Separate `symbiosis` and `community-ecology` words.** With four papers,
  one word for interactions between organisms is enough. Split it when the
  holdings warrant it.
- **`chronobiology` for the spawning paper.** One paper is too few to name
  a topic. Revisit if more work on biological timing arrives.
