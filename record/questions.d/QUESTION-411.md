# QUESTION-411: Does an independent replicated comparison of applied nucleation, plantation and natural regeneration, 15 years or more after maintenance ended, show both planted treatments raising later-successional seedling and sapling recruitment and lowering among-site compositional variability relative to natural regeneration, while the tree layer in every treatment stays dominated by a few pioneers and the planted cohort, whose own seedlings seldom reach the sapling layer?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1488](../theory.d/THEORY-1488.md) reports one experiment, in ten southern Costa Rican sites in a
seed-rich landscape, with planting intensity confounded with treatment.
The authors suggest recruits may yet converge across treatments.

## What would answer it

A replicated comparison elsewhere, 15 years or more after maintenance
ended, finding the same recruitment and variability contrasts with a
canopy still of pioneers and the planted cohort, would answer yes; natural
regeneration catching up would answer no.
