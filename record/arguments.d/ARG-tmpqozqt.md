# ARG-tmpqozqt: Synchronization phenomena, used as model systems, can separate the record's distinctions of compatibility, dependence and suppression, because a driven and suppressed oscillator and a coupled pair with a relative phase separate them by intervention, though a drive fixes where control lies and not whether it is coercive

## The inference

1. In [CASE-tmpecdgv](../cases.d/CASE-tmpecdgv.md), a forced Stuart–Landau oscillator, a self-sustained,
   a damped and a suppressed oscillator all answer the drive. Only the
   withdrawals tell them apart: removing the drive alone, or the drive and
   the control together. A12: "The distinction cannot be recovered from
   the output alone." So dependence for timing, dependence for
   performance and suppression of a latent capacity are three things
   there.
2. In [THEORY-1082](../theory.d/THEORY-1082.md), phase relations on a graph can each be met alone and
   not all together. The obstruction is the phase accumulated around a
   cycle, and the smallest eigenvalue of the twisted Laplacian measures
   what is left over.
3. In [THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md), a coupled pair has a collective variable, relative
   phase, with its own stable states and a switch between them at a
   critical frequency.
4. The record's distinctions have the same shape. Feasible against
   realized is [CLAIM-567](../claims.d/CLAIM-567.md)'s split, which [CLAIM-tmpk1015](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk1015.md) carries over to
   locking. Supplied against endogenous maintenance is [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s and
   [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)'s. A combined form with a collective variable of its own is
   [CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md)'s.
5. So oscillator models in which each of these can be set independently
   are a laboratory for the distinctions ([CLAIM-tmpg7aqc](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpg7aqc.md)).

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is that the respect the
  conclusion needs?** In each premise an intervention or a construction
  separates what the output does not. The conclusion needs exactly that.
  Its defeat condition asks for an arm "that could come out otherwise",
  and [CASE-tmpecdgv](../cases.d/CASE-tmpecdgv.md)'s two withdrawals are such arms. The claim needs the
  distinctions to be separable and testable in the model. It does not
  need organizations to be oscillators: in its own words, "the claim is
  about model systems, not analogy". The form is analogy all the same,
  since what the laboratory is worth to the record rests on the model's
  distinctions having the shape of the target's.
- **What relevant difference is there?** Four.
  - **A drive is not coercion.** A12 §4.3: "A biological pacemaker, an
    externally supplied clock, a therapeutic intervention, and a coercive
    controller can all produce phase entrainment. The dynamics alone do
    not establish the normative relationship." In [CASE-tmpecdgv](../cases.d/CASE-tmpecdgv.md) the
    control u holds the oscillator at rest whatever its standing. So the
    third mapping, the driven oscillator as a model of autonomy against
    coercion, transfers the location of control and not its legitimacy.
    Legitimacy is [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)'s question of domination, and nothing in the
    dynamics models it. The claim's title already carries the
    qualification.
  - **Energetic against ordinal frustration.** [THEORY-1082](../theory.d/THEORY-1082.md)'s residual
    eigenvalue and [THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md)'s potential in relative phase are energies
    on one scale. [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)'s frustration is ordinal: "In its
    basic form it is an empty intersection of acceptance sets and needs
    no common scale." The oscillator setting can show that a set of
    demands is infeasible, which is an ordinal fact. Its measure of how
    far the demands miss is not [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)'s, and [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)'s strain, demands
    met jointly at a cost, has no counterpart in it that is not an energy.
    [ARG-054](ARG-054.md) records the same difference for a mechanical network.
  - **The phase variable.** A suppressed oscillator at rest has no phase,
    so a demand stated on phase differences does not apply to it. The
    suppression arm of [CASE-tmpecdgv](../cases.d/CASE-tmpecdgv.md) and the locking demands of
    [CLAIM-tmpk1015](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk1015.md) are stated on different variables. Combining them in
    one model needs amplitudes as well as phases.
  - **A switch is not a combination.** [THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md)'s switch is between two
    patterns of a pair that is already coupled, not the onset of a
    combined form. A12 §11, as the claim quotes it, says the move from
    externally organized coherence to internal maintenance "is not
    necessarily a synchronization transition". [THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md)'s evidence for
    the switch is thin as well: the variability rose "in a few subjects and
    with no statistical test".
- **What transfers, and what does not?** What transfers is that the
  distinctions are coherent and can be separated by intervention, and with
  it the design of the tests: withdrawal arms, locking set against
  feasibility, a collective variable's switch. What does not transfer is
  any finding that an organization behaves as the model does, the
  normative relation between controller and controlled, or the energetic
  measure of frustration.
- **Does [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)'s objection apply?** [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md), now Superseded by
  [ARG-016](ARG-016.md), objected that "synchronization" among an agent's valuations is
  analogy without mechanism. In coordination dynamics synchronization has
  "coupled components, a collective variable (relative phase) and
  transitions between stable patterns", and valuations that fail to cross
  have none of these. [ARG-016](ARG-016.md) keeps that verdict: its question "what
  relevant difference is there?" fails on the mechanism. It does not apply
  to the source here. Literal oscillators have all three parts by
  construction. Nor does it apply to the laboratory claim, so far as that
  claim's results are existence results about model systems, like
  [CASE-tmpecdgv](../cases.d/CASE-tmpecdgv.md)'s: an existence result needs a coherent case, not a
  shared mechanism. It applies again at every transfer. A reading of an
  oscillator result as a fact about an organization has to find the
  coupling, the collective variable and the transitions in the
  organization, or it is [ARG-016](ARG-016.md)'s analogy again. The claim concedes this
  in what it does not say: "That the oscillator results transfer to
  organizations without a mapping argued case by case."

Proposed. The respect holds, and the differences limit what transfers
without defeating the claim. The defeat condition, that the setting adds
no constraint a result could fail, waits on the first project. That
project is [CASE-179](../cases.d/CASE-179.md)'s study with the three arms A12 adds (forcing
withdrawal, suppression of oscillation, adaptive capacities), and it has
not been run.

## What it does not show

- That forcing is coercion, or that synchrony marks an individual
  ([CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)).
- The fourth mapping, entrainment and phase locking as individuation
  transitions. Only [THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md) bears on it, and it shows a pattern
  switching, not parts combining.
