# TERM-tmpk4a7y: outcome valence (Lewin's sense)

## Definition

A property of an outcome, directed at the system: the outcome draws the
system's movement toward itself or drives it away. In the record's model of
winning dynamics ([CLAIM-tmp650up](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp650up.md)), the pull of a terminal component of
the condensation is the probability of ending in it. That holds for any
terminal component, including a cycle, so an organization can have an
outcome valence toward indecision. scratch/studies/lead-tmpuszbx/indecision.py
computes it as 0.5 or 0.8, depending on the distribution over starts.

Its job is to name the quantity the owner's "valence toward indecision"
measures, without equivocating with [TERM-076](TERM-076.md).
