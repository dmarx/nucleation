## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request for Dennett. It is listed for what
it would say to the record's consciousness thread: Dennett's own adoption of
Frankish's word "illusionism" for the position of "Quining Qualia"
([LIT-tmprhpc4](LIT-tmprhpc4.md)) and "Facing Backwards" ([LIT-tmp3fuxm](LIT-tmp3fuxm.md)), both read. The record

([LIT-tmpn694x](LIT-tmpn694x.md)), which argues that only strong illusionism dissolves the hard
problem and that it is false. A reading should check whether this paper
answers that kind of objection or only sets the burden of proof.

**On the society-of-mind bridge ([THEORY-tmp31lxe](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp31lxe.md)).** Whether the paper bears
on it cannot be said unread. If illusionism is the default, the THEORY's
narrowed question, whether a group has experience beyond agency, becomes the
question whether a group has the illusion, that is, an introspective model
that represents its states as phenomenal. That framing is mine.
