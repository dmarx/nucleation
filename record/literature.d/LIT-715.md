# LIT-715: The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology, Culture, and Deviance at NASA

Diane Vaughan (1996), *The Challenger Launch Decision: Risky Technology,
Culture, and Deviance at NASA*, University of Chicago Press —
DOI-10.7208/chicago/9780226851785.001.0001

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What the record holds of it comes through the
Columbia Accident Investigation Board's chapter 8 ([LIT-734](LIT-734.md)). The
chapter cites the book for its central claim that engineering decisions on
O-ring erosion normalized the anomaly, and cites its pages for the
Challenger decision history. Vaughan was a researcher for the Board, but
the chapter is unsigned, so nothing it says is attributed to her here.
