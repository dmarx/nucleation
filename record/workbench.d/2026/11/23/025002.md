- **The reruns were the pass.** Every simulation CASE the batch filed was rerun from scratch by a reviewer and reproduced byte for byte, except [CASE-402](../../record/cases.d/CASE-402.md). For it, the summaries regenerated and one SGD job and two posterior blocks were rerun, but not the whole sweep, which would take hours; its history says so.
- **Three errors in entries filed in the last two days,** all found by re-deriving: a false clause on causal states ([CLAIM-1071](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1071.md), filed by the coordinator the same day; a two-state Markov drive keeps 0.38 nats the next input does not carry), a join-closure the proof had not shown ([CLAIM-1046](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1046.md)), and a title more general than its body ([CLAIM-1150](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1150.md)).
- **[ADR-127](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md) alternatives hold most of what is left.** Of the forty-seven kept, about a third wait on a reading the owner holds, filed as alternatives or as an objection to the owner's own thesis. The pass did not settle any of them under the delegation, since the regime puts the choice among alternatives with the owner.
- **Concessions on the owner's claims were not written in.** Promoting [CLAIM-1185](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1185.md), [CLAIM-1039](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1039.md) or [CLAIM-1108](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1108.md) would make a concession due on [CLAIM-1024](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1024.md), [CLAIM-1133](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1133.md) or [CLAIM-1201](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1201.md), each the owner's.
