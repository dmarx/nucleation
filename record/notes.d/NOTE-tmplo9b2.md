# NOTE-tmplo9b2: Postman (1992) — Technopoly, chapter 1 "The Judgment of Thamus"

## Contribution

The opening statement of the book's argument.

## Key results

1. "Every technology is both a burden and a blessing".
2. Technology redefines "freedom", "truth" and "intelligence", among "all the words we live by".
3. "Technological change is neither additive nor subtractive. It is ecological."
4. The chapter's last sentence: "I call it Technopoly."

## Assumptions

As in [NOTE-tmpu15jn](NOTE-tmpu15jn.md).

## Concepts

Trade-off; ideological bias of tools; ecological change; Technopoly.
