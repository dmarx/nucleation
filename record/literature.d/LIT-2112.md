# LIT-2112: Integrating local energetics into Maxwell-Calladine constraint counting to design mechanical metamaterials

Rocks, J. W. & Mehta, P. (2024). Integrating local energetics into Maxwell-Calladine constraint counting to design mechanical metamaterials. *Physical Review E* 110(2): 025002. doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.110.025002. arXiv:2208.07419 (v1 2022-08-15).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1557](../notes.d/NOTE-1557.md). Sections are the arXiv v2's.*

- **The classical count's blind spot.** "its exclusive reliance on the
  geometric relationships between constraints and degrees of freedom
  completely neglects the actual energetic costs of deforming individual
  components" (abstract).
- **A generalized index theorem.** Stated in susceptibilities, it "holds
  even in the presence of nonzero prestress or heterogeneous bond
  stiffnesses" (Sec. IV).
- **Same stresses, different forces.** "The ZDTs and SSSs are equivalent"
  (Theorem 1), but "in general, the LZEFs and LZMs span different
  subspaces" (Sec. V B).
- **Symmetry decides.** "LZMs are governed by geometric symmetry, while
  LZEFs are governed by the overall symmetry of the elastic response"
  (Sec. VI A); symmetry-breaking prestress makes the exciting force
  localize "to the line of prestressed bonds" (Sec. VI C).
- **Linear only.** The counts "still do not fully capture the effects of
  prestress-induced rigidity" (Sec. VII).

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on constraint architectures cites it for the
classical Maxwell–Calladine identity and its caveat, which the paper
re-derives (Sec. II E) but does not contribute; the paper's own results
are Theorem 1, the symmetry theorem and the use of prestress to steer
which force excites a mechanism. The synthesis also credits it with the
point that redundant constraints can create states of self-stress; the
paper does not say this (the word "redundant" does not occur in it).
Damavandi et al. ([LIT-2041](LIT-2041.md)) state it, and Connelly and Whiteley
([LIT-2080](LIT-2080.md)) give a stronger instance. Filed from it: [THEORY-1414](../theory.d/THEORY-1414.md).
