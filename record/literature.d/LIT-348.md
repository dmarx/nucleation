  [ARXIV-1812.06162](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06162). The paper derives
  a local model in which the loss progress one step can make at batch size
  B is ΔL_max/(1 + B_noise/B), with B_noise = tr(HΣ)/GᵀHG (eqs. 2.7–2.8).
  Averaged over a run at fixed B, the model gives the steps/examples
  hyperbola (S/S_min − 1)(E/E_min − 1) = 1, which defines the critical
  batch B_crit = E_min/S_min (eqs. 2.11–2.12). Across eight tasks, B_crit
  spans about six orders of magnitude (from 20 to over 10 million, §3),
  and the Hessian-free B_simple = tr(Σ)/|G|² tracks it to about an order
  of magnitude (Table 1: run-averaged B_simple/B_crit runs from 0.05 for
