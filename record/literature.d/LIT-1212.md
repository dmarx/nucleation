# LIT-1212: Multiple-gradient descent algorithm (MGDA) for multiobjective optimization

Désidéri, J.-A. (2012). Multiple-gradient descent algorithm (MGDA) for multiobjective optimization. *Comptes Rendus Mathématique* 350(5–6): 313–318. doi:10.1016/j.crma.2012.03.014. First as INRIA Research Report RR-6953, "Multiple-Gradient Descent Algorithm (MGDA)", June 2009.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1401](../notes.d/NOTE-1401.md)).

## Why this is here

A33 of the exchange used its criterion, without a source: "If zero lies in the convex hull of the task gradients, there is no common strict first-order descent direction" (0 ∉ conv{g_1,…,g_n}). A34's dual certificate and A35's residual build on it. Bears on [CLAIM-366](../claims.d/CLAIM-366.md) (common first-order descent is invariant under reparameterization; the sign of a gradient inner product is not) and [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) (the residual's dual certificate and governance regret).
