# LEAD-056: Find studies in which a term is added to, lost from or merged in a language's inventory and the use of its neighbours is measured against terms outside the field

## What it would do

Search the lexical-field literature.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-082](../claims.d/CLAIM-082.md): "It waits on a study that adds, loses or merges a term in a
  language's inventory and measures the neighbouring terms' use against
  terms outside the field."

## What would count

Neighbours that shift while outside terms do not supports [CLAIM-082](../claims.d/CLAIM-082.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out as a reading lead

A delegated worker carried out the lead on branch
claude/lead-056-lexical-fields; the queries and what each returned are in
scratch/studies/lead-056/search.md, the steps in PROGRESS.md beside it.

- **Found.** No study that adds, loses or merges a term and measures the
  neighbours' use against terms outside the field as a control. The
  literature has three near kinds: natural additions in colour with the
  old terms re-measured, contact-driven restructuring measured against
  monolingual speakers, and corpus studies of what a rising word takes
  from its neighbours, measured in frequency.
- **Read.** Karjus, Blythe, Kirby and Smith 2020 ([LIT-tmpfcwau](../literature.d/LIT-tmpfcwau.md),
  [NOTE-tmpfs86b](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpfs86b.md), [THEORY-tmpqveji](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpqveji.md)); Malik-Moraleda et al. 2023
  ([LIT-tmpyky5z](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyky5z.md), [NOTE-tmpkl6ez](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpkl6ez.md), [THEORY-tmplzcav](../theory.d/THEORY-tmplzcav.md)); the Nafaanra CogSci
  abstract ([LIT-tmpom3sr](../literature.d/LIT-tmpom3sr.md), [NOTE-tmpimci6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpimci6.md), [THEORY-tmp7i21v](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7i21v.md)), standing in
