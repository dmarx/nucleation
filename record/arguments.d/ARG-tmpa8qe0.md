# ARG-tmpa8qe0: Rocks and Mehta's prestress, placed on different bonds of a self-stress space fixed by geometry, changes which force excites a zero mode and retunes stability while the zero modes and self-stresses stay fixed, and so gives a mechanical parallel to redistributing and exploiting a mismatch without relief, though it shows the exciting force moving and not a given load

## The inference

1. In a central-force network, the geometry fixes the zero modes and the states of self-stress. Prestress is a load carried on a self-stress (NOTE-1557).
2. Prestress placed on different bonds of the same self-stress space leaves both unchanged. It moves the force that couples to a zero mode onto the prestressed bonds (Sec. VI C–D, two networks). Prestress can also stabilize the network with the counts unchanged (Sec. VII: "the numbers of LZMs and LZEFs do not change").
3. So the mechanical case has, in parallel, CLAIM-588's second and third targets without its first.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is it the respect the conclusion needs?** In both, a structure fixed by the demands is left unchanged: the compatibility complex in one case, ker C and ker Cᵀ in the other. Meanwhile something carried on that structure, or done with it, changes. That is the respect CLAIM-588's "without the first" needs.
- **What relevant difference is there?** There are three.
  - A designer places the mechanical load. In the paper's examples it is added rather than moved: Fig. 3(a) has none.
  - What localizes is the force that excites a mode, not the load.
  - The frustration is energetic, which TERM-060's is not. CLAIM-588 already says its table carries across the two senses without identifying them.
- **What transfers, and what does not?** The separation transfers: what is carried, and what it does, can change while the compatibility structure stays fixed. "A given mismatch moved among the places it can sit" does not transfer from this reading. That point is CLAIM-538's, which stays the witness.
