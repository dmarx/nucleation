# THEORY-2123: Focal points come from team or level reasoning

## The account

Coordinators who team-reason choose the rule that is best for the pair,
expecting the partner to do likewise; coordinators who use cognitive
hierarchy reasoning guess what a naive picker would choose. Both modes
occur, in different contexts.

## Evidence

Two experiments with adult students (N = 161 and N = 134).
