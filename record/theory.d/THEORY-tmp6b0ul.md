## The account

Chesñevar et al. (2006) propose, as a draft consensus specification, an Argument Interchange Format
whose core represents arguments as directed graphs of information nodes (claims) and scheme-
application nodes (inference, preference and conflict applications), forbids direct links between
information nodes so that every connection between claims passes through a scheme application,
treats attack, rebut, undercut and defeat as derived concepts, and expects the core to evolve by
community consensus, leaving open how that community should agree on core concepts and extensions.
