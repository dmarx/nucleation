# LIT-tmpgjlp7: Identity fusion: The interplay of personal and social identities in extreme group behavior

Swann, W. B., Jr., Gómez, Á., Seyle, D. C., Morales, J. F. & Huici, C. (2009). *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 96(5), 995–1011.

Read in full in NOTE-tmp3g9n4, from the published version.

## Why this is here

It contradicts self-categorization theory's functional antagonism ([NOTE-tmp3dav9](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp3dav9.md)) and is the strongest empirical form of the owner's thesis that group sacrifice is self-interest on an enlarged self ([CLAIM-tmpjylq8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjylq8.md)).
