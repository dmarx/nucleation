# LIT-2267: Cycle flows and multistability in oscillatory networks

Debsankha Manik, Marc Timme and Dirk Witthaut (2017). "Cycle flows and multistability in oscillatory networks." *Chaos* 27(8): 083123. [ARXIV-1611.09825](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09825) DOI-10.1063/1.4994177.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-2339](../notes.d/NOTE-2339.md)). Phase locking split into flow conservation and a cycle condition; coexisting locked states differ by cycle flows and winding numbers; rings of four or fewer nodes are not multistable; strengthening one edge can destroy locking. Its 'geometric frustration' is not the record's sense.
