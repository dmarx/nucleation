# ADR-012: contextuality joins the topics

## Context

The record held five works on contextuality, all read in full. It had no word
to find them by. "Contextuality" appeared only as one item in the blurb of
`quantum-foundations`, so a reader browsing that topic met contextuality mixed
in with interference, toy theories and quantum thermodynamics.

The five works also formed two threads that nothing connected:

- **Kochen–Specker contextuality in sheaf form:** Abramsky & Brandenburger.
- **Spekkens's generalized contextuality:** Schmid et al.'s structure theorem,
  Spekkens's epistemically restricted theories, and Catani et al. on
  interference.

A sixth work, Grangier & Auffèves, uses the word for something else.

On 2026-09-27 the owner asked for the tag and for the threads to be brought
together.

## Decision

Add `contextuality` to `topics`, with a label and a blurb. It stands outside
the `philosophy` group, beside `quantum-foundations`.

Its blurb covers three things:

- both formal notions;
- noncontextual models and their limits;
- contextuality outside physics, since the Contextuality-by-Default programme
  applies the notion to behavioural and linguistic data.

| work | tags now |
| --- | --- |
| Abramsky & Brandenburger | `contextuality` first |
| Schmid et al. | `contextuality` first |
| Spekkens (2014), Catani et al. | `contextuality` second, after `quantum-foundations` |
| Grangier & Auffèves | `contextuality` second: the work is about context-dependence, even though it does not establish contextuality in either formal sense (see the THEORY placing it) |

Considered and not tagged, each with its reason in the curation entry of this
date:

- works that use "contextual" in an unrelated sense (contextual analysis,
  contextualism, contextual embeddings);
- the generalized-probabilistic-theory introductions;
- van Rijsbergen's *The Geometry of Information Retrieval*, whose seed gives no
  evidence that it treats contextuality.

`quantum-foundations` keeps "contextuality" in its blurb. A quantum-foundations
work about contextuality should carry both words.

## Alternatives

- **Leave it inside `quantum-foundations`.** That is what failed. The topic
  cannot be browsed, and its outside-physics leg has nowhere to go.
- **Two words, one per formal notion.** That would split works that bear on
  both, such as the Spekkens (2005) bridge and the review. Relating the two
  notions is exactly what the THEORY documents are for.
