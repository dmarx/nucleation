summary: >-
  Bragg et al. (2025), AstaBench: Rigorous Benchmarking of AI Agents with a
  Scientific Research Suite. The paper argues that existing agent benchmark
  suites lack standard reproducible tools, ignore confounders (cost, tool
  access), lack standard task interfaces for general agents, lack tasks
  drawn from real product use, and lack comprehensive baselines. It sets out
  five principles and builds AstaBench to meet them: 11 benchmarks and
  2,400+ problems in four categories, namely literature understanding, code
  and execution, data analysis, and end-to-end discovery. Alongside come a
  reproducible literature-search environment with date cut-offs (Asta
  Scientific Corpus), a cost-accounting toolkit and leaderboard
  (agent-eval), and an agent-baselines suite. It evaluates 57 agents across
  22 classes. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
