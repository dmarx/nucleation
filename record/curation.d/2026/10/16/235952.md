The owner asked how the record stood on Granger causality, and then for
the gaps found to be filled. The term turned up in nine documents, all
through other authors: [NOTE-053](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-053.md) and [THEORY-641](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-641.md) on transfer entropy, the
causal-emergence readings ([NOTE-010](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-010.md), [NOTE-726](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-726.md), [NOTE-791](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-791.md), [THEORY-497](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-497.md)) and
[NOTE-122](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-122.md) on IIT. Four gaps were named. The defining works were not held,
the record had no argument of its own on the subject, [NOTE-010](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-010.md)'s C5 was
unchecked, and [LIT-048](../../record/literature.d/LIT-048.md) did not carry `causality`.

## The record's own argument

- [TERM-tmpq0ms2](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpq0ms2.md) fixes the word: predictive, relative to an information set,
  its lags and, for the measure, a model class.
- [CLAIM-tmp6nu4a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6nu4a.md) answers C5. Measuring every relevant variable is
  necessary and not sufficient. Two series with nothing hidden, where a
  acts on b within one sampling step, give Granger noncausality and an
  intervention effect of ργ. It was checked by hand and by simulation.
- [CLAIM-tmpa4kr6](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa4kr6.md) is the disclaimer the Seth reading called for. Granger
  causation from a macro variable to the micro variables it supervenes on
  is not an interventional effect, because no intervention on the macro
  variable holds its micro base fixed.
- No QUESTION was filed. C5 was the open question, and the claim answers
  it.
