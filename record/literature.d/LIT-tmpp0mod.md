  tasks are possible and which impossible. A set of attributes is an
  information variable when all its permutations and its cloning are
  possible; distinguishability is defined from that, which gives the
  recursive definition of distinguishing a base. Conjectured principles
  (interoperability, consistency of measurement, ensemble
  distinguishability and others) cover classical information. One further
  prohibition, disjoint information observables whose union is not one
  ("superinformation"), yields indistinguishable states, no-cloning,
  complementarity, deterministic unpredictability, measurement disturbance
  and locally inaccessible information; quantum information is an
  instance.

## Why this is here

A11 called it "the strongest new connection to fundamental physics": a
constraint stated as the structure of possible and impossible
transformations, realized by physical substrates, which "could sharpen
what Nucleation means by a constraint being real". A11 also said it
"does not itself entail relational constitution". It bears on [CLAIM-tmpmand1](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmand1.md); the reading tests both remarks
([NOTE-tmpbcj9n](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpbcj9n.md), Bearing on the record).
