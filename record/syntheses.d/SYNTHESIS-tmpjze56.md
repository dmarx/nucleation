# SYNTHESIS-tmpjze56: Freedom of will comes in degrees along separable dimensions, compared by inclusion of what an agent can viably reach, and in the operational sense the record proposes it is an agent's governance of its own governability; sensitivity and controllability fall short of it, self-formation is the production of a new distinction or channel that outlasts its occasion, and explaining efficacy does not settle whose the will is

*A part account of the will-organization line (ADR-083, ADR-084); the line's account is built over it.*

## The account

**Degrees, not a scalar.** CLAIM-354 (the owner's U9 of 2026-10-17, worked
out by A9, A10 and A15; Active) rests on CLAIM-079, CLAIM-025 and CLAIM-345:
freedom of will and autonomy come in degrees along several dimensions,
compared "by inclusion of what they can viably reach under matched
conditions", a partial order. CLAIM-345 (A9 and A12; demonstrated, Active):
equal controllability rank does not fix the cost of control. CLAIM-352 (the
owner's U11, worked out by A11; Active) rests on CLAIM-079 and CLAIM-027:
Sen's opportunity aspect is what viable reachability can stand for only
through a map to valued functionings, and will-organization gives a causal
account of part of the process aspect.

**Sensitivity is not control.** CLAIM-542 (demonstrated, Active) rests on
CLAIM-345: sensitivity does not fix the allocation or authorship of
control. CLAIM-533 (the owner's U16 and U20; Active) rests on CLAIM-354,
CLAIM-345 and CLAIM-542 and bears on QUESTION-071: the sensitivity formal
chaos gives Kane's self-forming actions magnifies the agent's efforts,
fluctuations and outside inputs alike.

**What normative efficacy needs.** CLAIM-523 (the owner's synthesis at review
of 2026-10-31; Active) uses TERM-064, TERM-023 and TERM-085 and bears on
QUESTION-364: legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability, any of
which can fail while the others hold. CLAIM-535 (A33; demonstrated,
Active) rests on CLAIM-350: a policy acceptable in every circumstance exists
exactly when the circumstances an observation cannot tell apart share an
acceptable action, so which distinctions must be legible depends on the
policy. CLAIM-622 (A58; demonstrated, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-535: two
organizations can each be minimally adequate while drawing incomparable
distinctions.

**Governance of governability.** CLAIM-545 (A26's wording at the owner's
request for the strong form; Proposed) rests on CLAIM-354, CLAIM-523,
CLAIM-485, CLAIM-424, CLAIM-025, CLAIM-027, CLAIM-404 and CLAIM-533 and bears
on QUESTION-364 and QUESTION-071: freedom of will in the operational sense
is "an agent's governance of its own governability". Its "Admission"
section gives the separating case (two agents matched in present
organization, one manipulated) and closes "relevant" by CLAIM-523.
CLAIM-544 (the reader's draft at the owner's U31; Proposed) rests on
CLAIM-545 and CLAIM-170: governing one's governability is in part governing
one's own individuation. CLAIM-610 (A7, in the supposed voice; Proposed;
distributed-agency first) rests on CLAIM-545, CLAIM-027, CLAIM-025 and three
claims of other lines: if the record's hypotheses held, agency and autonomy
would be further organizations of one individuated unit. CLAIM-620
(Proposed; pragmatic-transport first) rests on CLAIM-535 among six premises:
concepts are individuated over time by the transmission criterion, so in
reflexive social systems meaning and will remake each other. CLAIM-610 and
CLAIM-620 each carry an "Admission" section.

**Self-formation.** CLAIM-532 (A33's account, sharpened by the reader;
Active) rests on CLAIM-025, CLAIM-347, CLAIM-522, CLAIM-540 and CLAIM-535 and
uses TERM-099: self-formation is the production, by the agent's own
activity on a discrepancy, of a maintained new distinction or channel that
outlasts it and serves a class of later ones; it is told apart from a
learned action by transfer and from outside installation by interventions
on its production. TERM-099 (Active) refines TERM-023 and fixes the sense;
its summary says CLAIM-532 "is the thesis that it is what TERM-023's
transformation dimension produces". CASE-191 (Active) illustrates CLAIM-532,
a construction in which the first test passes and the second is narrowed
to interventions made during formation. ARG-060 (Proposed) rests on
CLAIM-535 and concludes CLAIM-532 from CASE-194: a new distinction and a new
channel are separate repairs. CLAIM-540 (A13; disclaimed, demonstrated,
Active) rests on CLAIM-414: the persistence a Dirichlet–categorical
hierarchy gives is an observer's learning, "not a change in what selects".

**Whose the will is.** CLAIM-022 (the paper's, Section 7.2; disclaimed,
Active) uses TERM-100: explaining practical efficacy does not settle whose
the will is. TERM-100 (Active) separates formation, maintenance and source
provenance, after CASE-191 found that only maintenance provenance can be
read off a present organization. QUESTION-071 (Open) asks whether a
will-organization can be manipulated with nothing in its present
structure showing it; QUESTION-364 (A29's; Open) asks what makes a revision
the continuation of the agent's will. CLAIM-356 (the owner's U35;
Active; learned-structure first) uses TERM-100: an objective that
originates outside a learning system can become a goal of the training
organization.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim.

- **A learned authority prior as self-formation.** That CLAIM-443's learned
  prior over which processes govern meets CLAIM-540's condition (what
  selects changes) and fails CLAIM-532's (no new distinction or channel).
  An application of a definition, so trivial. It would bear on CLAIM-532's
  defeater, "only a reweighting of existing regimes", only if an entry
  counted such a reweighting as self-formation, and none does: TERM-099
  leaves it open.

## Not stated on the record

- Whether a reweighting of existing regimes by the agent's own activity is
  self-formation (TERM-099 leaves it open; CLAIM-532's defeater turns on it).
- A historical condition on freedom: CLAIM-545's "What it does not say"
  leaves THEORY-040 and QUESTION-071 as its defeater, not something it
  answers.

## What it does not say

- That the agent's governance is justified (CLAIM-545, CLAIM-523).
- Anything about metaphysical free will or ultimate responsibility
  (CLAIM-354, CLAIM-022).
- That the present profile settles whose the will is (CLAIM-022,
  QUESTION-071).
- Anything with standing of its own.
