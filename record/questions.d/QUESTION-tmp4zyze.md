# QUESTION-tmp4zyze: On a contested shared canvas, do lasting artworks as a population receive sustained overwrites and repairs, and does adding interaction features make coalition survival predictable early?

## Why it is a question

Wu and Silva ([LIT-1032](../literature.d/LIT-1032.md), read in full in [NOTE-724](../notes.d/NOTE-724.md)) recover coalitions,
failed ones included, from the 2017 r/place placement log. Two of their
results stop short of what the record would like to say. Successful
coalitions are shown working under sustained overwriting, but only in ten
chosen lifecycles, so it is not known whether lasting artworks typically
persist that way or mostly go unattacked. Falmagne et al. ([THEORY-214](../theory.d/THEORY-214.md))
find that some compositions are safe through neglect. And survival is
barely predictable early from size, area, start time and colour entropy,
but no interaction feature was tried, so the unpredictability may lie in
the feature set and not in the dynamics. Until 2026-10-19 both were
[THEORY-216](../theory.d/THEORY-216.md)'s promote_when. [THEORY-216](../theory.d/THEORY-216.md)'s body disclaimed them, so they moved
here when its title was narrowed to what the study measured.

The first half bears on [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md)'s `defeated_if` and on [CLAIM-273](../claims.d/CLAIM-273.md)'s
reading of a maintained artwork as a dissipative structure. The second
bears on [QUESTION-093](QUESTION-093.md)'s latent-coalition dynamics, and the recovery
method's reliance on behaviour alone bears on [QUESTION-084](QUESTION-084.md).

## What would answer it

A measurement on full placement logs, in r/place or a comparable
contested canvas, that tracks recovered or recorded coalitions through
time. It would answer the first half yes if artworks lasting to the end
received sustained overwrites and repairs throughout, measured as a
population and not in hand-picked lifecycles, and no if lasting artworks
turn out mostly unattacked. It would answer the second half by adding
interaction features (who attacks whom, shared members, alliance ties) to
the state features. If early outcomes stay near-unpredictable, the
unpredictability is in the dynamics. If they become predictable, it was
in the feature set. More illustrated lifecycles cannot settle it, and
neither can better final-canvas segmentation scores, which validate the
recovery method and not the dynamics.
