## The finding for MuEdit

AlphaEdit's footnote 1 gives the numerical null space: eigenvectors of
K0K0ᵀ with eigenvalues at most 10⁻². This is the criterion MuEdit's
Conflict Index must rest on, which that filing found unstated. The NOTE
records it, naming MuEdit by pull request. MuEdit's own entries are left
as filed. Its NOTE already says the index presumably rests on AlphaEdit's
preprocessing, and this confirms that AlphaEdit has such a threshold.
