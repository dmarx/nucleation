# LEAD-tmpotdfw: Tell a governance heuristic from an action heuristic: after clear feedback in one of several tasks that share a cue, test whether the shift toward deliberation generalizes across the tasks that share it, including those where intuition is still right, measured by response time and markers of deliberation apart from the choice

## The gap

[CLAIM-tmpn754o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpn754o.md) (A's, Proposed): beside heuristics that select what to do
"there are governance heuristics, learned priors over which perspective or
procedure determines what to do". Its note of 2026-11-24 gives the
prediction that separates the two, and Article 4 §4.8.3 says "no lead
holds it". [CLAIM-tmpysg95](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpysg95.md) rests on it.

## The design, fixed before any run

From [CLAIM-tmpn754o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpn754o.md)'s note of 2026-11-24, unchanged:

1. **Context**: several tasks share a cue, and intuition was reliable in
   all of them (a training phase in which fast answers are right).
2. **Change**: alter the cue–outcome regularity in some of the tasks, so
   that the intuitive answer is now wrong there while explicit analysis is
   still available and right.
3. **Feedback**: clear feedback in one of the changed tasks only.
4. **Measures**: accuracy per task; response time and markers of
   deliberation (for example requests for the available analysis), which
   measure the allocation apart from the choice.
5. **Tasks read**: the task with feedback; the other changed tasks; and
   the unchanged tasks that share the cue.

## Expectations, stated before any run

- **An action heuristic** is corrected task by task: errors fall in the
  task that got feedback, and nothing changes in the others.
- **A governance heuristic** is an allocation learned on the context:
  before feedback, fast intuitive answers persist in the changed tasks;
  after it, the shift toward deliberation generalizes across the tasks
  that share the cue, *including those where intuition is still right*.
- **Defeaters**, as checks the record can run: (a) deliberation markers
  rise only in the task with feedback; (b) they rise in all tasks whether
  or not they share the cue, which is a general caution and not an
  allocation learned on the context; (c) the shift appears in choice but
  not in response time or deliberation markers, so the term would name a
  learned response, which [CLAIM-tmpn754o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpn754o.md)'s `defeated_if` forbids.

Because the design gives clear feedback, [CASE-346](../cases.d/CASE-346.md)'s identification limit,
which needs the protecting posture to mask the outcome, does not carry to
it ([CLAIM-tmpn754o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpn754o.md), note of 2026-11-25).

## Relation to other leads

[LEAD-tmpr4xwc](LEAD-tmpr4xwc.md) tests [CLAIM-tmp5atd3](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5atd3.md)'s co-variation of bias measures under
one intervention; this lead tests a single allocation's generalization
across tasks. The two could share a sample. Kahneman and Klein's
conditions for intuitive expertise ([LIT-tmpyshsf](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyshsf.md), Deferred) say where the
training phase's reliability is earned.
