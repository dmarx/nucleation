# SYNTHESIS-tmpoclez: The realizability line is four accounts, of reaching under resources, of budgeted individuation, of assembly and productive nucleation, and of persisting organized individuals, which meet at the separation of assembling, persisting and regenerating a form; the first three are densely joined, the persistence part points out and receives nothing, and no link between the parts that no part states passes the claim test

*The account of the realizability line ("Resource-bounded realizability
and individuation", ADR-073), over the accounts of its four parts
(ADR-083). It integrates every entry whose `line` includes realizability
after ADR-tmpqp8c4 (2026-11-11): 98 entries through the parts, and
CLAIM-566 directly. ADR-tmpqp8c4 gave QUESTION-398, QUESTION-399 and the
withdrawal entries transient-support as their first line, and none keeps
realizability. SYNTHESIS-003 carries the line and is Superseded by
CLAIM-611 (ADR-084), which SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2 integrates; SYNTHESIS-010 still lists
the line and is Superseded by CLAIM-600, now in transient-support. Neither
is integrated again.*

## The parts

- **Reaching under resources (SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o), 53 entries.** Feasible, reachable
  and reliably reached come apart (CLAIM-567); a transition's threshold is
  the least peak over admissible paths and can be infinite (CLAIM-571); a
  budget enlarges the compatibility complex only up to the unbudgeted one
  (CLAIM-586), and neither the compatibility nor the reachability
  filtration fixes the other (CLAIM-560); failed transitions sort into
  obstructions with their own repairs (CLAIM-568, CLAIM-556), and telling
  which one holds can be hard (CLAIM-575); an added constraint can improve
  reliability and never reach (CLAIM-582); and where an intervention acts
  can predict better than how much (CLAIM-562). A's general object and
  universality hypothesis are Proposed (CLAIM-576, CLAIM-573).
- **Budgeted individuation (SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0), 15 entries.** Observing splits and
  acting under peak costs merges (CLAIM-574, CLAIM-581), so individuation
  is indexed by two budgets (CLAIM-557), with computable frontiers
  (CLAIM-570) and an agreement the cost order of probes decides
  (CLAIM-592); the choice of category decides what counts as the same
  (CLAIM-590), and isomorphism is not persistence (CLAIM-593).
- **Assembly and productive nucleation (SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl), 20 entries.** A resource
  that sets its own costs gives a window (CLAIM-594); abundance helps or
  hurts by which steps consume it (CLAIM-598); persistence and
  productivity vary independently (CLAIM-595); yields cannot tell a
  generative nucleus from a head start (CLAIM-596); productive sets have no
  closure (CLAIM-599); and a productive nucleus is defined against a
  declared target, comparator and horizon (TERM-117).
- **Persisting organized individuals (SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2), 10 entries, all claims
  Proposed.** What persists is the organization that keeps a repertoire
  realizable (CLAIM-611), identity over time its causal transmission
  (CLAIM-615), histories need not glue and can be terminal (CLAIM-623,
  CLAIM-625), and synchronization is offered as a laboratory (CLAIM-616).
- **CLAIM-566** (A's, interpretive, Active; also distributed-agency): that a
  form assembles, that it persists over a specified horizon and that the
  system regenerates it after turnover are three properties with separate
  probabilities, and "a structure that assembles and persists without
  producing the constraints that would regenerate it does not show
  organizational closure".

## Where they meet

Every link here is stated by a component; the part accounts give each with
its words.

- **The shared threshold (SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o with SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0).** CLAIM-581 and CLAIM-590
  rest on CLAIM-571 and CLAIM-563: the threshold is "the hom of the
  max-enriched category of CLAIM-590" (CLAIM-581). CASE-206 is the toy of
  both parts: CLAIM-590 grounds on it, and it illustrates CLAIM-572 and
  CLAIM-592 and carries CLAIM-570's frontiers. CLAIM-572 reads a
  reallocation between observing and acting as CLAIM-571's threshold "in
  budget space rather than configuration space"; CLAIM-574 calls CLAIM-564

  three requirements.
- **Placement and the three properties (SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o with SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl).** CLAIM-599
  rests on CLAIM-562, and CASE-215 illustrates it; CASE-217, CASE-219 and
  CASE-220 illustrate CLAIM-567; CASE-216 illustrates CLAIM-569 and
  CLAIM-571; QUESTION-380 asks for "reliable emergence and not only
  feasibility (CLAIM-567)".
- **Fixed costs as a special case (SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0 with SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl).** CLAIM-594
  complements CLAIM-581 and CLAIM-570: budgeted reachability with fixed
  costs "is the special case of a resource that leaves the costs
  unchanged"; QUESTION-374's note of 2026-11-07 finds its integration held
  by CLAIM-592, CLAIM-570, CLAIM-572 and CLAIM-594.
- **The repertoire and the persisting individual (SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o and SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0 with
  SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2).** CLAIM-611 rests on CLAIM-576 and CLAIM-567; CLAIM-615 rests on
  CLAIM-593 and bears on QUESTION-393, whose table gives QUESTION-379 as its
  row for realizability.
- **The hinge.** CLAIM-566 is linked from every part. CLAIM-556 rests on
  it for its regeneration level (SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o); QUESTION-393 and QUESTION-382
  cite its separation of persisting from regenerating (SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0); CASE-219
  illustrates it and TERM-117 sets the productive nucleus apart from it
  (SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl); CLAIM-611 rests on it for its layer of maintenance (SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2).
  CLAIM-567's note of A's synthesis on DNA origami reads A's closing as
  "this claim together with CLAIM-566".

Outside the line, CLAIM-600 (transient-support) rests on CLAIM-566

- That the four parts are one inquiry; they are kept in one line because
  three are densely joined and the fourth rests on them, not because a
  component says they are.
