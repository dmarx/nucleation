summary: >-
  Singh (1999), An ontology for commitments in multiagent systems. Singh
  proposes a "descriptive ontology" of social commitments for multiagent
  systems. A commitment is a four-place relation C(x, y, G, p): debtor x,
  creditor y, context group G and discharge condition p (a condition to be
  achieved, evaluated in the world, not in any agent's mind). Six operations
  act on commitments: create, discharge, cancel, release, delegate, assign;
  all but discharge are typically performatives. Groups are first-class
  named agents; a "sphere of commitment" is a group with its roles and their
  commitments. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
