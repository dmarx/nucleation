# TERM-tmp4zjar: answerability, of an actor to a party concerning an action or governing power, under a declared standard, and which dimension: the normative relation in which the actor owes the party some form of answer, an account, reasons, responsiveness, an effective opportunity to contest, or judgment and redress; normative (whether it is owed), institutional (who may demand, assess and act on it), effective (whether it can become consequential) or maintained (whether it stays so); not the accountability mechanisms that realize it

## Definition

A83 §1 of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b), answering
the owner's U36 ([QUESTION-tmpea19q](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpea19q.md)), first separates five things "we have
been treating as answerability":

| Concept | What is being demanded? |
|---|---|
| **Intelligibility** | An account of what happened and how a decision was made |
| **Justification** | Reasons that could make the exercise of power permissible or authoritative |
| **Responsiveness** | Appropriate consideration of objections, evidence, and changed circumstances |
| **Contestability** | A practically effective opportunity to challenge the action or its governing standard |
| **Accountability and remedy** | The possibility of authoritative judgment, correction, consequences, or redress where warranted |

"An institution can explain a decision without justifying it", and can
invite objections "while denying them any practical influence". Then:
"For our purposes, I'd reserve **answerability** for the broader normative
relationship and **accountability mechanisms** for its institutional
realization."

As a relation, A83 §4 writes it Ans_N(A, B, x, φ): under N, A owes B the
form φ of answerability concerning x. When it is owed, and to whom, is
[CLAIM-tmpnhesz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnhesz.md).

A106 §17 splits it by dimension:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| **Normative answerability** | Does the actor owe reasons, consideration, or correction to this party? |
| **Institutional answerability** | Who possesses authority to request, assess, and act upon those reasons? |
| **Effective answerability** | Can the party's protected claims actually become consequential under foreseeable conditions? |
| **Maintained answerability** | Will those capabilities remain sufficiently available as the institution develops and changes? |

"These dimensions come apart. A defendant can owe a claimant a remedy even
when the claimant cannot afford to pursue it." The term is the first
dimension; the others are said with their qualifier.

## What it is for

The record already uses the word. [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty is "a second-order
duty of answerability"; [TERM-124](TERM-124.md) makes domination relative to "a declared
standard of answerability"; [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) says an arrangement's
"capacity to be effectively corrected by others" degrades apart from its
capacity to act. This term gives those uses one sense, with the
dimensions to say which part is at stake. [TERM-124](TERM-124.md)'s declared standard is
then a standard saying, for the arrangement in question, which forms of
answer are owed in which dimensions.

## What it is not

- **Not accountability mechanisms.** A83 §1: "One can owe an answer even
  when no adequate forum currently exists to demand it." Else "an actor
  could escape answerability merely by preventing the creation of an
  institution capable of holding it accountable."
- **Not a duty to everyone affected.** [CLAIM-tmpyqfrf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpyqfrf.md): influence does not
  by itself ground a duty to answer.
- **Not A74's "dynamic answerability"**, which is reachability of
  compliant arrangements and correctability ([CLAIM-tmpx1g4r](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx1g4r.md)). That is the
  effective and maintained dimensions without the normative one.
- **Not [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s ownership of a will**, the record's other use of
  "answerable", which [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) already set aside.
- **Not protected contestability** ([TERM-tmpsit04](TERM-tmpsit04.md)), which is one form of
  answer, contestation, made a maintained capability.

## What would settle it

Adoption: the owner's call. It would be unsettled by a use the record
needs that does not fit the relation, for instance an answerability owed
by an arrangement that is not an agent, which [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) leaves open.
