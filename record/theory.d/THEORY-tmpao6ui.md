## The account

Dierickx et al. propose that the quality of datasets used for machine learning in journalism be assessed against three principles of journalism ethics, accuracy, fairness and transparency, turned into data-quality checks across collection, cleaning, augmentation and labelling, on the view that trust in AI outputs starts with trust in their data.
