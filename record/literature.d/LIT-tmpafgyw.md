# LIT-tmpafgyw: The Ghost in the Machine

Arthur Koestler (1967), *The Ghost in the Machine*, London: Hutchinson; New York: Macmillan — <https://archive.org/details/ghostinmachine0000unse>

## Key takeaways

*Unread. From general knowledge, to be checked against the text.*

- **The holon.** Every stable subsystem is Janus-faced: autonomous as a
  whole, dependent as a part.
- **Two tendencies.** A self-assertive tendency that preserves the holon's
  individuality, and an integrative tendency by which it functions as part
  of the larger whole.
- **Holarchy.** A hierarchy of holons. Koestler draws on Simon (1962,
  [LIT-tmp6qp14](LIT-tmp6qp14.md)), whose watchmaker parable he retells; that he cites it is
  from general knowledge.
