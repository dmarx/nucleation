# LIT-995: Deep sequence models tend to memorize geometrically; it is unclear why

Shahriar Noroozizadeh, Vaishnavh Nagarajan, Elan Rosenfeld and Sanjiv Kumar (2025), *arXiv:2510.26745* (ICML 2026, per the arXiv comment).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpojahx](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpojahx.md).*

- **In-weights reasoning without step-wise aid.** On path-star graphs of up
  to 5×10⁴ nodes, a transformer and Mamba predict the first hop of unseen
  paths from first-token supervision alone, up to 100% against chance 1/d.
- **Geometry from local facts.** Training on edges alone already clusters
  each path's leaf and first node. On tiny graphs, the same architectures
  can store the edges as a lookup (frozen embeddings), find it in about 2
  steps rather than 100, and pay no more than about twice the bits or norm.
- **The two-layer spectral bias.** A wide weight-tied Node2Vec-style model
  under full-softmax cross-entropy ends with embeddings on the top
  eigenvectors of the symmetrized random-walk Laplacian, more skewed than
  the spectrum itself, with no bottleneck or regularizer. The account in
  App. F is informal and rests on an unproved observation.
- **Not pressure-free in every set-up.** Under unit-normal initialization
  some runs end associative. Weight decay, dropout and higher learning
  rates strengthen the geometry. The large-graph runs used weight decay.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-060](../questions.d/QUESTION-060.md), and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-tmpojahx](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpojahx.md). [THEORY-tmpn31af](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpn31af.md) is filed from it as a partial answer that
names the dynamics of training as a third determinant of the learned
variables, beside the objective and cheap representability. The seeded
reading holds, with two cautions. The spectral account covers only the
two-layer model. The paper's refutations target straightforward versions
of each pressure, and the authors leave subtler ones, such as flatness,
open. The paper's suggestion to make transformer memory more geometric is
practice and belongs to the anthology. The anthology-candidate flag stays.
