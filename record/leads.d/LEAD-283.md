# LEAD-283: Compare populations matched on present outcomes under a common disturbance, diversified against single-industry economies, car-dependent against multimodal cities, unionized against non-unionized workforces, to test whether equal performance hides different maintenance, development and authority

## The plan (A5 §6)

| Population A | Population B | Disturbance |
|---|---|---|
| Diversified local economy | Single-industry economy | Major employer closure |
| Car-dependent city | Multimodal city | Fuel supply interruption |
| Unionized workforce | Otherwise comparable non-unionized workforce | Unilateral contract changes |
| Proprietary infrastructure | Interoperable infrastructure | Provider withdrawal |
| Centralized public administration | Polycentric administration | Novel local crisis |
| Highly automated research institution | Less automated research institution | Invalidated evaluation standard |

The hypotheses, with the held claim each carries:

- **H1, maintenance provenance**: "Comparable present performance can
  conceal differences in the mechanisms that sustain it" ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)).
- **H2, developmental divergence**: comparable capabilities, different
  ability to develop new ones ([CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)).
- **H3, authority divergence**: comparable collective capabilities,
  different distributions of effective authority ([CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md)).
- **H4, readiness decay**: "Efficiency improvements can reduce future
  response capacity when the mechanisms maintaining that capacity are
  costly and infrequently exercised" ([CLAIM-921](../claims.d/CLAIM-921.md)).
- **H5, governed specialization**: externalization raising agency "when
  access to externalized capacities remains secure and effectively
  governable" ([CLAIM-603](../claims.d/CLAIM-603.md), [CLAIM-1364](../claims.d/CLAIM-1364.md)).

## What it needs before it is specified

A5: "Testing them would require careful counterfactuals, attention to
historical selection effects, and operational definitions of authority
and reachability." In particular:

- matching on present outcomes chosen before the disturbance, so that the
  match is not fitted to the response;
- for H1, an intervention or natural experiment that reaches the
  maintaining mechanism, since [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) shows recovery from state
  perturbation cannot tell the mechanisms apart, and a disturbance is a
  state perturbation unless it removes a maintainer;
- for H3, a measure of effective authority, which the record does not
  have ([LEAD-254](LEAD-254.md) asks for actor-indexed reachability).
