The owner asked for the record to account for the exchange of 2026-10-16
(workbench entry of 2026-10-16). It cited about thirty works. Each was
looked up before anything was filed, by a search that checked Crossref,
arXiv and OpenAlex for title, authors, identifier and dates.

**Corrections the lookup made to the exchange.** A6's relocation paper is
Wegner, Delia and Baker, "Fan response to the identity threat of potential
team relocation". A6's unnamed 2016 rivalry survey is Berendt and Uhrich,
"Enemies with benefits". A6's "Identity Trajectories" is Cloutier and
Ravasi (2020). Biel's title continues "European common in-group identity
formation through football fandom". A7's oral history is Alan Siegel's in
The Ringer (2024-03-20); its Kinberg quote comes via Polygon. The
Juggernaut parody was conceived in November 2005 and released on
2006-02-14, not in 2005; it redubbed an episode of the *X-Men* animated
series, as A7 said. Krakauer et al. has an arXiv preprint from 2014, which
dates it.

**Dates.** [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md)'s earlier-date rule decided four: Marquis and Tilcsik
(OpenAlex online date 2013-03-06, before Crossref's 2013-06), Wegner et al.
(online in January 2019, before the 2020 issue), DeMarzo et al. (the 2001
SSRN working paper) and Jia et al. (Crossref's month-only 2015-01, before
its online 2015-08-06). The two Jenkins books carry the print year, since
their DOIs are a 2020 online platform's.

**Not in the anthology.** A grep of the anthology's record/ (clone of
2026-10-16, commit d1573d2) for authors and titles found none of them.
None is about machine learning.
