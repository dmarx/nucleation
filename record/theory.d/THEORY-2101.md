# THEORY-2101: Tomasello proposes that agency is feedback-control organization built up in grades over the human lineage, goal-directed agency in the first vertebrates, intentional agency with an executive tier monitoring and controlling action and attention in the first mammals, metacognitive or rational agency with a second tier monitoring and controlling the executive tier in the first great apes, and shared agency, joint then collective, with normative self-regulation in early humans

## The account

Agents are feedback control systems that pursue goals, decide how to
pursue them and monitor execution. Early vertebrates make go/no-go
decisions; early mammals add an executive tier that monitors and controls
action and attention, choosing among options; great apes add a
metacognitive tier that monitors and controls executive decisions; early
humans share agency, first jointly with partners, then collectively with
the group, regulating themselves by shared norms.

## Evidence

Restated from the 2022 book with a few ape and child studies; no new data.

## Note of 2026-11-20: restated in a 2026 interview

Tomasello restates the typology orally ([LIT-2905](../literature.d/LIT-2905.md), [NOTE-2196](../notes.d/NOTE-2196.md)):
reptiles, as models of the first land vertebrates, are basic control
systems making simple decisions; mammals add an executive layer with
imaginative representations (intentional agency); great apes metacognitive
or rational agency; humans shared agency. This is a second, independent
statement by Tomasello of the four grades with their tiers and lineages,
close to what the promote_when asks; but his label for the first grade
wavers ("basic agency and then the goal directed agency"), so promotion is
left to the owner.
