---
title: 'An owner''s request to file a manuscript as a work was read as a LIT, when the record has a works vocabulary for it'
created: '2026-11-23T03:20:19'
tags:
- retro
---

**Going wrong, found by the owner.** Asked to "file it as a work" for the
manuscript ChatGPT drafted at U24 and U25 of "Explain First Contact RL",
the assistant read "work" as the LIT scheme's sense, a work read from
outside, and filed a LIT. The owner meant the `works` vocabulary, a
manuscript the record's claims are marshalled into, which ADR-104 and
ADR-106 had used twice before for manuscripts drafted the same way. The
LIT was reverted within the hour and ADR-tmpsfjxu filed instead.

**Why it happened.** The record uses "work" in two senses: docs/record.md
labels the LIT scheme "Works", and the `works` field is "a manuscript the
record's claims are marshalled into". The read-and-file skill speaks only
of the first. The signal that should have decided it was the source: an
assistant's draft written at the owner's request from the record's own
entries is the record's argument, not a reading, and both precedents said
so.

**What would catch it.** When the owner asks for a manuscript drafted in
an exchange to be filed, check the `works` vocabulary and ADR-104 first.
A line in the read-and-file skill's intake step would carry it; offered,
not applied.
