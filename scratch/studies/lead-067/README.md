# LEAD-067: three overlapping evaluative contexts, derived transport, and the null of trivial holonomy

Run on 2026-11-22 for LEAD-067 (QUESTION-130, A19 §8). Simulated constructions only; nothing downloaded.

- `transport.py`: the construction. Each of three contexts A, B, C holds vectors in R^d for the commitments it holds, in its own coordinates (gauge group O(d), which preserves the context's comparisons, its inner products). The transport T[b,a] is derived from the overlap as the orthogonal Procrustes fit of a's representations of the shared commitments onto b's; it is flagged `determined` only when the fit is unique (the cross-covariance of the anchors is nonsingular). The holonomy is H_A = T[A,C] T[C,B] T[B,A]. Constructions: `null_construction` (one configuration, three random frames), `planar_construction` (R^2; each overlap is a pair of commitments of one shape, and inside context c the incoming and outgoing pairs sit at a relative angle theta_c, a comparison internal to c), `sphere_construction` (local linear charts, by the log map, of a sphere of normalized evaluative profiles; fits on centred anchors), `underdetermined_family` (one shared commitment in R^3, so every member of a one-parameter family of transports fits exactly), `noisy_null`. Also `glue` (place every commitment in one configuration along the open path B -> A, C -> B -> A) and `route_discrepancy` (a comparison context A imports from B directly and via C).
- `test_transport.py`: 18 tests (`python3 -m pytest -q test_transport.py`): the null's derived transports are the frame changes g_b g_a^T and its holonomy is I while its coordinates differ on every shared commitment; transports and holonomy are covariant under regauging and the holonomy's class functions invariant; regauging preserves each context's comparisons; the planar holonomy angle is ± the sum of the internal angles; with exact determined overlaps gluing succeeds exactly when H = I (200 random draws); the two routes of an imported comparison are gauge-invariant and differ in the planar case, agree in the null; the sphere charts' holonomy converges to the spherical excess while flat charts are flat; an underdetermined overlap lets the holonomy run over a range with exact fits; Procrustes flags rank deficiency; noisy-null holonomy scales with the noise.
- `run_study.py [out.json]`: the study (seconds). Rerun: `python3 -I run_study.py results.json`. `results.json` holds every figure.

Results (seed 20261122):

- **Null.** 1000 draws each at (d, k) = (2, 2), (3, 3), (5, 6): largest ||H − I||_F 2.2e-14, with median frame differences ||g_a − g_b|| of 1.6 to 2.9. One draw at (3, 3) had an overlap fit flagged undetermined (near-coplanar anchors).
- **Planar.** 12 draws of theta_c in U(−1.2, 1.2): |holonomy angle| equals |sum of theta_c| reduced to (−pi, pi] to printing precision; every overlap fit exact (residual < 1e-15); gluing fails by 0.03 to 2.0.
- **Curved charts.** Triangle sides 0.2 to 1.2 rad: mean |holonomy angle| over 20 draws is within 0.3% of the spherical excess at anchor spread 0.0025 (0.0201 vs 0.0201; 0.0822 vs 0.0821; 0.3537 vs 0.3538; 0.8758 vs 0.8755).
- **Noisy null** (d = 3, 500 draws): with k = 10 anchors per overlap, median ||H − I|| ≈ 1.3σ and q95 ≈ 2.4σ, no orientation flips. With k = 3, 0.2% to 23% of draws flip orientation (det H = −1, ||H − I|| ≥ 2) as σ goes from 0.001 to 0.1: a gross holonomy produced by noise on a near-degenerate overlap.
- **Underdetermined.** One shared commitment in R^3: every member of the family fits exactly; ||H − I|| runs from 0.006 to 2.83 over the free angle.
