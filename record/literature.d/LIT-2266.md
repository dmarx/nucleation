# LIT-2266: Metastability demystified — the foundational past, the pragmatic present and the promising future

Fran Hancock, Fernando E. Rosas, Andrea I. Luppi and eight others (2024). "Metastability demystified — the foundational past, the pragmatic present and the promising future." *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 26(2): 82–100. DOI-10.1038/s41583-024-00883-1.

## Key takeaways

- Metastability is "unstable attraction", a property of dynamics and not
  of data; multistable switching needs noise, metastable passage does
  not.
- Symmetry breaking is a common enabler across its routes: a fixed
  point's ghost, cycles of saddles, chimeras, chaotic itinerancy.
- Dwell times: exponential for noise-driven multistability; gamma-like in
  metastable models, a proposed test.
