# CLAIM-tmpic0m1: Valence emerges from the will-organization

## The claim

CLAIM-tmphyvh3 makes valence a disposition, and grounds it in the
standing structure that settles contests. The owner draws the consequence,
that valence emerges from that structure, and names it: the
will-organization. TERM-023 (Active) defines it as "the maintained
organization of relations governing the conditions under which an
agent's available action-generating regimes acquire, retain, lose, or
alter practical efficacy". Its dimensions are accessibility, precedence
and persistence, together with TERM-017's precedence, jurisdiction and
suspension, and transformation. TERM-104 already joins it to the
negotiation: a motivating perspective's significance "conditions action
readiness, a claim to control that the will-organization may or may not
grant".

Two clauses are needed for the consequence to hold.

1. **The will-organization applied to rankings.** The will-organization
   fixes which regime prevails in which contest. The content of the
   verdict comes from the ranking of the perspective whose claim prevails.
   Valence emerges from the pair, not from the will-organization alone.
   TERM-076's kind clause, that the prevailing ordering ranks states by
   their bearing on maintenance, is then a further fact about which
   rankings the will-organization favours.
2. **Which sense of "organization".** The relations are TERM-074's sense 1,
   a relational arrangement: its own example, whether "a safety engineer
   can stop a deployment", is an arrangement of authority. The historical
   clause is sense 2, the maintenance that reproduces or revises the
   arrangement.

Version 1 needed authority "in the wide sense", because CLAIM-283 has
claims that govern without a grant. TERM-023 already covers that case.
The conditions under which a regime acquires efficacy include those under
which a strongly claimed regime overrides, so impulsive action is
something the will-organization permits, not something outside it.

## What it buys

- **Two routes of change.** Valence changes when rankings change, and also
  when the will-organization is reorganized with the rankings fixed. The
  second route is CLAIM-442's mindfulness case.
- **Affect is designed through governance.** CLAIM-432's profile of
  "threat without remorse" can be changed by reorganizing the
  corporation's will-organization, as well as by redesigning its concerns.
  The organizational-affect line then becomes a consequence of the
  will-organization line.
- **History.** The will-organization is maintained and revised through
  past settlements: CLAIM-443's learned prior over which processes govern
  is trained on them. Valence carries that history.

## What it costs

- **Circularity.** If the will-organization can be specified only by the
  settlements it is said to explain, the claim says nothing. The way out
  is TERM-023's own: the will-organization is the maintained relations,
  present before the contest, and not its outcomes.
- **The agent index.** TERM-023 is "a role in an agent already warranted
  as one". For an organization's valence, the organization must first be
  warranted as an agent. That is CLAIM-432's premise and not this claim's.

## What it does not say

That valence is the will-organization: it is the will-organization applied
to rankings. Nor that the formal grant scheme is the will-organization:
that is LEAD-tmpuszbx.
