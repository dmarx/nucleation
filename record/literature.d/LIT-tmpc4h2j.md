# LIT-tmpc4h2j: Why revolution is no longer possible

Byung-Chul Han (2014). "Why revolution is no longer possible". First published in German in the *Süddeutsche Zeitung*, 3 September 2014; read in the English version reprinted by the Schirn Kunsthalle Frankfurt's magazine "with the kind permission of Süddeutsche Zeitung".

## Key takeaways

- The power maintaining the system is "no longer repressive, and instead seductive".
- "There is no person opposite, no enemy who represses liberties".
