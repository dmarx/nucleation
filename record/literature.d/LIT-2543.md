# LIT-2543: Doing your part: Joint obligation and individual-level transmission

Rowan Mellor (2025). Doing your part: Joint obligation and individual-level transmission. *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 111(2), 386–406. DOI-10.1111/phpr.70031.

## Key takeaways

*Not read; as A describes it.* A12 §3: against the view that a joint obligation automatically gives each participant an obligation to do their part; what a group ought to do and what its members ought to do need separate answers. A draws that the reverse derivation, from a whole to obligations toward its parts, faces a greater burden. Unverified until read.
