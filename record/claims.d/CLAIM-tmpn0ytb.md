# CLAIM-tmpn0ytb: Microchimerism, cells of mother and offspring persisting in each other for decades and some of them taking part in the host's regulation, bears on the view that biological individuality is graded or processual: the separation that gestation begins is not completed at birth at the level of cells

## The claim

**The owner's link.** A9 §2 reported the record's reading of Meincke
([THEORY-448](../theory.d/THEORY-448.md), [NOTE-115](../notes.d/NOTE-115.md)): gestation as a process in which two organisms come
apart, so that "biological individuality may be graded or processual,
rather than a property acquired at a single instant." The owner, at U5:
"this reminds me of some recent developments in microchimerism", with a
journal article on HLA and fetal microchimerism ([LIT-tmpyiplw](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyiplw.md)), a preprint
on maternal cells in the human brain ([LIT-tmp3n00g](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3n00g.md)), a preprint on a
detector of foreign-genotype cells ([LIT-tmp5q8o7](../literature.d/LIT-tmp5q8o7.md)) and a research-news post
on maternal cells that sustain tolerance ([LIT-tmpokfxi](../literature.d/LIT-tmpokfxi.md)).

Meincke's question is about gestation. The owner's link moves it past
birth: cells pass both ways, persist for decades and, in some cases, do
work in the host. If individuation is a process, microchimerism is
evidence that the process does not finish at the organism's boundary when
the organisms separate.

**A's elaboration (A11), Proposed.** A separates four boundaries that
microchimerism shows can fall apart: genetic (which genome a cell
carries), anatomical (where it sits), functional (which organization it
helps maintain) and immunological (what is tolerated). A reads the tolerance
result as "intervention-based evidence, not merely evidence of
coexistence", and grades the evidence in steps: detection, then phenotypic
incorporation, then causal contribution to host function, then any part in
"the organization of a self or consciousness", which "would require
substantially more". A's own caution is part of the claim's edge: "I would
treat that as a research hypothesis rather than an established
conclusion. Biological individuals can still have objectively meaningful
boundaries; heterogeneity alone does not establish that individuality is
graded."

## Evidence

- [THEORY-tmp6t2l2](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp6t2l2.md) (the owner's post, read): in mice, one marked subset of
  maternal cells sustains the offspring's tolerance of maternal antigens,
  and the rest persist without it. Presence and function come apart by
  intervention.
- [THEORY-tmp4rc81](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4rc81.md) (Chua and colleagues' review, read in place of the three
  unread sources): bidirectional exchange, persistence for decades,
  colonization of almost any tissue including the brain.
- [LIT-tmpyiplw](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyiplw.md) and [LIT-tmp3n00g](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3n00g.md) (Unfetched; abstracts only): persistence
  in the mother depends on the immune relation between maternal receptors
  and fetal HLA-C; maternal cells in the brain take neuronal and glial
  identities.

[ARG-tmp51yxc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp51yxc.md) says what this evidence carries: the premise, not the
gradedness.

## Where it sits

- [THEORY-448](../theory.d/THEORY-448.md) (Meincke) is about gestation and gives "something in between
  one and two" no formal content. This claim adds a later stage and an
  empirical handle, not a measure.
- [THEORY-443](../theory.d/THEORY-443.md) (evolutionary and physiological individuality come apart):
  a lineage that is genetically the mother's serving the offspring's
  physiological regulation is an instance.
- [CLAIM-508](CLAIM-508.md) (gene ancestries alone cannot tell a partner from a source of
  transferred genes): the same point at the level of cells.
- [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md) (when is a combined form one system, and where is its
  boundary): the answer differs with the target: genome, tissue, immune
  regulation.

## What it does not say

That mother and offspring are one organism after birth, or partly so. That
anything follows for moral standing or bodily authority: A11 §6's
"genetic origin, organizational membership, and moral ownership need not
coincide" is A's, and [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md) requires standing to be argued separately.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Mpakosi et al. (2024), Deciphering the Role of Maternal Microchimerism in Offspring Autoimmunity: A Narrative Review, read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpsdg4x](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpsdg4x.md) ([LIT-tmp0kv3y](../literature.d/LIT-tmp0kv3y.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: **Supports the premise**, independently of Chua et al.
([LIT-tmpjik3n](../literature.d/LIT-tmpjik3n.md)): maternal cells persist "even into adulthood", take part in
regulation (tolerance via MSCs; immune maturation), and may cross
generations, which adds a lineage dimension the claim does not state. Its
autoimmune associations show functional incorporation can be pathological,
which bears on A's "functional boundary" step and on whether participation
is integration. It **leaves the gradedness conclusion open**, like the read
sources ([ARG-tmp51yxc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp51yxc.md)). Its account ([THEORY-tmpxmmhb](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpxmmhb.md)) is filed as corroborating this claim's premise, and nothing more.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Schepanski et al., read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpe8k6k](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe8k6k.md) ([LIT-tmpqn7ic](../literature.d/LIT-tmpqn7ic.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: strongly supports the "functional incorporation at the
level of cells" premise. Maternal cells not only persist in offspring but
regulate the host's brain development, and depleting them changes the host.
It does not by itself establish that individuality is graded (the claim's
own `defeated_if` and [ARG-tmp51yxc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp51yxc.md) already say heterogeneity/function alone
do not).

## Note of 2026-11-12: Boddy, Fortunato, Wilson Sayres and Aktipis, read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpqbilf](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpqbilf.md) ([LIT-tmpv8dr2](../literature.d/LIT-tmpv8dr2.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: supports the persistence-and-functional-involvement
premise and adds the evolutionary gloss that persisting fetal cells may be
agents of a distinct (offspring) fitness interest — which, if anything,
strengthens "not completed at birth": the cells are not just present but
potentially pursuing a different optimum inside the host. This directly
serves the claim's "functional incorporation" leg and even its
distributed-agency framing. It does not settle gradedness (the review
stresses causation is unproven).

## Note of 2026-11-12: Castela and others (2017), read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpx8y0m](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpx8y0m.md) ([LIT-tmpyfxly](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyfxly.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: supports this claim's premise, and
[CLAIM-tmpu69tn](CLAIM-tmpu69tn.md)'s "foreign-origin cells serving the host" (noted there).
Fetal cells persisting in the mother for her
lifespan are recruited to repair her tissue, a foreign-lineage cell doing
the host's maintenance work, with CCR2 dependence and a virgin-mouse
negative control establishing the fetal-cell mediation.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Kinder, Stelzer, Arck and Way, read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmp1h3p0](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp1h3p0.md) ([LIT-tmp1pvq3](../literature.d/LIT-tmp1pvq3.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: **supports the premise, not the gradedness**. It
documents bidirectional, decades-long persistence and an intervention
showing maternal cells do work in the offspring's immune regulation, and its
authors draw an identity conclusion for the immune system ("forces
reconsideration of how we define immunological identity"). That is A11's
"immunological boundary" coming apart from the genetic one, exactly; it says
nothing about organismal individuality being graded. Consistent with
[ARG-tmp51yxc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp51yxc.md)'s reading ("the premise, not the gradedness"). The
instigators-not-beneficiaries line (result 5) is a function claim, in A11's
third step (causal contribution to host function) for tolerance only.
