# TERM-tmpuhia8: frame, as a measurement context

## Definition

A24: "Instead of beginning with a fixed communicative act U that gets expressed
in different frames, consider a communicative situation described by
C = (u, s, a, k, g)": utterance, socially situated speaker, audience, shared
knowledge and conventions, genre. A pragmatic judgement is context-indexed,
P(J = j | C). Translation changes u (and k, g) while trying to keep s;
reattributing the words changes s while keeping u.

## What it is not

Not Goffman's footing, though it absorbs it. Footing was a participant role
(A14); here it is an index on judgement distributions, and the change was not
remarked. Not the operator sense of [TERM-tmppital](TERM-tmppital.md): a context indexes a distribution,
an operator changes a state. The manuscript keeps both: contexts as the cover
of §3, framing operations in §9.
