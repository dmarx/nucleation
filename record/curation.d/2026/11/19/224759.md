The owner asked on 2026-11-19, after ADR-109 gave the record a scheme for
its research opportunities: "launch an audit to identify potential leads
we should document explicitly in our backlog", and asked for the leads
found to be documented as LEADs. This entry files them.

## Method

Six read-only auditors each read one slice of the record for opportunities
the record names and has not taken up: a named test, a study designed and
not run, a reading said to be owed, a construction or proof asked for, a
measure that is missing. Each listed candidates with method, what they
advance, the CASE that holds a design, a verbatim source quote and a value
rating. The slices were: q1, QUESTION-001 to QUESTION-250; q2,
QUESTION-251 onward; c1, CLAIM-001 to CLAIM-380; c2, CLAIM-381 onward; k,
cases, terms, arguments, syntheses and workbench; r, curation leftovers,
the Deferred and Unfetched works, and "would settle" lines in notes.

This merge joined candidates that name the same study or work across
slices, took the union of what they advance, kept the best quotes and the
highest rating, and split a candidate where one part must be done before
another, linking the two by `presupposes`. Every code in a relation field
was checked to exist on this branch and not to be retired; `advances`
holds only open questions and claims not Superseded or Rejected. Every
quotation in a lead was checked against its source file. Tags and lines
were taken from the entries each lead advances. Stage is `idea` throughout
except two leads whose source states the design and its cost, as ADR-109
requires for `specified`.
