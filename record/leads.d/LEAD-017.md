# LEAD-017: Extend the synthetic separation with three organizations of matched behaviour and different endorsement relations, measuring registration apart from the experimenter's setting

## What it would do

Build A13's three organizations and a measure of registration that does
not read the setting.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md): "Registration is not measured apart from the
  experimenter's setting"

## What would count

Registration measured independently meets the second requirement of
[QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: the three organizations built, and registration measured

Carried out by the assistant at the coordinator's brief
(scratch/studies/lead-017/). CASE-tmp9e5vf builds three synthetic
organizations calibrated to the same pursuit of an outcome that grows
unpleasant: one denying uptake to a registered evaluation, one
accommodating it under a tolerance that compensation widens, one never
registering it. The registration measure perturbs the evaluating process
at random and reads the organization's record, holding the outcome, the
action and the pursuing process's value fixed; it never reads the
registration setting. On held-out agents it told registered from
unregistered without error, where behaviour could not, and resisted a
decoy that fooled a passive reading. That is CLAIM-tmpphfb4.

What would count, "registration measured independently", is met within
the stipulated model. What remains:

- The endorsement relation, which distinguishes A13's three, is told apart
  only weakly, by a separate probe of pursuit (43% of accommodating agents
  at 4.5% false calls). A longer episode or a denser probe protocol is the
  next step.
- Which three organizations A13 meant is the owner's reading; the case
  files a second reading, in which all three register and differ only in
  the endorsement relation. It was not run.
- The measure needs the other routes into the record named as covariates;
  a natural organization has no such list.
