# LIT-938: Evaluating Compositional Scene Understanding in Multimodal Generative Models

Shuhao Fu, Andrew Jun Lee, Anna Wang, Ida Momennejad, Trevor Bihl, Hongjing Lu and Taylor Webb (2025), *Transactions on Machine Learning Research*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, NOTE-tmpv2vga.*

- **Familiar single relations are largely solved.** Agreement is above
  0.8 for 'on', 'near' and 'in' (DALL-E 2: below 0.4), and higher than
  DALL-E 2 on every relation tested.
- **Reversal breaks asymmetric relations.** "A rabbit chasing a tiger"
  fails where "a tiger chasing a rabbit" succeeds (t(29) = 3.67). The
  drop is large for 'on', 'in' and 'chasing', small for 'near' and
  'touching'.
- **Binding, not objects.** Among the worst reversed images, the errors
  are mostly the wrong relation or the roles swapped back.
- **Two relations are mostly too many.** Most combinations among three
  entities get low agreement.
- **The why is thin.** Training-data likelihood is proposed on a
  correlation of r = 0.09 with a GPT-3 proxy. A multi-object binding
  problem is proposed by citation.
- **VLMs.** Five models learn relational concepts few-shot below human
  level; four are at chance with six objects, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet is
  above it. As judges of image–prompt match they correlate with humans at
  r ≈ 0.43–0.56, against 0.91 between halves of the human raters.
