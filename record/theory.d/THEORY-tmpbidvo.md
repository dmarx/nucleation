# THEORY-tmpbidvo: Ge, Xie, Sun, Fan, Zhang and Liu find in a randomized trial that a week of brief smartphone mindfulness training combining monitoring and acceptance shortens Stroop and Go/No-go reaction times, without changing accuracy or delay discounting, and that no effect lasts two weeks; they attribute the effect to acceptance joined with monitoring

## Source

Ge, Xie, Sun, Fan, Zhang and Liu (2024), *Mindfulness* 16: 448-464,
LIT-916, read in NOTE-tmp1yxz6.

## What was actually shown

Seventy students in the lowest 27% of trait mindfulness were randomized
to monitor-and-acceptance training (24), monitor-only training (23) or
waiting (23), 10 minutes a day for a week. At post-test the
mindfulness arm was faster on the Go/No-go task than both other arms
(p < 0.05) and on the Stroop task than the monitor arm (p = 0.011) and,
at trend, the waiting arm (p = 0.077). Accuracy stayed above 95% in every
arm. The share of immediate choices in a delay-discounting task did not
differ between arms at any time. No effect remained at the 15-day
follow-up.

## The account

Monitor and Acceptance Theory: monitoring trains attention, acceptance
changes one's relation to what is monitored, and only together do they
produce a "de-automatization" of impulsive responding; brief training
does it momentarily.

## What it does not show

That any measure of self-control under temptation changed, or that a
change in self-control was or was not carried by discounting: no
mediation was tested, and the outcome that changed is response speed.
