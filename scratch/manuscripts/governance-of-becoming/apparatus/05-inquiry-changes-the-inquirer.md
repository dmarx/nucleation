## 5. Limits (moved from the draft's "Standing, voices and limits")

- *The promotions this article stands on were made in this work's own process.* The pass of 2026-11-24 that set CLAIM-1115, CLAIM-1190 and CLAIM-1095 Active was driven by LEAD-tmp0j28l (Open, underway), which the owner asked for at U33 of the exchange that motivated this series. - *Using the record as a case is circular.* A51 §6 proposes the record as "a running constructive case". Its grammar, status passes and join screening were designed, run and described by the same assistants, under the owner's delegated rulings, partly in this work. The article's §5.13.3 reports CASE-300 as an illustration only.
- *Models, not measurements.* The joint-frontier results are constructions with stipulated costs; CASE-396, CASE-397 and CASE-412 are simulations. CASE-412's range is "from the executed CASE-412 model, not estimates of real-world institutions" (A51's caption).
- *Readings not held.* A63 sets the account beside Kitcher, Massimi and Andersen, but "The record holds no reading of these works" (SYNTHESIS-122, "Not stated on the record"). Hess's *Undone Science* is registered and unread (CLAIM-1140, note of 2026-11-24). The article makes no comparison with that literature.
