# CLAIM-247: The bearer of communicative identity reversed, from an operational object defined on observed responses to a kind that can outrun observation, and no turn argues the change; both stances stand in the record

## The objection

**The operational stance.** [CLAIM-038](CLAIM-038.md) (A78): "we no longer need to
postulate a global semantic object and then ask how translations represent
it. We can begin with local observational data and investigate whether a
global object exists." [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md): "pragmatic identity and fidelity should
be defined on observable responses before any equivalence of internal
states is assumed". The record files the object in two Active TERMs, both
defined on observations: [TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md), "an equivalence class of situations
relative to a chosen family of measurements", and [TERM-042](../terms.d/TERM-042.md), "a
context-indexed system of empirically admissible local assignments or
distributions". [CLAIM-112](CLAIM-112.md) says what kind of claim this was: "[CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md)
defines fidelity on observations. This says that is a choice of what to
measure, not a theory of what meaning is."

**The realist stance.** [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s ontological clause is a theory of what
identity is: "A communicative kind's identity is grounded in its
relational organization rather than in any expression or encoding". Its
unit, [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md), is "Not an observational class ([TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md))", and [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)
quotes A218 Ch7: "A structural kind may be real while remaining
indistinguishable under an insufficient family of empirical probes."

The two stances disagree on a case. Take two realizations that agree on
every probe in the family used. On [TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md) they are one communicative
object, since that is what the equivalence class is. On [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md) they may
be two kinds. The bearer of identity has moved from the observational
profile to something the profile may fail to separate.

**The change is recorded and not argued.** [TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md)'s "Where it went":
"The equivalence class stops being the identity criterion and becomes a
limit on knowing a kind ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)). No turn addresses this entry."
[CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md) names it in one sentence: "It does not withdraw [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md)'s
methodological rule. Evidence still comes from observable responses. What
changes is what that evidence is *of*." That sentence keeps [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md) as a
rule of method and says the evidence is now of a kind. It does not argue
for the kind. The owner's turns over the change asked for the argument to
be laid out (U56), tied together (U59) and turned into a proposal (U60)
([CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s summary).

[CLAIM-112](CLAIM-112.md)'s distinction makes the gap exact. The operational account was
a choice of what to measure. [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) adds a theory of what is measured,
and the exchange concedes that the data will not choose between it and its
rival. [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) quotes A214 §13: "A traditional ontology
of intrinsically individuated objects plus relational properties might
reproduce the same statistical predictions."

Both stances stand. [CLAIM-038](CLAIM-038.md) is a Proposed thesis, [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md) has been
Active since 2026-10-27, [CLAIM-112](CLAIM-112.md) is Active, [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) is Proposed, and [TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md), [TERM-042](../terms.d/TERM-042.md) and
[TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md) are all Active. No claim says which bearer the line now holds, and
[CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), the manuscript's thesis, uses no TERM for it.

## What it does not say

It does not say the realist reading is wrong, nor that the two stances
cannot be reconciled. [CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md)'s reading, method kept and object changed,
is one coherent reconciliation. It says the change from one bearer to the
other happened between A203 and A218 without an argument, as [CLAIM-140](CLAIM-140.md)
says of the receiver, and that the record keeps both without saying which
is the line's.
