# LEAD-062: Find or design a study that measures the self's extent apart from caring and tests whether concern for future people and for one's legacy follows it, including legacy priming where no one will know

## What it would do

Search for studies that measure felt continuity or inclusion of other in
self apart from caring; failing one, specify Chan's design with a measure
of concern added, and a legacy-priming arm where no one will know.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-301](../questions.d/QUESTION-301.md): "Measure felt continuity with one's legacy or with future
  generations, and test whether concern for them follows that measure or
  follows caring alone."
- [QUESTION-299](../questions.d/QUESTION-299.md): "Whether such concern varies with a measured continuity
  between the self and the future, apart from caring."
- [NOTE-1280](../notes.d/NOTE-1280.md): "with a measure of concern for the group's welfare added,
  would answer [CLAIM-492](../claims.d/CLAIM-492.md)"

## What would count

Concern that follows measured continuity apart from caring supports
[CLAIM-492](../claims.d/CLAIM-492.md) and the thesis [QUESTION-299](../questions.d/QUESTION-299.md) tests; concern that follows caring
alone counts against it.

Carried out on 2026-11-22 by a delegated worker.

- **The search** used the Europe PMC REST interface and the arXiv API only,
  with no email address sent and no bot check met: nine Europe PMC queries
  and two arXiv queries, logged in scratch/studies/lead-062/search.md. **No
  study was found that measures felt continuity with future people or
  with one's legacy apart from caring and tests whether concern follows
  it, and none varies whether anyone will know.**
- **Read**: Simić, Sacchi and Perugini 2025 ([LIT-3081](../literature.d/LIT-3081.md), [NOTE-2302](../notes.d/NOTE-2302.md),
  [THEORY-2212](../theory.d/THEORY-2212.md)), in full from the Europe PMC interface. It manipulates
  a group's expected sameness fifty years on, which correlated .14 with
  national attachment, and finds its mediated effects on attitudes toward
  immigrants only among the highly attached. A neighbour of the test, not
  the test.
- **Registered, not read**: Law et al. 2025 ([LIT-3083](../literature.d/LIT-3083.md), Unfetched: the
  Europe PMC full text answered HTTP 500 three times); Law et al. 2023
  ([LIT-3082](../literature.d/LIT-3082.md), Unfetched: an OSF preprint outside this run's routes);
  Law et al. 2026 ([LIT-3084](../literature.d/LIT-3084.md), Deferred: abstract only). Of all the
  works found, [LIT-3083](../literature.d/LIT-3083.md) is the likeliest to hold the test, since by
  its abstract it intervenes on "the interconnectedness of people across
  time" and measures concern for future generations.
- **The design**: [CASE-323](../cases.d/CASE-323.md), recorded below as this lead's
  `operationalized_in`. It is Chan's field design as [NOTE-1280](../notes.d/NOTE-1280.md) reports it,
  with three additions. Concern for the group's welfare is measured. The
  self's extent is measured by self-definition, cognitive inclusion and
  continuity with the group's future members. And a legacy prime is
  crossed with a credited gift against a gift no one will ever know of.
  Both branches of the lead were taken, because what was found does not
  meet "What would count". Not run.
- **What remains**: reading [LIT-3083](../literature.d/LIT-3083.md) and [LIT-3082](../literature.d/LIT-3082.md) by another route,
  which is a call for the owner; and running or commissioning the design.

## Note of 2026-11-22: Syropoulos, Law, Coleman and Young, read

[LIT-3082](../literature.d/LIT-3082.md) is read ([NOTE-2315](../notes.d/NOTE-2315.md); [THEORY-2225](../theory.d/THEORY-2225.md)) and now Active. It is
**not the test** this lead asks for. Its measure of the self's extent toward
future people mixes felt connection, empathy and the overlap of circles, no
caring measure is held apart from it, and nothing varies whether anyone will
know. It does supply an overlapping-circles item for "future generations of
people", used alone in its last experiment, which [CASE-323](../cases.d/CASE-323.md) could adopt
as its continuity measure, and two manipulations that failed to move
identification, a warning for the case's salience arm. The lead's "no study
separates continuity with future people from caring" stands.
