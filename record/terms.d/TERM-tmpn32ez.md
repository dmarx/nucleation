# TERM-tmpn32ez: Campbell effect

## Definition

What fails is the *measured*. The activity the indicator was meant to observe
reorganizes around the indicator. Teachers teach to the test, police record
to the clearance rate, and pages are written for the ranker. The indicator
can still describe the reorganized activity accurately. Its job is to name
the failure that a better indicator does not cure.

From the Goodhart effect (TERM-tmp56tms), by DISTINCTION-tmpnbw70.
