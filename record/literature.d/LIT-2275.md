  Mo, Zheng et al. (2026), AgentIdeaBench: Benchmarking Scientific Ideation
  in the Agent Era. A benchmark for LLM scientific ideation under two
  matched settings: Static (the model reads a curated set of reference
  papers and writes one 80–150-word hypothesis) and Active (the model gets
  only the subfield name and a Semantic Scholar search tool with a 10-call
  budget, and must find its own evidence). Hypotheses are scored by an
  ensemble of LLM critics on originality, feasibility, clarity, impact and
  specificity, with originality argued against prior art retrieved at
  judging time. Across 33 matched models and 40 densely scored subfields in
  five disciplines, Active evaluation separates models more than Static,
  improves about twice as fast across model generations, and its gain is
  capability-gated; the gain comes from grounding (feasibility, clarity,
  specificity) and leaves measured originality unchanged. Read for the
  exchange "luria experiment design".
