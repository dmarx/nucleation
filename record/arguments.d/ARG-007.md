# ARG-007: Northstar emergency → acting and governing differ

## The inference

The viability distinction supplies the formal possibility; the emergency case
shows the three persistences separating; together they give two achievements
where the operative-will picture saw one.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes.
- **Does it separate what the conclusion says comes apart?** Yes, if the
  mapping holds.
- **The mapping premise.** Applying viability needs a constraint set K, and the
  paper says K is "always indexed" but gives no non-arbitrary way to choose the
  maintenance constraints for an agent's regulatory capacities. Unanswered,
  and it is the weakest step: "precarious" is relative to that choice.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a second instance, in training

A31 of the exchange of 2026-10-17: "acquiring capacities ≠ governing the
acquisition of capacities". A conventionally trained network gains capacities
while the governance of its updates stays with the optimizer and the trainer
([CLAIM-367](../claims.d/CLAIM-367.md)). That is this argument's conclusion, effective action and
the capacity to govern among regimes as different achievements, in a case
where the second is held outside the system altogether. It is an instance,
not a new premise, and the mapping question above applies to it unchanged:
which capacities count as the network's to maintain.

## Note of 2026-11-22: a rule for the mapping premise, from [LEAD-053](../leads.d/LEAD-053.md)

The critical question above left the mapping premise unanswered: no
non-arbitrary way to choose the maintenance constraints. [LEAD-053](../leads.d/LEAD-053.md) proposes
one, [CLAIM-916](../claims.d/CLAIM-916.md), Proposed. K holds the constraints that realize the
capacity at issue and every constraint they depend on for their production
([TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s sense), each at the least level at which what depends on it is
still produced, with a union over substitutable routes. Once the agent and
the capacity are named, the one choice left is the agent's boundary: an
outside-produced dependency is a condition on the narrow reading
([CLAIM-917](../claims.d/CLAIM-917.md)) and a member of K on the wide one ([CLAIM-915](../claims.d/CLAIM-915.md)). Which
reading this argument uses is the owner's call.

The test, [CASE-342](../cases.d/CASE-342.md), runs the rule on this argument's ground, [CASE-036](../cases.d/CASE-036.md),
as a finite toy: K comes from the case's own text (deferred maintenance,
lost legibility, anomalies that meet the criteria), and the operative
trajectory leaves K at its second step while the emergency and its
criteria hold throughout. The two readings agree there, since every
dependency is Northstar's own. On the training instance of the note of
2026-10-18 they part: the narrow reading says the network has no capacity
to transform to maintain, the wide one that it holds it through the
training process. If the rule is adopted, the mapping premise is answered
up to that choice. No status changes.
