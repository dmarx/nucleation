# LEAD-tmp37b40: Read the literature on concepts revised under counterexample and on cooperation without shared meaning, to test whether misunderstanding detects or creates distinctions and when leaving it unresolved is productive

## What it would do

Read, under the read-and-file skill:

- **Lakatos, *Proofs and Refutations*.** The history of Euler's polyhedron
  formula as a sequence of counterexamples, each forcing a concept
  ("polyhedron") to be split or redefined. This is the closest worked
  history of a misunderstanding detecting a distinction, and of the
  choice between refining the concept and excluding the case.
- **Waismann, "Verifiability" (1945), on open texture.** That empirical
  concepts leave cases undecided that no one has yet met: the latency
  [CLAIM-tmpm76n5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm76n5.md) describes, stated as a feature of concepts.
- **Star and Griesemer 1989** ([LIT-2243](../literature.d/LIT-2243.md)): the boundary-object case for
  [CLAIM-tmpgx2ej](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgx2ej.md).
- **"Working misunderstanding"** in anthropology and history: find the
  originating use and one study of it.

## What it would settle

- Whether the record's sorting of detected, transferred and created
  distinctions ([CLAIM-tmpm76n5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm76n5.md)) matches how Lakatos's cases actually went.
- Whether the boundary-object literature describes the timing [CLAIM-tmpgx2ej](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgx2ej.md)
  proposes: separating cases avoided or made cheap, not merely absent.
