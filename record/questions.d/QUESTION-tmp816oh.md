# QUESTION-tmp816oh: Does a communicative act's identity split into a factor internal to the interpreter and a relational factor fixed by world and community, and which of the two must a translation preserve?

## The question

[QUESTION-029](QUESTION-029.md) asks what constitutes the identity of a communicative act. The record holds two answers that pull apart. [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) locates what survives in what "a receiving system can still reconstruct and act upon", which is internal to the interpreter. [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) makes the act partly constituted by relations to participants, norms and uptake, which are external. This question asks whether identity has both factors, how they relate, and which one fidelity tracks.

## Why the record needs it

The transfer has a named mechanism. An utterance's content is fixed by the content of the representations it expresses plus the public-language relations it enters. So a factoring of representational content constrains the factoring of an act's content. If the act has two factors, [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) are not rivals. They describe different factors, and a translation can keep one while losing the other. That is [CLAIM-118](../claims.d/CLAIM-118.md)'s split between interpreter and context made into a split in identity. [CLAIM-046](../claims.d/CLAIM-046.md) puts observable responses first, and an answer would say which factor those responses measure.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-089](../theory.d/THEORY-089.md): content has "a narrow conceptual role inside the head and a referential factor relating it to the world". Role "determines the nature of the referential factor" without fixing reference. Social meaning sits in the referential factor or in a third, social factor. Block notes that long-arm roles "are better for thinking about translation and communication". Limit: it is about mental representations, not acts. It gives no identity conditions for roles, and the social factor is not built.

## What would answer it

A case where two renderings keep the receiver's inferential and decision profile but change who is addressed or what uptake is expected. Or the reverse case. With either, a statement of which change the record's fidelity measures would count as a loss.
