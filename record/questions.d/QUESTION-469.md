# QUESTION-469: When a localized pattern persists on a carrier that is itself a persisting structure, as a soliton on a vortex filament or a vortex line on its worldsheet, can a model derive both persistences together, and does the pattern's identity then depend on the carrier's?

## Why it is a question

The filing's readings each derive one level of a two-level individual and
take the other as given:

- **Hasimoto's soliton** ([THEORY-1868](../theory.d/THEORY-1868.md)). It is a pattern in a
  filament's curvature and torsion, derived under local induction. The
  filament is a thin, unstretched line vortex assumed from the start.
- **The Nielsen–Olesen vortex line** ([THEORY-1869](../theory.d/THEORY-1869.md)). It follows a
  Nambu–Goto worldsheet for a finite time, from initial data built to
  follow it. Whether such a line forms is not addressed.

[CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md) sorts single structures by what holds them. It does not ask what
happens when one structure holds another. And [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s senses of
individuation, descriptive, dynamical, constitutive and autonomous, are
stated for one level at a time.

## What would count as an answer

- **A derived pair.** A model in which both levels' persistence follows
  from one dynamics: a field whose vortex lines form and persist, carrying
  solitons that form and persist on them.
- **A test of dependence.** What happens to the pattern when the carrier
  is perturbed, and to the carrier when the pattern is.
- **A failure.** If no such model can be found, an argument that the two
  levels need separate assumptions would answer the question the other
  way.

## Note of 2026-11-18: Holm and Stechmann (2004), read

<!-- inactive-ok-file: THEORY-tmp405mh — new; the reading's account -->

Holm and Stechmann ([LIT-tmp3py9h](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3py9h.md), [NOTE-tmpja35d](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpja35d.md); [THEORY-tmp405mh](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp405mh.md)) take the
filament as given, vorticity concentrated on a curve (eq. 2.1), and choose
its Hamiltonian. With helicity in place of kinetic energy the filament is
not a material line: "so in general ω will not be frozen into the fluid
motion". The carrier's persistence is assumed, and how it moves is fixed by
the Hamiltonian chosen for it rather than derived from the flow. The model
therefore derives the pattern's persistence (solitons of the complex mKdV)
and not the carrier's, which is this question's first half. It answers
neither half.
