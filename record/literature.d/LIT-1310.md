# LIT-1310: Approximate Causal Abstractions

Beckers, S., Eberhardt, F. & Halpern, J. Y. (2020). Approximate Causal Abstractions. *Proceedings of the 35th Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence Conference*, PMLR 115: 606–615. Preprint: arXiv:1906.11583 (27 June 2019), as "Approximate Causal Abstraction".

Read in full ([NOTE-1045](../notes.d/NOTE-1045.md)).

## Why this is here

A25's first reading: "What, exactly, is novel about governance-response sufficiency?" A25: "We should not present governance-preserving coarse-graining as a new general theory of causal abstraction." A26 §1 set it against [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md), [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md) and [THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md). Bears on [CLAIM-407](../claims.d/CLAIM-407.md), [CLAIM-409](../claims.d/CLAIM-409.md) and [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md). The record holds causal abstraction only through interchange interventions ([THEORY-243](../theory.d/THEORY-243.md)); [LIT-038](LIT-038.md)'s status note sends a reader wanting causal abstraction to Beckers–Halpern.

## Note of 2026-10-27: causal abstraction, now held further

"Why this is here" says the record held causal abstraction only through
interchange interventions ([THEORY-243](../theory.d/THEORY-243.md)). The circuits works read on
2026-10-26 at the owner's request add two holdings. Geiger et al.'s
distributed alignment search ([LIT-tmpsavwt](LIT-tmpsavwt.md), [NOTE-tmpzb21n](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzb21n.md)) grounds
interchange-intervention accuracy in their own approximate causal
abstraction, which holds on average over inputs, a cousin of this
paper's approximation. Causal scrubbing ([LIT-tmpqtkua](LIT-tmpqtkua.md), [NOTE-tmp4zrzd](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp4zrzd.md)),
which Geiger et al. count as causal abstraction analysis, tests a
hypothesis by resampling and scores it through the partitions of the data
it induces ([THEORY-tmpblgxz](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpblgxz.md)). Status and summary are unchanged.
