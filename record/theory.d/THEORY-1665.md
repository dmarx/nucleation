# THEORY-1665: Gao, Lee and Murphy (2020) find that after a local daily newspaper closes, a county's municipal borrowing costs rise by about 5 to 11 basis points in the long run, with government wages, staffing and deficits rising too, and attribute this to the loss of the press's monitoring of local government rather than to local economic decline.

## The account

Gao, Lee and Murphy (2020) find that after a local daily newspaper closes, a county's municipal borrowing costs rise by about 5 to 11 basis points in the long run, with government wages, staffing and deficits rising too, and attribute this to the loss of the press's monitoring of local government rather than to local economic decline.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1761](../notes.d/NOTE-1761.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
