# ADR-tmp6wnv0: A line of its own for flourishing and meaning in life, flourishing-and-meaning: what makes a life go well and be meaningful, and how arrangements and technologies widen or narrow the lives open to people

## Context

A line is "a named line of the record's own argument, so that a line can
be set aside as a whole and what depends on it can be found" (the `lines`
vocabulary, [ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). The vocabulary is closed and grows by decision:
[ADR-073](ADR-073.md) added two lines for argument filed under the nearest ones, and
[ADR-082](ADR-082.md), [ADR-087](ADR-087.md) and [ADR-089](ADR-089.md) split lines whose parts barely related.

The filing of the exchange "Analyze technological civilization trap"
(2026-11-13, https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597;
pull request [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489)) filed A's bridge to the record and left most of A's
synthesis, "Convivial Modernity". Its curation entry gives the reason:
"No line of the record's argument holds it. Claims need a line from the
closed vocabulary, and opening one is the owner's decision." What was
left includes:

- Wolf's meaning in life as a third dimension beside happiness and
  morality, the origin of a desire against its worth, and "the
  industrialization of meaning", in form [TERM-108](../terms.d/TERM-108.md)'s proxy objective
  applied to meaning;
- fallibilist value pluralism, and Sen's capability, not functioning,
  applied to meaningful engagement;
- agency freedom against well-being freedom, and conversion factors;
- the claim that a civilization succeeds insofar as it widens the
  substantive freedom to pursue worthwhile lives under non-domination,
  which the filing judged an evaluative standard no admission route
  reaches.

The owner brought Wolf (U7) and Sen (U8) into that synthesis, and has now
asked for the line ("add the ... flourishing and meaning line").

The subject is not new to the record's reading. The `well-being` topic
already holds "flourishing, vitality and meaning in life" as psychology
measures them ([ADR-025](ADR-025.md)), and Sen's capability approach is read in
[THEORY-025](../theory.d/THEORY-025.md) and [THEORY-411](../theory.d/THEORY-411.md). What is missing is a line for the record's own
argument about it.

## Decision

- **One line is added** to the closed `lines` vocabulary:
  `flourishing-and-meaning`, "Flourishing and meaning in life". Its blurb:

  > what makes a life go well for the one living it and what makes it
  > meaningful — meaning in life as subjective attraction to what is
  > objectively worthwhile (Wolf), capability and agency freedom against
  > functionings achieved and well-being freedom (Sen), basic needs against
  > what only raises reported well-being, self-realization and its
  > distortions when a life is organized around a proxy for its good, and
  > how arrangements and technologies widen or narrow the lives open to
  > people. Whose a will is stays with authenticity, what organizes
  > efficacy with will-organization, judging an organization with
  > organizational-normativity, and a whole's success against its members'
  > good with distributed-agency (ADR-tmp6wnv0)

- **Where it ends.** The neighbouring lines, in their own words:
  - **authenticity**, "what makes a will-organization the agent's own,
    and normativity across the scales an agent spans. Set aside on
    2026-10-05 as future work". Whose a will is, and whether it is
    integrated, is not whether the life it directs goes well or means
    anything. A will can be wholly one's own and set on a worthless
    project, which is Wolf's case for meaning as a separate ideal. The
    record's Wolf readings, [THEORY-118](../theory.d/THEORY-118.md) and [THEORY-122](../theory.d/THEORY-122.md) (from [LIT-685](../literature.d/LIT-685.md),
    [LIT-688](../literature.d/LIT-688.md) and [LIT-690](../literature.d/LIT-690.md)) and [THEORY-147](../theory.d/THEORY-147.md) (from [LIT-747](../literature.d/LIT-747.md)), are on freedom and
    responsibility. They serve [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md) and stay with authenticity.
    Self-realization belongs here only as a question of the good of a
    life; as a question of ownership it is authenticity's.
  - **will-organization**, "what organizes practical efficacy in an
    agent, and how that organization becomes precarious. Normative
    judgment about organizations is a line of its own ([ADR-089](ADR-089.md))". It
    gives the causal structure of choice. This line asks which of the
    lives reachable through that structure are worth reaching.
  - **organizational-normativity**, "whether an organization's own
    dynamics ground norms of function and failure, and what judging an
    organization needs beyond them". It judges the organization. This
    line judges the lives of the people an arrangement touches. Claims
    about domination and exploitation through dependence ([CLAIM-669](../claims.d/CLAIM-669.md),
    [CLAIM-670](../claims.d/CLAIM-670.md), [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md)) stay there. A claim of this line that uses one
    of them rests on it, and that is a crossing ([ADR-085](ADR-085.md)).
  - **transient-support**, "when support that establishes or sustains a
    party can be withdrawn without its decline ...". Its account of
    capacity learned by doing ([CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)) is a dependency of this line,
    not a part of it. [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489)'s claim that capability is assessed as a
    trajectory rests on [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) and [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md), so it can carry this line
    first and cross into transient-support.
  - **evidence-for-experience**, "what could show that a system,
    organized, non-biological or without a cortex, has experience and not
    only access or function ...". It asks whether there is a subject. This
    line asks how a subject's life goes.
  - **distributed-agency**, "what an organized whole is, and how its
    operative direction relates to its members'". [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md), that a
    whole's success does not establish its constituents' well-being,
    viability or autonomy, is that line's thesis about wholes and parts,
    cancer cells among them. Claims of this line that use it rest on it,
    so it does not take this line.

- **First application ([ADR-071](ADR-071.md)).** [ADR-085](ADR-085.md) adds a line where an entry
  takes part in the line's argument. A new line has no members yet for
  that rule's relations to point at, so the seed is chosen by subject,
  as [ADR-073](ADR-073.md) chose its lines' members. An entry takes the line when its
  question or claim is about what makes a life go well, and keeps its own
  lines first. Two held entries do:
  - **[QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md)** (distributed-agency): what would show that an
    experience is a basic psychological need, something functioning
    requires, rather than something that reliably raises reported
    well-being. That question, whether a life goes well by its needs or by
    its reports, belongs to this line.
  - **[CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md)** (will-organization): Sen's opportunity aspect of freedom
    can be stood for by viable reachability "only through a map from
    trajectories to valued functionings". That is a claim about what
    capability needs beyond reachability, which is this line's subject.

  Each gains the line after its own, with a history note. Neither first
  line changes, so under [ADR-085](ADR-085.md)'s version 2 nothing passes on from them,
  and the rule adds no further entry.
- **Declined, with reasons.**
  - [QUESTION-451](../questions.d/QUESTION-451.md), on whether need frustration is a distinct process: a
    question about a measure, asked for [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md)'s account of an
    organization's frustration. It stays with distributed-agency.
  - [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md): a dependency, as above.
  - [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md), on degrees of freedom of will: it borrows [THEORY-411](../theory.d/THEORY-411.md)'s
    partial ordering, but its subject is freedom of will.
  - [QUESTION-299](../questions.d/QUESTION-299.md) and [QUESTION-301](../questions.d/QUESTION-301.md), on future generations and legacy: they
    cite Scheffler on what matters to us, but ask what is owed and whether
    legacy is self-interest, which is the `altruism` and
    `future-generations` inquiry they are filed in.
  - The readings on meaning in life, Fischer's "Stories and the Meaning of
    Life" ([LIT-689](../literature.d/LIT-689.md)), Scheffler's *The Afterlife* ([LIT-1779](../literature.d/LIT-1779.md), [THEORY-1577](../theory.d/THEORY-1577.md)),
    and Browning and Veit ([LIT-116](../literature.d/LIT-116.md)), are works and readings, not the
    record's own argument. Lines are carried by the record's own entries,
    and a reading takes the lines of the entries that use it ([ADR-090](ADR-090.md)).
    No held entry uses these yet.
- **No question is filed to seed the line.** No rule asks a line for a
  number of members or for a question, and [QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md) is already a
  question in it. A question written only to fill the line would be an
  idea without content of its own ([ADR-072](ADR-072.md)).

## Alternatives considered

- **File the programme under the nearest line.** will-organization would
  take Sen, authenticity Wolf, organizational-normativity the evaluative
  standard. Each fits a sentence and not the argument, and setting any
  of those lines aside would take part of this inquiry with it, the
  outcome [ADR-073](ADR-073.md) rejected for readiness and realizability.
- **Widen authenticity.** It is set aside as future work, and its subject
  is ownership of a will. Folding meaning and capability into it would
  either revive it for a different inquiry or retire the new inquiry
  with it.
- **One line for [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489)'s whole bridge.** The bridge runs through five
  existing lines and [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489) filed it under them. Only the part about how
  lives go lacks a home.
- **Wait until the works are read.** [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489) registered Wolf's and Sen's
  works and the critics of technology unread, and a reading pass would
  file their accounts first. But the readings need no line. The claims
  built on them do, and the owner has decided the inquiry is one the
  record pursues.

## Consequences

- `luria.yaml` gains the line. docs/record.md and the argument pages are
  regenerated by CI, not edited.
- **The line starts thin**: one question and one claim, each also in
  another line. The record has read little on meaning in life, and most
  of what it holds on Sen is used by will-organization.
- **The intended next application is pull request [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489).** Once both are
  merged:
  - its claims whose subject is how lives go take the line. The first
    candidate is the claim that capability is assessed as a trajectory,
    which rests on [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md). The ratchet, the threshold of domination and
    the burden of justification argue about arrangements, and take it
    only where they argue about the lives those arrangements leave open;
  - the programme it left can be filed: Wolf's meaning as a third ideal,
    the origin against the worth of a desire, the "industrialization of
    meaning" as [TERM-108](../terms.d/TERM-108.md)'s proxy objective applied to meaning, pluralism
    about worthwhile lives, and agency freedom against well-being freedom;
  - its integrating standard, which no admission route reached, stays
    unfiled unless a narrower sentence passes.
- **Setting the line aside** is a status change on its members ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)).
  [QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md) and [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) also stand in their first lines, so that
  setting this line aside does not by itself retire them.
- **No account yet.** A line held in two entries has no account to
  restate ([ADR-083](ADR-083.md)). The first synthesis review after [#489](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/489)'s programme is
  filed gives it one.
- The name and blurb are the assistant's proposal (Claude, in this
  session). The owner may rename the line before merging. A rename is a
  vocabulary edit plus the two `line` values.
