# THEORY-2115: Consciousness theories are meat-neutral

## The account

A computation has a role and a realizer. Computational functionalism ties
consciousness to roles; the meat hypothesis to the biological realizers.
We have both, so extrapolating from ourselves can go either way, and the
leading theories do not decide. Extrapolating on roles favours AI, on
realizers simple animals. The realizer may lie in electrochemical, as
against purely electrical, nervous systems.

## Evidence

Conceptual argument with comparative neurobiology.
