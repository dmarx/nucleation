## The account

Yin and colleagues report that an LLM agent able to inspect and rewrite its own running code,
including its self-improvement routine, raised its benchmark accuracy from a chain-of-thought
start to levels matching or beating a meta-learned agent designer on four reasoning benchmarks,
and that in 100 MGSM trials it ended below its starting policy 14% of the time, with its rare
crashes typically caused by editing its own improvement module.
