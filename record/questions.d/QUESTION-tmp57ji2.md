# QUESTION-tmp57ji2: Can degrees of freedom bound to a topological defect or interface come to maintain the order-parameter structure that localizes them, and if so, is the composite individuated as one organization rather than as a defect plus what it hosts?

## Why it is a question

A10 §3 of the exchange "Topological protection explained"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afe6dd0-9d78-83ea-9782-f55680665dcf) read the
Jackiw–Rebbi mode as a second layer: "the defect acts as a constitutive
condition for a new localized degree of freedom". The bound mode is
individuated dynamically but not autonomously, since its existence
condition lies in the kink. A10 then asked whether the dependence can
become mutual: "If occupying the bound state changes the wall's effective
energy, position, or stability, the relationship becomes bidirectional."
A7 §6 gave the field-theory form, in which the texture shapes the
excitation spectrum and the excitations push back on the texture.

## What the record already says

- Persistence needs a production cycle, and assembly, persistence and
  regeneration come apart ([CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md), [CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md)); closure is [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s.
- A productive partial organization becoming a self-maintaining nucleus is
  [QUESTION-399](QUESTION-399.md), a general form of which this is a field-theory case.
- Topological charge is one route by which a localized structure persists
  ([CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md)), and it fixes a sector, not a token ([CLAIM-694](../claims.d/CLAIM-694.md)).
- Interface ordering: where the order parameter vanishes at a wall, a
  competing order can condense locally (A7 §4), a mechanism for
  [QUESTION-378](QUESTION-378.md)'s new interface degrees of freedom.

## What would answer it

A positive case links localization to self-maintenance; a negative case,
a reason in general why it cannot happen, shows why protection falls
short of autonomy. A10 §6 proposed coupling a Dirac field to the φ⁴ kink
of [CASE-269](../cases.d/CASE-269.md)'s testbed, withdrawing the support that holds the kink, and
asking whether occupation of the bound mode changes the kink's survival.
A's caveat stands: differing frequencies or phases alone do not guarantee
protection, so a dynamical interface needs its own invariant before
anything here is topological.

## Note of 2026-11-19: readings for the exchange "Topological protection explained"

Omidvar and colleagues ([THEORY-tmp5hurd](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp5hurd.md)), read: a partial case of A10's bidirectional relation. In their buckled-beam racetrack, a mode localized at a domain wall softens under the opposite domain's pressure until it goes unstable, and the instability moves the wall, so the hosted mode decides where the defect is. But the mode is the wall's own fluctuation mode, not a separate bound field, and it displaces the wall rather than maintaining it. The wall exists only while an external static compression holds. The maintenance half of the question stays open.

No status change.
