# SYNTHESIS-tmpogcmo: Restricting capabilities, and who controls the terms of waiting and contracting: the grounds of a restriction are kept apart from the authority to impose it, a capability is decomposed into what a restriction can reach, a restriction justified by incompetence can produce the incompetence, an opposition can be free as an actor while another holds its efficacy, and control over a deferred reward or over what a contract left open is control over the other party's terms

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line, filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day. Every component is new to any account. The question is the owner's and Open; the claims and the term are A's and Proposed; the case is a reported study the record has not read, with A's analysis. Where a component cites another, the account says which entry does.*

## The account

**The question.** [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md) (the owner's, U20; Open; "and who
may restrict it" is A45's refinement, added by the filer): under what
conditions should a capability be restricted, and who may restrict it?
Its body lists what the record held before: [CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md), [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md),
[CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) with [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), [TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md) with [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md), and [CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md), as
conditions for particular cases and no general answer. It sorts the
exchange's parts of the question: the grounds and the authority
([CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), "which answers this question in part"), what is
restricted ([TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)), what a restriction does over time
([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)), the capability to change the conditions of one's own
challenge ([QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), with [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) as
two such capabilities), and who sets the terms (A53, at the owner's
U24). It holds A49 §13's five candidate conditions as "a list to test,
not yet an answer".

**Grounds and authority.** [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md) (A's, A45 §6, A41 §5 and A49
§12; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also organizational-normativity):
that a capability's exercise should be limited and that a given party may
limit it are separate conclusions, neither entailing the other; the power
to restrict is itself a capability, whose legitimacy turns on how it is
bounded and answerable. It answers [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md), bears on
[QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md), uses [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md), and complements [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) (its
summary: "the special case in which the ground is a judgment of worth"),
[CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), [CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md) and [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md). Its body adds A49 §12's
third, relational justification, under which "A protective restriction
may become dominating if its continuation depends entirely on unchecked
discretion".

**What a restriction reaches.** [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md) (A's, A41 §1, A45 §4 and
A49 §3; Proposed): capability decomposed for restriction into
competence, exercise, authority and governance, which vary
independently, so a restriction on one is not a restriction on another.
Its body says a restriction on exercise can erode competence where
competence is kept by use ([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)), and that it is not
[CLAIM-678](../claims.d/CLAIM-678.md)'s dimensions of power, [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s resource requirements,
Sen's capability or [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s correctability.

**A restriction that produces its own warrant.** [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md) (A's,
A41 §8, A45 §9 and A49 §8.1; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
transient-support): where competence is kept by use and lost with
disuse, and a restriction is lifted when competence is shown, a
restriction justified by a lack of competence lowers the competence it
waits for, so that without a route to practice it produces the evidence
for its own continuation. It bears on [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md), uses
[TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md), and complements [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) and [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md); its
body says it needs only [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md)'s and [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s premise of decay with
disuse, that A45 §9's instance is [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) reached by restriction
rather than substitution, and that [CLAIM-714](../claims.d/CLAIM-714.md) asks whether the party
that keeps the trap closed is responsible. Its body states the
obligation A41 §8 draws: "*Who is responsible for making condition X
feasible?*"

**Controlled incapacity.** [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md) (A's, A37 §10 to §11, at the
owner's U18; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): an opposition can be
independent as an actor while its capacity to change the arrangement it
opposes depends on another's control of its resources, standing,
corrective authority or time; that controlled incapacity, not
incapacity or failure as such, makes contained opposition a mark of
domination. It bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), and
complements [CLAIM-686](../claims.d/CLAIM-686.md) (its summary: "this adds that such failure can be
held by another party") and [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md) (its body: "the converse
arrangement"). Its body corrects A's gloss of [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md).

**Control over a deferred reward.** [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) (A's, A51 §4 and A53
§2, from the owner's U23 and U24; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
will-organization): where one party gives up something now for a benefit
another controls later, whether waiting is good judgment depends on the
controller's reliability, so a controller not answerable for delivery
can make the other's patience a means of its control. It grounds on
[CASE-281](../cases.d/CASE-281.md), uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), and complements [CLAIM-601](../claims.d/CLAIM-601.md) (its body: "the
same misreading from the other side") and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md). Its body
checks A53 §2's inequality, wait only if 2pδ > 1, and calls [TERM-110](../terms.d/TERM-110.md)'s
ratchet "a mechanism by which a controller changes delivery after
restraint is shown". [CASE-281](../cases.d/CASE-281.md) (the owner's association at U23, A's
analysis in A51 and A53 §2; documented reported study, Proposed, its
work unread; first line will-organization): children who had seen an
experimenter break a promise waited about a quarter as long. It
illustrates [CLAIM-344](../claims.d/CLAIM-344.md); its body says [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) grounds on it, that
waiting behaviour alike over different organizations is [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s
form, and that the case applies the owner's method ([CLAIM-718](../claims.d/CLAIM-718.md)).

**Residual control.** [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md) (the connection to contract theory
the owner's, U24; the residual-control reading A's, A53 §2; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed): in an incomplete contract the holder of residual
rights of control decides what the contract left open, so a relationship
fair in every specified term can leave one party's terms at the other's
discretion, exercised and seen only when an uncovered contingency
arises. It uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) and complements [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) (its summary: "this
names a form that control takes in a contract"), [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) and
[CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), which its body calls "control acquired by violation"
against this, "the authorized case". Its body also reads [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s
parting under intervention as two contracts that part "at the first
uncovered contingency".

## Arm B

- **[QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md)'s answers as one answer** (stage 1's B2: CLAIM-
  tmpmeabu, [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md), [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), across four
  lines). The candidate: a restriction is justified only if its ground
  holds, its imposer has authority, and, where lifting waits on shown
  competence, the restrictor supplies the route back. Route tried: a
  derivation from [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md)'s three justifications and
  [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)'s trap. Most of it is stated already: the obligation of
  a route back is in [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)'s body (A41 §8, A49 §8.1), and the
  question's body holds the conjunction, A49 §13's five conditions, as a
  list to test. The residue, that the trap fails relational
  justification even when lifting is decided by an independent examiner,
  has a separating case (that examiner) but turns on [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md)'s
  "acceptable arrangement", which absorbs every case. Fails for a free
  parameter. [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) answer a different part,
  which capability, as the question's body already sorts them; they do
  not integrate with the other two.
- **One form across waiting, contracting and opposing**
  ([CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md), [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md), [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md)): the act is the
  party's own and another controls the conditions that decide its

  control, and [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md) complement each other.
  The pairing with [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md) adds nothing beyond [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) and is
