principle. *Journal of Economic Methodology*, 20(4), 309–329.

Not read. The summary is drawn from what the paper is standardly cited
for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

[CLAIM-tmpqodkz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqodkz.md) needs the general condition under which a judgement's
circulation bears on its own truth. Soros's reflexivity is one statement of
it: a view held by participants is both an attempt to know a situation and
an intervention in it. His point that the two functions interfere is the
reason [CLAIM-tmpqodkz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqodkz.md) keeps visibility as evidence about a future and not
as a warrant: where circulation shapes the outcome, the outcome is open,
and a forecast's popularity can make it more likely or, through
counter-moves, less.
