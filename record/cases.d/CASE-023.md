# CASE-023: Northstar

## The case

"A fictional technology company whose official commitments give reliability
priority over release speed" (Section 1). Its operating regimes: routine
deployment, cautious verification, incident response, emergency suspension,
recovery.

## What it can show

Fictional, so it holds fixed what no real company would. It earns its place
through its variants: [CASE-003](CASE-003.md), [CASE-002](CASE-002.md) and
[CASE-036](CASE-036.md).

Before the rewrite the name was reused for at least six different scenarios
(incident response, an unwilling addict, product suspension, an extractive
culture, a Goodhart metric). Each variant is its own entry now, so a claim
says which Northstar it means.
