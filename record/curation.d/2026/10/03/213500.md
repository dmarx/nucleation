The owner asked for Susan Wolf's reasons-based account of responsibility,
the Reason View. Neither record held any of her free-will work. This
record named her only for meaning in life ([LIT-183](../../record/literature.d/LIT-183.md), [LIT-186](../../record/literature.d/LIT-186.md)).

Three works are filed, all Deferred:

- "Asymmetrical Freedom" (1980, [LIT-tmpbc48b](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbc48b.md));
- "Sanity and the Metaphysics of Responsibility" (1987/88, [LIT-tmpooua4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpooua4.md));
- *Freedom Within Reason* (1990, [LIT-tmp2u6df](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp2u6df.md)).

None has a lawful open copy:

- Unpaywall lists all three as closed.
- JSTOR, PDCnet and Cambridge Core are paywalled, and PhilPapers served a
  bot challenge.
- Re-posts on unrelated personal sites, Scribd and Course Hero were left
  unopened.

Each LIT states what the reports say: the SEP's *Compatibilism* §4.3
([LIT-291](../../record/literature.d/LIT-291.md)), Holton's MIT lecture handout, and the book's Crossref chapter
openings. Each claim is labelled as second-hand.

The book is filed as well as the papers for three reasons: [NOTE-270](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-270.md)'s
"Wolf 1990, p. 29" otherwise dangles, the Autonomy, Real Self and Reason
View frame is the book's own, and its chapter 5 defence of ability is
in neither paper.

No relation to Frankfurt ([LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md)) is declared yet. "Sanity" is reported to
target Frankfurt 1971, Watson and Taylor, none of which the record holds,
and both entries are unread. The LIT says to declare `rivals` against
[LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md) if a reading shows Wolf's objection reaches wholeheartedness.

No theory is filed, because nothing here has been read. The two
candidates, kept for when a paper is read, are:

- the Reason View: responsibility needs a normative competence that
  identification does not supply;
- the praise/blame asymmetry.

Fischer & Ravizza's reasons-responsiveness, which the phrase usually
names, is closed too and was not filed.

## What would unblock it

A PDF of "Asymmetrical Freedom" (JSTOR, 16 pp.) or of "Sanity" (Schoeman
ch. 3, or the reprint in Watson's *Free Will*). Either would allow a read
NOTE and the theories.
