# LIT-tmpetw17: The free-energy self: a predictive coding account of self-recognition

Apps, M. A. J. & Tsakiris, M. (2014). *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 41:85–97. doi:10.1016/j.neubiorev.2013.01.029. PMC3848896.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp69t3e](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp69t3e.md).*

- **The self as the best explanation.** "One's own body is the one which
  has the highest probability of being 'me' as other objects are
  probabilistically less likely to evoke the same sensory inputs" (§4).
  Touch seen on the skin and touch felt become associated, so the body is
  "the object that touch is predicted to be experienced upon".
- **Illusions as explaining away.** In the rubber-hand illusion, felt
  touch synchronous with seen touch on the rubber hand is surprising, and
  multimodal areas explain the surprise by raising the probability that
  the rubber hand is "me" (§5.1, Fig. 1). The same account is offered for
  enfacement and the rubber-voice illusion.
- **Two competing models.** The real body and the other object are
  rival generative models of who is "me", and recognition follows their
  relative fit, by analogy with binocular rivalry (§5.2). Cooling and
  histamine changes in the real hand are read as the real limb being
  "partially rejected".
- **Context is the prior.** The illusion weakens or fails for a
  non-corporeal object, a hand of a different skin tone or one in an
  incongruent position; culture and priming shift self-face bias (§5.3).
- **Three anatomical predictions (§6).** No unimodal area is
  self-specific; multimodal areas (TPJ, IPS, anterior insula, IFG) are
  engaged by self-recognition and by the illusions, but not exclusively;
  a predicted self-stimulus suppresses activity (self-tickle, seeing one's
  own face touched).
- **Its own caveats (§7).** "There is, as yet, little direct evidence that
  predictions and prediction errors related to self-stimuli are processed
  anywhere in the brain"; the theory is "hard to falsify using standard
  neuroscientific methods"; whether the self is represented
  probabilistically has had no direct test.

## Standing in the record

Filed in the body-ownership batch and read the same day. It is the bridge
between the illusion experiments and the record's active-inference
material ([LIT-1181](LIT-1181.md), [NOTE-960](../notes.d/NOTE-960.md)). Its account runs entirely through
perception and perceptual learning. The participant's hand stays still
and the evidence is delivered by an experimenter. In the 2010
formulation that is only the training phase, with action "precluded"
([NOTE-960](../notes.d/NOTE-960.md)). This paper says in so many words that it describes
self-labelling "in situations where actions are not a driving factor for
the minimisation of free-energy" (§6). Filed as [THEORY-tmpugoyc](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpugoyc.md), Proposed.
It carries no instruction for machine-learning practice.
