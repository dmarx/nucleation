# NOTE-tmpg26n5: Lyre — Neurophenomenal structuralism

## Contribution

The paper states neurophenomenal structuralism as an ontology, where its
companion paper (Fink, Kob & Lyre 2021) had stated it as a method. Every
specific phenomenal character is said to be fixed by its place in the
structure of a quality space, and that structure is said to be mirrored in
neural maps. It then follows the thesis out: to the Newman problem, to
reduction, to structural representation, to holism and to qualia
inversion. It is an agenda. The author says most of its topics "deserve a
much more detailed consideration" (Conclusion).

## Key insight

Only differences cross the boundary between the world and the nervous
system, so the brain works on relations, and so does what it makes
experienced. An orange experience is orange because of where it sits among
all possible colour experiences, between yellow and red, and nothing
intrinsic is added. Whether the state is conscious at all is a separate
question, answered by some general mechanism the paper does not choose.

## Assumptions

- **Change detection.** "Neural sensory systems work by change detection,
  and only relational properties can cross the 3N boundary" (Conclusion).
  The 3N boundary is the neural/non-neural interface, inward transduction
  and outward motor transformation. Footnote 3 likens it to a Markov
  blanket.
- **Two assumptions of the programme** (Introduction; restated in
  "Structure, homomorphisms, and structural individuation"):
  "Any phenomenal experience is fully individuated by its place in a
  Q-structure. Phenomenal content and character are relational, not
  intrinsic properties of sentient subjects." And: "Q-structure is mirrored
  in N-structure. The mirroring has to be understood in terms of structural
  similarity, more precisely, in terms of a surjective homomorphism from N
  to Q."
- **Specific, not generic.** It "does not tell us when a mental state is
  conscious in general" (Introduction). Phenomenal character is
  "experienced content", a composite of the neural correlate of content and
  a general neural correlate of consciousness, after Marvan and Polák. The
  general mechanism is "at best system-intrinsic, not state-intrinsic", so
  character is "as much relational as content" ("Phenomenality, content,
  and characters").
- **Structure** is a set of relations over relata that "are not specified
  otherwise than by the relations in which they stand" (Introduction).
  "Leibnizian individuality and intrinsicality must be given up."
- **Metaphysically necessary supervenience.** Asserted, not argued: "There
  is, I admit, no argument for upholding Q–N supervenience with
  metaphysical necessity ... other than the request to adhere to the
  overall scientific, more properly physicalist, spirit" ("Reductive
  physicalism").
- **Colour as the case.** Only colour is worked through. Interoception and
  the body are set aside "for reasons of space" ("The neural/non-neural
  interface").

## Key results

All results are arguments. By section:

- **The Newman problem of the brain.** If the brain had relations only, it
  could know nothing of the world but cardinality, which is Newman's
  objection to Russell. Lyre takes Russell's reply to Newman: we are
  "directly acquainted" with some spatiotemporal relations. Spatial
  separation on the skin or retina and temporal order are carried over
  into the separation and order of neural activity, giving "spatiotemporal
  grounding of N in W via perception". Sensory intersections (seeing a
  surface and feeling it warm) spread that grounding through the whole
  system. Footnote 6: "it is the nature of spatiotemporal relations, and
  only their nature, that can pass the 3N boundary."
- **Structural individuation.** The nature of the relations is fixed and
  "the relata are determined up to isomorphy only". A structure with no
  symmetries, such as three relata at 3, 4 and 5 m from each other, makes
  them "maximally structurally individuated", and still "the structural
  individuation does not amount to the attribution of intrinsic properties
  to the relata" ("Structure, homomorphisms, and structural
  individuation").
- **Quality spaces.** Discriminability is the root relation, and
  similarity is its dual, after Clark: "similarity is naught but a failure
  to discriminate". Just-noticeable differences give units and similarity
  ratings give order, from which the colour solid is built ("Quality
  spaces, Q-structure, and psychophysics").
- **Neural maps.** Self-organizing (Kohonen) maps place neighbours in
  feature space next to each other in the wiring. A map from cone
  opponency onto cortex "mirrors the structure of color space"; Brouwer &
  Heeger 2009 found colour-space similarity structure in V4, which FKL
  take as a candidate structural correlate. Plasticity and multiple
  realizability make the map many-to-one, hence "surjective homomorphism"
  ("N-structure"; "Empirical evidence and consciousness studies").
- **Method.** Ask "how a given stimulus relates to other stimuli", not
  whether it is conscious. Similarity ratings and representational
  similarity analysis are the tools ("Empirical evidence and consciousness
  studies").
- **Not structural realism.** "In particular, neurophenomenal
  structuralism is no realism about the phenomenal." Q-structure is
  mirrored in N-structure, the neural maps bear the content, and an
  exclusion argument gives all causal work to N ("Structural realism").
- **Reductive physicalism.** Shapiro's dilemma removes the multiple
  realizability argument: realizers that mirror Q-structure share the
  causally relevant relational properties, so "structurally construed
  Q-types can a fortiori be reduced to N-types". With necessary
  supervenience, a "tightened exclusion argument" follows: "the Q-structure
  plays no causal role at all; all causal work is taken over by the
  N-structure" ("Reductive physicalism").
- **Structural representation.** Neural maps are a third type of
  structural representation, of feature structure, beside spatial and
  temporal ones. They mirror "abstract (rather than concrete
  spatiotemporal) W-structure" by partial homomorphism, so they refer to
  the world only indirectly. Exploitability makes the programme
  "pragmatic or instrumentalist", and content is "instrumental at best"
  ("Structural representation"; "Reductive physicalism").
- **Holism.** "Think of the Q-structure as an elastic web." Any change can
  change the whole. Its predictions: red-green dichromats "will experience
  all colors differently"; ageing listeners do not notice that what they
  now hear as very high was once only high, because "what changes are not
  single experiences, but the web" ("Neurophenomenal holism").
- **Qualia inversion.** Inversion needs a non-trivial automorphism of
  colour space. Actual inversion is ruled out because human colour space
  is "highly asymmetric". Nomological inversion is ruled out by sensory
  intersections: "dark-colored objects absorb heat better than
  light-colored objects". Metaphysical inversion with quiddistic qualia
  "cannot be ruled out", and "certainly cannot be captured by any
  structuralist conception". The programme "upholds the identity of
  Q-space isomorphs" ("Qualia inversion and (finally) the hard problem").
- **The hard problem.** "what-it's-like character reduces to holistic
  phenomenal content (which in turn reduces to neural vehicle
  representations)". Since it addresses only specific consciousness, "it
  can never fully solve the hard problem, but may contribute to its
  solution" (Conclusion).
- **Footnote 21, IIT.** IIT "is intrinsicalism about phenomenality, and
  thus the opposite of structuralism", despite its qualia space.

## Concepts

- **W-, Q-, N-domain**: worldly stimuli, phenomenal states, neural states.
  A B-domain, the body, sits between N and W and is set aside.
- **3N boundary**: the neural/non-neural interface, "boundary of neural
  transduction" inward and "boundary of neuromotor transformation" outward.
- **Q-structure, Q-space**: the structure of a quality space, the totality
  of discriminations a sensory system can make.
- **Structural individuation**: individuating relata only by the relations
  they stand in, with the nature of the relations fixed. Relata are then
  fixed up to isomorphism.
- **Surjective homomorphism (epimorphism)**: a structure-preserving,
  many-to-one map, here from N onto Q.
- **Type-1, -2, -3 structural representations**: of spatial, temporal and
  feature structure. Neural feature maps are type 3.
- **Neurophenomenal holism**: each experience's identity depends on the
  whole Q-structure, so local change propagates.

## Connections

It builds on Fink, Kob & Lyre 2021 and, most of all, on Austen Clark's
quality-space theory (1993, 2000). It takes Isaac (2013, 2014) for
structural representation and for structural realism about colour
qualities, which it calls "otherwise quite similar" but about the
world–experience relation where this paper is about the neural–experience
relation. It takes the Newman objection from Newman 1928 and the reply
from Russell's letter. It puts itself beside Malach 2021 and Tsuchiya &
Saigo 2021 among structuralist neuroscience. Negro 2025 ([LIT-tmp86j0r](../literature.d/LIT-tmp86j0r.md)) and
Aldé 2026 ([LIT-tmpac9l2](../literature.d/LIT-tmpac9l2.md)) both quote its first assumption as the
explicit statement of the view they attack. Isaac 2025 ([LIT-tmpwhbpm](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwhbpm.md)) was
not reached. One cross-reference slips: the qualia section places neurophenomenal
holism in the section "Structural realism", where the section
"Neurophenomenal holism" is meant.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmpdga80](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdga80.md) (constitution by interpretive organization).** Lyre's
  thesis and the claim share a shape: character is fixed by organization,
  not by an intrinsic quale. They differ in three ways that matter. Lyre's
  relations are discriminations, a similarity structure; the claim's are
  interpretive, with context and consequence ([TERM-tmpgqmop](../terms.d/TERM-tmpgqmop.md)). Lyre makes
  structure fix only *which* experience a conscious state is, and leaves
  *whether* it is conscious to a separate general mechanism; the claim
  makes organization constitute occurrence as well ([TERM-tmpe36gg](../terms.d/TERM-tmpe36gg.md), A11:
  "occurrence, character, and unity"). And Lyre takes the reductive and
  instrumentalist route, with content "instrumental at best". So Lyre is
  the nearest held statement of the claim's character half and no support
  for its occurrence half. The comparison is mine.
- **[CLAIM-tmp1lvwf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1lvwf.md) and [QUESTION-tmpc4j7p](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpc4j7p.md).** Lyre's split between neural
  correlates of content and a general correlate of consciousness is the
  same gap these entries name, conceded from the structuralist side:
  structure alone does not say when a state is conscious. He declines to
  fill it, listing workspace, recurrent processing, higher-order theories,
  synchrony and critical-brain views as compatible.
- **[CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md) (communicative relata without primitive identity).** Lyre
  explicitly denies being a structural realist, and his relata are
  experiences, not communicative acts. But his account of structural
  individuation is the strict version of [CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md) in another domain: relata
  "are not specified otherwise than by the relations in which they stand",
  are fixed "up to isomorphy only", and gain no intrinsic properties even
  when maximally individuated. His treatment of inversion shows what the
  strict version costs. Where colour space had a symmetry, the structuralist
  must identify the symmetric experiences ("the identity of Q-space
  isomorphs"), and Lyre avoids that only by appeal to the empirical
  asymmetry of colour space. [CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md)'s weak version, numerical plurality
  without differentiation, is not available to him, since two
  indiscernible experiences would be one experience. Lyre also restates
  non-eliminative moderate OSR as "there are relations and relata, but ...
  there is nothing more to the relata than the relations in which they
  stand", which matches the claim's own description of it.
- **[CLAIM-138](../claims.d/CLAIM-138.md) (Newman and independently specified relations).** Lyre's
  reply to the Newman problem is that "we must specify the nature of the
  relations", spatiotemporal ones, by acquaintance. That is the remedy
  [CLAIM-138](../claims.d/CLAIM-138.md) says concedes non-structural input. Lyre does not see a cost; he
  makes spatiotemporal relations primitive and carried across. A further
  instance for [CLAIM-138](../claims.d/CLAIM-138.md)'s parallel, outside the communicative domain.
- **[THEORY-095](../theory.d/THEORY-095.md) (Inverted Earth).** Lyre names Block's inverted earth as
  an "outlandish" case and replies only to inversion within colour space.
  His structuralism is internal in [THEORY-095](../theory.d/THEORY-095.md)'s sense, Q mirrored in N,
  so it is one of the accounts [THEORY-095](../theory.d/THEORY-095.md) leaves open.
- **[THEORY-633](../theory.d/THEORY-633.md) (illusionism).** Lyre is a realist about specific character
  and reductive about it. He calls intrinsic qualia "mysterious", not
  illusory, so he is not an illusionist, but his "Qualitative properties
  seem to be intrinsic properties, but they are not" (quoting Clark) is
  the move [THEORY-633](../theory.d/THEORY-633.md) generalizes.
- **THEORY warranted.** The record holds no statement of phenomenal
  structuralism as a positive thesis. [THEORY-tmpv5rmc](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpv5rmc.md) states Lyre's, with
  its holism and inversion consequences, so that the two objections read
  in the same batch ([THEORY-tmp7uasf](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7uasf.md), [THEORY-tmp4u3l2](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4u3l2.md)) have a target.
- No instruction for machine-learning practice; nothing for the
  anthology.

## Limitations

- Programmatic. The author calls it "largely of programmatic nature" and
  offers "neither a clear proof for structuralism nor a knockout argument
  against intrinsicalism".
- The reduction rests on metaphysically necessary supervenience, which
  the author says he has no argument for.
- The empirical support for the neural half is one imaging study of V4,
  cited, and representational similarity analysis in general, which
  footnote 19 says gives only "proxy vehicles".
- Only colour is worked through. Interoception, the body and other
  modalities are named as future work.
- The holism predictions (dichromats, ageing hearing) are offered without
  data, and Negro's novel experience argument ([NOTE-tmp0dy5a](NOTE-tmp0dy5a.md)) turns
  exactly this consequence against the view.
- The nomological case against inversion turns on a heat-absorption
  correlation between colour and temperature experience. That ties colour
  experience to other experiences; it does not show that no symmetry of the
  whole cross-modal structure exists.

## Open questions

- Which general mechanism makes a structurally individuated content
  experienced? The paper leaves it open and says it is compatible with
  many.
- Does the holism prediction hold? A test would compare similarity
  structures for colours both groups discriminate, in dichromats and
  trichromats, and ask whether the shared part differs.
- If spatiotemporal relations are carried across the boundary by their
  nature, is the view still structuralist about those relations, or does
  it rest on something non-structural, as [CLAIM-138](../claims.d/CLAIM-138.md) would say?
- Can the unique hues and the opponent structure of colour be read off the
  geometry of the colour space? Aldé ([NOTE-tmp0qd7y](NOTE-tmp0qd7y.md)) argues they cannot.
