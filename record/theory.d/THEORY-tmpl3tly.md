# THEORY-tmpl3tly: An emotional state is a large-scale selection of mental resources, switched on and off by Selectors that Critics trigger on recognising a type of problem, and so of the same kind as an intellectual way to think; feelings are the mind's descriptions of these cascades, and the body is one more resource the brain uses, not the substance of emotion

## Source

Minsky (2006), *The Emotion Machine*, [LIT-432](../literature.d/LIT-432.md), read in [NOTE-348](../notes.d/NOTE-348.md) from the
author's 2005 draft. The published book was not seen.

## What was actually shown

**The thesis (§§1-5, 1-7).** "Each of our major 'emotional states' results
from turning some set of resources on and turning another set of them
off." Selectors do the switching. When the switched set includes other
Selectors, the change spreads into a "cascade" that alters goals,
perception and priorities together ([NOTE-348](../notes.d/NOTE-348.md), C1, weak: a design
proposal).

**The loop (§7-3).** A Critic recognises a type of obstacle ("I've made
many attempts with no success"), and a Selector turns on a different set
of resources, a different Way to Think. Emotional states are the subclass
with popular names. At the lowest level a Critic and a Selector are just
an If and a Then.

**Feelings (§§3-3, 9-6).** "What we call 'feelings' are attempts (by
various parts of our minds) to describe large-scale aspects of mental
conditions." Their objects are brain cascades, and they need reflective
self-models.

**The body (§§3-8, 7-6).** The body is "just one more set of resources to
exploit". Bodily expression may keep a state alive as a "dependable
external memory device", which grants James something, but "exaggerating
the body's role in emotions can lead to serious misconceptions" (C7,
weak: argued against James with no data).

**Elliot (§7-2).** Damasio infers that reduced emotion degraded decision.
Minsky proposes the reverse: lost Critics degraded decision, and lost
cascades followed. No evidence is offered, and the gambling-task data are
not discussed (C8, speculation).

**What could have come out otherwise.** Nothing in the draft could have:
the arguments are vignettes and dialogues, and the neural claims are
guesses about what scans "should" one day show.

## What this does not say

- **That emotion is opposed to reason, or separable from it.** Minsky calls
  emotion-free rationality "a myth", which Damasio also holds.
- **That the mechanism is specified.** Critics, Selectors, panalogies and
  micronemes are named, none precisely enough to implement or test, as
  Singh ([LIT-444](../literature.d/LIT-444.md)) says of the earlier book ([NOTE-348](../notes.d/NOTE-348.md), Limitations).
- **That the six levels are an architecture.** "I agree that those
  boundaries are indistinct" (§5-6). Model Six and the Critic–Selector
  model are two views to switch between.
- **That the body plays no part.** It grants bodily expression a role as
  external memory; it denies that the body is what an emotion is.
- **That consciousness is explained.** "Consciousness" is unpacked as a
  suitcase word for 27 activities, and the sense that it is one thing
  could come from a detector of four co-occurring features (C4, moderate
  as a conceptual argument, weak for the detector).
