# LIT-tmpv7ysw: Questions, Options, and Criteria: Elements of Design Space Analysis

Allan MacLean, Richard Young, Victoria Bellotti and Thomas Moran (1991), *Human–Computer Interaction* 6(3–4):201–250 — DOI-10.1207/s15327051hci0603&4_2

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the abstract only: Design Space Analysis
represents design rationale in a semiformal notation, QOC. Questions
identify key design issues, Options are possible answers, and Criteria
assess and compare the Options. The analysis is not a record of the design
process but a coproduct of design, built alongside the artifact. Its claimed
return is support for reasoning about the design and its changes, and for
communication within the team and with later maintainers. The authors say
plainly that these claims are goals they have not yet achieved, since the
work emphasises the representation over the process of creating it.
