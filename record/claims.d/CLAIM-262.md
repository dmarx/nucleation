# CLAIM-262: Whether an agent's regimes are coordinated by discrete switches or by continuous reorganization depends on how the agent is constituted; both occur

## The claim

The owner's position, given on 2026-10-12: "both can be possible
depending on how the agent is constituted. An example of the continuous
case is the navigation regimes of the Twitch Plays Pokemon collective,
which operationalizes an approximation of the
agent-as-population-subject-to-drift-diffusion-decision-dynamics case."

**The continuous case.** Take an agent whose parts each push, noisily,
toward one option or another, and whose choice is the accumulated
balance of the pushes. Its state is a drift-diffusion process. Under the
conditions of [THEORY-068](../theory.d/THEORY-068.md), two competing leaky accumulators reduce to
one when leak and inhibition balance. Such an agent's state moves as the
balance moves, and nothing in it is a switch between stable patterns
until the choice is read out.

The crowd of [CASE-075](../cases.d/CASE-075.md) steers one avatar this way. The owner
judges democracy mode the better demonstration, especially with the
running tally visible while the votes come in ([CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md)). There
each move is a choice made by accumulating votes. In anarchy mode it is
only the avatar's position that drifts.

**A dial between the two.** Drawn by the assistant from [CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md):
with the tally hidden, the counts are balanced integrators, the drift
diffusion model itself for a two-way vote. With the tally visible, a
leading count draws more votes, the net leak turns negative, and the
process commits early, toward winner-take-all. One parameter of the
crowd's constitution moves it from the continuous form toward the
discrete one.

**The discrete case.** An agent whose parts take control in turn, or
whose interactions have several attractors, moves between metastable
states. [LIT-080](../literature.d/LIT-080.md)'s account of brain states is of this kind. When
inhibition dominates leak in [THEORY-068](../theory.d/THEORY-068.md)'s network, the accumulators
also become winner-take-all.

So the form is a property of the constitution, and the rivalry between
[LIT-638](../literature.d/LIT-638.md) and [LIT-080](../literature.d/LIT-080.md) may be a difference between systems, or between
scales, rather than a dispute that one side must win.

## What it means for the transition test

[CLAIM-163](CLAIM-163.md)'s third demand is transitions that "behave as switches between
stable patterns", with hysteresis and critical slowing. Drawn by the
assistant: the test must be stated relative to the agent's constitution.
In an agent built to switch, a missing switch is evidence against
coordination. In an agent that accumulates continuously, it is what the
account predicts. The test there should look for the drift and the
boundary instead: a bias in the aggregate that tracks the agent's goal,
and crossings that its accumulated evidence predicts.

## What it does not say

It does not say which form any particular human will takes. It does not
say that Twitch Plays Pokémon has been fitted by a drift-diffusion model.
The case is the owner's reading, and the record holds no source for it.
And it does not carry [THEORY-068](../theory.d/THEORY-068.md) beyond its own conditions, which
are two alternatives and stationary evidence. A crowd with eight buttons
and a shifting goal satisfies neither, so the grounding is by analogy.
