# THEORY-095: Phenomenal character is not fixed by long-arm functional role or by externally individuated representational content: on Inverted Earth it stays the same while both invert, so only internal accounts, short-arm functional or physical, remain open

## Source

- Block (1990), *Inverted Earth*, [LIT-634](../literature.d/LIT-634.md), read in full in
  [NOTE-504](../notes.d/NOTE-504.md): pp. 53–58 (the two contents, long-arm roles), 62–71 (the
  case, its advantages and the objections), nn. 26–29. Page numbers are
  the journal's.
- Block (2003), *Mental Paint*, [LIT-635](../literature.d/LIT-635.md), read in full in
  [NOTE-511](../notes.d/NOTE-511.md): pp. 165, 168–171 (water emigrant, swampman) and 183–188
  (Inverted Earth against representationism, long-term memory).

## What was actually shown

**Two contents.** Block (1990), [LIT-634](../literature.d/LIT-634.md), separates the intentional
content of a colour experience, what it represents, from its qualitative
content, what it is like (p. 54). Intentional content is fixed by what the
experience is normally caused by and used about, so it is functional in
"long-arm" roles that include real things in the world as inputs and
outputs (p. 58).

**The case.** Inverted Earth has the complementary colour of every object
and inverted colour vocabulary. A subject kidnapped there and fitted with
inverting lenses notices no change. On the first day his intentional
contents are still Earth's, and wrong. "After enough time has passed on
Inverted Earth, your embedding in the physical and linguistic environment
of Inverted Earth would dominate", and fifty years on he uses 'red' to
mean green as the natives do. "Once your intentional contents have
inverted, so do your functional states" (p. 64). In the interpersonal
version, a twin with a molecularly identical brain, raised there with the
lenses, has the same qualitative content looking at his sky and inverted
intentional content and long-arm role. So, for a functional state
identity theory, one qualitative state would be identical to two distinct
functional states, "Q = F₁, Q = F₂ and ~(F₁ = F₂), which contravenes
transitivity of identity" (pp. 64–65). The case needs no internal switch,
so it escapes the doubt about testimony that sinks the intrapersonal
inverted spectrum, and identical brains make sameness of qualia the
supervenience default (pp. 67–68).

**Against externalist representationism.** Block (2003), [LIT-635](../literature.d/LIT-635.md),
restates the case against the view that phenomenal character is, or is
determined by, representational content individuated externally. When a
Twin Earth emigrant's water content changes, sameness of experience is
explained by represented appearances (colour, sheen, flow) that did not
change, and the representationist wins that round (pp. 168–170). For
colour there is no further represented appearance left over, so what
stays the same on Inverted Earth must be a feature of the experience:
"Mental paint is what stays the same". Lycan's reply, that the
traveller's experience shifts gradually without his knowing, makes
ordinary phenomenal memory "inherently defective", "an error theory"
with nothing to recommend it beyond saving the view under attack
(pp. 186–188). And if the traveller's contents never shift, he is
inverted relative to the natives, which defeats representationism too
(p. 188).

**What remains open.** Block concedes that the brain-internal functional
descriptions of the twins are identical, and that a "two factor"
functionalism, long-arm roles for intentional content and short-arm,
purely internal roles for qualia, would escape: "if there is some
functional characterization of qualia, that will sink the Inverted Earth
Argument". His answer is a burden of proof, to say what those short-arm
states are, the qualia in "the 'program' of the mind": "Good luck to
him!" (pp. 69–71). His own bet, n. 29, is that "the best bet for a theory
of qualia lies in neurophysiology", and in 2003 he calls himself "a
physicalist phenomenist" (p. 182). So the account rules out two families
and leaves internal ones standing.

What could have come out otherwise: the externalist could have found an
internal appearance content for colour, as for water, or a reason to
doubt the traveller's memory that does not assume the conclusion. Block
argues neither is available.

## What this does not say

- **It does not refute functionalism about qualia as such.** Only its
  long-arm form. Chalmers's organisational invariance ([LIT-392](../literature.d/LIT-392.md), [LIT-406](../literature.d/LIT-406.md))
  is a principle about fine-grained internal organisation, and Block's
  twins are internally identical, so the two agree that the twins share
  qualia ([NOTE-504](../notes.d/NOTE-504.md), Bearing). This account must not be cited
  against it.
- **It does not refute internalist representationism.** Inverted Earth
  holds the brain fixed, so a view on which phenomenal content is
  representational content that locally supervenes on the brain is
  untouched by it. Block presses that view with a different case, Erisa,
  raised among colours that keep changing ([LIT-635](../literature.d/LIT-635.md), pp. 166–168),
  which is a separate argument, offered as pressure, and not part of this
  claim.
- **It rests on externalism for intentional content.** The shift after
  fifty years is an externalist intuition (Burge, Putnam), and readers
  who deny it lose the intrapersonal case; the interpersonal twins are
  Block's answer to them ([NOTE-504](../notes.d/NOTE-504.md), Limitations).
- **It assumes qualia realism.** The argument presupposes that there are
  intrinsic features of experience to stay fixed. Someone who denies that
  there are qualia at all is not refuted by it.
- **The empirical latitude is assumed.** If human physiology rules out an
  undetectable inverter, Block's reply is that a race very like us whose
  physiology does not would do (p. 64).

## Rivals, and why none is declared

**Dennett.** "Quining Qualia" ([LIT-433](../literature.d/LIT-433.md)) argues that from the inside a
subject cannot tell a change of qualia from a change of memory-linked
reactions, and concludes that "there simply are no qualia at all".
[LIT-634](../literature.d/LIT-634.md) answers it by name: Block grants "some justice" in the
memory-malfunction diagnosis of the intrapersonal inverted spectrum, and
builds Inverted Earth so that there is no internal disturbance for it to
attach to (p. 61, n. 20). The two works rival each other, and the LITs say
so. But they disagree about whether there are qualia, which this account
assumes, not about what fixes them, and the record holds no theory
stating Dennett's view. So no `rivals` is declared here. If the record
files qualia eliminativism as a theory, it rivals this account's
presupposition rather than its claim.

**Metzinger and the self-model.** Metzinger ([LIT-568](../literature.d/LIT-568.md), [LIT-450](../literature.d/LIT-450.md)) holds that
phenomenal content is representational content with specific functional
properties, and that it locally supervenes on the brain: a brain in a vat
could have it ([NOTE-460](../notes.d/NOTE-460.md), [NOTE-372](../notes.d/NOTE-372.md)). That is an internalist representationism,
one of the accounts this claim leaves open. [THEORY-064](THEORY-064.md) (the phenomenal
self is a transparent self-model) is about what is experienced as a self,
not about what fixes phenomenal character, and [THEORY-063](THEORY-063.md) (the unity of
experience as one functional cluster) does not depend on representationism
about character either ([NOTE-511](../notes.d/NOTE-511.md), Bearing). Neither is a rival, and
neither is touched by Inverted Earth, which holds the brain fixed.

**Troubles with Functionalism.** [LIT-407](../literature.d/LIT-407.md) is where the case began: Block
presents Inverted Earth as a better version of an example there, inverting
lenses and switched colour words in a village without standing colour
beliefs ([LIT-634](../literature.d/LIT-634.md), nn. 21–22), and its point that psychophysics would
come out the same with inverted qualia is what the later case turns into
an argument that needs no internal switch. It is a precursor, not a
source: this account would stand without it.

## Connections

- **[THEORY-023](THEORY-023.md)** (AI consciousness cannot currently be settled). If
  phenomenal character were fixed by long-arm role or by externally
  individuated content, a system's history of causal contact with the
  world would bear on what its experience is like. This account says it
  does not, so the dispute that account describes is between internal
  accounts, short-arm functional and biological, which is where Block's
  2002 and 2009 papers already place it. No relation is declared.
- **Two-factor content** ([THEORY-089](THEORY-089.md), filed alongside). Block's
  note 26 cites "Functional Role and Truth Conditions" ([LIT-625](../literature.d/LIT-625.md)) for
  the equivalence of long-arm roles to short-arm roles plus reference. On
  that analysis, what inverts on Inverted Earth is the referential factor,
  and the short-arm role, the twins' shared internal factor, does not.
  This account asks the functionalist to do for qualia what the two-factor
  theory does for content, and says nobody has.
