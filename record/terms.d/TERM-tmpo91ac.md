# TERM-tmpo91ac: correspondence structure, and how it is held: the maps that say which local states or descriptions of interacting systems count as agreeing, distinct from the physical coupling between them, and held as formed, discovered or adapted

## Definition

A **correspondence structure** on a family of locally described systems is the
family of maps ρ_{i,e}: V_i → W_e that send each system's local description
into a space shared with a neighbour. Two local states agree on the edge e when
ρ_{i,e}(x_i) = ρ_{j,e}(x_j). Collected over the network, the maps define a
coboundary δ, and the jointly compatible assignments are its kernel, the global
sections (Hansen and Ghrist, [NOTE-tmpzqos6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzqos6.md)). "Compatible", "glued" and
"frustrated" are all relative to a correspondence structure.

It is held in one of three ways ([CLAIM-tmpdjt64](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdjt64.md)):

| how held | what it is | example the record has read |
|---|---|---|
| **formed** | physical interconnection fixes which joint behaviour is admissible | Baez and Fong's port conditions, [NOTE-tmpigjto](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpigjto.md) |
| **discovered** | measurement establishes a correspondence that already held | Singer's relative angles, [NOTE-tmplhrxg](../notes.d/NOTE-tmplhrxg.md); Robinson's geometric maps, [NOTE-tmpdshmx](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpdshmx.md) |
| **adapted** | an apparatus revises its own rules of comparison | discourse sheaves, [NOTE-tmpe4zh5](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe4zh5.md) |

## The distinctions it draws

- **From coupling.** The coupling is what moves the states, for instance a
  dissipative Laplacian flow. The correspondence structure is what counts
  as agreement. A coupling attracts to the specified compatible space only
  if it enforces that structure ([CASE-tmpl995z](../cases.d/CASE-tmpl995z.md)). Setting the two apart is
  what makes the proposed study informative.
- **From a representation of it.** An apparatus's model of the
  correspondences is not the operative structure ([CLAIM-tmpdjt64](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdjt64.md); A23 §IX:
  ĉ_t ≠ c_t in general). An adapted structure changes the apparatus.
  It changes the systems compared only if it is implemented.
- **Between the three ways of holding it.** Only formation bears on the
  thesis that interaction generates constraints. Discovery is knowledge of
  a correspondence, and its adequacy needs evidence the correspondence does
  not itself produce ([CLAIM-143](../claims.d/CLAIM-143.md)). Adaptation can succeed at agreement by
  changing what is compared ([THEORY-tmptkmt9](../theory.d/THEORY-tmptkmt9.md)).

## Why it is proposed

The word was doing three jobs in one claim before the owner's review separated
them, and four entries filed the same evening use it. [QUESTION-tmplxq7l](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmplxq7l.md) asks
when interaction changes the operative structure, and it needs the term to ask
it. What would settle it: adoption if claims cite it to say which sense they
mean; rejection if "gluing" and "compatibility" carry the distinction without
it.
