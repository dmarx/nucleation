# LIT-975: Contextures: The Mechanism of Representation Learning

Runtian Zhai (2025), *arXiv:2504.19792, PhD dissertation*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, NOTE-tmp7o2ar.*

- **The object is a singular value decomposition, not an eigenspace.**
  The operator (T_P⁺g)(x) = E[g(A)|x] maps functions of the context to
  functions of the input. Its adjoint is given by Bayes' rule, and the
  self-adjoint operators T_P⁺T_P⁺* and T_P⁺*T_P⁺ share the squared singular
  values (Lemma 1.4). The density ratio expands as
  Σ s_i μ_i(x)ν_i(a) (Lemma 1.5). Both compositions are assumed
  Hilbert–Schmidt.
- **The optimality theorem.** If 1 − ε ≤ s₁, an encoder minimizes the
  worst-case approximation error of a linear probe over tasks of
  compatibility at least 1 − ε if and only if it spans the top-d left
  singular functions. The error is then (s₁² − (1 − ε)²)/(s₁² − s²_{d+1})
  (Theorem 3.4).
- **Compatible tasks are nearly invariant.** They vary little across two
  context draws for the same input (Theorem 3.2).
- **Objectives that reach it.** Balanced MSE, the spectral contrastive
  loss, covariance-constrained non-contrastive learning and a two-encoder
  objective (SVME) reach the span at their population optimum (Theorems
  2.4, 2.9, 2.11). Cross-entropy and InfoNCE are outside the theory.
- **Weakly supported.** Alignment of trained networks with the span is
  shown on one tabular dataset. The diminishing-returns argument about
  scaling rests on it. The moderate-association metric correlates with
  error at a median of 0.587 across 28 datasets and fails on some.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for QUESTION-052, and read on 2026-10-14 in
NOTE-tmp7o2ar. It supplies the singular-function result the question asked
for, for a pairing between two spaces. THEORY-tmp1iy33 is filed from it as
a partial answer. The directed case on a single space, a non-reversible
kernel with A = X, is not worked in the thesis and stays open. It bears
on THEORY-009: here self-adjointness comes from composing the operator
with its adjoint, with no Riesz. It is a nearer miss for that account's
promote_when than LIT-228, but it does not analyse a BYOL-type objective.
It also bears on QUESTION-060, where it treats architecture as an unworked
part of the context. It is also held in the Anthology of the SOTA, as
ANTH-LIT-459. Holding it in both follows ADR-013: the anthology reads it
as a theory of what pretraining objectives learn, and this record reads it
for QUESTION-052. The training recipes belong there.
