# LIT-tmp8dmxy: The effect of brief mindfulness training on momentary impulsivity

Mark R. Dixon, Dana Paliliunas, Jordan Belisle, Ryan C. Speelman, Karl F. Gunnarsson and Jordan L. Shaffer (2019), "The effect of brief mindfulness training on momentary impulsivity", *Journal of Contextual Behavioral Science* 11: 15–20 — [doi:10.1016/j.jcbs.2018.11.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcbs.2018.11.003)

## Key takeaways

*From the abstract as summarised on the institutional repository's record page; the paper is unread.*

- **Design.** Twenty-three participants were randomised to a 5-minute mindful-breathing video (n = 11) or a contemporary music video of similar length (n = 12). A monetary delay-discounting questionnaire was given before and after.
- **Result.** Impulsive responding decreased in the mindfulness group, and the control group showed no significant change.
- **Why the record wants it.** If mindfulness changes the discount curve itself, it re-synchronizes the interests' valuations, which is the flow route rather than the suspension route [CLAIM-tmp7lzto](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7lzto.md) proposes. This is evidence for the rival reading, at n = 23, and is filed as such.
