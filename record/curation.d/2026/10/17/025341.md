After [#158](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/158) merged, the owner asked for the gaps it left to be filled
where copies could be had. The three unavailable works are Granger
(1969), Geweke (1982) and Kaiser and Schreiber (2002), and they stay
unavailable. The other two gaps were open. Griffith's view, which Harder,
Salge and Polani argue against, was not held. And no held measure gave ΦID
a redundancy function with the identity property for more than two
sources. The assistant chose four works with open arXiv copies, and two
parallel readers read two each.

## What entered

- **Griffith and Koch (2014)**, [LIT-tmpx01yr](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpx01yr.md), arXiv v6 read in full, with v1
  and v2 read for the passage v6 withdraws; [NOTE-tmp71sln](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp71sln.md), [THEORY-tmpims74](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpims74.md).
  Table 1 reproduces. Harder, Salge and Polani quote v1 and v2 accurately,
  and v6 concedes their point.
- **Griffith, Chong, James, Ellison and Crutchfield (2014)**, [LIT-tmpk8n6l](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpk8n6l.md),
  [NOTE-tmpm0azr](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpm0azr.md), [THEORY-tmp7uuzn](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp7uuzn.md). I_∧ is defined for any n and is nonnegative,
  but it fails identity and gives negative atoms already for two sources.
- **Rauh, Bertschinger, Olbrich and Jost (2014)**, [LIT-tmpeurmb](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpeurmb.md), [NOTE-tmp0uphx](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp0uphx.md),
  [THEORY-tmp06heo](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp06heo.md). XORCOPY's arithmetic was checked. The reader noted that the
  proof uses two premises not listed as axioms.
- **Ince (2017)**, [LIT-tmpeucmj](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpeucmj.md), §§1–6 in full and §§7–8 at lower resolution;
  [NOTE-tmprcqzl](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmprcqzl.md), [THEORY-tmp65fz9](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp65fz9.md), Proposed. The named examples reproduce.

## The gap's verdict

It cannot close as stated. On the Williams–Beer lattice, which ΦID
inherits, no redundancy function has both identity and nonnegative atoms
from three sources up. The record files this as [CLAIM-tmpqjpx1](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqjpx1.md): a ΦID
verdict on three or more parts must name the axiom it gives up. Two-part
analyses are unaffected. [CLAIM-335](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-335.md)'s examples now have the same values
under four measures.

## What remains

- A multivariate decomposition off the Williams–Beer lattice, which Rauh
  et al. leave open. No held work proposes one.
- Granger (1969), Geweke (1982) and Kaiser and Schreiber (2002), until
  copies are supplied.

## Boundary

None of the four is held in the anthology. A grep of its `record/`
(d1573d2) for the authors and identifiers found nothing. None carries an
instruction for machine-learning practice.
