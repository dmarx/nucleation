# QUESTION-tmp5jhwd: What would a platform for governing an institution's alignment and ongoing development have to do, on the record's account, if it were designed without Luria as a model?

## Why it is a question

The owner's turn, U8 of the exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-26) (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c):

> let's pretend luria didn't already exist and we were trying to come up
> with a platform for governing institutional alignment and ongoing
> development. according to nucleation's account, what should our
> requirements be?

It came after A13 had read Luria as a response to the misalignment the
exchange discussed (CLAIM-tmpoh8gm). Reading a tool against a theory
built partly from that tool's own history risks the circularity the
manuscript from-obstruction-to-reorganization admits ("The framework was
built from the case"). Setting the tool aside and asking what the account
requires turns the comparison round: the requirements come from the
account, and Luria can then be measured against them.

## What would count as an answer

A list of requirements each traced to a claim the record holds, with a
test that would show a platform meeting it or not; and, against that
list, which requirements Luria meets, which it meets only for
representations, and which it does not address. A15's answer is
CLAIM-tmpygs06; its estimate is that Luria would be the first of four
stages.
