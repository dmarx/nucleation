# LIT-tmp120sh: Performing the Racial Scale: From Colonial Saint-Domingue to Contemporary Hollywood

Julia Prest (2017). Performing the Racial Scale: From Colonial Saint-Domingue to Contemporary Hollywood. *Insights* (Institute of Advanced Study, Durham University) 10, article 7. ISSN 1756-2074. Handle 10023/12607.

## Key takeaways

The theatre upheld the racial scale: free people of colour in segregated boxes (second-tier in Port-au-Prince, upper back boxes in Le Cap), black roles played by whites in blackface, actors of colour only in the 1780s; Minette, a free woman of colour, rose "significant, though ultimately limited" (NOTE-tmpe0dw6).
