# THEORY-tmp9gllh: Bumpus and colleagues define time-varying data as narratives, sheaves or cosheaves on posets of time intervals valued in any suitable category, relate the persistent and cumulative views by an adjunction that can lose information, and show that a sequence of snapshots does not determine a narrative without the relations between successive times

## Source

Bumpus, Fairbanks, Karvonen, Leal and Simard (2026; arXiv 2024),
[LIT-2238](../literature.d/LIT-2238.md), skimmed in [NOTE-tmpgeiy5](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpgeiy5.md).

## What was actually shown

Definitions; a proposition on what determines a discrete narrative; an
adjunction proved for value categories with limits and colimits;
temporal-graph examples.

## What this does not say

Which narratives are records of some dynamical system, or how to tell.
