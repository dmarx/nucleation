# LIT-tmpbgf6y: The absence of finite-temperature phase transitions in low-dimensional many-body models: a survey and new results

Gelfert & Nolting (2001). Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 13,
R505–R524. arXiv cond-mat/0106090. DOI 10.1088/0953-8984/13/27/201.

## Key takeaways

- Mermin and Wagner's procedure, outlined step by step (§3): with
  Bogoliubov's inequality the 2D bound gives
  M² ≤ ξ₄β / ln(1 + ξ₃k₀²/|B₀M|), and "As B0 → 0, the denominator
  diverges logarithmically, thus forcing the magnetization to vanish",
  for ferro- and antiferromagnetic order alike.
- The scheme "ultimately rests on the divergence of the integral"
  ∫d^Dk/k² in one or two dimensions (§3.2).
- On fractal and disordered lattices "whose spectral dimension is less or
  equal to 2", Cassi shows that "at finite temperature no spontaneous
  magnetization can exist for the classical O(n) and the ferromagnetic
  (quantum) Heisenberg model" (§3.4).
