# THEORY-tmpnu25d: Ni and colleagues show that, in DNA origami cross-tile self-replication, binding a monomer tile to a template raises the effective concentration of its tethered sticky ends from about 1 nM to about 0.8 mM and shifts their melting temperature by about 50 °C, that a two-state model with three entropy terms, fitted once, predicts the melting temperatures of 1- to 4-tile assemblies to about 2 °C, and that with externally supplied tiles, a thermal cycle and UV crosslinking the per-cycle replication rate falls from about 1.9 for dimers to 1.6 for tetramers

## Source

Ni, Zhou, Zhu, Bershadsky, Ai, Wang, Sha, Seeman and Chaikin (2026),
[LIT-tmpairr7](../literature.d/LIT-tmpairr7.md); read in [NOTE-tmp79i4g](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp79i4g.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Effective concentration** (Fig. 3): about 1 nM unbound against about
  0.8 mM bound, "around a million fold increase resulting in a shift in
  melting temperature of ~50°C"; FRET fit about 0.2 mM.
- **Model** (Eqs. 4-8, 12): tether, rotational and flexibility entropies,
  fitted once by FRET and one gel set, then predicting the melting of
  other 1- to 4-tile assemblies with 7, 8 and 9 bp ends; the abstract's
  "predicts melting temperatures to ~2°C where there is a 50°C shift
  arising from cooperativity".
- **Replication** (Fig. 1D): "about 1.9 replication rate for dimers
  reducing to 1.6 for tetramers", with horizontal linking (h ≈ 0.8) the
  loss.
- **Could have come out otherwise**: the fitted entropies could have
  failed to predict the other assemblies, or the template could have
  failed to raise the horizontal ends' melting into the working window.

## What this does not say

- That anything in the system maintains its own replication: tiles, the
  thermal program, UV and sample splitting are all supplied.
- That large patterns can be replicated: the upper limit on Tm(N) is
  extrapolation, tetramers barely separated, and the Discussion rules out
  an infinite 1D chain.
- That replication is regeneration: no member is restored after turnover;
  copies are multiplied from fresh parts.
- Anything about kinetics, hysteresis or how long daughters persist: none
  was measured.
