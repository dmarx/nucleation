The owner asked the assistant to "merge 404 then proceed systematically
filling all of those identified gaps". The gaps were the ones the
assistant listed after the supplied-maintenance filing. This branch takes
the models among them. The readings go on branches of their own
([ADR-071](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-071.md)).

- **Built.** [CLAIM-605](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-605.md), with scratch/formal/capability_trap_toy.py.
  - Threshold learning gives full substitution a deadline, 3.10 time
    units in the example.
  - A taper of substitutive support longer than 9.13 periods traps a
    party that an abrupt cut would have saved.
  - Capacity-first support needs no taper and cannot ignite.

  The assistant had guessed 6.8 periods for the longest safe taper.
  Bisection gave 9.13, and the script asserts the bisected value.
- **Reconciled.** Azam et al.'s optimal up-front cut, for substitutive
  aid, and the Kenya exit study's success with gradual exits, in which
  participants already did the work.
- **Not built, and why.**
  - Coproduction with a standing input. Output that needs both parties'
    inputs collapses when either is withdrawn by definition. A model
    would restate what [CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) already holds from Evans and Watson.
  - Attention as a capacity, distinct from the buffer as a reserve.
    Modelling it needs a decision process the record has not read, and
    is left open.
- **A dated note** goes on [CLAIM-602](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), whose limit this models.
