## What did not enter, and why

- **The nested HDP ([LIT-002](../../record/literature.d/LIT-002.md), [NOTE-023](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-023.md)).** A model construction and an
  inference algorithm. The only comparative finding, multiple paths
  beating a single path, rests on three corpora of at most 5,000
  abstracts, with Gibbs nCRP winning on one, one run per fold and the
  error bars undefined. At scale no nCRP baseline was run, and the flat
  baselines differ in capacity and initialisation. The printed global
  updates also contradict the derivation. Nothing could be stated as true
  that the reading supports.
- **Hierarchical Pitman–Yor topic models ([LIT-013](../../record/literature.d/LIT-013.md), [NOTE-008](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-008.md)).** A tutorial
  on an existing sampler. Its central result, the modular collapsed
  posterior, is cited from Chen et al. (2011), not proved here, and the
  TNTM results rest on one hashtag-queried corpus with undefined error
  bars. Its one clean data point (power-law discounts barely change
  perplexity) is a single ablation row.

So 23 of 25 readings became sources, which fits the owner's hypothesis
for these topics. The caveat is the same as in the first round: these
are mostly mathematical papers, whose theorems are findings by
construction. Of the eighteen, twelve rest on proofs, and the
empirical ones are scoped in their titles to the setting tested.

## Choices made

- **Declined THEORYs filed anyway.** Four NOTEs declined a THEORY, and two ([NOTE-589](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-589.md), [NOTE-591](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-591.md)) named a
  candidate without filing it. Both are now filed, [NOTE-589](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-589.md)'s
  interpretive half as a "does not say" line. For
  [LIT-577](../../record/literature.d/LIT-577.md) ("a construction plus illustrations") and [LIT-797](../../record/literature.d/LIT-797.md) ("the record
  would be stating the model's consequence") the THEORY is `Proposed`,
  with the condition in the title, and the summary says what the NOTE
  said. For [LIT-839](../../record/literature.d/LIT-839.md) (the anthology holds the metrics account) and [LIT-868](../../record/literature.d/LIT-868.md)
  (exact but narrow) the declined reason was not "no finding", so the
  proved result is filed `Active` and the summary says why. [LIT-836](../../record/literature.d/LIT-836.md)'s
  "No new THEORY" meant no existing one was changed.
- **Grouping.** The five cohomology readings became two THEORYs, split at
  the one result that changes the object (the iterated joint model).
  [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md)'s "does not say" already carried this lineage in prose;
  it is now an account of its own, and [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md)'s line stays. The two
  language studies share one THEORY, since each alone is a single
  unsecured or stipulated result.
- **No `rivals`.** The ICL account and the implicit-gradient account
  ([THEORY-169](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-169.md)) are at different levels and can both be true. The
  transitivity argument targets the random-variable reading, not the
  sheaf criterion of [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md). The language THEORY bears on
  [THEORY-013](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-013.md) as evidence, not as an extension of its mechanism, so no
  `extends` was declared.
- **Not filed from [LIT-281](../../record/literature.d/LIT-281.md).** Chapters V–VII of the thesis (valuation
  algebras, AvN stabiliser groups, strong non-locality of three-qubit
  states) were not checked by the reading, and Chapter V is close to a
  restatement of the definitions.
- **Shannon's title.** It reports that Shannon rests entropy's
  justification on the coding theorems, rather than asserting it as the
  record's own view.
