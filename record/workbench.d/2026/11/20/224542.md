**What the owner asked.** "File the ten principles, the predicted cycle and the census findings as entries in the record in an independent PR." All three come from the owner's manuscript on the record, *From Obstruction to Reorganization*, kept in `scratch/manuscripts/` on its own branch. Its §7.1 states ten principles (P1–P10) of a general theory of learning and inquiry, each joining entries the record holds. Its §7.2 states a predicted eight-stage cycle. Its Part III reports a census of the record's decisions.

**The census.** Filed as CASE-tmpnkuie (executed study, documented), with its data and tested scripts in `scratch/formal/decision-census/`. Two of its findings were not held, so they got codes:

- CLAIM-tmpmtv1b (Proposed, empirical): noticing moved from filing agents to review procedures, while revisions stayed mostly with the owner.
- CLAIM-tmpj5aiv (Proposed, empirical): grammar changes are carried through entries where entries move, not where only a meaning changes.

A third finding, that misfits are absorbed before they are noticed (63 of 71), is CLAIM-181's own content, so it went in as a note there (ADR-072). CLAIM-419 took a note with the census's count of amendments.

**What was found along the way.**

- **Every principle had dropped a scope condition** that its components state. The pattern runs from CLAIM-402's "or when the effect of that use is modelled" to CLAIM-577's "unless the budget is raised from outside". Generalizing across lines tends to shed exactly the conditions that make each result true. The accounts restore them.
- **The accounts are thematic, not scoped to a line.** ADR-083's one account per scope assumes the scope is a line or a part of one, and these cut across several lines. Whether thematic accounts are a kind the record wants is a question for the owner.
- **The owner raised a related worry, for later:** that ADR-075's language may discourage documenting useful syntheses the record has already constructed. It bears on a goal the owner named for the future, a manuscript manifested as a view over the record. This session found the friction concretely. Ten integrations the owner wanted on the record had no place to go except as claims they could not be, or as accounts of a scope ADR-083 does not name. No decision is proposed here.
- **The manuscript needs correcting** where the audit found errors: P2, P5, P9, P10 and the cycle as a sequence. That is done on its own pull request, not here.
