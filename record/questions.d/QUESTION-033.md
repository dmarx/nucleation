# QUESTION-033: In a system described at more than one level, does "who drives?" have an answer at a given level, and what kinds of answer are there?

## Why it is a question

[CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md) left "who drives?" as the information a system's
architecture carries once membership is settled. Within one level the
answer was simple: one member drives (a hierarchy), the members drive each
other (peers), or the drive is diffuse. Across levels it is not simple. A
higher level can constrain its parts, the parts can produce the higher
level, both can happen, or the dynamics can live at the higher level with no
part in charge.

The owner's framing ([CLAIM-158](../claims.d/CLAIM-158.md), [CLAIM-316](../claims.d/CLAIM-316.md)): "who drives" may or
may not have an answer with respect to a particular level of description.
The kinds form a spectrum, and the answer is not constant.

## What would count as an answer

A way to apportion drive between levels of a described system, for a stated
macro-variable and target, that can come out differently for different
systems and can be wrong. The record holds the materials for one:
- **Information decomposition** ([LIT-025](../literature.d/LIT-025.md)) separates downward causation from
  causal decoupling.
- **Proportional causation** ([THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md)) makes a higher level the cause
  exactly when the effect does not depend on how that level is realized.
- **Interventions** ([THEORY-138](../theory.d/THEORY-138.md)) say what any of these claims asserts.

An answer must also say how the apportionment changes over time. The
proposed vocabulary is [TERM-051](../terms.d/TERM-051.md).

A demonstration that higher levels "matter" in some system is not an answer.
Nor is the bare assertion that every level contributes.

## Note of 2026-11-12: a driver whose ends differ from the driven, from "Medical Ethics and Media Enshitification"

The exchange (2026-11-11) gave this question three new kinds of driver. A
parasite whose adaptations steer the host's behaviour through the host's own
machinery ([CASE-tmp10ykm](../cases.d/CASE-tmp10ykm.md), [CLAIM-tmpoxhqq](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpoxhqq.md)). The enteric level between
microbes and person (note on [CASE-047](../cases.d/CASE-047.md)). And a firm's design procedure, where
A31 §10 answers "who drives" twice: the operative driver is "the firm's
optimization procedure, while the distribution of players' marginal economic
responses influences what that procedure selects" ([CLAIM-tmpmz271](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmz271.md)). A21 §4
adds the caution that "observing where causal drive sits is not equivalent
to identifying which organism's interests a regulatory change serves".
