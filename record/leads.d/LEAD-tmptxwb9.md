# LEAD-tmptxwb9: Test dependency against division of labour by the capability trajectory under withdrawal, in a microbial co-culture whose supplied metabolite can be removed

## What it would do

Read the co-culture literature, then run CLAIM-603's three supply arms
(one-way, reciprocal, and a third) and withdraw the supply.

Where the record names it:

- CLAIM-603: "Moderate: microbial co-cultures in which a supplied
  metabolite can be removed are the cheapest system, and the record has read
  none of that literature"
- The curation entry of 2026-11-13 (072318): "the capability trajectory
  under withdrawal tells dependency from division of labour"

## What would count

supports CLAIM-603.
