# QUESTION-tmpkd7a1: Does an experiment on a 2D DNA origami, resolving domains or staples during annealing, show that most staples bind fully soon after their first domain binds and that assembly proceeds without a measurable nucleation barrier?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1437](../theory.d/THEORY-1437.md) reports a review of simulations ([NOTE-1626](../notes.d/NOTE-1626.md)). The authors themselves cite evidence that full binding fails in a larger 3D-lattice-based design. They also report that the experimental evidence on origami hysteresis is mixed.

## What would answer it

The condition as it stood on [THEORY-1437](../theory.d/THEORY-1437.md):

> An experiment on a 2D DNA origami, resolving domains or staples during annealing (FRET, or oligomer inclusion), shows that most staples bind fully soon after their first domain binds, and that assembly proceeds without a measurable nucleation barrier.
