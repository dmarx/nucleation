# CLAIM-614: A self-maintaining organization that harms, and also reproduces the representational projections that make its harm non-legible, commits a counter-normativity distinct from the harm and attributable to it: among what it maintains are the conditions under which the harm cannot be recognized

## The claim

The owner, at U27 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and
Disposition", second share
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c; the first
share is https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e):
"help me characterize the precise nature of counter-normativity being
committed by a self-maintaining system that not only harms but reproduces
the representational projections that make the harm non-legible."

The turn asks a question, and the question presupposes a thesis. Its parts:

1. **The bearer** is a self-maintaining organization. The owner's "system"
   is, in the record's framework, a kind of organization, as an
   institution is. Self-maintaining is meant in the record's sense: its
   maintenance regenerates its own constraints ([TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md), [CLAIM-516](CLAIM-516.md),
   [CLAIM-566](CLAIM-566.md)).
2. **Two acts.** It harms, and it reproduces the representational
   projections that make the harm non-legible. The projections are the
   record's coarse-grainings ([CLAIM-348](CLAIM-348.md), [CLAIM-350](CLAIM-350.md), [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md)), and
   legibility is [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s, which is directed: legible to whom. Because
   the reproduction is part of the organization's self-maintenance, what
   it keeps going includes the conditions of its own blindness.
3. **The thesis.** In doing both, the organization commits a
   counter-normativity: a wrong of its own, not the same as the harm, and
   attributable to the organization ("being committed by").

A58 §6, the reply to the owner's U26, had called an institution "coherently
blind" through selective exclusion. U27 is the owner's step from that
blindness to a wrong committed by maintaining it.

**Correction (the owner's, 2026-11-07).** The reader's report set the
owner's "system" against "an organization or an institution", as if the
bearer were wider. The owner rejects that contrast: "I'm reasonably confident that within our framework,
a 'system' is just a kind of 'organization'". The claim is stated for
organizations accordingly.

## What is already held

The parts are on the record; the wrong is not. That a coarse-graining
adequate for an organization's performance can merge states its members'
claims must distinguish, and need not be a false representation, is
[CLAIM-348](CLAIM-348.md), with [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md) as its case. That a regime can shift the evidence
used to judge it and the authority of those who judge it, without bad
faith, is [CLAIM-445](CLAIM-445.md) and [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md)'s protective lock-in. That legibility,
volitional efficacy and correctability are separate achievements is
[CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md). What the owner adds is the normative step: reproducing the
blindness is itself a wrong. [CLAIM-620](CLAIM-620.md)'s last clause, that the
activity can remake the concepts and organizations that made it possible,
is the descriptive base it complements.

## A qualification from A's review

This section is A's, not the owner's, and it leaves the owner's thesis as
worded. A's review of the pull request dmarx/nucleation#422 (item 7) asks
that the counter-normativity claim say explicitly: "The organization can
maintain the blindness without maintaining the ignorance of every
participant." Its proposed statement:

> The failure is indexed to the organization's operative legibility and governance regime, not necessarily to the knowledge of its participants. A violation may be understood by affected people and dissenting members while remaining unavailable or ineffective within the procedures governing organizational action.

So the harm that "cannot be recognized" in the owner's title is
unrecognizable in the organization's operative representation and
procedures, which is [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s directed legibility: legible to whom. Some
participants "may correctly recognize the harm", and "The organization's
pathology can persist because these perspectives are denied the
conditions needed to become practically effective." The record holds the
parts. [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md) separates a contribution's admission, recognition,
credibility, uptake and part in revising the standards, each possible
without the later ones; [CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md) separates legibility from volitional
efficacy and correctability. A: "This keeps us from mistaking organized
exclusion of knowledge for universal ignorance", and it "creates a
concrete empirical distinction between representational incapacity and
selective practical authority". The wording is A's; adopting it is the
owner's call.

## What it does not say

- What the wrong is. That is the question the owner asked
  ([QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md)). A's answer, a second-order wrong of answerability,
  is [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md); A's counter-corrective disposition is [CLAIM-619](CLAIM-619.md).
- Which standard fixes what counts as harm. That is [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md), and
  [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) says the record has not established which standards are
  admissible.
- That the organization is an agent, or that each member is culpable
  ([CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md)).
