summary: >-
  Leitgeb and Carus (2020), Rudolf Carnap. The entry presents Carnap's
  philosophy as "conceptual engineering": philosophical problems are largely
  artifacts of inherited language, and the philosopher's task is to design
  better concepts (explication) and whole language frameworks, choosing
  among them for purposes rather than for truth. Frameworks fix logical
  (and, later, inductive) consequence; truth and existence questions are
  internal to a framework, while choosing a framework is a practical
  question. From 1932 Carnap was a pluralist ("principle of tolerance").
  Explication replaces an inexact concept (explicandum) with a more exact
  one (explicatum) judged by four desiderata held as matters of degree:
  similarity to the explicandum, exactness, fruitfulness, simplicity;
  explication is iterative and allows several explicata of one concept. Read
  for the exchange "luria experiment design".
