Part of the THEORY backfill the owner asked for on 2026-10-16: for each
LIT with a `Read` NOTE that no THEORY cites as `source`, decide whether the
reading supports an account, and file the ones it does. This group held
eleven such LITs: eight in social-ontology, one each in game-theory,
religion and design-rationale. Every NOTE was `Read`; none was a skim.

## What entered

Nine THEORYs, one `Active` and eight `Proposed`. Codes are the temporary
ones `luria new` assigned.

- **Groups.** Ritchie 2013 ([THEORY-549](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-549.md); sources [LIT-479](../../record/literature.d/LIT-479.md), [LIT-486](../../record/literature.d/LIT-486.md)): a
  structured group is not its members, since coincident groups of one kind
  are two; plurality, fusion, aggregate and set theories cannot allow it.
  Ritchie 2018 ([THEORY-575](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-575.md); [LIT-486](../../record/literature.d/LIT-486.md), [LIT-479](../../record/literature.d/LIT-479.md)): organized groups are
  structured wholes, feature groups are social kinds. It `extends` the
  first.
- **Social kinds.** Haslanger ([THEORY-667](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-667.md); [LIT-480](../../record/literature.d/LIT-480.md)): gender and race as
  hierarchical social positions, filed with its ameliorative status in the
  title and its descriptive claim as what `promote_when` can test.
- **Institutions, as rivals.** Searle's status functions ([THEORY-625](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-625.md);
  [LIT-482](../../record/literature.d/LIT-482.md)) against Hindriks & Guala's rules in equilibrium
  ([THEORY-414](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-414.md); [LIT-481](../../record/literature.d/LIT-481.md)). They disagree about what a constitutive rule
  adds and whether institutions need one, and neither has settled it, so
  the edge is `rivals`, not `corrects`.
- **The social fact.** Durkheim ([THEORY-648](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-648.md); [LIT-484](../../record/literature.d/LIT-484.md)): exteriority and
  constraint, general because collective.
- **Grounding and anchoring.** Epstein ([THEORY-487](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-487.md); [LIT-487](../../record/literature.d/LIT-487.md)), from a
  survey and the author's précis, filed as `Proposed` because the
  distinction's sharpness is disputed and the survey gives the dissents a
  sentence each.
- **Non-redundant group attitudes.** List & Pettit's replies
  ([THEORY-536](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-536.md); [LIT-730](../../record/literature.d/LIT-730.md)), with `answers: [QUESTION-009]` as a partial
  answer of one kind.
- **Bayesian persuasion.** Kamenica & Gentzkow ([THEORY-404](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-404.md); [LIT-1021](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1021.md)),
  `Active`: proved results, stated with their conditions (common prior,
  commitment, one ideal receiver, sender-preferred tie-breaking).

## What did not enter, and why

- **De Cruz, Religion and Science ([LIT-194](../../record/literature.d/LIT-194.md), [NOTE-107](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-107.md)).** A survey with no
  thesis of its own. Its characterisations of scholarly consensus are the
  author's, with no systematic evidence (C2, C5); its one argued point,
  that Barbour's categories are neither exhaustive nor exclusive, is an
  informal argument from one counterexample that offers no replacement.
  Its reading says there is no THEORY here to support or contradict.
- **Kunz & Rittel, IBIS ([LIT-758](../../record/literature.d/LIT-758.md), [NOTE-811](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-811.md)).** A seven-page design
  specification. Its three general claims are asserted (all `weak` in the
  NOTE), and its reading already declined to file a THEORY. It names Rittel
  and Webber's wicked problems as the place to file C1 from, if read.

So nine of eleven readings became sources, and every social-ontology
reading did. That fits the owner's hypothesis here, with a caveat: only
one of the nine is `Active`. Most of these works are definitions,
frameworks or conceptual analyses that their readings mark as argued by
example, so the accounts they support are proposals. The two that entered
nothing are a survey and a specification.

## Choices made

- **Status.** Only the proved result is `Active`. Each social-ontology
  account went `Proposed`, and each `promote_when` names an argument or
  case of a kind the source did not supply, and says why more of the
  source's own kind of example cannot settle it.
- **Haslanger as a claim.** The paper is stipulative by design. The title
  states the account with its condition ("defined as a feminist and
  antiracist theory needs them"), and `promote_when` points at the
  descriptive claim the paper ties its definitions to, since the
  ameliorative half cannot be settled by evidence.
- **`answers` edges.** One only: [THEORY-536](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-536.md) on [QUESTION-009](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-009.md). Searle's
  account was not linked to [QUESTION-007](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-007.md): the desire-independence that would
  make it a grounding of normativity is asserted and disputed by its rival.
  Bayesian persuasion was not linked to [QUESTION-070](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-070.md) or [QUESTION-071](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): the
  paper does not call it manipulation, and its case is visible influence on
  belief, not invisible influence on a will. Both are in Connections.
- **No sources added to existing THEORYs.** [LIT-730](../../record/literature.d/LIT-730.md) bears on [THEORY-127](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-127.md) and
  [THEORY-139](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-139.md), as its reading says, and Ritchie and Epstein are used in
  [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md)'s body; none is load-bearing for those accounts, and this task
  edits no existing THEORY.
- **Codes from parallel work not cited.** The moral-psychology batch's
  Tomasello account (joint commitment and obligation) is the nearest
  THEORY to Searle and Durkheim, but it is not on this branch; [LIT-125](../../record/literature.d/LIT-125.md) is
  cited instead.
