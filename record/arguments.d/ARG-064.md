# ARG-064: A self-maintaining protective regime, whose interventions shift the evidence and authority that judge it, carries over to a harm-producing organization once a normative predicate replaces the evidence of continued need: the reproductive mechanism transfers and the verdict does not

From the second share of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c).

## The inference

1. [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md): a protective regime becomes self-maintaining when the interventions it authorizes shift the evidence used to judge its necessity and the authority and resources of those who judge it, with no bad faith needed ("no fabrication of threat required").
2. A60 §3 and A62 §3B give the same form for a harm-producing organization: the regime (r, g, m) is moved by its own projections and actions, (r, g, m)′ = M(r, g, m, h_r(w), u).
3. Replace [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md)'s evidence of continued need with a normative predicate N, whose violations the regime keeps from registering. Then the organization reproduces the conditions that keep N from constraining it, which is [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s layer 2.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is it the respect the conclusion needs?** In both, a regime's own outputs move the evidence and the authority by which it is judged, and lock-in needs no intent. That is the reproductive layer of the conclusion.
- **What relevant difference is there?** [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md)'s failure is measured against evidence about a threat, which could in principle settle whether the regime is still needed. [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s is measured against a justified standard N, and which standards are admissible is the half of [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) the record has not established ([QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md)).
- **What transfers, and what does not?** The mechanism transfers: [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s layer 2 is held, and its claim to novelty there is corrected. The verdict does not: that the reproduction is a wrong, and not only a failure of responsiveness, needs the duty and condition 5, which [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) does not supply.
