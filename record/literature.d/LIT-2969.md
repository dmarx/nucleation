# LIT-2969: Ordering, metastability and phase transitions in two-dimensional systems

Kosterlitz & Thouless (1973). Journal of Physics C: Solid State Physics
6, 1181–1203. DOI 10.1088/0022-3719/6/7/010.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws"
(2026-11-20 to 21), where A names it at A12, A27 for the BKT transition,
"topological order through vortex unbinding", and as criticality and
order in two dimensions "that evades the Mermin-Wagner theorem". Not
read: Unfetched since 2026-11-22 (the IOP bronze PDF behind a Cloudflare
challenge).

"Evades" is loose: by both neighbours there is still no long-range order
below the transition; what the work adds is a different kind of order
compatible with the theorem. "Topological order" here is the lecture's
1970s sense, winding-number sectors kept apart by barriers ∝ ln L, not
the later quantum sense.

Two neighbours stand in for it ([ADR-114](../decisions.d/ADR-114.md)), and how far they reach matters:
