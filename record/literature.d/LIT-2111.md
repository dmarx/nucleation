# LIT-2111: A Geometrically Convergent Solution to Spatial Hypercube Queueing Models

Hua, C., Luo, J., Swersey, A. J. & Wen, Y. (2026). A Geometrically Convergent Solution to Spatial Hypercube Queueing Models. arXiv:2603.03019.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1618](../notes.d/NOTE-1618.md): §1, the model statement of §2, the
TOUR paragraph of §3 and the comparison of §5.*

- **The extension.** Exact solution with heterogeneous service rates,
  converging at a geometric rate (abstract; proofs not read).
- **Speed.** "over 1,000 times faster than the sparse solver and more than
  500 times faster than discrete-event simulation" (abstract), and against
  a reimplementation of Larson's method, "In the case of 20 units, our
  algorithm is approximately 83% faster in terms of total time" (§5).
- **On Larson (1974).** "The state-of-the-art solution method for solving
  the homogeneous service rate problem is the alternating hyperplane
  approach developed by Larson (1974)" (§1).
