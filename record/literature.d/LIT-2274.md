summary: >-
  Tempich, Pinto, Sure and Staab (2005), An Argumentation Ontology for
  DIstributed, Loosely-controlled and evolvInG Engineering processes of
  oNTologies (DILIGENT). DILIGENT is a process for building a shared
  ontology among distributed, loosely controlled participants: a small team
  builds an initial ontology, users adapt it locally but may not change the
  shared one, a control board collects change requests with their arguments,
  analyses local adaptations, decides which changes enter the next version,
  and users then update their local ontologies. The paper presents an OWL DL
  ontology, adapted from IBIS, for recording the arguments exchanged in that
  process: Issues (what should be in the conceptual model), Ideas (how to
  formalize it, linked to concrete ontology change operations), Arguments
  (Justifications and Challenges, with Examples, Evaluation&Justification,
  Counter Examples, Alternative&Contrast), Positions (agree/disagree), and
  Decisions (status under discussion, postponed, discarded, agreed), with
  Actors that are human or machine (ontology-learning algorithms arguing by
  measures such as TFIDF). A discussion from an 11-person, 90-minute
  experiment is modelled after the fact. Read for the exchange "luria
