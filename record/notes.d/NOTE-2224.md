# NOTE-2224: Lukierska-Walasek and Topolski — Random walk on p-adics in glassy systems

## Key results

1. **The model** (§III): Ogielski and Stein "consider a regular Cayley tree with M levels and fixed branching ratio p", distance the height of the closest common ancestor.
2. **Same equation**: for p = 2 its eq. (31) "has the same form as corresponding equation (6) from" Ogielski and Stein.
3. **Power law** (eq. 33): barriers growing linearly with distance give "the temperature dependent power law decay", P_t ∼ t^(−T ln 2/Δ), with a_k the total intensity of jumps to distance k.
4. **Kohlrausch** (eq. 34): barriers growing as Δ ln k give the stretched exponential, "the Kohlrausch law".

## Bearing on the record

Stands in, with Bachas and Huberman ([NOTE-2218](NOTE-2218.md)), for [LIT-2954](../literature.d/LIT-2954.md). Its exponent is in total-rate form, ln 2 over the log of the ratio of total escape rates, which agrees with Bachas and Huberman's per-pair form once the branching is counted.

## Limitations

A short paper; the identification with Ogielski and Stein's eq. (6) is the authors' statement, not checked against the original.
