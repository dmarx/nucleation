# LEAD-tmp3x6rg: Find a documented case of a contextual self: three audiences meeting pairwise

## What it would do

Search the context-collapse and audience-segregation literature, and
documented public cases, for a person whose three audiences met pairwise,
with each pair's presentations compatible and incoherence or sanction
nonetheless. For each candidate, record:

- the three contexts and their pairwise meeting occasions;
- what each occasion required;
- whether any single presentation could have met all three.

A case found becomes a CASE and grounds CLAIM-tmprejdn. A careful search
that finds none bears on its defeater.
