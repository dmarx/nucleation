# LEAD-tmpzraiq: Run the assisted-learning study: learners matched on starting skill, practising under an assistant that does the hard step for them or one that does not

## What it would do

Run [CASE-262](../cases.d/CASE-262.md) for one human skill, with a later unassisted test and a
withdrawal of the assistant.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md): "Whether capacity in a real skill is learned by doing, decays
  with disuse and needs capacity present to grow is the question [CASE-262](../cases.d/CASE-262.md)
  would test."

## What would count

Lower unassisted skill after doing-for assistance supports [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) and
the trap of [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md); equal skill defeats their application to people.
