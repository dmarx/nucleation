# LEAD-tmpffmfr: Model Northstar with control and metacontrol mechanisms alone and then with a will-organization over them, price the difference, and set planning, control and institutional theory beside the account

## What it would do

Model the case both ways and price the difference; read planning theory,
control theory's regulation and institutional theory for what each already
explains.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-056: "Take a case such as Northstar, model it with control and
  metacontrol mechanisms alone, then with a will-organization over them, and
  price the difference as CLAIM-174 does."
- CLAIM-024: "Planning theory and institutional theory have not been set
  beside the account at all."

## What would count

A difference the control-only model cannot produce at matched complexity
supports CLAIM-487's gain; none, or one an existing theory already gives,
defeats CLAIM-024.
