# THEORY-1539: Poverty causes stress and negative affect, and these in turn make people more present-biased and risk-averse independent of credit constraints and background risk, so that behaviour taken as the poor's own preference may partly be an effect of poverty and may help perpetuate it

## Source

Haushofer and Fehr (2014), [LIT-2152](../literature.d/LIT-2152.md); their account is read in [NOTE-1681](../notes.d/NOTE-1681.md)
and reported, Active, in [THEORY-1462](THEORY-1462.md).

## What was actually shown

See [THEORY-1462](THEORY-1462.md): field causal support for the first link, laboratory
inductions only, with small samples and acute stress, for the second,
and no nonlinearity that would make a trap. Nothing beyond that reading
is held here.

## What this does not say

That the thesis has been tested. The condition above names what would
settle it.
