# ARG-248: Achievement partial order → memetic dimensions

## The inference

[CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md): "From interconnection to individuation, the relationships are
distinct achievements that form a partial order, not a nested sequence:
none implies the next". A4 §6 lists nine properties of memetic success
(fidelity, fecundity, longevity, nucleation capacity, generativity,
structural reach, entrenchment, organizational dispensability,
corrigibility), says "These properties should not be treated as a
hierarchy", and cites [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) for it. A4 §10 then lists [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) among
what its hypothesis rests on.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is that the respect the
  conclusion needs?** [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)'s achievements are stages an organization
  may reach (compatibility, dynamics, individuation, autonomy, inquiry),
  ordered by what each requires of the others. A4's properties are
  measures of a transmitted item's effects. That one set is partially
  ordered says nothing about whether the other is: independence of
  properties is shown by cases that separate them, not by analogy to
  another set's order.
- **What relevant difference is there?** [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)'s conclusion is that
  "none implies the next". A4's examples do that work directly ("A highly
  transmissible meme may organize very little. A rarely transmitted meme
  may induce profound organization."), which is [CLAIM-1230](../claims.d/CLAIM-1230.md)'s claim and
  needs data, not [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md).
- **What transfers, and what does not?** Nothing the hypothesis uses.
  [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)'s content, that a transient structure can start an
  organization and that three persistences differ, rests on [CLAIM-463](../claims.d/CLAIM-463.md),
  [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md), [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) and [CLAIM-420](../claims.d/CLAIM-420.md). Its being true would not make
  [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) more or less credible, nor the reverse.

## Verdict

Rejected. [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) is cited accurately for what it says, and does not
bear on either conclusion. It is left out of [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)'s `rests_on`.
