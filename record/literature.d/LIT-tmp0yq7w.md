# LIT-tmp0yq7w: Generalized Stability Theory. Part II: Nonautonomous Operators

Brian F. Farrell and Petros J. Ioannou (1996), *Journal of the Atmospheric Sciences* 53(14), 2041–2053 — `doi:10.1175/1520-0469(1996)053<2041:gstpin>2.0.co;2`

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for.

- For a time-dependent linear operator there are no fixed normal modes, so
  stability must be stated through the propagator over an interval.
- Finite-time growth is given by the propagator's singular values, and
  asymptotic growth by Lyapunov exponents and their vectors.
- The analysis is applied to predictability: how errors grow in a flow
  that changes in time.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-11 at the owner's request, with Part I ([LIT-tmpnkkua](LIT-tmpnkkua.md)). An
argument under development is the nonautonomous case: its structure
changes as it is worked on, so a stability analysis with fixed modes would
not fit it even in principle. A reading should say what carries over to an
operator that changes as fast as the state does.
