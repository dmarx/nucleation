# TERM-tmpxwa7g: pseudo-self: a multi-perspectival organization with more than one independently addressable carrier that is causally integrated relative to a declared probe family and history, reproducing some of the integration, persistence and adaptive governance associated with selves, while leaving open whether it has one overarching perspective or one subject of experience

## Definition

The owner's word, U10 of the exchange "Explain First Contact RL"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6b037165-3a1c-83ea-9d7c-311c7807acd3): "a team as
a pseudo-self where the perspectives are necessarily separable"
([CLAIM-tmpm54la](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm54la.md)). A21 §6:

> A multi-perspectival organization that reproduces some of the
> integration, persistence, and adaptive governance associated with
> selves, while leaving open whether it possesses a single subject of
> experience or satisfies a stronger criterion of selfhood.

A22 §5, after the owner asked A to formalize singularity (U11):

PseudoSelf_{Q,H}(S) = Integrated_{Q,H}(S) ∧ |C_person(S)| > 1,

where S = (C, P, G, H) has carriers C, realized organizational perspectives
P (overlapping, possibly crossing carriers), governing relations G and
history H, and integration "includes historically maintained, revisable
coordination of interpretation and action, rather than mere behavioral
correlation".

Integrated_{Q,H} (A22 §4): interventions in the probe family Q reveal
cross-component dependencies that a model treating two subsets of processes
as independently organized (given their declared common inputs) cannot
reproduce, and the dependencies contribute to maintaining coherent
activity. A's diagnostic: P(Y_B | do(q_A)) ≠ P(Y_B | do(q'_A)), beyond
common external causes; "But coupling alone is insufficient: two connected
thermostats can influence one another." It also needs the organization of
interpretation and governance [TERM-072](TERM-072.md)'s organizational sense requires.

## What it does not assume

- One overarching perspective (organizational singularity), or one subject
  of experience (phenomenal singularity), in [TERM-tmpm0s7g](TERM-tmpm0s7g.md)'s senses. "If the
  team develops a distinctive organizational perspective, that is an
  additional achievement—not something the definition assumes."
- That the team's perspectives are separable because its members are
  ([CLAIM-tmp7580l](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7580l.md)).

## Limits

- "Integrated" is schematic: A22 calls it "a proposed operational
  criterion, not a theorem that all three properties have been reduced to
  one metric". The coupling diagnostic is necessary, not sufficient, and
  "contributes to maintaining coherent activity" has no measure.
- The carrier count is relative to a level ("at the declared level of
  description"): a person has one carrier at the level of persons and many
  at the level of neural processes. The term counts persons, so an
  individual is never a pseudo-self under it, though A's argument is that
  an individual's unity may be of the same kind.

## Note of 2026-11-23: what holds it back

Its 'integrated' is schematic, as its Limits say: the coupling diagnostic is
necessary and not sufficient, and 'contributes to maintaining coherent
activity' has no measure, so the definition is not settled; [CLAIM-tmpm54la](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm54la.md),
its one use, has not said which reading it takes.
