# NOTE-2228: Bordelon, Noci, Li, Hanin and Pehlevan — Depthwise hyperparameter transfer

## Contribution

A branch scaling and learning-rate rule giving transfer across depth; a DMFT derivation of a joint infinite-width and infinite-depth feature-learning limit with diffusion from initialization and drift from learning; finite-size error rates.

## Key results

1. **No depth factor in the learning rate** (§2, eq. 3): "after the 1/√L factors have been introduced the correct scaling is simply η = η0γ0²N".
2. **Diffusion from initialization** (App. E.3): "The Gaussian source variables u^k are all uncorrelated across layers", and their sum behaves "as integrated Brownian motion since each layer's contribution is independent and order √dτ ∼ 1/√L".
3. **Coherent learned contributions** (App. E.3): the learned double sum is O(1) because gradients are correlated across all layers; "This fact arises from the skip connections, which generate long-range correlations across layers. This behavior is very different from non-residual networks".
4. **Drift and diffusion** (App. E.4, eq. 62): the feature learning term "adds a drift term to the layerwise dynamics compared to the diffusion term from the initial weights". App. K.5 puts both: the random field "has a scale of 1/√L and will behave as a Brownian motion", the gradient-correlated update has the 1/L scaling.
5. **A family of scalings** (App. M): stability holds "for any α ≥ 1/2 provided the learning rate is scaled as η = η0γ0² L^(2α−1) N"; α = 1 is "the ODE scaling", α = ½ the SDE scaling.
6. **Multi-layer blocks** (App. K.2.1): inner weights act as if static.
7. **Rigour** (§6): "at the level of rigor of physics, rather than formal proof".

## Assumptions and concepts

Width to infinity first, then depth; one weight matrix per block in the main theory; no normalization layers; O(1) training time and data. Concepts: layer time τ = ℓ/L, DMFT, feature-learning rate γ₀, Brownian against drift terms, SDE and ODE scalings.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-767](../claims.d/CLAIM-767.md)** (supports both rows directly; defeater (b) not met). The paper names the random part diffusion and the learned part drift, and the skip connection as the source of the correlation. Qualification written into version 2: α = ½ is the smallest exponent that keeps diffusion of order one, not one the random part needs; with η ∝ L^(2α−1) the learned change per block is 1/L across the family (the reader's arithmetic). The no-skip row is not addressed here.
- **[CLAIM-773](../claims.d/CLAIM-773.md)** (supports by analogy): eq. 62 is the copied-with-error-plus-bias case in continuous depth, with state-dependent drift and non-Gaussian fields.
- **[CLAIM-771](../claims.d/CLAIM-771.md)** (weak support, with caution): the limit treats blocks as a continuum with no built-in rate differences between groups; App. K.2.1's frozen inner layers are a rate difference within each block.
- **Recheck**: [THEORY-187](../theory.d/THEORY-187.md) (Saxe and colleagues: in deep linear networks without skips the step size falls with depth) is a contrast worth noting; Hayou, Li and colleagues' covariance SDE, Noci and colleagues and Hayou and Yang are not held.

## Limitations

Physics-level rigour; width before depth; single-layer blocks for the main result; short training runs; no normalization in the theory; general DMFT equations intractable outside lazy and linear cases.
