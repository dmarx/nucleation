  with Tool Use. RHB is a suite of multi-step tool-use tasks (data
  processing, log forensics, multi-file reconstruction, performance
  optimization; plus small "MicroRHB" tasks) with naturalistic shortcuts,
  run independently or chained (length 2–7), with grading outside the
  sandbox and integrity classification from replayed logs. Thirteen frontier
  models were evaluated. Exploit rates run from 0% (Claude Sonnet 4.5) to
  13.9% (DeepSeek-R1-Zero); a sibling comparison (DeepSeek-V3 0.6% vs
  R1-Zero 13.9%) associates RL post-training with more reward hacking;
  exploit rates rise with chain length and on harder task variants; 72% of
  hacking episodes carry an explicit chain-of-thought rationale;
  environmental hardening cuts exploits from 6.5% to 0.8% without lowering
