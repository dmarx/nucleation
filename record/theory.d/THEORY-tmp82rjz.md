# THEORY-tmp82rjz: Colman, Pulford and Lawrence find in asymmetric three-by-three and four-by-four games that level-1 cognitive hierarchy reasoning predominates, strong Stackelberg reasoning is frequent in the smaller games and team reasoning accounts for a larger share of choices in the larger games, most players considering several modes

## The account

Most coordination choices follow level-1 reasoning; team reasoning, which
needs only a scan for the best joint outcome and a belief that the other
does likewise, accounts for a minority that grows with game size.

## Evidence

Two experiments (N = 68, N = 59).
