**Legacy.** The owner noted on 2026-10-29 that Johnston's *Surviving Death*
opens another angle: notions, and the psychology, of legacy. The owner's
thesis (CLAIM-tmpjylq8) extends the self across people; legacy extends it
across time, past death. QUESTION-tmpruhu4 states the question. Every work
was checked against Crossref or Open Library, and Unpaywall was asked for
an open copy; only Hershfield 2011 has one, in Europe PMC, which refused this
client, so it is Unfetched. The others are Deferred, no further copy yet
sought.

None was found for Haslam et al. 1992. The readings of the psychology were
drafted by four reading agents, each from the full text, and checked
against the sources before filing. The copies came from authors' and
institutions' own pages and from open journals; the owner approved the
Groningen and VU repositories' own download paths. Copies on course pages,
Scribd and third-party sites were not used, and fetches refused by bot
