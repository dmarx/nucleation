  DeVille and Lerman (2013), arXiv 1303.3907. In a coordinate-free
  framework of networks of control systems on manifolds, they prove that a
  graph fibration (Boldi and Vigna's unique lifting of incoming edges,
  equivalently input-tree isomorphism) induces a map of dynamical systems
  in the opposite direction for every modular vector field. Surjective
  fibrations, which include quotients by balanced relations, embed the
  quotient's dynamics as an invariant synchrony polydiagonal. Injective
  fibrations project a network onto the subsystem that drives it, which
  they call an abstraction. Only this direction is proved, and stability
  is not treated. Here because it is the fibration form of the balanced-
