# SYNTHESIS-047: Collectives persist by transmission, not likeness

*A part synthesis of the distributed-agency line: the part on what persists
when a collective replaces its members or a self goes inactive, how the
organization that produces a collective is transmitted, how participants
count successions, and how higher-level perspectives would be identified
from members' activity. The line's synthesis is built over it and the
line's other parts. Realizability's persisting-individuals part
(SYNTHESIS-046) sets out the same claims of identity over time from the
side of an individual's repertoire. CASE-028 carries the line and is
Superseded by CASE-006, which is integrated; it is not integrated again. It
adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

When a collective replaces all its members, or a self goes inactive, what
makes the later organization the earlier one persisting, and not a similar
one produced anew? When several later collectives each continue it in a
different respect, which successor relation, if any, is its persistence
(QUESTION-118)? And how would the organization or perspective that
persists be found in the activity of its members?

## The argument

**Persistence through turnover is transmission, not resemblance.** A
pattern can persist while every constituent is replaced, so the
persistence of a product and of the organization producing it are separate
facts (CLAIM-280, on the r/place case, with A31's cooperative CASE-006 and
the owner's college program CASE-102). What the producer's own persistence
consists in, when its members are all replaced, is causal transmission of
the organization that produces it: reproducing a kind is not persisting as
an individual (CLAIM-329, Active since 2026-11-20). The senses of an
organization's persistence, state, dynamical, historical, viable and
reinstatable, come apart, and a claim should say which (TERM-071, adopted
by the owner); lineage is the historical sense and what CLAIM-329 says
persistence through turnover consists in, separate from provenance, common
ancestry and causal descent (TERM-084). Causal connection alone is not the
criterion: a figure's influence can persist as story, transmitted
perspective or descent, and descent is not inheritance of a perspective
(CLAIM-420, the owner's, corrected by A). A self that goes inactive is the
dormant form: what persists through inactivity is the capacity to reinstate
an organization, not its continuous running, and that continuity can branch
and merge (CLAIM-287), which qualifies the dual-filed thesis that a self
persists by a conserved coherence measure γ (CLAIM-161, Proposed because
everything rests on γ; QUESTION-043 asks which γ for a person). CASE-116's
constructions keep slowness, member regeneration and transmission apart,
and its arm C, a rule passed through an archive, sits under both CLAIM-287
and CLAIM-329.

**Successors can be plural without identity branching.** When bundled
continuities separate, several later collectives can each be a genuine
successor in a different respect without numerical identity branching
(CLAIM-328, answering QUESTION-290, on the Washington Senators of CASE-105).
A party can stay legally continuous while its coalition and orientation
change, the case where the organization was transmitted and its
perspectives only partly (CASE-133). A fork can build a new organization
through which a disposition the parent's governance kept ineffective gains
efficacy (CLAIM-613). A revised jurisdiction continues the earlier willed
agent as a matter of continuation, not of resemblance (CLAIM-544).

**Identity over time is historical, and a maintainer can be its channel.**
Isomorphism is not persistence in either direction (CLAIM-593), and by
ARG-075's analogy the first direction carries over to organized
individuals whole. CLAIM-615, resting on CLAIM-611, CLAIM-329, CLAIM-593,
CLAIM-328, CLAIM-287 and CLAIM-280, generalizes CLAIM-329 beyond member
replacement: identity over time is carried by the causal transmission of
the relations that keep a repertoire realizable, so a structuralism about
individuals has to be historical. Its admission case, a mechanism passed to
an unrelated workshop, is realistic (a car that changes garages), and it
was closed for LEAD-163 by one shared step: a maintainer that restores a
thing from the organization it finds in it transmits that organization
through the thing, as CLAIM-329's newcomers take a collective's
organization from the collective they join (ARG-216). The two readings
part on organization held only by a maintainer. On CLAIM-983 a maintainer
is a channel and never what is transmitted, so an unrelated team that
takes a project's code continues the project, changed, and participants'
verdict that "it's not the same thing" is a cause of succession, not
evidence of a break. On CLAIM-986 an outside maintainer's organization is
transmitted just insofar as the individual does not carry it and a
competent unrelated replacement would not supply it, so a project's
maintainers, who hold conventions and plans its code does not, can make a
takeover continue the project only in the code's respects (the assistant's
lean). The owner chooses. A collective identity read as a slow mode of its
question–answer game, including the means of revising its rules, takes
CLAIM-615's two-part criterion without citing it (CLAIM-1165, A's).

**What must be transmitted is still open.** "Any causal descendant" is too
much, and the record holds candidates, none of which separates numerical
continuation from descent: routines, layered imprints, and Griesemer and
Shavit's organized sample, which separates descent from independent
reconstitution only (QUESTION-118's notes). A computed candidate is the
channel from a collective's state to its next, held invariant under
interventions on the rest; Bourrat's interventional criterion tracks the
endogenisation of a scaffold, but a boundary drawn to take in the scaffold
passes it before the cells hold the collective together, and neither
measure separates a transition from a collective that absorbs its scaffold
(CASE-320, CLAIM-884). The transfer test is CASE-148, CLAIM-329's
transmission broken into its parts. Fidelity is not lineage: a faithful
successor is not thereby the predecessor continuing (QUESTION-361), and a
successor regulator close to a certifying one at every boundary context
keeps a viable set invariant while whether it continues the predecessor
stays CLAIM-329's question (CLAIM-517). For teams, members are separable
sites of intervention, not separable units of perspective, so one
collaborator at a time can be replaced (CLAIM-1128, Active), and the
supposition that a governed record partly bears a team's perspective makes
the record a candidate for what must stay continuous (CLAIM-1159, supposed;
LEAD-221 is the test).

**How participants count succession has now been read in two settings.**
The owner's design compares contested with accepted successions at a change
of steward, with the lag between lost legitimacy and lost productive
capability as its measure (CASE-223), and participants' verdicts are
proposed to be causally productive of the succession they judge (CLAIM-617;
ARG-067 finds its instances do not discriminate it from CLAIM-329's
criterion alone). Run on eleven open-source successions' public records
(CASE-411): recognition followed the maintainers and not the name, going to
the successor where the creator or the core moved wholesale and splitting
where part of them moved (CLAIM-1158, which corroborates CLAIM-329 as
evidence of how participants judge persistence, not of what it is); an
original line whose steward pays its producers kept producing after losing
legitimacy (CLAIM-1041); and independent verdicts appear only as the stated
reasons for the speaker's own move, so the records cannot time them apart
(CLAIM-1067, the run's lean). The alternative reading of that last result
is that such a verdict is given by moving, a participation that makes the
succession rather than a prior judgement that causes it (CLAIM-1154), which
would favour CLAIM-617's constitutive gloss over its causal title. For the
2005 Nationals, coded from fourteen public statements (CASE-392), no one
counts them the original Senators continued: the franchise runs through
Montreal, and what the club and supporters claim is Washington's baseball
history (CLAIM-1047). What that does to CLAIM-329's defeater is filed two
ways: the Nationals are no case of continuation without a causal path, and
the city's baseball persists, if it does, through the supporters who
carried it across 1972 to 2004 (CLAIM-1129, the run's lean); or that
tradition is one club persisting by place and name across three
organizations with no transmission between them, the case CLAIM-329's
defeater asks for (CLAIM-1206, a counter). They turn on whether supporters'
memory is transmission, which is QUESTION-118's open part.

**A slow variable belongs to a collective only when its members read and
regenerate it.** A slowly changing collective variable is part of a
collective's dynamics when its members read it, condition what they do on
it and so regenerate it (CLAIM-327, after Flack, on the reputation of
CASE-103 and the rivalry of CASE-098, whose perceived reciprocity
QUESTION-298 asks after). A level has a temporal facet, but slowness is a
mark of a level and not its definition (CLAIM-331), and CASE-116's arms
show slowness neither sufficient nor necessary for endogeneity. Whether
dynamical slaving can realize authority is open (QUESTION-440).

**A feature recovered from members' activity is at most a constituent of a
perspective.** How overlapping higher-level perspectives could be found in
members' activity, their number not fixed and coactivity from a common
cause excluded, is open (QUESTION-276, the owner's, with CASE-165 as its
most worked-out method and CASE-101's common-input arm as its control). A
latent feature so recovered is at most a constituent of an organizational
perspective (CLAIM-454); a model whose components are exchangeable given
their weights is organizationally flat however many levels its prior has
(CLAIM-543); and which mixture model is meant matters for the overlap
requirement (CLAIM-536). "Organization" has four senses, and these claims
use the first (TERM-074).

## Where it stands

The criterion is firm in its negative half: persistence through turnover
is not resemblance (CLAIM-329, CLAIM-280, CLAIM-328, CLAIM-287 and
CLAIM-593 are Active), and the documented successions are what CLAIM-329
expects (CLAIM-1158, CLAIM-1047). Its positive half, what must be
transmitted, is open, and the generalization to individuals (CLAIM-615)
rests on CLAIM-611, the one premise not in force, and waits on the owner's
choice between CLAIM-983 and CLAIM-986. The counting studies say how
participants count, not what persistence is, and the records could not
time verdicts apart from moves, so CLAIM-617's causal claim is untested
(its survey arm is unrun). The Nationals case is left two ways.

Filed since version 1 and not integrated here, on the two inquiries the
argument's last two paragraphs set out: on identifying perspectives from
members' activity, CASE-337, CASE-362, CASE-367, CLAIM-910, CLAIM-945,
CLAIM-946 and CLAIM-954, with CLAIM-1044, CLAIM-1174, CLAIM-1175 and
CLAIM-1196, which only SYNTHESIS-122 integrates; on slow and regenerated
variables, CLAIM-1045, CLAIM-1074, CLAIM-1126 and CLAIM-1168; and CASE-266,
CLAIM-694, CASE-365 and CLAIM-957, which reach the part through CLAIM-593
and QUESTION-361. This version was scoped to QUESTION-118. The perspectives
inquiry has grown to about twenty entries and may want an account of its
own; the review reports it rather than folding it in here.

## Not stated on the record

- **That reinstatable and historical persistence are one criterion.**
  CLAIM-287's capacity to reinstate after inactivity and CLAIM-329's
  transmission through turnover as one relation, with the gap in activity
  in one and in members in the other. CASE-116 places arm C under both;
  TERM-071 keeps them apart. Whether memory traces in a sleeper are "passed
  on" is a free parameter that makes the identification hold of every
  case.
- **That a slow variable the members regenerate carries persistence through
  turnover.** That CLAIM-327's third stage suffices for CLAIM-329's
  historical persistence. The Washington tradition is the realistic case
  against it if CLAIM-1206 is right, and no case against it if CLAIM-1129
  is; nothing in the record decides between them.
- **Three separate persistences of a collective.** That a product, its
  carrier and its perspective each persist by their own criterion. Two
  pairs are held (CLAIM-280; CLAIM-329's note of 2026-10-22 with CASE-133);
  no case on file separates a product from a perspective.
- **The coded successions against the two readings of the slot.** CASE-411
  finds the parting CLAIM-986 says CASE-223 could find: where part of the
  core moved and the name and a paid team stayed, recognition split, and
  where the core moved wholesale, the line that kept the name was not
  recognized as continuing. No entry sets this against CLAIM-983 and
  CLAIM-986. Tested at this review, it does not discriminate them: it
  counts for CLAIM-986 only on that reading's own premise that recognition
  is evidence of what was transmitted, and CLAIM-983 takes the same
  pattern as a cause of succession, as its text says. And CLAIM-329's note
  of 2026-11-23 reads the maintainers as the project's own producers,
  where CLAIM-983's example treats them as outside it, so which reading
  applies turns on where the project's boundary is drawn, a choice
  CLAIM-152 makes relative to a target.
- **ARG-216's step and the Washington tradition.** On ARG-216, a club that
  takes up Washington's baseball from the supporters and memory it finds
  in the city would be a channel of that tradition's transmission, so
  CLAIM-1206's tradition, if it is a collective, would persist with
  transmission and would not defeat CLAIM-329. No entry joins LEAD-163's
  step to LEAD-099's readings. Tested at this review, the link turns on
  ARG-216's own limit, that the analogy fails where the thing carries too
  little to work from, and on whether supporters' memory counts as
  organization found in the thing: the same free parameter as the first
  item above, which absorbs every case. Not filed.

## What it does not say

What must be transmitted, or which successor is the persistence (CLAIM-329,
CLAIM-328 and CLAIM-617 each say so); that identity branches (CLAIM-328,
CLAIM-287 and CLAIM-615's correction hold plural succession, not fission);
that participants' recognition is what persistence is; that any slow
variable of a real collective is read and regenerated by its members, or
that any firm has an organizational perspective (CLAIM-454); or that γ is
fixed (CLAIM-161, QUESTION-043).
