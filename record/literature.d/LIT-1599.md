# LIT-1599: Responsibility for Failures of Government

Thompson, D. F. (2014). Responsibility for failures of government: The problem of many hands. *The American Review of Public Administration*, 44(3), 259–273. doi:10.1177/0275074014524013.

Not read. The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited
for and from the exchange's description of it. A reading should check it
first.

## Why this is here

A8 and A9 read it as separating distributed causal contribution from distributed duties of correction, which is [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md)'s distinction and the CECOT case's ([CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md)): "a structure of joint causal efficacy does not automatically supply a structure of joint corrective responsibility".
