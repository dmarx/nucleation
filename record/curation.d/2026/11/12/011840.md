At the owner's word ("do cases and terms next"), after `bounds` ([ADR-094](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-094.md)),
[ADR-095](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-095.md) adds `draws_on` from a case or a term to a reading it uses
other than as its source. This is its first application ([ADR-071](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-071.md)).

## The list

Every case and term in force whose body cites a reading in force with no
relation to it, `reported_in` excluded: 156 citations from cases and 153
from terms when counted, 308 checked, across 119 entries. Four readers,
two on cases and two on terms, each in its own worktree; the parts
touched disjoint entries and combined without conflict.

## Verdicts

| part | pairs | draws_on | none | reported_in | flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cases A | 62 | 51 | 10 | 1 | 1 |
| cases B | 93 | 78 | 12 | 0 | 3 |
| terms A | 87 | 72 | 14 | 0 | 1 |
| terms B | 66 | 41 | 25 | 0 | 2 |
| all | 308 | 242 | 61 | 1 | 7 |

Each case or term that now draws on a reading has one history entry
naming every reading with its own words quoted. The heaviest users are
[CASE-165](../../record/cases.d/CASE-165.md), the team-layer study (fourteen readings, from the hierarchical
beta process to its samplers), [CASE-155](../../record/cases.d/CASE-155.md) (seven readings of learning
dynamics), [TERM-057](../../record/terms.d/TERM-057.md) and [TERM-058](../../record/terms.d/TERM-058.md) (transfer entropy and directed
information against eight readings), and [TERM-076](../../record/terms.d/TERM-076.md) (eight readings of
regulatory meaning and its measures).

**None, by kind.** Attributions and homonyms ([TERM-041](../../record/terms.d/TERM-041.md)'s "character");
contrasts that do no work in the case or term; pointers to where a work
is read; illustrations of what a term names ([TERM-064](../../record/terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s instances of the
regime); readings named only as out of scope or in a history note;
[TERM-100](../../record/terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s index of the entries that turn on its sense; and four readings
of works the case already reports, cited for what they report about it
(provenance).

The near cases the readers met are now point 4 of the decision.
