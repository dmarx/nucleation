# LIT-1653: Posthumanist Performativity: Toward an Understanding of How Matter Comes to Matter

Barad, K. (2003). Posthumanist performativity: Toward an understanding of how matter comes to matter. *Signs: Journal of Women in Culture and Society*, 28(3), 801–831. doi:10.1086/345321.

Not read: no lawful full text was reached on 2026-10-27 (see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for, the exchange's description and the passages quoted in [NOTE-1230](../notes.d/NOTE-1230.md) and [NOTE-1220](../notes.d/NOTE-1220.md).

## Why this is here

A4 recommended it as "a much shorter entry point that presents intra-
action, agential cuts, agency, and material-discursive practices", and
A6 §1 and A9 §11 cite it for apparatuses as evolving material practices
through which local determinacy emerges. It is the primary source for
the sense of *apparatus* and *agential cut* in [TERM-087](../terms.d/TERM-087.md), and the
weakest footing of [CLAIM-476](../claims.d/CLAIM-476.md), which compares luria with it. The
record held Barad only at second hand, through Griesemer and Shavit's
borrowing of intra-action ([NOTE-173](../notes.d/NOTE-173.md)) and Meincke's ([NOTE-115](../notes.d/NOTE-115.md)).
