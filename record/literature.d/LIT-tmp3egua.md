  14 (NIPS 2001), read from the proceedings PDF on papers.nips.cc.
  Laplacian eigenmaps embed the vertices of a heat-kernel-weighted
  neighbourhood graph by minimizing the Dirichlet energy Σ(y_i − y_j)²W_ij
  under yᵀDy = I and yᵀD1 = 0, which gives the eigenvectors of Ly = λDy
  with the smallest non-zero eigenvalues. The constraints are there
  because the energy alone collapses every vertex onto a constant, and
  each connected component is treated separately. The graph Laplacian is
  argued, heuristically, to approximate the Laplace-Beltrami operator. The
  authors speculate that the natural clusters it brings out might ground
  perceptual categories. Here because A27 grounded the owner's U12
