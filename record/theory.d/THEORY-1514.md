# THEORY-1514: Goligher and colleagues hold that mechanical ventilation harms the diaphragm at both ends of effort, atrophy following low effort from over-assistance or sedation and injury possibly following excessive effort, so that ventilation and sedation should be titrated to keep an effort close to that of quiet breathing; that support proportional to the patient's own neural drive makes disuse from over-assistance unlikely, since assist falls as diaphragm activity falls; and that trials confirming the benefit of this approach are still required

## Source

Goligher, Jonkman, Dianti, Vaporidi, Beitler, Patel, Yoshida, Jaber, Dres,
Mauri, Bellani, Demoule, Brochard & Heunks (2020), [LIT-2216](../literature.d/LIT-2216.md); read in
[NOTE-1704](../notes.d/NOTE-1704.md).

## What was actually shown

Nothing new: the review argues from cited studies.

- **Low effort.** "Low respiratory muscle effort, due to ventilator
  over-assist or sedation, may result in muscle atrophy, while high effort
  has been associated with load-induced injury" (introduction), resting on
  donor biopsies (Levine et al. 2008, [LIT-2212](../literature.d/LIT-2212.md)) and ultrasound studies
  of thickness under low effort.
- **High effort.** Fibre injury in biopsies and acute thickening on
  ultrasound "suggested" load-induced injury, and "this hypothesis requires
  further confirmation".
- **The target.** Physiological arguments, summarized elsewhere by the
  authors, that "a respiratory effort level similar to that of resting
  quiet breathing is probably optimal"; Table 1 gives bedside ranges.
- **NAVA.** "low diaphragm activity will immediately reduce inspiratory
  assist", from the mode's design.

## What this does not say

- **That the approach improves outcomes.** Trials "are required to confirm
  the benefit".
- **That high effort injures the human diaphragm.** Hypothesized.
- **That 64% of patients lose diaphragm strength within 24 h of
  ventilation.** The figure is from a study of weakness on ICU admission.
- **Where exactly the band lies** for a given patient; the targets are
  ranges argued from physiology.
- For the record, it qualifies [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) (the work the party does has an
  upper bound as well as a lower one) and gives [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) a worked shape of
  supply covering only what the party cannot do. Both readings are the
  filer's analogies.
