# LIT-242: On the Stepwise Nature of Self-Supervised Learning

James B. Simon, Maksis Knutins, Liu Ziyin, Daniel Geisz, Abraham J. Fetterman, Joshua Albrecht (2023), *Proceedings of the 40th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2023), PMLR 202:31852–31876* — [ARXIV-2303.15438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15438)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-741](../notes.d/NOTE-741.md).*

- **The model.** The loss is L = ‖WΓWᵀ − I_d‖²_F, with
  Γ = (1/2n) Σ_i (x_i x′_iᵀ + x′_i x_iᵀ), symmetrized by hand. This is
  Barlow Twins with λ = 1 and no batch norm, on f(x) = Wx, trained by
  gradient flow.
- **Exact steps from aligned initialization (Proposition 4.1).**
  s_j(t) = e^(4γ_j t) / √(s_j(0)^(−2) + (e^(8γ_j t) − 1)γ_j). Mode j waits
  until τ_j = −log(s_j(0)²γ_j)/(8γ_j), then jumps to γ_j^(−1/2), and the
  loss drops by about one per mode.
- **Which optimum (Proposition 4.3).** Of the many zero-loss solutions,
  the top spectral W are exactly the minimum-Frobenius-norm ones. For
  generic small initialization, training follows the aligned path
  (Result 4.6). That result is derived informally, as the authors say.
- **Kernel PCA (Proposition 5.1).** For any fixed kernel, the final
  embedding is f(x) = U S̃ [b_1 … b_d]ᵀ K̃^(−1/2) [K_xX K_xX′]ᵀ, from the
  top eigenvectors of K_Γ = K̃^(−1/2) Z K̃^(−1/2). It is the
  minimum-RKHS-norm zero-loss solution. With identical views, it is the
  base kernel's top eigenmodes. With two towers (CLIP), K_XX′ = 0 and the
  solution still holds.
- **ResNets.**
  - Barlow Twins, SimCLR and VICReg on STL-10 show stepwise rank growth,
    clearest from small initialization, and also in hidden layers.
  - BYOL did not show steps.
  - The initial NTK predicts poorly. The final empirical NTK captures
    0.52–0.62 of the embedding subspace, against 0.025 by chance.
- **Speed-ups (App. G).** The proposals are untested: "theoretical
  speculation".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped under
the heading *Representation learning as a spectral approximation*. Read in
[NOTE-741](../notes.d/NOTE-741.md). It is held as the dynamical side of that heading. The
optimum-based accounts ([LIT-249](LIT-249.md), [LIT-258](LIT-258.md), [LIT-228](LIT-228.md)) say which subspace is
optimal. This paper says which zero-loss optimum gradient flow reaches and
in what order its directions arrive. It is the record's evidence for any
claim about the order or speed of SSL training. Its stepwise result is filed
as [THEORY-229](../theory.d/THEORY-229.md).

It is a supporting source of [THEORY-007](../theory.d/THEORY-007.md), and there it supports by analogy
only: its operator Γ, or K_Γ, is built from the model's kernel and the
sample pairs, not from K⁺. It is a source of [THEORY-009](../theory.d/THEORY-009.md) for the
hand-symmetrized Γ. Its two-tower case bears on that theory's open
question about cross-modal pairs. The NOTE sets out both points. [LIT-855](LIT-855.md)
extends the method to word2vec-like models.

Tagged `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../decisions.d/ADR-005.md)): the seed judged it chiefly about
machine-learning practice, and App. G's untested speed-up proposals are
practice. It is kept here by the owner's decision of 2026-09-26 that new
work stays in nucleation until a transfer is judged appropriate ([ADR-010](../decisions.d/ADR-010.md)).
