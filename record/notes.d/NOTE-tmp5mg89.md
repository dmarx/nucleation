# NOTE-tmp5mg89: SEP — Philosophy of statistical mechanics

## Contribution

The entry presents statistical mechanics as the attempt to explain macroscopic behaviour, above all equilibrium and the approach to it, from microscopic dynamical laws plus probabilistic assumptions. Because SM lacks an agreed theoretical framework, the entry surveys the competing approaches rather than interpreting one formalism. It sets out Boltzmannian and Gibbsian SM and their variants, then treats further issues: the meaning of SM probabilities, Maxwell's demon, the Gibbs paradox, uses of SM outside physics, and reduction.
