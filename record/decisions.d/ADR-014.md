# ADR-014: emotion-and-affect joins the topics

## Context

The owner asked for Antonio Damasio's work to be added to the record. His
subject is emotion and feeling: the somatic marker hypothesis, feelings as
the experience of the body's homeostatic state, and feelings as the source
of consciousness. The vocabulary had `neuroscience`, `consciousness`,
`cognition` and `agency`, and no word for emotion. Each of those holds part
of the subject, and none of them is it.

The gap was older than Damasio. [LIT-379](../literature.d/LIT-379.md), on anger driving the spread of fake
news, was filed under `social-science` and `society-and-governance` only,
though its finding is about an emotion.

On 2026-10-01 the owner asked for one or more tags for emotion and affect.

## Decision

Add `emotion-and-affect` to `topics`, with a label and a blurb, in the
`philosophy` group. It sits beside `consciousness` and `cognition` because it
is the same kind of word: a part of the mind that philosophy and the sciences
both study. Like `cognition`, it is about the thing, not about one discipline's
view of it.

The blurb names the bodily basis of feelings, including the homeostatic and
interoceptive states they are said to report, so the Damasio works and their
neighbours are found under one word.

Retagged: [LIT-379](../literature.d/LIT-379.md) takes `emotion-and-affect` second, after `social-science`.

Considered and not retagged:

- [LIT-128](../literature.d/LIT-128.md) (affective fictionalism). It is about reading mental-state
  attributions as normative evaluations, not about emotion.
- [LIT-190](../literature.d/LIT-190.md) (coherence and fluency). Its "feeling" is the metacognitive feeling
  of processing fluency, which is its own subject.
- [LIT-207](../literature.d/LIT-207.md) and [LIT-212](../literature.d/LIT-212.md). They mention affective states only in passing, while
  arguing about something else.

## Alternatives

- **Two words: `emotion-and-affect` and `homeostasis-and-interoception`.**
  Homeostasis is Damasio's mechanism rather than his subject, and no other
  work in the record has it as a subject. A word with only one author's works
  under it would split them in two, and a reader looking for feelings would
  have to know the mechanism to find them. Add it when a second line of work
  needs it.
- **Stretch `consciousness` or `cognition`.** The vocabulary is closed so that
  every tag is one somebody chose. Reaching for the nearest wrong word is the
  failure the closure exists to prevent.
- **Outside the philosophy group.** The group is a label over the words, not a
  rule ([ADR-003](ADR-003.md)), and `consciousness` and `cognition` both sit in it.
