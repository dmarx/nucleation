# THEORY-1924: Care theory judges and repairs bad care

## Source

Parra Jounou and Tronto (2024), the neighbour's new LIT (`source:` to be set to its code); the
reading is [NOTE-2027](../notes.d/NOTE-2027.md).

## What was actually shown

Tronto's statements: "The danger of privileged irresponsibility is to cut off one’s reaction to
others" (p. 279), and "I realize that I have been writing about bad care for all my career, because
privileged irresponsibility is a kind of bad care" (p. 282). Her current project is "A Field Guide
to Bad Care".

## What this does not say

- No argument for the concept and no account of how responsibilities are allocated.
- Nothing about organizations or blame.

## From the second reading (2026-11-17)

The second reading adds the coach image from Caring Democracy as Tronto
glosses it ("Once you climb into it, you pretend that you don't know all
those people pushing the carriage"), and the same limit: an interview
answer and an image, not an account of the mechanism.
