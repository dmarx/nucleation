# LIT-1185: Map-like representations of emotion knowledge in hippocampal-prefrontal systems

Ma, Y. & Kragel, P. A. (2026). *Nature Communications* 17:1518. doi:10.1038/s41467-025-68240-z.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-950](../notes.d/NOTE-950.md).*

- **Design.** Reanalysis of the public Emo-FilM data: 29 viewers, 14 films, no task. Labels are consensus continuous ratings from 44 other people. PLS decoders, leave-one-film-out.
- **Dissociation.** Hippocampus: categories z = 0.0525 against space z = 0.0428. vmPFC: space z = 0.0956 against categories z = 0.0838. Double dissociation p = .001. Entorhinal cortex shows neither.
- **Model.** A Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine, which takes an observation and an action at each step, learns an 11 × 11 grid whose emotion nodes are placed by MDS of rating correlations, by random walk. Hippocampus decodes its conjunctive layer p at small scales, vmPFC its structural layer g at large scales (Δz = 0.0712, d = 2.67).
- **Open, by the authors' account.** Whether the concepts reflect shared regularities or are "constructed more flexibly as ad hoc categories shaped by individual experience" (p. 7); the map may be learned "rather than being an innate structure" (p. 8).

## Why this is here

Registered at the owner's request (U9) for constructed emotion ([NOTE-434](../notes.d/NOTE-434.md), [THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md)) and [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md)'s diachronic form: emotion knowledge as a learned map through which later episodes are read. The reading answers less than the registration hoped. Nothing is learned or measured as learned in the participants, who only watched; whether the map shapes how a later episode is read is not tested; and the labels are other people's judgements. The only learner is the model, and it learns by acting, so where the paper models map learning it uses an action–perception loop, not tested against an action-free one. For [THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md) it is within-study classifier evidence, which that theory's promote_when says cannot settle it; the authors leave the constructed-versus-shared question open. It neither supports nor undermines [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md), and gives [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) only an analogy: a structural code reused across contexts, bound to content.
