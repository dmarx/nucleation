# LIT-231: The Hidden Uniform Cluster Prior in Self-Supervised Learning

Mahmoud Assran, Randall Balestriero, Quentin Duval, Florian Bordes, Ishan Misra, Piotr Bojanowski, et al. (2022), *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2023, poster)* — [ARXIV-2210.07277](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07277)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-850](../notes.d/NOTE-850.md).*

- **The anti-collapse term prefers equal clusters.** MSN's regularizer,
  the entropy of the mean assignment, is a KL divergence to the uniform
  distribution plus a constant; in the zero-temperature limit the loss is
  K-means plus a penalty on unequal cluster sizes (Proposition 3). SwAV's
  Sinkhorn step enforces equal sizes outright (Proposition 4).
- **For VICReg and SimCLR the link is weaker.** VICReg, simplified and
  with γ ≫ α, reduces to K-means plus a centroid-orthogonality term, by
  an argument whose last step is informal (Proposition 2). The uniform
  prior then rests on K-means's cited preference for equal clusters.
  SimCLR is covered only through Garrido et al.'s equivalence.
- **Batches of few classes hurt these methods.** With two to eight
  ImageNet classes per batch (same marginal sampling), ImageNet linear
  accuracy falls by 11.1 (SimCLR), 17.7 (MSN) and 17.7 (VICReg) points,
  and by 0.8 and 0.1 for data2vec and MAE, which compute no batch
  statistics. Depth tasks barely move. Architectures differ across
  methods.
- **PMSN.** MSN with a KL to any target over cluster sizes. On iNat18,
  the true class distribution gives +3.0 and a hand-set power law +1.0
  on iNat18 linear accuracy; the same power law on ImageNet costs 15–20
  points. Single runs.
- **Table 1 mislabels a column.** Its CIFAR100 figures are the CIFAR-100
  1% results of the appendix tables, and a few cells differ from those
  tables.
