# LIT-1144: Imprinting: Toward a Multilevel Theory

Marquis, C. & Tilcsik, A. (2013). *Academy of Management Annals* 7(1):195–245.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmpegckt). It separates transmission
(secondhand imprinting) from shared resemblance (cohort effects), and does
not say when an entity bearing another's imprint is that entity continuing.
