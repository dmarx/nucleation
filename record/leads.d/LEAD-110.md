## Note of 2026-11-22: worked through, two alternative readings filed

[CASE-256](../cases.d/CASE-256.md) was worked through by argument, for LEAD-110. The case admits two
readings that are filed differently, and both are filed, marked as
alternatives:

- [CLAIM-960](../claims.d/CLAIM-960.md) (the assistant's lean), with its inference [ARG-207](../arguments.d/ARG-207.md)
  (contrast): read for the default sense of "whole" ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) sense 1, a bare
  relational arrangement), the case yields its **second result for the ground**
  and its **third for the bearer**. No relation of part to whole grounds a norm
  of its own; being a constituent adds nothing the part is not already owed on
  account of its vulnerability, interests, rights and exposure, which an
  identically placed non-constituent shares; the part-whole relation only
  locates where those obligations apply. And because a sense-1 arrangement is
  not an agent ([CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)), the bearer is the agents or roles that maintain,
  control or benefit from it, not the whole.
- [CLAIM-959](../claims.d/CLAIM-959.md) (the alternative), supposed: read for a normatively thick
  membership (a collective institution that is an agent, or a cooperative
  scheme), the case yields its **first result**, a genuine whole-part ground,
  conditional on an associative-obligation principle the record has not
  adopted, with the whole as bearer where it can owe.

What remains the owner's: which sense the case is really about, whether to adopt
an associative-obligation principle (and so whether to hold [CLAIM-959](../claims.d/CLAIM-959.md)), and
the usual status/stage calls. [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)'s prior part, whether a non-agent
whole can owe anything beyond the functional, is untouched and stays open;
neither reading needs it settled. The stage is unchanged.
