# ADR-055: A TERM says what job its definition does (disambiguation, construct, criterion or position), piloted on a sample before it becomes a field, and a criterion TERM carries an application contract that keeps its criterion apart from its indicators

## Context

At U11 of the exchange of 2026-10-26, "About the Chagossians", the owner
asked for a TERM for "organizational achievement", and A11 gave it "a
**target-relative, causally discriminating definition**" ([TERM-tmpzgft9](../terms.d/TERM-tmpzgft9.md)). At
U12 the owner quoted that phrase: "I feel like we need a lot of our TERMs
to be like this, maybe this is a particular 'kind' within the TERM schema
we should distinguish. There's probably a refinement availability here."

The record's TERMs already do different jobs, which their titles show and
the schema does not. Six are "X, and which sense" maps: [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md) (self),
[TERM-068](../terms.d/TERM-068.md) (closure), [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md) (persistence), [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) (perspective),
[TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) (organization) and [TERM-075](../terms.d/TERM-075.md) (attributed self). Others state
when a concept applies, with something to compute or check:
[TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md) (operational availability), [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) (legibility), [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md)
(observational equivalence). Others introduce an object of the record's
own theory, as [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) (will-organization) does, and [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) names a
position without adopting it. [ADR-050](ADR-050.md), Proposed, is the nearest rule: a
claim that uses "organized", "effective", "agent" and similar words says
which sense or criterion it means. It governs uses of words; this
decision governs what a TERM is for.

The schema has the precedent. [ADR-042](ADR-042.md) gave CASE a `kind`, orthogonal to
its `standing`, and [ADR-043](ADR-043.md) gave CLAIM a `warrant`, orthogonal to its
status and role. And TERM already has the relations a "refinement" would
use: `refines`/`refined_by`, `rivals` and `complements` ([ADR-046](ADR-046.md)).

## Decision

Proposed, for the owner's adoption.

- **Four kinds**, from A12:

| kind | job | examples A12 gave |
|---|---|---|
| `disambiguation` | separates senses that must not be conflated | [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md), [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md), [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) |
| `construct` | introduces a defined theoretical object or mechanism | [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) |
| `criterion` | states the conditions under which a concept applies, with a formal, causal, observational or normative way of assessing them | [TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md), [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md), [TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md) |
| `position` | names an articulated position without adopting it | [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) |

  A12 preferred `criterion` to `operational` because "Not all meaningful
  criteria are empirical tests": a criterion of moral standing gives
  normative reasons, not a behavioural probe.

- **What the field says.** A12: "TERM kind should identify the
  definition's function. It should not communicate its truth, warrant, or
  adoption status." Those stay with `status` ([ADR-046](ADR-046.md)) and, for the
  claims stated in a term, with `warrant`.

- **An application contract for a criterion TERM**, in its body, not as
  new fields: **bearer** (what can have the property), **target** (for
  which task or question), **conditions** (contexts, interventions,
  resources, scales, times), **criterion** (what licenses applying it),
  **discrimination** (which nearby concept it rules out), **evidence**
  (what counts for or against an application) and **non-entailments**
  (what stronger claims do not follow).

- **Criterion is not indicator.** A12: "A constitutive criterion says what
  makes something count as an instance of the concept. A diagnostic
  indicator supplies evidence that the criterion is satisfied." A reliable
  behavioural indicator of consciousness is not a condition of it; one
  successful episode is evidence of a maintained competence, not the
  competence. A12 proposed adding the distinction to [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s table; it
  is recorded here and left there for the owner.

- **The division of labour.** A12: "A TERM supplies the criterion; a CLAIM
  argues that some proposed generalization holds; a CASE supplies
  observations or a construction; an ARG connects them." A criterion TERM
  need not prove its own applications.

- **Refinement stays a relation.** The owner's "refinement availability" is
  [ADR-046](ADR-046.md)'s `refines`, and it stays separate from `kind`. A12's caution:
  "We should not create a `refines` edge just because two concepts sound
  related. The narrower term needs additional conditions on the broader
  term, not a change in what type of thing it denotes." So organizational
  competence could refine [TERM-tmpzgft9](../terms.d/TERM-tmpzgft9.md)'s capacity mode, but not an
  achievement defined as an event.

- **Pilot first.** The field is not added to `luria.yaml` by this decision.
  The pilot classifies a deliberately mixed sample, at least A12's nine
  ([TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md), [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md), [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md), [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md), [TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md), [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md), [TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md),
  [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) and [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)), and the terms filed with this decision, and
  reports where the kinds force an arbitrary choice. [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) is the
  stress test: it is both a map of three senses and a substantive
  definition of the first. A12: "if it consistently creates incompatible
  expectations for what belongs in the entry, the solution may be to split
  the sense map from the specific construct." The field becomes optional
  in the schema only if the pilot finds that the kinds sort the record
  without arbitrary choices and give a view or a review decision the record
  lacks; A12: "Merely classifying all existing TERMs successfully does not
  establish the benefit of the new field."

## Alternatives

- **No kinds; prose only.** The titles already carry "and which sense" for
  one kind. The cost is the one the owner named: a definition meant as a
  criterion, like [TERM-tmpzgft9](../terms.d/TERM-tmpzgft9.md)'s, reads like a construct unless its contract is
  asked for.
- **A required field now.** [ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s route was to pilot and then require.
  A required field on 79 terms before the kinds are tested would force the
  arbitrary choices the pilot is meant to find.
- **Fields for the contract.** Seven structured fields per criterion term
  would be ceremony before anyone knows the contract is the right one. The
  body is enough for a pilot.
- **Make "target-relative, causally discriminating" the definition of
  `criterion`.** A12 rejected it: "it does not follow that every useful
  definition must be target-relative **and causally discriminating**. Some
  definitions are purely formal. Others explicitly separate meanings.
  Normative concepts require justificatory criteria rather than only causal
  comparisons."

## Consequences

- Six TERMs are filed with this decision as its first cases, all
  Proposed: [TERM-tmpzgft9](../terms.d/TERM-tmpzgft9.md) (organizational achievement, a criterion while its
  comparison requirement stands), [TERM-tmpsuzzj](../terms.d/TERM-tmpsuzzj.md) (correctability, a criterion),
  [TERM-tmpi9ctf](../terms.d/TERM-tmpi9ctf.md) (consciousness) and [TERM-tmpfkv5u](../terms.d/TERM-tmpfkv5u.md) (collective intention), both
  disambiguations, and [TERM-tmpiiy3n](../terms.d/TERM-tmpiiy3n.md) (moral standing) with [TERM-tmphg3oi](../terms.d/TERM-tmphg3oi.md) (moral worth),
  a normative criterion and a disambiguation. None carries a `kind` field;
  the kinds named here are this decision's reading of them.
- If adopted, the pilot is the next filing, and its report goes in a
  curation entry.
- If the owner adopts [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) as well, the criterion-or-indicator row is
  the natural addition to its table.
