# LEAD-tmp8q1aw: Read the μP, depth-scaling and modular papers registered on 2026-11-20, to test the three claims that relate the record's rate and drift results to them: the order of learning under spectral steepest descent, residual depth scaling as a choice of drift kind, and parameterization as an allocation of rates among modules

## What to read for

- **Tensor Programs VI and Bordelon and colleagues.** Whether learned
  changes land at order 1/L per block under depth-μP while random
  contributions are kept of order 1/√L, [CLAIM-tmp2yj4t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2yj4t.md)'s two rows; and
  whether the "fundamental limitations" of deeper blocks are a further kind
  of accumulation.
- **Yang and Hu, the spectral condition, Chizat and colleagues.** Whether the
  lazy limit is a separation of rates between features and fit
  ([CLAIM-tmpqwoxz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqwoxz.md), defeater (a)), and how each layer's rate exponent is
  stated.
- **The modular norm and modular duality.** Whether the modular norm carries
  a per-module mass that apportions update size ([CLAIM-tmpqwoxz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqwoxz.md), defeater
  (b)); and how dualization of a linear layer relates to the exact polar
  factor [CLAIM-tmpmugkp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmugkp.md) assumes.
