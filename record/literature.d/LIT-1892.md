# LIT-1892: Macroscopic stochastic thermodynamics

Falasco, G., & Esposito, M. (2025). Macroscopic stochastic thermodynamics. *Reviews of Modern Physics*, 97, 015002.

Read in part in [NOTE-1472](../notes.d/NOTE-1472.md) (Skimmed; the parts on large deviations, metastability and escape, and dissipation read in full).

## Why this is here

A14 cited it for the large-deviation account of escape from a maintained state, where a barrier and a system-size parameter set the lifetime. Read with Langer ([LIT-1664](LIT-1664.md)) for metastable decay. The reading confirms that account and corrects one implication: the barrier is in a quasi-potential computed from the dynamics, not in a free energy, and dissipation only brackets it ([THEORY-1264](../theory.d/THEORY-1264.md)). For [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md) and [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md) it supplies the finite-size reading of a fold: a maintained regime is an attractor with its own steady dissipation, whose lifetime collapses as the barrier closes at the saddle-node. For the A14 analogy with SLT ([THEORY-087](../theory.d/THEORY-087.md)) it supplies the disanalogy: the quasi-potential belongs to a dynamics with circulating currents and housekeeping dissipation, and a Bayesian posterior is the detailed-balance case, with neither. Against [THEORY-671](../theory.d/THEORY-671.md) it shows that entropy-production extremum principles hold only in special regimes ([THEORY-1270](../theory.d/THEORY-1270.md)).
