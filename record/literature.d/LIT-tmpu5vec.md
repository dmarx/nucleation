# LIT-tmpu5vec: Flow bei der Arbeit, doch Glück in der Freizeit

Falko Rheinberg, Yvette Manig, Reinhold Kliegl, Stefan Engeser and Regina
Vollmeyer (2007). "Flow bei der Arbeit, doch Glück in der Freizeit.
Zielausrichtung, Flow und Glücksgefühle." *Zeitschrift für Arbeits- und
Organisationspsychologie* 51(3): 105-115. DOI-10.1026/0932-4089.51.3.105

## Key takeaways

- Flow and happiness were both reported at the signal, during the ongoing
  activity (83% of reports within ten minutes of it).
- With flow measured by all its components, the paradox of work holds:
  flow M = 5.24 at work against 4.81 in leisure; happiness/satisfaction
  4.95 against 5.15.
- Across single reports, flow and happiness are "nicht negativ sondern
  leicht positiv" related: r = .36 raw, r = .30 within-person.
- Goal-directedness of the current activity raises flow at work and in
  leisure; it leaves happiness unchanged at work and lowers it in leisure.
