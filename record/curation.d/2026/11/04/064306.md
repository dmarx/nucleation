## Terms of art: what A said, and what it would have proposed

Each item below records (a) the idea as A stated it, (b) the proposal the
intended sense would have produced, and (c) the owner's correction, in the
entry named.

- **"Nucleation" (A1).** A read it as the physical process. The owner's U2
  corrected it: the inquiry's name. A's idea is held as QUESTION-281.
  Read as intended, A would have asked whether a learning transition is
  relief in the compatibility complex of the training demands. That is in
  the note on QUESTION-275.
- **"Competition" for frustration (A6).** The owner's U7 corrected it. Read
  in TERM-060's sense, niche differentiation becomes relief, and frustration
  splits into structural and governance cases. See CLAIM-tmpv67b2 and the
  note on QUESTION-131.
- **The sheaf account (A3, A4).** A used generic gluing. Through CLAIM-538,
  relief needs one of four things:
  - enlarged tolerances;
  - a changed H¹ class;
  - cutting the witnessing cycle;
  - covering it.

  A25's figure has no cycle, so it cannot show a structural obstruction.
  See the note on CLAIM-538.
- **Endorsement (A11–A16, A28, A29).** A used the reflective, Frankfurtian
  sense, which TERM-053 excludes, and misplaced Frankfurt's unwilling
  addict in its 2×2 table. In TERM-053's causal sense:
  - a tolerance is the boundary where the sign of endorsement changes
    (CLAIM-tmpgjdby);
  - a complaint taken up is negative endorsement (CLAIM-tmpu69za);
  - a perspective has a threshold for acting and another for endorsing
    (the note on TERM-053).

  Frankfurt's satisfaction, read as every perspective inside its
  tolerance, is wholeheartedness as the full face (CLAIM-tmpu69za). The
  reflective sense gets its own code, TERM-tmp0evbm.
- **"Psychological flexibility" (A19, A20).** A's sense uses an external
  standard and drops ACT's relation to private events. Read in ACT's sense
  (LIT-555), the proposal would have been organizational experiential
  avoidance, which CLAIM-446 already holds. A also claimed that CLAIM-349
  "already supplies" a definition of psychological flexibility; it doesn't.
  See the notes on CLAIM-446 and CLAIM-349.
- **"Frustration" in A27's code**, a drive that rises without authority.
  In the record's sense, the toy's three-way requirement is structurally
  frustrated, and that is what A could have measured. See the note on
  CASE-153.
- **"Jurisdiction" (A29)** for TERM-017's suspension. See the note on
  CLAIM-465.
- **"Cognitive dysregulation" (A29)**, read as attentional. See the
  assumption below, and the note on CLAIM-286.
