## What A got wrong, across the works

- **Quotations.** A's quoted lines for Berry et al. and Murray and
  Sastry, and its sentences for Zang et al., Chen, Choi and Mahadevan, Li
  and Choi and Stern, Pinson and Murugan, are A's paraphrases.
- **Attribution.**
  - the point that redundant constraints create states of self-stress is
    Damavandi et al.'s, not Rocks and Mehta's;
  - the origami-springs paper's title was wrong;
  - "morphological computation" is A's label for Dollar and Howe.
- **Overstatements.**
  - FACT's 26 matching pairs hold only for ideal pure-force constraints;
  - the 64 switch states are a counting convention, the defects were
    reached by hand, and height and stiffness do not identify every
    state;
  - DeLuca et al.'s trapping is simulated, found in the large design, and
    not called nucleation;
  - the origami reservoir learns only in its linear readout;
  - Zang et al.'s frustration is prestress applied from outside;
  - the interface freedoms of Kane and Lubensky and of Chen et al. need
    unequal polarizations, and otherwise give stressed lines;
  - A's bottleneck budget keeps only the lowest barrier (Mirth et al.).
- **Missed.** Stern, Pinson and Murugan's main point: actuator placement
  predicts success far better than count.
