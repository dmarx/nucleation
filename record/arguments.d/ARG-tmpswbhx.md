# ARG-tmpswbhx: The parity triangle shows that translation changes the global structure of meaning

## The inference

CASE-037 is the parity triangle with uniform weights: a no-signalling,
strongly contextual model, with contextual fraction 1. Changed in one
context, it gives CLAIM-105's recovered pair, with fractions 1 and 0. So a
step that keeps most local judgements can change their global
compatibility. That is CLAIM-105: "translation preserves local meanings
while changing the global structure of meaning". CLAIM-219 calls the case
"the one instance of the headline in the record".

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes,
  and this is the question that fails. CLAIM-219: "On the first horn the
  headline has no case but a constructed one", and "CASE-037 is
  stipulated." The model exists whatever documented chains do. CLAIM-105's
  version 3 speaks of what "documented reconstruction chains" commonly do,
  so a constructed case is at most an existence proof, and an existence
  proof does not bear on what is common.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No. The rival is
  CLAIM-325: the behavioural data the record has read show no
  contextuality proper. Dzhafarov, Zhang and Kujala (LIT-264) found "None
  is contextual" in five kinds of behavioural data. A stipulated model
  cannot tell that rival from the headline.
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** Within the case, yes.
  The contextual fraction is defined for a no-signalling model, and
  CASE-037 is one. The fault is the premise's provenance, not the measure.

## Verdict

Rejected. CLAIM-219 undercuts it. Its dilemma is about the subject matter:
a change in global compatibility over a fixed scenario is either
contextuality proper, which the data read show none of, or a change in
overlap consistency, which moves by at most 2ε when each context's
distribution moves by ε, and is ordinary context dependence. CLAIM-325, which CLAIM-219 rests
on, could undercut it too. The argument does not say a contextual
pragmatic data set cannot exist; one, documented with its coding fixed in
advance, would be a different premise.
