The owner pointed to [MOM-CLM-088](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-088.md) ("hey take a look at …CLM-088.md"), and
after the assistant's reading asked for it all to be filed. Under [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md)
the MOM claim gets an entry of this record's own.

## What entered

- **[MOM-CLM-088](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-088.md) → [CLAIM-tmpk4fd7](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4fd7.md)** (Active). The entry states what this
  record needs from the MOM claim, not the MOM claim's headline. MOM's
  claim is the manuscript's thesis, "a semantic system is a sheaf of
  predicates over a category of contexts", with the critique in its
  body. This record needs the critique: sections over disjoint contexts
  glue, a failure needs an overlap, and the cover decides.

## How it was checked

- **The argument** is the sheaf condition applied to a cover whose
  members do not meet. It was re-derived, not taken from MOM.
- **The quotations** were checked against MoM's source chapters in a local
  clone at d987e2a: v1 "Sheaf Semantics of Context" §10.4 and §10.6, and
  "Epistemic Drift and Semantic Simulation" §11.3.3. All are verbatim.
- **Not dual-filed:** v1 §11.5 of the same chapter, "Identity as Global
  Section". It treats a person's roles as sections over family,
  professional and social contexts, and is the same move applied to the
  self. MOM has no claim on that section. [CLAIM-tmpk4fd7](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4fd7.md) cites it by
  section and does not depend on it.

## Boundary

This is mathematics read for a question about meaning and the self, which
no anthology topic holds. There is no work here to dual-hold, only a MOM
claim, which [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md) covers.

## Then: §11.5 ported, with boyd (2008)

The owner asked for the §11.5 passage to be ported "if it's interesting and
defensible". As written it is not defensible: it calls contradictory roles a
failure of identity to unify, which is [CLAIM-tmpk4fd7](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4fd7.md)'s error for the self.
Restated on overlaps it is both, and outside evidence was looked for before
filing it.

- **[LIT-tmpviq1w](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpviq1w.md), boyd (2008), *Taken Out of Context***, read in part
  ([NOTE-tmp12jp1](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp12jp1.md)) from the author's copy, licensed CC BY-NC-ND 3.0. It
  supplies collapsed contexts, the Carmichael example after Meyrowitz, and
  scripted collisions such as weddings. Not held in the anthology.
- **Looked for and not filed.** Marwick & boyd (2011), "I tweet honestly, I
  tweet passionately" (doi:10.1177/1461444810365313), is closed access with
  no repository copy (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar). Goffman (1959), *The
  Presentation of Self in Everyday Life*, had no lawful full text reachable;
  the Internet Archive copies are lending items or uploads of uncertain
  standing, and none was read. Meyrowitz (1985), *No Sense of Place*, was not
  looked for. All three are cited through boyd only.
- **Filed from it:** [CLAIM-tmp37bdg](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp37bdg.md) (Proposed), and [CASE-tmpdat4f](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpdat4f.md)
  (stipulated, because its facts are secondhand).
