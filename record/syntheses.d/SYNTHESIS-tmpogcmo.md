# SYNTHESIS-tmpogcmo: Restricting capabilities, and who controls the terms of waiting and contracting: the grounds of a restriction are kept apart from the authority to impose it, a capability is decomposed into what a restriction can reach, a restriction justified by incompetence can produce the incompetence, an opposition can be free as an actor while another holds its efficacy, and control over a deferred reward or over what a contract left open is control over the other party's terms

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line, filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day. Every component is new to any account. The question is the owner's and Open; the claims and the term are A's and Proposed; the case is a reported study the record has not read, with A's analysis. Where a component cites another, the account says which entry does.*

## The account

**The question.** QUESTION-tmp9oq97 (the owner's, U20; Open; "and who
may restrict it" is A45's refinement, added by the filer): under what
conditions should a capability be restricted, and who may restrict it?
Its body lists what the record held before: CLAIM-684, CLAIM-680,
CLAIM-682 with TERM-124, TERM-120 with CASE-128, and CLAIM-688, as
conditions for particular cases and no general answer. It sorts the
exchange's parts of the question: the grounds and the authority
(CLAIM-tmpmeabu, "which answers this question in part"), what is
restricted (TERM-tmprcm2c), what a restriction does over time
(CLAIM-tmp8wki5), the capability to change the conditions of one's own
challenge (QUESTION-tmpcz1ui, with CLAIM-tmp3t16f and CLAIM-tmpl0sgt as
two such capabilities), and who sets the terms (A53, at the owner's
U24). It holds A49 §13's five candidate conditions as "a list to test,
not yet an answer".

**Grounds and authority.** CLAIM-tmpmeabu (A's, A45 §6, A41 §5 and A49
§12; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also organizational-normativity):
that a capability's exercise should be limited and that a given party may
limit it are separate conclusions, neither entailing the other; the power
to restrict is itself a capability, whose legitimacy turns on how it is
bounded and answerable. It answers QUESTION-tmp9oq97, bears on
QUESTION-tmplax2r, uses TERM-tmprcm2c, and complements CLAIM-680 (its
summary: "the special case in which the ground is a judgment of worth"),
CLAIM-682, CLAIM-tmpx1g4r and CLAIM-tmpcpijj. Its body adds A49 §12's
third, relational justification, under which "A protective restriction
may become dominating if its continuation depends entirely on unchecked
discretion".

**What a restriction reaches.** TERM-tmprcm2c (A's, A41 §1, A45 §4 and
A49 §3; Proposed): capability decomposed for restriction into
competence, exercise, authority and governance, which vary
independently, so a restriction on one is not a restriction on another.
Its body says a restriction on exercise can erode competence where
competence is kept by use (CLAIM-tmp8wki5), and that it is not
CLAIM-678's dimensions of power, CLAIM-569's resource requirements,
Sen's capability or TERM-085's correctability.

**A restriction that produces its own warrant.** CLAIM-tmp8wki5 (A's,
A41 §8, A45 §9 and A49 §8.1; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
transient-support): where competence is kept by use and lost with
disuse, and a restriction is lifted when competence is shown, a
restriction justified by a lack of competence lowers the competence it
waits for, so that without a route to practice it produces the evidence
for its own continuation. It bears on QUESTION-tmp9oq97, uses
TERM-tmprcm2c, and complements CLAIM-602, CLAIM-673 and CLAIM-675; its
body says it needs only CLAIM-602's and CLAIM-605's premise of decay with
disuse, that A45 §9's instance is CLAIM-673 reached by restriction
rather than substitution, and that CLAIM-tmp0wx69 asks whether the party
that keeps the trap closed is responsible. Its body states the
obligation A41 §8 draws: "*Who is responsible for making condition X
feasible?*"

**Controlled incapacity.** CLAIM-tmpm95mo (A's, A37 §10 to §11, at the
owner's U18; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): an opposition can be
independent as an actor while its capacity to change the arrangement it
opposes depends on another's control of its resources, standing,
corrective authority or time; that controlled incapacity, not
incapacity or failure as such, makes contained opposition a mark of
domination. It bears on QUESTION-tmpcz1ui, uses TERM-124, and
complements CLAIM-686 (its summary: "this adds that such failure can be
held by another party") and CLAIM-404 (its body: "the converse
arrangement"). Its body corrects A's gloss of CLAIM-523.

**Control over a deferred reward.** CLAIM-tmpt091d (A's, A51 §4 and A53
§2, from the owner's U23 and U24; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
will-organization): where one party gives up something now for a benefit
another controls later, whether waiting is good judgment depends on the
controller's reliability, so a controller not answerable for delivery
can make the other's patience a means of its control. It grounds on
CASE-tmp07hx9, uses TERM-124, and complements CLAIM-601 (its body: "the
same misreading from the other side") and CLAIM-tmpdic1g. Its body
checks A53 §2's inequality, wait only if 2pδ > 1, and calls TERM-110's
ratchet "a mechanism by which a controller changes delivery after
restraint is shown". CASE-tmp07hx9 (the owner's association at U23, A's
analysis in A51 and A53 §2; documented reported study, Proposed, its
work unread; first line will-organization): children who had seen an
experimenter break a promise waited about a quarter as long. It
illustrates CLAIM-344; its body says CLAIM-tmpt091d grounds on it, that
waiting behaviour alike over different organizations is CLAIM-364's
form, and that the case applies the owner's method (CLAIM-tmp613ja).

**Residual control.** CLAIM-tmpdic1g (the connection to contract theory
the owner's, U24; the residual-control reading A's, A53 §2; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed): in an incomplete contract the holder of residual
rights of control decides what the contract left open, so a relationship
fair in every specified term can leave one party's terms at the other's
discretion, exercised and seen only when an uncovered contingency
arises. It uses TERM-124 and complements CLAIM-690 (its summary: "this
names a form that control takes in a contract"), CLAIM-tmpt091d and
CLAIM-tmp3t16f, which its body calls "control acquired by violation"
against this, "the authorized case". Its body also reads CLAIM-364's
parting under intervention as two contracts that part "at the first
uncovered contingency".

## Arm B

- **QUESTION-tmp9oq97's answers as one answer** (stage 1's B2: CLAIM-
  tmpmeabu, CLAIM-tmp8wki5, CLAIM-tmp3t16f and CLAIM-tmpl0sgt, across four
  lines). The candidate: a restriction is justified only if its ground
  holds, its imposer has authority, and, where lifting waits on shown
  competence, the restrictor supplies the route back. Route tried: a
  derivation from CLAIM-tmpmeabu's three justifications and
  CLAIM-tmp8wki5's trap. Most of it is stated already: the obligation of
  a route back is in CLAIM-tmp8wki5's body (A41 §8, A49 §8.1), and the
  question's body holds the conjunction, A49 §13's five conditions, as a
  list to test. The residue, that the trap fails relational
  justification even when lifting is decided by an independent examiner,
  has a separating case (that examiner) but turns on CLAIM-tmpmeabu's
  "acceptable arrangement", which absorbs every case. Fails for a free
  parameter. CLAIM-tmp3t16f and CLAIM-tmpl0sgt answer a different part,
  which capability, as the question's body already sorts them; they do
  not integrate with the other two.
- **One form across waiting, contracting and opposing**
  (CLAIM-tmpt091d, CLAIM-tmpdic1g, CLAIM-tmpm95mo): the act is the
  party's own and another controls the conditions that decide its
  effect. Already stated: each claim uses TERM-124 for exactly that
  control, and CLAIM-tmpt091d and CLAIM-tmpdic1g complement each other.
  The pairing with CLAIM-tmpm95mo adds nothing beyond TERM-124 and is
  listed above as not stated. Nothing new is filed.
