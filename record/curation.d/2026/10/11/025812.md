The owner asked: "register MoM as a remote and then dual file anything we
need from there on nucleation directly". [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md) records the decision. This
entry records what was filed, why, and how each was checked.

## The question it serves

What makes a combined form one system, and where its boundary lies. The
cases are the dancing couple ([CASE-tmpbv3ts](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md), the owner's [CLAIM-tmpygda8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpygda8.md)),
the firm at its all-hands ([CASE-tmpk52n6](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpk52n6.md)) and the person across time. A
survey of Mathematics of Meaning, at commit d987e2a, found the five
entries below. It also found the record's own verdict on the wider question:
[MOM-CLM-170](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-170.md) records that the corpus's list of five boundary criteria
"terminates in a shrug". The gap criterion is that record's answer.

## What was dual-filed, and how it was checked

All five are CLAIMs in `distributed-agency`, each naming its MOM code:

- **[MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md) → [CLAIM-tmp45kkg](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp45kkg.md)** (Active). The Occam-priced reification
  threshold. Re-run: exact Bayes factor against the rule in the conjugate
  case, agreement to 9e-16 in eight settings.
- **[MOM-CLM-144](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-144.md) → [CLAIM-tmpjsasy](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjsasy.md)** (Active). The gap picks the object.
  Davis–Kahan, plus a re-run of MOM's stability table at 150 resamples. The
  pattern was reproduced; the exact angles vary with the draw.
- **[MOM-CLM-165](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-165.md) → [CLAIM-tmp3u2m7](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3u2m7.md)** (Active, abelian case). Unity as a
  monodromy over at least three contexts. Re-run: gradient descent against
  the closed form in all eight settings, and the two-context case always
  zero.
- **[MOM-CLM-153](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-153.md) → [CLAIM-tmpdqsrb](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdqsrb.md)** (Proposed, though Active in MOM). Selfhood
  as γ conserved under the system's own dynamics. A sound repair, but γ is
  unspecified and nothing was measured.
- **[MOM-POS-001](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/positions.d/POS-001.md) → [CLAIM-tmp3qyzh](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3qyzh.md)** (Active). Sufficiency-based
  individuation is target-relative. True by definition, and the standing
  caution on the other four.

The scripts are not kept in the record. They re-implement each check from
the claim's own statement and are described in each entry's history.

## Fired before trusting

A citation of a MOM code that does not exist, [MOM-CLM-999](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-999.md), was planted in a
draft of this entry and the pipeline was run. **The lint did not catch it.**
MOM codes resolve by the code-only filename convention, which builds a URL
without checking that the file exists, and `luria repair` linked the bad
code like a good one. `luria remotes --check` reported every MOM document as
"unverifiable: repo not public": the repository is private, so anonymous
requests 404. It reports the same for ANTH from this environment. The only
check that worked was against a local clone: all eight MOM codes cited in
this record exist at MOM commit d987e2a, checked by script. The planted
line was removed. Until the remote is pinned, a bad MOM code is caught only
by checking against a clone. [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md) says so.
