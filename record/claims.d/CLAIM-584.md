# CLAIM-584: Maintaining a specialized capability that is seldom or never exercised for its specialized function is wasteful

## Not claimed

The record does not claim this. It is the conclusion of [ARG-053](../arguments.d/ARG-053.md),
which A proposed and the record rejects: the observation that a
specialized resource is kept at a cost and seldom used fits waste and fits
insurance against contingencies that did not occur. [CLAIM-561](CLAIM-561.md) shows
that even a high cancellation rate can be what an optimal policy
produces. Filed after [ARG-042](../arguments.d/ARG-042.md)'s pattern, where the conclusion of a
rejected inference is a disclaimed claim of its own.

## What it does not say

That the proposition is false. For some capabilities, demands, costs and
alternatives, maintenance is wasteful. What would show it is an
evaluation of the kind [QUESTION-395](../questions.d/QUESTION-395.md) asks for, not a count of uses.
