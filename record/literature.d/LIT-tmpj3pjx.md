  positioned as moral crumple zones. They were held responsible for the
  quality, verification and use of outputs from immature, opaque tools they
  could not shape, and poor outputs were framed as user error. Five mechanisms
  produce this: oversight and liability taken for granted, immature tools

(A17 §5, A19, A17 reading list) is accurate: two Finnish organizations, 2.5 years of ethnography,
