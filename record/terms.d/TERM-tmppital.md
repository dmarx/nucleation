# TERM-tmppital: frame, as an operation on an interpretive state

## Definition

A30: "**interpretive frames aren't merely alternative coordinate systems for
describing an unchanged meaning. Applying one frame can transform the state on
which subsequent frames operate.** That is a significant departure from our
earlier relativistic analogy." Frames are operations T_f on a reader's
interpretive state, and T_f T_g ≠ T_g T_f is possible ([CLAIM-tmpevciu](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpevciu.md)).

## What it is not

Not a projector in a Hilbert space by default: A30 shows classical stochastic
operations can also fail to commute ([TERM-tmpnvqh8](TERM-tmpnvqh8.md), [CASE-tmp11vtm](../cases.d/CASE-tmp11vtm.md)). The manuscript §9: "Write
A and B for stochastic framing operations."
