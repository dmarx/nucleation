# LIT-2693: Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition

Umpleby, S. A. (2016). Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition. *Journal of Systemics, Cybernetics and Informatics* 14(5): 65–67. https://www.iiisci.org/Journal/PDV/sci/pdfs/IP020LL16.pdf.

## Key takeaways

- "Lefebvre contended that the dominant ethical systems in the West and
  the Soviet Union were fundamentally different" (p. 65).
- "In the first ethical system, the end does not justify the means. In the
  second ethical system, the end does justify the means" (p. 65); a saint
  compromises in the first and confronts in the second (p. 66).
- The evidence is anecdote, and the claim that the theory "was used at the
  highest levels in both the US and the USSR" (p. 67) is unsourced.
