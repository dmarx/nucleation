# THEORY-1684: Rockström and colleagues (2026) set out the Earth Commission's programme for 2024–2027: to extend quantified "safe" boundaries (protecting Earth-system resilience) and "just" boundaries (minimizing significant harm to people, other species and future generations) to novel entities and the ocean, and to translate them, by transcription into aggregate pressure budgets and then allocation among actors under stated fairness principles, into targets for countries, cities and companies, treating justice as a structural condition of planetary stability.

## The account

Rockström and colleagues (2026) set out the Earth Commission's programme for 2024–2027: to extend quantified "safe" boundaries (protecting Earth-system resilience) and "just" boundaries (minimizing significant harm to people, other species and future generations) to novel entities and the ocean, and to translate them, by transcription into aggregate pressure budgets and then allocation among actors under stated fairness principles, into targets for countries, cities and companies, treating justice as a structural condition of planetary stability.

The reading in [NOTE-1796](../notes.d/NOTE-1796.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
