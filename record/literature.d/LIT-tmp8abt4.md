# LIT-tmp8abt4: Discourse and the Translator

Basil Hatim and Ian Mason (1990), London and New York: Longman (Language in
Social Life series), xiv, 258 pp. — DOI-10.4324/9781315846583 (Routledge
reissue, 2014)

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read*: no lawful full text was reachable (see the history
note). What follows restates the publisher's description and contents.

- The publisher's description: the book "both incorporates and moves
  beyond previous studies of translation", drawing on sociolinguistics,
  discourse studies, pragmatics and semiotics to analyse translation in
  its social contexts, and presents the translator as a mediator between
  cultures.
- Contents, from the publisher's page: 1. Issues and Debates in
  Translation Studies; 2. Linguistics and Translators: Theory and
  Practice; 3. Context in Translating: Register Analysis; 4. Translating
  and Language as Discourse; 5. Translating Text as Action: The Pragmatic
  Dimension of Context; 6. Translating Texts as Signs: The Semiotic
  Dimension of Context; 7. Intertextuality and Intentionality; 8. Text
  Type as the Translator's Focus; 9. Prose Designs: Text Structure in
  Translation; 10. Discourse Texture; 11. The Translator as Mediator;
  glossary, list of texts quoted, bibliography, index.
