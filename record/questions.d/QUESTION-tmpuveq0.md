# QUESTION-tmpuveq0: What exactly is the counter-normativity committed by a self-maintaining organization that harms and reproduces the representational projections that make the harm non-legible, what makes it reproductive, and when is it the organization's own rather than its participants'?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U27 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and
Disposition", second share
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c): "help me
characterize the precise nature of counter-normativity being committed by a
self-maintaining system that not only harms but reproduces the
representational projections that make the harm non-legible." The owner's
"system" is a kind of organization (the owner's correction of 2026-11-07,
"within our framework, a 'system' is just a kind of 'organization'").

It asks three things.

1. **What the wrong is.** A's answer is a second-order wrong of
   answerability with a five-condition criterion (CLAIM-tmptfuky), and a
   disposition to turn challenges against future challenge
   (CLAIM-tmplgp4k). Neither is the owner's.
2. **What makes it reproductive.** The record holds the dynamical form:
   CLAIM-445 and TERM-020's protective lock-in, where a regime shifts the
   evidence and authority that judge it. CASE-tmpnlf5c is A's toy model of
   it, corrected by the reader.
3. **When it is the organization's own** rather than its participants' or
   an outside maintainer's. CLAIM-418 keeps perspective apart from agency,
   and TERM-100's maintenance provenance asks whose process keeps the
   blindness going. This part is open; A62 §8 only names it.

It refines QUESTION-129 because every answer to part 1 that A gave needs
an "independently specified standard", which is the half of TERM-059 the
record has not established.

## What would count as an answer

An account that says what the wrong is, shows it is not reducible to the
harm's continuation, and gives a test, such as an intervention on the
production mechanism (CLAIM-522), that separates an organization
maintaining its own blindness from one whose blindness is maintained from
outside.
