# TERM-tmpdk711: joint inquiry frontier, of an agent at a budget and horizon: the question–answer pairs it can bring about together, formulating the question and producing and evaluating an answer to a stated acceptance standard within the same resources; its question side is the inquiry frontier's investigable set, and its answer side, indexed by question, is the answer frontier

## Definition

For an agent with concepts K, budget B, horizon H and an acceptance standard
κ for the kind of inquiry in hand ([TERM-095](TERM-095.md)'s 𝓚: explanation, prediction,
proof, design, demonstration), the **joint inquiry frontier** is

  𝓕(B, H; κ) = {(q, a) : some process available to the agent formulates q,
  produces a ∈ A_q and evaluates it as meeting κ, within B and H}.

It has three projections, each already a set the record can name:

- **its question side**, proj_Q 𝓕: [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s investigable set, read with
  "pursue it to a stated standard" as attaining an answer that meets κ (A58's
  "resolvable"). It lies inside [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s askable set.
- **the answer frontier** of q, {a : (q, a) ∈ 𝓕}: the answers attainable
  for q when formulating q is paid from the same budget.
- **the conditional answer frontier** of q: the answers attainable when q is
  supplied at no cost. It contains the answer frontier and can be larger.

What fixes 𝓕 beyond the askable set and the conditional answer frontier is
the coupling: the composition rule for costs, the schedule (sequential,
parallel), and what a way of formulating q does to the cost of answering it
([CLAIM-tmpj15c6](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj15c6.md)). Authorization, where required, is one more term in that
coupling ([CLAIM-tmp3l7is](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3l7is.md)).

## What it is not

- Not [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s inquiry frontier, which stays the askable set; under this
  alternative [TERM-092](TERM-092.md) is unchanged, and its four sets keep their meanings.
- Not the operational frontier ([CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md)): answering q is not doing what q
  specifies (A58 §4).
- Not a credence on any answer: a pair is in 𝓕 when an answer meeting κ is
  attainable, not when it is true.

## Why this alternative

[TERM-092](TERM-092.md) is Active and five claims are stated in it, two of them Active
([CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md), [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md)) and using its askable set by name. Keeping it and
adding the joint term leaves them as they are. The owner's U30 put the name
on the joint object; if that is wanted too, a later retitling of [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)
("query frontier") would carry it, and that step is the owner's.
