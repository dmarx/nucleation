# LEAD-060: State the criterion that separates reframing a communicative act from changing it, and test it by having judges sort pairs of renderings into the two classes

## What it would do

Write the criterion, starting with [CASE-056](../cases.d/CASE-056.md) and [CASE-012](../cases.d/CASE-012.md), and have judges
sort pairs by it.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-004](../claims.d/CLAIM-004.md): "What would promote it is that criterion stated, and judges
  sorting pairs of renderings by it above chance."
- [CLAIM-004](../claims.d/CLAIM-004.md): "supplies the documented first pair"

## What would count

Sorting above chance promotes [CLAIM-004](../claims.d/CLAIM-004.md); chance sorting defeats it.
