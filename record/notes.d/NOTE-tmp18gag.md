# NOTE-tmp18gag: Investigating Environmental Links Between Parent Depression and Child Depressive/Anxiety Symptoms Using an Assisted Conception Design

## Contribution

It turns the donor-gamete design onto the postnatal side. A parent who
raises a child conceived from a donor's gamete shares no genes with it,
so an association between the parent's symptoms and the child's in such
pairs is environmental, by the design's definition.

## Key results

- **The design's logic** (Statistical Analyses): "The presence of an
  association in unrelated mother–child pairs would provide evidence of
  environmental transmission because inherited links are removed".
- **Mothers** (Abstract, Results): mother-rated child symptoms, unrelated
  r = 0.32, related r = 0.31, both p < .001. Father-rated, 0.26 and 0.22.
  The groups do not differ (Fisher Z = 0.28, p = .39).
- **Fathers** (Results): paternal depression symptoms and child symptoms,
  unrelated (sperm or embryo donation) r = 0.17, p < .05; related
  r = 0.23, p < .001. They do not differ.
- **Shared adversity** (Results): associations "remained significant and
  unattenuated" after adjustment for negative life events, family income
  and social class.
- **Girls** (Results): in the unrelated group the association was larger
  for girls than for boys at the first wave (Z = 1.71 and 1.73,
  p < .05).

## Assumptions

- Parent-rated child symptoms. Children were not asked, partly because they
  might not know how they were conceived.
- The design's "environmental" includes everything the rearing parent
  does, and any shared adversity not among the three measured.

## Concepts

- **Assisted conception design**: the classic adoption logic, with
  unrelated rearing from birth (or, for the mother, from conception).

## Bearing on the record

- **QUESTION-297, the parenting route** (evidence, by parallel). A
  parent's distress reaches the child through rearing alone. The exposure
  is depression symptoms, not trauma. Danieli, Norris and Engdahl
  (NOTE-1307) locate a parenting route among survivors' children, but in
  families where the parent is also the genetic parent. This design
  supplies the removal of genes that theirs lacks, for a different
  exposure.
- **CLAIM-tmp3qsdg** (ground). In a donor-gamete design, rearing falls on
  the same side as the womb. That is the reason such a design separates
  "what travels with the gamete" from "what travels with the family", and
  no finer.

## Limitations

- Cross-sectional associations of symptom scales. Reverse effects, child on
  parent, are not excluded.
- The discussion was not read, so the authors' own caveats are not
  recorded here.
- The exposure is not trauma, so the result bears on the route, not on
  the transmission of trauma.
