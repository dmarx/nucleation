# QUESTION-tmpjs7gh: Does the effect of forming implementation intentions stay reliably above zero, and of the same small order, once tests published after September 2019 are added or preregistered multi-site replications are run with correction for publication bias, and does it hold outside the lab and online?

## Why it is a question

THEORY-918 holds that across 642 tests to September 2019 the pooled
d = .36 falls to a model-averaged d = .15 under robust Bayesian
meta-analysis, which still found extreme evidence of an effect. The search
was not updated, only the overall estimate was corrected, and effects were
smaller in the field and online than in the lab and in person.

## What would answer it

A meta-analysis that adds the tests published since September 2019, or a
set of preregistered multi-site replications, estimating the effect with
correction for publication bias and finding it reliably above zero and of
the same small order. The effect is refuted if bias-corrected estimates in
an updated or registered set fall to zero, and narrowed if it holds only in
the lab and for in-person delivery. A larger uncorrected pooled d cannot
answer it.
