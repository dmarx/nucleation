# TERM-tmpc64yu: character-like structural signature, as a realization-independent invariant of a communicative kind, called a character only when it is the trace of a specified representation

## Definition

A character-like structural signature is a proposed invariant χ_A of a
communicative kind ([TERM-tmp7encb](TERM-tmp7encb.md)). It takes the same value, or nearly the same,
on every realization of the kind: A194 §2 asks for "a suitable invariant
profile χ" on which the spoken, translated and visual realizations agree. It
must also keep the distinctions the theory cares about. A194 §7: "the
character-like invariant preserves independently specified pragmatic and
decision-relevant distinctions."

The word "character" is reserved. A194 §2 first marked it: "Here χ is a
proposed character-like invariant, not necessarily the trace of a group
representation." A203 §36 made it a rule: "The word *character* would be used
literally when χ is a trace character of a specified representation.
Otherwise, it should be described as a **character-like structural
signature**. That terminological distinction prevents the proposal from making
stronger representation-theoretic claims than its construction supports."
A214 §11 applies it to a learned signature Ẑ: when no genuine group
representation is available, "Ẑ remains a *character-like signature*, without
claiming the completeness theorems of ordinary character theory." A218 Ch3.11
lists "Character-like signatures and their mathematical limitations".

A203 §36 asks three things of it: stability under the designated
transformations, sufficiency for a family of decisions, and power to
discriminate designated contrasts. Whether any signature meets all three is a
question, not part of the definition.

## What it is not

- Not Propp's character functions ([THEORY-167](../theory.d/THEORY-167.md)). Those are dramatis
  personae of the wondertale. This is the group-theoretic sense of
  "character", a trace.
- Not yet built. A194 named the construction as "The next formal
  challenge": an explicit communicative character "even for a small toy model
  of teasing, reprimand, and promise-making". No later turn constructs one.
- Not a factorization's latent coordinates. Those are fixed only up to a
  gauge, an invertible change of coordinates, which is not a symmetry of the
  relational system ([CLAIM-tmpro4wi](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpro4wi.md)). A signature must be a function of the
  data that does not depend on that choice.
- Not a genuine character when it is approximate. The characters of
  representations of a finite group are integer combinations of the
  irreducible characters, so for two of them ‖χ_A − χ_B‖² = Σ(m_i − n_i)² is
  a non-negative integer. A tolerance d_χ ≤ ε with ε < 1 then forces
  equality. An approximate signature must live in a continuous space:
  normalized characters, class functions in general, or traces of fitted
  operators. A194 §7's distance "based on differences between character
  values" falls into this trap if χ is read literally.
