# LIT-2982: Universal Fluctuations of Growing Interfaces: Evidence in Turbulent Liquid Crystals

Takeuchi & Sano (2010). Physical Review Letters 104, 230601. arXiv
1001.5121. DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.230601.

## Key takeaways

- The DSM2 front in a thin homeotropic MBBA cell at 26 V belongs to the
  (1+1)-dimensional KPZ class: α = 0.50(5), β = 0.336(11), Family–Vicsek
  collapse (p. 2).
- The local-radius fluctuations follow the GUE Tracy-Widom distribution
  "at least up to the fourth order cumulants"; skewness and kurtosis,
  "which do not depend on the parameter estimates", converge to about
  0.2241 and 0.09345 and rule out GOE and Gaussian (p. 3).
- The geometry picks the ensemble: GUE for curved fronts, GOE for flat
  (p. 3), "the geometry-dependent universality" of the class (p. 4).
- Experiments rarely find KPZ at all; this one does, the authors argue,
  because growth is strictly local, the noise is intrinsic and there is
  no quenched disorder (pp. 1, 4).
