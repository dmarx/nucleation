# LEAD-264: Read the published rivals of memetic nucleation, complex contagion, cultural attraction and dual-inheritance cultural evolution, for the threshold baseline a nucleation study must beat and the transmission models a seed thesis must exclude

## The gap

Article 7 §7.8 of the manuscript governance-of-becoming: the record has
read iterated learning, performativity, Merton, Land and Soros, and not
"Sperber's cultural attraction …, Boyd and Richerson, complex contagion".
"The baseline in [LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md) and the defeater of [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md) both
turn on complex contagion, so its absence is the gap that matters most.
Until it is read, the nucleation reading has no published rival on the
record to beat." By search of 2026-11-25 no LIT names Centola, Boyd or
Richerson, and no work of Sperber's on cultural transmission is
registered ([TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)'s note: "Sperber's works stay unregistered").

## What it would do

1. **Register and read** the core works, verifying each identifier
   first: for complex contagion, Centola and Macy's "Complex Contagions
   and the Weakness of Long Ties" (2007) and Centola's experiment on
   behaviour spreading in an online network (2010), with Granovetter's
   threshold models (1978) as their origin; for cultural attraction,
   Sperber's *Explaining Culture* (1996) and a later statement of
   attraction theory; for dual inheritance, Boyd and Richerson's
   *Culture and the Evolutionary Process* (1985). Which editions and
   papers is the reader's call, stated in the reading.
2. **For each, file** a THEORY with what its models produce: whether
   threshold adoption yields critical masses and hysteresis, so that "a
   lasting regime after withdrawal is not by itself evidence of
   nucleation" ([LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md), step 1); whether attraction explains
   agreement between source and carrier without transmission, which
   bears on [CLAIM-1285](../claims.d/CLAIM-1285.md)'s measure of fidelity (its note of
   2026-11-25); and which biases of transmission dual inheritance
   separates.
3. **Say what each rules out.** For [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)'s defeater, which
   rival models already produce the seed's effects, and what measure
   ([LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md)'s new persistent relations) none of them produces.

## What would count

A reading that shows threshold models produce persistence after
withdrawal with no relational excess meets [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)'s defeater for
those data, and fixes [LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md)'s baseline. A reading that finds a
measure the rival models cannot produce gives the nucleation thesis its
discriminating prediction. Either way the record gains the published
rival it lacks.

## Standing

Open, at stage idea. Checked first: no held lead reads these works;
[LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md) fits threshold models as its baseline and does not read
them.
