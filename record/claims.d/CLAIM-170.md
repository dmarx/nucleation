# CLAIM-170: The agents in a system are a factorization of it relative to a level of description and a target; agents in general are identified by adaptation, and willed agents are individuated by their will-organization: a willed agent is the jurisdiction of a will-organization

## The claim

The owner, verbatim, on 2026-10-11: "My suspicion is that what we consider an
'agent' is probably at least partly a function of not only a given context
but also a level of description. the agents in a given system are how we
factorize that system relative to a given level of description. I also
wonder/suspect if agents are identified by their will (and/or
will-organization)". Offered the reading "jurisdiction of a
will-organization", the owner answered: "yes, jurisdiction of a
will-organization".

As the record states it:
1. **Relativity.** Fix a level of description and a target. The agents are
   a factorization of the system: a partition of its regimes into factors,
   each described as an agent, with coupling between them.
2. **Individuation.** In version 1, every agent was a jurisdiction. In
   version 2, agents in general are identified by the agent tests
   ([TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md)). A *willed* agent ([TERM-048](../terms.d/TERM-048.md)) is the jurisdiction
   ([TERM-047](../terms.d/TERM-047.md)) of one will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)), meaning the regimes
   whose efficacy one maintained organization governs. The owner drew the
   distinction ([CLAIM-155](CLAIM-155.md)).
3. **Operative will is not enough.** Operative will is whichever regime is
   acting now, and it changes as drive moves ([CLAIM-316](CLAIM-316.md)). If agents
   were identified by it, the microbiome would become an agent whenever it
   drove hunger. A will-organization is maintained, so identity by
   jurisdiction is stable while drive moves within it.

## What follows

- **Different levels, different agents.** At the level of the room, a
  dancing couple mid-dance is one agent, if the dance's conventions govern
  both dancers' regimes. At the level of the pair it is two, a lead and a
  follow, each with its own organization. Neither factorization is wrong.
  They answer to different levels.
- **The microbiome is inside the person's body but outside the person's
  jurisdiction.** It is an input. Whether it is part of the combined form ([TERM-049](../terms.d/TERM-049.md)) is
  the threshold question, and whether it is an agent is a separate question
  at its own level.
- **A firm is an agent to the extent its governance decides which teams'
  regimes act.** That is the Northstar case ([CASE-023](../cases.d/CASE-023.md)).

## What it does not say

- **How to tell whether a factor has a will-organization.** That is the
  admission test, [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md).
- **That relativity makes agency a matter of taste.** Given a level and a
  target, a factorization can be wrong.
- **That it is free of circularity.** [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) is defined for an agent
  already given, and this claim reverses the order ([CLAIM-168](CLAIM-168.md)).

## Note of 2026-10-29: what holds it back

[CLAIM-168](CLAIM-168.md) is unanswered in the part that bears on this claim. [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md) turns
the circle into a fixed-point condition, but it says itself that whether a
fixed level and target can have several self-consistent factorizations is the
objection's open part, and several that disagree on which regimes each
will-organization governs is this claim's `defeated_if`. [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md) is also
Proposed, so even that reply is not held.
