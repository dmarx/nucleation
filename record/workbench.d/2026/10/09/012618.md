references filed and read first, without the manuscript, so that each reading

2026-10-09). The manuscript came afterwards, and this session puts it beside

**What the readings say against it.** Five points, each filed as a granted
claim that objects to the thesis it qualifies. None of them defeats a thesis.
Each says the manuscript cites a work for more than the work shows, or for
something slightly different.

- **Saussure** ([CLAIM-072](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-072.md)). "Purely differential" is said of signifier
  and signified taken apart; the sign as a whole is "un fait positif". Value
  is not signification, and the referent is not Saussure's contrast at all.
- **Contextuality-by-Default** ([CLAIM-069](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-069.md)). The 2016 criterion was
  superseded by multimaximal couplings. That leaves the parity example alone,
  but not the general cover.
- **Baez and Fong** ([CLAIM-094](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-094.md)). Kf = f is the mean-only condition. The
  cited theorem's own counterexample shows mean conservation is weaker than
  symmetry.
- **Iterated learning** ([CLAIM-066](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-066.md)). Kirby, Cornish and Smith measured
  no prior, and their structure came from the experimenter's homonym filter.
  Griffiths and Kalish's convergence to the prior holds for sampling learners
  only.
- **T2I-CompBench** ([CLAIM-097](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-097.md)). It has an interaction category, scored
  by CLIPScore and near human ceiling. That category is the baseline Case III
  extends, not an object-centered one.

**What the readings support.**

- Baez and Fong is cited accurately, diagonal restriction included.
- The direction of Blackwell's theorem the manuscript uses is the right one.
  The 1953 paper also anticipates the restricted family Q: its k-decision
  orders, and Theorem 10 for two states.
- The hedges on Composable Diffusion are better than the paper's own. The
  paper never examines conditional independence at the noisy state.
- Both text-to-image readings back the premise that embedding similarity
  misses binding and relations.
- Du Bois is paraphrased fairly. In the reading, the stance triangle is one
  act with two evaluative sides and an alignment scale.

    Φ[A,B] − [A′,B′]Φ = E_A B + A′E_B − E_B A − B′E_A. Expanding ΦAB and ΦBA
