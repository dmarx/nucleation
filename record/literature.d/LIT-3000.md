  10.1162/neco.2007.12-06-414. In two-layer networks under averaged
  online gradient descent, the flow near an overlap singularity has a
  universal first integral, whose constant decides whether learning
  reaches the line of coinciding units or, as in most cases, the
  elimination singularity. The on-singularity plateau is a noise-driven
  random walk along a Milnor-like attracting line of equivalent models;
  the near-singularity plateau, shorter, is a crossing where the loss
  changes at fourth order. No time course is derived.

# LIT-3000: Dynamics of Learning Near Singularities in Layered Networks

Wei, Zhang, Cousseau, Ozeki & Amari (2008). Neural Computation 20, 813–843. DOI 10.1162/neco.2007.12-06-414.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A invokes it without naming it at A37, A39 for learning dynamics near singularities and the plateaus they cause. Read the same day (NOTE-tmpl89m0) and filed as THEORY-tmpg4aat. It derives orbits, not time courses, so it is moved out of the grounds of CLAIM-811 and CLAIM-844 (ARG-tmp0cg6w); its on-singularity plateau, a noise-driven walk along a line of equivalent models, is a third rival to criticality and algebraic relaxation as an account of slow training.
