reinforcement learning. It held almost nothing: game-theoretic and
organizational neighbours, no MARL. The owner then asked for the core works
to be registered and read in a new pull request. Four sub-agents of this
session each registered and read one group under a shared brief: Crossref
or arXiv checks, lawful copies only, every line read except references,
quotations checked by script, and key proofs re-derived where feasible.

**What the readings corrected in the lead's descriptions.**
- Cross learning follows the replicator dynamic only in expectation and at
  finite horizons. Actual runs lock on to pure profiles.
- "Replicator plus entropy" comes from forgetting under softmax. In plain
  Q-learning, bandit feedback adds a frequency factor.
- Regret matching reaches the set of correlated equilibria in time
  average, not a point.
- Nash averaging resists copies only with a unique equilibrium. A
  counterexample was re-derived when filed.
- α-Rank's limit ranking is not characterized, and it is sensitive to
  copies; that second point is the reader's finding.
- The spinning-top geometry is empirical, not proved, and one proof in
  that paper is defective.
- COMA's counterfactual is the agent's own policy-averaged alternative,
  with the others held fixed.
- IGM and monotone mixing are not currency conditions. They are ordinal
  separability conditions.
- LOLA's shaping was benign and symmetric.
- Peer prediction's truthful equilibrium is one of several.
- Mixture-of-experts load balancing is imposed diversity, not protected
  access.
- Gating in Jacobs et al. shows competence-earned authority, not collapse.
- Mean-field MARL needs interchangeable agents.
- The social-dilemma environments have no depletable commons and no
  protective regime.

**Reader findings, not the papers'.**
- The Nash-averaging counterexample, re-derived when filed.
- α-Rank's sensitivity to copies. Not re-checked.
- The acyclic quotient of the win graph.
- Sato & Crutchfield's rest points as logit equilibria.
