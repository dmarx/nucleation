The owner asked for a PR that "approves and operationalizes" the proposed
warrant field ([ADR-043](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-043.md)). It is now Active.

**What was done.** A closed vocabulary `claim-warrants` (demonstrated,
empirical, interpretive) and an optional `warrant` field on CLAIM in
`luria.yaml`; the CLAIM template documents it and sets it by default; the
map in CLAUDE.md names it beside status and role. The pilot the ADR asked
for was run on twenty-six claims: the thirteen of the 2026-10-14 exchange,
A9's three older ones, and the claims filed on 2026-10-15. The table and
its findings are in [ADR-043](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-043.md).

**The split.** [CLAIM-298](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-298.md) was compound, as the proposal had already noticed.
Rather than supersede it, its empirical half was filed as [CLAIM-tmp3bdbx](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3bdbx.md),
scoped to the comparative-question paradigm, and [CLAIM-298](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-298.md) now rests on it
and is marked interpretive. Its title already stated the general claim, so
nothing in the argument that cites it moves.

**Two decisions the pilot made.** `unestablished` is dropped, since every
claim it would have marked was interpretive and Proposed. And a claim that
cites a theorem but concludes something further is interpretive, not
demonstrated ([CLAIM-199](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-199.md), [CLAIM-314](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-314.md)); that rule is in the ADR so the next
classifier does not have to rediscover it.

**Required, and the whole record classified.** The first version of the
PR left the field optional because about three hundred claims were
unclassified. The owner: "There's no point adding this if we don't use
it", and "'failing' is a forcing function to make you fill in the field.
this is a feature, not a bug." The field is now required on every CLAIM.
The other 289 claims were classified by eight readers in parallel, one
batch each, and applied: with the pilot, 87 demonstrated, 12 empirical and
216 interpretive. The readers disagreed over untested empirical
hypotheses; four were set to interpretive so that `empirical` means some
observation the record holds supports the claim. Nineteen claims were
flagged compound and given the warrant of their main conclusion; four are
already split in effect by a separate claim, and fifteen are listed in
[ADR-043](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-043.md) for the owner. Two readers found their scratch files overwritten by
others working in the same directory; both re-read their claims from the
record, and every batch's output was checked to cover exactly its claims.

**Not built.** The lint for a demonstrated claim resting on an
undemonstrated one, because that pattern is legitimate when the premise is
stated as a condition.
