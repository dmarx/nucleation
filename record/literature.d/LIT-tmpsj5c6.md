# LIT-tmpsj5c6: A Perspective on Incentive Design: Challenges and Opportunities

Ratliff, L. J., Dong, R., Sekar, S. & Fiez, T. (2019). A Perspective on Incentive Design: Challenges and Opportunities. *Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems* 2: 305–338. doi:10.1146/annurev-control-053018-023634.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, NOTE-tmp5upz4. Page numbers are the manuscript's 1–37, not the journal's.*

- **Three partial toolkits.** Economics works "in static environments" and
  is "very heavily model-based" (p. 4); control's adaptive results need
  "stable and persistently exciting dynamics—the latter of which is very
  difficult to verify" (p. 18); online learning assumes i.i.d., stationary
  environments, "both of which are far removed from reality" (p. 5).
- **The open problem.** "it is still an open problem to solve incentive
  design with repeatedly returning agents whose decision-making processes
  evolve with time" (§4, p. 24).
- **The ratchet, unnamed.** "an agent who interacts with the principal for
  multiple rounds may seek to benefit by resorting to low effort levels if
  it can influence the payment mechanism offered in subsequent rounds"
  (§3.3.2, p. 23), raised as unaddressed, with no citation to the ratchet
  literature.
