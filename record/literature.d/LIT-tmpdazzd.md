# LIT-tmpdazzd: Contestable AI by Design: Towards a Framework

Alfrink, Keller, Kortuem, Doorn (2022). *Contestable AI by Design: Towards a Framework*. Minds and Machines 33(4): 613–639 (2023). DOI 10.1007/s11023-022-09611-z. Open access, CC BY.

## Key takeaways

A design framework for contestable AI: five features and six practices from a systematic review. Contestability before a decision takes effect comes through built-in safeguards (decisions pass a second system, disagreement flagged for review), interactive override and ex-ante contestation of design choices; contestation by subjects of discrete decisions is necessarily post hoc. Untested.
