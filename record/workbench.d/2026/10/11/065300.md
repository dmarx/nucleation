**The owner's prompt**, verbatim: "do the sweep in this PR too".

**Scope.** All 175 claims were scanned by script for "self", "agent", "will"
and the combined-form vocabulary, and each hit was read in context. Most hits
on "self" were compounds: self-control, self-consistent, self-map, Levin's
"Self", IFS's "Self". They were left alone.

**What the sweep found and changed.**
- **A missing sense.** The founding phrase of the session, "the self as a
  multi-agent system", uses "self" in [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s behavioral-integration sense.
  [TERM-045](../../record/terms.d/TERM-045.md) had mapped only three of [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s four unities, so version 2
  adds sense 6, *integrated*. That is the second real gap the concept pass
  surfaced, after the two senses of δ/ε.
- **Two claims whose sense of "self" was load-bearing and unstated** now say
  which: [CLAIM-169](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) (integrated) and [CLAIM-172](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-172.md) (subject). Each got a
  version bump for that one change.
- **`uses:` links** were added where "self" or "agent" carries the argument
  ([CLAIM-157](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-157.md), [CLAIM-167](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-167.md), [CLAIM-175](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-175.md)). They were also added to
  two older will-line claims stated in will-organization without the link
  ([CLAIM-021](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-021.md), [CLAIM-122](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-122.md)).
- **The older claims' "agent"** is recorded in [TERM-044](../../record/terms.d/TERM-044.md) (version 2), not
  rewritten. The will paper and the authenticity line use "agent" for an
  agent *supposed* ([CLAIM-080](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-080.md)). [CLAIM-104](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-104.md)'s "agents at one level" already
  makes agency level-relative.

**Left alone on purpose.** The text of the older essay claims. Their
"agent" is the paper's term of art, and the TERM now says how it relates to
the tiers. Rewriting them would change what the will paper said.
