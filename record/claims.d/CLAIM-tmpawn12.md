# CLAIM-tmpawn12: For a communicative kind, the standard of sufficiency is the uptake of competent participants who know the standings and norms in play, so the judges are the criterion a probe family is tested against, and a kind outruns only the families coarser than that standard

## The claim

This is the record's reply of 2026-10-10 to [CLAIM-tmp6r6t6](CLAIM-tmp6r6t6.md), and it supplies
the argument [CLAIM-tmpsxsr8](CLAIM-tmpsxsr8.md) found missing. It reconciles more than it
refutes, and it is a proposal: it rests on [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md), which is Proposed, and
its defeat condition is empirical.

The argument has four steps.

1. **The record never says a kind outruns every family.** The objection's
   first horn supposes it can. What the record says is narrower. [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md) and
   [CLAIM-135](CLAIM-135.md) both quote A218 Ch7: "A structural kind may be real while
   remaining indistinguishable under an insufficient family of empirical
   probes." The kind outruns an *insufficient* family.
2. **"Insufficient" needs a standard.** A family is insufficient only
   relative to something it fails to separate. So the record's own wording
   presupposes a standard of what must be separated, beyond any family it
   calls insufficient.
3. **For a communicative kind, competent uptake is that standard.** On
   [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md), which act an utterance performs is "partly constituted by its
   relations to participants, norms and expected uptake". Whether a remark
   was teasing or reprimand is then partly fixed by how competent
   participants, who know who is speaking to whom and under what norms, take
   it. Their same-act verdicts are not one more probe of a fact fixed
   elsewhere. They are part of what makes the fact. So the judges, when they
   are such participants and know the standings in play, are the criterion
   a probe family is tested against.
4. **Hence the tests.** Analysts' relational probes (factorizations,
   signatures, response profiles) are checked against the standard's
   verdicts. A family is sufficient for a kind when it separates what the
   standard separates, and insufficient when it is coarser. That is what
   [CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md), restated in version 2, and [CLAIM-129](CLAIM-129.md) test. [QUESTION-031](../questions.d/QUESTION-031.md) asks
   when a family is rich enough; this says rich enough relative to what.

**The first horn fails.** It supposed a kind that might outrun the judges as
it outruns any other family. On step 3, competent uptake is not a family the
kind can outrun, because it is partly constitutive of the kind. The judges'
verdicts can therefore confirm and defeat claims about kinds.

**The second horn is granted and disarmed.** In the tests the kind's
extension is the class the standard fixes. That is an observational class
relative to one family, the competent participants' ([TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md)). What the
objection takes to follow, that [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)'s distinction then does no work in
any test, does not. The distinction separates the standard's class from
coarser analysts' classes, and every test the programme runs is a test of
whether an analyst's family reaches the standard. [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)'s gloss "An
equivalence class relative to a family of measurements cannot be" (that is,
cannot be unseparated by an insufficient family) holds only of the same
family, and is corrected in [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md) version 2.

**The misfiring condition.** The objection's trivial probe, "ask the judges
whether the two realizations are the same act", meets [CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md)'s version 1
condition because it is the standard, not a probe. [CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md)'s version 2
excludes probes that elicit or are fitted to the judges' verdicts.

## Reconciling the two stances

[CLAIM-tmpsxsr8](CLAIM-tmpsxsr8.md) shows that the bearer of identity moved, from an operational
object defined on observed responses ([TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md); [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md)) to a kind that
can outrun observation ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)), with no argument. The argument above
reconciles the two without a contradiction.

- [CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md)'s rule of method stands: evidence comes from observable
  responses, and competent participants' verdicts are observable responses.
- [TERM-014](../terms.d/TERM-014.md)'s class, relative to an analyst's chosen family, is the projection
  of the kind onto that family.
- [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)'s kind is the class the standard fixes.

[CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md) named this in one sentence, "What changes is what that evidence is
*of*", and did not argue it. Step 3 is the argument: the evidence is of the
class competent uptake fixes, because uptake partly constitutes the act.

## What it does not say

- It does not say participants are infallible about institutional status. A
  participant who does not know a speaker is a judge may misread a sentence.
  Hence "with the standings and norms in play made known to them".
- It does not say the standard is one population. It is indexed to a
  population, and which population is [QUESTION-tmppstva](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmppstva.md)'s.
- It does not say relations constitute the kind wholesale ([CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md)).
  [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md) says "partly", and this entry inherits the qualifier.
- It does not reach [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s ontological clause. A kind that outruns
  even competent uptake is not excluded by argument here; it is simply not
  what any test needs.
