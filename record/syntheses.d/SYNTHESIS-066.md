# SYNTHESIS-066: The pragmatic-transport line is twelve part accounts, of communicative kinds, of fidelity relative to receivers, of the communicative object and its obstructions, of what the line adds and how it is tested, of language models as a test bed, of composition in conditional generation, of directed transport by kernels, of the act a rendering keeps besides its proposition, of Saussure's value, of frames and the judgement–observation loop, of reconstruction chains, and of conservation under kernels, with eleven moved promotion conditions integrated directly; the parts meet at what survives a rendering, which most of their questions refine, at the anchoring of a correspondence, at fidelity as a profile and at the frame as a configuration; four claims are filed by derivation and no link between the parts passes the claim test

*The account of the pragmatic-transport line, over the accounts of its
parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). It integrates every entry whose `line` includes
pragmatic-transport on 2026-11-11: 391 in-force entries when the review
began, and the four claims it filed, 384 of them through twelve part
accounts and 11 directly. A mechanical community map of the line (stored
relations and mentions) gave eight parts; reading them, three fell into
two or three inquiries each, so there are twelve accounts. Two entries
moved between the map's parts: [CLAIM-257](../claims.d/CLAIM-257.md) from contextuality to fidelity,
and [TERM-011](../terms.d/TERM-011.md) from fidelity to frames, and the study part took [QUESTION-024](../questions.d/QUESTION-024.md)
from contextuality. The Superseded entries that carry the line,
[SYNTHESIS-005](SYNTHESIS-005.md) and [SYNTHESIS-011](SYNTHESIS-011.md) among them, are not integrated.*

## The parts

- **Communicative kinds ([SYNTHESIS-068](SYNTHESIS-068.md)), 78 entries.** A
  communicative kind is a repeatable relational organization that
  distinct events realize, proposed as partly constituted by relations to
  roles, norms and expected uptake ([CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md), [CLAIM-204](../claims.d/CLAIM-204.md), both Proposed),
  and recognized through probes that fix it only relative to the family
  used. Its hubs are [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md), [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md), [QUESTION-029](../questions.d/QUESTION-029.md), [CLAIM-137](../claims.d/CLAIM-137.md) and
  [CLAIM-217](../claims.d/CLAIM-217.md).
- **Fidelity relative to receivers ([SYNTHESIS-064](SYNTHESIS-064.md)), 77 entries.**
  Blackwell's order on rendering procedures sets the ceiling a rendering puts on report decisions ([CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md), [CLAIM-199](../claims.d/CLAIM-199.md)); fidelity is a profile of directed
  distortions ([TERM-043](../terms.d/TERM-043.md)) that only a task's weighting ranks ([CLAIM-128](../claims.d/CLAIM-128.md), Proposed);
  and it is relative to the receivers' decisions ([CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md)). It holds the
  line's root question, [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md), and the claim filed with it,
  [CLAIM-636](../claims.d/CLAIM-636.md).
- **The communicative object ([SYNTHESIS-069](SYNTHESIS-069.md)), 57 entries.** A
  communicative object is a context-indexed system of empirical
  constraints over a cover of contexts ([CLAIM-038](../claims.d/CLAIM-038.md)), whose global
  realizability is to be found out; local descriptions that disagree are
  not thereby incompatible ([CLAIM-143](../claims.d/CLAIM-143.md)); ambiguity and contextuality differ
  ([CLAIM-044](../claims.d/CLAIM-044.md), Proposed); and the behavioural data read show no
  contextuality proper.
- **What the line adds and how it is tested ([SYNTHESIS-070](SYNTHESIS-070.md)), 24
  entries.** What the line adds is placed, after conceded prior art, in a
  testable account ([CLAIM-202](../claims.d/CLAIM-202.md), [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md), both Proposed) whose
  discriminating study ([CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md)) has not been run.
- **Language models as a test bed ([SYNTHESIS-059](SYNTHESIS-059.md)), 8 entries.** A
  test bed only with generator and evaluator separated, stimuli held out
  and agreement across model families.
- **Composition in conditional generation ([SYNTHESIS-061](SYNTHESIS-061.md)), 9
  entries.** A case for the theory and not a realization of it.
- **Directed transport by kernels ([SYNTHESIS-063](SYNTHESIS-063.md)), 40 entries.**
  Directed transport keeps overlap consistency and global extendability under two sufficient conditions, but constrains a translation only as a property of a rendering procedure ([CLAIM-207](../claims.d/CLAIM-207.md); [CLAIM-228](../claims.d/CLAIM-228.md), Proposed); a transport is admissible only if it keeps specified distinctions apart ([CLAIM-077](../claims.d/CLAIM-077.md)), and intertwining with an uninformative correspondence is vacuous or lossy ([CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md)). Decision-relevant information across a change of cover stays open ([CLAIM-125](../claims.d/CLAIM-125.md)). It integrates the two claims filed with it, [CLAIM-637](../claims.d/CLAIM-637.md)
  and [CLAIM-638](../claims.d/CLAIM-638.md).
- **The act a rendering keeps ([SYNTHESIS-058](SYNTHESIS-058.md)), 27 entries.**
  Renderings that keep the proposition can perform a different act
  ([CLAIM-061](../claims.d/CLAIM-061.md)), and no measure of footing or stance is validated.
- **Saussure's value ([SYNTHESIS-065](SYNTHESIS-065.md)), 7 entries.** Value is fixed by
  contrast within one language state; whether it supports transport is
  open.
- **Frames and the loop ([SYNTHESIS-062](SYNTHESIS-062.md)), 34 entries.** A frame is an
  operation on an interpretive state ([TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md)); telling an interpreter
  about a situation is a different intervention from changing it
  ([CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md)); and what keeps a judgement–observation loop from
  confirming itself is evidence that does not pass through the
  interpretation it tests ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)). Several of its members are first
  in distributed-agency.
- **Reconstruction chains ([SYNTHESIS-067](SYNTHESIS-067.md)), 17 entries.** Local
  transport errors along a chain are bounded by per-step discrepancies
  weighted by later sensitivity ([CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md)), and repeated reconstruction
  is proposed as attraction toward conventional regions ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md),
  Proposed).
- **Conservation under kernels ([SYNTHESIS-060](SYNTHESIS-060.md)), 6 entries.** A
  conservation law for reconstruction kernels has to be derived
  ([CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md)), and the claim filed with it, [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md), says exact
  conservation keeps information about the start.

## Claims filed at this review

Each passed [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s step 5 by derivation, and each says in its
"Admission" section that it is a short composition, which is the owner's
to weigh.

- **[CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)** (conservation): an exactly conserved observable that
  is not constant where the chain starts keeps information about the
  start at every generation, so such a chain cannot settle on a law
  independent of its start.
- **[CLAIM-637](../claims.d/CLAIM-637.md)** (kernels): the restated observational distortion and
  its Le Cam deficiency vanish exactly on garblings, so they charge a
  rendering nothing for what it loses.
- **[CLAIM-638](../claims.d/CLAIM-638.md)** (kernels): under one kernel for both orders, a
  rendering's order effect can grow by at most its two distortions;
  kernels fitted per order bound nothing.
- **[CLAIM-636](../claims.d/CLAIM-636.md)** (fidelity): where a literal rendering loses nothing
  on the declared report problems, a compensating rendering made from the
  source alone is never the better report for an ideal receiver.
