# CLAIM-tmplz7ng: An evolving inquiry can be understood as an atlas of partial representations whose objects and relations must stay identifiable across changes of perspective, purpose and grammar; progress can consist in finding distinctions or transformations that improve compatibility among the representations, and since their adequacy is itself under inquiry the atlas must be governed rather than assumed fixed, so class stability is a relation across representations and not a demand that one classification stay unchanged

## The claim

A49: "An evolving inquiry can be understood as an atlas of partial
representations whose objects and relationships must remain identifiable
across changes of perspective, purpose, and representational grammar.
Progress may consist not only in adding information within an existing
representation, but in discovering distinctions or transformations that
improve compatibility among representations, allowing an inquiry to
preserve and reuse its results across contexts. Because the adequacy of
these representations is itself subject to inquiry, the atlas must be
governed rather than assumed fixed." And: "class stability becomes a
relationship across representations, not a demand that any one
classification remain unchanged."

A49's correspondence: the underlying space is "The developing inquiry
and its intellectual objects"; a chart "A representation organized for a
particular task or conceptual framework"; coordinates "The categories,
vocabulary, and relations available in that representation"; a
transition map "Rules for relating the descriptions across
representations"; the compatibility condition "Constraints on what must
remain consistent across overlapping descriptions". Hyperarticles are
then "one chart over a region of the inquiry", and the three articles of
U21 "different charts over overlapping regions of a larger inquiry".

## A49's own limit

"A differential-geometric atlas assumes mathematically well-defined
compatible coordinate transitions. Research representations don't
necessarily have that property", so compatibility "should be something
the record can represent, test, challenge, and revise", and "In ordinary
differential geometry, the space is given and the charts describe it. In
inquiry, that relationship is less straightforward." What follows from
that limit, and what the record's design already does about it, is the
assistant's gap fill [CLAIM-tmp6xzjy](CLAIM-tmp6xzjy.md).
