# SYNTHESIS-tmpfhzxg: What learning is and what it does to what can still be learned: learning as an experience-dependent change in a costed repertoire; three frontiers, operational, developmental and inquiry, that move separately; overconsolidation, the owner's generalization of overfitting, better now but more costly to teach new tasks, with a supposed deadline for its relief and a proposed Pythia study; and the owner's open questions on optimal learning and on what it is for a learning system to know

## The account

- **What learning is.** [TERM-tmp1ix99](../terms.d/TERM-tmp1ix99.md) (A's; Proposed): an
  experience-dependent change in retained organization that alters what a
  system can later do, at what cost and how reliably, judged against a
  counterfactual, and kept apart from relaxation, selection and a fixed
  controller.
- **Three frontiers.** [CLAIM-tmp1l2s7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1l2s7.md) (A's; Proposed, admitted as a
  discriminating prediction at this review) rests on [CLAIM-697](../claims.d/CLAIM-697.md) (the
  owner's idea, A's formalization; Proposed), [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md) (Active),
  [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) (Active) and [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) (the owner's; Active), and uses
  [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)'s inquiry frontier. The operational frontier is [CLAIM-697](../claims.d/CLAIM-697.md)'s
  resource-conditional disposition; the developmental frontier, what can
  be learned within an acquisition budget and a declared learning
  interface, is the claim's own; the three move separately.
- **Overconsolidation.** [CLAIM-tmp8ljvp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ljvp.md) (the owner's generalization of
  overfitting, U22, in A's wording; Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) and
  [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md): continued stabilization can raise present competence while
  raising the cost of later acquisition, without overfitting, and is a
  failure only relative to a declared family and horizon (version 2,
  after the readings of 2026-11-19: the family lies outside the training
  task distribution, and acquisition is priced with held capabilities
  kept, so collateral forgetting counts), as [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md) says
  of a corrective capacity. Its signature is [CLAIM-tmp1l2s7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1l2s7.md)'s: the
  operational frontier grows while the developmental one does not contain
  its predecessor. [CLAIM-695](../claims.d/CLAIM-695.md) (the owner's idea; Proposed) is its RL case.
- **A deadline for relief.** [CLAIM-tmp5vg7p](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5vg7p.md) (the assistant's, accepted by
  the owner; Proposed, version 2 after the readings) rests on [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)
  and [CLAIM-tmp8ljvp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ljvp.md): if the capacity to acquire decays with disuse,
  renewed practice restores it before a threshold and not after; if
  training uses it up, practice never restores it and reinitialization
  does at any point. Dohare and colleagues' plasticity loss fits the
  second.
- **The test.** [CASE-tmphsr99](../cases.d/CASE-tmphsr99.md) (A's design at the owner's request; not run)
  measures acquisition cost across Pythia checkpoints, with arms for
  in-context learning, test-time training, sampling, meta-learning and
  active acquisition; [CLAIM-tmp5vg7p](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5vg7p.md)'s per-checkpoint interventions are
  among them, and [CLAIM-tmp1l2s7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1l2s7.md)'s admission names it.
- **Open.** [QUESTION-tmpwqepn](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpwqepn.md) (the owner's, U20–U21): what would make
  learning optimal when its most valuable consequences cannot be
  specified in advance. [QUESTION-tmpo8ddc](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpo8ddc.md) (the owner's, U33): what it is
  for a learning system to know, reopening what [TERM-079](../terms.d/TERM-079.md) set aside.
  [QUESTION-tmprj00l](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmprj00l.md) (A's): which partition a physically costed memory
  keeps under feedback.
