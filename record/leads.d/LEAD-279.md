# LEAD-279: Join learned excision to self-certification: let the institution that learns to stop inquiring choose whether to excise or to revise a demand the specification does not name, and score each run against the original specification, its own and the true demands

## The gap

- **[CASE-396](../cases.d/CASE-396.md)** (Active): an institution penalized for every recognized
  violation, and unrewarded for discovery, "learns to make no inquiry in
  every setting while its internal record shows almost no violations"
  ([CLAIM-1064](../claims.d/CLAIM-1064.md), Active, demonstrated). The choice not to look is learned.
- **[CASE-397](../cases.d/CASE-397.md)** (Active): a demand the specification does not name enters
  the matched-performance model mid-run; an organization that never enters
  it "scores zero violations against the original specification and its
  own while the harm runs for all 120 periods, so only the true demand set
  ranks excision below revision". The choice is set by the modeller.

Together they would show an organization that learns to excise and then
certifies itself clean on its own scorecard. Neither shows it.

## The design, fixed before any run

1. **[CASE-397](../cases.d/CASE-397.md)'s world**: 50 quiet periods, then holding the current
   configuration causes a harm the specification does not name, with a
   harm-free configuration reachable over a path of peak 2.5, under
   [CASE-412](../cases.d/CASE-412.md)'s regimes and reserves.
2. **[CASE-396](../cases.d/CASE-396.md)'s learner chooses**: at the new demand, the institution
   chooses between never entering it (excise) and reviewing it for three
   periods and then meeting it (revise), learning from the score it is
   given, under [CASE-396](../cases.d/CASE-396.md)'s governance regimes (the penalty on recognized
   violations with discovery unrewarded; internal scoring without the
   penalty; an independent evaluator).
3. **Three scorecards** for every run: the original specification, the
   organization's own, and the true demands.

## Expectations, stated before any run

- Under the penalty with discovery unrewarded, the learner excises, and
  scores clean on the first two cards while the harm runs ([CLAIM-1064](../claims.d/CLAIM-1064.md)'s
  mechanism choosing [CASE-397](../cases.d/CASE-397.md)'s excision).
- Under an independent evaluator, it revises where the reserve allows the
  review to start ([CASE-397](../cases.d/CASE-397.md)'s peak rule: "a review that needs capacity can
  never start" without one).
- **Defeaters**, as checks: the learner revises under the penalty (the
  penalty on the new demand's recognized violations is outweighed by
  something [CASE-396](../cases.d/CASE-396.md) lacked), or the learned choice tracks the reserve and
  not the scoring regime, which would make excision a matter of
  capacity, not of certification.
