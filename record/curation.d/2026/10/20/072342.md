The owner asked for the three entries proposed in the curation entry of
06:48 today to be filed.

- **[CASE-121](../../record/cases.d/CASE-121.md)**, the melody chain in Boyle's *The Public Domain*: from
  the Bailey Gospel Singers' "I've Got a Savior" through Ray Charles,
  Kanye West and Jamie Foxx to The Legendary K.O. It illustrates [CLAIM-002](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-002.md)
  and [CLAIM-210](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-210.md). Its three links are three different relations: a
  rewording, a licensed re-performance and a sample.
- **[CASE-122](../../record/cases.d/CASE-122.md)**, *Warhol v. Goldsmith*: one rendering, measured by
  the purpose of its use (the majority) and by its meaning (the
  dissent), with opposite verdicts. It illustrates [CLAIM-034](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-034.md) and
  [CLAIM-210](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-210.md) and bears on [QUESTION-002](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-002.md).
- **[CLAIM-393](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-393.md)**, a counter to [CLAIM-174](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) from Boyle's
  inscrutability paradox in *The Line*. It does not dispute the test. It
  says public and legal attributions of agency to artifacts track
  whether the mechanism is understood, so they cannot be the
  "independent marks" [CLAIM-174](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-174.md)'s defeater appeals to.

Each case uses `illustrates`, not `grounds`. Neither was asked to bear
weight, and adding grounds would change the claims' own entries.

Checking corrected the draft in three places:

- Several of Boyle's page numbers were off. The Legendary K.O. story is
  at pp. 125–126 and 144, not 143–146.
- The *Warhol* case's first draft said the other tribute covers did not
  copy Goldsmith. People's cover was another Goldsmith photograph, used
  under licence.
- "Merely programmed" was the record's paraphrase. It is replaced with
  Boyle's own words.
