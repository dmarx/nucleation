# THEORY-tmpmnv43: Battiston and colleagues report that phase reduction of oscillators with purely pairwise physical coupling yields nonpairwise phase interactions at higher order in the coupling, which capture indirect influence, so that the order of the physical and of the effective interactions can differ

## Source

Battiston, Bick, Lucas, Millán, Skardal and Zhang (2026; arXiv 2025),
LIT-2239, skimmed in NOTE-tmpejuy1.

## What was actually shown

Nothing new: a review stating, with references, that higher-order phase
reductions of pairwise-coupled oscillators contain triplet and quadruplet
terms, with Ashwin and Rodrigues's phase equations as an example of such
terms.

## What this does not say

Which physical systems need the higher-order terms, or how large they
are.
