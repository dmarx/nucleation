## Reading of 2026-11-16

Read in [NOTE-1997](../notes.d/NOTE-1997.md); [THEORY-1944](../theory.d/THEORY-1944.md) states its finding. A21 §4's description is accurate with caveats: the drop in
perceived discretion is significant at .10 only, and "enforcement behavior" is self-reported style. The
earlier LIT summary misread the result: style did change; what was not found is that perceived discretion
explains it. For [CLAIM-710](../claims.d/CLAIM-710.md) it offers one detail: INSPECT put a supervisor's permission in front of any
departure from its verdict (p. 49), adding friction to the override rather than removing friction from
the decision. Nothing bears on [CLAIM-705](../claims.d/CLAIM-705.md).
