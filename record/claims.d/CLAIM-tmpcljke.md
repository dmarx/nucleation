# CLAIM-tmpcljke: A plurality of pseudo-selves can be ecologically unified without being a pseudo-self, and what marks the difference is whether some whole-level slow variable is maintained as the whole's: members that read it, condition on it and regenerate it make the plurality integrated, while members coupled only through each other's conditions of existence make it an ecosystem

## The claim

TERM-tmpxwa7g asks of a pseudo-self that its carriers be causally integrated:
each perspective contributes to maintaining coherent activity. CLAIM-tmp37le7
allows a self-like organization to be unified ecologically, its
differentiated perspectives "viable, mutually consequential and able to
reorganize their relations", with no single perspective. An ecosystem of
pseudo-selves can meet the second without the first: its members are
mutually consequential through each other's conditions of existence (prices,
supply, shared infrastructure), yet there may be no activity of the whole
whose coherence their coupling maintains.

The criterion offered: CLAIM-327's slow collective variable, one "its
members read …, condition what they do on …, and by doing so regenerate". A
plurality is integrated, a candidate pseudo-self, when such a variable exists
at the level of the whole (a firm's culture, a team's plan, a sector's
standard held as its own); it is an ecosystem when its members are coupled
and no such variable is regenerated as the whole's.

## Admission

By a discriminating prediction. Under a fixed regime, adopting a shared
standard that members regenerate should move an industrial ecosystem across
a threshold on the integration diagnostic, from coupled to integrated, and
removing it should move it back. CLAIM-tmpptr3w predicts no threshold, only a
change with the horizon. LEAD-tmplrmhl would test it.

## What it does not say

- Not that an ecosystem lacks unity: on CLAIM-tmp37le7 it can have
  ecological unity.
- Not that the criterion is regime-free: the variable is identified under a
  declared probe family and horizon, as TERM-tmpxwa7g requires.

## Note of 2026-11-23: defeated as worded

The owner's objection CLAIM-tmpt93dp, read by the assistant in CLAIM-
tmps5yc5, defeats this claim as worded: a predator–prey oscillation is a
whole-level slow variable an ecosystem's members read, condition on and
regenerate, so CLAIM-327's criterion does not separate a pseudo-self from an
ecosystem. Two alternatives follow: CLAIM-tmpklms4 narrows the criterion to
a variable members address as the whole's own; CLAIM-tmpptr3w lets the
difference collapse into the regime of analysis. The owner chooses. The
claim's status is left for the owner.
