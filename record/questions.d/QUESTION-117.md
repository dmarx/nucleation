# QUESTION-117: Does coupling the construction of meaning to the allocation of interpretive authority produce modes, attractors or transitions that established models of opinion dynamics with evolving influence (reflected appraisal, adaptive networks, persuasion bias) cannot reproduce as economically?

## Why it is a question

A15: "**Does coupling the construction of meaning to the allocation of
interpretive authority produce causal modes, attractors, or transitions
that cannot be reproduced as economically by established models of
opinion formation with evolving influence networks?**" It is the
explanatory-novelty half of A15's question; the other half, whether such
modes persist through turnover and individuate an organization, is
[QUESTION-115](QUESTION-115.md) and [QUESTION-118](QUESTION-118.md). A15: "We can establish the first
without establishing the second."

The candidate predecessors, as A15 described them (registered, unread):

- **Reflected appraisal** (Jia, MirTabatabaei, Friedkin and Bullo, [LIT-1146](../literature.d/LIT-1146.md)): opinions
  converge on each issue, and each member's social power on the next
  issue is set by its influence on the last. A15's mapping: the record's
  momentary endorsement, trust and authority ([CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md)). The difference
  A15 claims: the record's operators decide what is retrieved, what counts
  as evidence and how the next query is framed, and their authority is
  indexed by role ([CLAIM-281](../claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)).
- **Adaptive networks** (Holme and Newman, [LIT-1128](../literature.d/LIT-1128.md); Gross and Blasius, [LIT-1139](../literature.d/LIT-1139.md)): opinions and
  ties co-evolve, with a phase transition between regimes.
- **Persuasion bias** (DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel, [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)): agents who do not
  discount repeated information give social position influence apart
  from accuracy. A15: "Endorsement-mediated reinforcement could sometimes
  amount to repeated counting of correlated evidence."

The record already holds two that A15 named as new: Golub and Jackson's
naïve learning ([THEORY-321](../theory.d/THEORY-321.md), [LIT-999](../literature.d/LIT-999.md), read), on which A15 rested
[CLAIM-276](../claims.d/CLAIM-276.md)'s fidelity, and Kirby, Cornish and Smith's cumulative cultural
evolution ([LIT-771](../literature.d/LIT-771.md), read), on which it rested selection for
transmissibility over accuracy. Its readings of Zollman ([THEORY-143](../theory.d/THEORY-143.md)) and
Holman and Bruner ([THEORY-146](../theory.d/THEORY-146.md)) are right.

This is what [CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md) demands of the operator framework: an outcome it
forbids. If a reduced [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) is a DeGroot–Friedkin system, A15: "it
would let us adopt an established stability analysis and reserve the new
work for the mechanisms that actually differ."

## What would count as an answer

A derivation placing a reduced [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) inside one of these models, or a
phenomenon from A15's suite (capture without fidelity, hysteresis,
role-sensitive recovery, persistence through operator turnover) that the
coupled meaning–authority model produces and they cannot at matched
complexity. [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s instability condition is the linear part
any of them would have to reproduce.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a crossed ablation, and the condition it must meet

A32 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 proposed a design for this question while
applying the coupled model to learning: crossed ablations in a learner with
specialized operators, an influence-allocation mechanism and an internal
estimate of strain, in four conditions (strain represented or not, able to
change endorsement or not). Its prediction is not lower loss in the fully
coupled condition but "distinctive **persistent regulatory modes**:
coordinated shifts in which operators are recruited, which evidence is
inspected, and which adaptation strategies are maintained". And it named this
question's condition without citing it: "the model must make a prediction
that its constituent frameworks do not make separately."

The design answers this question only if a persistent mode appears in the
coupled condition that a model of evolving influence with fixed operators
does not produce at matched complexity. The comparison classes are the ones
above, and [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)'s defeater, reproduction by a single adaptive-retrieval
process, is the same test.

## Note of 2026-10-21: a fifth candidate predecessor

A25 §2 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 asked this question again without
citing it: "whether our second authority variable changes the dynamics in
ways that cannot already be represented as endogenous network weights,
strategic signaling, or standard adaptive learning", and "[CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s
instability condition is valid mathematics. Its novelty cannot rest on the
eigenvalue calculation itself." It adds a candidate: Mostagir, Ozdaglar
and Siderius, "When Is Society Susceptible to Manipulation?"
(Management Science 2022, doi:10.1287/mnsc.2021.4265), now registered
unread as [LIT-tmp8x4zb](../literature.d/LIT-tmp8x4zb.md). A25 says it "concerns strategic interference with
social learning, rather than a dynamically evolving allocation of
authority over interpretation". That is the third exchange to pose the
question, after A15 of 2026-10-16 and A32 of 2026-10-17. The predecessors
registered here ([LIT-1146](../literature.d/LIT-1146.md), [LIT-1128](../literature.d/LIT-1128.md), [LIT-1139](../literature.d/LIT-1139.md), [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)) are still unread.
A25's other first-ranked work, Weatherall, O'Connor and Bruner, is
registered as [LIT-tmpx95z2](../literature.d/LIT-tmpx95z2.md).

Both were sought on 2026-10-21. Mostagir, Ozdaglar and Siderius is
Unavailable: copies exist (Deep Blue, CORE, an author's site) but none
could be fetched here, so the check against it stays open. Weatherall,
O'Connor and Bruner was read in full (NOTE-tmp8rwaa): its dynamics are
open-loop, with no coupling from the audience back to the evidence or the
scientists, so it is not a predecessor of the second authority variable;
A25's "alongside" fits. The question stays Open.
