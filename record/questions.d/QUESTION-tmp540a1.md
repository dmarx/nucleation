# QUESTION-tmp540a1: Relative to a level of description and a target, what makes a factor of a system an agent, and what further makes it a willed agent?

## Why it is a question

The will paper set this question aside on purpose. It supposed its agent
([CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md)) so as not to face the permissiveness objection, that every
control system has a will ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)). This session has built the
materials an answer needs:
- **which blocks are one system** ([CLAIM-tmp8tsw5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8tsw5.md));
- **how drive is apportioned among them** ([TERM-tmpuyzeb](../terms.d/TERM-tmpuyzeb.md));
- **a way to price a description against its cost** ([CLAIM-tmp45kkg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp45kkg.md)).

The owner then proposed ([CLAIM-tmptd6s2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptd6s2.md)) that agents are a factorization
of a system at a level, and that each is individuated by its
will-organization.

## What would count as an answer

A test, run on a candidate factor at a stated level and for a stated
target, that admits some factors as agents and refuses others, and could
be wrong. It must:
- **refuse a thermostat** ([CASE-tmp6ve1p](../cases.d/CASE-tmp6ve1p.md)) or explain why it should pass;
- **say whether a dancing couple, a firm and a microbiome are agents,** and
  at which levels;
- **avoid presupposing the agent it is meant to identify**
  ([CLAIM-tmpm5571](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm5571.md)).

The proposed answer is [CLAIM-tmpwv313](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwv313.md).
