# THEORY-tmpmkl9i: In 30 four-person student groups discussing 15 choice dilemmas, a member's self-reported closure to influence on the next issue rose with their influence over prior outcomes, increasingly so along the sequence, and the groups' influence networks thinned and their discussions shortened while the rate of consensus did not change: evidence for a reflected-appraisal step by which influence concentrates in deliberating groups

## Source

LIT-1482, read in full in NOTE-1126. What the source measured is THEORY-902, which this entry extends.

## The claim

Friedkin, Jia and Bullo argue that in a group deliberating a sequence of
issues, each member's closure to influence on the next issue is set by their
realized influence on the last (reflected appraisal), and that iterated over
issues this concentrates influence, toward one closed member. Their
laboratory groups showed the individual association and structural signals
of it.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried word for word from THEORY-902 when that entry was narrowed. More readings of the same source cannot settle it.
