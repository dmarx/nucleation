# NOTE-2412: Simons — Vinnie Jones on X-Men disappointment, Guy Ritchie, and turning down Rambo

## Contribution

The actor's fuller account of how the Juggernaut line was added: a call
from the director after the main shoot, a single pickup day at Elstree
for that one line, and the director's reason as the actor understood it,
that he had seen it online.

## Key insight

The line entered the film by one person's request, after the shoot, for
a thing he had met online. The actor is first-hand about the request and
the pickup, and hedged about the source ("I think").

## Assumptions

- An interview; the quotations are Jones's, the connecting text the
  writer's ("the actor reveals that was a late addition by Ratner").
- Memory, eighteen years on.

## Key results

- "That was afterwards, they came to the Elstree [Studios, in the UK] to
  film that."
- "We did the movie in Vancouver and then Brett Ratner phoned me and he
  said, 'oh, I want you to do this line'. He'd seen it online, I think,
  and so he said, 'I want you to do it' so we had to go to Elstree for
  the day and we did that one line. But, I was very disappointed with
  that."
- The film was "a shambles"; Ratner "brought in so many other moving
  parts and so many other actors that mine was the one that got
  diluted" (the writer's caption, quoting Jones).

## Concepts

- **pickup**: a short shoot after principal photography for a few shots
  or lines; Jones's "Elstree for the day".

## Limitations

- "I think": the source is the actor's belief, not his knowledge.
- No date for the call or the pickup; it must fall between the shoot's
  end and the 26 May 2006 release.
