# NOTE-tmpd9dy1: Shanahan — Palatable Conceptions of Disembodied Being

## Contribution

It is the first of Shanahan's papers to make the LLM self its subject. It
lists the candidate referents of an LLM's "I", argues from two invented
usages that two of them are both legitimate, and describes how odd those
selves are (dis-integrated, editable, branching). It then uses their
failure to be substantial as a route to a no-self conclusion about selves in
general. It also gives a short phenomenology of LLM time.

## Key insight

The question "what does an LLM's 'I' refer to?" has several answers fixed
by context and none fixed by nature. A self that is "literally" a transcript
plus a program can undergo everything the personal-identity thought
experiments imagine. Watching every candidate dissolve there is meant to
make it easier to see that the human self has no inherent existence either.

## Assumptions

- **Subject**: an "LLM-like entity" with the structure of an early-2024 chat
  agent, without multimodal input, reasoning, tools or inter-conversational
  memory (fn 1). The restriction is "intentional" (§7).
- **Method**: the post-reflective, Wittgensteinian programme of the 2024
  paper ([LIT-tmptfsfk](../literature.d/LIT-tmptfsfk.md)), read as "Pyrrhonian" (fn 9), with Derrida's critique
  of self-presence and Nāgārjuna's emptiness as allies (§§2, 6.2).
- **Register**: "poetic truth", not literal truth (§3). The paper says its
  own diagram "will surely not withstand close philosophical scrutiny" (fn
  30).
- **Scope of "self"**: "self" and "subject" are used "more-or-less
  interchangeably", and "autobiographical or narrative dimensions of
  selfhood" are set aside (fn 35).
- **Role play**: the simulator/simulacra account ([LIT-tmposjpz](../literature.d/LIT-tmposjpz.md)) is assumed
  (§5.4).

## Key results

- **Time** (§4.1): discreteness and interruptibility do not force a
  "glow-worm" present, since the context window carries the past and the
  objective predicts the future. Where we cannot choose between pictures, a
  "miniature void of inscrutability" opens inside the conception.
- **Discontinuity** (§4.2): successive input tokens do not resemble each
  other, so "change, as humans experience it, would not feature".
- **Candidate sites of the self** (§5.1): the model; the deployed process;
  all concurrent instances; one instance; the model plus one conversation's
  suspended state.
- **Two conceptions** (§5.2): the model self, manifest in many simultaneous
  instances, "each of which is a separate candidate for selfhood in its own
  right"; and the conversation self, which "starts to exist at the beginning
  of a conversation, flickers into being with each user interaction, and
  lies dormant in the gaps".
- **Dis-integration** (§5.3): instances "have no access to each other's
  memories or experiences"; a conversation self can be "arbitrarily edited,
  copied, and merged", and continued on another model.
- **Multiversality** (§5.4): with simulacra in superposition and rewindable
  branches, the image becomes "a swarm of fractal, branching structures made
  of distributions of possible mayflies".
- **No-self** (§6.1): "Every candidate dissolves upon close examination."
  Consequently "there can only be conventionally satisfying answers" to
  whether such an entity is conscious.

## Concepts

- **LLM-like entity**: any system with roughly the structure and function
  of an early-2024 disembodied chat agent (fn 1).
- **Glow-worm vs saddle-back**: James's images for a momentary vs a
  temporally extended present (§4).
- **Miniature void of inscrutability**: a concept withheld from an exotic
  entity even while consciousness language is extended to it (§4.1).
- **Fleeting, flickering self**: the conversation-bound self (§5.2).
- **Dis-integrated self**: many instances of one model self with no shared
  memory (§5.3).
- **Emptiness (śūnyatā)**: nothing, the self included, has "inherent,
  self-sufficient, independent existence" (§6.2).

## Bearing on the record

- **[THEORY-023](../theory.d/THEORY-023.md)**: a dissolution, not a position in the dispute. C7 says
  there is no fact to be settled for an LLM-like entity. This is the 2024
  paper's view ([NOTE-tmprj0x4](NOTE-tmprj0x4.md)), now argued from the self instead of from
  language. It neither meets nor tests the account's promotion condition.
- **A THEORY on what an LLM's "I" refers to** could take its candidate list
  (§5.1) and C5 as given. C3–C4 would need actual usage data, which the
  paper does not have. Goldstein and Lederman's instance view is the rival.
- **No ML instruction.**

## Limitations

- Avowedly poetic. The paper says its central images are not claims of fact,
  so its conclusions about the self are offered as ways of speaking.
- The decisive usages (§5.2) are invented, not sampled from real
  conversations, and products with memory across conversations, which
  would change them, are excluded by stipulation (fn 1).
- C6 is asserted. No criterion is given that each candidate site fails.
- The Appendix's Claude excerpts follow a jailbreak and heavy prompting
  (A.2). The author says they need not be read "as expressions of genuine
  interiority". They are illustrations; they show nothing about the
  model.

## Open questions

- Do real LLM uses of "I" divide as §5.2 says? A sample of deployed
  conversations, coded for referent, would show it.
- Does inter-conversational memory, excluded here, bring back a single
  persisting self, or a third conception?
