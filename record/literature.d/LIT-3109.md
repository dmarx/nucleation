# LIT-3109: How bilinguals solve the naming problem

Eef Ameel, Gert Storms, Barbara C. Malt and Steven A. Sloman (2005),
*Journal of Memory and Language* 53(1), 60–80 — doi:10.1016/j.jml.2005.02.004

## Key takeaways

- Unread. Reported: bilinguals' Dutch and French container categories
  converge on a common pattern, e.g. a single name for most bottles where
  French monolinguals split *bouteille* and *flacon*.

## Standing in the record

Registered by [LEAD-056](../leads.d/LEAD-056.md) ([THEORY-159](../theory.d/THEORY-159.md)'s promote_when): a field's terms in
each of a bilingual's languages shift toward the other's, measured against
monolingual speakers. It bears on the account if the shifts are read as
the terms of one field yielding space to a neighbour's contrasts, and it
lacks terms outside the field as a control. Not read in this batch.
