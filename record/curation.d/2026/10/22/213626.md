After the exchange "Graph Learning Applications" was filed (curation entries
10:38:23 and 16:33:54), the owner asked for its registered works to be read.
All twenty-nine were sought on 2026-10-22, from lawful copies only.

**How they were read.** Six sub-agents of this session each took three to
six works under a shared brief:
- look for a lawful full text through Unpaywall and OpenAlex, then the
  publisher, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, arXiv, government sites, Project
  Gutenberg and authors' or institutions' pages;
- file a NOTE that says how deeply the work was read, and a THEORY only
  where the work establishes something the record does not hold;
- check every quotation by script against the fetched text.

The social-theory agent stopped twice on an API error before finishing
its quotation check, and this session finished it: every quotation in its
four NOTEs and its THEORY was found in the fetched texts, allowing for OCR
errors in the French scan and for p. 277 of Swidler, which was read from
the page image.

Each NOTE's history names the copy read. Two works were read only through
a precursor, and their NOTEs say so: Pierson's 1997 working paper for the
2000 article, and two texts Abbott posts for the 2005 article. Four long
works are Skimmed, with the parts read listed: Rogers (volume 1, chapters V
to VIII and the recommendations), NIMS (command and coordination), Cooley
(chapters I, III, V, VI and VIII), and Abbott.

**What the reading corrected.** Dated notes record each correction on the
entry it touches. The main ones:
- **Three case titles were wrong about their sources.**
  - [CASE-136](../../record/cases.d/CASE-136.md): the doorway effect falls on the object carried through, not
    on the one set down.
  - [CASE-127](../../record/cases.d/CASE-127.md): the NTSB did not find that the United 173 crew knew the fuel
    was running out; it found the fuel was not monitored against time.
  - [CASE-135](../../record/cases.d/CASE-135.md): the Brookhollow mayday was a stair roof collapsing on two
    companies, with no firefighter injuries reported, not four
    firefighters caught.
- **Sources cited for what they do not say.**
  - Helmreich et al. never say CRM kept the captain's command ([CASE-127](../../record/cases.d/CASE-127.md));
    the documented source is the NTSB's recommendation X-79-17.
  - GAO-02-195 is about the Mars failures and never discusses Challenger
    ([CASE-138](../../record/cases.d/CASE-138.md), [CLAIM-422](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-422.md)).
  - The Rogers report recommends the 1986 safety office but does not record
    it ([CASE-138](../../record/cases.d/CASE-138.md)).
- **An earlier correction undone in part.** The commercial reading of the
  Gracie and luta livre rivalry, set down on 2026-10-22 as the exchange's,
  is in Penglase's body, though not his abstract ([CASE-130](../../record/cases.d/CASE-130.md)).
- **Two sources cited together disagree.** Swidler's repertoire is
  explicitly not dispositional and Wacquant's habitus is ([CLAIM-417](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-417.md)).
- **Two phenomena run together.** The majority illusion is a skewed
  sample seen accurately, pluralistic ignorance a misjudgement of what
  others hold; the exchange and Lerman et al. both conflate them
  ([CLAIM-415](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-415.md)).
- **The interruption claim's doorway illustration fits interference
  better than activation interruption**, and returning to the room did
  not reinstate access ([CLAIM-421](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-421.md)). The claim's defeater already allowed
  for losing the illustration.

**Not found, and why.**
- **Failed on availability**: Wilson 1995 and 2008, Prentice & Miller and
  Taylor. No lawful copy was found anywhere searched.
- **Failed on access**: Bénabou & Tirole, Putnam, Schickler and Su. Each
  status note names the copy the owner could reach by hand: Bénabou's own
  posted copy behind a bot check, a lending-library copy of Schickler, and
  publisher pages.

**A difference in practice, for the owner.** The record has read from
publishers' PDFs posted on instructors' course pages before (curation
entries of 2026-10-03, 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-16), and Swidler and Sewell
were read that way here. The politics agent declined the same kind of copy
for Putnam and for Pierson's 2000 article, judging that a course page shows
no sign of permission. The two practices are inconsistent. Whether course
copies count as lawful for this record is the owner's call. If they do,
Putnam and the 2000 Pierson can be read next.

**Left out.** No reading carried an instruction for machine-learning
practice. The Ledger of Will keeps its `anthology-candidate` flag; its
proposals are untested. Its five sibling papers are not registered.
