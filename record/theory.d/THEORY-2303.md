# THEORY-2303: Maltreated fathers' sperm methylation differs

## The account

Tuulari, Bourgery, Iversen, Koefoed, Ahonen, Ahmedani, Kataja, Karlsson,
Barrès, Karlsson and Kotaja (2025), [LIT-3189](../literature.d/LIT-3189.md). FinnBrain fathers with
Trauma and Distress Scale scores of 39 or more were compared with fathers
scoring 10 or less, using purified sperm collected about nine years after
their child's birth. RRBS in 55 men found three DMRs, near CRTC1, WFIKKN1
and GBX2. Small RNA-seq in 30 men found 47 miRNAs, 21 tsRNAs and 4 piRNA
clusters at different levels. Lower miR-34c-5p repeats Dickson et al.
Most differences survive adjustment for semen quality, age, BMI, smoking,
alcohol and current symptoms. The authors speculate about effects on
offspring brain development and say "epigenetic inheritance per se was not
addressed".

## Evidence

A cross-sectional case-control comparison of extreme groups, with
false-discovery correction and partial replication of an earlier miRNA
result. The result could have been null: most sncRNA classes did not
differ overall.

## What it does not show

- That the marks reach an embryo, or affect a child.
- That the maltreatment, rather than a life path it set, produced the
  marks. One sample per man, decades after the exposure.

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-297](../questions.d/QUESTION-297.md)'s germline route, and Horsthemke's second step ([THEORY-1618](THEORY-1618.md)).
It is a ground of [CLAIM-1017](../claims.d/CLAIM-1017.md) for what the paternal design now has and
lacks.
