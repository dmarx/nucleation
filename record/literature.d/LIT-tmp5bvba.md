# LIT-tmp5bvba: H-France Forum 19(5): Julia Prest, Public Theatre and the Enslaved People of Colonial Saint-Domingue

Ashley M. Williard, Olivia Sabee, Karine Bénac, Jennifer E. Row, and Julia Prest (response) (2024). Forum on Julia Prest, *Public Theatre and the Enslaved People of Colonial Saint-Domingue*. *H-France Forum* 19(5), #1–#5. Society for French Historical Studies.

## Key takeaways

Reviews by Williard, Sabee, Bénac and Row, and Prest's response: the theatre's reliance on enslaved labour, mitigated spectatorship, runaway advertisements beside theatre notices, blackface as control, and graded speculation ([NOTE-tmpee3r7](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpee3r7.md)). Book quotations are the reviewers'.
