# THEORY-1622: Siegel's oral history of the Juggernaut redub dates its making to November 2005 and its posting to 14 February 2006, and relays the film's cowriter's remark that the line was a reshoot addition by the director, but gives no production testimony on why the line was added

## The account

The redub was made in November 2005 in Chicago, from a downloaded
episode, with the line improvised by Randy Hayes, and posted on 14
February 2006; "I think we hit in the millions." X-Men: The Last Stand
(May 2006) has Juggernaut shout the line. The only production voice is
the cowriter, at second hand: "That was a reshoot Ratner line." Siegel
writes that the director "was likely a fan of Nazario and Hayes's
video".

## Evidence

- The creators' recollection gives the dates, consistent with the
  record's earlier check.
- Xavier Nazario recalls a news segment that "mentioned the Juggernaut
  video making it into the new X-Men movie" before release, and that
  "Word got around that the line was being added."
- Everyone interviewed is on the creators' side; no one from the
  production speaks to why the line was added.
