# LIT-1367: Culture in Action: Symbols and Strategies

Swidler, A. (1986). Culture in action: Symbols and strategies. *American Sociological Review*, 51(2), 273–286. doi:10.2307/2095521.

Read in full on 2026-10-22 (NOTE-tmp787j2). Its two-model account is held as
THEORY-tmptap5g, Proposed.

## Why this is here

After the owner's U30 ("we basically have all of sociology to lean on here"), the replies put Swidler's repertoires among the accounts the perspective view should build on rather than rename. Bears on CLAIM-417.

**From the reading, 2026-10-22.** CLAIM-417 cites this paper with
Wacquant (LIT-1377) for a perspective's "dispositional repertoire". The
repertoire here is not dispositional: culture works through "publicly
available meanings", not "enduring psychological proclivities implanted in
individuals by their socialization" (p. 283). And the organized thing is
the strategy of action built from the kit, not the kit. The paper also
bears on CLAIM-420, through the Protestant ethos that outlasted its
doctrine, and on CLAIM-416, through competing ideologies selected by
the situations they are enacted in. See NOTE-tmp787j2.
