# THEORY-tmpc1lto: Geddes (2022) argues that Kingma's case that the foster is part of the gravida fails, because her four organism-delineation criteria are either blind to the difference between parthood and proper overlap or, in the immunological case, point to proper overlap, and that the philosophy of biology currently gives no grounds to rule proper overlap out.

## The account

Geddes (2022) argues that Kingma's case that the foster is part of the gravida fails, because her four organism-delineation criteria are either blind to the difference between parthood and proper overlap or, in the immunological case, point to proper overlap, and that the philosophy of biology currently gives no grounds to rule proper overlap out.
