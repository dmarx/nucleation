- **Corrections to the assistant's sketch.**
  - Identity through collisions belongs to integrable systems, not to
    conservative ones in general.
  - Herr et al. never name hysteresis; they show path dependence.
  - The formation threshold for an integrable soliton is a reader's
    derivation from a quoted formula, not a sentence in any source.
- **The assistant's claim.** [CLAIM-tmp5d91s](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5d91s.md) sets out three routes: internal
  balance, supplied balance and topological charge, with the oscillon as a
  fourth case. Its main consequence is a qualification of [CLAIM-604](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-604.md):
  thresholds and abrupt ends appear without self-sustaining upkeep, so
  only withdrawing the supply identifies the route.
