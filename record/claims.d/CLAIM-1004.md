# CLAIM-1004: Parallel processing may complicate the joint boundary of inquiry and response, and persisted alternative perspectives may serve as conceptual nucleation sites for pursuing inquiry in depth in parallel

## The claim

The owner's words, at U28, quoting A52's "The particularly promising next
mathematical question is whether we can derive **a joint inquiry–response
feasibility or viability boundary** that cannot be recovered by
independently calculating the query frontier and the answer frontier":
"maybe this is complicated by parallel processing? maybe persisted
alternative perspectives serve as conceptual nucleation sites for parallel
depth pursuit?"

Two conjectures:

- **Parallelism.** A52's model was sequential, T_Q + T_A ≤ H. With
  several lines of inquiry run at once, what bounds the response is the
  longest chain still to be done, not the sum of the work.
- **Nucleation sites.** A perspective kept alive, not only written down,
  is where a line of inquiry can be taken up in depth when it becomes
  relevant, so several kept at once let several lines be pursued in depth
  in parallel.

## Where the record stands

- The first is held in its time part: [CLAIM-1114](CLAIM-1114.md) (stages that overlap
  contribute the longest of theirs). That the joint set depends on such
  structure, which the separate frontiers do not record, is the
  assistant's [CLAIM-1025](CLAIM-1025.md).
- The second uses the record's own figure. [CLAIM-185](CLAIM-185.md): "The nucleation
  threshold of an argument is whatever makes a structure persist"; a
  persistent structure is an anchor on which more structure can grow.
  [CLAIM-182](CLAIM-182.md): storage is not anchoring, addressability is. And [CLAIM-1001](CLAIM-1001.md)
  holds that a shared record can keep an ecology of perspectives rather
  than a monoculture. The conjecture carries the figure from arguments in
  a record to perspectives in an organization, and from anchoring to
  parallel pursuit in depth.
- A54's development: the levels of preservation and the maintained-nucleus
  model are [CLAIM-1148](CLAIM-1148.md), the portfolio of parallel routes [CLAIM-1002](CLAIM-1002.md),
  and the governance thesis [CLAIM-1177](CLAIM-1177.md).
