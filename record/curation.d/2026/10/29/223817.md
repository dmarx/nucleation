## What the readings settle

- **[THEORY-787](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-787.md)'s defeater is met for effective information.** Its
  promote_when says it is "in trouble if the macro advantage disappears
  whenever the same intervention distribution over micro states is used at
  both levels".
  - **The bound.** Under any one distribution, macro EI is I(f(X); f(Y)),
    which by data processing is at most I(X; Y): the macro can at most tie
    ([THEORY-tmphfpe7](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmphfpe7.md), re-derived and checked numerically).
  - **Where the gain comes from.** Hoel's positive causal emergence is the
    micro chain's gain from switching to a different distribution, minus a
    loss that is never negative.
  - **The other side.** Comolatti & Hoel concede the stationary case.
    Their remaining evidence is per-transition and normalized measures on
    a bipartite chain built to have a deterministic macro, which is the
    hand-built toy the promote_when says cannot settle the question.
  - **Status.** A move to Rejected, or a narrowing to measures that data
    processing does not bound, is put to the owner in the entry's note.
    The status is not changed here.
  - **[QUESTION-204](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-204.md).** Its first half is answered no. Its second half, which
    asks for agreement with an independent interventionist test, stays
    open.
- **Symmetric assembly is acyclic ([CLAIM-369](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-369.md)).** Serván & Allesina show
  that MacArthur's function orders the sub-communities, so the assembly
  graph has no cycles. [THEORY-tmplmes0](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmplmes0.md) carries the step to Hofbauer &
  Schreiber's invasion graph, which the chapter asserts and never writes
  out. It was checked numerically on 3,274 equilibria and 14,266 edges.
  Assembly ends in the same community whatever the order only when the
  matrix is positive definite. A Gram matrix of independent features always
  is, so the claim's class meets [LIT-1233](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1233.md)'s acyclicity hypothesis. That
  settles the point the claim's note left open.
- **[CASE-118](../../record/cases.d/CASE-118.md)'s 1 − ρ is Chesson's niche difference, not his overlap.**
  This comes through Letten et al. ([NOTE-1377](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1377.md)), since Chesson 1990 itself
  was not reached. The case's coexistence bound is exactly Chesson's
  ρ < f₂/f₁ < 1/ρ.
- **Ito & Kaneko is the like-and-like predecessor ([QUESTION-117](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-117.md)).**
  Identical units with normalized incoming weights form a persistent core
  and periphery. An ablation shows the state–weight coupling is needed for
  it. An answer offering persistent authority classes as new is matched by
  this paper.
- **The RefraMED mechanism analysis identifies nothing ([QUESTION-045](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-045.md),
  [QUESTION-292](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-292.md)).**
  - **Weak instruments.** The instrumental-variable mediation failed: the
    instruments were too weak (Cragg–Donald ≤ 1).
  - **The wrong mediators.** They were sessions attended, alliance and
    skills, not flexibility or emotional coping, and they were measured at
    the same time as the outcome.
  - **Adverse events.** The monograph's p < 0.01 does not reproduce either,
    and it gives 25 participants with SAEs where its own breakdown gives 24.
  - **Intervals.** For the same differences, its intervals are about 1.8
    times narrower than the journal paper's, which is unexplained. That
    makes the abstract's "throughout" true of the monograph's analysis and
    not of the paper's own table.
- **Contact.**
  - **Lemmer & Wagner** report extended contact separately: 0.42 (k = 8),
    against 0.25–0.31 for direct contact. The comparison is between studies
    and rests on immediate self-reports only. So [CLAIM-430](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-430.md)'s "no test"
    becomes "no within-study test".
  - **Scacco & Warren** measure conduct apart from stated prejudice, and
    conduct moved where stated prejudice did not. Most of the contrast is
    the single-religion classes becoming *more* discriminatory. The effect
    toward strangers holds only for Christians, and the registration is
    dated after the endline.
- **The remand ([QUESTION-279](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-279.md)).**
  - **What the court required.** It did not ask for a certificate of
    inability. It required a sworn account of the steps taken, and it read
    "facilitate" against the government's own record of returns.
  - **What it opened.** Discovery, including into who authorized the
    detention.
  - **What it leaves open.** It decides nothing about whether return was
    possible.
- **The record's citation of Bond is wrong.** The chapter is pp. 185–199,
  in Wilson & Hayes (eds.); Biglan wrote the foreword. The correction is in
  a dated note on [LIT-1218](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1218.md).

## Errors found in the works

- **Comolatti & Hoel:**
  - a sign slip in §3.1;
  - P(e|c) written where P(e|C) is meant, and deg(c) where deg(e) is meant;
  - a normalization in §7.1 that the code does differently;
  - "only the observational EI failed" cannot hold for system-level EI in
    their own symmetric model.
- **Eberhardt & Lee:**
  - (11)'s micro EI is 0.544, not 0.55;
  - their ambiguity example (13) is strongly lumpable, so the charge rests
    on (14);
  - in (17) the coarse-graining commutes with time, and only the EI choice
    of partition changes.
- **Ito & Kaneko:** Table I gives a core of 16 where the text says
  "typically 14".
- **Serván & Allesina (dissertation chapter):** the step from the Lyapunov
  function to acyclicity is asserted, not proved; there are index slips.
- **The RefraMED monograph:**
  - the SAE count (25 against 24);
  - the unrelated-SAE count (13 against 12);
  - an interval sign in the summary;
  - £7048 given "per unit" where it is the cost difference;
  - "nearly significant" said of p = 0.036.
- **Lemmer & Wagner (manuscript):**
  - 16/79 printed as 22.3%;

  - duplicated references.
- **Scacco & Warren:** "₦4" is ₦4.7; the registration (720 subjects, five
