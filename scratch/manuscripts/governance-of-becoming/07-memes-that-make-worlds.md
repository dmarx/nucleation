# Article 7. Memes That Make Worlds

## Pragmatic transport, cultural nucleation, hyperstition, and operative norm formation

**Abstract.** A transmitted expression can keep a relation, rebuild a distinction, settle on a convention that remembers nothing of its source, or help something new get organized. These outcomes need not coincide. This article sets out what the Nucleation record holds about them, and with what standing. The floor is Active and formal: a chain can forget where it started while staying far from it, so convergence is not preservation. The memetic theses built on that floor are almost all Proposed. They hold that a meme can be a seed around which organization forms, that it can carry one characteristic value of a perspective, and, in the owner's further point, that its persistent reproduction can seed related but distinct perspectives. The phrase "I am whatever you say I am" gets an Active formal result: with the grounds held fixed, conformity and defiance lie in the same component of a response, and how large that component is measures how far an attribution sets the terms of response. Hyperstition gets two Active results, each in a stated model. In the first, circulation amplifies realization exactly when the product of the gains exceeds the product of the decays. In the second, a realization outlasts its representation only if the realized organization has a second stable state of its own. One executed study, a simulation, tells a class that reshapes what it describes from a class that only wins on the evaluation regime's measures. The separating measure is engagement under randomized exposure. Concentration, fit and stability do not separate them. A second simulation finds that restoring a deviation, the record's proposed mark of an operative norm in a different setting, is passed by plain conformity. No study of real people, platforms or norms is the record's own here.

### 7.1 What circulation keeps

A51 grounds the article in the pragmatic-transport line, and the record supports that order. The line's accounts are SYNTHESIS-066 (twelve part accounts), SYNTHESIS-068 (communicative kinds) and SYNTHESIS-115 (transmission, convergence and anchoring across four lines). All three are Proposed. Each maps held entries, and none adds a claim of its own. The steps they map are these.

- **Convergence is not preservation.** "a chain can forget its initial state while its distribution stays far from where it began, so convergence is not preservation and losing information about the origin is not degradation" (CLAIM-270, Active, demonstrated). The same reading for serial reconstruction is CLAIM-081 (Active, interpretive): "transmission is not degradation". Its warrant was corrected from demonstrated to interpretive, because what it adds to CLAIM-270 is the reading of serial reconstruction as such a chain.
- **Local fidelity does not bound global drift.** "the same local error can be damped, accumulated or amplified, and local similarity can coexist with large global drift" (CLAIM-056, Active, interpretive). The bound "measures departure from a reference transport, not whether the act survives".
- **What does keep the start.** "an observable conserved exactly at every step and not constant on the support of the starting law keeps information about the start at every generation", while "a bounded conservation defect does not prevent forgetting" (CLAIM-635, Active, demonstrated).
- **Where chains settle.** A cultural attractor is "a shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object" (TERM-027, Active, after A110 §20). Reaching an attractor "is evidence of forgetting the start" (TERM-027, "What it is not").
- **Reconstruction filtered by bias.** "Serial transmission is reconstructive and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases" (CLAIM-090, Proposed, empirical). It is held back because no chain the record holds ends on a prior measured on its own transmitters beforehand (its note of 2026-11-20). ARG-035 (Proposed) holds that convergence to the prior is proved for sampling learners only (THEORY-155, Active). THEORY-489 (Active) finds human function-learning chains converging on a favoured function, "not shown to be convergence to their prior".
- **What a human chain kept.** In two risk-message chains, "Content got shorter, less accurate and more divergent; the evaluative signal survived and drifted negative" (CASE-068, Active, reported study).
- **Significance by role and uptake.** "Which communicative act an utterance performs is partly constituted by its relations to participant roles, norms and expected uptake" (CLAIM-151, Proposed, interpretive; four live entries rest on it). The line's discriminating study "has not been run" (SYNTHESIS-066, on CASE-055).

What this floor gives the rest of the article is a caution, not a theory of memes. That an expression is still circulating, or that its carriers now agree, says nothing by itself about whether anything of its origin survived. The record holds that much formally. It holds the human side only by reading.

### 7.2 Seeds, values and invitations

**The owner's two theses.** At U2 the owner offered "memes as nucleation sites", filed as "A meme can act as a nucleation site: a seed around which beliefs, practices and relations organize, and not only a unit that copies itself" (CLAIM-tmp1ng5k, Proposed, interpretive). A2's gloss is quoted in the claim: "It only needs to provide a structure around which existing information can assemble." At U4 the owner offered "memes as characteristics (qua representation theory) of perspectives". The record filed two readings as alternatives. In one, a meme is a whole character (CLAIM-tmphi3mx, Proposed, interpretive). In the other, "Memes are characteristic values of perspectives: each records how a perspective responds to one kind of transformation of a situation, so a family of memes samples a perspective's character without being it" (CLAIM-tmp4mk0t, Proposed, interpretive). The owner ruled on 2026-11-24 that "both readings are valid. I mainly meant 'meme as value'". So CLAIM-tmp4mk0t is the primary reading, and CLAIM-tmphi3mx "stands beside it" (CLAIM-tmp4mk0t, note of 2026-11-24).

**The owner's further point: the death of the author.** The same ruling adds that "persistent reproduction of a meme can nucleate related but distinct perspectives (e.g. 'death of the author' type phenomena)". This is filed as CLAIM-tmp590sw (Proposed, interpretive; the owner's), resting on CLAIM-tmp1ng5k and CLAIM-tmp4mk0t. On the value reading, "Reproduced, the value travels without the rest of the character that produced it. Each carrier that takes it up completes it from its own character". So circulation "can diversify the perspectives it touches rather than copy one". CLAIM-171 (Proposed, interpretive) holds the reader's side, that "an unread text has possible meanings and no fixed one". What the owner's claim adds is "that the meanings become maintained perspectives, not passing readings". Its bearing on CLAIM-621 is "divergence by completion, not by contest". Barthes's essay is not yet registered.

**A's development, all Proposed.**

- "the persistence of the seed, of the organization it nucleates and of the perspective it carries are distinct phenomena" (CLAIM-tmpgvsgg, interpretive; A4 §10; no entry rests on it). Its defeater requires the rival transmission models to "include threshold (complex-contagion) models, which already produce critical masses and hysteresis".
- "A viral meme produces copies; a generative meme produces structures" (A2 §6, in CLAIM-tmp4uj40, interpretive). The claim's title says the two "come apart", so a widely copied meme "can organize little and a rarely copied one much". It does not say they are opposed.
- A meme "can carry a portable specification of a governance or inquiry protocol … which is not a contract but one around which contracts can be organized" (CLAIM-tmpdq0ah, interpretive).
- A meme can change "the conditions under which later memes are assessed" (CLAIM-tmp158r8, interpretive).
- "Memetic fidelity can be the preservation of characteristic relations rather than the copying of content" (CLAIM-tmpjitli, interpretive; A5 §8; it rests on CLAIM-tmp4mk0t). Its defeater says the thesis "Must answer" CLAIM-249, the problem of fixing one transformation group across carriers.
- "symbolic stability can persist while conceptual stability fails" (CLAIM-621, interpretive; A41 §5 on the owner's "anti-vax"). Its summary says: "Weak: it may be [CLAIM-280] restated." CLAIM-280 (Active, interpretive; the owner's r/place case, A8's wording) holds that "the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts".

**The seed window, formally.** In the seeded fold model, "a transient seed leaves a maintained organization only inside the bistable window and only by carrying the state past the unstable threshold". Because "the meme enters only as a scalar forcing", "the model expresses the environment's receptivity but not which organization a seed nucleates" (CLAIM-tmpq1lyz, Active, demonstrated; the assistant's gap fill). Article 1 treats the seed window. Here it bounds what a memetic model with one maintained state can say. It cannot tell one seed's organization from another's, so the owner's diversification (CLAIM-tmp590sw) cannot even be stated in it.

**What a character can and cannot carry.** "characters classify representations only up to isomorphism and only in characteristic zero and forget the isomorphisms, the group and the action" (SYNTHESIS-077, Proposed). That is a limit on both U4 readings. A meme recognized as the same value in two carriers is the same only up to what the character forgets.

**Fidelity against convergence (J7.1).** This article joins CLAIM-tmpjitli to the floor of §7.1. CLAIM-tmpjitli reads success as χ(P_A) ≈ χ(P_B), a source and a carrier that agree in their characteristic relations. CLAIM-270 and TERM-027 hold that a chain can arrive at an agreement whatever its start. So agreement in characteristic relations between source and carrier is not, by itself, evidence that those relations were transmitted. They may be where reconstruction tends. Evidence of transmission would need the start to matter: different sources leading to different endpoints, or an invariant of the kind CLAIM-635 describes that the chain conserves. CLAIM-tmpjitli's own defeater compares memes with propositions and not with convergence. TERM-027's note of 2026-11-24 contrasts an attractor with a nucleator ("A meme need not be an attractor to nucleate an attractor"), not fidelity with convergence. The owner's CLAIM-tmp590sw fits the join, since on it reproduction diversifies rather than converges.

**Perspectival invitation.** The owner asked, at U2 of "Ben Franklin Effect Explained", for Franklin's favour to be read as a "perspectival invitation" in the record's sense. The record had not used the phrase. A3 "found the nearest entries … and coined it" (CLAIM-430). The claim: "An interaction can make available a way of relating that its participants' operative perspectives did not hold, by giving each a role to enact in it, so that taking part can come before, and help produce, the reinterpretation it later seems to express" (CLAIM-430, Proposed, interpretive; member). It keeps the further questions apart: "whether the change stays with the partner, generalizes to a category, or is reproduced beyond the encounter". Article 3 places it among invocation and contract. For this article, the invitation is the encounter-level form of the seed. Whether a transmitted expression can carry an invitation beyond the encounter is the third of those further questions, and the record leaves it open.

**Meaning and will.** CLAIM-620 (Proposed, interpretive) is the thesis of U18 to U21 of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" in A's wording, whose course the owner set: "meaning at U18, symbols and concepts with 'anti-vax' and the organizational perspective at U19, and will at U20". It holds that "the coupling of meaning and will runs through what is transmitted and can remake the concepts, perspectives and organizations it runs in", "without that persistence showing truth, consensus or autonomy". Its own defeater lists the objection that it "only restates the owner's CLAIM-336 … with CLAIM-402's categories confirmed through use".

### 7.3 "I am whatever you say I am"

The phrase is the owner's, from U39 of the exchange of 2026-10-22. That turn also raised "the perception of a plurality of opinion where one may not exist". At U7 of "Memes, Contracts" the owner asked to "apply this framework to probe the phrase".

**What is held, interpretively.**

- "A person can be organized by a judgement they attribute to a collective that does not hold it: the audience in a self-model can be a false unity, and a judgement can shape a person who rejects it, because resisting it makes it consequential" (CLAIM-415, Proposed, interpretive; member). Its note of 2026-10-30 says: "The third clause, that resisting a judgement makes it consequential, has no read result: it rests on Cooley's statement and the constructed [CASE-142]."
- TERM-075 (Active) separates the attributed self that others hold from the one the person imagines, from the imagined audience, and from the one the person takes up as their own. CLAIM-415 "depends on their doing so".
- CASE-141 (Active, thought experiment) is the label that confirms itself through others' treatment. CASE-142 (Active, thought experiment) is the label confirmed by the resistance of someone who rejects it. Of CASE-142: "Not shown: that resistance always confirms".
- A perspective governs itself here by making the authority of others' judgements "depend on their grounds, not on their visibility, repetition or attributed plurality; neither absorbing every judgement nor resisting every one meets this" (CLAIM-425, Proposed, interpretive; in `self-without-a-sovereign`).

**Three routes of confirmation.** A10 §8, recorded in CLAIM-415's note of 2026-11-24, separates "**Self-fulfillment:** I acquire characteristics that were initially misattributed to me", "**Adversarial constitution:** I become organized through my opposition to the characterization" and "**Interpretive self-sealing:** Whatever I do is taken as confirmation, regardless of whether my character changes". "Only the first necessarily involves realization of the attributed content." CASE-142 "can be the second or the third", and which it is "turns on whether the person became hostile or was only read so". CASE-141 "shows the first and third together".

**The formal result.** A8 §4 observed that conformity (r = s) and opposition (r = −s) transform alike when the attribution s is reversed. A9 then read this as an equal slope. The assistant corrected the reading: "Under reversal of a social attribution with its grounds held fixed, every response splits uniquely into a part that reverses with the attribution and a part that does not: conformity and defiance lie wholly in the first and a response governed by the grounds alone wholly in the second, so the size of the reversing part, not its direction, measures how far the attribution sets the terms of response" (CLAIM-tmpj4pw3, Active, demonstrated; the assistant's gap fill). "Its sign does not enter: defiance scores as conformity does." The title adds that "reversing the attribution together with its grounds measures nothing". That is the precise form of CLAIM-425's "both let the judgements set the terms of response". It is also a condition any test must meet.

**What the result does not decide.** CLAIM-tmpj4pw3 measures how far the attribution sets the response. It does not say by which route. A person whose reversing part is large may have become what the label says, may be organized against it, or may simply be read through it. The routes differ in whether the referent changed, which is the distinction §7.5 takes up.

**A seeded capacity to revise.** A8 §9 proposed that the phrase "could subsequently provide a conceptual resource for recognizing and revising that condition". A9, at the owner's U8, made this its integrating claim, filed as "A capacity for reflexive self-governance can itself be seeded by a transmissible expression … but holding the representation, or believing oneself autonomous, is not having the capacity, which shows only in effective revisions of dependencies met after the exposure" (CLAIM-tmp5tjtg, Proposed, interpretive; A's; not a member).

**The test not run.** LEAD-tmp0w114 (Open, idea; the assistant's design after A8) would present the same evidence about a person with a characterization in one direction or the other, and estimate each respondent's reversing part. *Expectation, stated in the lead before any run:* "Conformity and defiance should show reversing parts of opposite sign and similar size; [CLAIM-425]'s self-governing response a small one together with responsiveness to changes in the grounds". "If defiant respondents show no larger reversing part than indifferent ones, [CLAIM-415]'s 'resisting it makes it consequential' loses its measurable form." CASE-230 (Proposed, proposed study), on the label "anti-vax", is also not run.

### 7.4 Hyperstition

**The owner's thesis.** On 2026-10-23, after CLAIM-425 was filed, the owner wrote: "this is reminding me of 'hyperstition'". It is filed as CLAIM-426 (Proposed, interpretive, a counter to CLAIM-425; in `self-without-a-sovereign`): "Where a judgement's circulation helps bring about what it says, its visibility, repetition and attributed plurality are evidence about what it will bring about". So CLAIM-425's rule holds for judgements of what already is, "and not without qualification for judgements of what someone or something will become". Its note of 2026-10-30 says the read sources, Land (NOTE-1378) and Soros (NOTE-1362), "give no condition for telling which judgements' circulation helps make them true". At U9 of "Memes, Contracts" the owner asked to "relate to hyperstition".

**When circulation amplifies, in a model.** A10 §4's linear model, with salience m and realization r, is ṁ = αr − μm, ṙ = βm − νr. "the representation's circulation amplifies its own realization exactly when the product of the two gains exceeds the product of the two decay rates, so hyperstitional efficacy is a property of the coupling between a representation and the activity that can realize it, not of the representation" (CLAIM-tmpvic6m, Active, demonstrated; A's model, checked). The claim says what it is not: "Not a way to tell, in a case, which judgements' circulation helps make them true." "The gains are not observed". A10: "This construction demonstrates a possible mechanism, not an empirically established law of cultural evolution." CLAIM-426's note of 2026-11-24 says the open item is "narrowed, not closed".

**Whether the realization outlasts the representation.** A's thesis: what a representation helps bring about "can come to be maintained by processes of its own and outlast the representation's circulation … or it can stay dependent on continued circulation; which of the two holds is a fact about maintenance provenance, told apart by withdrawing the circulation once the realization has formed" (CLAIM-tmpyp5bu, Proposed, interpretive; A10 §2, §9; member). The assistant found that A10's model cannot show the first outcome. "coupling alone never lets a realization outlast its representation: once circulation stops the realization decays at its own rate whatever the gains were; it persists only if the realized organization has a self-maintaining loop of its own strong enough to have a second stable state, and the realization crossed that loop's threshold while the representation still circulated" (CLAIM-tmpnws9d, Active, demonstrated; the assistant's gap fill). In its check, with self-maintenance strength k = 2.5 above the threshold 4ν = 2, realization was 0.806 at withdrawal and settled at 0.724. With k = 1.5 it went to zero. The claim also limits the withdrawal test: "Withdrawal before the threshold is crossed ends the realization in both kinds of system, so a single withdrawal at an unknown stage does not discriminate."

So A's possibility (CLAIM-tmpyp5bu) has a condition in the model (CLAIM-tmpnws9d). The record says the result is "[CASE-189]'s transient encounter and maintained connector, in hyperstition's variables" (CLAIM-tmpnws9d's summary). CASE-189 (Active, construction) is the construction in which a transient encounter forms a connector "maintained by what it enables". That identification is held, not this article's (JR7.1).

**One shape, three places (J7.2).** This article goes one step further than the record. Three held results share the shape of a second stable state. A realization outlasts its representation only with one (CLAIM-tmpnws9d). A norm in A22's model, once established past a threshold, holds where a weakly established one would not (CLAIM-tmpjg5gx, Proposed, interpretive; Article 3), and "a bistable norm ends by a push past its threshold rather than by fading" (CLAIM-tmps8ou2, Proposed, interpretive; the assistant's concession to the owner's CLAIM-tmp8l5s9). A capacity lost past a threshold "returns only under conditions better than those it was lost under" (CLAIM-463, Active, demonstrated). No entry joins the hyperstition result to the norm model. The join carries a caution that is held. Threshold-contagion models "already produce critical masses and hysteresis" (CLAIM-tmpgvsgg's defeater). So the shared shape does not by itself pick out nucleation, and LEAD-tmpiktom says so: "a threshold or a lasting regime after withdrawal is not by itself evidence of nucleation" (JR7.2).

**The nearest data.** Krohn and Weninger find that links to subreddits that did not yet exist "preceded the creation of a sixth of the subreddits later created" (THEORY-2070, Proposed), and they report it "as correlation, not cause". The links are proposals or jokes, not judgements (CLAIM-426, note of 2026-11-19). No field case of hyperstition with a withdrawal is on the record.

### 7.5 A changed referent against apparent confirmation

A51's last distinction, between "genuine transformation of the referent" and "apparent confirmation produced by the evaluation regime", is held three ways. One is a criterion, one is a decomposition, and one is a run.

**The criterion.** "Predictive success of a category across contexts warrants treating it as a real effective kind only when the evidence in those contexts does not pass through the category's own use, or when the effect of that use is modelled: a category that speakers or institutions act on can produce the regularities that confirm it" (CLAIM-402, Active, interpretive; member; six Proposed entries rest on it). The claim "defeats one inference and is not a criterion of kindhood". Its premise: what keeps a loop from confirming itself "is evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the interpretation it tests" (CLAIM-342, Active, interpretive). A44 §5, in CLAIM-402's note of 2026-11-24, gives the consequence for metrics: "**Improved correspondence between a measure and its object does not establish that the measure was originally adequate, or that the resulting transformation is desirable.**" In the read case, law schools "changed what they did in order to move their rank" (THEORY-862, Active).

**The decomposition.** CLAIM-402's note of 2026-10-24 writes the learned distribution's derivative as two terms. One is "performative change of the world sampled". The other is "endogenous change of how it is represented". These "need different interventions to separate: change the reporting regime with the policy fixed, or change the policy with raw outcomes observed independently." A10 §6's table, in CLAIM-426's note of 2026-11-24, makes the same cut for hyperstition. It "separates a changed reality (hyperstition) from a changed reading of evidence (interpretive capture, confirmation bias) and from counterperformativity". A10 §6 also gives the danger: "the causal consequences of a representation become evidence for it, while the formative role of the representation becomes increasingly illegible."

**The run (CASE-367).** CASE-367 (Proposed, executed study; filer-run) is the owner's genres example (CASE-296) built as a laboratory for LEAD-030.

- *The criterion, set before the run* (LEAD-030, "What would count"): "Classes that reshape preferences only when used supports the reading of [CLAIM-762] the case was filed for."
- *The design.* There are two worlds. In one, genres are planted in 600 users' preferences. In the other, clusters are made by exposure alone. Each world runs five arms over four seeds. The decisive probe shows each user 20 items at random, so the gap in engagement it measures does not pass through the classes' use.
- *Results* (start to end, taste world). Steering exposure by the classes over fixed preferences raised concentration from 0.42 to 0.90 and fit to raw counts from 24.5 to 41.1 millinats per cell. The random-exposure gap moved from 0.352 to 0.357, identical "to every digit" to the unsteered arm, "because the runs share random numbers and the preferences did not move". With users and creators adapting, the same steering raised the gap to 0.482, and the true gap rose from 0.344 to 0.466. The same adaptation without the classes lowered the gap to 0.231. So "in this model the classes reshape preferences only when used", which is what the criterion said would count. A's three outcomes all occurred: "The loop *reinforced* planted genres … *created* a small distinction where there had been none … *erased* one when exposure followed popularity".
- *What fit and stability said.* Clusters that exposure alone had made fitted raw counts better than planted genres (9.6 against 3.7 millinats) and were perfectly stable (ARI 1.00). The closure test, conditioned on exposure and scored on randomized exposure, passed the planted genres (0.86) and failed the exposure-made clusters (−0.01).

The claims drawn from it are both Proposed and empirical. First: "only engagement under randomized exposure tells classes inferred, classes used and classes that reshape preferences apart" (CLAIM-954). Second: "Fit to engagement data and stability over time favour clusters that exposure made … so neither can warrant an engagement category as a kind" (CLAIM-955). CLAIM-954 calls itself "an instance of [CLAIM-402]'s worry, not an answer to it". QUESTION-028's note of 2026-11-22 limits the separating test: "In the model it is necessary, not sufficient: classes reshaped by their own use pass it too". The case's own limit: "The laboratory shows that the measures can tell the cases apart when the cases are known, not that real genres are reshaped."

**What the run means for this article (J7.3).** The run fills in the cut of the decomposition. In the arm with fixed preferences, every measure the policy's own data supply rose, and the referent did not move. That arm is apparent confirmation by the evaluation regime, as near to pure as a model can make it. In the adaptive arm the referent moved, and only the probe that did not pass through the classes registered the move. This article reads that separation as the model form of A51's distinction. It also reads it as the population-scale form of the phrase's routes: self-sealing is a regime's measures confirming with nothing changed, and self-fulfillment is the referent made to fit (§7.3). CLAIM-954 ties the run to CLAIM-402. No entry ties it to hyperstition or to the phrase. There is one caveat. CASE-367's "reshaped" classes are reshaped by use, so they are also CLAIM-402's case of a category that produces the regularities confirming it. A changed referent is not a vindicated category. As CLAIM-tmpyp5bu puts it, persistence does not make "the fiction true when it circulated".

### 7.6 From regularity to operative norm, and not to legitimacy

On U17 ("relate this to norm formation and stabilization"), A22 proposed a ladder and a stabilizer. Article 3 treats both and the owner's correction of the model's reading, and this article does not repeat them. Three steps matter here.

- **The marker.** "An expectation is an operative norm rather than a prediction when a discrepancy is met by bringing the behaviour back into line with the expectation instead of revising the expectation to fit the behaviour" (CLAIM-tmphq6hp, Proposed, interpretive; A22 §2; member). It needs "no represented belief about what ought to happen".
- **Reliance as stabilizer.** "A norm can be stabilized by the reliance it attracts" (CLAIM-tmpjg5gx, Proposed, interpretive; member). Its model's reading was corrected at the owner's objection (CLAIM-tmp8l5s9, Proposed, empirical; the owner's case of race-based power after abolition). The correction is that "A norm's original justification and its baseline support come apart" (CLAIM-tmps8ou2). The assistant's general lesson is CLAIM-tmpyxemn (Proposed, interpretive): a model's verbal conclusion "often reads a parameter as the concept it was introduced to stand for".
- **Not legitimacy.** CLAIM-tmphq6hp: "Not that an operative norm is legitimate". CLAIM-tmpjg5gx: "Not that the stabilized norm is good." A22 §3: "The pathology does not consist simply in stabilization. It concerns what becomes stabilized, whose interests it serves and whether revision remains possible." The ladder from repetition to "Its withdrawal is treated as a violation" (CLAIM-tmp0i6c0, Proposed, interpretive; member) makes something owed "only under a further principle".

**A test of restoration, run in another setting.** CASE-362 (Proposed, executed study; LEAD-087; filer-run) asked whether a population shares a perspective. It did not ask about norms. Its criterion was "committed with the study's model, thresholds and predicted verdicts before any code was written". The criterion included a restorative reading, TERM-148 (Proposed): a deviation "is corrected back toward the shared structure" rather than averaged. It also included A4's failure rule: "if plain conformity passes condition 3 … the criterion fails to make the distinction". *Result:* "Plain conformity restores a minority's deviation (ρ = 0.024, against the averaging benchmark 0.25 and the threshold 0.125)". The claim filed from it: "Restoring a deviation to the shared structure does not tell a population whose members evaluate one another's interpretations from one whose members copy one another's answers" (CLAIM-946, Proposed, empirical). The case says "Not real groups."

**The tension (J7.4).** This article carries CLAIM-946 over to CLAIM-tmphq6hp's marker. If members who only copy one another's answers about a shared world, most of them agreeing, bring a deviant back into line, then observed restoration does not tell an enforced norm from a majority copied. The carry-over may fail, because the objects differ. CASE-362 restores interpretive prototypes, and CLAIM-tmphq6hp restores behaviour against an expectation. CLAIM-tmphq6hp's own defeater asks whether "every corrective response is also a revision of expectation". That is a different worry. Neither entry cites the other. If the join holds, the ladder from regularity to operative norm needs a marker beyond observed restoration. One candidate is a disposition that holds against a majority, of the kind CASE-362's evidential condition tests in direction. The record does not yet state it.
