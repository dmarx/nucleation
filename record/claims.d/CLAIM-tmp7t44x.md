# CLAIM-tmp7t44x: A record of an inquiry's justifications adds nothing for decisions its current state already suffices for: what it can add is a wider family of decisions for which what is kept suffices, and decisions a bounded agent can make that it otherwise could not

## The claim

A10 closed: "The central insight is that justificatory history is not
extra information beyond a sufficient state. Its potential value lies in
expanding the set of decisions for which the retained state is
sufficient, or making those decisions computationally feasible."

It separated three comparisons, "not automatically additive":

- **Informational value.** What a narrower state discarded: R*_Q(S) −
  R*_Q(H). Zero when S is sufficient for Q ([CLAIM-tmpp7a8x](CLAIM-tmpp7a8x.md)).
- **Computational value.** What a bounded agent gains from a
  representation that makes relations easy to reach: R^(r)_Q(S) −
  R^(r)_Q(J). It can be positive "without adding information". A10's
  example: forty dependents of a challenged premise among ten thousand
  claims, found by reverse reachability in an index and by search in a
  flat archive.
- **Option value.** The expected gain when future families differ from
  the one the state was optimized for ([QUESTION-tmpzf5on](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpzf5on.md)).

A5's second claim is the specific mechanism: "When conclusions depend on
distinguishable sources, assumptions, and inferential steps, preserving
those dependencies can enable targeted reconsideration after a premise
or source is challenged." [CASE-tmpccqh1](../cases.d/CASE-tmpccqh1.md) and [CASE-tmpohd6m](../cases.d/CASE-tmpohd6m.md) illustrate the two operations
this supports, tracing descendants of a defeated premise and reopening
an alternative whose defeater is defeated.

## Where it stands against [CLAIM-250](CLAIM-250.md)

A10 §7: [CLAIM-250](CLAIM-250.md) is "a proposal about what accountable inquiry ought to
preserve". This claim concerns what preserving it can buy. "These four
propositions can support one another without being reduced to one
another." A10 kept the fallback in view: if a latent sufficient state
does as well once costs are counted, the record's structure "might still
be justified by inspectability, auditing, and shared human–agent
coordination, rather than by superior predictive updating."

## What it does not say

- Not that topology is enough ([CLAIM-tmp2deky](CLAIM-tmp2deky.md)).
- Not that history is always worth keeping. A10: the argument "only
  establishes an option value for retained information. It does not imply
  retaining everything is worth its cost." That is [CLAIM-tmpygqjz](CLAIM-tmpygqjz.md) and [QUESTION-tmp2vbup](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp2vbup.md).
- Not that a record that keeps justifications is correct ([CLAIM-tmpb7qd9](CLAIM-tmpb7qd9.md)).
