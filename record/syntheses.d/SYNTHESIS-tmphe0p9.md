# SYNTHESIS-tmphe0p9: A normative judgment about an organization is well-defined only once its relata are fixed and can then be false; viability does not establish what ought to persist; judgments of continuity, reform and obligation can need histories that a present state does not show; and the wrong beyond harm that an organization commits by reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining it is proposed as a breach of a bounded duty of answerability

*The account of the organizational-normativity line, "Normative judgment about organizations", split from will-organization (ADR-tmpczmfi), where it was a part account. Its premises from will-organization, CLAIM-523 and CLAIM-445, are dependencies across lines.*

## The account

**The limit.** CLAIM-075 (the paper's, Sections 6.10 and 7.3; disclaimed,
Active): viability does not establish what ought to persist; "An unjust
institution may be highly viable." QUESTION-128 (the owner's U16 of
2026-10-17; Open) asks whether the organizational account gives a path to
normativity from first principles, and what more practical and moral
authority need.

**Relata, then truth.** CLAIM-363 (the owner's U17 and U18; Active) rests on
CLAIM-075 and CLAIM-152 and uses TERM-059: a normative judgment is
well-defined only once its relata are fixed, "and that dependence does not
by itself make it relative". TERM-059 (the owner's name, Active) states
relational normative realism and says its third condition, constraints on
admissible perspectives, is not established; QUESTION-129 (Open) refines
QUESTION-128 to ask whether it can be met.

**Histories.** CLAIM-518 (the owner's U26 of 2026-10-31, narrowed the same day
and again on 2026-11-06; Active) rests on CLAIM-363 and CLAIM-350 and bears
on QUESTION-128 and QUESTION-256: judgments about an organization's
continuity, reform or obligations can depend on diachronic relations that
present states and terminal outcomes do not show. ARG-043 (Proposed)
concludes it from Bencherki's checklist and daycare. CLAIM-512 (the
assistant's, A26 and A27; counter, Active) objects to CLAIM-518 and
CLAIM-608 with a dilemma and a distinction; CLAIM-518's version 3 concedes
the broad horn, and the clause it dropped is CLAIM-608 (the record's gloss;
Proposed), which rests on CLAIM-518 and CLAIM-363 and holds that histories
add a relatum only if one of three tests is met. QUESTION-256 (A26's, from
the owner's case of Clive Wearing; Open) asks which continuities
self-governance needs.

**Corrigibility, not claimed.** CLAIM-511 (Proposed, disclaimed) is the bare
proposition the owner asked about, that a self-maintaining organization
ought to preserve its corrigibility; it bears on QUESTION-128 and
QUESTION-129, and its summary says the record's constructions do not
establish it and its value is conditional (CLAIM-520).

**The wrong beyond harm.** QUESTION-441 (the owner's U27, verbatim;
distributed-agency first) refines QUESTION-129 and asks what the
counter-normativity of a harming organization that reproduces what makes
its harm non-legible is. CLAIM-614 (Proposed) files the owner's
presupposition, resting on CLAIM-348 and CLAIM-523 and bearing on
QUESTION-441 and QUESTION-129: such an organization commits a
counter-normativity distinct from the harm. CLAIM-624 (A's, A60 to A62;
Proposed) rests on CLAIM-614, CLAIM-348, CLAIM-523, CLAIM-445 and CLAIM-462:
the wrong is a breach of a second-order duty of answerability, "a verdict
only given an independently justified standard and a feasible
alternative". It was admitted on that narrower title (its history, version 3), and its
body states where it diverges from the owner's thesis on unavoidable
projections. Five arguments conclude it:

- ARG-055 (Proposed), from CASE-229 (A's matched-harm construction):
  the second-order wrong comes apart from the harm and its continuation;
- ARG-058 (Proposed), resting on CLAIM-520: an unconditional duty would
  entail CLAIM-511, which the record disclaims, so the duty is bounded;
- ARG-062 (Proposed), from THEORY-140 and CASE-138: an organization can
  reproduce the conditions that keep a known hazard from constraining it,
  "though it says nothing of a second-order wrong";
- ARG-064 (Proposed), resting on CLAIM-445: "the reproductive mechanism
  transfers and the verdict does not";
- ARG-071 (Rejected), from Wells Fargo (CASE-151): occlusion and blocked
  uptake, not reproduction.

CLAIM-619 (A's, A62; Proposed) rests on CLAIM-402 and CLAIM-406, uses TERM-118
and bears on QUESTION-441: an organization can be disposed to answer a
credible challenge by reducing the capacity for future challenge, and the
test alone does not say whether the response "finks the disposition to be
corrected, masks it, or changes only the challengers' access and
incentives". ARG-059 (Proposed) concludes it from the
self-confirming label (CASE-141); ARG-070 (Deferred) offers Pohlhaus,
unread.

## Not stated on the record

  CLAIM-624 leave it open.

## What it does not say

- That corrigibility is unconditionally desirable (CLAIM-511 is disclaimed).
- That any organization on the record commits the second-order wrong: the
  arguments to CLAIM-624 are Proposed, and ARG-071 is Rejected.
- That relational normative realism is established (TERM-059).
- Anything with standing of its own.
