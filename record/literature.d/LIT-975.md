# LIT-975: Contextures: The Mechanism of Representation Learning

Runtian Zhai (2025), *arXiv:2504.19792, PhD dissertation*.

Filed for [QUESTION-052](../questions.d/QUESTION-052.md): it says that representations learn the top singular functions of the
expectation operator of an input–context pairing, and the pairing need not be symmetric. The
question asks for exactly that kind of result for a directed pair operator. It also bears on
[QUESTION-060](../questions.d/QUESTION-060.md). It is also held in the Anthology of the SOTA, as [ANTH-LIT-459](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-459.md). Holding it in both
follows [ADR-013](../decisions.d/ADR-013.md): the anthology reads it as a theory of what pretraining objectives learn, and this
record for [QUESTION-052](../questions.d/QUESTION-052.md).
