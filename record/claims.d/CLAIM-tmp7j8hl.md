# CLAIM-tmp7j8hl: Nothing here is new: planning theory, control theory, executive control and institutional theory already study every mechanism the account names

## The claim

"Planning theory already studies temporally extended intentions. Control
theory studies regulation. Cognitive science studies executive control and
metacontrol. Institutional theory studies authority, procedure, and
exception."

## Why it is still open

The paper's own standard: "If no such explanatory gain exists, the concept
should not be retained merely as a new label." The comparison with
control/metacontrol literature is on the submission worklist and not done.
