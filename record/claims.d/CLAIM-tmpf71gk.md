# CLAIM-tmpf71gk: Maintaining a reserve and redesigning a transition are distinct routes to readiness: one keeps enough capacity to cross an existing transition barrier, the other lowers the barrier itself

## The claim

In A48's model (§9), configuration A is held at a cost of 1, and the
transition to B passes through a configuration costing 3 (CLAIM-577's
case; its threshold is CLAIM-571's least peak over paths). Regime II
keeps a budget of 3 despite needing 1; Regime III "invests in an
alternative transition mechanism", a staging path whose peak is 2. "One
preserves enough capacity to cross the existing transition barrier. The
other changes the transition barrier itself."

## Standing

Proposed, in A's voice. CLAIM-tmpg8yl2 shows that under §9.1's own budget
rule the redesigned route is lost like the original unless a reserve is
also held, so the two are distinct in what they change but not
alternatives.
