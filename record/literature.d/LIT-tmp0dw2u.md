## Key takeaways

*Not read; as A describes it.* A3 and A6: enslaved people took part in the colonial theatre as spectators, workers, performers and property; "mitigated spectatorship" names partial, constrained access (present for work, hearing rather than seeing); chapters titled "Mitigated Spectators" and "Concealed Contributors" recover labour that made performances possible; the institutions depended on that labour though the accounts made it invisible.
