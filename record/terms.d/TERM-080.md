# TERM-080: collective intention, and which sense: participatory shared intention, institutionally constituted intention, or attributed collective intention

## Definition

A12: "An intention directed toward a joint or group-level course of action
whose attribution depends on more than the observation that several
participants produced a common outcome." A claim says which of three:

1. **Participatory shared intention.** The participants have suitably
   interrelated intentions and expectations about their parts in a joint
   activity.
2. **Institutionally constituted intention.** An authorized decision
   procedure fixes an operative intention for a group or institution.
3. **Attributed collective intention.** An observer reads coordinated
   behaviour as purposive at group level, without either of the above
   being established. A12: "The third is an *attribution*, not
   automatically a genuine instance of the first or second." [CLAIM-415](../claims.d/CLAIM-415.md)
   is the case in which the observer is a member who is organized by a
   judgement attributed to a collective that does not hold it.

Rival criteria for the first two, which A12 said "should be preserved
rather than merged into a falsely consensual definition", and which the
record already holds, though A12 cited only Tuomela:

- Bratman's interlocking intentions: [LIT-471](../literature.d/LIT-471.md) and [LIT-488](../literature.d/LIT-488.md), both
  Unavailable; his intrapersonal analogue is [THEORY-076](../theory.d/THEORY-076.md).
- Gilbert's joint commitment: [LIT-477](../literature.d/LIT-477.md), Unfetched.
- Tuomela's we-intentions: [LIT-476](../literature.d/LIT-476.md), Unavailable.
- List and Pettit's corporate agency: [LIT-391](../literature.d/LIT-391.md), Unavailable; List's related
  papers ([LIT-700](../literature.d/LIT-700.md), [LIT-705](../literature.d/LIT-705.md)) are read in [THEORY-127](../theory.d/THEORY-127.md).

## What it is not

- **Common behaviour, aggregated preference, conformity, a shared
  perspective or joint causal efficacy** (A12). [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) holds the
  perspective half; [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md) the efficacy half: a coalition able to
  realize an outcome need share no intention.
- **Collective agency.** An institution can form an operative intention by
  procedure (sense 2) without meeting an agency criterion ([TERM-044](TERM-044.md),
  [TERM-063](TERM-063.md)), and the converse question is left to those terms.
- **Responsibility.** A12: "The ability of several institutions to produce a
  coordinated outcome does not entail that they possess one shared
  intention. Nor does the absence of such an intention entail that no
  institution bears responsibility" ([CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md)). [CLAIM-332](../claims.d/CLAIM-332.md) is the
  record's case of a loosely organized population changing an institution
  through separately motivated acts, with no collective intention.
