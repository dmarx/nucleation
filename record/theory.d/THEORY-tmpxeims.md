# THEORY-tmpxeims: Ord argues that, with value summed over time and no pure time preference, advancing progress is worth an amount that does not grow with the future's duration, while reducing near-term existential risk multiplies the whole future's value like an enhancement, so pure advancements rarely rival existential risk reduction

## The account

Humanity's value per unit time is a curve, the future's value the area under
it, and an intervention's value the area between two curves. Value must be
roughly quantitative and separable across time, with no pure time preference
(pp. 3–4, 9).

An advancement, the curve shifted earlier by δt, is worth about (v_τ −
v_0)δt, independent of the future's duration and the curve's shape; if the
end comes sooner too, it is worth about −v_0δt, a loss (pp. 15–16). A
speed-up scales with (v_τ − v̄)τ, a gain with τ, an enhancement with v̄τ (p.
26). Raising near-term survival by a factor γ_p is worth (γ_p − 1)v̄τ,
"almost identical" to an enhancement, and irreversibility makes its
contingency easier to meet (p. 28).

So "On a million-year lifespan (that of a typical species) a one-year
advancement would be roughly as important as a one-in-a-million improvement
in nearterm survival probability — but the latter seems much more
achievable" (p. 29).

## Evidence

Derivation within the model, with stated approximations; diagrams are
deliberately not to scale and the changes marginal and idealised. Risk after
t = 0 is set aside, which is where Tarsney's and Thorstad's objections live
(NOTE-1269, Connections). The framework is silent, by its own statement, on
morality unrelated to value (p. 4).

## What it bears on

QUESTION-299's note of 2026-10-29 reads this work, with NOTE-1317 and a
paper by MacAskill, Thomas and Vallinder, under "The impersonal ground's
accounting", in which "nothing about anyone's self enters". It also reports
Ord's aside that people discount benefits to future generations no more than
benefits to their children, and says the record should read that study
(Frederick 2003) before relying on it. The reading bears on the impersonal
ground; that aside is not part of this reading's title.
