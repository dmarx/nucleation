# SYNTHESIS-tmp7nvem: In the record's exchanges with collaborators who keep no memory, the argument loses the identity of what was said: observed across six exchanges, Proposed in general, and read three ways, as retrieval failure outside a critical distance, as transport toward the collaborators' framings, and as a whole's course coming apart from its members'

*A part account of the nucleating-argument line (ADR-tmpyihxy, version 2); the line's synthesis is built over it and its two sibling parts.*

## The account

In the record's own exchanges, what goes wrong is identity, not missing
structure. Concepts are redefined silently, objections and readings come
back as though new, and the thesis changes with no turn arguing for the
change. That is CLAIM-177: the record's finding, empirical, Active, over six
exchanges with one owner and one assistant family.

- CLAIM-324 (the record's, empirical, Active) is the documented case: the
  manuscript drafted from the crystallized argument changed the thesis "with
  no turn arguing the move".
- TERM-052 (the owner's term, Active) holds that "Maturing drops things
  legitimately", so an absent item is either dissolution or loss.
- CLAIM-483 (the record's, empirical, Proposed) holds that such arguments
  fail mainly at identity, beyond this population.
- TERM-056 (A's, Active) keeps question drift apart from query drift and
  from CLAIM-177's concept drift.

Three readings of the drift sit beside the observation, each the
assistant's, interpretive and Proposed:

- **CLAIM-180:** retrieval by content fails outside a critical distance, and
  a code bypasses retrieval by content. It rests on CLAIM-177 and CLAIM-182.
- **CLAIM-191:** drift is iterated transport toward the collaborators'
  framings. Its body says CLAIM-180's reading "by itself has no direction".
- **CLAIM-192:** the thesis moves as an organized whole's course comes apart
  from its members' intentions (CLAIM-030).

QUESTION-099 (A's, Open) asks whether history can stay recoverable while
becoming infeasible to reconstruct. QUESTION-037 (Open) is the line's root
question; its body names CLAIM-177, with CLAIM-185 and CLAIM-178, as the
candidate answer.

## Admission

Unadmitted: each link above is stated by the entry cited for it. Two
integrations within the part were tested, and both fail on a free
parameter.

- **CLAIM-180 and CLAIM-191 as one mechanism**, distant restatements settling
  on the collaborators' framings. CLAIM-180 places a drifted restatement in
  "a neighbouring memory" and disclaims the mapping to a model of the
  collaborator, so which store supplies the attractors is an open slot.
- **CLAIM-191 and CLAIM-192 set against each other.** CLAIM-192 is defeated
  if nothing beyond the members is needed to explain the move. Whether one
  member's framings, iterated, count as beyond the members is not fixed.

## Not stated on the record

- Whether the direction CLAIM-191 predicts and the neighbouring-term
  settling in CLAIM-180 are one failure or two.
- Whether drift toward one member's framings counts, for CLAIM-192, as a
  course apart from the members.

## What it does not say

- That drift has been measured against restatement distance, direction or
  anchoring rate: CLAIM-180, CLAIM-191 and CLAIM-192 each name an untested
  test.
- That the identity failure holds beyond one owner and one assistant family
  (CLAIM-483 is Proposed).
- Anything with standing of its own.
