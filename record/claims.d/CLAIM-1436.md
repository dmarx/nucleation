# CLAIM-1436: Pressure tactics spend the target's peak budget, and flooding spends its total budget

## Two budgets, two families of tactic

[CLAIM-1404](CLAIM-1404.md) says a bounded mind has a peak budget, what working memory
holds at once, and a total budget, what it will spend over a task. Influence
can exhaust either.

- **Peak.** Pressure, urgency, distraction and arousal raise what must be held
  at the moment of decision. The target cannot weigh the case then, and the
  decision does not wait.
- **Total.** Flooding raises what checking costs overall: many claims, many
  sources, contradictions, the "firehose of falsehood" as the RAND account
  names it (from the assistant's memory). Each claim could be checked. All of
  them cannot.

## What flooding buys

A target out of total budget does one of two things:
- **It defers.** That is [CLAIM-1249](CLAIM-1249.md)'s manufactured doubt. The evidence is
  recognized and action waits, which is what the campaign wanted.
- **It falls back on the cheapest heuristic,** usually trust in a source.
  Then the contest moves from the claims to the sources, where the influencer
  may be stronger.

## The asymmetry

Producing a claim costs less than checking it. [CLAIM-1405](CLAIM-1405.md) says the load
of making something legible is paid by someone. Flooding is the producer
refusing to pay, and leaving the load to the evaluator. A language model
lowers the producer's cost close to nothing, so the asymmetry widens. That
is the outside-in case of [CLAIM-1399](CLAIM-1399.md), turned on human readers.
