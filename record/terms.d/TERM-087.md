# TERM-087: cut, and which sense: a Cartesian cut, Pattee's epistemic cut, or Barad's agential cut, enacted by an apparatus

## Definition

"Cut" in this record means one of the following, and a claim should say
which.

1. **Cartesian cut.** A separation of subject from object, or observer from
   observed, taken as fixed before any measurement and independent of it.
   It is Barad's name for what she rejects (A6 §2: "the subject/object
   distinction is treated as antecedent and ontologically fixed").
2. **Epistemic cut (Pattee).** The separation of symbol from referent,
   description from dynamics, controller from controlled, which dynamical
   laws cannot describe without losing the function across it. Measurement
   codes a rate-dependent state into rate-independent symbols, and control
   is its converse; both are executed by non-integrable constraints. Pattee
   holds the cut epistemic, not ontological ([LIT-1190](../literature.d/LIT-1190.md), read in [NOTE-952](../notes.d/NOTE-952.md)).
3. **Agential cut (Barad).** A differentiation enacted within a
   phenomenon, through which some boundaries and properties become
   determinate and others are excluded, so that what functions as
   measuring agency and what as object are told apart "without requiring
   these to have been independently individuated in advance" (A6 §2). Barad's own words, in the one text of
   hers read here ([LIT-1869](../literature.d/LIT-1869.md), [NOTE-1458](../notes.d/NOTE-1458.md)): "A specific intra-action
   enacts an agential cut (in contrast to the Cartesian cut – an inherent
   distinction – between subject and object) effecting a separation between
   “subject” and “object”. That is, the agential cut enacts a “local”
   resolution within the phenomenon of the inherent ontological
   indeterminacy" (2012, p. 32). The
   result is agential separability, "exteriority-within-phenomena": real
   but not absolute separation. What enacts it is an **apparatus**, in
   Barad's sense not an instrument but "an open-ended, dynamic
   configuration of material practices through which particular
   differences become real and intelligible", itself a phenomenon and
   itself reconfigured (A6 §1). Barad's own words, as Faye and Jaksland
   quote them: "apparatuses are the material conditions of possibility and
   impossibility of mattering; they enact what matters and what is
   excluded from mattering" (Barad 2007, 148; [NOTE-1220](../notes.d/NOTE-1220.md)). The cut is
   ontological on Barad's account, and it is movable: "no agential cut is
   an inherent distinction" (Barad 2007, 140, as Jaksland gives it;
   [NOTE-1230](../notes.d/NOTE-1230.md)). It is the Heisenberg cut, movable as Heisenberg held
   against Bohr, made ontological: Jaksland glosses it as "more commonly
   known as a Heisenberg cut" (p. 367), and Faye and Jaksland note that a
   movable separation of object and apparatus is "a view that Heisenberg
   advocates in opposition to Bohr". Of Barad's texts only the 2012 essay is
   read here. It confirms the cut, intra-action and agential separability in
   her words, but uses "apparatus" only of the two-slit set-up and says
   nothing of the cut's movability or of Heisenberg; those parts rest on the
   exchange's account and on Faye and Jaksland's quotations of Barad 2007
   ([LIT-1657](../literature.d/LIT-1657.md), unavailable).

A9 §4 drew the contrast that makes the term worth having: "**Pattee's
epistemic cut is a functional distinction between symbolic description
and physical dynamics. Barad's agential cut is a locally enacted
differentiation within a phenomenon.** They are not interchangeable
concepts. Pattee gives us a clearer mechanism for explaining how
descriptions become physically consequential. Barad challenges the idea
that the describing and described systems have fundamentally independent
identities."

## What it is not

- **A partition.** A cut in the second or third sense is not any division
  of a system into parts. A boundary found by a block criterion
  ([CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md)) is a partition; whether it is also an agential cut is
  [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)'s question.
- **One thing in the record and in physics.** [CLAIM-476](../claims.d/CLAIM-476.md) uses the
  third sense for a record's objects only. That a split scheme is an
  agential cut there says nothing about whether measurement is one.
- **Bohr's separation.** Bohr's own split between object and
  instrument is, on Faye and Jaksland's reading, fixed and epistemic,
  justified by the need for unambiguous classical description, with both
  relata real ([THEORY-1076](../theory.d/THEORY-1076.md)). It is not the third sense, which is
  movable and ontological. Whether it is a Cartesian cut in Barad's sense
  (the first) is not settled here. The Heisenberg cut, movable, is the
  third sense's ancestor (above), and also neighbours the second sense
  through Pattee's use of the von Neumann regress.

## What would settle it

Adoption by the owner, and a reading of Barad herself that confirms or
corrects the third sense. Jaksland 2024 and Faye and Jaksland 2021, read on
2026-10-27, corrected its relation to the Heisenberg cut and supplied
Barad's definition of an apparatus at second hand; the text above is
corrected in place, the TERM being unadopted. Barad 2012 ([NOTE-1458](../notes.d/NOTE-1458.md)),
read 2026-10-30, confirms the cut in her own words and leaves the apparatus
clause and the movability where they were.

## Note of 2026-10-30: what holds it back

The core of the agential sense is now confirmed in Barad's words (2012, p.
32; [NOTE-1458](../notes.d/NOTE-1458.md)). What is not confirmed is that an apparatus enacts the
cut, Barad's definition of an apparatus, and the cut's movability: these
rest only on Faye and Jaksland's quotations of Barad 2007 ([NOTE-1220](../notes.d/NOTE-1220.md),
[NOTE-1230](../notes.d/NOTE-1230.md)). The 2012 essay says "a specific intra-action enacts an
agential cut" and uses "apparatus" only of the two-slit set-up. Those
clauses, which [CLAIM-476](../claims.d/CLAIM-476.md) uses, wait on a reading of [LIT-1657](../literature.d/LIT-1657.md).

## Note of 2026-10-31: one paper, two senses

<!-- inactive-ok-file: CLAIM-476 — Proposed; the claim that uses the third sense -->

Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy (LIT-tmp3mudg) cite Barad (2003, p. 815) for
the agential cut and use it in two senses. At p. 1357: "Describing an
action by starting from a human being is but an analytical choice that
isolates some aspect in a series of continuous individuation processes;
it corresponds to an 'agential cut' ... or the selection of a link in a
chain of agencies", and so "agency is a matter of perspective". That is an
analyst choosing a description of a process individuated without the
choice. It is none of this term's three senses, and nearest an observer's
choice of coarse-graining. At p. 1366, researchers "can only investigate
individuation processes by/while individuating into others". That is
sense 3, a differentiation enacted within a process the analyst belongs
to. The paper does not separate the two, and NOTE-tmpfw3f7's limitation,
that perspectivism and realism pull against each other in it, is this
ambiguity. A citation of the paper for "agential cut" should say which
page it means. No status, title or warrant changes.
