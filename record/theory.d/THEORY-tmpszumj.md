# THEORY-tmpszumj: Kochen–Specker noncontextuality is measurement noncontextuality plus outcome determinism for sharp measurements, and preparation noncontextuality implies that determinism, so every Kochen–Specker proof refutes universal generalized noncontextuality while measurement noncontextuality alone is consistent with quantum theory

## Source

Spekkens (2005), [LIT-tmpevs68](../literature.d/LIT-tmpevs68.md), §III, §VIII.A–B, §IX. Supporting: Budroni et al. ([LIT-tmp0el4c](../literature.d/LIT-tmp0el4c.md)); Abramsky & Brandenburger, [LIT-016](../literature.d/LIT-016.md), Thm 8.1 and Prop 3.1; Spekkens (2014), [LIT-007](../literature.d/LIT-007.md), §IV.B.

## What was actually shown

Spekkens shows that the traditional (Kochen–Specker) notion is measurement noncontextuality — independence of a sharp measurement's representation from how its projectors are grouped — together with outcome determinism, sharp measurements being represented by indicator functions (§III). He then proves that preparation noncontextuality forces outcome determinism for every projective measurement: the ontic supports of an orthonormal basis's preparations must cover the ontic space, because they all decompose the maximally mixed state, whose representation is unique (§VIII.A, Eqs. 77–88). So universal noncontextuality implies Kochen–Specker noncontextuality, and every Kochen–Specker proof is a proof against the universal notion. Measurement noncontextuality on its own is consistent with quantum theory: the Beltrametti–Bugajski model is measurement-noncontextual for all POVMs, and pays with outcome indeterminism and preparation contextuality (§VIII.B). "One cannot confine all the contextuality into the measurements" (§IX).

The held record already uses the link in one direction: Spekkens 2014 rules out a nonnegative quasiprobability representation of the qubit stabilizer theory because that theory contains the Mermin-square and GHZ Kochen–Specker proofs ([LIT-007](../literature.d/LIT-007.md) §IV.B, by citation).

The record's own join: by [LIT-016](../literature.d/LIT-016.md) Thm 8.1 and Prop 3.1, a sheaf-contextual model built from projective measurements has no outcome-deterministic, measurement-noncontextual model; by the above, the operational theory it comes from then has no universally noncontextual model — provided it contains the preparations §VIII.A uses.

## What this does not say

- That the two notions coincide (they do not: see the qubit claim in this group).
- That the implication holds in restricted fragments. §VIII.A uses quantum theory's full preparation set; inside a stabilizer or interferometric fragment ([LIT-019](../literature.d/LIT-019.md)) it must be re-checked.
- Anything about noisy or unsharp Kochen–Specker tests, which is Kunjwal & Spekkens's programme (not held, not read).
- That the sheaf-form statement appears in any source; it is the record's join.
