# SYNTHESIS-tmpy0g7w: A potential is how the record tests whether some ordering prevails: on a finite set it exists exactly when winning moves never cycle and is fixed only up to a class, a least-squares gradient is a cardinal object that can reverse what the wins settle, an ordering is a currency on a finite set and not on a continuum, and in symmetric resource-mediated competition population dynamics descend a convex objective whose split into gradient and remainder depends on a chosen metric

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line: one of the line's eleven
small clusters, four entries on when a process has a potential and what
kind. The line's account is built over it and its sibling parts. [CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md)
and [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md) are also in organizational-affect, where [SYNTHESIS-020](SYNTHESIS-020.md) sets
them out, and [CLAIM-369](../claims.d/CLAIM-369.md) is also in distributed-agency, where [SYNTHESIS-051](SYNTHESIS-051.md)
integrates it; this account integrates them again because their `line`
includes formal-apparatus. [CLAIM-369](../claims.d/CLAIM-369.md) joins the other three by one mention
only (below).*

## The account

- **The ordinal potential.** [TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md) (the record's, from the hierarchy
  the review of PR [#327](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/327) proposed; Active, version 2): an ordering of a
  negotiation's options that every winning move climbs; it exists exactly
  when the wins never cycle, which is ordinal extendibility, and it is
  fixed only up to a class. Of the four senses of "common currency" it
  separates: "They are not one chain of implications."
- **Its existence, and what the Hodge gradient is not.** [CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md) (the
  formal core of the rename of 2026-10-23, with counterexamples from the
  pull request's reviews; thesis, demonstrated, Active): on a finite set a
  potential exists exactly when no chain of winning moves cycles, and the
  longest chain ending at an outcome is one. A perfectly transitive
  complete binary record leaves a cyclic share of (n − 2)/3n under the
  Hodge decomposition. In part 4, "So the gradient is not an ordinal
  potential, and its cyclic share mixes uneven margins with cycles."
  Parts 5 to 11 add that netting erases alternation, that a record fixes
  a class, that a best-fitting ordering of a cycle is no Lyapunov function
  and that a fitted ordering overstates its agreement.
- **Ordering and currency.** [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md) (the formal half of the owner's
  reframing of 2026-10-23; thesis, demonstrated, Active, version 4): on a
  finite set every complete preorder has a real-valued representation; on
  the unit square the lexicographic order has none; a continuous complete
  preorder on a connected separable space has a continuous one; so
  "admitting an ordering is strictly weaker than admitting a currency".
  A lexicographic semiorder can cycle, and successive currencies need not
  share exchange rates.
- **A gradient flow in population dynamics.** [CLAIM-369](../claims.d/CLAIM-369.md) (from a synthesis
  the owner relayed on 2026-10-18, checked and given a proof; thesis,
  demonstrated, Active): with A = UᵀU and r = Uᵀb − c, Lotka–Volterra
  dynamics are multiplicative gradient flow on ½‖b − Un‖² + cᵀn, invasion
  fitness is the negative partial derivative and uninvadable equilibria
  are its KKT points. Its "What it does not say": for a non-potential
  field, "the split depends on a chosen metric and convention and is not
  unique without one". Its note of 2026-10-29, from [THEORY-1168](../theory.d/THEORY-1168.md): "So rᵀx
  rises along every edge and the graph has no cycles."
