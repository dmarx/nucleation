# THEORY-2198: Le Doussal's retrospective holds that for d > 2 the KPZ coupling has an intermediate fixed point separating a weak-noise phase where the nonlinearity is irrelevant from the strong-noise KPZ phase, that the tilt symmetry fixes χ = 2 − z, and that the 1986 conjecture of dimension-independent exponents failed, with χ = 0.3869(4) in two dimensions

## Source

Le Doussal (2025), "Dynamic scaling of growing interfaces", arXiv
2507.08341, [LIT-3062](../literature.d/LIT-3062.md), p. 4, skimmed in [NOTE-2293](../notes.d/NOTE-2293.md).

## The account

From the flow ∂_ℓ g = (2 − d)g + g² + O(g³): "for d ≤ 2 the nonlinear
coupling g flows to large values into the strong noise phase", and "for
d > 2, there is an intermediate fixed point, at g = g* ≃ d − 2, which
separates a weak noise phase where the nonlinearity is irrelevant, and
the strong noise phase". "Galilean invariance (i.e. the STS symmetry)
ensures the relation χ = 2 − z." Kim and Kosterlitz's χ = 2/(d + 3) "is
not exact, with, in d = 2, χ = 0.3869(4)".

## What it does not say

That the strong-noise phase above two dimensions is reached generically:
on this account it needs the coupling past g*, a tuned threshold.
