# SYNTHESIS-023: The authenticity line holds, set aside, that a will is the agent's own to the degree its commitments arise through, remain integrated with and are answerable to its constitutive organization, and that this grounds normativity from a whole to its constituents but not between peers; it was deferred because its ownership criteria do not survive manipulation, and what it has gained since is a candidate content for "integrated", non-domination of the processes that carry a commitment, with the objection that non-domination cannot be read from low supervisory cost, or from override alone, but only under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or from history

## The account

The line was built to answer the ownership question, what makes a
will-organization the agent's own rather than merely effective within it
([QUESTION-011](../questions.d/QUESTION-011.md)). Its answer is [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md), stated in [TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md): a will is the
agent's own "to the degree that its operative commitments arise through,
remain integrated with, and are answerable to its constitutive
organization". [TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md)'s thick form absorbed [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md), cross-scale
concordance, "which is where its circularity worries came from" ([TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md)).
On that account the owner drew a limit: authenticity "gives us an account
for normativity at least from the downward direction of
superagent-->constituents", but not "across agents that share an
environment and level of organizational complexity" ([CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md), quoting
U75). The cross-agent question is [QUESTION-007](../questions.d/QUESTION-007.md), and the assistant's answer
to it, reciprocal agency, is [CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md). Both [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md) and [CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md) rest
on [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md). [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md)'s body records that the exchange did not keep the
directions fixed: A271 "re-mapped authenticity to the 'inward' direction
and left 'downward' to concordance, without noting the change", and
[CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md) says it "presupposes authenticity as the inward term of a
three-direction taxonomy".

The whole line was set aside at the audit of 2026-10-05: "ownership
criteria remain insufficiently defended" ([CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s summary). The reason
was manipulation: "A sophisticated manipulator could engineer all four"
of A263's ownership conditions ([CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s body). [CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md) was deferred
as needing "independent moral argument" (its summary), and [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md)
because "Evaluative standards remain underspecified". Every component
filed before the audit is Deferred.

Later filings kept the line's questions in view without reopening it.

- **Grounds.** [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s note of 2026-10-12 adds [THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md), [THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md),
  [THEORY-029](../theory.d/THEORY-029.md) and [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) as grounds, which "support this claim's 'arise
  through, remain integrated with' against the hierarchical account", and
  notes that [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) finds no historical condition shown to escape its
  own objection. [QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md) asks whether integration has any mark other
  than the agent's avowal: "If it has none, the ownership criterion cannot
  be told apart from endorsement." [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md) asks what settling
  ownership would buy, and whether accountability needs conditions
  ownership does not supply.
- **A candidate content for "integrated".** The owner, at U45 of the
  exchange of 2026-10-17, proposed that "'authenticity' should be treated
  as an organizational achievement characterized by non-domination of
  subcomponents" ([CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)). [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) calls itself "an alternative
  reading of [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s middle condition, not a premise of it", and "a
  candidate constituent of authenticity ... not a sufficient criterion of
  ownership". A46 refined non-domination to "continued access to
  consequential roles". [TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md)'s note of 2026-10-18 says [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) gives
  the term's "remain integrated with" a content.
- **The objection.** [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md), A45's: processes conditioned never to
  object "coordinate without override at the same behaviour and
  supervisory cost as processes that negotiate", so the two part "only
  under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or in their history".
  It is "the manipulation objection ([THEORY-1118](../theory.d/THEORY-1118.md)) ... applied to the new
  criterion", and "does not say the criterion fails, only that cost cannot
  certify it" (its summary). The inference it defeats is [ARG-030](../arguments.d/ARG-030.md), from
  [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md)'s economy to [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)'s non-domination, which [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)
  undercuts "while leaving [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) standing" ([ARG-030](../arguments.d/ARG-030.md)'s verdict).
  On [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s manipulation defeater, its note of 2026-10-18 says it "is
  not met by either" [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) or [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md), and [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s body that it
  "is still outstanding".
- **The cases.** [CASE-129](../cases.d/CASE-129.md), the alarm that learned and the alarm that was
  disabled, makes [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s point a pair: "a quiet signal may represent
  either successful learning or a loss of the capacity to learn", and the
  test is "**response to changed evidence**". [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md), the voluntary
  delegation, marks the converse limit: "whether a constituent is
  dominated cannot be read from whether it is overridden, but from who
  authorized the override and whether it stays answerable". [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s
  note of 2026-10-22 joins the two: "So whether a constituent is
  dominated cannot be read from whether it is overridden."

Whether the line reopens is the owner's call: [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md), [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md) and
[ARG-030](../arguments.d/ARG-030.md) each say so, and the Deferred components' later notes leave their
status unchanged.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each route of the test ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5) was
tried against the components as written.

- **Override in neither direction shows domination.** That domination is
  read neither from the absence of override ([CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)) nor from its
  presence ([CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)), but from authorization, answerability and response
  under intervention. It is stated: [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s note of 2026-10-22 ("So
  whether a constituent is dominated cannot be read from whether it is
  overridden") and [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)'s body. A18's summary, quoted in that note:
  "The common mistake is to infer something about the **distribution of
  practical authority** from the **absence of overt struggle**."
- **Authenticity gives at most a downward ground, and the cross-agent one
  needs independent argument.** Stated: [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md), [QUESTION-007](../questions.d/QUESTION-007.md)'s body,
  and [CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md)'s note of 2026-10-18 ("interdependence ⇏ obligation").
- **Non-domination does not repair [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md) against manipulation.**
  Stated: [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) ("it doesn't yet settle the manipulation objection",
  A45), [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s body, [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s note of 2026-10-18.
- **Non-domination as a non-avowal mark of integration ([QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md)).**
  Not stated; see below. As a derivation it is trivial once its parameter
  is closed, and as a prediction no component bears on it.
- **Non-domination as the content of the downward norm ([CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md)).** Not
  stated; see below. On the reading that the whole owes its constituents
  non-domination it runs against [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)'s "We are proposing an
  organizational criterion, not asserting rights for neural modules"; on
  the other reading it needs a premise no component holds.

**Whether the line falls into parts.** Not into separate ones. Counting

either's body or summary, and dividing the line into ownership ([CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md),
[TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md), [QUESTION-011](../questions.d/QUESTION-011.md), [QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md), [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md), [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md), [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md),

[CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md), [QUESTION-007](../questions.d/QUESTION-007.md), [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md)): 18 pairs within the first, 2 within
the second, and 5 across. All 5 run through [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md) or [TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md): the two
`rests_on` from [CLAIM-096](../claims.d/CLAIM-096.md) and [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md), [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s `uses` of [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md),

## Not stated on the record

Integrations the components leave open. Each would need to pass a route to
be filed.

- **Non-domination as a mark of integration other than avowal.**
  [QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md) asks whether integration has a mark other than avowal;
  [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) offers non-domination as a candidate constituent of
  integration; [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md) and [CASE-129](../cases.d/CASE-129.md) give interventions that part
  negotiated from conditioned coordination. No entry joins them. Read as
  "a partial mark", it follows trivially once constituency is granted; read
  as "a mark of integration", [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) concedes it is not sufficient.
  [QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md)'s answer condition, a measure that "predicts something
  avowal does not and survives a multitrait-multimethod design", is met by
  no component.
- **Non-domination as what the downward ground requires.** [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md)'s
  downward direction and [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)'s non-domination are not linked by any
  entry, and [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) disclaims standing for subprocesses.
- **Whether [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)'s authorization condition escapes manipulation.**
  [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md) makes "who authorized the arrangement" the question; [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)
  was deferred because a manipulator could engineer its conditions, and
  [QUESTION-011](../questions.d/QUESTION-011.md)'s note of 2026-10-31 says a recognition-based account is
  "more open to the same objection, not less". No entry asks whether an
  authorization condition is open to it too.
- **Whether settling ownership by non-domination would settle
  accountability.** [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md) asks it of ownership in general; no entry
  asks it of [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)'s candidate.

## What it does not say

- That the line is reopened. Every pre-audit component is Deferred, and
  [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md), [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md) and [ARG-030](../arguments.d/ARG-030.md) were filed without reopening it.
- That non-domination is ownership. [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) is "a candidate constituent
  ... not a sufficient criterion of ownership", and its defeater excludes
  cases that show insufficiency.
- That the non-domination criterion fails. [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md): "Non-domination
  stated counterfactually, as A45 and A46 state it, survives the
  objection."
- That history settles manipulation. [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md): [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) "notes that no
  historical condition in the record is shown to escape its own
  objection".
- That an agent who overrides is not the author of the act ([CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md),
  after [CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md)).
- Anything with standing of its own.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Étienne Brown, read

From the reading in [NOTE-1792](../notes.d/NOTE-1792.md) ([LIT-2425](../literature.d/LIT-2425.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: **leaves open; adds a relational-epistemic dimension
the line lacks.** Brown's volitional alignment is the Frankfurtian hierarchy
the line's ownership account goes beyond, and he does not address the
manipulation objection that set the line aside (whether a recommender could
shape the second-order desires themselves). His self-understanding
dimension, relational and fed by others' (and systems') testimony, is a
dimension the synthesis does not name: authenticity as partly knowing one's
commitments, with the risk (Farah) that an opaque source produces false
self-attributions. That bears on "answerable to": a commitment revised on a
false reading of oneself is answerable to a mistaken model. It neither
supports nor defeats the line's criteria.
