# CASE-036: Northstar emergency governance that sustains itself

## The case

Deployments frozen, production access restricted, review suspended. Deferred
maintenance and workarounds accumulate, the system becomes harder to read,
and the anomalous signals it emits satisfy the emergency criteria.

## What it can show

That the operative regime and its governing organization can persist while the
capacity to transform them degrades. It is the premise of [ARG-007](../arguments.d/ARG-007.md).

Not shown: that the anomalies come from the regime rather than from the
underlying failure. The first draft's test for that ("contributes materially")
did not survive the rewrite.
