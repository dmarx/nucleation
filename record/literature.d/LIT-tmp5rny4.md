# LIT-tmp5rny4: Negligible fronto-parietal BOLD activity accompanying unreportable switches in bistable perception

Brascamp, Blake & Knapen (2015), Nature Neuroscience 18(11): 1672-1678. DOI 10.1038/nn.4130.

## Key takeaways

- Rivalry switches can occur without being noticed: in the same-colour
  condition detection is at chance by d', and hits are about an eighth of
  the conspicuous rate.
- Fronto-parietal switch responses then match a response proportional to
  reportability, with no remainder a causal role would leave.
- Subtracting replayed switches overestimates a causal component.
