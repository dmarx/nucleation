# THEORY-tmpfgob7: Affect is the felt transition of a body's power to affect and be affected toward a diminished or augmented capacitation

## Source

Massumi, interviewed by McKim, Inflexions 3 (2009), [LIT-1905](../literature.d/LIT-1905.md), pp. 1–3,
read in [NOTE-1488](../notes.d/NOTE-1488.md). What Massumi says is [THEORY-1282](THEORY-1282.md), which this entry
extends.

## The claim

That affect is what Massumi's proposition says: a transition in a body's
power to affect and be affected, toward a diminished or augmented
capacitation, and felt. He attributes it to Spinoza as Deleuze took it up,
and he offers no evidence for it.

## What would settle it

A way to tell, before an episode resolves, that capacitation changed, in
which direction, and that the change was felt, picking out cases an
account in terms of emotion or appraisal does not. The promote_when says
so. Massumi himself refuses that use of the definition (p. 3).
