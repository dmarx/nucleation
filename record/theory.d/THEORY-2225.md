# THEORY-2225: Future-self link to posterity is correlational

## The account

Across ten US studies, Syropoulos, Law, Coleman and Young find that
continuity with one's own future self and consideration of one's own future
consequences correlate with felt responsibility for future generations
(meta-analytic r = .21 and .54), partly through identification with and
efficacy toward future generations. Writing tasks that raised future-self
continuity raised responsibility in one pre-registered experiment and not in
its replication, and never raised identification, so the route from self to
future others is shown as association, not cause.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-2315](../notes.d/NOTE-2315.md); its key results are quoted there by study.
The identification measure mixes connection, empathy and perspective-taking.

## Bearing

On [QUESTION-301](../questions.d/QUESTION-301.md), as evidence on the bridge from prudence to legacy:
continuity with one's own future self goes with concern for future people
at r = .21, and manipulating it does not reliably raise that concern.
Legacy itself is not measured.
