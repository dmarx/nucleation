# SYNTHESIS-056: An organizational perspective is not thereby an agent, and what an organization can see, take up and correct are separate achievements that its success and coherence do not show: a description adequate for the whole can merge what its members' claims distinguish, which distinctions must be legible is relative to a task and to a policy, an arrangement of institutions can keep its effectiveness while its correctability degrades, and the wrong of reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining an organization is proposed as a breach of a bounded duty of answerability

*A part account of the distributed-agency line ("Distributed agency"): the
part filed mostly from the exchanges of 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-27 (on
effective description, legibility, perspective and agency, and "About the
Chagossians") and from the two shares of "Individuation, Possibility, and
Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07). The line's account is built over it and
the line's other part accounts. The mechanical community map put two
other inquiries with it, which this round drafts as accounts of their own
(availability and arrangement, [SYNTHESIS-057](SYNTHESIS-057.md); partners coming to depend on one another, [SYNTHESIS-053](SYNTHESIS-053.md)).
What stays here is one inquiry: 182 pairs of its members are linked by a
relation or a mention, 135 among the entries on legibility, correction and
the wrong, 27 among those on perspectives, and 20 joining the two. Several
members are first in another line and are also set out in that line's
accounts: [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md) (organizational-normativity) holds the wrong
beyond harm, [SYNTHESIS-035](SYNTHESIS-035.md), [SYNTHESIS-039](SYNTHESIS-039.md), [SYNTHESIS-041](SYNTHESIS-041.md) and [SYNTHESIS-044](SYNTHESIS-044.md)
(will-organization) hold [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md), [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md) and
[CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md), [SYNTHESIS-020](SYNTHESIS-020.md) (organizational-affect) holds [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) and
[CASE-221](../cases.d/CASE-221.md), and [SYNTHESIS-025](SYNTHESIS-025.md) (learned-structure) holds [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) and
[QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md). This account integrates them again because their `line`
includes distributed-agency.*

## The account

### Perspectives, selves and agents

- **Perspectives for selves.** [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md) (the owner's, U31 of 2026-10-22;
  thesis, interpretive, Proposed): what the record explained by "self" in
  its dispositional, persisting, self-governing and integrated senses "is
  better explained by organizational perspectives", with a perspective, its
  carriers, its lineage and its representations kept apart. Its note of
  2026-10-30 says what holds it back: habitus and transposable schemas
  "may explain the same transitions, and no model in the record
  out-predicts them at matched complexity, which is the critical question
  [ARG-025](../arguments.d/ARG-025.md) fails". [ARG-025](../arguments.d/ARG-025.md) (the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration of [CLAIM-427](../claims.d/CLAIM-427.md); evidence,
  Proposed) concludes [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md) from the lab's runs and the reported
  Northstar study, and its verdict is "The third grade is still unmet."
  [CLAIM-427](../claims.d/CLAIM-427.md) (Superseded by [ARG-025](../arguments.d/ARG-025.md)) held the three grades. The cases:
  [CASE-130](../cases.d/CASE-130.md) (Gracie jiu-jitsu and luta livre), [CASE-137](../cases.d/CASE-137.md) (Clive Wearing) and
  [CASE-139](../cases.d/CASE-139.md) (Chinese martial traditions through the Cultural Revolution),
  each documented and Active, illustrate [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md); [CASE-166](../cases.d/CASE-166.md) (Northstar as
  reported, Proposed) illustrates [CLAIM-427](../claims.d/CLAIM-427.md) and [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md).
- **A perspective is not thereby an agent.** [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) (A38's, at the
  owner's U38; thesis, interpretive, Active): possessing, enacting or being
  shaped by an organizational perspective "does not establish an
  independent agent", and "perspectival boundaries, agency boundaries, and
  institutional boundaries need not coincide". [ARG-048](../arguments.d/ARG-048.md) (Proposed) adds
  Tsoukas and Chia's radial extension as a mechanism for its third part,
  "A second line for an Active claim, not its ground". [ARG-047](../arguments.d/ARG-047.md) (Rejected)
  withdraws a reading of Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy as a version of it:
  their perspective "is the analyst's choice of where to cut a chain of
  agencies". [CASE-145](../cases.d/CASE-145.md) (A4's proposed study; stipulated, Active) would test
  the separation in a constructed system. [QUESTION-260](../questions.d/QUESTION-260.md) (A38's, as A39
  phrased it; Open) asks when a perspective is constitutive of an agent's
  practical organization rather than one that influences it. [TERM-080](../terms.d/TERM-080.md) (the
  owner's U12, A12's sense map; Proposed) separates participatory,
  institutionally constituted and attributed collective intention.
- **Interruption and reinstatement.** [CLAIM-421](../claims.d/CLAIM-421.md) (A37's, at the owner's U37;
  thesis, interpretive, Active): a perspective's enactment can cease while
  "the dispositions, capacities or reproductive relationships from which it
  can be reinstated" remain, and the interruption can be of activation, of
  enactment or of reproduction. [CASE-139](../cases.d/CASE-139.md) is among its grounds; [CASE-136](../cases.d/CASE-136.md) (the
  doorway effect; documented reported study, Active) illustrates it, in
  [CLAIM-421](../claims.d/CLAIM-421.md)'s words "one illustration, not a ground of the distinction". [QUESTION-257](../questions.d/QUESTION-257.md) (A37's, in
  A39's wording; Open) asks how the kinds are told apart in a case: "[CLAIM-421](../claims.d/CLAIM-421.md)
  names the kinds. It does not say how evidence tells them apart".
- **Competition over the test.** [CLAIM-416](../claims.d/CLAIM-416.md) (from the owner's U35 and
  A35–A37; thesis, interpretive, Active): "Competing perspectives compete
  over the tests that measure them as well as under them". It grounds on
  [CASE-130](../cases.d/CASE-130.md), which calls itself "[CLAIM-416](../claims.d/CLAIM-416.md)'s case".
- **Timescales and authority.** [CLAIM-423](../claims.d/CLAIM-423.md) (the owner's U28, A28's
  strongest line; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md) and
  holds that components changing at different speeds differ in "which
  forms of influence each can accumulate, exercise and keep", so a state
  can stay continuous while "the organization producing its positions
  changes under it".

### The whole and its members

- **Success at one level.** [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md) (the owner's thread at U37 and U39;
  thesis, interpretive, Active): "An encompassing organization's success
  does not establish the well-being, viability or autonomy of its
  constituents — and the converse holds too". It grounds on [CASE-172](../cases.d/CASE-172.md).
- **Coarse-graining fixed by the target.** [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) (A21 §18's Candidate
  2; thesis, interpretive, Active) rests on [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md), [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) and
  [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md): a description adequate for an institution's performance "can
  merge states its members' claims must distinguish", and "the target
  cannot be justified solely by the description that preserves it".
  [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md) (A22's Construction C; fictional construction, Active) is its
  ground: total output preserves every institutional judgment and merges
  (6, 6) with (10, 2). Its own body says "That the logical point is new" is
  not claimed: "It is [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) and [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md) together. What the exchange
  added is the coarse-graining form and the clause against circularity."

### Legibility and what it is for

- **The term.** [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) (the owner's, U22 of 2026-10-20, defined by A22;
  Active): legibility is "a relation among a representation, an observer's
  rule class, its resources and a task", bounded by what the source allows
  an ideal receiver, and directed.
- **Relative to a task.** [CLAIM-455](../claims.d/CLAIM-455.md) (stated in [CASE-154](../cases.d/CASE-154.md); thesis,
  demonstrated, Active) uses [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md): one transformation can make one
  task's distinction legible and another's illegible for the same bounded
  observer, "so legibility regimes are ordered only relative to a task".
  [CASE-154](../cases.d/CASE-154.md) (stipulated construction, Active) is its ground and adds that
  "a failure of one perspective under a legibility regime may be a
  resource limit and not an exclusion".
- **Relative to a policy.** [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) (A33 §4, with A30 and A32; thesis,
  demonstrated, Active; first line will-organization): a policy acceptable
  in every circumstance exists exactly when the circumstances an
  organization cannot tell apart share an acceptable action, and "which
  distinctions must be made legible depends on which acceptable action the
  organization commits to". [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md) (A30's, the second and third
  conditions the reader's; thesis, demonstrated, Active; first line
  will-organization) is a concrete instance, "[CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) states the same in
  general". [CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md) (A58 §3's; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; first line
  will-organization) rests on [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md): "a difference in the distinctions
  two perspectives draw is not by itself evidence that either is
  deficient".
- **Coherence produced by what is admitted.** [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) (counter,
  interpretive, Active; first line organizational-affect) objects to
  [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md): where a legibility regime selects which contests are admitted
  or recorded, "observed coherence is not evidence that the organization's
  perspectives are integrated". [CASE-221](../cases.d/CASE-221.md) (the reader's construction;
  Proposed) and [ARG-066](../arguments.d/ARG-066.md) (Proposed) build its existence half; [ARG-073](../arguments.d/ARG-073.md)
  (Proposed) finds [THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md) corroborates only its weaker half.
- **The rungs of uptake.** [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md) (A24 §6, on the owner's U24; thesis,
  interpretive, Active) rests on [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md): admission, recognition,
  credibility, uptake and a part in revising the standards of uptake are
  separable, "so an organization can widen access while every channel by
  which a less authoritative contribution could change the criteria of
  authority stays closed". [CASE-132](../cases.d/CASE-132.md) (A18's CASE O; fictional, Active)
  illustrates it: "Recognition without uptake."
- **Three achievements.** [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) (the owner's synthesis of 2026-10-31;
  thesis, interpretive, Active; first line will-organization) uses
  [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md), [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) and [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): for a normatively relevant discrepancy
  to change what an organization does, legibility, volitional efficacy and
  correctability are needed, "and any can fail while the others hold".

### Correction across institutions

- **The term.** [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md) (A10's α_J, on the owner's U10; Active):
  correctability is the chance that an arrangement "finds, authorizes,
  coordinates and carries out a plan achieving the objective before the
  deadline and within its resources". Its note of 2026-10-31 records A25's
  bound: correctability is bounded by legibility, "and it is not filed as a
  new claim".
- **Effective action is not correctability.** [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) (A8's and A9's;
  thesis, interpretive, Active) rests on [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md) and [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) and uses
  [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): an arrangement of institutions "can keep, or increase, its
  capacity for effective action while its capacity to be effectively
  corrected by others degrades".
- **An intervention can change what correcting it takes.** [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) (A7
  to A9, from the owner's U6 and U7; thesis, interpretive, Active), by
  three mechanisms: physical irreversibility ([CASE-170](../cases.d/CASE-170.md), the East Wing),
  enforcement-mediated entrenchment ([CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md), the US Institute of Peace)
  and jurisdictional displacement ([CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md), CECOT), each a documented
  specimen, Active.
- **Restoration is not reversal.** [CLAIM-463](../claims.d/CLAIM-463.md) (A4's candidate, filed at the
  owner's request; thesis, demonstrated, Active) grounds on [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md) (A7
  §4's normal form; fictional construction, Active): "Restoring the
  conditions under which a capacity was lost need not restore the
  capacity".
- **No unilateral power is not no power.** [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md) (A8's and A9's, on
  the owner's CECOT case; thesis, demonstrated, Active) grounds on
  [CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md): "a true statement of unilateral inability is not a statement
  that the outcome is unattainable".
- **The cases.** [CASE-169](../cases.d/CASE-169.md) (the Chagossians), [CASE-172](../cases.d/CASE-172.md) (federal science and
  education), [CASE-170](../cases.d/CASE-170.md), [CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md) and [CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md), all the owner's cases from
  the exchange of 2026-10-26, documented specimens, Active.
- **Open.** [QUESTION-041](../questions.d/QUESTION-041.md) (filed in the fourth synthesis pass; Open) asks
  what channels from members keep a whole's course correctable.
  [QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md) (A9's; Open) refines it to several institutions "without
  their forming one collective agent". [QUESTION-279](../questions.d/QUESTION-279.md) (A10's, on the owner's
  U10; Open) asks what would certify "that no remedy is available to an
  institutional arrangement".

### The wrong beyond harm

- **The reproductive form.** [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) (the owner's, 2026-10-23; thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed; also will-organization) rests on [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md): a
  protective regime becomes self-maintaining when its interventions shift
  the evidence and authority that judge it, "and it is a failure only where
  the regime has become less responsive to evidence that it is no longer
  needed".
- **The owner's thesis.** [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md) (the owner's presupposition at U27 of
  the second share; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; first line
  organizational-normativity) rests on [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) and [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) and grounds
  on [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md): a self-maintaining organization that harms and reproduces the
  projections that make its harm non-legible "commits a counter-normativity
  distinct from the harm and attributable to it". Its body: "The parts are
  on the record; the wrong is not." [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md) (the owner's U27,
  verbatim; Open; first line organizational-normativity) asks what that
  counter-normativity is.
- **A's answers.** [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) (A's, A60 to A62; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed, admitted on a narrower title) rests on [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md), [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md),
  [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) and [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md): the wrong is "a breach of a
  second-order duty of answerability", and "a verdict only given an
  independently justified standard and a feasible alternative". [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md)
  (A's, A62; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; first line
  organizational-normativity) rests on [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) and [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md): an
  organization can be disposed to answer a credible challenge by reducing
  the capacity for future challenge, "so that the later absence of
  challenges reads as success".
- **The constructions and arguments.** [CASE-228](../cases.d/CASE-228.md) (A62 §4, rerun and
  corrected by the reader; fictional construction, Proposed) is a variant
  of [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md) and illustrates [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md); [ARG-063](../arguments.d/ARG-063.md) (Proposed) argues from it
  and "shows nothing normative". [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md) (A's matched-harm construction;
  stipulated, Proposed; first line organizational-normativity) is the ground
  of [ARG-055](../arguments.d/ARG-055.md) (Proposed; first line organizational-normativity), which
  concludes [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md). [ARG-064](../arguments.d/ARG-064.md) (analogy, Proposed; first line
  organizational-normativity) rests on [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) and concludes [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md):
  "the reproductive mechanism transfers and the verdict does not".
