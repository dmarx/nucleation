# NOTE-tmpkzn27: George and Bates (2022) — Mechanisms Underlying Influence of Bioelectricity in Development

## Contribution

A review of how ion channels and membrane potential (Vmem) act in development: human channelopathies with developmental phenotypes, conserved roles across species, spontaneous calcium oscillations in non-excitable tissue, and the cellular processes (death, proliferation, polarity, migration) and signalling pathways (BMP, Notch, Wnt, Hedgehog) that channels touch. It proposes calcium as the main mediator and a model in which Vmem and calcium oscillations, shaped by mechanical forces, coordinate cells across a tissue.

## Key results

- Synthesis: bioelectric signalling may be "a high-level mechanism to coordinate the complex process of development of a tissue."
- "Calcium appears to be the major player in bioelectrical signaling."
- On mechanism: "While it is becoming increasingly evident that ion channels are essential for proper development, the mechanism by which they act is still unclear."
- Open question: "it is not well understood whether these effects are due to direct regulation of these pathways or due to downstream effects on cell cycle, cell death, or cell migration."
- Barrier: "there is a limited ability to control a single ionic pathway without impacting others."

## Assumptions

None stated beyond the above.

## Bearing on the record

**How far it stands in.** Restates the developmental-bioelectricity evidence and the gaps in mechanism. It does not cover tissue repair and disease contexts, endogenous electric fields and electrotaxis, or anything after early 2022.

It neither supports nor refutes Levin's interpretation; it shows the mainstream does not need it.
