# Article 4. The Architecture of Will

## Authority, organizational affect, and the negotiation of incompatible demands

**Abstract.** For an organization to act together, it has to settle which concerns, proposals and interventions take effect. The Nucleation record calls the relations that settle this a will-organization. It holds that precedence, jurisdiction and suspension are distinct relations of practical authority, and that the precedence a motive claims differs from the authority it is granted. This article sets out that account and its standing. One executed study (CASE-325, Active; rerun on 2026-11-25 by a session other than its filer's, with every figure reproduced) compares a model of these relations with five control-only models in a stipulated organization. Where the organization is as stipulated, the relational model wins, and it alone predicts combinations of changes in authority that training never showed together. It loses where authority is idle or idiosyncratic. In a world where one relation composes otherwise than the model says, it still wins the training evidence and then mispredicts. The record's own reading of the study (CLAIM-890, Proposed) concedes that for an organization institutional theory already writes these relations, so against institutional theory what the study supports is not a gain. Against control theory and executive control, the same reading holds that the will-organization variables are "implemented and not replaced". The article then takes up the formal results on recursive grants and directed tolerances, the owner's thesis that frustration and not distress is the primitive of regulatory difficulty, and the record's functional account of organizational affect. Every entry that states that account is Proposed, and the objection that holds it back is unanswered. Last come learned accommodation, flow, and orderings that cannot be integrated. That low conflict and fluent coordination do not show accommodation to be adequate is held in parts of different standing: a quiet perspective need not be satisfied (CLAIM-549, Active); where a legibility regime selects which contests are recorded, coherence is no evidence of integration (CLAIM-448, Active); and conditioned and negotiating processes look alike in behaviour and supervisory cost (CLAIM-364, Proposed). That a learned accommodation lowers the cost of arbitration is argued, not shown (CLAIM-358, Proposed). The record has priced the per-encounter cost that escalation would save, in one fit to published human choices (CASE-382, Proposed), but not the recurrence.

## 4.1 Why a theory of will-organization beside control theory

A51 leads with CASE-325, and so does this article. Its question is QUESTION-056's (Open): "When the mechanisms composing a will-organization are described by control theory and executive-control research, does that description implement the will-organization account or replace it, and what result would show the agent-level description had become idle?"

### 4.1.1 What was run, and what was to count

LEAD-089 (Done since 2026-11-25; run on 2026-11-22, by its note of that date) asked for Northstar (CASE-023) to be modelled "with control and metacontrol mechanisms alone and then with a will-organization over them", for the difference to be priced, and for "planning, control and institutional theory" to be set "beside the account". The assistant carried it out as a delegated worker. The study is CASE-325 (Active, executed study, the assistant's; scratch/studies/lead-089/).

*The criteria, written before the run.* QUESTION-056: "Replacement is shown if the mechanism model predicts as well with no will-organization variables." LEAD-089: "A difference the control-only model cannot produce at matched complexity supports CLAIM-487's gain; none, or one an existing theory already gives, defeats CLAIM-024." The results below are graded against these.

- **Episodes.** Each episode decides which of three regimes becomes effective: routine deployment, cautious verification or emergency suspension.
- **Authority.** Three bits, each one of the case's own variants (CASE-002, CASE-003). The first is whether an objection suspends deployment. The second is who classifies severity. The third is whether senior management may override a freeze. "The proposals are the same under every arrangement", and so are each party's propensities.
- **The models.** The will-organization model (WO) has four parameters, and "Maintained relations decide which proposal becomes effective". Against it are five control-only models, C0 to C4. "Each sees the arrangement of authority as an input. None has a variable for a relation." C2 is a metacontrol gate, "the form of an expected-value-of-control choice among regimes". The control models have 16, 128, 14, 38 and 64 parameters.
- **Training and test.** Training sees "the baseline and each single change of authority". The test is "2,000 fresh episodes under each of the four combinations never seen in training". There are four worlds (governed, idle, idiosyncratic, interacting), four sample sizes and 20 seeds.

### 4.1.2 What was found

The results are CASE-325's, reproduced in full by the rerun of 2026-11-25. The finding drawn from them is CLAIM-889 (Proposed, demonstrated, the assistant's).

- **Governed world.** "WO beats every control-only model by more than its Occam cost on 80 of 80 seeds". The median log Bayes factor, with its range over seeds, runs from 17.1 (10.0 to 22.2) at n = 30 to 61.3 (49.1 to 70.7) at n = 1000. On the unseen combinations "WO is within 0.015 nats per episode of the truth, and every gate is 0.03 to 0.26 nats per episode away". By QUESTION-056's criterion, the control models do not predict as well, so replacement is not shown.
- **The gap does not close.** At 10⁶ episodes per training arrangement, the gates' regret on the unseen combinations stays at 0.08–0.11 (C2), 0.022–0.034 (C3) and 0.009–0.018 (C4). The case's explanation: "A log-additive gate cannot represent how the relations compose. The products of two authority bits that would let it are never observed when each change is tried alone."
- **Idle and idiosyncratic worlds.** "WO loses on every seed, by 0.9 to 2,551 nats." "So the test can come out against the will-organization."
- **Interacting world.** This is the world A51 leaves out. It has the same relations, "except that where the reliability group classifies it also takes objections over, so an objection no longer suspends by itself", which "shows only in combined arrangements". In this world "WO wins the training evidence by 10 to 82 nats on every seed, because the misplaced relation never shows in training. It then predicts the unseen combinations worse than the additive gate C2: 0.26 to 0.32 nats per episode against 0.16 to 0.20". So a wrongly composed will-organization passes CLAIM-174's test 4 when that test is priced on training data alone. CASE-325 concludes: "The pricing has to be done on held-out interventions." CLAIM-889 puts it into its title: "priced on the training arrangements alone, the same test also passes a will-organization whose relations compose otherwise than it says".

A51 calls the study "considerably better evidence than a demonstration that a relational model can reproduce ordinary behavior". That is accurate about the design: it has a comparison, held-out interventions and worlds in which it can fail. It overstates what the record has done with the result. The run now has a second session's check, but CLAIM-889 is Proposed. A51 also omits the interacting world, which shows that the study's headline measure, evidence on the training arrangements, cannot tell a right composition from a wrong one. Only the held-out combinations tell them apart.

## 4.2 Differentiated authority

The vocabulary is Active. A will-organization is "The maintained organization of relations governing the conditions under which an agent's available action-generating regimes acquire, retain, lose, or alter practical efficacy" (TERM-023). Three of its relations are "easily conflated: precedence orders applicable constraints, jurisdiction governs which apply, suspension governs when an applicable one may be disabled" (TERM-017). "Precedence, jurisdiction and suspension can vary independently" (CLAIM-057, Active, demonstrated, on Northstar's classification variants). The will paper's central thesis is that which regime becomes effective "depends on maintained relations — accessibility, precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation — that are not reducible to the contents of the competing regimes, whatever mechanisms realize them" (CLAIM-027, Active, interpretive). In CASE-325 the three authority bits are a suspension, a jurisdiction and an override of precedence. The study varies them separately, and its result turns on how they compose. Holding the proposals fixed while the arrangement changes is the design CLAIM-122 (Active, interpretive) states: "changing the conditions of efficacy changes behaviour while the competing preferences stay fixed".

Two distinctions keep authority apart from what it is often taken for.

- **Claimed against granted.** "The precedence a motive claims over action and the authority the rest of the organization grants it are different quantities: a motive can claim control without receiving it, and be granted control it does not claim" (CLAIM-283, Active, interpretive; A4 §2, reading Frijda; made Active at the owner's ruling). The claim "does not say which wins". Its first version also identified internal conflict with the gap between the two. That identification is now a separate claim, "Internal conflict is the gap between the precedence a motive claims over action and the authority the rest of the organization grants it" (CLAIM-484, Proposed). This article uses only the distinction.
- **Activation, grant and efficacy.** The owner's U18 objected to an intervention threshold set by expected benefit: "an intervention doesn't need to be predicted to be effective to be motivating. consider someone punching a wall in anger." The record holds that a process's "activation, the authority the organization grants it and the efficacy of what it does are separate quantities, so a threshold on expected net benefit is one special case of an activation threshold and not its general form" (CLAIM-548, Active, interpretive, the owner's point in A's wording). Its rival, a goal-directed reading of costly aggression (NOTE-1117), is cited in the claim.

## 4.3 Recursive grants

A51 names SYNTHESIS-087 (Proposed) as "especially relevant". Its components are Active and demonstrated, and they come from A2's analysis of the owner's U2 design.

- "Forbidding self-granting does not stop an operator from amplifying its own authority: under damped recursive delegation, reciprocal and cyclic grants recycle authority, so an operator's authority depends on its own grants, while one-hop allocation within an epoch, from budgets and baselines fixed independently of outgoing grants, makes it exactly independent of them" (CLAIM-282, Active, demonstrated, role granted).
- Under one-hop allocation, granting influence to a nearer ally "raises how far its preferred policy is realized while its own authority stays fixed, so the two conditions of the grant scheme hold together" (CLAIM-375, Active, demonstrated).

The account declines the tempting summary. "Only one-hop allocation, among the rules on record, keeps both conditions" fails "as adding nothing on record, and is open beyond it" (SYNTHESIS-087, "Not stated on the record"). All three components disclaim "That any rule closes the social route of moving others' grants" (ibid.). ## 4.4 Tolerances as endorsement criteria

The owner's U12 of "Organizational Nucleation and SLT" (2026-11-04): "i was thinking more along the linesof tolerances expressing endorsement criteria relative to some given perspective's priorities. maybe I'm confusing necessary vs sufficient conditions." The record answers in A's wording, sharpened by the reader:

> "A perspective's tolerance for another process's contribution is its criterion for conferring influence on that contribution, set by its own priorities: tolerances are directed, and the discrepancy each measures is the perspective's own, so inside its tolerance the perspective's interventions raise the other's share of future influence and outside it they lower it; meeting the tolerance is necessary for that conferral where the criterion binds, and sufficient only for a grant, under a rule that grants influence on it" (CLAIM-552, Active, interpretive; promoted under a delegated ruling).

The owner's worry about necessary and sufficient conditions is answered inside the claim. Meeting a tolerance is necessary for conferral "where the criterion binds". It is sufficient only for a grant, and only "under a rule that grants influence on it". It is not sufficient for influence, which the channel or the rule may fail to carry: the defeater excludes "a grant that fails to raise influence because the channel or the rule does not carry it".

Four results surround it, and none of them makes a quiet perspective a consenting one.

- **Where a tolerance comes from.** This is the first link of the owner's U19 chain, "motivational threshold -> motivational tolerances". If activation is z = b(c) + α(c)d and the perspective acts with probability σ(κ(z − θ)), the discrepancy it tolerates while acting with probability at most ε is "(θ + κ⁻¹ logit ε − b(c))/α(c)". So one fixed threshold gives different tolerances across contexts, and "raising a threshold enlarges tolerance without relieving any" demand (CLAIM-550, Active, demonstrated; re-derived at its status review). SYNTHESIS-044 notes that only this first link is given: whether QUESTION-372's chain "holds link by link" is not stated on the record.
- **Quiet need not be satisfied.** "The quiescent complex … is not the compatibility complex of their requirements … a perspective can be quiet while its requirement is unmet and active while it is met; raising thresholds can only enlarge the quiescent complex and leaves the requirement complex as it was, so it is not relief of frustration" (CLAIM-549, Active, demonstrated).
- **Not intervening is not endorsing.** The owner's U15, as corrected at U17, gives: "a perspective that does not intervene has not thereby endorsed" (CLAIM-553, Proposed, interpretive).
- **Compatibility of bounds.** The owner's U27 of "Assess SDT Coverage" asked about the "compatibility of tolerance bounds". On a graph of contexts, an assignment within every tolerance exists "exactly when no cycle's holonomy exceeds the sum of its tolerances", and "local adjustment can move a mismatch among the overlaps of a cycle and never remove it" (CLAIM-538, Active, demonstrated). The feasibility criterion is classical, "so the result is granted, not the record's discovery" (ibid.). CLAIM-537 (Active, demonstrated) adds disturbance and what the agent can read.

**Already done, in part.** A49 made a directed tolerance, Q_{i→j} = 1[d_i ≤ τ_{i→j}], its formal centre. The record holds directedness (CLAIM-552), derives a tolerance from a threshold (CLAIM-550), and gives compatibility (CLAIM-538, CLAIM-537), but CLAIM-538 and CLAIM-537 use symmetric bounds on an edge. A model in which directed tolerances and authority evolve together has been proposed and not built. A32 §6 proposes letting d_i, τ and the grant conditions "evolve through the history of interaction", giving "endogenously acquired contractual terms, rather than fixed acceptance criteria supplied by the modeler". CLAIM-552's note of 2026-11-24 records this: the extension "would meet the burden QUESTION-128 and CASE-239 state (the standard learned rather than supplied), and A32 does not construct it; it is an extension proposed, not made". QUESTION-128 (Open) asks: "can norms of function and failure be defined from an organization's own dynamics". CASE-239 (Proposed, proposed study) is a forager that would learn its own criteria of failure. Its forager was built and run as CASE-421 (Active, executed study), for criteria of failure, not for tolerances (CASE-239, note of 2026-11-22). Neither QUESTION-128 nor CASE-239 has directed tolerances in it. The construction is now held as LEAD-tmpobng3 (Open, idea), which extends CASE-421's forager to learned tolerances with a fixed-threshold control; it is not run.

## 4.5 Frustration among demands

### 4.5.1 The owner's primitive

At U33 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 the owner wrote: "maybe we should focus less on distress and more on frustration". The record holds:

> "The primitive for an organization's regulatory difficulty is frustration, not distress: a relation between maintained, consequential demands and the incompatibilities, constraints or countervailing processes that limit their realization, independent of strain, the burden of sustaining activity, and distinct from distress, a possible integrated response to either" (CLAIM-368, Active, interpretive, the owner's).

Its sharp edge, in its summary: "it says what to start from, not that a frustrated organization is distressed or feels anything". At U37 of the same exchange the owner corrected A's quantitative turn: "We can do quite a lot with access to an ordering, even if it doesn't give us a true measure." That became:

> "Frustration among an organization's demands is an ordinal fact before it is a quantity: the jointly satisfiable sets of demands form a downward-closed complex whose minimal non-faces are the minimal conflicts, relief is strict enlargement of that complex for the same declared demands, reorganizations that relieve different conflicts are incomparable, and dropping or suppressing a demand is not relief" (CLAIM-347, Active, interpretive, the owner's correction).

The term is "the failure of the demands it maintains to be jointly realizable under its admissible transformations and its governance" (TERM-060, Active). Relief is judged "against a fixed reference set of its original demands", and "an endogenous revision of what the organization maintains is a transformation of it and not relief" (CLAIM-361, Active, interpretive). The part account (SYNTHESIS-044, Proposed) gathers these. It separates a perspective's "activation, tolerance, claims and grants" from the ordinal fact, "so a quiet perspective need not be satisfied and raising a threshold is not relief".

### 4.5.2 Frustration is not thwarting

SYNTHESIS-044 declines one reading outright: "That frustration is thwarting: CLAIM-357 and CLAIM-631 give necessary conditions only." The residual and the regret "identify none of them as thwarting" (CLAIM-357, Active, interpretive). A model answers QUESTION-131 (Open) in one setting: "With operative thwarting identified by silencing a process, structural frustration and thwarting are independent in both directions". Thwarting where joint satisfaction is attainable "needs a resolution that weighs authority or preference beyond the demands" (CLAIM-923, Proposed, demonstrated). That last clause is where frustration meets §4.2. A will-organization that weighs authority, or preference, can thwart a demand that could have been met.

An executed model bears on the authority arm. In the coupled meaning–influence model, "the frustration of satisfiable demands follows the allocation of influence when search and information are held fixed, but a captured allocation acts as a fixed weighting rule, so the model shows the governance-induced kind caused by allocation without showing conferral dynamics causing what no fixed weighting would" (CLAIM-880, Proposed, empirical; its run is CASE-317, Proposed, executed study). It is a partial instance of CLAIM-500 (Proposed), that "The governance-induced kind of frustration is not idle".

### 4.5.3 Pathology among processes

At U14 of the exchange of 2026-11-24 the owner proposed that processes "absolutely can enterpathological regimes of incompatible behavior requiring intervention and reorganization of commitments to resolve systemic frustration", though "it mightnot make sense to describe these processes as having moral duties to each other precisely". The record holds: "Processes need not owe one another moral duties for a system of them to enter a pathological regime of incompatible behaviour, one that takes intervention and a reorganization of their commitments to resolve its systemic frustration" (CLAIM-tmpe6yfm, Proposed, interpretive, the owner's). It does not say "that every pathological regime is resolved by reorganizing commitments; some need resources or new capacities". Its minimal instance is priority inversion (CASE-tmptpna1, Proposed, construction, A16 §4). Article 3 (§3.6) reads that case. Here it shows only that a relief can move a delay onto a third process instead of removing it.

### 4.5.4 What a demand needs in order to move action

CLAIM-523 (Active, interpretive), the owner's synthesis of 2026-10-31, places frustration among the conditions of normative efficacy. "three distinct achievements are needed and any can fail while the others hold: legibility …; volitional efficacy …; and correctability". It adds that "none establishes that the demand or the revision is justified". The three are necessary conditions. No conjunction of them is sufficient for a demand to be justified, and the claim does not make them sufficient for action either.

## 4.6 Appraisal, action readiness and valence

A51's affective sketch has four elements: "frustration, appraisal, action readiness and the difference between a concern's claimed precedence and its practical efficacy". Three of them are Active and one is Proposed. Frustration is CLAIM-368 and CLAIM-347 (§4.5). Action readiness is TERM-094. Claimed against granted precedence is CLAIM-283, with CLAIM-548 on efficacy (§4.2). Appraisal is CLAIM-440, which is Proposed.

### 4.6.1 What is Active

Action readiness is Frijda's: "a state of preparation for action, not a representation of a desirable state, that claims control over behaviour by persisting until an end state, resuming after interruption, capturing attention and taking priority over other actions" (TERM-094, Active). "A constructionist can use this term", and using it commits a claim to neither half of Frijda's account (TERM-094). The claimed side of CLAIM-283's distinction is this readiness. The granted side is the will-organization's.

### 4.6.2 What is Proposed

- **The motivating perspective.** The owner's U28 of "Organizational Nucleation and SLT": "I'm wondering if the notion of a "motivating perspective" might be a useful construction to make precise". The record's term is "an organizational perspective whose interpretations give circumstances a significance relative to concerns it maintains, and whose significance conditions action readiness, a claim to control that the will-organization may or may not grant" (TERM-104, Proposed).
- **Functional affect.** "Affective states can be properties of organized systems, characterized functionally as Frijda characterizes emotions, without implying subjective experience" (CLAIM-039, Proposed, interpretive, the owner's U19–U20; reopened by the owner on 2026-10-23). "The objection to answer first is THEORY-101's identity of valence with a felt state." CLAIM-432, CLAIM-440 and CLAIM-355 rest on it.
- **Appraisal as negotiation.** The owner, 2026-10-23: "what if the 'appraisal' is the process by which competing perspectives negotiate amongst themselves?" The record holds that an organization's appraisal is that negotiation, and that goal-conduciveness valence is "a regime in which an ordering of the organization's own states by their bearing on its maintenance prevails in the negotiation". It also holds that "ambivalence, where no ordering prevails, differs from neutrality" (CLAIM-440, Proposed, interpretive; rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-039; term TERM-076, Proposed).
- **Wanting and liking.** The owner: "I'm not sure if this resolves anything, but I think it least positions the wanting-liking distinction closer to the record's account." The claim is that wanting and liking are "saliences relative to different, overlapping processes", so "valence is then relative to a perspective" (CLAIM-437, Proposed).
- **Emotion as claim, valence as grant.** The assistant's: "an emotion, action readiness claiming control precedence, is a claim, a perspective's valence is read on its claims, and the system's valence is read on what the negotiation grants" (CLAIM-442, Proposed, interpretive; rests on CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-437).

These are not reconciled with each other. The record states the disagreement rather than resolving it: "Whether a perspective's valence stays fixed when grants change. CLAIM-442 says a change in grants with claims fixed "leaves the perspectives' emotions and valences as they were". CLAIM-437 defines a perspective's valence as "its ranking and its progress against its own reference course" … No entry reconciles the two" (SYNTHESIS-020, Proposed, "Not stated on the record"). How perspectives are individuated "without appeal to the dissociations they explain" is CLAIM-437's second defeater, unanswered (ibid.).

THEORY-101 is not the only objection. An Active counter holds that the potential account's "no frustration, no valence" "runs together two relations: perspectives' contested rankings … and demands that are jointly infeasible", so "the consequence needs one of them named" (CLAIM-449, Active, interpretive, counter). It is stated on CLAIM-347's complex, the junction of §4.5 and this section. An empirical counter holds that the potential "tracks 'wanting', not 'liking'" (CLAIM-438, Proposed, empirical, counter). The concession that the account is "of goal-conduciveness valence, not of intrinsic pleasantness" is CLAIM-433 (Proposed).

## 4.7 Corporate affect and institutional remorse

A51 §3 corrects its own earlier suggestion. "SYNTHESIS-018 and CLAIM-432 already distinguish harm that becomes an organizational concern from harm that becomes consequential only as an external threat." A51 assigns the argument to "the center of the institutional-sociopathy article", which is A51's Article 12. This article states its standing, which A51 does not.

**The thesis.** The thesis is the record's reading of Bakan (LIT-1430, unread). It was "filed at the owner's request on 2026-10-23 and revised the same day when the owner pointed out that the record's framework assigns affect to corporation-like things". A corporation "lacks remorse, functionally, when harm it does to others is not among the concerns its own appraisals track and gains precedence over what it does only when a regulator or a market turns it into a threat to the corporation's concerns". That profile, "threat without remorse", "is changed by redesigning the corporation's concerns, not its members' characters" (CLAIM-432, Proposed, interpretive; rests on CLAIM-039 and CLAIM-271). Its documented case is Wells Fargo's Community Bank, 2002–2016 (CASE-151, Active, specimen). In that case "signals of the harm reached people who could not act on them".

**The objection, unanswered.** "A corporation's functional remorse cannot be told from prudence: any procedure or role duty that makes others' harms its concern was put there for its own ends, reputation, liability or recruitment, so the line between harm as concern and harm as threat … does not hold, and the clinical word adds nothing to a description of its incentives" (CLAIM-431, Proposed, interpretive, counter). SYNTHESIS-018 lists among what it does not say: "That the remorse–threat line is drawable for a corporation: CLAIM-431 is unanswered".

**Two readings of the one case.** The record holds two readings of Wells Fargo, and it does not choose between them. This article states both.

- *Threat without remorse (CLAIM-432).* Customer harm was "narrowly construed to mean only financial harm such as fees and penalties". The 2004 investigation's recommendation to cut goals "was not escalated". The model was abandoned only after the regulators' settlements of September 2016 and the press. On this reading, harm reached the bank only as a threat.
- *Remorse claimed and not granted (CLAIM-442).* This reading puts an emotion on the claim side: "The claim is the emotion whether or not it is granted". If a unit's appraisal under a role duty is the corporation's own, "as CLAIM-432's part 3 allows", then "CASE-151's 2004 investigation is functional remorse claimed and not granted, where CLAIM-432 reads the bank as without remorse" (CLAIM-442, note of 2026-11-11).

"The two disagree about the case on that condition", and "neither entry settles whether such an appraisal is the corporation's" (ibid.; CLAIM-432's note of the same date states it from its side). The line's account declines to integrate them: "A contradiction between components is not an integration, so nothing is filed" (SYNTHESIS-021, Proposed). A further condition narrows CLAIM-432's own reading. Under CLAIM-440's admission condition, CASE-151's cross-sell potential "is shown only as the admitted negotiation's, not as the bank's" (CLAIM-432, note of 2026-11-11). A third reading, A46's on the owner's U29, is "a candidate answer … that differs from CLAIM-432": the profile "the word sociopathy points at is legibility without volitional efficacy for others' claims, not a failure to understand them" (CLAIM-tmptrn75, Proposed, interpretive). No entry applies it to CASE-151.

**The test, run and inconclusive.** LEAD-101 (Open; run on 2026-11-23 by a delegated lead worker, CASE-405, Proposed) asked whether corporations changed only by sanction keep weighing others' harms once the sanction lifts. Its answer was "**No case meets the lead's standard**", so the defeater is "neither met nor answered" (CLAIM-432, note of 2026-11-23). ## 4.8 Learned accommodation and its limits

### 4.8.1 The owner's thesis, in conditional form

At U45 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 the owner wrote: "processes which can navigate disagreement amongst themselves require fewer resources from the system than processes which need to activate additional subsystems to resolve disagreement". At the owner's review of the filing, A45's conditional form was adopted:

> "Learned local accommodation can reduce the amortized cost of recurrent arbitration: where a recurring conflict among an agent's processes admits a stable accommodation, settling it among the processes can cost less over repeated encounters than escalating each instance to a higher-level process that mediates or overrides, though local negotiation and the relations it needs are not free" (CLAIM-358, Proposed, interpretive, the owner's).

The claim says "can", and its antecedent is a stable accommodation. It does not say "That local settlement is always cheaper". A49 Article 4's abstract says "Implicit contracts and learned tolerances are shown to provide a potential means of amortizing recurrent regulatory negotiation". That overstates the claim: it is argued, not shown (curation entry of 2026-11-24 20:56, section "U31 and A49", "Standing told as settled"). A51's abstract says the claim correctly ("can reduce"). The owner's case behind it is CASE-109 (Active, thought experiment): Person A ("repeated escalation") and Person B ("local coordination"), with the same effort and the same quality of work, worked by A45. It is "used for CLAIM-358. Stipulated, so it shows what the claim distinguishes, not that people differ this way."

Three guards keep the thesis from proving too much. Two are Active.

- The owner's Hebbian prior is "a candidate mechanism for durable authority and for amortized governance; but persistence is not amortization, which is shown only when earlier work replaces later computation, communication or recruitment" (CLAIM-443, Active, interpretive; the owner's question, "what if we just treat this as a kind of momentum term? or like a hebbian prior?").
- "Repeated arbitration can fall away from a collaboration by three different mechanisms, valuational concordance, learned practical coordination and externalized governance", and rules that settle recurring conflicts automatically "do not show that the collaborators have learned to coordinate" (CLAIM-1139, Active, interpretive; A20 of "Explain First Contact RL"). On this article's reading, the second is the nearest to CLAIM-358's route.
- The routes by which a divided agent acts as one, "arbitration, a change in which interest may act, coinciding valuations, local accommodation", "are distinct and differently costly" (SYNTHESIS-037, Proposed).

**Done in part.** The study the thesis needs has two parts: the price of each escalation, and the saving over a recurring conflict. The first has been done, for single choices.

- *The criterion, written before the fit.* LEAD-128 (Open) asked for "a fit of the expected-value-of-control model in which control intensity falls with its cost". Its "What would count": "Intensity falling with cost promotes CLAIM-358."
- *The result.* Carrying out LEAD-128 on 2026-11-22, the assistant fitted the model to the cognitive-effort-discounting choices of 122 participants from a published, openly licensed study (CASE-382, Proposed, executed study). For about three-quarters of them "the control intensity elected falls as its cost rises, while a need-for-cognition minority seek effort". The finding is CLAIM-981 (Proposed, empirical), which "corroborates" CLAIM-358. CLAIM-358's note of 2026-11-22, "the missing cost-side fit now has a candidate", says what it supplies: "the intrinsic cost that, on THEORY-070, gives escalation the price this claim says a learned accommodation saves — the per-encounter cost this claim's amortization presupposes, not the recurrence."
- *Its limits.* It is "single-shot effort pricing", and "Not amortised supervisory cost" (CASE-382). It measures intensity by revealed choice, which is not the operationalization THEORY-070's condition names, and "whether it promotes that ground is the owner's call, so this claim's status is unchanged" (CLAIM-358, note of 2026-11-22). LEAD-128's criterion would promote CLAIM-358 on the cost direction alone, but the fit does not price the recurrence. LEAD-128 is still Open.

What stays open is the recurrence, and no executed study prices it. The study that bears nearest bears against reading persistence as amortization. Its own check was set before its run: "if a Bayesian learner with matched information costs, or a habit model with matched memory, reproduces every intervention response, the extra authority state has not earned its place" (CASE-153, Active, quoted in CLAIM-924's summary). In the self-protecting sentry, "under success feedback its update is the habit of protecting, and a habit of matched memory reproduces the responses to belief correction, authority reset and restored evidence" (CASE-346, Active, executed study; rerun by a session other than its filer's).

*Expectation for the recurrence, stated before any run.* It is now the design of LEAD-tmpqn4dk (Open, specified), copied in as fixed. Take two populations of processes that meet one recurring conflict with a stable accommodation, as CASE-109's two persons do: one settles it locally and one escalates each instance, each escalation priced as in CASE-382. If CLAIM-358 holds as conditioned, the local population's cumulative cost should fall below the escalating one's after some number of encounters, the break-even, that depends on the cost of forming the relation. That cost is separate from the cost of keeping it: "having enough to sustain it is not having enough to acquire it" (CLAIM-569, Active, demonstrated). In a matched population whose recurring conflict has no stable accommodation, the ordering should not appear. CLAIM-443's guard adds a necessary check. The saving has to show up as later arbitration work that is not done. A persisting allocation that does the same work again would not count. CLAIM-1139's split adds another: the saving must come from learned coordination, not from an external rule. This expectation is the drafter's. It is a suggestion for a lead, not a result.

### 4.8.2 Low conflict does not show adequate accommodation

A51's abstract: "low conflict and fluent performance do not establish the adequacy of accommodation". Entries of different standing hold parts of this; the restatement is JR4.1 below.

- "Processes conditioned never to object coordinate without override at the same behaviour and supervisory cost as processes that negotiate, so the two part only under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or in their history" (CLAIM-364, Proposed, interpretive, counter; A45's). Its summary quotes A45: "A quiet subordinate process isn't necessarily a satisfied one." What those interventions test is narrowed by Part I's passed join: the two kinds "share their maintenance provenance", so the interventions that part them "test how the constituents' own processes answer dissent, not whose process keeps the arrangement" (CLAIM-tmp9ia3w, Proposed, demonstrated).
- "where an organization's legibility regime selects which contests are admitted or recorded", "observed coherence is not evidence that the organization's perspectives are integrated" (CLAIM-448, Active, §4.6.3). It holds only under that condition.
- A quiet perspective need not be a satisfied one: "a perspective can be quiet while its requirement is unmet" (CLAIM-549, Active, §4.4).
- SYNTHESIS-037 does not say "That low supervisory cost shows non-domination".

**Already done, in part.** Intervention separates registered from unregistered evaluation in a stipulated model, and endorsement only weakly (CASE-355, §4.6.3). A record that is not stipulated has not been read.

## 4.9 Flow and nonintegrable evaluations

A51's final section is "learned accommodation, organizational flow, competing demands and nonintegrable evaluations".

**Flow.** The record's conjecture is that in flow "an activity's immediate reward and its longer-run value coincide, so no interest has to be overridden" (CLAIM-162, Proposed). It is grounded "only weakly": "the flow literature is registered unread". The assistant's counter holds that this "tells against the potential account's condition that valence is undefined where no pair is contested", since flow is reported as positive (CLAIM-441, Proposed, counter). CLAIM-449 (Active, §4.6.2) says the absorbed writer "tests whichever is chosen" of the two relations it separates. Which of CLAIM-441's four exits holds is not stated (SYNTHESIS-020). A's capability reading is Active: "the achievement is not flow as a state but flow-regulation as an organizational capability: to enter, sustain, interrupt and recover fluent coordination without losing the history of what was learned" (CLAIM-1075, Active, interpretive; promoted under a delegated ruling).

**Cycles.** The owner, 2026-10-23: "rather than asserting potentials always have to exist, could we characterize cycles as a kind of internal tension or cognitive dissonance? preference/perspective/will instability?" The record keeps three things apart. A cycling record of wins is "organizational nonintegrability". It "becomes organizational tension only when realized as costly reversal against demands the organization maintains, and cognitive dissonance only when that tension is detected and appraised as aversive". Learning can relieve it "by accommodation, by entrenched precedence or by cheap periodic coordination, which observed coherence does not tell apart" (CLAIM-451, Proposed, interpretive; rests on CLAIM-434). Each "only when" is a necessary condition, and the claim states no sufficient one. The account adds: "That a potential must exist. CLAIM-451: "That a potential is the default and a cycle a pathology. They are regimes."" (SYNTHESIS-020, "What it does not say"). A related link is declined: that entrenched authority "relieves frustration by removing demands from contention" is "a free parameter, whether a demand no process presses is removed or only quiet" (SYNTHESIS-049, "Not stated on the record"). CLAIM-451's "relieve" refers to tension in its own sense, not to relief of frustration in CLAIM-347's sense. This article keeps the two apart.

**What the article's contribution can say.** A51 ends: "This paper establishes why an organizational individual may require an architecture of differentiated authority rather than a single scalar objective." The record supports less, in several senses of "scalar", and this article does not join them (J4.2). An ordering need not be a currency (CLAIM-436). A record of wins need not have a potential (CLAIM-434, CLAIM-451). Where an aggregate revealed preference exists, "it is fitted to one regime of authority, so an intervention on authority that changes the winning moves changes the fitted preference" (CLAIM-482, Active, interpretive, counter). A will-organization with decisive jurisdictions, unanimity and an unrestricted domain "cannot always choose", and richer information does not escape this (CLAIM-353, Active, interpretive, from the owner's U36). In one fictional population, scalar grants "fail either way" (CASE-420). CASE-325's gates fail for another reason. They fail not because they are scalar but because the products of authority bits are never observed in single changes, and "A gate with products of authority bits can represent the composition. It cannot learn those products from single changes without a prior that is the relations again" (CASE-325, "What it cannot show"). None of these establishes that an organization requires differentiated authority.

## 4.11 Misunderstanding as an occasion of relief

This section reports a join (J4.1), now tested under ADR-075. As first drafted, it said that a misunderstanding is productive in the owner's sense "exactly when" its clarification is a relief. That turned the owner's "when" into a biconditional, and an ordinary case defeats it (Joins, J4.1). What follows is the corrected form, which passed and is filed as CLAIM-tmp7rl3d.

The owner's sidebar of 2026-11-24 was: "'productive misunderstanding' - identification and clarification of ambiguity as detection of and reorganization around novel consequential distinctions". The record holds: "A misunderstanding is productive when identifying and clarifying the ambiguity it exposes is the detection of, and a reorganization around, a novel distinction that has become consequential" (CLAIM-tmpupcws, Proposed, interpretive, the owner's). The "when" states a sufficient condition. The claim "names misunderstanding as an occasion" of TERM-099's self-formation. The assistant qualified it: a misunderstanding "dates the distinction's relevance rather than its existence", and clarifying "can coarsen as well as refine" (CLAIM-tmpm76n5, Proposed). The assistant also objected: "A misunderstanding can be productive because it is left unresolved" (CLAIM-tmpgx2ej, Proposed, counter). Article 3 (§3.7) places the sidebar among the kinds of ambiguous invocation.

The record's construction of self-formation driven by frustration is CASE-191 (Active, construction, the assistant's). There, "an agent's own frustration at two demands one shared output cannot meet drives production of a sensor and a differentiating channel". Below a threshold, "smaller discrepancies are tolerated, at a standing frustration, and form nothing". The two demands are for different contexts, which the sensor tells apart. CLAIM-532 (Active, interpretive) says that an unresolved discrepancy is "an occasion for self-formation … and not a guarantee of it". A held, Active instance of relief by differentiation is niche differentiation: "a strict enlargement of their compatibility complex over viable histories for the same demands", where "the exclusion of a constituent … relieves no structural frustration" (CLAIM-554, Active, interpretive).

**The claim.** Take each party's reading of an ambiguous expression as a demand on their joint outcomes. That step is the claim's condition, and no entry holds it. A consequential misunderstanding is then a frustrated pair in CLAIM-347's sense. The record now holds:

> "Taking each party's reading of an ambiguous expression as a demand on their joint outcomes, clarifying a consequential misunderstanding relieves frustration by a new distinction only where the readings' demands bear on different situations the expression conflated; where they demand different joint outcomes in the same situation, no distinction relieves them, only an outcome that meets both, and settling on one reading or rewording the demands is not relief" (CLAIM-tmp7rl3d, Proposed, demonstrated conditional on its first step; rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361, CLAIM-532, CLAIM-tmpm76n5; grounds CASE-191; the wording an independent reader's, filed by the assistant, with adopting it and its membership the owner's call).

So clarifying can go these ways:

- *A distinction, where the demands bear on situations the expression conflated.* Splitting the situations lets each demand be met in its own. That is relief, by CASE-191's route.
- *A distinction, where the demands bear on one situation and require different joint outcomes there.* No distinction relieves them. A distinction lets what is done depend on a feature, and it cannot make one outcome in one situation take two values. Only an outcome that meets both does, which in CLAIM-532's terms is a new channel.
- *Settling on one reading.* That drops a demand, which by CLAIM-347 "is not relief", however much quieter the relation becomes. A quiet perspective need not be satisfied (CLAIM-549).
- *Restricting or rewording the demands.* That changes what is compared: "an endogenous revision of what the organization maintains is a transformation of it and not relief" (CLAIM-361).

What the claim does not say matters as much. It does not say that a settlement on one reading is unproductive. A joint settlement on one reading, with the new distinction kept for later use, is productive by CLAIM-tmpupcws and by CLAIM-tmpisfi1 ("resolved jointly, it is a productive misunderstanding"), and is not relief. So the owner's productive misunderstanding and relief come apart. They coincide only in the first kind. CLAIM-tmpgx2ej's misunderstanding, productive because left unresolved, is not excluded, and CLAIM-tmpm76n5's coarsening is not classed. The claim's caution is that "the same situation" is fixed by the joint outcome the demands bear on. A feature invented after the fact to separate situations, such as who is asking, "absorbs every case and defeats nothing" (CLAIM-tmp7rl3d, `defeated_if`). Whether settling drops the losing demand or only quiets it is the free parameter that SYNTHESIS-049's "Entrenchment as relief" bullet declines over (§4.9). The test the first draft proposed, that a settling clarification leaves the losing demand pressing again where a refining one does not, cannot tell a dropped demand from a quieted one. It would become a test only if maintenance were measured before the clarification. That measurement is now LEAD-tmp8nbac (Open, specified), by withheld probes in which meeting the demand costs nothing (G4.4).

**Limits.** CASE-191 is one stipulated agent, and the owner's claim is about a dyad or an organization. CLAIM-532 makes a discrepancy an occasion, and states a sufficient condition for self-formation in the agent's own activity producing a maintained capacity, not in the discrepancy. Neither it nor CLAIM-tmp7rl3d makes frustration sufficient for a productive misunderstanding, or for self-formation. Whether imposed clarification can still be "productive" is open (CLAIM-tmpro73c; CLAIM-tmpisfi1, Proposed, which Article 3 (§3.7) cites).

## 4.12 What the article claims, and with what standing

1. Precedence, jurisdiction and suspension are distinct relations of practical authority, and claimed precedence differs from granted authority (TERM-017, TERM-023, CLAIM-027, CLAIM-057, CLAIM-283; Active). Activation, grant and efficacy are separate quantities (the owner's U18; CLAIM-548, Active).
2. In one stipulated organization, a will-organization model beats five control-only models by more than its Occam cost and alone predicts unseen combinations of changes in authority. It loses where authority is idle or idiosyncratic. A wrongly composed one wins on training evidence and loses on transfer (CASE-325, Active, rerun by a session other than its filer's on 2026-11-25; CLAIM-889, Proposed, demonstrated). For an organization, institutional theory already gives the difference (CLAIM-890, Proposed; CLAIM-021, Active). What would be new is one description across realizers, which is untested (CLAIM-487, Proposed; LEAD-141 found no such test in the literature for a person).
3. Forbidding self-grants does not stop recursive amplification, and the rules on record differ in what they guarantee (CLAIM-282, CLAIM-375, CLAIM-502; Active, demonstrated).
4. A tolerance is a directed criterion for conferral, necessary where it binds and sufficient only for a grant under a granting rule. A quiet perspective need not be satisfied, and raising a threshold is not relief (the owner's U12, U19; CLAIM-552, CLAIM-550, CLAIM-549; Active).
5. Frustration, ordinal and not distress, is the primitive, and dropping a demand is not relief (the owner's U33, U37; CLAIM-368, CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361; Active). Frustration is not thwarting: SYNTHESIS-044 declines their identity, and thwarting where joint satisfaction is attainable runs through authority or preference (CLAIM-923, Proposed, demonstrated).
6. Organizational affect, characterized functionally, is situated at the claim–grant distinction. Every entry that states it is Proposed (CLAIM-039, CLAIM-440, CLAIM-437, CLAIM-442). THEORY-101's objection is unanswered, CLAIM-449's Active counter is unmet, and CLAIM-442 and CLAIM-437 are unreconciled.
7. A corporation can be read as lacking remorse when others' harm reaches it only as threat (CLAIM-432, Proposed). The objection that this cannot be told from prudence is unanswered (CLAIM-431). The record's two readings of Wells Fargo disagree (CLAIM-432, CLAIM-442), and the test on open sources was inconclusive (CASE-405).
8. Learned accommodation can lower arbitration cost where a stable accommodation exists (the owner's U45; CLAIM-358, Proposed, argued). The per-encounter price of escalation has one fit to human choices (CASE-382, CLAIM-981, Proposed), and the recurrence is unpriced. Low conflict and fluent coordination do not show accommodation adequate: a quiet perspective need not be satisfied (CLAIM-549, Active), coherence is no evidence of integration where a legibility regime selects the contests recorded (CLAIM-448, Active), and conditioned and negotiating processes match in behaviour and supervisory cost (CLAIM-364, Proposed).
9. Regimes of authority can outlast what justified them (CLAIM-446, CLAIM-tmp0gs0t, CLAIM-tmpn754o; Proposed; CASE-036, Active, a thought experiment). In the one model run, the authority state cannot be told from a habit except by intervening on the evaluation (CASE-346, Active; CLAIM-924, Proposed).
10. Taking readings as demands, clarifying a misunderstanding relieves frustration by a distinction only where the readings bear on situations the expression conflated (CLAIM-tmp7rl3d, Proposed, demonstrated conditional on that step).
