# TERM-tmpzxb0m: canvas temperature: the rate and spatial spread of placements that follow no coalition's plan, modelled as a stochastic field on the canvas rather than as agents

## Definition

The owner: "If it helps, we can even model this random behavior directly and
treat stochastic fluctuation as a property of the canvas itself rather than
as a property of the population of the ensemble acting on it to make the
necessity for maintenance explicit."

Split each placement into one that serves some coalition's plan and one
that serves none. The second kind is modelled as a field: T(x, t) is the
rate at which placements following no plan land at site x at time t. A
pattern nobody maintains is then corrupted at rate T. Holding it takes a
flow of repair that matches that rate, plus whatever rivals add. In this
form the canvas has a thermal decay, and maintenance is visibly necessary
([CLAIM-tmp4zg6p](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4zg6p.md), version 2).

T varies in time. It is high early and falls as uninvested participants
leave ([CLAIM-tmp6phbn](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6phbn.md)). That is a cooling schedule. It also varies in space,
since unaffiliated placers are drawn to visible regions, so T is not
uniform.

## What it is not

- **Not plan-free.** Whether a placement follows no plan is relative to the
  plans in play. [CLAIM-tmpgfyls](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgfyls.md) holds that stabilizing and destabilizing are
  relative to a policy. Falmagne et al. could not tell an attack from an
  "innovation that will eventually be adopted into the image"
  ([THEORY-tmp07t7w](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp07t7w.md)). So T is defined against a stated set of plans. The Void
  is noise to every artwork and a plan to itself.
- **Not thermodynamic temperature.** It is a rate of corruption. No entropy
  or energy is defined, as [CLAIM-tmp4zg6p](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4zg6p.md) says.
