## Search plan

1. Active inference, the free-energy principle, predictive processing and
   Markov blankets: every reading, not only the titles grep found.

3. Agency without overt action: will-organization, regimes, mental action,

   interpretation after intervention, and whatever states the model whole.

### Thread 5: the underlying model (checked against the files)

**No entry states the underlying model.** The workbench entry of 20:08 and
CLAIM-tmpq9do8 already say so. The pieces are on the distributed-agency
line:

- **CLAIM-297** (Proposed) has the owner's loop already written down, with
  no action in it. The circuit (A22 §2): "frame → retrieval →
  interpretation → endorsement → future retrieval policy". In equations:
  - meaning: x_{t+1} = Σᵢ w_{i,t} P_i(q_t)ᵀ x_t
  - influence: w_{t+1} = G(w_t, x_t, o_t, E_t)
  - queries: q_{t+1} = Q(x_{t+1}, w_{t+1}, g)

  Mapping it onto U1: the query q is the judgement's measurement context.
  P_i(q) is the judgement as an operator. x is the interpretation it
  yields, which becomes the next state. Nothing acts on the world. The
  world appears only as o_t inside G.
- **CLAIM-297's own claim is CLAIM-tmpki5gp's objection.** Authority scored
  against the interpretation the operators made dominant "can reinforce an
  initial interpretive advantage without corresponding improvement in
  independent evidential fidelity". The record already holds the
  self-confirming minimum as a property of its own model. So the
  objection is internal to the model, not an outside attack.
- **CLAIM-330** (Active, demonstrated) is a linearization: "only the loop"
  destabilizes symmetry, at βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ). Its Regime B, which scores
  operators independently (γ ≈ 0), removes capture. That is a
  *demonstrated* answer to what anchors the loop, and the answer is not
  action. The anchor is that the score of an operator does not run
  through the interpretation it produced. Compare A1's proposal (the world
  fixes the outcome distribution per context). CLAIM-330 is sharper: what
  matters is whether evidence o_t enters G independently of x_t.
  **To check: read CLAIM-330 in full before relying on this.**
- **CLAIM-279**: the owner at U5 says operator roles "are just different
  regimes in the dynamics of our general process". A8: whether an
  intervention is denoising or re-noising is "relative to a policy and a
  representational frame". This is the nearest the record comes to "a
  frame is a regime", the bridge the owner's U2 asserts.
- **CLAIM-298** (pragmatic-transport): "a frame can change a judgement by
  changing which of an interpreter's stored distinctions are reachable".
  With CLAIM-297 it is the one explicit bridge between the two lines.
- **CLAIM-275, 277, 285, 286 and TERM-053** (endorsement) carry the rest of
  the model. The owner's statement of the two canvases is at CLAIM-285
  :55-61.

**Consequence for the filing.** CLAIM-tmpq9do8 should probably rest on
CLAIM-297's circuit (or cite it) as well as CLAIM-275, and CLAIM-tmpki5gp
should be answered from CLAIM-330 rather than from A1's proposal. Not
changed yet; waiting on the other threads.

The owner on Friston, active inference or cybernetics: nowhere before U1
of 2026-10-16. Earlier mentions are all in readings (NOTE-169, 421, 434,
727, 785; THEORY-066, 072, 073, 285).

## Problems with the first filing (verified against the files)

1. **The strong form may rest on the wrong kind of contextuality.**
   THEORY-010:42 (Proposed): "a single system probed context after context
   presents no overlapping contexts, so no global-section obstruction in
   the sense of LIT-016 can arise." The owner's loop *is* one system judged
   context after context, which is the sequential case. So CLAIM-009's
   sheaf / Contextuality-by-Default sense cannot make the loop nonclassical
   by itself. The escape route is QUESTION-067:61: Spekkens's preparation
   contextuality "can show with two binary judgements never made together.
   A sheaf test cannot see it." **Action:** CLAIM-tmpq9do8's strong form,
   and its defeater (a), should name the Spekkens notion (QUESTION-067),
   not CLAIM-009 alone.
2. **The record already marks the key premise as open, and I cited it as
   support.** TERM-039:62-68: "Whether eliciting a judgement is also an
   intervention is open, and the exchange answers it both ways." A129's
   graph gives the context an arrow into the response only, not into the
   interpreter's state. CLAIM-127:149-151: "Which route holds is empirical.
   Sequential designs ... separate them." CLAIM-tmpq9do8 cites TERM-039 as
   though it supported "the context enters the outcome". It supports only
   the weak reading (a direct influence on the response). The owner's
   "generates interpretations" is the open route, an arrow from the context
   into the interpreter's state. **Action:** say so in CLAIM-tmpq9do8, and
   name sequential designs as the test.
3. **The record had this thesis before, and demoted it.** CLAIM-109
   (Superseded): "the pragmatic frame ... participates in determining which
   meaning becomes actual." Its defeated_if: every frame and order effect
   "reproduced by a classical model in which the frame only selects among
   determinate meanings." It was demoted to CLAIM-009 after ARG-002 was
   undercut (CLAIM-041). **Action:** CLAIM-tmpq9do8 should cite CLAIM-109 as
   its history. The new thesis is CLAIM-109 generalized from translation to
   cognition, with Friston as the foil.
4. **Current data.** CLAIM-325:56 (Active): "Behavioural data have plenty
   of the first [context dependence] and, so far, none of the second
   [contextuality proper]." The strong form has no positive evidence in
   the record yet.

## Thread 2: judgement as measurement (beyond the problems above)

- **CLAIM-141** (Active, granted): "Passing from values to distributions
  creates solutions." Any loop of judgement, then observation, then model
  has a self-consistent distributional fixed point, so a self-confirming
  solution always exists. What bites is a constraint that prescribes
  marginals. This sharpens CLAIM-tmpki5gp, and also the reply: the world
  anchors the loop only by fixing per-context marginals (CLAIM-038 /
  TERM-042: the communicative object as context-indexed empirical
  constraints).
- **THEORY-339** (amortized inference, Dasgupta and Gershman): the only
  evidence in the record that a judgement changes the judge. An earlier
  answer's error carries into a later one when the queries share a
  hypothesis space. That is classical reuse, the weak form.
- **NOTE-636 / THEORY-166** (the QQ equality): the projection model treats
  the first answer as changing the state (generating). It predicts an
  invariant, but data that satisfy it are noncontextual (CLAIM-041).
- **CLAIM-253 / CLAIM-046**: interpretive-state defects are not identified
  from judgement data. So "generates a state" must be put on observables,
  which defeater (a) does.
- **Gaps**: nothing on constructed preference (Lichtenstein & Slovic),
  mere-measurement or question-behaviour effects, or self-generated
  validity. These are the empirical literatures on judging changing the
  judge, and the most direct evidence the thesis could have.

## Thread 3: agency without overt action

- **NOTE-497:73-75** (Block, read): "Owen et al.'s 'vegetative' patient
  who imagines tennis on request shows an intentional act without report."
  The lead in CASE-tmpxgcq2 is already in the record, though without a LIT
  for Owen. It is framed as an *intentional act*, so CLAIM-tmp9cjyo applies.
- **THEORY-090:200, THEORY-298:55-64, QUESTION-048:40**: command-following
  marks access, not experience. No-report markers still need validating
  through report or executive control. "Every kind of evidence the record
  holds about experience reaches it through access." This generalizes
  CASE-tmpxgcq2's asymmetry: it is the evidence-for-experience line's own
  standing result. **Action:** cite these from the case.
- **TERM-023** (will-organization) is defined over "action-generating
  regimes". The owner's "same underlying model" has to say what happens to
  that line if action is cut. The likely answer is that judgement is
  itself an action-generating regime acting on the canvas (CLAIM-275).
  That is the owner's to say.
- **CLAIM-156** (mindfulness): a will-organization operation with no
  action on the world. It suspends an urge's claim to act. This is the
  nearest analogue to an action-free judgement.
- **CLAIM-287**: a dormant coalition can be reactivated. A loop that went
  quiet in CLIS may be dormant rather than extinguished, a third reading
  of Kübler & Birbaumer.
- **THEORY-285 / TERM-020 / QUESTION-045 / CLAIM-286** (rumination as a
  recurrent coalition): the lock-in family. Rumination is the
  internal-loop form.
- **Gaps**: no Owen, Monti, cognitive motor dissociation, Seligman, curare
  or sensory deprivation.

## Thread 4: cybernetic and enactive accounts

- **NOTE-121** (Elshatlawy et al., read; LIT-133 Rejected): the closest
  rival. "Observation and control are then the same loop, run in opposite
  directions" (p. 53). It *requires* feedback closure through the
  environment. The thesis denies exactly that condition.
- **NOTE-450** (the Markov blanket trick, read): "Active-inference models
  smuggle perception and action into hand-built generative models." "Active
  inference presupposes perception and action rather than explaining
  them." This supports the owner's complaint about the diagram rather than
  the bound.
- **NOTE-447 / THEORY-066 / THEORY-073**: the blanket is placed, not found.
  So "one blanket inward" (CLAIM-tmp9cjyo) is a modelling choice, which
  weakens that counter: if blankets can go anywhere, saying the loop sits
  inside one explains nothing.
- **NOTE-729** (Bickhard, interactivism): error detectable "by the system
  itself". This is internal anchoring, which bears on CLAIM-tmpki5gp. But
  Bickhard's differentiations are interactions, i.e. action. Prior art to
  distinguish from.
- **NOTE-719** (Di Paolo) / **THEORY-071**: "Events that provoke the same
  regulative response are not meaningfully distinguishable." Discrimination
  depends on regulating coupling. The enactive answer is the opposite of
  the thesis.
- **THEORY-324** (Ashby, ultrastability): "there must be a channel from the
  essential variables to those parameters". A second loop that
  reconfigures the first rather than acting on the world, which is formally
  close to the thesis. But its anchor is the essential variables, which
  are world-coupled, and its adaptation is blind search, not
  interpretation.
- **NOTE-878 / NOTE-180** (Kirsh & Maglio; Clark & Chalmers): epistemic
  action, changing the world to aid recognition. The obvious reply that
  action belongs inside the judgement loop.
- **NOTE-434** (Barrett): "Predictions are concepts. Completed predictions
  are categorizations." Categorization constructs the percept, close to
  "judgement generates the observation", but housed inside active
  inference. **The nearest thing in the record to the thesis already made
  within Friston's framework.**
- **NOTE-785** (Brouillet & Friston): felt fluency "rendered opaque by the
  mental action that attends its recognition", where mental action is
  "top-down or endogenous attention". More for CLAIM-tmp9cjyo.
- **NOTE-299 / LIT-312** (Aerts & Gabora): a context acts as a projector on
  a concept state, prior art for judgement as measurement in cognition.
  But its contexts are diagonal in one basis, so commutative and classical.
- **NOTE-249** (measurement as sheafification) and **NOTE-174** (Rovelli,
  RQM): the physics side of relative facts.
- **Gaps**: no reading of Pattee's epistemic cut, QBism, Wheeler's
  participatory universe, Powers's perceptual control theory, O'Regan &
  Noë, or the good regulator theorem ("Not held", NOTE-421:163). Pattee and
  QBism are the measurement-first prior art the thesis should cite or
  distinguish itself from. Perceptual control theory ("behavior is the
  control of perception") is the cybernetic rival closest to the owner's
  wording.
