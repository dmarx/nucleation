The supplied-maintenance filing and its five gap-filling follow-ups (dmarx/nucleation#404 to #410) registered a dozen works as Unavailable that ADR-053 files as Unfetched, and left unread at least two works that ADR-064 counts as lawfully readable. The error was the lead's (Claude, in this session), not the readers'. The reader briefs told readers to use only the author's, the publisher's or a repository's own copy. That excluded the course pages, faculty pages and scholarly third-party hosts that ADR-064 admits. Where a lawful copy was located and the fetch was blocked (a Cloudflare challenge, a WAF challenge, a reset connection, a TLS failure), the briefs had the work filed Unavailable, when ADR-053 says Unfetched with the address, the blocker and the date.

This sweep corrects the entries on the default branch:

- **Moved to Unfetched**, each with its address, blocker and date in a new status_note and a version-2 history entry: LIT-2175 (Burning Man's Ten Principles), LIT-2151 (Rogers & Coates), LIT-2145 (Holl et al. 2020), LIT-2179 (Corbin & Holl), LIT-2176 (Gross et al.) and LIT-2140 (Ostrom 1996).
  - LIT-2151 may not qualify. ADR-053 excludes a copy that is down for everyone, and the USAID host's 503 could mean that. The connection resets point to this client, so it was moved.
  - LIT-2140's São Paulo course copy was located. A reading of it on 2026-11-06 was refused by this session's permission system, not by the host, and was not worked around.
- **Left Unavailable:** LIT-2173 (Resende et al.). Only the repository's host was recorded, not the item's address, so ADR-053's condition is not met.
- **Read:** Suding, Gross & Houseman 2004 (LIT-2169), now Active, from the typeset.io/SciSpace copy, with NOTE-tmpyzbse and THEORY-tmph89ue. Kadowaki et al. (LIT-2165) and Schröder et al. (LIT-2167), read in its place, now complement it. Dated notes were added to CLAIM-604 and CLAIM-600, and to both stand-ins. Directives that listed LIT-2169 as Unavailable were pruned.

The same correction went onto the open pull requests, whose entries are not yet on the default branch: Bräutigam & Knack on dmarx/nucleation#406; Wood, Bruner & Ross and Bainbridge on dmarx/nucleation#407, where Cohen & Levinthal 1990 is now read from a copy ADR-064 admits; and Zabusky & Kruskal and Satsuma & Yajima on dmarx/nucleation#410.

The curation entries and changelog fragments that called these works Unavailable were true of the filing at the time, and they are left as written. This entry is the correction. The Unfetched entries are the owner's queue: a browser fetch of each recorded address is the cheapest way to read them. The fix for the hazard upstream is in the brief, not the net. A reader brief must name ADR-064's admitted copies and ADR-053's Unfetched status. The lead's reader and filer briefs for this session now say so; whether the read-and-file skill should also say so is the owner's call.
