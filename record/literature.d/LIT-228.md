# LIT-228: Contrastive and Non-Contrastive Self-Supervised Learning Recover Global and Local Spectral Embedding Methods

Randall Balestriero, Yann LeCun (2022), *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 35 (NeurIPS 2022), pp. 26671–26685* — [ARXIV-2205.11508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11508)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-752](../notes.d/NOTE-752.md).*

- **VICReg.** Its invariance term is the Dirichlet energy 2 Tr(ZᵀLZ) on G.
  - With a squared variance term (in place of the hinge) and α = β, the
    optimum is the top eigenvectors of I − (1/N)11ᵀ − (γ/α)(D − G),
    scaled, and only γ/α matters (Theorem 1).
  - With variance and covariance forced to zero and a constant degree,
    it is Laplacian eigenmaps (Theorem 2).
  - With a linear network it is LPP, and LDA when G is the label graph
    (Theorem 3).
- **SimCLR.** Its softmax similarity is the solution of an
  entropy-regularized graph estimation (Theorem 4). For the Euclidean
  comparison ‖G − Ĝ‖²_F, the optimum is Z* = (U_G Σ_G^(1/2))_(:,1:K), a
  generalized MDS "akin to ISOMAP", with rank min(K, rank(G))
  (Theorem 5, Proposition 2).
- **Barlow Twins.** In the linear regime it is CCA:
  W_a* = top-K eigenvectors of C_aa⁻¹C_abC_bb⁻¹C_ba, with weight sharing
  when G is symmetric (Theorem 7). The CCA identification is asserted
  rather than derived.
- **Downstream.** Any of the three gives zero linear-probe loss on the
  training set iff the task's left singular vectors lie in G's
  (Theorem 9, sketched). The ranking "prefer VICReg, then BarlowTwins and
  finally SimCLR" under a misaligned G rests on Theorem 10, which has no
  proof, and on synthetic experiments (N = 256–512).
- **Global and local.** Contrastive methods map to global spectral
  methods and non-contrastive ones to local ones. This is an analogy, not
  a result.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped under
the heading *Representation learning as a spectral approximation*. Read in
[NOTE-752](../notes.d/NOTE-752.md). It is held as the method-by-method map from SSL losses to
named spectral embedding methods, on a finite sample. Its own statement
is that all the losses align the representation's left singular vectors
with those of the pair graph G. With the constant degree it assumes,
those are the eigenvectors of the positive-pair chain on the sample. That
is where it meets HaoChen et al. ([LIT-249](LIT-249.md)) and Johnson et al. ([LIT-258](LIT-258.md)).

It is a supporting source of [THEORY-007](../theory.d/THEORY-007.md). The NOTE answers that theory's
question about which of its dictionaries is right: they pick the same
eigenvectors. Its Theorem 7 is an SVD-type (CCA) account of a
non-symmetric pair structure, which bears on [THEORY-009](../theory.d/THEORY-009.md), though that
theory does not cite it. Simon et al. ([LIT-242](LIT-242.md)) supply the training
dynamics it lacks.

Tagged `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../decisions.d/ADR-005.md)): the seed judged it chiefly about
machine-learning practice, and its advice on choosing and tuning SSL
methods is practice. It is kept here by the owner's decision of 2026-09-26
that new work stays in nucleation until a transfer is judged appropriate
