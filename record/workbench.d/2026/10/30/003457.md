**A finding about earlier passes.** Reviewer C pointed out that [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md)'s "Where a
narrowing's dropped claims go", one of the evidence rulings [ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md) keeps,
sorts each clause a narrowing drops into four kinds:

- **Kind 1, a generalization the source asserts.** It becomes a Proposed
  THEORY that keeps the wide wording, `extends` the narrowed entry and
  inherits the old promote_when.
- **Kind 2, the record's own gloss.** It becomes an interpretive CLAIM.
- **Kind 3, a mis-statement.** It gets a note only.
- **Kind 4, open scope nobody asserts.** It becomes a QUESTION.

From pass 8 on, the review's rulings narrowed readings and moved the
promote_when to a new QUESTION every time. Where the source asserts the wide
form, that dropped it. The fault is in the rulings given here, not in the
reviewers' work: the briefs cited [THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md) → [QUESTION-303](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-303.md) as the pattern,
and nobody checked it against the later section of [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md).

Twenty-eight QUESTIONs on main hold a promote_when moved this way. They
cover about thirty readings, from [THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md) (pass 8) to [THEORY-140](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-140.md)
(pass 14). Some are genuinely kind 4. The others need a kind-1 entry. That
sweep is its own branch, after this pass.

**Checks run before anything was applied.** The formal cluster's two scripts
were re-run here.

- **Theorem 44** ([THEORY-174](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-174.md)), on the binary triangle as both scenarios:
  52,472 deterministic procedures and 12,152 distinct maps.
  - The direct semantics and the hom-scenario semantics agree exactly on 20
    random procedures × 10 no-signalling models, the strongly contextual one
    included.
  - The linear program recognises random classical maps to 10⁻¹⁴, and the
    procedure it recovers reproduces the map on contextual models.
  - Two contextual hom models with non-classical maps are rejected (residual
    1.63).
  - A contextual hom model that induces a classical map is accepted. So
    e ↦ F_e is not injective, and the theorem's "some" matters.
  - A signalling source makes the result depend on the choice of context
    (0.50), which shows the check can see it.
- **[THEORY-186](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-186.md)'s random-start claim.** From small random starts, the
  off-diagonal part of the product falls from 15% to 2% of the largest
  strength as the scale falls from 10⁻¹ to 10⁻⁴. A decoupled start gives the
  exact solution, and the shallow control shows no ordering. This agrees
  with the reading, but its promote_when asks for a proof and rules out
  simulation, so it stays Proposed.
