# NOTE-tmphkmh6: Boem and others (2024) — Minding the gut: extending embodied cognition and perception to the gut complex

## Contribution

The "gut complex" concept; the proposal that non-neural gut components are
constitutive of cognition; a call for microbiota–cognition studies to
consider visceral perception as a mediator.

## Key results

1. **Constitution, not only influence (Introduction).** The gut complex "does
   not merely influence cognition and perception, but that—within an embodied
   paradigm of cognition—it also participates in the constitution of them."
2. **Two perspectives (Premise and aim).** The first concerns "the perceptual
   and cognitive characteristics of the subject as a whole"; the second that
   "the gut-complex has features attributable to its own forms of
   proto-cognition and perception that are in interesting ways independent of
   other structures".
3. **Open question (Introduction).** "the main question in this respect
   remains whether these factors are causal, constitutive, or enabling with
   respect to cognition."
4. **Human microbiota–cognition evidence (Extending the loci).** "it is too
   early to come to a definitive conclusion with certain studies showing
   beneficial effects (Baldi et al., 2021) and others none (Kelly et al.,
   2017)"; mediators proposed rarely "invoke visceral interoception".
5. **Motility not reflex (Gut motility as a basis of proto-cognition).** "Gut
   motility is not a hard-wired reflex, but the result of plastic and flexible
   crosstalk between muscularis macrophages ..., enteric neurons, and gut
   microbiota".
6. **Microbiota and early learning (Taking an evolutionary point of view).**
   Nerve-net animals that learn also host microbiota, so findings "cannot be
   interpreted to indicate that associative learning depends only on neuronal
   connections and suggest the causal relevance of the influence of
   microbiota" (an inference, not a result).
7. **Microbiota as both part and environment (Introduction).** "the
   microbiota ... represents not only a component of the biological individual
   as a functional whole, but also a component of the environment".
8. **Background gut feeling (Extending the loci).** "a general satiated state
   of wellbeing is a background “gut feeling” that modulates embodied
   cognition".

## Assumptions

A "liberal" and "extended" notion of cognition (sensing, valuing,
integrating, adapting, learning, anticipating, after Lyon et al. 2021);
non-representational embodied accounts are preferred; evolutionary priority
of the ENS is taken as reason for present cognitive roles; footnote 1 denies
AI cognition per se on embodiment grounds.

## Concepts

Gut complex; internalized external environment; proto-, basal or minimal
cognition; skin-brain thesis; affordances (Gibson; hierarchical affordance
competition); somatic marker hypothesis; Embodied Interoceptive Coding
(EPIC); holobiont; building-block model (their critique); causal vs
constitutive vs enabling roles.

## Bearing on the record

**On the LIT's and the exchange's characterization.** A15's account ("proposes extending embodied cognition to a 'gut complex' of
enteric nervous system, immune and endocrine systems, tissue and microbes")
is **accurate**. A15 recommends it as "the theoretical framing"; note that it
is a programmatic argument with no new evidence, that it leaves open whether
the gut's role is "causal, constitutive, or enabling", and that its second
perspective (gut proto-cognition of its own) is broader than Hasler's
partial-subject thesis (it concerns cognition, not experience).

- **CLAIM-166** (microbiome can drive higher-level behaviour and
  experience; human evidence modest): **qualifies; supports the framing, not
  the evidence**. It agrees the human microbiota–cognition evidence is
  inconclusive ("too early"), and suggests visceral perception as an
  unstudied mediator: a route through which microbial changes might reach
  affect, beside metabolites and the vagus. Its holobiont point (microbiota
  as both component and environment) bears on the claim's "membership is
  disputable".
- **CASE-047** (gut microbes and the host's hunger and mood): **supports the
  case's framing**. The "gut complex" names the intermediate level the case's
  2026-11-12 note adds; the paper's insistence that the four systems work
  together supports the case's "drive from below that is not a single part".
  It adds no data.

Other held entries it bears on (found on recheck):
- **QUESTION-033** ("who drives?" across levels): the paper's causal /

- **THEORY-874** (organicist vs informationalist, cognitivist vs embodied):

## Limitations

Programmatic; a broad, explicitly liberal notion of cognition that makes the
second perspective easy to satisfy; evolutionary arguments from ancestry to
current function; uneven and partly speculative empirical support; no tests
proposed beyond research directions.
