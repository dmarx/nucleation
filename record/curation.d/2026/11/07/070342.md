The first synthesis review, run as ADR-tmpw6bwa's demonstration under the
`synthesis-review` skill. Four read-only readers audited the shortlist,
three candidates each, with ADR-075's five checks and three admission
routes. Each checked its quotes of six words or more mechanically (every one
matching) and reran the mathematics. The filer checked the
admitted drafts against the entries before filing. The readers could not
write their reports to files in this harness. Their reports were saved
from their messages to the session's scratchpad; this entry keeps what
they found.

## CLAIM-474: admitted, its move left to the owner

CLAIM-474 passes the hypothesis route. The case is a stipulated theorem
that persistence of a robust boundary implies partial self-production. Its
"special classes" exemption needs a bound.

Moving it into the scheme would demote it. It is Active by a status-review
ruling the owner delegated, but a review files every SYNTHESIS as Proposed
and never sets one Current. It would also lose relations, because SYNTHESIS
has no `grounds`, `uses` or `corroborated_by`.

So it stays an Active CLAIM. Two things are for the owner:

- whether to move it and set the synthesis Current at once;
- whether the scheme should gain those fields first.

If it moves, these would be repointed:

- CASE-179's `illustrates`;
- SYNTHESIS-004's `integrates` and `premises`.
