# NOTE-tmp5dpvo: Dierickx and others (2024) — A data-centric approach for ethical and trustworthy AI in journalism

## Contribution

Proposes the Accuracy-Fairness-Transparency (AFT) framework for assessing the quality of datasets used to build machine-learning tools in newsrooms, deriving data-quality checks from three core principles of journalism ethics, and placing the work in the shift from model-centric to data-centric AI.

## Key results

- Abstract: the framework "relies on three of the core principles of ethical journalism—accuracy, fairness, and transparency".
- Framework: "accuracy, given the need to respect the facts; fairness, to avoid bias or unbalanced data; and transparency, which is fundamental when working with AI systems." Objectivity is left out "Because of the tensions surrounding this concept".
- "trusting the outcomes of AI systems should start with trusting the data that feeds them."
- Fairness "involves identifying the data producer’s data management practices, tracing the data lifecycle from creation to validation".
