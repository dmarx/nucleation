# NOTE-tmp8721q: Ellul (1983) — The Search for Ethics in a Technicist Society (posted introduction)

## Contribution

The author's own compact restatement of the thesis of The Technological Society, with the book's definition of technique quoted from its preface by Fowler.

## Key results

1. Book preface, via Fowler: "Technique is the totality of methods, rationally arrived at" and of absolute efficiency in every field.
2. "Technique is the complex and complete milieu in which human beings must live".
3. Its characteristics: "Autonomy, Unity, Universality, Totalization".
4. "It is not yet able to control its errors and dysfunctions".
5. "Apart from the city, the only choices left are" urbanization of the countryside or "desertification".
6. "Behavior and particular choices no longer have much significance."

## Assumptions

The technical phenomenon is treated as one system; Ellul offers description, not a model.

## Concepts

Technique; technicist society; autonomy; self-augmentation; reaction (self-correction).

## Limitations

A few paragraphs of a longer essay; the book's analyses of the economy and the state are not covered.
