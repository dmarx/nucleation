## Two rulings for later passes

- **When a NOTE is Read.** A NOTE goes to Read when every part its title draws on was read in full, with the quotes checked, and the unread rest does not bear on the title. The history entry lists what was skipped and why. A title that says a source "shows" a theorem draws on the proof, so skipping the proof keeps the NOTE Skimmed (Zhuk, Bulatov).
- **[ADR-074](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-074.md) and part-books.** [ADR-074](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-074.md)'s exclusion of uploads of whole in-copyright books covers an upload of part of one: a chapter of a monograph posted to a document-sharing site is still a piece of the book, not the author's or a course's copy. Pereboom's SciSpace chapter was set aside on this ground.
