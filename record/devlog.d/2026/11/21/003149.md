**The record worked as memory.** In the session of 2026-11-20 the owner
asked a run of questions: how narrowing degrees of freedom relates to an
entry's life cycle, then to contraction, then to drift, hierarchy and
hyperbolicity, depth scaling. Each time, searching the record before
answering found that it already held a key formal piece: [CLAIM-635](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-635.md) (an
exactly conserved observable keeps information about the start), [THEORY-960](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-960.md)
(DeGroot's consensus keeps exactly πF), [CLAIM-581](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-581.md) (peak-cost thresholds are
an ultrametric), Saxe's deep linear dynamics ([THEORY-186](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-186.md), [THEORY-740](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-740.md)),
Schoenholz's depth scales ([THEORY-2041](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-2041.md)). The new results were mostly joins
between held entries, which is what the record is for.

**The owner's challenges improved the claims.** Twice the owner questioned
the framing of an answer, and both times the challenge was right:

- "Are you sure these are different things? … Isn't stabilization just
  monotone contraction?" The assistant had said forgetting a source and
  anchoring to it are different mechanisms. They are one contraction told
  apart by what each step conserves; the claim was rewritten before
  merging.
- "maybe I'm being brash calling it 'scale separation' and a more
  appropriate -- or at least easier to demonstrate -- assertion would call
  it 'rate differences'". Checking the sentence that prompted this found it
  wrong, and the restated claim (rate differences between levels exceeding
  those within) is both better supported and more precise. [CLAIM-478](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-478.md) took a
  new version.

**Checking by simulation before asserting** caught one error in time: a
designed hierarchy with noisy rates looked like it produced "a finer tree";
a direct comparison of tree topologies showed it kept the designed one, and
the claim was corrected before the owner built on it.

To revisit: the pattern of asking the assistant to relate a new idea to
the record's existing account, then challenging the framing, then filing.
It produced six pull requests in a day; whether that pace is sustainable
for review is a question for the retrospective.
