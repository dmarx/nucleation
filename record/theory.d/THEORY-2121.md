# THEORY-2121: Second-order false belief lags by years

## The account

Five-year-olds who fail second-order questions are not answering from
reality but applying a first-order strategy; with experience they come to
select the second-order one.

## Evidence

An experiment with 5- to 6-year-olds and an ACT-R model.
