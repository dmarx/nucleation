## The account

Johnson and Hayes-Roth describe BB1, in which domain knowledge sources work on a domain blackboard while control knowledge sources post strategies, foci and heuristics to a separate control blackboard whose current plan the scheduler uses to rate pending actions, and argue that this one explicit, dynamic control plan lets a system integrate hierarchical planning, opportunistic focusing and goal-directed reasoning through a uniform mechanism.
