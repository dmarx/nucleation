# LIT-tmpc3h2w: The Standard of Living (The Tanner Lectures on Human Values, Lectures I and II)

Amartya Sen (1985). "The Standard of Living". The Tanner Lectures on Human Values, Lecture I "Concepts and Critiques" and Lecture II "Lives and Capabilities", delivered at Clare Hall, Cambridge, 11–12 March 1985. Also published in G. Hawthorn (ed.), *The Standard of Living* (Cambridge UP, 1987), not checked.

## Key takeaways

- "A functioning is an achievement, whereas a capability is the ability to achieve" (Lecture II).
- Opulence and utility both fail as the standard; the partial ordering by ranges of weights suffices.
- The fasting/starving contrast and the three notions of agency achievement, well-being and the standard of living are here, in 1985.
