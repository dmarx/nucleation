# THEORY-tmpeqsoz: Tomasello argues that uniquely human thinking evolved in two steps from great apes' individual intentionality, first joint intentionality in dyadic collaboration and cooperative communication, yielding perspectival representation, recursive inference and social self-monitoring, then collective intentionality in cultural groups, yielding objective representation, self-reflective inference and normative self-governance

## The account

Thinking is off-line representation, inference and self-monitoring. Great
apes think with schematic iconic representations, causal and intentional
inference and individual self-monitoring. Early humans, collaborating
under joint goals and communicating cooperatively, came to represent
perspectives, infer recursively and monitor themselves from a partner's
view. Modern humans in cultural groups took the view of anyone who would
be one of us: objective representation, self-reflective inference and
normative self-governance.
