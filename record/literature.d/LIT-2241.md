# LIT-2241: Emergence of metastability in frustrated oscillatory networks: the key role of hierarchical modularity

Enrico Caprioglio and Luc Berthouze (2024). "Emergence of metastability in frustrated oscillatory networks: the key role of hierarchical modularity." *Frontiers in Network Physiology* 4: 1436046. [ARXIV-2405.14542](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14542) DOI-10.3389/fnetp.2024.1436046.

## Key takeaways

- Hierarchical modularity alone, with identical oscillators and phase
  frustration, yields stable, breathing, metastable and alternating
  chimeras.
- Modules act as "a single macro-oscillator" only while the spectral gap
  between the lowest two layers is large (cutoff near 2 in their model).
- Coarse-graining to the slow modes reproduces the two-population model
  only with a large gap; otherwise "the contribution from the fast modes
  should not be discarded".
