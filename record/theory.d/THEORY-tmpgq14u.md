# THEORY-tmpgq14u: Tomasello and Vaish hold that human morality is a form of cooperation that arose in two steps, repeated in child development, first collaboration with particular partners giving a second-personal morality of helping, equal sharing and commitment, then group life giving an agent-neutral morality of mutually known norms enforced on others and, through guilt and shame, on oneself

## The account

Morality is a subset of cooperation. In phylogeny and in development it
comes in two steps: a second-personal joint morality toward particular
collaborative partners (helping, equal sharing of jointly earned spoils,
commitment), then an agent-neutral collective morality of group norms,
which individuals enforce on others and, through guilt and shame, on
themselves.

## Evidence

Review of experiments with great apes and young children, mostly from the
authors' lab.
