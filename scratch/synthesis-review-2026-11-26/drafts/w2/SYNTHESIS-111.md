# SYNTHESIS-111: Collective correction is a run of separable steps

*The account of the line self-correction, "How an organization can know and fail to correct" (atlas scope 10, ADR-146). It began, at version 1, as a thematic account of principle P10 of the owner's manuscript from-obstruction-to-reorganization, and the line was named at the synthesis review of 2026-11-23 to gather it. It is also a part account of learning-and-inquiry (SYNTHESIS-118). The correction of a public regulator, across the normative lines, is the neighbouring account SYNTHESIS-121. Its 26 components carry twelve lines among them, mostly distributed-agency (19), self-correction (10, every entry of the line but its one LEAD), will-organization (7) and organizational-normativity (5). Version 4 is written as one argument under ADR-151. It adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

How can an organization possess accurate information about a problem and
still fail to correct it? And what decides whether a system that has
critics and feedback can correct itself?

## The argument

**Having a signal is not acting on it.** What a system has, what it knows
it has and what it can deploy within a deadline are different, and the
last is a relation, not a property (CLAIM-296). For an organization's own
signals, access, the signal's authority over action and action on it are
separable (CLAIM-349). What carries a distinction from one to the next is
legibility, which is a relation among a representation, an observer's
rules, its resources and a task, and is directed: what is legible to a
governing institution and what of its governing is legible to others are
different relations (TERM-064). So a report can be true and recognized
and still reach no one who can act. One complaint about an unsafe
practice is correctly classified both by equipment and by workers'
obligations, and the class the intake procedure makes primary decides
whether it reaches a body that can change working conditions (CASE-132).

**For a discrepancy to change what an organization does, three things are
needed, and any can fail while the others hold.** It must become legible,
a corresponding demand must be able to move action, and a fitting
revision must be executable within deadline and resources (CLAIM-523).
The three come in no fixed order, and none of them shows that the demand
or the revision is justified (CLAIM-523). For contributions from others,
the same separation runs further: admission, recognition in apt terms,
credibility, uptake, and a part in revising the standards of uptake are
each possible without the later ones, so an organization can widen access
while every channel by which a less authoritative contribution could
change the criteria of authority stays closed (CLAIM-403, resting on
CLAIM-349). The complaint case is that ladder's middle rungs coming
apart: in its variant the report is admitted and recognized under both
classes and taken up under one (CASE-132). Challenger to Columbia shows
the ladder in one night's teleconference, a warning whose standing
changed as it moved, and then a correction made in the rules whose
capacity had eroded by the next accident (CASE-138). For a protected
objection the decomposition is carried to six stages that must hold
together, with success bounded by the weakest (CLAIM-732, resting on
CLAIM-523); that bound is Proposed and about objections.

**Which distinctions must be legible depends on what the organization
commits to.** An organization acting on what it observes has a policy
acceptable everywhere exactly when, for each observation, the
circumstances it cannot tell apart share an acceptable action; where
acceptable actions overlap, the coarsest sufficient observations are
several and incomparable, so which distinctions must be made legible
depends on which acceptable action the organization commits to
(CLAIM-535, demonstrated). Legibility is therefore not only upstream of
action: the commitment fixes part of what must be seen.

**Feedback present, correction failing: the evidence and the scoring.**
What keeps a judgement–observation loop from confirming itself is
evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the
interpretation it tests; a loop that selects its own evidence can lock in
with action available (CLAIM-342). An organization can be governed from
outside, without any decision made for it, through the evidence its
procedures receive or through the criteria by which they weigh it; the
two routes are distinct, and where an evaluator's evidence or resources
depend on what it evaluates, formal independence of its procedure does
not make its evaluation independent (CLAIM-404). The two routes need
different remedies: in the lab's runs, weighting admitted evidence by
known admission probabilities undid lock-in from selection and did
nothing for lock-in from self-scoring (CLAIM-466, with an oracle's
propensities). Perspectives compete over the tests that measure them, and
one that gains influence over its test changes what later success shows
(CLAIM-416). Volkswagen's defeat device is that claim's limiting case,
influence by detection, uncovered when outside researchers measured
emissions on the road rather than through certification (CASE-tmptm1pi,
A's account, unverified until its source is read).

**What decides it: the coupling of evidence, evaluation and authority.**
The owner's principle is that a system's ability to correct itself
depends on how evidence, evaluation and authority are coupled and on what
it is prevented from rewriting or rejecting (CLAIM-1024), and it rests on
CLAIM-342 and four models from the governance leads. A sensor network
whose correspondences drift improves accuracy only against beacons its
revision can neither rewrite nor refuse; revised by its own disagreement
it regains consistency, not accuracy (CLAIM-1183). An evaluator can be
captured through the loop with the party it evaluates while a resource
channel runs, even when its conferral reads no interpretation, and what
prevents it is a return channel too weak to close the loop (CLAIM-1202).
Grants indexed by role, with critics scored by observed relevance, leave
neither factions nor capture, where scalar grants scored by agreement do
not (CLAIM-1029). Where no process holds evidence on a block of tasks
that does not come from the proposals, a rule that treats its members
alike cannot tell honest dissent from an attack pushing the same block
(CLAIM-1194). These are four models, and the generalization is the
owner's interpretive step (CLAIM-1024). Protection has a price as well as
a use: in the same network a protected beacon that is itself miscalibrated
drives the network's error to its own, the network's revision does not
lower it, and in the runs it fell only when a further, rarer protected
channel revised the beacon (CLAIM-1185).

**Effectiveness is no evidence of correctability.** An arrangement can
keep or increase its capacity for effective action while its capacity to
be corrected by others degrades, because the two are kept by different
relations (CLAIM-462, resting on CLAIM-099's distinction between keeping
a regime of action and governing among regimes). In the same way, an
institution can be reliably responsive to reasons within its governing
mechanism and unresponsive to justified reasons for revising it, so
first-order responsiveness is no evidence of the second order (CLAIM-719).
The Challenger-to-Columbia history is evidence that an organization can
reproduce the conditions that keep a known hazard from constraining it,
reforms undone included (ARG-062, grounded in CASE-138); by that
argument's own critical questions it does not isolate reproduction from
budget and schedule pressure, and it says nothing of a wrong.

**Correcting the correction process.** The top rung of the ladder, a part
in revising the standards of uptake, is where a closed organization stays
closed (CLAIM-403). A purpose an institution adopts can come to govern
the mechanisms by which it could be reconsidered, which evidence counts,
who holds authority, which criticism is admissible, until proposals to
revise it must show they serve it; the claim's remedy is to hold apart
the authority to pursue a purpose and the authority to decide whether it
should go on governing (CLAIM-tmp6xxuc, A's). Where an organization
reproduces representations and allocations of authority that keep a
justified standard from constraining it, the wrong beyond the violation
is proposed to be a breach of a second-order duty of answerability, to
keep relevant violations knowable, contestable and correctable, and it is
a verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible
alternative (CLAIM-624, A's; ARG-062 is offered for its reproductive
layer, not for the duty).

**Where it is firm and where it is open.** The separations, from having
to correcting, are held by the record and argued, not built; CLAIM-535
and the four coupling results are demonstrated, each in its own model.
The owner's principle that joins the models, the six-stage bound, the
purpose's capture of its review and the duty of answerability are
Proposed, and the last two are A's wording. How the human members of a
collective keep the ability to recognize, authorize and carry out a
correction as more of its work is automated is a plan, not a finding
(LEAD-tmp9snzi), and whether the coupling conditions are separate
requirements would be tested by LEAD-220.

## Not stated on the record

- **That the two halves are one map.** The separable achievements
  (CLAIM-523, CLAIM-403) and the coupling of evidence, evaluation and
  authority (CLAIM-1024 and its instances) are joined here by the line's
  blurb and this account's order, and in stored relations only by
  CLAIM-404's use of TERM-064 and CLAIM-342's citation of CLAIM-349. No
  entry says that legibility fails where evidence passes through the
  interpretation, volitional efficacy where authority is coupled back,
  or correctability where scoring follows agreement. *Verdict under
  ADR-075, step 5: fails, a free parameter.* Which achievement each
  coupling acts on is not fixed by any component (evaluation could be
  credibility, uptake or correctability), so no case can separate it.
  Not filed.
- **A collective learns only if every link holds, up to revision of the
  standards of uptake.** The manuscript's P10 stated this. CLAIM-403's
  separability, and CLAIM-523's three achievements, which do not include
  the top rung, allow a collective to change its procedures on a
  contribution while its standards of uptake stay fixed.
- **Its self-evaluation confirms itself without independent evidence, as
  a necessity in general.** CLAIM-342 says such a loop "can lock in". A
  necessity is held in one model and for one class of rule (CLAIM-1194).
  That every self-evaluating system fails without independent evidence
  is not stated.
- **That the owner's conditions are separate requirements, and a third
  independence among them.** CLAIM-1024's four instances are in four
  models, and whether each condition is needed when the others hold is
  open (LEAD-220). The exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" read
  CLAIM-466 as showing that "evaluator independence, evidence
  independence and independence of authority allocation are distinct
  requirements" (CLAIM-466's note of 2026-11-26); the third is near
  CLAIM-1202, which shows in one model that informational independence
  does not stop capture while a resource channel runs, but no entry
  derives it as a requirement apart from the others.
- **That a purpose governing its own reconsideration is CLAIM-719's
  second order for purposes.** CLAIM-tmp6xxuc relates itself to
  standing, narrow optimization and evaluation that decides authority,
  not to CLAIM-719. *Verdict: fails, a carry*: it relabels the purpose as
  the mechanism and states no inference. Not filed.
- **The principle carried to a regulator** (the manuscript
  governance-of-possibility's join JD6, §13.6; version 3). The manuscript
  reads each coupling condition for a public regulator. CLAIM-404 holds
  the organizational form; no component states the carriage, and the
  four instances are a sensor network and populations on the
  meaning–influence model. *Verdict: fails, a carry*, and its positive
  half waits on the item above. Not filed; the regulator's correction is
  SYNTHESIS-121's scope.
- **That the weakest stage bounds the ladder of uptake.** The bound is
  CLAIM-732's, about objections.
- **That the steps form a sequence.** CLAIM-523 says its achievements
  come in no fixed order, and CLAIM-535 runs the commitment back to what
  must be legible. An ordering proposed from outside the record for the
  neighbouring line is recorded in SYNTHESIS-118.
