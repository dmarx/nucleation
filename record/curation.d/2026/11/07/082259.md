**The admitted, in brief.**

- **[SYNTHESIS-010](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-010.md) (from [CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md)).** The case is a village water
  scheme that meets every condition, judged before withdrawal. It fails
  once the field officer whose visits kept contributions common knowledge
  is withdrawn. Two corrections are marked:
  - the conditions are judged, and standing inputs named, before
    withdrawal, which closes a free parameter;
  - the defeater's clause on the length of substitutive support, which the
    title dropped on 2026-11-05, is removed.

  It moved as Current under [ADR-077](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-077.md), since the claim was Active and its
  four premises are Active.
- **[SYNTHESIS-011](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-011.md) (from [CLAIM-336](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-336.md)).** The defeater's second clause is
  narrowed to the held-out test of matched complexity that A55 §7 gave and
  [ARG-065](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-065.md) holds. The case is one where transport observables are fixed by
  the receiver's state at reading time. The thesis stays the owner's.
- **[SYNTHESIS-009](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-009.md) ([QUESTION-395](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-395.md)).** Records kept per capacity fix only
  the marginals of [CLAIM-587](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-587.md)'s conjunction, so they bound readiness by the
  Fréchet inequalities. A refutation from the weakest capacity survives
  decay ([CLAIM-558](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-558.md)); a certification does not. Its bounds are textbook. The
  criterion and the asymmetry are new, and the reader flagged that a filer
  could fairly have filed it as a derived claim instead. With
  [SYNTHESIS-010](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-010.md), it gives the readiness line its first syntheses.

  - its citation of [THEORY-752](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-752.md) reads more than the reading shows;

    which are in neither its `grounds` nor its `rests_on`.
- **[CLAIM-425](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-425.md)** calls itself "the same achievement" as [CLAIM-424](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-424.md), but its
  title never requires revising standards. Either the title gains that, or
  the body says "the analogous regulation".
- **By-products, not filed:**
  - a demonstrated corollary of [CLAIM-366](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-366.md), answering part of [QUESTION-127](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-127.md).
    Common descent is constant on the orbits of every function-preserving
    symmetry that is a diffeomorphism of the parameters. A test with a
    fixed Euclidean budget is not: with f = v·relu(ux) and a rescaling by
    one half, the squared gradient norm is 612 at (4, 1) and 288 at its
    image (2, 2);
  - on [CASE-206](../../record/cases.d/CASE-206.md)'s vertex, a non-trivial agreement between outside and
    inside needs a shared budget of 4.125. That is more than the cheapest
    readiness (1.866) and more than full observation (4);
  - UFI is monotone in the actuator set, so it cannot be [QUESTION-380](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-380.md)'s
    predictor where adding a nucleus can hurt.
