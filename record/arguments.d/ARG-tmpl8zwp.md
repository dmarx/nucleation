# ARG-tmpl8zwp: Wolf's Tanner Lectures carry the premise of three of the flourishing claims, that fulfilment and meaning come apart, that no one in particular has authority over worth, and that meaning pursued for its own sake defeats itself, but not their application to arrangements and institutions, which Wolf disclaims and which is A's

## The inference

1. **[CLAIM-tmp58u4k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp58u4k.md)** needs fulfilment and meaning to come apart. Wolf: "meaning arises when subjective attraction meets objective attractiveness"; "mere passive recognition and a positive attitude toward an object's or activity's value are not sufficient for a meaningful life"; Sisyphus Fulfilled is worse off in a respect however fulfilled he feels ([NOTE-tmpzth5f](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzth5f.md), results 1, 2, 4). The premise holds.
2. **[CLAIM-tmpfyxes](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfyxes.md)** needs worth to be real and judged fallibly, with no one holding authority over it. Wolf's answer to "Who's to say?" is "no one in particular", with fallibilism; the objectivity of value "is an unsolved problem in philosophy" (results 5, 6). The premise holds.
3. **[CLAIM-tmpplibk](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpplibk.md)** needs the pursuit of meaning for its own sake to undermine it. Wolf: "we can recognize a paradox of meaningfulness, similar to but deeper than the paradox of hedonism" (result 7). The premise holds.

## Critical questions

- **Does the work say this, read in context?** Yes for each premise; each is a thesis of the lectures, not an aside.
- **Is it said within the scope the claims need?** No. Wolf's account is of a life, and she disclaims practical guidance ("of little or no practical use"). Each claim applies it to arrangements: an arrangement's satisfying preferences (1), an institution's limits (2), an institution's measuring meaning (3). Those steps are A's, and each claim's body says what it adds: [CLAIM-tmp58u4k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp58u4k.md) by a stipulated separating case, [CLAIM-tmpfyxes](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfyxes.md) as a political boundary Wolf does not draw, [CLAIM-tmpplibk](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpplibk.md) as a mechanism through which institutions recruit their members into the paradox.
- **Does any claim lean on the reading for more than it holds?** Not as filed. Each takes Wolf as ground for its premise only. Wolf's own note that "many people, through no fault of their own, simply lack the opportunity for meaning" (result 8) is the nearest she comes to arrangements, and it supports the cultivation side of [CLAIM-tmpfyxes](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfyxes.md), not its limit on imposition.

## Verdict

Proposed. [THEORY-tmp4ajaz](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4ajaz.md) grounds the three claims' premises and nothing beyond them; the applications are A's, interpretive, and stand or fall as the claims' own.
