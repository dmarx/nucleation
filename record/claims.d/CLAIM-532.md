# CLAIM-532: Self-formation, in the sense the record holds (the transformation dimension of a will-organization), is the production by an agent's own activity on a discrepancy it cannot yet resolve of a maintained capacity, a new distinction it can make legible or a new channel by which it can act, that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later ones; it is told apart from learning an action by transfer to discrepancies of the same class after the inducing conditions are gone, and from a capacity installed or upheld from outside by interventions on the processes that produce and maintain it

## The claim

The record's self-formation is the transformation dimension of a
will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)) and the capacity to transform the governing
organization ([CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md)). This claim says what such a transformation
produces.

- **From a discrepancy it cannot yet resolve.** A demand or a conflict its
  current regimes do not meet ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)'s missing face; Jennings's
  stalemate, [THEORY-1341](../theory.d/THEORY-1341.md)).
- **A new distinction or a new channel.** Either the agent comes to tell
  apart circumstances it could not, which by [CLAIM-535](CLAIM-535.md) is what a policy
  needs where acceptable actions differ; or it comes to act along a direction
  it could not, which is [CASE-115](../cases.d/CASE-115.md)'s split of one shared parameter into
  task-routed ones. Either enlarges what is co-satisfiable, which is relief in
  [CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)'s sense, not suppression.
- **Maintained, and outlasting the discrepancy.** The new capacity persists
  after the conflict that induced it is gone. That needs a productive cycle
  that keeps it ([CLAIM-516](CLAIM-516.md), [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md)).
- **Serving a class.** It applies to later discrepancies of the same kind, not
  only the one that occasioned it.

**Two tests.** Against learning one action: remove the inducing conditions
and present a new discrepancy of the same class. A formed capacity transfers;
a learned response does not. Against outside installation: intervene on the
processes that produce and maintain the capacity. By [CLAIM-522](CLAIM-522.md) only such
interventions separate an agent that formed a capacity from one in which it
was installed or is upheld from outside. And by [CLAIM-540](CLAIM-540.md) the change
must be in what selects, not in an observer's estimate of it.

**The construction, built** ([CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md), 2026-11-03). [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md)'s
maintained connector joined to [CASE-115](../cases.d/CASE-115.md)'s split: frustration at two demands one
output cannot meet drives production of a sensor and a channel that then
maintain each other. The capacity forms past a threshold of discrepancy,
outlasts the conflict, and meets a later conflict of the same class with no
frustration, where an unexposed agent must first form it. That is the first
test passed in a model.

**The second test, narrowed by it.** A capacity upheld from outside is told apart
afterwards, by knocking out the outside supply or the internal maintenance. A
capacity installed by an outside pulse and then maintained by the agent's own
cycle is not: after formation it is the same organization as one the agent
formed, under every knockout. Only a knockout of the frustration-driven
production made during formation separates them. So "interventions on the
processes that produce" the capacity has to mean interventions while it is
being produced. After the fact, whether a self-maintained capacity was
self-formed is a question about its history ([QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)), and the present
organization does not answer it. That matters wherever origin
matters. It is the structural accounts' manipulation problem ([THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md),
[THEORY-1118](../theory.d/THEORY-1118.md)), and the case for Fischer and Ravizza's historical ownership
([THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md)) and Kane's ultimate responsibility ([CLAIM-022](CLAIM-022.md)). It is how
reflexive control by a one-time perturbation escapes detection ([CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md),
[CASE-123](../cases.d/CASE-123.md)). And it is where self-determination theory's claim that the
history of acquisition shapes autonomy ([THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md)) would have to show up, in
the capacity's relations to the agent's other demands rather than in the
capacity itself. [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md)'s section on why the provenance result matters
sets these out.

## What it does not say

- **That self-formation is free in Kane's sense.** It is a non-libertarian
  account; ultimate responsibility is set aside ([CLAIM-022](CLAIM-022.md)).
- **That every new capacity is self-formed.** Installation and outside upkeep
  are what the tests exclude.
- **That the steps form a developmental sequence** ([CLAIM-474](CLAIM-474.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-07: the action route, from the second share of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

A58 §8 reads this claim rightly. Its second route, "**An organization can overcome a limitation by changing what it can do, not only by improving what it can see.**", is this claim's new channel. A calls it "easy to overlook", but [CLAIM-572](CLAIM-572.md) prices it against observation on one budget.
