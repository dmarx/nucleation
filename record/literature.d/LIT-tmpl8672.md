## Why this is here

Berendt & Uhrich ([NOTE-tmpp2j50](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpp2j50.md)) take their definition of rivalry from it. A rivalry that depends on the relationship, not the stakes, is [CASE-098](../cases.d/CASE-098.md)'s subject.
