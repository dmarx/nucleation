# LIT-tmp7ib47: Monitoring AI-Modified Content at Scale: A Case Study on the Impact of ChatGPT on AI Conference Peer Reviews

Weixin Liang, Zachary Izzo, Yaohui Zhang, Haley Lepp, Hancheng Cao, Xuandong
Zhao, Lingjiao Chen, Haotian Ye, Sheng Liu, Zhi Huang, Daniel A. McFarland
and James Y. Zou (2024). "Monitoring AI-Modified Content at Scale: A Case
Study on the Impact of ChatGPT on AI Conference Peer Reviews" *Proceedings
of the 41st International Conference on Machine Learning, PMLR 235*.
[ARXIV-2403.07183](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07183).

## Key takeaways

AI-modified review text rose sharply at ML conferences after ChatGPT and
concentrates in hurried, low-confidence and homogeneous reviews.
