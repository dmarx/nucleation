### Added

- A protocol for writing a manuscript from the record (ADR-tmpbdfmk, Proposed) and the `write-manuscript` skill, with eight invariants: a manuscript is a view outside the record; every step cites its entry with standing; nothing in it is new to the record, its joins filed as accounts (the owner's ruling); voices stay separate; a motivating draft is audited first; results are executed; gaps go back to the record; every substantive version is checked, and relevant uncited readings are cited. The steps were revised on the record of how the first manuscript was made (pull requests 555 to 623, RETRO-006 to RETRO-008), at the owner's question. CLAUDE.md names the skill.
