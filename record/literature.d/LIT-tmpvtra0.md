# LIT-tmpvtra0: Wave-functionalism

Valia Allori (2021), *Synthese 199(5–6), 12271–12293 (2021)* — DOI-10.1007/s11229-021-03332-z

## Key takeaways

- The wave function is best understood functionally, as whatever plays its empirical, nomological and explanatory roles in the theory. This makes it non-material, non-epistemic and non-representational, yet ontic, multiply realizable (by a phase-shifted ψ or a density matrix), and part of the law, which avoids the main objections to nomological, epistemic and pragmatist views.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Allori proposes treating the wave function as a non-material, non-epistemic, non-representational entity, defined functionally by the roles it plays in quantum theory. The view shares features with the nomological account of the wave function and with pragmatist and epistemic approaches. She argues that it avoids the main objections each of those faces.
