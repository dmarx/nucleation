summary: >-
  Peyré, Cuturi & Solomon (2016). An entropic Gromov–Wasserstein discrepancy between similarity matrices of any size and loss, computed by an iteration in which each step is a Sinkhorn problem, and a barycenter of such matrices as a Fréchet mean of user-chosen size, histogram and weights. Block coordinate relaxation alternates coupling updates with a closed-form matrix update: for square loss the weighted average of the inputs realigned by their couplings, for KL a weighted geometric average, so PSD and infinitely divisible kernels stay in their classes. Convergence at the step size used is observed, not proved, and only stationary points are sought. Applications: shape barycenters, point-cloud k-means and molecular energy regression.

# LIT-1797: Gromov-Wasserstein Averaging of Kernel and Distance Matrices

Peyré, G., Cuturi, M., & Solomon, J. (2016). Gromov-Wasserstein averaging of kernel and distance matrices. *ICML 2016*, PMLR 48, 2664–2672.

## Why this is here

A6's "emergent interlingua", held as high-risk: a barycenter of several communities' response structures is a reconstructed common structure, not the meaning common to all languages, and it can average away the distinctions that matter to minorities ([CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md)). Its use would be as a test of where common structure fails.

The reading ([NOTE-1343](../notes.d/NOTE-1343.md)) locates the risk: with couplings fixed, one input's distinction enters the barycenter with that input's weight, shrunk rather than removed, but the size, weights and regularization fixed in advance, and seed-dependent stationary points, can remove or redistribute it ([THEORY-1121](../theory.d/THEORY-1121.md)).
