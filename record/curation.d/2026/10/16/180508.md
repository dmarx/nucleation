emotion-and-affect, psychopathology-and-treatment, causality, logic,
operations-research, philosophy-of-mathematics, embodied-cognition,

## What did not enter, and why

- **The withdrawn sheaf paper ([LIT-813](../../record/literature.d/LIT-813.md), [NOTE-629](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-629.md)).** Its authors withdrew
  it, its central construction fails as stated ([NOTE-629](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-629.md) gives a
  counterexample to Proposition 5), and the results that survive are proved
  in general by [LIT-788](../../record/literature.d/LIT-788.md), which is now a THEORY's source.
- **Sayed Ahmed's note ([LIT-956](../../record/literature.d/LIT-956.md), [NOTE-817](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-817.md)).** [NOTE-817](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-817.md) files no THEORY
  because what the paper asserts is either standard and better sourced
  elsewhere or stated without proof. That holds: Theorems 4.1 and 5.1–5.5
  have no proofs, and the one proved theorem (7.6) rests on the author's
  unheld preprints.

So 16 of 18 readings became sources. That fits the owner's hypothesis for
this group, with caveats. Several of the THEORYs are proved or computed
results stated with their scope (the lottery, Łoś, Shampoo, the averaging
counts), which the readings had already set out. Nine of the sixteen
NOTEs said there was no THEORY. For [NOTE-113](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-113.md), [NOTE-139](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-139.md), [NOTE-177](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-177.md), [NOTE-141](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-141.md),
[NOTE-015](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-015.md) and [NOTE-332](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-332.md) that meant no existing THEORY was supported; [NOTE-332](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-332.md)
sketched the one now filed. [NOTE-453](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-453.md) declined only a THEORY comparing IFS
with ACT, which is still not filed. [NOTE-456](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-456.md) meant that the source carries
no claim beyond its definition; its THEORY is Proposed and says so.
[NOTE-696](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-696.md) meant that the excerpts ground no claim about language; its
THEORY is about the operator only.

## Choices made

- **Skims.** Three THEORYs rest wholly or partly on a skim ([LIT-788](../../record/literature.d/LIT-788.md),
  [LIT-894](../../record/literature.d/LIT-894.md), [LIT-591](../../record/literature.d/LIT-591.md)). All are Proposed and say what was read.

  result and its neural mapping have different standing, so they are
  separate, the second presupposing the first. Wenmackers's three chapters
  make three unrelated claims.

  open problem that he set aside. [THEORY-422](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-422.md) extends [THEORY-143](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-143.md). No
  other rivals were declared: Desmond and Fricker ([THEORY-327](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-327.md)) are
  compatible, and Corfield and De Haro and Butterfield use "duality" for
  different relations without disagreeing on a fact.
- **Existing documents edited.** [CLAIM-166](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-166.md) now grounds on [THEORY-560](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-560.md),
  and [CLAIM-127](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-127.md) and [CLAIM-130](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-130.md) on [THEORY-419](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-419.md), each with a version bump
