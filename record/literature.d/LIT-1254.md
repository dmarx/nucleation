# LIT-1254: Information loss under coarse graining: A geometric approach

Raju, A., Machta, B. B. & Sethna, J. P. (2018). Information loss under coarse graining: A geometric approach. *Physical Review E* 98(5): 052112. doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.98.052112. Preprint: arXiv:1710.05787 (16 October 2017).

Read in full in [NOTE-972](../notes.d/NOTE-972.md). [THEORY-742](../theory.d/THEORY-742.md) states what it proves.

## Why this is here

A2 recommended it at high priority as a bridge from information-preserving abstraction to the geometry of distinguishability, asking whether learning accessibility can be related to the contraction of distinguishable directions under coarse-graining. Bears on [QUESTION-217](../questions.d/QUESTION-217.md), and on [THEORY-194](../theory.d/THEORY-194.md) and [THEORY-202](../theory.d/THEORY-202.md).
