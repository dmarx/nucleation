# CLAIM-tmp85bhw: Claimed and granted readiness are one kind at two levels

*Alternative E to QUESTION-tmppt23x, the owner's. It merges A (CLAIM-tmpy367s) and B (CLAIM-tmp8ikja).*

## The claim

A put affect on the claim side of CLAIM-442's gap and B put it on both
sides. The owner says B already satisfies a formalization of action
readiness, so the difference between them is not a difference of kind.
The record supports that.

- **Formalized, action readiness is concern-relative potentiation with
  precedence** (CLAIM-tmpaqezl, TERM-094).
- **A perspective's readiness** is a potentiation of what that perspective
  would have the system do, offered to the negotiation. Seen from the
  system, it is a claim.
- **The system's readiness** is the potentiation of what the system will
  do: the ordering the negotiation grants, and the one its contests would
  be settled along (CLAIM-tmphyvh3). That ordering has TERM-094's marks at
  the system's level. It persists, resumes after interruption, captures the
  system's attention and takes priority over the system's alternatives.
  TERM-076 already reads Frijda's marks as "how a potential shows itself in
  time".
- **So "claim" and "grant" name a relation between levels.** What is
  granted at one level is a claim at the next. A firm's granted readiness
  is a claim on its market, or on the regulator that can grant or refuse
  it.

## What it changes

- **A and B stop being rivals.** Both are kept, as the two levels of
  alternative E.
- **CLAIM-442 is restated.** "An emotion ... is a claim" and "the system's
  valence is read on what the negotiation grants" describe one kind seen
  from below and from above.
- **Alternative D (CLAIM-tmps1h34) fits.** On D, emotion is readiness
  communicated to the level that can grant it. E says that level is the
  next one up.
- **Veit's objection (THEORY-101) reaches the whole of affect on E.**
  Granted readiness is affect too, and CLAIM-435 reads Veit's common
  currency as a regime of the potential. E does not answer that objection.
  It locates it.

## What it does not say

That the levels are only two. The recursion is the point. Nor that every
level's readiness is felt; that is CLAIM-439's question.
