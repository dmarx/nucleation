# Two departments perform no extrications during ordinary operation, but only one can satisfy a deadline-constrained extrication demand under intervention

## The case

**Department A** has a trained, staffed and equipped rescue squad.
**Department B** has the same ordinary resources and no specialized
extrication capability.

**Ordinary operation.** Over an incident history in which no incident
needs extrication, both perform the same ordinary interventions, and both
perform zero extrications. Their records of completed work are the same.

**Intervention.** Impose a time-critical incident that needs specialized
rescue within a deadline. By construction, Department A has an admissible
response that meets the deadline and Department B does not.

## What it can show

The assistant A: "Observed nonuse does not identify the presence, absence, or
operational adequacy of a latent capability." The case also separates
successful selective nonaction, Department A's squad not used because no
incident called for it, from nonaction caused by incapacity, Department
B's squad never used because B has none. The two look the same in routine output.

This is [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s pattern carried from interpretation to a capability:
systems that agree under ordinary operation differ, and a specified
intervention exposes the difference that passive observation of the
matched trajectories does not. [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) rests on its own constructions,
not on this one, so the case illustrates it without bearing its weight.
It is one premise of [ARG-tmp5fhgc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp5fhgc.md), the inference from low exercise to
waste, which it helps defeat. [QUESTION-tmpw46vw](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpw46vw.md) asks what observations
would tell the two departments apart short of the intervention; in [TERM-tmpuc919](../terms.d/TERM-tmpuc919.md)'s
terms they differ in readiness for one contingency.

## What it cannot show

- **That keeping Department A's capability was worth its cost.** That needs an
  objective, a distribution of incidents or a robust reliability
  requirement, and a comparison with alternatives such as mutual aid.
- **Anything about a real department.** It is a two-row construction, and
  the difference is put in by stipulation.
- **That observation can never tell them apart.** Inventories, training
  records and drills would. What fails is the record of completed
  interventions alone.
