# ARG-tmprfa3j: Kosterlitz's Nobel lecture and Drouin-Touchette's notes, read as sources in place of the unfetched 1973 paper, support the line-of-fixed-points row of the claim that a power law does not by itself indicate criticality, with the fixed line due to Kosterlitz's 1974 renormalization group rather than to the 1973 paper or the Mermin–Wagner theorem

## The inference

[CLAIM-841](../claims.d/CLAIM-841.md)'s XY row holds that the 2D XY low-temperature phase is scale
free over a range of temperature with no tuned direction.
[THEORY-tmpwdka8](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpwdka8.md) and [THEORY-tmpaojie](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpaojie.md), read as sources standing in for
[LIT-2969](../literature.d/LIT-2969.md), would carry it; [THEORY-tmpqma2p](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpqma2p.md) bears on which work the row may
cite.

## Critical questions (source)

**Does the work say this, read in context?** Yes. Drouin-Touchette: the
flows "terminate on a line of fixed points at which y0 = 0", where "the
fugacity becomes irrelevant" (§3.3), for every T below T_KT; spin waves
give η(T) = k_BT/2πJ (§2.2), matching the row's T/2πρ_s with ρ_s the
renormalized stiffness. Kosterlitz: "∆F = ∆E − T∆S = kBT(πK0(T) − 2)
ln(L/a)" (p. 92), so bound vortices are stable through a range of T.

**Is it said within the scope the claim needs?** Two qualifications.
First, the attribution: both readings place the RG and the fixed line in
Kosterlitz (1974), and the lecture says the 1973 self-consistent
treatment's approximation "led to incorrect results" (p. 93); whether the
1973 paper states η(T) is unchecked. Second, the words: Drouin-Touchette
call the phase "critical" ("at all low-temperatures, the system is
critical", §2.2), in the sense of ξ = ∞, not of a tuned transition, so
the usage does not contradict the claim, whose sense of criticality is
the tuned one; and the transition ending the phase is not a power-law
critical point, ξ diverging as an essential singularity (§3.4).

**Is the source in a position to know?** The lecture is the first
author's, 43 years on; the notes are student-level and unrefereed, but
standard. Neither is the 1973 paper. Halperin: the Mermin–Wagner theorem
"sheds no light on the question of whether quasi-long-range order can
exist" (§II), so [LIT-2985](../literature.d/LIT-2985.md) cannot carry this row.
