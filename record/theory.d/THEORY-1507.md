# THEORY-1507: A configuration of finite energy must approach a vacuum at spatial infinity, and the map from the boundary into the vacua falls into homotopy classes that cannot be continuously deformed into one another, so the space of configurations splits into sectors labelled by a topological charge measured at the boundary; the energy, action or tension in a sector is bounded below by that charge and the soliton saturates the bound, and a kink, which interpolates between distinct vacua at its two ends, has its charge fixed by that boundary data

## Source

Tong (2005), [LIT-2207](../literature.d/LIT-2207.md); read in [NOTE-1694](../notes.d/NOTE-1694.md) for the topological charge and the kinks.

## What was actually shown

- **Instantons (§1.1).** The homotopy classification of maps from the
  boundary sphere into the gauge group, the charge as a surface integral
  ("The charge k splits the space of field configurations into different
  sectors"), and the Bogomolny bound S ≥ 8π²|k|/e², saturated by self-dual
  solutions. Derivation, not experiment.
- **Monopoles (§2).** The finite-energy fall-off toward the boundary and
  the bound of the mass by "a surface term which measures a topological
  charge".
- **Vortices (§3.1).** Winding at S1∞ and a tension bound, saturated with
  couplings "tuned to critical values".
- **Tong's kinks: domain walls (§4.2).** Isolated vacua imply a wall interpolating
  between them; its tension is bounded by the difference of boundary
  values; the charge needs "a statement of the vacuum at left and right
  infinity".
- **Annihilation (§3.8.3).** Vortex-antivortex pairs annihilate in string
  reconnection, shown in a figure, in a moduli-space picture; assumed
  there: "expected (at least for suitably slow collisions)".

## What this does not say

- That a topological soliton can end only by annihilation or at a
  boundary. That is the reader's inference from the points above; Tong does
  not say it.
- Anything about the plain φ4 kink, or about saturation of the bound where
  couplings are not tuned: Tong works only BPS solitons of supersymmetric
  gauge theories.
- Anything about what happens when the vacua at infinity are not fixed, as
  on a finite domain; the classification assumes them fixed.
- Anything about dynamics beyond the vortex picture: how fast or under what
  conditions pairs annihilate.

## Note of 2026-11-06: what holds it back

[NOTE-1694](../notes.d/NOTE-1694.md) is Skimmed (four passages of 139 pages), and the title is stated
for any field theory with a kink, while the parts read treat instantons,
monopoles and domain walls in gauge theories and never work through the
plain kink. A full reading of a standard treatment of kinks and topological
charge, or of the rest of these lectures, is what must come first;
[CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md)'s topological route waits on it.
