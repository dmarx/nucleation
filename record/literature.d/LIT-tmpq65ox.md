The record held Arrow only through Sen ([LIT-282](LIT-282.md), [NOTE-263](../notes.d/NOTE-263.md); [LIT-285](LIT-285.md), [THEORY-027](../theory.d/THEORY-027.md)) and O'Donnell's Fourier proof ([LIT-346](LIT-346.md)). Bears on [CLAIM-tmp442x9](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp442x9.md) (aggregation as an obstruction independent of feasibility) and [CLAIM-tmpi5t5f](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpi5t5f.md), which turns on the difference between Arrow's theorem and Sen's liberal paradox.
