# TERM-098: concept, as realized by an organization: a reusable, systematically consequential pattern of distinctions, applicable across a stated range of contexts with inferential or operational consequences; distinct from the feature or representation that carries it, from the organization that realizes it, and from what it is about, and distinct from conceptual corrigibility, the further capacity to find and revise its inadequacies

## Definition

**Conceptual organization**, which this term names, is a pattern of
distinctions an organization draws, together with what follows from
drawing them: the inferences, actions or further distinctions it
licenses. Two marks separate it from a discriminator that merely sorts:

- **Reuse.** The same distinctions serve across a stated range of
  contexts and tasks, not one.
- **Systematic consequence and counterfactual reach.** Drawing them has
  consequences beyond the sorting itself. They apply to cases the
  organization has not met in the way they apply to those it has.

**Conceptual corrigibility** is a further capacity, and not part of the
definition. It is the capacity to find and revise inadequacies in a
concept's application, its inferential role or its specification. The
corrections may come from what the concept is applied to, from proof,
from inconsistency, or from changed inferential commitments. It may be a
developmental achievement of conceptual organization rather than a
precondition of it. An incorrigible concept is still a concept.

"Concept" names a role an organization's distinctions play, not a kind of
substance. A5: "Being an effective constraint is not sufficient for being
conceptual."

## What it is not

- **Not the feature or representation that carries it.** A feature, in the
  sense of [THEORY-965](../theory.d/THEORY-965.md), is a unit a model computes with. A concept may be
  carried by many features, or by none in particular. "A change in the
  local learning coefficient is not itself concept formation" (A4).
- **Not the organization that realizes it.** Two organizations can realize
  one concept, as a communicative kind has many tokens ([TERM-040](TERM-040.md)).
- **Not its object.** A concept is about something it is not. Where it
  partly constitutes its object, as the perceiver's concepts group emotion
  categories ([THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md)) or status functions make a legal person
  ([THEORY-625](../theory.d/THEORY-625.md)), that is the constitutive sense of individuation
  ([TERM-088](TERM-088.md)), and it must be shown case by case.
- **Not a thing or entity.** A thing is an individuated organization. An
  entity (A6) is anything treated as a distinct bearer of properties in a
  domain. A concept can be an entity in a theory without being a thing.
- **Not its corrigibility.** A frozen classifier, an entrenched
  misconception or a rigid ideological category can carry a concept it
  cannot revise.

## What would settle it

Whether reuse and counterfactual reach arrive together, and whether
corrigibility comes with them or separately, in a learning system ([QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md)).
If reuse and reach come apart, the term may need to split.

## Note of 2026-11-07: the owner asks whether concepts individuate, from "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

At U19 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07; https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e) the owner asked that the individuation framework be applied to "symbols and concepts themselves", with "anti-vax" as the example ([CASE-222](../cases.d/CASE-222.md)). That is the first owner turn found that tests this term's clause "Not a thing or entity. A thing is an individuated organization." A did not cite the clause. A41 §6 used "individuated concept" for what this term calls conceptual organization, answering with this term's marks (reuse, systematic inference, correction as a further achievement). A41 §10 found the concept's organizational autonomy "Not established", which is [TERM-088](TERM-088.md)'s fourth sense, and A41 §11 proposed a profile of application, inferential role, response and update to be measured before deciding concept identity. A41 §6's "Individuation of a concept and adequacy of a concept are separate properties" supports the corrigibility clause. The "thing" clause is left open, as [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md). The term is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-11-07: corrigibility kept apart, from the second share of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
