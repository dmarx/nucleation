## 2. Central theses (CLAIMs)

Each entry gives the claim's status and its warrant and role (the warrant is demonstrated, empirical or interpretive), then its `rests_on` premises.

### 2a. Why learning systems have the structure they have (learned-structure)

- **CLAIM-366** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). The common-descent test 0 ∉ conv{g_i} "is invariant under smooth invertible reparameterization, while the sign of a Euclidean gradient inner product is not, and a negative inner product does not exclude common descent; a budgeted version is invariant only with the metric pulled back."
- **CLAIM-629** (Proposed; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-366 and CLAIM-365). Common descent is constant on the orbits of any function-preserving symmetry that acts as a diffeomorphism, such as permutation, ReLU rescaling or orthogonal maps of a residual stream. A Euclidean-budgeted residual is constant on an orbit "only where the symmetry's derivative is orthogonal", so it "changes along rescalings".
- **CLAIM-555** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-347). The compatibility complex of the demands and SLT's singular geometry "are two invariants of one object". The complex is the nerve of the demands' sublevel acceptance sets. "the nerve is coarse, the learning coefficient finer, and over a fixed architecture the complex changes in training only through which configurations are accessible."
- **CLAIM-367** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-152, CLAIM-170, CLAIM-174 and CLAIM-027). "in conventional training most of that governance falls to the optimizer–model–data system, so a condition of training is not thereby a condition of the network."
- **CLAIM-356** (Active; interpretive; thesis). An exogenous objective "can become a goal of the training organization, through its causal role in selecting, stabilizing and reproducing the learned structure it rewards". "what is selected is performance on the updating distribution, not generalization."
- **CLAIM-263** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). Two trained networks are compared by "quotienting the architecture's full symmetry group and measuring what remains: a barrier that survives alignment marks a different mechanism".
- **CLAIM-524** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). "Whether functionally equivalent realizations are connected depends on which paths are admissible". For f = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂, the realizations (1,1,0,0) and (0,0,1,1) are joined by a curve at f = 1, while the straight line between them dips to ½, and "under any resource budget below √2 times the endpoints' cost no path with f = 1 joins them".
- **CLAIM-633** (Proposed; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-524). The two realizations "lie on one orbit of the symmetry that swaps the network's two paths", so "once that symmetry is quotiented the budgeted set is connected".
- **CLAIM-387** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). The optimal deterministic information-bottleneck partitions at two budgets "need not be nested". The optimum is {0,1,2 | 3} for 6.91 < β < 9.26 and {0,1 | 2,3} for 9.26 < β < 16.06.
- **CLAIM-392** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-387). The same holds for the stochastic optima at β = 8 and β = 12: "raising the budget reorganizes the optimum even with noisy encoders."
- **CLAIM-394** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; first line distributed-agency). In a reversible linear Gaussian VAR(1), "every slow-feature direction is a left eigenvector of A, so any span of slow features is an exactly dynamically independent coarse-graining".
- **CLAIM-360** (Active; interpretive; thesis). The deep-versus-shallow results "separate three effects that aggregate loss conflates: a larger realizable function class, a larger set reachable within a fixed data and compute budget, and a different internal organization with the same attainable outcomes".
- **CLAIM-382** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). An autoencoder's learned representation is invariant in law under the orthogonal maps that commute with its activation. An elementwise activation "leaves the standard axes free to be privileged" (SYNTHESIS-030: its group "permits it and does not force it").
- **CLAIM-290** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). A kernel's minimal realizations "are unique up to a unitary". Two representations with the same centred Gram matrix "differ by a translation and an orthogonal map".
- **CLAIM-698** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). The mathematics of modes "applies to how learning systems acquire, execute and compose computations". It "earns its place" only where a description in "independently justified modes and their couplings" out-predicts feature, circuit and parameter accounts.
- **CLAIM-696** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). A mode bears explanatory weight "only if it is defined invariantly under the network's function-preserving symmetries".
- **CLAIM-697** (Proposed; demonstrated; thesis). A disposition is "resource-conditional": its best success probability is "a nondecreasing function of the budget over nested procedure classes". Coverage under repeated sampling is 1 − (1 − p)^N, and "coverage is not delivery". "generation, recognition and selection are separate capacities with separate costs."
- **CLAIM-695** (Proposed; empirical; thesis; rests on CLAIM-697). RL on verifiable rewards can amortize search into the policy "without enlarging what the base reaches at large budgets". Whether a given run sharpened or expanded "is an empirical question per run".
- **CLAIM-699** (Proposed; empirical; thesis; rests on CLAIM-697 and CLAIM-695). "where that verifier runs at test time 'RL buys economy, not reach'" (SYNTHESIS-096). "the substitutability of training and search is fixed by the cost of recognition."
- **CLAIM-754** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-697, CLAIM-569, CLAIM-675 and CLAIM-475). Acquired organization fixes three frontiers that "move separately": the **operational**, the **developmental** and the **inquiry** frontier. Hence "no scalar measure of competence or plasticity ranks learners or learning processes independently of context".
- **CLAIM-758** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-675 and CLAIM-569). **Overconsolidation** is "a generalization of overfitting": continued stabilization "can keep improving its present objective while raising the budget it needs to acquire" a declared later family, "and it is a failure only relative to that family and horizon".
- **CLAIM-757** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-605 and CLAIM-758). If the capacity to acquire decays with disuse, renewed practice restores it "before a threshold point in training and not after". If training uses it up, only renewal such as reinitialization restores it. "the evidence read so far fits the second."
- **CLAIM-620** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-615, CLAIM-336, CLAIM-283, CLAIM-402, CLAIM-330 and CLAIM-535). "Concepts are individuated over time by the transmission criterion that governs organized individuals". The coupling of meaning and will "runs through what is transmitted", "without that persistence showing truth, consensus or autonomy".
- **CLAIM-621** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-280). "symbolic stability can persist while conceptual stability fails, and a durable dispute over a term shows a durable relation among its uses, not one concept." Its own hedge: "Weak: it may be CLAIM-280 restated" (SYNTHESIS-025).

### 2b. What survives translation, retelling and iterated transmission (pragmatic-transport and neighbours)

- **CLAIM-056** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-106 and CLAIM-267). Local transport errors are bounded "by the per-step discrepancies weighted by the sensitivity of the later reconstructions, so the same local error can be damped, accumulated or amplified". "the bound measures departure from a reference transport, not whether the act survives."
- **CLAIM-267** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; formal-apparatus). The general recursion: "closeness at every step can coexist with large end-to-end divergence". "For stochastic kernels in total variation, contraction coefficients do not exceed one, so amplification needs another metric or a nonlinear, adaptive state."
- **CLAIM-081** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-056 and CLAIM-270). "a chain can forget where it started while staying far from the original, so convergence is not preservation and transmission is not degradation." Its body quotes A86: "a message can become more legible while becoming less informative about what originally happened."
- **CLAIM-270** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; formal-apparatus). The general form of CLAIM-081: I(U₀; Uₙ) and D[P(Uₙ), P(U₀)] "answer different questions". "The data-processing inequality bounds the first and says nothing about the second."
- **CLAIM-090** (Proposed; empirical; thesis). "Serial transmission is reconstructive and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases, so a stable endpoint can be far from the ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable." Defeated by a chain "whose stationary output tracks the ancestral input rather than any measured prior of the transmitters". ARG-035 (Proposed) concludes it.
- **CLAIM-066** (**Superseded**, by ARG-035; granted; interpretive). "Kirby, Cornish and Smith show structure emerging under transmission with an experimenter's filter, not filtering by measured inductive biases, and Griffiths and Kalish's convergence to the prior holds only for sampling learners." The content now lives in ARG-035: "the stable endpoint far from the ancestor survives for sampling learners, and whether people learn that way is QUESTION-137" (SYNTHESIS-067).
- **CLAIM-068** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-090). "Repeated reconstruction is attraction toward conventional regions of communicative possibility: pragmatic stances, not only lexicon and grammar, can be stabilized by transmission."
- **CLAIM-059** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-056, CLAIM-267 and CLAIM-068). Chains "fall into different regimes under conditions assigned in advance, contracting toward conventions, accumulating neutrally or amplifying". Amplification "needs a metric other than total variation, an enlarged state or state-dependent dynamics".
- **CLAIM-031** (Active; interpretive; granted). A chain "reorganizes observational constraints rather than only accumulating metric error: a text can change greatly while keeping a few consequential constraints, and one changed constraint can change the act."
- **CLAIM-114** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-056). Drift "can be marked by a pragmatic phase change, the first generation at which a different communicative configuration dominates, which per-step error in surface content does not show".
- **CLAIM-208** (Active; demonstrated; counter). The compositional predictions "are upper bounds whose constants cannot be estimated from serial-reproduction data in a metric fixed beforehand". With true constants the recursion "holds for every chain: it is a theorem, and no data can contradict it" (SYNTHESIS-067).
- **CLAIM-260** (Active; demonstrated; granted). Approximate transports compose to "within Lε₁ + ε₂", and additively only "if the distortion obeys a triangle inequality and later kernels are nonexpansive".
- **CLAIM-107** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). Under Markov kernels a harmonic observable's expectation is invariant, and an approximately harmonic one "drifts by at most Σ ε_i". "on a finite irreducible chain only constants are harmonic."
- **CLAIM-094** (Active; demonstrated; granted). "A harmonic observable conserves only its mean", and mean conservation "does not make an observable a symmetry".
- **CLAIM-635** (Proposed; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-107 and CLAIM-270). An exactly conserved, non-constant observable "keeps information about the start at every generation", so such a chain "cannot converge to a law independent of its start, while a bounded conservation defect does not prevent forgetting".
- **CLAIM-002** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "A text's identity across retellings is a family of relations, namely identity of the artifact, continuity of transmission and similarity of communicative function", which is why a distant retelling can be "recognizably descended from an original it no longer resembles".
- **CLAIM-191** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; line nucleating-argument; rests on CLAIM-177, CLAIM-182, CLAIM-002, CLAIM-270, CLAIM-267 and CLAIM-090). "Restating an argument across memoryless sessions is iterated transport with only a weak channel back to the source: without anchors, restatements drift toward the collaborators' own framings rather than at random, and a code restores continuity of transmission." Its counter is **CLAIM-189** (Proposed; counter): "Fidelity is the wrong measure of a developing argument … what a record can secure is traceability."
- **CLAIM-177** (Active; empirical; thesis; nucleating-argument). In exchanges with memoryless collaborators, "concepts are redefined silently, objections and readings come back as though new, and the thesis changes without anyone arguing for the change."
- **CLAIM-182** (Proposed; empirical; thesis; rests on CLAIM-177). "Storage is not anchoring, addressability is."
- **CLAIM-180** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). "Drift is retrieval failure outside a critical distance". CLAIM-191 says this "by itself has no direction".
- **CLAIM-301** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; complements CLAIM-191). "Iterative retrieval can turn a provisional interpretation into a premise … the question being investigated drifts as well as the queries."

### 2c. Fidelity relative to receivers (the Blackwell and garbling comparisons)

- **CLAIM-106** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "Pragmatic fidelity is a graded, potentially asymmetric property of transport, not an equivalence relation; equivalence is its zero-distortion limit."
- **CLAIM-050** (Active; interpretive; thesis). For report fidelity, "Blackwell's order on rendering procedures, over a designed distribution of situations, makes precise the ceiling a rendering sets on those decisions."
- **CLAIM-225** (Active; demonstrated; counter; rests on CLAIM-270). "Blackwell's order measures what an ideal decision-maker could do, not what a given receiver can reconstruct".
- **CLAIM-199** (Active; interpretive; thesis; lines pragmatic-transport and formal-apparatus). - **CLAIM-326** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-380). D⁺_Q(U → V; π), the positive part of the Bayes-risk increase on the same problem, "is directed, is zero from a garbling back to its source, vanishes for every finite problem and prior exactly when the rendering is at least as informative as the source in Blackwell's order". - **CLAIM-128** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). No single ranking of translations holds across tasks, and "the theory of fidelity fixes no weighting".
- **CLAIM-074** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-118). "fidelity under a fixed interpreter, across interpreters and across states of adaptation are different quantities."
- **CLAIM-049** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-074). "learning can make some relational invariants more stable and others less."
- **CLAIM-223** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-140). "Whether a distinction survived is fixed by what the receivers can recover, and whether it can be shown to have survived is fixed by what the analyst's probes can identify".
- **CLAIM-220** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-061 and CLAIM-050). For a named population and task family, "what survives … is the set of task-relevant communicative distinctions those receivers can still recover from it".
- **CLAIM-061** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "preserving the proposition is not sufficient for preserving the communicative event." Its necessity half was split off as **CLAIM-486** (Proposed).
- **CLAIM-636** (Proposed; filed by derivation at the review of 2026-11-11). Where a literal rendering loses nothing on the declared report problems, a compensating rendering made from the source alone "is never the better report for an ideal receiver" (SYNTHESIS-066).

## 3. Formal results and empirical findings, stated precisely

### 3a. Learning dynamics and learned structure

1. **Invariance of frustration diagnoses.** The test 0 ∉ conv{g_i} is chart-invariant. The sign of ⟨g_i, g_j⟩ is not: the counterexample is (1, 1) and (1, −½) under diag(½, 2) (CLAIM-366, defeated_if). Common descent is constant on the orbits of diffeomorphic symmetries. A Euclidean residual is constant on an orbit only where the symmetry's derivative is orthogonal (CLAIM-629). "Dividing by CLAIM-263's symmetry group before testing for common descent changes nothing" (CLAIM-629 item 3, quoted in SYNTHESIS-026). Symmetries that are not diffeomorphisms, such as blow-ups, are not covered (SYNTHESIS-026).
2. **One checked model of the complex.** In the toy model of superposition, the posterior's 5 → 6 transition "is relief only for common tolerances from 0.0482 to 1/3", and "the complex alone says whether the transition is relief" (CASE-197, Active, construction, in SYNTHESIS-026). The transition "changes no Betti number" (SYNTHESIS-026).
3. **Connectedness relative to the path family.** The results are given under CLAIM-524 and CLAIM-633 above. CASE-273 (Proposed) shows two orthogonal task updates "whose joint effect exceeds the sum of their separate effects", although the cross term "can be small in practice" when models are fine-tuned from one start (SYNTHESIS-028). CLAIM-498 (Proposed) is the record's step from pairs of models to many-model averages. It is measured only for soups of 5 and 10 fine-tunes from one start (Zhou et al. 2024). CLAIM-499 (Proposed), on depth after alignment, is unmeasured (SYNTHESIS-028).
4. **Non-nesting of optimal compressions.** The values are given under CLAIM-387 and CLAIM-392 above. Nesting is guaranteed in three cases: under the β → ∞ optimum (THEORY-743), by a hull test (THEORY-753), and at every budget when Y is a function of X or both are binary (THEORY-757) (QUESTION-291, Answered). In the one case computed, forcing nesting costs "0.49% of the budget" (SYNTHESIS-029). Under QUESTION-220, if optima fail to nest, "an optimal learner's trajectory may merge or reshuffle categories rather than split them, and a coarse-to-fine reading of it is wrong."
5. **A fourth order.** Under squared-error gradient flow in the kernel's moving eigenbasis, ȧ = −Λ_τ a − Ω_τ a. Learning splits into relaxation along the current modes and "the order in which the space of modes is reorganized" (QUESTION-217's note of 2026-11-15, corrected 2026-11-18, in SYNTHESIS-029).
6. **Acquisition.** The partial answers are each "proved or measured in one setting" (SYNTHESIS-034):
   - eigenspace order in the kernel regime (THEORY-086);
   - depth and plateaus in deep linear networks (THEORY-187);
   - the optimizer setting sequential onset in a toy model (THEORY-253);
   - sample-count thresholds (THEORY-283, THEORY-293);
   - the moment a composition becomes available, in small attention-only transformers (THEORY-973);
   - Dong's "single joint step" for conjunction circuits, single-author and unreplicated.

   QUESTION-039 holds evidence both ways on whether acquisition is one kind of event (THEORY-967, THEORY-1353). Under QUESTION-363, SGD at n = 1,000 "ends short of the 6-gon the posterior favours" (THEORY-1275). Dong records learning rate as "a clock, never a temperature" for transformers.
7. **Modes need invariance.** In CASE-270 (Proposed), permuting hidden units leaves the outputs unchanged and moves "the low-frequency share of a smooth update's spectrum … from 0.985 to 0.193", while the singular values stay fixed. A spectrum over unit order therefore "cannot be the reason" FREE-Merging works (SYNTHESIS-097).
8. **Retrieval relative to a query family.** In CASE-271 (Proposed), readdressing, readout suppression and deletion all read zero at the original query, and further queries separate them. Two pathways that cancel at the output are indistinguishable from none "until one is silenced" (SYNTHESIS-096).
9. **Last-layer collapse.** Collapse is measured in one protocol (THEORY-083). It is derived only for squared error (THEORY-278) or as an endpoint (THEORY-286), and is absent under lazy training (THEORY-294). "The derivation for cross-entropy … is still open" (SYNTHESIS-033, QUESTION-051).

### 3b. Transmission

1. **The chain recursion.** e_n ≤ (∏ L_j) e₀ + Σ ε_i ∏_(j>i) L_j (CLAIM-056, CLAIM-267). It has three regimes: contractive, neutral and expansive. In total variation, stochastic kernels cannot expand (CLAIM-267). The bound cannot be falsified with true constants. A serial-reproduction chain gives "one retelling of one input at each node" and so cannot estimate a sensitivity (CLAIM-208).
2. **Composition.** The composite of an ε₁-transport and an ε₂-transport lies within Lε₁ + ε₂. There are counterexamples under squared Hellinger distance with an expansive kernel, and "In total variation both conditions hold" (CLAIM-260). What holds for directed, non-metric distortion is QUESTION-017's open question.
3. **Retention against drift.** In E1's contractive chain (CASE-017, Active, stipulated, executed on hand-chosen matrices), "the separation between chains initialized in different pragmatic states approached zero, while the resulting distribution remained substantially different from the original" (CLAIM-081 body). No run "exercised the amplifying regime" (SYNTHESIS-067).
4. **Conservation.** If K f = f, the mean of f is invariant. If ‖K_i f − f‖∞ ≤ ε_i, the mean drifts by at most Σ ε_i. On a finite irreducible chain only constants are harmonic (CLAIM-107). A non-trivial exactly conserved observable "needs reducible dynamics with several closed classes, a restricted state space, or time-dependent kernels" (CLAIM-107 note, SYNTHESIS-060). CLAIM-635 gives I(S₀; Sₙ) ≥ (E|g(S₀)|)² / (2‖g‖∞²) nats for every n, with g = f − E f(S₀). In contrast, "a two-state chain with defect p forgets its start" (CLAIM-635 summary).
5. **Measured chains.** In CASE-068 (Active, documented), the risk-message diffusion chains kept "the evaluative signal" and it "drifted negative". The "transmitters' own risk perception … predicts the direction of the change", and "re-exposure to the balanced source did not undo the drift". This is "Documented chain evidence for evaluative stance; none for footing." For CLAIM-090 it gives "A clean prior. CLAIM-090 is met only partly". For CLAIM-068 it is a limit: "Every Jagiello and Hills chain started balanced" (SYNTHESIS-067).
6. **Blackwell and the decision term.** The properties of D⁺_Q are given under CLAIM-326 above (and CLAIM-380, for finite outcomes). A86's D_Q "is not Blackwell's order", because it reduces each risk set to one number and "its absolute value counts improvement as distortion" (CLAIM-218, Active, demonstrated). 7. **Kernel freedom.** "Every overlap-consistent target is naturally transported from every source at zero observational distortion, whatever the translation did", by a constant kernel (CLAIM-207). An admissibility condition that the kernel keep distinctions apart excludes the constant kernel (CLAIM-077), but "Separation narrows the freedom and does not remove it" (CLAIM-207).
8. **Order effects.** Under one outcome correspondence, "the target's order effect differs from the transported source order effect by at most 2ε" (CLAIM-231). At poll sizes, "2ε is about as large as the effect it would certify" (CASE-065). Noncommuting operations need not be nonclassical: "AB = A and BA = B, so AB ≠ BA, and every state is an ordinary probability vector" (CASE-001, SYNTHESIS-062).
9. **Probes trained on and certified by the same family.** In CASE-041 (Active, documented), a Projected FastGAN trained against ImageNet features matches StyleGAN2's FID, which is also computed in ImageNet features, "while doing worse in CLIP space and with human judges" (CLAIM-266 summary). In CASE-071 (Active, documented), MBR decoding picks translations that neural metrics rate better, while human MQM raters find them "no better, and for four system and language-pair combinations significantly worse".
10. **What the record does not have.** "No validated measure of footing or stance exists in the record" (CLAIM-227, Active). FactBank reaches κ = 0.82, and interpersonal stance only α = 0.40 to 0.57 (CASE-058). "The behavioural data the record has read show no contextuality proper" (CLAIM-325, Active).

## 6. Digester's notes

*Everything in this section is the digester's reading, not the record's. Codes mark what each step leans on, and none of the syntheses below is a claim the record holds. Where a step goes beyond its sources, it says so.*

### (a) Bearing on a general theory of learning and inquiry for arbitrary systems

1. **A recurring form: structure is relative to an admitted family.** Across both lines, a property first read as belonging to a learned or transmitted thing comes out relative to something declared outside it:
   - a chart or symmetry group (CLAIM-366, CLAIM-629);
   - a path family or budget (CLAIM-524, CLAIM-633);
   - a probe family (CLAIM-142, CLAIM-574);
   - a category or enrichment (CLAIM-590);
   - a budget and reader (CLAIM-697);
   - a task weighting (CLAIM-128, TERM-043);
   - a receiver population and its rule class (CLAIM-199, CLAIM-220);
   - a declared system and horizon (TERM-131, CLAIM-758).

   A general theory could take this as its first axiom: every statement about what a system has learned or kept is indexed to a declared family of admissible operations, and is relative to that family, not false, when a richer family breaks it (CLAIM-574's "relative to its regime, not false"). The record states each instance and never states the schema (section 4).
2. **Drift has a direction, and the direction is the learner's prior.** For iterated learners, CLAIM-090 (Proposed), ARG-035 and THEORY-155 put the attractor at the transmitters' inductive biases, not at the source and not at random. CASE-068 is measured evidence for evaluative stance only. For trained networks, CLAIM-356's "what is selected is performance on the updating distribution" and CLAIM-367's location of governance in the optimizer–model–data system play the analogous role. The "prior" a trained system drifts toward belongs to the training organization. The record makes no such cross-line identification.
3. **Convergence is not preservation, and stability is not truth.** CLAIM-081 and CLAIM-270 (retention against drift), TERM-027 (the attractor as "a shared decoding convention"), CLAIM-621 (symbolic stability without conceptual stability) and CLAIM-620 ("without that persistence showing truth, consensus or autonomy") all say the same thing at different scales. For a theory of inquiry: a community or record converging on a formulation is evidence of a shared prior, and of fidelity only if something else shows it.
4. **What anchors.** The record offers four candidate anchors:
   - exact conservation, which provably keeps information about the start (CLAIM-635), while approximate conservation does not;
   - evidence that does not pass through the interpretation it tests (CLAIM-342, CLAIM-402);
   - a correspondence anchored to independently measured distinctions and evaluated on held-out tasks (CLAIM-011, CLAIM-149, CLAIM-266);
   - continuity of transmission by address rather than by resemblance (CLAIM-002, CLAIM-182, CLAIM-191).

   SYNTHESIS-066 declined to file these as "one requirement" because "what the independent measure is in each" is a free parameter. A general theory might keep them as four mechanisms of one function: lowering the weight of the learner's own prior in what is retained.
5. **New distinctions: refinement versus reorganization.** CLAIM-387 and CLAIM-392 show that a larger budget can reorganize an optimal partition rather than refine it, and QUESTION-217's fourth order separates relaxation within modes from reorganization of modes. CLAIM-031 and CLAIM-114 say the same of chains: one changed constraint or a "phase change" changes the act, and per-step error does not show it. CLAIM-574 adds that along nested probe families distinctions "only grow". So new distinctions can appear without reorganization only where the family is nested. Where the optimum or the family is not nested, gaining capacity can merge or reshuffle categories (QUESTION-220). This bears on the manuscript's title, "from obstruction to reorganization". It is the digester's link, and no entry states it.
6. **Learning changes what can still be learned.** TERM-131, CLAIM-754, CLAIM-758 and CLAIM-757 make the developmental frontier a separate object from present competence. On the transmission side, CLAIM-049 says learning makes "some relational invariants more stable and others less". Read together (the digester's reading), consolidation is the receiver-side counterpart of attraction to a convention. It stabilizes some distinctions at the cost of the capacity to draw others.

### (b) Operationalizing this in a record restated by memoryless collaborators and revised by decisions

1. **The record is a chain.** The record already holds this premise: CLAIM-191 (Proposed) reads restatement across sessions as iterated transport "with only a weak channel back to the source", and CLAIM-177 (Active) is the observed loss of identity. The transport results then predict three things, all untested in this setting:
   - drift toward the assistant's stock framings (CLAIM-090, CLAIM-191);
   - convergence that is not preservation (CLAIM-081);
   - per-step errors whose effect depends on the chain's later sensitivity (CLAIM-056).
2. **The record's codes are a conservation device.** A code carried forward unchanged is, in CLAIM-635's terms, an exactly conserved, non-constant observable. It therefore guarantees that information about the origin survives at every generation, which, by the same claim, "a bounded defect does not prevent forgetting". This is the digester's reading of CLAIM-635 applied to CLAIM-182 and CLAIM-191. A practical consequence: a claim's title and `defeated_if` matter less than its stable address and its history field, which carry lineage (TERM-084), not similarity.
3. **Do not certify with the training probes.** Under CLAIM-266 and CLAIM-149, a synthesis review that checks an account against the same components and wording that produced it is evaluation under the training probes. A held-out check would use probes not used in drafting: a different reader, the owner's own wording, or a later reading the account did not cite. The record's habit of having one session file and another review is a partial instance. Whether it is independent enough is CLAIM-085's worry about readers "from the same assistant family" (cited in CLAIM-191).
4. **Measure retention and drift separately.** CLAIM-270's two profiles suggest two audits per thesis:
   - **retention:** can a reader recover the original thesis from the current entry and its history;
   - **drift:** how far the current wording has moved toward the assistant's default framings.

   CLAIM-189's counter is a constraint here. A developing argument should drift, and the record secures "traceability, change that is announced and located". On that reading, schema-revising ADRs are announced drift, the record's analogue of a do(S) intervention rather than a do(I) one (CLAIM-130). Silent redefinition (CLAIM-177) is unannounced drift.
5. **Schema revisions as changes of probe family or category.** A decision that adds a field or vocabulary term enlarges the probe family. By CLAIM-574, distinctions drawn before it should survive. A decision that merges or replaces a vocabulary is a change of category (CLAIM-590) and can merge configurations that were apart. A postmortem could ask of each schema ADR which of the two it was. That test is the digester's proposal.
6. **Watch for phase changes, not gradual distance.** By CLAIM-114 and CLAIM-031, the event to detect is the first session at which a different configuration dominates, such as a thesis's role or warrant silently changing. Accumulated lexical distance is not that event. The record's `history` notes already mark announced versions, so an unannounced change of role, warrant or `rests_on` across restatements is the observable that matters.
7. **Self-confirming loops in curation.** CLAIM-402's condition applies to the record's own categories. A topic, line or term the assistant files under, and then finds well supported because later filings use it, has predictive success that passes "through the category's own use". SYNTHESIS-030's suggestion that the learned-structure line may be several inquiries, from link counts rather than from the line label, is an instance of checking a category by evidence outside its use.
