# LIT-tmpre45d: A One-Year Longitudinal Investigation of the Association between Self and Other-Oriented Future Concern

K. F. Law, L. Young and S. Syropoulos (2026). PsyArXiv preprint. DOI-10.31234/osf.io/e4y58

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* By the abstract: random-intercept cross-lagged panel models
over four waves (baseline, 3, 6 and 12 months) of Intergenerational
Concern, Future Self-Continuity and Consideration of Future Consequences.
"People with greater other-oriented Intergenerational Concern later
reported greater self-oriented Future Self-Continuity, while the reverse
pathway was not supported." Unverified until read.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-22 by the LEAD-062 search. It is the only work found
that orders continuity and concern in time. It measures continuity with
one's own future self, not with future people or a legacy, and does not
separate caring; so it is not the lead's test. Its reported direction,
concern before continuity, would count against reading concern for future
people as prudence on a self extended in time (QUESTION-301), and fits
LIT-tmpa7f10 only if that work's correlations run the same way.
