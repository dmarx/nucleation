  second is [CLAIM-011](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-011.md)'s remedy. The two are joined in the manuscript or not at
  all. That is the owner's call, so neither claim is changed here.

  test** is [CLAIM-126](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-126.md) as filed.

  asks for: [CLAIM-072](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-072.md) (Saussure), [CLAIM-094](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-094.md) (Baez–Fong), [CLAIM-066](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-066.md)

## What is not filed, and why

- **The principle "preserve the type of epistemic relationship".** Not filed
  as a DP. The review presents it as extending a principle, "Conceptual
  Revision Requires Formal Reconciliation", but the record holds no such
  principle. A DP is added on the second re-derivation of the same reasoning,
  and this is the first. Most of the six types already exist as relations or
  statuses ([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)):
  - revision is a version or `supersedes`;
  - refutation is `objects_to` or `undercuts`, then Rejected;
  - derivation is `rests_on`, or an ARG.

  The other three have no type of their own:
  - **Qualification** is written in prose. [CLAIM-125](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-125.md) supersedes [CLAIM-100](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-100.md)
    "narrowed", and [CLAIM-011](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) objects to [CLAIM-117](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-117.md) "as stated, not to its
    truth".
  - **Operationalization** and **empirical test** have no relation at all.

  [ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md) rejected "borrowing a full argumentation ontology". Adding
  relations for these three would be a new decision, and it belongs to the
  owner.
- **The automated check the review proposes already runs, untyped.** Luria
  warns on every citation of a document that is not in force, a `rests_on`
  into a Rejected claim included. The warning is silenced only by an
  `inactive-ok` annotation that states a reason. It is a warning and not an
  error, and it does not tell a premise from a passing mention. The review's
  second check would catch a term whose type changes while equations still
  use it the old way. Nothing does that, because the record holds no
  equations as structured objects.
- **The second-order research question.** The review asks whether the
  preservation and validity of scientific claims can be formalized as their
  conceptual dependencies change. It is not filed. It would need a line of
  its own, and `lines` is closed by decision. It is also a question about the
  record, not about pragmatic transport.
