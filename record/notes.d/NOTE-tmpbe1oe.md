# NOTE-tmpbe1oe: Bonica, "The Mothership Vortex" (Democratic spam PACs)

## Contribution

The author traces the high-pressure Democratic text and email fundraising ecosystem to one firm, Mothership Strategies, founded in 2014 by former DCCC digital staff. He describes it as sitting at the centre of an interlocking set of PACs (Progressive Turnout Project, Stop Republicans, End Citizens United, and others), some co-founded by its principals. Following the disbursements in FEC data, he argues that most of the money goes to consulting, texting vendors, payroll and further fundraising, and very little reaches campaigns. He calls on the party to cut ties with such vendors and to set efficiency standards.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- Origins, as the author tells them: Greg Berlin and Charles Starnes left the DCCC and "privatized" a "churn and burn" fundraising playbook. Berlin and Starnes co-founded End Citizens United in 2015, and it became a major client.
- The money figures he reports: $678M raised from individuals; $159M paid to Mothership, out of $282M in total Mothership revenue; $22.5M to the texting vendor Message Digital LLC; about $150M consulting/fundraising and $70M payroll; $19M to Shawmut Services for canvassing (Progressive Turnout Project).
- The headline number: at most $11M reached candidates, campaigns or national party committees, "a fundraising efficiency rate of just 1.6 percent", and about half of that went to the DNC, DCCC and DSCC.
- The author says that none of this appears to be illegal, and that party establishment clients (House Majority PAC, Jaime Harrison) also hire the firm.

## Open questions

- The "efficiency" denominator and numerator depend on how disbursements are classified. Before any number is cited, the replication code should be checked, especially whether independent expenditures, canvassing and advocacy count as "reaching campaigns".
- It is an investigation published as a newsletter post, not peer-reviewed, and is attributed to its author. The named firms' responses are not included.
- It has a data-analysis angle (FEC entity resolution), but no ML connection.
