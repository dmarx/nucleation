# THEORY-1600: Sandoval-Obando and colleagues report that in 430 Chilean teachers a 14-item Spanish Loyola Generativity Scale has three factors, a three-item Generative Legacy factor on symbolic continuity standing apart from Generative Action (r = .45), from an analysis they call confirmatory that chooses the number of factors from the data

## The account

Within one generativity scale, wanting to leave something that lasts and
doing generative things come apart as related but distinct factors:
Generative Legacy "represented motivations related to symbolic
permanence and societal contribution" (p. 8), and correlates .448 with
Generative Action.

## Evidence

- 430 elementary teachers in four Chilean regions, stratified then
  voluntary (pp. 3, 5–6).
- Three factors: 55.1% of variance, RMSEA = 0.043, CFI = 0.989; four fit
  slightly better (RMSEA = 0.032) and were judged over-extracted (pp.
  6–8).
- Action with Legacy .448 (95% CI .370–.538); Inefficacy with Action
  −.340 and with Legacy −.295 (p. 8). Reliability of factor scores .795
  to .863 (p. 9).
- [NOTE-1260](../notes.d/NOTE-1260.md) finds the number of factors chosen from scree, eigenvalues
  and the Hull method, which is exploratory, and the stated MSA rule
  inconsistent with the items removed.

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-301](../questions.d/QUESTION-301.md)'s note of 2026-10-29: "A recent validation separates a
three-item "Legacy" factor from generative action (Sandoval-Obando et
al., [NOTE-1260](../notes.d/NOTE-1260.md)), but its items are not printed." The question's first
answer criterion asks for a measure of felt continuity with one's legacy
that could be set against caring. This is a candidate such measure,
unrun as a test, and whether its items concern outliving oneself or the
future's good is what decides between the thesis's reading and
Scheffler's.
