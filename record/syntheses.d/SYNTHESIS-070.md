# SYNTHESIS-070: What the pragmatic-transport line adds is placed, after conceded prior art, in a testable account of which communicative distinctions a translation keeps for stated receivers and tasks; the central defeat test as first stated could not tell that account from rivals that hold the pragmatic point and let predictor and criterion share a method, so the theses were restated against one discriminating study that has not been run, and a win there over a static pragmatic profile is now held not to tell the line from functional and relevance-theoretic accounts, which leaves a strong contextual baseline and prediction under an unobserved composition as the harder tests

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part on what the line
adds to the accounts it must be told apart from, the defeat conditions of
its central theses, and the discriminating studies filed to test them. The
line's account is built over it and over the accounts of the line's other
parts. The mechanical map put forty entries in this part. They fall into
three inquiries that barely relate: this one (24 entries, 64 linked
pairs among them), language models as a test bed (8 entries with [QUESTION-024](../questions.d/QUESTION-024.md), moved
here from the contextuality part; 13 pairs) and composition in
conditional generation (9 entries, 14 pairs), with four pairs across the
three. Each has its own account; this is the first. The
links to the other two are given below.*

## The account

- **The contribution, after the concessions.** [CLAIM-023](../claims.d/CLAIM-023.md) (A35, A38 and
  A40 of the exchange, sharpened against Gutt and Krifka; disclaimed,
  Active): the contribution lies "not in discovering that translation is
  pragmatic or that speech acts change contexts, but in giving a
  specified, experimentally assessable account of which communicative
  transitions a translation preserves". Three prior-art concessions narrow
  the claim to that residue. [CLAIM-019](../claims.d/CLAIM-019.md) (A102's, granted, Active):
  Lévi-Straussian transformational analysis "is direct prior art for
  structural transport, not only a philosophical precursor", though read,
  the software that models it "encodes and runs an analyst's
  transformations; it does not find or test them". [CLAIM-088](../claims.d/CLAIM-088.md) (A86's,
  granted, Active): task-sensitive preservation is established in semantic
  and goal-oriented communication, and with the prior art read "What the
  manuscript can still claim is the context-indexed, sheaf-structured
  observation, not task-relative preservation." [CLAIM-146](../claims.d/CLAIM-146.md) (the owner's
  objection at U58, conceded by A210; granted, Active): recovering latent
  relational structure from an interaction matrix "is decades-old prior
  art", and "What the manuscript can add lies past that recovery, in the
  claims [CLAIM-023](../claims.d/CLAIM-023.md) already names". [CASE-042](../cases.d/CASE-042.md) (proposed by the owner at U57
  as a laboratory and built by A207; a documented specimen, Active) is the
  prior art: collaborative filtering and topic models recover structure
  "up to gauge, or uniquely under anchor conditions", and it shows "that
  recovery is possible and limited, not that a recovered component is a
  communicative kind".
- **The distinctive thesis and its rival.** [CLAIM-202](../claims.d/CLAIM-202.md) (the record's reply
  of 2026-10-10; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) states [CLAIM-023](../claims.d/CLAIM-023.md)'s account
  "as something a study could find false": the context-indexed profile,
  its response to later framings and its decision relevance predict
  receivers' recovery beyond a static pragmatic profile. [CLAIM-205](../claims.d/CLAIM-205.md) (the
  interlocutor's reading, relayed by the owner on 2026-10-12; counter,
  Active since Nida, Nord and Gutt were read) denies the premise that made
  the static profile the rival: these accounts "need not fix one context
  or a static stance profile of the rendering". [ARG-031](../arguments.d/ARG-031.md) (the inference
  from a win over the static profile in [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) to what the line adds;
  Rejected) is undercut by it. [CLAIM-504](../claims.d/CLAIM-504.md) (the interlocutor's proposal,
  split from [CLAIM-205](../claims.d/CLAIM-205.md) by the twenty-first status review; Proposed): the
  rivals' strongest operationalization is "a rich contextual baseline of
  context-response profiles and supervised prediction", though "No
  argument in the record shows it to be the strongest operationalization
  the rivals license, rather than one strong one." [QUESTION-074](../questions.d/QUESTION-074.md) (the
  interlocutor's reading; Open) names the test the reading takes to be
  distinctive: whether transport structure fitted to single transformations
  predicts receivers' recovery under a composition no data were fitted on,
  within the conditions under which approximate transports compose.
- **The central thesis, restated.** [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md) (the record's reply of
  2026-10-10, adopted in place of [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) on 2026-10-29; thesis,
  Proposed): for a named population of receivers and a stated family of
  tasks, what survives is what those receivers can still recover, and
  measured preservation of the pragmatic observables predicts that
  recovery better than wording or proposition, with or without its force.
  It "does not say the line's transport structure is needed": "[CLAIM-202](../claims.d/CLAIM-202.md)
  carries that claim, with its own test." [CLAIM-252](../claims.d/CLAIM-252.md) (the record's reply;
  disclaimed, Active) sets the account's scope: it "says how to tell which
  ones a rendering kept for stated tasks and receivers", and what ought to
  be kept "is set by the purpose the translation serves". [CLAIM-243](../claims.d/CLAIM-243.md) (the
  record's audit of 2026-10-10, structure lens; counter, Active) is the
  objection both answer: read as description the manuscript's thesis is
  "close to true by definition", read as a criterion it "is a schema until
  the decision family Q is fixed". [CLAIM-078](../claims.d/CLAIM-078.md) (A110 §X, recovered; thesis,
  Proposed) is the older success criterion, against text similarity and
  along reconstruction chains; its note of version 3: "Meeting this
  criterion does not meet [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md)'s."
- **The central defeat test and why it failed.** [CASE-184](../cases.d/CASE-184.md) (filed by the
  [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration; stipulated, Active): four thesis-level defeat
  conditions "state one test", judged pragmatic observables against a
  text-similarity score as predictors of human fidelity judgements.
  [ARG-026](../arguments.d/ARG-026.md) (the inference from that test to the theses; Rejected) fails on
  two critical questions. [CLAIM-233](../claims.d/CLAIM-233.md) (the record's audit, structure and
  empirical lenses; granted, Active): the conditions pit the theses "only
  against embedding, propositional or surface similarity", and Nida's
  dynamic equivalence and a proposition with its force "also predict that
  judged pragmatic observables beat text similarity". [CLAIM-501](../claims.d/CLAIM-501.md) (the
  record's own extrapolation, split from [CLAIM-233](../claims.d/CLAIM-233.md) and promoted once Gutt's
  thesis was read; thesis, Active) adds Gutt: "Judged stance and footing
  are nearer to what the account preserves than embedding similarity is."
  [CLAIM-232](../claims.d/CLAIM-232.md) (the record's audit, empirical lens; counter, Active): two human
  ratings of one rendering share "rater construal, scale use and a general
  impression, which a machine similarity score shares with neither".
- **The discriminating studies.** [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) (the record's design of
  2026-10-10; stipulated, Active) is the repair: renderings built to
  dissociate wording, proposition, static stance and transport structure,
  a behavioural criterion, "All human-rated predictors are rated by raters
  other than those who supply the criterion", named populations and an
  interlingual arm. "It has not been run, and its margins are not yet
  fixed." [CASE-185](../cases.d/CASE-185.md) (from A4 to A6 of the exchange "Paper assessment";
  stipulated, Active) is [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) "with a third language and a second
  route", adding path discrepancy, controls and a correspondence arm; "Not
  run; every margin is left to a preregistration."
- **Topic is not act.** [CLAIM-129](../claims.d/CLAIM-129.md) (A210 and A214, recurring from U56 to
  U60; thesis, Proposed): "A factorization recovers structure visible
  through that family, not automatically every relation constituting the
  phenomenon", so "A communicative kind ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)) must be tested against
  probe families chosen for their bearing on the act". [CASE-040](../cases.d/CASE-040.md) (A214 §8,
  named by A218 as the first experiment; stipulated, Active) crosses topic
  with act. [ARG-011](../arguments.d/ARG-011.md) (the inference from that design to [CLAIM-129](../claims.d/CLAIM-129.md); Rejected
  for the design as filed): "a pass shows that the items were built as
  intended"; [CLAIM-129](../claims.d/CLAIM-129.md)'s version 2 adds naturally occurring items in reply.

What stays open is everything empirical: no study in this part has been
run, every margin is the owner's to fix in a preregistration, and whether
the transport structure adds anything over a strong contextual baseline,
or under an unobserved composition ([QUESTION-074](../questions.d/QUESTION-074.md)), has no result.
