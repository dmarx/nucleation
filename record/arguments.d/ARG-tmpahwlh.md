# ARG-tmpahwlh: Coupled interest model → support seeds lasting interest

## The inference

Premise ([CASE-tmpaqihm](../cases.d/CASE-tmpaqihm.md)): ċ = αv(1 − c) − μc, v̇ = βc(1 − v) − νv + s(t),
with the origin unstable when αβ > μν. Conclusion ([CLAIM-tmp1njmb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1njmb.md)): an
activity first taken up for other reasons or under outside support comes
to be maintained without the stimulus that began it.

## Critical questions

1. **Does the model satisfy the conditions the conclusion needs?** The
   conclusion has two parts: that a self-maintained state exists, and
   that support can bring it about where it would not otherwise arise.
   The model meets the first when αβ > μν. It cannot meet the second: by
   [CLAIM-tmpyk6bi](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpyk6bi.md), the outcome after the support ends is fixed by αβ
   against μν whatever the support was.
2. **Do the model's terms mean what the conclusion takes them to?** s(t)
   is read as mentorship or encouragement. In the model it shifts timing
   only, so "temporary external support" is not doing the work support
   does in the transient-support line, where support must carry the
   party past a threshold ([CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md)).
3. **Is there a variant that would?** Yes: a coupling weak at low
   competence gives bistability and a support threshold ([CLAIM-tmpyk6bi](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpyk6bi.md)
   step 4). The conclusion would then follow in the model, still only as
   a possibility, with the empirical question of which coupling holds.

Verdict: Rejected as support for the transient-support clause of
[CLAIM-tmp1njmb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1njmb.md). The model supports the coupling clause, that reciprocal
growth of competence and valuation can sustain an interest.
