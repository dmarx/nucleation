# LIT-tmpmu3q0: Organisational closure in biological organisms

Mossio, M., Moreno, A. (2010). Organisational closure in biological
organisms. *History and Philosophy of the Life Sciences*, 32(2-3), 269–288.
HAL halshs-00792436 (authors' final version).

## Key takeaways

- "whereas all organisms are a kind of FFE self-maintaining systems, not all
  FFE self-maintaining systems are organisms" (pdf p. 2); a flame is "a
  physical example of a self-maintaining system" (pdf p. 5).
- "dissipative structures realize a minimal form of self-maintenance, in the
  sense of depending on the work of a single constraint acting on its own
  boundary conditions"; "The mutual dependence between a set of constraints
  is what we call an organisational closure" (pdf p. 7).
- Closure "underdetermines, in itself, the whole set of requirements" of an
  organism (pdf p. 13).
