# QUESTION-006: Which pragmatic observables are conserved, exactly or within a bounded defect, by actual reconstruction kernels, and how does that depend on frame, genre and adaptation?

## Why it is a question

A110 §IX.3: "Given a reconstruction kernel K, determine which observables satisfy
Kf = f and which satisfy a bounded defect condition ‖Kf − f‖ ≤ ε. Investigate how
these quantities depend on interpretive frames, genres, and model adaptation."
The argument's §7 had boxed the general form: "Which observables are invariant
under which communicative transformations?", with the answer depending on the
class: literal paraphrase, cross-cultural translation and text-to-image admit
different transformations (A106).

## Where it stands

The manuscript §4 gives the conservation criterion ([CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md)) and asks "at what
reconstruction stage does the relation characterizing an affiliative act cease to
be approximately invariant?" The dependence on frame, genre and adaptation is not
asked. It joins this question to [CLAIM-074](../claims.d/CLAIM-074.md) and [CLAIM-049](../claims.d/CLAIM-049.md).
