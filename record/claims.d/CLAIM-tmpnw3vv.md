# CLAIM-tmpnw3vv: The strongest operationalization of the functional and relevance-theoretic rivals to the transport structure is a rich contextual baseline of context-response profiles and supervised prediction

## The claim

The interlocutor's reading of 2026-10-12, as [CLAIM-205](CLAIM-205.md) quotes it,
proposes a fourth row for [CLAIM-202](CLAIM-202.md)'s comparison, between the static
pragmatic profile and the transport model: a "strong contextual baseline"
of "rich context-response profiles, supervised predictive methods". "What
matters is whether transport structure adds predictive or explanatory
power over the strong contextual baseline, not merely over static
pragmatic judgments."

[CLAIM-205](CLAIM-205.md), on which this rests, holds that the functional and
relevance-theoretic accounts, as Nida, Nord and Gutt state them, need not
fix one context or a static stance profile. This claim adds that the
strongest form of those rivals, for the purpose of the comparison, is
such a baseline.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the transport structure will fail against the baseline.
- It does not specify the baseline beyond the reading's table.

## Note of 2026-10-30: what holds it back

The baseline is not specified beyond the reading's table. No argument in
the record shows it to be the strongest operationalization the rivals
license, rather than one strong one.
