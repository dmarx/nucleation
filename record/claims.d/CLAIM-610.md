# CLAIM-610: If the record's hypotheses held, agency and autonomy would be further organizations of the same individuated unit: the relations deciding which of an individual's interventions become effective would belong to that individual under the criterion that individuates it, and the governance of governability would act on those relations; persistence is transmission, and none of the achievements implies the next

## The claim

A7's thesis: "**An individual is a historically realized organization of
mutually constraining processes that achieves a degree of causal and
dynamical coherence, acquires a distinguishable repertoire of possible
transformations, and persists through the ongoing production,
reproduction, or regeneration of the constraints that make that
repertoire possible.**" And: "Agency arises where such an organization can
intervene in its circumstances. More sophisticated agency arises where it
organizes which interventions become effective. Autonomy arises where it
contributes to maintaining and revising the conditions of its own
efficacy."

The supposition is the record's own hypotheses taken as demonstrated. The
architecture, layer by layer:

1. **Individuality.** Relational but not arbitrary: "These criteria are
   not arbitrary once specified." This is [CLAIM-405](CLAIM-405.md) (Active, A's wording on
   the owner's U8 question), with the senses of [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md) (Active).
   Detecting, maintaining and constituting a boundary are kept apart, as in
   [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md) (Open), which A7 cites rightly.
2. **Persistence.** Transmission, not resemblance: "It would depend on how
   organizational capacities and constraints are causally transmitted,
   reproduced, or regenerated across time." This is [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) (Proposed,
   A's) and [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md) (Active). Formation and maintenance provenance come
   apart ([CLAIM-522](CLAIM-522.md), Active, demonstrated), and present coupling can stay
   strong as maintenance fails ([CLAIM-521](CLAIM-521.md), Active, demonstrated, cited
   rightly). These illustrate the layer and are not premises.
3. **Agency.** Will-organization, "maintained relations governing which
   available processes are accessible, which have priority, which exercise
   authority": [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) and [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md) (Active, record), with [CLAIM-030](CLAIM-030.md) for
   collective intention.
4. **Autonomy.** "**governance of one's own governability**": [CLAIM-545](CLAIM-545.md)
   (Proposed, A's phrase, resting on the owner's [CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md) and [CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md)),
   with [CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md)'s separability of regime, governance and transformation,
   and the readiness results ([CLAIM-567](CLAIM-567.md), [CLAIM-602](CLAIM-602.md), Active).

The order is [CLAIM-474](CLAIM-474.md)'s (Active), a partial order in which "none implies
the next". What this claim adds is the relation between the levels. Each
level is an organization of the conditions under which the one below is
effective. Agency organizes which of an individual's interventions take
effect, and autonomy governs the conditions of that organizing. A7's
emergency-response pair shows the levels parting: [CLAIM-414](CLAIM-414.md), [CLAIM-558](CLAIM-558.md) and
[CLAIM-602](CLAIM-602.md). The cross-realizer reach ("a single explanatory framework that
applies, with different realizations") is [CLAIM-487](CLAIM-487.md) (Proposed), which this
claim complements and does not rest on.

## Corrections to A's version

- **Correction: the stack is a partial order.** A7's "Agency arises where
  … More sophisticated agency arises … Autonomy arises" reads as a ladder.
  [CLAIM-474](CLAIM-474.md), which A7 draws on through A5, says "a later one need not
  require every earlier one". The title keeps "none implied by the one
  before" and the defeater allows a level without the one below.
- **Correction: "nucleation" is not the thesis term.** A7 §1: "*Nucleation*
  would name the process by which a relatively small configuration of
  relationships becomes productive of a larger, sustained organization."
  Nucleation is the record's name, not a term of its argument. The claim
  does not use the word.

A7's own qualifications are kept. "the description of an *individual*
must not make self-maintenance a universal requirement", and its §8:
"Consciousness would not follow from organizational perspective or
agency"; "Organizational success would not imply constituent welfare";
"There would be no single universal test for individuality."

## What it does not say

- **That the hypotheses hold.** It is supposed, and its role says so.
- **That relations constitute the individual.** A7 §8: "The stronger
  constitutive interpretation would require an argument showing why
  relations partly constitute the individual". [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md) stays open.
- **That a self is a fifth level.** A7 names "being a self" among the
  accomplishments, but the self-governing sense is [CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md) and [CLAIM-424](CLAIM-424.md),
  and the record's owner has recast selves as organizational perspectives
  ([CLAIM-417](CLAIM-417.md)). The claim leaves the self out.

## Later versions

The exchange's proposed theses ([CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) and its successors) are not
versions of this one. They integrate different components, and they put
the account forward instead of supposing it.

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test, the integration must be able to fail while every component holds. Two of the three links between levels cannot: "persistence is the transmission of what produces the individual" is [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md), and "autonomy governs the conditions of agency's efficacy" is [CLAIM-545](CLAIM-545.md) by definition. The third can. [CLAIM-405](CLAIM-405.md) lets differently fixed criteria pick out units whose boundaries cross. A firm's precedence and jurisdiction relations, which [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md) says the firm has, decide which of a worker's interventions become effective, and they belong to a unit whose boundary crosses the worker's under every criterion that picks out the worker. Every component holds, and agency is not a further organization of the individual whose interventions it orders. The claim is admitted on that link alone, and the title says so.
