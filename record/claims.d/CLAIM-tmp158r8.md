# CLAIM-tmp158r8: A meme can act above the content of beliefs, on how interpretations are evaluated or on how the evaluating organization is revised, so that what it changes is the conditions under which later memes are assessed and inadequacies become recognizable

## The claim

A4 §5: a meme "can reorganize the space of available possibilities by
changing what an organization" recognizes as a distinction, considers a
legitimate inference, treats as a relevant question, permits as an
action, and "Accepts as grounds for correction". "The meme can
participate in restructuring the machinery by which beliefs are produced,
assessed, retained and revised. This is a second-order memetic effect: the
meme changes the conditions under which subsequent memes are evaluated."
Its example: "a scientific methodological principle is consequential not
simply because researchers believe it, but because it changes what they
subsequently regard as evidence, explanation and adequate justification."

A6 §8 gives the levels, in its notation P₀, interpretation of evidence;
P₁, evaluation of interpretations; P₂, revision of evaluative
organization; and (its \(P_0\) and so on written here as P₀): "A
particularly consequential meme might not transmit a false proposition at
P₀. It might participate in establishing constraints at P₁ or P₂ that
prevent some inadequacies from becoming recognizable."

## Where the record stands

- **Concept as a pattern of distinctions** with consequences, kept apart
  from "conceptual corrigibility, the further capacity to find and revise
  its inadequacies": TERM-098 (Proposed). A4 cites it accurately.
- **Jurisdiction over contexts of interpretation**: CLAIM-627 (Proposed),
  that who governs which contexts count acts "on the conditions of
  interpretation and not only on its outputs". A4 cites it accurately.
  This claim puts a transmitted item where CLAIM-627 puts a
  will-organization's jurisdiction.
- **The pathologies at P₁ and P₂** are held: CLAIM-619 (answering a
  credible challenge by reducing the capacity for future challenge),
  CLAIM-614 (reproducing the projections that make a harm non-legible),
  CLAIM-624 (a second-order duty of answerability) and CLAIM-tmpq1213 (the
  wrong in standing). What is new here is that a meme can be the vehicle.

## Gap

The levels are not defined apart from content. "Correlation is not
causation" (A6 §2) is a proposition at P₀ and a rule of admission at P₁.
The claim needs the levels told apart by what the item does (whether it
changes how later items are taken up) and not by its wording; the
defeater says so.
