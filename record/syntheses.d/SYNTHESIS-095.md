# SYNTHESIS-095: The dependence-and-domination line is two accounts that meet where the controllers of an arrangement are named as those who owe: what wholes and parts owe and claim, and what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe; the line makes no claim beyond its parts, and its parts relate through one complement

*The account of the dependence-and-domination line, "Wholes, parts and dependence", split from organizational-normativity ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)) at the owner's approval of 2026-11-15 ("yes on the two line split"). It is a line account over the accounts of its two parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)), which were filed as parts of organizational-normativity at that line's synthesis review the same day. "Organization" is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default, and the bearer of an obligation is kept apart from its ground; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none.*

## Where they meet

Each link below is a front-matter relation of the entry named first,
unless a body is cited.

- **The threshold and what is owed before it.** [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) (fourth part)
  complements [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) (third). Its body: who owes is "those who
  control" the arrangement, "the agents [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)'s second condition
  names", and its summary calls it a scaling rule for what is owed before
  [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)'s threshold.
- **Ground and bearer, in bodies.** [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md)'s summary keeps "the parties
  who are owed, and the bearers of what is owed" apart "as [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)
  keeps them".
- **Who owes.** Every component of either part that names a bearer names
  an agent or a role: [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md) "a responsible agent", [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) the
  agent with discretion over the terms, [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) "an agent or role",
  [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) and [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) "those who control" the arrangement, [CASE-256](../cases.d/CASE-256.md)'s
  third result "agents or roles maintaining the arrangement". This is a
  map of the components as filed, as [SYNTHESIS-092](SYNTHESIS-092.md) says, not a thesis.

account, version 4, storing front-matter relations as `line_parts.py`
does: 29 within the third part, 2 within the fourth, and one between

together by its source, one exchange, more than by its relations, and is
a part of this line, not a line of its own ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)).

## Not stated on the record

- How [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s rule, that accountability rises with how indispensable
  an arrangement has become, relates to [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md)'s, that the magnitude of
  a dependence sets only how much rides on it and control over the terms
  sorts it. Neither cites the other.
- How [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) and [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) relate ([SYNTHESIS-092](SYNTHESIS-092.md)).
- Whether [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty of answerability, in organizational-normativity,
  falls on an arrangement's controllers, and so whether [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md)'s erosion
  of governing capacity breaches it ([SYNTHESIS-093](SYNTHESIS-093.md)).
- That the two parts are one inquiry beyond the bearer they name in common.
- Each part account lists the links its components leave unstated.

- That any whole, in any sense of [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md), is a bearer of obligations:
  [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)'s prior part is open.
