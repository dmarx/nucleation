# THEORY-877: Learning a generative model of sensory data extracts real patterns in Dennett's sense, so the manifest image is a structural description of the sensory signal, constrained by its external source but silent on what realizes the structure; two subjects whose sensory streams match, one envatted, therefore have equally veridical manifest images, and what differs between them, the realizers, lies outside what perception can represent

## Source

Gładziejewski (2025), *Synthese* 206:225, [LIT-1360](../literature.d/LIT-1360.md), read in full in
[NOTE-1056](../notes.d/NOTE-1056.md): §2 (pp. 4–10), §3 (pp. 11–15) and footnotes 2, 4, 5, 6 and
9.

## What was actually shown

- **Compression and inference (§2).** With optimal codes, maximising the
  posterior is minimising −log P(M) − log P(D|M), the total description
  length, so "Bayesian inference is real-pattern extraction" (p. 8). This
  is a derivation (footnote 3), re-checked in the NOTE. Free energy's
  complexity and accuracy terms are "analogous" to MDL's model and data
  terms (p. 10). That is an analogy.
- **Structure (§3).** Generative models "specify probabilistic and causal
  structural descriptions of sensory patterns" (p. 12). They are not freely
  constructed, for two reasons: compressibility is a mathematical fact,
  and the signal is shaped by its external source "at least unless the
  model is used to run fully off-line simulations, as may be the case in
  imagery or dreams" (p. 13).
- **Veridicalism (p. 13).** Matched sensory streams are "necessarily ...
  equally compressible by equivalent higher-level (manifest) structural
  descriptions". So the envatted twin's door is "as real as the door you
  open". This step is valid given its premise, since equal inputs have
  equal compressions.
- **What lies outside (p. 14).** What realizes the structural roles, purely
  intrinsic properties, and deeper structure not induced from the senses.

## What this does not say

- **That manifest content is only structural.** The veridicalism needs it,
  and the paper assumes it. A qualitative content that matched streams do
  not fix would break the step.
- **That every manifest property is descriptive.** Footnote 9 allows a
  continuum to "projections", with cuteness as the example.
- **That the brain uses optimal codes**, which footnote 4 leaves open, or
  that predictive processing is literally true, which footnote 2 assumes.
- **Anything about experience as such.** The manifest image is made of the
  "entities that we represent in our conscious experience and/or in our
  intuitive theories" (p. 3). Nothing is said about what makes a
  representation conscious.
- **That the world behind the senses is unknowable.** Deeper structure is
  noumenal only "in the limited sense" that it is not induced from the
  senses (p. 14). Science may reach it.

  this account the envatted door is grounded as a structural role,
  constrained by an independent source, and ungrounded as to its
  realizer. That is one way to state the claim's point that the envatted
  experience is ungrounded "with respect to the tree" while grounded in
  the apparatus. It supports the claim's "Ungrounded ≠ Unconstrained": the

  character is manifest content and not structure, the veridicalism has a
  counterexample.
