# SYNTHESIS-tmprxn12: Relative to a stated target, a combined form is proposed to be one system while it is a gap-isolated block whose gap beats the cost of naming it, a test that passes one-way drive, reads peers or hierarchy from the direction of coupling, cannot tell a produced boundary from an imposed one, and has an unanswered necessary half because a closed collective mode can exist with no block; drive between levels is apportioned by a profile that changes over time; a factor is proposed to be an agent when it is one system, is better described as pursuing goals than as a device and would adapt its policy, and a willed agent when a will-organization over several regimes describes it better still; and in the self-governing sense what endogenous governance adds is the capacity to revise its own standards, which a population optimizing objectives the modeller fixed does not show

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the part on when a
combined form is one system, where its boundary lies, when a structure is
worth naming as a unit, how drive is apportioned in it, and what makes a
factor an agent, with the self-governing sense of "self" that the agent
tiers end in. The line's account is built over it and its six sibling
parts (conferral, normative-institutional, individuation, frustration,
persistence, collective-stance). Five components are first in another
line and are integrated here because their `line` includes
distributed-agency: CLAIM-159 (will-organization, where SYNTHESIS-036 sets
it out), CLAIM-356 (learned-structure, also SYNTHESIS-026 and
SYNTHESIS-041), CLAIM-367 (learned-structure, SYNTHESIS-026), CLAIM-409
(formal-apparatus) and CASE-024 (organizational-affect, SYNTHESIS-018).
Eleven more also carry formal-apparatus. The part was found by a mechanical
community map; its 81 components are linked by 260 pairs of relations or
mentions among themselves.*

## The account

- **The question.** QUESTION-035 (Open; raised by the dancing couple, Otto's
  notebook and the firm's all-hands on 2026-10-11, and coded at its third
  appearance) asks, relative to a stated
  target, "when is a combined form one system, where does its boundary
  lie, and how long does it last?" It "sharpens QUESTION-009" to the prior
  question of system-hood and keeps agency and selfhood apart. Its
  adequacy conditions: "the couple after the song must come out two", and
  one-way drive "may come out one system with a hierarchical
  architecture" (version 2 reversed version 1's requirement, at
  CLAIM-167).
- **The criterion.** CLAIM-157 (the record's proposed answer, interpretive,
  Proposed) joins three dual-filed results. "Simon's near-decomposability
  says what to compare: interaction inside a candidate block against
  interaction across its edge. The gap theorem says the unit is the block,
  and the Occam factor says when the gap is big enough to name it.
  Persistence is how long the block keeps clearing the threshold." It
  answers the owner's "privileged nomenclature" (CLAIM-175) "with a test".
  - CLAIM-154 (dual-filed from MOM-CLM-160, demonstrated, Active) is the
    threshold: by the Savage–Dickey ratio, keep a candidate when
    d'² exceeds the Occam term, and "The threshold is not a taste
    parameter." Version 3 narrows it, conceding CLAIM-188, to candidates
    whose model "stays regular at the parameters that switch it off".
  - CLAIM-188 (the record's, from a synthesis review, counter,
    demonstrated, Active): where that model is singular, "near the
    threshold the rule prunes candidates the Bayes factor keeps", checked
    on CASE-152's product model.
  - CLAIM-164 (dual-filed from MOM-CLM-144, demonstrated, Active) is the
    unit: "The unit is the block or cluster that a gap sets off, not any
    one member or coordinate." Its body: "In the dancing couple that is
    the pair's joint mode, not either dancer's own. That application is
    this record's."
  - CLAIM-152 (dual-filed from MOM-POS-001, demonstrated, Active) is "the
    standing caution on every boundary criterion filed beside it". It
    "does not say boundaries are arbitrary, only that each is relative to
    a stated target."
  - TERM-049 (Active), "combined form", is "The record's neutral word for
    what such a whole is, used before asking whether it is an agent or a
    self"; it *is* one system while it clears CLAIM-157's test, and its
    architecture and drive profile "are further facts about it, not
    conditions on it".
- **What holds the criterion back.** CLAIM-157's note of 2026-10-29: its
  `defeated_if` "makes clearing the threshold necessary for one system,
  not only sufficient, and that half is unanswered". Version 9 concedes
  CLAIM-194 (individuation): the test "names a unit of description, and it
  cannot tell a boundary the form produces from one imposed on it". Its
  duration clause "has no window or tolerance", and holds only if
  "persists" is read on the trajectory (NOTE-1215).
- **Entrainment and one-way drive.** CLAIM-172 (the record's reading at
  the owner's prompt, interpretive, Active): mutual entrainment
  "individuates a new system by a variable that only the pair has", and
  "Mutual entrainment is sufficient for a combined form, not necessary,
  and it says which kind." Its version 5 sharpening, from THEORY-265:
  "shared timing alone is not the mark, synchrony matched to the strong
  couplings is." CLAIM-173 (the record's anticipated objection, counter,
  Rejected) said a metronome and a musician, or a nerve and a muscle,
  clear CLAIM-157's threshold "without being one system". CLAIM-167 (the
  owner's challenge, "and why shouldn't they?", with three reasons set out
  by the assistant, interpretive, Proposed) rejected it: "the direction of
  coupling gives the system's internal architecture, not its membership".
  Its note of 2026-10-30: "Its defeat condition is met by its own example
  or cannot be met."
- **Selfhood kept apart.** CLAIM-175 (the owner's reply, interpretive,

- **Closure without a block.** QUESTION-218 (Open; A5's Q2) asks whether
  a closed collective mode that is not a weakly coupled block can be one
  system. CLAIM-388 (from A5, made exact by the record, demonstrated,
  Active) shows the two come apart: under uniform coupling "the sum of all

  reading, demonstrated, Active) gives the aggregate's one-step closure

  column sums". QUESTION-219 (Open; A3 and A5) asks whether flux picks out
  units neither test finds. QUESTION-222 (Open; A3 and A5) asks about
  memory: CLAIM-389 (demonstrated, Active) "memory is the round trip Y →

- **Agents.** QUESTION-009 (Deferred) asks "When does a pattern of
