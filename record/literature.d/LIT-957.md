# LIT-957: Relative fluency (unfelt vs felt) in active inference

Denis Brouillet and Karl Friston (2023), *Consciousness and Cognition* 115: 103579.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-785](../notes.d/NOTE-785.md).*

- **Relative, not absolute, fluency drives judgement.** After Whittlesea,
  what matters is ease against expectation. In the hension effect, new
  pronounceable non-words draw more false recognitions than real words
  that are read faster.
- **Misattribution.** A discrepancy in fluency is surprising, its source
  is not introspectable, and it is attributed to the most salient
  candidate: the stimulus, a past study list, a typing error.
- **Fluency as precision.** The proposal is that fluency is the
  recognition of the precision of one's predictions, which active
  inference equates with attention.
- **Unfelt and felt.** Unfelt fluency is a "non-conscient" recognition of
  a change in attentional set, likened to exogenous attention. Felt
  fluency is a "hierarchically deeper representation", made opaque by
  mental action, that is, endogenous attention.
- **Cause and consequence.** Felt fluency sets priors on lower-level
  precision and is revised by a "prediction error of prediction errors".
- **No model, no new data.** The formal claims lean on cited simulations
  of attention and meta-awareness; the experiments are reviewed briefly,
  many from the first author's laboratory.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), as a
separation of felt from unfelt fluency and "a candidate mechanism for
what feeling adds to a functional state". Read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-785](../notes.d/NOTE-785.md), which does not bear that out. Fluency here is a
metacognitive feeling of ease, not affect, and "felt" means made
reportable by endogenous attention: a higher functional position, not
something added to function. The paper is at most one more instance of
the identity side of [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), alongside Veit ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)), and its
felt states are about the brain's own processing rather than the body,
which sets it beside, not against, [THEORY-045](../theory.d/THEORY-045.md). No THEORY was filed: its
own contribution is a proposal without a model, and its empirical base
is second-hand.
