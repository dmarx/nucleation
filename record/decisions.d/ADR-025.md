# ADR-025: Six topics for gaps the agency and self batches found

## Context

Four batches in one session asked the vocabulary for words it did not
have:

- psychopathology ([ADR-021](ADR-021.md));
- self-determination theory ([ADR-023](ADR-023.md));
- agency and self ([ADR-024](ADR-024.md));
- the readings that followed them.

The filing agents reported the same gaps independently, and each time the
work went under the nearest word. Learning in organisms went under
`learning-theory`, whose blurb is the machine-learning sense. Meaning in
life went under `social-science`. The demarcation of life went under
`natural-sciences` and `philosophy-of-science`. The owner asked that the
vocabulary be updated without hesitation.

[ADR-021](ADR-021.md) and [ADR-023](ADR-023.md) said each gap should get its word "when a second work
needs it". Each of these six now has several works that need it.

## Decision

Add six words:

- **`well-being`**: how well a life goes for the one living it, as
  psychology measures it. SDT defines its needs by their effect on it.
  Sheldon & Elliot and Schlegel et al. take it as their outcome. McAdams
  ties narrative to it.
- **`learning-and-conditioning`**: learning in organisms from contingencies
  and consequences. It covers Pavlovian and instrumental conditioning,
  reward learning in brains, habit and goal-directed control, behaviour
  analysis, and relational frame theory's relational responding. The blurb
  says it is not machine learning.
- **`psychometrics`**: measuring minds. It covers scales and their
  validity, latent-variable and network models (Borsboom), and model-based
  estimation of cognitive parameters (HDDM).
- **`philosophy-of-biology`** (philosophy group): what life, organisms and
  functions are. It covers organisational closure, biological autonomy and
  the demarcation question in [NOTE-094](../notes.d/NOTE-094.md).
- **`embodied-cognition`** (philosophy group): enactive, embodied,
  embedded and extended cognition, coordination dynamics, and situated
  robotics.
- **`free-will`** (philosophy group): free will and volition as philosophy
  and as science, from compatibilism and manipulation to the readiness
  potential.

The words were applied to existing documents in the same contribution.

## Alternatives considered

- **Broaden existing words.** `learning-theory` would then mean two things,
  machine generalization and animal conditioning, and a reader of one would
  wade through the other. `cognition` is already the largest word in the
  philosophy group.
- **One decision per word.** Each would repeat the same context. They were
  found together and are recorded together.
- **Not yet added.** These gaps were also reported:
  - social cognition and intersubjectivity: one work so far, De Jaegher &
    Di Paolo;
  - cognitive-modelling methods: covered here by `psychometrics` and
    `probabilistic-modeling`;
  - language as behaviour: covered by `learning-and-conditioning` for
    relational frame theory.
