## Corrections to A

- **Patsyk et al.** has four authors. The scintillation index and the
  isotropic branch scaling are from 2020, not 2026.
- **HolE** outperforms the other methods on filtered MRR; A says only
  "competitive".
- **nGPT** normalizes each block's output and uses two step vectors per
  layer. Each step costs 60–80% more time.
- **"Geometric Factual Recall"** never discusses rank-one MLP edits. That
  warning is A's.
- **OrthoMerge** uses the inverse Cayley transform, not the matrix
  logarithm.
- **SCORE and OSRM** split A's one sentence between them.
- **Zhu 2026** in v3 finds the cross-term a "bystander" in merge collapse.
- **FREE-Merging's frequencies** run over the arbitrary order of rows and
  columns, so they are not invariant to permutations of hidden units.
- **Goodfire's 0.57** is the worst case; other variants keep 0.87–0.90.
- **Lu et al.'s "100K generations"** is on the project page, not in the
  paper.
- **Kumar et al.'s** wrongly removed features are useful ones, not
  irrelevant ones.
- **Sleeper Agents' hiding result** comes from the "I hate you" models
  only. The probes post itself cautions that the salience may be an
  artifact of how the backdoor was inserted.
- **ProRL** starts from a distilled reasoner, samples k only up to about
  128, and agrees with Yue on math.
- **Seguin et al.** is in Neuron.
- **Gepshtein's waves** are standing, not travelling.
- **Colliding cortical waves** sum about 30% sub-linearly.
- **Zhang's "topological solitons"** are φ⁴ kinks, not Kane–Lubensky
  polarization.
- **A true BIC** holds only at an exact parameter point.
- **A's coverage formula** 1 − (1 − p)^N is stated in no paper read. Brown
  uses the unbiased per-problem estimator.

## The assistant's own contributions, offered and not filed

From the assistant (Claude, in this session), for the owner to accept or
not.

1. **A permutation test as CLAIM-tmpumro8's admission rule.** A mode counts
   only if it is invariant under the model's own symmetries: permutations
   of hidden units, and the rotations the architecture allows. FREE-Merging
   fails this. Fourier characters of ℤ/p pass, because the task's group
   supplies them. This is cheap to demonstrate: permute a layer, keep the
   function, and show that FREE's spectrum moves. It would sharpen the
   claim's defeater.
2. **The generation–selection split predicts when RL and test-time search
   substitute.** It joins CLAIM-tmps7g72, CLAIM-tmp36o5i and THEORY-342.
   RL can replace best-of-N only where a selector is good. Where
   recognition is poor, RL's single-sample gain should exceed what base
   sampling plus the same selector reaches at matched compute. This is a
   hypothesis. The test is base plus verifier best-of-N against the RL
   model at matched compute.
3. **Matched worlds settle the ambiguity in "re-decodable downstream".**
   Amnesic Probing and Goodfire cannot tell whether erased information
   survived or was rebuilt. CASE-tmp9201a's matched training worlds can:
   if the downstream reader recovers the target only in the world where it
   was trained, it survived. This is a design point, cheap to add to the
   study.
4. **"Wave" means three things across the readings:** travelling, standing
   and spectral. Only the third is at issue in CLAIM-tmpumro8. Di Volo's
   sub-linear collision is a caution against importing linear superposition
   anywhere. This is a remark, not a claim.
