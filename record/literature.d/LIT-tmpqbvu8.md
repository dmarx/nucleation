  ontological levelism (levels in the system itself) and keeps an
  epistemological one, the method of levels of abstraction. An observable
  is an interpreted typed variable; an LoA is a finite non-empty set of
  observables; a moderated LoA adds a behaviour predicate; a gradient of
  abstractions (GoA) is a family of moderated LoAs with mutually inverse
  relations Rᵢ,ⱼ under which pⱼ ⇒ P_Rᵢ,ⱼ(pᵢ). Fixing the LoA fixes which

  LoA) and tokens (the model). The method is shown on Kant's antinomies,
  and set apart from Marr's, Pylyshyn's and Dennett's levels (which, it

  realism. It proves nothing about GoAs; its value is the vocabulary.

# LIT-tmpqbvu8: The Method of Levels of Abstraction

Luciano Floridi (2008), *Minds and Machines 18(3), 303–329 (online 15 August 2008; September 2008 issue)* — DOI-10.1007/s11023-008-9113-7

## Key takeaways

- The paper keeps levels as an epistemological method and drops them as
  ontology. "Nature does not know about LoAs either" (p. 35).
- The method's work is done by definitions: observable, LoA, behaviour,
  moderated LoA and gradient of abstractions (§2). An LoA fixes the range of
  questions that can be meaningfully asked and answered in principle
  (p. 18). Adopting an LoA commits a theory to types, and endorsing the
  model it generates commits it to tokens (pp. 20–21).
- Levels of organisation are ontological and levels of explanation are
  pragmatic. LoAs underlie both, and the three are not to be confused
  (§4.1). On this account Marr's, Pylyshyn's and Dennett's tripartite
  schemes are GoAs that leave their ontological commitment hidden (p. 28).
- Explicit LoAs are offered as giving pluralism without relativism (§4.3)
  and realism without descriptivism (§4.4). Both are stated more than
  argued.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-10 at the owner's request, with the line of Floridi's work
on artificial agency that Floridi 2025 ([LIT-tmp6juhh](LIT-tmp6juhh.md)) builds on. This is the
method that line uses. Floridi says he and Sanders "were forced to develop
the method of abstraction when we encountered the problem of defining the
nature of agents (natural, human and artificial)" in Floridi & Sanders 2004
([LIT-tmpdg6qq](LIT-tmpdg6qq.md)) (p. 35). This paper states the method in general form and
refines it, for instance by relating values of types as well as observables
(fn. 8). That is the `extends`. The earlier and longer joint statement,
Floridi & Sanders 2004, "The Method of Abstraction" (Yearbook of the
Artificial, Peter Lang), is not held here.

It is held here and not in the anthology because it is philosophical
method, with no instruction for machine-learning practice. The anthology
held none of this batch's works when checked at commit d8b5ba5.
