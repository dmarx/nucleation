# LEAD-244: Find a field case of hyperstition with a withdrawal: a representation whose circulation is withdrawn, or stops, after what it describes has formed, with a record of whether the realization persisted

## The gap

[CLAIM-1339](../claims.d/CLAIM-1339.md) (A10) holds that what a representation helps bring about
can come to be maintained by processes of its own, "told apart by
withdrawing the circulation once the realization has formed".
[CLAIM-1305](../claims.d/CLAIM-1305.md) (Active, demonstrated) gives the model's condition: the
realization persists only if it has "a self-maintaining loop of its own
strong enough to have a second stable state" and crossed its threshold
while the representation still circulated, and "a single withdrawal at
an unknown stage does not discriminate". Article 7's table marks
hyperstition's condition and outlasting "done, for the model", and leaves
open "Measurement; a field case with withdrawal". Krohn and Weninger
([THEORY-2070](../theory.d/THEORY-2070.md)) "report all of it as correlation, not cause", and
[CLAIM-426](../claims.d/CLAIM-426.md)'s note of 2026-11-19 says its links are proposals or jokes, not
judgements.

## What it would take

1. **A case with a withdrawal.** A representation that circulated
   (a forecast, rating, rumour or projection) and whose circulation
   stopped for a reason outside what it described: a retraction, a
   platform removal, a discontinued index, a ban. Natural experiments are
   the likeliest source, in forecasting, credit ratings, bank-run
   rumours or online communities ([LEAD-003](LEAD-003.md)'s Reddit data could hold
   removals).
2. **Two withdrawals, or a known stage.** Because one withdrawal at an
   unknown stage does not discriminate ([CLAIM-1305](../claims.d/CLAIM-1305.md)), the case needs
   withdrawals at more than one stage, across comparable instances, or an
   independent record of whether the realization had its own
   maintenance (step 3) at the time.
3. **The self-maintaining loop, measured apart.** Evidence of what
   maintains the realization other than the representation's
   circulation (investment made, ties formed, rules adopted), recorded
   before the outcome after withdrawal is read.
4. **Prediction fixed first.** Realizations with their own maintenance
   past threshold persist after withdrawal; those without decay at their
   own rate. A threshold-contagion account ([LEAD-264](LEAD-264.md)) predicts
   persistence too, so the case must also say which.

## What would count

Persistence after withdrawal only where an independent loop was present
favours [CLAIM-1339](../claims.d/CLAIM-1339.md) as conditioned by [CLAIM-1305](../claims.d/CLAIM-1305.md); persistence
without one, or decay with one, counts against the model's reading.
A case where the representation's circulation is shown to have helped
bring about its object at all would also narrow [CLAIM-426](../claims.d/CLAIM-426.md)'s open item,
"which judgements' circulation helps make them true".

## Standing

Open, at stage idea. Checked first: no held lead seeks such a case;
[LEAD-003](LEAD-003.md) studies subreddit formation and does not test withdrawal, and
[LEAD-260](LEAD-260.md)'s seed removal concerns memetic nucleation, not a
representation's realization.
