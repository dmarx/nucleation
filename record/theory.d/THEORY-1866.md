# THEORY-1866: Susen reads Rosa as holding that the good life is measured not by resources or options but by the quality of a person's relation to the world, resonance, a responsive relation in which subject and world affect and transform each other and which cannot be produced at will, against alienation

## The account

Susen reads Rosa as defining resonance as "a form of world-relation, in which subject and world meet and transform each other", which "cannot be brought about at will", and as holding that "access to resources is a necessary but not sufficient condition for human fulfilment".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1937](../notes.d/NOTE-1937.md).

## What it does not say

Anything about dependence, domination or institutions.
