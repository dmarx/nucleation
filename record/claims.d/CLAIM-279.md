# CLAIM-279: Whether an intervention stabilizes or destabilizes a pattern is relative to a policy and a frame, so denoiser, critic and re-noiser are regimes an operator falls into, not kinds of operator

## The claim

The exchange began with "a population of denoisers" (U1). The owner, at U3:
"maybe rather than strictly 'denoisers', we should think of these as more
generalized operators. for example, maybe rather than 'denoising', a given
agent will actually want to *re*noise the region it has influence over e.g.
because it disagrees with the direction of the current work." A3 §3 took
re-noising as an operation in its own right: "**meaningful re-noising is not
necessarily randomizing**. It may be highly targeted and structured."

A4 §7 listed "specialized transformative processes: stabilization,
disruption, translation, criticism, synthesis, and action." The owner, at
U5: "our process model is sufficiently general and abstract that we don't
need to specify these, they are just different regimes in the dynamics of
our general process. actually, thinking more about this though, I imagine
niche specialization effects would emerge". A5 §2 granted it: "An operator
is not *essentially* a critic, stabilizer, translator, or re-noiser. Those
are behavioral regimes". Its mechanism is comparative advantage, which is
recognized and endorsed, and the endorsement brings more exposure and more
competence.

A8 §2 grounded the relativity in the owner's r/place case ([CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md)).
"For the first group, changing an opposing pixel back to its intended color
is denoising ... For the second group, making the same modification in the
opposite direction is also denoising relative to *its* preferred pattern.
And a third group might deliberately introduce apparently random pixels to
disrupt both." So: "the classification of an intervention as denoising,
re-noising, stabilizing, or destructive is **relative to a policy and a
representational frame**."

The structure is [CLAIM-152](CLAIM-152.md)'s. An individuation built on sufficiency is
relative to its target. A classification of acts as signal-restoring or
noise-adding is relative to the pattern taken as signal.

## What it does not say

- It does not say niches always emerge. The owner's own condition is "if
  permitted to evolve appropriately". A7's H1 and A10's Generation A propose
  the test, and none has been run.
- It does not say the frame is arbitrary. On r/place, which pattern a group
  defends is fixed by its plan.
- It does not say dissent improves an organization. A3 §9: "A re-noising
  operator may be locally successful but globally harmful, or locally
  disruptive but globally beneficial." Exploitation crowding out exploration
  is March's reading ([THEORY-142](../theory.d/THEORY-142.md)). Correctability of a whole's course is
  [QUESTION-041](../questions.d/QUESTION-041.md).
