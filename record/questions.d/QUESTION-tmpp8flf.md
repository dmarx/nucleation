# QUESTION-tmpp8flf: What makes an organizational contract persist as the same contract through renegotiation, a change of the processes party to it and a substitution of how it is implemented, and is it that its terms and its responses to deviation survive when the processes are replaced?

## The gap

An organizational contract (TERM-tmplpplx) is a relation among processes.
Two of the record's tests tell it apart from a mere regularity:

- CLAIM-tmp7hqtl's four marks, all necessary;
- the assistant's proposed fifth condition there: change the terms and
  keep the processes, and see whether behaviour follows the terms.

Neither says when it is the same contract after its parties or its
implementation change. A49 names the question and gives no answer.

## A candidate answer, and how it would be tested

The converse of the fifth condition: replace the processes and keep the
terms, then check whether the relation's responses to deviation survive.
CLAIM-tmpersgx's substitute-provider probe is the nearest held test. It
asks whether reliance concerns the function or its provider, not whether
the contract survives the provider's replacement.

An answer is defeated by a contract the parties treat as the same through a
full replacement of its processes while its responses to deviation change,
or by one whose responses survive replacement and which they treat as new.
