# CLAIM-tmpswafr: Maintaining a regime of effective action and maintaining the capacity to govern among regimes are not the same achievement

## The claim

"An agent may succeed at the first while progressively losing the second. This
possibility becomes visible only once operative will is distinguished from the
organization governing practical efficacy."

## What it does not say

What ought to persist ([CLAIM-tmpk5av7](CLAIM-tmpk5av7.md)), or that the agent owns its
will-organization ([CLAIM-tmp64xks](CLAIM-tmp64xks.md)).
