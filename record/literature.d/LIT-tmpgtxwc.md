# LIT-tmpgtxwc: The Black Queen Hypothesis: Evolution of Dependencies through Adaptive Gene Loss

Morris, J. J., Lenski, R. E. & Zinser, E. R. (2012). The Black Queen Hypothesis: Evolution of Dependencies through Adaptive Gene Loss. *mBio* 3(2): e00036-12. doi:10.1128/mBio.00036-12.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A1 and A2 cite it as the case where dependency arises from selection for individual gene loss, not from selection for the collective; A2 rates it "Critical". One correction from the abstract: the hypothesis is stated for free-living community members, and it attributes the reduction common in endosymbionts to drift. The exchange applies it to symbiosis generally. A reading should say whether the paper extends it to endosymbionts.
