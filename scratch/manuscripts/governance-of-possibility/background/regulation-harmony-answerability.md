## 2. Regulation and the demand specification (§2, §2.1)

- **CLAIM-tmpz67ew** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; the owner's, U16; lines distributed-agency, realizability; not a member of the work). "regulations as constraints which operationalize a demand specification to optimally steer harmonious governance across an industrial ecosystem". Its glosses: a demand is "an acceptance set on the histories the ecosystem can realize, as in CLAIM-347"; a regulation is "the enforceable conduct constraint that is meant to bring it about"; harmonious governance is "a plurality whose constituents' demands are jointly satisfiable (CLAIM-347, CLAIM-554), with coexistence not taken for cooperation (CLAIM-546), and viability not taken for legitimacy (CLAIM-075)". The industrial ecosystem is read as governed "from outside by a regulator who is not one of the constituents whose demands are specified". The assistant's section gives two readings of "optimally": "**Relative to a declared objective**" and "**As maximality**" ("undominated in the profile A33 §8 proposes"). "What it does not say": "It does not say who sets the demand specification, or what makes it legitimate. A33 §7 calls that the hardest problem." Its objection is CLAIM-tmphjm0q.
- **CLAIM-tmphjm0q** (Proposed; interpretive; counter; objects to CLAIM-tmpz67ew; rests on CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-tmpx9nrj; member, §6, §9.5, §14). "There is no optimal regulation until the demand specification, the instruments, the horizon and the trade-offs among demands are declared, and a regime is better judged by a profile of possibility, realization, resilience, accountability and cost than by a scalar". A33 §8: "There is no generally justified conversion from, say, a reduction in ecological risk to a particular quantity of industrial profit". The assistant's note: two regimes whose complexes each have a face the other lacks "are unordered under inclusion". "It does not say regulation cannot be optimized." CLAIM-tmpz67ew's own defeater says this objection "qualifies 'optimally' and leaves the rest standing".
- **CLAIM-tmplgnjf** (**Active** since 2026-11-23; interpretive; thesis; refines CLAIM-tmpz67ew; complements CLAIM-357; bears on QUESTION-tmpn9t5a; not a member). "A demand specification is not a regulation: regulations are constraints on conduct meant to induce constraints on outcomes, and a regime succeeds only if its conduct constraints make the demanded outcomes reliable under the participants' responses, so a rule can be perfectly enforced and fail its purpose". A31 §3: c_k(x, a) ≤ 0 on conduct and ∫ f_j dμ_g ≤ b_j on outcomes; "The first specifies what participants must do. The second specifies what the ecosystem must achieve." Reliability: P_g(Γ_H ∈ V_j) ≥ 1 − ε_j. "This makes regulation a problem of steering the distribution of possible futures, rather than specifying one ideal future." On instrument choice: "The choice depends on observability, enforceability, and uncertainty." It denies that enforcement is code validation: "Legal interpretation, discretion, political legitimacy, and contested interests cannot be collapsed into automated consistency checking."
- **CLAIM-tmpk4bou** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-582, CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-361; member, §2). "A regulation that only adds hard constraints to a fixed dynamical model can only shrink the compatibility complex of the public demands, so it cannot relieve their frustration; a regulation relieves frustration among the original demands only by changing the dynamics, through the technologies, infrastructure or access it brings about".
- **CLAIM-582** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; member, §2, §9.3). "Under a fixed dynamical model an added hard constraint cannot enlarge the set of admissible trajectories, so it cannot enlarge what some admissible path reaches or lower the least cost of reaching it; what it can improve is a property required of every path".
- **CLAIM-tmpx9nrj** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-347; member, §2.1). "Possibility and realization are separate dimensions of a regulatory regime: its compatibility complex says which sets of public demands some attainable history meets, the probability of meeting them under a model of participants' behaviour says how reliably the regime selects such a history, and a regime can improve either without the other". CLAIM-tmptfpf7's body: A34 §6 adds a third intervention, "change the test functions by which a history is judged acceptable", recorded as a dated note on this claim.
- **CLAIM-tmptfpf7** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-tmpk4bou, CLAIM-tmpx9nrj and CLAIM-410; complements CLAIM-025; member, §1, §13). A34: "Regulation governs possibility by determining which actions, relationships, capabilities, and forms of coexistence are admissible, attainable, sustainable, and revisable within an organized system." Regulation "is *generative* when it enables forms of organization previously unattainable; *stabilizing* when it preserves valuable capabilities under disturbance; and *reflexive* when its own specifications and mechanisms remain subject to effective correction." "There is no choice between governed and ungoverned possibility—only between different organizations of possibility." Its three levels (conduct, capabilities, governability) are "the regulatory case of CLAIM-025's three achievements". Its admission: in an ecosystem where regimes only select among already attainable histories, "regulation is never generative"; "What the claim adds is that real regimes are not of this kind".
- **CLAIM-410** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-086, CLAIM-057 and CLAIM-282). "Removing a constraint on an organization's members does not move it along one scale of more or less governance: it changes a profile of who holds governing positions, among them rule-making, monitoring, enforcement, appeal and revision".
- **CLAIM-347** (Active; interpretive; thesis). Frustration "is an ordinal fact before it is a quantity": the jointly satisfiable sets form "a downward-closed complex whose minimal non-faces are the minimal conflicts", and relief is "strict enlargement of that complex".
- **CLAIM-361** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-284 and CLAIM-287; grounds CASE-115; member, §9.6). Relief "must be judged for an identified organization against a fixed reference set of its original demands": "a demand excised or suppressed has changed the comparison and has not been satisfied". A37: "If the system simply deletes a difficult demand, it has changed the comparison problem rather than shown an expansion of its ability to realize the original demands." A43's four outcomes: achievement, reprioritization, constitutive transformation, suppression; "Only achievement is relief."
- **CLAIM-560** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-586 and CLAIM-571). The compatibility and reachability filtrations "both grow with the budget and neither determines the other". The curation entry: §2.1 credits it with the separation of existence, reachability and reliable reaching, "which separates only the first two (CLAIM-589 and CLAIM-tmpx9nrj hold reliability)".
- **CLAIM-725** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "A demand's satisfaction condition does not carry its warrant: representing a requirement by the histories that satisfy it fixes what meets it, not its source, who bears it, to whom it is addressed or why it binds, so that the joint satisfiability of demands cannot certify that any deserves fulfilment".
- **CLAIM-738** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "Rights, demands and commitments are distinct and none establishes another".

## 3. Diagnosis: detection, authority, affordability, reproducibility (§10)

- **CLAIM-523** (Active; interpretive; thesis; uses TERM-064, TERM-023 and TERM-085; member of this work and of capability-restraint-relational-freedom; cited by A48 nowhere, stated in §10 and §13). Three achievements, "any can fail while the others hold": "**Legibility.** The discrepancy becomes recognizable"; "**Volitional efficacy.** A corresponding demand can influence action"; "**Correctability.** A fitting revision can be carried out, found, authorized, coordinated and executed within deadline and resources". Failures: "the reason never becomes legible (epistemic blockage)"; "it becomes legible and is not given authority (volitional blockage)"; "it is given authority and no revision can be executed in time." Its two cautions on 𝓗_exec ∩ 𝓗_N: "**A nonempty intersection does not show the agent will choose such a history.**" and "**An empty intersection does not show the agent is beyond criticism.** Its earlier actions may have destroyed the capacities required." What it does not say: "That any of the three justifies the demand or the revision", that they "come in a fixed order", or "Who has the authority to decide that a change is warranted" (QUESTION-364). Note of 2026-10-31 (Bencherki): "the classification that makes a demand recognizable can also be the reason it is refused."
- **CLAIM-349** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-025 and CLAIM-027). "An organization's access to a signal, the signal's authority over action, and action on it are separable, and adaptive regulation keeps the first while making the second conditional on evidence; overcontrol … is a maladaptive regime and not more control".
- **CLAIM-296** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "What a system has, what it knows it has, and what it can deploy within a task's deadline are different".
- **CLAIM-568** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-571; member, §10). "A transition can fail in four ways that call for different remedies: a feasible path exceeds a resource ceiling, no path exists in the fixed configuration space, a path exists that no available input can execute, or a path exists that cannot be reliably followed".
- **CLAIM-556** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-568, CLAIM-566, CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-586; member, §10). Before the four obstructions "stands a failure of compatibility … repaired by changing the constraints, since dropping a demand is not relief; and after them a failure of regeneration, the capability not surviving turnover, repaired by maintenance or reproduction; so a diagnosis runs through six levels, each with its own repair". The ADR's note: A48 §10 "states a capability that succeeds once while the capacity to repeat it deteriorates."
- **CLAIM-422** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-025). "A successful corrective act, an executable procedure and a maintained organizational capacity are three different things, so an organization can record a correction, even rewrite its rules, without the corrected capacity being maintained".
- **CLAIM-711** (Proposed; interpretive; counter to CLAIM-710). "standing constraints set slowly in advance and enforced by mechanisms faster than the process can foreclose alternatives keep it open to intervention without delaying it". (A48 §10's "A slow institutional commitment can enable fast behavior" is near it; CLAIM-711 is a member through §7.)
- **CLAIM-423** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-331). In a political collective, components that change at different speeds "are not only nested by timescale". CLAIM-462's body: A9's "the mechanisms that produce a harmful outcome may operate much faster than the mechanisms authorized to evaluate and reverse it", "which is CLAIM-423's timescales deciding who holds authority."

## 4. Harmony as conserved capability, not coexistence (§12)

- **CLAIM-075** (Active; interpretive; disclaimed; member of this work and of organization-of-will, §12, §14). "Viability does not establish what ought to persist". "We cannot infer from that fact alone that the capacity or agent has moral value." A16's restatement: "**self-generated normativity and normative legitimacy must remain separate**"; A25's: effective authority "without necessarily possessing **legitimate authority**".
- **CLAIM-546** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; grounds THEORY-1048, THEORY-567, CASE-118). "Negative frequency dependence that keeps two types in coexistence does not make their interaction cooperative": Hawk–Dove with C > V coexists at p* = V/C "with Hawks still taking the whole resource from Doves"; "whether types persist together and whether they cooperate are separate questions, and a coexistence criterion answers only the first".
- **CLAIM-554** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361 and CLAIM-369; grounds CASE-118, CASE-115 and CASE-119). Niche differentiation matters "as possible relief of structural frustration among its constituents' persistence demands … and not as reduced competition"; "the exclusion of a constituent or a redistribution of influence relieves no structural frustration of the original demands, and specialization can relieve frustration where nothing competes". A7: "If persistence is the demand, our acceptance sets must concern viable histories".
- **CLAIM-547** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "Cooperation within groups and the coexistence of groups are distinct targets that can trade off".
- **CLAIM-tmpyakra** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-556 and CLAIM-075; bears on QUESTION-tmpn9t5a; filed from A48 §12, in A's voice; member). "What regulation should conserve in an industrial ecosystem is often a capability and the conditions of its reproduction, its infrastructure, expertise and access, and not the organization that now performs it". "Keeping the occupant is one way to keep the capability, and not the only one." Defeated by "A case where the function cannot be reproduced apart from its present occupant … or a showing that CLAIM-556 or CLAIM-916 already states this."
- **CLAIM-603** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-602). "A capability lost because another reliably supplies it is the Black Queen's loss … whether it is dependency or division of labour is decided by the supply, not the loss".
- **CLAIM-tmp37le7** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; grounds CASE-119; uses TERM-tmppvlfi; not a member). "A self-like organization need not be unified by convergence on a single perspective: it can be unified ecologically, by a historically maintained regime in which differentiated perspectives stay viable, mutually consequential and able to reorganize their relations". A24's table: "Rare species can recover when conditions favor them | A marginalized perspective can regain influence when relevant".
- **CLAIM-tmp31b3v** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-tmp37le7; not a member). "Harmonious integration and organizational singularity are independent".
- **CLAIM-362** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; lines authenticity, will-organization). Non-domination of subcomponents "is a candidate constituent of authenticity … a process that rarely decides keeps access to the roles in which it is consequential". TERM-tmppvlfi: "Correctability is CLAIM-362's 'continued access to consequential roles'".
- **CLAIM-916** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; grounds THEORY-134 and CASE-342; not a member). "A regulatory capacity's constraint set is fixed by what realizes it … so once the agent and the capacity are named only the agent's boundary is left to choose". It answers ARG-007's premise that the viability paper "gives no non-arbitrary way to choose the maintenance constraints for an agent's regulatory capacities". The boundary choice is filed as two readings, CLAIM-917 (narrow) and CLAIM-915 (wide), both Proposed. "Regulatory" here is an agent's capacity to regulate itself, not public regulation.

## 5. Who specifies demands; action against correctability; effective answerability (§13)

### 5a. Action is no evidence of correctability

- **CLAIM-462** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-422 and CLAIM-099; grounds CASE-176, CASE-175 and CASE-170; uses TERM-085; member, §13, cited there). "An arrangement of institutions can keep, or increase, its capacity for effective action while its capacity to be effectively corrected by others degrades, because the two are maintained by different relations, so the first is no evidence of the second". A9's four capacities "are not different degrees of one quantity": effective action, restraint, correction and reconstruction. A9: "The institutions are formally continuous. The organization through which they exercised mutual constraint is not necessarily continuous." "The cases show the pattern, not its frequency."
- **CLAIM-099** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "Maintaining a regime of effective action and maintaining the capacity to govern among regimes are not the same achievement".
- **CLAIM-719** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; uses TERM-128; member, through §4.1). "An institution can be reliably responsive to reasons within its governing mechanism and unresponsive to justified reasons for revising that mechanism, and because its own actions change the mechanism, responsiveness at the first order is no evidence of responsiveness at the second". A108 §6: "A system can be responsive to reasons within a fixed framework while being entirely unresponsive to justified reasons for changing the framework." A110: "**An organization can influence its future ability to recognize and act upon reasons that constrain it.**" SYNTHESIS-104 lists as not stated "Whether CLAIM-719's ρ^(2) can be defined: its body says no case family or response set is given."
- **CLAIM-464** (Active; interpretive; thesis; grounds CASE-176, CASE-175, CASE-170 and CASE-111). "An intervention can change what it would take to correct it … while the standards that govern that outcome stay the same".
- **CLAIM-086** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "A constraint may govern conduct without governing the conditions under which it remains authoritative".
- **CLAIM-727** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "Authority over a decision does not carry authority over the conditions on which it may be challenged … where an actor's authority is meant to be constrained by independently justified claims, its own judgment that it has met them cannot always establish that it has".
- **CLAIM-740** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-523). Explanation, contestation and interruption "are separate entitlements": "explanation without contestation is transparency without accountability, contestation without interruption a right that arrives too late, and interruption without reasons an arbitrary veto".
- **CLAIM-732** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-523). An objection "is effective only if six achievements hold together".
- **CLAIM-624** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-614, CLAIM-348, CLAIM-523, CLAIM-445 and CLAIM-462). The wrong beyond violation is "a breach of a second-order duty of answerability: to maintain feasible means for relevant violations to become knowable, contestable and correctable; it is a verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible alternative".
- **CASE-138** (Active). "Challenger to Columbia: NASA corrected its launch decisions and its safety organization after 1986, and the Columbia board found in 2003 that related organizational failure mechanisms had recurred".

### 5b. The correction-coupling principle (the owner's, 2026-11-23) and its account

- **CLAIM-tmp4h9rc** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; the owner's; rests on CLAIM-tmpvqmwc, CLAIM-tmpz8q70, CLAIM-tmp5wozo, CLAIM-tmpxoxys and CLAIM-342). "A system's ability to correct itself depends not simply on having critics or feedback, but on how evidence, evaluation, and authority are coupled—and what the system is prevented from rewriting or rejecting." The assistant's reading: CLAIM-342 "already holds the evidence half. What the claim adds is two further conditions": "**authority's coupling**" and "**protection**". Admission: its negative half by derivation (each component "exhibits them with correction failing"); its positive half as a discriminating prediction: "The claim, read strictly, predicts separate requirements. In one model that hosts all four, removing any one alone should defeat correction while the others hold." LEAD-tmpdzbyn proposes that model. "If one condition is found to entail the others, the claim reduces to that one." Defeated also if it "only restates CLAIM-342".
- **CLAIM-tmpvqmwc** (Active; demonstrated; thesis; with CASE-tmp33vf4, Active). "revision improves prediction on positions the network did not choose only when it answers to beacons whose position and channel the revision can neither rewrite nor refuse; revision by the sensors' disagreement alone restores consistency and not accuracy". CASE-tmp33vf4's "What it cannot show": "That the condition is necessary in general", and "Anything about who places the beacons. Here the world draws them".
- **CLAIM-tmpz8q70** (Proposed; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-tmp0v3wu). "informational independence does not prevent it while a resource channel runs, and what does is a return channel too weak to close the loop".
- **CLAIM-tmp5wozo** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). "grants indexed by role with the critic role scored by observed relevance leave neither belief factions nor capture, while scalar grants fail one way or the other"; "indexing by role without a separate score buys nothing".
- **CLAIM-tmpxoxys** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). "Where no process holds evidence on a block of tasks that does not come from the proposals themselves, a governance rule that treats its members alike cannot tell honest dissent from an attack that pushes the same block as hard".
- **CLAIM-tmpw11e6** (Proposed; demonstrated; counter; objects to CLAIM-tmp4h9rc; grounds CASE-tmp33vf4; the assistant's). - **CLAIM-342** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-330 and CLAIM-141; objects to CLAIM-339). "What keeps a judgement–observation loop from confirming itself is evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the interpretation it tests … a loop that selects its own evidence can lock in".
- **CLAIM-403** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-349). Admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and "its contributor's part in revising the standards of uptake are separable, each possible without the later ones".
- **CLAIM-404** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "An organization can be governed from outside without any of its decisions being made for it, through the evidence its own procedures receive or through the criteria by which they weigh evidence".
- **CLAIM-466** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). Lock-in from selected evidence and from self-scoring "need different remedies".
- **SYNTHESIS-111** (Proposed; lines learning-and-inquiry, distributed-agency, will-organization; version 2 integrates the principle). ### 5c. Answerability, authority and legitimacy

- **CLAIM-746** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-560, CLAIM-075 and CLAIM-737; lines organizational-normativity, realizability, rightful-power; member of capability-restraint-relational-freedom, not this work). Four judgments: "the warrant of the obligations it is to serve, its jurisdiction over the matter, its arrangement's capacity to realize those obligations together, and its capacity to reach compliant arrangements and correct failures by permitted means". "'Dynamic answerability' is therefore the stronger of the two, not a fourth independent axis." Its warning: "'Answerability' in A74's label means reachability plus correctability, not the directed normative relation of TERM-126 and CLAIM-739. The record should not read the two as one."
- **CLAIM-739** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). "Answerability is directed and role-specific … the party owed, the recipient of reasons, the forum that judges and the party that remedies can differ; and what is owed is indexed to the claim, not to how much the party is affected".
- **CLAIM-749** (Active; interpretive; counter to CLAIM-687). "Influence over others does not by itself ground a duty to answer to them".
- **CLAIM-687** (Active; interpretive; thesis). Accountability owed by those who control an arrangement "should rise with how indispensable the arrangement has become".
- **CLAIM-737** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). "the power to restrict is itself a capability, whose legitimacy turns on how it is bounded and answerable and not on whether the limit it imposes is right".
- **THEORY-859** (Proposed), cited in CLAIM-tmpz67ew's "What it does not say": "Regulatory capture can operate through the evaluative framework a regulator adopts rather than through its decisions".
- **SYNTHESIS-100** (Proposed; lines rightful-power, dependence-and-domination, organizational-normativity). A106's thesis: "The legitimacy of a governing relationship concerns not only which actions and principles are justified, but also how the relationship allocates, develops, and maintains the capabilities through which justified claims can influence conduct, constrain authority, and occasion correction." The audit: beyond its components "it adds a definition and a question … 'The rest is recap.'" ## 7. How the region bears on the manuscript (the digester's own reading)

### What §2 can rest on (Active)

CLAIM-tmpk4bou is the claim the manuscript should cite, since it names "technologies, infrastructure or access". §2 currently cites only CLAIM-582.
- **Conduct against outcome.** CLAIM-tmplgnjf has been Active since 2026-11-23. Its separation of four layers (specification, design, dynamics, outcomes), its reliability condition P_g ≥ 1 − ε_j and "a rule can be perfectly enforced and fail its purpose" are exactly what §2's title promises, "regulation as more than constraints on conduct". The curation entry excluded it only because A48 does not state it. If the manuscript adds it, it gains an Active member.

### §12: the weakest-supported section

- **Active ground.** CLAIM-075 (viability is not legitimacy), CLAIM-546 (coexistence is not cooperation, demonstrated) and CLAIM-554 (relief, not reduced competition) are Active. They carry the negative half: "coexistence itself does not establish harmony".

- **Internal tension.** CLAIM-tmpyakra says to conserve "a capability and the conditions of its reproduction, … not the organization that now performs it". QUESTION-tmpn9t5a says the record "has no account of how a capability is individuated apart from the firms that hold it". So the claim presupposes the individuation the question says is missing. CLAIM-tmpyakra's defeater (a function not reproducible apart from its occupant) is the open question's first item. LEAD-tmpy0jkr would decide it, and it is at stage idea.
- **Harmony is not defined in the text.** A48 §12 never defines harmony. It drops TERM-tmppvlfi's correctability conjunct, which §13 then reintroduces as "effective answerability". The record's definition would join §12 and §13 if the manuscript used it: harmony = viability ∧ accommodation ∧ correctability. It would also make the owner's "harmonious governance" (U16) precise. Using it would bring in a Proposed term with an unmet condition.
- **Independently justified demands.** §12's "independently justified public demands" has Active support for the negative half, CLAIM-725: joint satisfiability "cannot certify that any deserves fulfilment". Nothing on the record supplies the positive half, the justification (SYNTHESIS-038: no "path from the organizational account to practical or moral authority").
- **Dependence.** CLAIM-603 (Proposed) bears on §12's "dominant firm and its dependent suppliers": supply, not the loss of a capability, decides dependency against division of labour.

### §13: rich, but answerability is mostly Proposed

- **Action against correctability.** CLAIM-462 (Active, cited) and CLAIM-099 hold it. CLAIM-464 and CLAIM-086 (Active) give the mechanism: an intervention changes what it would take to correct it, and a constraint governs conduct and not its own authority. CLAIM-727 and CLAIM-740 (Active) give §13's "pathways through which affected parties … can challenge" a structure: authority over a decision is not authority over its challenge; explanation, contestation and interruption are separate.
- **Effective answerability.** This is TERM-126's "effective" dimension. TERM-126 is Proposed. CLAIM-746 warns that a looser use, "reachability plus correctability", must not be read as the directed normative relation. The manuscript uses "effective answerability" for both technical adaptability and political contestability, so it should say which sense it means. A48's own contrast ("technically adaptable" against "politically unanswerable") is close to CLAIM-746's adequacy against warrant and jurisdiction.
- **Who specifies the demands.** The record does not answer this. QUESTION-485, QUESTION-480, QUESTION-482, QUESTION-364 and QUESTION-129 are all Open. CLAIM-tmpz67ew says so expressly. The manuscript's statement that "These questions cannot be answered by control theory alone" matches the record exactly. It should be presented as an open question, not a result.
- **The correction-coupling principle.** It is new since the A48 draft. It gives §13 a mechanism where A48 has only an aspiration ("institutions capable of being changed by evidence, legitimate challenge"). Three components are Active and demonstrated in models: protected evidence (CLAIM-tmpvqmwc), scoring apart from agreement (CLAIM-tmp5wozo) and the identifiability limit (CLAIM-tmpxoxys). The capture result (CLAIM-tmpz8q70) is Proposed. The principle itself (CLAIM-tmp4h9rc) is the owner's and Proposed, and whether its conditions are separate requirements is open (LEAD-tmpdzbyn).
