# ARG-058: If the second-order duty of answerability were unconditional it would entail that a self-maintaining organization ought to preserve its corrigibility, which the record disclaims, so the duty must be bounded by obligation, capability and reasonable alternatives

From the second share of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c).

## The inference

1. Suppose the second-order duty were unconditional: every organization must maintain means for every relevant violation to become knowable, contestable and correctable.
2. Then a self-maintaining organization ought to preserve its corrigibility whatever it costs, which is [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md), filed as disclaimed (Proposed; [ARG-042](ARG-042.md) Rejected).
3. [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md): keeping a corrective capacity is worth k(0.25 − 0.5ε) − C in the exchange's model, negative when its cost is large or the decisions it bears on are few. So an unconditional duty would sometimes require what is worse by the duty's own lights.
4. So the duty is bounded by the organization's "obligations, capabilities, and reasonable alternatives" (A62 §3, condition 5), as A60 §6 also says: "That duty is not an unconditional command to maximize transparency or investigate every conceivable harm."

## Critical questions

- **Is the consequence really unacceptable, or merely surprising?** The record disclaims [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md) rather than rejecting it as false; it is a boundary the record declines to cross. So the reductio shows the unconditional duty goes beyond what the record holds, not that it is refuted. [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md)'s model gives the stronger point, that the unconditional duty can require a net loss.
- **Is the premise the one to blame?** Yes: the premise to drop is the unconditional form, not the duty itself. A bounded duty does not entail [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md), and A's own statements of it (A60 §6, A62 §3) are already bounded. The remaining risk is the other horn of [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s defeat condition: a bound so loose that every organization is excused.
