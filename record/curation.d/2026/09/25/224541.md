**What the survey was for.** The owner asked for a pass over the anthology's
curation record to find readings it had skipped. The purpose was to pick out
early-triage candidates for this record: work the anthology set aside because
it was out of scope. The survey scanned all 239 anthology curation entries,
every ADR, issue [#290](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/290) and the papers feed.

**What it found: 70 candidates.**

- **One named decline.** *The Work Capacity of Channels with Memory*
  ([LIT-tmp6pfj4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp6pfj4.md)) is the only scope decline the anthology named by title that
  neither record held.
- **Fourteen unassigned works.** These have five or more reading sessions and
  are the clusters the 09/25 triage named only as a group.
- **Fifty-five works below the triage's ten-minute line.** None had been given
  a disposition.

The owner asked for all 70. Five went to the anthology as boundary works, and
the other **65 are filed here**. Each is `Deferred`, seeded from its abstract
and a skim, and was filed against the vocabulary as [ADR-003](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-003.md) enlarged it.

**Depth.**

- 60 carry a `Skimmed` NOTE.
- 5 are at abstract depth, because their full text was paywalled or blocked:
  - the Dual EC post
  - *The Homological Nature of Entropy*
  - *Dynamics of collectives with opinionated agents*
  - the polycrystalline-water video
  - Kupiainen's renormalization-group comment

## First: read in full, in these pairs

1. **Bourrat's coarse-graining account of individuality ([LIT-tmp90xyi](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp90xyi.md)),
   alongside his *Adding causality…* ([LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md), already read).** It is the same
   author's next move. [LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md)'s reading found that its interventional measure
   was never computed and that one of its identities fails. Whether this paper
   repairs that is the question.
2. ***The Work Capacity of Channels with Memory* ([LIT-tmp6pfj4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp6pfj4.md)).** This is the
   anthology's one named decline. The skim found an
   information-thermodynamics theory: when actions affect what an agent later
   perceives, the agents that extract the most energy must trade prediction
   against forgetting. It carries no ML practice, which supports the decline.
   It went to Physical Review X in 2026, but the DOI Crossref lists has
   placeholder metadata, so it was left off until it can be confirmed.
3. **The linguistics triad:** Haspelmath on word segmentation ([LIT-tmpfdz4e](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpfdz4e.md)),
   Bruening ([LIT-tmpjuuq5](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpjuuq5.md)) and Krauska & Lau ([LIT-tmp0iss5](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0iss5.md)) against
   lexicalism. Haspelmath is filed under its 2011 first appearance, not the
   2017 reprint the survey found. It is the one work here with a plausible
   bearing on tokenization. That bearing is the seed reader's inference, not
   the paper's claim, so the work stays here unless a reading finds otherwise.
4. **The quantum-foundations anchors:**
   - the PBR theorem ([LIT-tmpf31j2](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpf31j2.md)), which the graphical-calculus paper
     ([LIT-tmpbydeh](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbydeh.md)) and the knowledge-and-reality paper ([LIT-tmpw9qc6](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpw9qc6.md))
     position themselves against;
   - Barandes's stochastic-quantum correspondence ([LIT-tmpubwzo](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpubwzo.md)), read
     against *What is quantum in quantum randomness?* ([LIT-tmpoehzw](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpoehzw.md)). Both
     reach the Born rule through the same unistochastic matrices.

## Third: the skim is enough

The remaining **38** are rated low. They include:

  dropping;

They are filed because the owner asked for all of it. Each note says where its
value lies.

    word and a behavioural-modelling word.

  the better source.

  reports: DHS v. D.V.D., No. 24A1153, 3 July 2025.
