[LEAD-059](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-059.md) asked for a corollary to be filed that was already filed, as
[CLAIM-629](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-629.md), by the same review whose curation entry (2026-11-07, 082259)
listed it as not filed. The backlog audit of 2026-11-19 took such "left"
lists at their word. This audit, delegated by the assistant in this
session, checks the open leads that audit created for targets the record
already holds.

## Scope and method

Of the 184 Open leads whose summary says they were filed by the backlog
audit of 2026-11-19, 150 had neither a dated note of 2026-11-22 nor a
lead-NNN changelog fragment, so had not been carried out today. Within
about an hour, 33 were checked: 19 whose target is a single deliverable
(file, write, derive, build, read one work), and 14 reading leads, whose
named works were checked for a NOTE. Each target was searched by code, by
the entries whose relations or prose cite the codes the lead advances, by
title words and distinctive numbers, and in the curation entry it cites,
with git history for the order of filing. The table, with evidence for
every verdict, is scratch/lead-audit/results.md.
