# ARG-tmp5xhiv: Ott and Antonsen's exact reduction and its attraction without a rate condition, as an existence case, show that a closed collective law can be in place without a rate difference, and so meet the rate-difference claim's first defeater within globally coupled phase oscillators

## The inference

Premises: on an invariant manifold the order parameter of a first-harmonic
oscillator population obeys a closed law exactly, while the distribution's
own modes neither decay nor slow (THEORY-tmpmvcnr); for any frequency spread
Δ > 0 the order parameter is attracted to that law, with no condition on
other rates (THEORY-tmp0d58z). Conclusion: an effective constraint can be
in place without a rate difference, which is CLAIM-478's defeater (a) for
this class (CLAIM-tmpervl4).

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes. Both results are proofs, under stated
  conditions: infinite N, first-harmonic coupling, analytic initial data,
  a Lorentzian frequency distribution for the attraction. Neither is
  rechecked by simulation here; CLAIM-tmpo6ehq rechecks the invariance's
  dependence on the first harmonic.
- **Does it separate the very things the conclusion says can come
  apart?** In part. It separates a closed law from a *faster settling*:
  the proof's condition is Δ > 0 alone, and in the locked regime with
  K > 4Δ the guaranteed approach rate Δ is below the order parameter's own
  rate on the manifold. It does not plainly separate the law from
  *persistence*: the manifold is exactly invariant. Defeater (a) asks for
  both, "nothing they consist of is slower or more persistent". Whether an
  invariance fixed by the form of the coupling is persistence of something
  the constraint consists of is the reading on which CLAIM-tmpa694z and
  CLAIM-tmpervl4 differ.
- **Does one case reach (a)?** Only with typicality: (a) counts its
  candidates "only if they are the typical case and not exceptions". An
  existence case cannot supply typicality; it comes from how the class
  sits among the systems the record's individuation claims treat. Within
  synchronization models the class is standard; across systems it is
  narrow (CLAIM-tmpo6ehq).

## Verdict

Proposed, with the owner's reading of (a) deciding it: Active if

assistant leans to Rejected, for the reasons CLAIM-tmpa694z gives.
