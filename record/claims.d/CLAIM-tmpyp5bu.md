# CLAIM-tmpyp5bu: A hyperstition's success is not its representation's memetic fitness: what a representation helps bring about can come to be maintained by processes of its own and outlast the representation's circulation, so that it no longer needs to be believed in to exist, or it can stay dependent on continued circulation; which of the two holds is a fact about maintenance provenance, told apart by withdrawing the circulation once the realization has formed

## The claim

A10 §2 separates what succeeds: virality ("Circulation of the
expression"), fidelity, generativity, nucleation, hyperstitionality
("Realization of circumstances represented by the expression") and
autonomy ("Maintenance of the resulting organization through its own
processes"). "These properties form no necessary hierarchy. A hyperstition
can succeed at producing its referent while ceasing to circulate itself."
"Hyperstitional success is therefore distinguishable from memetic
fitness."

A10 §9 gives the phases: anticipation ("A represented possibility helps
coordinate activity"), nucleation, and autonomous maintenance ("The
realized organization sustains itself without continued reliance on the
originating representation"). "The originating fiction may be forgotten.
Its organizational consequences may nevertheless persist." "A successful
hyperstition might ultimately produce something that no longer needs to be
believed in order to exist. The reverse is also possible: an organization
might remain dependent on the continued circulation of the representation
that sustains it. Distinguishing these outcomes would be a central research
question." A10 ties it to [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s formation and maintenance provenance
and to the record's transient-support line.

**What the record holds.** [CLAIM-426](CLAIM-426.md) holds the circuit and Land's
definition ([NOTE-1378](../notes.d/NOTE-1378.md)): circulation can help bring about what a judgement
says. [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md) holds the two provenances and the test for the second,
knockouts of what upholds the capacity now. [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md)'s second construction
is the shape of the first outcome in another setting: a transient
encounter forms a connector that is then maintained by what it enables.
The claim puts these together for a representation's circulation: its
formation provenance is the representation's; its maintenance provenance
can stay the representation's or pass to the realized organization, and
withdrawal of circulation after formation is the knockout that tells which.

## What it does not say

- **Not that persistence vindicates the representation.** A realization
  that outlasts its fiction does not make the fiction true when it
  circulated ([CLAIM-402](CLAIM-402.md), [CLAIM-426](CLAIM-426.md)'s "not of whether it was right").
- **Not that withdrawal is easy to observe.** A representation that has
  stopped circulating may persist in institutions' rules, which are a
  second channel; the test needs the representation's every channel
  withdrawn, or it reads residual circulation as self-maintenance.
- **Not that the earlier distinction between spread and organizing is
  new.** That a widely transmitted meme can organize little and a rare one
  much was put earlier in the exchange (U1–U5); the claim adds the
  hyperstitional case, where what is realized is what the representation
  says.
