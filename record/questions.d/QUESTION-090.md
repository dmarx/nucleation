# QUESTION-090: Under what conditions does a population of heterogeneous processes develop a self-maintaining, internally differentiated governance structure that acts coherently without suppressing the plurality of processes it emerges from?

## Why it is a question

A4: "**The central question then becomes: under what conditions does a
population of heterogeneous meaning operators develop a self-maintaining,
internally differentiated governance structure capable of acting coherently
without suppressing the plurality of processes from which its agency
emerges?**" The owner, at U5: "This is a great formulation of the question".
A9 restated it as the core research question of the synthesis.

It refines [QUESTION-034](QUESTION-034.md), which the owner raised on 2026-10-11 from the same
starting point, the self as a multi-agent system, and which asks what makes
a population of interests act as one: bargaining, a change in which may act,
or agreement. This question adds what the exchange made central:

- **Self-maintaining.** The governance is produced by the processes it
  governs, not given. Whether that is closure in Montévil and Mossio's
  sense ([LIT-582](../literature.d/LIT-582.md)) and not just feedback was left open (A4 §5).
- **Internally differentiated.** Authority differs by region and role
  (A3's authority field), so "acting as one" need not mean one controller.
- **Without suppressing plurality.** [QUESTION-078](QUESTION-078.md) asks whether acting as one
  requires the interests to synchronize. This question requires that it not.

The exchange proposed partial answers, all Proposed: grants without
self-allocation ([CLAIM-285](../claims.d/CLAIM-285.md)), trust as a regularity of conferral
([CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md)), and the self-governing self ([CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md)). An answer
would give conditions, on the aggregation rule, memory and coupling, under
which coherence and plurality coexist, and show where one is lost.

## Note of 2026-10-18: the converse problem, from insider threats

At the owner's U41 question on insider threats, A41 of the exchange of
2026-10-17 stated this question's last clause as a security problem: "how
can an organization protect itself against harmful internal activity
without suppressing the disagreement, experimentation, and
self-transformation on which its continued adaptability depends?" It is the
converse of its first question, how to give constituents "enough authority
to function, learn, and govern themselves without becoming vulnerable to
the misuse of that authority". Together they are this question with an
adversary added: coherence must survive constituents that try to capture
it, and plurality must survive the defences.

A41's federated-learning testbed has a row for exactly the plurality at
stake: a "Distributed but valid minority signal" is a "Productive difference
that should not automatically be suppressed", and "A crude defense might
suppress those updates as suspicious, improving some aggregate security
measure while harming the organization's ability to learn from minority
environments." Filed as [CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md) (robustness lives in the processes
that allocate and audit authority, and trades off against revision) and
[CASE-108](../cases.d/CASE-108.md) (A41's proposed study). An answer here would say when that
trade-off can be moved.

## Note of 2026-11-05: A's second synthesis on DNA origami

From the second synthesis on DNA origami by another assistant (A), pasted by the owner on 2026-11-05.

A asks, of DNA origami, "what additional reaction network or organizational arrangement would make regeneration endogenous, rather than simply externally supplied", to be investigated "in stages: assembly, damage, component replacement, and the maintenance of the processes enabling replacement". The readings in this pull request reach the first stage and part of a replicating one (Ni et al. 2026, [NOTE-1630](../notes.d/NOTE-1630.md)). None reached replacement of damaged parts when this note was first written. Scheckenbach et al. 2021 ([NOTE-1636](../notes.d/NOTE-1636.md)), read later in this pull request, do show replacement from a supplied strand pool. No reading reaches maintenance of the processes of replacement, since every replacement part comes from outside ([CLAIM-597](../claims.d/CLAIM-597.md)). So DNA origami does not yet bear on how a self-maintaining structure emerges.

No status changes.
