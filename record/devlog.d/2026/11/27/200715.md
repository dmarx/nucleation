**Going wrong.** The search before the SEP readings followed ADR-tmpn87w5: open pull requests, case-insensitive, by mechanism words. But the words chosen were the topic's: deception, self-deception, delusion, Mele. The mechanism CLAIM-tmpu6c7h turns on is reward selecting a belief, and "reward" was not searched. The self-deception reader found NOTE-443 (Ainslie), which holds that premise directly.

**Going right.** The readers' rechecks, which search again with what the reading surfaced, caught it, along with three more: NOTE-998, NOTE-1000 and NOTE-1450. The rule's second search is doing its job.

**For the next retro.** The mechanism words for a claim are its premises' words, not its topic's. Before briefing, list the premises of each claim the reading bears on, and search for those.
