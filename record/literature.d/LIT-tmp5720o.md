The renormalization group grew out of ad hoc procedures for handling divergences in perturbative calculations. This short Comment surveys the programme of making RG calculations mathematically rigorous. The reference list suggests the ground covered: Gawędzki–Kupiainen and Feldman–Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor constructive work, Bałaban on gauge theory, Aizenman–Duminil-Copin on φ⁴ triviality, Giuliani–Mastropietro–Porta, Bauerschmidt–Brydges–Slade, and Kennedy–Rychkov.
