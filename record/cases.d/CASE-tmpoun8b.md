# CASE-tmpoun8b: The absorbed writer

## The case

The same writer and the same commitment as [CASE-013](CASE-013.md), and the same available
distractions, with nothing blocked. This afternoon the argument is coming
together. Each paragraph rewards the next, and the writer works for three
hours without checking email. Asked afterwards, they report no struggle and
no rule. Then a notification sounds. They look, answer two messages, and
going back to the draft is now the effortful thing that every other
afternoon is.

Held fixed across the three afternoons: the writer's endorsed commitment,
and what the distractions offer.

## What it can show

The three afternoons are three ways one agent comes to act, or fails to:

- **Avoidance wins.** The sooner reward overtakes the commitment
  ([CASE-013](CASE-013.md)).
- **A device enforces.** The efficacy conditions change while the
  preferences stay put ([CASE-013](CASE-013.md) and [ARG-005](../arguments.d/ARG-005.md)).
- **Nothing has to enforce.** The interests are synchronized
  ([CLAIM-tmpf301t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf301t.md)).

It is fictional and stipulated to fit, so it illustrates and bears no
weight. Whether real absorption has no reversal inside it is the claim's
prediction, not something the case shows.
