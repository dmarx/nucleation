# LEAD-tmphc6xs: Build a small stochastic assembly model in which support can be withdrawn at chosen intermediate states and the nucleus's relations switched on as a state and as a rate modifier separately

## What it would do

Write the Markov chain and compute the difference in committor between
supported and withdrawn at each intermediate state, with the rival "the
system merely completed before withdrawal" excluded.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-398: "A system where support can be withdrawn at chosen
  intermediate states, with the committor measured afterwards from each, in
  molecules or in a model."
- QUESTION-397: "a small Markov chain in which the nucleus's relations are
  both a state and a modifier of other rates, so the two effects can be
  switched on separately."

## What would count

A difference that persists after withdrawal from some states answers
QUESTION-398 in the model; the two effects switched on separately answer
QUESTION-397.
