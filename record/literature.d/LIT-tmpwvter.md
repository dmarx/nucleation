## Key takeaways

*Nothing here is read. What follows is what the record expects the chapter to establish, from its standing in the literature, so a reader can check it against the text.*

- **What flow is said to be.** Absorption in an activity with clear goals and immediate feedback, when perceived challenge and skill are both high and in balance; action and awareness merge, reflective self-consciousness drops out, and the activity is experienced as worth doing for itself (autotelic).
- **Why the record wants it.** The record's claims about intrapersonal synchronization ([CLAIM-tmpf301t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf301t.md)) treat flow as the case in which no interest has to be overridden. That reading leans on the autotelic condition, that the activity's immediate reward and its longer-run value coincide. Whether the chapter states the condition that way is unchecked.
