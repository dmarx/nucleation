**What the pass took.** Five clusters from the worklist, from the most
relied on down: CLAIM-027, THEORY-067 with CLAIM-169, THEORY-162 with
CLAIM-061 and CLAIM-004, CLAIM-050 with CLAIM-106, and CLAIM-130 on
QUESTION-030, carried over from the fourth pass. Four reviewers read one
cluster each, read-only, and every recommendation was checked against the
entries before it was applied.

**The common finding: concessions never written into their targets.**
ADR-059 says a conceded objection is recorded by revising its target. Four
of the five clusters had granted or Active objections whose concessions
lived only in a note ("The text above is unchanged") or in `defeated_if`.

- CLAIM-050 had conceded CLAIM-225 and CLAIM-230 in its condition on
  2026-10-10, but its title still claimed precision about "the decisions
  the receiver must make". Narrowed to report fidelity and a ceiling.
- CLAIM-061's 2026-10-10 note called its own "necessity fails in CASE-018,
  read in reverse" mistaken and left the text. Narrowed to sufficiency; the
  necessity half is CLAIM-tmp6rohb.
- CLAIM-027 had CLAIM-021's concession ("narrows CLAIM-027 to
  cross-realization unification") in the concession's body only.

The pattern is cheap to check mechanically: an Active `granted` claim
whose `objects_to` target has no version later than the granted claim's
date. That is a candidate forcing function, not filed here.

**A hidden claim in CLAIM-027.** CLAIM-024, "Nothing here is new", does not
dispute CLAIM-027's title, which says what decides efficacy and claims no
novelty. It disputes the explanatory gain the paper stakes itself on, which
no entry stated. That gain is now CLAIM-tmpnvpfe, and CLAIM-024 objects to
it. CLAIM-021, a granted concession, was filed as `objects_to` CLAIM-024,
which made an Active claim read as an answer to the objection it agrees
with; it is now `complements`. CLAIM-027 stays Proposed: both arguments
for it are Proposed, and ARG-009's unanswered question "Does changing R
change anything else?" is CLAIM-027's own defeater.

**CLAIM-130's premise.** It rested on CLAIM-127, which is Proposed for its
proposal about the formalism (index the empirical model by intervention).
CLAIM-130 needs only that do(I) and do(S) act on different variables,
which THEORY-419 and THEORY-138 ground. The edge is now `complements`, and
CLAIM-130 is promoted. QUESTION-030 split: QUESTION-tmphmweu is the part
CLAIM-130 settles.

**Readings that wait on something not reading.** THEORY-067 (Ainslie) and
THEORY-162 (dynamic semantics) are read in full, and both are held back by
their `promote_when`, which asks for something else. THEORY-067 needs a
result that varies only perceived precedence, and the précis concedes the
mechanism is hard to test. THEORY-162 needs a static rival read and
checked; Mandelkern (2024) and Elbourne (2005) have no open copy.

**ADR-058 moves.** Two readings were grounds for claims that do not stand
on them: THEORY-067 for CLAIM-344, which "is not a rival mechanism", and
THEORY-162 for CLAIM-061, since applying it to translation "is a further
step these sources do not take". Both are now `corroborated_by`.
