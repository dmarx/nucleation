# THEORY-2224: Burning Man's 10 Principles, written by Larry Harvey in 2004 and offered as an ethos rather than rules, state gifting as unconditional, ground decommodification in preserving the spirit of gifting, and state radical self-reliance only as reliance on one's inner resources, so that the ethos draws no line between what is self-provided and what is given

## The account

Burning Man's 10 Principles, written by Larry Harvey in 2004 and offered as
an ethos rather than rules, state gifting as unconditional ("Gifting does
not contemplate a return or an exchange for something of equal value"),
ground decommodification in preserving the spirit of gifting, and state
radical self-reliance only as reliance on one's inner resources. So the
ethos draws no line between what is self-provided and what is given.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-2314](../notes.d/NOTE-2314.md); the principles are quoted there in full where
the record uses them. The page is current and undated, so the 2004 wording
is not established.
