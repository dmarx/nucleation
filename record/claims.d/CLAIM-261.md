# CLAIM-261: Synchronization of an agent's interests is desirable, but not necessary for acting as one: internal conflict shows a human agent acting while its interests stay divided

## The claim

The owner's position, given on 2026-10-12: synchronization of an agent's
interests ([TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md)<!-- ref::uses: TERM-050 -->) is "*desirable*, but absolutely not
necessary. Internal conflict is an easy demonstration in the case of
human agents."

**It is not necessary.** A person who wants the cake and keeps to the diet
acts, and the act is theirs. The interest that lost has not gone away.
It is still pressing while the person acts, and it is still the person's
interest. The reluctant writer ([CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md)) finishes the article with
distractions blocked and the wish to look at them intact. Unity of
action is achieved over a division that remains. That is the
"differentiated interests in a coordinated organization" side of
[QUESTION-078](../questions.d/QUESTION-078.md).

**It is desirable.** When the interests agree, as on the absorbed writer's
afternoon ([CASE-051](../cases.d/CASE-051.md)), nothing has to be overridden or enforced.
[CLAIM-162](CLAIM-162.md) takes that to be why flow is reported as effortless. The
divided agent pays for its unity in effort, devices and lapses.

## What it bears on

- [CLAIM-162](CLAIM-162.md) holds that flow is synchronization. This claim adds that flow
  is the good case, not the only case, of acting as one. [CLAIM-162](CLAIM-162.md)'s
  summary already disclaims "the best or the normal way to act as one"
  for synchronization. This claim says what the other ways are.
- [CLAIM-198](CLAIM-198.md) says a self is divided over a choice only on an overlap.
  This claim says that a self divided on an overlap can still act as one
  there.
- [CLAIM-163](CLAIM-163.md) objects that synchronization lacks a mechanism. If
  synchronization is not what acting as one consists in, the objection
  bears on the account of the good case and not on the account of
  integration as a whole.

## What it does not say

It does not say how a divided agent comes to act as one: by bargaining,
by one interest taking control, or by a governing level. That is
[QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md) and [QUESTION-054](../questions.d/QUESTION-054.md). It does not say that every divided
agent acts as one, since some lapse. And it does not say that
synchronization is desirable for every agent or in every respect. A
population that never disagrees may also never notice what it is
missing.

## Note of 2026-10-18: what the divided agent pays for

A44 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 proposed a correction on effort: "**An
authentically maintained goal may demand more effort, not less.**" It was
set against authenticity ("the simplistic assumption that authenticity
reduces effort"), not against this claim, which is the record's effort
claim and which A44 did not cite. The owner, at U45, said what the
effortful case is: "The "more effortful" intrapersonal conflict case is
the one in which the internal modules don't negotiate amongst themselves
and instead require escalating to higher level processes to mediate."

CLAIM-tmpmr1t8 refines this claim's "The divided agent pays for its unity
in effort, devices and lapses". It pays for escalation. A divided agent
whose regimes settle their disagreement among themselves pays less while
staying divided, which is practical synchronization (TERM-tmpnfj83)
without synchronization in TERM-050's sense. CLAIM-tmpmr1t8 rests on this
claim. A45's Person A (CASE-tmpffqbr) is this claim's divided agent. The
status is unchanged.
