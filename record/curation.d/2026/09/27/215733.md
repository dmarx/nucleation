The owner asked for Carù's DPhil thesis, *Logical and Topological
Contextuality in Quantum Mechanics and Beyond* (Oxford, 2019, supervised by
Abramsky). The reader obtained it from the Oxford Research Archive (open,
CC BY; DOI 10.5287/ora-kznbp4k4y, which I checked resolves to the ORA
record). It is filed as [LIT-tmpb5bvv](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpb5bvv.md), read as [NOTE-tmp07tuj](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp07tuj.md), `Active`,
and it `extends` [LIT-280](../../record/literature.d/LIT-280.md).

- **Read in full:** Chapters I–V and VIII. Chapter IV was diffed against
  arXiv 1807.04203.
- **Skim-read, statements only:** Chapters VI (AvN triples) and VII
  (minimum resources for strong non-locality). The brief allowed this.
- **Date:** ORA gives the year only, so `published:` is its deposit date.

**The question was whether the thesis repairs the 2018 preprint beyond
cycles. It does not.**

- **Republished unchanged.** Chapters III–IV republish [LIT-279](../../record/literature.d/LIT-279.md) and [LIT-280](../../record/literature.d/LIT-280.md),
  with "joint" renamed "line".
  - Def 7.1 → Def IV.26, with the same universal definition.
  - Thm 7.7 → Thm IV.32, which holds.
  - Prop 8.2 → Prop IV.34, reworded but still false on the same table.
  - Conjecture 9.1 → Conjecture IV.41, restated as open.
- **Other errors carried over.** The wrong level-0 equation of §6.1 and
  [LIT-279](../../record/literature.d/LIT-279.md)'s false Prop 6.1 (now Prop III.8) are both kept.
- **Two new claims are false.**
  - "No model without the CCP is known" (p. 88). The §8 cover is one.
  - The §8 cover has a unique false ℤ/2 family (p. 91). A second exists.
- **The reader's computation on the §8 cover.** Under the thesis's own
  definition, the reader ran the 2018 scripts unchanged, since the
  definitions are unchanged. The §8 cover of [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md) is detected on 6 of
  15 sections at every level from 0 to 3, and its non-detected section
  lifts compatibly through level 4. The context-wise reading, which the
  thesis never states, detects all 15 sections at level 2.

**What is new in the thesis.**

- **Probabilistic line models** (Thm IV.40).
- **Chapter V (with Abramsky)** recasts contextuality in valuation algebras.
  It gives inference algorithms with bounds such as
  O(k^n·l^{k(n−1)+1}), never implemented.
- **Chapter VI** characterises All-vs-Nothing stabiliser subgroups by AvN
  triples. Its proof was not checked.
- **Chapter VII:** strong non-locality needs three qubits in the GHZ class.

**[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) (version bump).** The cohomology line cites the thesis for the
cyclic result, and says its extension and conjecture are restated without
repair. It adds that neither paper nor thesis analyses cost. The claim
itself is unchanged. The reader's optional stabiliser-characterisation line
was not added, because its proof was not checked.

**Where the line stands.** A complete, computable cohomological test is
established only on cyclic covers. [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md)'s §8 cover remains the standing
counterexample to the general conjecture, and the context-wise variant that
does detect it has not been published by anyone. The bibliography's "Car17
= arXiv:1701.00242" is the proceedings volume; [LIT-279](../../record/literature.d/LIT-279.md) is 1701.00656.
