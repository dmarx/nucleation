# CASE-tmpnaqgj: A rendering that keeps the solidarity–criticism ambiguity open, and one that settles it

## The case

A34 §4: "One translation might preserve an ambiguity between amused solidarity
and moral disapproval. Another might settle that ambiguity immediately. Their
static judgment profiles could look similar while their interpretive dynamics
differ." A48 §7.5: "One translation sustains an ambiguity between solidarity and
criticism. Another fixes the speaker as explicitly condemnatory, reducing
sensitivity to subsequent affiliative framing", with the hypothesis "that such
differences may explain fidelity judgments not captured by static similarity
measures."

## What it can show

Why endpoints can agree while routes differ ([CLAIM-tmpx6akp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx6akp.md)): ambiguity is a property of
how an utterance responds to later frames, not of any single judgement of it.
Stipulated; the renderings are not specified. It is the most concrete form of
the manuscript's §9 claim, which states it abstractly ("preservation of the
effects of future frames"). Absent from A52 and from the manuscript.
