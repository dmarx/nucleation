Right after [QUESTION-025](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-025.md) was opened (do correlated or hierarchical attributes
in co-occurrence still give linear attribute directions, and a non-Boolean
concept lattice?), the owner asked for six works, then a seventh, then six
more by link. Each was read on its own merits by a separate reader, and each
has an Active LIT and a Read NOTE. Where the owner gave only an author and a
year, or only a link, the reader confirmed the work before filing.

## What entered

**Hierarchy in learning and data.**
- Cagnetta et al., the random hierarchy model ([LIT-tmpz5v25](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpz5v25.md)), with
  [THEORY-tmpzjtfu](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpzjtfu.md) on the sample complexity of learning hierarchical data.
- Larsson, Maity & Tsiotras 2022 ([LIT-tmpbl4wx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbl4wx.md)), information-theoretic
  pruning of a given quadtree. No THEORY; despite its title, no hierarchy
  emerges.

**Hyperbolic geometry of networks.**
- Krioukov et al. 2010 ([LIT-tmp0u9c9](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0u9c9.md)), with [THEORY-tmp1y92d](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp1y92d.md) stating the
  model result only.
- Bianconi & Rahmede 2017 ([LIT-tmp9eclr](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp9eclr.md)), with [THEORY-tmpp6w9v](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpp6w9v.md): the
  curvature comes from an equal-length convention, not from the growth.
- García-Pérez, Boguñá & Serrano 2018 ([LIT-tmpbx6w0](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbx6w0.md)), with [THEORY-tmp5ul7m](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp5ul7m.md):
  the network model is approximately closed under geometric
  coarse-graining.

**Hyperbolic embeddings in machine learning.**
- Sala, De Sa, Gu & Ré 2018 ([LIT-tmpt10fk](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpt10fk.md)), with [THEORY-tmpzw8vz](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpzw8vz.md) on the
  precision cost of tree embeddings.
- Ganea, Bécigneul & Hofmann 2018 ([LIT-tmp5o7bs](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp5o7bs.md)), with [THEORY-tmp5l4pi](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp5l4pi.md) on
  entailment cones; their transitivity is asserted, not proved.
- Lin, Coifman, Mishne & Talmon 2023 ([LIT-tmpjwrpt](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpjwrpt.md)). No THEORY; the reader
  found a gap in one proof step.
- Yang et al. 2023 ([LIT-tmpocqly](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpocqly.md)). No THEORY; trained hyperbolic models do
  not place tree levels by radius on their own.

**Hyperbolic geometry in brains and perception.**
- Zhou, Smith & Sharpee 2018 ([LIT-tmpp74b9](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpp74b9.md)), with [THEORY-tmp7qz7l](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp7qz7l.md) on the
  olfactory space.
- Zhang, Rich, Lee & Sharpee 2022 ([LIT-tmpvydv3](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpvydv3.md)), with [THEORY-tmpae88o](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpae88o.md) on
  the hippocampus.
- Sharpee 2019 ([LIT-tmpuycxh](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpuycxh.md)), a perspective. No THEORY; its argument from
  Zipf's law is an analogy.

**Renormalization and information.**
- Koch-Janusz & Ringel 2018 ([LIT-tmprgaew](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmprgaew.md)), with [THEORY-tmpsylqv](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpsylqv.md): the
  coarse-graining that stays informative about the environment is the
  relevant one. It shares a Nature Physics issue with García-Pérez et al.
  but is not a companion paper.

## Bearing on [QUESTION-025](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-025.md)

None answers it; their bearings are written into the question's new
section. The nearest thing to an answer was already in the record: Saxe et
al. 2019 ([LIT-862](../../record/literature.d/LIT-862.md)) gives tree-shaped linear directions for hierarchical
features in a linear network, one per branching rather than one per
attribute. The batch adds that hierarchy may be held by distances or by
containment rather than directions, that hyperbolic curvature alone is not
evidence of hierarchy, and that none of the geometric encodings gives a
lattice's meets and joins.

## The boundary

None is held in the anthology clone (commit d8b5ba5, possibly stale). The
machine-learning papers (Cagnetta, Sala, Ganea, Lin, Yang, Koch-Janusz &
Ringel) carry `anthology-candidate`; the network, neuroscience and
information-theory papers do not.

## Found along the way

- Several readers reported no word for hyperbolic or metric geometry, for
  statistical physics and the renormalization group, or for perception.
  `mathematics`, `network-science`, `natural-sciences` and `cognition`
  stand in. No word was added.
- The olfaction paper's date is its online date, 29 August 2018, not the
  issue's cover date of 3 August, which predates the article. The
  earlier-date rule settles real doubt about first appearance; a cover
  date before the article existed is not one.
- Works these papers build on that neither record holds: three follow-ups
  to the random hierarchy model (arXiv 2402.16991, 2404.10727,
  2406.00048); Koch-Janusz & Ringel's sequels (arXiv 1809.09632,
  2101.11633) and Mehta & Schwab (arXiv 1410.3831), which they argue
  against; Ganea et al.'s *Hyperbolic Neural Networks* (NeurIPS 2018).
