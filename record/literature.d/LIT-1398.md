# LIT-1398: The Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

Merton, R. K. (1948). The self-fulfilling prophecy. *The Antioch Review*,
8(2), 193–210. doi:10.2307/4609267.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmprvkcw). On CASE-142's question, it
covers the resistance route in part, for groups and under a second name:
out-groups that answer the in-group's definition are condemned for the
answer as well, by "moral alchemy", which renames conduct rather than
producing it.

## Why this is here

[CASE-141](../cases.d/CASE-141.md), the label that confirms itself, is a stipulated case of the
mechanism this paper named. It is registered so that the case's mechanism
has its classic source, and so that a reading can check whether Merton's
account already covers the resistance route of [CASE-142](../cases.d/CASE-142.md), where the
label is rejected and still confirmed. The record holds the nearest
documented application through Espeland and Sauder ([LIT-1330](LIT-1330.md)), who count
self-fulfilling prophecy among the mechanisms of reactivity to rankings.
