  Song, Cheng, Zhou and Wang (2022), Linking Scholarly Contents: The Design
  and Construction of an Argumentation Graph. The authors build a knowledge
  graph of the argumentation inside and across scientific papers. Their data
  model reuses their earlier Scientific Paper Argumentation Ontology (SAO,
  built on AMO/Toulmin, DEO, CoreSC, EXPO) with 18 unit classes (background,
  claim, warrant, backing, rebuttal, conclusion, hypothesis, data, method,
  limitation, …), 12 intra-article relations (e.g. proves, supports, is part
  of, is replaced by) and CiTO-based inter-article relations (e.g. works
  for, provides principles for, provides assertion for). Two tools: an
  annotation platform (SAPSP) and a d3.js visualizer (VSAG) over Neo4j.
  Twelve papers on the Technology Acceptance Model and Theory of Planned
  Behavior (1989–2012) were annotated by hand into 1,262 nodes and 1,818
  relations. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
