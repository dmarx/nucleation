# LIT-tmpufhcl: Real patterns, the predictive mind, and the cognitive construction of the manifest image

Gładziejewski, P. (2025). Real patterns, the predictive mind, and the cognitive construction of the manifest image. *Synthese* 206(5): 225. doi:10.1007/s11229-025-05311-0.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp220h1](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp220h1.md).*

- **Inference is compression.** With optimal codes, maximising the
  posterior is minimising total description length, so "Bayesian inference
  is real-pattern extraction" (p. 8). Variational free energy's complexity
  and accuracy terms are analogous to the model and data terms of minimum
  description length (p. 10). The first is a derivation, the second an
  analogy.
- **The manifest image is structure in the senses.** Generative models
  "specify probabilistic and causal structural descriptions of sensory
  patterns" (p. 12). They are constrained by the signal except in off-line
  simulation such as imagery or dreams (p. 13).
- **Veridical in the vat.** Matched sensory streams compress alike, so an
  envatted twin's door is "as real as the door you open" (p. 13). What
  differs, the realizer, lies outside the manifest image (p. 14). This
  follows only if manifest content is wholly structural.
- **The self is a compression.** The self-model "achieves compression by
  positing a single, stable entity as the common cause of many endogenous
  patterns" (p. 16). Real as a pattern, unreal as a substance. "The
  conflict between those positions is apparent", and it lies in the
  criteria of reality (p. 17).
- **Dissociation as worse compression.** Some dissociative states may be
  better compressed by a model positing two or more narrative selves
  (footnote 10, p. 17).
- **Ordinary objects.** On Bird's (2023) compression criterion a cat
  composes and "whiskers + crust + dust" does not, so perception is
  evidence for ordinary objects (§4.2).
- **Its limits.** A sketch, by its own account, and conditional on a
  realist reading of predictive processing (footnote 2).

## Why this is here

The owner's exchange of 2026-10-21, "Experience and interpretation", asked whether experience is constituted by interpretation or produced by it. The exchange cited this paper for joining real patterns to predictive processing and compression, and for its treatment of the self as a real pattern whose representation can exaggerate its unity. Bears on [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md).

**Correction from the reading.** The first half of that description is
right. The second is not in the paper. It never says the self's
representation exaggerates its unity. It says the self-model compresses by
positing one common cause, and that whether the result is a real self
depends on which criterion of reality one adopts. For [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) this cuts
both ways. It supports the claim's split between a real pattern and a
constructed single owner. But it makes the single owner a lossy
compression rather than a misdescription, which is the Drayson objection
[CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) already names. Its one case of a single-self model going wrong
is footnote 10's dissociation. The paper does not mention consciousness,
illusion or collectives.
