# CLAIM-012: Will is a second- or higher-order attractor in an agent's dynamics

## The claim

Will as a higher-order attractor: a stable regime that governs which
first-order regimes become operative, connected to the synchronization and
entrainment dynamics of the earlier sessions (U34).

## Why it was superseded

It is the claim [CLAIM-010](CLAIM-010.md) objects to, and the fix that lasted was not to
answer that objection but to change the claim: index it to an agent, and make
its object the organization of efficacy ([CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md)).
