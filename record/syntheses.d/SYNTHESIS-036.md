# SYNTHESIS-036: Which of an agent's available regimes becomes effective is decided by maintained relations of efficacy that are not further preferences and are indexed to an agent rather than to any regulator; precedence, jurisdiction and suspension among them vary separately; and whether describing them as one organization explains anything that the fields studying each realizer do not is open

*A part account of the will-organization line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)). The line's account is built over it and five sibling parts. "The will paper" is the owner's essay, The Organization of Will (the `works` vocabulary), cited in components by section.*

## The account

**The thesis and what it rests on.** The paper asks its question in its own
words ([QUESTION-015](../questions.d/QUESTION-015.md), Answered): given an independently warranted agent with
several available regimes, what maintained relations govern which becomes
effective. [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) (thesis, interpretive, Active since the status review
of 2026-10-28) answers it: accessibility, precedence, persistence,
jurisdiction, suspension and transformation, "not reducible to the
contents of the competing regimes, whatever mechanisms realize them". It
rests on [CLAIM-047](../claims.d/CLAIM-047.md), that possessing an action-generating regime ([TERM-019](../terms.d/TERM-019.md))
is not its being effective, which grounds on Frankfurt's unwilling addict
([CASE-181](../cases.d/CASE-181.md)), and on [CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md), the supposition that scopes the paper: the
agent is given, and the account asks how its efficacy is organized.
[TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) states the organization those relations make up. [QUESTION-062](../questions.d/QUESTION-062.md) and
[QUESTION-066](../questions.d/QUESTION-066.md) refine [QUESTION-015](../questions.d/QUESTION-015.md) with readings no other question placed.

Three lines of support conclude [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), all Proposed: the reluctant
writer's precommitment device ([ARG-005](../arguments.d/ARG-005.md), from [CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md)), which "does not
rest on the supposition that a firm is an agent"; Northstar-A against
Northstar-B ([ARG-009](../arguments.d/ARG-009.md), from [CASE-003](../cases.d/CASE-003.md), a variant of [CASE-023](../cases.d/CASE-023.md)), conditional on
[CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md); and mind-wandering read through Metzinger ([ARG-051](../arguments.d/ARG-051.md)), which would
give suspension in one mind and is held back by two unanswered questions.
[QUESTION-367](../questions.d/QUESTION-367.md), filed from the same readings of Metzinger, asks whether an
agent's representation of its own capacity to control a process is a
condition of that capacity's efficacy; [QUESTION-407](../questions.d/QUESTION-407.md) is a share-of-time
clause moved off one of them ([THEORY-1297](../theory.d/THEORY-1297.md)), and no component cites it.

**Agent-indexing answers permissiveness.** The account first stated will as a
higher-order attractor ([CLAIM-012](../claims.d/CLAIM-012.md), [TERM-003](../terms.d/TERM-003.md), both Superseded by [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)
and [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)). [CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md), the permissiveness objection (Rejected), objects
to [CLAIM-012](../claims.d/CLAIM-012.md) and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md); its summary says it is "Answered by

objects to it: regulation, even higher-order, does not establish a will,
with the thermostat and adaptive controller ([CASE-034](../cases.d/CASE-034.md)) as illustration. The
owner's reply that every such regulator may count ([CLAIM-062](../claims.d/CLAIM-062.md)) is

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim.

- **Sen's paradox and the redescription objection.** That a will-organization
  meeting [CLAIM-353](../claims.d/CLAIM-353.md)'s conditions has no aggregate revealed preference, so
  [CLAIM-089](../claims.d/CLAIM-089.md) is unavailable for it. Held in substance: [CLAIM-482](../claims.d/CLAIM-482.md) says the
  objection is "unavailable for one whose record cycles", and [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) of
  organizational-affect says Sen's theorem and [CLAIM-353](../claims.d/CLAIM-353.md) show a negotiation
  "need not produce an ordering". The remainder is a composition of the two.
- **Distribution and the paradox.** That of [CLAIM-410](../claims.d/CLAIM-410.md)'s eliminated,
  distributed and transferred authority only distribution carries
  [CLAIM-353](../claims.d/CLAIM-353.md)'s impossibility. Trivial once "distributed" is closed to
  pairwise decisiveness, which [CLAIM-353](../claims.d/CLAIM-353.md)'s own edge leaves open; Sen's
  theorem already needs two decisive parties.
