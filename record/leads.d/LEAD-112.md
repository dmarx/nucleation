# LEAD-112: Find cases where access and experience come apart and each kind of evidence follows one of them: superblindsight, inconspicuous rivalry recorded intracranially, a marker accepted in advance

## What it would do

Search the literature for each kind of evidence (report, no-report,
deficit, architectural) for a case where access and experience dissociate,
and register what is found.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md): "Superblindsight, inconspicuous rivalry recorded
  intracranially, or a marker both sides accept in advance would each do
  this."
- [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md): "A recording in which one content is available to one
  consumer and demonstrably absent from another at the same time"

## What would count

A case for each kind answers [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md) and settles what [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) and
[CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md) need from it. Finding none for some kind says that kind cannot
tell access from experience.
