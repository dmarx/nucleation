# LEAD-tmp9snzi: Run the Luria study under rising automation: periodically make the inherited ontology or evaluation criteria inadequate, and measure whether the human–AI collective still recognizes the inadequacy, authorizes, implements and stays ready for the next revision

## The plan (A4 §15)

"The central outcome would not be how well the collective performs under
its existing standards. It would be whether it retains the ability to
recognize the inadequacy of those standards, authorize revisions,
implement them, and remain ready for further revisions."

A4's table of questions and measures, kept as A's:

| Experimental question | Possible operational measure |
|---|---|
| Does automation preserve human governing capacity? | Ability to independently identify and correct consequential errors |
| Does externalizing governance improve it? | Correction quality, time and cost with infrastructure |
| Can governance survive component replacement? | Performance after changing models, operators or tools |
| Does governance become self-confirming? | Divergence between internal evaluations and independently measured outcomes |
| Does the system remain correctable over time? | Readiness for repeated, unanticipated revisions |

## Against the record

- The four outcomes are [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s three achievements (a discrepancy made
  legible, given authority, acted on) plus the readiness for the next one,
  which is [TERM-167](../terms.d/TERM-167.md)'s second-order kernel; the plan measures them for the
  humans separately from the collective, which is what [LEAD-254](LEAD-254.md)'s
  actor-indexing asks to be defined.
- [CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md)'s outcome, "a justified correction without material
  regressions within budget", is the collective's; the new outcome is the
  human members' share of it, the constitutional capability of
  [TERM-tmp35z0s](../terms.d/TERM-tmp35z0s.md).

## What it needs before it is specified

- automation levels that a Luria collective can actually run;
- a measure of the humans' unassisted ability to identify and correct an
  error, taken with the automation withdrawn, as [QUESTION-504](../questions.d/QUESTION-504.md) says a joint
  score cannot show it;
- inadequacies injected without the participants' knowledge of when, so
  that recognition is measured, not only response.
