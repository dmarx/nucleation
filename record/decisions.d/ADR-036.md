# ADR-036: learning-theory widens to cover training dynamics

## Context

The topics vocabulary in `luria.yaml` is closed, and a word it lacks is added
by decision, with a label and a blurb. `learning-theory`'s blurb read:
"why learning generalizes — compression and description length, Kolmogorov
complexity and sufficient statistics, rate–distortion and PAC-style bounds,
model selection".

On 2026-10-09 the record took in a run of papers whose subject is how
networks learn rather than why they generalize: exact and closed-form
training dynamics ([LIT-855](../literature.d/LIT-855.md), [LIT-859](../literature.d/LIT-859.md), [LIT-862](../literature.d/LIT-862.md)), grokking ([LIT-858](../literature.d/LIT-858.md)), the
statistical physics of attention ([LIT-857](../literature.d/LIT-857.md)) and topological change during
training ([LIT-861](../literature.d/LIT-861.md)). Five readers reported the same gap. Each tagged the work
`learning-theory` as the nearest fit and said the blurb did not cover it.
Older entries had done the same ([LIT-370](../literature.d/LIT-370.md), [LIT-242](../literature.d/LIT-242.md)).

## Decision

`learning-theory` keeps its label and widens its blurb to:

> why learning generalizes and how it proceeds — compression and description
> length, Kolmogorov complexity and sufficient statistics, rate–distortion
> and PAC-style bounds, model selection; and training dynamics: solvable
> models of learning trajectories, stages, plateaus and phase transitions in
> training, and the implicit bias of optimisation. A work whose subject is
> how to train a model better is ML practice and goes to the anthology

The entries already tagged this way stand as tagged. No retagging is needed.

## Alternatives

- **A separate `training-dynamics` word.** Offered to the owner, who chose
  widening instead. The works
  sit on both sides at once: implicit bias explains generalization through
  the dynamics, and grokking is a generalization result reached through a
  trajectory. Two words would split one literature.
- **Leave the blurb.** Rejected: the guard catching the same gap five times
  in a day is a bug report about the vocabulary, not about the readers.

## Consequences

Readers filing training-dynamics work now have a word that says what they
mean. The boundary with the anthology is unchanged: theory of how learning
proceeds is held here; advice on training is the anthology's
(`training-optimization`).
