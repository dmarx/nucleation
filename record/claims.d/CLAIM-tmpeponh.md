# CLAIM-tmpeponh: Fidelity is always fidelity with respect to some selected structure: a specified family of communicative observations, interventions and permitted continuations

## The claim

A35 §8: "**Fidelity is always fidelity with respect to some selected
structure.** The dynamical account doesn't eliminate the problem of selecting
invariants. It gives us a more expressive language for formulating and
investigating that selection." A38 §6.4, the third proposition: "**Pragmatic
translation fidelity can be defined relative to a selected observational and
interactional equivalence relation**", with "different translation purposes
justify different equivalence strengths". A40 §7.5: "**Pragmatic fidelity is
relative to a specified family of communicative observations, interventions, and
permitted continuations.**"

It is the abstract form of what the mother's translation did to [CLAIM-tmp5yknw](CLAIM-tmp5yknw.md).

## Why it was replaced

The manuscript sharpens "selected structure" into decision problems: fidelity
relative to the decisions the receiver must make, compared by Blackwell's order
([CLAIM-tmpek80j](CLAIM-tmpek80j.md)), with "no universal choice of weights or observational tasks" (§6) and
the choice "itself normative and empirically contestable" (§11).
