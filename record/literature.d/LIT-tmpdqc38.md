# LIT-tmpdqc38: A soliton on a vortex filament

Hasimoto, H. (1972). A soliton on a vortex filament. *Journal of Fluid Mechanics* 51(3): 477–485. doi:10.1017/S0022112072002307.

## Key takeaways

*From the abstract only; the body is unread.*

- The intrinsic equation for the curvature K and torsion τ of "an isolated
  very thin vortex filament without stretching in an incompressible
  inviscid fluid is reduced to a non-linear Schrödinger equation", for the
  complex variable ψ = κ exp(i∫₀ˢ τ ds).
- That equation "yields a solution describing the propagation of a loop or
  a hump of helical motion along a line vortex, with a constant velocity
  2τ".
- "The relation to the system of intrinsic equations derived by Betchov
  (1965) is discussed."

## Standing in the record

Filed from the exchange of 2026-11-15 on solitons in a continuous medium.
The exchange's characterization holds in substance against the abstract.
Two of its points are its own: that the hump travels "while preserving its
characteristic shape", which the abstract does not say, and the two-level
hierarchy (continuous field, string-like structure, soliton on the
structure). In the model only the second level is derived. The filament is
a given thin line vortex treated as a curve.

How far the neighbour stands in:

- **Van Gorder (2014)**, LIT-tmpezzs5, read in part. It names the
  approximation (local induction) and uses the same transformation. Its
  zero-friction limit is the cubic NLS. Its standing sech soliton
  "corresponds to the Hasimoto soliton" as the friction vanishes.
- **Not carried by anything read**: the travelling hump with velocity 2τ,
  the explicit filament shape, Hasimoto's treatment of Betchov, and
  anything he says on stability or collisions. Van Gorder reports, without
  being read for it here, that Hopfinger and Browand (1982) observed the
  predicted solutions in experiments.
