### Changed

- A synthesis now states its scope's argument as one argument, in prose,
  and no longer lists its components with their standings. An exchange
  gets no synthesis of its own: it is taken into account by revising the
  syntheses of the lines it advanced. A manuscript argues for a reader
  outside the record, with the record's apparatus in its README
  (ADR-tmpw306d). The scheme's description in luria.yaml and the
  synthesis-review, read-and-file and write-manuscript skills are revised.
  First applications:
  - SYNTHESIS-114, version 2;
  - SYNTHESIS-tmpg0a57, the first synthesis of the line
    scholarly-publishing, which takes "Hyper article interpretation" into
    account;
  - governance-of-possibility's Part A.
