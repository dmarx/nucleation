# LIT-252: Spectral Inference Networks: Unifying Deep and Spectral Learning

David Pfau, Stig Petersen, Ashish Agarwal, David G. T. Barrett, Kimberly L. Stachenfeld (2018), *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2019); first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-1806.02215](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.02215)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-824](../notes.d/NOTE-824.md).*

- **The objective.** For a "symmetric (not necessarily positive definite)
  kernel" and ⟨f, g⟩ = E_p[fg], the top-N eigenspace of K maximizes
  Tr(E[uuᵀ]⁻¹ E[k(x,x′)u(x)u(x′)ᵀ]), equivalently Tr(Π) with Σ = I
  (Eqs. 6–7). This is Edelman et al.'s matrix result carried to function
  spaces by analogy. It fixes only the span.
- **Ordering.** Masking the gradient of each Λ_ii = (L⁻¹ΠL⁻ᵀ)_ii to its
  own output, and reading off v = L⁻¹u, is claimed to give ordered
  eigenfunctions. The authors say the argument is "not meant as
  mathematically rigorous proofs" (App. A.2).
- **Bias.** The gradient is nonlinear in Σ, so minibatch estimates are
  biased; moving averages of Σ and its Jacobian make training a
  two-timescale bilevel problem. The Jacobian costs about N² backward
  passes per step.
- **SFA.** With the graph-Laplacian kernel on adjacent frames, the
  objective is Slow Feature Analysis (Eq. 8, after Sprekeler 2011).
- **Evidence.** The 2-D hydrogen atom's eigenvalues are recovered with the
  bias correction and missed without it. The video and Atari features are
  judged by eye.
- **Symmetry.** It is a precondition on k. The SFA kernel is symmetric by
  the form (u(x) − u(x′))(u(x) − u(x′))ᵀ, and the remark that reversible
  dynamics make the transition operator symmetric (§5.2) is an aside.

kernel. No THEORY was filed: the paper's mathematical content is Edelman

algorithm. What it does with a non-symmetric kernel, the question the
seed asked, the paper does not say. Johnson et al. ([LIT-258](LIT-258.md), App. E.2)
name it as one way to extract the eigenfunctions of K⁺, and Balestriero
and LeCun ([LIT-228](LIT-228.md)) name it as "a perhaps more direct connection".
