# THEORY-tmpf3zwk: Tomasello argues that infants and great apes pass implicit false-belief tasks by tracking what others see and know, a capacity evolved for competition, whereas children come to understand false belief at about four to five by coordinating conflicting perspectives, including an objective one, in the cooperative interaction and communication of shared intentionality

## The account

Tracking what others see and know is old and serves competition;
understanding belief requires coordinating one's own, another's and an
objective perspective, which children learn in joint attention and
conversation, where partners want to be understood. Three-year-olds have
an emerging objective perspective but coordinate it poorly.

## Evidence

Theoretical synthesis of developmental and comparative studies.
