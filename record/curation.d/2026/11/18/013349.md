On 2026-11-17 the owner asked to "launch readers for the deferred works":
the 85 works registered unread from the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(pull request 512). Ten readers, briefed with the read-and-file skill's
reader's brief and ADR-074's list of lawful copies, drafted one report per
work in the scratchpad. Four filers then verified the quotes against the
downloaded texts and filed the readings. Work stopped once on the usage
limit and resumed from the same state.

  honestly) and a Proposed THEORY whose `promote_when` asks only for what

  reading, with the reading's verdict on how the exchange described it.

## Copies admitted by decision

Two copies fit none of ADR-074's kinds:

- Strawson's "Freedom and Resentment", from a scan its host says is
  "published with permission of the British Academy and Galen Strawson".
- Scanlon's "Reply to Leif Wenar", posted by Wenar beside his own article.

The owner ruled on 2026-11-18 that "both permission copies count as
lawful" and asked for the skill to be updated by an ADR. ADR-tmp976n0
adds the two kinds. The read-and-file skill's step 5 carries them, and the
two LITs and NOTEs cite the decision.

## What the readings changed

- **The exchange's characterizations.** Most were faithful. The
  corrections are recorded in each LIT. Among them:
  - Johnson's 1995 term was "patriarchal terrorism", not "intimate
    terrorism", and nothing read confirms his "mutual violent control".
  - The NTSB made the operator's distraction *the* probable cause, with
    Uber's failures contributing.
  - Hohfeld counts eight conceptions, not four positions.
  - Cuéllar is in the *Yale Law Journal Forum*.
  - "Answerability" means a capacity in Shoemaker and a stage of
    accountability in Bovens, both unlike TERM-tmp4zjar's directed
    relation. The notes on the term record both homonyms.
  - Shue's triad is avoidance, protection and aid, not "respect, protect
    and fulfil" (a note on TERM-085).
- **Relations written on the THEORYs.** Readings support claims
  independently (`corroborates`, ADR-058):

  - Elish → CLAIM-tmpb47ul;
  - Citron → CLAIM-tmph28l6 and CLAIM-410;

## Support judgements

Three readings show that a claim cited a work for more than the work
carries. Each is load-bearing (the claim names the work), so each got an
ARG (Proposed):

- ARG-tmpgjuto: Keller's teachers case does not instance CLAIM-tmpciy9q.
  The teachers do no wrong, and the anticipated publicity is a smear.
- ARG-tmpkf20b: Grossman and Hart carry CLAIM-tmpdic1g's definition, not
  unilateral decision or control seen only at the contingency.
- ARG-tmpttrcb: Locke carries CLAIM-tmp6vck6's form, not its general
  ground, and admits forfeiture.

Not filed as ARGs, because the claims do not cite the works:

- Harsey and Freyd on CLAIM-tmpciy9q: DARVO casts the accusation as false.
- Lohmann et al. on CLAIM-tmpkivlz: the associations are cross-sectional.
- Miller and Eggert on CLAIM-tmpa2l21.

The dated notes record each of them.

## For the follow-up synthesis review

The readings add corroborating THEORYs to most of the six new part
accounts' claims, and three ARGs that narrow claims in the exploitation,
restriction and rights parts. The follow-up review of
dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity should fold them
in before a line or a work is proposed.
