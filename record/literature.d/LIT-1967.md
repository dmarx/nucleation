# LIT-1967: We know this much is (meta-analytically) true: A meta-review of meta-analytic findings evaluating self-determination theory.

Ryan, R. M., Duineveld, J. J., Di Domenico, S. I., Ryan, W. S., Steward, B. A., Bradshaw, E. L. (2022). We know this much is (meta-analytically) true: A meta-review of meta-analytic findings evaluating self-determination theory.. *Psychological Bulletin, 148(11-12), 813-842*. doi:10.1037/bul0000385.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-1500](../notes.d/NOTE-1500.md)), from the accepted manuscript.

- 60 meta-analyses, sorted by mini-theory (Table 1) and domain (Table 2);
  relationships motivation theory has none.
- The continuum of regulation is supported as an ordering, but integrated
  regulation is not distinguished from identified (Howard et al. 2017, as
  reported; [LIT-1996](LIT-1996.md) is unread).
- Need satisfaction correlates with well-being across cultures; the link
  of need frustration to ill-being "has yet to be meta-analytically
  investigated" ([THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md)).
- Mostly cross-sectional; heterogeneity high and "far from" explained.
- Written by the theory's co-founder; several included meta-analyses are
  the authors' own. [THEORY-1327](../theory.d/THEORY-1327.md).

## Standing in the record

A meta-review of SDT's meta-analyses, the broadest test of the theory's evidence. The record's SDT readings ([LIT-558](LIT-558.md) to [LIT-561](LIT-561.md)) are the theory's own statements; this is the nearest thing to an audit of them.
