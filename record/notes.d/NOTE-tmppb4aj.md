# NOTE-tmppb4aj: Nicol J — Depp II v News Group Newspapers Ltd & Anor [2020] EWHC 2911 (QB)

## Contribution

To the record this is a primary source: a court's findings of fact on
each pleaded allegation, with its reasons, made to the civil standard in a
libel action to which Ms Heard was not a party (para 576). What the court
decided was whether the published meaning was substantially true; it made
no finding about the relationship's structure of power as such.

## Key insight

The court reached a verdict on the published meaning by deciding the
pleaded acts one at a time and then standing back to view the evidence as
a whole.

## Assumptions (the court's framework)

- The civil standard, more probable than not, with the evidence required
  rising with the seriousness of the allegation (paras 41–42).
- The pleadings fix what must be decided; matters not pleaded, such as
  the Bahamas in December 2015, were not resolved (paras 457–458).
- A point not put to a witness in cross-examination generally cannot be
  relied on against them (para 404), which decided Incident 11.

## Key results (the court's findings)

- **Standard** (para 41): "is it more likely than not that the claimant
  did what the articles alleged?"; (para 42) "the more serious the
  allegation ... the stronger must be the evidence".
- **Meaning** (para 80): "The Claimant had committed physical violence
  against Ms Heard", causing "significant injury" and "On occasion ...
  fear for her life".
- **Method** (para 107): "I shall then consider individually each of the
  incidents ... I shall then step back".
- **Records** (para 204): "neither Mr Depp, nor Ms Heard has any previous
  convictions for violence".
- **Recorded admissions** (para 175): "no great weight is to be put on
  these alleged admissions by Ms Heard to aggressive violent behaviour".
- **Incident 1** (para 210): "Seen in isolation, the evidence ... might not
  be sufficient. However, taken with the evidence as a whole, I find that
  it did occur."
- **Incident 5** (para 274): "more likely than not that Mr Depp did push Ms
  Heard on at least one occasion ... I am not able to conclude whether
  there was more than this one assault."
- **Incident 6** (para 278): "I am not persuaded that Incident 6
  constituted a physical assault".
- **Incident 8** (para 370): "she was the victim of sustained and multiple
  assaults by Mr Depp in Australia ... I accept that Mr Depp put her in
  fear of her life"; "I do not accept that Ms Heard was responsible for
  the injury to Mr Depp's finger". The court called her "hostage
  situation" description "something of a hyperbole".
- **Incident 9** (para 386): "there was violence on both sides"; "As she
  admitted, she did punch him, but I accept that was in defence of her
  sister".
- **Incident 11** (para 405): "the omission to put this incident to Mr Depp
  means that I do not accept that it is proven."
- **Incident 13** (para 476): "I do not accept that Ms Heard assaulted Mr
  Depp on 21st April 2016."
- **Incident 14** (para 573): an assault found "despite the testimony of
  the witnesses who I have previously listed", on contemporaneous accounts
  and photographs, one of which "could not have been the product of later
  manufacture".
- **Overall** (para 575): "the great majority of alleged assaults ... have
  been proved to the civil standard ... The exceptions are Incidents 6, 11
  and the additional confidential allegation regarding Hicksville."
- **Hoax** (para 578): the court rejected "the 'hoax' or 'insurance
  policy' thesis".
- **Disposition** (paras 583–585): the words were "substantially true in
  the meanings I have held them to bear"; "this claim is dismissed"; the
  court had examined "in detail the 14 incidents on which the Defendants
  rely".

## Concepts

Defence of truth (s.2 Defamation Act 2013); substantial truth of a
meaning; Chase levels of meaning; burden on the defendant; the civil
standard with the Re D and Hunt gloss; the global "step back"; the
good-character direction; hearsay weight; a complainant who is not a
party.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CASE-279](../cases.d/CASE-279.md)** (Carol and Dan). Left open. The judgment is what A
  abstracts from, but its findings concern pleaded assaults and the truth
  of a meaning; it makes no finding on who controlled finances, isolation
  or the relationship's terms, which the case stipulates. Its Incident 9
  finding of "violence on both sides", with Ms Heard's admitted punch
  accepted as in defence of her sister, is the only finding near the
  case's premise that the dominated partner may also strike.
- **[CLAIM-709](../claims.d/CLAIM-709.md)** (who harmed whom and who had power are separate
  questions). The court's method answers the first question only, act by
  act, and the published meaning is about acts and injury (para 80). That
  shows an act-by-act verdict reached without deciding the allocation of
  power; it does not test the claim's other half. Not filed as a
  relation.
- **[LIT-2715](../literature.d/LIT-2715.md)** (Johnson's typology) and **[LIT-2675](../literature.d/LIT-2675.md)** (Stark,
  coercive control) classify the kind of facts found here; neither is
  read.
- The judgment cannot ground anything about what the US jury found in
  2022, and says nothing of any appeal.

## Verdict on the exchange's characterization

Faithful. A2: a British judge "examined 14 alleged incidents of Depp
physically assaulting Heard and found 12 established on the balance of
probabilities". Para 575 names the exceptions as Incidents 6 and 11 (and a
confidential sub-allegation). Two precisions: Incident 5 was found as "at
least one" push, and Incident 1 only "taken with the evidence as a whole".
"Depp's attempt to appeal was unsuccessful" is not in this judgment.

## Limitations

The Confidential Annexe was not read, so the confidential parts of
Incidents 3 and 8 are known only by their outcome. The findings bind
within this action and its pleadings only. The appeal history is not in
this document.
