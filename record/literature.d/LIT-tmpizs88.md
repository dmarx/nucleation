# LIT-tmpizs88: Hypervigilance and depression as predictors of eye tracking to ambiguous pictures in trauma survivors

Kimble, Cappello and Fleming (2023), *International Journal of
Psychophysiology* 187: 27–33. DOI 10.1016/j.ijpsycho.2023.01.007.

## Standing in the record

Registered for [LEAD-106](../leads.d/LEAD-106.md) as the nearest study of how trauma survivors scan
ambiguous scenes. Its direction, more scanning with more hypervigilance,
is against the attenuation [QUESTION-303](../questions.d/QUESTION-303.md) asks about, but it has no recall
manipulation, no group design and no measure of disambiguating periphery.
The scoping review [LIT-tmpogxg7](LIT-tmpogxg7.md) does not include it.
