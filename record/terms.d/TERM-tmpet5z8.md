# TERM-tmpet5z8: meme

## Definition

Three senses, which the exchange moves between and which should be named
when the word is used:

1. **Replicator.** A1, after Dawkins (1976; [LIT-tmpjl855](../literature.d/LIT-tmpjl855.md)): "a unit of
   cultural information that spreads from one individual to another through
   imitation, learning, or communication", whose success A1 gives by
   Dawkins's three replicator properties, "Longevity", "Fecundity" and
   "Copying fidelity". A1 adds the standard objection: "cultural
   information is difficult to divide into discrete replicating units, and
   ideas are frequently reconstructed rather than faithfully copied." The
   record holds the reconstruction side as [TERM-027](TERM-027.md) and [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md).
2. **Seed.** The owner's U2, "memes as nucleation sites" ([CLAIM-tmp1ng5k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1ng5k.md)).
   A2: "A meme need not contain all the information necessary to construct
   a worldview. It only needs to provide a structure around which existing
   information can assemble." A4's reframing: "A meme can function as a
   transmissible seed through which an environment acquires new
   organizational constraints, without the seed itself specifying,
   containing, or autonomously maintaining the resulting organization."
3. **Expression of character.** The owner's U4, "memes as characteristics
   (qua representation theory) of perspectives", filed in two readings
   ([CLAIM-tmphi3mx](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmphi3mx.md), [CLAIM-tmp4mk0t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4mk0t.md)). A5's development ([CLAIM-tmpjitli](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjitli.md)): a
   meme as "a transportable expression of character that also participates
   in the realization of that character".

## Roles

A4 §2 lists roles a meme can play in an organization, "The same meme may
occupy different roles at different times":

- **seed**, "initiating organization";
- **scaffold**, "temporarily supporting organization" (the record's
  transient support, [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md));
- **constraint**, "participating in ongoing organization";
- **product**, "regenerated by the organization" ([CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md)'s second
  construction, where the connector is "maintained by the processes it
  enables");
- **carrier**, "through which organization is reconstituted elsewhere".

A5 §4 adds three that "need not coincide": **diagnostic** (a probe that
"Reveals characteristic aspects of an existing perspective"; [CLAIM-142](../claims.d/CLAIM-142.md)
bounds what a probe family can show), **constitutive** and **nucleating**.

## What it is not

- Not an internet meme, which A1 calls "merely a subset".
- Not a presupposed individual. A4 §1 and the closing of A4: "I would
  investigate **memeticity as a relational organizational role**". Whether
  the symbol, the concept, the perspective or the lineage is what persists
  is [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md)'s and [TERM-088](TERM-088.md)'s question, and [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), [CLAIM-420](../claims.d/CLAIM-420.md) and
  [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md) already separate them.
- Not a cultural attractor. A4 §7: "A meme need not be an attractor to
  nucleate an attractor." The contrast is in [TERM-027](TERM-027.md)'s note of
  2026-11-24.

## Why it is proposed

The exchange's argument ([CLAIM-tmpgvsgg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgvsgg.md), [CLAIM-tmpjitli](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjitli.md), [CLAIM-tmpjlxfy](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjlxfy.md))
turns on moving from sense 1 to senses 2 and 3, and an entry that says
"meme" without the sense cannot be checked against them.
