# SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj: A collective that replaces all its members, or a self that goes inactive, persists on the record's account by causal transmission of the organization that produces it or by the capacity to reinstate it, not by resemblance or a running quantity: persistence has senses that come apart, its successors can be plural without identity branching, what must be transmitted is still open, a slow variable belongs to a collective's dynamics only when its members read and regenerate it, and a feature recovered from members' activity is at most a constituent of a perspective

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the part on what persists
when a collective replaces its members or a self goes inactive, how the
organization that produces a collective is transmitted, and how higher-level
perspectives would be identified from members' activity. It was filed from
the exchanges on sports fandom (2026-10-16), of 2026-10-17, "Graph Learning Applications" (2026-10-22), "Experimental
Infrastructure Review" (2026-10-26), "Assess SDT Coverage" (2026-11-02) and
"Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07), among
others. The line's account is built over it and the line's other parts. The
mechanical map put six entries on world-modeling in this part; they barely
relate to the rest (two linked pairs), and they have their own part account, [SYNTHESIS-tmptxoa2](SYNTHESIS-tmptxoa2.md). Several entries here are integrated also by
accounts of other lines, said where below; this account integrates them
again because their `line` includes distributed-agency.*

## The account

- **The criterion: transmission, not resemblance.** [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) (A's, from A5,
  A8 and A9 of the exchange of 2026-10-16; interpretive, Proposed): a
  collective that replaces all its members persists, when it does, "by
  causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by
  resemblance". Of A9's three persistences, state, dynamical and historical,
  the claim "is that the third is what persistence through total turnover
  consists in". Against resemblance: "Reproducing a kind is not persisting as
  an individual." Against conservation alone, [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md) "is about persisting
  as the same self and says nothing against this; the two are tests of
  different persistences". Its note of 2026-10-22 limits it: "the claim's
  criterion is one for a carrier's persistence, and a perspective carried
  needs its own ([CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md))". Its note of 2026-10-30 says what holds it
  Proposed: without an answer to [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) it "cannot tell continuation
  from causal descent where every candidate is causally continuous".
- **Product and producer.** [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) (A8 on the owner's r/place case;
  interpretive, Active): a pattern can persist while every constituent is
  replaced, so "the persistence of a product and the persistence of the
  organization producing it are separate facts". It grounds on [CASE-006](../cases.d/CASE-006.md) (A31's
  fictional cooperative, Active), "persistence through turnover with the
  founding intentions lost", which substitutes for the owner's [CASE-028](../cases.d/CASE-028.md)
  (Superseded, not integrated). [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md): "This claim says what the
  producer's own persistence is when its members are all replaced." [CASE-102](../cases.d/CASE-102.md)
  (the owner's college program, stipulated, Active): "It is [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md)'s
  product and producer again, with the producer more nearly observable".
- **A self through inactivity.** [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md) (dual-filed from the
  mathematics-of-meaning record; interpretive, Proposed): a system persists
  as the same self when a coherence measure γ is approximately conserved
  along its own trajectory; it stays Proposed "because everything rests on
  γ". [QUESTION-043](../questions.d/QUESTION-043.md) (Open) asks which γ for a person, and whether a life
  story is a candidate. [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md) (A6 and A10; interpretive, Active): what
  persists through inactivity "is the capacity to reinstate an organization,
  not its continuous running", and that continuity "can branch and merge, so
  it is not an identity relation". It qualifies [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md): "Along a
  trajectory with a gap, what is conserved is the capacity to reinstate an
  organization, not any running quantity." [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md)'s note of 2026-10-30
  says the same, and that its title "does not yet say so".
- **The senses, and descent.** [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md) (the owner adopted it, Active) lists
  an organization's persistence as state, dynamical, historical, viable and
  reinstatable, "and a claim should say which"; it declines "reconstitution"
  as a name because "independent reconstitution" is what [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) holds
  is not continuation. [TERM-084](../terms.d/TERM-084.md) (the owner adopted it, Active) separates
  provenance, lineage, common ancestry and causal descent; lineage is
  "[TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md)'s historical persistence, and what [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) says persistence
  through total turnover consists in". [CLAIM-420](../claims.d/CLAIM-420.md) (the owner's U38 of
  2026-10-22, corrected by A38; interpretive, Active): a figure's influence
  persists as story, transmitted perspective or descent, and "causal descent
  is not perspectival inheritance". It is "[CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) applied to one life and
  sharpened at one point": "causal connection alone is not [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s
  criterion". [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md) (A3 and A4 of 2026-10-17, rechecked; construction,
  Active) dissociates slowness, member regeneration and transmission; its
  arm C, a rule passed through an archive, "is [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md)'s dormant
  coalition" and "[CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s 'a lapse in its coherence need not end it'",
  and A4: "causal transmission alone does not settle whether A and B are
  numerically the same collective."
- **Plural succession.** [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md) (A4's H5 in its weak form, held at the
  owner's word; interpretive, Active): when bundled continuities separate,
  "several later collectives can each be a genuine successor of the earlier
  one in a different respect", without identity branching. It grounds on
  [CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md) (the owner's subcase, corrected by A2; documented, Active), and
  answers [QUESTION-290](../questions.d/QUESTION-290.md) (Answered). It leaves to [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) "Which successor
  relation, if any, is persistence", and reads [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s criterion as "the
  interpersonal respect". [CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md): "If that claim counts as continuation,
  transmission is not necessary", of the 2005 Nationals' claim on the city's
  history. [CASE-133](../cases.d/CASE-133.md) (the owner's U29 and U30, in A29's corrected form;
  documented, Active) illustrates [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) and [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md): "Realignment is
  the case where the organization was transmitted and its perspectives were
  only partly".
- **Succession at a change of steward.** [CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md) (the owner's design at U16,
  A's arms and measures; proposed study, Proposed) compares contested with
  accepted successions, with "the delay between a loss of recognized
  legitimacy and a loss of productive capability" as the key measure, and
  gives the reader's verdict that [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) picks Libera in the Freenode case. [CLAIM-617](../claims.d/CLAIM-617.md) (A's,
  the defeat condition the reader's; interpretive, Proposed): participants'
  verdicts are "causally productive of that succession"; its summary: "the
  verdict moves the producers, and under [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) moving the producers moves
  the persistence". [ARG-067](../arguments.d/ARG-067.md) (the assistant's wording; evidence, Proposed)
  concludes [CLAIM-617](../claims.d/CLAIM-617.md) from [CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md) and finds that the instances "do not
  discriminate the claim from [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s criterion alone". [CLAIM-613](../claims.d/CLAIM-613.md) (A's;
  interpretive, Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md) and [CLAIM-047](../claims.d/CLAIM-047.md) and
  complementing [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md): a fork can be "the construction of a new
  organization through which a disposition that the parent's governance
  kept ineffective gains efficacy".
- **Historical structuralism.** [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md) (the filer's construction from A's;
  demonstrated, Active): "Isomorphism is not persistence, in either
  direction". [ARG-075](../arguments.d/ARG-075.md) (the reader's; analogy, Proposed) carries it to
  organized individuals: "the first carries over to organized individuals and
  the second only as consistency". [CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md) (A's integration, much of it the
  owner's U14 to U16, corrected by the filer; interpretive, Proposed), resting
  on [CLAIM-611](../claims.d/CLAIM-611.md), [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md), [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md), [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md) and [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md):
  identity over time is "carried by the causal transmission of the relations
  that keep its repertoire realizable", so "a structuralism about individuals
  has to be historical". Its layer 3, that structure may change, "is the step
  beyond [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), whose case is member replacement". [CLAIM-544](../claims.d/CLAIM-544.md) (the
  reader's draft at the owner's U31; interpretive, Proposed): whether a
  revised jurisdiction continues the earlier willed agent "is a question of
  continuation, not of resemblance"; [CLAIM-544](../claims.d/CLAIM-544.md)'s note of 2026-11-05 says that
  [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md) gives its point "outside agents".
- **What must be transmitted.** [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) (A5 §7, with the branching part
  from A2 and A4; Open) asks "what must stay causally continuous for the
  later organization to be the earlier one persisting", since "any causal
  descendant" is too much. Its notes hold the candidates read: routines
  (Feldman and Pentland), layered imprints (Marquis and Tilcsik), and
  Griesemer and Shavit's organized sample, which "separates descent from
  independent reconstitution" but "does not separate numerical continuation
  from descent". [CASE-148](../cases.d/CASE-148.md) (A4 of 2026-10-23; proposed study, Active):
  "That is [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s causal transmission, broken into its parts, and
  [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) asked as an experiment." [QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md) (the owner's U15 of
  2026-10-31; Open) asks whether recursive production is self-distillation,
  and keeps continuity apart: "A faithful successor is not thereby the
  predecessor continuing. That is [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md)'s question, and fidelity is
  not lineage ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md))." [CLAIM-517](../claims.d/CLAIM-517.md) (A16, with the regularity conditions
  the filer's; demonstrated, Active): a successor regulator close to a
  certifying one at every boundary context keeps a viable set invariant, so
  "fidelity measured in ordinary operation certifies nothing about
  viability"; and "whether it continues the predecessor is [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s
  question".
- **Slow variables and levels.** [CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md) (A9 after Flack; interpretive,
  Proposed): a slowly changing collective variable is part of a collective's
  dynamics "when its members read it, condition what they do on it, and by
  doing so regenerate it". [CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md) (the owner's U8, qualified by A8;
  interpretive, Active): a level has a temporal facet, though "slowness is a
  mark of a level and not its definition". [CASE-098](../cases.d/CASE-098.md) (a rivalry, from the
  owner's U2; stipulated, Active) illustrates [CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md): "a relation between
  two collectives can be continuous while every carrier is replaced", and
  [QUESTION-298](../questions.d/QUESTION-298.md) (Open) asks whether the reciprocity fans perceive in it is
  real. [CASE-103](../cases.d/CASE-103.md) (a fanbase's reputation, from U2; stipulated, Active) is
  one of [CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)'s grounds. [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md)'s arm A shows "slowness is not
  sufficient for endogeneity", and arm B, within A4's limit of weak
  maintenance, that "slowness is not necessary for endogeneity".
  [QUESTION-440](../questions.d/QUESTION-440.md) (A's, from A16; Open) asks whether dynamical slaving can
  realize authority, and lists "Against slowness as authority: [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md)'s
  arm A".
- **Identifying perspectives from members' activity.** [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md) (the
  owner's T18 to T22, with the replies' conditions; Open) asks how
  overlapping higher-level perspectives can be identified from members'
  activity, not inserted, their number not fixed, and told from coactivity a
  common cause produces. [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md) (from the replies T18 to T24;
  interpretive, Active): a latent feature recovered from members' activity
  "is at most a constituent of an organizational perspective"; "It is the
  criterion [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md)'s answer has to meet." It grounds on [CASE-101](../cases.d/CASE-101.md) (A11
  to A13; proposed study, Active), whose common-input arm forms no
  perspective. [CASE-165](../cases.d/CASE-165.md) (the owner's T22 and T23; construction, Active) "is
  the candidate method of [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md) in its most worked-out form", and "its
  §10 division is [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md)'s". [CLAIM-543](../claims.d/CLAIM-543.md) (the owner's U8 of 2026-11-02,
  with A9's correction; interpretive, Active): "a model whose components are
  exchangeable given their weights is organizationally flat however many
  levels its prior has"; it "applies [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md)" to a persona distribution.
  [CLAIM-536](../claims.d/CLAIM-536.md) (the owner's U12, stated precisely; demonstrated, Active): the
  categorical and the LDA-style model differ in where the mixture lives, and
  "Which H2 the record means matters for [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md)'s overlap
  requirement." [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) (Active, at the status review's ruling) gives
  "organization" its four senses; [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md) and [CLAIM-543](../claims.d/CLAIM-543.md) use sense 1.

What stays open is [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md), the criterion of persistence and which
successor relation, if any, is it; [QUESTION-043](../questions.d/QUESTION-043.md), γ for a person;
[QUESTION-298](../questions.d/QUESTION-298.md), whether perceived reciprocity is real; [QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md), whether
distillation is the organization's own; [QUESTION-440](../questions.d/QUESTION-440.md), whether slowness or
speed carries authority; and [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md), how a perspective would be
identified at all.
