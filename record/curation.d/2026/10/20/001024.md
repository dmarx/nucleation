The owner asked for the THEORYs proposed in the rubber-hand batch (entry of
23:53 yesterday) to be filed. Four are filed, all Proposed. Each one was
written from the five NOTEs, not from the readers' one-line proposals, and
each claims no more than its sources show.

- **[THEORY-tmpef8lf](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpef8lf.md)**, the synchrony effect, from Botvinick & Cohen alone.
  Its promote_when asks for a replication with effect sizes and a control
  for suggestion. The 1998 page does not discuss demand characteristics,
  and its predicted items were guessable.
- **[THEORY-tmpxdoe8](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpxdoe8.md)**, ownership as a graded belief about the common cause
  of multisensory input, from Apps & Tsakiris. The source has no data and
  grants the account is hard to falsify. Its promote_when asks for a
  model-based test with held-out conditions, and for the real hand's loss
  of ownership to be shown on a measure other than temperature or
  histamine.
- **[THEORY-tmpom3z2](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpom3z2.md)**, interoceptive signals in ownership, from Seth. All
  its evidence is second-hand, and one of the two cardio-visual studies is
  Seth's own. Its promote_when asks for the control the NOTE named: a
  non-bodily periodic signal at heart rate.
- **[THEORY-tmpuehzc](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpuehzc.md)**, minimal selfhood as identification with the whole
  body, from Blanke & Metzinger with Lenggenhager et al. The part–whole
  split rests on what the rubber-hand studies did not measure. The agency
  thesis rests on adults whose agency was intact. The promote_when asks
  for both to be tested.

The fourth sits in tension with the second and third, which treat part and
whole ownership as one inference. No THEORY settles that tension, and it
is named in their Connections.

The Lenggenhager proposal, on self-location shifting partway toward a
seen body, was not filed separately. It is a part of [THEORY-tmpuehzc](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpuehzc.md)'s
evidence, and its own-body result is too inconsistent between the two
studies to stand alone.

No relation is declared on any of them: no open question in the record asks
how body ownership arises or what the minimal self is made of. If such a
question is opened, these four answer it.
