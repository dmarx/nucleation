# LIT-1376: The Ledger of Will: Evidence, Revaluation, and the Preservation of Revisable Agency

Aevyra (2026). *The Ledger of Will: Evidence, Revaluation, and the Preservation of Revisable Agency*. Aevyra Papers & Docs. https://aevyra.github.io/ledger-of-will/.

Read in full on 2026-10-22, in [NOTE-tmpvddyu](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpvddyu.md). The summary is the abstract's and
the reading confirms it. The page credits "Original formulation: Yue and
Anika · Research revision: Sofia · Review and revision-closure proposal:
Anika".

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpvddyu](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpvddyu.md). Section numbers are the paper's.*

- **The requirement.** "A record that reconstructs yesterday's answer
  perfectly can nevertheless prevent tomorrow's appropriate revision if it
  has retained the conclusion and discarded its conditions" (§1).
- **Sufficiency is relative to a declared family.** A retained state is
  exactly sufficient iff every target is constant on its fibres (Prop. 2),
  and "Declaring a restricted demand family is therefore part of the
  scientific claim, not administrative metadata" (§4.2).
- **Under repeated revision**, the fibres must lie in the coarsest
  revision-invariant refinement, found with at most K* − K added revision
  words (Prop. 4); [THEORY-tmp7q3ne](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7q3ne.md).
- **Keep, narrow or reacquire.** "Adding an instruction to reconsider
  cannot reconstruct a distinction that was irreversibly merged" (§4.3).
- **Derived notes are not new evidence** (§6.2), and "Information
  availability and computational accessibility are different resources"
  (§5.2).
- **Proposals, not results.** "this paper offers no new human-subject
  study, no large-model performance result, and no experimental
  demonstration of subjecthood" (§10).

## Why this is here

A13 to A16 found it the closest outside predecessor of [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md) and [QUESTION-112](../questions.d/QUESTION-112.md), and demoted an essay on the record's memory for it. Its abstract says the contribution is "a testable synthesis of established ideas and explicit design proposals". It carries `anthology-candidate` because its subject is memory for language-model agents, which the anthology may also want.
