# LIT-194: Religion and Science

Helen De Cruz (2017), *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* — <https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religion-science/>

## Key takeaways

- The science–religion relationship is not one relationship: it varies by religious tradition and by specific claim, and the field is moving from global compatibility questions to "science-engaged theology" on particular pairs of claims.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The entry introduces the interdisciplinary field of science and religion and the questions of compatibility and mutual influence it studies. It outlines the history and scope of both terms and the main taxonomies of their interaction, then surveys how science has related to five traditions — Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism and Judaism. It closes with contemporary topics where they meet, focusing on divine action and creation and on human origins.
