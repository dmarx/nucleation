# CLAIM-1272: Beside insufficient adjustment and selective accessibility, an anchor may act by nucleation, helping a new response organization form that then maintains itself, so its influence outlasts the anchor; the mechanisms part on what follows the anchor's removal, influence that declines with further adjustment against persistence, hysteresis or transfer, and the same contrast separates a transient framing effect from a durable change of perspective

## The claim

A11 §7: "Anchoring is conventionally associated with excessive influence
of an initial reference point. Nucleation offers a broader dynamical
interpretation: an initial condition can influence which organizational
regime becomes realized." Against ordinary adjustment, x_{t+1} = x_t +
η(e_t − x_t), it sets dynamics with several stable regimes, ẋ = μx + κx² −
x³ + u(t), which "can exhibit bistability and hysteresis under suitable
parameters, as in Nucleation's [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md) and [CLAIM-463](CLAIM-463.md). An initial
perturbation may have consequences that outlast its presence because it
participates in selecting a persistent organization."

Its table: insufficient adjustment, "The anchor's influence gradually
declines with further adjustment"; selective accessibility,
"Anchor-consistent information becomes preferentially available";
nucleation, "The anchor facilitates formation of a new, maintained response
organization." And: "The third mechanism is the proposed extension. It
would require evidence of persistence, transfer, hysteresis or
organizational transformation that the first two mechanisms do not
adequately explain. This is a concrete opportunity for the inquiry to
generate a discriminating prediction."

A11 §8 draws the same line for framing, after Tversky and Kahneman's
disease problem ([LIT-3210](../literature.d/LIT-3210.md)): "The framework should consequently
distinguish transient frame activation from durable perspectival
transformation. A one-off framing effect does not establish that a new
perspective has formed."

## Where the record stands

- [CLAIM-463](CLAIM-463.md) (Active) is the dynamics: restoring the conditions under which
  a capacity was lost need not restore it where its maintenance is
  self-reinforcing. [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md) (Active) constructs the fold, both states
  stable for −κ²/4 < μ < 0. The claim rests on these for the possibility;
  whether an anchor ever moves a judge across such a fold is what it
  proposes.
- [CLAIM-315](CLAIM-315.md) holds the anchoring shift and [THEORY-335](../theory.d/THEORY-335.md) the record's reading
  of selective accessibility, whose accessibility step five preregistered
  experiments did not find. A11 reports the two mechanisms as already
  distinguished by the literature; on the record's reading the second is
  not yet measured, so the nucleation mechanism would be tested against an
  account whose own step is open.
- [CLAIM-298](CLAIM-298.md) holds that a frame changes which stored distinctions are
  reachable. The durable branch is what this claim adds.

## What it does not say

That ordinary anchoring in a one-shot numerical estimate is nucleation. The
comparative paradigm asks one question once, and nothing there can persist
or show hysteresis; the claim needs a task in which a response organization
is maintained across judgements ([LEAD-240](../leads.d/LEAD-240.md)).
