# LIT-tmpb6b92: Energy and Equity

Ivan Illich (1974). *Energy and Equity*. London: Calder & Boyars. First published in *Le Monde*, early 1973. Reprinted in Illich, *Toward a History of Needs* (New York: Pantheon, 1978). DOI-10.1558/isbn.9780714521008.

## Key takeaways

- "Past a certain threshold of energy consumption, the transportation industry dictates the configuration of social space."
- Radical monopoly: "Because of its hidden, entrenched, and structuring nature, I call this a radical monopoly"; it arises "by virtue of its acquired ability to create and shape the need which it alone can satisfy."
- "The magnitude of voluntary limits is a matter of politics; the encroachment of radical monopoly can be pinpointed by social analysis."
