# TERM-tmple6rq: observational equivalence, under a probe family: two models, states or distributions agreeing on the outcome law of every probe in a declared admissible family; coarser than identity unless the family is rich enough, and, when probes are run one at a time, weaker on their marginals than on the joint law across them

## Definition

Fix a family Φ = (φ_i) of admissible probes: procedures that, applied to an
object, yield an outcome with a law. Two objects P and Q are
**observationally equivalent under Φ** when (φ_i)#P = (φ_i)#Q for every i.
A13: "Two models or states are equivalent when every admissible probe
produces the same observable law."

What the record holds of it ([CLAIM-264](../claims.d/CLAIM-264.md)):

- **It is coarser than identity** unless the family is rich enough: two
  objects can agree on every probe in a restricted family without being
  isomorphic.
- **Rich families separate.** All one-dimensional linear projections
  determine a distribution (Cramér–Wold). Homomorphism counts from every
  finite structure determine a finite relational structure (Lovász). The
  hom-functor with its action identifies an object (restricted Yoneda,
  [THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md)).
- **Marginals against the joint.** Matching each probe's marginal "leaves
  the joint across probes unconstrained", so equivalence on probes run one
  at a time is weaker than on probes run together.

Special cases the record already holds:

- **Response trajectories as the probes.** [TERM-033](TERM-033.md), fidelity as
  observational equivalence over response trajectories, compares
  P_o(Y_1:k | a_1:k) with P_t(Y′_1:k | a′_1:k) under corresponding
  interventions. [TERM-033](TERM-033.md) names [TERM-015](TERM-015.md) as its successor (`superseded_by`, though its status is Active), and [TERM-043](TERM-043.md)'s causal
  component keeps its comparison. It is the narrower term, so the relation
  runs from [TERM-033](TERM-033.md) to this one, and is left in prose.
- **A communicative object** ([TERM-014](TERM-014.md)) is "an equivalence class of
  situations relative to a chosen family of measurements", a class of this
  relation. That is a use, not a narrowing, and also stays in prose.
- **The coarse variable's law as the probe.** [CLAIM-389](../claims.d/CLAIM-389.md)'s coarse variable is
  observationally equivalent, under every probe of its own law, to an
  autonomous AR(1), while one receives 0.120 nats per step from the rest of
  its system and the other nothing ([CLAIM-397](../claims.d/CLAIM-397.md)'s "What it does not say").

## What it is not

- **Not identity, nor isomorphism** ([CLAIM-264](../claims.d/CLAIM-264.md)).
- **Not statistical equivalence when the probes are resource-bounded.**
  [CLAIM-302](../claims.d/CLAIM-302.md): "Equivalence under feasible tests can be much coarser than
  statistical equivalence".
- **Not evidence that structure was preserved,** unless the probes were
  chosen independently of the objects ([CLAIM-322](../claims.d/CLAIM-322.md), [CLAIM-142](../claims.d/CLAIM-142.md)).
- **Not [TERM-037](TERM-037.md)'s observable,** which is one probe in the communicative
  setting.
- **Not interventional equivalence** unless the family contains the
  interventions. Two systems equivalent under every passive probe can
  respond differently to an intervention on their mechanism
  ([CASE-tmpdc0tl](../cases.d/CASE-tmpdc0tl.md), [CLAIM-tmppi0xf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmppi0xf.md)).
