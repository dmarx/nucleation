# CLAIM-010: On this account every control system has a will

## The claim

A112: "doesn't this make every control system possess a will? ... a
thermostat has a will, a river has a will, and a reef has a will."

## How it was answered

Three ways, in order:

1. The owner bit the bullet ([CLAIM-062](CLAIM-062.md)); the assistant withdrew the
   objection at A116.
2. It came back regardless — A118, A147, A180, A185, A256 and A279 each raise
   it again, A98 even as "a new objection the reef allows us to confront".
3. The manuscript answers it by making the account agent-indexed: "Does every
   organized institution have a will? Again, no." (Section 7.4.)

Rejected as an objection to [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md). Against [CLAIM-012](CLAIM-012.md) it was never
answered, only made moot by the supersession.
