# CASE-tmpohd6m: The rehabilitated hypothesis: an objection that displaced a hypothesis turns out to rest on a faulty measurement, and the hypothesis can be reopened because its defeat was recorded

## The case

H_A is preferred, then displaced by H_B after objection O. The record
keeps H_A, O, and O's measurement assumption A. Years later A fails.

| conclusion-only memory | accountable memory |
|---|---|
| H_B is currently preferred | H_B displaced H_A because of O |
| no record of why H_A was rejected | O depended on assumption A |
| may not retrieve H_A when A fails | identifies H_A for reconsideration |

A7: "The key outcome is not necessarily returning to H_A. The researcher
may still endorse H_B. What matters is that the earlier alternative
becomes available for renewed assessment".

## What it can show

That keeping superseded positions and the reasons they were superseded
widens what can be reconsidered. A12's variant: argument 1 supports H, O
defeats argument 1, later evidence defeats O. Nothing is thereby established
for H; argument 1 "becomes newly eligible for assessment". "Losing a defeater
may reopen support without establishing a conclusion."

## What it cannot show

A7: "That discarded hypotheses should always be revisited, or that
retaining every rejected possibility is worth its cost." It sits beside
[CLAIM-184](../claims.d/CLAIM-184.md)'s warning that anchors entrench and [CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md)'s that legitimate
revision drops commitments.
