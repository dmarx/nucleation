The owner sent eight readings: four on large-scale neural dynamics and the
neural correlates of seeing, and four on theories of consciousness and the
biophysics that Orch OR leans on. None was held in either record, so
nothing needed an [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) second entry, and none carries an instruction
for machine-learning practice.

## What was filed

Eight LITs and six NOTEs. Six works were read in full from lawful copies:
publisher open access, PMC, arXiv, or a co-author's own deposit.

- **Neural dynamics:**
  - Verzhbinsky et al. 2026 on co-ripple co-firing ([LIT-633](../../record/literature.d/LIT-633.md));
  - Xu et al. 2023 on interacting cortical spirals ([LIT-638](../../record/literature.d/LIT-638.md)). It
    `rivals` the discrete-substate account of Vohryzek et al. ([LIT-080](../../record/literature.d/LIT-080.md)).
    Neither tests the other, and the two may yet prove to be two levels of
    description of the same dynamics;
  - Vishne et al. 2023 on sustained conscious visual perception
    ([LIT-644](../../record/literature.d/LIT-644.md)).
- **Consciousness and biophysics:**
  - Babcock et al. 2024 on tryptophan superradiance ([LIT-645](../../record/literature.d/LIT-645.md)). The
    paper never mentions consciousness, and the record does not read it as
    evidence for Orch OR;
  - Maldarelli & Güntürkün 2025, "Conscious birds" ([LIT-650](../../record/literature.d/LIT-650.md));
  - Newen & Montemayor 2025, the ALARM theory ([LIT-626](../../record/literature.d/LIT-626.md)). It `rivals`
    Veit ([LIT-105](../../record/literature.d/LIT-105.md)) on what consciousness was first for.
- **Deferred, because no lawful full text exists:**
  - Muller et al. 2026, the traveling-waves review ([LIT-647](../../record/literature.d/LIT-647.md)). Its
    accepted manuscript opens in September 2027;
  - Derakhshani et al. 2022 on spontaneous radiation and Orch OR
    ([LIT-627](../../record/literature.d/LIT-627.md)). It is closed access.

## Theories

Seven, all Proposed. Each rests on one paper.

- [THEORY-088](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-088.md): slow cortical activity organised by interacting
  spiral defects, as in a pattern-forming medium. The title does not say
  "excitable": the paper's model is an oscillator lattice, and the
  evidence does not decide between the two kinds of medium.
- [THEORY-097](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-097.md): long-range forebrain coordination carried by co-ripples.
- [THEORY-102](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-102.md): a stable ventral population code while amplitude
  decays, with prefrontal content arriving at onset only.
- [THEORY-090](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-090.md): basic affective consciousness is subcortical and
  serves as an alarm. It extends [THEORY-045](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-045.md).
- [THEORY-101](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-101.md): Veit's valence-as-currency account, filed so that the
  ALARM rivalry has a counterpart. The two `rivals` each other.
- [THEORY-100](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-100.md): conscious report does not need laminar cortex (corvid
  NCL).
- [THEORY-099](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-099.md): collective superradiant states in microtubule
  tryptophan networks. It says explicitly that this is not evidence for
  Orch OR.

One candidate was declined: "Orch OR in its simplest gravity-collapse form
is excluded by spontaneous-radiation bounds". Its only source is the
Deferred Derakhshani paper. The record holds no reading of the underground
collapse test (Donadi et al. 2020), and Gao ([LIT-121](../../record/literature.d/LIT-121.md)) argues against
Penrose's collapse on theoretical grounds, not with radiation bounds.

## Gaps noticed

- **Vocabulary.** There is no word for biophysics or quantum biology:
  `natural-sciences` holds Babcock et al. for now. There is also no word for
  comparative or animal cognition. Either is worth a decision if more such
  works arrive.
- **Unread primary sources.** The crow neurophysiology (Nieder et al. 2020;
  Wagener & Nieder 2024) is held only second-hand, and [THEORY-100](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-100.md)
  would be promoted by reading it.
