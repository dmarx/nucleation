  Teo and Kane (2010), arXiv 1006.0690 (Phys. Rev. B 82, 115120). A
  unified classification of defects in non-interacting insulators and
  superconductors. It uses the homotopy class of the Bloch or
  Bogoliubov–de Gennes Hamiltonian H(k, r) on a surface around the defect,
  which depends only on the symmetry class and δ = d − D, together with a
  generalized bulk-boundary correspondence, observed in examples rather
  than proved, that pairs a nontrivial class with protected gapless modes
  bound to the defect. In the Jackiw–Rebbi kink, the Jackiw–Rossi vortex
  and the dislocation, the defect's structural charge fixes the mode
  invariant. In a disordered weak topological insulator the protected
  modes outlive the structure that produced them. It is here for the
  relation between defect topology and bound modes (CLAIM-606, CLAIM-694).
  It shows these are two topologies of different objects. What is
  protected is the hosted modes, not the defect's persistence. Skimmed in
