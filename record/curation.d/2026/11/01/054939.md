**What the debate does to the record's maintenance claims.** [CLAIM-522](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)
holds that only interventions on mechanisms, relative to a declared
boundary, separate an internal producer from an outside controller. The
debate sharpens that and does not overturn it.
- **Mutual manipulability does not separate the two.** Both pass its
  conditions. Only its parthood condition does, and that is the boundary
  the experiments presuppose ([CLAIM-tmpg57ci](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpg57ci.md)).
- **Fat-handedness does not reach [CLAIM-522](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-522.md).** Knocking out a production
  pathway acts only after a delay, so the pathway is a cause even by
  Baumgartner and Gebharter's temporal criterion ([ARG-tmp39a6w](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp39a6w.md), Active).
- **"Realized" has two senses.** Produced through over time is causal and
  shown by intervention. Consists in at a time is underdetermined by
  manipulation data and inferred abductively. The line between them moves
  with the time scale ([CLAIM-tmp1qybv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1qybv.md); notes on [CLAIM-515](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-515.md) and [CLAIM-150](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-150.md)).
- **The constructions are de novo, how-possibly models.** In Bechtel and
  Abrahamsen's sense, [CASE-189](../../record/cases.d/CASE-189.md) and [CASE-190](../../record/cases.d/CASE-190.md) can show how a maintained
  organization could behave, not that any real one does. Their knockouts
  are clamping tests (notes on both cases).
- **Griesemer and Shavit's modes of interaction.** Only those criteria
  stated on interactions can individuate an organization without
  presupposing it ([CLAIM-tmpbij2b](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbij2b.md)). Generative entrenchment alone does
  not give closure ([CLAIM-tmpr242r](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpr242r.md)).
