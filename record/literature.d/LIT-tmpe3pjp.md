# LIT-tmpe3pjp: The fundamental structures of human reflexion

Lefebvre, V. A. (1987). *Journal of Social and Biological Structures* 10(2):129–175. doi:10.1016/0140-1750(87)90004-2.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

Registered as a journal source for Lefebvre's theory of reflexion, for
[CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md). The search abstract, the only part seen, describes a
paper on the psychology of self-image: an inborn internal "computer" that
models the self and others, described by the algebraic model of reflexion.
So it is a source for the images of self and other that reflexive control
acts on, not for reflexive control itself. A version is cited as appearing
in Wheeler (ed.), *The Structure of Human Reflexion* (Peter Lang, 1989 or
1990).
