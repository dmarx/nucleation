# SYNTHESIS-tmpoclez: The realizability line is four accounts, of reaching under resources, of budgeted individuation, of assembly and productive nucleation, and of persisting organized individuals, which meet at the separation of assembling, persisting and regenerating a form; the first three are densely joined, the persistence part points out and receives nothing, and no link between the parts that no part states passes the claim test

*The account of the realizability line ("Resource-bounded realizability
and individuation", [ADR-073](../decisions.d/ADR-073.md)), over the accounts of its four parts
([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). It integrates every entry whose `line` includes realizability
after [ADR-088](../decisions.d/ADR-088.md) (2026-11-11): 98 entries through the parts, and
[CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md) directly. [ADR-088](../decisions.d/ADR-088.md) gave [QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md), [QUESTION-399](../questions.d/QUESTION-399.md) and the
withdrawal entries transient-support as their first line, and none keeps
realizability. [SYNTHESIS-003](SYNTHESIS-003.md) carries the line and is Superseded by
[CLAIM-611](../claims.d/CLAIM-611.md) ([ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)), which [SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2](SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2.md) integrates; [SYNTHESIS-010](SYNTHESIS-010.md) still lists
the line and is Superseded by [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md), now in transient-support. Neither
is integrated again.*

## The parts

- **Reaching under resources ([SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o](SYNTHESIS-tmpmm13o.md)), 53 entries.** Feasible, reachable
  and reliably reached come apart ([CLAIM-567](../claims.d/CLAIM-567.md)); a transition's threshold is
  the least peak over admissible paths and can be infinite ([CLAIM-571](../claims.d/CLAIM-571.md)); a
  budget enlarges the compatibility complex only up to the unbudgeted one
  ([CLAIM-586](../claims.d/CLAIM-586.md)), and neither the compatibility nor the reachability
  filtration fixes the other ([CLAIM-560](../claims.d/CLAIM-560.md)); failed transitions sort into
  obstructions with their own repairs ([CLAIM-568](../claims.d/CLAIM-568.md), [CLAIM-556](../claims.d/CLAIM-556.md)), and telling
  which one holds can be hard ([CLAIM-575](../claims.d/CLAIM-575.md)); an added constraint can improve
  reliability and never reach ([CLAIM-582](../claims.d/CLAIM-582.md)); and where an intervention acts
  can predict better than how much ([CLAIM-562](../claims.d/CLAIM-562.md)). A's general object and
  universality hypothesis are Proposed ([CLAIM-576](../claims.d/CLAIM-576.md), [CLAIM-573](../claims.d/CLAIM-573.md)).
- **Budgeted individuation ([SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0](SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0.md)), 15 entries.** Observing splits and
  acting under peak costs merges ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md), [CLAIM-581](../claims.d/CLAIM-581.md)), so individuation
  is indexed by two budgets ([CLAIM-557](../claims.d/CLAIM-557.md)), with computable frontiers
  ([CLAIM-570](../claims.d/CLAIM-570.md)) and an agreement the cost order of probes decides
  ([CLAIM-592](../claims.d/CLAIM-592.md)); the choice of category decides what counts as the same
  ([CLAIM-590](../claims.d/CLAIM-590.md)), and isomorphism is not persistence ([CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md)).
- **Assembly and productive nucleation ([SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl](SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl.md)), 20 entries.** A resource
  that sets its own costs gives a window ([CLAIM-594](../claims.d/CLAIM-594.md)); abundance helps or
  hurts by which steps consume it ([CLAIM-598](../claims.d/CLAIM-598.md)); persistence and
  productivity vary independently ([CLAIM-595](../claims.d/CLAIM-595.md)); yields cannot tell a
  generative nucleus from a head start ([CLAIM-596](../claims.d/CLAIM-596.md)); productive sets have no
  closure ([CLAIM-599](../claims.d/CLAIM-599.md)); and a productive nucleus is defined against a
  declared target, comparator and horizon ([TERM-117](../terms.d/TERM-117.md)).
- **Persisting organized individuals ([SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2](SYNTHESIS-tmprngy2.md)), 10 entries, all claims
  Proposed.** What persists is the organization that keeps a repertoire
  realizable ([CLAIM-611](../claims.d/CLAIM-611.md)), identity over time its causal transmission
  ([CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md)), histories need not glue and can be terminal ([CLAIM-623](../claims.d/CLAIM-623.md),
  [CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md)), and synchronization is offered as a laboratory ([CLAIM-616](../claims.d/CLAIM-616.md)).
- **[CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md)** (A's, interpretive, Active; also distributed-agency): that a
  form assembles, that it persists over a specified horizon and that the
  system regenerates it after turnover are three properties with separate
  probabilities, and "a structure that assembles and persists without
  producing the constraints that would regenerate it does not show
  organizational closure".
