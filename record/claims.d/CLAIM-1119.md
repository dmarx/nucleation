# CLAIM-1119: Maintained alternative perspectives are alternative legibility regimes: each lowers the cost of making some kinds of question intelligible and testable within it, so with protected resources and a route to wider uptake one can develop an inquiry until its results are legible elsewhere, and keeping a perspective able to do this means keeping its practices, expertise, channels and capacity for work, not only an archive of its ideas

## The claim

A56 §6. A specialist community has its own categories, methods and
standards of evidence. A proposal illegible to the dominant management may
be cheap to evaluate within it. With perspectives i, each with evaluator
o_i, resources B_i and task family 𝒬_i, the legibility of one proposal m_q
differs across them: L_(o_i, 𝒬_i, B_i)(m_q) ≠ L_(o_j, 𝒬_j, B_j)(m_q), in
[TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s operationalization. "They reduce the cost of translating an
emerging possibility into an inquiry" within their own regime, and with
limited resources or a recognized route to broader authorization they can
take it far enough to produce evidence legible elsewhere. A56's progression:
locally legible possibility, protected exploratory inquiry, locally
validated result, cross-perspective translation, broader uptake.

"That is also why the maintenance of an alternative perspective cannot be
reduced to archiving its ideas. It may require preserving practices,
expertise, communication channels, and some capacity for continued work."

## What it does not say

That alternative perspectives are exempt from scrutiny. The point is to
"avoid requiring that they already satisfy the dominant perspective's
criteria of justification" before they have had the chance to produce the
evidence scrutiny needs.

## Neighbours

[CLAIM-455](CLAIM-455.md): a transformation can make one task's distinction legible and
another's illegible for the same observer, so regimes are ordered only
relative to a task. That is the premise that some regime is cheaper for some
questions. [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s body holds the legibility regime itself, and
Kleinberg and Raghavan's case that a shared regime can cost an organization
its diversity ([THEORY-835](../theory.d/THEORY-835.md)). The owner's U28 conjecture that persisted
perspectives are nucleation sites is [CLAIM-1004](CLAIM-1004.md), and A54's three levels
of preservation (a maintained nucleus, an archive, active parallel inquiry)
are [CLAIM-1148](CLAIM-1148.md), which this complements: A54 says what must be kept for a
perspective to be usable, A56 what keeping it buys, a cheaper route to
making some questions intelligible.
