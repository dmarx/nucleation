The owner asked for arXiv 1011.6123: Abramsky & Heunen, *H\*-algebras and
nonunital Frobenius algebras* (AMS PSAPM 71, 2012). The previous entry had
named it as the test of [THEORY-017](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-017.md)'s finite-dimensional boundary. It is
filed as [LIT-tmp7e2en](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp7e2en.md), read in full as [NOTE-tmp4nt96](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp4nt96.md) (arXiv v3, the final
version), `Active`, and it `extends` [LIT-274](../../record/literature.d/LIT-274.md).

**Checked against the text when filing:** Lemma 3, Theorem 22,
Proposition 23 and Theorem 35 read as the reader reported.

**What it proves.**

- **Lemma 3.** A unital Frobenius algebra exists in Hilb only in finite
  dimension.
- **Theorem 22.** Without the unit, a commutative special †-Frobenius
  algebra on a complex Hilbert space of any dimension is induced by an
  orthonormal basis exactly when it satisfies Ambrose's H\*-axiom,
  equivalently when it is semisimple, or a directed colimit of finite unital
  ones. It adds an approximate-identity form for separable spaces.
- **Proposition 23.** It leaves open whether the axiom is automatic, i.e.
  whether a radical part can exist.
- **Tools.** It uses Gelfand theory and Ambrose, not GNS or C\*-algebras.
- **Relations.** In categories of relations the axiom is proved, for
  quantale-valued relations only under Theorem 35's hypothesis q² = 1 ⇒
  q = 1. The abstract's "always coincide" overstates this.

**[THEORY-017](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-017.md) (version 3).** The "stays finite-dimensional" caveat is
narrowed. A discrete basis is extra structure supplied as an algebra on the
space in any dimension. Only continuous observables, which the paper defers,
remain outside, and that is where Carroll's and the GNS strand's "an algebra
of observables must be supplied" applies. The real-scalar caveat now holds
in every dimension: the reader found that ℂ regarded as a real algebra
satisfies the H\*-axiom and has no copyable vectors. I checked the other
axioms numerically in the previous contribution; the H\*-axiom check is the
reader's. The document stays `Proposed`, on the same remaining
`promote_when` condition.

**Small corrections recorded in the NOTE.** The paper's "Theorem 1
[CPV09]" is Coecke, Pavlovic & Vicary's §6 (special/orthonormal) form, not
their Theorem 5.1 (orthogonal). The orthogonal case appears here only as a
sketch in a footnote.

**Candidate follow-up, not filed.** Poinsot, *Hilbertian Frobenius algebras*
(arXiv 2003.04149, 2020). By its abstract, which is all the reader saw, every
commutative special Hilbertian Frobenius algebra is semisimple. If the body
bears that out, Proposition 23's question is closed and [THEORY-017](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-017.md) can drop
"was left open there".
