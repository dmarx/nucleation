## Search plan

1. Active inference, the free-energy principle, predictive processing and
   Markov blankets: every reading, not only the titles grep found.
2. Judgement as measurement: measurement contexts, contextuality, order
   effects, what a judgement datum is in the pragmatic-transport line.
3. Agency without overt action: will-organization, regimes, mental action,
   attention, executive control, the evidence-for-experience line
   (report, no-report, access), locked-in and disorders of consciousness.
4. Perception as activity: enactivism, autonomy and closure, sensorimotor
   accounts, perceptual control theory, affordances.
5. The underlying model: operators on a meaning canvas, endorsement,
   interpretation after intervention, and whatever states the model whole.
