# THEORY-1788: Bencomo, Götz and Song (2019) find, in a review of 271 papers, that research on models at run time has concentrated on architecture-level structural models kept causally connected to a running system and used mainly for self-adaptation through model transformations

## The account

Bencomo, Götz and Song (2019) find, in a review of 271 papers, that research on models at run time has concentrated on architecture-level structural models kept causally connected to a running system and used mainly for self-adaptation through model transformations. Distributed runtime models, metamodels at runtime and keeping model and system safely in step remain little studied.
