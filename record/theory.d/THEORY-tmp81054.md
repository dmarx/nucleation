# THEORY-tmp81054: Propp proposes, from his analysis of Afanasyev's tales 50–151, that the Russian wondertale is built from 31 character functions, each defined by its consequence for the action, occurring in one order and falling to seven roles, so that wondertales share one composition and differ in who performs the functions and how

## Source

Vladimir Propp (1928), *Морфология сказки* [Morphology of the Folktale],
[LIT-828](../literature.d/LIT-828.md), read in [NOTE-615](../notes.d/NOTE-615.md) from the 1928 Russian text, chapters I–IX in
full, with appendices II and III read in a second Russian text (the 1969
edition) on 2026-10-20.

## The proposal

Abstract each wondertale from its characters and their attributes, and
describe it by what the characters do, each act defined by what it leads to
in the story. Propp proposes that the acts then form a short list, 31
functions after an initial situation; that they occur in one order; and
that every tale of the class is a selection from that one sequence, which
omits some functions, repeats or trebles others, realises each in one of a
range of forms and assigns the functions to characters. The functions fall
to seven roles: villain, donor, helper, princess and her father,
dispatcher, hero and false hero. So the tales "differ in who performs the
functions and how". He ends by defining the wondertale as the tale built
on this sequence.

The material is Afanasyev's tales 50–151, analysed by Propp alone. The
order is shown on 45 printed schemes: of fifty tabulated tales, he set aside
No. 94 as of another kind and Nos. 123, 127, 139 and 159 because "the
canonical sequence is also not strictly kept" ([NOTE-615](../notes.d/NOTE-615.md), note of
2026-10-20).

## What this does not say

- **That the proposal is true.** That is [THEORY-167](THEORY-167.md)'s question, which waits
  on a coding by other analysts with agreement measured.
- **That it was tested for reliability.** The book reports no second coder,
  and functions are assigned by consequence, which leaves the analyst
  latitude.
- **That the order was checked here.** The 45 schemes are on a fold-out
  insert that neither text the record holds reproduces, and they were
  chosen partly for keeping the order.
- **That all folktales share the structure, or that the wondertale has a
  single source tale or a mythic origin.** Propp offers the last two as
  hypotheses the book does not support.
