# QUESTION-tmpseqwl: Can entailment cones help reason about progressive learning dynamics, the order in which a network comes to draw finer distinctions?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U21: "i wonder if considering entailment cones might help us
reason about progressive learning dynamics".

The record holds Ganea's construction of nested angular cones in the
Poincaré ball and when they are transitive (THEORY-189), the partial reading
of hierarchical level by radius (THEORY-421), and QUESTION-217, which asks
whether the refinement, relevance and acquisition orders of a learner's
representations share a chain. Cone inclusion is a refinement order in
geometry. Whether it helps with the dynamics, the order in which training
acquires distinctions, is open.

## What would count as an answer

- A positive answer: an observable defined by cones that tracks acquisition
  independently of the cones' own nesting, so that it can confirm or refute
  coarse-to-fine learning. The other assistant's proposals are filed as
  CLAIM-tmpr4k9z (two partial orders), CLAIM-tmp6mjfc (what occupancy cannot show) and
  LEAD-tmpp88ld (the measurements).
- A negative one: an argument that any cone-based order of acquisition is
  fixed by the cone geometry itself, so that cones add a language and no
  evidence. CLAIM-tmp6mjfc shows this for first-entry times into nested cones.
- A bound on the cost: how dimension sets what cone restriction means
  (CLAIM-tmpqqngq, CLAIM-tmpdwm3z), and whether that carries from spherical caps to
  hyperbolic cones (ARG-tmpwo5ou).
