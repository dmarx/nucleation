# ARG-tmpdl16p: In the Dual State ordinary norms governed conduct while a prerogative order held authority over whether they bound, so a constraint can govern conduct without governing its own authority

## The inference

A historical case exhibits the structure; the structure is general.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are the cases alike?** In structure only: one governing
  order holding authority over another's applicability.
- **What transfers?** That precedence within a domain and authority over the
  domain are distinct, and that the second can be asymmetric (Section 5.8).
- **What does not?** Moral or historical character; any suggestion that
  exception is pathological as such; any model of agency. Fraenkel's subject is
  a state, not an independently warranted agent in the paper's sense, so the
  paper's own scope condition is not met by the source case.
