# QUESTION-539: How do transmissible structures such as memes take part in forming, maintaining and transforming the organizations through which they become consequential, and when does the seed's own persistence matter to the organization's?

## Why it is a question

A4 ends: "This shifts the central question from: *How do ideas reproduce
themselves?* to: **How do transmissible structures participate in the
formation, maintenance and transformation of the organizations through
which they become consequential?**" A4 §6 adds the case that makes the
second clause pressing: "A meme may also successfully nucleate an
organization that subsequently eliminates it. That last possibility is
difficult to characterize using replicative fitness alone."

## What the record holds toward it

- Answers proposed: the owner's [CLAIM-1218](../claims.d/CLAIM-1218.md) (memes as nucleation
  sites), A4's [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md) (the memetic nucleation hypothesis) and A5's
  [CLAIM-1285](../claims.d/CLAIM-1285.md) (memes as expressions of perspectival character).
- When transient support can be withdrawn: [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md), whose conditions
  are the seed's dispensability in another setting.
- Which persistence is which: [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) (a product and the organization
  producing it persist separately), [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) and [CLAIM-420](../claims.d/CLAIM-420.md) (transmission,
  resemblance and descent), and [QUESTION-439](QUESTION-439.md) (what is individuated when
  rival communities share an expression).
- When a small structure makes a larger organization likely: [QUESTION-380](QUESTION-380.md).

## What would settle it

Interventions that separate the seed from what forms: [LEAD-260](../leads.d/LEAD-260.md)
proposes them. A seeded dynamical model that only adds the meme as a
forcing cannot ([CLAIM-1310](../claims.d/CLAIM-1310.md)).
