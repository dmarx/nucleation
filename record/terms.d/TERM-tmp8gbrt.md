# TERM-tmp8gbrt: goal-conduciveness valence, organizational: a regime in which the negotiation among a system's perspectives has, approximately, a potential, an ordering of the system's own states by their bearing on its maintenance along which the moves that win its conflicts are improvements, persisting and restored after interruption; signed by whether the system runs ahead of or behind a reference course along that potential, and as intense as the potential's share of the negotiation's preference flow

## Definition

In [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s notation, let an organization 𝒪 face, at time t, the
attainable options X_t. Each of its perspectives i ranks X_t by a
preorder ≿ᵢ and holds an authority that the organization's endorsement
dynamics set ([CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md), [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)). The organization's **appraisal** is
the negotiation N_t that takes the profile of rankings and authorities
to a collective relation R_t on X_t.

- **A common currency at t**: R_t is complete and transitive on X_t.
- **Potential.** Read the perspectives as players, each with its own
  ranking. A ranking ≿_v is a *potential* of the negotiation when every
  move that wins one of its conflicts is an improvement along ≿_v: the
  perspectives' rankings differ, but what prevails among them climbs one
  shared ordering. This is Monderer and Shapley's ordinal potential,
  carried from a game's unilateral deviations to a negotiation's
  conflicts. Exact potentials are rare, so the term says *approximately*:
  the negotiation's preference flow, the record of which side won each
  contested pair, splits by the Hodge decomposition into a gradient part,
  which is a potential, and a cyclic part, which no ordering absorbs
  (Candogan, Menache, Ozdaglar and Parrilo for games; Jiang, Lim, Yao and
  Ye's HodgeRank for pairwise comparisons). Frijda's three marks of
  control precedence ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)) are how a potential shows itself in
  time:
  - *priority*: on pairs where ≿_v and other perspectives disagree, the
    conflict is decided along ≿_v;
  - *persistence*: the potential is the same across the interval;
  - *resumption*: after a perturbation the dynamics climb ≿_v again, the
    potential serving as a Lyapunov function.
- **The kind.** ≿_v ranks the system's own states by their bearing on
  its maintenance: its essential variables in Ashby's sense ([THEORY-324](../theory.d/THEORY-324.md)),
  the graded norm of Di Paolo's adaptivity ([THEORY-628](../theory.d/THEORY-628.md)), what [THEORY-045](../theory.d/THEORY-045.md)
  calls a regulatory state's meaning "for staying alive".
- **Goal-conduciveness valence** is a regime in which a ranking of that kind
  prevails across the organization's perspectives.
  - Its **sign** is whether the system runs ahead of or behind a
    reference course along ≿_v: ahead is positive, behind negative, on
    course neutral. This is Carver and Scheier's account, whose neutral
    point is a reference rate of progress, not rest ([LIT-tmp63om1](../literature.d/LIT-tmp63om1.md), read
    through [LIT-tmpdmzgc](../literature.d/LIT-tmpdmzgc.md) and [NOTE-tmpcc9vz](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpcc9vz.md)): a system standing still
    where it expected to advance is behind. Comparing positions on an
    ordering needs no measure, so the clause stays ordinal.
  - Its **intensity** is the gradient share: the part of the
    negotiation's preference flow that the potential accounts for.
  - **Ambivalence** is a large cyclic share: no ordering absorbs the
    conflicts, as Sen's theorem allows ([THEORY-027](../theory.d/THEORY-027.md)) and Tversky's cycles
    show ([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md), part 3b). **Neutrality** is a potential with the
    system on its course.

## Revision of 2026-10-23: goal conduciveness, not hedonic

Drafted as "hedonic valence", the owner's word. Revised before merge, at
the owner's direction, after Berridge's liking–wanting dissociation
showed that the regime this term defines tracks 'wanting', not 'liking'
([THEORY-tmp0bbxk](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp0bbxk.md), [CLAIM-tmpe0iew](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpe0iew.md)). On the component process model's
levels of valence ([LIT-tmpkywoj](../literature.d/LIT-tmpkywoj.md), [CLAIM-tmp5lpdu](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5lpdu.md)) the ordering defined
here is goal conduciveness, need satisfaction and goal attainment, and
intrinsic pleasantness, 'liking', is a separate appraisal with its own
ordering. Hedonic valence in that sense is not what this term defines.

## What it is not

- **Not "dominance", and not "ascendancy".** The notion was drafted as
  "dominance" and renamed on 2026-10-23, because the word is taken: in
  affective science "dominance" is the third axis of Mehrabian and
  Russell's pleasure–arousal–dominance model, the felt sense of being in
  control, which Fontaine and colleagues' potency–control dimension
  continues ([LIT-tmp3deyb](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3deyb.md)) and which the component process model's own
  authors call "dominance" ([LIT-tmpkywoj](../literature.d/LIT-tmpkywoj.md), p. 4); in decision theory a
  dominated option is one worse on every count. It was renamed
  "ascendancy" the same day, and renamed again, at the owner's direction,
  to "potential", because the game-theoretic notion says exactly what was
  meant and brings its theorems with it: an ordering prevails when the
  negotiation has it as a potential, and how far it prevails is the
  gradient share of a Hodge decomposition. "Precedence" was not used,
  because [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) uses it for the priority a motive claims, as against
  the authority it is granted. "Potential" in physics and in free-energy
  accounts is compatible: a function the dynamics descend or climb.
- **Not intrinsic pleasantness.** 'Liking', the hedonic impact of a
  stimulus, is a separate appraisal (see the revision above). Whether it
  can be the potential of some ordering is open ([CLAIM-tmp5lpdu](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5lpdu.md)).
- **Not a felt state.** The term is functional. Whether the regime is
  felt is [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), and whether it is identical with felt valence is
  Veit's thesis ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)), not this term's.
- **Not frustration** ([TERM-060](TERM-060.md)). Frustration is a relation among
  demands that no admissible transformation meets jointly. Valence on
  this term presupposes it: where the perspectives never conflict, no
  ranking has anything to prevail over.
- **Not any potential goal.** A ranking of world states, however
  potential, is not valence until it ranks the system's own states by
  their bearing on its maintenance. Whether a firm's potential metric is a
  ranking of that kind is a question about the firm ([QUESTION-tmprsrxs](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmprsrxs.md)).
- **Not a scale.** Neither sign nor the ordering needs a measure. Where
  the potential ranking is continuous it has a numerical representation;
  where it is a graded lexicographic override it has none
  ([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md)).
