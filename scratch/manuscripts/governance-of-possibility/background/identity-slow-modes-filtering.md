## 2. Central theses (CLAIMs)

For each claim: its status, warrant and role, then its `rests_on` premises and a short quotation from its file.

### 2a. Identity as historical continuity (§3)

- **CLAIM-329** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "A collective that replaces all its members persists, when it does, by causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by resemblance: an organization of the same kind that arises independently is not its continuation, and a lapse in its coherence need not end it". SYNTHESIS-047 records what holds it back: without an answer to QUESTION-118 it "cannot tell continuation from causal descent where every candidate is causally continuous".
- **CLAIM-287** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "What persists when a self persists through inactivity is the capacity to reinstate an organization, not its continuous running, and continuity of that kind can branch and merge, so it is not an identity relation".
- **CLAIM-280** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts".
- **CLAIM-284** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "A self, in the self-governing sense, is a historically continuous organization of processes that takes part in governing the conditions of its own persistence, differentiation and transformation, not merely one that persists".
- **CLAIM-615** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-611, 329, 593, 328, 287 and 280, per SYNTHESIS-047). Identity over time "is carried by the causal transmission of the relations that keep its repertoire realizable, not by its constituents or by any structure invariant across its realizations, so a structuralism about individuals has to be historical".
- **CLAIM-tmp7pe33** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-284 and CLAIM-574). A self is "a historically sustained organization of coupled dispositions, whose apparent decomposition into perspectives is relative to a declared family of interventions, observation horizons and resource bounds". Its third clause ("may disappear") is corrected by **CLAIM-tmp0dyx8** (Active; demonstrated; counter): a distinction "cannot disappear as a probe regime is enriched along a nested family".
- **CLAIM-tmptxsin** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-284, CLAIM-532 and CLAIM-574). A15's revision: the self's "unity, if it has one, must be established through the causal processes that coordinate, maintain and transform those dispositions". Its discriminating test is LEAD-144 (Open).
- The curation entry **excludes** CLAIM-tmp7pe33 and CLAIM-tmptxsin from the work: "§3 uses only a generic 'historical continuity'". It also finds that §3's "Nucleation approaches organizational identity as the historical continuity…" describes "A's own Proposed claims … as the record's".

### 2b. Fast activity makes slow structure, and slowness is not enough (§3.1–3.2)

- **CLAIM-478** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-477 and CLAIM-331; grounds LIT-1292, LIT-905, LIT-582). "Rate differences help make structure and not only mark it: where some degrees of freedom settle faster than others by more than the rates vary among them, the relation they settle into is a constraint on the system over the window between the two rates, so what is a realization at one rate works as a constraint at another; and organization can make new rate differences in turn, as when modes slow near an instability and become order parameters". Version 5 (2026-11-20) replaced "scale separation" with "rate differences" at the owner's word ("maybe I'm being brash calling it 'scale separation'"), adding the condition "between against within": "Where the rates within a level vary more than they differ between levels, the rates still make structure, but not that level." What it does not say: "Not that slowness makes a constraint effective"; "Slowness makes a constraint *available*." And "Not new as mathematics. … The claim is the interpretation". Its defeater (a) is effective constraints that need no slowness (exact lumpability, CLAIM-394's reversible case, renormalization fixed points), "only if they are the typical case and not exceptions".
  - Its readings add limits the manuscript can use. Flack 2017: "failure may just take longer if the time scale separation is very large". Ashwin et al. 2012: separation "can be lost because the input speeds up, with no mode slowing". Scheffer et al. 2009 (note of 2026-11-03): "slowness alone does not show new organization forming". Caprioglio and Berthouze 2024 (2026-11-22) corroborate the version-5 condition.
  - On the record itself, CASE-180 (Active) measured a hierarchy of rates in the settled record, but "only as rates: nothing shows a relaxation", and "slowness and enforcement are not separated by this measurement".
- **CLAIM-331** (Active; interpretive; thesis; grounds LIT-905, THEORY-661, CASE-102, CASE-098, CASE-116). "higher-level variables are characteristically slower and longer-lived than the activity that produces them, though slowness is a mark of a level and not its definition". A8, quoted: "fast interactions reproduce slow variables, and slow variables condition subsequent fast interactions." What it does not say: "Not that higher means slower in every case" (a market crash, a stadium chant); "Not that a slow variable is a level"; "A variable's persistence is not, by itself, evidence of the persistence of the organization that generates it." Its note of 2026-10-18, on TTUR: "Slowness alone supplies no authority or motivational hierarchy."
- **CLAIM-327** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; grounds CASE-103, CASE-102, CASE-116; complements CLAIM-277, 331 and 478). "A slowly changing collective variable, such as a reputation, a rivalry or a standing, is part of a collective's dynamics and not only an observer's average when its members read it, condition what they do on it, and by doing so regenerate it". A9's three stages are statistical regularity, member-accessible structure and self-reinforcing organization. Its tests: "timescale separation (τ_Z ≫ τ_X, not sufficient)", predictive usefulness over a baseline, endogenous maintenance, and causal uptake under intervention. The notes cut its support. Of 2026-10-27: "the three stages are the exchange's synthesis of 2026-10-16, with regeneration where Flack has convergence … it is not Flack's." Of 2026-10-29: "the paper this claim quotes is evidence for the first stage only."
- **CLAIM-481** (Active; demonstrated; counter). "Coupling does not by itself separate time scales". Separation comes "where the coupling dissipates relative differences faster than collective ones", with rates a and a + 2k.
- **CLAIM-423** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-331). In a political collective, components at different speeds differ in "which forms of influence each can accumulate, exercise and keep, and so which of them holds authority over the others".

### 2c. Identity stabilization as temporal filtering (§4, §4.1)

- **CLAIM-tmppqdnv** (**Active** since 2026-11-23; demonstrated; thesis; no premise; complements CLAIM-tmph25dl and CLAIM-tmpx8csq). "an organizational variable that adapts at rate k to a noisily observed demand tracks variation slower than k and ignores variation faster, its tracking error grows with the demand's frequency as ω²/(k² + ω²) while the noise it passes grows with k as k²/(k² + ω²), so neither the slowest nor the fastest adaptation is optimal in general, and the per-frequency optimum S_d/(S_d + λS_n) passes variation where genuine demand outweighs noise". Checked: "0.7273 against the formula's 0.7273". A44's qualifications: an organization "selects which variations are allowed to alter which relationships", and "Fourier spectra cannot fully characterize event-triggered obligations, irreversible harms, or response deadlines". A dated note of 2026-11-23 draws a Daodejing analogy, with no edge.
- **CLAIM-tmp76xaw** (**Active** since 2026-11-23; interpretive; thesis; complements CLAIM-361, CLAIM-403 and CLAIM-466). "What an organization's filtering treats as noise is itself governed … governing the distinction between demand and disturbance is a higher-order task, and the capacity to recognize a demand as demand-worthy is a slow capability that must stay open to correction". A44: "the process deciding what merits a response has itself become an entrenched organizational mode." Its separating case: "the classification of signal against noise is a variable of the institution's temporal organization, and is not fixed by its admission rules."
- **CLAIM-719** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; no rests_on; uses TERM-128; a borderline member, §4.1). "An institution can be reliably responsive to reasons within its governing mechanism and unresponsive to justified reasons for revising that mechanism … responsiveness at the first order is no evidence of responsiveness at the second". Its own admission: "ρ^(2) is named, not defined: A gives no case family or response set for revisions of M."
- Neighbours held Active: **CLAIM-403** (admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and a part in revising standards "are separable"); **CLAIM-466** ("Lock-in from selectively admitted evidence and lock-in from self-scoring need different remedies"); **CLAIM-407** (a coarse-graining "exactly informationally closed under a process's ordinary dynamics need not preserve its responses to an intervention on the mechanism"), which CLAIM-tmp76xaw cites for "why the architecture must be tested by interventions, not ordinary operation".

### 2d. Which modes to stabilize (§5, §5.1)

- **CLAIM-626** (**Active** since 2026-11-23; demonstrated; thesis; grounds CASE-189; complements CLAIM-478, 521 and 481; bears on QUESTION-125). "Critical slowing has no single organizational meaning: when a collective mode slows, reduction to it improves; when the mode that restores a collective relation slows, as two detuned phase oscillators near the edge of locking, the reduction fails while the locked state still exists; when the maintenance of the coupling slows, the reduction stays good while the organization nears its fold". The transverse rate is λ⊥ = √(4K² − Δω²). "So an observed slow mode does not say, by itself, whether organization is forming, a reduction is failing, or a maintained capacity is failing." What it does not say: "That early-warning signals can tell the three apart"; "That the three kinds are exhaustive". Its note of 2026-11-21 adds two more readings of slowness, a conserved quantity spreading and a symmetry-protected soft mode, and "Neither signals a fold". Its note of 2026-11-22 records A40's governance reading: "a slow mode cannot automatically be interpreted as a stabilizing identity." Its note of 2026-11-23 calls A48 §5.1 a "Restatement, with drift": the manuscript "recasts whether the collective reduction stays good as whether coordination weakens or the relationship continues to function", and its lineage section "calls the claim Proposed, which it was until the status review of this date".
- **CLAIM-521** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). The maintenance's recovery rate "goes to zero at its saddle-node while the coupling's spectral gap there stays near 0.989". SYNTHESIS-048 quotes: "The coupled units are as tightly integrated at the edge of collapse as well inside the regime."
- **CLAIM-tmpbzwmr** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-tmph25dl, CLAIM-331 and CLAIM-tmpx8csq; complements CLAIM-626). "An organization's identity can be carried by slow collective degrees of freedom that constrain faster activity, while its adaptability requires those slow degrees of freedom to stay selectively controllable and the means of controlling them to stay viable". A40: "The same mathematics could describe forced conformity." Its admission is a discriminating prediction: "Among organizations with matched relaxation spectra, those whose slow modes lack actuated, affordable control directions should fail the demands that need those modes moved." Its note "what holds it back": the third clause "is CLAIM-tmph25dl's central clause, and CLAIM-tmph25dl is Proposed, waiting on CLAIM-478 and CLAIM-758."
- **CLAIM-tmpm985y** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-tmph25dl, CLAIM-099 and CLAIM-916; complements CLAIM-tmpcag0t and CLAIM-626). "Identity stabilization and adaptability are different achievements of one temporally organized system, not two competing quantities … some of its most important identity-bearing structures may be those that keep its other identity-bearing structures revisable". A44's transverse and tangent split: "Fast restoration of organization + slow transformation within organization", and "A strongly stabilized organization can be highly adaptable precisely because it does not need to reconstruct its basic coordination after every disturbance." Its admission: "for self-governing organizations, revisability is carried inside the organization's identity. This answers the owner's U20 on the weak reading of 'inherent'".

### 2e. Relaxation, control and reproduction timescales (§5.2)

- **CLAIM-345** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). "Equal controllability rank does not fix the cost of control … the least effort is Δxᵀ W_T^† Δx, set by the Gramian and not by its rank". Its sharp edge: "comparing costs across systems needs inputs in common units."
- **CLAIM-tmpx8csq** (**Active** since 2026-11-23; demonstrated; thesis; rests on CLAIM-345; complements CLAIM-589 and CLAIM-626). "A mode's slowness says how it persists, not how hard it is to redirect: for ȧ = −γa + bu the least quadratic effort to move a from 0 to Δ by time H is 2γΔ²/(b²(1 − e^{−2γH})), which falls as γ falls, so a slower mode is cheaper to redirect through the same input, and a mode with b = 0 cannot be redirected through it at any rate". It names "τ_relax (natural persistence), τ_control (least effective intervention time) and τ_reproduce (renewal of the mode's supports)". The monotonicity statement and wording are the assistant's. Its note of 2026-11-23 adds A42's third factor, a mode's "consequence for the demanded outcomes (Cv_j)" ("Another mode may be slow but irrelevant to the specified outcome"), and a fourth timescale, sensing.
- **CLAIM-589** (Active; demonstrated; thesis). "permitted directions, actuated directions and transitions achievable at acceptable cost are three objects, the second contained in the first at each instant".

### 2f. The tension and its timescale-indexed answer (the owner's U20; §3, §15)

- **CLAIM-tmp2k3c5** (**Active**; demonstrated; thesis). ### 2g. The temporal architecture of governance (§7)

- **CLAIM-711** (**Proposed**; interpretive; **counter**, objects to CLAIM-710; complements CLAIM-478 and CLAIM-730). ### 2h. Overconsolidation and lock-in (§11)

- **CLAIM-758** (**Proposed**; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-675 and CLAIM-569, both Active; uses TERM-131; lines learned-structure and readiness). "Overconsolidation, a generalization of overfitting: relative to a declared family of later acquisitions lying outside the training task distribution, continued stabilization of a learner can keep improving its present objective while raising the budget it needs to acquire that family … and it is a failure only relative to that family and horizon". What it lacks: "a case where present performance rises, the constrained cost of a declared out-of-distribution family rises with training time, and forgetting is measured, all in one run." Its note of 2026-11-23 calls A48 §11 an "Extension" to industrial ecosystems, "with the justification standard left open".

- **CLAIM-577** (Active; demonstrated): "any transition whose threshold exceeds the cost of holding the current configuration is lost after one period spent holding it". **CLAIM-679** (Active; demonstrated): at the limit "the organization can still hold where it is and can reach no configuration whose transition carries any reconfiguration slack". - **CLAIM-684** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis). "the burden of justifying it should rise with the size and the irreversibility of the narrowing". ## 3. Formal results and constructions (CASEs and derivations)

- **CASE-116** (Active; construction). Three finite-state systems. *Arm A*: a register flipping with probability 0.01, second eigenvalue 0.98, never read by the members: "Strong timescale separation exists, but constituent agents never read or regenerate Z." *Arm B*: members read and rewrite the register, second eigenvalue 0.2, do(Z_t) moving the next value 0.4 against 0.6: "slowness is not necessary for endogeneity", within A4's limit that "This demonstrates only *weak* endogenous maintenance." *Arm C*: a rule passed through an archive between episodes that never run together, which is transmission without running.
- **CASE-189** (Active; construction). It has reciprocal production holding a coupling, with "a saddle-node at damage 0.5", and a connector maintained by what it enables, with "a saddle-node at damage ≈ 0.691 where the coupling's gap is still ≈ 0.989". It grounds CLAIM-626's maintenance arm and CLAIM-521.
- **The locking computation** (CLAIM-626). φ̇ = Δω − 2K sin φ, τ_rel = 1/(2K√(1 − r²)), which diverges at r = 1. The one-unit reduction holds while λ⊥H ≫ 1, "so it is lost before the locked state is". "The reader reran every number".
- **The filter** (CLAIM-tmppqdnv). H_k(s) = k/(s + k), and the Wiener optimum T* = S_d/(S_d + λS_n), checked numerically.
- **The Gramian** (CLAIM-tmpx8csq, CLAIM-345). The one-mode least effort 2γΔ²/(b²(1 − e^{−2γH})), checked by quadrature at three settings and for monotonicity on 200 values of γ.
- **The retuning bounds** (CLAIM-tmp2k3c5 and CLAIM-tmp947v0). A necessary half, vH ≥ 1 − δ − ε ("tight when the practice can track freely within its band"), and a sufficient half, LV/γ ≤ ε, which "reaches the bound LV/γ exactly in the limit".
- **CLAIM-tmpdvgrf** (Active; demonstrated; rests on CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-tmpk4bou). The deadline-indexed complex "contained in the complex of the same demands without deadlines, so two demands can be compatible statically and incompatible in time". The containment step is the assistant's. A44: "one possible mechanism, not a guarantee of monotone expansion".
- **CASE-316** (Proposed). A queueing model, run as CASE-285's design. At ten times the speed, "the share of objectionable actions stopped before they became irreversible fell from 0.99 to 0.40". Precommitted limits won back what they covered, "keeping 0.92 to 0.99 of the fast arm's benefit", and "outside what they foresaw nothing was won back". The claim that states the result is CLAIM-879 (Proposed; demonstrated). Recorded on CLAIM-730: "the slow level kept the fast process answerable only while it had the capacity to hear what it held", and "The first clause is not tested: the model has no affected party and never revises a limit".
- **CASE-180** (Active). Measured from git, the record's prose is revised "about once in 22 days", its status "once in 128". It is evidence of rates, not of relaxation (CLAIM-478's note).
- **CASE-294** (Proposed study) and **LEAD-010 / LEAD-012** (Open, specified). The Pythia test of CLAIM-758: across checkpoints, whether "the cost of acquiring held-out task families rises while the pretraining objective improves, with forgetting measured". Not run.

## 4. Open QUESTIONs

- **QUESTION-391** (Open). "When does an effective constraint cease to be a good approximation because previously suppressed degrees of freedom become dynamically important?" CLAIM-478 `bears_on` it.
- **QUESTION-125** (Open). "When a candidate block gains or loses the gap that isolates it as a parameter varies, does the change in its identifiable boundary coincide with a bifurcation of its dynamics". SYNTHESIS-048: "The third region, a gap closing with the regime and its behaviour intact, still has no case."
- **QUESTION-500** (Open). What rate ratio between levels, "set against the spread of rates within a level", is enough for a level to show. This is the sharp threshold behind CLAIM-478's version 5.
- **QUESTION-440** (Open). "Can a relation of dynamical slaving realize the precedence, jurisdiction or suspension one process has over others in an organization, and does the authority it realizes go with the slower variable, the faster one, or neither?"
- **QUESTION-118** (Open). "what must stay causally continuous for the later organization to be the earlier one persisting, and not a similar one produced anew". It holds CLAIM-329's criterion short of settling continuation.
