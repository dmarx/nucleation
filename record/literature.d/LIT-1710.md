# LIT-1710: Reducing intergroup bias: The benefits of recategorization

Gaertner, S. L., Mann, J., Murrell, A. & Dovidio, J. F. (1989). *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 57(2), 239–249.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

Cited by Turner et al. 1994 ([NOTE-1284](../notes.d/NOTE-1284.md)) for the widening of self-categorization, and the experimental origin of the common in-group identity model behind Biel ([NOTE-1299](../notes.d/NOTE-1299.md)).

## Read in its place

The work cannot be read here, so the record holds it at second hand.

Dovidio et al. 2009 ([NOTE-1286](../notes.d/NOTE-1286.md)) report the experiment in one sentence
(p. 194): against a two-group condition, decategorization reduced bias by
making former in-group members less attractive, and recategorization by
making former out-group members more attractive. They also say the 1997
helping study used a procedure "similar to" its group manipulation.
Missing still: the design, the manipulations, the samples, the bias
measures and every number.
