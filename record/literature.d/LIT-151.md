# LIT-151: Stating Structural Realism: Mathematics-First Approaches to Physics and Metaphysics

David Wallace (2021), *Philosophical Perspectives 36(1), 345–378 (2022)* — DOI-10.1111/phpe.12172

## Key takeaways

- Once physical theories are read "math-first", with content given by mathematical structure plus representation rather than by sentences, scientific realism already is structural realism. The ESR/OSR choice then becomes a choice between language-first and math-first metaphysics, and on either view objects, properties and relations are derivative except, at most, at the fundamental level.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Wallace answers the charge that structural realism never clearly states an alternative to object/property/relation metaphysics done in predicate logic. He proposes a math-first view of theories, close to the semantic view, on which a theory's content is its mathematical structure and its representational relations to systems. Adopting this view is already a form of structural realism. The ESR/OSR choice becomes a choice between language-first and math-first metaphysics. He then examines the status of objects in fundamental and non-fundamental physics on both versions.
