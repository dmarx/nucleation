# LIT-1709: The Common Ingroup Identity Model: Recategorization and the Reduction of Intergroup Bias

Gaertner, S. L., Dovidio, J. F., Anastasio, P. A., Bachman, B. A. & Rust, M. C. (1993). *European Review of Social Psychology*, 4(1), 1–26.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

The model Biel ([NOTE-1299](../notes.d/NOTE-1299.md)) and Reimer et al. ([NOTE-1251](../notes.d/NOTE-1251.md)) use, and behind Pettigrew's recategorization ([LIT-1408](LIT-1408.md)).

## Read in its place

The work cannot be read here, so the record holds it at second hand.

Dovidio et al. 2009 ([NOTE-1286](../notes.d/NOTE-1286.md)) restate the model the 1993 paper set
out: four representations (one group, two subgroups within one group, two
groups, separate individuals) with distinct consequences, and the dual
identity as a second form of recategorization. They extend it to helping.
They do not cite the 1993 paper. Missing still: its account of how the
contact hypothesis's conditions work through recategorization, and its
laboratory and field evidence.
