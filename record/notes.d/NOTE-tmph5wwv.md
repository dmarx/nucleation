# NOTE-tmph5wwv: Zuboff (2022) — Surveillance Capitalism or Democracy?

## Contribution

A four-stage institutional model of surveillance capitalism built on the book's concepts.

## Key results

1. p. 3: "Who decides who knows?"
2. p. 4: "nearly all roads to economic, political, and social participation lead through its institutional terrain".
3. p. 4: conditions of practical and psychological "no exit".
4. The firms' leaders "were never elected to govern".

## Assumptions

Institutional orders compete; knowledge is power.

## Concepts

Surveillance capitalism; epistemic inequality; instrumentarian power; no exit.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpkjv7u**: a worked case of all three conditions: essentiality (result 2), consequential discretion by controllers no one elected (result 4), and no effective exit or challenge (result 3), with asymmetry of knowledge as the source of the discretion. Corroborates. It does not test the claim's other half.
- **CLAIM-tmpbypt7**: "no exit" supports the mechanism qualitatively, framed as institutionalization, not as the non-adopter's options.
