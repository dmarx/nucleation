The owner asked for Abramsky, Mansfield & Barbosa's cohomology of
contextuality, the follow-up the Ghose entry named. It is arXiv 1111.3620,
*The Cohomology of Non-Locality and Contextuality* (QPL 2011, EPTCS 95,
2012). It is filed as [LIT-tmpiz975](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpiz975.md) and read in full as [NOTE-tmppkatn](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmppkatn.md) (v2,
compared with v1 word by word). It is `Active`, and it `extends` [LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md).

**What it shows.**

- **The obstruction.** For a section s of context C₁, the obstruction γ(s)
  is the class of δ⁰(s, s₂, …, sₙ) in the relative group Ȟ¹(𝒰, F_{C̄₁}),
  where F is the presheaf of ℤ-combinations of support sections.
- **What vanishing means.** γ(s) = 0 iff s extends to a compatible family of
  ℤ-combinations (Prop 4.2), which need not be a global section. So a
  non-vanishing class witnesses possibilistic or strong contextuality, and
  only in one direction: it is sufficient, not necessary.
- **Where it works.** γ is non-zero for the PR box, GHZ, the 18-vector
  Kochen–Specker set and Peres–Mermin.
- **Where it fails.** γ vanishes on all of Hardy's model, and on 9 of 15
  sections of a strongly contextual Kochen–Specker cover.

The reader recomputed every example over ℤ and ℤ/2.

**A v1 error, verified.** v1's "strongly contextual" false-positive cover,
{A,B,C}, {A,D,E}, {B,D,E}, {A,D,F}, {A,E,G}, is not strongly contextual. I
enumerated it when filing: it has exactly two global Kochen–Specker
assignments. v2 silently replaced it.

**[NOTE-249](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-249.md) corrected (Ghose).** Its description of this construction was
written from general knowledge. "Not the class of a 0-coboundary" was wrong
as worded: γ(s) is a coboundary's class, taken relative to C₁ and built from
data that are not cochains of the relative presheaf. The note now says what
Ghose's [δs] drops (the relativisation, the anchoring and the cover), and
why that makes it vanish. The verdict on Ghose is unchanged.

**[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) bounded (version bump).** Its "does not say" gains a line: the
cohomological witness is a computable relaxation of the exact
global-section criterion, not an equivalent. The claim itself is unchanged.
