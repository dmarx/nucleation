# CLAIM-283: The precedence a motive claims over action and the authority the rest of the organization grants it are different quantities: a motive can claim control without receiving it, and be granted control it does not claim

## The claim

A4 §2: "Frijda's *control precedence* also suggests a useful addition to our
parameterization: p_i(x, t) = priority claimed by operator i. This is not
the same as granted influence I_i. An operator can urgently demand control
without receiving it. That distinction between *claimed precedence* and
*endorsed authority* may be essential to modeling internal conflict."

For Frijda ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)) an emotion is a state of action readiness that
claims control precedence. [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) has regulation as one motive state
checking another: anger held back by fear of retaliation. Put together with
the endorsement scheme, anger claims precedence and fear works by
withholding it. The conflict is the difference between what a state claims
and what it is granted. [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md)'s precedence is a maintained relation
among regimes, and [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md) says which regime is effective depends on such
relations. This claim splits precedence into a claim and a grant.

## What it does not say

- It does not say granted authority always wins. Impulsive action is the
  case where the claim outruns the grant and still governs.
- It does not say that internal conflict is the gap. That identification
  was this claim's second half until 2026-10-27, and is now [CLAIM-tmpxb1wf](CLAIM-tmpxb1wf.md),
  Proposed.
- It does not say the grant is made by a separate regulator. On [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md)
  it is made by other motive states, and [QUESTION-054](../questions.d/QUESTION-054.md) is open.

## Note of 2026-10-18: the gap as frustrated precedence

A34 §3 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 restated this claim's gap as p_i − w_i,
"a candidate indicator of frustrated precedence when the quantities have
been defined on comparable scales", and called it "the missing bridge"
between Frijda and the endorsement model. It is this claim's gap, not an
extension of it. A34 added the caution the claim's own "What it does not
say" implies: "Nor does disagreement between claimed and granted
authority necessarily indicate dysfunction: a good organization may
correctly prevent an unreliable process from taking control." And it
widened what frustration may depend on: "not merely on incompatible
goals, but on a process's inability to acquire the authority needed to
pursue a goal that remains consequential to the organization." The
frustration account rests on this claim ([CLAIM-368](CLAIM-368.md)): a demand that
is not jointly realizable is a precedence claimed and not granted. Whether
grants follow the demands that bind is [QUESTION-126](../questions.d/QUESTION-126.md).

## Note of 2026-10-23: two documented cases, and a split

From an audit of the will and affect neighbourhoods (workbench entry of
2026-10-24, U5). [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md) (Wells Fargo's risk and HR functions holding
relevant information without the power to change sales practices) and
[CASE-127](../cases.d/CASE-127.md) (United 173, a fuel concern that did not become effective in the
captain's decisions) illustrate the access–authority half of this claim:
recognizing significance and granting practical authority are separable,
and the path from one to the other runs through governance. They do not
show that any motive claimed precedence; that would need evidence of how
the actors evaluated the situation. [THEORY-603](../theory.d/THEORY-603.md) (Iowa gambling: patients
who could state the advantageous strategy and did not adopt it) is a third
instance, for [CLAIM-047](CLAIM-047.md) as much as here; [THEORY-500](../theory.d/THEORY-500.md)'s wider somatic-marker
mechanism stays Proposed. The audit suggested splitting this claim, the
analytical separation of claimed precedence from granted authority apart
from the identification of internal conflict with the gap, and auditing
the first for promotion under [ADR-047](../decisions.d/ADR-047.md). Not done here.


## Note of 2026-10-27: the concept, not the account

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md)) moved this claim from grounding
in [THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md) to using [TERM-tmp3mqer](../terms.d/TERM-tmp3mqer.md), Frijda's action readiness with
control precedence. The claim uses the concept. It does not need the
account's thesis about what elicits an emotion, which [THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md) contests
and which stays Proposed.

## Note of 2026-10-27: narrowed and promoted

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md)) narrowed this claim to the
distinction and promoted it. Each of its four dependents ([CLAIM-368](CLAIM-368.md),
[CLAIM-440](CLAIM-440.md), [CLAIM-442](CLAIM-442.md) and [CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md)) uses the distinction and none uses
the identification of conflict with the gap, which is now [CLAIM-tmpxb1wf](CLAIM-tmpxb1wf.md).
Its premises are the TERMs it uses, both Active. [THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md) and [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md)
had been grounds. The directive above already said "nothing here rests on
their being settled". The first is replaced by its concept, [TERM-tmp3mqer](../terms.d/TERM-tmp3mqer.md).
The second is cited in the body as one account of how the grant is made,
which the claim leaves open. No objection is filed. Its defeater's first
clause is against the documented cases ([CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md), [CASE-127](../cases.d/CASE-127.md), [THEORY-603](../theory.d/THEORY-603.md)),
where significance was recognized and authority not granted. Its second
clause asks for an argument that granted authority adds nothing once the
competing motives are counted. [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) is the closest account, and on
it the grant is the outcome of the competition, which is still a different
quantity from what one motive claims.
