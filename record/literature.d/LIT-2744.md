# LIT-2744: Property and Ownership

Jeremy Waldron and James Penner (2004; substantive revision 2026). "Property and Ownership". *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, first published 6 September 2004, substantive revision 21 January 2026.

## Key takeaways

*From §§1–2 ([NOTE-2050](../notes.d/NOTE-2050.md)).*

- Private property assigns resources "to the decisional authority of
  particular individuals", who may act "without giving anyone else an
  explanation"; so it "is continually in need of public justification".
- The bundle of rights (Honoré) "has encountered resistance (Penner 1996;
  Smith 2012; Klein & Robinson 2011)"; bundle theorists usually see the
  right to exclude as key, others stress "the owner's power of
  agenda-setting".
- Default title: "one (Hohfeldian) liberty which is protected with one
  (Hohfeldian) claim right, and two (Hohfeldian) powers".
