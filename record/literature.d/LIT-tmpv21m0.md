# LIT-tmpv21m0: Hydrodynamic Stability Without Eigenvalues

Lloyd N. Trefethen, Anne E. Trefethen, Satish C. Reddy and Tobin A. Driscoll (1993), *Science* 261(5121), 578–584 — DOI-10.1126/science.261.5121.578

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for.

- For plane Couette and Poiseuille flow, eigenvalue analysis predicts
  stability at Reynolds numbers where turbulence is in fact observed.
- The linearized operators are highly non-normal, so disturbances can grow
  transiently by large factors, increasing with Reynolds number, before
  they decay.
- Pseudospectra reveal the potential for transient growth that eigenvalues
  hide, and nonlinearity can take an amplified disturbance into
  turbulence.
