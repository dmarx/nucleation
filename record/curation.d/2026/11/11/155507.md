The second by-question reading review ([ADR-091](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-091.md)) flagged [QUESTION-297](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-297.md),
[QUESTION-299](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-299.md) and [QUESTION-301](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-301.md), and in part [QUESTION-298](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-298.md) and [QUESTION-302](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-302.md):
their evidence was some seventy works read into NOTEs on 2026-10-29 and
2026-10-30 with no reading filed, so nothing the argument could weigh. At
the owner's word to proceed, this pass files them under [ADR-068](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-068.md).

## The list

[NOTE-1246](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1246.md) to [NOTE-1322](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1322.md): 71 NOTEs whose LIT no THEORY names in `source`.
Each reader grepped `record/theory.d/` for its LITs before filing; none
had a reading, so none was skipped. Three more were added to the legacy
part: [NOTE-644](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-644.md) (Abramsky and Sadrzadeh, [LIT-841](../../record/literature.d/LIT-841.md)), which [QUESTION-026](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-026.md)
names and no reading covered; and the two passages [QUESTION-366](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-366.md) quotes,
[LIT-1902](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1902.md) p. 1366 ([NOTE-1484](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1484.md)) and [LIT-1922](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1922.md) proposition 4 ([NOTE-1483](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1483.md)),
filed because both NOTEs hold them.

Four readers, by subject, each in its own worktree; the filer combined
them without conflict, since every file is new.

## Left for the owner or a status review

- **Promotion.** Each NOTE is a reading in full ([NOTE-1312](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1312.md) skimmed; a few
  read from a working paper or another version, which the summaries say),
  so most `promote_when` are likely met now. A status review can move them.
- **Stale NOTE lines.** Most of these NOTEs say "No THEORY filed", some
  with a reason (a framework, thin evidence, a normative model). The
  attributed titles meet those reasons; the lines were left as written.
  [NOTE-1260](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1260.md) and [NOTE-1261](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1261.md) point at "NOTE-YYYY" placeholders, and [NOTE-1259](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1259.md)
  cites itself as Wade-Benzoni et al.
- **Grounds on a LIT that now has a reading.** [CLAIM-491](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-491.md) grounds on
  [LIT-1735](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1735.md) and [LIT-1703](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1703.md), [CLAIM-494](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-494.md) on [LIT-1729](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1729.md), [CLAIM-044](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-044.md) on [LIT-841](../../record/literature.d/LIT-841.md).
  Moving those grounds to the readings is the claims' side.
- **[CLAIM-491](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-491.md) and Eyerman, Volkan.** The claim's note says they "support
  its shape and move its boundary"; the NOTEs call the support inference
  and Volkan's "narrower". Not tied: a contested support wants an ARG.
- **[QUESTION-300](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-300.md) has twenty readings.** The weakest ties are Reimer et
  al. ([NOTE-1251](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1251.md)), Rubin and Hewstone ([NOTE-1278](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1278.md)), Akerlof and Kranton
  ([NOTE-1311](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1311.md)) and Scheepers and Ellemers ([NOTE-1315](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1315.md)).
- **Horsthemke on [QUESTION-297](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-297.md)** may be an offered part-answer ([ADR-060](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-060.md)),
  since the question calls his argument "the second answer it allows".
- **The gluing obstruction.** [NOTE-644](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-644.md)'s summary, [LIT-841](../../record/literature.d/LIT-841.md) and [QUESTION-026](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-026.md)
  say inconsistency is the only obstruction to gluing. The NOTE's own key
  results give a non-linguistic example that fails without it; the new
  reading's title says "in their linguistic examples".
- **Ord and MacAskill.** [QUESTION-299](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-299.md)'s note groups the significance ×
  persistence × contingency accounting with Ord's trajectory chapter; it
  is MacAskill, Thomas and Vallinder's. The readings attribute it so.
- **Duplicate readings of [LIT-1922](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1922.md)**: [THEORY-1276](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1276.md) with [THEORY-1534](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1534.md), and
  [THEORY-1277](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1277.md) with [THEORY-1535](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1535.md).
- **A count in the record disagrees.** [NOTE-1274](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1274.md) says Batson et al. 1997
  ran "a pair of experiments"; elsewhere the record says four.
