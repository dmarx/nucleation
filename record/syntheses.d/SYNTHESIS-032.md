# SYNTHESIS-032: Transient support can be withdrawn safely only under conditions the record states jointly in one claim: supply that does the upkeep never becomes optional, supply that does the party's work lowers the capacity it keeps and, where capacity is needed to grow capacity, has a deadline after which withdrawal does not restore it, a savings buffer is a stock of time, and a capability lost to reliable supply is dependency or division of labour by who supplies it; most of the line's questions are replications of the readings it rests on

*The account of the transient-support line, "Withdrawing transient
support", split from readiness ([ADR-087](../decisions.d/ADR-087.md)), where it was a part
account. It is the part filed from the DNA
origami, aid and development, ecology, urban, medicine and windfall
readings of 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06, on A's [QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md). Since [ADR-tmpqp8c4](../decisions.d/ADR-tmpqp8c4.md)
(2026-11-11) [QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md) and the entries of the withdrawal inquiry have
transient-support first, QUESTION-399 with them, and carry realizability
only where they bear on a realizability-first entry: none does, and only
CASE-225, which has it first, carries it. [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md)
also keeps readiness, because it bears on [QUESTION-386](../questions.d/QUESTION-386.md) ([ADR-085](../decisions.d/ADR-085.md)), and
the readiness line's account integrates it too.*

## The account

- **Supplied upkeep.** [CLAIM-597](../claims.d/CLAIM-597.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated,
  Active): where every replacement part comes from outside, "an
  organization can become independent of what founded it but never of
  what feeds it"; the intact share holds at ρs/(ρs + d) and decays as
  e^(−dt) once the supply stops. Its body adds, for readiness, that such a
  cost is "easy to read as waste while it is working".
- **Supply that does the work.** [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active): where capacity is learned by doing and decays
  with disuse, supply used first lowers the capacity kept and lengthens
  recovery, while supply used after capacity leaves learning alone, so
  "what decides dependence is the order in which supply and capacity are
  used, not the amount of supply". [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active), resting on [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) and [CLAIM-604](../claims.d/CLAIM-604.md), adds the
  threshold: full substitution has a deadline (3.10 time units in the
  example), and "a slow taper of such support is worse than an abrupt
  cut". [CASE-225](../cases.d/CASE-225.md) (A's, Proposed) separates dependence for timing,
  dependence for performance and suppression of a latent capacity, and
  says suppression becomes atrophy only through a slow variable, which is
  [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md)'s and [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s disuse decay.
- **A buffer as time.** [CLAIM-601](../claims.d/CLAIM-601.md) (the assistant's, after the owner's
  reading that deprivation adds time pressure; demonstrated, Active): "A
  savings buffer is a stock of time"; below a buffer that rises with a
  payoff's delay, declining a profitable investment is the policy that
  meets the deadline, and covering needs through the delay buys the time.
- **Who supplies.** [CLAIM-603](../claims.d/CLAIM-603.md) (the assistant's, on the owner's remark
  linking assured help to specialization; interpretive, Proposed): a
  capability lost to reliable supply is the Black Queen's loss, and
  whether it is dependency or division of labour is decided by the
  supply, so "the aim framed as self-sufficiency is better framed as
  reciprocity". Its note of 2026-11-06 records two holds: the title states
  the favoured loss generally against counterpoint readings that find no
  such response in poor households, and Moore's thesis ([THEORY-1459](../theory.d/THEORY-1459.md)) is
  not held.
- **When withdrawal is safe.** [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) (the assistant's, interpretive,
  Active), resting on [CLAIM-597](../claims.d/CLAIM-597.md), [CLAIM-604](../claims.d/CLAIM-604.md), [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) and [CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md),
  states the conditions together, as necessary since its version 8: a
  self-sustaining process on a lasting flux, support large enough to pass
  its threshold, the threat gone, and the party doing each subtask itself
  at a bearable effort; standing inputs stay supplied, and "persistence
  after withdrawal is needed to tell which, though slow reversion can
  mimic it". Its title: "meeting them all is not enough, since support can
  carry a function nobody names before withdrawal". Its "Why it no longer
  claims sufficiency" section gives the village water scheme, which met
  every condition and still failed; its "Admission" section gives a case
  that separates the narrower claim from its premises, and the conditions
  are judged before withdrawal.

What stays open is [QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md) (A's, Open): under what conditions a
partial organization becomes independent of the intervention that
established it, with A's measure D(s), and A's separation "The nucleus
can become unnecessary while the organization it helped establish
persists". [QUESTION-432](../questions.d/QUESTION-432.md) refines it for aid; [QUESTION-401](../questions.d/QUESTION-401.md), [QUESTION-413](../questions.d/QUESTION-413.md) and
[QUESTION-427](../questions.d/QUESTION-427.md) bear on it.
