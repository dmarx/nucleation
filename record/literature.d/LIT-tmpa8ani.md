summary: >-
  Di Tella & Franceschelli (2011; NBER 2009, revised 2010). Across the four main Argentine newspapers, April 1998–December 2007, monthly government advertising correlates negatively with front-page coverage of the government's corruption scandals, within newspaper and month and within newspaper-president pairs: one standard deviation of advertising goes with 0.31 fewer front pages a month, 25 percent of a standard deviation, in the 2010 working paper (0.23 and 18 percent in the published abstract). The association holds for advertising placed by the presidency's agency and not for autonomous agencies, and not for scandals of non-political actors.
