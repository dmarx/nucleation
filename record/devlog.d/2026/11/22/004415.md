---
title: 'Going wrong, caught by the owner: read the owner''s bulk as the many directions when the owner meant the dominant ones, and treated a falling training loss as less than learning'
created: '2026-11-22T00:44:15'
tags:
- retro
---

**What happened.** Relaying the reading of Song, Ahn and Yun, the assistant wrote that their "bulk" means "everything outside the top k, not the small-eigenvalue tail", and that the paper "doesn't show that small directions carry information", because "'Learning' here means only that the training loss falls". The filed notes on [QUESTION-511](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-511.md) and [NOTE-tmpw0eql](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpw0eql.md) repeated the second point.

**How it was found.** The owner: "you have my intended meaning of 'bulk' backwards, I meant that in the sense of the domain which carries the dominant density. in the context of e.g. the 80-20 rule, the *bulk* of the population does not contribute the *bulk* of the activity. Spectrally, the top eigenvalues definitionally are associated with the dominating 'bulk' of the operator's activity." And: "training loss falling is evidence that learning is happening. the capabilities are being measurably developed. "learning" doesn't just mean the loss is falling, that's a measure of the learning that is happening, which is concretely the changes that are happening within the organization of the model's representational space."

**The fix.** [TERM-tmpf5ymd](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpf5ymd.md) separates the bulk by count from the bulk by weight. Dated corrections on [QUESTION-511](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-511.md) and [NOTE-tmpw0eql](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpw0eql.md) say what Song et al. do show in the owner's terms: capability develops through the non-dominant directions.

**What to keep.** Two habits gave the slip. The first took a source's technical word, Song's "bulk subspace", for the owner's ordinary one. The second set the bar for "learning" at decodability, so that the paper's own measure of learning counted for less. When a source and the owner use the same word, say which sense is meant before arguing from it.
