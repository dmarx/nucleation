The owner asked how the record covered social ontology, then for the
literature to be filed. The record held the philosophy of mind of groups
(Schwitzgebel's conscious United States, Kammerer, List, with List & Pettit
and Huebner unread) and almost nothing on what a group, an institution, a
convention or a social kind is. Searle appeared only for the Chinese Room.
`social-ontology` was added first ([ADR-017](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-017.md)) and six existing works took
it; then three agents filed 24 works in parallel. Eight were read in full.

## What it did to the record

[THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) v3 takes a scoping note from Ritchie and Epstein: groups with
the same members can be distinct, only organised groups are concrete
wholes, and a group's facts may depend on non-members. So any criterion
for group minds has to say which group it is applied to. None of these
readings discusses group experience, so the account itself does not move.

The filing agent for Haslanger and Ásta noted that nothing in the
vocabulary names gender, race or feminist philosophy, and proposed
`feminist-philosophy`. Two works do not make a topic. It is left for a
filing that brings more.
