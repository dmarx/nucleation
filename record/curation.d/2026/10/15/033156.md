The owner asked: "I think there are some (albeit half baked, maybe
quackish but at the very least directionally correct) ideas built around
LEGO case studies that would be useful for us to port to (or modify for)
the nucleation record: see `Main Project/Geometric Epistemology/The Lego
Parable`". Mathematics of Meaning was read at commit d987e2a. Every MOM
code cited below was checked against that clone, since [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md) says a bad
MOM code is caught no other way.

## What was read

- **The monograph.** About 57,000 words in three parts, LLM-drafted. Read
  in full: the Preface, the outlines, Part II (the LEGO analogy, chapters
  1–9 and 7b), and from Part I the chapters on SDT, singularity resolution
  and multiple collectors, and "Definitions and Theorems". From Part III:
  the LEGO/LDA chapters and the worked example on archetype persistence.
  The rest of Part III, and Part I's chapters 0, 1, 3–5 and 7–9, were not
  read.
- **MOM's own grading of it.** [MOM-CLM-135](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-135.md), [MOM-CLM-136](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-136.md), [MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md),
  [MOM-CLM-138](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-138.md), [MOM-ARG-047](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/arguments.d/ARG-047.md) and [MOM-CLM-166](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-166.md). MOM had already done the
  checking, so this port builds on its verdicts rather than repeating
  them.
- **The owner's own parable.** `homelab/own-words.md`, 2026-07-02. It is
  not in the monograph and is the best LEGO material in the repository:
  retrieval, similarity and the time–space trade, told alongside the EMT
  case this record already holds ([CASE-085](../../record/cases.d/CASE-085.md)).

## What was filed

- **[CASE-tmpd5exw](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpd5exw.md)**, the owner's LEGO parable, a stipulated thought experiment. It
  is the second time [CASE-085](../../record/cases.d/CASE-085.md)'s pattern has turned up, so it gets a code.
- **[CLAIM-tmp6u639](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6u639.md)** (Active), from [MOM-CLM-166](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-166.md). Retrieval and evidence are
  different criteria for a category, and they move oppositely with copy
  count. It complements [CLAIM-154](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-154.md), the detection criterion dual-filed from
  [MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md) on 2026-10-11. MOM's table was re-run and reproduced, and a
  simulation of linear search agreed with m/(c+1) to within about 1%. One
  correction: that the criteria disagree on every row holds for MOM's
  deadline, not in general.
- **[CASE-tmpjgubn](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpjgubn.md)** (fictional construction) and **[CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md)** (Proposed). This is the
  directionally correct part of the monograph's contextuality, modified.
  As descriptions, sortings of bricks always share a joint refinement
  ([MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)) and their events stay Boolean ([MOM-ARG-047](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/arguments.d/ARG-047.md)), so the
  monograph's central example is the non-contextual case of [CLAIM-037](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-037.md) and
  [CLAIM-131](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-131.md). As arrangements under a bin budget, they really compete. The
  cheapest depends on the query mix and on what stands out within a bin.
  The monograph's "Sheafification as Descriptive Compromise" survives as the choice
  between the meet and the join of two partitions.
- **[QUESTION-tmplg07t](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplg07t.md)**, refining [QUESTION-095](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-095.md): is sorting amortized search, and is
  database index selection the cost model [QUESTION-095](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-095.md) lacks?
- **[QUESTION-tmpjnf86](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpjnf86.md)**: evidence, retrieval and persistence across refinement as
  three criteria for naming a category. It takes Part III's persistence
  example, with its spectrum checked here, and the observation
  [MOM-CLM-138](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-138.md) kept after rejecting the metaphysics.
- **[LIT-tmp9g55n](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp9g55n.md)** and **[LIT-tmp95pcw](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp95pcw.md)**, Treisman and Gelade 1980 and Duncan and
  Humphreys 1989, Deferred. Both are the standard sources for the
  similarity effect in visual search, which [CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md) relies on. Citations
  checked against Crossref. Neither is in the anthology (grep of its clone
  at d1573d2).

## Not ported, and why

- **The Epistemic Geometry Theorem** (Part II chapter 9) and Part I's
  Theorem 4.3. Their commutativity condition holds for every pair of
  sortings ([MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)), so the equivalence would make every presheaf a
  sheaf. The extended version, which adds "bounded Rademacher complexity"
  as an equivalent condition, has no proof and confuses consistency with
  capacity.
- **The topos chapter, the Čech cocycle definition, and "meta-contexts"
  and epistemic stacks.** These are standard definitions with no instance
  that is not empty in this framework, and the cocycle definition is
  garbled. [CLAIM-153](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-153.md) already holds the record's worked monodromy case.
- **SDT as non-neutral measurement** (Part I chapter 2). Correct, and
  already held: [THEORY-031](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-031.md) and [QUESTION-103](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-103.md).
- **Identifiability as a quotient, and singularity resolution** (Part I
  chapter 6, "Definitions and Theorems" modules 1–3). [MOM-CLM-135](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-135.md) grades
  these correct. [CLAIM-264](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-264.md) and [CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md) already hold restricted
  observational equivalence. Nothing here needed the blow-up, so it was
  not filed.
- **LEGO sets as LDA documents** (Part III). A sound analogy for a
  hierarchical mixture, with no claim of its own to test.
- **Two collectors with different collections** (Part I chapter 10). Its
  contextuality claim is [MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)'s defect again. What it gestures at,
  that a builder's bins are fitted to their own collection, follows from
  [CLAIM-tmp6u639](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6u639.md): bins depend on copy counts and use.
