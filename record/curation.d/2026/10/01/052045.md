The anthology's issue 180
(https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/issues/180) works through the
owner's reading feed by how often a paper was returned to: papers opened on
three or more separate days, ranked from the papers-feed snapshot of 30
September. Twenty-nine of them were absent from the anthology. This entry
records what that list meant for this record.

**The trap: the ranking checked one record.** The worklist was computed
against the anthology's `arxiv:` ids only, so six papers already held here
looked unfiled: the work capacity of channels with memory ([LIT-041](../../record/literature.d/LIT-041.md)),
collective intelligence ([LIT-046](../../record/literature.d/LIT-046.md)), community detection with Ricci flow
([LIT-004](../../record/literature.d/LIT-004.md)), spectral networks ([LIT-017](../../record/literature.d/LIT-017.md)), sharing disinformation incidents
([LIT-083](../../record/literature.d/LIT-083.md)) and ZipNN ([LIT-268](../../record/literature.d/LIT-268.md)). Any future pass over the feed should
check both records' ids before calling a paper absent.

**Filed here.** Chuai & Zhao's *Anger makes fake news viral online*
([LIT-tmpnay4k](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpnay4k.md)) came here at the owner's direction: how emotion drives the
spread of false news is not something an anthology topic holds. It is a
`Deferred` seed, read for triage only.

**Held in both, under [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md).** Two works now have an entry on each side,
and each side reads them for a question of its own:

- *On Context-Content Uncertainty Principle* ([LIT-tmp7gmxt](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp7gmxt.md), with
  [NOTE-tmpjkarl](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpjkarl.md)). This record holds the v1 the owner read, a cognitive and
  information-theoretic framework, and rejects it on its proofs: a theorem
  stated backwards, a false KL inequality (q = (.5, .25, .25),
  p = (.8, .1, .1) gives 0.322 bits against the 0.578 the inequality needs),
  and simulations the abstract promises and the paper does not contain. The
  anthology holds the author's v2, a different paper under a new title that
  makes claims about generalization, mixture-of-experts routing and
  alignment, as a seed with no experiments.
- *ZipNN* ([LIT-268](../../record/literature.d/LIT-268.md)). This record reads it for where the redundancy in stored
  weights sits; the anthology files it as a systems paper on checkpoint
  storage and transfer.

The entries name each other once the anthology's codes are permanent: its
side is filed with temporary codes and is renamed when it merges, so the
`ANTH-LIT-…` citations [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) asks for are a follow-up.

**Also held in the anthology now.** The work-capacity, collective
intelligence and Ricci-flow papers were first read on the anthology side and
not filed there, because no anthology topic held them honestly. The owner
approved the topics its reader proposed (`agents-and-environments` and
`graphs-and-networks`), and they are filed there as well, each read for what
it says about agents or graph models, where this record reads them for
information theory, complex systems and network science.

**Also found:** [LIT-041](../../record/literature.d/LIT-041.md)'s arXiv id now serves a v2 (30 September 2026) under a
new title, which failed the source check on `main`. The note records both
titles, and its reading covers v1 only.
