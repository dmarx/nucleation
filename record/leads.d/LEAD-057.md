# LEAD-057: On one dynamical model, compare a fixed preexisting partition, one recovered from measured dynamics, and one produced by coupling to an apparatus, and ask whether they make different counterfactual predictions

## What it would do

Pick one model and work the three partitions; say which of the three any
answer is about, as the note of 2026-11-07 asks.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md): "whether the three accounts make different counterfactual
  predictions, and whether any evidence distinguishes ontological
  constitution from ordinary causal modification."

## What would count

Different counterfactual predictions answer [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md) and say what
evidence would decide among them; identical ones say the dispute is not
empirical.
