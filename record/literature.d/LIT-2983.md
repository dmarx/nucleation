# LIT-2983: A Random Matrix Theory Perspective on the Spectrum of Learned Features and Asymptotic Generalization Capabilities

Yatin Dandi, Luca Pesce, Hugo Cui, Florent Krzakala, Yue M. Lu & Bruno Loureiro (2024). arXiv 2410.18938; AISTATS 2025 (PMLR 258), unconfirmed from the copy read.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A cites it at A32 for spikes in the feature spectrum after a gradient step. Read on 2026-11-22 (NOTE-tmpdac0o) and filed as THEORY-tmpcij35. The spike is in the weights; in the features, learning shows chiefly as a reshaped bulk. It grounds CLAIM-799's point that the spiked picture of learned features is a theorem, and qualifies it (ARG-tmp2mzk1). Ba and colleagues (LIT-tmpnwvnx), Moniri and colleagues (LIT-tmp4kae5), Cui and colleagues (LIT-tmpj4og3) and Wang, Engel and colleagues (LIT-tmpcyeab) are registered from this reading.
