# LIT-107: Quasi-set theory: a formal approach to a quantum ontology of properties

Federico Holik, Juan Pablo Jorge, Décio Krause, Olimpia Lombardi (2021), *Synthese 200(5), article 401 (2022)* — DOI-10.1007/s11229-022-03884-8

## Key takeaways

- If quantum systems are bundles of property-instances with no principle of individuality, then standard logic and set theory, which are built on individual constants and variables, cannot describe them. Quasi-set theory can, with bundles formed by a choice over qsets of indistinguishable property-instances.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Earlier work by the authors (Lombardi, da Costa and others) proposed an ontology of properties for quantum mechanics, in which quantum systems are bundles of properties that lack any principle of individuality. This paper argues that quasi-set theory, built to handle collections of items that are not individuals, is a suitable meta-language for that ontology and its structure.
