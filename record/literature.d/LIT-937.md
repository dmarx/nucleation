# LIT-937: Post-Hoc Concept Disentanglement: From Correlated to Isolated Concept Representations

Eren Erogullari, Sebastian Lapuschkin, Wojciech Samek, Frederik Pahde (2025), *arXiv:2503.05522*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-816](../notes.d/NOTE-816.md).*

- **Separate fits align.** Pattern-CAVs, argmin ‖Z − t wᵀ − b‖² fitted
  per concept, give a cosine matrix whose blocks match the label
  correlation matrix. CelebA shows two blocks, which the paper calls
  female- and male-associated. FunnyBirds shows its injected 70%
  beak–tail correlation. Anti-correlated concepts point in opposite
  directions.
- **Orthogonal directions exist.** Adding α‖CCᵀ − I‖²_F to the
  objective and fine-tuning for 300 epochs reaches near-perfect
  orthogonality in every dataset–model pair. AUROC is "largely
  preserved". Per-concept relative changes fall within about ±20%, with
  gains and losses in both blocks. For α between 10⁻⁴ and 10⁰, average
  AUROC is slightly above the unpenalised fit.
- **So the alignment is in the fit.** By the reader's derivation, Eq. 2
  gives w_c ∝ Cov(z, t_c), which mixes the directions of correlated
  concepts in proportion to their label covariance.
- **Steering.** Moving a diffusion autoencoder's latent along the
  baseline "necktie" direction also adds a mustache and eyeglasses, and
  the orthogonal direction does not. In a controlled shortcut
  experiment, removing the orthogonal "timestamp" direction costs less
  clean accuracy. Both are shown in figures only.
- **Limits.** There are no values, seeds or error bars in the text, and
  AUROC is the only check of meaning. The paper tests two small CNNs,
  one layer, and correlated (not hierarchical) attributes.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-025](../questions.d/QUESTION-025.md), and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-816](../notes.d/NOTE-816.md). It was registered as "a measured case of correlated
attributes yielding non-orthogonal, entangled concept directions". The
reading corrects that. What is measured is the alignment of separately
fitted directions. The paper's own result is that orthogonal directions
separating each concept about as well exist. So, in supervised CNN
features, correlated attributes keep linear directions. That is filed as
[THEORY-292](../theory.d/THEORY-292.md), which partially answers the correlated branch of
[QUESTION-025](../questions.d/QUESTION-025.md). It does not reach hierarchical attributes or the lattice.
It sits beside [THEORY-185](../theory.d/THEORY-185.md)'s case of correlated attributes in a PMI
embedding and [THEORY-117](../theory.d/THEORY-117.md)'s passage from entangled to factored features.
The steering results are ML practice, hence the `anthology-candidate`
flag.
