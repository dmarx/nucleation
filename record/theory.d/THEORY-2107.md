# THEORY-2107: Tomasello argues that a participant can understand a social role only inside cooperation structured by shared intentionality, a joint goal with individual roles, and that taking part in such dual-level collaboration, which chimpanzees do not, leads young children to perspectival and objective thinking, role-based concepts, self-other equivalence and fairness, and a normative moral identity

## The account

Roles arise for participants only within collaboration under a joint goal
with individual roles. The dual-level structure, shared goal and attention
above, individual roles and perspectives below, yields perspectival and
then objective thinking, role-based relational concepts, self-other
equivalence and fairness with role ideals as the first shared normative
standards, and concern for how we judge me, ending in moral identity and
guilt.

## Evidence

Review of comparative and developmental experiments, mostly from the
author's lab.
