# THEORY-2255: Discourse sheaves settle by a Poisson equation

## Source

Bosca and Ghrist (2026), [LIT-2259](../literature.d/LIT-2259.md), skimmed in [NOTE-2346](../notes.d/NOTE-2346.md).

## What was actually shown

Theorems on linear gradient flows: convergence of constrained opinion and
structure flows, an auxiliary sheaf whose Laplacian is the structure
Laplacian, a conservation law for the joint flow, and displacement bounds
under timescale separation.

## What this does not say

That people or agents follow these flows. That the bounds are sharp. That
agreement reached by adapting expressions is distinguishable, from inside
the model, from agreement reached by changing beliefs.
