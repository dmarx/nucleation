# LIT-1095: An Online Approach to Physical Design Tuning

Bruno, N. and Chaudhuri, S. (2007). An online approach to physical design tuning. In *Proceedings of the 23rd IEEE International Conference on Data Engineering (ICDE 2007)*, pp. 826–835. DOI 10.1109/ICDE.2007.367928

## Key takeaways

Read in full ([NOTE-896](../notes.d/NOTE-896.md)). An always-on index tuner for SQL Server. It keeps, for each candidate or existing index, the running sum of what each query would have saved with it (what-if optimizer estimates, with update costs counted as losses). It creates an index once that sum has risen by the index's build cost above its lowest point, and drops it once the sum has fallen by the build cost from its highest point. For one index with no space limit, the authors call this 3-competitive against the optimal schedule that knows the workload in advance. The proof only gives a workload on which the ratio approaches 3, and the paper cites no competitive-analysis work, ski rental included. The general case (many interacting indexes and a storage limit) uses heuristics with no guarantee. All costs in the experiments are optimizer estimates, on synthetic and TPC-H workloads.
