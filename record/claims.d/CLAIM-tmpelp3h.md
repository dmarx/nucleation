# CLAIM-tmpelp3h: No validated measure of footing or stance exists in the record, and the one repeated assignment of footing, the analyst's own, changed between passes

## The objection

Footing and stance are what the line's theses say survives or is lost.
[CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s defeat condition measures "pragmatic observables (stance,
footing, alignment, expected uptake)". [CLAIM-061](CLAIM-061.md) says renderings can differ
"in footing and illocutionary force". [CLAIM-084](CLAIM-084.md) makes the primary outcome of
a reconstruction study "pragmatic-frame retention judged blind to
condition", and the A70 passage it quotes says that "requires independently
elicited judgments about footing, stance, and communicative function". The
designs fix the response categories in advance: [CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md) elicits
"condemnation, solidarity, amusement and irony", and [CASE-040](../cases.d/CASE-040.md) collects
interpretive judgements of "tone, stance, solidarity, and authority".

The only footing judgement the record has made twice of the same texts
disagreed with itself. [CASE-012](../cases.d/CASE-012.md): "The speaker labels drifted. A38 §2.2
called the four 'affectionate teasing, cynical complicity, resigned
observation, and social criticism'; A39 §1.2 made them 'A fellow
participant teasing an acquaintance', 'A worldly insider articulating an
informal rule', 'A sympathetic observer recognizing a recurrent failure'
and 'A critic of consumption and lifestyle inflation', so that 'The party
always gets its cut' went from cynical complicity to an informal rule
without comment." Its conclusion: "The speakers are the analyst's
attribution, not a property of the wording." The judge was one analyst
labelling texts it had itself written and knowing what each was meant to
do, and its labels did not hold between two passes.

No design in the record supplies what a measure of footing needs:

- a definition given to judges ("footing" is a technical term, Goffman's,
  [LIT-780](../literature.d/LIT-780.md), which the record holds unread);
- a response format beyond the analyst's category list;
- a reliability target, between judges or between passes of one judge;
- a validation against a contrast whose answer is fixed in advance, such
  as renderings built to differ only in the attributed speaker.

The manuscript's split annotators (§10, as [QUESTION-005](../questions.d/QUESTION-005.md) reports it) guard
against fitting a measure on its own test data. They do not show that the
judgements are reliable. And the readings do not reach the footing half.
[CLAIM-061](CLAIM-061.md)'s "Support from dynamic semantics" ends: "None of the three
addresses footing." Austin and Goffman "are cited for it and unread here"
([CLAIM-061](CLAIM-061.md); [LIT-785](../literature.d/LIT-785.md), [LIT-780](../literature.d/LIT-780.md)).

The record concedes neighbouring points and not this one. [CLAIM-014](CLAIM-014.md)'s
defeat condition allows that performative updates may not be "elicited as
observables with stable response distributions", but only for
commitments, obligations and permissions. [QUESTION-005](../questions.d/QUESTION-005.md) notes that A49's
admissibility condition "takes the observable distinctions as given",
which is the analyst's category list; it asks how to fix a correspondence
of contexts, not whether one context's footing can be judged reliably.

## What it does not say

It does not say footing or stance cannot be measured, nor that lay readers
cannot tell teasing from reproach. It says the record holds no evidence
that they can do so reliably in the categories the designs use, and one
piece of evidence that the analyst could not. It does not object to
[CLAIM-061](CLAIM-061.md) or [CLAIM-084](CLAIM-084.md) as stated, only to the evidence they can presently
draw on: until a measure is validated, a defeat test run on these
categories tests the categories as much as the thesis.
