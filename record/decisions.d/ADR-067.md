# ADR-067: line takes several values in every scheme, and CASE and ARG require it

## Context

The `lines` vocabulary names the record's lines of argument. CLAIM declared
its `line:` field `many: true`; QUESTION, CASE, TERM and ARG declared it
single-valued. Twice the docs-check lint failed on an entry that named two
lines, and twice the fix was to drop one: [QUESTION-365](../questions.d/QUESTION-365.md) lost
`distributed-agency` and [ARG-050](../arguments.d/ARG-050.md) lost `formal-apparatus`. Each bore on
both lines, so the cut made the line pages incomplete. On 2026-11-01 the
owner said the cut was a mistake: "any use of the line field on the
nucleation record should permit multi assignment".

## Decision

Every scheme in `luria.yaml` that declares a `line:` field declares it
`many: true`. Today that is QUESTION, CLAIM, CASE, TERM and ARG. A scheme
that gains a `line:` field later takes it many-valued too.

QUESTION, CLAIM, CASE and ARG require the field. TERM does not: a term is
vocabulary, which a line may borrow without the term being on it.

The two entries cut to pass lint get their second line back.

### Where the missing lines came from

Every ARG had a line already. Nine CASEs did not. Their lines were inferred
from the graph in `luria export`'s SQLite database. The query took the
lines of every lined entry joined to the CASE by a typed edge, in either
direction:

```sql
WITH unlined AS (
  SELECT d.code FROM documents d
  WHERE d.scheme = 'CASE'
    AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM fields f
                    WHERE f.code = d.code AND f.field = 'line')),
nbr AS (
  SELECT u.code, e.target AS other FROM unlined u JOIN edges e ON e.source = u.code
  UNION ALL
  SELECT u.code, e.source FROM unlined u JOIN edges e ON e.target = u.code)
SELECT n.code, f.value AS line, count(*) AS weight
FROM nbr n JOIN fields f ON f.code = n.other AND f.field = 'line'
GROUP BY n.code, f.value ORDER BY n.code, weight DESC;
```

- **One hop was enough for six:** CASE-006, CASE-025, CASE-034, CASE-151, CASE-181 and CASE-182. CASE-151 touches CLAIM-432, which is on two lines, and takes both.
- **Two Superseded CASEs, CASE-007 and CASE-028,** are joined only to their successors. They take the successor's line.
- **CASE-014 (Deferred) has no typed edge.** Its body cites QUESTION-019, which is on organizational-affect, and QUESTION-015, which superseded it and is on will-organization, along with three will-organization cases. It takes both lines, will-organization first, because the case was redirected from phenomenology to perspective on 2026-10-22.

The query gives candidates, not verdicts. Each assignment was read against
the CASE's title before it was written.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep one line outside CLAIM and name the others in prose.** This is
  what the two cuts did. The line pages are built from the field, so the
  entry vanished from a line it bears on.
- **Primary line plus a separate `also_lines:` field.** It would need a new
  field and would give the first value a weight nobody has asked for. Tags
  already put the primary first, and a list can do the same.
- **Leave CASE and ARG optional.** A CASE with no line appears on no line
  page. Nine of 190 were in that state, and nothing flagged them.
- **Require it for TERM too.** That would force a line onto vocabulary
  that serves several lines or none.

## Consequences

An entry can now sit on every line it bears on, and a new CASE or ARG must
say which. Lint no longer catches a second line added by mistake. That costs little, because a line is a
judgement about the entry, and a wrong one is corrected like any other
field.
