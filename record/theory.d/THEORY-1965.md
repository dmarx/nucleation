# THEORY-1965: Property allocates authority, so needs justifying

Waldron & Penner, "Property and Ownership" (SEP, 2026 revision),
[LIT-2744](../literature.d/LIT-2744.md).

## The account

"various contested resources are assigned to the decisional authority of
particular individuals"; the owner "may act on her own initiative without
giving anyone else an explanation"; so "private property is continually in
need of public justification" (§2). Default title is "one (Hohfeldian)
liberty which is protected with one (Hohfeldian) claim right, and two
(Hohfeldian) powers" (§2).

## What it does not show

- Who may change a property regime.
- Which justification of private property succeeds: §5 was only searched.
