# SYNTHESIS-tmphd6ep: Responsibility for another's conditions, and moral exploitation: who harmed whom is kept apart from who dominated whom, arranging another's wrong is proposed as a wrong of its own and its true accusation as an instrument of further control, an agent can be governed through its own sound values, and an arrangement can be carried by its members' commitments while blame lands on them

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line, filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day. Every component is new to any account. The claims and cases are A's and Proposed; the questions are Open, two raised from A's turns and one the owner's. Where a component cites another, the account says which entry does.*

## The account

**Harm done and power held.** CLAIM-tmpkivlz (A's, A3 §7 and "Where I
land", restated at A7 §9; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): who harmed
whom, act by act, and who had power over whom, in the ongoing
relationship, are separate questions, so neither settles the other. It
grounds on CASE-tmpzt3zf and uses TERM-124 and TERM-tmp8pnnr; its body
says the separation holds because domination in TERM-124's sense is a
capacity, "used or not", while a tally counts exercises. CASE-tmpzt3zf
(A's, A3 §1 and §5; fictional thought experiment, Proposed): Carol strikes
Dan more often, while Dan controls their money, monitors her messages,
isolates her and threatens her if she leaves.
The case's summary says it shows CLAIM-tmpkivlz's separation; it names
no real couple.

**Arranging another's wrong.** CLAIM-tmpa2l21 (A's, A9, at the owner's
U5; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency): wrongfully
structuring another's circumstances so that they are pressured, induced
or conditioned to commit a wrong is a wrong of its own, against that
person as well as those harmed, whether or not they comply; it stands
beside their culpability without taking it over, and is limited by
control, knowledge and exploitation. It uses TERM-tmp8pnnr and bears on
QUESTION-449; its body says it bears on that question without answering
it for organizations, and that it shares the word "second-order" with
CLAIM-624 and not a content.

**The true accusation as an instrument.** CLAIM-tmpciy9q (A's, A7 §5,
A9 §6, A11 and A13 §8; the owner's U6 brought in reflexive control;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency): a party can
arrange the conditions of another's real wrong and use the true
accusation, before an audience whose judgement it anticipated, to
justify further control; the truth of the accusation neither vindicates
the arranger nor excuses the doer. It rests on CLAIM-445, whose
mechanism its summary says it has "with the target's own conduct as the
shifted evidence", and uses TERM-tmp8pnnr and TERM-124. Its body says it
uses the wrong CLAIM-tmpa2l21 names "as an instrument", and that
CLAIM-tmp8wki5 (Part B's) has "the same shape with incapacity in place of
a wrong". CLAIM-tmpa2l21's body says the same of CLAIM-tmpciy9q.

**Governed through one's own values.** CLAIM-tmp76x3e (A's, A11 §4, A13
and A15 §7, at the owner's U7 and U8; thesis, interpretive, Proposed;
also will-organization): an agent can be governed through its sound
values, undeceived and with unchanged criteria, by a party that changes
the situation they respond to; a commitment becomes exploitable when the
party controls its trigger while avoiding the cost of fulfilling it. It
rests on CLAIM-404 (Active, the owner's), to whose evidence and criteria
routes its body says it adds a route through the situation, and uses
TERM-tmp8pnnr. It bears on QUESTION-tmpbzq36 and QUESTION-071. Its body
calls CLAIM-tmpe5u7i "the institutional, unintended form".

**The moral subsidy.** CLAIM-tmpe5u7i (A's, A13 §4, §10 and A15 §3 to
§4; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also organizational-normativity): an
arrangement that does not fund what it requires can be carried by its
members' commitments to those it serves; the commitment that keeps them
from withdrawing turns its need of them into its hold on them, the
shortfall falls hardest on the most conscientious, and the controllers
can rely on it without intent. It rests on CLAIM-690, whose hold it says
the commitment reverses; its body marks that inference as the filer's
statement of what A's case shows. It bears on QUESTION-461 (its body: "a
candidate mechanism for the second branch") and QUESTION-tmpbzq36. Its
body calls CLAIM-tmp76x3e "the deliberate, interpersonal form", says
CLAIM-tmpb47ul "carries the same members on to blame", and points to
CLAIM-tmp0ogo4 for the loop and CASE-tmpm0rpn for the construction.

**Control, obligation and blame apart.** CLAIM-tmpb47ul (A's,
"asymmetric moral accountability", A17, with Elish and Tronto relayed;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency): control over
the conditions of action, the practical obligation to make it succeed
and blame when it fails can rest with different parties; where they
diverge, downstream conscientiousness keeps the arrangement running and
supplies the terms in which its failure is told as theirs, credit goes
to the arrangement, and those upstream set the standards. It rests on
CLAIM-tmpe5u7i ("The worker's conscience does double duty"), uses
TERM-tmp8pnnr, and bears on QUESTION-tmpvoufs and QUESTION-468. Its body
names CLAIM-tmp238zy as one way blame lands downstream, and CLAIM-687 and
CLAIM-624 as what is unmet when it does.

**Scrutiny as evidence of ownership.** CLAIM-tmp238zy (A's, A23 §7,
developing the owner's U11, CLAIM-tmp11xvc; thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; first line distributed-agency): friction that makes people
think differs from friction that makes the institution answerable, and
the added scrutiny can serve as evidence that individuals owned
decisions whose conditions they could not change. It rests on CLAIM-349
(Active, the owner's) for the split of access from authority and on
CLAIM-tmpb47ul for "the perverse case", which A calls "a possible
*moral crumple zone* mechanism"; it grounds its cognitive half on
LIT-2283 (Active).

**The hidden-shortfall loop.** CLAIM-tmp0ogo4 (A's, A33 §5 after the
owner's U16; role supposed, interpretive, Proposed; also readiness): if
members absorb what an arrangement leaves unmet, resources are set by
measured performance and complaints count only past a threshold, the
compensation hides the shortfall from the rule that sets resources,
spare capacity is removed and the burden returns, a loop that can lock
in without misconduct; where revealing capacity invites its removal,
members' best responses feed it. It rests on CLAIM-577 (Active,
demonstrated), CLAIM-549 (Active, demonstrated) and CLAIM-tmpe5u7i, and
bears on QUESTION-386. Its body says each link but one is held, the one
not held being "that compensation hides the shortfall from the rule that
sets resources"; that the strategic layer is TERM-110's ratchet; that
CLAIM-445 and CLAIM-619 are loops "of the same family" and CLAIM-tmpciy9q
and CLAIM-tmp8wki5 "its interpersonal and restrictive forms"; and it
corrects A's paraphrase of CLAIM-577's rule.

**Two constructions.** CASE-tmpm0rpn (A's, A53 §7, at the owner's U24;
stipulated thought experiment, Proposed): three hospitals with the same
output, budget and staff, differing in how extra work is drawn out,
negotiated, competitive or moralized. It illustrates CLAIM-tmpe5u7i
(arm C) and CLAIM-tmp76x3e, and bears on QUESTION-463 and QUESTION-468;
its body says arm B shows the same extraction with no appeal to
conscience, so the subsidy is "one of at least two routes", and calls
arm B's unilateral authority over expectations CLAIM-tmpdic1g's
residual control in a workplace. CASE-tmpohiyo (A's, A33 §3, §6 and
§10 B; stipulated proposed study, not run, Proposed; first line
will-organization): with conditions held fixed, measure acceptance,
seen discrepancy, motivation, protected channel and efficacy, and
compare protected against costly escalation, to tell satisfaction from
tolerance. It illustrates CLAIM-553, CLAIM-549 and CLAIM-tmp0ogo4, the
last "observed at its quiet link".

**Three questions.** QUESTION-tmpvoufs (raised from A17's close; Open)
asks what lets agents hold principals accountable for the conditions
they must act under. It refines QUESTION-461; its body says CLAIM-687,
CLAIM-624 and CLAIM-tmpb47ul supply the downward half and none the
instruments, and that CLAIM-tmpe5u7i gives one reason dependence may
not become authority. QUESTION-tmpbzq36 (raised from A30 §4; Open) asks
whether a party's ability to activate another's genuine obligations is
control over the terms of their dependence. Its body asks it of
CLAIM-690 and TERM-124, and says that, if the answer is yes,
CLAIM-tmp76x3e says how and CLAIM-tmpe5u7i that it can happen without
intent; if no, "those are exploitation without domination".
QUESTION-tmp6kgwm (the owner's, U8, filed at the owner's request; Open;
the wording and its formalization are the assistant's) asks for a
resource theory of moral commitments in the formal sense of THEORY-1397.
Its body says the economic reading A15 gave is filed in CLAIM-tmp76x3e,
CLAIM-tmpe5u7i and CLAIM-tmpb47ul, and that a formal answer would make
QUESTION-tmpbzq36 exact.

## Arm A

Stage 1 ranked CLAIM-tmp0ogo4 as a possible account: three premises
across lines. The sentence it adds to CLAIM-577, CLAIM-549 and
CLAIM-tmpe5u7i: an unfunded compensation that keeps measured performance
acceptable is invisible to a budget rule set on that performance, which
then removes capacity in use. No premise states it. CLAIM-577's rule is
peak spending, which never rises and cuts nothing; CLAIM-549 is about
thresholds; CLAIM-tmpe5u7i writes no budget rule. The claim's body says
the same ("The link that is not held"). Route, a separating case: all
three premises hold in a hospital whose unpaid extra hours are logged
and read by the budget rule as demand. The subsidy is unfunded and the
quiet is there, but the shortfall is not hidden, so no capacity is cut.
The case is realistic. It meets the claim's own defeater ("the budget
rule responds to the compensation") only if "measured performance"
covers logged inputs, so that phrase is a free parameter. Closed as
output measured against obligations, it puts the case outside the
claim's properties: the claim is separate from its premises and not
shown false. Verdict: it adds something and stays a claim, integrated
here. The closing reading of "measured performance" belongs in the
claim's body under "Admission"; this account does not edit it.

## Arm B

- **Manufactured culpability and asymmetric accountability as one
  wrong in two forms** (CLAIM-tmpciy9q, CLAIM-tmpb47ul). Route tried: a
  separating case. A frontline worker blamed for a failure that was the
  understaffing's alone satisfies CLAIM-tmpb47ul, which allows blame
  without the worker's own wrong, and not CLAIM-tmpciy9q, which needs a
  real wrong truly charged. As an identity the link is false of cases
  the components allow; as "the two can coincide" it adds nothing.
  Fails; listed above as not stated.
- **Activating an obligation is control over terms** (CLAIM-tmp76x3e,
  CLAIM-tmpe5u7i, TERM-124, answering QUESTION-tmpbzq36). Route tried: a
  derivation. It goes through only if TERM-124's "terms on which a party
  can act, exit, contest or renegotiate" covers a party's reasons as
  well as its options. Read so, the link is definitional; read as
  options only, it fails. The word "terms" carries the whole verdict,
  and fixing it is what the question asks. Fails for a free parameter.
- **A family of self-justifying loops** (CLAIM-tmpciy9q, CLAIM-tmp0ogo4,
  with CLAIM-tmp8wki5 and CLAIM-445 outside the part). Already stated:
  CLAIM-tmp0ogo4's body names CLAIM-445, CLAIM-619, CLAIM-tmpciy9q and
  CLAIM-tmp8wki5 as loops of its family, and CLAIM-tmpciy9q's names
  CLAIM-tmp8wki5 as the same shape. Nothing new is filed.
