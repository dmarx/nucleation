# ADR-010: representation-learning and learning-theory join the topics

## Context

On 2026-09-26 the owner asked this record to file a list of works under four
of their own headings:

- *Knowledge Graphs*;
- *Relating K-complexity to minimum sufficient statistics*;
- *Riesz Representation Theorem*;
- *Representation learning as a spectral approximation*.

Most of them are machine-learning theory:

- spectral accounts of contrastive and non-contrastive self-supervised
  learning;
- the information bottleneck and the conditional entropy bottleneck;
- sheaf-theoretic knowledge-graph embedding;
- compression, rate–distortion and description-length bounds on
  generalization;
- the Kolmogorov structure function as model selection.

The vocabulary has no word for either strand:

- `information-theory` is blurbed as information theory "read for [itself]"
  and "outside machine learning";
- `probabilistic-modeling` is Bayesian inference and statistical models;
- `mathematics` is mathematics read for itself.

Each is the nearest wrong word for a paper on why a contrastive loss recovers
a spectral embedding. The record's rule is to add the word rather than reach
for the nearest wrong one ([ADR-006](ADR-006.md), [ADR-007](ADR-007.md),
[ADR-009](ADR-009.md)).

Filing them here at all follows the owner's decision of 2026-09-26 that new
work stays in nucleation until a transfer is judged appropriate. The works
chiefly about ML practice carry `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](ADR-005.md)).

## Decision

Add two words to `topics`, each with a label and a blurb:

- **`representation-learning`**: how learned systems come to represent their
  data. That takes in self-supervised and contrastive objectives, spectral
  embeddings, information bottlenecks, knowledge-graph embeddings, and whether
  representations converge.
- **`learning-theory`**: why learning generalizes. That takes in compression
  and description length, Kolmogorov complexity and sufficient statistics,
  rate–distortion and PAC-style bounds, and model selection.

Neither joins the `philosophy` group.

## Alternatives

- **One word, `machine-learning`.** It would bind a spectral account of SimCLR
  to an MDL generalization bound as if they were about the same thing. The
  owner's own headings already separate the two strands.
- **Stretch `information-theory` to cover them.** Its blurb excludes machine
  learning deliberately. Several of these works are information-theoretic *and*
  about learning, and they carry both words.
- **File them in the anthology.** That record is for practice and the claims
  behind it. These are mostly theory, and the owner asked for them here.
  `anthology-candidate` marks the ones a transfer should consider.
