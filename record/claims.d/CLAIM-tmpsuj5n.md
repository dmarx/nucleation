# CLAIM-tmpsuj5n: The prediction that a corporation changed only by sanction reverts when the sanction lifts, while one whose concerns were redesigned does not, separates remorse from prudence only in the redesigned arm and only with reputation and benefit held fixed: reversion of a sanction-only corporation is predicted by the reading on which every installed concern is prudence as well, and so is persistence of a redesigned one whose procedure still pays

## The derivation

Take the two readings as the record states them.

- **R ([CLAIM-432](CLAIM-432.md)).** A harm to others reaches a corporation's decisions
  either as its own concern, through a procedure or role duty (functional
  remorse), or as a threat to its other concerns, through a regulator or
  market (functional fear). Part 5: "a corporation changed only by sanction
  reverts when the sanction lifts, and one whose concerns were redesigned
  does not."
- **P ([CLAIM-431](CLAIM-431.md)).** Every such procedure "was put there for its own ends,
  reputation, liability or recruitment", so harm reaches the decisions, one
  step back, as a threat; there is no remorse apart from fear.

Four situations, by arm and by whether keeping the harm-weighing still pays
the corporation once the sanction is gone (through reputation, avoided
liability, recruitment, or the cost of dismantling a procedure):

| arm | still pays | R predicts | P predicts | separates? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sanction only | either | reverts (part 5) | reverts: the threat that was the only route is gone | no |
| redesigned | yes | persists (part 5) | persists: the procedure still serves its ends | no |
| redesigned | no | persists (part 5) | reverts: nothing it was installed for remains | **yes** |

The first row is the case P adds no premise to: with no procedure, both
readings route the harm through the sanction alone. The second is P's own
claim: an installed concern persists while it serves the end it was
installed for. Only the third row gives different predictions. So:

1. Evidence that sanction-only corporations revert is predicted by both
   readings and is no evidence between them.
2. Evidence that redesigned corporations persist is evidence between them
   only where keeping the procedure no longer pays, which is what the
   lead's "with reputation and benefit held fixed" requires.

## What it does not say

- That the third row cannot be observed. It needs post-lift conduct that
  was private at the time and costly to the firm, which internal records
  released later can show.
- That [CLAIM-431](CLAIM-431.md) or [CLAIM-432](CLAIM-432.md) is right. It says only which observation
  would tell.
- That P's other route to an answer (origin is not current function, as in
  a person) depends on this test. It does not.

## Bearing

The reversion found in [CASE-tmphvbov](../cases.d/CASE-tmphvbov.md), in the quantitative arm
([CLAIM-tmpm9a3z](CLAIM-tmpm9a3z.md)) and in the Purdue case, sits in the first row. The one
redesigned case coded, Siemens, sits in the second: its compliance leaders
explained the system by prosecution and exclusion, and its costly
weighing happened while the monitor was there.
