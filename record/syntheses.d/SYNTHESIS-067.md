# SYNTHESIS-067: Along a chain of reconstructions, per-step discrepancies weighted by later sensitivity bound the departure from a reference transport but give no testable prediction and do not say whether the act survives, which a reorganized constraint or a phase change can decide; approximate transports compose additively only under a triangle inequality and nonexpansive kernels; a chain can forget its start while staying far from the original; and attraction toward conventional stances, filtered by the transmitters' biases, is proposed, with chain evidence for evaluative stance only

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line ("Contextual pragmatic
information transport"): the part on repeated reconstruction chains, drift
bounds and attraction toward conventional regions. The line's account is
built over it and the accounts of the line's other parts. The mechanical
map put 22 entries in this part; five of them ([CLAIM-040](../claims.d/CLAIM-040.md), [CLAIM-093](../claims.d/CLAIM-093.md),
[CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md), [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md) and [QUESTION-006](../questions.d/QUESTION-006.md)) are a second inquiry, conservation
of observables under reconstruction kernels, that barely relates to the
other seventeen, and have their own account ([SYNTHESIS-060](SYNTHESIS-060.md)).
The counts are under "Whether the part holds together". [CLAIM-260](../claims.d/CLAIM-260.md) also
carries formal-apparatus; [CLAIM-267](../claims.d/CLAIM-267.md) and [CLAIM-270](../claims.d/CLAIM-270.md), in formal-apparatus
only, hold the general results [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) and [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) apply.*

## The account

- **The bound.** [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) (A50 §2–3, kept in the manuscript §8; thesis,
  interpretive, Active): with local discrepancy ε_i and later sensitivity
  L_i, e_n ≤ (∏ L_j) e_0 + Σ ε_i ∏_(j>i) L_j, so "the impact of local
  translation errors depends on the dynamics of subsequent
  interpretation", with three regimes, contractive, neutral and
  expansive. The manuscript adds: "Crucially, the bound quantifies
  departure relative to a reference transport, not loss of every
  communicative virtue." The general recursion is [CLAIM-267](../claims.d/CLAIM-267.md)'s (note of
  2026-10-13); what [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) adds "is that a serial reconstruction chain
  is a composition of transports measured against a reference transport
  ([CLAIM-106](../claims.d/CLAIM-106.md))" (note of 2026-10-29).
- **What the bound does not say, conceded.** [CLAIM-031](../claims.d/CLAIM-031.md) (A75's
  self-critique; granted, interpretive, Active) objects to it: a chain "is
  not simply the accumulation of metric error", and "changing one crucial
  constraint—such as the speaker's alignment with the audience—may change
  the identity of the communicative act even if most other observables
  remain stable." [CLAIM-208](../claims.d/CLAIM-208.md) (found in the record's audit of the line of
  2026-10-10, empirical lens, "no turn of the exchange raises it"; counter,
  demonstrated, Active) objects to [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md), [CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md) and [CLAIM-078](../claims.d/CLAIM-078.md): with
  true constants the recursion "holds for every chain: it is a theorem,
  and no data can contradict it", a sensitivity "is a property of the
  reteller's kernel", which a serial-reproduction chain, giving "one
  retelling of one input at each node", cannot estimate, and no design
  fixes the metric. [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md)'s version 3 (2026-10-29) concedes both in its
  title and defeat condition, which now names [CLAIM-208](../claims.d/CLAIM-208.md)'s design.
- **A phase change.** [CLAIM-114](../claims.d/CLAIM-114.md) (A73, recovered; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md): drift "can be marked by a pragmatic
  phase change, the first generation at which a different communicative
  configuration dominates, which per-step error in surface content does
  not show". Its body: "[CLAIM-031](../claims.d/CLAIM-031.md) is the same point made about
  constraints." Its note of 2026-10-29: no chain the record holds has
  per-generation judgements of configuration.
- **Regimes in real chains.** [CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md) (A110 §19, from A50 §3 and A52
  §5.3; thesis, interpretive, Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md), [CLAIM-267](../claims.d/CLAIM-267.md)
  and [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md): real chains "fall into different regimes under
  conditions assigned in advance", and "amplification needs a metric
  other than total variation, an enlarged state or state-dependent
  dynamics". Its body: "The contracting regime is [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md); the
  amplifying one needs the enlarged state of [CLAIM-049](../claims.d/CLAIM-049.md)." Version 2
  restated its condition after [CLAIM-208](../claims.d/CLAIM-208.md), fixing the metric in advance;
  its note of 2026-10-29 says that "no metric is fixed and no condition is
  assigned, so the test it names has nothing yet to test".
- **Composition of approximate transports.** [CLAIM-260](../claims.d/CLAIM-260.md) (found in the
  audit of 2026-10-10, formal lens; granted, demonstrated, Active; also
  formal-apparatus) objects to [CLAIM-098](../claims.d/CLAIM-098.md)'s "category or semigroup": the
  composite of an ε₁-transport and an ε₂-transport is within Lε₁ + ε₂,
  additive when the distortion obeys a triangle inequality and the later
  kernel is nonexpansive, with counterexamples under squared Hellinger
  distance and an expansive kernel. "In total variation both conditions
  hold", and "So the gap is for the other distortions, and [CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md) says
  amplification needs one". It answers [QUESTION-293](../questions.d/QUESTION-293.md) (split off by the
  status review of 2026-10-27; Answered), which refines [QUESTION-017](../questions.d/QUESTION-017.md)
  (from the tension in A50 between a metric bound and directed
  distortion; Open). [QUESTION-293](../questions.d/QUESTION-293.md) ends: "What weaker bound holds where the
  additive one fails, and what holds for directed distortion, remain
  [QUESTION-017](../questions.d/QUESTION-017.md)'s." [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) does not say the bound holds "for directed,
  non-metric distortion ([QUESTION-017](../questions.d/QUESTION-017.md))", and [CLAIM-208](../claims.d/CLAIM-208.md) sets its own point
  apart: [QUESTION-017](../questions.d/QUESTION-017.md) "is a question about the theorem. This objection is
  about measuring its constants."
- **Forgetting the start is not preserving the original.** [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) (C1
  Appendix D and A59; thesis, interpretive, Active), resting on [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md)
  and [CLAIM-270](../claims.d/CLAIM-270.md): retention and drift are different profiles, "so
  convergence is not preservation and transmission is not degradation".
  [CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md) (E1, run at U23 on hand-chosen matrices; stipulated, executed
  study, Active) is its instance: "The contractive row is the two
  forgettings of [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md)." [CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md) adds that no run "exercised the
  amplifying regime of [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md)".
- **Attraction toward conventions.** [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) (A50 §3 and §6, A52 §5.5
  and §7.4–7.5, recovered; thesis, interpretive, Proposed), resting on
  [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) and stated in [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md): "Repeated reconstruction is attraction
  toward conventional regions of communicative possibility: pragmatic
  stances, not only lexicon and grammar, can be stabilized by
  transmission." [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md) (A110 §20; Active) reads the attractor as "a
  shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object",
  and says "reaching it is evidence of forgetting the start ([CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md))".
  [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)'s body: "E1's contractive chain ([CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md)) is the stipulated
  instance of convergence to a convention ([CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md))". [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) does not
  say that drift is random: "A50 went on to attraction ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md))", and
  [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) leaves which profile matters to "whether the question is about
  the original or about the convention ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md))".
- **Filtering by the transmitters' biases.** [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) (the manuscript's
  §8; thesis, empirical, Proposed): "Serial transmission is reconstructive
  and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases, so a stable endpoint
  can be far from the ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable."
  Its evidence is Kalish, Griffiths and Lewandowsky's function-learning
  chains, with "The prior was taken from earlier studies rather than
  measured on these learners." [ARG-035](../arguments.d/ARG-035.md) (filed by the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration
  from [CLAIM-066](../claims.d/CLAIM-066.md); source, Proposed) concludes it from Griffiths and Kalish
  and Kirby, Cornish and Smith, who "show different things": "the stable
  endpoint far from the ancestor survives for sampling learners, and
  whether people learn that way is [QUESTION-137](../questions.d/QUESTION-137.md)". [QUESTION-137](../questions.d/QUESTION-137.md) (split from
  [THEORY-155](../theory.d/THEORY-155.md)'s promotion condition at the owner's direction; Open) asks
  whether human chains converge to an independently measured prior.
  [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md)'s and [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)'s notes of 2026-10-29 both say they wait on
  it.
- **The stance extension and its evidence.** [ARG-027](../arguments.d/ARG-027.md) (filed by the
  [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration from [CLAIM-234](../claims.d/CLAIM-234.md); evidence, Rejected) concluded [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)
  from the iterated-learning readings: "None measures an interpersonal
  stance, a footing or a speech-act function along a chain." It is "minor
  for the argument: [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) feeds [CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md)'s regimes". [CASE-068](../cases.d/CASE-068.md)
  (two reported diffusion-chain studies found by a search the owner asked
  for; documented, Active) illustrates [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) and [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md): the
  evaluative signal "survived and drifted negative", and the transmitters'
  measured risk perception "predicts the direction of the change". Its
  summary: "Documented chain evidence for evaluative stance; none for
  footing." For [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) it says "A clean prior. [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) is met only
  partly", and for [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) that "Every Jagiello and Hills chain started
  balanced", so convergence from different starts is untested. [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)'s
  note of 2026-10-29 reads [CASE-068](../cases.d/CASE-068.md) so: "an evaluative signal kept and
  amplified, not chains from different stances converging."

What stays open is whether real chains fall into regimes under conditions
fixed in advance ([CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md)), whether a configuration flips at a marked
generation ([CLAIM-114](../claims.d/CLAIM-114.md)), what bound holds for directed distortion
([QUESTION-017](../questions.d/QUESTION-017.md)), whether chains reach a prior measured on their own
transmitters ([QUESTION-137](../questions.d/QUESTION-137.md)), and whether chains started from different
stances converge ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)'s defeat condition).
