# CASE-145: Perspective without collective agency: populations given the same structured norms, categories or interpretive training, some interacting and some acquiring similar dispositions independently, with and without any mechanism for collective decision or coordinated intervention

## The design

A4: "Set up multiple agents exposed to the same structured norms,
categories, or interpretive training. Some populations interact; others
acquire similar dispositions independently. Vary whether they possess any
mechanism for collective decision-making or coordinated intervention."
Then estimate independently their shared patterns of interpretation,
reciprocal influence, adaptive coordination, and response to joint
perturbation.

## What is measured

Whether the interacting populations show a relational organization the
independently trained ones lack, in their response to a perturbation of
some members, and whether that organization is present with no mechanism
for collective decision.

## What the outcomes would show, and what they would not

- **[CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)'s separation.** A perspectival organization present where
  no collective agent is would show perspectival and agential boundaries
  coming apart in a constructed system.
- **The risk the design must meet first.** A4: "a shared perspective will
  simply mean correlated responses or socially learned norms. The study
  only helps the theory if it specifies, ahead of time, what additional
  relational organization distinguishes a perspective from those simpler
  phenomena." The independently trained populations are the control for
  correlation; the defeater of [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md) is the control for the rest.
- **Against the category.** A4: "If it cannot make that distinction, the
  result should count against the theoretical usefulness of the
  category." The record should keep that outcome possible.
- **Related evidence.** Honeybee swarms reach collective decisions with
  no central controller by cross-inhibition of recruitment (Seeley et al.,
  [LIT-1406](../literature.d/LIT-1406.md)); A5: "collective decision-making is not automatically
  collective interpretation."

## Note of 2026-10-29: the swarm's stop signals, read

Seeley et al., [LIT-1406](../literature.d/LIT-1406.md), cited above under "Related evidence", is now
read ([NOTE-1411](../notes.d/NOTE-1411.md)). It supports the citation: nest-site scouts end
rival scouts' dances with stop signals, and no arbiter settles the
choice. Two precisions.

- **Targeted only while choosing.** In the decision phase the signals go
  mostly to dancers for the other site (213 contra against 24 ipsi); once
  the swarm has chosen, they go to all dancers in proportion. The
  targeting result rests on two swarms in one island setup.
- **Deadlock-breaking is a model result.** That cross inhibition is what
  lets a swarm facing two equal sites pick one comes from the ODE model
  in the paper's supplement, which was not read, and holds only above a
  critical rate of signalling. Whether the swarms observed were above it
  is not shown.

A5's caution stands: the paper shows a collective decision, not a
collective interpretation. The design is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-11-12: a factorial version for agent teams, and the studies that already ran its first part, from "luria experiment design"

## Note of 2026-11-12: DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, read

Read on 2026-11-12 among the works of the exchange "luria experiment design"; each reading's NOTE and THEORY hold the detail.

- **DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus** (THEORY-tmpv6qhj): **supports a measurement constraint on the design and qualifies "shared interpretation" as an outcome.** The case's own risk is that a shared perspective "will simply mean correlated responses". DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus show that agreement-style and rating-scale measures of sharedness (closest to "correlated responses") do not predict team process, while structural similarity does. So a test of CASE-145 should measure the structure of agents' interpretations (relations among categories), not only agreement on responses, if it wants a sharedness measure that tracks interaction. It does not bear on the collective-agency half of the case.

The case is unchanged.
