# LIT-2993: Hyperbolic Learning with Supervision from any Granularity

Ghadimi Atigh, van Spengler, Long, Ayoughi, Kasarla & Mettes (2026).
AISTATS 2026, PMLR 300:2674–2682.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/atigh26a.html.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A33 for hyperbolic learning from labels at mixed granularity. It bears on the exchange's use of hyperbolic geometry for coarse and fine labels.

Read on 2026-11-22 in [NOTE-2282](../notes.d/NOTE-2282.md); the reading is [THEORY-2192](../theory.d/THEORY-2192.md). The
first summary is right. [CLAIM-839](../claims.d/CLAIM-839.md)'s "nearby" is accurate as far as it
goes: the nesting is semantic, a label's cone containing its
descendants', applied to each sample at one time, not a restriction over
training time, and §3.2's "Coarse-to-fine" names a label projection, not
an order of acquisition. It weakly corroborates the claim's semantic half
only ([ARG-194](../arguments.d/ARG-194.md)).
