# CASE-146: Two friendships across a group line: in one, the friend becomes an exception and the person's view of the friend's group is unchanged; in the other, what happens with the friend changes how the person takes other members of the group, though the friendships are alike in warmth and in what each learns

## The case

Two people hold the same unfavourable view of a social group G. Each
becomes friends with a member of G through sustained cooperation. The
friendships are alike: equally warm, equally long, and each person learns
the same favourable things about their friend.

- **The first** comes to treat the friend as unlike the rest of G. Their
  response to the friend changes; their response to a stranger from G does
  not.
- **The second** comes to take other members of G differently: ambiguous
  conduct by a stranger from G is read less harshly, and what members of
  G say carries more weight.

## What it shows

- **The state and the coupling.** [CLAIM-429](../claims.d/CLAIM-429.md)'s distinction in a
  person. The first friendship changes the state of the person's
  interpretive organization (one favourable exception). The second changes
  how far outcomes with members of G may revise the interpretation that
  governs G. A5: "It is the difference between introducing a positive
  exception and changing how exceptions are allowed to revise the rule."
- **Why contact sometimes does not generalize.** A4 cited a meta-analysis
  finding that generalization from individuals to groups depends partly
  on typicality and number. The case holds those fixed, so any difference
  lies in the person's organization, not in the friend.
- **A relational frame reading.** A5: relational frame theory ([THEORY-044](../theory.d/THEORY-044.md))
  gives a mechanism for the second outcome, the transfer of a stimulus's
  changed function through the relation of membership in G.

## What it does not show

- Which outcome is more common, or what makes the difference. It is
  stipulated.
- That the second outcome is better in every respect. A change in how far
  outcomes may revise a category can also be exploited ([CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md)).
