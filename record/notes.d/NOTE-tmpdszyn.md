# NOTE-tmpdszyn: Bayer — Jenkins!

## Contribution

The result of the Hudson community's vote to rename the project Jenkins, and the plan to move its infrastructure, dated by the document itself.

## Key results

- **The vote.** "More than half of the total votes were from ineligible voters, but the result would have been the same either way. The final result of all eligible votes is as follows: 214 votes to rename, and 14 for the status quo."
- **The move follows the vote.** "In the coming days, we will be renaming the existing Google Groups to jenkins-*@googlegroups.com, renaming the Twitter account from @hudsonci to @jenkinsci, and renaming our organization at Github from hudson to jenkinsci."
- **Who leads.** "the initial, interim governance board will consist of me, Kohsuke and, if he and Oracle are willing, Winston" (Kohsuke Kawaguchi is Hudson's creator).

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. It is a statement by an interested party: what it
says of its own reasons is its stated reason, not its motive.

## Concepts

The continuation of a project apart from its name and steward.

## Bearing on the record

The one verdict in the study dated before the move it decided: the community voted, then the lists, accounts and code organization moved. Its voters are the community that then moved, so it is not a verdict by non-movers.

## Limitations

One document, by one party, on one day. It records a stated position, and
says nothing of how many people shared it.
