# LIT-tmpoeqhr: Mereological implications of the ethics of pregnancy

Vilius Dranseika (2026). "Mereological implications of the ethics of
pregnancy" *Journal of Medical Ethics, commentary*.
DOI-10.1136/jme-2026-112352.

## Key takeaways

A single mereology can support competing ethical positions and one position
can be defended under several mereologies; choosing a mereology to fit a
commitment is legitimate but does not then count as evidence for it.
