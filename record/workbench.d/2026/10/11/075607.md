**What started it.** The owner gave a manuscript idea as a title only:
"luria: nucleating an argument". The assistant (Claude Code, working in
dmarx/luria and dmarx/nucleation) first registered the title in `works` and
asked for an outline. The owner objected: the assistant had "stumbled across
the rough idea ... independently multiple times in separate contexts", and
the two records should have been enough to start from.

**What the records already held.** Searching both records turned up the
idea in pieces. Each piece had been written separately, and none of them
named the others.

- [ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)'s lesson from the will exchange: identity, not structure, is what
  goes wrong. Its filing rule is that a thing gets a code the second time it
  turns up.
- The workbench reconstructions of the translation exchange. They keep
  recording re-derivation without notice, and changes of thesis that no turn
  argues. Twice the owner asked for "the crystallized argument", and each
  time the manuscript drafted from it dropped part of it.
- A125, catalogued in workbench 2026/10/10/040455 as a "flourish, not
  coded": "Nucleation itself is a case study".
- Luria's rediscovery tax ([LU-ADR-001](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-001.md)), its "same catch twice" agreement,
  and the mnemonist's cautionary half ([LU-ADR-011](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-011.md)).

**The reading.** It is the assistant's reading of the title, not the
owner's statement of it. Read through the title, the pieces form one
argument, in the order [QUESTION-tmpdj25c](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpdj25c.md) asks it:

1. Argument developed with collaborators who keep no memory is
   supersaturated. It fails at identity, not at structure ([CLAIM-tmp4qgbo](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4qgbo.md)).
2. Codes are nucleation sites, and recurrence is the threshold
   ([CLAIM-tmp2jolp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2jolp.md)). That this manuscript's own idea recurred across
   separate sessions before anyone named it is an instance.
3. Agent filing removes the disparity that design-rationale systems were
   abandoned over, and moves the risk to flooding ([CLAIM-tmp6b44r](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6b44r.md)).
4. The manuscript is a view over the argument, not its only copy
   ([CLAIM-tmp5o4dd](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5o4dd.md)). Views read back become instruments ([CLAIM-tmpors2p](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpors2p.md)).
5. The objection to answer is that the lattice belongs to the seed: the
   record shapes what it claims only to stabilize ([CLAIM-tmpas8q0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpas8q0.md)).

**Filed.** A line, `nucleating-argument`, and the `works` term were added,
with one question and six claims, all Proposed. The A125 flourish is now
coded as the frame of [CLAIM-tmpors2p](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpors2p.md), because its second occurrence is this
session.

**Open.** Whether this is the argument the owner meant. The owner has not
seen it yet. The pun in the title has three senses (the tool, this record,
the phase transition), and the reading uses all three. None of them has
been confirmed.
