# CASE-tmpxqgai: Three finite-state systems that dissociate slowness, member regeneration and transmission: a slow flip register no member reads (second eigenvalue 0.98), a register members read and rewrite that forgets fast (second eigenvalue 0.2, nonzero intervention effect), and a rule passed through an archive between two episodes that never run together

## The construction

A4's three properties: "**D — Dynamical separation:** A macrovariable
evolves much more slowly than constituent activity", "**E — Endogenous
maintenance:** Constituents respond to a shared variable and, through
their behavior, regenerate it", and "**H — Historical transmission:** A
later organization inherits an operative structure from an earlier one
through a causal process."

**Arm A, slow without uptake.** State (Z, U₁, U₂) ∈ {0, 1}³. Z_{t+1} = Z_t ⊕
B_t with B_t ~ Bernoulli(0.01); U₁ and U₂ are resampled independently at
each step. The Z chain has transition matrix [[0.99, 0.01], [0.01, 0.99]],
with eigenvalues 1 and 1 − 2(0.01) = 0.98. The resampling of (U₁, U₂) is a
rank-one matrix, with eigenvalues 1, 0, 0, 0. The joint chain is their
Kronecker product, with eigenvalues 1, 0.98 and six zeros, as A4 says.
Dependence on Z's initial state decays as 0.98^t (0.904 at t = 5, 0.133 at
t = 100); the members forget in one step. A4: "Strong timescale separation
exists, but constituent agents never read or regenerate Z. It is a
persistent statistic".

**Arm B, regenerated without slowness.** Two members read a shared
register Z_t and act: A_i = Z_t ⊕ N_i, N_i ~ Bernoulli(0.4). Member 1's
action writes the next register, Z_{t+1} = A₁. So P(Z_{t+1} = Z_t) = 0.6
and the transition matrix is [[0.6, 0.4], [0.4, 0.6]], with eigenvalues 1
and 0.2. Under do(Z_t = 0), P(Z_{t+1} = 1) = 0.4; under do(Z_t = 1), 0.6.
The members' reading of Z makes a difference to its next value, and the
register forgets as 0.2^t, on the members' own timescale. A4: "A shared
variable can be causally regenerated by members without exhibiting strong
temporal persistence."

**Arm C, transmitted without continuous operation.** Episode A runs with
rule θ_A, which is written to an archive. Episode B, which never runs at
the same time as A, reads it and executes b_B = θ_B = θ_A. Intervening on
θ_A changes B's behaviour. Neither episode needs ongoing feedback or a slow
internal state.

Every eigenvalue, the decay values in A4's chart and the interventional
probabilities were rechecked by hand and numerically for this filing. A3 said it checked them "using exact rational arithmetic", in
a bundle that never reached the repository.

## What it can show

- **Arm A: slowness is not sufficient for endogeneity.** That is already
  [CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)'s "timescale separation (τ_Z ≫ τ_X, not sufficient)" and
  [CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md)'s "slowness is a mark of a level and not its definition". The
  arm makes it a construction, and a trivial one: Z does not depend on the
  members at all.
- **Arm B: slowness is not necessary for endogeneity.** This is the
  converse, which the record did not have. It holds within A4's limit,
  kept verbatim: "This demonstrates only *weak* endogenous maintenance. If
  maintenance is defined to require robust long-term persistence, this
  counterexample no longer establishes the separation." A3 put the next
  test the same way: whether the separation "survives stronger definitions
  of maintenance".
- **Arm C: transmission without running.** It is [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md)'s dormant
  coalition, the capacity to reinstate an organization rather than its
  continuous running, and [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s "a lapse in its coherence need not
  end it". A3's refinement: "**Dynamical separation and endogenous
  maintenance are properties of processes. Historical continuity is a
  relation between processes at different times.**"

## What it cannot show

- **Identity.** A4: "causal transmission alone does not settle whether A
  and B are numerically the *same* collective. That requires an additional
  individuation criterion." [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md) says continuity of this kind can
  branch, so it is not identity.
- **A general theorem.** A4: the constructions "do **not** establish a
  model-independent theorem about collective identity." They separate the
  properties under the operational definitions chosen here, D as a second
  eigenvalue, E as a nonzero intervention effect of the members' reading.
- **That members of arm B are agents, or that Z is a level.** Member 2
  reads Z and its action changes nothing, and nothing in arm B integrates
  the members ([CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)'s "Not that the variable individuates a
  collective").
