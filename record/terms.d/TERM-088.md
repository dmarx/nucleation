# TERM-088: individuation, and which sense: descriptive (an inquiry can distinguish and track it), dynamical (its organization persists under specified dynamics and perturbation, with robust identity and boundaries), constitutive (its relational organization helps fix its identity and boundary), and organizational autonomy, the further achievement of contributing to the maintenance of its own constitutive constraints

## Definition

"Individuation" in this record means one of the following, and a claim should
say which (after A18 §6 and A23 §VI):

| sense | what would justify it |
|---|---|
| **descriptive** | an inquiry can distinguish and track it |
| **dynamical** | its organization persists under specified dynamics and perturbations, with robust identity and boundaries |
| **constitutive** | its relational organization helps fix its identity and boundary |
| **organizational autonomy** | in addition, its processes contribute to maintaining its constitutive constraints ([TERM-070](TERM-070.md)) |

These are claims about a candidate individual. The genetic, or operational,
sense of Simondon's readers, individuation as the operation by which a
metastable system resolves a disparity and yields a provisional individual,
is not one of them: it names the process that would produce a candidate, not
a claim about one (note of 2026-10-31). This term excludes it, and a claim
that means it says so.

The senses are not stages, and not degrees of existence. A pattern can be
dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside, like a convection
roll fed by an imposed gradient. That is individuation without autonomy. A
question can individuate a pattern descriptively without its persisting at all.

## The distinctions it draws

- **Individuation from autonomy.** The owner's second review: "Individuation
  asks which persistent organizations have sufficiently robust identity and
  boundaries; organizational autonomy asks which additionally contribute to
  maintaining their own constitutive constraints." [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md), version 2,
  is stated in this split.
- **Dynamical individuation from persistence.** Persistence ([TERM-071](TERM-071.md)) is a
  family of continuities. Dynamical individuation adds that the persisting
  organization has a boundary that survives specified perturbations.
- **From the criteria.** [QUESTION-115](../questions.d/QUESTION-115.md) asks which criteria pick out which
  boundaries (retention, dynamical independence, Ψ). This term names what
  is claimed, not how it is measured. [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md)'s block criterion is one
  measure of the dynamical sense.

## Why it is proposed

The word recurs in nearly every claim filed from the exchange. The owner's review
had to correct a title that ran two of its senses together. What would settle
it: adoption if claims cite the sense they mean; supersession if [TERM-074](TERM-074.md)'s
senses of "organization" are found to carry it.

## Note of 2026-10-29: what holds it back

## Note of 2026-10-31: the sense Simondon's readers use, which this term does not list

The readings filed today on Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy 2024 and its
Simondonian sources use "individuation" throughout. Their sense is none of
the four here.

- **Genetic, or operational.** In those readings, individuation is the
  operation by which a metastable system resolves a disparity between
  differently ordered domains, and the individual is its partial,
  provisional product ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p. 1355; [LIT-1911](../literature.d/LIT-1911.md), pp. 10–12,
  [THEORY-1276](../theory.d/THEORY-1276.md)). The four senses here are claims about a candidate
  individual: that it can be tracked, that it persists, that its relations
  fix it, that it maintains its own constraints. The readings' sense names
  the process that would produce such a candidate. The record asks about
  that process in [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md), how forms come into being and pass away,
  without a word for it. A claim citing these readings should say that it
  means the genetic sense and not one of the four.
- **The product is never complete.** An individual "does not exhaust the
  tensions that allowed its constitution" (Simondon 1989, quoted by Bencherki 2017, [LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md),
  p. 782), and "an individuation process does not
  'use up' or 'consume' the preindividual it integrates" ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p.
  1355). So a Simondonian individual carries unresolved potential by
  definition. That sits uneasily with the dynamical sense's "robust
  identity and boundaries". Whether a unit can be robustly individuated
  and still metastable is the kind of question this term's senses let the
  record ask: dynamical individuation is relative to specified
  perturbations, and a system can pass for those and stay metastable for
  others.
- **"Not stages" is said of something else there.** Bencherki and
  Iliadis's proposition 2, that persons and organizations "are not at
  different stages of individuation, but rather different informational
  levels" ([LIT-1922](../literature.d/LIT-1922.md), ms. p. 21; [THEORY-1277](../theory.d/THEORY-1277.md)), is about levels of
  entities. This term's "the senses are not stages" is about kinds of
  claim. The two agree in form only. [CLAIM-526](../claims.d/CLAIM-526.md) answers the levels
  proposition.

No status, title or warrant changes.

## Note of 2026-11-01: "constitutive" in the mechanists' sense is another relation

The mechanistic literature filed today ([NOTE-1493](../notes.d/NOTE-1493.md), [NOTE-1490](../notes.d/NOTE-1490.md),
[NOTE-1494](../notes.d/NOTE-1494.md)) uses "constitutive relevance" for a part's behaviour
realizing a whole's behaviour at a time, after Craver. This term's
constitutive sense is a relational organization fixing an entity's
identity and boundary. The two differ in relata and in what they need.
The mechanists take the boundary as given (spatiotemporal parthood), which
the term's sense is meant to account for.

The debate does give one reason the term's senses need different
evidence. The dynamical sense can be shown by perturbation. A
constitutive dependence of a whole on its parts at a time cannot be shown
by any finite set of interventions, because every intervention on the
whole also acts on a part along another path ([THEORY-1288](../theory.d/THEORY-1288.md)). It can
only be inferred abductively or from further principles. A claim citing
this debate should say it means constitutive relevance in Craver's sense.
A claim that calls something constitutively individuated should say what
evidence it has, since interventions alone will not supply it
([CLAIM-528](../claims.d/CLAIM-528.md)).

No status, title or warrant changes.

It also missed [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md), the owner's bridge between will and individuation.
The join is filed as [CLAIM-544](../claims.d/CLAIM-544.md), which names its sense: constitutive, at
[CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md)'s level and target.

The term is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-11-05: the resource-bounded realizability readings

The third paste of the pasted synthesis on resource-bounded realizability gives a table, recorded in [QUESTION-393](../questions.d/QUESTION-393.md), of what a categorical framework could establish for each of this term's senses, which A says "mirrors the distinctions in TERM-088, rather than collapsing them". Restricted Yoneda profiles at a budget address the descriptive sense directly. Dynamical individuation needs relations between profiles over time, and "persistence need not mean that the profiles remain isomorphic". The constitutive sense needs an account of how relations help determine identity, and autonomy needs processes that maintain the supporting constraints. Of the profile construction A says that it might help with the other senses but "does not automatically establish them". No status changes.

## Note of 2026-11-05: A's fourth paste, and the assistant's contributions

A's fourth paste proposes a division of labour among the senses: Yoneda characterizes relational identity, resource-enriched process theory says what is affordable, and dynamical and organizational theories say how individuals form and persist; "None reduces entirely to the others." On the genetic sense, a category of architectural transformations "may help describe processes of genetic individuation", but "the existence of such a category doesn't itself show that a process creates a new individual." A's proposed synthesis, "Effective individuation is the stabilization of a system of distinctions across a declared repertoire of computationally and physically realizable interactions", is offered as "not a replacement for the existing definitions" here, and is the descriptive sense made resource-relative. [CLAIM-557](../claims.d/CLAIM-557.md) adds that the descriptive sense and the reachable classes run in opposite directions with resources. The term is unchanged. No status changes.
