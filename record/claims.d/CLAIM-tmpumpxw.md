# CLAIM-tmpumpxw: Adaptive governance is the maintenance of an ecology of partially independent inquiry-and-response capacities, differentiated enough for parallel exploration and integrated enough for coordinated action, in which persisted perspectives keep problem formulations and paths of development that the prevailing organization's demands for resources and authority would otherwise extinguish, so their value lies in keeping different capacities to discover which questions are worth asking

## The claim

A54:

> **Adaptive governance is the maintenance of an ecology of partially independent inquiry-and-response capacities, whose differentiation permits parallel exploration and whose integration permits coordinated action.**
>
> **Persisted perspectives act as conceptual nucleation sites within this ecology: they preserve alternative problem formulations and developmental pathways that would otherwise be extinguished by the resource and authority demands of the prevailing organization.**

and:

> **The value of alternative perspectives is not merely that they supply different answers to a question. It is that they preserve different capacities for discovering which questions are worth asking—and for developing answers without requiring the entire organization to redirect itself first.**

Its steps, in A54's summary: inquiry and realization are resource-bounded;
shared resources and dependencies create competition, and "Serial
authorization can eliminate opportunities before they can be realized";
persistent organization amortizes prior inquiry; parallel perspectives
preserve alternative pathways; governance allocates the conditions of
their persistence; and "Adaptive viability requires maintaining this
ecology."

On where nuclei live, after CLAIM-418 (perspectives are not agents):
"**parallel depth pursuit need not mean establishing a separate department
or authority for every idea**. A conceptual nucleus can be distributed
across documents, methods, specialists, shared practices, and temporary
collaborations."

- The extinguishing: CLAIM-406 and CLAIM-721, the loops in which a

- The questions worth asking: CLAIM-475 (what can be asked is a product of

- Maintaining the joint frontier: A54 §7 calls this "much closer to our
  earlier viability-of-reorganization thesis", which is TERM-tmpz28l1; the
  joint set is CLAIM-tmp4u0mj.

## Standing

Proposed, in A's voice. It is a synthesis of A54's steps, each filed or
held above; what it adds is the final clause, that the diversity worth
keeping is diversity of askable questions, not only of answers.
