# THEORY-1685: Gutiérrez-Manjón, Gómez-García and Bonales-Daimiel (2026) classify monetization-oriented dark patterns in games into temporal, monetary and social kinds and, coding nine of the top-grossing games-as-a-service of 2024 by direct observation, find these patterns in nearly all of them, with none imposing a spending limit and the five mobile titles using them more densely and aggressively than the four console and PC titles.

## The account

Gutiérrez-Manjón, Gómez-García and Bonales-Daimiel (2026) classify monetization-oriented dark patterns in games into temporal, monetary and social kinds and, coding nine of the top-grossing games-as-a-service of 2024 by direct observation, find these patterns in nearly all of them, with none imposing a spending limit and the five mobile titles using them more densely and aggressively than the four console and PC titles.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1735](../notes.d/NOTE-1735.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
