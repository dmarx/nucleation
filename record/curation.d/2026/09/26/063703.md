The owner asked for a pass over the `mereology` tag. It held six works: two
read and four skimmed seeds from the morning's philosophy sweep. All four
seeds were read in full, and each is now `Active`:

| work | what the reading found |
| --- | --- |
| Varzi, SEP *Mereology* ([LIT-124](../../record/literature.d/LIT-124.md)) | the formal reference: M ⊂ MM ⊂ EM ⊂ GEM, with the exact axioms. GEM is reached from M by exactly three pairings of a supplementation principle and a sum principle. |
| Carroll, *Reality as a Vector in Hilbert Space* ([LIT-123](../../record/literature.d/LIT-123.md)) | a programme, not a result: parts are factorisations chosen afterwards, to suit the dynamics |
| Register, *The Depth of the Body* ([LIT-138](../../record/literature.d/LIT-138.md)) | a moral "body part" relation, bounded below by depth, applied to BCIs hosted in the cloud |
| Meincke, *One or two?* ([LIT-142](../../record/literature.d/LIT-142.md)) | process ontology keeps the foster as a part, and makes the count indeterminate |

## What the readings corrected

Each skim overstated its paper, in the same direction: a proposal was reported
as a result.

- **The SEP entry** was summarised as calling the partial-order core
  "uncontroversial". §2.1 says the opposite: all three core axioms have serious
  dissenters, and Antisymmetry "can hardly be regarded as constitutive".
- **Carroll** was credited with a uniqueness result for the quasi-classical
  factorisation. He does not claim one. The only uniqueness claim is for local
  factorisations, and it is borrowed from Cotler et al. Carroll's own criterion
  needs inputs beyond the spectrum, which he admits are "somewhat cheating".
  Fields were reported as "recovered". The essay only hopes to recover them.
- **Carroll's finiteness figure, e^(e^123),** is what the paper prints (p. 5).
  It does not follow from the paper's own premise, S = πr²/G, which gives a
  single exponential. Quote the order of magnitude, never the figure.
- **Register** was summarised as resting his ethics on the real-definition
  metaphysics and dissolving the ownership conflict. He says the principles do
  not depend on that ontology, and the conflict is narrowed, not dissolved.
- **Meincke** was summarised as offering a rival to the Parthood View. She keeps
  parthood and rejects only its pairing with substance ontology.

## Eight works that were about parts and wholes and did not say so

The tag's blurb reads "parts and wholes — composition, individuation of
systems and collectives". The test was whether someone browsing that topic
would expect the work. Eight held works pass, and now carry the tag after their
existing topics:

- **Composition of wholes:** [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md) (ΦID causal emergence) and [LIT-027](../../record/literature.d/LIT-027.md)
  (emergence as the conversion of information).
- **Constitution:** [LIT-097](../../record/literature.d/LIT-097.md) (*The Extended Mind*).
- **Individuation:**
  - [LIT-157](../../record/literature.d/LIT-157.md) (Bourrat, reproducees and reproducers)
  - [LIT-167](../../record/literature.d/LIT-167.md) (Baedke, *The Organism*)
  - [LIT-168](../../record/literature.d/LIT-168.md) (SEP, *Biological Individuals*)
  - [LIT-188](../../record/literature.d/LIT-188.md) (Griesemer & Shavit, scaffolding individuality)
- **A collective as a candidate subject:** [LIT-159](../../record/literature.d/LIT-159.md) (Schwitzgebel, the United
  States as conscious).

Considered and not tagged: *Philosophy of Microbiology*, collective
intelligence and the ant/human puzzle study. They discuss collectives, but not
as a question about parts and wholes.

## The tag is wider than its reference

The SEP entry excludes two senses of "mereology":

- the systems-theory and ecological sense (its note 1);
- quantum part–whole formalisms (its note 18).

The tag's blurb names both on purpose: "individuation of systems and
collectives". So [LIT-124](../../record/literature.d/LIT-124.md) is the background for the philosophical works on the
tag ([LIT-142](../../record/literature.d/LIT-142.md), [LIT-138](../../record/literature.d/LIT-138.md) and the individuality cluster). It is only loosely the
background for [LIT-123](../../record/literature.d/LIT-123.md), [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md) and [LIT-027](../../record/literature.d/LIT-027.md), which use "part" for a tensor
factor or a coarse-grained variable. A reader should not expect the SEP's
axioms to hold of those.

## Still open on the tag

- **The two Bourrat papers** ([LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md), [LIT-049](../../record/literature.d/LIT-049.md)) remain `Proposed`. Each would
  be settled by a worked model: an explicit computation of its individuality
  measure on a model of an evolutionary transition. Another reading cannot
  supply that.
- **Six of the newly tagged works are still `Deferred`:** the four
  individuality works, *The Extended Mind* and Schwitzgebel. They are the
  natural next readings for this tag.
