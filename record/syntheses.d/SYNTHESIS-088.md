# SYNTHESIS-088: The formal-apparatus line is eighteen part accounts and forty-nine entries integrated directly, because the line is cross-cutting and sparse: most of its entries argue first in another line, the map found seven parts, eleven small clusters and forty-six entries linked to nothing, and most of those forty-six are promotion conditions whose links run to THEORY entries that carry no line; the parts meet mainly where an effective description's adequacy meets its response to intervention, and where budgets meet joint satisfiability; three claims are filed by derivation, and no link between the parts passes the claim test

*The account of the formal-apparatus line ("Formal apparatus", [ADR-040](../decisions.d/ADR-040.md)),
over the accounts of its parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). It integrates every entry whose
`line` includes formal-apparatus on 2026-11-11: 234 in-force entries when
the review began, and the three claims it filed. 187 are integrated
through eighteen part accounts and 50 directly. Most of the line's
entries are first in another line, and many are integrated by that
line's accounts as well; the part accounts name them. A mechanical
community map (stored relations and mentions) found seven parts, eleven
small clusters of two to six entries with no link to the rest, and 46
entries linked to no other entry of the line. Read, two of the seven parts held two inquiries each (interventions; naming), the eleven clusters gave eight accounts, and three of the unlinked entries one more, so there are eighteen. [SYNTHESIS-008](SYNTHESIS-008.md) and
[SYNTHESIS-009](SYNTHESIS-009.md) carry the line and are Superseded; they are not
integrated.*

## Integrated directly

The 46 entries the map found linked to nothing in the line, apart from
the three in [SYNTHESIS-084](SYNTHESIS-084.md), and three clusters of two that join
nothing else. The unlinked ones are unlinked mostly because of how they
were made: 25 of their 27 questions are promotion conditions moved off a
THEORY entry at the promotion audit of 2026-10-19 and 20 ([ADR-047](../decisions.d/ADR-047.md)) or at
later status reviews, and seven of their 19 claims were filed by that
audit. Their links run to their parent THEORY entries, which carry no
line. Where a member shares a ground with a part account's members, the
nearest account is named; that is a shared ground, not a link the
entries state.
