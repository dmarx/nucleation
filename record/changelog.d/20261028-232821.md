- [THEORY-tmpo85ke](record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpo85ke.md) (Proposed): self-categorization theory's core claim, that the level, kind, content and prototype of a self-category vary lawfully with the comparative frame by metacontrast and normative fit, with depersonalization as salience rises.
