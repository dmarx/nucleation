# ARG-047: Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy's "agency is a matter of perspective" supports the claim that perspectival, agential and institutional boundaries need not coincide

## The inference

[NOTE-1484](../notes.d/NOTE-1484.md), filed with the paper, says of [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md): "The paper's 'agency
is a matter of perspective' is a version of it." The passage (p. 1357):
"Describing an action by starting from a human being is but an analytical
choice that isolates some aspect in a series of continuous individuation
processes; it corresponds to an 'agential cut' (Barad, 2003, p. 815) or the
selection of a link in a chain of agencies ... Hence, agency is a matter of
perspective or perspectivism (Kuipers, 2022): Who or what has agency is a
premature question." If this supports [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md), it would be as a reading
in which where agency lies can come apart from other boundaries.

## Critical questions

- **Does the work say this, read in context?** No. This is the question
  that fails. The paper's perspective belongs to whoever describes the
  action, and it decides where the description begins. [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)'s
  perspective is [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) sense 1: an organization of what can matter in
  practice and how, which a martial tradition or a firm can hold and an
  agent can act through. The paper's sentence is about the analyst's
  cut. It says nothing about whether an organized significance can be
  shared by several agents or exist with no agent governing it.
- **Is it said within the scope the claim needs?** No. [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) needs
  three kinds of boundary that can each be drawn and compared: the
  perspectival, the agential and the institutional. The paper declines
  to draw the agential one ("a premature question"), so it has nothing
  to set against the other two.
- **Does the claim lean on the reading for more than the reading holds?**
  [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) does not cite the paper. The over-reading is in the first
  filing's note. What the sentence does bear on is [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md): whether an
  agent is found depends on where the description is cut. The paper
  leaves the cut unconstrained, and [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md) says a verdict becomes
  assessable once the criterion is fixed.

## What would bear on the conclusion

Bencherki 2017 ([LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md), read in [NOTE-1482](../notes.d/NOTE-1482.md)) does. Its Table 1 (p.
787) separates whether an action may count toward a member's
individuation from whether it may count toward the organization's.
Usurpation, the army taking the spouses' volunteer daycare as "one of the
services we offer" (pp. 791–792), is a case where agential attribution
and institutional recognition come apart. That is [THEORY-1285](../theory.d/THEORY-1285.md)'s
connection, and it stands.
