**The statute, checked.** The lead had recalled "the statute barring NTSB
reports from civil litigation" as 49 U.S.C. § 1154(b). The section is
right, and the NTSB's Tempe report cites it. The recollection was broader
than the text: § 1154(b) bars the Board's report from evidence or use "in a
civil action for damages resulting from a matter mentioned in the report",
and the regulations confine it to the Board's report of its determinations,
leaving investigators' factual reports admissible (49 CFR 835.2). Nothing
read mentions criminal proceedings.

**No email address** was sent to any service. Crossref, OpenAlex and the
Wayback availability API were queried without one.

**The lead's four proposals**, each assessed against the readings. All
four are in the assistant's voice: role `thesis` where they are claims,
Proposed, each summary ending that adopting it is the owner's call.

1. **Provenance without blame**, [CLAIM-tmpx4kjt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx4kjt.md), with [ARG-tmp67frz](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp67frz.md)
   (existence, Proposed). Proposed as a qualification of [CLAIM-733](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-733.md) that
   would object to or narrow it. **Changed in two ways.** It is filed with
   `complements`, not `objects_to`. [CLAIM-733](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-733.md) already says "Not that
   provenance is only a matter of blame", so the new claim denies nothing
   it states. **And it is filed weaker.** The proposal said [CLAIM-733](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-733.md) holds
   only where no independent causal-tracing institution is maintained. The
   evidence is one specimen, which shows that blame is not the only
   carrier of provenance and no more. The specimen was verified against
   [CASE-292](../../record/cases.d/CASE-292.md) and its sources. The NTSB, no-blame by regulation and kept out
   of damages suits by § 1154(b), named Uber's culture and Arizona's
   oversight as contributing. Rechecked on the NTSB's PDF: it addressed
   its recommendations (p. 60) to NHTSA, Arizona, the motor vehicle
   administrators' association and Uber ATG, none to the operator.
   Interpretive warrant. Lines: rightful-power, organizational-normativity
   (bears_on [QUESTION-476](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-476.md) and [QUESTION-470](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-470.md)).
2. **"Blame the system" as the new crumple zone**, [CLAIM-tmp5g7xc](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5g7xc.md)
   (empirical, Proposed), and the proposed study [CASE-tmpw9202](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpw9202.md). Filed as
   proposed. No reading tests the prediction or undercuts it. The SRE
   chapter's design fits its premise: review is internal, with senior
   management taking part, and indicting any team is excluded. Dekker and
   Nyce argue its mechanism for hospitals. Admitted by a discriminating
   prediction ([ADR-075](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-075.md)). The claim `complements` [CLAIM-707](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-707.md) and
   [CLAIM-tmpx4kjt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx4kjt.md); the case `illustrates` the claim, `bears_on`
   [QUESTION-476](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-476.md) and `draws_on` the SRE reading. [CLAIM-707](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-707.md) takes no new
   relation beyond the complement. Line: rightful-power for both.
3. **Blameless near-miss reporting**, a dated note on [CLAIM-700](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-700.md) (citing
   [CLAIM-702](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-702.md)): an unchecked mechanism hypothesis. **Corrected.** The
   proposal likened it to the statutory-ratio hospital in [CLAIM-700](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-700.md)'s
   Admission. That case keeps the compensation unseen and removes the
   budget rule. Reporting does the reverse, as in the interim review's
   logged-hours hospital, which the Admission says no longer separates. Two
   limits are added. Cook says most safety-creating work is routine, so it
   produces no near-miss to report. And seen is not used: reports break
   the loop only if they reach the budget.
4. **Action-item completion as a measure of second-order
   responsiveness**, a dated note on [CLAIM-719](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-719.md): a measurement proposal.
   **No reading supports** that completion is a known weak spot of
   postmortem practice, and the note says so: it is the lead's
   understanding. Qualified by Cook (point 15): remedies are often
   "end-of-the-chain", so completion is not adequacy. Only
   mechanism-revising items count.
