The synthesis review of 2026-11-15 (pull request [#505](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/505), since merged)
found that
organizational-normativity falls into four parts and is a candidate to
split by [ADR-083](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s signal, and left the decision to the owner. The
assistant proposed splitting it in two. The owner approved: "yes on the
two line split" (2026-11-15). [ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md) records the decision, and this
contribution is its first application ([ADR-071](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-071.md)).

## What was decided

- organizational-normativity keeps the review's first two parts, what a
  normative judgment needs and the wrong beyond harm with becoming
  normatively less informed, and its root questions [QUESTION-128](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and
  [QUESTION-129](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-129.md). Its blurb is narrowed to them and points to the new line.
- The new line `dependence-and-domination`, "Wholes, parts and
  dependence", takes the third and fourth parts: what wholes and parts owe
  and claim, and what those who govern an arrangement others depend on
  owe. Its blurb says "organization" there is [TERM-074](../../record/terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1 unless an
  entry says otherwise, and that bearers of obligations, agents or roles,
  are kept apart from the ground of the obligation.
- The fourth part is not its own line: two relations inside it, held
  together by its source, and joined to the third part through [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s
  complement onto [CLAIM-682](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-682.md).

The evidence is the review's count: 93 of the line's 102 stored relations
fall inside the four parts. Of the 9 that cross, 7 are `refines` or
`bears_on` onto [QUESTION-128](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) or [QUESTION-129](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-129.md), and the other 2 are [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s
`complements` onto [CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) and [CLAIM-682](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-682.md).

## Found along the way

- **[QUESTION-464](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-464.md) keeps distributed-agency first.** Its first line was
  distributed-agency, not organizational-normativity, so the in-place
  change leaves dependence-and-domination second, as [ADR-087](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-087.md)'s split did
  for entries whose first line was another.
- **[CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) drops organizational-normativity.** Its one tie to the
  remaining line is a `complements` onto [CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), which [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md) does not
  count as participation.
- **No moved entry rests on an entry of the first two parts.** Their
  `rests_on` steps go to [CLAIM-428](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-428.md), [CLAIM-523](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-603](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-603.md), [CLAIM-605](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) and
  [CLAIM-457](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-457.md), in other lines, as before.
- **Nothing gains the new line from outside.** The only entries outside
  the two parts that point at a moved entry are [CLAIM-677](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)'s `complements`
  onto [CLAIM-670](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-670.md), which does not count, and readings bearing on
  [QUESTION-460](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) and [QUESTION-461](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-461.md), whose lines are derived ([ADR-090](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-090.md)).
- **[TERM-122](../../record/terms.d/TERM-122.md)** stored `line` as a scalar; it is now a list.
- **[ADR-099](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-099.md)** placed [CLAIM-669](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-669.md), [CLAIM-670](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-670.md) and [CLAIM-671](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-671.md) in
  organizational-normativity. It takes a history note; its body is
  unchanged.

The other accounts were changed because each states its components'
lines, and a stated line that is no longer true would misstate a
component, which their defeaters name. [SYNTHESIS-036](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-036.md) and [SYNTHESIS-049](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-049.md)
name organizational-normativity only in history or as will-organization's
former sixth part, and are unchanged.

**One line account over two parts, not one merged account.** The
alternative was to make [SYNTHESIS-092](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-092.md) the line's account, absorbing
[SYNTHESIS-093](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-093.md)'s nine components, and set [SYNTHESIS-093](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-093.md) Superseded. The
synthesis-review skill gives a line that falls into parts an account per
part and a line account over them ([ADR-083](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-083.md)), and does not force one
account over parts that hang together by little: the two parts share one
stored relation, and the fourth hangs together by its source. Both part
accounts were filed hours earlier and no review has found either
unfaithful, so superseding one would retire a faithful account to save a
file. The line account states what joins them.
