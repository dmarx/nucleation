# SYNTHESIS-tmpvz4mj: The formal-apparatus line is eighteen part accounts and forty-nine entries integrated directly, because the line is cross-cutting and sparse: most of its entries argue first in another line, the map found seven parts, eleven small clusters and forty-six entries linked to nothing, and most of those forty-six are promotion conditions whose links run to THEORY entries that carry no line; the parts meet mainly where an effective description's adequacy meets its response to intervention, and where budgets meet joint satisfiability; three claims are filed by derivation, and no link between the parts passes the claim test

*The account of the formal-apparatus line ("Formal apparatus", ADR-040),
over the accounts of its parts (ADR-083). It integrates every entry whose
`line` includes formal-apparatus on 2026-11-11: 234 in-force entries when
the review began, and the three claims it filed. 187 are integrated
through eighteen part accounts and 50 directly. Most of the line's
entries are first in another line, and many are integrated by that
line's accounts as well; the part accounts name them. A mechanical
community map (stored relations and mentions) found seven parts, eleven
small clusters of two to six entries with no link to the rest, and 46
entries linked to no other entry of the line. Read, two of the seven parts held two inquiries each (interventions; naming), the eleven clusters gave eight accounts, and three of the unlinked entries one more, so there are eighteen. SYNTHESIS-008 and
SYNTHESIS-009 carry the line and are Superseded; they are not
integrated.*

## The parts

- **Reaching under a budget (SYNTHESIS-tmpc35sn), 39 entries.** Feasible, reachable and reliably reached come apart, and a transition's threshold is the least peak over admissible paths (CLAIM-567, CLAIM-571, CLAIM-581); it integrates CLAIM-tmpopi1b, filed with it.
- **Effective descriptions (SYNTHESIS-tmpa7ef9), 27 entries.** An effective description is adequate only relative to a declared regime (TERM-065, QUESTION-252), and equivalence under probes is coarser than identity (CLAIM-264).
- **Interventions and closure (SYNTHESIS-tmpe15k3), 20 entries.** Agreement in ordinary operation does not fix the response to an intervention on the mechanism (CLAIM-407, CLAIM-409), and recovery under interventions on state cannot show where maintenance is realized (CLAIM-522).
- **Anchoring correspondences (SYNTHESIS-tmp0hoh6), 6 entries.** An intertwining through an uninformative map is vacuous or lossy (CLAIM-011), and comparing experiments by deficiency (CLAIM-381, CLAIM-503).
- **Joint satisfiability (SYNTHESIS-tmp68daz), 19 entries.** Whether demands or local descriptions can be met together is decided against a declared structure of comparison, with failure certified by a cycle or a set of multipliers (CLAIM-538, CLAIM-365).
- **Gluing (SYNTHESIS-tmphb9rl), 17 entries.** The event sheaf always glues, and contextuality is a failure of extension over supports or distributions (CLAIM-131, CLAIM-037).
- **When a structure is worth naming (SYNTHESIS-tmpgtv7b), 10 entries.** A candidate is kept when its effect-to-noise ratio beats its Occam cost (CLAIM-154), and a spectral gap makes definite a subspace, not a direction (CLAIM-164).
- **What an invariant keeps and forgets (SYNTHESIS-tmpdbary), 4 entries.** Characters classify representations only up to isomorphism (CLAIM-132), and a factorization is fixed only up to a gauge (CLAIM-148).
- **Stability against persistence (SYNTHESIS-tmpemzd4), 10 entries.** Stability does not entail viability (CLAIM-079); it integrates CLAIM-tmpsh6jv, filed with it.
- **Conservation under Markov kernels (SYNTHESIS-tmp002mq), 6 entries.** A conservation law has to be derived (CLAIM-107, CLAIM-094).
- **Granger causality and transfer entropy (SYNTHESIS-tmp2gov9), 6 entries.** Predictive quantities relative to a stated information set (CLAIM-333, CLAIM-335).
- **Readouts and kernels (SYNTHESIS-tmpk32cm), 4 entries.** A regularized linear readout separates exactly what the kernel separates (CLAIM-379, CLAIM-269).
- **Ordinal potentials (SYNTHESIS-tmpy0g7w), 4 entries.** A potential exists on a finite set exactly when winning moves never cycle (CLAIM-434, TERM-077).
- **Capture in the linearized meaning–authority model (SYNTHESIS-tmprfpxq), 3 entries.** CLAIM-340, CLAIM-400 and CLAIM-346.
- **Delegation and self-amplification (SYNTHESIS-tmpsomlg), 3 entries.** CLAIM-282, CLAIM-375 and CLAIM-502.
- **Productive nuclei (SYNTHESIS-tmpdtd6m), 3 entries.** CLAIM-595, CLAIM-596 and CLAIM-599.
- **Random projections and sparse recovery (SYNTHESIS-tmppyfkv), 3 entries.** QUESTION-120, QUESTION-168 and QUESTION-251.
- **Coarse-graining and renormalization (SYNTHESIS-tmppu0yx), 3 entries.** QUESTION-185, QUESTION-205 and QUESTION-232, all open.

### What a change of model does to contextuality

- **CLAIM-070** (Active, demonstrated; first line pragmatic-transport):
  transport induced by one global stochastic kernel carries a global
  extension of the source model to one of the target model.
- **CLAIM-372** (Active, demonstrated): within a fixed scenario, the
  contextual fraction is a piecewise-linear and Lipschitz-continuous
  function of the empirical model's probability table.

  one context can take the contextual fraction from 1 to 0. It quotes
  THEORY-165's Lipschitz bound, which is CLAIM-372's result after a split;
  neither claim names the other.

- **Sentence.** CLAIM-267 (the assistant's restatement of CLAIM-056's
  general content, split under ADR-040; thesis, demonstrated, Active)
  bounds the end-to-end error of a chain of maps by its per-step

### A positive-pair kernel (filed at this review)

- **CLAIM-tmpz1gnk** (the assistant's at this review; demonstrated,
  Proposed; rests on CLAIM-291 and CLAIM-370): the kernel
