# THEORY-tmpxw0zo: Democracy, on Tronto's account, is the allocation of caring responsibilities in a political community, with equal voice for all in making the allocations; care is misallocated by excluding those one wants to saddle with the hardest tasks, and by exempting oneself through appeal to more important duties such as protection and production

Tronto (2014), [LIT-tmptq6ih](../literature.d/LIT-tmptq6ih.md), read in [NOTE-tmpfpn7t](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpfpn7t.md).

## The account

"Democracy as the allocation of caring responsibilities in a political
community and the method of giving equal voice to all members of the
community in making these allocations". Two misallocations: "to exclude
those you want to saddle with the most difficult tasks", and exemption,
"absent oneself from responsibilities because one has more important
duties: protection, production, taking care of one's own".

## What it does not show

The slides state the account; they do not argue it. Blame and control are
not addressed.
