# CLAIM-tmp8swu5: Which of an agent's available regimes becomes effective depends on maintained relations — accessibility, precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation — that are not reducible to the contents of the competing regimes

## The claim

Section 1: "Agents may possess multiple action-generating regimes whose
practical efficacy depends upon maintained relations governing their
accessibility, precedence, jurisdiction, persistence, suspension, and
transformation."

It has two independent lines of support, [ARG-tmpuhkwv](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpuhkwv.md) (Northstar-A/B) and
[ARG-tmpgkhf9](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpgkhf9.md) (the reluctant writer), which is what lets the paper claim
the role spans scales. Its standing objections are [CLAIM-tmp25zdy](CLAIM-tmp25zdy.md) (answered)
and [CLAIM-tmppqo06](CLAIM-tmppqo06.md) and [CLAIM-tmp7j8hl](CLAIM-tmp7j8hl.md) (both still open).

## What it does not say

That the dimensions are a complete taxonomy (Section 7.5), or that the
mechanisms are new ([CLAIM-tmp5znml](CLAIM-tmp5znml.md)).
