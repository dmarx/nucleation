# CLAIM-tmp11j0f: An organization can lose adaptability by two routes, becoming less able to answer new questions or its institutions becoming less able to ask them; the second is harder to detect, since a question nobody formulates leaves no recorded failure to answer it, so an ecosystem can grow better at its established questions while becoming less able to answer unfamiliar ones and to discover which of them matter

## The claim

A50 §2 defines two sets at time t: the queries "that can be formulated",
which is TERM-092's frontier, and the queries "the organization can
adequately answer", a superlevel set of TERM-115's readiness (A50 §8 writes
it 𝓔(S)). "These are not necessarily nested, and neither is automatically
contained in the other. Some questions can be asked but not answered. Some
capabilities exist but nobody has yet formulated the question that would
elicit or evaluate them."

Hence: "A system can lose adaptability either because it becomes less
capable of answering new questions or because its institutions become less
capable of asking them. The second kind of loss can be especially difficult
to detect, because an organization that cannot formulate a relevant
question will not ordinarily record its failure to answer it."

A50 §8 turns the two routes into "two capacities to maintain": "The
capacity to formulate informative, legitimate questions" and "The capacity
to develop viable answers", and "Both can deteriorate despite apparently
successful current performance." The payoff: "an ecosystem may become
better at answering its established questions while becoming less capable
of answering unfamiliar ones—and less capable of discovering which
unfamiliar questions matter".

## Where the record stands

- CLAIM-475 makes asking resource-bounded, so its capacity can decline; this
  claim needs that.
- CLAIM-tmp76xaw says the capacity to recognize a demand as demand-worthy is
  a slow capability that must stay open to correction. This claim adds the
  second route's asymmetry of detection.
- CLAIM-462 separates the capacity for effective action from the capacity to
  be corrected by others, which is the same shape at another level.
- CASE-tmp95f8e constructs the asymmetry: an organization that never enters
  an unnamed demand "scores zero violations against the original
  specification and its own while the harm runs".

## What it does not say

It does not say the second loss is undetectable, only that the
organization's own record of failures will not show it; a party outside
its query family can.
