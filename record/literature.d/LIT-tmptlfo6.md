# LIT-tmptlfo6: Slammed for Bribery, Siemens Continued to Ignore Red Flags

Knight (2021), 100Reporters — https://100r.org/2021/07/siemens/

## Key takeaways

- **A signal exempted during the monitorship.** A June 2010 memo by two
  senior compliance officers declared that the red-flag tool "does not
  apply to Models A, B, and D", while its footnote admitted "We cannot
  exclude the possibility that the [import/export company] and the
  end-customer abuse the structure and make other dealings under the
  table."
- **After it.** A Chinese verdict reports that "in the same year that
  Waigel concluded his monitorship, a Siemens business manager offered to
  pay a hospital president" ¥2 million; and a 2013 email from the most
  senior sales manager at Siemens China asked of the "huge gap between
  biddings and contracts", "Where's the gap going?"

## Standing in the record

Reporting, not an account; its post-monitorship evidence is a verdict
against an official and an allegation by a dismissed manager. No THEORY is
filed from it.
