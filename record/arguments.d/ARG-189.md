# ARG-189: Cai's InfoNCE energy → weak active-matter reading

## The inference

[CLAIM-798](../claims.d/CLAIM-798.md): "the weak reading follows from gradient flow, the strong one
has to be shown". Cai and colleagues: in the large-batch limit unimodal
InfoNCE is a free energy, alignment potential over τ minus entropy, with
"the unique Gibbs-form minimizer" (Proposition 3.1); "uniformity" is
"entropy-driven spreading inside the alignment basin", "not ... a global
force opposing alignment" (§1). The symmetric multimodal objective is a
single scalar F^Sym over both modalities' densities (Definition 4.3).

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** If the
  strong reading held for contrastive objectives, one would expect a
  non-variational term, a forcing not derived from one energy. None
  appears: dispersion is the cross-entropy against the model's own
  smoothed density, a deterministic part of the objective; and with two
  modalities there is still one joint energy, so the coupling between them
  is reciprocal and joint gradient flow is covered by [CLAIM-818](../claims.d/CLAIM-818.md)'s Lyapunov
  argument.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** Only in part. The
  paper is about the objective, not the dynamics ("the geometry of the
  objective rather than the trajectory"), so it cannot rule out activity
  from the optimizer or the noise: how stochastic optimization explores
  the landscape "remains an important open problem" (App. G.1). It rules out the objective itself as the source. Alternating
  best responses of two encoders against each other's barrier are a place
  where non-gradient-looking behaviour could arise; the paper does not
  study it.
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** The "bifurcation" is a
  structural difference between the unimodal and the multimodal objective,
  not a transition in a control parameter, so it is not evidence of a
  dynamical bifurcation.

## Verdict

Supports [CLAIM-798](../claims.d/CLAIM-798.md)'s weak reading as evidence that a contrastive
objective's population picture is passive; gives nothing toward the strong
one. Proposed.
