# LEAD-089: Model Northstar with control and metacontrol mechanisms alone and then with a will-organization over them, price the difference, and set planning, control and institutional theory beside the account

## What it would do

Model the case both ways and price the difference; read planning theory,
control theory's regulation and institutional theory for what each already
explains.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-056](../questions.d/QUESTION-056.md): "Take a case such as Northstar, model it with control and
  metacontrol mechanisms alone, then with a will-organization over them, and
  price the difference as [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) does."
- [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md): "Planning theory and institutional theory have not been set
  beside the account at all."

## What would count

A difference the control-only model cannot produce at matched complexity
supports [CLAIM-487](../claims.d/CLAIM-487.md)'s gain; none, or one an existing theory already gives,
defeats [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: run, and what remains

Carried out by a delegated worker on 2026-11-22 (scratch/studies/lead-089/).
Northstar was modelled both ways and the difference priced: [CASE-tmpokq28](../cases.d/CASE-tmpokq28.md)
(the executed study), [CLAIM-tmp92xul](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp92xul.md) (the finding) and [CLAIM-tmp773u9](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp773u9.md) (planning,
control and institutional theory set beside it, from readings the record
already holds; nothing was fetched).

- **The model.** It is the simplest that prices the difference. The case's
  own three variants of authority are crossed. Three regimes are possible,
  and the attitudes are held fixed. Training is on single changes, and the
  test is on combinations never seen. Five control-only models see the
  arrangement as an input. Discrete outcomes give exact or quadrature
  evidence for all but the logistic gates, which are Laplace and regular.
- **The result.** Where the case is as stipulated, the will-organization
  wins by 10 to 71 nats beyond its Occam cost. It alone predicts the unseen
  combinations, and more data does not close the gap. It loses where
  authority is idle or idiosyncratic. Training evidence alone also passes a
  wrongly composed relation.
- **The fronts.** Control theory's regulation and executive control give
  the models that miss. Planning theory, with the plan fixed, gives none.
  Institutional theory writes the same relations. "What would count" above
  reads as if inverted: a difference an existing theory already gives
  supports [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md) on that front rather than defeating it. [CLAIM-tmp773u9](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp773u9.md)
  reads it so.

What remains:

- A second realizer, a person such as [CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md), modelled with the same
  variables. That is [CLAIM-487](../claims.d/CLAIM-487.md)'s cross-realizer gain, which one realizer
  cannot test.
- Bratman's account of shared agency ([LIT-488](../literature.d/LIT-488.md), Unavailable), which could
  change the planning verdict.
- [QUESTION-041](../questions.d/QUESTION-041.md), which this lead also advances, is not touched: the
  study does not cut or restore members' channels.
