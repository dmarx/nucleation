# LIT-tmpp3m1q: A theoretical model of phase transitions in human hand movements

Hermann Haken, J. A. Scott Kelso and Heinz Bunz (1985), *Biological
Cybernetics* 51(5):347–356.

The brief's citation (Biological Cybernetics 51, 1985) is correct.

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read. What follows is Kelso's 2021 account of the paper
([LIT-tmpw3fkg](LIT-tmpw3fkg.md)), not a reading of it.*

- The order-parameter equation for relative phase and its potential, with
  bistability for b/a > 1/4 and only in-phase stable below it.
- The derivation of that equation from two coupled "hybrid" oscillators
  (Kelso 2021, Eqs. 4–6; that paper calls the order-parameter equation
  the model's "top tier", above the coupled components).
- The stochastic version, and the observed critical fluctuations, are in
  Schöner, Haken & Kelso (1986) rather than in this paper, by Kelso's
  account.
