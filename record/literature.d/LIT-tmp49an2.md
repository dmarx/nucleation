# LIT-tmp49an2: The concept of transduction and its use in organization studies

Styhre, A. (2010). The concept of transduction and its use in organization studies. *Emergence: Complexity & Organization*, 12(3), 115–131.

Not read: only the abstract was reached (see the status note).

## Why this is here

The owner asked for the works Bencherki, Brummans & Vézy (2024,
LIT-tmp3mudg) lean on. The parent (p. 1356) quotes Styhre twice to
define transduction, which "Simondon refers to ... as communication or
transduction":

- **p. 125**: "a concept introduced to explore the individuation of an
  organism, a technology, ... a social system or a social formation such
  as an organization".
- **p. 120**: "[t]ransduction is potentially useful in organization
  analysis because it focuses on the folding of different forces and
  elements together in the individuating of individuals, collectives and
  entities".

Neither quotation could be checked. Both agree with the abstract's
"heterogeneous resources are brought together and temporarily
stabilized into functional units". The parent also lists Styhre among
those who theorize constitution "without theorizing the vital role of
agency" (pp. 1354–1355). The abstract's subjects, Schatzki's site of
the social and Czarniawska's action nets, are frameworks of action,
so that characterization needs the full text to judge. Bencherki
(LIT-tmp1rpyl, p. 782) cites Styhre for transduction as propagation from
"kin to kin".
