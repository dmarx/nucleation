# CLAIM-tmpvim0a: Communicative identity is characterized by the observational structures and relations invariant or equivariant under specified classes of transformation, and fidelity by their preservation, deformation or loss

## The claim

A105: "**Communicative identity is characterized by the observational structures
and relations invariant or equivariant under specified classes of
transformation. Translation, conditional generation, and repeated
reconstruction transport these structures, with fidelity determined by their
preservation, deformation, or loss.**" "The question *What survives
translation?* becomes mathematically sharper: **What are the invariants of
communicative transformation, which classes of transformation preserve them,
and what happens when those symmetries are broken?**"

## Why it was replaced

The manuscript §1 keeps it in softer words: "communicative identity should be
described through a system of relationally organized observables", whose
identity "depends on which patterns of differences, compatibility relations, and
inferential consequences remain identifiable under transformation" ([CLAIM-tmpww3q0](CLAIM-tmpww3q0.md)).
Versions after U37 are outside this chunk.
