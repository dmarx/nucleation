The reading order of `2026/09/25/204231` is done. **27 of the 29 works now
have a `Read` NOTE.** Each is written from the full text, with a claims table
and a section listing what the reading contradicted in the seed. The two left
over are the Toxicon venom proteomics paper (`LIT-026`) and the concussion
review (`LIT-029`). Neither is open access. Every route to a full text failed:
PMC and Europe PMC, the publishers, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and
ResearchGate. Both stay `Deferred` at abstract depth. Reading either needs a
PDF supplied by hand.

**Bourrat.** The measure it proposes is never computed on any system. Its
claim that the measure equals the observational one fails on a two-bit
counterexample: take S′ = S ⊕ E with uniform inputs. Then A = 1 bit, and the
interventional measure is 0.

**The Eulalia paper.** It is a plausibility model. Every lunar date scales
from one sample-dated crater. Its five fitted breakup ages span 785–965 Myr,
not the 800–900 its prose says.

**Spectral Networks.** It is unrefereed, and several theorem statements are
broader than their proofs.

**Lindgren & Liukkonen.** It puts the imaginary unit in twice by hand:
through √g instead of √(−g), and through a complex noise variance chosen to
force linearity.

## The pattern, held across all 27

Every skim carried at least one claim that the full text does not support.
Every one of those errors pointed in the same direction: toward the work being
more connected, more general or more conclusive than it is. The first five
readings (`2026/09/25/213054`) found this. Twenty-two more did not break it.
The corrections include:

- **Theorems stated more broadly than proved.** Ni et al.'s Theorem 4.1.
  Casals & Nho's Theorem 3.
- **Printed equations that do not do what the text says.**
  - Paisley et al.'s SVI update re-adds the prior at every step.
  - Weber et al.'s workflow silently uses the flow form the paper had just
    rejected.
  - Grimaldi et al.'s finite-size prefactors have the wrong power of N. A
    numerical check confirmed this.
- **Headline numbers the paper's own tables contradict.**
  - The Eulalia age range.
  - The waste-routing GA's "consistently" better routes.
  - The ~30% optimality gaps the skim left out.
- **Attributions that were syntheses.** The "Russian military definition" in
  the ISW report is Clark's composite, not a quotation.

Several readers also did their own arithmetic, and those checks are part of
the record:

- a comb-network simulation;
- a linear-programming check of a lemma;
- a Kesten–Stigum detectability calculation, which puts the stochastic
  block model's limit near 0.78, where Ni et al. read collapse as absence;
- ontic-state counts for the structure theorem, which match the GPT
  dimension exactly.

Where a reader went beyond the paper, its NOTE says so.
