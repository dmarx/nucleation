# LIT-232: Knowledge Sheaves: A Sheaf-Theoretic Framework for Knowledge Graph Embedding

Thomas Gebhart, Jakob Hansen, Paul Schrater (2021), *AISTATS 2023 (Proceedings of the 26th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics, PMLR vol. 206); first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-2110.03789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03789)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-843](../notes.d/NOTE-843.md).*

- **The dictionary.** Entity types and relation types form a schema
  graph Q. A knowledge sheaf on Q gives each relation two linear maps,
  head and tail, into a comparison space. Pulled back to the knowledge
  graph, every edge of one relation shares its maps, and entity
  embeddings are a 0-cochain.
- **Scores are coboundaries.** Structured Embedding's score is
  ‖F_h x_h − F_t x_t‖², and its sum over the graph is the sheaf Laplacian
  form xᵀLx. A relation vector gives TransR, identity maps TransE, a
  diagonal complex map RotatE. Bilinear models (RESCAL, ComplEx, HolE,
  NTN, QuatE) are not covered.
- **Consistency is free; contrast is the content.** The zero embedding is
  a section, "consistent, but useless". Negative subgraphs must fail to
  be sections, relaxed to a margin γ that the margin ranking loss
  enforces exactly. Such an embedding ranks pairs, but cannot classify a
  single triple.
- **Queries by harmonic extension.** Fix the known entities on a query
  template and minimize the Laplacian energy over the rest. The cost is a
  Schur complement, zero exactly when the fixed values extend to a
  section. No training on complex queries is needed.
- **Results are preliminary.** No tuning, one entity type. Harmonic
  extension beats summing TransE vectors on ip and pi queries (NELL-995
  easy ip MRR 0.296 against 0.104), not on intersections, and the models
  sit well below BetaE and Query2Box.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped
under the heading *Knowledge Graphs*, with a skim ([NOTE-211](../notes.d/NOTE-211.md)). Read in
full on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-843](../notes.d/NOTE-843.md), which supersedes the skim. The
account filed from it is [THEORY-299](../theory.d/THEORY-299.md): coboundary-type embeddings are
sheaf cochains, and because a sheaf of vector spaces always has the zero
section, their content is a contrastive margin. That is also what keeps
the paper apart from the record's sheaf-theoretic contextuality line
([LIT-016](LIT-016.md), [THEORY-012](../theory.d/THEORY-012.md)), where the phenomenon is that no global section
exists. The paper cites no contextuality work, and it belongs to the
cellular-sheaf lineage that [NOTE-016](../notes.d/NOTE-016.md) already separated from Abramsky and
Brandenburger.
