# THEORY-tmp960op: Foschi, Hull and Ku give closed-form equations in the tangents of half the folding angles that describe the full configuration space of any degree-4 rigid origami vertex, Euclidean or non-Euclidean, as two folding modes, which meet at the flat state of a developable vertex and are disconnected when the sector angles do not sum to 2π

## Source

Foschi, Hull and Ku (2022), LIT-tmpegvxm; read in part in NOTE-tmpt4jsa.

## What was actually shown

- **The equations** (§§II–III): one relation for opposite and one for
  adjacent folding angles, in tan(ρ/2), for any degree-4 vertex; proofs in
  the appendices, not read.
- **Two modes** (Corollary 1): the configuration space consists of two
  curves in R⁴.
- **Disconnection** (§III, Example 1): in the worked example the elliptic
  and hyperbolic configuration spaces are disconnected and the developable
  one connected, because a vertex whose sector angles do not sum to 2π
  cannot have every folding angle zero at once.
- **Discrete case** (§V A): the square-twist pouch's rigid configuration
  space is discrete, three states.

## What this does not say

- Anything about vertices of degree other than 4.
- Which parts of each curve avoid self-intersection; those are drawn, not
  excluded.
- That the disconnection is proved in general in the main text: it is shown
  in an example with a general reason.
