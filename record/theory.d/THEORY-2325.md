# THEORY-2325: Chatbots may fuel delusions through dissonance and opacity

Østergaard (2023), [LIT-3302](../literature.d/LIT-3302.md), read in [NOTE-2428](../notes.d/NOTE-2428.md).

## The account

Two triggers act whatever the chatbot says: dissonance about an interlocutor that seems a person and is known not to be, and opacity about how it works. The chatbot is what the delusion is about.

## Evidence

None. The examples are "strictly hypothetical", and the comparison with internet chat between humans is argued, not shown.
