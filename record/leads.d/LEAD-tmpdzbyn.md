# LEAD-tmpdzbyn: Build one model that hosts the four mechanisms of the governance leads together, evidence from outside the proposals, protection of that evidence from revision and refusal, a return channel from evaluated to evaluator, and scoring apart from agreement, and test whether each is a separate requirement for self-correction or whether one entails the others; add a miscalibrated protected anchor and a slower survey of it

## The design

Start from [LEAD-190](LEAD-190.md)'s population on the coupled meaning–influence model,
which already has grants, critics and factions. Add four switches:

1. a block of tasks on which some process holds evidence that does not
   come from the proposals ([LEAD-180](LEAD-180.md)'s condition);
2. whether that evidence can be rewritten or refused by the revision
   ([LEAD-177](LEAD-177.md)'s);
3. a return channel from the evaluated party's standing to the
   evaluator's resources, with a strength ([LEAD-015](LEAD-015.md)'s);
4. whether critics are scored by observed relevance or by agreement
   ([LEAD-190](LEAD-190.md)'s).

Score correction on ground truth the population never sees, and on the
honest minority's standing, as [LEAD-180](LEAD-180.md) does.

## What would settle it

- If each switch, turned off alone, defeats correction while the others
  hold, they are separate requirements, as [CLAIM-tmp4h9rc](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4h9rc.md) read strictly
  predicts.
- If turning one off is harmless whenever another holds, the principle
  narrows to fewer conditions.
- Also, a miscalibrated protected anchor with and without a slower
  survey, to test [CLAIM-tmpw11e6](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw11e6.md) in a second model.
