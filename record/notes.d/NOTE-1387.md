# NOTE-1387: Hoel — Causal emergence 2.0

## Contribution

It reworks Hoel's causal emergence (CE 1.0, [LIT-1038](../literature.d/LIT-1038.md)) in three ways. The
measure is no longer effective information but "causal primitives" (CP):
a normalized determinism and a normalized degeneracy, the
information-theoretic forms of sufficiency and necessity. A system is
analysed along a micro→macro path of successive coarse-grainings, and
each step's gain ∆CP is credited to that scale, so several scales can
contribute. The spread of the gains along the path is a new number,
"emergent complexity". It is one paper, sole-authored, on three toy
chains.

## Key insight

EI = (determinism − degeneracy) · log₂ n, so EI rewards a scale for having
many states as well as for clean transitions. Drop the size factor and
measure only the bracket (CP, called the effectiveness in CE 1.0); then
compare each scale with the one just below it. A coarse-graining
"contributes" exactly when it makes transitions more deterministic or
less degenerate than its refinement, and the contributions along a path
form a profile rather than a single winner.

## Assumptions

- **A known Markov chain.** The microscale is a given TPM; macroscales
  are coarse-grains of its states, built as in Hoel et al. 2013 and Klein
  and Hoel 2020 (cited, not restated).
- **Uniform interventions at each scale.** P(C) is uniform over the
  states of the scale being scored, not a coarse-graining of the micro
  P(C). §6.1 argues gains survive other choices, citing Comolatti and
  Hoel, and Fig. 4 shows one case with observational P(C) at both scales.
- **Dynamical consistency.** S2: a macroscale is kept only if random
  walkers started at every state give the same coarse-grained
  trajectories for 5 steps (summed KL divergence zero); others are
  discarded.
- **One chosen path.** The endpoint is the macroscale of greatest CP gain
  (ties to the least reduced), and the path is the longest consistent
  one up to it (§3.3).

## Key results

- **Definitions (§2.2, §3.2).** determinism = 1 − ⟨H(E|c)⟩/log₂ n;
  degeneracy = 1 − H(E)/log₂ n with H(E) the entropy of the effect
  distribution under uniform P(C); specificity = 1 − degeneracy;
  CP = determinism + specificity − 1. CE = total ∆CP along the path.
- **Fig. 2.** An 8-state chain with an equivalence class (4,5,6,7): CP
  rises from 0.66 at the microscale to 1 at the macroscale that lumps the
  class, then gains stop. CE = 0.33.
- **Emergent complexity (§4).** EC = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ with pᵢ = ∆CPᵢ/Σ∆CP
  over the path's steps. A "top-heavy" system with two pure equivalence
  classes scores 1.67 bits; a near-copy whose states 0 and 4 differ from
  their classmates scores 2.07 bits, because ∆CP peaks before the
  endpoint.
- **CE 1.0 misses cases (§6.2, Fig. 4).** In a two-block chain whose
  within-block probability is moved step by step onto self-loops, EI
  shows no causal emergence at any step while ∆CP starts positive and
  falls to zero as the chain becomes a permutation.
- **An SVD heuristic (S3).** Dropping σ₁ from Zhang et al.'s
  dynamical-reversibility average ([LIT-1129](../literature.d/LIT-1129.md)) gives γ*, which tracks CP
  under added noise and common causes; σ₂ − γ* estimates total CE, and
  singular values above the mean of the rest flag contributing
  "directionalities": one (0.61) for the top-heavy system, three (0.489,
  0.06, 0.06) for the mesoscale one.

## Claims

| id | claim | strength | support |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | CP is EI with the size term removed | strong | the decomposition EI = effectiveness · log₂ n of Hoel et al. 2013 |
| C2 | Along a chosen path, ∆CP apportions causal contribution to scales | moderate | definition; three 8-state examples |
| C3 | EC separates a system with mesoscale structure from one without | weak | two hand-built chains, 1.67 against 2.07 bits |
| C4 | CE 2.0 "captures all macroscale causation" that CE 1.0 misses | weak | one family of chains (Fig. 4); no proof of "all" |
| C5 | CE 2.0 does not depend on the choice of P(C) | weak | asserted from a cited paper and one figure |
| C6 | γ* and σ₂ − γ* estimate CP and CE without search | weak | qualitative agreement on the paper's own examples |

## Method

Enumerate coarse-grains of the TPM, discard inconsistent ones, compute CP
at each, pick the endpoint and the longest path to it, record ∆CP at each
step, normalize the gains and take their entropy. For large systems a
diminishing-returns stop (∆CP below ε, or a run of shrinking ratios)
replaces enumeration.

## Concepts

- **Causal primitives (CP)**: here both the pair (sufficiency, necessity)
  and the scalar determinism + specificity − 1; the paper uses the word
  for both.
- **Micro→macro path**: a chain π⁽¹⁾ → … → π⁽ᵏ⁾ of consistent
  partitions, each a coarse-grain of the last.
- **Causal apportioning schema**: crediting each step its ∆CP over the
  previous step.
- **Emergent complexity**: entropy of the normalized gains along a path.

## Connections

It is the successor of Hoel, Albantakis and Tononi 2013 ([LIT-1038](../literature.d/LIT-1038.md),
[THEORY-222](../theory.d/THEORY-222.md)), whose EI and effectiveness it reuses. It answers the
criticism of CE 1.0's uniform-intervention assumption by Eberhardt and Lee
(2022, cited as [25], not registered) and Aaronson. Jansma and Hoel's
"Engineering emergence" ([LIT-1123](../literature.d/LIT-1123.md)) extends it to all paths at once and, in
doing so, drops the consistency filter this paper relies on. Its SVD
heuristic adapts Zhang et al. ([LIT-1129](../literature.d/LIT-1129.md)). It contrasts itself with Rosas et
al.'s information decomposition ([LIT-1244](../literature.d/LIT-1244.md)) and Barnett and Seth's
dynamical independence ([THEORY-257](../theory.d/THEORY-257.md)), on the ground that both rest on
observational mutual information, and with Rosas et al.'s "software in
the natural world" ([LIT-1306](../literature.d/LIT-1306.md)), on the ground that consistency or closure
alone does not measure a contribution. Flack's coarse-graining as downward
causation is cited for the thermostat example.

## Bearing on the record

- **[QUESTION-115](../questions.d/QUESTION-115.md).** The question lists effective information among its
  criteria. CE 2.0 is the same family with the size factor removed and a
  per-scale credit added, and it reports a profile along a path, which is
  what A12 asked of the joint search. It does not search over boundaries
  of a system (which variables are in it), only over partitions of one
  chain's states, so it bears on the macrostate leg of the question and
  not on the boundary leg.
- **[THEORY-222](../theory.d/THEORY-222.md).** Unaffected: CE 2.0 keeps uniform interventions at each
  scale and gives the same macroscale as CE 1.0 where the two overlap
  (§6.2). It adds a case CE 1.0 scores zero.
- **The candidate it was filed as.** The paper fits A10's description
  ("searching for coarse-grainings rather than assuming them") only in
  part: it searches along one path, chosen by maximum gain, and calls
  the full search future work.

No instruction for machine-learning practice; nothing for the anthology.
The closing paragraph's suggestion that CE 2.0 may serve interpretability
of deep networks is a remark, not a method.

## Limitations

- **Three toy chains.** No system above 8 states is analysed; no TPM
  entries are printed, so the examples cannot be re-run from the text.
- **One path.** §5 concedes that integrating "the full set of
  non-commensurate micro→macro paths" is open, and the answer depends on
  the path chosen.
- **"All macroscale causation"** (abstract, §6.2) rests on one
  manipulated family, not on an argument.
- **Errors and slips.** The degeneracy is printed as 1 − H(E | C)/log₂ n
  (§2.2), but the entropy then written out is that of P(e | C) summed over
  causes, i.e. H(E); with H(E | C) the formula would be the determinism
  again. §1 says macroscales are "reducible to their underlying
  macroscales" for microscales. The S1 text cites "Fig. 2D" for the
  100-state run that is Fig. S2D. Fig. S3's caption says "γ* is used
  instead of γ*". §3.2 calls CP both "sufficiency and necessity added
  together" and "determinism and specificity", which differ.
- The author's acknowledged funder (Softmax) is noted, nothing more.

## Open questions

- What does the apportioning give when the path is not chosen, and are
  the multi-path answers of [LIT-1123](../literature.d/LIT-1123.md) consistent with this paper's
  single-path ones on the same chains?
- Does any ∆CP profile recover a known boundary, not just a known
  lumping, in a system whose variables are split between agent and
  environment?

## Note of 2026-10-29: the criticism and the reply it cites, read

Eberhardt and Lee 2022, which Connections gives as cited [25] and not
registered, is now registered and read: [LIT-1839](../literature.d/LIT-1839.md), [NOTE-1413](NOTE-1413.md). So is
Comolatti and Hoel 2022, which §6.1 cites for gains surviving other
choices of P(C): [LIT-1843](../literature.d/LIT-1843.md), [NOTE-1416](NOTE-1416.md). Comolatti and Hoel concede
that EI under the stationary distribution at both levels shows no
emergence. The record's [THEORY-1167](../theory.d/THEORY-1167.md) shows why, in general: under any
one distribution over micro states at both levels, macro EI cannot exceed
micro EI, by data processing. That bound is for EI. This paper's CP is
normalized by the size of the state space, and the bound does not by
itself decide Fig. 4's case with observational P(C) at both scales, which
was read from its caption only. Whether that case's gain survives as a
gain of EI was not checked.
