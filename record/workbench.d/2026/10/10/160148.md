*Not a reconstruction of the exchange: a working session on the record's own
argument, in reply to the 34 objections filed earlier the same day from the
audit of the line (PR [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/121)).*

**What was asked.** The owner asked to "attempt to address/refute/resolve/reconcile
anticipated objections". Each objection was given one disposition: refuted,
reconciled, repaired or conceded. A planner read the 34 and every thesis they
target, then three writers filed the replies, re-deriving each mathematical
step. Every reply says whether it is decisive and checkable or a proposal.

**Conventions kept.** A counter that was right about its target as written went
Active, with a history note naming the repair ([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md): "a conceded one went
Active"; precedent [CLAIM-040](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-040.md)). Rejected was kept for the two objections found
mistaken. [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md) were not edited at all: their repairs are
filed beside them as proposals. Manuscript-quoted claims were changed only in
the record's own fields (a `defeated_if`, a relation or a note), and each
history note quotes the replaced wording. [CLAIM-236](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-236.md) keeps its role, `granted`, and
went Rejected at version 2: the concession was withdrawn.

**Refuted (2).**
- [CLAIM-236](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-236.md): [CLAIM-111](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-111.md)'s criterion compares each language's own listener posterior,
  so it needs no correspondence of alternative sets ([CLAIM-214](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-214.md), Active).
- [CLAIM-246](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-246.md): fidelity belongs to a transport and sharing a kind does not, so an
  independent utterance of the same kind has no fidelity to the source ([CLAIM-210](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-210.md),
  Active).

**Reconciled (7).**

  [CLAIM-244](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-244.md) stays open).
- Competent uptake as the standard of a communicative kind, which answers [CLAIM-206](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-206.md)'s
  dilemma and supplies the argument [CLAIM-247](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-247.md) found missing ([CLAIM-217](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-217.md); [TERM-040](../../record/terms.d/TERM-040.md) version 2).

  by the receiver, whether it can be shown to have survived by the analyst's
  probes. This gives the `complements` link between [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md) a
  content reason ([CLAIM-223](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-223.md), for [CLAIM-211](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-211.md)).
- Saussure as precedent at the level of use, not of the language system ([CLAIM-242](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-242.md),
  for [CLAIM-254](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-254.md)).
- Report against re-performance fidelity ([CLAIM-212](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-212.md), for [CLAIM-258](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-258.md)).
- Possible against usable information, with an XOR example of a garbling that
  helps a restricted receiver ([CLAIM-199](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-199.md), for [CLAIM-225](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-225.md)).

**Repaired (23).**

  built to separate wording, proposition, static stance and transport
  structure; rival predictors that include proposition with force, rated by
  people other than those who supply the criterion; a behavioural criterion
  tied to stated tasks; named receiver populations; an interlingual arm; margins
  fixed in a preregistration.
- Two revised theses that state their defeat conditions on that design: [CLAIM-220](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-220.md)
  beside [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-204](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-204.md) beside [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md). The line's distinctive contribution

  shown to be meetable by argument ([CLAIM-239](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-239.md)); measuring footing opened as a question
  ([QUESTION-076](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-076.md)).

  items, which excludes the constant kernel and ties the kernels to the text

  least as informative as the source ([CLAIM-259](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-259.md); the plan had the direction reversed,
  and the writer corrected it); order effects bounded on observed responses
  alone ([CLAIM-231](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-231.md), Active).
