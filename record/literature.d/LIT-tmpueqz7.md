# LIT-tmpueqz7: On the People's Terms: A Republican Theory and Model of Democracy

Philip Pettit (2012). *On the People's Terms: A Republican Theory and Model of Democracy*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. DOI-10.1017/CBO9781139017428.

## Key takeaways

*From the publisher's openings only (NOTE-tmpenh5u).*

- "It is one thing to argue that the social order imposed by a state is
  just, it is quite another to argue that the political imposition of that
  order is legitimate" (p. 130).
- "people will control the state just insofar as they have an
  individualized, unconditioned and efficacious influence that pushes it in
  a direction that they find acceptable" (p. 239).
- "unconditioned independence by the resistive character of the
  citizenry" (p. 239).
