# LEAD-170: Run repeated reconstruction chains, cross-modal (text, image, caption, image) against text-only of the same length, scored with an overlapping-context design at each stage

## What it would do

Fix [CLAIM-087](../claims.d/CLAIM-087.md)'s margin and rate, then run [CASE-009](../cases.d/CASE-009.md)'s chains under its four
conditions against text-only chains of equal length, scoring each stage
with overlapping contexts.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-004](../questions.d/QUESTION-004.md): "Repeated reconstruction chains"
- [QUESTION-014](../questions.d/QUESTION-014.md): "Independently sampled chains under stable and shifting
  frames, scored for footing by raters blind to condition"
- [CLAIM-087](../claims.d/CLAIM-087.md): "The defeat condition's margin and rate are still not fixed"

## What would count

Faster drift in cross-modal chains than in text-only ones beyond the fixed
margin supports [CLAIM-076](../claims.d/CLAIM-076.md) and [CLAIM-087](../claims.d/CLAIM-087.md); equal drift defeats them.
