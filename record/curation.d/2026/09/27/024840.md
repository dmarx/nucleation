The owner allowed a work to be held in both records ([ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md)). They asked for
Xiong's *The Lattice Representation Hypothesis of Large Language Models* to
be re-read here in light of van Rijsbergen's *The Geometry of Information
Retrieval* ([LIT-262](../../record/literature.d/LIT-262.md)). The anthology holds it as [ANTH-LIT-460](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-460.md).

**Reason for a second entry** ([ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) requires one). The anthology reads the
paper for concept geometry in LLMs. This record reads it as a piece of the
older mathematics of retrieval: formal concept analysis, Galois connections,
and the logic of lattices of classes. That is van Rijsbergen's second
chapter, and the anthology holds none of it.

The paper was read in full (arXiv v3, ICLR 2026, with appendices). It is
`Proposed` here with a `Read` note ([LIT-267](../../record/literature.d/LIT-267.md), [NOTE-240](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-240.md)).

## What the re-reading found

- **The construction is van Rijsbergen's, with one change.** The paper's
  concept lattice is the (tr, in) Galois connection his chapter 2 builds for
  inverted files; its closed pairs are his monothetic kinds. The change: the
  object–attribute incidence is a thresholded linear functional on an
  embedding, not a given index-term incidence.
- **Theorem 1 is formal concept analysis's standard fact.** Any incidence has
  a complete concept lattice. The paper itself says the geometry plays no
  further role once the incidence is fixed.
- **Distributivity is never discussed, and it fails.** The reader's own
  checks, marked as such in the note:
  - the object-level lattice can fail distributivity (a three-object
    diagonal context gives M₃), exactly as van Rijsbergen shows for kinds;
  - the region lattice is Boolean for linearly independent directions;
  - it is non-distributive for dependent ones.
- **No projectors, no trace-rule probability, no non-commuting operations.**
  So van Rijsbergen's quantum-probabilistic layer has no counterpart here,
  and contextuality has no purchase on the text. Two further observations,
  both the reader's own:
  - the paper's soft incidence is a sign-sensitive per-attribute marginal,
    not a probability measure on the lattice;
  - its inclusion score is not reflexive.
- **The evidence.** On five WordNet domains, linear probes recover an
  incidence generated by GPT-4o at F1 69.7–83.2. No lattice law is tested.

## The anthology's entry needs correcting

Per [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) this record reports these and does not edit the anthology. I
checked the first against the paper's text; the rest are the reader's
findings.

1. **The join.** [ANTH-LIT-460](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-460.md) says the join is "the conic hull of the two
   concepts' attribute directions". Definition 7 defines it as the set union
   R(Y_A) ∪ R(Y_B), which "can be approximated by the conic hull". A union of
   cones is generally not a region of the family, so it is not a lattice
   join. The experiments use neither: they use coordinatewise min and max of
   attribute profiles.
2. **The evidence.** The attribute schema and the "ground-truth" incidence
   were generated by GPT-4o (§4), not taken from WordNet, which supplies only
   the hierarchy.
3. **The complete lattice** proved is the finite object-level concept
   lattice, not a lattice of regions.
4. **The soft version** is thresholded to a crisp incidence before any
   lattice appears. No soft lattice is proved.
5. **The canonical-form condition** is that Dc = τ is solvable. Linear
   independence of the directions is sufficient, not the condition itself.
6. **The causal inner product** appears only in the preliminaries. The
   experiments use Fisher-LDA directions on raw hidden states.

Items 1, 3 and 4 bear on [ANTH-THEORY-034](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/theory.d/THEORY-034.md)'s account, which the anthology
should re-check.
