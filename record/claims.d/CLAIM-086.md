# CLAIM-086: A constraint may govern conduct without governing the conditions under which it remains authoritative

## The claim

"The point is stronger than the observation that rules can conflict ... one
governing order may possess authority over the domain, applicability, or
continued force of another."

Two arguments conclude it: [ARG-003](../arguments.d/ARG-003.md), an analogy from the Dual State, and
[ARG-001](../arguments.d/ARG-001.md), an existence proof from Northstar. The analogy is
undercut in part ([CLAIM-032](CLAIM-032.md)); the claim does not fall with it.
