# CLAIM-330: In the coupled meaning–influence model linearized at its symmetric state, with each field stable alone and nonnegative couplings, the coupling destabilizes symmetry exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ): only the loop can do it, and the direction that grows mixes meaning and authority

## The claim

The construction and its proof are [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md). In brief: with
α, β, γ, δ ≥ 0 and α, δ < 1, λ+ = ½[(α + δ) + √((α − δ)² + 4βγ)] > 1
exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ). When βγ = 0 the matrix is triangular
and its eigenvalues are α and δ. When β, γ > 0 the leading eigenvector
is (β, λ+ − α), with both components positive.

For [QUESTION-100](../questions.d/QUESTION-100.md) this says three things about the linearization of
[CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md). Capture, if it starts at symmetry, needs both arrows of
[CLAIM-297](CLAIM-297.md)'s circuit: authority shaping interpretation and interpretation
shaping endorsement. Scoring operators independently of the dominant
interpretation (Regime B, γ ≈ 0) removes the coupling-driven
instability, whatever the memory of endorsement. And the variable that
grows is neither a meaning nor an authority but a joint lean of both,
which is A14's "combined meaning–authority mode".

## What it does not say

- That the full model has this linearization: the signs and the size of
  γ in Regime A are assumptions about an unbuilt model.
- That the bounded nonlinear system has two captured attractors, or how
  far it hysteretically holds one.
- That capture costs fidelity, which is the rest of [CLAIM-297](CLAIM-297.md).
- That the mixed mode is an individual or a level. A14: "Feedback
  instability ⇏ Causal emergence ⇏ Historical individuality."
- That it is new mathematics. It is the standard instability condition for
  a nonnegative 2×2 linear map. Whether reflected-appraisal models
  already contain the circuit is [QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md).
