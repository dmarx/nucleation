# CLAIM-766: Equal sharing: joint motive, conspicuous content

## The claim

The owner, on 2026-11-20, in reply to the assistant's suggestion that
Schelling and Tomasello "may not be rivals at all":

> yeah that's where I'm at. the "we" motivates the need for a change, and
> the 50-50 split is the "conspicuous" solution. His notion of conspicuity
> is what I would normally describe using the language of "affordance"

Two things are needed to divide jointly earned spoils equally: a reason to
settle the division at all, and a division to settle on. On this claim the
joint commitment of [THEORY-495](../theory.d/THEORY-495.md) supplies the first and the conspicuousness
of the equal split, in Schelling's sense, supplies the second. Schelling
himself joins them: "the moral force of fairness is greatly reinforced by
the power of a" focal point (1957, pp. 211–212).

## What the readings show at age three

- **No mutual expectation.** Explicit first-order false belief arrives at
  about 4 ([THEORY-2112](../theory.d/THEORY-2112.md)), second-order belief one to three years later
  ([THEORY-2121](../theory.d/THEORY-2121.md)), and recursive mindreading is recruited for
  coordination at 6 ([THEORY-2113](../theory.d/THEORY-2113.md)). Tomasello himself holds that
  three-year-olds coordinate perspectives poorly ([THEORY-2108](../theory.d/THEORY-2108.md)). So at
  3 the equal split cannot be a focal point reached by "I expect you to
  expect".
- **No tacit coordination on salience.** In a coordination game without
  communication, only 5- and 8-year-olds chose the salient option above
  chance, not 3-year-olds ([LIT-2909](../literature.d/LIT-2909.md), abstract); tacit coordination
  appears from about 4–5 ([THEORY-2122](../theory.d/THEORY-2122.md)).
- **The norm is already known.** By 3 children "state explicitly that
  equality is a norm" but with windfalls "keep more for themselves"
  ([THEORY-2111](../theory.d/THEORY-2111.md)); after collaboration they split equally ([THEORY-495](../theory.d/THEORY-495.md),
  [NOTE-185](../notes.d/NOTE-185.md)).

So at age three the conspicuous solution is best read as a default the
child already holds, an affordance in the owner's plain sense, which the
"we" makes the child act on. Schelling-style salience reasoning, finding
the solution by meeting of minds, arrives at about five. Common ground
with a particular partner, built in joint attention, may carry the shared
character of the default without recursion (Moll and Kadipasaoglu, in the
readings of the same day).

## What would defeat it

If equal division after collaboration does not track the joint commitment,
the "we" is not what supplies the motive. If three-year-olds do not hold
the equal norm before collaborating, the content is not a known default.
The test is LEAD [LEAD-198](../leads.d/LEAD-198.md).
