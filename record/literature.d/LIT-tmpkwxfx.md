# LIT-tmpkwxfx: Children use salience to solve coordination problems

Sebastian Grueneisen, Emily Wyman and Michael Tomasello (2015). "Children use salience to solve coordination problems". Developmental Science 18(3), pp. 495–501 (abstract read at scholars.duke.edu/publication/1167966).

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-20 and read at the level of its abstract only. Its
finding, quoted from the abstract: "Children were significantly more
likely to choose the salient option in the experimental condition than in
the control condition. However, only the two older age groups chose the
salient box above chance levels." It is the record's evidence that
three-year-olds do not coordinate tacitly on salience.
