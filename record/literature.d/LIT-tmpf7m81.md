# LIT-tmpf7m81: Issues as Elements of Information Systems

Werner Kunz and Horst W. J. Rittel (1970), *Issues as Elements of Information Systems*, Working Paper No. 131, Institute of Urban and Regional Development, University of California, Berkeley — <https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5cj786v8>

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the abstract only: IBIS is meant to support the
coordination and planning of political decision processes. It guides the
identification, structuring and settling of issues raised by problem-solving
groups, and its elements are topics, issues, questions of fact, positions,
arguments and model problems. In 1970 three manually operated versions were
in experimental use by government agencies, and computerization was in
preparation. Whether the paper says anything about what keeping an IBIS
costs its participants is not known here.
