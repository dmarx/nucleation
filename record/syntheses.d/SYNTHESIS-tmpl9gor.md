# SYNTHESIS-tmpl9gor: The flourishing line holds that neither preference satisfaction, the outcome assistance delivers now, nor a capability set at one moment measures how the lives an arrangement leaves open go; that worth is judged fallibly, so institutions may cultivate it but not impose it; and that whether a standard joining capability, worth and non-domination can fail stays open

*An account of the line flourishing-and-meaning, "what makes a life go well for the one living it and what makes it meaningful" (its blurb, [ADR-099](../decisions.d/ADR-099.md)). It maps every in-force entry of the line and adds nothing ([ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)). Most of the line was filed from A's turns in the exchange "Analyze technological civilization trap" (2026-11-13), and where a component says "the wording is A's; adopting it is the owner's call", that holds here too.*

## The account

### The standard the line's claims take part in

[QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) (Open) asks whether A's standard, that an arrangement "succeeds insofar as it widens people's substantive freedom to pursue worthwhile lives without domination", can be stated so that some outcome would count against it, and how its parts are weighed when capability, worth and non-domination conflict. It records that the integration "was audited on 2026-11-13 and admitted by no route of [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)", since "its own pluralism absorbs every counterexample", and so is filed as a question, not a claim. It names its parts: Sen's substantive freedom ([THEORY-1852](../theory.d/THEORY-1852.md)), Wolf's worth ([THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md)) and non-domination as a threshold for dependence ([CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), on another line). Its "Bears on" section names [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md), [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md), [CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md), [CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md) and [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) as "the claims of this line that take part in it", and it calls [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) and [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)'s measure narrower sentences "that could fail". Its note of 2026-11-13 maps the exchange's recaps of the standard onto the record: [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) for substantive freedom, [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) for meaningful engagement, [CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md) for participation, [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) for pluralism and humility, [TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md) (with [CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md), off the line) for restraint, [CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md) for "sovereign time" and [CASE-263](../cases.d/CASE-263.md) for "regressive progress". "The question is unchanged."

[CASE-263](../cases.d/CASE-263.md) (Proposed; stipulated thought experiment, A's, A8 §XII) is the test [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) asks for on its first half: an innovation that raises output while raising insecurity and concentrating power, and a medical advance that adds dependence while greatly reducing suffering. A standard passes if it ranks the first below and the second above, "for reasons it states", which "separates the standard from growth and from anti-dependence". The case says what it does not test: how the standard weighs its own parts, since "in both innovations its parts agree", which is [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md)'s second half. It bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) and names [CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md) as the dashboard's "sovereign time".

Under that standard the line's components answer five sub-questions.

### 1. What capability measures

- **[CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md)** (Active; thesis, interpretive; the owner's connection at U11 of the exchange of 2026-10-17, worked out by A11; lines will-organization first). Sen's opportunity aspect is what viable reachability can represent "only through a map from trajectories to valued functionings, which control theory does not supply"; will-organization gives a causal account of part of the process aspect, "not of its fairness or legitimacy". Its version-5 history note brings it to this line as "a claim about what capability needs, the line's subject". Its note of 2026-11-13 says the meaning question after Wolf "is beyond this claim" and that [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) rests on it.
- **[CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)** (Active; thesis, demonstrated; A's, A17 §V and A23 §6; lines transient-support and will-organization before this one). Two supports giving the same capabilities now can leave different capabilities later, where capacity is learned by doing and decays with disuse, so support "has to be assessed by the capability trajectory it produces". It rests on [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) and [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) (off the line), and its derivation is run in [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s model (C = 0.156 against 0.7 at t = 5). Its body says the reachable set "itself moves under the support, so the opportunity aspect has to be read along the trajectory", reading [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md).
- **[ARG-105](../arguments.d/ARG-105.md)** (Proposed; form evidence; the filer's verdict). Gardner's objection that Sen's preference for capability over achievement is unargued ([THEORY-1850](../theory.d/THEORY-1850.md)) "qualifies the framing" of [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) and "leaves its derivation standing", since the two supports diverge in achieved functionings too. Its verdict: "capability trajectory" should be read as "the trajectory of what the party can reach and achieve", and [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)'s tie to Sen through [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) "carries Gardner's open question with it".
- **[CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md)** (Proposed; thesis, interpretive; A's, A8 §VI.C, A11 §18, A13 §VII.1). Time taken by activities a person "cannot reasonably refuse" limits what they can viably pursue apart from income and rights, so equal resources can leave unequal substantive freedom; "the contrast that matters is between time committed to demanding activity and time surrendered to necessity". It rests on [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md), applying its opportunity aspect to "a limit A adds and Sen's readings on the record do not name". It uses [CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md) in its example: what assistance can add "depends on the time they have to use it". It names [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md)'s line for "a right to meaningful opportunity, not a guarantee of meaningful employment". Its note of 2026-11-18 says what holds it back: no mechanism for the title's "or smaller". It bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md).

### 2. What satisfying preferences does not show

- **[CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md)** (Active; thesis, interpretive; A's, A9 §III and A10 §V; lines distributed-agency second). An arrangement can satisfy preferences more fully and raise reported well-being while narrowing engagement with what is worthwhile, so success at satisfying preferences "does not show that the lives an arrangement leaves open are going better". Grounded in [THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md) (Wolf). Its body says the step to arrangements is A's ([ARG-106](../arguments.d/ARG-106.md)) and that Marcuse ([THEORY-1853](../theory.d/THEORY-1853.md)) and Illich ([THEORY-1861](../theory.d/THEORY-1861.md)) corroborate "from other premises" ([ARG-102](../arguments.d/ARG-102.md)). It complements [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md); it bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) and [QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md). It names [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md) for whether a preference's origin matters and [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) for which activities are worthwhile and who may say.
- **[ARG-106](../arguments.d/ARG-106.md)** (Proposed; form source; the filer's verdict). Wolf's Tanner Lectures carry the premise of [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) (fulfilment and meaning come apart), [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) (no one in particular has authority over worth) and [CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md) (meaning pursued for its own sake defeats itself), "but not their application to arrangements and institutions, which Wolf disclaims and which is A's". Its note of 2026-11-15 says the scope gap for [CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md) is narrower since that claim was narrowed.
- **[ARG-102](../arguments.d/ARG-102.md)** (Proposed; form source; the filer's verdict). Marcuse's false needs and Illich's needs that productivity cannot satisfy corroborate [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md)'s "separation of satisfaction from how lives go, not a measure of worth", since Marcuse's criterion runs partly on origin, "which [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md) says cannot decide alone", and Illich's on a level of conviviality. Neither supplies [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md)'s standard of worth, "which rests on Wolf ([ARG-106](../arguments.d/ARG-106.md))".
- **[CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md)** (Active; thesis, interpretive; A's, A9 §III). Whether a desire was socially formed and whether its object is worth desiring are separate questions; "a critique of produced wants cannot rest on their social origin as such", and can ask whether satisfying them engages people with anything worthwhile. Grounded in [THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md). Its body presses Marcuse's criterion ([THEORY-1853](../theory.d/THEORY-1853.md)), noting that [ARG-108](../arguments.d/ARG-108.md) calls it contested and "this claim says where"; it sits at [ADR-099](../decisions.d/ADR-099.md)'s boundary with the authenticity line ([QUESTION-057](../questions.d/QUESTION-057.md), off the line); it names [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) for which objects are worthwhile. Its narrowing of 2026-11-18 put the wider clause in [CLAIM-753](../claims.d/CLAIM-753.md).
- **[CLAIM-753](../claims.d/CLAIM-753.md)** (Proposed; thesis, interpretive; the record's own extrapolation, filed under [ADR-047](../decisions.d/ADR-047.md) at [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md)'s narrowing). A critique of produced wants "cannot rest on how they were formed, by whatever process, without appeal to the worth of their objects or to their effects". It rests on [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md). What holds it back is its rival, self-determination theory's introjection ([THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md)), which is unanswered.
- **[QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md)** (Open; lines distributed-agency first). What would show that an experience is a basic psychological need, "something an organism's functioning requires, rather than something that reliably raises reported well-being", when self-determination theory interdefines need and well-being. Its history note brings it to this line as "a question of what makes a life go well". Its note of 2026-11-13 says [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) bears on it "from outside self-determination theory": if reported well-being can rise while a life goes worse, effects on reported well-being cannot alone identify what a life requires; "It does not answer the question".

### 3. What assistance leaves the person

- **[CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md)** (Active; thesis, interpretive; A's, A9 §IV and §XI). Assistance delivering the same outcome can leave different participation in worthwhile activity, liberating, enabling or displacing, so it "is assessed by the participation it enables or displaces as well as by its outcomes". It complements [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md): "this one needs no dynamics", comparing now "by participation rather than capability" (its body; [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)'s note of 2026-11-13 says the same from the other side). Vallor ([THEORY-1847](../theory.d/THEORY-1847.md), [THEORY-1856](../theory.d/THEORY-1856.md)) corroborates. It names [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) for which participation people have reason to value, and [CLAIM-603](../claims.d/CLAIM-603.md) (off the line) as the liberating case. It bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md).
- **[TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md)** (Proposed; A's, A13 §X). A *value-constituting limit*: a limit on what an agent can control that is part of what makes its relation to something worth having, set apart from limits that produce deprivation (to be overcome) and domination (to be contested). It draws on [THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md) and [THEORY-1866](../theory.d/THEORY-1866.md) (Rosa through Susen). Its "How it is used" section says [CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md)'s displacing assistance is assistance that removes a value-constituting limit, and its last clause names [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) for who may judge which limits are of which kind.

### 4. Who may judge worth, and what institutions may do with it

- **[CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md)** (Proposed; thesis, interpretive; A's, A9 §VII–§VIII and A10 §III, §IX). That some ways of living are more worthwhile, judged fallibly, "licenses institutions to cultivate the conditions in which people can engage with what is worthwhile, not to impose a conception of it". Grounded in [THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md) for the fallibilism; the political boundary is A's ([ARG-106](../arguments.d/ARG-106.md)). It names [CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md) for what goes wrong when an institution measures meaning. Its note of 2026-11-17 makes it an instance of [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md) (off the line), and its note of 2026-11-18 says it can rest on that claim once [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md) is held. It bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md).
- **[CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md)** (Proposed, version 2, narrowed at the owner's request; thesis, interpretive; A's, A9 §XII). When an institution makes measured meaningfulness "a target that shapes participants' incentives or self-understanding", it can induce proxy optimization ([TERM-108](../terms.d/TERM-108.md)'s form) or self-conscious meaning-seeking (Wolf's paradox, [THEORY-1836](../theory.d/THEORY-1836.md)) that displaces engagement. [ARG-106](../arguments.d/ARG-106.md) argues how far Wolf carries it. Its body names protecting time, access and education as "the cultivation [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) allows". It bears on [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md).

### 5. What an arrangement leaves open

- **[CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)** (Proposed; thesis, interpretive; A's, A3, A8, A11 §4, A13 §II; lines distributed-agency first). A technology can become practically compulsory without any rule requiring it, so "the freedom an arrangement leaves is measured by what remains open to a non-adopter, not by what it lets adopters do". Grounded in [THEORY-863](../theory.d/THEORY-863.md). Its history note brings it to this line for its measure; "its mechanisms stay with distributed-agency". Its note on this line says [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) "complements the measure from the other side", and that Marcuse's objection ([ARG-108](../arguments.d/ARG-108.md)) "now has a claim on this line to press against: [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md)". Its notes record two qualifications of "without any rule requiring it", from Cayley ([THEORY-1862](../theory.d/THEORY-1862.md)) and Energy and Equity ([THEORY-1863](../theory.d/THEORY-1863.md), [ARG-107](../arguments.d/ARG-107.md)), with the title kept.
- **[ARG-108](../arguments.d/ARG-108.md)** (Proposed; form source; lines distributed-agency first; the filer's verdict). One-Dimensional Man corroborates [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)'s mechanism and "argues against measuring freedom by the options left open, since what can be chosen and what is chosen are shaped as well", an argument of the kind the claim's second defeater describes, without meeting it. Whether the measure should narrow "to a necessary condition of freedom, is the owner's call".
- **[QUESTION-462](../questions.d/QUESTION-462.md)** (Open; lines transient-support second). Whether arrangements can make capability gains self-reinforcing, "the dependency ratchet runs the other way", past what threshold and on what supply. Its summary sets the loop as "the reverse of the ratchet by which adoption narrows alternatives ([CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md))"; it bears on [CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md) ("time, A's third link") and on [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) (off the line), and it brings [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md), [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) and [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) (off the line) to what an answer needs.

## Not stated on the record

- **That the standard follows from the line's claims, or is their conjunction.** [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) says the integration was admitted by no route; no entry derives it. The claims that bear on it are narrower sentences, by [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md)'s own account, not its parts proved.
- **A non-domination component on this line.** [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) names non-domination as one of the standard's three parts, but the entry it cites, [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), is on dependence-and-domination, as are [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) and [CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md), which its note maps. No in-force entry of this line states what non-domination contributes to how a life goes.
- **A rule for weighing the parts when they conflict.** [QUESTION-465](../questions.d/QUESTION-465.md) names Sen's partial ordering ([THEORY-411](../theory.d/THEORY-411.md)) as a candidate and the dashboard as declining to answer; [CASE-263](../cases.d/CASE-263.md) says it does not test this. No entry states such a rule.
- **That [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)'s measure is [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md)'s opportunity aspect.** The options left to a non-adopter look like a reachable set, but no entry identifies the two, or says whether the map to valued functionings applies to the non-adopter's options.
- **How [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md)'s measure ranks [CASE-263](../cases.d/CASE-263.md)'s pair.** The case's second innovation adds dependence; no entry says whether it narrows what is left to a non-adopter, or how the measure ranks either innovation.
- **That [CLAIM-683](../claims.d/CLAIM-683.md)'s measured meaning and [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md)'s satisfied preferences are the same failure.** Both separate a measured or reported quantity from engagement with worth, but no entry joins them.
- **That time under necessity ([CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md)) bears on the capability trajectory ([CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)).** [CLAIM-681](../claims.d/CLAIM-681.md) joins time to assistance through [CLAIM-676](../claims.d/CLAIM-676.md), not to the trajectory; no entry says time is a condition of capacity kept by doing.
- **That [CLAIM-689](../claims.d/CLAIM-689.md)'s separation of origin from worth bears on [QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md).** Both separate what raises reported well-being from what a life needs or is worth, but neither names the other; [QUESTION-369](../questions.d/QUESTION-369.md)'s tie to the line is [CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md) alone.
- **That the self-reinforcing loop of [QUESTION-462](../questions.d/QUESTION-462.md) would be assessed by [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)'s trajectory.** [QUESTION-462](../questions.d/QUESTION-462.md) cites [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) and [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) on the transient-support line, not [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md).
- **That [TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md)'s value-constituting limits are what [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) forbids institutions to remove or impose.** [TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md) names [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) only for who judges which limit is which; no entry says how cultivation and imposition apply to limits.
