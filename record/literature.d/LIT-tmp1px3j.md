# LIT-tmp1px3j: Blameworthy but Unblamable: A Paradox of Corporate Responsibility

Shoemaker, D. (2019). Blameworthy but Unblamable: A Paradox of Corporate Responsibility. *The Georgetown Journal of Law & Public Policy* 17: 897–.

## Key takeaways

- Three types (p. 913): "Attributability, answerability, and
  accountability are the labels for three distinct types of responsibility
  (drawn from labels already in the literature)", "radically independent"
  (n. 70).
- Answerability is a capacity (p. 912): "To have these capacities is to be
  answerable, to be able in principle to answer the question, “Why did you
  do that?”"
- Anger belongs to accountability (p. 913): "gratitude and anger aptly
  respond to empathic and emotional (dis)regard."
- Other pluralists, Watson's 2004 essays among them, lack the third type
  (n. 64): "none of them have an independent notion of answerability."

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-18 as the read neighbour of Shoemaker (2011),
