# LEAD-195: Rule on whether a symmetry or conservation law, or a possibility fact in Deutsch and Marletto's sense, counts as realizing a constraint

## What it would do

Argue the case against [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md)'s definition.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md): "Defeater (b)'s candidate is still unruled: whether a
  symmetry or conservation law"

## What would count

Either ruling settles defeater (b).

## Note of 2026-11-22: the ruling, filed as alternatives

**Not already held.** [QUESTION-450](../questions.d/QUESTION-450.md) (2026-11-11) states the question and
lists three answers with no argument for any of them. [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md)'s note of
2026-11-02 rules on exact conservation laws for its own defeater (a) only
("the limit of slowness"). [LEAD-094](LEAD-094.md)'s [CLAIM-1058](../claims.d/CLAIM-1058.md), [CLAIM-1088](../claims.d/CLAIM-1088.md) and
[ARG-220](../arguments.d/ARG-220.md) concern that defeater, not [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md)'s (b). The rulings here
build on both and do not repeat them.

**Filed**, as two pairs of alternatives, each answering [QUESTION-450](../questions.d/QUESTION-450.md):

- Symmetries and conservation laws: [CLAIM-1094](../claims.d/CLAIM-1094.md) (thesis, leaned to).
  An effective law meets [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md)'s definition, realized and maintained by
  the organization below it ([THEORY-158](../theory.d/THEORY-158.md)'s transition graph is the worked
  case). A fundamental one is maintained by nothing. But defeater (b) asks
  for a result, and no result shows a law fundamental, so the candidate
  becomes a condition on the claim's scope, not a defeat. [CLAIM-1105](../claims.d/CLAIM-1105.md)
  (counter): the symmetries of current fundamental theory meet (b) now for
  elementary particle kinds, and [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) holds only above them.
- Possibility facts: [CLAIM-1161](../claims.d/CLAIM-1161.md) (thesis, leaned to). A possibility fact
  is a fact about the laws and is ruled with them. Its definition's
  retention clause is the frame's "realized and maintained" in modal form.
  [CLAIM-1037](../claims.d/CLAIM-1037.md) (counter): if constructor theory's programme holds, the
  laws are possibility facts, modal maintenance is not maintenance, and (b)
  is met for information media.

The pairs can be combined either way. A Proposed Reframing of [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md)'s
defeater (b) and "What it does not say" is in that claim's note of today,
not applied.

**What remains** is the owner's: which reading of (b) the claim intends,
whether a result or the best current theory, and whether an actual
maintainer or one the laws admit. Then whether to adopt the Reframing.
Status and stage unchanged.
