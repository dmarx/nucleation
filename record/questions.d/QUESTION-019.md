# QUESTION-019: What is it like to be Microsoft?

## Why it was a question

The owner proposed it at U9 as the thesis frame, after the assistant's first
proposals treated distributed agency as three rival pictures of the whole.
It held the work together through the organizational-affect line: corporate
"sensory organs", immune-like incident response, compartmentalized access.

## Why it was reframed

By the manuscript outline of 2026-10-04 evening the question had split. What a
collective *is* became a supposition rather than a result ([CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md)),
and what the paper could answer was narrower: given an agent, what organizes
which of its regimes acts ([QUESTION-015](QUESTION-015.md)). The original question is kept here
because [CASE-014](../cases.d/CASE-014.md) was committed to it, and that commitment outlived it.
