# LIT-2559: The Idea of Justice

John Gardner (2012). "The Idea of Justice" (critical notice of Amartya Sen, *The Idea of Justice*). SSRN, DOI-10.2139/ssrn.2081682. Read from the author's own site.

## Key takeaways

- The differences between Sen and Rawls "seem more temperamental than strictly philosophical" (p. 5).
- On Sen's capability-over-achievement passage (IoJ p. 238): "But where is the argument?" (p. 8).
