Earmarked for the retro by the owner, on pull request 570, 2026-11-21. The
draft of the dialectic regime (ADR-tmpzgon7) listed what it files without
asking as "claims, questions, terms, arguments, cases and leads". The owner:
"this class of items should probably have a referent so we can extend it
later and implicitly be included in the ADR. PS: this feels like a good
example of noticing a distinction."

**What went right.** A list in a rule is a closed set; a named class is
open. The list would have left a scheme added later outside the regime until
someone noticed and amended the decision. The revision names the class, the
argument schemes of ADR-032 and those that have joined them, so it extends
as the record does.

**Why it may generalize.** It is the same move as the descriptor and
imposition distinction filed the same night: a rule stated by enumeration
describes the present set, while a rule stated by its referent holds as the
set grows. Worth a look, at the retro, at other decisions that enumerate
schemes or fields where they mean a class.

**What is still missing.** The referent is prose. `luria.yaml` has no field
marking a scheme as part of the record's own argument, so nothing checks
membership. A field would be the next rung, and a change to `luria.yaml`, so
the owner's to make.
