# LEAD-tmp0n5t8: Run the discriminating fidelity study: renderings built to dissociate wording, proposition, static stance and transport structure, judged by named receivers, against a proposition-plus-force rival and a text-similarity baseline

## What it would do

Run CASE-055, which absorbs and repairs CASE-184, once its margins and the
footing measure are fixed. Include CASE-065's order-effect items as an arm
and the flattening arm CLAIM-637 names.

Where the record names it:

- CLAIM-228: "What holds it is a missing result: CASE-055 has not been
  run"
- CLAIM-220: "a study designed and not run"
- ARG-017: "It waits on a CASE-055 result."
- QUESTION-002: "What would count as an answer here is a specification of
  the preserved object that can be measured on real translations, retellings
  and cross-modal renderings, and that predicts human judgements of
  communicative fidelity better than semantic similarity does."
- CLAIM-637: "a collapsing or flattening arm in that study"

## What would count

For the theses: transport measures predicting receivers' recovery beyond
the propositional and static-profile rivals by more than the fixed margin.
Against them: no gain inside the margin, which meets CLAIM-202's and
CLAIM-228's defeat conditions and answers QUESTION-002 in the negative for
the populations tested.
