# ARG-tmpd3vkm: Gallo, Lynch and Tomy's finding that a monitor's effect on violations ends with the agreement, as evidence of the reversion a corporation changed only by sanction is predicted to show, supports the reading that such a corporation has fear without remorse

## The inference

1. [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md), part 5: "a corporation changed only by sanction reverts when
   the sanction lifts, and one whose concerns were redesigned does not."
2. [THEORY-tmpvtotj](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpvtotj.md): the fall in penalised violations a monitor brings ends
   with the agreement.
3. So the prediction is confirmed for a monitor's share of sanction, and
   [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) gains support.

## Critical questions

- **Is the evidence the predicted kind?** Partly. Part 5 is about
  harm-weighing; the study counts penalised violations. Fewer penalties
  under watch and more after is what a corporation weighing only the
  threat would show, but also what one whose harm-weighing is unchanged
  would show if enforcement attention moved; the study's EPA test speaks
  against the second for one agency.
- **Does a rival predict it as well?** Yes. The prudence objection
  ([CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)) predicts that a corporation whose harm reaches it only as
  threat reverts when the threat lifts, which is the same observation
  ([CLAIM-tmpsuj5n](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsuj5n.md), first row). Evidence both predict equally does not
  favour either.
- **Are the comparison arms the claim's arms?** No. Both are sanctioned;
  neither is coded as redesigned. The half of part 5 that separates the
  claims is not observed.
- **Is reputation or benefit held fixed?** No.

## Verdict

The study confirms what [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) predicts of a sanction-only
corporation, and supports [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) only against a reading on which
sanction leaves lasting change, which neither the record nor [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)
holds. Against [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md), its live objection, it is no support. The run
leans to Rejected; the status is left Proposed for the owner.
