# ADR-073: Two new lines of inquiry, readiness and realizability, for the argument filed from the readiness, constraint-architecture and realizability syntheses, so that each can be set aside as a whole

## Context

A line exists "so that a line can be set aside as a whole and what depends
on it can be found" (ADR-032). The lines vocabulary is closed and grows by
decision; formal-apparatus came in by ADR-040.

Three filings in dmarx/nucleation#401 added the record's own argument on
two subjects the existing lines do not name:

- **The emergency-readiness bibliography** and A's additions: provisional
  dispatch, readiness as response capability, slack, ratchets, coverage,
  skill decay.
- **The constraint-architecture synthesis:** the transition threshold,
  reconfiguration slack, obstructions to a transition, the three resource
  requirements of a form, budget rules that pin an organization.
- **The resource-bounded realizability syntheses:** compatibility against
  reachability, budgeted composition, finding against navigating, Yoneda
  profiles under bounds, isomorphism against persistence, origami as the
  laboratory.

Their 89 claims, questions, cases, terms and one argument were filed under
distributed-agency ("what an organized whole is, and how its operative
direction relates to its members'") or will-organization ("what organizes
practical efficacy in an agent") as the nearest available, with
formal-apparatus on the general results. Neither substantive line describes
most of them, and setting distributed-agency aside would now retire both
arguments with it.

## Decision

- **Add two lines to `luria.yaml`:**
  - `readiness`, "Readiness and slack": what it takes to keep, reach and
    pay for capacities whose value lies in rare circumstances;
  - `realizability`, "Resource-bounded realizability and individuation":
    what a constrained system can realize, reach, tell apart and become
    under computational and physical bounds.
- **Re-tag the 89 entries:**
  - the new lines replace distributed-agency and will-organization;
  - distributed-agency stays on four entries that are about how an
    organized whole forms or governs itself: the nucleus question, who
    supplies a bottleneck resource, assembling against persisting against
    regenerating, and programmable against self-governing reconfiguration;
  - formal-apparatus stays on the general results, so setting either new
    line aside does not retire them;
  - an entry that bears on both, such as the transition threshold
    (CLAIM-tmpbi4ca), the three requirements of a form (CLAIM-tmpancvu) and
    the degree-4 vertex (CASE-tmpe48yq), carries both.
- **Held entries keep their lines.** The dated notes that tie them to these
  arguments are enough; a held entry joins a new line only when it is next
  revised for that argument.

After the re-tag the 89 entries carry realizability 61 times, readiness
38, formal-apparatus 29 and distributed-agency 4.

## Alternatives

- **One combined line** ("resource-bounded capability"). Rejected: the two
  arguments can stand or fall separately. The readiness argument is about
  organizations and budgets; the realizability argument is mathematical and
  about individuation. One line would make setting either aside take the
  other.
- **A line for individuation.** Rejected: individuation runs through
  distributed-agency already. What is new is its dependence on resource
  bounds, which belongs to realizability.
- **Status quo.** Leaves 89 entries on lines that do not describe them, and
  ties the retraction of distributed-agency to two unrelated arguments.

## Consequences

- **Setting a line aside.** Either new line can be set aside by a status
  change on its members. Their formal results survive under
  formal-apparatus, and the four organizational entries under
  distributed-agency.
- **Filing.** New filings on these subjects take the new lines. The
  `read-and-file` skill needs no change: a QUESTION already needs a
  `line:`.
- **Generated views.** `docs/record.md` and the argument pages are
  generated on the default branch and will list the new lines after merge.
- **Numbering.** The ADR is numbered 73 because 72 is taken in
  dmarx/nucleation#402, which is not yet merged.
