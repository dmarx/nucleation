*The owner supplied a package of proposed record updates, which it calls
"Priority A": four new cases numbered 161 to 164, sixteen notes to append
to existing entries, a pull-request body and a script that would apply
them. It described the Perspective Population Lab from v1.1 to v2.2 and
cited a zip archive for provenance. The lab's code is now in
dmarx/PerspectiveLab: v1.0 at @41e56df, v1.1–v1.8 at @7e2edc2, v1.9–v2.2
at @9f82bfd, and a comparator pilot ("Priority B") at @dce302b. The
package was treated as a proposal. Its script was not run, and its fixed
numbers were not used: CASE uses merge allocation, so the cases carry tmp
codes.*

**How it was checked.** Every number kept was read against the lab's
stored results (results/*.json and *.csv) and reports, and the lab's own
study entries were read for how each run maps onto this record. The test
suites of five experiments were re-run at dmarx/PerspectiveLab@11c1495
and passed (regime atlas, stochastic population, maintained population,
finite organization, joint parameter identification). The runs themselves
were not regenerated.

**The cases.** All four are Proposed and stipulated.

- [CASE-tmpr1vuv](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpr1vuv.md) (the package's 161) covers v1.1–v1.3. Its numbers agree.
  One description was wrong: v1.1's comparison is a 2 × 2 factorial of
  four cells, not four arms. The package filed it as a construction; it
  is filed as an executed study, since its facts are seeded runs, not its
  definition. It adds what the package left out and the record needs:
  v1.1 reproduces [CASE-158](../../record/cases.d/CASE-158.md)'s table and gives the router the same audit
  delay and cost, which answers a caveat [CASE-158](../../record/cases.d/CASE-158.md) records.
- [CASE-tmpwxgtj](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpwxgtj.md) (162) covers v1.8 and v1.9. One reading was corrected. The
  package said the refinement witness shows that "refining the evolving
  macro-target need not improve informational closure". The witness, a
  defect of 0, 0.380 and 0 under refinement, is in the global
  strong-lumpability defect, not in informational closure. The lab's own
  study entry says the same. So the case illustrates only [CLAIM-409](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-409.md)'s
  monotone half and says that its other pattern is in another sense. The
  package gave two lines and the scheme takes one; formal-apparatus was
  kept.
- [CASE-tmpfmdj6](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpfmdj6.md) (163) covers v1.6–v1.7. Its numbers agree. The
  wrong-incumbent counts, the 80-update outages and the 33 of 48 runs in
  which rules were lost and authority was right were added from the v1.6
  report. It is filed as an executed study.
- [CASE-tmpw6jjd](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpw6jjd.md) (164) covers v2.0–v2.2. Its numbers agree. Two descriptions
  were made exact: v2.2's audit probe lowers audit integrity to 0.46 and
  is not complete, and its scores are one three-probe policy's among
  eight. The nominal parameters are not all grid points, which the package
  did not say and which bears on why joint fitting hurts at them.

**The notes.** Fifteen of sixteen were applied, each rewritten in the
record's convention: a version bump, a history item, and a section dated
2026-10-26. Every one was cut back to what the stored results show. The
package's closing advice ("retain Proposed", "keep Open", "no status
change recommended") was dropped, since a note changes no status and says
so. Several gained what the package lacked. [CLAIM-336](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-336.md)'s note says that
the lab its note of 2026-10-25 called "none of it held" is now held.
[CLAIM-278](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-278.md)'s, [CLAIM-427](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-427.md)'s and [QUESTION-117](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-117.md)'s cite the comparator pilot,
whose same-kernel tie is the sharpest form of their point. [QUESTION-086](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-086.md)'s
says that [CASE-126](../../record/cases.d/CASE-126.md) and [CLAIM-414](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) were an uncited appearance, and leaves
for the owner whether [CLAIM-414](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) answers a finite-model child under
[ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md). Notes the package attributed to the wrong case were
re-attributed. [QUESTION-222](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-222.md)'s v1.5 fixture is not in any case, and the
router comparison was dropped from [CLAIM-297](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)'s note: the router is not
the matched adaptive-retrieval process that claim's defeater names.

**Declined.** The note on [CLAIM-443](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-443.md). It said that the lab's authority
histories are not a reward-trained Hebbian prior and record no
amortization budget. That is true, but nothing claims otherwise. The lab
neither implements nor cites the claim, so the note would record only an
absence.

**Picked up.** [QUESTION-031](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-031.md)'s history item for its split was dated
2026-10-21, and the split was made on 2026-10-25.
