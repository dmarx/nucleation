**What started it.** After round 3, 102 Active or Proposed THEORYs still had
no edge to any claim or question. The assistant said that placing them would
take new questions, not edges, and gave five examples. The owner asked for
questions for all of them.

**Two new lines ([ADR-039](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-039.md)).** The assistant at first treated the closed
`lines` vocabulary as a reason not to add lines. The owner pointed out that
closedness only asks for a blurb. Two clusters fit no line without strain:
consciousness science on what could show experience, and learning dynamics
on why trained networks have the structure they do. The owner approved both:
`evidence-for-experience` and `learned-structure`. The second stays on
understanding, because instruction for practice belongs to the anthology.

**How.** Four agents each drafted questions for one topic batch, under a
brief: as few questions as honestly cover the batch; each on one line; each
bearing on something already filed; transfer only with a named mechanism;
and a THEORY that fits no question listed rather than forced. Every quoted
phrase in the drafts (113) was checked mechanically against the THEORY it
is attributed to. Four did not match. Two were formatting only. Two were
corrected: a paraphrase in quotation marks, and a phrase of the question's
own attributed to a THEORY. Codes were removed from four titles.
