# LIT-259: Noncommutative analysis, Multivariable spectral theory for operators in Hilbert space, Probability, and Unitary Representations

Palle Jorgensen, Feng Tian (2014), *arXiv preprint (a 442-page lecture-notes monograph, v8 2015-08-23). A book by the same authors titled "Non-commutative Analysis" was published by World Scientific (Crossref: DOI 10.1142/10317, dated 2016-09-27); that it is this text is likely but unverified.* — [ARXIV-1408.1164](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1164)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-829](../notes.d/NOTE-829.md), which covers the cited passages only.*

- **The kernel construction, named GNS.** Remark 1.34 builds a Hilbert
  space from a positive definite ϕ on a set: form Σc̄_xd_yϕ(x,y) on finite
  sums, quotient the null vectors, complete. It calls this "the GNS
  construction". Chapter 4's GNS applies the same recipe to ϕ(a*b) on a
  *-algebra. The book does not state a reduction of one to the other.
- **Uniqueness, stated not proved.** Corollary 1.35: ϕ is positive
  definite iff ϕ(x,y) = ⟨Φ(x), Φ(y)⟩ for some Φ: X → H, and two minimal
  realizations are related by a unitary. The proof is "left as an
  exercise to the student". Equation (1.24) is printed "UΦ₁(x) = Φ₂(x)U",
  a typo for UΦ₁(x) = Φ₂(x).
- **Uniqueness, proved, for GNS.** Theorem 4.35: for a state, the cyclic
  representation is unique up to unitary equivalence, by the isometry
  π₁(A)u₁ ↦ π₂(A)u₂ extended by density. The same argument proves
  Corollary 1.35's uniqueness with Φ_i(x) in place of π_i(A)u_i.
- **Commutative GNS is L²(μ).** Example 4.28: in the abelian case states
  are Borel probability measures. Example 4.86: the GNS space of
  f ↦ ∫f dμ on C(X) is L²(μ), with multiplication operators and cyclic
  vector 1. Lemma 3.28 does the same inside the proof of the spectral
  theorem. Both are for compact X.
- **RKHS by Riesz.** Def. 11.1: bounded evaluation gives K_s "by Riesz";
  Claim 11.2, proved, K_s = E_s*(1).
