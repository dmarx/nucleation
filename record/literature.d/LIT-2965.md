## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A invokes it without naming it at A23 for causal decomposition applied to energy transfer between scales in turbulence. It bears on the exchange's account of causal structure across scales in a turbulent cascade. Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-2275](../notes.d/NOTE-2275.md)) and filed as [THEORY-2187](../theory.d/THEORY-2187.md). It keeps transfer and dependence apart as objects and fits [CLAIM-810](../claims.d/CLAIM-810.md)'s turbulence row without testing its separations ([ARG-187](../arguments.d/ARG-187.md), Rejected as that route), and its "causality" is predictive information, an instance of [CLAIM-335](../claims.d/CLAIM-335.md) ([ARG-185](../arguments.d/ARG-185.md)).
