# LIT-1992: Decision-making through irruption theory: how to use and interpret the Haken-Kelso-Bunz model

Sánchez-Cañizares, J. (2026). Decision-making through irruption theory: how to use and interpret the Haken-Kelso-Bunz model. *Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences*. doi:10.1007/s11097-026-10164-0.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-1506](../notes.d/NOTE-1506.md)).

- The time-switched HKB model and its rise-and-fall of variability,
  checked by re-simulation; under the stated noise q = 1 the spread
  settles near 0.40, not 0 as the paper says ([THEORY-1301](../theory.d/THEORY-1301.md)).
- IAT*: the mind controls which outcomes exist (the landscape's
  parameters), not which the state reaches ([THEORY-1302](../theory.d/THEORY-1302.md)).
- No chaos and no Kane: the model is a one-dimensional gradient flow.
