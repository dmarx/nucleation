  Chen et al. (2015), Motiv. Emot. 39, online 12 November 2014. Two
  cross-sectional studies, Belgium and China (N = 683) and Belgium, China,
  the USA and Peru (N = 1,051). They build the 24-item Basic Psychological
  Need Satisfaction and Frustration Scale: six factors fit better than three
  and are metric-invariant across the four countries. Satisfaction relates
  mainly to life satisfaction and vitality, frustration mainly to depressive
  symptoms, with no detectable country differences and no moderation by need
  valuation or need desire. All frustration items are negatively worded and
  all satisfaction items positively.
