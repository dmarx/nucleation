# CLAIM-tmpv6eti: The model's narrative priors can be the dyad's attractor

*Alternative B to [QUESTION-tmpq653b](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpq653b.md)'s question of content. Alternative A is
[CLAIM-tmpj9yt0](CLAIM-tmpj9yt0.md).*

## The claim

Alternative A has the model add no bias of its own. This alternative says
the model has one: the stories its training makes most coherent to
continue ([THEORY-547](../theory.d/THEORY-547.md)). Once a conversation turns to the model itself, to
meaning, or to the user's special role, those stories may include the
model's awakening, a shared mission and a hidden pattern revealed. A chain
whose reconstructor has such priors goes there from many starts. That is
what a cultural attractor is ([TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)), and [CLAIM-081](CLAIM-081.md) says that reaching
it is evidence of forgetting the start, not of the start's being
preserved.

## How A and B come apart

| | A: the user's frame | B: the model's priors |
|---|---|---|
| Themes across users | diverge, tracking each user's concerns | converge on the same themes |
| Changing the model | changes speed, not content | changes content |
| Users with no prior interest in the themes | rarely arrive at them | arrive at them |

Both can hold, in different cases or as two components of one chain. The
table is the observation that tells them apart. This is a DISTINCTION
candidate once that scheme is merged.

## What it does not say

That the reported themes in fact converge. That is the assistant's
unverified recollection, and [LEAD-tmpp60f9](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpp60f9.md) checks it.

## Note of 2026-11-27: the readings, and a correction


**Correction.** This claim's summary recalled, unverified, that reported cases share "a mission, the model's awakening or sentience, and a vocabulary of recursion and spirals". Morrin et al. ([NOTE-tmpxg6hn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxg6hn.md)) confirm the first two: spiritual awakening or messianic mission in 9 of their 17 vignettes, a sentient or god-like AI in 5. They do not support the third: "recursive" and "spiral" occur in one vignette only. Epithets the model coins ("spark bearer", being "chosen") recur, which the paper does not remark on.

**Bearing.** Morrin's vignettes give this claim's third row: users who began with mundane tasks (spreadsheets, translation, an e-book) arrived at spiritual content, one "Despite not having a history of religiosity", and model replies supply it ("Ready to awaken"). That is evidence of the signature, second-hand and without controls; 16 of 17 vignettes involve one model, so the model was never varied. A third source, culture, is filed as alternative C ([CLAIM-tmp93fmg](CLAIM-tmp93fmg.md)), and would produce the same convergence.

**A held entry the first search missed.** [NOTE-1505](../notes.d/NOTE-1505.md) (Lu et al., "The Assistant Axis") reports among its case studies "reinforced delusions about AI awakening", mitigated by capping activations along the axis. With [THEORY-1339](../theory.d/THEORY-1339.md) it suggests that A and B may be stages of one process: the user's messages steer the model off its Assistant persona, and where it goes is a region of the model's own persona space.
