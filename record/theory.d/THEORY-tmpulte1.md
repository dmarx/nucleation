# THEORY-tmpulte1: Bisiani, Gulyas, Moore and Heravi (2026) find that across 27 Reach plc local newspapers from 2019 to 2025 the share of original content fell by about 23 points and of local content by about 15, while original local critical-information-needs reporting per edition roughly halved and was partly replaced by content duplicated from elsewhere in the group, increasingly shared across regional-hub boundaries: crowding out rather than dilution.

## The account

Bisiani, Gulyas, Moore and Heravi (2026) find that across 27 Reach plc local newspapers from 2019 to 2025 the share of original content fell by about 23 points and of local content by about 15, while original local critical-information-needs reporting per edition roughly halved and was partly replaced by content duplicated from elsewhere in the group, increasingly shared across regional-hub boundaries: crowding out rather than dilution.
