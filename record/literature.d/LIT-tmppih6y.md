# LIT-tmppih6y: The Weirdness of the World

Eric Schwitzgebel (2024), *Princeton University Press*, 376 pp., ISBN 9780691215679 (hardcover), 9780691239309 (e-book) — DOI-10.1515/9780691239309

## Key takeaways

- Every viable theory of how consciousness fits into the cosmos, and of the cosmos's basic structure, is both bizarre (contrary to common sense) and dubious (not rationally compelling), so something that seems almost too crazy to believe must be true.
