# THEORY-tmptlzgt: Buchnera's metabolism interlocks with its aphid host's: it makes the essential amino acids its host cannot from host-supplied glutamate and aspartate, depends on the host for the non-essential amino acids and its membrane phospholipids, and cannot live outside the bacteriocyte

## Source

Shigenobu, Watanabe, Hattori, Sakaki & Ishikawa (2000), [LIT-1596](../literature.d/LIT-1596.md), read in full in [NOTE-1236](../notes.d/NOTE-1236.md). The authors infer from gene content, and from earlier physiology, that the partners' metabolisms interlock and that Buchnera presumably lives only inside the bacteriocyte.
