## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-03 at the owner's request, as the canonical citation for
Korsgaard's theory of practical identity and normativity. It is `Deferred`
because it was not read. The Tanner Lectures ([LIT-tmp18qr5](LIT-tmp18qr5.md), read in full)
stand in for it. This book **extends** them in the LIT scheme's sense: the
lectures call it "a longer version" and it keeps their order. The fourth
chapter takes the title of the lectures' last section, "The Origin of Value
and the Value of Life", in a revised form. Korsgaard's "Normative
Constitution of Agency" ([LIT-tmpnjho3](LIT-tmpnjho3.md)) cites the book's Lecture 3 (pp.
90–130) for the claim that reflective self-consciousness places the agent "in
a kind of relationship of authority over herself".

What the record lacks without a reading is the exchange: four commentaries,
two of them (Geuss's "Morality and identity" and Nagel's "Universality and the
reflective self") on practical identity by their titles, and Korsgaard's
forty-page reply. Those are
the chapters to read first, because [NOTE-tmpsutam](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpsutam.md)'s open questions (whether
practical identity can be specified without the agent's participation, and
how conflicting identities are resolved) are the commentators' questions.

No anthology topic holds it, and it carries no instruction for
machine-learning practice.
