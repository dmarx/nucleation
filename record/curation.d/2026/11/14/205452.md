## Support judgements

The NOTE says that this paper qualifies Sun et al.'s "abrupt" at
landscape scale, and that the two can both be right at their own scales.
That is a relation between two readings, not support for a claim of the
record's. A `rivals` relation is not written, because the two accounts
concern different objects and can both hold. No ARG is filed.
