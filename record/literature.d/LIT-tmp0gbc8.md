## Why this is here

Reflexive control is the influence the record's anchor question did not anticipate: conveying to a target prepared information so that it reaches, by its own reasoning, the decision the controller wants. In the record's terms it acts on the judgement–observation loop of [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) by shaping the observer, and it makes [QUESTION-121](../questions.d/QUESTION-121.md)'s 'unselected' evidence selected by someone else ([CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md)). The curation entry of 2026-10-13 named this work as unfiled. It is the bridge between Lefebvre's formal theory and the record's readings on Russian information operations ([THEORY-488](../theory.d/THEORY-488.md), [NOTE-070](../notes.d/NOTE-070.md), [THEORY-659](../theory.d/THEORY-659.md)).
