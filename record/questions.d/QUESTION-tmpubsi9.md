# QUESTION-tmpubsi9: What organization turns an interaction into a measurement, a measurement into a record that something uses, and observation into inquiry, without a conscious investigator; and what keeps an apparatus that revises its own discriminations answerable to what it did not produce?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U19, after [CLAIM-tmpalzo4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpalzo4.md) and [CLAIM-tmpi1bjh](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpi1bjh.md): "let's double click
on the relationship between inquiry and interaction, since this might
elucidate (or connect us to) a generic notion of observation/measurement that
doesn't require the presence of a conscious investigator. I think some of our
earlier readings touched on this, maybe I'm connecting us back to apparatus
here?"

A19 put the question as "**What additional organization turns an interaction
into a measurement—and what turns measurement into inquiry?**" It answered with
achievements that can come apart, "not automatically stages of one universal
ladder":

| achievement | what is added | a source the record holds or registered |
|---|---|---|
| relational event | an interaction makes one system's state consequential for another | Rovelli ([LIT-tmp57r0s](../literature.d/LIT-tmp57r0s.md), read; [THEORY-621](../theory.d/THEORY-621.md)) |
| measurement | the interaction separates alternatives into distinguishable outcomes, K_A(m, s′ given s), and may change s | Pattee ([LIT-1190](../literature.d/LIT-1190.md), read in [NOTE-952](../notes.d/NOTE-952.md)) |
| stable record | the outcome stays accessible far longer than the interaction took | A19 §3; no source the record holds shows it (Di Biagio and Rovelli's stable facts are a different notion; see the note of the readings below) |
| redundant record | many fragments of the environment hold the same outcome | Zurek ([LIT-tmpb4amz](../literature.d/LIT-tmpb4amz.md), unread) |
| use | the record changes what the system does next | chemotaxis, in A19 §6 |
| inquiry | the criteria of discrimination themselves change in response to what is found | [CLAIM-tmpalzo4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpalzo4.md) |

A19 §2 gave a working definition: "**A measurement is an interaction organized to
make particular differences consequential in the state of another system.**" A
thermometer implements the question of which temperature, without understanding
it. "**Specification can be realized as selective physical sensitivity.**" A19
added that "we should distinguish *implementing a discrimination* from
*understanding what the discrimination means*."

The bacterium is A19's bridge case (§6). E. coli compares present receptor
activity with a methylation memory that adapts more slowly. It runs or tumbles
on the comparison, and so implements "*Is the chemical environment improving
relative to the recent past?*" without entertaining the sentence. The memory's
time scale has to match the cell's motion. It has every rung up to use. Whether
it has the last one, revision of its own discriminations, is the question's
hard case.

A19 §7 proposed a criterion for the last rung: "**Inquiry is an organized
process in which distinctions acquired through interaction are evaluated
relative to some operative criterion, and the results can affect the system's
future ways of interacting, discriminating, or evaluating.**" A thermostat has a
fixed rule. A bacterium adapts. A learner revises its policy. A science
revises its instruments and standards. A19's formal model has an apparatus A_t
that produces records M_t, acts on them, and is then updated by
A_{t+1} = G(A_t, M_t, U_t, E_t). With A fixed this is measurement. With A
adaptive it is learning. When G can change which distinctions A makes, it is
reflexive inquiry.

## Why it is open here

**Each rung needs a criterion, and the candidates differ.** RQM, operational
measurement theory and Pattee draw the measurement line in different places.
For RQM, any interaction fixes a relative fact. For Pattee, measurement codes a
dynamical state into a rate-independent symbol. Not every trace is a symbol in
his sense.

**Barad's reading is stronger than any rung.** A19 §5 separated three readings
of an apparatus. On the informational reading it makes correlations. On the
operational reading it implements and stabilizes distinctions. On the
constitutive reading it participates in fixing the phenomenon's boundaries
([LIT-tmpikjbr](../literature.d/LIT-tmpikjbr.md), [LIT-tmpn1p0x](../literature.d/LIT-tmpn1p0x.md), unread). The first two admit mathematics and
experiment. "The third is a metaphysical claim that needs additional argument."
Dynamical stabilization does not derive it (A23 §VII). [CLAIM-tmpl228b](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl228b.md) is stated
in the agential sense.

**The top rung can confirm itself.** [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) holds that each judgement
reshapes the observer that makes the next observation. [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) holds that
what keeps the loop honest is evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass
through the interpretation it tests. A19 §8: "A self-reinforcing feedback system
can be organizationally robust and epistemically defective." "**the world must
have an opportunity to resist the inquiry's specification**." A21 found the same
failure in gluing. An apparatus that revises its correspondence maps only to
reduce disagreement can send them to zero and agree with everything ([THEORY-299](../theory.d/THEORY-299.md);
[CLAIM-tmpdjt64](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdjt64.md)). Revising by G is not enough. What has to be specified is what
G is not allowed to rewrite.

**Inferential descriptions are not inference.** The free-energy principle offers
a partition of internal, sensory, active and external states. The record's
readings ([NOTE-450](../notes.d/NOTE-450.md), [NOTE-447](../notes.d/NOTE-447.md)) hold that a statistical partition admitting an
inferential description does not show that the system infers or has that
boundary. A19 §9 kept the principle as "one candidate formalization of
nonconscious inference".

**Measurement has costs that must be stated correctly.** A19 §10: measuring a bit
does not by itself require dissipating k_BT ln 2. Landauer's bound is on
logically irreversible erasure ([LIT-360](../literature.d/LIT-360.md), read). The costs arise when records
must be robust, repeated, erased or used for control. [CLAIM-tmpi1bjh](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpi1bjh.md) needs that
precision.

## What would count as an answer

1. A criterion for each rung that a constructed system can meet or fail.
2. A system in which two adjacent rungs come apart. A19 suggested the
   strategy: "construct systems with identical sensory correlations but
   different memory persistence, downstream use, and adaptive capacities.
   Then determine exactly which observable behaviors each additional
   feature makes possible."
3. For the top rung, a structural condition on G, the part of the
   apparatus its own revisions cannot reach, under which revision improves
   prediction on interventions the system did not choose, and without which
   it does not. Distributed sensor networks that compare their reports through
   fixed correspondences (Robinson, [LIT-tmpn521a](../literature.d/LIT-tmpn521a.md)) are a classical,
   conscious-free test bed, once their correspondences are allowed to be
   revised; Robinson's are not.

## Note of 2026-10-27 (evening): the readings of the U15–U23 works

Five works the question cites were read. Two attributions were corrected in
place:
- the "stable record" row named Di Biagio and Rovelli's stable facts;
- the last paragraph said Robinson's networks recalibrate, and they do not.

- **Stable is not persistent.** In Di Biagio and Rovelli 2021
  (NOTE-tmppgt2i; THEORY-tmphf61a), a fact relative to F is stable *for a
  third system W*: W may sum over it classically, because F has interacted
  with an environment W cannot reach. Stability comes from inaccessibility,
  not duration. Their measurement has three stages that come apart (pp.
  5–6), and "It is the way that W, F and E couple to each other that makes F
  a measuring apparatus for W" (p. 6). Being an apparatus is a role relative
  to W (TERM-tmpjdel0).
- **An exact criterion for the correlation rung, and nothing above it.** Di
  Biagio and Rovelli 2025 (NOTE-tmp2eky0) define "B has information about A"
  as I_{A:B} ≠ 0, without any observer (p. 3). Nothing in the paper
  separates a measurement from any interaction that correlates. Its
  threshold for a fact is "high enough for the level of accessible
  resolution or confidence needed" (p. 3), so a purpose-relative threshold
  comes back exactly where the question would draw the measurement line.
- **The record rung.** Zurek (NOTE-tmpvsoha; THEORY-tmp08c0w) derives the set
  of recordable outcomes from repeatability alone, eq. (5), which is a
  criterion for measurement that needs no observer. He also separates
  decoherence from usable records: a completely mixed environment "is
  useless (even though it can still induce decoherence!)" (p. 4).
  Redundancy as objectivity stays argued, not derived (THEORY-tmp0d0qe).
- **A negative case for the top rung.** In discourse sheaves (NOTE-tmpe4zh5;
  THEORY-tmptkmt9) the apparatus revises its expression maps using only the
  disagreement among its own outputs. It converges to agreement that its
  authors call "learning to lie". Its guards keep the solution from
  collapsing; they do not make it answerable to anything outside.
- **A positive case.** Robinson's (NOTE-tmpdshmx) is small but real: the
  true crash site "was withheld from the data presented to the sheaf model"
  and used only to score the result (p. 25). Something the apparatus never
  sees is the check, which is CLAIM-342's condition in practice.
