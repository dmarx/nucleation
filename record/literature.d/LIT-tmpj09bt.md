# LIT-tmpj09bt: Epistemic communities and their situated practices: Perspectival realism—a primer

Michela Massimi (2023). "Epistemic communities and their situated practices: Perspectival realism—a primer." *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences* 1523(1): 5–10. DOI-10.1111/nyas.14981. Open access (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

## Key takeaways

- Scientific perspectives are the situated practices of communities;
  they "methodologically intersect" and "historically interlace", and
  reliability is a product of the plurality, not of any one community.
- Realism is about modally robust phenomena, stable events re-identified
  across perspectival inferences; natural kinds are open-ended groupings
  of them.
- Severing and trademarking communities' contributions is an epistemic
  injustice with political consequences.
