## Article 4

### J4.1: FAILS as stated. A corrected sentence passes by derivation.

**What the join says.** §4.11 reads each party's reading as a demand. Clarifying then either:

- *refines*: a distinction lets "each reading govern where it applies". The same declared demands become jointly satisfiable, which is relief, by CASE-191's route;
- *settles for one reading*, which drops a demand and is not relief (CLAIM-347, CLAIM-549);
- *merges agreeing readings*, which leaves the complex unchanged.

It concludes that "a misunderstanding is productive in the owner's sense exactly when its clarification is a relief, and not merely a quieting."

**The refine bullet misapplies CASE-191.** CASE-191's two demands are indexed by context. The targets a and b are for contexts A and B, and the sensor S tells A from B. The differentiating channel then lets one output serve both, because the demands bear on different situations. A misunderstanding's separating case is different. It is one case at which R₁ and R₂ prescribe different responses (CLAIM-tmpm76n5: "the first case where R₁ and R₂ prescribe different responses"). If both readings bear on that same case and the same joint outcome, no distinction over cases can meet both there. A distinction lets the policy depend on a feature (CLAIM-532's "new distinction"). It cannot make one outcome at one case take two values.

There is a second route that is also not relief. A "refinement" that restricts each reading's demand to a sub-domain changes the demands. CLAIM-361: "an endogenous revision of what the organization maintains is a transformation of it and not relief". CLAIM-347: relief compares complexes "for the same declared demands".

**The biconditional is defeated by a case that could plainly occur.** Take two parties and the phrase "deliver it Friday", which one reads as Friday morning and the other as close of business. A Friday-morning meeting is the separating case. They clarify jointly:

- the distinction "Friday AM / Friday COB" enters their shared vocabulary, and they reuse it;
- on this occasion one reading governs.

By CLAIM-tmpupcws this is productive: the parties detected the distinction, they reorganized around it, and it is new to the dyad. By CLAIM-tmpisfi1 it is productive too ("resolved jointly, it is a productive misunderstanding"). Yet it is not relief in CLAIM-347's sense. On the declared demands, one is dropped at the case. Going forward, the demands are re-specified each time, which CLAIM-361 counts as a transformation. Every premise holds: no component's `defeated_if` is met. So "productive exactly when relief" is false of an ordinary case, and the draft should drop it from §4.11.

**The free step is the draft's own.** It reads each interpretation as a maintained demand, and the draft flags this. SYNTHESIS-049's "Entrenchment as relief" bullet should be cited beside it. That bullet declines a link over the same free parameter, "whether a demand no process presses is removed or only quiet". The settle bullet turns on this parameter: does settling drop the losing demand or suppress it? So does the drafters' discriminating consequence.

**The drafters' discriminating consequence does not pass as stated.** The consequence: a settling clarification leaves the losing demand pressing again on a later case, and a refining one does not. It fails for two reasons.

- It has a free parameter. Whether the losing reading carried a maintained demand is read off whether the demand presses again. If it does not press again, the demand was "dropped" or the party was persuaded, so every case is absorbed. CLAIM-549 says pressing (activation) and the requirement come apart, so non-recurrence cannot tell a dropped demand from a quieted one.
- By the derivation below, refining a same-situation conflict leaves the losing demand as unmet at the case as settling does. Restricted to conflated-situation conflicts, the refine half is CASE-191's transfer result: "meets the later conflict with no frustration". That result is held.

It could become a test only if maintenance were measured before the clarification, independently of recurrence. That is a LEAD design, not a claim.

**The corrected sentence passes by derivation.**

- *Premises*:
  - relief is strict enlargement of the complex for fixed declared demands (CLAIM-347), under a fixed correspondence, where revising a demand is a transformation (CLAIM-361);
  - a new distinction is the policy coming to depend on a feature, and a new channel is the reachable outcomes growing (CLAIM-532);
  - refinement is the parties' partition growing finer (CLAIM-tmpm76n5);
  - the construction in which a distinction relieves context-indexed demands (CASE-191, grounds).
- *Derivation*: suppose each reading carries a demand on joint outcomes.
  1. If the demands bear on situations S₁ and S₂, disjoint but lying in one cell of the dyad's partition, no policy measurable on that partition meets both. Splitting the cell enlarges the attainable policies, and {1, 2} becomes a face: relief, as in CASE-191.
  2. If S₁ and S₂ share a situation c and the demands require different joint outcomes at c, then no refinement of the partition meets both. A refinement changes what the policy can depend on, not what is attainable at c. Only an enlargement of the outcomes attainable at c, an outcome that meets both (a new channel, in CLAIM-532's terms), makes {1, 2} a face. Any clarification that leaves those outcomes fixed either drops a demand (CLAIM-347) or revises them (CLAIM-361). Neither is relief.
- *Why it is nontrivial and unheld*: it splits CLAIM-tmpm76n5's "refine" into two kinds. Only one of them is relief, and only one is CASE-191's route. It also shows that the owner's productive misunderstanding coincides with relief only in the first kind. No CLAIM relates misunderstanding to frustration. The search found none, and the draft found none.
- **Title:** *Taking each party's reading of an ambiguous expression as a demand on their joint outcomes, clarifying a consequential misunderstanding relieves frustration in CLAIM-347's sense by a new distinction only where the readings' demands bear on different situations the expression conflated; where they demand different joint outcomes in the same situation, no distinction relieves them, only an outcome that meets both, and settling on one reading or rewording the demands is not relief*
- **rests_on:** CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361, CLAIM-532, CLAIM-tmpm76n5. **grounds:** CASE-191. **complements:** CLAIM-tmpupcws, CLAIM-tmpisfi1, CLAIM-tmpgx2ej.
- **Warrant:** demonstrated, conditional on the antecedent. The mapping of readings to demands stays a stipulation in the title, not a premise.
- **defeated_if:** a clarification that makes the two demands jointly realizable by a distinction alone, with the outcomes attainable at the shared situation and the declared demands held fixed under CLAIM-361's correspondence. That would show an error in step 2. Or relief judged after a demand is revised, which would remove the premise CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-361 supply. The objection that it only restates CLAIM-347 applied to a dyad goes in too. The answer to it is that the split in step 2 is in no component.
- **Caution:** "same situation" must be fixed by the joint outcome, not by the feature set. One can always invent a feature, such as who is asking. It relieves only if the joint outcome may vary with it, and at one joint outcome it cannot.
