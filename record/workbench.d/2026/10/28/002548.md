**[ARG-006](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-006.md) and [CLAIM-063](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-063.md).** Under [ADR-059](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-059.md), the new check listed [ARG-006](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-006.md) as
the record's one sound argument whose conclusion is not held. On reading,
nothing was wrong. [ARG-006](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-006.md)'s inference is the exact algebra, that
intertwining each framing operation preserves their commutator. [CLAIM-063](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-063.md)
goes on to measured order effects. [ARG-006](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-006.md)'s two held undercutters already
say what the inference does not carry:

- [CLAIM-011](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) limits it to Φ's image.
- [CLAIM-253](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-253.md) says the identity reaches observed order effects only with a
  readout-intertwining defect, which the claim does not state.

The check now leaves out an ARG whose held undercutter narrows it, with a
test. [CLAIM-063](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-063.md)'s blocker, and what would clear it, are on the entry.

**[CLAIM-277](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-277.md) built.** It is the most relied-on unblocked claim, with 10
dependents. It said that durable authority need not be a primitive, and
that the same loop entrenches whatever gained influence early. The record
had stated both loops and constructed neither. [CASE-182](../../record/cases.d/CASE-182.md) builds them
under one endorsement rule (scratch/formal/trust_loop.py, 200 seeds).

- **Scored every round:** the more accurate operator ends ahead in
  200/200 seeds, even after a head start for the other.
- **Scored only when acting, under sharp selection (β ≥ 30):** the early
  leader is never displaced, 200/200.
- **Soft selection (β = 8):** the better operator recovers, after a median
  of 460 rounds.

The first run printed "True" for a check it never made. The line was a
literal, and it was caught on reading and replaced by the measurement. The
first run also showed no entrenchment at β = 8. That is a finding and not a
failure: entrenchment needs selection sharp enough that the stale estimate
is never revisited. The body said [CLAIM-277](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-277.md) rests on [CLAIM-282](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-282.md), which is
Active, and the frontmatter now says so too. [QUESTION-083](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-083.md) is split, and its
child, [QUESTION-295](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-295.md), is Answered.

[ADR-060](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-060.md) version 2 says an offered part-answer keeps `answers` and is
split under [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md) once it is held. Patterns the three readers found
independently:

- **Reading-THEORYs.** THEORYs filed from a single reading almost never
  answer synthesis questions. Those questions' own bodies say "answers it
  in part; the body gives each one's limit".
- **Wide-form THEORYs.** THEORYs filed by [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md) v4's narrowing sweep,
  whose question is their old promote_when, are the cleanest ANSWERS.
- **Self-description.** An entry describing itself as "answers in part"
  is a weak signal. On reading, most such entries were bearing.
