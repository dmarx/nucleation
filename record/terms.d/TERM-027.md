# TERM-027: cultural attractor

## Definition

A110 §20: "**The emergence of stable meaning within a community need not imply
faithful retention of ancestral meaning.** A **cultural attractor** may represent
a shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object." Earlier:
A50's "attraction toward certain regions of communicative state space", A86's "a
natural information-theoretic explanation of cultural attractors", and C6 §8's
heading "Iterated reconstruction, drift, and cultural attractors".

## What it is not

Not a preserved original: an attractor is where the chain goes whatever its start,
so reaching it is evidence of forgetting the start ([CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md)). The manuscript
keeps the phenomenon ("A stable conventional endpoint may be far removed from the
ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable", §8) and drops the word and the
reading as a convention ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-24: the exchange 'Memes, Contracts, and Systemic Normativity', A2 and A4 §7

A2 §5 names Sperber's cultural attractor theory as a parallel to the owner's
"memes as nucleation sites" ([CLAIM-tmp1ng5k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1ng5k.md)), and A4 §7, citing this term and
[CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) accurately, sets an attractor against a "memetic nucleator": an
attractor concerns "Where reconstruction tends" and may forget its history; a
nucleator concerns "How organization becomes established" and "May become
dispensable". "A meme need not be an attractor to nucleate an attractor. It
need not even bring about convergence: it might instead initiate a persistent
ecology of competing regimes." The contrast is recorded as sense 2 of
[TERM-tmpet5z8](TERM-tmpet5z8.md). Bearing: a complement, no change to this term; Sperber's
works stay unregistered, A2 naming no title. The wording of this note is the
