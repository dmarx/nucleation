**Corrections the readings made.**

- Di Tella and Franceschelli: A21's 0.23 fewer front pages (18%) is the
  published abstract's; the working paper read gives 0.31 per standard
  deviation (25%). The LIT gives both.
- Barnehl and Schumacher: the measure has two poles, regime-owned and
  uncoercible opposition outlets, not one, and the country is Nicaragua;
  the paper defines capture by coercion and measures alignment.
- Bergemann and Morris: §3.3's strategic-substitutes rule (p_B = x + ε,
  r_B = 0) is infeasible under its own assumption (x > ½, |ε| ≤ x − ½); the
  linear programme's optimum is p_B = x/(1 − 2ε), r_B = 2p_B − 1. It is
  right only for x + ε ≤ ½. Found in the accepted manuscript; the
  published text was not compared.
- Schram and Ule: the text's "only 50% invest by period 5" without capture
  does not match their Table 4 (63% and 84%).
- Short, Hill and Brown: the introduction says "four times" and the
  analysis "seven times" for the same gap.
- Arthur: the 1989 article was not reached; the reading is of the 1983
  working paper, a different text, and A19's citation is accurate to it.
- Smaller ones, in the LITs and NOTEs: McCombs and Shaw's summary (an
  aggregate correlation, not yet committed voters, the authors' hedge);
  the OECD definition includes the captor's intent; Beckers, Eberhardt and
  Halpern weight low-level implementations of one high-level intervention,
  not the interventions; James et al.'s summary sharpened; slips in
  Xu et al., Beckers and Halpern, Beckers, Eberhardt and Halpern, Besley
  and Prat, Szeidl and Szucs and Weatherall et al. noted where found.
