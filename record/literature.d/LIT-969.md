# LIT-969: Agency Is Frame-Dependent

David Abel, André Barreto, Michael Bowling, Will Dabney, Shi Dong, Steven Hansen, Anna Harutyunyan, Khimya Khetarpal, Clare Lyle, Razvan Pascanu, Georgios Piliouras, Doina Precup, Jonathan Richens, Mark Rowland, Tom Schaul and Satinder Singh (2025), *arXiv:2502.04403*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-793](../notes.d/NOTE-793.md).*

- **The thesis.** "The agency of any system is relative in the sense that
  it depends on arbitrary commitments that we collectively call a
  reference frame."
- **Four conditions, each two-valued.** On an account "due largely to
  Barandiaran et al. (2009)", agency needs a boundary, being the source of
  one's action, goals and adaptivity. For each the paper cites a result
  showing that it can come out either way:
  - Jiang's boundary-dependence of the optimal policy;
  - Kenton et al.'s relativity to the choice of causal variables
    ([LIT-909](LIT-909.md));
  - inverse RL's underdetermination of reward by behaviour;
  - Zadeh's "what ... it is adaptive [to]".
- **A frame** is the four commitments together. It is not formally
  defined. "Our definitions, claims, and arguments are purely
  philosophical."
- **How to choose a frame is left open.** Dennett's intentional stance
  "could be formalised as a principle for selecting frames in terms of
  predictive or explanatory power".
- **Weaker than it reads.** The premises are existential and the
  conclusion universal, and "arbitrary" is asserted, not argued.
  Barandiaran's requirement that the system individuate itself "without
  an observer" is not engaged.

## Standing in the record

Filed 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-032](../questions.d/QUESTION-032.md) and read 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-793](../notes.d/NOTE-793.md).
Its thesis is held as [THEORY-249](../theory.d/THEORY-249.md), Proposed. It supports
[QUESTION-032](../questions.d/QUESTION-032.md)'s relativization of agency to a level and a target, and
widens it to the choice of causal variables, the principle for
meaningful goals and the class of meaningful adaptations. It gives no
test and no verdict on the thermostat ([CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md)). It generalizes the
decomposition-relativity that [THEORY-232](../theory.d/THEORY-232.md) (Watson, [LIT-990](LIT-990.md)) concedes for
one measure, without bearing on comparisons made within a frame. It
bears on [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md)'s tests 2 and 3, whose frame-relativity that claim
already concedes. Against [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md) it changes the subject from
self-individuation to observer-drawn boundaries rather than refuting it.
