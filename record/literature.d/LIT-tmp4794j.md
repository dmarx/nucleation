# LIT-tmp4794j: The Way I Am

Eminem (Marshall Mathers). (2000). "The Way I Am". On *The Marshall
Mathers LP*. Aftermath / Interscope. Released 23 May 2000; second single,
3 October 2000. Reference page:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Way_I_Am_(Eminem_song).

Not studied as a text. The record cites one couplet and what the reference
page says of the song's subject: "the negative side of the fame that he
had gained", being "harassed by fans", and "the pressure put on him to top
his earlier work".

## Why this is here

The owner's U39 of the exchange "Graph Learning Applications" asked for an
examination of the phrase "I am whatever you say I am". A39 identified the
song and read the line three ways, and [CLAIM-415](../claims.d/CLAIM-415.md) keeps the third:
"A person can be shaped by a judgment they reject, because opposition to
the judgment becomes an organizing feature of their activity."

The couplet's second line matters to that reading and the exchange did not
use it. "If I wasn't then why would I say I am" turns the attribution
into a self-assertion: the speaker claims the label as his own. The line is borrowed from Eric B. &
Rakim's "As the Rhyme Goes On" (1987), a boast. So the couplet holds both
of [TERM-tmpo92fy](../terms.d/TERM-tmpo92fy.md)'s movements at once: an identity held by others, and its
appropriation by the person it is held of.
