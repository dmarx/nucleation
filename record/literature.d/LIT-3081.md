# LIT-3081: Our ways will not change: Future collective continuity increases present prosocial considerations

Andrej Simić, Simona Sacchi and Marco Perugini (2025). *British Journal of Social Psychology* 64(1): e12847. DOI-10.1111/bjso.12847

Read in full in [NOTE-2302](../notes.d/NOTE-2302.md).

## Key takeaways

- Future collective continuity, a group's expected sameness in time, can
  be measured with future-self-continuity items and manipulated, and its
  similarity component, not vividness or positivity, carries its
  relations to intergroup attitudes.
- The manipulation lowered collective angst and outgroup anxiety; its
  effects on prejudice and collective action intentions were not
  significant as preregistered and appeared only with political
  orientation as a covariate.
- National attachment, measured before the manipulation and correlated
  .14 with FCC similarity, moderated the mediated effects: significant
  for high identifiers, not for low.
