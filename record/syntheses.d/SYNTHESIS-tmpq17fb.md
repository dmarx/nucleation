# SYNTHESIS-tmpq17fb: A communicative kind is a repeatable relational organization that distinct events realize, proposed as partly constituted by relations to roles, norms and expected uptake and recognized through probes that fix it only relative to the family used: identification underdetermines constitution, a character or other invariant classifies kinds only up to isomorphism and only once a symmetry is anchored on the situation, competent uptake is offered as a defeasible standard against which probe families are tested, sharing a kind is neither transport nor descent, and the constitution claim stays open against intention-based accounts, the change of audience a translation makes, and the individuation of tokens

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line ("Contextual pragmatic
information transport"): the part on communicative kinds and the identity
of communicative acts, filed mostly from the October exchange (A187 to
A240, U52 to U62), the record's audit of the line of 2026-10-10, its
replies of that date, and the cases searched for at the owner's request.
The line's account is built over it and its seven sibling parts (fidelity,
contextuality, the discriminating study, kernels and admissible transport,
the act and its proposition, frames, and chains). Five members are first in
another line: CLAIM-452 (formal-apparatus), CLAIM-477 (distributed-agency),
and CLAIM-574, CLAIM-590 and CLAIM-593 (realizability), whose part account
SYNTHESIS-040 integrates the last three already. They are integrated again
here because their `line` includes pragmatic-transport. Every member is in
`integrates`, including four Rejected ones, which are integrated as the
record's verdicts.*

## The account

- **The question and its unit.** QUESTION-021 (A18's, Open) asks whether
  there is a frame-independent act that translations approximate or a
  family of frame-relative realizations. QUESTION-029 (the assistant's
  question at A203, Open) refines it in three parts: what constitutes the
  identity, how it is recognized apart from a representation, and under
  which transformations it persists. Its body: "One that answers only the
  second and third is QUESTION-002 again." TERM-040 (A218's, Active) files
  the unit as a communicative kind, "what two realizations share when they
  count as the same act", kept apart from a token and from a position; its
  gap is that "A218 never defines a kind formally". TERM-014 (Active), the
  observational equivalence class, becomes in TERM-040 a limit on knowing a
  kind rather than its criterion. TERM-038 (A191's, Active) separates
  descriptive, causal and constitutive relations, "Not a chain of
  entailments". TERM-041 (A194 and A203's, Active) is the character-like
  signature, "Not yet built". QUESTION-028 (Open) asks when such a kind is
  real rather than an arbitrary taxonomy, QUESTION-030 (Open, also
  distributed-agency) which interventions tell constitutive relations from
  diagnostic ones, QUESTION-036 (Open) whether a kind's symmetries can be
  reconstructed from its realizations, and QUESTION-050 (Open) whether
  identity splits into an interpreter-internal and a relational factor.
- **Partial constitution, and what presses on it.** CLAIM-151 (the
  assistant's at A187, after the owner's U52; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed): which act an utterance performs is partly constituted by its
  relations to participant roles, norms and expected uptake. It answers
  QUESTION-021 for its second position and rests on CLAIM-061 (another
  part). Its argument "stops there" at social acts; CLAIM-477 (the owner's
  U8, sharpened by A8; Proposed; first line distributed-agency) is the
  unrestricted frame, "Filed unrestricted in domain, as the frame of which
  CLAIM-151 and CLAIM-136 are domain cases". CLAIM-215 (A240's T1,
  Proposed) is a weaker ontological clause: its third sentence "does not
  tell relational constitution from its rival", since an intention-based
  account meets it too. Against CLAIM-151: CLAIM-221 (the record's audit,
  counter, Proposed) names the intention-based rival; read since
  2026-10-13, the dispute "now turns on whether standing that enters
  through the content of the speaker's intention counts as a relation in
  CLAIM-151's sense". CLAIM-224 (the audit's, counter, Proposed): a
  translation changes all the relations CLAIM-151 makes constituents, so
  survival "turns on a correspondence of relations the structure cannot
  supply from inside"; CLAIM-151 version 2 took its remedy of roles, and
  the residue is "whether the target culture's norms define the same
  roles". QUESTION-073 (Open) holds the case where both bite, teasing. The
  cases: CASE-018 (fictional) is the four speakers; ARG-024 (Rejected)
  holds that it "shows a possibility" and is no evidence. CASE-069
  (documented, Labov) is the first documented case for QUESTION-073 and
  grounds CLAIM-151. CASE-064, CASE-061 and CASE-073 (documented)
  illustrate CLAIM-224: a change of audience with the token fixed, the
  last with the speaker's intention declared.
- **Intended meaning beside the act.** CLAIM-160 (the owner's, Proposed):
  intended meaning is fixed by the write; since the owner's ruling noted on
  2026-10-28 it "bears on that question and does not answer it"
  (QUESTION-029). CLAIM-171 (the record's counter, Proposed) objects that
  the projected receiver is one more reading. CLAIM-255 (the interlocutor's
  reading of 2026-10-12, Proposed) rests on both CLAIM-160 and CLAIM-151 and
  splits intending, performing and being recognized, so competent uptake
  is "constitutive for some acts, evidential for others and irrelevant to
  the existence of still others". CASE-049 and its variant CASE-043
  (stipulated) are the tree and the unread write; CASE-048 (stipulated),
  the joke seen at two ages, illustrates both CLAIM-160 and CLAIM-171.
- **Identification is not constitution.** CLAIM-150 (the exchange's guard,
  with the record's stronger form; disclaimed, Active): an ontology of
  intrinsically individuated objects can reproduce the identifications and
  predictions, "so identification underdetermines constitution". It
  undercuts ARG-023 (from the Yoneda lemma) and ARG-038 (from the
  character analogy), both Rejected. CLAIM-135 (the assistant's, A207 and
  A214; Active) separates reconstruction, identification and constitution,
  and "identifiability theory reaches the second at most". CLAIM-046 (A39's;
  Active, the line's adopted method) defines identity and fidelity on
  observable responses, and CLAIM-112 (disclaimed, Active) says that is "a
  choice of what to measure, not a theory of what meaning is". ARG-029
  (abduction, Proposed) is the step from that operational object to a kind
  that can outrun observation, for which "no turn argues for the kind".
- **Characters and invariants.** CLAIM-137 (the owner's U54, its first
  part; Proposed) identifies a kind by a realization-independent invariant
  "as a group character identifies a representation up to isomorphism",
  once the group is anchored on the situation; it rests on CLAIM-151.
  CLAIM-132 (granted, demonstrated, Active; also formal-apparatus) bounds
  it: characters classify kinds, "not tokens or positions", in
  characteristic zero. CLAIM-249 (the audit's, granted, demonstrated,
  Active) shows the group is fixed only up to automorphism: "the collapse
  problem again, at the level of the symmetry group". CLAIM-148 (granted,
  demonstrated, Active; also formal-apparatus): a factorization's gauge "is
  not a symmetry of the relational system", and identifiability must come
  first. CLAIM-043 (granted, Active; also formal-apparatus) is the
  entropic case of an invariant kept and structure lost. CASE-057
  (stipulated on a documented basis, Brown and Gilman) gives CLAIM-249 a
  group it bites on. CLAIM-187 (Proposed) carries the mode-connectivity
  protocol, quotient the admissible group and read the residue, to
  communicative kinds, and calls the carry-over its weak point.
  QUESTION-027 (Open) asks for a signature stable, sufficient and
  discriminating, identified outside the probes; QUESTION-052, QUESTION-141,
  QUESTION-182, QUESTION-183 and QUESTION-216 (Open) and QUESTION-245
  (Answered) are its spectral sub-questions.
- **Relata without primitive identity.** CLAIM-136 (the owner's U54, its
  second part; Proposed), in a strict and a weak version the owner has not
  chosen between. CLAIM-134 (the record's counter, Proposed):
  Chakravartty's dilemma, and the weak version "concedes a non-relational
  ground of identity". CLAIM-138 (counter, Proposed): Newman is the
  collapse objection, and the remedy "concedes that relational structure
  alone does not fix communicative identity". CASE-060 (stipulated, two
  identical ballots) forces the choice. CLAIM-593 (A's, filer's
  construction; Active; first line realizability) shows "Isomorphism is
  not persistence, in either direction".
- **Probes and the standard they answer to.** CLAIM-142 (the owner's U45
  pointer, A158's form; Proposed): observational equivalence is relative
  to the probes, and "only independently justified probes evaluated on
  held-out cases are evidence that structure was preserved". CLAIM-206
  (the audit's, counter, demonstrated, Proposed) puts a dilemma to it and
  to the October thesis. CLAIM-217 (the record's reply; Proposed) makes
  competent participants' uptake the standard, defeasible since it
  conceded CLAIM-241 (A240's refinement pressed by the record; counter,
  Active): "Partial constitution gives uptake a vote, not the last word."
  CLAIM-213 (the interlocutor's, Proposed) reads a kind as "a hypothesis
  about invariance across admissible changes of measurement and context".
  CLAIM-209 (A240's T2, Proposed; also formal-apparatus) answers
  QUESTION-027 with restricted recoverability; CLAIM-193 (Proposed) is its
  linear case. CLAIM-149 (A184's, Active): training probes cannot certify,
  grounded in CASE-041 and illustrated by CASE-071; CASE-070 grounds
  CLAIM-142's first half. QUESTION-031 (Open) asks when a family is rich
  enough; QUESTION-272 (Answered) holds its linear part, answered by
  CLAIM-452 (Active; first line formal-apparatus). CLAIM-574 and CLAIM-590
  (Active; first line realizability) bear on QUESTION-031: distinctions
  only grow along nested families, and the chosen category or enrichment
  decides which configurations count as the same.
- **The integrative thesis and its parts.** CLAIM-204 (the record's
  restatement of the October thesis, which it superseded; Proposed)
  answers QUESTION-029 with four clauses. CLAIM-216 (A240's objection,
  Active) holds the clauses should stand apart, and CLAIM-204 version 3
  concedes it. CLAIM-210 (the record's reply, demonstrated, Active):
  "Sharing a kind and being a rendering are different relations", against
  CLAIM-246 (the audit's, Rejected). CLAIM-002 (A50 and A52's, Active)
  holds a text's identity across retellings to be three relations, and
  CASE-054 and CASE-121 (documented) put kind-sharing, descent and
  transport on record material.

What stays open: whether constitution reaches teasing (QUESTION-073), which
interventions show it (QUESTION-030), when a kind is real (QUESTION-028),
whether a signature exists outside the probes (QUESTION-027, QUESTION-052)
and when a family suffices (QUESTION-031), whether a kind's symmetries can
be reconstructed (QUESTION-036), and how identity splits between the
interpreter and the relations (QUESTION-050).
