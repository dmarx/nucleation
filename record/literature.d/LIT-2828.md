  Javed and White (2019), arXiv 1905.12588, NeurIPS 2019. OML meta-trains
  a representation by differentiating through online SGD on correlated
  streams, then freezes it beneath a small predictor that learns online.
  On Incremental Sine Waves and Split-Omniglot this gives far less
  forgetting than pre-trained or sparse representations, and learning
  faster than approximate IID training. The representation turns out
  highly sparse with no dead units. It is here because A54 named it for
  representations meta-trained against forgetting, bearing on
  overconsolidation ([CLAIM-758](../claims.d/CLAIM-758.md)). It bears mostly on forgetting: it
  secures retention and fast acquisition together by freezing the
  representation, and it says nothing on plasticity over long training.
