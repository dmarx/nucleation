The owner suggested on 2026-11-19 that Kuhn is corroborating theory for
learning that facilitates future learning, and asked for the three works
proposed in reply to be read: The Structure of Scientific Revolutions (2nd
ed., 1970), "Second Thoughts on Paradigms" (1974) and "The Essential
Tension" (1959). Three readers ran in parallel, with running notes on
claude/readers/kuhn (ADR-108); the filer rechecked every quotation by
script.

## Rulings and copies

- Structure was read from a whole-book PDF in a course-papers folder on a
faculty site. ADR-074 admits course-page copies of articles and
manuscripts and excludes uploads of whole in-copyright books; the filer
read this case as excluded and asked. The owner ruled: "treat it as
lawful". The ruling is for this copy.
- "Second Thoughts on Paradigms" was read from an unofficial transcription
on a university lecture page, without original pagination; page references
in its NOTE are to that copy.
- "The Essential Tension": no lawful copy found (only whole-book uploads,
not read, and no open scan of the 1959 proceedings). LIT-tmp5651k is
Unavailable; Kindi's essay (LIT-tmpvmfv7) stands in for its thesis and two
sentences.

## Where the record already held it

No Kuhn work was held. The record used him secondhand: THEORY-834
(Collins), NOTE-564 (Zollman, "against Kuhn"), THEORY-673 (Luque,
generalization-sketch). The closest held argument is CLAIM-475, with
TERM-092 and CLAIM-761, rather than the claims first named.

CLAIM-756 and CLAIM-475.
- "Second Thoughts on Paradigms": LIT-tmphef14, NOTE-tmpvzqac,

- "The Essential Tension": LIT-tmp5651k (Unavailable); the stand-in

## Findings

- All three bear on the learning claims as interpretive history about
communities of scientists, not learners in training; the corroborations
are of that kind.
- Corrections: NOTE-564's "against Kuhn" needs qualifying (Kuhn argues for
diversity as risk distribution); THEORY-673's citation "Kuhn 1970, p. 465"
is not in Structure.
- A candidate the readings raise, not filed: consolidation that raises the
cost of a later acquisition can lower the cost of detecting that it is
needed. Offered to the owner.

## What was left, and why

The case-history chapters of Structure were read in part. Bearings too
loose to note: THEORY-1358 and THEORY-1363 (Kemp, learning to learn),
THEORY-1520 and THEORY-1547 (worked examples), QUESTION-028, QUESTION-360,
CLAIM-532, CLAIM-184, CLAIM-276, THEORY-2006; each NOTE names its own. The
1959 essay's own text on textbooks and examples remains unread.
