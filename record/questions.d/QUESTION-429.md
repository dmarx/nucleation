# QUESTION-429: Does an independent re-analysis of Banerjee, Hanna, Kreindler and Olken's harmonized micro-data, or a further pooled analysis of randomized evaluations of government cash transfer programmes, find no systematic effect of the transfers on whether adults work or on their weekly hours, with moderate reductions excluded for men?

## Why it is a question

Banerjee, Hanna, Kreindler and Olken ([THEORY-1471](../theory.d/THEORY-1471.md)) pooled seven
randomized evaluations themselves; the pooling has not been re-run by
anyone else. An independent re-analysis or a further pooled analysis
finding the same null, with moderate reductions for men excluded, would
answer it; a systematic reduction would count against.
