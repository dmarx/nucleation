# ARG-tmpqy0xr: The hierarchy of Gödel's incompleteness, in which a consistent theory cannot prove its own consistency and the stronger theory that can has its own unprovable consistency, shows by analogy the shape of the regress of anchors, a system unable to certify its own ground from within with each added level certifying the one below and not itself, and not its mechanism, which is a symmetry the system's reports cannot break and not a diagonal sentence

## What transfers

The second incompleteness theorem says a consistent, recursively
axiomatized theory strong enough for arithmetic cannot prove its own
consistency. The theory with Con(T) added can prove T's consistency, but
not its own. Iterated, this gives a progression of theories, each
certifying the last and none certifying itself. Tarski's undefinability
of truth has the same shape: truth for a language is definable only in a
stronger one. (Both are cited from standard knowledge, not from a reading
the record holds.)

The anchor regress has this shape:
- the network's own criterion, consistency, cannot certify its
  accuracy;
- the beacon certifies the network but not itself;
- the survey certifies the beacon but not itself ([CLAIM-tmpw11e6](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw11e6.md)).

Coherence is not truth, and certification comes from the level above. So
far the owner's comparison holds.

## What does not

- **The mechanism differs.** Gödel's limit comes from a diagonal
  sentence, a theory talking about its own provability. The anchor's limit
  comes from a symmetry. Two worlds, one with a miscalibrated anchor and
  one with a correct anchor and a shifted world, give identical reports
  ([CLAIM-tmpklggb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpklggb.md)). No self-reference is involved.
- **Each level closes differently.** In arithmetic, no further axiom
  closes the gap without opening another, whatever it is. Here a survey
  of the right kind removes the beacon's error entirely, and what remains
  is the survey's own calibration. The regress is real, but each step
  fully resolves its predecessor. In the arithmetic case each step
  resolves only the predecessor's consistency statement.
- **The limit is contingent on what is observed.** Evidence that breaks
  the symmetry, say an independent measurement of the beacon, ends this
  regress at whatever is not revised. That is a decision about where to
  trust, not a theorem about all systems. It is the "dogmatic stop" horn of
  Agrippa's trilemma, taken deliberately, and the entrenchment risk of
  [CLAIM-184](../claims.d/CLAIM-184.md).

## Critical questions

- **Are the two alike in the respect the conclusion uses?** In the shape,
  yes: internal criteria cannot certify the property, and certification is
  relative to a further level. In mechanism, no.
- **Is there a closer analogue?** Agrippa's trilemma for justification,
  and the identifiability limits the record already holds ([CLAIM-tmpxoxys](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpxoxys.md),
  [CLAIM-148](../claims.d/CLAIM-148.md)), are closer. The analogy to Gödel is apt for the shape and
  misleading if read as saying the limit is self-referential.
