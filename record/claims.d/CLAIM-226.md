# CLAIM-226: The defeat tests pool unnamed judges and treat their disagreement as noise, though the theory makes the act partly constituted by its audience; for historical sources there is no source audience at all, and for interlingual ones population differences are read as distortion

## The objection

**Whose judgements.** The line makes the act depend on who receives it.
[CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md): the act is "partly constituted by its relations to participants,
norms and expected uptake". [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md): fidelity "under a fixed interpreter,
across interpreters and across states of adaptation are different
quantities". [CASE-026](../cases.d/CASE-026.md) is the record's own instance: modern slang can make an
insider "sound like an outsider imitating that subculture", and "The
audience may understand the observation perfectly while losing the sense of
complicity". Whether the footing is lost depends on which audience hears it.

A theory of that kind predicts structured disagreement between judges: one
community hears teasing where another hears reprimand. Averaging the
judgements of a convenience sample then measures the act for an unnamed
population, and treats the disagreement the theory predicts as error. No
entry fixes the population of judges, a sampling frame, or how disagreement
is modelled. Claims, questions, cases and terms mention annotators only to
say that the manuscript splits them ([QUESTION-005](../questions.d/QUESTION-005.md), [CLAIM-085](CLAIM-085.md), [CLAIM-084](CLAIM-084.md)).
[CASE-042](../cases.d/CASE-042.md) notes the mirror risk: "If all interpreters share the same
misconception, matrix factorization may recover a highly stable
representation of that misconception." [LIT-851](../literature.d/LIT-851.md), which the line reads for how
much signalling there is in natural-language meaning selection, labelled its
interpretations "by hand, with no agreement measure" ([NOTE-654](../notes.d/NOTE-654.md)).

**No source audience.** [CLAIM-005](CLAIM-005.md)'s distance is D(τ) = Σ_C w_C
d(e_C^source, e_τ(C)^target), and e_C^source is a distribution of
judgements by the source's receivers. [QUESTION-023](../questions.d/QUESTION-023.md) records that the
manuscript's notion of fidelity descends from A7's "what contemporary line
produces the same effect Henley's produced on his audience?". For Villon's
fifteenth-century argot, Henley's nineteenth-century cant and Marot's
sixteenth-century French there is no such audience to ask. Any estimate puts
present-day experts or lay readers in its place, and by [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md) they are
different interpreters. What is measured is fidelity to a modern reading of
the source.

**Two populations.** For a live interlingual case, e^source must come from
readers of the source language and e^target from readers of the target
language. The distance then mixes what the translation changed with how the
two communities read. A difference between populations is read as distortion
by transport. Bilingual judges remove the confound only by making the
receiver a bilingual, who is not the target-language reader [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s
"receiving system" stands for.

## What it does not say

- It does not say fidelity across populations is meaningless. It says the
  tests must say whose fidelity they measure.
- It does not say bilingual or expert judges are wrong, only that they are a
  different receiver.
- It is not [QUESTION-005](../questions.d/QUESTION-005.md)'s question of who fixes the correspondence of
  contexts. That question is about τ; this one is about the populations
  whose judgements fill both sides of it.

## What would answer it

- A named receiver population and sampling frame in each design.
- Disagreement between judges modelled as signal, by modelling judges or
  groups of judges rather than averaging them.
- A design that separates population from transport: for instance, one
  population judging both source and target renderings it can read, or
  matched communities on the two sides.
- For historical sources, a statement that the source term is a modern
  reading, and a fidelity claim scoped to it.

## Note of 2026-10-31: outside readings that disagreement is structured, and what they leave to the record

<!-- inactive-ok-file: THEORY-tmpuh0eb THEORY-tmp5ux44 THEORY-tmpywwei CLAIM-240 — Proposed; readings filed on 2026-10-31, and the line's task-weighting claim, cited as open -->

Three readings filed on 2026-10-31 hold this objection's premise, that
receivers' disagreement is not noise, from outside the line:

- **Bencherki 2017**: "Concurrent significations, then, are not mere
  misunderstandings, but rather the outcomes of simultaneous individuation
  processes" ([LIT-tmp1rpyl](../literature.d/LIT-tmp1rpyl.md), p. 783; [THEORY-tmpuh0eb](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpuh0eb.md)). The example is the
  spouses' demand for a dental plan, "political activity" to the army and
  a legitimate demand to the spouses (p. 793).
- **Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy**: "Coexisting meanings of the same action
  indicate that several individuations are ongoing" ([LIT-tmp3mudg](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3mudg.md), p.
  1358; [THEORY-tmp5ux44](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp5ux44.md)). They set this against Weick's reduction of
  equivocality.
- **Massumi**: bodies cued by one event "will have been attuned –
  differentially – to the same interruptive commotion"; "There is no
  sameness of affect. There is affective difference in the same event"
  ([LIT-tmp8hcfz](../literature.d/LIT-tmp8hcfz.md), p. 6; [THEORY-tmpywwei](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpywwei.md)). Uniform response happens only
  where "other factors must have been active to pre-channel tendencies".

None of them supplies what this objection asks for: a named population, a
model of disagreement, or a design that separates population from
transport. They say why disagreement is to be expected, not how to model
it, and on the second the record already has more than they do. It
separates four sources of a split:

- differences of access ([CLAIM-458](CLAIM-458.md));
- of task weighting ([CLAIM-240](CLAIM-240.md));
- of interpreter history ([CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md));
- of the act a population's uptake partly constitutes ([CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md)).

Massumi's pre-channelling corresponds to [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md)'s adaptational history:
a shared history makes responses alike, and differing histories make them
differ. The correspondence is of structure, not of mechanism ([ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md)),
since his is affect before any content and the line's is judgement.
"Concurrent individuations" picks out none of the four, so it adds no
category a design could estimate. [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md)'s judge and group random
effects remain the record's answer to this objection.
