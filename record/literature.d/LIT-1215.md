# LIT-1215: Towards a Neuronal Gauge Theory

Sengupta, B., Tozzi, A., Cooray, G. K., Douglas, P. K. & Friston, K. J. (2016). Towards a Neuronal Gauge Theory. *PLoS Biology* 14(3): e1002400. doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1002400.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmp0gxyg).

## Why this is here

A19 of the exchange cited it in answer to the owner's self-hedged idea at U19 ("are we perhaps moving towards a gauge theoretic account of something here?"). It is the nearest published gauge-theoretic treatment of a self-organising system. Bears on [QUESTION-130](../questions.d/QUESTION-130.md) (whether evaluative transport between contexts admits a natural connection with nontrivial holonomy). A reading should check where its connection comes from, the question A19 itself raised ("Where does the connection come from?"), and whether its gauge freedom is more than the representational kind [CLAIM-148](../claims.d/CLAIM-148.md) holds.
