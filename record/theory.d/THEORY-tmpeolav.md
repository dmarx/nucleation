# THEORY-tmpeolav: In slices of striatum, visual and prefrontal cortex and hippocampus, Hebbian co-activation leaves a synaptic eligibility trace, lasting about 1 s in striatum, 3 to 10 s in cortex and about 2 s in CA1, that a later neuromodulator or plateau potential converts into a weight change and whose sign it can set: experimental support for three-factor learning rules on behavioural time scales

## Source

Gerstner, Lehmann, Liakoni, Corneil & Brea, [LIT-tmpovskz](../literature.d/LIT-tmpovskz.md), read in full in
[NOTE-tmpa6d2r](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpa6d2r.md) from the version of record: section 3 (pp. 7–11) and
Figure 2, reviewing Yagishita et al. (2014), He et al. (2015), Brzosko et
al. (2015, 2017) and Bittner et al. (2017).

## What was actually shown

Each experiment paired presynaptic with postsynaptic activity and gave the
third signal at a controlled delay.

- **Striatum.** Dopamine during or up to 1 s after the pairing enlarged
  spines; at 4 s it did not. The trace decays "on a time scale of 1 s"
  (p. 7).
- **Cortex.** Noradrenaline within 5 s of pre-before-post pairing gave
  LTP, at 10 s not; serotonin within 2.5 s of post-before-pre pairing gave
  LTD, at 5 s not (p. 9). The modulator chose the direction.
- **Hippocampus.** A plateau potential within about 2 s of CA3 input
  potentiated it by about 200% in five pairings (p. 10). Dopamine within
  about a minute turned an LTD protocol into LTP (p. 10), which the
  reviewers think may be consolidation.

What could have come out otherwise: a delayed modulator could have had no
effect at any delay, as the classical Hebbian picture expects, or the
window could have been the hour of synaptic tagging. In striatum and
cortex it was seconds.

## What this does not say

- **That these traces carry learning in behaving animals.** Almost all of
  it is slices with imposed protocols.
- **That eligibility traces are the brain's solution.** The reviewers:
  "there is nothing—apart from conceptual elegance—to favor eligibility
  traces over alternative neuronal mechanisms" (p. 12), and the results
  "do not exclude that other mechanisms work in parallel".
- **That the third factor is reward.** It was a neuromodulator or a
  plateau potential delivered by the experimenter. Whether in life it
  signals reward, surprise or novelty is open.
- **Anything above the synapse.** Nothing here is about processes
  recruiting processes.
