# LEAD-168: Construct three institutions in two arrangements alike in routine, one with enforcement independent of the executive and one in which the executive can condition it

## What it would do

Write the construction and find where the two arrangements first differ.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md): "one in which enforcement of corrective orders is
  independent of the executive and one in which the executive can condition
  it"

## What would count

The first difference answers [QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md) by construction.

Carried out by the assistant. [CASE-380](../cases.d/CASE-380.md) builds the three institutions

first differ depends on the agents, so two readings are filed as
alternatives for the owner to choose between. [CLAIM-978](../claims.d/CLAIM-978.md): with fixed
policies they agree under every routine probe and part only on an adverse
order the executive withholds, first at execution after identical rulings
(a hold parts timing first, outcome only past the deadline).
[CLAIM-979](../claims.d/CLAIM-979.md): with anticipation the first difference moves to the
ruling, and to the executive's act where independent enforcement would
deter it, so "alike in routine" holds only where that deterrence does not
bind. What remains: whether either reading, or a model of another kind,
does better than a conventional game-theoretic account, which A9 doubted
the construction would settle; and calibration to any case. No status or
stage changed.
