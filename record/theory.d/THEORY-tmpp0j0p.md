# THEORY-tmpp0j0p: In relational quantum mechanics as Di Biagio and Rovelli reformulate it, classical subsystems whose variables couple properly, so as to get correlated, come to agree, so that the merging of perspectives answers the combination problem and the facts held relative to different outside systems never conflict about the same variables

## Source

Di Biagio and Rovelli (2025), LIT-1676, arXiv 2510.11349v2, §VI (pp. 4–5);
read in NOTE-1214. What the paper computes is THEORY-1074, which this entry
extends.

## Why it is Proposed

Every agreement the paper shows is read off one joint distribution, relative
to a single outside system, where agreement follows from probability
theory. "Coupling correctly" is not stated in general, and no proof is
given.
