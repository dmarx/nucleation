# LIT-2424: Financing dies in darkness? The impact of newspaper closures on public finance

Pengjie Gao, Chang Lee and Dermot Murphy (2020). "Financing dies in darkness? The impact of newspaper closures on public finance." *Journal of Financial Economics 135(2): 445-467*. DOI-10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.06.003.

## Key takeaways

Skimmed on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1761](../notes.d/NOTE-1761.md)). Evidence that the local press's
monitoring of government has a measurable price in public finance, with
several identification strategies, extending the media-and-governance
literature from corporate to municipal finance.
