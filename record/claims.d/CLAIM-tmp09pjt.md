# CLAIM-tmp09pjt: Where the capacities a collective needs to govern an arrangement are learned by exercise and lost with disuse, and the arrangement does the work through which they are exercised, the arrangement can erode the capacity to govern it, so that whether a collective can correct an arrangement is not independent of the arrangement

## The claim

A8 §XVIII of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597):

> A civilization needs wise and capable human beings to govern its
> technological systems, yet those technological systems help determine
> whether people develop the wisdom and capabilities necessary to govern
> them.

The judgment that governing needs, A says, "requires the very
capacities—attention, reflection, practical knowledge, institutional
independence, and openness to competing values—that some technological
arrangements can weaken."

## Why it holds, given its condition

CLAIM-605 (Active, demonstrated) models a capacity learned by doing,
needing capacity already present to grow, and decaying with disuse.
Support that does the work has a deadline: after it, withdrawal no longer
restores the capacity. Take the capacity to be the collective's capacity
to judge and correct an arrangement, and the support to be the
arrangement itself, doing the work (selecting, assessing, deciding)
through which that capacity is exercised. Then, under CLAIM-605's
conditions, the arrangement can run the collective's governing capacity
below its threshold, and after that the collective cannot recover the
capacity to correct the arrangement by withdrawing from it, since
withdrawing is the correction it no longer has the capacity for.

The condition is the claim's interpretive part. A asserts that governing
capacities are weakened by some arrangements; whether they are kept by
exercise in CLAIM-605's sense is not shown in the exchange, and the
proposed study CASE-tmpf71w4 would test the trap for a single human skill,
not for a collective's judgment.

## How it sits with the record

- **CLAIM-462**: an arrangement can keep its capacity for effective action
  while its capacity to be corrected by others degrades. This claim gives
  one route by which the second degrades: the arrangement wears down the
  correctors' capacity, not the channels to them.
- **CLAIM-tmp6wuql**: two supports with the same present capabilities can
  leave different capabilities later. Here the capability in question is
  the one that would judge the support.
- **CLAIM-tmpwvg2o**: a collective's capacity to decline a technology can
  depend on the competition it is in. Both claims say a collective's
  self-direction over a technology is not settled by its own will at the
  moment of choice.

## What it does not say

- Not that any arrangement does erode governing capacity. The claim says
  it can, under its condition.
- Not that governing capacities must be kept by everyone. A division of
  labour in governing is consistent with it, as long as someone keeps
  them; whether a division of labour or a dependence is what results is
  CLAIM-603's question.
- Not A's remedy. A says the answer "cannot consist solely of better
  technologies or better institutions" and needs "a cultural and ethical
  transformation"; that is left with A's broader programme.
