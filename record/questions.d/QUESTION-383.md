# QUESTION-383: Which aspects of FACT's freedom–constraint duality survive in nonlinear, stochastic, hybrid systems with endogenous constraints?

## Why it is a question

A states the duality as "local, idealized screw-system relationships":
twists and wrenches paired by power, ⟨w, t⟩ = fᵀv + τᵀω; reciprocity,
⟨w, t⟩ = 0; C = F^⊥; dim F + dim C = 6 for independent ideal
constraints; in parallel F = F1∩F2 and C = C1+C2; in series F ≈ F1+F2 in
a common frame (Hopkins 2013, [THEORY-1374](../theory.d/THEORY-1374.md)). Each part is a statement
about first-order motions of ideal constraints. A's organizational model
is none of these: ẋ=f(x,u;z), ż=g(z,x,u), u∈U(x,z), constraints that
evolve with the state they constrain.

The readings show three places the first-order picture already breaks in
mechanics:

- **At degenerate configurations.** Chen and Santangelo ([NOTE-1614](../notes.d/NOTE-1614.md);
  [THEORY-1427](../theory.d/THEORY-1427.md)): "the degeneracy of the unfolded state makes a linear approach
  impossible there". Chen et al. ([NOTE-1624](../notes.d/NOTE-1624.md)) find extra zero modes "at
  linear order which do not extend to higher order".
- **Where stress decides.** Connelly and Whiteley ([NOTE-1588](../notes.d/NOTE-1588.md); [THEORY-1389](../theory.d/THEORY-1389.md))
  order first-order rigidity, prestress stability, second-order rigidity
  and rigidity, each implying the next and not conversely. Rocks and Mehta
  ([NOTE-1557](../notes.d/NOTE-1557.md); [THEORY-1414](../theory.d/THEORY-1414.md)) and Damavandi et al. ([NOTE-1570](../notes.d/NOTE-1570.md); [THEORY-1419](../theory.d/THEORY-1419.md))
  show prestress deciding rigidity that linear counts do not see.
- **Where the energy scale decides.** Silverberg et al. 2015 ([NOTE-1554](../notes.d/NOTE-1554.md);
  [THEORY-1399](../theory.d/THEORY-1399.md)): the number of degrees of freedom is "a variable quantity
  that changes with the experimental energy scale".

## What would answer it

For each extension (nonlinear, stochastic, hybrid, endogenous) and each
part of the duality (complementarity of dimensions; intersection of
freedoms and sum of constraints in parallel; sum of freedoms in series),
whether it holds, in what replacement form (cones or semialgebraic sets
for nonlinear constraints, second-order conditions at degenerate points,
distributions of admissible motions under noise), and under what
conditions. Results for some cells of that table would answer it in
part.

## What would not answer it

An analogy between an organization's options and a freedom space, or a
table of correspondences. A calls its own table "a conceptual comparison,
not a claim of mathematical equivalence".
