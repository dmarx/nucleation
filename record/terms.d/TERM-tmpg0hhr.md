# TERM-tmpg0hhr: self, and which sense

## Definition

"Self" in this record means one of the following, and a claim should say
which:

1. **Subject.** Being a subject, with a point of view or experience that is
   its own. This is [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s `self`, and the sense the dancers objection
   keeps ([CLAIM-tmpk62ey](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk62ey.md)).
2. **Dispositional, or extended.** What a cognitive agent is, including its
   standing beliefs and coupled resources. This is Clark & Chalmers'
   extended self ([NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)).
3. **Persisting.** Being the same over time. Coherence conserved along the
   system's own dynamics ([CLAIM-tmpdqsrb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdqsrb.md)), or unity across contexts
   ([CLAIM-tmp3u2m7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3u2m7.md)).
4. **Narrative.** The unity of a life as a story ([ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s `narrative-unity`).
5. **Self-governing.** Governing oneself by one's own standards ([ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s
   `self-governance`). A willed agent ([TERM-tmpllrr1](TERM-tmpllrr1.md)) has something like this
   without needing sense 1 ([CLAIM-tmp4g4je](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4g4je.md)).
6. **Integrated.** Many control systems acting as one ([ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s
   `behavioral-integration`). This is the sense of "the self as a
   multi-agent system" and of Ainslie's population of interests that is
   "just the resultant" ([CLAIM-tmpmf12z](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmf12z.md)). It is numbered last because it
   was added in version 2.

## What it is not

- **Not one property with five aspects.** The senses come apart. A firm may
  have 5 without 1. A dreamer may have 1 without 5. Otto's notebook bears on 2
  only.
- **Not "person"** ([ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s `personhood`).
