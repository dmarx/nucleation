summary: >-
  Kane & Lubensky (2014; arXiv 2013). The dynamical matrix of a
  ball-and-spring frame is QQᵀ, Q the equilibrium matrix; its "square
  root" has the symmetries of a topological band class, so gapped
  isostatic lattices are classified by winding numbers of det Q(k). A
  local index theorem follows: the zero-mode count in a region is a local
  count of sites and bonds, the classical Maxwell term, plus a topological
  count fixed by the bulk's topological polarization at the region's
  boundary. So zero modes can arise where there is no local mismatch of
  constraints and freedoms. A 1D rotor chain mapping onto the SSH model
  and a deformed kagome lattice show end, edge and domain-wall modes:
  floppy modes on walls of one orientation, states of self-stress on the
  other.

# LIT-tmpldjyt: Topological boundary modes in isostatic lattices

Kane, C. L. & Lubensky, T. C. (2014). Topological boundary modes in isostatic lattices. *Nature Physics* 10(1): 39–45. doi:10.1038/nphys2835. arXiv:1308.0554 (v1 2013-08-02).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpp5rni](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpp5rni.md).*

- **Counting is incomplete.** "Maxwell’s count is incomplete, though,
  because N0 can exceed dNs − Nb if there are Nss states of self-stress",
  and the corrected relation "is valid for infinitesimal distortions"
  (Introduction).
- **Two origins of zero modes.** From a mismatch of sites and bonds, or,
  "at a domain wall", "in a location where there is no local mismatch"
  (index theorem).
- **A local and a topological term.** ν_L^S is a local count and ν_T^S "is
  a topological count, which depends on the topological structure of the
  gapped phases on the boundary of S".
- **Stress localizes too.** The 1D interface of Fig. 1h has "a state of
  self-stress localized to the middle three bonds, in addition to floppy
  modes localized at either end".
- **Continuum theory is blind to it.** "Elastic energies are restricted to
  small wavenumber and cannot by themselves determine topological
  characteristics".

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on constraint architectures cites it, rightly, for
boundary zero modes in marginal lattices predicted from the bulk; it
leaves out that the count has a local term as well as a topological one,
that domain walls bind states of self-stress as well as floppy modes, and
that the boundary modes were known before (Sun et al. 2012) and this paper
gave their topological account. Chen et al. ([LIT-tmpchla8](LIT-tmpchla8.md)) carry the
topological polarization to origami and kirigami. Filed from it:
[THEORY-tmpmju6r](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpmju6r.md). No instruction for machine-learning practice.
