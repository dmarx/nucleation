# LIT-2231: How causal is “circular causality”?

Katrin Schmietendorf (2026). "How causal is “circular causality”?." *Frontiers in Network Physiology* 6: 1858616. DOI-10.3389/fnetp.2026.1858616.

## Key takeaways

- Descriptive and causal readings of slaving (after Stephan): the shift
  from one to the other "amounts to a post hoc ergo propter hoc fallacy"
  without further argument.
- Weak emergence makes enslavement reducible, diachronic downward
  causation; downward causation through constraints makes formed
  structures irreducible internal constraints.
- "Synergetics does not, by itself, establish ontological
  anti-reductionism".
