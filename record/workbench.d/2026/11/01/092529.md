The first filing of The Individual and Its Conditions put its prose in
OUTLINE and SECTION documents. Each section had a loose `draws_on` list.
The owner was not in love with it and asked to look at the other
registered works before brainstorming.

**What the other works showed.** None held prose in the record. What
Survives Translation? had 45 claims and 5 ARGs; The Organization of Will
had 16 claims, which point into the essay from their summaries ("Section
6.11", "the paper's main result"). Four works existed only as essays folded
into The Organization of Perspective, and that fact was a phrase in a YAML
blurb. A work had no status, no date and no successor. Against that, the
first cut had four faults: it inverted the convention, sections citing
claims where claims cite manuscripts; it was a chunked copy, with no tie
between a sentence and the claim it asserts, which is how [CLAIM-324](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-324.md)'s
silent drops happen; it restated the vocabulary blurb in an outline; and
it used credence to mean "draft".

**The options put to the owner:**
- (A) works as documents;
- (B) manuscripts assembled from claims alone;
- (C) prose anchored to the argument;
- (D) prose kept outside, with claims pointing in;
- (E) an audit.

The owner chose A, C and E. [ADR-067](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-067.md) records the decision.

**What changed.**
- [WORK-001](../../record/works.d/WORK-001.md) to [WORK-010](../../record/works.d/WORK-010.md) replace the vocabulary, in the order the works were
  registered. Each `works:` value on 146 documents was rewritten to a code.
  [WORK-006](../../record/works.d/WORK-006.md) to [WORK-009](../../record/works.d/WORK-009.md) are Superseded by [WORK-005](../../record/works.d/WORK-005.md); the claims keep them as
  history. The luria and self-as-recursive-ecology works are at stage
  `idea`; the rest that have drafts are `drafted`.
- [SECTION-001](../../record/sections.d/SECTION-001.md) to [SECTION-009](../../record/sections.d/SECTION-009.md) lost `works` and `draws_on`. Every paragraph
  now ends in `ref::asserts` and `ref::discusses` directives. Assigning
  them was a reading of each paragraph. Where the prose is more cautious
  than a claim, it discusses the claim and does not assert it: section 7
  discusses [CLAIM-329](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), because it says only that neither resemblance nor
  descent settles every identity question. Paragraphs that claim what the
  record does not hold are named in each section's revision notes. The
  most important is section 5's *defeasible constitutive warrant*, the
  article's most direct answer to [QUESTION-tmp9hvu1](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp9hvu1.md) and a candidate to
  file.
- The ledger, at its first render: [CLAIM-474](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) and [CLAIM-tmpx8nob](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx8nob.md) are what
  the article stakes that the record holds only as Proposed. Every member
  is placed, and no entry has changed since the prose.

**Fired before trusting.** On a sabotaged copy of the tree, dropping
section 7's anchor made the ledger list [CLAIM-329](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) as unplaced. Backdating
section 4 flagged its six entries as revised since. The lint patterns
were fired the same way; the result is in the pull request.
