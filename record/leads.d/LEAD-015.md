# LEAD-015: Derive the two-institution version of the symmetry-breaking condition, building the coupled meaning-influence model for two regulators

## What it would do

Extend the model to two regulating institutions and derive the condition
under which their symmetric state is unstable, as [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md) does for one.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md): "it is a local instability condition for [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md)'s unbuilt
  model, and the two-institution version has not been derived."

## What would count

A derived condition gives [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md) a worked model and may move its
warrant to demonstrated. A model in which no such instability exists
defeats it.

## Note of 2026-11-22: what [LEAD-090](LEAD-090.md)'s build gives this lead

[LEAD-090](LEAD-090.md), which this lead presupposes, was carried out as [CASE-tmpz4n6b](../cases.d/CASE-tmpz4n6b.md). The one-institution model is now built (scratch/studies/lead-090/model.py), with its mean-field map, its Jacobian, the closed-form threshold gσ² > 2(1 − φ(1 − ε)) ([CLAIM-tmp6f8w8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6f8w8.md)), and an exact rewriting as weighted averaging with stubborn sources and agreement-weighted adaptive weights ([CLAIM-tmpw3bak](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw3bak.md)). Its fixed points are a Curie–Weiss equation, so a two-institution version would be two such fields coupled, with its symmetric-state condition read from a 4×4 Jacobian of the same kind of entries. What remains is this lead's own step: to say how the regulator's evidence or resources depend on the regulated institution's state ([CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md)'s closing of the loop between the two) and derive that condition. No status or stage is changed.
