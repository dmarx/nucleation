# SYNTHESIS-021: The organizational-affect line is three accounts joined at the potential account of valence: what valence is in an organization, a persistent potential of its perspectives' negotiation; what affect a corporation can bear and what that does to the line between remorse and prudence; and when such a regime is felt and whose it is taken to be; the account of valence rests on the functional thesis about corporate affect, the account of feeling rests on the account of valence, and the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the organizational-affect line, over the accounts of its three parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)). The line was set aside on 2026-10-04 and reopened by the owner on 2026-10-23 ([CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)'s note of that date); the reviews of 2026-11-07 found it had no account (curation entry of 2026-11-07, 170741).*

## The parts

- **[SYNTHESIS-020](SYNTHESIS-020.md), the potential account of valence and its tests** (26
  components). [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md): goal-conduciveness valence is a
  persistent potential of the negotiation among an organization's
  perspectives that ranks its own states by their bearing on its
  maintenance, with the formal results that say what a record of wins can
  show ([CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md), [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md)), the concession that the regime tracks
  'wanting' ([CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md), [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md)), valence relative to a perspective
  ([CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md)), the claim and grant sides ([CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md)), and the objections
  on contest, flow, intensity, admission and cycles.
- **[SYNTHESIS-018](SYNTHESIS-018.md), corporate affect and responsibility** (9 components).
  [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md): affect can be a functional property of an organized system.
  [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) reads corporate psychopathy through it, with Wells Fargo
  ([CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)) as its case and [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)'s objection unanswered; [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md)
  and [QUESTION-053](../questions.d/QUESTION-053.md) ask what fixes such affect; the line's first framing
  ([QUESTION-019](../questions.d/QUESTION-019.md), [CASE-014](../cases.d/CASE-014.md)) is retired, and [CASE-024](../cases.d/CASE-024.md) is held without links.
- **[SYNTHESIS-022](SYNTHESIS-022.md), feeling and access** (7 components). [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md): binding real,
  owner constructed. [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md): a regime felt, in the access sense, when
  modelled. [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), whether functional affect must be felt;
  [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md), whether the two self-models are one; [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md), [CASE-033](../cases.d/CASE-033.md)
  and [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md) on compartmented access. Three of its components are
  shared with evidence-for-experience.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each route of [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s test (step 5) was tried
against links between the parts that no part account states.

- **Remorse as a claim or as a grant.** [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md) puts an emotion on the
  claim side: "The claim is the emotion whether or not it is granted".
  [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) counts a corporation as lacking remorse when others' harm "is
  not among the concerns its own appraisals track and gains precedence
  ... only when a regulator or a market turns it into a threat", while
  granting that "the signal can claim precedence, without being granted
  it". Read through [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md), [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)'s signals (the 2004 investigation,
  which traced cheating to the goals and recommended cutting them; the
  2012 HR project; the regional leaders' objections) would be functional
  remorse claimed and not granted, and the bank's profile one of remorse
  without grant rather than threat without remorse. The link turns on
  whether a unit's appraisal is one of the corporation's own. [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s
  own route through role duties says it is, and then the two claims
  disagree about the case. A contradiction between components is not an
  integration, so nothing is filed; it is recorded below.
- **Wells Fargo under the admission condition.** [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s part 3,
  since [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) was written in, reads a potential as the organization's
  only on a record shown to admit the contests that bear on it. [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)
  documents contests filtered and blocked on the way to authority.
  Applied, the cross-sell potential [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s note reads as the bank's
  is shown only as the admitted negotiation's. This is an application of
  a stated clause to a stated case, and adds nothing a claim would hold;
  it is recorded below because [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s note predates the clause.
- **Veit's identity and the concession to goal conduciveness.** [CLAIM-435](../claims.d/CLAIM-435.md)
  reads Veit's common currency as a compensatory regime of [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s
  potential, and [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md) places that potential's valence at goal
  conduciveness, so restricting the account to goal conduciveness does
  not take it out of [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s reach. Held in substance: [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)
  says "Veit identifies felt valence with the currency ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md));
  adopting that identity for this regime is a further step the record has
  not taken", and [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s defeater names the route. The remainder
  answers a position no entry takes.
- **[CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) as an answer to the objection that holds [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md).** Stated
  as not one: [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md) records that [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) "declines the phenomenal
  sense this question asks about", and [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s identity concerns that
  sense.
- **[CLAIM-548](../claims.d/CLAIM-548.md) against [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md).** That a process can activate without
  expecting benefit would let an installed harm-weighing procedure act on
  harm and not on prudence. [CLAIM-548](../claims.d/CLAIM-548.md) states a possibility for processes,
  and [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)'s demand, what keeps an installed concern answerable to
  its end, is about corporations; the discriminating test is already
  [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s part 5. It fails as a derivation.
- **Responsibility and the constructed owner.** [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) takes fitness
  to be held responsible from Pettit's authorised avowal, a collective
  self-description, and [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) makes the single owner such a
  description presents a construction. Joined, the corporation would be
  responsible as an organization and not as the owner its avowal
  presents. "Responsible as" is a free parameter: [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) allows the
  represented owner to match the distribution of control, where "Drayson
  is right and nothing is illusory", and no entry says what responsibility
  attaches to. Not filed.
- **The potential account and [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md).** If valence is read off the
  organization's record of wins, the potential account would fix
  corporate valence by the organization and not the attributor. It turns
  on the kind clause, an ordering "by their bearing on its maintenance",
  whose criterion [QUESTION-267](../questions.d/QUESTION-267.md) asks for, and on which contests are
  admitted ([CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)): where the measurer fixes either, [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md)'s
  note of 2026-10-18 already says the attributor comes back. A free
  parameter; not filed.
