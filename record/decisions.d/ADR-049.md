# ADR-049: A QUESTION is Answered by an Active CLAIM or THEORY, and a partly answered question is split into an Answered child and a narrowed Open parent

## Context

The QUESTION scheme ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)) has five statuses. Until 2026-10-21 two of
them had never been used: no question was Answered and none Dissolved. Of
240 questions, 229 were Open.

The promotion audit ([ADR-047](ADR-047.md)) has been moving CLAIMs and THEORYs to
Active, often after narrowing them. An audit of the questions on
2026-10-21 (curation entry of that date) found that 44 Open questions
already listed an Active CLAIM or THEORY in `answered_by`. None of the 44
was answered as posed. The Active entries answered one half, one model
class, or a sufficient condition where the question asked for a
characterization. In 16 of them the answered part could be stated
exactly.

Two things kept those answers out of view. The first is that `luria.yaml`
defined Answered as "the record holds an Active claim that answers it". It
did not say whether an Active THEORY counts. Read literally, a question
answered by the literature would need a CLAIM restating the reading before
it could be marked Answered. The second is that no rule said what to do
with a question that is only partly answered, so it stayed Open, and its
answered part could be found only by reading its body.

## Decision

**An Active THEORY can answer a question.** An Active THEORY is "the current
account", the best explanation the record holds. That is the standing an
answer needs. Answered means the record holds Active CLAIMs, Active
THEORYs, or both, that together answer the question as posed. A CLAIM is
the record's own assertion ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). It is not filed only to restate in
the record's voice what a reading already establishes. A CLAIM is still
filed when the answer is the record's own: a derivation, a combination of
readings that no source makes, or a negative finding drawn across several
readings.

**Answered means answered in full scope.**

- A sufficient condition does not answer a question that asks which
  things have a property.
- An answer for one model class does not answer a question posed for all
  of them.
- A negative answer is an answer: a counterexample to "must X?" answers
  it.
- The question's own "what would count as an answer", where it has one,
  is the test.

**A partly answered question is split.**

1. **The child.** The answered part is filed as a new QUESTION that
   `refines` the parent. It is worded to exactly what the Active entries
   settle, and it is marked Answered. Its body names the answering entries
   and says in what sense each one answers. The answering entries' `answers`
   links move to the child.
2. **The parent.** The parent is reworded to the part that is still open,
   with a version bump and a history note naming the child, and it stays
   Open. Entries that bear on the open remainder keep their `answers` link
   to it.

A split is not a reframing. The parent's remainder is the same question
narrowed, and its other children still refine it, so the parent is not
Superseded. Superseded remains for a question replaced as a whole by a
better-framed one.

**Answered is not permanent.** If an answering entry is demoted, Rejected,
or narrowed below what the answer needs, the question returns to Open with
a history note. If the remaining answerers still answer part of it, it is
split again.

**Size.** Splits follow [ADR-047](ADR-047.md)'s limit. A pull request files at most three
splits. Each split is one status change, the child's: rewording the parent
changes no status. Repairs to `answers` and `refines` links change no
status either, and can go in their own pull request. The audit that finds
the candidates is recorded apart from the changes it proposes.

## Alternatives considered

- **Require an Active CLAIM, as the old blurb read.** This was the first
  proposal. The owner rejected it. Every answer from the literature would
  need a CLAIM that restates a THEORY. Then the CLAIM scheme would hold
  assertions the record relies on others to justify, beside the ones it
  makes itself. That blurs the split between the record's own argument and
  its readings, which [ADR-032](ADR-032.md) exists to keep.
- **Mark a partly answered question Answered.** This overclaims. A reader
  who sees Answered would stop looking, and the open part would vanish from
  the open-questions view.
- **Reframe the parent into two new questions,** one answered and one open,
  and mark the parent Superseded. This mints a code that only renames the
  parent. It also moves the parent's other children, its history and its
  inbound links onto a successor, for no gain over rewording the parent in
  place.
- **Status quo: leave such questions Open and note the answered part in the
  body.** That is what had happened. The answered parts could not be seen
  in any index, and Answered was never used.

## Consequences

- The Answered blurb in `luria.yaml` says "an Active CLAIM or THEORY, or
  several together" and cites this decision.
- The audit of 2026-10-21 found these split candidates:
  - **Answered on Active CLAIMs:** [QUESTION-022](../questions.d/QUESTION-022.md), 100, 121, 220, 221 and
    222.
  - **Answered on Active THEORYs, now eligible under this decision:**
    [QUESTION-010](../questions.d/QUESTION-010.md), 052, 055, 067, 093, 103, 114, 115, 120 and 217.

  [QUESTION-103](../questions.d/QUESTION-103.md) and [QUESTION-114](../questions.d/QUESTION-114.md) may still want a CLAIM. [QUESTION-103](../questions.d/QUESTION-103.md)'s link
  to signal detection is the record's own, and [QUESTION-114](../questions.d/QUESTION-114.md)'s negative answer
  is drawn across several readings.
- Each split adds a question, so the record's question count rises with no
  new inquiry behind it. The child's `refines` link keeps the pair
  readable as one.
- An Answered question's standing depends on its answerers. A promotion
  pull request that narrows or demotes an entry has to check the questions
  that entry answers.
