# ARG-tmp2kh2e: CASE-191's pulse-installed capacity and CASE-123's one-time perturbation, as existence proofs, show that an outside influence on how an organization forms can leave an organization the target then maintains itself, and so carry the possibility half of the claim that reflexive control can act on formation; they do not show that a transmissible cultural expression can be the installing influence

## The inference

CLAIM-tmpp0ufw has two halves. The possibility half: an influence on
formation can leave an organization that the target maintains itself, so
the effect outlasts the source. The carrier half: the influence can be a
transmissible expression the target takes up.

CASE-191 is a coherent construction in which a capacity installed by an
outside pulse is afterwards maintained by the agent's own cycle and meets
later conflicts of its class, while "one installed by an outside pulse and
then self-maintained is told apart only by a knockout during formation".
CASE-123's one-time-perturbation variant has the same shape for an
institution's interpretation–authority loop. Both are existence proofs of
the possibility half.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes: both constructions are checked models.
- **Does it separate what the conclusion says comes apart?** For the
  possibility half, yes: formation provenance outside, maintenance
  provenance inside (TERM-100). It does not separate a formation
  influence from a decision influence by transfer to circumstances never
  presented. CASE-191's later conflicts "of the same class" are the
  nearest, and they meet the agent with the capacity already formed, not
  after an outside campaign aimed at a class of decisions.
- **The mapping premise.** The pulse in CASE-191 is a parameter kick in a
  model of one agent. Reading a meme as that pulse needs the exchange's
  thesis that an expression taken up can seed an organization without
  specifying it, which is filed from U1–U5 and is Proposed. Nothing built
  on the record has a cultural expression as the installing influence;
  LEAD-tmpuxizu would build one for evidence selection.

So the inference holds for the possibility half and is open for the
carrier half. The claim's status should not move on these grounds alone.
