summary: >-
  MacAskill, Thomas & Vallinder (2022). A short technical note that formalises the heuristic of *What We Owe the Future*, chapter 2. The value an originating event E contributes through a feature F of the world factors exactly as significance (ΔV/ΔT, value per unit of the extra time), persistence (T, how long F lasts) and contingency (ΔT/T, the share of F's duration due to E), with marginal and expected-value forms in which the factors are ex ante quantities, not expectations of the factors. An alternative makes significance independent of the counterfactual (V/T) and contingency the sum of the semi-elasticities of significance, persistence and the probability that F is present. The SPC estimate feeds the "importance" factor of the importance–tractability–neglectedness framework, and the note proposes reading tractability as overall easiness times "leverage". It assumes expected-value maximisation and makes no ethical argument. **status_note**: worth reading as the formal statement of how *What We Owe the Future* values an act's effect on the long-run future, read in place of the book; it gives the book's accounting, not its argument that future people count.
