# CLAIM-tmpnjaik: Shortcut learning is greedy agency at learning time

## The claim

[TERM-179](../terms.d/TERM-179.md) defines greedy agency as pursuing "the improvements its own
evaluation rewards without due account of what they do beyond it". A learner
that takes a shortcut does exactly that, in both of the term's senses.

| | Greedy agency ([TERM-179](../terms.d/TERM-179.md)) | Shortcut learning ([CLAIM-tmpgmvhl](CLAIM-tmpgmvhl.md)) |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal | discounts the developmental consequences of immediate gains | takes the earliest correlate that makes training answers reliable, which removes the pressure toward the truthmaker; [SYNTHESIS-105](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-105.md)'s overconsolidation, better now and harder to teach later, is the cost |
| Jurisdictional | gives no authority to the demands and standing of other affected processes | gives no weight to cases outside the declared family: they enter only through the family's statistics |
| Not cured by | any length of planning horizon | any more data drawn the same way |
| Cured by | giving the excluded parties standing ([CLAIM-1252](CLAIM-1252.md)) | giving the excluded cases standing in the family: data on which the correlate fails |

The paperclip maximizer is jurisdictional greed in its plainest form: people
count only through paperclips. A shortcut learner treats cases outside its
training family the same way.

[CLAIM-1287](CLAIM-1287.md) continues the parallel. A narrow optimizer comes to value
weakening the constraints that discipline it. At learning time, the
constraint is the part of the evaluation that would have separated the
correlate from the truthmaker. Reward tampering removes it outright.

## What it does not say

That a shortcut learner and a paperclip maximizer fail in the same way.
[DISTINCTION-tmp8wqwj](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-tmp8wqwj.md) separates them: one optimizes its objective badly off
the family, the other optimizes it well.

## Note of 2026-11-27: the record's fuller account

The owner pointed to the record's fuller account of paperclip maximization, in the exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" (dmarx/nucleation#837, not yet merged into staging), which this claim's search missed. That account centres on optimization jurisdiction, the domain of decisions a criterion has authority over. [TERM-179](../terms.d/TERM-179.md)'s jurisdictional greed, used here, is a neighbouring sense: others' standing, as against the criterion's domain. Two claims apply the fuller account. [CLAIM-tmpzh1sy](CLAIM-tmpzh1sy.md): a shortcut's harm is set by the jurisdiction it is granted. [CLAIM-tmpy41h5](CLAIM-tmpy41h5.md): the sycophancy-to-tampering path is that account's three-stage pathology. Once [#837](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/837) merges, this claim should name the account's entries by code.
