Under the dialectic regime, on 2026-11-23, while the manuscript
governance-of-possibility was being revised, the owner asked: "the JD2
declination has me wondering if maybe we're getting closer to identifying a
reasonable distinction between a collective that satisfies a pseudo-self
and an ecosystem of collectives that do but the ecosystem itself does not?

The occasion: SYNTHESIS-tmp4zamw declined "a polity as an ecological
pseudo-self" against TERM-tmpxwa7g's integration condition, while the
record also holds unity without singularity (TERM-tmpm0s7g, CLAIM-tmp31b3v,

## What was filed

- QUESTION-tmp4fkkx, the owner's question, in the owner's words.
- Two answers, as alternatives, the assistant's:
  - CLAIM-tmpcljke, structural: a plurality of pseudo-selves is integrated
    when some whole-level slow variable is maintained as the whole's
    (CLAIM-327's criterion: read, conditioned on, regenerated), and an
    ecosystem when its members are coupled only through each other's
    conditions of existence;
  - CLAIM-tmpptr3w, regime-relative and deflationary: integration is
    indexed by a probe family and a horizon (TERM-tmpxwa7g, CLAIM-157), the
    same firms can be coupled at a short horizon and integrated at a long
    one, and at a fixed regime there is no further difference in kind.
- LEAD-tmplrmhl, which tells them apart: the integration diagnostic at two
  horizons around a shared standard's adoption; a threshold at a fixed
  horizon for the first, a smooth change with the horizon for the second.

## Where the two meet

They are not simply rivals: the structural criterion is itself evaluated
under a regime, so it inherits the regime-relativity. They part on whether,
at a fixed regime, the difference is one of kind. The owner chooses.

## A correction in the turn's answer

The answer cited CLAIM-574 for the regime-relativity of a division into
parts. CLAIM-574 says distinctions only grow as nested probe families grow,
which is a different point; CLAIM-157, relativity to a stated target, is
the premise, and is what CLAIM-tmpptr3w rests on.
