# NOTE-tmpo8u7u: DeVault — SourceHut is leaving Freenode

## Contribution

A project's statement that freenode's new leaders are illegitimate, made in the post announcing its own move to Libera Chat the same day, dated by the document itself.

## Key results

- **The verdict.** "The Freenode network we once loved is the victim of a hostile takeover by corporate interests. We entirely reject the illegitimate new leaders who have used legal threats and back-room deals to steal the network."
- **The reason given for the destination.** "The dedicated volunteers at the heart of Freenode's success — the staff — have left for Libera Chat. We are sad to hear news of Freenode's fall, but proud to be following them".
- **The move, in the same post.** "We will be moving our channels to Libera Chat, effective today." Update of 2021-05-25: freenode's new staff "have been re-opening channels which moved to Libera.chat".

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. It is a statement by an interested party: what it
says of its own reasons is its stated reason, not its motive.

## Concepts

The continuation of a project apart from its name and steward.

## Bearing on the record

The study's first independent response in the freenode case: an identity verdict by a party outside the founders, on the day of the succession, and made in the same act as the speaker's own move. It names the producers' move ("following them") as its reason, which is CLAIM-329's criterion stated by a participant.

## Limitations

One document, by one party, on one day. It records a stated position, and
says nothing of how many people shared it.
