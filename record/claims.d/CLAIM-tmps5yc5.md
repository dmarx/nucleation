# CLAIM-tmps5yc5: Slow collective variables that govern an ecosystem divide into common drivers its members read and condition on but do not regenerate, such as tides, day and night, lunar cycles, seasons and imposed human cadences, which entrain them without integrating them, and variables their interactions regenerate, such as predator–prey oscillations, which meet the criterion of a constitutive collective variable, so the structural answer as worded is defeated by the second kind

## The claim

The owner's examples (CLAIM-tmpt93dp) are of two kinds.

- **Common drivers.** Tides, day and night, lunar cycles, seasons, and
  cadences that human activity imposes on an ecology. Members read them and
  condition what they do on them, but do not regenerate them: the ecosystem
  does not make the seasons. They fail the third of CLAIM-327's conditions
  ("by doing so regenerate it"). They coordinate by entrainment, and the
  record keeps coordination by a common driver apart from coordination by
  mutual regulation: the two "come apart under interventions on coupling"
  (CLAIM-tmptxsin's admission; LEAD-144 would test it), and a form constituted
  by mutual entrainment "is not thereby a self" (CLAIM-165).
- **Regenerated variables.** Predator–prey oscillations: predators read prey
  abundance, condition what they do on it, and by doing so regenerate the
  cycle. All three of CLAIM-327's conditions hold. Boundary effects at an
  interface can be of either kind: a gradient a neighbouring ecology imposes
  is a driver; an edge the two communities' activity keeps up is regenerated.

The second kind defeats CLAIM-tmpcljke as worded: an ecosystem with a
predator–prey cycle has a whole-level slow variable its members read,
condition on and regenerate, which the claim said makes a plurality
integrated. Either such an ecosystem is integrated at that regime, or the
criterion needs more (CLAIM-tmpklms4 offers what).

## What it does not say

- Not that common drivers are unimportant: they set the timescales on which
  regenerated variables run.
- Not which of CLAIM-tmpklms4 or CLAIM-tmpptr3w follows; that is the
  owner's choice.
