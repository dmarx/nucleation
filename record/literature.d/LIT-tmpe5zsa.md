# LIT-tmpe5zsa: Rubber hands 'feel' touch that eyes see

Botvinick, M. & Cohen, J. (1998). *Nature* 391(6669): 756. doi:10.1038/35784. PMID 9486643.

Not read: no lawful full text was reached (see the status note). The
summary is drawn from the publisher's abstract. What it says of the
questionnaire is Lush's description ([LIT-tmpozu5y](LIT-tmpozu5y.md), [NOTE-tmptyehi](../notes.d/NOTE-tmptyehi.md)). A
reading should check both first.

## Why this is here

It is the founding report of the rubber-hand illusion, and the source of
the nine statements, three "illusion" and six "control", that later
studies reuse. Lush ([LIT-tmpozu5y](LIT-tmpozu5y.md)) tests the control logic built on those
statements and finds it confounded by expectancy ([THEORY-tmpa88hs](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpa88hs.md)). A
reading should ask how the paper itself presented the six statements:
as controls for suggestion, or only as other possible experiences. Haans
et al., as Lush notes, take the second view. It bears on [NOTE-372](../notes.d/NOTE-372.md)'s C6,
the claim that body ownership can be manipulated by multisensory
conflict, which Metzinger ([LIT-450](LIT-450.md)) cites through the later full-body
work ([LIT-tmpeggnf](LIT-tmpeggnf.md)) that extends this paradigm.
