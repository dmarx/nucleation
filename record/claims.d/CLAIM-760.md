# CLAIM-760: That an organization makes an activity cheaper for those who perform it does not show that work was saved: the work may have been transferred, to an earlier time, to upkeep, or to other parties, and the efficiency an organization appears to have depends on the boundary of the system whose costs are counted; work saved, work transferred and valuable capability expanded are three separate achievements

## The claim

A62 §9 of the exchange "Topological protection explained"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afe6dd0-9d78-83ea-9782-f55680665dcf):

> Moving work into an earlier or persistent organization can redistribute work among agents, not just across times.

> A bureaucracy might make processing claims cheap for administrators by requiring claimants to perform more work expressing their circumstances in approved categories.

> So the apparent efficiency of an organization depends partly on the boundary of the system whose costs are counted.

> We should therefore distinguish work saved, work transferred, and valuable capability expanded.

## Admission

By a separating case. A claims office adopts a form that sorts
circumstances into fixed categories. Its cost per claim falls from 3 to 1.
Each claimant now spends 4 putting their case into the categories, where
before they spent 1 describing it. Counted at the office, the work
fell by 2 per claim; counted over office and claimant, it rose from 4 to
5. No one can do anything they could not do before. All three of the
claim's achievements are separately false or true here: work was
transferred, not saved, and no capability expanded. [CLAIM-303](CLAIM-303.md)'s own
criterion, reuse covering cost, is met for the office and says nothing of
the claimants.

## Rests on

[CLAIM-303](CLAIM-303.md), whose accounting this claim says must declare whose costs it
counts. [CLAIM-761](CLAIM-761.md)'s capability expansion is the third achievement,
and whether that expansion is an expansion of freedom for those affected
is [CLAIM-352](CLAIM-352.md)'s question, not this one's.

## The stronger reading it invites and does not support

That organizations which transfer work are wrongful. Whether a transfer
is unjust is judged against a declared standard ([CLAIM-363](CLAIM-363.md)), and the
contesting cost a transfer imposes is [CLAIM-731](CLAIM-731.md)'s subject.

## Note of 2026-11-19: readings for the exchange "Topological protection explained"

Ibrahim ([THEORY-2030](../theory.d/THEORY-2030.md)), read in her 2013 piece as a stand-in for [LIT-2823](../literature.d/LIT-2823.md): a collective capability, one "the individual alone would neither have nor be able to achieve", is a case of the claim's third achievement, capability expanded, apart from work saved or transferred. Alkire's objection that crediting a group with a capability "may overlook some significant dis-benefits or heterogeneities" is the claim's boundary point about costs, made about benefits. It supports the claim's third category; it says nothing on saved against transferred work.

No status change.

application of CLAIM-760's broader lesson. Work and discretion can be
