# CLAIM-tmpzxah5: Communicative objects are characterized through overlapping constraints on observables at partially ordered levels; translation transports these locally constrained structures, and repeated reconstruction changes both local interpretations and their global compatibility

## The claim

A81, proposal v5, titled at A84 *What Survives Translation? Observational
Geometry and the Dynamics of Communicative Reconstruction*: "Communicative
objects are characterized through overlapping constraints on observables
distributed across multiple, partially ordered levels of description.
Translation transports these locally constrained structures between
communicative environments. Its fidelity is the graded, directed preservation
of observational, pragmatic, and dynamical relationships, while repeated
reconstruction changes both local interpretations and their global
compatibility structure."

"The decisive conceptual change": "a global communicative object is no longer
stipulated in advance." A84's positioning of the contribution: "**directed,
potentially lossy transport between context-indexed empirical models whose
observational covers can change during communicative reconstruction**", with
quantum cognition reserved "for a dedicated section rather than the title".

## Why it was replaced

U31 brought information theory in, and A86 put the thesis in terms of
decision-relevant information ([CLAIM-tmp817r3](CLAIM-tmp817r3.md)). C6's abstract fuses the two.
