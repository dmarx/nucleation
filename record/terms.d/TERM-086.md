# TERM-086: organizational achievement, of a specified target: its attainment or maintenance, under declared conditions, where an identifiable arrangement of relations among components makes an explanatory difference that a comparison with admissible alternatives can show, as an episode, a maintained capacity or the reproduction of what sustains that capacity

## Definition

The owner, at U11, after A10 ended on "accountability is partly an
organizational achievement of making justified corrections findable and
executable before they cease to be possible": "we should probably
engineer a TERM for 'organizational achievement', and/or terms in that
neighborhood."

A11: "An **organizational achievement** is the attainment or maintenance
of a specified outcome, relation, or capacity to which an identifiable
organization of component activities makes an explanatory causal
contribution." "Organization" is [TERM-074](TERM-074.md)'s sense 1, a relational
arrangement, so the arrangement need not be one institution or an agent.
An attribution states five things:

1. **Target.** What outcome, relation or capacity, with success fixed
   independently of the candidate organization.
2. **Organization.** Which relations among components contribute:
   coordination, communication, constraint, allocation of authority,
   mutual regulation, transmission.
3. **Conditions.** The tasks, environments, timescales, resources and
   interventions under which it is attributed.
4. **Contribution.** The evidence that the arrangement, and not its
   components, a common input or another supporting organization, makes
   the difference: for a target J and context φ, compare p_O(J | φ) with
   p_O′(J | φ) for an admissible alteration O → O′ that holds components,
   resources, information and conditions fixed where it can. Redundant
   arrangements can show no difference link by link, and an arrangement can
   matter by sequencing or timing existing capacities, not only by being
   superadditive. Where no intervention is possible, mechanism-specific
   historical evidence can stand in, with its limits stated.
5. **Mode.** A11: "Achieving J, being capable of achieving J, maintaining
   the capacity to achieve J."
   - *Episodic*: the arrangement contributed to J on one occasion. It does
     not show a disposition to repeat it.
   - *Dispositional*: it sustains a capacity for J across a declared family
     of conditions, exercised or not in the period observed. One episode is
     not enough evidence of it.
   - *Reproductive*: it takes part in maintaining, regenerating or adapting
     the relations the capacity depends on. Neither stronger mode is part
     of the general term.

The modes are [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md)'s act, procedure and maintained capacity, read
as kinds of achievement, and the third is [TERM-074](TERM-074.md)'s organizational
maintenance. A11's three questions for any use of the phrase: "What was
achieved? Through which relations? What evidence shows that the
organization made the difference?"

## What it is not

From A11's list:

- **Not an output.** That an output occurred does not show a distinctive
  arrangement explains it.
- **Not collective agency.** The contributing arrangement can span people,
  artefacts, institutions and environments without a further
  decision-making entity ([CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)). A11: an effective system of
  judicial correction "may span all of those actors. But it need not
  coincide with any one of their institutional boundaries."
- **Not autonomy, or self-maintenance.** External support can sustain an
  achievement; whether its arrangement maintains itself is the further
  question [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) and [CASE-126](../cases.d/CASE-126.md) separate.
- **Not persistence or identity.** An achievement can cease while an
  institution survives ([CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md)), and one of the same kind can arise
  elsewhere without continuity ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)).
- **Not praise.** A11: "If our definition classified accountability as an
  organizational achievement but refused to classify an effective coercive
  apparatus because it is morally objectionable, we would have smuggled our
  normative preferences into the ontology." Whether an achievement is
  valuable or legitimate needs a standard the term does not supply
  ([CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md)).
- **Not emergence.** A causal contribution of an arrangement does not by
  itself establish a new individual.
- **Not organizationally maintained capacity.** A11 considered that as a
  second, narrower term and advised against filing it: "A new label is
  warranted only if work on different cases reveals genuinely different
  uses." Its content is the dispositional and reproductive modes here.

## Uses in the record it would discipline

[CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) calls authenticity "an organizational achievement characterized
by non-domination of subcomponents"; [TERM-006](TERM-006.md), deferred with its line,
defines organizational authenticity. A11: the term "can characterize the
**mode of realization** without deciding the philosophical significance of
the property realized", so it can say which arrangement produces and keeps
non-dominated participation without saying that this is ownership. The
phrase also stands, undefined, in [CASE-117](../cases.d/CASE-117.md) and [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md).

A12 proposed it as the first test of a `criterion` kind of TERM
([ADR-056](../decisions.d/ADR-056.md)): it is a criterion only while the comparison requirement in
item 4 stands; without it, it is a construct. And: "A TERM supplies the
criterion; a CLAIM argues that some proposed generalization holds; a CASE
supplies observations or a construction."

## Note of 2026-10-31: an augmented ability to act, with no target named

<!-- inactive-ok-block: THEORY-tmpcaccx THEORY-tmpkdsex THEORY-tmpuh0eb — Proposed; the readings filed 2026-10-31, cited as context -->
Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy define agency as "the affective force that
governs the transition from a lesser to an augmented ability to act"
(LIT-tmp3mudg, p. 1354; THEORY-tmpcaccx). Massumi's definition, from
which theirs is taken, runs both ways, to "a diminished or augmented
state of capacitation" (LIT-tmp8hcfz, p. 1; THEORY-tmpkdsex). The paper
keeps only the augmenting half, and the record's reading found that this
"makes a verdict depend on a judgment of whose capacity counts", which
the paper does not supply. This term shows what is missing. Read through
it, "an augmented ability to act" is a dispositional achievement with
its first element, the target, left blank. A11's first question, "What
was achieved?", has no answer in the paper's account. Its third, what
shows the organization made the difference, is answered by the
analyst's reading of hesitations and the eventual agreement.

The term also does what the paper's definition cannot. It is "an
attribution, not a compliment", so it can say what a channeling or a
usurpation achieves. In Bencherki 2017's daycare (LIT-tmp1rpyl, pp.
791–792; THEORY-tmpuh0eb), the army maintains a service that runs on the
spouses' volunteer work and is credited to itself, "a caring
organization at their expense" (p. 792). With the army's service as the
target, that is a maintained organizational achievement. With the
spouses' standing as the target, the same arrangement is the one that
blocks it. Both verdicts can be stated, each with its target. The
paper's vocabulary can state only one of them as agency, and the other
as its lack.
