# LIT-113: Duality and ontology

Baptiste Le Bihan, James Read (2018), *Philosophy Compass 13(12), e12555* — DOI-10.1111/phc3.12555

## Key takeaways

- Read naively, duality-related theories give rival ontologies. The interpretive options form a six-way taxonomy (underdetermination, discrimination or nihilism about the dual worlds, each with or without the common core as a candidate), plus two alternatives to the common core: an overarching theory, and a novel "pluralism" on which all dual structures are co-instantiated.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

A duality is a formal map between the solution spaces of two empirically equivalent theories, and dualities are pervasive in string theory and QFT. Read naively, dual theories disagree about spacetime structure, fundamental ontology and mereology, which raises underdetermination questions. The authors give a non-technical survey of the possible ontological interpretations. They offer an enriched taxonomy, several of whose options are new to the literature.
