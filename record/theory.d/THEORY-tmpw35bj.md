# THEORY-tmpw35bj: In Patrick, Knee, Canevello and Lonsbary's three correlational studies, perceiving that a romantic partner fulfils one's needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness goes with better relationship quality, with relatedness the strongest unique predictor and the partner's fulfilment adding to one's own; but the measure does not separate the needs, its autonomy and competence subscales correlating .96 once corrected for unreliability

## Source

Patrick, Knee, Canevello and Lonsbary (2007), LIT-tmpek1lj, *JPSP* 92,
434–457; read in NOTE-tmpi2z1s.

## What was actually shown

**Association** (Study 1, Table 2, p. 440). Eight samples, N = 1,918, 81%
women, corrected and pooled: overall need fulfilment with satisfaction
r = .72, commitment .57, perceived conflict −.61, defensiveness −.51,
avoidant attachment −.56, self-esteem .42.

**Relatedness first** (Table 3, p. 440; p. 449). Partial r of relatedness
with satisfaction .65 and commitment .59, against autonomy .11 and .07 and
competence .09 and .05; in the diary study relatedness alone predicts
post-disagreement satisfaction (β = .39) and commitment (β = .46).

**Partner effects** (Study 2, Table 5, p. 444). In 66 couples, the
partner's overall fulfilment predicts one's own satisfaction (β = .31),
perceived conflict (−.17) and defensiveness (−.14) beyond one's own;
relatedness fulfilment interacts across partners (satisfaction
t(62) = 3.19).

**Motivation as a carrier** (Study 3, Table 7, p. 451). Fulfilment
predicts the relative autonomy of one's reasons for being in the
relationship (β = .62); with those reasons controlled, fulfilment's β on
post-disagreement satisfaction falls from .42 to .15 and on commitment
from .41 to .02.

**The needs are not separated** (pp. 439, 441). Autonomy–relatedness .86,
competence–relatedness .84, autonomy–competence .96 (CI .88 to 1.0)
corrected, .68 uncorrected; autonomy's α is as low as .59.

This could have come out otherwise: the three subscales could have been
distinct, and each could have predicted relationship outcomes equally.

## What this does not say

- **That fulfilment causes relationship quality.** "all of the studies
  were correlational, thus precluding causal inferences" (p. 454).
- **That autonomous motivation mediates.** The predictor and mediator are
  measured together, once; the diary outcomes follow both.
- **That relatedness matters more than autonomy and competence as needs.**
  With subscales this collinear, partial correlations split shared
  variance; the paper's composite check (p. 441) addresses autonomy and
  competence, not relatedness against them.
- **That item overlap is ruled out.** It is argued (p. 441), not tested.
