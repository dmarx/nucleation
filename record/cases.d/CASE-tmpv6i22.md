# CASE-tmpv6i22: A smoke detector that sets off sprinklers in a building no one is in

## The case

The owner, at U2 of the exchange "Meaning Transmission Explained"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6af517f7-cb48-83ea-8395-df7d52333dc7,
2026-11-07): "let's use an alarming system as a toy model to probe this.
consider for example a smoke or fire detector that triggers an automated
mitigation like deploying sprinklers". "This" is U1's question, the
record's account of "how meaning can be transmitted independent of any
sentient emitter or receiver".

Smoke from a fire reaches a detector. The detector crosses its threshold
and goes into its alarm state, and the alarm state opens the sprinkler
valves. A4 adds a simplification: "assume the detector directly controls
the sprinklers, even though conventional sprinkler systems often use
independent heat-triggered heads."

Held fixed: the physical chain, smoke to detector to valve. Varied, in
A's variants below: what the alarm state is wired to, whether there is a
fire, and whether anyone is present.

## A's variants (A4)

- **Same signal, different downstream organization (A4 §2, §5).** System
  A's detector opens the sprinklers; System B's lights "a decorative
  light"; a third is disconnected. "Both transmit exactly the same
  electrical information." A4: "meaning isn't contained entirely in the
  electrical signal. It depends on the **relational organization into
  which the signal enters**." A4 grants that applying the record's
  fidelity "to a fire detector is an extrapolation beyond its principal
  linguistic examples".
- **The false alarm (A4 §3).** Steam from a shower sets the detector off
  and the sprinklers run with no fire. A4: "**Meaningfulness is not the
  same as truthfulness.**" The alarm reliably indicates that "the
  detector has entered its triggered state", not that there is a fire.
  The system can "*misclassify* the environment while still operating
  according to its control policy", and A4 asks "whether that norm
  belongs intrinsically to the system or is supplied by the humans who
  designed and evaluate it."
- **The abandoned building (A4 §4).** No one is present, monitors the
  alarms, or will ever inspect the system. A fire starts and the
  sprinklers deploy. "The causal organization is unchanged." A4: this
  "doesn't yet prove meaning is fully independent of sentience in its
  *origins*: people designed this particular system and assigned its
  protective purpose."
- **Which content (A4, closing).** "Does the detector's alarm mean *there
  is smoke*, *there may be a fire*, or *deploy the sprinklers*?" These
  have "different conditions of correctness".

## What it can show

That the record's operational sense of meaning applies with no sentient
party at either end. The detector differentiates, its state is retained
for as long as the valve needs it, and the valve enforces it: three of
TERM-089's four functions, with no observer presupposed. That is the rung
of QUESTION-285 A19 called use: "the record changes what the system does
next". The unheard tree (CASE-049) has no apparatus and so no rung above
the interaction. That is the respect this case changes.

What it cannot show, being stipulated and designed:

- **That the norm is the system's own.** What counts as a false alarm is
  fixed by the designer's purpose, which is A4's own caveat. Whether
  norms of function and failure can come from an organization's own
  dynamics is QUESTION-128's question. The readings the record holds on it
  are THEORY-220 (Bickhard: functions relative to a self-maintaining
  system, with indications that can be false "by its own lights"),
  THEORY-628 (Di Paolo: a graded norm needs adaptivity) and THEORY-217
  (Barandiaran: malfunction against a normative field). The detector has
  no viability of its own, so on all three the norm is borrowed.
- **Which sense of "mean" is in play.** Grice's natural meaning is
  factive: "x means that p entails p" (NOTE-735; THEORY-444). In that
  sense the steam alarm does not mean there is a fire, and A4's "a false
  alarm is still an alarm" holds of the alarm state, not of the fire.
  The sense in which a false alarm still "means fire" is a designed,
  non-factive one, nearer Grice's bus bell, whose timeless meaning
  THEORY-444 grants is awkward for the analysis.
- **That downstream wiring fixes meaning.** A4's System A and B show that
  what the alarm does depends on what receives it. That is the record's
  receiver-relativity in another medium: TERM-034 makes fidelity
  relative to an interpreter, and in Bencherki and Iliadis's reading of
  Simondon (LIT-1922, as QUESTION-285's note of 2026-10-31 quotes it;
  THEORY-1276) "The difference that is made is the signification". The
  case is an instance of both, not further evidence for either.

one.
