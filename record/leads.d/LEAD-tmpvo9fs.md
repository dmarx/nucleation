# LEAD-tmpvo9fs: Test whether corrective processes stay effective when their findings go against the interests of the authority that controls their resources, evidence, criteria, personnel or continued existence

## The idea

A40 §3.4 lists what an authority A may control of a corrective process R:
its resources, evidence, criteria, personnel, occasions to intervene and
continued existence. "These powers need not be exercised improperly to have
organizational consequences. Their availability can change expectations,
reliance and behavior."

## What it would settle

Whether control of a corrective process correlates its failures with the
controller's interests (CLAIM-tmplc4m8), and for which correctives
(CLAIM-tmpqhxsz): inside the executive, such as inspectors general and
independent commissions, and outside it but dependent on its evidence or
enforcement (CLAIM-404's informational route).

## What it needs first

A design that separates the controller's interest from the merits of the
finding: matched findings against and not against the controlling
authority, by controller's power over the corrective (removal protection,
budget control, control of access to evidence). The availability effect A40
names needs a comparison of correctives with the same findings and
different exposure. Not specified in the exchange.
