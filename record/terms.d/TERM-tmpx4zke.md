# TERM-tmpx4zke: constitutional regeneration, of an organization: its capacity to maintain valuable constitutive relationships through changes in the processes that realize them, while remaining answerable to justified claims concerning those relationships

## Definition

A40 §9: "Constitutional regeneration is the capacity of an organization to
maintain valuable constitutive relationships through changes in the
processes realizing them, while remaining answerable to justified claims
concerning those relationships."

A38 §6's first version: "an organization's capacity to maintain or
reconstruct valuable constitutive relationships while adapting the means
through which they are realized". A40 adds answerability.

## Its dimensions

A40 §9 separates six, "No single dimension establishes the others":
lineage (historical continuity), character (relational invariants kept),
capacity (the achievements still realizable), legitimacy (the arrangements
justified), correctability (inadequacies can become consequential) and
regeneration (these survive changes of personnel, environment and
implementation). A38 §6 lists six components: historical fidelity,
operational coherence, differentiated authority, reflexive inquiry,
normative answerability and regenerative capacity.

## Where it sits

- It turns on A38 §3's realization–character distinction: "Preserving an
  organization's character may require modifying its realization", as an
  SRE team keeps a service's guarantees while replacing its implementation.
  The record separates character, realization and lineage in [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md).
- Beside [TERM-163](TERM-163.md) (adaptive inquiry readiness), which keeps pathways to
  investigate future demands, and [TERM-128](TERM-128.md) (normative governability), which
  is the capacity to respond to justified considerations. This term is about
  what is kept through the response.
- A40 §4.5's "conservative transformation" (refactoring, migration,
  compatibility layers) is its method; A38 §8's "The object of conservation
  may itself be a capacity for becoming" is its slogan.
- Burke's "A state without the means of some change is without the means of
  its conservation" ([LIT-tmpus6zs](../literature.d/LIT-tmpus6zs.md)) is A38 §8's warrant for it.

## What it leaves open

"Valuable" and "justified claims" are not defined by the term: like
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s verdict, its use needs an independently justified standard. And
"maintain" needs a criterion of sameness for the relationships kept, which
[CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md)'s point applies to: the individuation is well defined only once
the criterion is fixed.
