# THEORY-2127: Language is an invisible-hand phenomenon

## The account

Languages are neither natural objects nor artefacts but phenomena of the
third kind, like paths trodden across a lawn. Speakers pursue local goals
under shared maxims; their systematic deviations accumulate and become the
new conventions. An explanation names the motives, the cumulative process
and the resulting structure, and predicts only structurally.

## Evidence

Theoretical argument with historical examples from German.
