# LIT-3188: Backup driver of an autonomous Uber pleads guilty to endangerment in pedestrian death

The Associated Press (2023, 28 July). "Backup driver of an autonomous Uber pleads guilty to endangerment in pedestrian death". NPR. https://www.npr.org/2023/07/28/1190866476/autonomous-uber-backup-driver-pleads-guilty-death

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-2389](../notes.d/NOTE-2389.md)).*

- The operator, charged with negligent homicide, pleaded guilty to
  endangerment, an undesignated felony, and was sentenced to three years
  of supervised probation by Maricopa County Superior Court Judge David
  Garbarino.
- Prosecutors had declined to charge Uber as a corporation.
- The prosecutor's case: "The defendant had one job and one job only",
  "And that was to keep her eyes in the road." The defence's: "There were steps that
  Uber failed to take".
