## What was found

Two works measure an earlier acquisition lowering a later one's cost
with the earlier cost counted (Hu and colleagues; Chen and colleagues).
The direction of the owner's example is not borne out by these: Liu and
colleagues find specialized data early and mixed better than a long
general stretch first, and Feng and colleagues find developmental order
within child conversation makes little difference. Neither is the
owner's recalled experiment; the search continues under [LEAD-tmpjdtrl](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpjdtrl.md).
