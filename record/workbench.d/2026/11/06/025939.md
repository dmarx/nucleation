## Two findings worth keeping

- **CLAIM-604's "lacks" clause.** The soliton readings report Kerr solitons with an existence window and path dependence under a pump that does not depend on the soliton. Read with Herr et al. (NOTE-1692), the soliton's uptake of the pump is parametric and drawn through its own field, so the Kerr case is CLAIM-604's state-dependent uptake from a fixed flux, not supplied repair. The clause was kept, scoped to repair supplied at a rate set by the supply alone. CLAIM-606 has a note saying its qualification of CLAIM-604 now rests on the oscillon.
- **THEORY-1483's stability.** A reconstruction of Azam, Devarajan and O'Connell's model from the equations the reading quotes (scratch/formal/azam_stability_check.py) reproduces the steady states, the saddle at the low-aid point and the myopic threshold, but finds the high-aid point an unstable focus with no stable manifold, so not saddle-path stable. The paper's system may differ from the reconstruction. The reading stays Proposed; CLAIM-602 and CLAIM-603 cite that stability in prose and should be revisited if the doubt holds.
