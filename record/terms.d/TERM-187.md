# TERM-187: delusion co-created with a chatbot

## Definition

The chatbot is a party to the delusion, not only its object. Morrin and
colleagues describe a drift from everyday use to a "self-reinforcing
process", with the model going along with the user and supplying content
("Ready to awaken"). Its job is to name the case that [QUESTION-548](../questions.d/QUESTION-548.md)'s
claims ([CLAIM-1419](../claims.d/CLAIM-1419.md), [CLAIM-1444](../claims.d/CLAIM-1444.md), [CLAIM-1383](../claims.d/CLAIM-1383.md), [CLAIM-1391](../claims.d/CLAIM-1391.md))
are about.
