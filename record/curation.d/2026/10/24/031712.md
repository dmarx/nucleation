**Corrections the lookups made to the exchange.**

- The "2026 randomized DBT skills-group study" is Amestoy, Gulamani and
  Uliaszek, online July 2025. Its 54 participants were university
  students, randomized to DBT skills or positive psychotherapy. The
  mediators were impulse control, access to emotion-regulation strategies
  and acting with awareness. Distress tolerance and the other mindfulness
  facets did not mediate.
- The 56 DBT completers are Edwards et al. 2021, a correlational study in
  an intensive outpatient programme.
- The "2026" review of 116 avoidance-learning studies is Mancinelli and
  Bach, a preprint of June 2025, in print November 2026. Its abstract
  favours inference-based over associative accounts and does not name
  expectancy accounts.
- The 2024 exploration review is Chou, Wilson and Smith. The increase it
  finds is in directed, not random, exploration, and in foraging tasks
  anxiety consistently goes with less exploration.
- De Kleine et al.'s 139 patients are 33 OCD, 40 panic disorder with
  agoraphobia and 66 PTSD.
- Bjørnskov, Voigt and Khesali say "unlawful" of the 115 declarations.
- The February 2026 five-country study is a research summary of the
  Israel Democracy Institute (Dagan and Benvenisty: South Korea, India,
  Hungary, Venezuela, Turkey). It is not peer-reviewed.
- Friedkin, Jia and Bullo's 2016 paper is "A Theory of the Evolution of
  Social Power: Natural Trajectories of Interpersonal Influence Systems
  along Issue Sequences".
- The Pierson the record reads in [THEORY-885](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-885.md) is the 2000 paper on
  increasing returns. The 1993 paper on policy feedback that the exchange
  described is registered separately.

usually cited as 1998. Bjørnskov et al. take 2021-10-02, the date of the
