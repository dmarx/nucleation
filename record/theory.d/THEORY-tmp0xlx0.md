## The account

Eyal (2011/2025) surveys the justifications of informed consent — protection, autonomy, abuse-prevention, trust, self-ownership, non-domination, personal integrity — and argues that each faces serious objections, so the requirement is best understood as a resilient invasive-refusal "core" plus a weaker, more overridable "rim" that may rest on different grounds.
