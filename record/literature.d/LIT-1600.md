# LIT-1600: The Supreme Court, 1978 Term

Fiss, O. M. (1979). The Supreme Court, 1978 Term — Foreword: The Forms of Justice. *Harvard Law Review*, 93(1), 1. doi:10.2307/1340507.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1364](../notes.d/NOTE-1364.md)), from Yale Law School's
repository.

## Why this is here

A8 used it to separate legal restitution from organizational reconstruction ("Reversal ≠ Repair ≠ Structural reform"), the distinction [CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md) and [CASE-172](../cases.d/CASE-172.md) draw, and as a challenge to restoration as return to an earlier state: sometimes the earlier arrangement is what violated the right.
