## What the skill should say

The readers found the same gaps independently:

- **A cluster with no question.** There is no test for when a cluster of
  readings no entry reaches calls for a question rather than a hold. The
  clusters were model-organism and circuits case studies (nine), Moral
  Foundations (four) and technical quantum foundations (about thirteen).
- **Premises of Proposed readings.** A reading used only as the premise
  of a Proposed reading the argument uses has no named kind of hold.
- **A regime set aside by an unregistered citation.** [CLAIM-535](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) and 572
  exclude dual control by citing Feldbaum, who is unregistered. A reading
  in that regime cannot `bounds` them, so it went to a raise.
- **ANTH hits.** Searching for `LIT-NNN` also matches `ANTH-LIT-NNN`.
- **A reading that weakens a case's reading of its own outcome**
  ([THEORY-1135](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1135.md) against [CASE-185](../../record/cases.d/CASE-185.md)) has no relation. It went to a raise.
- **Readings of a work's other results**, read beside the result a line
  uses, could be held as a set, so that later runs need not re-triage
  them.
