# NOTE-tmpomjp5: Baseball-Reference — Washington Nationals Team History & Encyclopedia

## Contribution

The standard statistical reference's franchise page: the seasons and records it counts as one franchise.

## Key results

- **The Nationals' franchise.** "Team Names: Washington Nationals, Montreal Expos"; "Seasons: 58 (1969 to 2026)". No Senators season is listed.
- **The check.** The Twins' page: "Team Names: Minnesota Twins, Washington Senators, Washington Nationals"; "Seasons: 126 (1901 to 2026)". The original club's seasons, including the years it was officially the Nationals, are counted with the Twins.

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. Statements by an interested party are taken as
their stated position, not their motive.

## Concepts

Continuation of a club by franchise (legal and statistical succession)
against heritage by place (a city's baseball history).

## Bearing on the record

The statistical count that league and press use, as kept by the standard reference: the Nationals continue the Expos, and the original Senators' seasons belong to the Twins. Coded C-EX and D-SEN (statement 9).

## Limitations

A reference site, not a party; it shows the conventional count, not why anyone keeps it.
