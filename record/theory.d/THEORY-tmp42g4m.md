# THEORY-tmp42g4m: Zhang and colleagues report that agents whose improvement procedure is itself editable (DGM-H) improved both task performance and their ability to generate improvements, measured by holding a meta agent fixed from a fixed starting agent and budget (imp@k), and that meta agents evolved in paper review and robotics transferred to Olympiad math grading with imp@50 of 0.630 against 0.0 for the initial meta agent, while the evaluator and parent selection were kept fixed.

## The account

Zhang and colleagues report that agents whose improvement procedure is itself editable (DGM-H)
improved both task performance and their ability to generate improvements, measured by holding a
meta agent fixed from a fixed starting agent and budget (imp@k), and that meta agents evolved in
paper review and robotics transferred to Olympiad math grading with imp@50 of 0.630 against 0.0
for the initial meta agent, while the evaluator and parent selection were kept fixed.
