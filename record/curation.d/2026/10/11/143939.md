The owner corrected the assistant's crystal analogy ("a crystal takes the
shape of its seed"), and then asked what to read on nucleation, crystal
formation and pattern formation. The assistant suggested a list from
memory. The owner chose seven works to file. Each was checked against
Crossref and registered unread and Deferred, on what it is standardly
cited for. No full text was sought. The anthology holds none of them (a
grep of its record/ at commit d8b5ba5 for each DOI and author found
nothing), and none of its topics holds crystal growth.

## What entered

The polymorph pair, on what a seed fixes:

- **Dunitz & Bernstein 1995, "Disappearing Polymorphs"** ([LIT-tmp0j1es](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0j1es.md),
  doi:10.1021/ar00052a005). Grounds [CLAIM-tmpas8q0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpas8q0.md). Once a more stable form
  has appeared, seeds of it can make the old form unobtainable. This is
  the strongest version of the narrowed counter.
- **Ostwald 1897, the rule of stages** ([LIT-tmpabr1n](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpabr1n.md),
  doi:10.1515/zpch-1897-2233). Not attached. A possible reading of the
  translation exchange's thesis sequence as successive metastable forms.

Nucleation itself:

- **De Yoreo & Vekilov 2003, "Principles of Crystal Nucleation and
  Growth"** ([LIT-tmplhx5f](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmplhx5f.md), doi:10.2113/0540057). Grounds [CLAIM-tmp2jolp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2jolp.md):
  the critical nucleus is where "threshold" comes from.
- **Vekilov 2010, "The two-step mechanism of nucleation of crystals in
  solution"** ([LIT-tmpom5dr](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpom5dr.md), doi:10.1039/c0nr00628a). Not attached. A
  possible refinement of [CLAIM-tmp2jolp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2jolp.md): recurrence forms a loose cluster,
  and the threshold is when the cluster is recognized. The assistant had
  first pointed to Vekilov's Crystal Growth & Design paper of the same
  year (doi:10.1021/cg1011633, "Nucleation"). This is the paper whose
  subject is the two-step mechanism. The owner later asked for the other
  one as well, and it was filed the same day (see the end of this entry).

Shape and instability:

- **Langer 1980, "Instabilities and pattern formation in crystal growth"**
  ([LIT-tmpqiriw](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpqiriw.md), doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.52.1). Grounds [CLAIM-tmpvc1pj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpvc1pj.md).
- **Witten & Sander 1981, diffusion-limited aggregation** ([LIT-tmpojehs](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpojehs.md),
  doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.1400). Grounds [CLAIM-tmpvc1pj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpvc1pj.md): tips catch
  what arrives next.

Order parameters:

- **Haken 1977, *Synergetics*** ([LIT-tmph4z7k](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmph4z7k.md), doi:10.1007/978-3-642-96363-6).
  Not attached. A possible picture of a thesis as the argument's order
  parameter.

## What was not filed

The assistant also suggested Kashchiev's *Nucleation* (2000), Mullins &
Sekerka 1964, Ben-Jacob & Garik 1990, Libbrecht 2005, Ball's *The
Self-Made Tapestry* and Thompson's *On Growth and Form*. The owner did not
choose them. The record already held Turing 1952, Cross & Hohenberg 1993,
Nicolis & Prigogine 1977 and Kelso 1995 before this batch.

## What a reading should do first

Four of the seven ground claims while unread. They are held for what the
physics standardly says, and each LIT names the check a reading should
make. Langer and De Yoreo & Vekilov come first, because the claims lean
hardest on them.

## Readings

The owner then asked for readings. Each of the eight was looked for in a
lawful open copy. Unpaywall and Europe PMC found one for Vekilov's
"Nucleation" (PMC2995260, an NIHPA author manuscript). Ostwald's volume is
in the public domain and scanned, unrestricted, at the Internet Archive.
The other six have no open copy and stay Deferred, each with the search
recorded.

- **Ostwald 1897** ([NOTE-tmpn2e2e](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpn2e2e.md)), read in full in German from the OCR
  text. The LIT is now Active. The version-1 takeaways were wrong in three
  places: the rule names the nearest form in free energy, not the least
  stable; a metastable intermediate waits for a seed unless it is labile;
  and the paper records no exceptions but explains them away. The word
  "Stufenregel" does not appear. The use the LIT was filed for, a stage
  reading of the thesis sequence, does not hold up. The seed experiments
  support [CLAIM-tmpas8q0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpas8q0.md) within the metastable zone.
- **Vekilov 2010, "Nucleation"** ([NOTE-tmpmm4u0](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpmm4u0.md)), read in full; the
  figures were images and not seen. The LIT is now Active. The version-1
  takeaways overstated the paper: CNT's error is placed in the prefactor,
  for one protein, and the classical barrier is kept inside the cluster.
  The paper also introduces a solution–crystal spinodal, which version 1
  missed.

Both readings complicate [CLAIM-tmp2jolp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2jolp.md): neither physical threshold is a
count. A note on that claim says so, and a note on [CLAIM-tmpas8q0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpas8q0.md) records
Ostwald's limit. Two unread LITs were corrected from the readings
([LIT-tmpom5dr](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpom5dr.md)'s "short-lived" clusters) or given what the readings say
about them ([LIT-tmplhx5f](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmplhx5f.md), [LIT-tmp0j1es](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0j1es.md)). Each draft NOTE was written by a
separate agent from the full text, then checked by quoting its key
passages against the source before filing.
