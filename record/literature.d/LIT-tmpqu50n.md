summary: >-
  Di Biagio & Rovelli (2021), Found. Phys. 51:30. A fact happens at every
  interaction and is relative to the system it affects. Facts relative to
  F are stable for another system W when W may use them in the classical
  total-probability rule; the rule's failure is interference. If F is
  entangled with an environment whose states overlap by at most ε and W
  does not interact with it, the rule holds for W up to O(ε): stability is
  approximate and relational, and a fact can be stable for W before it is
  a fact for W. The ontology is put in relative facts, not stable ones, and
  the Frauchiger–Renner contradiction and the Local Friendliness violation
  are read as the classical rule used across contexts where facts are not
  stable.

# LIT-tmpqu50n: Stable Facts, Relative Facts

Di Biagio, A., & Rovelli, C. (2021). Stable facts, relative facts. *Foundations of Physics*, 51(1), 30. doi:10.1007/s10701-021-00429-w.

Read in full on 2026-10-27 (NOTE-tmppgt2i).

## Why this is here

A19 §3 called it "extraordinarily close to our multiscale thesis": a
relation established by interaction becomes stable enough for an
effective observer-independent description. A19 and A23 §VII used it for
observation without a conscious observer. It is the record's next step
past Rovelli 1996 ([LIT-tmp57r0s](LIT-tmp57r0s.md), read). It bears on [QUESTION-tmpubsi9](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpubsi9.md)
and [CLAIM-tmpmx2ql](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmx2ql.md).
