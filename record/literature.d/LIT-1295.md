# LIT-1295: A Contraction Theory Approach to Singularly Perturbed Systems

Del Vecchio, D. & Slotine, J.-J. E. (2013). A Contraction Theory Approach to Singularly Perturbed Systems. *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 58(3): 752–757. doi:10.1109/TAC.2012.2211444. Conference version: Proc. 50th IEEE CDC-ECC (2011), pp. 5831–5836, doi:10.1109/CDC.2011.6160340, open at MIT DSpace (hdl 1721.1/79864).

Read in full in [NOTE-1020](../notes.d/NOTE-1020.md), from the authors' revised manuscript.

## Key takeaways

- Contraction turns singular perturbation's local, "ε small enough"
  statements into global bounds with explicit constants, valid at any ε
  (Lemma 2, Theorem 3).
- The fast state approaches the slow manifold at rate λ_z/ε and then stays
  within εχ_z(d + K)/λ_z of it, where d bounds how fast the manifold moves
  (Lemma 2).
- The quasi-steady-state reduction, a memoryless (Markovian) model of the
  slow state, is accurate to O(ε) uniformly in time, but only if the
  reduced system itself is contracting. Partial contraction of f in x, the
  hypothesis printed, is not enough (counterexample in [NOTE-1020](../notes.d/NOTE-1020.md)).

## Standing in the record

It is the published proof [THEORY-771](../theory.d/THEORY-771.md)'s promotion condition names
for the robustness lemma that Bousquet and Slotine ([LIT-1292](LIT-1292.md)) build
on. It proves that lemma for constant metrics only. The general-metric
version that Bousquet and Slotine's hypotheses need, and the passage of the
fast state to O(ε), are re-derived in [NOTE-1020](../notes.d/NOTE-1020.md), not taken from
the paper. [THEORY-773](../theory.d/THEORY-773.md) holds the corrected Theorem 3.
