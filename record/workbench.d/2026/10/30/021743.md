**Checks run here.**

- **The [THEORY-265](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-265.md) model** was re-run from [NOTE-773](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-773.md)'s equations. Results:
  - Learned phases reach the global optimum in 50 of 50 test runs, and so
    does synchrony matched to the modules and imposed by hand.
  - Random phases and shuffled phases reach it in 0 of 50.
  - Global synchrony reaches it in 5 to 19 of 50, as a transient
    system-wide reset rather than modules responding to one another.
  - The self-coupling variant behaves the same way.
  - The effect holds only where modules are isolated enough to lock in:
    random phases already reach the optimum once the coupling across
    modules is 0.1 or more.
