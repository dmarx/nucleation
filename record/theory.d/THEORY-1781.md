# THEORY-1781: - **Sentence**: Edge et al. (2024) propose GraphRAG, which answers whole-corpus questions by summarizing a hierarchy of communities in an LLM-extracted entity graph and combining the community answers, and report that LLM judges prefer its answers to vector RAG's on comprehensiveness and diversity, while claim-based measures find no significant difference from map-reduce summarization of the source text, over which its advantage is lower token cost. -

## The account

- **Sentence**: Edge et al. (2024) propose GraphRAG, which answers whole-corpus questions by summarizing a hierarchy of communities in an LLM-extracted entity graph and combining the community answers, and report that LLM judges prefer its answers to vector RAG's on comprehensiveness and diversity, while claim-based measures find no significant difference from map-reduce summarization of the source text, over which its advantage is lower token cost.
