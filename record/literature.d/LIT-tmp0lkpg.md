# LIT-tmp0lkpg: A Mathematical Theory of Communication

C. E. Shannon (1948), *Bell System Technical Journal* 27(3):379–423 and
27(4):623–656 — DOI-10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for: Shannon
models communication as a source, encoder, channel, decoder and destination,
takes entropy as the measure of the information a source produces, and proves
that sources can be compressed to their entropy and that a noisy channel has a
capacity below which reliable communication is possible.
[LIT-317](LIT-317.md), the 1949 "Communication in the Presence of Noise", is
held separately.
