**What the sources show about the paper.** Its quotations are exact where
they could be checked, but several are used for more than they say.
- **Massumi.** The paper's definition is the Spinozan formula, offered as
  a proposition and running both ways. Massumi denies that affect is
  transmitted.
- **Hernes.** "Agency without agents" is an objection he anticipates, not
  his thesis.
- **Tsoukas & Chia.** Their organizational becoming is carried by
  reflexive actors.
- **Wilhoit.** She does not say that Cooren's analyses entify agents.
- **Other citations.**
  - Iliadis is cited for "organizational realities" he does not discuss.
  - "Disindividuation (Simondon, 1989)" is, in Bencherki 2017,
    Barthélémy's gloss.
  - Letiche & Moriceau's doubt about transduction is omitted.

[NOTE-1484](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1484.md) records each of these in its section "The sources, read".
