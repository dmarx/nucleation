# LIT-tmp9dwb5: Re-Engineering Philosophy for Limited Beings: Piecewise Approximations to Reality

William C. Wimsatt (2007). *Re-Engineering Philosophy for Limited Beings: Piecewise Approximations to Reality*. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press. DOI-10.2307/j.ctv1pncnrh.

## Key takeaways

*Seeded from the citing paper, not read.* Griesemer and Shavit ([LIT-188](LIT-188.md)) take two concepts from it. Generative entrenchment: a core element that later-evolved ones "depend on for their existence and character, so that perturbations of it will tend to be catastrophic to the whole system while changes in the later interactions tend to lead to tolerable system changes" (§2). Aggregativity: contributions that sum without depending on their organization, the mark of coordination in a collection (§1). Wimsatt's paper on aggregativity is held as [LIT-707](LIT-707.md), Unavailable.
