# LIT-tmpoyd9z: Responsibility Incorporated

Philip Pettit (2007), *Ethics* 117(2):171–201 — DOI-10.1086/510695

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* The argument is read in Pettit's 2017 chapter
([LIT-tmp486vh](LIT-tmp486vh.md)), which restates it. Those are the conditions of a
significant choice, the capacity to make a normative judgment about the
options, and control over acting on that judgment. It argues that a
corporation meets them because it must form a mind of its own, not borrow
one from any mechanical aggregation of its members' votes.
