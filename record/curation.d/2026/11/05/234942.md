The owner asked the assistant to "proceed systematically filling all of
those identified gaps". One gap was assistance that does the work and the
loss of the skill it replaces, in instruction, firms and automation.

- **Read.**

  - Griffith, Redding and Van Reenen 2004 (LIT-tmpzbgxy). It stands in
    only for the core claim of Cohen and Levinthal 1990 (LIT-tmpwa0ib,
    Unavailable).
  - Macnamara et al. 2024 (LIT-tmpbg80l), Ahn 2025 (LIT-tmp09gwf) and
    Simkute, Tankelevitch et al. 2024 (LIT-tmpaw2id). They stand in poorly
    for Bainbridge 1983 (LIT-tmph5vta, Unavailable), and nothing is put in
    her name from her own words.
- **Unavailable.** Wood, Bruner and Ross 1976 (LIT-tmpri7lu): Wiley's
  bronze copy returned a Cloudflare check.
- **What the readings change.**
  - **Capacity-first is the tutors' rule.** They hold that help should be
    withheld only sparingly, and help the learner controls turns into
    substitution.
  - **Disuse inside daily work.** Disuse can sit inside daily work at the
    level of a subtask.
  - **An unplanned withdrawal.** Automation hands work back at its
    failures, a withdrawal at the hardest case.
