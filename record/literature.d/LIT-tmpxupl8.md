# LIT-tmpxupl8: Evolution of koinobiont parasitoid host regulation and consequences for indirect plant defence

Maximilien A. C. Cuny and Erik H. Poelman (2022). "Evolution of koinobiont
parasitoid host regulation and consequences for indirect plant defence"
*Evolutionary Ecology 36(3): 299-319, review*.
DOI-10.1007/s10682-022-10180-x.

## Key takeaways

Parasitoids regulate host development and feeding with an effector toolkit,
and the effect reaches the plant the host eats.
