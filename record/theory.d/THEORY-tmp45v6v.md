# THEORY-tmp45v6v: Shenhav, Botvinick and Cohen propose that control is allocated top-down by a dedicated specifying system: dACC computes the expected value of a control signal, its probability-weighted payoff less an intrinsic cost rising with intensity, and specifies the signal that lateral prefrontal and subcortical structures implement

## Source

Shenhav, Botvinick and Cohen (2013), LIT-585, read in full in NOTE-446: the
model (Eqs. 1–3; the retrieved text drops the equations themselves), §§4–7
and the open questions.

## The proposal

A control signal has an identity, the parameter it targets, and an
intensity, how far it displaces that parameter from its default. Its
expected value is the sum over outcomes of their probability given the
signal and the state, times their value, minus a cost that rises with
intensity: "like physical effort, mental effort is assumed to carry
intrinsic disutility". Value is recursive, immediate reward plus discounted
future EVC. dACC monitors control-relevant states and specifies the signal;
lateral prefrontal cortex and subcortical structures implement it.

So the proposal places the allocation of control in a dedicated specifier
above the structures it directs. That placement is what CLAIM-159 uses: it
puts this account in the top-down cell of the drive profile.

## What this does not say

It does not say the proposal is true. The paper "reports no new data" and
its model "is stated, not fitted". A fit of the computation and a
dissociation of specifying from implementing are THEORY-070's
`promote_when`, and THEORY-070 stays Proposed until the record holds them.
