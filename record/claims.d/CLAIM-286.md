# CLAIM-286: Habit, akrasia, rumination, rigidity and insight can be modelled as regimes of one organization, in which processes transform shared meaning and allocate one another's influence, with trust, specialization and higher-level agency arising from its history and not imposed

## The claim

A7: "A self may be modeled as a metastable, recursively compositional
organization of heterogeneous processes that transform meaning and regulate
one another's opportunities to influence subsequent cognition and action.
Authority, trust, specialization, and higher-level agency arise endogenously
through history-dependent interaction rather than being imposed by a
privileged executive." Its §9: "**General hypothesis:** Several psychological
phenomena typically assigned to different faculties can be modeled as
distinct dynamical regimes of a shared organizational architecture."

A5 §4 gave the first mappings, "explanatory hypotheses, not established
mappings":

- ambivalence as coalitions endorsing incompatible actions;
- habit as historically reliable policies keeping influence with little
  evaluation;
- rumination as a recurrent coalition re-exciting a narrow trajectory;
- insight as weakly coupled regions becoming connected;
- identity change as a durable reconfiguration of the influence topology.

A6 added cascades (Hawkes processes), and A7 §9 procrastination and akrasia.

The record already reads several of these this way: procrastination
([CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md), [CLAIM-047](CLAIM-047.md)), lock-in ([TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md), [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md)) and preference
reversal ([CLAIM-198](CLAIM-198.md)). What this claim adds is that one set of variables
should cover them all. A5 §8 gave them: persistence of the influence
topology, responsiveness to feedback, diversity of influential processes,
concentration of authority and cross-regional transport. It bears on
[QUESTION-088](../questions.d/QUESTION-088.md), whose test is the same as this claim's defeater.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the organization is a self. A7: "A useful theory of
  psychological organization should stand even if its constitutive claims
  remain contested." The constitutive half is [CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md).
- It does not say the mappings are established. A7 §14: "Computational
  similarity to a phenomenon is insufficient evidence that the same
  mechanism explains the human case."
- It is under A7 §14's objection, which A7 granted, filed as [CLAIM-278](CLAIM-278.md)
  and now [ARG-036](../arguments.d/ARG-036.md), which finds the lab's evidence does not yet support it.

## Note of 2026-10-29: what holds it back

No prediction in the record follows from the shared organizational
variables and not from phenomenon-specific or central-executive models
with flexibility penalized, which is this claim's defeated_if and
[QUESTION-088](../questions.d/QUESTION-088.md)'s test. The one argument concluding it, [ARG-036](../arguments.d/ARG-036.md), fails its
own critical question (the lab's matched comparators find the
organizational vocabulary adds no constraint), and [CLAIM-467](CLAIM-467.md) undercuts
it.

## Note of 2026-11-04: an application to maladaptation, from "Organizational Nucleation and SLT"

The owner's U29: "apply our framework interpretively to explain maladaptive
behaviors, cognitive dysregulation, etc." A29 ran this claim's programme
uncited: "A behavior can be locally appropriate to the concerns of one
motivating perspective while being maladaptive for the individual as a
dynamically organized whole." Its seven regimes are "not psychiatric
diagnoses". Most are on file: corrective lock-in is [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md), avoidance is
[CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md) and [CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md), release is [CLAIM-465](CLAIM-465.md), and the proposed simulation is
[CASE-153](../cases.d/CASE-153.md). New: rumination split into reactivation and failed release, and
capacity kept apart from governance ([CLAIM-537](CLAIM-537.md)'s note). Stated as fact
without support: "Rumination can recruit mechanisms ordinarily involved in
detecting and resolving problems", and that attention and control are
"limited resources", a contested modelling choice.

- **Cognitive dysregulation.** (a) Read as attentional (§7). (b) In DBT's
  sense, A would have proposed that high activation narrows the
  interpretation governance can draw on. At the owner's review this is
  its own claim, [CLAIM-551](CLAIM-551.md), standing beside (a). (c) None.
- **Experiential avoidance.** (a) §2 avoids a task. (b) In ACT's sense
  ([THEORY-050](../theory.d/THEORY-050.md)) the protective perspective avoids anticipated private
  events. (c) None.
- **Endorsement.** (a) §6's "reflective endorsement" is Frankfurt's. (b) In
  [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s sense, now conferral ([TERM-103](../terms.d/TERM-103.md)): why is the reflective process's effect on the pursuit
  process's future influence near zero, though its evaluation is negative
  ([CLAIM-447](CLAIM-447.md), [CASE-181](../cases.d/CASE-181.md))? (c) None.

The wording is A's, sharpened by the reader; adopting it is the owner's
call. No status changes.
