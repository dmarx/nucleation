**The owner's prompt**, verbatim, quoting the assistant's previous answer:
"> I didn't edit the text of the older essay claims. "Agent" there is the
paper's own term, and rewriting it would change what the will paper said.
where we are invoking a concept specifically as its defined on the record,

**What moved.** The rule is filed as an ADR, Proposed for the owner. It
resolves the older-claims question. The paper's "agent" stays unlinked
because it is not the record's defined agent. The paper's "will-organization",
"action-generating regime" and "precedence, jurisdiction and suspension"
*are* record-defined, and now carry their codes. A script matched
distinctive TERM phrases across the argument schemes. Twenty-five sites were
linked after reading and one was excluded: "will-organization line" names
the line, not the concept.

**What reading caught that the script could not.** The `uses:` sync proposed
seventeen additions. Four were refused because those claims cite the term
as history or contrast, not as the language they are stated in. Inline
citation and `uses:` are different relations, and the ADR says so. The
multi-version terms, fidelity, frame and communicative object, were not
swept. Which version a passage means is a reading judgement.
