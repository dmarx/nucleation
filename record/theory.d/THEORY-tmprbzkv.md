# THEORY-tmprbzkv: van der Weele argues that care theorists' conceptions of dependency diverge because each rests on a different empirical paradigm case (the infant, the physically disabled person, the profoundly intellectually disabled person, the refugee), each bringing its own ethical sensibility toward care; Kittay's paradigm of complete, one-way dependence makes care a social responsibility

## The account

van der Weele argues that care theorists' conceptions of dependency diverge because each rests on a different empirical paradigm case (the infant, the physically disabled person, the profoundly intellectually disabled person, the refugee), each bringing its own ethical sensibility toward care; Kittay's paradigm of complete, one-way dependence makes care a social responsibility

The skimmed reading in NOTE-tmp4gf0a.

## What it does not say

Kittay's own argument about the Family and Medical Leave Act, or that a whole owes its parts care as parts.
