# CLAIM-458: A reception split between populations with unequal access to the source is partly a difference of access and not of weighting, so a default task's weights can be estimated only on the components every population compared can perceive

## The claim

[CLAIM-240](CLAIM-240.md) holds that a holistic fidelity judgement is fidelity under the
judges' default task, a weighting of the profile's components that can be
estimated on some renderings and tested on others. Its note of 2026-10-10,
on [CASE-067](../cases.d/CASE-067.md), Deborah Smith's *The Vegetarian*: "It also shows a limit of
the model. Part of the split is access, not weights. Some divergences,
such as the confusion of 'arm' (pal) and 'foot' (bal), are invisible to
readers without Korean ('Most English readers will simply glide over them
unaware'). For those components the Anglophone weight is zero by
blindness, not by a default task."

The objection is that a model which weights the components judges
perceive does not cover a population that cannot perceive some of them,
so where access differs, a fitted difference in weights can be a
difference in access. A comparison of two populations' default tasks
then has to separate the components both can perceive from those only
one can, and fit weights on the first kind only, as the note says. Without
that, [CLAIM-240](CLAIM-240.md)'s estimates are confounded across exactly the populations
reception splits are about.

## What it does not say

- **That holistic judgement is not a default task's weighting.** Within a
  population, or on components every population perceives, [CLAIM-240](CLAIM-240.md)
  stands.
- **That access explains the whole split.** The Booker judges' "on their
  own terms" against comparison in Korea is a difference of task too, as
  the case records.
- **A measure of access.** Deciding which components a population can
  perceive is itself an empirical step the objection requires and does not
  supply.
