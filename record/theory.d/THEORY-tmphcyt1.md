# THEORY-tmphcyt1: Moral judgment draws on several innately prepared intuitive foundations, at least Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority and Sanctity, each a response to a different adaptive challenge, and Sanctity descends from pathogen avoidance rather than from cooperation

## Source

Graham, Haidt, Koleva, Motyl, Iyer, Wojcik & Ditto (2013), [LIT-499](../literature.d/LIT-499.md), read in
full in [NOTE-396](../notes.d/NOTE-396.md). Haidt & Joseph (2004), [LIT-502](../literature.d/LIT-502.md), read in [NOTE-400](../notes.d/NOTE-400.md), the
first statement. Atari et al. (2023), [LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md), read in [NOTE-398](../notes.d/NOTE-398.md), the
revised questionnaire.

## What was actually shown

**The account.** Four claims ([LIT-499](../literature.d/LIT-499.md)): a first draft of the moral mind is
organized in advance of experience, culture edits it, it works through fast
intuitions ([THEORY-tmpjpjf8](THEORY-tmpjpjf8.md)), and there are several foundations because there were
several adaptive challenges. Table 2.1 pairs each foundation with its
challenge: Care with protecting children, Fairness with two-way
partnerships, Loyalty with coalitions, Authority with hierarchies, Sanctity
with communicable disease.

**Where the list came from.** Haidt & Joseph ([LIT-502](../literature.d/LIT-502.md)) counted the concerns
shared by five surveys of moral content and found suffering, hierarchy and
reciprocity. They added purity from their own work on disgust, and named
ingroup as the likeliest fifth (fn. 15). No data are reported there.

**Evolutionary models (Table 2.4).** Care: kin selection and attachment.
Fairness: reciprocal altruism. Loyalty: multilevel selection. Authority: rank
and dominance. Sanctity: disgust and the behavioural immune system. Primate
and infant evidence is listed for Care, Fairness and Loyalty. Authority has
primate evidence only. Sanctity is "not yet shown in NHP or infants", and is
presumed the most recent foundation, "perhaps coevolving with human
religiosity" (p. 112).

**Revision ([LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md)).** The MFQ-2 splits Fairness into Equality and
Proportionality, reversing the 2013 chapter's "We do not believe that there
is an 'equality' foundation" (p. 114). Six factors fit across 19 nations
([THEORY-tmpqgklr](THEORY-tmpqgklr.md)).

## What this does not say

- **That these five, or six, are the foundations.** The chapter grants the
  list may change (p. 57). Its criteria screen candidates; they do not
  generate them. Liberty, Efficiency, Ownership and Honesty are named
  candidates, and the authors suspect Loyalty and Authority may be one.
- **That Sanctity's origin is shown.** Its preparedness evidence is the
  thinnest in the table. The disgust evidence it relied on was later
  disputed: a meta-analysis and replications find disgust does not amplify
  condemnation, and pathogen-free "spiritual purity" violations do not
  produce disgust's facial expression (reviewed in [LIT-101](../literature.d/LIT-101.md), [NOTE-132](../notes.d/NOTE-132.md)).
- **That the foundations were derived from evolutionary theory.** They were
  matched to existing models. The match for Loyalty rests on group-level
  selection, which the chapter notes is disputed (fn. 5).
- **That pluralism is shown by the MFQ's factor structure alone.** The MFQ-1
  fits poorly in several samples ([LIT-496](../literature.d/LIT-496.md), [LIT-513](../literature.d/LIT-513.md)), and MFQ purity items
  form a factor in some samples and not others.

## Connections

- **Rivals.** Morality-as-cooperation ([THEORY-tmp8vbpr](THEORY-tmp8vbpr.md)) derives its domains from
  games and leaves Sanctity out. Fitouchi, André & Baumard ([THEORY-tmpbzrkk](THEORY-tmpbzrkk.md)) explain
  puritanical condemnation by perceived self-control, not disgust. Rai &
  Fiske ([THEORY-tmpreimy](THEORY-tmpreimy.md)) fold Sanctity and Loyalty into one motive, Unity.
- **The evidence it rests on.** The liberal–conservative profile ([THEORY-tmp4dj55](THEORY-tmp4dj55.md)) is
  the theory's strongest finding, and it is a finding about self-report.
  That purity is moralized at all ([THEORY-tmps5brs](THEORY-tmps5brs.md)) does not distinguish this account
  from its rivals; why it is moralized does.
