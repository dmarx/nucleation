The owner asked on 2026-10-24 for arXiv:2410.17517 to be read and filed. It
was not held. It is filed as [LIT-1484](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1484.md) and was read in full from arXiv
v6 (21 July 2026, CC BY 4.0) in [NOTE-1132](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1132.md). The version of record in
Swarm Intelligence (doi:10.1007/s11721-026-00264-7, 17 October 2026, per
Crossref) was not sought. `published:` is v1's date, 2024-10-23. Per
[ADR-048](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-048.md), no anthology check was made; the work carries
`anthology-candidate` because the authors offer it for designing
collective RL systems ([ADR-005](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-005.md)).

Two Proposed THEORYs:
- [THEORY-899](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-899.md): the proved equivalence. Imitation of success is Cross
  Learning and the weighted voter model is Maynard-Cross Learning, in
  expectation, for an infinite well-mixed population. The derivation was
  re-checked when filed.
- [THEORY-905](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-905.md): the simulation-scoped limits. Finite populations lose
  options, pure imitation cannot re-explore after a change, and comparison
  is faster than blind imitation.

The NOTE says how the reading bears on the distributed-agency line,
[THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md), [CLAIM-443](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-443.md), [CLAIM-369](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-369.md) and [THEORY-142](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-142.md). No claim was revised.
