**Corrections the lookup made to the exchange.**

- A36's two-generals source, Akkoyunlu, Ekanadham and Huber (1975), was
  cited without a title. Its title is "Some constraints and tradeoffs in
  the design of network communications".
- Crossref lists Elizabeth Vitanza as Pradeu's co-author. She is the
  translator.
- A27's "Revonsuo's 1999 treatment" could be either of two papers in that
  issue of *Consciousness and Cognition*. The record takes "Binding and the
  Phenomenal Unity of Consciousness" (doi:10.1006/ccog.1999.0384), not the
  issue introduction with Newman (doi:10.1006/ccog.1999.0393).
- The PBFT paper Ak cited is the OSDI '99 paper. The DOI usually attached
  to it (10.1145/571637.571640) belongs to the longer 2002 TOCS paper with
  proactive recovery.
- A39 labelled Slovic ([LIT-1187](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1187.md)) as "Tversky–Kahneman". The author is
  Slovic.

**Identifications that are the record's, not the exchange's.**
