# NOTE-2202: Butterfill and Apperly — How to Construct a Minimal Theory of Mind

## Contribution

A construction of a theory of mind that is less than the full
propositional one.

## Key results

1. **Power.** "Minimal theory of mind is rich enough to explain systematic
   success on tasks held to be acid tests" (p. 1).
2. **Registration.** "an individual registers an object at a location if
   and only if she most recently encountered it at that location" (§4.3,
   p. 13).
3. **Limit.** Registration differs from belief in "lacking
   representational content and the possibility of embedding" (§7, p. 27).

## Assumptions

Propositional attitudes are demanding because their contents nest
arbitrarily.

## Concepts

Encountering; registration; minimal versus full theory of mind; signature
limits.

## Bearing on the record

- **The owner's question**, background: a minimal mindreader could track
  that a partner has encountered the same salient thing, but not the
  partner's registration of its own registration; any coordination it
  supported would be first-order (the reader's inference; the paper says
  nothing of coordination).

## Limitations

Says nothing of coordination, common knowledge or joint action; manuscript
pagination.
