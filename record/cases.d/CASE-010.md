# CASE-010: Henley's rendering of Villon, "Booze and the blowens cop the lot"

## The case

U3 linked the poem and asked for modernized translations. A6: "Henley's version
of François Villon's poem is *already* a radical translation: it replaces
15th-century French criminal argot with 19th-century English underworld
slang", and "Henley's opacity is part of its charm."

## What it can show

That a celebrated translation can keep almost none of the source's wording and
still be taken as Villon. Its opacity raised [QUESTION-023](../questions.d/QUESTION-023.md). The modern renderings
made from it are [CASE-012](CASE-012.md).
