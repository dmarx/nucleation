# NOTE-tmpu15jn: Postman (1998) — Five Things We Need to Know About Technological Change

## Contribution

The author's compact restatement of Technopoly's principles, with the irreversibility and mythic claims stated outright.

## Key results

1. "Technology giveth and technology taketh away."
2. "The consequences of technological change are always vast, often unpredictable and largely irreversible."
3. Standardized tests "resulted in our reorganizing the curriculum to accommodate the tests."
4. A mythic technology is "not easily susceptible to modification or control".

## Assumptions

A cultural, not formal, account.

## Concepts

Trade-off; winners and losers; ecological change; mythic technology.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpbypt7**: corroborates its institutional and cultural mechanisms: the curriculum reorganized around the tests (result 3) and mythic acceptance (result 4).
- **CLAIM-tmpsmg1c**: supports its premise (result 2: vast, unpredictable, largely irreversible); Postman counsels caution and states no burden rule.
- **TERM-tmpt8p9e**: winners and losers are the declared parties whose claims diverge.

## Limitations

A short talk; the book's typology of cultures is not here.
