# CLAIM-tmpsze11: Sheaf-theoretic contextuality has to be extended to directed transport between observational scenarios whose covers change, and that extension is the theory's open mathematical problem

## The claim

A93 §1: "**Research opportunity:** Extend these structures to directed transport
between observational scenarios, including changing covers and stochastic maps
between local outcome spaces. That would make the theory about more than
detecting contextuality within a fixed scenario. It would let us investigate how
compatibility structures evolve during translation and repeated
reconstruction." A94, bridging Fritz, Blackwell and Abramsky–Brandenburger:
"Under what conditions can a directed stochastic transport preserve the
decision-relevant information of a context-indexed empirical model while
changing its observational cover or global compatibility structure?", "one of
the central mathematical problems".

## Where it stands

The manuscript's Propositions 1 and 2 ([CLAIM-tmpze62b](CLAIM-tmpze62b.md), [CLAIM-tmpjl8xd](CLAIM-tmpjl8xd.md)) cover transports that
commute with restriction or come from one global kernel. §6: "some
postprocessing classes preserve noncontextuality, whereas cover-changing or
incompatible maps demand separate analysis." Fritz ([LIT-772](../literature.d/LIT-772.md)), the bridge's
first term, is in the references and uncited. The owner's answer is [CLAIM-tmp06kdp](CLAIM-tmp06kdp.md).
