The owner asked for the works and ideas that the self-categorization and
fandom readings pointed to to be filed. The owner then added the context of
the original question: their position that there is no selflessness, only
self-interest on selves of different sizes. Every work was checked against
Crossref (and Open Library for books) before it was filed. Under ADR-002's
rules, `published:` takes the print month when Crossref's online date is
a later backfile.

**Corrections the lookup made.** Tyler and Cobbs 2015 is in the *European
Sport Management Quarterly* 15(2), not the *Journal of Sport Management*.
Tajfel and Turner 1979 has no DOI of its own; the URL is the LCCN record
of the Austin and Worchel volume, and the 2004 reprint's DOI is noted.
No large preregistered direct replication of the minimal-group effect was
found. Robertson, Akles and Van Bavel 2024, a preregistered replication of
a moral-hypocrisy study that found minimal-group favouritism along the
way, is filed as the nearest.
