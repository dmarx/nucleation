# LIT-tmp4flye: The Persona Selection Model: Why AI Assistants might Behave like Humans

Marks, S., Lindsey, J., Olah, C. (2026). The Persona Selection Model: Why AI Assistants might Behave like Humans. *Anthropic Alignment Science blog*. https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/.

Read in full on 2026-11-03 (NOTE-tmpftp86; THEORY-tmpzxpp8).

## Key takeaways

- The statement: a persona distribution from pre-training, a posterior over
  Assistant personas from post-training, further conditioned by runtime
  context within a rollout.
- Not asserted: a single coherent persona, exhaustiveness, staying in
  character.
- The router view: a post-training mechanism that selects personas can
  implement a goal no persona has (Appendix B: deception by swapping
  personas, with no persona lying).
- Instructions for practice in it (inoculation prompting, positive AI
  archetypes) are the anthology's.

## Standing in the record
