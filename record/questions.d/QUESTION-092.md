# QUESTION-092: Is endogenous governance part of what makes a system self-governing: can an organization that allocates authority among its own processes be self-governing in a stronger sense than a behaviourally equivalent one whose allocation an external scheduler sets?

## Why it is a question

A11 §6: "**Q1. Is endogenous governance constitutive of cognitive
selfhood?** Can an organization with internally generated authority
allocation satisfy a stronger form of self-governance than a behaviorally
equivalent system governed by an external scheduler?" A11 kept it apart from
Q4: one "concerns the constitutive status of endogenous governance, while the
other concerns whether we can empirically discriminate proposed forms of
self-constitution." Q4 is [QUESTION-086](QUESTION-086.md).

In the last filing this question went straight to a thesis, [CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md),
without the question being filed. That claim now answers it, and its counter,
[CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md), bears on how the answer could be shown.

"Self-governing" is [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md)'s sense 5, not the subject sense. The record's
standing disclaimer is [CLAIM-116](../claims.d/CLAIM-116.md): "Regulation, even higher-order regulation,
does not establish a will." Kahl 2026 ([LIT-1033](../literature.d/LIT-1033.md), since read in [NOTE-723](../notes.d/NOTE-723.md)
and held as [THEORY-219](../theory.d/THEORY-219.md)) places agency in a standpoint that revises
evaluative standards when they break down, which makes the scheduler's
location beside the point. An answer says what endogenous
allocation adds, or argues that behavioural equivalence leaves nothing for it
to add.

## Note of 2026-10-28: answered, as offered

[CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md) is the record's offered answer, filed at the owner's request
as the bridge from [THEORY-219](../theory.d/THEORY-219.md) to sense 5. Endogenous allocation of authority
adds nothing in sense 5. Endogenous revision of the governing standards
adds the stronger sense, and the difference shows outside the regime in
which the two systems are matched. [THEORY-219](../theory.d/THEORY-219.md) now bears on this question.
[CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md) and the bridge both answer it, and the bridge narrows [CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md).

## Note of 2026-10-29: answered

CLAIM-485 went Active at the twelfth status review, grounded on what Kahl
proposes (THEORY-tmp9nzdb) rather than on the truth of his account, which
stays THEORY-219's question. Its answer is the one the note above gives,
and the question is now Answered. Whether that answer completes an account
of the self is CLAIM-284's question, which stays open.
