# SYNTHESIS-tmplr2wo: Frustration among an organization's demands is an ordinal fact about which sets of them can be jointly met, and relief is the enlargement of that complex for the same demands, judged against a fixed reference set; the residual, governance regret and budgets refine it without identifying thwarting, quiet, thresholds and tolerances are kept apart from it, niche differentiation matters as possible relief that the open questions ask whether operators can reach by themselves, and smaller families on cycle obstructions, coexistence without cooperation, affect and roles hang from it

*A part account of the distributed-agency line (ADR-083, ADR-084): the part the community map of the line calls frustration, 52 entries. The line's account is built over it and the accounts of its six sibling parts (conferral, normative-institutional, unit-boundary, individuation, persistence, collective-stance). Most of its core came from the exchange of 2026-10-17 (U33 to U38), the reviews the owner relayed on 2026-10-18 and the synthesis relayed the same day; the rest from "Organizational Nucleation and SLT" (2026-11-04), "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07) and readings. Twenty-five of its entries carry another line, and eleven have another line first. Several are integrated by other lines' accounts as well, listed in the section on where else they are integrated; this account integrates every member again.*

## The account

**The primitive.** CLAIM-368 (the owner's U33 of the exchange of
2026-10-17, granted by A33; interpretive, Active) makes frustration, not
distress, the primitive for an organization's regulatory difficulty: the
owner, "maybe we should focus less on distress and more on frustration";
A33, "Frustration is the most promising theoretical primitive of the
three." It rests on CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-027, and states the link: "A demand
that is not jointly realizable is a precedence claimed and not granted".
CLAIM-283 (A4 §2, reading Frijda; interpretive, Active) is that
link's premise: "An operator can urgently demand control without receiving
it." Its note of 2026-10-18 records A34's p_i − w_i as "a candidate
indicator of frustrated precedence", and says "The frustration account rests
on this claim (CLAIM-368)". TERM-060 (A33's term at U33, made ordinal at
U37; Active) defines frustration as "the failure of the demands it maintains
to be jointly realizable under its admissible transformations and its
governance", structural or by governance, apart from strain and distress,
and "not CLAIM-153's physics sense, though it shares that sense's
three-demand minimal obstruction". Its note of 2026-10-18 records an error
in the definition and three corrections the claims now carry, and leaves the
relation to operative thwarting to QUESTION-131. CLAIM-368's version 2 made
strain an independent dimension.

**Ordinal first: the complex.** CLAIM-347 (the owner's correction at U37
with A37's construction, corrected twice in review; interpretive, Active)
rests on CLAIM-368 and uses TERM-060. The owner: "We can do quite a lot with
access to an ordering, even if it doesn't give us a true measure." The
jointly satisfiable sets of demands form a downward-closed complex whose
minimal non-faces are the minimal conflicts; three gradients at 120° are
pairwise compatible and jointly infeasible; relief is strict enlargement for
the same declared demands; and its title ends "dropping or suppressing a
demand is not relief". Its notes index the complex to a horizon and a
budget, read it as a nerve, and set it beside the readings on constraint
architectures, CLAIM-586 and CLAIM-564. CASE-119 (a construction from the
synthesis of 2026-10-18; Active) is its three-member minimal frustrated
collection, relieved when a composite policy enlarges the attainable set:
"relief in CLAIM-347's sense, by changing what is attainable, not by
weakening a demand". CASE-196 (A8's Proposal A, the reader's thresholds;
Proposed), a variant of CASE-119 and CASE-118 that illustrates CLAIM-347 and
CLAIM-534, derives such a missing triangle from ecological dynamics and
finds a band in which the face depends on what compatibility is declared to
be: "It shows that the face criterion is part of what is declared."

**Quantities that refine it.** CLAIM-365 (A34 §4 and A35 §3, checked;
demonstrated, Active) proves that the local residual Φ_r equals a maximum
over the simplex, so infeasibility has a certificate and any admissible
update exceeds Φ_r by a nonnegative regret; since its version 2 it
complements CLAIM-347 rather than resting on it, "a quantitative refinement
of a more basic order-theoretic feasibility problem, not the definition of
frustration itself" (A37). CLAIM-357 (A's, A34 and A35 restated at A39 and
A40; interpretive, Active) rests on CLAIM-365 and CLAIM-368 and separates
structural infeasibility (Φ > 0), realized violation (Γ > −Φ when Φ ≤ 0)
and minimax suboptimality (Γ > 0), and "causal governance failure, which
neither quantity identifies". Governance-induced shortfall needs "an
intervention on the allocation with search and information held fixed", and
it quotes A35: "Importantly, these dual multipliers are not endorsement weights." Its
note of 2026-11-04 recomputes A22's toy as realized violation with no
structural infeasibility, "and CASE-119 is the same three-demand complex".
CLAIM-351 (A33 §5; demonstrated, Active) shows that "A zero aggregate
gradient does not show that the demands it aggregates are reconciled", on
CASE-115. CASE-115 (A33, A35 and A38's construction; Active) is the shared
instance: two quadratic demands frustrated at every budget, relieved by
splitting the parameter. CLAIM-351, CLAIM-361, CLAIM-365 and CLAIM-554
ground on it, its front matter illustrates only CLAIM-360 (learned-structure),
and its body lists what it shows for CLAIM-351, CLAIM-365, CLAIM-357,
CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-360. QUESTION-126 (A35's question; Open) asks whether
the weights governance grants track the multipliers that bind: "That is
CLAIM-357's governance-induced frustration seen from the weights."
CLAIM-586 (A's proposal, shown in the claim; demonstrated, Active; realizability first)
rests on CLAIM-347 and bears on QUESTION-131: the complex at a budget is
monotone in it and bounded by the unbudgeted complex, "So a missing face is
either short of resources at b or structurally frustrated, and the
unbudgeted complex tells which", and "CLAIM-365's worked case, where no
budget helps, is a non-face of K_Q".

**Three obstructions, and three limitations.** CLAIM-346 (owner-seeded at
U36, A36's table; demonstrated, Active) uses TERM-060 and complements
CLAIM-368: feasibility, aggregation and epistemic coordination "each can
occur with the other two absent", relative to fixing the demands as the
constituents' own acceptance conditions. CASE-194 (A8's Proposal E,
sharpened by the reader; Proposed), a variant of CASE-115, gives three
organizations at equal score whose limitations are structural,
observational and governance, and says "It is not CLAIM-346's triple": only
its structural arm matches CLAIM-346's feasibility. It illustrates
CLAIM-346, CLAIM-357 and two claims of the normative-institutional part.

**Relief against a fixed reference set.** CLAIM-361 (the owner's prompt at
U38, worked by A38, A39 and A43; interpretive, Active) rests on CLAIM-347,
CLAIM-284 and CLAIM-287 and grounds on CASE-115: relief is judged for an
identified organization against a fixed reference set D₀ under an explicit
correspondence. A38: "Resolving a conflict, reorganizing the individual
that bears it, and eliminating one of the parties to the conflict are
different transformations". It adds to Watson's decomposition-relative
comparison the case where the parts change. QUESTION-123 (A39's; Open) asks
what the correspondence is when the bearers branch or merge, which
CLAIM-361's defeater excludes.

**From structural frustration to thwarting.** QUESTION-131 (raised in the
reviews of 2026-10-18; Open) keeps three facts apart, structural
incompatibility, unrealized attainability and counteracted realization,
holds three candidate accounts, none adopted, and says "Merely failing to
realize an attainable set of requirements is neither." Its note of
2026-11-04 says the competition row of A7's table is held by no entry and
"Candidate account 3 is developed in CLAIM-554." CLAIM-368, CLAIM-549,
CLAIM-554, CLAIM-586 and CLAIM-631 bear on it. CLAIM-631 (the assistant's
derivation at the synthesis review of 2026-11-07, formerly SYNTHESIS-008;
demonstrated, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-549: structural
frustration forces an unmet member that acts toward its demand "exactly
when no attainable arrangement leaves each member either met or quiet", and
it gives a necessary condition of thwarting, not thwarting. QUESTION-369
(A3's; Open) asks what would show an experience is a need rather than what
raises reported well-being, and names CLAIM-368 as inheriting "whatever this
question finds about need frustration's measurement"; CLAIM-368's note of
2026-11-03 records that the parallel it draws from self-determination theory
is not established of the theory's main measure.

**Quiet, thresholds and tolerances are not frustration.** CLAIM-549 (A17,
A19, A20, A26 and A28 at the owner's U17, the example the reader's;
demonstrated, Active; will-organization first) rests on CLAIM-347 and
CLAIM-361: the quiescent complex is not the requirement complex, "either can
hold a face the other lacks", and raising thresholds "leaves the requirement
complex as it was, so it is not relief of frustration". CLAIM-552 (the
owner's U12; interpretive, Active; will-organization first) rests on
CLAIM-534 and CLAIM-347 and complements CLAIM-283: a perspective's tolerance
is its directed criterion for conferring influence, grants "can be jointly
frustrated", and "A widened tolerance is not automatically relief of
frustration under the original demands" (A13). CLAIM-553 (the owner's U15
as corrected at U17; interpretive, Proposed; will-organization first) rests
on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-417 and CLAIM-534 and grounds on CASE-119: observed
intervention factors into propensity and channel, "a perspective that does
not intervene has not thereby endorsed", and wholeheartedness is a full face
of the requirement complex, so "a structurally frustrated individual cannot
act wholeheartedly unless its demands change". CLAIM-631's item 4 puts
CLAIM-553's tolerances into its met-or-quiet complex.

**Niche differentiation as relief.** CLAIM-554 (A7 after the owner's U7,
the governance case the reader's; interpretive, Active; will-organization
first) rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361 and CLAIM-369, grounds on CASE-115,
CASE-118 and CASE-119, and bears on QUESTION-131 to QUESTION-135. A7:
"The key achievement is not reduced opposition as such. It is a change in
joint realizability." Its table keeps contested rankings, competition,
structural and governance frustration, operative thwarting, strain and
coexistence apart; competitors that coexist within CASE-118's bound
"compete without frustration of their persistence demands"; and CASE-115's
split relieves frustration where "No two specialists competed before it."
The owner's U7 is the correction it records.

**Whether operators reach it themselves.** QUESTION-133 (from the synthesis
of 2026-10-18; Open) asks whether jointly infeasible operators can develop
a repertoire that makes their demands jointly feasible "without
suppressing, weakening or deleting any of those demands"; CASE-119 and
CASE-115 show it is possible, and its note of 2026-11-03 names CASE-117 as
the study that would test differentiation driven by frustration. CLAIM-538
bears on it. QUESTION-083 (A10's first decisive question, narrowed;
Open) asks whether processes sharing one update rule come to hold mutually
regulated authority with niches; its note of 2026-10-18 lists four
refinements, QUESTION-135, QUESTION-132, QUESTION-133 and QUESTION-134,
and names CASE-117 the proposed study. QUESTION-132 and QUESTION-135 refine
it in front matter. CLAIM-369 (from the synthesis of 2026-10-18, with the
record's proof; demonstrated, Active; formal-apparatus first) makes
symmetric resource competition multiplicative gradient flow, so the
recovery test is a marginal value, and grounds on CASE-118 (Active), whose
bound |δ| < (1 − ρ)/(1 + ρ) shows "feature geometry alone does not fix
coexistence". QUESTION-135 (Open) asks when the Gram spectrum predicts
coexistence; its note of 2026-11-04 turns CASE-118 into a persistence edge
of the complex and closes: "CLAIM-554 reads niche differentiation as relief
of frustration among persistence demands." QUESTION-132 (Open) separates
stabilizing coexistence, imposed diversity and entrenched asymmetry by a
recovery exponent; QUESTION-134 (Open) asks whether influence-dependent
training destroys a marginalized specialist's competence; QUESTION-349
(a replication condition moved from THEORY-937; Open) defers to
QUESTION-132 whether an idle expert could regain responsibility. CASE-117
(a proposed study; Active, stipulated) measures among other things
"changes in the compatibility complex of the operators' demands" for
QUESTION-133 and CLAIM-347, and says even a positive result would not show
self-constitution.

**Coexistence is not cooperation.** CLAIM-546 (the reader's, from a
synthesis the owner supplied; demonstrated, Active) grounds on CASE-118,
complements CLAIM-547 and CLAIM-554 and bears on QUESTION-132: in Hawk–Dove
the types coexist and the Hawk still takes the resource, so "a coexistence
criterion answers only the first". CLAIM-554's table cites it for its
coexistence row. CLAIM-547 (the reader's, from the same synthesis;
interpretive, Active) bears on QUESTION-371 and QUESTION-083: in Nowak's
group-selection rule cooperation within groups is bought by removing groups.
ARG-052 (Rejected) rejects the inference to CLAIM-547 from Van Lange and
Bergmann, which supports one link only. QUESTION-371 (Open) asks whether a
moral norm can work as a stabilizing mechanism among groups. CLAIM-603 (the
assistant's; interpretive, Proposed; transient-support first) complements
CLAIM-554: "That claim says specialization can relieve frustration where
nothing competes. This one adds when specialization is safe: when what is
given up is matched by something supplied back."

**Cycles of contexts.** CLAIM-153 (dual-filed from the Mathematics of
Meaning; demonstrated, Active) is the monodromy obstruction around a cycle
of contexts, "and the minimal model of the obstruction needs three"; its
note of 2026-10-18 sets CLAIM-347's update case beside it, "This is the
shape only." CLAIM-538 (the owner's U27, worked at A28, A29 and A32;
demonstrated, Active) rests on CLAIM-153, complements CLAIM-347, CLAIM-365
and CLAIM-361, and bears on QUESTION-133: tolerances on the overlaps of a
graph of contexts are jointly met exactly when no cycle's holonomy exceeds
its summed tolerance, local adjustment moves a mismatch and never removes
it, and omission fakes compatibility. Among what it does not say: "That a
minimal non-face of CLAIM-347's complex is a cycle obstruction." CLAIM-618
(the owner's conjecture at U4, A's construction; demonstrated, Proposed)
rests on CLAIM-347 and complements CLAIM-153: an Arnold tongue's locking
region lies inside the compatibility region of the same demands "without
needing to equal it". ARG-061 (the assistant's at the owner's review;
Proposed) concludes it and finds two premises the claim does not state.
QUESTION-438 (A's; Open) asks when changes in the complex coincide with
bifurcations of the locked states. CLAIM-196 (ported from the Mathematics
of Meaning at the owner's request; interpretive, Proposed) rests on
CLAIM-197 and CLAIM-317 and complements CLAIM-153: roles conflict only on an
overlap, and "CLAIM-153 supplies the mathematics once overlaps exist".
CLAIM-317 (its empirical half, split under ADR-043; empirical, Proposed)
and CASE-052 (a stipulated specimen; Active), which illustrates CLAIM-196
and CLAIM-197, carry the sociology.

**Affect beside frustration.** CLAIM-440 (the owner's of 2026-10-23;
interpretive, Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on CLAIM-347,
CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-039 and complements CLAIM-368 and CLAIM-451: valence
"presupposes contested rankings, not frustrated demands", and since its
version 4 (conceding CLAIM-449) "What the condition needs is contest, not
frustration (TERM-060)". CLAIM-451 (the owner's of 2026-10-23;
interpretive, Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on CLAIM-434 and
complements CLAIM-368 and CLAIM-440: a cycle in the record of wins becomes
tension only when realized as costly reversal, which "needs reference to
the demands maintained (TERM-060, CLAIM-368's frustration), not graph
theory". CLAIM-432 (the record's reading of Bakan's thesis; interpretive,
Proposed) answers QUESTION-265, grounds on CASE-151 and complements
CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-440: "a corporation can hold a signal of its harm, and
the signal can claim precedence, without being granted it". CLAIM-431 (a
counter; Proposed) objects to it and answers QUESTION-265: "the line between
harm as concern and harm as threat ... does not hold". QUESTION-265 (the
owner's of 2026-10-23; Open) asks whether "psychopathic" is a category error
of a corporation. CASE-151 (documented, from the board's report; Active)
is Wells Fargo; CLAIM-283's note of 2026-10-23 uses it for "the
access–authority half of this claim".

**A nucleus as a face.** QUESTION-380 (A's; Open; realizability first)
asks when a small compatible subsystem makes global organization more
likely, and makes internal compatibility exact as "a face of its
compatibility complex (CLAIM-347)".

## Links tested as claims

account integrates it.

- **The minimal conflicts among overlap demands are the violating cycles.**
  For CLAIM-538's demands, |x_v − x_u − a_e| ≤ δ_e, the minimal non-faces of
  CLAIM-347's complex over the overlaps are exactly the simple cycles whose
  holonomy exceeds their summed tolerance. Passes by derivation; no entry
  states it, and CLAIM-538 disclaims the general link. Filed as CLAIM-tmp9hy40.
- **Every non-relief the record names changes a demand or the evaluation,
  never the attainable set.** Raising a threshold (CLAIM-549), widening a
  tolerance (CLAIM-552), raising acceptance (CLAIM-553), excising or
  suppressing (CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361), lower competition (CLAIM-554).
  Stated piecewise, each entry citing CLAIM-347 or CLAIM-361; the
  conjunction adds nothing.
- **CLAIM-357's capacity-limited case is CLAIM-586's shortage of
  resources.** With the norm ball as the budget, Φ_r > 0 at r and ≤ 0 at a
  larger r is a face of the unbudgeted complex missing at r. A
  substitution; CLAIM-586 already reads CLAIM-365's worked case as a
  non-face of the unbudgeted complex.
- **Weights at a stall certify the conflict.** If governance's weights w
  make the aggregate gradient vanish at nonzero per-demand gradients
  (CLAIM-351), then w/Σw is a feasible point of CLAIM-365's dual and
  Φ_r ≥ Σw_iε_i/Σw_i for every r, which bears on QUESTION-126. A one-line
  substitution, which CASE-115 shows at λ = (½, ½); not filed.
- **The record's complexes form a chain.** Locking inside requirement
  (CLAIM-618), requirement and quiescent incomparable (CLAIM-549), both
  inside met-or-quiet (CLAIM-631), budgeted inside unbudgeted (CLAIM-586).
  A conjunction of held inclusions over different attainable sets; not
  filed.
- **Differentiation of features relieves frustration of persistence
  demands.** Stated: QUESTION-135's note of 2026-11-04 with CLAIM-554. That
  this answers QUESTION-133 fails: CASE-118's ρ is set from outside, and
  QUESTION-133 asks for endogenous change.
- **Group selection is not relief.** CLAIM-547's spread of cooperation by
  group extinction read through CLAIM-554's "the exclusion of a
  constituent". An application, and which demands are the reference set
  (groups' persistence or cooperators') is a free parameter.
- **Directed tolerances keep the cycle test.** CLAIM-552's directed
  criteria on one overlap intersect to one interval, so CLAIM-538 applies.
  Holds only if each criterion is an interval in the offset, which
  CLAIM-552 denies in general ("Not a ball around i's own output"): a free
  parameter.
- **Audience segregation as relief.** Reading CLAIM-196's segregation as
  relief and context collapse as a shrinking of the attainable set in

  its style) collapse shrinks the attainable set with the demands fixed; on
