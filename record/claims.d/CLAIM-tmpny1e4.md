# CLAIM-tmpny1e4: Endorsement indexed by role lets a process empower another as a critic while opposing its preferred policy, so an organization that allocates authority by role can keep disagreement working inside it instead of sorting into factions

## The claim

A2 §8: "If agents endorse others exclusively because their policies align,
the influence system risks degenerating into factions of mutually
reinforcing beliefs. A more powerful system would permit denoisers to
endorse agents that disagree with them when those agents contribute
epistemic or functional value." Its example: i prefers policy A and j
prefers B, but j has "exceptional error-detection capabilities.
Consequently, i may endorse j's role as an error detector without endorsing
j's authority over final policy selection." So endorsement "should be
**role- or function-indexed**, rather than represented by a single scalar",
E_i(j, r, c, t). "An agent could endorse another as an epistemic critic while
opposing its preferred policy. This is a way to preserve productive conflict
within an otherwise cooperative system."

A4 §3 found the same need in Daw, Niv and Dayan's arbitration: "a skeptical
operator can be highly reliable at finding defects without being reliable at
producing final decisions. We therefore need operation-conditioned
competence estimates."

It complements [CLAIM-tmpxuigm](CLAIM-tmpxuigm.md). That claim lets a process gain by empowering
an ally, and this one lets it gain by empowering an opponent in a bounded
role. It answers [QUESTION-tmplh9fr](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmplh9fr.md) in part: dissent helps when it has a role
with authority attached. The worked case is [CASE-tmpejqc0](../cases.d/CASE-tmpejqc0.md).

## What it does not say

- It does not say roles stay separate. Authority to criticize can become
  authority to veto. A2 §9's "third-order endorsement", trusting another's
  judgement about whom to trust, is where roles collapse into constitutive
  influence ([CLAIM-086](CLAIM-086.md)), and A2 itself said it "should arguably have
  different constraints".
- It does not say the order of an endorsement settles anything. [THEORY-029](../theory.d/THEORY-029.md)'s
  regress stands.
