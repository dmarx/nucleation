# LIT-tmp8ibhd: Le structuralisme

Jean Piaget (1968), *Le structuralisme*, Paris: Presses Universitaires de
France (Que sais-je? 1311), 125 pp.; English translation *Structuralism*,
trans. and ed. Chaninah Maschler, New York: Basic Books, 1970 —
https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1303734W

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read*: no lawful full text was reachable (see the history
note). Known here only by the edition's description in its Open Library
work record (OL1303734W), which reads, in translation: it is often said
that structuralism is hard to characterise because it has taken too many
forms to share a common denominator, and the "structures" invoked have
acquired increasingly different meanings; comparing the senses it has
taken in the contemporary sciences, a synthesis nevertheless seems
possible: there is a common ideal of intelligibility that all
"structuralists" reach or seek, while their critical intentions vary
without limit. Nothing beyond that description is asserted here.
