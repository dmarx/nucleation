# LIT-2188: Blazing Grace: The Gifted Culture of Burning Man

St John, G. (2017). Blazing Grace: The Gifted Culture of Burning Man. *NANO: New American Notes Online* 11 ("Economies of the Gift in an Age of Austerity"), July 2017, pp. 1–16. ISSN 2160-0104. https://nanocrit.com/issues/issue11/Blazing-Grace-The-Gifted-Culture-of-Burning-Man.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1662](../notes.d/NOTE-1662.md). Page numbers are the PDF's footers.*

- **Gifting stated as unconditional (p. 4).** "The value of a gift is
  unconditional. Gifting does not contemplate a return or an exchange for
  something of equal value."
- **No obligation, yet circulation (p. 5).** "While there is an absence of
  an obligation on the part of the recipient to reciprocate", gifts
  "nevertheless inspire gratitude and thus a response that keeps the gift
  in motion": generalized reciprocity, after Sahlins.
- **The return goes to the whole (p. 7).** A Burner expresses "through
  countless labors in subsequent events a gratitude for untold gifts
  bestowed unto them".
- **The threat is non-participation (pp. 3, 7–8).** Non-participants
  "becoming an audience to an event produced by others"; the "Plug-n-Play"
  camps of 2014; met by "reform of theme camp registration criteria and
  placement policy".
- **Interpretive.** No measure of how often gifts are returned, to whom, or
  how many free-ride.
