# ADR-011: information-retrieval joins the topics

## Context

On 2026-09-27 the owner asked the record to file C. J. van Rijsbergen, *The
Geometry of Information Retrieval* (Cambridge, 2004).

The book recasts information retrieval's vector-space, probabilistic and
logical models in the language of Hilbert space. Documents are vectors.
Relevance and aboutness are observables. Retrieval is measurement, with a
non-Boolean logic of projectors.

The words the vocabulary had describe its formalism, not its subject:

- `mathematics`;
- `quantum-foundations`;
- `logic`;
- `probabilistic-modeling`.

The seed named the gap (`wants: information-retrieval`). The record's rule is
to add the word rather than tag with the nearest wrong one
([ADR-006](ADR-006.md), [ADR-007](ADR-007.md), [ADR-009](ADR-009.md),
[ADR-010](ADR-010.md)).

One held work already needs the word as well. Gebhart, Hansen & Schrater's
*Knowledge Sheaves* embeds knowledge graphs in order to answer multi-hop
queries.

## Decision

Add `information-retrieval` to `topics`, with a label and a blurb. It does not
join the `philosophy` group.

| work | tags |
| --- | --- |
| van Rijsbergen, *The Geometry of Information Retrieval* | `information-retrieval` first, then `mathematics`, `quantum-foundations`, `logic` |
| Gebhart, Hansen & Schrater, *Knowledge Sheaves* | `information-retrieval` added |

The book's seed also proposed `probabilistic-modeling`. It is not carried. The
book's probability is quantum probability over projectors, which
`quantum-foundations` covers. The topic's blurb, Bayesian inference and
statistical models, would mislead a reader browsing it.

## Alternatives

- **Tag the book by its formalism only.** A reader browsing for retrieval
  would never find it. A reader browsing `quantum-foundations` would find a
  book about search engines.
- **Fold retrieval into `representation-learning`.** Retrieval predates
  learned representations. Most of the book's models are not learned at all.
