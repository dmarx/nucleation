**The owner's prompt.** The owner opened with "self as a multi agent system".
Asked what to do with it, they answered: "connect these ideas to
synchronization through temporal discounting, mindfulness, and flow state".
Nothing filed in this session is the owner's beyond those two lines.
Everything below was proposed by the assistant, and the owner has endorsed
none of it.

**What the record already held.** Plenty on the self as many: Minsky,
Selfridge, Dennett and Levin, with [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) on composition. Ainslie's
interests are [THEORY-067](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-067.md), against Bratman's [THEORY-076](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-076.md). On mindfulness,
ACT's self as context ([THEORY-051](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-051.md)), and [THEORY-064](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-064.md) on how a self-model
becomes opaque. Effort cost is [THEORY-070](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-070.md), and coordination dynamics is
[THEORY-069](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-069.md). Nothing on flow at all, and nothing joining any of these to
another. The one entry that had joined will to synchrony, [CLAIM-012](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-012.md) (will as
a higher-order attractor), was superseded on 2026-10-05.

**What moved.**

- **The question.** [QUESTION-034](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-034.md) refines [QUESTION-015](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-015.md) for the
  population case. Is acting as one a matter of enforcement among the
  interests, a change in which interest may act, or agreement among them?
- **The term.** [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md), intrapersonal synchronization: no regime's
  valuation of the course crosses within the horizon. It is defined without
  dynamics on purpose.
- **Discounting is the source.** On Ainslie's account the population is
  produced by crossing curves ([CLAIM-169](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-169.md)). Where nothing crosses there
  is one interest. Multiplicity that differs in method rather than delay
  (IFS, [THEORY-062](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-062.md)) is set aside as a second axis.
- **Flow is agreement** ([CLAIM-162](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-162.md)). The autotelic condition makes the
  short-delay and long-delay rewards the same reward, so nothing is
  overridden and nothing costs effort. The illustration is [CASE-051](../../record/cases.d/CASE-051.md), a
  third afternoon for the reluctant writer. Dietrich's hypofrontality is
  named as the rival, in which one system yields rather than all agreeing.
- **Mindfulness is suspension** ([CLAIM-156](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-156.md)). It leaves the crossing
  and breaks the step from dominance to control, which makes it a second
  individual-scale instance of [CLAIM-027](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-027.md). The one direct study, as
  summarised ([LIT-906](../../record/literature.d/LIT-906.md)), points the other way, and the claim says so.
- **The objection** ([CLAIM-163](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)): synchronization without coupling, an
  order parameter or transitions is a name, not a mechanism. It is left
  open, with the parts that would answer it listed.

**What was learned that the claims cannot say.** The three routes in the
prompt come apart cleanly only once each is described by what it does to
the crossing: outweigh it, remove it, or leave it and change who may act.
Without that, "mindfulness and flow both synchronize the self" would have
filed two different mechanisms as one. Whether the word "synchronization"
is earned turned out to be the open question the owner's framing raises,
and it is filed as an objection rather than assumed.

**Not done.** None of the flow or mindfulness literature was read; five
works are registered unread (see the curation entry of the same date).
Neither [THEORY-067](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-067.md) nor the new claims connect to Bratman's rival
([THEORY-076](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-076.md)). Whether resolute intention is a fourth route, or the
bargaining route under another name, is left for a second occasion.
