summary: >-
  Wang, Wu & Fan (2024), Nonlinear spiked covariance matrices and signal propagation in deep neural networks, COLT 2024 (PMLR 247:4891–4957); arXiv 2402.10127. The outliers of a nonlinear spiked covariance model match those of a linear one. At random Gaussian weights an input spike passes a BBP-type test at every layer or is absorbed into the bulk, so the number of outliers and their alignment with the input never increase with depth; growth with depth appears only in a simulation after training. After a few gradient steps at unit-order step size, a two-layer network's test-data conjugate kernel has a label-aligned outlier only above a threshold. # LIT-2984: Nonlinear spiked covariance matrices and signal propagation in deep neural networks

Zhichao Wang, Denny Wu & Zhou Fan (2024). COLT 2024 (PMLR 247:4891–4957); arXiv 2402.10127.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A32 for spiked covariance through nonlinear layers. It bears on the exchange's question of how a BBP-type transition carries through a deep network. Read on 2026-11-22 (NOTE-tmpa8a45) and filed as THEORY-tmp9ltxi, which replaces this work in CLAIM-799's grounds as a third case of signal before outlier (ARG-tmppbt1o). Benigni and Péché (LIT-tmp9isrs) are registered from this reading. It is the bounded-step (η of order one) sibling of Dandi and colleagues' maximal-update analysis, and proves Ba and colleagues' Conjecture 4 (LIT-tmpnwvnx).
