# From Obstruction to Reorganization: working draft

The successor to the owner's manuscript "luria: nucleating an argument" (the work `luria-nucleating-an-argument`, proposed 2026-10-11, whose root question is QUESTION-037). It carries that manuscript's account of drift and anchoring, the account of what a record of inquiry can secure, and the account of inquiry as specification. It adds a reflexive turn: the record's grammar is part of the inquiry, and its governed revision is studied in the record's own first eight weeks.

**Provenance.** The first version was written out from the ChatGPT exchange "Branch · Audit Luria Evolution" (2026-11-20), whose abstract, outline and measurements it follows. At the owner's direction (2026-11-20), the second version adds §2, the argument as the record holds it, and §3, luria mapped onto it, and reframes the paper as the predecessor's successor.

**Status.** A working document under version control. It is not part of the record: luria does not read `scratch/`, and codes are cited bare. Figures come from three observation points, kept apart in §5.1. Where the draft depends on a Proposed entry, it says so. The theses T1–T4 (§2.7) and the mapping tables (§§3.3–3.4) are the draft's own integrations, not claims the record holds.
