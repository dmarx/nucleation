# CLAIM-tmpxkeu9: A luria record is an atlas of charts over an inquiry

## The claim

The owner, U23: "luria record as an atlas of charts". In differential
geometry an atlas is a collection of charts, each giving coordinates for
part of a space, with transition maps where charts overlap, and no chart
needing to cover the whole. The thesis reads a luria record's views,
its generated indexes, accounts and manuscripts, in that way: each
covers part of one inquiry in its own terms, and where they overlap they
must agree on what they are describing.

## Where it is developed

A49 develops the analogy, with its limits ([CLAIM-tmplz7ng](CLAIM-tmplz7ng.md)), and asks what
must stay invariant across representations ([QUESTION-tmpvpsv1](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpvpsv1.md)). The
assistant's gap fill ([CLAIM-tmp6xzjy](CLAIM-tmp6xzjy.md)) says what the analogy's
compatibility condition requires, and where the record's own design
already secures it: views derived from one source, as luria derives them
([LU-DP-003](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md#dp-3)), behave like charts of one given space.

## What it does not say

That a record is a manifold, or that its transitions are smooth or
invertible. The owner's words are a figure; the mathematics that fits it
on the record is local-to-global gluing, which the formal-apparatus line
holds ([CLAIM-143](CLAIM-143.md), [CLAIM-153](CLAIM-153.md), [CLAIM-197](CLAIM-197.md)).
