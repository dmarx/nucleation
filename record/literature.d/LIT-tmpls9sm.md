# LIT-tmpls9sm: Designing for monetization: A taxonomy of dark patterns in top-grossing GaaS video games

Sergio Gutiérrez-Manjón, Salvador Gómez-García and Gema Bonales-Daimiel (2026). "Designing for monetization: A taxonomy of dark patterns in top-grossing GaaS video games." *Tripodos 59: 76-97*. DOI-10.51698/tripodos.2026.59.05.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-tmp4nq1v](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp4nq1v.md)). An updated, three-dimension taxonomy of
game dark patterns with key references, one new category (automated play),
and a presence/absence census of it in the commercially leading GaaS titles
of 2024; plus a terminological proposal.
