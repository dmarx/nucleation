### Added

- **[LEAD-128](record/leads.d/LEAD-128.md) carried out.** A fit of the expected-value-of-control model in which control intensity falls with its cost. A search of open literature (Europe PMC, arXiv) found no published fit demonstrating the cost direction as its model-compared result — the closest open EVC accumulator fits (Leng et al. 2021, PMC8746743; Masís Obando et al. 2025, PMC12595486) show the payoff direction — so the fit was made, as the lead provides for.
- **An executed study** ([CASE-tmprt0hl](record/cases.d/CASE-tmprt0hl.md)): the EVC effort-cost softmax V(n, r) = r − c(n) fitted to 122 participants' cognitive-effort-discounting choices (OSF vnj8x, CC-BY 4.0). The fitted cost of control rises with task load, the cost term is required (likelihood-ratio χ²(3) = 10.5, p = 0.015; AIC favours it), group-mean subjective value falls monotonically with intensity (0.92 → 0.60), and three-quarters of agents have a positive marginal cost of control (median 0.26 €/level); a need-for-cognition minority seek effort. Scripts, synthetic parameter-recovery and model-comparison tests under scratch/studies/lead-128/.
- **The finding** ([CLAIM-tmpwma3v](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwma3v.md), Proposed, warrant empirical): the record's first fit of the cost direction, on a revealed-choice measure of intensity; corroborates [CLAIM-358](record/claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) and [CLAIM-162](record/claims.d/CLAIM-162.md), supplying the per-encounter cost of control their grounds presuppose.
