# CLAIM-297: When an operator's authority is updated by how well it performs on the interpretation it helped make dominant, the coupled meaning and influence of an organization can amplify an initial interpretive advantage without any gain in fidelity to independent evidence

## The claim

A22 §8: "When operator authority is updated using performance assessed
against the interpretation that those operators help determine, the
coupled system can reinforce an initial interpretive advantage without
corresponding improvement in independent evidential fidelity." The
circuit, A22 §2: frame → retrieval → interpretation → endorsement →
future retrieval policy.

A22 wrote it as two coupled processes. Meaning: x_{t+1} = Σᵢ w_{i,t}
P_i(q_t)ᵀ x_t over interpretations. Influence: w_{t+1} = G(w_t, x_t, o_t,
E_t), with [CLAIM-282](CLAIM-282.md)'s damped delegation g = (1 − η)b + ηEᵀg as one
form. Queries: q_{t+1} = Q(x_{t+1}, w_{t+1}, g). A23 VII.D: this "describes
redistribution of interpretive activation, not necessarily Bayesian
updating or a literal diffusion map in the spectral-geometric sense".

## Where it comes from in the record

The owner asked at U22 for the record's formulation of "a distributed
agent as a dual relation between diffusion maps". The record holds it as
two canvases: the owner's own words at U2 of 2026-10-13, "dual to the
'meaning' canvas, we have an 'influence' canvas", with denoising operators
on the first ([CLAIM-285](CLAIM-285.md), [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)). A22 did not cite those two. It cited
the claims around them: [CLAIM-275](CLAIM-275.md) (meaning, policy and influence
co-evolve), [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md) (trust earned by being right, and its entrenchment
loop), [CLAIM-276](CLAIM-276.md) (coherence, fidelity and plurality are separate),
[CLAIM-281](CLAIM-281.md) (role-indexed endorsement) and [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md). This claim names a
mechanism by which [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md)'s entrenchment would run, and the sense in
which it fails [CLAIM-276](CLAIM-276.md)'s fidelity.

## What it does not say

- Not that coupling must capture. A22: "these outcomes are not guaranteed
  merely by writing coupled diffusion equations. We'd need to specify
  update rules and derive the equilibria and their stability."
- Not that concentration is error. "It may reflect genuine superior
  evidence or expertise."
- Not anything about selves. A23 XIV.H: "A coupled interpretive system
  might exhibit expertise, habit, and lock-in without satisfying any
  stronger criterion of agency or selfhood." [CLAIM-288](CLAIM-288.md)'s objection to
  modeller-fixed objectives applies to [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) as built.

## Note of 2026-10-18: the loop again, applied to affect

A32 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 applied this claim's circuit to a learner
with an internal monitor. If the monitor's threat interpretation "increases
the monitor's authority, it may gain greater influence over which future
signals are inspected", and "The resulting distress-like regime could
persist even if the external difficulty has diminished." It split two
mechanisms: "**Evidence-tracking distress:** Endorsement changes because a
regulator accurately identifies consequential difficulties." "**Self-confirming
distress:** Endorsement changes because an existing interpretation selects
evidence favoring its own continuation." And: "The observable behavior might
look similar. Only independent feedback, counterfactual interventions, and
tests of evidence selection can distinguish the mechanisms."

This is the second appearance of the self-confirming loop, applied to affect.
The self-confirming kind is this claim's amplification without fidelity, run
on a regulatory interpretation; the evidence-tracking kind is the case this
claim's "Not that concentration is error" leaves open. The record's lock-in,
[TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md), is the regime the self-confirming kind would keep in place. U33
later made frustration, not distress, the primitive ([CLAIM-368](CLAIM-368.md)).


## Note of 2026-10-26: two running realizations, without retrieval

<!-- inactive-ok-file: CASE-162 — Proposed; the lab's runs filed 2026-10-26, cited as an illustration, nothing here rests on it -->

Two models of the Perspective Population Lab ([CASE-162](../cases.d/CASE-162.md),
dmarx/PerspectiveLab@7e2edc2) run this claim's circuit with authority,
record and competence and no retrieval.

- In a four-variable deterministic model, self-scoring holds a declared
  history with A in charge from c ≈ 0.4469, although B is the better
  perspective at every competence the model allows. When competence
  grows with access, the onset falls to 0.39.
- In a 32-member event-level population with every cue admitted,
  self-scoring at c = 0.5 leaves the worse alternative in charge in 39 of
  64 worlds, and at c = 0 in none.

Both are an initial advantage amplified with no gain in fidelity, in
stipulated models. Neither is the analysis of [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) that the
defeater names. Neither model has a meaning state or retrieval, and
neither is set against a single adaptive-retrieval process of matched
capacity. The selection effect in the same population, a reversal of the
sign of the admitted evidence, is [CASE-156](../cases.d/CASE-156.md)'s and is not new here. The
claim is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-10-29: persuasion bias, read, is a second route, and its companion paper is the loop

DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel, [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md), is now read ([NOTE-1392](../notes.d/NOTE-1392.md)). It
shows that even without this claim's loop, fixed listening weights give
an agent influence out of proportion to its accuracy: by its Theorem 2 an
unbalanced network's consensus departs from the precision-weighted
optimum with no change in anyone's accuracy. So this claim's loop is an
addition to that route, not the only route to influence without fidelity.

The paper's conclusion points to a companion, DeMarzo, Vayanos and
Zwiebel (2001), "Social Networks and Financial Markets", in which agents
choose whom to listen to by trading profits, and stable structures have
everyone listening to the same experts, who "need not be the ones with
the best information, and consequently, influence can be
self-perpetuating independently of accuracy". That is the closest
published statement of this claim's loop the record has met. It is not
registered, and it should be read before the loop is called new. The
claim is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-10-29: the companion paper, registered and not reached

DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel 2001, "Social Networks and Financial
Markets", which the earlier note of this date calls not registered and
says should be read before the loop is called new, is now registered as
[LIT-tmpapuas](../literature.d/LIT-tmpapuas.md). It is Unavailable: no copy of the working paper was found.
So that condition stays unmet. The description in [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md) leaves one
difference open. In this claim an operator's authority is scored against
the interpretation it helped make dominant. In the companion, as
described, listening is scored by trading profits, which depend on prices
and on what the asset pays, so the score need not be self-referential.
Whether it is, and whether the stable structures are reached from a small
initial advantage, can only be read from the paper. The claim is
unchanged.
