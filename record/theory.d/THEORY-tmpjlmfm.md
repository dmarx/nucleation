# THEORY-tmpjlmfm: Shoemaker holds that moral responsibility comes in three independent types, attributability, answerability and accountability, grounded in the capacities for quality of character, quality of judgment and quality of regard and each answered by its own family of responses, anger belonging to accountability

Shoemaker (2019), [LIT-tmp1px3j](../literature.d/LIT-tmp1px3j.md), after Shoemaker (2011), [LIT-tmpxsadz](../literature.d/LIT-tmpxsadz.md).

## The account

"Attributability, answerability, and accountability are the labels for
three distinct types of responsibility" (p. 913), "radically independent"
(n. 70). Answerability is being "able in principle to answer the question,
“Why did you do that?”"; accountability is having "the capacities for
emotions and empathy" (p. 912); "gratitude and anger aptly respond to
empathic and emotional (dis)regard" (p. 913). Corporations are answerable,
so open to protest, but not accountable, so not to anger (p. 915).

## What it does not show

- **The 2011 arguments.** This is the later form.
- **Answerability in the record's directed sense.** Shoemaker's is a
  capacity of the agent, not an answer owed to a party ([TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md)).
- **That blameless practice is possible for persons.** The model is built
  for agents that lack the capacities anger needs.
