On 2026-11-15 the owner asked to "read and file"
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14910. A single work, in its authors' voice.

## The boundary

The paper is a machine-learning method. Under [ADR-048](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-048.md), nucleation files
any work the owner wants, but an instruction for machine-learning
practice belongs to the anthology. It is filed here for its account of
why editing across domains fails, the THEORY. The recipe (conflict-aware
ordering, rank compression, the window update) is described in the
NOTE and not filed as a recommendation. The LIT and the THEORY carry the
`anthology-candidate` flag.

## What was filed

- [LIT-2554](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2554.md) (Active), [NOTE-1911](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1911.md) (Read), and [THEORY-1828](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1828.md)
  (Proposed).

The read found three things the paper does not say:

- The Conflict Index, as defined, is degenerate at the paper's own
  sizes unless null spaces are numerical. The arithmetic is in the NOTE.
- In its seven-task test, greedy ordering is within the 95% interval of
  the mean random order on every task.
- Its "safety" domain injects unsafe behaviour.

## Support judgements

The NOTE judges that Table 5 supports the benefit of ordering less than
the paper says. That concerns the authors' support for their own
account, inside one THEORY. No claim of the record's concludes from it,
so no ARG is filed. The THEORY's summary and its "What this does not
say" carry the judgement.
