Batch D of the reading queue that the sweep of the Unfetched works produced
(curation 2026/11/22/104609, PR [#644](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/644)): [LIT-1947](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1947.md), [LIT-1996](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1996.md) and [LIT-2175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2175.md), whose
copies the sweep fetched, and [LIT-3082](../../record/literature.d/LIT-3082.md), registered by the [LEAD-062](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-062.md)
search (PR [#646](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/646)). One reader wrote the reports (`scratch/readers/` on
`claude/readers/sweep-e`), and the filer checked each against the
downloaded copy before filing, as a claim to verify, not a finding. Batches
A to C are on another branch (PR [#649](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/649)); this branch is cut from staging and
cites none of their codes.

Readers read three works each in sequence, not one reader per work in parallel, to stay under the account's usage limit.

## How each work was read

- **[LIT-1947](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1947.md)** (Metzinger 2021): read in full from the version of record
  deposited on Zenodo (record 7034864), fetched through Zenodo's records
  API; references skimmed.
- **[LIT-1996](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1996.md)** (Howard, Gagné and Bureau 2017): the authors' manuscript in
  Curtin's espace repository, fetched through Figshare's documented API
  ([ADR-101](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-101.md)) after the Curtin website showed an AWS WAF challenge. Pages
  1–34 and Tables 1–2 read in full; references, the other tables, figures
  and the supplement skipped.
- **[LIT-2175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2175.md)** (Harvey, the 10 Principles): the organization's own page,
  saved on 2026-11-22, read in full. It has no revision date.
- **[LIT-3082](../../record/literature.d/LIT-3082.md)** (Syropoulos, Law, Coleman and Young): the authors' CC BY
  preprint from OSF, fetched once with no bot check; the file is the
  manuscript of 21 May 2024, read in full; supplements and data not in the
  file.

Every quote that a title, a status note or a dated note leans on was
rechecked against the copy's text, and all matched (Metzinger's PDF text
layer garbles ligatures and quotation marks, so those quotes were matched
after repair).
