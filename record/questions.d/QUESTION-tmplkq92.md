# QUESTION-tmplkq92: How is the joint inquiry frontier indexed by the contractual ecology among an organization's processes to be defined, and can changing the contracts alone, with every process's own capabilities held fixed, change which question–answer pairs the organization can bring about?

## Why it is a question

[TERM-160](../terms.d/TERM-160.md) (Active) defines the joint inquiry frontier of an agent at a
budget and horizon: "the question–answer pairs it can bring about
together", formulating and answering "within the same resources".
[CLAIM-1016](../claims.d/CLAIM-1016.md) puts authorization's cost in it as a third cost on the shared
budget. A18 §3 generalizes from authorization to any contract term and
gives one worked widget (formulation 3 hours, per-question authorization
4, investigation 5, deadline 10: per-question authorization takes 12 and
misses, a standing grant takes 8 and meets it, checked in [CLAIM-1016](../claims.d/CLAIM-1016.md)'s
note), with its own limit: "Its initial establishment and continuing
maintenance have costs not included in this per-inquiry calculation."

Three things are open:

1. **What C is**: the set of contracts among processes, each with its
   terms (who may draw on whom, under which tolerance, with which
   authorization), as [CLAIM-tmpersgx](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpersgx.md)'s "maintained ecology of interprocess
   contracts"; or only the authorization terms, as in the widget.
2. **Where C enters**: as a change in the costs of formulating and
   answering (then the index is a reparametrization of [TERM-160](../terms.d/TERM-160.md)'s budget),
   or as a change in which processes can contribute at all (then it
   changes the frontier's support, not only its costs).
3. **What C costs**: whether the frontier indexed by C should be read net
   of the cost of establishing and keeping C, which A18 sets aside.

## What would count as an answer

- **A definition** of the indexed frontier F(b, h; C), with C's components
  named, that reduces to [TERM-160](../terms.d/TERM-160.md) when C is fixed.
- **A construction** in which two contractual ecologies over the same
  processes, with their own capabilities fixed, give frontiers neither of
  which contains the other, net of the cost of C; or a proof that, net of
  that cost, every such change is a change of budget only.
