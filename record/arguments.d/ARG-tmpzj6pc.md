# ARG-tmpzj6pc: Monotonicity along nested probe families, applied to a self's perspectives

## The inference

[CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md): distinctions drawn by restricted profiles only grow as nested
probe families grow. Map its objects to the candidate organizations z of a
self (A13 §3, A15 §2), its probes to admissible interventions q with
response laws D_z(q), and its probe subcategories to families Q. Then a
pair of organizations separated under Q1 stays separated under every
Q2 ⊇ Q1. The conclusion, [CLAIM-tmp0dyx8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0dyx8.md), follows: a distinction disappears
only under a regime that is not an enrichment, or when the organization
probed is not the one probed before.

## Critical questions

- **Does the mapping premise hold?** It needs a probe's outcome to be a
  function of the organization probed, the same at each application. That
  is the setting of A13 §3 and A15 §2, and A15 §2 says so ("when the
  objects and response laws are held fixed"). Where probing changes the
  organization (A13 §4, A15 §4), the premise fails, and that failure is
  case 4 of the conclusion: the argument does not claim monotonicity there,
  it classifies the vanishing as a change of the organization.
- **Are the model's assumptions met?** [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md) needs an every-probe
  comparison and fails for averaged distortions. A probe family of a self
  read through summary scores is therefore not covered, which is case 2 of
  the conclusion. The conjecture's "observation horizons" enter as probes
  read over windows, so a shorter horizon is a different probe, case 3.
- **Is the application more than a relabelling?** What it adds over
  [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md) is the split between hidden distinctions and ended ones, which
  [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md) does not need to draw because its objects never change.
