# SYNTHESIS-093: Duties of governing what others depend on

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line ("Wholes, parts and dependence"), filed at the synthesis review of organizational-normativity on 2026-11-15. It was then a part of that line, whose account, [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md), was built over it and three sibling parts. Since the split of organizational-normativity ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)) it is a part of the new line, whose account, [SYNTHESIS-095](SYNTHESIS-095.md), is built over it and [SYNTHESIS-092](SYNTHESIS-092.md). Every component is new to any account. The part hangs together by its source more than by its relations: of its members' stored relations two point inside it, and most point to claims in other lines ([CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md), [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md), [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md), [CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md), [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)). Where a component declares which sense of "organization" it means ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)), the account says so.*

## The account

**Power has separable dimensions.** [CLAIM-678](../claims.d/CLAIM-678.md) (A's, A11 §5; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md) and [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md): power over a
technological arrangement has at least three dimensions, operational,
economic and epistemic, "that can be held and distributed separately",
so widening one does not widen the others. Its summary: neither
[CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md) nor [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) "separates ownership from operation". No sense
declared.

**The burden of justification.** [CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md) (A's, A13 §VIII, "the
reversibility principle"; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements
[CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md): when a choice about a technology or an institution could
substantially and durably narrow the possibilities later open to those it
affects, "the burden of justifying it should rise with the size and the
irreversibility of the narrowing". It answers the ratchet A described,
[CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md), which is in distributed-agency and flourishing-and-meaning and
not this line. [ARG-103](../arguments.d/ARG-103.md) (Proposed) concludes it from Winner ([THEORY-1859](../theory.d/THEORY-1859.md)):
Winner supports the premise and the occasion, "the scaling rule is the
claim's own". No sense declared.

**Accountability scales with indispensability.** [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) (A's,
"technological constitutionalism", A8 §VII, A11 §24 to 25, A13 §VI;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), [CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md) and
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md): the accountability that those who control an arrangement owe
those who depend on it "should rise with how indispensable the
arrangement has become to them", so an effectively compulsory arrangement
owes public justification, and those affected owe no deference to
expertise or ownership as such. Its body: "'Arrangement' is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s
sense 1; who owes is 'those who control' it, the agents [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)'s second
condition names." Its summary calls it a scaling rule "for what is owed
before" [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)'s threshold, "of the same shape as [CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md)'s burden".

**An arrangement can erode the capacity to govern it.** [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) (A's,
A8 §XVIII; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also transient-support) rests on
[CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md) and complements [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) and [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md): where the capacities a
collective needs to govern an arrangement are learned by exercise and lost
with disuse, and the arrangement does the work through which they are
exercised, "the arrangement can erode the capacity to govern it". Its
summary: that governing capacities are of [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s kind "is the premise
A assumes and the claim makes a condition". No sense declared.

**Declining a technology.** [CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md) (A's, A8 §IX and §XV, A11 §29, A13
§X and §XI; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md) and
complements [CLAIM-677](../claims.d/CLAIM-677.md) and [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md): a collective's capacity to decline a
feasible technology can depend more on its competition than on its own
organization, and "exists, if anywhere, in coordination among them". No
sense declared.

**Institutions that revise their standards.** [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) (A's, Proposition
D of A20 §XV; thesis, empirical, Proposed) bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and
complements [CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md): institutions able to recognize, authorize and carry
out revisions of their own governing standards avoid persistent
normatively significant failures better than institutions that only
monitor outcomes against fixed standards, where the inherited standards
are inadequate, "and not on routine work". Its section "Which sense of
'organization'" declares [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 3. Its summary says it is the
one part of A's "reflexive conviviality" that passes the admission test,
"by the route of a discriminating prediction", and is untested.

**Concordance, with its standards made relata.** [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md) (A's, A23 §7, at
the owner's U13; Proposed) rivals [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md) (Deferred): cross-scale
concordance, relative to declared parties, claims and horizon, of an
encompassing organization's operation with the independently justified
claims of those who constitute, sustain, depend on or are affected by it,
"together with the effective answerability of its governing arrangements
to those claims". Its summary: "Organization" is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1 by
default, and "the parties who are owed, and the bearers of what is owed,
are kept apart as [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) keeps them". Whether it cures [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md)'s
defect "or only names it is the owner's call".

**Purpose divergence.** [QUESTION-464](../questions.d/QUESTION-464.md) (raised from A's "emergent purpose
divergence", A20 §I; Open; first line distributed-agency) asks which
procedure of evaluation fixes the purposes a whole's trajectory is said
to diverge from. Its body says the descriptive half is [CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md)'s and
names [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md)'s relativization as one form an answer could take. No
sense declared.
