# THEORY-tmp0usde: DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus (2010) find, across 23 studies, that similarity of team members' mental models predicts team process only when it is measured in a way that captures the structure of their knowledge, while it predicts team performance however it is measured

## The account

DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus (2010) find, across 23 studies, that similarity of
team members' mental models predicts team process only when it is measured in a way that
captures the structure of their knowledge, while it predicts team performance however it is
measured.
