# LIT-3165: Investigating Environmental Links Between Parent Depression and Child Depressive/Anxiety Symptoms Using an Assisted Conception Design

Lewis, G., Rice, F., Harold, G. T., Collishaw, S. & Thapar, A. (2011). *Journal of the American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry*, 50(5), 451–459.e1.

## Key takeaways

- Same cohort and design as Rice et al. 2010 ([LIT-3183](LIT-3183.md)), turned on the
  postnatal side: the parent who raises the child but did not supply its
  gamete.
- Parental depression symptoms go with child symptoms in unrelated pairs
  as much as in related ones. The parenting route is real without
  inheritance.
- Cross-sectional, parent-rated, and about depression symptoms, not
  trauma.
