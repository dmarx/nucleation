# CLAIM-tmp6u4yn: Consequentiality can need jointly acquired capacities

## The claim

A23 §7: "The underlying geometry therefore needs to represent
complementarities, not just distances." The hypergraph {A, B, C} → d
"would generalize the idea of a transition bottleneck to include
distributed prerequisites, coordination and the formation of new causal
channels".

## Scope (the assistant's)

The neglect A23 describes is a property of the search, not only of the
structure. If whether d is consequential is the conjunction of A, B and
C, each capacity's marginal value from a state lacking the others is
zero, and a greedy step that adds the capacity with the largest marginal
gain has no reason to add any of them; an optimizer that values bundles,
or looks two or three steps ahead, does. The claim is therefore that a
marginal optimizer can neglect jointly sufficient capacities, not that
every optimizer does. The enabling structure is a monotone Boolean
function of the capacities, of which the hypergraph's hyperedges are the
minimal sufficient sets.
