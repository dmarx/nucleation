summary: >-
  Clark and Brennan (1991), Grounding in communication. Every collective
  action rests on common ground: mutual knowledge, mutual beliefs and mutual
  assumptions. Communication needs that common ground to be updated moment
  by moment. The process that updates it is **grounding**. Its target is the
  grounding criterion: contributor and partners mutually believe the
  partners have understood well enough for current purposes. **How a
  contribution works.** - A contribution has a presentation phase and an
  acceptance phase. - Participants seek **positive** evidence of
  understanding, not merely the absence of negative evidence. Read for the
  exchange "luria experiment design".
