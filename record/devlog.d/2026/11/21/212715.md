title: 'Going wrong, caught by the owner: Rejected arguments titled with their conclusions, so that rejecting a route read as rejecting a conclusion the owner thinks probably true'
created: '2026-11-21T21:27:15'
tags:
- retro
---

**What happened.** The filing of "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" made five Rejected ARGs. The assistant wrote their titles as the inferences' conclusions, for example "The behaviour of sloppy models carries over to overparameterized neural networks…". Each body said that only the route failed. But the title is what the index shows, and with "Rejected" beside it the title read as the conclusion refuted.

**How it was found.** The owner, asking about the choice of keeping them Rejected or filing each failure as a claim: "populating the arg with the prose of the conclusion and marking it rejected predisposes future inquiry to treat that conclusion (which I think is actually probably correct) as rejected, rather than this particular path to that conclusion as rejected. maybe we need to be more careful about how we construct our args?"

**The fix.** Two alternative decisions, [ADR-tmp44npm](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmp44npm.md) (route first; a standing sentence) and [ADR-tmpcjfyy](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpcjfyy.md) (title as verdict), with the five ARGs retitled under the first. The owner's view that the conclusion probably holds is now its own claim, [CLAIM-tmp7ijze](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7ijze.md).

**What to keep.** The assistant's own previous answer framed the choice as "keep Rejected, or file the failure as a claim". That framing missed the point, because it treated the failure as the thing worth stating, when the conclusion was the thing at risk. A title has to survive being read without its status's meaning in mind. Of the record's 40 Rejected ARGs, most lead with their grounds ("X shows that Y"), which mostly survives that reading. The ones that state the bare conclusion are what the sweep under [ADR-tmp44npm](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmp44npm.md) would fix.
