# THEORY-094: Neither report nor no-report paradigms isolate the neural basis of consciousness, because post-perceptual cognition that tracks the percept persists without report; only a design that removes differential post-perceptual cognition does

## Source

- Block (2019), *What Is Wrong with the No-Report Paradigm and How to Fix
  It*, [LIT-637](../literature.d/LIT-637.md), read in [NOTE-495](../notes.d/NOTE-495.md): the logic of the no-report
  paradigm, "The Need for a No-Post-perceptual Cognition Paradigm",
  Brascamp et al., "Anticipating an Objection", Concluding Remarks.
- Block (2007), [LIT-646](../literature.d/LIT-646.md), read in [NOTE-497](../notes.d/NOTE-497.md): §§1–7, the
  methodological puzzle and correlationism. Target article only.

## What was actually shown

**The puzzle.** Block (2007), [LIT-646](../literature.d/LIT-646.md), states the circle: to find the
neural basis of experience one must decide whether it includes the
machinery of cognitive access, and any evidence of experience seems to
come through access. With report, a correlate of a percept is also a
correlate of judging it, holding it in working memory and choosing a
response. Block (2019), [LIT-637](../literature.d/LIT-637.md), cites that paper as the source of the
problem the no-report paradigm was built to solve.

**Why removing report is not enough.** The no-report paradigm uses reports
to calibrate an indicator, such as optokinetic nystagmus for the
perceived direction of a rivalrous grating, and then has subjects make no
report. Block's argument is that this removes report and not the thing
report expressed. Monkeys spend hours watching gratings with nothing to
do but fixate, and "might have some cognitive states – thinking,
wondering, questioning, musing, and the like – concerning the grating
moving up when it is moving up". This is not mind-wandering, which would
not engender systematic error. It is cognition "systematically aligned
with one or another of the rivalrous percepts". Hence: "Eliminating report
is successful in isolating the neural basis of conscious perception only
if it eliminates post-perceptual cognitive processing such as thought and
judgment about the reportable properties that is systematically
correlated with one of the perceptual representations." The point cuts
both ways. Prefrontal decoding of rivalry contents in no-report monkeys
does not show prefrontal cortex is part of perception. Block also applies
it to the older human no-report studies, "since nothing was done to
prevent subjects thinking about their alternating percepts".

**What would isolate it.** The target is "a no-differential (between
rivalrous percepts)-post-perceptual cognition paradigm". The word
*differential* is there "to avoid begging the question against views that
take conscious perception to be cognitive". Brascamp et al. made rivalry
switches inconspicuous: dots changed direction and coherence every 300 ms,
so subjects could not tell a change of dominant eye from the constant
background changes (d′ for detecting switches indistinguishable from
chance). Density reports and temporal regularities confirmed real
rivalry. The switches "were accessible but mostly not accessed", so no
differential thought followed them.

What could have come out otherwise: the argument stands or falls on
whether uninstructed subjects form percept-aligned thoughts. That is an
empirical premise, and Block argues for it from the situation, not from a
measurement.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say what the neural basis is.** Block (2019) says the
  article's "main point ... is to home in on the methodological issue
  rather than supporting one side". The substantive claim is filed
  separately, as [THEORY-098](THEORY-098.md).
- **It does not say a general fix exists.** "There may be no general
  solution to this problem, but at least in the case of binocular rivalry
  a solution may be at hand." Extending the method beyond rivalry is
  among Block's outstanding questions.
- **It does not say no-report data are worthless.** They remove report
  and its motor and decision components, and they are what a
  no-differential-cognition design refines.
- **It does not rule on higher-order theories in general.** A
  higher-order state that is post-perceptual is ruled out of the basis of
  experience by such a design. Block notes that versions where the
  higher-order state is a simultaneous pointer are not ([NOTE-495](../notes.d/NOTE-495.md),
  Limitations).

## How it bears on the crow account

[THEORY-100](THEORY-100.md) holds that a neural correlate of conscious report in
crows is carried by the nidopallium caudolaterale (NCL), the functional
analogue of prefrontal cortex. Its own caveat, that a report paradigm
cannot separate access from phenomenal consciousness, is the problem this
account states. Its `promote_when` asks, for the "conscious" half, for a
no-report or report-independent paradigm in birds. By this account that
would not be enough. A bird watching rivalrous stimuli with nothing to do
may still process the percept cognitively, and NCL, as the prefrontal
analogue, is where that would show. What the crow account needs for its
"conscious" half is a design in which the bird cannot single out the
perceptual change, such as inconspicuous rivalry, with NCL recorded. No
relation is declared. This account does not rival the crow account,
which already concedes the access problem. It does not replace that
account's reasoning, which is about lamination. And it does not carry its
mechanism further. It bears on the evidence that account names as
promotion.

## Connections

- **[THEORY-102](THEORY-102.md) (Vishne et al.).** Its analysed trials needed no
  report, but each image still had to be judged a non-target. That is
  differential post-perceptual cognition by design, so its transient
  prefrontal content at onset is ambiguous between perception and thought
  about the image. Its sustained ventral temporal code is not ambiguous in
  this way.
- **[THEORY-103](THEORY-103.md)** answers the same puzzle a different way. It does not
  try to isolate a correlate. It infers from capacity that experience
  outruns access.
- **Block (1995), [LIT-643](../literature.d/LIT-643.md)**, supplies the distinction the puzzle
  turns on: access consciousness is a content poised for reasoning and the
  rational control of action and speech, and report is its least weighty
  element. That is why removing report alone was never going to remove
  access.
