# SYNTHESIS-tmpt1te9: The learning-and-inquiry line is eight part accounts and three claims integrated directly, which meet only through relations their components store, most of them at self-formation, and which no entry orders as a sequence

*The account of the learning-and-inquiry line, over the accounts of its
parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). The line was added by [ADR-130](../decisions.d/ADR-130.md) (Active) on 2026-11-21. It
integrates every entry whose `line` includes learning-and-inquiry on that
date: eight part accounts, [SYNTHESIS-109](SYNTHESIS-109.md) to [SYNTHESIS-116](SYNTHESIS-116.md), each a thematic
account of one principle of the owner's manuscript (P2 and P4 to P10),
and three claims integrated directly, [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) (P1), [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) (P3) and
[CLAIM-794](../claims.d/CLAIM-794.md). The parts are taken in the order of the line's blurb:
distinctions, learning, maintained capacity, inquiry, transmission,
collectives. That order is the blurb's, not a claim that the parts form a
sequence (see "Not stated on the record"). It adds nothing; its standing
is its components'.*

## The account

### Distinctions, relative to a target and a budget

**When a distinction must be drawn ([CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md), Active, demonstrated,
thesis; integrated directly, and a component of [SYNTHESIS-110](SYNTHESIS-110.md)).** An
organization acting on what it observes has a policy acceptable in every
circumstance "exactly when, for each observation, the circumstances it
cannot tell apart share an acceptable action". A finer observation never
removes such a policy, and where acceptable actions overlap the coarsest
sufficient observations are several and incomparable, so "which
distinctions must be made legible depends on which acceptable action the
organization commits to". This is the manuscript's P1.

**How categories change as resources grow ([SYNTHESIS-109](SYNTHESIS-109.md), Proposed;
P2).** More resource for observing splits what can be told apart and more
resource for acting merges what can be turned into one another, so
budgeted individuation is indexed by a pair of budgets ([CLAIM-557](../claims.d/CLAIM-557.md)). What
can be told apart only grows along nested probe families compared on every
probe ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)); classes reachable within a budget nest into one tree
under peak-capacity costs ([CLAIM-581](../claims.d/CLAIM-581.md)); the two partitions agree at
partitions common to both ([CLAIM-592](../claims.d/CLAIM-592.md)). Optimal information-bottleneck
partitions are a different object and need not nest ([CLAIM-387](../claims.d/CLAIM-387.md),
[CLAIM-392](../claims.d/CLAIM-392.md)), and closure ([CLAIM-409](../claims.d/CLAIM-409.md)) and bounded observers ([CLAIM-455](../claims.d/CLAIM-455.md)) set
further limits. All eight components are Active and demonstrated. The
account records as not stated that only an ideal observer with nested
probes is guaranteed only to split, and that what can be told apart and
what is optimal to keep are one ordering.

**When a distinction earns a name ([SYNTHESIS-110](SYNTHESIS-110.md), Proposed; P4).** Drawing
a distinction is settled by [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md); keeping a candidate structure as a
unit, by evidence against Occam cost ([CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md), with its limit at
singular models, [CLAIM-188](../claims.d/CLAIM-188.md)); carving a category, by retrieval under a
deadline, which pulls opposite to evidence with copy count ([CLAIM-307](../claims.d/CLAIM-307.md)).
A third criterion, survival under refinement and compression, is open
([QUESTION-113](../questions.d/QUESTION-113.md)), and reuse of classes closed under a process's operations is
Proposed ([CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md)). The account records as not stated that naming is a
separate decision from drawing.

**When a category's success is evidence of a kind ([CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md), Active,
interpretive, thesis; integrated directly).** Predictive success across
contexts warrants treating a category as a real effective kind "only when
the evidence in those contexts does not pass through the category's own
use, or when the effect of that use is modelled", since a category acted
on can produce the regularities that confirm it. It rests on [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md). It
defeats one inference and is not a criterion of kindhood, so [QUESTION-028](../questions.d/QUESTION-028.md)
stays Open. This is the manuscript's P3.

### Learning: a maintained capacity, distinction or channel, occasioned by a discrepancy

**Self-formation ([SYNTHESIS-116](SYNTHESIS-116.md), Proposed; P5).** Regulatory difficulty is
frustration among maintained demands ([CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md)), ordinal, with relief as
strict enlargement and not suppression ([CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)). An unresolved
discrepancy is an occasion for self-formation "and not a guarantee of
it"; it suffices that the agent's own activity produce a maintained new
distinction or channel that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of
later ones, tested by transfer and by intervention on the producing
processes ([CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md), Active, interpretive, thesis; [TERM-099](../terms.d/TERM-099.md) names the
product). Persistence needs a productive cycle among a capacity's
ancestors ([CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md)); an observer's updated estimate is not a change in
what selects ([CLAIM-540](../claims.d/CLAIM-540.md)); [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) constructs the threshold; [QUESTION-488](../questions.d/QUESTION-488.md)
leaves a uniform change of priority open. [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md) (Proposed) defines
learning more broadly, with no discrepancy required. The account records
as not stated that learning is the formation of a maintained capacity
occasioned by a discrepancy: [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) is a sufficient condition, not a
definition.

### Maintained capacity

**Forming, keeping and moving into a capacity ([SYNTHESIS-112](SYNTHESIS-112.md), Proposed;
P6).** A form has three resource requirements, to construct, maintain and
move into or out of it, none fixing another ([CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)). Reaching a
configuration has its own threshold, the least peak over admissible paths
([CLAIM-571](../claims.d/CLAIM-571.md)), whose difference from holding is reconfiguration slack
([TERM-106](../terms.d/TERM-106.md)). Feasible, reachable and reliably reached are three properties
([CLAIM-567](../claims.d/CLAIM-567.md)). A budget pegged to last period's peak never rises and loses
transitions that need slack, under that rule only ([CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md)). All five
components are Active.

**Capacities kept for rare use ([SYNTHESIS-113](SYNTHESIS-113.md), Proposed; P7).** A
capability seldom called on decays with disuse and is shown only by a
retention test or the contingency ([CLAIM-558](../claims.d/CLAIM-558.md); [TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md)). A correction, a
procedure and a maintained capacity are three things ([CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md); [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md),
Challenger to Columbia). Support that does the work a capacity is learned
from can lower it, has a deadline, and can leave different capabilities
later ([CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md), [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)), and can erode the capacity to
govern it ([CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md)). Matched trajectories hide differences an
intervention exposes ([CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)), and only interventions on production show
where maintenance is realized ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md); [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)). All components are
Active. The account records as not stated that maintenance is known only
by intervention for capacities in general.

### Inquiry: specification that can revise its own language, answers and criteria

**Inquiry as specification ([SYNTHESIS-114](SYNTHESIS-114.md), Proposed; P8).** "Inquiry is a
form of specification … with one difference, that an inquiry can revise
what an answer could be" ([CLAIM-470](../claims.d/CLAIM-470.md)), written as a tuple that is reflexive
when any of its parts becomes what it pursues ([TERM-095](../terms.d/TERM-095.md)). Specifying is
resource-bounded, and stored specification cheapens some later questions
and can harden others ([CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md); [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md), the inquiry frontier). A
relational vocabulary can leave a constraint inexpressible ([TERM-091](../terms.d/TERM-091.md)).
Inquiry is one of a partial order of achievements, not a nested sequence
([CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)). On self-governance: [QUESTION-388](../questions.d/QUESTION-388.md) (Open), [CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md), [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)
and [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) (regime, governing organization and capacity to transform it
"can persist or fail separately"); [CLAIM-761](../claims.d/CLAIM-761.md) on stored work (Active since 2026-11-20; [SYNTHESIS-114](SYNTHESIS-114.md) gives it as Proposed);
[QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md) (Open) on improving the improver. The account records as not
stated that reflexive inquiry is self-governed only where its criteria of
revision are its own and remain revisable.

**Refinement exposes misfits ([CLAIM-794](../claims.d/CLAIM-794.md), Proposed, empirical, thesis;
integrated directly).** In a record that amends its grammar when a move
fits nothing, refining a category makes misfits in the refined region more
noticeable, not fewer. It rests on [CLAIM-419](../claims.d/CLAIM-419.md), [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) and [CLAIM-470](../claims.d/CLAIM-470.md), and
is the one part of the manuscript's predicted cycle that passed the test
for a claim whose premises are claims. Its test is a recode of the
decision census, [CASE-300](../cases.d/CASE-300.md).

### Transmission

**Transmission and anchoring ([SYNTHESIS-115](SYNTHESIS-115.md), Proposed, version 2; P9).**
Convergence is not preservation ([CLAIM-270](../claims.d/CLAIM-270.md), [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md), [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)). Serial
transmission is filtered by the transmitters' biases ([CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md),
Proposed), with convergence to the prior proved only for sampling learners
([ARG-035](../arguments.d/ARG-035.md), Proposed). Local error can coexist with global drift ([CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md)),
and arguments restated across memoryless sessions lose identity ([CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md);
[CLAIM-191](../claims.d/CLAIM-191.md), Proposed). Four candidate anchors are held separately
([CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md), [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md), [CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md), [CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md)), and anchoring is proposed as
one contraction told apart by what is conserved ([CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md), [ARG-124](../arguments.d/ARG-124.md)), as
securing what its reference is tied to ([CLAIM-790](../claims.d/CLAIM-790.md)), with re-reading a kept
source settling on an offset ([CLAIM-793](../claims.d/CLAIM-793.md)) and exact copies of a revised
source going stale ([CLAIM-789](../claims.d/CLAIM-789.md)); anchors tied to nothing are bias
([CLAIM-315](../claims.d/CLAIM-315.md)) and anchors entrench ([CLAIM-184](../claims.d/CLAIM-184.md)), all Proposed. What a record
can secure is held to be traceability ([CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md), Proposed), with
sufficiency, accountability and correctness coming apart ([CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md)) and no
withdrawals computed ([CLAIM-664](../claims.d/CLAIM-664.md)). The account records as not stated that a
stable code is [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s conserved observable, and that the four anchors
are one requirement.

### Collectives

**A collective's correction ([SYNTHESIS-111](SYNTHESIS-111.md), Proposed; P10).** Separable
achievements: having, knowing and deploying ([CLAIM-296](../claims.d/CLAIM-296.md)); access, authority
and action ([CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)); the ladder of uptake, each rung possible without
the later ones ([CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)); legibility, volitional efficacy and
correctability ([CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)); and, Proposed, six stages for an objection
bounded by the weakest ([CLAIM-732](../claims.d/CLAIM-732.md)). Lock-in from selected evidence and from
self-scoring need different remedies ([CLAIM-466](../claims.d/CLAIM-466.md)), with [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md), [CLAIM-416](../claims.d/CLAIM-416.md)
and [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) on governance through evidence and criteria. Effective action
can grow while correctability by others degrades ([CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md)), effective
action and the capacity to govern are separate achievements ([CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md)),
and first-order responsiveness is no evidence of responsiveness to reasons
for revising the mechanism ([CLAIM-719](../claims.d/CLAIM-719.md), Proposed); [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) is the case. The
account records as not stated that a collective learns only if every link
holds, up to revision of the standards of uptake.

## Not stated on the record

- **That the parts form a sequence,** from distinctions to learning,
  maintained capacity, inquiry, transmission and collectives, or that they
  are the stages of the manuscript's cycle. [CLAIM-794](../claims.d/CLAIM-794.md) says it does not
  claim the manuscript's eight-stage cycle is a sequence, and points to
  [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) and [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md), which both deny that such steps form one. The
  order above is the blurb's.
- **That learning and inquiry are one process, or that inquiry is a kind of
  learning.** [SYNTHESIS-116](SYNTHESIS-116.md)'s self-formation and [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md)'s learning are
  not tied by any entry to [CLAIM-470](../claims.d/CLAIM-470.md)'s specification. The stored relations
  between the two parts ([CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) with [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md), [CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md) and
  [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md)) concern governance and stored specification, not an identity.
- **That re-carving of optimal categories is learning.** [SYNTHESIS-109](SYNTHESIS-109.md)
  holds that optimal partitions need not nest, and [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) that a new
  distinction refines the agent's partition. No entry says whether a
  re-carving that is not a refinement is self-formation or learning in
  [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md)'s sense.
- **That [CLAIM-794](../claims.d/CLAIM-794.md)'s refinement is [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)'s.** [CLAIM-794](../claims.d/CLAIM-794.md) is about a
  record's grammar and rests on inquiry's claims, not on [SYNTHESIS-109](SYNTHESIS-109.md)'s
  results on what can be told apart.
- **That self-formation carries [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s three requirements.** [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)
  separates formation from maintenance and [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) uses it, but no entry
  ties [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s construction, maintenance and transition costs to
  [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s route.
- **That transmission is a case of a collective's correction, or the
  reverse.** [SYNTHESIS-115](SYNTHESIS-115.md) and [SYNTHESIS-111](SYNTHESIS-111.md) share [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) and no
