  One-layer transformers on modular arithmetic mod 97: freezing an
  embedding or a body pre-grokked on addition, subtraction or
  multiplication and training the rest on another operation lowers the
  smallest training fraction at which grokking occurs for some pairs
  (elementary operations to linear expressions), and for quadratic and
  cubic polynomials more often raises it or prevents grokking. In the
  factorizable rows, which are h∘g with g elementary, transferring g's own
  module rarely helps. Three seeds; first-stage cost not counted. Read in
  [NOTE-2168](../notes.d/NOTE-2168.md); account [THEORY-2086](../theory.d/THEORY-2086.md).
