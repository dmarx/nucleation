## What was missing, and is now filed

- **Four works, registered Deferred and unread:**

  - Henrich and Muthukrishna 2021, [LIT-tmps92qc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmps92qc.md);
  - Van Lange and Bergmann 2026, [LIT-tmpblw9a](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpblw9a.md);
  - Maynard Smith and Szathmáry, *The Major Transitions in Evolution*, [LIT-tmp8l5fd](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8l5fd.md).

  The synthesis names the first and last as foundational and cites the
  middle two in §4 and §7. Each entry says where a copy is listed, and
  none has been fetched.
- **[CLAIM-tmp47w4i](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp47w4i.md)** (§3, demonstrated). Coexistence by frequency
  dependence is not cooperation: Hawk–Dove coexists at V/C Hawks with
  exploitation intact. The record held the Black Queen case and the
  invasion criterion, not the point.
- **[CLAIM-tmpjnqe9](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjnqe9.md)** (§7, interpretive). Cooperation within groups and
  the coexistence of groups can trade off, and Nowak's group-selection
  rule is an exact instance. Its cooperation is bought by group
  extinction, which the reader checked against the paper's text. Local
  cooperation, coexistence of groups and impartial concern are three
  achievements.
- **[QUESTION-tmp22a7y](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp22a7y.md)** (§9). Can moral norms be stabilizing mechanisms
  among groups in Chesson's sense?

## Support, added 2026-11-04

Under ADR-061, an ARG is filed where the record says something about
support. One filing did. Van Lange and Bergmann were cited as the human
case of CLAIM-tmpjnqe9 before they were read, and the reading carries only
the claim's first link. That verdict is ARG-tmpv5t3t (source, Rejected as
an inference to the claim as titled). The review moves from the claim's
grounds to corroboration: THEORY-tmpmr4g4 corroborates CLAIM-tmpjnqe9
(ADR-058). West et al.'s Table 1 is independent evidence for CLAIM-474, so
THEORY-tmpm0ct3 corroborates it; no doubt about that support is in
question, so it needs no ARG. West et al. and THEORY-141 differ on which
mechanism mattered most, not on what is possible, so they are not rivals,
and the note on THEORY-141 is enough.
