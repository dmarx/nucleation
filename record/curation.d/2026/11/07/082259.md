**The admitted, in brief.**

- **SYNTHESIS-tmpfupae (from CLAIM-600).** The case is a village water
  scheme that meets every condition, judged before withdrawal. It fails
  once the field officer whose visits kept contributions common knowledge
  is withdrawn. Two corrections are marked:
  - the conditions are judged, and standing inputs named, before
    withdrawal, which closes a free parameter;
  - the defeater's clause on the length of substitutive support, which the
    title dropped on 2026-11-05, is removed.

  It moved as Current under ADR-077, since the claim was Active and its
  four premises are Active.
- **SYNTHESIS-tmprnoj1 (from CLAIM-336).** The defeater's second clause is
  narrowed to the held-out test of matched complexity that A55 §7 gave and
  ARG-065 holds. The case is one where transport observables are fixed by
  the receiver's state at reading time. The thesis stays the owner's.
- **SYNTHESIS-tmpfp6zi (QUESTION-395).** Records kept per capacity fix only
  the marginals of CLAIM-587's conjunction, so they bound readiness by the
  Fréchet inequalities. A refutation from the weakest capacity survives
  decay (CLAIM-558); a certification does not. Its bounds are textbook. The
  criterion and the asymmetry are new, and the reader flagged that a filer
  could fairly have filed it as a derived claim instead. With
  SYNTHESIS-tmpfupae, it gives the readiness line its first syntheses.

## Found along the way, for the owner

- **CLAIM-404** quotes CLAIM-348's version-3 wording, "cannot be justified
  by the description that preserves it". CLAIM-348 narrowed it on
  2026-10-29 to "cannot be justified solely by".
- **CLAIM-572's summary** says greedy stopping misses the least total "in
  about a quarter of random systems". The rate depends on the generator:
  the reader's gave about half. "Of the random systems checked" would be
  exact.
- **CLAIM-401:**
  - its citation of THEORY-752 reads more than the reading shows;
    QUESTION-222 carries the projection-relativity;
  - its dynamical-independence cell rests on THEORY-820 and THEORY-733,
    which are in neither its `grounds` nor its `rests_on`.
- **CLAIM-425** calls itself "the same achievement" as CLAIM-424, but its
  title never requires revising standards. Either the title gains that, or
  the body says "the analogous regulation".
- **By-products, not filed:**
  - a demonstrated corollary of CLAIM-366, answering part of QUESTION-127.
    Common descent is constant on the orbits of every function-preserving
    symmetry that is a diffeomorphism of the parameters. A test with a
    fixed Euclidean budget is not: with f = v·relu(ux) and a rescaling by
    one half, the squared gradient norm is 612 at (4, 1) and 288 at its
    image (2, 2);
  - on CASE-206's vertex, a non-trivial agreement between outside and
    inside needs a shared budget of 4.125. That is more than the cheapest
    readiness (1.866) and more than full observation (4);
  - UFI is monotone in the actuator set, so it cannot be QUESTION-380's
    predictor where adding a nucleus can hurt.
