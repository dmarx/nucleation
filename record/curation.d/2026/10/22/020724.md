The owner asked for the readings registered from the exchange on experience
and interpretation (curation entry of 2026-10-21) to be added "where you can
find them". Every work was sought on 2026-10-22, using lawful copies only.

**How they were read.** Seven sub-agents of this session each took two to
five works under a shared brief. Each one:
- looked for a lawful full text through OpenAlex and Unpaywall, then the
  publisher, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, arXiv, bioRxiv, PsyArXiv/OSF and
  authors' repositories;
- read every line except reference lists;
- filed a NOTE and a THEORY for each distinct assertion;
- checked every quotation attributed to a work against its extracted text
  by script.

Each NOTE's history names the copy read. Where a version of record was
closed, the preprint or accepted manuscript was read, and the NOTE says
so. That applies to Whyte et al., Laukkonen et al., Bo et al.,
Chandia-Jorquera et al., Woods et al. and Ward. No reading claims more
than its copy.

**Outcomes.** Eighteen read; four Unavailable under [ADR-041](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-041.md).

Two works got no THEORY. Barrett et al.'s claims are already held as
[THEORY-057](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-057.md) and [THEORY-045](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-045.md). The higher-order survey argues for no thesis of
its own.

**What the reading corrected in the exchange.** Each correction is
recorded in the work's LIT.
- IWMT does not require current sensory coupling. Imagination runs on the
  same world model "decoupled from sense-data". What it needs is a
  capacity for coupling and a history of it.
- Ward never discusses brains in vats, development or grounding. The
  developmental objection credited to him is the exchange's. His
  objection is organicist.
- Barrett et al. never mention CBT or reappraisal.
- In Bo et al., most emotion-responsive regions, cortical ones included,
  were unchanged by reappraisal, not only a subcortical system.
- Representationalism is nearest the constitutive thesis only on the
  character of experience.
- Lee-Youngzie et al. formalize binding, not unity, and their worked
  topology is wrong as printed.
- Gładziejewski does not say that the self-model exaggerates its unity.
- Graziano's "small" illusionism still denies phenomenal essence.
- Chandia-Jorquera et al. found no link between content ratings and the
  EEG.
- Woods et al.'s preprint is on MindRxiv, not PsyArXiv.

[CLAIM-355](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) record the bearing of each reading.
- **The thesis.** None of the eighteen supports the constitutive identity.
  Several offer rival identities or bridges.
- **The objection.** Every reading that bears on the counter-claim
  supports it.
- **The demonstrated claim.** Gurnee et al. supply a real-system parallel
  to its first separation.
- **The unity question.** The Penrose tribar is a candidate case of
  non-gluing within one percept. That inference is the record's own.
