# LIT-1459: Pleasure systems in the brain

Kent C. Berridge & Morten L. Kringelbach (2015), *Neuron* 86(3):646–664 —
DOI-10.1016/j.neuron.2015.02.018

## Key takeaways

- **Three components of reward.** Reward is "liking (core reactions to
  hedonic impact), wanting (motivation process of incentive salience), and
  learning (Pavlovian or instrumental associations and cognitive
  representations)". Their mechanisms can be told apart ("A neuroscience
  of pleasure").
- **Liking in quotes is objective.** 'Liking' names a measurable hedonic
  reaction, neural or behavioural. Plain liking is the subjective
  experience. The two are measured independently, and "Subjective ratings
  are not always more accurate about hedonic impact than objective hedonic
  reactions".
- **Taste reactivity measures liking without report.** Sweet tastes draw
  tongue protrusions and lip licking, and bitter tastes draw gapes. These
  reactions are homologous in newborn humans, apes, monkeys, rats and mice.
  They track satiety, and they come apart from intake and seeking under
  dopamine blockade.
- **Hotspots generate liking.** Each hotspot is about 1 mm³ in the rat. One
  lies in the rostrodorsal medial shell of nucleus accumbens, and one in
  the posterior ventral pallidum, the one site whose loss abolishes liking.
  Candidates are emerging in orbitofrontal cortex, insula and parabrachial
  nucleus. Opioid stimulation in a hotspot can double sweetness 'liking'.
  Dopamine stimulation at the same site raises 'wanting' and leaves
  'liking' unchanged.
- **Wanting without liking.** Dopamine elevation, lateral hypothalamic
  stimulation (the rats ate four times as much while their reactions to
  sweetness grew no more positive) and the deep-brain electrodes of Heath
  and of Schlaepfer et al. all raise 'wanting' without 'liking'.
  Incentive sensitization explains addiction as wanting magnified while
  liking stays flat or tolerates.
- **Liking can be unconscious.** Objective 'liking' can occur "alone and
  unconsciously … without any subjective pleasure feeling at all" and
  still change goal-directed behaviour.
- **An affective keyboard.** Running front to back along the accumbens
  shell, local inhibition releases desire and then dread. Environment and
  cortex can retune it.
