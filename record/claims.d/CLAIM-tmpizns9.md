# CLAIM-tmpizns9: Pragmatic fidelity is approximate preservation of the ways an utterance conditions, transforms and constrains later interpretation and interaction, across transient framing and learned interpretive dispositions

## The claim

A45 §10: "**The pragmatic fidelity of a translation can be characterized by the
extent to which source and target utterances induce corresponding patterns of
context-sensitive interpretation and subsequent communicative behavior, across
both transient framing conditions and learned interpretive dispositions.**" A48,
outline v3, retitled *What Survives Translation? Communicative Frames,
In-Context Learning, and Dynamical Pragmatic Fidelity*: "fidelity may be
understood as approximate preservation of the ways an utterance conditions,
transforms, and constrains subsequent interpretation and communicative
interaction." Its primary hypothesis (A48 §1.4), "Translations can exhibit
static pragmatic equivalence while differing in the structure of the
interpretive and communicative trajectories they induce", is [CLAIM-tmpx6akp](CLAIM-tmpx6akp.md).

## Why it was replaced

"Corresponding patterns" is still an equivalence, approximate or not. U20
objected to exactly that ([CLAIM-tmpumy4f](CLAIM-tmpumy4f.md)), and A50 replaced it with [CLAIM-tmpt20oo](CLAIM-tmpt20oo.md).
