The first two batches of the reading queue that the sweep of the Unfetched
works produced (curation 2026/11/22/104609, PR [#644](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/644)): the open copies that
answered. Batch A was [LIT-1980](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1980.md), [LIT-1983](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1983.md) and [LIT-1138](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1138.md); batch B was [LIT-1874](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1874.md),
[LIT-2373](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2373.md) and [LIT-2363](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2363.md). Two readers wrote reports
(`scratch/readers/` on `claude/readers/sweep-a` and `claude/readers/sweep-b`),
and the filer checked each against the downloaded copy before filing, as a
claim to verify, not a finding.

Readers read three works each in sequence, not one reader per work in parallel, to stay under the account's usage limit.

  held before (no hit for the DOI, the title or "Red Cross"). Read in full

  University of Ljubljana repository copy (CC BY 4.0), SAGE's online-first

- **[LIT-1874](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1874.md)** (Mulgan 2018): read in full from the Springer open-access

rechecked against the copy's text. Two page references in the reports were
