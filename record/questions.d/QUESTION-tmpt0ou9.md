# QUESTION-tmpt0ou9: Can readers judge footing and stance reliably and validly in the categories the designs use?

## Why it is a question

Footing and stance are what the line's theses say survives or is lost.
[CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md)'s defeat condition measures "pragmatic observables (stance,
footing, alignment, expected uptake)", [CLAIM-061](../claims.d/CLAIM-061.md) says renderings can differ
"in footing and illocutionary force", and [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md) makes the primary
outcome of a reconstruction study "pragmatic-frame retention judged blind
to condition". Each test of these claims is a judgement of footing or
stance by somebody.

The record holds one footing judgement made twice of the same texts, and it
disagreed with itself. [CASE-012](../cases.d/CASE-012.md): the analyst's four speaker labels for its
own refrains "drifted" between A38 and A39, so that "'The party always gets
its cut' went from cynical complicity to an informal rule without comment".
The judge knew what each text was meant to do, having written it, and still
did not hold its labels between two passes. [CLAIM-tmpelp3h](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpelp3h.md), granted on
2026-10-10, adds that no design in the record gives judges a definition of
footing, a response format beyond the analyst's category list, a
reliability target, or a validation against a contrast whose answer is
fixed in advance.

So the question is not whether footing exists, nor whether lay readers can
tell teasing from reproach. It is whether readers' judgements in the
categories the designs use ([CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md)'s "condemnation, solidarity, amusement
and irony", [CASE-040](../cases.d/CASE-040.md)'s "tone, stance, solidarity, and authority") are
reliable and measure what they are said to measure. Until that is known, a
defeat test run on those categories tests the categories as much as the
thesis.

The construct comes from Goffman's "Footing", in *Forms of Talk*
([LIT-780](../literature.d/LIT-780.md)), which decomposes the speaker into animator, author and
principal. The record holds the book unread, so the definition judges
would be given has no reading under it yet.

## What would count as an answer

A study that reports, for a fixed definition and a fixed response format:

- **Reliability between judges and between passes** of one judge, each
  against a target fixed in advance. Which coefficient, and what target,
  needs a source on reliability coefficients that the record does not yet
  hold.
- **Known-groups validity**: judgements that separate renderings built to
  differ only in the attributed speaker, such as [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md)'s reattributions
  of one sentence to a friend, a parent, a judge and a comedian, and that do
  not separate renderings built to differ only in wording with the speaker
  held fixed.

Stage 0 of the discriminating study ([CASE-tmp1uzfn](../cases.d/CASE-tmp1uzfn.md)) is that design. A
negative answer would not show that footing is unreal. It would show that
the designs' categories cannot carry the line's defeat tests, and the
categories would have to be revised before the theses are tested.

## Note of 2026-10-10: a documented case

The search of 2026-10-10 that the owner asked for found annotation studies
that bear on this question, and [CASE-tmp58yn4](../cases.d/CASE-tmp58yn4.md) files two of them.

What they answer. FactBank labels one event's factuality separately for each
source that reports it, so the proposition is held fixed while the
source-relative label varies, and trained annotators agree at κ = 0.82. A
stance label that is separable from the proposition, close to Goffman's
principal, can be annotated reliably when its categories are defined by
discriminating tests and the task is decomposed. Kiesling et al. (2018)
annotate Du Bois's stance triangle ([LIT-779](../literature.d/LIT-779.md)), with an investment dimension
defined as the split of animator from principal, and reach only α = 0.40 to
0.57, below 0.4 for investment and alignment in most of their material, with
agreement that changes from one community to another. Interpersonal stance,
the construct nearest the designs' categories, is only moderately reliable
even for trained judges. That is a negative-leaning answer, and it supports
this question's worry that a defeat test in those categories tests the
categories.

What they leave. Neither reports agreement between two passes of one judge,
which is the failure [CASE-012](../cases.d/CASE-012.md) shows. Neither runs the known-groups test,
renderings built to differ only in the attributed speaker. Neither uses the
designs' categories ([CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md)'s, [CASE-040](../cases.d/CASE-040.md)'s) or lay readers. No study found
labels Goffman's participation roles as such. The question stays open, and
Stage 0 of [CASE-tmp1uzfn](../cases.d/CASE-tmp1uzfn.md) is still the design that would answer it. FactBank
suggests how to write that stage: decompose the judgement and give a test
for each category. Kiesling et al. cite Craggs and Wood (2004) on
Krippendorff's α, a source on reliability coefficients this question says
the record lacks.
