# LIT-tmpkq6x5: Dynamic Changes in Phase-Amplitude Coupling Facilitate Spatial Attention Control in Fronto-Parietal Cortex

Szczepanski, S. M., Crone, N. E., Kuperman, R. A., Auguste, K. I., Parvizi, J., Knight, R. T. (2014). Dynamic Changes in Phase-Amplitude Coupling Facilitate Spatial Attention Control in Fronto-Parietal Cortex. *PLoS Biology, 12(8), e1001936*. doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1001936.

## Key takeaways

- Delta/theta (2–5 Hz) phase to high-gamma amplitude coupling during held spatial attention, in 123 of 273 electrodes across frontal, parietal and visual cortex (NOTE-tmpryuio).
- Coupling strength predicts reaction time trial by trial in 28 electrodes; neither band's amplitude does.
- No manipulation, no waveform-shape control: it shows that coupling tracks attention, not that it improves it (THEORY-tmp9pem2).

## Standing in the record

Reported as Jennings's evidence for attention as cross-frequency coordination. Unread, and A5's account of how the paper uses it is unverified.
