# SYNTHESIS-tmpgtv7b: When a structure is worth naming: a candidate is kept when its effect-to-noise ratio beats its Occam cost, but only where its model stays regular at the switch-off, and there the regular count overcharges it; what a spectral gap makes definite is the subspace it isolates, the Fisher and the NTK Gram matrix share their gaps, and a closed collective variable needs no block; evidence and retrieval carve in opposite directions with copy count, how good a mixture is is not how discriminable it is, persistence stated for eigenvectors fails on the monograph's own example, and how the criteria relate stays open

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line ("Formal apparatus",
ADR-040), the part on when a structure or distinction is worth naming
as a unit. The line's account is built over it and the accounts of the
line's other parts. The mechanical community map put this part together
with four entries on what an invariant keeps and forgets (CLAIM-043,
CLAIM-132, CLAIM-148, CLAIM-209). Read through, those are a separate
inquiry, with one pair of entries linking the two (CASE-097 illustrates
CLAIM-148), and they have their own part account, drafted with this one.
Most of this part's entries are first in another line, where accounts
already set them out: CLAIM-154, CLAIM-164, CLAIM-188 and CLAIM-388 in
the distributed-agency line's account of when a combined form is one
system (SYNTHESIS-054), and CLAIM-307 in its account of deploying before a
deadline (SYNTHESIS-057). This account integrates them again because
their `line` includes formal-apparatus. CLAIM-309, CLAIM-377, CASE-096,
CASE-097 and QUESTION-113 are in this line only, and no account integrated
them before.*

## The account

- **The evidence criterion.** CLAIM-154 (dual-filed from MOM-CLM-160,
  thesis, demonstrated, Active): by the Savage–Dickey ratio, "ln BF(keep) =
  ½(d'² − Occam), so a direction, block or sector is real enough to name
  exactly when δ/ε > τ with τ² = ln(s²/σ²), the information the data added
  about it." "The threshold is not a taste parameter." In the conjugate
  Gaussian case "the rule is the Bayes factor exactly", re-checked here.
  It does not find a candidate: "The rule prices a candidate; it does not
  find one."
- **Where it holds.** CLAIM-188 (the record's, from a synthesis review,
  counter, demonstrated, Active) objects to it: where the model with the
  candidate is singular at the parameters that switch it off, the penalty
  is λ log n − (m − 1) log log n, and "the rule overcharges a candidate and
  prunes structure the exact Bayes factor would keep". "Because the gap
  grows with log n, more data does not wash it out". Its note of
  2026-10-29 checks this on CASE-152's product model at every n from 10²
  to 10¹². CLAIM-154's version 3 concedes it, and its title now carries
  the condition: "where the model with the candidate stays regular at the
  parameters that switch it off".
- **What is named.** CLAIM-164 (dual-filed from MOM-CLM-144, thesis,
  demonstrated, Active): "the gap picks the object, and the object is a
  vector only when its own gap is wide". Where values are degenerate, "the
  k-th vector" names nothing. Its note of 2026-10-20 adds that the
  dimension of the subspace "is not the number of slow variables", and
  that of 2026-10-29 that Zhang et al. keep the subspace without requiring
  a gap.
- **One exact instance.** CLAIM-377 (the record's own, split from
  THEORY-084 at the owner's direction, thesis, demonstrated, Active): the
  model's Fisher under Gaussian noise of precision β is βJᵀJ, and its
  non-zero eigenvalues are β times those of the empirical NTK Gram matrix
  JJᵀ, with the same multiplicities. "It says nothing about what the
  spectrum looks like, which is THEORY-084's."
- **Closure without a block.** CLAIM-388 (A5's slogan of the exchange of
  2026-10-19, made exact by the record, thesis, demonstrated, Active): in a
  VAR(1) with uniform coupling, "the sum of all units is exactly
  dynamically independent at every stable coupling strength", while every
  partition into two or more groups "couples as strongly across its edges
  as within them". "So closure of a collective variable does not imply
  that it is the aggregate of a weakly coupled block." In the eigenbasis of
  A the system is exactly decomposable.
- **Evidence against retrieval.** CLAIM-307 (from MOM-CLM-166, reading the
  owner's LEGO parable, CASE-094; thesis, demonstrated, Active): evidence
  that a distinction exists grows with its copies, while the search a bin
  saves shrinks with them, "so the bins a deadline forces are often the
  ones the data least supports". "That the criteria disagree on every row
  is a fact of these parameters, not a theorem." "Use frequency enters
  retrieval and not detection."
- **Value against discriminability.** CLAIM-309 (the record's reading of
  the smoothie note in the owner's LEGO monograph, thesis, demonstrated,
  Active): under linear mixing a smoothie's distance from milk is
  (1 − f)|s − m|, which only falls, so "a preference that rises and then
  falls with milk is over the ratio and cannot be a d′". CASE-096 (the
  owner's line, stipulated, a thought-experiment, Active) is the case: "The
  preference is over a composition, not an amount."
- **Persistence, stated for eigenvectors.** CASE-097 (the record's
  computation on Appendix H of the owner's LEGO monograph, a fictional
  construction, Active): the castle topic, kept unchanged across
  refinement, "persists as no eigenmode": the eigenvector nearest it is the
  second at rank 2 and the first at rank 3, about 51° apart. That "castle
  persists" "is true of the factor the matrices were built from and false
  of their eigenvectors." It does not show "That spectral methods cannot
  recover topics."
- **The question over them.** QUESTION-113 (filed 2026-10-15, Open) sets
  out three criteria for when a distinction earns a name, evidence
  (CLAIM-154), retrieval (CLAIM-307) and persistence (the monograph's Part
  III), and asks "do the three ever pick the same categories?" It offers
  that persistence "looks like evidence summed over scales", and gives
  against it that persistence "is relative to the refinements tried and
  the threshold chosen". It carries the owner's objection from the
  monograph's drafting: "our definition of "object like" needs to apply to
  objects as well."

## Links the components state, and where

- **The narrowing.** CLAIM-188 objects to CLAIM-154, and CLAIM-154's
  `defeated_if` excludes a family singular at the switch-off: "CLAIM-188
  concedes that the rule overcharges such a candidate." CLAIM-154's summary:
  "Narrowed to candidates whose model stays regular where they are switched
  off; where it is singular the rule overcharges (CLAIM-188)." CLAIM-188's
  body: "That is the kind of failure CLAIM-154's own defeater counts."
- **The gap and its price.** CLAIM-154 and CLAIM-164 complement each
  other. CLAIM-164's summary: "With CLAIM-154 it is a criterion: the gap
  picks the object, and the Occam factor says whether the gap is big
  enough." CLAIM-154's body: "CLAIM-164 says what the named object is, and
  this claim says how big the gap must be." CLAIM-164 leaves "How large
  the gap must be" to CLAIM-154.
- **The Fisher and the NTK.** CLAIM-377 and CLAIM-164 complement each
  other. CLAIM-164's note of 2026-10-19: "A gap in the Fisher's spectrum is
  the same gap in the NTK Gram's, and the subspaces the two gaps isolate
  correspond, so a gap test run on either is the same test." It adds that
  the block inheritance and the threshold "are still THEORY-084's,
  Proposed. This claim does not need them: its core is Davis–Kahan."
- **A mode with no block.** CLAIM-388's "What it does not say": "The gap
  that makes the mode definite is the one CLAIM-164 names, between A's
  eigenvalues α + (n−1)β and α − β. That is a gap between modes, not
  between groups of members."
- **Two criteria for carving.** CLAIM-307 and CLAIM-154 complement each
  other. CLAIM-307's summary: "CLAIM-154 says when a distinction is real
  enough to name. Its evidence grows with the number of copies." Its body
  takes d'² and the Occam term "from CLAIM-154's own statement".
- **The same reversal twice.** CLAIM-309 and CLAIM-307 complement each
  other. CLAIM-309's "The pattern": "In both, a signal-detection quantity
  is put in place of the owner's quantity, and the owner's own words, kept
  beside the formal reading (in the same file here, a link away there),
  contradict it." CLAIM-309 grounds on CASE-096, and CASE-096 illustrates
  it: "Its second reading, in signal detection terms, has the direction
  wrong (CLAIM-309)."
- **The three criteria.** QUESTION-113 names CLAIM-154 for evidence,
  CLAIM-307 for retrieval and, for persistence, CLAIM-164: "the gap picks
  the object." Its body: "CASE-097 shows that, stated for eigenvectors,
  persistence fails even in the monograph's own example." CASE-097 answers
  in kind: "A persistence criterion for naming a category (QUESTION-113)
  has to be stated for factors recovered under such conditions, not for
  eigenvectors."

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no component states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **Persistence judged on the subspace a gap isolates.** That QUESTION-113's
  persistence criterion, stated for CLAIM-164's object rather than for
  eigenvectors, singles out the categories that persist. It fails on
  CASE-097's own matrices, as computed at this review: at both ranks the
  span of the non-zero modes contains the refined-away spaceship topic
  wholly, as it contains the castle topic, so they do not
  single out castle. CASE-097 itself puts the criterion on factors
  recovered under identifying conditions, not on any spectral object.
- **The opposition survives the singular correction.** That CLAIM-307's
  opposition of evidence and retrieval holds as well under CLAIM-188's
  penalty. Both penalties grow logarithmically in the count and evidence
  linearly, so the derivation is one line, and what plays d'² in a
  singular model is left open.
- **Value as a fourth criterion.** That how good an arrangement is
  (CLAIM-309) is a criterion for naming beside QUESTION-113's three.
  "Value" is a free parameter, and CLAIM-307 already carries how often an
  item is wanted into retrieval; CLAIM-309 is about a mixture, not a
  category.
- **The Occam cost of a Fisher direction read from the NTK.** That
  CLAIM-154's price for each Fisher eigendirection follows from CLAIM-377's
  shared spectrum. It is held outside the part, by THEORY-080 (Proposed),
  for a linearised network with a Gaussian prior.
- **Pruning a latent dimension of a factorization is the singular case.**
  That CLAIM-188's overcharge applies when CLAIM-154's rule prunes a
  dimension of the sibling part's factorization (CLAIM-148): once a column
  of one factor is zero, the matching column of the other leaves the
  likelihood. It is held outside the line, by THEORY-079 (Proposed) for
  reduced-rank regression and by THEORY-087's worked case of it, and
  CASE-152's product model is its one-dimensional form.
- **Persistence as evidence summed over scales.** QUESTION-113 states it as
  the open half of its question; it is not filed apart from it.

- That the rule's magnitude is trustworthy away from the threshold

- That the three criteria are one, or three; QUESTION-113 is open.
