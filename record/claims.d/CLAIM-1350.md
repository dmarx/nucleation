# CLAIM-1350: An unconstituted purpose is not an inaccessible one

## The claim

A21 §7 sorts why an institution cannot realize a possibility, each with
its intervention: it values it but lacks resources ("Supply resources")
or capability ("Develop competence"); it lacks the distinctions to
recognize it ("Develop understanding"); it recognizes it but does not
value it; some constituents value it "but cannot make it consequential"
("Reform authority or contestation mechanisms"); or "The collective
concern has not yet formed" ("Enable new associations and cooperative
activity"). The last is the one the claim is about. "Before the relevant
concepts, techniques, collaborations, and phenomena were available, its
characteristic research questions may not have existed as organized
concerns anywhere. Its purpose was not simply waiting to be unlocked."

A23 §12's examples of generation: a new game whose "rules constitute new
kinds of moves, strategies, achievements and failures", and a friendship
whose history makes an act "constitute loyalty, betrayal, care or
neglect". "The organization participates in constituting the objects of
evaluation." "We would need something closer to a family of evolving
spaces, together with transformations that preserve, identify, generate
or destroy distinctions."

## How it stands to the record

- It is [QUESTION-546](../questions.d/QUESTION-546.md)'s fourth part, a changing language, stated as a
  claim about purposes; A19's distinction between "a fixed mathematical
  universe large enough to represent all later developments" and what "a
  developing agent itself had access to" is the reason counting fails.
- [CLAIM-762](CLAIM-762.md), the owner's, is one route of generation: equivalence classes
  reused until they become new distinctions.
- [CLAIM-1036](CLAIM-1036.md) shows the evaluative cost: a policy's value to a self not
  yet formed has no value under the present self's valuation, and an
  outcome past the inquiry frontier ([TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)) has none at all.
- [CLAIM-1445](CLAIM-1445.md) is the organizational case: purposes constituted, not
  aggregated.

## What it does not say

That a theorist cannot model generation. A theorist's richer model can
contain the later concern; the claim is that the organization's own
reachable set, in its own terms, does not.
