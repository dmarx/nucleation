# LIT-2973: Does SGD really happen in tiny subspaces?

Minhak Song, Kwangjun Ahn & Chulhee Yun (2025). ICLR 2025; arXiv 2405.16002.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A cites it at A41 for the finding that the top Hessian subspace carries little of the learning. Read the same day ([NOTE-tmpw0eql](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpw0eql.md)) and filed as [THEORY-tmpthmd7](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpthmd7.md). A41's characterization is accurate for training-loss descent; the paper measures nothing about representations. It corroborates [CLAIM-835](../claims.d/CLAIM-835.md) weakly, within one metric ([ARG-139](../arguments.d/ARG-139.md)), is an instance of [TERM-137](../terms.d/TERM-137.md)'s sense 3, and leaves [QUESTION-511](../questions.d/QUESTION-511.md) open.
