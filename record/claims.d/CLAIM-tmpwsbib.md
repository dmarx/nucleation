# CLAIM-tmpwsbib: Drift along a reconstruction chain can be marked by a pragmatic phase change, the first generation at which a different communicative configuration dominates, which per-step error does not show

## The claim

A73: track "two different quantities", D_local(i) = W_d(q_(i-1), q_i) and
D_global(i) = W_d(q_0, q_i), over the distribution of inferred communicative
configurations at each generation. Of E4's two chains ([CASE-tmpyegzd](../cases.d/CASE-tmpyegzd.md)): "the first chain
preserves a particular **higher-order relational pattern** while the second
undergoes a pragmatic phase change, so to speak, in which the speaker's
relationship to the audience and the subject is reorganized. The exact point of
transition could be studied by identifying the first generation at which a
different communicative configuration becomes dominant."

## Where it went

It was stated over latents, and went when U28 demoted them ([CLAIM-tmp1tgj2](CLAIM-tmp1tgj2.md)). It does not
need them: the same event can be defined on judged observables. The
manuscript's §8 bounds cumulative error by per-step error ([CLAIM-tmpghha4](CLAIM-tmpghha4.md)) and says
the trajectories of invariants "need not coincide", but it has neither the pair
of distances nor the transition. [CLAIM-tmp9ras2](CLAIM-tmp9ras2.md) is the same point made about
constraints.
