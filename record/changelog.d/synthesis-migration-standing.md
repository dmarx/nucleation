- [ADR-tmpdne5p](record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpdne5p.md) (Proposed): a claim moved into the SYNTHESIS scheme keeps its standing and its relations. An admitted Active CLAIM becomes a Current SYNTHESIS and a Proposed one stays Proposed. The scheme gains `grounds`, `uses` and `corroborated_by`, and the converses (`supports`, `used_by`, `corroborates`) now accept a SYNTHESIS. A review still never sets a new synthesis Current.

[CASE-179](record/cases.d/CASE-179.md), [THEORY-1351](record/theory.d/THEORY-1351.md) and [SYNTHESIS-004](record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-004.md) are repointed to it.
