# LEAD-tmpswgqi: Measure the owner's own noticing of misfits over time against the share noticed by procedures

## What it would do

The manuscript's §19.5: "Measure the owner's own rate of noticing misfits
over time, against the share noticed by procedures. [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md) says to
judge support by the capabilities it leaves."

1. From the census ([CASE-300](../cases.d/CASE-300.md)) and the devlog, code each misfit noticed by
   who noticed it (owner, procedure, assistant) and by date.
2. Code each revision by who proposed and who decided it, and whether the
   owner's decision departed from the proposal.
3. Track, by month, the owner's absolute count of misfits noticed and of
   departures from proposals, not only their shares, since a falling share
   with a steady count is delegation and a falling count is erosion.

## What would count

[CLAIM-tmpxf70b](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpxf70b.md) predicts the owner's counts hold or grow as the record
grows, with procedures adding to them; its `defeated_if` is met if they
fall while the share decided on proposal rises. [CASE-262](../cases.d/CASE-262.md) is the design
that would test the same with learners, where withdrawal can be imposed.
