# CLAIM-945: Where members interact only through answers about stimuli a shared world supplies, a deviation in some members' interpretation reaches the others' interpretations only weakly, so a criterion of shared perspective that measures dependence by how far the others move finds no shared perspective even where members evaluate one another's answers against a common standard; the coupling shows in how fast the deviants are drawn back and in whether the others' response follows the standard

## The claim

**What the stated criterion found.** In [CASE-362](../cases.d/CASE-362.md), a fifth of the
members had their interpretation of one category moved. In the three
populations that interact (conformity, enforcement by a referee, and
checking disagreements against the world), the remaining members moved
by at most 2% of that deviation over the following 300 rounds, against a
twin run with the same random numbers. The criterion stated in advance
([TERM-149](../terms.d/TERM-149.md)) asked for 5%. So no population shares a perspective by
it, and [CASE-145](../cases.d/CASE-145.md)'s question, whether a perspective can be present with no
collective agent, gets no constructed instance from this study. Nothing
counts against the category either: the controls failed where they
should, and conformity did not pass the evidential condition.

**Where the coupling is.** The same populations differ plainly in two
other quantities.

- The deviants are drawn back by 11% (evidential) to 55% (conformity) of
  the deviation beyond what the world alone does; independent and
  socially trained populations, not at all.
- The others' response follows the standard in the evidential arm only:
  their uptake of a warranted deviation exceeds their uptake of an
  unwarranted one by 0.025, against 0.004 for conformity (paired contrast
  0.021 [0.015, 0.028]), and they move away from an unwarranted one.

Both held in each of four further settings run after the verdict (more
deviants, less feedback from the world, faster social learning, a
shorter window), while the others' displacement stayed under 3%.

The reason is the medium. Members exchange answers about stimuli the
world supplies, and a moved prototype changes a member's answers only
near category boundaries. Most of what passes between members is the
world's, not the deviants'. A8's test, that "disagreement has
consequences", is then met in who corrects whom and on what standard,
not in how far the majority moves.

## What follows, and what does not

- A criterion of shared perspective for such populations would measure
  dependence by the deviants' recovery and by the selectivity of the
  others' response. That restatement was made after seeing the data. To
  count, it must be stated in advance again and run again.
- This does not say a perspective was present and missed. It says the
  stated criterion could not have seen one of this kind, and what one that
  could would measure.
- Not about populations that exchange states, rules or reasons directly,
  where the others' displacement may be large.
- [CLAIM-946](CLAIM-946.md) is the companion finding: the rival reading's
  restoration condition is passed by plain conformity.
