# THEORY-2032: Shankar, Souslov, Bowick, Marchetti and Vitelli review topology in active matter as two physically distinct classes of phenomena that share the mathematics of integer invariants: topological defects, whose winding number is protected under continuous deformation of the order parameter and which in active nematics self-propel and unbind through flows they themselves generate, and topologically protected edge modes, whose band invariant is protected under continuous change of the medium's parameters while the bulk gap stays open

## The account

The review separates two topological phenomena. A defect's winding number is a property of the order parameter. It is a net count of defects in a region, and it cannot change under continuous deformation of the field, so defects are created and annihilated in opposite pairs in 2D. An edge mode's Chern number is a property of the band structure. It is protected under continuous change of material parameters while the gap stays open, and it forces modes at edges and at interfaces between topologically distinct band structures. In active matter, internally generated flows make the +1/2 disclination self-propel, unbind defect pairs above an activity threshold, and nucleate charge-neutral loops in 3D. Activity can also break the symmetries that nontrivial band topology needs, as in circulator lattices or active-fluid lattices.

## What it does not show

- Any case in which modes bound at a defect maintain the defect or its order-parameter texture.
- That the two classes reduce to one: they are linked ("intertwined") but kept physically distinct.
- Primary results of its own: as a review, its claims rest on the cited works.
- That Chern numbers are always defined in active fluids: it says they are not.
