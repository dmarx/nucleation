Under the dialectic regime, on 2026-11-23, the owner proposed a principle
the governance experiments may be converging on: "A system's ability to
correct itself depends not simply on having critics or feedback, but on
how evidence, evaluation, and authority are coupled—and what the system
is prevented from rewriting or rejecting." The owner gave four instances,
from [LEAD-177](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-177.md), [LEAD-015](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-015.md), [LEAD-190](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-190.md) and [LEAD-180](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-180.md). The owner's words are

## Follow-up: the regress and Gödel

The owner, the same evening: "the bottomless regress issue reminds me of
Godel Incompleteness".
- **The owner's comparison:** [CLAIM-1111](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1111.md), filed as supposed.
- **The mechanism, the assistant's:** [CLAIM-1125](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1125.md) (demonstrated). The
  beacon's miscalibration is unidentifiable from inside by a translation
  symmetry, checked in `scratch/formal/beacon_gauge_check.py` (1,000
  trials, no mismatch). It is the kind of limit [LEAD-180](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-180.md)'s relabelling
  shows.
- **The assessment, the assistant's:** [ARG-233](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-233.md) (analogy). The
  comparison holds in shape: a system cannot certify its own ground from
  within, and each added level certifies the one below and not itself. It
  fails in mechanism, a symmetry and not a diagonal sentence. And here each
  further anchor fully removes the error below it, unlike adding Con(T).
  Agrippa's trilemma is the closer ancestor.
- **Not registered:** the incompleteness theorems and Agrippa's trilemma
  are cited from standard knowledge. The record holds no reading of them,
  and nothing here relies on more than their statements.
