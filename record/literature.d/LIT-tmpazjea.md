The post reports and criticises a Supreme Court order in the third-country-removal litigation. Eight noncitizens had been held at a US base in Djibouti after the district court ruled that their removal violated its injunction. The Court's 3 July order, granting the government's motion to clarify, allowed the removal to go ahead. Geidner quotes Kagan's concurrence (she disagreed with the earlier stay but could not see how a district court can compel compliance with an order the Supreme Court has stayed) and the Sotomayor–Jackson dissent. He sets the order alongside other events of the day: passage of the budget bill, and letters from Attorney General Pam Bondi about the TikTok law.
