# ADR-007: philosophy-of-mathematics and religion join the topics

## Context

The 2026-09-26 close readings on the `metaphysics` tag turned up two gaps. The
record's rule is to add a missing word rather than approximate it, as
[ADR-006](ADR-006.md) did for `epistemology` and `logic`.

- **Philosophy of mathematics.** The `mathematics` topic is "mathematics read
  for itself". Four held works are about mathematics rather than mathematics
  read for itself:
  - Carson's Element, *Philosophy of Mathematics from Descartes to Kant* (a
    history of the philosophy of mathematics);
  - Weyl's *Philosophy of Mathematics and Natural Science*;
  - Leitgeb & Ladyman on criteria of identity in mathematical structuralism,
    whose reader named the gap;
  - Corfield on duality as a category-theoretic concept.
- **Religion.** The reader of the SEP *Religion and Science* entry found no
  topic for its subject, and recommended one, as its primary topic.
  - A second held work is also about religion: the paper on epistemic
    injustice in the clinical care of practitioners of Afro-Brazilian
    religions.

## Decision

Add both words to `topics`, each with a label and a blurb.

- **`philosophy-of-mathematics`** joins the `philosophy` tag group
  ([ADR-003](ADR-003.md)).
- **`religion`** stays outside it, as `social-science` and `linguistics` do:
  - one of its two works is a philosophical survey, the other a study in
    medical ethics;
  - the group labels philosophical questions, and religion is a subject that
    such questions, and others, take.

Retagged:

| work | tags now |
| --- | --- |
| Carson | `philosophy-of-mathematics` first, replacing `mathematics` |
| Weyl | `philosophy-of-mathematics` added |
| Leitgeb & Ladyman | `philosophy-of-mathematics` added |
| Corfield | `philosophy-of-mathematics` added |
| the SEP entry | `religion` first |
| the epistemic-injustice paper | `religion` added |

`mathematics` stays on every work where mathematics is also read for itself:
- Weyl;
- Leitgeb & Ladyman, whose graph theory is worked;
- Corfield, whose category theory is worked.

## Alternatives

- **Keep `mathematics` for the philosophy of mathematics.** That is the nearest
  wrong word. Carson's Element contains no mathematics read for itself.
- **Put `religion` in the philosophy group.** Only one of its two works is
  philosophy.
