# CLAIM-tmp99nuu: Systems differ in whether "who drives?" has an answer at a given level of description, and the kinds form a spectrum: fully top-down, bidirectional, emergent from the whole, and between

## The claim

The owner, verbatim: "let's take up the agency question. I think it's
probably worth differentiating between systems where 'who drives?' has an
answer wrt a particularly level of description or not. I think there's is
probably a spectrum/gradient/distribution of kinds here. for two level
systems, we have systems that experience fully top-down causation, systems
where there is bidirectional causation, systems where there driving 'will'
is emergent from the system as a whole, etc."

## How the record grounds it

The kinds are separable by quantities the record already reads:
- **The information decomposition** ([LIT-025](../literature.d/LIT-025.md)). A macro-variable's synergy
  splits into downward causation, where it predicts parts' futures, and
  causal decoupling, where it predicts its own future. Zero synergy is
  the aggregate end.
- **Proportional causation** ([THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md)). The higher level is the cause,
  rather than its realizer, exactly when the effect is insensitive to how
  the higher level is realized.

[TERM-tmpuyzeb](../terms.d/TERM-tmpuyzeb.md) arranges these as a drive profile. Because both quantities
are continuous, the owner's "spectrum/gradient/distribution" is the natural
reading. The named kinds are regions, not classes.

One refinement the record adds. Read strictly, "fully top-down" cannot mean
that the higher level drives without its parts, because it is realized by
them. The defensible reading is [THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md)'s: the parts' detail is
irrelevant given the higher level's state. That is graded, and it can be
checked.

## What it does not say

- **That any particular system sits where intuition puts it.** That is
  measurement, relative to a chosen macro-variable.
- **That a driving level is an agent.**
