# QUESTION-013: Under iterated transport, when do observational, structural and decision distortion converge together, diverge, or evolve independently?

## Why it is a question

A110 §IX.5: "For composed reconstruction kernels, characterize the evolution of
(D_obs(n), D_struct(n), D_dec(n)). Determine when these quantities converge
together, diverge, or evolve independently. This includes the possibility that a
chain becomes conventionally interpretable while losing ancestral pragmatic
structure."

## Where it stands

Manuscript §8: "We propose observing how selected invariants, decision risks, and
compatibility structures evolve together; their trajectories need not coincide."
Narrower forms are already filed: sufficiency along a chain ([QUESTION-016](QUESTION-016.md)), and one
task family kept while another is lost ([QUESTION-010](QUESTION-010.md)). [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) gives one case of
divergence.
