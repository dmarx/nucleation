# NOTE-tmplqpif: The owner — investigating identity formation as a concrete example of grokking dynamics (+ NameBasis)

## Contribution

A hypothesis: "we should be able to identify phase changes in 'grokking'
specific identities if we monitor the learning process", with four rough
phases: identity overridden by name-part biases; coarse components
stabilize; the model "grokks unique identity, can flexibly apply to
creative scenarios"; identity overfit.

## Key points

1. Sub-hypothesis: a "detectable change in network weights" where "the
network 'knows that it knows' a particular named entity", and a change
from composing an identity from concepts to giving it its own concept
region or direction; with the caveat "maybe this never happens and all
identities are compositional?"
2. Measures proposed: "identity rigidity", the distribution of attributes
across images generated from one name; embedding dispersion (determinant
and trace of the covariance); "as variance goes down, identities
individuate".
3. No model training is specified; the phases are hypotheses about a learning
process the document does not run.

## Bearing on the record

- CLAIM-697 and CLAIM-695 (the filer's reading): rigidity across samples is
a reliability measure, so an identity hit only sometimes is a disposition
reachable by sampling, and a rigid one is reliable; the transition between
them in training is the regime CLAIM-695 describes, where a disposition
reachable by search becomes cheap to realize.
- CLAIM-tmpvnjh2 and QUESTION-tmpihjeu: the owner's question whether an
identity gets its own concept direction or stays compositional is whether
a learned identity, identifiable in behaviour, also becomes localized.
- QUESTION-039: a hypothesis about the order and moment at which a structure
(an identity) is acquired.

## Limitations

Undated brainstorm; no protocol; the phases are not operationalized.
