# TERM-tmpq5zvd: combined form

## Definition

A **combined form** is a candidate system whose members are more than one
otherwise separable thing, considered as one system relative to a stated
target. It *is* one system while the members' joint dynamics are nearly
decomposable with the form as a block whose gap beats its Occam factor
([CLAIM-tmp8tsw5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8tsw5.md), in the notation of [TERM-tmpgthc0](TERM-tmpgthc0.md)). It lasts while the
block keeps clearing the threshold.

Its internal architecture (peers or hierarchy) and its drive profile
([TERM-tmpuyzeb](TERM-tmpuyzeb.md)) are further facts about it, not conditions on it
([CLAIM-tmpl4ha3](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl4ha3.md)).

## What it is not

- **Not an agent and not a self.** Those are [TERM-tmp1913d](TERM-tmp1913d.md), [TERM-tmpllrr1](TERM-tmpllrr1.md)
  and [TERM-tmpg0hhr](TERM-tmpg0hhr.md), each a further question.
- **Not "consort".** "Consort" was proposed in chat on 2026-10-11 for the
  same idea and was not adopted. "Combined form" came from the owner's
  wording ([CLAIM-tmpygda8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpygda8.md)) and is the term the claims use.
- **Not target-free.** Relative to a different target, the same members can
  group differently ([CLAIM-tmp3qyzh](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3qyzh.md)).
