# LIT-tmpwcrya: The Use of Knowledge in Society

Hayek, F. A. (1945). The Use of Knowledge in Society. *American Economic Review* 35(4): 519–530.

## Why this is here

A18 brought it in as the informational case for some decentralized arrangements: "decentralized decision-making can use local information unavailable to a central planner", which A18 said is "an argument for the informational advantages of certain governance arrangements, rather than evidence that every restriction on private action impairs information use."
