  under Distributed Information in Multi-Agent LLMs. The paper adapts the
  Hidden Profile paradigm from social psychology into a 65-task benchmark
  for multi-agent LLMs. In each task shared facts favour a wrong option and
  facts distributed one per agent jointly support the right one; agents are
  not told the information is asymmetric. Tasks are admitted only if single
  agents with full information get at least 80% and agents with the hidden
  profile at most 20% before discussion. Across 15 models, groups reach
  30.1% after discussion against 80.7% for single agents with complete
  information. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
