# LIT-073: General probabilistic theories: An introduction

Martin Plávala (2021), *Physics Reports 1033:1–64 (2023); arXiv preprint first* — [ARXIV-2103.07469](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07469)

## Key takeaways

- A self-contained, convex-geometric textbook introduction to GPTs, the operational framework containing finite-dimensional classical and quantum theory and exotic theories like boxworld (PR boxes). It proves standard results such as "the identity channel is measure-and-prepare ⇔ a universal broadcasting channel exists ⇔ the state space is a simplex (classical)".

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The review introduces general probabilistic theories: operational theories that generalize finite-dimensional classical and quantum theory and also include more exotic ones, such as boxworld with its Popescu–Rohrlich boxes. It explains the basic concepts and elements of the framework in depth and proves several well-known results. It aims to be self-contained and consistent. Its main tool is convex geometry, supplemented by a diagrammatic notation used to write many equations.
