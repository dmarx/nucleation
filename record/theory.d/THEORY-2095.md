# THEORY-2095: Kuhn holds that the exemplars of a science, shared concrete problem-solutions, teach a learned perception of similarity rather than rules, by which a new problem is seen as like one already solved so that its formalism follows and only manipulative difficulties remain, and that knowledge stored in that similarity relation differs from knowledge stored as rules

## The account

Paradigm in the narrow sense is the exemplar. Symbolic laws are generalization-sketches given a new form in each application; scientists cannot state correspondence rules for them. Working exemplars teaches a similarity relation, prior to any criteria, by which new problems are seen as like solved ones (Galileo to Huyghens to Bernoulli). The knowledge is in the relation, and it serves within the environment where it was learned.

## Evidence

A teaching observation, a historical chain, a thought experiment and a sketched computer model; no measurement.
