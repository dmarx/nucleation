# CLAIM-tmpas8q0: The record does not only stop drift, it shapes what the argument can become: a closed vocabulary of schemes, roles and relations formalizes early, and what does not fit a CLAIM or an ARG is either forced into one or lost

## The claim

Shipman and Marshall ([LIT-756](../literature.d/LIT-756.md)) found that users of interactive systems
resist formalizing. When they are made to formalize early, the structure
that results is the system's and not theirs. A crystal grown on a seed
takes the seed's lattice.

The record does three things that fit the objection:

- It decides what counts as a thing. A move becomes a CLAIM, a TERM or an
  ARG, or it is left as a "flourish, not coded".
- [ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) rejected roles for literature beyond premise (foil, method,
  analogue, boundary marker), even though they "recurred", so they are kept
  in prose.
- A thesis has to state `defeated_if` when it is filed, which may come
  before the argument knows what would defeat it.

If the objection holds, part of what the record shows as the argument
settling is the argument being shaped to the schemes.

## What would answer it

Something that crystallized in the exchange and that the record could not
hold, or held only by distorting it. Or the reverse: every case of drift
the record caught was drift by the exchange's own standards, and not only
by the record's.
