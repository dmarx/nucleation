# LIT-263: Kochen-Specker contextuality

Costantino Budroni, Adán Cabello, Otfried Gühne, Matthias Kleinmann, Jan-Åke Larsson (2021), *Reviews of Modern Physics 94, 045007 (2022)* — [ARXIV-2102.13036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13036)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpmru91](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpmru91.md).*

- **Definition (§IV.A.1).** An NCHV model (Eq. 15) exists iff a global
  distribution reproduces the context marginals (Eq. 16, Fine; Suppes &
  Zanotti). Local consistency is "sometimes called the sheaf condition".
  The marginal-problem, polytope, sheaf, graph and hypergraph
  formulations are asserted to be substantially equivalent.
- **Contexts (§IV.B.2).** Adapting Fritz (2012), POVMs p(ab|xy)𝟙 on a
  one-dimensional space realize every nonsignalling, and in general every
  nondisturbing, behaviour as jointly measurable. So joint measurability
  alone trivializes contextuality. The review defines contexts by
  sequences of outcome-repeatable, nondisturbing measurements.
- **Imperfections (§IV.C).** Four noise models, each an explicit
  assumption: cumulative noise, context-independent evolution,
  ε-faithfulness, and Kujala et al.'s maximally noncontextual models. The
  KCBS data stay contextual under the last (Eq. 55).
- **Structure (§V.B.2).** Vorob'ev: consistent marginals on an acyclic
  hypergraph always glue. Contextuality needs an induced cycle of length
  ≥ 4, so CHSH and KCBS are the smallest contextual scenarios.
- **Spekkens (§IV.E).** The qubit's preparation contextuality is
  restated (Eqs. 67–70). Mazurek et al.'s inequality uses preparation
  and measurement noncontextuality together, and its experimental
  violation assumes tomographic completeness. KS's and Bell's qubit
  models are preparation contextual.
- **Applications (§VI).** For odd prime d, stabilizer-measurement
  contextuality coincides with Wigner negativity (Howard et al.). The
  full stabilizer subtheory has an efficient contextual simulation
  (Hindlycke & Larsson).
