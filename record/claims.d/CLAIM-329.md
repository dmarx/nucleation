# CLAIM-329: A collective that replaces all its members persists, when it does, by causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by resemblance: an organization of the same kind that arises independently is not its continuation, and a lapse in its coherence need not end it

## The claim

A9 §9 separated three persistences:

- **State persistence.** A characteristic variable changes slowly,
  Z_{t+Δ} ≈ Z_t.
- **Dynamical persistence.** "The organization continues generating
  characteristic responses even as its internal states change", so
  P(Y_{t+h} | Z_t) stays stable for a stated range of horizons.
- **Historical persistence.** "The processes responsible for producing
  the organization are themselves causally transmitted and reconstituted
  across successive populations of constituents."

"A reputation may persist even though the processes generating the actual
behavior have changed. A program may maintain its characteristic
capabilities while its present reputation changes. A fandom may preserve
its historical lineage even while losing its characteristic norms."

This claim is that the third is what persistence through total turnover
consists in. Two consequences mark it off from its neighbours:

- **Against resemblance.** A5: "Two unrelated universities might
  independently develop almost identical sporting programs. Whatever
  distinguishes those cases must involve a history of causal
  transmission, not just similarity of structure or output." Reproducing
  a kind is not persisting as an individual.
- **Against conservation alone.** A5 §2: a program that loses its whole
  roster and staff, falls apart and rebuilds its capacity over several
  seasons may fail [CLAIM-161](CLAIM-161.md)'s conserved-coherence test at the trough and
  still be the same program, because what persisted was "a capacity to
  restore or regenerate a characteristic organization". [CLAIM-161](CLAIM-161.md) is
  about persisting as the same self and says nothing against this; the
  two are tests of different persistences.

[CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) separated a persistent product from a persistent producer.
This claim says what the producer's own persistence is when its members
are all replaced. [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) recorded A10 §4.3's "causal-organizational
continuity" of 2026-10-13 as "a candidate of the same shape" and did not
file it. This is its second appearance, from a different case.

A6 §3 found the organizational-studies form: Feldman and Pentland's
ostensive and performative routines ([LIT-1124](../literature.d/LIT-1124.md), unread), where
enactments reproduce and modify a pattern that organizes later
enactments. Whether that literature already separates "the continuity of
a *particular organization* from reproduction of an *organizational
kind*" was A6's first question for it.

## What it does not say

- **What must be transmitted.** That is [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md). Without an answer
  the claim risks A6's warning that it "risks making any causal descendant
  a continuation of the original entity".
- **How to decide between several descendants.** [CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md) has three,
  each continuous with the original in a different respect.
- **That transmission suffices for being a self, an agent or one
  system.** It is a persistence condition only, as [CLAIM-161](CLAIM-161.md) is.

## Note of 2026-10-21: the persistences re-derived, and reconstitution

A10 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 (§4, §10) re-derived this claim's three
persistences as "structural", "generative", "viability", "interventional"
and "historical" continuity, without citing it. A13 and A14 then proposed
a TERM, "organizational reconstitution", for processes that "restore,
reproduce, or transform" an organization's relations. In this record
reconstitution is what this claim says is not continuation: "an
organization of the same kind that arises independently" ([QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md),
[THEORY-418](../theory.d/THEORY-418.md), [CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md)). The proposed TERM would merge the two. The
persistences are filed instead as a disambiguation TERM under another
name, [TERM-tmpzdpdt](../terms.d/TERM-tmpzdpdt.md).
