# LEAD-090: Build the coupled meaning-influence model with its linearization, and place a reduced form of it inside its predecessor models or find a phenomenon it produces that they cannot

## What it would do

Write [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md)'s model, linearize it about its symmetric state, and
compare a reduced form with the opinion-dynamics models [QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md) names
at matched complexity.

Where the record names it:

- [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md): "It is how [QUESTION-100](../questions.d/QUESTION-100.md) would be answered and [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)
  tested"
- [QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md): "A derivation placing a reduced [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) inside one of
  these models"

## What would count

A phenomenon the coupled model produces and the predecessors cannot at
matched complexity answers [QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md) for the coupling. A reduction that
places it inside one of them answers it the other way and weakens what
[CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md) adds.

## Note of 2026-11-22: built, linearized and compared, in [CASE-363](../cases.d/CASE-363.md)

The lead was carried out the same day. [CASE-363](../cases.d/CASE-363.md) writes [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md)'s model as an agent-level simulation and reduces it to its mean field (code and tests in scratch/studies/lead-090/). Its linearization is [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)'s, with the threshold gσ² > 2(1 − φ(1 − ε)), free of the conferral memory and the meaning rate ([CLAIM-947](../claims.d/CLAIM-947.md)). The reduced form is exactly a weighted-averaging model with two stubborn sources and weights adapting toward an agreement kernel, smooth bounded confidence on a balanced corpus; against fixed-weight models, DeGroot–Friedkin, Friedkin 2011 and Gaussian bounded confidence it produces authority chosen by the initial interpretation and switched by the evidence with hysteresis ([CLAIM-948](../claims.d/CLAIM-948.md)). Both halves of "What would count" are met, for different predecessors; which one answers [QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md) depends on whether its "adaptive networks" names the class or the read works, set out in [CLAIM-948](../claims.d/CLAIM-948.md) for the owner. What the reduction leaves out (role-indexed authority, turnover, more operators) is not done here. No status or stage is changed.
