# ARG-016: Valuations that do not cross, read through coordination dynamics, make flow a synchronization of interests

## The inference

[CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) holds that an agent is many interests over a choice only as far
as its valuations of that choice cross within the choice's horizon. In
flow the activity pays at once and is also what the agent values at a
distance, so no competing curve overtakes it. On [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) control has an
intrinsic cost, and here none is paid. [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) names that state
synchronization ([TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md)), after coordination dynamics ([THEORY-069](../theory.d/THEORY-069.md)), in
which coupled components settle into a stable relative phase. The
analogy's source is [THEORY-069](../theory.d/THEORY-069.md), and its target is the agent's interests.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is that the respect the
  conclusion needs?** In both, components that could pull apart act
  together. [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) needs more than that: its "synchronization"
  explains why flow is effortless and predicts that flow "ends by
  desynchronization".
- **What relevant difference is there?** The mechanism, and this is the
  question that fails. In coordination dynamics synchronization has
  "coupled components, a collective variable (relative phase) and
  transitions between stable patterns, with critical fluctuations and
  slowing before a switch". [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md): "Interests whose curves do not
  cross are not coupled, have no phase, and do not 'lock'."
- **What transfers, and what does not?** The word transfers. [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md):
  "the word adds nothing that 'the valuations do not cross' does not
  already say." [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md) defines synchronization without dynamics, so
  [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) survives with the word replaced, which concedes the point.
  The reading that flow is literally neural synchrony does not transfer,
  and nothing here argues it.

## What would make it a mechanism

[CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md) named the parts that would turn the analogy into a mechanism:

- **A coupling.** Ainslie's self-prediction couples successive choices,
  since each choice changes the expected value of the series. [CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md)
  develops this: the agent's prediction of its own later valuation makes
  the overlap on which interests bear at once, "and it is the coupling
  [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md) found missing".
- **An order parameter.** For example, the share of choices in a domain
  that keep the same ranking across delays. A45 of the exchange of
  2026-10-17, in the broader sense of [TERM-061](../terms.d/TERM-061.md), offers how often a
  supervisory intervention is needed.
- **Transitions.** Entering and leaving flow, or a rule breaking into a
  lapse, as switches between stable patterns, with hysteresis and
  critical slowing before a lapse. [LIT-638](../literature.d/LIT-638.md) ([THEORY-088](../theory.d/THEORY-088.md)) claims cortical
  transitions are continuous, not discrete switches, which bears on the
  form of this test ([QUESTION-079](../questions.d/QUESTION-079.md)).

[CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md), the visible-tally variant of Twitch Plays Pokémon, has all three
parts in toy form, but its parts are voters, not one person's interests.

## On [CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md)

[CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md) `objects_to` [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md), and `objects_to` cannot name an
argument. It is left pointing at [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md) and recorded here. It is not a
premise of this argument: the inference [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) draws does not start
from it. [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) rests on [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md)'s crossing curves, and [CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md) is a
later candidate for one of the three missing parts, the coupling. It
supplies no order parameter and no transitions, and none of it is
measured. If the parts are found, the argument's next version would take
[CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md) as a premise, with a history entry.

[CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) is stated in the broader sense of synchronization and inherits
this objection.

## Verdict

Proposed. The analogy carries no mechanism as filed. Candidate parts are
named, none is measured in a person or a model, and the question is
open.
