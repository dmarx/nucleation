# TERM-006: organizational authenticity

## Definition

> An agent exhibits organizational authenticity insofar as its operative
> commitments arise through, remain integrated with, and are answerable to its
> own constitutive organization. (A252)

It was given thin and thick forms (A254), and the thick form absorbed
[TERM-012](TERM-012.md), which is where its circularity worries came from.

## Why it is set aside

Not rejected: deferred. The audit (A276): "Ownership criteria remain
insufficiently defended."
