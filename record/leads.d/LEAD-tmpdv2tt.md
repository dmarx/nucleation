- **Bovier and colleagues (LIT-tmpl98s1).** Whether their metastable
  hierarchy is the budgeted tree of CLAIM-581 and CLAIM-tmpxi826; how they

  CLAIM-tmpxi826's bound in general. Also whether they bear on
  QUESTION-tmpv6fxp's converse.
- **Fenichel (LIT-1638).** The condition under which a slow manifold
  persists, stated as a ratio of normal to tangential rates, and whether it
  is the rate ratio CLAIM-tmpy75jv identifies with the spacing of the tree.
