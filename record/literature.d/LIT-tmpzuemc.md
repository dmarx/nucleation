## Why this is here

For [CASE-tmpxgcq2](../cases.d/CASE-tmpxgcq2.md): the single CLIS patient that Kübler and Birbaumer said would disprove their extinction hypothesis, if it holds. Two cautions. The authors' earlier CLIS papers were retracted after a DFG misconduct finding (reported by STAT, 2022-03-22), and outside researchers urged caution about this one. And communication came only once a contingency between intention and outcome was restored, through neurofeedback, so it shows lucidity that a new action channel could reach, not judgement running with no action at all.
