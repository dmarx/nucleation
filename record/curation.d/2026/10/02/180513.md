The owner asked how the record covered dissipative structures. It held
nothing filed on the subject: the term appeared only inside Nahas &
Sachs's paper on naturalizing teleology ([LIT-192](../../record/literature.d/LIT-192.md)), as the demarcation
dispute, and as one cluster of definitions in "What Lives?" ([LIT-211](../../record/literature.d/LIT-211.md)).
Prigogine was absent. Three agents filed 23 works, 14 read in full and one
skimmed, and all three independently proposed the word the vocabulary
lacked, so `thermodynamics` was added ([ADR-020](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-020.md)).

The lecture is explicit that the minimum-entropy-production theorem holds
only in the strictly linear regime near equilibrium, and that far from it
behaviour can be "even opposite": Bénard convection raises entropy
production. Cross & Hohenberg quote Glansdorff & Prigogine conceding that
"the search for a universal kinetic potential has proved to be
unsuccessful", and correct the lecture's history: Bénard's own hexagons
were driven by surface tension, not buoyancy.

its advocate, writes that "no one would argue that MEP is well

## What it did to the record

[LIT-192](../../record/literature.d/LIT-192.md)'s demarcation question is still open. The canonical dissipative
structures are the counter-examples any definition of life must exclude,
and none of the readable texts excludes them on principle. Friston alone
names the candle flame, in one sentence. The two accounts that claim a
real line, Deacon's teleodynamics and Moreno & Mossio's closure, are
unread. [NOTE-094](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-094.md) v3 says so.

Perunov, Marsland & England and [THEORY-026](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-026.md) use "adaptation" in opposite
senses (dissipating more against dissipating less), and the record should
keep them apart. Friston's free energy is variational, so he is not a
second source for [THEORY-026](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-026.md), nor for the equivalence Seth ([LIT-135](../../record/literature.d/LIT-135.md))
assumes.

## For the next person

Moreno & Mossio's *Biological Autonomy* has an authors' CC BY-NC deposit
in HAL (hal-01151449) behind a bot challenge aimed at scrapers; a person
can open it lawfully, and it is the cheapest way to answer the
demarcation question. Crooks 1998 (J. Stat. Phys. 90:1481) is the paper
[THEORY-026](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-026.md)'s source is actually built on, and is not yet in the record.
