# LIT-1788: Surviving Death, Again

Johnston, M. (2023). *TheoLogica*, 8(2), 261–314.

Read in full in [NOTE-1257](../notes.d/NOTE-1257.md), from the published version.

## Why this is here

Read in place of Johnston's *Surviving Death* ([LIT-1759](LIT-1759.md)), which could
not be reached. It states the book's surrogate for survival in a paragraph
and then withdraws the account of the self it rested on. That matters for
how the record cites the book against the owner's thesis ([CLAIM-491](../claims.d/CLAIM-491.md)).
