# NOTE-2396: Lebovitz — How Vinnie Jones Really Feels About His Role In X-Men - Exclusive

## Contribution

A first-hand production voice on the Juggernaut line. The actor who
spoke it attributes it to the director, and says the director took it
from the internet. Before this the record held only the co-writer's
"reshoot Ratner line" ([LIT-1142](../literature.d/LIT-1142.md), via Polygon), which dates the line
but gives no source.

## Key insight

The production's own account of the line's origin is one sentence from
the actor: it was the director's, found online. It names no one who
brought it to him.

## Assumptions

- An interview, condensed by the writer: the words in quotation marks are
  Jones's, the framing around them Looper's ("he told Looper").
- Jones speaks of Ratner's viewing at second hand: what Ratner told him
  or what he supposes.

## Key results

- On the role: "When I signed on, Matthew Vaughn was going to be the
  director. And when Brett Ratner came on, my character got dissolved."
- On the line: "Jones called it "Brett's line," noting that the director
  "found that on the internet and wanted to do that."" He would embrace
  it more had he played the role "as written and as he wanted" (Looper's
  paraphrase).
- Jones had "a big meeting with Brett Ratner" about the role and was
  "assured ... that they were writing new stuff".

## Concepts

None new.

  which the case lacked: the director, from the internet. Coded F1 in

## Limitations

- One sentence, partly paraphrased; no date for the viewing or the call.
- Second-hand as to where Ratner saw it.

## Open questions

- Did anyone show or send the video to Ratner? Only Ratner can say.
