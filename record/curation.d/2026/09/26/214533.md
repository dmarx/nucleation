## The readings

Eleven works were read in full, and all are `Active`: [LIT-118](../../record/literature.d/LIT-118.md), [LIT-131](../../record/literature.d/LIT-131.md),
[LIT-139](../../record/literature.d/LIT-139.md), [LIT-158](../../record/literature.d/LIT-158.md), [LIT-161](../../record/literature.d/LIT-161.md), [LIT-162](../../record/literature.d/LIT-162.md), [LIT-163](../../record/literature.d/LIT-163.md), [LIT-165](../../record/literature.d/LIT-165.md), [LIT-175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-175.md), [LIT-189](../../record/literature.d/LIT-189.md) and
[LIT-213](../../record/literature.d/LIT-213.md). Two readings were of author versions rather than the version of
record: [LIT-118](../../record/literature.d/LIT-118.md) (the 2015 Durham working paper) and [LIT-161](../../record/literature.d/LIT-161.md) (the PSA2018
manuscript). Each NOTE says so.

The corrections to the seeded summaries ran one way: the seeds overstated.

- Desmond ([LIT-158](../../record/literature.d/LIT-158.md)) argues that distrust *can be* valid reasoning. He calls it
  a "how-possibly" argument, not the showing that distrust is justified which
  the abstract implies.
- Douglas ([LIT-139](../../record/literature.d/LIT-139.md)) does not say every internal choice carries inductive risk.
- Worrall ([LIT-175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-175.md)) calls selection bias "a cast-iron argument for
  randomization"; the seed had listed it among the arguments that fail.
- Wenmackers' "under 2%" inconsistency figure ([LIT-163](../../record/literature.d/LIT-163.md)) is a per-agent,
  single-update, numerical result for two and three atomic sentences. At the
  population level it reaches 16.9%.
- Hartmann & Trpin's measure ([LIT-165](../../record/literature.d/LIT-165.md)) escapes Roche & Sober by two examples,
  not by a theorem.

**One status was overridden.** The reader rated [LIT-163](../../record/literature.d/LIT-163.md) `Superseded`: each
chapter has a later journal version. None of them is on this record, and
`Superseded` has to name a successor that is. The thesis also holds two things
nothing else does: the Ch. 1 survey and the Ch. 4 synthesis. So it is `Active`,
and its status note lists the four journal versions as the better citations
for the other chapters.
