# CLAIM-tmp8hi2v: Search engine optimization is a population breaking the correlates a ranking reads

## The claim

**The ranking reads correlates.** Inbound links, keywords, freshness and
dwell time accompany relevance and quality on a web nobody is optimizing.
Over that family the ranking is reliable the way [CASE-416](../cases.d/CASE-416.md)'s watermark reader
is. [CLAIM-tmpgmvhl](CLAIM-tmpgmvhl.md)'s remedy for a shortcut is a family in which the
correlate is unreliable. In search, publishers supply that family, and they
supply it adversarially. Optimizing the correlate breaks its link to the
quality. That is Goodhart's law with a population as the agent.

**The engine retrains.** Each ranking changes what is published, and the
engine retrains on the web it induced. That is performative prediction.
[THEORY-861](../theory.d/THEORY-861.md) says retraining converges to a stable point only when the
population's response is weak enough: the distribution map's sensitivity ε
below γ/β, the loss's strong convexity over its smoothness. Publishers who
respond fast and at scale make ε large. The arms race, with its named
algorithm updates and penalties, is that regime: no stable point, only
retraining. [THEORY-839](../theory.d/THEORY-839.md) adds that even a stable point need not be optimal.

**How it ends.** It ends where the ranking reads something publishers cannot
manufacture more cheaply than the quality itself. In [CLAIM-1086](CLAIM-1086.md)'s terms, that
is an *effect* of quality rather than a correlate of it: reading smoke, not a
watermark. Where faking the feature costs as much as having the quality,
gaming it and improving the page coincide.

## Two kinds of optimization

Not all optimization is gaming. Publishers who make their quality easier to
read (structured data, speed, clear titles) supply *evidence* of the quality.
Those who manufacture the feature without the quality supply a *counterfeit*.
The first helps the engine's ranking; the second breaks it. On the
[DISTINCTION-tmp8wqwj](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-tmp8wqwj.md) test, the first is an honest signal relative to the
user's objective. The second makes the page a proxy optimum ([TERM-108](../terms.d/TERM-108.md))
relative to the ranking's objective.

## What it does not say

That the engine's objective is right. The ranking may itself be a proxy of
what users need, and [CLAIM-tmpcrves](CLAIM-tmpcrves.md) takes that up.
