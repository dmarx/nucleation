# CASE-428: The tobacco industry's management of demand and doubt

## The case

A8 §1 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c), "optimizing the consumer rather than the product":

> A 2006 federal court judgment documented a decades-long pattern of
> conduct that included manipulating cigarette design to sustain
> addiction, misleading consumers about health consequences, suppressing
> research, and falsely denying marketing to young people.

A sets out its structure:

| Component | Realization |
|---|---|
| Objective | Recurring sales |
| Optimization substrate | Corporate research, marketing and management |
| Behavioural lever | Nicotine dependence |
| Epistemic lever | Public understanding of health risks |
| Governance failure | Suppression and distortion of corrective information |

The point A draws: "consumer demand was not an independent input to the
optimization process. It was something the industry could influence." An
organization "can increase sales either by satisfying existing preferences
or by altering the conditions under which preferences are formed and
exercised."

## What it can show

Documented, and so a specimen of the mechanisms the court found, not of
any reading of them. It illustrates three claims:

- **[CLAIM-1249](../claims.d/CLAIM-1249.md)**, manufactured doubt raising the threshold for action
  rather than reversing belief: the tobacco campaign is that claim's own
  canonical example, now filed as a case.
- **[CLAIM-1430](../claims.d/CLAIM-1430.md)** (A's, filed with this case): revealed demand stops
  being evidence of alignment once the optimizer can shape it. Tobacco is
  its second rung, demand engineered through dependence.
- **[CLAIM-1443](../claims.d/CLAIM-1443.md)** (A's): optimization turns pathological when its
  success erodes the mechanisms that evaluate it. "tobacco's suppression
  of research" is one of A8's three examples of that stage.

The research suppression is also an instance of [CLAIM-1287](../claims.d/CLAIM-1287.md)'s mechanism,
an optimizer weakening a constraint on itself (independent evaluation),
which A11 §5 lists among the constraints such an optimizer erodes.

## What it cannot show

That the conduct was produced by the sales objective rather than chosen
by people who knew what they were doing: the court's findings are of
conduct and knowledge, the opposite of the reading A gives the cod
fishery ([CASE-432](CASE-432.md)), "No unitary agent needed to want the destruction
of the fishery". The case illustrates an
optimizer shaping its own demand, not optimization without intent. Nor
does it show how much of smoking's prevalence the levers explain.
