# LIT-997: How LLMs Distort Our Written Language

Marwa Abdulhai, Isadora White, Yanming Wan, Ibrahim Qureshi, Joel Z. Leibo, Max Kleiman-Weiner and Natasha Jaques (2026), *arXiv:2603.18161*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-750](../notes.d/NOTE-750.md).*

- **Voice and stance, user study (N = 100).** Heavy LLM users (27 of 55
  with access) rated their essays less in their own voice
  (t(69) = 5.799, p < 0.001) and less creative, but no less satisfying.
  A gpt-4o judge labelled 66.7% of their essays neutral on "Does money
  lead to happiness?", against 39.5% of controls (§§4.1, 4.3). Users who
  only consulted the model looked like the controls.
- **Counterfactual editing (ArgRewrite-v2).** The 2021 drafts were
  revised by gpt-5-mini, gemini-2.5-flash and claude-haiku with the
  experts' feedback, under five prompts. The LLMs moved the essays further
  than their authors did, and all in one direction in sentence-embedding
  space (Figs. 1, 5). This held under "Revise this essay for grammar".
  Shown in plots, not tested.
- **Conclusions changed in examples, not counted.** In one grammar edit,
  "the perks do not outweigh the persistent cons" became "the drawbacks
  do not outweigh the benefits" (Fig. 2).
- **Surface.** Lexical divergence from the draft is about 0.2–0.3 JSD
  for human revisions and 0.476 for claude-haiku's. LLM revisions use
  fewer pronouns and more adjectives, and more emotional and more
  analytic language.
- **Reviews.** In about 18k ICLR 2026 reviews, those Pangram labels
  LLM-generated name clarity as a strength 32% less often and
  reproducibility 136% more often. Their mean score is 4.43 against 4.13.
- **What "meaning" is here.** It is embedding position and judge labels.
  No writer or reader was asked whether the meaning survived.

## Standing in the record

Held for [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md) as a measured case of what a rewriting fails to
preserve: topic and genre stay, stance, voice and evaluative emphasis
move. It does not answer the question. Its measure of meaning is the
semantic similarity that [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md) says an answer must beat, and it
collects no fidelity judgements. ArgRewrite-v2, under its five prompts and
with each essay's human counterfactual, is a corpus on which
[CLAIM-147](../claims.d/CLAIM-147.md)'s relational measures could be tested against surface similarity.
[NOTE-750](../notes.d/NOTE-750.md) lists where the paper's abstract says more than its body.
Its embedding-displacement result is filed as [THEORY-226](../theory.d/THEORY-226.md).

It carries no instruction for machine-learning practice as a finding.
The anthology may want it for its own question ([ADR-013](../decisions.d/ADR-013.md)); it is not held
there.
