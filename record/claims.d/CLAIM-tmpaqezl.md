# CLAIM-tmpaqezl: Action readiness is subthreshold potentiation

## The claim

The owner asks whether Frijda's action readiness is closer to the
activation thresholds the record already uses. It is, on Frijda's own
account. [NOTE-433](../notes.d/NOTE-433.md) reads Frijda, Ridderinkhof and Rietveld ([LIT-540](../literature.d/LIT-540.md)) as
saying that states of action readiness are "subthreshold preparatory
potentiations" of relational actions and coping skills, and that they
"can be felt without overt action".

[CLAIM-550](CLAIM-550.md) (Active) gives the record's activation model. A perspective's
activation is z = b(c) + α(c)d, rising with its discrepancy d, and it
acts with probability σ(κ(z − θ)). Mapped onto it:

- **Readiness** is a concern-relative rise in z, through the baseline b
  or the gain α, for a class of actions. It is *sub*threshold while
  z < θ: prepared, possibly felt, not yet acted on.
- **Control precedence** ([TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md)'s persistence, resumption, attention
  capture and priority) is what the potentiated class shows once it
  crosses θ and competes with other actions for execution.
- **Valence**, in the owner's sense of the direction of the
  predisposition, is which actions the potentiation favours. "Given a
  conditioning context which includes knowledge of affect, the valence of
  an agent's affect tells us what behaviors they would be predisposed
  towards, i.e. the actions that would be triggered."

## What it joins

- **[CLAIM-tmphyvh3](CLAIM-tmphyvh3.md)'s disposition.** Subthreshold potentiation is a
  disposition that is present before any action. Frijda's readiness felt
  "without overt action" is the individual case of valence unobserved in
  conduct.
- **[CLAIM-549](CLAIM-549.md)'s quiescence.** A perspective below threshold can still be
  potentiated, so quiet is not the absence of readiness.
- **[CLAIM-548](CLAIM-548.md).** Activation, the authority granted and efficacy are
  separate quantities. Readiness is the first, and a grant ([CLAIM-283](CLAIM-283.md)) is
  what lets a crossing govern action.

## What it does not say

That readiness is only a threshold shift. Frijda's control precedence
also includes interrupting other activity and resuming after interruption,
which a static threshold does not model. Nor does it settle whether
"valence" should name the direction of predisposition, as the owner uses
it, or [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)'s sign of position. That is [CLAIM-tmpi0oub](CLAIM-tmpi0oub.md)'s question.
