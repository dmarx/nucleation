# SYNTHESIS-tmpnakke: What a record of a developing argument can secure is traceable change over its accountability structure, which is neither sufficiency for revision nor correctness: current conclusions and support topology do not fix revisions, a sufficient state makes history redundant only while its model and decisions hold, and what kept justifications can buy is a wider family of decisions and decisions a bounded agent could not otherwise make

## The account

A developing argument drops things legitimately, so a record of it cannot
aim at preservation: what it can secure is "change that is announced and
located" ([CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md)), and a manuscript is then a view checked against
that record rather than the argument's only copy ([CLAIM-178](../claims.d/CLAIM-178.md)). The
interlocutor's reading gives traceability an object: "the relevant
fidelity object is different", the accountability structure of what was
asserted, why, how it was challenged and what justified its revision
([CLAIM-250](../claims.d/CLAIM-250.md)). Both are Proposed.

The exchange of 2026-10-14 (workbench entry of 2026-10-15) then fixed,
by construction, what such a record is and is not. Two histories can
agree on every current conclusion and call for different revisions
([CLAIM-294](../claims.d/CLAIM-294.md), [CASE-093](../cases.d/CASE-093.md)), and two records with the same support graph can
call for different revisions when one support fails ([CLAIM-293](../claims.d/CLAIM-293.md),
[CASE-089](../cases.d/CASE-089.md)), so neither current conclusions nor the topology of
justification fixes revision. A state sufficient for a decision family
under a model makes the rest of the history worthless for those
decisions, and stops being sufficient when a source is discredited, the
model is revised or the decisions change ([CLAIM-300](../claims.d/CLAIM-300.md), [CASE-082](../cases.d/CASE-082.md)).
Sufficiency, accountability and correctness are three properties, each
able to hold while the other two fail ([CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md), with [CASE-082](../cases.d/CASE-082.md), [CASE-084](../cases.d/CASE-084.md)
and [CASE-093](../cases.d/CASE-093.md)); [CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md)'s body draws the link to [CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md): "This says
traceability is not sufficiency either." What a kept record of
justifications can add is therefore not information for decisions the
current state already suffices for, but a wider family of decisions and
decisions a bounded agent could not otherwise afford ([CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md), resting
on [CLAIM-294](../claims.d/CLAIM-294.md) and [CLAIM-300](../claims.d/CLAIM-300.md); Proposed). [CASE-086](../cases.d/CASE-086.md) and [CASE-090](../cases.d/CASE-090.md) illustrate
the two operations this supports: tracing what a defeated premise
carried, which propagates "obligations to review", and reopening an
alternative whose defeater is defeated.

What stays open is the optimum and the test: the least state a family of
revisions needs, and whether it looks like provenance, dependencies and
defeated alternatives ([QUESTION-105](../questions.d/QUESTION-105.md)); which mechanism a structured
record's advantage runs through ([QUESTION-108](../questions.d/QUESTION-108.md), with [CASE-091](../cases.d/CASE-091.md) as the
design); how to judge sufficiency when inquiry changes its own decision
family ([QUESTION-112](../questions.d/QUESTION-112.md)); and what a manuscript's choice of claims must
preserve ([QUESTION-258](../questions.d/QUESTION-258.md)).

## Admission

Not admitted. Each route was tried against the components as written.

- **Derived.** The valid inferences from the demonstrated claims and
  cases are already held. That what the record secures is not
  sufficiency is in [CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md)'s body; that it is not correctness is
  [CASE-084](../cases.d/CASE-084.md), which A6 called "a direct companion" to [CLAIM-181](../claims.d/CLAIM-181.md) and
  [CLAIM-190](../claims.d/CLAIM-190.md) ([CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md)'s body; [CLAIM-190](../claims.d/CLAIM-190.md) is Superseded). That keeping
  justificatory structure does not keep what revision needs is
  [CLAIM-293](../claims.d/CLAIM-293.md) against [CLAIM-250](../claims.d/CLAIM-250.md) and [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md), said in [CLAIM-250](../claims.d/CLAIM-250.md)'s note of 2026-10-30. That history's value, where the state
  suffices, is not informational is [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md)'s title, resting on
  [CLAIM-294](../claims.d/CLAIM-294.md) and [CLAIM-300](../claims.d/CLAIM-300.md). That conclusions fall short and a sufficient
  state is enough for a fixed family, so the optimum lies between, is
  [QUESTION-105](../questions.d/QUESTION-105.md)'s body ("bound it from both sides"). That this record's
  own `rests_on` and `grounds` record "that support exists, not how
  much" is [CLAIM-293](../claims.d/CLAIM-293.md)'s summary, and that they propagate review and not
  belief is [CASE-086](../cases.d/CASE-086.md). The remaining combinations are conjunctions, which
  are trivial. One candidate does not follow: that whether a dropped
  commitment was dissolution or loss ([CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md)) is relative to a
  decision family the argument itself changes ([CLAIM-300](../claims.d/CLAIM-300.md), [QUESTION-112](../questions.d/QUESTION-112.md)).
  It needs a premise no component holds, that a legitimate drop is a drop
  of what is redundant for the argument's decisions; [CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md) makes
  legitimacy a matter of rejection, and the record keeps what it drops
  with its status.
- **Hypothesis.** Taken whole, the account conjoins its components, and
  a conjunction cannot fail while every conjunct holds. The narrower
  integrating sentence, that what keeping the accountability structure
  buys is [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md)'s value, has three readings. As "can buy", it is
  stated: [CLAIM-250](../claims.d/CLAIM-250.md)'s note says [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md) "gives it a possible
  instrumental justification", and [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md)'s summary says "this says
  what preserving it could buy". As "is worth keeping for", it turns on
  cost, which [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md) disclaims and leaves to [QUESTION-098](../questions.d/QUESTION-098.md), a free
  parameter; as "buys only that", it is against [CLAIM-295](../claims.d/CLAIM-295.md)'s own fallback,
  that the structure "might still be justified by inspectability,
  auditing, and shared human–agent coordination".
- **Integration.** The discriminating comparison the line names is
  [CASE-091](../cases.d/CASE-091.md), and [QUESTION-108](../questions.d/QUESTION-108.md) holds its three outcomes open. No component
  gives evidence for one outcome, so an integration settling it would
