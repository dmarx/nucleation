summary: >-
  Frey and Schneider (2020). Drawing on Hirschman, they distinguish
  affective voice (expression that seeks to persuade power holders) from
  effective voice (speech with a binding effect through transparent
  processes) and argue that online platforms make exit and affective voice
  cheap while effective voice is nearly absent and control is
  consolidated. They propose mechanisms: rules binding on administrators,
  selection and removal of those in authority, binding petition
  thresholds, internal blocs and designed occasions for structural change.
