# LEAD-tmpywiwn: Read the two semicontraction papers, to see whether contraction with respect to a seminorm states in one theory what the record puts together from Markov retention and partial contraction, and whether its conditions say which features a restatement conserves

## What to read for

- **The conserved part.** How each paper characterizes the subspace a
  semicontracting system does not contract (the kernel of the seminorm),
  and whether the limit is fixed by the start's projection onto it, as
  DeGroot's πF is ([THEORY-960](../theory.d/THEORY-960.md)).
- **Markov chains.** De Pasquale and colleagues name Markov chains and
  ergodic coefficients. Whether their result gives [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s retention
  bound, or a sharper one, and whether it covers time-dependent steps.
- **Approximate conservation.** What the theory says about a subspace that
  is conserved only approximately, against [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s "a bounded
  conservation defect does not prevent forgetting" and [CLAIM-tmpw6wkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw6wkt.md)'s
  1/n rate.
- **Conditions a design could meet.** Whether a condition on the step (a
  seminorm in which it contracts) could be read as a design rule: what a
  record must copy exactly for a feature to be conserved.

## Then

File a NOTE and a THEORY for each. If the reading carries [CLAIM-tmpdx6l8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdx6l8.md)'s
unification, add it to the claim's `grounds` and revise [ARG-tmp5ahr5](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp5ahr5.md)'s
critical questions; if it does not, say why in a dated note on the claim.

## Note of 2026-11-21: read

Both semicontraction papers were read on 2026-11-21 ([THEORY-tmprwljl](../theory.d/THEORY-tmprwljl.md), [THEORY-tmpq3h4k](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpq3h4k.md)). Semicontraction does not join the record's pieces into one theory of what is kept: it contracts only off a seminorm's kernel and keeps nothing by itself, and where the limit is fixed an exact conserved functional fixes it. [CLAIM-tmpdx6l8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdx6l8.md) (version 3) and [TERM-tmp7i7ed](../terms.d/TERM-tmp7i7ed.md) (version 2) were revised accordingly. Nothing in either paper covers approximate conservation.
