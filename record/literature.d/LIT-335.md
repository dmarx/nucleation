  Döring & Isham (2008), DOI-10.1063/1.2883742. A projector P̂ is mapped
  to the clopen sub-object δ(P̂) of the spectral presheaf Σ over the poset
  V(H) of abelian von Neumann subalgebras, by "daseinisation" (de Groote's
  V-support): δ(P̂)_V = the least projector in V above P̂. The map is
  injective and preserves ∨, but only δ(P̂∧Q̂) ⪯ δ(P̂)∧δ(Q̂). This
  represents the propositional language PL(S) in the Heyting algebra
  Sub_cl(Σ). A pure state gives the truth object T^ψ, and "A ε Δ" gets the
  sieve-valued truth value {V′ ⊆ V | ⟨ψ|δ(Ê[A∈Δ])_{V′}|ψ⟩ = 1}.

# LIT-335: A topos foundation for theories of physics: II. Daseinisation and the liberation of quantum theory

Andreas Döring, Chris Isham (2008), *Journal of Mathematical Physics 49(5):053516* — DOI-10.1063/1.2883742

[NOTE-tmph2vm6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmph2vm6.md) is the close reading of 2026-09-29, and it placed the work: **Active** — worth reading as the core reference for "quantum propositions as clopen sub-objects of the spectral presheaf" (daseinisation, Sub_cl(Σ), truth objects). It is a cleaned-up restatement of Isham–Butterfield ([LIT-325](LIT-325.md)) plus de Groote's operation, not a new no-go result. Several proofs are sketched or omitted, and the "no global elements" statement it relies on needs dim H > 2, which it never says.
