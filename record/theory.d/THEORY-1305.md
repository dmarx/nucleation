# THEORY-1305: In Kasser and Ryan's two cross-sectional samples, holding constant how much a person aspires to overall, the relative importance of extrinsic aspirations (money, fame, image) goes with less self-actualization and vitality and more physical symptoms, and of intrinsic aspirations (growth, relationships, community, health) with the reverse; the paper does not test whether this survives the reasons for pursuing the goals

## Source

Kasser and Ryan (1996), [LIT-1977](../literature.d/LIT-1977.md), *PSPB* 22, 280–287; read in
[NOTE-1525](../notes.d/NOTE-1525.md).

## What was actually shown

**Two kinds of aspiration.** Seven domains load on two varimax factors,
intrinsic and extrinsic, for both importance and likelihood, in both
samples (Table 2, p. 282).

**Study 1, 100 adults** (Table 3, p. 283). With overall importance
entered first: extrinsic importance with self-actualization β = −.52,
vitality −.60, physical symptoms .46; intrinsic .40, .46, −.35.

**Study 2, 192 students** (Table 4, p. 285). Extrinsic importance with
self-actualization −.67, vitality −.34, depression .30, narcissism .35,
physical symptoms .43; intrinsic .59, .31, −.27, −.31, −.35. The pattern
survived social desirability entered first, with depression and physical
symptoms dropping to marginal (p. 285).

**The scoring carries the result.** The overall score averages all
domains (p. 282); the result is about the balance of aspirations, the
same reading Bradshaw et al. 2022 later gave through relative-centrality
scoring (as reported in [LIT-1967](../literature.d/LIT-1967.md)).

This could have come out otherwise: with overall striving held fixed,
the content of aspirations could have been unrelated to well-being.

## What this does not say

- **That extrinsic aspirations lower well-being.** "all data are
  correlational and cross-sectional, so no conclusions regarding causality
  can be made" (p. 286).
- **That the pattern holds beyond the reasons for the goals.** No measure
  of reasons; the authors call that test "interesting" (p. 286).
- **That two independent results were found.** Intrinsic and extrinsic
  Step-2 results are close to one contrast with opposite signs.
- **That needs mediate.** Need satisfaction is invoked (p. 280), not
  measured.
