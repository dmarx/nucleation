# CLAIM-tmpddl2d: The failure of a race reading of folding islands in a single-target constraint-growth system does not tell against it where it was built, since that system has no coherent alternative branch for a small-island seed's territory to fall into, which is how the self-folding sheets misfold; the result bounds the reading's reach and leaves its home case and any system with competing self-consistent folds untested

## The objection

Reading A ([CLAIM-tmpf0417](CLAIM-tmpf0417.md)) takes [CASE-tmp2a9lm](../cases.d/CASE-tmp2a9lm.md) to show that a predictor of
productive nuclei needs the order and threshold of growth. The study's
model differs from the self-folding sheets in the respect that matters for
the predictor it tested:

- **No coherent distractor.** Its ambiguous relations admit one random
  distractor pair from each side, so a wrong state seldom extends into a
  consistent wrong region; it jams or stays alone. In the sheets, a degree-4
  vertex has two branches, each consistent with its neighbours, and a wrong
  branch can propagate as a fold of its own ([THEORY-1418](../theory.d/THEORY-1418.md)).
- **So the race has nothing to win.** Voronoi-island coverage scores the
  territory a small-island seed takes before larger islands arrive. Where
  that territory cannot settle into a consistent wrong mode, taking it
  costs little; the harmful seeds the study found were large-island ones,
  acting by another route.

The study therefore bounds the race reading's reach, to systems with
coherent alternatives, and does not test it there. A study with two or
more self-consistent global configurations sharing a region (a shared body
with two seams, as in [CASE-374](../cases.d/CASE-374.md), but grown on a lattice with compatible
seeds) would.

## What it grants

- That the race reading failed where it was tested, and that the predictor
  which worked reads the growth order ([CLAIM-tmpzo914](CLAIM-tmpzo914.md), [CLAIM-tmpzi0ri](CLAIM-tmpzi0ri.md)).
- That reading A holds for systems like the one tested.
