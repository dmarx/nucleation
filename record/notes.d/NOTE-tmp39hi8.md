# NOTE-tmp39hi8: Krohn and Weninger — Subreddit Links Drive Community Creation and User Engagement on Reddit

## Contribution

The first large-scale study of links between subreddits, including links
to subreddits that do not yet exist ("inventive" links), with a
population-level association between such links and later creation.

## Key results

1. **Name first (p. 2).** Links are sometimes "made to subreddits that do
   not exist" "(often as a joke), and occasionally these links spur the
   creation of a new community"; the joke share is not measured.
2. **Correlation only (p. 4).** "we do not claim to know if the link is
   directly or causally responsible for the creation of subreddit"; "we
   can only study the correlation between links and subreddit creation".
3. **Precedence (p. 5).** "16.60% of all created subreddits were
   referenced by a subreddit link at some point before their creation";
   "8.78% of all subreddits were created within 1 day of an inventive
   link". Success "reaches a saturation point of 33% at about 20
   repetitions". Most inventive links never see their subreddit created,
   and "the probability a link will produce a new community has decreased
   over time" (p. 4). The paper gives "715,106 (13.60%)" as eventually
   successful, though its own counts make that 16.6%.
4. **The comparison (p. 5).** A survival comparison against a random
   later-created subreddit, not a natural experiment in the econometric
   sense: "inventive links are not a guarantee of future subreddit
   creation, they are highly and immediately correlated to the birth of
   new communities". Activity in the linking subreddit "was not found to
   be predictive of the creation of a referenced subreddit".
5. **Links to existing subreddits (pp. 7–8).** "the effect is not
   long-lasting or of a high magnitude"; more were "reactive" than
   "proactive": "While some links are the cause of increased activity,
   others appear to be the effect of previous activity".
6. **Left open (p. 10).** "Were these users previously members of the
   linking community?" and "Are communities created following an
   inventive link more or less likely to succeed?"

## Assumptions

Every well-formed link treated as deliberate; creation at any later time
as success; existence apparently dated by first activity in the data;
links by a subreddit's eventual creator not excluded; Reddit only.

## Concepts

Promotional, inventive and self links; reactive and proactive links;
monthly subreddit link graph; information foraging.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CASE-tmp6idy5](../cases.d/CASE-tmp6idy5.md).** The only measured evidence on record for the case's
  third class, identities arising inside Reddit: a name-first route to a
  community, used at scale, correlational. ChatGPT's account of the
  paper (A82) as "natural-experiment" evidence that links "drive"
  formation, and as "symbolic reification becoming institutional
  construction", overstates it: nothing institutional (governance,
  persistence, viability) is measured, and where members come from is
  left open.
- **[CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md).** A route to a named unit that starts from a symbol, not
  from collapsing differences.
- **Self-fulfilling reference.** An inventive link is a name whose use can
  bring about its referent ([CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md), [THEORY-854](../theory.d/THEORY-854.md)), and success rises with
  repetition ([CLAIM-426](../claims.d/CLAIM-426.md)).

## Limitations

Observational, causation disclaimed; unmatched random control; viability
of created communities not tracked; an arithmetic error and two internal
inconsistencies (the magnitude of the promotional effect, and its
comparison with the control).
