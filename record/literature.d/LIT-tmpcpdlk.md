## Why this is here

The theoretical statement of legacy motivation as a source of what looks like altruism toward the future, the temporal version of the owner's thesis ([CLAIM-tmpjylq8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjylq8.md)). For [QUESTION-tmpruhu4](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpruhu4.md).

## Read in its place

The work cannot be read here, so the record holds it at second hand.

Wade-Benzoni, Sondak and Galinsky (2010) cite Wade-Benzoni 2006 three
times. Legacy gives "a symbolic form of immortality" (p. 7). A legacy
needs an enduring effect on other people: one cannot leave it "by
affecting merely one's own future self" (p. 12). And the 2006 chapter is
among the sources of their list of what marks an intergenerational
decision: power asymmetry, no direct reciprocity, a self–other trade-off,
a time delay, role transition and growing consequences (p. 19). Their
experiments show the programme the chapter framed: legacy concern raised
by burdens, and more generosity to future others with burdens than with
benefits. Missing still: the chapter's own argument, whether it calls
intergenerational beneficence altruism or egoism, any evidence it reports,
and what it proposes for promoting decisions that favour future
generations.
