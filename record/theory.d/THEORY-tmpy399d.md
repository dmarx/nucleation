# THEORY-tmpy399d: Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy hold, quoting Simondon, that research into individuation consists in following the being in its genesis and accomplishing the genesis of thought with the genesis of the object, so that researchers individuate into what they study, and they leave open what methods would do this

## The account

Quoting Simondon (2020, p. 14), the authors hold that research consists
in "following the being in its genesis, in accomplishing the genesis of
thought at the same time as the genesis of the object" (p. 1366).
Researchers are then "individuating into others" (p. 1366): the inquiry
individuates along with what it studies. What methods would let
researchers "individuate into" what they study the authors leave open.

## Evidence

- A stated demand, without an argument beyond the quotation, and with no
  method given for meeting it.
- The paper's own reanalysis is of a published excerpt of another
  author's ethnography (pp. 1359–1363), so it is not carried out in the
  way the demand asks.
- [NOTE-1484](../notes.d/NOTE-1484.md) notes that "Agency is a matter of perspective" (p. 1357)
  sits uneasily with the realism of the later pages, and that the paper
  does not reconcile the two.

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-366](../questions.d/QUESTION-366.md) asks whether an inquiry needs the record of its own
development to know its object, "as Simondon's method and its
organization-studies readers hold", and quotes this passage (p. 1366) as
one of the two methodological demands it tests against what the record
has shown. The reading is that demand, filed so the question can cite
it.
