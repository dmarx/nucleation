# LIT-784: Translation and Relevance: Cognition and Context

Ernst-August Gutt (2000), *Translation and Relevance: Cognition and Context*,
2nd edition, Manchester: St. Jerome — DOI-10.4324/9781315760018

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for: Gutt
applies relevance theory to translation, treating it as interpretive use of
language across languages, in which the translation is to resemble the
original interpretively, enough to be adequately relevant to its audience. He
distinguishes direct translation, which preserves the original's communicative
clues, from indirect translation.
