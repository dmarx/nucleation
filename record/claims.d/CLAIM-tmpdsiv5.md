# CLAIM-tmpdsiv5: Per audit or reframing a claim undergoes, the chance that it takes a substantive revision falls as inbound rests_on and grounds edges accrue, because each edge adds a downstream recheck; a count of revisions over time cannot test this, since citation also draws the audits

**One of two readings of CASE-tmpdcoyb; the other is CLAIM-tmpylhdj.** This reading keeps what CLAIM-778 says about constraint and moves it to the right denominator. If citation draws scrutiny, revisions per unit of time can rise with citation even while each look at a cited claim is less likely to change it. The sentence is the one CLAIM-778's admission of 2026-11-21 wrote for this case.

## What would test it

A count of audits per claim. The material exists: status reviews, synthesis reviews, sweeps and objection threads each name the claims they touched, in curation entries and pull requests. Code each as one audit of each claim it names, with its date; then compare substantive revisions per audit across inbound counts at matched age and matched audit count, by the method of CASE-tmpdcoyb. The 148 revisions that fall within a day of a first edge suggest the audits are frequent and clustered, which is what this reading needs and the other does not explain away.

## What it does not say

It does not rescue CLAIM-778 as stated: that claim speaks of the revisions an entry can still take narrowing over its life cycle, which the count bears on directly. This is a narrower claim, and it may be the one the record meant.
