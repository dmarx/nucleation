# QUESTION-035: Relative to a stated target, when is a combined form one system, where does its boundary lie, and how long does it last?

## Why it is a question

It has come up three times in one day:
- **The dancing couple** ([CASE-046](../cases.d/CASE-046.md)). The owner holds that it "merits some
  privileged nomenclature" ([CLAIM-175](../claims.d/CLAIM-175.md)).
- **Otto and his notebook** ([LIT-097](../literature.d/LIT-097.md); [CASE-043](../cases.d/CASE-043.md)).
- **The firm at its all-hands** ([CASE-050](../cases.d/CASE-050.md)).

The record has candidate tests, each measuring something different:
- **Mutual entrainment** ([CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md)), which measures timing.
- **Reciprocal compensation** ([LIT-902](../literature.d/LIT-902.md), unread), which measures whether
  a goal is preserved.
- **Near-decomposability** priced by an Occam factor ([CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md),
  [LIT-905](../literature.d/LIT-905.md)), which measures strength.
- **Conserved coherence** ([CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md)), which measures persistence.
- **Gluing** ([CLAIM-153](../claims.d/CLAIM-153.md)), which measures unity across contexts.

[QUESTION-009](QUESTION-009.md) asks when coordination makes an additional *agent*. This asks
the prior question: when it makes one *system*. Selfhood stays a further
question again, kept apart by [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md).

## What would count as an answer

A criterion that, given a stated target and measurements of a candidate,
returns a verdict that can be wrong. It should say which variables are
inside, from when to when, and with what margin. It must also handle the
cases the record already holds: the couple after the song must come out
two. One-way drive, such as the metronome or the nerve and the muscle, may
come out one system with a hierarchical architecture. Version 1 required
the opposite, and that requirement was withdrawn ([CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md)). A
criterion that every pair of interacting things satisfies is not an answer ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)'s permissiveness, in another form).

The proposed answer is [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md). Its first objection, that one-way
drive should not pass ([CLAIM-173](../claims.d/CLAIM-173.md)), was rejected by [CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md).
