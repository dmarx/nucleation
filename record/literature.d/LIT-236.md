# LIT-236: Rate-Distortion Theoretic Generalization Bounds for Stochastic Learning Algorithms

Milad Sefidgaran, Amin Gohari, Gaël Richard, Umut Şimşekli (2022), *Conference on Learning Theory (COLT) 2022, PMLR 178 (pp. 4416–4463 as cited by [LIT-233](LIT-233.md)); arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-2203.02474](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02474)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpdhrvi](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpdhrvi.md).*

- **What is compressed.** Not one trained model. It is a block of m
  independent training runs, (S_i, W_i), covered by a book of e^{mR}
  candidate output blocks to within average distortion ε in
  generalisation error (Def. 1).
- **The bound.** |E gen(S, W)| ≤ √(2σ²·R_E(ε)/n) + ε, with R_E(ε) the
  least I(S; Ŵ) over surrogates within ε (Theorem 4). For general spaces
  the proof is Xu–Raginsky applied to Ŵ.
- **Lossless is Xu–Raginsky.** At ε = 0 it is |E gen| ≤ √(2σ²I(S;W)/n)
  (Corollary 5). For finite alphabets I(S;W) is an achievable lossless
  rate (Theorems 2–3).
- **Lossy and Lipschitz.** RD(ε/(2L); P_W, ρ) of the output's marginal law
  replaces I(S;W) (Corollary 6), and as ε → 0 the rate-distortion
  dimension of P_W (Corollary 7, under a uniform-convergence assumption).
- **Tail.** With probability ≥ 1 − δ, gen ≤ √(2σ²(R_p(δ, ε) +
  log(1/δ))/n) + ε, with R_p a supremum over laws within KL log(1/δ) of
  P_{S,W} (Theorem 10). It is one-sided, and can be worse than Hoeffding
  when S and W are independent.
- **Also recovered.** Steinke–Zakynthinou's CMI bound and VC bounds, in
  expectation and in tail form (Appendix A).
- **Limits.** Every bound depends on μ. None is computed for a network.
  The Gaussian-mean example's d/n rate rests on a units slip.
