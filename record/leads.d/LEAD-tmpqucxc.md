# LEAD-tmpqucxc: Extend perspective transplantation to memes: transfer chosen memes between matched populations and test whether characteristic response profiles, their blind spots included, are reproduced when evidence is held fixed and its source, medium and implications are varied

## The gap

CASE-148 exchanges "learned interpretive rules, specialist parameters,
training histories, institutional rules and shared memory" between two
populations matched in behaviour. A5 and A6 propose adding memes to what
is transferred, and A6 §9 gives the probe: "hold evidence fixed while
changing its source, implications, medium or relationship to existing
commitments", reading each response as the profile (L, E, R, C) of
TERM-tmp9mag8. Neither says what the meme is when transferred, nor how a
profile is told apart from the propositions the memes state.

## The plan

1. **The transferred item.** Transfer a maxim as text alone, and the same
   maxim with the practice of applying it (which conclusions it is
   applied to), so that CLAIM-tmpgc8vi's positional claim is tested
   against its content.
2. **The control.** Transfer the propositions the memes state, without
   the memes, to meet CLAIM-tmpjlxfy's defeater (limitations "adequately
   explained by ... independently transmitted propositional beliefs").
3. **The probes.** A family fixed in advance and evaluated on held-out
   situations, since agreement on a restricted probe family does not show
   shared structure (CLAIM-142).
4. **The transformations.** Say which changes of situation count as the
   same transformation in both populations, the correspondence CLAIM-249
   says comparison needs, before comparing profiles.
5. **Populations.** Artificial agents first, where CASE-148's transfers
   are cheap.

## What each outcome would show

Profiles, blind spots included, reproduced by the memes and not by their
propositions would support CLAIM-tmpjitli and CLAIM-tmpjlxfy. Profiles
reproduced equally by the propositions would meet both defeaters.
