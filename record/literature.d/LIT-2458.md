# LIT-2458: Irrational Random Utility Models

Daniele Caliari and Henrik Petri (2024). "Irrational Random Utility Models"
*arXiv preprint (econ.TH), v2 2025-05-28*. [ARXIV-2403.10208](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10208).

## Key takeaways

Random-utility data have irrational representations whenever preferences are
sufficiently uncorrelated; welfare conclusions depend on the analyst's
interpretation, not on data alone.

## Standing in the record

Registered as a stand-in for [LIT-2363](LIT-2363.md). It shares the author and the
theme, that choice data underdetermine welfare-relevant preferences, at the
aggregate level; it carries none of the target's individual-level results.
