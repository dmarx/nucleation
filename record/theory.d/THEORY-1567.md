# THEORY-1567: MacAskill, Thomas and Vallinder factor the change in value an event makes through a feature of the world as significance times persistence times contingency, give its marginal, ex ante and alternative forms, and place it as an estimate of the importance factor of the importance, tractability and neglectedness framework

## The account

With ΔV and ΔT the changes an event makes to a feature's total value and
duration, significance is ΔV/ΔT, persistence T and contingency ΔT/T, so
ΔV is their product (eqs. 1–2). Contingency asks "how much of the
feature's value can be attributed to a particular agent's decision at a
particular point in time" (p. 1). The framework's "main goal is to help
estimate the instrumental value (typically interpreted as the expected
value) of an event, compared to the relevant counterfactual" (pp. 1–2).

## Evidence

- Under uncertainty the ex ante factors keep the product but are not the
  expectations of the factors (§2.2, eq. 6).
- An alternative takes significance as V/T and makes contingency, at the
  margin, the sum of the semi-elasticities of significance, persistence
  and the probability that the feature is present (§3, eqs. 10–16).
- Summed over additive features, the estimates give the importance
  factor (§4, eqs. 18–20); the authors grant such features "may often be
  difficult to identify" (p. 9).

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-299](../questions.d/QUESTION-299.md) holds this reading as "The impersonal ground's accounting
(MacAskill, Thomas and Vallinder, [NOTE-1300](../notes.d/NOTE-1300.md) ...). Value is significance
times persistence times contingency, and nothing about anyone's self
enters." The reading is that premise.
