*The owner relayed an interlocutor's reading of PR [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/121) on 2026-10-12. The
reading covers the PR itself, its reconciliation comment of the same day and
the underlying claims. The owner said it is "an interlocutor's reading, file
it as advice". No share link or turn numbers were given, so it is cited here
by date.*

**The reading's diagnosis.** "The project is oscillating between three
different theories of meaning and identity without always making the
transitions explicit": a production-centred theory (intention fixes what was
meant), a relational-constitutive one (roles, norms and uptake partly fix
the act), and an operational one (what can be recovered depends on probes,
populations and tasks). "These need not be competing theories. But they have
different identity conditions, and treating their objects as interchangeable
creates much of the apparent conflict."

**The four open tensions, as the reading takes them.**
1. Whose uptake constitutes the act: different objects. Intending,
   performing and being recognized are distinct variables. Filed as the
   thesis [CLAIM-255](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-255.md), which partly answers [QUESTION-073](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-073.md).
2. Circularity: "[CLAIM-152](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) has the stronger argument as currently
   formulated". Competent uptake is "a locally privileged criterion". This
   repeats [CLAIM-241](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-241.md)'s point, which is now noted there as a second
   occurrence. No new code.
3. Fidelity and development: "mostly reconcilable". The fidelity object of a
   developing argument is its "accountability structure". Filed as the
   thesis [CLAIM-250](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-250.md), beside [CLAIM-189](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-189.md).
4. Cover: three operations, only one of which engages gluing. Filed as the
   counter [CLAIM-251](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-251.md) to [CLAIM-201](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-201.md).

**Beyond the four.**
- The decomposition into production, social constitution, interpretation and
  operational recovery. "Recoverability does not imply constitution", and
  "constitution does not imply identifiability". Most of this is already held
  ([CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md), [CLAIM-135](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-135.md), [TERM-040](../../record/terms.d/TERM-040.md)). The new part is the production level,
  which [TERM-040](../../record/terms.d/TERM-040.md) has no slot for. That is noted on [TERM-040](../../record/terms.d/TERM-040.md).
- The empirical contribution. The reading favours [CLAIM-202](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-202.md) as the
  principal empirical thesis, but says its rival set lacks a strong
  contextual baseline. Filed as the counter [CLAIM-205](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-205.md). The test it calls
  distinctive, predicting an unobserved composition, is [QUESTION-074](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-074.md).
  That question is scoped by [CLAIM-260](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-260.md)'s conditions, under which alone
  approximate transports compose.
- The tension the reading would leave open, realist against operationalist.
  Its middle position, a kind as a hypothesis of invariance with
  extrapolation to new measurement families, is filed as [CLAIM-213](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-213.md).

**Its five priorities, recorded as advice and not taken.**
1. Resolve the referent of identity first.
2. Retain target relativity, with population, competence and disagreement as
   explicit parameters.
3. Separate preservation from evaluation.
4. Formalize the measurement scenario before invoking sheaf theory.
5. Make structural generalization the empirical target.

Its closing reformulation of the line's question is quoted in [CLAIM-213](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-213.md):
"Ask which relations are invariant, under which transformations, relative
to which observational capacities—and which of those invariances support
predictions beyond the observations that established them."
