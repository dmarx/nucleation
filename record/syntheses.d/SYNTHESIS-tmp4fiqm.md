# SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm: Remedies foreclosed before review, by faits accomplis, speed, friction and siege: acting before an opportunity to intervene can take effect is proposed as a wrong of its own, an irreversible act is proposed to convert a holder's consent into a claim to compensation and to give the breacher leverage over its own settlement but no power over what is owed, an opponent can put a remedy out of reach by raising what pursuing it costs, the cost of imposing a decision and of contesting it can move apart, and the baseline against which terms are judged can be the actor's own product

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency lines, on remedies foreclosed before they can be reviewed: by acting first, by outlasting, by moving friction, and by setting the baseline. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and every component is new to any account. Most components complement or rest on CLAIM-464 (Active, the owner's earlier observation that an intervention can make a corrective outcome costlier or unreachable before review while the standards stay the same). The account cites CLAIM-464 and does not integrate it. The dependence-and-domination line's account is SYNTHESIS-095; this part is not yet among its parts. Its sibling, on answerability and contestability, is SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa.*

## The account

**The wrong of acting first.** CLAIM-tmpazi8a (the owner's, U27, with A59's
four conditions, three kinds of standing and the narrowing to one mechanism
of bullying; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements CLAIM-464,
CLAIM-624, CLAIM-tmpyo35b, CLAIM-tmpl0sgt and CLAIM-tmp3t16f: where a party
has standing to authorize, suspend or contest an act, an actor who without
adequate justification makes its consequences irreversible before that
standing can be exercised defeats it, "even where the objection would have
failed". Its body says CLAIM-464 "holds the timing" and "is causal and says
nothing about standing"; it keeps the single act apart from "the wrong of
constructing a relationship in which such acts can routinely prevail",
which it calls CLAIM-624's. It names the family "wrongful unilateral
action", with remedial hold-up (CLAIM-tmp3t16f) and counter-corrective
domination (CLAIM-619) among its kinds (A59 §9).

**The rule converted.** CLAIM-tmpl0sgt (the connection the owner's, U26;
the form A's, A57, after Calabresi and Melamed, unread here; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) complements CLAIM-464, CLAIM-tmp3t16f,
CLAIM-tmpa0wdv and CLAIM-tmpazi8a: an irreversible act against a
consent-protected entitlement leaves the holder in practice only a claim to
compensation, so the actor has changed the rule of allocation "from consent
to liability". Its body sets the pairs: it is "the rule changing" and
CLAIM-tmp3t16f "the terms reached once it has"; CLAIM-tmpazi8a "names the
wrong in the order of moves; this one names what the wrong converts"; and
with CLAIM-tmpa0wdv "the two agree".

**The leverage gained.** CLAIM-tmp3t16f (A's, A55 §3 and §9, at the
owner's U25; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements CLAIM-464,
CLAIM-619, CLAIM-tmpl0sgt, CLAIM-tmpa0wdv and CLAIM-tmpdic1g, and bears on
QUESTION-tmp9oq97: a breach can lower the injured party's position if it
refuses to settle, so a settlement it rationally accepts can leave the
breacher ahead, "the leverage coming from the violation". Its body says
CLAIM-464 holds "the reachability half exactly" and this adds the
bargaining channel, and it quotes A55 §8 against inferring from B's choice
that "The circumstances making that settlement B's best available option
were legitimate."

**The power not gained.** CLAIM-tmpa0wdv (A's, A85 §5, A87 §2 and §10, A90
V and VII, A97; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
organizational-normativity and distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-464,
complements CLAIM-tmp3t16f, CLAIM-tmpl0sgt, CLAIM-tmp6vck6 and
CLAIM-tmpssfc1, and uses TERM-085: making a remedy unreachable gains a
wrongdoer no normative power over what is owed; the claim persists while
its remedy's correctability falls to zero, and a settlement reached under
the conditions the violation created does not authorize the violation. Its
body calls its third part "the normative complement" of CLAIM-tmp3t16f, the
conversion of CLAIM-tmpl0sgt "practical, not normative", and A87 §10's
"Litigation by attrition" the owner's siege, CLAIM-tmpyo35b.
QUESTION-tmp14i7s (A's, A68 §4, from Fiss as the record read him; Open;
line distributed-agency) asks whether the capacity to make remedies costly
can bend, over time, the standard they are measured against. Its body says
that if it can, CLAIM-464's premise holds at a time and can fail over time,
and CLAIM-tmpa0wdv "would have a route by which it gains that power after
all"; it names Levinson (LIT-1592, Unfetched) as the reading to do first.
It has no front-matter relations.

**Siege.** CLAIM-tmpyo35b (the owner's, U28; thesis, demonstrated,
Proposed) grounds in CASE-tmphljcr, bears on QUESTION-tmpcz1ui, uses
TERM-085 and TERM-105, and complements CLAIM-464, CLAIM-619, CLAIM-601,
CLAIM-tmpazi8a, CLAIM-tmp3t16f, CLAIM-tmpa0wdv and CLAIM-tmph28l6: an actor
can make a legally recognized corrective objective unreachable for another,
with the merits, the law and that party's preferences unchanged, by raising
what pursuing it costs, in spending or in time, above what that party can
sustain. Its body records that A64 told the point as its own "key
insight"; sets "Temporal preemption" (CLAIM-tmpazi8a) and "Attritional
exhaustion" beside each other as sharing one result (A64's close); calls
A61 §5's resource-mediated hold-up CLAIM-tmp3t16f; and calls A61 §10's
split of adjudicative from attritional friction "related" to
CLAIM-tmph28l6. CASE-tmphljcr (A's constructions, A61 and A64, every number
rechecked and five of A's statements corrected by the filer; construction,
fictional, Proposed) bears on QUESTION-tmpcz1ui: the four- and seven-month
game, the exponential race and the threshold. Its correction of A64 §9's
vulnerability gap requires the baseline course to be among the opponent's
admissible strategies, and it points to CLAIM-tmpbqunx for "the
adversarial quantity itself".

**The measure.** CLAIM-tmpbqunx (A's, A76 VIII.4 and A81 §3; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; lines realizability and dependence-and-domination)
complements CLAIM-537, CLAIM-568 and CLAIM-tmpyo35b, bears on
QUESTION-tmpcz1ui and uses TERM-085: against a challenged party who can act
on the means of correction, correctability is robust to a declared class
of opposition, depends on who moves first, and splits permitted from merely
available opposition, failure against the first showing the institution
inadequate and against the second a wrong by the opponent as well. Its body
says it gives "the measure on which a siege succeeds and the split that
says whether the siege was a wrong", and that its third part "says where
the answer enters" to QUESTION-tmpcz1ui: "in what goes into Σ_permitted".

**Whether siege is wrong.** QUESTION-tmpcz1ui (A's, asked at the close of
A61, A64 and A68; Open) refines QUESTION-tmp9oq97: may a party under a
justified constraint set or raise the cost another must bear to make it
effective, and within which limits? Its body names A64's Candidate C as "a
normative proposal requiring independent justification", says A64 §10's
definition moves the question into a counterfactual course "which must
itself be defended", and lists CLAIM-tmpcpijj (in SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa) as what an answer denying the party such
authority "would rest on".

**Friction.** CLAIM-tmp11xvc (the owner's, U11, with A21's scope; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; lines will-organization and distributed-agency)
rests on CLAIM-523: procedural friction exposes, and may characterize, the
opportunities for intervention, so reducing it can remove them. Its body
lists CLAIM-tmph28l6, CLAIM-tmpohafo, CLAIM-tmp238zy, CLAIM-tmpgh3v2 and
CASE-tmpdn372 as developed from it. CLAIM-tmph28l6 (A's, A23 §2, at the
owner's U12; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-462 and
CLAIM-tmp11xvc and uses TERM-124: the cost of imposing a decision and the
cost of contesting it are separate, so an institution can lower the first
and gain power with no change in its formal authority. CLAIM-tmpgh3v2
(A's, A33 §4 and §8; supposed, interpretive, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-478
and CLAIM-423 and uses TERM-124: a slowly revised rule over a fast process
can dominate those it harms with no one acting, and, where the rule is open
to revision on demand, keep the fast process accountable instead. Its body
calls CLAIM-tmph28l6 "the same asymmetry stated as cost". CASE-tmpdn372
(A's proposed study, A33 §10 C; stipulated, proposed-study, Proposed) is a
variant of CASE-003, illustrates CLAIM-tmph28l6, CLAIM-tmpgh3v2 and
CLAIM-tmp11xvc, and bears on QUESTION-278: the same overseer, a slow and a
fast acting institution, then precommitted limits, automatic suspension and
pauses. Its body says it could support CLAIM-tmpgh3v2's second clause over
its first, and its summary calls it a test of CLAIM-464 and CLAIM-462. It
is not run.

**The baseline.** CLAIM-tmpyn6oi (A's, A102 §6 and A106 §9, at the owner's
U44; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-670 and CLAIM-464: the
alternatives against which a relationship's terms are assessed can be
products of that relationship, so an actor cannot justify its terms by the
narrowness of options it helped narrow without justification, and the
assessment must separate the existence of a dependence, the legitimacy of
its creation, the authority exercised through it and the responsibilities
of maintaining it. Its body says it rests on CLAIM-464 "for the parallel
point about assessment".

## Arm B

- **QUESTION-tmpcz1ui's four bearings** (CLAIM-tmpyo35b, CASE-tmphljcr,
  CLAIM-tmpbqunx, and CLAIM-tmpcpijj from SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa). The sentence tried: a party under a justified constraint
  may raise the cost of making it effective only by opposition the
  procedural rules permit, and its authority over the constrained conduct
  does not make it the one to say what is permitted. Route: a derivation
  from CLAIM-tmpbqunx's third part and CLAIM-tmpcpijj. Verdict: it fails.
  Both halves are already stated, in CLAIM-tmpbqunx's body ("in what goes
  into Σ_permitted") and CLAIM-tmpcpijj's (the answer "cannot come from the
  party's authority over the constrained conduct"; CLAIM-tmpbqunx "gives
  the measure"). What remains, which strategies are permitted, is a free
  parameter, and it is the question itself; CLAIM-tmpbqunx says A81 §3
  "doesn't settle" it. CLAIM-tmpyo35b and CASE-tmphljcr give only the
  possibility. The four bearings do not integrate into an answer, and the
  question stays open.
- **Preemption and attrition as two routes to one result.** Route: a
  derivation. Verdict: already stated, in CLAIM-tmpyo35b's body, from A64's
  close. Nothing filed.
- **The chain from fait accompli to settlement** (CLAIM-tmpazi8a,
  CLAIM-tmpl0sgt, CLAIM-tmp3t16f, CLAIM-tmpa0wdv). Route: a derivation.
  Verdict: each link is stated pairwise in the components' complements and
  bodies, as the account records. Nothing filed.
- **The hold-up's baseline is endogenous** (CLAIM-tmp3t16f with
  CLAIM-tmpyn6oi). Route: a derivation. Verdict: it fails as trivial. It
  instantiates CLAIM-tmpyn6oi with CLAIM-tmp3t16f's mechanism, and its
  content is A55 §8 in CLAIM-tmp3t16f's body and CLAIM-tmpa0wdv's third
  part. The missing cross-reference is listed above.
- **Speed alone separates action from correction** (CLAIM-tmph28l6,
  CLAIM-tmpgh3v2, CLAIM-tmp11xvc, CASE-tmpdn372). Route: a discriminating
  prediction. Verdict: already stated, as CASE-tmpdn372's prediction, and
  untested. Nothing filed.
