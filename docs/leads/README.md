**[Distributed agency](line/distributed-agency.md)** (0) — what an organized whole is, and how its operative direction relates to its members' — where the essay started.

**[Will-organization](line/will-organization.md)** (0) — what organizes practical efficacy in an agent, and how that organization becomes precarious. Normative judgment about organizations is a line of its own (ADR-089).

**[Normative judgment about organizations](line/organizational-normativity.md)** (0) — whether an organization's own dynamics ground norms of function and failure, and what judging an organization needs beyond them — fixed relata before a judgment is well-defined, viability that does not establish what ought to persist, histories a present state does not show, a failure norm set by a system's own upkeep, and a bounded duty of answerability for the wrong beyond harm, with the routes by which an organization becomes normatively less informed; split from will-organization (ADR-089). What wholes and parts owe each other, and what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe, is a line of its own (ADR-100).

**[Wholes, parts and dependence](line/dependence-and-domination.md)** (0) — what wholes and their parts owe and can claim of each other, and when a dependence becomes domination or exploitation — control over the terms on which a dependence is met, not the size of the dependence, decides which way it runs and whether a whole's need of a part is the part's hold; exploitation and domination are judged against a standard declared with the judgment; and those who control an arrangement others depend on owe accountability and justification that rise with how indispensable it has become and how durably their choices narrow what is later open. "Organization" here is TERM-074's sense 1, a relational arrangement, unless an entry says otherwise, and the bearers of obligations, agents or roles, are kept apart from the ground of the obligation; split from organizational-normativity (ADR-100).

**[Rightful power: restriction, answerability and rights](line/rightful-power.md)** (0) — what makes power over others' capabilities rightful and keeps it so — when a capability should be restricted and by whom, where legitimacy and adequacy come from, when answerability is owed and to whom, which rights no institution creates or extinguishes, how remedies can be foreclosed before review, and accountability for correction without blame; the argument filed from the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse", carried beside dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity, which its entries keep (the decision adding this line).
