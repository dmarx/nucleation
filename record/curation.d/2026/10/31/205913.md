The owner asked for Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy (2024), *Organization
Studies* 45(9), doi 10.1177/01708406241266310, to be read and filed. It was
not held: literature.d was searched by DOI, authors and title.

**Finding a copy.** The article is open access under CC BY-NC 4.0, from
Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar, so lawful copies exist. None could
be fetched by this client:
- **The publisher's PDF and full-text pages** at journals.sagepub.com:
  served a bot-check page, or 403 to WebFetch.
- **The R-libre (TÉLUQ) deposit** at r-libre.teluq.ca/3348/: every
  connection was reset, over HTTPS and HTTP.
- **CORE's mirror**, core.ac.uk/download/618447888.pdf: served the same bot
  check, and CORE's API withholds full text from public users.

A headless browser was not used to get past the bot checks. The
owner then supplied the publisher's PDF, which was read. Under [ADR-053](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-053.md) the
work would have been Unfetched; with the owner's copy it is read and
Active.

**Filed.**
- **[LIT-tmp3mudg](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp3mudg.md)**, Active.
- **[NOTE-tmpfw3f7](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpfw3f7.md)**, Read. The body was read in full, and the transcript was read
  from the page images.
- **Two Proposed THEORYs.** [THEORY-tmpcaccx](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpcaccx.md) is the paper's definition of agency
  without agents. [THEORY-tmp5ux44](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp5ux44.md) is its account of communication as transduction.

The reading's verdict: a vocabulary for redescribing agency in Simondon's
and Massumi's terms. It has no criterion by which an observation could
count against it, and its one illustration is a reanalysis. It bears on
[CLAIM-355](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-355.md), [CLAIM-418](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-418.md), [CLAIM-405](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-405.md), [CLAIM-430](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-430.md), [CLAIM-515](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-515.md) and [CLAIM-523](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), each
recorded in the NOTE. No claim was filed: the paper's thesis is the
record's [CLAIM-355](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) in a stronger and less discriminating form.

**Also asked.** Chen, Lau, Mendel, Wei and Murfet 2023 (arXiv 2310.06301)
is already held as [LIT-1894](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1894.md). It was read on 2026-10-31 in [NOTE-1462](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1462.md), with
its derivation appendices G–J skimmed for their statements. It was not
read again.

**Not registered.** The paper leans on works the record does not hold:
- Hernes 2014, *A Process Theory of Organization*, and his 2016 paper;
- Massumi 2002, 2015 and 2017;
- Simondon's *L'individuation psychique et collective*, 1989;
- Cooren 2018, the source of the reanalysed excerpt;
- Chia 1995, 1996, 2010 and 2017;
- Lapoujade 2021, *The Lesser Existences*.

They are named here in case the owner wants any of them. Simondon 2020,
the English translation, is [LIT-1648](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1648.md), Unavailable.
