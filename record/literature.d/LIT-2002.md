# LIT-2002: Human cooperation is strong among individuals but fragile between groups

Van Lange, P. A. M., Bergmann, P. K. (2026). Human cooperation is strong among individuals but fragile between groups. *Nature Reviews Psychology, 5, 546–559*. doi:10.1038/s44159-026-00580-z.

## Key takeaways

- Dyads cooperate more readily than groups, through reciprocity, empathy
  and a partner who cannot be excluded at no cost (p. 550).
- Mistrust between groups needs only categorization. In-group favouritism
  "need not imply ... any harmful behaviour towards outgroups" (p. 553).
- Conflicting interests, as perceived, drive escalation, and "the same
  processes that can establish ingroup cooperation can also fuel
  intergroup conflict" (p. 554).
- Cooperation between groups can end in their merger "into larger
  collectives" (p. 555).
