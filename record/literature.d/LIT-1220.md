  Edmondson (1999). Learning behavior mediated between psychological safety and observer-rated team performance, and safety between context support and leader coaching and learning.

# LIT-1220: Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams

Edmondson, A. (1999). Psychological Safety and Learning Behavior in Work Teams. *Administrative Science Quarterly* 44(2): 350–383. doi:10.2307/2666999.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmpd0qzq).

## Why this is here

A41 of the exchange cited it as "an important complementary result" for robust governance under internal compromise: the capacity to challenge has to be protected, or the audit that robustness depends on goes quiet. Bears on [CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md) (robustness to compromised constituents is a property of the processes that allocate and audit authority) and [CASE-108](../cases.d/CASE-108.md).
