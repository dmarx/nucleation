### Added

- A new scheme, DISTINCTION (record/distinctions.d), which binds two or more
  TERMs or CLAIMs (a term's senses, a term's neighbours, a split claim) by
  what separates them in theory and the observation that tells them apart.
  The first, [DISTINCTION-004](record/distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-004.md), holds goal-conduciveness valence apart
  from an outcome's pull ([ADR-153](record/decisions.d/ADR-153.md)).

### Changed

- A TERM characterizes one concept; the distinction is no longer part of its
  description, and its template's "What it is not" section is now
  "Distinctions".
