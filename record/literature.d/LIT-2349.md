# LIT-2349: Crossing the Line: Factors Associated With Escalating Pornography Use

Melissa S. de Roos and Laura Willkomm (2026). "Crossing the Line: Factors Associated With Escalating Pornography Use." *Journal of Interpersonal Violence 41(17-18): 6252-6277*. DOI-10.1177/08862605261462151.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1789](../notes.d/NOTE-1789.md)). An arousal-discounted, per-category
escalation index; a five-factor structure of escalation; evidence that
different clusters have different correlates; a finding that the
"Non-Traditional Niche" cluster, not the Extreme/Harmful one, is the one
associated with reported CSAM engagement.
