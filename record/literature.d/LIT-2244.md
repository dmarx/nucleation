# LIT-2244: Self-organizing Chimera States in Adaptive Networks

Felix Augustsson, Rok Cestnik, Matthias Wolfrum, Christian Bick, Serhiy Yanchuk and Erik Andreas Martens (2026). "Self-organizing Chimera States in Adaptive Networks." arXiv preprint. [ARXIV-2608.20517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20517).

## Key takeaways

- Chimeras self-organize from unstructured starts through co-evolving
  phases and weights, with no group structure imposed.
- Membership of the synchronized cluster persists through stationary,
  breathing and chaotic regimes of the other group, and changes only at
  crisis-like jumps.
- Which cluster sizes occur depends on initial conditions and sweep
  direction.
