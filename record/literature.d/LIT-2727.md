# LIT-2727: Catholic Legal Immigration Network, Inc. v. Rubio, No. 1:26-cv-00858 (S.D.N.Y. Aug. 21, 2026)

United States District Court for the Southern District of New York (2026). *Catholic Legal Immigration Network, Inc. v. Rubio*, No. 1:26-cv-00858 (JAV), Opinion and Order, ECF No. 83, 21 August 2026 (Vargas, J.).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-2054](../notes.d/NOTE-2054.md)).*

- "Consular officers were ordered to refuse immigrant visas to nationals of
  the 75 designated countries, regardless of whether the consular officer
  had, following an individualized assessment, determined that the
  applicant was not likely to become a public charge and that the applicant
  was otherwise eligible for a visa" (pp. 1–2).
- Held "contrary to law and ... in excess of Secretary Rubio's statutory
  authority" (p. 2), on nationality discrimination (p. 34), § 1201(g) and
  § 1104(a); not on the public-charge provision (p. 32); for the Government
  on notice-and-comment (p. 47).
- "whatever determinations they make as to eligibility ... are ultimately
  irrelevant. The outcome is predetermined" (p. 42).
- The vacatur "does not extend to denial of immigrant visas on any ground
  other than the Policy" (p. 57).
