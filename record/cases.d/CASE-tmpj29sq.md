# CASE-tmpj29sq: A manipulated will: one outside input carries an agent's precedence of an egoistic regime over a considerate one across the threshold of its own maintaining cycle, landing on exactly the state its own revision would have reached, the input's channel is then removed, and from then on the agent's own processes keep, endorse and stand ready to revise the new precedence

## The agent

Plum has two action-generating regimes that conflict on a class of
occasions: a considerate one, R_c, and a strongly egoistic one, R_e, the
reasoning process Pereboom's Case 1 installs ([THEORY-328](../theory.d/THEORY-328.md)). Which of them
becomes effective when they conflict is set by a precedence relation p,
one of [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s maintained relations, with the rest of Plum's
will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)): accessibility, persistence, jurisdiction,
suspension and transformation.

Three things hold of p, by stipulation:

- **It is maintained.** Each time a regime prevails, its exercise
  strengthens its precedence, so p has two stable states, p_c
  (considerate first) and p_e (egoistic first), with a threshold θ between
  them. This is [CASE-191](CASE-191.md)'s structure (a self-maintaining cycle with a
  separatrix) applied to a precedence relation rather than a capacity.
  Plum's maintenance provenance ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md), sense 2) is his own at both.
- **It is revisable by Plum.** His transformation relation is a revision
  process that, when a discrepancy among his maintained demands persists
  that the present precedence cannot meet ([CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s frustration), pushes
  p toward the regime that would meet it, and past θ if the discrepancy is
  large and long enough ([CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s occasion for self-formation).
- **It is endorsed.** Once past θ, the revision process, consulted on
  later occasions, finds no discrepancy the new precedence leaves unmet,
  so it holds p where it is: p_e is a fixed point of Plum's own revision.
  He is reasons-responsive and his higher-order attitudes conform to the
  regime that acts, Pereboom's stipulations for Cases 1 and 2 ([THEORY-328](../theory.d/THEORY-328.md),
  [THEORY-1118](../theory.d/THEORY-1118.md)).

The state at any time, x, includes every slow variable of the agent: the
two regimes' contents, all the relations, the state of the revision
process, and Plum's memory, including his memory of how he came to rank
as he does.

## The case

At t0 Plum is at p_c. In the interval from t0 to t1, a team with a
channel into p delivers one input that carries p across θ. They choose the
input so that at t1 Plum's whole state is exactly x_U(t1), the state his
twin reaches by his own revision in the same interval ([CASE-tmp06m60](CASE-tmp06m60.md)),
memory of the episode included: Plum at t1 remembers having worked the
question through. At t1 the channel is removed; nothing of it remains in
or around Plum. From t1 on he receives the same inputs as his twin, his
own cycle maintains p_e, his own revision endorses it, and on the next
occasion of conflict R_e prevails.

The case requires that x_U(t1) be in the team's reachable set from Plum's
state at t0, which is the team's power in Pereboom's cases. The twin
requires the converse, that the state the team installs be in Plum's own
reachable set: what his channels and ordinary inputs could bring about
from the same start. Here both hold, since the twin is defined as the agent
who got there his own way.

## What is held fixed between the pair

From t1 on, Plum and his twin are matched in every respect the
will-organization account names, and in each of the further ones
[QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) and its notes have raised:

- the regimes and their contents;
- every efficacy relation (accessibility, precedence, persistence,
  jurisdiction, suspension) and the transformation relation, as states
  and as dispositions;
- maintenance provenance: both are kept by their own cycle, so every
  knockout of maintenance or of outside supply after t1 gives the same
  result ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md), [CASE-191](CASE-191.md));
- the frustration profile among maintained demands and motivational
  integration ([CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md));
- the response to every intervention made after t1, including a
  constituent's dissent ([CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s test);
- legibility, efficacy, control and revisability under matched
  interference ([CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)'s profile);
- reasons-responsiveness and hierarchical endorsement (Pereboom's
  conditions);
- outside channels: none after t1 in either;
- all inputs after t1.

They differ in one fact: which channel carried p across θ between t0 and
t1. That is formation provenance ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md), sense 1).

## Variants

Each drops one condition of the case. The first is the one the
alternatives disagree on; the others are failures of the match, not
features that separate a matched pair.

1. **The channel is kept.** The team keeps "the ability to manipulate
   Professor Plum's neural states at any time", as Pereboom's Case 1 does
   in its 2024 wording ([NOTE-856](../notes.d/NOTE-856.md)), and does not use it again. Plum and his
   twin now differ in what an outside channel reaches of Plum's governing
   relations. Whether that is a feature of his will-organization is
   [CLAIM-tmp9jo1z](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9jo1z.md) (yes) against [CLAIM-tmpner6t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpner6t.md) (no).
2. **The channel upholds instead of installing.** The team supplies p's
   maintenance from outside and Plum's own cycle does not. A knockout of
   the outside supply after t1 separates him from his twin ([CASE-191](CASE-191.md)'s
   "upheld" agent). Present structure shows this manipulation.
3. **The installed precedence is not endorsed by Plum's own revision.**
   p_e leaves a standing discrepancy that his revision process keeps
   pushing against; the precedence holds only while the team's input or
   some other pressure outweighs it. Present structure shows the conflict
   ([THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md)'s introjected, not integrated, regulation). It does not show
   manipulation: an ordinarily controlling upbringing, Pereboom's Case 3,
   leaves the same.
4. **The installed state is out of Plum's own reach.** The team installs
   a relation that no input Plum's channels or ordinary life could supply
   would produce from his start: say, a jurisdiction of R_e over a class
   of occasions his revision process has no channel to. Then no twin
   exists. Present structure and the dynamics show that his own processes
   could not have produced it; that reads as installation only against a
   fact about where agents of his kind start, which is a fact about
   histories.
5. **Original design.** t0 is the start of Plum's life and the team
   designs his initial state (Pereboom's Case 2, Mele's Ernie;
   [THEORY-328](../theory.d/THEORY-328.md)). His twin is Pereboom's Case 4 Plum, whose same initial
   state came about without a designer. The pair is matched from the
   start, and no bypassed capacity exists to record.
6. **The match is only asymptotic.** The team carries p across θ and lets
   Plum's cycle bring him to p_e, without landing on x_U(t1). The two
   trajectories converge on the same attractor but differ at every finite
   time by a transient that decays. In an exact deterministic model that
   transient is a present trace of formation (Juarrero's history carried
   on the back, as [CASE-191](CASE-191.md) notes), and any noise larger than it erases it.
   The case proper removes it by stipulating the exact landing.

## What it can show

Given its standing, it shows nothing about how often, or whether, real
agents are manipulated this way. It shows what the will-organization
account can register. What is stipulated is one state at one time,
x_U(t1), and that the two agents are of one kind; the match in the
account's extra content, its maintenance, its capacity to transform and
its response to intervention, then follows from the agents' own dynamics
rather than being stipulated with it. That is the difference from the
present-organization indistinguishability arguments [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)'s note of
2026-10-21 found true by definition. The variants say which manipulations
the account does see. [CLAIM-tmpz9dqf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpz9dqf.md) argues from it.
