## The account

Zhu and colleagues (2025) find, across six collaborative and competitive
scenarios, that LLM multi-agent task success depends mainly on the
underlying model rather than on coordination, that a graph-mesh topology does
best and group-discussion planning worst in their research scenario, and that
adding agents beyond a few lowers milestone achievement.
