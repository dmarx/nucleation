Lynch, T. R., Hempel, R. J., Whalley, B., Byford, S., Chamba, R., Clarke, P., Clarke, S., Kingdon, D. G., O'Mahen, H., Remington, B., Rushbrook, S. C., Shearer, J., Stanton, M., Swales, M., Watkins, A. & Russell, I. T. (2020). Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1358](../notes.d/NOTE-1358.md)).

## Why this is here

A42 of the exchange cited the RefraMED trial as the main controlled evidence for RO DBT. Bears on [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md): the trial's gain in flexibility without a lasting gain on the primary outcome is a caution on reading overcontrol as the whole of the problem.
