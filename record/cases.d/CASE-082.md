# CASE-082: The sufficient posterior without its history: a Bayesian agent that keeps only its current posterior updates as well as one that keeps everything, and cannot say how it got there

## The case

Two agents process the same evidence under a correctly specified model,
observations conditionally independent given Θ. Agent A keeps the whole
sequence and the reasons for each revision. Agent B keeps p(Θ | E₁:t). On
E_{t+1} both reach p(Θ | E₁:t+1).

## What it can show

That keeping justificatory history is not necessary for competent
updating within a fixed model. A6: "Without it, we risk quietly
identifying epistemic competence with the particular conception of
accountability implemented by nucleation."

## What it cannot show

A6: "That justification history is unnecessary for other decisions,
including auditing scientific misconduct, identifying procedural errors,
revising the model class, or explaining how a conclusion was reached."
A10 §2C placed audit: the target changes from the world to the inquiry,
and a posterior over (Θ, J_t) with J_t the relevant features of its
history would serve audit too.
