# ARG-tmpr43gg: The DeGroot–Friedkin result, in which every issue ends in consensus while members not every other member listens to, even indirectly, end with no power, shows that a coherent record can coexist with excluded members, not that a maintained regime admitting some contests produces the coherence

## The inference

1. In the DeGroot–Friedkin model every issue ends in consensus, so the
   record of outcomes is always coherent ([THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md)). With a reducible
   network, members who are not globally reachable end with zero power
   ([THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md), Section 5).
2. So a coherent record and the exclusion of some members' evaluations
   can hold together: coherence is not integration. That is [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)'s
   weaker half.
3. [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)'s stronger half is that a maintained legibility regime,
   selecting which contests are admitted, can produce the coherence, and
   that accommodation and suppression then look the same. The model has no
   such regime: its network is fixed and exogenous, and "nobody decides who
   is heard" ([THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md), "What this does not say").

A57 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c):
"another shows that a coherent institutional decision record can be
produced by selectively excluding dissent". In the record [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) is role
`counter`, warrant `interpretive`, and its "What would settle it" names a
three-perspective agenda model not yet built.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes. It is a proved special case of a model,
  read with proofs skimmed ([THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md), Proposed).
- **Does it separate the very things the conclusion says can come apart?**
  In part. It separates coherence from inclusion. It does not separate
  accommodation from exclusion by a regime that selects contests, which is
  [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)'s distinctive content. [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) says so itself: both readings
  it cites "exclude by uptake through a fixed network, with no maintained
  regime deciding who is heard; that regime is what this claim adds".

**Verdict.** It corroborates the weaker half, as [THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md)'s
`corroborates` already records. A57's "shows" goes beyond it. The
reader's construction of [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)'s own agenda model, in which a chair
admits only some pairwise contests, is filed as [CASE-tmp3l0hh](../cases.d/CASE-tmp3l0hh.md) and argued
in [ARG-tmpmk7of](ARG-tmpmk7of.md). It gives the regime the stronger half needs, a chair
selecting contests, where this reading has none. Neither shows that such
coherence persists or what real interventions do.
