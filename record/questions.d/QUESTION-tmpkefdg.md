# QUESTION-tmpkefdg: What makes the terms on which an organization's sustaining activities are organized unjust, so that an organization reproducing them exploits the participants it depends on and does not only subordinate them?

## Why it is a question

A23 §4 of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597), after separating contribution, dependence,
effective authority and moral standing, proposed "A new concept":

> We could provisionally define *constitutive exploitation* as an
> arrangement in which the activities through which participants sustain
> an organization are organized under unjust terms that systematically
> deprive those participants of appropriate benefits, protections, or
> influence.

and conceded that it "would require an independent account of what makes
those terms unjust". Without that account the term names [QUESTION-461](QUESTION-461.md)'s
second branch, an organization that reproduces "the arrangements that keep
their indispensable contributions from becoming effective claims on it",
and adds a verdict it cannot yet reach.

The record holds several candidates for the missing standard, and none is
yet stated as one:

- an arrangement that exploits through the access it grants, or produces
  the dependence it exploits ([CLAIM-669](../claims.d/CLAIM-669.md), [CLAIM-670](../claims.d/CLAIM-670.md));
- a dependence organized as coercive leverage rather than mutual
  opportunity ([CLAIM-428](../claims.d/CLAIM-428.md));
- the participants' moral standing ([TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md)) together with a duty of
  answerability whose breach keeps a justified standard from constraining
  the organization ([CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md));
- domination by the arrangement's controllers ([CLAIM-tmpkjv7u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkjv7u.md)), which is a
  threshold for domination, not for unjust terms.

## What would answer it

- **A standard and a separating case.** A stated standard of unjust terms,
  with two organizations that subordinate indispensable contributors
  alike, one on terms it counts unjust and one not, so the standard does
  work that subordination does not.
- **Or a reduction.** An argument that constitutive exploitation is one of
  the held entries above under a new name, so the term adds nothing.
