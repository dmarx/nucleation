# ARG-006: If the transport intertwines each framing operation exactly, it intertwines their commutator, so a translation that preserves the framing operations preserves their noncommutativity

## The inference

A34 §4: "If these relations hold exactly for linear operators, then
Φ[A_o,B_o] = [A_t,B_t]Φ. This follows algebraically ... **If a translation
preserves the relevant framing operations, it also preserves their algebraic
relationships—including their noncommutativity.**" A40 §6.4 asked for the
approximate version, "bounds on commutator mismatch from the errors of
individual intertwining relations and the norms of the operators", which the
manuscript §9 supplies as an identity.

## Critical questions

- **Do the premises hold?** Exact linear intertwining is an idealization; the
  manuscript's identity covers the approximate case.
  And they cannot be checked against data. CLAIM-253: "Its first critical
  question, whether the premises hold, cannot be answered from data,
  because the premises are about operators the data do not identify." The
  defects are defined on interpretive states, and two models equal on
  every observation can carry defects of 0 and of the largest size. The
  algebra still holds; what fails is the step from data to the premises.
- **Does the formal result apply?** Only to Φ's image. A40 §6.4: "unless Φ is
  sufficiently informative, preserving its image of a commutator does not
  establish preservation of the entire commutator structure" ([CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md)).

## Note of 2026-10-27: the edge says what the claim uses

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md), [ADR-058](../decisions.d/ADR-058.md)) re-pointed `rests_on` from [CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md) to [CLAIM-373](../claims.d/CLAIM-373.md) (Active, demonstrated), whose identity Φ[A,B] − [A′,B′]Φ = A′E_B + E_A B − B′E_A − E_B A gives this inference's exact case with zero defects, as [CLAIM-373](../claims.d/CLAIM-373.md)'s history says. The inference takes the intertwining as a hypothesis. It does not use [CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md)'s thesis that fidelity requires it.
