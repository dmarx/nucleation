# CLAIM-tmpartp6: Communicative meaning is constituted through systems of distinctions and relations, and translation transports these systems, selectively preserving or transforming their differential, probabilistic and interactional structure

## The claim

A102: "**structuralism may provide the philosophical foundation that our
mathematical framework has been approaching from the opposite direction**." Its
thesis: "Communicative meaning is constituted through systems of distinctions
and relations. Translation transports these systems across representations and
contexts, selectively preserving or transforming their differential,
probabilistic, and interactional structure." With Saussure: "affectionate
teasing ... is partly constituted by its contrasts with admonition, ridicule,
sympathy", and "Saussure gives us differential value. Sheaf theory gives us a
language for the locality and compatibility of those differential
relationships" ([CLAIM-tmpnt2nd](CLAIM-tmpnt2nd.md)). Its example of a structural change: "a translation
could preserve the distinction between teasing and sincere praise while
collapsing the distinction between teasing and condemnation. That would be a
meaningful structural change even if the translated utterance remained
semantically close to the original."

A102 also brought in the manuscript's third refrain, "You should stop wasting
your money and start saving" ([CASE-tmpcc78z](../cases.d/CASE-tmpcc78z.md)), as an inversion "Shared implication →
External evaluation", likened to a myth transformation.

## Why it was replaced

It came with a contrast between structuralist invariance and transport
([CLAIM-tmp4fgys](CLAIM-tmp4fgys.md)), which the owner objected to at U37 ([CLAIM-tmpbr0mt](CLAIM-tmpbr0mt.md)). A105 restated the
thesis in terms of invariance and equivariance ([CLAIM-tmpvim0a](CLAIM-tmpvim0a.md)).
