# TERM-tmpy2t4f: fidelity, as preservation of selected observables and structural symmetries

## Definition

A105: "Fidelity ≈ preservation of selected observables and structural
symmetries. The approximation symbol is important: this is a proposed
operational criterion, relative to a selected class of observables, not a
universal identity." It "formalizes the distinction between an exactly
preserved communicative observable and one that drifts gradually through
repeated reconstruction" ([CLAIM-tmpuwwjx](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpuwwjx.md)).

## What it is not

Not a term of [TERM-tmp9qq3b](TERM-tmp9qq3b.md). The manuscript §4 separates exact preservation,
equivariance, approximate preservation and reorganization ([CLAIM-tmpwnnx4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwnnx4.md)), and §6 gives
the distortion functional. The two notions sit side by side and are never
related: a transport can score well on L and break a symmetry, or the reverse.
