# LIT-2258: Dynamical Systems and Sheaves

Patrick Schultz, David I. Spivak and Christina Vasilakopoulou (2019). "Dynamical Systems and Sheaves." *Applied Categorical Structures* 28(1): 1–57. [ARXIV-1609.08086](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08086) DOI-10.1007/s10485-019-09565-x.

## Key takeaways

Skimmed on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-tmp1hzsa](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp1hzsa.md)). Machines as spans of interval sheaves form a wiring-diagram algebra; totality, determinism and inertia survive every composition; history-dependent contracts are presheaves, not sheaves.
