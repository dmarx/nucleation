# SYNTHESIS-107: From equivalence to recursive capability, as a chain of separable achievements: differences cease to matter to a family of operations, the resulting classes become operative units, persistent organization replaces repeated work, operating, learning and inquiry costs change, possibly in different directions, and the new capabilities shape later experience and organization; each step is stated by its own entry, each can fail without the next, and the chain as a whole is a proposed sequence the record does not assert

## The parts

- [SYNTHESIS-108](SYNTHESIS-108.md): how an equivalence becomes a unit
  (formal-apparatus).
- [SYNTHESIS-106](SYNTHESIS-106.md): stored work and the cost structure of possibility
  (realizability).
- [SYNTHESIS-105](SYNTHESIS-105.md): what learning does to what can still be learned
  (learned-structure).

## The chain, step by step

ChatGPT's five steps, each with the entry that states it, the entry that
says it can hold without the next, and the test the record has for it.

1. **Selective identification → operative reification.** Stated by
   [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md) (the owner's, U10): collapsing differences that do not
   matter is how classes come to be reified. *Can fail:* a partition
   need not be operative; [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md) itself makes the claim
   substantive only where the classes are closed under the system's
   operations and reused, and [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md) says when a unit is worth
   naming. *Test:* [CASE-296](../cases.d/CASE-296.md)'s closure test and its intervention
   arm; in a social system, the owner's Reddit case ([CASE-297](../cases.d/CASE-297.md),
   proposed), where a joke or rivalry becomes a community people join and
   govern.
2. **Operative unit → historical amortization.** Stated by
   [CLAIM-761](../claims.d/CLAIM-761.md) (the owner-endorsed thesis): a stabilized constraint
   can be stored work, replacing later work with cheaper operations.
   *Can fail:* an operative distinction need not save work, [CLAIM-443](../claims.d/CLAIM-443.md)
   (persistence is not amortization), and a slow variable can save none,
   [CLAIM-755](../claims.d/CLAIM-755.md).
3. **Amortization → capability transformation.** Stated by
   [CLAIM-761](../claims.d/CLAIM-761.md): under a per-task budget, stored work moves tasks
   across the budget line. That costs can move in different directions
   for operating, learning and inquiry is [CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md). *Can fail:*
   saved work need not expand a valuable capability: [CLAIM-760](../claims.d/CLAIM-760.md) (it
   may be work transferred), and [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) (the owner's: an affordable
   option is not yet a freedom). *Test:* [CASE-294](../cases.d/CASE-294.md)'s primary
   estimand, acquisition cost by checkpoint, for the learning side.
4. **Capability → recursive feedback.** Stated by [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) (the owner's:
   the questions an agent can ask are a product of what it has realized)
   and [CLAIM-756](../claims.d/CLAIM-756.md) (an acquisition can lower the cost of others).
   *Can fail:* a new capability need not support further organization:
   [CLAIM-758](../claims.d/CLAIM-758.md) (the owner's overconsolidation, where present
   competence rises as later acquisition gets costlier) and [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)
   (below a threshold the recursion traps instead of accumulating).
   *Test:* [CASE-297](../cases.d/CASE-297.md)'s developmental productivity, whether a
   community makes its descendants cheaper to form (proposed).

## Not stated on the record

- **The chain as a mechanism.** That systems which emerge commonly go
  through all five steps, or that each step tends to produce the next,
  is stated by no entry. ChatGPT offered it as a "proposed causal
  sequence, not a universal law". Filed as a claim it would need one of
  [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s routes; as it stands it is a research map.
- **That identification is where the chain starts.** [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md)'s own
  completion makes differentiation between basins a second route to
  classes ([CLAIM-408](../claims.d/CLAIM-408.md)); the chain begins at identification by choice.
- **That any step has been shown in one system end to end.** No case on
  record runs through more than two steps.

## What it does not say

That emergence progresses through the steps automatically: each
separation above is a held entry saying it does not.
