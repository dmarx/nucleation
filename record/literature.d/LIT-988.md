# LIT-988: Scientific discovery in a model-centric framework: Reproducibility, innovation, and epistemic diversity

Filed for [QUESTION-047](../questions.d/QUESTION-047.md): it models a scientific community in which epistemic diversity, not the
reproducibility of single results, sets whether the community finds the truth and stays on it. That
is a community-level mechanism for what the group comes to believe.
