**Checks run before anything was applied.**

- **CLAIM-450's garbling inequality.** The script was re-run.
  - The Fisher information of the source minus the target's has no
    eigenvalue below −10⁻¹⁵ over 300 θ-free garblings.
  - Two controls behave: a θ-dependent kernel breaks the order in 250 of
    300, and the identity kernel gives equality.
  - CASE-152's matrix has rank 1 off the origin and rank 0 at it.
- **CLAIM-443 and CLAIM-358's reuse inequality.**
  - N(c_d − c_π) > F + C_maintenance + C_error is exactly the direct cost
    comparison when the upkeep terms are totals over the N encounters.
  - Read as fixed amounts, it gives the wrong verdict in 89,690 of 200,000
    draws.
  - It never pays when c_π = c_d.
  - So the inequality is accounting, and whether it is ever met is
    CLAIM-358's empirical content.
