# QUESTION-tmpc4j7p: What separates an interpretive organization that constitutes an experiential perspective from one that only processes information, or only represents a perspective, when the separation must hold whether or not the interpretations are grounded, and must not be drawn from the verdict it gives on groups and machines?

## The question

[CLAIM-tmpdga80](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdga80.md) identifies experience with an organized interpretive process,
and [CLAIM-tmp1lvwf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1lvwf.md) shows that interpretation alone does not qualify. The
question asks for the condition that does. It sets three contrasts:

1. **Processing against perspective.** The level step in A7's table, from
   coordinated interpretation to "a candidate organizational perspective".
2. **Perspective against a representation of one.** A7: "A perspectival
   organization is not necessarily the same thing as a representation of a
   perspectival organization." A system may have the first without the
   second, or represent itself as a unified experiencer without being one.
   [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) puts the illusion, where there is one, in the second.
3. **Constituting against simulating or reporting.** A6: the same
   interpretations may be realized by a system that only "simulate[s] or
   report[s]" them. A7's test of an answer: say "what a sophisticated
   nonexperiential interpreter would lack".

The separation must not depend on grounding ([CLAIM-tmpjw8ev](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjw8ev.md)): a dream
or an envatted brain is a case it must cover.

## Why it is a question

[QUESTION-046](QUESTION-046.md) asks for a criterion that tells a subject from an agent,
applied one way to modular minds and to groups. This question is that
criterion as the constitutive thesis would have to state it, in terms of
interpretation and perspective. It inherits [QUESTION-046](QUESTION-046.md)'s standard. A7:
specify the properties independently, "and then examine what
classifications follow, including uncomfortable ones", since otherwise
"we could keep adding new criteria whenever an intuitively nonconscious
system qualifies".

The exchange's candidates, none yet a criterion:

- an operative reference frame "of relevance, temporal continuity, and
  possible change" (A7 §8), which A7 conceded a corporation could have;
- an organization of possibilities as well as actual states, the
  counterfactual structure of a perspective (A7 §8);
- endogenous valuation (A7 §12), kept apart from felt distress as
  [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) keeps frustration apart from it;
- higher-order or recursive self-interpretation (A3, A4);
- the coherent world model of an embodied agent, as in IWMT ([LIT-tmp5k0u3](../literature.d/LIT-tmp5k0u3.md)),
  which builds in the embodiment the owner's thesis does not assume.

Its evidential side is [QUESTION-048](QUESTION-048.md)'s. A5: complete answers about
integration, self-attribution and grounding "would not, by themselves,
establish phenomenal consciousness unless we accept an additional
constitutive premise". Language models with a functional workspace
([LIT-tmp9k9lx](../literature.d/LIT-tmp9k9lx.md)) make the overgeneration side concrete. A6: "The significant
result is not that language models experience anything."

## What would answer it

A property of a realized, temporally extended organization, A7's
\(\mathsf P(\mathcal O_{[t,t+\tau]})\), stated without reference to
experience or report. It must fall on the right side of blindsight and
ordinary perception, give a verdict on a firm and on a language model
that follows from the property and not from intuition, and survive a
change in grounding with the internal dynamics held fixed. A measure that
correlates with reports would not answer it. A7: "We would want an
account connecting its structural features to the organization of
experience and showing why proposed counterexamples fail."
