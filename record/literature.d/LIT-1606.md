# LIT-1606: Noem v. Abrego Garcia

Supreme Court of the United States (2025). *Noem v. Abrego Garcia*, No. 24A949, 604 U.S. ___ (10 April 2025), on application to vacate injunction.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1376](../notes.d/NOTE-1376.md)).

## Why this is here

The legal anchor of [CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md): an acknowledged unlawful removal into another state's custody, after which the question became which government could, or had to, act to undo it. Only the first page was read when filing.
