# CLAIM-116: Regulation, even higher-order regulation, does not establish a will

## The claim

"The existence of regulation does not establish the existence of a will"
(Section 1). With [CLAIM-080](CLAIM-080.md), this is the paper's answer to
[CLAIM-010](CLAIM-010.md).

## Why it is filed

As a disclaimed claim it is the record saying what it does *not* hold, with an
address. The exchange wrote this sentence, or one like it, dozens of times;
filed once, a new draft can cite it instead of re-deriving it.
