# THEORY-1682: Boem, Greslehner, Konsman and Chiu (2024) argue that embodied accounts of cognition should include the "gut complex", the enteric nervous, endocrine, immune and microbial systems with gut tissue acting together at the interface with the gut lumen, as constitutive of the whole subject's perception and cognition, and that on a liberal criterion of minimal cognition the complex also has proto-cognition of its own.

## The account

Boem, Greslehner, Konsman and Chiu (2024) argue that embodied accounts of cognition should include the "gut complex", the enteric nervous, endocrine, immune and microbial systems with gut tissue acting together at the interface with the gut lumen, as constitutive of the whole subject's perception and cognition, and that on a liberal criterion of minimal cognition the complex also has proto-cognition of its own.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1765](../notes.d/NOTE-1765.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
