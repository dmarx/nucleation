# THEORY-tmpbgf6v: Zuboff argues that surveillance capitalism is an institutional order that commodifies behaviour, concentrating knowledge about people in a few firms, so that epistemic inequality and a power to modify behaviour arise that compete with democratic governance and leave people practically without exit

## The account

Zuboff argues that surveillance capitalism concentrates knowledge about people ("Who decides who knows?"), that "nearly all roads to economic, political, and social participation lead through its institutional terrain", and that the result is "no exit".
