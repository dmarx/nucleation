# NOTE-tmp9s41z: The Document Foundation — OpenOffice.org Community announces The Document Foundation

## Contribution

The founders' announcement of The Document Foundation and LibreOffice, with the endorsements of named outside organizations printed in it, dated by the document itself.

## Key results

- **The claim to continue.** "From now on, the OpenOffice.org community will be known as "The Document Foundation"." Oracle "has been invited to become a member of the new Foundation, and donate the brand the community has grown during the past ten years. Pending this decision, the brand "LibreOffice" has been chosen for the software going forward."
- **Who moved.** "leading independent members of the OpenOffice.org community, including several project leads and key members of the Community Council".
- **Endorsements in the announcement.** Canonical's founder: "the Ubuntu Project will be pleased to ship LibreOffice from The Document Foundation in future releases of Ubuntu"; Google ("proud to be a supporter"), Novell ("Viva la LibreOffice"), Red Hat ("proud to join this effort"), the FSF's president, the OSI, the GNOME Foundation and NeoOffice.

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. It is a statement by an interested party: what it
says of its own reasons is its stated reason, not its motive.

## Concepts

The continuation of a project apart from its name and steward.

## Bearing on the record

The study codes the endorsements as prearranged: verdicts by organizations outside the founding group, but printed in the founders' own announcement on its day, so arranged with them. They are the earliest outside verdicts in this case and are not independent of the move.

## Limitations

One document, by one party, on one day. It records a stated position, and
says nothing of how many people shared it.
