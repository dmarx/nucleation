# CLAIM-tmpy4mba: A self considered as a sloppy mess of connected perspectives (colloquial reading)

## The claim

The owner, U6: "therefore, so is our ability to form distinctions relevant
to distinguishing (perhaps 'separating' is Nucleation's sense?)
perspectives. Consider a 'self' as a literal sloppy mess of connected
perspectives."

Read colloquially: a self is many perspectives, connected, overlapping and
interfering, with no clean boundaries and no executive above them; and the
lines one draws between them are drawn by the tests one can afford, so they
are as budget-relative as the perspectives' addresses (TERM-tmpwc4la).

## What the record holds of it

- **The inference.** "Separating" is the record's word, in TERM-138's
  sense of classes: an observer or readout under its budget telling two
  states or populations apart. That this is budget-relative and grows with
  nested probe families is CLAIM-574 and SYNTHESIS-109.
- **Perspectives without an executive.** CLAIM-418 (several perspectives in
  one agent, one across agents), CLAIM-355 (binding real while the single
  owner is attributed), CLAIM-454 (a recovered mode is at most a
  constituent of a perspective), QUESTION-276 (identifying overlapping
  perspectives without fixing their number).
- **A self as an organization of processes**, not a further perspective
  over them: CLAIM-284, CLAIM-417.

What the proposal adds is to take the mess as the self rather than as an
obstacle to finding it: no privileged decomposition need exist. A13 §6 and
A15 developed it (CLAIM-tmp7pe33, CLAIM-tmptxsin).
