# LEAD-tmpicg4k: Run a multi-agent test of whether a reflexively governed organization constitutes a maintained collective concern that survives removal of its stimulus, a change of membership and conflict with local objectives, against a fixed global objective, local optimization and negotiated commitments

## What to do

A17 §12's four conditions:

| Condition | Structure |
|---|---|
| Fixed collective objective | A global reward is specified in advance. |
| Local optimization | Agents pursue individual rewards without constitutional mechanisms. |
| Negotiated organization | Agents establish commitments and shared arrangements. |
| Reflexively governed organization | Agents can additionally reconsider the criteria and relationships governing those arrangements. |

"The experimental question would be whether the latter conditions can
generate maintained collective concerns that are not simply externally
specified global objectives." The tests, after "an apparently novel
collective purpose emerges": "remove the original stimulus that prompted
it. Change the composition of the group. Introduce conflicts between the
emergent purpose and the local objectives. Test whether the collective
recognizes the same concern in circumstances it has not previously
encountered."

## What it needs before it can run

- **A measure of a collective concern** that is not the reward of the
  fixed-objective arm: A17 asks to "distinguish genuinely new evaluative
  organization from merely learning an effective intermediate strategy
  for maximizing a fixed reward". One candidate is CLAIM-tmpk8knm's test,
  a distinction that becomes consequential to the collective's choices
  across circumstances and was not before.
- **The platform**: LEAD-250's contract-governed agent, whose arms run
  from fixed priority to a reflexive contract ecology, gives the third
  and fourth conditions.

## What it would move

- **CLAIM-tmpvuiy4**: a concern that survives all four tests in the
  reflexive arm and in no other is evidence that purposes can be
  constituted, not aggregated; one that appears equally under a fixed
  objective is an instrumental subgoal.
- **CLAIM-tmp0o2mp**: whether operative, articulated and constituted
  concern come apart in the runs.
