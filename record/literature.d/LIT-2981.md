It bears on the exchange's cost of precision in driven systems. Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-tmpri2np](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpri2np.md)) and filed as [THEORY-tmp9t69m](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp9t69m.md).
