# LIT-1235: What can ecosystems learn? Expanding evolutionary ecology with learning theory

Power, D. A., Watson, R. A., Szathmáry, E., Mills, R., Powers, S. T., Doncaster, C. P. & Czapp, B. (2015). What can ecosystems learn? Expanding evolutionary ecology with learning theory. *Biology Direct* 10: 69. doi:10.1186/s13062-015-0094-1. arXiv:1506.06374. Erratum: doi:10.1186/s13062-016-0132-7.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-tmpc6gy4](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpc6gy4.md)).

## Why this is here

The synthesis the owner relayed on 2026-10-18, connecting the record's distributed-agency line to statistical feature learning and ecological coexistence theory, recommended it for reading. It is the one work in the batch that argues from the learning side: an ecological community without a selected-for whole can still learn, which is the organization-without-a-coded-governor that [QUESTION-083](../questions.d/QUESTION-083.md) asks about.
