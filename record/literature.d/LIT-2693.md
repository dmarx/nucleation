# LIT-2693: Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition

Umpleby, S. A. (2016). Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition. *Journal of Systemics, Cybernetics and Informatics* 14(5): 65–67. https://www.iiisci.org/Journal/PDV/sci/pdfs/IP020LL16.pdf.

## Key takeaways

Two ethical systems from one formal model; first (West): the end does not justify the means, compromise is saintly; second (USSR): the end justifies the means, confrontation is saintly. Anecdotal, uncritical.

## Standing in the record

Stands in thinly for [LIT-2697](LIT-2697.md) and [LIT-2663](LIT-2663.md): it restates the existence of the two systems, their formal origin in a combination rule and their cultural association, and nothing of the model. [NOTE-2017](../notes.d/NOTE-2017.md), [THEORY-1934](../theory.d/THEORY-1934.md).
