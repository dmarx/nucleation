# LIT-757: Groupware and social dynamics: eight challenges for developers

Jonathan Grudin (1994), *Communications of the ACM* 37(1):92–105 — DOI-10.1145/175222.175230

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the opening of the article only: computer
support had focused on organizations and individuals, and groups are
different. Many expensive failures of software meant to support groups are
not due to technical problems. They come from not understanding the demands
this class of software makes on developers and users. The article outlines
the origins of groupware, describes eight problem areas, and examines
groupware successes. The eight challenges themselves were not seen here, and
they extend [LIT-759](LIT-759.md)'s argument.
