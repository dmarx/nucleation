# LIT-tmp1p2zd: Minding the gut: extending embodied cognition and perception to the gut complex

Federico Boem, Gregor P. Greslehner, Jan Pieter Konsman and Lynn Chiu (2024). "Minding the gut: extending embodied cognition and perception to the gut complex." *Frontiers in Neuroscience 17*. DOI-10.3389/fnins.2023.1172783.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-tmphkmh6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphkmh6.md)). The "gut complex" concept; the proposal
that non-neural gut components are constitutive of cognition; a call for
microbiota–cognition studies to consider visceral perception as a mediator.
