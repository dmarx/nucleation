# LEAD-tmple60r: Read the transaction-cost and dynamic-optimization literature that Article 3 of the manuscript governance-of-becoming must be compared against: Coase, Williamson, and resource-bounded planning

Register and read:

- Coase, "The Nature of the Firm" (1937);
- Williamson on transaction-cost economics and asset specificity;

anything transaction-cost economics does not, and whether the "relational
