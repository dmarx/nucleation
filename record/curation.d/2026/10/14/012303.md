## How it was scored

**Value** came from three signals, read together:

- the entry's own "Priority for a deeper reading" line, where it has one (46
  of 167 do: 16 high, 19 medium, 11 low);
- whether a THEORY, QUESTION, CLAIM or CASE cites the work, or the work's
  status note says which question it was registered for;
- how much the record has worked on that question, counted by mentions in
  `claims.d`, `arguments.d` and `workbench.d` on 2026-10-14. The most worked
  were [QUESTION-005](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-005.md) (83), [QUESTION-027](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-027.md) (37), [QUESTION-029](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-029.md) (36), [QUESTION-002](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-002.md)

  (27 each), and [QUESTION-054](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-054.md) (20). Works registered for a question that is
  itself `Deferred` ([QUESTION-008](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-008.md), [QUESTION-059](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), [QUESTION-061](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-061.md)) were ranked
  down.

**Availability** was checked as follows. An `arxiv:` source counts as open
(77 works). For the 73 works with a DOI, OpenAlex was asked for an
open-access location: 48 had none (`closed`) and 25 had one. For 25 of the
works with an OpenAlex location, this client then fetched it once. Most
returned the full text. MDPI, the AMS, the Royal Society, ACM and the American
Meteorological Society returned 403, and bioRxiv returned 429. The 17 `url:` works were
judged by their host. SEP, eScholarship, NASA NTRS and PMC are open. A
publisher's catalogue page or an Open Library listing is not open.
