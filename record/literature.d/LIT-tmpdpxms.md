## Key takeaways

*Not read. From the abstract on the OpenAlex record, unverified against
the text.*

- **Scope**: a survey of continuum topology optimization for compliant
  mechanisms, over about twenty years to 2019.
- **Why compliant mechanisms**: motion through deformation rather than
  joints, so less friction, lubrication and assembly, and suited to small
  scales.
