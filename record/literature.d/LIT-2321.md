  Liu, Huang, Hu, Zhou and Tan (2025), HypoBench: Towards Systematic and
  Principled Benchmarking for Hypothesis Generation. HypoBench defines
  hypothesis generation as producing natural-language explanations of
  observed phenomena, distinguishes it from research ideation, and builds a
  benchmark of 7 real-world and 5 synthetic task families (194 datasets).
  Real-world tasks are scored by practical utility (accuracy of predictions
  made from the hypotheses, in and out of distribution) plus LLM-rated
  novelty, plausibility and clarity; synthetic tasks, whose generating rules
  are known, are scored by hypothesis discovery rate (HDR). Four LLMs × six
  methods are compared. Data-driven methods beat zero/few-shot baselines;
  literature plus data is best on real tasks; but on synthetic tasks
  recovery falls steeply with noise, distractors and interaction depth. Read
