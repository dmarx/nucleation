# LIT-2268: Topological Flowscape Reveals State Transitions in Nonreciprocal Living Matter

Hyunseok Lee, EliseAnne Koskelo, Shreyas Gokhale and seven others (2026). "Topological Flowscape Reveals State Transitions in Nonreciprocal Living Matter." *Physical Review X* 16(3): 031061. [ARXIV-2511.11815](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11815) DOI-10.1103/tq6h-f5ws.

## Key takeaways

- Weak nonreciprocity promotes structural order; strong nonreciprocity
  disrupts it.
- The passage from the traveling to the fluctuating state is abrupt,
  with a fall in the rate of structural change and in estimated
  dissipation.
- Dissipation biases rearrangements toward ordered motifs, read as
  "emergent proofreading".
