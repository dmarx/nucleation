# THEORY-tmp593fy: Robertson and colleagues (2023) find, in a pre-registered analysis of 12,448 randomized Upworthy headline tests, that negative words in a headline causally raise its click-through rate (about 2.3% per negative word in a headline of average length) and positive words lower it (about 1.0%).

## The account

Robertson and colleagues (2023) find, in a pre-registered analysis of 12,448 randomized Upworthy headline tests, that negative words in a headline causally raise its click-through rate (about 2.3% per negative word in a headline of average length) and positive words lower it (about 1.0%).

## Evidence

The reading in NOTE-tmpyh8fv; its key results are quoted there with locations.
