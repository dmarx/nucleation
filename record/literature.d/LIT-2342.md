# LIT-2342: IP-Adapter: Text Compatible Image Prompt Adapter for Text-to-Image Diffusion Models

Hu Ye, Jun Zhang, Sibo Liu, Xiao Han and Wei Yang (2023). "IP-Adapter: Text Compatible Image Prompt Adapter for Text-to-Image Diffusion Models." arXiv 2308.06721; arXiv preprint (v1, the only version; no venue on the arXiv record).

## Key takeaways

Read in [NOTE-1722](../notes.d/NOTE-1722.md); the account is [THEORY-1639](../theory.d/THEORY-1639.md). A lightweight adapter, trained once per base model, that adds image prompts to a frozen text-to-image diffusion model by decoupled cross-attention, and matches fully fine-tuned image-prompt models while staying compatible with text prompts, with community models fine-tuned from the same base, and with structural controls.
