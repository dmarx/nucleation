**What went wrong.** Four readers for [LEAD-215](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-215.md) (Kolmogorov, Kleene, Martin-Löf, Dunfield and Krishnaswami) stopped at a usage limit on 2026-11-22 with their pull requests open or unopened. The check-in scheduled to resume them fired two days late, after a worker restart, when the readers could no longer be resumed. In between, the nightly promotion made the batch's codes permanent and renamed the entries the readers had noted (`CLAIM-949` to [CLAIM-949](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-949.md), `LEAD-215` to [LEAD-215](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-215.md), and so on). A plain merge of staging then gave modify/delete conflicts on every renamed entry, and the branches cited codes staging no longer had. The Martin-Löf reader, restarted, hit the limit again; its work survived only because its brief told it to commit and push after each step.

**What went right.** Every reader branch's edits to held entries were of three shapes only: a dated note appended at the end, an `inactive-ok-file` directive inserted after the frontmatter, and a `draws_on` item. So the resolution was mechanical: take staging's version at the permanent path, reinsert the directives and list items, append the notes, copy the new files, and map each temporary code to its permanent one through staging's `formerly` fields. A script did it for three branches; each diff against staging was additions only, and the remaining temporary codes were the branches' own. Later conflicts between the readings were append against append, resolved by keeping both, staging's first.

**What to change.**
- A reader's brief says to push after each step, as the Martin-Löf restart's did; a stop then costs a step, not the reading.
- A branch whose cited entries are concretized under it can be carried, not refiled: the shapes above are the only edits a reading makes to held entries. If it recurs, the carry belongs in luria (a `luria carry` or a repair step that maps `formerly` codes on a branch), not in a scratch script.
- A resume check-in is not a guarantee that the readers still exist. On firing, check the branches first, and finish the work from them if the agents are gone.
