# CLAIM-tmpzkkx2: What persists when a self persists through inactivity is the capacity to reinstate an organization, not its continuous running, and continuity of that kind can branch and merge, so it is not an identity relation

## The claim

A6 §7: "An identity-like organization could then emerge as a
**self-maintaining pattern of recurrent causal activity and mutually
reinforced authority**. That need not imply the organization is continuously
active. A dormant coalition can remain available for reactivation because
learned parameters, memory traces, and endorsement structures persist." It
separated:

- *activation continuity*, whether a process is running;
- *organizational continuity*, whether its causal structure can be
  reinstated;
- *policy continuity*, whether reinstated activity keeps its characteristic
  preferences.

"A self might remain organizationally stable despite considerable
fluctuations in activation and momentary policy. Conversely, a system could
remain highly active while undergoing profound changes in its
identity-defining organization."

A10 §4.3 took the next step: "a completely independent duplicate might
behave identically without being numerically the same persisting self", so
identity goes by "**causal-organizational continuity**", a relation of
historical descent. "This is not automatically an equivalence relation.
Organizational histories can branch, merge, and partially overlap ...
recursion permits cases analogous to fission, fusion, gradual replacement,
and distributed continuity."

The record's persistence claim is [CLAIM-161](CLAIM-161.md): a self persists when a
coherence measure is conserved along its own trajectory. This claim
qualifies it. Along a trajectory with a gap, what is conserved is the
capacity to reinstate an organization, not any running quantity. It answers
[QUESTION-043](../questions.d/QUESTION-043.md) in part, by saying what kind of thing the conserved measure
must be. [CLAIM-tmpjhd7t](CLAIM-tmpjhd7t.md) is the same distinction for collectives: the product
persists, and so may the producer.

## What it does not say

- It is not a theory of personal identity. Branching shows it is not one.
- It does not say any reinstatement counts. A10's duplicate is excluded by
  requiring descent.
