# LIT-943: From Lazy to Rich: Exact Learning Dynamics in Deep Linear Networks

Clémentine C. J. Dominé, Nicolas Anguita, Alexandra M. Proca, Lukas Braun, Daniel Kunin, Pedro A. M. Mediano and Andrew M. Saxe (2025), *ICLR 2025* — [ARXIV-2409.14623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14623)

## Key takeaways

- **Relative scale sets the regime.** With the absolute scale held fixed,
  the relative scale λ of the two layers moves the network from rich
  (λ → 0, sigmoidal singular-value transitions) to lazy (|λ| → ∞,
  exponential transitions, static NTK) (Theorem 5.1).
- **λ is conserved under gradient flow.** The λ-balanced condition, if it
  holds at initialization, "will persist throughout training"
  (Assumption A2). That is the rescale conservation law.
- **Where the task lives.** At convergence, the layer initialized large
  stays task-agnostic and near the identity. The layer initialized small
  carries the task structure, at a scale ∝ 1/|λ| (Theorems 5.2, C.4).
- **Architecture and sign.** Funnel networks go lazy as λ → +∞ and
  inverted funnels as λ → −∞. In the opposite limits each has a lazy phase
  followed by a delayed rich phase.
