# LIT-1975: Adjustable robust solutions of uncertain linear programs

Ben-Tal, A., Goryashko, A., Guslitzer, E., & Nemirovski, A. (2004). Adjustable robust solutions of uncertain linear programs. *Mathematical Programming*, 99(2), 351–376. doi:10.1007/s10107-003-0454-y.

Read on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-1503](../notes.d/NOTE-1503.md); [THEORY-1303](../theory.d/THEORY-1303.md)).

## Key takeaways

- RC: ∃v ∀ζ; ARC: ∀ζ ∃v (eqs. 5–6). Equal under constraint-wise
  uncertainty (Theorem 2.1).
- Information basis: each decision may read a prescribed part of the data
  (p. 353); named, used in §5, not developed in general.
- AARC: adjustments affine in the data, tractable with fixed recourse.
- Inventory at 20% uncertainty: on-line 34,583, standard 35,121, four-period
  delay and none infeasible (Table 2).

## Standing in the record

The framing of [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md): a compatible configuration may be chosen after
a disturbance, fixed before it, or chosen from what is observed of it. The
record's question there is which of the three an agent's legibility gives it.
