# CLAIM-1061: Stability vs adaptability is the wu-wei paradox

## The supposition

The paradox of wu-wei, as it is usually summarized, Slingerland's
framing among others: effortless right action cannot be reached by
trying for it, yet it is what one is trying to reach. The early sources
are commonly read as offering two routes.

- **Cultivation (Confucian).** Long practice of ritual stabilizes
  structure until right response is spontaneous. The Analects' Confucius
  at seventy, following his heart's desire without overstepping the
  bounds, is the usual example.
- **Unlearning (Daoist).** Shedding the accretions of convention to
  return to an uncarved simplicity. The Daodejing's uncarved block and
  return to infancy are the usual images.

(Both are cited from common knowledge of the texts and the secondary
literature, not checked. [LIT-1994](../literature.d/LIT-1994.md) is unread.)

## The correspondence

- **Cultivation is stabilization that enables.** It is A44's "first
  inversion": "A strongly stabilized organization can be highly adaptable
  precisely because it does not need to reconstruct its basic coordination
  after every disturbance." It is also A44's "commitment is slow; its
  application is fast" ([CLAIM-711](CLAIM-711.md)). Its failure is ritual that has become
  the husk, a rigidity the Daoist texts name. That is overconsolidation
  ([CLAIM-758](CLAIM-758.md)) and an entrenched filter ([CLAIM-1040](CLAIM-1040.md)).
- **Unlearning keeps the spectrum retunable** ([CLAIM-1070](CLAIM-1070.md)). Its
  failure is dissolving the structure that made response cheap, the risk
  the cultivation texts name, which is a loss of what [CLAIM-711](CLAIM-711.md)'s slow
  commitments supply.
- **The resolution.** [CLAIM-1135](CLAIM-1135.md) treats stabilization and
  adaptability as two achievements of one organization, with some
  identity-bearing structures being those that keep the others
  revisable. On this supposition that resolution is a dynamical
  statement of the Confucian–Daoist dispute: which relations to conserve,
  and which to keep revisable. On the weak reading of "inherent" in the
  owner's U20 ([CLAIM-1144](CLAIM-1144.md)), the tension is inherent as the paradox is:
  never removed, always managed.

## Precedence (the owner's ruling of 2026-11-23)

The account is [CLAIM-1135](CLAIM-1135.md)'s, in dynamical terms. The paradox and
the two routes gloss it. They name a familiar shape and point the account
at its two failure modes, but they are not evidence for or against it.
[CLAIM-1187](CLAIM-1187.md) records the ruling.

## Why supposed

The mapping rests on a summary of the sinology that the record has not
read. If the sources do not support the two routes as stated, the
correspondence fails even though [CLAIM-1135](CLAIM-1135.md) may stand.
