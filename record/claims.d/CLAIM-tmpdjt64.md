# CLAIM-tmpdjt64: Interactions generate constraints that act as gluing conditions: where locally described systems interact, the interaction fixes or restricts a correspondence between their descriptions on what they share, and the composed correspondences determine which joint configurations or histories of the whole are admissible; the frames so glued are local observational contexts, not inertial frames, whose transformations no interaction makes

## The claim

The owner, at U20, after [QUESTION-tmpubsi9](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpubsi9.md): "interactions generate constraints
that manifest as gluing conditions between intertial frames (I'm not a
physicist so it's possible I'm using more precise and technical language than
is merited here, but you get the idea of what I'm trying to express, and also
maybe this precision is indeed warranted under our theory). and coupling
inertial frames necessarily induces scale separation (way less confident about
this one than the former assertion)." The second sentence is [CLAIM-tmpfw510](CLAIM-tmpfw510.md).

A20 §2: "**“Gluing conditions” is a mathematically legitimate way to express
much of the idea.**" A20 also said the frames had to go. The record had already
said so in [CLAIM-144](CLAIM-144.md): inertial observers who disagree about temporal order
"differ in coordinates, not in a failure to glue". Their transformations
follow from spacetime geometry, and no interaction generates them. The frames
the claim needs are local observational contexts and local physical frames.
These are the distinctions available to particular systems where they
interact: sensors, cells, clocks, agents, locally defined observables.

**The form.** Each system i has a local description x_i in a space V_i. Where i
and j interact along e, each maps its description into a shared comparison
space, ρ_{i,e}: V_i → W_e. Compatibility is ρ_{i,e}(x_i) = ρ_{j,e}(x_j), which
is agreement on what is shared and not identity (A21 §1). "**Interaction can
constrain the relationships among local descriptions without erasing their
individuality.**" Collected over a graph, the conditions are the kernel of a
coboundary δ, the global sections H⁰ = ker δ (Hansen and Ghrist, [LIT-tmpnpxhs](../literature.d/LIT-tmpnpxhs.md)).
Discourse sheaves ([LIT-tmpf2wmd](../literature.d/LIT-tmpf2wmd.md)) add agents who agree on what they express
while their private states differ.

**Histories, not states.** A22 §5 and A23 §III preferred Willems's behavioural
form ([LIT-tmpnyd4r](../literature.d/LIT-tmpnyd4r.md)). A system is the set of trajectories its laws admit, and
interconnection imposes conditions on shared variables. The jointly admissible
behaviour is those tuples of trajectories that satisfy every interconnection.
A23: "**Interaction can restrict the space of jointly admissible histories.**"
That lets the claim cover delays, memory and sustained phase differences. Two
oscillators can keep a fixed phase difference and never agree in state.

**Physical gluing.** In port-Hamiltonian systems ([LIT-tmp6zanw](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6zanw.md)) an
interconnection is a condition on the effort and flow at a port, such that
power leaving one system enters the other, and such interconnections compose
(A22 §6). There the gluing condition is a constraint on actual energy flow. It
is more than agreement between descriptions.

## Three things the word covers

A23 §III.2: "**A correspondence rule is not automatically a physical
interaction.**" The claim needs three distinct operations:

| operation | what it is | example |
|---|---|---|
| correspondence | a rule relating two local descriptions on what they share | a unit conversion, a phase offset g_ij |
| evidence | an interaction that leaves records from which a rule can be inferred | two clocks exchanging signals that estimate an offset |
| enforcement | a mechanism that drives states toward a rule | a synchronizing controller, a diffusive coupling |

A21 §5's clocks separate them. Measuring offsets does not synchronize the
clocks, synchronizing them chooses no absolute time, and the cycle condition
d₁₂ + d₂₃ + d₃₁ ≈ 0 causes nothing. The claim's "generate" is literal where
an interaction leaves records or imposes an interconnection. It is not literal
where a rule only redescribes relations already there. A20 §2: "**Interactions
provide constraints on correspondences; gluing expresses the coherence
required of those correspondences.**" A22 §8 added the error this guards
against, "assuming that changing a mathematical description directly changes
the physical system it describes."

[CLAIM-143](CLAIM-143.md) already holds the epistemic half: local disagreement shows no
obstruction "until the correspondence between observations has been fixed and
independently validated". A21 §6 drew the cost: "we should distinguish
**failure to glue** from **inability to find a gluing within available
resources**" ([CLAIM-tmpi1bjh](CLAIM-tmpi1bjh.md)).

## Composition matters, and failure organizes

The admissible whole is not settled by checking each relation alone (A23
§III.3). The record holds three kinds of local-to-global obstruction, and they
are different mathematics:

- **Empirical contextuality.** The event sheaf always glues, so the
  obstruction lies in the supports or distributions ([CLAIM-131](CLAIM-131.md), [THEORY-012](../theory.d/THEORY-012.md),
  [LIT-016](../literature.d/LIT-016.md)).
- **Cycle holonomy.** In a gauge-coupled phase model, g₁₂g₂₃g₃₁ ≠ 1
  forbids a consistent nonzero assignment though every edge is satisfiable.
  Torres-Hugas et al. ([LIT-tmpre2ri](../literature.d/LIT-tmpre2ri.md)) prove that a twisted Laplacian has a
  zero mode exactly when every cycle holonomy vanishes, in their model.
- **Frustrated edge differences.** In a frustrated graph energy
  ½‖Dx − b‖² with a + b + c ≠ 0 around a cycle, the minimizer leaves a
  residual in ker Dᵀ, the cycle space (A22 §3). This is the graph form of a
  Hodge decomposition.

A21 §5's caution belongs with the second: nontrivial holonomy "is not a
failure of the bundle's gluing conditions". It forbids a globally covariantly
constant section, not a global geometry.

A22 and A23 drew from the third that "**individuation need not be the
achievement of perfect agreement. It can involve the stable organization of
differences, including differences that cannot all be reconciled.**" Whether a
residual is a physical stress or a modelling error has to be shown each time.

## What it does not claim

- **That a glued whole is one individual.** "Globally compatible local
  descriptions ⇏ One physical individual" and "Synchronized dynamics ⇏ One
  autonomous individual" (A21 §7). Two oscillators can agree because a common
  drive moves them ([QUESTION-063](../questions.d/QUESTION-063.md)). A20 §7 separated coupling, compatibility
  and dynamic integration, and any one can occur without the others.
  Stewart, Golubitsky and Pivato ([LIT-tmpwhycq](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwhycq.md)) separate an invariant
  synchrony pattern, which the architecture allows, from an attracting one,
  which the dynamics selects.
- **That coupling separates time scales.** That is [CLAIM-tmpfw510](CLAIM-tmpfw510.md), answered by
  [CLAIM-tmpywecm](CLAIM-tmpywecm.md).
- **That every constraint comes from an interaction.** Conservation laws and
  symmetries constrain interactions without being produced by them (A23
  §XII). The constraints composition makes are effective, not new
  fundamental laws ([CLAIM-tmpose3y](CLAIM-tmpose3y.md)).

## A failure mode it brings

If the correspondence maps can be revised to reduce disagreement and nothing
else, they can be sent to zero. Then everything is compatible and nothing is
distinguished. The record has this already: [THEORY-299](../theory.d/THEORY-299.md), from knowledge sheaves,
notes that unconstrained consistency admits the all-zero embedding. A21 §6:
"**Self-consistency is not the same as correspondence with reality.**" That is
[CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md)'s self-confirming loop in the gluing setting. Revising the gluing
needs evidence that the revision does not control.
