**What started it.** The owner asked for a review of the record's current
state and its synthesis opportunities.

**What the structure showed.**
- Of 206 Active or Proposed THEORY entries, 163 had no edge to any CLAIM or
  QUESTION.
- Every cross-line claim edge ran between `will-organization` and
  `distributed-agency`. The two largest neighbours, `pragmatic-transport`
  and `nucleating-argument`, shared none.
- 19 open questions had no `answers` edge, 18 of them on
  `pragmatic-transport`.
- Several THEORYs were named in argument prose without a typed edge.

Three agents then read separate regions: the transport and nucleating
lines, the agency lines against nucleating, and the unlinked THEORYs. Their
key citations were checked against the files before anything was ranked.
The owner had the top of the ranking filed.

**What was filed.**

1. **A narrowing of an Active claim.** [CLAIM-tmpczjuu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpczjuu.md) objects to [CLAIM-154](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-154.md).
   The reification rule's Gaussian Occam term is exact in the conjugate case
   where it was re-checked. For candidates whose removal leaves a singular
   model, [THEORY-087](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-087.md) and [THEORY-079](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-079.md) put the penalty at λ log n and the
   Laplace error growing with log n. This ranked first because it corrects
   something the record already believed. [CLAIM-154](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-154.md) is dual-filed from the
   mathematics-of-meaning record, which this record does not edit.
2. **The constraint-set point.** [CLAIM-tmptfikp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfikp.md): for an argument, the set
   persistence is relative to is fixed by an amendable decision. It is the
   item workbench 214308 left as notes, and it meets the will line's open
   mapping question ([CLAIM-079](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-079.md), [ARG-007](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-007.md)) without answering it. [CLAIM-075](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) now
   bounds [CLAIM-185](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-185.md), since persistence is not correctness.
3. **The first bridge between pragmatic-transport and nucleating-argument.**
   - [CLAIM-tmpnf0d8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnf0d8.md): restating an argument across memoryless sessions is
     iterated transport, so drift should have a direction, toward the
     collaborators' prior, and a code restores continuity of transmission.
   - Its counter, [CLAIM-tmpkkx3m](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkkx3m.md): fidelity is the wrong measure of a
     developing argument, so what the record secures is traceability.
4. **Links.**
   - [QUESTION-025](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-025.md) now has the partial answers its prose already cited.
   - Grounds were added: [THEORY-132](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-132.md) on [CLAIM-184](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-184.md), [THEORY-137](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-137.md) on [CLAIM-183](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-183.md),
