# THEORY-1214: Cicada hosts whose endosymbiont Hodgkinia has split into many interdependent lineages compensate by sending more Hodgkinia cells to each egg, with Sulcia numbers and the transmission route unchanged, so that every egg still receives every lineage; the raised titre is a host adaptation driven by the symbiont's fragmentation, and a cost of it

## Source

Campbell et al. (2018), [LIT-1590](../literature.d/LIT-1590.md), read in full in [NOTE-1235](../notes.d/NOTE-1235.md). The paper's title asserts it: "Changes in Endosymbiont Complexity Drive Host-Level Compensatory Adaptations in Cicadas".

## Where the evidence stands

What the paper shows, and what it does not, is [THEORY-1106](THEORY-1106.md), Active, which this entry extends: that entry is the reading, this one the thesis. The `promote_when` above is [THEORY-1106](THEORY-1106.md)'s condition before the twenty-second status review promoted it, carried here unchanged, because it asks what would make the thesis true and not what the paper says.
