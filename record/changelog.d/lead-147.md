### Added

- CLAIM-tmpe87cm, a derivation for LEAD-147. Under multiplicative co-occurrence with any implication order among binary attributes, every attribute is an exact linear readout of the full-rank PMI embedding. On a balanced tree, the PMI eigenvectors are branch contrasts with eigenvalues falling strictly by depth, so a truncated embedding keeps exactly the shallow attributes and merges siblings below the cut.
- CASE-tmpby7xp, an executed study for LEAD-147 on text8 with WordNet 3.0 and the word2vec analogy set. The PMI is far from additive in every analogy family, but the families and quadruples nearer additivity complete more often. WordNet attributes carry a small, real additive part of the PMI.
- scratch/studies/lead-147/: the scripts, tests and per-item results.
