# LIT-1589: Legal Consequences of the Separation of the Chagos Archipelago from Mauritius in 1965

International Court of Justice (2019). *Legal Consequences of the Separation of the Chagos Archipelago from Mauritius in 1965*, Advisory Opinion of 25 February 2019.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-tmp87q7f](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp87q7f.md)), from UN document A/73/773;
the separate opinions were not read.

## Why this is here

A3 used it to separate interstate sovereignty from the claims of the displaced, the distinction [CASE-169](../cases.d/CASE-169.md) brings to [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md): a settlement adequate to the dispute between states need not decide whether the people expelled can return.
