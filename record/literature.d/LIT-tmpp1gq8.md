# LIT-tmpp1gq8: The political effects of X’s feed algorithm

Germain Gauthier, Roland Hodler, Philine Widmer and Ekaterina Zhuravskaya (2026). "The political effects of X’s feed algorithm." *Nature 652(8109): 416-423*. DOI-10.1038/s41586-026-10098-2.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-tmp76oz9](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp76oz9.md)). First independent randomized evidence
that a major platform's feed algorithm, switched on, shifts political
attitudes (not partisanship), with a mechanism (algorithm-induced follows)
that would make a switch-off experiment show nothing even when the algorithm
matters.
