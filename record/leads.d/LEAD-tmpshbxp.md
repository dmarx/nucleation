# LEAD-tmpshbxp: Search for human studies that separate the germline route of intergenerational trauma from the uterine, parenting and cultural routes, and for studies of descendants' distress against group identification

## What it would do

Search for paternal-line and adoption designs, and read the registered
works.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-297: "children of trauma-exposed fathers conceived after the
  trauma and raised apart from them, or adoptees."
- NOTE-1291: "A study measuring descendants' identification with the
  group, separately from family exposure, would answer the question's second
  part"

## The works

- LIT-1692 (Unfetched): Yehuda 2016, "Holocaust Exposure Induced
  Intergenerational Effects on FKBP5 Methylation"
- LIT-1715 (Unfetched): Yehuda 2018, "Intergenerational transmission of
  trauma effects: putative role of epigenetic mechanisms"
- LIT-1764 (Unfetched): Pembrey 2005, "Sex-specific, male-line
  transgenerational responses in humans"
- LIT-1678 (Unfetched): Ord 2020, "The Precipice: Existential Risk and the
  Future of Humanity"

## What would count

A study that separates the routes answers QUESTION-297, or shows that no
separation has been achieved.
