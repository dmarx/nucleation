# LEAD-tmpi367t: Read cognitive load theory for its account of the three loads, element interactivity and the expertise reversal effect

## Read

- **Sweller (1988)**, "Cognitive load during problem solving: effects on
  learning", Cognitive Science. The origin.
- **Sweller, van Merriënboer and Paas (1998)**, "Cognitive architecture and
  instructional design", Educational Psychology Review. The three loads.
- **Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler and Sweller (2003)**, "The expertise reversal
  effect", Educational Psychologist.
- **A later revision.** Sweller's own later work recasts germane load as a
  share of working-memory resources, not a third load. Find it.
