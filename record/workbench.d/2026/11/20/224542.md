**What the owner asked.** "File the ten principles, the predicted cycle and the census findings as entries in the record in an independent PR." All three come from the owner's manuscript on the record, *From Obstruction to Reorganization*, kept in `scratch/manuscripts/` on its own branch. Its §7.1 states ten principles (P1–P10) of a general theory of learning and inquiry, each joining entries the record holds. Its §7.2 states a predicted eight-stage cycle. Its Part III reports a census of the record's decisions.

**What was found along the way.**

- **Every principle had dropped a scope condition** that its components state. The pattern runs from [CLAIM-402](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-402.md)'s "or when the effect of that use is modelled" to [CLAIM-577](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-577.md)'s "unless the budget is raised from outside". Generalizing across lines tends to shed exactly the conditions that make each result true. The accounts restore them.
- **The accounts are thematic, not scoped to a line.** [ADR-083](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope assumes the scope is a line or a part of one, and these cut across several lines. Whether thematic accounts are a kind the record wants is a question for the owner.
- **The owner raised a related worry, for later:** that [ADR-075](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s language may discourage documenting useful syntheses the record has already constructed. It bears on a goal the owner named for the future, a manuscript manifested as a view over the record. This session found the friction concretely. Ten integrations the owner wanted on the record had no place to go except as claims they could not be, or as accounts of a scope [ADR-083](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-083.md) does not name. No decision is proposed here.
- **The manuscript needs correcting** where the audit found errors: P2, P5, P9, P10 and the cycle as a sequence. That is done on its own pull request, not here.
