The owner sent sixteen readings that run from evidence-based model
comparison to the Fisher geometry of trained networks. They asked that any
already held be checked and the rest filed here, in both records where the
anthology held one.

## The check against both records

Only one was held anywhere: Watanabe's 2009 book, here, as a Deferred seed
([LIT-354](../../record/literature.d/LIT-354.md)). The anthology holds later Shampoo and K-FAC derivatives and SAE
work, but none of these papers. So nothing needed a second, [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) entry.

The owner's question here, which the anthology does not ask, is how one
line of inquiry connects:

- Bayesian model comparison: evidence, Savage–Dickey, model reduction,
  singular learning theory;
- the spectra of the Fisher information in trained models, the object
  their thresholding acts on;
- the kernel and feature eigenstructure beside them.

The machine-learning works carry `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-005.md)).

The check also found that CLAUDE.md still said "nothing is filed in
both", eight days after [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) narrowed that rule. The map was corrected
in the same contribution. Two topics were added for the line ([ADR-026](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-026.md)):
`model-comparison` and `information-geometry`.

## What the readings say together

The filing agents found the links that make this one line, and each
marked them as its own reading where the papers do not draw them:

- Savage–Dickey is the point-mass case of Bayesian model reduction.
- The Occam factor is a volume ratio, set by curvature in regular models
  and by the real log canonical threshold (RLCT) in singular ones.
- Amari's efficiency result needs an invertible Fisher, the same
  regularity WBIC's account drops.
- Papyan finds the Fisher's top outliers in class-mean directions, and
  K-FAC's single Kronecker factor misplaces them.
- The NTK Gram matrix and the Gauss–Newton Fisher share their non-zero
  spectrum. So the harmonic-degree blocks of the kernel papers should
  appear as Fisher plateaus, and a spectral threshold is well posed only
  between blocks.
