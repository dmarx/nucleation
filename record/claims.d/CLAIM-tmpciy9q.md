# CLAIM-tmpciy9q: A party can arrange the conditions under which another commits a real wrong and then use the true accusation, before an audience whose judgement it anticipated, to justify further control over them; the truth of the accusation neither vindicates the party that arranged it nor excuses the one who did wrong

## The claim

Three moves, which A states across four turns of the exchange
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b):

1. **Arrange a real wrong.** A party controls conditions under which the
   other is likely to do wrong: concealment under punishment, or a
   disproportionate answer to a provocation. A9 §6: "A repeatedly
   punishes B for disagreeing. B learns to conceal disagreement and
   sometimes lies to avoid conflict. A discovers the lies and uses them to
   justify increased surveillance." A calls this "**creating the behavior
   that subsequently becomes the rationale for A's exercise of power**".
2. **Use the true accusation.** A7 §5: "**Being entitled to recognize a
   wrong is not the same as being entitled to impose whatever consequences
   you choose for it.**" A11's close: "*manufacturing culpability*", which
   "captures the possibility that someone can deliberately generate real
   wrongdoing in another person, then exploit the fact that the wrongdoing
   is real." "It's an exceptionally powerful form of manipulation
   precisely because the accusation against the target need not be
   false."
3. **Before an anticipated audience.** A13 §8: "The target's moral
   reasoning influences its decision. The audience's moral reasoning
   influences how that decision is judged. And the initiator attempts to
   shape both." "The strategy can therefore operate across two distinct
   audiences". A11's close: "the manipulator doesn't merely induce a
   decision; they arrange for the moral responsibility attached to that
   decision to become a strategic resource."

The two halves of the verdict are held apart. A13 §8: "The fact that B was
provoked doesn't automatically excuse B's conduct. The fact that B behaved
wrongly doesn't automatically vindicate A. Both forms of responsibility
can coexist."

**Its interstate form** (A11 §3, after the owner's U6). In a grey-zone
confrontation, A provokes, B answers with excessive force, and A points to
the excess. "The resulting moral and political controversy is not
incidental to the strategy. It may be the strategy's intended product."

**The two audiences in an institution** (A13 §3, A's account of an example
it attributes to Simon Keller, not yet checked against his text).
Administrators announce worse terms just before examinations: if teachers
do not strike, "Administrators gain leverage"; if they strike,
"Administrators gain a narrative", since "they can be publicly portrayed
as abandoning their students." The record files no case of it until the
work is read.

## How it sits with the record

- **[CLAIM-445](CLAIM-445.md)** holds the loop's mechanism: a protective regime becomes
  self-maintaining when its interventions shift "the evidence used to
  judge its necessity". Here the shifted evidence is the target's own
  conduct, and the regime is one party's control over another. That is
  why the claim rests on it.
- **[CLAIM-619](CLAIM-619.md)**'s normative inversion turns a complaint into evidence
  against the complainant. This turns a wrong the arranger induced into
  evidence against the one who committed it. The two differ in whether the
  thing used is a wrong.
- **[CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)** (the owner's) governs an organization through the premises
  or criteria of its decisions; this governs a party through its wrongs and
  through others' judgement of them.
- Beside it, from the same exchange: [CLAIM-tmpa2l21](CLAIM-tmpa2l21.md) (the wrong of
  arranging another's wrong, which this uses as an instrument) and
  [CLAIM-tmp8wki5](CLAIM-tmp8wki5.md) (a restriction that produces the incapacity cited to
  justify it, the same shape with incapacity in place of a wrong).
- The senses of responsibility kept apart are [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md)'s; the further
  control the accusation justifies is domination in [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)'s sense.

## What it does not say

- Not that an accusation of manufactured culpability is itself evidence of
  it. A11 §6: "We need evidence of intention, anticipation, capacity, and
  causal influence", and "a theory about manipulating others' perceptions
  can itself become a lens through which observers mistakenly perceive
  everything as manipulation."
- Not that every accusation by a more powerful party is of this kind, nor
  that public accountability is manipulative. A13 §8: "This is not a claim
  that all public accountability or criticism is manipulative."
- Nothing about any real dispute or state.
