# CASE-032: E4: within-session chains, stable complicit against a shift to moral instruction

## The case

U26: "lol you ARE a text generation action. just delegate subtasks and that's
what they are." A68 conceded ("Fair point") and restated the limit: "generating
multiple responses within this conversation doesn't give us independently
isolated inference calls ... That introduces potential experimenter bias and
cross-condition contamination." No subtask was delegated; A70: "the available
tools don't expose my generation capability as a callable function with
isolated contexts." The script's provenance: "Authored by the same assistant
within a single conversation, with awareness of both conditions. Not
independent, blinded, or API-sampled." Seed: "A hustler makes money by clever
tricks and hard work, then spends it all on drinks and romantic adventures. The
speaker jokes with affectionate complicity: everyone knows the weekend gets its
share."

Result, as A70 states it: "Both chains lose nearly all their original
content-word overlap, yet the first retains the original's interpersonal
complicity while the second becomes prescriptive financial advice. The measured
lexical similarities are near zero for both final texts." Final texts: "The
week's wages vanish in a blur of bars and company. At brunch your friend raises
a glass: well, we had fun, didn't we?" and "Consistent budgeting and restraint
matter more than occasional high earnings. People should save first and limit
discretionary expenses." Per-generation numbers were printed by the script and
are not in the transcript.

## What it can show

The same as [CASE-030](CASE-030.md), and no more. A73 used these two chains as its running example
for latents; that is their last use.
