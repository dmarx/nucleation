# QUESTION-tmpmz64i: Can a persistent pattern of transfer through a system, kept while its constituents are replaced, pick out a unit that neither a block test nor a closed collective mode finds, and what separates such organized throughput from mere transport?

## Why it is a question

A5 separated three kinds of organization:

- **containment**: a group of constituents forms a weakly coupled
  subsystem ([CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md), [THEORY-661](../theory.d/THEORY-661.md));
- **mode**: a closed collective coordinate spans many constituents
  ([THEORY-257](../theory.d/THEORY-257.md), [CLAIM-tmpkoi6k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkoi6k.md));
- **flux**: "a patterned transfer process persists despite the replacement
  or reorganization of constituents", as a vortex, a convection cell or a
  metabolism does.

A3's ground for the third: in the inertial range of three-dimensional
turbulence, energy flux across a scale is dominated by comparable scales
(Eyink and Aluie, [LIT-tmp2snx1](../literature.d/LIT-tmp2snx1.md), read 2026-10-20 in [NOTE-tmp5es2s](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp5es2s.md)). That is
locality of coupling across scales. The paper sketches the bounds and
refers to Eyink 2005 ([LIT-tmpko78q](../literature.d/LIT-tmpko78q.md), Unavailable) for the proofs. They hold
under an unproved scaling hypothesis, 0 < σ_p < 1, and the record holds
their averaged form as a conditional ([THEORY-tmpfcxzq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpfcxzq.md)). That it is not
near-decomposability is the record's inference, not the paper's. Coupling
falls off as a power of the scale ratio with no gap, and in a
constant-flux range the whole flux crosses every scale, so no contiguous
group of scales is weakly coupled to the rest. Eyink 2005 ([LIT-tmpko78q](../literature.d/LIT-tmpko78q.md)) treats forward and inverse cascades in one
framework, so A3 separated four properties that "coarse to fine" runs
together: that scales exist, that coupling between them is local, the
direction of transfer, and what is transferred.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md) (organisms against dissipative structures such as a candle
  flame), [THEORY-532](../theory.d/THEORY-532.md) (gradient-degrading structures, continuous with
  convection cells), [CLAIM-273](../claims.d/CLAIM-273.md) (r/place art held by "a sustained flow of
  repair") and [QUESTION-085](QUESTION-085.md) (self-maintenance like a flame's).
- None of these is set beside the block and closure tests as a criterion
  of unity, and no entry holds a cascade.

## What would count as an answer

A5's candidate test, which it said "should not be attributed to Eyink or to
the existing record": a subset of processes exchanging a quantity q with
fluxes J_ij whose transfer pattern (1) keeps a stable relational structure
over a stated timescale, (2) has a nontrivial balance of inflow, outflow,
production and dissipation, (3) restores or reorganizes itself after
perturbation, and (4) depends on internal organization rather than only on
imposed forcing. An answer would apply such a test to a system where the
block test fails, and show it finds a unit that an independent mark of
unity confirms, or that it finds only transport. A5's limit: "physical flux
requires a balance law for a specified transferred quantity; it is not a
generic scalar that exists for every agent-based system." A3's: energy and
information transfer "should not be identified merely because both can be
assigned a direction across scales" (Granero-Belinchón et al.,
[LIT-tmp8dl8g](../literature.d/LIT-tmp8dl8g.md)).
