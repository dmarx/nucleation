  Ansuini, Laio, Macke & Zoccolan (2019), Intrinsic dimension of data representations in deep neural networks, NeurIPS 2019; arXiv 1905.12784. With the TwoNN estimator, the intrinsic dimension of trained image CNNs' representations is orders of magnitude below layer width, rises over the early layers (not in a small MNIST network) and falls to about 12 to 25 in the last hidden layer, and is one to two orders below a 90%-variance PCA count, which does not tell trained from random networks apart: the manifolds are curved. Across 14 ImageNet models the last-layer value correlates with accuracy (r = 0.94), across seeds of one network not at all. Read in NOTE-tmptf55k; filed as THEORY-tmpwn1xp.
