## What was read

- **A43 in full.** This is "The Cost Structure of Possibility:
  Historical Investment, Effective Constraints, and the Emergence of
  Capabilities" ("Working manuscript — theoretical sketch, November
  2026"), with its abstract, §1 to §13 and its references.
- **Later passages offered for a manuscript.** A search of the exchange
  for "manuscript" after A43 finds two. A46 §10 is "the thesis I'd put at
  the center of the next manuscript", on learning. A60 §9 would
  "strengthen our manuscript's central thesis" as "Learning as the

- **The candidates.** An earlier mapping proposed eighteen claims. A

## Members filed from the exchange itself

At the owner's request of 2026-11-19 this decision's pull request was
merged into dmarx/nucleation#528, which files the exchange. Four of the
claims filed there are members, found by reading A43 against each:

- **CLAIM-tmpqpd2p** (the owner-endorsed thesis, constraint as stored
  work): §1's second and third components, §4.1 ("A change from
  organization M to M′ can expand the repertoire if it lowers some
  realization costs below the budget threshold") and §10's first
  proposition state it.
- **CLAIM-tmpkrk41** (work transferred, not saved): §7.3 ("shift
  maintenance costs to less powerful participants") and §11's fifth
  limit ("an organization can shift costs rather than reduce them").
- **CLAIM-tmp5gbzr** (an acquisition valued by its effect on others):
  §4.3 ("It may be the ability to produce additional capabilities") and
  §5.2 ("A learned skill may lower the cost of learning advanced
  skills") use it. A judgement call: A43 states the recursion, not the
  reversal of ranking the claim demonstrates.
- **CLAIM-tmpw8xkt** (the owner's U10 hypothesis): §7.2 uses it as a step,
  equivalence classes in engagement data becoming reusable structure and
  then "operational reification", and §6 states its form for histories.

Not members: CLAIM-tmp449rr (A43 never separates dynamical from economic
separation), CLAIM-tmp1l2s7, CLAIM-tmp8ljvp, CLAIM-tmp5vg7p and
CLAIM-tmpj540o (later turns; A43 does not state them).

## Passages held by no entry, left

- **§1's thesis and §13's chain** ("Past work → persistent organization
  → changed realization costs → new capabilities → further
  organization"). They are the thesis as a whole, and its parts are the
  members above.
- **§10's propositions.** Proposition 1 "follows directly from the
  definition"; Proposition 2 is "an elementary accounting result".
  Hypotheses 1 to 3 are untested conjectures, and §10.1's three regimes
  are a design. None is filed.
- **§11's fifth limit**, that an organization can shift costs rather
  than reduce them. A43 states it without argument, and no entry was
  matched to it in this general form.
- **§7's examples** are illustrations. Their nearest entries are
  [CLAIM-695](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-695.md), [CLAIM-402](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) and [CLAIM-358](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-358.md), none a member.

## A43 audited

- **Attribution.** A43 credits no entry in its text. Its "We propose"
  and "We develop" cover mechanisms that are the owner's on this
  record: time-scale separation as a source of constraints ([CLAIM-478](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-478.md),
  the owner's U12 of 2026-10-27); stored retrieval as amortized
  computation ([QUESTION-095](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-095.md)); specification stored so that later
  questions are cheaper ([CLAIM-475](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-475.md), the owner's U17 of 2026-10-27); and
  Sen's two aspects mapped onto viable reachability ([CLAIM-352](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-352.md), the
  owner's connection at U11 of 2026-10-17, worked out by A11). §8 calls
  the last "a proposed control-theoretic representation". The reference
  list names these as "research context", without saying whose they
  are.
- **[TERM-117](../../record/terms.d/TERM-117.md) redefined as new.** §5.1 says "We define a productive
  nucleus" and gives the term's definition and measure. The term is A's
  own, from the DNA origami synthesis of 2026-11-05.
- **[CLAIM-354](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-354.md) mis-glossed.** See above.
- **Standing.** Hypothesis 4 gives [CLAIM-675](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-675.md)'s proposition as a
  hypothesis that "require[s] comparative tests". The record holds it
  Active, demonstrated in [CLAIM-605](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s model, and §8.1 states it as
  settled. An empirical test is a separate question. A43 does not call
  [CLAIM-478](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-478.md) established. §2.2 derives its first half inside a slow–fast
  model, where it holds, and does not use its second half, that
  organization can make new separations. The record's Proposed standing
  is for the general claim.
