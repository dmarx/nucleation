# SYNTHESIS-049: The distributed-agency line is ten part accounts, of conferral and authority, of availability before a deadline, of partners coming to depend on one another, of perspectives, legibility and correction, of the unit of a combined form, of individuation, of frustration among demands, of persistence, of world-modeling and of collective stance, with twenty-one entries integrated directly; conferral, frustration and perspectives are joined by complements over self-confirming authority and what a legibility regime admits, unit and individuation by the boundary test and its objection, and persistence by the slow variable and the senses of persistence; no link between the parts that no part states passes the claim test

*The account of the distributed-agency line ("Distributed agency": what an organized whole is, and how its operative direction relates to its members'), over the accounts of its parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). It
integrates every entry whose `line` includes distributed-agency on
2026-11-11: 474 entries through ten part accounts, and 21 directly. A
mechanical community map of the line (stored relations and mentions)
gave seven parts; reading them, three fell into two or three inquiries
each, so there are ten accounts. [CASE-028](../cases.d/CASE-028.md) carries the line and is
Superseded by [CASE-006](../cases.d/CASE-006.md), which [SYNTHESIS-047](SYNTHESIS-047.md) integrates; it is not
integrated again. Many members carry another line, often first, and are
integrated by that line's accounts too; the part accounts say where.*

## The parts

- **Conferral and authority ([SYNTHESIS-050](SYNTHESIS-050.md)), 99 entries.** Conferral
  is any effect on another process's share of future influence, and a
  grant is conferral through a maintained relation. How grants are
  counted and scored decides whether authority tracks reliability or
  entrenches: scored against the interpretation it helped make dominant,
  the loop can amplify an initial advantage without fidelity ([CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)),
  and what keeps a judgement–observation loop from confirming itself is
  evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the
  interpretation it tests ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)). Capture runs through the evidence
  an evaluation receives or the criteria it weighs by ([CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md)).
- **Availability ([SYNTHESIS-057](SYNTHESIS-057.md)), 21 entries.** What a system can
  deploy before a deadline is not what it has or knows it has ([TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md));
  a failed search shows absence only with a certificate about the
  unsearched remainder; retrieval and evidence pull apart when a category
  is carved.
- **Partners coming to depend on one another ([SYNTHESIS-053](SYNTHESIS-053.md)), 11
  entries.** Which properties each route to dependence brings about, and
  which make a partnership a new individual, is open ([QUESTION-277](../questions.d/QUESTION-277.md)); the
  documented symbioses show those properties coming apart.
- **Perspectives, legibility and correction ([SYNTHESIS-056](SYNTHESIS-056.md)), 63
  entries.** An organizational perspective is not thereby an agent
  ([CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)); an encompassing organization's success does not establish
  its constituents' well-being, and the converse holds ([CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md)); which
  distinctions must be legible is relative to a task ([TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)) and to a
  policy ([CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md)); an arrangement can keep its effectiveness while its
  correctability degrades; and the wrong of reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining an organization is proposed as a breach of a bounded
  duty of answerability.
- **The unit of a combined form ([SYNTHESIS-054](SYNTHESIS-054.md)), 81 entries.**
  Relative to a stated target, a combined form is proposed to be one
  system while it is a gap-isolated block whose gap beats the cost of
  naming it ([CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md), Proposed); any individuation built on sufficiency
  is relative to its target ([CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md)); the test cannot tell a produced
  boundary from an imposed one ([CLAIM-194](../claims.d/CLAIM-194.md)). A self in the self-governing
  sense takes part in governing the conditions of its own persistence
  ([CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md)).
- **Individuation ([SYNTHESIS-048](SYNTHESIS-048.md)), 71 entries.** From
  interconnection to individuation the record holds distinct achievements
  in a partial order. An individuation is well defined once its
  criterion is fixed ([CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md)), its senses need different evidence
  ([TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)), and whether relations constitute an entity stays open
  ([QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)).
- **Frustration ([SYNTHESIS-051](SYNTHESIS-051.md)), 53 entries.** Frustration among an
  organization's demands is an ordinal fact about which sets of them can
  be jointly met ([CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)); relief enlarges that complex for
  the same demands; the residual and governance regret refine it without
  identifying thwarting ([CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md)). For demands on offsets between
  contexts, the minimal conflicts are the cycles whose holonomy exceeds
  their tolerance ([CLAIM-634](../claims.d/CLAIM-634.md), filed with this review, Proposed).
- **Persistence ([SYNTHESIS-047](SYNTHESIS-047.md)), 40 entries.** A collective that
  replaces all its members persists by causal transmission of the
  organization that produces it, not by resemblance ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md),
  [CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md)); persistence has senses that come apart ([TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md)); a slow
  variable belongs to a collective's dynamics when its members read and
  regenerate it ([CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)), and a level has a temporal facet ([CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md)).
- **World-modeling ([SYNTHESIS-055](SYNTHESIS-055.md)), 6 entries.** Competent behaviour
  across a family of goals fixes the world model an observer must ascribe
  without identifying a mechanism; producing, maintaining and revising it
  are further achievements ([TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md)).
- **Collective stance ([SYNTHESIS-052](SYNTHESIS-052.md)), 29 entries.** What a
  collective is credited with need not be what its members hold: an
  organized whole's operative intention is not the expectation of its
  members' intentions, a diffuse population can show a public stance
  without a collective intention, and a person can be organized by a
  judgement attributed to a collective that does not hold it.

## What it does not say

- That the ten parts are one inquiry. They are kept in one line because
  they are densely joined and the line's heading, what an organized whole is, covers them, not because a component says they are.
- That the ten questions without links belong in this line. Whether they
  should carry another line is the owner's call.
