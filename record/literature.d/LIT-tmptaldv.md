# LIT-tmptaldv: "Making Out" While Driving: Relational and Efficiency Games in the Gig Economy

Cameron (2022). *"Making Out" While Driving: Relational and Efficiency Games in the Gig Economy*. Organization Science 33(1): 231–252. DOI 10.1287/orsc.2021.1547. Read in the author's manuscript on the Wharton faculty site.

## Key takeaways

Ride-hail drivers build two workplace games around the customer and the app: a relational game the app's ratings make winnable, and an efficiency game it frustrates, which drivers track themselves and sometimes play by manipulating the algorithm. Both sustain engagement, with amicable and adversarial stances toward the platform.
