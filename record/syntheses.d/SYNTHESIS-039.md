# SYNTHESIS-039: Maintaining an effective regime and maintaining the capacity to govern among regimes are different achievements, since stability does not entail viability: a protective regime can keep itself in place by shaping the evidence and authority that would end it, an organization can record a correction without maintaining the capacity it needs, and resolve, release and a learned prior over which processes govern are among the relations that keep or lose that capacity

*A part account of the will-organization line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)); the line's account is built over it.*

## The account

**The main result.** [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) (the paper's, Section 6.2; Active): an operative
regime, the governing organization among regimes and the capacity to
transform that organization can persist or fail separately. It grounds on
[CASE-036](../cases.d/CASE-036.md) (Northstar's self-sustaining emergency, a variant of [CASE-023](../cases.d/CASE-023.md)),
[CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md) and [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md), and uses [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md), protective lock-in. [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md) (the
paper's, from viability theory; demonstrated, Active): stability does not
entail viability. [ARG-007](../arguments.d/ARG-007.md) (Active) rests on both, grounds on [CASE-036](../cases.d/CASE-036.md) and
concludes [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) (Active), the paper's main result: maintaining a regime
of effective action and maintaining the capacity to govern among regimes
"are not the same achievement". [CLAIM-018](../claims.d/CLAIM-018.md) (Active) reads Canguilhem's
capacity for norm-changing as transformation capacity; its summary says
its absence from [ARG-007](../arguments.d/ARG-007.md)'s premises "is how the record says so", that it is
an interpretation and not a premise. [CASE-036](../cases.d/CASE-036.md)'s note of 2026-10-22 reads the
emergency as a loss of the evidence needed to lift it, and release as a
separate capacity.

**Access, authority and action.** [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md) (the owner's idea at U42, in A42's
form; Active) rests on [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) and uses [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md): access to
a signal, its authority over action, and action on it are separable;
overcontrol "is a maladaptive regime and not more control". [CASE-127](../cases.d/CASE-127.md)
(United 173 and crew resource management, the owner's case) illustrates it
and [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md).

**Correction, release and resolve.** [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md) (A24 and A25 on the owner's
cases; Active) rests on [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) and grounds on [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) (Challenger to
Columbia) and [CASE-135](../cases.d/CASE-135.md) (the Brookhollow fire): a corrective act, a
procedure and a maintained capacity are three things. [CLAIM-465](../claims.d/CLAIM-465.md) (A24's
line, found in the audit of 2026-10-27; Proposed) grounds on [CASE-135](../cases.d/CASE-135.md) and
complements [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) and [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md): flexibility needs release conditions as
well as escalation, and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s relations and [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md) "name
suspension, not release". [CLAIM-344](../claims.d/CLAIM-344.md) (the owner's frame at U37, A37's
definition; Active) rests on [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md), [CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md) and [CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md): resolve
keeps a policy effective while keeping the capacity to revise it, and mere
persistence is not resolve. [CASE-188](../cases.d/CASE-188.md) (proposed by the owner) illustrates it
as a study separating corrigible resolve from entrenchment. [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md) (A18's,
at the owner's U18; Active; authenticity first), a variant of [CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md),
illustrates it too: a password given to a friend in calm and refused under
temptation, beside the same restriction imposed without consent; its
summary says the warrant for the override "can be the agent's own earlier
authorization, and that it is read from history, not present behaviour". [CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md) (the
owner's question at U41, A41's answer; Active; distributed-agency first)
rests on [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) and [CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md): robustness to compromised constituents
lies in the processes that allocate, audit and revise authority, and can
trade off against self-governance.

**Learned authority and governance-maintained anxiety.** [CLAIM-443](../claims.d/CLAIM-443.md) (the
owner's, 2026-10-23; Active) rests on [CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md): a learned prior over which
processes govern is a candidate mechanism for durable authority, "but
persistence is not amortization". [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md) (the fourth synthesis pass;
Open) asks whether a disorder that outlasts its trigger is a protective
lock-in at the individual scale; [QUESTION-292](../questions.d/QUESTION-292.md), split from it by the status
review of 2026-10-27, is Answered by [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md). [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md) (the owner's,
2026-10-23; Proposed) answers [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md), resting on [CLAIM-443](../claims.d/CLAIM-443.md), [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)
and [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md) and using [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md): some persistent anxiety may be
governance-maintained, since under the avoidance that authority selects
danger and safety predict the same observation. [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) (the owner's;
Proposed; distributed-agency first) rests on [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md) and carries the
mechanism to institutions and states: a protective regime becomes
self-maintaining when its interventions shift the evidence and authority
that judge it.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim.

- **Governance-maintained anxiety as escalation without release.** That
  [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)'s regime is [CLAIM-465](../claims.d/CLAIM-465.md)'s case. Wrong on the components' own terms:
  [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)'s regime has a release condition, disconfirmation, which the
  avoidance it selects keeps from being observed. The narrower link, that a
  release condition is idle where the escalated regime controls the evidence
  that would meet it, is [CASE-036](../cases.d/CASE-036.md)'s note of 2026-10-22.
