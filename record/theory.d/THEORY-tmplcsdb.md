# THEORY-tmplcsdb: Signals of processing quality from different senses, such as fluency, familiarity and coherence, pool into one content-nonspecific feeling, and that pooled feeling contributes to the unity of conscious experience across modalities, alongside the integration of specific features; in support, coherence in one modality shifts judgments of regularity, familiarity and possibility about unrelated targets in another

## Source

Winkielman, Ziembowicz & Nowak (2015), "The coherent and fluent mind",
*Frontiers in Psychology* 6:83, [LIT-190](../literature.d/LIT-190.md), read in full in [NOTE-146](../notes.d/NOTE-146.md). None of
the cited empirical studies, Ziembowicz et al. (2013) included, was read;
their findings are as the article states them.

## What was actually shown

**The proposal (pp. 1–3).** The standard account puts cross-modal unity in
the integration of specific features at perceptual or decisional stages
(McGurk, double flash, bouba/kiki). The article adds a route through
processing dynamics: signals of processing quality from different
modalities combine into one global, "free-floating" feeling of how well
processing is going, and that feeling is then interpreted for its source
and relevance. "The creation of a unified consciousness is facilitated by
an experiential mechanism that combines signals of processing quality"
(p. 3).

**The evidence it reports (p. 2).** In Ziembowicz et al. (2013), across
three experiments, participants judged targets in one modality while
exposed to regular or irregular backgrounds in another: tone-grammar
melodies with visual figures, or the reverse. The kinds of regularity were
unrelated (a tone-sequence grammar against 3-D realisability). Coherent
backgrounds raised judgments of "regular" (Experiment 1), "familiar"
(Experiment 2) and "possible" (Experiment 3), whatever the judgment type
or target modality. No effect sizes are given.

**Why a feeling.** In other paradigms, participants report changed ease,
effort or familiarity in "many (though not all)" experiments, fluency has
physiological markers of positivity, and discounting removes the effect:
telling participants that background music affects their "sense of
familiarity" reduced false recognition across formats (Fazendeiro et al.
2005). Fluency and familiarity can be amodal and summed across sources
(Curran & Dien 2003; Miller et al. 2008).

**What could have come out otherwise.** Unrelated coherence in one
modality could have left judgments about the other untouched. As reported,
it did not.

## What this does not say

- **That the transfer runs through experience.** The authors say direct
  evidence is needed that the cross-modal effects involve changed
  subjective experience and that the integration is causal (p. 2). The
  support for the experiential route comes by analogy from other
  paradigms ([NOTE-146](../notes.d/NOTE-146.md), C3).
- **That the feeling is always the one posited.** Under hidden semantic
  coherence, participants "cannot report and re-attribute changing levels
  of fluency" and are "only aware of affective (hedonic) consequences"
  (Topolinski & Strack 2009, p. 2).
- **That unity was measured.** No study cited measures unity of experience.
  The step from "processing-quality feelings pool across modalities" to
  "this facilitates unified consciousness" is the conclusion, not a result
  ([NOTE-146](../notes.d/NOTE-146.md), C6).
- **That a network model explains the data.** The Hopfield-type network
  with a feedback loop on an order parameter (Lewenstein & Nowak 1989), and
  Drogosz & Nowak's mere-exposure simulations with it, are an analogy; no
  model of the cross-modal data is presented.
- **That this is integrated information.** The integration meant is a
  psychological blending of felt quality, not IIT's Φ ([THEORY-tmpfj3d3](THEORY-tmpfj3d3.md)).

## Connections

- **Unity.** [THEORY-063](THEORY-063.md) (Metzinger) puts the unity of experience in the
  integration of one global world-model into a single functional cluster.
  This account offers a candidate psychological contributor to felt unity
  at another level; neither engages the other, and both could be true.
  Kleiner & Ludwig ([THEORY-tmpd2ifs](THEORY-tmpd2ifs.md)) take the subsumptive unity thesis as a
  premise and derive a structure from it, not a mechanism.
- **Global signals.** The pooled feeling is a scalar monitor, not a
  broadcast of content, so it is a different kind of global variable from
  the workspace of [THEORY-096](THEORY-096.md). Treating it as an indicator of consciousness
  in AI ([LIT-056](../literature.d/LIT-056.md)) would be the reader's extrapolation.
