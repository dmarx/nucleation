## What to read for

| Observation | Bears on |
|---|---|
| Do themes converge across users with different prior concerns? | [CLAIM-tmpj9yt0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj9yt0.md) against [CLAIM-tmpv6eti](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpv6eti.md) |

| What is the course after use stops: persistence or decline? | [CLAIM-tmpca3a7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpca3a7.md)'s withdrawal test |

## Where to start

Østergaard (2023) and Morrin et al. (2025), both to be found and checked,
then the case series they cite. Find the sources before relying on
anything recalled in the claims.

## Constraint

Clinical reports concern identifiable people. File findings about
patterns, not about individuals.
