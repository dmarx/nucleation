# ADR-009: philosophy-of-language joins the topics

## Context

On 2026-09-26 the close reading of Grindrod, Porter & Hansen, *Distributional
Semantics, Holism, and the Instability of Meaning* ([LIT-208](../literature.d/LIT-208.md)), found that its
`philosophy-of-science` tag was not justified. The paper is philosophy of
language: meaning holism, and how similar two speakers' meanings must be for
communication to succeed. Duhem–Quine appears only as the lineage of the view
it defends. Its other tag, `linguistics`, is blurbed as language "as linguists
pose [it], not as models encode [it]", and this paper is about meaning as
models encode it. So neither of its tags was right. The reader named the gap
and recommended the word, which is the move the record asks for
([ADR-006](ADR-006.md), [ADR-007](ADR-007.md)).

Four other held works are about meaning or assertion in the same sense:

- Marabini on Brandom's inferentialism and the meaning–entitlement connection
  ([LIT-106](../literature.d/LIT-106.md));
- Almagro & Isern-Mas, *Blunting concepts*, on how popularising psychotherapy
  language changes what its words mean ([LIT-214](../literature.d/LIT-214.md));
- Mallory, *Large Language Models Are Stochastic Measuring Devices*
  ([LIT-140](../literature.d/LIT-140.md)), which calls error theory the most defensible view of what LLM
  text means;
- Bottou & Schölkopf, *Borges and AI* ([LIT-178](../literature.d/LIT-178.md)), which denies that a
  language model asserts anything.

## Decision

Add `philosophy-of-language` to `topics`, with a label and a blurb, and add it
to the `philosophy` tag group ([ADR-003](ADR-003.md)).

| work | tags now |
| --- | --- |
| Grindrod et al. | `philosophy-of-language` alone: both old tags were wrong |
| Marabini | `philosophy-of-language` first, then `logic`, then `linguistics` |
| Almagro & Isern-Mas | `philosophy-of-language` first, then `linguistics`, then `ethics` |
| Mallory | `philosophy-of-language` added; `linguistics` removed, as in the cognition pass |
| Bottou & Schölkopf | `philosophy-of-language` added |

`logic` keeps "inferentialist accounts of meaning" in its blurb. Marabini
carries both words, because the paper is about both inference and meaning.

## Alternatives

- **Keep `linguistics` for these works.** That is the nearest wrong word. Its
  own blurb excludes meaning as models encode it, and theories of meaning
  (Brandom, holism) are philosophy rather than linguistics.
- **Stretch `cognition` to mental and linguistic content.** `cognition` is the
  mind as information processing. What a sentence means, and whether a model
  asserts, are different questions from how a mind processes information.
