# LIT-3156: The Nationals Should Stop Claiming Expos History

Bestwick (2023): https://districtondeck.com/posts/the-nationals-should-stop-claiming-expos-history-01gs361hfqsv

## Key takeaways

- **The legacy.** "The legacy of the Washington Nationals goes back not to Canada, but to the rich baseball history" that happened in Washington; "many would attempt to convince us that this legacy belongs to Minnesota or Texas".
- **What the club does.** It has "been awkwardly drawing upon both the Expos and the Senators rather than just picking one and sticking to it"; it honours "Senators greats along with Homestead Grays legends like Josh Gibson and Cool Papa Bell."
- **What he asks.** "harken back instead to the proud Major League tradition that has existed in Washington since 1901." "I'd much rather see a Senators jersey."
