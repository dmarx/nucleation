# ADR-020: thermodynamics joins the topics

## Context

The owner asked how the record covers dissipative structures, then for the
literature to be filed. Three agents filed it in parallel: Prigogine's
programme and the canonical systems, the critics and the fluctuation
theorems, and life as dissipation. Each one, separately, reported the same
gap and proposed the same word. Every work in the second batch had to take
`natural-sciences` as its first tag, which is true of a physics paper and
says nothing about which physics.

The record already held a thermodynamic strand with nowhere to put the
fact: Still et al.'s "Thermodynamics of Prediction", the stochastic
thermodynamics of learning, Landauer 1961 and its commentary, quantum
thermodynamics, the philosophy of statistical mechanics, and [THEORY-026](../theory.d/THEORY-026.md)
and [THEORY-030](../theory.d/THEORY-030.md). Those carried `information-theory` and `natural-sciences`.

## Decision

Add `thermodynamics` to `topics`, beside `natural-sciences`, in no group.
Its blurb: "the second law and its extensions read for themselves —
entropy and entropy production, irreversibility, nonequilibrium steady
states and their stability, dissipative structures, fluctuation theorems
and stochastic thermodynamics, and the thermodynamics of computation and
of life".

**Tagged.** No tag was removed.

- **First**, where `natural-sciences` was standing in for it: Glansdorff &
  Prigogine, Landauer 1975, Martyushev & Seleznev, Dewar 2003, Grinstein &
  Linsker, Kleidon 2009, Jarzynski, Crooks, Seifert, Schneider & Kay,
  Morowitz & Smith, England 2013 and Perunov, Marsland & England.
- **Second**, after the subject: Prigogine's Nobel lecture and Nicolis &
  Prigogine (after `complex-systems`), Schrödinger's *What Is Life?*,
  quantum thermodynamics ([LIT-010](../literature.d/LIT-010.md)), [THEORY-026](../theory.d/THEORY-026.md) and [THEORY-030](../theory.d/THEORY-030.md).
- **After `information-theory` or `learning-theory`:** [LIT-327](../literature.d/LIT-327.md), [LIT-308](../literature.d/LIT-308.md),
  [LIT-328](../literature.d/LIT-328.md), [LIT-360](../literature.d/LIT-360.md) and [LIT-041](../literature.d/LIT-041.md).
- **Last:** [LIT-082](../literature.d/LIT-082.md) (observational entropy), [LIT-114](../literature.d/LIT-114.md) (philosophy of
  statistical mechanics) and Cross & Hohenberg, whose §XIII.B is the
  record's statement against general extremum principles.

Left without it, on purpose: Friston's "Life as we know it", whose free
energy is variational and computes no heat or work; Turing 1952 and the
BZ reaction, which are read for their patterns; and Deacon and Moreno &
Mossio, which are about organization, not thermodynamics.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep `natural-sciences`.** It is the record's word for physics,
  chemistry and biology read outside any machine-learning claim. It
  cannot tell Jarzynski from a planetary-science paper, and the record
  now holds enough thermodynamics that a reader looking for it needs a
  page.
- **A narrower word, `nonequilibrium-thermodynamics`.** It would leave out
  Landauer's principle, quantum thermodynamics and the philosophy of
  statistical mechanics, which are the strand the record already held.
- **`philosophy-of-biology` as well.** One agent proposed it for Deacon,
  Moreno & Mossio and the teleology debate. Two unread books and one
  paper are not yet enough; it waits for a filing that brings more.
