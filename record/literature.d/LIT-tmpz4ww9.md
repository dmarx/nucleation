# LIT-tmpz4ww9: On the mechanics of goal striving: Experimental evidence of coasting and shifting

J. Lukas Thürmer, Michael F. Scheier and Charles S. Carver (2019 online; 2020), *Motivation Science* 6(3):266–274 — DOI-10.1037/mot0000157, PMC8022896

## Key takeaways

- Randomly assigned feedback that one goal is ahead of target is followed
  by worse performance on that goal and better performance on a
  competing goal, in two experiments.
- Ahead-of-target feedback leaves mood better than behind-target feedback,
  though mostly because behind-target feedback lowers it.
- An if-then plan to ignore one's feelings removes coasting and shifting,
  which the authors take as causal evidence for affect's role.

## Standing in the record
