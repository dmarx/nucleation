# LIT-061: Mike Johnson's Institutional Betrayal

Don Moynihan (2026), *Can We Still Govern? (Substack newsletter)* — <https://donmoynihan.substack.com/p/mike-johnsons-institution…>

## Key takeaways

- Moynihan argues that, in the 2026 DHS funding lapse, Speaker Johnson's choice to back the President's House bill and the President's claimed authority to pay TSA staff, over the Senate's bill, surrendered Congress's power of the purse. He treats this as an institutional betrayal.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Moynihan, writing as a public-administration commentator, recounts the state of a Department of Homeland Security shutdown. The Senate passed a bill funding DHS except ICE and CBP. The House, under Johnson, passed instead a Trump-backed bill funding all of DHS, and both chambers recessed. Meanwhile the President announced he would pay TSA employees without naming a legal appropriation source. Moynihan argues that letting the executive both impound appropriated money and spend unappropriated money guts Congress's central check. He quotes Matt Glassman and Josh Chafetz in support.
