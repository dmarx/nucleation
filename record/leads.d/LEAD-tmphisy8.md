# LEAD-tmphisy8: Extend the four-mechanism governance model with a protected anchor placed by a party with interests in what it reads, and measure how far correction answers to the anchor's placement rather than to the world

## The idea

In LEAD-tmpdzbyn's model, with its miscalibrated protected anchor and
slower survey, let the anchor be placed by a party whose payoff depends on
the population's estimate, against the world-drawn anchor of CASE-tmp33vf4.
Vary the placer's interest, and whether the slower survey is made by a
party independent of the placer (QUESTION-482's condition).

Measures: late error on ground truth the population never sees, the bias
of the population's estimate toward the placer's interest, and internal
consistency, which CLAIM-tmpklggb predicts stays high whatever the anchor's
error.

## Checks the record can run

- CLAIM-tmpw11e6 predicts the network converges on the anchor's error. With
  an interested placer, if the error tracks the placer's interest and a
  survey by the same party does not reduce it while an independent survey
  does, protection without independence of placement is shown to fail.
- Negative control: a placer with no interest must reproduce the
  world-drawn case.

The design waits on LEAD-tmpdzbyn's model; stage idea.
