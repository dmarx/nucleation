# LIT-953: Flickering Emergences: The Question of Locality in Information-Theoretic Approaches to Emergence

Thomas F. Varley (2022), *Entropy* 25(1), 54.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-756](../notes.d/NOTE-756.md).*

- **The identity underneath.** Local mutual information
  i(x; y) = log P(x,y)/(P(x)P(y)) is negative whenever P(x,y) < P(x)P(y),
  while its expectation I(X; Y) is never negative. Local excess entropy
  e(x) = i(x_{t−1}; x_t) is negative when the past makes the realised
  future less likely than chance: "The past transiently ceases to inform
  of the future and instead misinforms."
- **Coarse-graining.** A four-node Boolean network, coarse-grained by AND
  over pairs, is emergent on Hoel's measure (log(F̄(X̃)/F̄(X)) ≈ 0.202),
  yet ≈52.73% of informative micro transitions map to misinformative macro
  ones. They are the low-probability transitions, so the average holds.
- **ΦID synergy.** The same toy's macro system has temporal synergy
  ≈0.031 bit on average and a negative local value at 31.25% of its
  sixteen transitions.
- **A connectome.** On one Human Connectome Project matrix with Infomap
  macro-nodes, ≈22.47% of informative micro edges map to misinformative
  macro edges, and ≈80.4% of misinformative edges run between
  communities.
- **How general.** The possibility is argued to hold for any measure built
  on time-delayed mutual information; two further frameworks (Barnett and
  Seth, Chang et al.) are expected to show it, not computed.
- **What a negative moment means is open.** It may be fluctuation or a
  change in causal structure; the statistics alone do not say.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md), and
read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-756](../notes.d/NOTE-756.md). The seed claim, that emergence can
hold on average and fail at particular configurations, is confirmed. The
reading adds that an average verdict on unity gives no verdict about a
moment, which bears on the duration clause of [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) and on the
persistence test of [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md). [THEORY-268](../theory.d/THEORY-268.md) is filed from it: emergence
measured as an expectation belongs to the ensemble of transitions, not to
each moment. It complements [THEORY-222](../theory.d/THEORY-222.md), which holds the average
effective-information gain of the same coarse-graining account.
