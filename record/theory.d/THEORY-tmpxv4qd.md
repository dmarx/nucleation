# THEORY-tmpxv4qd: Among 56 of 88 patients in one DBT intensive outpatient programme, kept because they finished a four-month cycle and gave enough diary data, skills-homework completion in months 1–3 correlated with larger falls in suicide and substance-use urges (r ≈ .35) and phone-coaching frequency with a larger fall in self-harm urges (r = .31); neither correlated with change in how often the behaviours occurred, and only homework separated patients with and without a target behaviour in month 4

## Source

Edwards et al., [LIT-tmpa6pg6](../literature.d/LIT-tmpa6pg6.md), read in full in [NOTE-tmp2u3za](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2u3za.md) from the
version of record posted on the Yale CANLab site: pp. 509–513 and 517–519,
with Tables 2–4.

## What was actually shown

- **Urges (p. 513; Table 4).** Homework completion with change in suicide
  urges r = .35 and in substance-use urges r = .36 (.37 in the table);
  with alcohol urges r = .25, p = .06. Coaching calls with change in
  self-harm urges r = .31. The other pairings were not significant.
- **Behaviour (p. 513).** "Regarding frequency of engagement in target
  behaviours, results reflected no significant associations."
- **Month 4 (p. 513).** Patients with no target behaviour (27) against
  those with one or more (27), controlling for attendance: homework 91.3%
  against 79.8% (p = .02); coaching calls 6.00 against 3.85 (p = .096).

What could have come out otherwise: homework and coaching could have been
unrelated to change, as attendance was.

## What this does not say

- **That homework or coaching causes the change.** Correlational; reverse
  order and confounding by motivation or functioning cannot be excluded
  (p. 518).
- **That they change behaviour.** No association with behaviour
  frequency; the one behavioural difference is homework and any behaviour
  in month 4.
- **That generalization is the mechanism.** No measure of skill use
  between sessions.
- **That it holds for all completers.** 28 of 88 were dropped for missing
  diary data.
- **That the result survives correction.** None was applied (p. 519).
