# THEORY-1688: Jia and colleagues, reviewing cancer neuroscience, describe tumours as actively recruiting and reprogramming the host's nerves (by neurotrophic recruitment, synaptic integration in the brain and perineural invasion) and using sympathetic signalling to build an immunosuppressive microenvironment, a pattern they name tumor neuron hijack, by analogy with immune escape

## The account

Jia and colleagues, reviewing cancer neuroscience, describe tumours as actively recruiting and reprogramming the host's nerves (by neurotrophic recruitment, synaptic integration in the brain and perineural invasion) and using sympathetic signalling to build an immunosuppressive microenvironment, a pattern they name tumor neuron hijack, by analogy with immune escape.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1758](../notes.d/NOTE-1758.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
