# THEORY-tmpdn4s2: Questionnaire items written for morality-as-cooperation's seven domains form seven factors when rated for relevance to moral decisions, in the UK, the US and Turkey, but not when rated as agreement with moral statements, and the two formats cannot be pooled into seven domains

## Source

Curry, Jones Chesters & Van Lissa (2019), [LIT-496](../literature.d/LIT-496.md), read in [NOTE-412](../notes.d/NOTE-412.md) from
the PsyArXiv manuscript. Yilmaz, Harma & Doğruyol (2021), [LIT-513](../literature.d/LIT-513.md), read in
[NOTE-397](../notes.d/NOTE-397.md): the same instrument, translated, by a team outside the programme.

## What was actually shown

**The instrument.** The MAC-Q copies the MFQ's two formats. Relevance asks how
relevant a consideration is when deciding right and wrong. Judgement asks for
agreement with moral statements. The authors wrote 57 Relevance and 97
Judgement items from the theory, then kept three per domain, chosen for
theoretical consistency and item-total correlation within the domain.

**UK, n = 1,392 ([LIT-496](../literature.d/LIT-496.md), Study 1).** Relevance, seven factors: RMSEA .03, CFI
.98, SRMR .03. Judgement, seven factors: RMSEA .06, CFI .87, SRMR .07, which
the paper's own CFI criterion (above .90) rates unacceptable. Both formats as
seven domains: CFI .73. The best combined model keeps fourteen correlated
factors, seven per format (CFI .93).

**US, n = 469 ([LIT-496](../literature.d/LIT-496.md), Study 3a).** Relevance gives seven factors. Judgement
gives five by parallel analysis (Family, Group/Reciprocity, Martial Virtues,
Fairness, Property), and seven only when forced.

**Turkey, n = 445 ([LIT-513](../literature.d/LIT-513.md), Study 1).** Relevance, seven factors: CFI .949,
RMSEA .053, SRMR .046. A single factor fits far worse (CFI .682). Judgement:
CFI .828. Full form: CFI .752.

**What could have come out otherwise.** The Relevance items could have
collapsed to fewer factors in a new language and population. In Turkey they
did not, though a second-order two-factor model also fits (CFI .937).

## What this does not say

- **That the seven are distinct moral kinds.** The domains were written into
  the items, which were then selected for fit within each domain, so
  recovering seven factors is partly a check on construction. The latent
  correlations in Turkey run high: fairness–property .874,
  reciprocity–fairness .808, family–group .800.
- **That the Judgement format measures the same seven.** It does not fit in
  any of the three samples, and the paper's description of the UK result as
  "a good fit for both scales" (p. 23) overstates it.
- **That morality is only about cooperation.** The MAC-Q has no
  non-cooperative item, so it cannot test that. Where non-cooperative items
  appear, the MFQ's purity items, they are moralized ([THEORY-tmps5brs](THEORY-tmps5brs.md)).
- **That the MAC-Q outperforms the MFQ.** It does on alphas, retest and
  Relevance fit. On criterion and incremental validity the authors call the
  two "broadly comparable" (p. 38).
- **That relevance tracks cooperation.** In Turkey the seven subscales
  explain 5.1% of self-reported prosocial intentions. Group is the only unique
  predictor, and it is negative (β = −.17).
