# CASE-tmp7f9bu: Open Horizon

## The case

"The mechanisms that sustain its continued operation gradually become more
influential in its decisions than those expressing its original mission." (A31)

## What it can show

That an organization's operative commitments can persist, change and part from
its founders' without any single member intending the change.
