# THEORY-tmpauuea: The Brussels Collaboration on Bodily Integrity (2024) argues that clinicians may perform nonvoluntary genital cutting or surgery on a prepubescent child, of any sex characteristics, only when it is medically necessary in a strict sense, a serious and time-sensitive threat to physical health with no less invasive or postponable alternative, because what grounds the consensus protecting girls is not a benefit–risk judgment by others but the child's right, held in trust, to make intimate decisions about their own sexual anatomy later, and that right does not vary with sex.

## The account

The Brussels Collaboration on Bodily Integrity (2024) argues that clinicians may perform nonvoluntary genital cutting or surgery on a prepubescent child, of any sex characteristics, only when it is medically necessary in a strict sense, a serious and time-sensitive threat to physical health with no less invasive or postponable alternative, because what grounds the consensus protecting girls is not a benefit–risk judgment by others but the child's right, held in trust, to make intimate decisions about their own sexual anatomy later, and that right does not vary with sex.
