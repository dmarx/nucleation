summary: >-
  Jawaid & Mansuy (2019). A short review from Mansuy's laboratory, the proponents' side. Rodent studies show that parental trauma, stress, drugs and in-utero insults pass behavioural phenotypes to offspring, in some models to the third or fourth generation, and that parental environmental enrichment passes benefits, and can prevent the transmission of trauma's effects. Germline dependence is shown by IVF and by injecting sperm RNA, which reproduces the phenotypes; non-coding RNAs are the best candidate carriers. Human studies of genocide survivors' descendants are offered as similar, and the review itself tables them as intergenerational. How a stimulus reaches the germline, and how effects pass beyond the first generation, are unknown. Germline reprogramming and the sceptics' objections are not discussed.
