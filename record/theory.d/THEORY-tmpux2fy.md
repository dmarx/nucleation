# THEORY-tmpux2fy: In relational quantum mechanics as stated in Rovelli (1996), rules (i) and (ii), that there is no state except within a further observer's information and no information without interaction, exclude any contradiction between observers' views: what a third system predicts of two observers' information always agrees with what it finds on interacting with them

## Source

Rovelli (1996), [LIT-1635](../literature.d/LIT-1635.md), arXiv quant-ph/9609002v2, §§II.D and III.D; read
in [NOTE-1227](../notes.d/NOTE-1227.md). What the paper states and shows is [THEORY-1080](THEORY-1080.md), which this
entry extends.

## Why it is Proposed

Consistency is shown for the single S–O–P case, by von Neumann's
correlation (p. 9). That rules (i) and (ii) leave "no way in which
contradiction may develop" is asserted for the general case, not proved.
