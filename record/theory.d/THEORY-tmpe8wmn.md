## The account

Hegde et al. (2025) present KGCL, a data model and controlled natural language for ontology and knowledge-graph changes in which one change object serves both as a prospective patch and a retrospective diff, applied through an agent (Ontobot) that turns curators' requests into pull requests that cannot be merged without a curator's approval, and they report a community survey in which 82% rated understanding ontology changes very important and 8% were satisfied with existing ways of viewing them.
