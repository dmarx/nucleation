# LIT-2540: Does clinical ethics need a Land Ethic?

Alistair Wardrope (2019). Does clinical ethics need a Land Ethic? *Medicine, Health Care and Philosophy* 22(4), 531–543. DOI-10.1007/s11019-019-09890-x.

## Key takeaways

Clinical ethics should take in Leopold's Land Ethic, read as three components: the community concept (communities have intrinsic value), the ethical sequence (that value extends, not replaces, individual value) and the ecological conscience. The ethical sequence answers the charge of environmental fascism ([NOTE-1898](../notes.d/NOTE-1898.md)).
