# LIT-2691: Agency Laundering and Information Technologies

Rubel, Castro, Pham (2021). *Agency Laundering and Information Technologies*. Algorithms and Autonomy: The Ethics of Automated Decision Systems (Cambridge University Press), ch. 7, pp. 137-. DOI 10.1017/9781108895057.007.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of 2026-11-17; the reading of 2026-11-16 is below.*

*Chapter 7 only.*

- **Definition** (p. 143), in five conditions: the agent is morally
  responsible in a domain; it ensures that a process has causal
  responsibility there; it "ascribes (implicitly or explicitly) morally
  relevant qualities" to the process ("relevance, neutrality,
  reliability"); thereby it obscures its own causal role; and so it "fails
  to adequately account" for what it is responsible for.
- **The wrong** (p. 144): it undermines "the ability of others to ask the
  agent to provide an account."
- **Not every delegation** (pp. 144–145): an honest delegation, "Democratic
  Chair", is not laundering; nor, on the authors' reading, are Uber's goal
  prompts or COMPAS in Loomis.
- **Humans in the loop** (pp. 159–160) "can be a means of laundering just as
  well as automation itself."

## Reading of 2026-11-16

Read in full from the publisher's open-access PDF. The account is
conceptual: a five-condition definition (p. 143) grounded in an
accountability view of responsibility, whose defining wrong is
"forestalling others’ ability to demand an account" and which "does not
require that one infringe one’s substantive role responsibilities" (n. 21).
Four cases test it, two of them as non-laundering, so the concept does not
cover every algorithmic deployment: "Loomis addresses condition (5), and it
is not a case of agency laundering" (p. 155). The mechanism need not be
technological: "any process or socio-technical system can be a mechanism
for laundering" (p. 158), humans in the loop included (p. 160).

What the exchange says of it (A19 §1) is accurate on the cases. Its gloss
in A19 §1 and A26 §V, choices appearing as impersonal technical outputs, is
A's and drops the condition that does the work, the failure to account; A21
§9's link to frictionless processing is A's extension.
