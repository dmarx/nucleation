# LEAD-118: Retry the Unfetched works whose fetch stopped at a PMC or Europe PMC check, through the Europe PMC REST route

## What it would do

For each Unfetched LIT whose status note records a refusal at PMC or
Europe PMC, fetch the full text by the Europe PMC REST route that [ADR-101](../decisions.d/ADR-101.md)
admits, and either read it or record the new refusal. Never send an email
address to the service. The members are listed below; the reading leads
filed with this one name the same works where they bear on a question or
claim.

proceed. A work still refused is recorded as such, and the owner can
supply a copy.
