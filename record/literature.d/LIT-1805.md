# LIT-1805: Towards Optimal Transport with Global Invariances

Alvarez-Melis, D., Jegelka, S., & Jaakkola, T. S. (2019). Towards optimal transport with global invariances. *AISTATS 2019*, PMLR 89, 1870–1879.

Read in full in [NOTE-1333](../notes.d/NOTE-1333.md).

## Why this is here

A6's foundational distinction for [QUESTION-130](../questions.d/QUESTION-130.md): GW treats the registration of whole metric spaces as arbitrary, this treats one global transformation as arbitrary, and lattice gauge theory ([LIT-1820](LIT-1820.md)) a separate frame at every node. Keeping the three apart stops an arbitrary orientation being read as path dependence. The reading confirms the middle term: the paper assumes "an unknown function f in a pre-specified class F which characterizes the global correspondence between spaces" (p. 4), one map for every point. It also shows that the first two terms meet: for maps on a Frobenius sphere, with whitened target vectors of equal norm, this problem is GW with cosine similarity (Lemma 4.3; [THEORY-1135](../theory.d/THEORY-1135.md)). Neither has a frame per node, so neither can carry holonomy.
