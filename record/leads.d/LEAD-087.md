# LEAD-087: Run the study of perspective without collective agency, after saying in advance what distinguishes a shared perspective from correlated responses

## What it would do

Write the criterion that separates a shared perspective from correlated
responses, then run [CASE-145](../cases.d/CASE-145.md).

Where the record names it:

- [CASE-145](../cases.d/CASE-145.md): "the most philosophically important new experiment"

## What would count

A group with a shared perspective by that criterion and no collective
agency supports [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) and answers [QUESTION-260](../questions.d/QUESTION-260.md) by example.

## Note of 2026-11-22: criterion stated, then run

Carried out by the assistant at the coordinator's brief
(scratch/studies/lead-087/).

- **Said in advance.** [TERM-tmpsu4df](../terms.d/TERM-tmpsu4df.md) and [TERM-tmpo4pxh](../terms.d/TERM-tmpo4pxh.md), rival readings of
  what distinguishes a shared perspective from correlated responses,
  committed with the study's thresholds and predictions before any code.
- **Run.** [CASE-tmpez9id](../cases.d/CASE-tmpez9id.md), five populations crossed with a vote, 20
  seeds, and four further settings after the verdict.
- **Result.** No population shares a perspective by the criterion as
  stated: the unperturbed members take up at most 2% of a deviation,
  under the 5% asked ([CLAIM-tmpjmfmd](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmfmd.md)). The evidential reading orders the
  populations as predicted below its stated size; the restorative
  reading is passed by plain conformity and fails A4's test
  ([CLAIM-tmpupq89](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpupq89.md)). The vote gives collective agency, its absence none.
  So what would count, a shared perspective with no collective agency,
  was not found.

What remains, for the owner:

- Choosing between [TERM-tmpsu4df](../terms.d/TERM-tmpsu4df.md) and [TERM-tmpo4pxh](../terms.d/TERM-tmpo4pxh.md), or neither; the run
  tells against the second.
- Whether to state again, in advance, a dependence condition measured by
  the deviants' recovery and the selectivity of the others' response, as
  [CLAIM-tmpjmfmd](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmfmd.md) suggests after the fact, and run it again.
- Populations that exchange states, rules or reasons rather than
  answers, and a vote that feeds back into members' interpretations,
  were not run.
