# LEAD-069: Decide whether the record individuates the Game of Life block and eater, so that the Life constructions meet both halves of the answer to the achievement question

## What it would do

Apply the record's criteria of individuation to the block and the eater of
[CASE-259](../cases.d/CASE-259.md) and [CASE-260](../cases.d/CASE-260.md) and say whether each is a form it individuates.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md): "It does not meet the first half's "a form the record
  individuates", since the record has not said whether a block is one."

## What would count

If they are, [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md) has a formal answer; if not, the constructions
need a different substrate.
