# THEORY-tmp9qlii: Li, Naito and Shirado report that on 65 hidden-profile tasks, multi-agent LLM groups pool distributed facts poorly (30.1% after discussion against 80.7% for one agent with every fact), that the loss lies in surfacing unshared facts rather than integrating them, since forced full disclosure nearly restores accuracy, and that a two-stage exchange-then-decide protocol raises accuracy on a subset of the tasks. `

## The account

Li, Naito and Shirado report that on 65 hidden-profile tasks, multi-agent LLM groups pool
distributed facts poorly (30.1% after discussion against 80.7% for one agent with every fact),
that the loss lies in surfacing unshared facts rather than integrating them, since forced full
disclosure nearly restores accuracy, and that a two-stage exchange-then-decide protocol raises
accuracy on a subset of the tasks.
