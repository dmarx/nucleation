# QUESTION-tmpt2p0s: When does an effective constraint cease to be a good approximation because previously suppressed degrees of freedom become dynamically important?

## Why it is a question

An effective constraint is the relation an eliminated part imposes on
what remains ([TERM-090](../terms.d/TERM-090.md)). It is a good approximation while the eliminated
degrees of freedom stay where the elimination put them. The record holds
the time-scale form of when that is:

- [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md): fast degrees of freedom that settle impose a constraint on
  the slow ones over the window between the two time scales;
- [CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md): eliminating a slowly relaxing interior leaves a
  history-dependent constraint, and separation of time scales decides
  only whether it is approximately memoryless;
- [QUESTION-287](QUESTION-287.md), answered: in a deterministic singularly perturbed system
  whose eliminated variable is fast and contracting, the quasi-steady-state
  reduction predicts the coarse variable with an error bounded uniformly
  in time.

The readings give a form those do not. Silverberg et al. 2015
([NOTE-tmp0l0mn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp0l0mn.md); [THEORY-tmpixfqm](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpixfqm.md)): the square twist's crease pattern admits no rigid
folding motion, yet the sheet folds by bending its facets, a hidden
degree of freedom with its own energy scale. Their thesis is that the
number of degrees of freedom is "a variable quantity that changes with
the experimental energy scale". Below the bending scale facet planarity
is a constraint; above it, a degree of freedom; and the ratio of bending
to crease stiffness decides whether the structure is bistable. The
separation is of energies, not times, and the reading notes that it maps
onto a time-scale separation only where stiffer modes also relax faster,
which the paper does not discuss. Stern, Pinson and Murugan ([NOTE-tmp9dvve](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp9dvve.md);
[THEORY-tmpq2qdv](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpq2qdv.md)) give a second sign: for soft sheets careful actuation "needs to be
maintained until a larger folding angle", so how long the flat-state
constraint structure governs depends on stiffness.

## What would answer it

Conditions, stated on the loads or energies a system meets and on the
stiffnesses of its suppressed modes, under which an effective constraint
holds to a stated accuracy, and the threshold past which a suppressed
mode becomes a freedom; and whether those conditions reduce to the
time-scale conditions the record holds, or are independent of them. For
organizations, what plays the energy scale: the size of a demand or
shock against the cost of reopening a settled arrangement.

## What would not answer it

A further case of a time-scale reduction that holds, which the record
already has; or a case of a constraint failing with no account of which
suppressed degree of freedom became important and why.

## Note of 2026-11-05: the resource-bounded realizability readings

The pasted synthesis on resource-bounded realizability reads the square twist ([CASE-tmpez9ok](../cases.d/CASE-tmpez9ok.md)) as a case of this question. In a rigid-facet model, admitting facet bending enlarges the admissible process class; in an elastic model the bending was always there, at a high energy cost. A: "These descriptions are compatible if the first is understood as a low-energy effective model of the second." A proposes "a hierarchy of effective constraint theories indexed by resource scale", in which what acts as a hard constraint at low resources becomes a penetrable barrier or a further mode at higher ones, and asks a resource-bounded theory to say "which impossibilities are structural within the declared model and which are effective exclusions at a given scale".

That bears on [CLAIM-tmpuxuo4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpuxuo4.md): its structurally frustrated sets are frustrated relative to the declared configurations, and a change of scale that admits new ones is, in its terms, a change of architecture. A's hierarchy is a proposal; it gives no conditions of the kind the question asks for. No status changes.
