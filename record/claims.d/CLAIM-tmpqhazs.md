# CLAIM-tmpqhazs: The hyperarticle atlas can be an account only as an account over accounts: a cross-line SYNTHESIS integrating each scope's account, which needs every scope to have one first

Two things stop the atlas from being an account as it stands, both stated
by A65 itself:

- its outlines add joins: they "may propose transitions that no existing
  ARG or CLAIM establishes", and an account adds nothing;
- its fibers are starting sets, not closures (its section VI), so an
  account built on them would fail the fidelity test of leaving out an
  in-force component of its scope.

The synthesis review already provides the form it could take: when a line's
account falls into parts, "the line's account [becomes] one over them, with
the part accounts in `integrates`". The atlas is that at the level of the
record. The steps are:

1. a current account for each of the fifteen lines, starting with the four
   new ones ([SYNTHESIS-111](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-111.md) is the candidate for self-correction), which is
   also the fiber closure A65 leaves open;
2. then the atlas's account, integrating the fifteen, with each of the
   outlines' transitions filed as a claim where it passes [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s test
   and listed as not stated where it does not.

This is compatible with [CLAIM-tmpcd1e0](CLAIM-tmpcd1e0.md): a series work now, and its account once the
line accounts exist. The assistant's lean is both, in that order.
