# LIT-tmp39gs5: Toward an integrative psychometric model of emotions

Jens Lange, Jonas Dalege, Denny Borsboom, Gerben A. van Kleef & Agneta H.
Fischer (2020), *Perspectives on Psychological Science* 15(2):444–468 —
DOI-10.1177/1745691619895057

## Key takeaways

- **Three properties** (pp. 445–448). From affect-program theories,
  distinct emotions. From constructionist theories, variation between and
  within persons. From appraisal theories, causal relations among a
  multitude of components. A psychometric model is a construct theory,
  so a model with all three would help unify the theories.
- **Latent-variable models fail** (pp. 448–455). A reflective model makes
  the emotion a common cause separate from its components, and implies
  local independence, which evidence contradicts. A formative model makes
  the emotion an index of its components, which leaves the components'
  relations uninformative.
- **The network model** (pp. 455–458). "For emotions, this would imply
  that the network of causal relationships between emotion components
  represents the emotion" (p. 456); "the network constitutes the emotion"
  (p. 456). A distinct emotion is a small-world community of clusters,
  weakly emergent: "a stable system can emerge independently of which
  emotion component was activated first or whether multiple components
  were activated simultaneously (i.e., the emergent state is autonomous).
  These systems of interactions then force each emotion component into a
  specific state (i.e., weak downward causation)" (p. 457).
- **Prediction: duration** (p. 460). "As the connections are strong, the
  components continuously reactivate each other, sustaining the activity
  of the network. Thereby, the duration of the emotion is extended, and
  it becomes more difficult to switch back to a deactivated state." This
  is hysteresis, and "In less strongly connected networks, hysteresis
  does not occur." Appraised relevance may strengthen the connections.
- **Limits they name** (pp. 460–461). Edge weights say nothing about how
  far components are endorsed. Cross-sectional networks can hide
  different persons' structures. Cross-sectional, contemporaneous and
  temporal networks can disagree in sign. Estimation is exploratory.

([CLAIM-tmpz8lli](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpz8lli.md), [TERM-tmp8gbrt](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8gbrt.md)), and for the measurement question

record holds. Lange et al. give persistence a mechanism, self-sustaining
activation in a strongly connected network with hysteresis. But what
persists is a pattern of components, not one perspective's ranking
prevailing over others, and the model has no sign. The bearing is set out
in [NOTE-tmp7qgo0](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp7qgo0.md).
