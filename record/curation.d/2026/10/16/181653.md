LITs, each with a `Read` NOTE and no THEORY citing it as `source`. Codes
below are the temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

## What did not enter, and why

- **Pandemonium ([LIT-427](../../record/literature.d/LIT-427.md), [NOTE-360](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-360.md)).** A proposal written before the runs
  it promises. Its results are three sentences in the discussion, one of
  which reports that subdemon selection "did not do what we hoped". Its
  design argument for modularity is an assertion. It stays named in
  [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) as the decomposition's starting point.
- **Singh's exposition ([LIT-444](../../record/literature.d/LIT-444.md), [NOTE-339](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-339.md)).** Uncritical by design, with
  no implementation and no argument for the theory. Its one firm claim,
  that the Society of Mind has not been implemented, is historical and is
  better carried by the reading than by a THEORY.

So fifteen of seventeen readings became sources, which fits the owner's
hypothesis. Two caveats. Three of the twelve rest on works the readings
call proposals ([LIT-410](../../record/literature.d/LIT-410.md), [LIT-432](../../record/literature.d/LIT-432.md)) or a précis ([LIT-649](../../record/literature.d/LIT-649.md)), and each says so
and stays `Proposed`. And the two that entered nothing are the group's two
works with no claim of their own to test.

## Choices made

- **The coupling point is filed apart from the marks.** Adams and Aizawa's
  readings rate the logical point strong and the two marks assumed. One
  document would have had to be `Proposed` for the marks while the record
  believes the point. [CLAIM-172](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-172.md) and [CLAIM-173](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-173.md) now ground on the coupling
  THEORY instead of the bare [LIT-455](../../record/literature.d/LIT-455.md).
- **Two notes said no THEORY.** [NOTE-337](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-337.md) (frames) says the memo is "a
  source of hypotheses, not of support". [THEORY-462](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-462.md) takes only the
  half its reading calls correct, monotonicity, and marks the no-repair
  claim as the memo's assertion. [NOTE-611](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-611.md) (word learning) said no THEORY
  because the findings are specific to a small corpus and the record held
  nothing they correct. The second reason concerns existing THEORYs, so the
  account is filed `Proposed` with its scale in the title.
- **Active.** The coupling point, the case against machine-table states,
  and the human iterated-learning finding, each with its scope in the title.
  The machine-table account is a philosophical argument the reading rates
  strong against its stated target, and it leaves functionalism standing.
- **No `answers` edges.** [QUESTION-035](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-035.md) (when a combined form is one system)
  is where the coupling point bears, but [CLAIM-172](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-172.md) deliberately sidesteps
  parthood, so the THEORY constrains a route the record does not take.
  [QUESTION-046](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-046.md) (a criterion for group experience) is where Chalmers's
  directness criterion bears, and [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) already carries that
  application as the record's own.
- **Overlap with part 1.** Part 1 holds [LIT-097](../../record/literature.d/LIT-097.md) (the extended mind),
  [LIT-398](../../record/literature.d/LIT-398.md) (True Believers) and [LIT-402](../../record/literature.d/LIT-402.md) (K-lines). The accounts here were
  written not to state those works' theses. If part 1 files the 1998
  parity thesis, it is the account [THEORY-512](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-512.md) revises, and the three
  rivals edges here should be extended to it.
