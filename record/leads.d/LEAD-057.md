# LEAD-057: On one dynamical model, compare a fixed preexisting partition, one recovered from measured dynamics, and one produced by coupling to an apparatus, and ask whether they make different counterfactual predictions

## What it would do

Pick one model and work the three partitions; say which of the three any
answer is about, as the note of 2026-11-07 asks.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md): "whether the three accounts make different counterfactual
  predictions, and whether any evidence distinguishes ontological
  constitution from ordinary causal modification."

## What would count

Different counterfactual predictions answer [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md) and say what
evidence would decide among them; identical ones say the dispute is not
empirical.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out on one model

Done by a delegated lead worker. The model: a four-unit linear Gaussian VAR(1) with intrinsic pairs and an apparatus that pairs the units the other way, chosen as the smallest system with two disjoint pairings and a coupling an apparatus can turn up or down. Five interventions, a gain sweep and a variant with probe states, filed as CASE-tmp06fbt; script, tests and results under scratch/studies/lead-057/. The reading, a dilemma for the apparatus-dependent account, is CLAIM-tmpy1gmq. Every answer is said to be about dependence on an arrangement, the third relation of QUESTION-284's note of 2026-11-07.

What remains: the other two relations (relations helping fix their relata, synchronic part–whole constitution) are not touched; only one licence a partition gives, block-local prediction, is tested; and the model is linear with stipulated parameters. Whether the lead is done is the owner's call.
