# TERM-012: cross-scale concordance

## Definition

As first stated (A249): a graded relational property, not a Pareto
improvement, of how an encompassing agent's governing organization affects the
viable futures of its constituents and environmental dependencies. Later split
into internal, dependency and affected-party concordance (A263).

## Why it is set aside

The audit (A276): "Evaluative standards remain underspecified." Five
definitions in under a day is the drift a TERM exists to make visible.
