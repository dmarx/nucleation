# CLAIM-tmp9ras2: A reconstruction chain reorganizes observational constraints rather than only accumulating metric error: a text can change greatly while keeping a few consequential constraints, and one changed constraint can change the act

## The claim

A75: a chain X_0 ⇝ X_1 ⇝ ⋯ ⇝ X_n "is not simply the accumulation of metric
error. It's a sequence of **reorganizations of the observational constraints
that constitute the communicative object**. This offers an explanation for
something our earlier metric formulation obscured: a text can change
dramatically while retaining a relatively small set of highly consequential
relational constraints. Conversely, changing one crucial constraint—such as the
speaker's alignment with the audience—may change the identity of the
communicative act even if most other observables remain stable."

## What it changes

It limits [CLAIM-tmpghha4](CLAIM-tmpghha4.md): a scalar error can be small while the act changes, and large
while it does not. Manuscript §8: "We propose observing how selected invariants,
decision risks, and compatibility structures evolve together; their
trajectories need not coincide." The typology (constraints survive, drop,
sharpen, or appear) did not reach the manuscript.
