# THEORY-tmpuynw0: In laboratory avoidance learning in healthy humans, an avoidance response that remains available during extinction persists, in some participants for hundreds of unpunished trials, and keeps expectancy of the aversive outcome higher than in people denied it; the more effective and the cheaper the response, the slower it extinguishes

## Source

Mancinelli & Bach (preprint 2025; NBR 2026), [LIT-1477](../literature.d/LIT-1477.md), read in full from
the PsyArXiv preprint in [NOTE-1134](../notes.d/NOTE-1134.md). What the review reports, with its
ratings and counts, is [THEORY-901](THEORY-901.md), which this entry extends. The primary
studies were not read.

## The claim

The review states as a phenomenon of human avoidance learning, rated A,
that an avoidance response left available during extinction persists and
protects threat expectancy from extinction (nine studies): in two older
studies 42% and 47% of participants still avoided by the 500th would-be-
unpunished trial, and people allowed to avoid kept higher expectancy of
the aversive outcome in a later test than people denied the option. It
states, also rated A, that less effective or more effortful responses give
less protection from extinction (six studies, one exception). [NOTE-1134](../notes.d/NOTE-1134.md)
records both as the review's claim C3, strong.

The authors explain the persistence by effective avoidance leaving fewer
chances to detect that the contingency has changed, and mark that
explanation "putatively". This entry does not carry the explanation, only
the phenomena stated as general for the laboratory paradigm.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried from [THEORY-901](THEORY-901.md) unchanged: a study, preferably
in people with an anxiety disorder as well as healthy controls, that
varies the effectiveness of an available avoidance response and measures
threat expectancy trial by trial. [QUESTION-327](../questions.d/QUESTION-327.md) asks the same. Further
healthy-volunteer studies with signalled, instructed button responses
cannot settle it.
