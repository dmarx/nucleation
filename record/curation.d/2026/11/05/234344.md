The owner asked the assistant to "proceed systematically filling all of
those identified gaps". One gap was that the record's state-level
aid-dependency claims rested on review essays. This branch sought
identified state-level evidence.

- **Read.** Knack, aid dependence and the quality of governance
  ([LIT-tmp0gnxg](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0gnxg.md)), from World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 2396.
  The journal version was not read.
  - The finding: an instrumented cross-section finds more aid going with
    less improvement in governance.
  - Technical assistance carries most of the effect.
  - Steady aid harms more than erratic aid of the same mean.
  - The paper's own errors are recorded: two standard errors for one
    estimate, no first stage, and a gap in its section numbering.
