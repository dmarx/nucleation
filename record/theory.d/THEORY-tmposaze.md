# THEORY-tmposaze: Barad holds agential realism to be naturalized metaphysics justified by quantum mechanics and applied once, to the one reality, with its scope extended to the lifeworld; she rejects both reading it as an analogy and reading it as a template that quantum mechanics and other domains each instantiate

## Source

Jaksland (2024), LIT-tmpvh3kt, pp. 371–373 and note 21; read in
NOTE-tmpwiza2.

## What was actually shown

Jaksland separates three conceptions. *Analogy*: quantum concepts used
metaphorically outside physics. *Template*: "an overarching metaphysical
template that is claimed to be instantiated by quantum mechanics and which
may also be instantiated in other domains", its concepts "content-neutral
generalizations of their quantum mechanical counterparts" (p. 372).
*Naturalized metaphysics*: "constrained and justified by science
irrespective of the context in which this metaphysics is applied".

Barad's text rules out the first two. Against analogy: "I am not
interested in drawing analogies between particles and people, the micro and
the macro, the scientific and the social, nature and culture; rather, I am
interested in understanding the epistemological and ontological issues
that quantum physics forces us to confront" (2007, 24). Against the
template, which would stratify ontology into realms: "quantum mechanics is
not a theory that applies only to small objects … As far as we know, the
universe is not broken up into two separate domains" (2007, 85). Note 21:
agential realism "is only deployed and applied once, as an account of
fundamental reality. It thus never leaves physics, but rather extends its
scope from there to the macroscopic contexts." Hollin et al. (2017) read it
as a template, and Jaksland reads against them.

The reading could have come out otherwise: Barad could have licensed
structural transfer, as generalized quantum theory does (note 22). Her
text does not.

## What this does not say

- That Barad's conception is right, or that agential realism is correct
  quantum mechanics (THEORY-tmpggk6i).
- That the template conception is illegitimate in itself. It is a
  coherent way to use the concepts; it is not Barad's, and its use in a
  domain then needs that domain's own warrant.
- What follows for the lifeworld. That turns on metaphysical approximation
  (THEORY-tmprm5rv).
