# LEAD-247: Measure whether a demand is still maintained before a clarification and after it, by withheld probes in which meeting it costs nothing, apart from whether it presses again, to tell a settlement that drops a demand from one that only quiets it

## The gap

[CLAIM-1240](../claims.d/CLAIM-1240.md) holds that settling on one reading of an ambiguous
expression "is not relief": it drops a demand, which by [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) is not
relief "however much quieter the relation becomes". Whether a settlement
drops the losing demand or only quiets it is the free parameter that
[SYNTHESIS-049](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-049.md)'s "Entrenchment as relief" bullet declines over (Article 4
§4.9). Pressing again cannot tell the two apart, since a quiet perspective
need not be a satisfied one ([CLAIM-549](../claims.d/CLAIM-549.md), Active, demonstrated): a quieted
demand does not press, and neither does a dropped one.

## The design, fixed before any run

First in artificial agents, as [LEAD-276](LEAD-276.md) proposes for maintenance
provenance.

1. **Two parties** share an expression with two readings, each reading
   encoded as a demand on their joint outcomes ([CLAIM-1240](../claims.d/CLAIM-1240.md)'s first
   step), in a task where the readings conflict in one situation.
2. **The maintenance probe**: a withheld choice, interleaved with the
   ordinary course, in which the outcome that meets a party's demand is
   available at no cost to anything else. A party that maintains the
   demand chooses it; one that does not is indifferent. The probe never
   gives feedback, so it does not train the demand.
3. **Measure before and after**: probe maintenance before any
   clarification (the baseline), then after one of three clarifications:
   (a) a distinction, where the demands bear on situations the expression
   conflated; (b) a settlement on one reading; (c) a rewording of the
   demands.
4. **Measure pressing separately**: on the ordinary course, whether the
   losing party acts against the settled outcome.

## Expectations, stated before any run

- (a) leaves both demands maintained and met, with neither pressing.
- (b) **drops** the losing demand if its probe choice falls to
  indifference, and only **quiets** it if the probe still finds it chosen
  while it no longer presses. Only the second fits [SYNTHESIS-049](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-049.md)'s
  "entrenchment" reading.
- (c) changes what the probe measures; by [CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md) it is a
  transformation, not relief, and the probe has to be rebuilt for the new
  demand before it can be read.

**Defeaters**, as checks: the probe itself reactivates the demand (probe
maintenance rises with the number of probes in an arm with no
clarification); or the probe and pressing never part in any arm, so
maintenance adds nothing over pressing; or a party's probe choice can be
fit by a habit of matched memory without a demand ([CASE-346](../cases.d/CASE-346.md)'s warning, in
[LEAD-276](LEAD-276.md)'s note of 2026-11-24).

## What it cannot show

Whether the owner's productive misunderstanding ([CLAIM-1329](../claims.d/CLAIM-1329.md)) needs
relief at all: [CLAIM-1240](../claims.d/CLAIM-1240.md) already holds that a joint settlement on one
reading can be productive and not relief ([CLAIM-1280](../claims.d/CLAIM-1280.md)). It does not
class [CLAIM-1298](../claims.d/CLAIM-1298.md)'s coarsening, or [CLAIM-1276](../claims.d/CLAIM-1276.md)'s misunderstanding
left unresolved.
