# SYNTHESIS-038: The organizational-normativity line is four accounts that meet at its root questions and the older parts' questions: what a normative judgment needs; the wrong beyond harm and becoming normatively less informed; rights, demands and commitments; and accountability without blame and normative governability; what wholes and parts owe and claim, and what those who govern, restrict or answer for an arrangement owe, are the line dependence-and-domination, whose entries that carry this line second are integrated here; the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the organizational-normativity line, "Normative judgment about organizations", split from will-organization (ADR-089), where it was a part account. Since version 4 (2026-11-15) it is the line's account over the accounts of its parts (ADR-083, ADR-084); versions 1 to 3 mapped what is now the first two parts directly. At version 5 the line's third and fourth parts, SYNTHESIS-092 and SYNTHESIS-093, became the line dependence-and-domination, at the split of this line (ADR-100), and that line's account is SYNTHESIS-095. Version 6 (2026-11-17) adds the two parts filed at the interim synthesis review of that day, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and records that SYNTHESIS-091 and SYNTHESIS-094 were revised then (each to version 3). "Organization" is TERM-074's sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none. Every link below is attributed to the part account or component that states it.*

  SYNTHESIS-091 at QUESTION-129 and QUESTION-454, into SYNTHESIS-094 at

candidate to split, and by these counts this line is one: three of its
four parts keep most of their relations inside. Two things the counts do
not settle: the new parts are a day old, and nothing on the record has
yet had occasion to point into them; and every relation they send out

QUESTION-456 or QUESTION-449) or, once, to CLAIM-662, much as the older
parts met at QUESTION-128 and QUESTION-129 before the split. The review reports the
candidate to the owner; a split is a decision about the lines vocabulary
(ADR-082), not this account's.

four parts, storing front-matter relations as `line_parts.py` does, by
the part each starts in: 93 of 102 fell within parts (24 in the first,
38 in the second, 29 in the third, 2 in the fourth). Of the nine across,

mostly closed, so by ADR-083's signal the line was a candidate to split.
The assistant proposed a split in two and the owner approved it on
2026-11-15 ("yes on the two line split"); ADR-100 records it. The counts
are kept here as that record.
