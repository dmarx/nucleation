# LIT-tmp7sbn6: Information Processing Capacity of Dynamical Systems

Dambre, Verstraeten, Schrauwen & Massar (2012). Scientific Reports 2: 514. DOI 10.1038/srep00514.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A invokes it without naming it at A15 for the memory and nonlinearity trade-off in reservoir computing. It bears on the exchange's account of what a reservoir near criticality can compute.

Read on 2026-11-21 in [NOTE-tmpkp5gf](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpkp5gf.md); the reading is [THEORY-tmpr5fuq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpr5fuq.md). A15's
characterization is accurate, with three refinements: the bound holds
without fading memory, which is the condition for equality; the regimes
that redistribute capacity in the paper are input scalings, not distances
from the transition; and the linear readout is built into the measure.
