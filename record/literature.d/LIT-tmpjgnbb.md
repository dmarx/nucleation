# LIT-tmpjgnbb: Why CSCW applications fail: problems in the design and evaluation of organizational interfaces

Jonathan Grudin (1988), *Proceedings of the 1988 ACM Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative Work* (CSCW '88), 85–93 — DOI-10.1145/62266.62273

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* As its abstract is reported: applications that support cooperative work have received heavy investment and fallen far short of expectations. Three factors are named:

- the disparity between those who will benefit from an application and those who must do additional work to support it;
- the lack of management intuition for such applications;
- the extreme difficulty of evaluating them.

The first is the cost-and-benefit argument the design-rationale literature takes as its own.
