# NOTE-tmp6llzw: Rosa (2003; Spanish 2011) — Social Acceleration: Ethical and Political Consequences of a Desynchronized High-Speed Society

## Contribution

The theory from which the book grew.

## Key results

1. p. 9: three dimensions, technological acceleration, acceleration of social change and of the pace of life.
2. p. 21: "permanecer detenido es equivalente a quedarse atrás".
3. p. 21: people keep pace with change "para evitar la pérdida de opciones y conexiones".
4. p. 22: acceleration cycles "son un proceso cerrado y autoimpulsado".

## Assumptions

Modernity is characterized by acceleration across the three dimensions.

## Concepts

Social acceleration; acceleration cycle; slippery slope; desynchronization.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmpbypt7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbypt7.md)**: corroborates its competitive mechanism (result 2, formally voluntary, strategically compelled) and its measure (result 3, keeping pace to avoid losing options and connections, which is what a non-adopter loses).
