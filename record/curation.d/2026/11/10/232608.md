**[CLAIM-633](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-633.md)'s derivation.** [CLAIM-524](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-524.md)'s network computes u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ with
resource ½ Σ(uᵢ + vᵢ). By AM–GM the resource is at least √(1 − p) + √p,
where p = u₂v₂, and that bound peaks at √2 when p = ½. A budget below √2
therefore splits the zero-loss set into a piece near p = 0 and a piece
near p = 1. Swapping the paths sends p to 1 − p and keeps the resource.
Each piece retracts to its endpoint without the resource rising: first
along a hyperbola to its balanced point, then along balanced points. No
entry names the swap, the orbit or the exchange. [CLAIM-263](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-263.md)'s step 1
quotients the full symmetry group. The claim is Proposed, and its
wording is the assistant's.

**Arm A.** No claim on either line only maps its components. [CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md),
[CLAIM-632](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-632.md) and [CLAIM-620](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) have "Admission" sections, and their premises have

**[CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md)'s admission case meets its own defeater.** The village water
scheme in its "Admission" section was offered as a stipulated case
separating the claim from its premises. It could plainly occur. As
written, it meets the first clause of [CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md)'s `defeated_if`: every
condition is judged met before withdrawal, and the scheme declines within
one turnover. When [CLAIM-600](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) was an admitted synthesis, the case showed only
separation. Since [ADR-084](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-084.md) it is a claim again, and the skill's step for a
realistic separating case applies: the case is possible evidence against
the claim. [SYNTHESIS-032](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-032.md) lists it under "Not stated on the record".
The claim is unchanged, and its status is for the owner.
