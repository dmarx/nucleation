# THEORY-tmpypzp0: Ellul holds that technique, the totality of efficient methods and not machines alone, has become the milieu in which modern humans must live, autonomous, unified, universal and totalizing, progressing by self-augmentation and not yet able to correct its own errors, so that particular choices count for little

## The account

Ellul holds that "Technique is the complex and complete milieu in which human beings must live", with the characteristics "Autonomy, Unity, Universality, Totalization", progressing by self-augmentation; it "is not yet able to control its errors and dysfunctions", and "particular choices no longer have much significance".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmp8721q](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp8721q.md), results 1–6. Corroborates [CLAIM-tmpbypt7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbypt7.md): outside the technical city "the only choices left" are two (result 5).

## What it does not say

That no one could build the capacity to correct it: Ellul says such a reactive capability may be emerging.
