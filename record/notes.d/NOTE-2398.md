# NOTE-2398: Wamsley — Backup Driver Of Autonomous Uber SUV Charged With Negligent Homicide In Arizona

## Contribution

The date and charge of the prosecution.

## Key results

- **The charge.** "Rafaela Vasquez has been charged with negligent
  homicide in the death of a pedestrian struck by an autonomous Uber SUV
  in March 2018." She "appeared in court on Tuesday in Maricopa County,
  Ariz. She pleaded not guilty to the charge".
- **The NTSB, as quoted.** "The Uber Advanced Technologies Group did not
  adequately recognize the risk of automation complacency and develop
  effective countermeasures to control the risk of vehicle operator
  disengagement, which contributed to the crash".
- **The company.** "Last March, an Arizona prosecutor determined that
  Uber was not criminally liable in Herzberg's death."

## Assumptions

- A news report of an arraignment.

## Concepts

None beyond the charge.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-751](../claims.d/CLAIM-751.md)** (bears on). The charge came ten months after the NTSB
  adopted its report (19 November 2019), which had named the company's
  safety culture as contributing. The no-blame finding upstream did not
  keep the criminal process from settling on the operator; the statute
  ([THEORY-1999](../theory.d/THEORY-1999.md)) bars the report only from civil damages suits.

## Limitations

- It does not say how the charge was brought (by grand jury or
  otherwise); the record does not rely on that.
