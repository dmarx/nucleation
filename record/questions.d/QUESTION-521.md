# QUESTION-521: Is there a principled distinction between a collective that is a pseudo-self and an ecosystem of such collectives that is not one, or is the difference a function of the order or scale from which the system is analyzed?

## The question

The owner's words: "the JD2 declination has me wondering if maybe we're
getting closer to identifying a reasonable distinction between a collective
that satisfies a pseudo-self and an ecosystem of collectives that do but the
ecosystem itself does not? or maybe this is a function of the order/scale
from which the perspective is analyzing the system?"

Two things are on the record. A pseudo-self is a multi-perspectival
organization "causally integrated relative to a declared probe family and
history" ([TERM-166](../terms.d/TERM-166.md)). A self-like organization "need not be unified by
convergence on a single perspective: it can be unified ecologically"
([CLAIM-1015](../claims.d/CLAIM-1015.md)). The question is whether a plurality of pseudo-selves can be
ecologically unified without itself being a pseudo-self, and, if so, what
marks the difference: something in the system, or the regime it is analyzed
under.

## Answers filed

Two, as alternatives, both the assistant's: [CLAIM-1074](../claims.d/CLAIM-1074.md) (a structural
criterion: a whole-level slow variable maintained as the whole's) and
[CLAIM-1153](../claims.d/CLAIM-1153.md) (the difference is relative to the analyzing regime, and at a
fixed regime there is none in kind). [LEAD-225](../leads.d/LEAD-225.md) would tell them apart.

## What would count as an answer

A criterion that classifies a plurality as pseudo-self or ecosystem under a
declared regime, with a case where the two classifications differ; or a
showing that every such criterion flips with the regime's horizon or probe
family, so that the distinction is the analyst's.

## Note of 2026-11-23: criteria that might divide the cases

<!-- inactive-ok-block: CLAIM-1048 CLAIM-1126 CLAIM-1172 — Proposed or new; the criteria, the answer they come near and the objection they meet -->
A64b of the exchange's branch of 2026-11-23 08:51 proposed five criteria for a pseudo-self ([CLAIM-1048](../claims.d/CLAIM-1048.md)), which bears on this question. Integration alone does not divide pseudo-self from ecosystem, given [CLAIM-1172](../claims.d/CLAIM-1172.md). Consequential governance (authority allocated among the members' contributions) and reflexive revisability (the rules of inquiry taken as its object) might, since an ecosystem does neither; that is near [CLAIM-1126](../claims.d/CLAIM-1126.md)'s answer. Like the criteria already filed, they are stated without a regime, so [CLAIM-1153](../claims.d/CLAIM-1153.md)'s relativity applies to them. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
