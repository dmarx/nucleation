# TERM-tmpexetd: value-constituting limit: a limit on what an agent can control in a person, practice or thing that is part of what makes the agent's relation to it worth having, so that removing the limit destroys or diminishes that good; set apart from limits that produce deprivation, to be overcome, and limits that produce domination, to be contested

## Definition

A limit on what an agent can control is **value-constituting** for a good
when the good consists partly in a relation to something the agent does
not control, so that removing the limit removes or diminishes the good.
It is one of three kinds A13 §X distinguishes:

| kind of limit | A's "appropriate response" |
|---|---|
| deprivation-producing (disease, deprivation, physical exclusion) | "Overcome or mitigate where possible" |
| domination-producing (a limit another imposes) | "Challenge and reform the institutions that impose them" |
| value-constituting | "Recognize that overcoming them may destroy or diminish something worthwhile" |

The kind is relative to a good, not a property of the limit alone: the
independence of another person limits what I can make of our friendship,
and is value-constituting for the friendship; the same independence can be
a limit of the other two kinds for other ends.

## Source

A13 §X of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597):

> A friendship would lose something essential if one person could program
> the other's responses.

and "some goods depend upon relationships with independent realities
rather than perfect control." A's other examples: an education "reduced
entirely to receiving answers without intellectual engagement", and a
political community in which "disagreement could simply be engineered
away". A8 §IV ("The importance of meaningful resistance") and A9 §IV's
cello learner, who "encounters something that resists complete mastery",
are the same idea from the side of the activity.

## Where its sense comes from

- **Wolf** ([THEORY-tmp4ajaz](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4ajaz.md)): meaning is engagement with something of
  worth beyond the agent's own satisfaction, so a good can depend on the
  independence of its object.
- **Rosa, through Susen** ([THEORY-tmpzibpi](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpzibpi.md)): resonance is a responsive
  relation that "cannot be produced at will".

A9 §IV's step from these to a limit on control is A's, and A says so ("the
resulting position goes beyond either thinker's own theory").

## How it is used

- [CLAIM-tmp6yhlh](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6yhlh.md)'s displacing assistance is, in this term, assistance that
  removes a value-constituting limit: the drudgery a liberating
  assistance removes is a deprivation-producing limit, the difficulty of
  a practice can be a value-constituting one.
- A13 §X's "virtue of restraint", the capacity to decline a capability
  "when doing so would undermine more important goods", is filed with the
  competition that bounds it as [CLAIM-tmpwvg2o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwvg2o.md).

## What it does not say

- Not that struggle is good. A: "The point isn't that struggle is always
  good."
- Not which limits are of which kind. That is a judgment about worth, and
  who may make it for others is [CLAIM-tmpfyxes](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfyxes.md)'s question; A adds that
  "Restrictions justified as preserving meaning can become instruments of
  coercion".
