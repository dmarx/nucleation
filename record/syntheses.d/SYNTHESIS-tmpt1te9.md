# SYNTHESIS-tmpt1te9: The learning-and-inquiry line is eight part accounts and three claims integrated directly, which meet only through relations their components store, most of them at self-formation, and which no entry orders as a sequence

*The account of the learning-and-inquiry line, over the accounts of its
parts (ADR-083). The line was added by ADR-130 (Active) on 2026-11-21. It
integrates every entry whose `line` includes learning-and-inquiry on that
date: eight part accounts, SYNTHESIS-109 to SYNTHESIS-116, each a thematic
account of one principle of the owner's manuscript (P2 and P4 to P10),
and three claims integrated directly, CLAIM-535 (P1), CLAIM-402 (P3) and
CLAIM-794. The parts are taken in the order of the line's blurb:
distinctions, learning, maintained capacity, inquiry, transmission,
collectives. That order is the blurb's, not a claim that the parts form a
sequence (see "Not stated on the record"). It adds nothing; its standing
is its components'.*

## The account

### Distinctions, relative to a target and a budget

**When a distinction must be drawn (CLAIM-535, Active, demonstrated,
thesis; integrated directly, and a component of SYNTHESIS-110).** An
organization acting on what it observes has a policy acceptable in every
circumstance "exactly when, for each observation, the circumstances it
cannot tell apart share an acceptable action". A finer observation never
removes such a policy, and where acceptable actions overlap the coarsest
sufficient observations are several and incomparable, so "which
distinctions must be made legible depends on which acceptable action the
organization commits to". This is the manuscript's P1.

**How categories change as resources grow (SYNTHESIS-109, Proposed;
P2).** More resource for observing splits what can be told apart and more
resource for acting merges what can be turned into one another, so
budgeted individuation is indexed by a pair of budgets (CLAIM-557). What
can be told apart only grows along nested probe families compared on every
probe (CLAIM-574); classes reachable within a budget nest into one tree
under peak-capacity costs (CLAIM-581); the two partitions agree at
partitions common to both (CLAIM-592). Optimal information-bottleneck
partitions are a different object and need not nest (CLAIM-387,
CLAIM-392), and closure (CLAIM-409) and bounded observers (CLAIM-455) set
further limits. All eight components are Active and demonstrated. The
account records as not stated that only an ideal observer with nested
probes is guaranteed only to split, and that what can be told apart and
what is optimal to keep are one ordering.

**When a distinction earns a name (SYNTHESIS-110, Proposed; P4).** Drawing
a distinction is settled by CLAIM-535; keeping a candidate structure as a
unit, by evidence against Occam cost (CLAIM-154, with its limit at
singular models, CLAIM-188); carving a category, by retrieval under a
deadline, which pulls opposite to evidence with copy count (CLAIM-307).
A third criterion, survival under refinement and compression, is open
(QUESTION-113), and reuse of classes closed under a process's operations is
Proposed (CLAIM-762). The account records as not stated that naming is a
separate decision from drawing.

**When a category's success is evidence of a kind (CLAIM-402, Active,
interpretive, thesis; integrated directly).** Predictive success across
contexts warrants treating a category as a real effective kind "only when
the evidence in those contexts does not pass through the category's own
use, or when the effect of that use is modelled", since a category acted
on can produce the regularities that confirm it. It rests on CLAIM-342. It
defeats one inference and is not a criterion of kindhood, so QUESTION-028
stays Open. This is the manuscript's P3.

### Learning: a maintained capacity, distinction or channel, occasioned by a discrepancy

**Self-formation (SYNTHESIS-116, Proposed; P5).** Regulatory difficulty is
frustration among maintained demands (CLAIM-368), ordinal, with relief as
strict enlargement and not suppression (CLAIM-347). An unresolved
discrepancy is an occasion for self-formation "and not a guarantee of
it"; it suffices that the agent's own activity produce a maintained new
distinction or channel that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of
later ones, tested by transfer and by intervention on the producing
processes (CLAIM-532, Active, interpretive, thesis; TERM-099 names the
product). Persistence needs a productive cycle among a capacity's
ancestors (CLAIM-516); an observer's updated estimate is not a change in
what selects (CLAIM-540); CASE-191 constructs the threshold; QUESTION-488
leaves a uniform change of priority open. TERM-131 (Proposed) defines
learning more broadly, with no discrepancy required. The account records
as not stated that learning is the formation of a maintained capacity
occasioned by a discrepancy: CLAIM-532 is a sufficient condition, not a
definition.

### Maintained capacity

**Forming, keeping and moving into a capacity (SYNTHESIS-112, Proposed;
P6).** A form has three resource requirements, to construct, maintain and
move into or out of it, none fixing another (CLAIM-569). Reaching a
configuration has its own threshold, the least peak over admissible paths
(CLAIM-571), whose difference from holding is reconfiguration slack
(TERM-106). Feasible, reachable and reliably reached are three properties
(CLAIM-567). A budget pegged to last period's peak never rises and loses
transitions that need slack, under that rule only (CLAIM-577). All five
components are Active.

**Capacities kept for rare use (SYNTHESIS-113, Proposed; P7).** A
capability seldom called on decays with disuse and is shown only by a
retention test or the contingency (CLAIM-558; TERM-115). A correction, a
procedure and a maintained capacity are three things (CLAIM-422; CASE-138,
Challenger to Columbia). Support that does the work a capacity is learned
from can lower it, has a deadline, and can leave different capabilities
later (CLAIM-602, CLAIM-605, CLAIM-675), and can erode the capacity to
govern it (CLAIM-673). Matched trajectories hide differences an
intervention exposes (CLAIM-414), and only interventions on production show
where maintenance is realized (CLAIM-522; TERM-100). All components are
Active. The account records as not stated that maintenance is known only
by intervention for capacities in general.

### Inquiry: specification that can revise its own language, answers and criteria

**Inquiry as specification (SYNTHESIS-114, Proposed; P8).** "Inquiry is a
form of specification … with one difference, that an inquiry can revise
what an answer could be" (CLAIM-470), written as a tuple that is reflexive
when any of its parts becomes what it pursues (TERM-095). Specifying is
resource-bounded, and stored specification cheapens some later questions
and can harden others (CLAIM-475; TERM-092, the inquiry frontier). A
relational vocabulary can leave a constraint inexpressible (TERM-091).
Inquiry is one of a partial order of achievements, not a nested sequence
(CLAIM-474). On self-governance: QUESTION-388 (Open), CLAIM-485, CLAIM-424
and CLAIM-025 (regime, governing organization and capacity to transform it
"can persist or fail separately"); CLAIM-761 on stored work (Active since 2026-11-20; SYNTHESIS-114 gives it as Proposed);
QUESTION-457 (Open) on improving the improver. The account records as not
stated that reflexive inquiry is self-governed only where its criteria of
revision are its own and remain revisable.

**Refinement exposes misfits (CLAIM-794, Proposed, empirical, thesis;
integrated directly).** In a record that amends its grammar when a move
fits nothing, refining a category makes misfits in the refined region more
noticeable, not fewer. It rests on CLAIM-419, CLAIM-475 and CLAIM-470, and
is the one part of the manuscript's predicted cycle that passed the test
for a claim whose premises are claims. Its test is a recode of the
decision census, CASE-300.

### Transmission

**Transmission and anchoring (SYNTHESIS-115, Proposed, version 2; P9).**
Convergence is not preservation (CLAIM-270, CLAIM-081, TERM-027). Serial
transmission is filtered by the transmitters' biases (CLAIM-090,
Proposed), with convergence to the prior proved only for sampling learners
(ARG-035, Proposed). Local error can coexist with global drift (CLAIM-056),
and arguments restated across memoryless sessions lose identity (CLAIM-177;
CLAIM-191, Proposed). Four candidate anchors are held separately
(CLAIM-635, CLAIM-342, CLAIM-011, CLAIM-182), and anchoring is proposed as
one contraction told apart by what is conserved (CLAIM-772, ARG-124), as
securing what its reference is tied to (CLAIM-790), with re-reading a kept
source settling on an offset (CLAIM-793) and exact copies of a revised
source going stale (CLAIM-789); anchors tied to nothing are bias
(CLAIM-315) and anchors entrench (CLAIM-184), all Proposed. What a record
can secure is held to be traceability (CLAIM-189, Proposed), with
sufficiency, accountability and correctness coming apart (CLAIM-299) and no
withdrawals computed (CLAIM-664). The account records as not stated that a
stable code is CLAIM-635's conserved observable, and that the four anchors
are one requirement.

### Collectives

**A collective's correction (SYNTHESIS-111, Proposed; P10).** Separable
achievements: having, knowing and deploying (CLAIM-296); access, authority
and action (CLAIM-349); the ladder of uptake, each rung possible without
the later ones (CLAIM-403); legibility, volitional efficacy and
correctability (CLAIM-523); and, Proposed, six stages for an objection
bounded by the weakest (CLAIM-732). Lock-in from selected evidence and from
self-scoring need different remedies (CLAIM-466), with CLAIM-404, CLAIM-416
and CLAIM-342 on governance through evidence and criteria. Effective action
can grow while correctability by others degrades (CLAIM-462), effective
action and the capacity to govern are separate achievements (CLAIM-099),
and first-order responsiveness is no evidence of responsiveness to reasons
for revising the mechanism (CLAIM-719, Proposed); CASE-138 is the case. The
account records as not stated that a collective learns only if every link
holds, up to revision of the standards of uptake.

## How the parts relate

Every relation here is stored in a component's frontmatter or stated in its
body, and is given with the entry that states it. None is this account's.

**Components two accounts share.**

- **CLAIM-535** is a component of SYNTHESIS-110 and is integrated here
  directly (ADR-130).
- **CLAIM-342** is a component of SYNTHESIS-111 (governance through
  evidence) and of SYNTHESIS-115 (independent evidence as an anchor).
  CLAIM-402 rests on it, and so does CLAIM-404 (SYNTHESIS-111).
- **CASE-138** is a component of SYNTHESIS-111 and of SYNTHESIS-113.
  CLAIM-422 (SYNTHESIS-113) takes it as a ground, and it illustrates
  CLAIM-403 (SYNTHESIS-111).

**Self-formation rests on three other parts.** CLAIM-532 (SYNTHESIS-116)
stores these relations:

- it rests on CLAIM-535: a new distinction refines the agent's partition
  of situations, "which by CLAIM-535 is what a policy needs where
  acceptable actions differ" (its body);
- it rests on CLAIM-522 and uses TERM-100 (SYNTHESIS-113): its test
  against outside installation is an intervention on the processes that
  produce and maintain the capacity;
- it rests on CLAIM-025 and complements CLAIM-485 and CLAIM-475
  (SYNTHESIS-114).

Beside it, TERM-100 (SYNTHESIS-113) and TERM-099 (SYNTHESIS-116) complement
each other; CASE-191 (SYNTHESIS-116) illustrates CLAIM-522
(SYNTHESIS-113); and CLAIM-540 (SYNTHESIS-116) rests on CLAIM-414
(SYNTHESIS-113) and complements CLAIM-025 (SYNTHESIS-114).

**The separation of a regime from the capacity to transform it.** CLAIM-025
(SYNTHESIS-114) is a premise in three other parts: CLAIM-532
(SYNTHESIS-116), CLAIM-422 (SYNTHESIS-113) and CLAIM-349 (SYNTHESIS-111)
each rest on it.

**Categories and capacity.** CLAIM-581 (SYNTHESIS-109) rests on CLAIM-571
(SYNTHESIS-112): its distance between configurations is CLAIM-571's
transition threshold. CLAIM-557 (SYNTHESIS-109) and CLAIM-577
(SYNTHESIS-112) complement each other, CLAIM-557 calling the ratchet "one
decoupling" of the observing and acting budgets. CLAIM-567 (SYNTHESIS-112)
and CLAIM-347 (SYNTHESIS-116) complement each other: CLAIM-347's
compatibility complex registers feasibility and not reachability or
reliable reaching (CLAIM-567's body).

**Correction and maintained capacity.** CLAIM-462 (SYNTHESIS-111) rests on
CLAIM-422 (SYNTHESIS-113), and CLAIM-673 (SYNTHESIS-113) complements
CLAIM-462. CLAIM-404 (SYNTHESIS-111) uses TERM-100 (SYNTHESIS-113).

**Inquiry and the other parts.**

  (SYNTHESIS-113).

  by fast mixing in a chain with separated rates are CLAIM-581's budgeted

## Not stated on the record

- **That the parts form a sequence,** from distinctions to learning,
  maintained capacity, inquiry, transmission and collectives, or that they
  are the stages of the manuscript's cycle. CLAIM-794 says it does not
  claim the manuscript's eight-stage cycle is a sequence, and points to
  CLAIM-474 and CLAIM-532, which both deny that such steps form one. The
  order above is the blurb's.
- **That learning and inquiry are one process, or that inquiry is a kind of
  learning.** SYNTHESIS-116's self-formation and TERM-131's learning are
  not tied by any entry to CLAIM-470's specification. The stored relations
  between the two parts (CLAIM-532 with CLAIM-025, CLAIM-485 and
  CLAIM-475) concern governance and stored specification, not an identity.
- **That re-carving of optimal categories is learning.** SYNTHESIS-109
  holds that optimal partitions need not nest, and CLAIM-532 that a new
  distinction refines the agent's partition. No entry says whether a
  re-carving that is not a refinement is self-formation or learning in
  TERM-131's sense.
- **That CLAIM-794's refinement is CLAIM-574's.** CLAIM-794 is about a
  record's grammar and rests on inquiry's claims, not on SYNTHESIS-109's
  results on what can be told apart.
- **That self-formation carries CLAIM-569's three requirements.** TERM-100
  separates formation from maintenance and CLAIM-532 uses it, but no entry
  ties CLAIM-569's construction, maintenance and transition costs to
  CLAIM-532's route.
- **That transmission is a case of a collective's correction, or the
  reverse.** SYNTHESIS-115 and SYNTHESIS-111 share CLAIM-342 and no
  stored relation beyond it.
- **That CLAIM-402's condition on evidence is one of SYNTHESIS-110's
  criteria for naming a category.** CLAIM-762 declines that question, and
  QUESTION-113 does not list it.
- **That the line answers how a system draws distinctions, learns and
  inquires as one account.** ADR-130 found that the eight accounts
  together answer that question; no entry states the answer as a claim,
  and the relations above are pairwise.
