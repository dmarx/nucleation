After the essay's own sources were filed, the owner asked what else the
record would need given the draft's weaknesses. I recommended six works,
and the owner said to file them. Each answers one gap:

- §5.3 has no criterion for an agentive explanation: List & Menzies.
- §§7–8 need corporate endorsement and corporate responsibility: Pettit.
- §6.1's stability-versus-viability claim has an older source: Holling.
- §5.4's lock-in needs a standard of evidence: Scheffer.
- §6 needs an empirical mechanism of precariousness: Rasmussen.
- §8.3 describes double-loop learning without naming it: Argyris.

## What the readings say about the essay

- **List & Menzies give §5.3 its missing test.** A higher-level property is
  the cause, and its realizer is not, when the outcome would survive
  re-realizing it. For Northstar: would the outcome survive different
  people in the same roles? The answer can go either way, which is the
  point. Pernu ([LIT-147](../../record/literature.d/LIT-147.md)), already held, disputes the paper's main example.
  It is declared as a rival.
- **Pettit grounds corporate endorsement without a regress.** What makes an
  attitude the corporation's is authorised avowal plus procedures that
  correct its aggregate, not a higher-order attitude. He also attributes
  normative competence to corporations with an argument, which is what the
  essay's §8 does with Wolf without one. His fitness to be held responsible
  is not legitimacy, a distinction the essay already draws.
- **Holling is the older source for §6.1.** His resilience is not Aubin's
  viability. Resilience concerns a domain of attraction of the system's
  own dynamics, and viability concerns a constraint set given apart from
  them. A regime can be resilient while violating what it needs, which is
  protective lock-in exactly.
- **Scheffer & Carpenter set the bar for §5.4.** A persistent regime is
  not thereby a self-maintaining alternative state. It needs hysteresis,
  or a lasting shift after a temporary push, against the confound of slow
  return. Hughes's reef plots fail the test, and Lake Zwemlust's six clear
  years before slipping back show the confound.
- **Argyris names §8.3 and explains §8.5.** The essay's procedures for
  reconsidering the standards through which policies acquire authority
  are double-loop learning. His Model I and organizational defensive
  routines explain why an organization that espouses revisability fails
  at it: the inconsistency is made undiscussable, and so is the
  undiscussability. The evidence is his own workshops.
- **Rasmussen supplies §6's mechanism.** Under cost and effort gradients,
  work migrates toward the edge of acceptable performance, and redundant
  defences erode unseen. That is a different route from Northstar's
  lock-in, and the essay could use both.
