# THEORY-tmpqv5vx: Butterfill and Apperly construct a minimal theory of mind in which encountering and registration stand in for perception and belief, and argue that it suffices for systematic success on false-belief tasks about location but cannot track beliefs involving identity or quantification, because registration lacks representational content and embedding

## The account

An agent can predict others' actions from the objects they have
encountered and the locations at which they last encountered them, without
representing beliefs with content. This suffices for location false-belief
tasks and fails, as a signature, on identity and quantity, since
registrations cannot embed.
