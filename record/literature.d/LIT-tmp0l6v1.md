## Why this is here

The owner's exchange of 2026-10-21, "Experience and interpretation", asked whether experience is constituted by interpretation or produced by it. The exchange cited this chapter for its semantic externalism: an envatted brain's thoughts may not refer to what a normally embodied subject's thoughts refer to, though the experiences are alike. On the exchange's reading, the constitution of an experience and the fixing of its reference can come apart.
