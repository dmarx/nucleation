# THEORY-tmpsn2t0: Arslan, Taatgen and Verbrugge report that children pass verbal second-order false-belief tasks one to three years after first-order ones, that five- to six-year-olds who fail them mostly answer at first order rather than from reality, and model this as failure to select a second-order strategy for lack of experience

## The account

Five-year-olds who fail second-order questions are not answering from
reality but applying a first-order strategy; with experience they come to
select the second-order one.

## Evidence

An experiment with 5- to 6-year-olds and an ACT-R model.
