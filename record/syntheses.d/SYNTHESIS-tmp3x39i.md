## The account

**What can fail when a regulator is to be corrected (JC6).**
- **Three achievements.** For a normatively relevant discrepancy to change
  what an organization does, "three distinct achievements are needed and
  any can fail while the others hold": legibility, volitional efficacy and
  correctability. They "form no necessary sequence" (CLAIM-523, Active,
  interpretive; the owner's synthesis). Its note of 2026-11-23 records
  that the manuscript's motivating draft states "this claim's three
  achievements, detection, authority and correction within a deadline ...
  without citing it".
- **For an organization's own signals.** Access to a signal, its authority
  over action and action on it "are separable" (CLAIM-349, Active,
  interpretive; the owner's idea).
- **Affordability.** A transition can fail in four ways that call for
  different remedies, the first being that "a feasible path exceeds a
  resource ceiling" (CLAIM-568, Active, interpretive; A's). Correctability
  is "the chance that the arrangement ... finds, authorizes, coordinates
  and carries out a plan achieving the objective before the deadline and
  within its resources" (TERM-085, Active; A's α_J).
- **Success once.** "A successful corrective act, an executable procedure
  and a maintained organizational capacity are three different things"
  (CLAIM-422, Active, interpretive; A's, on the owner's cases).
- **Six levels.** CLAIM-556 (Active, interpretive; A's) orders a
  diagnosis in six levels, "each with its own repair". The manuscript uses
  it only as a list, since two synthesis reviews found it adds nothing to
  its premises.
- **Each failure the draft names is stated by the entry given**, and
  CLAIM-523's note states the first two as the draft's.

**Harmony's correctability and the regulator's answerability (JD1).**
- **Harmony.** Harmonious integration is viability, accommodation and
  correctability together. Correctability is "a constituent can still make
  a consequential intervention when the organization needs to change". It
  is to be operationalized "by an intervention test such as recovery when
  rare (QUESTION-132) or CLAIM-362's access to consequential roles"
  (TERM-tmppvlfi, Proposed; A's). Its note of 2026-11-23 says its own
  condition for adoption is unmet.
- **Access to consequential roles.** Non-domination of subcomponents is "a
  candidate constituent of authenticity" (CLAIM-362, Proposed,
  interpretive; the owner's).
- **Answerability's dimensions.** The effective dimension asks "Can the
  party's protected claims actually become consequential under foreseeable
  conditions?" "These dimensions come apart" (TERM-126, Proposed; A's,
  at the owner's U36).
- **Correction by others.** Capacity for effective action can grow while
  "its capacity to be effectively corrected by others degrades" (CLAIM-462,
  Active, interpretive; A's).

**Ecological unity (JD2).**
- **The claim.** A self-like organization "can be unified ecologically, by
  a historically maintained regime in which differentiated perspectives
  stay viable, mutually consequential and able to reorganize their
  relations" (CLAIM-tmp37le7, Proposed, interpretive; A's). Its note of
  2026-11-23: "no case in the record shows a self-like organization ...
  unified by harmonious integration without a single perspective".
- **Unity apart from singularity.** TERM-tmpm0s7g (Proposed; A's) gives
  three senses of singularity. "Harmonious integration and organizational
  singularity are independent" (CLAIM-tmp31b3v, Proposed, interpretive;
  A's). TERM-tmpxwa7g (Proposed; A's) is the pseudo-self, whose
  integration condition SYNTHESIS-tmp4zamw's decline used.

**Conserving a capability (JD3, stated).**
- **The claim.** What regulation should conserve "is often a capability
  and the conditions of its reproduction ... and not the organization that
  now performs it" (CLAIM-tmpyakra, Proposed, interpretive; A's).
- **The question and its note.** QUESTION-tmpn9t5a (Open; A's) asks
  whether a capability "reduced to rarity recovers when demand for it
  returns without continuing support". Its note of 2026-11-23 records
  CLAIM-tmpyakra as a partial answer: "conserve a capability and its
  conditions of reproduction, not its occupant".
- **Individuation.** QUESTION-tmpw2rz2 (Open; the assistant's, gap GD3)
  asks how a capability is identified apart from its holders.
- **JD3's test is stated by QUESTION-tmpn9t5a, with its note.** It reads
  conservation of the capability as recovery when rare without support.
  TERM-tmppvlfi names recovery when rare as its correctability test. The
  review of 2026-11-23 (Part B) found it a recap.

**Self-certification (JD4).**
- **The limit.** Where an actor's authority "is meant to be constrained by
  independently justified claims, its own judgment that it has met them
  cannot always establish that it has" (CLAIM-727, Active, interpretive;
  A's).
- **Excision is not relief.** "A demand excised or suppressed has changed
  the comparison and has not been satisfied" (CLAIM-361, Active,
  interpretive; the owner's prompt, worked out by A).
- **A model of it.** Expanding the recognized demand set "cannot improve
  conjunctive compliance", so an organization "may acquire an incentive not
  to improve its identification of its obligations" (CLAIM-662, Active,
  demonstrated; the owner's and A's). In a simulated institution penalized
  for recognized violations, "it learns to make no inquiry ... while its
  internal record shows almost no violations" (CASE-tmp8mk6w, Active; the
  assistant's).
- **The reference set.** QUESTION-tmphovmg (Open; the assistant's, gap
  GC6) asks against which reference set an organization is scored for a
  demand that was never in its specification.

**Answerable and correctable, kept apart (JD5).**
- **Two senses.** "'Answerability' in A74's label means reachability plus
  correctability, not the directed normative relation of TERM-126 and
  CLAIM-739. The record should not read the two as one" (CLAIM-746,
  Proposed, interpretive; A's, as corrected). TERM-126 says the same from
  its side: A74's sense is "the effective and maintained dimensions
  without the normative one".
- **Contestable and not correctable in time.** With challenges arriving at
  rate λ and resolved at μ < λ, "the formal right to challenge stays with
  everyone while its preventive effect cannot" (CLAIM-705, Proposed,
  demonstrated; A's). "Contestation without interruption" is "a right that
  arrives too late" (CLAIM-740, Active, interpretive; A's).
- **The draft's two failures.** CLAIM-523's note records the draft's
  "formally contestable institution that cannot correct in time".
- **That the two come apart is stated by TERM-126, CLAIM-746 and
  CLAIM-705.** That a regulator is required to have both is not (below).

**The protected anchor (JD7, stated).**
- **The anchor.** A protected but miscalibrated beacon drives the network's
  error to its own. "This is CLAIM-342 again, one level up. The regress
  stops at whatever is not itself revised" (CLAIM-tmpw11e6, Proposed,
  demonstrated, counter to the owner's CLAIM-tmp4h9rc; the assistant's).
- **Why nothing inside can see it.** The miscalibration "is unidentifiable
  from inside the sensor network by a symmetry", so correcting it "needs
  evidence that breaks the symmetry from outside" (CLAIM-tmpklggb, Active,
  demonstrated; the assistant's).
- **The evidence.** "What keeps a judgement–observation loop from
  confirming itself is evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass
  through the interpretation it tests" (CLAIM-342, Active, interpretive;
  the assistant's synthesis).
- **JD7's regress is stated by CLAIM-tmpw11e6.** Carrying it to a
  regulator is the manuscript's.

## Not stated on the record

Each item gives the join, what no component states, and its verdict under
ADR-075, step 5.

- **JC6, the four failures as one diagnostic sequence.** Each failure is
  stated by its entry. Their order as a sequence is not. *Verdict: fails,
  a recap.* It is a map of held separations, and as a sequence it goes
  against CLAIM-523, which says the achievements "form no necessary
  sequence". SYNTHESIS-043 declined the nearest analogue, "Capability as
  not one quantity", as "a conjunction of the separations, which cannot
  fail while they hold".
- **JD1, harmony's correctability conjunct read as the regulator's
  effective answerability, kept apart from TERM-085's correctability.** No
  entry relates TERM-tmppvlfi's correctability to TERM-126. *Verdict:
  fails, an interpretive mapping.* It sets two definitions side by side
  and states no inference. The fit is also partial. TERM-tmppvlfi's
  conjunct needs no party to be owed an answer, while TERM-126's dimensions
  presuppose one ("the actor owes the party"). The disambiguation the join
  keeps, a constituent's access as against an arrangement's chance of
  correcting, is TERM-tmppvlfi's own pairing with CLAIM-362 set against
  TERM-085's definition.
- **JD2, CLAIM-tmp37le7's ecological unity carried from a self or team to
  an industrial ecosystem.** Not stated, as SYNTHESIS-tmp4zamw's note of
  2026-11-23 says. *Verdict: fails, a carry.* CLAIM-tmp37le7's own note
  finds no case of a self-like organization unified this way, and the
  record holds no industrial case. The manuscript itself says the carriage
  "adds nothing that §12.2's correctability conjunct does not, except a
  name for the kind of unity it yields". That makes it a recap of
  TERM-tmppvlfi as well.
- **JD4, the manuscript model's excision of an unnamed demand read as
  CLAIM-727's self-certification limit, through CLAIM-361.** Not stated.
  No component cites CLAIM-727 for a model, and the manuscript's
  novel-demand result is not on the record (its gap GD7). *Verdict: fails
  as a claim, an instance.* CLAIM-727's clause is a possibility ("cannot
  always establish"). A model in which an arrangement scores itself clean
  on the specification it controls while the harm runs is an instance of
  it, not a further sentence. Every premise holding and the reading
  failing is not a coherent case, because the reading is the clause. Its
  content belongs on the record as a CASE that illustrates CLAIM-727 and
  CLAIM-361, as CASE-tmp8mk6w is a held model of the same pattern for
  hidden requirements. Neither is filed here. Which reference set scores
  the unnamed demand stays QUESTION-tmphovmg.
- **JD5, effective answerability and correctability as two separate
  requirements on a regulator.** That they are separate is stated (above).
  That a regulator is required to have both is not. *Verdict: fails.* The
  separateness is a recap of TERM-126, CLAIM-746 and CLAIM-705, which is
  already the separating case. The requirement adds a normative premise no
  component states: who is owed an answer, which the manuscript leaves to
  its gap GD1 and the record holds open in QUESTION-480. The review of
  2026-11-23 found it "Mostly S", and this is the rest.
- **JD7 carried to a regulator.** That a regulator's protected evidence
  and commitments fix what its correction answers to, so that "each level
  of governance answers to something it does not itself revise", is not
  stated beyond CLAIM-tmpw11e6's one model. *Verdict: fails, a carry.*
  SYNTHESIS-111 also leaves unstated that every self-evaluating system
  fails without independent evidence, as a necessity in general.

## Cross-line audit (ADR-129)

Its 28 components come from ten lines. Counted by script over their
`line` fields: distributed-agency 18, dependence-and-domination 6,
readiness 5, will-organization 4, organizational-normativity 4,
realizability 3, rightful-power 2, authenticity 1, formal-apparatus 1,
pragmatic-transport 1.

- **An unheld question.** No. Dependence-and-domination's blurb names the
  accountability and justification owed by those who control an
  arrangement others depend on. Organizational-normativity's names "a
  bounded duty of answerability". Distributed-agency holds correction by
  others (CLAIM-462, TERM-085).
- **Recurrence.** Yes. The theme recurs in SYNTHESIS-103, in SYNTHESIS-111
  and in this manuscript.
- **Findability.** No. A reader looking for answerability finds it under
  dependence-and-domination, and correctability under distributed-agency.

*Verdict: a bridge.* The account stays cross-line. It fails the first and
third questions.

## What it does not say

That any regulator meets or fails these conditions. Every model here is of
a sensor network, a population on the meaning–influence model, a queue or
a simulated institution, and the manuscript's gap GD5 says the record holds
no industrial case. The manuscript's other joins are mapped by
SYNTHESIS-tmp8j9h0 (timing of response and revision) and
SYNTHESIS-tmpfxv00 (readiness under budget rules, and lock-in).
