Status follows the readers' checks. A THEORY is Active where the paper
establishes it and the reader checked it, by re-derivation, numerical
reproduction or an independent script. It is Proposed where the evidence
is single runs, a circular fit, or argument without a model.

**What the readings corrected in the exchange.**
- **Teoh.** A15 and A16 read its student as seeing less context than its
  teacher. In the paper's main heads both see the same prefix and differ
  in depth of computation. The conditional-projection limit A15 derived is
  Hossain et al.'s Proposition 1.
- **Chen et al.** They do not conjecture that SGD's order and the
  posterior's coincide. Their §5.1 denies "a necessary relation", and
  their hypothesis runs one way. This entry's predecessor of 08:48 and the
  workbench entry of 08:48 overstated it. A3's use of the paper for a
  separation was not wrong.
- **Barato and Seifert.** A13 read them as tying the resource cost to the
  precision of maintenance. The relation bounds the precision of currents
  only. It excludes occupation observables and does not price how long a
  maintained state lasts. It is proved in linear response and for
  single-cycle networks, and conjectured beyond.
- **Hennick and De Baerdemacker.** Their tempered posterior holds within a
  region of constant diffusion. The "accessibility" between regions is
  not derived.

**What the readings corrected in the registration.**
- **Liang et al.** The finite-lifetime point was credited to them. It is
  Remlein et al.'s.
- **Kolathaya and Ames.** The safe set's enlargement is a class-K function
  of the disturbance bound, not proportional to it.
- **Mossio, Montévil and Longo.**
  - They propose two principles, not three.
  - `published:` moves to the manuscript's online date, 10 August 2016.
  - A HAL `url:` is added.
- **Hennick and De Baerdemacker.** The v2 PDF carries the ICLR title; only
  the arXiv metadata keep the older one. "(if it exists)" qualifies §3.4,
  not the abstract.
- **Mandt et al. 2017.** The JMLR article number is not in the PDF.

**Errors found in the papers.**
- **Vellela and Qian:**
  - a spurious 1/V in their large-volume rate formulas;
  - a flipped sign in (3.37)–(3.38);
  - the volume-driven exchange of stability is captured by the
    asymptotics they say fail, once the O(1) prefactor is kept.
- **Mobahi et al.:**
  - the caption's ε = 0.04 is infeasible (their code uses 0.045);
  - their example's Gram matrix is singular.
- **LaMont and Wiggins.** Their table drops Watanabe's log log n term, so
  with multiplicity m > 1 the learning capacity approaches λ only as
  λ − (m − 1)/log n. It is 0.40 at n = 10⁴ on CASE-152's model.
- **Richens et al.** Algorithm 1 as printed can miss its bound by up to
  1/(2n).
