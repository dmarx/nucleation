# LIT-176: Events and the Ontology of Quantum Mechanics

Mauro Dorato (2015), *Topoi 34(2), 369–378 (2015)* — [ARXIV-1503.01140](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01140)

## Key takeaways

- Events are the metaphysical category best suited to quantum mechanics: they fit the flash and matter-density GRW theories, Bohmian mechanics and even wave-function antirealisms, and so bridge the manifest and scientific images. Insofar as some realism about the wave function is needed, it should be read as referring to dispositions (powers) of events.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Dorato argues that an ontology of events is precise, flexible and general enough to serve all three main primitive-ontology formulations of quantum mechanics, and also antirealist views of the wave function. Because these formulations put their basic entities in four-dimensional spacetime, events can connect the quantum picture with the manifest image. If some realism about the wave function is also needed, it must be realism about some kind of power. The second part raises difficulties for the proposal (Esfeld et al.) that in Bohmian mechanics this power is held holistically by all the particles in the universe.
