# THEORY-tmpc4jdo: The strongest evidence that a quality-space model targets phenomenal experience, and not only behaviour or neural activity, is convergence between models of the same space derived through different behavioural procedures, since experience is what those procedures share

## Source

Isaac (2025), LIT-1361, §5 with footnote 15; read in full in NOTE-1163.
What Isaac argues, and what he concedes, is THEORY-953, which this entry
extends.

## Why it is Proposed

Two procedures can agree because they share the stimulus's physical
geometry or a common decision process, without sharing an experience. No
comparison measures agreement against those nulls.
