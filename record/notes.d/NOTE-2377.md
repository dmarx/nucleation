# NOTE-2377: Federal Reserve — Wells Fargo no longer subject to the 2018 asset growth restriction

## Contribution

Dates the end of the central sanction on the bank of [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md) and says what
it rested on.

## Key results

- "The Federal Reserve determined that Wells Fargo has met all the
  conditions required by the 2018 enforcement action for removal of the
  growth restriction."
- The conditions: "improve its governance and risk management program,
  and complete a third-party review of these improvements".
- "The other provisions in the 2018 enforcement action will remain in
  place until the bank satisfies the requirements for their termination."

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind.

## Concepts

Asset growth restriction; enforcement action; termination conditions.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md) (dating).** The record's documented case of threat without
  remorse now has an ended sanction, so [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s part 5 test can in time
  be run on it. On 2026-11-23 the window after the lift is under six
  months, and part of the sanction is still in force.
- **[CASE-405](../cases.d/CASE-405.md).** Coded "unripe": the arm cannot yet be read from what
  the release says (governance and risk management, not a harm-weighing
  procedure).

## Limitations

A press release; the Board's assessment and the third-party reviews are
not public.
