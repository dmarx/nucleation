## 7. What the theory implies

### 7.1 Ten principles

Sections 2 to 6 each ended with a principle. Collected here, they are the theory's claims about any system that learns and inquires. Each joins entries the record holds; none is itself an entry. Where a principle rests on Proposed entries, it inherits their standing.

| | Principle | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **A distinction is needed exactly when situations treated alike call for different acceptable actions under the target committed to.** Otherwise merging loses nothing. Whether to split depends on commitments as well as data. | CLAIM-152, CLAIM-350, CLAIM-535, CLAIM-622, CLAIM-348 |
| **P2** | **Growth in resources can call for splitting, merging or re-carving categories.** What can be told apart only splits along nested probes. What is optimal to keep can re-carve, though some learners are guaranteed nested optima. | CLAIM-557, CLAIM-574, CLAIM-581, CLAIM-387, CLAIM-392, CLAIM-409 |

The principles are meant to hold for any system. Two examples, both from the record's own readings, show the generality:

- **A trained network.** P2 is the information-bottleneck result, and the spectral threshold below which sample PCA cannot see a direction a readout already separates (CLAIM-tmp5y856, Proposed). P3 is the probe result, and the cone-occupancy measure that rises under collapse (CLAIM-tmp6mjfc, Proposed). P5's new distinction has a minimal model in a factored feature that switches on at a threshold (CLAIM-tmp9bp04, Proposed). P6's ledgers have a machine-learning form in overconsolidation: continued stabilization can raise the cost of later learning (CLAIM-758, Proposed). P8's question about whose criterion governs is CLAIM-356's observation that a training objective can become a goal of the training organization, not of the network.
- **An institution.** P7 is Challenger to Columbia. P10 is the ladder of uptake. P6 is the budget ratchet that leaves no slack for reorganization.

### 7.2 The flywheel the theory predicts

Take any system that holds its inquiry in an explicit, revisable apparatus: a language of types and relations, an answer space of statuses, criteria for promotion and admission. Suppose it inquires for long enough that its apparatus meets moves it cannot hold. The principles together predict a cycle of recurring moments, with a characteristic failure at each.

The moments are numbered for reference, not as a sequence. The record holds that achievements of this kind "form a partial order, not a nested sequence" (CLAIM-474), and that the steps from discrepancy to revised governance "are not a sequence" (CLAIM-532). A misfit can be noticed without being diagnosed, and a revision made without being carried through. Maintenance can lapse at any point.

1. **A misfit occurs** (P5, P8). The inquiry produces a move the apparatus cannot hold faithfully: a contribution with no type, a distinction with no field, a status that merges situations needing different handling.
   - *Predicted default:* the move is forced into the nearest category or kept in free text. That is, the misfit is absorbed and borne, not learned from.
   - *Predicted failure:* it is never noticed (P3, CEGAR's missing oracle). A misfit registers only against a reference the inquiry cannot reinterpret at will (§5.3), so an apparatus without anchors absorbs its misfits silently.
2. **The misfit is noticed and becomes an obstruction** (P3, P10). Noticing needs evidence that does not pass through the categories that absorbed the misfit: a different reader, a count, an independent procedure.
   - *Predicted failure:* noticing depends on whoever happens to meet the misfit, so it is incidental and uneven.
   - *A model of the gap, in two readings (added 2026-11-21; the analogy is this draft's).* The record's models of learned representations put noticing and change at different times, in either order. In a factored feature, the change comes first: the feature is active and improves the fit for a window, of order √(D/N), before sample PCA sees it (CLAIM-tmpqbo50, Proposed). In a self-generated alignment, noticing comes first: the ordering mode becomes spectrally visible before the bifurcation, an early warning (CLAIM-tmpk33au, Proposed). In both, a probe tied to the task sees sooner than a passive one (CASE-tmp59xy5). Read onto the cycle, the first is absorption before noticing (E1), and the second is a misfit that announces itself before the apparatus breaks.
3. **The obstruction is diagnosed against a target** (P1, P4). Do the merged situations call for different actions under what the inquiry commits to? Is the distinction worth naming?
   - *Predicted outcomes:* sometimes a new distinction, sometimes a widening, sometimes restraint.
   - *Predicted failure:* splitting for its own sake (over-naming) or failing to split where actions differ.
4. **The apparatus is changed** (P2, P8), by refinement or by revision. Revision includes merging, re-carving and withdrawing a distinction that did not earn its keep, not only splitting. In a learner, one distinction can gate the next, so changes come in a partial order of dependencies, not a fixed chronology (CLAIM-tmpl9zw4, Proposed; added 2026-11-21). That agrees with the moments not being a sequence.
5. **The change is carried through what already exists** (P6, P9). Existing entries are re-read and re-classified. This is the transition cost, and it must be traceable, or the change is itself a silent redefinition (P9 applied to the apparatus).
   - *Predicted failure:* when only a meaning changes, nothing forces the entries to follow. (Untested on the record: its history holds no such change, §15.2.)
6. **The change is maintained** (P7). A check enforces it, a review exercises it, a procedure carries it into the next session.
   - *Predicted failure:* a procedure is recorded but not exercised, and decays. Or a correction stops one level short, reaching the source but not what is derived from it.
7. **The new distinction or channel serves later cases** (P5's transfer test, P8's frontier). Questions that were expressible but not askable become askable. New misfits become noticeable because there are now finer categories to misfit. Kuhn, as one reading has him, makes the same point about sciences: firm commitment to a tradition is what lets research recognize where the tradition fails (THEORY-2096, Proposed).
   - *Predicted cost:* entrenchment (P8). Costs grow with the record (P6). The apparatus can persist without being productive (§3.8).
8. **The capacity to run the cycle is itself formed, kept and possibly eroded** (P7, P10).
   - *Predicted gain:* noticing (stage 2) can move from incidental to institutional, as procedures are built to look.
   - *Predicted risk:* help that does the noticing or the judging can erode the capacity of those who govern the apparatus to do either. Correctability can erode while the cycle runs smoothly.

The cycle is a flywheel in two senses. Each turn makes the next turn easier, because procedures built for one turn serve later turns, and because finer categories make further misfits visible. That second mechanism is the one part of the cycle with content of its own (§7.4). And each turn stores work that later turns rely on, which is also what entrenches.

### 7.3 What would count against the theory here

If the theory is right, a record of inquiry that runs this cycle should show the following. Each expectation has a failure condition.

| | Expectation for a record that operationalizes the theory | It fails if | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** | Misfits are absorbed, forced or held in prose, before they are noticed | Most grammar changes answer misfits that left no residue | P5, P3 |
| **E2** | Some changes to the apparatus are revisions, not only refinements: merges, re-carvings, withdrawals, changes to what counts as an answer | Every change is a refinement | P2, P8 |
| **E3** | Changes are carried through existing entries, and the carry-through is traceable, except where only a meaning changes | Changes are made without decision, or entries are left with a changed meaning and no re-reading even when entries move | P9, P6 |
| **E4** | Changes cost transition work, and some cause errors that only a later check reveals | No migrations, or no later errors traced to changes | P6, P7 |
| **E5** | Noticing moves from incidental to institutional over time | Who notices does not change, or noticing stays with whoever meets the misfit | P10, P7 |
| **E6** | Judgment about revision stays with whoever governs the criteria. Delegation of refinement comes before delegation of revision | Revisions are delegated as readily as refinements | P8, P7 |
| **E7** | The record's measures of itself establish use, not adequacy | Not a prediction, but a bound on every finding | P3 |

These expectations were written after the history they are tested against. They are postdictions of a theory that was itself developed partly from that history (§4.1, §18). The census in Part III is the main safeguard: it tests them against every decision the record made, not against cases chosen to fit.

**The learned-representation material and the expectations (added 2026-11-21).** The entries filed on 2026-11-21 concern trained networks, not records, so they test none of E1–E7. They bear on the principles the expectations come from, and so on how the expectations should be read. The readings are this draft's.

- **E1** (absorption before noticing) has an analogue in CLAIM-tmpqbo50's window. The analogue also says what shortens the gap: more samples, or a probe tied to the task. Read onto the record, those are more cases and an independent reader.
- **E2** (revisions, not only refinements) would gain a learner's case of re-carving if the owner's U23 is read as (b), CLAIM-tmpgzb8v. Read as (a), CLAIM-tmp85eq8, it gains nothing, and ARG-tmpbmruy argues that (a) fails.
- **E7** (the record's measures establish use, not adequacy) is P3's bound. CLAIM-tmp6mjfc and CLAIM-tmp41gfr strengthen it. A measure moved by the categories it scores, or one that shows the same regularity with no learning, establishes nothing about adequacy. Part III's counts of the record's own categories are measures of the first kind.

### 7.4 How the record received these principles

At the owner's direction, the principles and the cycle were offered to the record as entries (dmarx/nucleation#557). The record admits a claim whose premises are claims only if it adds something to them: a separating case, a nontrivial derivation, or a discriminating prediction (ADR-075, step 5, as settled by ADR-131 below). An independent reader, briefed neutrally, audited each principle against that test.

**Two principles are already stated.** P1 is CLAIM-535, with CLAIM-350 and CLAIM-348. P3 is CLAIM-402, with CLAIM-342.

**Eight are conjunctions of entries the record holds.** P2 and P4 to P10 either restate their components or add a link that fails. The failing links have been corrected in this draft:

- **P2:** "only an ideal observer only splits", refuted by a nesting result the record holds.
- **P4:** naming kept apart from drawing, which no component separates.
- **P5:** learning identified with self-formation; latent learning separates them.
- **P8:** criteria of revision that are "the system's own", which is an open question (QUESTION-388).
- **P9:** a code read as a conserved observable, which CLAIM-635's hypotheses exclude. The record has since filed a narrower form that survives the objection: CLAIM-772 and ARG-124, Proposed, under which what is conserved is the text a verbatim read copies, not the code, and not the interpretation (§5.3). Two objections to it and a general account of anchoring followed (CLAIM-793, CLAIM-789, CLAIM-790, all Proposed). P9 now rests on the general account: an anchor secures what it is tied to. The review of 2026-11-21 admitted CLAIM-772 only as a narrower, empirical sentence about this record, and CLAIM-191 only for its direction clause (§§5.2–5.3).
- **P10:** a collective that learns "only" through every link, which CLAIM-403's separability contradicts.

The eight are documented as SYNTHESIS accounts, which map their components and assert nothing of their own: SYNTHESIS-109 (P2), SYNTHESIS-110 (P4), SYNTHESIS-116 (P5), SYNTHESIS-112 (P6), SYNTHESIS-113 (P7), SYNTHESIS-114 (P8), SYNTHESIS-115 (P9, version 2) and SYNTHESIS-111 (P10), all Proposed. The audit also found that the principles, as first drafted, had dropped scope conditions their components state. The accounts restore them, and this draft has been corrected to match.

**The cycle as a sequence fails.** It contradicts CLAIM-474 and CLAIM-532. One narrowed part has content of its own, by the discriminating-prediction route: refining a category makes misfits in the refined region more noticeable, not fewer (CLAIM-794, Proposed, empirical; its test is a recode of the census, CASE-300). The components leave the direction open. Finer categories offer more slots to force a move into, and also more boundaries to fall between.

**From accounts to a line (added 2026-11-21).** Three decisions adopted at the promotion of 2026-11-21 changed how the record holds what this section describes. All three are Active.

- **ADR-129** permits accounts whose scope crosses lines, and reviews each one at the synthesis review as a sign of a missing line.
- **ADR-130.** The first such audit found the eight accounts to be bridges, and the set a candidate line. ADR-130 added the line, `learning-and-inquiry`, and gave it to the eight and to CLAIM-535, CLAIM-402 and CLAIM-794.
- **ADR-131**, alternative A of three, settles what the admission test decides. It decides whether a synthesis also yields a claim, not whether the synthesis is kept. A synthesis that organizes held components is filed as an account either way. The other two alternatives were Rejected (ADR-133, ADR-134).

The review of 2026-11-21 filed the line's account (SYNTHESIS-118, Proposed). It integrates the eight part accounts and the three claims, gives the relations between parts that their components store, and adds nothing. Most of those relations meet at self-formation: CLAIM-532 rests on CLAIM-535, CLAIM-522 and CLAIM-025. Its list of what is "not stated on the record" is this paper's list of joins. It includes:

- that the parts form a sequence;
- that learning and inquiry are one process, or inquiry a kind of learning;
- that re-carving optimal categories is learning;
- that CLAIM-794's refinement is CLAIM-574's;
- that the line answers its question as one account.

Each is a place where this paper's §§2–6 say more than the record does. The re-carving link now has a candidate case, reading (b) of the owner's U23 (§2.3), which is no entry's link either.

The outcome is itself an instance of the theory's P1 and P3. The record's admission test is a distinction drawn for a target: what a new code must add. It is applied by a reader independent of the drafting. Applied here, it found that a general theory assembled from the record is mostly the record, rearranged. Its content of its own lies in a few places: one prediction, a census, and the correction of several overreaching links.
