**What went wrong.** Asked how the hyperarticle atlas should be filed (a synthesis, or a manuscript composed of manuscripts), the assistant filed the question as [QUESTION-tmptcvtu](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmptcvtu.md) and two answers as CLAIMs in its own voice, [CLAIM-tmpcd1e0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcd1e0.md) and [CLAIM-tmpqhazs](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqhazs.md), with defeaters and lines, beside the record's argument. The owner: "these are proposed procedural decisions being asserted as if they are part of the inquiry."

**Why.** The dialectic regime says to file the argument an exchange develops, and the assistant took every argued turn as argument. A turn about how the record files itself develops a decision, not a proposition of the inquiry. The record already separates the two: ADRs and DPs reason about the record, the argument schemes hold the inquiry ([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)).

**What to change.** Before filing under the regime, ask whose subject a turn is about. If it is the record's procedure (what to file, where, under which scheme or vocabulary), it is an ADR or a revision of one, with the alternatives in its Alternatives section; it is never a CLAIM or QUESTION. Claims about the record as an object of the inquiry (record-as-apparatus) are still claims. Corrected here: the three entries are retired by status and [ADR-tmpm72rh](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpm72rh.md) version 2 holds their content.
