summary: >-
  O'Donnell (2014), DOI-10.1017/CBO9781139814782. Every f: {−1,1}ⁿ → ℝ has
  a unique multilinear (Fourier–Walsh) expansion f = Σ_S f̂(S)χ_S over the
  parities χ_S(x) = ∏_{i∈S} x_i, which are an orthonormal basis (Thm 1.1,
  Thm 1.5) and are the characters of the group F₂ⁿ, χ_S(x+y) =
  χ_S(x)χ_S(y) (eq. 1.5; §3.2; §8.5). The central operators are all
  diagonal in this basis with eigenvalues depending only on |S|: the noise
  operator T_ρχ_S = ρ^{|S|}χ_S (Prop. 2.47) and the Laplacian Lχ_S =
  |S|χ_S (Prop. 2.37). Influence, noise stability and Arrow's Theorem (via
  Stab_{−1/3}) become statements about the Fourier weight at each level
  (Thms 2.20, 2.38, 2.49, 2.56).
