Three of them need more than a note:

- **CLAIM-308's bound was wrong.** Its title said no deterministic rule
  without foresight does better than twice the hindsight optimum. With a
  per-use cost a = c_a + u on the filed state, the break-even rule is within
  2 − a/c_r, and nothing does materially better. So "twice" holds only as
  a/c_r goes to 0. Filing once the overpayment has *reached* the cost of
  filing can exceed 2 (3.55 in the reviewer's search). The reviewer derived
  the bound and checked it numerically in p18/rent_or_buy.py. It was
  re-checked here independently on 20,000 random instances, with no excess
  over 2 − a/c_r.
  - The title is corrected at version 3, which also writes in CLAIM-314's
    objection: the guarantee needs recognition to cost a fixed amount per
    use.
  - It stays Proposed until a second reviewer re-derives the bound, and
    until CLAIM-303 answers the concession it would then owe.
- **CLAIM-396 was computed.** In the linear Gaussian pair, the drive sets
  the delayed-MI asymmetry, not the coupling.
  - One-way flow is not monotone in the drive.
  - With the drive recorded, conditioned transfer entropy separates a
    symmetric pair from a one-way pair without intervention.
  - It is the same model class as THEORY-275, so it is not QUESTION-186's
    second system.
- **CLAIM-179's premise was hidden.** Its grounds named five LITs, four of
  them Unavailable, while THEORY-311 says outright that CLAIM-179 rests on
  it. It now grounds on THEORY-311, so the worklist shows that one Proposed
  reading as its sole blocker.
