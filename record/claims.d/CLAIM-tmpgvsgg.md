# CLAIM-tmpgvsgg: Transmissible cultural structures can take part in the genesis of organizations by enabling relations and processes that would otherwise be less realizable, and the persistence of the seed, of the organization it nucleates and of the perspective it carries are distinct phenomena

## The claim

A4 §10, verbatim: "Transmissible cultural structures can participate in
the genesis of organizations by enabling relations and processes that
would otherwise be less realizable. The resulting organizations may
subsequently maintain, transform, reproduce or dispense with those
structures. The persistence of the seed, the persistence of the
organization it nucleates, and the persistence of the perspective it
carries are distinct phenomena."

A4's opening reframing states the mechanism: "A meme can function as a
transmissible seed through which an environment acquires new
organizational constraints, without the seed itself specifying,
containing, or autonomously maintaining the resulting organization." And
its closing states the stance: "I would investigate **memeticity as a
relational organizational role**. An idea is memetic insofar as it
participates in cultural transmission. It is nucleating insofar as its
introduction enables organization. Neither establishes that the idea is
itself an autonomous individual."

## What it rests on

  meme into that construction.
- **Three persistences kept apart:** [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) (a product and the

  persists by causal transmission of its organization, not resemblance)

## Gaps

- **The model.** A4 §3's seeded model is [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md)'s equation with a forcing
  term. Within it the meme acts only through the size and timing of the
  push it gives, so the model shows that the environment sets the
  threshold, not that different seeds nucleate different organizations
  ([CLAIM-tmpq1lyz](CLAIM-tmpq1lyz.md)).
- **The baseline.** The defeater asks for "a suitably specified
  transmission model". Threshold models of adoption, in which an agent
  adopts once enough of its contacts have, already have critical masses
  and hysteresis, so threshold behaviour alone cannot favour nucleation
  over transmission. What would is a variable transmission models do not
  have: A4 §8's "whether exposure to a meme produces new, persistent
  relations among participants, rather than merely transmitting content
  through their existing relations". [LEAD-tmpiktom](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpiktom.md) makes that the
  measure. The note on the defeater is the assistant's (Claude, in this
  session).

## Standing

Interpretive and Proposed. A4 marks it "Proposed" itself, and its §8
says it "should earn its place by distinguishing outcomes that a
conventional transmission model would not predict".
