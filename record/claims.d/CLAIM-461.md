# CLAIM-461: Compensation is not only a re-performance notion: for a reader whose code gives the literal word a meaning the source lacked, the compensating rendering can be the more faithful report, so assigning compensation to re-performance alone needs narrowing

## The claim

[CLAIM-212](CLAIM-212.md) separates fidelity as a report from fidelity as a re-performance,
and its "What it does not say" assigns compensation to the second:
"Compensation ([CLAIM-091](CLAIM-091.md)) is a re-performance notion." Its note of
2026-10-10, on the search that found [CASE-063](../cases.d/CASE-063.md), recorded a case against
that and left it as a note: "Translators who rendered 'Son of God' with
non-biological terms for Muslim readers defend the compensating rendering
as the better *report*: the literal word 'add[s] a procreative meaning
that was absent from the original'. If that holds, compensation can serve
report fidelity for a reader whose code differs, and the line should say
that compensation is mainly, not only, a re-performance notion."

The objection is to that sentence, not to [CLAIM-212](CLAIM-212.md)'s distinction. A
report is evidence about the source's situation for a reader who decides
about it; if the reader's code attaches to the literal word a content the
source did not have, the literal rendering misreports, and the rendering
that changes the word to keep the content reports better. Compensation is
then a means to report fidelity, which [CLAIM-212](CLAIM-212.md) says it is not.

## What it does not say

- **That the translators were right.** The renderings are known only
  through a critic and the evidence about readers is the translators' own,
  which is why the note did not make the narrowing and this objection is
  Proposed.
- **That report and re-performance collapse.** [CLAIM-212](CLAIM-212.md)'s two decision
  targets stand; the objection moves one tool from one column to both.
- **Where [CASE-063](../cases.d/CASE-063.md) fits.** [CASE-063](../cases.d/CASE-063.md) is the Ace Attorney localization,
  [CLAIM-212](CLAIM-212.md)'s documented case; the "Son of God" renderings are its
  runner-up, a section the case itself says "cuts against one line of the
  claim". A case of their own, filed from a source that is read, is what
  would settle the objection.
