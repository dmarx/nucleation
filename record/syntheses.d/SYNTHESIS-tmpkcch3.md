# SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3: Whether organizational affect is felt is held apart from whether it is functional: the binding of a distributed response can be real while the single owner it is attributed to is a self-model's construction, a regime of valence is felt in the sense of attended and available when the system models its own potential, and access within one agent may be compartmented; whether the model that makes valence felt is the one that presents an owner, and whether a state meeting a functional characterization of affect must be felt, are open

*A part account of the organizational-affect line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)): feeling and access. The line's account is built over it and its two sibling parts, [SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y](SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y.md) (the potential account of valence) and [SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0](SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0.md) (corporate affect). Three of its components, [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md), [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) and [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md), are also in the line evidence-for-experience, whose account integrates them too.*

## The account

**Binding without a further owner.** [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) (the owner's U26 and U27
of the exchange of 2026-10-17, in A26's and A27's wording; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; primary line evidence-for-experience). The
binding of appraisal, readiness and governance into one response can be
real, while the single owner a self-model or collective
self-description attributes it to is a representational construction;
the illusion, where there is one, is in the attribution and belongs to
whichever system adopts it. A27: "The illusion might concern the owner,
not the binding". It answers [QUESTION-053](../questions.d/QUESTION-053.md) ([SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0](SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0.md)) in part, by
separating a state of the group from a subject that owns it. It leaves
open whether the integrated state is felt, and its defeater says so:
"Not defeated by evidence that the integrated state is felt". Its notes
of 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-06 say what holds it back: Drayson's
objection is answered only conditionally, and [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md)'s test, a
unified self-model misdescribing who controls what, has not run.

**Felt as modelled.** [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) (filed at the owner's direction,
2026-10-23; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md), [SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y](SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y.md);
second line evidence-for-experience). A regime of valence is felt, "in
the sense of attended and available to the system's other processes",
when a deeper process infers which ordering wins the negotiation, which
way the system moves along it and how strongly it prevails, and the
inference is used elsewhere. It extends Brouillet and Friston's unfelt
and felt fluency ([THEORY-662](../theory.d/THEORY-662.md)) and Hesp et al.'s affective precision, and
is offered for [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s open sufficiency condition, for affect. It
names two rivals, Grandjean, Sander and Scherer's synchronization
trigger and the beautiful-loop account, and its own limit: if the
potential it models is motivational, "the claim is a theory of felt
motivation". For an organization the model is its reporting of what
wins its conflicts to those who govern it, so Wells Fargo's board,
told that terminations were "in the range of 200-300", had "mis-felt
valence, not unfelt valence" ([CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md), [SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0](SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0.md)). Its note of 2026-10-24
records a suggested extension, "not yet the claim", to process-relative
evaluations prevailing or not; its note of 2026-10-31 says Massumi's
"felt" is this claim's unfelt.

**Must functional affect be felt?** [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md) (Deferred) asks
whether a state that meets a functional characterization of affect
thereby has to be felt, or whether feeling needs a further mechanism an
organization lacks. It lists the identity side ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)) and the
mechanism side ([THEORY-045](../theory.d/THEORY-045.md), [THEORY-093](../theory.d/THEORY-093.md), [THEORY-090](../theory.d/THEORY-090.md), [THEORY-095](../theory.d/THEORY-095.md)), and
[CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) with its limit: "it declines the phenomenal sense this
question asks about". Its note of 2026-10-18: "This question is where
the binding thesis [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) meets [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md): if the integrated state is
the feeling, the claim's illusion empties for that case."

**The two self-models.** [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md) (the assistant's, Open; primary
line evidence-for-experience; refines [QUESTION-266](../questions.d/QUESTION-266.md), [SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y](SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y.md)) asks whether
the self-model that makes a regime felt in [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s sense is the one
that presents [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)'s single owner. The review of 2026-11-07 "tried
to integrate them and found the link was a new conjecture".

**Compartmented access.** [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md) (the owner's U26; thesis,
interpretive, Deferred): a workspace need not give every subsystem the
same access, in brains as in organizations, so "global" overstates it.
[CASE-033](../cases.d/CASE-033.md) (the owner's U26; a fictional thought experiment, Active),
driving home while daydreaming, illustrates it and "cannot by itself
establish two simultaneous conscious workspaces". [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md) (Deferred)
asks the brain half. The notes of 2026-10-18 on [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md) and
[QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md) record A27's third quantity, self-attribution, beside
integration and access, and [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md)'s note: "[CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) places the
unified owner in that third quantity."

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each link above is in the entry cited for it.
Three candidates within the part were tried.

- **[CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s model and [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)'s owner as one.** Asked, not
  claimed: [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md).
- **[CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) as an answer to [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md).** Stated as not one:
  [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md)'s list and [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s version 2 note, since it adopts the
  access sense of "felt" and declines the phenomenal one.
- **Derived: feltness relative to a consumer.** [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) makes a regime
  felt when its model is "available to the system's other processes";
  [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md) holds that a content can reach some consumers and not others.
  Joined, a regime would be felt for some processes and not others. That
  turns on a free parameter, whether "available" means to some processes
  or to all, which [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s body leaves at "used elsewhere in the
  system". On "some", the join adds nothing; on "all", it needs [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md),
  which is Deferred, as a premise in force. Not filed.

## What it does not say

- That any organization feels, in any sense. [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) is a condition,
  and its test is [QUESTION-266](../questions.d/QUESTION-266.md) ([SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y](SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y.md)).
- That experience is unreal: [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) refuses that inference.
- That feeling in the phenomenal sense is settled either way:
  [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md) is Deferred and [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) declines that sense.
- Anything with standing of its own.
