**Why a sweep.** After pass 23 the worklist held nothing to rule on. Its six
must-be-empty sections were empty, no claim was ready, and every sole blocker
and blocker note was covered by a note under 14 days old. The one standing
doubt was ADR-065's survey. It found its 58 readings by keyword flags, and it
missed at least THEORY-871 and THEORY-1100. So this pass re-checked the record
by reading it.

**Triage.** Five read-only reviewers took the 570 Proposed readings that have
a source read in full. Each worked from a digest of title, condition and
sources, opening the files only when the digest could not decide. They were
told to put a title in the narrow-first class whenever a number or scope word
in it could not be matched to its source. The result:

| Class | What it means | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Report | the condition asks more than the title asserts | 28 |
| Proof | waiting on a second checker | 7 |
| Narrow first | the title must be narrowed before it can be ruled on | 51 |
| General, internal-validity or not promotable | the condition stands | about 484 |

About 160 of the general entries are the wide forms filed beside reported
entries on 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30, so they are general by construction.

**Rulings.** Four reviewers took the 35 report and proof entries, and a fifth
took three leftovers: CLAIM-471, THEORY-871 and CLAIM-160.

- **29 readings went Active.** About half were narrowed first.
- **Seven were kept.**
  - THEORY-339, 355, 359 and 476 assert general claims.
  - THEORY-777's condition disputes the study's noise floor.
  - THEORY-277's key lemma never closed. A reviewer wrote a new proof of it, which waits on a second checker.
  - CLAIM-160 stays held: its objection CLAIM-171 can be neither answered nor written in.
- **CLAIM-471 went Active** narrowed to "estimate". The stronger "establish" is a new interpretive claim, held until a case checks a measured correspondence against independent evidence.
- **The wider claims went beside their readings:**
  - 21 Proposed THEORYs where the source asserts the wider thesis;
  - 14 QUESTIONs where it does not.

**Titles that misstated their sources.** As in pass 22, reading to promote
found titles that said more than, or other than, their sources. Some
examples:

- **THEORY-245.** Posterior cortex carried identity for every category except
  faces, and one exploratory prefrontal site did respond at stimulus offset.
- **THEORY-1120.** "Better than XCOMET" rested on 0.813 against 0.80, a
  difference that was not tested.
- **THEORY-1051.** Its "only if" turned a sufficient condition into a
  necessary one.
- **THEORY-480.** It said the court held that data sharing would restore
  competition. The court ordered the sharing and called the effect an
  expectation.
- **THEORY-928.** It omitted the conditions its paper states, and without two
  of them the title is false. Separately, the paper's Eq. 11 is conserved
  only when the two temperatures are equal.
- **THEORY-942.** Its own body said "only the converse is proved", but it was
  the converse that needed the unproved conjugation step.

**Re-derivations now written into the bodies.** Every proof checked here
carries its derivation and its script's result in the entry itself. THEORY-1161
goes further and embeds its re-run script whole; the reviewer ran that embedded
copy and got the same output.

**A correction.** Pass 23 said CLAIM-448's ground `LIT-Jia-2015-1146` "named no
document". It is LIT-1146's derived alias, which luria accepts in a reference
field. A luria agent sent to close a lint gap found that no such gap exists.
The removal stands on the prior-art reason, and CLAIM-448's history and pass
23's entry now say so.

**Placeholders.** A new luria check, a heading must carry its own document's
code, fired on eight NOTEs headed `# NOTE-xxxx:` from their authoring commits.
Five more `NOTE-xxxx` citations sat in prose. All thirteen are replaced.

**Luria, in parallel.** Four pull requests came out of this pass on
dmarx/luria:

- **Pattern predicates.** Patterns can now say "not equal" and "one of".
- **Converse fixer.** It now reads HEAD through aliases, so a withdrawn edge
  spelled by alias is no longer written back.
- **Heading codes.** Lint and concretize now check a heading's code.
- **Settled acknowledgements.** `repair` takes an in-force code out of an
  `inactive-ok` directive.

The pattern predicates express two worklist checks as configuration. "Grounded
on an unread work" matched the worklist exactly. "Ready for a ruling" differs
by 23 claims, and each difference traces to judgement that reads history dates
or body text. Those parts of the worklist stay in Python. One proposal was
withdrawn: making `repair` remove a stale converse on its own. Luria's ADR-084
deliberately keeps converse pairs symmetric, so that question is the owner's.
