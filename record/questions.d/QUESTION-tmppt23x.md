# QUESTION-tmppt23x: What is affect, as the kind the record's affective entries are kinds of: claims to precedence, any concern-relative regime claimed or granted, or no single kind?

## Why it is a question

The record defines the species and leaves the genus unnamed.

- [TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md) (Active): emotion as action readiness with control precedence,
  "a state of preparation for action ... that claims control over
  behaviour".
- [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) and [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md): goal-conduciveness valence as a regime in which
  an ordering of the organization's own states "prevails in the
  negotiation".
- [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md): the two sit on opposite sides of the gap between claimed
  precedence and granted authority; "an emotion ... is a claim", "the
  system's valence is read on what the negotiation grants".
- [CLAIM-527](../claims.d/CLAIM-527.md): "Viable reach, valence and emotion are three quantities in an
  organization, not one".
- [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) (Active): frustration, a relation between demands and what
  limits them, is the primitive, and distress "a possible integrated
  response".

Yet [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md), the line's root, is stated of "affective states";
[CLAIM-1233](../claims.d/CLAIM-1233.md) speaks of "a single affectively organized regime"; and [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)'s
anxiety is a regime in which threat "grants defensive processes authority",
a grant-side state. No entry says which of these the word covers.

## Why the record needs it

It fixes the reach of [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md), which identifies hedonic valence with a
coordinated regulatory state and which [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)'s notes name as the
objection to answer first. If affect is the claim side, Veit's identity
concerns something that is not affect, and the limit [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)'s note of
2026-10-12 already records ("limited to hedonic valence in animals, while
this claim uses Frijda's action readiness") becomes structural. If affect
spans claim and grant, [CLAIM-435](../claims.d/CLAIM-435.md)'s reading of Veit's common currency as a
regime of the potential puts the whole line within the objection's reach.
If there is no single kind, [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) is several claims and the objection
reaches some of them.

## What would answer it

A reason, from the record's own uses, to prefer one extension of the word:
an entry whose argument goes through only on one reading, or a case
classed differently by the readings. [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)'s governance-maintained
anxiety is a candidate test: it is called affect and is located on the
grant side.

## Alternatives filed

- [CLAIM-tmpy367s](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpy367s.md), alternative A: affect is the claim side.
- [CLAIM-tmp8ikja](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ikja.md), alternative B: affect is any concern-relative ordering
  with control precedence, claimed or granted.
- [CLAIM-tmp23xa5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp23xa5.md), alternative C: "affect" names no single kind.
- CLAIM-tmps1h34, alternative D, the owner's proposal of 2026-11-26:
  affect is a perspective's concern-relative state, valence its sign, and
  emotion the act of communicating it as a claim to precedence.

The question and the alternatives are the assistant's (Claude, in this
session); which, if any, the record adopts is the owner's call.
