# QUESTION-478: Under what conditions should a capability be restricted, and who may restrict it?

## Why it is a question

The owner's U20, after A39's account of what distinguishes productive from
coercive relationships: "i think a question we may be circling here is
"under what conditions should a capability be restricted?"".

A41 restated it: "Under what conditions does restricting one capability
preserve or enlarge a justified organization of agency, and under what
conditions does that restriction subordinate some agents to the purposes
or discretion of others?" A's point, which the record's entries bear out,
is that restriction is neither good nor bad as such. The same limit can
protect agency (a patient's refusal binds a surgeon) or remove it (staff
forbidden to override an automated recommendation, A41 §3), and the two
"may be behaviorally indistinguishable in a particular moment" (A41 §13).

The record holds conditions for particular cases and no general answer:

- **[CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md)**: the burden of justifying a choice rises with how much and
  how irreversibly it narrows what others can later do. A41 §4 relays it
  as a ground for restriction: "A capability may warrant restriction when
  its unqualified exercise foreseeably destroys or seriously impairs
  capabilities that the relationship has independent reasons to
  preserve."
- **[CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md)**: that a way of living is worthwhile licenses cultivating
  it, not imposing it. That is the authority half for one kind of ground.
- **[CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)** and **[TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)**: when another's discretion over a
  dependence is domination. A restriction can remove such discretion or
  create it.
- **[TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md)** and **[CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)**: some limits make a relation worth having,
  and the same limit can be self-imposed or imposed.
- **[CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md)**: a collective's capacity to decline a technology can depend
  on the competition it is in.

## Its parts, as the exchange took them

- **The grounds and the authority.** A45 §6: "The fact that a capability
  should be limited does not establish that every actor capable of
  limiting it has the authority to do so." Filed as [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), which
  answers this question in part.
- **What is restricted.** A45 §4 and A49 §3 separate competence, exercise,
  authority and governance ([TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)). A restriction on one is not a
  restriction on the others.
- **What the restriction does over time.** A restriction justified by
  incompetence can produce the incompetence ([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)).
- **The capability to change the conditions of one's own challenge.** A55
  §10 names "The capacity to unilaterally alter the baseline against which
  one's own obligations will subsequently be enforced or renegotiated" as
  a capability that may warrant restriction, and A68 closes on the same
  question: "Under what conditions should an actor's capability be
  restricted, particularly when that capability can change the conditions
  under which its own exercise will subsequently be challenged or
  judged?" Its sharpest case is [QUESTION-479](QUESTION-479.md). [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and
  [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) are two such capabilities.
- **Who sets the terms.** A53, at the owner's U24: "Who has the power to
  determine the terms on which other agents must offer, withhold, develop,
  or surrender their capabilities?"

## What would count as an answer

- **Conditions on the grounds**, stated relative to a declared standard as
  [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) requires, that separate restrictions that protect a
  relationship's capacities from those that subordinate a party. A49 §13's
  five candidate conditions (grounding, authority, relational,
  proportionality and feasibility, developmental and corrective) are a
  list to test, not yet an answer.
- **An account of the authority to restrict** that does not regress. A41
  §5 and A45 §7 propose "a sufficiently justified arrangement of mutually
  constraining powers" in place of ever higher authorities, which is a
  direction, not a criterion. F4's [CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md) (four separable
  judgments about an authority) is the general form.
- **A limit from cost.** [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md) and [ARG-058](../arguments.d/ARG-058.md) hold that not every
  corrective capacity is worth keeping. An answer must say when keeping a
  capability unrestricted, or keeping a check on a restriction, costs more
  than it protects.
- **A dissolution**, if the question turns out to be [QUESTION-485](QUESTION-485.md)'s
  (the grounds of legitimacy) asked of one kind of act.
