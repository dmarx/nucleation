summary: >-
  A validation of McAdams and de St. Aubin's 20-item Loyola Generativity Scale (LGS) in Spanish, with 430 Chilean elementary teachers. Four items were dropped for low sampling adequacy, and a three-factor solution on 14 items was retained (RMSEA = 0.043, CFI = 0.989): Generative Inefficacy (three reverse-scored items), Generative Action (eight items on teaching, creativity and community work) and Generative Legacy (three items on symbolic continuity and contribution to future generations). Action and Legacy correlated .45; Inefficacy correlated −.34 and −.30 with them. Factor-score reliabilities were .80 to .86. The authors read the three factors as an extension of the original two (positive generativity and generative doubts) produced by the teaching context.
