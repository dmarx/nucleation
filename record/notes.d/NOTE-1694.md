# NOTE-1694: Tong — TASI Lectures on Solitons

## Contribution

Lectures for the TASI school on solitons in supersymmetric gauge theories,
from instantons down in co-dimension to monopoles, vortices and domain
walls, with "Emphasis ... placed on the moduli space of solitons"
(Abstract). For this record it supplies the general mechanism of
topological protection that Copeland, Gleiser & Müller invoke and that
oscillons lack.

## Key insight

A topological soliton persists because of what the field does far away,
not because of anything it does where it is. Finite energy forces the
field to a vacuum at infinity; how it winds there is a discrete charge
that no continuous change can alter; and that charge forces a minimum of
energy to sit somewhere inside. The structure is held by its boundary
data.

## Assumptions

- Classical field configurations of finite energy (or action) in gauge
  theories with scalars whose couplings are not arbitrary: "they have been
  tuned to critical values" (§3.1), or, for monopoles, "There is no
  potential for φ" (§2.1).
- Vacua at infinity held fixed: the classification is of maps from the
  boundary at infinity into the vacuum manifold.
- No worked φ4 kink: "BPS domain walls exist in many supersymmetric
  theories (for example, in Wess-Zumino models [209])", but §4 works only
  the walls of U(N) gauge theories with 8 supercharges and isolated vacua.
- Mathematical exposition; no experiment and no simulation.

## Key results

- **Homotopy classes (§1.1).** Two maps lie in the same class "if they can
  be continuously deformed into each other"; the charge k is a surface
  integral over the boundary sphere at infinity, and "The charge k splits
  the space of field configurations into different sectors". A
  configuration of non-trivial winding cannot be transformed into trivial
  winding "while remaining in the pure gauge ansatz", so it "must have
  non-zero action".
- **Charge forces energy (§1.1).** A configuration with k ≠ 0 cannot stay
  pure gauge throughout space: "it must have nonzero action". The bound
  (eq. 1.7) is S ≥ 8π²|k|/e², "a surface term which measures some
  property of the field configuration on the boundary", and a self-dual
  solution "minimizes the action in a given topological sector".
- **Monopoles (§2).** For finite energy the scalar kinetic term must fall off at least as fast as 1/r² toward the boundary; "the space of field
  configurations decomposes into different topological sectors", and "we’ve
  succeeded in bounding the energy by a surface term which measures a
  topological charge" (§2.1.2).
- **Vortices (§§3.1–3.2).** "the scalar fields q must wind around S1∞ at
  spatial infinity"; the tension of the charge |k| vortex is bounded below,
  and the bound is saturated by the vortex equations, with couplings "tuned
  to critical values".
- **Kinks (§4).** §4 opens: "co-dimension 1 domain walls, or kinks as
  they're also known". "The existence of isolated vacua implies the existence
  of a domain wall, a configuration that interpolates from a given vacuum"
  at one end to a distinct one at the other; its tension is bounded below by
  the difference of boundary values (eq. 4.8, read from the page layout);
  with the other sign "we must swap left and right infinity and consider
  the anti-wall". §4.2.2: "a strict classification of the topological
  charge requires a statement of the vacuum at left and right infinity".
  "Outside of the wall, the fields asymptote exponentially to their vacuum
  values" (§4.2.1).
- **Annihilation (§3.8.3, Fig. 21).** In the reconnection of cosmic
  strings, "Slicing vertically, one sees a vortex-anti-vortex pair
  annihilate": objects of opposite charge, total zero, disappearing
  together. The annihilation itself is assumed: "The former is expected (at
  least for suitably slow collisions)."

## Concepts

- **Topological sector**: the set of configurations with the same winding
  at infinity, closed under continuous deformation.
- **Topological charge**: the integer (or vector) labelling a sector,
  measured at the boundary.
- **Bogomolny bound**: energy, action or tension bounded below by the
  charge; solitons (BPS states) saturate it.
- **Domain wall / kink**: a configuration interpolating between distinct
  isolated vacua at the two ends.

## Connections

- **Copeland, Gleiser & Müller (1995), [NOTE-1699](NOTE-1699.md).** Their §I says
  "Topological conservation laws guarantee the stability of these
  configurations" of defects; this reading supplies the mechanism. It
  restates none of their oscillon results, which concern a field with no
  topological charge.
- **Fodor (2019), [NOTE-1698](NOTE-1698.md).** In φ4, "oscillons can easily form by the
  collision of a kink and an antikink, after radiating out the unnecessary
  energy": annihilated charge leaves a long-lived but impermanent remnant.
  The two kinds of persistence meet there.
- **The other works of this batch.** Herr et al. (2014) and Grelu &

  a non-topological soliton decaying under damping.

## Bearing on the record

- **The reader's inference, not Tong's.** From the points above the reader
  infers that a topological soliton cannot decay except (i) by meeting an
  opposite charge, so the total is zero, as the vortex-antivortex pair
  does; (ii) by a change at the boundary, through a finite domain, an edge,
  or vacua at infinity not held fixed; or (iii) by a discontinuous or
  infinite-energy process. Tong does not list these or state the thesis in
  words. The record should cite it as an inference from Tong. Tong's own
  annihilation is "expected", and in integrable sine-Gordon kink and
  antikink pass through each other (Fodor, NOTE-1698), so (i) is not how
  every opposite pair meets.
- **THEORY-1507.** Confirmed for Tong's four solitons in his theories; its
  general wording, and its saturation clause beyond tuned couplings, are
  not.
- **[TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md).** Individuation by topological charge is dynamical in the
  strongest sense, persisting under all continuous dynamics, and it needs no
  upkeep: it is fixed by boundary data, not maintained.
- **[CLAIM-597](../claims.d/CLAIM-597.md) and [CLAIM-604](../claims.d/CLAIM-604.md).** Do not apply: nothing is supplied and
  nothing is kept up. The contrast marks a route to persistence that both
  claims leave out.
- **[CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md).** A topological structure is the case in which support can
  be withdrawn with nothing to lose, but only because there was no support
  to withdraw; the analogy to organizations would need something that plays
  the role of fixed boundary data, which this reading does not supply.
- **[THEORY-088](../theory.d/THEORY-088.md).** Supplies the general logic behind its opposite-charge
  annihilation.
