## The account

Ghareeb and colleagues report that Robin, a set of LLM literature and data-analysis agents, generated
and ranked therapeutic hypotheses for dry age-related macular degeneration, proposed the assay,
analysed the resulting data and proposed follow-ups, leading to in vitro evidence that ripasudil
and KL001 enhance retinal pigment epithelium phagocytosis, with humans selecting candidates and
executing all experiments.
