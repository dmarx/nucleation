# CLAIM-1170: Physics is the subset of mathematics relevant to phenomena it is possible to observe

## The claim

The owner: "physics as the subset of mathematics that's relevant to
phenomena for which it is possible to be observed". It follows the
regress of anchors ([CLAIM-1185](CLAIM-1185.md)). The physical witness ends that
regress ([CLAIM-1146](CLAIM-1146.md)), but only through a defined standard
([CLAIM-1143](CLAIM-1143.md)).

## The assistant's refinement

[CLAIM-1151](CLAIM-1151.md), with [ARG-235](../arguments.d/ARG-235.md): not a subset but a quotient, together
with a coordination. Physics is mathematics taken up to the equivalences
observation cannot break, tied to phenomena by definitions mathematics
does not contain.
