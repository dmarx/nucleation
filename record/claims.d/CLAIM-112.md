# CLAIM-112: The operational account does not say that meaning is nothing but observable behaviour, nor that no stable content exists because interpretation is context-dependent

## The claim

A38 §10.1: "Avoid the inference that no stable semantic content exists merely
because pragmatic interpretation is context-dependent." A38 §10.2: "Distinguish
pluralism about acceptable equivalence criteria from unrestricted interpretive
relativism." A40 §13.1: "Distinguish this operational claim from the stronger
assertion that meaning is nothing but observable behavior."

Manuscript §12: "The right question is neither whether a translation duplicates
an invariant semantic substance nor whether all meanings are endlessly fluid."

## What it protects

[CLAIM-046](CLAIM-046.md) defines fidelity on observations. This says that is a choice of what
to measure, not a theory of what meaning is.
