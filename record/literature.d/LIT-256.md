# LIT-256: Mutual Information Neural Estimation

Mohamed Ishmael Belghazi, Aristide Baratin, Sai Rajeswar, Sherjil Ozair, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville, R Devon Hjelm (2018), *Proceedings of the 35th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2018), PMLR 80:531-540* — [ARXIV-1801.04062](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.04062)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-828](../notes.d/NOTE-828.md).*

- **The identity.** I(X;Z) = E_{P_XZ}[log dP_XZ/d(P_X⊗P_Z)] (Eqs. 1, 3–4).
  The Donsker–Varadhan gap for a critic T is exactly KL(P‖G_T), with G_T
  the Gibbs reweighting e^T dQ/E_Q[e^T] (App. 8.2.1, Eq. 25). So the bound
  is tight at T = log dP/dQ + C, and only there. A real-valued critic
  attains it only if the joint and the product of marginals are mutually
  absolutely continuous; that consequence is the reader's.
- **Consistency.** On a compact domain with Lebesgue densities, the
  population supremum over some compact network family is within any η
  of MI, and the empirical supremum converges to it almost surely
  (Lemmas 1–2, Thm 2). This is for the exact empirical maximizer. The
  approximation proof truncates the log ratio only from above, and the
  estimation lemma states a stronger quantifier than the uniform law of
  large numbers gives.
- **Sample complexity.** Uniform deviation ε with probability 1 − δ after
  2M²(d log(16KL√d/ε) + 2dM + log(2/δ))/ε² samples, with d the parameter
  count, for bounded, Lipschitz critics (Thm 3).
- **Not a bound at finite n.** The paper treats only the gradient bias,
  with a moving-average heuristic it does not prove. Poole et al.
  ([LIT-246](LIT-246.md)) show that the estimate is neither an upper nor a lower bound.
- **Applications.** MI as a regularizer for GANs, ALI and the information
  bottleneck. The IB is stated with entropies, not in Radon–Nikodym
  form.
