**Three more, registered on the way.**

- Harris et al. 2019 ([LIT-tmpnfh60](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpnfh60.md)), failed replications of the
  selective-accessibility measure, turned up in the search for the 1999
  paper. Read ([NOTE-tmpodfzg](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpodfzg.md)) from the authors' manuscript on UCL
  Discovery.
- Goldreich 1990 ([LIT-tmp5gb4p](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp5gb4p.md)), which the book cites for the equivalence
  between samplable separations and one-way functions without proving it.
  Filed at the owner's request. Unavailable: Goldreich posts it, but neither his server nor the Internet Archive could be reached from here.
- Hutchins's "How a cockpit remembers its speeds" ([LIT-tmpn2s1p](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpn2s1p.md)), which
  he posts himself and which is closer to the EMT case ([CASE-tmp9qlz6](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmp9qlz6.md))
  than the book. Registered and read ([NOTE-tmpav8gn](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpav8gn.md)) at the owner's
  request. It adds to [TERM-tmp5f7cu](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmp5f7cu.md) and [CLAIM-tmp9gt89](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9gt89.md) a factor they lack:
  retrieval done before the request and left in the environment.

**What the readings changed.**

- [CLAIM-tmpvdl53](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpvdl53.md): the separation is unconditional (the book's
  Proposition 3.3); only an efficiently samplable witness needs an
  assumption. Its defeat condition overstated.
- [CLAIM-tmpzk2fj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpzk2fj.md) and [CASE-tmp51hxx](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmp51hxx.md): wording tightened to what the book
  proves; postprocessing is an exercise there, not a theorem.
- [CLAIM-tmp9snb1](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9snb1.md): the selective-accessibility account is theory with
  indirect support, its most direct measure does not replicate, and its
  defeat condition no longer assumes retrieval and evaluation separate.
- [CLAIM-tmp2rwpl](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2rwpl.md): its full-belief counterpart, Hansson's Abe and Bob.
- [QUESTION-tmpga1fv](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpga1fv.md): narrowed. Bases and orderings are one-step answers,
  and neither is provenance.
- [LIT-tmp0f102](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0f102.md) and [LIT-tmpr5312](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpr5312.md): the first had Hansson's point, the
  second phrases that are A10's paraphrase.
