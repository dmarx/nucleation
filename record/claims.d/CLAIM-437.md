# CLAIM-437: 'Wanting' and 'liking' are saliences relative to different, overlapping processes, pursuit and consummation, each ranking states by their bearing on its own concerns; valence is then relative to a perspective, every perspective in the negotiation has its own, the system's valence is that of the perspective whose ordering is the negotiation's potential, and ambivalence is strong saliences of opposite sign with no potential absorbing them

## The claim

The owner, 2026-10-23: "maybe 'wanting' and 'liking' can be
differentiated by characterizing them as saliencies relative to different
(possibly overlapping) processes?"

**Salience relative to a process.** Frijda's concern pertinence is
already relative to a concern ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)). Read with the record's
negotiation, each perspective has its own salience map: how much an event
bears on that perspective's concerns, and which way.
- **'Wanting'** is salience relative to the pursuit process, the one that
  ranks what to do next. Berridge's incentive salience is this
  ([THEORY-893](../theory.d/THEORY-893.md), [THEORY-895](../theory.d/THEORY-895.md)).
- **'Liking'** is salience relative to the consummatory process, the one
  that evaluates a state once it is reached.
- **Overlapping.** The processes share components. Berridge and
  Kringelbach's hedonic hotspots lie within the larger mesolimbic system
  that generates 'wanting', which is why the two usually move together;
  sensitization re-keys one without the other, which is why they can come
  apart.

The precedent is in the component process model: Shuman, Sander and
Scherer's levels of valence turn on "the qualitative difference between
perspectives with which the same object may be approached (e.g.,
pleasantness or goal conduciveness of food)" ([LIT-1458](../literature.d/LIT-1458.md), p. 13). The
claim says those perspectives are processes in the record's negotiation.

**Valence relative to a perspective, and the system's valence.** Every
perspective has its own valence, its ranking and its progress against its
own reference course. The system's valence is that of the perspective
whose ordering is the negotiation's potential ([TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)). That is
Shuman and colleagues' micro-valences and macro-valence, the latter "an
integrative "common currency" to compare alternatives for choices" (p. 1),
and it is Carver's many goal loops each with a valence of its own, of
which one governs. The micro-valences are the perspectives' rankings
and the macro-valence is the class of orderings that fit the
negotiation's wins ([TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md)). A record need not single out one
perspective: several perspectives' orderings can fit the same wins
([CLAIM-434](CLAIM-434.md), part 7). Until an intervention contests the pairs on
which they differ, "the perspective whose ordering is the potential" is
any member of that class, and the system's valence is reported for the
class. This was revised before merge after a review; the draft read the
macro-valence as the gradient part of a Hodge decomposition, which can
reverse what the wins settle (part 4).

## What it answers

1. **The relapsing addict** ([CLAIM-433](CLAIM-433.md)'s third defeater). There is no
   single sign to get wrong. The pursuit perspective prevails and its
   valence is positive; the perspective that wants to abstain has negative
   valence, regret; the system's valence is the prevailing one's. Relapse
   is craving relieved and regretted at once.
2. **The asymmetry of the kind clause** ([CLAIM-433](CLAIM-433.md)'s note of
   2026-10-23). 'Wanting' and 'liking' each rank states by their bearing on
   their own process's concerns, so they are of one kind. The kind clause
   becomes relative to a perspective, and [QUESTION-267](../questions.d/QUESTION-267.md) becomes the
   question when the prevailing perspective's concerns have come apart
   from the organism's maintenance; sensitization is the case of a pursuit
   perspective prevailing on a cue-keyed proxy.
3. **Ambivalence** (the evaluative space model's coactivation): two
   perspectives with strong saliences of opposite sign and no potential
   absorbing them: wins that cycle within a window, or a pair's outcome
   reversing across windows.

## What it does not say

- **Why 'liking' so rarely governs action.** On this reading 'liking' is
  usually a perspective that does not prevail, valence without control,
  which fits small, fragile hotspots and 'liking' reactions that run
  unfelt. That is a description, not an explanation.
- **How pursuit and consummation are individuated** without appeal to the
  dissociations. That is [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s open problem, with stakes now.
- **Whether the system's valence is more than bookkeeping.** If every
  perspective has its own, the system's may be nothing beyond the
  prevailing perspective's.
- **Which valence is felt.** On [CLAIM-439](CLAIM-439.md), whichever the system
  models, which allows a felt pleasure that does not govern action and a
  governing 'wanting' that is not felt.

## Note of 2026-10-24: where hedonic drive sits

The owner: "where does hedonic drive fit into our model?" (workbench
entry of 2026-10-24, U23). Inside the distributed motivational
organization, not as a fourth coordinate beside [CASE-124](../cases.d/CASE-124.md)'s meaning,
authority and legibility. Four quantities: hedonic impact (how pleasant an
experienced outcome is, the consummatory perspective); anticipated
pleasure (a prospective model's prediction under an intervention,
[CLAIM-447](CLAIM-447.md)); incentive wanting (cue-triggered pull); and hedonic
authority (how much any of these influences the negotiation). A schematic
pursuit score W = Q^H + κC lets cue-triggered pursuit diverge from
expected pleasure; it is not a neural equation. Two consequences: a strong
hedonic drive can lose the negotiation, and a process can control pursuit
while yielding little pleasure. Wanting and liking then differ not only as
evaluations but in their capacity to govern, which raises
[QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md). Relief after avoidance is reduced distress, kept apart
from positive pleasure and from reinforcement ([CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md)). Nothing
here makes pleasure the organism's ultimate value: [CLAIM-433](CLAIM-433.md) keeps
pleasantness apart from goal-conduciveness, and [QUESTION-266](../questions.d/QUESTION-266.md) keeps the
inferred potential, a hedonic marker and the self-model apart.

## Note of 2026-10-26: wanting and liking as separate quantities in a synthetic model

The lab's v2.6 (dmarx/PerspectiveLab@74621fb
experiments/affective_dissociation/) builds on this claim's distinction
and on [QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md). It makes wanting and liking separate operational
quantities. Liking is an exogenous outcome value, read by an assay that
does not use action. Wanting is a proxy, anticipated pleasure plus cue
drive plus another pursuit motive, read with noise of its own. It leaves
out habit and the final choice, and the study's inference does not use
it. After devaluation every class has liking −1 and pursues with
probability 0.668. In the cue class the wanting proxy is +1.9. In the
habit class it is −0.5, so pursuit there runs with neither wanting nor
liking by these measures. The claim's first defeater asks for a
wanting–liking dissociation, and this is action dissociated from both.
Whether habit belongs to the pursuit process the model does not say.

A longitudinal run, with liking dropping from +1 to −1 in one step,
shows anticipated pleasure and pursuit adjusting at different rates in
different classes. The rates are programmed: the learning rate is 0.045
in the belief-error class and 0.028 in the others, and only the habit
class has a habit term that grows and decays. Three of the five classes
follow the same course.

The model has no pursuit or consummatory process ranking states by its
own concerns. Wanting and liking are terms in one logit. So it does not
individuate the processes, which is [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s open problem, and it has
no perspectives' valences, no prevailing ordering and no ambivalence. Its
relations are stipulated and are not evidence about incentive
sensitization ([THEORY-895](../theory.d/THEORY-895.md)). It neither meets nor defeats the claim. The
claim is unchanged.
