# NOTE-130: Floridi — The Informational Nature of Personal Identity

## Contribution

Floridi proposes an informational account of personal identity. Plato's chariot, read as a multiagent system, sets the problem of what holds the self together as a coherent unity. He separates two branches of a theory of the self ("egology"), the individualisation of the self and its identification, argues that both presuppose an informational approach, and holds that individualisation comes first. His positive proposal is a three-membranes model of the self, which he uses to interpret ICTs as technologies of the self. Using Aristotle's *anagnorisis* ("realisation"), he defends a Spinozian conclusion within informational structural realism: selves are the final stage in the development of informational structures. The paper ends by calling for an ethics, or "ecology", of self-shaping.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- "Plato and the Problem of the Chariot" (pp. 550–552): the chariot is read literally as an engineered multiagent system. This licenses a constructionist, design-oriented approach to the self, on which classic MAS problems (coherence, coordination, communication) are "AI translations" of problems in the philosophy of the self.
- "Egology as Synchronic Individualisation" (pp. 554ff.): "individualisation logically precedes identification". Lockean (memory and consciousness) and Narrative (Schechtman) accounts are both treated as needing a prior answer to what the self is.
- "A Reconciling Hypothesis: The Three Membranes Model" (pp. 557–560): the self emerges as successive encapsulations. A corporeal membrane (organism, chemical bonds), a cognitive membrane (intelligent animal) and a consciousness membrane (mind, "softwired") each detach an inside from an outside.
- "ICTs as Technologies of the Self" (pp. 560–563): ICTs "re-ontologise" self-poiesis by enhancing the corporeal, empowering the cognitive and extending the consciousness membrane. The illustrations cover embodiment ("from dualism to polarism"), space, time, memory and perception.
- "The Logic of Realisation" (pp. 563–565, Fig. 1): anagnorisis is a later piece of information that forces a reinterpretation of the whole prior flow, and it is distinguished from falsification. Selves are "the ultimate negentropic technologies", informational structures through which information becomes self-aware.

## Open questions

- A standard reference for an informational, level-of-abstraction approach to personal identity. Useful as a foil to the extended-mind thesis ([LIT-097](../literature.d/LIT-097.md)) and to identity questions about AI instances (compare [LIT-206](../literature.d/LIT-206.md)'s "instance agent").
- Check whether the three-membranes model does real work on the individuation problem or restates an evolutionary story. The argument for "individualisation before identification" is the load-bearing claim.
- The metaphysical backdrop, informational structural realism, is assumed from Floridi 2008 and 2011 rather than defended here.
