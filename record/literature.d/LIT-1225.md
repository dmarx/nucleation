# LIT-1225: Division of labour and the evolution of extreme specialization

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-tmpckr3f](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpckr3f.md)).

Bears on [CLAIM-279](../claims.d/CLAIM-279.md) and [QUESTION-083](../questions.d/QUESTION-083.md).
