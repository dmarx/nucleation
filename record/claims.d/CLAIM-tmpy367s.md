# CLAIM-tmpy367s: Affect is the claim side

*Alternative A to QUESTION-tmppt23x. Alternatives B and C are CLAIM-tmp8ikja and CLAIM-tmp23xa5.*

## The claim

Affect is what TERM-094 defines, read through TERM-104: a perspective's
concern-relative significance conditioning a state of readiness that claims
control precedence. On CLAIM-442's division, "an emotion ... is a claim",
and the system's valence "is read on what the negotiation grants". This
reading keeps the word on the first side. The negotiation's prevailing
ordering is then the arena affects contend in, not an affect, and
CLAIM-368's frustration is what they are responses to.

What it buys: CLAIM-039 becomes a claim that an organization can host
action readiness with control precedence, which is what its Frijda
characterization states. THEORY-101's identity concerns hedonic valence,
which on this reading is not affect, so the objection bears on CLAIM-039
only by way of a further thesis that claims to precedence are themselves
valenced.

## What it does not say

That the grant side is unreal or unimportant; TERM-076's valence stands
as defined. Nor that THEORY-101 is answered: CLAIM-440 and CLAIM-432 use
valence and stay within its reach.
