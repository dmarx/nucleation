# LIT-1371: Aircraft Accident Report: United Airlines, Inc., McDonnell-Douglas DC-8-61, N8082U, Portland, Oregon, December 28, 1978

National Transportation Safety Board (1979). *Aircraft Accident Report: United Airlines, Inc., McDonnell-Douglas DC-8-61, N8082U, Portland, Oregon, December 28, 1978*. NTSB-AAR-79-7, adopted 7 June 1979. https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/2022-11/AAR79-07.pdf.

Read on 2026-10-22 in [NOTE-tmpoojls](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpoojls.md) (the CVR transcript skimmed).

## Why this is here

The owner proposed the history behind Crew Resource Management as a case (U25). This accident is its standard starting point. The case is [CASE-127](../cases.d/CASE-127.md).

**From the reading, 2026-10-22.** The report supports the case's facts
and its probable and contributing causes. It does not support the case
title's "a crew that knew the fuel was running out": the Board finds the
crew aware of the quantity at various times, finds no monitoring of it
against time, and leaves open whether the first officer and flight
engineer grasped the criticality. Its recommendation X-79-17 is the
earliest official statement of the reform [CASE-127](../cases.d/CASE-127.md) calls "authority
without abolishing command". See [NOTE-tmpoojls](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpoojls.md) for its bearing on
[CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md), [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md) and [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md).
