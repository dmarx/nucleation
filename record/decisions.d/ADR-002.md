# ADR-002: published: is the exact date of first appearance where a source gives one

## Context

The LIT template, ported from the anthology, said `published:` is "the arXiv
posting month, from the id: 2205.11487 → 2022-05", written as the first of
that month. Every arXiv abstract page states the v1 submission day, and every
court filing, dated report and blog post in this record states its day too.
Recording `2025-10-01` for a paper posted on 23 October is not a coarser
truth; it is a false date that looks exact. It surfaced when a close reading
([NOTE-005](../notes.d/NOTE-005.md)) flagged the discrepancy and the record's first answer was to defend
the convention. The owner's ruling: use exact dates where we have them.

## Decision

`published:` is the exact date of first appearance:

- **arXiv:** the v1 "Submitted on" date from the abstract page.
- **DOI without arXiv:** the earliest full (year-month-day) date Crossref reports
  among posted, published-online, published-print and issued.
- **URL-only documents:** the date the document itself carries (a filing date,
  a hearing date, a post date).
- **No day available:** the first of the month, as before. This is the only
  case where `-01` means "month precision", and it should be rare.

Deposit or indexing dates (Crossref `created`) are not publication dates and
are not used.

**When unsure, the earlier date.** Where sources disagree, or the rules above
leave open which appearance came first, `published:` takes the earliest date
any of them gives, and the history note names the alternatives. That covers
a preprint posted before the journal version under its own DOI or title,
a publisher's online date earlier than every Crossref field, and an arXiv
copy posted after the journal version. (Owner's ruling, 2026-10-09.)

## Alternatives

- **Keep the month convention** — rejected: it records a date nobody meant
  and discards one every source gives.
- **Add a separate `published_precision:` field** — rejected for now: every
  current LIT but three has a day, so a field to mark the exceptions would be
  a field almost every record shares (the anthology's [DP-002](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-2)).

## Consequences

All 29 LITs were backfilled on 2026-09-25 by script
(`exact_dates.py`: arXiv abstract pages, then Crossref) and by hand from the
documents for the six URL-only filings. Three keep month precision because
no source gives a day: the ISW report, the Toxicon paper (Crossref gives only
the print month) and the concussion review. The template comment now says
"exact date". The anthology carries the same decision.
