# LIT-2961: Feature Lottery? A Bifurcation Theory of Concept Emergence

Yang (2026). arXiv 2605.24057.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A42 for concept emergence as a bifurcation. It bears on the exchange's return to rich-get-richer dynamics inside learning.

Read on 2026-11-22 in [NOTE-2280](../notes.d/NOTE-2280.md); the reading is [THEORY-2193](../theory.d/THEORY-2193.md). A42's
citation, "a pitchfork account of concept emergence in a Gaussian-mixture
probe framework", is accurate. The first summary's "small initial
advantages decide which features win" needs a qualification: noise and
cubic terms pick directions on a shared unstable manifold, and the choice
persists; nothing compounds, so the paper is not a rich-get-richer
account, and it never presents itself as one. That link was A's framing.
The pitchfork is the probe's, not the network's, and the probe is
detached from the encoder. It parallels [CLAIM-807](../claims.d/CLAIM-807.md)'s pitchfork and its
caveat that the symmetry broken is one of the parameters, and grounds
neither that claim nor anything about learning coefficients.
