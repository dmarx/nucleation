# LEAD-tmpf2lhq: Code the owner's corrections in the exchange Hyper article interpretation under a codebook fixed in advance, with a second coder, to test whether the corrections that changed what the argument could infer were distinctions that stabilized a class

## The idea

A47 lists seven corrections the owner made in the exchange and says of
each that it "stabilized" a class: the research object against its
presentation, a feed against a view with a memory, theory against
demonstration against proposal, and so on. A47 chose the examples after
the fact, to illustrate the thesis it was asked to illustrate (U22), so
they cannot test it.

## What it would settle

Whether, in one exchange, the owner's corrections that changed the
argument's later inferences were mostly distinctions that kept a class
stable (CLAIM-tmpyaro8, CLAIM-tmph11zb), or mostly something else: new
evidence, a change of scope, a correction of fact, a matter of style.

## The design

- **Units.** Every owner turn U1 to U28 that corrects, narrows or
  redirects the preceding A turn, segmented before any coding.
- **Codebook, fixed before coding.** For each unit: (a) whether it draws
  a distinction, and if so which of A47's three routes (split a class,
  merge instances into one class, separate an object's identity from
  properties allowed to change); (b) otherwise whether it is new
  evidence, a scope change, a factual correction or a style instruction.
- **Outcome, coded separately and blind to (a).** Whether the next A
  turns draw an inference, evaluate an item apart or ask a question that
  the unit made available and that the preceding turn could not state.
- **Two coders**, the second not the one who wrote the codebook, with
  agreement reported before any rate.
- **Control.** The same coding on an exchange of the record whose owner
  turns are mostly not corrections, so that a high rate of
  distinction-type corrections is not just the base rate of the owner's
  interventions. CASE-306 coded an exchange move by move under a codebook
  fixed in advance and is the model to follow.

The result would be the persisted case A47 proposes, filed as a CASE of
kind executed-study.
