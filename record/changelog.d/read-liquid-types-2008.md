### Added

- [LIT-tmplw76n](record/literature.d/LIT-tmplw76n.md), [NOTE-tmpnd1kd](record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpnd1kd.md) and [THEORY-tmp01mu6](record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp01mu6.md): Rondon, Kawaguchi and Jhala 2008, "Liquid Types", registered and read for [LEAD-tmp3xevc](record/leads.d/LEAD-tmp3xevc.md). Liquid types are dependent types {ν : B | φ} whose predicates, where a type is inferred, are conjunctions of qualifiers from a finite set. Inference weakens to the strongest conjunction a conservative decidable check verifies, and a program with no such type is reported, not changed.
- [ARG-tmprmcn5](record/arguments.d/ARG-tmprmcn5.md) (Proposed): the reading, by analogy, bears out [CLAIM-tmpnookt](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnookt.md)'s descriptor as a refinement inhabited, in predicate form, narrowed to a finite conjunctive vocabulary for inferable descriptors, conservative checking, and no carried evidence.
- [ARG-tmp246r1](record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp246r1.md) (Rejected): the reading shows inference as informative update but not checking as requirement.
- [CLAIM-tmpcmt21](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcmt21.md) (Proposed, counter, interpretive): in liquid types checking moves no program, and a failure to check may lie in the program, the qualifier set or the checker.
