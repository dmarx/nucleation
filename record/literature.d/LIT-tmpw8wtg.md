  https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1979/51. The TMS records, for each belief
  (node), the reasons (justifications) for it, and keeps the set of current
  beliefs consistent with those reasons. A node is believed (in) if at least
  one of its justifications is valid, out otherwise. An SL-justification is
  valid when every node in its in-list is in and every node in its out-list
