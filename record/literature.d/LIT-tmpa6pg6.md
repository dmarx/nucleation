# LIT-tmpa6pg6: Skills‐homework completion and phone coaching as predictors of therapeutic change and outcomes in completers of a DBT intensive outpatient programme

Edwards, E. R., Kober, H., Rinne, G. R., Griffin, S. A., Axelrod, S. & Cooney, E. B. (2021). Skills‐homework completion and phone coaching as predictors of therapeutic change and outcomes in completers of a DBT intensive outpatient programme. *Psychology and Psychotherapy: Theory, Research and Practice* 94(3): 504–522. doi:10.1111/papt.12325.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp2u3za](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2u3za.md). Page numbers are the journal's.*

- **Urges, not behaviour frequency.** Homework with change in suicide
  urges r = .35 and substance-use urges r = .36; coaching with self-harm
  urges r = .31; "Regarding frequency of engagement in target behaviours,
  results reflected no significant associations" (p. 513).
- **Month 4.** No target behaviour went with 91.3% homework completion
  against 79.8% (p = .02); coaching calls 6.00 against 3.85, p = .096
  (p. 513).
- **A selected sample.** 4 of 88 dropped out and 28 were excluded for
  missing diary data (p. 509).
- **No correction.** "statistical adjustments for multiple comparisons
  were not feasible, increasing the likelihood of type I error" (p. 519).

## Why this is here

The exchange cited it at turn U10, unnamed, as a study of 56 DBT completers in which homework and phone coaching went with better behavioural outcomes: practice carried into daily contexts is where the skill is generalized. The abstract matches. The design is correlational, in completers only.

**Corrected 2026-10-24, from the reading.** The associations are with
falling urges, not with how often the behaviours occurred, and the one
behavioural result (no target behaviour in month 4) holds for homework
only. The 56 are not all completers but those of 88 who also kept enough
diary cards. The study supports, weakly, that DBT practice and outcome go
together; it measures neither skill use nor the cost of deploying a
skill, so it cannot bear on [CLAIM-tmp0qupq](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0qupq.md)'s limits or on [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)'s note
that practice makes a skill cheap to deploy. Filed from it:
