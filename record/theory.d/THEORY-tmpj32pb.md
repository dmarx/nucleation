# THEORY-tmpj32pb: Sen argues that a person's standard of living is a matter of the functionings achieved and the capabilities held, not of opulence or utility, since the same commodities yield different living conditions under different bodily, climatic and social circumstances, and that valued functionings can be ranked by a partial ordering without a complete index

## The account

Sen argues that the standard of living "is really a matter of functionings and capabilities, and not a matter directly of opulence, commodities, or utilities". Commodities convert into living conditions differently with body, climate and custom. A functioning is an achievement and a capability the ability to achieve; valued functionings are ranked by a dominance partial ordering, extended by ranges of weights. Agency achievement, well-being and the standard of living are distinct, and unchosen options matter (the faster and the starver).
