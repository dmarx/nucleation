# THEORY-1692: Hasler (2026) argues that the enteric nervous system's scale, autonomy and sensory resolution make it a candidate substrate of consciousness on several theories, but that this evidence supports only the contributory thesis, that the gut helps constitute the whole animal's single experience; the partial-subject thesis, that the gut is a second locus of experience, is open and would need enteric contents integrated among themselves but not with the central ones, persisting when the connection to the brain is cut.

## The account

Hasler (2026) argues that the enteric nervous system's scale, autonomy and sensory resolution make it a candidate substrate of consciousness on several theories, but that this evidence supports only the contributory thesis, that the gut helps constitute the whole animal's single experience; the partial-subject thesis, that the gut is a second locus of experience, is open and would need enteric contents integrated among themselves but not with the central ones, persisting when the connection to the brain is cut.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1737](../notes.d/NOTE-1737.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
