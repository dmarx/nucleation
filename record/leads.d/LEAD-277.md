# LEAD-277: Read the historiography and the quantitative work on the persistence of race-based power after American abolition, to check the owner's case and to find comparisons that separate maintenance by reliance from maintenance by sanction

## What it would do

- Check [CASE-422](../cases.d/CASE-422.md)'s dates and holdings against sources.
- Read Woodward on whether Jim Crow was carried over from slavery or built
  after Reconstruction. That decides how directly the case bears on
  [CLAIM-1245](../claims.d/CLAIM-1245.md).
- Read Klarman on Brown and the resistance that followed. That bears on the
  discontinuous end and on the hysteresis after it ([CLAIM-1318](../claims.d/CLAIM-1318.md)).
- Read the county-level work on slavery's political legacy. It compares
  places that differ in how much was built on slavery, which is the
  comparison [CLAIM-1284](../claims.d/CLAIM-1284.md)'s defeater asks for and [ARG-256](../arguments.d/ARG-256.md) says the
  national case cannot supply.
