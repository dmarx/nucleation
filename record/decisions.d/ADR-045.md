# ADR-045: databases joins the topics

## Context

Five works were registered and read on 2026-10-15 for [QUESTION-114](../questions.d/QUESTION-114.md), which asks
whether sorting a collection is amortized search:

- Finkelstein, Schkolnick and Tiberio 1988 ([LIT-1084](../literature.d/LIT-1084.md)), index selection;
- Yao 1981 ([LIT-1097](../literature.d/LIT-1097.md)), lower bounds on lookup;
- Idreos, Kersten and Manegold 2007 ([LIT-1090](../literature.d/LIT-1090.md)), database cracking;
- Sleator and Tarjan 1985 ([LIT-1093](../literature.d/LIT-1093.md)), self-organizing lists and paging;
- Harinarayan, Rajaraman and Ullman 1996 ([LIT-1094](../literature.d/LIT-1094.md)), which answers to precompute.

No topic fitted. They were tagged `information-retrieval`, whose blurb is
"finding what is relevant to a need — document and query representation,
relevance and aboutness, ranking and feedback". That is a different
problem: these works take the query as given and ask how the data should be
arranged to answer it cheaply. The curation entry of 2026-10-15 03:31
flagged the stopgap, and the owner answered: "don't hesitate to extend the
vocabulary as needed".

A search of the record's titles found no earlier work, LIT or THEORY, whose
subject this is.

## Decision

Add `databases` (Databases and data structures). It covers:

- physical database design and index selection;
- materialized views and the choice of which answers to precompute;
- adaptive and self-organizing structures;
- lower bounds on lookup (cell-probe and the like);
- caching and paging;
- the cost of organizing ahead of use against organizing in use.

Machine-learning approaches (learned indexes, learned physical design) go
to the anthology, as for every topic here.

How it is applied:

- **Primary** on the five works above, replacing `information-retrieval`,
  which none of them is about. Where a second topic fits it is added:
  `operations-research` for the two optimization papers ([LIT-1084](../literature.d/LIT-1084.md), [LIT-1094](../literature.d/LIT-1094.md)),
  `mathematics` for the two that prove bounds ([LIT-1097](../literature.d/LIT-1097.md), [LIT-1093](../literature.d/LIT-1093.md)).
- **Added** to [QUESTION-114](../questions.d/QUESTION-114.md), the question they were read for.

## Alternatives

- **Keep `information-retrieval`.** It is free, but it puts relevance
  ranking and index selection on one page, and a reader browsing either
  finds the other.
- **`data-structures`, narrower.** It would leave physical design,
  materialized views and the cost models of database systems without a
  word, and those are most of what was read.
- **`operations-research` alone.** It fits the two optimization papers and
  not the bounds or the adaptive structures.
