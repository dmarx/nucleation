# LIT-2367: A data-centric approach for ethical and trustworthy AI in journalism

Laurence Dierickx, Andreas Lothe Opdahl, Sohail Ahmed Khan, Carl-Gustav
Lindén and Diana Carolina Guerrero Rojas (2024). "A data-centric approach
for ethical and trustworthy AI in journalism" *Ethics and Information
Technology 26(4): 64*. DOI-10.1007/s10676-024-09801-6.

## Key takeaways

Principles of journalism ethics can be turned into checks on the data a
newsroom's tools are built from.
