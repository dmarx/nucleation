# CASE-tmpk52n6: The all-hands: a corporate event as a synchronization mechanism for the firm

## The case

A firm of several hundred people has drifted. Teams pursue local goals on
their own timescales. Sales optimizes the quarter, research the year,
infrastructure the next outage. Each team's work makes sense from where it
sits, and the firm acts less and less as one. Leadership holds an all-hands
(or an offsite, or an annual kickoff). Everyone is in one room at one time.
Strategy is presented, teams present to each other, and there are shared
rituals: applause, a story about the founding, a dinner. A deck and a memo
go out afterwards, and goals are restated with incentives tied to them.
Over the following months the drift resumes, and the next event is
scheduled.

Held fixed: the firm's members, structure and stated mission. Varied:
whether, and how often, the event happens.

## What it can show

The event does at once, at firm scale, the things this session separated
at the individual scale:

- **Entrainment by co-presence.** One room, one rhythm, a shared emotional
  arc. [THEORY-126](../theory.d/THEORY-126.md) reads collective emotion as "synchronous convergence in
  affective responding" across individuals. Barsade's groups ([LIT-706](../literature.d/LIT-706.md))
  moved from no shared mood to a measurable one. If the coupling is
  mutual (the room responds to the stage and the stage to the room),
  this is [CLAIM-tmptsdgt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md)'s criterion met by people rather than a person
  and a tool.
- **Storage.** The deck, the memo and the restated goals are written for
  receivers who are not in the room, including people hired later and the
  attendees themselves six months on. On the owner's storage claim
  ([CLAIM-tmpdfqoo](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdfqoo.md)), their intended meaning is fixed by that write. How
  faithfully each team decodes it is a separate, receiver-relative matter.
- **Realignment.** Tying incentives to the firm's goal makes each team's
  short-run reward and the firm's long-run reward closer to the same
  reward. That is the firm-scale analogue of flow's condition
  ([CLAIM-tmpf301t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf301t.md)): less to override.
- **Cadence.** Drift resumes between events, so the event works like
  periodic forcing rather than permanent coupling. That suggests a
  prediction: firms whose units drift faster, as after a merger or a
  reorganization, hold such events more often.

## What it cannot show

- **That the firm is a self.** The case illustrates the open objection as
  much as the claim. [CLAIM-tmpk62ey](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk62ey.md) holds that entrained members form a
  coordination and stay many selves. The record's group-agency readings
  ([THEORY-043](../theory.d/THEORY-043.md), [LIT-401](../literature.d/LIT-401.md)) keep agency and phenomenal selfhood apart. The
  owner's phrase, a "kind of a 'self'", is the hypothesis, not the
  finding.
- **That the event synchronizes at all.** It may be theatre: signalling
  to staff, investors or the leaders themselves, with no change in what
  the teams do. Whether priorities converge after the event, and for how
  long, is an empirical question the stipulated case cannot answer.
- **That one message reaches everyone the same way.** Each team receives
  the strategy against its own common ground. The pragmatic-transport
  line would predict one write and several decodings.
