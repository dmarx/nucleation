emotion to be filed. The owner was open to replacing long works with
shorter ones that serve the same role. The record held none of the fifteen

added ([ADR-021](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-021.md)). Three agents filed eighteen notes. Seven of the requested
works were read in full, three were replaced by shorter readable works by
the same authors, and eight works are Deferred seeds.

The record had no copy of either book, and none of the shorter candidates
could be read either. For Frijda the agent found his own late,
open-access statement of action readiness and control precedence. It is
narrower than the book: it has nothing on experience or on the laws of
emotion. For Lazarus nothing could stand in. His 1991 *American
Psychologist* paper is the right substitute, and someone with library
access could read it.

Barrett names Lazarus as her example of the causal appraisal theory she
rejects. Frijda's 2014 paper says regulation is not a process separate
from emotion, which takes a side on the open problem Gross set aside. The
LIT scheme has no relation for a rivalry between theories, so all three
notes state it in prose.

## What the batch says together

Two independent lines say that a treatment working is not evidence for
its theory of change: Beckian CBT (Salkovskis et al., [LIT-tmp2bpgh](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp2bpgh.md)) and
contextual behavioural science (Hofmann & Hayes, [LIT-tmpeg79d](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpeg79d.md)). Only
specific, ordered mediation is evidence, and moving the theorised process
without moving the outcome disconfirms the theory. Three lines, none
citing the others, put self-reinforcing maintenance loops where a latent
cause would be: Borsboom's among symptoms ([LIT-tmpmi1g3](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpmi1g3.md)), Salkovskis et
al.'s between threat appraisals and safety-seeking, and Kendler's across
levels ([LIT-tmp2h31z](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp2h31z.md)). Salkovskis et al. call their common mechanism
"psychological flexibility", but cite none of the ACT literature that uses
the term. Nothing is filed as a THEORY yet. The proposals are in the pull
request.
