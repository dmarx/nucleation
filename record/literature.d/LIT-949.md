  network with lateral prefrontal cortex. Across propofol anaesthesia

# LIT-949: A synergistic workspace for human consciousness revealed by Integrated Information Decomposition

Andrea I. Luppi, Pedro A. M. Mediano, Fernando E. Rosas, Judith Allanson, John D. Pickard, Robin L. Carhart-Harris, Guy B. Williams, Michael M. Craig et al. (2024), *eLife 12*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-799](../notes.d/NOTE-799.md).*

- **The workspace, operationally.** Regions are ranked by total synergy
  and by total redundancy. Those whose synergy rank exceeds their
  redundancy rank form the workspace (from the authors' earlier paper).
  Within it, the sign of (synergy-network participation-coefficient rank
  − redundancy-network rank) marks gateways (positive) and broadcasters
  (negative). Every workspace region is one or the other by
  construction.
- **Where they fall.** Gateways lie mainly in the default mode network,
  and broadcasters in the executive control network, with lateral
  prefrontal cortex prominent. This replicates with a coarser
  parcellation and with data-driven modules.
- **What tracks consciousness.** Pairwise ΦR is
  I(past; future) − I(X_past; X_future) − I(Y_past; Y_future) + Red.
  A shared-effect test across three contrasts was used: propofol against
  wake, propofol against recovery, and DOC against controls. Edges with
  a consistent decrease link precuneus, medial prefrontal and inferior
  parietal cortex, all gateways. The decreases in medial prefrontal and
  posterior cingulate or precuneus are robust to parcellation and
  threshold.
- **What does not.** The original whole-minus-sum Φ finds no common
  decrease. Increases near lateral prefrontal broadcasters do not
  survive robustness checks.
- **Limits the authors state.** Resting-state BOLD, pairwise and
  undirected measures on IIT 2.0's Φ, and no claim that ΦR causes or is
  consciousness. Possibly disconnected but conscious participants under
  propofol. Resting state may overweight the DMN. The shared-effect test
  is "described in a future publication".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md), as
"measured evidence on whether conscious access is one uniform broadcast or
has distinct parts". Read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-799](../notes.d/NOTE-799.md). The reading
narrows that description. The paper measures the state of consciousness
at rest, with no contents and no report, so it cannot show a content
reaching some consumers and not others, which is what [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md) asks
for. And its division of the workspace into gateways and broadcasters is
imposed by the method. What it does show is that the
consciousness-tracking change is localised to one side, default-mode
gateways, which is a measured non-uniformity of the workspace with
respect to state. Filed as [THEORY-240](../theory.d/THEORY-240.md), with no `answers:` relation,
since the question is Deferred. It sits beside the GNW account
([THEORY-096](../theory.d/THEORY-096.md)): it agrees that lateral prefrontal cortex broadcasts, and
locates the change elsewhere. It does not bear on [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md)'s compartmented
access.
