At the owner's word ("add bounds, and do cases and terms later"),
[ADR-tmppj46c](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmppj46c.md) adds `bounds`, a reading that marks the limit of a claim's
scope. This is its first application ([ADR-071](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-071.md)).

## The list

Every claim in force with a "What it does not say" section, or a
`defeated_if`, that cites a reading in force with no relation to it:
114 pairs from scope sections, 10 from `defeated_if`, 124 in all, on
claims split three ways by code. Three readers read each claim's passage
and the reading's title and summary. The filer combined the parts; nine
readings were touched by two parts, and each keeps both changes as
successive versions.

## Verdicts

| part | pairs | bounds | corroborates | none | flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 37 | 27 | 2 | 8 | 0 |
| 2 | 49 | 34 | 0 | 15 | 0 |
| 3 | 38 | 30 | 0 | 8 | 0 |
| all | 124 | 91 | 2 | 31 | 0 |

Each `bounds` quotes the claim's words in the reading's history. The two
`corroborates` are passages that use the reading as support, not as a
limit: [THEORY-1115](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1115.md) on [CLAIM-491](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-491.md) (fusion and León's "we" "fit 'larger'
better") and [THEORY-1088](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1088.md) on [CLAIM-496](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-496.md) (a supporting parallel).

**None, by kind.** Unanswered objections the claim names as such
([CLAIM-362](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) and [CLAIM-545](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-545.md) on [THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md); [CLAIM-009](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-009.md) on [THEORY-013](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-013.md));
attributions and prior art ([CLAIM-385](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-385.md), [CLAIM-402](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-402.md), [CLAIM-495](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-495.md), [CLAIM-409](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-409.md));
rival or neighbouring accounts the claim weighs as positions ([CLAIM-430](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-430.md),
[CLAIM-613](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-613.md), [CLAIM-644](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-644.md) on the sceptics' school); readings named in
passing; and every `defeated_if` pair, which all named a result that
would defeat the claim rather than a regime it excludes.

## What the decision now says

The readers met the same near cases, and the decision's point 2 now
settles them: a reading that holds what the claim leaves out ("that is
THEORY-n's question") bounds it; a reading given as the reason for a
disclaimer bounds it; a reading weighed as a position, named as the
defeater or placed as prior art does not; the words decide where a limit
also supports the headline.

## For whoever runs parallel readers next

Use a unique scratch file name per reader; one reader read another's file
by name. `luria relate` reads standard input, so it is not run inside a
`while read` loop without `</dev/null`.
