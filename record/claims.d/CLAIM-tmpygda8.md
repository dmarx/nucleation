# CLAIM-tmpygda8: A partner-dancing couple, while dancing, is one combined cognitive form on the extended-mind thesis's own terms, more clearly than Otto and his notebook, and merits a name of its own

## The claim

The owner, on first hearing the dancers objection ([CLAIM-tmpk62ey](CLAIM-tmpk62ey.md)): "my
immediate thoughts are that the objection fails and that by the extended
mind thesis ... consider a pair of social dancers, like a lindy hop or
tango or salsa dancing couple. under extended mind thesis, I'd argue that
the pair, while dancing together, comprises a combined form that merits some
privileged nomenclature of some kind".

The argument from the record's readings:

- **Clark & Chalmers already allow it.** Their §5 endorses socially
  extended states: in "an unusually interdependent couple" one partner can
  play the notebook's role for the other, given "a high degree of trust,
  reliance, and accessibility" ([NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)). A dancing couple meets that
  bar for the length of a dance. Its coupling is also two-way and
  continuous, which Otto's is not ([CASE-tmpbv3ts](../cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md)).
- **The objection's source cuts the other way.** De Jaegher & Di Paolo
  ([LIT-571](../literature.d/LIT-571.md)) do require the interactors to stay autonomous. They also call
  the interaction "an emergent and autonomous process". Their picture is
  nested autonomy: two dancers and a dance. The objection cited the first
  half and not the second.
- **Two selves remaining is no argument against one combined form.** Otto
  stays Otto, and the extended-mind thesis never needed the notebook to
  stop being a notebook.

## What it does not say

- **That the pair is a self in the subject sense.** [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md) keeps "self"
  for being a subject, and nothing here gives the pair a subject. Whether
  that sense, or Clark & Chalmers' dispositional sense of an extended self
  ([NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)), is the right one to ask about is the next question. The
  owner's method of 2026-10-11 is to name and characterize the form first,
  then ask about "self".
- **What the name is.** "Consort", an extended "coordinative structure"
  and "holon" were discussed in chat. None is adopted.
