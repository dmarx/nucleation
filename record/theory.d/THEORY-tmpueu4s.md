# THEORY-tmpueu4s: Anger makes fake news viral online: on Weibo fake news carries more anger and less joy than true news, and the anger in a post causes it to be shared more

## Source

Chuai & Zhao (2020), LIT-379 (arXiv 2004.10399v3), read in full in
NOTE-852. What their corpus showed is THEORY-329, which this entry
extends.

## The thesis

The paper's title is its thesis, and the text calls the effect causal
throughout: fake news is angrier than true news, and anger in a post makes
it spread.

## Why it is not held

- **The causal half.** The evidence is regression on observational data,
  with no experiment, instrument or natural experiment, and the models
  leave nearly 90% of the variance unexplained. Content carrying anger
  words may carry other features that do the work.
- **The comparison half.** Fake posts were tagged by Weibo's fact-checkers
  after they had spread widely, and true posts come from verified
  accounts, so the fake-against-true gap mixes falsity with selection on
  spread and on source. Highly retweeted posts carry more anger whatever
  their label (Table S5).
