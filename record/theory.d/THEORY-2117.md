# THEORY-2117: Apes don't point, lacking shared intentionality

## The account

Apes gesture flexibly and follow gaze, but no ape points for another. Five
reasons, all one: apes do not understand communicative intentions, lack
shared common ground, lack motives to help and share information, do not
track what is new for the other, and learn gestures by ritualization, as
one-way procedures.

## Evidence

Review of observational and experimental work with apes and infants.
