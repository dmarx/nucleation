# THEORY-tmpuerfk: Perez-Zapata, McKenzie-Smart, Charest and Apperly find that children aged four to five and six to seven, like adults, converge above chance on the same answers in pure coordination games without communication, though less than adults and sometimes on different answers from adults

## The account

Without communication, children from 4–5 match each other's answers above
chance in pure coordination tasks, less often than adults, and sometimes
on salient options that differ from adults'.

## Evidence

Two studies with children and adults, one pre-registered.
