# LIT-tmpabr1n: Studien über die Bildung und Umwandlung fester Körper

Wilhelm Ostwald (1897), *Zeitschrift für Physikalische Chemie* 22, 289–330 — DOI-10.1515/zpch-1897-2233

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for.

- The rule of stages (Stufenregel): of the phases a supersaturated system
  can form, the one that appears first is usually not the most stable, but
  the one closest to the initial state.
- The system then converts to more stable phases in steps, each metastable
  form giving way to the next.
- It is an empirical rule with known exceptions, and it is standardly
  cited as such.
