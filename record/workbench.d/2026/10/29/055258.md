**What the pass took.** The worklist's new section listed 14 Active granted
claims whose `objects_to` target had never been revised after the
concession ([ADR-059](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-059.md)), on 10 targets. Three reviewers read five clusters
read-only, and every recommendation was checked against the entries before
it was applied.

**Eight targets revised.**

- [CLAIM-137](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-137.md) now states the condition its 2026-10-10 reply to [CLAIM-249](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-249.md)
  relied on: one symmetry group anchored on the situation, not on labels of
  a medium. [CLAIM-132](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-132.md)'s bounds were already in version 1.
- [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md), [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-143](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-143.md) keep their titles, which are A218's
  wording and the manuscript's thesis. [CLAIM-238](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-238.md)'s concession is a fault in
  their defeat conditions, nulls with no margin over observables the thesis
  could re-choose, so the conditions now fix observables, weights, a margin
  and a power before the data. [CLAIM-143](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-143.md)'s modal disjunct is marked as met
  only by argument ([CLAIM-239](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-239.md)). [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md) and [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) are rivals on the
  fidelity clause, so they no longer complement each other ([CLAIM-211](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-211.md)), and
  [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md) answers [QUESTION-002](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-002.md).

- [CLAIM-005](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-005.md) is narrowed to say the match constrains a translation only with
  one correspondence fixed across many items, and charges for loss and not

- [CLAIM-084](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-084.md)'s recommended outcome and its defeat condition now wait on a
  reliable footing measure ([CLAIM-227](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-227.md)).

**What the check misses.** Three things the reviewers found about the
worklist itself:

- It reads only `role: granted`. [CLAIM-222](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-222.md), [CLAIM-226](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-226.md) and [CLAIM-243](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-243.md) are
  `role: counter`, went Active under the old "a conceded one goes Active"
  convention, and concede against [CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md), [CLAIM-139](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-139.md) and [CLAIM-005](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-005.md). This
  pass took in [CLAIM-222](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-222.md)'s and [CLAIM-226](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-226.md)'s repairs; [CLAIM-243](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-243.md)'s needs a new
  title, which is [CLAIM-220](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-220.md)'s.
- It cannot tell a target filed with the concession already in its text.
  Exempting targets dated on or after the grant would be wrong: [CLAIM-013](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-013.md)
  was filed the same day as [CLAIM-097](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-097.md) and still overstated.
- `live_objections()` counts a granted objector as live after its target
  has been narrowed, though [ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md) says narrowing answers it. Every target
  revised here stays off the "unblocked" list for that reason alone.

**The owner's delegated rulings (same day).** The owner left the five
questions to the reviewer's judgement, and said that superseding a claim
others rest on is a feature: the dependents move to the successor, and the
lint's findings are the list of that follow-up, not a reason against it.

- **CLAIM-220 supersedes CLAIM-115, and CLAIM-204 supersedes CLAIM-139.**
  Both restatements had waited since 2026-10-10, and each answers objections
  its predecessor could meet only by a new title (CLAIM-243) or a replaced
  disjunct (CLAIM-206). CLAIM-078 and CLAIM-450 now rest on CLAIM-220.
  CLAIM-078 keeps its weaker comparator, text similarity, and says so.
  ARG-019 and ARG-040 conclude CLAIM-220. ARG-033 still concludes CLAIM-115,
  because its inference is CLAIM-115's rest on CLAIM-082, which CLAIM-220

  CLAIM-115's `works` and its bearing on QUESTION-021, and not its rest on
  CLAIM-486, since a comparison of predictors does not need necessity to
  fail. Both successors' defeat conditions now fix the power and the weights
  before the data, so CLAIM-238's repair carries over.
- **No `rivals` relation for CLAIM.** The one case was CLAIM-139 against
  CLAIM-115, and both are now superseded. Their successors are reconciled
  by CLAIM-223's reading, not opposed.
- **CLAIM-005's defeat condition is restated**: one correspondence fixed
  across items and scored held out (CLAIM-207), a margin and a power
  (CLAIM-238), and a named population of judges (CLAIM-226). CASE-184
  notes that this repairs the margin, and not CLAIM-232's or CLAIM-233's
  faults.
- **CLAIM-013 stays general, and Proposed.** Scoped to the four benchmarks
  the record has read, it would be a survey fact, and the manuscript's

- **CLAIM-295's defeat condition** now tests the title's computational

  it does not say" already disclaimed. It stays Proposed, because a state

- **The worklist gaps** are fixed in a separate pull request. CLAIM-301 and
  CLAIM-295 use its new "filed with its concessions" heading. CLAIM-071
  against CLAIM-001, which that change newly lists, is left for the next
