The owner asked for arXiv 1807.04203: Carù, *Towards a complete cohomology
invariant for non-locality and contextuality*. It is filed as [LIT-280](../../record/literature.d/LIT-280.md)
and read in full as [NOTE-253](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-253.md) (v1, the only version, 46 pp.). It is
`Active`, and it `extends` [LIT-279](../../record/literature.d/LIT-279.md). No published version was found. The
author's 2019 DPhil thesis was not obtained, so whether it repairs §8 is
unverified.

**What holds: Theorem 7.7.** On cyclic covers, ℤ/2 cohomology of the
(N−1)-th iterated joint model is exact for logical and strong
contextuality. The reader implemented the construction and checked it on
659 random contextual cycle models. It detects:

- Carù 2017's (2,2,4) model at level 3, on 22 of 22 sections;
- Hardy's one contextual section at level 3.

The paper claims both, and the computation confirms them. So both of the
record's gaps on cycles are closed.

**What fails.**

- **Conjecture 9.1 is refuted.** Under the paper's Definition 7.1, [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md)'s
  §8 cover is undetected at every level. The reader built the lifted
  witness family explicitly up to level 4, which has 1350 contexts. I re-ran
  the reader's level-1 witness script when filing, and its independent
  compatibility check passes.
- **Prop 8.2 is false** on the paper's own Table 5 model.
- **The paper's recount of the §8 cover is wrong** (4/15 against the correct
  9/15).
- **The worked examples use a weaker, context-wise definition** than the
  theorems. That weaker version detects the §8 cover at level 2; that
  finding is the reader's.
- **The abstract's "vast majority of empirical models"** is established only
  for cycles.
- **Cost** is never analysed. The iterated objects grow exponentially:
  5, 10, 30, 150, 1350 contexts on the §8 cover.

**[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) (version bump).** Its cohomology line records the partial
repair on cycles and the refutation off them, and "still open" is
restated. The claim itself is unchanged.
