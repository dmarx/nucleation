  bottleneck learns a stochastic abstraction T of discrete variables X by
  trading the information T keeps about X against Ic(Y | do(T)), the
  entropy reduction in a target Y when T is set by intervention under a
  uniform protocol over X. Intervening on T is defined as a mixture of
  interventions on X through Bayes inversion of the encoder, and is
  identifiable by a backdoor formula. At full weight on control the

  macrovariables, which the ordinary bottleneck misses under
  confounding. Three toy SCMs confirm it.

# LIT-1802: The Causal Information Bottleneck and Optimal Causal Variable Abstractions

Simoes, F. N. F. Q., Dastani, M., & van Ommen, T. (2025). The causal information bottleneck and optimal causal variable abstractions. *UAI 2025*, PMLR 286, 3878–3897.

## Why this is here

A7 joined three of the record's ideas through it: task-relative fidelity ([CLAIM-259](../claims.d/CLAIM-259.md)), legibility for a bounded receiver ([CLAIM-455](../claims.d/CLAIM-455.md)), and the difference between supporting prediction and supporting control. A rendering may keep what a task's control needs while losing most of the source. [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md)'s caution stands: optimizing for one task does not make it the task that matters.

The reading ([NOTE-1347](../notes.d/NOTE-1347.md)) bears this out, with one addition: control
here is relative to a chosen intervention protocol as well as to the
target, and the intervention on T is a stipulation, not a mechanism.

## Note of 2026-10-29: whose causal macrovariables, and why not `extends`

The summary identifies the full-control optimum with "Chalupka et al.'s
causal macrovariables". Two of the Chalupka papers are now registered and
read: the multi-level cause–effect paper, LIT-tmplfiq6 (NOTE-tmpiw2bv),
and the overview, LIT-tmpmefe6 (NOTE-tmpbrnim). Checked against arXiv
2410.00535v5 on 2026-10-29: this paper takes its Definition 19, of causal
classes, partitions and macrovariables, from Chalupka et al. 2016a,
"Unsupervised discovery of El Nino using causal feature learning on
microlevel climate data" (arXiv:1605.09370), which is not registered. The
multi-level paper is its 2016b and the overview its 2017, and Appendix B.3
shows the coincidence with "the causal macrovariables from Chalupka et al.
[2016a, 2017]". Its related work presents them as "one other line of work
investigating a problem related to our search", with a definition
"distinct from our definition", and says "Our method subsumes theirs". So
the paper recovers Chalupka et al.'s partition as a special case; it does
not build on LIT-tmplfiq6, and no `extends` is added. The same partition,
from the cause side under a protocol, is THEORY-1125's; THEORY-tmpyfcov
finds it, with effects as well, where EI under one common distribution
loses nothing.
