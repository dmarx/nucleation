# LIT-2537: Colonialism and Slavery in Performance: Theatre and the Eighteenth-Century French Caribbean

Jeffrey M. Leichman and Karine Bénac-Giroux, eds. (2021). *Colonialism and Slavery in Performance: Theatre and the Eighteenth-Century French Caribbean*. Oxford University Studies in the Enlightenment. Liverpool University Press. ISBN 9781800348042.

## Key takeaways

*Not read; as A describes it.* A3: the chapters treat colonial blackface, Creole theatrical identity, military spectacle and the politics of enslaved people's dance; Bernard Camier's chapter on *Jeannot et Thérèse* reads a local Creole identity, and Sean Anderson's the political potential of enslaved people's *calenda* and its regulation. A3 draws the point that cultural difference is not automatically political resistance. Unverified until read.
