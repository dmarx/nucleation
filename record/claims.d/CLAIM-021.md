# CLAIM-021: The mechanisms composing a will-organization are not novel

## The claim

"We do not claim that the individual mechanisms composing a will-organization
are novel." The concession narrows [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md) to cross-realization
unification.

## Why it is filed as granted

It is accepted and it cuts against a stronger reading of the thesis. Filing it
means a later draft cannot quietly claim the mechanisms after all.
