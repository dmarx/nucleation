# THEORY-tmpqise3: In the abelian Higgs model a thin vortex line follows a Nambu–Goto string: for a timelike minimal surface with zero initial velocity there are solutions whose energy concentrates near it and that stay close to the Nielsen–Olesen vortex across it, for a finite time, wherever the two-dimensional vortex problem has a minimizer ordered by charge, which is known at critical coupling and for large coupling

Czubak & Jerrard (2012), LIT-tmp0jm6f.

## The result

Theorem 1.4 (§1.7). Take a smooth, closed, embedded timelike minimal
surface with zero initial velocity. The authors construct initial data
whose solution satisfies two bounds on a finite time interval: it stays
within Cε² of the Nielsen–Olesen profile near the surface, and its energy
away from the surface is at most Cε². The hypothesis is that the 2d
problem has a minimizer with energies ordered by charge. Jaffe and Taubes
conjectured this for every coupling; it "is known to be true in the case
λ = 1" and for large λ (Remark 1.2).

## What it does not show

- **That vortex lines form, or how they end.** The initial data are
  constructed, and the interval is finite.
- **That every vortex configuration of a sector is a single string.**
  Above critical coupling the symmetric vortex with |n| ≥ 2 is linearly
  unstable (Remark 1.1). What a sector contains depends on the coupling.
- **That fundamental strings are vortices.** It concerns the classical
  field theory only.
