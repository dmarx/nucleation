# LEAD-tmptupsj: Read the communicative accounts of emotion against the claim reading

## What it would do

Read, by the read-and-file procedure, the accounts that treat emotion or
its expression as communication. Registered 2026-11-26, Deferred:
LIT-tmpp11wp (Fridlund), LIT-tmpfgz0s (Van Kleef), LIT-tmpfg9h1
(Scarantino) and LIT-tmpo0t2n (Keltner and Haidt).

- Fridlund's behavioral ecology view of facial displays, in which displays
  are social tools that signal intentions and are not readouts of an inner
  state (*Human Facial Expression: An Evolutionary View*, 1994).
- Van Kleef's emotions-as-social-information model, in which others'
  emotions inform an observer's inferences and evoke affective reactions in
  them (2009).
- Scarantino's theory of affective pragmatics, in which emotional
  expressions do things the way speech acts do (2017).
- Keltner and Haidt on the social functions of emotion at four levels of
  analysis (1999).

## What it would settle

- Whether a communicative account can keep Frijda's action readiness
  ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)) as the content communicated. [CLAIM-tmps1h34](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmps1h34.md) needs this, and
  Fridlund's view, which denies that displays read out a state, may refuse
  it.
- Whether affective pragmatics maps onto [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s claim and grant:
  emotional expression as a directive, and the grant as its uptake.
- What honest and dishonest emotional signalling looks like, beside
  Trivers ([THEORY-663](../theory.d/THEORY-663.md)), for [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)'s question of remorse and prudence.

Each work is one reading. Delegating the reading waits for the owner.
