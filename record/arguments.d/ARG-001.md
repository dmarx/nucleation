# ARG-001: Moving severity classification, or adding a business-critical override, changes outcomes without changing the rule, so precedence, jurisdiction and suspension come apart

## The inference

One coherent case in which each relation is varied while the others are held
fixed.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes; it is a plausible institutional arrangement.
- **Does it separate what the conclusion says comes apart?** Yes for
  jurisdiction (who classifies) versus precedence (the rule's force), and for
  suspension (the override) versus both.
