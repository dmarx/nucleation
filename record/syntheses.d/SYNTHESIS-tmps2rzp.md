# SYNTHESIS-tmps2rzp: A communicative object is a context-indexed system of empirical constraints whose global realizability is to be found out, not assumed: where it fails, the obstruction lies in the supports or distributions, not in the event sheaf, in contexts that do not overlap or in frames whose correspondence is fixed, and it is neither ordinary context dependence nor ambiguity; within a fixed scenario the headline that translation keeps local meanings while changing global structure has only constructed cases, since the behavioural data read show no contextuality proper, and it is carried forward as a change in which questions readers can decide, whose standing as a change of cover is disputed; translation operations form a semigroup of directed lossy maps whose invertible core is the relabellings; whether communicative categories form a concept lattice is open; and many of the line's defeat conditions cannot be met by any study

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part on the
communicative object as context-indexed constraints, on where and how such
an object can fail to have one global realization, and on what the record
holds of the headline that translation keeps local meanings while changing
the global structure of meaning. The line's account is built over it and
its sibling part accounts. It integrates 57 entries. Two entries the
mechanical map placed here are left to sibling parts, because their
relations and text point there: CLAIM-257, the restated observational
distortion, to the fidelity part (it answers CLAIM-256, uses TERM-043 and
complements CLAIM-076, all there), and QUESTION-024, on inducing a frame
from examples, to the study part (its body calls it "the U17 claim
(CLAIM-029) at its most testable"). Their links to this part are listed
below. Several components are also integrated elsewhere: CLAIM-627 and
ARG-057, whose first line is will-organization, by SYNTHESIS-036, and
QUESTION-160 and QUESTION-162, whose first line is learned-structure, by
SYNTHESIS-030; this account integrates them again because their `line`
includes pragmatic-transport. The account adds nothing; its standing is
its components'.*

## The account

- **The object.** TERM-042 (A129 §2, at the owner's U41, Active) defines
  it: "A communicative observational object is a context-indexed system of
  empirically admissible local assignments or distributions, equipped
  with restriction relationships. Its global realizability is a property
  to be investigated, not part of its definition." CLAIM-038 (A78, thesis,
  interpretive, Active) is the thesis stated in it: "Local interpretations
  need not be fragments of a single globally realizable interpretation",
  and in its version 2 what need not extend "are the supports or
  distributions the data impose on those sections". It "does not
  presuppose that they are not" contextual (CLAIM-009). QUESTION-332
  (Answered) takes from it that the object is a presheaf over contexts.
- **Where non-gluing lives.** CLAIM-131 (A125 and A129, granted,
  demonstrated, Active): "In the ordinary event sheaf, a compatible family
  of local sections already glues to a global section", so the
  obstruction lies in supports or distributions; a negative answer over
  the supports gives one over the distributions. It does not cover causal
  scenarios, where the strategy presheaf is not a sheaf, and it does not
  grade the obstruction. TERM-042 gives the three grades, possibilistic,
  strong and probabilistic. CASE-035 (the manuscript's §3, stipulated,
  Active) is the parity obstruction, "an obstruction, not a claim about
  actual judgments"; CASE-037 (A129 §2, with the reader's addition, Active)
  gives it uniform weights, "a no-signalling, strongly contextual model
  beside a family of point sections that glues".
- **Levels and observables.** CLAIM-042 (the owner's U28, accepted by A75,
  thesis, interpretive, Active): levels organize observables, not
  objects, and an object is identified jointly by constraints from several
  levels, an intersection only where the observables share one state
  space; it says A78 "generalized it to local sections (CLAIM-038)".
  TERM-009 (the owner's U28, Active) is that sense of level, and TERM-037
  (the manuscript §2, Active) defines an observable as "a reproducible
  elicitation or coding procedure that assigns outcomes to communicative
  realizations".
- **The logic of the object.** QUESTION-247 (Answered): over a presheaf
  of contexts the negation is "the Heyting pseudo-complement" that the
  base category fixes. QUESTION-055 (Open) keeps "over which base category
  is it one, and does some probe family split it into sectors that no
  probe connects?".
- **Contextuality is not context dependence.** CLAIM-037 (the manuscript's
  §3 and §9, thesis, interpretive, Proposed): formal contextuality is a
  failure of global extension, "Order effects (AB ≠ BA) occur in classical
  Markov systems and establish no contextuality", and it needs
  Contextuality-by-Default when marginals shift with context. Its note of
  2026-10-29 says that "needs" is contested by two sheaf routes that allow
  for signalling. ARG-022 (the ADR-061 migration, Rejected) held that the
  2016 Contextuality-by-Default paper grounds the criterion the
  manuscript's general covers need; it was rejected on its own named
  condition, a worked system noncontextual under CbD 1.0 and contextual
  under 2.0.
- **Contextuality is not ambiguity.** CLAIM-044 (A81 and A82, recovered,
  thesis, interpretive, Proposed): "an ambiguous utterance admits several
  compatible global assignments, a contextual one admits none". Its note
  of 2026-10-30 says the title fails under both its readings, on Wang et
  al.'s tap/box model, and that its defeat condition "cannot be met".
- **Whether pragmatic judgements are contextual.** CLAIM-009 (A24 and
  A30, supposed, interpretive, Proposed) is the supposition the manuscript
  makes in order to test it. QUESTION-001 (A24, Open) asks how far "an
  utterance's pragmatic identity" can be "reconstructed from a globally
  consistent collection of context-relative judgements, and what fails
  when it cannot". QUESTION-067 (Open) asks under what probe families
  renderings count as operationally equivalent, so that Spekkens's
  generalized notion applies; QUESTION-246 (Answered) holds what each kind
  of failure refutes, and that the generalized notion is strictly broader.
  QUESTION-102 (A15 §6, Open): "Failure to compute a global section is not
  the absence of one."
- **Disagreement is not obstruction.** CLAIM-143 (A164, after the owner's
  U47, thesis, interpretive, Active): "whether there is an obstruction can
  be assessed only relative to a specified class of admissible
  correspondences between their observations", sorting disagreements into
  coordinate difference, structural transformation and global
  obstruction, with the proviso that a correspondence fitted to remove the
  disagreement "decides nothing". CLAIM-144 (A164's correction with the
  record's two repairs, granted, demonstrated, Active): inertial observers
  "differ in coordinates, not in a failure to glue"; it is CLAIM-143's
  "positive control". CASE-039 (the owner's scenario, documented, Active)
  is the case. ARG-010 (the owner's inference at U46 and U47, Rejected)
  offered causal paradoxes as evidence for CLAIM-038 and is undercut by
  CLAIM-144 and CLAIM-141. CLAIM-141 (A161, sharpened, granted,
  demonstrated, Active): passing to distributions affects causal loops and
  contextual models "oppositely".
- **The cover decides.** CLAIM-197 (dual-filed from MOM-CLM-088, thesis,
  demonstrated, Active): "a failure needs an overlap carrying sections
  that disagree, and which families count as covers decides whether
  anything glues"; its note of 2026-11-07 records that for presheaves of
  realizable observations its first clause fails too, leaving the theorem
  about sheaves untouched. CLAIM-627 (A43, thesis, interpretive, Proposed;
  first line will-organization) carries the choice of cover to
  jurisdiction: who decides which contexts count "fix part of the cover
  on which its uses must glue". ARG-057 (Rejected) finds that its case
  "measures outcomes and not compatibility", and leaves its distinctive
  clause on CLAIM-197 and CLAIM-057 "with a bridging premise no entry
  supplies".
- **The headline and its possibility.** CLAIM-105 (A78's thesis, thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed): "In documented reconstruction chains, steps
  that keep most local judgements commonly change their global
  compatibility". Its reading of the contextual fraction: it is undefined
  where marginals depend on context, "cannot rise along free operations",
  and "does not jump within a scenario". CLAIM-320 (split from it,
  demonstrated, Active) is the possibility it needs: two no-signalling
  models agreeing on all but one context and every marginal, with
  contextual fractions 1 and 0. CLAIM-319 (split from CLAIM-201,
  demonstrated, Active) is the general odd-cycle form, at weighted
  distortion 1/n, and "any change of global extension needs a contextual
  model on one side".
- **The data and the dilemma.** CLAIM-325 (split from CLAIM-219,
  empirical, Active): "The behavioural data the record has read show no
  contextuality proper". CASE-053 (documented, Active) collects the tests
  and scopes it: "the case documents an *absence of adequate tests* as
  much as a null". CLAIM-219 (the record's audit, counter, demonstrated,
  Active) objects to CLAIM-105: a change of global compatibility over a
  fixed scenario is either contextuality proper, which the data read lack,
  or a change in overlap consistency, which "moves by at most 2ε when each
  context moves by ε". ARG-032 (the ADR-061 migration, Rejected) is the
  inference from CASE-037 to CLAIM-105, which CLAIM-219 undercuts: "a
  constructed case is at most an existence proof".
- **The headline narrowed.** CLAIM-201 (the record's reply of
  2026-10-10, thesis, interpretive, Proposed) concedes the fixed-scenario
  form and proposes that "the form of the headline those data leave open
  is a change of cover", made operational: "a context is a question whose
  answers readers give determinately". It "does not say a change of cover
  is contextuality". CASE-072 (Hafez's "Shirazi Turk", documented, Active)
  is the half in which the rendering decides what the source left open;
  CASE-059 (Tatiana's letter, documented, Active) the half in which the
  source decides what a rendering makes undecidable. Neither measures
  reader agreement. CLAIM-251 (the interlocutor's reading, corrected,
  counter, demonstrated, Proposed) objects that such a change "is a change
  of empirical model", not of cover in the sheaf sense, and that "the
  operational notion needs its own name until a functorial link is
  shown". QUESTION-004 (A52 §12.4, Open) asks whether drift under repeated
  reconstruction "systematically increase or reduce measured contextual
  dependence".
- **Transformations, symmetry and the relativity analogy.** ARG-004 (A18,
  Rejected) inferred that translation is a change between pragmatic frames
  preserving invariants. CLAIM-035 (A18's own limit, granted,
  interpretive, Active) undercuts it: translation operations "are
  generally not invertible, need not compose by a fixed rule, and preserve
  no structure given in advance". CLAIM-004 (A18, thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed) undercuts it too: "Some differences are changes to what is
  being framed", still without a criterion that sorts pairs of renderings.
  CLAIM-098 (the manuscript's §4, thesis, interpretive, Active): they
  "form a category or semigroup of directed stochastic transformations, in
  which invertible symmetries are special cases". CLAIM-318 (split from
  CLAIM-103, demonstrated, Active): among Markov kernels between finite
  sets "the invertible core is the relabellings". CLAIM-103 (A110 §25,
  recovered, thesis, interpretive, Proposed): symmetries' main use is "to
  supply the invariants against which non-symmetric transports are
  assessed". CLAIM-113 (A105, after the owner's U37, thesis, interpretive,
  Active): "Transformations and invariants define one another". TERM-004
  (A105, Active) is equivariance, and TERM-036 (A105, Active) fidelity as
  "preservation of selected observables and structural symmetries".
- **Categories as a lattice.** CLAIM-119 (A75 §7, recovered, thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed): communicative categories "form a concept
  lattice rather than a hierarchy". QUESTION-025 (Open) asks whether
  correlated or hierarchical attributes still give linear attribute
  directions and "a concept lattice that is not Boolean";
  QUESTION-178, QUESTION-160 and QUESTION-162 (Open) refine it.
- **Defeat conditions and method.** CLAIM-238 (the record's audit,
  granted, demonstrated, Active): "Many of the line's defeat conditions
  cannot in practice be met". CLAIM-239 (the record's reply, thesis,
  demonstrated, Active) narrows its fourth point: a condition that says
  something cannot be done "is met by an argument that it cannot, not by a
  record of failures". CLAIM-095 (the owner's U19, accepted by A49, thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed): "Anticipated objections are better met by
  precise definitions, constructions and results than by hedges". CASE-029
  (A35, A38 and A40, a proposed study, Active) is the programme whose
  falsifiers that rule's sweep removed.

## Not stated on the record

Integrating sentences across the components that no entry states, tested
by ADR-075's step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **A change of cover is a change of ambiguity.** That where the data are
  noncontextual (CLAIM-325), a question that is indeterminate for one
  side's readers and determinate for the other's (CLAIM-201) is an
  ambiguity in CLAIM-044's sense on the first side, since a noncontextual
  model is a mixture of global assignments and a spread answer needs more
  than one of them. As a derivation it is valid and trivial. "Ambiguity",
  read as any mixture of several global assignments, is a free parameter:
  it absorbs every source of low agreement CLAIM-251's reading lists,
  "ambiguity, heterogeneity, uncertainty, or an inadequately defined
  response variable", and CLAIM-044's own reading of the term waits on the
  owner (its note of 2026-10-30). CLAIM-251 already states the narrower
  link, that low agreement "may indicate ambiguity".
- **No exact symmetry changes the cover.** That a relabelling, the whole
  invertible core of CLAIM-318, keeps how concentrated each answer
  distribution is, so a change in which questions readers decide
  (CLAIM-201) lies wholly outside the symmetries of CLAIM-098's semigroup.
  Valid and trivial: an exact transport keeps every property invariant
  under relabelling. And whether the undecided question has a distribution
  on the other side, as CLAIM-251 says, or is no context there, as
  CLAIM-201 says, is the open point the sentence would need fixed.
- **Drift cannot reduce contextuality on these data.** That on the record's data QUESTION-004's
  "reduce" branch is empty, since the data read are noncontextual
  (CLAIM-325) and the contextual fraction cannot go below zero, while its
  "increase" branch needs a step that is not a free operation (CLAIM-105's
  reading of the contextual fraction). "Measured contextual dependence" is
  a free parameter: read as signalling, it moves freely, and the
  contextual fraction is undefined at any step that signals (CLAIM-105).
  Read as contextuality proper, CLAIM-325 is scoped to the data read, not
  to chains, so the sentence says only that nothing has yet been found to
  reduce.
- **Jurisdiction over relevance is a choice of cover.** That by
  CLAIM-251's sorting of changes into those of scenario and those of
  empirical model, deciding which contexts count as relevant changes the
  scenario, so CLAIM-197 applies to CLAIM-627's jurisdiction. This is the
  bridging premise ARG-057 says "no entry supplies", and CLAIM-627 asserts
  it. ARG-057's reading of CASE-002, where jurisdiction changes only the
  section acted on, is a realistic case in which it fails; it is not
  CLAIM-627's own defeat, which is a universal over institutions.
- **One place for every obstruction.** That every failure of global
  realization the part considers lies in supports or distributions: none
  in the event sheaf (CLAIM-131), across disjoint contexts (CLAIM-197),
  between frames once the correspondence is fixed (CLAIM-143, CLAIM-144),
