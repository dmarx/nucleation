# LEAD-233: Read whether animal migration routes are socially learned and regenerated by use, and whether they are lost when the group's knowledge is: the empirical premise of the migration reading of the pseudo-self question

## What to read

Work on whether migration routes in ungulates and birds are learned from
conspecifics, how translocated or reintroduced populations acquire them, how
long acquisition takes, and whether routes are lost when knowledgeable
individuals are. Also work on how migrants reshape the landscape that cues
the next migration.

## What would settle it for the claim

- Social learning and loss with the group: the premise holds, and the
  route is a variable held as the group's ([CLAIM-1045](../claims.d/CLAIM-1045.md) stands).
- Routes fixed by inheritance or by gradients alone: the premise fails, and
  migration is a driver or an individual trait ([CLAIM-1045](../claims.d/CLAIM-1045.md) is defeated by
  its first clause).
