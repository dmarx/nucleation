The owner asked whether arXiv:2506.02970 bears on the record, then asked for
it to be read and filed. Before filing, a search of the record for
Lindbladian, integrals of motion, slow modes, operator growth and
open-system terms found no earlier work in open quantum many-body dynamics.
The paper bears on the scale-separation line and the slowness readings next
to it, not on the quantum-foundations material.

The citation was checked against arXiv metadata and Crossref, sending no
email address ([ADR-008](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-008.md)). The text was extracted with pypdf (PyMuPDF and
pdftotext are not on this host), and the figures came through as captions
only.

**Filed from it.**
- [THEORY-tmpggrkf](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpggrkf.md) (Active): what the paper derived exactly and what exact
  diagonalization showed at nine sites or fewer, with the ranking's
  dependence on noise strength.
- [THEORY-tmpull8q](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpull8q.md) (Proposed): the large-system argument through a gap of
  order min(γL, √(v_Bγ)), which the authors qualify themselves.

**What the reading does to the record.** Dated notes, none changing a claim:
- **[CLAIM-478](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-478.md).** A route to separation, conservation under weak local
  noise with operator growth setting the fast rate, and support for the
  first half.
- **[CLAIM-331](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-331.md).** What slowness marks depends on the environment, on a
  measure of size, and on the noise strength.
- **[CLAIM-394](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-394.md).** A non-self-adjoint generator where slow-decaying and
  eigen-directions nearly coincide under a small parameter.

**Not flagged `anthology-candidate`.** The paper carries no instruction for
machine-learning practice, and the anthology's topics are about ML. Its
cited neighbours (Mori 2024; Jacoby, Huse and Gopalakrishnan 2025; Wurtz
and Polkovnikov; Moudgalya and Motrunich 2024) are not held here. They
would be the next reading if the line wants the Mori gap itself.
