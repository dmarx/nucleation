# QUESTION-334: Outside NASA, does an organization's first acceptance of a deviation, traced prospectively, predict widening acceptance and later failure more often than in comparable organizations that eliminate deviations, and does an independent safety function interrupt the pattern?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md) holds the Board's reconstruction of two NASA accidents, made
after the fact. The general theory is Vaughan's ([LIT-715](../literature.d/LIT-715.md)), which the
record has not read, and retrospective reconstructions select the cases
that ended badly.

## What would answer it

[THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md)'s former promote_when, unchanged:

Evidence beyond the two Shuttle accidents that the pattern is general
and causal: organizations in which accepted anomalies can be traced
prospectively, showing that the first acceptance of a deviation predicts
widening acceptance and later failure more often than in comparable
organizations that eliminate deviations. It would also need evidence that
an independent safety function, as the Board recommends, interrupts the
pattern. More retrospective accident reconstructions cannot settle it,
since they select the cases that ended badly.

## Note of 2026-10-31: a general mechanism for widening acceptance, and a test of the same design

Tsoukas and Chia ([LIT-1913](../literature.d/LIT-1913.md); [THEORY-1279](../theory.d/THEORY-1279.md)) give a general mechanism
of which widening acceptance of deviations would be one case. An
organization's categories are radially structured. Applying one to a
nonprototypical case is "an imaginative extension" that extends "the
radius of application of the concept, thus transforming it" (p. 574).
The extended category then governs the next case. Read this way, a first
accepted deviation is an application of "acceptable" at its periphery,
and the next deviation is judged against the widened category. They add
that "the indeterminacy of extension does not indicate arbitrariness" (p.
574), so the mechanism does not make each acceptance unreasonable.

This is an analogy of structure. Tsoukas and Chia do not discuss safety,
deviations or failure, and their examples are a statute, a call centre
and a housing routine. What it adds to this question is a test of the
same design. [THEORY-1279](../theory.d/THEORY-1279.md)'s promote_when asks for categories
classified as prototypical or nonprototypical "before seeing what
happens", and predicts more change where more nonprototypical cases are
met. That is this question's prospective tracing, generalized from
deviations to any rule. A study designed for one would serve the other,
if it recorded both the cases a rule met and the failures that followed.
It also names a second route this question does not separate. On Tsoukas
and Chia, reflexive redescription changes categories independently of
the cases met (p. 575).
