The owner asked for *Contextuality, Cohomology and Paradox*: Abramsky,
Barbosa, Kishida, Lal & Mansfield, arXiv 1502.03097, CSL 2015. It is filed
as [LIT-tmp5bjvi](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp5bjvi.md) and read in full as [NOTE-tmp92a59](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp92a59.md). That covers v2, which
matches the published LIPIcs text, and v1, which holds the only proof of
Prop 16. It is `Active`, and it `extends` [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md).

**What it proves.**

- **Theorem 21.** For a connected cover, AvN_R(S) ⇒ SC(Aff S) ⇒ CSC_R(S) ⇒
  CSC_ℤ(S) ⇒ SC(S). So a model with an All-vs-Nothing argument, over any
  commutative ring, is strongly contextual, and its cohomological
  obstruction is non-zero on every section.
- **Theorem 4.** Stabiliser states of n-qubit "AvN triples" of Paulis supply
  such models (qubits only).
- **GHZ(3).** §4.1 is a complete hand proof, the case [NOTE-016](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-016.md) had recorded
  as only sketched.

**The paradox half proves little.**

- **One exact case.** The length-4 Liar cycle is the PR box.
- **The rest is analogy.** The cohomology of paradox is deferred.
- **Scope of the encoding.** It fails for the classic Liar (N = 1) and for
  N = 2. For N ≥ 3 the Liar cycles are All-vs-Nothing over ℤ₂; I checked
  that when filing. The strong contextuality then follows from Theorem 21,
  though the paper doesn't say so.

**[NOTE-250](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-250.md)'s gaps are not closed.** The reader found that Hardy's affine
closure is the whole event sheaf, so no All-vs-Nothing argument reaches
Hardy. By Theorem 21, the §8 strongly contextual cover of [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md) has no
All-vs-Nothing argument over any ring. The paper mentions neither, and v2
deletes v1's sentence about Hardy.

**[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) (version bump).** Its cohomology line now says where the
witness *is* complete: on All-vs-Nothing models. It also says that both of
[LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md)'s documented failures lie outside that class. The derivation for
covers failing the GCD condition is marked as the reader's own. The claim
is unchanged.
