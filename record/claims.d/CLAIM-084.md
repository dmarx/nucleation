# CLAIM-084: Lexical similarity cannot distinguish preservation of communicative footing from a change of speech-act function, so the primary outcome of a reconstruction study is pragmatic-frame retention judged blind to condition, once a measure of footing and stance has been shown reliable

## The claim

A63: "The useful distinction is between **lexical retention and pragmatic
retention**." A70: "This illustrates exactly why lexical similarity is
inadequate for our theory: it cannot distinguish preservation of communicative
footing from a substantial change in speech-act function ... **our primary
outcome should be a measure of pragmatic-frame retention, not lexical
retention**. That requires independently elicited judgments about footing,
stance, and communicative function, ideally without revealing which
transmission condition produced each text."

Manuscript §8: "The structural measurements are more informative than lexical
comparisons alone. A chain might conserve the overspending theme while moving
from speaker–audience solidarity to authoritative advice. Another might change
nearly every word while preserving the invitation to shared recognition."

## What it does not say

That the authored chains show it ([CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md), [CASE-032](../cases.d/CASE-032.md)). They illustrate it, and their
pragmatic retention was asserted, not measured.

## Note of 2026-10-29: revised

[CLAIM-227](CLAIM-227.md) is granted. No design in the record gives judges a definition of
footing, a response format beyond the analyst's categories, a reliability
target, or a validation against a contrast fixed in advance, and the
analyst's own labels of [CASE-012](../cases.d/CASE-012.md)'s refrains changed between A38 and A39.
Until such a measure exists, the outcome this claim recommends is not yet a
measurement, and a test of its defeat condition would test the categories as
much as the claim. [QUESTION-076](../questions.d/QUESTION-076.md) asks whether one can be had.

## Note of 2026-10-29: what holds it back

It has had no evidence of its own since [ARG-039](../arguments.d/ARG-039.md) was Rejected, and no
validated footing measure exists to test the defeat condition.
