## Key takeaways

*Not read.* From the abstract: a computational method that identifies rare
foreign-genotype cells in single-cell transcriptomes, accurate down to
0.05% of cells or lower.

## Standing in the record

The owner's source. It bears on what can be seen, not on what is the case:
a detector makes mixture at the level of cells visible and so testable.
