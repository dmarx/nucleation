# TERM-027: cultural attractor

## Definition

A110 §20: "**The emergence of stable meaning within a community need not imply
faithful retention of ancestral meaning.** A **cultural attractor** may represent
a shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object." Earlier:
A50's "attraction toward certain regions of communicative state space", A86's "a
natural information-theoretic explanation of cultural attractors", and C6 §8's
heading "Iterated reconstruction, drift, and cultural attractors".

## What it is not

Not a preserved original: an attractor is where the chain goes whatever its start,
so reaching it is evidence of forgetting the start ([CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md)). The manuscript
keeps the phenomenon ("A stable conventional endpoint may be far removed from the
ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable", §8) and drops the word and the
reading as a convention ([CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)).
