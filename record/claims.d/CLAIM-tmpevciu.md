# CLAIM-tmpevciu: Interpretive framing operations generally do not commute, and this can be investigated without committing to quantum probability

## The claim

A30: "**H1 — Pragmatic noncommutativity.** Interpretive framing operations
generally do not commute: T_f T_g ≠ T_g T_f. This can be investigated without
committing to quantum probability." Manuscript §9: "Order-dependent framing is
central to pragmatic reconstruction. A prompt emphasizing camaraderie followed
by one emphasizing condemnation may lead to another response distribution than
the reverse order."

## What it does not say

Nothing about nonclassicality ([CLAIM-tmpbwst7](CLAIM-tmpbwst7.md)). It is untested; [CASE-tmpn2q0k](../cases.d/CASE-tmpn2q0k.md) and the
manuscript's Case II (alternating frames) would test it. [CLAIM-tmpx6akp](CLAIM-tmpx6akp.md) rests on it.
