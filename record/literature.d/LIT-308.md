summary: >-
  Goldt & Seifert (2017), DOI-10.1103/PhysRevLett.118.010601. For a
  perceptron whose weights follow overdamped Langevin dynamics and whose
  predicted labels are fast two-state processes, the information learnt
  about fixed random labels is bounded by the weights' total entropy
  production: Σ_μ I(σ_T^μ:σ^μ) ≤ Σ_n [ΔS(ω_n) + ΔQ_n] (Eq. 16, k_B = T =
  1). This defines a learning efficiency η ≤ 1. The cost side includes the
  change in the weights' own Shannon entropy, and in the paper's toy model
  η → 0.99 as the heat ΔQ → 0 (slow driving). So the bound is not a heat
  floor of k_BT ln 2 per bit learnt.
