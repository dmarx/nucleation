## Why this is here

A8 drew from it the distinction between preliminary relief and final remedy, central to [CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md) and [CASE-170](../cases.d/CASE-170.md): a temporary restraining order is about what may happen before judgment, and the two are different intervention problems ([CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md)).
