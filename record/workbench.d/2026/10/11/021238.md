**The method (prompt 1).** Name the thing neutrally, characterize it, and
only then ask whether "self" fits. This is [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s discipline applied to
the session's own drift. In chat the assistant proposed a vocabulary, none
of it adopted:

- **The thing:** "consort", a collection of members or stages that differ
  in value, timing or decoding and act as one only while a maintained
  coordination holds them.
- **Routes:** entrainment, inscription, alignment, suspension and
  bargaining.
- **Failures:** reversal, misreading and drift.
- **Dimensions:** span, horizon, coherence and cadence.

**The adopted alternatives (prompt 2).** In chat, from general knowledge
(neither source is held):

- **Coordinative structure** (Bernstein; Turvey; Kugler, Kelso & Turvey
  1980). A task-specific, soft-assembled unit with no controller, whose
  signature is *reciprocal compensation under perturbation* (Kelso et al.
  1984, the jaw-perturbation studies). It fits assembly, drift and the
  absence of a governor, and it supplies a sharper membership test. But it
  is synchronic, has no place for inscription, and takes its goal from
  outside.
- **Holon** (Koestler 1967, after Simon 1962). A whole to its parts and a
  part to its whole, with self-assertive and integrative tendencies. It
  fits nesting and names the dancers' tension. But it names a position,
  not an achievement, and it would bring back the permissiveness
  objection ([CLAIM-010](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)).

The suggestion was to take the compensation test from the first and the
two tendencies from the second, and keep a word for the maintained thing.
Not decided.

**What moved (prompt 3).** The owner's reply is filed as [CLAIM-tmpygda8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpygda8.md),
and the couple as [CASE-tmpbv3ts](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md). On rereading, the assistant found the
objection had misused its source. [LIT-571](../../record/literature.d/LIT-571.md) requires that the dancers stay
autonomous, and it also calls the interaction "an emergent and autonomous
process". Clark & Chalmers endorse socially extended cognition in an
interdependent couple. So [CLAIM-tmpk62ey](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk62ey.md)'s version 1 failed. Version 2
keeps only the subject sense of "self", and leaves open whether that is the
sense worth asking about. [CLAIM-tmptsdgt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md)'s title said "extended self",
ahead of the owner's method. Version 2 says "combined form".
