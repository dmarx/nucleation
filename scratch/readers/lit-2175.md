### Summary
A one-screen statement. Preamble: "Burning Man co-founder Larry Harvey wrote
the 10 Principles in 2004 as guidelines for the newly-formed Regional
Network. They were crafted not as rules to govern how we should act, but as a
projection of the community's ethos as it developed organically in the
desert, and a set of cultural behaviors reflecting shared values." They are
"Offered, in no particular order", each as a short paragraph: Radical
Inclusion, Decommodification, Gifting, Radical Self-reliance, Communal Effort,
Radical Self-expression, Civic Responsibility, Leaving No Trace,
Participation, Immediacy (page order).

### Contribution
The organization's own statement of the ethos, and the primary source for
the wording every secondary account (St John 2017, LIT-2188) quotes.

### Key results (the principles, quoted in full where the record uses them)
1. **Status of the principles**: "not as rules to govern how we should act,
   but as a projection of the community's ethos ... and a set of cultural
   behaviors reflecting shared values"; written "as guidelines for the
   newly-formed Regional Network" (2004).
2. **Gifting**: "Burning Man is devoted to acts of gift giving. The value of a
   gift is unconditional. Gifting does not contemplate a return or an exchange
   for something of equal value." (Identical to St John's quotation.)
3. **Radical Self-reliance** (page spelling, lower-case r): "Burning Man
   encourages the individual to discover, exercise and rely on their inner
   resources." That is the whole principle. It speaks of *inner* resources,
   not material self-provision, and sets no limit on what may be gifted.
4. **Decommodification**: "In order to preserve the spirit of gifting, our
   community seeks to create social environments that are unmediated by
   commercial sponsorships, transactions, or advertising. ... We resist the
   substitution of consumption for participatory experience." It is
   justified by gifting.
5. **Radical Self-expression**: "arises from the unique gifts of the
   individual. ... It is offered as a gift to others. In this spirit, the giver
   should respect the rights and liberties of the recipient."
6. **Communal Effort**: "Our community values creative cooperation and
   collaboration."
7. **Participation**: "Our community is committed to a radically
   participatory ethic. ... We achieve being through doing. Everyone is invited
   to work. Everyone is invited to play."
8. **Leaving No Trace** (the page's name; St John and the LIT write "Leave No
   Trace"): "We are committed to leaving no physical trace of our activities
   ... We clean up after ourselves". It does **not** contain "pack in all
   their supplies"; that is St John's description of practice (NOTE-1662, p. 3).
9. **Civic Responsibility**: organizers "must also assume responsibility for
   conducting events in accordance with local, state and federal laws."
10. **Radical Inclusion**: "Anyone may be a part of Burning Man. We welcome and
    respect the stranger. No prerequisites exist for participation".
11. **Immediacy**: "Immediate experience is, in many ways, the most important
    touchstone of value in our culture. ... No idea can substitute for this
    experience."

### Assumptions
Not an argument; it states values. Implicit: that a culture can be described
by principles that are "not rules"; that gifting and decommodification are
compatible with paid tickets and a nonprofit organization (not addressed).

### Concepts
gift / gifting (unconditional); decommodification; radical self-reliance as
inner resources; radical self-expression as a gift; communal effort;
participation ("being through doing"); civic responsibility; leaving no trace;
radical inclusion; immediacy; Regional Network.

### Verdict on the record's characterization
**Mostly right; three corrections.**
- Author and year: confirmed by the organization itself ("Larry Harvey wrote
  the 10 Principles in 2004"), not only by St John. Purpose added: "as
  guidelines for the newly-formed Regional Network".
- The list of ten: confirmed, with two naming differences: the page says
  "**Leaving** No Trace" and spells "Radical Self-reliance" / "Radical
  Self-expression". The page's order differs from St John's (Decommodification
  before Gifting), and the page says "in no particular order".
- The Gifting wording St John quotes: confirmed verbatim.
- `url:`: the canonical address is now https://burningman.org/about-us/10-principles/.
- Unverified still: that the current wording is the 2004 wording.

### Bearing on codes
- **CLAIM-603**, section "What the gift readings add":
  - "Generalized" bullet: the Gifting quotation is confirmed first-hand; the
    claim's "(LIT-2175, Unavailable, read through St John)" can cite the page
    directly (note also that the status is Unfetched, not Unavailable).
  - **"Gifts sit beside self-reliance" bullet: needs a correction.** It reads
    Radical Self-reliance as self-provision ("participants bring their own
    supplies ... Both keep some capabilities self-provided and let the rest
    circulate"). The principle's text is only "discover, exercise and rely on
    their inner resources". So as written the principle concerns inner
    resources, not which material capabilities stay self-provided; the
    bring-your-own-supplies practice is St John's report (under Leave No
    Trace), not the principle's wording. The parallel with Mauss ("compter sur
    soi") is weaker than stated: the principle draws no line between what is
    self-provided and what circulates.
  - Supports the claim's point "whether the receiver has any path by which to
    give": Participation ("Everyone is invited to work") and Radical
    Self-expression ("offered as a gift to others") make every participant a
    potential giver. Read so, the ethos builds in the receiver's path to give.
  - Decommodification is grounded in gifting ("In order to preserve the spirit
    of gifting"): the anti-market principle serves the gift principle.
- **NOTE-1662**, open question "How is Radical Self-Reliance worded, and does
  it limit what may be gifted?": answered. It is worded as reliance on "inner
  resources", and it sets no limit on gifting.
- **THEORY-1497 / THEORY-1546** (St John's generalized reciprocity): the page
  confirms the premise (a gift that "does not contemplate a return"), but says
  nothing of gratitude, circulation or return to the community; those remain
  St John's reading.
- **LIT-2188**: its stand-in role ends for the list and the Gifting wording;
  the record can now quote the other nine directly.
- **LEAD-025** (withdrawal episodes): no bearing; the page reports no episode
  of support withdrawn.

### Limitations
- A current web page, revisable; no date of last revision. The 2004 text is
  not established by this copy.
- Statements of values, not descriptions of practice; nothing about whether
  the event lives up to them (St John's ethnography is the record's source
  for that).
- "Not as rules" (preamble): the principles' normative force is left vague;
  the page does not say how conflicts between them (e.g. Radical Inclusion vs
  Decommodification of ticketing) are settled.
