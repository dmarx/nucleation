# LIT-984: Data Mixing Can Induce Phase Transitions in Knowledge Acquisition

Xinran Gu, Kaifeng Lyu, Jiazheng Li, Jingzhao Zhang (2025), *NeurIPS 2025; arXiv:2505.18091*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmplnu3m](../notes.d/NOTE-tmplnu3m.md).*

- **No transition without mixing.** On equally frequent facts alone, the
  best loss under capacity M is C + p·max{Htot − M, 0}: linear in M
  (Theorem 4.2).
- **With mixing, an onset.** On an orthogonal mixture with ratio r, an
  optimal capacity-bounded learner learns none of the facts while the
  web domain's marginal loss reduction −F′(M) exceeds r·p/(1−r), and all
  of them once M passes that point by Htot (Theorem 4.3).
- **Power-law case.** M_thres ~ (1/(r·p))^(1/(α+1)), r_thres ~
  1/(p·M^(α+1)), and a fact's threshold frequency f_thres ~ M^(−(α+1)).
- **In trained models.** 70M Pythia on SynBio-320k + FineWeb-Edu: 0.4%
  recall at r ≤ 0.2, 58.1% at r = 0.45 (three seeds). Thresholds in size
  from 14M to 410M, and in r for 410M, 2.8B and 6.9B models.
- **Longer training barely helps.** At r = 0.2, 4–16 times more tokens
  leave recall near zero; steps to 40% accuracy outgrow an exponential
  fit in 1/r.
- **One exponent checked.** Threshold frequency against size has slope
  −1.152; the theory predicts −1.283 from the fitted web-loss exponent.
- **What it does not show.** That SGD reaches the optimal allocation, or
  when in training acquisition happens: the theory is static.
