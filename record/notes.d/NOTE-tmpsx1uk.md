# NOTE-tmpsx1uk: Emotions are emergent processes: they require a dynamic computational architecture

## Contribution

A short statement of the component process model (CPM), written to
recommend it as the blueprint for computational models of emotion over
basic-emotion and constructivist theories. It ends by calling for
nonlinear dynamic models. The evidence is deferred to the 2009 review
([LIT-tmpboyvn](../literature.d/LIT-tmpboyvn.md), cited as "in press a").

## Key insight

An emotion is not an output of appraisal. It is a state of the whole
organism, many subsystems pulled into synchrony by appraisal results, and
its existence is a matter of degree of coherence (§1, §2c).

## Assumptions

- Four uncontroversial features of emotion, after Frijda and Scherer
  (2009): elicitation by relevant events, action readiness, involvement
  of several components that "tend to cohere", and control precedence
  (§1).
- That the order of appraisal checks is fixed by "systems economy and
  logical dependencies", each check needing its predecessor's result
  (§3c).
- That subsystems behave as coupled oscillators, so that synchronization
  is the right description of their coherence (§4).

## Key results

A model, not results:

- **Components**: appraisal (cognitive), autonomic (ANS), somatic motor
  (SNS), motivation (action tendencies), and feeling (§3, §3e).
- **SECs**: novelty, intrinsic pleasantness, goal conduciveness and
  their successors, grouped under the four objectives, run in parallel
  but closing in sequence. The checks are recursive, repeated "until the
  monitoring subsystem signals termination of or adjustment to the
  stimulation that originally elicited the appraisal episode" (§3c).
  They are integrated "through context-dependent weighting functions"
  (§4).
- **Patterning**: each check's result drives efferent changes in the
  other components, "differentially and cumulatively" (§3d).
- **Feeling**: integrates a representation of all components. "Quality,
  intensity and duration of the feeling are determined by these
  integrated inputs" (§3e).
- **Dynamics**: attractor basins, hysteresis and a cusp catastrophe. "A
  hysteresis function predicts that the intensity of anger will change
  abruptly for specific degrees of frustration, whereas a linear function
  assumes continuously rising anger with increasing frustration" (§4).

## Concepts

- **stimulus evaluation check (SEC)** — one appraisal criterion, run at
  sensory-motor, schematic or conceptual level.
- **component synchronization** — the coherence of subsystem changes in
  an episode, the criterion of an emotion's existence.
- **monitoring** — the feeling component's integrated representation of
  the other components and their coherence.
- **control precedence** — Frijda's: "claiming (not always
  successfully) priority in the control of behaviour and experience".

## Connections

Takes action readiness and control precedence from Frijda ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)).
Cites Lewis (1996) on circular causality. Lewis (2005,
[LIT-tmpj284p](../literature.d/LIT-tmpj284p.md)) builds an attractor model of the same kind, and the
two exchanged sharply in its BBS commentary, where Lewis replied that
"Scherer's model and mine share an emphasis on synchronization in emotion
episodes" (p. 225).

## Bearing on the record

**Is synchronization a measurable analogue of dominance?** It is a
correlate dominance would produce, not dominance itself. The record's
term ([TERM-tmp8gbrt](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8gbrt.md)) defines dominance by which ranking wins the
negotiation's conflicts, persists and resumes. Scherer's synchronization
is coherence among response subsystems, the same changes appearing in
appraisal, body, face and motive. One ranking dominating many
perspectives would produce that coherence, since all the perspectives it
governs would be pulled the same way. But coherence does not say which
ranking does the pulling, or whether it is a ranking of the system's own
states, or what sign the state has. Synchronization is unsigned, like
dominance strength, so at best it is an analogue of the term's
*intensity* clause. Scherer himself has the integrated inputs fix the
feeling's quality, intensity and duration, not synchrony alone.

Two points tie the CPM closer to the record than this suggests:

- **The definition already contains control precedence**, glossed as
  "claiming (not always successfully) priority". That parenthesis is
  [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s distinction between the precedence a motive claims and the
  authority it is granted.
- **The integration rule is a negotiation.** "Weighted integration in
  the form of dynamically adaptive differential activation and inhibition
  thresholds are likely to be more appropriate modelling principles as
  compared with fixed weights in regression or decision structures"
  (§4). Context-dependent weights are compensatory; activation and
  inhibition thresholds are not. The CPM does not choose between
  [CLAIM-tmp83ge2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp83ge2.md)'s two regimes. It expects both.

**An operational measure?** None here. The paper says "all we can
currently measure is the individual's verbal account of a consciously
experienced feeling" (§3e), and in their commentary on Lewis, Sander and
Scherer concede that synchronization models "have not progressed beyond
a preliminary stage" and that nonlinear theorizing "does not lend itself
readily to designing appraisal experiments and analyzing multimodal
data" (Lewis 2005, p. 216). Whatever indices the 2009 review uses, such
as cross-component correlation or coherence of time series, are its
business, and it is unread. For [QUESTION-tmpsqwu5](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpsqwu5.md) this is a lead, not an
answer.

**Is the monitoring component a version of "felt = the system's model of
its own dominance"?** In part. Scherer makes two separate claims, and
the record should keep them apart.

- **(A) Feeling represents the coherence.** The feeling component
  "needs to integrate and centrally represent all information about the
  continuous patterns of change and their coherence in all other
  components." That is close to the proposal. Feeling is a model, held
  by one subsystem, of the coordination of the others. It differs in two
  ways. What is modelled is coherence among components, not which
  ordering wins. And the model covers everything, the appraisal profile,
  the body and the action tendencies, not the dominance alone.
- **(B) Synchronization generates consciousness.** "It is hypothesized
  that it is the degree of synchronization of the components ... that
  will generate conscious experience." Here consciousness is tied to the
  degree of the regime itself, not to a model of it. That is closer to
  Veit's identity ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)) than to a self-model view. It also faces
  the counterfactual eraser ([THEORY-252](../theory.d/THEORY-252.md)) if degree is read
  dispositionally.

Scherer's Venn model puts the monitoring representation (circle A) only
partly inside consciousness (circle B). So on his account a system can
model its own coherence without that model being felt. If the record
adopts "felt = model of own dominance", Scherer is a precedent for the
model and a warning that the model is not sufficient. That is
[QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md)'s gap.

**On [CLAIM-tmp83ge2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp83ge2.md).** Scherer's hysteresis is a third pattern beside
the continuous curve and the threshold, and the claim does not place it.
If anger jumps at one frustration level going up and drops at a lower
one coming down, choice depends on the path, not only on the current
cost–resource pair. The relation on pairs is then not well defined. A
trade-off experiment should sweep cost up and down, and a fold would be
evidence of dominance changing regime with history. The claim's second
defeater should name it alongside stochastic switching.

## Limitations

Programmatic. The dynamics are illustrated with figures, not fitted; the
evidence is elsewhere; the components and checks are specified, but the
transfer functions that would make synchronization computable are not.

## Open questions

What synchronization index the 2009 review uses, and whether it can be
computed per episode. Whether the feeling component's representation of
coherence is a ranking-sensitive model (which subsystem leads), or only
a degree.
