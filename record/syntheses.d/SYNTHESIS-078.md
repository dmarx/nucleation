# SYNTHESIS-078: Neither persistence nor yields nor minimal sets identify a productive or generative nucleus: the committor ignores how long a state persists, two assemblies can agree in both arms of the nucleus intervention and differ in the downstream rate, and productive sets need not be closed upward because two productive nuclei can combine into a trap

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line: one of the line's eleven
small clusters, three entries from the DNA origami filing of 2026-11-05,
all first in realizability, where [SYNTHESIS-045](SYNTHESIS-045.md) sets them out. This
account integrates them again because their `line` includes
formal-apparatus. The line's account is built over it and its sibling
parts.*

## The account

- **Persistence is not productivity.** [CLAIM-595](../claims.d/CLAIM-595.md) (A's wording, with the
  assistant's proof; thesis, demonstrated, Active, version 2): in the
  committor sense persistence and nucleating productivity vary
  independently, since a state's committor depends on where its exits lead
  and not on how fast it leaves; an intermediate can persist while
  lowering the target's chance against an admissible alternative. For
  success by a deadline the independence fails: "Point 1 above holds for
  the committor, and for deadline success it fails."
- **Yields do not identify the route.** [CLAIM-596](../claims.d/CLAIM-596.md) (the assistant's;
  thesis, demonstrated, Active, version 2): a generative nucleus and one
  that only gives a head start can have the same success with and
  without the nucleus. "Since two systems can agree in both arms, no
  function of these yields separates a generative nucleus from a head
  start." The downstream rate, compared with and without the nucleus,
  differs.
- **Productive sets have no closure.** [CLAIM-599](../claims.d/CLAIM-599.md) (the assistant's, on A's
  §5; thesis, demonstrated, Active, version 2): because establishing more
  relations can lower the chance of the target ([CLAIM-562](../claims.d/CLAIM-562.md)), two
  productive nuclei can combine into a trap, so the minimal productive
  sets describe the productive ones only where V is monotone, and the
  productive sets form no complex.
