# LIT-tmpuedwt: Improving Supply Chain for Essential Drugs in Low-Income Countries: Results from a Large Scale Randomized Experiment in Zambia

Vledder, M., Friedman, J., Sjöblom, M., Brown, T. & Yadav, P. (2019). Improving Supply Chain for Essential Drugs in Low-Income Countries: Results from a Large Scale Randomized Experiment in Zambia. *Health Systems & Reform* 5(2), 158-177. doi:10.1080/23288604.2019.1596050.

## Key takeaways

- Not read. What the record knows of it comes from Leung et al.
  ([LIT-tmpa48v2](LIT-tmpa48v2.md)): the two arms, and "a substantial reduction of the average
  number of days without stock for these products in intervention B
  compared with intervention A and control facilities".

## Standing in the record

Registered for [LEAD-044](../leads.d/LEAD-044.md): two interventions at two tiers of one supply
chain, the comparison that lead asks for if the arms' costs are reported
and similar. Whether they are is in this paper and was not reached; its
reading would decide whether the pilot meets the lead's standard. Leung et
al. stand in for the design and the headline result only.
