# THEORY-tmp3va8j: Stewart shows that on any coupled cell network of finite type the balanced equivalence relations, the patterns of synchrony invariant under every dynamics the architecture admits, form a complete lattice under refinement whose join is that of equivalence relations but whose meet is not intersection, with a unique coarsest element computable from input trees

## Source

Stewart (2007), LIT-2249, read in NOTE-tmp14xdi.

## What was actually shown

A proof through the order-reversing bijection between balanced relations
and flow-invariant polydiagonals, whose intersections are again
polydiagonals; an eight-cell counterexample to closure under
intersection; a characterization of the top element on the universal
cover.

## What this does not say

Which balanced patterns are stable or observed. That the meet has a simple
combinatorial form.
