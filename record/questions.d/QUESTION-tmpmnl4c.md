# QUESTION-tmpmnl4c: Have the DNA-origami self-replication and tile proofreading results that the record holds from single groups been reproduced independently: the melting shift and replication rates of templated cross-tile replication, mutant takeover in a fed dimer replicator, and the speed-error gain of proofreading tile sets?

## Why it is a question

Each of these readings is Active as a report of one group's study or
model, read in full, with its hedges kept. None has been reproduced by
another group in anything the record holds. A reproduction, or a failure
to reproduce, of any one of them answers the question for that reading;
the conditions they carried, word for word, say what would count.

## The conditions it holds

### [THEORY-1448](../theory.d/THEORY-1448.md)

> An independent group reproduces, for DNA origami tiles tethered on a
> template, the melting-temperature shift attributed to raised effective
> concentration and the model's prediction of melting temperatures
> across assembly sizes, or reports per-cycle replication rates for the
> cross-tile system matching these within error.

### [THEORY-1449](../theory.d/THEORY-1449.md)

> An independent replication of mutant takeover in a templated, enzyme-free
> origami replicator fed monomers each cycle, or a fit of the
> species fractions over cycles by a first-order master equation in
> growth and mutation rates published with its parameters, showing that
> a species produced only by mutation of a few percent per copy reaches
> dominance within about ten cycles when it grows faster, and that
> species growing alike approach equal shares.

### [THEORY-1451](../theory.d/THEORY-1451.md)

> An independent kTAM simulation or analysis, or an experiment with DNA
> tiles, reproduces all three of the following: the change in the
> speed/error scaling from ε² to ε¹ for 2×2 proofreading near the
> melting transition; the shortfall from ε^(2/K) at K = 3; and the
> attribution of that shortfall to growth on facets.
