# LIT-1405: How Does Intergroup Contact Reduce Prejudice? Meta-Analytic Tests of Three Mediators

Pettigrew, T. F. and Tropp, L. R. (2008). How does intergroup contact reduce prejudice? Meta-analytic tests of three mediators. *European Journal of Social Psychology*, 38(6), 922–934. doi:10.1002/ejsp.504.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-tmpdp52y](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpdp52y.md)).

## Why this is here

The exchange cited it for the mechanism: anxiety reduction and empathy mediate more than knowledge does. The abstract agrees, with one qualification the exchange's wording could lose: knowledge mediates too, only less strongly; the abstract does not say it fails. For the owner's "perspectival invitation" reading, the point is that contact changes how the other is felt and taken, more than what is known about them. Bears on [CLAIM-275](../claims.d/CLAIM-275.md) and [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md).
