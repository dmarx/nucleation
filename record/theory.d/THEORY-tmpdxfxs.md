# THEORY-tmpdxfxs: The Digital Services Act forbids online platforms to design or operate interfaces that deceive, manipulate or otherwise materially distort users' ability to make free and informed decisions, except where consumer or data-protection law already governs the practice; it requires platforms to disclose the main parameters of their recommender systems and why items are suggested, and requires very large platforms to offer at least one recommender option not based on profiling

## The account

The Digital Services Act forbids online platforms to design or operate interfaces that deceive, manipulate or otherwise materially distort users' ability to make free and informed decisions, except where consumer or data-protection law already governs the practice; it requires platforms to disclose the main parameters of their recommender systems and why items are suggested, and requires very large platforms to offer at least one recommender option not based on profiling.
