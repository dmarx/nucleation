# THEORY-1116: Football fans can identify with a superordinate community of fans across clubs and countries, a European fan community, alongside their club identity rather than in place of it; that identification goes with particularized trust in foreign fans and cross-border viewing, and is independent of attitudes to the sport's governing institutions

## Source

- Biel (2026), [LIT-1133](../literature.d/LIT-1133.md), read in full in [NOTE-1299](../notes.d/NOTE-1299.md) from the CC BY
  published article.

## What the account says

Fandom is nested. A fan of one club can also count themselves part of a
community of fans in their country and of a European community of fans.
The European identification is related to the others but distinct from
them. It goes with trusting fans from elsewhere and watching football
from elsewhere. It is unrelated to how critical one is of the bodies
that run the game.

## What was actually shown

One cross-sectional survey, 2023, four countries, single 0–10 items for
each level. About half of fans scored 6 or more on the European item.
The text's percentages imply a denominator of about 3,980, not the 3,685
analysed. In the two-factor solution the European item loads alone; the
nation item's loading exceeds 1. In OLS with 23 predictors, R² = .37:
trust in a European fan is the largest coefficient, and governance
critique is null.

## What this does not say

- **Not that contact causes the identification.** The common in-group
  identity model predicts it, but no contact was measured.
- **Not that the European community is a group entity.** It is a
  self-categorization reported on one item; whether fans perceive a
  European fan collective with shared intentions ([THEORY-1112](THEORY-1112.md)) is not
  asked.
- **Not about non-fans**, who were excluded.

## Bearing

- **[THEORY-1114](THEORY-1114.md).** SCT predicts a superordinate category becomes
  salient when the comparison frame widens. This study measures standing
  identification with no frame varied, so it is compatible with SCT but
  does not test it.
- **[CASE-104](../cases.d/CASE-104.md).** The governance null is the European-level form of the
  case's separation between fans' community and their judgment of those
  who run it.
