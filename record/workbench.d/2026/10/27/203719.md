**Two hubs that were definitions and an easy half.**

- **THEORY-156 (Blackwell).** It had 20 transitive dependents and held
  three Active claims over a Proposed account. Ten of its twelve direct
  dependents used only Blackwell's definitions, or the easy direction that
  a garbling never helps an unrestricted decision-maker. THEORY-tmp5sao6
  states both on any outcome spaces, with the two-line proof (composition
  of kernels, then Fubini) written out under ADR-047 v2's ruling. Only
  CLAIM-259 and CLAIM-326 use the converse, and they stay on THEORY-156.
- **THEORY-174.** Its monotonicity half was already Active as THEORY-720.
  All five dependents used that half or the definition of a classical
  simulation, and none used the unproved characterization.

**The inversions.** Three readers reviewed the 27 dependents behind the
remaining 38 Active-over-Proposed edges, read-only. Their finding drove a
config change. Nearly every edge was a reading linked to a claim as
evidence: a note headed "evidence now linked", an instance, an outside
echo, an attribution. None was a premise. `corroborates` (ADR-044) ran
only from claim to claim, so readings had no place to go but `grounds`.
ADR-058 lets a THEORY corroborate a CLAIM.

- **Moved to `corroborated_by`:** 15 edges.
- **Re-grounded on what is used:**

  - CLAIM-235 on THEORY-722;
  - CLAIM-249 on CLAIM-318;
  - CLAIM-225 on CLAIM-270;
  - CLAIM-334 on Seth's paper (LIT-1170);
  - CLAIM-030 on CASE-138;
  - CLAIM-060 on CASE-172;
  - ARG-006 on CLAIM-373.
- **Changed from `rests_on` to `complements`:** CLAIM-207, CLAIM-247,
  CLAIM-256 and CLAIM-452. In each, the dependent is a theorem or a
  comparison of texts whose proof or reading does not use the claim.
- **Reversed:** CLAIM-225 no longer rests on CLAIM-087. CLAIM-087's
  garbling bound uses CLAIM-225's result, so CLAIM-087 now rests on
  CLAIM-225. CLAIM-087's body had worried about a cycle, and the
  dependence runs one way.
- **Made conditional:** CLAIM-305's clause on the Bayesian penalty, which
  only Watanabe's singular result, held as cited, supports.
- **Left for the owner:** three edges, below.

**Questions with an Active answer.** ADR-049 says a partly answered
question is split, and the answered part becomes an Answered child.
QUESTION-127 was split by hand as the template. Three agents took the
other 36, with questions grouped so that no answering entry was edited by
two of them. Eight were split:

- QUESTION-017, -045, -060, -105, -112, -118, -220 and -222, with
  QUESTION-tmpgjyn2 the only one kept to its old title, since it already
  asked only the remainder.

Twenty-six were left, each with the reason that its Active answerer settles
no delimited part, for example a sufficient case for a which-question, or
one model for a general one. The count stays at 34 because `answers`
also links entries that bear on a question without settling any part of
it. A question such as QUESTION-025, with five Active answerers none of
which treats correlated attributes, is not ADR-049 debt. It is a relation
used in two senses, which is the next thing to decide.

**ADR-049's size clause** followed ADR-047's limit of three splits per pull
request. ADR-047 is Superseded, so ADR-049 version 2 defers to ADR-057's
pass size. This pull request is past what one sitting reads, and the next
pass should open its own.

**Owner rulings this pass leaves:**

- **What an ARG's status means.** Either the inference is accepted, or its
  premises are held. ARG-008's body reads it the first way. On that
  reading, neither ARG-006 nor ARG-008 was an inversion.
- **CLAIM-069.** It is granted, and its second clause uses exactly what
  THEORY-168 keeps Proposed. Either the title is narrowed to the authors'
  assertion, or the claim goes Proposed.
- **CLAIM-438.** It is Active because the owner conceded it on
  2026-10-23. ADR-032 says a conceded counter goes Active. Its evidence,
  THEORY-893, is Proposed until it is replicated. The two conventions
  disagree here.

**Traps.**

- **Two agents and a stop hook.** The stop hook asked for a commit while
  two agents were still writing. The commit excluded every file the
  running agent could touch, worked out from its questions' answerers, and
  its files went in a separate commit once it reported.
- **A conflict with the bot.** The bot's repair commit conflicted with a
  later note on CLAIM-225, because both had appended to the same tail.
  The repair's linked text and the new note were both kept.
