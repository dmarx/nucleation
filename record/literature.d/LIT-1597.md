# LIT-1597: The hydrogen hypothesis for the first eukaryote

Martin, W. & Müller, M. (1998). The hydrogen hypothesis for the first eukaryote. *Nature* 392(6671): 37–41. doi:10.1038/32096.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A1 said the eukaryogenesis papers "help discriminate between explanations based on metabolic syntrophy, cellular complexity preceding mitochondrial acquisition, and integration following endosymbiosis", and cited none of the explanations. This is the classic syntrophy explanation, a mitochondrion-early scenario that rests on metabolic dependence.
