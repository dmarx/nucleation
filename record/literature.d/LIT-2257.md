# LIT-2257: Using timescale as a state coordinate reveals the metastable geometry of behavior

Rajpreet Kaur, Kanishk Jain and Gordon J. Berman (2026). "Using timescale as a state coordinate reveals the metastable geometry of behavior." arXiv preprint. [ARXIV-2605.24135](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24135).

## Key takeaways

- Slow behavioral states appear as linear arms from a hub in the
  transfer operator's slow eigenvectors, one arm per metastable basin.
- In flies the basins shift over minutes against leg movements at tens
  of hertz; a fixed-resolution analysis misses them.
- Residence times are heavy-tailed; the mechanism is not yet
  discriminated.
