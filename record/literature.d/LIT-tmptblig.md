# LIT-tmptblig: Causality and signalling of garden-path sentences

Daphne Wang and Mehrnoosh Sadrzadeh (2024). *Philosophical Transactions
of the Royal Society A* 382(2268): 20230013. DOI-10.1098/rsta.2023.0013.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-22 by [LEAD-182](../leads.d/LEAD-182.md)'s literature check. It is not the
test the lead asks for: its probabilities come from a language model. It
is here because it finds signalling in syntactic disambiguation and ties
its degree to human reading times, which is evidence, if it holds when
read, that the direct influences a CbD analysis must discount are
psychologically real in sentence processing ([CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md)). Not read.
