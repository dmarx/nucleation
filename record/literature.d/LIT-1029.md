# LIT-1029: Autonomy, Function, and Representation

Bickhard, M. H. (2000). Autonomy, Function, and Representation. *Communication and Cognition – Artificial Intelligence* 17(3–4), 111–131. https://www.lehigh.edu/~mhb0/autfuncrep.html

## Key takeaways

Read in full ([NOTE-729](../notes.d/NOTE-729.md)).

- **A ladder of autonomy.** Energy-well stability needs no exchange;
  far-from-equilibrium stability does. Above a pumped chemical bath come
  self-maintenance (a candle flame), energy-directing infrastructure with
  no alternatives, and recursive self-maintenance (a bacterium that swims
  up a sugar gradient and tumbles going down it).
- **Function is contribution to one's own persistence**, always relative
  to the system served: a parasite's gut is functional for the parasite and
  dysfunctional for the host. The norm comes from the thermodynamic
  asymmetry between far-from-equilibrium and equilibrium processes. A flame
  has functions in this sense.
- **Having a function** is being of a type the whole's processes presuppose
  to meet a condition, whether or not it does. This is what grounds
  malfunction, and it is sketched and deferred to Christensen & Bickhard.
- **Etiological function is epiphenomenal**, since a molecular duplicate
  with no history would lack it, and only current state is efficacious.
- **Representation is the indication that a further interaction will
  succeed.** Differentiations have no content. The indication implicitly
  predicates its conditions of success, can be false, and can be found
  false by the system. Information semantics, Fodor and Dretske are argued
  to make such error impossible.

## Why this is here

A11 named it as a foundational interlocutor for the difference between
genuine autonomy and generic self-organizing dynamics. The filing's
summary was right in outline. It did not say that the line Bickhard draws
for function falls below the flame, so self-maintenance alone, which the
exchange took to be generic self-organization, already has norms on this
account. What separates the autonomy that matters for cognition is
recursive self-maintenance, and with it error the system can detect.

What the reading found:

- **[CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md).** Its premise is supported by a second route.
  Function is relative to the system whose persistence is served, and a
  function that exists only in an observer's ascription is not natural. A
  modeller's reward is no operator's persistence condition. The
  application to simulations is the record's.
- **[CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md).** Vocabulary, not evidence. Recursive
  self-maintenance is governing among regimes; transforming the governing
  organization does not appear, nor do selves or authority.
- **[QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md).** A partial answer: an authority structure can be
  functional for itself and dysfunctional for the organization, judged by
  the organization's own persistence conditions. Persistence is the only
  standard, so captured authority that keeps the organization alive is not
  dysfunctional. That is [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)'s limit, which the paper does not
  dispute.
- **[THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md).** A disagreement about whether a flame's contributions are
  functions, not about the organism line. Mossio, Saborido & Moreno deny
  the flame functions; Bickhard grants it serving-a-function.
- **[THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md) and [LIT-1017](LIT-1017.md).** The same bacterium; self-maintenance against
  recursive self-maintenance is close to Di Paolo's robustness against
  adaptivity, five years earlier. Bickhard has no individuality condition.
