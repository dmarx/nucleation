# THEORY-1858: Cybernetic totalism is dogma, not technology

## The account

Lanier names "cybernetic totalism" and rejects it: people are not only information patterns, subjective experience matters, Darwinian algorithms are not the account of all creativity, and software's unwieldiness ("legacy and lock-in") makes the predicted cataclysm unlikely. He disclaims "glum rejection of digital technology".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1946](../notes.d/NOTE-1946.md), results 1–5.

## What it does not say

Anything about business models, which the LIT for the book had attributed; the book's later themes are not in the essay.
