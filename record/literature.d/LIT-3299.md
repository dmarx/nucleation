# LIT-3299: Delusions by design? How everyday AIs might be fuelling psychosis (and what can be done about it)

Morrin, Nicholls, Levin, Yiend, Iyengar, DelGuidice, Bhattacharyya, MacCabe, Tognin, Twumasi, Alderson-Day and Pollak (2025), a PsyArXiv preprint.

## Key takeaways

- Themes shared across cases: mission or awakening in 9 of 17 vignettes, a sentient AI in 5, attachment in 5; several began with mundane tasks.
- The course after use stops, and onset without prior vulnerability, are open by the authors' own account.
- One persona was shared one-to-many by a public figure: "a digitally transmitted delusional system".

## Standing in the record

Read for [LEAD-308](../leads.d/LEAD-308.md)'s table: whether themes converge across users, exposure dose, course after use stops, personas carried between instances, and whether the model treats vulnerable users differently.
