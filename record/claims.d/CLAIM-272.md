# CLAIM-272: Giving every member an equal budget of actions does not equalize their influence, because coordination lets a member's plan direct other members' actions, so a cap on self-allocation constrains action and not authority

## The claim

A8 §3: "a participant who creates a successful design template might have
almost no direct pixel-writing capacity beyond any ordinary user, yet
exercise enormous influence over the pixels that thousands of other users
place. Their direct semantic influence is small, but their constitutive
influence over the collective's behavior is large. And that influence need
not arise through formal appointment." Then: "Reddit's software imposed a
cooldown and common action budget, but did not give participants equal
*effective* influence. Coordination, recruitment, attention, and automation
could produce very unequal collective power."

The cooldown is an equal per-member budget and a perfect ban on allocating
oneself extra moves ([CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md)). It did not equalize influence, because
following a plan turns other members' moves into the planner's effect. That
is endorsement in [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s sense. So [CLAIM-285](CLAIM-285.md)'s constraint has
to be stated over authority, not over actions. A budget on actions leaves
the question untouched. The planner's influence is [CLAIM-086](CLAIM-086.md)'s kind,
governing the conditions under which moves count.

Twitch Plays Pokémon in democracy mode ([CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md)) is the contrast. There the
aggregation rule is fixed and public, and influence is roughly one input,
one vote, apart from herding on the visible tally.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the inequality is illegitimate. It came from others'
  choosing to follow.
- It does not say coordination is the only source of unequal influence.
  Use of the equal cooldown was itself heavy-tailed. In 2017 the average
  account placed 14 pixels and the most active 545, and 17% of 2023 actions
  came from accounts exceeding the cap (Wu and Silva, [THEORY-216](../theory.d/THEORY-216.md)).
  Influence was unequal before any plan.
- The degree of inequality due to plans has not been measured. The
  placement log does not record who followed whom ([CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md)).
  Litherland and Mørch ([THEORY-218](../theory.d/THEORY-218.md)) show from the forums that a few
  central members admitted or refused additions to a design that placers
  then carried out. That is the mechanism, observed, though not its size.
