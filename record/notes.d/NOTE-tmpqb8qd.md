# NOTE-tmpqb8qd: Gepshtein et al. — Spatially distributed computation in cortical circuits

## Contribution

A single mechanism, the interference of intrinsic neural waves, accounts
for classical selectivity and for lateral (non-classical) effects. It
predicts a new interaction, which reanalysed MT data confirm.

## Key insight

In a chain of coupled E/I motifs, a point input sets up a standing,
damped spatial oscillation whose period the weights fix. Tuning is what
the interference of those oscillations selects.

## Assumptions

A 1-D chain with nearest-neighbour E/I coupling. Linearity for most of the
analysis. MT preferences read as outputs of a generic circuit; population
aggregates for the TF result.

## Key results

- **Standing wave** ("Neural wave interference"): the stationary state "is
  a distributed spatial pattern of activation ... it is a standing
  spatially damped wave". The period "is determined by the weights of
  excitatory and inhibitory connections between cells; it is an intrinsic
  property of the circuit". Inputs a half period apart "cancel one
  another".
- **Two zones**: "The SF of the response in zone 2 is independent of the
  stimulus SF." In humans the lateral modulation period "does not
  systematically depend on the stimulus SF" (two subjects).
- **MT** ("Spatiotemporal selectivity in biological cortical circuits"):
  "In both monkeys, the preferred TF follows a nonmonotonic function of
  stimulus SF", "a hitherto unknown phenomenon predicted by the model"
  (Discussion).
- **Beyond linearity** ("Stimulus contrast and cortical selectivity"): at
  higher contrast the interaction "additionally depends on the amplitudes
  of the neural wave".
- **Untested**: "the two-dimensional array model whose predictions we have
  not tested" (Discussion). Travelling waves are a stated future direction.

## Concepts

Wilson–Cowan motif; neural wave (standing, damped); interference; intrinsic
SF/TF; spatial resonance; classical and non-classical receptive field.

## Bearing on the record

No held entry. Within the batch: these are not the propagating waves of
[LIT-tmp0aok6](../literature.d/LIT-tmp0aok6.md). Di Volo & Férézou ([LIT-tmpzhtw7](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzhtw7.md)) measure sub-linear
summation of colliding waves, so linear interference is this model's
low-contrast regime, not a general law of cortical wave interaction. The
closing suggestion for AI ("hybridizing principally different learning
paradigms") is speculation.

## Limitations

Methods and supplement unread; the resonance conditions taken from the
Results prose. Psychophysics n = 2. One earlier MT data set.
