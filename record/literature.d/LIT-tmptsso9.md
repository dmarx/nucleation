  Tudorache, Nyulas, Noy and Musen (2013), WebProtégé: A collaborative
  ontology editor and knowledge acquisition tool for the Web. WebProtégé is
  a browser-based, lightweight ontology editor built on the Protégé
  collaborative back end, meant to let domain experts, not only knowledge
  engineers, edit ontologies. Its interface is a declarative, per-project
  and per-user configurable portal of portlets and knowledge-acquisition
  forms bound to ontology properties. Collaboration support includes change
  tracking (every authoring operation recorded in the Changes and Annotation
  Ontology, ChAO, with author, time and description), threaded typed notes
  attached to entities, watches with notifications, change-analysis
  statistics and a configurable access policy down to property level. Its
  main deployment is iCAT, WHO's platform for the ICD-11 revision: 200
  registered users, over 105,000 changes and 19,000 notes recorded. Read for
