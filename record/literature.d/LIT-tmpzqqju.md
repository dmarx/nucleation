summary: >-
  A preregistered multisite replication of Study 1 of Greenberg et al. (1994), the standard mortality-salience test: writing about one's own death, against writing about watching television, was to raise preference for a pro-US over an anti-US essay author. Seventeen US university labs contributed 1,550 usable participants. Seven ran a protocol designed with the original authors; ten built their own. The pooled effect was Hedges' g = 0.07 (95% CI −0.03 to 0.17), against the original d = 1.34, and no exclusion rule, protocol type or subset of TMT-knowledgeable labs produced a significant effect. Effects did not vary across labs more than chance allows, so the test of whether expert involvement helps replication was left without an effect to act on.
