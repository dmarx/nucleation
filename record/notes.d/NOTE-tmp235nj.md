# NOTE-tmp235nj: Morganti — From OSR to metaphysical coherentism

## Contribution

Morganti argues that the usual structuralist claim that symmetries and invariances are fundamental, especially in quantum physics, should not be read as a claim that physical relations are fundamental. It should be read as metaphysical coherentism: object-like parts of reality stand in symmetric relations of ontological dependence. He develops the view and argues that symmetric dependence need not make metaphysical explanations uninformative. He also contrasts coherentism with existing structuralisms and holisms.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- §1–2 (pp. 1–5): assumes OSR is justified and asks instead what its metaphysics amounts to. Structuralist evidence comes from symmetry groups, permutation invariance, and entanglement, including AQFT.
- §3 (pp. 5–9): strong (eliminativist) OSR struggles to reduce all properties to relations. Moderate OSR (Esfeld & Lam 2006; McKenzie 2014) posits objects without intrinsic features and faces the Lowe objection that dependence tied to explanation cannot be symmetric.
- §4 (pp. 9–15): develops coherentism against foundationalism, including a note that well-foundedness is not an axiom of mereology (fn. 19, p. 11). It answers the circular-explanation worry about symmetric dependence and draws on Fine's notion of reciprocal dependence (fn. 21).
- §4.2 (pp. 15–18): distinguishes coherentism from Teller's relational holism and other holisms. Entangled systems come out as individuals that become mutually dependent with respect to their properties (with Calosi & Morganti).
- §5 (pp. 19–20): science does not force an ontology of irreducible physical relations. Structuralist talk translates into sets of mutually dependent objects whose joint existence fixes structures that constrain each element. fn. 32 concedes that for spacetime the gap between coherentism and moderate OSR is small.
