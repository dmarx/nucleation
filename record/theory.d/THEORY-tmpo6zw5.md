# THEORY-tmpo6zw5: Choice is possible without a maximum

Schwartz, "Rationality and the Myth of the Maximum" (1972) and *The Logic of Collective Choice* (1986).

## The account, as expected

Schwartz proposes that rational choice does not require a best element: where preference cycles or is incomplete, choice can be made from the union of the minimal sets that no outside alternative beats, the Schwartz set, which coincides with the top cycle for a complete relation and is defined for incomplete ones.
