# LIT-2407: Incentivizing High-Quality Content in Online Recommender Systems

Xinyan Hu, Meena Jagadeesan, Michael I. Jordan and Jacob Steinhardt (2023). "Incentivizing High-Quality Content in Online Recommender Systems." *FORC 2026, LIPIcs 368, 15:1–15:18*. DOI-10.4230/lipics.forc.2026.15.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1774](../notes.d/NOTE-1774.md)). A formal result that a platform's
learning dynamics, not users' tastes, can by themselves drive equilibrium
content quality toward zero, and a design principle that an incentive-aware
platform must react "more quickly and directly" to producer actions;
technical tools bounding how much an online learner's arm probabilities move
when rewards change.
