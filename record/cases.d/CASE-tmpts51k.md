# CASE-tmpts51k: The collapse of the northern cod fishery

## The case

A8 §2 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c):

> Improvements in fishing technology expanded the capacity to harvest
> cod. Economic activity and entire communities became organized around
> maintaining the fishery.

A moratorium came in 1992. The Royal Commission (LIT-tmplbt41) "identified
excessive harvesting capacity, political pressure to maintain quotas,
dependence on the industry for employment, and reluctance to acknowledge
the declining resource among the contributing factors". A's reading:
"The population's dependence on the prevailing regime made transitioning
away from it costly, even as maintaining it became increasingly
dangerous", and "No unitary agent needed to want the destruction of the
fishery."

## What it can show

- **CLAIM-464** (Active): an arrangement can change what it would take to
  correct it. Here the change is in the distribution of livelihoods: each
  season of fishing at capacity made a cut in quota cost more people more,
  while the standard (a sustainable stock) stayed the same.
- **CLAIM-tmpv3frk** (A's): A8 lists "economic lock-in around the cod
  fishery" under its third stage, in which an optimization "compromises
  the mechanisms that could identify, constrain or reverse its
  consequences". In this case the mechanism is political: the pressure to
  maintain quotas and the reluctance to acknowledge decline are the
  corrective channel bending to the dependence.

It sits beside held readiness entries without illustrating them as
stated: an efficient holding policy that destroys the route out
(CLAIM-577) and the reorganization viability kernel (TERM-167) are about
budgets for a transition, and the case is about a transition whose cost
rose with each period of not making it. It is also a commons: the case
does not need A's "optimization" to be told, since harvesting by many
firms under quotas set by a regulator is enough, and what A's frame adds is
only the regulator's dependence on what it regulates.

## What it cannot show

Which of the causes mattered most: A itself lists "overfishing, inadequate
stock assessments, environmental conditions and other factors", and the
Commission's attribution is read here only through A. Nor does it show a
selection mechanism (CLAIM-tmp3epdy): nothing in A's account says that
fleets which expanded displaced ones that did not.
