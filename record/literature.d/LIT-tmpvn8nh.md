# LIT-tmpvn8nh: Reflexive Analysis of Groups

Lefebvre, V. A. (2009). Reflexive analysis of groups. In S. Argamon & N. Howard (Eds.), *Computational Methods for Counterterrorism*, pp. 173–210. Berlin, Heidelberg: Springer. doi:10.1007/978-3-642-01141-2_10.

Not read: no lawful copy was reached (see the status note). The summary is
drawn from the chapter abstract on the publisher's page. A reading should
check it first.

## What the abstract and the volume say

The abstract (Springer chapter page) gives four ideas: relationships "between
the subgroups of agents, not just pairs of agents", represented as "a
decomposable graph corresponding to an agent or a group of agents as a tree of
subgraphs"; a correspondence "between decomposable graphs and polynomials";
"the interpretation of the tree of polynomials as an agent who has images of
the self, which can have images of the self, etc."; and "putting an equation
into correspondence to the agent, allowing us to find out the agent's state".
The last is the point to check against [MOM-CLM-132](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-132.md)'s "depth is an arms race,
not a defence": if the agent's state is the solution of an equation, the
nesting has a determinate resting point rather than an unending escalation.
Whether it does, and what it is a fixed point of, needs the chapter.

The volume preface (pp. XII–XIII) says the chapter "develops an algebra of strategic
choice within and among groups of interacting agents, based on the author's
previous work on mathematically models of individual choice", and that it
"extends game-theoretic constructs with psychological insights within a formal
graph-theoretical framework". The next chapter, Grice and McDaniel, "Evaluating
Self-Reflexion Analysis Using Repertory Grids" (from p. 211), reports an
empirical test of Lefebvre's models.

## Why this is here

Reflexive control is the influence the record's anchor question did not anticipate: conveying to a target prepared information so that it reaches, by its own reasoning, the decision the controller wants. In the record's terms it acts on the judgement–observation loop of [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) by shaping the observer, and it makes [QUESTION-121](../questions.d/QUESTION-121.md)'s 'unselected' evidence selected by someone else ([CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md)). Registered for the group form, which bears on [QUESTION-070](../questions.d/QUESTION-070.md) (manipulation by organized wholes) and on the operator model ([CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md)).
