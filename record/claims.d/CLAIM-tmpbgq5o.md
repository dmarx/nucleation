# CLAIM-tmpbgq5o: The record's grammar is fixed at any moment but amendable by decision, and in practice a move that fit no scheme or relation has been treated as a defect of the grammar and answered by amending it, so a move the grammar cannot hold is not only forced or lost; whether every such move is caught is not shown

## The claim

[CLAIM-181](CLAIM-181.md) says the record "fixes the grammar an argument is expressed in",
and that "a move that fits no CLAIM, TERM, ARG or declared relation is
either forced into one or lost". It asked to be answered by "a move that
mattered in the exchange and that the record could not hold", or by
showing that every case of drift the record caught was drift by the
exchange's own standards.

The owner, at U18, quoting A17's "epistemic accountability requires not
only traceability within the current conceptual scheme but also some means
of contesting the scheme used to make inquiry traceable": "this definitely
aligns with how we've been using luria: when we've encountered
representational challenges, we've generally interpreted those as a
symptom of a represntational weakness of the record and used that
opportunity to mature the luria configuration, featureset, or both."

The record's decisions bear it out. Each was a move the grammar could not
hold, recognized as such and answered by amending the grammar:

- **[ADR-044](../decisions.d/ADR-044.md)** (2026-10-15): a claim that gave evidence for another without
  the other depending on it could only be written as `supports`, and the
  link fixer then wrote a false `rests_on`. The grammar asserted an
  inference nobody made, and `corroborates` was added ([CASE-tmp8jhbm](../cases.d/CASE-tmp8jhbm.md)).
- **[ADR-043](../decisions.d/ADR-043.md)** (2026-10-15): claims of different kinds of support were
  indistinguishable, and `warrant` was added.
- **[ADR-042](../decisions.d/ADR-042.md)** (2026-10-14): a thought experiment, a construction and a real
  specimen were told apart only by prose, and CASE gained `kind`.
- **[ADR-046](../decisions.d/ADR-046.md)** (2026-10-18): a term that rivalled or narrowed another could
  not be filed as Proposed, and could be.
- **[ADR-049](../decisions.d/ADR-049.md)** (2026-10-21): a question partly answered had no status, and
  partly answered questions split.

So the grammar is fixed at any moment and amendable over time, which is
[CLAIM-195](CLAIM-195.md)'s point that the constraint set is "fixed by an amendable
decision". A move that fits nothing is not only forced or lost: a third
outcome is that the failure to fit becomes evidence against the grammar.
A18 named the condition: "A record becomes second-order corrigible when
failures of its representational distinctions can themselves become
objects of inquiry, argument, and justified revision", and its test of
openness, whether failures of the categories can become "recognizable,
contestable, and actionable *without requiring the failed categories to
validate the challenge in advance*". A18 found the bootstrap in the
record's practice: "ordinary prose, source files and observed
discrepancies can provide that provisional external language."

## What it does not say

- **Not that the grammar catches everything.** The evidence is the
  amendments made. A move the grammar forced into a wrong shape without
  anyone noticing leaves no decision behind, so the record of decisions
  cannot show its absence. [CLAIM-190](CLAIM-190.md)'s point stands: measures computed in
  the grammar cannot certify it alone.
- **Not that every misfit was amended.** [ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) kept the literature
  roles (foil, method, analogue, boundary marker) in prose though they
  "recurred", which is [CLAIM-181](CLAIM-181.md)'s own evidence. That was a decision not to
  amend, and it is recorded, but the move is still held only in prose.
- **Not that amendment is free of the risk A18 named.** Its anti-ad-hoc
  constraint: an amendment should answer a recurring misfit and not be
  made to save a particular claim.
- **Warrant.** Empirical: it rests on the record's own decision history,
  observed, not on an argument about grammars in general.
