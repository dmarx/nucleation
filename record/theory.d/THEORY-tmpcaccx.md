# THEORY-tmpcaccx: Agency can be redescribed without agents, as the affective force by which one individuation process takes up another's excess action in a communicative event and both pass from a lesser to an augmented ability to act; agents are then effects of such events, and the absence of the force is disindividuation

## Source

Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy (2024), [LIT-tmp3mudg](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3mudg.md), pp. 1350–1357, and the abstract;
read in [NOTE-tmpfw3f7](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpfw3f7.md).

## What was actually shown

Nothing empirical. The paper defines agency by adapting Massumi, for whom
affect is "a power to affect and be affected [that] governs a transition,
where a body passes from one state of capacitation to a diminished or
augmented state of capacitation" (p. 1353). Agency keeps the augmenting
direction only: "the affective force that governs the transition from a
lesser to an augmented ability to act" (p. 1354). Its mechanism is
Simondon's, and it occurs when an individuation process incorporates
"the excess action of another individuation process" (p. 1351).

The paper then argues that agents are not presupposed. "Agency happens
before any becoming process can be recognized as an individual agent"
(p. 1357). Identifying an agent is "an 'agential cut'".

One meeting is reread to show the vocabulary in use (pp. 1359–1363): an
MSF representative asked to train local physicians first resists and
then reorganizes how he describes MSF so that it can accept.

## What this does not say

- **That intentional or human agency is unreal.** The paper denies it is
  primary, not that it occurs.
- **How to tell, in advance, that an episode will augment rather than
  diminish ability to act.** The reanalysis identifies the force after
  the fact, from hesitations and the eventual agreement.
- **Which ability matters.** Building augmentation into "agency" makes a
  verdict depend on a judgment of whose capacity counts. The paper does
  not supply one.
- **That the account is distinct in its predictions** from interaction
  analysis of the same data, which is what would promote it.
