## Note of 2026-11-22: the tension stated, an objection filed, two resolutions offered

Not already held: no claim objected to or revised either claim, and no
dated note named the tension. The tension: [CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) says without
qualification that a probe family supplying the training signal cannot
certify what is preserved, and that preservation must be shown under
probes not used to train; [CLAIM-517](../claims.d/CLAIM-517.md)'s coverage step certifies viability
from the very distillation loss the successor was trained to. They agree
where the boundary contexts are not covered and disagree only where they
are.

Filed: [CLAIM-1127](../claims.d/CLAIM-1127.md) (Proposed, thesis, demonstrated), which `objects_to`
[CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) and `rests_on` [CLAIM-517](../claims.d/CLAIM-517.md): training probes do certify a property
that a stated inequality bounds by their value over the contexts they
cover, the value estimated out of sample. Dated notes on [CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) and
[CLAIM-517](../claims.d/CLAIM-517.md) give the two resolutions as alternatives: A, [CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) narrows
(the lean), with a proposed Reframing of its title; B, neither narrows and
the objection is answered. [CLAIM-517](../claims.d/CLAIM-517.md)'s note proposes, under either, a
clarification that its ε is the population loss estimated on fresh
draws. Nothing is applied.

What remains is the owner's: choose A or B, apply or decline the two
Reframings, and the objection's status. This lead's title speaks of
"curation as revision and on admission", which describes neither claim;
the content is training probes and certification. The status and stage
are unchanged.
