**Its thesis.** The distributed-agency line could be developed as "a
theory of endogenous functional differentiation: how initially
interchangeable processes become specialized, acquire differential
influence, and maintain a viable organization without losing the capacity
to reorganize". Feature learning supplies models of how differentiation
emerges. Coexistence theory supplies conditions under which differentiated
components persist. The record's own question is a third: when a
population of differentiated components is a self-governing organization,
not a collection selected and regulated from outside.

**A review before merge.** The owner relayed a review of the PR. It kept
the structure and asked for two corrections and three refinements.

- **Abundance is not authority share** (CLAIM-tmpc36bo, QUESTION-tmp0885h).
  The theorem holds for unnormalized abundances. Normalizing,
  w_i = n_i / Σ n_j, gives replicator dynamics,
  ẇ_i = w_i(f_i − Σ w_j f_j). Evaluating fitness on the simplex gives a
  common Lagrange multiplier, ∂_i L = λ for active components. The claim
  now says n_i is abundance or unnormalized participation intensity, and
  treats normalized endorsement as a separate model.
- **Recovery does not require reintroduction** (QUESTION-tmp0885h,
  CASE-tmprbebk). An invasion exponent is evaluated from a small positive
  start, with no continuing intervention. The study had placed every
  mechanism that supplies rare opportunities outside the organization. The
  review's point: "protecting rare specialists should not appear merely
  as an externally imposed exception to selection. It may be an
  endogenous achievement of the organization itself." External
  reintroduction and internally maintained exploration are now separate
  conditions.
- **The sign condition** (CLAIM-tmpc36bo). The algebra holds for any real
  U. Reading A as competitive overlap needs nonnegative off-diagonal
  entries. Negative inner products make an interaction facilitative.
- **A stronger decisive comparison** (CASE-tmprbebk). Match
  context-conditioned participation, task performance and resource
  budget, not just overall usage. Keep a fixed authority-update rule
  apart from one the organization revises (CLAIM-284).
- **Three ways to keep a specialist** (QUESTION-tmp0885h, CASE-tmprbebk):
  active participation, dormant but recoverable capability, and
  reconstruction by later learning.

The review advised against bringing in catastrophe theory here. Only a
cross-reference was added, from QUESTION-tmp0885h to the open
nonlinear-transition questions (QUESTION-125, CLAIM-330, THEORY-136). It
comes with the caution that positive feedback and exclusion do not by
themselves imply bistability or a cusp.
