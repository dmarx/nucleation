The THEORY backfill's complex-systems group: seventeen LITs with a `Read`
or `Skimmed` NOTE that no THEORY cited as `source`. Each NOTE was read in
full against its LIT, and the theory directory was searched for an
existing account first. Fourteen of the seventeen readings became sources
of a THEORY, and three did not. That fits the owner's hypothesis for this
group, with the caveat that two of the fourteen ([LIT-046](../../record/literature.d/LIT-046.md), [LIT-141](../../record/literature.d/LIT-141.md)) enter
as Proposed accounts their NOTEs had declined to file, under the ruling
that a NOTE's "no THEORY" for a survey or framework yields to a claim its
own reading supports.

## What entered

Thirteen THEORYs, seven `Active` and six `Proposed`. Codes are the
temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

- **Emergence and individuality.**
  - [THEORY-tmp343c3](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp343c3.md) (Active; [LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md)). Observational mutual-information
    individuality measures track association, not propagation; [NOTE-027](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-027.md)'s
    XOR system has Krakauer's conditional A at 1 bit where Bourrat's
    interventional Â is 0. It `answers` [QUESTION-115](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-115.md) in the constraining
    sense: temporal retention is not causal persistence.
  - [THEORY-tmppkom6](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmppkom6.md) (Proposed; [LIT-049](../../record/literature.d/LIT-049.md)). Bourrat's coarse-graining account:
    individuals in a transition are a summary description, quasi-ontological
    only by indispensability under finite resources. Filed with its
    degenerate criterion in "does not say", and `rivals` [THEORY-141](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-141.md)
    (Okasha: the level of selection changes in fact).
  - [THEORY-tmpcxkrx](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpcxkrx.md) (Proposed; [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md)). The ΦID account: a supervenient
    feature can carry irreducible predictive information, and a system
    hosts one iff its dynamics carry synergy about their own future. The
    record holds the theorem only second-hand.
  - [THEORY-tmpm3bsn](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpm3bsn.md) (Active; [LIT-027](../../record/literature.d/LIT-027.md)). Synergy bias can differ between two
    exactly consistent descriptions with equal mutual information; small,
    one redundancy function, and reversed for deterministic systems.
  - [THEORY-tmpwo63m](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpwo63m.md) (Proposed; [LIT-141](../../record/literature.d/LIT-141.md)). With novelty as the definition,
    the other marks of emergence are separate questions: Kondo, liquid–gas,
    BKT and Butterfield's reductions as counterexamples.
- **Collectives.**
  - [THEORY-tmpzaqyd](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpzaqyd.md) (Active; [LIT-035](../../record/literature.d/LIT-035.md)). Pooling under multiplicative noise
    raises each member's time-average growth from μ − σ²/2 to
    μ − σ²/(2N); a sufficient mechanism, not a solution of the cooperation
    puzzle.
  - [THEORY-tmppr4ux](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmppr4ux.md) (Active; [LIT-075](../../record/literature.d/LIT-075.md)). Ant groups improve with size on a
    piano-movers puzzle; human groups barred from speech do 32% worse than

  - [THEORY-tmp05zxn](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp05zxn.md) (Proposed; [LIT-046](../../record/literature.d/LIT-046.md), [LIT-075](../../record/literature.d/LIT-075.md)). Collective intelligence
    as resources against coordination costs. [NOTE-055](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-055.md) judged the paper a

- **Dynamics and pattern.**
  - [THEORY-tmpsuuh4](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpsuuh4.md) (Active; [LIT-036](../../record/literature.d/LIT-036.md)). Cyclic non-reciprocal coupling gives

  - [THEORY-tmpimkz9](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpimkz9.md) (Proposed; [LIT-057](../../record/literature.d/LIT-057.md)). Delay destabilises a random
    Lotka–Volterra community by a Hopf bifurcation; the diversity–stability

  - [THEORY-tmpyw70u](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpyw70u.md) (Active; [LIT-516](../../record/literature.d/LIT-516.md), [LIT-527](../../record/literature.d/LIT-527.md)). Minimum entropy production

  - [THEORY-tmphslyk](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmphslyk.md) (Active; [LIT-535](../../record/literature.d/LIT-535.md), [LIT-527](../../record/literature.d/LIT-527.md)). Turing's diffusive

## What did not enter, and why

- **[LIT-021](../../record/literature.d/LIT-021.md) (Artime et al., [NOTE-007](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-007.md)).** An editorial with no result. Its
  one formal item sorts emergence by whether a pattern is "expected", which
  is never defined, and its status note says not to cite it for a
  definition.
- **[LIT-150](../../record/literature.d/LIT-150.md) (Rizi, [NOTE-153](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-153.md)).** A position statement. Its coarse-graining
  criterion has no distortion or predictive measure, so it cannot decide a
  case, and its LLM sentence is unsupported. [NOTE-153](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-153.md) says no THEORY
  follows; this audit agrees, and names it in [THEORY-tmpwo63m](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpwo63m.md).
- **[LIT-933](../../record/literature.d/LIT-933.md) (Nowak et al., [NOTE-768](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-768.md)).** A programmatic heuristic with no
  model or prediction that could fail. Its one sharp contribution, a broad
  definition of synchronisation, is a definition. [NOTE-768](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-768.md) files no THEORY,
  and its bearing is already registered on [QUESTION-053](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-053.md) and [QUESTION-063](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-063.md).

## Choices made

- **Status.** A derivation within a stated model, a theorem the field
  holds, or a finding described with its scope went `Active`. An account
  the reading calls argued, a framework, a survey's conclusion, or a
  preprint result at one parameter setting went `Proposed`.
- **One rivalry.** Bourrat's descriptive account against Okasha's factual
  change of the level of selection ([THEORY-141](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-141.md)). The ΦID and
  effective-information accounts were not made rivals: their disagreement
  on [NOTE-010](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-010.md)'s two-bit permutation depends on the redundancy function.
  The dissipative-structure account was not made a rival of maximum
  entropy production ([THEORY-tmpxjvy1](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpxjvy1.md)), since it says no selection
  principle is established, not that none holds.
- **Edits to existing documents.** [THEORY-222](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-222.md) (v2): its Connections said
  the record held no ΦID THEORY; it now names [THEORY-tmpcxkrx](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpcxkrx.md). [CLAIM-157](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-157.md)
  (v4) and [CLAIM-331](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-331.md) (v2): `grounds` gains [THEORY-tmpx1n2e](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpx1n2e.md), the
  near-decomposability result both cite Simon for. `luria link --fix`
  wrote the converses on [THEORY-141](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-141.md) and [QUESTION-115](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-115.md).
