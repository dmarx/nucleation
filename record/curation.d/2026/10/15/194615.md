The owner asked for the compound claims listed in [ADR-043](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-043.md) to be split, in a
contribution stacked on the one that made `warrant` required.

Four readers drafted the splits from a shared brief, following the
precedent of [CLAIM-298](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-298.md) and [CLAIM-315](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-315.md): the original keeps its code and
main conclusion, the differently warranted part becomes its own claim
unless one already exists, and the original rests on it when its argument
needs it. The drafts were applied by script, and titles and defeat
conditions changed by hand. [ADR-043](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-043.md) records the outcome claim by claim.

Calls made in applying them:

- **[CLAIM-240](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-240.md) was not split.** Its reader pointed out that the part to be
  split off has no observation under it, so both halves are interpretive.
- **New demonstrated claims are Active where the original was Proposed**
  ([CLAIM-318](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-318.md), [CLAIM-320](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-320.md), [CLAIM-319](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-319.md), [CLAIM-323](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-323.md)). The
  record doubted the readings, not the constructions, and other Active
  claims already relied on them.
- **[CLAIM-316](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-316.md), the time-indexed drive profile split from [CLAIM-166](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-166.md),
  stays Proposed.** [CLAIM-166](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-166.md) says the record accepts "the conceptual
  point", but does not say which one, so the split part was not promoted.
- **[CLAIM-321](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-321.md), empirical, drops the `formal-apparatus` line** it had
  copied from [CLAIM-123](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-123.md); that line is for general formal results.
- **[CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md) now also rests on [CLAIM-322](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-322.md)**, the evidential rule split
  from [CLAIM-264](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-264.md), because [CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md) uses that rule.
- **[CLAIM-319](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-319.md) complements [CLAIM-320](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-320.md)**: the second is the
  three-context case of the first's construction.
