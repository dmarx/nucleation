# ADR-042: A kind field on CASE, from a closed vocabulary of six, so that a proposed study, a thought experiment, a formal construction and a real specimen are told apart by something other than how much of each is fact

## Context

The owner, on 2026-10-14, after a proposed survival analysis was filed as a
case ([CASE-078](../cases.d/CASE-078.md)): "are we able to construct something like a typology
of CASEs? I'm wondering if maybe there's a new vocab term, axis, or scheme
we should leverage to distinguish e.g. proposed empirical experiments vs
thought experiments vs. steel-man vs. etc".

A CASE has one vocabulary field besides status, tags and line: `standing`,
which says "how much of a CASE is fact" ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). Its three terms are
documented, stipulated and fictional. The survey of all 81 cases found
`standing` carrying a second question it was not written for, what kind of
thing the case is:

- `stipulated` is defined as "a real referent whose relevant facts are
  assumed rather than verified here — usable as a thought experiment". It
  holds ten proposed studies ([CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md), [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md), [CASE-078](../cases.d/CASE-078.md) …), which
  have no referent yet. It holds six formal constructions (the reset
  matrices, [CASE-001](../cases.d/CASE-001.md), and the parity triangle, [CASE-037](../cases.d/CASE-037.md)), which have no
  referent at all. It holds real companies with assumed facts (CoreWeave,
  [CASE-007](../cases.d/CASE-007.md)). And it holds two runs made in session ([CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md), [CASE-020](../cases.d/CASE-020.md)).
- Two runs made in session, [CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md) and [CASE-032](../cases.d/CASE-032.md), are `fictional`, because
  the assistant wrote the texts. The other two are `stipulated`. The same
  kind of thing has two standings.
- Nothing lets a reader list the proposed studies, the record's open
  empirical agenda, without reading every case.

| kind \ standing | documented | stipulated | fictional |
|---|---|---|---|
| specimen | 25 | 6 | 0 |
| thought-experiment | 1 | 11 | 13 |
| construction | 0 | 6 | 0 |
| reported-study | 5 | 0 | 0 |
| proposed-study | 0 | 10 | 0 |
| executed-study | 0 | 2 | 2 |

`stipulated` spans five kinds, and thought experiments span all three
standings. Kind cannot be derived from standing. It is a second axis.

## Decision

Approved on 2026-10-14, with the sixth term renamed from the draft's
"run-here" to `executed-study`. The owner offered "executed-study" or
"performed-study". "Executed" was taken because "performed" could be read as
a performance. Add a closed vocabulary `case-kinds` and a required field `kind`
on CASE.

- **specimen**: a real instance, artefact, event or kind of thing, examined
  as it is. Henley's Villon, the Dual State, a coral reef, r/place. It may
  be documented, or stipulated when its facts are assumed.
- **thought-experiment**: an imagined scenario, usually a contrast built to
  hold everything fixed but one thing, used to test an intuition or a
  concept. Northstar-A and -B, the reluctant writer, the tree in the woods,
  the twin pattern-keepers.
- **construction**: a formal or mathematical object built to exhibit or
  refute a property, whose facts are its definition. The reset matrices,
  the parity triangle, the two identical ballots, the reciprocal pair.
- **reported-study**: an empirical study others ran and published, used as
  a case. The contextuality tests ([CASE-053](../cases.d/CASE-053.md)), the risk-message diffusion
  chains ([CASE-068](../cases.d/CASE-068.md)), Voita et al. ([CASE-070](../cases.d/CASE-070.md)).
- **proposed-study**: an empirical design not yet run. Its body says what
  would be measured, the main threat to inference, and what each outcome
  would show. The factorial study ([CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md)), the dancing-couple
  motion-capture study ([CASE-045](../cases.d/CASE-045.md)), the r/place survival analysis
  ([CASE-078](../cases.d/CASE-078.md)).
- **executed-study**: something the record's collaborators ran in session, with
  results the record itself produced. The synthetic stochastic computations
  ([CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md)), the dry-run harness ([CASE-020](../cases.d/CASE-020.md)), the assistant-written chains
  ([CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md), [CASE-032](../cases.d/CASE-032.md)). Kept apart from reported-study because its
  provenance and its limits are the record's own.

`kind` is required, so that every new case says what it is ([DP-3](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-3)). A case
whose kind changes, such as a proposed study that is then run, gets a new
version with the change in its history, or a successor case if the run
differs from the design.

Kind and standing stay orthogonal. One clarification of standing follows:
for a proposed study, `stipulated` describes its materials and setting, not
results it does not yet have. The blurb of `stipulated` is widened to say
so, and nothing else in `case-standing` changes.

### Steel-man is not a kind

A steel-man is the strongest form of a position, put in order to be met.
That is a voice, not a kind of case, and the record already has it: a CLAIM
with `role: counter` ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). [CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md) and [CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) are
recent examples. When a case is built to make an opposing view strongest,
its kind is whatever it is (usually thought-experiment or construction), and
the steel-manning shows in its relation, `counterexample_to` or `supports`,
to the counter claim. A seventh term would duplicate that and blur two axes
again. If steel-manned cases need finding, the place is a relation from case
to counter claim, decided separately.

Analogy is set aside for the same reason. The coral reef and the gut
microbes are specimens used by analogy, and the use is the relation
(`illustrates`), not the kind.

## Alternatives considered

- **More terms in `standing`.** Adding "proposed" or "formal" to standing
  would answer two questions with one field, which is the present problem.
  Rejected.
- **A separate scheme for studies** (STUDY-, with design, threats and
  outcomes fields). It would give proposed studies more structure. But a
  study is used by claims exactly as a case is, through the same relations,
  and a second scheme would split the index and the relations. Rejected for
  now. If proposed studies need structured fields, they can be added to CASE
  conditioned on kind.
- **Tags.** The topics vocabulary says what a work or case is about, not
  what it is. Rejected.
- **Finer kinds** (vignette apart from thought experiment; analogy;
  counterexample). Vignettes are thought experiments that bear no weight,
  and the `illustrates` relation already says so. Counterexample is a
  relation. Rejected: six is what the 81 cases need.

## Consequences

- `luria.yaml` gains `case-kinds` (six terms with labels and blurbs) and
  the CASE field `kind`, required. luria renders `docs/cases/kind/` as it
  renders `standing/`, so the proposed studies have an index page.
- All 81 cases get `kind:` as tabled below. Retired cases get one too, so
  that the field is never missing.
- Four classifications were judgement calls, and the owner may move them:
  - [CASE-004](../cases.d/CASE-004.md) is the stimuli of a study design (proposed-study), not a
    thought experiment.
  - [CASE-062](../cases.d/CASE-062.md), "a constructed study, built on documented halves", is
    proposed-study, not reported-study.
  - [CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md), Dennett's thermostat, is thought-experiment, not specimen.
  - [CASE-039](../cases.d/CASE-039.md), the relativity craft, is a documented thought experiment.
- The template's comment for CASE names the field and its terms.
