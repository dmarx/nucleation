  Brown and colleagues (2020), GPT-3, arXiv 2005.14165. Eight
  autoregressive transformers from 125M to 175B parameters, each
  pretrained on 300B tokens, evaluated with zero, one or a few task
  demonstrations in the context and no weight updates: few-shot
  performance rises with size faster than zero-shot, and larger models
  reach a level with fewer examples. Scale here is model size at fixed
  data; there is no comparison without pretraining. The authors leave open
  whether in-context learning acquires a task or recognizes one learned in
  pretraining. Read in [NOTE-2160](../notes.d/NOTE-2160.md); account [THEORY-2062](../theory.d/THEORY-2062.md).
