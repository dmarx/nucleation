# LIT-tmphoqwi: Shop Class as Soulcraft: The case for the manual trades

Matthew B. Crawford (2006). "Shop Class as Soulcraft". *The New Atlantis* 13 (Summer 2006), pp. 7–24.

## Key takeaways

- "the degradation of work is ultimately a cognitive matter."
- The new frontier is "draining it of its cognitive elements" in office work.
