## The account

Jung and colleagues (2026) find that LLM agent memory systems which store a
dependency rule and a later change to its premise still answer with the
pre-change value, scoring 3% on propagating the change and 1% on recognizing
that a dependent fact has become uncertain, because the change is not
retrieved or not used; only a configuration that writes the consequence at
the time of the change partly succeeds, at about 70 times the cost.
