# THEORY-tmp8ypke: Bogliacino, Pejsachowicz, Liva and Lupiáñez-Villanueva propose judging an interface by whether it raises the rate of choices that contradict a separately elicited or assigned valuation, rather than by whether it changes behaviour; in an online experiment with 7,430 Europeans, dark patterns raised such inconsistency by 6 to 12 percentage points and a confirmation-prompt remedy did not significantly reduce it, and in a market experiment the same emotionally manipulative interface raised inconsistency when it steered against data protection and lowered it when it steered toward it, which a test of behavioural change cannot tell apart

## The account

Bogliacino, Pejsachowicz, Liva and Lupiáñez-Villanueva propose judging an interface by whether it raises the rate of choices that contradict a separately elicited or assigned valuation, rather than by whether it changes behaviour; in an online experiment with 7,430 Europeans, dark patterns raised such inconsistency by 6 to 12 percentage points and a confirmation-prompt remedy did not significantly reduce it, and in a market experiment the same emotionally manipulative interface raised inconsistency when it steered against data protection and lowered it when it steered toward it, which a test of behavioural change cannot tell apart.
