# LIT-2976: Power laws, Pareto distributions and Zipf's law

Newman (2005). Contemporary Physics 46, 323–351. arXiv cond-mat/0412004;
DOI 10.1080/00107510500052444.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A1, A5 for the survey of power-law distributions and the mechanisms that generate them (URL at A1, arXiv id at A5). It bears on the exchange's main reference for the catalogue of power-law mechanisms (rich-get-richer, criticality, first passage, mixtures).

Read on 2026-11-22 in NOTE-tmpa7yd2; the reading is THEORY-tmp6jdas. The
exchange's characterization (Yule process, critical phenomena, random
walks, combinations of exponentials) is accurate, with two corrections to
this entry's first summary:

- **Not "mixtures".** Newman's "combinations of exponentials" is an
  exponential of an exponentially distributed variable, x ∝ e^(by), which
  is CLAIM-795's item 6 (growth at a spread of ages), not a mixture of
  exponential rates (item 4). The review has no rate-mixture or
  superstatistics mechanism.
- **Not "first passage".** Newman treats the first return to the origin
  of a symmetric walk (gambler's ruin), by generating functions, not
  first passage to a boundary. The exponent, 3/2, is the same.

It covers four of CLAIM-795's seven items (ARG-tmp3ppjy), and does not
carry CLAIM-815's identity (ARG-tmp275n1).
