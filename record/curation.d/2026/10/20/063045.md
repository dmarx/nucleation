The owner asked for the two proposals left open in the entry of 01:49 to
be applied. Neither claim changes, and neither status moves. Both THEORYs
take version 2.

- **[THEORY-458](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-458.md)** (Gallagher's ownership and agency). Kalckert & Ehrsson
  2012 ([LIT-1273](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1273.md)) is added as a source. Its rotated, actively moved
  model hand gave agency reports without ownership. That closes the second
  refutation route in the promote_when, which was that the two "could not
  be dissociated outside involuntary movement". It is closed only for now:
  the reports have no expectation control, and [THEORY-766](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-766.md) holds that
  rubber hand reports have not been separated from expectation. A new
  section records three further points. The two senses interact (they
  correlate only when the hand is owned, r = 0.503). The comparator is not
  tested. And "agency" there is felt control of a model finger.
- **[THEORY-531](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-531.md)** (Williams's two tellings). Nichols & Bruno 2010
  ([LIT-1269](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1269.md)) is cited in "What this does not say" and Connections, not
  as a source.
  - Among their students, verdicts did not follow the person of the
    telling, against Williams's remark on his own two arguments.
  - Memory still mattered when the memories went to no one, which weakly
    undercuts his experimenter's-will explanation.
  - The survey asks nothing about fear, and the promote_when already rules
    out folk reports, so the claim is untouched.
