# LIT-2433: Fetal Cells Traffic to Injured Maternal Myocardium and Undergo Cardiac Differentiation

Rina J. Kara, Paola Bolli, Ioannis Karakikes, Iwao Matsunaga, Joseph Tripodi, Omar Tanweer, Perry R. Altman, Neil S. Shachter, Austin Nakano, Vesna Najfeld and Hina W. Chaudhry (2011). "Fetal Cells Traffic to Injured Maternal Myocardium and Undergo Cardiac Differentiation." *Circulation Research 110(1): 82-93*. DOI-10.1161/circresaha.111.249037.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1727](../notes.d/NOTE-1727.md)). An experimental demonstration (mouse)
that fetal microchimeric cells are recruited selectively to an injured
maternal organ and differentiate there into functional tissue types, with
Cdx2+ (placental/trophoblast-associated) cells proposed as a candidate cell
type for cardiac regenerative therapy.
