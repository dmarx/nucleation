# ARG-tmpdg5zy: A still life that is a fixed point alone and is broken and rebuilt in a role, by itself or by the pattern it serves, shows that the mechanism keeping a form in being can change with the form's role

## The inference

One coherent system, Life, in which a form's shape is held fixed while
its role varies, and in which its mechanism of persistence is observed
to vary with the role. The block and the eater are each fixed points
alone. In CASE-tmpnqx8e's gun, each block has two of its four cells dead
at one generation of every cycle and is rebuilt by the gun. In
CASE-tmpjucsj, the eater across the gun's stream has one cell dead every
30 generations and regrows it from its own cells. Each form is the same
shape throughout, and only its role differs.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes, and more than coherent: it is exact.
  Each step follows from the rule B3/S23 and was computed. Every starting
  configuration is in the cases, so the computation can be repeated.
- **Does it separate the very things the conclusion says can come
  apart?** The conclusion separates a form's identity (its shape) from
  the mechanism of its persistence, and links the mechanism to the role.
  - The shape is held fixed: the same four or seven cells, alone and in
    the role.
  - The mechanism differs, and is identified cell by cell: no cell ever
    dies alone; a cell dies and is reborn in the role.
  - The role differs: nothing depends on the lone form, while the gun
    stops emitting without its blocks, and the eater bounds the gun's
    output.

  What the case does not separate is the role from the perturbation it
  brings, because in both constructions the role is to take strikes. The
  conclusion is stated accordingly: a role *can* change the mechanism.
  It is not said that roles as such do, and the claim's "What it does not
  say" records the limit.
