# CLAIM-tmpsrc5a: Each position of a gamed evaluator yields to a different remedy

## The claim

DISTINCTION-001 tells the positions apart by two interventions, and those
interventions are remedies:

| Position | Fresh evaluator | Signals costly to forge | Independent evaluator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trained learner | works until retrained | helps | works |
| Outside agent | fails: it adapts | needed | works only if many and costly to fit |
| Inside, a motive | fails: the motive moves with the mind | rarely available | works: another person's judgment |

So a defence chosen without asking where the optimizer sits can fail. A
ranking that rotates its evaluators without raising the cost of forgery
only resets an outside agent's search. A chatbot user given a new chatbot
keeps their own motive.

## What it does not say

That the table is complete. It names the remedy that fits each position,
not every remedy that helps.
