# SYNTHESIS-tmpoihy8: An organization's regulatory difficulty is frustration among its maintained demands, an ordinal fact about which sets of them can be jointly met; its perspectives' activation, tolerance, claims and grants are quantities separate from that fact, so a quiet perspective need not be satisfied and raising a threshold is not relief; and motivational integration is the co-satisfaction of the demands within tolerances the will can recognize

*A part account of the will-organization line (ADR-083, ADR-084); the line's account is built over it. Five of its claims have distributed-agency as their first line, and four organizational-affect or formal-apparatus; CASE-129's is authenticity.*

## The account

**Frustration, ordinal.** CLAIM-368 (the owner's U33 of the exchange of
2026-10-17, Active; distributed-agency first) rests on CLAIM-283 and
CLAIM-027: the primitive for regulatory difficulty is frustration, a
relation between maintained demands and what limits their realization,
distinct from strain and from distress. CLAIM-347 (the owner's correction
at U37, Active) rests on it: jointly satisfiable sets form a complex, relief
is its strict enlargement for the same demands, and "dropping or
suppressing a demand is not relief". CLAIM-357 (A's, Active) rests on
CLAIM-368 and CLAIM-365 and separates structural infeasibility, realized
violation and minimax suboptimality, none of them thwarting. CLAIM-500
(Proposed), split from CLAIM-357 by the eighteenth status review, rests on
it: governance-induced frustration is not idle. CLAIM-631 (Proposed,
demonstrated; the derivation that was SYNTHESIS-008) rests on CLAIM-347 and
CLAIM-549 and gives a third complex, met or quiet, that decides when
frustration forces a member that acts while unmet.

**Relief by niche differentiation.** CLAIM-554 (A7, Active) rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-361 and
CLAIM-369: niche differentiation matters as possible relief, not as reduced
competition.

**Integration and tolerance.** CLAIM-534 (A29's wording at the owner's U33,
Active) rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-538, CLAIM-357 and CLAIM-079: CLAIM-354's
motivational integration is the extent to which maintained demands are
jointly met within tolerances the will can recognize, across contexts and
under its own dynamics, with realizability, realization, maintenance and
allocation coming apart. CLAIM-537 (demonstrated, Active) rests on
CLAIM-538 and gives the cycle conditions under disturbance; CLAIM-628
(demonstrated, Proposed; formal-apparatus first) rests on CLAIM-537 and
CLAIM-538 for a class of disturbances the agent cannot tell apart.

**Activation is not satisfaction.** CLAIM-548 (the owner's U18, Active)
rests on CLAIM-283 and uses TERM-094: a process can become active and act
without expecting success, so activation, authority granted and efficacy
are separate. CLAIM-549 (A's construction at the owner's U17,
demonstrated, Active) rests on CLAIM-347 and CLAIM-361, grounding on CASE-109
and CASE-129: the quiescent complex is not the requirement complex, and
"raising thresholds can only enlarge the quiescent complex" without relief.
CASE-129 (A18's, at the owner's U18; Active; authenticity first) is two
alarms quiet in ordinary operation, only one of which responds to a new
hazard: "A quiet signal may be calibration or suppression". It also grounds
CLAIM-553, and illustrates CLAIM-349 and CLAIM-364 of other parts.
CLAIM-550 (demonstrated, Active) rests on CLAIM-347 and derives a
perspective's tolerance from its activation threshold. CLAIM-551 (Active),
the DBT reading of the owner's U29, rests on CLAIM-550: cognitive
dysregulation as a consequence of emotion dysregulation, which makes the
effective will mood-dependent. CASE-192 (Proposed) illustrates it against
an attentional reading.

**Tolerance as conferral.** CLAIM-552 (the owner's U12, Active) rests on
CLAIM-534 and CLAIM-347: a perspective's tolerance is its criterion for
conferring influence, directed and set by its own priorities. CLAIM-553 (the
owner's U15 as corrected at U17, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-347, CLAIM-417 and
CLAIM-534: observed intervention is a propensity times legibility times a
channel, "so a perspective that does not intervene has not thereby
endorsed", and wholeheartedness read through Frankfurt lies within every
perspective's tolerance. It uses TERM-102. QUESTION-372 (the owner's U19,
verbatim) asks whether threshold induces tolerance, tolerance compatibility,
and compatibility flexibility or effectiveness; CLAIM-548 and CLAIM-550 bear
on it, and it refines QUESTION-090. CASE-195 (Proposed) is a construction
for its last step, flexibility spread by leverage and culture.

**Claims and grants.** CLAIM-484 (Proposed), split from CLAIM-283 by the
status review of 2026-10-27, rests on it: internal conflict is the gap
between the precedence a motive claims and the authority it is granted.
ARG-049, from the MSF transcript, is Rejected. CLAIM-442 (the assistant's,
Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on CLAIM-283 and CLAIM-437 and
puts Frijda's emotion (TERM-094) on the claim side and system valence on
the grant side; since its version 5 (2026-11-11) its body cites CLAIM-484,
not CLAIM-283, for internal conflict as the gap, and says its title uses
only CLAIM-283's distinction. CLAIM-527 (Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on
CLAIM-440 and CLAIM-079: viable reach, valence and emotion are three
quantities. CLAIM-447 (the owner's, Proposed; organizational-affect first)
rests on CLAIM-437: model-based wanting ranks predicted futures under
interventions. TERM-079 (Proposed) proposes belief of a process on the same
footing, and QUESTION-270 asks when an accurate evaluation is registered and
still denied authority; CLAIM-552 and CLAIM-553 bear on it.
