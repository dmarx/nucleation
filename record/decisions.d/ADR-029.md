# ADR-029: presupposes joins the THEORY relations

## Context

On 2026-10-05 the owner noticed that most theories relate to papers but not
to other theories. A count bore it out:

- 129 of the 153 theories name another theory in their bodies;
- 58 declare any theory-to-theory relation;
- 319 named pairs are undeclared;
- 35 bodies say outright "no relation is declared".

The scheme had three relations:

- `extends` is a refinement or widening of the same mechanism;
- `rivals` is another account of the same phenomenon that cannot also be
  right ([ADR-022](ADR-022.md));
- `corrects` replaces the other's reasoning. It has never been used.

The undeclared mentions are mostly contrasts and parallels, but a common
kind is a real commitment none of the three can say: one account takes
another's conclusion as a premise for a claim about a different
phenomenon. Pettit's corporate responsibility ([THEORY-139](../theory.d/THEORY-139.md)) rests on List
and Pettit's result that a rational group must form its judgments
holistically ([THEORY-127](../theory.d/THEORY-127.md)). It argues from that result to a corporation
having a mind of its own, and from there to responsibility. It was filed
as `extends` because `extends` was the nearest relation available, and
that is false: responsibility is not a refinement of the aggregation
mechanism.

## Decision

Add `presupposes` to the THEORY scheme, with the converse
`presupposed_by`, written by `luria link --fix`.

- **Meaning.** This account takes the other's conclusion as a premise for a
  claim about something else.
- **Consequence for status.** If the presupposed account is Rejected, the
  presupposing one loses a premise and needs another route to its
  conclusion. It is not thereby wrong. That is the difference from
  `extends`, where the extension usually falls with what it extends.
- **Not in the chain.** The `theory` chain draws lines of explanation from
  `extends` and `corrects`, with the invariant that linked accounts share a
  topic. Presupposition often crosses topics (social ontology to ethics,
  ecology to method), and those are the links a reader most needs, so it
  joins no chain and the topic invariant does not apply to it.
- **Explained where held.** As with every relation, the body says in prose
  what is taken from the other account.

First uses:

- [THEORY-139](../theory.d/THEORY-139.md) `presupposes` [THEORY-127](../theory.d/THEORY-127.md), replacing its `extends`.
- [THEORY-136](../theory.d/THEORY-136.md) `presupposes` [THEORY-132](../theory.d/THEORY-132.md). Scheffer and Carpenter's standard of
  evidence for alternative stable states is built on Holling's picture of
  basins of attraction and resilience as basin size. It is a claim about
  how to test for alternative states, not a refinement of Holling's claim
  that stability and resilience come apart.

The remaining undeclared pairs are left for an audit, which should look for
misfiled `extends` as well as missing relations.

## Alternatives considered

- **Prose only.** This is what filings did before this decision. It is true
  but invisible to the views and to the older entry, which never gets a
  back-reference.
- **Stretching `extends`.** That is what happened with [THEORY-139](../theory.d/THEORY-139.md), and it
  misstates the claim. It would also put cross-topic presuppositions into
  the lines of explanation, where the topic invariant flags them as
  unbound.
- **A generic `related` or `see_also`.** That would let a link stand
  without saying what it commits to, which defeats the point of declared
  relations. Contrasts and parallels stay in prose because they commit to
  nothing that could fail.
- **Adding it to LIT as well.** Papers already have `extends`, `rivals`,
  `corrects` and `compared_against`. No case has yet needed presupposition
  between papers, so the LIT scheme is unchanged.
