The owner asked how THEORYs are distributed against LITs and NOTEs, and
then asked for the low-coverage topics to be audited, lowest first. The
owner's working hypothesis is that a work read closely enough to carry a
NOTE will usually support at least one THEORY. The owner expects that old
readings hold accounts that were never filed, and that filing them would
strengthen the record's arguments.

## The count that chose the topic

On 2026-10-16 the record held 1,161 LITs, 933 NOTEs and 400 THEORYs. 894
LITs carry a NOTE; 505 of those are a THEORY's `source`, and 389 are not.
By primary tag, moral-psychology had 18 LITs and no THEORY. Fourteen of
the 18 had a `Read` NOTE. The other four are `Unavailable`. Counted over
all tags, moral-psychology had three THEORYs, all on responsibility, and
none drew on these fourteen.

## What entered

Twelve THEORYs, four `Active` and eight `Proposed`. Codes are the
temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

- **Two rival accounts.** Morality-as-cooperation (THEORY-tmp8vbpr) from
  LIT-503, LIT-047 and LIT-496. Moral Foundations Theory (THEORY-tmphcyt1)
  from LIT-499, LIT-502 and LIT-500. Each is filed with the half of it the
  evidence does not reach. For MAC that is the prediction that
  non-cooperative behaviour is morally neutral (LIT-503, p. 40). For MFT
  it is Sanctity's pathogen origin.
- **Four findings, `Active`.** The 961-of-962 ethnographic result, with
  its detectability caveat (THEORY-tmpvm5mu). The MAC-Q's seven Relevance
  factors in three countries (THEORY-tmpdn4s2). The liberal–conservative
  foundation profile (THEORY-tmp4dj55). Purity moralized in MAC's own
  questionnaire data (THEORY-tmps5brs).
- **Six more accounts, `Proposed`.** The MFQ-2's six factors
  (THEORY-tmpqgklr, extending MFT). Puritanical morality as cooperation,
  from LIT-101 (THEORY-tmpbzrkk). Fairness by partner choice, from LIT-508
  (THEORY-tmp7eb7s). Relationship regulation, from LIT-507
  (THEORY-tmpreimy). The social intuitionist model, from LIT-511
  (THEORY-tmpjpjf8), which MFT presupposes. Obligation from joint
  commitment, from LIT-125 (THEORY-tmpc6rib).
- **`rivals` edges.** MAC against MFT and against partner choice. MFT
  against the puritanical, relationship-regulation and MAC accounts.

## What did not enter, and why

- **Moral Molecules (LIT-509, NOTE-399).** Its reading says it "should
  not be cited as evidence that morality is combinatorial". Its 21 cells
  were each filled by searching for a confirming instance, with no
  protocol, and the paper says the theory does not predict which molecules
  exist. There is no finding to state. It is named in the MAC account's
  "does not say".
- **The 256-society dictionary study (LIT-512, NOTE-406).** It cannot
  detect valence. Its agreement with the sixty-society hand codes is κ
  .08–.25. It is in the Connections of THEORY-tmpvm5mu as a rough
  replication of presence, not of valence. It is not a source, because
  that THEORY does not rest on it.
- **The Turkish validation (LIT-513).** It entered as the second source
  of the MAC-Q THEORY, not as a THEORY of its own. Its prosociality and
  scarcity results are nulls or near-nulls and are reported there.

So twelve of fourteen readings became sources. That fits the owner's
hypothesis for this topic. Two caveats apply. Several readings were
already written with a THEORY in view (NOTE-034's "Any THEORY document
citing it…"). And the two that entered nothing are the programme's
weakest papers, which their readings had already judged.

## Choices made

- **Status.** A finding was made `Active` when the record believes it as
  stated: as a description of self-report, or of a coded corpus, with the
  caveats in its title or "does not say". An account went `Proposed`
  when its readings mark it untested or argued rather than shown. No
  account here is `Active`.
- **No `answers` edge.** QUESTION-007 asks what grounds normativity
  between agents at one level. Tomasello's account describes how the
  sense of obligation between peers arises. Its author concedes that it
  cannot say what anyone ought to do. It is named in the THEORY's
  Connections and not linked as an answer.
- **Error carried forward.** The hawk–dove attribution to Maynard Smith
  and Price, which NOTE-034 and NOTE-408 correct, is repeated in the MAC
  THEORY as a correction rather than as the paper's lineage.
