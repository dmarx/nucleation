# LIT-2983: A Random Matrix Theory Perspective on the Spectrum of Learned Features and Asymptotic Generalization Capabilities

Yatin Dandi, Luca Pesce, Hugo Cui, Florent Krzakala, Yue M. Lu & Bruno Loureiro (2024). arXiv 2410.18938; AISTATS 2025 (PMLR 258), unconfirmed from the copy read.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A cites it at A32 for spikes in the feature spectrum after a gradient step. Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-2258](../notes.d/NOTE-2258.md)) and filed as [THEORY-2170](../theory.d/THEORY-2170.md). The spike is in the weights; in the features, learning shows chiefly as a reshaped bulk. It grounds [CLAIM-799](../claims.d/CLAIM-799.md)'s point that the spiked picture of learned features is a theorem, and qualifies it ([ARG-166](../arguments.d/ARG-166.md)). Ba and colleagues ([LIT-3039](LIT-3039.md)), Moniri and colleagues ([LIT-3032](LIT-3032.md)), Cui and colleagues ([LIT-3038](LIT-3038.md)) and Wang, Engel and colleagues ([LIT-3035](LIT-3035.md)) are registered from this reading.
