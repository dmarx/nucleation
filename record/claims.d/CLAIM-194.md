# CLAIM-194: A gap or coupling criterion cannot tell a boundary a system produces from one imposed on it: a membrane the cell makes and a beam joining two pendulums pass alike, so the block test cannot by itself supply the individuality minimal agency needs

## The claim

[CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) makes a combined form one system, relative to a target, when its
members form a nearly decomposable block whose gap beats the Occam factor.
[CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md) takes "one system there" as its first condition for agency.
Both read the boundary off the strength of interaction inside a candidate
block against interaction across its edge.

[THEORY-073](../theory.d/THEORY-073.md) says of the Markov blanket that "the formalism also gives the
same description to a membrane the cell makes and a beam nobody's states
make". [THEORY-066](../theory.d/THEORY-066.md) gives the formal reason: the partition "takes the
internal set as an input" and "never outputs the internal set". The gap
criterion is better off than the blanket on the second point, since it
compares candidate partitions and does not take one as given. It is not
better off on the first. Two pendulums joined by a beam clear the threshold
for a target such as the next swing, and so does a cell. The criterion has
no term for who made the coupling.

The record holds two accounts that put the line there:

- [THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md): an organism differs from a candle flame by "several distinct
  constraints that produce one another at different time scales". That is
  production, not coupling strength.
- [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md): minimal agency needs individuality in which "the system
  distinguishes itself from its environment, and an observer does not do it
  for the system".

So the block test can find a system in the sense of a unit of description,
but the individuality [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md) requires is a further condition. When
[CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md) uses the block test as its first condition, it admits jointly
coupled forms whose boundary is maintained from outside.

## What it does not say

- **That the block criterion is wrong as an answer to [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md).** As a
  test of when a description should name a unit, the counter leaves it
  standing. It bears on [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md)'s use of it as the individuality
  condition of agency.
- **That observer-relativity is the problem.** [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md) already concedes
  that its tests are relative to level and target. This counter holds even
  with the target fixed.
- **That production is measurable where coupling is.** [THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md) is
  Proposed, and its own promote_when asks for a flame and a protocell to be
  analysed the same way. Neither has been done.

## What would answer it

Either a case showing that, for a natural target, imposed boundaries
systematically fail the gap test while produced ones pass it, so the
criterion tracks production after all. Or a reply that [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md)'s later
conditions (adaptation of the policy, governance over regimes) already
exclude externally maintained blocks, so the first condition need not.

## Note of 2026-10-21: regulation of exchange

A9 §3 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 separated predictive closure from
organizational closure and proposed that "A living or agentive boundary
may be distinguished by its regulation of exchange, rather than by the
absence of exchange". That is [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md)'s interactional asymmetry, which
A9 does not cite. A8 §1 adds the informational side of this claim's point:
since agents are coupled to their environments, exact closure "cannot
straightforwardly serve as a necessary condition for agency", and since
noise is closed, it is not sufficient either. So neither a coupling
criterion (this claim) nor a closure criterion ([THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md)) tells a
produced boundary from an imposed one. The two closures are separated in
[TERM-tmpfezzd](../terms.d/TERM-tmpfezzd.md) and [TERM-tmpnjo1t](../terms.d/TERM-tmpnjo1t.md).
