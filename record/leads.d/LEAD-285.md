# LEAD-285: Run separating cases for potential consequentiality: presently equivalent states that diverge after capability building, equally predictive compressions that differ in later learning cost, jointly but not separately consequential capacities, and a recognized distinction that loses efficacy under a change of authority without becoming less legible

## What to do

A23 §15:

| Proposed experiment | Discriminating observation |
|---|---|
| Latent distinction | Two presently equivalent states diverge after a controlled capability-building intervention. |
| Abstraction | Two equally predictive compressions differ in the cost of learning later tasks. |
| Interest development | New competence produces sustained evaluative sensitivity after initial incentives are removed. |
| Perspective diversity | Equally performing collectives differ in their ability to recognize unfamiliar problems. |
| Institutional capture | A recognized distinction loses practical efficacy following a change in authority, without becoming less legible. |
| Complementary capabilities | Joint acquisition produces consequential distinctions not available through separate acquisitions. |

## What it would move

- **Latent distinction**: [TERM-185](../terms.d/TERM-185.md)'s D_{B,T} against present
  equivalence, [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)'s monotonicity in the probe family.
- **Abstraction**: [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md) and the overconsolidation of [SYNTHESIS-105](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-105.md),
  through A23 §8's "abstraction is a redistribution of consequentiality
  across organizational scales".
- **Interest development**: [CLAIM-1423](../claims.d/CLAIM-1423.md)'s test of a new evaluative
  distinction and [CLAIM-1432](../claims.d/CLAIM-1432.md)'s axiological frontier; with the
  support schedule varied, it also decides between [CASE-430](../cases.d/CASE-430.md)'s linear
  coupling, where support does not change whether the interest lasts,
  and [CLAIM-1454](../claims.d/CLAIM-1454.md)'s threshold coupling, where it does.
- **Perspective diversity**: [CLAIM-1038](../claims.d/CLAIM-1038.md) and [CLAIM-1002](../claims.d/CLAIM-1002.md).
- **Institutional capture**: [QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md).
- **Complementary capabilities**: [CLAIM-1370](../claims.d/CLAIM-1370.md).

## Cautions A23 states

"I would avoid using the total number of potentially consequential
distinctions as a universal measure of developmental value. Many
distinctions are harmful, distracting, redundant, or costly to maintain.
Moreover, the definition of a distinction depends on the granularity of
the available language."
