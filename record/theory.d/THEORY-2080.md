# THEORY-2080: Newer affix subreddits are usually far smaller

## The account

Pairs of subreddits whose names differ by an affix (science and
askscience) and whose content is related are common, and the affixed
community is usually the newer and much less active. About half share few
early participants, so name and content similarity do not imply a shared
founding population. Where a newer community did draw more than a tenth
of its first hundred participants from the older (a spinoff), users who
tried it were, in most kinds of pair, more likely than matched users who
did not to increase their activity in the original over the next 30
days; for place and nsfw spinoffs and some pairs, less likely. The
authors read exploration as a signal of interest, not a cause.

## Evidence

Observational analysis of all of Reddit to 2014, with matched controls
on prior activity. No causal design.
