The triage of 2026-10-29 (curation entry of 20:59) filed it for
[QUESTION-252](../questions.d/QUESTION-252.md), which asks which conditions on an effective description
entail or are parameters of the others. Rosas et al. ([NOTE-1030](../notes.d/NOTE-1030.md)) credit

generalize and re-derive, and Krakauer et al. ([NOTE-1400](../notes.d/NOTE-1400.md)) cite it for
the general case of closure implying sufficiency. [CLAIM-409](../claims.d/CLAIM-409.md) rests on
those THEORYs. The record holds these results second-hand.
