# LIT-tmp4ummt: The ideal victim: A critical race theory (CRT) approach

Long (2021). *The ideal victim: A critical race theory (CRT) approach*. International Review of Victimology 27(3):
344-362. DOI 10.1177/0269758021993339.

## Key takeaways

Christie's ideal victim (weak, respectable, blameless, harmed by a "big and bad" stranger) and ideal offender, applied
to race and policing: victim status is conferred, and the police can act as the counter-power that withholds it.

## Standing in the record

Filed as a partial stand-in for [LIT-tmpyjztu](LIT-tmpyjztu.md) (Christie 1986): it restates his definition and attributes, not his
wider argument, and applies them to race and policing rather than partner violence.
