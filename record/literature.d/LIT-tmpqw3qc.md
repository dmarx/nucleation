Filed for [QUESTION-tmprg4fj](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmprg4fj.md): it gives a transformation-invariant, data-driven criterion for
macroscopic variables that have their own dynamics. That is a coarse-graining fixed by the system
rather than by an analyst's target. It bears on whether the target can be system-supplied and on
whether the winning partition is unique.
