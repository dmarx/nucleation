# LIT-tmpdq493: Investigative journalism: Market failures and government intervention through public broadcasters

Ole-Andreas Elvik Næss (2024). "Investigative journalism: Market failures and government intervention through public broadcasters." *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 229: 106870*. DOI-10.1016/j.jebo.2024.106870.

## Key takeaways

Skimmed on 2026-11-12 (NOTE-tmpaa0uz). A public-goods model plus survey
experiments. Each person's payoff from investigative journalism is shared; a
firm selling access can collect only private willingness to pay (WTP), so
when marginal cost lies between one person's marginal benefit and the sum
over N people, provision is Pareto-inefficiently low. Surveys measure WTP
for three days of investigative journalism and support for funding it
through public broadcasters versus subsidies to private media; treatments
test exclusive access (a Coasian property-rights fix), widespread
dissemination, and a promise of political neutrality.
