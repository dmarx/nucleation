# LEAD-tmpjdtrl: Find and read work on what data early in pretraining does to the cost of learning other data later: the owner's recollection of social-media or forum text first easing domain text later, code first easing natural language, and the neighbours the first search found

## What it would do

1. **The owner's recollection**, narrowed: Reddit, social media or forum
   text early in pretraining making harder domain-specific text easier to
   learn later. A search for it is under way.
2. **Code first**, the owner's suggestion: work that trains on code before
   natural language, or mixes it in early, and measures the effect on
   later natural-language learning. Hu and colleagues' formal-language
   first phase ([THEORY-tmpk9cmk](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpk9cmk.md)) is the nearest held result.
3. **Unread neighbours from the first search**, identifiers verified there:
   Akter and colleagues on front-loading reasoning data (arXiv
   2510.03264); Blakeney and colleagues on domain upsampling late (arXiv
   2406.03476); Feng and colleagues on two-phase pretraining (arXiv
   2412.15285); Zhang and colleagues on curricula in pretraining (arXiv
   2506.11300); Yıldız and colleagues on domain order in continual
   pretraining (arXiv 2402.17400); Hernandez and colleagues on scaling
   laws for transfer, which measures pretraining as effective data
   transferred (arXiv 2102.01293).

## What would count

As for [LEAD-tmp5lpoy](LEAD-tmp5lpoy.md): the cost of a later acquisition with and without an
earlier one, the earlier one's cost stated, and order compared at fixed
total data.
