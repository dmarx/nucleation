## What changed in the record's threads

- **[THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md)** (the bridge from Minsky to Schwitzgebel) gains a
  section from these readings:
  - Dennett's discharge of homunculi explains why the downward move is
    licensed for intelligence and the upward one is not.
  - Chalmers's organisational invariance narrows the dispute to
    architecture.
  - The combination-problem paper reaches the account's two routes
    independently.
  - Two new candidates for a principled line are named: Dennett &
    Kinsbourne against a conscious central part, and Chalmers 2019's
    "not mediated" condition. Neither yet meets `promote_when`.
  - Searle and "Quining Qualia" are recorded as refusing the framing.
- **[THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md)** (AI consciousness) gains candidate evidence, not yet
  added to it:
  - Chalmers's LLM paper, a fifth prior: under 10% for current models, 25%
    or more within a decade;
  - footnote 26 of the meta-problem paper;
  - Turing's other-minds reply;
  - Searle's concession that behaviour licenses ascription until the
    program is known.

## Corrections the readings found

Each is held in the reading that found it; none is applied to the older
note it concerns.

- **Adams & Aizawa 2001 does not contain "coupling-constitution
  fallacy".** The phrase is from their later work; [NOTE-180](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-180.md) misattributes
  it.
- **Clark's 2005 quotation of the parity principle differs from [LIT-097](../../record/literature.d/LIT-097.md)'s
  text.**
- **Chalmers's fading-qualia paper misdates Block** (1980/1981; it is 1978).
  It also says Block finds group minds "bizarre", which Block's n. 19
  disclaims.
- **Levin's "Chalmers 2013" is *Panpsychism and Panprotopsychism*, not the
  combination paper.**
- **"Artificial Intelligence as Philosophy and as Psychology" appeared first
  in *Brainstorms* (1978), not in Ringle (1979).**
