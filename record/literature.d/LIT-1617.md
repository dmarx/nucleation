## Why this is here

Adjacent material for A2's "selection alignment" (S: "within-collective conflict relative to between-collective selection") and "host control of transmission", for which neither reply named a work. This is the standard model of the conflict, and it shows that host control can fail to evolve even where it would raise mean host fitness.
