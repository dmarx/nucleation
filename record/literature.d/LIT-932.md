# LIT-932: Hydrodynamic Stability Without Eigenvalues

Lloyd N. Trefethen, Anne E. Trefethen, Satish C. Reddy and Tobin A. Driscoll (1993), *Science* 261(5121), 578–584 — DOI-10.1126/science.261.5121.578

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-839](../notes.d/NOTE-839.md).*

- **The anomaly.** Eigenvalues predict instability above R = 5772 for
  Poiseuille flow and never for Couette flow. Transition is seen near
  R = 1000 and R = 350.
- **Three linear quantities, all large.** Transient growth sup_t
  ‖exp(−itL)‖ is R/29.1 (Couette) and R/71.5 (Poiseuille). The maximum
  resonance is (R/8.12)² and (R/17.4)², where a normal operator would give
  O(R). The smallest destabilizing operator perturbation is its inverse,
  O(R⁻²). The abstract's "factors on the order of 10⁵" is the resonance,
  not the transient growth.
- **The amplified structures are 3D.** Streamwise vortices that become
  streaks, at streamwise wavenumber zero. Two-dimensional perturbations,
  the traditional focus, amplify far less.
- **Pseudospectra unify them.** The ε-pseudospectrum is where
  ‖(ωI − L)⁻¹‖ ≥ 1/ε, equivalently the spectra of all perturbations of L
  of norm ≤ ε. Its reach into the unstable half-plane bounds transient
  growth from below.
- **Transition is conjectured, not shown.** In a 2 × 2 model, linear
  growth with an energy-neutral quadratic term gives a threshold
  amplitude O(R⁻³). The authors conjecture that shear-flow transition
  works the same way, with threshold O(R^γ), γ < −1.

## Standing in the record

Read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-839](../notes.d/NOTE-839.md). It is the short, widely read
statement of the non-modal view beside Farrell & Ioannou ([LIT-931](LIT-931.md),
[LIT-929](LIT-929.md)). Two accounts are filed from it. [THEORY-304](../theory.d/THEORY-304.md) holds the
shear-flow scalings, with the transition conjecture set outside the
claim. [THEORY-323](../theory.d/THEORY-323.md), with [LIT-931](LIT-931.md), holds the general statement that
the eigenvalues of a non-normal operator govern only the long-run limit.

[CLAIM-186](../claims.d/CLAIM-186.md) cites it for: "Shear flows judged stable by their eigenvalues
turn turbulent because a transient amplification hands the disturbance
to nonlinear dynamics." The paper shows the amplification. The
"because" is its conjecture, introduced with "we speculate" and
supported by a 2 × 2 model, and [CLAIM-186](../claims.d/CLAIM-186.md) should cite it as such. The
model itself suggests a narrower reading across: a burst becomes lasting
only if something feeds the grown disturbance back to where it started,
and the disturbance needed for that falls steeply as the amplification
grows.

machine-learning practice.
