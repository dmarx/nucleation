# QUESTION-442: In a distributed agent, is the self-model that makes a regime of valence felt, in the sense of attended and available, the same model that presents a single owner of the response, so that a change in who the owner is taken to be changes what is felt; or can feltness and the attribution of ownership come apart?

## Why it is a question

The record holds two self-models and does not say whether they are one.

- **[CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)** (Proposed): a regime of valence is felt "in the sense of
  attended and available to the system's other processes" when a deeper
  process models it, inferring which ordering wins the negotiation, which
  way the system is moving and how strongly it prevails. Its model is
  accurate or not about the potential.
- **[CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)** (Proposed): the binding of appraisal, readiness and
  governance can be real while "the single owner a self-model or
  collective self-description attributes it to is a representational
  construction". Its model is accurate or not about how the response is
  organized and to whom it is attributed.

If one model does both, attributing the response to a different owner
should change what is felt. If they are two, a system could feel its regime
accurately while misattributing whose it is, or the reverse. Neither claim
says which. The synthesis review of 2026-11-07 tried to integrate them and
found the link was a new conjecture, not something the two claims jointly
support, so it is asked here.

## What would answer it

The discriminating intervention changes who the owner is taken to be while
holding the model of the potential fixed, and the reverse. [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s three
layers ([QUESTION-266](QUESTION-266.md), which this refines) give the measurement of the
potential and of its model. What is missing is a measurement of the
attribution apart from report. Where the outcome can be read only through
report, it meets [QUESTION-048](QUESTION-048.md)'s difficulty: report is evidence of access,
and here of both models at once.

## What it does not ask

- **Whether experience in the phenomenal sense is present.** [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)
  declines that sense, as [QUESTION-059](QUESTION-059.md) records ("it declines the phenomenal
  sense this question asks about"), and so does this question.
- **Whether the owner is real.** [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) already holds that the binding
  can be real while the single owner is a construction; the question is
  whether that construction is also what makes the regime felt.
