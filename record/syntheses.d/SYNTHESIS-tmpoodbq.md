# SYNTHESIS-tmpoodbq: Rights, demands and commitments: the three are distinct and none establishes another, a demand's satisfaction condition carries no warrant so that an institution's maintained requirements cannot certify their own authority, a right that protects a choice constrains a policy and not a history, a demand ceases legitimately only by a valid normative power, inalienability limits what a holder can confer, rights once justified organize later justification, and a duty to help establish a needed institution is bounded by reachability

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, on rights, demands and commitments. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day, from which every component comes; none was in any account before. Several components also carry dependence-and-domination, and two cases carry it and not this line (below). Its sibling on accountability without blame and normative governability, filed at the same review, is SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9.*

## The account

Each link is stated by the entry named first, in its front matter unless
its body is cited. Every component is Proposed, or Open for a question.

**The contrast.** CLAIM-tmpnb4v4 (the owner's contrast, U41, "rights vs
demands vs commitments"; the definitions and non-implications are A's,
A95 and A97; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also will-organization):
rights, demands and commitments are distinct, none establishes another,
and each passage between them "needs its own warrant". It uses TERM-060
and complements CLAIM-tmpc7shc, CLAIM-tmpssfc1 and CLAIM-523. Its body
says CLAIM-tmpc7shc files the demand side "in its sharpest form" and
CLAIM-tmpssfc1 "the one passage A97 X singles out", and that the aquifer,
CASE-tmp9vd9x, "puts all three to work in one arrangement".

**A demand carries no warrant.** CLAIM-tmpc7shc (A's, A106 §4; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) stores no relation. Its body: a demand's
acceptance set fixes "what meets it, not its source, who bears it, to
whom it is addressed or why it binds", so the compatibility problem
"cannot establish that each deserves fulfillment"; it calls itself the
formal reason for CLAIM-tmpnb4v4's point, says CLAIM-tmpol7nz holds the
other half of A's two kinds of compatibility, and says CLAIM-tmpw7pli
applies it. CLAIM-tmpw7pli (A's mechanism and wording, A108 §1 and §4, at
the owner's U46, whose specimen it is; thesis, interpretive, Proposed;
also dependence-and-domination) rests on CLAIM-tmpc7shc and grounds in
CASE-tmpoe934: a categorical administrative gate can make evidence the
governing standard requires to count unable to change a decision, and
that the criterion is imposed and complied with "establishes neither its
legal authority nor its justification". It makes no finding on motive.
CASE-tmpoe934 (the owner's specimen; documented, Proposed; also
dependence-and-domination) illustrates CLAIM-tmpc7shc: the 75-country
immigrant-visa pause of January 2026 and its vacatur on 2026-08-21. Its
body records the scope of closure and the motive as the owner's
characterization, with A108 §1's judgement that "all" avenues is too
broad.

**Rights that protect a choice.** CLAIM-tmpol7nz (A's, A74, A85, A90,
A97 III, A102 §4; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also will-organization)
grounds in CASE-tmpaasso, bears on QUESTION-tmptinzk, and complements
CLAIM-347, CLAIM-523, CLAIM-tmpnb4v4 and CLAIM-tmp6vck6: whether an
arrangement respects a right that protects a choice "is a property of its
policy over the protected choices, not of any realized history".
CASE-tmpaasso (A's construction, checked on filing; stipulated, Proposed;
also will-organization) bears on QUESTION-454: under a right of refusal,
two arrangements produce identical histories whenever the patient
consents, and only one respects the right.

**Release, not suppression.** CLAIM-tmpssfc1 (A's, A97 X; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; first line distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-361,
bears on QUESTION-456, and complements CLAIM-tmpnb4v4, CLAIM-tmpa0wdv and
CLAIM-662: a demand owed to a party ceases legitimately only by a valid
normative power, which changes the reference set; an institution's
ceasing to count it changes only the set it recognizes. Its body says
some rights "may not be releasable at all", citing CLAIM-tmp6vck6.

**Inalienability.** CLAIM-tmp6vck6 (A's, A85 §2 and §4 as corrected by
A87 §4, at the owner's U37; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; first line
dependence-and-domination) uses TERM-083, bears on QUESTION-tmptinzk,
QUESTION-tmpgeqju and QUESTION-454, and complements CLAIM-tmpa0wdv and
CLAIM-tmpcpijj: no delegation, contract, collective decision or success
in imposing an arrangement confers authority over whether a person keeps
the standing from which claims against it arise, "because the holder
lacks the power to confer it". CASE-tmpgp2qo (A's, A85 §11 Case A;
stipulated, Proposed; in dependence-and-domination only) illustrates
CLAIM-tmp6vck6 and bears on QUESTION-tmptinzk and QUESTION-454: two
informed, voluntary delegations, one limited and one purporting to be
unlimited. QUESTION-tmptinzk (the owner's, U37; Open; also
dependence-and-domination) refines QUESTION-129: can rights no
institution creates or can extinguish serve as the institution-independent
criteria the record's judgments need? QUESTION-tmpgeqju (A's, A85 §8 and
A90 XII; Open; first line dependence-and-domination) stores no relation:
who has standing to change an arrangement's governing structure, and
which protections lie beyond revision. Its body says CLAIM-tmp6vck6
bears on the limit half ("no reconstitution by those who delegated can
either"), and that QUESTION-tmptinzk asks whether rights of that kind can
be had.

**Rights in later justification, and the reply to Scanlon.**
QUESTION-tmpk7u3e (the owner's, U44; the alternatives and the test are
A's; Open) stores no relation: does the record's account only supplement
contractualism, or show an allocation of normative powers reasonable
rejection cannot explain without importing principles of standing or
authority? Its body names CLAIM-tmpol7nz, CLAIM-tmplidg6, CLAIM-523,
CLAIM-624 and CLAIM-tmpyn6oi as pieces a reply would use, and
QUESTION-tmpkqvxv as narrower. CLAIM-tmplidg6 (A's, A102 §3 and A106 §19,
at the owner's U44; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) stores no relation:
once justified, rights and powers become "constitutive elements of the
structure within which further justification occurs", so they can be
prior in organization where principles are prior in justification. Its
body relates it to CLAIM-086, to CLAIM-tmpnb4v4 ("This claim says what a
right does once the passage is made") and to CLAIM-tmpol7nz, and says it
is compatible with QUESTION-tmpk7u3e's supplement answer.

**Duties to establish, and feasibility.** CLAIM-tmpes6ef (A's, A102 §5,
after A's report of Scanlon's *Contractualism and Intuitionism*; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-560: the duties to comply with a
justified rule, to support a justified institution and to help establish
a needed one are distinct, and the third is bounded by reachability by
permissible, resource-bounded transitions and by whose part it is. Its
body says CASE-tmp9vd9x illustrates the gap the bound names, that
CLAIM-624's duty is A's third illustration, and that it leaves
QUESTION-tmpgeqju open. CASE-tmp9vd9x (A's construction, every number
rechecked; stipulated, Proposed; lines dependence-and-domination and
realizability) illustrates CLAIM-586, CLAIM-560, CLAIM-690 and
CLAIM-tmpnb4v4 and bears on QUESTION-tmpkqvxv: two households, an
aquifer, and a recycler that makes all three demands compatible and that
one household owns and uses as leverage for voting authority.
QUESTION-tmpkqvxv (A's, A100 §8B and §13, at the owner's U43; Open; also
realizability) stores no relation: can expanding what an arrangement can
realize change which principles governing it can reasonably be rejected?
Its body puts it to the aquifer and sets it beside QUESTION-tmpk7u3e.

## Not stated on the record

- Whether CLAIM-tmpw7pli's gate is a policy-level failure in
  CLAIM-tmpol7nz's sense. CLAIM-tmpw7pli states the gate
  counterfactually (evidence satisfying the individualized criteria
  "without that evidence changing the categorical refusal"); neither
  cites the other, and CLAIM-tmpc7shc cites both without saying so.
- Whether CLAIM-tmpssfc1's test, with CLAIM-tmplidg6, is an instance of
  CLAIM-518's thesis that judgments of obligation can need histories.
  Both make whether a demand binds depend on past valid acts; neither
  cites CLAIM-518.
- Whether CLAIM-tmpes6ef's reachability bound bears on
  QUESTION-tmpkqvxv's choice between a feasibility filter on demands and
  a feasibility effect on principles. CLAIM-tmpes6ef does not cite the
  question; the two meet only in CASE-tmp9vd9x.
- Whether CLAIM-tmpol7nz or CLAIM-tmplidg6 meets QUESTION-tmpk7u3e's
  test for the stronger answer. The question lists them as pieces; no
  entry says either meets it.
- Which rights are inalienable, and who supplies a demand's warrant:
  QUESTION-tmptinzk and QUESTION-129, which CLAIM-tmp6vck6 and
  CLAIM-tmpc7shc leave open.

## Arm B

Integrations tested at this review under ADR-075's step 5. None passes,
so nothing is filed.

- *CLAIM-tmpw7pli with CLAIM-tmpol7nz*: a categorical gate fails a right
  to individualized consideration at the level of policy, so no audit of
  realized refusals of otherwise ineligible applicants can show it.
  Route: derivation. Fails: it is a substitution instance of
  CLAIM-tmpol7nz's second separation in CLAIM-tmpw7pli's model, an
  inference CLAIM-tmpol7nz already holds. Left under "Not stated".
- *CLAIM-tmpssfc1 with CLAIM-tmp6vck6*: a demand grounded in inalienable
  standing cannot cease to apply by release. Already stated, in
  CLAIM-tmpssfc1's body.
- *CLAIM-tmpssfc1 with CLAIM-tmplidg6*: whether a demand still binds is
  fixed by the history of valid normative acts, not by the recognized
  set. Route: derivation. Fails as trivial: it restates CLAIM-tmpssfc1's
  N_t against N̂_t, and CLAIM-tmplidg6's promise example states it ("The
  promisor's later preferences do not automatically extinguish the
  obligation"). Its classification under CLAIM-518 is left under "Not
  stated".
- *CLAIM-tmpes6ef with QUESTION-tmpkqvxv and CASE-tmp9vd9x*: the
  reachability bound filters duties, not principles, and so is an
  instance of the question's independence answer. Route: discriminating
  prediction. Fails: it predicts nothing CLAIM-tmpes6ef does not, and
  CLAIM-tmpes6ef says nothing about principles, which is the question's
  open part. Left under "Not stated".
- *CLAIM-tmp6vck6 with QUESTION-tmpgeqju*: no reconstitution can
  extinguish standing no delegation could confer. Already stated, in
  QUESTION-tmpgeqju's body.
- *CLAIM-tmpc7shc with CLAIM-tmpol7nz*: requirement compatibility and
  normative compatibility come apart for two reasons, unwarranted demands
  and rights over choices. Already stated, in CLAIM-tmpc7shc's body.

The candidate across this part and its sibling, CLAIM-tmpw7pli with
CLAIM-tmp69o4u, is recorded in SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9.
