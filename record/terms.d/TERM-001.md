# TERM-001: fidelity, as the number of preserved constraints

## Definition

A4: there is "**no translation that independently maximizes all of these**.
Translating therefore isn't decoding; it is selecting a point on a Pareto
frontier", with a weighted objective T* = argmax [w_s S + w_f F + w_r R +
w_t Tn + w_p P]. Its sharpest form is "quality ≈ number of preserved
constraints" ([CLAIM-020](../claims.d/CLAIM-020.md)).

## Why it was replaced

The mother's translation ([CASE-011](../cases.d/CASE-011.md)) "doesn't work either" on this measure and
was judged best. A18 replaced the checklist with a pragmatic signature
([TERM-013](TERM-013.md)).
