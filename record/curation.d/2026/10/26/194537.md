The owner asked for the discussion behind the PerspectiveLab experiments
to be added to the record (the exchange "Experimental Infrastructure
Review", shared at
https://chatgpt.com/share/6adfa6e4-a0b0-83e9-b3a0-0b53e6000277). The share
could not be fetched; the saved copy the owner uploaded holds turns T18 to
T24 only, and T24's reply, which had failed to render, was pasted by the
owner. No dates survive in the page. T18–T20 cite no work. T21–T23 cite
the Bayesian nonparametric literature on topic hierarchies and latent
features, almost always by a bare host name ("jmlr.org",
"proceedings.mlr.press", "Journal of the ACM", "IBM Research") and an
author surname, never by title. T24 cites interpretability work on training
dynamics by paraphrase alone, with no author, title or link.

Thirteen works are registered here. They were filed `Deferred`, unread,
and the owner then asked for all of them to be read ("go ahead and read
all of them"); all thirteen were read the same day (below). Each was
identified from the reply's wording, checked against the arXiv record,
Crossref, or the JMLR or PMLR page (OpenAlex for access, until its free
daily allowance ran out; Semantic Scholar refused for rate), and
literature.d was searched by surname, title words and identifier first;
none was held. Per [ADR-048](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-048.md), no anthology check was made. Two the replies
already relied on are held: Elhage et al.'s *Toy Models of Superposition*
([LIT-323](../../record/literature.d/LIT-323.md), T24) and the nested HDP ([LIT-002](../../record/literature.d/LIT-002.md), [NOTE-023](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-023.md); T21–T23).

**Corrections the lookups made.**

- The "Rosen-Zvi and colleagues" author–topic work "applied … to
  corporate email" (T21) is the ACM TOIS paper of 2010, the only one of
  their author–topic papers with the Enron corpus; the UAI 2004 paper
  that introduced the model (arXiv 1207.4169) used NIPS and CiteSeer.
- The "dependent IBP of Williamson, Orbanz, and Ghahramani" (T22, T23) is
  AISTATS 2010, PMLR 9. It is not the IBP compound Dirichlet process
  (Williamson, Wang, Heller and Blei, ICML 2010), which this filing's plan
  had listed as the candidate.
- "Correlated nonparametric latent feature models" (T22, no authors) is
  Doshi-Velez and Ghahramani, UAI 2009, which PMLR reissued as volume R7;
  hence the reply's "proceedings.mlr.press".
- The three IBP links of T22–T23 ("jmlr.org" twice, "jmlr.csail.mit.edu"
  for finite beta–Bernoulli approximations) are taken as one work,
  Griffiths and Ghahramani's JMLR review of 2011, which derives the
  finite beta–Bernoulli model in its §3.
- hLDA is attributed at T21 to "Blei, Griffiths, and Jordan", with the
  JACM link. The JACM paper (2010; arXiv 2007) is registered as cited; hLDA
  itself first appeared in an earlier paper with Tenenbaum (below).
- All six works T24 cites without author or title were identified: the induction-heads paper
  (Olsson et al. 2022), MIB (Mueller et al. 2025), the refined-LLC paper
  (Wang, Hoogland, van Wingerden, Furman and Murfet 2024; the record's
  reader had named it from memory, and the lookup confirmed it), the
  cross-checkpoint crosscoders of Bayazit, Mueller and Bosselut (2025),
  whose abstract ends in the reply's words "emergence, maintenance, and
  discontinuation", the fine-tuning circuit paper of Wang et al. (2025),
  and the "July 2026 theoretical study", which is Musat, Pimentel, Zucchet
  and Hofmann, arXiv 2607.11875, submitted 13 July 2026. The fine-tuning
  identification rests on a single sentence ("circuit connectivity can
  change substantially even when the components involved remain similar")
  matching that abstract's main finding, and is the least certain.
- The paraphrases of MIB (SAE features no better than neurons; supervised
  DAS best) and of Olsson et al. (stronger causal evidence in small
  attention-only models) match the abstracts.

**Dates.** Under [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md), the JACM paper, the blockmodel paper and every
interpretability work but one take their arXiv v1 dates. The exception,
found when it was read, is the induction-heads paper, which first
appeared on the Transformer Circuits site on 8 March 2022, months before
its arXiv copy, and takes that date as [LIT-323](../../record/literature.d/LIT-323.md) does. Doshi-Velez and
Ghahramani take the UAI date PMLR gives (18 June 2009), earlier than its
arXiv copy of 2012. The author–topic paper takes Crossref's online date
(29 January 2010). Thibaux and Jordan and Williamson et al. take their
PMLR dates. The IBP review keeps a month placeholder (April 2011, from the
PDF's "Published 4/11"), JMLR giving no day.

**Named but not registered.**

- "temporal stochastic blockmodels", "temporal relational event model" and
  "conventional agent-based organizational models" (T23; T18–T20 for the
  last) name families, not works. For the last, the record already holds
  March's model of organizational learning ([LIT-729](../../record/literature.d/LIT-729.md)), which the replies
  did not use.
- Two originals the replies did not cite: Griffiths and Ghahramani's NIPS
  2005 paper introducing the IBP, and Blei, Griffiths, Jordan and
  Tenenbaum's NIPS 2003 paper introducing hLDA. The registered JMLR review
  and JACM paper restate them.
- T24's "developmental interpretability research has tracked
  attention-head specialization" is taken as the refined-LLC paper, not a
  further work.

an identification.

**What the readings corrected.**

- In the replies. The IBP review gives αH_N for the infinite limit only,
  so T23's quotation of it for sixteen features has no support there
  ([THEORY-tmppc921](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmppc921.md)), as [CASE-tmplcm41](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmplcm41.md) had found. The correlated
  latent-feature paper supports features that reinforce or depend on one
  another, not ones that "inhibit" each other, which T22 cited it for
  ([THEORY-tmpwm0kx](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpwm0kx.md)). The fine-tuning identification held on reading.
- In the works. The dependent IBP's marginals are an IBP for the whole
  matrix only in its collapsed form ([THEORY-tmpumk4q](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpumk4q.md)). The blockmodel's
  §5 says its block matrix and memberships do not trade off; its
  likelihood has a continuum of trades, and its printed sparsity estimate
  is the fraction of absent edges whatever the fit ([THEORY-tmplo0xb](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmplo0xb.md)). The
  author–topic model is unidentified beyond the permutations it names
  ([THEORY-tmp7wy54](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp7wy54.md)). hLDA's level conditional uses the stick's parameters
  reversed, and one of its settings is out of range ([NOTE-tmpcuo7o](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpcuo7o.md)).
  The induction-heads paper gives two different token windows for its
  phase change, and its small models' window overlaps learning-rate
  warm-up ([NOTE-tmpabelf](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpabelf.md)). The fine-tuning paper's node-against-edge
  contrast is produced by a fixed edge budget alone in the record's null
  simulation, and its abstract's 2.46% gain could not be reproduced from
  its tables ([THEORY-tmpoirdq](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpoirdq.md)). The invariant-dynamics paper is
  inconsistent about the size of its aiding directions' effect
  ([NOTE-tmpcuw4h](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpcuw4h.md)).
- In these entries. The dependent IBP's "Why this is here" was narrowed to
  the collapsed form; the induction-heads date was moved (above); and the
  benchmark's "four tasks" and "five models" were corrected in its summary.
