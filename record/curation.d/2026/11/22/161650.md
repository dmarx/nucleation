## The verdict, in short

(a) Confirmed: for an existential, Kleene's realizer is ⟨m, k⟩, the witness
m kept with a realizer of the instance (van Oosten p. 5). (b) The witness
half of the pair has an exact analogue; the certificate half has none in
the realizer: realizability carries "information" rather than proofs
(p. 4), and a true atomic sentence's realizer is 0. Its nearest kin are
glued realizability with P the provable sentences and the
proof-to-realizer translation behind soundness. The descriptor and
imposition distinction has no analogue: realizability is a static relation
between realizers and sentences, with no notion of a predicate added that
an object passes or must be moved to pass. Further: realizability is a
classical satisfaction-like relation, the truth definition in the
effective topos (p. 15), so it sits between [CLAIM-949](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-949.md)'s
model-theoretic and proof-relevant readings; the typed, proof-relevant
form is Kreisel's modified realizability (1959).
