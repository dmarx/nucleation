# LIT-tmpn09mk: Binding and Consciousness

Revonsuo, A. & Newman, J. (1999). Binding and consciousness.
*Consciousness and Cognition*, 8(2), 123–127.

Not read. A copy is located on the Santa Fe Institute wiki and could not
be fetched on 2026-10-29 (see the status note).

## Why this is here

The triage of 2026-10-29 put it in the File tier for [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) and the
open questions of [NOTE-1406](../notes.d/NOTE-1406.md). A27 of the owner's exchange credited
"Revonsuo's 1999 treatment" with a distinction between functional and
phenomenal binding and gave a five-part list of bindings. [NOTE-1406](../notes.d/NOTE-1406.md) read
Revonsuo's own paper in the same issue, [LIT-1208](LIT-1208.md), and found neither: his
terms are stimulus-related and consciousness-related binding, and his
five kinds are all phenomenal. Whether this introduction, by Revonsuo and
Newman, uses the words "functional" and "phenomenal", or gives A27's
list, would settle the attribution [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) quotes. Until it is read,
the record should not credit A27's scheme to either 1999 paper.
