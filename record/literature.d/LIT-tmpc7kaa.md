summary: >-
  Larremore, Clauset and Jacobs (2014), arXiv 1403.2933. They formulate a
  degree-corrected bipartite stochastic block model, in which communities
  are pure-type groups with the same expected connection pattern to the
  other side's groups. It finds better partitions faster than the
  unconstrained SBM and recovers planted structure that one-mode
  projections hide. In their actor–movie example (IMDb, 1995–2000), five
  of six movie blocks were language groups and one was a genre (Adult),
  and the authors read the blocks as traces of "multiple mechanisms
  responsible for the existence of edges". Here because it is the standard
  method behind the owner's U10 genres example, [CASE-tmpsjjgx](../cases.d/CASE-tmpsjjgx.md): it supplies
