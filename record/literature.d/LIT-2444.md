# LIT-2444: Constitutivism's plight: inescapability, normativity, and relativism

Olof Leffler (2024). "Constitutivism's plight: inescapability, normativity, and relativism." *The Philosophical Quarterly*. DOI-10.1093/pq/pqae123.

## Key takeaways

- The question is why norms constitutive of agency have force, not which
  norms apply (p. 2, footnote 2).
- Of four inescapability proposals only a reconstructed plight
  inescapability passes the four features, and it entails relativism:
  "agents with different constitutions have different norms applying to
  them as forceful" (p. 20). Constitution independence is out of its
  reach (p. 21).
- Exploratory: the author defends none of the views as true (p. 3).
