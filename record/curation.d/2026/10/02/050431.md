The owner asked how the record covered morality-as-cooperation (MAC). It
held the theory's 60-society test ([LIT-047](../../record/literature.d/LIT-047.md), `Proposed`) read with its
commentaries, Fitouchi, André & Baumard's reply to the purity objection
([LIT-101](../../record/literature.d/LIT-101.md)) unconnected to it, and Tomasello on obligation ([LIT-125](../../record/literature.d/LIT-125.md)). It held
none of the programme's other papers, none of its rivals and none of the
evolutionary sources its lineage names. `moral-psychology` was added first
([ADR-018](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-018.md)); three agents then filed 21 works, 17 read in full, and
`game-theory` ([ADR-019](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-019.md)) was added when two of them independently proposed
it.

[LIT-047](../../record/literature.d/LIT-047.md) stays `Proposed`. None of the five runs the test [NOTE-034](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-034.md) asks for,
and Curry 2016 shows the test is the theory's own: it predicts that
behaviour unrelated to cooperation will be "morally neutral". The
questionnaire has no non-cooperative items; the 256-society study cannot
see valence and found admired theft in four more societies. The Turkish
validation is independent of the authors and replicates the factor
structure, with a single higher-order factor fitting too.

MAC's claim to be broader than Moral Foundations Theory ([NOTE-034](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-034.md), C7)
does not survive the MFT texts: MFT grounds Care in kin selection and
Fairness in reciprocity and names ownership; only bravery is missing, and
MAC in turn leaves out Sanctity. The narrower charge stands: no MFQ item
asks about returning a favour. Purity is where the theories really part,
and there are now three cooperative accounts of it on record ([LIT-101](../../record/literature.d/LIT-101.md),
Rai & Fiske's Unity, Baumard et al.'s Response), none worked out inside
MAC's seven domains.

else: Nowak's rules are all donation games. MAC and [NOTE-034](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-034.md) credit

Hawk–Dove game; [NOTE-034](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-034.md) v3 says so, and the display and deference domains
are left without a source.
