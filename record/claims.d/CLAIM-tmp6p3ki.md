# CLAIM-tmp6p3ki: Whichever way a whole and its part depend on each other, the same magnitude of dependence can run either way, and what sorts it is who controls the terms on which it is met, exit, renegotiation and the conditions of supply, while the magnitude and the integration that produced it set only how much rides on it: with the part able to withdraw or renegotiate, the whole's need of it becomes the part's hold on its terms; with the terms at an agent's discretion, the same need gives the part none, and the part's own dependence becomes vulnerability to that agent, on whom, under a principle grounding obligations in such vulnerability, the strengthened responsibility falls

## Which sense of "whole"

[TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) sense 1 by default: a relational arrangement, which need not be
an institution or an agent. The claim needs two things the sense does not
supply. Something must control the terms, an agent or a role, since
domination and discretion are capacities of something that can choose
([TERM-tmpc9uv8](../terms.d/TERM-tmpc9uv8.md)). And the obligation clause needs a part with standing
([TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md)). Where no agent controls the terms, as for an organism and its
cells, the claim says nothing about who bears an obligation, and
[QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) keeps that open.

## The claim

The record holds the whole-part dependence in two directions, in entries
filed apart.

- **The whole depends on the part.** [QUESTION-461](../questions.d/QUESTION-461.md) asks when an
  organization's dependence on participants it subordinates becomes their
  effective authority. A's second candidate answer there: the organization
  "might preserve exclusion because its governing coalition retains
  control over alternatives and institutional rules."
- **The part depends on the whole.** [CLAIM-671](CLAIM-671.md), as the owner's review
  restated it, says a transformation that increases a constituent's
  vulnerability "to the discretionary choices of a responsible agent" can
  strengthen that agent's responsibilities, under an independently
  justified principle. The review: the mechanism is "increased
  vulnerability to another's discretionary power, rather than integration
  itself."

In both, what decides is the same variable: control over the terms on
which the dependence is met. That means whether the dependent-upon party
can withdraw, whether either side can renegotiate, and who sets the
conditions of supply. The magnitude of the dependence is not the decider
in either direction, and neither is the depth of integration that produced
it.

- **Whole on part.** Where the part can withdraw or renegotiate, the
  whole's need of it is the part's hold on its terms. Where an agent sets
  the terms and the part cannot leave, the same need gives the part none.
- **Part on whole.** Where an agent sets the terms, the part's dependence
  is vulnerability to that agent's discretion. That is [CLAIM-671](CLAIM-671.md)'s
  antecedent, and under its principle the strengthened responsibility
  falls on that agent. Where the terms are bargained, the same dependence
  is not exposure to anyone's discretion, and [CLAIM-671](CLAIM-671.md)'s antecedent is not
  met.

The general form is [CLAIM-428](CLAIM-428.md)'s: one dependence can be organized "as
mutual opportunity, as competitive vulnerability or as coercive leverage",
and "payoff coupling alone does not settle which". A6 §2 of the exchange
said the same about dependence and domination: "What differs is not
necessarily the magnitude of dependency. It is the organization of
control over that dependency." That formulation was mapped to [CLAIM-428](CLAIM-428.md) in
[#488](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/488) and not filed.

## What it adds to [CLAIM-428](CLAIM-428.md)

- **Both directions of a whole-part relation.** [CLAIM-428](CLAIM-428.md) is about
  parties of one level, states trading, whose coupling is organized by the
  interests their institutions recognize. It does not say that the same
  variable sorts a whole's dependence on its part and the part's on the
  whole, or that the two questions the record asks of them
  ([QUESTION-461](../questions.d/QUESTION-461.md) and [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)) share it.
- **The bearer, not the ground.** The owner's review of [#488](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/488) asked that
  the ground of an obligation be kept apart from its bearer. This claim
  says nothing about the ground: whether vulnerability grounds special
  obligations is Goodin's principle, unread ([LIT-2531](../literature.d/LIT-2531.md)). It says where,
  given the principle, the strengthened responsibility falls: on the agent
  who controls the terms. That is why it is filed as a thesis with a
  conditional clause, and not as a supposition like [CLAIM-671](CLAIM-671.md).
- **Integration kept apart.** The review: "deeper integration need not
  monotonically increase vulnerability". [CLAIM-603](CLAIM-603.md)'s route, a capability
  given up because another supplies it, is how a dependence comes about.
  Held fixed, as in [CASE-tmpzh595](../cases.d/CASE-tmpzh595.md), it leaves open which way the dependence
  runs. Integration and magnitude scale what is at stake; control sorts
  it.

## Admission

A discriminating prediction ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s third route). The components leave
open whether the magnitude of dependence or control over its terms is
what moves a dependence between hold and vulnerability. The claim
predicts that, across pairs equal in dependence and integration and
different in control, both outcomes flip together: the whole's need
becomes the part's hold in one and not the other, and the part's
dependence is exposure to discretion in the other and not the one. Across
pairs equal in control and different in magnitude, neither flips; only
the size of the stakes changes. [CASE-tmpzh595](../cases.d/CASE-tmpzh595.md) builds the first kind of
pair in a toy model. What would go against it is (a) in `defeated_if`.

## What it does not say

- **That magnitude is idle.** It sets how much rides on the relation. In
  [CASE-tmpzh595](../cases.d/CASE-tmpzh595.md)'s bargained workshop, P's share rises by half a unit for
  each unit of the workshop's need.
- **That control is the only ground of obligation.** Membership may
  ground obligations of its own ([CASE-256](../cases.d/CASE-256.md)'s first result). The claim
  holds membership fixed and does not deny it.
- **That the part with a hold is not exploited, or that the part without
  one is.** Exploitation needs a declared standard ([TERM-tmp169d8](../terms.d/TERM-tmp169d8.md),
  [CLAIM-363](CLAIM-363.md)). Predatory inclusion is a hold given up at entry, on terms
  that leave an encumbrance ([CLAIM-669](CLAIM-669.md)).
- **That this is how power works in general.** Emerson's
  "Power-Dependence Relations" (American Sociological Review 27(1), 1962,
  doi:10.2307/2089716), not held or read here, is the best-known account
  that ties power to dependence. Whether it counts the availability of
  alternatives inside dependence, and so absorbs this claim's control
  variable into its measure, is to be checked on reading. That is (b) in
  `defeated_if`.
- **That the colonial theatre shows it.** [CASE-257](../cases.d/CASE-257.md) is near the
  discretion side for its enslaved contributors. It differs from any
  bargained case in more than control, so it cannot be the contrast.
