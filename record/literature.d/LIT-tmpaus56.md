# LIT-tmpaus56: Polydnaviruses as Symbionts and Gene Delivery Systems

Michael R. Strand and Gaelen R. Burke (2012). "Polydnaviruses as Symbionts
and Gene Delivery Systems" *PLoS Pathogens 8(7): e1002757*.
DOI-10.1371/journal.ppat.1002757.

## Key takeaways

A virus of once separate lineage, now part of the wasp's genome, is the
wasp's tool for altering a third organism.
