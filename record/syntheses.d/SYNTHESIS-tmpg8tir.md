# SYNTHESIS-tmpg8tir: Conferral among an organization's processes is any effect on another's share of future influence, and a grant is conferral through a maintained relation; how grants are counted and scored decides whether authority tracks reliability or entrenches: an accounting rule can keep an operator's authority independent of its own grants, trust can be the regularity of grants earned by being right, and when authority is scored against the interpretation it helped make dominant the same loop can amplify an initial advantage without fidelity, which in the linear model only the two-way coupling starts and independent scoring removes; capture runs through the evidence an evaluation receives or the criteria it weighs by, with different remedies, and the standard that would tell capture from merited trust is not supplied

*A part account of the distributed-agency line ("what an organized whole
is, and how its operative direction relates to its members'"): the part
on conferral, grants and endorsement among an organization's processes,
and on the coupling of interpretation to authority. A mechanical map of
the line's stored relations and mentions divided its in-force entries
into seven parts; this is one of them, and the line's account is built
over it and its siblings. Several members are also in other lines, and
other lines' accounts already integrate some of them: SYNTHESIS-036
(CLAIM-336, CLAIM-341, CLAIM-410, QUESTION-088), SYNTHESIS-037
(CLAIM-453, QUESTION-090), SYNTHESIS-038 (CLAIM-511), SYNTHESIS-039
(CLAIM-344, CLAIM-359, CLAIM-443, QUESTION-313, QUESTION-314) and
SYNTHESIS-044 (CLAIM-500, QUESTION-372). This account integrates them
again because their `line` includes distributed-agency. It integrates
two Superseded entries (TERM-053, CLAIM-278), one Rejected argument
(ARG-042) and four Answered questions, because the part's links run
through them. It adds nothing; its standing is its components'.*

## The account

- **Conferral and grant.** TERM-103 (Active) is TERM-053's definition
  renamed by the owner's decision: "any effect one process has on
  another's share of future influence, of which an explicit vote is one
  case; negative conferral withdraws influence". It keeps "three
  quantities" apart: "the authority a process holds, what it confers on
  others, and how far its preferred policies are realized (CLAIM-285)".
  TERM-101 (Active, A's narrowing adopted by the owner) refines it: a
  grant is conferral that "runs through a maintained relation of
  granting, delegation, eligibility or support", and "Suppressing j's
  rivals confers influence on j and grants j nothing." TERM-053
  (Superseded by both) keeps the history: the owner's "vote" at U2, the
  note of 2026-10-18 that "being reinforced ≠ endorsing", and the
  owner's reading of tolerances as "endorsement criteria", which
  CLAIM-552 holds. TERM-066 (Proposed) names a regime of that channel:
  "Conferral is the channel: any effect of one process on another's
  share of influence. Capture is a regime of that channel."
- **Counting grants.** CLAIM-282 (A's analysis of the owner's design,
  granted, demonstrated, Active): under damped recursive delegation
  "reciprocal and cyclic grants recycle authority", so a ban on
  self-grants does not stop self-amplification; CASE-077 (A's
  counterexample, stipulated, Active) is the reciprocal pair. CLAIM-502
  (the status review's construction, demonstrated, Active) objects to
  CLAIM-282's old title: leave-one-out delegation keeps "an operator's
  authority exactly independent of its own grants" while grants carry
  beyond one hop. CLAIM-375 (split from CLAIM-285 at the owner's
  direction, demonstrated, Active) is the existence result under
  one-hop allocation: delegating to a nearer ally "raises how far its
  preferred policy is realized while its own authority stays fixed".
  CLAIM-285 (the owner's design at U2, made precise by A, Proposed)
  holds that the incentive does work in a population and need not be
  confined to one hop; its body: "Own influence ≠ Capacity to realize
  one's preferred policies." CLAIM-502 adds that "CLAIM-375's guarantee
  does not carry over" beyond one hop. CLAIM-272 (A's reading of the
  owner's r/place case, empirical, Active) qualifies CLAIM-285: "the
  budget constrains moves, and conferral moves authority", grounded in
  CASE-079 (the owner's case, documented, Active).
- **Grants by role, and regimes by frame.** CLAIM-281 (A's, on the
  owner's design, Proposed): granting by role lets a process "back j as
  an error detector without backing j's authority over the final
  choice"; CASE-080 (A's thought experiment, stipulated, Active) is its
  worked example. Its clause on factions "rests on A2's argument alone".
  CLAIM-279 (the owner's, granted by A, Active): denoiser, critic and
  re-noiser are "regimes an operator falls into, not kinds of operator",
  grounded in CASE-079.
- **Trust as the regularity of grants, and entrenchment.** CLAIM-277
  (the owner's objection at U5, granted by A, interpretive, Active):
  "trust can be the temporal regularity of grants that policies earn by
  being right often, and the same loop entrenches whatever gained
  influence early". It rests on CLAIM-282 and grounds on CASE-182 (the
  status review's construction, Active): scored every round the more
  accurate operator holds the lead, scored only when it acts "whichever
  gained influence early keeps it". QUESTION-295 (Answered) is answered
  by it. CLAIM-277 gives no criterion "for when entrenched authority is
  capture and not merited trust". CLAIM-443 (the owner's suggestion, A's
  working, first line will-organization, Active): "CLAIM-277's durable
  authority from repeated conferral becomes this prior", with the limit
  that "persistence is not amortization". CLAIM-344 (the owner's frame,
  A's definition, first line will-organization, Active) rests on
  CLAIM-277 for "the rigidity risk inside the mechanism". CLAIM-406 (the
  owner's reading at U24, granted by A, Active) rests on CLAIM-277 and
  CLAIM-330: structural pre-emptive testimonial injustice has the loop's
  form, and "the loop alone cannot tell perpetuated privilege from
  merited expertise"; QUESTION-322 (Open) asks whether Fricker's account
  of the wrong is right.
- **The coupled loop.** CLAIM-297 (A's narrow claim from the owner's
  U22, Active on the owner's assent) rests on CLAIM-277 and CLAIM-275:
  when authority is updated "by how well it performs on the
  interpretation it helped make dominant", coupled meaning and influence
  "can amplify an initial interpretive advantage without any gain in
  fidelity to independent evidence". Its body: "This claim names a
  mechanism by which CLAIM-277's entrenchment would run, and the sense
  in which it fails CLAIM-276's fidelity." CLAIM-275 (A's sentence and
  the owner's assent, Active): "An intervention can come before the
  interpretation it makes available". CLAIM-276 (A's, Proposed):
  coherence, fidelity and plurality are separate, and its last clause is
  held back until restated as "not by itself evidence of its truth".
- **The linear model.** CASE-092 (A's proposed study, not built, Active)
  is how QUESTION-100 "would be answered and CLAIM-297 tested". CASE-099
  (A's construction, checked, Active) linearizes it, and CLAIM-330
  (demonstrated, Active) answers QUESTION-241 (Answered): the symmetric
  state is unstable "exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ)", "only the loop
  can do it", and "Scoring operators independently of the dominant
  interpretation (Regime B, γ ≈ 0) removes the coupling-driven
  instability". CLAIM-330's body: "Capture, if it starts at symmetry,
  needs both arrows of CLAIM-297's circuit". CASE-106 (the record's
  construction for QUESTION-121, Active) and CLAIM-340 (demonstrated,
  Active, answering QUESTION-239, Answered): commitment before evidence
  "is not by itself Regime B"; the coupling has the sign of κ′ − κ̄, and
  "So CLAIM-342's anchor ... is in this model the condition κ′ ≤ κ̄
  together with commitment." CASE-124 (the owner's construction with the
  record's additions, Active) and CLAIM-400 (demonstrated, Active,
  answering QUESTION-253, Answered) add a maintained legibility state:
  at the threshold it reduces to stronger two-variable couplings, and
  away from it "a lean in the regime can outlast the advantages it was
  built from". CASE-156 (the exchange's construction at the owner's
  prompt, Active) gives the legibility arrow a mechanism, selective
  admission, and finds that "lock-in needs χ above about 9.8".
- **The replicator reading.** CASE-155 (filed at the owner's direction,
  stipulated, Active): evidence-tracking authority is Bayes' rule, and
  authority-dependent scoring has "an unstable threshold π* = (1 −
  h/c)/2, above which a process the evidence disfavours captures the
  organization"; "protective lock-in (CLAIM-446) needs no bias in any
  process, only scoring that depends on who governs". CLAIM-444 (the
  owner's direction, interpretive, Active) rests on CLAIM-400: the
  replicator is the special case with the interpretation an
  authority-weighted mixture, no maintained regime, full information and
  one standard, so "the replicator model is the null model for the
  record's governance dynamics". CASE-157 (the owner's 2014 model,
  re-run and extended, Active): "An endogenous metric suffices for
  lock-in under full feedback", "Outcome-conditional observation
  suffices too", and age-targeted renewal "defeats both short of full
  endogeneity". CASE-159 (stipulated, Active) and CLAIM-452
  (demonstrated, first line formal-apparatus, Active): the response
  geometry never exceeds the authority geometry, and the shares are
  identifiable exactly when the stacked profiles are affinely
  independent. CASE-160 (A's derivation, checked, Active) and CLAIM-453
  (the owner's hypothesis, demonstrated, Active): a population makes an
  exact drift-diffusion choice under stated conditions, and "two
  interpretations are necessary for a one-dimensional reduction, not
  sufficient". CLAIM-408 (A's model on the owner's question,
  demonstrated, Active): a symmetric constraint that weakens past κ = α
  makes unequal allocations stable "without determining which party
  gains the larger share".
- **Capture: routes and remedies.** CLAIM-404 (the owner's cases at U20
  and U21, A's constructions, Active) rests on CLAIM-330, CLAIM-340,
  CLAIM-342, CLAIM-348 and CLAIM-410: an organization "can be governed
  from outside without any of its decisions being made for it, through
  the evidence its own procedures receive or through the criteria by
  which they weigh evidence", and the loop can close between two
  institutions. Its body: "CLAIM-342 holds the informational half and
  CLAIM-348 the evaluative half". It uses "capture" in the institutional
  sense TERM-066 declines. CASE-123 (A's thought experiment, Active)
  illustrates it, CLAIM-330 and CLAIM-340. CLAIM-466 (stated in
  CASE-162, demonstrated, Active): "correcting for how evidence was
  selected is not correcting how it is scored"; its body: "CLAIM-404
  holds that the informational and evaluative routes are distinct; this
  says their remedies are too." CASE-162 (an executed study filed at the
  owner's direction, Active) runs the circuit without retrieval:
  "authority scored against what it made dominant can hold the worse
  perspective in place, which is CLAIM-297's amplification without
  fidelity". CLAIM-410 (A's answer to the owner's U18, Active): removing
  a constraint "changes a profile of who holds governing positions", and
  rests on CLAIM-282 because "forbidding one route of
  self-amplification does not stop authority circulating through
  others". CLAIM-359 (A's answer to the owner's U41, Active) puts
  robustness in "the processes that allocate, audit and revise
  authority", rests on CLAIM-282, and names audits independent of the
  audited (CLAIM-342) and a role that can criticise without controlling
  (CLAIM-281) as safeguards that serve both security and
  self-governance; CASE-108 (A's proposed study, not run, Active) is its
  case. CASE-153 (proposed at the owner's request, Active) is "The
  smallest model of the protective lock-in in CLAIM-446", built on
  CASE-092 and CASE-099. CLAIM-500 (split by a status review, Proposed)
  says governance-induced frustration is not idle, and "The mechanism
  the record already holds is a loop of the kind CLAIM-297 describes";
  QUESTION-124 (Open) asks when conferral turns a transient conflict
  into persistent frustration.
- **The judgement–observation loop and its anchor.** CLAIM-341 (the
  owner's, Active) rests on CLAIM-297 and CLAIM-275: "The record's model
  already has this loop", and action on the world "is not part of the
  loop". CLAIM-337 (A's counter, Proposed) and CLAIM-339 (A's counter,
  narrowed, Active) object to it. CLAIM-342 (A's synthesis filed at the
  owner's request, Active) rests on CLAIM-330 and CLAIM-141 and objects
  to CLAIM-339: what keeps the loop from confirming itself is "evidence
  whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the interpretation it
  tests; action is one source of such evidence, not a necessary one".
  CLAIM-338 (the owner's mapping, A's working, Proposed) rests on
  CLAIM-297 and CLAIM-336: active inference's selective sampling is the
  β arrow, and "the free-energy agent is a coupled loop run without
  independent scoring". CASE-107 (the owner's case, documented, Active)
  "cannot show judgement running with no action at all". QUESTION-121
  (Open) asks what plays independent scoring within one mind. CLAIM-336
  (the owner's thesis, A's mapping, Proposed, with an Admission section)
  rests on CLAIM-297, CLAIM-286 and CLAIM-279: the pragmatic-transport
  and will-organization lines "meet at CLAIM-341's loop". QUESTION-285
  (Open) asks what keeps an apparatus that revises its discriminations
  answerable, and names CLAIM-341 and CLAIM-342.
- **Evaluation against a router.** QUESTION-271 (Open, filed at the
  owner's direction) lists four ways the evaluation that allocates
  authority can fail. CASE-144 (A's proposed study, Active), its variant
  CASE-158 (A's run, reported and later reproduced, Active), and
  CLAIM-467 (stated in CASE-158, Active): "Under full-information,
  independent audits, a learned allocation of influence among
  specialists is no better than a contextual router trained on the same
  audits"; it "gives CLAIM-278's objection a definite form for this
  model class" and undercuts ARG-036 (Proposed), which concludes
  CLAIM-286 (A7's thesis, Proposed) from CASE-161 and CASE-163 and fails
  its critical question: "The organizational vocabulary adds no
  constraint that the conventional model lacks." CLAIM-278 (Superseded
  by ARG-036) is that objection as first filed. QUESTION-088 (Open) is
  the owner's question whether the framing predicts anything, and
  QUESTION-313 and QUESTION-314 (Open, first line will-organization)
  refine it for habit and implementation intentions.
- **Maintained correction and its value.** CASE-161 (executed, Active),
  CASE-163 (executed, Proposed), CASE-186 (A's proposed study, Active)
  and CASE-190 (A's construction, checked, Active) show what keeps a
  corrective capacity up and what breaks it. ARG-042 (Rejected) finds
  they do not show that "self-maintaining organizations ought to
  preserve their corrigibility" (CLAIM-511, disclaimed, Proposed):
  every measure in them is of capacity, and it quotes A24, "We shouldn't
  quietly convert a proof of capacity into a proof of value." CLAIM-520
  (from A24, demonstrated, Active) undercuts
  ARG-042: the value of keeping a corrective capacity "is conditional on
  objectives, uncertainty and cost".
- **Interaction before interpretation.** CASE-143 (the owner's case,
  documented, Active) illustrates CLAIM-275: "The help came before the
  liking." CLAIM-275's note of 2026-10-29 calls it an instance. CLAIM-430 (A's coinage at the owner's request, Proposed)
  rests on CLAIM-275: "It is CLAIM-275's intervention before
  interpretation applied between people"; ARG-044 (Proposed) supports
  its parts 1 and 3 and finds part 2's order reversed in the MSF
  meeting. CASE-147 (documented, Active) is "The nearest field contrast
  to CLAIM-430's defeater". CLAIM-429 (A's distinction, Active) rests on
  CLAIM-297 and CLAIM-330: an intervention can change the loop's state
  or its coupling, and CASE-146 (A's thought experiment, Active) sets
  the two side by side. CLAIM-428 (A's, on the owner's U6, Proposed) is
  "the commercial-peace debate read through CLAIM-429", grounded in
  CASE-150 (documented, Active), which shows the material level only.
- **The open questions.** QUESTION-100 (Open) asks, beyond the
  linearization, when the coupling produces "stable expertise" and when
  "self-reinforcing interpretive capture"; it refines QUESTION-087
  (Open), whether a self-reproducing authority structure can be
  pathological by the organization's own standard. QUESTION-117 (Open)
  refines QUESTION-100 against reflected appraisal, adaptive networks
  and persuasion bias. QUESTION-089 (Open) asks when dissent improves an
  organization; QUESTION-090 (Open) when a population develops
  self-maintaining governance without suppressing its plurality, with
  QUESTION-259 refining it, QUESTION-262 and QUESTION-264 refining
  QUESTION-259, and QUESTION-372 (first line will-organization) refining
  QUESTION-090. QUESTION-091 asks whether influence regulates cascades,
  QUESTION-094 what keeps judgments consistent when jurisdiction is
  allocated by grant, and QUESTION-273 whether the owner's three
  mechanisms of influence differ at matched budgets.
