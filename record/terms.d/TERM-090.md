# TERM-090: effective boundary constraint, of a composite: the relation its interconnected parts impose on the variables an outside system can reach, once the interior is eliminated; instantaneous or history-dependent, and possibly of a kind the parts' relations could not state

## Definition

Take interconnected systems with jointly admissible behaviour 𝓑_G, and a set of
variables y an outside system can reach. The **effective boundary constraint**
is the projection of 𝓑_G onto y, the external behaviour in Willems's sense.
It is the relation the composite imposes on whatever interacts with it only
through y ([CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md)). It comes in three forms:

- **instantaneous**, when the interior relaxes fast relative to the boundary.
  The two-spring chain gives the coupling k₁k₂/(k₁ + k₂) between its ends;
- **history-dependent**, when it does not. Eliminating the interior then
  leaves a memory kernel, a relation over boundary trajectories;
- **of a new kind**, when the reduced relation cannot be stated in the parts'
  relational vocabulary ([TERM-091](TERM-091.md)). Hansen and Ghrist's star reduces
  to v₁ = v₂ + v₃, which no pairwise sheaf on the boundary expresses
  ([THEORY-1090](../theory.d/THEORY-1090.md)).

## The distinctions it draws

- **A constraint with or without memory.** A history-dependent relation is a
  constraint on admissible trajectories. Separation of time scales decides
  only whether it is approximately memoryless ([CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md), version 2).
- **Effective, not fundamental.** It is realized by the interior and changes
  when the interior does. It is not a new law (A23 §XII).
- **Interface, not individual.** A composite with a well-defined boundary
  constraint need not be an individual in any of [TERM-088](TERM-088.md)'s senses.

## Why it is proposed

Four entries filed this evening use the notion, under several names: "effective
boundary relation", "boundary law", "external behaviour". It is the clearest
place where the record's general frame, that constraints are realized by
organization ([CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md)), has a demonstrated case. What would settle it:
adoption if the claims cite it; rejection if Willems's "external behaviour"
serves and the record files that instead.
