# TERM-047: jurisdiction of a will-organization

## Definition

Given a will-organization W ([TERM-023](TERM-023.md)), its **jurisdiction** is the set of
action-generating regimes ([TERM-019](TERM-019.md)) whose practical efficacy W governs.
These are the regimes for which W maintains the conditions under which they
acquire, retain, lose or alter efficacy. A regime is inside the
jurisdiction if changing W changes when that regime acts. It is outside if
W's changes leave it untouched.

On [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md) version 2, a *willed* agent ([TERM-048](TERM-048.md)) at a level
is the jurisdiction of a will-organization. Its boundary is where governance
stops. Agents in general are identified otherwise ([TERM-044](TERM-044.md)).

## What it is not

- **Not [TERM-017](TERM-017.md)'s jurisdiction.** That jurisdiction is a relation *inside* a
  will-organization: which constraints count as applicable to a case. This
  term names the *extent* of the whole organization's governance over
  regimes. The words overlap because the second is the outer limit of the
  first: no constraint can be applicable to a regime outside the
  organization's reach.
- **Not influence.** A regime that changes what the person does without
  being governed by W is an input to the jurisdiction, not part of it. The
  microbiome is the case ([CASE-047](../cases.d/CASE-047.md)): it can drive hunger, but nothing
  in the person's will-organization governs when it does.
- **Not the operative will.** That is whichever regime is acting now, and it
  changes with the drive profile ([TERM-051](TERM-051.md)).
