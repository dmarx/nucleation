# THEORY-tmp1hdru: About 800 Myr ago the breakup of the Eulalia parent body beside the Jupiter 3:1 resonance sent an impact shower to the terrestrial planets and the Moon that made the cluster of large lunar craters dated near 800 Ma

## Source

Bottke, Vokrouhlický, Dykhuis & Zellner (2026), [LIT-009](../literature.d/LIT-009.md), read in full in
[NOTE-002](../notes.d/NOTE-002.md) from the arXiv preprint 2606.05036v1. What the model shows is
[THEORY-499](THEORY-499.md), which this entry extends.

## The thesis

The paper's title asserts the event: "An 800 Myr-old Impact Shower on the
Terrestrial Planets from the Breakup of the Eulalia Parent Body". Its
conclusion is that the breakup "can plausibly account for" the ~800 Ma
lunar crater cluster, and it links the same shower, as its own
speculation, to events on Earth, Mars and Venus.

## Why it is not held

The model is an existence argument: only the most favourable of five
solutions is carried through, one factor is tuned by eye, and on average
its largest lunar impactor is too small for Copernicus. The seven craters
are dated by N(1) scaled to Copernicus, the only one with a sample age, so
the cluster's absolute date is Copernicus's. Whether the shower happened
needs the other craters dated, or the projectile identified, as the
condition says.
