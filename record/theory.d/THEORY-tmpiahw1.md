# THEORY-tmpiahw1: Schultz and Spivak model behavior types as sheaves on a topos of time intervals, which include families whose short-window compliance does not compose and families that are the solution set of no differential equation, and give a constructive temporal logic, sound but undecidable, in which a composed system's strongest contract is its components' contracts conjoined with the latent variables existentially quantified

## Source

Schultz and Spivak (2017), LIT-2235, skimmed in NOTE-tmp43jkl.

## What was actually shown

A book-length construction and soundness proof, read here only in its
overview and its section on systems and contracts.

## What this does not say

Which behavior types are realizable by physical systems, or at what cost.
That the logic decides anything: it is undecidable.
