# CLAIM-276: A shared interpretation's coherence, its fidelity to evidence and the plurality of live alternatives are separate quantities, and a population can raise the first while lowering the other two, so a collective interpretation's stability is no evidence of its truth

## The claim

A1 §6: "There is an important distinction between meaning as a final decoded
signal and meaning as an ongoing organizational process." On the second
reading, meanings are "**metastable patterns of mutually reinforcing
constraints**", and an interpretation becomes collectively meaningful when it
is reconstructed by many, survives transformation, steers attention and
holds under perturbation. "But high stability alone is insufficient:
delusions, dogmas, and self-reinforcing errors can also become metastable."
So: "We therefore need to distinguish at least three objectives", namely
coherence C(M), evidential fidelity F(M, Y) and plurality of viable
interpretations P(M). "A population can increase coherence while decreasing
fidelity and plurality." A1's table added a mechanism. Influence
concentration "can accelerate agreement while making global interpretation
more sensitive to influential agents' errors".

The record's readings bear on it from two sides. In Zollman's model
([THEORY-143](../theory.d/THEORY-143.md)), sparser communication makes a community likelier to settle on
the better option, at the cost of speed. Denser coupling buys coherence and
pays in fidelity. Holman and Bruner ([THEORY-146](../theory.d/THEORY-146.md)) show a community biased
toward a worse treatment with nobody corrupted, which is coherence without
fidelity by selection. The record's cultural attractor ([TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)) is a
stable endpoint of reconstruction, and stability is all it claims.

## What it does not say

- It does not say coherence is bad or plurality good in themselves. A1:
  "*good* collective meaning-making may operate near a balance between
  integration and differentiation, although such an operating region is a
  hypothesis to test rather than a universal law."
- It does not say how to measure fidelity where there is no ground truth.
  On contested meanings, Y may be the very thing in dispute.
- Drift toward the collaborators' framings in this record's own sessions
  ([CLAIM-191](CLAIM-191.md)) is a case of the same kind, in the nucleating-argument line.
