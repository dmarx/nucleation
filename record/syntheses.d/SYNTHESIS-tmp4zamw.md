## The parts

**First Contact and an adaptive interface (U1 to U4).**
- **The owner's design.** U2 proposed a smart touch-scroll interface
  that would use First Contact's co-adaptation. U3 set its constraint:
  inference latency against perceptual latency. Both are in
  CASE-tmptzhse.
- **The answer to U3** is CLAIM-tmpk8ude: learning off the input path,
  with parameters changed only between gestures. Three timescales come
  apart there: response, adaptation and evaluative horizon.
- **The assistant's fill, CLAIM-tmpx7oaj.** A4 said that the information
  between gesture and displacement cannot choose a gain. That holds only
  for a noiseless mapping. Under noise or quantization the score rises
  with the gain. Either way, an interior optimum must come from the
  user's commands changing with the gain.
- **Capacity against realized behaviour.** CLAIM-tmpcfo3s separates
  First Contact's score from empowerment's capacity.
- **A8's amortization.** A8 said the learned parameters were stored work
  in CLAIM-761's sense. ARG-tmpzxz7y rejects that, because scrolling is
  feasible at every gain.

**The dispositional address and separability (U5, U6).**
- **The term.** U5 asked for the record's account of addressing a
  perspective by its dispositions qua test functions. The record held the
  pieces (TERM-118, TERM-067, TERM-069, CLAIM-574) but no entry
  assembling them. A11's assembly is TERM-tmpwc4la.
- **The owner's sloppy self.** U6 carried A11's "the address of a
  perspective is thus relative to the tests we can afford to perform" to
  the separation of perspectives. It then proposed "a 'self' as a literal
  sloppy mess of connected perspectives". That proposal is filed twice,
  as alternatives: colloquially, as CLAIM-tmpy4mba, and in the technical
  sense of sloppiness, as CLAIM-tmptsbhv. The owner chooses.
- **The assistant's two fills.** CLAIM-tmp0dyx8, with ARG-tmpzj6pc from
  CLAIM-574, shows that a distinction cannot disappear as a nested probe
  family is enriched. CLAIM-tmp7qzux shows that the partitions affordable
  probes induce are far fewer than all partitions, and closed under common
  refinement but not under joins.

**The self thesis, in the order A gave it.**
1. **A13 §6, CLAIM-tmp7pe33.** A self is a historically sustained
   organization of coupled dispositions. Its decomposition into
   perspectives is relative to a regime of interventions, horizons and
   budgets. It rests on CLAIM-284 and CLAIM-574. The owner quoted it at U7
   and asked A to "double-click on this". Quoting is not endorsing. Its
   clause that distinctions "may disappear" is corrected by
   CLAIM-tmp0dyx8.
2. **A15, CLAIM-tmptxsin.** The revision locates unity, if there is any,
   in causal processes of coordination, maintenance and transformation.
   It admits several equally adequate factorizations, and lets the self
   acquire new distinctions (CLAIM-532). Its discriminating test is
   LEAD-144's design.
3. **A22, CLAIM-tmp3xo35, at U10 and U11.** Self-like unity does not
   require perspectival singularity. It is stated in TERM-tmpm0s7g's
   three senses of singularity and TERM-tmpxwa7g's pseudo-self, and rests
   on CLAIM-418.
4. **A24, CLAIM-tmp37le7, at U12.** The unity can be ecological
   integration, not convergence. It grounds on CASE-119 and is stated
   with TERM-tmppvlfi. CLAIM-tmp31b3v holds that harmony and singularity
   are independent. It complements CLAIM-tmp3xo35.
5. **A26, CLAIM-tmp9pgdu, at U13 and U14.** A self as a historically
   evolving, measure-valued organization of dispositions. It rests on the
   owner's thesis, CLAIM-tmphxbli, and on two constructions, CLAIM-tmpwb6dg
   and CLAIM-tmprq9q5.
6. **A29, CLAIM-tmp6ksyy, at U15.** The same, ordered into three levels:

- **A's supposition, CLAIM-tmpqr0tn.** A team's organization can be

- **Mechanisms.** CLAIM-tmpmk036 gives three mechanisms by which

**Teams and singularity (U10, U11).**

- **A21's objection**, CLAIM-tmp7580l, says the members are separable as

**Measures and representations (U13 to U15).**

**Regulation (U16, U17).**

- **A's qualification.** CLAIM-tmphjm0q holds that "optimally" needs the

  - QUESTION-tmpn9t5a: recovery of a capability from rarity.

## The account

On the record's entries, the exchange's argument runs as follows. Each
link is attributed to the entry that states it.

1. **A perspective is addressed by its response profile.** It is
   addressed under a declared probe family (TERM-tmpwc4la), so what can be
   told apart is relative to the probes (CLAIM-574, TERM-069).
2. **Enriching the probes refines; it never merges.** Along a nested
   family, distinctions only refine (CLAIM-574). A distinction that
   vanishes was hidden by a change of regime, or ended by a change in
   the organization (CLAIM-tmp0dyx8). The partitions an observer can
   afford are a small part of all partitions (CLAIM-tmp7qzux).
3. **Where unity lies.** A self is proposed to be an organization whose
   decomposition into perspectives is regime-relative (CLAIM-tmp7pe33).
   Its unity, if any, lies in causal coordination and maintenance
   (CLAIM-tmptxsin).
4. **Unity is not singularity.** The unity need not be singularity
   (CLAIM-tmp3xo35, in TERM-tmpm0s7g's senses). It can be ecological
   integration (CLAIM-tmp37le7, TERM-tmppvlfi), and harmony and
   singularity are independent (CLAIM-tmp31b3v).
5. **A measure-valued self.** On the owner's measure-theoretic proposal
   (CLAIM-tmphxbli), the self can be modelled as a measure-valued
   organization (CLAIM-tmp9pgdu). Its perspectives are overlapping
   submeasures (CLAIM-tmprq9q5), and their separability is set by the
   test family's pseudometric (CLAIM-tmpwb6dg). A29's three levels
   restate this (CLAIM-tmp6ksyy).
6. **A team has separable carriers, not necessarily separable
   perspectives.** That is A21's objection to the owner's U10
   (CLAIM-tmp7580l). It makes a team a place where interventions on
   members are possible while its perspectives still overlap
   (CLAIM-tmpl1hgv).
7. **What a governed record can do.** It can synchronize collaborators
   without making them converge (CLAIM-tmp86z76). It may carry part of a
   team's organization (CLAIM-tmpqr0tn, supposed). It can let arbitration
   fall away by externalized governance, which is a different mechanism
   from learned coordination (CLAIM-tmpmk036).
8. **Regulation.** On the owner's regulation thesis (CLAIM-tmpz67ew),
   regulations are conduct constraints meant to induce outcome
   constraints (CLAIM-tmplgnjf). At fixed dynamics they cannot relieve
   frustration among the public demands (CLAIM-tmpk4bou). Possibility and
   realization are separate (CLAIM-tmpx9nrj), and no optimum exists
   before the commitments are declared (CLAIM-tmphjm0q).

## Not stated on the record

Each of these links A asserted. No component states it.

- **"Amortized sensorimotor coordination" as stored work (A8).** The
  analogy fails, as ARG-tmpzxz7y shows: no expansion of what is feasible.
- **"This is exactly the distinction we were reaching for when
  describing a team as a pseudo-self" (A29 §6).** The owner's U10
  asserted the opposite, that the perspectives are necessarily separable.
  The distinction was A21's objection to it. Telling it as "we" blurs
  who held what. This is an attribution finding, not a link.
- **"A polity as an ecological pseudo-self" (A31).** No component
  states that an industrial ecosystem satisfies TERM-tmpxwa7g's
  integration condition. Left as an analogy.
- **"An ecology of regulatory perspectives" (A33 §7).** Its components
  are CLAIM-342 and CLAIM-403. Beyond them it is a label.
- **That the regulation thread and the self thesis are one framework
  (A29's closing, A31's synthesis).** They share vocabulary: compatibility
  complexes, harmony, pseudo-selves. No entry derives one from the other.
- **"Stable governance against lock-in", cited to CLAIM-711 (A33 §6).**
  CLAIM-711 concerns interruptibility, not lock-in or the means to revise
  constraints.

## What it does not say

It does not say that any version of the self thesis is true, that a team
or an industrial ecosystem is a self, or that Luria makes its
collaborators converge or not. Each of these is an open claim, question
or lead above.
