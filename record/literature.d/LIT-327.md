summary: >-
  Still et al. (2012), DOI-10.1103/PhysRevLett.109.120604. For a system
  driven by a stochastic signal through a fixed Markov kernel, the average
  work dissipated as the signal steps x_t → x_{t+1} equals, in units of
  k_BT, the system's instantaneous nonpredictive information, I[s_t;x_t] −
  I[s_t;x_{t+1}] (Eq. 14). Summed over a protocol, this total "nostalgia"
  lower-bounds dissipation and excess work (Eq. 18), and it adds to
  Landauer's bound: −β⟨Q⟩ ≥ I_e + I_mem − I_pred (Eq. 21). All three are
  averages over paths and protocols. None of them is a per-operation or
  per-bit floor on the cost of storing or using predictive information.
