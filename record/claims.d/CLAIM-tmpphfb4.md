# CLAIM-tmpphfb4: Whether an organization registers a process's evaluation can be measured without reading how the experimenter set it, as the response of the organization's record to a randomized perturbation of the evaluation with the other routes into the record held fixed; reading the record passively, for whether it carries the evaluation, misreads a record that carries the evaluation's cause by another route

## The claim

Registration, in [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md)'s ladder, sits between access and uptake: the
organization holds the evaluation in what its processes and reports read
from. [QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md) asks for it to be measured "separately (its
self-model, its record, its reports)", and its note of 2026-10-26 (later)
found that the constructions so far set it instead.

The claim is that registration is measurable as a dependence: the
record's response to the evaluation itself, holding fixed what else could
bring the same information in. Two conditions do the work, and
[CASE-tmp9e5vf](../cases.d/CASE-tmp9e5vf.md) shows each is needed.

- **Hold the other routes fixed.** The record carries the outcome, which
  drives the evaluation. Without covariates for it, the record decodes the
  liking assay in 24% of unregistered agents.
- **Perturb the evaluation, not merely observe it.** A record can carry
  the evaluation's cause, here a physiological state, by a route that does
  not pass through the evaluating process. Read passively, that record
  looked registered in 77% of agents; perturbed, in 2%.

The perturbation is of the evaluation, not of registration or authority,
so the measure reads neither setting. In the case it separated denied
uptake from evaluative isolation, which pursue identically.

## What it does not say

- Not that registration is measurable in a natural organization. The
  measure needs a probe of the evaluating process and a list of the other
  routes into the record; a route left off the list defeats it as the
  decoy defeats the passive reading.
- Not that the endorsement relation is measured. In the case it was told
  apart only weakly, by a separate probe of pursuit (43% of accommodating
  agents at 4.5% false calls).
- Not that registration is graded faithfully: its correlation with the
  generator's gain was 0.56, and weak registration was mostly missed.
