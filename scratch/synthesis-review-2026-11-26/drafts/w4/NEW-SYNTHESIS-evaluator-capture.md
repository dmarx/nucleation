# CODE: Evaluator capture can close between institutions

*A part synthesis within the conferral-and-authority part of the
distributed-agency line (SYNTHESIS-050): the built meaning–influence model
and the coupling of an evaluator to the party it evaluates, from LEAD-090
and LEAD-015. SYNTHESIS-050 integrates it. Written at the synthesis review
of 2026-11-26, when CLAIM-1021 and the results it rests on were in no
synthesis. CLAIM-CODE is the claim the review filed with it, to be given
its code when filed. It adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

When does coupling the construction of meaning to the allocation of
interpretive authority capture an institution's reading, and when the
institution evaluates a party that affects its evidence or resources, what
does the loop between the two add, and what prevents it (QUESTION-100,
beyond the linearization)?

## The argument

**Inside one institution, only the loop breaks symmetry, at a threshold
its rates do not move.** In the coupled model linearized at its symmetric
state, with each field stable alone, the coupling destabilizes symmetry
exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ): only the loop can do it (CLAIM-330).
Built with two hypotheses and two specialists and reduced to its mean
field (CASE-363), the condition is gσ² > 2(1 − φ′); the conferral memory
and the meaning rate set how fast capture comes, not whether or where, and
the fixed points solve a Curie–Weiss equation (CLAIM-947). The reduced
model is weighted averaging with two stubborn sources whose weights relax
toward an agreement kernel, so it adds nothing to that class, but it gives
authority chosen by the initial interpretation and switched by the
evidence with hysteresis, which fixed-weight and reflected-appraisal
models cannot (CLAIM-948); whether that is enough of a difference is
QUESTION-117. Committing predictions before the evidence does not by
itself remove capture (CLAIM-340).

**Between institutions, the loop can close across them.** An organization
can be governed from outside through the evidence its procedures receive
or the criteria they weigh it by, and where it evaluates a party that
affects its evidence or resources, the loop can close between the two, so
that formal independence does not make its evaluation independent
(CLAIM-404). CASE-401 builds that: two of CASE-363's institutions coupled
by four channels. The pair's symmetric state is unstable exactly when the
round-trip gain exceeds one; the rates and memories cancel, and each
institution's own loop enters only through its margin (CLAIM-1000, resting
on CLAIM-947). So two institutions with no loop inside either can be
captured by the loop between them, and an evaluator whose conferral reads
no interpretation at all can be captured; informational independence does
not prevent it while a resource channel runs (CLAIM-1202).

**What prevents it depends on how the party's resources act.** CLAIM-404's
"resources" is read two ways, both filed as true of their models and the
choice left to the owner. Read as direction, an additive tilt of the
evaluator's conferral, resources have a first-order term, and what
prevents capture is a return channel too weak to close the loop: the
evaluator's reading not moving the party's standing or evidence
(CLAIM-1202). Read as capacity, scaling how strongly the evaluator's
conferral answers agreement, they drop out of the linear condition, yet
capture the evaluator from a large enough push below it, toward the side
they favour only, even with its evidence independent of the party
(CLAIM-1021). On the same reading, cutting the return channel prevents
capture only while the party's own reading sits at symmetry: if the party
leans, its resources raise the evaluator's gain until its own loop passes
CLAIM-947's threshold, and it is captured to either side (CLAIM-CODE,
derived from CLAIM-947 and CLAIM-1021, filed at this review). So the local
test of CLAIM-1000 bounds the risk under the directional reading and
understates it under the capacity reading, in two ways.

## Where it stands

Every result here is demonstrated and of its model; all are Active except
CLAIM-CODE, which is new. The model is fictional by construction, and
which reading of resources fits any real evaluator is not studied. Beyond
the linearization, where captured states below threshold begin was not
mapped (CLAIM-1021), and QUESTION-100 stays open.

## Not stated on the record

- **Where an institution's own loop is near its threshold.** CLAIM-1000
  says an institution near its own threshold is captured by a weak cross
  loop, and CLAIM-CODE that capacity resources can push an evaluator past
  its threshold; no entry says how far real evaluators' margins are from
  zero, and none could be read from the model.

## What it does not say

That any real evaluator is captured; which reading of resources CLAIM-404
means; or anything about decision rules or conferral forms other than the
model's.
