`mereology`. The tag held 18 works:

- **3 already read:** [LIT-041](../../record/literature.d/LIT-041.md), and [LIT-167](../../record/literature.d/LIT-167.md) and [LIT-168](../../record/literature.d/LIT-168.md) from the mereology pass.

- **3 seeded from the abstract only.**

not be read.

## What changed besides the readings

- **`anthology-candidate` removed from [LIT-182](../../record/literature.d/LIT-182.md).** The seed flagged it as ML
  practice. The reading found no method, data or empirical claim in it.
- **Two works newly tagged `agency`:**
  - **[LIT-046](../../record/literature.d/LIT-046.md)**, Pilgrim et al. on collective intelligence: a collective that
    senses, decides and acts.
  - **[LIT-211](../../record/literature.d/LIT-211.md)**, *What Lives?*, on the authors' own manual coding: 17 of its 68
    definitions are coded autonomy, agency or goal-directedness.

  My first rationale for [LIT-211](../../record/literature.d/LIT-211.md) counted its "Cognitive Autonomy" cluster as
  two thirds of the definitions. That fraction included the thermodynamic
  cluster, and the reading caught it. The rationale was corrected before
  commit.
- **Considered and not tagged:**
  - Bourrat's reproducers ([LIT-157](../../record/literature.d/LIT-157.md)): the autonomy there is reproductive.
  - Seth ([LIT-135](../../record/literature.d/LIT-135.md)) and Vieira ([LIT-126](../../record/literature.d/LIT-126.md)): consciousness and moral patiency.
  - The ant puzzle study ([LIT-075](../../record/literature.d/LIT-075.md)): collective memory.
  - The reciprocal-causation pair ([LIT-110](../../record/literature.d/LIT-110.md), [LIT-156](../../record/literature.d/LIT-156.md)): causal structure.
