**The readings of 2026-10-25.** Each work read gained a `url:`, except
Kwon and Perchet, already filed by its PMLR page: the
authors' 2013 preprint of Bartók et al. (the publisher's page returned
403), the authors' 1995 technical report of Chaloner and Verdinelli in
the UMN Digital Conservancy (Project Euclid's copy was refused by a bot
check), the PMLR page of MultiDiffusion, and the OpenReview forum of
Multi-LoRA Composition. What the readings corrected:

- **MultiDiffusion's Eq. 4.** As printed it puts the weights W inside the
  norm, so its minimizer weights by W², not W. Eq. 5, with weights to the
  first power, is exact only for 0/1 weights, which are all the paper
  uses. Its Proposition 3.1 is correct but needs zero loss at every step,
  so it covers neither disagreeing overlaps nor masked region generation
  (THEORY-tmpt2q0g).
- **Multi-LoRA Composition's §5.1.1** attributes LoRA Switch's scores of
  6.56 and 5.08 to LoRA Composite, whose scores are 6.09 and 4.53.
- **The unrescaled merge.** Multi-LoRA Composition's weight-merging
  baseline sums k adapters at 0.8 each, so its scale grows with k, and no
  rescaled merge was run. The finding that merging degrades as k grows is
  confounded with that (THEORY-tmpg6v1v).
- **Bartók et al.'s adversarial classification** covers only
  non-degenerate games. Lattimore and Szepesvári later corrected it and
  extended it to all games (THEORY-tmpz3a05). The stochastic case covers
  all games.
