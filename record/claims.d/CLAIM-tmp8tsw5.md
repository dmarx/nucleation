# CLAIM-tmp8tsw5: Relative to a stated target, a combined form is one system while its joint dynamics are nearly decomposable with the form as a block whose gap beats the Occam factor, and it lasts as long as that block persists; the unit is the gap-isolated block, not any member

## The claim

Fix a target, meaning what the description must predict ([CLAIM-tmp3qyzh](CLAIM-tmp3qyzh.md)).
For the dancing couple it might be the next beat's positions. Model the
joint dynamics of the candidate's members and their surroundings, for
example as a linear model of their state variables. Then:

1. **Block.** The candidate is one system when, with its members grouped,
   the interaction matrix is nearly decomposable ([LIT-tmp6qp14](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6qp14.md)). The
   couplings inside the group are strong and those across its edge are
   weak.
2. **Threshold.** The gap that sets the block off clears the Occam factor
   for that block: δ/ε > τ, with τ² the log prior-to-posterior variance
   ratio ([CLAIM-tmp45kkg](CLAIM-tmp45kkg.md)). Below it, the block is not worth naming.
3. **Unit.** What is named is the gap-isolated block, the projector onto
   the pair's joint mode, not either member or any coordinate
   ([CLAIM-tmpjsasy](CLAIM-tmpjsasy.md)).
4. **Duration.** The form lasts while the block keeps clearing the
   threshold. The couple is one system for the song and two after it.
   Simon's short-run independence and long-run aggregate dependence say
   what the boundary licenses while it holds.

This answers the owner's "privileged nomenclature" ([CLAIM-tmpygda8](CLAIM-tmpygda8.md)) with a
test. The name is earned when the gap beats the cost of keeping it.

## How it sits with the other tests

- **Mutual entrainment ([CLAIM-tmptsdgt](CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md))** requires coupling both ways, and
  says something about timing. This claim measures strength, not
  direction. The two are complementary, and the open objection
  ([CLAIM-tmpw0mrc](CLAIM-tmpw0mrc.md)) is where they differ.
- **Reciprocal compensation** ([LIT-tmp11x8k](../literature.d/LIT-tmp11x8k.md), unread) is a perturbation test.
  A block that clears the threshold should also compensate. If it does not,
  that is evidence against this claim (`defeated_if`).
- **Persistence and unity** are further tests. [CLAIM-tmpdqsrb](CLAIM-tmpdqsrb.md) asks whether a
  coherence measure is conserved within the block. [CLAIM-tmp3u2m7](CLAIM-tmp3u2m7.md) asks
  whether the block's contexts glue. A form can clear the threshold and
  still fail either.
- **Otto's notebook** is not a dynamical partner. Its coupling is a write
  and a later read, and a block test on synchronous dynamics will not see
  it. The storage route ([CASE-tmp3lyh3](../cases.d/CASE-tmp3lyh3.md)) needs a time-lagged version of
  the criterion, which is not built.

## What it does not say

- **That the verdict is target-free.** A different target can group the
  members differently.
- **That a form clearing the threshold is an agent or a self.** Those are
  [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md) and the subject sense of [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md).
- **That anything has been measured.** [CASE-tmpazmsq](../cases.d/CASE-tmpazmsq.md) is the study it would
  need.
