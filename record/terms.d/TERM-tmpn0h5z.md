# TERM-tmpn0h5z: communicative object, as an intersection of constraint regions

## Definition

U28: "the 'objects' are intersections of regions of observables". A75: an
observation induces R(o, v) = {s : o(s) = v}, and X = ∩_i R(o_i, v_i). "Here, X
is generally a **region**, not a point. Many underlying states can satisfy the
same constraints." So ambiguity is natural, and a category such as teasing "is
recognizable through a particular conjunction of linguistic, intentional,
evaluative, social, and sequential observables".

## Why it was replaced

A78: the intersection "presupposes a single ambient state space in which all
the regions are already defined" ([TERM-tmp1afp0](TERM-tmp1afp0.md)). The manuscript §2 keeps it with that
caveat: "This simple construction, however, presumes a shared ambient S."
