## What did not enter, and why

- **The world-models editorial ([LIT-195](../../record/literature.d/LIT-195.md), [NOTE-136](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-136.md)).** It argues nothing of
  its own. Its three senses of "world model" are posed conditionally, each
  anchored to one citation, and its verdicts are glosses on seventeen
  contributions the record has not read. A THEORY would rest on readings
  the record does not hold.
- **K-lines ([LIT-402](../../record/literature.d/LIT-402.md), [NOTE-352](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-352.md)).** The NOTE says "No THEORY in the record
  should rest on this memo". The reason is that it has no finding: the
  memo is half-built by its author's account, and nothing is simulated or
  measured. That bars a THEORY rather than only noting that none existed,
  so it was respected.
- **Jokes ([LIT-408](../../record/literature.d/LIT-408.md), [NOTE-361](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-361.md)).** The NOTE says "No THEORY in the record
  should cite it as support". It is an essay with no data and no program,
  and its unifying claim bends by its own Note 16. It was respected for
  the same reason.

So fifteen of eighteen readings became sources, which fits the owner's
hypothesis for this half of the topic. Two caveats apply. Three of the
fifteen NOTEs said "No new THEORY is indicated" or "No THEORY bears on
it" ([NOTE-355](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-355.md), [NOTE-349](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-349.md), [NOTE-331](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-331.md)). Each meant that no existing THEORY was
supported, or that nothing belonged in the anthology, not that the work
had no finding, so under the owner's ruling they did not bar a new one.
And several accounts are positions argued from thought experiments
(Turing, Searle, Dennett, Putnam, the extended mind). They are filed
because the record's other THEORYs already argue with them in prose, and
naming them lets those arguments become relations.

## Choices made

- **One THEORY for the extended mind, two sources.** Clark's 2005 reply
  restates and defends the 1998 argument and confirms in his own words
  that parity was not argued, so it went in as a second source rather than
  a THEORY of its own.
- **One THEORY for Aerts and Gabora I and II.** Part I's lattice is the
  proposal and Part II's model its realisation; the finding is about the
  pair.
- **What the Active findings claim.** Each is stated as what the published
  model or the reported statistics show, with the paper's stronger reading
  in "does not say".
- **Putnam on a skim.** It is Proposed under the owner's ruling for a
  well-established result discussed elsewhere. The record's full readings
  of Block, Chalmers and Schwitzgebel quote the same clause, and the
  THEORY says what it rests on.
