## Theories

Four Proposed theories:

- **Convergence ([THEORY-tmpund3j](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpund3j.md)).** It extends both contagion and
  [THEORY-055](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-055.md).
- **Genuinely collective emotion ([THEORY-tmpgc3z0](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpgc3z0.md)).** Huebner's account
  is a possibility, not a demonstrated case.
- **Contagion ([THEORY-tmp6iizp](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp6iizp.md)).**
- **Judgment aggregation ([THEORY-tmpwsee8](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpwsee8.md)).**

Convergence and genuinely collective emotion are compatible, not rivals.
They answer different questions: whether members converge, and whether
the group bears a state of its own. In Huebner's surviving case the
members diverged. The proposed "three kinds of collective emotion" theory
was declined, because List says one sentence about emotions.
