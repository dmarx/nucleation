# LIT-2386: Enshittification of Algorithmic Recommendation: An Evolutionary Model of Platform-Creator Dynamics

Eladio Montero-Porras, Annelien Smets and Tom Lenaerts (2026). "Enshittification of Algorithmic Recommendation: An Evolutionary Model of Platform-Creator Dynamics." *HHAI 2026, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence and Applications, pp. 268-277*. DOI-10.3233/faia260512.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1787](../notes.d/NOTE-1787.md)). A minimal formal mechanism for platform
decay with path dependence: a transient incentive shock that pushes the
state across a separatrix produces decay that the removal of the shock does
not undo. It is offered as a baseline, not a calibrated model.
