  general physical laws from simulated noisy data across 46
  classical-mechanics experiments without prior physical concepts. It keeps
  an experiment base and a theory base of symbols, concepts and laws written

  dynamical (e.g. velocity as a derivative), intrinsic (e.g. mass, defined
  by the measurement procedure of a named experiment), and universal
  constants. Trials select an experiment and concepts via a UCB-style
  recommendation engine with an adaptive network, search for specific laws
  by symbolic regression, and extend general laws by "plausible reasoning":
  when a general law holding elsewhere fails in the current experiment, add
