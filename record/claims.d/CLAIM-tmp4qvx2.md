# CLAIM-tmp4qvx2: That an entry has stopped changing does not show what stopped it: contraction toward the record's conventions settles entries as a narrowing of their constraints does, and a chain can forget its start while staying far from it, so the two are told apart only by retention against a reference fixed outside the record's later restatements, measured as a diagnostic of mechanism and not as a standard of quality

## The claim

[CLAIM-tmpvm90n](CLAIM-tmpvm90n.md)'s first proposed test was a count: an entry's revision rate
should fall as its inbound `rests_on` and `grounds` grow. That count is worth
making ([LEAD-tmp6do6f](../leads.d/LEAD-tmp6do6f.md)), but its result cannot carry the claim alone.

[CLAIM-081](CLAIM-081.md), resting on [CLAIM-270](CLAIM-270.md): retaining information about where a chain
started and drifting from the original are different profiles; "convergence
is not preservation". An idea restated across sessions until it stops
changing has converged. If it converged onto the transmitters' convention
([CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md)) it has been contracted, not anchored, and its revision rate falls
all the same. A falling rate is predicted by both [CLAIM-tmpvm90n](CLAIM-tmpvm90n.md)'s mechanism
and contraction ([CLAIM-tmpdx6l8](CLAIM-tmpdx6l8.md)). What separates them is the second profile:
how much of the content at the originating turn the settled entry retains,
judged against a reference fixed before the record restated it, in a metric
fixed in advance ([CLAIM-208](CLAIM-208.md)'s demand). [LEAD-tmp84yvk](../leads.d/LEAD-tmp84yvk.md) is that measure.

**Relation to [ARG-028](../arguments.d/ARG-028.md).** [ARG-028](../arguments.d/ARG-028.md)'s open critical question, carried from
[CLAIM-190](CLAIM-190.md), is that the anchoring measures are computed in the grammar that
shapes the argument, so they cannot certify that drift fell. This claim
names a mechanism for that worry: contraction onto the grammar's own
convention ([CLAIM-tmpdx6l8](CLAIM-tmpdx6l8.md)) produces the same settling the measures read as
anchoring. Its remedy is [ARG-028](../arguments.d/ARG-028.md)'s, a reference the record did not supply,
applied to retention rather than to drift.

## What it does not say

- Not that retention is the goal. [CLAIM-189](CLAIM-189.md) holds that a developing argument
  legitimately drops and changes things, so fidelity is the wrong measure of
  its worth. Here retention is a diagnostic: it tells which mechanism settled
  an entry, not whether the settled entry is better.
- Not that the revision-rate count is uninformative. Combined with
  retention it separates the four cases: settled and retained (constraint),
  settled and not retained (contraction), unsettled and retained, unsettled
  and not retained.
