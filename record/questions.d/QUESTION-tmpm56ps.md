# QUESTION-tmpm56ps: Relative to a stated target, when is a combined form one system, where does its boundary lie, and how long does it last?

## Why it is a question

It has come up three times in one day:
- **The dancing couple** ([CASE-tmpbv3ts](../cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md)). The owner holds that it "merits some
  privileged nomenclature" ([CLAIM-tmpygda8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpygda8.md)).
- **Otto and his notebook** ([LIT-097](../literature.d/LIT-097.md); [CASE-tmp3lyh3](../cases.d/CASE-tmp3lyh3.md)).
- **The firm at its all-hands** ([CASE-tmpk52n6](../cases.d/CASE-tmpk52n6.md)).

The record has candidate tests, each measuring something different:
- **Mutual entrainment** ([CLAIM-tmptsdgt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md)), which measures timing.
- **Reciprocal compensation** ([LIT-tmp11x8k](../literature.d/LIT-tmp11x8k.md), unread), which measures whether
  a goal is preserved.
- **Near-decomposability** priced by an Occam factor ([CLAIM-tmp45kkg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp45kkg.md),
  [LIT-tmp6qp14](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6qp14.md)), which measures strength.
- **Conserved coherence** ([CLAIM-tmpdqsrb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdqsrb.md)), which measures persistence.
- **Gluing** ([CLAIM-tmp3u2m7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3u2m7.md)), which measures unity across contexts.

[QUESTION-009](QUESTION-009.md) asks when coordination makes an additional *agent*. This asks
the prior question: when it makes one *system*. Selfhood stays a further
question again, kept apart by [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md).

## What would count as an answer

A criterion that, given a stated target and measurements of a candidate,
returns a verdict that can be wrong. It should say which variables are
inside, from when to when, and with what margin. It must also handle the
cases the record already holds: one-way drive (the metronome, the nerve and
the muscle) must come out not one system, and the couple after the song
must come out two. A criterion that every pair of interacting things
satisfies is not an answer ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)'s permissiveness, in another form).

The proposed answer is [CLAIM-tmp8tsw5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8tsw5.md), and its open objection is
[CLAIM-tmpw0mrc](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw0mrc.md).
