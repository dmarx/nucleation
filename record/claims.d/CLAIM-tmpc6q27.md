# CLAIM-tmpc6q27: Equal entropy does not entail equal relational structure, and a collection of local entropies need not characterize global compatibility

## The claim

A86 §4: "a collection of local entropies need not characterize the global
compatibility structure", and of sheaf structure and information: "Neither
structure is reducible to the other." The example, A = B against A ≠ B for fair
bits. Manuscript §5: "equal entropy does not entail equal relational structure:
perfect correlation and perfect anticorrelation of two fair binary observables
have equal entropy yet reverse a fundamental relationship."

## What it changes

It is why the manuscript needs both a sheaf model and information measures. §11:
"The discipline of keeping these notions separate is what permits their
synthesis." Granted, because it is elementary.
