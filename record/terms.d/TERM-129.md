# TERM-129: capability, decomposed for restriction: competence (the skill, knowledge, equipment or capacity to act), exercise (whether the act can be performed here and now), authority (who is entitled to decide whether it proceeds) and governance (who can set and revise the relations fixing the other three), which vary independently, so that a restriction on one is not a restriction on another

## Definition

A49 §3 of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b),
"Capability is not a single property":

> **Competence** concerns whether an actor possesses the relevant skill, knowledge, equipment, or operational capacity.

> **Exercise** concerns whether and under what circumstances the actor can perform the activity.

> **Authority** concerns who is entitled to decide whether the activity may proceed.

> **Governance** concerns who can establish and revise the relations determining competence, exercise, and authority.

"These dimensions can vary independently." A's examples: a surgeon with
the competence to operate who needs the patient's consent (competence
without authority); an analyst who sees a fraudulent transaction and
cannot stop it; a citizen with a right of appeal and no route to relief
before the consequences become irreversible (authority without exercise).
So "A restriction on exercise is therefore not necessarily a restriction
on competence, and a restriction on competence is not necessarily a
restriction on governance."

The term's use is in judging restrictions. A45 §4 stated it as a rule:
"**Restrictions on the exercise of a capability should not be assumed
equivalent to restrictions on its acquisition, maintenance, or
governance.**" Some restrictions on exercise give reasons to keep the
competence (emergency powers held in reserve; human expertise kept while
autonomous execution is restricted), and a restriction on exercise can
erode competence where competence is kept by use ([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)).

**Two readings of exercise.** A45 §4 defined the objects of restriction
as acts on the actor ("**Exercise:** Preventing an actor from performing
a particular action under particular conditions"; "**Competence:**
Preventing an actor from acquiring or maintaining the ability";
"**Authority:** Preventing an actor from determining when, how, or
whether the ability is exercised"). A49 §3's are dimensions of the
capability. The term takes A49's, so that a restriction is described by
which dimension it reaches.

**The question it serves.** A41 §1 asked three questions of any
capability: "**Can the actor do it?**" (competence and exercise), "**May
the actor do it?**" (authority) and "**Under what conditions ought the
actor retain the capacity to do it?**" (governance). It drew the first
rule from them: "**restricting the exercise of a capability and
eliminating the capability are fundamentally different interventions**".

## What it is not

- **Not [CLAIM-678](../claims.d/CLAIM-678.md)'s three dimensions of power** (operational, economic,
  epistemic). Those divide power over an arrangement among those who hold
  it; this divides one actor's capability to act.
- **Not [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s three resource requirements** (to construct, maintain
  and move into or out of a form). Those are what having a form costs;
  competence here can be dear to acquire and cheap to keep for that
  reason, but the term does not price it.
- **Not the capability approach's capability** (Sen, [LIT-289](../literature.d/LIT-289.md)), a person's
  substantive freedom to achieve functionings. That is a set of
  attainable beings and doings; this is the structure of one ability and
  of the control over it.
- **Not [TERM-085](TERM-085.md)'s correctability**, a probability that an arrangement
  achieves an objective. A capability in this sense can be fully present
  in all four dimensions and still fail for want of time or resources.

## What would settle it

Adoption would add the vocabulary to the record's argument on
restriction ([CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md) answers [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md) in it). It would be
unsettled by a restriction the record needs to describe that reaches none
of the four, or by a pair of the four that cannot in fact vary
independently, so that the decomposition double counts.
