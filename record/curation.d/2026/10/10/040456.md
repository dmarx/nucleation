Reconstructing owner turns U40–U60 of the exchange behind "What Survives
Translation?" (2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10) filed new claims that stand on four
standard works the record did not hold. The exchange names each of them or
plainly relies on it. They are registered here as LITs so that the claims can
name them in `grounds`. None was read. Each is Deferred, worded "filed, not
read", and no NOTE is filed.

## What entered

- **Pearl, *Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference*, 2nd ed., 2009**
  ([LIT-tmph1v0q](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmph1v0q.md), doi:10.1017/CBO9780511803161). It grounds the do() layer:
  [CLAIM-127](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-127.md) version 2 and the distinction between telling an interpreter
  about a situation and changing it ([CLAIM-tmp2i7yj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2i7yj.md)). The owner proposed
  do() at U41.
- **Serre, *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*, 1977**
  ([LIT-tmp5vc66](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp5vc66.md), doi:10.1007/978-1-4684-9458-7). It grounds the limits of
  characters as classifiers ([CLAIM-tmp3sn40](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3sn40.md)), which bound the owner's U54
  appeal to character theory.
- **Deutsch, "Quantum mechanics near closed timelike lines", 1991**
  ([LIT-tmp1mxid](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp1mxid.md), doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.44.3197). It grounds the contrast between
  causal-loop consistency and sheaf contextuality ([CLAIM-tmpc4chg](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpc4chg.md)), which
  answers the owner's U46.
- **Esfeld & Lam, "Moderate structural realism about space-time", 2008**
  ([LIT-tmpme4lk](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpme4lk.md), doi:10.1007/s11229-006-9076-2). A194 places the owner's
  non-eliminative structural realism "close to" it. It grounds that position
  ([CLAIM-tmp8yo01](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8yo01.md)) and the objection to it ([CLAIM-tmp4xhdn](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4xhdn.md)).

Each identifier was checked against Crossref on 2026-10-10.

## How deeply

Not at all. [ADR-001](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-001.md) leaves the depth of each filing to the curation
log, and this is the statement for this batch. The owner has said that in this
record registering a work means reading it (the curation entry of 2026-10-09,
00:41). These four are an exception made on purpose: each is cited for what
it is standardly cited for, and no claim depends on a reading of it beyond
that. The `inactive-ok` annotations on the citing entries say so. Reading any
of them would move it to Active with a NOTE.

## The boundary

None of the four is held in the Anthology of the SOTA. I grepped a clone of
its record/ (commit d8b5ba5, 2026-10-09, possibly stale) for each DOI and for
"Serre", "Esfeld" and "Pearl" in its literature, and found nothing. No
anthology topic holds causality, group theory, the physics of closed timelike
curves or metaphysics, so the four belong here, and [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) does not
apply. None carries `anthology-candidate`.
