# THEORY-tmp8xp3z: Because persistence (mean absolute fitness at least 1) and selection (fitness differences) are logically independent, niche construction theory's evolutionary niche, read externally as the factors producing fitness differences in a population, differs in extension from the realized population ecological niche even after unit and modality are matched, so it is not a simple translation of Hutchinson's niche

## Source

Trappes (2021), [LIT-169](../literature.d/LIT-169.md) (*Biology & Philosophy* 36:31), read in full in
[NOTE-155](../notes.d/NOTE-155.md).

## What was actually shown

**The two concepts.** The evolutionary niche is "the sum of all the natural
selection pressures to which the population is exposed" (Odling-Smee,
Laland & Feldman 2003:40). Read externally, it is the environmental factors
that, given the phenotypes present, lead to fitness differences in a
population (pp. 8–9). The ecological niche is "the environmental conditions,
both biotic and abiotic, that permit the continued existence of an
individual, population or species" (p. 6).

**Three gaps, two closed by restriction (pp. 7–9).** They differ in
reference unit (population only, against individual, population or
species), in modality (actual, against fundamental or realized), and in
effect (fitness differences, against persistence). Restricting the
ecological niche to the realized population niche closes the first two.
The modality gap alone already contradicts the 2003 book's claim that its
niche translates the *fundamental* niche (p. 8).

**The third gap does not close (p. 9).** With no migration, a population
persists when its average absolute fitness is at least 1 over generations.
A selection pressure exists when there are fitness differences. Neither
implies the other. The reading grades this step, and the non-identity it
yields, strong ([NOTE-155](../notes.d/NOTE-155.md), C2 and C6).

**Three kinds of non-coincidence (pp. 10–14).**
- Habitat degradation: in the evolutionary niche, not the ecological one.
  Snow geese overgrazing Hudson Bay salt marshes; depleted resources select,
  while the degraded habitat "likely" no longer permits persistence. This
  is OLF's own negative niche construction.
- Commensalism: in the ecological niche, not the evolutionary one.
  *Tupiocoris notatus* on *Datura wrightii* has no fitness effect and no
  interaction effect (Hare & Elle 2002), assuming no unmeasured
  interactions. The author's first candidate, the same bug on *Nicotiana
  attenuata*, failed: there it primes defences and so selects.
- Non-limiting resources: atmospheric CO2 for land plants, offered as a
  "possible example" on a between-lineage difference. Direct evidence is
  "difficult to locate".

**What could have come out otherwise.** Selection and persistence could
have been tied by definition, or every candidate case could have failed as
the first commensal did. The first did not happen; the second nearly did.

## What this does not say

- **That the two niches differ much.** They "still coincide to a large
  extent" (p. 15), and the size of the non-coincidence is not measured.
- **That niche construction theory as a whole uses this niche.** Only the
  2003 book is analysed. Later concepts (Stotz 2017's selective and
  developmental niches; constitutive and relational niche construction) are
  set aside, and the author says so.
- **That a phenotype-inclusive evolutionary niche would differ as much.**
  The result rests on the externalist reading of selection pressure, which
  the paper calls "standard, though contested". A niche that includes
  phenotypic change "may" show greater similarity (p. 9).
- **That each case is established.** Two of the three rest on a single
  study or a hypothetical. The non-identity does not depend on them; the
  claim that real systems fall in the gap does.
- **That niche construction has no evolutionary significance.** The
  conclusion is that its significance "would not be a given": it is to be
  shown case by case, not read off the definition (pp. 16–17).
