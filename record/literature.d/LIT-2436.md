# LIT-2436: Pregnancy-induced maternal microchimerism shapes neurodevelopment and behavior in mice

Steven Schepanski, Mattia Chini, Veronika Sternemann, Christopher Urbschat, Kristin Thiele, Ting Sun and others (2022). "Pregnancy-induced maternal microchimerism shapes neurodevelopment and behavior in mice." *Nature Communications 13(1)*. DOI-10.1038/s41467-022-32230-2.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1759](../notes.d/NOTE-1759.md)). Causal evidence that maternal
microchimeric cells perform a functional regulatory role in fetal brain
development, rather than being an inert "placental leak out".
