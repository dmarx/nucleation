# THEORY-tmpc3mjh: Márquez and co-authors review how, in a volume hologram, multi-plane diffraction makes reconstruction Bragg-selective in the angle (in transmission) or wavelength (in reflection) of the reading beam, so that holograms recorded in the same volume but detuned beyond the Bragg selectivity range are reconstructed independently, one per reading beam, sharing the material's dynamic range; on this basis angle multiplexing is the most widespread holographic-data-storage method, with more than 1000 holograms stored, and thick layers are needed to keep crosstalk between pages down

Márquez, Li, Beléndez, Maier & Ren (2023), [LIT-tmp3d5f9](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3d5f9.md); skimmed in [NOTE-tmpgrvfy](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpgrvfy.md).

## The result

The review separates thin (Raman–Nath) from thick (volume) holograms by
the Klein–Cook parameter. In a thick hologram the reading beam crosses
many recorded fringe planes, so reconstruction is very sensitive to its
angle, most strongly in transmission, or its wavelength, most strongly in
reflection. Holograms recorded in the same volume and detuned beyond the
selectivity range are reconstructed independently; in Bragg-based
multiplexing only one hologram is constructively generated for each
reading beam, and all share the material's dynamic range. Angle
multiplexing is listed as the most widespread HDS method, with more than
1000 holograms, citing Mok (1993); thick layers are needed against
crosstalk between pages; and van Heerden's bound puts storage density
proportional to V/λ³. These are the review's statements of the primary
literature, not new results.

## What it does not show

- How sharp the selection is: no selectivity-width formula or dependence
  on thickness is given, so A's "quite sharp" is not quantified.
- Any primary measurement: Kogelnik's theory and Mok's 5000-hologram
  demonstration are cited, not read here.
- That this is the source A cited: A's Optica and doi.org links were not
  identified.
- Anything about records or learning. That keyed readout from a
  superposed store shows an address need not be a location ([CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md)),
  and that it parallels Plate's HRR ([THEORY-tmp713mr](THEORY-tmp713mr.md)), is the record's
