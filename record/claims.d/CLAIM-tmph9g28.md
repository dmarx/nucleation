# CLAIM-tmph9g28: Intelligence may be understood as the adaptive organization of epistemic ecological richness: richness is what an ecology of differentiated perspectives makes available or can develop, intelligence is the capacity to mobilize, coordinate, evaluate and expand it under resource-bounded demands, so diversity contributes to intelligence without being a synonym for it, and two systems equally competent today can be unequally intelligent for the demands they may meet tomorrow

## The claim

A62b's central move: "Intelligence is not merely the capacity to produce correct answers to given questions. It is the maintained capacity of an epistemic ecology to generate distinctions, formulate questions, develop and evaluate answers, and reorganize those capacities as its circumstances change." It then separates two things the owner's phrase joins: "Richness concerns what an ecology makes available, or can develop. Intelligence additionally concerns whether that richness can be productively mobilized under the demands, constraints, and timescales of an actual situation."

Its steps, in A62b's order:

1. **Today against tomorrow (§1).** Two systems can answer the same familiar questions, one by a specialized routine, one with several ways of understanding the problem. Resting on CLAIM-754's split of operational from developmental capability: "Two systems can be equally competent today but unequally intelligent with respect to the demands they may encounter tomorrow."
2. **More than any constituent (§2).** From THEORY-402: a group's intelligence is not the sum of its members', and can exceed or fall below it. Different perspectives supply different access: one formulates a question another cannot, a second observes what the first cannot interpret.
3. **Differentiation, not number (§3).** Diversity measures differentiation, coverage measures how much of the query ecology is adequately addressed, and intelligence the capacity to maintain, mobilize and develop that coverage, so "epistemic diversity is a contributor to intelligence, rather than an interchangeable synonym for it". The coverage model is A60's, held as CLAIM-tmp7iie0 with its conditions.
4. **Generativity (§4).** Intelligence "is partly the capacity to expand and reorganize the space of possible inquiry": an answer can introduce a distinction that makes a new question askable. That is reflexive inquiry, TERM-095.
5. **Maintenance and integration (§5, §6).** A rich system must maintain "differentiation without requiring continual full utilization", and needs "integration that preserves useful differences". A system rich in independent perspectives that its authority structure keeps apart "possesses considerable latent epistemic richness but may exhibit poor collective intelligence".

A62b's boxed relation, epistemic intelligence ∼ viable organization of epistemic richness, is, it says, "deliberately schematic. It expresses a proposed conceptual relation, not a numerical identity."

## Where the record already held it

Most steps are held; what is new is the separation of intelligence from richness and the claim that intelligence is the organization of richness.

- Step 1 is CLAIM-754, applied. A62b's "developmental epistemic richness", the family of inquiry relations reachable "through affordable learning", is CLAIM-754's developmental frontier carried to the joint inquiry relation of TERM-tmpdk711.
- Step 2 is THEORY-402, and NOTE-339's reading of the Society of Mind.
- Step 3's model is CLAIM-tmp7iie0's, with the conditions ARG-tmpbchnj sets on it.
- Step 5's allocation among exploiting, investigating, maintaining, developing and revising standards is CLAIM-tmpagnsw's intertemporal allocation; the maintained perspectives are CLAIM-tmpoexcl's nuclei; integration that keeps differences is CLAIM-tmpumpxw.
- §9's consequence for the self, "we might begin with an ecology of capabilities whose historically maintained integration gives rise to a self-like organization exhibiting intelligence", is the thesis of selves already filed from this exchange (CLAIM-tmptxsin, CLAIM-tmp37le7), with intelligence added.

## A correction

§3 says, of its pseudometric d_μ(P_i, P_j) = ∫ |r_i − r_j| dμ: "A collection of arbitrarily different, consistently wrong responses could have high diversity." Under that metric it could not. CLAIM-tmpjk3nm shows that no two perspectives are further apart under d_μ than the ecology's coverage, so consistently wrong perspectives are close under it however different their answers. The point A62b wanted, diversity without coverage, needs a metric on what is answered, not on how often it is adequate. The step it illustrates, that diversity is not a synonym for intelligence, stands: under d_μ high coverage with no diversity is possible (one perspective that answers everything), though high diversity with low coverage is not.
