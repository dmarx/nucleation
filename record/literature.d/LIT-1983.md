# LIT-1983: Distinguishing between basic psychological needs and basic wellness enhancers: the case of beneficence as a candidate psychological need

Martela, F., Ryan, R. M. (2019). Distinguishing between basic psychological needs and basic wellness enhancers: the case of beneficence as a candidate psychological need. *Motivation and Emotion, 44(1), 116-133*. doi:10.1007/s11031-019-09800-x.

## Key takeaways

- Beneficence frustration correlates with every ill-being indicator, but "in
  no case was it independently connected to any well-being or ill-being
  indicator when controlling for the contribution of the three basic need
  frustrations" (p. 128).
- Without competence frustration as a control it does predict ill-being:
  "it is especially competence frustration that accounts for the
  insignificant relations" (footnote 2, p. 124).
- Beneficence "might be better considered a basic wellness enhancer"
  (p. 130), a proposal offered tentatively; "it is premature to make any
  conclusions" (p. 130).
