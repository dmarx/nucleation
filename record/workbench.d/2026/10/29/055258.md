**What the pass took.** The worklist's new section listed 14 Active granted
claims whose `objects_to` target had never been revised after the
concession (ADR-059), on 10 targets. Three reviewers read five clusters
read-only, and every recommendation was checked against the entries before
it was applied.

**Eight targets revised.**

- CLAIM-137 now states the condition its 2026-10-10 reply to CLAIM-249
  relied on: one symmetry group anchored on the situation, not on labels of
  a medium. CLAIM-132's bounds were already in version 1.
- CLAIM-139, CLAIM-115 and CLAIM-143 keep their titles, which are A218's
  wording and the manuscript's thesis. CLAIM-238's concession is a fault in
  their defeat conditions, nulls with no margin over observables the thesis
  could re-choose, so the conditions now fix observables, weights, a margin
  and a power before the data. CLAIM-143's modal disjunct is marked as met
  only by argument (CLAIM-239). CLAIM-139 and CLAIM-115 are rivals on the
  fidelity clause, so they no longer complement each other (CLAIM-211), and
  CLAIM-139 answers QUESTION-002.
- CLAIM-013 is narrowed to what the four benchmarks the record has read
  leave out: stance, alignment and uptake (CLAIM-097).
- CLAIM-005 is narrowed to say the match constrains a translation only with
  one correspondence fixed across many items, and charges for loss and not
  for addition (CLAIM-207, CLAIM-256).
- CLAIM-084's recommended outcome and its defeat condition now wait on a
  reliable footing measure (CLAIM-227).

**One edge dropped, two false positives.** CLAIM-132 denies only that the
character analogy supports CLAIM-136, which CLAIM-136 already says; it does
not deny CLAIM-136, so the `objects_to` edge goes. CLAIM-301 and CLAIM-295
were filed the same day as CLAIM-292 and CLAIM-293 with the concession
already in "What it does not say". A version bump there would record a
revision that never happened, so they get blocker notes and stay on the
list until the check can tell.

**What the check misses.** Three things the reviewers found about the
worklist itself:

- It reads only `role: granted`. CLAIM-222, CLAIM-226 and CLAIM-243 are
  `role: counter`, went Active under the old "a conceded one goes Active"
  convention, and concede against CLAIM-115, CLAIM-139 and CLAIM-005. This
  pass took in CLAIM-222's and CLAIM-226's repairs; CLAIM-243's needs a new
  title, which is CLAIM-220's.
- It cannot tell a target filed with the concession already in its text.
  Exempting targets dated on or after the grant would be wrong: CLAIM-013
  was filed the same day as CLAIM-097 and still overstated.
- `live_objections()` counts a granted objector as live after its target
  has been narrowed, though ADR-057 says narrowing answers it. Every target
  revised here stays off the "unblocked" list for that reason alone.

**For the owner.**

- Whether CLAIM-204 supersedes CLAIM-139 and CLAIM-220 supersedes CLAIM-115,
  open since 2026-10-10. This pass revised both in place.
- Whether CLAIM gets a `rivals` relation, so the rivalry is not only prose.
- Whether CLAIM-005's defeat condition is restated, which ripples to
  CASE-184, CLAIM-233 and ARG-026.
- Whether CLAIM-013 is scoped to the benchmarks the record has read, which
  would make it promotable, and whether CLAIM-295's defeat condition is
  aligned with its title, which would too.
