harm: where those affected have standing to contest the harm with the
organization, reproducing the projections that keep their claims from
registering defeats that standing, a wrong directed at the conditions of
their claims' uptake and not at the interests the harm sets back; it needs
no intending actor and no ignorance on their part. ARG-tmpl0o7n argues it,
and CLAIM-614, from a constructed pair of water utilities with the harm,
its continuation and its expected continuation matched, differing only in a
reproduced complaint taxonomy; it carries CLAIM-723's form of wrong from
timing to representation. CLAIM-tmpvv7fw is the instrumentalist objection
it answers. Dated notes on CLAIM-614 (with a proposed Reframing, not
applied: the thesis conditioned on standing), CLAIM-624, QUESTION-441 and
LIT-2532.
