**Legacy.** The owner noted on 2026-10-29 that Johnston's *Surviving Death*
opens another angle: notions, and the psychology, of legacy. The owner's
thesis ([CLAIM-491](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-491.md)) extends the self across people; legacy extends it
across time, past death. [QUESTION-301](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-301.md) states the question. Every work
was checked against Crossref or Open Library, and Unpaywall was asked for
an open copy; only Hershfield 2011 has one, in Europe PMC, which refused this
client, so it is Unfetched. The others are Deferred, no further copy yet
sought.

past reaching the living, where legacy is the living reaching the future.

descendants, and whether on the identity route an ancestor's injury becomes
part of the descendant's self. Sixteen works are filed with it, each checked
against Crossref; ten have open copies (Unpaywall).

**Memory through metamorphosis.** The owner recalled work on memory that
persists through metamorphosis, and a child's science-fair experiment on
whether caterpillars would remember him as butterflies. The search found
that the publication came first. Blackiston, Silva Casey and Weiss 2008
showed odour aversion surviving from caterpillar to moth. Jo Nagai, a
schoolboy in Kobe, repeated the design with swallowtails, advised by Weiss.
His work was reported in 2026 as presented in Japan but unpublished, with a
claim of transmission to offspring that rests on secondary accounts, and it
is not filed. Filed with [QUESTION-302](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-302.md):
- Blackiston et al. 2008 ([LIT-1680](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1680.md)), open;
- Alloway 1972 on beetles ([LIT-1781](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1781.md));
- Shomrat and Levin 2013 on planarian memory through head regeneration
  ([LIT-1732](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1732.md));
- Blackiston, Shomrat and Levin's 2015 perspective ([LIT-1707](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1707.md)), open.
