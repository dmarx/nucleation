# CLAIM-621: An expression's reproduction can come apart from agreement on the concept it carries: communities that use it against one another, and contest whom it describes, can keep it in circulation, so symbolic stability can persist while conceptual stability fails, and a durable dispute over a term shows a durable relation among its uses, not one concept

## The claim

A41 §5 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e), on the
owner's example at U19 ([CASE-222](../cases.d/CASE-222.md)): "A symbol may persist because
several communities continually use it in opposition to one another, even
while disagreeing about what it means and whom it properly describes."
"The symbol's reproduction does not require semantic consensus." "In fact,
the disagreement itself may contribute to its perpetuation." A41 separated
symbolic, conceptual and perspectival stability, and concluded: "The
existence of a durable semantic dispute is evidence of a durable relational
organization, not proof of one globally consistent concept."

A41 §10 named the type "An antagonistic focal symbol", "as a proposed
category for this inquiry", one that "persists partly because opposing
perspectives continually respond to each other's use of it". It set it
apart from a boundary object, which supports coordination across
perspectives (Star and Griesemer, not registered), and from a cultural
attractor ([TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)), a convergence of reconstructions. A43 §5 added that
the opposed efforts reproduce the expression without forming one will: the
symbolic system "may be dynamically coherent without being a unified
organization of will", which [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md) already holds.

**What is new.** The record holds the general form: a pattern can persist
while constituents work against it ([CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md)), and an antagonism can be
inherited by new carriers ([CASE-098](../cases.d/CASE-098.md)); [CASE-142](../cases.d/CASE-142.md) has a label confirmed by
the resistance it meets. What this claim adds is the semantic instance:
the carrier (the expression) and the concept ([TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)) separate, and use
against rival users is what does the reproducing.

## What it does not say

- That the rival uses are several concepts, or one. That is
  [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md).
- That the expression is an autonomous individual. A41 §10: "none of those
  roles automatically makes the symbol an autonomous individual".
- That any measurement has separated reproduction by rivalry from
  reproduction by a shared profile. None is cited.
