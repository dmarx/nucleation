# THEORY-tmpoak8v: Killen and Smetana hold that Piaget was right that peer relations foster concepts of fairness and that strict equality precedes equity, but wrong that early morality is heteronomous, since by about three to four children judge moral transgressions wrong independently of authority, take intentions into account and prefer equal distribution

## The account

Morality emerges early: what develops is the competence to apply it to
complex situations. Peers and friendship matter, as Piaget said, but
children are not first heteronomous; by 3–4 they distinguish moral from
conventional rules and weigh intentions, and they prefer equal division,
turning to merit and need with age.

## Evidence

Review of developmental research across paradigms.
