*Follows the four reconstruction entries of 2026-10-18 (U1–U47), the same
contribution, before merge.*

The owner reviewed the filing. They judged its strongest parts to be "the
treatment of ordinal frustration, its constrained optimization refinement,
the individuation-sensitive account of relief, and the distinction between
non-dominating integration and cheap conformity". They also found
"several issues that matter to the substance of the theory, not just
presentation". The entries are unmerged and stay at version 1. Each
correction is stated in the entry it changes, and listed here.

**Two foundational corrections.**

- **Ordinal frustration was trivially satisfiable** ([CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md),
  [TERM-060](../../record/terms.d/TERM-060.md)). The primary definition took A37's acceptance set
  A_i = {u ∈ U : u ⪰_i u₀}. The owner: "if u₀ is an admissible
  transformation and ⪰_i is a preorder, reflexivity guarantees u₀ ∈ A_i
  for every process", so ⋂ A_i ⊇ {u₀} and "Every collection of demands
  becomes trivially compatible." The worked examples were unaffected,
  since they use strict or threshold sets, but the construction is the
  one several entries depend on. Acceptance sets are now stated as
  requirements that can exclude the baseline: strict improvement
  (u ≻_i u₀), a required level t_i, or a context-indexed obligation.
  Weak Pareto improvement is a condition on the whole collection (at least
  one demand improves strictly) and is not an intersection of per-demand
  sets, so the term says it falls outside the complex. The first filing
  repeated A37's definition without checking it against reflexivity.
- **Practical synchronization was filed as generalizing [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md)**
  ([TERM-061](../../record/terms.d/TERM-061.md)). The first filing followed A45 in calling [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md)'s
  sense "the special case captured by the previous definition". The
  owner: "That inclusion does not follow. [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) describes agreement in
  rankings without requiring coupling. The new term requires actual
  coordination through interactions. Processes might agree about what
  should happen while lacking any working mechanism to achieve it.
  Conversely, processes can coordinate effectively while maintaining
  different rankings." The term now sets out the owner's two dimensions:
  valuational concordance against practical coordination, with
  "agreement without effective coordination" as the cell that makes them
  independent. The question whether [TERM-061](../../record/terms.d/TERM-061.md) should supersede [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) is
  withdrawn: neither generalizes the other, and both stay Active. The
  notes on [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) and the U35–U47 entry are corrected to match. The owner
  noted that this also meets [CLAIM-163](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)'s objection more directly, since the
  mechanism that objection asks for belongs to the coordination dimension.

**Five narrowings.**

- **[CLAIM-358](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-358.md)** stated the owner's escalation thesis unconditionally in
  its title while its body held A45's conditional form. Retitled, in the
  owner's suggested wording: "learned local accommodation can reduce the
  amortized cost of recurrent arbitration". The owner: "Local negotiation
  and maintaining specialist relationships are not free."
- **[CLAIM-351](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-351.md)** said every demand was "locally satisfied" when every
  gradient vanishes. That is joint first-order stationarity only, which a
  maximum or saddle also has. The title and the body's definition now say
  so.
- **[CLAIM-359](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-359.md)** said security and self-governance "trade off". The
  owner: the discussion supports "that safeguards can suppress legitimate
  revision, not that security necessarily competes with autonomy. Some
  mechanisms may improve both." The claim is now conditional and separates
  adverse trade-offs from complementary safeguards (independent audit,
  [CLAIM-342](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-342.md); critic without control, [CLAIM-281](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)). Its defeater was rewritten
  to fit.
- **[CLAIM-362](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)** said "Authenticity is ..." non-domination. Retitled as
  "a candidate constituent of authenticity ..., not a sufficient criterion
  of ownership", as A45 had put it. Its defeater no longer counts an
  installed arrangement as a defeat. Such a case shows insufficiency,
  which the claim concedes, and the manipulation problem stays open
  ([CLAIM-364](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)).
- **[TERM-059](../../record/terms.d/TERM-059.md)** gave A18's third realist condition, constraints on
  admissible perspectives, only in its body. The title, summary and the
  first line of the definition now say it is unmet, "to avoid giving the
  impression that normative realism has already been established".

**What the review found missing.** The owner: "The contribution is strong
as a genealogy of claims, but weaker as an integrated account of our
resulting position." They asked for "a short synthesis entry, probably a
manuscript-position note rather than another standalone claim". It is the

**What the review confirmed.** The owner found the minimax-duality

**A second review, before merge.** The owner read the revisions and found
five more points, "more about making the theory precise, preventing
accidental overcommitment, and ensuring the record remains usable".

- **[CASE-113](../../record/cases.d/CASE-113.md)** said the tasks "conflict: g_1ᵀg_2 < 0". That
  contradicted [CLAIM-366](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-366.md), which shows the sign is not invariant and
  does not exclude common descent. The case now calls it "a negative
  Euclidean inner product ... in the stipulated parameterization", a
  heuristic indicator, and separates it from the test of frustration
  against declared acceptance conditions. The same caution is added where
  [CLAIM-367](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-367.md) and [QUESTION-122](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-122.md) quote the inequality. The owner:
  "the contrast between superficial gradient conflict and genuine
  organizational frustration is one of the manuscript's main technical
  contributions."
- **[CLAIM-351](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-351.md)** still called stationarity "necessary, not sufficient"
  for reconciliation. The owner's counterexample: a threshold demand
  L(θ) = θ² ≤ 1 is satisfied at θ = 0.5, where ∇L = 1. The body now
  keeps stationarity (derivatives), compatibility (the joint existence of
  acceptable outcomes) and satisfaction (whether the selected outcome
  meets the requirements) apart. It says stationarity is neither
  necessary nor sufficient for satisfaction, and leaves satisfaction to
  the ordinal account.
- **[TERM-061](../../record/terms.d/TERM-061.md)** said its definition excluded conditioned conformity.
  The owner: "I don't think those requirements exclude it." Conditioned
  regimes can sustain compatible activity through local interaction with
  no active supervisor. The term is now explicitly descriptive.
  Conditioned conformity can count as practical synchronization and fail
  non-dominating integration ([CLAIM-362](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)), which preserves "the possibility that two
  systems are equally synchronized but differ profoundly in their
  autonomy".
- **The position note** set Chesson's stabilizing mechanisms against
  equalizing ones. The owner: "The contrast is too sharp." On Chesson's
  account stabilizing mechanisms are needed for stable coexistence, and
  equalizing ones can also contribute, by shrinking fitness inequalities
  that would otherwise overwhelm the stabilizing differences. The note
  now pairs complementary opportunities for consequential participation
  with the prevention of exclusionary authority disparities. The same
  correction is made in the [CLAIM-279](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-279.md) note.
- **[CLAIM-358](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-358.md)**'s defeater still let one negative comparison refute a
  claim that savings can occur. It now asks for a showing, over a stated
  class of conflicts that admit accommodation, that the savings never
  occur. The owner: "defeat conditions should test the actual proposition
  rather than a stronger, disclaimed version."

The owner also asked that the position note not present its account "as
though it were one necessary developmental progression", but as "a
network of possible organizational dependencies and transformations".
They asked that its central position "emphasize an organization of
differentiated processes whose effective relations can themselves be
maintained and revised, rather than imply that every organism, learner,
or institution necessarily develops toward the same form of
authenticity". The note is rewritten to say so. It now lists the
dissociations among its items: differentiation without prior
frustration, synchronization without a cultivating governor, and
resolve-like governance without non-domination.

**Third and fourth reviews: the frustration term, proposed and withdrawn.**
After [#161](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/161) merged, a third review relayed by the owner proposed a
replacement definition of frustration: "a condition in which one or more
organizationally consequential demands are obstructed from realization".
It was filed on the follow-up branch as a new term that superseded
[TERM-060](../../record/terms.d/TERM-060.md), introduced as "The owner's wording". A fourth review, also
relayed by the owner, found the attribution wrong. The wording "was
drafted by me in our previous conversation as a recommended revision",
and the owner "hadn't explicitly adopted my proposed replacement". The
review also found the definition itself premature. It "uses obstruction to
cover incompatibility, non-attainment, and causal interference, although
these are different kinds of phenomena". Its operative criterion "doesn't
establish an obstruction. It establishes non-attainment." And "an
obstruction needs a target". The proposed term was therefore withdrawn
before merge, and [TERM-060](../../record/terms.d/TERM-060.md) is not superseded. What changed instead:

- **[QUESTION-tmpyc009](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpyc009.md)** holds the open question, whether frustration
  belongs primarily to the structure of incompatible demands or to the
  dynamics in which a demand meets resistance. It records both candidate
  definitions as proposals, each correctly attributed, with the record's
  two precedents: [CLAIM-153](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-153.md)'s structural sense and [THEORY-052](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-052.md)'s
  thwarting.
- **[TERM-060](../../record/terms.d/TERM-060.md)** gets a dated note. It records an error in it: a
  weak-preference set is a legitimate maintenance demand, so the sentence
  calling it "not a demand" is wrong. The fourth review found that this
  overstatement came from the correction made in the first review, and the
  same overstatement was in the withdrawn term. The note also records that
  strain is not the alternative to frustration, that non-attainment is not
  obstruction, and the refinements to keep whatever definition is adopted.
- **[CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)** (version 2) restates the compatibility complex on
  attainable outcomes, with improvement, maintenance and threshold demands
  kept apart. It shows that an incompatibility belongs to a collection, not
  to a single member.
- **[CLAIM-357](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-357.md)** (version 2) is retitled. The residual and the regret
  diagnose feasibility and avoidable shortfall, not thwarting: "The
  mathematics should not inherit conclusions from the terminology."
- **[CLAIM-368](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-368.md)** (version 2) is retitled. Frustration comes before distress
  and is independent of strain, and the claim no longer fixes which sense
  of frustration is primary.

**On attribution in this entry.** The four reviews were relayed by the
owner from their own conversation with an assistant. The quotations above
introduced as "The owner:" are from those relayed texts. The record does
not know which of their wording the owner wrote and which they adopted.
The fourth review shows that at least one proposal in them was the
assistant's and was not adopted. A reader should treat the reviews'
proposals as relayed recommendations unless an entry says the owner
endorsed them.

**Fifth review: the formal core is not in question.** A further review
relayed by the owner found that withdrawing the replacement term had
"retreated too far by treating the very meaning of our mathematical
frustration concept as an open question". The exchange had a concrete
definition, and it was "doing the work in our earlier reasoning". A
collection of demands is structurally frustrated when no outcome the
organization can attain within its horizon and budget meets them all. The
open question is how that relates to operative thwarting of a
particular constituent, not whether the record has a definition. "We
didn't lose the definition. We lost track of the distinction between
defining structural frustration and explaining the different
organizational phenomena to which that definition might be relevant."

- **[CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)** now defines structural frustration: S is structurally
  frustrated exactly when S ∉ 𝒦(𝒪; D), that is ℛ(𝒪; H, B) ∩ ⋂_{i∈S} A_i
  = ∅. It says the criterion is settled by the definitions and the
  organizational interpretation is open. Relief is strict enlargement of
  𝒦 under fixed reference demands, with no compatible collection lost.
  The claim stays Proposed: the construction is well defined, but its
  explanatory role and the meaning of relief are interpretive.
- **[QUESTION-tmpyc009](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpyc009.md)** is narrowed to: how does structural frustration
  relate to operative thwarting, and when can one occur without the other?
  It treats them as two levels of attribution, a collection against a
  constituent, not as rival definitions.
- **[CLAIM-357](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-357.md)** now keeps four things apart: structural infeasibility
  (Φ > 0), realized violation (m(d_actual) > 0, equivalently Γ > −Φ when
  Φ ≤ 0), minimax suboptimality (Γ > 0) and causal governance failure.
  The review's counterexample, checked: one demand with ε = 1, g = 1,
  U = [−2, 0] gives Φ = −1, and the update d = −1 meets the requirement
  with Γ = 1. "suboptimality, unsatisfied requirements, and frustration
  are three different claims."
- **[TERM-060](../../record/terms.d/TERM-060.md)** is not superseded. Its dated note now points to [CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)
  for the exact structural definition.
