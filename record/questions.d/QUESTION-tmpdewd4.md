# QUESTION-tmpdewd4: Can a will-organization be manipulated without anything in its present structure showing it, and if so, what in its history would have to be recorded to tell a manipulated will from one that is merely effective?

## The question

The will line describes an agent's practical efficacy by how its regimes
are organized now. [CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) already says that such an explanation does not
settle whose the will is. Manipulation is the sharpest case. In Pereboom's
four cases ([LIT-tmpa238g](../literature.d/LIT-tmpa238g.md)), an agent's present structure is produced by
someone else and matches an unmanipulated agent's in every respect the
account names.

The question has two parts:

1. **Is manipulation invisible to present structure?** [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) says yes
   for every structural condition in the record's readings. Does the will
   line's notion of organization, with its governance among regimes and its
   capacity to transform, escape that, or is it one more structural
   condition?
2. **If it is invisible, what history would show it?** [THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md) says a
   regulation acquired under controlling conditions is introjected, not
   integrated, so how a value was acquired fixes how autonomous acting on
   it is. Pettigrew ([LIT-202](../literature.d/LIT-202.md)) makes the criterion "the space and resources
   those mechanisms leave for the free exercise of agency". [THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md)
   makes the mechanism the agent's own only once she has taken
   responsibility for it. Each names a historical fact. The question is
   which of these facts could be recorded, and by whom.

## Why the record needs it

- **[CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md)** becomes testable. If no present-structure fact separates
  the cases, explanation of efficacy and ownership come apart exactly as it
  says.
- **[CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)** (Deferred) is defeated by "a manipulation case that
  satisfies every condition of integration and answerability". This
  question asks for that case's structure on a line that is active.
- The analyses of manipulation now filed unread, Noggle ([LIT-tmpb2z9y](../literature.d/LIT-tmpb2z9y.md)),
  Susser, Roessler and Nissenbaum ([LIT-tmp4ihbb](../literature.d/LIT-tmp4ihbb.md)) and Coons and Weber
  ([LIT-tmp5elg6](../literature.d/LIT-tmp5elg6.md)), say what makes an influence manipulation, as against
  persuasion or coercion. The four-case argument assumes that, and the
  record has not stated it.

## What the record holds

- **[THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md):** present-structure conditions are met by the manipulated
  agent, and no historical condition in the readings is shown to escape.
- **[THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md):** controlling conditions of acquisition produce
  introjection. This is empirical, from self-determination theory.
- **[THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md):** ownership of the mechanism through taking
  responsibility, a historical condition the four-case argument targets.

## What would answer it

Either a feature of will-organization that differs between the
manipulated agent and its unmanipulated twin. Or an argument that none
can, together with a statement of what a record of the will's formation
would have to contain.
