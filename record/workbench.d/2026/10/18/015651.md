*Follows the four reconstruction entries of 2026-10-18 (U1–U47), the same
contribution, before merge.*

The owner reviewed the filing. They judged its strongest parts to be "the
treatment of ordinal frustration, its constrained optimization refinement,
the individuation-sensitive account of relief, and the distinction between
non-dominating integration and cheap conformity". They also found
"several issues that matter to the substance of the theory, not just
presentation". The entries are unmerged and stay at version 1. Each
correction is stated in the entry it changes, and listed here.

**Two foundational corrections.**

- **Ordinal frustration was trivially satisfiable** ([CLAIM-tmp55mls](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp55mls.md),
  [TERM-tmp9lmlt](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmp9lmlt.md)). The primary definition took A37's acceptance set
  A_i = {u ∈ U : u ⪰_i u₀}. The owner: "if u₀ is an admissible
  transformation and ⪰_i is a preorder, reflexivity guarantees u₀ ∈ A_i
  for every process", so ⋂ A_i ⊇ {u₀} and "Every collection of demands
  becomes trivially compatible." The worked examples were unaffected,
  since they use strict or threshold sets, but the construction is the
  one several entries depend on. Acceptance sets are now stated as
  requirements that can exclude the baseline: strict improvement
  (u ≻_i u₀), a required level t_i, or a context-indexed obligation.
  Weak Pareto improvement is a condition on the whole collection (at least
  one demand improves strictly) and is not an intersection of per-demand
  sets, so the term says it falls outside the complex. The first filing
  repeated A37's definition without checking it against reflexivity.
- **Practical synchronization was filed as generalizing [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md)**
  ([TERM-tmpnfj83](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpnfj83.md)). The first filing followed A45 in calling [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md)'s
  sense "the special case captured by the previous definition". The
  owner: "That inclusion does not follow. [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) describes agreement in
  rankings without requiring coupling. The new term requires actual
  coordination through interactions. Processes might agree about what
  should happen while lacking any working mechanism to achieve it.
  Conversely, processes can coordinate effectively while maintaining
  different rankings." The term now sets out the owner's two dimensions:
  valuational concordance against practical coordination, with
  "agreement without effective coordination" as the cell that makes them
  independent. The question whether [TERM-tmpnfj83](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpnfj83.md) should supersede [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) is
  withdrawn: neither generalizes the other, and both stay Active. The
  notes on [TERM-050](../../record/terms.d/TERM-050.md) and the U35–U47 entry are corrected to match. The owner
  noted that this also meets [CLAIM-163](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)'s objection more directly, since the
  mechanism that objection asks for belongs to the coordination dimension.

**Five narrowings.**

- **[CLAIM-tmpmr1t8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmr1t8.md)** stated the owner's escalation thesis unconditionally in
  its title while its body held A45's conditional form. Retitled, in the
  owner's suggested wording: "learned local accommodation can reduce the
  amortized cost of recurrent arbitration". The owner: "Local negotiation
  and maintaining specialist relationships are not free."
- **[CLAIM-tmpf67p3](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf67p3.md)** said every demand was "locally satisfied" when every
  gradient vanishes. That is joint first-order stationarity only, which a
  maximum or saddle also has. The title and the body's definition now say
  so.
- **[CLAIM-tmpp9ox0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpp9ox0.md)** said security and self-governance "trade off". The
  owner: the discussion supports "that safeguards can suppress legitimate
  revision, not that security necessarily competes with autonomy. Some
  mechanisms may improve both." The claim is now conditional and separates
  adverse trade-offs from complementary safeguards (independent audit,
  [CLAIM-342](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-342.md); critic without control, [CLAIM-281](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)). Its defeater was rewritten
  to fit.
- **[CLAIM-tmpqofon](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqofon.md)** said "Authenticity is ..." non-domination. Retitled as
  "a candidate constituent of authenticity ..., not a sufficient criterion
  of ownership", as A45 had put it. Its defeater no longer counts an
  installed arrangement as a defeat. Such a case shows insufficiency,
  which the claim concedes, and the manipulation problem stays open
  ([CLAIM-tmptg7io](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptg7io.md)).
- **[TERM-tmp9b3gk](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmp9b3gk.md)** gave A18's third realist condition, constraints on
  admissible perspectives, only in its body. The title, summary and the
  first line of the definition now say it is unmet, "to avoid giving the
  impression that normative realism has already been established".

**What the review found missing.** The owner: "The contribution is strong
as a genealogy of claims, but weaker as an integrated account of our
resulting position." They asked for "a short synthesis entry, probably a
manuscript-position note rather than another standalone claim". It is the
next entry, the position note of 2026-10-18. It includes the owner's
request to make "context-sensitive recoverability of a specialist's
influence" explicit.

**What the review confirmed.** The owner found the minimax-duality
derivation of [CLAIM-tmpue67e](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpue67e.md) "correctly formulated under its stated
finite-dimensional convex assumptions". They found that the
reparameterization argument of [CLAIM-tmpuia0a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpuia0a.md) "preserves the essential
distinction between coordinate invariance and optimizer-dependent
dynamics".

**A second review, before merge.** The owner read the revisions and found
five more points, "more about making the theory precise, preventing
accidental overcommitment, and ensuring the record remains usable".

- **[CASE-tmplvjrp](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmplvjrp.md)** said the tasks "conflict: g_1ᵀg_2 < 0". That
  contradicted [CLAIM-tmpuia0a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpuia0a.md), which shows the sign is not invariant and
  does not exclude common descent. The case now calls it "a negative
  Euclidean inner product ... in the stipulated parameterization", a
  heuristic indicator, and separates it from the test of frustration
  against declared acceptance conditions. The same caution is added where
  [CLAIM-tmpw4w7a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw4w7a.md) and [QUESTION-tmp124n3](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp124n3.md) quote the inequality. The owner:
  "the contrast between superficial gradient conflict and genuine
  organizational frustration is one of the manuscript's main technical
  contributions."
- **[CLAIM-tmpf67p3](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf67p3.md)** still called stationarity "necessary, not sufficient"
  for reconciliation. The owner's counterexample: a threshold demand
  L(θ) = θ² ≤ 1 is satisfied at θ = 0.5, where ∇L = 1. The body now
  keeps stationarity (derivatives), compatibility (the joint existence of
  acceptable outcomes) and satisfaction (whether the selected outcome
  meets the requirements) apart. It says stationarity is neither
  necessary nor sufficient for satisfaction, and leaves satisfaction to
  the ordinal account.
- **[TERM-tmpnfj83](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpnfj83.md)** said its definition excluded conditioned conformity.
  The owner: "I don't think those requirements exclude it." Conditioned
  regimes can sustain compatible activity through local interaction with
  no active supervisor. The term is now explicitly descriptive.
  Conditioned conformity can count as practical synchronization and fail
  non-dominating integration ([CLAIM-tmpqofon](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqofon.md)), which preserves "the possibility that two
  systems are equally synchronized but differ profoundly in their
  autonomy".
- **The position note** set Chesson's stabilizing mechanisms against
  equalizing ones. The owner: "The contrast is too sharp." On Chesson's
  account stabilizing mechanisms are needed for stable coexistence, and
  equalizing ones can also contribute, by shrinking fitness inequalities
  that would otherwise overwhelm the stabilizing differences. The note
  now pairs complementary opportunities for consequential participation
  with the prevention of exclusionary authority disparities. The same
  correction is made in the [CLAIM-279](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-279.md) note.
- **[CLAIM-tmpmr1t8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmr1t8.md)**'s defeater still let one negative comparison refute a
  claim that savings can occur. It now asks for a showing, over a stated
  class of conflicts that admit accommodation, that the savings never
  occur. The owner: "defeat conditions should test the actual proposition
  rather than a stronger, disclaimed version."

The owner also asked that the position note not present its account "as
though it were one necessary developmental progression", but as "a
network of possible organizational dependencies and transformations".
They asked that its central position "emphasize an organization of
differentiated processes whose effective relations can themselves be
maintained and revised, rather than imply that every organism, learner,
or institution necessarily develops toward the same form of
authenticity". The note is rewritten to say so. It now lists the
dissociations among its items: differentiation without prior
frustration, synchronization without a cultivating governor, and
resolve-like governance without non-domination.
