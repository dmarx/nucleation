# LIT-2373: Does AI undermine democratic deliberation? How engagement algorithms hijack epistemic agency

John Dorsch, Mark Coeckelbergh and Tillmann Vierkant (2026). "Does AI undermine democratic deliberation? How engagement algorithms hijack epistemic agency." *Synthese* 208, article 164. DOI-10.1007/s11229-026-05820-6. Open access, CC BY 4.0.

## Key takeaways

- "the real filter bubbles are not bubbles of exposure, but bubbles of
  asymmetric credibility and evaluative framing" (p. 11).
- "fluency does not make opposing claims more persuasive; it makes their
  rejection feel more epistemically warranted" (p. 10).
- "Our claim is straightforward, though not yet directly tested
  empirically" (p. 9).
