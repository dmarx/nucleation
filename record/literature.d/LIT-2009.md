  from strokes, sub-strokes drawn from a learned library of primitives,

  as images. Its priors are fit to 30 background alphabets. On 20-way

  erred 11.0% and 14.0%. In four visual Turing tests, judges picked BPL's
  productions from people's at about chance (49–59%). With five background

# LIT-2009: Human-level concept learning through probabilistic program induction

Lake, B. M., Salakhutdinov, R. & Tenenbaum, J. B. (2015). Human-level concept learning through probabilistic program induction. *Science* 350(6266): 1332–1338. doi:10.1126/science.aab3050.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of the main text, [NOTE-1542](../notes.d/NOTE-1542.md). Page numbers are the journal's.*

- **A generative model of generative models.** Types are programs, tokens
  their executions, images their renderings; priors over each level are
  learned "by developing hierarchical priors that allow previous experience
  with related concepts to ease learning of new concepts" (p. 1333).
- **Human-level one-shot classification.** "People were skilled one-shot
  learners, achieving an average error rate of 4.5% (N = 40). BPL showed a
  similar error rate of 3.3%" (p. 1335).
- **Lesions.** Without learning to learn (token level) or compositionality,
  11.0% and 14.0% errors (p. 1335); the type-level lesion left
  classification at 3.5% (SM §6.1).
- **Generation.** "For each visual Turing test, fewer than 25% of judges
  performed significantly better than chance." (p. 1336).
- **Prewired structure.** "Causal representations are prewired in our
  current BPL models" (p. 1337).

## Standing in the record

Cited by A's synthesis as a hierarchical probabilistic model of concept
learning. Its hierarchy is type, token and image, with the priors fit to
background alphabets as the level above. The record has no other reading
on human one-shot concept learning; [LIT-615](LIT-615.md) approaches concept learning by
active-inference structure learning, and the few-shot accounts of language
models ([THEORY-614](../theory.d/THEORY-614.md), [THEORY-251](../theory.d/THEORY-251.md)) concern machines that hold no explicit
generative model. Filed from it: [THEORY-1357](../theory.d/THEORY-1357.md).
