# QUESTION-tmpl1yih: By which dynamical transitions do the distinctions individuation needs come into being and pass away (instability that amplifies small differences, nucleation across a finite barrier from a locally stable state, bifurcation of the set of attractors, noise- or rate-induced tipping), and are a form's creation, its stabilization and its coming to have a constitutive role three separate achievements?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U14, after the scale-separation synthesis ([CLAIM-tmpmx2ql](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmx2ql.md)):
"does this elucidate other work we should account for? maybe in the
neighborhood of dynamics? bifurcation/catastrophe theory? far from equilibrium
dynamics? pattern formation?"

A14 separated routes by which structure appears, each with a held or
registered source:

- **Bifurcation.** In ẋ = μx − x³ the set of stable states changes at μ = 0.
  A14: "**Possibility formation:** Which organizations become dynamically
  admissible and stable? **Realization selection:** Which of those
  organizations does the system actually occupy?"
- **Instability and pattern.** A homogeneous state stable at k = 0 can be
  unstable at a finite wavelength, so "Interactions need not merely constrain
  the configurations of preexisting parts. They can generate the very spatial
  distinctions that later allow parts to be recognized" (Turing, [LIT-535](../literature.d/LIT-535.md);
  Cross and Hohenberg, [LIT-527](../literature.d/LIT-527.md)).
- **Nucleation.** "In classical nucleation, the initial state remains locally
  stable, and a sufficiently large finite fluctuation is required to cross an
  activation barrier", which differs from instability in onset, waiting times
  and the role of seeds (Langer, [LIT-tmpp5xml](../literature.d/LIT-tmpp5xml.md); Vekilov, [LIT-919](../literature.d/LIT-919.md), read in
  [NOTE-708](../notes.d/NOTE-708.md)).
- **Hysteresis and loss.** In the cusp, which form is present can depend on
  history, and a stable state can disappear (Thom, named). Tipping can be
  bifurcation-, noise- or rate-induced, and in the last "A persistent
  organization may be destroyed not because its equilibrium ceases to exist,
  but because it cannot reorganize fast enough" (Ashwin et al., [LIT-tmpj4ix1](../literature.d/LIT-tmpj4ix1.md)).
- **Localized structures.** Fronts pinned to a pattern's phase give a bounded
  patch that grows by cells while keeping its organization, "a persistent
  dynamical individual without being a fixed collection of particles"
  (Knobloch, [LIT-tmpcdunk](../literature.d/LIT-tmpcdunk.md)).

And it separated three achievements: "**The appearance of a new mode is one
event; its stabilization into a persistent organized pattern is another**". A
linear instability says which perturbations grow, not which pattern survives.
That is settled by nonlinear saturation, wavelength selection, defects and
boundaries. A third achievement is the constitutive role: "An attracting
pattern does not automatically become an independent individual; it may simply
be a useful macro-description."

## Why it is open here

The record's individuation claims are about forms already present. [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md)
says when a combined form is one system and that "it lasts as long as that
block persists". [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md) says the verdict is fixed once its criterion is.
[QUESTION-035](QUESTION-035.md) asks how long a form lasts, not how it begins. A14's routes bear
on both ends:

- A transient can leave a regime whose every eigenvalue is stable. Non-normal
  growth does so ([THEORY-724](../theory.d/THEORY-724.md); Trefethen et al., [LIT-932](../literature.d/LIT-932.md), read).
- A form can be lost while its frozen-parameter attractor survives
  (rate-induced tipping).
- A form can be present without a gap, by exact closure ([CLAIM-394](../claims.d/CLAIM-394.md)) or
  renormalization.

Each of these is a way for a form's persistence to come apart from what an
instantaneous criterion reads. The record reads Cross and Hohenberg only in
part: [NOTE-418](../notes.d/NOTE-418.md) is Skimmed, and its technical core on amplitude and phase
equations, defects and pattern selection, the part that answers "which pattern
survives", was not read.

## What would count as an answer

For a stated system with a form the record individuates, the transition by
which the form arose, the mechanism that stabilized it, and the change that
would end it, each shown separately. Then a case in which two of the three come
apart, a form created but not stabilized or stabilized without any constitutive
role, which would show that they are separate achievements and not one
described three ways.

## Note of 2026-10-27 (later): two readings

- **Toner and Tu 1995 is a worked case of creation and stabilization coming
  apart** ([NOTE-tmpte6av](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpte6av.md)). The αv − β|v|²v terms of their equation (1) are
  the normal form ẋ = μx − x³: a local speed appears when α changes sign.
  In two dimensions that bifurcation alone gives no persistent order; the
  linearized theory loses long-range order for d ≤ 2, as the equilibrium XY
  model does (p. 4). A separate mechanism, the convective nonlinearity,
  stabilizes it (pp. 5–9; [THEORY-tmpzaxy0](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpzaxy0.md), Proposed). The paper says nothing
  about a constitutive role; its "birds" stay in scare quotes, which bears out
  A14's caution.
- **Ashwin et al. 2012 corrects the question's gloss of rate-induced
  tipping** ([NOTE-tmp429c3](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp429c3.md); [THEORY-tmpksbqt](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpksbqt.md)). The attractor keeps its
  existence, stability and basin at every frozen parameter (§3.2.2). What
  bifurcates is "the ability to track the QSE" (p. 11), and the critical rate
  depends on the distance to the threshold as much as on relaxation. But
  "destroyed" is too strong: just below the critical rate the climate model
  "returns to the QSE after a transient" (fig. 7a), and in §3.3 the
  equilibrium is unique and globally stable. A form is lost for good only
  where something lies past the threshold. And rate can preserve a form: "B-tipping
  may be suppressed for fast enough variation of parameters" (p. 19).

## Note of 2026-10-27 (U15–U23): three routes, and composition

A23 §V sorted how interconnection produces structure into routes that bear on
this question's first half:

- **Suppression.** Dissipative coupling damps relative differences and leaves
  collective modes, which is consensus, synchronization and slow
  hydrodynamic modes ([CLAIM-tmpywecm](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpywecm.md)).
- **Amplification.** An instability selects and grows differences, which is
  Turing patterns, bifurcation and nucleation across a barrier. These are
  the routes this question already lists.
- **Frustration.** Constraints that are satisfiable pairwise but not around
  a cycle leave a residual the system has to distribute. Cycle holonomy in
  gauge-coupled phase models (Torres-Hugas et al., [LIT-tmpre2ri](../literature.d/LIT-tmpre2ri.md), unread) and
  the cycle-space residual of ½‖Dx − b‖² are the exchange's cases
  ([CLAIM-tmpdjt64](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdjt64.md)).

A23: "**We should not mistake consensus for the general mechanism of
individuation.**" Frustration is a route the question did not name. A form can
be stabilized by organizing differences that cannot all be reconciled, not
only by eliminating them.

Composition is a fourth way a form gets a role, and it bears on the question's
"constitutive role". A composite whose interior relaxes fast presents its
boundary with a constraint that no part has ([CLAIM-tmpose3y](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpose3y.md)). That is a role
acquired at the interface, without the composite being an individual.
