# LIT-tmpek1lj: The role of need fulfillment in relationship functioning and well-being: A self-determination theory perspective.

Patrick, H., Knee, C. R., Canevello, A., Lonsbary, C. (2007). The role of need fulfillment in relationship functioning and well-being: A self-determination theory perspective.. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 92(3), 434-457*. doi:10.1037/0022-3514.92.3.434.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-tmpi2z1s](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpi2z1s.md)).

- Need fulfilment by a partner tracks relationship quality; relatedness
  most.
- The partner's fulfilment adds to one's own (APIM, 66 couples).
- Autonomous relationship motivation carries the association, in a
  cross-sectional mediation.
- The three needs are not separated by the measure (.84–.96 corrected).
