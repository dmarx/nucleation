# LIT-1257: Renormalization-Group Analysis of Turbulence

Smith, L. M. & Woodruff, S. L. (1998). Renormalization-Group Analysis of Turbulence. *Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics* 30: 275–310. doi:10.1146/annurev.fluid.30.1.275.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A3 cited it as a warning: RG applied to turbulence is not uniformly Wilsonian, so exact filtering identities, rigorous bounds, perturbative RG and fitted closures stand at different levels of justification. Bears on [THEORY-202](../theory.d/THEORY-202.md). Yakhot & Orszag (1986) is not filed.
