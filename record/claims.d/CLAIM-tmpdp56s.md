# CLAIM-tmpdp56s: Power over a technological arrangement has at least three dimensions that can be held and distributed separately: operational power, over how it works; economic power, over who owns it and takes its benefits; and epistemic power, over the knowledge needed to understand, evaluate and challenge it; so widening one, by public ownership, consumer choice or technical literacy, does not widen the others

## The claim

A11 §5 of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597):

| Dimension | Central concern |
|---|---|
| Operational power | Who determines how the system works? |
| Economic power | Who owns the assets and captures the benefits? |
| Epistemic power | Who possesses the knowledge needed to understand, evaluate, and challenge it? |

> A society may democratize one dimension without democratizing the
> others.
>
> Public ownership does not guarantee transparency.
>
> Consumer choice does not guarantee meaningful control.
>
> Technical literacy does not guarantee economic power.

## Why it is a claim and not only a list

The table is a taxonomy, and the first filing left it as one. The sentence
after it is a claim: that the three dimensions vary independently. A's
examples each hold one dimension widened and another fixed: ownership
widened, knowledge not; choice among providers widened, control over how
the system works not; knowledge widened, ownership not. Each is a coherent
case, and together they show three of the six separations; the other
three (operation without ownership, knowledge without operation,
ownership without knowledge) are as easily stated. That is the claim's
warrant: interpretive, by cases, not by a model.

## How it sits with the record

- **[CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md)**: a contribution's admission, recognition, credibility and
  uptake are separable. Epistemic power, in this claim, is the capacity to
  make a challenge credible and taken up, which [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md) already splits.
- **[CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md)**: legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability are
  separable achievements of an organization. Epistemic power over an
  arrangement is what makes its failures legible to outsiders; operational
  power is what correctability needs. Ownership has no counterpart there.
- **[CLAIM-tmpwgiwq](CLAIM-tmpwgiwq.md)**: the accountability owed for an indispensable
  arrangement. This claim says accountability can be owed along three
  dimensions and met along one.
- A attributes the three dimensions to Mumford, Feenberg and Zuboff
  together. The record's readings of them ([THEORY-tmpvlt2n](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpvlt2n.md), Winner as
  Mumford's stand-in; [THEORY-tmpa59p3](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpa59p3.md); [THEORY-tmpbgf6v](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpbgf6v.md)) were not checked for
  the separation, and no relation is written.

## What it does not say

- Not that the three are all the dimensions of power.
- Not that a free arrangement must distribute all three equally. A: "A
  genuinely free technological civilization must address all three",
  which is A's programme, not this claim.
