# WORK-006: The Self Without a Sovereign

## About

The owner's essay, proposed 2026-10-22 as one of four 6,000-word essays
from the exchange "Graph Learning Applications" (A33), later folded into
organization-of-perspective: distributed agency, organizational
perspectives and the fiction of a single owner. Many functions credited to
a unified self are better explained by historically organized, partly
overlapping perspectives that can represent themselves as one owner
without that owner being a further executive entity; Clive Wearing as the
opening case

Until [ADR-067](../decisions.d/ADR-067.md) this work was the `works` vocabulary term `self-without-a-sovereign`.
