  Bansal et al. (2021), Does the Whole Exceed its Parts? The Effect of AI
  Explanations on Complementary Team Performance. Earlier studies found AI
  explanations raise human-AI team accuracy, but only where the AI alone
  beat the team, so the human was a drag. This study sets AI accuracy equal
  to human accuracy (84% vs ~85–87% on sentiment of beer and book reviews;
  65% vs 67% on LSAT logic questions) so that complementary performance —
  the team beating both — is possible. With ~100 MTurk workers per condition
  (1,626 in all), showing the AI's recommendation and calibrated confidence
  already yielded complementary performance on all three tasks. No
  explanation condition (highlighting the predicted class, both classes, an
  adaptive switch by confidence; AI- or expert-written) beat confidence
  alone. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
