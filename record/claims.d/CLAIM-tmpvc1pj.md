# CLAIM-tmpvc1pj: How fast an argument grows sets how it grows: a fast, prolific exchange branches and drifts, as a crystal grown far from equilibrium does, and a project memory stabilizes an argument partly by slowing its growth, not only by supplying sites

## The claim

A crystal grown slowly, close to equilibrium, is faceted. Its shape is
reproducible and does not depend on the seed's outline. A crystal grown
fast, at high supersaturation, loses its flat faces to an instability:
bumps grow faster than the surface around them, and the crystal branches
into dendrites. Their form records fluctuations in the growth conditions.

An exchange with a chat assistant can run at very high supersaturation.
Every reply offers more connections, terms and proposals than the turn
asked for. The translation exchange shows what the branching looks like:

- In 51 minutes on 2026-10-10 (U49–U56, workbench 2026/10/10/040457), the
  thesis moved twice, to [CLAIM-133](CLAIM-133.md) and then to the first form of
  [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md). At U50 a passing remark about FID was escalated into a
  THEORY, a new metric, three LITs, a CLAIM, a QUESTION and chapter
  placements, until the owner pulled it back at U51.
- Each of the two "crystallized arguments" (U38, U56) came at the end of a
  run of fast turns, and each lost material. The first lost it when it was
  drafted into C7 ([CLAIM-tmp5o4dd](CLAIM-tmp5o4dd.md)). The second, A203, dropped Tannaka and
  two open problems in the crystallization itself.

The record acts on growth rate as well as on nucleation. Filing between
turns is a pause. The second-occurrence rule ([CLAIM-tmp2jolp](CLAIM-tmp2jolp.md)) declines to
grow anything that has not come back. Lint fails a filing whose
references do not resolve. Each of these lowers the effective supersaturation,
so the argument faces over instead of branching.

## What it does not say

- It does not say fast exchanges are bad. Dendrites grow fast, and the
  branching is where new material is found. The claim is about stability,
  not yield.
- It does not say the physics carries over. Morphological instability is
  an analogy here, and the claim is that drift tracks pace. That can be
  tested without the physics.
- One exchange, and no slow one to compare it with. The comparison that
  would test it does not exist yet.
