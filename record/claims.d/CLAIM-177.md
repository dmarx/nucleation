# CLAIM-177: In the record's exchanges with collaborators who keep no memory, the argument repeatedly loses identity: concepts are redefined silently, objections and readings come back as though new, and the thesis changes without anyone arguing for the change

## The claim

[ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) drew the lesson from the will exchange: "What went wrong was
identity, not missing structure." In that exchange, "will" was redefined
five or more times. One worked case changed identity six times. The
permissiveness objection was re-derived six or more times, and readings
the record already held were recommended three times.

The translation exchange shows the same pattern, and it was found without
looking for it.

- The passive/active distinction ([CLAIM-004](CLAIM-004.md)) "kept being re-derived after the
  manuscript dropped it". The ordinary-control objection ([CLAIM-024](CLAIM-024.md)) was
  re-derived three times, with different criteria each time.
- The thesis changed four times, and the first two changes were never
  announced. [CLAIM-017](CLAIM-017.md) became [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), and then [CLAIM-133](CLAIM-133.md), without a turn
  arguing either move. Only at A203 was the change of question stated
  ([CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)).
- When the owner asked what to file (U48), the assistant offered "three
  genuinely new connections". Only one of them, TV Tropes, was new to the
  record. The proposed Yoneda THEORY duplicated [THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md), and the
  relativity analogy had been rejected as [ARG-004](../arguments.d/ARG-004.md) (workbench
  2026/10/10/040456).

None of these is a missing premise. In each case something already said
lost its identity between turns.

## What it does not say

- It does not say structure is never missing. In the final will manuscript,
  7 of about 30 claims had more than one independent line of support.
- It does not blame the assistant. A collaborator with no memory between
  sessions, or whose context has been compacted, can only re-derive. The
  owner reintroduced the relativity analogy after it had been rejected
  ([ARG-004](../arguments.d/ARG-004.md)).
- Two exchanges, both owner with chat assistant, are a pattern, not a
  measurement.

## Note of 2026-10-12: a filed instance on the translation line

PR [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/121) filed a further instance of this claim's third failure, a change
of thesis that no one argues. [CLAIM-247](CLAIM-247.md), granted and Active, finds
that "The bearer of communicative identity reversed", from an operational
object defined on observed responses to a kind that can outrun
observation, "and no turn argues the change". [CLAIM-211](CLAIM-211.md), also
granted, is a related finding: the manuscript's thesis and the October
thesis give rival answers, yet the record links them by `complements`.
Both are readings of the What Survives Translation? exchange, this claim's
second source. [CLAIM-217](CLAIM-217.md) later supplied an argument for the reversal
[CLAIM-247](CLAIM-247.md) records.

## Note of 2026-10-27: narrowed and promoted

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md)) narrowed this claim to what the
record observed, and promoted it. Version 1 also said that such an
argument fails at identity *rather than* at structure, in general. That
comparison was counted in one exchange, with one owner and one assistant
family, which this entry's own body called "a pattern, not a measurement".
It is now [CLAIM-tmpuhp12](CLAIM-tmpuhp12.md), Proposed, with version 1's defeater.

The observation has been made again since it was filed, in exchanges this
claim did not cite:

- In the exchange of 2026-10-17, A12 rebuilt the will essay without citing
  it ([CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md)'s note of 2026-10-18). [CLAIM-122](CLAIM-122.md) was re-derived three times
  without being cited.
- In the exchange of 2026-10-20, neither reply cited [CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md), or the
  [THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md) that grounded it, while restating both ([CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md)'s note of
  2026-10-21).
- Notes on [CLAIM-086](CLAIM-086.md), [CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md), [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md) and [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) record the same
  thing for the exchanges of 2026-10-13 and later.

Six exchanges, one owner and one assistant family. The title says that
and no more.
