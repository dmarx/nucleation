## Gaps filled (the assistant's, Proposed)

- **Whether the query–answer relation is a game (A50 §3).** It is
  strategic only when one prepared policy serves several queries through
  shared resources, or when query distributions are restricted
  ([CLAIM-1032](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1032.md), [ARG-230](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-230.md); checked on a three-query LP).
  [QUESTION-524](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-524.md) holds the non-adversarial forms.
- **What A50's anchor buys.** Measured compliance understates inadequacy
  unless κ is comparable to α+β ([CLAIM-1050](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1050.md)).
- **A correction to A50 §6.** With meanings fixed, a new question cannot
  change implications among demands, and a new answer can only remove
  them ([CLAIM-1171](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1171.md)).
- **A50's capability trap** has no model; [LEAD-218](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-218.md) proposes one.
- **A52's depth optimum.** If F is log-concave, the best depth never
  falls as the deadline lengthens. A bimodal F reverses this
  ([CLAIM-1203](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1203.md)).
- **"Cannot be recovered from separate frontiers."**
  - The frontiers give an outer bound and, along one way of posing, a
    sequential inner bound ([CLAIM-1025](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1025.md)).
  - The real reason is the shared budget, not A58's projection argument
    ([CLAIM-1115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1115.md), [ARG-221](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-221.md)). Its construction 4 qualifies the
    inner bound.
- **A54's nuclei model.** Upkeep is paid whether or not a nucleus is
  used. A sigmoidal growth rule gives a threshold and hysteresis
  ([CLAIM-1195](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1195.md)).
- **A52's "slow modes amortize"** is narrowed to the economic sense
  ([ARG-238](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-238.md)).
- **Authorization by domain** breaks A56's evidence–permission circle by
  changing the unit of authorization ([CLAIM-1016](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1016.md)).
- **A60's coverage model.**
  - It presupposes a selector that can tell questions apart, so it
    reverses under parallel attempts and a shared budget, and it reduces
    demand uncertainty to the mean ([CLAIM-1042](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1042.md), [ARG-222](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-222.md)).
  - A60's "transient variety" is not Zollman's transient diversity
    ([ARG-226](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-226.md)).
  - When diversity tracks richness is open ([QUESTION-529](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-529.md)), with
    readings for it in [LEAD-232](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-232.md).
- **A62b's illustration.** Under its own metric, consistently wrong
  responses cannot be highly diverse ([CLAIM-1118](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1118.md)).
- **A64b's response kernel**, compared directly, is not observational.
  The bisimulation metric repairs it ([CLAIM-1095](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1095.md), objecting to
  [CLAIM-1156](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1156.md)).
- **A62b's test** has its design in [LEAD-229](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-229.md).
