# CLAIM-014: Krifka's performative updates fix which changes in conversational standing count, and Blackwell comparison tests whether a rendering preserves the information needed to recover them

## The claim

A93 §5: "Krifka tells us which changes in conversational standing count as
performative updates; Blackwell gives us a way to compare whether different
utterances preserve the information necessary to recover or predict those
updates." It "could become one of the manuscript's distinctive theoretical
integrations."

## Where it went

Krifka (2024) was named closest prior art from A33 on ([CLAIM-023](CLAIM-023.md)), cited in
C6's introduction, and ranked ninth in A93's priorities. The manuscript drops
him. It keeps Blackwell over a task family Q ([CLAIM-050](CLAIM-050.md)) whose tasks ("infer whether
a statement licenses criticism or shared laughter") are performative updates in
all but name. The pairing would give Q a principled source. It would also do
the work of the dropped performative fidelity ([CLAIM-054](CLAIM-054.md)).

## Krifka, read

Krifka ([LIT-835](../literature.d/LIT-835.md)) gives "change in conversational standing" a precise
object: the propositions an update makes true. Brandom's deontic score
([LIT-794](../literature.d/LIT-794.md)) is a perspectival alternative. Neither offers observables, so
this claim's defeat condition is untouched.
