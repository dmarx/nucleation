The owner asked whether the record had an account of contextuality. It had the
works: five were read in full. What it lacked was the account: no tag to find
them by, and nothing connecting the two formal threads they form.

The owner asked for the tag, and for the threads to be brought together with
theory entries. [ADR-012](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-012.md) adds the tag. A research agent read the five works'
notes and, where needed, the texts. It drafted the claims below and seeded the
four bridging works the record lacked:

- Spekkens (2005), the bridge between the two notions ([LIT-266](../../record/literature.d/LIT-266.md));
- Abramsky, Barbosa & Mansfield's contextual fraction ([LIT-265](../../record/literature.d/LIT-265.md));
- Budroni et al.'s 2022 review ([LIT-263](../../record/literature.d/LIT-263.md));
- Dzhafarov, Zhang & Kujala on behavioural and social data ([LIT-264](../../record/literature.d/LIT-264.md)).

## The account in seven claims

| claim | status |
| --- | --- |
| [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md): Kochen–Specker contextuality is the failure of context distributions to glue into one global distribution. Signed ones always exist, so negativity is no marker. | Active ([LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md), read) |
| [THEORY-016](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-016.md): generalized noncontextuality is equivalent to a positive quasiprobability representation, which is a rigid, exact frame. | Active ([LIT-003](../../record/literature.d/LIT-003.md), read) |
| [THEORY-015](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-015.md): Kochen–Specker noncontextuality is measurement noncontextuality plus outcome determinism, and preparation noncontextuality forces the determinism. Every Kochen–Specker proof therefore refutes the universal notion. | Proposed (Spekkens 2005, skimmed) |
| [THEORY-011](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-011.md): the generalized notion is strictly broader: a qubit is already preparation-contextual. | Proposed (skimmed) |
| [THEORY-014](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-014.md): in both formalisms, classicality is positivity of a representation that always exists over the reals. | Proposed (the record's inference joining two read theorems) |
| [THEORY-013](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-013.md): behavioural and word-meaning data published as contextual show none, once context-dependent marginals are separated out. | Proposed (skimmed; a working hypothesis of its authors) |
| [THEORY-010](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-010.md): Grangier & Auffèves's "contextual objectivity" is an ontological postulate, not contextuality in either formal sense. | Proposed (the record's placement) |

**The shape.** The first two claims are the two threads, each proved in a work
the record has read. The third and fourth are how the threads relate:

- the Kochen–Specker notion sits inside the generalized one;
- the containment is strict.

Both rest on Spekkens (2005), the one source that states the link, which is
seeded and not yet read.

**Inferences.** The fifth claim notices that the threads share a
mathematical shape; it is marked as the record's inference. The sixth takes
the topic outside physics. The seventh places a sixth work, Grangier &
Auffèves, that uses the word differently.

**Relations.** The third claim extends the first two, the fourth extends the
first, and the fifth extends the first two.

## What was not claimed

- **Restricted fragments.** Spekkens's proof that preparation
  noncontextuality forces outcome determinism uses quantum theory's full
  preparation set. Whether it holds inside a stabilizer or interferometric
  fragment is open. That bears on Catani et al.'s unproved claim that its toy
  theory is noncontextual.
- **Sheaf ⇄ GPT.** Whether a sheaf-noncontextual empirical model yields a
  noncontextual model of the corresponding GPT fragment is not addressed by
  either work.
- **Representation learning and retrieval.** No held or seeded source
  connects contextuality to the kernel THEORY documents or to van Rijsbergen
  ([LIT-262](../../record/literature.d/LIT-262.md)). One observation is recorded as an open question: measurements
  that are all functions of one random variable always have a global
  section. So linear probes on one fixed representation cannot be
  Kochen–Specker-contextual. Any contextuality in a learned system would
  have to come from the representation changing with the context. That is
  the record's reasoning from [LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md) Prop 3.1, not a published result.
- **The contextual fraction.** It would extend the first claim as a graded
  measure. It is left as a candidate until its supplementary proofs are read.
