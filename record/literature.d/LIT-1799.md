# LIT-1799: TyDiP: A Dataset for Politeness Classification in Nine Typologically Diverse Languages

Srinivasan, A., & Choi, E. (2022). TyDiP: A dataset for politeness classification in nine typologically diverse languages. *Findings of EMNLP 2022*, 5723–5738.

Read in full in [NOTE-1339](../notes.d/NOTE-1339.md).

## Why this is here

A5 offered it as a measurement resource for [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md)'s Stage 0, not as a definition of fidelity: politeness labels across languages, with their reliability limits.

The reading bears this out: TyDiP does report human agreement, which makes it a reference for a Stage 0 reliability target, but its annotators were screened for agreeing with a model and no judge rated twice ([NOTE-1339](../notes.d/NOTE-1339.md)).

## Key takeaways

- Human agreement is reported: 0.33–0.60 by language, pairwise Spearman over 15-item tasks; no test–retest ([NOTE-1339](../notes.d/NOTE-1339.md)).
- The same people agree less in their second language, in eight of nine languages.
- Annotators were kept for agreeing with a classifier, so its agreement and human-accuracy figures are upper estimates.
