On 2026-11-20 the owner shared the reference list of a 2026 interview with
Michael Tomasello, sixteen works with timestamps, and asked to read and
file any not already on the record.

## Where the record already held it

Four of the sixteen: A Natural History of Human Morality (LIT-504,
Unavailable), Lewis's Convention (LIT-473, Unavailable), Dennett's
"Intentional Systems" (LIT-440, Active), and Tomasello and colleagues'
"Two Key Steps in the Evolution of Human Cooperation", filed in the pull
request this one is stacked on (LIT-tmpsh1xk). The other twelve were not
held (greps of titles, DOIs and authors).

## The owner's rulings
