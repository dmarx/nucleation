# LIT-tmpks817: The when and how of planning: Meta-analysis of the scope and components of implementation intentions in 642 tests

Sheeran, P., Listrom, O. & Gollwitzer, P. M. (2024). The when and how of planning: Meta-analysis of the scope and components of implementation intentions in 642 tests. *European Review of Social Psychology* 36(1): 162–194. doi:10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpauwdm](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpauwdm.md). Page numbers are the journal's.*

- **Reliable but small.** Pooled d = .36 over 642 tests; Egger's
  b = 1.06; RoBMA d = .15 with tau = 0.27. "Thus, implementation
  intentions appear to have a robust impact on outcomes though the effect
  size is small according to conventional guidelines" (p. 170).
- **Broad scope, uncorrected.** Behaviour .27, cognitive performance .47,
  affect regulation .66, social judgement .52 (Table 1); lab .49, field
  .27 (Table 2).
- **The link matters.** Contingent if-then plans .43, schedule plans .29
  (Table 3, p. 173).
- **Do not revisit the plan.** Rehearsal once .45 against .33; recording
  .31 against .44; "when participants had the opportunity to revisit their
  plans (e.g., because they retained copies), those plans were less
  effective" (p. 180).
- **Motivation gates it.** High against low motivation d = .79 (k = 8);
  for physical activity, planning "had no effect when intentions were
  weak" (p. 177).
- **Out of date.** The search ended in September 2019 (p. 168).

## Why this is here

The exchange cited it at turn U10 as "a 2024 meta-analysis covering 642 independent tests" of implementation intentions. The abstract confirms the count and adds what the exchange did not use: the effect depends on the if-then format, on motivation and on rehearsal. The print issue is 2025.

**Corrected 2026-10-24, from the reading.** The abstract's range
(.27 ≤ d ≤ .66) is uncorrected for publication bias, which the authors
find substantial; their bias-corrected estimate is d = .15. For
[CLAIM-tmp0qupq](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0qupq.md) the review confirms that if-then links of situation to
response change behaviour, adds that the conditional link and not the
schedule carries the effect, and does not test what happens when the
planned response becomes the wrong one. Filed from it: [THEORY-tmpk5znk](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpk5znk.md)
(the effect and its bias), [THEORY-tmpbhjiw](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpbhjiw.md) (format, rehearsal and
recording), and a share in [THEORY-tmp8y1fr](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp8y1fr.md) (goal-dependence).
