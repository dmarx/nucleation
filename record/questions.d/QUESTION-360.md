# QUESTION-360: When does a learned distinction become a concept rather than a discriminator or a habit (by being reused systematically, transferring to counterfactual cases, integrating with other capacities, or being correctable), is the transition graded or categorical, and how is a concept told apart from the organization that realizes it and from the thing it is about?

## Why it is a question

The record had no term for "concept" ([TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) is now a Proposed
candidate). The exchange supplied three working definitions.

- **A4:** a concept as discrimination, inferential role, relational
  organization and developmental realization, plus grounding. "A change in
  the local learning coefficient is not itself concept formation."
- **A5:** "a reusable, corrigible organization of distinctions", marked by
  aboutness, counterfactual generality and correctability, set against a
  thing, an individuated organization. Both are roles, not substances.
  "Being an effective constraint is not sufficient for being conceptual."
- **A6:** "entity" as the umbrella, "a distinct bearer of properties,
  relations, or identity within a specified domain", with thing,
  individual, object and concept below it. "Neither identification nor
  causal influence automatically implies constitution."

What they share is that a concept is realized by an organization and is
not identical with it, and that it is about something it is not. What they
leave open is the transition, which is this question.

The record's own pieces:

- A5's three "events" (ontic individuation, conceptual individuation,
  constitutive conceptualization) are [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s descriptive, dynamical
  and constitutive senses of individuation, renamed.
- Type and token is [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)'s communicative kind and token.
- A group entity against the category of its members is [TERM-096](../terms.d/TERM-096.md).
- That a feature is not yet a concept bears on [THEORY-965](../theory.d/THEORY-965.md) (features as
  units a model computes with) and on [NOTE-1186](../notes.d/NOTE-1186.md) (token-specific features
  becoming abstract grammatical ones over training).
- Barrett's emotion categories, grouped by the perceiver's concepts
  ([THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md)), are the record's case of a concept that partly constitutes
  its object. Searle's status functions ([THEORY-625](../theory.d/THEORY-625.md)) and Epstein's grounding
  and anchoring ([THEORY-487](../theory.d/THEORY-487.md)) are the institutional case A5 reached for with
  *legal person*.

## Corrections to the exchange

- A5 used [CLAIM-476](../claims.d/CLAIM-476.md) for representational apparatuses in general and as
  backing for *legal person*. That claim is restricted to a record's
  objects, "and only for them".
- A6 read [CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md) as the general thesis that entities have no
  independent identity. It is about communicative acts and their
  participants.

## What would count as an answer

Criteria that separate concept, discriminator and habit, each measurable
in a learning system: reuse across tasks, transfer to counterfactual
inputs, integration with other capacities, and correction by evidence.
Then a system where they can be tracked over training ([QUESTION-275](QUESTION-275.md)'s
setting), to see whether they arrive together or apart. The candidate
term [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) would stand, split or be rejected on the result.

## Note of 2026-10-31: a negative control

At the owner's review, [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) version 2 took correctability out of
the definition of a concept. Conceptual corrigibility is now a further
achievement. The question's list of criteria keeps correction by evidence
as one property to track. The term no longer treats it as a condition of
being a concept.

A study of this question should include a negative control: a
representation that is transferable and conceptual by the other criteria,
but that the system using it cannot revise, such as a frozen component
reused across tasks. The record's working answer is that this is an
incorrigible concept, not something that has stopped being a concept. A
reason to answer otherwise would need to be shown.

## Note of 2026-11-07: measure the profile first, from "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

In the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07), A41 §11 proposed measuring a concept-in-perspective profile, of application rule, inferential role, response structure and update organization, before deciding whether two uses are one concept: "We can measure the differences first." A41 §12 added a vignette design (boundaries, inferences and revision, with the label and without, then longitudinal). Under [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md) the vignettes are do(I) interventions: they measure an interpreter's profile, not the classification's effects on those classified, which need field data. The question for rival uses of one expression is [QUESTION-439](QUESTION-439.md), which refines this one. The question stays Open.

## Note of 2026-11-19: the exchange "Topological protection explained"

A46 §4 offered levels of retained organization: episodic memory, reusable association or habit, generalized representation or procedure, adaptable learning architecture, reflexive learning organization. A called them "analytical distinctions, not a necessary progression", which leaves this question's graded-or-categorical point open. Its habit and generalized procedure are this question's habit and the reused, transferring side of a concept; it adds that the levels differ in "different kinds of future work that experience can replace". A's definition of learning that frames them is filed as [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md).

A73 §7's ladder (retained answer, reusable procedure, relational understanding, generative epistemic competence) comes with a test this question can use: a learner that memorized f(2) = 4, f(3) = 9, f(4) = 16 and one that found f(x) = x² answer alike, but on a shift to x² + c the second adapts from little evidence and the first relearns. That separates a discriminator from a concept by the cost of the next acquisition rather than by present answers. It is filed with the knowledge question, [QUESTION-493](QUESTION-493.md).

No status change.

## Note of 2026-11-22: the modular-addition run does not reach this question

LEAD-172 lists this question among those it advances. Its reduced run (CASE-tmpb4hg0) measured how early the task's couplings form and when the network generalizes to held-out pairs. Generalization to held-out pairs is a form of the question's transfer criterion. The run did not measure reuse across tasks, integration with other capacities or correction by evidence, so it says nothing on whether those arrive together or apart. One observation bears on whether the transition is graded. Under weight decay 2.0 the selection-rule coupling and test accuracy rose early and stalled at 0.64 to 0.86. The network held most of the distinction on unseen pairs without completing it. No status change.
