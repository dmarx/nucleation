# SYNTHESIS-031: The readiness line is two accounts, of keeping a rare capability and judging its worth and of reaching and paying for configurations under budgets, which meet at budget rules, slack and what a reserve costs; they are closed by relations and joined by mentions, and no link between them that no part states passes the claim test; when transient support can be withdrawn, once a third part, is a line of its own

*The account of the readiness line ("Readiness and slack", [ADR-073](../decisions.d/ADR-073.md)), over
the accounts of its two parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). It integrates every entry whose
`line` includes readiness after the split of 2026-11-10 ([ADR-087](../decisions.d/ADR-087.md)):
49 entries through the parts, and [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) directly. [SYNTHESIS-009](SYNTHESIS-009.md) and
[SYNTHESIS-010](SYNTHESIS-010.md) also carry the line; both are Superseded by the claims they
returned to ([CLAIM-632](../claims.d/CLAIM-632.md) and [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)), and they are not
integrated again. [CLAIM-600](../claims.d/CLAIM-600.md) is now in transient-support.*

## The parts

- **Keeping and judging ([SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md)), 25 entries.** Readiness is a
  probability of response to an imposed contingency ([TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md)), so use
  and cancellation counts do not show waste ([CASE-210](../cases.d/CASE-210.md), [CLAIM-561](../claims.d/CLAIM-561.md),
  [ARG-053](../arguments.d/ARG-053.md) Rejected); a capacity's value depends on the others it works
  with ([CLAIM-587](../claims.d/CLAIM-587.md)); records per capacity bound readiness and do not
  identify it ([CLAIM-632](../claims.d/CLAIM-632.md), Proposed); and a capability kept for rare
  events decays without practice outside operation ([CLAIM-558](../claims.d/CLAIM-558.md)).
- **Reaching and paying ([SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md)), 24 entries.** A transition has a
  threshold of its own ([CLAIM-571](../claims.d/CLAIM-571.md)); feasible, reachable and reliably
  reached come apart ([CLAIM-567](../claims.d/CLAIM-567.md)); obstructions have distinct remedies
  ([CLAIM-568](../claims.d/CLAIM-568.md), [CLAIM-556](../claims.d/CLAIM-556.md)); a form costs separately to build, keep and move
  ([CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)); several resources give a frontier ([CLAIM-585](../claims.d/CLAIM-585.md)); and a
  spend-matching budget loses transitions without a sign in output
  ([CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md)).
- **[CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md)** (the assistant's, demonstrated, Active), first line
  transient-support since the split: supply that does the party's work
  lowers the capacity it keeps. It keeps readiness because it bears on
  [QUESTION-386](../questions.d/QUESTION-386.md) ("Funding that rewards disbursing, or that pays for the
  work rather than for the organization doing it, selects substitution").

Withdrawing transient support, the third part of version 1, is the line
transient-support, with its own account ([SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md)).

## Whether the line falls into parts

The counts below are version 1's, over the three parts, and are the
record of why the line was split. Counting each pair of the 79
components linked by a front-matter relation or a mention in either's
body or summary, there are 163 pairs:

| | [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) | [SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md) | [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) | 50 | 28 | 5 |
| [SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md) | | 59 | 1 |
| [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) | | | 20 |

Of the 28 pairs across [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) and [SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md), 4 are front-matter relations
and 24 are mentions; 16 of the 28 involve a term of [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) ([TERM-107](../terms.d/TERM-107.md),
[TERM-110](../terms.d/TERM-110.md), [TERM-112](../terms.d/TERM-112.md), [TERM-113](../terms.d/TERM-113.md), [TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md)) or [CASE-210](../cases.d/CASE-210.md). Counted as
`scratch/formal/line_parts.py` counts, by stored relations only and
directed, 69 of 79 relations fall within a part: [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) 22 inside, 3 out,
6 in; [SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md) 25 inside, 4 out, 2 in; [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) 22 inside, 3 out, 2 in.
Twenty-one components link to no component at all: eighteen replication
questions in [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) and [QUESTION-434](../questions.d/QUESTION-434.md), [QUESTION-435](../questions.d/QUESTION-435.md) and [QUESTION-444](../questions.d/QUESTION-444.md) in
[SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md). So [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) is mostly closed by either count, and [SYNTHESIS-027](SYNTHESIS-027.md) and
[SYNTHESIS-024](SYNTHESIS-024.md) are closed by relations and joined by mentions. The line is a
candidate to split ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)), the support part most clearly; every
[SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) entry that links to another component already carries
realizability. The owner split it ([ADR-087](../decisions.d/ADR-087.md)): the support part
became transient-support, and keeping and reaching stayed together,
because they share 28 pairs, though mostly mentions, against 5 and 1
with the support part.

## What it does not say

- That the readiness argument applies to any real organization; its
  constructions and models say their organizational readings are
  analogies.
- That keeping and reaching are one inquiry; they are kept in one line
  because they are densely joined by mentions, not because a component
  says they are.
- Anything with standing of its own.
