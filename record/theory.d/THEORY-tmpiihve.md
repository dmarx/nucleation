# THEORY-tmpiihve: Crawford argues that skilled manual work is cognitively demanding and a ground of agency, and that work is degraded cognitively, by moving judgment out of the job into management or a system so that skilled workers can be replaced by cheaper ones, a method competition spreads across an industry and expert systems now carry into office work

## The account

Crawford argues that "the degradation of work is ultimately a cognitive matter": scientific management moves judgment out of the job; Ford "destroyed the possibility of an alternative way of working"; and the method is now "draining" office work "of its cognitive elements".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmpjk31w](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpjk31w.md). Corroborates [CLAIM-tmp6wuql](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6wuql.md)'s premise and [CLAIM-tmpbypt7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbypt7.md)'s competitive mechanism.

## What it does not say

Evidence on individual skill decay; the argument is historical.
