  Yang, Steinhardt & Hu (2023), Are Neurons Actually Collapsed? On the Fine-Grained Structure in Neural Representations, ICML 2023 (PMLR 202); arXiv 2306.17105. ResNet-18 trained on CIFAR-10 with labels merged into five super-classes reaches neural collapse on the coarse labels yet keeps the original classes as sub-clusters, recoverable without labels well enough that a linear probe on the recovered labels reaches over 93% on the ten-class test set. The sub-clusters form before the label-driven merging and persist to 1,000 epochs; they are weaker for similar sub-classes and absent in VGG.
