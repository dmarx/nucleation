# LIT-723: Making Things Happen: A Theory of Causal Explanation

James Woodward (2003), *Making Things Happen: A Theory of Causal
Explanation*, Oxford University Press — DOI-10.1093/0195155270.001.0001

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What the record holds of its account of
intervention comes from Woodward's Stanford Encyclopedia entry
([LIT-713](LIT-713.md)), which states conditions M1–M4 and cites this book as the
source of its own account. What it holds of invariance comes only from the
publisher's chapter abstract: a generalization is invariant if it is stable
in the right way under interventions, and laws are one kind of invariant
relationship but not the only kind. Chapter 6, "Invariance" (pp. 239–314),
is the argument the essay's §5.3 would need before claiming anything about
higher-level or organizational causes.
