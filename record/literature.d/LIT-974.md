# LIT-974: Neural Collapse Under MSE Loss: Proximity to and Dynamics on the Central Path

X. Y. Han, Vardan Papyan, David L. Donoho (2021), *ICLR 2022; arXiv:2106.02073*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpenm87](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpenm87.md).*

- **Collapse under MSE (Appendix A).** NC1–NC4 occur under MSE as under
  cross-entropy, on MNIST, FashionMNIST, SVHN, CIFAR10 and STL10 with VGG,
  ResNet and DenseNet (Figures 3–8). Activation collapse is faster under
  MSE than under cross-entropy (Figure 10). CIFAR100 and ImageNet are left
  out because MSE needs rescaling there.
- **The decomposition (Theorems 1–2).** L = L_NC1 + L_NC2/3 + L⊥_LS, where
  L⊥_LS = ½ tr{(W̃ − W̃_LS)(Σ̃_T + μ̃_G μ̃_Gᵀ + λI)(W̃ − W̃_LS)ᵀ} ≥ 0 measures
  how far the classifier is from least squares.
- **The central path is measured (Figure 2).** L⊥_LS becomes negligible
  from an early epoch, in all 15 cells. L_NC2/3 falls first, then the
  within-class term.
- **The derivation (Lemma 1, Proposition 2, Corollaries 1–2).**
  - With λ = 0, on the path, L = ½ Σ_j 1/(ω_j² + C) in the singular
    values of SNR = Σ_W^(−1/2)M̃.
  - Under the re-whitened flow, dω_j/dt = (1/N) ω_j/(C + ω_j²)².
  - So ω_j ~ (4t/N)^(1/4) for every j, with ratios tending to 1. That is
    NC1 and a simplex ETF, and NC3–NC4 follow.
  - The limiting orientation is set by the singular vectors at entry to
    the path.
- **Not shown.** That ordinary training is the re-whitened flow, anything
  for cross-entropy, or anything with weight decay.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-051](../questions.d/QUESTION-051.md), and read in [NOTE-tmpenm87](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpenm87.md). It is
held as the record's one derivation of collapse dynamics. That derivation
is filed as [THEORY-tmpqg4nq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpqg4nq.md), at the strength of its model: unconstrained,
re-whitened features under MSE with no weight decay. It answers part of
[QUESTION-051](../questions.d/QUESTION-051.md)'s first half, for MSE and not for the cross-entropy the
question names. It bears on [QUESTION-080](../questions.d/QUESTION-080.md): in this model collapse is a
power-law limit whose orientation is set at the start, not a fixed point.
Its MSE runs replicate the measurements of [THEORY-083](../theory.d/THEORY-083.md) ([LIT-618](LIT-618.md)) under a
second loss, with the same protocol.
