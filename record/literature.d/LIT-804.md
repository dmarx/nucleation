# LIT-804: Computational challenges to test and revitalize Claude Lévi-Strauss transformational methodology

Albert Doja, Laurent Capocchi and Jean-François Santucci (2021), *Big Data
& Society* 8(2), July–December 2021, 1–19 (special theme "Machine
Anthropology") — DOI-10.1177/20539517211037862

## Key takeaways

- **The aim.** "A generative analysis and a dynamic visualization of
  selected narratives, aimed at validating and revitalizing the
  transformational and morphodynamic theory" of Lévi-Strauss, using the
  DEVS formalism (Zeigler) in the authors' DEVSimPy environment.
- **The reading of Lévi-Strauss.** Myths "think each other in people's
  minds" and so behave like informal algorithms; variants form a group of
  transformations (terms and functions; operations of homology,
  inversion, opposition, symmetrization; codes read vertically like a
  score). The canonical formula f_x(a) : f_y(b) :: f_x(b) : f_{a⁻¹}(y)
  is read as a "double twist" with a third, external "boundary condition"
  (crossing a territorial, linguistic or social boundary), and placed in
  a lineage of catastrophe-theoretic and algebraic readings (Petitot,
  Maranda, Morava and others).
- **What is implemented.** A myth is an interconnection of mythemes in a
  DEVS coupled model; MythGen instantiates a reference myth (M1 Bororo,
  from *Le cru et le cuit*); TransformationADEVS generates a new myth by
  "operations which have to be performed", held as an attribute; the
  results are drawn as a graph of myths. This part is summarised from
  Santucci, Doja and Capocchi (2020, *Symmetry* 12(10):1706).
- **What is validated.** The 20 mythemes of a Corsican folktale, *U
  Lurcu* ("the ogre"), set around the Monte Revincu megaliths, are read
  from a text file with hand-entered coordinates and placed one by one on
  Google Earth through a KML file, with dynamic-structure DEVS models
  (MythVisu, PointMyth, SIGviewer). "Validation" means the software
  displays the narrative in order on the map.
- **The programme.** A "neo-structural model of canonical formalization"
  for identity transformations in US and EU politics, with Bayesian
  inference, probabilistic and Markov DEVS, clustering and maps;
  proposed, not begun here.
