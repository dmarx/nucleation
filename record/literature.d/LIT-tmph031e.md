# LIT-tmph031e: Extended Contact Effects: Is Exposure to Positive Outgroup Exemplars Sufficient or Is Interaction With Ingroup Members Necessary?

Cernat, V. (2011). Extended contact effects: Is exposure to positive
outgroup exemplars sufficient or is interaction with ingroup members
necessary? *The Journal of Social Psychology*, 151(6), 737–753.
doi:10.1080/00224545.2010.522622

Not read: a full text appears to be posted on ResearchGate, which this
client could not fetch (see the status note).

## Why this is here

The owner asked on 2026-10-29 to "find those new candidate readings". The
reading of Lemmer and Wagner's meta-analysis (LIT-tmpn3fwx, NOTE-tmpgbgvt)
named this work, and the curation entry of 22:38 listed it as possibly
the nearest within-study test of CLAIM-430's second defeater, that
information of equal favourable content, received without taking part,
changes as much as taking part; QUESTION-262's first contrast is the
same.

From the abstract and the indexed method text, it is not that test. The
"interaction" in its title is between two characters in a story, an
ingroup member and an outgroup member, not between the participant and
anyone. All three arms read; the contrast is whether the outgroup
exemplar's friend in the story is one of the reader's own group. It
bears on what kind of information works, not on information against
taking part.
