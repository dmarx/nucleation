# LIT-3001: Influence Dynamics and Stagewise Data Attribution

Lee, Smith, Adam & Hoogland (2025). ICLR 2026. arXiv 2510.12071.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A43 for how training examples' influence changes across developmental stages. It bears on the exchange's account of stagewise development.

Read on 2026-11-22 in [NOTE-2279](../notes.d/NOTE-2279.md); the reading is [THEORY-2191](../theory.d/THEORY-2191.md). The
first summary and [CLAIM-828](../claims.d/CLAIM-828.md)'s attribution are accurate as statements of
the paper's predictions and observations, with three cautions: the
theory is for the Bayesian posterior, and its link to SGD is the paper's
own stated gap; peaks are predicted only "under some conditions"; and
the language-model stages are matched to known transitions, not divided
by the LLC. Its toy orders the influence peaks by the modes' strengths,
so it does not test [CLAIM-828](../claims.d/CLAIM-828.md)'s order by dependency ([ARG-191](../arguments.d/ARG-191.md)). Its
A.2 names the Type-B transition (equal loss, falling LLC), a candidate
for [QUESTION-518](../questions.d/QUESTION-518.md).
