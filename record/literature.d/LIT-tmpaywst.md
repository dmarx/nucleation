# LIT-tmpaywst: Morality beyond the WEIRD: How the nomological network of morality varies across cultures

Mohammad Atari, Jonathan Haidt, Jesse Graham, Sena Koleva, Sean T. Stevens,
Morteza Dehghani (2023), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*
125(5), 1157–1188; preprint PsyArXiv q6c9r (2022) — DOI-10.1037/pspp0000470

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 in the morality-as-cooperation batch ([ADR-018](../decisions.d/ADR-018.md)). It is
MFT's current statement and instrument, and it extends Graham et al.'s
2013 chapter, [LIT-tmpa221k](LIT-tmpa221k.md), in the way that chapter said it might: one
"continent" turned out to be two, so Fairness becomes Equality and
Proportionality. The split is taken from Rai and Fiske's relationship
regulation, [LIT-tmpiuf8w](LIT-tmpiuf8w.md), which the paper credits. No anthology topic
holds it. It carries no instruction for machine-learning practice; the
MFQ-2 is widely used to probe language models, but that use is in other
papers, not this one.

**Its bearing on morality-as-cooperation**, set out in [NOTE-tmp3za6r](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp3za6r.md):

- It names the dispute. Curry et al. 2019 ([LIT-047](LIT-047.md), read in [NOTE-034](../notes.d/NOTE-034.md))
  "left out Purity as an important and less-WEIRD moral foundation" (p.
  64). The evidence offered is that Purity is the foundation most strongly
  associated with cultural distance from the U.S. and with religiosity.
- That evidence is weaker than the sentence. Purity is the only one of the
  six subscales whose item intercepts fail the paper's own invariance
  threshold (39.5% non-invariant against 25%), and the authors themselves
  say Purity means should be compared across groups "with caution" (p. 30).
- The MFQ-2 still has no item about returning a favour. The paper defines
  Equality as including "balanced reciprocity" (p. 60), but all six
  Equality items are about equal income or resources (Appendix). The
  measurement half of [LIT-047](LIT-047.md)'s charge that MFT conflates reciprocity with
  fairness therefore stands for the new instrument too.
- Its six foundations and MAC's seven still do not nest. The MFQ-2 adds
  Proportionality, which MAC's "fairness" (dividing disputed resources)
  only partly covers. It has no bravery or property scale: ownership is
  among the candidates the paper says it cannot yet measure (p. 64), and
  bravery is not mentioned.

[NOTE-tmp3za6r](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp3za6r.md) is the close reading of 2026-10-02, and it placed the work:
**Active**. It is the instrument any new MFT result will use, and its
cross-national design is a real advance on the MFQ-1's English-language
internet samples. Its populations are still online, educated and mostly
urban, as the limitations section says.
