# LEAD-022: Run the study of a fall in complaints: with working conditions fixed, measure separately whether members find them acceptable, see the discrepancy, are motivated and are able to voice it

## What it would do

Run [CASE-290](../cases.d/CASE-290.md): in a workplace where complaints fell with conditions
unchanged, measure acceptability, perceived discrepancy, motivation and
ability to voice separately.

Where the record names it:

- [CASE-290](../cases.d/CASE-290.md): "the five measures are [CLAIM-553](../claims.d/CLAIM-553.md)'s factors made measurable"

## What would count

A fall carried by lost ability or motivation to voice, with acceptability
unchanged, tells tolerance from satisfaction for [QUESTION-474](../questions.d/QUESTION-474.md) and supports
[CLAIM-553](../claims.d/CLAIM-553.md). A fall carried by acceptability counts against reading it as
manipulation.
