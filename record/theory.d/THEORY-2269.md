# THEORY-2269: Scaffolds can impose Darwinian properties first

## Source

Bourrat et al. (2024), [LIT-3137](../literature.d/LIT-3137.md), read in [NOTE-2362](../notes.d/NOTE-2362.md).

## What was actually shown

Nothing is computed. The paper argues for the externalist mode of
explanation, restates the patch model of [LIT-1940](../literature.d/LIT-1940.md), states endogenization
as a landscape condition (Figure 2), and proposes a constructive variant
in which cells' waste makes the patches. It asserts that "a boundary
shift between what is considered internal and external occurs during the
emergence of new levels of individuality".

## What this does not say

- That every transition starts from a scaffold; the authors say other
  routes, such as incomplete cell separation, exist.
- That the two-peaks condition is sufficient; Figure 2c needs the
  population close to the collective peak when the scaffold goes.
- How to tell, at a given time, whether a constructed scaffold is inside
  or outside the collective.
