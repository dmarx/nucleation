The owner reviewed LEAD-006 on 2026-11-19: building an environment
is one way to operationalize it; "we may be able to find prior research
that satisfies the experiment we're curious about". The lead was
rewritten as an investigation of task composition, reading first. The
owner then asked: "yes, run the task-composition search and read them".
One searcher verified identifiers and recommended eight works. Eight
readers then ran in parallel, each keeping running notes on the branch
claude/readers/task-composition (ADR-108). The filer rechecked every
quotation by script against the downloaded texts, and dates for the two
Nature Neuroscience papers against Crossref, without an email address.

**Finding.** No work runs the lead's full design. Its three parts are
covered separately, and LEAD-006's note maps the coverage:
- (a) later cost: Zhang, Goudar, Ito, Driscoll and Furuta;
- (b) organizations compared: He et al. only, not matched;
- (c) intervention: Zhang by transplant, Goudar by readout, and Maennel
  by covariance resampling.

Two readings cut against the lead's hope:
- Thomm et al.: mixing sub-tasks in gives little.
- Furuta et al.: transplanting g's own grokked module often raises the
  cost of h∘g.

Maennel et al. show that a memorization arm needs input-statistics
controls. The lead stays at stage idea: the combined experiment is not
in the literature found.

## What was left, and why

- **Candidates registered nowhere.** The search's other candidates were
  not registered or read, by the reading list's scope:
  - Xu et al. on embedding transfer for grokking;
  - Jain et al. on fine-tuning procedurally defined tasks;
  - Zhang et al. on initialization and reasoning against memorizing,
    which is a ready handle for matching organizations;
  - Gerace et al.;
  - Tse et al. on schemas;
  - Harlow's 1949 learning sets, whose content was not verifiable;
  - several theory papers.

  The searcher's notes on claude/readers/task-composition list them.
- **Gaps the readers named.**
  - Lepori, Serre and Pavlick 2023 (structural compositionality).
  - Wang et al. 2024 (grokking of implicit reasoning), which He et al.
    build on.

  Neither is held. Both are candidates for a later reading.
- **Bearings too loose to note.** THEORY-966 and THEORY-970 (uniqueness
  of subnetworks), CLAIM-519 (behaviour does not fix mechanism),
  QUESTION-360 and THEORY-984 (two-hop bridges), THEORY-1358, THEORY-2046
  and THEORY-2019 (learning to learn), THEORY-279, THEORY-2027 and
  THEORY-1353 (input statistics, plasticity), THEORY-022 (Fourier
  circuits), and CLAIM-647 and CLAIM-184. Each NOTE names its own.
- **Goudar's supplement.** The supplement sat behind a download
  challenge. It was not read, and nothing worked around the challenge.
