### Added

- **Works are documents, and a manuscript is anchored to the argument**
  ([ADR-068](record/decisions.d/ADR-068.md)). The `works` vocabulary becomes the WORK scheme ([WORK-001](record/works.d/WORK-001.md) to
  [WORK-010](record/works.d/WORK-010.md), the four folded essays Superseded by [WORK-005](record/works.d/WORK-005.md)). A manuscript's
  prose is SECTION documents whose paragraphs say in `ref::asserts` and
  `ref::discusses` directives what they stake on the record's claims. Each
  manuscript renders a reading copy and a ledger, and four patterns warn
  when a section drops a member, asserts outside its work, or asserts a
  Rejected or Superseded claim.
- The Individual and Its Conditions ([WORK-010](record/works.d/WORK-010.md)) in nine sections, the
  first manuscript held in the record.

### Changed

- `works:` on claims, arguments, cases and questions now holds WORK codes,
  and the per-work facet pages under `docs/claims/works/` and
  `docs/arguments/works/` are replaced by the WORK pages.
