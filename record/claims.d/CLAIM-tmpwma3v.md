# CLAIM-tmpwma3v: An expected-value-of-control effort-cost model fits cognitive-effort-discounting choices: the fitted cost of control rises with task load and the cost term is required, so for the modal agent the control intensity elected falls as its cost rises — the record's first fit of the cost direction, on a revealed-choice measure of intensity

## The claim

[CASE-tmprt0hl](../cases.d/CASE-tmprt0hl.md) fits the expected-value-of-control computation
([THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md), [LIT-585](../literature.d/LIT-585.md)) to 122 participants' cognitive-effort-discounting
choices (OSF vnj8x, CC-BY), modelling the net value of an option as its
reward minus an intensity-dependent cost of control, V(n, r) = r − c(n),
with choice by softmax. Three convergent results hold: the group-mean
subjective value of a control level falls monotonically with the level
(0.92 → 0.60 from 1- to 4-back); a per-participant fit gives a positive
marginal cost of control for three-quarters of agents (median 0.26 €/level);
and the model that prices control beats a reward-only null (likelihood-ratio
χ²(3) = 10.5, p = 0.015; AIC favours it). So the control intensity an agent
elects rises with the payoff on offer and falls with the cost of that
intensity — the cost direction [LEAD-128](../leads.d/LEAD-128.md) sought.

This gives [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) the cost-side evidence its `promote_when` (1) named
and the record did not hold, and so supplies the per-encounter price of
control that [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) (escalation costs because control is specified at a
cost) and [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) (flow is cheap because that cost goes unpaid)
presuppose. It corroborates them without their resting on it.

## What it does not say

- **Not that [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) is promoted.** The fit measures intensity by
  revealed choice of effort level, not by the accumulator parameter or
  conflict adaptation `promote_when` (1) names, and touches none of
  `promote_when` (2)'s dissociation of specifying from implementing. Whether
  the evidence suffices to promote the account is the owner's call; this
  claim changes no status.
- **Not a universal law of effort.** About a quarter of participants are
  effort-seeking, the source article's own need-for-cognition finding. The
  claim is about the modal agent and the group mean; the single-population
  pooled fit is weak (BIC favours the null).
- **Not the cost's form or origin.** Whether the cost of control is a fixed
  function of load, intrinsic or an opportunity cost, is left open, as
  [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) leaves it.
- **Not amortised supervisory cost.** This is single-shot effort pricing, not
  [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md)'s saving over recurring conflicts; it grounds the per-encounter
  cost that accounting assumes, not the recurrence.

The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the
owner's call.
