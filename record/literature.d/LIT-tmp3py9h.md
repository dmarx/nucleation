# LIT-tmp3py9h: Hasimoto Transformation and Vortex Soliton Motion Driven by Fluid Helicity

Holm, D. D., & Stechmann, S. N. (2004). Hasimoto transformation and vortex
soliton motion driven by fluid helicity. arXiv:nlin/0409040.

## Key takeaways

- The attribution: "Hasimoto [1972] discovered the fascinating map from
  vortex filament solutions of Euler's equations for ideal incompressible
  fluids in the Local Induction Approximation (LIA) to soliton solutions of
  the NonLinear Schrödinger (NLS) equation" (§1).
- The local induction approximation, for a tube of radius ε, "where ǫ is
  the radius of the thin vortex tube which our filament approximates"
  (eq. 4.4), with "log ǫ−1 is approximately constant" absorbed into time.
- Its own result: "soliton solutions of (4.12) yield LIA ω-filament
  solutions driven by helicity, and vice versa" (Theorem 4.1), the
  complex mKdV in place of the cubic NLS.

## Standing in the record

Filed as the second read neighbour of Hasimoto (1972), [LIT-2611](LIT-2611.md), beside
Van Gorder (2014), [LIT-2612](LIT-2612.md). On the reduction it is weaker than Van
Gorder: it names the map and the variable, and writes neither the cubic
NLS nor its soliton. It adds the thin-tube cutoff, which the record held
nowhere. Its own result, the helicity Hamiltonian and the complex mKdV,
is not used by the record's argument.
