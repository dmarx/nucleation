# LIT-tmp5i00q: Recommendation to remove Siemens AG from the watch list of the Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global

Council on Ethics for the Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global (2012) — https://files.nettsteder.regjeringen.no/wpuploads01/blogs.dir/275/files/2019/02/Siemens-av-listen-2012-ENG-endelig-sladdet.pdf

## Key takeaways

- **The verdict.** The Council "finds it unlikely that there currently is a
  higher risk of corruption in Siemens than in other comparable companies"
  (Section 1).
- **A costly refusal, under the monitor.** In Kuwait in 2011 an internal
  investigation "prevented the payment of the bribe"; three managers were
  dismissed, the SEC and the Munich prosecutor were notified, and "The
  incident resulted in Siemens losing the tender competition" (Section 4.2).
- **The stated reason for compliance.** Violations "may lead to external
  investigations being initiated, for example pursuant to the Foreign
  Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) or the United Kingdom Bribery Act. They may
  also lead to exclusion from projects from multilateral development banks,
  which will have major financial consequences for the company" (Section
  4.3).

## Standing in the record

A primary document, not an account: an outside body's assessment, used to
code Siemens's compliance system as a redesign. No THEORY is filed from it.
