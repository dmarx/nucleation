# ADR-013: A work may be held in both records when each reads it for its own question

## Context

[ADR-001](ADR-001.md) set the boundary between the two records:

- a work goes wherever a topic can hold it;
- "nothing is filed in both";
- a work that belongs on the other side moves there, as [LIT-064](../literature.d/LIT-064.md) did.

That rule assumed a work has one question worth asking of it. Some works have
two, one for each record:

- **The anthology's question** is what a paper implies for ML practice, or
  what it explains about ML systems.
- **Nucleation's question** is what the same paper means inside a line of
  mathematics, logic or retrieval theory that the anthology does not hold.

On 2026-09-27 the owner asked for exactly this case. Xiong's *The Lattice
Representation Hypothesis of Large Language Models* is held in the anthology
([ANTH-LIT-460](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-460.md)), read there for concept geometry. The owner wants it re-read
here against van Rijsbergen's *The Geometry of Information Retrieval*
([LIT-262](../literature.d/LIT-262.md)): formal concept analysis, Galois connections and lattices of
classes are that book's second chapter.

## Decision

A work may hold a LIT in each record when each record reads it for a question
of its own.

- **Each record's entry is its own reading.** Each has its own NOTE, status,
  tags and relations, filed by that record's rules. A work can be `Active` in
  one and `Proposed` in the other; the statuses answer different questions.
- **The entries name each other.** The nucleation LIT cites the anthology
  entry by code (ANTH-LIT-…), in its body and in its first history note. The
  anthology names nucleation works in prose, as it already does.
- **Disagreements are reported, not fixed across the boundary.** If a
  nucleation reading finds the anthology's entry wrong, the nucleation NOTE
  says so. The fix is then made in the anthology by that record's process,
  not edited from here.
- **The default is unchanged.** A work still goes to one record. A second
  entry needs a reason: a question the first record's reading does not ask.
  The curation entry that files the second entry states that reason.
- **Moving is still moving.** When a work simply belongs on the other side,
  it moves and the old entry is `Superseded`, as [LIT-064](../literature.d/LIT-064.md) was. Dual holding is
  for two readings, not for hedging about where a work belongs.

[ADR-001](ADR-001.md)'s "nothing is filed in both" is narrowed to "nothing is filed in
both without its own reading".

## Alternatives

- **Keep one entry and cite across.** This is the old rule. It fails when the
  second question needs a full reading, with its own NOTE, relations to this
  record's works, and THEORY sources, that the other record's LIT cannot carry.
- **Move the work.** That loses the first reading's standing in its own
  record, where it is doing work.
- **Mirror everything.** Rejected in [ADR-001](ADR-001.md) for the reason it gave: it would
  invite filing in the wrong place, and two readings of one work are only
  worth having when they ask different things.
