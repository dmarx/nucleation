*The owner noticed unusual structure on the lines-of-argument page and
asked whether the lines should be refactored. The answer became [ADR-040](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-040.md).*

**What the page showed.** pragmatic-transport held 188 of 262 claims and
113 of 153 `rests_on` edges. The other lines rested on it for general
results, not for its account of translation. That meant setting it aside
([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)) would retire theorems, and with them premises of
will-organization, distributed-agency and nucleating-argument.

**First draft: move claims to a new line.** The owner proposed instead
adding a value to `line:`. That turned out to be supported:

- luria reads a vocabulary field as a list when the scheme declares it
  `many: true` (`values_of` in `luria/contract.py`). A list without it is
  a lint finding that names the fix.
- The chains already treat a scalar as a set of one.
- The restriction was this record's configuration, not luria's.

Adding a value keeps every claim in the chains that use it, and needs no
title changes.

**The trap the sort found.** Several general-looking claims were filed
resting on translation theses: [CLAIM-056](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) on 106, 142 on 046 and 011, and
so on. Tagging those with `formal-apparatus` would let a claim outlive its
own premise under the set-aside rule. So the test for the second value has
two parts:

- the claim survives the retraction of every substantive thesis;
- it rests on no claim that would not.

The second part did the work. It moved 7 of the 31 candidates from "tag"
to "split".

**The splits.** Each group B claim was split in the same way:

- a new formal-apparatus claim states the general content, resting on
  nothing;
- the original rests on it and keeps its application, with a dated note;
- [CLAIM-189](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-189.md), 190 and 191 re-point their `rests_on` to the general forms,
  and their bodies' citations of the originals stand.

nucleating-argument's remaining premises in pragmatic-transport are
[CLAIM-034](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-034.md) and [CLAIM-002](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-002.md), which are its real dependence on the theory of
retelling.

**Still to check after merge.** The view generator (`docs-generate`) does
not run on pull requests. So the first rendering of claims with two lines,
in the claims index and the argument page, happens on main. Lint accepted
the plural field on the PR.
