# LIT-967: Evidence for the representation of non-hierarchical structures in language

Yngwie A. Nielsen and Morten H. Christiansen (2026), *Nature Human Behaviour* 10(3), 579–588.

Filed for [QUESTION-026](../questions.d/QUESTION-026.md): preregistered priming evidence that linguistic representation uses linear part-of-speech chunks, not constituents. It is a case against assuming that composition by grammatical constituents fixes how a whole utterance's observables are carried.
