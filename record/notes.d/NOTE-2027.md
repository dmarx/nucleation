# NOTE-2027: Parra Jounou and Tronto (2024) — Care ethics in theory and practice: Joan C. Tronto in conversation with Iris Parra Jounou

## Contribution

Tronto's own retrospective glosses on her concepts, including privileged irresponsibility.

## Key results

1. p. 279: "The danger of privileged irresponsibility is to cut off one’s reaction to others."
2. p. 282: "privileged irresponsibility is a kind of bad care".
3. p. 278, the Bellamy passage from Caring Democracy (2013, p. 44): "The effect of such a delusion
   in moderating fellow feeling for the sufferings of the mass of men into a distant and
   philosophical compassion is obvious." Tronto: "Once you climb into it, you pretend that you don’t
   know all those people pushing the carriage."
4. p. 280, caring with: "care becomes reliable, predictable, normal, shared, if we talk about care
   over time".

## Assumptions

Interview, not argument.

## Concepts

Privileged irresponsibility; bad care; caring with; attentiveness.

## Bearing on the record

- **Connections.** A stand-in, with Tronto (2018), for [LIT-2708](../literature.d/LIT-2708.md). It restates the concept in
  Tronto's words (results 1-2) and the book's moral stake, responsiveness to others. It does not
  restate Moral Boundaries' argument. It gives one passage of Caring Democracy (result 3) for
  [LIT-2669](../literature.d/LIT-2669.md).
- **[CLAIM-707](../claims.d/CLAIM-707.md), [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md)**: result 3 is privilege as not seeing those whose labour carries
  you, close to the invisibility the claims describe, but said of classes, not organizations.
- **The exchange** (A17 §2): its "privilege of not noticing" is close in spirit to results 1 and 3;
  the words are A's.

## Limitations

Two sentences on the concept; an interview.
