# LIT-951: Physics of Skill Learning

Ziming Liu, Yizhou Liu, Eric J. Michaud, Jeff Gore and Max Tegmark (2025), *arXiv:2501.12391* (preprint, under review).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-794](../notes.d/NOTE-794.md).*

- **The Domino effect is the optimizer's.** The Geometry model has loss
  Σ p_i L((θ − θ₀)·t_i) with orthogonal task vectors. Under SGD, task 2
  takes about 1/p₂ as long as task 1. Under SignGD it waits for task 1 to
  finish and then needs a constant extra delay: t₂/t₁ → 2. Adam lies
  between.
- **Rotation matters.** Take a hierarchical quadratic loss.
  - SGD behaves the same in any basis.
  - SignGD learns every direction together when the loss is aligned with
    the coordinates, and in Domino sequence when it is rotated.
- **The order is frequency order throughout.** The optimizer changes
  whether onsets overlap and how the n-th onset scales: ∝ n under sign
  descent, against ∝ n^α if the time is ∝ 1/p.
- **A one-parameter ODE.** The Resource model,
  du_i/dt = −η_eff p_i u_i/(Σ p_j u_j + N₀), fits the SignGD curves with
  N₀ ≈ 0.003. It conserves u_i^(1/p_i) exactly. Its strong-hierarchy
  limit is the strict-sequence Domino model.
- **Dependency is a second cause.** A two-layer MLP learning
  x₁⊕x₂, x₃⊕x₄ and x₁⊕x₂⊕x₃⊕x₄ starts the third only after the first two
  are perfect. An independent 4-parity is delayed more.
- **Scaling claims rest on fits.** At Zipf α = 1, the Geometry model
  gives α_N ≈ 0.34, which "happens to be" Chinchilla's value. The
  reweighting and optimizer proposals are machine-learning practice.
