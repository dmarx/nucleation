# QUESTION-054: Is there a governing level among an agent's regimes, a system that specifies control apart from the systems that carry it out, or is governance the interaction of the regimes themselves?

## Why it is a question

Three readings disagree, and no `rivals` edge marks it:

- [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md): "Emotion regulation is not a process separate from emotion
  generation: most of it is one motive state checking another, with no
  distinct regulating system above them."
- [THEORY-062](../theory.d/THEORY-062.md): which of model-based and model-free control wins "follows
  their relative reliability, not the preference of a supervising
  system".
- [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md): control is "decided by a cost-benefit computation ... in a
  specification system distinct from the structures that implement the
  control".

The will-organization line speaks of a governing level. [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)
separates the operative regime, "the governing organization among
regimes", and the capacity to transform it. [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) separates
maintaining effective action from maintaining "the capacity to govern
among regimes". If [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) and [THEORY-062](../theory.d/THEORY-062.md) are right, the governing
organization may be a pattern of interaction, not a system. If [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md)
is right, it may be a distinct one.

What would count as an answer: a dissociation between specification and
implementation that meets [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md)'s standard, a process change specific
to the specification system that precedes the outcome. Or a showing that
the line's claims need only a pattern of interaction, so that the
question does not bear on them.

## Note of 2026-10-12: typed partial answers

The THEORYs this question cites now carry `answered_by` edges. [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) and [THEORY-062](../theory.d/THEORY-062.md) answer on the interaction side, and [THEORY-153](../theory.d/THEORY-153.md) (Beer's distinct metasystem) on the distinct-level side, beside [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md). Beer's metasystem may be distributed, which bears on [THEORY-149](../theory.d/THEORY-149.md)'s durable governance without central control.
