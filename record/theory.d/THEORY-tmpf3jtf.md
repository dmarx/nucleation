# THEORY-tmpf3jtf: If every entity's O(d) frame may change independently and relation transports change as Tᵢⱼ ↦ gᵢTᵢⱼgⱼ⁻¹, then functions of the transports alone carry no invariant information except around cycles, a triangle's invariants are exactly the class functions of its holonomy H = TᵢⱼTⱼₖTₖᵢ, and an equivariant update of an edge from one cycle through it is Ψ(H)Tᵢⱼ with Ψ conjugation-equivariant; the symmetry fixes a family of updates, not one mechanism, and says nothing about whether a task has the symmetry

## Source

Sarkar, Mukherjee and Sato (2026), [LIT-tmpr8qf7](../literature.d/LIT-tmpr8qf7.md), §2 (pp. 2–3) and App. B
(p. 7) of the arXiv preprint 2610.37656v1; read in [NOTE-tmp8rf0b](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp8rf0b.md). The
premise is from the companion paper, [LIT-tmpbihhf](../literature.d/LIT-tmpbihhf.md), read in part in
[NOTE-tmp4j67k](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp4j67k.md).

## What was actually shown

**The setting.** Each edge of a graph carries Tᵢⱼ ∈ O(d) with
Tⱼᵢ = Tᵢⱼ⁻¹. A gauge transformation is an independent gᵢ ∈ O(d) at every
entity, acting by Tᵢⱼ ↦ gᵢTᵢⱼgⱼ⁻¹ (p. 2).

**No cycle, no interaction.** On an edge set with no cycle, gauge fixing
along a spanning tree sends every transport to I. So any invariant function
of the transports is constant, and an equivariant update of an edge
commutes, at the identity, with the residual diagonal O(d). By Schur's
lemma it is ±I, so U = ±Tᵢⱼ (p. 2). This holds for the transports alone.
With entity vectors, xᵢxⱼᵀ is an equivariant edge term with no cycle, as
the paper notes.

**Triangle invariants.** For a triangle, gᵢ = I, gⱼ = Tᵢⱼ, gₖ = TᵢⱼTⱼₖ send
(Tᵢⱼ, Tⱼₖ, Tₖᵢ) to (I, I, H), leaving only H ↦ gHg⁻¹. The orbits are the
conjugacy classes of H, and the continuous invariants are the class
functions, generated by tr Hʳ, r ≤ d (Procesi). For b cycles the invariants
are functions of (H₁, …, H_b) under simultaneous conjugation (p. 2). The
same move is [THEORY-1082](THEORY-1082.md)'s spanning-tree step, with U(1) replaced by O(d).

**The forced edge update (Theorem 1).** In the gauge (I, I, H) define
Ψ(H) = U(I, I, H). Equivariance under the residual diagonal gives
Ψ(gHg⁻¹) = gΨ(H)g⁻¹, and undoing the gauge gives U = Ψ(H)Tᵢⱼ (p. 7). The
claim that Ψ lies in the closure of Σᵣ cᵣ(H)Hʳ rests on Procesi's
polynomial classification and Stone–Weierstrass, and is sketched, not
proved in full. Ψ = H⁻¹ gives composition through the third vertex,
U = TᵢₖTₖⱼ.

**Checked here.** For random O(3) frames on six entities, the two-hop
aggregate Π(Σₖ αₖTᵢₖTₖⱼ), with αₖ from the trace scores of the squares that
avoid (i, j), was gauge-covariant and its scores invariant, to machine
precision.

**What could have failed.** An invariant could have depended on a tree
edge, or an equivariant update could have used an edge-adjacent transport
outside every cycle. The gauge-fixing argument rules out both.

## What this does not say

- **That one mechanism is forced.** Ψ is any conjugation-equivariant map,
  and scores are any class functions. The paper's "the unique form the
  symmetry permits" is true of the family. Its weights, softmax, mean and
  polar projection are choices within it.
- **That a task has the symmetry.** The companion paper calls the premise
  "a chosen inductive bias, not a consequence of fresh names"
  ([LIT-tmpbihhf](../literature.d/LIT-tmpbihhf.md), p. 13). Its CLUTRR control, where orthogonal transports
  cost 64.6 points, is a task where the O(d) form is the wrong one.
- **Anything about relations that are not invertible.** Every step uses
  Tⱼᵢ = Tᵢⱼ⁻¹ and Hᵀ = H⁻¹.
- **Where the transports come from.** They are given. The result
  constrains how supplied relations may interact. It does not say which
  relations a system has ([QUESTION-130](../questions.d/QUESTION-130.md), [QUESTION-282](../questions.d/QUESTION-282.md)).
- **That this is new mathematics.** The paper's contribution is the
  statement on general relational graphs and the reading across three
  literatures, which it says itself (p. 3).
