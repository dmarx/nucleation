# THEORY-tmp7j1bc: In a preregistered multimodal test with clearly seen images, prefrontal cortex carried a seen image's category but not its identity, and its orientation at most weakly, while posterior cortex carried all three; no prefrontal site responded at stimulus offset

## Source

Cogitate Consortium, Ferrante, Gorska-Klimowska, Henin et al. (2025),
*Nature* 642:133–142, [LIT-973](../literature.d/LIT-973.md), read in [NOTE-tmpuu1fr](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpuu1fr.md): "Decoding of
conscious content", "Maintenance of conscious content", Discussion,
Methods, Extended Data Figs. 3–4 and 7.

## What was actually shown

**The design.** Suprathreshold faces, objects, letters and false fonts,
each in 20 identities and three orientations, shown for 0.5, 1.0 or
1.5 s. Participants searched for rare targets. Analyses used
task-irrelevant stimuli, and orientation and duration were never
task-relevant. iEEG (29–31 patients; 576–655 prefrontal and 583–657
posterior electrodes), MEG (n = 65) and fMRI (n = 73) on preregistered,
theory-defined regions.

**Category reaches prefrontal cortex.** Face against object generalised
across tasks in prefrontal ROIs: about 70% in iEEG for about 0.2–0.4 s,
and about 60% in fMRI, in middle and inferior frontal cortex. Posterior
cortex reached over 95% (iEEG) and about 75% (fMRI).

**Orientation and identity do not.**
- *Orientation.* Face orientation was decoded in posterior cortex in all
  three modalities. It was not decoded in prefrontal cortex in iEEG
  (BF01 = 5.11–8.65) or fMRI (34–55% of prefrontal voxels BF01 ≥ 3, 1–9%
  for the alternative). MEG gave a weak 35% against 33% chance, with
  possible leakage.
- *Identity.* Cross-temporal RSA found sustained object identity in
  posterior cortex and "identity information was absent for all
  categories" in prefrontal cortex.
- *No added content.* Adding prefrontal electrodes to posterior ones did
  not improve decoding of category or orientation (iEEG BF01 up to
  1.91 × 10⁵).

**No offset response.** GNWT's proponents predicted content-specific
ignition 0.3–0.5 s after onset and after offset. Of 655 prefrontal
electrodes, 99 responded at onset non-selectively and 24 selectively.
"None ... measured the temporal profile predicted by GNWT", with
BF01 > 3 for each. The same models found an onset–offset profile in 10
early visual electrodes. RSA found prefrontal category information at
onset and not at offset, even when the category was task-relevant.

What could have come out otherwise: prefrontal cortex could have held
orientation and identity alongside category, as it would if it
broadcast the whole of a conscious content. It could have responded when
the image went off, as it would if every change of conscious content
ignited it. Both were preregistered as possible outcomes.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say the missing dimensions were unaccessed or
  unconscious.** No one reported identity or orientation, and the paper
  assumes they were experienced because the stimulus had them. The test
  is of representation in a region, not of access by a consumer.
- **It does not say prefrontal cortex lacks them in every signal.** The
  signals were high gamma, MEG source power and BOLD. Single units,
  low-frequency activity and activity-silent states were not tested, and
  the authors note the lack of single units.
- **It does not refute the global workspace.** It challenges GNWT's
  proposed implementation, prefrontal cortex as the hub. The authors note
  a theory can keep its computational core and revise the
  implementation. The paper's question is whether prefrontal cortex
  broadcasts "all conscious content ... or only a subset".
- **It does not say category coding in prefrontal cortex is conscious
  content.** Category may stay partly task-related. The authors "cannot
  entirely rule out residual task engagement with respect to category".
- **The offset null is for content-specific onset-and-offset activity in
  these regions and bands.** One inferior frontal sulcus electrode showed
  the pattern earlier than predicted, and MEG alpha results were
  inconclusive.
