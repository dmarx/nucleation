# THEORY-1995: Taking the local induction approximation of fluid helicity, instead of kinetic energy, as the Hamiltonian of a vortex filament under the Rasetti–Regge Dirac bracket gives a space-curve motion that the Hasimoto transformation maps to the complex modified KdV equation, the second equation of the NLS hierarchy

## Source

Holm and Stechmann (2004), [LIT-2778](../literature.d/LIT-2778.md), read in [NOTE-2079](../notes.d/NOTE-2079.md).

## The result

Theorem 4.1: ψ_t = ψ_sss + (3/2)|ψ|²ψ_s, and "Thus, soliton solutions of
(4.12) yield LIA ω-filament solutions driven by helicity, and vice versa."
The map from eq. 4.9 is Langer and Perline's: "This equation was shown in
Langer & Perline [1991] to map into the complex mKdV equation (4.11) under
the Hasimoto transformation."

## What it does not show

Any vortex soliton: "We shall, however, leave these issues for other
publications and other researchers." Anything beyond the second flow:
the higher Hamiltonians "apparently have no fluid dynamical
significance".
