# CLAIM-1349: Affect is the claim side

*Alternative A to [QUESTION-544](../questions.d/QUESTION-544.md). Alternatives B and C are [CLAIM-1348](CLAIM-1348.md) and [CLAIM-1347](CLAIM-1347.md).*

## The claim

Affect is what [TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md) defines, read through [TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md): a perspective's
concern-relative significance conditioning a state of readiness that claims
control precedence. On [CLAIM-442](CLAIM-442.md)'s division, "an emotion ... is a claim",
and the system's valence "is read on what the negotiation grants". This
reading keeps the word on the first side. The negotiation's prevailing
ordering is then the arena affects contend in, not an affect, and
[CLAIM-368](CLAIM-368.md)'s frustration is what they are responses to.

What it buys: [CLAIM-039](CLAIM-039.md) becomes a claim that an organization can host
action readiness with control precedence, which is what its Frijda
characterization states. [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s identity concerns hedonic valence,
which on this reading is not affect, so the objection bears on [CLAIM-039](CLAIM-039.md)
only by way of a further thesis that claims to precedence are themselves
valenced.

## What it does not say

That the grant side is unreal or unimportant; [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)'s valence stands
as defined. Nor that [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md) is answered: [CLAIM-440](CLAIM-440.md) and [CLAIM-432](CLAIM-432.md) use
valence and stay within its reach.
