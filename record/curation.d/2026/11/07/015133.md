## The dispositional reframing, withdrawn before merge

A52 §17's synthesis, with A55 §11 and A58 §10 as later versions, was
filed in this pull request as a Proposed CLAIM, at version 3, under a
temporary code. It did not pass ADR-tmpe7y42's admission test by any of
its three routes, so the owner directed that it be withdrawn before
merge, not retired by status. Its content is not refuted: every clause is
held by the entries the admission section names. No permanent code was
ever assigned. What it said, and its audit, are kept here.

**Title as filed.** The organized possibility in which the record's account locates an individual is dispositional: what a declared bearer would manifest across declared conditions, grounded in its actual organization and persisting while unmanifested; so disposition is a dimension of the capacities, perspectives, concepts and will-organizations the record already names, not a further layer or object, and an individual's dispositional profile is its character, not its identity; and its dispositional equivalence to another, its historical continuity, its numerical identity and its autonomy are four properties, none following from another

**Summary as filed.** The thesis of U22 to U24 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07), from A47 §9 to A52 §17, the version filed and the exchange's last account. The reframing is the owner's: U22 asked whether "disposition" might be "a clarifying tool (not necessarily a new thing, could be an improved reframing of a term of art we've already adopted)", and the term filed from it is TERM-tmp6ersv. A52 tells it as "The recent turn to dispositions" and uses the owner's TERM-086 phrase "organizational achievement" without citing it. The integration is A's, from A47 §8 and §9. A52 §2 makes dispositional organization a layer of its own, against A47 and A50; the claim takes A47's form. The audit finds it the thinnest of the exchange's syntheses, mostly a renaming of held distinctions. Version 2 takes A55 §11's four-way independence clause and success test, from the second share; A55 tells the owner's U4, U6, U14 to U16, U18 and U22 as what the work "taught us", and they are credited here. It reframes the thesis on meaning and will (CLAIM-tmpnjz70) and rests on the two on possibility and history. The integration's wording is A's, corrected by the filer; adopting it is the owner's call.

**defeated_if as filed.** Some part of the account that is not dispositional: a capacity, perspective or will-organization the record attributes to a bearer that makes no difference to what it would manifest under any declared condition, or a merely possible manifestation, with no ground in the bearer's actual organization, that individuation must still count. Or two individuals the record must count as one although they differ in dispositional profile and in nothing else, so that the profile is identity after all. Also defeated as a thesis of its own if it only restates TERM-tmp6ersv with CLAIM-421 (dispositions outlast enactment), CLAIM-287 (persistence as capacity to reinstate) and TERM-086's dispositional mode, with "disposition" put where those say capacity. A52 §16 concedes the risk: "The novel contribution cannot be simply renaming those phenomena." Not defeated by an observed behaviour that misleads about the disposition (masking, mimicking, finks), which the claim expects. Also defeated if the dispositional account tells apart no pair of organizations matched under ordinary observation that CLAIM-414, CLAIM-407, CLAIM-521 and CLAIM-522 do not already tell apart. A55 §11 states that test: "whether the enriched theory can distinguish organizations that look the same under ordinary observation but differ in what they are capable of doing, what determines whether those capacities become effective, how those capacities are maintained, and what survives when the organization changes". And defeated in its last clause by a case in which one of the four properties follows from another, as identity would from an identical profile.

#### The claim

A52 §17: "These relationships ground a structured repertoire of
dispositions: possible manifestations across relevant circumstances,
including capacities not currently exercised." "The individual is not
simply its present state, its current collective mode, or its
dispositional profile." A47 §9: "**My strongest recommendation is to treat
disposition as the modal dimension of organizational capacity, not as a new
ontological primitive or a replacement for will.**" A50 §11: "**organized
possibility that has a real causal basis, even when it is not presently
manifest.**"

The architecture, in layers:

1. **What is dispositional.** The repertoire CLAIM-tmp6xhn3 (Proposed, A's)
   says persists. In TERM-tmp6ersv's terms (Proposed, the owner's U22, A's
   wording) it is an ability across declared conditions, or a tendency to
   manifest. Vetter's split between "fragile" and "breakable" is the
   record's source for the two (THEORY-670, Proposed; LIT-109 and NOTE-125,
   Read).

   The repertoire has three dimensions (A58 §10, second share, answering
   the owner's U26): the **discriminative**, "What circumstances can the
   organization distinguish?", which is TERM-064's legibility; the
   **practical**, "What actions can it undertake in those circumstances?";
   and the **transformative**, "How can it revise its distinctions,
   actions, and the relationships governing them?", which is TERM-023's
   transformation, with CLAIM-025 and CLAIM-532. "The first two jointly
   determine which tasks it can perform", as CLAIM-535 and CLAIM-572 do:
   a hedge can stand in for a distinction. *The reader's correction:* A
   drops CASE-194's third repair, authority, which lies between the
   practical and the transformative.
2. **It persists while unmanifested.** A47 §9: "a disposition provides a way
   of describing continuity through nonmanifestation". The record holds it.
   A perspective's enactment can stop while its dispositions remain
   (CLAIM-421, Active). What persists through inactivity is the capacity to
   reinstate (CLAIM-287, Active). Possessing a regime is not its being
   effective (CLAIM-047, Active).
3. **A dimension of the existing terms.** The motivating perspective is
   "motivating as a disposition" (TERM-104, Proposed, the owner's U28 of
   2026-11-04). The dispositional mode of an organizational achievement is
   TERM-086's (Proposed, the owner's term). A disposition is read through a
   governance-response profile (TERM-067, Active).
4. **Character, not identity.** A52 §11 and A47 §7: an identical profile in
   a fork does not give one individual. Identity over time stays with
   CLAIM-tmpcdshj (Proposed).

What the integration adds is A52's "And now we can see how these are
connected through dispositions": the claim that one modal notion, borne by
a declared bearer, is the common dimension of the record's capacity,
perspective, concept and will terms.

#### Admission

Under ADR-tmpe7y42's admission test, a synthesis is filed only if its integration can fail while every component holds. This one cannot. "Persists while unmanifested" is CLAIM-421, CLAIM-287 and CLAIM-047; "character, not identity" is CLAIM-593 with CLAIM-tmpcdshj; the four-way independence is held piecewise by CLAIM-593, CLAIM-329 and TERM-088; and "a dimension, not a layer" is the vocabulary TERM-tmp6ersv already chose. The one candidate is the grounding clause. Left free ("a declared bearer"), it absorbs any externally grounded possibility by declaring a wider bearer, so no case can be stated. Fixed as the individual, it is defeated by CLAIM-522's outside controller, which keeps a repertoire realizable that is not grounded in the individual's own organization, and that construction is a premise of this claim through CLAIM-tmp6xhn3. Its success test names no pair the dispositional account separates and CLAIM-414, CLAIM-407, CLAIM-521 and CLAIM-522 do not. Retired by status, Rejected; the record keeps it and its audit.
