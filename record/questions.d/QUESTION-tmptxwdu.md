# QUESTION-tmptxwdu: What share of conscious time is unintentional mind-wandering, once sampling across the waking day and sleep separates it from deliberate task-unrelated thought?

## Why it is a question

Metzinger (2013, 2015; NOTE-1495, NOTE-1497) puts roughly two thirds of conscious life without M-autonomy; on his own minutes it is 631 of 1207, 52% (THEORY-1297). Both rest on 30–50% figures for task-unrelated thought caught by probes, which the record has not read and which do not separate deliberate daydreaming, which keeps M-autonomy, from unintentional mind-wandering, which loses it.

## What would answer it

Sampling across the waking day and sleep that separates intentional from unintentional task-unrelated thought, since only the second counts. A larger or smaller figure for task-unrelated thought alone cannot settle it.
