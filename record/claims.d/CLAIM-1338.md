# CLAIM-1338: The record holds most of the series' research

## The claim

The owner, at U32: "this is framed as a research program, but most of the
research has already been achieved. check nucleation again for
opportunities to fill gaps in your account, e.g. you propose several
experiments which I agree would be interesting, but I think their role in
our account is largely already satisfied by prior work. rework and
strengthen your series of proposals."

Two parts. A claim about the record: the experiments A49 proposed are, in
the work they would do for the articles, already done. And a consequence
for the work governance-of-becoming: its articles mostly need
consolidation and exposition, with new research kept for what is
genuinely open.

## What it does not say

That nothing is left to research. The owner agrees the experiments "would
be interesting"; the claim is about their role in the account. Nor that a
model result shows the mechanism in a real institution: A51 itself keeps
that line ("We should not confuse demonstrating the possibility of an
architectural pathology with demonstrating its presence in a particular
political institution").
