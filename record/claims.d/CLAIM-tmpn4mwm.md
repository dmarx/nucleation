# CLAIM-tmpn4mwm: Psychedelic meaningfulness is apophany held at a reversible stage

*Alternative A. Alternative B is CLAIM-tmpqwhst.*

## The claim

Psilocybin and LSD reliably raise the meaning a person attaches to what
they perceive. Preller and colleagues (2017) report that songs rated
meaningless became personally meaningful under LSD, and that ketanserin, a
serotonin-2A antagonist, reduced the effect. The model-psychosis tradition
holds that this is apophany produced on demand: the same raised
significance, reaching the same serotonin system, as the delusional mood
(Trema) and apophany of beginning psychosis.

On this reading, what keeps a psychedelic state from becoming a delusion is
not a difference in the experience. It is that the state ends, and that the
person knows its cause, so the meaning is held as the drug's and not the
world's. That knowledge is an outside frame, the one CLAIM-tmprblj4 says
apophany loses.

## What it predicts

People who lose track of the drug as the cause, or whose state does not end,
move from psychedelic meaningfulness into delusion, by Conrad's stages.

## What it does not say

That psychedelic states are psychotic, or harmful. The resemblance is to a
stage, not to the illness.
