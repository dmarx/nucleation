# LIT-3010: Regularities Unseen, Randomness Observed: Levels of Entropy Convergence

Crutchfield & Feldman (2003). Chaos 13, 25–54. arXiv cond-mat/0102181.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A invokes it without naming it at A13 for excess entropy as the measure of a process's stored structure. It bears on the exchange's use of excess entropy as a measure of structure. Read on 2026-11-22 from the 2001 preprint ([NOTE-2286](../notes.d/NOTE-2286.md)) and filed as [THEORY-2200](../theory.d/THEORY-2200.md). The characterization is fair for "stored information" and overstated for "the measure of structure": the paper calls E "a measure of one kind of memory", shows the transient information separating processes of equal E, and assigns structure proper to computational mechanics. [TERM-141](../terms.d/TERM-141.md) draws on the reading and is corrected in place; the reading corroborates [CLAIM-805](../claims.d/CLAIM-805.md) and bears on [LEAD-208](../leads.d/LEAD-208.md) (two-dimensional excess entropies differ by definition) and on [NOTE-689](../notes.d/NOTE-689.md) (power-law entropy convergence of text is infinitary).
