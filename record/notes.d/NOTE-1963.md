# NOTE-1963: Muller et al. — The evoked response in awake monkey V1 is a propagating wave

## Contribution

A phase-latency detector that tells a travelling wave from a stationary
pulse on single, unsmoothed trials. With it, the authors claim the first
systematic demonstration of stimulus-evoked waves in awake primate V1.

## Key insight

The evoked population response is a wave that trial averaging erases. It
travels at the speed of horizontal fibres, with fixed delays.

## Assumptions

The VSD signal reflects mostly superficial dendritic activity. Detection is
in a 5–20 Hz band and requires a speed between 0.05 and 0.8 m/s, so the
criterion builds in the horizontal-fibre range. The network model is 1-D,
7 mm long. Two animals; most numbers come from one session of 40 trials.

## Key results

- **Waves on most trials** ("Phase-latency maps"): "we found stimulus-evoked
  propagations over the imaging array in 32 of 40 trials", while on blank
  trials "no propagations were detected at the chosen temporal point".
- **Speed** (same section): 0.25–1.35 m/s, median 0.57, "in close
  correspondence to the observed range of horizontal conduction speeds in
  monkey V1".
- **Averaging** ("VSD Imaging"): "on the trial-averaged level, distal
  responses are heavily attenuated as a result of the averaging".
- **Fixed delays** ("Trial variability and network model"): "the
  cross-trial phase variability of the waves does not increase with
  distance from their source". A model with variable conduction speed is
  "in stark contrast to the experimental data", so the propagation "must
  be mediated by the horizontal fibre network".
- **V1 and V2** ("Dual, independent propagations"): phase-correlated but
  "consistent with the interpretation that each wave is generated
  independently". No wave crosses the border.

## Concepts

Propagating wave vs stationary pulse; phase latency; horizontal fibre
network; trial-invariant dynamics.

## Bearing on the record

- **[THEORY-088](../theory.d/THEORY-088.md)**: none. The waves are radial, from a point source, at
  millimetre scale, with no rotation or phase singularity. Its colour
  "pinwheel" (Fig. 5a) marks wavevector directions around the source of an
  outward wave.
- **[LIT-647](../literature.d/LIT-647.md)**: a partial neighbour of that review, for its awake-cortex
  claim only.
- A's sentence holds for this setting. The latency–distance correlations
  are, by the authors' own word, "significant, but not overly strong".

## Limitations

Two monkeys, VSD only. Mechanism inferred from a 1-D model. Supplementary
controls for false positives not opened.
