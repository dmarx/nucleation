# LIT-987: Internal Causal Mechanisms Robustly Predict Language Model Out-of-Distribution Behaviors

Jing Huang, Junyi Tao, Thomas Icard, Diyi Yang and Christopher Potts (2025), *arXiv:2505.11770* (ICML 2025).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-765](../notes.d/NOTE-765.md).*

- **Two methods (§4.3).** Counterfactual simulation scores whether a test
  input's value of the located variable, transplanted into k verified
  examples, makes the outputs follow the high-level model (Eq. 5–6).
  Value probing classifies that value linearly (Eq. 7–8).
- **Out of distribution (Table 3).** Counterfactual simulation is best on
  8 of 10 shifts, e.g. IOI with Chinese names 0.997 against 0.767 for the
  best confidence score. Confidence wins once (UnlearnHP, longer passage)
  and value probing once (MMLU, changed option symbols).
- **Causal against background features.** A probe of the located variable
  beats a probe of its orthogonal complement on 7 of 10 shifts and loses
  on IOI rephrased, MMLU with a distractor and UnlearnHP rephrased.
- **In distribution (Table 2).** Causal features are best or tied. The
  last-token probe scores identically to the causal-variable probe on four
  of five tasks.
- **Confidence degrades with shift (Table 7).** On RAVEL, as accuracy
  falls from 89% to 72%, confidence falls from 0.903 to 0.693 AUC-ROC while
  counterfactual simulation stays near 0.9.
- **Interventional fit is not enough (Figure 4).** AUC-ROC rises with IIA
  until IIA nears 1. Some early-layer representations with high IIA
  predict poorly.
- **Scope.** One main model, shifts selected for lowering accuracy, given
  high-level models, linear localization.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-030](../questions.d/QUESTION-030.md) and [QUESTION-028](../questions.d/QUESTION-028.md), and read on
2026-10-14 with Makelov et al. ([LIT-968](LIT-968.md)) and the reply ([LIT-961](LIT-961.md)), in
[NOTE-765](../notes.d/NOTE-765.md). It does not cite either; it belongs to the dispute because
it uses DAS-located residual-stream variables, validated by IIA, for
prediction. The registration said "Intervention here separates
constitutive structure from diagnostic structure". The reading corrects
that. The paper separates variables located by intervention as mediating
from features chosen without causal analysis and from the rest of the
same representation, and it claims nothing about constitution. Nor are
its comparison features "merely correlated" ones.

For the empirical half of [QUESTION-030](../questions.d/QUESTION-030.md) it is a measured case, in a
network, of intervention-identified variables predicting behaviour across
changes the mechanism ignores. For [QUESTION-028](../questions.d/QUESTION-028.md) it is an instance of
intervention-relevant structure that generalizes, with nothing said about
real kinds. Filed as [THEORY-287](../theory.d/THEORY-287.md). Its purpose, correctness estimation
for deployed models, and its guidance on which method to use when are
practice for the anthology. An anthology topic on interpretability could
hold it, so it keeps the `anthology-candidate` flag; it is held here for
its own question under [ADR-013](../decisions.d/ADR-013.md).
