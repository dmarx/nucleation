# CLAIM-1234: Luria's own decision record shows Nucleation's transfer forcing changes to luria, answered as configurable mechanisms rather than code for one record: of the 22 decisions dated from Nucleation's first decision on, 17 name Nucleation, so the evidence of generality is that its misfits were answered by generalizing, not that none arose

## The claim

The owner's inference ([CLAIM-1247](CLAIM-1247.md)) would be strongest if Nucleation had
needed nothing of luria but configuration. That is not what happened, and it
is not what generality needs. Luria's decisions record the transfer:

- Of luria's 143 decisions, 22 are dated on or after 2026-09-25, Nucleation's
  first decision, and 17 of those name Nucleation; none of the 121 earlier
  ones does. Counted with `python3 -I` over the files' `date:` fields and
  text, at luria's checkout of 2026-11-25.
- Some say Nucleation occasioned them. Luria's `ADR-126` (a reference may name
  several schemes): "It came up again from a downstream record. Nucleation,
  which keeps literature (LIT) and accounts drawn from it (THEORY), is
  designing schemes for developing its owner's own arguments." Luria's
  `ADR-133` (a pattern page nests its matches as an outline): "The case that
  forced the question was nucleation's dialectic". Luria's `ADR-142` (a
  hands-free regime) was "Upstreamed at the owner's request from the
  Nucleation record". Others name Nucleation only as a record a change was
  tried on (luria's `ADR-129`: "Tried on dmarx/nucleation").
- The answers their titles name are general mechanisms: a reference naming
  several schemes, named path patterns and pages of their matches, views as
  templates over a record, configuration as the only source of relation
  semantics. Each is configured per record; none is, by its title, a
  behaviour only Nucleation uses.

So the evidence bears on A30's test ([CLAIM-1300](CLAIM-1300.md)) in the second way it
names: the inquiry needed differences the framework did not have, and they
were taken in by generalizing it. It is the pattern [CLAIM-419](CLAIM-419.md) finds in the
record's own grammar, one level down, in the tool.

## What it does not say

- Not that every Nucleation-occasioned decision was general: the reading is
  from titles and a few contexts, not a classification of all 17.
  [LEAD-261](../leads.d/LEAD-261.md) would do that.
- Not that 17 of 22 measures Nucleation's share of the cause: naming is not
  occasioning.
- Luria's decisions are cited by luria's codes ("luria's `ADR-126`"); they are
  not this record's decisions of the same numbers, and are written in backticks so that they stay mentions.
