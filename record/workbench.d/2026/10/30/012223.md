**What went wrong.** From pass 8 to pass 14, the status review narrowed
readings so it could promote them. Each time, it moved the old promote_when
to a new QUESTION and dropped the old wide title. The fault was in the
rulings, not the reviewers' work. The briefs cited [THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md) → [QUESTION-303](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-303.md)
as the pattern, and nobody checked that pattern against [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md)'s later
section, "Where a narrowing's dropped claims go". [ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md) keeps that section
among the evidence rulings it carries forward. The section sorts every
dropped clause into one of four kinds:

1. **A generalization the source asserts** becomes a Proposed THEORY. It
   keeps the old wording, extends the narrowed entry, and inherits the old
   promote_when.
2. **The record's own gloss** becomes an interpretive CLAIM.
3. **A mis-statement** gets a dated note.
4. **Open scope nobody asserts**, and only that, becomes a QUESTION.

The fifteenth review found the gap (workbench entry of 2026-10-30, 00:34).

**What the sweep did.** Four agents took the 28 QUESTIONs those passes filed.
Between them the QUESTIONs cover 31 readings from [THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md) to [THEORY-140](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-140.md).
For each reading the agent sorted every dropped clause, citing the NOTE in
which the source asserts it or does not.

| Kind | Result |
|---|---|
| 1, wide form the source asserts | 22 new Proposed readings |
| 2, the record's own extrapolation | 2 new interpretive claims |
| 3, mis-statement | 7 dated notes |
| nothing new to file | 6 readings |

- **Kind 1, 22 readings.** Each new reading extends its narrowed Active entry,
  inherits its promote_when, and bears on the QUESTION that held it.
- **Kind 2, 2 claims:**
  - that the barrier left after alignment rises with depth, which no source
    states for aligned barriers;
  - that averaging weights within a basin averages features, which [NOTE-512](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-512.md)
    offers as the record's own candidate.
- **Kind 3, 7 notes, on [THEORY-335](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-335.md), -481, -913, -904, -146, -917 and -920.**
  Examples:
  - [THEORY-904](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-904.md)'s d = .79 is a difference between goal intentions, not a plan
    effect.
  - [THEORY-917](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-917.md)'s old title turned "no study links them" into "no relation".
  - [THEORY-146](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-146.md)'s "only if" was a necessity the paper never claims.
- **Nothing new to file, 6 readings:**
  - [THEORY-922](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-922.md), -910, -903, -900 and -912 were not narrowed at all; only
    their promote_when moved, so their QUESTIONs are open scope (kind 4).
  - [THEORY-140](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-140.md)'s one dropped clause was already judged kind 3 at the
    fourteenth review.

**The QUESTIONs stay Open.** [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md) rejected filing a dropped clause only as
a QUESTION, "which asks without proposing". It did not forbid a QUESTION
beside the proposal. So each kind-1 reading bears on its QUESTION and nothing
is retired.

**Where the title is not verbatim.** Four of the 22 titles are trimmed or
reworded:

- **Mixed kinds, trimmed.** Where an old title mixed a kind-1 core with a
  kind-2 or kind-3 clause, the new reading carries only the core:
  - from [THEORY-112](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-112.md), the depth clause;
  - from [THEORY-111](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-111.md), the averaging clause;
  - from [THEORY-904](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-904.md), the misread effect size;
  - from [THEORY-917](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-917.md), the null on random exploration.

  Each summary says what was left out. This follows [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md): the routing is
  clause by clause.
- **Reworded:** the reading kept from [THEORY-146](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-146.md) restates "counteracts this
  only if it discounts" as "a funder that discounts counteracts this",
  dropping the necessity.

**Close calls the agents flagged.**

- **[THEORY-214](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-214.md)'s equilibrium of attack and repair:** kind 1, not kind 3. The
  neglect finding ([QUESTION-169](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-169.md)) qualifies the claim rather than
  contradicting it.
- **Readings that differ from their Active entries mainly in voice:**
  [THEORY-918](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-918.md)'s "reliably improved", [THEORY-908](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-908.md)'s causal verbs, and [THEORY-897](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-897.md)
  and [THEORY-920](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-920.md). Each restates its source's own conclusion as true. By the
  rule that is kind 1, but the Active entry already says the same thing as
  "the review reports". These are the weakest of the 22, and a later pass
  may judge some of them not worth keeping.
- **Inherited promote_whens that fit their new titles badly:**
  - [THEORY-923](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-923.md) and [THEORY-915](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-915.md): the promote_when asks for human or animal
    data on a claim about simulated agents.
  - [THEORY-917](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-917.md): the promote_when still contains the null clause the new
    title drops.

  Each body says so.

**The template is fixed.** The review brief now routes dropped clauses by
the four kinds. It no longer cites [THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md) → [QUESTION-303](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-303.md) as the pattern.
The fifteenth review already followed the rule for [THEORY-164](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-164.md) and [THEORY-174](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-174.md).
