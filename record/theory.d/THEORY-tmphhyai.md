## The account

Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos report that in a lay nutrition task with a simulated AI advisor,
interaction designs that make people think before or without seeing the advice (on demand,
update, wait) reduced their adoption of wrong AI suggestions compared with simple explanations,
without eliminating it and without improving overall accuracy, and that people liked and trusted
those designs less.
