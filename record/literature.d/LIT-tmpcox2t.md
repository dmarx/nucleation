# LIT-tmpcox2t: Two Treatises of Government

John Locke (1689; title page 1690). *Two Treatises of Government*. A cites the *Second Treatise*, §§22–23 and 135. Cambridge Texts edition, ed. Peter Laslett, 1988: DOI-10.1017/CBO9780511810268.

## Key takeaways

*From the sections read ([NOTE-tmpzqo4b](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzqo4b.md)).*

- "a man, not having the power of his own life, cannot, by compact, or his
  own consent, enslave himself to any one"; "No body can give more power
  than he has himself"; but life may be "by his fault forfeited" (§23).
- "no body can transfer to another more power than he has in himself; and
  no body has an absolute arbitrary power over himself" (§135).
- "the legislative being only a fiduciary power to act for certain ends"
  (§149); "The people shall be judge" (§240).
