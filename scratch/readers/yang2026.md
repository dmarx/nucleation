## Summary

The paper attaches a *passive* K-prototype isotropic Gaussian-mixture probe
with one shared learned precision β to an encoder's output z, and analyses
the Hessian of its negative log-likelihood at the collapsed state where all
prototype means equal the data mean. The lowest anti-symmetric eigenvalue
crosses zero at βc = 1/λmax(Cov z) (Rose et al. 1990's deterministic-annealing
critical temperature, in its own convention); above it the prototypes split
along the top principal axis through a supercritical pitchfork. The paper
*defines* concept emergence as this event, makes βc time-dependent as the
encoder learns (Prop. 1, an intermediate-value argument), separates the
crossing from the visible split (Remark 1: a dissipation-controlled
metastable lag), and uses log(β/βc) as a label-free training coordinate.
Empirically: four trajectory shapes in (log β/βc, log NC1) across an SAE on
frozen Pythia-160M, DINO/SimCLR on CIFAR, grokking and a rotation control;
grokking read as crossing (step ~40), plateau, and weight-decay-driven escape;
and an "SAE feature lottery": atoms' POS purity at 5% of training predicts
their purity at convergence.

## Contribution

1. A dynamic version of Rose's static annealing criticality: βc(t) moves
   with the encoder, and a crossing theorem under monotonicity assumptions.
2. The distinction between the crossing (symmetric state becomes a saddle)
   and the macroscopic transition (order parameter grows from noise), with
   the lag set by dissipation.
3. A per-atom prediction from the (K−1)-fold degenerate unstable subspace:
   each atom's direction is chosen by noise and cubic terms, and persists
   (Prop. 2), tested as early-vs-late POS-purity rank correlation.
4. A practical diagnostic for training health.

## Key results

- **Critical precision** (§3.2, Eq. 4; App. A.3). "λ⊥ᵢ(β) = (β/K)(1 − βσᵢ²)
  … The lowest such eigenvalue … crosses zero exactly at the critical
  precision"; "The unstable direction is the principal eigenvector of Σ in
  spatial space combined with any anti-symmetric w in component space."
  Checked (above).
- **Normal form** (§3.2, Eq. 5): "ε̇ = (β − βc)ε − αε³ + noise, α > 0." The
  reduction is stated, not derived in the main text; α is not computed.
- **Endogenous crossing** (Prop. 1, §3.4; App. B). Its proof "is essentially
  a continuity argument"; "The substantive content is in the hypotheses."
- **Metastability** (Remark 1, §3.5): "The crossing event of Prop. 1 marks
  when S0 becomes unstable, not when the system reaches the broken-symmetry
  state"; growth "on a characteristic timescale that scales as 1/(β − βc)".
- **Grokking, three acts** (§4.2, Tab. 2–3). Crossing at steps 34–60 in all
  15 runs, including WD = 0; grok at 8,900 ± 864 (WD 1.0) to 147,167 (WD 0.1);
  "the γ = 0 control fails to escape in any of 3 seeds within 50k steps";
  fit "τesc ∝ WD−1.23" (ΔAIC = +19.3 over a Kramers form). "the model
  becomes eligible to acquire it within the first ∼ 40 steps and then spends
  thousands of steps trying to escape a saddle."
- **Drift-dominated escape** (App. C). Adds an ad hoc tilt γU(ε) "from the
  encoder's upstream loss component that favors the memorization basin";
  says "The output is empirical, not theoretical; we do not claim a
  closed-form prediction for τesc from the bifurcation framework alone."
- **Feature lottery** (§5.1). ρid "+0.03 noise floor" at step 46, "+0.16
  bifurcation onset" at 261, "+0.41" at 1,000 (5%), "+0.88" at 10,000. Top
  decile at 5% reaches purity "0.82 ± 0.03 … 12.3 ± 0.4× this uniform-random
  baseline" (1/15). Stable across K ∈ {256…8192}, ρid ∈ [0.26, 0.41] (§5.4),
  and across token-frequency quintiles (Tab. 5).
- **Prop. 2** (§5.5; App. D): directional persistence E[dk(0)ᵀdk(T)] > 0 in a
  finite window, under weak coupling γ ≪ α and an initial perturbation above
  the noise floor; "the specific magnitude (+0.41) … is not predicted by the
  theory."
- **Hierarchy and reversal** (§3.3): secondary βc⁽²⁾ = 1/λmax(Σwithin);
  "decreasing β continuously merges the broken-symmetry branches back into
  S0"; on CIFAR with the contrastive driver off, "split and merge are the
  same pitchfork" (App. F).

## Assumptions

- The probe is **joint-detached**: "the encoder's gradients never see the
  probe" (App. K). Every bifurcation the theory derives is in the *probe's*
  parameters on a given representation, not in the network's weights.
- Isotropic components with one shared β; analysis at the exactly
  collapsed state; β(t) non-decreasing and βc(t) non-increasing (Prop. 1).
- One-dimensional Langevin reduction with quasi-static geometry and SGD
  noise as Langevin (App. C, Limitations).
- Prop. 2: decoupled or weakly coupled modes, finite horizon.

## Concepts

Critical precision βc; symmetric collapsed state S0; anti-symmetric channel
((K−1)-fold degenerate); supercritical pitchfork normal form; endogenous
criticality; post-critical metastability; dissipation-controlled escape
(drift- vs activation-dominated); bifurcation arc and its four shapes (full V,
fold-back, delayed escape, no arc); feature lottery; identity lock;
hierarchical (secondary) bifurcation; reverse traversal as merge. Terms of
art: deterministic annealing (Rose 1990), NC1 (neural collapse, Papyan
2020), Kramers' law (Berglund 2013), lottery ticket (Frankle & Carbin 2019).

## Verdict on how the exchange characterized it

- CLAIM-807's "Related work": A42 cites it "for a pitchfork account of
  concept emergence in a Gaussian-mixture probe framework". **Accurate.**
- LIT-2961's summary: "concept emergence … treated as a bifurcation, in
  which small initial advantages decide which features win." **Mostly
  accurate, needs one qualification**: the per-atom choice is made by
  "initialization noise and the cubic terms" on a shared unstable manifold
  (App. A.4), and then persists; the paper's claim is about *which* atom
  becomes *which* feature, with persistence a finite-window effect (Prop. 2),
  not a rich-get-richer or preferential-attachment mechanism. The paper never
  frames it as rich-get-richer; the link to the exchange's theme is A's.
- "Bifurcation theory of concept emergence" in the title is an operational
  definition, stated as such: "We adopt this Hessian-pitchfork event as our
  operational definition of concept emergence" (§1).

## Bearing on the named codes

- **CLAIM-807: parallel, and it supports CLAIM-807's caveat.** Both are
  supercritical pitchforks with normal form ε̇ = με − αε³ and the plateau
  time ~ (1/μ) ln(1/x₀) (Yang: "scales as 1/(β − βc)"; Eq. 16 has the log).
  In both, the symmetry broken is a parameter symmetry: here the labelling
  of prototypes (any anti-symmetric w; "which prototype goes where" is "not
  fixed by the linear analysis", App. A.4), there the sign of a = b. My
  inference, not the paper's: in both, a predictor-level quantity grows as
  ε², so linearly in μ (here the mixture's departure from a single Gaussian
  at means ±εu; there q* = s − λ). The paper's bifurcation parameter is the
  probe's precision, not a weight-decay threshold, and the paper carries
  nothing on the local coefficient (no mention of singular learning, RLCT or
  Watanabe: zero hits). Its weight decay plays CLAIM-807's "something
  drifts" role only loosely: in grokking WD sets the escape time after the
  crossing, it does not cause the crossing.
- **QUESTION-tmphkb1w (LLC decreases): leaves open.** Nothing on learning
  coefficients. The nearest construction is the reverse pitchfork (merge as
  β decreases, §3.3, App. F), but below βc the merged state is a
  nondegenerate minimum, so it gives no route to a decrease of λ̂. The
  collapse-mode diagnostics (§6) are representational collapse in DINO, not
  the layer-norm collapse of the question's candidate answer.
- **CLAIM-802 (reading a): contrast.** The paper's transition is a dynamical
  instability of a probe's loss at a saddle, nothing like training
  performing a blow-up.
- **CLAIM-823 (reading b): contrast.** No Bayesian crossover in n; a
  training-time bifurcation in one probe parameter. It sits on A37's
  "dynamical" side of the three transitions CLAIM-823 keeps apart, and its
  "semantic" side only through NC1 and POS purity, which use labels.
- **CASE-302: background.** The 3/4 coefficient at a merge stratum is not
  in this paper; the parallel is only that merged prototypes (S0) are where
  the anti-symmetric directions go soft at βc.

## Limitations

- **The pitchfork is the probe's, and the probe is detached.** The theory is
  Rose's annealing analysis applied to a GMM fitted to z(t). What it shows of
  the network is λmax(Cov z(t)) relative to the probe's β, whose growth is set
  by the probe's own optimizer (lrβ = 10⁻², log β0 = −2.5). The crossing time
  is therefore partly a probe hyperparameter (App. K: Act 1 is invariant in
  Kprobe but the paper does not sweep lrβ or β0).
- **The grokking plateau identifies two different order parameters.** ε in
  Eq. 5 is the probe's prototype split, which the probe's own dynamics would
  resolve quickly at β/βc ≈ e³; the plateau measured is NC1 of the encoder's
  features against output labels. The bridge is the ad hoc tilt γU(ε) of
  App. C, not derived. A rival reading: the crossing at step ~40 says only
  that the residual stream became anisotropic, and the grokking escape is the
  known weight-decay-driven circuit formation, independent of the probe.
- βSAE, "the SAE's own precision", is not defined anywhere I found.
- Prop. 1 is an IVT step whose content is its hypotheses; Prop. 3's
  "four shapes" is a taxonomy of three binary axes, not a derived count.
- Feature lottery: one dimension of interpretability (POS purity, 15
  classes, spaCy labels); three seeds; the §6 diagnostics are single-seed.
- The τ ∝ WD^−1.23 fit is six points, called "specific to the
  modular-arithmetic grokking setup".
- "Sharp dynamical phase transition" (§5) is the author's phrase; by
  Kuehn's criterion (THEORY-1429) a supercritical pitchfork is not a
  critical transition, since no fast jump follows.

## Candidate THEORY

Yang reports that a passive isotropic Gaussian-mixture probe with shared
precision β, fitted to an encoder's output, has its collapsed state lose
stability through a supercritical pitchfork at β = 1/λmax(Cov z), that the
visible split can lag that crossing by orders of magnitude under weak
dissipation (in modular-addition grokking the crossing comes near step 40 and
generalization after thousands of steps, sooner with more weight decay and not
within 50,000 steps without it), and that in sparse autoencoders on Pythia-160M
an atom's part-of-speech purity at 5% of training predicts its purity at
convergence (Spearman about 0.4).

`promote_when`: an independent reproduction, on the released code or fresh
runs, of the Hessian threshold at 1/λmax(Cov z) on real encoder features, of
the weight-decay dependence of the grokking escape with the crossing time
unchanged, and of a positive early-to-late per-atom purity correlation in an
SAE.

## Recheck: held entries the first search missed

- **LIT-2037 / THEORY-1370** (Berglund, Kramers' law; Proposed): the very
  work the paper cites for its Kramers form (App. C). Background; the paper
  rejects the Kramers regime for its data.
- **THEORY-1429** (Kuehn, critical transitions; Proposed): qualifies the
  paper's language. Kuehn: supercritical pitchforks are not critical
  transitions; slowing scales with exponent 1 for the pitchfork, as in the
  paper's 1/(β − βc). The paper's delayed escape is close to delayed loss of
  stability under slow drift, which Kuehn's frame covers and the paper does
  not cite.
- **CLAIM-827** (BBP threshold vs alignment bifurcation; Proposed): parallel.
  βc = 1/λmax(Cov z) ties the split to the top covariance eigenvalue, the
  same quantity a BBP-style early warning watches; CLAIM-827's point that a
  detection threshold and a bifurcation differ applies to the paper's
  "label-free indicator".
- **CASE-112** (tilted pitchfork ẇ = μw − w³ + h; Active): parallel to App.

- **THEORY-863** (Arthur, lock-in by early chance; Active): contrast for
