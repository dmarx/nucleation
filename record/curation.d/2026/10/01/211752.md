**Three are unread because no full text could be reached.** The 1989 paper is
behind ScienceDirect with no open copy. Man & Damasio has no arXiv version and
no open copy. The 2023 paper has a free PDF at MIT Press, but its bot
challenge refused every request. Each is a `Deferred` seed whose
`status_note` says what it rests on.

**A word for emotion ([ADR-014](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-014.md)).** Damasio's subject had no tag, so the owner
asked for one and `emotion-and-affect` joined the vocabulary.

**What the readings found.**

- **Bechara et al. 1997 claims more in its abstract than its statistics show.**
  All 10 controls and none of the 6 patients developed anticipatory skin
  conductance responses. But the controls' preference for the good decks
  before any hunch is not significant. The early skin-conductance effect is
  reported only as a group-by-period interaction, with no test of bad decks
  against good. "Before knowing" holds for the stated strategy, not for
  awareness in general. The record does not yet hold Maia & McClelland's 2004
  critique of the awareness timing.
- **The 1996 paper is a hypothesis paper.** Its evidence is summarised from
  other work without statistics. Two corrections are in its NOTE.
- **The 2013 paper's one shown result** is that feeling survives bilateral
  insula damage, from a single patient. The evolutionary account, animal
  feeling and the cellular proposal are asserted.

**Two things checked along the way.**

- **[NOTE-120](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-120.md) does not cite the 2023 *Neural Computation* paper.** Its
  "Damasio & Damasio 2023" is a different commentary, *Sensing is a far cry
  from sentience* (*Animal Sentience* 33(16)), cited through [LIT-193](../../record/literature.d/LIT-193.md). [NOTE-120](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-120.md)
  reports it correctly. That commentary is the natural next filing for the
  plant-sentience thread.
- **Man & Damasio carries `anthology-candidate`.** Its abstract proposes a
  class of machines without building or measuring one. The anthology's
  `agents-and-environments` topic could hold it, so under [ADR-001](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-001.md) it may be
  theirs. Whether to transfer it is for whoever reads the paper.
