conditions they are asked to meet, the memory kernels of linear coarse
variables, and where a system's causes are found. It was set apart by a

## The account

- **The regime.** [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md) (A3's, A5's and A11 to A14's construction, with
  T taken out at the owner's second review, adopted by the owner, Active)
  is ℛ = (X, 𝒬, ℐ, ℬ, ε, τ), the system, queries, interventions,
  resources, tolerance and horizon under which a description T is
  assessed, Adequate(T; ℛ). Its point is A5's: "Adequacy is relational,
  but whether the relation holds can be objective." Keeping T out of the
  regime is what lets two descriptions be compared under one regime.
- **Sufficiency is relative to a target, a model and a decision family.**
  [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) (dual-filed from the Mathematics of Meaning record's
  position, demonstrated, Active): "Any individuation built on sufficiency
  is relative to the target it is sufficient for". It "does not say
  boundaries are arbitrary, only that each is relative to a stated
  target." [CLAIM-300](../claims.d/CLAIM-300.md) (A10's answer to the owner's U10, with A8's case and
  A10's constructions, demonstrated, Active): a state sufficient for a
  family of decisions under a model makes the rest of the history
  worthless for them, and "Sufficiency was always relative to the model and
  the family." [CASE-087](../cases.d/CASE-087.md) (A10's construction, stipulated, Active) is its
  relativity half: two pairs of readings with mean 5 that the mean cannot
  tell apart once one instrument is discredited or a mixture is
  considered. [CLAIM-294](../claims.d/CLAIM-294.md) (A5's claim made exact by A8's construction,
  demonstrated, Active): two histories with P(H) = 0.9 update to 1.0 and
  0.8 on C = 1, so "Keeping only the marginal merges histories that need
  different updates." [CLAIM-293](../claims.d/CLAIM-293.md) (A6's case, granted, demonstrated,
  Active): "`rests_on` and `grounds` record that support exists, not how
  much."
- **Coarse-graining for a judgment.** [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md) (A21's and A22's,
  granted, demonstrated, Active): a coarse-graining C preserves a
  deterministic judgment J exactly when J is constant on C's fibres, so
  "The coarsest coarse-graining that preserves J is therefore J's own
  partition". It "says when a description keeps a judgment, never whether
  the judgment is right", and observational adequacy is not causal
  adequacy.
- **The conditions are of different kinds.** [CLAIM-401](../claims.d/CLAIM-401.md) (A4's and A5's on
  the owner's U4, with the record's addition on memory, interpretive,
  Active): predictive sufficiency and approximation fidelity are adequacy
  relative to a declared target, metric and horizon; tractability
  restricts the operations; dynamical independence and memory closure are
  relations of the representation to the system, memory relative to a
  chosen projection; "so a matrix over them as independent binary axes
  conflates kinds". Of the five, "only dynamical independence needs
  nothing declared beyond the micro state and the coarse-graining".
  [QUESTION-324](../questions.d/QUESTION-324.md) (split off by the thirteenth status review, Answered by
  [CLAIM-401](../claims.d/CLAIM-401.md)) holds that answer. [QUESTION-252](../questions.d/QUESTION-252.md) (the owner's idea at U3, in
  A3's corrected form, Open) asks which of the five can fail while all the
  others hold, and which configurations are impossible in which model
  classes. "An impossible configuration is itself a substantive result."
  The record holds pairwise non-implications only, "none a leave-one-out
  witness".
- **Probes and feasible tests.** [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md) (A13's definition, the
  mathematics [CLAIM-264](../claims.d/CLAIM-264.md)'s, adopted by the owner, Active): two objects are
  observationally equivalent under a probe family when every probe gives
  the same outcome law. [CLAIM-264](../claims.d/CLAIM-264.md) (the record's restatement split from
  [CLAIM-142](../claims.d/CLAIM-142.md), corrected at the owner's promotion audit, demonstrated,
  Active): such agreement can fall short of isomorphism, a dense family
  gives full faithfulness and a strong generator only conservativity, and
  "matching each probe's marginal leaves the joint across probes
  unconstrained". [CLAIM-322](../claims.d/CLAIM-322.md) (its evidential half, split under [ADR-043](../decisions.d/ADR-043.md),
  interpretive, Active): agreement is evidence of shared structure only to
  the degree the family was chosen independently and evaluated on held-out
  cases, "so a family selected to make two objects look alike shows only
  that it can be selected". [CLAIM-302](../claims.d/CLAIM-302.md) (A15's witness, developed at the
  owner's U15 and corrected by the reading of Goldreich, demonstrated,
  Active): "Equivalence under feasible tests can be much coarser than
  statistical equivalence"; the separation is unconditional, and only an
  efficiently samplable witness needs pseudorandom generators. [CASE-083](../cases.d/CASE-083.md)
  (A15's witness, stipulated, Active) is that witness, G(Uₙ) against U₂ₙ.
  [CLAIM-304](../claims.d/CLAIM-304.md) (A14's failure mode and A18's candidate, the construction the
  record's, demonstrated, Active): "A rendering can keep a distinction
  recoverable in principle and make it infeasible to recover".
- **Questions on bounded observers.** [QUESTION-103](../questions.d/QUESTION-103.md) (the owner's, Open) asks
  whether computational indistinguishability is the bounded-observer form
  of signal detection's chance ROC; its note of 2026-10-15 finds "The
  bridge is narrower than first stated", since ideal-observer efficiency
  measures a garbling of the input and [CLAIM-302](../claims.d/CLAIM-302.md) a restricted class of
  tests. [QUESTION-110](../questions.d/QUESTION-110.md) (A18's sixth candidate, Open) asks how to define the
  cost of recovering a distinction, relative to an encoding, a class of
  procedures and a success criterion; V-information "fixes two of the
  three parameters and has no cost of recovery".
- **Memory and dependence in linear Gaussian dynamics.** [CLAIM-389](../claims.d/CLAIM-389.md) (the
  reader's derivation from Chorin, Hald and Kupferman, demonstrated,
  Active): in a stable VAR(1), exact dynamical independence implies zero
  Mori–Zwanzig memory but not conversely; "memory is information that
  leaves the coarse variable and comes back to it", and "A coarse variable
  can receive news it never sent." [CLAIM-397](../claims.d/CLAIM-397.md) (the reader's derivation
  from Lin and Lu, demonstrated, Active): the memory kernels are fixed by
  the autocovariance alone, vanish iff it is geometric, and have Hankel
  rank d − 1. [CLAIM-398](../claims.d/CLAIM-398.md) (the reader's derivation from Chorin and Lu,
  demonstrated, Active): a scalar coarse variable is ARMA of orders at most
  (n, n − 1), so its memory "is carried exactly by finitely many
  coordinates that are functions of Y's own past and innovations".
  [CLAIM-390](../claims.d/CLAIM-390.md) (found in the reading of Chorin, Hald and Kupferman, derived
  and checked at filing, demonstrated, Active): a
  block's dynamical dependence is of second order in the coupling into
  it, "whatever the coupling out of it". [CLAIM-386](../claims.d/CLAIM-386.md) (the reader's
  derivation from Shpak, Stadler, Wagner and Altenberg, demonstrated,
  Active): the Simon–Ando aggregate's one-step closure defect is exactly
  QA(I − VQ), and its part from the blocks' own dynamics vanishes iff each
  block has equal column sums. [CLAIM-394](../claims.d/CLAIM-394.md) (the reader's derivation from
  Schüler and Wiskott, demonstrated, Active): in a reversible VAR(1) any
  span of slow features is exactly dynamically independent, and "off
  reversibility a slow feature is in general neither independent nor free
  of memory".
- **Where causes are.** [CLAIM-497](../claims.d/CLAIM-497.md) (filed at the owner's direction to
  "adopt the proportionality thesis", the interpretive step the record's,
  Active): "A system's causes are found at the coarsest description whose
  interventions are unambiguous", and no coarse-graining carries more
  causal information than its micro description. "A macro can be the
  proportionate description. It is never a richer one." [ARG-041](../arguments.d/ARG-041.md) (filed
  at the owner's direction, formal application, Proposed) gives the
  route: the bound ([THEORY-1167](../theory.d/THEORY-1167.md)) and its ties ([THEORY-1169](../theory.d/THEORY-1169.md)) are
  demonstrated, and step 4, proportionality, "is the record's own".
  [QUESTION-335](../questions.d/QUESTION-335.md) (filed at the rejection of [THEORY-787](../theory.d/THEORY-787.md), Open) holds the case
  neither reaches, macros whose step spans several micro steps.

What stays open is the leave-one-out ([QUESTION-252](../questions.d/QUESTION-252.md)), a measure of the
gap between the ideal and a class of bounded observers on real observers
([QUESTION-103](../questions.d/QUESTION-103.md)), a cost of recovery ([QUESTION-110](../questions.d/QUESTION-110.md)), and causal emergence
in temporal macros ([QUESTION-335](../questions.d/QUESTION-335.md)).
