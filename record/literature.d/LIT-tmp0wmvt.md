David J. Chalmers and Kelvin J. McQueen (2021), *arXiv preprint; chapter in S. Gao (ed.), Consciousness and Quantum Mechanics, OUP* — [ARXIV-2105.02314](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02314)

- Consciousness-collapse can be made precise: take consciousness (on IIT, phenomenal Q-shape) to resist superposition and give it a continuous-collapse dynamics. Simple versions fail on the quantum Zeno effect; others remain open and are in principle testable.
- The thesis need not be dualist, since on a materialist IIT it is a fundamental physical law about a complex physical property. On a property-dualist reading a consciousness-to-physics law gives non-physical consciousness a causal role.
- The authors argue that wave-function collapse "appears to be precisely such a gap" in causal closure as non-physical consciousness might fill, so the closure argument against interactionist property dualism does not reach the consciousness-collapse view.
