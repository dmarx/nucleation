# ARG-tmphr0a1: The record's iterated-learning readings show that pragmatic stances and footing are stabilized by transmission

## The inference

[CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) holds that "pragmatic stances, not only lexicon and grammar, can
be stabilized by transmission", and quotes A52 §7.5 extending "iterated
learning from lexical and grammatical structure to speaker footing and
communicative conventions". Its defeat condition is about stances: chains
"started from different stances do not converge toward a small set of
conventional stances". The readings it and [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) draw on are the
premises.

## Critical questions

- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** No. This is the question
  that fails. [CLAIM-234](../claims.d/CLAIM-234.md) (2026-10-10), on what each reading measures:
  - Griffiths and Kalish ([THEORY-155](../theory.d/THEORY-155.md)): a theorem about learners who share
    a prior over languages; sampling learners converge to the prior. No
    measurement.
  - Kirby, Cornish and Smith ([LIT-771](../literature.d/LIT-771.md)): an artificial 27-item lexicon,
    whose compositional structure appeared only under the experimenter's
    homonym filter.
  - Kalish, Griffiths and Lewandowsky ([THEORY-489](../theory.d/THEORY-489.md)): function learning, 32
    chains converging mostly to a positive linear function.
  - Raviv, Meyer and Lev-Ari ([LIT-825](../literature.d/LIT-825.md)): the systematicity of invented
    languages in groups of four and eight. [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md): "This is horizontal
    interaction, not a chain."
  - Propp ([LIT-828](../literature.d/LIT-828.md)): the structure of a corpus of tales. [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md): "not a
    measurement of retellings".
  - Farrell, Gopnik, Shalizi and Evans ([LIT-848](../literature.d/LIT-848.md)): a mechanism stated and
    not tested. [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md): "so it is weak support".

  "None measures an interpersonal stance, a footing or a speech-act
  function along a chain. The extension to footing has no study under it."
- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes.
  Lexical, structural and functional convergence would be found whether or
  not stances converge.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No. Which learner
  model applies decides "whether the endpoint reflects the learners or the
  transmission filter" ([CLAIM-066](../claims.d/CLAIM-066.md), now [ARG-tmpvlysu](ARG-tmpvlysu.md)), and none of these
  readings says anything of stance under either.

Rejected as an inference to [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md). It does not say that stances are
not stabilized by transmission, nor that [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) is false. It is minor
for the argument: [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md) feeds [CLAIM-059](../claims.d/CLAIM-059.md)'s regimes, not the central
theses [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-139](../claims.d/CLAIM-139.md).

## Absent support

Bartlett's *Remembering* ([LIT-768](../literature.d/LIT-768.md)), the classic serial-reproduction study,
is unread. On 2026-10-10 a search of the record for the
transmission-chain work on social and affective content (Mesoudi,
Bebbington, Stubbersfield, Breithaupt, Moussaïd, "negativity bias") found
nothing.

## A second line, not filed

The search the owner asked for that day found two chain studies, filed as
[CASE-068](../cases.d/CASE-068.md): Moussaïd, Brighton and Gaissmaier (2015) and Jagiello and Hills
(2018). Each codes the evaluative stance of a message toward a hazard at
every link; the content decays, and the evaluative signal is kept and
drifts negative. For **evaluative stance** the record now has chain
evidence, which would be a separate, Proposed line from [CASE-068](../cases.d/CASE-068.md) to
[CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md). It lacks chains started from different stances, which [CLAIM-068](../claims.d/CLAIM-068.md)'s
defeat condition needs, and a clean prior. For **footing**, interpersonal
stance and speech-act function, there is still none: neither study codes
who stands behind a statement, whom it addresses or whether it teases or
reproaches. A footing chain would need the measurement [QUESTION-076](../questions.d/QUESTION-076.md) asks
for first. The Anthology of the SOTA was checked and holds neither study.
