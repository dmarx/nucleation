# THEORY-tmpaygr8: Mossio and Moreno (2010) hold that a flame maintains itself through a single constraint acting on its own boundary conditions, while an organism maintains itself through organisational closure, the direct mutual dependence of several constraints; closure is necessary for an organism but not sufficient, which also needs templates and control between parts working at different time scales

## The account

Mossio and Moreno (2010) hold that a flame maintains itself through a single
constraint acting on its own boundary conditions, while an organism
maintains itself through organisational closure, the direct mutual
dependence of several constraints. Closure is necessary for an organism but
not sufficient: it also needs templates and control between parts working at
sufficiently different time scales.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmpnhdly](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpnhdly.md); its key results are quoted there with
locations. The flame's single constraint is asserted, not analysed; the wick
and wax pool are not considered.

## Bearing

On [THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md), which draws the same line and asks whether the authors fold
differentiation into closure: here the multiplicity of constraints is part
of closure, and time-scale differentiation is a further condition, for
control.
