# ARG-tmpkf20b: Grossman and Hart carry the definition of residual rights of control, and not the claim that their holder decides an uncovered contingency alone or that the control is exercised and seen only when the contingency arises: in their model the parties renegotiate, and the allocation acts in advance through investment

## The inference

From Grossman and Hart (1986), with Hart's 2016 lecture, to [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md):
in an incomplete contract the party holding residual rights of control
decides the contingencies the contract left open, and that discretion is
exercised, and seen, only when an uncovered contingency arises.

## Critical questions (form: source)

- **Does the source carry the definition?** Yes (§I.B, §V). [THEORY-tmpt4dyh](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpt4dyh.md)
  corroborates the first clause.
- **Does it carry unilateral decision?** No. "The distribution of ex post
  surplus, however, will be sensitive to ownership rights" (§I.C). The
  holder's right sets the threat point in a renegotiation, and the
  renegotiation reaches the efficient use. The claim's "decides" reads the
  right as the outcome.
- **Does it carry "only when an uncovered contingency arises"?** No. The
  inefficiency is in investment, made in anticipation, "in spite of the
  fact that the noncooperative outcome never occurs!" (§III.A). Hart (2016):
  "the power plant does anticipate that it will be at the mercy of the
  coal mine". This is close to the claim's own defeat condition
  (discretion "exercised and visible in ordinary operation"), though
  anticipation is not exercise.
- **Does the worker example follow?** Only through assets and
  replaceability. Hart: "purchasing the mine would not be worth much if the
  coal mine manager is indispensable".

## Verdict

The inference holds for the first clause and fails for the last two. The
claim should be revised to allow ex ante effects through anticipation, and
to say that the holder's control sets the terms of renegotiation rather
than the outcome. Until it is, the source supports less than the claim
states.
