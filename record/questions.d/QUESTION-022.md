# QUESTION-022: Which stochastic transports carry compatible empirical models to compatible ones, and which preserve global extendability?

## Why it is a question

A110 §IX, "Five central mathematical questions", the first: characterize the
stochastic maps that carry compatible models to compatible ones. "A starting
result is that restriction-compatible stochastic maps preserve overlap
consistency. When the local maps arise from a common global stochastic kernel,
they also preserve global extendability under the appropriate correspondence
conditions."

## Where it stands

Those starting results are the manuscript's Propositions 1 and 2 ([CLAIM-121](../claims.d/CLAIM-121.md),
[CLAIM-070](../claims.d/CLAIM-070.md)), both sufficient conditions. C7's Appendix A: "The theorem therefore
identifies a sufficient preservation class and a clear empirical target: measure
departures from this class rather than treating arbitrary changes of measurement
cover as intrinsic contextuality." The cover-changing case is [CLAIM-100](../claims.d/CLAIM-100.md). None of the
five questions appears in the manuscript, which has no section of open
problems.
