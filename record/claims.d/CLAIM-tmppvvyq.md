# CLAIM-tmppvvyq: Serial transmission is reconstructive and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases, so a stable endpoint can be far from the ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable

## The claim

§8: "Bartlett's serial reproduction experiments and Bayesian iterated-learning
models both show that transmission is reconstructive and filtered by inductive
biases rather than neutral copying." The manuscript adds the data-processing
inequality: information about the ancestor cannot increase along a Markov
chain, even as later retellings become easier to process.

## What it does not say

It does not say messages get less comprehensible along a chain; the manuscript
is explicit that conventionalization can make them easier. Bartlett is unread
here ([LIT-768](../literature.d/LIT-768.md)).
