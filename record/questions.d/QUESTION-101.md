# QUESTION-101: When does whether a resource is available for an action depend on the organization of an agent–environment system rather than on what any one member knows or can do?

## Why it is a question

A17 §5: "No single participant necessarily possessed everything needed
to complete the action. The team's effective competence depended on
whether these distributed components could be coordinated within the
deadline." It distinguished "Collective information ≠ Collective
operational competence", and noted the converse: "a trained team can
execute a familiar workflow effectively without any individual being able
to articulate all of the information supporting it."

The distributed-agency line asks when a whole has capacities its parts do
not. This is that question asked of one capacity, deploying a resource
in time, where the answer is checkable: [TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md) is a probability, and the
counterfactuals A17 §6 listed (a labelled compartment, a partner who knows,
more time, a standard layout) each change one factor.

An answer says, for a stated class of tasks, when the availability of a
resource is a property of the system and not of any member, and gives a
case where changing the arrangement alone changes it.

## Note of 2026-11-12: the distinction applied to a shared record, from "luria experiment design"

A34 §1 of the exchange "luria experiment design" (2026-11-10, https://chatgpt.com/share/6af35bfc-35f8-83e9-a63d-d594703eabda) applies this question's distinction, with CASE-085, to a
record that several agents share: "A shared record might contain all the
necessary information, with correct provenance and relationships, while a
team still fails because its agents cannot discover, interpret, authorize,
or act on that information before a deadline." It separates information
retained, accessible, actionable, authorized and acted on, and proposes
the test: "give a team everything it needs somewhere in its distributed
record, then measure whether it can coordinate the correct intervention
under a time and communication budget." QUESTION-tmpxq2sb asks it of the
record. Open.
