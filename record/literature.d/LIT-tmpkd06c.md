# LIT-tmpkd06c: The Emotions

Nico H. Frijda, *The Emotions*, Studies in Emotion and Social Interaction,
Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1986 (with Editions de la Maison des
Sciences de l'Homme, Paris). Open Library gives a median of 544 pages and the
ISBNs above. No DOI was found: Crossref returns none for the book. Only the
year is known, so `published:` carries it as the first of January. The `url:`
is the Open Library record, the only stable identifier found. It is a
catalogue entry, not a copy.

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read. The following is what read works in the record say the
book holds, attributed to them.*

- **Action readiness and control precedence** are the book's constructs,
  according to Frijda, Ridderinkhof and Rietveld ([LIT-tmp57opn](LIT-tmp57opn.md)), who cite it
  for both. In that paper action readiness is "the key to what we call
  'emotions'".
- **Emotions prepare rapid motor responses, and cognitive change is the
  modification of appraisal steps.** Gross ([LIT-tmptr6ik](LIT-tmptr6ik.md)) cites the book for
  both (pp. 273, 284). He also cites it as the source of the view that emotion
  regulation is "part and parcel of emotion" (p. 286).
