# SYNTHESIS-094: The wrong beyond harm and how an organization becomes normatively less informed: reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining it is proposed as a breach of a bounded duty of answerability, and an organization can lose sight of unmet obligations by the exit of the harmed, by discovery it is penalized for, or by weakening the channels challenge succeeds through, routes the record keeps apart

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, filed at the synthesis review of the line on 2026-11-15. The line's account, [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md), is built over it and one sibling part, [SYNTHESIS-091](SYNTHESIS-091.md); the other two parts it was first built over became the line dependence-and-domination ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)). Its first twelve components were mapped in [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md) versions 1 to 3, and that mapping is kept here unchanged; the rest were filed from 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12 and are new to any account. Version 3, at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, integrates [ARG-tmptw59w](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptw59w.md), filed from the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" that day. Most members are also in distributed-agency, and [SYNTHESIS-056](SYNTHESIS-056.md), that line's account of perspectives, legibility and correction, integrates them again. Where a component declares which sense of "organization" it means ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)), the account says so; none of this part's claims declares one, and several carry readings that are about sense 3.*

## The account

**The question.** [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md) (the owner's U27, verbatim; Open; also
distributed-agency) refines [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md): what is the counter-normativity
committed by a self-maintaining organization that harms and reproduces
what makes the harm non-legible, what makes it reproductive, and when is
it the organization's own. Its notes of 2026-11-12 file readings that are
sense-3 cases.

**The wrong beyond harm.** [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md) (the owner's presupposition;
Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) and [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), uses
[TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) and [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md), and bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md): such an
organization commits a counter-normativity distinct from the harm. [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)
(A's, A60 to A62; Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md),
[CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) and [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md): the wrong is a breach of a
second-order duty of answerability, "to maintain feasible means for
relevant violations to become knowable, contestable and correctable", "a
verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible
alternative". It was admitted on that narrower title (its history,
version 3); its body states where it diverges from the owner's thesis on
unavoidable projections. A note of 2026-11-12 says a sense-3 reading, the
state and its institutions, corroborates it. Seven arguments conclude it:

- [ARG-055](../arguments.d/ARG-055.md) (Proposed), from [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md) (A's matched-harm construction): the
  second-order wrong comes apart from the harm and its continuation;
- [ARG-058](../arguments.d/ARG-058.md) (Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md): an unconditional duty would
  entail [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md), which the record disclaims, so the duty is bounded;
- [ARG-062](../arguments.d/ARG-062.md) (Proposed), from [THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md) and [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md): an organization can
  reproduce the conditions that keep a known hazard from constraining it,
  "though it says nothing of a second-order wrong";
- [ARG-063](../arguments.d/ARG-063.md) (Proposed; in distributed-agency only), from [CASE-228](../cases.d/CASE-228.md): a harmful
  outcome and the regime that keeps it unrecorded can sustain each other
  without intent, and it "shows nothing normative";
- [ARG-064](../arguments.d/ARG-064.md) (Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md): "the reproductive mechanism
  transfers and the verdict does not";
- [ARG-071](../arguments.d/ARG-071.md) (Rejected), from Wells Fargo ([CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)): occlusion and blocked
  uptake, not reproduction;
- [ARG-tmptw59w](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptw59w.md) (Proposed; A's inference rule, A110 §5 and §6 of "Assess
  Depp Heard Abuse", with the filer's verdict), resting on [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md),
  [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md) and [CLAIM-tmpcpijj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcpijj.md): given a means-end principle of
  responsibility, an authority that owes a duty whose fulfilment needs a
  capability it is responsible for and can maintain at justified cost
  owes the maintenance of an adequate one, absent a defeater. Its body
  says it is "valid with the principle as a premise", that the principle
  is A's and not held on the record, that it reaches the duty (layer 4)
  and not the wrong beyond the harm or its non-reduction, and that given
  the duty "a reproduced occlusion is its breach". A dated note of
  2026-11-17 on [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) records it, with no status change.

**The counter-corrective route.** [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) (A's, A62; Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) and [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md), uses [TERM-118](../terms.d/TERM-118.md) and
bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md): an organization can be disposed to answer a
credible challenge by reducing the capacity for future challenge, and the
test alone does not say whether the response "finks the disposition to be
corrected, masks it, or changes only the challengers' access and
incentives". [ARG-059](../arguments.d/ARG-059.md) (Proposed) concludes it from the self-confirming
label ([CASE-141](../cases.d/CASE-141.md)); [ARG-070](../arguments.d/ARG-070.md) (Deferred) offers Pohlhaus, unread. A note of
2026-11-12 says a sense-3 reading, about universities, corroborates the
phenomenon, not its test.

**Who installed it.** [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md) (the assistant's, raised by the reading
review of 2026-11-11; Open) refines [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md): when an organization
reproduces a mechanism that keeps its harm non-legible, does it matter
that another installed it, or does only whose process now maintains it
decide? [CASE-232](../cases.d/CASE-232.md) (Stute's story, [THEORY-1561](../theory.d/THEORY-1561.md); specimen, stipulated,
Active; also distributed-agency) bears on it and illustrates [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md): a
compiler Trojan formed by one party, reproduced by the host's own
compiling, hidden from the host.

**Selective disappearance.** [CLAIM-652](../claims.d/CLAIM-652.md) (the assistant's, accepted by the
owner on 2026-11-12; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-655](../claims.d/CLAIM-655.md), grounds in [CASE-246](../cases.d/CASE-246.md), bears on
[QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md) and complements [CLAIM-662](../claims.d/CLAIM-662.md) and [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md): an organization that
weighs a harmful practice by the failures its present users report "can
expand the practice with no act against complaint", as the harmed leave.
[CASE-246](../cases.d/CASE-246.md) (the assistant's toy; stipulated, Proposed) illustrates [CLAIM-655](../claims.d/CLAIM-655.md)
and bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md); [ARG-092](../arguments.d/ARG-092.md) (Proposed) concludes [CLAIM-652](../claims.d/CLAIM-652.md) from it.

**Costly discovery.** [CLAIM-662](../claims.d/CLAIM-662.md) (A's, A44 §3, narrowed by the owner's
review of [#485](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/485) on 2026-11-12; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md) and
[QUESTION-456](../questions.d/QUESTION-456.md), and complements [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) and [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md): expanding the
recognized demand set cannot improve conjunctive compliance or decrease
recognized violations, and "where governance penalizes newly recognized
violations without rewarding their discovery", an organization "may
acquire an incentive not to improve its identification of its
obligations". Its summary says the monotonicity facts are checked by
enumeration and the incentive is conditional. [QUESTION-456](../questions.d/QUESTION-456.md) (A's, A42 §8;
Open; also distributed-agency) refines [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md): how the demand set is
to be established defensibly when the organization's own governance shapes
who counts as a claimant. [CASE-249](../cases.d/CASE-249.md) (A's construction, not run;
stipulated, Proposed; first line realizability) illustrates [CLAIM-662](../claims.d/CLAIM-662.md) and
[CLAIM-663](../claims.d/CLAIM-663.md) and bears on [QUESTION-456](../questions.d/QUESTION-456.md) and [QUESTION-279](../questions.d/QUESTION-279.md).

**Two routes, kept apart.** [CLAIM-657](../claims.d/CLAIM-657.md) (the owner's, from the review of [#485](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/485)
on 2026-11-12; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency)
rests on [CLAIM-652](../claims.d/CLAIM-652.md) and [CLAIM-662](../claims.d/CLAIM-662.md), bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md), and complements
[CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) and [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md): an organization can become normatively less
informed without censoring anyone by selective disappearance and by costly
discovery; "neither needs deliberate suppression, and both differ from the
counter-corrective weakening of the channels through which challenge
succeeds, so that they should not be conflated in attributing
responsibility". [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md)'s note of 2026-11-12 records the two routes.

**News.** [CLAIM-656](../claims.d/CLAIM-656.md) (the owner's, U16 of the same exchange; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; first line distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-658](../claims.d/CLAIM-658.md),
grounds in [CASE-247](../cases.d/CASE-247.md) and bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md): where attention is the
revenue, optimizing for it works against investigative journalism, whose
findings "cannot be shown to be in demand before they exist". [CASE-247](../cases.d/CASE-247.md)
(A's thought experiment; stipulated, Proposed) illustrates [CLAIM-656](../claims.d/CLAIM-656.md),
[CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) and [CLAIM-463](../claims.d/CLAIM-463.md) and bears on [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md): a newsroom whose
inability to observe the need for investigation "becomes part of the
justification for not investigating".
