# THEORY-tmpgm24m: Frustration of the needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness is a transdiagnostic process in psychopathology

## Source

- Vansteenkiste, Ryan & Soenens (2020), [LIT-tmplhywo](../literature.d/LIT-tmplhywo.md), read in [NOTE-tmpsn285](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpsn285.md):
  Theme 2, need frustration (pp. 9–12).
- Deci & Ryan (2000), [LIT-tmpc4275](../literature.d/LIT-tmpc4275.md), read in [NOTE-tmpnji9e](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpnji9e.md): need thwarting and
  its accommodations (pp. 247–251).
- Ryan & Deci (2000), [LIT-tmpckq0r](../literature.d/LIT-tmpckq0r.md), read in [NOTE-tmpwwoeb](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpwwoeb.md): need deprivation as
  "a principal source of human distress" (p. 74).

## What was actually shown

**Frustration is a separate dimension.** "The absence of need satisfaction
does not necessarily imply the presence of need frustration, whereas the
presence of need frustration denotes the absence of need satisfaction"
([LIT-tmplhywo](../literature.d/LIT-tmplhywo.md), p. 9). Measured directly (BPNSFS and similar scales),
frustration predicted burnout, eating symptoms and negative affect beyond low
satisfaction (p. 10), and covaries with depressive symptoms, stress and
suicidal ideation, the last even controlling for depression (p. 10).

**The transdiagnostic claim.** "If the psychological needs are truly
pervasive ..., need frustration should play a transdiagnostic role"; Campbell
et al. (2018a) found it "not only predicted adolescents' depressive symptoms
and eating pathology, but also largely accounted for their co-occurrence and
co-evolution across time" (p. 12). The authors frame it under "the continuity
hypothesis": no "abrupt demarcation" between growth and psychopathology
(p. 12).

**A route.** Deci and Ryan ([LIT-tmpc4275](../literature.d/LIT-tmpc4275.md)) describe three accommodations to
thwarting: need substitutes (Kasser et al. 1995: cold, controlling mothering
predicted materialism), controlled and amotivated regulatory styles, and
"rigid behavior patterns" that keep people "from dealing with their inner
experiences" and "persist into new situations in which they are not needed"
(p. 250), with anorexia as the worked case (Strauss & Ryan 1987). "The etiology
of various forms of psychopathology resides primarily in developmental
deprivations concerning basic psychological needs" (p. 247).

The transdiagnostic claim is the 2020 authors' proposal, which they call
"promising" and "young" (p. 12). One longitudinal study is offered for it.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say need frustration is the only or the primary process.**
  The 2000 paper's "primarily" is asserted, cited to a chapter.
- **It does not say direction is known.** The authors themselves note that
  symptoms "may sustain need frustration" in "a negative vicious cycle"
  ([LIT-tmplhywo](../literature.d/LIT-tmplhywo.md), p. 12).
- **It does not rest on independent measures.** Most findings correlate a
  self-report of frustration with a self-report of distress.

## Connections

- **[THEORY-tmpdbe64](THEORY-tmpdbe64.md)** (psychological inflexibility is a transdiagnostic
  process). Not declared `rivals`: both can be true. Need frustration could
  produce the experiential avoidance and rigid patterns the inflexibility
  account describes, and Deci and Ryan's "rigid behavior patterns" that keep
  people from their inner experiences ([LIT-tmpc4275](../literature.d/LIT-tmpc4275.md), p. 250) describe the same
  phenomenon from the other side. They compete only if one is claimed as the
  process to target; the record's evidence for neither is causal. The two
  literatures do not cite each other in the papers read.
- **[THEORY-tmpybbhj](THEORY-tmpybbhj.md)** (disorders maintained by self-reinforcing loops). The
  vicious cycle the 2020 authors name (frustration sustains symptoms, which
  sustain frustration) is a loop of that kind, though they do not model it.
- **Process-based therapy (Hofmann & Hayes, [LIT-544](../literature.d/LIT-544.md)).** The 2020 hope that
  interventions on "the origins of psychological need frustration" would bring
  "multiple benefits" ([LIT-tmplhywo](../literature.d/LIT-tmplhywo.md), p. 12) is a process-based programme with
  need frustration as the process; it does not yet meet that paper's standard
  of a process shown to mediate change.
