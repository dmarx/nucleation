# CLAIM-460: In the completely locked-in state, evidence of lucid judgement has to cross a channel the patient drives, which is action in active inference's sense, so such cases can show the judgement–observation loop running down but never running with no action at all, and a null result cannot tell lost judgement from a lost channel

## The claim

[CASE-107](../cases.d/CASE-107.md): "**CLIS is the test, and it is asymmetric.** Evidence of
lucidity in CLIS has to cross some channel, and a channel the patient
drives is an action in active inference's sense. Chaudhary et al.'s
patient communicated only once neurofeedback restored a contingency
between intention and outcome. So the case can show the loop running down
(Kübler and Birbaumer's prediction, [CLAIM-339](CLAIM-339.md)), but it cannot show
judgement running with no action at all, which is [CLAIM-341](CLAIM-341.md)'s defeater (b)
turned around." And from the case's reading of the fMRI study of unresponsive patients,
where the same limit holds:
"**Negative results are uninterpretable.** When a question got no signal,
'Whether the patient fell asleep ... or lost consciousness cannot be
determined'."

[CLAIM-341](CLAIM-341.md) holds that the loop keeping a lucid agent's model in touch with
what it models runs between judgement and observation, without action on
the world; its defeater (b) is lucid judgement shown to fail with every
contingency removed, by evidence that restores none. This claim says the
locked-in state, the case that looks made to decide it, cannot: whatever
shows lucidity is itself a restored contingency, and whatever shows none
is ambiguous between the judgement and the channel. It is the
evidence-for-experience line's standing point ([QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md): "Every kind
of evidence the record holds about experience reaches it through access")
applied to judgement rather than experience.

## What it does not say

- **That [CLAIM-341](CLAIM-341.md) is true, or false.** Its defeater (b) stays as stated;
  this says the locked-in state cannot supply it either way.
- **That locked-in patients lack judgement.** The point is about what
  evidence can show.
- **That no other case could.** A case with a contingency the patient
  does not control, if one were validated, would escape the asymmetry.
