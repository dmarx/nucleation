# SYNTHESIS-127: Governing possibility: what a system can still become

*The first synthesis of the line governing-possibility (atlas scope 14, [ADR-146](../decisions.d/ADR-146.md)), written under [ADR-151](../decisions.d/ADR-151.md). It takes the exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-26) into account; the exchange has no synthesis of its own, and its map of turns is in the curation entry. It adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

The line takes regulation to be the governance of which organizations a
system can attain, sustain and revise, not only of its conduct within the
organization it has. What does such governance govern, what is the shape
of the space it acts on, and what would it take for an institution to
govern its own development?

## The argument

**Governing among regimes is a different achievement from acting within
one.** The line's root is the main result of the paper it was filed
from: acting within a regime and governing among regimes differ
([CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md)). It is concluded from two separations held elsewhere, that a
stable regime need not be viable ([CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md)) and that a regime, its
governance and its capacity to transform fail separately ([CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)). If
regulation is to secure more than present conduct, its object is what the
system can still reach.

**Reach has a cost structure, and ordinary budgeting erodes it unseen.**
Reaching a configuration can cost more than holding it: the threshold of a
transition is the peak requirement along its cheapest path, which can
exceed what either end requires ([CLAIM-571](../claims.d/CLAIM-571.md), in realizability, grounded by
the construction case [CASE-233](../cases.d/CASE-233.md), the support argued in [ARG-077](../arguments.d/ARG-077.md)). The
assistant joined A's budget ratchet to that threshold to say what a common
budget rule does. If each period's
budget is the last period's peak spending, an organization that only
holds its configuration spends the holding cost, its budget falls to that
cost, and every transition that needs slack is lost without anyone
choosing to give it up and without any sign in routine output
([CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md)). It is stability without viability produced by a budget rule.
A simulator of a heavy-rescue squad shows the loss happening fast and
being prevented by drilling or by carrying the unspent part over
([CASE-315](../cases.d/CASE-315.md), its rates invented), and a model run under six budget regimes,
output matched by construction, meets the claim's conditions exactly, and
its readiness and cost rankings come out as stated; a status review ruled
it demonstrated ([CASE-412](../cases.d/CASE-412.md)). The readiness such a
rule erodes is defined on the record not as one number but as a family of
probabilities of an effective response by a deadline, one per imposed
contingency ([TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md)). Nor can
a rule buy reach by forbidding: shrinking the admissible set cannot
enlarge the set of admissible histories, and what a constraint can
improve is only a property quantified over what a disturbance or a
process may do ([CLAIM-582](../claims.d/CLAIM-582.md)).

**The space has a geometry, and its logic depends on how resources
compose.** The owner asked how well the record characterizes possibility
space, and whether modal logic could help; A19 answered that the record
holds several geometries and no account of how they relate. The question
is filed in the owner's voice, its sharper form drawn from A19
([QUESTION-546](../questions.d/QUESTION-546.md)). The
exchange settled one piece. Read as a modal accessibility relation,
reachability at a fixed budget validates S5 when moves are reversible and
costs compose by peak, the ultrametric case the record already held
([CLAIM-581](../claims.d/CLAIM-581.md)), and S4 in the one-way case; holding must itself be
affordable or reflexivity fails ([CLAIM-1439](../claims.d/CLAIM-1439.md), the assistant's
derivation for A19's proposal, in realizability). A consequence follows
for lock-in. In S5 a distinction that can become entrenched is already
entrenched, so entrenching a latent one needs an irreversible move or a
drop in budget ([CLAIM-1414](../claims.d/CLAIM-1414.md), the assistant's, demonstrated, filling a
gap in A23's table of modal expressions).

**What is governed includes which differences could come to matter.** The
owner asked for the geometry of "potential consequentiality", and A23 put
the term forward as perhaps more basic than possibility space: not which
states are available, but which differences could come to matter, to
what, through which interactions and under what developmental conditions
([TERM-185](../terms.d/TERM-185.md)). Its measures restate budgeted profiles the record holds; what
is new is that the family of witnesses is extended by development. Its
cost of making a difference matter, the least capacity needed before the
difference between two states matters, is not a new geometry beside A23's
own budgeted distinguishability but its level-set inverse, held already
for observing budgets as the least budget that separates two states
([CLAIM-570](../claims.d/CLAIM-570.md)), and it ranks pairs the other way round from a transition
distance ([CLAIM-1416](../claims.d/CLAIM-1416.md), the assistant's check, in realizability). A23 adds that a
distinction may become consequential only through capacities acquired
jointly, each worth almost nothing alone, which a marginal optimizer would
neglect ([CLAIM-1370](../claims.d/CLAIM-1370.md)).

**What a system can become includes what it can come to value and
intend.** A17 extends the record's claim that satisfying preferences does
not show better lives ([CLAIM-674](../claims.d/CLAIM-674.md)) from engagement to the formation of
valuing: closing a possibility can eliminate the chance to become someone
able to value what is now unintelligible ([CLAIM-1356](../claims.d/CLAIM-1356.md)). A21 adds that
an unrealized purpose need not be an existing purpose that is
inaccessible: a purpose not yet constituted is generated, not activated,
and cannot be characterized by counting the reachable states of a
present model ([CLAIM-1350](../claims.d/CLAIM-1350.md), complementing A17's purposes constituted
rather than aggregated, [CLAIM-1445](../claims.d/CLAIM-1445.md), in distributed-agency). To the
record's three learner frontiers, operational, developmental and of
inquiry ([CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md)), A21 proposes adding an axiological frontier, what one
can come to care about, and a constitutional one, what can acquire
legitimate authority over one's organization, which need not move with
the others ([CLAIM-1432](../claims.d/CLAIM-1432.md)).

**Who can govern it is a capability of its own, and it can fall
behind.** A4 sorts capabilities by role: constitutional when they let an
organization's constituents take part in determining, contesting and
revising its purposes and arrangements, productive when they let it
accomplish them ([TERM-180](../terms.d/TERM-180.md)). A16 states the three reaches over an
institutional configuration of purposes, capacities, authority,
dependencies, knowledge and governing mechanisms, with purpose part of
the developing state, and observes that an institution can widen its
operational reach while narrowing its purposive or constitutional reach
([TERM-189](../terms.d/TERM-189.md)). A4's dynamic hypothesis is that governance can improve
and still fall behind what it governs, if the two grow at different rates
([CLAIM-1355](../claims.d/CLAIM-1355.md)); without a measure of the capacity it must govern, it is
interpretive.

**What a platform for governing development would need.** The owner asked
what the record's account requires of a platform for governing an
institution's alignment and development, designed as if Luria did not
exist ([QUESTION-545](../questions.d/QUESTION-545.md)). A15's answer is a design objective, developing
new collective capacities without losing the constituents' ability to
govern the conditions of their exercise, and fourteen requirements, each
derived from an entry the record holds ([CLAIM-1453](../claims.d/CLAIM-1453.md)): among them, that
the institution be represented as an evolving organization, that
heterogeneous demands keep independent standing ([CLAIM-1252](../claims.d/CLAIM-1252.md)), that an
optimizer's jurisdiction be explicit ([CLAIM-1287](../claims.d/CLAIM-1287.md), [CLAIM-1334](../claims.d/CLAIM-1334.md)), that
epistemic findings stay consequential when they lower the score
([CLAIM-1064](../claims.d/CLAIM-1064.md)), that the grammar be amendable ([CLAIM-419](../claims.d/CLAIM-419.md)), that
developmental possibilities be represented (the reorganization kernel,
[TERM-167](../terms.d/TERM-167.md)), that constitutional change be governed ([CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md)), and that
privacy and protected opacity be kept ([CLAIM-1251](../claims.d/CLAIM-1251.md)). Two corrections stand
against the answer. The assistant's note on the claim finds two tensions
the requirements leave open: legibility of overseers against protected
opacity, and a requirement that the platform answer for itself that is
met only through exit, so through the governance of dependence
([CLAIM-1326](../claims.d/CLAIM-1326.md)). The assistant's note on [TERM-167](../terms.d/TERM-167.md) finds that A15's formal
criterion is a constraint, not a kernel, and that if the governing
apparatus sets its own readiness thresholds, every state is viable and
the criterion says nothing. A15's tests are a battery of deliberate disturbances, at their
strongest applied to two institutions matched on ordinary performance and
different in governing architecture ([LEAD-304](../leads.d/LEAD-304.md)).

## Where it stands

The firm part of the line is formal: the budget ratchet and its
simulations ([CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md), [CASE-315](../cases.d/CASE-315.md), [CASE-412](../cases.d/CASE-412.md)), the monotonicity of hard
constraints ([CLAIM-582](../claims.d/CLAIM-582.md)), and the modal results the exchange added
([CLAIM-1439](../claims.d/CLAIM-1439.md), [CLAIM-1414](../claims.d/CLAIM-1414.md)), all demonstrated, with [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) the
in-force interpretive root. Everything the exchange added beyond the
modal results is A's and Proposed: potential consequentiality and its
joint capacities, the forming of valuing and of purpose, the two new
frontiers, constitutional capability, developmental freedom, the rate
hypothesis and the platform. None has been tested. The separating cases
that would tell potential consequentiality apart from reachability,
predictive accuracy, preference change and resource accumulation are
named and undesigned ([LEAD-285](../leads.d/LEAD-285.md)), developmental freedom asks for an
operationalization A16 says it lacks, and how the record's geometries fit
into one language stays open ([QUESTION-546](../questions.d/QUESTION-546.md)).

## Not stated on the record

- **The budget ratchet as a route to entrenchment.** [CLAIM-1414](../claims.d/CLAIM-1414.md)
  makes a drop in budget one of the two ways a latent distinction becomes
  entrenched, and [CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md) shows a budget rule that drops the budget
  without anyone choosing it. That last-peak budgeting entrenches whatever
  it leaves reachable is a natural join, and no component states it.
- **The axiological frontier measured by potential consequentiality.**
  [CLAIM-1432](../claims.d/CLAIM-1432.md) names what one can come to care about as a frontier, and
  [TERM-185](../terms.d/TERM-185.md) quotes A21's line that the geometry of possibility is
  perhaps the geometry of what we can come to care about. No entry says
  the separating budget of [CLAIM-1416](../claims.d/CLAIM-1416.md) measures that frontier.
- **Constraints that enable valuing.** [CLAIM-582](../claims.d/CLAIM-582.md) says a hard constraint
  cannot enlarge reach; [CLAIM-1356](../claims.d/CLAIM-1356.md) says closing a possibility can
  foreclose a valuing. Whether a constraint can still help a valuing form,
  as a property over what a process may do, no entry says.
- **[CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) and the platform.** That the platform's objective is the
  institutional form of governing among regimes is a reading of A15
  against the line's root; neither [CLAIM-1453](../claims.d/CLAIM-1453.md) nor [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md) says it.
