## The account

Li, Zoumpoulis, Puranam, Parker et al. (2026) report that, in building and
revising theories of inequality discourse against an empirical benchmark, LLMs matched or
outperformed most individual human scientists and produced more complex theories, but the
complexity did not improve prediction, human theories were more diverse and gained more from
aggregation, and LLMs revised more after seeing evidence without the revision tracking their
prior errors as humans' did.
