## Why this is here

A18 of the exchange named Michela Massimi, with Giere, and said she "especially emphasizes how scientific perspectives intersect and develop over time, which suggests a model for cross-perspectival accountability"; it cited no work. The identification of this book is the record's, as the full statement of her view; it is a likely referent, not one the exchange confirmed. Bears on [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md), [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md): her intersecting perspectives are the scientific form of the adjudication between standpoints that question asks about.
