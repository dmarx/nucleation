## 1. Core concepts (TERMs)

| Code | Status | Name | Definition, as the record states it |
|---|---|---|---|
| TERM-088 | Active | individuation, and which sense | The four senses are "descriptive (an inquiry can distinguish and track it), dynamical (its organization persists under specified dynamics and perturbation, with robust identity and boundaries), constitutive (its relational organization helps fix its identity and boundary), and organizational autonomy, the further achievement of contributing to the maintenance of its own constitutive constraints". "The senses are not stages, and not degrees of existence. A pattern can be dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside … That is individuation without autonomy." It excludes the "genetic, or operational, sense of Simondon's readers", individuation as a process that yields a candidate: "a claim that means it says so". The owner's second review: "A persistent externally sustained pattern may still be a dynamically individuated entity." Its note of 2026-10-29 found that only CLAIM-474 cites the sense it means. |
| TERM-072 | Active | perspective, and which sense: organizational, represented or phenomenal | Sense 1 (A11): "A dynamically realized regime of interpretive relationships in which the availability, significance, and efficacy of local interpretations are mutually conditioned through an identifiable organization of context, evaluation, and possible revision." Sense 2 is a system's model of its own standpoint, kept apart from a "represented owner". Sense 3 is "A lived point of view". A self-maintaining perspective is "a subclass of sense 1, not a fourth sense". Adopted 2026-10-29 "with character and lineage read as relations among realizations, not senses". The vocabulary note distinguishes realization, kind, lineage, distributed realization and representation. ## 2. Central theses (CLAIMs)

Each entry gives status, warrant and role, then its premises (`rests_on`), its voice and a short quotation.

### 2a. Individuation criteria and the partial order (§1, §6)

- **CLAIM-474** (Active; interpretive; thesis; rests on CLAIM-471, 480, 481, 470 and 475, all Active; uses TERM-088). "From interconnection to individuation, the relationships are distinct achievements that form a partial order, not a nested sequence: none implies the next, and a later one need not require every earlier one." The voice is the record's, filed at the owner's request of 2026-10-27. Its list of failed implications includes "Persistence without individuality" and "Persistence without autonomy. A pattern can be maintained entirely by external supply". Admitted by ADR-075's hypothesis route.
  - The owner's rule of method (note of 2026-10-31): "derive the dependency structure of the achievements separately from the order in which an inquiry met them".
  - Its central experiment is CASE-179 (Active, proposed study). Its first run, CASE-333 (Proposed, executed), "made the achievements … come apart on purpose".
  - Note of 2026-11-24: A32 §22 inserts contracts into the order ("A possible formation history. The achievements remain distinct and need not occur in this order"), "by A, not argued for beyond its statement".
  - ARG-tmpeukxb (**Rejected**) refuses A4's use of this claim for memetic nucleation: "CLAIM-474 orders other things".
- **CLAIM-194** (Active; interpretive; counter; objects to CLAIM-157 and CLAIM-174). "A gap or coupling criterion cannot tell a boundary a system produces from one imposed on it: a membrane the cell makes and a beam joining two pendulums pass alike". "The criterion has no term for who made the coupling." The voice is the record's. Two limits it states:
  - "That production is measurable where coupling is" is not claimed (THEORY-072 is Proposed).
  - Its note of 2026-11-24 marks A42 §7's citation of it as a miscitation: "no observer is involved".
- **CLAIM-157** (Proposed; interpretive; thesis) is the proposed answer to **QUESTION-035** (Open; refines QUESTION-009): "Relative to a stated target, when is a combined form one system, where does its boundary lie, and how long does it last?" An answer is "A criterion that, given a stated target and measurements of a candidate, returns a verdict that can be wrong." Its note of 2026-11-22: in CASE-333, "Mutual entrainment read from timing and gluing pass both alike; mutual entrainment by intervention and conserved coherence after the drive is withdrawn tell them apart" (CLAIM-903, Proposed, demonstrated).
- **CLAIM-405** (Active; interpretive; thesis). "An individuation is well defined only once its criterion is fixed … once the criterion is fixed a verdict can be false." **CLAIM-152** (Active; demonstrated): sufficiency-based individuation is target-relative.
- **CLAIM-075** (Active; interpretive; **disclaimed**; first line organizational-normativity). "Viability does not establish what ought to persist." Its quotation: "An unjust institution may be highly viable." Its note of 2026-11-24 quotes A40 §4.2: "Persistence is evidence that an arrangement has been reproducible. It is not sufficient evidence of what makes its reproduction possible or whether its reproduction is justified."
- **CLAIM-418** (Active; interpretive; not a member). "Possessing, enacting or being shaped by an organizational perspective does not establish an independent agent". **CLAIM-424** (Active; interpretive; not a member): reflexive governance "is a capacity some organizations of perspective have and others lack, not what makes something a perspective".

### 2b. Constitutive relations, maintenance and provenance (§2, formal centre)

- **CLAIM-522** (Active; **demonstrated**; thesis; grounds CASE-189; uses TERM-100). It is not a member: the audit counts it a loose anchor that the design "cuts against". The claim: "Recovery under interventions on state cannot show where maintenance is realized: an outside controller that measures the constraint capacities and supplies the internal production law as state feedback yields the same trajectory under every perturbation of state … only interventions on the production mechanisms, relative to a declared boundary, separate the two." From A10 §4, checked. "Full observation of c changes nothing."
  - What separates the two systems is "disabling a production pathway inside the declared boundary … or interrupting the controller".
  - Its limit is the ideal controller. Constant controllers are caught by perturbing the state: CASE-265, and CASE-332 (0 of 64 against 64 of 64), "outside this claim's ideal case".
  - Bourrat's release test (note of 2026-11-22) intervenes "on the imposing mechanism", relative to "a boundary declared in advance".
- **CLAIM-414** (Active; demonstrated; not a member). "Matched ordinary trajectories can conceal differences in an interpretation's efficacy, in the allocation of that efficacy, and in the maintenance of its enabling constraints".
- **CLAIM-528** (Active; interpretive) splits "realized" into "produced through" and "consisting in", "and interventions show only the first". **CLAIM-516** (Active; demonstrated): under turnover an acyclic production graph decays, so a persisting capacity needs a productive cycle, a condition "weaker than closure".
- **CASE-189** (Active, construction from A9 to A12). Two constraints producing each other hold a coupling, with a saddle-node at damage 0.5. A transient encounter forms a connector "maintained by what it enables", and "every single-pathway knockout ends it". The regime ends at damage ≈ 0.691 "where the coupling's gap is still ≈ 0.989" (CLAIM-521, Active: "as tightly integrated at the edge of collapse as well inside the regime"). The audit says CASE-189 belongs under Art. 1, not Art. 7.
- **CASE-191** (Active, construction, built at the owner's request). "a capacity upheld from outside is told apart by knockouts afterwards, but one installed by an outside pulse and then self-maintained is told apart only by a knockout during formation". "After the fact the two are the same organization."

### 2c. Persistence of product and producer (abstract)

- **CLAIM-280** (Active; interpretive; thesis; grounds CASE-079, CASE-006). "A pattern can persist while every constituent is replaced and some constituents work against it … so the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts." It is A8 §4 and §9 on the owner's r/place case: "An image can endure without an enduring organization behind it, while an organization can endure despite radically changing the image it produces." What it does not say: "that the pattern, or the coalition, is a self".
- **CLAIM-329** (Active; interpretive; not a member). A collective persists "by causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by resemblance". **CLAIM-615** (Proposed) holds that identity over time is carried "not by its constituents or by any structure invariant across its realizations".

## 6. The digester's own reading (not the record's)

**What Article 1 can rest on (Active).**
- Demonstrated: CLAIM-522, CLAIM-414, CLAIM-516, CLAIM-604 and CLAIM-597, CLAIM-249 and CLAIM-132, CLAIM-tmpqbaic, CLAIM-tmpq1lyz, CLAIM-152, and CLAIM-521 (on CASE-189).
- Interpretive: CLAIM-474, CLAIM-194, CLAIM-280, CLAIM-329, CLAIM-405, CLAIM-418, CLAIM-424, CLAIM-528, CLAIM-600 and CLAIM-075 (disclaimed).
- Vocabulary: TERM-088, TERM-072, TERM-100 and TERM-070.

The individuation half of the article (§§1, 2, 5, 6) is well grounded. Its character half (§4) is not.

**What is only Proposed.**
- The character side: CLAIM-137 (whose condition "is still unmet"), CLAIM-417 (held back by the habitus competitor), CLAIM-tmpjitli, CLAIM-tmp4mk0t and CLAIM-tmphi3mx.
- Contracts as character: CLAIM-tmpersgx, on a Proposed case.
- CLAIM-364, every nucleation-site claim (CLAIM-1004, CLAIM-tmp1ng5k, CLAIM-tmp590sw), CLAIM-762, CLAIM-157, CLAIM-615 and CLAIM-672.
- §4's "Investigate transformation-sensitive profiles, invariants and equivalence classes" has no built invariant for any perspective.

**Tensions the article must handle.**
- **The design against CLAIM-522, more exactly than the audit puts it.** "Exchange components" and "perturb relational constraints" are interventions on state, and CLAIM-522 says they cannot locate maintenance against an ideal controller. "Remove initiating supports" is an intervention on a mechanism, but on the *initiating* support. CLAIM-522's separating move is "interrupting the controller" that *maintains*, or disabling a pathway inside a declared boundary. Withdrawal also needs CLAIM-600's caution ("slow reversion can mimic it"). A boundary must be declared before any knockout (CLAIM-522, CLAIM-194, ARG-050).
- **"How it originated" is formation provenance.** The stated contribution is distinguishing "what an organization is from how it originated, how it is implemented and how it persists". TERM-100 and CASE-191 say formation provenance is not readable from the present organization: "After the fact the two are the same organization." The design as stated, on matched organizations already formed, cannot reach it without a record kept or an intervention during formation.
- **Sense slippage against TERM-088.** The abstract's "reflexive autonomy" is not TERM-088's organizational autonomy (closure). Reflexive governance is CLAIM-424's further achievement. The abstract's "individuals emerge through the formation and maintenance of constraints" is the genetic sense TERM-088 excludes unless declared. §1's "boundaries, coupling strength, coherence and persistence" are criteria; TERM-088 names "what is claimed, not how it is measured".
- **Character against lineage for persistence.** The abstract says persistence is "the maintenance and regeneration of characteristic relationships". CLAIM-329 (Active) says a collective persists "by causal transmission … not by resemblance". CLAIM-615 says not by "any structure invariant across its realizations". Characteristic relationships are a character, which is a kind (CLAIM-132). Taken as persistence, they risk being resemblance. The abstract's own "without identifying character with … developmental lineage" points the right way. TERM-072 and CLAIM-417 keep character and lineage as separate relations among realizations.
- **The character transfer is twice unearned.** It moves from groups to communicative kinds (CLAIM-137, Proposed, its condition unmet), then to perspectives ("new territory"), with CLAIM-249 against every unanchored comparison. A49's "without assuming every organizational transformation possesses group structure" concedes the difficulty without meeting it. CLAIM-tmpqbaic (Active) is the one firm result here, and it limits what a character sees: transformations, not merged states.
- **Contracts in character.** CLAIM-tmpersgx puts contracts in the character, which TERM-072 and CLAIM-417 do not list among a perspective's objects. Its separating probes are interventions. That is consistent with CLAIM-364 ("Every discriminating test is an intervention or a history") and with CLAIM-522.
- **The memes entries.** The owner's ruling makes CLAIM-tmp4mk0t primary, which fits A49 Art. 7 §3's hedge, yet only CLAIM-tmpjitli is a member. CLAIM-tmp590sw, filed after the ADR, bears on §3 and §4: divergence of perspectives by completion of a meme. CLAIM-474 must not be cited for memetic nucleation (ARG-tmpeukxb, Rejected).

**What is missing.**
- No case of matched organizations differing in maintenance provenance with a *perspective* as the maintained object. CASE-189 and CASE-191 maintain scalar capacities, and CASE-148 is not run.
- No built character invariant for any communicative kind or perspective.
- No answer to QUESTION-281's first clause for a form the record individuates. The Life readings are still the owner's choice.
- No entry for §3's "durable organizational regimes" beyond CLAIM-600, CLAIM-604, CLAIM-597 and CASE-189's folds.
- Nothing ties CLAIM-762's reification by equivalence classes to character as a classifier of kinds, though both concern equivalence classes. A link would need ADR-075's test.

- The request behind CLAIM-474 and its rule of method.
