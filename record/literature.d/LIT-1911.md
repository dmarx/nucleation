# LIT-1911: Informational Ontology: The Meaning of Gilbert Simondon’s Concept of Individuation

Iliadis, A. (2013). Informational ontology: The meaning of Gilbert Simondon's concept of individuation. *communication +1*, 2(1), 1–19. doi:10.7275/R59884XW.

Read in full in [NOTE-1485](../notes.d/NOTE-1485.md).

## Key takeaways

- Simondon's notion of information extends the cyberneticists' (Shannon,
  Wiener, Osgood) rather than rejecting it: it adds "interoperability"
  and "indeterminacy" and treats information "as" reality, not only
  "about" or "for" it (pp. 5–9).
- Four concepts carry the ontology (pp. 10–16): metastability (information
  exists only between "two disparate realities"), individuation
  (information "is the formula of individuation"), transduction (the
  meeting of two disparate informational realms, which "points to the
  emergence of a new informational structure, one that resolves a
  disparity between fields", p. 13) and concretization (the technical
  object's non-additive self-integration, "the engine that drives
  individuation", p. 15).
- For communication research, an "individuative methodology" would study
  how communicative structures modulate in the act of communication
  rather than the transmission of meaning between pre-given entities
  (p. 17).

## Why this is here

The owner asked for the works Bencherki, Brummans & Vézy (2024,
[LIT-1902](LIT-1902.md)) lean on. The parent takes three things from Iliadis:

- **The epithet** (p. 1354): Simondon is "the first true philosopher of
  information [and communication]" (Iliadis p. 1). The words are
  Iliadis's own opening sentence. The bracket is the parent's
  addition: Iliadis argues that Simondon gives communication a
  philosophy only on pp. 17–18.
- **The definition of individuation's outcome** (p. 1355): "The
  provisional outcome of individuation is the meaning that emerges from
  'a new informational structure, one that resolves a disparity between
  fields' (Iliadis, 2013, p. 13)." The quotation is exact and the page
  is right. In Iliadis the phrase describes transduction, which "signals
  the beginning of the process of individuation". The parent makes it
  the outcome and adds "meaning". The addition has warrant in Iliadis's
  p. 11, where Simondon's information is "a meaning that arises when an
  operation of individuation discovers that the two disparate yet real
  dimensions may be a system of information". Faithful in substance.
- **Organizational realities** (p. 1351): Iliadis is cited beside
  Bencherki & Iliadis 2021 for "the communicative encounters of
  individuation processes (events) through which organizational
  realities are formed". Iliadis says nothing about organizations. The
  citation is loose: it holds for Bencherki & Iliadis ([LIT-1922](LIT-1922.md)),
  not for this paper.

The record holds Simondon's own book as [LIT-1648](LIT-1648.md), Unavailable, so this
is one of the record's second-hand readings of him; its quotations are
from the 2007 Aubier reprint of L'individuation psychique et collective
([LIT-1900](LIT-1900.md)), in Iliadis's translation. [THEORY-1276](../theory.d/THEORY-1276.md) states what it
supports.
