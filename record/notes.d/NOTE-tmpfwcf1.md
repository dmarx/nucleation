# NOTE-tmpfwcf1: Waldron and Penner — Property and ownership

## Contribution

A survey that separates the concept of property, specific regimes, and the
justification of private property, with a Hohfeldian analysis of default
title.

## Key results

- **Definition** (opening): "Property is a general term for rules governing
  access to and control of material resources and other things of value."
- **Private property** (§2): "various contested resources are assigned to
  the decisional authority of particular individuals"; the owner "may act
  on her own initiative without giving anyone else an explanation", and her
  right to decide "applies whether or not others are affected by her
  decision." Hence "private property is continually in need of public
  justification". Under regulation the owner may end up "functioning as
  steward of society's decisions."
- **The bundle** (§2): the owner's position "is best understood not as a
  single right to the exclusive use and control of the object in question,
  but as a bundle of rights, which may vary from case to case (Honoré
  1961)"; that conception "has encountered resistance (Penner 1996; Smith
  2012; Klein & Robinson 2011)". Among bundle theorists "the right to
  exclude is usually seen as the key to ownership, even if it is one among
  many other rights"; others would "place less emphasis on the right to
  exclude and more on the owner's power of agenda-setting".
- **Default title** (§2): "one (Hohfeldian) liberty which is protected with
  one (Hohfeldian) claim right, and two (Hohfeldian) powers (Penner 2020, ch
  1)": to use, to exclusive possession, to license and to transfer.
- **Use rights** (§4): "a system of use rights is extremely epistemically
  demanding, whilst a system of property rights is not".

## Assumptions

Mostly rights in physical things; the liberal default title of modern legal
systems as the model.

## Concepts

Common, collective and private property; bundle of rights; property as the
law of things; the right to exclude; agenda-setting; default title as a
liberty, a claim and two powers; use rights.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CASE-282](../cases.d/CASE-282.md).** Gives the incidents the recycler's owner holds (use,
  exclusive possession, licence, transfer) and the owner's right to decide
  "whether or not others are affected", which is the case's threat to cut
  off supply; "continually in need of public justification" and the
  steward point are the counter-pressure the case relies on.
- **[QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md).** Common, collective and private property are three
  answers to who decides; the entry does not ask who may change the regime.
- **[QUESTION-480](../questions.d/QUESTION-480.md)** and **[CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md).** Private ownership is a
  standard case of authority that owes no account ("without giving anyone
  else an explanation"), a boundary for any ground of answerability in
  consequential dependence.
- The exchange's A93 is partly faithful: the entry's discussion is
  three-way (bundle with exclusion as key; relation to a thing;
  agenda-setting), not bundle against "authority to exclude".

## Limitations

§§3–5 only searched. A survey with no single account.
