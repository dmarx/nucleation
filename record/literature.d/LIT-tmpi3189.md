# LIT-tmpi3189: Perceived responsibility towards future generations and environmental concern: Convergent evidence across multiple outcomes in a large, nationally representative sample

Syropoulos, S. & Markowitz, E. M. (2021). *Journal of Environmental Psychology*, 76, 101651.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A measure of felt obligation to the future, beside legacy motivation (Zaval et al., [LIT-tmpetcju](LIT-tmpetcju.md)), from the same research group. For [QUESTION-tmpmx9ha](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpmx9ha.md).

## Read in its place

The work cannot be read here, so the record holds it at second hand.

Read in place of Syropoulos and Markowitz 2021: Syropoulos, Kraft-Todd,
Mah, Markowitz and Young, OSF preprint wknuq (27 August 2023), the same
first author's larger later study of the same construct. It gives of the
2021 paper only a one-line citation, as "preliminary work" finding concern
for future generations' well-being "weakly or not at all related to
political identity or partisanship" (p. 3). It does show the construct's
later form: RFG endorsed by most people, half as tied to conservatism as
climate responsibility, predicting self-reported proenvironmentalism with
demographics controlled, which agrees with the LIT's summary in kind.
Missing still: the 2021 sample, its measures, its outcomes and its effect
sizes, and so whether its summary is right.
