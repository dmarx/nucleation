**J8.1 in detail.** The previous draft called defeated standing "a policy-level failure that no evaluation over realized outcomes can represent". CLAIM-741 has two separations: endpoint against history ("An evaluation of endpoints cannot tell them apart; one of histories can") and history against policy (on "the history in which the patient consents"). So the failed-objection case is the first separation; the policy level is reached only where no objection is made. The precedent: SYNTHESIS-102's Arm B tested "CLAIM-745 with CLAIM-741: a categorical gate fails a right … at the level of policy" and failed it as "a substitution instance of CLAIM-741's second separation …, an inference CLAIM-741 already holds".
