# NOTE-2314: Harvey (2004) — The 10 Principles of Burning Man

## Contribution

The organization's own statement of its ethos, and the primary source for
the wording that secondary accounts such as St John's ([LIT-2188](../literature.d/LIT-2188.md)) quote.

## Key results

1. **Status of the principles.** Written "as guidelines for the
   newly-formed Regional Network"; "They were crafted not as rules to govern
   how we should act, but as a projection of the community's ethos as it
   developed organically in the desert, and a set of cultural behaviors
   reflecting shared values." "Offered, in no particular order".
2. **Gifting.** "Burning Man is devoted to acts of gift giving. The value of
   a gift is unconditional. Gifting does not contemplate a return or an
   exchange for something of equal value." Identical to St John's quotation.
3. **Radical Self-reliance**, the whole principle: "Burning Man encourages
   the individual to discover, exercise and rely on their inner
   resources." It speaks of inner resources, not material self-provision,
   and sets no limit on what may be gifted.
4. **Decommodification.** "In order to preserve the spirit of gifting, our
   community seeks to create social environments that are unmediated by
   commercial sponsorships, transactions, or advertising"; "We resist the
   substitution of consumption for participatory experience." It is
   justified by gifting.
5. **Radical Self-expression** "arises from the unique gifts of the
   individual"; "It is offered as a gift to others. In this spirit, the
   giver should respect the rights and liberties of the recipient."
6. **Communal Effort.** "Our community values creative cooperation and
   collaboration."
7. **Participation.** "Our community is committed to a radically
   participatory ethic"; "We achieve being through doing. Everyone is
   invited to work. Everyone is invited to play."
8. **Leaving No Trace** (the page's name). "We are committed to leaving no
   physical trace of our activities". It does not say that participants
   pack in their supplies; that is St John's description of practice.
9. **Civic Responsibility.** Organizers "must also assume responsibility for
   conducting events in accordance with local, state and federal laws."
10. **Radical Inclusion.** "Anyone may be a part of Burning Man. We welcome
    and respect the stranger. No prerequisites exist for participation".
11. **Immediacy.** "Immediate experience is, in many ways, the most
    important touchstone of value in our culture"; "No idea can substitute
    for this experience."

## Assumptions

Not an argument: it states values. Implicitly, that a culture can be
described by principles that are "not rules", and that gifting and
decommodification sit with paid tickets and a nonprofit organization, which
it does not address.

## Concepts

Unconditional gift; decommodification; radical self-reliance as inner
resources; self-expression as a gift; communal effort; participation;
civic responsibility; leaving no trace; radical inclusion; immediacy;
Regional Network.

## Bearing on the record

- **On the LIT's characterization.** Mostly right. Author and year are
  confirmed by the organization itself, with the purpose ("guidelines for
  the newly-formed Regional Network"). The ten are confirmed, under the
  page's names "Leaving No Trace", "Radical Self-reliance" and "Radical
  Self-expression", in a different order from St John's. The Gifting
  wording is confirmed verbatim. The current address is
  https://burningman.org/about-us/10-principles/.
- **[CLAIM-603](../claims.d/CLAIM-603.md)**: corrects its reading of Radical Self-reliance as
  self-provision; supports its point that the receiver has a path by which
  to give.
- **[NOTE-1662](NOTE-1662.md)**: answers its open question on how Radical Self-reliance is
  worded.
- **[THEORY-1497](../theory.d/THEORY-1497.md)**, **[THEORY-1546](../theory.d/THEORY-1546.md)**: confirm their premise, the
  unconditional gift; the rest is St John's reading.
- **[LIT-2188](../literature.d/LIT-2188.md)**: its stand-in role is over.
- **[NOTE-1658](NOTE-1658.md)**, **[NOTE-1679](NOTE-1679.md)**: the "not reached" mentions are now stale.
- **No instruction for machine-learning practice.**

## Limitations

- A current web page, revisable, with no revision date; the 2004 text is
  not established by this copy.
- Statements of values, not of practice.
- "Not as rules" leaves the principles' force vague, and nothing says how
  conflicts between them are settled.
