# CLAIM-tmp812ex: A stronger candidate for a pseudo-self than a crowd answering questions or an ensemble run by an external controller is an organization with historically maintained integration, differentiated inquiry, consequential governance, reflexive revisability and adaptive viability

## The claim

A64b §6: "A mere crowd answering questions isn't necessarily a pseudo-self. Neither is a fixed ensemble of specialists coordinated by an external controller." A stronger candidate would exhibit:

- **historically maintained integration**: "its perspectives influence and reproduce a recognizable organization across interactions";
- **differentiated inquiry**: "its perspectives can make distinct contributions to the joint question–answer frontier";
- **consequential governance**: "the organization allocates authority and resources among these contributions";
- **reflexive revisability**: "the processes governing inquiry can themselves become objects of effective inquiry";
- **adaptive viability**: "the organization maintains the capacities needed to continue this activity and reorganize it as demands change".

"These are proposed criteria rather than established sufficient conditions for agency or selfhood." There "need not be one owner of the process"; the pseudo-self "is the organization of relations through which the perspectives collectively sustain the game". A64b adds: "In fact, I would consider whether pseudo-self undersells the possibility. It may be a genuine organizationally self-maintaining collective, even if it is not one phenomenal subject", keeping TERM-072's senses and CLAIM-418's caution that an organizational perspective does not establish an agent.

## Against the record's definition and question

TERM-tmpxwa7g defines a pseudo-self as a multi-perspectival organization with more than one addressable carrier, "causally integrated relative to a declared probe family and history". The first criterion is that integration; the other four are additions, and they make the term a graded candidacy rather than a test.

On QUESTION-tmp4fkkx, whether a pseudo-self differs in principle from an ecosystem of them: the integration criterion does not separate them, since CLAIM-tmpt93dp's ecosystems maintain regenerated slow variables. Consequential governance and reflexive revisability might: an ecosystem allocates no authority among its members' contributions and does not take its own rules as an object of inquiry. That is near CLAIM-tmpklms4's answer, that the integrated plurality is one whose members address a whole-level variable as the whole's own. The reading of the criteria against the question is the assistant's (Claude, in this session).

## Standing

Proposed, in A's voice. The criteria are stated without a probe family or horizon, so by CLAIM-tmpptr3w they inherit its relativity: whether a plurality meets them may depend on the regime it is analyzed under.
