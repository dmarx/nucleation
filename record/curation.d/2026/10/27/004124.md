The owner asked for the record to account for an exchange, "Repository
coverage assessment" (shared at
https://chatgpt.com/share/6adff120-f60c-83ea-a5a8-dc593a4cd4ad; 2026-10-27,
00:05 to 00:24 UTC). U1 asked how the record covers reductive evolution and
endosymbiosis, and U2 asked for "more". The replies, A1 and A2, audited the
record and proposed works, cases and a framework. The share page renders
client-side, so the text was read from the data embedded in the page.

**What the exchange found.** The record is strong on transitions in
individuality, multilevel selection and biological individuality. It is
moderate on holobionts, limited on eukaryogenesis, and has no treatment of
reductive genome evolution or of the endosymbiont-to-organelle transition.
A2's summary: "substantial second-order discussion of biological
individuality without much of the first-order biology of how obligate
associations evolve". The checks below confirm the absence. Before filing,
a search of record/ found no match for genome reduction, Black Queen, *Paulinella*,
*Tremblaya*, *Hodgkinia*, nitroplast, UCYN-A, McCutcheon, Husnik, Athreya,
organellogenesis or *Monocercomonoides*. "Endosymbio" occurs in three
files, [NOTE-114](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-114.md), [NOTE-159](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-159.md) and [LIT-528](../../record/literature.d/LIT-528.md), each in passing. The counts A1
reported hold: 1,529 LITs, 1,165 NOTEs, 46 LITs tagged
`philosophy-of-biology` and 18 tagged `ecology`.

**Registered.** Eighteen works, all Deferred, none read. Fifteen were cited
with a DOI or link. The other three were described without author or title.
They were identified from the exchange's own search results and confirmed
against their abstracts: the "2013 genomic study" of the mealybug system,
"Husnik et al. (2016)", and the "2019 genomic analysis" of
*Monocercomonoides*. Each was checked against Crossref and PubMed, and
Crossref was searched for a preprint under its title. The summaries are
drawn from the abstracts. Per [ADR-048](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-048.md), no anthology check was made.

**The argument.** A1 and A2 made one argument. Interdependence can grow
without cooperation, unity, autonomy or adaptation. Different routes to
dependence move different properties, and none of the routes is selection
for the collective. This is filed as [QUESTION-tmpzwgk0](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpzwgk0.md), refining
[QUESTION-035](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-035.md). The question also carries A2's five-dimension schema (D, G, R,
C, S), which A2 itself called "a conceptual schema, not an established
metric". It is the exchange's proposal and is not a CLAIM, because the
record has read none of the works it rests on. A2 tied it to three entries,
and the question cites each one: [CLAIM-060](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-060.md), [THEORY-418](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-418.md) and [THEORY-443](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-443.md).

**Corrections the lookups made to the exchange.**

- *Dates.* Three works first appeared earlier than the exchange's years.
  McCutcheon & Moran was online on 2011-11-08 (the exchange gives 2012).
  Sørensen et al. was online on 2024-12-10 (the exchange gives 2025).
  Athreya et al. was posted as a preprint on 2023-07-19 (the exchange gives
  2025). Under [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md), four other works take their preprint dates. Each
  LIT's history says which.
- *Black Queen.* The abstract states the hypothesis for free-living
  bacteria, Prochlorococcus and Pelagibacter. It attributes the genome
  reduction common in endosymbionts to drift. The exchange applies the
  hypothesis to symbiosis generally. [LIT-tmpgtxwc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpgtxwc.md) asks a reading to settle
  this.
- *Husnik et al. (2016)* has two authors, Husnik and McCutcheon.
- *How the record was described.* A1 quoted "Constitutive dependence does
  not entail alignment of interests" as [CLAIM-060](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-060.md)'s generalization. It is
  an assistant's line from an earlier exchange (A135), which [CLAIM-060](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-060.md)
  quotes, and [CLAIM-060](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-060.md)'s notes give it as the claim's converse. The claim
  itself is that an organization's success does not establish its
  constituents' well-being. A1 described [THEORY-418](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-418.md) as distinguishing
  "coordination, cooperation, and organizational integration". Its terms
  are coordination, cooperation and collaboration, with closure from
  generatively entrenched interactions. A2 gives this correctly. A1 said
  [LIT-188](../../record/literature.d/LIT-188.md), [THEORY-141](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-141.md), [THEORY-418](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-418.md) and [THEORY-443](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-443.md) "represent" frameworks.
  All four are Proposed.

**Tags.** The exchange suggested a `reductive-evolution` or
`endosymbiosis` topic "once several targeted works have entered the
record". No word is added. `ecology` already covers symbiosis and
host–microbe partnerships ([ADR-028](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-028.md)). `natural-sciences` covers biology read
outside any machine-learning claim. The eukaryogenesis and organelle-loss
works carry `natural-sciences` alone, since they are not about how
organisms interact. If the readings show the two words hiding a shared
mechanism, as A1 expected, the new word should be added by decision then.
