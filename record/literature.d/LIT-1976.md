  precuneus allocates spatial attention bottom-up to the salient item,

# LIT-1976: Where do our preferences come from? How hard decisions shape our preferences

Voigt, K. (2022). Where do our preferences come from? How hard decisions shape our preferences. *Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience*, 16, 956307.

## Key takeaways

- Choice-induced preference change is proposed to arise during hard choices, through fixation, not only after them ([NOTE-1517](../notes.d/NOTE-1517.md)).
- The tie-breaking attention is bottom-up: precuneus to the salient item, dlPFC holding it ([THEORY-1326](../theory.d/THEORY-1326.md), Proposed).
- Evidence correlational; an Opinion article, not a review.

## Standing in the record

The model Jennings ([LIT-1963](LIT-1963.md), p. 25) takes her stalemate mechanism from: with equally valued options, dorsolateral prefrontal cortex and precuneus raise attention on one, allowing a decision and an update of its value, so that hard choices shape later preferences. [THEORY-1341](../theory.d/THEORY-1341.md) rests on it at second hand. A Frontiers Opinion article, open access; read in [NOTE-1517](../notes.d/NOTE-1517.md), which finds the tie-breaking attention bottom-up and the value update correlational.
