summary: >-
  Eberhardt & Lee (2022), Philosophies 7, 30. A critique of Hoel's 2017
  account of causal emergence. Effective information is scored under a
  uniform intervention over the states at each level, and an intervention
  on a macro state is a uniform average of interventions on its micro
  states. Two numerical results follow. EI maximization merges micro
  states whose effects differ widely (in a 3×3 example, rows at absolute
  distance 0.8), so the macro variable has ambiguous manipulations in
  Spirtes and Scheines's sense. And the EI-optimal abstraction of a
  two-step matrix is not the square of the one-step abstraction, so
  abstraction and marginalization over time do not commute. The authors
  trace both to the maximum-entropy distribution, "extraneous to the
  system", and call causal capacity a normative, potential quantity.
