# LIT-2107: A Review of Incident Prediction, Resource Allocation, and Dispatch Models for Emergency Management

Mukhopadhyay, A., Pettet, G., Vazirizade, S. M., Lu, D., Jaimes, A., El Said, S., Baroud, H., Vorobeychik, Y., Kochenderfer, M. & Dubey, A. (2020). A Review of Incident Prediction, Resource Allocation, and Dispatch Models for Emergency Management. arXiv:2006.04200.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1583](../notes.d/NOTE-1583.md): §6.1 and Table 1.*

- **Deterministic coverage.** LSCP, MCLP and their extensions "assume
  deterministic system behavior in regards to response" (§6.1).
- **Why availability must be modelled.** Even with double coverage both
  covering facilities may be busy: "Accounting for such situations
  requires modeling facility availability explicitly" (§6.1).
- **MEXCLP and MALP.** MEXCLP "assumes that each facility has the same
  probability of being busy"; "MALP-II (ReVelle and Hogan, 1989), however,
  removes this assumption" (§6.1).
- **The link to the hypercube.** AMEXCLP relaxes MEXCLP's independence
  "by treating them as servers in a hypercube queuing system (Larson,
  1974)" (§6.1).
