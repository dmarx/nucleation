# SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9: Accountability without blame and normative governability: blame and accountability are proposed to come apart, so that correction does not wait on culpability, blame traced upstream supplies the provenance of an incapacity, an incapacity an institution brought about in breach of a duty does not excuse it, blame and repair do not substitute for each other, and an institution responsive within its governing mechanism need not be responsive to reasons for revising it

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, on accountability without blame and on normative governability. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day, from which every component comes; none was in any account before. Most components also carry distributed-agency. Its sibling on rights, demands and commitments, filed at the same review, is [SYNTHESIS-tmpoodbq](SYNTHESIS-tmpoodbq.md); the organizational wrong beyond harm and [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty, which several components here cite, are mapped in [SYNTHESIS-094](SYNTHESIS-094.md).*

## The account

Each link is stated by the entry named first, in its front matter unless
its body is cited. Every component is Proposed, or Open for the question.

**Blame is not accountability.** [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) (the owner's, U47; A110's
four questions, usage note, amendment of "unintentionally" to "unwitting"
and three assessments are A's and credited in its body; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) uses [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md) and
bears on [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md): an agent's diminished culpability for the reasons
it acts on "does not diminish the wrongfulness of what it does, the claims
of those it harms, or the need for correction, because the grounds of
correction are not exhausted by the grounds of blame". Its body reads
"accountable" in [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md)'s remedial sense, and says the claim
leaves open who owes the correction. [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md) (A's senses, A110 §2
with A5 §5, A9 §5 and A17 §9; the distinction it keeps is the owner's
U47; Proposed; also distributed-agency) draws on [THEORY-147](../theory.d/THEORY-147.md) and
[THEORY-207](../theory.d/THEORY-207.md): causal, attributability, culpability, remedial and
constitutive responsibility, "a verdict in one sense does not settle
another". Its body says [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md), [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md), [CLAIM-tmp0wx69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wx69.md)
and [CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md) are stated in it. Six claims of the exchange's first
parts, in dependence-and-domination, also use it.

**Blame as provenance.** [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md) (the owner's idea, U47; the label
"the provenance of normative incapacity" and the development are A110
§3's; its bearing on [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md) is A's, narrowed by the filer; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md),
uses [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md) and [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md), and bears on [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md) and
[QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): responsibility for a harmful act and for producing the
incapacity through which it was done "can come apart and attach to
different actors". Its body says it does not answer [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md), which
asks whose an organization's counter-normativity is. [CASE-tmpolqas](../cases.d/CASE-tmpolqas.md) (A's,
A110 §11 Case A, with A110 §7's negligent variant; fictional, Proposed)
illustrates [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md) and [CLAIM-tmp0wx69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wx69.md) and bears on [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md):
two officials make the same wrongful decision in good faith, and in one
institution a superior removed the review that would have caught it. Its
body says it cannot show that the removal breached a duty, which needs
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) and [ARG-tmptw59w](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptw59w.md), and that both arms are internal to one
institution, so it does not reach [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md)'s own case.

**Self-induced incapacity.** [CLAIM-tmp0wx69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wx69.md) (A's, A108 §5 and A110 §7;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md), uses
[TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md) and bears on [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md): a present incapacity to
recognize a violation does not show the party is not responsible for the
incapacity; where it dismantled the capability in breach of a duty, the
incapacity "does not excuse it", and where no one is culpable "the duty to
repair still stands". Its body says whether the dismantling breached a
duty needs [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s maintenance duty, argued in [ARG-tmptw59w](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptw59w.md), and that
it does not answer [QUESTION-449](../questions.d/QUESTION-449.md), which is about mechanisms installed by
another.

**Blame and repair do not substitute.** [CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md) (A's, A110 §8;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on
[CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) and uses [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md): assigning blame for a harmful
condition does not discharge present remedial duties, and assigning
remedial duties does not show blame, so treating one guilty official as
a repair is "blame displacement" and loading repair on whoever is nearest
is "remedial scapegoating". Its body names [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md) as a documented
instance of blame displacement, [CLAIM-707](../claims.d/CLAIM-707.md)'s moral crumple zone as the
mechanism of scapegoating, and [QUESTION-tmp2t6ve](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp2t6ve.md) as the question it bears
on most directly. [CASE-tmpd0hyp](../cases.d/CASE-tmpd0hyp.md) (A's, A110 §11 Case B; fictional,
Proposed; also distributed-agency) illustrates [CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md) and
[CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md): the same wrong through the same procedure with blameless
decision-makers, where one institution can remedy it and the other
cannot but a public authority can. Its body says it builds only the
second half of [CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md), and that whether jurisdiction grounds a
duty rather than a permission is a further premise.

**Blameless culture.** [QUESTION-tmp2t6ve](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp2t6ve.md) (the owner's suggestion, U48,
the exchange's last turn, unanswered; the wording is the filer's; Open;
also distributed-agency) stores no relation: does blameless post-incident
practice in engineering keep accountability while giving up blame, and
does it keep or lose the upstream provenance blame supplies? Its body
puts it to [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) and [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md), and names [CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md)'s
blame displacement "turned round" as one possible answer. The candidate
sources it names are the filer's, not the exchange's.

**Normative governability.** [CLAIM-tmp69o4u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp69o4u.md) (A's, A108 §6, A110 §9 and
§10, A106 §18; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) uses [TERM-tmphum9r](../terms.d/TERM-tmphum9r.md): an
institution can be reliably responsive to reasons within its governing
mechanism and unresponsive to justified reasons for revising it, and
first-order responsiveness "is no evidence of responsiveness at the
second". Its body says ρ^(2) "is named, not defined", and that the lack of
second-order responsiveness is a wrong only with [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty as a
further premise. [TERM-tmphum9r](../terms.d/TERM-tmphum9r.md) (A's term, A110 §13's version of four;
the link to reasons-responsiveness is the owner's, U46; Proposed) draws on
[THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md) and [THEORY-122](../theory.d/THEORY-122.md): the maintained, role-appropriate capacity to
recognize and respond to justified considerations, "including
considerations that require revising its own governing mechanisms";
relational, and not corrigibility, compliance or obedience. Its body
says the second-order clause is [CLAIM-tmp69o4u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp69o4u.md)'s responsiveness, that
A110 §13's last paragraph ("Deficiencies in normative governability may
explain wrongful conduct without excusing every duty arising from it")
ties the term to [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) and [CLAIM-tmp0wx69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wx69.md), and that
[CLAIM-tmp69o4u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp69o4u.md) is the first candidate claim stated in it.

## Arm B

Integrations tested at this review under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s step 5. None passes,
so nothing is filed.

- *[CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) with [CLAIM-tmpssfc1](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpssfc1.md)*: an agent's diminished
  culpability is not an exercise of a valid normative power, so the
  claim to relief persists through the agent's excuse. Already stated,
  in [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md)'s body: "The imprisoned person's claim to release does
  not become weaker merely because JoJo lacks ordinary moral competence".
- *[CLAIM-tmp0wx69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wx69.md) with [CLAIM-tmp69o4u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp69o4u.md)*: a governability deficiency an
  institution produced does not excuse what is done through it. Already
  stated, in [TERM-tmphum9r](../terms.d/TERM-tmphum9r.md)'s definition (A110 §13's last paragraph) and
  body.
- *[CLAIM-tmpw7pli](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw7pli.md) with [CLAIM-tmp69o4u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp69o4u.md)*: the visa gate is an institution
  reliable at the first order and unresponsive at the second. Route:
  discriminating prediction. Fails on a free parameter: indexed to the
  governing statute, the gate is a first-order failure (evidence the
  statute requires stops counting); indexed to the institution's own
  maintained requirements, it is first-order reliable. The sentence
  passes or fails by that choice, which no component fixes. Left under
  "Not stated".
- *[CLAIM-tmp4o53g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o53g.md) and [CLAIM-tmpsk25g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsk25g.md) with [CLAIM-tmpes6ef](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpes6ef.md) and
  [CASE-tmpd0hyp](../cases.d/CASE-tmpd0hyp.md)*: where the repair needs an arrangement no party can
  reach by permissible, resource-bounded means, a claim to correction
  persists with no one bound to complete it. Route: derivation. Fails:
  the persistence half is [CLAIM-tmpa0wdv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa0wdv.md)'s (a claim persists while the
  correctability of its remedy falls to zero), "complete" is a free
  parameter, and the "no one bound" half meets [ARG-tmptw59w](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptw59w.md)'s fallback
  from A110 §5D, duties to seek assistance, limit activity or inform.
- *[CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md) with [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)*: the provenance blame supplies is not
  descriptive provenance. Already stated, in [TERM-tmp8pnnr](../terms.d/TERM-tmp8pnnr.md)'s body ("Not
  [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s provenance") and [CLAIM-tmpixmhp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpixmhp.md)'s.

  avoids the word and says why.
- Anything with standing of its own.
