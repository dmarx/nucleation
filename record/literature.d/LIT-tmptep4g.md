  facets along the short diagonals of the rhombi, a hidden degree of

# LIT-tmptep4g: Origami structures with a critical transition to bistability arising from hidden degrees of freedom

Silverberg, J. L., Na, J.-H., Evans, A. A., Liu, B., Hull, T. C., Santangelo, C. D., Lang, R. J., Hayward, R. C. & Cohen, I. (2015). Origami structures with a critical transition to bistability arising from hidden degrees of freedom. *Nature Materials* 14(4): 389–393. doi:10.1038/nmat4232.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp0l0mn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp0l0mn.md).*

- **Zero DOF, yet it folds.** The square twist's "crease pattern has zero
  degrees of freedom (DOF) and therefore should not be foldable, can
  nevertheless be folded by accessing bending deformations that are not
  explicit in the crease pattern." (Abstract.)
- **An energy gap makes the bifurcation.** "These hidden bending DOF are
  separated from the crease DOF by an energy gap that gives rise to a
  geometrically driven critical bifurcation between mono- and
  bistability."
- **DOF depend on the energy scale.** Nf "should be thought of as a
  variable quantity that changes with the experimental energy scale."
- **Removable by design.** In the gel twist, "the addition of these creases
  removes the hysteresis (Fig. 4d)."

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on constraint architectures cites it as S3: the
square twist has zero rigid folding DOF, folds by panel bending, and goes
from monostable to bistable. All three are right. A misses the tuning
parameters (plane angle and stiffness ratio), the hysteresis and its
removal by added creases, and the paper's general thesis that the number
of degrees of freedom is "a variable quantity that changes with the
experimental energy scale". That thesis is the closest support in A's list
for A's question of when an effective constraint stops being a good
approximation, and it bears on [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md), with energy scales where the
claim speaks of time scales. Filed from it: [THEORY-tmpixfqm](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpixfqm.md).
