# LIT-2199: Interaction of "Solitons" in a Collisionless Plasma and the Recurrence of Initial States

Zabusky, N. J. & Kruskal, M. D. (1965). Interaction of "Solitons" in a Collisionless Plasma and the Recurrence of Initial States. *Physical Review Letters* 15(6): 240–243. doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.15.240.

## Key takeaways

*Not read. What follows is from Zabusky & Porter (2010), not from the letter.*

- Zabusky and Kruskal "published their numerical solutions of the KdV
  equation (and invented the term "soliton")" (Zabusky & Porter, §1).
- A sine-wave initial condition gives "right-propagating, large-amplitude
  solitons that arise from the initial condition", with near-recurrence
  (Zabusky & Porter, Fig. 3, whose source the article does not give).

## Standing in the record

Filed with the soliton batch of 2026-11-06, beside Kivshar & Malomed
(1989). Zabusky & Porter (2010), [LIT-2208](LIT-2208.md), stands in for the naming, the
numerical origin, the elastic collision with a phase shift and the
recurrence picture, in the first author's own later words
([THEORY-1501](../theory.d/THEORY-1501.md)). It does not carry the letter's equation parameters,
figures or numbers. A reading should check what the letter itself says
about the solitons' identity through interaction, and how near the
recurrence was.
