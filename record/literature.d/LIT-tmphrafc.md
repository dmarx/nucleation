# LIT-tmphrafc: A synthetic approach to Markov kernels, conditional independence and theorems on sufficient statistics

Tobias Fritz (2020), *Advances in Mathematics* 370:107239 —
[ARXIV-1908.07021](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07021)

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the abstract only: the paper develops Markov
categories, following Golubtsov and Cho and Jacobs, and treats in purely
categorical terms conditioning and disintegration, versions of conditional
independence, conditional products, "almost surely", and sufficient
statistics, proving versions of the theorems of Fisher–Neyman, Basu and
Bahadur. Its advantage is a uniform treatment of discrete, measure-theoretic
and Gaussian probability, and of stochastic processes of these kinds.
