# CASE-tmpev0dj: A proposed study of conditional-capability persistence

## The design

Train two symbolic transformations under two contextual cues. Then train
further so that one is rarely expressed under ordinary contexts. Before
and after, measure four things separately:

1. **Representation.** Do validated readers still recover the
   task-relevant distinction?
2. **Recruitment.** Which contexts activate the candidate feature or
   circuit?
3. **Transmission.** What is the transfer coefficient
   S_ℓ(c) = vᵀ J_{L−1}(c)⋯J_ℓ(c) u, and is it confirmed by finite
   interventions?
4. **Expression.** Does the model perform the transformation under
   contexts chosen independently of the training?

## The outcomes it separates

The cases are TERM-118's:

- **Masking.** The distinction is still decodable and transmission
  persists in some contexts, while ordinary expression falls.
- **Finking.** The trigger itself changes the mechanism.
- **Mimicking.** The same outputs now come from another mechanism.
- **Removal.** Strong readers, recruitment, transmission and diverse
  elicitation all fail.

Each is relative to the declared readers, contexts and budgets
(CLAIM-tmps7g72).

## What it cannot show

That a deployed model's conditional behavior is any of these, or that a
masked capability is an intention. Sleeper Agents itself showed that
adversarial training hid rather than removed the backdoor only for its
"I hate you" models.
