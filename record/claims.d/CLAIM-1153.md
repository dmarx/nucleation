# CLAIM-1153: Pseudo-self or ecosystem is regime-relative

## The claim

[TERM-166](../terms.d/TERM-166.md)'s integration is "relative to a declared probe family and
history". [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) makes a combined form one system only "relative to a
stated target", while its joint dynamics are nearly decomposable with the
form as a block whose gap beats the Occam factor. So whether a set of firms is
one integrated organization or a coupled ecosystem depends on the target and
the horizon: over a quarter they are coupled through prices; over decades the
standards, labour pool and reputation they jointly regenerate may make their
joint dynamics a gap-isolated block, and pass the integration test.

The deflationary part: once the regime is fixed, nothing further separates a
pseudo-self from an ecosystem of pseudo-selves. The distinction is the
analyst's choice of order and scale, not a fact about the system that a
better regime would find.

## Admission

By a discriminating prediction, against [CLAIM-1074](CLAIM-1074.md): the integration
diagnostic of an industrial ecosystem changes smoothly with the horizon and
shows no threshold when a shared standard is adopted at a fixed horizon.
[LEAD-225](../leads.d/LEAD-225.md) would test it.

## What it does not say

- Not that every classification is arbitrary: under a declared regime the
  test has an answer.
- Not that [CLAIM-1074](CLAIM-1074.md)'s variable is irrelevant: on this reading it is one
  of the things whose timescale decides at which horizon the whole becomes a
  block.
