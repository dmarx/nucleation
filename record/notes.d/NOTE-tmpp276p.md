# NOTE-tmpp276p: Kittay, interviewed by Robb (2026) — Disability, dependency, and care

## Contribution

Kittay's present statement of Love's Labor's central notions, with one change of view, on the state.

## Key results

1. [21:04]: "unless it's remunerated, we're really exploiting those who we put on the pedestal"
2. [30:33]: "when we care for another who's unable to care for herself, the carer needs care."
3. [30:33]: "So it's these nesting relations of dependency that I think are the basis of our society."
4. [34:22]: the tension "between insisting on support and seeing to it that there is non-domination on the part of those who give support."
5. [34:22]: "I never really thought that the state would be the only form of doulia, but I thought it would be the ultimate form of doulia."
6. [37:05]: "keeping the state pretty far away from the actual dependency relationships themselves."
7. [38:09]: "What I was really focused on in Love's Labor was the dependency worker"

## Assumptions

Human dependency is inevitable; social organization rests on relations of support, not on independent contractors.

## Concepts

Dependency worker; the dependency critique; doulia; nested dependency; caring communities; non-domination by supporters.

## Bearing on the record

- **[QUESTION-tmp9bbpk](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp9bbpk.md)**: sense 3 ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)), persons, carers and society. Doulia puts an obligation on the wider society toward those whose maintenance work sustains it, in nested relations Kittay calls "the basis of our society". That supports a constitutive-maintenance ground for sense-3 wholes. It does not transfer to sense 1, where parts need not be persons who work. Filed as `bears_on`.
- **[CLAIM-tmpwn3wr](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwn3wr.md)**: qualifies what the responsibility can require. Kittay now separates supporting dependency relations from controlling them and warns of domination by supporters (results 4–6). An agent whose responsibility is strengthened by its constituents' increased vulnerability (CLAIM-tmpwn3wr as retitled on 2026-11-13) would discharge it by support that keeps out of the relations themselves. Dated note on the claim.
- **[QUESTION-tmpojwyk](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpojwyk.md)**: unpaid dependency work is the paradigm of an indispensable contribution kept from becoming a claim (result 1); Kittay's answer is a public principle, doulia, not the contributors' bargaining power. Filed as `bears_on`.
