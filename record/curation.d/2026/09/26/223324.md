A pass over `philosophy-of-science`, the record's largest philosophy tag with
81 works. Most had been read in earlier passes. Nine works on the tag had
never had a close reading attempted.

## The audit

Four already-read works gained the tag:

| work | why |
| --- | --- |
| [LIT-021](../../record/literature.d/LIT-021.md), the emergence editorial | emergence in science; [LIT-141](../../record/literature.d/LIT-141.md) and [LIT-150](../../record/literature.d/LIT-150.md) already carry the tag |
| [LIT-077](../../record/literature.d/LIT-077.md), Grangier et al. | an informal argument about what makes quantum randomness quantum |
| [LIT-079](../../record/literature.d/LIT-079.md), causal claims in economics | the tag's blurb names "causation and evidence" |
| [LIT-086](../../record/literature.d/LIT-086.md), Barandes | a reformulation offered as an interpretation of quantum theory |

The technical quantum-foundations papers (structure theorems, graphical
calculi) were left alone. The `quantum-foundations` blurb already covers what
the theory says about the world, and adding a second word for the same thing
would say nothing new.

## The readings

Six works were read in full:

- **Active:** [LIT-110](../../record/literature.d/LIT-110.md), [LIT-114](../../record/literature.d/LIT-114.md), [LIT-127](../../record/literature.d/LIT-127.md), [LIT-169](../../record/literature.d/LIT-169.md) and [LIT-205](../../record/literature.d/LIT-205.md).
- **Proposed:** [LIT-208](../../record/literature.d/LIT-208.md).

Findings worth keeping:

- **Frigg & Werndl ([LIT-114](../../record/literature.d/LIT-114.md)).** The SEP entry has *three* frameworks, not two.
  The Boltzmann Equation (H-theorem, Lanford's theorem) was missing from the
  skim. The entry gains `information-theory` and `metaphysics`.
- **Takola & Schielzeth ([LIT-127](../../record/literature.d/LIT-127.md)).** They present the time-slice, prospective
  and trajectory views of the niche as complementary, not as a forced choice.
  They concede the concept is "mostly of a metaphorical value". The paper is
  behavioural ecology explicating its own term, so it now leads with
  `natural-sciences`. It does not cite Trappes ([LIT-169](../../record/literature.d/LIT-169.md)). Its "Trappes et al."
  is a different preprint.
- **Luque ([LIT-205](../../record/literature.d/LIT-205.md)).** The skim's section map was largely wrong. Hamilton's
  rule is only a footnote. The disputes the paper engages are van Veelen's and
  Nowak's: that the equation is a tautology, and that it lacks predictive
  power.
- **Grindrod et al. ([LIT-208](../../record/literature.d/LIT-208.md)).** `Proposed`: the claim that stability is
  "roughly proportional" to frequency is never measured.

## A missing word

[LIT-208](../../record/literature.d/LIT-208.md)'s reader found its `philosophy-of-science` tag unjustified: the paper
is philosophy of language. `linguistics` excludes meaning "as models encode
[it]", so neither of the paper's tags fit. Four more held works had the same
gap. [ADR-009](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-009.md) adds `philosophy-of-language` to the vocabulary and the
`philosophy` group, and retags all five.

## Three works that could not be reached

These stay `Deferred`, each with a status note:

- [LIT-108](../../record/literature.d/LIT-108.md) (Weyl): in copyright, lending-only scans.
- [LIT-201](../../record/literature.d/LIT-201.md) (Damuth & Heisler): paywalled at Springer.
- [LIT-180](../../record/literature.d/LIT-180.md) (Wigner): open in the PNAS archive, but not fetchable from here.

For Wigner, the reader's retries were refused by the session's permission
check. Nobody tried to route around the refusal. The next session should
simply try again.
