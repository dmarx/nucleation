The owner asked to improve the record's coverage of Ned Block, which held
only "Troubles with Functionalism" ([LIT-407](../../record/literature.d/LIT-407.md)). Notes already cited "Block
1995", "Block 1987", "Block 2009" and "Block 2023" with nothing to point
to. The anthology holds no Block, so nothing needed an [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md) second
entry. One work, "Psychologism and Behaviorism", carries
`anthology-candidate`: the anthology's evaluation topic could hold what it
says a behavioural test cannot establish.

Block's own site (nedblock.us) links his author copies of every work
filed. Each was read in full from those copies, except *The Border Between
Seeing and Thinking*, which was read through its PPR précis and seeded
Deferred.

## Two groups

- **Consciousness.** Seven papers, 1995 to 2019. They make one argument:
  access and phenomenal consciousness are distinct, phenomenology overflows
  access, and the neural basis is sensory rather than prefrontal. The 2007
  paper rivals Dennett ([LIT-431](../../record/literature.d/LIT-431.md), [LIT-433](../../record/literature.d/LIT-433.md)).
- **Functionalism, meaning and perception.** Block & Fodor 1972,
  Blockhead, two-factor conceptual role semantics, "Inverted Earth",
  "Mental Paint", the mind as software, and the précis on the border
  between seeing and thinking.

## The global workspace, filed beside Block

The owner then added two readings:

- **Mashour et al. 2020.** The record had no global-workspace source until
  now. The review rivals Block's overflow paper and his no-report paper,
  and it rivals IIT.
- **Damasio & Damasio 2022.** It rivals both IIT and the workspace.

The Damasio essay's publisher page answers automated requests with a
challenge. It was reached only through a fetch tool that summarises
pages, so its NOTE is Skimmed rather than Read, and its quotes are
unchecked. Damasio & Damasio 2023 ([LIT-387](../../record/literature.d/LIT-387.md)) is still Deferred for the same
reason. Its abstract matches the 2022 essay's opening nearly sentence for
sentence.

## Theories

Four theories come from Block's consciousness papers:

- overflow;
- a sensory, not prefrontal, basis;
- the limits of no-report designs;
- the confounded inference from a deficit to a function.

[THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md) takes Block 2002 and Block 2009 as sources.

Candidates not yet filed:

- psychologism;
- the failure of long-arm accounts of experience;
- two-factor content;
- global availability by ignition;
- consciousness supplied by homeostatic feeling.
