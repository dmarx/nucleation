# QUESTION-tmpgjiy3: Does a second source state that, in an infinite-horizon screening relationship with only short-term contracts and irreversible quitting, the patient limit is pooling with no learning when the prior that the agent is low-cost is below a threshold and firing of the high-cost agent when it is above?

## Why it is a question

Gerardi and Maestri prove the dichotomy for two types, verifiable quality
and irreversible quitting, in the limit as patience tends to one
(THEORY-1378, NOTE-1599). With rehiring it fails, by their folk theorem
(their Proposition 4). No other source on the record states it.

## What would answer it

A survey of dynamic contracting or a later paper that states or
re-derives the result. A paper that finds it fails under a nearby
assumption would also answer it, for example with more types, hidden
action or renegotiation.
