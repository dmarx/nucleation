## What this round found

- **Nucleating-argument's integrations are held as claims.** All three of
  its `complements` clusters have now been audited across the three
  rounds, and so has its open question QUESTION-038. Each integration is
  stated by a component's own body or notes. The line having no synthesis
  is a finding, as ADR-078 allows, not a gap to fill.
- **Moving a claim that has objections would strand them.** A claim's
  `objects_to` accepts only CLAIMs, and SYNTHESIS has no `objected_by`.
  None of the nine claims moved so far has an objection, so nothing is
  stranded now. A move of CLAIM-191 would strand CLAIM-189, and a move of
  CLAIM-440 would strand five. Like `rests_on` (ADR-080), the fix is
  probably a pattern for the `dialectic` chain. It should be decided with
  the first move that needs it, as that decision's demonstration.
- **A later candidate:** an Arm B synthesis of the organizational-affect
  account (CLAIM-440, 437, 439, 449, 451, 527 and 436). It would be Proposed
  at best. Five objections are open or conceded, and CLAIM-440's third
  defeater is partly met.

## Corrections to the earlier rounds

- **Organizational-affect was never set aside.** The entries of the first
  two rounds call the line "set aside", and this round's brief called
  CLAIM-039 Deferred. The owner reopened the line on 2026-10-23, and
  CLAIM-039 is Proposed. No verdict turned on it.

  Proposed". Four of its five are Active. It was audited this round and
  not admitted.
- **CLAIM-348 was shortlisted in error.** SYNTHESIS-002 integrates it, which
  the skill puts out of scope. It was audited anyway, and is not admitted.
