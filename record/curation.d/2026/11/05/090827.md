## The assistant's contribution

**[CLAIM-599](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-599.md),** demonstrated. Establishing more relations can lower the
chance of the target, so productive nucleating sets need not be closed
upward. Two productive nuclei can combine into a trap: V(∅) = 0.2,
V({a}) = V({b}) = 0.6, V({a, b}) = 0.1. A's minimal productive sets
therefore describe the productive ones only where V is monotone. Unlike
[CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s jointly satisfiable sets, which are closed downward, the
productive sets have no closure. Dunn et al.'s seams and Majikes et al.
2026's short folds are the molecular instances.
