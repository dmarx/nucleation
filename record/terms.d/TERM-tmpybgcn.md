# TERM-tmpybgcn: observable

## Definition

Manuscript §2: "We use 'observable' in an operational rather than narrowly
physical sense: a reproducible elicitation or coding procedure that assigns
outcomes to communicative realizations. Observable labels may concern lexical
register, causal relations, speaker alignment, entitlement to criticize, or a
plausible continuation. Such measurements are not necessarily independent; some
are constituted through contrasts with alternatives."

## What it is not

Not a physical observable, though A21 and A27 used the word in the quantum sense.
C6 §2 gave the operational sense as "labels for observational procedures"; the
contrastive clause is the structuralist turn of U36 (the chunk-5 reader's
observation). The levels it is organized by are [TERM-tmp8j1kl](TERM-tmp8j1kl.md).
