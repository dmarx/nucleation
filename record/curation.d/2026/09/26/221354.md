A pass over the `cognition` tag, the same shape as the epistemology and ethics
passes: audit the tag, then read every unread work on it in full.

## The audit

Twenty-seven works carried the tag. Nineteen had been read, and [LIT-128](../../record/literature.d/LIT-128.md) had
been found unreachable in the ethics pass. That left seven to read.

Two works gained the tag:

- **[LIT-147](../../record/literature.d/LIT-147.md)** (Pernu, mental causation via neuroprosthetics) is philosophy of
  mind, which the tag's blurb names.
- **[LIT-140](../../record/literature.d/LIT-140.md)** (Mallory, LLMs as stochastic measuring devices) takes the
  deflationary side of the machine-minds debate. It was unread, so it joined
  the list, making eight.

## The readings

Six works were read in full, and all are `Active`: [LIT-099](../../record/literature.d/LIT-099.md), [LIT-140](../../record/literature.d/LIT-140.md), [LIT-149](../../record/literature.d/LIT-149.md),
[LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md), [LIT-155](../../record/literature.d/LIT-155.md) and [LIT-178](../../record/literature.d/LIT-178.md). [LIT-140](../../record/literature.d/LIT-140.md) was read from the author's own September
2024 draft. The OUP chapter and the revised preprint could not be reached, and
the NOTE says so.

Corrections worth knowing:

- **Ivy & Mroczko-Wąsowicz ([LIT-099](../../record/literature.d/LIT-099.md)).** The paper stays neutral on whether any
  single percept is penetrated. The skim had it asserting that none is.
- **Martínez ([LIT-149](../../record/literature.d/LIT-149.md)).** Footnote 7 makes the account teleosemantic, which the
  skim missed. Without teleofunctions, any system can be made to look
  rate-distortion efficient.
- **Angius et al. ([LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md)).** The paper favours mechanistic explanation. Its
  primer describes transformer training as autoencoding, which is wrong.
- **Prinzing & Vazquez ([LIT-155](../../record/literature.d/LIT-155.md)).** "Outperform all other majors" ranks point
  estimates; it is not a test. All the test scores are self-reported.
- **Bottou & Schölkopf ([LIT-178](../../record/literature.d/LIT-178.md)).** The essay leaves open whether alignment
  can turn the "fiction machine" into a "verification machine". It never uses
  the word "sycophancy", which was the seed's gloss.

Two citations were corrected from "et al." to the two authors they are.

## Retags from the readings

- [LIT-155](../../record/literature.d/LIT-155.md) now leads with `social-science` and adds `epistemology`.
- [LIT-178](../../record/literature.d/LIT-178.md) and [LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md) drop `linguistics`. Its blurb excludes language "as
  models encode them", which is what both are about. [LIT-178](../../record/literature.d/LIT-178.md) now leads with
  `cognition`.
- [LIT-153](../../record/literature.d/LIT-153.md) and [LIT-149](../../record/literature.d/LIT-149.md) gain `neuroscience`: most of their evidence is neural.
