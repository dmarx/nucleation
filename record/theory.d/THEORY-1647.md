# THEORY-1647: Liang, Li, Aroles and Granter, reviewing 47 management-journal studies of content creators and platform algorithms, argue that platforms control nominally autonomous creators chiefly by allocating visibility through opaque algorithms, and that creators' metric-driven self-management and their folk theories of the algorithm, which platforms can dismiss as its authoritative interpreter, sustain that control rather than challenge it.

## The account

Liang, Li, Aroles and Granter, reviewing 47 management-journal studies of content creators and platform algorithms, argue that platforms control nominally autonomous creators chiefly by allocating visibility through opaque algorithms, and that creators' metric-driven self-management and their folk theories of the algorithm, which platforms can dismiss as its authoritative interpreter, sustain that control rather than challenge it.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1743](../notes.d/NOTE-1743.md); its key results are quoted there with locations.
