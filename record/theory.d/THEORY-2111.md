# THEORY-2111: Fairness signatures emerge in a set order

## The account

Fairness develops as a sequence of costly choices against equality: first
against getting less, then punishing unfairness, last against getting
more. Knowledge of the equality norm comes early, by 3; acting on it at a
cost comes later, with behavioural control, and its last signature depends
on local norms.

## Evidence

Review of developmental experiments with behavioural-economic games across
several societies.
