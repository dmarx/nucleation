# LIT-tmp0jm6f: Topological defects in the abelian Higgs model

Czubak, M. & Jerrard, R. L. (2012). Topological defects in the abelian Higgs model. arXiv:1210.2669. Published in *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems* 35 (2015), doi:10.3934/dcds.2015.35.1933.

## Key takeaways

- Nielsen and Olesen's 1973 proposal is a conjecture: vortex lines "should
  sweep out worldsheets that are approximately governed by the Nambu-Goto
  action" (§1).
- Theorem 1.4 proves it for initial data constructed near a given
  worldsheet, on a finite time interval, wherever the 2d minimization
  problem has a minimizer ordered by charge. That holds for λ = 1 and for
  large λ; elsewhere it is "open" (Remark 1.2).
- Finite energy puts a configuration in exactly one sector, labelled by a
  quantized vorticity (eqs. 1.13–1.14). The symmetric vortex is "linearly
  stable if |n| = 1 or λ ≤ 1, and linearly unstable" otherwise (Remark 1.1).
