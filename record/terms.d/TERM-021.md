# TERM-021: fidelity, as context-indexed distribution matching

## Definition

A24: "Let C be a family of pragmatic contexts, and let e_C denote the
distribution of communicative judgments observed in context C. A translation
τ induces a correspondence between contexts". Fidelity is
D(τ) = Σ_C w_C d(e_C^source, e_τ(C)^target): whether "the translation
preserves the pattern of context-dependent communicative judgments, rather than
... one canonical vector of meaning". It is "a candidate objective function,
not an established translation metric".

## What it is not

Not the whole of fidelity in the manuscript, where it is L_obs, one of three
terms (§6). It does not say how the context correspondence is found; A24: "the
correspondence between contexts cannot be assumed". The dynamical requirement of
[TERM-007](TERM-007.md) is added to it, not substituted for it.
