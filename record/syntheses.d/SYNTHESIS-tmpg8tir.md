# SYNTHESIS-tmpg8tir: Conferral among an organization's processes is any effect on another's share of future influence, and a grant is conferral through a maintained relation; how grants are counted and scored decides whether authority tracks reliability or entrenches: an accounting rule can keep an operator's authority independent of its own grants, trust can be the regularity of grants earned by being right, and when authority is scored against the interpretation it helped make dominant the same loop can amplify an initial advantage without fidelity, which in the linear model only the two-way coupling starts and independent scoring removes; capture runs through the evidence an evaluation receives or the criteria it weighs by, with different remedies, and the standard that would tell capture from merited trust is not supplied

*A part account of the distributed-agency line ("what an organized whole
is, and how its operative direction relates to its members'"): the part
on conferral, grants and endorsement among an organization's processes,
and on the coupling of interpretation to authority. A mechanical map of
the line's stored relations and mentions divided its in-force entries
into seven parts; this is one of them, and the line's account is built
over it and its siblings. Several members are also in other lines, and
other lines' accounts already integrate some of them: [SYNTHESIS-036](SYNTHESIS-036.md)
([CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md), [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md), [CLAIM-410](../claims.d/CLAIM-410.md), [QUESTION-088](../questions.d/QUESTION-088.md)), [SYNTHESIS-037](SYNTHESIS-037.md)
([CLAIM-453](../claims.d/CLAIM-453.md), [QUESTION-090](../questions.d/QUESTION-090.md)), [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md) ([CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md)), [SYNTHESIS-039](SYNTHESIS-039.md)
([CLAIM-344](../claims.d/CLAIM-344.md), [CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md), [CLAIM-443](../claims.d/CLAIM-443.md), [QUESTION-313](../questions.d/QUESTION-313.md), [QUESTION-314](../questions.d/QUESTION-314.md)) and

## The account

- **Conferral and grant.** [TERM-103](../terms.d/TERM-103.md) (Active) is [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s definition
  renamed by the owner's decision: "any effect one process has on
  another's share of future influence, of which an explicit vote is one
  case; negative conferral withdraws influence". It keeps "three
  quantities" apart: "the authority a process holds, what it confers on
  others, and how far its preferred policies are realized ([CLAIM-285](../claims.d/CLAIM-285.md))".
  [TERM-101](../terms.d/TERM-101.md) (Active, A's narrowing adopted by the owner) refines it: a
  grant is conferral that "runs through a maintained relation of
  granting, delegation, eligibility or support", and "Suppressing j's
  rivals confers influence on j and grants j nothing." [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)
  (Superseded by both) keeps the history: the owner's "vote" at U2, the
  note of 2026-10-18 that "being reinforced ≠ endorsing", and the
  owner's reading of tolerances as "endorsement criteria", which
  [CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md) holds. [TERM-066](../terms.d/TERM-066.md) (Proposed) names a regime of that channel:
  "Conferral is the channel: any effect of one process on another's
  share of influence. Capture is a regime of that channel."
- **Counting grants.** [CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md) (A's analysis of the owner's design,
  granted, demonstrated, Active): under damped recursive delegation
  "reciprocal and cyclic grants recycle authority", so a ban on
  self-grants does not stop self-amplification; [CASE-077](../cases.d/CASE-077.md) (A's
  counterexample, stipulated, Active) is the reciprocal pair. [CLAIM-502](../claims.d/CLAIM-502.md)
  (the status review's construction, demonstrated, Active) objects to
  [CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md)'s old title: leave-one-out delegation keeps "an operator's
  authority exactly independent of its own grants" while grants carry
  beyond one hop. [CLAIM-375](../claims.d/CLAIM-375.md) (split from [CLAIM-285](../claims.d/CLAIM-285.md) at the owner's
  direction, demonstrated, Active) is the existence result under
  one-hop allocation: delegating to a nearer ally "raises how far its
  preferred policy is realized while its own authority stays fixed".
  [CLAIM-285](../claims.d/CLAIM-285.md) (the owner's design at U2, made precise by A, Proposed)
  holds that the incentive does work in a population and need not be
  confined to one hop; its body: "Own influence ≠ Capacity to realize
  one's preferred policies." [CLAIM-502](../claims.d/CLAIM-502.md) adds that "[CLAIM-375](../claims.d/CLAIM-375.md)'s guarantee
  does not carry over" beyond one hop. [CLAIM-272](../claims.d/CLAIM-272.md) (A's reading of the
  owner's r/place case, empirical, Active) qualifies [CLAIM-285](../claims.d/CLAIM-285.md): "the
  budget constrains moves, and conferral moves authority", grounded in
  [CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md) (the owner's case, documented, Active).
- **Grants by role, and regimes by frame.** [CLAIM-281](../claims.d/CLAIM-281.md) (A's, on the

  grounded in [CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md).
