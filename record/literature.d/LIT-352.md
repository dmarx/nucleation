summary: >-
  Murota et al. (2010), DOI-10.1007/s13160-010-0006-9. Given real
  symmetric A₁…A_m, the method finds an orthogonal P that
  block-diagonalises them all as simultaneously and finely as possible.
  That is the decomposition of the matrix *-algebra 𝒯 they generate into
  simple components (Algorithm 4.1) and then irreducible components
  (§4.2), with no group or algebra supplied in advance. The simple
  components are the connected components of the graph "Q_iᵀA_pQ_{i′} ≠ O"
  between the eigenspaces of one random combination A(r) = Σ r_pA_p. This
  is correct with probability 1 when span{I, A₁…A_m} = 𝒯 ∩ Sⁿ (Props.
  3.2–3.4, 4.2). The structure theorem it relies on, Theorem 3.1(C) "if 𝒯
  is irreducible then 𝒯 = Mₙ", is false over ℝ as stated. The companion
  paper (Maehara & Murota 2010) handles the complex and quaternion types.
