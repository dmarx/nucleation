## What the skill does not yet say

The readers met these, and answered them the same way:

- **A reading a claim cites only as a limit, a caution or a bound on its
  scope** (in "What it does not say") takes no relation: no relation runs
  from a reading to a claim it bounds. Such readings will keep showing as
  unused. [ADR-093](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-093.md) gives questions their cautions (`bears_on`), not
  claims.
- **A question that names a reading only as a "neighbour"** does not name
  it as evidence.
- **A link a claim itself calls an analogy** from another domain falls
  under the scope test and takes nothing.
- **A work with two readings**: the one whose content the entry's words use
  takes the relation.

Whether a claim's limits and cautions should have a relation of their own
is the one that changes what the view can show; it is for a decision.
