# CLAIM-tmphi3mx: Memes are characters of perspectives in the representation-theoretic sense: what a meme carries between carriers is a realization-independent invariant by which a perspective is recognized, not a particular content

## The claim

The owner's words, at U4, after A4 had related memes as nucleation sites
to the record: "memes as characteristics  (qua representation theory) of
perspectives". In representation theory the character of a
representation ρ is χ(g) = Tr ρ(g); for finite-dimensional complex
representations of a finite group it identifies ρ up to isomorphism,
whatever the coordinates ([CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md)).

**Reading (a), this entry.** A meme is the character of a perspective:
the invariant by which perspectives are recognized as of a kind across
different carriers. What a meme transmits is that kind, not a content, so
two carriers whose realizations differ entirely can carry "the same meme"
when their perspectives share a character.

**Reading (b), the alternative,** is [CLAIM-tmp4mk0t](CLAIM-tmp4mk0t.md): a meme is one value
of a character, a perspective's characteristic response to one kind of
transformation, and a family of memes samples the character.

The readings file differently. On (a) a meme is a whole classifying
invariant and two perspectives with a meme in common are of one kind; on
(b) perspectives can share some memes and differ in others, which is what
A5 §5's decomposition and A6 §2's shared meme with different characters
need.

## Where the record stands

- **The figure is the record's.** [CLAIM-137](CLAIM-137.md) (Proposed): a communicative
  kind is identified by an invariant "as a group character identifies a
  representation up to isomorphism, once one symmetry group is identified
  across the realizations by its action on the situation". [CLAIM-417](CLAIM-417.md)
  (Proposed) keeps a perspective, its carriers, its lineage and its
  representations apart, and its body lists "Its character" among the
  objects of inquiry. U4 puts memes in the place of the invariant.
- **Its limits are held.** [CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md): characters classify only for
  finite or compact groups over a field of characteristic zero, and
  "classify kinds, not tokens or positions". [CLAIM-249](CLAIM-249.md): comparing
  characters across realizations needs one group identified on both,
  fixed only up to automorphism.
- **A5's narrowing.** "Strictly speaking, however, a meme would not
  ordinarily be a mathematical character. It might instead encode,
  reveal, probe or help reproduce some part of a character-like
  structure." That is a move toward (b), filed as A's in [CLAIM-tmpjitli](CLAIM-tmpjitli.md).

## Note of 2026-11-24: the owner's ruling on the two readings of U4

The owner, 2026-11-24, on the two readings filed as alternatives: "I think
both readings are valid. I mainly meant 'meme as value', but also
persistent reproduction of a meme can nucleate related but distinct
perspectives (e.g. 'death of the author' type phenomena)". So this entry
is the other reading, which the owner also holds valid. The two are no longer alternatives: CLAIM-tmp4mk0t is the
primary reading and CLAIM-tmphi3mx stands beside it. The owner's further
point is filed as CLAIM-tmp590sw, which rests on the primary reading. No
change of status.
