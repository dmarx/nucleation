# QUESTION-441: What exactly is the counter-normativity committed by a self-maintaining organization that harms and reproduces the representational projections that make the harm non-legible, what makes it reproductive, and when is it the organization's own rather than its participants'?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U27 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and
Disposition", second share
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c): "help me
characterize the precise nature of counter-normativity being committed by a
self-maintaining system that not only harms but reproduces the
representational projections that make the harm non-legible." The owner's
"system" is a kind of organization (the owner's correction of 2026-11-07,
"I'm reasonably confident that within our framework, a 'system' is just a kind of 'organization'").

It asks three things.

1. **What the wrong is.** A's answer is a second-order wrong of
   answerability with a five-condition criterion ([CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)), and a
   disposition to turn challenges against future challenge
   ([CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md)). Neither is the owner's.
2. **What makes it reproductive.** The record holds the dynamical form:
   [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) and [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md)'s protective lock-in, where a regime shifts the
   evidence and authority that judge it. [CASE-228](../cases.d/CASE-228.md) is A's toy model of
   it, corrected by the reader.
3. **When it is the organization's own** rather than its participants' or
   an outside maintainer's. [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) keeps perspective apart from agency,
   and [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s maintenance provenance asks whose process keeps the
   blindness going. This part is open; A62 §8 only names it.

It refines [QUESTION-129](QUESTION-129.md) because every answer to part 1 that A gave needs
an "independently specified standard", which is the half of [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) the
record has not established.

## What would count as an answer

An account that says what the wrong is, shows it is not reducible to the
harm's continuation, and gives a test, such as an intervention on the
production mechanism ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)), that separates an organization
maintaining its own blindness from one whose blindness is maintained from
outside.

## Note of 2026-11-12: three stages and a passive route, from "Medical Ethics and Media Enshitification"

The owner's U18 of the exchange (2026-11-11) asked how the discussion's
cases could advance the record's normativity questions. A37 §5 separated
three stages with constructed cases: an optimization error the organization
corrects; "self-certifying blindness", in which it reads its selective data
as success; and "counter-corrective governance", in which a challenge leads
it to make later challenges less effective, which is [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md). Its newsroom
variant is filed as [CASE-tmpd5eqf](../cases.d/CASE-tmpd5eqf.md). The exchange adds a passive route that
needs no response to any challenge: a reward for few recognized failures
penalizes the inquiry that would recognize more ([CLAIM-tmpvceet](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpvceet.md)). A39 §7
recasts answerability as a demand A_C in the compatibility complex. The
question stays Open.
