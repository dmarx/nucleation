The owner asked for unfiled readings in their papers-feed snapshot
(dmarx/papers-feed, data/papers/gh-store-snapshot.json, taken
2026-10-11T21:49Z) that are likely to bear on this record's questions. After
seeing the list, they asked for all of it to be filed.

## How the list was made

1. **The snapshot.** It holds 8,032 entries. 4,467 have a title and
   abstract. Most of the rest are placeholder titles from blocked pages.
   1,904 of those were recovered through the DOI or arXiv id in their URL,
   using Crossref and arXiv. 755 ScienceDirect links carry no DOI and were
   left out.
2. **Matching against the records.** Entries already held here (131) or in
   the anthology (45) were matched by identifier and title.
3. **Ranking.** Each remaining entry (title and abstract, first 160 words)
   was embedded with a local open model, BAAI bge-small-en-v1.5, and so was
   each Open or Deferred question (title and its "The question" section).
   The question set covered main and the two open PRs. For each of 71
   questions, the 15 nearest entries were kept: 504 in all.
4. **Judging.** Four agents judged each of the 504 against the question it
   matched, from the abstract, and were told to be strict. 72 were judged
   useful to a question as posed (69 distinct works). 177 were judged
   anthology material only and 243 not useful.
5. **Drafting.** Four more agents confirmed each work's citation against
   arXiv, Crossref or bioRxiv and checked the anthology. The feed's metadata
   was wrong or incomplete for about a third of the works: missing
   co-authors, OpenReview links standing in for arXiv versions, and acceptance
   dates given as publication dates. The entries use the confirmed metadata.

## What a reading should do first

wording. The most direct match is Contextures for [QUESTION-052](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-052.md): it
claims the singular-function result for a pairing that need not be
symmetric, which is the answer the question names. It should be read first.
