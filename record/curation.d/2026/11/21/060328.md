was filed: "we should file our own local reading of the first contact
paper. we might have additional insights given our different context". The

## What the local reading added beyond the anthology's

- The average ρ = 0.43 is over all five domains, and the domain the score
  does not predict is ISQL, not shared autonomy, which is counted as
  predictive once scored at Δ = 10³.
- ISQL is itself assisted Lunar Lander, so both Lunar Lander datasets with
  assistance are where the score struggles: a test of [CLAIM-787](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-787.md).
- The failing assistant belongs to a prior study; First Contact never pairs
  its own learned interface with an assistant.
- User adaptation is an assumption, measured only within episodes; no
  transfer and no unassisted-skill measure, which bears on [CLAIM-532](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s tests
  and on [QUESTION-504](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-504.md).

  the data [CLAIM-787](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-787.md)'s ISQL test needs; registering and reading them
  is a reading the owner would start.
