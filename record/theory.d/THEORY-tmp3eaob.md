# THEORY-tmp3eaob: Tortoise reports that commissioned analysts judged more than half of about a million tweets critical of Amber Heard from 2020–21 to come from inauthentic accounts, and that coordinated pro-Depp networks operated, including bot networks in Thailand and Spain and accounts whose deleted Arabic posts praised the Saudi regime

Mostrous & Greenwood (Tortoise, 2024), [LIT-tmpbk8gi](../literature.d/LIT-tmpbk8gi.md).

## The report

"A new Tortoise investigation suggests that Heard was trolled by an army
of bots"; "In Chen's opinion, more than 50 per cent of the tweets in the
database came from inauthentic accounts"; "A coordinated network of
Twitter accounts which sent more than 100 identical messages at exactly
the same time to any brand which had worked with Amber" (written piece,
26 February 2024).

## What it does not show

- **The methods.** How inauthenticity was judged is not on the pages read.
- **Who organized it.** Nothing read attributes the activity to a party to
  the dispute; the Saudi link rests on one interviewee's opinion.
- **Any effect on a verdict.** The jury point is a question ("maybe
  jurors"), not a finding.
