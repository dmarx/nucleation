# SYNTHESIS-tmpic4aw: The organizational-affect line is three accounts joined at the potential account of valence: what valence is in an organization, a persistent potential of its perspectives' negotiation; what affect a corporation can bear and what that does to the line between remorse and prudence; and when such a regime is felt and whose it is taken to be; the account of valence rests on the functional thesis about corporate affect, the account of feeling rests on the account of valence, and the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the organizational-affect line, over the accounts of its three parts (ADR-083, ADR-084). The line was set aside on 2026-10-04 and reopened by the owner on 2026-10-23 (CLAIM-039's note of that date); the reviews of 2026-11-07 found it had no account (curation entry of 2026-11-07, 170741).*

## The parts

- **SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y, the potential account of valence and its tests** (26
  components). CLAIM-440 and TERM-076: goal-conduciveness valence is a
  persistent potential of the negotiation among an organization's
  perspectives that ranks its own states by their bearing on its
  maintenance, with the formal results that say what a record of wins can
  show (CLAIM-434, CLAIM-436), the concession that the regime tracks
  'wanting' (CLAIM-438, CLAIM-433), valence relative to a perspective
  (CLAIM-437), the claim and grant sides (CLAIM-442), and the objections
  on contest, flow, intensity, admission and cycles.
- **SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0, corporate affect and responsibility** (9 components).
  CLAIM-039: affect can be a functional property of an organized system.
  CLAIM-432 reads corporate psychopathy through it, with Wells Fargo
  (CASE-151) as its case and CLAIM-431's objection unanswered; QUESTION-044
  and QUESTION-053 ask what fixes such affect; the line's first framing
  (QUESTION-019, CASE-014) is retired, and CASE-024 is held without links.
- **SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3, feeling and access** (7 components). CLAIM-355: binding real,
  owner constructed. CLAIM-439: a regime felt, in the access sense, when
  modelled. QUESTION-059, whether functional affect must be felt;
  QUESTION-442, whether the two self-models are one; CLAIM-003, CASE-033
  and QUESTION-061 on compartmented access. Three of its components are
  shared with evidence-for-experience.

## Where they meet

The parts are joined by premises and by readings that components state.

- **SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y on SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0.**
  - CLAIM-440 rests on CLAIM-039. CLAIM-039's summary: "CLAIM-432 and
    CLAIM-440 rest on it." So the objection that holds CLAIM-039 holds
    CLAIM-440 too; CLAIM-440's note of 2026-10-30: "the premise CLAIM-039
    is held by THEORY-101's identification of valence with a felt state,
    which no claim in the record answers."
  - CLAIM-432's note of 2026-10-23 reads remorse in the potential
    account's terms: "Remorse is others' harm entering the potential
    ordering; threat without remorse is harm reaching the corporation
    only as a fall along it", and reads Wells Fargo's cross-sell ranking as
    potential in TERM-076's sense. On that reading, it says, whether a
    corporation has valence "becomes a question about its dynamics, not
    about whether it is conscious".
  - QUESTION-267 asks whether that cross-sell potential, keyed to a proxy
    for the bank's maintenance, was valence; QUESTION-268 names CASE-151 as
    a conflict record.
  - CLAIM-548 bears on QUESTION-044, and "is stated for processes".
    QUESTION-053's note of 2026-10-31 sets CLAIM-437 beside Massumi's
    differential attunement as "an analogy and not a shared mechanism".
- **SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y on SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3.**
  - CLAIM-439 rests on CLAIM-440 and supplies the condition CLAIM-440
    leaves open, when the regime is felt. CLAIM-440's revision of
    2026-10-23 records the hierarchy that ends there, which TERM-076
    states as ordinal coherence (TERM-077), its persistence (TERM-078),

    candidate only (CLAIM-439)".
  - The claims of SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y defer feeling to it: CLAIM-437 ("Which valence
    is felt. On CLAIM-439, whichever the system models"), CLAIM-442 ("That
    either side is felt. That is CLAIM-439's question."), CLAIM-527, and
    CLAIM-438, which names CLAIM-439's self-model as the candidate place

  - QUESTION-266's third layer is CLAIM-439's model, and QUESTION-442
    refines QUESTION-266.
- **SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0 on SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3.**
  - CLAIM-432's part 2 cites CLAIM-355 for the binding of appraisal,
    readiness and governance into one response being real.
  - CLAIM-355 would rest on CLAIM-039 and answers QUESTION-053 in part.
  - QUESTION-059 is where CLAIM-039's uncoupling of function from feeling

  - CLAIM-439 reads Wells Fargo's board, told of terminations "in the

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each route of ADR-075's test (step 5) was tried
against links between the parts that no part account states.

- **Remorse as a claim or as a grant.** CLAIM-442 puts an emotion on the
  claim side: "The claim is the emotion whether or not it is granted".
  CLAIM-432 counts a corporation as lacking remorse when others' harm "is
  not among the concerns its own appraisals track and gains precedence
  ... only when a regulator or a market turns it into a threat", while
  granting that "the signal can claim precedence, without being granted
  it". Read through CLAIM-442, CASE-151's signals (the 2004 investigation,
  which traced cheating to the goals and recommended cutting them; the
  2012 HR project; the regional leaders' objections) would be functional
  remorse claimed and not granted, and the bank's profile one of remorse
  without grant rather than threat without remorse. The link turns on
  whether a unit's appraisal is one of the corporation's own. CLAIM-432's
  own route through role duties says it is, and then the two claims
  disagree about the case. A contradiction between components is not an
  integration, so nothing is filed; it is recorded below.
- **Wells Fargo under the admission condition.** CLAIM-440's part 3,
  since CLAIM-448 was written in, reads a potential as the organization's
  only on a record shown to admit the contests that bear on it. CASE-151
  documents contests filtered and blocked on the way to authority.
  Applied, the cross-sell potential CLAIM-432's note reads as the bank's
  is shown only as the admitted negotiation's. This is an application of
  a stated clause to a stated case, and adds nothing a claim would hold;
  it is recorded below because CLAIM-432's note predates the clause.
- **Veit's identity and the concession to goal conduciveness.** CLAIM-435
  reads Veit's common currency as a compensatory regime of CLAIM-440's
  potential, and CLAIM-433 places that potential's valence at goal
  conduciveness, so restricting the account to goal conduciveness does
  not take it out of THEORY-101's reach. Held in substance: CLAIM-440
  says "Veit identifies felt valence with the currency (THEORY-101);
  adopting that identity for this regime is a further step the record has
  not taken", and CLAIM-432's defeater names the route. The remainder
  answers a position no entry takes.
- **CLAIM-439 as an answer to the objection that holds CLAIM-039.** Stated
  as not one: QUESTION-059 records that CLAIM-439 "declines the phenomenal
  sense this question asks about", and THEORY-101's identity concerns that
  sense.
- **CLAIM-548 against CLAIM-431.** That a process can activate without
  expecting benefit would let an installed harm-weighing procedure act on
  harm and not on prudence. CLAIM-548 states a possibility for processes,
  and CLAIM-431's demand, what keeps an installed concern answerable to
  its end, is about corporations; the discriminating test is already
  CLAIM-432's part 5. It fails as a derivation.
- **Responsibility and the constructed owner.** CLAIM-432 takes fitness
  to be held responsible from Pettit's authorised avowal, a collective
  self-description, and CLAIM-355 makes the single owner such a
  description presents a construction. Joined, the corporation would be
  responsible as an organization and not as the owner its avowal
  presents. "Responsible as" is a free parameter: CLAIM-355 allows the
  represented owner to match the distribution of control, where "Drayson
  is right and nothing is illusory", and no entry says what responsibility
  attaches to. Not filed.
- **The potential account and QUESTION-044.** If valence is read off the
  organization's record of wins, the potential account would fix
  corporate valence by the organization and not the attributor. It turns
  on the kind clause, an ordering "by their bearing on its maintenance",
  whose criterion QUESTION-267 asks for, and on which contests are
  admitted (CLAIM-448): where the measurer fixes either, QUESTION-044's
  note of 2026-10-18 already says the attributor comes back. A free
  parameter; not filed.
