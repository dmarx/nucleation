# LIT-2023: A Proof of the CSP Dichotomy Conjecture

Zhuk, D. (2020). A Proof of the CSP Dichotomy Conjecture. *Journal of the ACM* 67(5): 1–78. doi:10.1145/3402029. arXiv:1704.01914 (v1 2017). Conference version: A Proof of CSP Dichotomy Conjecture, *FOCS 2017*, 331–342, doi:10.1109/FOCS.2017.38.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1555](../notes.d/NOTE-1555.md). Page numbers are arXiv v11's.*

- **The theorem.** "Suppose Γ ⊆ RA is a finite set of relations. Then CSP(Γ)
  can be solved in polynomial time if there exists a WNU preserving Γ;
  CSP(Γ) is NP-complete otherwise." (Theorem 1.4, p. 3).
- **A simpler algorithm than Bulatov's.** It "just checks some local
  consistency and solves linear equations over prime fields" (p. 3); but
  "Bulatov’s algorithm works for infinite constraint languages, which is
  not the case for the algorithm presented in this paper" (p. 3).
- **Finite domains only.** "If we allow the domain to be infinite, the
  situation is changing significantly." (§10.4, p. 76): over the integers
  a CSP can express Hilbert's tenth problem and is undecidable.
- **Polynomial for a fixed language.** Both algorithms use "detailed
  knowledge of the algebra and depends exponentially on the size of the
  domain" (§10.1, p. 76).
- **The question changes the answer.** For quantified CSPs the conjectured
  trichotomy fails, with coNP-, DP- and Θ₂ᴾ-complete cases (§10.6).

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on resource-bounded realizability cites it, with
Bulatov's proof ([LIT-2115](LIT-2115.md)), for the dichotomy and as precedent for an
"algebraic component" of its proposed theory. It supports the dichotomy as
A states it, and only for decision problems over fixed finite languages on
finite domains. It is silent on solution-space connectivity: its "linked"
and "connects" are local-consistency notions about paths through an
instance's constraints, false friends. Its §10.6 shows how quickly the clean
classification fails once the question posed changes. Filed from it:
[THEORY-1380](../theory.d/THEORY-1380.md).
