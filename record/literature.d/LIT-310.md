  category Sys of physical systems, symmetric-monoidal under composite ⋄
  and under disjoint sum ⊔, whose arrows are translations of local
  languages. Each arrow j : S₁ → S is realised by a left-exact functor
  τ(j) between the systems' topoi, together with arrows φ(j) : Σ_{S₁} →
  τ(j)Σ_S and β(j) : τ(j)R_S → R_{S₁}. Classical physics satisfies the
  scheme exactly. In quantum theory the disjoint sum H₁⊕H₂ satisfies it

  not: the natural pull-back of δ̆(A₁) differs from δ̆(A₁⊗1̂) off the
