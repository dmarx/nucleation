# CLAIM-025: An operative regime, the governing organization among regimes, and the capacity to transform that organization can persist or fail separately

## The claim

"An organization may preserve the first two while degrading the third ... it
may become increasingly competent at operating inside the emergency regime
while becoming less capable of changing the organization that keeps the regime
in place."

## Note of 2026-10-12: evidence now linked

[THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md) (Argyris and Schön) now grounds this claim: errors are corrected within the governing values while the values themselves go unexamined. Two caveats. Argyris's "governing values" are not exactly this claim's governing organization among regimes. The evidence is from workshops.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a graded form, and three freedoms

- **A9 §9 of the exchange of 2026-10-17.** "**Freedom to endorse an
  alternative is not the same as freedom to make that endorsement
  effective; freedom to make it effective is not the same as freedom to
  sustain it; and freedom to sustain it is not the same as freedom to
  revise it.**" That is this claim's three-way split, with the attitude
  added in front, stated as degrees: "Those are distinct control
  capabilities with potentially different quantitative profiles." A9's
  Candidate C is this claim with endorsement added, and was not filed.
  A9's "endorse" is the reflective sense, not [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s.
- **A11 §4.** "Freedom to commit: The capacity to choose and establish
  constraints", "Freedom under commitment: The viable alternatives
  remaining after the commitment", "Freedom to revise: The capacity to
  reconsider or exit the commitment under appropriate conditions". "None
  can replace the others." The third is this claim's capacity to transform
  the organization. The case is [CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md).
- A7 §4 and A8 §4 gave models in which a capacity is lost apart from the
  regime it governs ([CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md), [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md)). The graded thesis built
  on this claim is [CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md).

## Note of 2026-10-21: the triad again, and organized ignorance

**The triad again.** A26 §5 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 proposed three
organizational conditions that "can diverge": persistence, flexibility and
self-governance. A26 §7 boxed "Capacity to update judgments ≠ Capacity to
revise how judgments acquire authority". A27 §3 listed behavioural,
governance and epistemic-governance adaptation. All three are this
claim's three things that persist or fail separately. §7's box is the
single-loop against double-loop learning of [THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md), which grounds this
claim. Neither reply cited this claim or [THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md). A26 cited [CLAIM-099](CLAIM-099.md)
for it as "retaining the capacity to change governing regimes", which is
this claim's third tier, not [CLAIM-099](CLAIM-099.md)'s "capacity to govern among
regimes". The new nuance is A27's epistemic form of the third tier:
changing "how evidence and evaluations influence those governance
decisions".

**Organized ignorance.** A24 §4, from Pohlhaus ([LIT-1311](../literature.d/LIT-1311.md)) and Dotson
([LIT-1329](../literature.d/LIT-1329.md)), both registered and, when sought on 2026-10-21, Unavailable: "An institution can be very good
at producing and evaluating knowledge *within its accepted categories*
while remaining poor at recognizing the inadequacy of those categories
... **the mechanisms determining what it learns are themselves insulated
from certain kinds of correction**." A25 §8 asked for "the ability to
recognize when the **evidentiary and evaluative regime itself** requires
revision". Both are Argyris's single-loop learning and self-sealing tests,
held in [THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md), and neither reply cited them. The difference is the
source of the insulation: on A24's account it is social position, not
defensive routine.

## Note of 2026-10-27: promoted

The status review of this date (ADR-057) promoted this claim. It is an
existence claim ("can persist or fail separately"), and two Active
constructions show the separation in a model. CASE-111's fold loses
maintenance while the maintained state persists, and CASE-112's tilted
bistability changes the operative regime apart from what governs it. Both
were cited in the note of 2026-10-18 and now ground the claim. CASE-036
is the organizational form. THEORY-145 no longer grounds it. Argyris's
single-loop against double-loop learning is the same separation observed
in organizations, and the directive above already said nothing here rests
on it. Its empirical thesis stays Proposed under its own promote_when. No
objection is filed, and its defeater asks for a case where degraded
transformation always shows up as an operative or governing failure,
which the constructions rule out within their models. ARG-007, Active,
already rested on this claim.
