# THEORY-2253: Schultz, Spivak and Vasilakopoulou model open dynamical systems as spans of sheaves on time intervals, so that any wiring of such machines, feedback included, is again one, with totality, determinism and inertia each preserved by every wiring, while behaviour contracts that depend on history form presheaves that are not sheaves, since two valid behaviours can glue to an invalid one

## Source

Schultz, Spivak and Vasilakopoulou (2019; arXiv 2016), [LIT-2258](../literature.d/LIT-2258.md), skimmed
in [NOTE-2344](../notes.d/NOTE-2344.md).

## What was actually shown

Definitions and propositions in category theory: machines as spans in
interval sheaves form wiring-diagram algebras, and the subcategories of
total, deterministic and inertial machines are subalgebras. The contract
point is a remark with an example.

## What this does not say

When a given family of observed behaviours is the behaviour of some
machine, or at what cost one can be built. Anything without a common clock.
