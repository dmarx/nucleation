# LIT-tmpht8ga: Pattern formation outside of equilibrium

M. C. Cross, P. C. Hohenberg (1993), *Reviews of Modern Physics 65(3):851–1112* — DOI-10.1103/RevModPhys.65.851

## Key takeaways

- Spatial and temporal patterns in driven systems (convection, Taylor–Couette flow, chemical reactions, morphogenesis models, solidification, optics) can be classified by the linear instability of a uniform state, by its characteristic wave-number q₀ and frequency ω₀, and described near onset by universal amplitude equations.
- There is no free energy or other Lyapunov functional selecting non-equilibrium patterns in general. Variational descriptions are perturbative and valid only near threshold. The authors read this as a reason the field has had to proceed system by system.
- A closed chemical system, like a closed fluid, must come to equilibrium. Its non-equilibrium patterns are either transients or are sustained by external pumping.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, as the Rayleigh–Bénard entry among
the canonical systems of the dissipative-structure programme. The request
offered Bénard (1900) or a standard review and preferred this one if
readable. It was readable as an authors' scan in the Caltech repository and
was chosen: it covers convection, Turing patterns ([LIT-tmptpgzu](LIT-tmptpgzu.md)) and the BZ
reaction ([LIT-tmpoej2u](LIT-tmpoej2u.md)) in one place, and corrects the usual story of
Bénard's experiment. It is 262 pages, and only the sections the record needs
were read, so the NOTE is `Skimmed`.

Its bearing on the record:

- **It is the main read check on the programme's ambitions.** Cross and
  Hohenberg use "dissipative structures" (citing Nicolis and Prigogine,
  [LIT-tmpd737a](LIT-tmpd737a.md)) only as one name, alongside "synergetics" and
  "self-organization", for steady macroscopic structure under constant
  non-equilibrium conditions. Their conclusion (§XIII.B) quotes Glansdorff
  and Prigogine ([LIT-tmpxgib0](LIT-tmpxgib0.md), p. 108) that "the search for a universal
  kinetic potential has proved to be unsuccessful". It agrees with the
  Nobel lecture's ([LIT-tmp452jw](LIT-tmp452jw.md)) own restriction of minimum entropy
  production to the strictly linear regime, and goes further: there is no
  global minimization principle for these structures at all beyond a
  perturbative one near threshold.
- **The demarcation question in [LIT-192](LIT-192.md), and [LIT-211](LIT-211.md)'s dissipative cluster.**
  The authors end by doubting that structures "appearing from nowhere in a
  dissipative system" are yet a modelling first step toward "the emergence of
  life from the primordial soup" (p. 1080). The absence of a global principle
  is their reason. They allow, as "plausible … (although by no means
  demonstrated)", that reaction–diffusion mechanisms may carry molecular
  information up to the cellular level. That is a physicist's version of
  the point [NOTE-094](../notes.d/NOTE-094.md) records: pattern-forming physics is shared by organisms
  and non-organisms, and by itself says nothing about what makes the former
  special.
