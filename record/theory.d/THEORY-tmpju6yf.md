# THEORY-tmpju6yf: Aspiration is rational self-transformation by proleptic reasons

Callard (2018), Oxford University Press, [LIT-tmph5kpk](../literature.d/LIT-tmph5kpk.md).

## The account, as expected

Callard proposes that aspiration, the activity by which a person comes to care about a value they do not yet hold, is rational though the reason for it is available only to the person they will become: aspirants act on proleptic reasons, acknowledged defective versions of the reasons they expect to grasp; the transformation involves intrinsic conflict between the old and the new point of view on value, resolved not by deliberation or choice but by work to see the world anew; and because a person can so create themselves, they are responsible for having become who they are.
