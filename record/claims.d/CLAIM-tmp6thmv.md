# CLAIM-tmp6thmv: Diversity indicates an ecosystem's richness because differentiated constituents that persist preserve partially independent routes through its possibility space, including questions not yet asked and responses not yet demanded, so diversity of consequential capabilities, not the number of constituents, tracks richness, and it is a generally informative proxy rather than a quantity that always rises with richness

## The claim

A60's conclusion: "Diversity is an indicator of ecosystem richness because
the persistence of differentiated organizations preserves a plurality of
partially independent routes through possibility space. Some routes
correspond to capabilities currently exercised. Others correspond to
questions not yet asked, responses not yet demanded, and developmental
possibilities not yet realized."

The argument, by section of A60:

1. **Distributed access** (§1). Each perspective *i* has a set of
   question–answer pairs it can jointly realize under its resources and
   horizon. The ecosystem's repertoire is at least their union where they
   can operate independently, and may include responses that only
   cooperation produces. So "The ecosystem's capabilities are not exhausted
   by those of its dominant or most active constituent", and removing a
   rare constituent can remove a region of the space while its loss is
   "nearly invisible during ordinary operation".
2. **Consequential capabilities, not counts** (§2). Three groups with
   nearly identical capabilities add redundancy; three in different niches
   add kinds of response. "Redundancy can improve reliability, especially
   against independent failures. Differentiation can improve the breadth
   of response." Niche differentiation counts as CLAIM-554 counts it: an
   enlargement of what the constituents can jointly realize, not mere
   coexistence of more types.
3. **Extensive and generative value** (§3). Perspectives equal in present
   repertoire can differ in what they can come to answer (CLAIM-754's
   developmental frontier; CLAIM-675).
4. **A hedge against unknown questions** (§4). Under a coverage model,
   added perspectives raise coverage where existing ones answer poorly and
   duplicates add little; and since future demand is not fully known,
   diversity "may preserve capacities for recognizing future demands that
   nobody can currently specify". A60 calls this "a plausible mechanism,
   not a universal guarantee".
5. **Maintained, not transient** (§5). Opinions voiced without enduring
   support are "diverse as utterances" but "The system displays diversity
   without maintaining much corresponding capacity". Where preserving a
   niche costs less than rebuilding it, which CLAIM-463 shows can happen
   ("restoring it can cost more than preserving it would have"), keeping
   seemingly underused diversity is justified. A60: "This inequality is
   conditional, not universal".
6. **Legibility** (§6). Diversity is also of legibility regimes, so it
   widens the questions an ecosystem can ask, not only its answers.

Hence diversity is "a generally informative proxy for the breadth of an
ecosystem's maintained possibility repertoire, especially where constituent
differences have been shaped by genuine niche differentiation and
independently maintained histories."

## What it does not say

- Not that more diversity always means more richness: "A hundred nearly
  interchangeable participants may contribute less functional
  differentiation than a few complementary ones, and additional
  participants can consume resources needed to maintain valuable existing
  capabilities."
- Not that every perspective is equally accurate or should govern; and not
  a measure of welfare: diversity "should be treated as evidence of
  potential richness, not as an unconditional measure of welfare or
  justice" (CLAIM-546).

## Limits found in filing

The assistant's, in filing (2026-11-23):

- **The coverage model supports the claim only under its assumptions.**
  CLAIM-tmp7iie0 checks §4's model. A perspective's marginal value is its
  excess over the best existing readiness, so duplicates add nothing, as
  A60 says. But with parallel independent attempts three similar
  generalists out-cover three specialists, a shared budget makes a
  duplicate lower coverage, and a selector that cannot tell questions
  apart erases the specialists' advantage. ARG-tmpbchnj sets out what
  that leaves of step 4.
- **The hedge argument leaves the model.** Since coverage is linear in the
  demand measure, uncertainty about demand over a known set of questions
  is the mean demand and nothing more (CLAIM-tmp7iie0). Step 4's hedge
  rests on worst-case evaluation or on questions outside the set, which
  the model cannot score; A60's sentence about demands "nobody can
  currently specify" is the second, and is interpretive.
- **Maintained pursuit is not maintained capacity.** THEORY-148 (Zollman)
  finds diversity of pursuit valuable when transient and harmful when
  made permanent, since the group then never settles. Step 5 holds of
  maintained capacity, not of maintained pursuit (ARG-tmpdnptq). A60's
  "transient variety" is unsupported utterance, a different thing from
  Zollman's "transient diversity", which is a virtue.

## Note of 2026-11-23: beside A54's parallel routes and adaptive governance

Two claims filed in the same batch from A54 say parts of this one at another
scale. CLAIM-tmp104zk, on parallel perspectives answering one question:
"distinct, causally consequential routes to answers", valuable "not ...
because it increases their count", is this claim's "consequential
capabilities, not the number of constituents" for one question under a
deadline. It also fixes what "partially independent routes" must mean for
the claim to hold: routes whose failures are not correlated. The two senses
of "distinct" differ. Here a route counts for differing in capability; in
CLAIM-tmp104zk a route counts for failing independently, and two copies of
one capability that fail independently add to the chance of success. That is
the redundancy this claim allows has value of its own, and it is why, in
CLAIM-tmp7iie0, parallel independent attempts can favour similar
generalists over specialists. CLAIM-tmpumpxw draws the governance
conclusion: what is worth maintaining is "different capacities for
discovering which questions are worth asking", the representational
dimension of TERM-tmp9lu6c, and its "integrated enough for coordinated
action" is the term's relational clause. Neither claim rests on this one
or this one on them. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this
session); adopting it is the owner's call.
