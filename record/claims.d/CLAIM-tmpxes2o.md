# CLAIM-tmpxes2o: If a willed agent is the jurisdiction of its will-organization (CLAIM-170), then governing one's own governability is in part governing one's own individuation, in TERM-088's constitutive sense at that level: a revision of the will-organization that changes which regimes it governs changes which willed agent there is, so a self-forming revision can be a re-individuation and not only a change in an agent's states, and whether the revised jurisdiction continues the earlier agent is a question of continuation, not of resemblance

## The claim

CLAIM-170, the owner's: willed agents are individuated by their
will-organization; a willed agent is the jurisdiction of a
will-organization. CLAIM-tmpxpdqi: freedom is an agent's governance of its
own governability, including revising the relations that govern it.

Put together:

- **Revision can move the jurisdiction.** When the agent revises its
  will-organization so that it governs regimes it did not, or no longer
  governs some it did, the jurisdiction changes. By CLAIM-170 so does which
  willed agent there is.
- **So reflexive governance reaches individuation.** A self-forming revision
  (CLAIM-tmpbeh2e) that adds a channel or a distinction under the
  organization's governance is, at this level, a re-individuation, not only a
  change of state in a fixed agent.
- **Continuation, not resemblance.** Whether the revised jurisdiction is the
  same agent is then QUESTION-118's question, asked of jurisdictions: a matter
  of how the later organization came from the earlier (CLAIM-329,
  QUESTION-364), not of how much they resemble each other.

This is the positive side of CLAIM-284, a self as "a historically continuous
organization of processes that takes part in governing the conditions of its
own persistence, differentiation and transformation". It says what the
taking part does: it can change which willed agent there is.

## What it does not say

- **That every revision re-individuates.** One that leaves the jurisdiction
  unchanged does not.
- **Anything about the other senses of individuation.** A pattern can be
  dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside (TERM-088;
  CLAIM-167). A31 conflated these, as the owner corrected on 2026-10-27.
- **That the re-individuated agent is the agent's own.** Whose revision it was
  is QUESTION-364's, and manipulation is QUESTION-071's.
