  learning, arXiv physics/0007070. Predictive information, the mutual
  information between the past and the whole future of a series, is the
  subextensive part of its entropy, and grows in one of three ways:
  bounded, logarithmically, or as a power. A model with K parameters drawn
  once from a prior gives (K/2) log N; an essential singularity in the
  density of nearby models gives N^(μ/(μ+1)) in the annealed
  approximation, √N for a smooth density under a field-theory prior. The
  divergent part is argued to be the unique measure of complexity.
