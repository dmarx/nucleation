## The brief's questions, answered

- (a) The checked type comes from the programmer's ascription, passed down
  through the context; intent enters only as an ascription. A failing
  check is world to word; a met one the paper leaves open (ARG-tmp7pf1t).
- (b) No rule moves a term; the elaboration clause is not borne out here
  (ARG-tmpdq64g, CLAIM-tmpvq4ru). CLAIM-tmp764wb is extended to a
  bidirectional system; CLAIM-tmpnookt's correspondence is restored for
  the checking side only.
- (c) The value restriction bears on TERM-tmp7s2aa: effects, beside
  divergence, are a reason a witness must be a value.
- (d) Bidirectional because inference is "most likely undecidable" and
  principal types do not exist (p. 2); what must be stated in advance is a
  function's several properties and invariants strong enough for recursion.

## Gaps in the work's own argument

In the NOTE's Limitations: completeness of checking is not characterized;
undecidability of inference is conjectured; the cost of the value
restriction is argued from examples; the checker is nondeterministic and
its cost not analysed. None is used by the record's argument, so none is
filled.

## Offered, not filed

Two cross-reading ideas are offered to the owner in the worker's report,
and not filed: that whether a specification can be read off or must be
stated depends on whether its vocabulary is closed (Freeman and Pfenning)
or open (Davies and Pfenning); and that a predicate about an entry's
future changes, like a ref type, is a requirement even when the entry
meets it now, so descriptors of the present can conflict as requirements
on the future.
