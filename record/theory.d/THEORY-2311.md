# THEORY-2311: Emotional expressions do things as speech acts do

Scarantino, the theory of affective pragmatics (2017).

## The account, as expected

Scarantino proposes that emotional expressions, like utterances, do more than express: beside expressing an emotion, an expression can direct others to act, represent how things are, and commit the expresser to a future course, so that expressions are means of social influence whose functions a pragmatics of emotion should classify, as speech-act theory classifies utterances.
