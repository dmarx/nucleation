# NOTE-tmprzmfk: Lee and colleagues 2025, skimmed for how a collective of starfish embryos passes between ordered and flocking states as its interactions grow less nonreciprocal, and how the rate of the passage is measured

## Contribution

An experimental system with tunable nonreciprocity, a state diagram, and
two analysis tools for structure and for transitions between structures.
The reading is THEORY-tmpp4w4k.

## Key results

1. **Order and asymmetry** (abstract): the state diagram spans
   "crystalline, flock-like, and fragmented states, revealing that weak
   nonreciprocity promotes structural order while stronger asymmetry
   disrupts it."
2. **The passage is not gradual** (§IV): "the observed transition from an
   M2 -dominated (disordered) state toward an M1 -dominated (ordered) state
   is not gradual"; around three hours the trajectory turns sharply.
3. **A rate shift** (§IV): the flowscape's "progress bar" "shows a clear
   decrease in the rate of information transfer that coincides with the
   independently measured shift in velocity polarization", and a
   lower-bound estimate of entropy production falls at the same time.
4. **Emergent proofreading** (§IV): dissipation disproportionately biases
   motif transitions toward the ordered motif, interpreted "as an emergent
   proofreading, in which higher dissipation selectively favors
   information flow and transitions towards target states."
5. **Their moral** (§V): "self-organization depends not only on the
   states it occupies, but also on the directed flows between these
   states."

## Assumptions

Nonreciprocity is set by the embryos' developmental ages and inferred
from pair trajectories; the entropy production rate is a lower bound from
motif transition statistics; the model's intermediate states are more
crystalline than observed (§IV), which the authors flag.

## Concepts

Nonreciprocal interaction; topological landscape (motif distribution);
topological flowscape (KL-divergence coordinates from reference states);
entropy production rate; proofreading.

## Bearing on the record

- **QUESTION-281** (bears on). A measured passage between collective
  states, made by a slowly drifting control parameter (development lowers
  nonreciprocity), that is abrupt rather than gradual and changes rate
  with dissipation. It names no bifurcation; the kind of transition is
  left open.
- **CLAIM-478** (note only). The drifting control parameter is slow
  against the collective's rearrangements, which is the setting of the
  claim's first half, but the paper does not use the separation.

## Limitations

Skimmed; the inference of interactions and the model were not checked.
The proofreading interpretation rests on a correlation between a
dissipation bound and a normalized flux, in one experiment.
