et al.: the arXiv preprint, v5 of 13 February 2013, which carries the

**Lookups and corrections.**

- **Three open copies were not fetched.** Unpaywall lists them, and each
  answered with an HTTP 403 Cloudflare challenge. A headless browser was not
  used to get past the challenges. This morning the Springer HTML of Faye and
  Jaksland was rendered in a headless browser after its PDF answered with a
  challenge; that page was the publisher's open-access full text, and whether the
  browser passed a challenge to render it was not checked. This time no
  browser fetch was attempted.
- **Dates under [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md).** Ashwin et al. (arXiv 1103.0169, v1 2011-03-01) and
  Toner and Tu (adap-org/9506001, v1 1995-06-08) now take their arXiv dates.
  Toner and Tu's preprint lists the authors as Tu and Toner, with the two
  halves of the title reversed.
- **Summaries rewritten from the texts**, with these corrections. Deutsch and
  Marletto define information variables by possible tasks and
  distinguishability after them, the reverse of the registration. Toner and
  Tu's stated mechanism is the convective term (v·∇)v, not coupling to
  density. Flack's paper is a review with no new data, and her criteria do
  not include "accessible".
- **Three statuses rest on looking without finding.** Simondon, Rheinberger and
  Rosen have no DOI to look up on Unpaywall, and OpenAlex's title search was
  out of its daily budget for this client, so "no open copy" for those three
  means none is known, not that none exists.

**What the readings found against the record.**

- **[CLAIM-327](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)'s third stage is not Flack's** ([THEORY-tmp7ps0w](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp7ps0w.md)). Regeneration
  of the variable by use is not among her criteria. What reinforces itself is
  agreement among members' estimates, and a new level needs her strong
  criteria, not use alone. Dated note on the claim, and on [CASE-116](../../record/cases.d/CASE-116.md), whose
  arm B runs against her stipulation of slowness rather than any finding.
- **[CLAIM-tmpmx2ql](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmx2ql.md) is supported and widened.** Flack states its first half for
  social systems, with a limit (a poor summary fails later the larger the
  separation). Ashwin et al. state its window and add a route by which
  separation is lost without any mode slowing. Toner and Tu add broken
  symmetry as a source of slow modes.
- **[QUESTION-tmpl1yih](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpl1yih.md) has a worked case.** In Toner and Tu, the bifurcation
  creates the local speed and a separate mechanism stabilizes the order in
  two dimensions. Ashwin et al. correct the question's gloss of rate-induced
  tipping ("destroyed" is too strong; rate can also preserve a form).
- **[THEORY-tmpggk6i](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpggk6i.md)'s source is corrected.** The 1996 RQM assumes systems prior
  to interactions, but the "sparse ontology of events" is Rovelli 2018.
- **[CLAIM-tmpmand1](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmand1.md)'s defeater (b) is pressed** by constructor theory's modal
  individuation, without being settled.
- Dated notes also on [CLAIM-157](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) and [THEORY-136](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-136.md).
