## The account

On the record's entries, the exchange's argument runs as follows. Each
link is attributed to the entry that states it.

1. **A perspective is addressed by its response profile.** It is
   addressed under a declared probe family ([TERM-tmpwc4la](../terms.d/TERM-tmpwc4la.md)), so what can be
   told apart is relative to the probes ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md), [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md)).
2. **Enriching the probes refines; it never merges.** Along a nested
   family, distinctions only refine ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)). A distinction that
   vanishes was hidden by a change of regime, or ended by a change in
   the organization ([CLAIM-tmp0dyx8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0dyx8.md)). The partitions an observer can
   afford are a small part of all partitions ([CLAIM-tmp7qzux](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7qzux.md)).
3. **Where unity lies.** A self is proposed to be an organization whose
   decomposition into perspectives is regime-relative ([CLAIM-tmp7pe33](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7pe33.md)).
   Its unity, if any, lies in causal coordination and maintenance
   ([CLAIM-tmptxsin](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptxsin.md)).
4. **Unity is not singularity.** The unity need not be singularity
   ([CLAIM-tmp3xo35](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3xo35.md), in [TERM-tmpm0s7g](../terms.d/TERM-tmpm0s7g.md)'s senses). It can be ecological
   integration ([CLAIM-tmp37le7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp37le7.md), [TERM-tmppvlfi](../terms.d/TERM-tmppvlfi.md)), and harmony and
   singularity are independent ([CLAIM-tmp31b3v](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp31b3v.md)).
5. **A measure-valued self.** On the owner's measure-theoretic proposal
   ([CLAIM-tmphxbli](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmphxbli.md)), the self can be modelled as a measure-valued
   organization ([CLAIM-tmp9pgdu](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9pgdu.md)). Its perspectives are overlapping
   submeasures ([CLAIM-tmprq9q5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmprq9q5.md)), and their separability is set by the
   test family's pseudometric ([CLAIM-tmpwb6dg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwb6dg.md)). A29's three levels
   restate this ([CLAIM-tmp6ksyy](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6ksyy.md)).
6. **A team has separable carriers, not necessarily separable
   perspectives.** That is A21's objection to the owner's U10
   ([CLAIM-tmp7580l](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7580l.md)). It makes a team a place where interventions on
   members are possible while its perspectives still overlap
   ([CLAIM-tmpl1hgv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl1hgv.md)).
7. **What a governed record can do.** It can synchronize collaborators
   without making them converge ([CLAIM-tmp86z76](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp86z76.md)). It may carry part of a
   team's organization ([CLAIM-tmpqr0tn](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqr0tn.md), supposed). It can let arbitration
   fall away by externalized governance, which is a different mechanism
   from learned coordination ([CLAIM-tmpmk036](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmk036.md)).
8. **Regulation.** On the owner's regulation thesis ([CLAIM-tmpz67ew](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpz67ew.md)),
   regulations are conduct constraints meant to induce outcome
   constraints ([CLAIM-tmplgnjf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmplgnjf.md)). At fixed dynamics they cannot relieve
   frustration among the public demands ([CLAIM-tmpk4bou](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4bou.md)). Possibility and
   realization are separate ([CLAIM-tmpx9nrj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx9nrj.md)), and no optimum exists
   before the commitments are declared ([CLAIM-tmphjm0q](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmphjm0q.md)).
9. **Regulation as governance of possibility.** It governs which
   possibilities are attainable, sustainable and revisable
   ([CLAIM-tmptfpf7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfpf7.md)). What it should maintain is reorganization readiness
   ([TERM-tmpz28l1](../terms.d/TERM-tmpz28l1.md)), whose loss can come from ordinary efficient operation
   ([CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md)). The owner's tension between stabilizing identity and
   adapting ([CLAIM-tmpntzjo](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpntzjo.md)) is, on A's account, not one scale: a
   constraint's adequacy is indexed to the demands' timescales
   ([CLAIM-tmph25dl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmph25dl.md), [CLAIM-tmp2k3c5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2k3c5.md)). Slowness fixes persistence, not
   redirectability ([CLAIM-tmpx8csq](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx8csq.md)), so identity in slow modes needs those
   modes kept controllable ([CLAIM-tmpbzwmr](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbzwmr.md)).
10. **The response spectrum.** Identity stabilization filters: it
   passes slow variation and absorbs fast ([CLAIM-tmppqdnv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmppqdnv.md)). What counts as
   noise is itself governed ([CLAIM-tmp76xaw](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp76xaw.md)). Governance can retune
   within bounds ([CLAIM-tmp947v0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp947v0.md), [CLAIM-tmp2k3c5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2k3c5.md)), and deadlines shrink
   what is jointly attainable ([CLAIM-tmpdvgrf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdvgrf.md)). On A's last version,
   stabilization and adaptability are two achievements of one organization
   ([CLAIM-tmpm985y](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm985y.md)), whose response spectrum, and the capacity to retune
   it, are objects of governance ([CLAIM-tmpcag0t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcag0t.md)).

## Not stated on the record

Each of these links A asserted. No component states it.

- **"Amortized sensorimotor coordination" as stored work (A8).** The
  analogy fails, as [ARG-tmpzxz7y](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpzxz7y.md) shows: no expansion of what is feasible.
- **"This is exactly the distinction we were reaching for when
  describing a team as a pseudo-self" (A29 §6).** The owner's U10
  asserted the opposite, that the perspectives are necessarily separable.
  The distinction was A21's objection to it. Telling it as "we" blurs
  who held what. This is an attribution finding, not a link.
- **"A polity as an ecological pseudo-self" (A31).** No component
  states that an industrial ecosystem satisfies [TERM-tmpxwa7g](../terms.d/TERM-tmpxwa7g.md)'s
  integration condition. Left as an analogy.
- **"An ecology of regulatory perspectives" (A33 §7).** Its components
  are [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) and [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md). Beyond them it is a label.
- **That the regulation thread and the self thesis are one framework
  (A29's closing, A31's synthesis).** They share vocabulary: compatibility
  complexes, harmony, pseudo-selves. No entry derives one from the other.
- **"Stable governance against lock-in", cited to [CLAIM-711](../claims.d/CLAIM-711.md) (A33 §6).**
  [CLAIM-711](../claims.d/CLAIM-711.md) concerns interruptibility, not lock-in or the means to revise
  constraints.

## What it does not say

It does not say that any version of the self thesis is true, that a team
or an industrial ecosystem is a self, or that Luria makes its
collaborators converge or not. Each of these is an open claim, question
or lead above.
