# LIT-941: A Theory of Unsupervised Translation Motivated by Understanding Animal Communication

Shafi Goldwasser, David F. Gruber, Adam Tauman Kalai and Orr Paradise (2022), *arXiv:2211.11081* (NeurIPS 2023).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpr0hmw](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpr0hmw.md).*

- **A prior instead of parallel data.** The learner sees unlabelled source
  texts and a prior ρ over target texts, and picks the translator that
  maximises Σ log ρ(f_θ(x_i)). A wrong translator is caught when some of
  its translations are impossible under the prior.
- **An Occam bound, unsupervised.** If every bad translator makes more than
  a γ fraction of translations impossible, (1/γ) ln(|Θ|/δ) samples suffice
  (Theorem A.2).
- **Common nonsense.** When prior and translations share a random
  α-fraction of impossible texts, err = O(log|Θ| / (α·min(m,|T|))), and no
  algorithm does better than a constant factor (Theorems 3.4, 3.5).
- **Knowledge graphs.** With agreement α and per-node knowledge pr,
  err = O(log n/(α²pr) + (1/α)√(r log n/m)): richer languages need less
  common ground (Theorem 3.2).
- **About as much data as supervised translation**, up to the factor 1/α,
  barring computation.
- **The limit.** Uniform, random, lossless models; one-to-one translators
  into a target that can say everything; no efficient algorithm.

## Standing in the record

Filed 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-020](../questions.d/QUESTION-020.md) and read 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-tmpr0hmw](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpr0hmw.md). Its
results are held as [THEORY-tmpkpfpe](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpkpfpe.md). It was registered as "a formal case of
the interpreter substituting for transmitted information". That is too
strong: the receiver's prior substitutes for parallel data in learning the
translator, at a rate of exchange 1/α against source data, and does not
shorten any message. It is the record's nearest formal result to
[QUESTION-020](../questions.d/QUESTION-020.md), not an answer to it. Its lossless assumption is the case
[THEORY-170](../theory.d/THEORY-170.md) says is not the general one. The `anthology-candidate` flag, for
its learning-theory content, is left as filed.
