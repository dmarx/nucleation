# LIT-2378: No Right to an Open Future

Joseph Millum (2025). "No Right to an Open Future." *Journal of Applied Philosophy 42(3): 871-886*. DOI-10.1111/japp.12790.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1734](../notes.d/NOTE-1734.md)). A reduction: everything the open-future
right is invoked for is either an existing right of the child (bodily
integrity) or an interest to be weighed; the right adds no ground and no
priority.
