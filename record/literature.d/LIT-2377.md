# LIT-2377: The evolution of online news headlines

Pietro Nickl, Mehdi Moussaïd and Philipp Lorenz-Spreen (2025). "The evolution of online news headlines." *Humanities and Social Sciences Communications 12(1)*. DOI-10.1057/s41599-025-04514-7.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1780](../notes.d/NOTE-1780.md)). A large, long-run, multi-outlet
measurement that the style of news headlines has shifted toward features
associated with clickbait and click success, and that the shift is not
confined to low-quality or partisan outlets.
