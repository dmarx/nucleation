## What the readings changed

- **[CLAIM-841](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-841.md)**: the KPZ row holds untuned for d ≤ 2 only, and in experiment untuned is not generic; the flocking row cites a diverging structure factor, not ΔN ∝ ⟨N⟩^φ; the XY row rests on Kosterlitz and Thouless, not on Mermin and Wagner.
- **[CLAIM-842](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-842.md)**: the graph version it supposes is a theorem for spin models in equilibrium (Cassi), with conditions (a symmetry generated by local generators, finite second moments) and exceptions; Toner–Tu bounds it out of equilibrium.
- **[QUESTION-508](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-508.md)**: four partial answers, and one shape across them ([CLAIM-862](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-862.md)).
- **[CLAIM-809](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-809.md)**: three cases with the optimum in three places (toward, below, and on the transition).
- **[CLAIM-799](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-799.md) and [CLAIM-800](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-800.md)**: a third proved case of signal before outlier (Wang et al.), and coarse-label collapse with fine classes recoverable (Yang et al.).
- **The owner's [CLAIM-854](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-854.md)** stays as worded; Wang et al. find the top outliers to be the signal in their settings, against its stop-word half, and Benigni and Péché, now registered, may test that half directly.
- **[CLAIM-828](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-828.md) and [CLAIM-807](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-807.md)** each lose a ground (Rejected ARGs); **[CLAIM-839](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-839.md)** gains four qualifications and a demonstrated consequence.
- **LIT corrections**: Shalizi and Moore (macrostates both subjective and objective), Wang et al. (spikes never grow with depth in theory), Ghadimi Atigh et al. (authors, venue, date), Lee et al. (authors, venue).
