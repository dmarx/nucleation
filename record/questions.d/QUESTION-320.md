# QUESTION-320: Do the precursors of abrupt overthrow found for crowd-maintained images on r/place (plural, partly the defenders' restoring, accepting and coordinating, and absent for many overthrows) hold in a second collectively maintained system with its own record of attacks and repairs?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-214](../theory.d/THEORY-214.md) shows, on the 2022 canvas and again on 2023, that the abrupt
overthrow of a crowd-built image is predicted by many image-level series
together far better than by any generic slowing-down indicator, that low
past restoring of attacked pixels is among them, and that about a quarter
of overthrows give no warning at the 20-minute range. Whether that is a
fact about collectively maintained patterns or about one game is open: both
test sets are r/place. And the reading of the precursors as the defending
community's conduct (accepted change, coordination, recruitment) is the
authors' conjecture from SHAP curves, since no plan, template or forum was
examined. Whether lasting images persist mainly by repair or by neglect is
[QUESTION-169](QUESTION-169.md)'s question, not this one.

## What would answer it

The condition [THEORY-214](../theory.d/THEORY-214.md) carried until 2026-10-29, unchanged. The account
is promoted by a study of collectively maintained patterns in a second
system, not r/place, with its own record of attacks and repairs, in which
(a) a predictor using several pattern-specific time series again beats
every generic indicator on held-out patterns, (b) the precursors it uses
include the defenders' own conduct (repair, recruitment, accepted change),
and (c) a sizeable share of overthrows remain unpredictable. It is weakened
if a single generic indicator, properly defined for images, matches the
combined predictor, or if the r/place precursors turn out to be artefacts
of the Atlas or of the stable-period filter. What cannot settle it: higher
accuracy on r/place alone, which says nothing about collective
organization; or SHAP readings of the same model, which are the conjecture
being tested.
