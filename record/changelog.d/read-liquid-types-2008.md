### Added

- LIT-tmplw76n, NOTE-tmpnd1kd and THEORY-tmp01mu6: Rondon, Kawaguchi and Jhala 2008, "Liquid Types", registered and read for LEAD-tmp3xevc. Liquid types are dependent types {ν : B | φ} whose predicates, where a type is inferred, are conjunctions of qualifiers from a finite set. Inference weakens to the strongest conjunction a conservative decidable check verifies, and a program with no such type is reported, not changed.
- ARG-tmprmcn5 (Proposed): the reading, by analogy, bears out CLAIM-tmpnookt's descriptor as a refinement inhabited, in predicate form, narrowed to a finite conjunctive vocabulary for inferable descriptors, conservative checking, and no carried evidence.
- ARG-tmp246r1 (Rejected): the reading shows inference as informative update but not checking as requirement.
- CLAIM-tmpcmt21 (Proposed, counter, interpretive): in liquid types checking moves no program, and a failure to check may lie in the program, the qualifier set or the checker.
