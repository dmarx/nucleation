# TERM-094: action readiness, with control precedence, in Frijda's sense: a state of preparation for action, not a representation of a desirable state, that claims control over behaviour by persisting until an end state, resuming after interruption, capturing attention and taking priority over other actions

## Definition

**Action readiness** is a state of preparation for action: "not
representations of desirable states, but actual preparations for action"
([LIT-540](../literature.d/LIT-540.md), C1 in [NOTE-433](../notes.d/NOTE-433.md)). A state of action readiness has **control
precedence** when it claims control over behaviour by four marks:

- **persistence** until an end state;
- **resumption** after interruption;
- **selective attention**: it captures attention;
- **priority** over other actions.

Control precedence is a claim the state makes. Whether the claim is granted
is a further question, which is [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s distinction between claimed
precedence and granted authority.

## The distinctions it draws

- **From [THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md).** That account says what elicits a state of readiness
  (an appraisal of pertinence to a concern) and that emotions differ by
  their mode of readiness. Both are contested by [THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md). This term says
  only what a state of readiness is and how it claims control. Using the
  term commits a claim to neither half of [THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md).
- **From [TERM-017](TERM-017.md).** [TERM-017](TERM-017.md)'s precedence is a maintained relation among
  an agent's regimes. This term's precedence is a property claimed by one
  state. [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) joins the two.
- **[TERM-078](TERM-078.md)** takes three of the marks (priority, persistence, resumption)
  as the marks of a persistent potential in a negotiation.
