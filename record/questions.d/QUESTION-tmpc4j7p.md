# QUESTION-tmpc4j7p: What separates an interpretive organization that constitutes an experiential perspective from one that only processes information, or only represents a perspective, when the separation must not require an episode's current contents to be veridically constrained by their referents, must not be drawn from the verdict it gives on groups and machines, and must justify the queries, interventions and timescales by which it individuates a perspective without appeal to the phenomenal unity it is meant to explain?

## The question

[CLAIM-tmpdga80](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdga80.md) identifies experience with an organized interpretive process,
and [CLAIM-tmp1lvwf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1lvwf.md) shows that interpretation alone does not qualify. The
question asks for the condition that does. It sets three contrasts:

1. **Processing against perspective.** The level step in A7's table, from
   coordinated interpretation to "a candidate organizational perspective".
2. **Perspective against a representation of one.** A7: "A perspectival
   organization is not necessarily the same thing as a representation of a
   perspectival organization." A system may have the first without the
   second, or represent itself as a unified experiencer without being one.
   [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) puts the illusion, where there is one, in the second.
3. **Constituting against simulating or reporting.** A6: the same
   interpretations may be realized by a system that only "simulate[s] or
   report[s]" them. A7's test of an answer: say "what a sophisticated
   nonexperiential interpreter would lack".

The separation must not require that an episode's current sensory
contents be veridically constrained by their purported external referents
([CLAIM-tmpjw8ev](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjw8ev.md)): a dream or an envatted brain is a case it must cover.
That is all the brain-in-a-vat argument supports. It does not establish
independence from developmental grounding, from ongoing bodily
regulation, or from the counterfactual sensorimotor organization of the
interpreting system. A dream can be rich while its current reference is
wrong and still depend on a lifetime of embodied learning. A criterion may
therefore require any of those three without contradicting the owner's
thesis. Which of them it requires decides which forms of enactivism,
active inference and embodiment theory the thesis actually opposes.

## Why it is a question

[QUESTION-046](QUESTION-046.md) asks for a criterion that tells a subject from an agent,
applied one way to modular minds and to groups. This question is that
criterion as the constitutive thesis would have to state it, in terms of
interpretation and perspective. It inherits [QUESTION-046](QUESTION-046.md)'s standard. A7:
specify the properties independently, "and then examine what
classifications follow, including uncomfortable ones", since otherwise
"we could keep adding new criteria whenever an intuitively nonconscious
system qualifies".

The exchange's candidates, none yet a criterion:

- an operative reference frame "of relevance, temporal continuity, and
  possible change" (A7 §8), which A7 conceded a corporation could have;
- an organization of possibilities as well as actual states, the
  counterfactual structure of a perspective (A7 §8);
- endogenous valuation (A7 §12), kept apart from felt distress as
  [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) keeps frustration apart from it;
- higher-order or recursive self-interpretation (A3, A4);
- the coherent world model of an embodied agent, as in IWMT ([LIT-tmp5k0u3](../literature.d/LIT-tmp5k0u3.md)),
  which requires a capacity for coupling and a history of it, not current
  coupling (note of 2026-10-22), so it is compatible with the grounding
  condition and parts from the thesis only on development.

Its evidential side is [QUESTION-048](QUESTION-048.md)'s. A5: complete answers about
integration, self-attribution and grounding "would not, by themselves,
establish phenomenal consciousness unless we accept an additional
constitutive premise". Language models with a functional workspace
([LIT-tmp9k9lx](../literature.d/LIT-tmp9k9lx.md)) make the overgeneration side concrete. A6: "The significant
result is not that language models experience anything."

## What would answer it

A property of a realized, temporally extended organization, A7's
\(\mathsf P(\mathcal O_{[t,t+\tau]})\), stated without reference to
experience or report. It must fall on the right side of blindsight and
ordinary perception, give a verdict on a firm and on a language model
that follows from the property and not from intuition, and survive a
change in current grounding with the internal dynamics and their history
held fixed. A measure that correlates with reports would not answer it.

**The perspectival-target problem.** Any organizational criterion is
assessed under an effective-description regime, [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md)'s
ℛ = (X, 𝒬, ℐ, ℬ, ε, τ). The queries 𝒬, interventions ℐ and timescale τ must
be chosen, and [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) shows that a different target picks a different
individuation. A macrostate can be excellent for predicting a firm's
decisions, an organism's locomotion or a brain's reports without being the
organization of a perspective. So an answer must also explain why its
chosen targets, interventions and timescales are relevant to perspectival
constitution, independently of its verdicts about consciousness. They must
not be defined in terms of the phenomenal unity or subjecthood the
resulting organization is supposed to explain. Predictive or
interventional adequacy for a declared target does not, by itself, show
that the target is phenomenally constitutive. A7: "We would want an
account connecting its structural features to the organization of
experience and showing why proposed counterexamples fail."

## Note of 2026-10-22: candidates tried, and the question's two halves

U8–U11 of the same exchange (workbench entry of 2026-10-22) worked on the
organizational side of this question, and the record's own entries
constrained each candidate:

- **Reciprocal integration** (A8). Local interpretations update a shared
  frame that constrains them in turn. A9 found the objection in the
  record: [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md) (Active) shows that the same reciprocity between
  interpretation and authority destabilizes symmetry exactly when
  βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ), so "it can generate pathological self-confirmation
  as readily as a coherent perspective". A9: "Reciprocal integration is a
  description of an organizational mechanism, not necessarily a distinctive
  mark of a perspective." That leaves open "What distinguishes a
  reciprocally organized interpretation that remains responsive to the
  significance of conflicts and alternatives from one that simply
  entrenches its existing interpretation?", which is [CLAIM-276](../claims.d/CLAIM-276.md)'s
  separation of coherence from fidelity.
- **Interventional sufficiency** (A8's causal-state frame, revised by A9).
  The frame is a class of histories with the same responses to
  interventions. Its predictive part is [TERM-062](../terms.d/TERM-062.md). [CLAIM-407](../claims.d/CLAIM-407.md) shows that
  predictive closure is not enough. A9: a candidate "must preserve the
  response profiles of the interpretive and evaluative mechanisms relevant
  to its claimed organizational identity" ([TERM-067](../terms.d/TERM-067.md)). The target must be
  declared first ([TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md), [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md)): "We cannot discover a perspectival
  macrostate by selecting whatever coarse-graining makes our preferred
  theory of experience come out true."
- **Self-maintenance** (A10). The organization maintains the constraints
  that govern its interpretation ([TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)). [CLAIM-tmpn2xr4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpn2xr4.md) shows that
  this is a separate, interventional fact. A11 withdrew it as a necessary
  condition, so it is a subclass ([TERM-tmpe36gg](../terms.d/TERM-tmpe36gg.md)).
- **Cross-context answerability** (A8, kept by A11). Changing an
  interpretation in one context alters the conditions under which another
  context's information acquires significance, through mechanisms the
  organization maintains. No test of it has been run. A8's three-
  architecture comparison (aggregation, external synchronization,
  reciprocal recontextualization, matched on outputs and self-
  descriptions) is its proposed design. Its negative control is a module
  that only declares unity, which is [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md)'s point.

The question now has two halves, which A9 said must be kept apart:

1. **Organizational.** "When does a context-dependent system of
   interpretive operators and influence-allocation mechanisms constitute an
   endogenously maintained causal organization, such that its significant
   distinctions and responses to interventions cannot be preserved by a
   merely aggregative or externally coordinated description?" This is
   [QUESTION-093](QUESTION-093.md)'s question (predictive autonomy, interventional autonomy,
   organizational closure, self-individuation) asked of interpretive
   regimes, with [QUESTION-115](QUESTION-115.md)'s multi-criterion search for a level.
2. **Phenomenal.** "If such an organization can be identified
   independently of phenomenal judgments, what justifies treating its
   realization as constitutive of an experiential perspective?" A11's
   sharper form: "Is perspectival organization merely what determines *how
   things appear*, or could the realization of that organization itself be
   what it means for anything to appear at all?" That is A3's thesis A
   (content is constructed) against thesis B (experience is constituted),
   in [TERM-tmpe36gg](../terms.d/TERM-tmpe36gg.md)'s words: does sense 1 settle sense 3?

An answer to the first half does not answer the second. A9: the exchange
risked becoming a theory "about the individuation of cognitive systems
rather than the constitution of conscious experience".

## Note of 2026-10-22: the readings

The works the exchange cited, read on 2026-10-22, offer candidates for the
organizational half. None answers the phenomenal half.

- **Access at an interface** (Whyte et al., [THEORY-tmpw20ca](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpw20ca.md)): conscious
  access where continuous inference meets discrete choice. Stated for
  humans, and tied to report.
- **Recursive precision modelling** (Laukkonen et al., [THEORY-tmpw19hw](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpw19hw.md)):
  as formalized, an ordinary hierarchical filter with a precision
  parameter per level would meet it.
- **A coherent, egocentric world model** (IWMT, [THEORY-tmpopaql](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpopaql.md)). It needs
  a capacity for coupling and a history of it, not current coupling, so it
  is compatible with the grounding condition above. It gives no
  threshold.
- **An attention schema** (Graziano, [THEORY-tmpncy2z](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpncy2z.md)): a representational
  condition, stated through report.
- **Higher-order representation** ([NOTE-tmpo7pcy](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpo7pcy.md)). This contests the
  question's second contrast. On higher-order views, representing one's
  own state is what makes it experiential, so constituting a perspective
  and representing one do not come apart as the question assumes.
- **Life** (Ward, [THEORY-tmpnwjjt](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpnwjjt.md)). An enactivist would test any answer by
  whether its property is distinctive of life or only characteristic of
  it. The question's ban on drawing the line from verdicts on machines
  cuts against that test.
- **A workspace without a point of view** (Gurnee et al., [THEORY-tmp111ci](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp111ci.md)).
  In the base model the workspace exists, and post-training installs the
  assistant's perspective. A candidate condition must say which of the two
  it tracks.

Barrett et al. ([NOTE-tmpr1xjh](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpr1xjh.md)) give no criterion for when categorization
is experiential.
