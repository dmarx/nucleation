# CASE-013: The reluctant writer

## The case

The commitment remains present and endorsed while email and minor tasks win.
Later the writer installs a precommitment device that removes the tempting
option.

## What it can show

Behaviour changing while preferences stay put — the individual-scale line of
support for [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), independent of Northstar ([ARG-005](../arguments.d/ARG-005.md)). The
audit asked for this side to be developed further: meta-regulatory dimensions
such as jurisdiction have only institutional examples.

## Note of 2026-10-13: re-derived as procrastination

The exchange of 2026-10-13 re-derived this case without citing it. A5 §4:
"Procrastination could then be a stable organizational regime in which
long-term goals remain explicitly endorsed but lack sufficient local
authority to govern task initiation", and "This explains how someone can
sincerely value an action while repeatedly failing to perform it". That is
this case and [CLAIM-047](../claims.d/CLAIM-047.md). What A5 added is a mechanism: the
avoidance coalition works by "increasing salience for uncertainties,
recruiting alternative activities, or destabilizing confidence in the task's
value", that is, on the conditions of influence and not by overpowering the
goal. Blocking the websites is A6's commitment, "an arrangement in which a
present configuration of operators constrains the authority allocations
available to their future configurations" ([CLAIM-122](../claims.d/CLAIM-122.md)). Workbench entry of
2026-10-13, 21:29.

## Note of 2026-10-18: three freedoms, and a manuscript that opens with this case

A11 §4 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 re-derived the second half of this
case: "A person who restricts access to a distraction might reduce
immediate behavioral flexibility while increasing their ability to pursue
an endorsed project." It split it in three, freedom to commit, freedom
under commitment and freedom to revise: "None can replace the others."
Blocking the websites uses the first and narrows the second, and whether
the writer keeps the third is open. So a precommitment shrinks what is
reachable while making the endorsed project realizable, and reachable-set
size is not monotone with autonomy ([CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md), [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md)).

The manuscript proposal of that exchange opens with this case and does not
cite it. A12 §1: "Begin with a familiar case: an agent reflectively
endorses completing a difficult project but repeatedly acts otherwise."
A15 §1 opens with three agents, the first of whom "endorses an action but
finds it prohibitively difficult to perform", and the second "reliably
acts on an endorsed commitment but cannot reconsider it effectively"
([CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md)). See the note of 2026-10-18 on [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md). [CASE-112](CASE-112.md) models
the case: the volition moves the balance of authority without moving the
regime, and a change in the structure of the competition switches it.
