# LIT-tmppg6h5: Federal Reserve announces Wells Fargo is no longer subject to the asset growth restriction from the Board's 2018 enforcement action against the bank

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (2025) — https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20250603a.htm

## Key takeaways

- **The lift.** "The Federal Reserve determined that Wells Fargo has met all
  the conditions required by the 2018 enforcement action for removal of the
  growth restriction."
- **What it rested on.** "the bank was required to improve its governance
  and risk management program, and complete a third-party review of these
  improvements".
- **Not the whole sanction.** "The other provisions in the 2018 enforcement
  action will remain in place until the bank satisfies the requirements for
  their termination."

## Standing in the record

A primary document: it dates the end of the growth restriction on the bank
of [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md). No THEORY is filed from it.
