The owner asked for every reference in their prior-art novelty map to be
registered in this record. The map is a private working document on concepts
as projectors, the linear representation hypothesis and their neighbours.

**How it was done.** Three agents worked in parallel, split by domain:

- quantum foundations, logic and representation theory;
- ML, information theory and the thermodynamics of learning;
- philosophy and linguistics.

For each reference they checked both records, pinned down the exact work and
verified its identifier. Identifiers were verified against Crossref, arXiv or
the publisher. Where no DOI covers the first edition, a stable Internet
Archive URL is used instead: Riesz 1907, Ryle 1949 and Armstrong 1978.

**The result.** The map names 63 works once multi-part works are split:
Aerts–Gabora I and II, Döring–Isham I–IV, Artin–Wedderburn as two papers,
and Cramér–Rao as Rao 1945 and Cramér 1946.

- **6 were already held.** Four were held first-hand: [LIT-262](../../record/literature.d/LIT-262.md), [LIT-273](../../record/literature.d/LIT-273.md),
  [LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md) and [LIT-220](../../record/literature.d/LIT-220.md). [LIT-254](../../record/literature.d/LIT-254.md) and [LIT-267](../../record/literature.d/LIT-267.md) were also already held.
- **57 are new seeds,** all `Deferred`, from metadata and published
  descriptions. None has been read.

**Held in both records ([ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md)).** Six of the new seeds are also held in the
anthology: Park 2023, Huh 2024, McCandlish 2018, Shwartz-Ziv & Tishby 2017,
Power 2022 and Nanda 2023. The reason for a second entry is the one [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md)
asks for: this record holds them for the owner's prior-art map, the
anthology for ML practice. Each seed names its anthology entry in its body.

**Near misses the agents caught.** These look held but are not:

- [LIT-263](../../record/literature.d/LIT-263.md) is a *review* of Kochen–Specker, not the 1967 paper.
- [LIT-270](../../record/literature.d/LIT-270.md) is Aerts 2025, not Aerts–Gabora 2005.
- [LIT-175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-175.md) is Worrall 2007, not 1989.
- [LIT-045](../../record/literature.d/LIT-045.md), [LIT-184](../../record/literature.d/LIT-184.md), [LIT-217](../../record/literature.d/LIT-217.md), [LIT-218](../../record/literature.d/LIT-218.md) and [LIT-219](../../record/literature.d/LIT-219.md) are Ladyman or Ross works,
  but not Ladyman 1998 or *Every Thing Must Go*.
- [LIT-042](../../record/literature.d/LIT-042.md) and [LIT-224](../../record/literature.d/LIT-224.md) are not Shannon 1949.
- The record's information-bottleneck items ([LIT-240](../../record/literature.d/LIT-240.md), [LIT-226](../../record/literature.d/LIT-226.md)) are follow-ups,
  not Tishby, Pereira & Bialek 1999.
- The Riesz cluster ([LIT-230](../../record/literature.d/LIT-230.md), [LIT-243](../../record/literature.d/LIT-243.md)) is not the 1907 paper. That paper is
  registered now.

**Loose references, filed under the agents' proposed reading.** These are
flagged in each entry and open to revision by the owner:

- **Kondor:** Kondor & Trivedi 2018.
- **Aristotle:** *Categories* ch. 10 and *De Interpretatione* ch. 7, in
  Ackrill's translation.
- **Armstrong:** *Universals and Scientific Realism*, both volumes as one
  entry.
- **Gelfand:** Gelfand–Naimark 1943. Its DOI is the 1994 reprint.
- **Stone duality:** Stone 1936.
- **Pontryagin:** 1934.
- **Peter–Weyl:** 1927.
- **Walsh:** 1923.
- **Shannon limit:** Shannon 1949.
- **Artin–Wedderburn:** both papers.
- **Haag:** the 1996 second edition.
- **Van Trees and Rao:** DOIs of later reissues, dated to first publication.
- **Murota et al. 2010:** the map's author list merges two papers. The
  registered one is Murota, Kanno, M. Kojima & S. Kojima. The companion,
  Maehara & Murota 2010, is not registered.

**Not registered, deliberately.** These are adjacent to the map's
references but not named by it:

- Isham–Butterfield parts II–IV;
- Lyons vol. 2;
- the Ganter–Wille second edition;
- Wille 2001;
- Sommers 1964;
- Gelfand 1941;
- Stone 1937;
- Haag's first edition;
- Maehara & Murota 2010;
- Fréchet's 1907 note.

**One inconsistency flagged for later reading.** For Engels 2024 and for
Tishby, Pereira & Bialek 1999, the agent's venue details were from memory.
The entries keep only what was verified.
