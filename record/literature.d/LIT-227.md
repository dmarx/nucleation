# LIT-227: Contrastive Learning Is Spectral Clustering On Similarity Graph

Zhiquan Tan, Yifan Zhang, Jingqin Yang, Yang Yuan (2023), *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2024)* — [ARXIV-2303.15103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15103)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpohlyt](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpohlyt.md).*

- **An exact identity.** InfoNCE over all n objects equals the
  cross-entropy between two Markov random fields on directed subgraphs
  with one outgoing edge per node, one built from π and one from the
  kernel Gram matrix of the embeddings (Thm 3.1, Step 1).
- **Its spectral form.** With a Gaussian kernel on the unit sphere and
  symmetric π, that objective is tr(Zᵀ L(π) Z) + log R(Z), where log R(Z)
  is the sum of InfoNCE's log normalizers (Thm 3.1, Step 2; the 1/τ on
  the trace term is dropped in the statement).
- **A wide definition.** "Spectral clustering" is any
  min_Z tr(Zᵀ L(W) Z) + E(Z) with any regularizer E (Def. 2.7). No
  minimizer is characterized, so the theorem does not say that InfoNCE's
  embedding is, or spans, the Laplacian's eigenvectors.
- **CLIP.** The same identity on a directed bipartite pair graph, called
  "generalized spectral clustering", with a two-sentence proof and a
  sampling scheme CLIP does not use (Thm 4.2).
- **Kernels.** The maximum-entropy argument (Thm 5.1) gives the softmax
  form for any similarity. Kernel-InfoNCE mixtures gain 1 to 4 points on
  CIFAR-10/100 and TinyImageNet; single Laplacian and γ = 0.5 kernels lose
  to SimCLR on CIFAR-100 at 400 epochs.
