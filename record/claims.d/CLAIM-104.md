# CLAIM-104: Authenticity grounds normativity downward, from an encompassing agent to its constituents, but not across agents at one level

## The claim

U75: "authenticity gives us an account for normativity at least from the
downward direction of superagent-->constituents, it however doesn't give us
normativity across agents that share an environment and level of
organizational complexity."

## A leak worth recording

A263–A265 had already separated authenticity from cross-scale normativity.
A270 accepted U75 as "an especially strong starting point" and in the same
turn conceded that "the first does not logically entail the second"; A271
then re-mapped authenticity to the "inward" direction and left "downward" to
concordance, without noting the change. Resting this on [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md) is
what would have made the dependency, and its retraction, mechanical.
