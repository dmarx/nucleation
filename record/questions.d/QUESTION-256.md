# QUESTION-256: What forms of continuity must a person keep to remain capable of governing changes in their own practical organization, and which of those forms can other processes, other people or the environment supply?

## Why it is a question

A26, after the owner proposed Clive Wearing at U26: "What forms of
continuity are required for an individual to remain capable of governing
changes in their own practical organization—and which of those forms can
be supplied by other processes, other people, or the environment?"

Its four continuities were skill continuity, short-horizon organization,
autobiographical continuity and experienced diachronic ownership. Wearing
has the first two and lacks the last two ([CASE-137](../cases.d/CASE-137.md)). A26 also asked
"Which forms of regulation require continuity of remembered experience,
and which can be maintained by skills, dispositions, current context, and
external support?", and individuated his competence "relative to the
stable environmental and interpersonal supports".

The record has parts of an answer and no answer:

- [CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md) makes the self-governing self an organization that takes part
  in governing the conditions of its own persistence. It does not say what
  memory that needs.
- [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md) says persistence through inactivity is a capacity to
  reinstate an organization. Wearing reinstates skills with no memory of
  having had them.
- Clark and Chalmers's Otto ([NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)), whose notebook holds what his
  memory cannot, is the record's case of a continuity supplied from
  outside. [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md)'s sense 2 is built on it.
- [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md) separates reflexive governance from the perspective it
  governs. This question asks what that governance needs to remember.

An answer would name, for each continuity, whether governing a change in
one's practical organization fails without it, and whether a person,
record or routine outside can stand in for it. It would also say what
kind of evidence settles that. A26 warned: "I would not treat clinical
cases as interventions on freely manipulable independent variables."
