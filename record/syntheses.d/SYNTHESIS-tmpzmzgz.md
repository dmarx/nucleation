# SYNTHESIS-tmpzmzgz: What a system can deploy before a deadline is not what it has or knows it has: a search that fails by the deadline shows that the item is unavailable and shows absence only with a certificate about the unsearched remainder, retrieval and evidence pull in opposite directions when a category is carved, sortings that never conflict as descriptions conflict as arrangements only when each item is stored once, and whether leaving results where they can be read back is one trade with amortized computation stays open

*A part account of the distributed-agency line ("Distributed agency"): the
part filed from the owner's EMT case and LEGO parable (2026-10-15) and the
database and retrieval readings filed beside them. The line's account is
built over it and the line's other part accounts. The mechanical community
map put this part together with two others; read through, it is a
separate inquiry, with 41 pairs of its members linked by a relation or a
mention, 12 pairs linking it to the rest of that map's part and 1 to the
rest of the line. Its two siblings from the same map are the accounts of
partners coming to depend on one another and of perspectives, legibility
and correction, SYNTHESIS-tmpqnl6z and SYNTHESIS-tmpvvtbk.*

## The account

- **Having, knowing and deploying.** CLAIM-296 (A17's, from the owner's
  CASE-085; thesis, interpretive, Active): "Possessing a resource,
  possessing information about that resource, and possessing the
  capability to deploy it are distinct conditions." The last is a relation
  among the resource, the agent, its environment, the task, the deadline
  and the cue. TERM-055 (A17 §3's α with A19's cue, Active) names it: "the
  chance that an agent–environment system, given the cue it actually has,
  deploys the resource for the task before the deadline". Its summary: "The
  term separates the deployability of a resource from its presence and
  from knowing it is present (CLAIM-296)." CASE-085 (the owner's own
  experience; stipulated specimen, Active) is the drug that was stocked,
  known to be stocked and not found in time; it "illustrates CLAIM-296 and
  is the source of TERM-055".
- **The medic's rule.** CLAIM-306 (first filed as negation as failure on
  a time bound, its voice not stated; thesis, demonstrated, Superseded)
  held that
  absence is shown "only by a pass that rules out every place it could
  be". CLAIM-311 (filed at the owner's request from the database readings;
  counter, demonstrated, Active) objects: a sorted table and the Threshold
  Algorithm establish absence from part of a collection, "So the asymmetry
  is not between one find and an exhaustive pass. It is between one find
  and a certificate about the remainder." CLAIM-312 (the restatement at the
  owner's request; thesis, interpretive, Active) rests on CLAIM-311 and
  supersedes CLAIM-306: "If I can't find it, it isn't there" asserts
  operational unavailability, "a search that fails by the deadline
  establishes unavailability outright", and absence needs a certificate
  "from exhaustion, from an invariant of the arrangement, or from a
  closed-world default". It keeps CLAIM-306's Bayes term as the gap between
  the two readings, which matters "when the statement travels to someone
  who acts on presence".
- **Carving for retrieval and carving on evidence.** CLAIM-307 (from
  Mathematics of Meaning's CLM-166, reproduced here; thesis, demonstrated,
  Active; first line formal-apparatus): evidence that a distinction exists
  grows with its copies and the search a bin saves shrinks with them, "so
  the bins a deadline forces are often the ones the data least supports".
  CLAIM-307 grounds on CASE-094 (the owner's parable; stipulated
  thought-experiment, Active), whose "Very few of but use often" is, in
  CLAIM-307's words, "the cell where retrieval demands a bin and the
  evidence is weakest".
- **Descriptions and arrangements.** CLAIM-310 (what Mathematics of Meaning
  graded right in the owner's LEGO monograph; thesis, demonstrated, Active;
  first line formal-apparatus): sortings of determinate objects never
  conflict as descriptions, and conflict as arrangements, where the
  cheapest depends on the bin budget, the query mix and "what the wanted
  item must still be told apart by". CLAIM-313 (filed at the owner's
  request from the hashing readings; counter, demonstrated, Active; first
  line formal-apparatus) objects that one set of bins realizes one
  partition only when each item is stored once, "which binds bricks but not
  index entries"; CLAIM-310's version 2 takes the one-copy condition, and
  CLAIM-313's note says "The record is persuaded."
- **The open programme.** QUESTION-101 (A17 §9, from CASE-085; Open) asks
  when availability is the system's rather than any member's; CLAIM-296's
  note of 2026-10-28 says it "bears on QUESTION-101 and does not answer
  it". QUESTION-097 (A18's eighth candidate; Open) asks when an observable
  distinction corresponds to a performable intervention. QUESTION-095 (the
  owner's, after the cockpit reading; Open; also nucleating-argument) asks
  whether leaving a result where it can be read back cheaply is the same
  trade as amortizing the computation; its readings "split the proposed
  single trade into four strategies". QUESTION-114 (Open) refines it for
  sorting and physical database design and finds no joint cost model: "The
  finding that most changes this question is Sleator and Tarjan's", that
  "reorganizing at the moment of use is within a constant of the best
  possible". Seven narrower questions hold promotion conditions moved from
  readings: QUESTION-194, QUESTION-224, QUESTION-227, QUESTION-230 and
  QUESTION-238 refine QUESTION-114; QUESTION-235 and QUESTION-308 refine
  QUESTION-095.

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **Copies dissolve the carving conflict.** That where items can be
  copied (CLAIM-313), a system can carve the bins retrieval demands without
  giving up the categories evidence supports, so CLAIM-307's opposition
  costs space instead of forcing a choice. It fails on an equivocation:
  CLAIM-307's carving is of a category, a distinction named, and its own
  "What it does not say" is that a system carving by retrieval alone "would
  make bins for distinctions that are not there"; copying changes the
  arrangement, not whether the distinction is there.
