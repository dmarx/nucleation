# ADR-070: [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s "endorsement" is split and renamed: any effect on another process's future influence is conferral, conferral through a maintained relation is a grant, and "endorse" is left to the reflective sense

## Context

[TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md) defined endorsement, between an organization's processes, as
"any effect one process has on another's share of future influence".
Suppressing a rival counted. The term said it was not Frankfurt's
identification with a desire.

The word kept being read the other way. In the exchange "Organizational
Nucleation and SLT" of 2026-11-04, A used "endorse" for the reflective
attitude at A11, A12, A15 and A16 ([TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s note of 2026-11-04). In
[CLAIM-286](../claims.d/CLAIM-286.md)'s note of the same date the reflective evaluation (a) and the
causal effect (b) came apart: a process can judge another negatively and
still confer nothing. The owner, reviewing that note on
dmarx/nucleation#397: "the disconnect between (a) and (b) is concerning
and has me wondering if maybe we should split the TERM or maybe come up
with a different word to use for non-Frankfurtian "endorsement" senses".

Three senses were in play:

1. the reflective attitude, Frankfurt's, now [TERM-tmp0evbm](../terms.d/TERM-tmp0evbm.md);
2. any causal effect on another's future influence, [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md);
3. an effect mediated by a maintained relation of granting, delegation,
   eligibility or support, A14 §4's proposed narrowing, held in [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s
   note for the owner's ruling.

## Decision

The owner decided, on 2026-11-04, to split and rename [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md) and to
adopt A14's narrowing, and chose the words.

- **Conferral** ([TERM-tmplu4ih](../terms.d/TERM-tmplu4ih.md)) is [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s definition, renamed: any
  effect one process has on another's share of future influence.
  Negative conferral withdraws influence.
- **Grant** ([TERM-tmp08c21](../terms.d/TERM-tmp08c21.md)) refines conferral: a conferral that runs
  through a maintained relation of granting, delegation, eligibility or
  support. Revoking the relation is revocation.
- **"Endorse"** is left to the reflective sense, higher-order volition
  ([TERM-tmp0evbm](../terms.d/TERM-tmp0evbm.md)).
- [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md) is Superseded by both, never deleted. Its body stays as the
  history of the word.

What must not break: a claim citing [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md) keeps its meaning. Its
`uses` moves to conferral, whose definition is [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s. Where a
claim's own argument needs a maintained relation, it moves to grant as
well. Prose elsewhere that says "endorsement" in [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s sense means
conferral until it is revised; the sweep of that prose is done use by
use, since some uses are reflective and stay "endorse".

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep "endorsement" with A14's stricter definition.** The narrowing
  removes suppression-of-rivals from the term but leaves the word, and
  the word is what readers misread.
- **Split without renaming**, as "causal endorsement" and "granted
  endorsement". Every use would need its qualifier, and an unqualified
  "endorsement" would stay ambiguous.
- **Rename without the narrowing.** It fixes the misreading but leaves
  facilitation and authorization under one term, which A14 and [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s
  own note on behaviour (A7 §14, "authorization, incidental coupling, and
  common-cause effects") say should be told apart.
- **Other words.** "Backing" carries the supportive attitude, "uptake"
  is already used for an intervention being taken up ([QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md)),
  "warrant" is a CLAIM field, and "sanction" also means a penalty.

## Consequences

- The six claims that name [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md) in `uses` name conferral, and grant
  where their argument needs a maintained relation.
- The claims filed from the exchange of 2026-11-04 are restated in the new
  words in the same pull request.
- The other prose uses of "endorsement" in [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)'s sense, across about
  thirty-six files, are revised in a later pull request, one use at a
  time.
