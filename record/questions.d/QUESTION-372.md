# QUESTION-372: Does a perspective's motivational threshold induce its tolerances, do those tolerances induce the organization's motivational compatibility, and does that compatibility, kept across changing contexts, give psychological flexibility or organizational effectiveness; and are those last two one property named at two scales, or two properties?

## Why it is a question

The owner, at U19: "motivational threshold -> motivational tolerances ->
motivational compatibility -> psychological flexibility / organizational
effectiveness".

Each link is a different kind of step.

1. **Threshold to tolerance.** In a linear-logistic model this is a
   derivation ([CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md)). Open: whether perspectives in persons or
   organizations have a threshold that stays fixed while baseline and gain
   move.
2. **Tolerance to compatibility.** A complex built from low-activation
   regions is downward-closed (A19, A20 §3). But its vertices are
   perspectives, while [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s are the demands an organization
   maintains. Its regions are also not acceptance sets: A19 says "T_i is a
   *nonactivation region*, not automatically an endorsement region". A19
   and A20 therefore split a quiet complex K^Q from an accommodated one,
   K^A, whose requirements "can include activation, inhibition, delegation,
   continued accessibility, and appropriate response" ([CLAIM-549](../claims.d/CLAIM-549.md)).
   [CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md) already holds "jointly met, within tolerances … across the
   contexts where they interact". Open: whether K^A is determined by the
   tolerances and the attainable set, or needs requirements stated
   separately.
3. **Compatibility to flexibility.** A20 §4 called "∀c ∃π_c ⇏ ∃π ∀c" "the
   key mathematical gap between local motivational compatibility and
   psychological flexibility". The record holds that gap:
   - [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)'s ordinal form: contexts that give the same evidence admit
     no policy that succeeds in both;
   - [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md)'s last clause: selection "depends on what the agent can
     read";
   - [CASE-153](../cases.d/CASE-153.md), a toy model of it.
   What is open is the link's name. "Psychological flexibility" is ACT's
   term: "the ability to contact the present moment more fully as a
   conscious human being, and to change or persist in behavior when doing
   so serves valued ends" ([LIT-555](../literature.d/LIT-555.md), [NOTE-431](../notes.d/NOTE-431.md), p. 7). A's sense, success
   across contexts against an "independently specified" standard, is
   [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)'s organizational flexibility. It is nearer Kashdan and
   Rottenberg's broader sense ([LIT-553](../literature.d/LIT-553.md)). [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)'s note of 2026-11-04 has
   the reading that keeps ACT's sense.
4. **The slash.** A19 set "Regulatory flexibility" apart from "Practical
   effectiveness": "A system can be flexible among inappropriate goals".
   The slash may instead pair an individual-scale name with an
   organizational-scale name. The record reads that transfer in Guida,
   Minutola and Presti ([LIT-1218](../literature.d/LIT-1218.md)) and in Bond ([LIT-1830](../literature.d/LIT-1830.md), unread).

## What would answer it

- **Link 1.** A measurement of a perspective's activation probability
  against discrepancy in two contexts. A fixed threshold with a shifted
  baseline would fit [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md). A threshold that moves with context
  would not.
- **Link 2.** A construction in which K^A is computed from tolerance
  regions and the attainable set alone, or a counterexample in which two
  organizations with the same regions and attainable set differ in K^A.
- **Link 3.** A20 §9's design. Hold every threshold fixed and vary only
  the governance. If the successful-context set R(π) changes, flexibility
  is not reducible to the perspectives' tolerances. Then remove the
  context information. If performance falls, flexibility depends on
  legibility ([TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)). [CASE-153](../cases.d/CASE-153.md)'s 2×2×2 design is the record's version,
  not yet run.
- **The slash.** The owner's gloss. Failing that, a case that is flexible
  and ineffective, or effective and inflexible, would show two properties
  and not one.

## Note of 2026-11-04: the second reading of the slash, as a case

At the owner's review the owner said the intended sense does not matter
here, and invited a case for any alternative reading that supports one.
The second reading of link 4, one property at two scales, does:
[CASE-195](../cases.d/CASE-195.md) joins psychological and organizational flexibility by
propagation through a conferral network, after Guida, Minutola and Presti
([LIT-1218](../literature.d/LIT-1218.md)). Under ACT's definition flexibility already serves the
system's own valued ends, so the two readings part only on the standard
for effectiveness: the organization's own values, or one specified
independently of them, A19's. No status changes.
