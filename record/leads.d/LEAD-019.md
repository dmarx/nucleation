# LEAD-019: Run the discriminating fidelity study: renderings built to dissociate wording, proposition, static stance and transport structure, judged by named receivers, against a proposition-plus-force rival and a text-similarity baseline

## What it would do

Run [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md), which absorbs and repairs [CASE-184](../cases.d/CASE-184.md), once its margins and the
footing measure are fixed. Include [CASE-065](../cases.d/CASE-065.md)'s order-effect items as an arm
and the flattening arm [CLAIM-637](../claims.d/CLAIM-637.md) names.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-228](../claims.d/CLAIM-228.md): "What holds it is a missing result: [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) has not been
  run"
- [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md): "a study designed and not run"
- [ARG-017](../arguments.d/ARG-017.md): "It waits on a [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) result."
- [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md): "What would count as an answer here is a specification of
  the preserved object that can be measured on real translations, retellings
  and cross-modal renderings, and that predicts human judgements of
  communicative fidelity better than semantic similarity does."
- [CLAIM-637](../claims.d/CLAIM-637.md): "a collapsing or flattening arm in that study"

## What would count

For the theses: transport measures predicting receivers' recovery beyond
the propositional and static-profile rivals by more than the fixed margin.
Against them: no gain inside the margin, which meets [CLAIM-202](../claims.d/CLAIM-202.md)'s and
[CLAIM-228](../claims.d/CLAIM-228.md)'s defeat conditions and answers [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md) in the negative for
the populations tested.
