# THEORY-2223: Howard, Gagné and Bureau's (2017) meta-analysis of 486 samples finds that self-determination theory's motivation regulations follow a continuum-like (simplex) ordering that one dimension of self-determination accounts for almost entirely, but that integrated regulation is empirically indistinguishable from identified regulation (pooled r = .91), as are the three subtypes of intrinsic motivation from each other, so that no validated scale measures the most integrated rung of the continuum separately

## The account

Howard, Gagné and Bureau's (2017) meta-analysis of 486 samples finds that
self-determination theory's motivation regulations follow a continuum-like
ordering that one dimension of self-determination accounts for almost
entirely, but that integrated regulation is empirically indistinguishable
from identified regulation (pooled r = .91), as are the three subtypes of
intrinsic motivation from each other.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-2313](../notes.d/NOTE-2313.md); its key results are quoted there with
locations, from the authors' manuscript. The structure is of self-report
correlations, disattenuated, with near-total unexplained heterogeneity.

## Bearing

On [THEORY-054](THEORY-054.md)'s first promote_when clause, which asks for a measure of
integrated regulation separable from identified regulation: across the
validated scales to 2016 there is none.
