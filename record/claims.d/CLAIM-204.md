# CLAIM-204: A communicative kind's identity is grounded in its relational organization, for which competent participants' uptake is the standard; statistical methods recover aspects of it from restricted observations; translation transports it; and what persists is what the receivers can still recover, of which the analyst can certify the independently identifiable part

## The claim

This is the record's restatement of 2026-10-10 of the October thesis
([CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)), in reply to seven of the audit's objections. It is a proposal,
Proposed until the study it names is run, and not adopted until the owner
decides. [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) itself is unchanged.

It keeps [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s four clauses and changes two of them and the defeat
condition.

- **Ontological.** Unchanged in content: identity is grounded in relational
  organization, partly ([CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md)). Added: competent participants' uptake is
  the standard against which the organization is individuated
  ([CLAIM-217](CLAIM-217.md)). This answers [CLAIM-206](CLAIM-206.md)'s first horn, and supplies
  the argument [CLAIM-247](CLAIM-247.md) found missing for moving the bearer of
  identity from the observational class to the kind.
- **Epistemic.** Unchanged: statistical methods recover aspects of the
  organization from restricted observations ([CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md), [CLAIM-135](CLAIM-135.md)).
- **Transport.** Unchanged: translation transports it ([CLAIM-077](CLAIM-077.md)).
- **Fidelity.** Changed. [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md): "Fidelity concerns which independently
  identifiable, decision-relevant relational distinctions persist through
  those transformations." Here: what persists is what the receivers can
  still recover, and the analyst can certify the part that is independently
  identifiable. That is [CLAIM-223](CLAIM-223.md)'s reading, which keeps both the
  receiver of [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) and the analyst of [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md), in different roles
  ([CLAIM-140](CLAIM-140.md); [CLAIM-211](CLAIM-211.md)).

**The defeat condition, against [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s.**

- *First disjunct.* [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s rival was "topic-factor, embedding or
  surface similarity". Added: a supervised text model, the strongest
  rival a text alone supports, and human-rated propositional sameness, a
  rival rated by the same method as the predictor ([CLAIM-233](CLAIM-233.md),
  [CLAIM-232](CLAIM-232.md)). Added: the relational
  profiles are measured on probes independent of the verdicts, so that the
  verdicts cannot be a probe of themselves ([CLAIM-206](CLAIM-206.md)).
- *Second disjunct.* [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s third, "no relational structure beyond the
  observed response profiles is needed to explain those judgements", is
  replaced. [CLAIM-206](CLAIM-206.md) showed that, read as prediction, it is always met,
  since every model the programme fits is a model of response profiles, and
  read as explanation, no data decide it. The replacement is the comparison
  that objection proposed: a relational model against a model of the
  response profiles alone, scored on held-out realizations, media and
  languages. The relational model must do better on transfer, where a model
  fitted to one set of profiles has no profiles of the new items to fit.
- *Third disjunct.* [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s second, kept, with "across languages or
  media" narrowed to languages, so that a cross-modal result cannot meet it
  ([CLAIM-222](CLAIM-222.md)).
- *The design.* All three are stated on a design with named receivers,
  stated tasks, an interlingual arm and margins fixed in advance
  ([CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md)), which [CLAIM-244](CLAIM-244.md), [CLAIM-222](CLAIM-222.md) and [CLAIM-238](CLAIM-238.md)
  asked for.

**Direction.** [CLAIM-246](CLAIM-246.md) objected that sharing a kind is symmetric and
blind to descent. The record answers it in [CLAIM-210](CLAIM-210.md): fidelity belongs
to a transport, from a given source to a target ([TERM-043](../terms.d/TERM-043.md)), and an utterance
made independently of the source shares its kind and has no fidelity to it,
because no transport relates them. This restatement keeps the transport
clause, and its defeat conditions are stated on renderings made from their
sources in [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md). Direction is thus fixed by the design, as a fact of
provenance, and not tested by any disjunct. What the restatement adds for
that objection is only this: the comparison is between a source and its
renderings, never between two realizations of a kind taken in either order.

**What stays untested.** The ontological clause, as [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) grants. A
traditional ontology of intrinsically individuated objects can reproduce the
same predictions ([CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md)). [CLAIM-217](CLAIM-217.md)'s standard makes the kind's
extension testable; it does not make its ontology so.

## What it does not say

- What [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) does not say: that factorization proves structural realism,
  that the thesis holds for objects in general, or that the act is wholly
  constituted by relations.
- It does not say the owner endorses it. [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md) is the thesis of the
  exchange's October proposal, which the owner asked for and did not argue;
  this is the record's restatement.
- It does not say the analyst's certification is unnecessary. A distinction
  receivers recover and no admissible probe identifies survives without
  evidence that it did ([CLAIM-223](CLAIM-223.md)).
