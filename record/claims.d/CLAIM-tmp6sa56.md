# CLAIM-tmp6sa56: If constructor theory's programme holds, Deutsch and Marletto's possibility facts meet the relata–realization frame's second defeater: being an information medium is an exact property of a substrate fixed by which tasks are possible, every constructor that could perform them is approximate and none need exist, and the laws are themselves statements of possibility that mention no constructor, so an exact individuating constraint is realized by no actual thing, and reading it as modal maintenance concedes that what individuates is the modal fact and not its realizers

## The objection

1. **The individuating property is exact.** A substrate "instantiates
   classical information" when an information variable of it is sharp and
   giving it the other attributes was possible, and this is "an exact,
   intrinsic property of the substrate" ([THEORY-1066](../theory.d/THEORY-1066.md), §4). It fixes a kind:
   information media.
2. **Every realizer is approximate, and none need exist.** "No perfect
   constructors exist in nature", and possibility needs no actual
   constructor ([THEORY-1078](../theory.d/THEORY-1078.md), §2). So no actual thing realizes the exact
   property. At most, approximate things approach it.
3. **The laws do not help.** [CLAIM-tmpr0sfe](CLAIM-tmpr0sfe.md) says a possibility fact is a
   fact about the laws and is ruled with them. But constructor theory's
   programme is that the laws are statements about which tasks are
   possible and impossible, made "*without* referring explicitly to
   constructors" ([THEORY-1078](../theory.d/THEORY-1078.md)). On the programme, reducing possibility to
   the laws reduces it to possibility. The reduction does not reach
   anything realized.
4. **Modal maintenance is not maintenance.** [CLAIM-tmpr0sfe](CLAIM-tmpr0sfe.md) reads the
   retention clause as the frame's "maintained by something" made modal.
   But [CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md)'s sense, from [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md), is actual. A constraint is
   conserved at one time scale and produced by processes at another. "Could
   be maintained to any accuracy" names no maintainer. A constraint fixed
   by what could be realized and maintained, and by no realization, is what
   (b) calls "a specification prior to every realization".

## What follows for [CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md)

If the programme holds, (b) is met for the kinds constructor theory
individuates, information media first, and the frame is restricted to
kinds whose constraints have actual realizers. This holds whichever way
the symmetry question goes ([CLAIM-tmpfbofs](CLAIM-tmpfbofs.md) or its alternative). Even an
effective law, realized by the level below, would be stated here as
possibility facts that mention none of what realizes it.

## What it does not say

- That constructor theory is true. The objection is conditional on
  principle I, the programme's conjecture.
- That information media are individuated without relation to anything.
  "Intrinsic" here means referring to no specific other substrate
  ([THEORY-1066](../theory.d/THEORY-1066.md)). The objection concerns (b), not (a).

## Why it is not leaned to

Point 3 proves less than it seems. That laws can be stated without
mentioning constructors does not show that nothing realizes them, any
more than stating a conservation law without naming the transition graph
shows the graph does no work ([CLAIM-tmpfbofs](CLAIM-tmpfbofs.md)). Point 4 is the real
disagreement: whether [CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md)'s "maintained by something" is met by
what the laws admit or needs an actual maintainer. That is the owner's
reading to give.
