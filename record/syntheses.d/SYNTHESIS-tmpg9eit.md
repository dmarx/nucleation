# SYNTHESIS-tmpg9eit: An agent made of a population of interests can act as one while they stay divided: synchronization of its interests is desirable and not necessary, the routes by which it acts as one (arbitration, a change in which interest may act, coinciding valuations, local accommodation) are distinct and differently costly, and whether its coordination switches or reorganizes continuously depends on how it is constituted

*A part account of the will-organization line (ADR-083, ADR-084); the line's account is built over it.*

## The account

**The question.** QUESTION-034 (the owner's prompt of 2026-10-11) refines the
paper's QUESTION-015 for an agent the record reads as a population: does it
act as one by bargaining, by a change in which interest may act, or by
agreement? QUESTION-090 (A4's, distributed-agency first) refines it for a
population that allocates authority to its own members.

**Where the population comes from.** CLAIM-169 (the record's reading of
Ainslie, THEORY-067; Proposed) bears on QUESTION-034: an agent is many
interests over a choice "only as far as its valuations of that choice
cross within the choice's horizon". TERM-050 (Active) defines
intrapersonal synchronization on that basis, and CLAIM-198 (Proposed),
resting on CLAIM-169 and CLAIM-197, adds that preference reversal is not yet
disunity: division needs an overlap, which self-prediction creates.

**Three routes, two answers filed.**

- *Agreement.* CLAIM-162 (Proposed) answers QUESTION-034, resting on
  CLAIM-169: in flow nothing has to be overridden, which is why it is
  reported as effortless. It was narrowed on 2026-10-28 to concede ARG-016
  (Proposed), the argument from CLAIM-169 to CLAIM-162 into which CLAIM-163's
  objection moved (CLAIM-163, Superseded): the word "synchronization"
  carries no dynamical mechanism here.
- *A change in which interest may act.* CLAIM-156 (Proposed) answers
  QUESTION-034, resting on CLAIM-169 and CLAIM-027: mindfulness suspends
  the step from an impulse's dominance to its control of action, in
  TERM-017's terms. It corroborates CLAIM-349 of the part on maintaining
  the capacity to govern.
- *Bargaining* has no answer filed; CLAIM-169's defeater names its
  signatures.

**Acting as one without synchronization.** QUESTION-078 (an interlocutor's
reading relayed by the owner) refines QUESTION-034; QUESTION-304 was split
from it and is Answered by CLAIM-261 (the owner's, Active): synchronization
is "desirable, but not necessary for acting as one". CLAIM-261 also bears
on QUESTION-078, which keeps whether differentiated interests can keep
distinct roles in a stable pattern. QUESTION-077 (the assistant's, from
CASE-074) refines QUESTION-078: is agreement by coupling a third route?

**Practical synchronization and its cost.** TERM-061 (Active), defined from
A45 at the owner's request, is a second dimension and not a generalization
of TERM-050: regimes sustaining compatible courses through their own
interactions, without recurrent arbitration, whether or not they rank the
same course first. CLAIM-358 (the owner's U45, Proposed) is stated in it
and rests on CLAIM-261: learned local accommodation can cost less over
repeated encounters than escalating each instance. CASE-109 (the owner's)
illustrates it, a variant of CASE-013 and CASE-051. CLAIM-358 bears on
QUESTION-054, whether there is a governing level among regimes at all.
QUESTION-365 asks for a third settlement, a demand taken up by another
organization; it names CLAIM-358 and CLAIM-484.

**Non-domination.** CLAIM-362 (the owner's U45; authenticity first;
Proposed) complements CLAIM-358: non-domination of subcomponents is a
candidate constituent of authenticity. CLAIM-364 (A45's counter;
authenticity first; Proposed) replies that processes conditioned never to
object coordinate at the same behaviour and cost, so the two part only
under intervention, dissent or history. TERM-061 records the consequence
for itself: conditioned conformity "can count as practical
synchronization" and fail CLAIM-362's condition.

**Effortlessness and affect.** CLAIM-541 (the owner's U17; Proposed)
complements CLAIM-162 and CLAIM-358: reported effortlessness has three
sources, no conflict to override, conflict settled by learned
accommodation, and conflict met by small, timed control. CLAIM-441 (the
assistant's; organizational-affect first; Proposed) rests on CLAIM-162 and
objects to CLAIM-440 of that line: flow's positivity sits badly with
valence undefined where nothing is contested. CASE-051, the absorbed
writer, illustrates CLAIM-162 and CLAIM-261.

**Switching or continuous reorganization.** QUESTION-079 asks it, and
CLAIM-262 (the owner's answer, Proposed) answers that it depends on the
agent's constitution, with CLAIM-163's transition test stated per
constitution. CASE-075 and CASE-074 (Twitch Plays Pokémon in anarchy and
democracy mode) illustrate it; CLAIM-453 (the owner's hypothesis,
demonstrated, Active; distributed-agency first) corroborates it with the
conditions under which a two-interpretation population decides by drift
diffusion. CLAIM-616 (the owner's U4; distributed-agency first; Proposed)
proposes synchronization phenomena as a laboratory for the record's
distinctions; it has no front-matter relation to any component here.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim.

- **Practical synchronization as integration.** That TERM-061's practical
  synchronization is the motivational integration of the frustration part
  (CLAIM-534) realized without arbitration. Already stated against: TERM-061
  counts conditioned conformity as practical synchronization, and CLAIM-534
  excludes suppression.
- **CLAIM-541 against CLAIM-441.** That flow's effortlessness may have
  CLAIM-541's second source, so contested pairs remain. Already stated:
  it is CLAIM-441's own second horn, "flow keeps contested pairs its
  processes settle among themselves".
