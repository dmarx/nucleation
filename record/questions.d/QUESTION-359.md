# QUESTION-359: Is the revision closure of Aevyra's The Ledger of Will, Proposition 4, the Moore–Nerode minimal-machine construction applied to the expanded target family {T_d ∘ J_u}, under the same hypotheses, as its authors say?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-884](../theory.d/THEORY-884.md) states Proposition 4's criterion and bound, and the record has
proved both. The authors call the result "a finite-machine refinement
construction in the tradition of Moore and Nerode" ([LIT-1376](../literature.d/LIT-1376.md), §4.4), and
[THEORY-884](../theory.d/THEORY-884.md)'s body repeats that. No classical source is read here.

## What would answer it

A reading of Moore (1956) or Nerode (1958), or of a standard automata
text, that identifies Proposition 4 as the minimal-machine construction
(Myhill–Nerode, Moore partition refinement) applied to the target family
{T_d ∘ J_u}, with its hypotheses: a finite state set closed under total
deterministic revisions, and a state that includes any law needed to make
revision Markovian. A classical statement that needs stronger hypotheses
than these would answer it in part.
