# TERM-005: frame, as an interpretive lens on the original

## Definition

A11: "**What fundamentally distinguishes translations is the interpretive frame
through which the original is reconstructed.** The important word here is
*reconstructed*." The translator is "making decisions about **what kind of thing
the original fundamentally is**". The frame "determines which properties of the
original are treated as worth preserving".

## Why it was replaced

It moved the frame from the rendering's delivery to the translator's reading of
the source, and said so only by emphasis. U7: "reconstructed sure, but
importantly also how it's *delivered*". A12 conceded: "your correction
identifies something important that my formulation missed. I was treating the
frame primarily as an **interpretive lens applied to the original**".
