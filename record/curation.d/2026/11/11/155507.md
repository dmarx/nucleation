The second by-question reading review (ADR-091) flagged QUESTION-297,
QUESTION-299 and QUESTION-301, and in part QUESTION-298 and QUESTION-302:
their evidence was some seventy works read into NOTEs on 2026-10-29 and
2026-10-30 with no reading filed, so nothing the argument could weigh. At
the owner's word to proceed, this pass files them under ADR-068.

## The list

NOTE-1246 to NOTE-1322: 71 NOTEs whose LIT no THEORY names in `source`.
Each reader grepped `record/theory.d/` for its LITs before filing; none
had a reading, so none was skipped. Three more were added to the legacy
part: NOTE-644 (Abramsky and Sadrzadeh, LIT-841), which QUESTION-026
names and no reading covered; and the two passages QUESTION-366 quotes,
LIT-1902 p. 1366 (NOTE-1484) and LIT-1922 proposition 4 (NOTE-1483),
filed because both NOTEs hold them.

Four readers, by subject, each in its own worktree; the filer combined
them without conflict, since every file is new.

Every reading is Proposed, with an attributed title stating what the
authors report or argue and a `promote_when` that asks only that a
reading in full find the work saying that (ADR-065). Each was written from
its NOTE, without fetching the work again; its history says so. All ties
are from the reading's side and rest on the question's or claim's own
words naming the work or its NOTE, quoted in the reading's last section.
`bears_on` throughout, not `answers`: the titles report, they do not
assert. No existing entry changed.

What the questions now have: QUESTION-297 fourteen readings, QUESTION-299
eighteen, QUESTION-300 twenty, QUESTION-301 fourteen, QUESTION-302 four,
QUESTION-298 one. The flags of the second by-question pass on 297, 299
and 301 are met.

Two readings carry no tie: Smith and Mackie's intergroup emotions theory
(NOTE-1285), cited only by CLAIM-492 as a candidate criterion, not as
evidence for it; and Botzung et al. (NOTE-1313), cited only by CASE-098,
which a reading cannot reach from its own side.

## Left for the owner or a status review

- **Promotion.** Each NOTE is a reading in full (NOTE-1312 skimmed; a few
  read from a working paper or another version, which the summaries say),
  so most `promote_when` are likely met now. A status review can move them.
- **Stale NOTE lines.** Most of these NOTEs say "No THEORY filed", some
  with a reason (a framework, thin evidence, a normative model). The
  attributed titles meet those reasons; the lines were left as written.
  NOTE-1260 and NOTE-1261 point at "NOTE-YYYY" placeholders, and NOTE-1259
  cites itself as Wade-Benzoni et al.
- **Grounds on a LIT that now has a reading.** CLAIM-491 grounds on
  LIT-1735 and LIT-1703, CLAIM-494 on LIT-1729, CLAIM-044 on LIT-841.
  Moving those grounds to the readings is the claims' side.
- **CLAIM-491 and Eyerman, Volkan.** The claim's note says they "support
  its shape and move its boundary"; the NOTEs call the support inference
  and Volkan's "narrower". Not tied: a contested support wants an ARG.
- **QUESTION-300 has twenty readings.** The weakest ties are Reimer et
  al. (NOTE-1251), Rubin and Hewstone (NOTE-1278), Akerlof and Kranton
  (NOTE-1311) and Scheepers and Ellemers (NOTE-1315).
- **Horsthemke on QUESTION-297** may be an offered part-answer (ADR-060),
  since the question calls his argument "the second answer it allows".
- **The gluing obstruction.** NOTE-644's summary, LIT-841 and QUESTION-026
  say inconsistency is the only obstruction to gluing. The NOTE's own key
  results give a non-linguistic example that fails without it; the new
  reading's title says "in their linguistic examples".
- **Ord and MacAskill.** QUESTION-299's note groups the significance ×
  persistence × contingency accounting with Ord's trajectory chapter; it
  is MacAskill, Thomas and Vallinder's. The readings attribute it so.
- **Duplicate readings of LIT-1922**: THEORY-1276 with THEORY-1534, and
  THEORY-1277 with THEORY-1535.
- **A count in the record disagrees.** NOTE-1274 says Batson et al. 1997
  ran "a pair of experiments"; elsewhere the record says four.
