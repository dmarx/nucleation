# LIT-1312: Learning from the Outsider Within: The Sociological Significance of Black Feminist Thought

Collins, P. H. (1986). Learning from the Outsider Within: The Sociological Significance of Black Feminist Thought. *Social Problems* 33(6): S14–S32. doi:10.1525/sp.1986.33.6.03a00020 (also doi:10.2307/800672).

Read in [NOTE-1042](../notes.d/NOTE-1042.md), in an authorized Portuguese translation.

## Why this is here

A24: "a person who participates in an institution yet experiences it from a marginalized position can sometimes perceive features of its organization that its more privileged members overlook." Bears on [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md) and [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md): what such a participant sees reaches the institution only if it is taken up.
