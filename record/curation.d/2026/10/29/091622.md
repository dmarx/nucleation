exchange "Paper assessment" (curation entry of 07:32), "as you're able". The

**What the readings changed in the record's own entries.**
- CASE-tmph0fxi was corrected twice. Neither PETra nor VakyArth supplies
  its items:
  - PETra has one English→X route per item and reports no annotator
    agreement.
  - VakyArth's translation and inference items barely overlap.

  Its criterion must be a panel mean, since single judges of pragmatic
  similarity agree at 0.45 (Ward and Marco's first session) and of
  politeness at about 0.46 (TyDiP). Its correspondence arm now carries the
  identification results of THEORY-tmpa43ta, THEORY-tmpvn82m and
  THEORY-tmp7cnkm, the composition limit of THEORY-tmp9mvg2, and a POT
  version requirement.
- CLAIM-tmpgwud2 is supported from three directions:
  - CycleGAN's hidden channel is the converse of its collapse case:
    routes agree because everything travels, out of the receiver's sight
    (THEORY-tmpocg1h).
  - xCOMET's score and spans agree with each other far more than with
    experts (THEORY-tmpa9y98).
  - Goguen and Burstall's Satisfaction Condition admits a translation that
    collapses every model, and only surjectivity on models rules it out
    (THEORY-tmpkmtoo).
- QUESTION-282 gains a datum: neural sheaf diffusion's separating sheaves
  are built from the labels, so the "right" correspondence is fixed by the
  task (THEORY-tmpfzykx).
- Nicholson's reading corrected LIT-tmptd6hn's "Why this is here". Weiss
  defines a system by the whole's persistence while its parts vary; the
  mutually producing triplet is Nicholson's further claim, and stronger
  than TERM-070's closure.

**Corrections to the exchange and to the LITs, found by reading.**
- Søgaard et al. test the adversarial MUSE method, not GW.
- POT's 2021 paper has neither fused GW nor co-optimal transport.
- PETra has about 3,000 pairs, not 2,900.
- Hashimoto et al.'s first page gives July 2016, so `published:` is
  2016-07-01.
- COTA's do-calculus term is constant wherever its transport constraints
  hold (THEORY-tmp1lvzk), so it does not do what the exchange said.
- Zeng et al.'s benchmarks are consistent by construction, so they cannot
  show the cost of imposing consistency that the exchange looked for.

**Errors found in the papers** are in each NOTE. The ones that touch
results:
- the convergence step of Zeng et al.'s Proposition 3;
- Proposition 13 of neural sheaf diffusion, whose construction also exists
  at d = 8;
- COOT's claim of strict positivity for unequal sizes;
- the non-submodular threshold cost of structured OT;
- the conformal factor in the hyperbolic matching paper.

**Flags.** Fifteen of the LITs carry `anthology-candidate`, last in their
tags: those that propose or benchmark ML methods.
