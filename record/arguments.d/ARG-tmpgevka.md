# ARG-tmpgevka: The invented renderings of one sentence by four speakers show that participant roles partly constitute the act

## The inference

[CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) grounds on [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md)'s sentence, "You blow it all on sex and
booze", said by a friend, a parent, a judge and a comedian. The act changes
as the speaker's relation to the hearer changes, so those relations are
constituents of the act.

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes. A
  rival that individuates acts by the utterance token plus the speaker's
  states would describe the four speakers the same way, because the
  readings were assigned, not measured.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No. [CLAIM-229](../claims.d/CLAIM-229.md):
  [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md)'s defeat condition "is met if a rival account 'would predict
  the [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md) variation as well'. The [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md) variation is A12's, A18's
  and A24's description of four imagined speakers. It is not an
  observation, so there is nothing yet for a rival to predict."
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** No. This is the question
  that fails. [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md): "Invented, so it shows a possibility". The thesis
  needs more than the possibility.

Rejected as evidence for [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md). [CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md)'s version 2 (2026-10-10)
took the point: its defeat condition was restated "on cases with intention
held fixed", and no longer asks a rival to predict [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md). Its grounds
now also hold [CASE-069](../cases.d/CASE-069.md), Labov's recorded sounding sessions, which is
documented rather than invented.
