## Why this is here

A24 of the exchange recommended Pradeu's "immunological approach to biological individuality", so as to "avoid making membrane self-production appear uniquely privileged", backed by two citations the share hides. The identification of this book is the record's, as the standard statement of the view; it is a likely referent, not a confirmed one. The record holds it only by report: [NOTE-114](../notes.d/NOTE-114.md) (the SEP entry on biological individuals, [LIT-168](LIT-168.md)) gives "Pradeu's immunological criterion (2012: 244)".
