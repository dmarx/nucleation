# THEORY-tmpl7lm5: Lanier holds that cybernetic totalism, the application of computer-science metaphors to people and reality, spreads through software design and grows the digital hive at the expense of individuality, and that information is alienated experience, which means nothing without a person to interpret it

## The account

Lanier: cybernetic totalism "applies metaphors from certain strains of computer science to people and the rest of reality"; "The digital hive is growing at the expense of individuality"; "Since implementation speaks louder than words, ideas can be spread in the designs of software"; "Information is alienated experience."
