# SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa: Answerability and contestability: answerability is proposed as a directed relation, owed on several grounds to a party with a claim and not to everyone affected, with forms and dimensions that come apart; authority over a decision is proposed not to carry authority over the conditions of its challenge; explanation, contestation and interruption, and the six achievements of an effective objection, are proposed as separable; and contestability is proposed as a maintained capability robust to opposition

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line, on answerability and contestability: when an answer is owed and to whom, who may set the terms of challenge, and what an effective challenge needs. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and every component is new to any account. The held claims these relate to are [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) (A's second-order duty of answerability; Proposed) and [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) (A's rule that accountability rises with indispensability; Proposed). The account cites them and does not integrate them. The part's sibling, on remedies foreclosed before review, is [SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm](SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm.md).*

## The account

**The question.** [QUESTION-480](../questions.d/QUESTION-480.md) (the owner's, U36, verbatim; Open;
lines dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity) asks when
answerability is required of an authority or governing capability, and
keeps three parts apart: when, to whom, and why. Its body says [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md),
[CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) and [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) hold pieces and "None asks when any actor owes an
answer at all", names [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) and [TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md) as A83's answer and
[CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md) as its principle, and carries A83's closing problem as a
sub-question: the minimum conditions under which a claim of answerability
stays effective without giving the claimant unlimited authority. It calls
the second side of that problem "the limit [CLAIM-749](../claims.d/CLAIM-749.md) files", and
leaves the why open.

**The term.** [TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md) (A's, A83 §1 and A106 §17; Proposed):
answerability, of an actor to a party concerning an action or governing
power under a declared standard, is the relation in which the actor owes
the party some form of answer (an account, reasons, responsiveness, an
effective opportunity to contest, or judgment and redress), in one of four
dimensions (normative, institutional, effective, maintained), and not the
accountability mechanisms that realize it. Its body says it gives one sense
to the word as [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) and [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) use it; that "when it is
owed, and to whom, is [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md)"; that [CLAIM-749](../claims.d/CLAIM-749.md) bounds it; that
A74's "dynamic answerability" ([CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md)) is its effective and
maintained dimensions without the normative one; and that protected
contestability ([TERM-130](../terms.d/TERM-130.md)) is "one form of answer, contestation, made a
maintained capability".

**When an answer is owed, and to whom.** [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) (A's, A83 §§3–5
and §10, A90 IV; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity) answers
[QUESTION-480](../questions.d/QUESTION-480.md), uses [TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md) and [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md), and complements
[CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md), [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md) and [CLAIM-749](../claims.d/CLAIM-749.md): answerability is
owed to a party with a claim concerning the matter, on one of several
grounds that need not presuppose the actor's legitimate authority
(interference with a protected interest, entrusted authority, collective
rule, consequential dependence, control over recourse); the party owed, the
recipient of reasons, the forum and the remedier can differ; and what is
owed is indexed to the claim, not to how much the party is affected. Its
body calls [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) the dependence ground "with an intensity", [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)
downstream of it, [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md) what the fifth ground "generates", and
says its fourth part "depends on" [CLAIM-749](../claims.d/CLAIM-749.md). It leaves open "why
anyone is owed reasons at all".

**The limit.** [CLAIM-749](../claims.d/CLAIM-749.md) (A's, A106 §10 and §17; counter,
interpretive, Proposed) objects to [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md): influence over others does
not by itself ground a duty to answer to them, since a legitimate right can
assign a decision to its holder. Its body says [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) survives if read
as stated, about arrangements others depend on, and "the counter then marks
where its slope starts"; that against [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) it "has less purchase",
since a protected sphere has no standard to answer to; and that it and
[CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md)'s limit "are the same point in different settings".

**Who sets the terms of challenge.** [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md) (A's, A83 §3E, §7 and
close; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md),
[CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) and [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md), bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md),
[QUESTION-482](../questions.d/QUESTION-482.md) and [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md), and uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md): authority over a
decision does not carry authority over the cost, timing, evidence, forum or
remedy of its challenge, and where an actor's authority is meant to be
constrained by independently justified claims, its own judgment that it has
met them cannot always establish that it has. Its body says [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s
duty "presupposes that someone may set the terms of contest" and this
claim says "the decider is not that someone by default"; that [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) is
"one way of exercising the second authority without justification"; and
that how independent the evaluator must be is [QUESTION-482](../questions.d/QUESTION-482.md).
[QUESTION-482](../questions.d/QUESTION-482.md) (A's, A79 §3, with A81 §5's authority-provenance graph;
Open; lines dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency) asks when the
allocation of authority to evaluate and constrain an actor is sufficiently
independent of that actor's capacity to influence it. Its body says the
record holds the mechanisms ([CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md), [CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md), [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md), [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md),
[CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md)) "and not the criterion", and calls itself "the institutional
side of [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md)".

**Three entitlements.** [CLAIM-740](../claims.d/CLAIM-740.md) (A's, A23 §6 and A19 §7, developing
the owner's U11; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) and
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md): explanation, contestation and interruption are separate
entitlements of an affected party, and each without the next fails in its
own way. Its body says [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s achievements are the organization's and
these the affected party's; that its split between giving an account and
answering "is what" [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty "requires of contestation"; and that
interruption is the owner's U11 ([CLAIM-710](../claims.d/CLAIM-710.md), in [SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm](SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm.md)) "in the form of an entitlement". It names
[TERM-130](../terms.d/TERM-130.md), [TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md) and [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) as from the same exchange.

**Six achievements.** [CLAIM-732](../claims.d/CLAIM-732.md) (A's, A79 §10, A81 §2 and §9; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; lines dependence-and-domination and
will-organization) rests on [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), complements [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md), [CLAIM-632](../claims.d/CLAIM-632.md) and
[CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md), uses [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md) and [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md), and bears on
[QUESTION-480](../questions.d/QUESTION-480.md): an objection is effective only if it becomes
formulable, legible, taken up by an authorized channel, sustainable by the
objector, corrected and maintained, so its chance is no greater than its
weakest stage, and a reform that raises one stage can lower the whole (A81
§9's numbers, checked). Its body says it extends [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s three
achievements upstream and downstream, that its bound is [CLAIM-632](../claims.d/CLAIM-632.md)'s upper
bound, that A's "Adversarial control over contestability" is [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)
restricted to contest, measured by [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md), and that the
institutional property built on it is [TERM-130](../terms.d/TERM-130.md).

**Protected contestability.** [TERM-130](../terms.d/TERM-130.md) (A's, A81 §11 and §14;
Proposed) complements [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): the maintained, resource-bounded capability
of those with standing to formulate and present objections and obtain an
appropriately consequential assessment of them, robust to a declared class
of opposition; not a guarantee of success, a veto or costless
adjudication. Its body defines it as a viability kernel over an adequacy
region built from [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md)'s robust capability, and says it is not
correctability, not [CLAIM-732](../claims.d/CLAIM-732.md)'s objection at one time, and not
[TERM-126](../terms.d/TERM-126.md)'s relation of owing an answer.
