# NOTE-2402: Müller 2026, skimmed for a synergetic hypothesis of social interaction in which order parameters across neural, physiological and behavioral layers constrain the individuals who produce them

## Contribution

A framework that carries Haken's order parameters and circular causality
into multilayer network models of social interaction, with stated
predictions. The reading is [THEORY-2288](../theory.d/THEORY-2288.md).

## Key results

1. **The hypothesis** (abstract): interpersonal coordination as "a
   self-organizing process in multilayer networks, where circular
   causation across levels gives rise to emergent order parameters";
   examples provide "preliminary support for the framework".
2. **Enslavement** (§ on the enslaving principle): order parameters,
   once established, enslave, "or constrain the behavior of the
   components that generated them", quoting Haken's puppeteer image and
   his gloss that "enslavement and consensus finding are two sides of the
   same coin".
3. **A prediction on coupling** (abstract): "stronger and more
   symmetrical coupling across neural, autonomic, and/or behavioral layers
   will produce more robust and persistent alignment"; but Table 1,
   Prediction 5: "Beyond an optimal range, excessively strong coupling may
   destabilize coordinated states."
4. **The test of circularity** (Table 1, Prediction 6): "Macroscopic
   coordination patterns should exert top-down constraints on individual
   dynamics while simultaneously emerging from bottom-up interactions",
   observable "as bidirectional influences between system-level order
   parameters and local interaction dynamics".
5. **Time scales** (§ on cross-frequency coupling): cross-frequency
   coupling "can link local and global processes operating on distinct
   time scales"; separation of time scales is not made the mechanism of
   the order parameters.

## Assumptions

That inter-brain and inter-body synchrony measures are order parameters
in Haken's sense; that the multilayer network is the right state space.

## Concepts

Order parameter (dynamical and structural); enslaving principle; circular
causation; multilayer network; within- and cross-frequency coupling;
hyper-brain network.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-526](../claims.d/CLAIM-526.md)** (note only). Prediction 6 is a test of the claim's
  question, whether a collective variable has dynamics of its own closed
  against its realization; but "bidirectional influences" between an
  order parameter and the signals it is computed from would not settle it,
  for the reason [CLAIM-334](../claims.d/CLAIM-334.md) gives for macro-to-micro Granger causation.
- **[CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)** (note only). The paper's social order parameters
  constrain individuals only if read and acted on, which is the claim's
  condition; the paper does not separate the cases.
- **[CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md)** (note only). The framework does not use rate separation;
  it takes synergetics' order parameters as given.
- **Schmietendorf 2026** ([NOTE-2394](NOTE-2394.md)) is the same journal's caution
  about reading enslavement causally; this paper reads it causally.

## Limitations

- Support is illustrative; the predictions are stated, not tested here.
- Key result 3 shows a gap in the paper's own argument: the abstract's
  monotone prediction (stronger coupling, more robust alignment) and
  Prediction 5 (excessive coupling destabilizes) need the "optimal range"
  stated in the first, which the abstract leaves out.
- Skimmed; the formalism and examples were not checked.
