# CLAIM-tmp2kfwd: Within an organization, being able to formulate a question, having standing to pursue it, having the resources to pursue it and being able to make its findings consequential are four separate achievements, and the entitlements that secure the last three make up an operational epistemic constitution

## The claim

A18 §4 distinguishes "Can formulate q", "Has standing to pursue q", "Has
resources to pursue q" and "Can make its findings consequential": "These
are separate organizational achievements. A process may possess a valuable
question while lacking any effective route through which that question can
acquire investigative resources."

The entitlements, each with its epistemic function: to submit a query
("Introduce a discrepancy into the inquiry"), reserve investigative
resources ("Prevent inquiry from being indefinitely deferred"), request
evidence, challenge an answer ("Initiate reevaluation"), interrupt
execution ("Preserve the opportunity to investigate before an irreversible
action"), escalate a discrepancy, challenge an acceptance criterion
("Initiate reflexive inquiry") and preserve a competing model. "These
arrangements constitute an operational epistemic constitution. Not every
process needs every entitlement."

## Where the record stands

- Formulating is [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)'s frontier and [TERM-095](../terms.d/TERM-095.md)'s inquiry; standing to
  pursue is [CLAIM-996](CLAIM-996.md)'s authorization, the questions authorized being
  a subset of those askable; resources are [CLAIM-1016](CLAIM-1016.md)'s budget, which
  authorization shares; consequentiality is the volitional efficacy of
  [CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md).
- What is new is that standing and resources are separated, as
  [TERM-tmpgiy6r](../terms.d/TERM-tmpgiy6r.md) separates operational standing from effective authority:
  a process may be entitled to pursue a question and be starved of the
  means, or hold the means without the entitlement. The interruption
  entitlement is [CLAIM-723](CLAIM-723.md)'s opportunity for intervention, turned inward
  to an organization's own inquiry.

## What it does not say

It does not say which process should hold which entitlement; A18: "The
scope of standing depends on its role, dependencies, competencies and the
commitments maintained by the encompassing organization."
