# THEORY-tmpwkuw5: A single holistic power of the universe's configuration, carried by bare matter points, cannot ground a subsystem's effective wave function without changing the ontology, because the masses in a decoupled subsystem's Hamiltonian must belong to that subsystem's particles; dispositions intrinsic to particles or events need no such change

## Source

Dorato (2015), [LIT-176](../literature.d/LIT-176.md), §5 (pp. 13–15), read in full in [NOTE-105](../notes.d/NOTE-105.md).

## What was actually shown

**The target.** Esfeld et al. (2013, 2015): bare matter points with no
intrinsic properties, individuated by metrical relations, and ψ as a
law-grounding power of the configuration of all particles in the universe.

**The argument (§5).**
- (a) A holistic power carried by relata with no intrinsic power is of
  unclear intelligibility. The fix is to give each particle an intrinsic
  power that helps give rise to the holistic one (p. 13). This is posed as
  questions, "not ... a definite rejection" ([NOTE-105](../notes.d/NOTE-105.md), C5, weak).
- (c) When a subsystem is effectively decoupled, the masses in its
  Hamiltonian must be intrinsic dispositions of its particles. So Esfeld et
  al. "need to alter the ontology" in passing from the universal to the
  effective wave function, while intrinsic event-dispositions need no
  change (pp. 14–15; C6, moderate).
- (b) For Bohmian spin, the analogue of fragility is "the disposition to
  have a definite spin in a given direction" in a measurement context
  (pp. 13–14; C8, weak).

**Independent support.** Gao ([LIT-121](../literature.d/LIT-121.md), ch. 3, fn. 5 p. 30) makes a parallel
objection: the effective ψ of a factorised subsystem does not encode the
influence of other particles ([NOTE-105](../notes.d/NOTE-105.md), [NOTE-140](../notes.d/NOTE-140.md)).

**What could have come out otherwise.** The effective wave function could
have been recoverable from the universal holistic power alone, with no
property of the subsystem's particles. Dorato argues it is not, because of
the masses.

## What this does not say

- **That events are needed.** The paper shows that an event ontology fits
  GRWf, GRWm, Bohmian mechanics and the ψ-antirealist views of Bohr,
  Rovelli, Haag and Pashby (§§3–4). Something every interpretation can host
  constrains none of them ([NOTE-105](../notes.d/NOTE-105.md), Limitations; C1, C3).
- **That events differ from matter points.** Dorato concedes the two views
  would differ only verbally if events lacked intrinsic powers (p. 8, C4).
  How events relate to powers is deferred (p. 15).
- **That holistic dispositionalism is refuted.** Nothing is proved against
  it; the effective-wave-function argument is the one step of moderate
  strength.
- **That intrinsic dispositions handle entanglement.** Whether they can
  reproduce non-factorisable dynamics without a holistic power is open.
  Belot's particle-in-a-box case ([THEORY-tmpt3zy6](THEORY-tmpt3zy6.md)) is the test Dorato does
  not address.
