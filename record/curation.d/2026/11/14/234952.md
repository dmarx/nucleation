On 2026-11-14, after the filing of Sun et al. (2026,
[LIT-2546](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2546.md)), the owner asked whether "we could construct an
alternate CASE for [QUESTION-281](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-281.md) in Conway's Game Of Life". The case filed
from Sun et al.'s model, [CASE-258](../../record/cases.d/CASE-258.md), found that model's fairy circles to be made
by a contraction imposed by hand and worn away by grass. That model
therefore did not show a form whose making and keeping were separate.
Life was proposed instead as a system where each step is exact.

## What was filed

- **[CASE-260](../../record/cases.d/CASE-260.md)** (Proposed, construction, standing documented): the

## How it was built

A Life simulator (B3/S23 on a torus large enough not to wrap during the
runs used) was written in the session's scratchpad. The constructions
came from computation, not memory:

- **A search over two-glider collisions.** 1,076 relative offsets and
  phases of a down-right and a down-left glider, kept only where the
  gliders started at least three cells apart. It found offsets that end
  in a single block, offsets where an isolated block appears and later
  vanishes, and offsets where both gliders annihilate. The collision used
  does both in one run, and its mechanism was traced cell by cell.
- **Gosper's gun.** Entered from its standard 36-cell form and checked:
  period 30, steady glider output. Each block was then removed in turn,
  and the disturbance of each block over a cycle was traced.
- **A glider strike on a block.** Offsets were searched until one left an
  empty lattice.

The code is not committed, since this record holds none. The case gives
every starting configuration, so any Life program can check it.

## What was left, and why

- **Eaters.** An eater survives a glider strike by repairing itself,
  which would give a third mechanism of stabilization: robustness to
  perturbation, as opposed to a fixed point or repair by a larger
  pattern. It was not built. The case already has a fixed point and a
  repaired form, and an eater is a further variant if the owner wants it.
- **Whether the gun's loop is closure in [TERM-070](../../record/terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s sense.** It is raised
  in the case and not decided.
- **No CLAIM.** The case shows that the three achievements come apart in
  one formal system. Turning that into a general claim, for example that
  a constitutive role can change how a form is stabilized, is the
  owner's call.

## 2026-11-15: the eater and the general claim

The owner asked for the eater to be built and filed as a case, and for
the general claim to be filed.

- **[CASE-259](../../record/cases.d/CASE-259.md)** (Proposed, construction, `variant_of` [CASE-260](../../record/cases.d/CASE-260.md)).
  Eater 1 was checked to be a still life. A search over glider directions,
  lanes and phases found the one lane in which a strike leaves exactly the
  eater, and the repair was traced cell by cell: the corner cell dies at
  t = 36 and is reborn at t = 37. A block in the same place, and the eater
  in other lanes, were struck for contrast. A search over placements found
  one where the eater absorbs the stream of Gosper's gun. There the eater
  repairs itself every 30 generations, and gun and eater form one bounded
  whole of period 30.
- **[CLAIM-672](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-672.md)** (Proposed, thesis, warrant demonstrated), grounded
  in both cases and `bears_on` [QUESTION-281](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-281.md): how a form is kept in being
  is not fixed by the form, and a constitutive role can change it. Its
  `defeated_if` names a recomputation, a reduction of the mechanisms to
  one, and prior holding by [CLAIM-280](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) or [CLAIM-456](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-456.md). Both were read and do
  not hold it.
- **[ARG-101](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-101.md)** (Proposed, form existence), because the cases ground
  the claim. The critical questions are answered in it, including the
  limit that, in both constructions, the role and the perturbation it
  brings are one fact.
- **The note on [QUESTION-281](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-281.md)** is extended. The question stays open.

Not done: no role was constructed that brings no perturbation, which
would show the limit directly. It is named in the claim as the reason
for "can".
