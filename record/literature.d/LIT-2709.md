# LIT-2709: Care ethics in theory and practice: Joan C. Tronto in conversation with Iris Parra Jounou

Parra Jounou, I. & Tronto, J. C. (2024). Care ethics in theory and practice: Joan C. Tronto in conversation with Iris Parra Jounou. *Contemporary Political Theory*, Critical Exchange. doi:10.1057/s41296-024-00680-6.

## Key takeaways

- "The danger of privileged irresponsibility is to cut off one's reaction
  to others."
- "privileged irresponsibility is a kind of bad care."
- On Caring Democracy's coach image: "Once you climb into it, you pretend
  that you don't know all those people pushing the carriage."

## Standing in the record

Registered as a weak read neighbour of Moral Boundaries ([LIT-2708](LIT-2708.md)) and
Caring Democracy ([LIT-2669](LIT-2669.md)). It confirms that privileged
irresponsibility is Tronto's and runs through both books, and its carriage
image is the nearest any text read comes to the exchange's "socially
organized inattention". It is an image and an interview answer, not an
account, and stands in for neither book's argument.
