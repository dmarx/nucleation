# THEORY-tmp7i21v: In Zaslavsky, Garvin, Kemp, Tishby and Regier's resurvey, Nafaanra colour naming in one village went from three terms in 1978 to seven in 2017, the three older terms now naming smaller categories, and both systems lie near the information-bottleneck efficiency bound

## Source

Noga Zaslavsky, Karee Garvin, Charles Kemp, Naftali Tishby and Terry
Regier (2019), abstract, CogSci 41, [LIT-tmpom3sr](../literature.d/LIT-tmpom3sr.md), read in full in
[NOTE-tmpimci6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpimci6.md). The journal paper, [LIT-tmpntsdw](../literature.d/LIT-tmpntsdw.md), was not reached.

## What was actually shown

As far as the abstract says: Nafaanra data from Banda Ahenkro, Ghana,
collected in 1978 for the World Color Survey (three terms: light/white,
dark/black, red) and again in summer 2017 by Garvin (seven terms). "The
three terms from 1978 are still used but they now name smaller
categories, and there are also new terms for (roughly) yellow, green, blue
and purple." Both systems, analysed as in [LIT-810](../literature.d/LIT-810.md), lie near the
information-bottleneck bound, an outcome the authors call "unlikely
without pressure for efficiency". It could have come out otherwise: the
old terms could have kept their extent with new terms used only as
occasional synonyms, or the later system could have fallen off the bound.

## What this does not say

- **Not how much the old categories shrank, or for how many speakers.**
  The abstract gives no numbers.
- **Not why.** Efficiency is the authors' reading; contact with English is
  left as future work, and Malik-Moraleda et al. ([LIT-tmpyky5z](../literature.d/LIT-tmpyky5z.md)) propose
  it as a rival or added cause.
- **Not that the two surveys are comparable.** The elicitation of each is
  not described.
- **Not a test of value by contrast.** Old categories shrinking when new
  terms arrive in a continuous space is expected on most accounts of
  colour terms; no terms outside the colour field are measured
  ([ARG-tmpcpvnb](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpcpvnb.md)).
- The year and count differ in later sources (2018, nine terms), which
  the journal paper would settle.
