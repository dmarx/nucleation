# TERM-044: agent (at a level of description)

## Definition

Fix a level of description and a target. A factor of a system is an
**agent** there when it passes three tests:
1. **System.** It is one system ([TERM-049](TERM-049.md), [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md)).
2. **Description.** A goal-directed description of it beats a device
   description by more than the Occam cost of the richer model ([LIT-911](../literature.d/LIT-911.md),
   priced as in [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md)).
3. **Adaptation.** Its policy would change if its actions influenced the world
   differently ([LIT-909](../literature.d/LIT-909.md)).

The test is [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md). Agency so defined is relative to the level and
target, and can be wrong given them.

## What it is not

- **Not every control system.** A bare thermostat is a control system and
  fails test 3. With its designers it passes ([CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md)).
- **Not a willed agent.** An agent need not have governance over several
  regimes. A trained policy's training process is an agent on Kenton et
  al.'s account, and need not be willed. That is [TERM-048](TERM-048.md).
- **Not a self** ([TERM-045](TERM-045.md)).
- **Not the will paper's "agent".** The will paper and the authenticity line
  use "agent" for an agent *supposed*, not tested ([CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md): "the account
  asks how a given agent's practical efficacy is organized, not what makes
  something an agent"). On the tiers those agents are willed agents
  ([TERM-048](TERM-048.md)). The older claims are not rewritten. Their sense is
  recorded here. [CLAIM-104](../claims.d/CLAIM-104.md)'s "an encompassing agent" and "agents at one
  level" already make agency relative to a level, as this term does.

## Note of 2026-10-21: rivals proposed, and a sense this term refines

A2 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 proposed this term with [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md) as a
paper, without citing either ([CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md), note of 2026-10-21).

A11, A12 and A14 proposed filing competing definitions of agent beside
this one. One is [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md)'s organizational sense, filed as a rival,
[TERM-tmp3p5ss](TERM-tmp3p5ss.md). The other is "agent, as an adaptive policy mechanism",
which is this term's test 3 taken alone, as [THEORY-801](../theory.d/THEORY-801.md) defines it. This
term refines that sense and does not rival it. [THEORY-583](../theory.d/THEORY-583.md)'s cybernetic
Self falls at the control-system tier, which this term places below
agency ("Not every control system") and which has no TERM of its own
([TERM-048](TERM-048.md), [CLAIM-155](../claims.d/CLAIM-155.md)). A14 proposed a comparison section on homeostats,
trained policies, training processes, organisms and collectives, marking
unestablished verdicts as unresolved. That would be a new version.
