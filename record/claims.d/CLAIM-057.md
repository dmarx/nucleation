# CLAIM-057: Precedence, jurisdiction and suspension can vary independently

## The claim

"An organization can therefore possess a formally absolute safety rule whose
practical significance is weak because another process controls entry into
the domain in which the rule applies."

## What it does not say

That they are independent in every organization; only that they can vary
separately, which is all [ARG-001](../arguments.d/ARG-001.md) shows.
