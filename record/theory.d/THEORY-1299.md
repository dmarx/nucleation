# THEORY-1299: Pure awareness, on Metzinger's account, is the content of a temporally thin model of the organism's own tonic alertness, its capacity to know; experience in this minimal form lacks an egoic self-model, a first-person perspective, time, space and even phenomenal oneness, but not self-modelling, and tonic alertness that is not so modelled is not experienced

## Source

Metzinger (2020), [LIT-1946](../literature.d/LIT-1946.md), §§2–4, pp. 9–38, read in full in
[NOTE-1498](../notes.d/NOTE-1498.md). Metzinger (2024), [LIT-1943](../literature.d/LIT-1943.md), chapters 4, 25 and 34, pp.
36–44, 300–306 and 455–478, read in [NOTE-1499](../notes.d/NOTE-1499.md).

## What was actually shown

**The three levels.** Arousal is "a graded physical property of the human
brain". Tonic alertness is "a graded functional property determining the
capacity for sustained attention". Wakefulness is "a graded phenomenal
property", "a representation of tonic alertness" (2020, pp. 32–33; 2024,
pp. 36–37). Tonic alertness "if it is not represented or internally
modelled, can exist without the conscious experience of wakefulness (as in
some cases of unresponsive wakefulness syndrome)". "Consciousness is knowing
that one is alert" (2020, p. 33; 2024, p. 37).

**The minimal case.** MPE lacks minimal phenomenal selfhood, time and a
spatial frame (2020, p. 2). It is "aperspectival: no epistemic agent model"
(2020, p. 11). It has no centre or periphery, and it "lacks the phenomenal
quality of singularity or numerical identity" (2020, p. 13). Yet it
"definitely has representational content" (2020, p. 38). It is "non-egoic
self-modelling" (2020, p. 36), "an internal model of an epistemic space"
whose epistemic subject "simply is the organism or conscious system as a
whole" (2020, p. 27).

**The 2024 model.** In a nested active-inference sketch, the fourth level
models "the system's global 'epistemic openness'". "It is level 4 that
really endows the content generated on all lower-order levels with
phenomenality". It is "a single, integrated model of its own epistemic
openness in the form of a highest-level but nonegoic self-model". A
full-absorption episode is "inference at the highest level, without any
perceptual inference at the levels below it" (2024, pp. 476–477). The
epistemic agent model, the layer of the self-model that "creates
subjectivity in the sense of a strong first-person perspective", is "what
is completely missing in all full-absorption episodes" (2024, pp. 302,
306).

**The evidence.** Quoted reports in 2020, and in 2024 a survey of meditators
with free-text reports ([THEORY-1295](THEORY-1295.md)). Both texts call the model a
hypothesis: "a hypothetical starting point" (2024, p. 477). The one
dissociation offered independently of contemplative report is unresponsive
wakefulness. There the absence of experience is itself inferred from
behaviour.

**What could have come out otherwise.** Reports of pure awareness could
have lacked wakefulness, which Metzinger finds "by far the most frequently
mentioned kind of phenomenal character" (2020, p. 11). In the survey, the
wakefulness items could have failed to cluster (2024, pp. 465–469). Neither
happened. But both checks run on report, and the reports were collected
under a definition that already named awareness of awareness.

## What this does not say

- **That consciousness needs no self-model.** It needs no *egoic*
  self-model and no epistemic agent model. The minimal case is itself
  described as a model of the system's own capacities, "non-egoic
  self-modelling". Whether that counts as a self-model in Being No One's
  sense is not settled in the texts. In that sense a self-model is one the
  system identifies with, and MPE is said to be able to become a
  "nonegoic unit of identification" (2024, pp. 470–471).
- **That the minimal case is a unified world.** It is neither a world nor
  experienced as one (2020, p. 13; 2024, p. 477). [THEORY-063](THEORY-063.md)'s "Selfless
  Snapshot", the presence of "one unified world", is a different minimal
  case.
- **That the model has been found.** No neural or computational measure of
  a represented tonic alertness is reported. The Triple Triangulation
  Project (2024, pp. 220–221) is a plan.
- **That a machine with such a model would be conscious.** The 2020 paper
  says tonic alertness "could be realized by very different physical
  properties in machines" (p. 32). That is a statement of possibility, and
  no criterion follows from it.
