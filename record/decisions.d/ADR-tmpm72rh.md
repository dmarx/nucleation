# ADR-tmpm72rh: A work atlas is added for the hyperarticle atlas, a series whose articles are works of their own: a claim is a member when the atlas's prose states it or uses it as a step, and its fiber lists are citations

<!-- inactive-ok-file: ADR-tmpenzcn ADR-tmpd3ckn ADR-tmp85hdc CLAIM-tmpcd1e0 CLAIM-tmpqhazs ARG-019 CLAIM-1100 CLAIM-1104 CLAIM-1135 CLAIM-181 CLAIM-646 CLAIM-682 CLAIM-723 — Proposed or Open; the precedent, the atlas's earlier decisions, the question and answers this applies, and members and near misses named whatever their standing -->

## Context

The atlas divides the record into fifteen scopes, each with a
microabstract, an outline and a core fiber ([ADR-tmpd3ckn](ADR-tmpd3ckn.md) made each scope a
line; [ADR-tmp85hdc](ADR-tmp85hdc.md) registered its three entry points' articles as works).
The owner asked whether it should be a synthesis or a manuscript composed of
manuscripts ([QUESTION-tmptcvtu](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmptcvtu.md)). Two answers were filed: a series work now
([CLAIM-tmpcd1e0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcd1e0.md)) and an account over accounts once the scopes' lines have
accounts ([CLAIM-tmpqhazs](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqhazs.md)). The owner then said the registration need not
wait.

## Decision

- **The works vocabulary gains `atlas`**, "Nucleation Hyperarticle Atlas",
  defined on the v0.3 text.
- **It is a series.** Its scopes that are works (from-obstruction-to-
  reorganization, cost-structure-of-possibility, what-survives-translation,
  organization-of-will, organization-of-perspective,
  capability-restraint-relational-freedom, governance-of-possibility, and
  the three of [ADR-tmp85hdc](ADR-tmp85hdc.md)) are its articles and keep their own
  membership; their members are not the atlas's on that account, as
  governance-of-possibility's are not governance-of-becoming's.
- **Its own members follow [ADR-104](ADR-104.md)'s rule**, read against its prose: the
  microabstracts, the outline beats, the "missing from the fiber" and
  "open seam" lines and the section V table. The core fiber lists are bare
  lists of codes and count as citations.
- **Its account waits** on the fifteen lines' accounts ([CLAIM-tmpqhazs](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqhazs.md)).

## Consequences

- 55 members join: 46 the prose cites and states, 9 it states without
  their codes ([CLAIM-646](../claims.d/CLAIM-646.md), 405, 1100, 349, 751, 1173 and 1152, and two filed
  from the same exchange). 17 cited codes are citations only.
- Borderlines the reader flagged are kept as members: [CLAIM-181](../claims.d/CLAIM-181.md) (scope 03),
  [CLAIM-577](../claims.d/CLAIM-577.md) (scope 14, through its effects), [ARG-003](../arguments.d/ARG-003.md) and [ARG-019](../arguments.d/ARG-019.md) (on their
  narrowed conclusions), [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md) (an open seam), and [CLAIM-1100](../claims.d/CLAIM-1100.md) and
  [CLAIM-1152](../claims.d/CLAIM-1152.md) (one phrase each). [CLAIM-023](../claims.d/CLAIM-023.md) and [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) are kept as
  citations.
- The search for claims stated without codes was targeted, not a sweep;
  [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) (scope 12) and [CLAIM-1135](../claims.d/CLAIM-1135.md) or [CLAIM-1104](../claims.d/CLAIM-1104.md) (scope 14) were near
  misses. A later membership pass may add more.
