# NOTE-tmpetk2e: Benjamin (2024) — Imagination: A Manifesto (publisher-licensed excerpt)

## Contribution

A manifesto for imagination as political.

## Key results

1. "Imagination is a field of struggle, not an ephemeral afterthought".
2. A world that relies on inequality "can only afford for a handful of people to imagine themselves" gifted.
3. The book is "a proposal for exorcising our mental and social structures from the tyranny of dominant imaginaries".

## Assumptions

Imagination is collective.

## Concepts

Imagination; dominant imaginaries; rationing of capacity.
