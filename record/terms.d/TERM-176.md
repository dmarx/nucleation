# TERM-176: organizational contract

## Definition

- **Between processes, not necessarily agents.** The owner's U14: "contracts
  can form between processes", and need not carry moral duties
  ([CLAIM-1293](../claims.d/CLAIM-1293.md)).
- **Explicit or implicit.** The owner's U16: "an unspoken understanding that
  evolved through evolution of expectations and assumptions based on
  consistently observed behaviors that evolved into dependencies"
  ([CLAIM-1210](../claims.d/CLAIM-1210.md)).
- **Contract-like only if it handles the abnormal.** [CLAIM-1239](../claims.d/CLAIM-1239.md)'s
  necessary conditions: it maintains reciprocal requirements, responds differently to
  their satisfaction and violation, settles which prevails in conflict, and
  is sustained or revised by some mechanism.

## Use

- Its ordinary course does not reveal it; breach and contention do
  ([CLAIM-1270](../claims.d/CLAIM-1270.md)).
- Where no organizational contract yet fixes what a relation requires, the
  uncertainty is constitutive and is resolved only by forming one
  ([CLAIM-1243](../claims.d/CLAIM-1243.md), [CLAIM-1280](../claims.d/CLAIM-1280.md)).
- Its priority structure is one place operational standing shows,
  though standing is not reducible to priority ([TERM-174](TERM-174.md)).

## Not to be confused with

- A legal contract: an organizational contract may be enforced by nothing
  but the dependencies it has created.
- The contractualist's contract, an idealized agreement about what can be
  justified to each person ([QUESTION-483](../questions.d/QUESTION-483.md)'s note).
