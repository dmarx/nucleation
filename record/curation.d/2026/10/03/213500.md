The owner asked for Susan Wolf's reasons-based account of responsibility,
the Reason View. Neither record held any of her free-will work. This
record named her only for meaning in life ([LIT-183](../../record/literature.d/LIT-183.md), [LIT-186](../../record/literature.d/LIT-186.md)).

Three works are filed, all Deferred:

- "Asymmetrical Freedom" (1980, [LIT-688](../../record/literature.d/LIT-688.md));
- "Sanity and the Metaphysics of Responsibility" (1987/88, [LIT-690](../../record/literature.d/LIT-690.md));
- *Freedom Within Reason* (1990, [LIT-685](../../record/literature.d/LIT-685.md)).

None has a lawful open copy:

- Unpaywall lists all three as closed.
- JSTOR, PDCnet and Cambridge Core are paywalled, and PhilPapers served a
  bot challenge.
- Re-posts on unrelated personal sites, Scribd and Course Hero were left
  unopened.

Each LIT states what the reports say: the SEP's *Compatibilism* §4.3
([LIT-291](../../record/literature.d/LIT-291.md)), Holton's MIT lecture handout, and the book's Crossref chapter
openings. Each claim is labelled as second-hand.

The book is filed as well as the papers for three reasons: [NOTE-270](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-270.md)'s
"Wolf 1990, p. 29" otherwise dangles, the Autonomy, Real Self and Reason
View frame is the book's own, and its chapter 5 defence of ability is
in neither paper.

No relation to Frankfurt ([LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md)) is declared yet. "Sanity" is reported to
target Frankfurt 1971, Watson and Taylor, none of which the record holds,
and both entries are unread. The LIT says to declare `rivals` against
[LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md) if a reading shows Wolf's objection reaches wholeheartedness.

## Fischer & Ravizza, and the theories (owner's follow-up)

The owner then asked for Fischer & Ravizza's reasons-responsiveness, which
is what the phrase usually names. The owner also asked for theories for
both, built from what could be found.

- **Fischer, "Responsiveness and Moral Responsibility" (1987/88,
  [LIT-687](../../record/literature.d/LIT-687.md)), and *Responsibility and Control* (1998, [LIT-684](../../record/literature.d/LIT-684.md)).**
  Both are closed, so both are Deferred.
- **The précis was not filed separately.**
- **Copies not opened:** copies of the chapter and the précis in
  collections kept by other people. Neither collection says it posts them
  with permission.
- **Fischer's "Stories and the Meaning of Life" (2009, [LIT-689](../../record/literature.d/LIT-689.md)).**
  It is open in SUNY's repository and was read in full. Its §I is
  Fischer's own statement of the framework.
- **The SEP *Moral Responsibility* entry ([LIT-691](../../record/literature.d/LIT-691.md))** was read in
  full. It is the record's only read source for JoJo and for guidance
  control.

Three Proposed theories rest on these and on [LIT-291](../../record/literature.d/LIT-291.md). Each says in its
body that the primary texts are unread, and none quotes them:

- the Reason View ([THEORY-122](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-122.md));
- the asymmetry ([THEORY-118](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-118.md)), which extends the Reason View;
- guidance control and semicompatibilism ([THEORY-121](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-121.md)).

The asymmetry and guidance control are rivals on blame. A Frankfurt-style
wrongdoer is blameworthy on Fischer & Ravizza's account and excused on
Wolf's. The Reason View and guidance control are not rivals: the SEP lists
Wolf among the reasons-responsiveness views.

## What would unblock it

A PDF of "Asymmetrical Freedom" (JSTOR, 16 pp.) or of "Sanity" (Schoeman
ch. 3, or the reprint in Watson's *Free Will*). Either would allow a read
NOTE and promote the Wolf theories. For Fischer & Ravizza, the PPR précis
or the book would do the same.
