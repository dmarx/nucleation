# LIT-1243: Scaling of information in turbulence

Granero-Belinchón, C., Roux, S. G. & Garnier, N. B. (2016). Scaling of information in turbulence. *EPL* 115(5): 58003. doi:10.1209/0295-5075/115/58003. Preprint: arXiv:1607.05511 (19 July 2016).

Read in full in [NOTE-970](../notes.d/NOTE-970.md). The record holds two results from it:
[THEORY-750](../theory.d/THEORY-750.md) (the entropy-rate scaling is the second-order structure
function in nats) and [THEORY-748](../theory.d/THEORY-748.md) (entropy rates are blind to the sign
of the cascade, and the sign-conditioned test detects one-point skewness
as readily as skewed increments).

## Why this is here

A3 recommended it with a caution the record keeps: energy transfer and information transfer should not be identified because both can be given a direction across scales. Bears on [QUESTION-219](../questions.d/QUESTION-219.md). The reading supports the caution and
goes further: the paper defines no information transfer at all.
