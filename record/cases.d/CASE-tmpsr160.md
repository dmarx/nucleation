# CASE-tmpsr160: Henley translated perfectly in the voice of an academic sociologist

## The case

A12: "Henley's translation is an especially revealing example because the
criminal slang doesn't merely *describe* an underworld. It creates the
impression that the poem is being spoken **from within that world**. If you
translated every term perfectly while making the speaker sound like an academic
sociologist, you'd have changed something fundamental even if every
proposition survived."

## What it can show

The sufficiency half of [CLAIM-tmphg89g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmphg89g.md): propositions held fixed, footing changed, and the
rendering fails. It is the cleanest form of that argument in the exchange, and
it was not carried into the manuscript, whose §1 states the conclusion without
it. As a thought experiment it relies on the reader's judgement that something
fundamental changed.
