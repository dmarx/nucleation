# CLAIM-1110: That the forager's gains vanish when its energy account is replaced by a matched score does not count against endogenous revision: it shows the learned warnings bound to the condition of the forager's own dissolution, which is what the proposed study's main threat asks for, and the study's second defeater as worded is met by every learner and so cannot tell endogenous criteria from supplied ones

## The claim

**One of two alternative readings; the other is [CLAIM-1141](CLAIM-1141.md). The owner
chooses.**

1. **The defeater as worded cannot discriminate.** [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md) counts it against
   endogenous revision if the gains "vanish when the viability variable is
   replaced by any other variable the designer could have chosen". A learner
   that tracks what it is given will always lose its survival gains when
   given something that omits what kills it; no outcome of the test could
   come out the other way. So meeting it is no evidence.
2. **What the replacement does show.** [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md)'s main threat sets the bar:
   the result does not show the norm is the forager's own "unless the
   viability variable is the condition of the forager's actual dissolution
   and not a score". In the run ([CASE-421](../cases.d/CASE-421.md)) the matched score and the
   energy account agree in training and part only where upkeep raised later
   brings the forager toward dissolution. The learner on the energy account
   survives (35 and 40 of 40 under the two delayed costs), the learner on the
   score does not (0 and 0). The gains are carried by the tie to dissolution,
   which is the bar.
3. **On the record's terms.** [CLAIM-648](CLAIM-648.md) needs a profile set by the system's
   own upkeep; the objection [CLAIM-920](CLAIM-920.md) grants that a profile stabilized
   by the system's own past upkeep, through learning, is a norm on the
   selection-based account. The learner's warnings are of that kind: what it
   came to warn on was fixed by what its own energy account did.

## What it does not say

That the forager's norm is its own in Bickhard's full sense: the modeller
still wrote the dissolution condition and the learning rule, which no
construction removes ([CASE-241](../cases.d/CASE-241.md), "What it cannot show"). Only that the
second defeater, as worded, is not the test of it.
