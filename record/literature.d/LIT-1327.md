# LIT-1327: What Is Ideological Capture and How Do We Measure It? Using Antitrust Reform to Understand Expert–Public Cleavages

Short, N., Hill, S. & Brown, J. (2026). What Is Ideological Capture and How Do We Measure It? Using Antitrust Reform to Understand Expert–Public Cleavages. *Perspectives on Politics* 24(2): 680–700. doi:10.1017/S153759272510323X. First published online 17 November 2025.

Read in [NOTE-1051](../notes.d/NOTE-1051.md), without the online appendix.

## Why this is here

A25 called it "arguably the most directly relevant new reference": capture through the categories of regulatory reasoning, "without any lobbying or improper exchange being necessary." A25: "We should not introduce the claim that capture can operate through the categories of regulatory reasoning as though that were new." A26 §4 put it at the level of control over the evaluative objective. Prior art for [TERM-066](../terms.d/TERM-066.md) and [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md).
