# TERM-048: willed agent

## Definition

A **willed agent**, at a level and for a target, is an agent ([TERM-044](TERM-044.md))
that also passes a fourth test:

4. **Will.** Its behaviour is better described with a will-organization ([TERM-023](TERM-023.md)) than
   without one. That means a maintained organization over *more than one*
   action-generating regime ([TERM-019](TERM-019.md)), deciding which regime acts:
   accessibility, precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension,
   transformation ([CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)).

It is individuated as that organization's **jurisdiction** ([TERM-047](TERM-047.md)):
the regimes whose efficacy it governs.

The tiers nest. Every willed agent is an agent, and every agent is a control
system. The reverse inclusions fail.

## What it is not

- **Not a self.** A willed agent has something like the self-governance unity
  of [ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md): standards that decide among its own regimes. Nothing in the
  definition gives it the subject unity, a point of view. A firm can
  plausibly be willed without being a subject ([CLAIM-155](../claims.d/CLAIM-155.md)).
- **Not a system with an operative will only.** Operative will is whichever
  regime acts now, and every agent has one. A willed agent has governance
  *over* which one acts.
- **Not a thermostat, even with a goal.** One regime gives nothing to govern.
