The owner asked for a reading of arXiv 1401.5980: Kartsaklis, Sadrzadeh,
Pulman & Coecke, *Reasoning about Meaning in Natural Language with Compact
Closed Categories and Frobenius Algebras* ([LIT-tmp26v1l](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp26v1l.md), [NOTE-tmps11ei](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmps11ei.md)).
While it was being read, the owner added the paper it builds on, arXiv
1003.4394: Coecke, Sadrzadeh & Clark, *Mathematical Foundations for a
Compositional Distributional Model of Meaning* ([LIT-tmpunb3l](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpunb3l.md), [NOTE-tmp1umwm](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmp1umwm.md)).
Neither record held any DisCoCat or pregroup work before. The 2014 paper is
related to the 2010 one as `extends`.

**Both are `Active`, as specifications rather than results.**

- **The 2010 paper** states no theorem and runs no experiment. Its sentence
  spaces are 1-d or 2-d truth values in a toy model, and it gives no method
  for building S or the verb tensors from data. It is still where the
  ε/η composition and the single sentence space come from. Its claim that
  the category distinguishes grammatical ambiguities fails, because the
  pregroup is posetal. Its printed similarities are unnormalised inner
  products, under its own cosine definition.
- **The 2014 paper** is the citable categorical statement of the Frobenius
  lift of verbs. Its experiments are the authors' COLING 2012 numbers,
  re-reported. I checked Table 2 against COLING 2012's Table 1 and they are
  identical. The chapter drops the COLING caveat that copy-object's lead is
  "statistically insignificant", and its abstract's "verify the theoretical
  predictions" has no prediction behind it. The best result is ρ = 0.172,
  against a human bound of 0.620.

**Where basis dependence enters.** The 2010 composition is invariant under
an orthogonal change of basis. The Frobenius copying map of 2014 is not: one
random rotation moved a sentence–subject cosine from 0.008 to −0.53. The
basis dependence belongs to the Frobenius extension, not to DisCoCat as
such. That is the point of contact with van Rijsbergen's basis-free
geometry ([LIT-262](../../record/literature.d/LIT-262.md)).

**A status overridden.** The 2014 reader proposed `Superseded`, by its COLING
2012 antecedent and by the later "Frobenius anatomy" papers and Kartsaklis's
thesis. None of those is in either record, and `Superseded` names its
successor. The antecedent also lacks the functorial recasting, which is the
chapter's stated contribution. So the status is `Active`, with the overlap
stated in the status note. If COLING 2012 or the thesis is filed later, this
should be revisited.

**Tags.** The 2014 paper keeps representation-learning, because its vectors
and verb matrices are built from a corpus. The 2010 paper drops it, because
it learns nothing. Neither takes quantum-foundations: both borrow the
categorical quantum formalism and say nothing about quantum theory.
