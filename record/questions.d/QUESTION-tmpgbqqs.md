# QUESTION-tmpgbqqs: Does a replication or recoding of state urban-renewal enabling legislation remove Collins and Shester's city-level effects of federal urban renewal, or show them driven by a confounder of when states legislated?

## Why it is a question

Collins and Shester ([THEORY-1470](../theory.d/THEORY-1470.md)) identify the effects of urban renewal
from when states enabled it, with a first-stage F of 13.8 and variation
mostly outside the Northeast and Midwest. A replication or recoding that
keeps the city-level effects would answer it in their favour; one that
removes them, or ties them to a confounder of legislative timing, would
count against.
