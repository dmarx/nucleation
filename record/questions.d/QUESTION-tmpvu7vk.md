# QUESTION-tmpvu7vk: Would settling whose a will-organization is settle the agent's accountability for what it does, or does accountability need conditions, such as normative competence and avoidability, that ownership does not supply?

## The question

[QUESTION-011](QUESTION-011.md) asks what makes a will-organization the agent's own. This question asks what an answer to it would buy. If ownership were settled, would accountability follow? Or does accountability need further conditions, such as being able to recognise the right reasons or having been able to act otherwise, which ownership does not give? Filed Deferred, as its line is.

## Why the record needs it

[CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) disclaims ownership: explaining practical efficacy does not settle whose the will is. [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md) tried to supply ownership and was deferred. Both assume that ownership is the missing piece that would make a will-organization something an agent answers for. If accountability comes apart from ownership, two things follow. Reopening the authenticity line would not by itself deliver responsibility. And the disclaimer in [CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) would leave less open than it seems to.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-122](../theory.d/THEORY-122.md): identification is not enough. JoJo "meets the structural conditions" and is excused for lacking normative competence. Limit: second-hand, and the primary texts are unread.
- [THEORY-147](../theory.d/THEORY-147.md): attributability and accountability are "independent kinds" of responsibility. Accountability needs only that the agent acted as she ought not, was in a position to know it, and that it was up to her. Limit: the positive view is Wolf's "hunch".
- [THEORY-118](../theory.d/THEORY-118.md): blame needs the ability "to have acted on the right reasons", and praise does not. So any avoidability condition applies to blame alone. Limit: second-hand, and it gives no answer to Frankfurt cases of wrongdoing.
- [THEORY-119](../theory.d/THEORY-119.md): responsibility needs no alternative possibilities when "what made the action unavoidable played no part in bringing it about". Limit: the record has read the dilemma defence and the replies only in survey.

## What would answer it

A case in which an agent's will-organization is plainly her own and she is still not accountable, or the converse, whose verdict survives the dispute between [THEORY-118](../theory.d/THEORY-118.md) and [THEORY-119](../theory.d/THEORY-119.md). Or an argument that ownership already contains the competence and control conditions. A further survey of Frankfurt cases would not answer it.
