# LIT-tmp3zns2: On the Hohenberg-Mermin-Wagner theorem and its limitations

Halperin (2018). Journal of Statistical Physics 175, 521–529. arXiv
1812.00220. DOI 10.1007/s10955-018-2202-y.

## Key takeaways

- The theorem needs a continuous symmetry whose generator is a sum of
  local operators, finite second moments of the interaction, T > 0 and
  equilibrium (§§I–III).
- It "sheds no light on the question of whether quasi-long-range order
  can exist in any particular system" (§II).
- Exceptions to the loose reading "no continuous symmetry breaking in
  2D": orientational order in a 2D crystal; ferromagnetic couplings
  falling slower than 1/r⁴; tethered membranes, where the bending
  constant's renormalization "removes the divergence" (§II).
