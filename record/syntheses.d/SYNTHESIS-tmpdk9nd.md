# SYNTHESIS-tmpdk9nd: Fidelity is relative to receivers and tasks: Blackwell's order on rendering procedures sets the ceiling a rendering puts on report decisions, a bounded receiver's usable information can rise under a garbling but never above that ceiling, the record's directed decision term measures the ceiling and the line's measures each see only part of a rendering, fidelity is a graded profile that only a task's weighting ranks, a receiver's learning history and access make fidelity a different quantity for different receivers, and what survives is fixed by the receivers while what can be shown to survive is the analyst's

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part the mechanical
map of 2026-11-11 put together on fidelity relative to receivers,
Blackwell's order, and what survives translation. The line's account is
built over this one and its seven sibling parts (the kinds of
communicative act, contextuality, the discriminating study, kernels, the
act, frames and chains). The map gave the part 76 members, and the contextuality reader moved
[CLAIM-257](../claims.d/CLAIM-257.md) in at this round. This account integrates 76. [TERM-011](../terms.d/TERM-011.md) (frame, as inferred from evidence) is left to the frames
part: its only stored link here is [CLAIM-111](../claims.d/CLAIM-111.md)'s `uses`, and its text sets it
among the frame terms [TERM-018](../terms.d/TERM-018.md) and [TERM-030](../terms.d/TERM-030.md). Several components carry a second
line ([CLAIM-304](../claims.d/CLAIM-304.md) first in formal-apparatus; [QUESTION-105](../questions.d/QUESTION-105.md) first in
records-of-inquiry; [QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md) first in distributed-agency); they are
integrated here because their `line` includes pragmatic-transport. The
central theses the part answers, the manuscript's thesis [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md) and the
October thesis [CLAIM-139](../claims.d/CLAIM-139.md), are Superseded by [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md) and [CLAIM-204](../claims.d/CLAIM-204.md), which
sit in the study and kind parts.*

## The account

- **The ceiling.** [CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md) (the manuscript's §5, narrowed at the status
  review of 2026-10-28, thesis, interpretive, Active): for report
  fidelity, fidelity is relative to receivers' decisions, and Blackwell's
  order on rendering procedures "makes precise the ceiling a rendering
  sets on those decisions". [CLAIM-225](../claims.d/CLAIM-225.md) (the record's audit, formal and
  structure lenses, counter, demonstrated, Active) showed the order
  "measures what an ideal decision-maker could do, not what a given
  receiver can reconstruct", and that a blind captioner's image is a
  garbling of the text. [CLAIM-199](../claims.d/CLAIM-199.md) (the record's reconciliation, thesis,
  interpretive, Active) keeps both standards: Blackwell's order bounds
  what a rendering makes possible, and a receiver's access is usable
  information, "which a garbling can raise for that receiver but never
  above what the source makes possible"; its XOR example shows the gap.
  [ARG-019](../arguments.d/ARG-019.md) (the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration, formal application, Proposed) carries
  the inference from [CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md) to [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md), with the verdict "a bound,
  not a measure"; [CLAIM-225](../claims.d/CLAIM-225.md) undercuts it. [CLAIM-014](../claims.d/CLAIM-014.md) (A93 §5,
  recovered, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md) to pair Krifka's performative
  updates with Blackwell comparison.
- **What the order needs.** [CLAIM-230](../claims.d/CLAIM-230.md) (the audit, empirical lens,
  counter, demonstrated, Active): the order needs the state each rendering
  is about, which natural texts do not supply, so a decision measure "is
  fixed by whoever labels the state", and it orders procedures, not single
  renderings. [CLAIM-258](../claims.d/CLAIM-258.md) (the audit, counter, interpretive, Proposed): the
  order compares experiments about one parameter, so it does not reach the
  normal translation case, where compensation changes the situation or a
  translator with context knowledge yields a non-garbling. Its worked case
  is [CLAIM-323](../claims.d/CLAIM-323.md) (split from it, demonstrated, Active): a source that tells
  whether Z = 1 and a rendering that tells whether Z = 3 are incomparable.
  [CLAIM-212](../claims.d/CLAIM-212.md) (the record's reply, thesis, interpretive, Proposed) separates
  report from re-performance: in report fidelity the parameter is common
  and the order is defined, and re-performance needs a correspondence of
  situations ([QUESTION-005](../questions.d/QUESTION-005.md)). [CLAIM-461](../claims.d/CLAIM-461.md) (the record's counter, from
  [CLAIM-212](../claims.d/CLAIM-212.md)'s note, interpretive, Proposed) objects that compensation can be
  the more faithful report "for a reader whose code gives the literal word
  a meaning the source lacked". [CASE-063](../cases.d/CASE-063.md) (documented, Active), the Ace
  Attorney localization with "textually accurate" set against
  "emotionally accurate", is the case [CLAIM-212](../claims.d/CLAIM-212.md) and [CLAIM-461](../claims.d/CLAIM-461.md) ground on.
- **Compensation.** [CLAIM-091](../claims.d/CLAIM-091.md) (A24, recovered, interpretive, Proposed): a
  translation can reach pragmatic equivalence by compensating for changed
  social coordinates with changed linguistic ones, "so lexical infidelity
  can be required for pragmatic fidelity". It grounds on [CASE-066](../cases.d/CASE-066.md)
  (documented, Active), Dolly's slip into ты and three English
  translations, whose evidence is the translators' and the author's, not
  readers'. [ARG-013](../arguments.d/ARG-013.md) (the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration, Rejected) rejects the invented
  [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md) as evidence for it.
- **The decision term.** [CLAIM-218](../claims.d/CLAIM-218.md) (the audit, formal lens, granted,
  demonstrated, Active): A86's D_Q is not Blackwell's order; it reduces
  each risk set to one number, needs paired tasks' losses on one scale,
  and its absolute value counts improvement as distortion. [CLAIM-259](../claims.d/CLAIM-259.md) (the
  record's repair, thesis, interpretive, Active): for report fidelity the
  decision term should be the positive part of the risk increase on the
  same problem. Its properties are [CLAIM-326](../claims.d/CLAIM-326.md) (demonstrated, Active, over a
  source whose outcome space is standard Borel) and [CLAIM-380](../claims.d/CLAIM-380.md) (demonstrated,
  Active, finite outcomes): directed, zero from a garbling back to its
  source, vanishing for every problem and prior exactly when the rendering
  is at least as informative, and with D_Q as its symmetrization.
- **A graded profile, ranked by a task.** [CLAIM-106](../claims.d/CLAIM-106.md) (the owner's U20,
  accepted at A50, thesis, interpretive, Active): fidelity is "a graded,
  potentially asymmetric property of transport", and equivalence is its
  zero-distortion limit. [TERM-043](../terms.d/TERM-043.md) (A129 §3 after the owner's U41,
  Active) makes fidelity a profile of directed distortions compared
  component by component, and places [TERM-010](../terms.d/TERM-010.md) (A100's weighted scalar, now
  one task's weighting), [TERM-015](../terms.d/TERM-015.md) (the owner's U20 and A50: graded,
  directed transport distortion), [TERM-033](../terms.d/TERM-033.md) (A35 and A39: the
  zero-distortion limit over response trajectories), [TERM-028](../terms.d/TERM-028.md) (A86 §3:
  inferential sufficiency) and [TERM-034](../terms.d/TERM-034.md) (A108: relative to an interpreter)
  in it. [CLAIM-128](../claims.d/CLAIM-128.md) (A129 §3, thesis, interpretive, Proposed): no single
  ranking of translations holds across tasks, and "the theory of fidelity
  fixes no weighting". [CLAIM-033](../claims.d/CLAIM-033.md) (A75, recovered, Proposed) claims such
  trade-offs are common in published translations.
- **Holistic judgement.** [CLAIM-244](../claims.d/CLAIM-244.md) (the audit, empirical lens, counter,
  interpretive, Proposed): the defeat tests took one holistic, task-free
  judgement as ground truth against the line's own relativity theses.
  [CLAIM-240](../claims.d/CLAIM-240.md) (the record's reply, thesis, interpretive, Proposed): a holistic
  judgement is fidelity under the judges' default task, a weighting fitted
  on some renderings and tested on others. [CASE-067](../cases.d/CASE-067.md) (documented, Active),
  the reception of The Vegetarian, illustrates it, and [CLAIM-458](../claims.d/CLAIM-458.md) (the
  record's counter from [CLAIM-240](../claims.d/CLAIM-240.md)'s note, interpretive, Active) narrows it:
  part of a reception split can be access and not weighting.
- **Receivers' histories.** [CLAIM-118](../claims.d/CLAIM-118.md) (A43 to A45 and the manuscript's §5,
  thesis, interpretive, Active): context and interpreter are separate
  sources of interpretive change, told apart by what each carries.
  [CLAIM-074](../claims.d/CLAIM-074.md) (A44 §4 and A48 §5.5, recovered, Active), resting on it:
  fidelity under a fixed interpreter, across interpreters and across
  states of adaptation are different quantities. [CLAIM-049](../claims.d/CLAIM-049.md) (A110 §22,
  recovered, Active), resting on [CLAIM-074](../claims.d/CLAIM-074.md): learning can make some
  relational invariants more stable and others less.
- **Survival and certification.** [CLAIM-140](../claims.d/CLAIM-140.md) (the record's reading of
  A218, granted, interpretive, Active): the October thesis replaced what a
  receiver can reconstruct with what the analyst's probes can identify, and
  the two come apart in both directions. [CLAIM-211](../claims.d/CLAIM-211.md) (the audit, structure
  lens, granted, demonstrated, Active), resting on it: read as worded, the
  two theses are rivals. [CLAIM-223](../claims.d/CLAIM-223.md) (the record's reply, thesis,
  interpretive, Active), resting on [CLAIM-140](../claims.d/CLAIM-140.md): survival is fixed by what
  receivers can recover and certification by what the analyst's probes can
  identify, so read so the two answer different parts of one question.
  [CLAIM-222](../claims.d/CLAIM-222.md) (the audit, empirical lens, counter, demonstrated, Active): no
  design in the programme includes an interlingual translation.
- **Preserving a distribution over situations.** [CLAIM-111](../claims.d/CLAIM-111.md) (A45 §6,
  recovered, Proposed): a translation can be faithful by preserving the
  text's distribution over communicative situations. [CLAIM-236](../claims.d/CLAIM-236.md) (the
  audit, granted, Rejected) said matching posteriors needs a
  correspondence of alternative sets; [CLAIM-214](../claims.d/CLAIM-214.md) (the record's reply,
  demonstrated, Active) answers that each language's own posterior is
  compared, so a literal rendering failing is the criterion detecting a
  lost implicature, and a target language may admit no passing rendering.
  [QUESTION-448](../questions.d/QUESTION-448.md) (the assistant's, Open) asks where that criterion sits in
  [TERM-043](../terms.d/TERM-043.md)'s profile, since its choice of divergence ranks failing
  renderings.
- **What media and measures see.** [CLAIM-076](../claims.d/CLAIM-076.md) (A110 §21, Proposed):
  cross-modal chains expose reconstruction, and a captioner may add
  relations. [CLAIM-087](../claims.d/CLAIM-087.md) (A97 §6 and A100, Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-225](../claims.d/CLAIM-225.md):
  cross-modal reconstruction often improves a receiver's access, though a
  rendering made without the situation cannot improve what an ideal
  receiver could do. [CASE-009](../cases.d/CASE-009.md) (A97 §6, a proposed study) is the chain
  design, and [CASE-062](../cases.d/CASE-062.md) (a stipulated study on documented halves)
  illustrates [CLAIM-199](../claims.d/CLAIM-199.md) with simplified texts. [CLAIM-256](../claims.d/CLAIM-256.md) (the audit, formal
  lens, granted, demonstrated, Active): D_obs sums over source contexts
  only, so "a rendering that adds a relation pays nothing". [CLAIM-257](../claims.d/CLAIM-257.md) (the
  record's repair, thesis, interpretive, Active; moved into this part from
  contextuality at this round) restates it over the contexts both covers
  can host, with the questions only one side can decide reported apart as
  a change of cover, "so that a rendering that adds a relation pays for
  it".
  [CLAIM-304](../claims.d/CLAIM-304.md) (the record's construction from A14 and A18, demonstrated,
  Active): a rendering can keep a distinction recoverable in principle and
  make it infeasible to recover. [CLAIM-496](../claims.d/CLAIM-496.md) (the record's constructions from
  A4 and A5, demonstrated, Active): agreement between routes is coherence,
  not fidelity, and neither bounds the other; [CLAIM-507](../claims.d/CLAIM-507.md) (the record's
  reading of xCOMET, Proposed) is an instance.
- **The cue.** [CLAIM-298](../claims.d/CLAIM-298.md) (A20's proposal, interpretive, Proposed): a frame
  can change a judgement by changing which stored distinctions are
  reachable, without supplying evidence. It rests on [CLAIM-315](../claims.d/CLAIM-315.md) (split from
  it, empirical, Proposed; also nucleating-argument): the anchoring shift
  is established, the accessibility step inferred.
- **Information of another kind.** [CLAIM-525](../claims.d/CLAIM-525.md) (the record's placing,
  interpretive, Active), resting on [CLAIM-118](../claims.d/CLAIM-118.md) and [CLAIM-257](../claims.d/CLAIM-257.md): the
  Simondonian readings' information is a change in the interpreter or in
  the questions receivers can decide, so Blackwell's order leaves it out
  by construction "and the two accounts are not rivals".
- **Smaller pieces.** [CLAIM-092](../claims.d/CLAIM-092.md) (A86 §2, recovered, Proposed) poses
  translation as a context-indexed information bottleneck; its own note
  of 2026-10-30 finds its defeat condition met by an identity. [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md)
  (A21 and A22, granted, demonstrated, Active; also formal-apparatus): a
  coarse-graining preserves a deterministic judgment exactly when the
  judgment is constant on its fibres. [CASE-005](../cases.d/CASE-005.md) (A45 §5, a proposed study)
  holds the language-model experiments, whose third would test [CLAIM-074](../claims.d/CLAIM-074.md).
