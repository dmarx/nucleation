# LEAD-tmp3ub60: Measure the dancing couple: is the pair a gap-isolated block during the dance and not after it, with direction, compensation and the time-lagged form of the block criterion

## What it would do

State the time-lagged block criterion, then apply it to motion-capture
data of dancing pairs (a reanalysis of Bigand and colleagues is the
candidate), with direction, a perturbation and compensation test, and the
block gap measured during and after the dance.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md): "the couple after the song must come out two."
- [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md): "[CASE-045](../cases.d/CASE-045.md) is the study it would need."
- [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md): "the notebook's coupling needs the time-lagged test
  [QUESTION-116](../questions.d/QUESTION-116.md) asks for"
- [CASE-045](../cases.d/CASE-045.md): "a reanalysis candidate"

## What would count

One block during the dance and two after supports [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) and gives
[QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md) a criterion that returned a verdict that could have been
wrong. A block that persists after the song, or none during it, defeats
[CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) as stated.
