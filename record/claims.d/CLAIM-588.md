# CLAIM-588: Relieving an incompatibility, redistributing an unavoidable mismatch and exploiting a mismatch dynamically are three design targets, which change respectively the compatibility complex, where residuals or loads concentrate, and stability, barriers or transition paths, and the last two can be achieved without the first

## The claim

A, Part 3, as a table: "Relieve incompatibility: Change the feasible set or
compatibility complex; Redistribute unavoidable mismatch: Change where
constraint residuals or loads are concentrated; Exploit mismatch
dynamically: Alter stability, energy barriers, or transition paths." And:
"The last two could occur without achieving the first."

**Relief.** The record has it exactly: relief is strict enlargement of the
compatibility complex for the same declared demands, and dropping or
suppressing a demand is not relief ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)).

**Redistribution without relief.** Demonstrated in [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md). On a graph of
contexts with offsets and tolerances, the feasible allocations depend on
the offsets only through their class in H¹, so local adjustment can move a
mismatch among the overlaps of a cycle and never remove it. The mechanical
form is Rocks and Mehta's ([NOTE-1557](../notes.d/NOTE-1557.md); [THEORY-1414](../theory.d/THEORY-1414.md)). Prestress that
breaks a symmetry leaves the zero modes as they were, while the force that
couples to the mode "no longer points in the same direction, but instead
localizes to the line of prestressed bonds". Where the load sits has
moved, and the count of freedoms has not.

**Exploitation without full relief.** Zang et al. ([NOTE-1569](../notes.d/NOTE-1569.md);
[THEORY-1417](../theory.d/THEORY-1417.md)): prestress in Kresling cells induces frustration that
"allows changing the energy barrier", and "by controlling the level of
prestress in the negative mode, we can switch the instability behavior
from bistable to monostable". It also gives a "programmable
(frustration-dependent) noncommutative state transition". On the record's
reading of the 56 ordered transitions as demands, prestress relieves too:
it opens 8 blocked paths ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md), note of 2026-11-05). The 16 paths
that need intermediate states stay unachievable while the stability and
the order of transitions are retuned. So exploitation went further than
relief.

**What it adds to [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md).** The term is ordinal: frustration is an empty
intersection of acceptance sets, and it is "Not Watson's total
frustration", an energy. On that reading only relief acts on frustration
itself. Redistribution acts on where its residual or load is carried
([CLAIM-365](CLAIM-365.md)'s residual is the record's quantity). Exploitation acts on what
the load does to dynamics. A design that does either has not made the
demands jointly realizable, and should not be reported as if it had.

## What it does not say

- **That the targets exclude one another.** One intervention can do two or
  three; Zang et al.'s prestress does two.
- **That redistributing or exploiting a mismatch is good.** A: "There is no
  general reason to assume such tensions are beneficial." Whether it can
  be is [QUESTION-387](../questions.d/QUESTION-387.md).
- **That mechanical prestress is organizational frustration.** The reading
  of Zang et al. calls their frustration energetic and applied from
  outside, which [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md) is not. The table is a classification of
  design targets that carries across the two senses, not an identification
  of them.
