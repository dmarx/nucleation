# QUESTION-028: When does independently generalizable, intervention-relevant relational structure warrant treating a communicative category as a real effective kind rather than an arbitrary taxonomy?

## Why it is a question

A218's RQ7, "Ontological interpretation", asks it in these words. It has been
asked four times before.

- A187 §8 proposed it for filing: "When is a communicative structure a real
  pattern?" A187 §4 had offered an answer in outline: "cross-context
  predictive and explanatory success provides grounds for treating certain
  patterns as more than arbitrary descriptions." TV Tropes was its example.
- A191 §7 gave four criteria: robust identification, cross-realization
  generalization, causal relevance, and nonredundant explanatory value ("A
  structural representation improves prediction or explanation beyond simpler
  models based on surface features, topic, or generic contextual
  correlations"). It added: "None of these independently establishes a
  metaphysical ontology." The criteria restate the manuscript's success
  criterion ([CLAIM-078](../claims.d/CLAIM-078.md)), that the theory succeeds if its measures predict
  changes of communicative identity that text similarity misses.
- A203's crystallized argument (§X, its last paragraph) ends on it: "whether
  their predictive robustness warrants treating them as real, effective
  communicative kinds".
- A214 §13: success "would support a **real-patterns interpretation** of
  communicative kinds", but "would not deductively establish that relations
  are metaphysically prior to all objects".

None of these says what would settle it. The exchange's guard
([CLAIM-150](../claims.d/CLAIM-150.md)) says only that predictive success does not establish
constitution.

It refines [QUESTION-021](QUESTION-021.md) and reopens it. [QUESTION-021](QUESTION-021.md) asked whether there is a
frame-independent act that translations approximate or whether the act is
constituted by its frame-relative realizations, and A24 refined it away into
[QUESTION-001](QUESTION-001.md). This question takes a third answer for granted, a repeatable
communicative kind ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)), and asks when such a kind is real.

## What the record holds toward an answer

The record already holds the real-patterns readings the exchange cites as
new, and they locate the difficulty.

- **Compressibility over-admits.** Dennett's criterion ([LIT-220](../literature.d/LIT-220.md)) makes a
  pattern real "if there is a description of the data that is more efficient
  than the bit map". [THEORY-036](../theory.d/THEORY-036.md) reads it as admitting almost every
  non-random pattern. A criterion of explanatory compression alone would make
  every stable TV Tropes page a real kind.
- **Projectibility does the work.** Ladyman 2024 ([LIT-219](../literature.d/LIT-219.md)) replaces
  compressibility with indispensability to projectible generalizations,
  relative to perspective and scale. Ladyman and Lorenzetti's effective
  ontic structural realism ([LIT-184](../literature.d/LIT-184.md)) is the read statement of "effective"
  kinds. [THEORY-036](../theory.d/THEORY-036.md) holds that these added clauses do the excluding, and
  that the text that would confirm it, *Every Thing Must Go* ([LIT-301](../literature.d/LIT-301.md)), is
  unread.

On that reading, the question's work is done by its qualifiers,
"independently generalizable" and "intervention-relevant". Those are the
projectibility clause in the record's terms. What is missing is a statement of
them that a communicative category could fail.

## What would count as an answer

A criterion, stated before the data, that a candidate communicative category
could fail while still being compressible: for instance, generalization to
realizations, media or speakers not used to define it, under probes chosen
independently of the category, together with predicted responses to
interventions on the relations said to constitute it. An answer would also
say which of these is necessary. A further demonstration that some category
predicts well within the data used to define it would not count.

## What it is not

Not the question of which relations constitute an act and which only
accompany it ([QUESTION-030](QUESTION-030.md)). A category could be a real effective kind
with its constitutive relations still unknown, and the converse.
