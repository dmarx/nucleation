# CLAIM-tmpmp2wh: Originalism does not assume exhaustiveness

## The gap

A40 §10 says a critique's application "would then require demonstrating
that the targeted theory actually depends upon the conflation in question".
The exchange does not demonstrate it for originalism, and its own reports
of the literature tell against it.

## The defenders' statements

From the assistant's knowledge of the literature; unverified until read
([LEAD-tmpdy0f8](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpdy0f8.md)). The works not cited by the exchange are named, not
registered.

- **Meaning and construction.** Solum's Fixation Thesis and Constraint
  Principle (A38 §1 states both) come with the interpretation–construction
  distinction: where the original meaning underdetermines a case, it is
  decided by construction. A38 §1 reports this itself. A theory with a
  construction zone does not hold that the formative specification
  "sufficiently determines its subsequent normative organization".
- **Grounds of authority.** The original meaning's authority is argued for,
  not read off its origin: as present law, by Baude and Sachs
  ([LIT-tmp3huft](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3huft.md)), whom A40 §2 reports; by popular sovereignty or
  supermajoritarian legitimacy (McGinnis and Rappaport); by the rule of
  law and the constraint of judicial discretion (Scalia, "Originalism: The
  Lesser Evil", 1989). These are A40 §2.1's "account of why the historical
  specification possesses continuing authority". They may fail, but the
  critique does not engage them.
- **A comparative claim.** Scalia's argument is that originalism is the
  lesser evil among interpretive methods, not that it suits every present
  problem. Its remedy for an inadequate specification is amendment and
  legislation.
- **Framework originalism.** Balkin's ([LIT-tmps708j](../literature.d/LIT-tmps708j.md)), which A40 §2.2 cites,
  treats the constitution as a framework filled in by construction.

## What originalism is, in the framework's own terms

A38 §1's table lists "Revision authority | Who may legitimately transform
it" beside formation provenance, maintenance provenance, lineage,
character and realization, and says "These properties cannot generally be
inferred from one another." Originalism, as its defenders state it, is a
claim about revision authority: courts may not revise the continuing
constitution by reinterpretation; the people and their representatives may,
by amendment and legislation. That claim is consistent with
[CLAIM-tmpazcha](CLAIM-tmpazcha.md). And A40 §2.5's worry, that "constitutional change may be
disguised as interpretation", is the originalist's own main charge against
living constitutionalism.

## What survives

- **The cost of the authorized route.** If revision is allocated to Article
  V, the critique's force lies in whether that route is adequate to "the
  kinds and timescales of inadequacy the organization encounters" (A38 §1).
  That is [CLAIM-571](CLAIM-571.md)'s reconfiguration threshold applied to amendment. It is
  a design and empirical question the exchange names and does not argue.
- **Relocated discretion.** A40 §2.4's point, that recovering meaning
  involves discretion of its own, bears on the Constraint Principle's
  rationale. A40 states it as a research question ([LEAD-tmp2x04m](../leads.d/LEAD-tmp2x04m.md)).
- **Popular versions.** Whether public or political appeals to original
  meaning make the conflation was not checked. If they do, the critique
  holds of them, not of the theory.

## The mark

The critique's target, as A38's table states it, is a strawman of
originalist theory: held by none of the theorists the exchange cites, and
conceded as such for Balkin by A40 §2.2. [ARG-tmptjsx2](../arguments.d/ARG-tmptjsx2.md) holds the inference
from [CLAIM-tmpazcha](CLAIM-tmpazcha.md) to the critique, rejected.
