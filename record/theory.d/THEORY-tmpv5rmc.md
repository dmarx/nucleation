# THEORY-tmpv5rmc: The specific phenomenal character of an experience is fixed by its place in a quality space, the structure of all the discriminations a sensory system can make, and that structure is mirrored many-to-one in neural feature maps; whether the state is conscious at all is left to a separate general mechanism; so experiences are holistic, a change anywhere in the space can change every experience in it, and spectrum inversion is excluded actually by the asymmetry of colour space and nomologically by its ties to the other senses

## Source

Lyre (2022), [LIT-tmp3sgbg](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3sgbg.md), *Neuroscience of Consciousness* niac012, read
in full in [NOTE-tmpg26n5](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpg26n5.md) from the PubMed Central deposit. The sections
"Structure, homomorphisms, and structural individuation", "Phenomenality,
content, and characters", "Quality spaces, Q-structure, and
psychophysics", "N-structure: neural activation spaces and neural maps",
"Neurophenomenal holism" and "Qualia inversion and (finally) the hard
problem".

## What was actually shown

Nothing was tested. The paper states two assumptions and argues from them.

- **Character.** "Any phenomenal experience is fully individuated by its
  place in a Q-structure. Phenomenal content and character are relational,
  not intrinsic properties of sentient subjects." A Q-structure is "the
  totality of all possible sensory discriminations", built in practice
  from just-noticeable differences and similarity ratings.
- **Mirroring.** "Q-structure is mirrored in N-structure", by "a surjective
  homomorphism from N to Q", many-to-one because neural realizations are
  plastic and multiple. Self-organizing feature maps are the proposed
  vehicles, and Brouwer & Heeger (2009), cited, found colour-space
  similarity structure in V4.
- **Specific, not generic.** Phenomenal character is "experienced
  content", a composite of a content-specific correlate and a general
  correlate of consciousness. Only the first individuates.
- **Holism.** Since each experience is placed by the whole structure,
  "any single change results in adjustments to large parts or even the
  entire web". Red-green dichromats "will experience all colors
  differently".
- **Inversion.** Actual inversion is excluded because colour space is
  "highly asymmetric". Nomological inversion is excluded because colour
  experience is tied to other experiences, for instance of heat. The
  metaphysical possibility of inversion with quiddistic qualia "cannot be
  ruled out".

What could have come out otherwise: the structure of colour similarity
might have had no counterpart in visual cortex. The one cited study says it
has one. The holism prediction could fail; no test is reported.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say what makes a state conscious.** The author: "it can
  never fully solve the hard problem". Any general theory of consciousness
  may be added, and the paper names several as compatible.
- **It is not a structural realism.** "neurophenomenal structuralism is no
  realism about the phenomenal". The Q-structure is reduced to the
  N-structure and "plays no causal role at all".
- **The reduction is not part of this THEORY.** It needs metaphysically
  necessary supervenience, which the author says has no argument "other
  than the request to adhere to the overall scientific, more properly
  physicalist, spirit". This THEORY carries the individuation thesis and
  its consequences, not the reduction.
- **It is shown for colour only, and argued, not shown, there.** Other
  modalities are cited as having quality spaces.
- **It does not exclude inversion of a symmetric space.** The exclusion is
  empirical: if a quality space had a symmetry, the thesis would identify
  the symmetric experiences, not distinguish them.

- **The objections.** Negro ([THEORY-tmp7uasf](THEORY-tmp7uasf.md)) argues that the holism this
  THEORY entails is false, and that a one-experience system, which it
  makes impossible, is possible. Aldé ([THEORY-tmp4u3l2](THEORY-tmp4u3l2.md)) argues that the
  geometry of the colour similarity space cannot yield the unique hues.
  Both quote Lyre's first assumption as their target.

  by organization and not by an intrinsic quale. It goes further, making
  organization constitute occurrence too, which this THEORY leaves to a
  separate mechanism. This THEORY is the held form of the claim's
  character half only.

  remedy [CLAIM-138](../claims.d/CLAIM-138.md) says concedes non-structural input.
