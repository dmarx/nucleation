# THEORY-tmppemix: Parfit argues that narrow person-affecting principles fail in non-identity and different-number cases, and that a wide person-affecting principle, on which being caused to exist with a life worth living is an intrinsic benefit and an outcome is in one way worse if it benefits people less, solves the non-identity problem and keeps the relation of being better transitive

## The account

Most choices of policy change who will exist. On the Narrow principles an
outcome cannot be worse, or an act wrong, if it is worse for no one, so a
cheaper energy policy that ruins later centuries would not be worse. Parfit
calls the implication that no future people would not be worse "deeply
mistaken" (p. 119). Appeals to rights or harm cannot separate Clare, who
could wait and have a different child, from Sarah, who could not (pp.
124–129).

Being caused to exist with a life worth living is an intrinsic "existential
benefit" (pp. 131–137). On the Wide Principle an outcome is in one way worse
if it is less good for people, benefiting them less than the other would.
The cheaper policy is worse for no one, "But this policy would be much less
good for these people than the more expensive, better policy would have been
for the different people who would have existed instead" (p. 130). The Wide
relation is about intrinsic goodness and is transitive (Cases Two and
Three).

A Wide Dual Principle counts benefits to all and benefits to each, and does
not imply the Repugnant Conclusion in Case Ten. Separately, giving lesser
benefits to present people may be defensible act by act, but "we and our
successors might together wreck the Earth" (p. 149).

## Evidence

Argument on stipulated cases, with equal benefits per year and no other
differences (p. 140). The solution depends on existential benefit, which is
contested ([NOTE-1263](../notes.d/NOTE-1263.md), Limitations). The draft lacked a conclusion; material
was "to be added" at two places, and Parfit grants the last section does not
show the Repugnant Conclusion can be avoided (p. 157).

## What it bears on

non-identity problem (Parfit, [LIT-1768](../literature.d/LIT-1768.md); ...) makes even harms to actual
future people hard to state". Its note of 2026-10-29 reads it as "the good
of future people, without anyone in particular". The reading offers a
person-affecting ground for what is owed, and leaves open the question's
