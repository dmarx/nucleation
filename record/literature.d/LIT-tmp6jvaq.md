# LIT-tmp6jvaq: Optimal experience in work and leisure

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi and Judith LeFevre (1989). *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 56(5): 815-822. DOI-10.1037/0022-3514.56.5.815

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* By the abstract: 78 workers followed for a week by experience sampling; flow (high challenge and high skill) affected the quality of experience more than whether one was at work or leisure, most flow was reported at work, yet respondents were more motivated in leisure. Unverified until read.
