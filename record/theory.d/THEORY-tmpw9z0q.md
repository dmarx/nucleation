# THEORY-tmpw9z0q: In Claude language models, projecting out the most active J-space directions in early workspace layers lowers the rate of felt, first-person, sensory language in the models' narrations of their own processing and in their descriptions of another person's experience, while norm-matched perturbations along other directions do not and the text stays coherent; so in these models the experiential register of report depends on the access-like workspace and is not specific to self-report

## Source

LIT-1347, read in full in NOTE-1069. What the source measured is THEORY-869, which this entry extends.

## The claim

The authors conclude from the ablation that the experiential register of
these models' reports depends on the J-space, their access-like workspace,
and that the dependence is the same for reports about others, so it is not
specific to self-report.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried word for word from THEORY-869 when that entry was narrowed. More readings of the same source cannot settle it.
