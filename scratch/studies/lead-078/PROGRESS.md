- Read (2026-11-22), findings kept here until filed:
  - Khan and Dhar 2007, whole article. Five experiments plus a prediction
    study and a link study in the General Discussion. Knowing the same
    choice recurs raises the vice share (Exp 1 80% vs 57.5%; Exp 2 83% vs
    53%; Exp 3 83.3% vs 57.0%; Exp 4 70% vs 47.5%; Exp 5 SS money 64.5% vs
    37.1%, only in high-self-control participants). Predictions of next
    week's virtue 67% vs actual 36%. Anticipated guilt mediates (Baron and
    Kenny, Exp 3). Link study (Kirby and Guastello's procedure): vice 90%
    repeated, 46.6% isolated, 53% repeated-with-link.
  - Schuhr thesis: chs 1, 4 (bundling treatments), 7 (design), 8 (parts),
    9. One session per treatment, n 29-37, GSU 2013, MPL, real pay.
    Free (T4, T6) and forced (T5, T7) series of choices made at once in
    one session. First bundled choices more often LL than T0; BUNDLED
    raises mixture probability of exponential discounting by 0.307
    (p = 0.019); FORCED lowers it by 0.330 (p < 0.001); SCALE n.s.
    Criticises Kirby and Guastello's suggested condition as reporting an
    unestablished "fact" to subjects.
  - Ge et al. 2024/2025 (Mindfulness 16:448-464), whole article.
    n = 70 low-trait-mindfulness students, 3 arms (monitor+acceptance 24,
    monitor 23, waitlist 23), 10 min/day for 7 days; Stroop, Go/No-go,
    DD at pre, post, 15-day follow-up. Post: shorter Stroop and Go/No-go
    RTs in the mindfulness arm; accuracy >95% and unchanged; no DD
    difference at any time; no effects at follow-up; no mediation
    analysis; not preregistered.
