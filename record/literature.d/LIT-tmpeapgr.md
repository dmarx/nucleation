  Slemp et al. (2024), JPSP 127. A psychometric meta-analysis of 881 samples
  (N = 443,556), over 70% cross-sectional and mostly recipient-reported.
  Perceived autonomy, competence and relatedness support each correlate with
  satisfaction of all three needs at ρ = .44–.62, with their frustration at
  −.20 to −.55, with autonomous motivation at about .4 and with performance
  at .14–.25. The supports intercorrelate .67–.71; in joint models
  competence and relatedness support add to autonomy support, and
  relatedness support carries relatedness satisfaction. Individualism
  moderated 2 of 18 relations.
