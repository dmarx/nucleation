# CLAIM-003: A global workspace need not give every subsystem the same access: access is compartmented in brains as in organizations, so "global" overstates it

## The claim

U26: "'global' is very likely a misnomer ... the neuronal workspace has
compartments, conscious experience can as well." Operationalized behaviourally
as "evocative of a planned response over a sustained timescale".

## Why it is set aside

Not needed by the will paper. The assistant's caution (A78) stands: the
driver case "does not, by itself, establish two simultaneously conscious
workspaces".

## Note of 2026-10-12: the rival accounts, not linked

Two THEORYs bear against this claim, and neither is written as a ground, since a ground supports. [THEORY-096](../theory.d/THEORY-096.md) is the account this claim argues against: content "globally accessed by local processors". It asserts uniform broadcast rather than measuring it, and its remark that V1 neurons join the workspace in tasks needing fine detail partly helps this claim. [THEORY-063](../theory.d/THEORY-063.md) (Metzinger) holds that conscious contents form one functional cluster. It bears only on the experiential half of this claim, and his "typically" leaves room.

## Note of 2026-10-18: taken up again, with self-attribution as a third quantity

A27 §7 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 used this claim for the binding problem:
"A common regulatory signal may change behavior throughout the organization
without every subsystem receiving the same information." It added a third
quantity beside integration and access, self-attribution, and a narrower
claim: "A coherent organizational state need not be uniformly accessible to
its constituent subsystems, and apparent unity in the organization's reports
may exceed the degree of information sharing actually present."

A27 wrote that "the record correctly cautions that compartmentalized behavior
does not establish multiple conscious workspaces", and credited it to the
global workspace account, [THEORY-096](../theory.d/THEORY-096.md). The caution is this claim's own, A78's as its
body records it: the driver case "does not, by itself, establish two
simultaneously conscious workspaces".

The third quantity is noted on [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md), and the unified owner it
concerns is [CLAIM-355](CLAIM-355.md). The status stays Deferred; whether the line
reopens is the owner's decision.
