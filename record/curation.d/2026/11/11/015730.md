The sixth synthesis review, at the owner's request, run in parallel with
the defect sweep of the fifth round. It covered the next two smallest
lines with no account, realizability (122 entries when it began) and
will-organization (209). The owner asked during the round why the rounds
went from the smallest line up. The answer was that this was a choice of
cost and nothing more, and that the largest lines carry most of the
argument and most of the splits. The rounds after this one start from
distributed-agency.

Two read-only readers each drafted one line, with the briefs of the fifth
round, and then revised their drafts after the sweep (dmarx/nucleation#456),
the rulings on CLAIM-600 and CLAIM-632 (#457), and the move below
(#458) had changed what they covered. The filer then:

- checked coverage and quotes mechanically;
- reran `line_parts.py` on the filed parts;
- removed the two Superseded syntheses the realizability drafts had
  integrated, as the earlier accounts do;
- recounted the parts once the withdrawal inquiry had left.

## The ranking

Stage 1 was not rerun. The fifth round's ranking stands, and the
lines were chosen by size.

## The withdrawal inquiry, found again

The realizability reader's first draft had five parts. One was the
withdrawal part that ADR-087 had moved to transient-support, mostly closed
inside realizability: 51 stored relations inside, 5 out and 2 in. Its
entries carried realizability because they bear on QUESTION-398 or
QUESTION-399, whose first line was realizability. Under ADR-085 an entry
takes the first line of the question it bears on.

ADR-tmpqp8c4 (dmarx/nucleation#458) gives both questions, and the entries
that argue them, transient-support as their first line. It keeps
realizability on them only where a relation to a realizability-first
entry supplies it. None has one, so twenty-one entries leave
realizability. A first version kept QUESTION-399 in realizability. The
recount showed the claims bearing on it closed off from the rest, with 14
stored relations inside and none out or in, so it moved too.
Transient-support's account, SYNTHESIS-032, now integrates the five new
members.

**Arm A.**

  separate from their premises, not false:

  - for CLAIM-534, an agent integrated by wholehearted ranking.

  The reader suggests adding each case under "Admission" in its claim. That
  is left for a follow-up.
- CLAIM-556 adds an ordering and two repairs.
- CLAIM-576 and CLAIM-573 are conjectures that can fail while their
  premises hold.

**CLAIM-600's new admission case is realistic.** The ruling of
2026-11-11 (#457) narrowed CLAIM-600 to necessary conditions. Its new
admission case is a reintroduced population that persists through the
refuges it has reached after fire suppression is withdrawn while the fire
regime persists. The case separates the narrower claim from its premises,
and the claim says so. The realizability reader points out that the case
is realistic: refugia are common in fire ecology, and a case of that kind
meets the first clause of the claim's own `defeated_if`. Under ADR-084
that counts as evidence against the necessity of the threat condition. It
is a weaker form of the water scheme's problem, because no actual case is
cited. The condition probably needs restating as "the party no longer
needs the support against the threat". That restating risks a free
parameter, so it is left for the owner, not made here.
