# THEORY-tmp5zzgh: Shavit and Sharon read the southern Levantine shift to the Neolithic as an evolutionary transition in individuality on Griesemer's three stages, coordination in Epipalaeolithic bands, cooperation in the Natufian and collaboration in the PPNA and PPNB, and hold that farming became irreversible once group traits grew mutually dependent and group fitness decoupled from individual fitness, so that several causes could have produced it

## Source

Ayelet Shavit and Gonen Sharon (2023), LIT-1935, read in NOTE-tmpots55.

## What was actually shown

An interpretation. The paper reviews the archaeological sequence (§1(b),
Table 1) and assigns its periods to the 3Cs stages (§1(c)), citing
Belfer-Cohen and Goring-Morris for the rise of community "signatures".
What could have come out otherwise is the predicted low fidelity of
group culture in the Natufian; the paper gives one example (varied
fish-hooks at Jordan River Dureijat) and no measure.

## What this does not say

- That the Neolithic meets ETI criteria; the authors say they do not
  examine it.
- That the cause debates are settled; it says they can coexist.
- That the irreversibility is shown by data; the "point of no return" is
  argued from the mutual dependence of group traits.
