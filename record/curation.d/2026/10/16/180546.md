The THEORY backfill continued with the ethics and agency readings: twenty
LITs, each with a `Read` NOTE and no THEORY citing it as `source`. Eleven
are tagged `ethics` first and nine `agency` first. Every THEORY rests on
the record's reading, not on a fresh look at the paper.

## What entered

Seventeen THEORYs, three `Active` and fourteen `Proposed`. Codes are the
temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

## What did not enter, and why

- **Browning & Veit, "The Desperate Search for Meaning in Life" ([LIT-116](../../record/literature.d/LIT-116.md),
  [NOTE-119](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-119.md)).** No THEORY. Its one thesis, that "without [otherness], the
  self cannot exist" (p. 9), is "stated, not argued", in a chapter with no
  references beyond two quotations. Its reading also finds a misdescription
  of Heidegger. A THEORY would state a claim that has no warrant in the
  work.
- **Floridi, "AI as Agency Without Intelligence" ([LIT-896](../../record/literature.d/LIT-896.md), [NOTE-697](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-697.md)).** No
  THEORY. The NOTE says an editorial "whose central claim is asserted
  cannot ground one", and that a THEORY should come from the 2025 paper.
  That THEORY now exists ([THEORY-208](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-208.md), from [LIT-897](../../record/literature.d/LIT-897.md)). It names this editorial
  in its Connections as the slogan's first statement. It does not argue
  from the editorial, so the LIT was not added to its `source`.

So eighteen of twenty readings became sources, sixteen as primary sources
and two as second sources ([LIT-146](../../record/literature.d/LIT-146.md), [LIT-286](../../record/literature.d/LIT-286.md)). That fits the owner's
hypothesis for this group, with two caveats. Four of the accounts are
reports of conceptual proposals whose evidence is their own coherence:
Hooker, Walsh, Floridi 2013 and Orseau et al. And Floridi 2013's NOTE had
said "no THEORY". Its reasons were the record's practice for that line,
and that the paper offers "a model and a coinage". The NOTE also named C1
and C7 as the support any later theory of moral thresholds would have. The
THEORY files C1, Proposed, and puts the analogy's failure in its title.

## Choices made

- **Status.** `Active` went to three accounts the record believes as
  stated. The first is a conceptual point the reading rates strong
  (precaution has no direction). The second is a description of
  experimental data with its limits in the title (Quillien). The third is
  an existence proof by construction (Brooks). Everything argued, assumed
  or conditional went `Proposed`. That includes Kenton et al., whose theorem
  is proved but whose definition of agency is a proposal.
- **Tags, minding [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md).** Velleman went to `self-governance`,
  `personhood` and `self`, not `agency`, because his subject is
  identification and autonomy set against personal identity. Brooks went to
  `behavioral-integration` first. Floridi 2013 went to `ethics` and
  `society-and-governance`, not `agency`, because its "supra-agents" are
  agents by fiat and its claim is about the moral value of outcomes. The
  parasocial account took `narrative-unity`, for Lindemann's narrative,
  social-practice identity.
- **Edits to existing documents.**
  - [THEORY-207](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-207.md) gained `presupposes` (version 2).
  - [CLAIM-045](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-045.md)'s `grounds` now point at [THEORY-689](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-689.md) in place of [LIT-436](../../record/literature.d/LIT-436.md)
    (version 2).
  - [THEORY-025](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-025.md) gained a Connections line to [THEORY-411](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-411.md).
  - `luria link --fix` wrote the converses on [THEORY-173](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-173.md) and [QUESTION-032](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-032.md).
    It dropped [CLAIM-045](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-045.md) from [LIT-436](../../record/literature.d/LIT-436.md)'s `supports`.
- **No `answers` for [QUESTION-011](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-011.md) or [QUESTION-009](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-009.md).** Velleman's account
  constrains [QUESTION-011](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-011.md) only negatively: what makes a will-organization
  one's own need not be what makes one persist. It is named in Connections.
  Floridi 2013 shows only how freely the word "agent" is used, which does
  not answer [QUESTION-009](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-009.md).

## Errors found in the record

- **[LIT-172](../../record/literature.d/LIT-172.md) is tagged `individuation`.** [NOTE-186](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-186.md) justifies an identity tag
  in the practical, narrative sense: who someone is over time as others
  track it. Under [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md) that is `personhood` or `narrative-unity`, not
  being one thing. The tag looks like a mechanical carry-over from the
  retired identity word. It is left for a tagging decision.
- **[LIT-198](../../record/literature.d/LIT-198.md)'s summary keeps the claim its reading corrected.** The summary
  says the view "escapes the collapse/incoherence dilemma and the
  rule-worship charge (§8)". [NOTE-160](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-160.md) says the incoherence reply holds only
  on the reflective-equilibrium route, and that each reply answers a
  different objection.
- **Stale cross-references.** [NOTE-093](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-093.md) lists [LIT-193](../../record/literature.d/LIT-193.md) as Deferred.
  [NOTE-181](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-181.md) lists [LIT-166](../../record/literature.d/LIT-166.md) and [LIT-193](../../record/literature.d/LIT-193.md) as Deferred. [NOTE-120](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-120.md) calls [LIT-166](../../record/literature.d/LIT-166.md)
  "seeded only". All three works have since been read. These are statements
  of what was true at reading time, so they were not rewritten.
- **[LIT-126](../../record/literature.d/LIT-126.md) (Vieira) is `Rejected`.** Several readings cite it as the
  position that denies AI moral patienthood on phenomenal-consciousness
  grounds. The THEORYs here cite it for that position only.
