summary: >-
  Shwartz-Ziv & Tishby (2017),
  [ARXIV-1703.00810](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00810). The paper studies
  small tanh networks (layer widths down from 12 to 2) trained by SGD on a
  12-bit, O(3)-symmetric synthetic rule, with mutual information estimated
  by binning each neuron into 30 bins over all 4,096 inputs. It reports
  two phases: a short "ERM" phase of a few hundred epochs in which I(T;Y)
  rises, and a long "compression" phase in which I(X;T) falls. The phases
  coincide with a drop in per-layer gradient SNR (from high-SNR drift to
  low-SNR diffusion, at about 350 epochs), and the converged layers sit on
  the IB curve at fitted β. Everything is shown on small synthetic tasks,
  with the explanations argued informally.

# LIT-324: Opening the Black Box of Deep Neural Networks via Information

Ravid Shwartz-Ziv, Naftali Tishby (2017), *arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-1703.00810](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00810)

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-09-29 at the owner's request, because the owner's prior-art novelty map (a private working
document on concepts as projectors, the linear representation hypothesis and their neighbours) cites it: row 12 must-cite: information-bottleneck account of learning dynamics.
It is seeded from metadata and published descriptions and has not been read here.

It is also held in the Anthology of the SOTA as [ANTH-LIT-508](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-508.md). Holding it here too follows

Identification notes: Held in anthology ([ANTH-LIT-508](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-508.md)); anthology also holds the critique Saxe et al. 2018 ([ANTH-LIT-509](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-509.md)) — worth noting since the IB-compression claim is contested.

It was filed `Deferred`, unread. [NOTE-298](../notes.d/NOTE-298.md) is the close reading of 2026-09-29, and it placed the work: **Active** — worth reading as the source of the IB account of deep-learning dynamics and of the drift/diffusion gradient-SNR phase picture, which McCandlish et al. cite. Its compression and generalisation claims are contested: the anthology's [ANTH-LIT-509](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-509.md) reports them refuted, which I have not checked. Read it as the claim, not the result, and never without the critique.
