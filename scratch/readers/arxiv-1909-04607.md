## Step 2: NeurIPS Paper.pdf read in full (pp. 1-12: intro, §1 background, §2 method, §3 setup, §4 findings, §5 conclusions, references)

- Method (§2): two networks; left (θ) = encoder + main-task policy/value + answer network (linear heads); right = "question network" (η) taking FUTURE observations o_{t+1:t+j}, outputting cumulant vector u_t and discounts γ_t. On-policy GVFs only: policy is implicitly the current main-task policy. Algorithm 1 / Eq. 3: inner loop L updates of θ on L^RL + L^ans; outer single update of η on the sum over k of L^RL(θ_{t,k}) ("area under the curve"); alternative "End Meta-Loss" on last batch. Myopic = k=1.
- Representation-learning setting (§4.1): encoder receives only answer-network gradients; question network fully independent.
- Joint setting (§4.2): encoder gets both; question network takes the encoded state as input "to accelerate".
- Figures inspected as rendered images (not only text): Fig. 3 x-axis runs to 2.0 x1e9 frames, while the IMPALA reference is "IMPALA (200M)"; Fig. 2 x-axis to 1.0 x1e7 frames vs "A2C (3M)". So "comparable" representation-only performance takes ~10x (Atari) and >3x (gridworld) the frames of the main-task-trained reference; the text says the reference "learned faster" (p. 8).
- Fig. 6 right (read off the plot, approximate): at N=57 the Discovered GVFs median (~210%) is BELOW IMPALA and Reward Prediction (~221%) and above Pixel Control (~205%); Discovered leads only on the top-N subsets N=10..40 chosen by pixel-control gains. Text does not state the N=57 ordering.
- Fig. 5: d (number of questions) and k (unroll) both sensitive; k=1,2 fail; k=25 best, k=50 worse; d=2 fails, d=128 slower. All other experiments use d=128, k=10.
- 3 seeds everywhere; single hyperparameter set across Atari games.

## Step 3: camera-ready appendix (Supplemental.zip, pp. 13-17, §6.1-6.6, Figs. 7-12, Table 1) read in full; arXiv v1 compared

- Appendix = arXiv v1's "6 Appendix" (same page numbers 13-17). Architectures: question network in representation runs matches agent hidden layers, heads of size D=128, arctan for cumulants, sigmoid for discounts. Joint runs: Deep ResNet (IMPALA); question net is two MLPs (256,128) on the last ResNet hidden layer. Table 1: meta lr 0.0006 (Adam), unroll 10, 128 questions, aux loss coefficient 0.0001 (pixel control 0.0001, reward prediction 0.001). Joint-run aux hyperparameters searched on ten games, then fixed.
- §6.4 derives the myopic approximation (Eqs. 4-11): ignores that θ is affected by η.
- §6.6 / Fig. 8: six Atari games, representation-only; Discovered GVFs beats hand-crafted baselines in all; exceeds IMPALA-200M reference in gravitar and frostbite (again over 2e9 frames).
- Fig. 9 (Puddleworld parameter study, read off the plot): non-monotone in both d and k; best k=10, k=20 and k=50 markedly worse in final return. Text calls it "consistent" with Collect-Objects (where k=25 was best).
- Figs. 10-12: per-game relative improvement over Pixel Control, IMPALA, Reward Prediction at 200M frames; each shows a sizable block of losses as well as gains (counts not legible at rendered resolution; not counted).
