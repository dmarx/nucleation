# ARG-tmpwamhy: The observational symmetry of the miscalibrated anchor and the defined standard that fixes a frame, applied to the relation between physics and mathematics, show that physical content is mathematics up to observational equivalence plus a coordination with phenomena, and so amend the owner's thesis that physics is the subset of mathematics relevant to observable phenomena

## The argument

If physics were a subset of mathematics, two distinct mathematical models
would be two physical claims. CLAIM-tmpklggb exhibits two distinct models
with every observation the same. Physics treats them as one situation, so
its content is the class and not the member. If physics were mathematics
alone, nothing beyond the mathematics would fix which structure is which
quantity. CLAIM-tmpnl4xk shows the frame is fixed only by a defined
standard, which is a coordination and not a theorem. Hence: quotient plus
coordination.

## Critical questions

- **Is "subset" the owner's considered word?** Perhaps not. "Relevant to
  phenomena" already gestures at coordination. The amendment makes it
  explicit and keeps the thesis's core.
- **Do realists about structure disagree?** Some would say the
  equivalence classes are the structures, so the quotient is itself a
  piece of mathematics, a subset of a different space. That reading
  rescues "subset" at the cost of moving it. The coordination point
  stands either way.
- **Does the third amendment overreach?** It says only that what counts
  as observable moves with the channels. That is TERM-069's and
  CLAIM-574's point, applied to physics.
