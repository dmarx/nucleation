The THEORY backfill reached the mathematics group: twenty-one LITs with a
`Read` or `Skimmed` NOTE and no THEORY citing them as `source`. Each NOTE was
read in full, with its LIT. The papers were not fetched; every THEORY rests
on the record's reading. The caller's ruling for this group was that a
proved result, stated with its conditions, can be `Active`.

## What entered

Eighteen THEORYs, fifteen `Active` and three `Proposed`. Codes are the
temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

  controls ‖f(A)‖ only for diagonalisable A, the numerical range always,

  [LIT-243](../../record/literature.d/LIT-243.md) and [LIT-326](../../record/literature.d/LIT-326.md)). Walsh's system and its Fourier-like convergence

## What did not enter, and why

- **[LIT-064](../../record/literature.d/LIT-064.md) (Chu & Raginsky, [NOTE-046](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-046.md)).** Moved to the anthology as
  [ANTH-LIT-726](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-726.md) at the owner's request, with the reading. A THEORY here
  would hold one reading in two places.
- **[LIT-237](../../record/literature.d/LIT-237.md) (bra–ket notation, [NOTE-855](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-855.md)).** The NOTE files none: the
  article adds no claim, and its one substantive point, that the bracket is
  an inner product only where Riesz holds, is the Riesz theorem. That point
  is in [THEORY-629](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-629.md)'s "does not say", citing [NOTE-855](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-855.md).
- **[LIT-040](../../record/literature.d/LIT-040.md) (nLab, two-level type theory).** Superseded by [LIT-095](../../record/literature.d/LIT-095.md). Its NOTE
  read the paper, and the THEORY is sourced to [LIT-095](../../record/literature.d/LIT-095.md), not to the page.
  [LIT-095](../../record/literature.d/LIT-095.md) has no NOTE of its own; its reading is [NOTE-047](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-047.md).

So nineteen of twenty-one readings now carry a THEORY, counting [LIT-040](../../record/literature.d/LIT-040.md)
through [LIT-095](../../record/literature.d/LIT-095.md). That fits the owner's hypothesis for this group, with a
caveat: most of these are classical theorems, and five NOTEs had declined
a THEORY outright ([LIT-237](../../record/literature.d/LIT-237.md), [LIT-243](../../record/literature.d/LIT-243.md), [LIT-803](../../record/literature.d/LIT-803.md), [LIT-827](../../record/literature.d/LIT-827.md), [LIT-981](../../record/literature.d/LIT-981.md)), mostly on
the ground that a standard theorem need not be stated separately.
The filed THEORYs earn their place mainly in "What this does not say":
Eckart–Young gives no uniqueness of factors, Riesz 1907 states no
uniqueness, Walsh 1923 has no characters, Pontryagin 1934 has no general
LCA duality, Gelfand–Naimark has no categorical duality, and Stanley has no
topes. Those are the points the owner's prior-art map leans on.

## Choices made

- **Overriding four NOTEs' "No THEORY".** [NOTE-854](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-854.md) says the Riesz theorem
  is standard and should be cited to a textbook, and [NOTE-289](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-289.md) that a modern
  proof supersedes the 1907 note as a source. The THEORY
  states it with the conditions [LIT-243](../../record/literature.d/LIT-243.md) proves, and records that advice.
  [NOTE-625](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-625.md) declined a THEORY on Backprop as Functor because a claim about
  backpropagation is the anthology's side. The anthology does not hold the
  paper, and the THEORY states the compositional theorem, not an
  instruction; the reason is recorded in its "does not say". [NOTE-601](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-601.md) and
  [NOTE-784](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-784.md) are `Skimmed`, so their THEORYs are `Proposed` and say so.
- **One THEORY for two Riesz readings.** The 1907 note is the priority
  source and the REU paper the proof; neither alone carries the account.
- **[LIT-017](../../record/literature.d/LIT-017.md) stays `Proposed`, and so does its THEORY.** The statements are
  narrowed to what the proofs cover: exact Betti Lagrangians, finite
  creative networks. Theorem 5 is left out.
- **No new `rivals` or `extends`.** Riehl–Shulman and two-level type theory
  are alternative answers to one question, and both can be right; they name
  each other in Connections.
- **Outside facts flagged as outside.** The 1 + √2 Crouzeix–Palencia bound,
  Frobenius's theorem on real division algebras and Schmidt's 1907 priority
  for low-rank approximation are named as not verified in the record.
