- **Branches.** The reader worktrees and local branches were removed after filing. Deleting the eight remote branches (`claude/readers/lit-…`) was refused by the session's git proxy (HTTP 403), so they remain for the owner to delete.

## Where works carried less than they were cited for

Each case moved the work from the claim's `grounds` to `corroborates` (or replaced the LIT with the reading's THEORY), with an ARG saying why (step 9):

- **Mitzenmacher.**
  - The paper does not carry A3's "preferential attachment alone does not guarantee this result" ([ARG-tmpuhci1](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpuhci1.md), [CLAIM-tmpepx9m](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpepx9m.md)).
  - Its floor is a reflecting barrier, not Kesten's additive term ([ARG-tmpk3nh6](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpk3nh6.md), [CLAIM-tmpiervv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpiervv.md)).
  - It keeps attachment and the age mechanism apart ([ARG-tmpp2xln](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpp2xln.md), [CLAIM-tmply70l](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmply70l.md)).
- **BBP.**
  - It covers only the top eigenvalue of complex Gaussian data with D ≤ N ([ARG-tmppdhhb](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmppdhhb.md)).
  - The real case, D > N and several outliers are Baik & Silverstein's ([ARG-tmpvalfs](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpvalfs.md)).
  - The eigenvector overlap is Paul's or Benaych-Georges & Nadakuditi's ([ARG-tmpnwmcr](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpnwmcr.md)).
  - The uncentred mean shift and ±1 mixture are Benaych-Georges & Nadakuditi §3.1 ([ARG-tmpe2vrk](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpe2vrk.md)).
- **Dambre.**
  - The capacity bound needs only i.i.d. input, a linear readout and infinite data. Fading memory with rank N is the condition for equality. [ARG-tmpubyzo](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpubyzo.md) is corrected in place.
  - [CLAIM-tmpd1shn](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpd1shn.md) is a case of Theorem 7 with noise counted as an input ([ARG-tmpr04sw](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpr04sw.md)).
  - The paper's "edge" is linear stability, and its sweep is input scaling ([ARG-tmp53559](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp53559.md)).
- **Transtrum 2015 and Bialek.**
  - The review gives no resolution-dependent dimension ([ARG-tmpu5yj6](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpu5yj6.md), [CLAIM-tmpj0k4q](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj0k4q.md)).
  - Bialek et al. assert without proof that predictive information diverges at critical points ([ARG-tmpvo4gr](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpvo4gr.md), [CLAIM-tmp99m8g](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp99m8g.md)).
  - Of [CLAIM-tmpyxiwv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpyxiwv.md)'s three growth laws, (d/2) ln T is in §4.1, T^(1/p) matches §4.5's annealed result in exponent only (the annealed coefficient is too large by about 1 + 1/p, checked), and (ln T)²/(4a) is not in the paper ([ARG-tmpy9b4f](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpy9b4f.md)).
- **The originators' network applications.** A small tanh network and an autoencoder's code do not carry [CLAIM-tmpficzj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpficzj.md) to overparameterized networks ([ARG-tmpv3nqz](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpv3nqz.md), Rejected).
- **Lau et al.** Its calibration is not evidence for [CLAIM-tmpcg4uu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcg4uu.md), since the paper puts its underestimates down to sampling ([ARG-tmpdvwy2](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpdvwy2.md), Rejected).
- **The singular edges.** The manifold's boundaries, where the metric is singular, press on [CLAIM-tmpcgvcp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcgvcp.md)'s defeater but do not meet it ([ARG-tmpddtmr](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpddtmr.md)). They support the owner's U24 at the edges, while the widths stay unconnected to strata ([ARG-tmp97cwb](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp97cwb.md)).

## Corrections and revisions

- **[TERM-tmptiim0](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmptiim0.md) ("sloppy"), version 2.** The sources say Fisher eigenvalues are not intrinsic, and put the invariant content in the model manifold's widths, which match √λ only in natural or log units, from a central point. Their "evenly spaced" log eigenvalues are recorded beside the term's own check, which found widening gaps. The manifold's boundaries are where the metric is singular, so a sloppy model is regular inside and singular at its edges. The separations from ill-conditioning and from a power-law spectrum are marked as the record's.
- **[TERM-tmp5z2du](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmp5z2du.md) (hyper-ribbon).**
  - The sources spell it "hyper-ribbon", and its widths are geodesic.
  - The link between widths and spectrum is empirical and conditional, and the widths argument is heuristic.
  - The caution against equating stiffness with renormalization-group relevance is A's: the 2015 review says "precisely those".
- **[CLAIM-tmpcg4uu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcg4uu.md).**
  - Widths go as √λ, not 1/√λ. The error likely came from the parameter-space ellipse.
  - The learning-coefficient estimate's inverse temperature is 1/log n, so the effective sample size is n/log n.
  - The localizer's precision γ is a second cut-off, checked by Monte Carlo.
- **[LEAD-tmpfrw11](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpfrw11.md).** Estimating at several inverse temperatures is marked as the record's proposal, not Lau et al.'s. It now also varies γ, holds the sampler's settings fixed, and checks that the sampler has settled.
- **[NOTE-1462](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1462.md).** Lau et al. is now held.
- **Bibliographies corrected from the texts.** Mitzenmacher's volume, issue and pages; PRL 104, 060201; PRE 83, 036701; J. Chem. Phys. 143, 010901. Lau et al.'s AISTATS venue is unconfirmed by the copy read and was removed.

The two filers' reports list them. The new readings were not yet published when the curation entry of this filing's exchange was written.
