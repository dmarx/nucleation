# QUESTION-tmpqba7g: What kind of game is search engine optimization, and has it a stable point, or is its arms race permanent?

## The players

- **The engine** commits to a ranking. It is meant to put first what users
  would find most relevant and best, and it can only read features that
  accompany those qualities.
- **Publishers** choose how to split effort between making better pages and
  manufacturing the features the ranking reads.
- **Users** give attention by rank, and so pay for both choices.

## The parts

1. What the ranking is, in the record's terms: a shortcut, a proxy, a signal?
2. Whether the engine's retraining against publishers' response can converge.
3. How authority-by-links (PageRank) is gamed, and what the record's account
   of authority says stops it.
4. What changes when the reader is a language model and not a ranked list.

## Note of 2026-11-27: paperclip maximization, enshittification and monoculture

<!-- inactive-ok-block: CLAIM-tmpzml5c CLAIM-tmp6n4ed CLAIM-tmpjom83 — new; filed at the owner's request to relate SEO to the three -->

At the owner's request, three more answers. [CLAIM-tmpzml5c](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpzml5c.md): search is a paperclip problem on both sides of the ranking, running the three-stage pathology. [CLAIM-tmp6n4ed](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6n4ed.md): enshittification of search is the engine optimizing its own ranking against its users, the same proxy-optimum structure as publishers', and possibly irreversible. [CLAIM-tmpjom83](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjom83.md): one dominant ranking makes the web a monoculture with correlated failures, and plurality is a remedy beyond competition.

## Note of 2026-11-27: Goodhart and Campbell

<!-- inactive-ok-block: DISTINCTION-tmpnbw70 CLAIM-tmpe27ub — new; filed at the owner's request to relate SEO to the two laws -->

At the owner's request, [DISTINCTION-tmpnbw70](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-tmpnbw70.md) separates the Goodhart effect, the indicator losing information (bought links), from the Campbell effect, the measured process reorganizing (the web written for the ranker). A better ranking cures the first and not the second. [CLAIM-tmpe27ub](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpe27ub.md) reads SEO as adversarial and causal Goodhart, with regressional and extremal Goodhart following from any noisy ranking. It sharpens the arms race's escape condition to effects of quality that cost at least as much to forge as to earn.

## Note of 2026-11-27: performative prediction

<!-- inactive-ok-block: CLAIM-tmph131t CLAIM-tmpgnw0o CLAIM-tmppjw67 — new; filed at the owner's request to relate SEO to performative prediction -->

At the owner's request, three answers in the terms of THEORY-839 and THEORY-861. CLAIM-tmph131t: a performatively stable ranking has retrained away its Goodhart effect and kept the Campbell effect, so convergence is no evidence of a good web. CLAIM-tmpgnw0o: the engine steers toward stability by lowering publishers' responsiveness, through penalties, delays and opacity, and the performative optimum favours signals costly to manipulate. CLAIM-tmppjw67: a ranking trained on clicks under itself locks in unless some evidence bypasses it (CLAIM-342).
