# LEAD-038: Construct a manipulated will and its unmanipulated twin and find a present-structure feature that tells them apart, or argue that none can and state what a record of the will's formation must contain

## What it would do

Write the pair of cases, then either name a feature of will-organization
that differs between them or argue that none can and say what a history of
formation must record.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): "Or an argument that none can, together with a statement
  of what a record of the will's formation would have to contain."
- [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): "[CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) becomes testable."

## What would count

Either outcome answers [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) and makes [CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) testable: a
feature gives it a present-structure test, an argument gives it a
historical one.
