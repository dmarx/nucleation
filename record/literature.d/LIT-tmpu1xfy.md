# LIT-tmpu1xfy: Positioning public sector practitioners as ‘moral crumple zones’: Mechanisms in the early use of generative AI work support tools

Marta Choroszewicz (2026). Positioning public sector practitioners as ‘moral crumple zones’: Mechanisms in the early use of generative AI work support tools. *Government Information Quarterly* 43(2): 102128. DOI 10.1016/j.giq.2026.102128.

## Key takeaways

- **Definition** (p. 3): "the disproportionate allocation of
  responsibility to LLM-based tools' users relative to their actual
  authority, control and capacity to influence these tools' design,
  functioning and quality of outputs."
- **Mechanisms** (§4): oversight "explicitly codified as a user burden";
  poor outputs "portrayed as the result of poor user interaction rather
  than issues with the tool's design"; persuasiveness that "may mask
  fallibility".
- **Consequence** (§5): users as testers, verifiers and interpreters,
  "creating a paradox where tools intended to save time instead generated
  additional workload"; the burden "insulates technologies and their
  developers from accountability".
