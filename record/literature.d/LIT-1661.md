# LIT-1661: Institutions: abstract model theory for specification and programming

Goguen, J. A., & Burstall, R. M. (1992). Institutions: Abstract model theory for specification and programming. *Journal of the ACM*, 39(1), 95–146. doi:10.1145/147508.147524.

Not read: a lawful open copy exists but could not be fetched on 2026-10-27 (see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for and from the exchange's description of it. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A9 §7 offered it as the formal frame for the owner's
specification–realization analogy, and for comparing record migrations
when the specification language itself changes: renaming, conservative
refinement, splitting or promoting a type, changing governing rules.
A9's correction stands with it: theory and realization "can be
mathematically related without either being ontologically prior", and
satisfaction is not production. It bears on [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) and
CLAIM-476.
