# LIT-tmplopkp: Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Rules 11, 26 and 37

Supreme Court of the United States (1937, as amended). *Federal Rules of Civil Procedure*, Rule 11 (signing of pleadings; sanctions), Rule 26 (duty to disclose; scope of discovery) and Rule 37 (failure to make disclosures or to cooperate in discovery). Text at the Legal Information Institute.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-tmpupv4l](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpupv4l.md)).*

- Rules 11(b)(1) and 26(g): no paper or request "for any improper purpose,
  such as to harass, cause unnecessary delay, or needlessly increase the
  cost of litigation".
- Rule 26(b)(1): discovery "proportional to the needs of the case",
  considering among six factors "the importance of the issues at stake in
  the action" and "the parties' resources".
- 2015 Committee Note: discovery is not to be used "to wage a war of
  attrition or as a device to coerce a party, whether financially weak or
  affluent."
- Rule 11 "does not apply to disclosures and discovery" (11(d)); Rule 37
  sanctions run to "rendering a default judgment".
