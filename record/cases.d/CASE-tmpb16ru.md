# CASE-tmpb16ru: The contested expression "anti-vax": reported variation in its application, framing, identification and practical consequences, with its conceptual identity and any causal feedback unresolved

## The case

The owner has named this example twice. At U29 of the exchange of
2026-10-22 it was one of the "super stable" organizational perspectives
(workbench 2026/10/22/103821). It was not filed then: [CASE-133](CASE-133.md)'s "What it
does not show" says of the owner's other U29 cases, "No sources for them
were registered, and none is filed", and [CLAIM-423](../claims.d/CLAIM-423.md)'s note says they "are
not filed as cases from the replies' summaries". At U19 of the exchange
"Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e) the owner
named it again: "As a concrete example, consider "anti-vax"". A41
analysed it and cited sources, which the record has not yet registered or
read.

**What is held fixed and what varies.** The expression is held fixed. A41
reports four kinds of variation:

- **The positions it is applied to** (A41 §2). Four hypothetical people:
  one rejects vaccination in general, one opposes a particular mandate,
  one faces practical barriers, one is uncertain about one new vaccine.
  "These positions are not equivalent." A grounds the variety in the WHO
  vaccine-hesitancy framework (confidence, complacency, convenience) and a
  systematic review in *Nature Human Behaviour* on how hesitancy is
  defined; neither is identified further.
- **The framing it carries** (A41 §3). A 2021 study of Australian media,
  2008 to 2018, found articles using *anti-vaxxer* more likely to
  characterize non-vaccinating parents negatively; A calls this "evidence
  of an association between labeling and framing". A 2023 discursive study,
  "Constructing the Anti-Vaxxer" (doi:10.1111/jtsb.12379), found the
  category acquiring attributes not established about the person
  discussed.
- **Its uptake by those labelled** (A41 §4). In a qualitative Australian
  study many non-vaccinating parents rejected the label; a 2017 survey of
  696 Australian vaccine-refusal participants found a positive movement
  identity; a US survey found self-identification with the label.
- **Its effect on inquiry** (A41 §3). In one setting the attribution
  "might prompt the question, *Which vaccine, which concern, and what
  evidence?*"; in another, "the same attribution might terminate inquiry".

The historical movements A41 §2 names, the British Anti-Compulsory
Vaccination League of the 1860s and the Anti-Vaccination Society of
America of 1879, are older than the expression's prominence.

## What it can show

That an expression's stability, the stability of a concept carried by it,
and the stability of perspectives organized around it can come apart in a
real classification. A41 §2: "The label's stability is not evidence that
the extension or inferential role of the concept is stable." A41 §4: "So
the label can function as an unwanted external attribution, an embraced
social identity, and a symbol of opposition—all while apparently naming
one category." It is [CASE-141](CASE-141.md)'s and [CASE-142](CASE-142.md)'s mechanisms at the scale of
a population, and it illustrates [CLAIM-tmpnza8k](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnza8k.md) and [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md).

## What it does not show

- **Any causal loop.** A says so itself: "The full causal loop therefore
  remains a research hypothesis" (A41 §9).
- **That the perspectives A41 §3 tabulates are perspectives in [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s
  sense 1.** Its rows (public-health research, polemical debate, some
  refusal communities, clinical care) are kinds whose realizations are
  particular newsrooms, clinics and forums (the reader's qualification).
- **That the expression names one concept.** That is [QUESTION-tmpdalhx](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpdalhx.md).
- **Anything about vaccine safety or efficacy.** A41 §2: "Context-dependent
  meaning does not make clinical facts relative."
- **Anything from its sources yet.** None is registered or read; the case
  stands on A's reports of them.
