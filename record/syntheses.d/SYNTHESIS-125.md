## The account

On the record's entries the exchange's argument runs as follows. Each link
is attributed to the entry that states it.

1. **A meme can seed organization.** A meme can act as a nucleation site
   ([CLAIM-1218](../claims.d/CLAIM-1218.md), the owner's), so transmissible structures can take
   part in forming organizations ([CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)). How widely a meme is
   copied does not fix how much forms around it ([CLAIM-1230](../claims.d/CLAIM-1230.md)), and a
   seed leaves a maintained organization, in the seeded fold model, only past a threshold and inside a window of the parameter ([CLAIM-1310](../claims.d/CLAIM-1310.md)).
2. **What a meme carries.** On the owner's U4, a meme carries a character
   of a perspective, whole or as one value ([CLAIM-1278](../claims.d/CLAIM-1278.md),
   [CLAIM-1227](../claims.d/CLAIM-1227.md)), so that uptake can help reconstitute a perspective in
   another carrier ([CLAIM-1285](../claims.d/CLAIM-1285.md)). A meme can also act on how later
   interpretations are evaluated ([CLAIM-1214](../claims.d/CLAIM-1214.md)), and carry a portable
   protocol around which contracts form ([CLAIM-1264](../claims.d/CLAIM-1264.md)).
