# LIT-tmpjcuzb: The Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction and Frustration Scales probably do not validly measure need frustration.

Murphy, B. A., Watts, A. L., Baker, Z. G., Don, B. P., Jolink, T. A., Algoe, S. B. (2023). The Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction and Frustration Scales probably do not validly measure need frustration.. *Psychological Assessment, 35(2), 127-139*. doi:10.1037/pas0001193.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-tmpswlms](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpswlms.md)). THEORYs: [THEORY-tmp7y7fk](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7y7fk.md)
(Active), [THEORY-tmp62iwr](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp62iwr.md) (Proposed).

- The BPNSFS frustration items lack thwarting content; two are
  reverse-worded twins of satisfaction items.
- Keying, not need, is the stronger grouping of the items: same-keying
  factor correlations .64–.75, same-need .17–.38; the keying higher-order
  model fits better.
- The frustration scales' stronger tie to ill-being tracks the negative
  keying of ill-being measures, clearly in three of five measures.
- Not dispositive by the authors' own account; their proposed test,
  reverse-worded twins of all 24 items, was not run.

## Standing in the record

A construct-validity objection to the need-frustration scale, that its frustration factor may be largely an item-keying artefact. Distinct from the shared-content objection [THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md) already notes, and new to the record.
