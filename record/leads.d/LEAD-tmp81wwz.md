# LEAD-tmp81wwz: Build resource-bounded profiles of two organizations matched on their ordinary trajectories, under nested probe families and a pair of observing and acting budgets, and find whether and at which budget the profiles tell them apart

## The gap

The record holds three Active, demonstrated results on profiles:

- "Distinctions drawn by restricted Yoneda profiles only grow as nested
  probe families grow", and "the monotonicity needs an every-probe
  comparison, which an averaged distortion is not" ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)).
- "equivalence of possibilities is not equivalence of resource-bounded
  capabilities" ([CLAIM-590](../claims.d/CLAIM-590.md)).
- Observing and acting budgets run in opposite directions, so budgeted
  individuation "is indexed by a pair of budgets, not one" ([CLAIM-557](../claims.d/CLAIM-557.md)).

All three are about configuration spaces. None is applied to an
organization, and [SYNTHESIS-040](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-040.md)'s title holds that "neither isomorphism
nor a profile settles persistence".

## The design, fixed before any run

1. **Two organizations matched on the ordinary course**: [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md)'s
   reciprocally produced and externally reset governing rules (Active),
   whose "ordinary trajectories" are identical and which part under loss,
   repair and producer interventions. If dmarx/PerspectiveLab is not
   attached (the owner's call), a re-implementation of the three
   arrangements at [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md)'s stated parameters.
2. **Nested probe families**: P₀ the ordinary course; P₁ adding state
   perturbations ([CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md)'s audit pulse); P₂ adding loss of one rule;
   P₃ adding producer interventions. Each probe's response is recorded
   probe by probe, not averaged ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)'s condition).
3. **Budgets**: an observing budget (how many probes, how long each is
   read) and an acting budget (how much each probe may disturb), varied
   separately ([CLAIM-557](../claims.d/CLAIM-557.md)).
4. **The profile** of each organization at each (family, budget) pair,
   and the comparison: the first family and budget at which the two
   profiles differ on some probe.

## Expectations, stated before any run

- The profiles agree on P₀ at every budget, and first differ at P₂ or P₃,
  in line with [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md)'s results; by [CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md) they never re-merge as the
  family grows.
- At a fixed family, a larger observing budget separates no sooner than
  the acting budget allows the separating probe at all.
- **Defeaters**, as checks: the profiles part already on P₀ (the match was
  not exact), or part at P₁ while [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md) says only loss and producer
  interventions part the arrangements, or an averaged distortion gives the
  same verdict as the every-probe comparison at every budget, so the
  profile adds nothing to a summary statistic.

## What it cannot show

That profiles settle persistence ([SYNTHESIS-040](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-040.md) holds they do not), or
anything about real organizations: [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md) is a stipulated population.
