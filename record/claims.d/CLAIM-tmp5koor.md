# CLAIM-tmp5koor: In the transmission criterion of identity over time, a maintainer that restores an individual from the organization it finds in it is a channel of transmission, never part of what is transmitted, so replacing the maintainer with an unrelated one breaks no continuation and the criterion is met or failed by the path through the individual itself

**Reading (a), an alternative to [CLAIM-tmp68okc](CLAIM-tmp68okc.md).** Filed for [LEAD-163](../leads.d/LEAD-163.md).

## The slot

[CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md)'s admission case: "A mechanism maintained by one workshop
loses a part and passes to an unrelated workshop that maintains it from
then on, with nothing of the first workshop's maintaining organization
transmitted; let the domain's continuation morphisms ... count it the same
mechanism by continuity of its remaining parts." Its `defeated_if`, second
clause: a succession "in which nothing of that organization was
transmitted and it must still be counted the same individual, as by
continuity of constituents ..., the maintaining organization having been
replaced by one with no causal tie to it." The synthesis review of
2026-11-11 found the case realistic, a car that changes garages, and so a
defeater, "unless 'the relations that keep its repertoire realizable' is
read as the object's internal organization", which layer 4 seemed to
forbid.

The open slot is which organization "that organization" names: the
relations that keep the repertoire realizable, or the processes that
maintain them. The admission case moves from the second ("nothing of the
first workshop's maintaining organization transmitted") to the first
("nothing of that organization was transmitted"). Left open, the move
goes through and the car defeats the claim.

## The claim

The slot is filled by keeping the two apart. What is transmitted is the
organization that keeps the repertoire realizable. A maintainer is a
process that restores that organization when it fails. When the
maintainer restores it from what it finds in the thing, as a garage
repairs a car to the car's own arrangement, the restored relations are
caused by the earlier ones, through the thing. That is a causal path in
[CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s sense: the later organization is not "an organization of the
same kind that arises independently". So a maintainer replaced by an
unrelated one is a change of channel, and breaks nothing, whether or not
anything of the first maintainer's own organization passes to the second.
The argument is [ARG-tmplptzc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmplptzc.md), from [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s newcomers by analogy.

On this reading, layer 4 of [CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md) is read as its title words it:
"the transmitting organization may lie outside the individual". The
processes that carry the organization forward may be external, as
[CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) separates a persistent product from its producer. What they
carry is the individual's.

Consequences:

- **The admission case is not a defeater.** The car's relations pass
  through the car and the new garage. Its continuity of parts is how the
  transmission ran here, not the criterion.
- **Constituents still part from transmission.** A ship repaired plank by
  plank from what each repair finds continues with no plank kept. Parts
  kept and rearranged to an unrelated design do not continue it. So
  "not by its constituents" keeps its content.
- **A failure case for admission remains.** A car destroyed and a new one
  built from the type by a maker with no tie to the token, which a
  stipulated continuation morphism counts as the same car (a registration
  transferred to a replica), has no causal path from the token's
  relations. [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md), [CLAIM-593](CLAIM-593.md), [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) and [CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) hold, and the
  integration fails. The record's criteria do not in fact count such a
  replica the same, so the case is stipulated, not realistic. That is
  what [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test asks for.
- **The residual risk is reassembly.** A car taken wholly apart and put
  back from its own interchangeable parts by the manual, by someone with
  no tie to it, keeps its parts, and its arrangement comes from the type.
  Whether its fit carries the token's relations, or only the type's,
  decides whether it is a continuation. Where a rival successor exists,
  as in the reassembled ship of Theseus, [CLAIM-328](CLAIM-328.md)'s plural succession
  answers it. With no rival, and a verdict that it is the same car, it
  presses this reading's second defeater.

## What it does not say

- **That a maintainer's own organization never matters.** It can matter
  causally: a garage that repairs badly changes the repertoire. A change
  of repertoire is not a break ([CLAIM-593](CLAIM-593.md)'s second direction).
- **That organization held only by the maintainer is part of the
  individual.** A software project's roadmap held by its maintainers and
  not in its code is, on this reading, a cause of what the project
  becomes, not part of what is transmitted. An unrelated team that takes
  the code continues the project, changed. Participants' verdict that "it's
  not the same thing" ([CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md)) is then a cause of succession
  ([CLAIM-617](CLAIM-617.md)), not evidence of a break. Here it parts from reading (b).
- **That [CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) is wrong.** It reads that claim's "that organization
  may lie inside the individual or outside it" as: the processes that
  realize it may lie outside the individual's boundary ([CLAIM-152](CLAIM-152.md)). Read
  instead as saying a maintainer's own organization can be what persists,
  [CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) favours reading (b).

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), the claim can fail while its premises hold. [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)
says nothing about maintainers outside a collective, and [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md) only
that product and producer persist separately. Both hold if restoration
from the thing is not counted a causal path, the second defeater.
