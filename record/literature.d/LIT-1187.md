# LIT-1187: The construction of preference.

Slovic, P. (1995). *American Psychologist* 50(5):364–371. doi:10.1037/0003-066X.50.5.364.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-949](../notes.d/NOTE-949.md).*

- **Reversals.** Choice and pricing, or choice and matching, order the
  same options differently. Las Vegas: equal choice, the $ bet priced
  higher about 88% of the time (p. 365). Highway safety: 67% choose the
  program that saves more lives, 4% of choices inferred from matching do
  (p. 368).
- **Not intransitivity.** About 90% of reversal patterns violate procedure
  invariance, 10% are intransitive; overpricing the high-payoff bet is
  nearly two thirds (p. 367).
- **Mechanisms.** Scale compatibility, an attribute weighing more when it
  shares the response's scale (reversals 41% with money, 24% without), and
  prominence, the important attribute weighing more in choice than in
  matching (pp. 367–369). Both are effects of the response mode on how the
  answer is computed.
- **Construction.** For "important, complex, and perhaps unfamiliar"
  options, preferences "are constructed on the spot" (p. 369).
- **Remedy.** Reversals were eradicated only "in market settings
  characterized by repetition, feedback, and harsh penalties for being
  inconsistent" (p. 366).

## Why this is here

Registered for [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) as the decision-research form of "a
measurement that generates interpretations", with the open question
whether the elicitation changes the judge and not only the response. The
reading answers it for this paper: it shows only the response. Every
result compares procedures, and the one sequential design it mentions,
matching and then choosing in a later session (p. 368), is analysed for the
discrepancy, not for carry-over. So it is evidence for the measurement
context entering the response, the route [TERM-039](../terms.d/TERM-039.md) attributes to A129's
model, and leaves the route into the interpreter's state open. It supports
the synchronic form, which [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) sets aside, and the claim's
diachronic form only indirectly, by showing that a judgement is a
construction. The mechanisms are classical. On the anchor of
[CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md), the one remedy it reports is outcome feedback with
penalties, which is independent scoring arriving through the consequences
of the respondent's own choices. It carries no instruction for
machine-learning practice.
