summary: >-
  Orzechowski, Tawfiq and Kosciolek (2026), What Does an Ontology Catch?
  Curated, LLM-Generated and Data-Derived Ontologies as Checkers of LLM
  Agents over Knowledge Graphs. LLM agents answering questions over
  knowledge graphs with SPARQL are often wrapped in checks derived from the
  graph's ontology. The authors make the checker the object of study: they
  compare checks from a curated ontology, LLM-generated ontologies
  (including ones written from a summary with identifiers masked) and a
  data-derived ontology, used as prompt context, as a check (a flag triggers
  one retry), or both, across seven open-weight and proprietary models, with
  confirmatory comparisons pre-registered on UniProt and exploratory results
  on other graphs. Every verdict is scored against a known correct answer,
  so catches, misses, false alarms and corruptions of correct answers are
  all counted, and every check is compared with a baseline that simply
  retries empty results. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
