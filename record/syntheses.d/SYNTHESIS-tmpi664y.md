# SYNTHESIS-tmpi664y: In an organization, goal-conduciveness valence is a persistent potential of the negotiation among its perspectives that ranks its own states by their bearing on its maintenance: what a record of wins can show is a class of such orderings, read only on the contests the record admits; the concession that the regime tracks wanting places the account at goal conduciveness, with each perspective holding a valence of its own and the system's read on what is granted; and its consequences on contest, intensity and ambivalence stand under objections that are open or written in

*A part account of the organizational-affect line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)): the potential account of valence and its tests. The line's account is built over it and its two sibling parts, [SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0](SYNTHESIS-tmpa0kd0.md) (corporate affect) and [SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3](SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3.md) (feeling and access).*

## The account

**The definition.** [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md), the owner's reframing of 2026-10-23
(thesis, interpretive, Proposed), and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) (Proposed), which carries
its definitions. Appraisal is the negotiation among an organization's
perspectives. A common currency is that negotiation admitting an
ordering, in the weakest of [TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md)'s four senses, ordinal
extendibility. Goal-conduciveness valence is a regime in which an
ordering of the system's own states by their bearing on its maintenance
prevails, as a persistent potential ([TERM-078](../terms.d/TERM-078.md), Active) over a non-empty
set of contested pairs. Its sign is whether the system runs ahead of or
behind a reference course along that ordering; its intensity is the
share of contested wins that agree with it; ambivalence, where no
ordering absorbs the conflicts, is not neutrality, where one prevails and
the system keeps to its course. [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) names its precedents (Lewis,
Moors, Marsella and Gratch, Carver and Scheier) and says what it adds:
the competitors are perspectives with no regulator above them, the
competition is not assumed to settle on a scale, and what prevails is an
ordering of a particular kind. "Neither Lewis nor Scherer gives the
regime a sign." The definition is functional: [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) is "Not a felt
state".

**What a record of wins can show.** [CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md) (demonstrated, Active;
also formal-apparatus) proves that on a finite set a potential exists
exactly when no chain of winning moves cycles, and then calibrates what
follows: the Hodge gradient can reverse a comparison the wins settle, a
record fixes a class of potentials and not one (part 7), the sign that
class gives can be set-valued (part 10), and an ordering fitted to a
record overstates its agreement there (part 11). [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md)
(demonstrated, Active; also formal-apparatus) separates an ordering
from a currency: they coincide on a finite set at an instant; a graded
lexicographic override has an ordering and no currency; a threshold
override with a discrimination threshold can cycle and has no ordering;
and successive currencies need not share exchange rates. [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) and
[CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) are written on these results: the potential is reported as a
class and identified by intervention, sign and intensity are reported
across the class, and the Hodge decomposition is kept for margins
([TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md), "Identification"; [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s revision of 2026-10-23, the
review). [TERM-078](../terms.d/TERM-078.md) adds that an approximate persistent potential is no
Lyapunov function, so resumption is read off the record.

**Veit and ALARM.** [CLAIM-435](../claims.d/CLAIM-435.md) (thesis, interpretive, Proposed; rests on
[CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md)) recasts Veit's common currency and ALARM's
override as compensatory and overriding regimes of one potential
ordering, so that their rivalry becomes whether the earliest potential
orderings are continuous, with a trade-off test whose design it gives.
Its note of 2026-10-29: "no held reading states either author's proposal
alone".

**Wanting, liking and whose valence.** [CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md) (counter, empirical,
Proposed), raised by the reading of Berridge and Kringelbach, objects
that potential as defined tracks 'wanting', not 'liking'. It was
conceded at the owner's direction on 2026-10-23, and the concession is
carried by the revision of [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) from "hedonic" to
"goal-conduciveness" valence ([CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md)'s note of 2026-10-27: "a
concession is not credence"). [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md) (thesis, interpretive, Proposed),
the reply the owner asked for, places the account on the component
process model's levels of valence: sign is goal conduciveness, the
potential's strength tracks 'wanting', and 'liking' is intrinsic
pleasantness, a separate appraisal. Its note of 2026-10-23 records two
pressures from incentive sensitization, the kind clause and the
relapsing addict. [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) (the owner's, thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)) takes 'wanting' and 'liking' as saliences
relative to pursuit and consummation, makes valence relative to a
perspective, and makes the system's valence that of the perspective
whose ordering is the potential, reported for the class where the
record does not single one out ([CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md), part 7). It answers the
relapsing addict and the kind clause on [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md)'s behalf. [CLAIM-447](../claims.d/CLAIM-447.md)
(the owner's, thesis, interpretive, Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md); also
will-organization) says what the pursuit process ranks, futures under
candidate interventions, keeps cue-triggered wanting as a second level,
and separates a process's causal model, its wanting and its practical
efficacy.

**Claims and grants.** [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md) (the assistant's, thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) and [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md); also will-organization)
puts Frijda's emotion on the side of the precedence a motive claims, a
perspective's valence on the claim side, and the system's valence on
what the negotiation grants, so that suspension, as mindfulness is said
to work, changes the system's valence and not the perspectives'.
[CLAIM-527](../claims.d/CLAIM-527.md) (thesis, interpretive, Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and
[CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md); also will-organization), filed from the readings of Massumi
and of Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy, adds a third quantity, viable
reach, and holds that "an organization can run ahead along its prevailing
ordering while its viable reach contracts"; it names protective lock-in
([TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md)) and [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md) as of that shape and [QUESTION-267](../questions.d/QUESTION-267.md)'s proxy firm
as another. [CLAIM-548](../claims.d/CLAIM-548.md) (the owner's point at U18, A's wording; thesis,
interpretive, Active; primary line will-organization; rests on
[CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)) separates a process's activation, the authority granted to
it and its efficacy, so that a threshold on expected net benefit is a
special case; it records that "The record already parts from Moors on the
common currency ([CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md))". [TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md) (Proposed; the owner's question,
A's wording) defines a motivating perspective from parts its table
attributes to [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md), [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md), [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md), [TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md), [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md),
[CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md) and [CLAIM-447](../claims.d/CLAIM-447.md), and names its own idle condition: every use
rewritable "as [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) plus [TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md) with nothing lost". [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md)
(the owner's, thesis, interpretive, Proposed; primary line
will-organization) is a candidate account of avoidance-maintained
anxiety whose five quantities set threat salience, a claim, apart from
defensive authority, a grant, continuing [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md); [CLAIM-447](../claims.d/CLAIM-447.md)'s dated
part of 2026-10-24 and [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md)'s note of the same date cite it.

**Objections to the consequences.**
- **Contest, not frustration.** [CLAIM-449](../claims.d/CLAIM-449.md) (A's, counter, interpretive,
  Active) found that "no frustration, no valence" ran together contested
  rankings and jointly infeasible demands. It is written into [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)
  (version 2) and [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) (version 4), whose first consequence now
  reads "No contest, no valence", and the absorbed writer ([CASE-051](../cases.d/CASE-051.md)) is
  left without goal-conduciveness valence: "the account leaves it
  undefined".
- **Flow.** [CLAIM-441](../claims.d/CLAIM-441.md) (the assistant's, counter, interpretive, Proposed;
  rests on [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) of will-organization) holds that flow, reported as
  positive with nothing contested, tells against the nonvacuity
  condition, and names four exits. Its note of 2026-10-30, on
  Abuhamdeh's review: "exit 3 is attributed, not shown". [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s note
  of 2026-10-30 and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)'s of the same date record it as unanswered.
- **Intensity.** [CLAIM-468](../claims.d/CLAIM-468.md) (counter, interpretive, Proposed), the
  difficulty [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s third clause records against itself: in Hesp et
  al.'s model negative valence is a fall in the preference ordering's
  precision, and positive affect lowers a goal's priority where goals
  compete (Orehek et al.). [CLAIM-505](../claims.d/CLAIM-505.md) (counter, interpretive, Proposed)
  objects to [CLAIM-468](../claims.d/CLAIM-468.md) that a goal's lowered priority need not be a
  weaker hold of the prevailing ordering, on Carver's satisficing
  account. [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s note of 2026-10-30: "If it cannot be argued,
  clause 3 is narrowed to negative valence or dropped."
- **Coherence by admission.** [CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md) (counter, interpretive, Active;
  also distributed-agency) holds that where a legibility regime selects
  which contests are recorded, an acyclic record can be coherent for the
  wrong reason. It is written into [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s part 3: a potential is
  read as the organization's "only on a record shown to admit the
  contests that bear on it". [CASE-221](../cases.d/CASE-221.md) (stipulated, Proposed; the reader's
  construction) builds its existence half in the smallest case, a chair
  admitting two of a majority cycle's three contests.
- **Cycles.** [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md) (the owner's question, thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed; rests on [CLAIM-434](../claims.d/CLAIM-434.md); also distributed-agency) reads a cyclic
  record as organizational nonintegrability, distinct from a
  perspective's preference instability and from governance instability,
  and holds that "ambivalence and nonintegrability entail neither one
  another". [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s note of 2026-10-23 says it "narrows consequence
  4".

**What stays open.** How potential is measured, and whether potential
on conflicts won agrees with potential as a disposition ([QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md),
Open, with an ordinal procedure in seven steps). Whether felt valence
can be operationalized by three layers measured apart, potential, a
hedonic marker and the system's model of its potential ([QUESTION-266](../questions.d/QUESTION-266.md),
Open). Whether a potential keyed to a proxy for maintenance is valence
([QUESTION-267](../questions.d/QUESTION-267.md), Open; its note of 2026-10-23 offers a candidate answer, a
proxy-keyed persistent potential as valence of the pursuit perspective
and a protective lock-in, "by analogy"). Whether the will-organization's
other relations change valence with the record of wins fixed
([QUESTION-269](../questions.d/QUESTION-269.md), Open).

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each link above is in the entry cited for it.
Three candidates within the part were tried against [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s routes.

- **Hypothesis: the system's valence is no more than the prevailing
  perspective's.** Stated as open in [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md)'s "What it does not say":
  "the system's may be nothing beyond the prevailing perspective's".
- **Derived: a lexicographic semiorder's cycles and [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md)'s
  diagnostics.** [CLAIM-436](../claims.d/CLAIM-436.md) (part 3b) and [CLAIM-435](../claims.d/CLAIM-435.md) read cycles along a
  chain of near-threshold options as a threshold override; [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md) reads
  cyclic wins as nonintegrability among perspectives, and its diagnostic
  for a single process is reversal under matched conditions, which a
  semiorder never shows. Joined, they say a cycle does not by itself tell
  the two apart. That turns on a free parameter, how perspectives are
  individuated, which [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md), [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md) leave to
  [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md). If the override's two dimensions are two perspectives,
  [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md) classifies the cycle rightly and nothing is added. If the
  override is one process, the case is evidence against [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md)'s
  diagnostics, at the second clause of its own defeat condition, not an
  integration. Not filed.
- **Integration: proxy-keyed potential as lock-in.** Stated: [QUESTION-267](../questions.d/QUESTION-267.md)'s
  note of 2026-10-23 and [CLAIM-527](../claims.d/CLAIM-527.md).

## Not stated on the record

- **Which of [CLAIM-441](../claims.d/CLAIM-441.md)'s exits holds.** It needs in-episode reports of
  flow or a measure separating intrinsic pleasantness from goal
  conduciveness ([CLAIM-441](../claims.d/CLAIM-441.md)'s note of 2026-10-30).
- **Whether intensity is the potential's strength.** [CLAIM-468](../claims.d/CLAIM-468.md) and
  [CLAIM-505](../claims.d/CLAIM-505.md) are both open, and no reading measures where the effort
  released by positive affect goes ([CLAIM-505](../claims.d/CLAIM-505.md)'s note).
- **Whether a perspective's valence stays fixed when grants change.**
  [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md) says a change in grants with claims fixed "leaves the
  perspectives' emotions and valences as they were". [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) defines a
  perspective's valence as "its ranking and its progress against its own
  reference course", and progress along a ranking depends on the course
  the grants produce. No entry reconciles the two.
- **Ambivalence after [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md).** [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s note of 2026-10-23 says
  [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md) narrows consequence 4, but consequence 4 and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)'s
  ambivalence clause still read ambivalence as conflict no ordering
  absorbs, which is [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md)'s nonintegrability.
- **Whether a semiorder's cycles are one process's or several
  perspectives'.** See the second candidate above.
- **How perspectives are individuated** without appeal to the
  dissociations they explain: [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md)'s second defeater, unanswered.

- That the regime is felt: [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md) are functional, and
  feeling is [SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3](SYNTHESIS-tmpkcch3.md)'s.

  [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md)).
- That a potential must exist. [CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md): "That a potential is the
  default and a cycle a pathology. They are regimes."
