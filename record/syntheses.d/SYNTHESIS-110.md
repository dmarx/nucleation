# SYNTHESIS-110: Whether a distinction must be drawn and whether it earns a name are settled in the record by different entries, the first by what acceptable actions require and the second by evidence against Occam cost and by retrieval under a deadline, which pull in opposite directions with copy count

*A thematic account, across lines, of principle P4 of the owner's manuscript (scratch/manuscripts/from-obstruction-to-reorganization/, §7.1, on its own branch). Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which [ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**When a distinction must be drawn.** [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) (Active, demonstrated, thesis): an organization acting on what it observes has a policy acceptable in every circumstance "exactly when, for each observation, the circumstances it cannot tell apart share an acceptable action". So "which distinctions must be made legible depends on which acceptable action the organization commits to".

**When a candidate structure is kept as a unit,** by evidence. [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md) (Active, demonstrated, thesis): "reify when d'² exceeds the log prior-to-posterior variance ratio", where the model with the candidate stays regular at the parameters that switch it off. [CLAIM-188](../claims.d/CLAIM-188.md) (Active, demonstrated, counter) marks the rule's limit. Where the model is singular there, the rule's Occam term overcharges keeping the candidate.

**When a category is carved,** by retrieval. [CLAIM-307](../claims.d/CLAIM-307.md) (Active, demonstrated, thesis): evidence and retrieval pressure "are different criteria for carving a category, and they move in opposite directions with copy count", so "the bins a deadline forces are often the ones the data least supports".

**A third criterion, open.** [QUESTION-113](../questions.d/QUESTION-113.md) (Open) asks whether a category's naming meets the data's support, a deadline's force, or survival under refinement and compression, and whether the three ever pick the same categories.

**A systemic criterion, Proposed.** [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md) (Proposed, interpretive) holds that classes a process keeps, by identifying micro-states by what they do, become the units of further computation where they are closed under the process's operations and reused.

## Not stated on the record

- **Naming a distinction is a separate decision from drawing it.** The manuscript's P4 stated this. No component separates naming from drawing. [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md)'s reification is whether to keep the structure in the model at all, and [CLAIM-307](../claims.d/CLAIM-307.md) is about carving. What the entries do show is that [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md)'s test for drawing and [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md)'s and [CLAIM-307](../claims.d/CLAIM-307.md)'s tests take different inputs, which is a trivial difference, not a claim.
- **"Reuse" as the third criterion.** P4 put reuse, which is [CLAIM-762](../claims.d/CLAIM-762.md)'s condition, in the place of [QUESTION-113](../questions.d/QUESTION-113.md)'s survival under refinement and compression.

## What it does not say

That the criteria ever agree. That is [QUESTION-113](../questions.d/QUESTION-113.md), open.

## Note of 2026-11-21: the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws"

Filed since, and bearing on this account's evidence criterion. For its next version to weigh:

- **Sloppiness beside singularity.** CLAIM-188 corrects CLAIM-154's Occam term where the model is *singular* at the parameters that switch a candidate off. The record now defines *sloppy* separately: a Fisher spectrum spread over many decades with no gap (TERM-tmptiim0, at the owner's suggestion). It holds that the two are different properties: a model can be "sloppy everywhere and singular nowhere or singular everywhere with no spread" (CLAIM-tmpcgvcp, A's).
- **The Occam cost at a finite sample.** At a finite sample, a direction too sloppy to resolve "contributes to the learning coefficient no more than a flat one". In a regular sloppy model the coefficient rises by about ½ for each direction resolved (CLAIM-tmpcg4uu, the assistant's). Read in CLAIM-154's terms, the cost of keeping a candidate structure is then charged on the directions the sample resolves, so what the evidence criterion names moves with the sample. That reading is the assistant's, not CLAIM-tmpcg4uu's.
- **Keeping and dropping units.** CLAIM-tmpnu4ar (A's, supposed, its ground unread) proposes that model reduction and feature differentiation are opposite movements through one stratified space. Reduction moves toward the model manifold's boundaries, where parameters stop mattering; differentiation moves away from singular strata, where redundant features become separately usable. If held, it bears on when a unit is dropped as well as when one is kept.
- **A threshold for a unit in a learner.** In CLAIM-tmp9bp04 (A's), a factored feature becomes part of the predictor only when its signal exceeds the weight decay. That is an evidence-against-cost threshold of this account's first kind, in a trained model. The reading is the assistant's.

Readings of the sloppiness papers and of the local learning coefficient are being filed. This account's "Not stated on the record" is unaffected: no new entry separates naming from drawing. No component, status or link of this account is changed. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session).
