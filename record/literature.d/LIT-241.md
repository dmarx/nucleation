# LIT-241: Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction (Wikipedia)

Wikipedia contributors (2026), *Wikipedia* — <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelfand%E2%80%93Naimark%E2%80%93Segal_construction>

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-857](../notes.d/NOTE-857.md).*

- **Construction (after Kadison, Thm 4.5.2).** For a state ρ on a
  C*-algebra A, ⟨a, b⟩ = ρ(b*a) is a semi-inner product on A. Its null
  elements form a left ideal I; A/I completed is the Hilbert space H; A
  acts by π(a)(b + I) = ab + I; the class of 1 (or the limit of an
  approximate identity) is a unit cyclic vector ξ with
  ρ(a) = ⟨π(a)ξ, ξ⟩.
- **Uniqueness (Kadison, Prop. 4.5.3, stated not proved).** Two cyclic
  representations whose cyclic vectors reproduce the same state are
  unitarily equivalent, by the unitary that maps π(a)ξ to π′(a)ξ′.
- **Purity.** The GNS representation is irreducible iff the state is an
  extreme point; for C(X) the states are probability measures and the
  pure ones point masses.
- **Significance.** It is the core of the Gelfand–Naimark theorem, builds
  the universal representation, and is generalized by Stinespring's
  theorem.
- **What it does not say.** Nothing on positive-definite kernels, RKHS,
  real scalars, or unbounded observables.
