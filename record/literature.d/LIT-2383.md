# LIT-2383: Operationalizing the Intolerable Suffering Criterion in Advance Requests for Medical Assistance in Dying for People Living with Dementia in Canada

Hayden P. Nix (2024). "Operationalizing the Intolerable Suffering Criterion in Advance Requests for Medical Assistance in Dying for People Living with Dementia in Canada." *Cambridge Quarterly of Healthcare Ethics 35(2): 94-100*. DOI-10.1017/s0963180124000264.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1731](../notes.d/NOTE-1731.md)). A short conceptual analysis that locates
the obstacle to advance-request MAID in dementia not in personal identity or
the authority of the earlier self but in the conjunction of (a) a
duty-of-care framing that requires occurrent intolerable suffering and (b) a
subjective concept of suffering that only the now-incapable patient can
apply.
