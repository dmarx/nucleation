# LIT-2661: From Street-Level to System-Level Bureaucracies: How Information and Communication Technology Is Transforming Administrative Discretion and Constitutional Control

Mark Bovens and Stavros Zouridis (2002). From Street-Level to System-Level Bureaucracies: How Information and Communication Technology is Transforming Administrative Discretion and Constitutional Control. *Public Administration Review* 62(2): 174–184. DOI 10.1111/0033-3352.00168.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of 2026-11-17; the reading of 2026-11-16 is below.*

- "The street level bureaucrats have vanished." … "System analysts and
  software designers are the key actors in these executive agencies"
  (abstract).
- Automating a step "demands the standardization and formalization of the
  preceding steps" (ms p. 9); a living person sees a case only on
  objection or appeal.
- The designers' discretion is "mainly a matter of political control and
  accountability rather than of legality and the rule of law" (ms p. 17),
  and the new arbitrariness is "excessive rigidity" (ms p. 18).
