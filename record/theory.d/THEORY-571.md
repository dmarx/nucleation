# THEORY-571: A brain state is better characterised by how its activity moves among a repertoire of weakly coupled metastable substates on a landscape constrained by the structural connectome, their occupancy, dwell times and transitions, than by static connectivity; health is a metastable balance, depression a landscape of over-deep attractors and psychedelic states a flattened one, at opposite ends of one spectrum

## Source

Jakub Vohryzek, Joana Cabral, Peter Vuust, Gustavo Deco and Morten L.
Kringelbach (2022), "Understanding brain states across spacetime informed
by whole-brain modelling", *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal
Society A* 380, 20210247, [LIT-080](../literature.d/LIT-080.md), read in full in [NOTE-054](../notes.d/NOTE-054.md).

## What was actually shown

Nothing new; this is a review. What it assembles:

**The description.** Time-varying functional connectivity is summarised
as a repertoire of substates with fractional occupancy, dwell time and
transition probabilities (§2, Fig. 2a). Connectome harmonics, the
eigenvectors of the structural connectome's graph Laplacian, give a basis
for activity patterns, and the slowest reproduce resting-state networks.

**The models (§3).** Whole-brain models fit empirical functional
connectivity and its dynamics best with small-world topology, weak
coupling and local nodes at the edge of a supercritical Hopf bifurcation
with noise. Structured noise on structural connectivity explains static
connectivity but not its non-stationarity, which is the stated reason for
nonlinear local dynamics.

**Psychedelics (§6a, cited).** Broader connectome-harmonic repertoire
under LSD and psilocybin; an enhanced repertoire of substates under
psilocybin; higher MEG signal diversity under LSD, psilocybin and
ketamine; within-network disintegration with between-network cohesion.

**Depression (§6b, cited).** Remitted-MDD patients spend less time in a
frontoparietal, default-mode, salience and striatal network and more in a
global pattern; two further studies report more within-DMN connectivity
and more global synchrony and temporal stability in MDD.

**The synthesis (Conclusion, Fig. 6).** An n-dimensional landscape of
substates; depression and psychedelics placed at opposite ends, deepened
against flattened; causal models with receptor maps proposed to find
perturbations that move between states.

**What could have come out otherwise.** Nothing in the review was tested
against the alternative. That is why this is Proposed.

## What this does not say

- **That depression is rigid dynamics.** The abstract says it flatly; §6b
  closes with "further studies will be required", and one of its three
  studies is of remitted patients. [NOTE-054](../notes.d/NOTE-054.md) advises citing the primary
  studies, after checking them, not this review.
- **That the two states lie on one axis.** No landscape is estimated from
  data, and no study places both.
- **That transitions are discrete switches.** The substate description is
  assumed, not tested against a continuous model.
- **That the brain is critical.** Criticality and metastability are
  premises taken from cited power-law work, not re-examined against its
  critiques.
- **That whole-brain models can design treatments.** The step needs
  out-of-sample prediction of an intervention's effect, which is not
  reported.
