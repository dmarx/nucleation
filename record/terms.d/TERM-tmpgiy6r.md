# TERM-tmpgiy6r: operational standing, of a process under an organizational arrangement: its maintained eligibility to have specified claims upon another process's activity, or upon their shared governance, become consequential; distinct from the claim it asserts, the authority it effectively exercises and moral standing, and not reducible to its priority

## Definition

A16 §3: "We can provisionally define **operational standing** as a
process's maintained eligibility, under an organizational arrangement, to
have specified claims upon another process's activity or upon their shared
governance become consequential. This need not imply moral consideration,
consciousness or personhood."

Four quantities that "can come apart":

| Property | A16's gloss |
|---|---|
| Asserted claim | "A process requests priority or resources." |
| Operational standing | "The arrangement recognizes its eligibility to make that request consequential." |
| Effective authority | "The request actually influences resource allocation or activity." |
| Moral standing | "The process or its bearer is owed moral consideration." |

"A process might have contractual standing but be systematically starved
of resources. Another might acquire substantial effective authority by
monopolizing a resource without possessing corresponding contractual
entitlement."

## Not priority

"operational standing is not reducible to priority. A low-priority process
could possess a protected guarantee of eventual service, an entitlement to
escalate a missed deadline, or immunity from arbitrary cancellation.
Standing concerns the structure of recognized claims, not simply one's
position in a queue."

## Relations

- It refines what the owner's U14 proposed ([CLAIM-tmpkvzcn](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkvzcn.md)): the
  prioritization structure is one way a contract secures standing, not
  what standing is.
- It sits beside [TERM-083](TERM-083.md) (moral standing), which it explicitly does not
  imply, and beside A15's kinds of standing (claim-right, consultative,
  intervention, consent, adjudicative, remedial), which [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) and
  [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) hold in the moral and institutional case.
- Asserted claim against effective authority is [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s split of
  claimed precedence from granted authority; A16 says the term "extends
  the distinction between conferral and grant" of [TERM-103](TERM-103.md) and [TERM-101](TERM-101.md).
  The new quantity is the middle one: an eligibility the arrangement
  recognizes, which can hold with no effective authority and be absent
  where effective authority is large.
- A18 §4 applies it to inquiry: standing to submit a query, reserve
  investigative resources, challenge an answer or an acceptance criterion
  ([CLAIM-tmp2kfwd](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp2kfwd.md)).

## What it leaves open

What makes an eligibility "maintained" and "recognized" without a
representing process is not said; [CLAIM-tmp7hqtl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7hqtl.md)'s conditions for a
contract-like relation are A16's answer for the arrangement that confers
it.
