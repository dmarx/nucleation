The owner asked how well the record covers conversational implicature and
Grice. The answer was that it covers both only through rational speech acts.
[LIT-816](../../record/literature.d/LIT-816.md), [LIT-821](../../record/literature.d/LIT-821.md) and [LIT-809](../../record/literature.d/LIT-809.md) are read, and [THEORY-172](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-172.md) rests on them. No work
by Grice, by the neo-Griceans or by relevance theorists is held. [CLAIM-221](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-221.md)
already names the gap: its objection to [CLAIM-151](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) appeals to the
intention-based account of illocution of Grice, Strawson and Bach and
Harnish. The record holds none of them, so the defeat condition of
[CLAIM-151](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) cannot fire. [QUESTION-073](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-073.md) asks for the same works. The owner
asked for them to be filed.

## What is filed

The four works [CLAIM-221](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-221.md) and [QUESTION-073](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-073.md) name:

- Grice, "Meaning" (1957), *The Philosophical Review*;
- Grice, "Logic and Conversation" (1975), in Cole and Morgan (eds.),
  *Syntax and Semantics 3*;
- Strawson, "Intention and Convention in Speech Acts" (1964), *The
  Philosophical Review*;
- Bach and Harnish, *Linguistic Communication and Speech Acts* (1979),
  MIT Press.

`pragmatics` is the primary tag for each ([ADR-034](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-034.md)). A grep of the
anthology's record/ (clone of 2026-10-13, commit d1573d2) for "Grice",
"Strawson", "Harnish", "Logic and Conversation" and "implicature" found
nothing, so these entries are for this record only.

Each entry says whether a lawful full text was reachable and, if it was,
how deeply it was read.
