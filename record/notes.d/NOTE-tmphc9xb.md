# NOTE-tmphc9xb: Lu et al. — Towards end-to-end automation of AI research

## Contribution

An integrated pipeline from ideation to paper and review. A model-based reviewer is validated against human acceptance decisions and then used as the fitness measure for comparing system configurations. A blind peer-review trial at a workshop.

## Key results

- One of three submissions exceeded the workshop's average acceptance threshold. Abstract: "the manuscript generated by this AI system passed the first round of peer review for a workshop of a top-tier machine learning conference. The workshop had an acceptance rate of 70%."
- Humans selected what was submitted. Human evaluation results, p. 916: "We manually filtered the most promising outputs at each stage".
- The internal judgement of the authors' own team, p. 916: "none met the higher bar for a main ICLR conference publication".
- The reviewer's agreement with conference decisions (Table 1). Balanced accuracy is 0.69 ± 0.04 on 2017–2024 papers and 0.66 ± 0.03 on the post-cutoff 2025 papers, against 0.66 for the NeurIPS human consistency experiment. Possible contamination is acknowledged (p. 916): "data contamination may exist".
- Quality scales with model and compute. The Fig. 1b fit is R = 0.517, P < 0.00001. Fig. 3c: "Scaling the number of experimental nodes in the agentic tree search shows improvements". Both are measured by the Automated Reviewer, which is the system's own judge.
- Failure modes (Limitations, p. 917): "the generation of naive or underdeveloped ideas, incorrect implementations of the main idea, a lack of deep methodological rigour" and "many types of hallucinations, such as inaccurate citations".
- Memory: an "experimental journal" is kept, in Methods: "This journal serves as a form of memory and informs the subsequent steps in the experimental plan."

## Assumptions

- That agreement with conference accept/reject decisions validates a reviewer as a judge of scientific quality.
- That workshop acceptance, at a 70% rate, is a meaningful bar. The authors themselves call it lower.
- That trends judged by the system's own reviewer track real quality. The paper does not test this independently beyond the three human-reviewed papers.

## Concepts

Automated Reviewer, an LLM ensemble of five reviews plus a meta-review. Idea archive (open-endedness). Template-based vs template-free mode. Experiment progress manager with four stages. Agentic tree search with buggy and non-buggy nodes. "AI scientist Turing test".

## Bearing on the record

The exchange says it "Demonstrates a pipeline for generating ML ideas, implementing experiments, analyzing outputs and drafting research papers". It also says the workshop result "is evidence of workflow capability, not proof of a generally reliable autonomous scientist; the reported workshop acceptance rate was 70%". **Accurate.** The paper reports the 70% rate itself. One point the exchange omits: humans filtered outputs at each stage before submission. The LIT summary carries no characterization beyond the title.

The LIT names no codes. The content curation (021453) gives the exchange's A14 survey of AI-assisted theory generation as the place these works sit, and says that framing was left "belonging in their readings". No new entry cites [LIT-2303](../literature.d/LIT-2303.md).
- **[QUESTION-tmp7a7l4](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp7a7l4.md)** (can a collective become a better improver): **leaves open**. The AI Scientist improves a product, papers, under a fixed improver, and the improvement measure is its own reviewer. It is an instance of A43's level 1, a better work product, evaluated by an internal judge. That is the evaluation pattern [LIT-2273](../literature.d/LIT-2273.md) (unread) is said to criticize.
- **[CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md)** (probes that supply an optimized system's training signal cannot also certify what it preserves): **illustrates, does not test**. The same Automated Reviewer serves as fitness signal (Fig. 1b, Fig. 3c) and as evaluation. The paper's only independent check is three workshop submissions.
- **[QUESTION-271](../questions.d/QUESTION-271.md) / [CASE-144](../cases.d/CASE-144.md)** (evaluation without capture): bears as an example. The authors validate the judge against human decisions once, then rely on it.
