# CLAIM-tmppqo06: Northstar simply prefers speed: the governing relations are revealed preferences of the organization, not a separate organization of efficacy

## The claim

"Perhaps the company simply values safety more highly", or speed: every
governing relation redescribed as one more preference.

## Why it is still open

The paper's reply ([CLAIM-tmpzeoja](CLAIM-tmpzeoja.md)) holds members' attitudes fixed and
changes authority. But "Northstar prefers speed" is most naturally an
aggregate, revealed preference of the organization, and constant individual
attitudes do not refute that reading. Nothing in the manuscript answers it.
