# THEORY-453: Basic physical objects can be nothing beyond the bearers of their relations: relations give them identity conditions wherever they discern them, and where nothing discerns them, as with entangled systems of one kind or points of a symmetric space-time, a primitive numerical diversity that is not a thisness suffices; general relativity's tensor and fibre-bundle formulations fit this reading but do not require it

## Source

Michael Esfeld and Vincent Lam (2008), "Moderate structural realism about
space-time", [LIT-895](../literature.d/LIT-895.md), *Synthese* 160(1): 27–46, read in full in [NOTE-695](../notes.d/NOTE-695.md).
Page numbers are the journal's.

## What was actually shown

**The position (§1).** The "master argument" for intrinsic properties has two
premises: relations require relata, and relata "have to be something in
themselves", with intrinsic properties beyond their relations (p. 29). Epistemic
structural realism accepts both, radical ontic structural realism rejects both,
and moderate structural realism accepts the first and rejects the second (p. 31).
"An object as such is nothing but that what bears the relations" (p. 32). The
denial concerns fundamental intrinsic properties of basic objects; composites
keep theirs (p. 28).

**Relations as identity conditions.** "Insofar as intrinsic properties account
for identity conditions, relations can perform that task as well" (p. 32).

**Where nothing discerns.** Entangled quantum systems of one kind are
indistinguishable and "do not have an identity in time". Space-time points can
stand in the same relations and be numerically distinct, "on certain symmetry
assumptions about space-time" (p. 33). For these, "one can simply accept a
numerical distinction (diversity) … as primitive". It tells us "that there is a
number of objects that is greater than one … and that is all that it tells us".
It "is not a primitive thisness, for it does not establish an identity in
time—or any other sort of an identity" (p. 33). Haecceity is rejected
separately, by the hole argument.

**No priority (p. 34).** Relations cannot be prior, because bundles fail to
distinguish entangled systems and symmetric points. Objects cannot be prior,
because that "would commit us to primitive thisness". So neither is.

**General relativity (§§2–3).** Epistemic structural realism individuates points
independently of the metric, and the hole argument punishes that (pp. 35–36).
Radical realism faces "What does a space-time relation … relate?" (p. 36). The
relata are "space-time points (not bare manifold points!)", whose "structural
identity" the metric fixes (p. 37). In the frame-bundle formulation a section
defines the points, and the hole argument cannot even be formulated (pp. 40–41).
The view is a non-reductive, "sophisticated" substantivalism (p. 43).

**What could have come out otherwise.** Indiscernible relata could have forced a
choice between thisness and elimination. The paper's reply is a third option,
and whether that option is stable is what remains open.

## What this does not say

- **That GR requires the view.** The formulations "naturally receive" it (p. 41).
  Leibniz equivalence and Stachel's bundle reading equally support other
  positions.
- **That the metric fixes which point is which in every space-time.** §2 says it
  "completely determines the structural identity" (p. 37), but §1's symmetric
  case is an exception, and the Bergmann–Komar labelling it cites works only
  "for space-times with no symmetries" and through "explicit breaking of active
  general covariance" (p. 39). In the familiar exact solutions, primitive
  diversity does the work.
- **That primitive diversity escapes Chakravartty's dilemma.** The paper denies
  it is an identity. Whether that meets the dilemma is a judgement, not a result
  ([NOTE-695](../notes.d/NOTE-695.md)).
- **That mass and charge are not intrinsic.** The paper never discusses
  state-independent properties.
- **That the no-priority argument is complete.** It eliminates bundles and
  thisness only. Weak discernibility, partial dependence and grounding accounts
  are not considered.
- **That it survives the loss of points.** Algebraic GR and quantum gravity are
  deferred to "a separate paper" (p. 44).
