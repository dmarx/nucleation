**Corrections the lookups made to the exchange.**

- Kwon and Perchet's title, as U29 and U30 give it, lacks its subtitle:
  it is "Online Learning and Blackwell Approachability with Partial
  Monitoring: Optimal Convergence Rates", AISTATS 2017, PMLR 54: 604–613.
  The rates U29 reports (T^-1/2 for signals independent of the decision,
  T^-1/3 for general signals) match the abstract. There is no DOI or
  arXiv copy, so it is filed by URL.
- The sequential probability ratio test that U35 cites as "classical",
  with no author, is Wald's 1945 paper in the Annals of Mathematical
  Statistics, registered as the primary source. The record held the test
  only as restated by Bogacz et al. ([LIT-569](../../record/literature.d/LIT-569.md)) and [THEORY-068](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-068.md).
- The "classical consensus and DeGroot models" of U36, cited without a
  title, are DeGroot's "Reaching a Consensus" (JASA 1974). The record
  read the model only through later work ([LIT-999](../../record/literature.d/LIT-999.md), [LIT-1146](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1146.md),
  [LIT-1482](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1482.md)).
- U29 labels Bartók et al. and Chaloner and Verdinelli "**Read:**". The
  exchange's reply did not read them in any sense the record uses, and
  neither has the record. Both are Deferred.
- The two works the record's reader named from memory were confirmed:
  MultiDiffusion is arXiv 2302.08113 (ICML 2023, PMLR 202), and Multi-LoRA
  Composition is arXiv 2402.16843 (TMLR 2024).
