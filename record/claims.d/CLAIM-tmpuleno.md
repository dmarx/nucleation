# CLAIM-tmpuleno: Corrective policies each adequate to its own process can compose into a feedback loop that makes the demands they serve jointly unrealizable, as retries do under overload and as reciprocal escalation may among political actors, so the remedy is a relation that regulates when pursuit is appropriate, such as a retry budget or backpressure that a downstream process has standing to exert, not more effective local pursuit

## The claim

A34 §9: "The local demand is legitimate within its own specification:
complete the request. The local corrective policy is also intelligible:
retry failed requests. Yet their composition can make the demands jointly
unrealizable." "The appropriate intervention is not necessarily to make
each process more effective at pursuing its local demand. It may be to
establish a relationship regulating when that pursuit is appropriate."

On backpressure, A34 lists what makes it work: "Legibility of the
downstream limitation", "A channel through which it can express that
limitation", "Standing under the protocol to regulate incoming work",
"Effective upstream throttling or suspension" and "Conditions for
restoring ordinary activity". "It is not simply information about
capacity. It is information endowed with practical significance through a
maintained relationship."

A36 §17 carries the loop to politics: "Departure from convention" →
"Revised expectations" → "Protective mobilization" → "New operative
precedent", which "resembles retry amplification in distributed systems.
Each actor's response may be intelligible relative to its own concerns,
while the accumulated interaction produces a less workable arrangement."
It bounds it: "The analogy does not establish that all participants
contribute equally to escalation, or that every countermeasure is
wrongful. A response can be justified while still participating in a
feedback circuit whose wider consequences require examination."

## Gap filled: what A34's retry model shows

A34's laboratory takes an offered rate L, a fixed per-attempt failure
probability p and up to r retries, so the load is L(1 + p + … + p^r).
Checked with `python3 -I`:

- At A34's defaults (L = 80/s, p = 0.3, r = 3) the load is 113.36/s,
  over the illustrative capacity of 100.
- With p fixed, one layer of retries multiplies load by less than
  1/(1 − p) however many retries are allowed: 1.43 at p = 0.3, 2 at
  p = 0.5, 10 at p = 0.9. Retries alone with a load-independent p cannot
  make load grow without bound.
- "If several layers independently retry", the factors multiply. In the
  worst case, every attempt failing, k layers of r retries give (r + 1)^k:
  64 for three layers of three retries. That is the "rapidly" in A34.
- A34's own caveat, "In an actual overload feedback loop, failure
  probability can itself increase with traffic", is what makes the loop a
  loop. With p = 1 − C/load and C = 100, an offered 110/s settles at about
  222/s, twice the offered rate. In that simple model the loop does not
  hold the system in overload once the offered rate drops below capacity;
  failures that persist after their trigger is gone, metastable failures
  ([LIT-tmp4gwsm](../literature.d/LIT-tmp4gwsm.md), unread), need something more, such as work spent on
  requests that time out.

So the composition claim holds for A34's model in the form "retries
amplify an overload that already exists, by a factor that multiplies
across layers"; the self-sustaining form needs a further mechanism. The
political half is an analogy with no model at all. The section is the
assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.

joint unrealizability is, and [CLAIM-357](CLAIM-357.md), whose residual separates
infeasibility from realized violation; it adds the mechanism by which
