# LIT-tmp8mpge: Quantum Thermodynamics

Sai Vinjanampathy, Janet Anders (2015), *Contemporary Physics 57(4), 545-579 (2016)* — [ARXIV-1508.06099](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06099)

## Key takeaways

- As of 2015-16 quantum thermodynamics had several inequivalent notions of "work" (ensemble-average, fluctuating single-run, single-shot and resource-theoretic), which the review presents side by side, and it notes that the field had produced few experimentally checkable new predictions.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The review introduces quantum thermodynamics as the extension of thermodynamics and nonequilibrium statistical physics to small, out-of-equilibrium, quantum ensembles. It notes that the field draws on statistical physics, many-body theory, mesoscopic physics and quantum information, which each bring their own tools. Topics covered include thermalisation of quantum systems, competing definitions of work, and the efficiency and power of quantum engines. It is pitched at postgraduate students and researchers.
