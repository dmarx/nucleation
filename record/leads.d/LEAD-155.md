## Note of 2026-11-26: the exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" (ChatGPT, U14–A25), A25's experiment table

A25 §13 proposes this lead's design without citing it: "We could design experiments with equivalent initial bodies of knowledge but different governing architectures", and "The important thing is to compare developmental trajectories rather than static snapshots." Its table gives six contrasts this lead could take as arms: prose against typed dependencies (cost and completeness of identifying affected claims); retained against discarded tentative distinctions (cost of later unexpected inquiries); fixed against governably revisable schemas; unstructured against reusable conceptual units; performance incentives governing findings (whether recognized discrepancies stay consequential); and open inquiry against fixed objectives. The first is the one this record can run on itself; the second is the forgetting experiment [LEAD-295](LEAD-295.md) designs. [CLAIM-1451](../claims.d/CLAIM-1451.md)'s findings difference is a measure this lead could report for each arm. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session). No status change.
