## Why this is here

A2 listed "host compensation" ("selection to maintain collective functionality", giving "transport, regulation, or metabolic rescue") as a mechanism, and named no work for it. This is a documented case of the host compensating at the level of transmission, so it bears on reproductive cohesion (R) in [QUESTION-277](../questions.d/QUESTION-277.md).
