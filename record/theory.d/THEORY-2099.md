# THEORY-2099: Computing value-laden decisions is irrational

## The account

After the Second World War, with the first computers, decision under
uncertainty was formalised as risk analysis: model the unknown as
probability, put goals on one currency, maximise expected return. Its
technologies became the core of computing and AI, and people came to be
measured against machines. The methods are locally valid, but most
decisions lie outside their sweet spots, so a pluralism of methods and
decisions grounded in human judgment are needed.
