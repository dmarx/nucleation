The seventh synthesis review, at the owner's request, and the first to
take the lines from the largest down. It covered distributed-agency, the
largest line with no account: 495 in-force entries when it began.

## How it ran

A Louvain community map of the line (stored relations weighted 2,
mentions 1) gave seven parts and 21 entries it left on their own. Seven
read-only readers each took one part, under one brief, and drafted its
account, its links to the other parts, and Arm A and Arm B. The filer
then:

- checked every quote of six words or more mechanically against the
  record, and checked by hand the ones the checker missed: nested quotes
  rendered with single quotes, and two quotes with elisions whose
  fragments run across line breaks;
- checked coverage: the part accounts and the line account together
  integrate every entry whose `line` includes distributed-agency,
  except CASE-028, Superseded by CASE-006;
- replaced the drafts' pointers to one another ("drafted beside this
  one") with the accounts' codes;
- wrote the line account over the parts.

## Three parts that were three inquiries

The map's parts are not always one inquiry each. Read through:

- **Normative-institutional (95) was three.** Availability before a
  deadline (21 entries: 41 linked pairs inside, 12 to the rest of the
  map's part, 1 to the rest of the line), partners coming to depend on one
  another (11: 15 inside, 3 to the rest of the part, all through CLAIM-060,
  2 to the line) and perspectives, legibility and correction (63). Each has
  its own account.
- **Persistence (47) held world-modeling.** Six entries with 12 linked
  pairs among them and 2 to the other 41, both through TERM-086. The
  reader also named a third candidate, seven entries on identifying
  perspectives (13 pairs inside, 6 out), and kept it in the main account.
- **The other five parts held together.**

Counted over the whole line rather than the map's part, availability and
dependence are no more closed than the larger parts: about one pair in
four crosses, against one in three for conferral. So neither is proposed
as a line of its own. The line account gives the counts.

## Claims

- **Filed (Arm B, derivation): CLAIM-tmp9hy40.** For CLAIM-538's demands
  on offsets between contexts, |x_v − x_u − a_e| ≤ δ_e, the minimal
  non-faces of CLAIM-347's compatibility complex are exactly the simple
  cycles whose holonomy exceeds their summed tolerance. It is the standard
  result on systems of difference constraints (a system is infeasible
  exactly when its constraint graph has a negative cycle), and the
  filer checked the reduction. CLAIM-538 disclaims the general link to
  CLAIM-347's complex, and no entry states it for this class. The
  frustration account integrates it.
- **No other Arm B link passed.** Each reader tested several sentences, and
  each failed by a separating case, a free parameter, or because an entry
  already states it. The line account lists four more tested across
  parts. The one the owner may want to see is the last of them: whether
  the unit's lasting (CLAIM-157) is what persistence by transmission
  (CLAIM-615) measures turns on what makes a later block "that block",
  which neither says.
- **Arm A: no claim in the line only maps its components.** CLAIM-622
  (one premise) is thin and its `defeated_if` already names restatement.
  CLAIM-348 adds an anti-circularity clause, and a stipulated case
  separates it; it has no "Admission" section, and could take that case.

## For the owner

- **CLAIM-615's admission case is realistic.** A mechanism passed to an
  unrelated workshop and counted the same by continuity of its parts (a
  car that changes garages) is almost word for word the second clause of
  its own `defeated_if`. It defeats the claim unless "the relations that
  keep its repertoire realizable" is read as the object's internal
  organization, and its fourth layer lets that organization lie outside
  the individual. The slot needs closing.
- **CLAIM-342's term is open.** Authority that reinforces itself without
  passing through any interpretation, held against independent evidence
  too weak to overturn it (CLAIM-408's normal form with a tilt below
  critical; Arthur's increasing returns), meets CLAIM-342's `defeated_if`
  if share-dependent advantage counts as independent scoring, and not if
  it counts as scoring that passes through what the loop tests. CASE-155
  frames the term as scoring "that depends on who governs"; CLAIM-342 says
  "interpretation".
- **CLAIM-613's `defeated_if`** can be met by one documented ordinary
  fork, which is easy for an existence claim.
- **Ten questions carry the line and link to nothing in it**:
  QUESTION-152, QUESTION-170, QUESTION-187, QUESTION-337, QUESTION-338,
  QUESTION-340, QUESTION-341, QUESTION-347, QUESTION-348 and QUESTION-352.
  Nine hold a promotion condition, or part of a claim, moved from a THEORY
  entry, and QUESTION-170 was filed by a promotion audit. `line` is
  required, and these seem to have taken distributed-agency for want of a
  fitting line: 28 of the 123 questions that say they hold a promotion
  condition formerly on a THEORY entry carry it as their only line. Four are near the line's subjects (QUESTION-152, QUESTION-170,
  QUESTION-337, QUESTION-348); six are not (an index's search cost, game
  search compute, cache eviction, a Lorenz 96 closure, materialized views,
  a court's remedy). Whether they take another line, or a line for
  promotion conditions is wanted, is a decision.
- **Line order.** CLAIM-614, CLAIM-624 and QUESTION-441 keep
  distributed-agency first while their companions (CLAIM-619, ARG-055,
  ARG-064, CASE-229) have organizational-normativity first, an artefact of
  the split that replaced the second line in place.
