# THEORY-2270: Transitions complete when scaffolds internalize

## Source

Pierrick Bourrat (2022 online), [LIT-1927](../literature.d/LIT-1927.md), read from the author's
accepted manuscript in [NOTE-2361](../notes.d/NOTE-2361.md).

## What was actually shown

Two things of different kinds. The criterion is argued: Lewontin's
conditions are met by arbitrary groupings, so a condition on their
source is added; external sources give only Darwinian-like properties;
"the broader the range of perturbations an entity can sustain, the
higher its score on individuality" (§2). The model is simulated: with
growth rate pleiotropically tied to leaving the patch and to resisting
invaders, scaffolded collectives evolve low growth; when the migration
ban is lifted after 1500 generations, growth stays below 0.5 for small T
and S and reverts to the maximum otherwise (§6.3, Figure 3c). The
reversion where T or S is large is what could have come out otherwise,
and did.

## What this does not say

- That the collectives are free of scaffolding after release. The
  periodic environment that makes every T-th generation a propagule
  generation, and the patches themselves, stay on in every regime.
- That the endogenized mechanism is a collective-level one. It is a
  cell trait.
- That endogenization is robust: one large mutation undoes it (§7),
  and the author calls the analysis "preliminary".
- That the line between internal and external is fixed: §3 calls it
  relative and §7 lets cells construct their own scaffold.
