# CLAIM-1060: Governance allocates resources across time

## The claim

A52's thesis:

> **Organizational governance is partly the intertemporal allocation of scarce resources between inquiry, response, and the maintenance of the structures that make both possible.**
>
> **Slow organizational modes emerge, in part, as ways of amortizing inquiry and coordination costs across repeated activity. But because the same stabilization raises the cost of revising established specifications, adaptive governance must preserve the capacity to reallocate resources between answering existing questions and developing the ability to ask and answer new ones.**

Its mechanism (§5), set out as expensive prior inquiry, then a maintained
specification, then a cheap, fast response; so "**An important function of slow organizational
structure is to shift inquiry costs away from the moment when a response
becomes urgent.**" And its cost: "The stored specification makes familiar
questions cheap while making unfamiliar questions comparatively costly."

Its ledger of costs, in place of stability set against adaptability: the cost
of asking, of answering, of maintaining reusable answers, and of revising
the question–answer architecture, which "can substitute for one another,
compete for common resources, and change one another over time".

## Where the record holds it

- The mechanism and its cost are [CLAIM-475](CLAIM-475.md)'s "stabilized specification is
  past inquiry stored so that later questions become cheaper to ask, and it
  can make other questions harder", and [CLAIM-761](CLAIM-761.md)'s stored work, with its
  guard that "the persistence that buys it can entrench".
- A52 cites [CLAIM-303](CLAIM-303.md) as the general mechanism. [CLAIM-303](CLAIM-303.md) is the record's
  own case of it, filing an argument, priced by totals; the general claim
  is [CLAIM-761](CLAIM-761.md).
- [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s note on Hutchins: preparation "moves the cost of search ahead
  of the deadline".
- The governance of an organization's temporal response structure is
  [CLAIM-1070](CLAIM-1070.md), which this complements with the resources that structure
  is bought with.

## Standing

Proposed, in A's voice. The second clause is the inference [ARG-238](../arguments.d/ARG-238.md)
states. [CLAIM-755](CLAIM-755.md) separates a dynamically slow variable from an economically
amortized result and shows that each can occur without the other, so
"slow modes" here means the economic sense: structures formed at a cost and
used cheaply many times. That some such structures are also slow in the
dynamical sense is a further fact.
