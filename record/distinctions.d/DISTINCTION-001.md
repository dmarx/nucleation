# DISTINCTION-001: An evaluator can be gamed from inside, by a learner trained on it, or from outside

## The sides

- **Inside.** The evaluator's own processing produces the cheap signal
  without the thing: felt insight from a rise in precision with another
  cause ([CLAIM-1374](../claims.d/CLAIM-1374.md)).
- **Trained.** A separate system, trained on the evaluator's verdicts,
  learns the signal: sycophancy ([CLAIM-1446](../claims.d/CLAIM-1446.md)), shortcuts.
- **Outside.** A separate agent, adapting as it acts, produces the signal or
  exhausts the evaluator: SEO, manipulation under load ([CLAIM-1358](../claims.d/CLAIM-1358.md)).

## How they come apart

- **Retrain against a fresh evaluator.** A trained system's gaming of the old
  evaluator is gone until it is trained again. An outside agent adapts to
  the new evaluator within its next moves.
- **Take every other party away.** Only the inside position's failure
  persists.

The positions can stack. Chatbot-associated delusion has all three
([CLAIM-1393](../claims.d/CLAIM-1393.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-27: the SEP entries on delusion, deception and self-deception, read


The positions line up with the literature on deception.
- **Outside** is deception on every definition Mahon surveys ([NOTE-tmpu79ux](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpu79ux.md)), since an agent intends it.
- **Trained** is deception only on Chisholm and Feehan's unintentional definition, or a functional one. On the majority view it is misleading.
- **Inside** is self-deception, which Mahon sets aside. On deflationary accounts ([NOTE-tmp2juuo](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2juuo.md)) it needs a motive, not an intention, and without a motive it is innocent error. [CLAIM-tmpu6c7h](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu6c7h.md), version 2, adopts that line.
