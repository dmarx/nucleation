The owner sent four coral readings, each with a note on why it matters.
The notes are the questions this record reads the papers for. Neither
record held any of the four.

All four were read in full, from PMC copies or, for Hughes, the
publisher's PDF posted on a Bennington College course page. Hughes is in
Elsevier's open archive.

Papers 1 and 2 are reviews and are weighed as reviews. Papers 3 and 4 are
experiments.

## How each paper meets the owner's note

- **Goulet et al. 2020 ([LIT-tmpkkylg](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpkkylg.md)).** The brief named Stévenne et al.;
  the paper with this title is Goulet et al. It makes the conceptual case
  for the holobiont as a composite system. The physiological case is thin:
  its one "not the sum of its parts" result comes from a sea anemone, not
  a coral. "Establishes" is too strong.
- **Boilard et al. 2020 ([LIT-tmpx0l32](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpx0l32.md)).** It is a proposed account of
  breakdown and reorganisation, as the owner says. Its use for the
  regulatory-disruption example is its account of who regulates whom. The
  authors themselves say microbial causation is untested.
- **Kaniewska et al. 2015 ([LIT-tmpm09g4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpm09g4.md)).**
  - **The environmental cue is shown experimentally.** The natural
    night-light pattern is necessary, not moonlight specifically.
  - **The endogenous cascade is correlational.**
  - **No coordination between colonies is tested.** The "coordination" is
    colonies responding to a shared cue.
- **Hughes et al. 2007 ([LIT-tmpr2ulq](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpr2ulq.md)).** It supports the note, and more
  strongly than stated: even established colonies survived better where
  herbivores grazed. Its limits:
  - plot scale;
  - all large fishes excluded, not herbivores alone;
  - the phase shift reversed within 30 days.

## Theories

Three Proposed theories:

- **Bleaching ([THEORY-tmpwypmh](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpwypmh.md)).** Bleaching is the breakdown of a
  regulated host–symbiont exchange, reached by more than one route. The
  microbiome clause was declined as untested.
- **Spawning ([THEORY-tmpezkoq](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpezkoq.md)).** The night-light regime is necessary for
  spawning, and a signalling programme tracks it. The cascade is held as a
  hypothesis.
- **Reef recovery ([THEORY-tmpap2wn](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpap2wn.md)).** Recovery after bleaching depends on
  herbivorous fishes keeping macroalgae down.

No relation is declared, because none of the papers cites or tests
anything the record holds. [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md) sets the holobiont boundary aside,
and [THEORY-111](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-111.md)'s "spawning" is the machine-learning term.
