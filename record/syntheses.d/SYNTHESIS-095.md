# SYNTHESIS-095: The dependence-and-domination line is six accounts: what wholes and parts owe and claim; what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe; responsibility for another's conditions, and moral exploitation; restricting capabilities, and who controls the terms of waiting and contracting; remedies foreclosed before review; and answerability and contestability; the parts meet mostly where the later four use the first part's term for domination and point at its claims on control of terms, and the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the dependence-and-domination line, "Wholes, parts and dependence", split from organizational-normativity ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)) at the owner's approval of 2026-11-15 ("yes on the two line split"). It is a line account over the accounts of its parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)). Version 1 had two parts. Version 2 (2026-11-17) adds the four filed at the interim synthesis review of that day, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46). "Organization" is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default, and the bearer of an obligation is kept apart from its ground; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none. Every link below is attributed to the part account or component that states it.*
