since docs/readings-by-line.md is not yet generated. It named 579 readings

[CLAIM-445](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-445.md), [CLAIM-348](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) and [TERM-100](../../record/terms.d/TERM-100.md), which those entries rest on or use.

**A defect in the view, found in stage 1.** 19 of the 55 are not unused.
In force claims or arguments ground on them, and the view does not see
it. The template reads a reading's uses from `t.supports`, the converse of
`grounds`, and from `t.reports`, the converse of `reported_in`. Both are
derived fields that no file stores, and under `views.render` both come
back empty for every reading ([THEORY-140](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-140.md)'s `supports` is empty though
[CLAIM-619](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) and [ARG-062](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-062.md) ground on it; so is [THEORY-911](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-911.md)'s, though [CLAIM-445](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-445.md)
grounds on it). The view therefore counts only `corroborates`, `answers`
and `bears_on`. Across the record, 129 of the 579 readings it calls
unused are grounded on by a claim or argument not retired, most of
those uses in distributed-agency and pragmatic-transport by first line. This line
is not at zero either: [CLAIM-619](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) and [ARG-062](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-062.md) ground on [THEORY-140](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-140.md), which
is in force ([ARG-043](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-043.md) grounds on [THEORY-1285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1285.md), which is Proposed and so
outside the view). The 19 were reviewed all the same, against this
line, since none of their uses is in it. The fix is the template's, not
this review's: derive the users from the claims and arguments
(`docs("CLAIM")`, `docs("ARG")`, and CASE's `reported_in`) rather than
from the converse fields.

## Verdicts

### Tied (4)

- **[THEORY-1119](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1119.md)** (Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde) `bears_on`
  [QUESTION-128](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-128.md). The question asks whether "norms of function and failure
  [can] be defined from an organization's own dynamics", and its body
  cites the work, through [THEORY-071](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-071.md) (Proposed), for "Self-generated
  normativity as a condition of agency". [THEORY-1119](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1119.md) is the Active
  reading of what the paper proposes. Also grounded by [CLAIM-284](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-284.md),
  [CLAIM-194](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-194.md) and [CLAIM-288](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) (distributed-agency).
- **[THEORY-1165](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1165.md)** (Kahl) `bears_on` [QUESTION-441](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-441.md). Its third part asks
  when the counter-normativity is "the organization's own rather than its
  participants' or an outside maintainer's"; Kahl's criterion is
  "whether revision of that rule under breakdown is internal or external
  to the candidate group agent", and institutional systems fail it
  because their standards are "authored elsewhere". Also `bears_on` the
  question raised below. Also grounded by [CLAIM-485](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-485.md) (distributed-agency).
- **[THEORY-1463](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1463.md)** (Pritchett, Woolcock and Andrews) `bears_on`
  [QUESTION-441](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-441.md), which asks what makes reproductive the counter-normativity
  of an organization that "reproduces the representational projections
  that make the harm non-legible". Isomorphic mimicry is "keeping
  legitimacy by imitating the forms of functional organizations without
  their function", with de jure scores above de facto ones. The authors
  offer it as how failure persists, untested against alternatives, and
  the harm is a capability failure; the history note says so.
- **[THEORY-837](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-837.md)** (Besley and Prat) `corroborates` [CLAIM-462](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-462.md), that an
  arrangement "can keep, or increase, its capacity for effective action
  while its capacity to be effectively corrected by others degrades,
  because the two are maintained by different relations". Under capture
  the incumbent keeps office and voters "never remove a bad incumbent";
  the relation degraded, the outlets' report, is one the incumbent's
  action does not need. [CLAIM-462](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) is distributed-agency's, and a premise
  of [CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) in this line. The model is one-shot, so it is not tied to
  [QUESTION-441](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-441.md)'s reproduction. Also grounded by [CLAIM-404](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-404.md).
