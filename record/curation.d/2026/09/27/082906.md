The owner asked for arXiv 1701.00656: Carù, *On the Cohomology of
Contextuality* (QPL 2016, EPTCS 236). It is filed as [LIT-tmpp63y4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpp63y4.md) and read
in full as [NOTE-tmpjdqyz](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpjdqyz.md) (v1, the only version). It is `Active`, and it
`corrects` [LIT-277](../../record/literature.d/LIT-277.md).

**The counterexample holds.**

- **The model.** It sits on the two-party, two-setting, four-outcome Bell
  cover.
- **Checks.** The reader reproduced [NOTE-250](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-250.md)'s counts first, as a
  calibration: Hardy 13/13, PR box 0/8, AMB §8 cover 9/15. Then, with exact
  ℤ and ℤ/2 linear algebra, the reader confirmed that the model is strongly
  contextual and that γ = 0 on all 22 sections. I checked the strong
  contextuality by hand when filing; the γ computation is the reader's.
- **Beyond the paper.**
  - The reader found, by linear programming, a no-signalling probability
    model with this support.
  - The model has no All-vs-Nothing argument. That follows from [LIT-278](../../record/literature.d/LIT-278.md)'s
    Theorem 21 by contraposition, and the reader also checked it directly
    over ℤ₄.

**Scope of the refutation.** It refutes Conjecture 8.1 for symmetry of the
cover, and for "no measurement confined to one context". It does not touch
covers whose contexts pairwise intersect (AMB's own §8 cover is one), or
symmetric models: this model has 2 automorphisms, against the PR box's 16.

**The rest of the paper is weaker.**

- **The hierarchy collapses.** The higher-obstruction hierarchy (Prop 5.5)
  is empty above level 0 for every no-signalling model, by the paper's own
  Prop 5.6. So the abstract's "hierarchy of levels of contextuality" is the
  one level AMB already had.
- **Prop 6.1 is false in its "only if" direction.** The PR box is a
  counterexample.
- **No positive completeness result.**

It closes neither the Hardy gap nor the §8 cover.

**[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) (version bump).** Its cohomology line records the refutation
and the open cases. The claim itself is unchanged.

**Follow-up, existence only.** Carù, arXiv 1807.04203, *Towards a complete
cohomology invariant*, is the natural next reading on this line.
