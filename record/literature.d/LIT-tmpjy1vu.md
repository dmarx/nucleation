# LIT-tmpjy1vu: The Costs and Benefits of Ownership: A Theory of Vertical and Lateral Integration

Sanford J. Grossman and Oliver D. Hart (1986). The Costs and Benefits of Ownership: A Theory of Vertical and Lateral Integration. *Journal of Political Economy* 94(4): 691–719. DOI 10.1086/261404.

## Key takeaways

- Residual rights of control are "the right to control all aspects of the
  asset that have not been explicitly given away by contract" (§I.B);
  "Ownership is the purchase of these residual rights" (abstract).
- "When residual rights are purchased by one party, they are lost by a
  second party, and this inevitably creates distortions" (abstract), and
  they do so "in spite of the fact that the noncooperative outcome never
  occurs!" (§III.A): the cost falls on investment beforehand.
- §V extends the idea to employment: "the employer has many of the
  residual rights of control".
