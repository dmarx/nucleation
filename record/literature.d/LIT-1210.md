## Why this is here

A18 of the exchange named "David B. Wong" on pluralistic moral relativism and cited no work; its realist thesis then had to answer "why should our position count as *realism* rather than a species of constrained relativism?" The identification of this book, the full statement of the view, is the record's; *Moral Relativity* (1984) is the other candidate. Bears on [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md) as the opposed view: Wong is where relational normative realism must show it is not relativism, which [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) asserts.
