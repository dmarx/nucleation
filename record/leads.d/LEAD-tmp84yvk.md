# LEAD-tmp84yvk: Measure how much of the owner's originating turn each settled claim retains, judged blind in a metric fixed in advance, to tell entries settled by their constraints from entries contracted onto the record's conventions

## Sketch

**Reference.** For claims filed from an exchange, the owner's turn that
raised the idea, quoted in the claim's own text or recoverable from the
exchange. It is fixed outside the record's later restatements, which is what
CLAIM-tmp4qvx2 requires.

**Pairs.** For each claim with an originating turn: the turn, the claim's
first version, and its current version.

**Judgement.** Raters blind to the hypothesis and to which version is which
score how much of the turn's content each version keeps, and separately how
far each version has moved toward the record's conventional framings. Both
scales fixed before any data are seen (CLAIM-208's demand).

**Reading.** Crossed with LEAD-tmp6do6f's revision count:

| | Retained | Not retained |
|---|---|---|
| Settled | constraint (CLAIM-tmpvm90n) | contraction (CLAIM-tmpdx6l8, CLAIM-068) |
| Not settled | still open | drifting |

Not-retained is not by itself a defect: CLAIM-189 holds that a developing
argument changes legitimately. The measure is of mechanism.

## Open before it can be specified

- Who the raters are. ARG-028 leaves open whether raters are the criterion
  (CLAIM-217) or one probe family among others (CLAIM-206). An assistant as
  rater would share the convention under test.
- What "content" a rater scores, without supplying the record's grammar as
  the scale.
