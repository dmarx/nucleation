**Why the procedure changed.** The owner asked for status changes and
relation changes on "claims that a lot of interesting downstream material
depends on but which is currently in proposed state". Then, when the old
promotion audit was about to resume: "the record has changed materially
since that last audit was interrupted. also, that was the first time we
utilized that process, and would likely benefit from changing it."
[ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md) gives the measurements. 611 commits came after the queue's revision,
and 230 of 308 decision files are stale. 15 of the 19 most-relied-on
Proposed entries never entered the queue, and about forty owner rulings sat
unasked in its hand-off string. The owner then asked for lint rules that
would have exposed the backlog. [ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md) proposes five. One is adopted:
`stale_days: 14`, because the default of 90 could not fire in a record 32
days old.

**The worklist.** `record/status-review/worklist.py` on 99ce85e6f ranked
[CLAIM-027](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) first among CLAIMs, with 19 transitive dependents. Ahead of it
were three THEORYs: [THEORY-055](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-055.md) (Frijda), [THEORY-120](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-120.md) (Frankfurt) and
[THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) (Blackwell). It also listed:

- 49 credence-inversion edges;
- 4 claims using Superseded TERMs;
- 37 Open QUESTIONs with an Active answer;
- 73 Proposed CLAIMs with all premises held and no live objection.

**[CLAIM-027](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) stays Proposed.** Its premises are Active, and nothing meets
its defeater. Two objections hold it back. Neither had been touched since
2026-10-05, though the record had grown the material for both:

- **[CLAIM-089](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-089.md), aggregate revealed preference.** [CLAIM-434](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-434.md) (Active,
  demonstrated) says when an ordering that every winning move climbs
  exists: exactly when the wins never cycle. [CLAIM-tmpnp8w6](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnp8w6.md) is filed as
  [CLAIM-089](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-089.md)'s counter on it. The aggregate preference is available only
  for an acyclic record. Where it exists, it is fitted to one regime of
  authority, and [CASE-003](../../record/cases.d/CASE-003.md)'s change in authority changes it, so it does not
  predict the change. The objection survives where a persistent potential
  ([TERM-078](../../record/terms.d/TERM-078.md)) holds across the change. The counter is Proposed, and holding
  it is the owner's call.
- **[CLAIM-024](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-024.md), nothing new.** [CLAIM-159](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-159.md) already placed will-organization
  against the expected value of control and Ainslie, and nothing linked it
  to the objection. It now `objects_to` [CLAIM-024](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-024.md) as a partial reply.
  Planning theory and institutional theory remain the missing comparison.

**An inversion that was an edge.** [CLAIM-365](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) (Active, demonstrated: the
dual of the local frustration residual) rested on [CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) (Proposed).
The proof does not use [CLAIM-347](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), which only gives the result its reading
as a cardinal refinement of an ordinal fact. The edge is now `complements`.
This also takes [CLAIM-365](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) off the chain from [CLAIM-027](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), so the reach is 18.

**A TERM for inquiry.** The owner asked for one mid-pass, since it is "an
important building block of our formal argument". The record had [TERM-092](../../record/terms.d/TERM-092.md)
(inquiry frontier), [TERM-056](../../record/terms.d/TERM-056.md) (question drift) and [TERM-088](../../record/terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s "an inquiry
can distinguish", and no definition of inquiry. [TERM-tmppwzer](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmppwzer.md) defines it
as A15 and A16's tuple (Σ, A, 𝓘, 𝓞, 𝓚, 𝓑) and calls it reflexive when a
component becomes what it pursues. It deliberately leaves out [CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md)'s
thesis, that inquiry is a form of specification, so the thesis is not
true by definition. [CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md) and [CLAIM-475](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) now `use` it.

**A trap.** `worklist.py`'s first fixture expected [CLAIM-002](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-002.md), which uses a
Superseded TERM, to count as unblocked. The code was right and the test was
wrong. A retired premise is a premise the record does not hold, and that
is the class the acknowledgements had been hiding.
