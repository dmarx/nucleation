# QUESTION-tmp8ifit: Can arrangements make gains in people's capabilities self-reinforcing, so that security, credible refusal, wider participation and more accountable institutions each raise the next and the dependency ratchet runs the other way, and if so past what threshold and on what continuing supply?

## Why it is a question

A13 §IX.3 of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597):

> We have described a negative dependency ratchet in which technological
> adoption progressively narrows alternatives.
>
> Could institutions generate a positive feedback process instead?

A's chain: material security "allows people to reject exploitative
employment"; bargaining power "encourages organizations to adopt less
dominating forms of management"; discretionary time "allows more people to
participate"; and "Those capabilities, in turn, improve society's capacity
to evaluate and govern technological change." A's caption: "A proposed
reinforcing process. It would depend on supportive economic and political
conditions rather than occurring automatically."

The loop is a hypothesis with each link asserted and no condition stated.
The record has the machinery to ask the right thing of it:

- **CLAIM-600** (Active): transient support can be withdrawn safely only if
  it started a process that keeps itself going on a flux that will stay
  available, carried the party past any threshold that process has,
  outlasted the threat, and left the work to the party throughout. A
  positive loop of capabilities is such a process, or it is not
  self-reinforcing.
- **CLAIM-605** (Active): where capacity needs capacity to grow there is a
  threshold below which no support that only covers the shortfall can lift
  it. The loop's first link, security, is the support; whether it lifts or
  only covers is the question.
- **CLAIM-tmp09pjt**: the capacity to govern an arrangement can be eroded by
  the arrangement. The loop's last link runs the opposite way, and the two
  cannot both dominate in the same place.

## What would answer it

- **A model or a case** in which the four links are stated as dynamics,
  with the threshold and the continuing supply named, and the loop either
  sustains itself after the initial support is withdrawn or does not.
- **Evidence on single links**, for instance whether income security in
  fact raises refusal of exploitative terms (A's first link), with what
  would show a link absent.

## Bears on

CLAIM-tmph4j5r (time, A's third link), CLAIM-tmpwgiwq (accountability,
the fourth), and the transient-support line's question of when support can
end.
