# CASE-033: Driving home while daydreaming

## The case

U26: "I can drive home and daydream at the same time ... when I get home, I
don't remember the drive and report feeling like teleporting there. I was not
unconscious."

## What it can show

That access within one agent need not be uniform. It illustrates
[CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md). It cannot by itself establish two simultaneous conscious
workspaces, which the assistant flagged at A78 with three rival readings.
