# LIT-tmpgn0w6: Evolution of the Internal Family Systems Model

Richard C. Schwartz (undated; earliest capture 2020), "Evolution of the
Internal Family Systems Model", IFS Institute —
<https://ifs-institute.com/resources/articles/evolution-internal-family-systems-model-dr-richard-schwartz-ph-d>

## Key takeaways

- **Synthesis.** IFS "brings concepts and methods from the structural,
  strategic, narrative, and Bowenian schools of family therapy to the world
  of subpersonalities". What it adds to earlier multiplicity views (Freud,
  object relations, ego states, psychosynthesis, schemata) is attention to
  "how these inner entities functioned in relation to each other", and "the
  attempt to understand all levels of human organization — intrapsychic,
  family, and culture — with the same systemic principles".
- **Three roles.** Managers try "to keep them functional and safe". Exiles
  carry "the emotions, memories, and sensations" of hurt and are "locked in
  inner closets". Firefighters "douse the inner flames of feeling as quickly
  as possible", through bingeing on "drugs, alcohol, food, sex, or work".
  Roles, not essences: like children in an alcoholic family, parts "once it
  seems safe ... gladly transform".
- **The Self.** "Everyone is at their core a Self containing many crucial
  leadership qualities." The Self "wasn't just a passive witness state" but
  "an active healing presence". Self-led people "hold their center, knowing
  that it was just a part of them that was upset", and become "the 'I' in the
  storm".
