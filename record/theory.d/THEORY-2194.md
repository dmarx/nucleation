# THEORY-2194: No magnetic order in D ≤ 2 Heisenberg models

## Source

Gelfert and Nolting (2001), Journal of Physics: Condensed Matter 13,
R505–R524, [LIT-3057](../literature.d/LIT-3057.md), §§3 and 3.4, skimmed in [NOTE-2290](../notes.d/NOTE-2290.md).

## The account

With Bogoliubov's inequality and operators chosen to put the
magnetization on the left, the 2D bound is M² ≤ ξ₄β / ln(1 + ξ₃k₀²/|B₀M|):
"As B0 → 0, the denominator diverges logarithmically, thus forcing the
magnetization to vanish", for ferro- and antiferromagnetic order (§3).
The scheme "ultimately rests on the divergence of the integral"
∫d^Dk/k² in one or two dimensions (§3.2). On fractal and disordered
lattices "whose spectral dimension is less or equal to 2", Cassi shows
that "at finite temperature no spontaneous magnetization can exist for
the classical O(n) and the ferromagnetic (quantum) Heisenberg model",
from "the infrared divergencies displayed by the corresponding Gaussian
models" (§3.4).

## What it does not license

- A criterion for systems without a continuous symmetry of a
  Hamiltonian, or out of equilibrium.
- Mermin and Wagner's own constants or wording.
