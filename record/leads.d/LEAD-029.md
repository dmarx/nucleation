# LEAD-029: Derive a prediction that follows from the shared organizational variables and not from phenomenon-specific or central-executive models, and a joint prediction of two lines through the mapping

## What it would do

Derive the prediction with flexibility penalized, then run A55 §7's
comparison of one model against separate models.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-286](../claims.d/CLAIM-286.md): "a prediction that follows from the shared organizational
  variables and not from phenomenon-specific or central-executive models
  with flexibility penalized"
- [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md): "no joint prediction of the pragmatic-transport and
  will-organization lines has been derived through the mapping"
- [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md): "the test that would tell them apart, which A55 §7 states, has
  not been run"

## What would count

A joint prediction that holds where separate models miss supports
[CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md); none defeats the unification.

## Note of 2026-11-22: derived and run, in simulation

<!-- inactive-ok-block: CASE-324 CLAIM-888 — new; this lead's study and its finding -->
Done by the assistant on this date (code, tests and outputs under scratch/studies/lead-029/). The joint prediction was derived in [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)'s operator model with [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md)'s mapping (frame as the context operators act under, judgement as the read-out of the shared state, governance as a conferral on one operator's authority) and filed as [CLAIM-888](../claims.d/CLAIM-888.md): a governance intervention's later effect under a new frame takes the sign of the treated operator's reading there, and survives in proportion to the operators' dissent under the frames passed through, growing where η·Δa²/2 > κ, which is [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s threshold for this model. A55 §7's comparison, as [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md) holds it, was then run on simulated data ([CASE-324](../cases.d/CASE-324.md)): the joint model beside a model with the loop cut, the same with more freedom, and an interpretation model plus a governance model, all of 15 to 19 parameters, fitted to the same 240 episodes and scored on held-out compositions of an intervention and a later frame change. With the loop in the generating world the joint model's error was two to thirty times smaller; with no loop it tied the cut model and the BIC preferred the cut model. Choices left open by the lead and made here: what "separate models" means (both a cut loop and an additive pair were run), the training design (no compositions in training, or compositions with some operator–context pairs withheld; both run), the loop's strength (above and below the threshold, and absent), and the score (the joint model's own, and a misspecified one).

What remains: the "What would count" asks for a joint prediction that holds where separate models miss. It holds in a world built to have the loop, where the joint model is the generating family, so the study shows the prediction and the test's power, not that any system has the loop. A run on observations not generated by the joint model (a person, a trained network, the PerspectiveLab engine) is still to do, as is the lead's first half for [CLAIM-286](../claims.d/CLAIM-286.md) and [QUESTION-088](../questions.d/QUESTION-088.md): the shared organizational variables against models built for habit, rumination, rigidity, akrasia or insight, or around a central executive. The A55 §7 wording was taken from the record, not rechecked against the exchange. Status and stage are the owner's.
