# QUESTION-048: What can report, no-report, deficit and architectural evidence each establish about experience as opposed to access, and does any of it survive in a system that lacks cortex or biology?

## The question

Every kind of evidence the record holds about experience reaches it through access: through report, through a capacity that is lost, or through an architecture that a theory says matters. The question is what each kind can establish about experience rather than access. It also asks whether any of it carries over to systems unlike the human brain: a bird's nuclear pallium, a machine, an organization.

## Why the record needs it

Two claims on the organizational-affect line depend on the answer. [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) needs functional affect to come apart from experience. [CLAIM-003](../claims.d/CLAIM-003.md) needs experience, not only access, to be compartmented. Neither can be tested until it is clear which evidence bears on experience at all. The same limits decide what the distributed-agency line could ever learn about whether a combined form is a subject ([CLAIM-165](../claims.d/CLAIM-165.md)).

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-091](../theory.d/THEORY-091.md) (deficits): when consciousness and a capacity are lost together, this does not show the capacity is experience's, because "access is lost too". Limit: one source; the test case, superblindsight, has not been found.
- [THEORY-094](../theory.d/THEORY-094.md) (report and no-report): neither isolates experience, because "post-perceptual cognition that tracks the percept persists without report". Only removing differential cognition does. Limit: shown for rivalry only.
- [THEORY-100](../theory.d/THEORY-100.md) (comparative): a correlate of report "does not require laminar cortex". So report evidence can be had without cortex, but it "cannot separate access from phenomenal consciousness". Limit: second-hand, one laboratory.
- [THEORY-023](../theory.d/THEORY-023.md) (architecture and behaviour): mimicry undercuts behavioural evidence, and architectural indicators "presuppose the disputed computational functionalism". So the dispute is "over what counts as evidence as well as the answer". Limit: about AI. The architectural half applies to any non-biological system; the mimicry half needs training on human output.

## What would answer it

For each kind of evidence, a case where access and experience come apart and the evidence follows one of them. Superblindsight, inconspicuous rivalry recorded intracranially, or a marker both sides accept in advance would each do this. More correlates found with report would not.
