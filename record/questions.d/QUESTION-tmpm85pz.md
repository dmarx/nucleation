# QUESTION-tmpm85pz: Do post-delisting census data for the Kirtland's Warbler show breeding-male numbers tracking the area of plantation habitat supplied, holding at a lower level when that area is reduced and not returning to the earlier level without the area being restored, with winter conditions accounted for?

## Why it is a question

THEORY-1482 reports a 100-year simulation in which the population tracks
the supplied plantation habitat by construction, through ceiling density
dependence. The projection is the model's; no census after delisting has
yet been read that could show the population following the habitat
supplied.

## What would answer it

Post-delisting census data in which breeding males fall to a lower level
when plantation area is cut, and do not recover until it is restored, with
winter precipitation accounted for, would answer yes. Numbers that hold
their level on less habitat, by higher density, would answer no.
