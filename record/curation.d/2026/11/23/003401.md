## A choice left to the owner

The ruling has a stronger and a weaker reading. On the stronger,
sinology glosses the account and cannot defeat it. On the weaker,
sinology is a lesser authority that still counts as evidence against the
account where they conflict. The application above takes the stronger.
If the owner meant the weaker, [CLAIM-541](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-541.md)'s version 2 defeater is what
would be restored.
