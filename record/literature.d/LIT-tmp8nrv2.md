# LIT-tmp8nrv2: Fibration symmetry-breaking supports functional transitions in a brain network engaged in language

Gili, Avila, Pasquini, Holodny, Phillips, Boldi, Gabrielli, Caldarelli, Zimmer and Makse (2024). arXiv 2409.02674; earlier as a Research Square preprint, DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-4409330/v1.

## Key takeaways

- The resting and task synchrony clusters are imposed on the inferred
  networks, not predicted from them: what the program establishes is that
  removals alone can make each observed partition balanced, and that the
  inferred networks lose the baseline's automorphisms.
- Rest and task partitions cross: Broca left joins SMA, Broca right joins
  the Wernicke pair. Neither refines the other.
- No dynamical model, stability test, null model of partitions or
  subject-level structural data.
