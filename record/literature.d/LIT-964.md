# LIT-964: The reusable holdout: Preserving validity in adaptive data analysis

Cynthia Dwork, Vitaly Feldman, Moritz Hardt, Toniann Pitassi, Omer Reingold and Aaron Roth (2015), *Science* 349(6248), 636–638.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-848](../notes.d/NOTE-848.md), of the Supplementary Material only.*

- **Max-information bounds overfitting (Theorem 2, proved).**
  P[S ∈ R(φ)] ≤ 2^{I∞(S;φ)}·max P[S ∈ R(φ)]; so I∞ ≤ log₂e·τ²n gives an
  overfitting probability at most e^{−τ²n} (Corollary 3).
- **Privacy bounds max-information (Lemma 5, proved).** ε-differential
  privacy gives I∞ ≤ log₂e·εn, and post-processing keeps it, so the
  analyst may choose queries freely from private answers.
- **Sharper versions (proofs in companion papers).** ε ≤ τ gives
  P[|P[φ] − E_S[φ]| > τ] ≤ 6e^{−τ²n}; an (ε, δ) version gives failure
  probability β.
- **Thresholdout (Theorem 11, proof elsewhere).** Answer with the
  training estimate unless it disagrees with the holdout beyond a noisy
  threshold. All m answers are within τ with probability 1 − β, while
  fewer than B overfitting events have occurred, for n =
  O(ln(m/β)/τ²·min{B, √(B ln(m/β))/τ}).
- **Scope.** [0, 1]-valued statistical queries on a discrete universe;
  the simulations used a different implementation from the one analysed.

## Standing in the record

Filed 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-040](../questions.d/QUESTION-040.md), the selection-bias half. Read in part
on 2026-10-14 ([NOTE-848](../notes.d/NOTE-848.md)): the Supplementary Material in full, the
main text not reached. [THEORY-318](../theory.d/THEORY-318.md), filed from it, answers
[QUESTION-040](../questions.d/QUESTION-040.md) in part: a search whose access to the test data runs through
a private mechanism is bounded in how far it can inflate a statistic. It
does not say how far an unmediated search has inflated one, which is the
form of the question's d′ correction. It complements [THEORY-028](../theory.d/THEORY-028.md) (Russo &
Zou, [NOTE-308](../notes.d/NOTE-308.md)), giving the high-probability, worst-case counterpart of
its expected bound. The reusable-holdout procedure as advice for tuning
models belongs in the anthology, which does not hold the paper.
