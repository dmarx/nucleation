# LIT-tmpgku8o: The Multiscale Wisdom of the Body: Collective Intelligence as a Tractable Interface for Next‐Generation Biomedicine

Michael Levin (2024). "The Multiscale Wisdom of the Body: Collective Intelligence as a Tractable Interface for Next‐Generation Biomedicine." *BioEssays 47(3)*. DOI-10.1002/bies.202400196.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-tmphgki6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphgki6.md)). A programmatic synthesis of Levin's
lab's framework (basal cognition, bioelectric control, cognitive light cone)
for a biomedical audience, with a classification of interventions by the
agency they presuppose. It is a position paper: claims rest on cited
experiments, mostly from the author's lab; no new data.
