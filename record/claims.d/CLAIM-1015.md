# CLAIM-1015: Ecological unity of a self-like organization

## The claim

A24: "A harmonious ecosystem is unified by the organization of its
interactions, not by the emergence of a single organism, a single
perspective, or a single objective." Applied to a self or a team: "a
plurality of perspectives can become more coherently organized precisely
by becoming differentiated, rather than by converging toward one
perspective". The conjecture as A24 closed: ecological integration is "a
historically maintained regime in which differentiated perspectives
remain viable, mutually consequential, and capable of reorganizing their
relationships", and "ecological harmony, rather than perspectival
singularity, is the relevant form of unity for some kinds of selfhood".

## The correspondence A24 drew

| Ecological system | Multi-perspectival self or team |
|---|---|
| Species retain distinct niches | Perspectives retain distinct competencies and concerns |
| Interactions regulate abundance and opportunity | Governance regulates influence and participation |
| Niche differences can stabilize coexistence | Differentiated roles can stabilize coordination |
| Rare species can recover when conditions favor them | A marginalized perspective can regain influence when relevant |
| Community persists through turnover | Organization persists despite changing participants |

A24's gloss on the fourth row: "A healthy plurality is not merely one in
which every constituent currently exists. It is one in which constituents
retain the capacity to become consequential again." That is held as
[CLAIM-362](CLAIM-362.md)'s continued access to consequential roles and [QUESTION-132](../questions.d/QUESTION-132.md)'s
recovery when rare.

A24 illustrated with the two-species model ṅ₁ = n₁(1 − n₁ − ρn₂), the
rare type's growth 1 − ρ > 0 for ρ < 1: coexistence "because each is
limited more strongly by its own abundance than by the other's". That is
held, with its proof, in [CLAIM-369](CLAIM-369.md); A24 flagged that normalized shares of
authority behave differently from abundances, as [CLAIM-369](CLAIM-369.md) does.

## Suppression and reorganization

A24 separated two routes to compatibility: suppression ("remove or
weaken one constituent's requirement until the others are compatible")
and ecological reorganization ("change the available roles, resources,
or interactions so that the original requirements become jointly
achievable"). Only the second is relief of structural frustration
([CLAIM-554](CLAIM-554.md)), and relief is judged against the original demands
([CLAIM-361](CLAIM-361.md)). Held.

## What it does not say

That a team or an ecosystem is a self, or that a harmonious organization
has a subject of experience. A24 §7 calls the ecological mode of
flow-like coordination "an analogy to psychological flow, not evidence
that the team experiences a flow state as a subject".
