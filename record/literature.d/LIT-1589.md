## Why this is here

A3 used it to separate interstate sovereignty from the claims of the displaced, the distinction [CASE-169](../cases.d/CASE-169.md) brings to [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md): a settlement adequate to the dispute between states need not decide whether the people expelled can return.
