**What was learned that the entries cannot say.** The criteria the record
had been collecting are not rivals. Strength, timing, goal-preservation,
persistence and gluing are separate measurements of one candidate, and the
interesting cases are where they disagree. The metronome is strong and
one-way. The notebook is diachronic, so a synchronous block test does not
see it at all. That is the first place the storage route and the
entrainment route visibly need different mathematics.
