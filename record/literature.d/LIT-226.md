# LIT-226: The Conditional Entropy Bottleneck

Ian Fischer (2020), *Entropy 22(9):999 (2020)* — [ARXIV-2002.05379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05379)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpxn4hq](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxn4hq.md).*

- **The criterion.** A representation Z of X for predicting Y meets the
  Minimum Necessary Information (MNI) point when I(X;Y) = I(X;Z) = I(Y;Z)
  (Eq. 1). Equivalently, I(X;Y|Z) = I(X;Z|Y) = I(Y;Z|X) = 0.
- **The objective is IB, reparametrised.** CEB minimises
  I(X;Z|Y) − γI(Y;Z) (Eq. 5). Under Z ← X ↔ Y,
  I(X;Z|Y) = I(X;Z) − I(Y;Z), so CEB and IB "are equivalent for
  γ = β − 1". What is new is the target, ρ = log γ = 0, and a compression
  term with a floor of zero.
- **A measurable distance to the target.** The residual information
  ReX = ⟨log e(z|x) − log b(z|y)⟩ ≥ I(X;Z|Y) uses a backward encoder
  b(z|y) where VIB has a marginal m(z). It "tells us exactly how many
  bits we are from the MNI point, assuming that our current classifier is
  optimal".
- **CatGen is InfoNCE.** Normalising b(z|y) over the K labels in a
  minibatch gives a decoder "mathematically equivalent to … CPC … after
  an offset of log K", by Poole et al.'s proof, and "constrained by
  log K" (§6.3).
- **The evidence is empirical.**
  - On Fashion MNIST, CEB beats VIB at every ρ in accuracy and targeted
    PGD robustness, with equal rates.
  - At ρ = 0 it is well calibrated and detects OoD inputs by the rate.
  - It never learns random labels.
  - On CIFAR-10, a CEB Wide ResNet (97.51%) beats Madry's adversarially
    trained model on PGD attacks, except L∞ ε ∈ [2, 7], without
    adversarial training.
  - The causal hypothesis has no theorem behind it.
- **Not a sufficiency result.** Footnote 2 avoids the word "sufficiency"
  so as not to suggest minimal sufficient statistics. The paper does not
  relate MNI to them formally, nor to Kolmogorov complexity.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped
under the heading *Relating K-complexity to minimum sufficient
statistics*. Read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-tmpxn4hq](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxn4hq.md). It is held as the
operational form of the IB point where Z keeps exactly the information
about X that bears on Y, and as the IB descendant that ties the IB to
InfoNCE. It names the in-batch case of the InfoNCE bound
that [THEORY-006](../theory.d/THEORY-006.md) leaves open, since CatGen's negatives are the other
labels in the minibatch. [THEORY-035](../theory.d/THEORY-035.md) names it as part of the IB
programme. It makes no claim about a compression phase of SGD, so it
does not bear on the account that theory rejects. Its argument that
compressed representations generalise rests on Shamir et al.'s
mutual-information bound, which [LIT-245](LIT-245.md) argues is vacuous in realistic
settings.

Tagged `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../decisions.d/ADR-005.md)). The paper carries a training
instruction (use CEB at ρ = 0), and that part belongs in the anthology.
The causal hypothesis is a separate claim and is not yet filed. It is
kept here by the owner's decision of 2026-09-26 that new work stays in
