The eighth synthesis review, at the owner's request, run while the
seventh's pull request (dmarx/nucleation#461) waited on CI. It covered
pragmatic-transport, the next largest line with no account: 391 in-force
entries when it began.

## How it ran

As in the seventh round: a Louvain community map of the line (stored
relations weighted 2, mentions 1, resolution 0.7) gave eight parts and
eleven entries it left on their own, and eight read-only readers each
drafted one part under one brief. A usage limit stopped all eight midway;
each resumed from its saved files once it lifted. The filer then checked
coverage mechanically (every in-force entry of the line integrated, once),
replaced the drafts' file pointers with codes, checked the four drafted
claims' derivations, added inactive-ok lines the drafts lacked for
Answered questions, and wrote the line account.

Three entries moved between the map's parts on reading: CLAIM-257 from
contextuality to fidelity, TERM-011 from fidelity to frames, and
QUESTION-024 from contextuality to the study part.

## Claims filed (Arm B, derivation)

The filer checked each derivation.

- **CLAIM-tmp008ey** (chains reader). If every kernel leaves f fixed,
  E[g(S_n) | S_0] = g(S_0) for g = f − E f(S_0); the test function
  sgn(g(x))·g(y) has mean E|g| under the joint law and 0 under the product,
  and is bounded by ‖g‖∞, so total variation is at least E|g|/(2‖g‖∞), and
  Pinsker gives I(S_0; S_n) ≥ (E|g|)²/(2‖g‖∞²) for every n.
- **CLAIM-tmpol63b** (kernels reader). Within a context, one kernel
  fitting every item exactly is the definition of a garbling, and a
  constant target is fitted by the constant kernel. So the restated D_obs
  and its Le Cam deficiency charge a rendering for what it adds about the
  item and nothing for what it loses. This bears on CLAIM-228's thesis
  that the restated measure separates what recovery separates: a
  procedure and its collapse are separated by recovery and not by the
  measure.
- **CLAIM-tmptimdb** (kernels reader). Triangle inequality and data
  processing under one kernel: a rendering's order effect exceeds the
  source's by at most the two order-context distortions. A permutation
  example shows kernels fitted per order bound nothing.
- **CLAIM-tmpmte91** (fidelity reader). Where a literal rendering is
  Blackwell-equivalent to the source on the declared problems, any
  rendering made from the source alone is a garbling of it, so it is
  never the better report for an ideal receiver; CLAIM-461's objection is
  then a gain in usable information under the readers' code (CLAIM-199).

Each says in its "Admission" section that it is a short composition;
whether that is enough to keep it is the owner's call.
