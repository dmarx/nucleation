# QUESTION-416: Does an independent long-run evaluation of greenfield serviced plots against later upgrading of squatter areas find the contrast Michaels and colleagues found in Tanzania: better housing and better-kept roads and water mains in the greenfield areas, and no gain in the upgraded ones?

## Why it is a question

Michaels and colleagues ([THEORY-1472](../theory.d/THEORY-1472.md)) find the greenfield advantage by a
spatial discontinuity on twelve areas, and the upgrading comparison only
descriptively. An independent long-run evaluation finding the same
contrast would answer it; upgraded areas doing as well as greenfield
ones would count against.
