# THEORY-tmpbkq7u: Among 31 original self-report scales of psychological need fulfillment found by a 2023 systematic search, 21 measure satisfaction only, one measures frustration only and one thwarting only; all are self-reports of experience or of the perceived environment, the field uses satisfaction, frustration, support and thwarting interchangeably in places, and several frustration scales are negatively worded satisfaction items

## Source

Kermavnar, Avsec, Huang and Desmet (2024), [LIT-tmpohjdb](../literature.d/LIT-tmpohjdb.md), Frontiers in
Psychology 15; read in [NOTE-tmpe2f2g](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe2f2g.md).

## What was actually shown

**The search** (§2): Scopus, Web of Science, PubMed and ProQuest, 21 August
2023, two reviewers, PRISMA; 121 eligible studies, 32 primary describing 31
original scales, 89 adapting 20 of them. 23 scales rest on self-determination
theory, 8 on other typologies.

**The states measured** (§3.2.2): satisfaction 21, satisfaction and
frustration 4 (BPNSFS, GNSF, NSFS, PNSS-S), support 3, frustration 1
(PNFS-PA, 2020), thwarting 1 (PNTS, Bartholomew et al. 2011, [LIT-tmp6as8m](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6as8m.md)),
support and thwarting 1 (IBQ). Every one is a self-report questionnaire
(the review's scope), of experience or of the perceived social environment.

**The faults it documents** (§§4.2.2–4.3.3): the terms used
interchangeably (two named studies); double-barrelled items joining
frustration to thwarting; items meant for satisfaction that ask about
thwarting; the BPNSFS's frustration scales built from "the 12 negatively
coded items" of a satisfaction measure; reverse-scored items read as
"absence of satisfaction" by one team and as "need frustration" by another.

**The needs** (Table 4): autonomy in 27 scales, relatedness 24, competence
23, more than 30 others in one to three each.

## What this does not say

- **What makes a need basic.** The review records "a current lack of
  consensus" and applies no criterion.
- **Which model is right.** "We neither endorse nor critique" the
  two-dimensional model (§5).
- **That the scales are invalid.** It reports what the developers did,
  not tests of its own. Its one validity verdict, on the BPNSFS, is
  Murphy et al.'s ([THEORY-tmp62iwr](THEORY-tmp62iwr.md)), stated more firmly than they state it.
- **How often the terms are confused.** The interchangeable use is shown by
  example, not counted.
