# THEORY-673: The Price equation follows from any ancestor–descendant mapping in a closed population and is, like f = ma, a generalization-sketch: a schema that is empty of predictions until a specific model supplies entities, characters, time step and causes, whose value lies in unifying and guiding model construction, so the charges that it is a tautology or lacks predictive power and dynamic sufficiency ask of it what only its specialisations can supply

## Source

Luque (2016), [LIT-205](../literature.d/LIT-205.md) (*Biology & Philosophy* 32(1):97–125), read in full
in [NOTE-101](../notes.d/NOTE-101.md) from the author's PhilSci-Archive manuscript, probably the
accepted text. Page numbers are the manuscript's.

## What was actually shown

**The derivation (§3, p. 7).** Δz̄ = Cov(w, z) + E(wΔz) follows from a
mapping of ancestors i (frequency q_i, character z_i, fitness w_i) to
descendants, with the one stated restriction that the population is closed:
every entity at t + 1 descends from, or is, an entity at t, so no
migration (p. 8). It holds over any time interval. Equivalent partitions
follow (Table 1), including Okasha's Cov(ω, z′) + E(Δz) and a three-term
form after Godfrey-Smith for non-additive selection and transmission.

**Unification (§4).** The diallelic viability-selection result
Δp = p(w*_A − w̄)/w̄ is re-derived from the covariance term (pp. 10–11).
Robertson's secondary theorem and the classical reading of Fisher's
fundamental theorem are stated as derivable, with derivations deferred.
Tables 2 and 3 collect about 25 derived identities and extensions across
selection theory, stochastic evolution, ecology, epidemiology and
non-genetic and cultural inheritance.

**The schema reading (§6).** Kuhn's generalization-sketch: a law whose
symbolic form changes from one application to the next, learned through
exemplars. Two cases specialise it: Day & Bonduriansky's
overlapping-generation versions, separating selection, reproductive
transmission and survival transmission (§6.1); and Rice's stochastic Price
equation, on which all moments of the fitness distribution contribute to
directional evolution (§6.2). Under Sober's distinction it is a
consequence law, not a source law (p. 21).

**The critics answered (§7).** Dynamic sufficiency belongs to particular
models, and van Veelen et al. (2012) concede as much. Sketches are
predictively empty by nature, and unification is an explanatory value in
its own right (Kitcher).

**What could have come out otherwise.** The equation could have needed
substantive assumptions beyond closure, or failed to yield the classical
results. It does not. The schema reading itself is an interpretation, and
nothing in the paper could have refuted it.

## What this does not say

- **That the equation is not a tautology in the critics' sense.** §7.1
  argues that mathematical truths are not logical tautologies (Gödel,
  Putnam). That answers the word. The critics' point, that the equation
  follows from notational definitions and adds no empirical content, is
  conceded in §7.2.
- **That the disputes are resolved.** The abstract says they "could be
  diverted", which the reading accepts; it does not support "resolved".
- **That Price-based modelling outperforms direct modelling.** The
  prescription is asserted. The case-study authors "found previous results"
  already derivable by other means (p. 27).
- **That the invariance is of a kind with physical symmetries.** It rests
  on Frank's (2012a) information-geometric identity with no derivation, and
  the analogy is informal (C4, weak).
- **That the partition is fixed.** The non-uniqueness of Price partitions,
  and their dependence on how ancestor–descendant maps are drawn, is noted
  but not treated as a problem for the reading.
- **What the biological source laws are.** Force in mechanics has source
  laws (gravitation, Coulomb); the paper does not say what plays that role
  for selection.
- **Anything about Hamilton's rule.** It appears only in fn. 13, through
  Birch's two versions.

## Connections

- The multilevel Price partition underwrites decomposing selection by level
  in the units-of-selection material, [LIT-160](../literature.d/LIT-160.md).
- No THEORY in the record states a general equation of this kind. The
  reusable move, granting that a general identity is predictively empty and
  locating its value in unification and model-building, is what [NOTE-101](../notes.d/NOTE-101.md)
  names as this paper's contribution to the record.

## Dated note, 2026-10-20

The derivation in this entry's first clause is now held on its own as
[THEORY-770](THEORY-770.md) (Active), split off by the promotion audit (batch 058).
What remains Proposed here is the reading: that the equation is a
generalization-sketch whose value lies in guiding model construction, and
that the tautology and predictive-power charges ask of it what only its
specialisations can supply. Wave 5 found no change to the premises, the
rivals or the promote_when since batch 056.
