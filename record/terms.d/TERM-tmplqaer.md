# TERM-tmplqaer: cultural reflexivity: the capacity, held by no single organization, of the many organizations that share a perspective to recognize, contest and revise the frameworks of that perspective through which their purposes are understood, as distinct from correcting violations of standards the perspective leaves unquestioned

## Definition

**Cultural reflexivity**, of a perspective shared across many
organizations: their joint capacity to make the frameworks of that
perspective, the terms in which they understand what their activities are
for, available for recognition, contest and revision. It is held by no
single organization and needs no governor of the perspective; it can be
carried by schools, critics, associations and research that no one
coordinates.

It is set apart from **institutional correction**, which enforces standards
the perspective takes for granted. A20 §III:

> A society may possess effective mechanisms for correcting violations of
> established standards while lacking meaningful opportunities to
> reconsider those standards.

Its example: an economy can correct unfairness "in workplace performance
reviews while leaving unquestioned the assumption that paid productivity
should dominate the organization of adult life."

## The parameter fixed

The first filing of the exchange recorded "cultural reflexivity" on [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)
as a label for that claim's capacity applied to a shared perspective. What
was open was whose capacity it is. A20 §III answers: "It is not the same as
collective agency. Nor does it require a single overarching cultural
authority." So the bearer is the set of organizations that share the
perspective, and the term holds only where [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)'s case obtains, a
perspective "reproduced with no agent governing it" and shared by several.

## How it sits with the record

- **[CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)**: reflexive governance of an organization's perspectival
  arrangements is a capacity some organizations have and others lack. This
  term is that capacity where there is no one organization to have it.
  [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)'s rider carries over: the capacity to revise a framework is not
  the capacity to revise it well.
- **[CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)**: a perspective can be shared by several agents and
  reproduced with no agent governing it. That is the case this term needs.
- **[CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)**: correction of a violation needs legibility, efficacy and
  correctability. Cultural reflexivity concerns the standard the violation
  is measured against, which [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) says it can also change ("behaviour
  or governing standards").

## What it does not say

- Not that a society lacking it is unfree in every respect; A ties its
  value to preserving "interpretive alternatives that dominant operational
  systems have little incentive to generate themselves".
- Not how much of it a society has. No measure is offered.
