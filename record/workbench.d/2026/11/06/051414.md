## About the sweep

- A title that reports what a source finds was most often wrong by dropping a hedge, not by inventing a result. Twenty of the 63 promotions needed a narrowing first.
- Many readings filed on 2026-11-05 and 06 carried promote_when conditions that asked for replication. ADR-065 moves those off the reading. Gathering them by topic, as T1 did, keeps the QUESTION list readable.
- Reviewers again differed on whether a dated note alone takes a version bump; dmarx/nucleation#416 would settle it.
