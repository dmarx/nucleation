The owner asked on 2026-11-19 to "get started on readings": the
forty-six works registered Deferred from the exchange "Topological
protection explained" (curation entry 040025). Each work was read by a
delegated reader under the read-and-file skill's brief: lawful copies
only, stopping at any bot check, no email address sent. Readers worked
in nine batches by theme and wrote drafts to the scratchpad. The filer
checked every quotation in the drafts against the text each reader
downloaded. Most mismatches were extraction artifacts: hyphenation,
Unicode, and quotation marks paired across a scare-quote. One quoted
paraphrase was found, in Reptile's account, and its quotation marks
were removed. Each work read got a NOTE and a Proposed THEORY, and its
LIT was made Active with a new history version.

## What the readings do to the filing's claims

Dated notes "readings for the exchange "Topological protection
explained"" were written on fifty entries, with the relations [ADR-093](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-093.md)
requires on the readings' THEORYs. The findings that matter:

- **Overconsolidation ([CLAIM-tmp8ljvp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ljvp.md)).** No reading shows its full
  signature: present competence rising, a declared later acquisition
  costing more, held capabilities kept.
  - Springer et al.'s catastrophic overtraining is mainly forgetting.
  - Yin et al. and Rajendran et al. show the signature, but name it task
    overfitting, which presses the claim's first defeater.
  - Dohare et al. show lost acquisition apart from both, without the
    present objective improving.

  The claim survives only by declaring its future family outside the
  training task distribution, and by deciding how collateral forgetting
  counts. The note says so; the text and status are unchanged.
- **The deadline ([CLAIM-tmp5vg7p](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5vg7p.md)).** Nothing read supports its
  antecedent for networks. Dohare et al. find the capacity to acquire
  declining gradually under continued varied practice, which is decay
  with use, not disuse. That presses the claim's second defeater and fits
  Dong's depletion reading ([THEORY-1353](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1353.md)).
- **The owner's fibration hunch ([QUESTION-tmpex36a](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpex36a.md)).** Velarde et al.
  show covers growing among units in training, with no symmetry forcing
  them. Their fibers are redundant copies, not a quotient of inputs by
  relabelling, and there is no rule task. The question narrows to whether
  learned covers align with role structure. [CLAIM-tmpj540o](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj540o.md) is compatible
  and untested.
- **The genres case ([CASE-tmpsjjgx](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpsjjgx.md)).** Larremore et al.'s actor–movie
  blocks were five language groups and one genre, so engagement data are
  needed for the example.
- **The Pythia study ([CASE-tmphsr99](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmphsr99.md)).** Design cautions from the
  readings:
  - Pythia's cosine learning-rate decay and repeated data on deduped
    models confound late checkpoints.
  - Test-time training needs meta-learned starting weights (Tandon).
  - In-context task learning appears only in large models (Pan).
  - Function-vector heads are still rising late (Yin and Steinhardt).
  - Report a frozen-readout baseline at every checkpoint (Raghu).
- **Contraction.** Lohmiller and Slotine treat full contraction. The
  partial contraction A29 needs is Wang and Slotine (2004), registered
  below at the owner's request.
- **Fibrations and quotients.** DeVille and Lerman's correspondence
  between a network and its quotient is one-to-one within their class of
  systems. [THEORY-1059](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1059.md) says a quotient admits more dynamics than the
  original. The note on [THEORY-1059](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1059.md) says which class each means.

## Revisions after the readings, at the owner's request

The owner asked on 2026-11-19 for the two claims the readings pressed
to be revised. Both are now at version 2, and each history keeps its old
title.

- **[CLAIM-tmp8ljvp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ljvp.md) (overconsolidation).**
  - The later family now lies outside the training task distribution,
    which keeps Yin and colleagues' and Rajendran and colleagues'
    task-level overfitting out.
  - Acquisition is now priced with held capabilities kept above a declared
    floor, so collateral forgetting is part of the cost. On that pricing
    Springer and colleagues' overtraining is a case, and their THEORY now
    corroborates the claim.
  - What it still lacks is one run showing present performance rising,
    the constrained cost rising with training time, and forgetting
    measured. [CASE-tmphsr99](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmphsr99.md)'s primary estimand is that measurement.
- **[CLAIM-tmp5vg7p](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5vg7p.md) (the deadline).**
  - Version 1 was conditional on [CLAIM-605](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s conditions, which Dohare and
    colleagues' finding reads against. Version 2 states two branches:
    - decay with disuse, where practice restores the capacity before a
      threshold and not after;
    - depletion by use, where practice never restores it and
      reinitialization does at any point.
  - The evidence fits depletion, and Dohare's THEORY now corroborates the
    claim.
  - Role changed from supposed to thesis, because the claim no longer
    rests on an unshown antecedent.
