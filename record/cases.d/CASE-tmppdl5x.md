# CASE-tmppdl5x: Two institutions under a stipulated duty to maintain independent review cause the same harm and reach the same remedy: Institution A keeps its challenge procedure, Institution B has disabled it and an outside intervention supplies the remedy, and B's governance reinstates the defective procedure whenever outside oversight ends; with the first-order harm held fixed, the breach of the procedural duty and the reproductive organization sustaining it remain

## The case

A's review of the pull request dmarx/nucleation#422, item 3, which it
calls its "most important substantive recommendation". The construction
is A's: "Consider two institutions with identical first-order outcomes.
Both initially cause the same harm, and in both cases the affected person
eventually receives the same remedy."

- **Institution A** "maintains a functioning, independently accessible
  challenge procedure."
- **Institution B** "has disabled an equivalent procedure, even though
  providing it was an explicit, justified obligation. An external
  intervention happens to produce the same remedy."

**The stipulated duty.** Both institutions are bound by the same
procedural duty, and it is stipulated, not argued: A says "The normative
premise must be stipulated explicitly—such as a contractual or
institutional duty to maintain independent review." Here it is a duty, on
each institution, to maintain an independently accessible procedure by
which those it affects can challenge its decisions. It is justified by
stipulation, and keeping it is stipulated to be feasible for both, so
[CLAIM-tmptfuky](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfuky.md)'s condition 5 is met by construction.

**What is held fixed.** The first-order harm, its remedy, and so the
first-order outcome: "The first-order outcome is matched. The
second-order conduct differs." The reader adds one stipulation to keep
the match from leaking: over the period the case covers, no further harm
occurs in either institution, so the risk of later harm does not differ
between them either.

**Adding reproduction.** A: "Now add reproduction: B's governance
continually reinstates the defective appeal procedure whenever outside
oversight ends." Set beside this a B that disabled the procedure once and
left it restored after the intervention (the reader's articulation of
A's step). The two Bs have the same harm, the same remedy and the same
breach; they differ in whether the breach is sustained by B's own
governance.

## What it can show

That, given the stipulated duty, three things come apart that a single
episode of harm runs together. A lists them:

- "The first-order harm." It is the same in A and B.
- "The breach of a justified procedural duty." It is present in B and not
  in A, with the harm held fixed. A: "In B, there has been an additional
  violation of the obligation to preserve a legitimate means of challenge,
  regardless of whether the final compensation happens to be identical."
- "The reproductive organization sustaining that breach." It is present
  in the B whose governance reinstates the defect, and not in the B that
  breached once.

So a wrong can be said of B beyond its first-order harm and beyond that
harm's continuation, which is what [CLAIM-tmptfuky](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfuky.md)'s first defeater asks
for. A: "If the claim can survive a case in which the first-order harms
are held constant, its proposed non-reducibility becomes much easier to
evaluate." The argument from the case to the claim is [ARG-tmp0fc4y](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp0fc4y.md).

## What it cannot show

- **That every organization has such a duty.** A: "The construction
  cannot establish that every organization has such a duty." The case
  holds only where an independently justified procedural duty does, so it
  leaves [CLAIM-tmptfuky](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfuky.md) conditional and does not lead to [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md)'s
  unconditional duty, which the record disclaims. Which standards justify
  such a duty is [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md).
- **That the extra wrong is a wrong of answerability in A62 §7's
  relational sense** ([CLAIM-tmptfuky](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmptfuky.md)'s layer 7, at home in [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)),
  rather than a breach of contract. The case shows a wrong beyond the
  harm; what kind of wrong it is, it leaves to the claim.
- **Anything about a real institution.** It is stipulated; no
  organization is reported to behave like B.
- **That the denial of the procedure harms no one.** The case holds the
  remedy fixed, not every loss. Someone who counts being denied the means
  of challenge as a harm in its own right can still say B harms more; what
  the case separates is the procedural wrong from the first-order harm and
  its remedy (the reader's qualification).
