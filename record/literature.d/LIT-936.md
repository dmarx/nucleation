# LIT-936: The information geometry of large language models is shared, learned, and controllable

Dario Picozzi (2026), *arXiv:2609.11063*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-755](../notes.d/NOTE-755.md).*

- **Behaviour fixes output geometry, not activation geometry.** The
  relational geometry, d_ij = 2 arccos Σ_a √(p_i(a) p_j(a)) over a battery
  of contexts, is a function of the conditional laws. Any A ∈ GL(d) applied
  to a hidden layer and undone downstream changes activation geometry and
  leaves behaviour unchanged (Proposition 1). Two models with identical
  outputs can have hidden geometries that rank-correlate at −4/17
  (Proposition 3).
- **Identification of the read-out subspace.** Under finite support and
  strictly positive laws, profiled predictive risk obeys K(S) ≥ c Δ(S, T)²,
  so a rank-matched read-out identifies the language's subspace T (SN3,
  Theorem 5).
- **Shared across architectures.** Ten models from 70M to 7B parameters
  (transformer, state-space, recurrent) agree at a mean of 0.88 on natural
  text, against 0.62 and 0.61 for mid- and last-layer activations.
  Agreement is 0.91 after mapping to the first decoded byte.
- **Why they agree.** Excess risk to a common law bounds the disagreement:
  E(d_A − d_B)² ≤ 4(ε_A + ε_B) (Theorem 6). On real models that bound is
  vacuous. The measured reason is that per-context disagreement, though
  large, lies nearly orthogonal to the relational structure (coherence
  about 0.03).
- **Little is shared beyond the laws.** Residual eigenvector sharing over a
  displacement-matched null is +0.063, "about 9% of the raw overlap". The
  model-specific residual has no semantic alignment and transfers below
  chance.
- **Transfer.** A probe on Fisher–Rao distances to 40 anchors, trained on
  one model, labels held-out prompts through another at 0.659 eight-way
  accuracy, against 0.724 within model and chance 0.125.
- **Origin.** In synthetic languages the assigned law, not the architecture,
  determines the learned geometry (0.998 of imposed separation recovered).
  Randomised evidence depth delays fact acquisition about 4.3×.
- **Control.** The minimum-disturbance step is δ = b A⁻¹q/(qᵀA⁻¹q) with
  A = G + αR, and its cost advantage over Euclidean steering is predicted in
  advance (median measured/predicted 0.967 over 3,515 observations). The
  advantage is local and shrinks with step size.

## Standing in the record

Held for [QUESTION-027](../questions.d/QUESTION-027.md). It gives the record its first measured relational
signature that is stable under an admissible class of transformations and
identified from probes. The transformations are output-preserving
reparameterisations of a network and changes of architecture fitted to one
law, and the probes are the next-token laws on a context battery. The
identification is the automatic, in-probe kind, because the signature is a
function of what is observed. The cross-model stability is that of
different fits to the same law, not of realizations whose laws differ. So it
illustrates the unrestricted form of [QUESTION-027](../questions.d/QUESTION-027.md) and supports [CLAIM-209](../claims.d/CLAIM-209.md)'s
unrestricted sentence. Its anchor-probe transfer and its cross-family human
alignment are measured decisions on prompts or models outside the fitted
ones. Those are the nearest the record holds to [CLAIM-209](../claims.d/CLAIM-209.md)'s restricted
clause, and they are empirical and narrow. Its anchor construction is
Moschella et al.'s ([LIT-849](LIT-849.md)), and its results agree with [THEORY-175](../theory.d/THEORY-175.md).
Propositions 1 and 3 are a networks case of [CLAIM-148](../claims.d/CLAIM-148.md). Its results
are filed as [THEORY-230](../theory.d/THEORY-230.md).

It cites Marconato et al. ([LIT-950](LIT-950.md)), whose all-or-none theorem is the
exact-equality counterpart of its approximate convergence, and the Platonic
Representation Hypothesis ([LIT-302](LIT-302.md)). The paper carries instructions for
machine-learning practice: steer, edit and fine-tune with the pullback
Fisher metric. That part belongs in the anthology, as the
`anthology-candidate` flag records. It is held here for its own question
under [ADR-013](../decisions.d/ADR-013.md).
