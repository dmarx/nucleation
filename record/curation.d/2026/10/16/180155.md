The THEORY backfill reached society-and-governance. Twenty LITs with that
primary tag had a `Read` NOTE and were no THEORY's `source`. Each NOTE was
read in full with its LIT, and existing THEORYs (including the first
round's `THEORY-tmp…` codes), QUESTIONs and CLAIMs were searched for the
same accounts.

## What entered

Eleven THEORYs, one `Active` and ten `Proposed`, from eleven readings.
Codes are the temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

- **One finding, `Active`.** The EEAS's 2024 FIMI sample: of 2,055
  channels recurring across incidents, 76.5% are not attributed to a state
  ([THEORY-659](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-659.md), from [LIT-084](../../record/literature.d/LIT-084.md)). The title carries the two limits the
  reading found: the sample is the EEAS's own and not representative, and
  the 76.5% is a share of a filtered graph, which the report's conclusions
  restate as a share of all channels.
- **Ten accounts, `Proposed`.**
  - The Google remedies opinion's account of scale as the fruit of

    divestiture ([THEORY-480](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-480.md), [LIT-006](../../record/literature.d/LIT-006.md)).
  - Doctorow's four constraints on platform decay ([THEORY-470](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-470.md),
    [LIT-033](../../record/literature.d/LIT-033.md)), filed as `extends` [THEORY-150](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-150.md), the 2023 enshittification

## What did not enter, and why

- **Primary legal and governmental records that decide or find nothing
  general.** The Minnesota show-cause transcript ([LIT-012](../../record/literature.d/LIT-012.md)), whose reading
  says citing it for a holding would be an error; the Broadview grand-jury
  transcript ([LIT-065](../../record/literature.d/LIT-065.md)); the Kerr County commissioners' transcript
  ([LIT-078](../../record/literature.d/LIT-078.md)). Each is a record of what was said, with no finding to state.
- **Doctrine.** Joint Publication 3-12 ([LIT-015](../../record/literature.d/LIT-015.md)) is definitional, "doctrine
  by stipulation" in its reading, with the operative authorities classified.
- **Commentary on single legal events.** Law Dork on the D.V.D. order
  ([LIT-052](../../record/literature.d/LIT-052.md)) and Vance on DOJ's reversal ([LIT-092](../../record/literature.d/LIT-092.md)). Their claims are
  readings of one order or filing, or predictions.
- **A district-court order under appeal.** The Rhode Island subpoena order
  ([LIT-024](../../record/literature.d/LIT-024.md)). Its holdings are contested in the First Circuit, one of the
  decisions it followed has been reversed, and its reading says not to cite
  it as settled. Its remedy point, that segregating a protected subgroup
  discloses membership, is a familiar de-identification fact stated as a
  legal finding, not a result of the order.
- **A weak result.** The Mothership post ([LIT-060](../../record/literature.d/LIT-060.md)). Its headline 1.6% is
  not reproducible from the published code, and the numerator is likely
  truncated. The LIT is `Proposed` with a `promote_when` that already names
  the run that would settle it; a THEORY would restate a number the record
  doubts.
- **A vendor's claim relayed by trade press.** The NEWSCASTER report
  ([LIT-072](../../record/literature.d/LIT-072.md)): one source, circumstantial attribution, no measure of effect.

So eleven of twenty readings became sources, and [LIT-023](../../record/literature.d/LIT-023.md) serves two. The
owner's hypothesis held for the argued and analytic works (eleven of
twelve, the exception being the irreproducible Mothership post). It did
not hold for the eight primary records, doctrine and single-event
commentary, which make up this topic's other half.

## Choices made

- **Proposed over Active.** Only the EEAS finding is `Active`, as a
  description of a coded sample with its limits in the title. Every account
  here is argued from chosen cases, relayed, or (DISINFOX) rests on a
  `Proposed` preprint.
- **No `answers` edges.** [QUESTION-070](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-070.md) names [LIT-179](../../record/literature.d/LIT-179.md)'s RL agent and
  [LIT-084](../../record/literature.d/LIT-084.md)'s tiered network as its two cases of manipulation with no
  intending member. The nudging and EEAS THEORYs describe those cases and
  name the question in Connections; neither says what manipulation is, so
  neither answers it. [QUESTION-119](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-119.md) is named from the Fridman account as a
  case, not an answer.
- **No `rivals` edges.** Clark and Fridman agree on the narrative and on
  its not being merely cynical, and differ in corpus. They are neighbours,
  not rivals; the Fridman account cites Clark as a second source.
- **No source added to [THEORY-150](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-150.md).** It already names [LIT-033](../../record/literature.d/LIT-033.md) in prose as a
  restatement. The talk's new causal claim is filed separately so that it
  can fail without taking the cycle with it.
