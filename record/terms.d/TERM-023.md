# TERM-023: will-organization

## Definition

> the maintained organization of relations governing the conditions under
> which an agent's available action-generating regimes acquire, retain, lose,
> or alter practical efficacy.

Agent-indexed: it is a role in an agent already warranted as one. Functional,
not additive: not another desire, an inner executive, or a causal substance.
Its dimensions are the efficacy relations (accessibility, precedence,
persistence) and the meta-regulatory ones ([TERM-017](TERM-017.md), plus transformation).

## What it is not

Not the operative will, which is whichever regime currently acts. Not
[TERM-003](TERM-003.md), which it replaced. The manuscript states it three times in
slightly different words (Sections 1, 3.2, 7.10); this entry is the one the
claims cite, so that a fourth wording would be a new version here and not a
silent drift.
