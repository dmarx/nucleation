# LIT-2541: Four Paradigm Cases of Dependency in Care Relations

Simon van der Weele (2021). Four Paradigm Cases of Dependency in Care Relations. *Hypatia* 36(2), 338–359. DOI-10.1017/hyp.2021.10.

## Key takeaways

Care theories rest on four paradigm cases of dependency (the infant, the physically disabled person, the profoundly intellectually disabled person, the refugee). Kittay's paradigm, full and one-way dependence, makes care a social problem and puts the dependency worker's claim in view ([NOTE-1892](../notes.d/NOTE-1892.md)).
