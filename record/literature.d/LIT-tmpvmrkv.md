# LIT-tmpvmrkv: Scale-free networks well done

Voitalov, van der Hoorn, van der Hofstad & Krioukov (2019), Scale-free networks well done, Physical Review Research 1, 033034; arXiv 1811.02071, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.1.033034. By its abstract, it defines a power-law degree distribution as a regularly varying one, which may deviate from a pure power law without changing the tail exponent, applies three exponent estimators from extreme value theory proved consistent for any regularly varying distribution, and concludes that real-world scale-free networks "are definitely not as rare as one would conclude based on the popular but unrealistic assumption that real-world data comes from power laws of pristine purity".
