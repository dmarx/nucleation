# THEORY-tmpz11fs: In Claude language models, a sparse set of residual-stream directions that a corpus-averaged Jacobian lens identifies as verbalizable (the J-space) is causally privileged for verbal report, for holding instructed concepts, for unspoken intermediate steps of reasoning and for passing one argument to many functions, while continuation, anomaly detection and line wrapping use the same information without routing through it; it carries workspace-like content only in a middle band of layers, holds on the order of tens of active directions and under a tenth of activation variance, and is amplified and relayed by the weights more than other directions

## Source

LIT-1347, read in full in NOTE-1069. What the source measured is THEORY-865, which this entry extends.

## The claim

Gurnee, Sofroniew et al. argue that the directions a model is disposed to
verbalize form a functional analogue of a global workspace: information
routed through them can be reported, held, used as an unspoken step and
passed to many functions, while routine processing uses the same information
without them.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried word for word from THEORY-865 when that entry was narrowed. More readings of the same source cannot settle it.
