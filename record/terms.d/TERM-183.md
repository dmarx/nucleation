# TERM-183: Goodhart effect

## Definition

What fails is the *measure*. The indicator stops carrying information about
the target, because the governed found ways to move it that do not move the
target. In [CLAIM-1375](../claims.d/CLAIM-1375.md)'s table, this is proxy failure. Its job is to name
the failure that a better indicator cures.

## Distinctions

From the Campbell effect ([TERM-190](TERM-190.md)), by [DISTINCTION-003](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-003.md).
