# LIT-tmpokfxi: Deeper Microchimerism Study Sheds New Light on a Mother’s Cellular Gift

Cincinnati Children's Research Horizons (2025). "Deeper Microchimerism Study
Sheds New Light on a Mother’s Cellular Gift." Post of 30 September 2025, on
research by Yanyan Peng, Sing Sing Way and colleagues.

## Key takeaways

- In mice, offspring's tolerance of non-inherited maternal antigens depends
  on a very small subset of maternal microchimeric cells, those marked by
  LysM and CD11c.
- Eliminating that subset removes the tolerance without eliminating most
  maternal microchimeric cells, so persistence and tolerogenic function
  come apart.
- The post gives the order of magnitude: about one cell in a million in
  the body is of maternal origin.
