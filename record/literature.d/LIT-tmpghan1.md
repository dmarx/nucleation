# LIT-tmpghan1: Dynamic Patterns: The Self-Organization of Brain and Behavior

J. A. Scott Kelso (1995), *Dynamic Patterns: The Self-Organization of Brain
and Behavior*, Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, xvii + 334 pp.

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read. Nothing below is a reading of the book.*

- Cited by De Jaegher & Di Paolo ([LIT-tmp9mw46](LIT-tmp9mw46.md)) for absolute versus
  relative coordination, with a quotation from p. 98: relative coordination
  is "far more variable, plastic and fluid... than pure phase locking".
- Cited by Kelso 2021 ([LIT-tmpw3fkg](LIT-tmpw3fkg.md)) as the source of its Box 1
  coordination potential, for the history of HKB (ch. 3), for metastability
  as the account of relative coordination, and for brain rhythms (ch. 8).
