# THEORY-tmpigigb: BDSM self-definition spans scene roles to identification

Niebudek and Iniewicz, "Between a role and identification" (2023), [LIT-tmpdbb0m](../literature.d/LIT-tmpdbb0m.md).

## The account

The authors' terms are "identification (as a process of developing an
identity) and a role (assumed in a given situation)" (p. 128).
Identification "also has reference to everyday life", while a role is
entered "only in the course of the session limited in time", and "it is
rather a continuum between them" (p. 141).

## What it does not show

- **What the continuum is a continuum of.** The paper never says.
- **That intermediate positions exist.** No participant is placed between
  the poles.
- **That the stages occur in sequence.** No individual's path is traced.
