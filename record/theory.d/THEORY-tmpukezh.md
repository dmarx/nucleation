# THEORY-tmpukezh: Facial displays signal social motives, not emotions

Fridlund, *Human Facial Expression: An Evolutionary View* (1994).

## The account, as expected

Fridlund proposes that facial displays are social tools that signal the displayer's social motives and intentions to an audience, real or imagined, and are not readouts of an inner emotional state; displays vary with the audience even when the displayer is alone, because the audience is implicit.

This is the account the record expects to find. It is not quoted from the
work, which is unread.

## Bearing on the record
