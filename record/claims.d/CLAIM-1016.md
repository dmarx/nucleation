# CLAIM-1016: Authorization granted question by question puts its cost between formulating a question and investigating it, on the same budget, while a standing grant pays it once for a domain before any question in it is formulated, so the remedies for the circle between evidence and permission work by changing the unit of authorization, and authorization's cost belongs in the joint inquiry relation as a third cost on the shared budget

## The gap

A56 §3 prices authorization per question: C_perm(q; S, G), and for a
sequential workflow T_perm(q) + T_investigate(q) + T_realize(q) ≤ H_q. The
order is formulate, make legible, evaluate, authorize, investigate. A56 §4
and §5 then propose "a small exploratory budget", "a limited experiment",
and, in the study ([CASE-388](../cases.d/CASE-388.md)), protected exploration with staged
approval. These grant authority over a domain, a budget or a stretch of
time before any one question in it is formulated or made legible. A56 does
not say why that answers the circle of §5 ([CLAIM-1140](CLAIM-1140.md)); per-question
pricing cannot say it, since under a standing grant no question is priced.

## The claim

- **Per-question authorization** puts C_perm and T_perm between
  formulating q and investigating it, and makes the evidence for q wait on
  q's authorization: the circle.
- **A standing grant** pays the cost once, for a domain D, before its
  questions are formulated. For each q in D, T_perm(q) is near zero, and
  evidence can be produced first and made legible after. The order becomes
  authorize D, formulate, investigate, then make the result legible for the
  consequential decision. That is A56's progression in [CLAIM-1119](CLAIM-1119.md), and
  the preauthorization that keeps A54's ten-day opportunity open in
  [CASE-386](../cases.d/CASE-386.md).
- So the remedies work by **changing the unit of authorization**, not by
  making any one proposal cheaper to justify. They are amortization: like
  stored work ([CLAIM-761](CLAIM-761.md)), a grant paid once changes which questions fit a
  fixed per-question budget.
- In A58's joint relation ([CLAIM-1190](CLAIM-1190.md)), authorization is a third cost
  in the same composition as formulating and answering, under the
  governance arrangement G. By [CLAIM-1115](CLAIM-1115.md) the composition rule is what
  the separate frontiers miss; the unit of authorization is part of that
  rule.

## What it does not say

That standing grants are better. They move the evaluation to the domain, and
a domain can be misjudged as a proposal can; and they move the cost of
legibility to the result, which must still be made legible before any
consequential action. The claim is about where the circle opens.
