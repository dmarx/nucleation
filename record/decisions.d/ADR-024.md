# ADR-024: Agent, individual, self and person; four unities kept apart

## Context

[ADR-015](ADR-015.md) gave the record `self-and-personhood`, one word for "what a self
or a person is and what makes one persist". It was carried by 42
documents. The owner asked for a batch of readings on agency, control,
the self and self-governance, and with it two distinctions:

- **Agent, individual, self and person should not be synonyms.**
- **Behavioural integration, phenomenal unity, narrative unity and
  normative self-governance should be represented separately.**

The single word made both conflations easy. A paper on personal identity
over time (Parfit) and a paper on the pre-reflective minimal self
(Gallagher) sat under one tag. A collective that pursues a goal was
called a "Self" in one summary and an "agent" in a theory, with nothing
in the record marking the difference.

## Decision

Retire `self-and-personhood`. Keep four words for four things:

- **`agency`**: being an agent, meaning goals, action, control and the
  capacity to act.
- **`individuation`**: being one thing, including biological
  individuality and organisational closure.
- **`self`**: being a subject. It covers the minimal, bodily and
  pre-reflective self, self-models, and the self as process or as
  context.
- **`personhood`**: being a person. It covers personal identity over
  time, what matters in survival, and persons as bearers of reasons and
  responsibility.

Each blurb says what the word is not.

Add four words for the four unities:

- **`behavioral-integration`**: how many control systems act as one. It
  covers action selection, competition between habitual and
  goal-directed or automatic and willed control, hierarchical and
  feedback control, intertemporal conflict, and evidence accumulation.
- **`phenomenal-unity`**: the unity of experience.
- **`narrative-unity`**: the unity of a life as a story.
- **`self-governance`**: governing oneself by standards that are one's
  own. It covers identification, planning agency, self-constitution,
  autonomy and authenticity.

Every word except `behavioral-integration` joins the philosophy group.
That one is mostly cognitive science and neuroscience.

### The retagging

- **The retired word.** All 42 documents that carried it were moved to
  `self`, `personhood` or both, with the replacement first wherever the
  old word was first.
- **The unity words.** Twenty-one further documents took one or more of
  them: Frankfurt, Velleman, the autonomy surveys, [THEORY-029](../theory.d/THEORY-029.md) and
  [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) took `self-governance`; Brooks, Minsky and Frijda took
  `behavioral-integration`; IIT and Dennett took `phenomenal-unity`.
- **[LIT-142](../literature.d/LIT-142.md).** One forced choice was undone. Meincke on pregnancy is
  about whether a pregnant organism is one individual or two, and does
  not discuss persons, so it keeps `individuation` and takes neither new
  word.

### Prose corrected

The retagging found sentences that ran the terms together, and they were
corrected:

- **Levin's "Selves" ([LIT-439](../literature.d/LIT-439.md), [THEORY-043](../theory.d/THEORY-043.md)).** They are goal-pursuing
  collectives. In this record's terms that makes them agents and
  individuals, with no account of a subject.
- **Shanahan's selves ([LIT-466](../literature.d/LIT-466.md)).** They pick out the same bearers as
  Goldstein and Lederman's agents. That is not the claim that self is
  agent.
- **[LIT-460](../literature.d/LIT-460.md).** Selfhood and personal identity are now separate
  questions.
- **[THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md).** It locates integration in "one's" values, as SDT does,
  not in "the person's".

## Alternatives considered

- **Subtopics under one word**, in the way the anthology placed
  `flows-and-transport` under `generative-modeling`. That would keep
  `self-and-personhood` as the parent. The owner's point is that these
  are not species of one thing: an agent need not be a self, and a self
  need not be a person.
- **A separate `unities` vocabulary on its own field.** It would be
  checkable apart from topics, but it is a second axis every filing must
  remember. The unities are also the subject of the documents that carry
  them, which is what topics are for.
- **Keep the word and fix the prose.** The conflation is structural. One
  tag gives a browsing reader one page for Parfit and for Gallagher, and
  no wording in the summaries fixes that.
