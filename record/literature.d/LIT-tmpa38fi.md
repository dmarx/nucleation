# LIT-tmpa38fi: On the Design of Compliant Mechanisms Using Topology Optimization

Sigmund, O. (1997). On the Design of Compliant Mechanisms Using Topology Optimization. *Mechanics of Structures and Machines* 25(4): 493–524. doi:10.1080/08905459708945415.

## Key takeaways

*Not read. What follows is the abstract on the DTU Orbit record and what a
later work says of it, unverified against the original.*

- **The method**, on the abstract's account: continuum topology
  optimization finds the compliant-mechanism topology for a given design
  domain and given input and output force positions and directions.
- **Stress control**, on the same account: by a constraint on the allowed
  displacement at the input port.
- **The objective**, on Krishnakumar and Suresh's account ([LIT-tmptb467](LIT-tmptb467.md),
  Sec. 2): mechanical advantage maximized subject to flexibility
  constraints, with the relationships among formulations discussed.
- **Prototypes**, on the abstract's account: some designs made in Nylon
  and in micromachined glass.

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on constraint architectures cites it as M4, saying
that it "explicitly develops a method for identifying compliant-mechanism
topologies from specified input and output behavior". The abstract
supports a method from given input and output forces; whether the paper
also specifies output displacements or ratios, and whether "explicitly"
is fair, cannot be checked unread. The sentence is A's paraphrase.

Krishnakumar and Suresh (2015), [LIT-tmptb467](LIT-tmptb467.md), read in its place, stand in
for it only partly: they restate its objective in one line and give the
setting of its problem, and nothing of its formulation, its stress
control, its examples or its prototypes. The record has no other entry on
compliant mechanisms or topology optimization. A lawful copy, from the
owner or from DTU, would return it to reading.
