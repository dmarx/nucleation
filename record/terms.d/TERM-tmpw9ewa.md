# TERM-tmpw9ewa: fidelity, as selective preservation of decision-relevant observational information under a communication constraint, relative to an interpreter

## Definition

A108 §12, fourth conclusion: "**Fidelity is selective preservation of
decision-relevant observational information under a communication constraint,
relative to an interpreter and its available side information.**" It combines
inferential sufficiency ([TERM-tmpsjjot](TERM-tmpsjjot.md)), the rate constraint that [TERM-tmp9qq3b](TERM-tmp9qq3b.md) carried at A100,
and the receiver's side information. Elsewhere the argument says "a family of
operational fidelity criteria" (§24), and its one-passage summary says "graded,
observer- and task-relative preservation of relational and informational
structure".

## What it is not

Not the manuscript's functional L = L_obs + λL_str + γL_dec, which has no rate
constraint and no interpreter term. The manuscript relativizes fidelity to the
receiver only in §5 (side information) and in its last sentence. The symmetry
sense ([TERM-tmpy2t4f](TERM-tmpy2t4f.md)) is a separate notion again.
