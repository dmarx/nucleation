# LIT-tmpypm1w: Full-body illusions and minimal phenomenal selfhood

Blanke, O. & Metzinger, T. (2009). *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 13(1):7–13. doi:10.1016/j.tics.2008.10.003. PMID 19058991.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpyzsg9](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpyzsg9.md).*

- **MPS defined.** "The experience of being a distinct, holistic entity
  capable of global self-control and attention, possessing a body and a
  location in space and time" (p. 7). It has three parts: self-identification
  with the body as a whole, spatiotemporal self-location, and a weak 1PP.
  The weak 1PP is "a purely geometrical feature" of a model of reality, its
  centre of projection.
- **Three readings of the first-person perspective.** The weak 1PP is
  constitutive of MPS. The strong 1PP, the self represented as directed at
  an object, needs MPS. The cognitive 1PP, a self-applied concept "I", is
  set aside.
- **Partial against global ownership.** Somatoparaphrenia and the
  rubber-hand illusion change ownership of a part. They "do not enable the
  investigation of MPS" (p. 9). Full-body illusions do.
- **Autoscopic phenomena as MPS profiles (Box 2, Fig. 1).** Autoscopic
  hallucination leaves all three parts normal. In heautoscopy, all three
  are disturbed and may alternate between bodies. In out-of-body
  experience, all three sit at the illusory body. Lesions differ: right
  parieto-occipital or temporo-occipital, left temporo-parietal, and right
  temporo-parietal respectively (Fig. 2).
- **The experiments.** Lenggenhager et al. and Ehrsson are read as
  manipulating self-identification and self-location (Lenggenhager), or
  self-identification and the weak 1PP (Ehrsson). Each produced "a state
  that is comparable to heautoscopy", not an out-of-body experience, and
  neither touched the strong 1PP.
- **Agency.** "A passive, multisensory and globalized experience of
  'owning' a body is sufficient for minimal conscious selfhood". So
  "bodily agency is a causally enabling, but not a constitutive condition,
  for phenomenal selfhood" (p. 13).

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-18 at the owner's request for body-ownership illusion
material. It is the second source of [NOTE-372](../notes.d/NOTE-372.md)'s C6, and the published form
of the claim that "Self models" ([LIT-450](LIT-450.md)) states as global body ownership
being "the most simple form of self-awareness". No anthology topic holds
it, and it carries no instruction for machine-learning practice.

The reading ([NOTE-tmpyzsg9](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpyzsg9.md)) places it **Active**, as the statement of a
conceptual framework, and as a programme rather than a finding. Its
account is filed as [THEORY-tmp6ib7r](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp6ib7r.md), Proposed. The conceptual distinctions
are clear and useful: part against whole, weak against strong 1PP, and the
autoscopic profiles. The empirical claims lean on one experiment
([LIT-tmpeggnf](LIT-tmpeggnf.md)) and describe it more strongly than its data do. C6's
"simplest form of self-awareness" turns out, at source, to be a proposal
about *global* ownership. It is made by defining MPS so that global
ownership is its core. It also excludes the body-part ownership that C6's
first half is mostly about.
