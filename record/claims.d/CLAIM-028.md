# CLAIM-028: No result from the exchange's four experiments reached a later draft: the authored chains were excluded by rule, and the synthetic constructions were dropped without a stated reason

## The claim

Four experiments were run or attempted at U23–U26 ([CASE-017](../cases.d/CASE-017.md), [CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md), [CASE-020](../cases.d/CASE-020.md), [CASE-032](../cases.d/CASE-032.md)).
None of their numbers, texts or plots appears in any later outline or draft,
or in the manuscript. A73 reused E4's chains as a worked example; that is the
last use.

## Why

- **The authored chains (E2, E4) were excluded by rule.** C6 Appendix F,
  "Computational protocol and preregistered tests": "Do not use
  assistant-authored examples as independent LLM samples or the same generative
  model's preferences as an unblinded ground truth." The rule codifies the
  provenance the assistant had attached to them ([CLAIM-085](CLAIM-085.md)). A89: "This remains a
  theoretical manuscript with a specified empirical program, rather than a paper
  claiming experimental validation."
- **The harness (E3) produced no data.** Its design survives as §10 Case II.
- **The synthetic constructions (E1) were dropped with no reason given.** They
  were not samples of anything, so Appendix F's rule does not touch them. They
  were exact existence constructions for three of the manuscript's claims:
  static agreement without dynamic agreement ([CLAIM-117](CLAIM-117.md)), classical order effects
  ([CLAIM-041](CLAIM-041.md)), and convergence without preservation ([CLAIM-081](CLAIM-081.md)). The manuscript states
  all three in prose and gives none of the constructions. The drafts-lineage
  reader also judges this drop inadvertent.

## What it does not say

That the manuscript should report E2 or E4: their authors called them
demonstrations of a measurement approach, not findings.
