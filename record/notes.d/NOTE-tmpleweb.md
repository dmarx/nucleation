# NOTE-tmpleweb: Almarzooqi and others (2026) — From association to causality: mechanistic pathways linking the gut microbiome to psychiatric disorders

## Contribution

A synthesis organized by causal-evidence grade rather than by taxon; explicit
cautions on psychotropic confounding, compositional data, germ-free effect
sizes and vagotomy's lack of specificity.

## Key results

1. **Central question (Introduction).** "do these alterations contribute
   causally to psychiatric pathophysiology, or do they mainly reflect
   downstream effects of illness, medication, diet, lifestyle, and chronic
   stress?"
2. **Graded causality (From association to causal criteria).** "causality in
   microbiome–brain research is best conceptualized as a probabilistic and
   graded construct rather than a binary determination."
3. **GF models overstate (Experimental evidence for causality).** "GF and
   related depletion models may overestimate effect sizes relative to the
   subtler and more heterogeneous forms of dysbiosis observed in humans."
4. **FMT gives sufficiency, not specificity (same section).** "Without
   identifying the active mechanisms, FMT provides evidence of sufficiency but
   not specificity."
5. **Vagotomy (Vagotomy studies).** Effects of *L. rhamnosus* (JB-1) "were
   abolished following vagotomy", but "Standard subdiaphragmatic vagotomy
   disrupts both afferent and efferent pathways".
6. **Human neural mediation (ENS section).** "Despite strong mechanistic
   evidence from animal models, human evidence remains largely associative".
7. **MDD verdict (Causal strength verdict).** "current evidence supports
   moderate causal involvement of GM alterations in depressive symptoms";
   interventions "show modest improvements ..., although direct mediation by
   microbiome changes remains unclear."
8. **Mediation missing (Symptom outcomes and causal inference).** "only a few
   intervention studies simultaneously measure microbiome changes and
   clinical outcomes, and formal mediation analyses are rare."
9. **Anxiety (Mechanistic support and causal strength).** "the overall causal
   evidence for microbiome involvement in anxiety disorders remains weak to
   moderate."
10. **Overall (Conclusion).** "much of the current evidence remains
    associative rather than definitively causal."

## Assumptions

Bradford Hill-style convergence as the standard of causal confidence;
psychiatric diagnoses as the outcome units; the authors' per-disorder grades
("moderate", "weak to moderate") are judgments, not a formal scoring scheme
(none seen in the parts read).

## Concepts

MGBA; graded causal hierarchy; necessity vs sufficiency vs specificity;
reverse causation; psychotropic confounding; compositional data; Mendelian
randomization; causal mediation; psychobiotics; germ-free and gnotobiotic
models; vagotomy; inflammatory subtype of MDD.

## Bearing on the record

**On the LIT's and the exchange's characterization.** A15's account ("a review stressing that associations between the gut
microbiome and psychiatric disorders still leave much of the causal picture
in humans unresolved") is **accurate**. A fuller statement: the review
grades MDD as "moderate causal involvement" while holding that human
mediation by the microbiome is unconfirmed.

- **[CLAIM-166](../claims.d/CLAIM-166.md)** (the microbiome can drive higher-level behaviour and
  experience: routes exist, causal in rodents, modest in humans):
  **supports the claim's grading as stated**. Routes (vagal, immune, HPA,
  metabolite) and rodent causal evidence (GF, FMT phenotype transfer,
  vagotomy-dependent probiotic effects) are reviewed as real; in humans,
  interventions show modest symptom effects but "direct mediation by
  microbiome changes remains unclear". This matches the claim's "can drive"
  established in animals and "does drive" in a person unshown, and its
  2026-10-29 note that no study shows a person's own microbiome change
  originating a change in hunger or affect. It also supplies two cautions the
  claim could carry: GF effect sizes overstate natural variation, and
  reverse causation (illness and medication altering the microbiome) is a
  live rival in every human association. It concerns psychiatric disorders,
  not everyday hunger and mood.

## Limitations

Narrative review, not systematic; no pooled estimates of its own; graded
verdicts without an explicit scoring rubric in the parts read; long
mechanistic sections rely heavily on rodent work; my reading skipped the
immune, HPA, barrier, PTSD, BD and SCZ sections.
