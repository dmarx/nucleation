# LIT-tmp6hqrz: MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems

Packer, Wooders, Lin, Fang, Patil, Stoica and Gonzalez (2023), arXiv
2310.08560.

## Key takeaways

- Not read.

## Standing in the record

Prior art for tiered memory under a bounded context: what an agent keeps in
its context window, what it pages out, and how it pages back in. The
difference A29 §18 draws is that MemGPT's tiers are one agent's, chosen by
the agent, while the record's would be shared and governed. A reading
would say what triggers MemGPT's paging, which bears on the reactivation
triggers in [TERM-tmp96b3g](../terms.d/TERM-tmp96b3g.md).
