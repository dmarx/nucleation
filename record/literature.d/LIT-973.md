# LIT-973: Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness

Cogitate Consortium, Oscar Ferrante, Urszula Gorska-Klimowska, Simon Henin, Rony Hirschhorn, Aya Khalaf et al. (2025), *Nature 642(8066), 133–142*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-812](../notes.d/NOTE-812.md).*

- **The design.** The proponents of both theories preregistered
  divergent predictions and pass/fail criteria. Theory-neutral teams ran
  one paradigm in several laboratories: faces, objects, letters and false
  fonts, in three orientations and three durations, all clearly seen and
  mostly task-irrelevant.
- **Content.** Category was decodable in posterior and prefrontal cortex.
  Face orientation and object identity were represented posteriorly and
  not detectably in prefrontal cortex in iEEG or fMRI, with Bayes factors
  for the null. Adding prefrontal sites never improved decoding.
- **Against GNWT.** None of 655 prefrontal electrodes showed the
  predicted onset-and-offset ignition, and prefrontal category
  information appeared at onset only. The authors ask whether prefrontal
  cortex broadcasts "all conscious content ... or only a subset".
- **Against IIT.** Content-specific synchrony between category-selective
  cortex and V1/V2 was early, brief, 2–25 Hz and mostly evoked, not the
  sustained gamma predicted. Orientation was not sustained in posterior
  cortex either. IIT passed its duration prediction for category.
- **What it tests.** Biological implementations, not cores: "theorists
  could respond ... by modifying the proposed biological implementation".
  No verdict is given.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md) and [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md), and read on
2026-10-14 in [NOTE-812](../notes.d/NOTE-812.md). The filing called it "direct evidence on
whether access is one all-or-none broadcast". That was too strong. It
shows that the prefrontal hub did not represent every dimension of a
clearly seen image, which bears on [QUESTION-061](../questions.d/QUESTION-061.md). But no dimension's
access was measured. [THEORY-245](../theory.d/THEORY-245.md) holds the prefrontal findings, and
[THEORY-296](../theory.d/THEORY-296.md) the failed posterior-synchronization prediction.

It is the "COGITATE results" that [THEORY-096](../theory.d/THEORY-096.md)'s and [THEORY-102](../theory.d/THEORY-102.md)'s
promote_when conditions name. For [THEORY-102](../theory.d/THEORY-102.md) it replicates the
onset-only prefrontal pattern for category in a larger independent
cohort, but finds orientation not sustained in posterior cortex. For
[THEORY-096](../theory.d/THEORY-096.md) it counts against broadcast of the whole content and against
ignition at every change of content. Those accounts are not edited here.
For [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md) it is task-based no-report evidence with percept and
stimulus never apart, so it stays within [THEORY-094](../theory.d/THEORY-094.md)'s limit.
