# NOTE-tmp6ywh9: Morozov (2013) — To Save Everything, Click Here (Carnegie Council talk)

## Contribution

The book's argument in the author's words.

## Key results

1. "We have suddenly acquired the means of perfection".
2. Those who refuse to self-track "will be treated with suspicion".
3. "It's no longer an autonomous decision" when institutions punish not self-tracking.
4. Ambitious reform should aim "to reform the system itself".

## Assumptions

Behaviour can be steered without moral appeal.

## Concepts

Solutionism; self-tracking; gamification; reform of the system.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpbypt7**: corroborates directly, its mechanism and its measure: adoption for real advantage, institutions adapting (insurance pricing), and non-adopters penalized, so declining is "no longer an autonomous decision" (results 2–3). Examples, not evidence. The LIT's earlier "No idea of the exchange rests on it" was wrong and is corrected there.
- **CLAIM-tmpu2cwc**: result 4's contrast between optimizing within a system and reforming it parallels the claim's; no comparative evidence.

## Limitations

A talk; the book's chapters on Internet-centrism and transparency not covered.
