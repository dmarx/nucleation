# CLAIM-475: Specifying is itself a resource-bounded realization: formulating a question takes time, concepts and computation as realizing a configuration takes energy and matter, so the questions an agent can ask are a product of what it has already realized; a stabilized specification is past inquiry stored so that later questions become cheaper to ask, and it can make other questions harder

## The claim

The owner, at U17, after [CLAIM-470](CLAIM-470.md): "probably also relevant that resource
constraints impact specification in exactly the same way as they impact
realization. I can't interrogate a question that takes me more time than I
have to even ask it."

A17's reading: "**Specifications are themselves resource-bounded
realizations.**" The owner's example is the limiting case. It is not too
little time to answer a question but too little to bring it into a form in
which answering could begin. A17 §1 separated three stages, each with its own
bound: specification (time, vocabulary, memory, computation), investigation
(instruments, interventions, coordination) and realization (energy, matter,
conditions). The boundaries are not sharp, "Specifying a question is already
a physical, cognitive, or computational realization."

A17 §2 proposed a set of askable questions for an agent a with concepts K,
budget B and deadline H. A question q is in it if some procedure available to
a, within B and H, formulates q, which means making q determinate enough to
pursue, not answering it. It is distinct from the investigable and the
answerable questions. "**the bottleneck can occur before the question exists as
an actionable epistemic object**."

## Three consequences

**A stabilized specification is stored inquiry.** Calculus makes questions
about instantaneous rates cheap to ask. Bifurcation theory does the same for
questions about stability loss and hysteresis. A17 §5: "The prior
specification becomes **cognitive infrastructure**. It amortizes the cost of
earlier inquiry over many subsequent inquiries." A17 §6 called this a time-scale
transformation: expensive historical inquiry, then a durable specification,
then cheap repeated operation. The specification works as a constraint "precisely
because the work of establishing it need not be repeated during every
realization." This is [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md)'s mechanism applied to inquiry. A slow,
costly process leaves a product that the fast process treats as fixed.

**The same storage entrenches.** Familiar distinctions are cheap and new ones
are dear, so "an apparatus may expand its capacity for inquiry while
simultaneously entrenching a particular mode of individuation" (A17 §5). For a
record: "If revising a schema is vastly more expensive than adding another
record under the existing schema, inquiry may remain trapped in a locally
convenient representation. … **The cost of changing a distinction influences how
long the distinction persists.**" This is [CLAIM-184](CLAIM-184.md)'s generative entrenchment
reached from cost instead of dependence.

**Progress can be measured in questions.** A17 §7: progress "can consist in
expanding the set of questions that can meaningfully be investigated under
fixed resources", and a new framework can change the shape of that set as
well as its size. It gives the claim a test (defeater b). A17's words name
the investigable set, the askable questions with budget left to pursue them
([TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)). Defeater (b) tests the askable set, which bounds the investigable
from above, so a framework that makes new questions formulable meets the test
even where pursuing them stays out of reach.

## What it changes in the record

**[TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md) takes its query family as supplied.** A17 §8 called this "A
correction to Nucleation's existing formalism": 𝒬 is "itself an output of
bounded activity". It proposed a budget-limited process that generates 𝒬_t
from concepts, budget and horizon, with the regime ℛ_t built on it and the
concepts revised by findings. It kept [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md)'s reason for fixing ℛ during a
comparison: "**The fixed inquiry regime is a local methodological commitment,
not a claim that inquiry regimes are exogenously given.**" This claim does not
amend [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md). It says what [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md) leaves outside its scope.

**[TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) asks whether a distinction is legible for a task.** A17 §4: the new
question is "whether the agent can **construct the task that would make that
distinction relevant**." So "Physical individuation ⇏ Feasible epistemic
individuation". Askability is not existence, and an agent can also articulate
distinctions with no causal significance.

**[TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s correctability** prices finding and carrying out a correction.
A17 named the missing extension: "situations in which the correct objective or
question has not yet become formulable."

## Prior art

- **Simon** ([LIT-1634](../literature.d/LIT-1634.md)): constructing the representation of an
  ill-structured problem is part of the work.
- **Lieder and Griffiths** ([LIT-1071](../literature.d/LIT-1071.md), read): resource-rational analysis prices
  cognitive operations. A17's addition: "even constructing the task
  representation and the candidate questions must be included in the
  resource budget. Otherwise, the model risks making the most important
  cognitive operation free."
- **Fagin and Halpern** ([LIT-1643](../literature.d/LIT-1643.md)): the awareness operator. An agent
  lacking the concepts cannot entertain the proposition.
- **Thorstad** ([LIT-1662](../literature.d/LIT-1662.md)): rational inquiry under bounds.
- **Hutchins** ([LIT-1060](../literature.d/LIT-1060.md), Unavailable): capacities that sit in artifacts and
  divisions of labor.

The physical half of the owner's symmetry has its own form. An apparatus can
only discriminate what its coupling, state capacity and dynamics allow (A19
§10). And recovering a global structure from pairwise evidence costs
computation, even when the structure exists (Singer, [LIT-1655](../literature.d/LIT-1655.md); A21 §8).

## What it does not claim

The owner wrote "in exactly the same way". The claim keeps the symmetry of
kind and not of measure. A18 §5: "Time, energy, money, memory, and political
authority cannot simply be added together without further assumptions." Nor
does a bound on askable questions bound what exists. A17 §9: those dependencies
"do not determine what exists independently of us. But they help determine
which aspects of reality can become objects of determinate investigation for
particular agents and institutions."

## Note of 2026-10-27 (evening): the readings of the U15–U23 works

- **Simon** ([NOTE-1232](../notes.d/NOTE-1232.md); [THEORY-1072](../theory.d/THEORY-1072.md)) supports the claim in his own
  terms:
  - "there are no WSPs, only ISPs that have been formalized for problem
    solvers" (p. 186);
  - AI theory had left "the processes for formulating that approximation"
    outside its scope (p. 186);
  - once a problem space exists for a kind of task, later tasks need none
    (p. 187), which is the amortization;
  - new information "exercises a strong influence in the direction of
    conserving existing problem formulations" (p. 199), which is the
    entrenchment.

  Two qualifications. He rejects the strong form, that structuring is the
  real problem solving (p. 187). And his bound is seriality, how much of
  what is known can bear on processing at once, not the time it takes to
  formulate a question. Carrying it to questions and inquiry is the
  record's step.
- **Singer** ([NOTE-1224](../notes.d/NOTE-1224.md); [THEORY-1067](../theory.d/THEORY-1067.md), [THEORY-1056](../theory.d/THEORY-1056.md)) separates
  three regimes where the claim and A21 had two:
  - below an information bound, no estimator of any cost recovers the
    configuration (Fano, §5);
  - between that bound and the eigenvector method's threshold, which only
    exists for L ≥ 7, an exhaustive search might succeed where the cheap
    method fails;
  - above both, recovery is cheap.

  So "inability to find a gluing within available resources" needs a third
  case beside "failure to glue": the evidence does not determine the gluing
  at all. In his model it is evidence, not computation, that binds.
