# LEAD-tmptd829: Read the work on biodiversity and ecosystem functioning, similarity-sensitive diversity measures and diverse problem-solving groups, and set what it found against the thesis that diversity generally measures an ecosystem's richness

## The plan

Register and read, under the `read-and-file` skill, candidates from three
bodies of work. The identifiers below are from memory and must be checked
at registration (step 3).

1. **Biodiversity and ecosystem functioning.** The insurance hypothesis
   (Yachi and Loreau, 1999, in PNAS): diversity buffers a community's
   functioning against environmental fluctuation because species respond
   differently. It is the ecological form of A60 §4's hedge, and it
   specifies the condition [CLAIM-tmp7iie0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7iie0.md) found the hedge needs: variation
   in what the future demands.
2. **Diversity indices that weigh similarity.** Leinster and Cobbold
   (2012, in Ecology) define a family of diversity measures sensitive to
   how similar species are. A60 §2's "diversity of consequential
   capabilities" asks for an index of this kind, with similarity taken
   over what constituents can ask and answer.
3. **Diverse problem-solving groups.** Hong and Page (2004, in PNAS) on
   groups of diverse problem solvers against groups of the best
   individual solvers, and the critiques of that result. It is the closest
   formal analogue of A60's coverage model in social epistemology, beside
   Zollman ([THEORY-148](../theory.d/THEORY-148.md)), which the record holds.

## What it would settle

Whether the conditions on aggregation, shared resources and routing that
[CLAIM-tmp7iie0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7iie0.md) found decisive in A60's model are the ones the empirical
and formal literature found decisive, and so whether "generally" in
[CLAIM-tmp6ik2v](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6ik2v.md) holds of the ecosystems that literature studied.

## What it would not settle

Whether the explanation through the query and answer frontiers adds
anything to the ecological one; that is the second part of
[CLAIM-tmp6ik2v](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6ik2v.md), and needs the reading's results set against it, not only
the readings.
