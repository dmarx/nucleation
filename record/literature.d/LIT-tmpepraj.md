# LIT-tmpepraj: From Plantation to Paradise? Cultural Politics and Musical Theatre in French Slave Colonies, 1764–1789

David M. Powers (2014). *From Plantation to Paradise? Cultural Politics and Musical Theatre in French Slave Colonies, 1764–1789*. Michigan State University Press. ISBN 9781611861204.

## Key takeaways

*Not read; as A describes it.* A1 and A3 describe it as an account of music, opera and theatre in the French Caribbean slave colonies (Saint-Domingue, Martinique, Guadeloupe), in which culture is one of the ways colonial power is produced and legitimized; racially stratified theatre seating, with the upper gallery (*paradis*) for nonwhite spectators, giving the title its irony; and free and enslaved Black performers, Minette and Lise among them, taking part in French opera without the racial order being overturned. A3 says Powers identifies peace, hierarchy and the promotion of French culture as linked colonial aims. A3 once calls Powers "her"; Crossref's records give David M. Powers. Unverified until read.
