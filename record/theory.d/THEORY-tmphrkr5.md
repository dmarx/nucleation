# THEORY-tmphrkr5: Jiménez Gutiérrez et al. report that indexing a corpus as a schemaless entity graph extracted by an LLM and retrieving passages by Personalized PageRank seeded from query entities improves single-step multi-hop retrieval over dense retrievers on MuSiQue and 2WikiMultiHopQA, matching iterative retrieval (IRCoT) at a fraction of its cost, and that new knowledge can be integrated by adding edges rather than re-summarizing.

## The account

Jiménez Gutiérrez et al. report that indexing a corpus as a schemaless entity graph extracted by an LLM and retrieving passages by Personalized PageRank seeded from query entities improves single-step multi-hop retrieval over dense retrievers on MuSiQue and 2WikiMultiHopQA, matching iterative retrieval (IRCoT) at a fraction of its cost, and that new knowledge can be integrated by adding edges rather than re-summarizing.
