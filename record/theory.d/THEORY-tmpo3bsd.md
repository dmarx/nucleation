# THEORY-tmpo3bsd: Clark and Brennan propose that collective action rests on common ground that participants must update by grounding — the collective process of reaching mutual belief that what was meant has been understood well enough for current purposes, ordinarily through positive evidence such as acknowledgment, a relevant next turn or continued attention — and that the techniques and costs of grounding vary with purpose and with media constraints such as copresence, cotemporality, reviewability and revisability.

## The account

Clark and Brennan propose that collective action rests on common ground that participants must update by grounding — the collective process of reaching mutual belief that what was meant has been understood well enough for current purposes, ordinarily through positive evidence such as acknowledgment, a relevant next turn or continued attention — and that the techniques and costs of grounding vary with purpose and with media constraints such as copresence, cotemporality, reviewability and revisability.
