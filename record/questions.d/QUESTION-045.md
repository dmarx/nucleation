# QUESTION-045: Is a mental disorder that outlasts its trigger a protective lock-in at the individual scale, a regime kept stable by behaviour that blocks its own disconfirmation while the capacity to leave it degrades, and what would show that the maintaining link, not the regime's content, is what keeps it in place?

## The question

[TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md) defines protective lock-in: a protective regime reorganizes activity so that it "generates the conditions that justify its own continuation and reduces the capacity to leave it". [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md) says a regime can be stable without being viable. [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) says the operative regime and the capacity to transform it can fail separately. The record's cases are firms. This question asks whether a maintained disorder is the same mechanism in a person, not an analogy: feedback through the conditions of efficacy, and not the contents of a fearful belief. It also asks what evidence would show that.

## Why the record needs it

An individual case would be a second realizer of [CLAIM-099](../claims.d/CLAIM-099.md)'s result, and one with a treatment literature that intervenes on the maintaining links. [CLAIM-018](../claims.d/CLAIM-018.md) reads the result through Canguilhem as an "illuminating analogue". This would test it on disorder itself.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-058](../theory.d/THEORY-058.md): disorders are "maintained by self-reinforcing causal loops" and "outlast their triggers": a stable state kept up by hysteresis. Safety behaviour "keeps the meaning from being disconfirmed". Limit: no individual time series yet.
- [THEORY-050](../theory.d/THEORY-050.md): experiential avoidance becomes "a general strategy" and "behaviour narrows". That is a loss of the capacity to leave the regime. Limit: correlational, mostly self-report.
- [THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md): thwarting yields "rigid behavior patterns" that "persist into new situations in which they are not needed". Limit: direction unknown.
- [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md) says what evidence counts: change in the theorised process that is "specific and comes before the outcome". Cutting a link while holding exposure fixed is the will line's intervention criterion ([QUESTION-015](QUESTION-015.md)) in clinical form.
- [THEORY-285](../theory.d/THEORY-285.md) ([LIT-983](../literature.d/LIT-983.md), [NOTE-766](../notes.d/NOTE-766.md)): in an active-inference model, an overly precise prior that one is in a harmful context makes exploration look uninformative, so the agent withholds the evidence that would correct it. Restoring exploratory sampling rebalances the prior; lowering precision only removes symptoms. It answers the question in part, at the model level only. Limit: illustrative simulation with no individual data, and the two intervention arms differ in environment, so exposure is not held fixed while the link is cut.

## What would answer it

An experiment that cuts one maintaining link, such as safety behaviour, while holding the regime's content fixed, and produces lasting change that the same exposure without the cut does not. Or individual data showing hysteresis.

## Note of 2026-10-18: overcontrol, and an organizational transfer

The exchange of 2026-10-17 brought a treatment literature this question did
not list. A42, at the owner's U42 prompt on ACT and DBT, added Lynch's
radically open DBT, which "explicitly addresses maladaptive *overcontrol*,
rather than treating deficient self-control as the primary problem". In this
question's terms overcontrol is a candidate individual-scale protective
lock-in whose maintaining link is inhibition itself: a regime of control that
restricts the openness and feedback that would show it is no longer needed.
The evidence is thin. A42: "promising but not definitive", with one
randomized trial (Lynch et al. 2020) and one systematic review (Hatoum and
Burton 2024). Neither is held yet.

A42 then ran the transfer the other way, from the individual to the
organization: tighter safeguards that yield "Less disclosure of near misses"
and "Fewer challenges to mistaken decisions". That is filed as
[CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md), whose defeater includes this question: the organizational
form must predict something the individual-scale lock-in account does not.

A29 §8 offered a dynamical form for "individual data showing hysteresis",
ż = μz − z³ + h, which [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md)'s note sets out with its caveat.
