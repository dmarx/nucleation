# THEORY-458: Within the minimal self, the sense that a movement or a thought is mine and the sense that I am its source are distinct and come apart, as in involuntary movement and thought insertion; on Frith's comparator model the sense of agency depends on a forward model that precedes action and ownership on sensory feedback, so failed forward monitoring would explain thought insertion and, by the same mechanism, why first-person reference ordinarily cannot misidentify its subject

## Source

Gallagher (2000), "Philosophical conceptions of the self: implications for
cognitive science", *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 4(1): 14–21, [LIT-453](../literature.d/LIT-453.md),
read in [NOTE-377](../notes.d/NOTE-377.md) from the preprint.

## What was actually shown

**Two selves.** The minimal self is "a self devoid of temporal extension",
limited to what is "accessible to immediate and present
self-consciousness". The narrative self is "extended in time to include
memories of the past and intentions toward the future". The review presents
them as distinct and both relevant to cognitive science; it does not argue
that they exhaust the field (C1).

**Ownership and agency.** The sense of ownership is that it is my body
moving or my experience; the sense of agency is that I am the initiator.
They coincide in voluntary action and come apart in involuntary movement.
Ownership is ecological self-awareness from sensory feedback (Neisser;
Gallagher and Marcel). Agency comes from what "precedes action", with
lateralised readiness potentials and supplementary motor area stimulation
cited (Haggard and Eimer; Haggard and Magno).

**The comparator.** On Frith's model a non-conscious forward model compares
efference copy with intention. Patients with thought insertion and
delusions of control are reported to show deficits in forward monitoring
but not in feedback-based control, with abnormal premovement potentials.
Extended to inner speech, failed forward monitoring gives thoughts that are
in one's own stream and do not seem self-generated: ownership without
agency. Frith's patient: "They come from this chap, Chris. They're his
thoughts."

**The immunity principle.** Using "I" as subject involves no
identification and so cannot misidentify (after Wittgenstein and
Shoemaker). Campbell offers thought insertion as a counterexample. Whether
it is one is left open, but if a mechanism explains the failure, the
working mechanism explains the principle.

**What could have come out otherwise.** Patients could have shown deficits
in feedback-based control as well, which would not separate the two senses.
As reported, the deficit is in forward monitoring.

## What this does not say

- **That the neuroscience is shown here.** It is reported from the cited
  studies, and the reading took the extension to thought on trust.
- **That thought insertion is a real exception to immunity.** The review
  leaves it open and cites doubts.
- **Which minimal self is meant.** The embodied self of newborn imitation
  (Meltzoff and Moore) and Strawson's reflective "bare locus of
  consciousness" differ in whether they are conceptual and conscious, and
  the review does not say which one the ownership and agency analysis is
  about.
- **That a robot has a minimal self.** Tani's robot, which takes its own
  state into account only when top-down and bottom-up dynamics conflict,
  has a momentary self in a functional sense, bought by dropping Strawson's
  requirement that the self be a conscious subject. The reading rates that
  claim weak.
- **That the narrative self is the sum of its narratives.** Gallagher's
  proposal, a self "decentered, distributed and multiplex" that holds its
  self-deceptions, is asserted in two paragraphs and is not part of this
  account.
- **That the text is the typeset one.** The preprint has reference slips
  ("Walpert", "Deprati", Meltzoff and Moore's dates) that may be corrected
  in print.

## Connections

- **The sense of agency.** [THEORY-322](THEORY-322.md) holds that the sense of agency over an
  outcome draws on prospective signals from action selection, not only on
  retrospective comparison of outcome with prediction, and reports its
  prospective part absent in schizophrenia. That qualifies the comparator
  account here rather than contradicting it: both put agency in processes
  that run before the outcome. [THEORY-300](THEORY-300.md) reports the sense of agency
  reduced under coercion.
- **Narrative selves.** Dennett's single centre of narrative gravity
  ([THEORY-691](THEORY-691.md)) is the model the review argues against. Strawson
  ([THEORY-684](THEORY-684.md)) is the source of the reflective minimal self here, and
  later counts revision against narrative views where Gallagher builds it
  in.
- **Self-models and contexts.** Metzinger's transparent self-model
  ([THEORY-064](THEORY-064.md)) and relational frame theory's self as context ([THEORY-044](THEORY-044.md)),
  which is set against the minimal self there, are the record's other
  accounts of the self at a moment.
- **Experience.** Tani's robot is a machine self-model with no claim about
  experience, which is the line [THEORY-023](THEORY-023.md) draws.
