# SYNTHESIS-tmp2p39p: Keeping the proposition does not keep the act: renderings that keep it can perform a different act, a rendering judged to keep the act while changing the proposition has one documented case, which a coarser proposition answers, understanding and the situation of saying are offered as what a rendering must also keep, and none of it is yet measured, since no measure of footing or stance is validated, the exchange's chains and harness gave no result, and the tests pool unnamed judges

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part on renderings
that keep the proposition and change the act, footing and stance, filed
from the exchange on Hofstadter, Henley's Villon and the manuscript "What
survives translation" (2026-10-06 to 2026-10-10), with the cases the
searches of 2026-10-10 found and the record's audit of the line of
2026-10-10. The line's account is built over it and the accounts of its
sibling parts. The mechanical map put seven structuralist entries in the
same part; they link to the rest by one pair, and their account is
separate (the value part). Every component carries pragmatic-transport
only.*

## The account

- **Not sufficient.** CLAIM-061 (the manuscript's §1, first stated at
  A12; thesis, interpretive, Active): "Renderings that keep the
  proposition can perform a different communicative act, so preserving
  the proposition is not sufficient for preserving the communicative
  event." Its grounds are CASE-027 (A12's, fictional, Active), Henley
  "translated every term perfectly while making the speaker sound like an
  academic sociologist", "propositions held fixed, footing changed"; the
  invented reattributions of CASE-018 (another part); and CASE-056's
  handbooks: where kissing is "exclusively an expression of amorous or
  erotic interest", a literal rendering keeps the proposition and changes
  the act. CASE-012 (A6 to A12's invented refrains, fictional, Active)
  illustrates it and shows "that such variation is possible, not how
  readers judge it". Its note of 2026-10-28: "What remains is modal and
  has a documented case."
- **Not necessary, contested.** CLAIM-486 (A12's sentence and the
  manuscript's §1, split from CLAIM-061 at the status review of
  2026-10-28; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): "A rendering can keep the
  communicative act while changing the proposition". Its one ground is
  CASE-056 (a documented specimen found at the owner's request, Active):
  Phillips renders Paul's "holy kiss" as "a hearty handshake all round",
  and Nida judged it to keep the greeting. CASE-056 sets out the
  defender's reply, that "the proposition worth keeping is the coarser
  'greet one another warmly'", and that it "settles nothing between the
  two individuations. It forces the choice." That reply is CLAIM-486's
  `defeated_if`. Two routes to the half are Rejected. ARG-021 (the
  manuscript's inference, existence, Rejected): on CLAIM-229's logic,
  CASE-018 read in reverse "is still the same words, so still P without
  A". ARG-020 (CLAIM-053's inference, cause-to-effect, Rejected): "The
  hypothesis says that lexical infidelity can be required for pragmatic
  fidelity, which is about words, and a change of wording can keep the
  proposition." CASE-031 (the exchange's founding case, documented
  on a report, Active) was checked for the role and "does not fill that
  role": its renderings "keep almost all of Marot's propositions".
- **Constraints are not the measure.** CLAIM-020 (Hofstadter's criterion
  as A4 reports it; supposed, Rejected): quality is the number of
  preserved constraints. CASE-011 (documented, Active) is its
  counterexample, a version that "violates many of them" and that
  Hofstadter came to judge perhaps the best; it is a variant of CASE-031,
  which shows "that several preservation targets compete".
- **What else must be kept.** CLAIM-054 (A35 §7, recovered; thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed): "interpretive and performative fidelity come
  apart", on CASE-026 (A35's, stipulated, Active), where contemporary
  slang may make the speaker "sound like an outsider imitating that
  subculture". CLAIM-064 (A12's, recovered; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed): "A translation reconstructs not only what is said but the
  situation of its saying"; it names "what is reconstructed, but not who
  must be able to recover it". QUESTION-023 (the assistant's at A6 and
  A9, Open) asks whether the opacity of insider vocabulary, as in
  Henley's Villon (CASE-010, documented, Active), can be "a preservation
  target in its own right".
- **No measure yet.** CLAIM-227 (the record's audit of 2026-10-10;
  granted, empirical, Active): "No validated measure of footing or stance
  exists in the record, and the one repeated assignment of footing, the
  analyst's own, changed between passes", on CASE-012's drifting labels.
  QUESTION-076 (raised by CLAIM-227, Open) asks whether readers can judge
  footing and stance "reliably and validly in the categories the designs
  use". CASE-058 (documented, Active) answers part of it, "in two
  directions": FactBank's source-relative labels reach κ = 0.82, and
  Kiesling et al.'s interpersonal stance only α = 0.40 to 0.57.
- **Lexical similarity is not the outcome.** CLAIM-084 (A63 and A70;
  thesis, interpretive, Proposed): lexical similarity "cannot distinguish
  preservation of communicative footing from a change of speech-act
  function", so the primary outcome is pragmatic-frame retention "once a
  measure of footing and stance has been shown reliable". Its chains,
  CASE-030 and CASE-032 (assistant-authored, fictional, Active),
  "illustrate it, and their pragmatic retention was asserted, not
  measured"; ARG-039 (CLAIM-229's verdict, evidence, Rejected) holds them
  Rejected as evidence.
- **The experiments and the chain question.** CLAIM-028 (granted,
  empirical, Active): "No result from the exchange's four experiments
  reached a later draft". CASE-020 (stipulated, Active) is the harness, of
  which "Only a dry run was executed". CASE-015 (the owner's case at U20,
  documented, Active), the telephone game, is the family the chains and
  harness vary. QUESTION-014 (A67's, Open) asks "Does a stable speaker footing
  measurably reduce pragmatic drift relative to shifting frames, even
  when both drift lexically?"; its note of 2026-10-12: "A footing chain would first need the
  measurement QUESTION-076 asks for."
- **Whose judgements.** CLAIM-226 (the record's audit of 2026-10-10;
  counter, interpretive, Active): "The defeat tests pool unnamed judges
  and treat their disagreement as noise". It grounds on CASE-026, and of it
  says: "Whether the footing is lost depends on which audience hears
  it." Conceded on
  2026-10-10 and repaired by CASE-055 (another part).

What stays open is whether readers can judge footing and stance reliably
(QUESTION-076), whether a stable footing reduces drift along a chain
(QUESTION-014), and whether opacity can be a preservation target
(QUESTION-023).

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **That CLAIM-061's narrowing leaves it within CLAIM-227's reach.** That
  the act difference CLAIM-061's title keeps is, by A14's point that the
  title is said to serve, a difference of footing and stance, so every
  test of its `defeated_if` runs on unvalidated judgements. As a general
  sentence, CASE-056's amorous kiss separates it: the change from
  greeting to erotic advance is a change of act that needs no footing
  judgement. Narrowed to CASE-027 and CASE-018, it is QUESTION-076's
  "Each test of these claims is a judgement of footing or stance by
  somebody".
- **That CASE-056 can show the act kept only for a modern reading of
  Paul.** CLAIM-226's second part applied to CLAIM-486's one ground. A
  single instantiation of a general clause, and CLAIM-486 is modal, so
  the scoping leaves it standing.
- **That the defender's coarse proposition is CLAIM-064's situation of
  saying.** CASE-056 states that the coarse individuation "concedes
  CLAIM-061's point: what has to be kept is fixed by function"; naming
  that CLAIM-064 is a relabelling.
- **That CLAIM-054 is a case of CLAIM-061.** Whether "interpretive
  content" includes the proposition is left open by CLAIM-054, and the
  components file the two as complements, answering different parts of a
  question.
- **That nothing in the part is measured.** A conjunction of CLAIM-028,
  CLAIM-227 and the notes of CLAIM-061, CLAIM-486, CLAIM-054, CLAIM-064
  and CLAIM-084, which cannot fail while they hold.

## What it does not say

- That keeping the proposition is unnecessary: CLAIM-486 is Proposed and

- That footing or stance cannot be measured (CLAIM-227, QUESTION-076).
- That any of the exchange's chains shows a result (CASE-030, CASE-032,
  CLAIM-028).
- Anything with standing of its own.
