  Competition of LLM agents. MultiAgentBench and its framework MARBLE
  evaluate LLM multi-agent systems in six scenarios: research co-authoring,
  Minecraft building, database error analysis, coding, bargaining and
  Werewolf. Agents are connected by an agent graph whose topology can be
  star, tree, chain or graph-mesh; planning can be naive, chain-of-thought,
  group discussion or "cognitive evolving". Task completion is scored by
  milestones that an LLM-based detector marks as achieved and credits to
  agents (a KPI), plus task scores and LLM-rated communication, planning and
  collaboration scores. Model capability drives task success more than
  coordination; the graph topology does best in research; group discussion
  does worst; more agents lower the KPI; coordination degrades past a number
  of iterations. Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
