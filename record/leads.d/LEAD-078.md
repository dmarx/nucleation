# LEAD-078: Find evidence on bundling by self-prediction rather than an imposed bundle, and on whether discounting mediates self-control change after mindfulness practice

## What it would do

Search for the two kinds of study.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md): "its mechanism, bundling by self-prediction, is untested."
- [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md): "no study read asks whether discounting mediates a change in
  self-control after practice."

## What would count

Evidence of self-predicted bundling promotes [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out, in part

Done on branch claude/lead-078-bundling
(scratch/studies/lead-078/search.md and PROGRESS.md).

- **Searched** both kinds of study (twelve queries, logged).
- **Read** three works: Khan and Dhar 2007 ([LIT-3113](../literature.d/LIT-3113.md), [NOTE-2326](../notes.d/NOTE-2326.md),
  [THEORY-2236](../theory.d/THEORY-2236.md)), Schuhr 2015 ([LIT-3114](../literature.d/LIT-3114.md), [NOTE-2327](../notes.d/NOTE-2327.md),
  [THEORY-2237](../theory.d/THEORY-2237.md)) and Ge et al. 2024 ([LIT-916](../literature.d/LIT-916.md), now Active; [NOTE-2331](../notes.d/NOTE-2331.md),
  [THEORY-2241](../theory.d/THEORY-2241.md), [ARG-206](../arguments.d/ARG-206.md)).
- **Registered** two unread: Kirby and Guastello 2001 ([LIT-3115](../literature.d/LIT-3115.md)) and
  Hofmeyr et al. 2011 ([LIT-3116](../literature.d/LIT-3116.md)), both Unavailable.
- **Result, self-predicted bundling.** One direct manipulation of
  perceived precedent found (Khan and Dhar's link study): it moves choice
  toward the virtue, back to about the isolated rate, with the precedent
  asserted by the experimenter. [THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md)'s `promote_when` (1) is met in
  part, not fully; dated notes on [THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md) and [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md).
- **Result, discounting as mediator.** No study found that tests it. Ge
  et al. dissociate faster responding from unchanged discounting and do
  not show [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md)'s route; dated note on [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md).

**What remains.** A copy of Kirby and Guastello and of Hofmeyr et al.;
a study in which the chooser's own prediction, not an instruction, makes
a choice a precedent; and a mindfulness trial that measures discounting
and a withheld-response or temptation outcome before and after practice
and tests mediation. The last two are new studies, not readings.
Status and stage are the owner's.
