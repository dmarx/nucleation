distinction between violence and coercive control, the research on it, and
the moral philosophy of blame. A8–A19: responsibility for creating the

moral commitments as resources, moral crumple zones and privileged
irresponsibility, and algorithmic decision-making. A20–A26: procedural
friction, the speed of executive action, and A's synthesis, "The Political
Economy of Moral Agency". A27–A35: points of contact with this record, read
from the repository by A, and three of its themes (resource bounds, slack
and timescale separation) at the owner's U16.
