# LIT-tmp5c0j0: Meaning and value attributed to non-therapeutic circumcision of boys among Danish parents of Jewish background

Tania Aase Dræbel, Jane Brandt Sørensen and Dan Wolf Meyrowitsch (2025).
"Meaning and value attributed to non-therapeutic circumcision of boys among
Danish parents of Jewish background" *Journal of Migration and Health 12:
100366*. DOI-10.1016/j.jmh.2025.100366.

## Key takeaways

For these parents circumcision is a taken-for-granted ritual of belonging,
not a decision weighed case by case.
