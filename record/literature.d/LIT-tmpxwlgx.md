# LIT-tmpxwlgx: Coercive Control: Update and Review

Stark, Hester (2019). *Coercive Control: Update and Review*. Violence Against Women 25(1): 81-104. DOI
10.1177/1077801218816191.

## Key takeaways

Coercive control is a pattern that attacks autonomy and liberty and need not involve violence; act-based measures miss
it; level of control predicts harm better than violence in the studies reviewed. In heterosexual relationships Stark
ties it to sexual inequality.
