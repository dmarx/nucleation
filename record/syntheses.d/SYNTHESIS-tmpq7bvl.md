# SYNTHESIS-tmpq7bvl: Whether an assembly reaches its target is not fixed by how much resource it has: a resource that also sets the costs it pays, as heat does, gives a window and lets a schedule beat every level, abundance helps or hurts by whether the competing steps consume it, persistence and productivity vary independently, yields cannot tell a generative nucleus from a head start, and productive nucleating sets need not be closed upward; a productive nucleus is defined by what its establishment does to later formation against a declared target, comparator and horizon

*A part account of the realizability line: the part filed from the DNA
origami readings chosen by the assistant at the owner's request of
2026-11-05, A's four syntheses on DNA origami pasted by the owner the same
day, and the assistant's claims and constructions on them. The line's
account is built over it and its four sibling parts.*

## The account

- **A resource that sets its own costs.** [CLAIM-594](../claims.d/CLAIM-594.md) (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active): where the resource that pays for crossing
  barriers also sets the costs, "the target can be reached only in a window
  of levels that more time widens, and a schedule of levels can reach what
  no constant level reaches"; budgeted reachability with fixed costs "is
  the special case of a resource that leaves the costs unchanged". [CASE-216](../cases.d/CASE-216.md)
  (the assistant's, Active) is its four-state annealing toy, in its own
  words "the construction for [CLAIM-594](../claims.d/CLAIM-594.md)".
- **Abundance.** [CLAIM-598](../claims.d/CLAIM-598.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Active): whether
  more of every resource helps an assembly turns, at each branch point, on
  whether the competing steps consume further resource, as Majikes et al.
  report for a single DNA origami fold that excess staples block. [CASE-217](../cases.d/CASE-217.md)
  (A's model, the assistant's extension, Active) computes it: "This is the
  assistant's [CLAIM-598](../claims.d/CLAIM-598.md)." [CASE-215](../cases.d/CASE-215.md) (the assistant's, Proposed) gives
  placement at equal cost and timing at equal integrated exposure: "That
  extends [CLAIM-598](../claims.d/CLAIM-598.md) from how much supply to when."
- **What a productive nucleus is.** [TERM-117](../terms.d/TERM-117.md) (A's, Active): a partial
  organization whose establishment, relative to a declared target,
  context, horizon and comparator, causally improves forming the target,
  "distinct from a structural seed and a kinetic nucleus"; its measure is an
  interventional difference, and the committor ranks intermediates by
  their futures, but in A's words "a high committor is not sufficient to
  prove causal productivity".
- **Persistence is not productivity.** [CLAIM-595](../claims.d/CLAIM-595.md) (A's, with the
  assistant's proof, demonstrated, Active): two intermediates of equal
  residence time can have different committors, since a state's total exit
  rate cancels from its committor; and "Point 1 above holds for the
  committor, and for deadline success it fails." [CASE-218](../cases.d/CASE-218.md) (A's toy,
  Proposed) gives the second half, a nucleus whose stabilization past an
  optimum makes it easier to form and lowers what forms.
- **What yields cannot tell.** [CLAIM-596](../claims.d/CLAIM-596.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated,
  Active): two assemblies agree in yield with and without the nucleus while
  one nucleus speeds a later step and the other only gives a head start;
  "Yields do not identify the route." It is a partial answer to
  [QUESTION-397](../questions.d/QUESTION-397.md) (A's, verbatim, Open), which asks by which route a partial
  organization helps.
- **Productive sets have no closure.** [CLAIM-599](../claims.d/CLAIM-599.md) (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active), resting on [CLAIM-562](../claims.d/CLAIM-562.md): because establishing more
  relations can lower the chance of a target, "two productive nuclei can
  combine into a trap, so the minimal productive sets describe the
  productive ones only where adding relations never hurts".
- **Designs.** [CASE-219](../cases.d/CASE-219.md) (A's design, Proposed) keeps feasibility, assembly
  and persistence apart in one stochastic model; [CASE-220](../cases.d/CASE-220.md) (A's design,
  Proposed), a variant of it, crosses routing with early stabilization and
  measures the committor.

What stays open is [QUESTION-380](../questions.d/QUESTION-380.md) (A's, Open): when establishing a small,
internally compatible subsystem makes global organization more likely, and
how to predict it from the architecture; [QUESTION-397](../questions.d/QUESTION-397.md), its route; and
[QUESTION-400](../questions.d/QUESTION-400.md) (A's, Open): whether reallocating a fixed stock selects which
form results. Five questions ask whether the DNA origami readings the part
rests on reproduce: [QUESTION-402](../questions.d/QUESTION-402.md), [QUESTION-410](../questions.d/QUESTION-410.md), refined by [QUESTION-445](../questions.d/QUESTION-445.md),
[QUESTION-419](../questions.d/QUESTION-419.md) and [QUESTION-428](../questions.d/QUESTION-428.md).
