Under the dialectic regime, on 2026-11-23, while the manuscript
governance-of-possibility was being revised, the owner asked: "the JD2
declination has me wondering if maybe we're getting closer to identifying a
reasonable distinction between a collective that satisfies a pseudo-self
and an ecosystem of collectives that do but the ecosystem itself does not?
or maybe this is a function of the order/scale from which the perspective
is analyzing the system?"

The occasion: SYNTHESIS-tmp4zamw declined "a polity as an ecological
pseudo-self" against TERM-tmpxwa7g's integration condition, while the
record also holds unity without singularity (TERM-tmpm0s7g, CLAIM-tmp31b3v,
CLAIM-tmp37le7), which the decline did not test (its dated note of this
date, in the manuscript's pull request).

## What was filed

- QUESTION-tmp4fkkx, the owner's question, in the owner's words.
- Two answers, as alternatives, the assistant's:
  - CLAIM-tmpcljke, structural: a plurality of pseudo-selves is integrated
    when some whole-level slow variable is maintained as the whole's
    (CLAIM-327's criterion: read, conditioned on, regenerated), and an
    ecosystem when its members are coupled only through each other's
    conditions of existence;
  - CLAIM-tmpptr3w, regime-relative and deflationary: integration is
    indexed by a probe family and a horizon (TERM-tmpxwa7g, CLAIM-157), the
    same firms can be coupled at a short horizon and integrated at a long
    one, and at a fixed regime there is no further difference in kind.
- LEAD-tmplrmhl, which tells them apart: the integration diagnostic at two
  horizons around a shared standard's adoption; a threshold at a fixed
  horizon for the first, a smooth change with the horizon for the second.

## Where the two meet

They are not simply rivals: the structural criterion is itself evaluated
under a regime, so it inherits the regime-relativity. They part on whether,
at a fixed regime, the difference is one of kind. The owner chooses.

## A correction in the turn's answer

The answer cited CLAIM-574 for the regime-relativity of a division into
parts. CLAIM-574 says distinctions only grow as nested probe families grow,
which is a different point; CLAIM-157, relativity to a stated target, is
the premise, and is what CLAIM-tmpptr3w rests on.

## The owner's objection, the same day

The owner answered the structural reading: "I'm not convinced. ecosystems
are absolutely governed by slow collective variables. tides. day/night
cycles. lunar cycles. seasons. predator-prey population oscillations.
boundary effects at interfaces with other ecologies. cadences set by human
activity. etc."

- CLAIM-tmpt93dp, the owner's objection, objects to CLAIM-tmpcljke.
- CLAIM-tmps5yc5, the assistant's reading: the examples divide into common
  drivers members read and condition on but do not regenerate (tides, day
  and night, lunar cycles, seasons, imposed human cadences), which entrain
  without integrating (CLAIM-tmptxsin, CLAIM-165), and variables members'
  interactions regenerate (predator–prey oscillations; some boundary
  effects), which meet CLAIM-327's criterion. The second kind defeats
  CLAIM-tmpcljke as worded; a dated note on it says so, and its status is
  left to the owner.
- Two alternatives follow, for the owner: CLAIM-tmpklms4 narrows the
  criterion to a variable members address as the whole's own (a reflexive
  condition, with a free-parameter risk its defeater names), and
  CLAIM-tmpptr3w lets the difference collapse into the regime of analysis.

## Migration, the owner's better example

The owner added: "migration dynamics might be a better endogenous example".
It is added to CLAIM-tmpt93dp (version 2). The assistant's reading,
CLAIM-tmp7pwo5: a migration route is followed by members as the group's,
regenerated by being followed, and, where socially learned, held as the
group's own, so it may meet even CLAIM-tmpklms4's narrowed criterion and
tips the question toward CLAIM-tmpptr3w. Its premise (social learning,
regeneration by use) is not held by any reading; LEAD-tmptu4gx would read
for it. The record holds only niche-construction readings near it
(THEORY-469, THEORY-446).
