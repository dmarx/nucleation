# LIT-1656: Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living

Maturana, H. R., & Varela, F. J. (1980). *Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living*. D. Reidel (Boston Studies in the Philosophy of Science 42). doi:10.1007/978-94-009-8947-4.

Not read: no lawful full text was reached on 2026-10-27 (see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for and from the exchange's description of it. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A9 §2 cited it for components whose identity as components is partly
constituted by their part in the organization, the first prior art for
the recursive half of [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md), that constraints are realized by
what they constrain. Its organization–structure split is close to the
owner's specification–realization pair. The record holds autopoiesis
through later readings ([LIT-1017](LIT-1017.md), [LIT-1018](LIT-1018.md), [LIT-1027](LIT-1027.md) and [THEORY-666](../theory.d/THEORY-666.md)),
and closure of constraints in [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md), but not this book.
