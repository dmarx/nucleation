# THEORY-tmph2ssk: Restoring the pressure-free conditions of a degraded ecosystem can fail because the degraded phase leaves a reinforcing legacy of its own, such as hardwoods that stop fire spreading, sludge that favours a floating competitor, or eroded beaver sites, so recovery needs direct biotic manipulation, by removal or by reintroduction in the right order, besides abiotic restoration

## Source

Kadowaki and colleagues (2018), LIT-2165; their account is read in
NOTE-1677 and reported, Active, in THEORY-1468.

## What was actually shown

See THEORY-1468: a toy model the authors call not novel and three cases
described from other studies, none tested for alternative stable states,
none with numbers, none followed after the intervention ends. Nothing
beyond that reading is held here.

## What this does not say

That the thesis has been tested. The condition above names what would
settle it.
