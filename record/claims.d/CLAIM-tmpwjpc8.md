# CLAIM-tmpwjpc8: In the record's account of wu-wei, the computational-mechanics and nonlinear-dynamics perspectives take precedence over the sinological one

## The ruling

The owner, 2026-11-23: "the computational mechanics and nonlinear
dynamics perspectives should take precedence over the sinological
perspective in our account."

## How it is applied (the assistant's)

The record's wu-wei entries make claims about effortless action as a
dynamical phenomenon:
- control timed by a system's own dynamics ([CLAIM-541](CLAIM-541.md));
- control directed along what its means reach strongly ([CLAIM-tmp0doyo](CLAIM-tmp0doyo.md));
- memory that pays only for what does not predict ([CLAIM-tmpcbdgm](CLAIM-tmpcbdgm.md));
- stabilization and adaptability as two achievements of one
  organization ([CLAIM-tmpm985y](CLAIM-tmpm985y.md)).

Under the ruling these are the account. What the classical texts and
their scholarship say bears on whether the name "wu-wei" fits that
account, and on what the account should look at. It does not defeat it.
A sinological finding that wu-wei excludes something the account
includes, say ongoing monitored correction, would show the name is an
imperfect gloss and leave the dynamical claims standing. Accordingly:

- **[CLAIM-541](CLAIM-541.md)** loses its sinological defeater (version 3).
- **[CLAIM-tmpaj28t](CLAIM-tmpaj28t.md)** is restated as a gloss on [CLAIM-tmpm985y](CLAIM-tmpm985y.md).
- **[CLAIM-tmp0doyo](CLAIM-tmp0doyo.md)'s** admission no longer leans on Slingerland.
- **[LEAD-tmpsyfgj](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpsyfgj.md)** becomes a check on the name, at low priority.

Read more weakly, the ruling would make sinology a lesser authority that
still counts as evidence against the account where it conflicts. The
application above takes the stronger reading, because "take precedence
... in our account" says whose terms the account is in. If the owner
meant the weaker reading, the sinological clause of [CLAIM-541](CLAIM-541.md)'s version 2
defeater is what would be restored.
