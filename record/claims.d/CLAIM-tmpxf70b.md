# CLAIM-tmpxf70b: Delegating a record's upkeep can widen its governance

## The claim

A13 §7 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c):

| Externalized capacity | Potential human capability gained |
|---|---|
| Remembering prior decisions | Governing a larger body of accumulated work |
| Tracking references | Recognizing the implications of revisions |
| Maintaining indexes | Navigating more complex arguments |
| Detecting inconsistencies | Exercising more informed judgment |
| Generating review worklists | Directing scarce attention toward unresolved questions |
| Drafting and synthesis | Considering more intellectual alternatives |

"The objective is not to preserve the owner's ability to perform all those
activities unaided. It is to expand the owner's capacity to govern an
increasingly sophisticated intellectual organization." A hedges at once:
"Whether it actually achieves that over extended periods is an important
empirical question."

## Against the record

- **[CLAIM-792](CLAIM-792.md)**: in this record noticing grammar misfits was delegated, to
  procedures, while revision stayed mostly with the owner. That is the
  claim's shape: productive labour and some governance labour delegated,
  the governing judgement kept.
- **[CLAIM-675](CLAIM-675.md)** (Active): two forms of support with the same present
  capabilities can leave different ones later, so support is judged by its
  trajectory. [CLAIM-tmpz33db](CLAIM-tmpz33db.md) (A4's) adds that a one-capacity model cannot
  weigh net empowerment. Both say what evidence this claim needs: a
  trajectory, not a snapshot.
- The manuscript from-obstruction-to-reorganization names the test in
  §19.5 and its conclusion: "Measuring the owner's own noticing over time
  would test whether the help that makes the cycle run is eroding the
  capacity to govern it." [LEAD-tmpswgqi](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpswgqi.md) files it.
