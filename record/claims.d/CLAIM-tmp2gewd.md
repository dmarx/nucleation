# CLAIM-tmp2gewd: A person's drive profile is time-indexed: who drives changes with state, such as hungry or sated and stressed or calm, with task and with regime

## The claim

The owner, on 2026-10-11, quoted in [CLAIM-166](CLAIM-166.md): "I think it's probably
also worth noting that 'who drives' isn't a constant."

A drive profile ([TERM-051](../terms.d/TERM-051.md)) is defined at a time, over a window. This
claim is that, for a person, it changes: "Who drives changes: by state
(hungry or sated, stressed or calm), by task, and by regime. That is what
the will line calls a change in which regime is effective ([CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md),
[CLAIM-159](CLAIM-159.md))."

[CLAIM-166](CLAIM-166.md) gives one source of such change, a lower-level collective, the
gut microbiome, that can at times move hunger and affect. This claim does
not depend on that source, and that one does not depend on this.

## What it does not say

- That a change comes from below. That is [CLAIM-166](CLAIM-166.md)'s part, and its
  evidence.
- That drive profiles have been estimated in a person over successive
  windows. None has been, in the record; the defeat condition names the
  observation that would test this.
- How fast or how often a profile changes, or that every change of
  behaviour is a change of who drives.
