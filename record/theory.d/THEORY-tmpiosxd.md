# THEORY-tmpiosxd: Collins, Cavanagh and Frank propose that the latent task-set structure people build incidentally during simple reinforcement learning shows in prefrontal EEG: a switch of the dimension a participant treats as context, but not of the other dimension, evokes task-switch potentials over lateral prefrontal cortex, and the size of the early switch positivity and the decoded attention to that dimension each covary across participants with how much they later transfer learned rules to new contexts

## Source

Collins, Cavanagh and Frank (2014), [LIT-tmp199ym](../literature.d/LIT-tmp199ym.md); read in [NOTE-tmptsczp](../notes.d/NOTE-tmptsczp.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Context-dimension switch potential.** A late cue-locked negativity
  over right dorsolateral PFC, larger for switches of the high dimension
  than stays, none for the low dimension: "this neural signature is
  sensitive only to change in high-level TS and not to the stimulus or
  action" (p. 4681).
- **Early positivity and transfer.** The early switch-high positivity "was
  not evident across the entire group of subjects" (p. 4681) but
  correlated with later positive transfer (ρ = 0.5, p = 0.003), and
  "Critically, transfer was only predicted by ERP to switches of the high
  dimension" (p. 4682).
- **Decoded attention and transfer.** A classifier trained on a separate
  attention localizer decoded attention to each participant's high
  dimension (p = 0.007), and the degree correlated with positive transfer
  (ρ = 0.35, p < 0.05; pp. 4683–4684).

## What this does not say

- That the late negativity predicts generalization: it did not.
- That the markers are one construct: they correlate only marginally
  (ρ = 0.33, p = 0.068), though the authors read them as "the same
  underlying construct" (p. 4683).
- That EEG reads out the structure independently of behaviour: the context
  dimension is assigned from switch-costs.
- That the sources are localized: "spatial limitations of EEG technique
  preclude more accurate estimation of true generative sources" (p. 4683).
- That it replicates in a new sample: these are the 2013 Experiment 2
  participants.
- Anything about out-of-sample prediction; "predictive" is a correlation
  across participants.
