# CLAIM-045: Architectural, dynamical and goal-directed accounts of distributed agency are complementary components of one account, each answering a different sub-question

## The claim

U7: "These three views are perfectly mutually compatible. They are not
different things. They absolutely can be treated as complementary components
of one theory."

A10 assigned each its sub-question: Brooks, how coherent behaviour arises
without a central executive; Kelso, how interaction produces collective
variables and regime transitions; Levin, how a higher-scale goal-directed
boundary forms.

## What it does not say

That the three are one theory already. It says they do not compete, which is
why the record wanted a `complements` relation. It has one between its own
claims; between a claim and a THEORY it waits on a luria fix
(dmarx/luria#358), so the accounts are premises here.
