# THEORY-tmpucfiy: Patriarca et al.'s scoping review of 321 academic publications (2003–mid-2020) on STAMP, STPA and CAST finds that STPA dominates the literature, that control structures are almost always presented while process models, safety constraints and validation are often omitted, that almost half the papers propose modifications or extensions, and that industry engagement is rarely visible.

## The account

Patriarca et al.'s scoping review of 321 academic publications (2003–mid-2020) on STAMP, STPA and CAST finds that STPA dominates the literature, that control structures are almost always presented while process models, safety constraints and validation are often omitted, that almost half the papers propose modifications or extensions, and that industry engagement is rarely visible.
