The owner asked for readings of the 57 `Deferred` seeds that registering the
prior-art novelty map added, wherever a work could be found to read.

**How it was done.** Eight agents split the seeds by subject: quantum
cognition and structural realism; thermodynamics of learning; topos quantum
theory (Döring–Isham, Isham–Butterfield); classic mathematics and physics;
interpretability; equivariance and Boolean analysis; learning dynamics and
information theory; and the philosophy and textbook tail. Each worked only from
legitimate copies: arXiv, publishers' free archives, GDZ, Math-Net.Ru, a
university preprint series, and Internet Archive scans of journal issues in
the US public domain (renewal records checked). Shadow libraries and
unauthorised uploads were refused even where they were the only copy found.

**The result.** 36 works were read and 21 could not be reached.

- **Read:** 34 in full and 2 in part. Each has a NOTE, and each LIT's status
  now comes from the reading: 35 `Active` and one `Proposed` (Döring–Isham IV,
  [LIT-310](../../record/literature.d/LIT-310.md), whose quantum composite fails the paper's own rules).
- **Skimmed:** the two book-length texts, Bronstein et al.'s geometric deep
  learning proto-book ([LIT-319](../../record/literature.d/LIT-319.md)) and O'Donnell ([LIT-346](../../record/literature.d/LIT-346.md)). Each NOTE says which
  chapters were read closely.
- **Unreachable** (still `Deferred`, each LIT recording what was tried): these
  are mostly in-copyright books.
  - Philosophy and linguistics: [LIT-301](../../record/literature.d/LIT-301.md), [LIT-303](../../record/literature.d/LIT-303.md), [LIT-307](../../record/literature.d/LIT-307.md), [LIT-318](../../record/literature.d/LIT-318.md), [LIT-320](../../record/literature.d/LIT-320.md),
    [LIT-336](../../record/literature.d/LIT-336.md), [LIT-342](../../record/literature.d/LIT-342.md), [LIT-350](../../record/literature.d/LIT-350.md).
  - Textbooks and monographs: [LIT-311](../../record/literature.d/LIT-311.md), [LIT-316](../../record/literature.d/LIT-316.md), [LIT-321](../../record/literature.d/LIT-321.md), [LIT-334](../../record/literature.d/LIT-334.md), [LIT-344](../../record/literature.d/LIT-344.md),
    [LIT-351](../../record/literature.d/LIT-351.md), [LIT-354](../../record/literature.d/LIT-354.md).
  - Paywalled papers: [LIT-317](../../record/literature.d/LIT-317.md), [LIT-328](../../record/literature.d/LIT-328.md), [LIT-331](../../record/literature.d/LIT-331.md), [LIT-332](../../record/literature.d/LIT-332.md), [LIT-337](../../record/literature.d/LIT-337.md), [LIT-349](../../record/literature.d/LIT-349.md).

topes, and neither the AMS copy (blocked) nor the MIT thesis was reachable.

**What the readings say about the map.** These findings are for the map's
owner to act on. Nothing in the map was edited.

- **Row 8 (the Stone leg).**
  - Isham–Butterfield I ([LIT-325](../../record/literature.d/LIT-325.md)) already owns the chain from Kochen–Specker
    to Stone duality (§2.3).
  - Stone 1936 ([LIT-353](../../record/literature.d/LIT-353.md)) contains no topology, Stone space, duality or
    ultrafilters; those are in Stone 1937, which is not registered.
  - In the finite case, ultrafilters are atoms, which may be faces rather than
    only topes.
- **Orthomodularity and negation.**
  - Birkhoff–von Neumann ([LIT-306](../../record/literature.d/LIT-306.md)) propose the *modular* law, not the
    orthomodular one. Citing them for "orthomodular" is a misattribution.
  - Döring–Isham use a Heyting negation, which clashes with the map's
    orthocomplement rows, and give no finite witness.
  - Kochen–Specker ([LIT-298](../../record/literature.d/LIT-298.md)) prove dimension 3 only. They never state
    "dimension ≥ 3".
- **Row 7 (predicate harmonics and irreps).**
  - "Harmonics = characters" is textbook abelian harmonic analysis
    (O'Donnell, [LIT-346](../../record/literature.d/LIT-346.md)), so KNOWN, not SYNTHESIS. Walsh 1923 ([LIT-300](../../record/literature.d/LIT-300.md)) has
    no characters at all.
  - "Multiplets = degeneracies" is the Schur corollary. Kondor–Trivedi
    ([LIT-305](../../record/literature.d/LIT-305.md)) supply the machinery but never state it, and Peter–Weyl
    ([LIT-329](../../record/literature.d/LIT-329.md)) does not state the converse "degeneracy implies symmetry".
  - Abelian characters give no multiplets. The cube's degeneracies come from
    the nonabelian signed-permutation group, which the equivariance reader
    checked numerically.
  - The equivariance papers ([LIT-314](../../record/literature.d/LIT-314.md), [LIT-305](../../record/literature.d/LIT-305.md), [LIT-319](../../record/literature.d/LIT-319.md)) and Engels et al.
    ([LIT-322](../../record/literature.d/LIT-322.md)) all take the group as given. So "they impose; you detect"
    holds, and Engels' weekday and month circles are the natural validation
    target.
  - Murota et al. ([LIT-352](../../record/literature.d/LIT-352.md)), the nearest algorithmic precedent, has a
    Theorem 3.1(C) that fails over ℝ, the reader checked numerically. It also
    has no noise model.
- **Row 12 (gradient as channel).**
  - McCandlish ([LIT-348](../../record/literature.d/LIT-348.md)) owns batch = gradient SNR and the critical batch. His
    progress law saturates, whereas the map's log(1 + SNR) keeps growing.
  - The channel frame belongs to Xu–Raginsky ([LIT-347](../../record/literature.d/LIT-347.md)), not McCandlish.
  - Shwartz-Ziv–Tishby ([LIT-324](../../record/literature.d/LIT-324.md)) is uncited prior art for "gradient SNR
    organises training".
  - The IB paper ([LIT-338](../../record/literature.d/LIT-338.md)) says nothing about gradients.
- **Row 13.**
  - Goldt–Seifert ([LIT-308](../../record/literature.d/LIT-308.md)) and Still et al. ([LIT-327](../../record/literature.d/LIT-327.md)) bound entropy
    production. They do not support a kT ln 2 heat floor per judgment, since
    heat is due only at erasure.
  - The three "temperatures" (McCandlish's ε/B, Gibbs β, IB β) are formal
    analogies, none of them k_B·T.
- **Rows 2 and 5.**
  - Eckart–Young ([LIT-330](../../record/literature.d/LIT-330.md)) is unique as a matrix only when λ_r > λ_{r+1}, and
    the factors are never unique.
  - Riesz 1907 ([LIT-326](../../record/literature.d/LIT-326.md)) states the representation without proof or
    uniqueness.
  - Park et al.'s ([LIT-304](../../record/literature.d/LIT-304.md)) causal inner product is fixed only by an
    arbitrary choice.
  - None of the three supports a "Riesz-forced unique factorisation".
  - Wick–Wightman–Wigner ([LIT-339](../../record/literature.d/LIT-339.md)) make cross-sector superpositions
    *mixtures*, not truth-value gaps.
- **Structural realism.** Worrall 1989 ([LIT-315](../../record/literature.d/LIT-315.md)) is epistemic structural
  realism, so it is the wrong citation for "to be is to be an invariant of
  structure". Ladyman 1998 ([LIT-342](../../record/literature.d/LIT-342.md)), the ontic source, was unreachable.
- **Aerts–Gabora ([LIT-340](../../record/literature.d/LIT-340.md), [LIT-312](../../record/literature.d/LIT-312.md)).** Their models are single-basis, so the
  map's "non-distributive" gloss should go.
- **Platonic Representation Hypothesis ([LIT-302](../../record/literature.d/LIT-302.md)).** Its measured convergence
  is small (a mutual-kNN score of 0.16), and the proof shows only a shared
  kernel. The map's "PRH-measured" needs these qualifiers.
- **Toy Models ([LIT-323](../../record/literature.d/LIT-323.md)).** The reader's own inference, checked numerically,
  is a bridge to the map: in the efficiently packed shapes the features form
  a tight frame, a rank-one POVM whose weights are the paper's feature
  dimensionalities. That would make superposition a compressed Boolean
  context, a candidate reading of "Boolean shadow".

**Disagreements with the anthology ([ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md)).** Several readings found faults in
the anthology's entries for the six dual-held papers. For example:

- The anthology's Park et al. entry reports "four quadruples" where the paper
  has one.
- Its Platonic Representation Hypothesis entry omits the 0.16 ceiling.
- Its grokking entries do not record that the headline run used no weight
  decay, or that dropout also produces grokking.
- Its McCandlish entry claims an "order of magnitude" fit that fails for the
  SVHN models.

Each is in the NOTE concerned, reported and not fixed. They are for an
anthology pass to take up.
