# LEAD-089: Model Northstar with control and metacontrol mechanisms alone and then with a will-organization over them, price the difference, and set planning, control and institutional theory beside the account

## What it would do

Model the case both ways and price the difference; read planning theory,
control theory's regulation and institutional theory for what each already
explains.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-056](../questions.d/QUESTION-056.md): "Take a case such as Northstar, model it with control and
  metacontrol mechanisms alone, then with a will-organization over them, and
  price the difference as [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) does."
- [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md): "Planning theory and institutional theory have not been set
  beside the account at all."

## What would count

A difference the control-only model cannot produce at matched complexity
supports [CLAIM-487](../claims.d/CLAIM-487.md)'s gain; none, or one an existing theory already gives,
defeats [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md).
