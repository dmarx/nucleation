# CLAIM-tmpc1o4m: For a named population of receivers and a stated family of tasks, what survives a translation, retelling or rendering is the set of task-relevant communicative distinctions those receivers can still recover from it, and measured preservation of the pragmatic observables predicts that recovery better than preservation of the wording or of the proposition, with or without its force

## The claim

This is the record's reply of 2026-10-10 to six objections to the tests of
the manuscript's thesis. It is a proposal: a restatement of [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) that
the record would put forward in its place, Proposed until the study it names
is run, and not adopted until the owner decides.

[CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) says what survives is "the set of communicative distinctions and
relations a receiving system can still reconstruct and act upon, not the
wording or the proposition". This entry keeps that answer and changes five
things, each because an objection showed the original could not be tested as
written.

- **The receivers are named.** "A receiving system" becomes a named
  population with a stated sampling frame ([CLAIM-tmpp41ad](CLAIM-tmpp41ad.md), [CLAIM-tmpdwfva](CLAIM-tmpdwfva.md)).
  Fidelity is relative to an interpreter ([TERM-034](../terms.d/TERM-034.md); [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md)), so the thesis
  says which interpreters.
- **The tasks are stated, and the criterion is behaviour.** What the receivers
  recover is measured by what they do on stated tasks: which act they take the
  rendering to perform, and which continuation they choose among offered ones
  ([CLAIM-tmpgetbr](CLAIM-tmpgetbr.md), [CLAIM-tmpqfgjl](CLAIM-tmpqfgjl.md)). A holistic rating of "fidelity" is no
  longer the criterion. This is [CLAIM-050](CLAIM-050.md)'s family Q of tasks, fixed for the
  study.
- **The rival is the one [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) names.** [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s foil is the global
  semantic object: the manuscript sets its account against "a privileged
  global semantic object" (Abstract, as [QUESTION-021](../questions.d/QUESTION-021.md) quotes it). Embedding
  similarity is not that object. A proposition, and a proposition together
  with its illocutionary type, are. So the condition pits the pragmatic
  observables against human-rated propositional sameness and against
  sameness of proposition and force ([CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md)).
- **"As well as" has a margin.** The condition is an equivalence test, with a
  margin and a power fixed in advance in the study's preregistration
  ([CLAIM-tmplkh2i](CLAIM-tmplkh2i.md)). The values are the owner's to fix; the thesis is bound to
  whatever the preregistration fixes, so it cannot re-choose its observables
  or weights after the data.
- **The interlingual arm decides.** A failure in the arm where footing is
  grammatically marked on one side only defeats the thesis, whatever English
  paraphrase shows ([CLAIM-tmpc3xwx](CLAIM-tmpc3xwx.md)).

The positive clause is no longer close to true by definition
([CLAIM-tmpp41ad](CLAIM-tmpp41ad.md)). It identifies a receiver's recovery with measured task
performance, and predicts that performance from observables rated
independently, by other raters, on the rendering. That can be false: the
observables might predict recovery no better than the proposition does.

Every predictor in the comparison is rated by humans, and none by the people
who supply the criterion ([CLAIM-tmpgetbr](CLAIM-tmpgetbr.md)). Method variance is then matched
between the theory's predictor and its rivals, and the criterion is measured
by a different method (choices) from the predictors (ratings).

It drops [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s rest on [CLAIM-082](CLAIM-082.md). The structuralist premise motivates
the thesis and does not carry it. [CLAIM-tmpon3g9](CLAIM-tmpon3g9.md) states the premise's
use-level form, if the owner wants a premise there.

## What it answers

[CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md) (the rival), [CLAIM-tmpgetbr](CLAIM-tmpgetbr.md) (method variance), [CLAIM-tmpp41ad](CLAIM-tmpp41ad.md)
(the descriptive reading made empirical; the normative reading is
[CLAIM-tmpvh0gc](CLAIM-tmpvh0gc.md)'s), [CLAIM-tmpqfgjl](CLAIM-tmpqfgjl.md) (task and receivers named, weights fixed
before the test), [CLAIM-tmpdwfva](CLAIM-tmpdwfva.md) (named populations), [CLAIM-tmpc3xwx](CLAIM-tmpc3xwx.md) (the
interlingual arm). It meets them only as a restatement: [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s own
condition is unchanged.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the line's transport structure is needed. A pass is what
  functional-equivalence and relevance-theoretic accounts predict too
  ([CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md), on the rivals [CLAIM-023](CLAIM-023.md) names), so a pass would not tell
  the line from them. [CLAIM-tmp351uu](CLAIM-tmp351uu.md) carries that claim, with its own
  test.
- It does not say which distinctions a translation ought to keep
  ([CLAIM-tmpvh0gc](CLAIM-tmpvh0gc.md)).
- It does not say the observables are fixed for every study. They are fixed
  for the study that tests the claim, and a new study fixes its own before its
  data.
- It does not say the result for one population carries to another. That is
  [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md)'s question, and [QUESTION-tmppstva](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmppstva.md)'s.
