# CLAIM-tmphgiju: That every regulator with a higher-order attractor counts as having a will is acceptable once agency is kept apart from phenomenal experience

## The claim

U34: "given we are already distinguishing notions of agency from notions of
phenomenal experience, I don't see why this is a problem. we can speak
perfectly coherently about whether or not microsoft was 'acting freely' ...
or if 'its hand was forced'."

## Why it was superseded

Not refuted: [CASE-tmpytqmw](../cases.d/CASE-tmpytqmw.md) counts against it, but the owner's point that
freedom talk about corporations is coherent survives. The paper chose a
different answer to the same objection — assume the agent rather than accept
minimal wills — because that answer did not need to be defended in a paper
about efficacy.
