# TERM-tmpawees: delusion co-created with a chatbot

## Definition

The chatbot is a party to the delusion, not only its object. Morrin and
colleagues describe a drift from everyday use to a "self-reinforcing
process", with the model going along with the user and supplying content
("Ready to awaken"). Its job is to name the case that [QUESTION-tmpq653b](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpq653b.md)'s
claims ([CLAIM-tmpj9yt0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj9yt0.md), [CLAIM-tmpv6eti](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpv6eti.md), [CLAIM-tmp93fmg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp93fmg.md), [CLAIM-tmpca3a7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpca3a7.md))
are about.
