# LIT-1146: Opinion Dynamics and the Evolution of Social Power in Influence Networks

Jia, P., MirTabatabaei, A., Friedkin, N. E. & Bullo, F. (2015). *SIAM Review* 57(3):367–397.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpkn2yq](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpkn2yq.md). Page numbers are the journal's.*

- **One parameter.** "the evolution and limiting values of the
  self-weights depend only upon the network structure" (p. 374), through
  the eigenvector centralities c.
- **Convergence.** Unless the network is a star, every non-autocratic start
  converges to one interior equilibrium ordered as c (Theorem 4.1, p. 380).
- **Accumulation.** "social power tends to accumulate at the top of the
  hierarchy" (p. 367): above a threshold, power exceeds centrality
  (Proposition 4.2).
- **Extremes.** Autocracy exactly for a star; democracy exactly for a
  doubly stochastic network: "If such structures are unusual, then so is a
  democratic equilibrium" (p. 376).
- **The unreached.** "the individuals who are not globally reachable in
  G(C) have zero social power in the equilibrium influence networks"
  (p. 384).
- **Limits the authors state.** "At the present time, we cannot assert
  robustness" (p. 386).
