# TERM-147: independent scoring, of a loop's evidence, in two senses that come apart: scoring that does not read the content of the interpretation under test, and scoring that reads nothing of the loop's current state, neither the interpretation nor the share of authority it holds

## Definition

A loop's scoring is the rule by which its evidence moves the state it holds: credence in an interpretation, or a share of authority to the process that carries one. The rule is independent

- **in the first sense, of the interpretation**, when it does not read what the interpretation under test says. No lean toward A raises A's score by having the evidence read as A would read it. This is [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)'s γ ≈ 0, [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md)'s Regime B, in which operators are scored "by independently assessed relevance".
- **in the second sense, of the loop's state**, when it reads nothing of the loop's current state: neither the interpretation's content nor the share of authority, adoption or attention that the interpretation, or the process carrying it, now holds. This is the first sense together with [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md)'s c = 0, which that case calls independent scoring ("lowering c, independent scoring, moves the threshold").

The second sense implies the first. The first does not imply the second.

## Where they come apart

On **share-dependent advantage**: a term in a process's score that rises with its own share, whatever the evidence says. [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md)'s c(2π − 1), [CLAIM-408](../claims.d/CLAIM-408.md)'s α, the increasing returns of Arthur's model ([THEORY-863](../theory.d/THEORY-863.md)) and the conformity scoring of [CASE-158](../cases.d/CASE-158.md) are of this kind. Such scoring is independent in the first sense and not in the second.

The senses separate only where the interpretation and the authority it holds are distinct states. In [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md) they are one vector ([CLAIM-444](../claims.d/CLAIM-444.md)), so reading the share is reading the interpretation and both senses give the same verdict. In [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)'s two-field form, share-dependent advantage appears as authority that persists or grows by itself, δ ≥ 1, which [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md) excludes by assumption (δ < 1, "each field stable alone"). [CASE-312](../cases.d/CASE-312.md) builds a loop with γ = 0 and share-dependent authority. In the first sense its scoring is independent, and it locks in, interpretation included; in the second sense its scoring is not independent.

## The word in the record

- **The first sense.** [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s title and thesis ("does not pass through the interpretation it tests") and its formal anchor, [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md): "Scoring operators independently of the dominant interpretation (Regime B, γ ≈ 0) removes the coupling-driven instability".
- **The second sense.** [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md), as above, and its conclusion that lock-in needs "scoring that depends on who governs". [TERM-066](TERM-066.md)'s interpretive capture, the condition independent scoring is meant to prevent, is evaluation "driven by the interpretation or party being evaluated", which names the party as well as the interpretation.
- **Unmarked.** [CLAIM-466](../claims.d/CLAIM-466.md): "Independent scoring is the natural one", the remedy for self-scoring, where self-scoring is [CASE-162](../cases.d/CASE-162.md)'s c.

## What it is not

- **Not independent arrival.** Evidence whose arrival does not pass through the interpretation, such as reported incidents in Ensign et al.'s model ([THEORY-1927](../theory.d/THEORY-1927.md)), is the other half of [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s disjunction. This term concerns the scoring half.
- **Not scoring against a null.** [CLAIM-821](../claims.d/CLAIM-821.md)'s null scoring compares a measure's reading with what the same measure shows with no learning. It concerns the measure, not whether the rule reads the loop's state, and this term takes no side between [CLAIM-851](../claims.d/CLAIM-851.md) and [CLAIM-831](../claims.d/CLAIM-831.md).
- **Not the absence of capture.** Independent scoring in either sense is a property of the rule. Whether an evaluation is captured is judged against an outside standard ([TERM-066](TERM-066.md)). And independent scoring pooled with a channel that is not independent anchors the loop only as far as it outweighs that channel (the first point of [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s "What it does not say"; [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md)'s |h| ≥ c).

## Use

An entry that says "independent scoring" says which sense it means where share-dependent advantage could be in play. Where the interpretation and authority are one state, the senses agree and the bare phrase is enough.
