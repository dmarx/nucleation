The owner asked for Cameron, Banko and Pierce's 2001 reply ([LIT-562](../../record/literature.d/LIT-562.md)) to be
read and filed with notes and theories. The paper was registered on
2026-10-03 from its abstract and filed Deferred. Its free PubMed Central
scan answered this environment with bot challenges, and the copy then found
on behavior.org, the Cambridge Center for Behavioral Studies, was set
aside because it is neither the publisher's nor an author's deposit. The
owner's instruction now covers that copy, as it did the Action Design copy
of Argyris and Bailey's collection of Fischer's papers. The text was
already extracted, and is read here end to end.

## What was read

- Pp. 1–27 in full, Tables 1 and 2, and Appendix B.
- Appendix A skimmed.
- Appendixes C–G and the references not read item by item.
- The per-contingency effect sizes in Figures 1–5 are images with no text
  layer. The record has those effects' directions and significance, and
  their sizes only where the prose states them.

## What it changed

- **[THEORY-046](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-046.md) promoted.** Its promotion condition named this reading, and
  the reading meets it. The reply's re-coding finds rewards offered for
  doing an interesting task lower free choice: −0.35 across 57 studies.
- **[THEORY-046](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-046.md)'s refutation clause kept.** The reply's durability evidence
  (two of seven delayed measures still significant) counts significant
  results instead of pooling them. So it does not meet the clause, and it
  is recorded as leaving durability open.
- **[THEORY-049](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-049.md) not moved.** Its refutation clause describes the reply's
  finding for rewards tied to beating others, but against a control given
  the same feedback. The reply pools the two kinds of control, so the
  clause is not met.
- **[THEORY-tmpmshgc](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpmshgc.md) filed.** It states the reply's own account and is
  declared a rival of [THEORY-049](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-049.md). A rival rather than a correction, because
  the authors allow that cognitive evaluation theory could be modified to
  fit.
- **No `corrects` between [LIT-557](../../record/literature.d/LIT-557.md) and [LIT-562](../../record/literature.d/LIT-562.md).** Having read both, neither
  shows the other wrong on something it claimed. They contest coding and
  agree on the core effect, so `compared_against` stays.
