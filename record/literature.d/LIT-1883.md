# LIT-1883: Overcoming Scaling Limits in On-Policy Self-Distillation for LLM Reasoning

Hossain, M. I., Kousar, H., & Tourni, I. C. (2026). Overcoming scaling limits in on-policy self-distillation for LLM reasoning. arXiv:2609.37915.

Read in full in [NOTE-1479](../notes.d/NOTE-1479.md).

## Why this is here

A16 used it for the "privileged teacher" case of self-distillation: what the teacher knows and the student cannot see is averaged away, which is the limit [QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md) starts from. The reading confirms that the paper states this limit ([THEORY-1259](../theory.d/THEORY-1259.md)) and adds its consequence: supervision where the teacher's advantage is privileged makes the student less certain, and the useful supervision is on states the student can already reach ([THEORY-1263](../theory.d/THEORY-1263.md)). Two qualifications. The teacher is the frozen initial model, not the evolving one. And the objective actually trained is a per-entry clipped KL, not the forward KL the proposition is about.
