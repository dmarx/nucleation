# CLAIM-tmpt5uc1: Variations in translation are transformations between pragmatic frames of reference that preserve selected invariants of the communicative act

## The claim

A18 §7: "**Variations in translation can be understood as transformations
between pragmatic frames of reference, where the challenge is to preserve
selected invariants of the communicative act—particularly its illocutionary
force, speaker footing, and interpersonal stance—despite changes in the
linguistic and social coordinates through which that act is realized.**" And
the "more provocative" form: "Just as the physical significance of an event
cannot be identified with its coordinates in a particular reference frame, the
communicative significance of an utterance cannot be identified with its
linguistic form in a particular pragmatic frame."
