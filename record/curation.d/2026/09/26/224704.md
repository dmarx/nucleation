This pass closes out the series of tag passes over the `philosophy` group. The
three tags left were small:

- `logic`: 3 works;
- `philosophy-of-mathematics`: 7 works;
- `philosophy-of-language`: 5 works, the topic [ADR-009](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-009.md) added in the previous
  pass.

Only two of their works had never had a close reading attempted: [LIT-106](../../record/literature.d/LIT-106.md) and
[LIT-145](../../record/literature.d/LIT-145.md). So this pass is one reading, one access failure, and an audit of all
three tags.

## The audit

| work | tag added | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LIT-032](../../record/literature.d/LIT-032.md), a type theory for synthetic ∞-categories | `logic` | a type theory is a formal theory of proof |
| [LIT-095](../../record/literature.d/LIT-095.md), two-level type theory | `logic` | the same |
| [LIT-054](../../record/literature.d/LIT-054.md), a complete graphical calculus for Spekkens' toy theory | `logic` | its result is a soundness and completeness theorem for an equational proof system |
| [LIT-151](../../record/literature.d/LIT-151.md), Wallace on math-first structural realism | `philosophy-of-mathematics` | it asks what reading a theory as mathematics commits one to |
| [LIT-207](../../record/literature.d/LIT-207.md), Roberts on the fiction of artificial minds | `philosophy-of-language` | it argues that chatbots' self-ascriptions are systematically untrue, and treats engaging with them as make-believe |

The sheaf-theoretic contextuality paper ([LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md)) was considered for `logic`
and left alone. Its logic is the vehicle of a result about quantum theory,
not its subject.

## The reading

[LIT-145](../../record/literature.d/LIT-145.md), Turner's *Ultrafilters as Propositional Theories*, is `Active`. It was
read from the author's 2021 preprint. The 2025 Wiley version was not
reachable. Its abstract ends at Łoś's theorem, so whether the preprint's
compactness section survived into print is unverified. The reader found four
slips in the preprint's proofs. The NOTE lists them.

[LIT-106](../../record/literature.d/LIT-106.md) (Marabini) could not be fetched. It is marked open access, but every
route to it failed. Its status note says which.

## What the philosophy passes could not reach

Every work in the group has now been read in full or had a reading attempted.
Twenty-one remain `Deferred` for access, and each status note records what was
tried.

**Open, but not fetchable from this environment (five).** These are the
cheapest to close: a session whose network reaches the Wayback Machine and
PhilArchive, or a PDF supplied by hand.

- [LIT-106](../../record/literature.d/LIT-106.md), Marabini;
- [LIT-171](../../record/literature.d/LIT-171.md), [LIT-181](../../record/literature.d/LIT-181.md) and [LIT-186](../../record/literature.d/LIT-186.md), Pettigrew (PhilArchive);
- [LIT-180](../../record/literature.d/LIT-180.md), Wigner (PNAS archive).

**Closed access (sixteen).** These need a library copy:

- [LIT-104](../../record/literature.d/LIT-104.md), [LIT-108](../../record/literature.d/LIT-108.md), [LIT-117](../../record/literature.d/LIT-117.md), [LIT-119](../../record/literature.d/LIT-119.md), [LIT-128](../../record/literature.d/LIT-128.md), [LIT-129](../../record/literature.d/LIT-129.md), [LIT-134](../../record/literature.d/LIT-134.md), [LIT-137](../../record/literature.d/LIT-137.md);
- [LIT-144](../../record/literature.d/LIT-144.md), [LIT-148](../../record/literature.d/LIT-148.md), [LIT-174](../../record/literature.d/LIT-174.md), [LIT-183](../../record/literature.d/LIT-183.md), [LIT-197](../../record/literature.d/LIT-197.md), [LIT-201](../../record/literature.d/LIT-201.md), [LIT-203](../../record/literature.d/LIT-203.md), [LIT-214](../../record/literature.d/LIT-214.md).

In two passes, philosophy of science and this one, a reader's fetches were
refused by the session's permission check. Each reader reported the
refusal and did not work around it. That is the behaviour wanted. It also
means that some of the five open works were never actually tried by every
route.
