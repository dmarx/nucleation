The first reading review, run under ADR-tmp5urkb as its demonstration, in
the by-line mode, on organizational-normativity: the line the view of
readings by line (ADR-tmp67tdg) showed using no reading. The line's
account is SYNTHESIS-038. Its question for this run: what should this line
be building on, and is not?

## The list

The view was rendered locally from record/views.d/readings-by-line.md.j2,
since docs/readings-by-line.md is not yet generated. It named 579 readings
in force that no line uses. From them the review took:

- **by topic:** the 47 with any of the topics `ethics`, `self-governance`,
  `social-ontology`, `moral-psychology` or `society-and-governance` (the
  line's account carries the first, second and fifth; the others are the
  vocabulary's words for the rest of the line's subject), in any position,
  not only first; `altruism`, `future-generations` and `social-identity`
  found none;
- **by question:** none. No unused reading has `bears_on` or `answers` to
  the line's questions, QUESTION-128, QUESTION-129, QUESTION-256 and
  QUESTION-441;
- **by text:** a search of the 579 for the line's terms (answerability,
  accountability, legitimacy, correctability, corrigibility,
  responsibility, normativity, obligation, duty, blame, wrong, unjust,
  institution, organization, governance, moral), title hits first, then
  the files with the most hits. Most hits are false (harmonic analysis,
  "institutions" in the logical sense, tags). Eight were added whose
  content might meet the line: THEORY-1119, THEORY-1306, THEORY-1335,
  THEORY-835, THEORY-636, THEORY-1117, THEORY-404 and THEORY-1289.

55 readings in all, each read in full, with its source and its relations.
The line's entries were read against them: the claims, questions, term
and arguments SYNTHESIS-038 integrates, with CLAIM-462, CLAIM-523,
CLAIM-445, CLAIM-348 and TERM-100, which those entries rest on or use.

**A defect in the view, found in stage 1.** 19 of the 55 are not unused.
In force claims or arguments ground on them, and the view does not see
it. The template reads a reading's uses from `t.supports`, the converse of
`grounds`, and from `t.reports`, the converse of `reported_in`. Both are
derived fields that no file stores, and under `views.render` both come
back empty for every reading (THEORY-140's `supports` is empty though
CLAIM-619 and ARG-062 ground on it; so is THEORY-911's, though CLAIM-445
grounds on it). The view therefore counts only `corroborates`, `answers`
and `bears_on`. Across the record, 129 of the 579 readings it calls
unused are grounded on by a claim or argument not retired, most of
those uses in distributed-agency and pragmatic-transport by first line. This line
is not at zero either: CLAIM-619 and ARG-062 ground on THEORY-140, which
is in force (ARG-043 grounds on THEORY-1285, which is Proposed and so
outside the view). The 19 were reviewed all the same, against this
line, since none of their uses is in it. The fix is the template's, not
this review's: derive the users from the claims and arguments
(`docs("CLAIM")`, `docs("ARG")`, and CASE's `reported_in`) rather than
from the converse fields.

## Verdicts

### Tied (4)

- **THEORY-1119** (Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde) `bears_on`
  QUESTION-128. The question asks whether "norms of function and failure
  [can] be defined from an organization's own dynamics", and its body
  cites the work, through THEORY-071 (Proposed), for "Self-generated
  normativity as a condition of agency". THEORY-1119 is the Active
  reading of what the paper proposes. Also grounded by CLAIM-284,
  CLAIM-194 and CLAIM-288 (distributed-agency).
- **THEORY-1165** (Kahl) `bears_on` QUESTION-441. Its third part asks
  when the counter-normativity is "the organization's own rather than its
  participants' or an outside maintainer's"; Kahl's criterion is
  "whether revision of that rule under breakdown is internal or external
  to the candidate group agent", and institutional systems fail it
  because their standards are "authored elsewhere". Also `bears_on` the
  question raised below. Also grounded by CLAIM-485 (distributed-agency).
- **THEORY-1463** (Pritchett, Woolcock and Andrews) `bears_on`
  QUESTION-441, which asks what makes reproductive the counter-normativity
  of an organization that "reproduces the representational projections
  that make the harm non-legible". Isomorphic mimicry is "keeping
  legitimacy by imitating the forms of functional organizations without
  their function", with de jure scores above de facto ones. The authors
  offer it as how failure persists, untested against alternatives, and
  the harm is a capability failure; the history note says so.
- **THEORY-837** (Besley and Prat) `corroborates` CLAIM-462, that an
  arrangement "can keep, or increase, its capacity for effective action
  while its capacity to be effectively corrected by others degrades,
  because the two are maintained by different relations". Under capture
  the incumbent keeps office and voters "never remove a bad incumbent";
  the relation degraded, the outlets' report, is one the incumbent's
  action does not need. CLAIM-462 is distributed-agency's, and a premise
  of CLAIM-624 in this line. The model is one-shot, so it is not tied to
  QUESTION-441's reproduction. Also grounded by CLAIM-404.

### Raised (1)

- **THEORY-1118** (the manipulation argument) raises QUESTION-tmpmus93,
  Open, in organizational-normativity, refining QUESTION-441: when an
  organization reproduces a mechanism that keeps its harm non-legible,
  does it matter to whether the counter-normativity is its own that
  another installed the mechanism, or does only whose process now
  maintains it decide? The record answers QUESTION-441's third part only
  in the present tense, by TERM-100's maintenance provenance and
  CLAIM-522's knockouts; TERM-100's formation provenance and CASE-191's
  construction are not carried to it. THEORY-1118 and THEORY-1165 bear on
  it from opposite sides. The wording is the assistant's. The same review
  also ties THEORY-1118 to QUESTION-071 (will-organization), whose summary
  cites THEORY-040 (Proposed) for the same argument; THEORY-1118 is its
  Active reading. Also grounded by CLAIM-364 (authenticity).

No claim was drafted. Nothing the 55 hold states a claim about normative
judgment of organizations that no entry states.
