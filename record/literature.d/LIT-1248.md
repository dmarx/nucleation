# LIT-1248: Onsager's conjecture on the energy conservation for solutions of Euler's equation

Constantin, P., E, W. & Titi, E. S. (1994). Onsager's conjecture on the energy conservation for solutions of Euler's equation. *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 165(1): 207–209. doi:10.1007/BF02099744.

Read in full on 2026-10-20 from the authors' manuscript, in [NOTE-982](../notes.d/NOTE-982.md).
The proof is held, re-derived, as [THEORY-749](../theory.d/THEORY-749.md).

## Why this is here

A3 cited it for a general pattern: whether the effect of unresolved structure vanishes in a limit depends on the regularity of the field. The exchange named it as "the classic Constantin–E–Titi work" without a title. Bears on [QUESTION-219](../questions.d/QUESTION-219.md). The reading narrows what it can be cited for. It proves that above exponent 1/3 the flux into unresolved scales must vanish as the scale goes to zero. It does not prove that a coarse-grained flux can survive for rough fields: that rests on the later negative half (Isett 2018 and others), which the record does not hold. Its estimate is the one behind [THEORY-754](../theory.d/THEORY-754.md), read at a fixed field as the scale goes to zero.
