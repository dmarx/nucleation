# SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9: Accountability without blame and normative governability: blame and accountability are proposed to come apart, so that correction does not wait on culpability, blame traced upstream supplies the provenance of an incapacity, an incapacity an institution brought about in breach of a duty does not excuse it, blame and repair do not substitute for each other, and an institution responsive within its governing mechanism need not be responsive to reasons for revising it

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, on accountability without blame and on normative governability. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day, from which every component comes; none was in any account before. Most components also carry distributed-agency. Its sibling on rights, demands and commitments, filed at the same review, is SYNTHESIS-tmpoodbq; the organizational wrong beyond harm and CLAIM-624's duty, which several components here cite, are mapped in SYNTHESIS-094.*

## The account

Each link is stated by the entry named first, in its front matter unless
its body is cited. Every component is Proposed, or Open for the question.

**Blame is not accountability.** CLAIM-tmpsk25g (the owner's, U47; A110's
four questions, usage note, amendment of "unintentionally" to "unwitting"
and three assessments are A's and credited in its body; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) uses TERM-tmp8pnnr and
bears on QUESTION-068: an agent's diminished culpability for the reasons
it acts on "does not diminish the wrongfulness of what it does, the claims
of those it harms, or the need for correction, because the grounds of
correction are not exhausted by the grounds of blame". Its body reads
"accountable" in TERM-tmp8pnnr's remedial sense, and says the claim
leaves open who owes the correction. TERM-tmp8pnnr (A's senses, A110 §2
with A5 §5, A9 §5 and A17 §9; the distinction it keeps is the owner's
U47; Proposed; also distributed-agency) draws on THEORY-147 and
THEORY-207: causal, attributability, culpability, remedial and
constitutive responsibility, "a verdict in one sense does not settle
another". Its body says CLAIM-tmpsk25g, CLAIM-tmpixmhp, CLAIM-tmp0wx69
and CLAIM-tmp4o53g are stated in it. Six claims of the exchange's first
parts, in dependence-and-domination, also use it.

**Blame as provenance.** CLAIM-tmpixmhp (the owner's idea, U47; the label
"the provenance of normative incapacity" and the development are A110
§3's; its bearing on QUESTION-449 is A's, narrowed by the filer; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-tmpsk25g,
uses TERM-tmp8pnnr and TERM-100, and bears on QUESTION-449 and
QUESTION-071: responsibility for a harmful act and for producing the
incapacity through which it was done "can come apart and attach to
different actors". Its body says it does not answer QUESTION-449, which
asks whose an organization's counter-normativity is. CASE-tmpolqas (A's,
A110 §11 Case A, with A110 §7's negligent variant; fictional, Proposed)
illustrates CLAIM-tmpixmhp and CLAIM-tmp0wx69 and bears on QUESTION-449:
two officials make the same wrongful decision in good faith, and in one
institution a superior removed the review that would have caught it. Its
body says it cannot show that the removal breached a duty, which needs
CLAIM-624 and ARG-tmptw59w, and that both arms are internal to one
institution, so it does not reach QUESTION-449's own case.

**Self-induced incapacity.** CLAIM-tmp0wx69 (A's, A108 §5 and A110 §7;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed) rests on CLAIM-tmpixmhp, uses
TERM-tmp8pnnr and bears on QUESTION-449: a present incapacity to
recognize a violation does not show the party is not responsible for the
incapacity; where it dismantled the capability in breach of a duty, the
incapacity "does not excuse it", and where no one is culpable "the duty to
repair still stands". Its body says whether the dismantling breached a
duty needs CLAIM-624's maintenance duty, argued in ARG-tmptw59w, and that
it does not answer QUESTION-449, which is about mechanisms installed by
another.

**Blame and repair do not substitute.** CLAIM-tmp4o53g (A's, A110 §8;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on
CLAIM-tmpsk25g and uses TERM-tmp8pnnr: assigning blame for a harmful
condition does not discharge present remedial duties, and assigning
remedial duties does not show blame, so treating one guilty official as
a repair is "blame displacement" and loading repair on whoever is nearest
is "remedial scapegoating". Its body names CASE-151 as a documented
instance of blame displacement, CLAIM-tmpb47ul's moral crumple zone as the
mechanism of scapegoating, and QUESTION-tmp2t6ve as the question it bears
on most directly. CASE-tmpd0hyp (A's, A110 §11 Case B; fictional,
Proposed; also distributed-agency) illustrates CLAIM-tmp4o53g and
CLAIM-tmpsk25g: the same wrong through the same procedure with blameless
decision-makers, where one institution can remedy it and the other
cannot but a public authority can. Its body says it builds only the
second half of CLAIM-tmp4o53g, and that whether jurisdiction grounds a
duty rather than a permission is a further premise.

**Blameless culture.** QUESTION-tmp2t6ve (the owner's suggestion, U48,
the exchange's last turn, unanswered; the wording is the filer's; Open;
also distributed-agency) stores no relation: does blameless post-incident
practice in engineering keep accountability while giving up blame, and
does it keep or lose the upstream provenance blame supplies? Its body
puts it to CLAIM-tmpsk25g and CLAIM-tmpixmhp, and names CLAIM-tmp4o53g's
blame displacement "turned round" as one possible answer. The candidate
sources it names are the filer's, not the exchange's.

**Normative governability.** CLAIM-tmp69o4u (A's, A108 §6, A110 §9 and
§10, A106 §18; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) uses TERM-tmphum9r: an
institution can be reliably responsive to reasons within its governing
mechanism and unresponsive to justified reasons for revising it, and
first-order responsiveness "is no evidence of responsiveness at the
second". Its body says ρ^(2) "is named, not defined", and that the lack of
second-order responsiveness is a wrong only with CLAIM-624's duty as a
further premise. TERM-tmphum9r (A's term, A110 §13's version of four;
the link to reasons-responsiveness is the owner's, U46; Proposed) draws on
THEORY-121 and THEORY-122: the maintained, role-appropriate capacity to
recognize and respond to justified considerations, "including
considerations that require revising its own governing mechanisms";
relational, and not corrigibility, compliance or obedience. Its body
says the second-order clause is CLAIM-tmp69o4u's responsiveness, that
A110 §13's last paragraph ("Deficiencies in normative governability may
explain wrongful conduct without excusing every duty arising from it")
ties the term to CLAIM-tmpsk25g and CLAIM-tmp0wx69, and that
CLAIM-tmp69o4u is the first candidate claim stated in it.

## Not stated on the record

- Who owes the correction in any case. CLAIM-tmpsk25g and CLAIM-tmp4o53g
  leave it open; CASE-tmpd0hyp says the public authority's duty needs a
  premise the record has not read.
- An answer to QUESTION-449. CLAIM-tmpixmhp, CLAIM-tmp0wx69 and
  CASE-tmpolqas bear on it, and each says it does not reach a mechanism
  installed by another.
- An answer to QUESTION-tmp2t6ve, or whether a blameless review is a
  mechanism of second-order responsiveness in CLAIM-tmp69o4u's sense. The
  question does not cite CLAIM-tmp69o4u or TERM-tmphum9r.
- Whether CLAIM-tmp69o4u's ρ^(2) can be defined: its body says no case
  family or response set is given.
- Whether the specimen of the sibling part, CASE-tmpoe934, is an
  institution reliable at the first order and unresponsive at the second.
  CLAIM-tmp69o4u's example (an eligibility rule followed while evidence
  of its conflict with a superior obligation is refused) resembles it;
  neither cites the other. See Arm B.

## Arm B

Integrations tested at this review under ADR-075's step 5. None passes,
so nothing is filed.

- *CLAIM-tmpsk25g with CLAIM-tmpssfc1*: an agent's diminished
  culpability is not an exercise of a valid normative power, so the
  claim to relief persists through the agent's excuse. Already stated,
  in CLAIM-tmpsk25g's body: "The imprisoned person's claim to release does
  not become weaker merely because JoJo lacks ordinary moral competence".
- *CLAIM-tmp0wx69 with CLAIM-tmp69o4u*: a governability deficiency an
  institution produced does not excuse what is done through it. Already
  stated, in TERM-tmphum9r's definition (A110 §13's last paragraph) and
  body.
- *CLAIM-tmpw7pli with CLAIM-tmp69o4u*: the visa gate is an institution
  reliable at the first order and unresponsive at the second. Route:
  discriminating prediction. Fails on a free parameter: indexed to the
  governing statute, the gate is a first-order failure (evidence the
  statute requires stops counting); indexed to the institution's own
  maintained requirements, it is first-order reliable. The sentence
  passes or fails by that choice, which no component fixes. Left under
  "Not stated".
- *CLAIM-tmp4o53g and CLAIM-tmpsk25g with CLAIM-tmpes6ef and
  CASE-tmpd0hyp*: where the repair needs an arrangement no party can
  reach by permissible, resource-bounded means, a claim to correction
  persists with no one bound to complete it. Route: derivation. Fails:
  the persistence half is CLAIM-tmpa0wdv's (a claim persists while the
  correctability of its remedy falls to zero), "complete" is a free
  parameter, and the "no one bound" half meets ARG-tmptw59w's fallback
  from A110 §5D, duties to seek assistance, limit activity or inform.
- *CLAIM-tmpixmhp with TERM-100*: the provenance blame supplies is not
  descriptive provenance. Already stated, in TERM-tmp8pnnr's body ("Not
  TERM-100's provenance") and CLAIM-tmpixmhp's.

## What it does not say

- That any actual institution dismantled a capability in breach of a
  duty: CASE-tmpolqas and CASE-tmpd0hyp are fictional, and the duty is
  CLAIM-624's, Proposed.
- That incapacity changes nothing, or that challengers get unlimited
  powers: CLAIM-tmpsk25g's body says neither.
- Which sense of "accountability" the literature uses; TERM-tmp8pnnr
  avoids the word and says why.
- Anything with standing of its own.
