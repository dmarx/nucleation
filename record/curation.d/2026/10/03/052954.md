The owner asked for theories to be filed from the psychopathology, emotion
and psychotherapy batch ([ADR-021](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-021.md)). The owner especially wanted ones that
support or engage the record's philosophy-of-mind holdings, and asked
explicitly for an account of ACT's theory of the self. Midway the owner
asked how the record covered self-determination theory, and then asked
for it to be filed in the same contribution. The record held none, so
`motivation` was added ([ADR-023](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-023.md)).

## Fifteen theories

One is Active. [THEORY-053](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-053.md) holds that a treatment's efficacy is not
evidence for its theory of change. It is close to a logical point, and
both wings of CBT state it while disagreeing about mechanism. The other
fourteen are Proposed, each with what would settle it.

**Emotion and affect.**

- [THEORY-045](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-045.md): felt affect is the body's regulatory state. Barrett
  ([LIT-542](../../record/literature.d/LIT-542.md)) and Damasio & Carvalho ([LIT-388](../../record/literature.d/LIT-388.md)) agree on that much. They
  disagree on whether what is felt is a prediction or a map, and on where
  it is generated. The theory records the disagreement instead of
  choosing. On the question of AI consciousness ([THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md)), it shows
  where the open question lies for affect.
- [THEORY-057](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-057.md) (emotion categories are perceiver-constructed
  populations) and [THEORY-055](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-055.md) (appraisal elicits action readiness)
  are declared `rivals`.
- [THEORY-056](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-056.md): regulation is not a process separate from generation.
  The two rival theories of emotion agree on it, and Deci & Ryan argue
  against it.

**Psychopathology.**

- [THEORY-048](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-048.md): biology implements psychological function and does not
  replace it. It is tied to Pernu on mental causation and to the
  record's emergence holdings.
- [THEORY-058](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-058.md): disorders persist through self-reinforcing loops.
  Three traditions say this without citing one another.
- [THEORY-050](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-050.md): psychological inflexibility is transdiagnostic.

**The self.**

- [THEORY-044](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-044.md) gives RFT's three selves. Self-as-content is the
  narrative self. Self-as-context is what Hume could not find and what
  Metzinger calls the transparent perspective. The open seam is
  Gallagher's minimal self: if self-as-context were that, RFT's claim
  that it is learned would contradict the neonatal evidence.
- [THEORY-051](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-051.md): strengthening self-as-context is how ACT helps. It
  extends [THEORY-050](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-050.md). Nothing in the record tests this directly.

**Motivation.**

- [THEORY-054](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-054.md): autonomy is graded integration, not order. It agrees
  with [THEORY-029](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-029.md)'s negative claim but does not escape Velleman.
- [THEORY-047](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-047.md): a regulation's history of acquisition fixes how
  autonomous it is. This is a historical condition with experimental
  support, beside [THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md).
- [THEORY-049](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-049.md): whether a reward undermines intrinsic motivation
  depends on whether it is experienced as controlling or as information
  about competence.
- [THEORY-046](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-046.md): expected engagement-contingent rewards lower free
  choice.
- [THEORY-052](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-052.md): need frustration is transdiagnostic. It can hold
  alongside [THEORY-050](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-050.md).

The authors post most SDT papers on their own site, which serves as the
lawful deposit.

Ryan & Deci 2006 barely engages Frankfurt. It cites him twice, in one
paragraph, and takes its detail from Dworkin. The connection to the
record's philosophy of autonomy is therefore made by the record, in
[NOTE-264](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-264.md), [NOTE-266](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-266.md), [NOTE-267](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-267.md), [THEORY-029](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-029.md) and [THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md). The papers do
not make it themselves.

**The behaviourist side of the reward dispute is unread.** Cameron, Banko
& Pierce's 2001 reply was read at first from a third-party host. PubMed
Central and Europe PMC both answered with bot challenges, and the record's
precedent ([LIT-545](../../record/literature.d/LIT-545.md)) refuses third-party copies. So the reading was set
aside, the note deleted before merge, and the LIT made Deferred with only
what the abstract says. A human can read the free PMC scan (PMC2731358)
in a browser and restore it.
