# LIT-tmppu6di: Measurement as Sheafification: Context, Logic, and Truth after Quantum Mechanics

Ghose (2025), *arXiv preprint (v2, 2025-12-16)* — [ARXIV-2512.12249](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12249)

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-27 at the owner's request, who linked v2. It restates the
sheaf-theoretic account of contextuality the record holds as [LIT-016](LIT-016.md) and
[THEORY-012](../theory.d/THEORY-012.md), and claims to extend it. `published:` is the arXiv v1 date
([ADR-002](../decisions.d/ADR-002.md)). No journal version is known.

It was filed `Deferred`, unread. [NOTE-tmppm8d2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmppm8d2.md) is the close reading of 2026-09-27, and it placed the work: **Rejected** — not worth a reader's time for its formal claims. Every correct formal ingredient is Abramsky & Brandenburger's ([LIT-016](LIT-016.md)) or Abramsky, Mansfield & Barbosa's, and both are better read in the original. What the essay adds is either unconstructed (the sheafification, the site, the seven-element Heyting algebra, the link from λ to cohomology) or wrong as stated: v2's obstruction class is zero by construction, a sheaf topos is not in general Boolean, and classical distributions do not form a sheaf. Its only value is as a record of a position, the Bohrian reading of contextuality plus the Jaina analogy.
