# LEAD-199: substantive revisions against inbound edges

The study filed as the CASE that carries out LEAD-199 (2026-11-22). Run by a delegated analyst on origin/staging's history from 2026-10-07 to 2026-11-22; copied here so the result can be rerun.

- `extract.py`: walks non-merge commits touching record/, follows claims through renames and `formerly:`, and writes revisions.json (not committed: 3.4 MB, regenerated by this script), edges.json and population.json.
- `classify.py`: decides whether a change is substantive (title, role, warrant, defeated_if, or the body outside dated notes, after normalisation); `test_classify.py` holds 14 synthetic-diff tests (`python3 -m pytest -q test_classify.py`).
- `analyze.py`: rates per 30 days at risk, Mantel-Haenszel rate ratios stratified by age (and age × week), risk-set matching over 7 days, cluster bootstrap over claims; environment variables SKIP (days dropped after filing) and BULK (drop commits touching more than this many claims). `matched_late.py`: the matched comparison for exposures a day or more after filing.
- `report.py` prints summary.txt; `sample.py` draws the hand-check samples; `check1.py` checks the edge counts.
- Results: results_main.json, results_skip1.json, results_bulk20.json, results_skip1bulk20.json, summary.txt, matched_late.txt; hand-checks: handcheck_substantive.txt, handcheck_nonsubstantive.txt, handcheck_tally.txt.
