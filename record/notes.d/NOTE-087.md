# NOTE-087: Kerr County Commissioners' Court transcript, 27 June 2016

## Contribution

This is the transcript of an ordinary regular session of the commissioners' court of Kerr County, Texas. It covers plats, job descriptions, right-of-way deeds, insurance renewals, a risk-control reimbursement application, a Wounded Warrior event, a contract with GrantWorks for procurement services on a hazard mitigation application and plan, septic contamination at the Castlecomb subdivision, LED lighting, bills and reports. In the reports section, Commissioner Moser announced a steering-committee meeting that Thursday on a Kerr County flood warning system, and the court discussed its cost and need.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- Agenda 1.5 (risk control reimbursement): Moser asks whether about $50k of Texas Association of Counties reimbursement could go to the flood warning system. Staff say no; the fund covers only workers' comp and public liability.
- Agenda 1.8: GrantWorks, the only bidder, is chosen for procurement services on the Hazard Mitigation Application and Plan. The contract was to be executed on 11 July.
- Agenda 1.13: a status report on continuing septic effluent contamination at Castlecomb, with possible flow to Silver Creek and the Guadalupe River. TCEQ acted after a letter from the office of Representative Murr.
- Reports 5.1 (flood warning): cost figures of a roughly $50k engineering analysis and about $20k of county spending the next year. Comal County spent about $320k on 8 sensors plus central command and control. Baldwin describes the old phone-tree system ("the Jones call the Smiths, and the Smiths call Camp Rio Vista") as still working. Moser: the audience includes "the general public ... and then the camps", and the questions are "do we need to do anything", what, and at what cost.
