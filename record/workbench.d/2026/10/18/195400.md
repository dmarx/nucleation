*The owner relayed a synthesis on 2026-10-18 and asked for it to be
addressed in a new contribution. It was written by an assistant in the
owner's own conversation, after reading parts of this record. It made no
repository changes and its proposals are not the owner's unless an entry
says so. Every code it cited was checked and exists with the content it
described. Its mathematics was rechecked here, numerically where possible.*

**Its thesis.** The distributed-agency line could be developed as "a
theory of endogenous functional differentiation: how initially
interchangeable processes become specialized, acquire differential
influence, and maintain a viable organization without losing the capacity
to reorganize". Feature learning supplies models of how differentiation
emerges. Coexistence theory supplies conditions under which differentiated
components persist. The record's own question is a third: when a
population of differentiated components is a self-governing organization,
not a collection selected and regulated from outside.

**What it found already held.** It did not propose a new general claim
that niche specialization matters to distributed agency, since that
"would duplicate the record". CLAIM-279 and its note, QUESTION-083's
recovery test, CLAIM-277's exclusion loop and CLAIM-362's access to
consequential roles already hold it. Chesson (LIT-1219) and Cooper and
West (LIT-1225) are registered and unread. What was missing was the
mathematics.

**What was filed, following its proposals A–E with one change.**

- **A, the invasion-fitness bridge**, is CLAIM-tmpc36bo, demonstrated and
  Active, with the proof and a numerical check. The synthesis proposed it
  as an ARG. In this record an ARG concludes a claim, and here the
  derivation is itself the claim, so it is filed as a demonstrated claim.
  Its two-component example, including the growth-asymmetry case in which
  the same Gram matrix supports coexistence or exclusion, is
  CASE-tmptwfjg. The limit is kept in the claim: the equivalence fails for

- **The first bridge's question**, whether feature geometry predicts

- **B** is QUESTION-tmp0885h, which refines QUESTION-083. It carries the

- **C** is QUESTION-tmpjwshe, with CASE-tmpuuf3p: three demands, every

- **D** is QUESTION-tmpn0znj. It separates loss of influence, of

- **E** is CASE-tmprbebk, the proposed population study. It includes the
