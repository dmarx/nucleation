# LIT-255: Information Theory with Kernel Methods

Francis Bach (2022), *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory (2023); first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-2202.08545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08545)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-837](../notes.d/NOTE-837.md).*

- **A distribution becomes a quantum state.** Under (A1), compact X and
  k(x,x) = 1, Σ_p is PSD with unit trace. It is injective in p when k²
  is universal (Proposition 1). It is uncentred: footnote 1.
- **Sandwiched between two KLs.** D(Σ_p‖Σ_q) ≤ D(p‖q) by joint
  convexity, with equality only for an orthonormal, discrete embedding
  (Proposition 4(d)). D(p̃‖q̃) ≤ D(Σ_p‖Σ_q) by monotonicity under a
  measurement that smooths p with h(x,y) = ⟨ϕ(x), Σ^{−1/2}ϕ(y)⟩² (Eq. 8).
  On the torus the gap is at most O(dσ²) for Lipschitz densities
  (Proposition 5).
- **Computable.** tr Σ̂ log Σ̂ = tr[(K/n) log(K/n)] (Proposition 6). It
  converges at about 1/√n without regularization (Proposition 7). As a
  Shannon entropy estimator it gets n^{−2/(d+4)}, the square root of the
  optimal rate.
- **Independence, yes; conditional independence, half.** Kernel MI
  D(Σ_{p₁₂}‖Σ_{p₁}⊗Σ_{p₂}) is zero iff the variables are independent
  (Proposition 9). Data processing and submodularity hold, but their
  equality cases characterize conditional independence only one way
  (Propositions 10–11). Conditional entropies do not decompose.
- **Variational inference.** The convex dual of the kernel KL gives
  upper bounds on log-partition functions. As the temperature goes to
  zero they become kernel sums-of-squares optimization, and they can be
  tightened by learning the kernel (§7).

The seed's links, checked against the text:

- **GNS.** Bach does not draw the link. The identity ⟨g, Σ_p f⟩ = E_p[fg]
  is a definition in §2.2, and GNS is not mentioned.
- **Representation similarity.** Fidelity, Bures distance and
  representation similarity are not mentioned either. For a
  unit-diagonal, uncentred kernel, K/n equals the trace-normalized
  kernel that [THEORY-233](../theory.d/THEORY-233.md) compares. Representation kernels are usually
  centred and linear, so Bach's results do not carry over to them as
  they stand.
- **KL.** The link to the Radon–Nikodym/KL strand holds as the seed said.
