# QUESTION-tmpj2knl: When the mechanisms composing a will-organization are described by control theory and executive-control research, does that description implement the will-organization account or replace it, and what result would show the agent-level description had become idle?

## The question

[CLAIM-021](../claims.d/CLAIM-021.md) grants that the mechanisms composing a will-organization are not novel. [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md) presses the counter that the existing fields already study every one of them. A lower-level account can stand in two relations to a higher-level one. It can implement it, saying how the stated functions are done. Or it can replace it, making the higher-level construct redundant, as mean kinetic energy did for temperature. The question is which relation holds here, and how to tell.

## Why the record needs it

The will paper's own standard is that a concept with no explanatory gain "should not be retained merely as a new label" ([CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md)). If the mechanism literatures replace the account, [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md) wins. If they implement it, [CLAIM-021](../claims.d/CLAIM-021.md)'s concession costs nothing, and the gain lies where [CLAIM-122](../claims.d/CLAIM-122.md) places it: in the conditions of efficacy. The transfer is not an analogy. Kendler's relation concerns psychological functions realized in agents, which is the target here. [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) already supplies a quantitative criterion of the right kind. An agent-level description is kept when it beats a lower-level one by more than its Occam cost.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-048](../theory.d/THEORY-048.md): lower-level findings "implement psychological functions rather than replacing them". Function is stated first, and the mechanism is how it gets done. The account is refuted for a domain if the lower-level account "makes the psychological description idle". Limit: argued from perception and memory, not from psychiatric cases, and the step to all of psychiatry "is asserted". It is about explanation, not about distinct causes.

## What would answer it

The comparison [CLAIM-024](../claims.d/CLAIM-024.md) says is undone. Take a case such as Northstar, model it with control and metacontrol mechanisms alone, then with a will-organization over them, and price the difference as [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) does. Replacement is shown if the mechanism model predicts as well with no will-organization variables.
