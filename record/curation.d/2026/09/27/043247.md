The owner asked for a nucleation reading of arXiv 2111.14046, Guo & Draper,
*Neural Tangent Kernel of Matrix Product States: Convergence and
Applications* ([LIT-271](../../record/literature.d/LIT-271.md)). Neither record held it. The full 19-page v1
(the only version; no published version was found) was read, and the reading
is [NOTE-244](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-244.md).

**Verdict: `Rejected`.** The paper's one sound calculation is that, in the
sequential infinite-bond-dimension limit, the MPS NTK is
Σ_k φ(x_k)·φ(x′_k) Π_{l≠k} σ_l² φ(x_l)·φ(x′_l). That is the MPS's own GP
product kernel times the constant Σ_k σ_k⁻², which follows in a line from the
model being multilinear in its tensors. I checked that reduction when filing,
and the paper never states it. Tensor Programs II ([ANTH-LIT-557](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-557.md)) covers this
kind of architecture rigorously. The distinctive claims do not hold up:

- **The lazy-training lemma is circular.** It uses the gradient at
  initialisation for all times and bounds entries, not the kernel.
- **Positive-definiteness is not proved.** The abstract says it holds
  "without any extra assumptions of the data set". The appendix proof shows
  at most semi-definiteness, and strict definiteness fails whenever
  m > Π|s_i|, the finite feature dimension the GP theorem itself assumes.
- **"Over-fitting is naturally avoided"** contradicts the paper's own
  memorisation result.
- **Several constants are off** by factors of n or 2^{−n}.
- **There are no experiments.**

The Born-machine closed form P_x(t)=1/m−(1/m−P_x(0))e^{−4mKt/Z} is correct;
the reader checked it numerically.

**Filing choices.** It is held here, not in the anthology, because it is a
theory paper with nothing for practice to act on. Its NTK ancestry is already
in the anthology ([ANTH-LIT-360](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-360.md), [ANTH-LIT-557](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-557.md), [ANTH-LIT-558](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-558.md)). The tags are
learning-theory, probabilistic-modeling (the GP limit and Born machines) and
mathematics. The reader proposed quantum-foundations; I dropped it, because
MPS here is a function class borrowed from physics, and nothing in the paper
is about the foundations of quantum theory. No existing nucleation work is
genuinely connected. [LIT-242](../../record/literature.d/LIT-242.md) (stepwise SSL as kernel PCA) was considered and
set aside.
