# The Governance of Becoming: working draft

*Individuation, Interdependence, and the Organization of Possibility.* A
series of articles, proposed at the owner's U31 of the exchange "Memes,
Contracts, and Systemic Normativity" (2026-11-24), arguing from the
Nucleation record that organizations individuate through maintained
relationships, implicit contracts among them; that cooperation changes its
participants, so exploitation, domination and agency without answerability
can arise from success; and that normative governance is the legitimate
organization of those transformations. It is a view over the record's
argument (ADR-tmpbdfmk): it states nothing the record does not hold in an
entry or an account.
