# LEAD-tmpqgbxs: Fix what the discriminating fidelity study must fix before data: a strong contextual baseline, named receiver populations and sampling frames, an equivalence margin with power, and an interlingual arm

## What it would do

Write the study's preregistration: the contextual baseline the incremental
thesis is restated against, the receiver populations and how they are
sampled, the equivalence margin and the power to detect it, the prevalence
rates it assumes, and one language pair for the interlingual arm.

Where the record names it:

- CASE-055: "It has not been run, and its margins are not yet fixed."
- CLAIM-205: "The incremental thesis restated against a strong contextual
  baseline, specified in advance, with the margin fixed as CLAIM-202 already
  requires."
- CLAIM-226: "A named receiver population and sampling frame in each
  design."
- CLAIM-222: "An interlingual design: at least one language pair in which
  footing or politeness is grammatically marked on one side only, with
  judges on both sides."

## What would count

The lead is done when the margins are fixed in CASE-055. It answers
CLAIM-226 and CLAIM-222 by meeting what they ask, and lets CLAIM-202 and
CLAIM-205 be defeated or not by data.
