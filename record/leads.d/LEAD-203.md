# LEAD-203: Read the metastability paper of Bovier and colleagues and, once a copy is supplied, Fenichel's geometric singular perturbation theory, to put the two halves of the hierarchy-and-hyperbolicity claims on readings: the small-noise time geometry of reversible chains, and the persistence of normally hyperbolic slow manifolds

## What to read for

- **Bovier and colleagues ([LIT-2951](../literature.d/LIT-2951.md)).** Whether their metastable
  hierarchy is the budgeted tree of [CLAIM-581](../claims.d/CLAIM-581.md) and [CLAIM-780](../claims.d/CLAIM-780.md); how they
  handle non-uniform stationary laws, where barriers are heights relative to
  wells; and whether they state transition-time asymptotics that give
  [CLAIM-780](../claims.d/CLAIM-780.md)'s bound in general. Also whether they bear on
  [QUESTION-501](../questions.d/QUESTION-501.md)'s converse.
- **Fenichel ([LIT-1638](../literature.d/LIT-1638.md)).** The condition under which a slow manifold
  persists, stated as a ratio of normal to tangential rates, and whether it
  is the rate ratio [CLAIM-781](../claims.d/CLAIM-781.md) identifies with the spacing of the tree.

## Then

A NOTE and THEORY for each under read-and-file. If the readings carry the
claims, add them to `grounds`; if Bovier states the bound in general,
[CLAIM-780](../claims.d/CLAIM-780.md)'s limit on symmetric rates can be lifted in a new version.

## Note of 2026-11-21: Bovier and colleagues read

Bovier, Eckhoff, Gayrard and Klein were read on 2026-11-21 ([THEORY-2137](../theory.d/THEORY-2137.md)). Their metastable hierarchy matches the budgeted tree in structure, through an ultrametric up to ln 3 on metastable points; non-uniform stationary laws are handled by barriers relative to wells; they give one-way exit times under genericity, not [CLAIM-780](../claims.d/CLAIM-780.md)'s commute-time bound, so the claim's restriction to symmetric rates stays. The reading also showed that bounded hyperbolicity of log commute time is trivial, which [CLAIM-780](../claims.d/CLAIM-780.md) (version 3) now says. Fenichel ([LIT-1638](../literature.d/LIT-1638.md)) remains Unfetched and needs a copy from the owner; that half stays open.
