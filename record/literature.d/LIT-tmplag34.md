# LIT-tmplag34: Reply to Leif Wenar

T. M. Scanlon (2013). "Reply to Leif Wenar". *Journal of Moral Philosophy* 10(4): 400–405. DOI-10.1163/17455243-01004013.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading (NOTE-tmpzarar).*

- "Contractualism seeks to explain not only first-order claim rights but
  also powers, as well as the prior liberties" (p. 403), including "why a
  promisee has the power to release the promisor from her obligation".
- "I don't think that we need to appeal to the idea of a right to explain
  why our moral situation has this form, and don't think it matters very
  much exactly which parts of this structure we denominate as rights"
  (pp. 403–404).
- Promisees are singled out by "the central role that their interest in
  assurance has in justifying principles of fidelity" and by those
  principles making the obligation "sensitive to their wills" (p. 404).
- Moral rights "place demands on what laws and other institutions must be
  like" (p. 401); free expression is "an immunity: a limit on the powers of
  government" (p. 402).
