# CLAIM-1175: A self's unity is established causally

## The claim

A15, "A sharper formulation", verbatim:

> A self is a historically developing organization of overlapping
> dispositions and perspectival relations, whose observable differentiation
> depends on the available tests, but whose unity—if it has one—must be
> established through the causal processes that coordinate, maintain, and
> transform those dispositions.
>
> Its perspectives need not be independently individuated constituents.
> They may be partially separable organizations, realized differently
> across contexts and timescales. The self may also acquire new
> distinctions and channels through their interaction, thereby changing the
> very structure in which subsequent perspectives become possible.

A15 §1 adds that "there may be multiple equally adequate ways to factor the
same self into perspectives", a decomposition into professional, social and
private perspectives predicting some behaviour and one into exploratory,
protective and habitual perspectives predicting other behaviour, which
"need not be rival claims about one uniquely correct inventory of inner
parts". No entry held that.

## What it adds

Its integration over the components is the claim that the self's unity is
located in the causal row of the table and not the observational one, with
the observational rows left test-relative. The record holds the causal
location for the self-governing sense ([CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md)) and the test-relativity
for distinctions in general ([CLAIM-574](CLAIM-574.md)); it did not hold their joint use
for the parts of a self, or the plurality of adequate factorizations.

## Admission ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md); the assistant's audit, 2026-11-22)

*A discriminating prediction.* Its premises ([CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md), [CLAIM-532](CLAIM-532.md),
[CLAIM-574](CLAIM-574.md)) leave open where a self's unity lies. The claim puts it in the
causal processes of coordination and maintenance. So it predicts that two
systems matched in ordinary behaviour, one coordinated by mutual
regulation and one by a common external driver, come apart under
interventions on their coupling or on the driver. If they stay
indistinguishable under rich intervention histories, the claim fails.
[LEAD-144](../leads.d/LEAD-144.md) runs that design.

## Note of 2026-11-23: what holds it back

Its admission names a discriminating prediction, matched systems coordinated
by mutual regulation and by a common driver coming apart under interventions
on coupling, and [LEAD-144](../leads.d/LEAD-144.md), which runs it, is Open. The clause that a self
admits several equally adequate factorizations into perspectives, which it
adds over its premises, has no case in the record.
