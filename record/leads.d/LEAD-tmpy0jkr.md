# LEAD-tmpy0jkr: Build a model industrial ecosystem with heterogeneous producers, a shared infrastructure bottleneck, an externality and a few declared public demands, and compare an unregulated baseline, a uniform conduct constraint and a regime that also changes infrastructure access, measuring the compatibility complex, realized violations and the recovery of rare capabilities

## The plan

The plan is A33 §9's testbed. It would test three entries at once.

- **[QUESTION-tmpn9t5a](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpn9t5a.md).** Does the access-changing regime give a rare
  capability positive growth when demand for it returns, where the
  uniform constraint does not?
- **[CLAIM-tmpx9nrj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx9nrj.md).** Can the regimes be found to move the complex and
  the probability of meeting the demands independently?
- **[CLAIM-tmpk4bou](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4bou.md).** If the uniform conduct constraint is a pure hard
  constraint on fixed dynamics, its complex must not exceed the
  baseline's. Any enlargement has to come from the access change.

## What it needs before it is specified

- a declared bearer of recovery, a firm or a capability (see the
  question);
- the public demands as acceptance sets over histories at a stated
  horizon;
- a behavioural model for participants, so that P_D is defined;
- a perturbation schedule fixed before the comparison is run.
