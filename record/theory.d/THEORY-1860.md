# THEORY-1860: Imagination is a collective political field

## The account

Benjamin argues that "Imagination is a field of struggle", rationed by institutions and confined by "the tyranny of dominant imaginaries".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1923](../notes.d/NOTE-1923.md).

## What it does not say

Anything about technology.
