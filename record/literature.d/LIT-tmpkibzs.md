# LIT-tmpkibzs: Structural Anthropology

Claude Lévi-Strauss (1963), *Structural Anthropology*, trans. Claire Jacobson
and Brooke Grundfest Schoepf, New York: Basic Books, 410 pp.; French original
*Anthropologie structurale*, Paris: Plon, 1958 — https://lccn.loc.gov/63017344

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for: the
essays apply the method of structural (Saussurean and Prague-school)
linguistics to kinship, myth and social organisation, treating them as systems
whose terms have meaning only in their relations and oppositions. "The
Structural Study of Myth" analyses a myth as the set of all its variants,
organised by oppositions and their mediation.
