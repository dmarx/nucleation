# LIT-tmppxwo6: Review of Lisa Tessman, Burdened Virtues: Virtue Ethics for Liberatory Struggles

Bell, Macalester (2006). Review of Lisa Tessman, *Burdened Virtues: Virtue Ethics for Liberatory Struggles* (Oxford University Press, 2005). *Notre Dame Philosophical Reviews* 2006.06.10. https://ndpr.nd.edu/reviews/burdened-virtues-virtue-ethics-for-liberatory-struggles/

## Key takeaways

Tessman's burdened virtues are traits that enable survival of or resistance to oppression while detracting from their bearer's well-being; oppositional anger is the worked case, to be held with regret. Bell doubts the taxonomy separates virtue from vice.
