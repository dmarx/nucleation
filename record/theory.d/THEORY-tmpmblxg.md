# THEORY-tmpmblxg: An agent is the set of parts that pursue a goal together, bounded by the farthest events it can sense and try to regulate; cells joined by bioelectric coupling share measurements and so form larger agents with larger goals without ceasing to be agents, so a living system is many nested agents, not one, and cancer is the larger agent shrinking back to the cell

## Source

Michael Levin (2019), "The Computational Boundary of a 'Self': Developmental
Bioelectricity Drives Multicellularity and Scale-Free Cognition", [LIT-439](../literature.d/LIT-439.md),
*Frontiers in Psychology* 10: 2688, read in full in [NOTE-341](../notes.d/NOTE-341.md). The cited
experiments were not checked against their sources.

## What was actually shown

**The definition.** "The scale and types of goals that a system can pursue
defines (determines) the boundaries and content of the putative 'agent'." The
boundary is the "cognitive light cone": "the most distant (in time and space)
set of events that this system can measure and attempts to regulate". A goal is
the set point of "a feedback system that operates to maximize some specific
state of affairs". Fig. 2 places a tick, a dog and a human on a time × space
diagram, called "semi-quantitative".

**Nesting.** "Individuals can overlap – the same biophysical system can support
a number of coexisting, coupled Selves with different cognitive borders."
Swarms, animals, organs, cells and intracellular networks "each have their own
cognitive horizon". "There is not necessarily one unique primary level of
organization"; the level to describe is the one giving prediction and control
"with the least effort".

**Binding by shared information.** Cells coupled by gap junctions share
measurements, so the coupled group can sense and regulate what no cell can see,
such as the length of a limb. The cells stay "equally selfish", with "the
relevant self that they defend … bigger in terms of space, time, and
complexity". An inner agent keeps its own Self "only to the extent that it
regulates (restricts) information flow from neighbors". Too little sharing fails
to bind, and too much gives "a homogenous soup".

**Cited evidence.** Left–right identity is randomized by whole domains, not cell
by cell. Depolarizing a specific cell population makes normal melanocytes
metastatic in genetically normal tadpoles, and hyperpolarization prevents
tumours from human oncogenes. Plastic barriers and gap-junction blockers induce
tumours. Most anaesthetics are gap-junctional uncouplers, offered as "no
coincidence".

**What could have come out otherwise.** Changing bioelectric coupling could have
left pattern and tumour outcomes unchanged. In the cited experiments it did not.

## What this does not say

- **That the boundary can be measured.** The light cone has no units or
  procedure, and "much more work is needed to fully flesh out this rubric". It
  cannot yet decide a colony, a holobiont or a nation.
- **That a goal-pursuing whole is anything more than a homeostat.** Goals are
  ascribed: "It is of course non-trivial empirical work to ascertain goals." On
  this definition any homeostat is a Self, which the paper accepts.
- **That the experiments show selves.** They show that voltage and coupling
  change pattern and tumour outcomes. That they change the boundary of a Self is
  the paper's interpretation.
- **That IIT is wrong.** The contrast is one bracket aimed at Tononi 2008. Levin's
  nesting is about goal pursuit; IIT's exclusion is about experience. The two
  are not yet in contradiction.
- **That tissues or groups experience anything.** "Nothing in this model
  explains" the Hard Problem. The suggestion that there is "something-it-is-like
  to being a tissue or organ" is a closing speculation.
- **How the larger agents evolved.** The infotaxis and surprise-minimization
  scenario is not modelled or tested.
