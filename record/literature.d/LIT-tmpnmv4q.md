## Why this is here

The owner's exchange of 2026-10-21, "Experience and interpretation", asked whether experience is constituted by interpretation or produced by it. The exchange cited this study for its finding across four species that integration breaks down under anaesthesia, and that thalamic stimulation reverses the breakdown in macaques. It measures integration, not the constitution of experience. The record holds the same group's synergistic workspace paper, [LIT-949](LIT-949.md).
