**[CLAIM-004](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-004.md) was not really a premise.** [CLAIM-143](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-143.md) and [CLAIM-151](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) both rested
on [CLAIM-004](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-004.md), but each cites it only as where its own distinction came from.
That is an attribution, and an attribution takes no edge ([ADR-058](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-058.md)). Neither
needs the criterion that [CLAIM-004](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-004.md) still lacks. Dropping the two edges freed
[CLAIM-143](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-143.md). [CLAIM-151](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-151.md) stays held for a different reason, [CLAIM-221](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-221.md)'s residue
for reproach, so [CLAIM-137](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-137.md) and [CLAIM-217](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-217.md), which really do rest on it, stay
held too. [CASE-056](../../record/cases.d/CASE-056.md) now gives [CLAIM-004](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-004.md) the documented first pair the fifth
review asked for. What is still missing is the criterion itself.

  it. Objections are now read from the objector's side too.

  Proposed. Such a claim now waits on what its arguments wait on.
