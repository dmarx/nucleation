# SYNTHESIS-037: An agent made of a population of interests can act as one while they stay divided: synchronization of its interests is desirable and not necessary, the routes by which it acts as one (arbitration, a change in which interest may act, coinciding valuations, local accommodation) are distinct and differently costly, and whether its coordination switches or reorganizes continuously depends on how it is constituted

*A part account of the will-organization line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)); the line's account is built over it.*

## The account

**The question.** [QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md) (the owner's prompt of 2026-10-11) refines the
paper's [QUESTION-015](../questions.d/QUESTION-015.md) for an agent the record reads as a population: does it
act as one by bargaining, by a change in which interest may act, or by
agreement? [QUESTION-090](../questions.d/QUESTION-090.md) (A4's, distributed-agency first) refines it for a
population that allocates authority to its own members.

**Where the population comes from.** [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) (the record's reading of
Ainslie, [THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md); Proposed) bears on [QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md): an agent is many
interests over a choice "only as far as its valuations of that choice
cross within the choice's horizon". [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md) (Active) defines
intrapersonal synchronization on that basis, and [CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md) (Proposed),
resting on [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) and [CLAIM-197](../claims.d/CLAIM-197.md), adds that preference reversal is not yet
disunity: division needs an overlap, which self-prediction creates.

**Three routes, two answers filed.**

- *Agreement.* [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) (Proposed) answers [QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md), resting on
  [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md): in flow nothing has to be overridden, which is why it is
  reported as effortless. It was narrowed on 2026-10-28 to concede [ARG-016](../arguments.d/ARG-016.md)
  (Proposed), the argument from [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) to [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) into which [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)'s
  objection moved ([CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md), Superseded): the word "synchronization"
  carries no dynamical mechanism here.
- *A change in which interest may act.* [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md) (Proposed) answers
  [QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md), resting on [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md) and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md): mindfulness suspends
  the step from an impulse's dominance to its control of action, in
  [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md)'s terms. It corroborates [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md) of the part on maintaining
  the capacity to govern.
- *Bargaining* has no answer filed; [CLAIM-169](../claims.d/CLAIM-169.md)'s defeater names its
  signatures.

**Acting as one without synchronization.** [QUESTION-078](../questions.d/QUESTION-078.md) (an interlocutor's
reading relayed by the owner) refines [QUESTION-034](../questions.d/QUESTION-034.md); [QUESTION-304](../questions.d/QUESTION-304.md) was split
from it and is Answered by [CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md) (the owner's, Active): synchronization
is "desirable, but not necessary for acting as one". [CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md) also bears
on [QUESTION-078](../questions.d/QUESTION-078.md), which keeps whether differentiated interests can keep
distinct roles in a stable pattern. [QUESTION-077](../questions.d/QUESTION-077.md) (the assistant's, from
[CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md)) refines [QUESTION-078](../questions.d/QUESTION-078.md): is agreement by coupling a third route?

**Practical synchronization and its cost.** [TERM-061](../terms.d/TERM-061.md) (Active), defined from
A45 at the owner's request, is a second dimension and not a generalization
of [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md): regimes sustaining compatible courses through their own
interactions, without recurrent arbitration, whether or not they rank the
same course first. [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) (the owner's U45, Proposed) is stated in it
and rests on [CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md): learned local accommodation can cost less over
repeated encounters than escalating each instance. [CASE-109](../cases.d/CASE-109.md) (the owner's)
illustrates it, a variant of [CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md) and [CASE-051](../cases.d/CASE-051.md). [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) bears on
[QUESTION-054](../questions.d/QUESTION-054.md), whether there is a governing level among regimes at all.
[QUESTION-365](../questions.d/QUESTION-365.md) asks for a third settlement, a demand taken up by another
organization; it names [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) and [CLAIM-484](../claims.d/CLAIM-484.md).

**Non-domination.** [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md) (the owner's U45; authenticity first;
Proposed) complements [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md): non-domination of subcomponents is a
candidate constituent of authenticity. [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md) (A45's counter;
authenticity first; Proposed) replies that processes conditioned never to
object coordinate at the same behaviour and cost, so the two part only
under intervention, dissent or history. [TERM-061](../terms.d/TERM-061.md) records the consequence
for itself: conditioned conformity "can count as practical
synchronization" and fail [CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)'s condition.

**Effortlessness and affect.** [CLAIM-541](../claims.d/CLAIM-541.md) (the owner's U17; Proposed)
complements [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) and [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md): reported effortlessness has three
sources, no conflict to override, conflict settled by learned
accommodation, and conflict met by small, timed control. [CLAIM-441](../claims.d/CLAIM-441.md) (the
assistant's; organizational-affect first; Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) and
objects to [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) of that line: flow's positivity sits badly with
valence undefined where nothing is contested. [CASE-051](../cases.d/CASE-051.md), the absorbed
writer, illustrates [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) and [CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md).

**Switching or continuous reorganization.** [QUESTION-079](../questions.d/QUESTION-079.md) asks it, and
[CLAIM-262](../claims.d/CLAIM-262.md) (the owner's answer, Proposed) answers that it depends on the
agent's constitution, with [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)'s transition test stated per
constitution. [CASE-075](../cases.d/CASE-075.md) and [CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md) (Twitch Plays Pokémon in anarchy and
democracy mode) illustrate it; [CLAIM-453](../claims.d/CLAIM-453.md) (the owner's hypothesis,
demonstrated, Active; distributed-agency first) corroborates it with the
conditions under which a two-interpretation population decides by drift
diffusion. [CLAIM-616](../claims.d/CLAIM-616.md) (the owner's U4; distributed-agency first; Proposed)
proposes synchronization phenomena as a laboratory for the record's
distinctions; it has no front-matter relation to any component here.
