# LIT-tmpokvfz: Epistemic welfare and algorithmic recommender systems: overcoming the epistemic crisis in the digitalized public sphere

Hyzen, A., Van den Bulck, H., Puppis, M., Kulig, M. & Paulussen, S. (2026). Epistemic welfare and algorithmic recommender systems: overcoming the epistemic crisis in the digitalized public sphere. *Communication Theory* 36(1): 46–57. doi:10.1093/ct/qtaf018.

Skimmed 2026-10-21 (NOTE-tmp4sv55), through a fetch tool; quotes unchecked.

## Key takeaways

- **Epistemic welfare**: "creating and maintaining the conditions and
  capabilities for individuals' epistemic agency in the public sphere".
- **Stages of capability.** Literacies "turn availability into access",
  and critical skills "turn access into knowledge acquisition".
- **Transparency is not enough.** Transparency duties, which are all the
  EU AI Act asks of media recommenders, are judged insufficient.

## Why this is here
