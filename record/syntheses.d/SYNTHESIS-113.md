# SYNTHESIS-113: A capacity kept for rare use decays with disuse and is shown only by a retention test or the contingency it serves, a correction recorded is not a capacity maintained, support that does work a capacity is learned from can erode it, and where a capacity's maintenance comes from is shown only by intervention on its production

*A thematic account, across lines, of principle P7 of the owner's manuscript (scratch/manuscripts/from-obstruction-to-reorganization/, §7.1, on its own branch). Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which [ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**Disuse.** [CLAIM-558](../claims.d/CLAIM-558.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis): "A procedural capability that operation seldom calls on decays with the time since its last use". A capability kept for rare events "is maintained by practice outside operation, not by operation". Its loss is not observed until a retention test or a real contingency. [TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md) (Active) defines readiness for a rare contingency "under intervention, so a record of what was done does not measure it".

**Correction is not capacity.** [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis): "A successful corrective act, an executable procedure and a maintained organizational capacity are three different things". [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) (Active, documented) is Challenger to Columbia, glossed "A correction recorded is not a capacity maintained."

**Support that does the work.** These hold where capacity is learned by doing and decays with disuse:

- Outside supply that does the work first "lowers the capacity kept", while supply covering only the shortfall leaves learning untouched ([CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), Active, demonstrated).
- Where capacity is needed to grow capacity, such support has a deadline after which withdrawal no longer restores capacity ([CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md), Active, demonstrated).
- Supports equal now can leave different capabilities later ([CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md), Active, demonstrated).
- An arrangement that does the work through which a collective governs it "can erode the capacity to govern it" ([CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md), Active, interpretive).

**Passive agreement hides differences.** [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) (Active, demonstrated): in two finite constructions, matched ordinary trajectories conceal differences in efficacy, its allocation and its maintenance, which a specified intervention exposes.

**Where maintenance comes from.** [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) (Active, demonstrated): recovery under interventions on state cannot show where maintenance is realized; only interventions on the production mechanisms can. [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md) (Active) separates formation, maintenance and source provenance.

## Not stated on the record

- **Maintenance is known only by intervention,** for capacities in general. The manuscript's P7 stated this. [CLAIM-558](../claims.d/CLAIM-558.md) and [TERM-115](../terms.d/TERM-115.md) hold it for capacities that operation seldom calls on. A capacity exercised daily is shown by operation.
- **That whether a capacity is maintained and where its maintenance comes from are one question.** [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) is about the second.
