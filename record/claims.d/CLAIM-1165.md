# CLAIM-1165: A collective identity may be a historically sustained organization of overlapping perspectives whose interactions constitute an evolving question–answer game, residing not in its members, questions, answers or rules but in the maintained organization of the game, including the means by which its own rules are revised, so that it is a slow mode of the game, and a collective that reproduces the capacity to revise is adaptive where one stable by suppressing alternatives is not

## The claim

A64b's core formulation: "A collective identity is a historically sustained organization of partially overlapping perspectives whose interactions constitute an evolving game of questioning, answering, evaluation, and revision." "The collective persists not because its constituents, questions, answers, or governing rules remain fixed, but because the relationships through which these activities become mutually consequential are maintained and reproduced." So "collective identity might be an emergent property of the organization of inquiry, rather than a preexisting entity that subsequently engages in inquiry."

Its steps:

1. **The game makes the plurality an organization (§2).** With the collective's state S_t = (μ_t, G_t, M_t, C_t), dispositions, governance, memory and capacities, a query and its answer update the state, S_{t+1} = F(S_t, q_t, a_t, e_t): "answering a question changes the organization that will answer subsequent questions". Who may ask, investigate and judge is part of the state, so the game is reflexive in [TERM-095](../terms.d/TERM-095.md)'s sense.
2. **Where identity resides (§4).** "The historically maintained organization of the question–answer game, including the mechanisms through which its own rules can be revised." Not small distance between response kernels: "Two independently formed organizations might have identical response kernels without being the same historically continuous organization", and a collective can change its dispositions much and continue. Hence identity needs "historical organizational continuity" and "maintained, revisable causal organization"; "The first distinguishes continuation from duplication."
3. **Identity as a slow mode (§7).** Questions and answers change fast, perspectival relations more slowly, legibility and authority regimes more slowly still, so "the collective identity may be modeled as a slowly maintained organization emerging from faster question–answer interactions". Two collectives can both have slow modes: one "continually reproduces the same questions, answers, and authority relations. It is stable because alternatives are suppressed"; the other reproduces the capacity to generate new questions and revise authority. "Only the second realizes the richer kind of adaptive governance we've been developing", an application, A64b says, of [CLAIM-099](CLAIM-099.md).
4. **Governance decides whether the mess stays productive (§5).** A collective that routes every consequential inquiry through one interpretive regime "can make the collective behave as though it possesses only one perspective". The variable is "perspectival consequentiality": "Under what circumstances can a perspective change what the collective asks, answers, does, or treats as authoritative?" That is [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md)'s separation of admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and a part in revising the standards.

## Where the record already held it

- Step 2's two components are [CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md)'s historical structuralism, filed 2026-11-07 from another exchange: an individual's identity is carried by "the causal transmission of the relations that keep its repertoire realizable", "two structurally identical copies can be two individuals", and the structure can change while the individual continues. A64b reaches it without citing it. What A64b adds is which relations: those of the question–answer game, rule revision included. A dated note on [CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md) records this.
- Step 3 is [CLAIM-1053](CLAIM-1053.md) (the slow mode of a coupled standard and response belongs to neither alone) and [CLAIM-1066](CLAIM-1066.md) (identity in slow modes, adaptability in their controllability), joined.
- Step 4 is [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md) and, for the cost of being heard, [CLAIM-1132](CLAIM-1132.md) and [CLAIM-1119](CLAIM-1119.md).
- §1's "sloppy" decomposition is [CLAIM-1044](CLAIM-1044.md) and, for teams, [CLAIM-1039](CLAIM-1039.md) (one person in several perspectives, several people reproducing one), with [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md) for the boundaries.

## What it does not say

That a collective identity is a subject of experience: A64b keeps [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s organizational and phenomenal senses apart. Nor that every collective playing a question–answer game has an identity: which do is [CLAIM-1048](CLAIM-1048.md)'s criteria and [QUESTION-521](../questions.d/QUESTION-521.md)'s open question.
