# LIT-983: Impaired context disambiguation as a key determinant of post-traumatic psychopathology: A neurocomputational account

Adam Linson, Heidi Anderson, Sarah Markham, Karl Friston and Michael Moutoussis (2025), *bioRxiv* preprint.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp75pvf](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp75pvf.md) (version 2).*

- **The maintaining link is a choice of what to sample.** A harmful and a
  harmless context share an ambiguous cue and differ only in the
  periphery. With a precise harmful-context prior, exploration is
  "falsely believed to be uninformative". The agent attenuates the
  periphery, and "no amount of observations will render prior beliefs
  obsolete".
- **Vulnerability is a learning rate.** Resilient and vulnerable agents
  differ in "the specific weights of learning and forgetting". After the
  same trauma, one rebalances and the other collapses into a single
  basin. Parameter values are not given.
- **Exposure against a drug.** Sixty harmless-only trials restore
  exploration and the priors rebalance durably. Lowering likelihood and
  transition precision, standing in for SSRIs or alcohol, suppresses
  symptoms without rebalancing, and they return when precision is
  restored. The arms also differ in environment (controlled against
  random).
- **The rest is narrative.** A pontine acetylcholine–noradrenaline loop
  under a serotonergic "brake", and flashbacks as top-down context in
  visual working memory, are proposed from the literature and not
  modelled.
- **Stated tests.** Cued recall should attenuate peripheral more than focal
  sensing. Context should modulate startle less in PTSD. Active
  inference and dual representation theory predict opposite weights on
  trauma-encoded context.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md), and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-tmp75pvf](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp75pvf.md), in version 2. It is what the filing said: an
individual-scale model of a regime kept stable by behaviour that blocks
its own disconfirmation, with a lost capacity to leave. It does model the
maintaining link and not only the content. The drug arm removes the
symptoms but leaves the link, and relapses. But it is a model. The
intervention arms are not matched on exposure, so it does not run the
comparison [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md) asks for. [THEORY-tmpta57o](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpta57o.md) holds the mechanism at
simulation strength. It gives [THEORY-058](../theory.d/THEORY-058.md)'s loop a mechanism, and its drug
arm is an instance of [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md)'s outcome-without-process. Version 3
should be read when bioRxiv can be reached, to check that the model and
