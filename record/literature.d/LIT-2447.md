# LIT-2447: Categorization of Loot Box Design: A Scoping Review of Problematic Features

Jan Ivern and Xavier Carbonell (2026). "Categorization of Loot Box Design: A Scoping Review of Problematic Features." *Current Addiction Reports* 13, article 64. DOI-10.1007/s40429-026-00768-6. Open access, CC BY 4.0.

## Key takeaways

- "These studies revealed 136 distinct loot box design features organized
  into 22 subcategories across six broader dimensions" (abstract).
- "Significant research gaps were identified, especially regarding the
  impact and interaction of these features" (abstract).
- Disclosed odds "may not consistently reflect the actual chances players
  face" where odds vary by account or behaviour (Table 2, p. 13).
