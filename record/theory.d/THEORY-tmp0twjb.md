# THEORY-tmp0twjb: Xie et al. (2026) report that on 300 tasks pairing paper-derived mechanisms with real datasets, AI agents' open-form mechanistic hypotheses predict the direction of responses under withheld conditions well below reference statements of the true mechanism

## The account

Xie et al. (2026) report that on 300 tasks pairing paper-derived mechanisms with real datasets, AI agents' open-form mechanistic hypotheses predict the direction of responses under withheld conditions well below reference statements of the true mechanism. AI-scientist workflows do not beat a general agent on the same model. Giving agents summaries of the evidence raises agreement by 10 points, while telling them to keep structured records of their findings raises it by about 2.
