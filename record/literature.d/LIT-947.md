# LIT-947: Unravelling the Flow of Information in a Nonequilibrium Process in the Presence of Hydrodynamic Interactions

Biswajit Das, Sreekanth K. Manikandan and Ayan Banerjee (2025), *Physical Review Applied* 24, 034039.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-788](../notes.d/NOTE-788.md).*

- **A known causal structure.** The coupling is symmetric, H₁₂ = H₂₁ =
  ε/γ, with ε = 3a/(2d) − (a/d)³. Only trap 1 is driven, and the drive λ
  is recorded.
- **The measures recover the order.** At θ = 0.56 and ε = 0.49, in
  experiment and numerics, MI_{λ→x₁}(τ) > MI_{x₁→λ}(τ) and
  MI_{x₁→x₂}(τ) > MI_{x₂→x₁}(τ). Transfer entropy agrees, with
  TE_{λ→x₁} ≫ TE_{x₁→λ}.
- **But the flow between the particles is two-way at moderate drive.**
  The reverse measures also peak (λ → x₁ ⇄ x₂). Only at θ = 5.6, in
  simulation, does the flow become one-way.
- **Coupling redistributes information.** A larger ε lowers MI_{λx₁} and
  raises MI_{x₁x₂} and MI_{λx₂}. The last two are too small to measure in
  the experiment's regime and are given from numerics.
- **The irreversibility "explanation" is a parallel of trends.** TE
  asymmetry and partial entropy production vary alike with θ and ε. No
  derivation links them.
- **With no drive, the coupled particles are independent.**
  P(x₁, x₂) = P(x₁)P(x₂), and the delayed measures are symmetric.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-063](../questions.d/QUESTION-063.md) and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-788](../notes.d/NOTE-788.md). The registration said its measures "tell an external drive
from coupling". They do not. The drive is applied to one particle and
recorded, so the measures locate where it enters. No unrecorded drive
shared by both particles, the case [QUESTION-063](../questions.d/QUESTION-063.md) is about, is studied.
What the paper does establish bears on the question as a limit. The
direction the measures report follows the drive and its strength, while
the coupling stays symmetric. That is filed as [THEORY-275](../theory.d/THEORY-275.md), which
answers [QUESTION-063](../questions.d/QUESTION-063.md) in part.

It also qualifies [CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md) and [CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md). A mutually coupled pair reads
as a "hierarchical interaction" once one member is driven hard, so
observational flow cannot tell peers from hierarchy without an
intervention on a member.
