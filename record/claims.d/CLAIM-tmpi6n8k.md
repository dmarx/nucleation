# CLAIM-tmpi6n8k: The record's iterated-learning readings bear on lexical, structural and functional transmission, and none measures stance, footing or speech-act function along a chain

## The objection

[CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md) holds that "pragmatic stances, not only lexicon and grammar, can
be stabilized by transmission", and quotes A52 §7.5 extending "iterated
learning from lexical and grammatical structure to speaker footing and
communicative conventions". Its defeat condition is about stances: chains
"started from different stances do not converge toward a small set of
conventional stances".

What the readings it and [CLAIM-066](CLAIM-066.md) draw on measure:

- **Griffiths and Kalish** ([THEORY-155](../theory.d/THEORY-155.md)): a theorem about learners who share
  a prior over languages; sampling learners converge to the prior. No
  measurement.
- **Kirby, Cornish and Smith** ([LIT-771](../literature.d/LIT-771.md)): an artificial 27-item lexicon,
  whose compositional structure appeared only under the experimenter's
  homonym filter ([CLAIM-066](CLAIM-066.md)).
- **Kalish, Griffiths and Lewandowsky** ([LIT-820](../literature.d/LIT-820.md)): function learning, 32
  chains converging mostly to a positive linear function.
- **Raviv, Meyer and Lev-Ari** ([LIT-825](../literature.d/LIT-825.md), [THEORY-164](../theory.d/THEORY-164.md)): the systematicity of
  invented languages in groups of four and eight. [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md): "This is
  horizontal interaction, not a chain."
- **Propp** ([THEORY-167](../theory.d/THEORY-167.md)): the structure of a corpus of tales. [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md):
  "not a measurement of retellings".
- **Farrell, Gopnik, Shalizi and Evans** ([THEORY-173](../theory.d/THEORY-173.md)): a mechanism stated
  and not tested. [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md): "so it is weak support".

None measures an interpersonal stance, a footing or a speech-act function
along a chain. The extension to footing has no study under it. Bartlett's
*Remembering* ([LIT-768](../literature.d/LIT-768.md)), the classic serial-reproduction study, is held
Deferred and unread. The transmission-chain work on social and affective
content is absent: a search of the record on 2026-10-10 for Mesoudi,
Bebbington, Stubbersfield, Breithaupt, Moussaïd and "negativity bias" finds
nothing. That work studies which kinds of content survive chains (social
information, threat, affect against fact), which bears directly on whether
complicity or moralizing is the attractor in [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md)'s running case. No
check has been made whether the Anthology of the SOTA holds any of it.

[CLAIM-066](CLAIM-066.md) already separates what the three iterated-learning citations
show, and says which learner model applies decides "whether the endpoint
reflects the learners or the transmission filter". It does not say that
none of them bears on stance.

## What it does not say

It does not say that stances are not stabilized by transmission, nor that
[CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md) is false. It says the record holds no evidence for the stance
version of the claim, and names the literature that would be its
evidence. It is minor for the argument: [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md) feeds [CLAIM-059](CLAIM-059.md)'s
regimes, not the central theses [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) and [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md). It is also a
reading-list gap for the owner, to be checked against the anthology before
anything is filed here.
