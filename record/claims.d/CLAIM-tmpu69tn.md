# CLAIM-tmpu69tn: Where an influence on an organism came from, what governs its practical efficacy, and whose interests its effects serve are three separate questions, so neither an origin outside the organism nor one inside it settles whether the influence belongs to the organism's self-governance: authorized external regulation, parasitic capture, cancer arising from the host's own cells and foreign-origin cells serving the host realize the combinations

## The claim

A21's table:

| Dimension | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Provenance | where the governing influence originated, and through what history it became effective |
| Control | whether it changes inputs, evaluative standards, execution, or the capacity to revise those |
| Alignment | whether it serves the organization's maintenance or another system's interests |

and its candidate thesis: "The location and provenance of an influence do
not determine whether an organization is self-governing. What matters
additionally is how the influence acquires practical efficacy, whether it
changes the organization's capacity to evaluate and revise its own
regulation, and how its effects relate to independently specified
interests or normative standards." A adds that "independently specified"
is essential: "We shouldn't define a host's interests simply as whatever its
current regulatory organization produces; that would make parasitic capture
indistinguishable from authentic preference change by definition."

The combinations, as A21 reports them:

- outside, authorized, serving the person: adaptive deep brain stimulation
  ([LIT-tmp6oofp](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6oofp.md)), where the device may act without the patient noticing;
- outside, unauthorized, serving another: the parasite ([CASE-tmp10ykm](../cases.d/CASE-tmp10ykm.md));
- inside by lineage, working against the organism: cancer that recruits and
  remodels neural inputs ([LIT-tmpj31gs](../literature.d/LIT-tmpj31gs.md));
- outside by lineage, serving the host: maternal cells that sustain the
  offspring's tolerance ([THEORY-tmp6t2l2](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp6t2l2.md)).

A21 adds two that cut further: wasps whose domesticated viral genes, once a
separate lineage, now let them alter a third organism ([LIT-tmpe26cm](../literature.d/LIT-tmpe26cm.md)), and
Wolbachia, which changes which reproductive futures an insect has rather
than its choices ([LIT-tmpzogx3](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzogx3.md)).

## Where it sits

- [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md) already separates formation provenance from maintenance
  provenance; this claim adds that neither fixes alignment.
- [CLAIM-060](CLAIM-060.md) (an organization's success does not establish its constituents'
  well-being, and conversely) is the alignment question at one level.
- [CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md) is the control question for organizations; [CLAIM-tmpoxhqq](CLAIM-tmpoxhqq.md)
  carries it to organisms.
- [CLAIM-530](CLAIM-530.md) (whether maintenance is realized inside depends on the parthood
  clause) is the boundary of the individual; this claim says the boundary
  of governance is a different line.

## What it does not say

That authorization makes external regulation self-governance: A21 asks it
as a question, "Under what conditions can externally implemented
regulation be part of genuine self-government?" Or how interests are
"independently specified", which is [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md)'s problem.

## Note of 2026-11-12: UNESCO General Conference (2025), read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpwjlut](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpwjlut.md) ([LIT-tmpxuh7j](../literature.d/LIT-tmpxuh7j.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: **bears on it, partly supports**. The Recommendation
does not treat external origin as disqualifying (it endorses medical
closed-loop stimulation) and makes legitimacy turn on consent and on "undue
influence or manipulation" — an authorization criterion, which the claim's
"What it does not say" leaves as a question. It thus supplies one
institutional answer to that question without arguing it. It does not
separate alignment from authorization.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Brenninger, Zug & Kokko (2025), read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmptfr59](../notes.d/NOTE-tmptfr59.md) ([LIT-tmpzogx3](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzogx3.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: **supports A21's use** of Wolbachia: the manipulation
acts on which offspring are produced or survive, not on the host's choices.
It also supplies two cases the claim's table lacks, from the review's §IX:
foreign-origin material that has become part of the host's own sex
determination (Armadillidium), and a host that now depends on the symbiont
for female development (Eurema). In these, origin outside, integration as
host machinery and mixed alignment come apart, which fits the claim.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Neuron–tumor crosstalk in cancer: molecular mechanisms and translational advances (Jia, Zhao, Tang, Xie, Huang, Liang, Yang, Wu, Sun, Kwan, Sun, Xu 2026), read

From the reading in [NOTE-tmpdrocb](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpdrocb.md) ([LIT-tmpj31gs](../literature.d/LIT-tmpj31gs.md)), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: supports as an illustration. The claim's example
"inside by lineage, working against the organism" cites this LIT, and the
review's frame fits it: cells of the host's own lineage co-opt the host's
nervous system against the host. It is a review, not a test of the claim's
three-way separation.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Schepanski et al., read

From the reading in NOTE-tmpe8k6k (LIT-tmpqn7ic), the reader's assessment as
checked by the filer: supports the foreign-origin-serving-host instance
directly and with causal, rescue-controlled evidence. MMc are another
organism's cells that sustain a capacity (healthy neurodevelopment) of the
host. This is a stronger case than a review for that cell of the claim's
matrix.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Castela and others (2017), read

From the reading in NOTE-tmpx8y0m (LIT-tmpyfxly), read as a stand-in for
LIT-tmps2df6: in mice, fetal cells that persist in the mother are recruited
by CCL2 to injured maternal skin and repair it, and the effect needs both
the fetal cells (none in virgin mice) and their CCR2 receptor. That is a
controlled case of the combination this claim lists as "outside by lineage,
serving the host": cells of another individual's lineage doing the host's
maintenance work. It supports the claim's point that lineage and service
come apart; it says nothing about authorization. No status change.
