# CASE-tmpp4tri: Verdant Compute

## The case

"Its workforce consists primarily of technically sophisticated people who
strongly favor decarbonization ... Its survival and expansion depend on
supplying that capacity at commercially viable prices." (A31)

## What it can show

Fictional, so it shows coherence, not frequency: that a firm's operative
direction *can* diverge from what its members want. It cannot show that real
firms do, which is why it illustrates [CLAIM-tmp9myih](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9myih.md) rather than being a
premise of it.
