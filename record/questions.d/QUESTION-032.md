# QUESTION-032: Relative to a level of description and a target, what makes a factor of a system an agent, and what further makes it a willed agent?

## Why it is a question

The will paper set this question aside on purpose. It supposed its agent
([CLAIM-080](../claims.d/CLAIM-080.md)) so as not to face the permissiveness objection, that every
control system has a will ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)). This session has built the
materials an answer needs:
- **which blocks are one system** ([CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md));
- **how drive is apportioned among them** ([TERM-051](../terms.d/TERM-051.md));
- **a way to price a description against its cost** ([CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md)).

The owner then proposed ([CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md)) that agents are a factorization
of a system at a level, and that each is individuated by its
will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)).

## What would count as an answer

A test, run on a candidate factor at a stated level and for a stated
target, that admits some factors as agents and refuses others, and could
be wrong. It must:
- **refuse a thermostat** ([CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md)) or explain why it should pass;
- **say whether a dancing couple, a firm and a microbiome are agents,** and
  at which levels;
- **avoid presupposing the agent it is meant to identify**
  ([CLAIM-168](../claims.d/CLAIM-168.md)).

The proposed answer is [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md).

## Note of 2026-10-12: a typed partial answer

[THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md) now carries an `answered_by` edge. Its criteria (individuality, interactional asymmetry, self-generated normativity) refuse a thermostat. They are not relative to level or target, which is the tension the counter [CLAIM-194](../claims.d/CLAIM-194.md) takes up.

## Note of 2026-10-12: a permissive answer

[THEORY-208](../theory.d/THEORY-208.md) (Floridi) now carries an `answered_by` edge. Interactivity is necessary for agency, and autonomy and adaptability are graded, which is close to [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md)'s "identified by adaptation". It is permissive: thermostats and rivers pass, by splitting agency into kinds. It is not relative to level or target, and it gives no test that could fail.

## Note of 2026-10-14: a comparative criterion relative to a decomposition

[THEORY-tmphw05b](../theory.d/THEORY-tmphw05b.md) (Watson, [LIT-990](../literature.d/LIT-990.md)) makes a whole more agential than its
parts when its organization lets them resolve more of their mutual
constraints than they do at the equilibrium of their former organization.
It is relative to a chosen decomposition, as this question asks, and it
gives the goal-directedness test of [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md) a behavioural mark in
delayed gratification. It does not supply the test asked for here: it
takes the parts' goals as given, so it does not say which factors are
agents, and it sets no threshold. It carries no `answers` edge.
