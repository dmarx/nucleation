# CLAIM-tmpy1gmq: Of a fixed partition, one recovered from the dynamics in force and one that depends on an apparatus, the first two make different counterfactual predictions whenever an intervention moves the coupling; the apparatus-dependent partition differs from the recovered one only if it tracks the apparatus rather than the dynamics the apparatus produces, and so read it is refuted wherever the apparatus is in place without dominating the coupling, so that constitution by an apparatus, as distinct from causal modification followed by discovery, makes no prediction of its own that survives

## The claim

QUESTION-284 asks, after A5, whether "a fixed preexisting partition, a
partition recovered from measured dynamics, and an arrangement-dependent
partition produced through coupling to an apparatus" make different
counterfactual predictions, "and whether any evidence distinguishes
ontological constitution from ordinary causal modification." A partition
predicts nothing by itself. What it predicts is what it licenses; the
licence the record's block criterion uses is that each block can be
forecast from its own past. Under that licence, on the model of
CASE-tmp06fbt:

- **Fixed against recovered: different predictions, decided by
  measurement.** Whenever an intervention moves the coupling (the apparatus
  switched on, a lasting change it leaves, the units rewired), the fixed
  partition stays and the recovered one follows the dynamics, and the
  fixed one's blocks fail at block-local prediction (7.5% excess error
  against 0.1% after rewiring). A fixed partition that licenses nothing
  about the dynamics escapes the test and makes no prediction.
- **Apparatus-dependent against recovered: a dilemma.**
  - *If it tracks the apparatus* (the boundary is wherever the apparatus
    joins units, whatever the coupling does), it differs from the
    recovered one exactly where the arrangement and the dynamics it
    produces come apart: the apparatus in place at low or zero gain, or
    the change outlasting the apparatus. In the first it predicts worse
    (7.5% against 0.1%); in the second it is silent where the dynamics
    still show the boundary.
  - *If it tracks the dynamics the apparatus produces*, it coincides with
    the recovered partition of the altered dynamics in every scenario. A5's
    own "a measuring apparatus could alter a system's dynamics, and a
    subsequent analysis could correctly discover the boundaries in the
    altered system" then accounts for every observable. When the
    apparatus has states of its own, the recovered partition of system and
    apparatus puts the apparatus inside the unit, so even the
    arrangement's role in the boundary is discovered.
- **So the evidence that distinguishes them refutes the constitutive
  reading, and the reading that survives is predictively identical to
  causal modification.** Whether the boundary in the second horn is
  "ontologically" rather than "epistemically" dependent on the apparatus,
  A5's harder question, is not settled by any observable of the system,
  since the joint dynamics fix them all.

This is the outcome QUESTION-284 foresaw ("If none does, that is itself an
answer"), shown for dependence on an arrangement in one model, alongside
CLAIM-150's underdetermination of constitution by identification and
THEORY-1288's for part–whole constitution. It extends CLAIM-194 in the
other direction: the block test cannot tell a boundary a system makes from
one imposed on it, and no evidence of this kind tells a boundary an
apparatus constitutes from one it causes.

## What it does not say

- **That constitution is false**, or that the constitutive reading is
  idle as metaphysics. It says the reading has no counterfactual
  prediction of its own here; CLAIM-476's constitutive claim for a
  record's objects is not tested by it.
- **That every licence a partition gives is block-local prediction.** A
  partition read as licensing other predictions (which interventions are
  surgical, what persists under perturbation) would need its own test; the
  dilemma's form, tracking the arrangement or the dynamics it produces,
  is expected to carry over, but that is not shown.
- **Anything about the other two relations** called constitution in
  QUESTION-284's note of 2026-11-07: relations helping fix their relata's
  identity, and synchronic part–whole constitution (CLAIM-528).
- **That history cannot matter.** An account on which the boundary is
  constituted by having been coupled agrees with the recovered partition
  when the change outlasts the apparatus, and so falls in the second horn;
  a history-dependent account that differed from the present dynamics
  would face the first.
