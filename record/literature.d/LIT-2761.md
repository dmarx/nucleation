# LIT-2761: Property-Rights Regimes and Natural Resources: A Conceptual Analysis

Edella Schlager and Elinor Ostrom (1992). "Property-Rights Regimes and Natural Resources: A Conceptual Analysis". *Land Economics* 68(3): 249–262. DOI-10.2307/3146375.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-tmpr5h7q](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpr5h7q.md)).*

- "A property right is the authority to undertake particular actions
  related to a specific domain" (p. 250); rights are "the product of
  'rules' and thus not equivalent to rules".
- Access, withdrawal, management, exclusion, alienation; collective-choice
  rights are "the difference between exercising a right and participating
  in the definition of future rights to be exercised" (p. 251).
- "The five property rights are independent of one another" but usually
  held cumulatively (p. 252); "Constitutional-choice actions entail devising
  collective-choice rules" (n. 2).
