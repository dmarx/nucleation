# CLAIM-tmpjitli: Memes can express characteristic aspects of a perspective's relational organization independently of its realization and, through uptake, take part in nucleating and reconstituting perspectives in other carriers, so memetic fidelity can be the preservation of characteristic relations rather than the copying of content

## The claim

A5 §8, verbatim: "Memes can express characteristic aspects of the
relational organization of perspectives independently of their
particular realization. Through their transmission and uptake, these
structures can participate in the nucleation, reconstitution and
transformation of perspectives across carriers. Memetic fidelity may
therefore consist in the preservation of characteristic organizational
relations rather than the faithful reproduction of representational
content."

A5 §3 gives the cycle, P_A → m → P_B by "expression" and "uptake", with
success read as χ(P_A) ≈ χ(P_B) "even though the two realizations differ
substantially in their constituent processes", and: "**What is reproduced
is potentially an organizational invariant, rather than a particular
informational object.**" A5 §7's example: a philosophical school can
survive "while its vocabulary, institutions, adherents and explicit
doctrines change", and "a school could preserve all its canonical texts
while losing the perspective that originally made those texts
consequential", which is [CLAIM-420](CLAIM-420.md)'s and [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s separation of
transmitted perspective, descent and resemblance.

## What it rests on

- **The owner's U4**, read as (b): memes sample a character
  ([CLAIM-tmp4mk0t](CLAIM-tmp4mk0t.md)). A5 itself declines (a): "Strictly speaking, however, a
  meme would not ordinarily be a mathematical character."
- **What characters do and do not classify** ([CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md)), and **persistence
  by causal transmission of organization** ([CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)).
- It extends [CLAIM-137](CLAIM-137.md) (character-like invariants classify communicative
  kinds) from communicative kinds to perspectives, which [CLAIM-417](CLAIM-417.md) calls
  "new territory, with [CLAIM-249](CLAIM-249.md) standing against any easy
  identification".

## Gaps and standing

- **The group.** A5 §6 names it "The critical mathematical obstacle", and
  says that if the transformations are irreversible "A category or
  semigroup of transformations may ultimately be more appropriate than a
  group, in which case ordinary character theory loses much of its
  classificatory completeness." The record already holds how much is
  lost: equal characters give "at most isomorphic semisimplifications"
  ([CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md), its ℤ example). So A5 §5's "memeplex" as a decomposition
  χ_P = Σ n_i χ_i would record which modes occur and how often, and lose
  exactly how one mode depends on another, the part A5 assigns to
  "organizational dynamics and will-organization". The note on [CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md)
  of 2026-11-24 says so.
- **Probes.** A5 §4's response profiles are evidence of a character only
  relative to the probe family ([CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md)).
- **Standing.** A5's close, "This would situate memetics within
  Nucleation's non-eliminative structural realism", tells as the record's
  position what [CLAIM-136](CLAIM-136.md) holds as a Proposed thesis.

## Note of 2026-11-25: fidelity against convergence, from the manuscript governance-of-becoming, Article 7
<!-- inactive-ok-file: CLAIM-tmp590sw — Proposed; the owner's claim the note says fits the point -->

The manuscript's Article 7 (§7.2, join J7.1) reads this claim's measure of
success against the record's transport floor. A5 §3 reads success as
χ(P_A) ≈ χ(P_B): a source and a carrier that agree in their
characteristic relations. But [CLAIM-270](CLAIM-270.md) (Active, demonstrated) holds that
"convergence is not preservation", and [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md) (Active) that an
attractor "is where the chain goes whatever its start, so reaching it is
evidence of forgetting the start". So agreement between source and
carrier in characteristic relations is not, by itself, evidence that the
relations were transmitted: they may be where reconstruction tends,
whatever the source held. Evidence of fidelity needs the start to matter,
either different sources leading to different endpoints under the same
reconstruction, or a relation the chain conserves at every step, which by
[CLAIM-635](CLAIM-635.md) (Active, demonstrated) keeps information about the start at
every generation.

This claim's defeater compares transmitted memes with transmitted
propositions, not with convergence, so a test built on it could pass by
attraction alone. [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md)'s note of 2026-11-24 contrasts an attractor
with a nucleator, not fidelity with convergence. A test of the claim
should therefore vary the source and hold the reconstruction fixed;
[LEAD-tmpqucxc](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpqucxc.md), which would test the claim by transferring memes between
matched populations, needs such an arm. The owner's [CLAIM-tmp590sw](CLAIM-tmp590sw.md)
fits the point from the other side: on it, reproduction diversifies the
perspectives it touches rather than converging them.
