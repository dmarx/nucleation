# CLAIM-tmpner6t: An alternative: another party's standing channel into an agent is a fact about the agent's situation, not a relation of its will-organization, which is the organization the agent's own processes maintain, so no feature of will-organization differs between a manipulated will and its unmanipulated twin even while the manipulator keeps its channel, and the channel belongs with formation provenance among what a record must contain

## The claim

*An alternative, the one that argues none can. Its rival is
CLAIM-tmp9jo1z.*

TERM-023 says will-organization is "agent-indexed" and the *maintained*
organization of the relations that govern efficacy. Read strictly, the
relations are those the agent's own processes keep: CLAIM-027's
accessibility, precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and
transformation, each a relation among the agent's regimes. A channel
another party holds is not maintained by the agent and is not a relation
among its regimes. While unused and unknown to Plum, it changes none of
them: which regime prevails, how long, and how Plum would revise, are the
same as in his twin. CASE-tmpj29sq's variant 1 therefore differs from its
twin in Plum's situation and in nothing the account counts as his will.

On this reading, CLAIM-tmpz9dqf's conclusion extends to Pereboom's Case 1
as it stands, and CLAIM-tmpz9dqf's premise 1 counts couplings only as
inputs to the dynamics, not as relations of the organization. The
channel still matters. It goes into what a record must contain, beside
formation provenance: who held a channel into the agent's governing
relations, and when. That is the record of a situation, which CLAIM-545's
domination clause describes, and freedom in CLAIM-545's sense can then
differ between the two agents while will-organization does not.

## What it does not say

- **That the channel is harmless or irrelevant to freedom.** CLAIM-545
  counts it; this claim places it outside will-organization, not outside
  freedom.
- **That an agent who knows of the channel matches its twin.** Knowledge
  and adjustment, such as anticipatory compliance, are relations the agent
  maintains and can differ.
