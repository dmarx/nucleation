# LIT-tmp9g55n: A feature-integration theory of attention

Treisman, A. M. and Gelade, G. (1980). A feature-integration theory of attention. *Cognitive Psychology* 12(1), 97–136. DOI 10.1016/0010-0285(80)90005-5

## Key takeaways

Not read. Cited for the contrast between search by a single feature, nearly independent of how many items there are, and serial search, as the work is standardly described. Read it
before anything leans on it.

## Why this is here

The owner's LEGO parable ([CASE-tmpd5exw](../cases.d/CASE-tmpd5exw.md)): "if it's a single black brick in a box
of white bricks: ok, it's buried but its' still detectable." [CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md) turns
that into a rule for arranging a collection: sort by what does not stand
out within a bin, and search by what does.
