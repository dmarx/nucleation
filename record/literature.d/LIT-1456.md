# LIT-1456: Beyond Bipolar Conceptualizations and Measures: The Case of Attitudes and Evaluative Space

John T. Cacioppo, Wendi L. Gardner & Gary G. Berntson (1997),
*Personality and Social Psychology Review* 1(1):3–25 —
DOI-10.1207/s15327957pspr0101_2

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* The authors' 2010 review ([LIT-1438](LIT-1438.md))
names this paper and its 1999 sequel as the statement of the evaluative
space model (ESM). It reports these postulates from them:

- positivity and negativity are functionally separable;
- they can be activated reciprocally, independently, or together;
- coactivation is objective ambivalence, which is distinct from the
  neutral low-low state although both yield the same middling net
  predisposition;
- the activation functions are asymmetric (negativity bias, positivity
  offset).

The 2010 review also says this paper extended the person-positivity bias
to non-human targets, "including fish and insects".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-23 for the record's proposal that hedonic valence is
