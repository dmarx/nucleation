# ADR-004: philosophy-of-science joins the philosophy group

## Context

[ADR-003](ADR-003.md) held `philosophy-of-science` back: the survey had found one work for
it (the SEP entry on structural realism), and a word with one member is a
field every record but one leaves empty. Seeding the survey's 65 candidates
found more: the open-systems view of quantum theory, the PBR theorem and the
knowledge-and-reality question it prompted are arguments about how to
interpret a physical theory, not results in physics. The owner asked for the
word.

## Decision

Add `philosophy-of-science` to `topics`, with a label and a blurb, and to the
`philosophy` group's members (still `require: any`).

## Alternatives

- **Keep using `metaphysics` for these** — it was the nearest wrong tag. What
  structural realism is about is the relation between scientific theories and
  the world, which is philosophy of science; the metaphysics is a consequence.
- **Fold it into `quantum-foundations`** — the word is not specific to quantum
  theory, and structural realism is not.

## Consequences

Retagged: *Structural Realism* ([LIT-045](../literature.d/LIT-045.md)) takes it as its primary topic; *The
Open Systems View* ([LIT-037](../literature.d/LIT-037.md)), the PBR theorem ([LIT-062](../literature.d/LIT-062.md)) and *Could
wavefunctions simultaneously represent knowledge and reality?* ([LIT-090](../literature.d/LIT-090.md)) take
it as a secondary topic.
