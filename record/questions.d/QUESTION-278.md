# QUESTION-278: When several institutions can together produce an outcome, what relations among them must hold for a challenge to it to produce authorized, timely and effective correction, without their forming one collective agent?

## Why it is a question

A9: "Given institutions capable of jointly producing an outcome, what
independently identifiable relationships are necessary for challenges to
that outcome to produce authorized, timely, and effective correction,
without requiring the institutions to form a single collective agent?"

The record has the question inside one organization. [QUESTION-041](QUESTION-041.md) asks
which channels from members keep a whole's course correctable;
[CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md) holds that a corrective act, a procedure and a maintained
capacity are three things, on NASA ([CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md)); [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md) separates
admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and a part in revising the
standards. The cases of this exchange put it between institutions:

- At USIP ([CASE-176](../cases.d/CASE-176.md)) a court, the police, executive officials and the
  incumbents each held a different part of the authority to act, and the
  part that enforced moved before the part that judged.
- At CECOT ([CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md)) the forward operation, selection, transfer and
  custody, was coordinated across two governments, and the reverse one was
  not organized at all. A9: "an asymmetry between the **forward
  organization of action** and the **reverse organization of
  correction**."
- At the East Wing ([CASE-170](../cases.d/CASE-170.md)) review came after the object of review was
  gone.

What makes it open:

- **The rung after uptake.** A9: "Even successful uptake into a judicial
  decision does not guarantee **effective execution**. That appears to be
  the missing rung" on [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s ladder.
- **No unified agent to do the correcting.** A9: "a system need not be a
  unified agent to have a functioning accountability structure", and
  modelling the government as one agent "could obscure the very
  distribution of authority that makes accountability possible"
  ([CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)). The record's accounts of self-correction ([CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)) are
  of an organization revising itself; this is correction by others it does
  not govern.
- **Which plans count.** A9's sets of plans for a corrective objective J:
  physically feasible, legally authorized, institutionally compellable,
  and actually willed, which overlap without nesting; effective remedy
  needs a plan in all four. [CLAIM-457](../claims.d/CLAIM-457.md) shows that a coalition can lie in
  the first when no member alone does. Which relations put a coalition in
  the third, so that a court can require it, is the question.
- **Rivals that may already answer it.** Hillebrecht's capacity, commitment
  and ruling ([LIT-1607](../literature.d/LIT-1607.md)), Thompson's prospective institutional
  responsibility ([LIT-1599](../literature.d/LIT-1599.md)), Sabel and Simon's experimentalist remedies
  ([LIT-1587](../literature.d/LIT-1587.md)), Leveson's control structure ([LIT-1613](../literature.d/LIT-1613.md)) and Pettit's
  contestability ([LIT-1614](../literature.d/LIT-1614.md)), all registered unread. A9: "This question should
  remain open because existing legal and systems theories may already
  explain much of the phenomenon." [CLAIM-427](../claims.d/CLAIM-427.md)'s standard applies.

An answer would name relations that could be checked in a case, would say
how each fails, and would distinguish, as A9's table of rivals does,
ordinary incapacity, refusal, jurisdictional fragmentation, a constraint
the challenged act created ([CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md)), degraded maintenance and strategic
entrenchment, which can coexist in one case. A9 proposed a minimal
construction: an executive, a reviewer and an enforcer, in two
arrangements alike in routine operation, one in which enforcement of
corrective orders is independent of the executive and one in which the
executive can condition it, parting under a challenge. A9: that "would
**not yet establish Nucleation's theoretical advantage**, because a
conventional state-space or game-theoretic model might explain the same
difference equally well."
