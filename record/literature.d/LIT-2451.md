# LIT-2451: Beyond Base Metric: The Critical and Overlooked Role of Meta-Metrics in Intersectional Fairness

Jeanne Monnier, Thomas George, Frédéric Guyard, Christèle Tarnec and Marios Kountouris (2026). "Beyond Base Metric: The Critical and Overlooked Role of Meta-Metrics in Intersectional Fairness." *Proceedings of the 2026 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency 7127-7143*. DOI-10.1145/3805689.3812417.

## Key takeaways

- The aggregation of subgroup fairness measurements into one score is a
  modelling choice with three axes, comparison, aggregation and
  weighting, usually defaulted to the worst gap (§§1, 3).
- Different meta-metrics agree only at zero disparity and can reverse
  rankings of the same profiles and models (§4).
- No disparity pattern is "inherently more problematic than another"; the
  choice must be justified by the conception of fairness in context (§5).
