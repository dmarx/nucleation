# CASE-tmpjvzb6: A tree falls in the woods with no one to hear it

## The case

A tree falls where no one is present to hear it. Pressure waves propagate.
No ear, instrument or record receives them.

Held fixed: the event happens. Varied in the puzzle: whether anything
perceives it.

## What it can show

That "sound" names two things, a physical event and a perceived one, and
the puzzle dissolves once they are told apart. For the pragmatic-transport
line it is the baseline with no sender: nothing in the fall was produced
for a receiver, so it has no intended meaning to fix and no fidelity to
measure. It is stipulated, a thought experiment, and shows a distinction,
not a fact.

The owner's storage variant ([CASE-tmp3lyh3](CASE-tmp3lyh3.md)) adds a sender and a projected
receiver, and takes away only the actual one.
