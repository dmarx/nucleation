# LIT-1000: Does neural computation feel like something?

Albert Gidon, Jaan Aru and Matthew E. Larkum (2025), *Frontiers in Neuroscience* 19, 1511972.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpxxz74](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxxz74.md).*

- **The model.** Five Hodgkin–Huxley-type neurons in a recurrent ring,
  driven by two LGN nodes: green light fires all five, red or blue only
  one. The recorded green response is replayed either by overwriting
  each neuron's voltage or by a voltage clamp that acts only on
  deviation.
- **The clamp supplies only what is missing.** With red light it injects
  exactly the absent LGN input. With green light it injects nothing:
  activity and actual neuron-to-neuron causation are as without it.
- **The computation still changes.** With the clamp on, transitions no
  longer depend on the input, leaving "a trivial computation consisting
  of a single sequence of states (i.e., universally realizable)".
- **Nothing type I and type II.** Clamp off, and clamp on but idle, give
  identical activity. On computational functionalism they differ in
  consciousness, because only the second removes the counterfactuals.
  The idle clamp is a "counterfactual eraser".
- **IIT agrees without paradox.** The clamp degrades the transition
  probability matrix and so Φ; IIT rests on what a system is, not what it
  does. Φ is not computed.
- **The positive suggestion is thin.** "Natural counterfactuals",
  embodied in the substrate, are offered in one paragraph as what
  matters; the paper ends that the biological brain is "the only known
  substrate capable of consciously experiencing the world".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md), as "a
simulation-based test" of whether meeting the functional role suffices
for feeling. Read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-tmpxxz74](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxxz74.md), which corrects that
description: the simulation is an illustration, the argument is from the
definition of computation, and the subject is consciousness in general,
not affect. Its bearing on [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md) is indirect: it poses a dilemma
for any identity of feeling with a functional role, including Veit's
([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)). It does not supply the case the question asks for.
[THEORY-tmp97p4x](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp97p4x.md), filed from the reading, holds the conditional it
establishes, with Block's lookup machine ([THEORY-092](../theory.d/THEORY-092.md)) and Frankfurt's
idle device ([THEORY-119](../theory.d/THEORY-119.md)) as its neighbours.
