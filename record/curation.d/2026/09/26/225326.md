Every one of the sixteen works on `information-theory` had already been read
in full. One of them, [LIT-064](../../record/literature.d/LIT-064.md), has since moved to the anthology. So this pass
is an audit only: which works *about* information did not carry the tag?

The tag's blurb is: entropy, channels, coding and capacity read for
themselves, including information-theoretic accounts of work, memory and
inference outside machine learning. Four works meet it and now carry the tag:

| work | why |
| --- | --- |
| [LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md), Bourrat 2024 | its subject is Krakauer et al.'s three mutual-information measures of individuality, and which of them measure it |
| [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md), Mediano et al. | ΦID, an integrated-information decomposition, defines causal emergence |
| [LIT-027](../../record/literature.d/LIT-027.md), Varley & Hoel | a partial information decomposition of past–future mutual information |
| [LIT-010](../../record/literature.d/LIT-010.md), Vinjanampathy & Anders | quantum thermodynamics, whose account of work runs through Landauer and entropy, which the blurb names |

Considered and left alone:

- **[LIT-049](../../record/literature.d/LIT-049.md)** (Bourrat 2023) and **[LIT-150](../../record/literature.d/LIT-150.md)** (Rizi). Their coarse-graining
  accounts of individuality and emergence mention mutual information in
  passing, but their machinery is prediction, not information.
- **[LIT-136](../../record/literature.d/LIT-136.md)** (Floridi on personal identity). It is philosophy of information
  in name: its tool is levels of abstraction, not entropy or channels.
- **[LIT-044](../../record/literature.d/LIT-044.md)** (Stigler on maximum likelihood). Fisher information appears
  there as history of statistics, which `probabilistic-modeling` already
  covers.
