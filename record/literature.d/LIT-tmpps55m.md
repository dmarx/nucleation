# LIT-tmpps55m: Moral Molecules: Morality as a Combinatorial System

Oliver Scott Curry, Mark Alfano, Mark J. Brandt, Christine Pelican (2021),
*Review of Philosophy and Psychology* 13(4):1039–1058 (online 11 August
2021; issue December 2022) — DOI-10.1007/s13164-021-00540-x

## Key takeaways

- If the seven cooperative domains combine pairwise, there are 21
  second-order moral concepts. The paper names one for each, from
  fraternity (family + group) to queuing (division + possession).
- The combinatorics is offered as a framework that "has the potential to
  explain all moral ideas, possible and actual". The evidence is an example
  per cell, found after the cell was proposed.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, with the rest of the
morality-as-cooperation programme ([ADR-018](../decisions.d/ADR-018.md)). It combines the seven domains of the founding statement ([LIT-tmpcsgn6](LIT-tmpcsgn6.md)).
The MAC-Q paper ([LIT-tmp0moou](LIT-tmp0moou.md)) announced the idea, and this paper works
it out.

[NOTE-tmp8fw85](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp8fw85.md) is the close reading of 2026-10-02, and it placed the work
**Proposed**. It does not bear on the empirical verdict in [NOTE-034](../notes.d/NOTE-034.md), because
it gathers no data on whether the seven are moralized or whether anything
else is. It makes the programme's method plainer, though. [LIT-047](LIT-047.md) coded only
for the seven, and the MAC-Q wrote items only for the seven. Here concepts
are proposed from the seven and then sought in the literature. At no step
is a moral concept taken from outside the scheme and checked against it.

Its footnote 9 matters for the purity objection. It says that a moral element
that "turned out to be non-cooperative" would require the theory to "be
revised more fundamentally". The paper offers one puritanical concept,
mendicance, the religious vow of poverty, read as humility plus property.
Fitouchi, André and Baumard ([LIT-101](LIT-101.md)) explain ascetic piety and ritual
differently, through beliefs about self-control.
