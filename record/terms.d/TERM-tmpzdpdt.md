# TERM-tmpzdpdt: persistence, of an organization, and which sense: state, dynamical, historical, viable and reinstatable

## Definition

"Persistence" of an organization in this record means one of the
following, and a claim should say which.

1. **State.** A collective variable changes slowly ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)). Slowness
   is a mark of a level, not its definition ([CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md)). [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md)'s slow
   flip register, which no member reads, has it and nothing else.
2. **Dynamical.** Characteristic responses continue ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)).
3. **Historical.** The organization that produces the collective is
   causally transmitted across successive populations of members
   ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md)). [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md)'s rule passed through an archive
   has it without the others.
4. **Viable.** Trajectories that keep satisfying a constraint set remain
   available. It is a question about a constraint set fixed independently
   of the dynamics ([THEORY-134](../theory.d/THEORY-134.md)), and stability does not entail it
   ([CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md)).
5. **Reinstatable.** The capacity to reinstate an organization after
   inactivity, not its continuous running ([CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md)). It can branch, and
   so is not identity.

Neighbours, kept apart: member regeneration, members reading and
rewriting the collective variable ([CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md); [CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md)'s second system),
and being reproduced as against reproducing ([THEORY-465](../theory.d/THEORY-465.md)).

**Where the exchange of 2026-10-20 had them.** A10 §10: "individuality may
require more than one kind of continuity", with structural, generative,
viability, interventional and historical continuity, none citing
[CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md). A12 proposed "individuality, and which continuity", "a
disambiguation of structural persistence, generative maintenance,
viability, reproductive continuity, and historical identity". A13 and A14
proposed "organizational reconstitution", "the renewal, repair,
replacement, or transformation of relations sustaining an organization
across constituent or environmental change", and the name recurs in
A15, A16, A21–A23, A25 and A26.

## What it is not

- **Not "reconstitution" as a name.** The record uses the word on both
  sides of the line this term must keep. [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s historical
  persistence has the producing processes "transmitted and reconstituted"
  across populations. But "independent reconstitution", an organization of
  the same kind arising independently, is what [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md), [THEORY-418](../theory.d/THEORY-418.md)
  and [CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md) hold is *not* continuation. A name that covers "restores,
  reproduces, or transforms" (A13) would merge the two, so A13's
  "organizational reconstitution" is not adopted here. A claim about
  maintenance through turnover says which of senses 2, 3 and 5 it means.
- **Not one property with several aspects.** The senses dissociate
  ([CASE-116](../cases.d/CASE-116.md): slowness without transmission, transmission without
  co-running).
- **Not [TERM-045](TERM-045.md)'s sense 3** ("persisting" self), which is sameness over
  time of a self. This is persistence of an organization, of any kind.
- **Not a criterion of individuality.** [QUESTION-086](../questions.d/QUESTION-086.md) and [QUESTION-093](../questions.d/QUESTION-093.md) stay
  open. A14: "A boundary that performs well for one target must not
  automatically be described as the individual's true physical boundary."
