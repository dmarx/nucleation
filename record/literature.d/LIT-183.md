# LIT-183: Fitting Fulfilment – Fitting Objective or Rational Attractiveness?

Susanne Hiekel (2018), *Zeitschrift für Ethik und Moralphilosophie, 1(1), 57–74* — DOI-10.1007/s42048-018-0007-y

## Key takeaways

- Examines whether the objective element in Susan Wolf's hybrid view of meaning in life ("subjective attraction meets objective attractiveness") should be understood as objective attractiveness or as rational attractiveness.

*Seeded from the abstract alone, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Hiekel takes up Susan Wolf's hybrid account, on which meaning in life arises when subjective attraction meets objective attractiveness. She identifies at least four features Wolf requires of the objective element: it is independent of the subject, it grounds the subject's fulfilment, the subject can recognise it as a source of fulfilment, and the subject can be wrong about that. The title's question is whether these features are better met by "objective" or by "rational" attractiveness; the conclusion was not reachable.
