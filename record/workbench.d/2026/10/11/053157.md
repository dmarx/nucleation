**The owner's prompts**, verbatim:
1. "let's take up the agency question. I think it's probably worth
   differentiating between systems where "who drives?" has an answer wrt a
   particularly level of description or not. I think there's is probably a
   spectrum/gradient/distribution of kinds here. for two level systems, we
   have systems that experience fully top-down causation, systems where
   there is bidirectional causation, systems where there driving "will" is
   emergent from the system as a whole, etc."
2. "I think it's probably also worth noting that "who drives" isn't a
   constant. Considering myself as a multi-level agent, part of my
   composition is my microbiome. my understanding is that my microbiome can
   impact things like my affect and level of hunger, so that lower level
   collective is capable of influencing my higher level behavior and even
   phenomenological experience."

**What moved.**
- **The question.** [QUESTION-033](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-033.md).
- **The owner's two theses.** [CLAIM-158](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-158.md), the spectrum. [CLAIM-166](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-166.md),
  non-constancy and the microbiome, with [CASE-047](../../record/cases.d/CASE-047.md).
- **The vocabulary.** [TERM-051](../../record/terms.d/TERM-051.md), "drive profile", whose name is provisional
  and was not chosen by the owner. It has two axes. Within a level: hierarchy,
  peers or diffuse. Across levels: aggregate, top-down, bidirectional or
  emergent. It is time-indexed.
- **The grounding was already in the record.** [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md)'s information
  decomposition splits a macro-variable's synergy into downward causation
  and causal decoupling, and [THEORY-133](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-133.md) makes a higher level the cause
  system by system. The owner's kinds are regions of that space.
- **A refinement offered to the owner.** "Fully top-down" can only mean that
  the parts' detail is irrelevant given the higher level, not that the
  higher level drives without them.
- **The assistant's claim.** [CLAIM-159](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-159.md) holds that the will line's three
  accounts sit in different cells: Ainslie aggregate or emergent, the
  expected value of control top-down, and will-organization as what
  governs the drive profile's *changes*. That last gives the owner's
  non-constancy a home in the will paper.
- **The evidence for the microbiome.** Two reviews were read for it
  (curation entry of the same date). The routes are real and the effects
  are causal in rodents. The human evidence is modest: small effects on
  subjective stress, and clean nulls. The claim stays Proposed on the
  evidence, not on the concept.
- **The dancing-couple study** gains a drive-profile measurement:
  whether "the dance leads".

**What was learned.** The microbiome example adds two things the
two-level picture lacked. The lower level can be a *collective of other
organisms*, with its own diffuse drive and a disputable membership: the
holobiont question meets the threshold criterion. And drive from below can
reach *experience*, by way of interoception, which connects this line to
the felt-affect theories ([THEORY-045](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-045.md)).
