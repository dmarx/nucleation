# LIT-2525: Predatory Inclusion and Education Debt: Rethinking the Racial Wealth Gap

Louise Seamster and Raphaël Charron-Chénier (2017). Predatory Inclusion and Education Debt: Rethinking the Racial Wealth Gap. *Social Currents* 4(3), 199–207. DOI-10.1177/2329496516686620.

## Key takeaways

Read in [NOTE-1902](../notes.d/NOTE-1902.md). Predatory inclusion defined; Black households' education debt roughly tripled, 2001–2013, and exceeded white households' by 2010, unexplained by attainment; student debt is hard to discharge even in bankruptcy; the drivers (for-profit colleges, private loans) are conjectured.
