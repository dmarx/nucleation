# THEORY-tmp5hgen: Han holds that late-modern society has passed from a disciplinary society of prohibition to an achievement society, whose subjects, free of any external instance of domination, exploit themselves in a freedom that coincides with constraint, and that this auto-exploitation produces depression and burnout

## The account

Han holds that society is "no longer a disciplinary society, but rather an achievement society"; that "the disappearance of domination does not entail freedom"; and that in it "Excess work and performance escalate into auto-exploitation": "The exploiter is simultaneously the exploited."

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmptncfp](../notes.d/NOTE-tmptncfp.md). It bounds [CLAIM-tmpkjv7u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkjv7u.md): complete constraint with no one holding discretion, outside the claim's test by its version 2.

## What it does not say

That this constraint is domination by another; Han denies it.
