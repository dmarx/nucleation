# LEAD-103: State the operational equivalences among renderings a pragmatic experiment assumes and derive the noncontextual inequality they imply

## What it would do

Write the equivalences, derive the inequality, and settle the related
points the curation entry of 2026-11-12 left unclaimed (sheaf against
generalized noncontextuality; linear probes on one representation),
reading Kunjwal and Spekkens (2015).

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-067](../questions.d/QUESTION-067.md): "A statement of the operational equivalences among
  renderings that a pragmatic experiment would assume. With it, a derivation
  of the noncontextual inequality those equivalences imply, separate from
  the sheaf criterion."

## What would count

An inequality an experiment could violate answers [QUESTION-067](../questions.d/QUESTION-067.md) and gives
the factorial study a contextuality test.
