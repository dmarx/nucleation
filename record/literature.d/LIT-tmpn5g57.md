# LIT-tmpn5g57: Discussion Paper on Agentic A.I. Governance in the Banking Sector

Hong Kong Monetary Authority (2026). *Discussion Paper on Agentic A.I. Governance in the Banking Sector*. November 2026.

## Key takeaways

- "The Discussion Paper is centred on intent-anchored accountability"
  (p. 5); accountability "cannot be passed to customers" (p. 27).
- "A formal approval step is not, by itself, meaningful human oversight"
  (p. 37): the approver must see the substance, have authority and
  practical ability to reject, regard rejection as legitimate and have
  time.
- Harm may come "before review catches up" (p. 18), so velocity limits
  should be "commensurate not only with technical constraints, but also
  with the demonstrated effectiveness of oversight arrangements" (p. 19).
