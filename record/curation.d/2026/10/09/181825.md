The owner gave five arXiv papers to register, with no stated context. Each
was read on its own merits by a separate reader, from the arXiv PDF, and
each now has an Active LIT and a Read NOTE. All five fall on one theme:
what can be solved or measured about how networks train and what their
weights hold.

## What entered

- **Single-head attention in high dimensions** (Boncoraglio et al.,
  [LIT-tmpmghyl](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpmghyl.md)), with a Proposed THEORY ([THEORY-tmpjhj8u](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpjhj8u.md)): weight decay on
  tied query–key weights is exactly a nuclear-norm penalty on their
  product. The spectral law that follows is derived, not proved; the paper
  calls its main results "Claims".
- **Closed-form dynamics of word2vec-like models** (Karkada, Simon, Bahri &
  DeWeese, [LIT-tmp2n2m4](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp2n2m4.md)), with a Proposed THEORY ([THEORY-tmpw5yp5](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpw5yp5.md)): a
  rank-limited contrastive embedding learns the top eigenvectors of the
  co-occurrence deviation one at a time, not the best low-rank PMI. Proved
  for a proxy of word2vec, not word2vec.
- **Exact learning dynamics of in-context learning in linear transformers**
  (Mainali & Teixeira, [LIT-tmpvzook](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpvzook.md)). No THEORY: a Saxe-style solution whose
  fixed point was already known; its non-linear half rests on timing
  coincidences.
- **Towards understanding grokking** (Liu et al., [LIT-tmptf5mc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmptf5mc.md)), with a
  Proposed THEORY ([THEORY-tmps9ng8](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmps9ng8.md)) on when a training set fixes the
  generalising representation in the toy model.
- **Small singular values matter** (Staats, Thamm & Rosenow, [LIT-tmp55jc0](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp55jc0.md)).
  No THEORY. Its claim that information sits in the lower outliers of the
  spectrum is inferred from decile ablations that do not isolate them.

## The boundary

Four are not held in the anthology clone (commit d8b5ba5, possibly stale)
and carry `anthology-candidate`. The fifth, Staats et al., is held there as
[ANTH-LIT-517](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-517.md) with its reading [ANTH-NOTE-263](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/notes.d/NOTE-263.md), and is held here too under
[ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md). The second question here is how far a random-matrix null can
locate information in a learned matrix. The reading here disagrees with
[ANTH-NOTE-263](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/notes.d/NOTE-263.md) on one point (the Llama key and value exception), recorded in
its NOTE and not corrected there.

## Found along the way

- Three of the five changed title between arXiv versions; each LIT uses the
  current title and records the earlier one.
- Boncoraglio et al. cite Staats et al. as their empirical source for
  transformer weight spectra.
- Liu et al. is the earliest work in the record to find the circle in
  modular-addition embeddings, which [THEORY-022](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-022.md) is about. Adding it to that
  THEORY's sources is left to a revision of [THEORY-022](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-022.md).
- The vocabulary has no word for training dynamics, random-matrix theory or
  distributional semantics. `learning-theory` stands in loosely. No word was
  added.
- The owner ruled the same day that, when sources disagree on a date, the
  earlier one is taken ([ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md), version 2).
