Part of the THEORY backfill the owner asked for on 2026-10-16 (see the
moral-psychology entry of the same day). The thermodynamics topic held ten
LITs with a `Read` NOTE and no THEORY citing any of them. All ten were
filed on 2026-10-02 in one batch on dissipative structures, maximum entropy
production and the fluctuation theorems. Every NOTE was read in full, with
its LIT. No paper was fetched; each THEORY rests on the record's reading.

## What entered

Eight THEORYs, three `Active` and five `Proposed`. Codes are the temporary
ones `luria new` assigned.

- **Three findings, `Active`.**
  - The fluctuation theorems ([THEORY-tmp3uu1d](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp3uu1d.md)), from Crooks ([LIT-522](../../record/literature.d/LIT-522.md)),
    Jarzynski ([LIT-517](../../record/literature.d/LIT-517.md)) and Seifert's review ([LIT-523](../../record/literature.d/LIT-523.md)). Exact given
    their premises, and filed with what they do not say: no proof of the
    second law, no selection principle for steady states, no practical
    reach beyond fluctuations of order k_BT.
  - England's macrostate bound ([THEORY-tmpy68ek](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpy68ek.md)), from [LIT-515](../../record/literature.d/LIT-515.md). The
    general bound is a re-derived coarse-grained second law. The title
    carries the condition the replication numbers need, since their
    reverse probability is argued, not proved.
  - The critique of Dewar's derivation of maximum entropy production
    ([THEORY-tmpy0c02](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpy0c02.md)), from Grinstein and Linsker ([LIT-525](../../record/literature.d/LIT-525.md)) and Dewar
    ([LIT-521](../../record/literature.d/LIT-521.md)). Its point about Dewar 2005 is held on the Comment's
    account and its spin chain, because that letter could not be read.

  - Maximum entropy production ([THEORY-tmpxjvy1](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpxjvy1.md)), from Kleidon ([LIT-519](../../record/literature.d/LIT-519.md))

  - Life and planetary MEP, Kleidon's thermodynamic Gaia

So all ten readings became sources. That fits the owner's hypothesis, with
a caveat on how it was met: five of the eight are `Proposed`, and three of
them come from readings whose NOTEs say "No new THEORY is indicated".

## What did not enter, and why

No LIT went without a THEORY. Two candidates were considered and folded
into others:

- **Kleidon's contrast with Prigogine** (minimum entropy production
  describes relaxation; MEP selects among steady states). It is in the
  title of the MEP account, not a THEORY of its own, because the review
  argues it by example and states neither principle's conditions.
- **Seifert's other results** (the Sagawa–Ueda feedback bound, efficiency
  at maximum power). The review is a source for the fluctuation-theorem
  finding only. The feedback bound bears on [THEORY-030](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-030.md)'s subject and is
  named there by the review's NOTE; filing it would need the primary
  papers, which the record does not hold.

## Choices made

- **Departing from three NOTEs.** [NOTE-416](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-416.md), [NOTE-415](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-415.md) and [NOTE-425](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-425.md) each
  end with "No new THEORY is indicated", meaning the claims are too weak
  to source a THEORY the record believes. Dissipative adaptation, gradient
  dissipation and the geochemical origin of life are each a definite
  account that the literature cites and that other THEORYs need to name.
  So they were filed as `Proposed`, each saying in its Source section that
  its NOTE judged it too weak, and each with a `promote_when` that names
  what would settle it and what cannot.
- **No `rivals` edges.** Schneider and Kay, and Morowitz and Smith, reject
  maximum entropy production, and Perunov et al. say maximizing mean
  dissipation is not predicted to be likely. None of the three argues the
  rejection in a way that makes the accounts unable both to be right:
  Schneider and Kay's restated law is compatible with a maximum among
  steady states. Each THEORY says so in Connections. Nor is any of them
  declared a rival of [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md): the life-as-dissipation accounts frame
  the line between organism and dissipative structure as one of degree,
  but their claims are about tendencies and channels, not about that line.
- **`presupposes`, not `extends`.** Dissipative adaptation takes the Crooks
  relation and England's macrostate form as premises for a claim about
  something else. Planetary Gaia takes MEP as a premise, explicitly ("under
  the presumption that MEP is justified sufficiently well").
- **No `answers` edge.** [QUESTION-085](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-085.md), on whether an r/place artwork is
  self-maintaining, is the only question near this topic. None of these
  accounts answers it. The gradient-dissipation THEORY names it.
- **Corrections carried forward.** Each THEORY follows its NOTE's
  corrections: England's inverted logarithm and rounded RNA figure;
  Kleidon's three entropy-budget figures printed in W for mW; Schneider and
  Kay's inconsistent Table 2 entry; Seifert's §7.2 bound read with T.

NOTEs say explicitly that they are not sources. [NOTE-420](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-420.md) recommends that

separate change.
