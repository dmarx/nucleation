# CLAIM-tmp7zzbd: Where members' compensating effort prevents failures that an organization's conditions would otherwise produce, the prevention leaves little trace while its lapse is visible, so the organization can be credited with outcomes the compensation produced and the members blamed for failures the conditions produced: credit and blame then follow different principles

## The claim

A17 §6 of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afa2a8d-2598-83ea-8d1a-d9ceb992a99c),
"Failure is visible, prevention is invisible": a nurse who repeatedly
catches deterioration and corrects errors leaves management seeing "a
functioning ward"; when she misses one sign, her failure is visible and the
history of prevention is not.

> **The system's resilience is attributed to the system. The system's
> failure is attributed to the person who stopped compensating for it.**

So, A17 §6: "**An institution can receive credit for good outcomes
produced by extraordinary individual effort while assigning individual
blame for bad outcomes produced by structural inadequacy.** Credit and
blame become distributed according to different principles." A17's
synthesis adds the conscience: "**The same moral commitment that allows a
person to keep an inadequate system functioning can make them the person
most vulnerable to blame when it finally breaks down.**"

## Why it is filed apart

It was part of CLAIM-tmpnfjln until the review of 2026-11-16, which
observed that the two are separable: an institution can use a frontline
worker as a moral crumple zone without relying on unseen compensation, and
can rely heavily on compensation while assigning blame fairly. The readings
of 2026-11-16 bear on them differently: Elish (THEORY-tmpdlw9l) supports the
blame mechanism and says nothing of compensation; Liedo et al. support the
reliance on care workers' commitment (CLAIM-tmpyjzi8) and not its
invisibility. Kept apart, each link can be looked for where it occurs.

## Where it sits

- CLAIM-tmpyjzi8, the implicit moral subsidy: the compensation this claim
  says goes unseen.
- CLAIM-tmp0tg7y: the loop in which compensation, read as adequacy, drives
  cuts toward present need. This claim is its measurement link, and the
  loop rests on it.
- CLAIM-tmpnfjln: the crumple-zone claim this was split from.
- CLAIM-614: an organization can keep harm non-legible by the projections it
  reproduces. Unseen prevention is a non-legibility of its own dependence.

## What it does not say

- **That it is common.** No reading of 2026-11-16 supports it; it is A's
  hypothesis.
- **That anyone intends it.**
