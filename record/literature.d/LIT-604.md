Where it would bear, once read. The record's readings of behavioural
control filed with it are Daw, Niv and Dayan ([LIT-580](LIT-580.md)), Shenhav,
Botvinick and Cohen ([LIT-585](LIT-585.md)) and Botvinick, Niv and Barto
([LIT-581](LIT-581.md)). All three formalize control as value maximization. This
review is the older tradition of control as reducing a discrepancy from a
reference value, and the record does not yet hold that tradition
first-hand. Shenhav et al. say their account "accords well with the
definition of control in other fields, most notably control theory in
engineering".
