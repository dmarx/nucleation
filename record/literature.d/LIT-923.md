# LIT-923: Synergetics: An Introduction. Nonequilibrium Phase Transitions and Self-Organization in Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Hermann Haken (1977), *Synergetics: An Introduction*, Springer, Berlin — DOI-10.1007/978-3-642-96363-6

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for.

- Near a point of instability, the modes that become unstable change
  slowly, and the stable modes follow them. A few order parameters then
  describe the whole system.
- The order parameters both arise from the parts and constrain them, which
  Haken calls circular causality.
- Self-organization in very different systems is treated as a
  nonequilibrium phase transition with the same mathematics.
