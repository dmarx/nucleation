# THEORY-tmpaojie: Drouin-Touchette's notes derive that the two-dimensional XY model's spin waves give correlations falling as r^(−k_BT/2πJ) at every low temperature, that below the Kosterlitz–Thouless temperature the vortex fugacity flows to zero on a line of fixed points, so the whole low-temperature phase has an infinite correlation length with nothing tuned, and that at the transition the correlation length diverges as an essential singularity, not a power

## Source

Drouin-Touchette (2022), "The Kosterlitz-Thouless phase transition: an
introduction for the intrepid student", arXiv 2207.13748, LIT-tmp6hh1r,
§§2–3, skimmed in NOTE-tmpurjns.

## The account

Spin waves give g(r) ≈ (πr/a)^(−η(T)), η = k_BT/2πJ, so "at all
low-temperatures, the system is critical" (§2.2). A free vortex costs
∆F = (πJn² − 2k_BT) ln(L/a) (§2.3). The RG flows "terminate on a line of
fixed points at which y0 = 0", where "the fugacity becomes irrelevant"
(§3.3). Above T_KT, ξ ≈ a exp((π²/8b)√(T_KT/(T − T_KT))): the free energy
"only has an essential singularity" (§3.4).

## What it does not say

- That "critical" here means a tuned transition: it means ξ = ∞ through
  the phase.
- Which of these results the 1973 paper itself contains.
