# CLAIM-039: Affective states can be properties of organized systems, characterized functionally as Frijda characterizes emotions, without implying subjective experience

## The claim

U19: affective states as "properties of agents/systems rather than subjective
experience". U20 asked for Frijda's characterization to be mapped onto a
distributed corporate agent rather than relying on loose examples.

## Why it is set aside

The paper moved from affect to will by the evening of 2026-10-04. The
assistant also found prior art (A64): it "can no longer present genuinely
collective, non-phenomenal affect as the paper's principal novel conjecture".
Deferred, not rejected: it is a different paper.
