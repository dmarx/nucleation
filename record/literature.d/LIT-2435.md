# LIT-2435: Revealed preference analysis under limited attention

Mikhail Freer and Hassan Nosratabadi (2026). "Revealed preference analysis under limited attention." *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 246: 107568*. DOI-10.1016/j.jebo.2026.107568.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1797](../notes.d/NOTE-1797.md)). It shows that welfare relevance under
limited attention depends almost entirely on a minimal attention assumption,
and that the assumption costs nearly nothing descriptively.
