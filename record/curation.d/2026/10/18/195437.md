On 2026-10-18 the owner relayed a synthesis, written by an assistant in
the owner's own conversation. It proposed connections between the
record's distributed-agency line ([QUESTION-083](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-083.md), [CLAIM-279](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-279.md), [CLAIM-277](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-277.md),
[CLAIM-362](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)), statistical feature learning and ecological coexistence
theory, and recommended eight works for reading. Seven are registered
here, Deferred and unread, with no NOTE and no copy yet sought. Each was
looked up first, against Crossref, OpenAlex and the arXiv API.

**Corrections the lookup made to the synthesis.**

- Marsland, Cui and Mehta: the synthesis gave the subtitle as "A Unifying
  Framework for Ecological Community Assembly". Crossref, the bioRxiv
  preprint and arXiv all give "A New Perspective on Niche Theory". The
  LIT carries the corrected title.
- Mehta, Cui, Wang and Marsland, and Blumenthal, Rocks and Mehta: the
  synthesis dated them by the journal (2019, 2024). Both appeared on
  arXiv the year before; the dates below follow [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md).
- Power et al.: the article has a published erratum
  (doi:10.1186/s13062-016-0132-7), which the LIT names.
- Nothing else in the synthesis's citations was wrong: authors, journals
  and years match.

author, title and identifier, in a clone pulled on 2026-10-18 at commit
d1573d2. It holds none of the seven works filed here.

Two works the synthesis leans on were already registered, unread, on
2026-10-18 from the exchange on synthesis opportunities: Chesson 2000,
"Mechanisms of Maintenance of Species Diversity" ([LIT-1219](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1219.md)), and Cooper
and West 2018, "Division of labour and the evolution of extreme
specialization" ([LIT-1225](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1225.md)). The synthesis recommends reading Chesson
first, since its stabilizing and equalizing terms are the vocabulary
Letten et al. and Hofbauer and Schreiber translate into. Letten et al.
is the natural second.

Open copies: all but Letten et al. and Bunin have an arXiv or bioRxiv
copy, and Power et al. is open access at *Biology Direct*.

**Chizat and Bach (2018), held in both records.** The lookup found it
already in the anthology as [ANTH-LIT-298](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-298.md) and did not file it. The owner
then asked for it to be cross-filed. It is [LIT-1232](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1232.md), under [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md). The
second question is this record's: whether a population of learned
features, as particles under a mean-field gradient flow, can be read as an
ecological community whose members differentiate and persist or are
excluded, and whether stationarity there has the invasion form that
[CLAIM-369](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-369.md) proves for the symmetric Lotka–Volterra case. The anthology
reads it for the training of over-parameterized models. Not read here.
