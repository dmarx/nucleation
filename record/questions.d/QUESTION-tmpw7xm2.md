# QUESTION-tmpw7xm2: Does an independent review of procedural skill retention among emergency physicians report the same gaps Oskar and colleagues found: decay seldom the primary outcome, few follow-ups beyond one year, reliance on simulation or checklist assessment, underrepresentation of attending physicians, and required procedures left unstudied?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1404](../theory.d/THEORY-1404.md) holds what Oskar and colleagues found in one scoping review of
37 studies, with descriptive counts and no quality appraisal. An
independent review, or a later one citing it, that reports the same gaps
would show the map does not depend on this review's search and inclusion
rule; one that finds long follow-ups, clinical measures or attending
physicians well represented would show it does.
