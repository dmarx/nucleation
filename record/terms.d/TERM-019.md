# TERM-019: action-generating regime

## Definition

A deliberately scale-neutral term for what competes for practical control:
in a person, a desire, habit, intention or plan; in an institution, a policy or
an operating mode such as routine deployment or emergency suspension.

## What it is not

It replaced "disposition" in the surgical rewrite (worklist B), because
"disposition" had been carrying both the person-level and the
institution-level reading and the claims slid between them.

## Note of 2026-11-07: the word returns, in another sense, from "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

A47 §8 reads the replacement correctly and supports it: "I think that change was justified." "An action-generating regime specifies an organized process capable of producing characteristic action. A disposition specifies a modal property or capacity associated with a bearer." This term's own summary is already dispositional in that sense ("capable of producing a characteristic pattern of agent-level action under appropriate conditions").

A50 and A52 then use the word without mentioning this entry, for a person's anger and for "An open-source community can possess a disposition to reject unilateral governance changes" (A50 §6), and A52 §2 makes "dispositional organization" a layer of its own. That is the slide this entry was made to prevent. [TERM-118](TERM-118.md) brings the word back only as a property of a declared bearer within a declared boundary, not as the regime. This term stands unchanged.
