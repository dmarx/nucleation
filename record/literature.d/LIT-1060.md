# LIT-1060: Cognition in the Wild

Hutchins, E. (1995). *Cognition in the Wild*. MIT Press. DOI 10.7551/mitpress/1881.001.0001

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A17 §5 called the EMT case ([CASE-085](../cases.d/CASE-085.md)) distributed cognition: the team knew the drug was stocked, the box encoded where, and no one member had everything needed in time. This is the founding study of that tradition, and the first reading for [QUESTION-101](../questions.d/QUESTION-101.md). The record cites Hutchins elsewhere only through other works ([LIT-438](LIT-438.md), [LIT-702](LIT-702.md)).
