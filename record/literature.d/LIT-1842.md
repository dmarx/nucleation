summary: >-
  Ito & Kaneko (PRL 2002; arXiv 2001). N = 100 logistic maps, each driven
  by a weighted mean of the others; each unit's incoming weights are
  renormalized to sum to 1 after strengthening those from units in
  similar states. In the desynchronized phase with high weight turnover
  (a = 3.97, c = 0.12), identical units split into a core of about 14 to
  16 that sends many strong connections, dense among themselves and to
  the periphery, and a periphery that sends almost none to the core
  (2 of about 1,344 possible). Membership stops changing after about
  3 × 10⁶ steps. Freezing the effect of weights on states removes the
  structure.
