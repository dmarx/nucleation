## What the readings changed in the record's argument

- **The "sloppy" term (TERM-140, version 3).** Gutenkunst et al. define sloppiness by two criteria, a spread of eigenvalues and eigenvectors "skewed from bare parameter axes". The term had only the first. Their "evenly spaced in log" means an even density over the log range, not a fixed ratio per rank. Recomputed from the paper's own Hessians, the resolved span stays at 5–10 decades whether a model has 9 parameters or 234. The term's claim that a sloppy spectrum covers decades in proportion to its parameter count, and its contrast with a power-law spectrum built on that claim, are withdrawn, and the term is retitled to match.
- **Where singularities sit.** In networks, the singular sets are interior cone apices at finite parameters (Amari, Park and Ozeki), not the boundaries of a model manifold. "Regular inside, singular at the edges" holds for the sloppy-model literature's own models, not for networks. Transtrum 2011's citation of Amari for "metric singularities (i.e. model boundaries)" stretches its source (ARG-tmpewh6z, Rejected: that route does not carry the owner's CLAIM-808 to networks).
- **MBAM** grounds the reduction half of CLAIM-833, A's duality conjecture, and needs a hierarchy of edges as well as widths (TERM-135). The learning half stays unshown.
- **Slow training.**
  - Wei et al. derive orbits, not time courses, so they no longer ground algebraic relaxation (ARG-tmp0cg6w, Rejected). They add a third explanation of slow training: a noise-driven walk along a flat line of equivalent models (CLAIM-844).
  - Amari's equation 5.13 is a real-model instance of CLAIM-811's t^(−1/2) law.
- **Small directions.** Xu is the record's best evidence that the logit metric and the predictive Fisher come apart in trained models.
  - The gap is not the reversal CLAIM-835 claimed. It peaks at the bottom band in a ratio, and the Fisher term is U-shaped. CLAIM-835 is corrected in place.
  - Xu's directions are quiet in the logit metric and loud in variance, so the "spectrally quiet subspace" of the owner's U25 thread (QUESTION-511) does not fit them.
  - "Concepts Whisper" measures the same logit spectrum, so the four findings ARG-139 groups are three spectra, not four.
  - Song et al. is within one metric.
  - None of the four tracks a direction through training.
- **Stages.** Hoogland et al. support only the weak half of reading (b) of the owner's U23 (CLAIM-823): stages leave a mark in the learning coefficient, in two small models. The paper calls its link to singular learning "an analogy". Its three decreases of the coefficient fit neither reading of U23 and are now QUESTION-tmphkb1w. Its estimates, made at an effective sample size of about 22 and 67, leave CLAIM-812's stiffening alternative open.
