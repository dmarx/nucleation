The owner asked whether the record held questions near psychological
autonomy, epistemic justice and personal agency. A search found the
following.

- **Autonomy:** well covered in readings: self-determination theory, the
  manipulation argument, identification, and planning agency. Its questions
  sit on the set-aside authenticity line.
- **Personal agency:** covered only as the metaphysics of what an agent is.
- **Epistemic justice:** absent. [LIT-129](../../record/literature.d/LIT-129.md) is filed unread, and Fricker
  reaches the record only through [NOTE-098](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-098.md)'s reading of Leonelli.

The assistant proposed four works to fill the gaps and one question, and
the owner had all of them filed. Each work was checked against Crossref and
is registered unread and Deferred. Unpaywall reports no open copy of any of
them. The anthology holds none of them: a grep of its record/ at commit
d8b5ba5 for each author, title and DOI found nothing, and none of its
topics holds the subject.

- **Fricker 2007, *Epistemic Injustice*** ([LIT-tmp6eyxb](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp6eyxb.md)): testimonial and
  hermeneutical injustice. It is the reading [QUESTION-tmpihzdz](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpihzdz.md) was filed ahead of.
- **Bandura 2001, "Social Cognitive Theory: An Agentic Perspective"**
  ([LIT-tmp80blg](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp80blg.md)): personal, proxy and collective agency, and self-efficacy.
  Proxy and collective agency bear on the distributed-agency line.
- **Haggard 2017, "Sense of agency in the human brain"** ([LIT-tmpkrmi8](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpkrmi8.md)):
  the experience of control, comparator against inferential accounts, and
  intentional binding. It is a natural reading for the
  evidence-for-experience line.
- **Mackenzie & Stoljar 2000, *Relational Autonomy*** ([LIT-tmpqa78t](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpqa78t.md)):
  autonomy as formed in social relations, and the problem of oppressive
  socialisation. It bears on [CLAIM-016](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-016.md) and on the manipulation argument
  ([THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md)).

[QUESTION-tmpihzdz](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpihzdz.md), on the nucleating-argument line, asks whether a project memory
distributes credibility by making some contributors' moves addressable and
not others'. It was filed ahead of its readings, at the owner's request.
