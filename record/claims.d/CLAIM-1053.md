# CLAIM-1053: Equilibrium may be a stabilized query–answer pair

## The claim

In the unanchored model the joint system has two modes, λ₁ = 0 and
λ₂ = −(α + β): "The fast mode is disagreement between question and answer.
The neutral mode is their shared value." A50 §5 reads the neutral mode as
"a collective mode of their coupled relationship", and draws:

> **A regulatory equilibrium may be the stabilization of a mutually
> reinforcing question–answer relationship rather than the discovery of an
> independently satisfactory answer.**

"The relevant intervention is then not simply to make the respondent faster
or more flexible. It is to change the coupling between query generation,
response production, and independent evaluation." And "the response
spectrum we discussed earlier should sometimes be calculated for the
combined query–target system, not for the industrial target in isolation."

A50's opening and payoff state the general form: "identity stabilization
may be understood as the stabilization of a particular relationship between
questions and answers, while adaptability becomes the capacity to
reorganize that relationship"; "Identity stabilization becomes the
reproduction of relatively persistent query–answer relationships."

A50 names the pathology "self-confirming coordination": "The regulator may
adjust its standards toward established industrial performance. The
industry may adjust its performance toward those standards. Eventually the
system exhibits excellent measured compliance." [CLAIM-1050](CLAIM-1050.md) gives the
size of the effect when an anchor is present.

## Where the record stands

[CLAIM-1066](CLAIM-1066.md) locates identity in slow collective degrees of freedom; this
claim puts one such mode between a standard and what it evaluates, where
neither party owns it. [CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md) is the anchoring condition, and [TERM-066](../terms.d/TERM-066.md)'s
interpretive capture is the condition this equilibrium would be one form
of, driven by the party evaluated.

## What it does not say

"May be": it does not say regulatory equilibria are in general
self-confirming, only that their persistence is not by itself evidence of
adequacy. And the neutral mode is the toy model's; with any anchor it is a
slow mode, not a neutral one ([CLAIM-1050](CLAIM-1050.md)).
