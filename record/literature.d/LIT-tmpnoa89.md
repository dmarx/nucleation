# LIT-tmpnoa89: Teacher Performance Pay: Experimental Evidence from India

Muralidharan, K. & Sundararaman, V. (2009). Teacher Performance Pay: Experimental Evidence from India. NBER Working Paper 15323. Published 2011, *Journal of Political Economy* 119(1), 39-77. doi:10.1086/659655.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmphqrk0](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphqrk0.md) (sections 1, 3, 7, 9 and Table 9 of the working paper).*

- **Four arms, one school system, one budget.** Two input arms (an extra
  contract teacher; a block grant for materials) and two incentive arms
  (group and individual bonus), 100 schools each, "calibrated so that the
  expected spending on the input and the incentive programs was roughly
  equal".
- **Equal spending, threefold effect.** "The individual incentive program
  spent the same amount per school as the input programs but produced
  gains in test scores that were three times larger than those in the
  input schools (0.27 SD vs. 0.09 SD)."
- **The channel is effort when present.** "the main mechanism for the
  impact of the incentive program was not increased teacher attendance,
  but greater (and more effective) teaching effort conditional on being
  present."
