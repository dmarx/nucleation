# NOTE-tmpf8oxn: State of Indiana — Complaint against Richard Sackler et al.

## Contribution

Indiana's complaint against eight Sackler family members as directors of
Purdue Pharma, filed 2019-05-21, quoting and paraphrasing board reports
year by year from the 2007 settlements to 2018.

## Key results

- **The sanction.** The 2007 guilty pleas and the corporate integrity
  agreement; the directors "each certified in writing to the government
  that he or she had read and understood the rules and would obey them".
- **Breach during the term.** In 2009 "staff reported to the Sacklers that
  Purdue had violated its Corporate Integrity Agreement with the U.S.
  government by failing to supervise its sales representatives".
- **Region Zero.** In July 2010 the board was given "a list of the specific
  problem prescribers by name, along with the exact number of prescriptions
  and dollars of revenue each provided to Purdue"; in 2011 it was told
  "that if Region Zero doctors stopped prescribing opioids, Purdue would
  lose almost 10% of its sales", and was shown a map "correlating dangerous
  prescribers in Indiana with reports of oxycodone poisonings, burglaries,
  and robberies".
- **After the term (2013).** Reported initiatives included promotion "to
  elderly patients on Medicare" and "for patients who had never taken
  opioids before", and savings cards that kept "more patients" on
  OxyContin "longer than 60 days".
- **Response to exposure.** After a 2013 subpoena, the detailed quarterly
  reports stopped and 2014 board reports were limited "to numbers and
  graphs", other information relayed orally.

## Assumptions

That the board reports quoted say what the complaint says they say; the
redactions hide some of them.

## Concepts

Region Zero (Purdue's list of prescribers it suspected of abuse and
diversion); the 2007 settlements.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CASE-tmphvbov](../cases.d/CASE-tmphvbov.md).** The Purdue case: a sanction-only arm, harm not
  weighed during or after the term, by allegation.
- **[CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) (illustration).** Region Zero is a procedure that made the
  harm visible to the board, and the harm reached it as revenue at risk: a
  signal held without precedence, in the claim's terms, alleged and not
  found.
- **[CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md) (illustration).** Exposure changed what was written down,
  not what was done: the response to the 2013 subpoena was to the threat
  of the paper trail.

## Limitations

Allegations by a party to litigation, redacted; not tested at trial here.
The Justice Department's 2020 plea documents, which would show which facts
Purdue admitted, were not read (bot check on the Department's site).
