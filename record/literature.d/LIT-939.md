# LIT-939: Modeling opinion misperception and the emergence of silence in online social system

Daniele Vilone and Eugenia Polizzi (2024), *PLOS ONE* 19(1): e0296075.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-810](../notes.d/NOTE-810.md).*

- **Beliefs fixed, expression free.** Agents hold a private belief ±1
  that never changes, and choose only what to express. Any
  misperception is therefore about the distribution of opinion, not a
  change in it.
- **Topology alone does little.** The distortion of the local climate
  relative to the global expressed opinion is "at most around 15%". In
  the second model it even moves perception *toward* the true beliefs.
  The authors call it "besides small, also non-universal".
- **Silence does most of the work.** When agents may stay silent, each
  picks the expressed value closest to a degree-weighted mean of its own
  belief and its neighbours' expressions. About 55–60% of agents, almost
  all of the majority, end silent, and they do so "regardless of K_c".
- **Number and centrality trade off.** A large, poorly connected
  minority and a tiny, highly connected one silence about the same
  share. At K_c = 20 each hard-core agent silences about 150 others,
  well beyond its neighbours.
- **No reversal below about 30%.** The minority looks bigger than it
  is, but overturns the expressed majority only when it exceeds about
  30% of the population.
- **Simulation only.** The authors call for validation against data on
  private and expressed opinion.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-047](../questions.d/QUESTION-047.md) and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-810](../notes.d/NOTE-810.md). What it shows is held as [THEORY-242](../theory.d/THEORY-242.md). It bears on the
question's "which community-level mechanisms", but for what a community
*appears* to hold, not for a belief it settles on. The seeded claim that
"the outcome is set by structure, not by any member's reasoning" was
half wrong. The paper finds the direct structural effect small, and
locates the bias in the agents' expression rule. Structure enters
through degree-weighted influence and the cascade.
