The owner asked how thoroughly the record covers Harry Frankfurt, and
whether a theory states his view of endorsement. Coverage was thin. The
record had read only the 2004 Tanner Lectures ([LIT-293](../../record/literature.d/LIT-293.md)). "Identification
and Wholeheartedness" ([LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md)) was Deferred. The 1969 and 1971 papers
were not held. Three theories argued against the endorsement view
([THEORY-029](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-029.md), [THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md), [THEORY-054](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-054.md)), but no theory stated the view itself.

## What was filed

- **Read first-hand:**
  - "Alternate Possibilities and Moral Responsibility" (1969,
    [LIT-694](../../record/literature.d/LIT-694.md));
  - "Freedom of the Will and the Concept of a Person" (1971,
    [LIT-692](../../record/literature.d/LIT-692.md)).

  Both come from instructors' course pages on university domains (LSE
  PH103; CUNY's "Personhood in Philosophy and Law"). Each is a JSTOR scan,
  read through OCR. The 1969 OCR was checked against Cambridge's publisher
  preview of the 1988 reprint.
- **Deferred, with no lawful copy:**
  - "The Importance of What We Care About" (1982);
  - "The Faintest Passion" (1992);
  - the 1988 collection.

  [LIT-575](../../record/literature.d/LIT-575.md) is still Deferred.

## Theories

Two theories, Proposed:

- **[THEORY-120](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-120.md), the hierarchical account.** A desire is the agent's
  own when a second-order volition, or later wholeheartedness, identifies
  with it. It rivals all of these:
  - [THEORY-029](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-029.md) (the regress);
  - [THEORY-054](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-054.md) (graded integration);
  - Wolf's Reason View ([THEORY-122](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-122.md)), on JoJo;
  - Fischer & Ravizza ([THEORY-121](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-121.md)), on the designed addict.
- **[THEORY-119](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-119.md), the PAP is false.** Fischer & Ravizza's guidance
  control now extends it, and Wolf's asymmetry rivals it on blame.

## What the first-hand reading changed

- **Decisive identification is already in 1971** (p. 16). This answers
  [NOTE-270](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-270.md)'s open question in favour of the IEP and Velleman, against the
  SEP supplement.
- **Frankfurt accepted the manipulated agent in 1971** (n. 10), not first
  in 2002. This is first-hand support for [THEORY-040](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-040.md).
- **The original Frankfurt case is schematic.** The voting and shooting
  cases are later retellings.

## What would unblock the rest

"The Faintest Passion" settles whether the resigned addict counts as
identifying, and [THEORY-029](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-029.md)'s promote_when names it. After it,
"Identification and Wholeheartedness": the 1988 collection, pp. 159–176.
