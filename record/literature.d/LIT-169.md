# LIT-169: Defining the niche for niche construction: evolutionary and ecological niches

Rose Trappes (2021), *Biology & Philosophy 36, article 31* — DOI-10.1007/s10539-021-09805-2

## Key takeaways

- NCT's "evolutionary niche" (the sum of selection pressures on a population) is not a simple translation of Hutchinson's ecological niche: even against the restricted realized population niche it differs in extension, so ecological and evolutionary findings about niches cannot be transferred between the fields without checking which niche is meant.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Niche construction theory (NCT) hopes to transform evolutionary biology and to unify it with ecology, but the debate has concentrated on "construction" and neglected what the niche is. Trappes compares NCT's definition, the niche as the sum of selection pressures acting on a population, with the definitions ecologists use. The evolutionary niche is narrower than the ecological niche in general. Even compared with a restricted ecological concept it has a somewhat different extension, and she identifies three kinds of case where the two come apart: extreme habitat degradation, commensalism, and non-limiting or super-abundant resources. These differences limit how far NCT can unify the two disciplines.
