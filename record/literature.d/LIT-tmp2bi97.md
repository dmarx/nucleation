# LIT-tmp2bi97: SourceHut is leaving Freenode

DeVault (2021): https://sourcehut.org/blog/2021-05-19-liberachat/

## Key takeaways

- **The verdict.** "The Freenode network we once loved is the victim of a hostile takeover by corporate interests. We entirely reject the illegitimate new leaders who have used legal threats and back-room deals to steal the network."
- **The reason given for the destination.** "The dedicated volunteers at the heart of Freenode's success — the staff — have left for Libera Chat. We are sad to hear news of Freenode's fall, but proud to be following them".
- **The move, in the same post.** "We will be moving our channels to Libera Chat, effective today." Update of 2021-05-25: freenode's new staff "have been re-opening channels which moved to Libera.chat".
