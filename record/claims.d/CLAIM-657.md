# CLAIM-657: An organization can become normatively less informed without censoring anyone by two distinct routes, selective disappearance of the harmed from the population it observes and costly discovery that keeps it from investigating the questions that would reveal unmet obligations; neither needs deliberate suppression, and both differ from the counter-corrective weakening of the channels through which challenge succeeds, so that they should not be conflated in attributing responsibility

## The claim

The owner's review of [#485](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/485) sets out two routes by which an organization
becomes normatively less informed without censoring anyone.

- **Route A, selective disappearance** ([CLAIM-652](CLAIM-652.md)). The harmed exit,
  so complaints are counted accurately, but from a shrinking population.
  What is selected is the observed population.
- **Route B, costly discovery** ([CLAIM-662](CLAIM-662.md)). An audit that would reveal
  unmet obligations worsens measured performance, so it is not done. What
  is selected is the set of questions investigated.

Neither needs deliberate suppression. Both differ from the stronger
counter-corrective model ([CLAIM-619](CLAIM-619.md)), in which an organization responds to
challenge by weakening the channels through which future challenges can
succeed. The owner: they should not be conflated, "particularly when
attributing responsibility".

## Why the distinction matters

- **What each route needs.** Route A needs only a cost measured by the
  failures that present users report, together with exit by the harmed.
  Route B needs only governance that penalizes newly recognized violations
  without rewarding their discovery. The counter-corrective model needs an
  act: a response to a challenge that restricts the challenger.
- **Where responsibility can fall.** Under route A the organization's
  record is accurate about those who remain; the failure is in what it
  takes the record to show. Under route B the omission is the
  organization's own choice not to inquire, shaped by its governance.
  Under the counter-corrective model there is an act against challenge.
  Each grounds a different kind of responsibility, and treating all three
  as suppression would overstate the first two.
- **Relation to occlusion.** [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) separates occlusion,
  self-certification and reproduction. Routes A and B are two ways an
  occluding projection can arise or deepen with no act of exclusion: one
  shrinks the population that the projection is taken over, the other
  holds fixed the demands it is taken against.

## What it does not say

That either route occurs in any given organization, or that the routes
cannot occur together; an organization can lose its harmed users and
decline an audit at once. The claim is that they are different mechanisms,
with different tests: for A, the composition of the observed population
over time against the harmed who left; for B, inquiry not undertaken whose
expected findings would have raised recognized violations.
