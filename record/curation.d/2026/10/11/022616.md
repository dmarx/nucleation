The owner asked to file "some of the additional readings suggested by our
latest exchanges". Those exchanges were about a name for the combined form,
with the dancing couple ([CASE-tmpbv3ts](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md)) as the paradigm case. Two adopted
terms were discussed from general knowledge: Kelso's coordinative structure
and Koestler's holon. These are the works behind them.

## What entered

Read, with a NOTE:

- **Simon (1962), "The Architecture of Complexity"** ([LIT-tmp6qp14](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp6qp14.md),
  [NOTE-tmpi3yt0](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpi3yt0.md)). Read in full from the JSTOR PDF on a University of
  Washington course page. The record has read from a JSTOR PDF on a course
  page before (McAdams 2019). It is the source of Koestler's holon
  (through the watchmaker parable), and it has two findings the session
  can use: near decomposability as a boundary criterion, and the claim
  that higher levels of an organization have slower dynamics and rarer
  interactions, which is the all-hands case's cadence.

Registered unread, Deferred:

- **Kelso, Tuller, Vatikiotis-Bateson & Fowler (1984)** ([LIT-tmp11x8k](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp11x8k.md),
  doi:10.1037/0096-1523.10.6.812). The jaw-perturbation studies, the
  source of reciprocal compensation as the signature of a coordinative
  structure. APA's copy is closed, and no Haskins status-report copy was
  found. The DTIC-hosted Haskins reports returned HTML pages.
- **Kugler, Kelso & Turvey (1980)** ([LIT-tmpspgqb](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpspgqb.md),
  doi:10.1016/s0166-4115(08)61936-6). Coordinative structures as
  dissipative structures. Closed.
- **Turvey (1977)** ([LIT-tmp5h9pe](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp5h9pe.md), the 2017 Routledge reprint of the
  volume, doi:10.4324/9781315467931). The usual source for the term.
  Closed.
- **Bernstein (1967)** ([LIT-tmpad4pz](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpad4pz.md)). The degrees-of-freedom problem. The
  Internet Archive copy is controlled lending only. Another upload there of
  unclear provenance was not used.
- **Koestler (1967)** ([LIT-tmpafgyw](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpafgyw.md)). The holon. Controlled lending only.

## Boundary

None is held in the anthology. A grep of its record for Bernstein,
Koestler, holon, Simon's title and "coordinative structure" found nothing
relevant. None carries an instruction for machine-learning practice.

## Depth

One of six read. The next reading that would move a claim is Kelso et al.
(1984). If the record adopts reciprocal compensation as a membership test,
the test should be quoted from the paper, not from memory. Of the takeaways
of the five unread works, those not taken from their titles are from
general knowledge and say so.
