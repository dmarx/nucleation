# QUESTION-tmphs0xr: Does a stable speaker footing measurably reduce pragmatic drift relative to shifting frames, even when both drift lexically?

## Why it is a question

A67: "The next result worth obtaining is whether *stable speaker footing*
measurably reduces pragmatic drift relative to shifting framing conditions, even
when both conditions experience substantial lexical drift." The authored chains
([CASE-tmpui6ic](../cases.d/CASE-tmpui6ic.md), [CASE-tmpyegzd](../cases.d/CASE-tmpyegzd.md)) were built to show this pattern, so they cannot answer it; the
harness that could ([CASE-tmpkuw2b](../cases.d/CASE-tmpkuw2b.md)) was never run.

## What would count as an answer

Independently sampled chains under stable and shifting frames, scored for
footing by raters blind to condition ([CLAIM-tmpo21gq](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpo21gq.md)). The manuscript's §10 Case II is
the design. A positive answer would bear on [CLAIM-tmpj8d91](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj8d91.md): a stable frame may hold a
chain away from a conventional attractor.
