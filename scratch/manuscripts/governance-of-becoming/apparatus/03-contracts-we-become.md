## 4. Limits

The argument was developed with AI assistants, and the same assistants file, audit and draft from the record that holds it. The record's checks on the argument are therefore checks by a party to it.

- *The record and this manuscript are not independent in places.* Record wording the article cites was corrected by this manuscript's own drafting or review on 2026-11-24: TERM-tmplpplx's version 2 ("Two corrections a manuscript digest found": the marks read as sufficient, "because" for "only if", corrected to necessary; and the priority clause brought into line with TERM-tmpgiy6r's narrowing); CLAIM-tmpisfi1's version 2 ("A manuscript drafter found the condition dropped", restoring "where the terms it sets fail a declared standard"); the curation entry's correction on who named "relational invocation" ("Found by the drafter of the manuscript's Article 2", that is, of this article in A49's numbering); CASE-tmptpna1's correction to its delay figure (the earlier note's "four units of delay from H to M"); LEAD-tmpl9349's corrected expectation for mere coupling; and CLAIM-569's note and CLAIM-tmpsf9hp's bullet on TERM-116. QUESTION-tmpp8flf, LEAD-tmpl9349, QUESTION-tmpzhnqy and LEAD-tmple60r were filed from this manuscript's gaps, and on 2026-11-25 QUESTION-tmpp8ue3, LEAD-tmpobng3, the Deferred LIT-tmp9hjn2 and LIT-tmpg47ie, the notes of that date on SYNTHESIS-068, LEAD-tmpl9349 and LEAD-tmp5l71z, and CLAIM-tmpu77ve from one of its joins. In these places a reader should not count the record as a second witness.
- *Models, not findings.* What is computed is a check of constructions: a three-process schedule, a dependency model and a norm model that A stipulated, and a cost model priced "from the sources' numbers where they exist" and "from illustrative values, tagged as such, where they do not (Hutchins measured nothing)", for which "Any measured cost" is the first thing it says it cannot show (CASE-326). None was chosen by a sampling frame. The one documented specimen cited (CASE-tmp3xxqg) is a single case.
- *Expectations.* The three expectation blocks of the earlier draft's §§3.3, 3.4 and 3.6 were written after the assistant's runs had been filed, and were marked as reconstructed against a known result. The article now states those runs as model results without expectation blocks; the expectations are kept here. §3.3: with service at 1, reliance should rise and capacity fall, and withdrawal should drop functioning to what capacity remains; the model fails as an illustration if functioning recovers. §3.4: two stable states and a threshold at the defaults; the high state reached only from above the threshold; an established practice holding the high state at baselines where a fresh one dies out (failing if established and fresh practices collapse at the same baseline). §3.6: H misses under fixed priorities and meets under inheritance; the reading as *relief* fails if the change only moves a cost onto another process. (The first draft also expected the two rules to give the same schedule when L holds no lock H needs. That holds by the definition of priority inheritance and was not simulated; it is not counted as evidence.) CASE-326's results are reported without expectations stated in advance, beyond LEAD-108's criterion. Only §3.10's and §3.12's expectations precede any run, and the article says so.
- *Who reran what.* The dependency model was rerun by the assistant at A20's defaults with withdrawal at step 40. ## 10. Departures from A51

A51's sketch for this article has four sections: coupling against contract, with biological dependence and reciprocal maintenance as limiting cases; implicit contracts through CLAIM-430, CLAIM-788 and CLAIM-552; ambiguous invocation through SYNTHESIS-066 and SYNTHESIS-068; and CASE-326's cost model given contractual readings, culminating in CLAIM-1156's response kernel. The article keeps that scope and departs from it in four places, each for a reason the record gives.

1. *Order.* The regularity-to-contract sections come first, and the cost model (§§3.8 and 3.9) comes before stateful invocation (§3.10), so that what the model prices is set out before what it does not.
2. *The pragmatic-transport machinery.* The record links relational invocation to that line through CLAIM-044 and CLAIM-151 only, cited in CLAIM-tmp80ny9. No entry joins SYNTHESIS-066 or SYNTHESIS-068 to it, and the article does not make the join; the question is QUESTION-tmpp8ue3 (§3.7; G3.4).
3. *The culmination.* A51 has the article culminate in CLAIM-1156's kernel, "explicitly identifying how invocation modifies the relational state". CLAIM-1156 is Proposed, its relational extension K_C is A32's, and no entry defines Φ. The article ends on that open item, not on a result.
4. *The cost table.* A51 maps CASE-326's terms to contractual readings. The record holds that mapping as ARG-tmpa5ped, whose critical questions find it loose for ρ, and it holds the erosion and staleness terms as fixed parameters, which a contract's invocations need not respect (§§3.8.3 and 3.10).

A51's "Actual remaining work", "what differentiates a maintained implicit contract from ordinary co-adaptation", is §3.12's open study. A51 §7 keeps the question open ("What differentiates implicit contracting from regulated co-adaptation?").
