# SYNTHESIS-tmplih4n: How an equivalence becomes a unit: the owner's hypothesis that collapsing differences that do not matter is how classes are reified, made substantive by closure under the system's own operations and completed by differentiation between basins; the owner's genres example and its closure test; the owner's hunch that role abstraction meets balanced partitions of units, with the check that symmetry alone forces one only where the relabelling group permutes the units; and the open comparison of criteria for a learned feature

## The account

- **The hypothesis.** [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md) (the owner's, U10; Proposed,
  interpretive): collapsing differences that do not matter into
  equivalence classes is how those classes come to be reified as new
  distinctions. It rests on [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md) (the owner's; Proposed), where a
  relation settled at one scale works as a constraint at another, and on
  [CLAIM-154](../claims.d/CLAIM-154.md) (Active, demonstrated), which says when a candidate is worth
  naming as a unit. It uses [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s concept, a reusable, consequential
  pattern of distinctions.
- **What makes it more than a partition.** [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md)'s own condition,
  answering A's objection that any partition yields distinguishable
  classes: the classes must be closed under the system's operations, the
  quotient commuting with the dynamics ([THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md)'s closure, cited in its
  body), and reused.
- **Its completion.** [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md)'s body, after A29 §5: identification
  within basins is the owner's mechanism; differentiation between them is
  a bifurcation's, as in [CLAIM-408](../claims.d/CLAIM-408.md)'s pitchfork and [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md).
- **A doubt about a rate condition.** [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md)'s body declines A's
  "selective contraction" condition (within-class variation decays
  faster): [CLAIM-388](../claims.d/CLAIM-388.md) (Active) holds an exactly closed collective variable
  with no weakly coupled block. [CLAIM-481](../claims.d/CLAIM-481.md) (Active) is the record's
  statement of when coupling separates mode rates; its note of 2026-11-19
  records the owner's U12 connection of dissipating violating differences
  to self-supervised learning.
- **The example.** [CASE-tmpsjjgx](../cases.d/CASE-tmpsjjgx.md) (the owner's example, A's laboratory;
  Proposed) illustrates [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md) with genres and audiences from
  engagement data. It carries the closure test (the assistant's, accepted
  by the owner): class-level against item-level prediction of later
  engagement, against a popularity partition. It names [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md)'s
  condition for when a category's own use voids its warrant.
- **Role abstraction and balanced partitions.** [QUESTION-tmpex36a](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpex36a.md) (the
  owner's U9 hunch; Open) asks whether role abstraction coincides with
  balanced-partition structure among units, the structure [THEORY-1065](../theory.d/THEORY-1065.md)
  (Active) holds. [CLAIM-tmpj540o](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj540o.md) (the assistant's, accepted by the owner;
  Proposed, demonstrated) bears on it: data symmetry forces such a
  partition only where the relabelling group permutes the units, and
  cannot for vocabularies larger than the width, so in Li and Webb's
  models any coincidence would be learned.
- **Competing criteria for a learned feature.** [QUESTION-tmpcb0bf](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpcb0bf.md) (A's;
  Open) asks whether domain walls, local causal states, local transfer
  entropy and intervention pick out the same features. [QUESTION-218](../questions.d/QUESTION-218.md) (Open)
  asks whether a closed mode that is not a block is one system; its note
  of 2026-11-19 adds balanced partitions and a conserved mixed coordinate
  as cases.
