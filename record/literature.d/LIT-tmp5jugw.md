  Rong He (2026), Does Failure Feedback Direct Agent Self-Modification?. The
  paper asks whether a self-improving agent's edits actually respond to the
  failure they were shown. It separates three quantities: -
  **Directedness:** is the shown failure repaired more often than the
  parent's other failures? - **Category-level spread:** are other failures
  in the same category repaired? - **Net score change:** failures repaired
  minus correct answers broken. In the Darwin Gödel Machine (DGM) the target
  failure is drawn at random, which makes an exact randomization or e-value
  test possible. Across 145 eligible modifications in the public DGM logs
  the paper finds no directedness. Read for the exchange "luria experiment
  design".
