# LEAD-tmpwlebp: Run institutional-alignment experiments on governed records kept by AI collectives, varying one governing arrangement at a time

## What it would do

A13 §10 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c):

| Experiment | Comparison | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Normative displacement | Single performance objective vs. heterogeneous governed standings | Frequency of legitimate concerns being ignored |
| Epistemic capture | Self-evaluation vs. independently governed findings | Undetected consequential errors |
| Constitutional adaptability | Fixed vs. governably revisable grammar | Successful correction under unforeseen requirements |
| Dependence formation | Extensive vs. limited cognitive delegation | Changes in effective human oversight capacity |
| Organizational continuity | Persistent governed record vs. conversational memory | Preservation of commitments across agent replacement |
| Readiness | Maintained vs. neglected corrective infrastructure | Cost and time of organizational recovery |
| Metric capture | Compliance-count incentives vs. discovery-sensitive evaluation | Actual violations relative to reported violations |

"One could observe not merely whether an AI collective succeeds, but how
it changes its representations, reallocates authority, loses or retains
distinctions, and responds to challenges to its existing standards."

## Against the record

- Organizational continuity is [LEAD-227](LEAD-227.md) (shared transcript, searchable
  documents and governed record compared); constitutional adaptability is
  [LEAD-085](LEAD-085.md) (governed schema adaptation with a performance-gated arm), on
  [CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md); [LEAD-250](LEAD-250.md)'s contract-governed distributed agent is near the
  first row.
- Metric capture tests [CLAIM-1064](../claims.d/CLAIM-1064.md) and [CLAIM-tmpoh8gm](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpoh8gm.md)'s reading of
  acknowledgements; it needs "actual violations" known to the experimenter
  and hidden from the collective, so the tasks must be seeded.
- A13 §12's own limit applies: a single-owner setting lacks material
  conflict and independent principals, so the next design would have
  several principals with incompatible interests.
