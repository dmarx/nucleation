# LIT-173: From ontic structural realism to metaphysical coherentism

Matteo Morganti (2018), *European Journal for Philosophy of Science 9(1), article 7 (2019)* — DOI-10.1007/s13194-018-0233-5

## Key takeaways

- What ontic structuralists say physics shows, that symmetries and invariances are fundamental, is better captured by symmetric (mutual) ontological dependence among object-like entities, "metaphysical coherentism", than by fundamental physical relations.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Morganti argues that the usual structuralist claim that symmetries and invariances are fundamental, especially in quantum physics, should not be read as a claim that physical relations are fundamental. It should be read as metaphysical coherentism: object-like parts of reality stand in symmetric relations of ontological dependence. He develops the view and argues that symmetric dependence need not make metaphysical explanations uninformative. He also contrasts coherentism with existing structuralisms and holisms.
