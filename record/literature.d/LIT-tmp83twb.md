# LIT-tmp83twb: Reality as a Vector in Hilbert Space

Sean M. Carroll (2021), *arXiv preprint (invited contribution to Quantum Mechanics and Fundamentality, ed. Valia Allori)* — [ARXIV-2103.09780](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09780)

## Key takeaways

- Carroll argues that the complete fundamental ontology can be a unitarily evolving vector in Hilbert space plus the Hamiltonian's energy spectrum, with subsystems, classicality, space and fields recovered as emergent, non-generic structure via "quantum mereology".

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Carroll defends the self-described extreme view ("Mad-Dog Everettianism" / Hilbert space fundamentalism) that the world is fundamentally just a vector in Hilbert space obeying the Schrödinger equation. On this view the laws of physics are fixed entirely by the energy eigenvalues of the Hamiltonian. Everything recognisable — space, and the fields in it — is to be recovered as a higher-level emergent description. He sketches a route to that recovery while conceding much remains to be done.
