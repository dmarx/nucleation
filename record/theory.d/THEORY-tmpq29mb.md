# THEORY-tmpq29mb: Crawford and Joler argue that even a small AI device rests on a planetary system of extraction of minerals, labour and data, whose value concentrates in a very thin layer, and that transparency without real choice and corporate accountability does not change the resulting power asymmetry

## The account

Crawford and Joler argue that each moment of convenience "requires a vast planetary network, fueled by the extraction of non-renewable materials, labor, and data", that wealth and power are "concentrated in a very thin social layer", and that without real choice and accountability "mere transparency won't shift" the asymmetries.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmp60nf7](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp60nf7.md).

## What it does not say

A test of when the asymmetry is domination.
