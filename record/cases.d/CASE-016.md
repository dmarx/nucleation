# CASE-016: The Dual State: normative and prerogative authority in National Socialist Germany

## The case

Fraenkel ([LIT-805](../literature.d/LIT-805.md)) distinguished the Normative State, where legal norms
and procedures continued to govern, from the Prerogative State, which could act
without equivalent constraint and decide whether ordinary rules applied.

## What it can show

One structural point: a constraint may govern conduct without governing the
conditions under which it remains authoritative. Not a model of organizations
or agency, and nothing about Northstar is morally or historically analogous
([CLAIM-032](../claims.d/CLAIM-032.md)). The record holds no reading of Fraenkel yet: the case
rests on the work as cited.
