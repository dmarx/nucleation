# LIT-tmpnhel1: Can models of evolutionary transition clarify the debates over the Neolithic Revolution?

Ayelet Shavit, Gonen Sharon (2023). "Can models of evolutionary transition clarify the debates over the Neolithic Revolution?" *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B* 378(1872): 20210413. DOI-10.1098/rstb.2021.0413.

## Key takeaways

*From the abstract, not read.* After a century of study, the cause, tempo and role of intention in the Neolithic shift remain disputed. An ETI perspective shows "how different causes and conditions lead to the same result", resisting reduction of the Neolithic to agriculture or to one climatic condition, inheritance system or model. It also poses a preliminary anomaly: "why and how could the Neolithic lifeway evolve at all, given the selfish interest of individuals in a hunter–gatherer group?" The authors "do not intend to solve the shift".
