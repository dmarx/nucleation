# LIT-tmp0moou: Mapping morality with a compass: Testing the theory of ‘morality-as-cooperation’ with a new questionnaire

Oliver Scott Curry, Matthew Jones Chesters, Caspar J. Van Lissa (2019),
*Journal of Research in Personality* 78:106–124; preprint PsyArXiv 42vbn
(2018) — DOI-10.1016/j.jrp.2018.10.008

## Key takeaways

- A seven-subscale questionnaire built from morality-as-cooperation's domains
  recovers seven factors on its Relevance items in UK and US samples, where
  the MFQ does not recover its five.
- It does not test whether anything outside cooperation is moralized: it has
  no such items, and the MFQ's purity items, which are such items, are rated
  morally relevant.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, with the rest of the
morality-as-cooperation programme ([ADR-018](../decisions.d/ADR-018.md)). The sixty-society paper
([LIT-047](LIT-047.md)) cites this one in its Reply as the test of the seven-factor
structure, and [NOTE-034](../notes.d/NOTE-034.md)'s lineage paragraph names it.

[NOTE-tmpzte80](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzte80.md) is the close reading of 2026-10-02, and it placed the work
**Proposed**. It does not move [LIT-047](LIT-047.md). [NOTE-034](../notes.d/NOTE-034.md) found that test weakly
diagnostic because its codebook defined the behaviours in virtue terms and
coded no comparison domain. The questionnaire repeats both features. Its
items describe the seven behaviours in virtue terms ("acted heroically",
"showed love for their family"), and it contains no non-cooperative item. So
the seven-factor structure shows that people distinguish seven kinds of
cooperation, not that morality is confined to them.

Its MFQ studies do bear on the purity objection, and they bear against the
theory as stated in [LIT-tmpcsgn6](LIT-tmpcsgn6.md). Purity items are rated morally relevant at
about the level of the loyalty and authority items. The authors concede that
this "appear[s] to contradict MAC's prediction". They meet it by saying
purity is not a coherent domain, and by proposing that sympathy and disgust
may be proximate mechanisms serving several cooperation problems at once
(manuscript p. 40 and endnote xv). Fitouchi, André and
Baumard ([LIT-101](LIT-101.md)) give the record's worked version of that reply. The
Turkish validation ([LIT-tmpwcir2](LIT-tmpwcir2.md)) is the independent replication of the
factor structure.
