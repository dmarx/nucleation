# LIT-tmpdmpbq: Major evolutionary transitions in individuality

West, S. A., Fisher, R. M., Gardner, A., Kiers, E. T. (2015). Major evolutionary transitions in individuality. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 112(33), 10112–10119*. doi:10.1073/pnas.1421402112.

## Key takeaways

- Two defining conditions, mutual dependence and negligible conflict, and
  each occurs without the other: social aphids, *Melipona* (Table 1).
- Two steps, group formation then transformation, which "may not have
  clear borders".
- Clonal, monogamous or vertically transmitted group formation, not
  policing or kin discrimination, removes conflict within species.
