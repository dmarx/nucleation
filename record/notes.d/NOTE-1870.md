# NOTE-1870: Bragg et al. — AstaBench: Rigorous Benchmarking of AI Agents with a Scientific Research Suite

## Contribution

Benchmarking principles, a controlled scientific-research environment with production-grade, date-restricted search, and cost-aware leaderboard reporting.

## Key results

- Headline (Abstract): "AI remains far from solving the challenge of science research assistance."
- Best scores (§5): the best open-weights agent "scores a terrible 11.1%"; the best open-source agent with closed LLMs reaches 53.0% (Asta v0).
- End-to-end discovery (§5): step completion appears reasonable, but "the likelihood of completing all experiment steps remains near zero", with a maximum of 5% (Table 20).
- Confounders (Appendix A, principle 3): "Reporting must account for confounding variables—especially computational cost and tool usage."
- Data analysis (§5): "the maximum score being only 34%".
- Workflow fragility (§5): specialized Asta agents do worse with gpt-5 than with earlier models while ReAct improves, a pattern of possible "diminishing value in application-specific workflows".

## Assumptions

LLM-as-judge rubric grading is adequate for the open-ended tasks. The authors list refining it as future work. Date-restricted search prevents contamination by later papers.

## Concepts

Five benchmarking principles; Asta Environment; time-invariant cost accounting; tool-use as a confounder; general vs specialized agents; E2E-Bench and E2E-Bench-Hard.

## Bearing on the record

The exchange rates AstaBench "High for scientific inquiry" and describes it as testing "Literature synthesis and research workflows, with and without persistent structured records". **Partly inaccurate.** AstaBench has no persistent-structured-record condition. That "with and without" describes A's proposed study, not the benchmark. A's own later turn correctly says AstaBench "is not primarily a test of persistent cross-task research memory" and that a longitudinal version would be "a new evaluation using AstaBench-inspired tasks". The LIT has no characterization beyond the title.

- The content curation lists "a longitudinal AstaBench (A27, A29)" under "Further evaluations named but not designed here". The reading confirms that framing was needed. AstaBench as published is single-task and gives an agent no carry-over between tasks, so a luria study would have to build the longitudinal layer.
- **[CASE-253](../cases.d/CASE-253.md) / [CASE-252](../cases.d/CASE-252.md)**, on controls: AstaBench's principle 3 (cost and tool access as confounders) **supports** the cases' control "Matched evidence, tools, models and budgets across arms". It is independent support from a benchmark-design paper.
- **[QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md)**: no direct bearing.
