# QUESTION-005: How can corresponding communicative environments and responses be operationalized without building the preferred notion of fidelity into the measurement procedure?

## Why it is a question

A48, after outline v3: "The main unresolved issue ... [is] **how to
operationalize corresponding communicative environments and responses without
building our preferred notion of fidelity into the measurement procedure
itself**."

Every fidelity measure in the record needs it. That includes the context
correspondence τ of [TERM-033](../terms.d/TERM-033.md) and the manuscript's §6, and the admissible transport
of [CLAIM-077](../claims.d/CLAIM-077.md). A24 had already said "the correspondence between contexts cannot
be assumed".

## Where it stands

A49's admissibility condition excludes collapse but takes the observable
distinctions as given. The manuscript preregisters the cover and splits
annotators (§10), and calls the choice of observables "itself normative and
empirically contestable" (§11). That makes the choice explicit without saying
how the correspondence of source and target contexts is fixed independently of
the fidelity it is used to measure.
