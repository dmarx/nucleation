# CLAIM-1308: Access restrictions can condition individuation

## The claim

A42 §6: "Our previous account of agnotology emphasized the dangers of making
relevant differences illegible. But not every form of illegibility is
deficient. Some forms protect the conditions under which independent
activity becomes possible." Its table sets concealed violation and
epistemic incapacity beside protected privacy, confidentiality,
encapsulation, deliberative privacy ("Protects exploration before
commitments are formed") and institutional secrecy ("May protect legitimate
functions or conceal misconduct"). "The difference requires specifying the
observer, purpose, relevant standing and justified terms of access."

A42 §7: "privacy can participate in the realization of autonomy rather than
merely protect an already constituted autonomous individual", and "An
individual need not become completely transparent to another process in
order to become reliably related to it." The model is software
encapsulation, with its limit stated: "human rights are not derived from
software design principles."

A42 §10, NAACP v. Alabama ([LIT-3251](../literature.d/LIT-3251.md), Deferred): compelled disclosure of
membership lists "could substantially impair freedom of association", so
A42 reads the case as "**protected opacity as a condition of collective
individuation**".

## Precedent and fidelity

- Nissenbaum ([LIT-3254](../literature.d/LIT-3254.md), Deferred), as A42 reports it: privacy as
  "appropriate information flows within particular social contexts, rather
  than simply through the absence of disclosure". A42: "information
  legitimately acquired does not automatically carry authority to intervene
  upon its subject."
- **The boundary.** A42 cites [CLAIM-194](CLAIM-194.md) as distinguishing "a boundary produced
  and maintained by an organization from a boundary merely identified or
  imposed by an observer". [CLAIM-194](CLAIM-194.md) says that a gap or coupling criterion
  cannot tell a produced boundary from one imposed by an outside coupling (a
  beam between two pendulums); it is not about observers. The point A42
  builds on, that "A meaningful organizational boundary may involve the
  capacity to regulate exchange across it, including informational
  exchange", is [THEORY-1119](../theory.d/THEORY-1119.md)'s interactional asymmetry, which [CLAIM-194](CLAIM-194.md)'s
  note of 2026-10-21 names. The informational extension is A42's.
