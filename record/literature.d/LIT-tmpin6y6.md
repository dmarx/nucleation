# LIT-tmpin6y6: Representation of a preference ordering by a numerical function

Gerard Debreu (1954), "Representation of a preference ordering by a
numerical function", in R. M. Thrall, C. H. Coombs & R. L. Davis (eds),
*Decision Processes*, Wiley, pp. 159–165. Reprinted in G. Debreu,
*Mathematical Economics* (Cambridge University Press, 1983), ch. 6 —
DOI-10.1017/CCOL052123736X.007

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What is said here comes from Debreu's
restatement in Theory of Value ([LIT-tmp1hbb9](LIT-tmp1hbb9.md)), not from this
paper.

- **The theorem as Debreu later credits it to this paper.** On a space
  satisfying the second axiom of countability, a continuous complete
  preorder has a continuous utility function, and connectedness is not
  needed. Theory of Value, p. 73: "the assumption of connectedness has
  been removed for spaces satisfying the second axiom of countability in
  G. Debreu [3]".
- **The lexicographic counterexample.** Theory of Value gives the
  lexicographic ordering of R² as a complete preordering with no
  real-valued representation (p. 72). Whether this paper also gives it
  is not known here.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-23 because [CLAIM-tmp85p0g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md) rests its third part on
"Debreu's representation theorem". That part states the theorem for "a
connected, separable topological space". In Theory of Value Debreu
credits that form to Eilenberg ("essentially a result of S. Eilenberg"),
and keeps this paper for the second-countable form without
connectedness. The claim's statement is correct, but the attribution
should be "Eilenberg (1941); Debreu (1954)", or the claim should use the
second-countable form if it means to cite this paper. See
[NOTE-tmpsug0b](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpsug0b.md).
