  [LIT-382](../../record/literature.d/LIT-382.md), which proves the isomorphism this paper only asserts. It is
  named in [THEORY-tmpqv891](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpqv891.md)'s Connections, not as a source.
- **[LIT-378](../../record/literature.d/LIT-378.md), [LIT-389](../../record/literature.d/LIT-389.md) and [LIT-200](../../record/literature.d/LIT-200.md)** entered as second or third sources, not
  as THEORYs of their own. [LIT-378](../../record/literature.d/LIT-378.md) is by its authors' account an amalgam of
  papers I–IV, and [NOTE-326](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-326.md) says no new THEORY is indicated. [LIT-389](../../record/literature.d/LIT-389.md) is a
  programmatic essay that proves nothing; it supports the base-category
  point. [LIT-200](../../record/literature.d/LIT-200.md) is a variant of the weak nomological reading.

So 21 of 22 readings became sources, and 18 of them primary sources. That
fits the owner's hypothesis strongly here. One caveat: several NOTEs said
"supports no THEORY document" ([NOTE-110](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-110.md), [NOTE-154](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-154.md), [NOTE-175](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-175.md)) or "the record
has no THEORY documents yet" ([NOTE-018](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-018.md), [NOTE-021](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-021.md), [NOTE-030](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-030.md), [NOTE-066](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-066.md),
[NOTE-080](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-080.md)). Those meant no existing THEORY. Most of the same NOTEs proposed a

## Choices made

- **Status.** A proved result, or a construction checked by the reading,
  went `Active` with its conditions in the title: PBR, steering, the
  fidelity and no-broadcasting theorems, the toy bit calculus, the
  interference model, the stochastic dictionary, the topos results. An
  interpretive account, or a result the paper asserts without proof, went
  `Proposed`: RQM, protective measurement, the nomological readings,
  Dorato, the open-systems ranking, Hance et al., the work notions and the
  composite failure.
- **Active from a review.** [THEORY-tmpagav0](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpagav0.md) rests on a review that
  reproduces the derivation in full; the result is standard, as the owner's
  first-round ruling allows. [LIT-010](../../record/literature.d/LIT-010.md) itself advises citing the primary
  papers, which the record does not hold.
- **Relations.** `rivals` between Gao and Rovelli, who cannot both be right
  about ψ. `presupposes` from Hance et al. to PBR, from the composite result
  to the quantity-value result, and from [THEORY-037](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-037.md) to [THEORY-tmpqv891](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpqv891.md).
  `extends` from the quantum Jarzynski equality to the classical fluctuation
  theorem. `corrects` from the sieve-semantics account to [THEORY-024](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-024.md), which
  was already Rejected and had no winner named.
- **`answers`.** Only [QUESTION-055](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-055.md), by [THEORY-tmpevmkz](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpevmkz.md): a presheaf over
  contexts gets the Heyting pseudo-complement its base fixes. The
  interference model bears on [CLAIM-041](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-041.md)'s point that context effects do
  not establish nonclassicality, but [CLAIM-041](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-041.md) does not rely on it, so its
  `grounds` were left alone.
