# THEORY-tmptueyh: Levin argues that cells and tissues are an agential material whose collective problem-solving in anatomical, transcriptional and physiological spaces, bound by bioelectric networks, is the tractable interface for regenerative medicine: interventions should rewrite the setpoints these collectives pursue rather than force molecular states, and cancer is cells reverting to smaller goals, so that 'cancer cells are not more selfish; they just have smaller selves', normalizable by restoring their bioelectric connection to the tissue

## The account

Levin argues that cells and tissues are an agential material whose collective problem-solving in anatomical, transcriptional and physiological spaces, bound by bioelectric networks, is the tractable interface for regenerative medicine: interventions should rewrite the setpoints these collectives pursue rather than force molecular states, and cancer is cells reverting to smaller goals, so that 'cancer cells are not more selfish; they just have smaller selves', normalizable by restoring their bioelectric connection to the tissue.
