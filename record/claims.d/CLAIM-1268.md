# CLAIM-1268: Where observation carries anticipated consequences, those observed adapt to it, so monitoring can sharpen what an organization sees of expressed behaviour while weakening how much that behaviour reveals of the observed's own concerns, and can produce the conformity it then records as evidence of the organization's adequacy

## The claim

A42 §5: "When participants know that particular activities are monitored
and may have consequential effects, they can reorganize their behavior
around the monitoring arrangement." Its circuit runs increased monitoring,
anticipated intervention, protective adaptation ("Expression and
exploration contract"), apparent conformity ("Fewer disagreements become
visible"), and back; "A possible reinforcing circuit, not an inevitable
outcome."

"The organization can increase the precision with which it observes
expressed behavior while decreasing the fidelity with which that behavior
reveals participants' independently developed concerns. More accurate
measurement of increasingly constrained activity need not produce better
knowledge of the organization."

## Evidence named

Penney (2016, [LIT-3261](../literature.d/LIT-3261.md), Deferred): A42 reports "changes in traffic to
privacy-sensitive Wikipedia articles following the June 2013 surveillance
revelations" and adds that "It does not establish a universal effect or
identify every underlying causal mechanism." Unverified until read; the
claim's warrant is interpretive until then.

## What is held and what is added

- [CLAIM-402](CLAIM-402.md): "a category that speakers or institutions act on can produce the
  regularities that confirm it". This claim is the case where the category
  is a monitored behaviour and the route is the observed's anticipation.
- [CLAIM-619](CLAIM-619.md): "the later absence of challenges reads as success". Here the
  absence comes before any challenge is answered, from anticipation alone.
- [CLAIM-445](CLAIM-445.md) and [CLAIM-700](CLAIM-700.md): the regime shifts the evidence used to judge it;
  quiet is read as adequacy.
- [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) separates legibility from fidelity ("Legibility can rise as fidelity
  falls", after [CLAIM-081](CLAIM-081.md)); this is a mechanism by which it does. Its
  note of 2026-11-15 says the term "has no place for an observer that
  induces the distinction it reports": monitoring that produces the
  conformity it records is such an observer.
- Perdomo et al. ([THEORY-861](../theory.d/THEORY-861.md)) give the formal analogue for a deployed
  model that shifts the distribution it is scored on; the analogy is
  noted, not used as support.

A42 ties it to agnotology, reflexive control and hyperstition; those are
other groups' filings of the exchange and the links are left to the
closing pass.
