# QUESTION-tmp3hvti: Under what conditions does an organization's responsibility to repair a harm survive the absence, uncertainty or dispersion of blameworthiness among the agents who contributed to it, so that causal responsibility, culpability, accountability and remedial responsibility come apart?

## Why it is open

Four questions about one harm:

| question | what it concerns |
|---|---|
| Who caused the outcome? | causal responsibility |
| Who acted wrongly? | culpability, blameworthiness |
| Who must answer for the outcome? | accountability |
| Who must repair the harm? | remedial responsibility |

An automated system can produce an error its designer took reasonable
precautions against and its operator could not have caught. No one may be
blameworthy, and someone is harmed. Whether the organization still owes
compensation, restored access, investigation and redesign, and on what
ground, is not settled by finding a culprit.

The record holds neighbours, not an answer:

- [CLAIM-tmpnw7ez](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnw7ez.md): responsibility for an act and for its conditions are
  distinct; it treats both as matters of answering for, not of repair.
- [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md): a second-order duty of organizational answerability.
- [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md): vulnerability to a responsible agent's discretion grounding
  special obligations.
- [CLAIM-tmpnfjln](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnfjln.md): blame settling where failure is visible, which a
  blame-centred accountability encourages.
- [QUESTION-068](QUESTION-068.md): whether settling ownership settles accountability.
- Readings: Young's social connection model ([LIT-2246](../literature.d/LIT-2246.md), Deferred), Williams
  on agent-regret ([LIT-tmpydurr](../literature.d/LIT-tmpydurr.md), Unfetched; read through Nelkin,
  [THEORY-tmps61fl](../theory.d/THEORY-tmps61fl.md)).

## What would count as an answer

A principle saying when remedial responsibility attaches to an organization
without established culpability (for instance, from benefit, from control
over the arrangement that produced the harm, or from capacity to repair),
tested against cases where blame is absent, uncertain or dispersed; or an
argument that remedial responsibility always tracks some culpability, so
that the four do not come apart.
