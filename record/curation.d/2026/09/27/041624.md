The owner asked for a nucleation reading of *Entropy* 28(6):622. It is Aerts
et al., *Identifying Quantum Structure in AI Language: Evidence for
Evolutionary Convergence of Human and Artificial Cognition*: DOI
10.3390/e28060622, arXiv 2511.21731, [LIT-270](../../record/literature.d/LIT-270.md).

**Access.** MDPI refused automated requests. The full published text was read
from Europe PMC's open-access XML. The figures (captions only), the
supplementary materials and the rows the published Table 4 omits were not
read.

**Verdict: `Rejected`.** Neither of the paper's two pieces of evidence
supports its conclusion that LLM "code is structurally quantum". The first of
the two failures is already on the record.

- **The Bell test fails on context-dependent marginals.** The paper runs its
  2011 "The Animal Acts" test on ChatGPT and Gemini and reports CHSH values
  above 2. The published v1 abstract called this "quantum entanglement"; the
  journal abstract says "non-classical probability model".

  But its own tables show outcome marginals that change with context. The
  paper's CHSH is computed as if the marginals were consistent. Under that
  confound, a value above 2 does not show that no classical model exists.

  This is exactly what the record's [THEORY-013](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-013.md) records, from [LIT-264](../../record/literature.d/LIT-264.md)'s
  Contextuality-by-Default analysis of the same test design on human
  subjects. That analysis found the published human violation disappears
  once marginals are handled. The paper does not cite it.

- **The Bose–Einstein fit is a relabelled Zipf curve.** It is fitted to the
  word counts of one story that Gemini wrote after being told about the
  research. From the paper's own constants, the fitted curve is essentially a
  Zipf–Mandelbrot law. Its Maxwell–Boltzmann comparator is an exponential,
  which cannot fit word frequencies. There is no goodness-of-fit statistic.

- **An internal inconsistency, checked.** Table 3 prints E(A′,B) = +1.000,
  where the text gives 0.556. The reported CHSH of 3.09 follows from 0.556;
  the table's value gives 3.53.

**Worth noting for later.** A published comment on v1 raises the same points:
Sienicki, arXiv 2601.06104. It is not held.
