# ADR-tmp2n1mu: The threads of the gamed-evaluator argument are persisted as five topics, and the integrating argument as one line

## Context

In the session of 2026-11-26 and 27 the record developed five threads of argument:
- false insight and delusion;
- LLM sycophancy;
- shortcut learning;
- search engine optimization and governing by indicators;
- persuasion, manipulation and coercion under cognitive load.

[CLAIM-1393](../claims.d/CLAIM-1393.md) then joined them as one structure, a bounded evaluator gamed through cheap signals, with [DISTINCTION-001](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-001.md), its counter [CLAIM-1441](../claims.d/CLAIM-1441.md) and the alternative [CLAIM-tmpu6c7h](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu6c7h.md).

No topic or line named any of them. Their entries carried broad lines (`learned-structure`, `pragmatic-transport`, `dependence-and-domination`) and broad topics (`cognition`, `learning-theory`, `epistemology`). A thread could be found only by remembering its codes.

The record's two vocabularies do different jobs:
- **A topic** says what a work or entry is about, and tags readings (LIT, THEORY) as well as the argument.
- **A line** names a line of the record's own argument, so that it can be set aside as a whole and its dependants found. Each line eventually gets a SYNTHESIS.

## Decision

- **Five topics**, added to the closed `topics` vocabulary with a label and a blurb each:
  - `sycophancy`
  - `shortcut-learning`
  - `search-and-ranking`
  - `persuasion-and-influence`
  - `insight-and-delusion`
- **One line**, `gamed-evaluators`, for the integrating argument: [CLAIM-1393](../claims.d/CLAIM-1393.md) and what rests on it or answers it.
- **A thread's topic is its entries' primary topic.** That is what the entries are about. The broad topics they carried stay after it.

## First application

The session's entries are retagged in this pull request:
- **The five topics:** 81 entries across QUESTION, CLAIM, TERM, DISTINCTION, ARG, LEAD, LIT and THEORY, each with its thread's topic first. [CLAIM-1372](../claims.d/CLAIM-1372.md), sycophancy as a shortcut, carries both `sycophancy` and `shortcut-learning`.
- **The line:** [CLAIM-1393](../claims.d/CLAIM-1393.md), [CLAIM-1441](../claims.d/CLAIM-1441.md), [CLAIM-tmpu6c7h](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu6c7h.md) and [DISTINCTION-001](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-001.md).

## Alternatives considered

- **Five lines.** Each thread could be set aside on its own. But the threads are subjects that readings are about too, which lines do not tag. Lines this narrow would sit beside `learned-structure` and `distributed-agency`, and would owe five SYNTHESIS entries.
- **Topics only.** This is cheapest, but the integrating argument would have no handle by which to set it aside as a whole.

## Consequences

- **Entries filed before this session that bear on the threads keep their tags.** Examples are [THEORY-225](../theory.d/THEORY-225.md) and [LIT-1007](../literature.d/LIT-1007.md) on manipulation, and [CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) on optimization gaming its probes. Retagging them is a sweep for later.
- **The new line owes a SYNTHESIS** under the synthesis review ([ADR-083](ADR-083.md)). It is not written here.
