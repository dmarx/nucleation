# THEORY-tmpn62lb: Tomasello argues that apes do not point for one another because they lack shared intentionality, so do not understand communicative intentions, lack joint attentional common ground and motives to help and share, and use gestures learnt by ritualization as one-way procedures rather than bidirectional conventions

## The account

Apes gesture flexibly and follow gaze, but no ape points for another. Five
reasons, all one: apes do not understand communicative intentions, lack
shared common ground, lack motives to help and share information, do not
track what is new for the other, and learn gestures by ritualization, as
one-way procedures.

## Evidence

Review of observational and experimental work with apes and infants.
