The owner asked for the rest of the readings I had suggested for the essay
on organizational will, beyond the six filed earlier. Four agents searched
for lawful copies and wrote digests with verbatim quotes. Three more agents
drafted the records from the digests. I checked 217 quotations against the
extracted texts by script and read the drafts before filing.

## Copies that need the owner's eye

- **Notices.** French and March are JSTOR prints on instructor pages
  (Brooklyn College and NTNU), with JSTOR's "personal, non-commercial use"
  cover sheet. The Holman & Bruner 2017 publisher PDF in ANU's repository
  carries a last-page notice permitting individual use.
- **Beer.** The copy is hosted by an institute Beer co-founded to keep his
  work available, and its rights chain to the journal is undocumented.
- **Fischer.** An agent read Fischer's 1987 paper and the Fischer &
  Ravizza précis from Andrew M. Bailey's collection of Fischer's papers. It
  judged the copies author-sanctioned because "Fischer's own site" links
  the collection. That site says it was "created by Andrew Law" in 2019, so
  it is a second third-party collection. The record had declined to open
  those collections without the owner's decision ([LIT-687](../../record/literature.d/LIT-687.md)), so the drafts
  were set aside. The owner then authorised the Bailey copies. The 1987
  paper ([LIT-687](../../record/literature.d/LIT-687.md), now Active, [NOTE-579](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-579.md)) and the précis
  ([LIT-728](../../record/literature.d/LIT-728.md), [NOTE-575](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-575.md)) were filed in a follow-up commit.
  [THEORY-121](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-121.md)'s promotion condition named exactly this reading, so the
  theory moved to Active with both as sources.
