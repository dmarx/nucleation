**Corrections the lookup made to the exchange.**

- "Kulig & Paulussen (2025/2026), Epistemic Welfare and Algorithmic
  Recommender Systems" has five authors: Hyzen, Van den Bulck, Puppis,
  Kulig and Paulussen. The title has a subtitle, "overcoming the epistemic
  crisis in the digitalized public sphere".
- Short, Hill and Brown's title has a subtitle, "Using Antitrust Reform to
  Understand Expert–Public Cleavages". It was online on 17 November 2025;
  the issue is June 2026.

  and Schumacher.

  Causal Abstraction"; PMLR's is "Approximate Causal Abstractions".
- Weatherall, O'Connor and Bruner's arXiv title writes "Policy Makers".
- "Rubenstein et al." are seven authors, and the URL A25 gave is PMLR's
  reissue (R15) of the UAI 2017 paper.
- A21's figure for Di Tella and Franceschelli, "approximately 0.23 fewer
  front pages of scandal coverage per month" per standard deviation of
  government advertising, is the abstract's. R5 could not verify it.
- Kwok's online date, 11 October 2026, is right.
- Nothing else was wrong: the other titles, journals, years and DOIs match.
