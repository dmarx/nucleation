## The account

Kittay holds that caring for those who cannot care for themselves makes the carer dependent in turn, that these nested relations of dependency are the basis of society, and that doulia, support for the carer, is owed by the wider society; she now doubts that the state should be its ultimate form, given the risk of domination by those who give support
