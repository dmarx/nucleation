  Ghareeb et al. (2026), A multi-agent system for automating scientific
  discovery. Robin chains LLM agents to run the reasoning parts of a
  drug-repurposing cycle. Crow (concise) and Falcon (deep) are literature
  agents built on PaperQA2; Finch analyses experimental data in Jupyter
  notebooks. Given a disease (dry age-related macular degeneration), Robin
  poses questions, proposes ten candidate disease mechanisms, writes reports
  on in vitro models for each, ranks them by an LLM judge's pairwise
  comparisons, proposes 30 drug candidates, ranks them in an LLM-judged
  tournament, and hands the ranked list to human scientists, who choose
  candidates and run a human-written protocol based on Robin's assay. Robin
  then analyses the data (eight independent Finch runs merged by
  meta-analysis) and proposes follow-ups. Read for the exchange "luria
