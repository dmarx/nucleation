# LEAD-048: Run maintained inquiry under damage: a second correction after the diagnostic and copying machinery is injured, in a world that leaves its hypothesis family, with evidence generation, admission and scoring separately lesionable

## What it would do

Run [CASE-186](../cases.d/CASE-186.md): let a simulated organization correct once after a regime
reversal, injure its diagnostic and copying machinery, and measure whether
it corrects a second time when the world leaves its hypothesis family,
lesioning evidence generation, admission and scoring one at a time.

Where the record names it:

- [CASE-186](../cases.d/CASE-186.md): "What is new is the primary endpoint, a second correction
  after the machinery is injured"

## What would count

A second correction that fails when one component is lesioned and survives
the others locates where maintenance is realized, for [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) and
[QUESTION-086](../questions.d/QUESTION-086.md). Correction that survives every lesion counts against
[CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s account of what keeps inquiry going.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out in reduced form

<!-- inactive-ok-block: CLAIM-901 — new; the run of LEAD-048 and the claim drawn from it -->
Carried out by a delegated worker on 2026-11-22. [CASE-332](../cases.d/CASE-332.md) is the run
and [CLAIM-901](../claims.d/CLAIM-901.md) the claim drawn from it. The code, tests and results are
in scratch/studies/lead-048/, written for reuse by [LEAD-140](LEAD-140.md).

**Done.** The reference apparatus was run in every combination of
maintenance, second change, nine injury branches, natural and matched first
stage, self-test on and off, and ample, lean and tight fuel. The other five
admission × scoring arms were run under ample fuel, and a no-audit baseline
beside them. That is 1,836 cells of 64 worlds, about ten minutes of CPU.

**What would count**, as this lead states it, was not found. Every
component lesion is fatal without the self-test and survivable with it
wherever a producer rebuilds, so no component locates maintenance. Only the
double lesion did. No arrangement but external restoration survived every
lesion, and no lesion removed the outside service for good.

**Remains:**
- the router baseline and the full-feedback condition;
- the non-reference arms under scarce fuel;
- a permanent loss of the outside service;
- a longer horizon after the internal producer is destroyed;
- self-test schedules offset from the injury, for a detection delay;
- partial lesions of single operations.

The status and stage are the owner's to set.
