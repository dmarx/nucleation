# TERM-020: protective lock-in

## Definition

1. a disturbance activates a protective regime;
2. the regime reorganizes ordinary activity;
3. the reorganization generates secondary disturbances or suppresses restorative processes;
4. those effects satisfy conditions that support continued activation;
5. continued activation further reduces the practical capacity to leave or revise it.

## What it is not

Not a mistake in the initial response: "Nothing in this sequence implies that
the initial intervention was mistaken." Not inertia or habit, which need no
feedback through the conditions of efficacy.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a dynamical form, a second name and an organizational case

The exchange of 2026-10-17 used this term three times under other names,
without citing it.

- **A dynamical form.** A29 §8: "A system may remain in a high-priority
  emergency mode after the disturbance that induced it has disappeared",
  with z the activation of an emergency regime and h the external pressure,
  ż = μz − z³ + h. Checked: equilibria satisfy h = z³ − μz, and for μ > 0
  two stable branches coexist while |h| < 2(μ/3)^{3/2}. A system driven onto
  the upper branch by h above that bound stays near z = √μ when h returns to
  0, and leaves only when h falls below −2(μ/3)^{3/2}. That is hysteresis,
  step 5's reduced capacity to leave in its simplest form. Two caveats. In
  this form the state at h = 0 is symmetric, so "off" is the lower branch
  −√μ, not z = 0. And A29's own: "That is an example of hysteresis, not
  evidence that all distress involves a bifurcation or that the relevant
  parameter can be identified in actual institutions." Nor does the cubic
  model steps 3 and 4, the feedback through the conditions of efficacy that
  separates lock-in from bistability.
- **A second name.** A40 §2.2's "**Regulatory lock-in:** The organization's
  own interpretive or governance dynamics perpetuate a frustrating regime"
  is this term.
- **An organizational case of overcontrol.** A42 translated radically open
  DBT's overcontrol into organizational terms: safeguards that reduce
  unauthorized acts while producing "Less disclosure of near misses",
  "Fewer challenges to mistaken decisions" and "Lower visibility into
  emerging risks". That is steps 2 to 5 with inhibition as the protective
  regime. Filed as [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md), which uses this term. [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md) holds
  the individual-scale side.
