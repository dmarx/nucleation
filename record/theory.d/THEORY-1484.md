# THEORY-1484: In pre-registered replications of all five experiments of their 2012 paper, with 876 to 997 online participants analysed in each, Shah, Mullainathan and Shafir again found that players given small budgets of shots or of seconds borrow a larger share against later rounds and do worse when they can borrow with interest while players given large budgets do not, with smaller effects than the originals, and that small-budget players aim longer; focus did not predict later borrowing, the previews effect was weaker, and their first study's fatigue effect reversed

## Source

Shah, Mullainathan & Shafir (2018), "Replications of Shah, Mullainathan,
and Shafir (2012)", [LIT-2157](../literature.d/LIT-2157.md); read in [NOTE-1686](../notes.d/NOTE-1686.md). The originals are
[LIT-2147](../literature.d/LIT-2147.md) (unread) and, for Experiments 3 and 4, the working paper
([THEORY-1458](THEORY-1458.md)).

## What was actually shown

All from Table 1 (pp. 3–5).

- **Shots** (Experiment 2): borrowed .20 against .03 (ηp2 = .37); the
  poor .44 unable to borrow against −.40 able, the rich .004 and −.003;
  interaction ηp2 = .04 (original .12). Aiming the first shot, log ms
  8.11 against 7.81 (ηp2 = .11).
- **Time** (Experiment 3): borrowed .28 against .07 (ηp2 = .30); the poor
  .39, .13 and −.55 across no borrowing, borrowing without and with
  interest, the rich unaffected (p = .79); interaction ηp2 = .04
  (original .09).
- **Time with debt** (Experiment 4): debt slope −.09 against −.02; the
  poor .42 against −.36; interaction ηp2 = .01 (original .10).
- **Neglect**: aiming time and later borrowing, r(616) = −.007; previews
  interaction F(1, 968) = 1.21, p = .27.
- **Fatigue** (Experiment 1): the poor scored 44.05 on the attention task,
  the rich 42.03, F(1, 995) = 4.15, the opposite of the original.

## What this does not say

- That an independent team has replicated the borrowing results: only
  Study 1 has had an independent replication ([THEORY-1469](THEORY-1469.md)).
- That focus causes the over-borrowing: the measured link is null here.
- That scarcity of time works through time pressure: shots, with no
  clock, gave the larger borrowing effect.
- That the effect is large at scale: the debt-game interaction fell to
  ηp2 = .01.
- Anything about the working paper's guess-budget or two-grid games, or
  its windfall studies, which were not in the published set or not
  replicated.
