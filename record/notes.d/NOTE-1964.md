# NOTE-1964: Li et al. — Spectral over-accumulation in model merging

## Contribution

A diagnosis, spectral over-counting of shared knowledge, distinct from
conflict. A closed-form projection analysis of it under weight addition.
A post-hoc, training-free and data-free correction, Singular Value
Calibration (SVC), that works on top of task arithmetic, TIES, DARE,
TSV-M, Iso-C and Iso-CTS.

## Key insight

Agreement between tasks can hurt as much as conflict: if several updates
share a direction, their sum overshoots along it.

## Assumptions

One pretrained start. A layer treated as a "local linear operator"
(Remark 3.1). The merged update's left singular basis as the shared
coordinates. The analysis is exact for plain addition; other methods are
handled empirically.

## Key results

- **Projection analysis** (§3.2, Eqs. 7–9, Lemma 3.2): with
  a_i^r = (u^r)ᵀ∆W_i along the merged update's r-th left singular vector,
  each task's projection coefficient is s_i^r = 1 + Σ_{j≠i}⟨a_j^r,
  a_i^r⟩/‖a_i^r‖². When many cross terms are positive, s_i^r > 1 and "the
  merged response over-counts this shared component."
- **Inflation** (Theorem 3.3): positive cross-task overlap "inflates the
  merged singular value above the projection-optimal magnitude".
- **Where** (Figs. 2–4): "large overlaps cluster in the top spectral
  subspaces". Other methods show the gap too, some with negative gaps
  (under-accumulation).
- **Vision** (Table 1, CLIP ViTs, 8 and 14 tasks): task arithmetic gains
  7.0 to 19.0 points with SVC; on ViT-B/32 with 8 tasks, 68.9 → 81.9.
  TIES 72.6 → 80.0, DARE 65.8 → 80.7, TSV-M 84.0 → 84.8, Iso-CTS 81.4 →
  85.6. On TSV-M the gains are 0.3 to 0.8.
- **Language** (Table 2): Llama-2-7B task arithmetic on AlpacaEval and
  GSM8K 49.1/46.1 → 51.7/52.2; BERT 56.9 → 69.0; T5 41.5 → 46.3; T0 with
  (IA)³ 53.5 → 65.8, while TIES there is unchanged at 54.1.
- **Output side** (Table 3): using the right (input-side) singular vectors
  instead "often removes the gains ... and can even reduce performance
  below the uncalibrated baseline".
- **α** (Fig. 5): suppression only gives "a stable improvement"; boosting
  under-accumulated subspaces is mixed.
- **Stated caveat**: "All reported results are produced by our own
  runs". The 14-task task-arithmetic baselines (46.4–57.7) sit below the
  pretrained models (56.6–70.4) in Table 1.
- **Headline discrepancy**: the abstract's "by 13.0%" and §5.2's "a 19%
  improvement" are absolute points from Table 1, not relative gains.

## Concepts

task matrix; spectral over-accumulation (over-counting); singular-value
inflation; projection coefficient s_i^r; subspace-wise interference
I_i^r; Singular Value Calibration; output-space against input-space
calibration.

## Bearing on the record

- **[THEORY-107](../theory.d/THEORY-107.md) (TIES)**: direct, and partly adverse. Its promote_when says
  the sign account is refuted "if a merge that only rescales and trims,
  with signs left to plain summation, matches the oracle-sign merge".
  SVC rescales only, with signs left to summation, and beats TIES in the
  same runs on CLIP ViT-B/32 (81.9 against 72.6). It is not compared with
  an oracle-sign merge and is not in [THEORY-107](../theory.d/THEORY-107.md)'s (IA)³ and T5 setting for
  that comparison, so it is evidence on the magnitude side, not the test.
  On T0 with (IA)³, SVC lifts task arithmetic to 65.8 and leaves TIES at
  54.1. Those numbers come from a different pipeline from TIES's own
  paper and are not comparable with it.
- **[LIT-2627](../literature.d/LIT-2627.md) (TSV)**: TSV's supplementary already notes "a double
  scaling effect when the vectors agree". SVC's small gain on top of TSV-M
  fits TSV-M having removed much of the overlap.
- **[THEORY-420](../theory.d/THEORY-420.md)**: inflation concentrates in the top singular directions,
  which that account finds carry the most of a trained matrix's function.
  The two concern different matrices (merged updates, trained weights).
- **A's point** (reader's inference): two task matrices can be orthogonal
  as flattened vectors and still overlap positively in one output
  subspace, so this is another instance of "orthogonality is not enough",
  at the level of layer responses.

## Limitations

Baselines rerun with different checkpoints. Theory for plain addition
and a layer taken as linear, with nothing on the nonlinearity between
layers. Magnitude rescaling is not separated from the overlap measure:
in the parts read, no merge rescales the same singular values by a
uniform factor as a control. Very recent, with no
independent replication seen.
