# LIT-tmplbjir: Contrast effects in stereotype formation and change: the role of comparative context

Wyer, N. A., Sadler, M. S., & Judd, C. M. (2002). *Journal of Experimental
Social Psychology*, 38(5), 443–458. DOI-10.1016/S0022-1031(02)00010-0

Not read. No abstract was available from Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic
Scholar, so this entry makes no claim about the paper's content beyond its
title.

## Why this is here

[THEORY-1114](../theory.d/THEORY-1114.md) holds that the content of a category varies with the
comparative frame of reference, and its promote_when counts a result only
if it "comes from outside the theory's originating group". This paper, by
the title, studies how the comparative context shapes the formation and
change of stereotypes, and its authors are not of the self-categorization
group. A reading should check whether it varies the frame while holding
the target fixed, which is what that promote_when asks for.
