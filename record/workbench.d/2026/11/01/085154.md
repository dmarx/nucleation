The owner shared the exchange "Explain Nucleation Website" (sixteen
turns, ending 2026-11-01), held with an assistant that could read this
record but not write to it. It began by asking what the record says about
when interacting components warrant treatment as a new unit, followed
that thread through the record, asked for its best-supported positions
and its novel syntheses, and then for a "fledgling general theory of
recursive individuation". From there the owner asked for a philosophy
article that could be written now: an outline, a prospectus, a full
draft of about 8,700 words, a critique of the draft, a survey of the
`works:` field, a proposed update to the record, and at last the
manuscript chunked into record objects that luria assembles as a view.

**How the thesis moved.** The record keeps this because [CLAIM-178](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-178.md) says a
manuscript drafted from an exchange changes its thesis without saying
so.

| Earlier formulation | Where it stands |
|---|---|
| A general theory of recursive individuation | Deferred, not refuted. [CLAIM-477](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) keeps the broader synthesis, Proposed |
| A universal account of organizational individuality | Not claimed by the article |
| Descriptive, dynamical, constitutive and autonomous determinations kept apart | Kept ([TERM-088](../../record/terms.d/TERM-088.md), [CLAIM-474](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)) |
| Causal provenance as a basis for constitutive identity | Narrowed: provenance constrains constitution and does not settle it. The gap is the new [QUESTION-tmp9hvu1](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp9hvu1.md) |
| An objective plurality of organizational boundaries | Kept as a separate thesis, in its modest form ([CLAIM-405](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-405.md)) |
| The distinction between product and producer | Raised to the article's organizing distinction: [CLAIM-tmpx8nob](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx8nob.md) |
| Agency, perspectival governance and reflexive inquiry | Moved to consequences and to neighbouring works |

The critique turn found the draft carrying three theses with no settled
order, overstating the scope of its two constructions ([CASE-189](../../record/cases.d/CASE-189.md)), and
missing the mechanistic-constitution literature. The restructured text
filed here answers the first two. The third the record answered on its
own the same day ([CLAIM-528](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-528.md), [CLAIM-530](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-530.md)); [SECTION-005](../../record/sections.d/SECTION-005.md) carries a revision
note that its prose does not yet engage them.

**What was not filed.** No objection that the constructions prove no new
ontology: [ARG-025](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-025.md) and [ARG-036](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-036.md) already carry that caution. If the article
later argues from provenance to individuality, that inference gets an ARG
of its own. No new definition of individuality ([TERM-088](../../record/terms.d/TERM-088.md) serves), no new
general theory, and no merging of the will, perspective, translation and
luria works into this one.

**The text.** The sections hold the assistant's last restructuring of the
article, about 3,000 words, written after the critique. It is not the
8,700-word draft verbatim; that draft is in the exchange only.
