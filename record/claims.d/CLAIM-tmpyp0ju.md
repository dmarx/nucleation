# CLAIM-tmpyp0ju: Most of the research the article series governance-of-becoming proposed has already been done on the record: the role its proposed experiments play in the account is largely filled by studies, constructions and derivations the record already holds, so the series is a program of consolidating and publishing results more than one of new research

## The claim

The owner, at U32: "this is framed as a research program, but most of the
research has already been achieved. check nucleation again for
opportunities to fill gaps in your account, e.g. you propose several
experiments which I agree would be interesting, but I think their role in
our account is largely already satisfied by prior work. rework and
strengthen your series of proposals."

Two parts. A claim about the record: the experiments A49 proposed are, in
the work they would do for the articles, already done. And a consequence
for the work governance-of-becoming: its articles mostly need
consolidation and exposition, with new research kept for what is
genuinely open.

The check, made on 2026-11-24 against the studies A51 names and the leads

Of ten experiment families, seven have held work that does the article's
job, though one of them (capability lost to supply) lacks the authority
index A49 added; two have only the method or formal precedents (implicit
contracts, memetic nucleation); and one is open (the shared state). That
supports the thesis as stated, "largely". It also shows a qualification:
of the executed studies the series would lean on, CASE-161, CASE-162,
CASE-325 and CASE-396 are Active and the rest Proposed, though each of
CASE-315, CASE-326, CASE-343, CASE-346 and CASE-401 reproduces exactly on
a rerun of 2026-11-24 (their notes of that date). The owner's U33 takes
that up (LEAD-tmp0j28l).

## What it does not say

That nothing is left to research. The owner agrees the experiments "would
be interesting"; the claim is about their role in the account. Nor that a
model result shows the mechanism in a real institution: A51 itself keeps
that line ("We should not confuse demonstrating the possibility of an
architectural pathology with demonstrating its presence in a particular
political institution").
