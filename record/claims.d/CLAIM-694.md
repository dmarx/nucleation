# CLAIM-694: A conserved topological charge individuates a kind, not a token: it fixes the sector a configuration is in and its net count of defects, but not where a defect is, which one persisted or, in more than one dimension, how many cores carry the charge; so on the topological route to persistence what the charge protects is the sector, and the identity of a particular structure needs a further, declared criterion

## The claim and its demonstration

The demonstration is in [CASE-266](../cases.d/CASE-266.md).

- Two φ⁴ kinks centred anywhere have the same charge and energy, so the
  charge does not locate a kink.
- Under relaxation a perturbed kink can return to the kink family while
  its centre moves, so form can be stable while location is not.
- A winding-two sector of the abelian Higgs model can be one core or two.
  Above critical coupling the symmetric single core is unstable
  (Czubak & Jerrard, [NOTE-1950](../notes.d/NOTE-1950.md)), so the charge does not fix the count
  of tokens.

Each is a configuration within the theory, so the separation is
demonstrated, not inferred.

A's condition goes with it: "The family 𝒮, its metric, and the
observations used to identify it must be declared. Otherwise one can always
rescue a disappearing structure by widening the identity criterion after
the fact" (A5 §2.1).

## What it changes

- **[CLAIM-606](CLAIM-606.md).** The topological row's "what carries its identity: the
  charge" is true of the kind and false of the token. A topological
  soliton is protected as a member of its sector. Which soliton it is
  depends on dynamics the charge does not govern: a pinning term, an
  interaction, the coupling.
- **[TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md).** Its dynamical sense of individuation has, on this route,
  two levels. The charge individuates descriptively and robustly at the
  level of the sector. Tracking a particular structure needs the dynamics.

## What sets the number of cores: the coupling

The assistant's addition, filed at the owner's request. The charge does not
fix how many cores carry it. What does is the coupling. Above critical
coupling, the symmetric vortex of charge |n| ≥ 2 is "linearly unstable ...
and hence not even a local energy minimizer" (Czubak & Jerrard,
[NOTE-1950](../notes.d/NOTE-1950.md)), so a winding-two sector tends to split into separate cores.
At critical coupling, by A's unchecked statement, they can sit anywhere.
Below it, vortices attract. So how many individuals carry a conserved
quantity is a fact about the interactions, not about the quantity. Read
organizationally, this is an analogy only: whether a mandate stays in one
body or splits would be set by how its holders interact, not by the
mandate. No test is proposed, and nothing here rests on the analogy.

## What it does not say

- **That the charge is irrelevant to identity.** It bounds what can
  happen: a nonzero net charge guarantees a defect somewhere.
- **That organizations carry charges.** A5 itself: "an arbitrary social
  organization has no automatically defined topological charge"; "The
  absence of a justified observable should be recorded, rather than filled
  in metaphorically."

## Note of 2026-11-19: the exchange "Topological protection explained"

A4 §8 adds a caveat on what a sector is. A real mass that changes sign is no obstruction if a second mass term that anticommutes with the kinetic Hamiltonian is allowed: the mass vector can then rotate around zero without closing the gap, unless a symmetry forbids that term. So the sector this claim says the charge protects is relative to the admissible class of deformations. That is the idea of CLAIM-524 and CLAIM-571, where which configurations are joined depends on which paths are admissible.

A10 restated this claim accurately: a defect may "protect an organizational possibility without protecting a particular individual" (A10 §8). A10 §7 then called two resource-relative equivalences, one by an observing budget and one by an acting budget, "a particularly novel extension". They are on the record already as CLAIM-557, CLAIM-574 and CLAIM-581.

THEORY-tmpvtafk's vortices come in ± pairs whose count varies by channel while the net charge stays near zero, which fits the split between kind and token.
