# QUESTION-021: Is there a frame-independent communicative act that translations approximate, or is the act's identity constituted by a family of frame-relative realizations?

## Why it is a question

A18, closing the relativity analogy: "**Is there a frame-independent
communicative act that translations approximate, or is the identity of the act
itself constituted through a family of frame-relative realizations?**" The
first position "has a more straightforward relativistic structure: an invariant
object underlying coordinate-dependent descriptions". The second is "closer to
Hofstadter's fluid conception of identity through analogy ... Our translations
help us discover what we take the poem to be."

A24 dropped the underlying act object ("we may have been searching for the
wrong kind of invariant") and refined the question into [QUESTION-001](QUESTION-001.md).

## Where it stands

The manuscript answers for the second position without saying so: communicative
identity is characterized "rather than a privileged global semantic object"
(Abstract), and "Neither a high-level latent coordinate nor a fixed hierarchy of
levels is assumed primitive" (§2). Its answer is [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md), which [CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md) superseded on 2026-10-29.
[CLAIM-220](../claims.d/CLAIM-220.md) bears on this question and is Proposed, so the question stays Open. What would settle it is an argument, or a result,
that the first position predicts worse: a latent act variable that adds nothing
once the relational observables are measured.

Three further answers were proposed on 2026-10-10, none argued against the
first position.

- **Relational constitution, for the second position.** A187 §3 made explicit
  what the manuscript had left implicit: "The identity of a communicative act
  is partly constituted by its relations to participants, norms, interpretive
  conditions, and possible conversational consequences"
  ([CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md)).
- **Neither horn, from the owner.** U54 models the act on a group character
  ([CLAIM-137](../claims.d/CLAIM-137.md)). A character does not depend on the basis, so it is
  frame-independent. But it is not a further object beside the
  representations: it is the invariant of their isomorphism class.
- **A repeatable kind.** A218's "communicative kind" ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)) is
  neither an underlying act object nor a bare family of realizations, but a
  repeatable organization that numerically distinct events realize. It
  reopens the question that A24 had refined away into [QUESTION-001](QUESTION-001.md).

The first two are Proposed claims, and the third is a term A218 never
defines formally, so the question stays Open. Two later
questions refine it: which interventions tell constitutive relations from
diagnostic ones ([QUESTION-030](QUESTION-030.md)), and when relational structure warrants
treating a communicative category as a real kind ([QUESTION-028](QUESTION-028.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-07: how a kind's relations are reproduced, from "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

In the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07), A39 §10 proposed "a useful third emphasis": "How are the relations that make a communicative kind recognizable causally reproduced and transformed across its successive realizations?" That is [CLAIM-002](../claims.d/CLAIM-002.md)'s descent that is not similarity and [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s transmission criterion, so it supports the second horn, identity constituted by a family of realizations. The question stays Open.
