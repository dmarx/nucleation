# ARG-tmpql8m1: The criminal process and the NTSB's investigation of the same Tempe crash, by contrast, show the process that assigns blame stopping at the operator where the process barred from it reached the company and the state

## The inference

1. The NTSB, which "does not assign fault or blame", named the operator's
   distraction as probable cause and the company's safety culture and the
   state's oversight as contributing, and sent its recommendations to the
   company and to public bodies, none to the operator ([CASE-292](../cases.d/CASE-292.md),
   [THEORY-1932](../theory.d/THEORY-1932.md)).
2. The prosecutors found "no basis for criminal liability for the Uber
   corporation", charged the operator with negligent homicide, and took
   her plea to endangerment ([THEORY-tmpfdyu6](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpfdyu6.md)). No one else was charged in
   the reports read.
3. Both processes took up the same death.
4. So on one failure, the process that assigns blame stopped at the
   operator and the process barred from it reached upward.

## Critical questions

**Same object?** Yes: the death of Elaine Herzberg on 18 March 2018. The
evidence base overlapped: the NTSB's factual findings were public by the
time of the charge (November 2019 and September 2020), and its report is
barred only from civil damages suits ([THEORY-1999](../theory.d/THEORY-1999.md)), not from criminal
proceedings, as far as the texts read say.

**Do the processes differ in the respect the conclusion names, and does
that difference explain the outcome?** In part. They differ in whether
they assign blame. They also differ in purpose (punishment against
prevention) and in their rules for organizations: criminal liability of a
corporation has elements of its own. The conclusion is stated for what
happened, the process that assigns blame stopping at the operator, and
does not claim the blame was the reason. [CLAIM-tmp64hmn](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp64hmn.md) presses the
difference in rules and the other processes that did reach the company.

## Verdict

Proposed. The contrast is documented. What it supports is the claim as
scoped, the criminal process, and not a claim about blame in general.
