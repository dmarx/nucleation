# ADR-050: A claim that calls something organized, a perspective, an agent, effective, exhausted, constituted or grounded says which sense, target or criterion it means

## Context

On 2026-10-22, after the filing of the exchange "Graph Learning
Applications" and its branch, the owner relayed a proposal, "Toward a
controlled vocabulary for Nucleation". Its starting point: "Several of
our most important conclusions depend on distinctions that the prose
sometimes preserves locally but the vocabulary does not yet enforce
consistently." Its central rule:

> The organization of a perspective, its causal efficacy, its
> attribution to an agent, its historical persistence, and its possible
> role in constituting that agent are different claims. None should be
> inferred from another without an argument.

The record bears it out. Across its claims, questions, terms and readings,
"effective" appears 395 times in 192 files, "organized" 172 times in 80,
"grounded" 88 times in 52, "constituted by" 48 times in 27, and "exhausted
by" 9 times in 6. The review of the same day (workbench entry 16:33:52)
found three places where the filing had already let one sense stand for
another: persistence for remembered continuity, a perspective for one
carrier, and a party's organization for a single perspective.

The record already has some of the grammar. [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md), [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md) and
[TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) are "X, and which sense" terms. [TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md) (operational
availability), [TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md) (effective-description regime) and [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md)
(endorsement) are three different technical objects that the bare word
"effective" can hide. [TERM-038](../terms.d/TERM-038.md) separates descriptive, causal and
constitutive relations, though for communicative acts only. [TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md) and
[TERM-063](../terms.d/TERM-063.md) are rival criteria of agency, left unsettled on purpose.

## Decision

Proposed, for the owner's adoption.

- **One new term, one revised.** "Organization" gets a term with four
  senses ([TERM-tmpmy9oq](../terms.d/TERM-tmpmy9oq.md)); relational arrangement is the default and the
  sense in "organizational perspective". [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s note of 2026-10-22
  (later) gains the objects a claim about "the same perspective" must
  choose among: a realization, a kind, a lineage, a distributed
  realization, a representation.
- **No new terms for the rest.** "Effective", "exhausted by", "constituted
  by", "grounded" and "agency" are governed by the table below, not given
  terms of their own. Five more terms would cost maintenance without
  adding distinctions the table does not already make.
- **The protocol.** A claim, question, term or reading that uses one of
  these in a substantive statement says what the table asks:

| When it says… | it specifies… |
|---|---|
| X is organized | which relations, for what explanatory target, and in which sense of [TERM-tmpmy9oq](../terms.d/TERM-tmpmy9oq.md) |
| X is one organization | what individuates it |
| P is a perspective | which sense of [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md): organizational, represented or phenomenal |
| P is the same perspective | the same realization, kind, lineage or continuing distributed arrangement |
| X exercises agency, or is an agent | which candidate system, under which criterion ([TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md) or [TERM-063](../terms.d/TERM-063.md)) |
| X is effective | the causal target, the task, the availability conditions ([TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md)) or the evaluation regime ([TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md)); "warranted", "legitimate" or "justified" when the claim is normative |
| X exhausts Y | whether descriptively, explanatorily or constitutively, and for what purpose; and not a depleted resource or a finished search |
| X constitutes Y | the account of constitution, not a correlation or a successful prediction |
| X is grounded | whether in acquisition, semantic content, situated activation, or the material or social conditions of enactment |
| P is interrupted | whether in activation, enactment or reproduction ([CLAIM-tmpj73lm](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj73lm.md)) |
| X has authority | whether practical or justified, and which of [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s stages |

- **The six adjectives.** The proposal's summary, adopted as the rule's
  gloss: "'Organized' is a structural claim. 'Effective' is a causal or
  task-relative claim. 'Agentic' is a claim about a system's capacities
  under an agency criterion. 'Self-governing' is a stronger organizational
  claim. 'Constitutive' is an ontological claim. 'Phenomenal' is a claim
  about experience."
- **Scope.** New and revised entries from adoption on. Existing entries
  are not rewritten in bulk; a flagged word in an older entry is fixed
  when that entry is next revised for another reason.

## Alternatives

- **A term for every word.** The proposal rejected it, and so does this
  decision: five terms on usage would need maintaining in every line, and
  the table says the same in one place.
- **A note in a curation entry only.** The proposal offered it. A curation
  entry is a journal, read once; a usage rule needs to be findable from
  the decisions index, where the record's other rules about itself are.
- **A lint pattern now.** [DP-3](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-3) says a norm repeated in corrections should
  be walked up from prose to mechanism. But a pattern that flags every
  "effective" would fire 395 times on current text, and most uses are
  already indexed by context. The mechanism waits for the owner's adoption
  and for a narrower pattern, such as bare "effective" or "exhausted by"
  in a claim's title.
- **Status quo.** The cost is the one the review of 2026-10-22 found: a
  sense slipping into another in a filing, caught only by a reader who
  happened to check.

## Consequences

- Adoption is the owner's. Until then the table is a convention filers
  may follow, and this filing's new entries already do in part.
- [CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md)'s objection stays in force against the vocabulary itself:
  "A vocabulary flexible enough to describe every arrangement doesn't
  thereby explain them" (the relayed proposal). Each sense must let a
  claim say something that could be wrong.
- If adopted, the natural follow-up is a lint pattern for the narrowest
  cases, and a decision on whether [TERM-038](../terms.d/TERM-038.md)'s three relations widen beyond
  communicative acts, as the proposal assumed.
