# THEORY-1818: Fineman argues that because vulnerability is universal and constant and individuals' resilience depends on the assets societal institutions confer, a state that brings those institutions into legal existence is responsible for monitoring them and for equal access and opportunity, and must justify any privilege it confers or tolerates

## The account

Fineman argues that because vulnerability is universal and constant and individuals' resilience depends on the assets societal institutions confer, a state that brings those institutions into legal existence is responsible for monitoring them and for equal access and opportunity, and must justify any privilege it confers or tolerates

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1899](../notes.d/NOTE-1899.md); its key results are quoted there with pages. [ARG-099](../arguments.d/ARG-099.md) says why it is not support for [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md), under its first title (growth with integration) or under the title it took at the owner's review of [#488](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/488) on 2026-11-13 (vulnerability to a responsible agent's discretion).

## What it does not say

That responsibility grows as a whole integrates its parts: her vulnerability is universal, not produced by integration. Nor that non-state wholes bear the same responsibility.
