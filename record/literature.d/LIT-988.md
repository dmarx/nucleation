# LIT-988: Scientific discovery in a model-centric framework: Reproducibility, innovation, and epistemic diversity

Berna Devezer, Luis G. Nardin, Bert Baumgaertner and Erkan Ozge Buzbas (2019), *PLOS ONE* 14(5), e0216125.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-792](../notes.d/NOTE-792.md).*

- **Consensus as a process.** The "global model" is the last winner of a
  pairwise AIC or SC comparison on fresh data. The no-replication process
  is a Markov chain over 14 linear models. The consensus has no memory,
  and no scientist holds a belief.
- **Reproducibility is causally idle.** A replication re-tests the same
  pair on independent data, so its outcome sums out of the transition
  probabilities (S23). The reproducibility rate cannot affect the
  long-run distribution, first passage time or stickiness.
- **Reproducible and wrong.** Boundary testers, who only add
  interactions, reach near-perfect reproducibility while spending 25%
  (AIC) or 48% (SC) of their time at the true model. Their correlation
  between reproducibility and time at the truth is −0.06, against 0.35
  to 0.69 in other populations.
- **Connectedness speeds discovery.** Mavericks, who propose from the
  whole space, reach the truth fastest. Under hard strategies a
  boundary-tester population takes about 1,600 steps.
- **Diversity as insurance.** The equal mix is never the worst
  population on any property and has the lowest variability. It is not
  the best on time at the truth, and it fails under high noise.
- **Method matters.** SC spends longer at the truth than AIC (medians
  27% and 20%) but varies more.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-047](../questions.d/QUESTION-047.md) and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-792](../notes.d/NOTE-792.md). What it shows is held as [THEORY-295](../theory.d/THEORY-295.md): with every test
sound, which alternatives a community puts up can hold its consensus on
a false model that its replications keep confirming. This is the
record's nearest model to the question's design. It is about a standing
view, not a belief. Its "epistemic diversity" is a permanent mix of
proposal strategies, distinct from the transient diversity of [THEORY-148](../theory.d/THEORY-148.md).
The seeded body's claim that diversity "sets whether the community finds
the truth" was too strong: which strategies are present, and the noise
level, set it.
