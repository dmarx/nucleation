# THEORY-2001: Meaning is active love of worthy objects

## The account

Wolf's fitting-fulfillment view ([LIT-2594](../literature.d/LIT-2594.md), read in [NOTE-1949](../notes.d/NOTE-1949.md)): meaning
arises "when subjective attraction meets objective attractiveness", with
the person actively engaged with the object. Fulfillment without a worthy
object, and contribution without the person's engagement, each fall short.
So meaningfulness is a third dimension of a good life, beside happiness
and morality.

## Why it is Proposed

[THEORY-1836](THEORY-1836.md) asserts only that Wolf argues this, and is Active on its
reading. This entry asserts the thesis. Wolf concedes that it needs a
standard of value that is not radically subjective, and that she offers no
theory of it; the record holds none either. Until it does, the objective
element is a placeholder, and the distinctness from happiness rests on it.
