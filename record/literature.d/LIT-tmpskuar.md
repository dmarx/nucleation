# LIT-tmpskuar: Motivational trade-offs and modulation of nociception in bumblebees

Matilda Gibbons, Elisabetta Versace, Andrew Crump, Bartosz Baran & Lars
Chittka (2022), *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*
119(31):e2205821119 — DOI-10.1073/pnas.2205821119

## Key takeaways

- **Design** (p. 1). Two high-quality feeders (40% sucrose) against two
  alternatives: "different groups of bees were offered 10%, 20%, 30%, or
  40% sucrose solution (Fig. 1)." Each bee was run unheated, then "with
  the high-quality feeders at 55 °C (heated) and the alternative feeders
  unheated." Feeders were marked by colour.
- **Result** (p. 1). "Bees avoided noxious feeders when the unheated
  feeders contained high sucrose concentrations, but progressively
  increased feeding from noxious feeders when the sucrose concentration at
  unheated feeders decreased." The drop in feeding on the 40% feeders as
  the alternatives got richer was steeper when they were heated (p. 2).
  "Thus, bees traded off their motivation to avoid noxious heat against
  their preference for high sucrose concentrations."
- **Central, not peripheral** (p. 2). Landings without feeding fell
  between the first and last bouts, so the bees chose by learned
  colour/spatial cues, not by tasting each feeder.
- **Inference** (pp. 1, 3). "This ability is consistent with a capacity
  for pain experiences in insects," although "it is not a formal proof."
- **Corrected** (2025). "We reported the output of the wrong model in the
  results section and in Fig. 1." One statistic changes, from
  z = −2.068, P = 0.039 to z = −2.132, P = 0.033. The conclusion stands.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-23 for [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s comparative test and [CLAIM-tmp83ge2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp83ge2.md)'s
recasting of it. The bearing is set out in [NOTE-tmpt0lr2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpt0lr2.md). In
short:

- at the one cost tested, the bees' choices vary gradually with the
  resource and show no override;
- because cost is not varied, the study cannot show a level threshold or
  an indifference curve;
- 55 °C did not stop any bee taking a 30-point sucrose advantage.

No instruction for machine-learning practice; nothing here belongs in the
anthology.
