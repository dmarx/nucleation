# THEORY-1607: Blackiston, Shomrat and Levin argue, from learned behaviour reported to survive brain remodelling in metamorphosing insects, regenerating planaria and hibernating mammals, that synaptic plasticity alone does not explain lasting memory, and propose, untested, that a planarian's memory may be stored outside the brain and imprinted on the regenerated one

## The account

If a memory outlasts the cells and connections that formed it, it was
not only in them: "there is a 'blueprint' of the memories that may be
kept safely in place by a mechanism other than the synaptic connections
themselves" (What mechanisms underlie memory?). For planaria the authors
go further: "it is possible that memory can be stored outside the brain
and imprinted on the nascent brain during regeneration" (Memory in
aneural systems). They propose that somatic and cognitive memory are
coupled through bioelectric signalling among non-neural cells.

## Evidence

- Insects: odour-shock aversions in Drosophila and Manduca survive
  metamorphosis; mushroom-body γ neurons prune completely and "the α'/β'
  neurons demonstrating partial pruning before establishing adult
  specific projections"; the mechanism "remains unsolved".
- Planaria: McConnell's savings in regenerated tail fragments and in
  worms fed trained worms' RNA, a field later "mired in controversy";
  and the authors' automated place-learning result, given in one
  sentence.
- Mammals: hibernating squirrels prune hippocampal dendrites; two
  operant tasks were "unimpaired following 6 months of hibernation", in
  a study of Alpine marmots.
- Against synapses alone: the instability of LTP, LTD and STDP,
  reconsolidation, the reported need for continual PKMζ activity, and
  one Aplysia study.

[NOTE-1258](../notes.d/NOTE-1258.md) records citation errors and a survey that mentions replication
failures without weighing them.

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-302](../questions.d/QUESTION-302.md) cites this perspective as collecting such cases, and its
note of 2026-10-29 records that "What carries it is still open" on this
reading. The perspective lists the candidate carriers the question asks
about, mushroom-body neurons, neoblasts, non-coding RNA and bioelectric
networks, and settles none. Its medical framing, that brain repair must
not distort "the decades of memory content and personality of the
patient", is the identity half of the question, which it does not
discuss.
