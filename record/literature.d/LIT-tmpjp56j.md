Christoforos Somarakis, Reza Hadadi, John S. Baras (2016), *2016 European Control Conference (ECC), pp. 1445–1450* — DOI-10.1109/ECC.2016.7810493

## Key takeaways

- For networks of agents that each have their own independently evolving opinion dynamics and communicate cooperatively with neighbours, non-negative matrix theory gives sufficient conditions for stability and convergence to a common synchronized opinion, for both identical and non-identical agents. The "scrambling" connectivity condition is presumably what the title names; I have not verified this from the full text.

*Seeded from the abstract alone, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The paper studies social or opinion networks of autonomous agents, each holding a personal view that follows its own dynamics while it exchanges information with neighbours through a cooperative algorithm. Using non-negative matrix theory, the authors analyse both identical and non-identical views and derive sufficient conditions for stability and for convergence to a common synchronized opinion.
