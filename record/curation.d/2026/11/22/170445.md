## The verdicts

- **(a)** The form is the paper's own: "base refinements of the form
  {ν : B | e}, where [...] e is a boolean-valued expression" (§2, p. 160).
  A liquid type is the subclass whose "refinement predicates are
  conjunctions of qualifiers from Q⋆" (p. 160), required only where a type
  is inferred: "By forcing the types to be liquid, we bound the space of
  possible solutions, thus making inference efficiently decidable"
  (p. 164). For a record, any predicate the entry passes can be held as a
  descriptor. But descriptors can be found mechanically only from a finite
  vocabulary fixed in advance, closed under conjunction and not
  disjunction (p. 164). And a descriptor checked by a conservative
  procedure can be true and fail to check: "exact implication checking is
  undecidable" (p. 163).
- **(b)** Yes, within limits. The result is "the strongest conjunction of
  qualifiers from Q⋆ that satisfies all the constraints" (p. 160), the
  unique minimum solution (p. 166). That is the strongest the vocabulary
  and the conservative check allow, not the strongest true. For a
  function's inputs it is relative to the calls in the program, "useless
  but sound" with none (p. 167). Inference is informative update: it
  starts from "the conjunction of all logical qualifiers" and weakens
  (p. 166), and never moves the program.
- **(c)** Rejected, not moved: "If Infer(Γ, e, Q) = Failure then there is
  no S" (Theorem 2, p. 166), and DSOLVE lists the operations "that could
  not statically be proven safe" (p. 167). The failure is ambiguous: "safe
  programs which cannot be well-typed [...] due either to an inappropriate
  choice of qualifiers or the conservativeness of our notion of
  subtyping" (p. 160). Telling the program, the qualifiers and the checker
  apart is future work (p. 169). The remedies are outside the checker:
  more qualifiers, run-time checks (proposed, after hybrid type checking),
  or a programmer's fix (blit, p. 168). This widens CLAIM-tmp764wb in
  CLAIM-tmpcmt21.
- **(d)** Proof-irrelevant, as Freeman and Pfenning's system is. Nothing
  is kept, and the inferred types are written to .annot files "as
  documentation" (p. 167). The filer notes, as its own reading, that an
  inferred assignment would make a certificate (each constraint one
  validity check). The paper does not keep or use it so.

## Gaps

In the paper's own argument, in NOTE-tmpnd1kd's Limitations: the proofs
of both theorems, and of the unique minimum solution, are deferred to a
technical report. The uniqueness rests on solutions being closed under
union, which is not stated. Failure conflates three causes. Run-time
check insertion is proposed, not done. The note on CLAIM-783 states the
closure step as a parallel. No gap is filled as an entry, since the
record uses none of the omitted proofs.

## Offered, not filed

For the owner's choice: a reading of Flanagan 2006, "Hybrid Type
Checking", where failed refinements become inserted run-time checks. It
is the nearest case of the elaboration CLAIM-tmpnookt names and the
counterpart of CLAIM-tmpcmt21. Also, a claim that the record's own checks
(lint, review) are conservative procedures, so that a failed check
locates a fault in the entry, the vocabulary or the check. CLAIM-tmpcmt21
states it for liquid types only.
