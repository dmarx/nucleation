# LIT-3271: TinyLFU: A Highly Efficient Cache Admission Policy

Einziger, Friedman and Manes (2015), arXiv 1512.00727; ACM Transactions on
Storage (2017).

## Key takeaways

- Not read.

## Standing in the record

Admission as a decision apart from eviction: whether a newly accessed item
is worth a place in a bounded cache at the expense of the item it would
displace. The record has many rules for admitting an entry to the record
and none for its place in the working set; a reading would say whether the
frequency sketch TinyLFU uses has an analogue when the "accesses" are the
record's own citations, which A29 §8 warns are endogenous ([CLAIM-1368](../claims.d/CLAIM-1368.md)).
