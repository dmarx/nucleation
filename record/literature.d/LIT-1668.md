summary: >-
  Torres-Hugas et al. (2026), Communications Physics. For a phase model
  whose edges carry antisymmetric phase lags, the twisted Laplacian
  Lα = δα*δα has a zero mode exactly when every cycle holonomy is 0 mod
  2π; otherwise its smallest eigenvalue measures the residual
  incompatibility, largest on an isolated cycle at holonomy π. The
  symmetric Sakaguchi–Kuramoto model is a different model, but on cactus
  graphs its lag gives each ℓ-cycle a path mismatch (ℓ − 2)α that an
  associated connection is built to carry as holonomy. In the
  triangle–pentagon remote-synchronization motif the pentagon's mismatch
  reaches π at α = π/3, where the locked branch is an exact two-group
  state; the branch has already lost transverse stability at
  αc ≃ 1.0164, so the holonomy marks geometry, not stability.

# LIT-1668: Cycle holonomy captures higher-order compatibility constraints in remote synchronization

Torres-Hugas, L., Duch, J., Gómez, S., & Arenas, A. (2026). Cycle holonomy captures higher-order compatibility constraints in remote synchronization. *Communications Physics*. doi:10.1038/s42005-026-02914-y. Preprint: arXiv:2604.19682.

Read in full on 2026-10-27 ([NOTE-1218](../notes.d/NOTE-1218.md)).

## Why this is here

A21 §4 found it two weeks after publication and gave it "especially high
priority": a constraint that belongs to no edge but appears when
relations are composed around a cycle, with a spectral criterion and
dynamical consequences. A23 §V cited it for frustration as a third route
to organization. It bears on [CLAIM-471](../claims.d/CLAIM-471.md) and [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md).
