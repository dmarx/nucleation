**What started it.** Round 2's workbench (2026/10/12/002640) left the
THEORYs with no edge to any claim or question unassessed. The owner asked
for a pass over them.

**How.** A fresh export found 138 Active or Proposed THEORYs with no such
edge. They were split by primary tag into four batches, and an agent read
each batch. The agents were told to be strict: an edge needs something an
argument would use, a topical overlap is not enough, and an analogy of
the "same structure, not the same mechanism" kind should be rejected
([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)). Every proposed edge's quoted phrases were then checked against
both files before anything was written.

**What was filed.**
- 39 typed edges, 23 `grounds` on claims and 16 `answered_by` on
  questions. Each target carries a dated note saying what the THEORY
  contributes and its caveat: simulation only, by eye, workshops, a single
  step, and so on. Six edges type citations the prose already made
  ([CLAIM-068](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-068.md), [QUESTION-028](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-028.md), and [THEORY-056](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-056.md), 062 and 125/126 on round 2's
  tension questions).
- One counter, [CLAIM-tmprrjrd](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmprrjrd.md), objecting to [CLAIM-157](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) and [CLAIM-174](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-174.md). Two
  of the four agents reached it independently. [THEORY-071](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-071.md) against
  [CLAIM-170](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-170.md) came from one, and [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md)/073 against [CLAIM-157](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) from the
  other, which makes it the second occurrence. The claim: with the target
  fixed, a gap or coupling criterion cannot tell a boundary a system
  produces from one imposed on it.

**A trap avoided.** Several agents proposed "CLAIM grounds THEORY
(against)". The converse of `grounds` is `supports`, so the edge would
have told the record that the THEORY supports the claim. Four such edges
were written by `luria relate` and then removed before commit. What they
carried went into prose ([THEORY-096](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-096.md) and 063 on [CLAIM-003](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-003.md), [THEORY-101](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-101.md) on
[CLAIM-039](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)) or into the counter ([THEORY-071](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-071.md)). The record has no typed
"counts against" from a THEORY to a claim. An objection with an argument
behind it is a counter-claim.

**What was not linked.** 103 THEORYs got no edge. The agents named, for
most of them, the near miss they rejected: topical overlap, a different
object, or an analogy only. The largest unlinked groups are physics-internal
contextuality results ([THEORY-011](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-011.md), 015, 016, 037, 041, 042), learning theory
with no claim that would cite it, and consciousness science with no claim
on AI or animal subjects. Linking these would need new questions, not edges.

**Unmarked tensions found, not filed.**
- [THEORY-108](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-108.md) (phase III: independently trained solutions not connected)
  against [THEORY-109](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-109.md) (SGD's solutions lie in one connected low-loss set).
  108 limits 109 to some load/temperature regimes.
- [THEORY-102](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-102.md) against [THEORY-096](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-096.md). Prefrontal and parietal content is
  transient at onset, which undercuts "sustains" in ignition.
- [THEORY-090](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-090.md) against [THEORY-096](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-096.md). Consciousness before and without cortex
  is compatible with 096 only if 096 is read as limited to perception.
- [THEORY-153](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-153.md) (a distinct metasystem) against [THEORY-149](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-149.md) (durable
  governance without central control). This is not strict, since Beer's
  metasystem may be distributed. It is noted on [QUESTION-tmpebtmy](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpebtmy.md).

Each may deserve a `rivals` edge or a qualifying note on the THEORY. That
needs a reading of the sources, not a structural pass.
