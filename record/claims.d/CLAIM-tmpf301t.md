# CLAIM-tmpf301t: Flow is intrapersonal synchronization: when an activity's immediate reward and its longer-run value coincide, no interest has to be overridden, and that is why focused action in flow is reported as effortless

## The claim

Flow is described as absorption in an activity done for its own sake, when
challenge and skill are matched and feedback is immediate ([LIT-tmpwvter](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwvter.md)).
Read through [CLAIM-tmpmf12z](CLAIM-tmpmf12z.md), "for its own sake" is the important condition.
The activity pays continuously and at once, and it is also what the agent
values at a distance. So the reward that dominates at short delay and the
reward that dominates at long delay are the same reward, and the curves for
the competing alternatives (email, the phone) never overtake it within the
episode. The interests are synchronized ([TERM-tmpu28lt](../terms.d/TERM-tmpu28lt.md)).

That explains the phenomenology the literature stresses. On [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md),
control carries an intrinsic cost, and patience is a "default override". On
[THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md), will is bargaining that is needed only where curves cross. In
flow nothing is overridden and nothing is bargained, so neither cost is
paid. Effortless attention ([LIT-tmpjoaq1](../literature.d/LIT-tmpjoaq1.md)) is then not cheap control. It is
the absence of any need for control. The fall of reflective
self-consciousness also fits: with no contest among interests, the
self-monitoring that personal rules perform ("they continually take their
own pulse") has nothing to monitor.

[CASE-tmpoun8b](../cases.d/CASE-tmpoun8b.md) sets this beside the reluctant writer's two afternoons
([CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md)): one where avoidance wins, one where a device enforces the
commitment, and one where nothing has to.

## What it predicts

- **Flow ends by desynchronization.** It breaks when a sooner reward enters
  whose curve does cross: a notification. Getting back into the activity
  then needs one of the other routes, and it is felt as effort.
- **No reversal inside the episode.** A choice probe taken during flow
  between going on and a sooner alternative should show no reversal at
  short delay.
- **Low effort is low specification, not cheap control.** On an
  expected-value-of-control model, flow should show little control
  specified, not control specified at a low price.

## What it does not say

- **That flow is the best way to act as one.** It is available only where
  an activity's immediate reward and longer-run value happen to coincide.
  Most self-control is needed where they do not.
- **That flow involves no suppression.** Dietrich's transient
  hypofrontality ([LIT-tmpqpq9d](../literature.d/LIT-tmpqpq9d.md)) is a rival reading, in which one system
  stands down rather than all of them agreeing. The two may be compatible:
  a supervisor with nothing to arbitrate may idle. That is not argued here.
- **That "synchronization" names a dynamical mechanism.** See the
  objection [CLAIM-tmpigdl0](CLAIM-tmpigdl0.md).
