# LEAD-111: Construct the operational cover as a family of contexts with restriction maps, or give the operational notion its own name

## What it would do

Attempt the construction; failing it, name the operational notion and mark
which sense each entry uses.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md): "no construction of the operational cover as a family of
  contexts with restriction maps"

## What would count

Either settles [CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: worked, both horns met; the verdict on the counter is the owner's

Carried out in this session. The operational notion was both constructed as a
cover and given its own name, and the two findings pull opposite ways on
[CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md), by a modelling choice the record has not fixed.

- [CASE-368](../cases.d/CASE-368.md) (construction, with a numpy/pytest study at
  `scratch/studies/lead-111/`, ten tests passing): fix a scenario (X, M, O)
  with the event presheaf and its restriction maps; the operational cover is
  the determinacy functional D_θ(e) = {x : max_o m_x(o) ≥ θ} of the empirical
  model. The induced family on the determinable base is a genuine down-closed
  sub-cover with inherited restriction maps (horn 1); the selection is a
  thresholded, non-injective readout of the model on a fixed cover.
- [TERM-155](../terms.d/TERM-155.md) (Proposed): "determinacy cover", the name for the
  operational notion (horn 2), so each entry can say which sense of "cover" it
  uses.
- Two alternative readings, filed as [CLAIM-961](../claims.d/CLAIM-961.md) (full question set is
  the fixed scenario → the operational cover is a model functional, corroborates
  [CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md), rename due) and [CLAIM-962](../claims.d/CLAIM-962.md) (determinable
  questions are the operational domain → the induced sub-site is the
  sheaf-theoretic link [CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md) asked for, objects to [CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md),
  corroborates [CLAIM-201](../claims.d/CLAIM-201.md)).

What remains: the owner chooses between the two readings (which settles
[CLAIM-251](../claims.d/CLAIM-251.md)), adopts or declines [TERM-155](../terms.d/TERM-155.md), and decides
whether to rename "cover" to the term across [CLAIM-201](../claims.d/CLAIM-201.md), [CLAIM-257](../claims.d/CLAIM-257.md),
[CLAIM-256](../claims.d/CLAIM-256.md) and [CLAIM-125](../claims.d/CLAIM-125.md). The lead's status and stage are left for the owner.
