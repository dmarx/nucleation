# THEORY-473: Scaled down to one organism, Hutchinson's niche, defined as the conditions that give that individual an expected lifetime reproductive success of at least one, is observed only as a point; the rest of it is counterfactual, reached by translocation in only a few dimensions and otherwise inferred from other individuals, so in practice it is the niche of a phenotype class, and its authors call it mostly metaphorical

## Source

Takola & Schielzeth (2022), [LIT-127](../literature.d/LIT-127.md) (*Biology & Philosophy* 37:25), read in
full in [NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md). The online table of 36 niche definitions was not read.

## What was actually shown

**The definition (p. 5).** The individualized niche is the range of
environmental conditions that gives a particular individual an expected
lifetime reproductive success of at least one surviving offspring, each
offspring counted 0.5 per parent in outcrossers. It is ecological: LRS is
the "currency of the phenotype-environment match", and inheritance and
relative fitness are excluded.

**Uniqueness (pp. 6–8).** At population level individuals are replicates,
so a niche is a hypervolume. At individual level the realized niche is a
point or small volume, and the potential niche is a space of outcomes that
did not happen. [NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md) rates this strong, since it follows from the
definitions (C2).

**The fixes offered.** Repeated measures with variance decomposition;
marginalizing phenotype-by-environment fitness functions across
individuals, treated as tokens of trait types; and experimental
translocation, which covers few dimensions and is bounded by lifespan. The
marginalization is shown only schematically (Fig. 2), with no data
([NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md), C3, weak).

**The boundary (pp. 14–16).** The boundary must be set by expected, not
realized, LRS, and the paper concedes there is "no empirical solution" for
separating an individual's LRS into its stochastic and deterministic parts
(p. 15). The isocline of 1 is preferred to 0 as a practical benchmark.

**The authors' verdict (p. 17).** "We see our concept mostly of a
metaphorical value." The abstract does not say this.

**What could have come out otherwise.** The paper could have estimated a
potential individualized niche for some real system. It does not, and it
does not claim to.

## What this does not say

- **That individual niches cannot be studied.** Realized, time-slice,
  prospective and trajectory-based niches are said to be quantifiable, and
  the potential niche to be partly inferable. The claim here is about what
  the "individual" in the estimate is.
- **That it is a rebranding of individual-differences research.** The
  paper distinguishes the two by the environment: the niche is a set of
  conditions, and the phenotype only mediates fit. [NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md) rates that
  distinction weak but does not reject it.
- **Anything about evolution.** Relative fitness and inheritance are
  excluded by design, so the account says nothing about how individualized
  niches evolve.
- **That prospective niches only shrink.** That claim (p. 10) assumes
  potentials are fixed at the zygote, and learning and reversible
  plasticity are not considered.
- **That the token/type tension is the paper's own finding.** The paper does
  not confront it explicitly. It is [NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md)'s reading of what
  marginalization across individuals implies.

## Connections

- Trappes ([LIT-169](../literature.d/LIT-169.md)) restricts the comparison of niche construction theory
  to population niches and leaves open whether an individualized
  *evolutionary* niche exists. This paper supplies only an ecological one,
  so her question stays open. Its E[LRS] ≥ 1 boundary is the individual twin
  of the persistence criterion she uses. The two papers do not cite each
  other ([NOTE-170](../notes.d/NOTE-170.md), corrections).
- Baedke et al. ([LIT-110](../literature.d/LIT-110.md)) name individualized niches as where experiential
  niche construction should meet selection. This is the object they would
  need.
- No other THEORY in the record states a niche concept.
