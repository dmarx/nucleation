## What the readings change

- **The supply is not neutral.** [CLAIM-597](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-597.md)'s supplied repair leaves the
  organization's own capacity alone. In every aid reading the supply
  lowers it, by the Samaritan's dilemma, by unused revenue machinery
  decaying, or by funding for form crowding out function.
- **What predicted lasting.** The Kenya summary names resources, capacity
  and motivation together, linkages formed early, and gradual exit in which
  participants operated independently before closure. Azam et al.'s
  optimal exit instead cuts aid up front, and their recipient is better off
  staying dependent.
- **Dated notes** go on [CLAIM-597](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-597.md), [CLAIM-566](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-566.md), [CLAIM-558](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-558.md), [QUESTION-398](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-398.md),

  dependency and self-sufficiency".

## The assistant's contributions

- **[CLAIM-tmpw4ai2](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw4ai2.md)** (demonstrated) connects [CLAIM-597](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-597.md) to the dissipative
  structures. Upkeep that a structure draws in through its own intact
  state, as a flame does, has an ignition threshold, a supply level below
  which it collapses, and an outage after which it does not relight.
  Supplied upkeep has none of the three. It gives [QUESTION-085](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-085.md) three
  signatures to look for in placement records.
- **[CLAIM-tmpoh07b](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpoh07b.md)** (demonstrated) models aid crowding out capacity that is
  learned by doing.
  - Azam et al. already have kept capacity falling with aid's share, and
    the claim credits them.
  - Not in their paper: recovery after exit grows linearly with how long aid
    did all the work, because work is capped by capacity. Aid used after
    the organization's own capacity leaves its learning untouched.
  - Azam et al. call the harmful rule "gap-filling", so the benign one is
    named capacity-first.
- **[CLAIM-tmpqa0qu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqa0qu.md)** (interpretive), after the owner remarked that assured
  help making recipients lose skill "sounds closely related to niche
  differentiation and specialization phenomena". The loss is the Black
  Queen's ([THEORY-1048](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1048.md)), favoured to the one who loses it. Whether it is
  dependency or division of labour is set by the supply: one-way supply
  from outside limits nothing and punishes withdrawal, while reciprocal
  loss, as in Buchnera ([THEORY-1103](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1103.md)), gives interdependence. So
  self-sufficiency may be better framed as reciprocity. A dated note goes
  on [QUESTION-277](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-277.md).
- **Reproduction.** scratch/formal/supplied_maintenance_toys.py asserts
  every number in both claims.

## The ecological-recovery readings

what differentiates transient support that induces dependency and is

wild and the ecological recovery literature as cases. Five readers went

**[CLAIM-tmpc3x0c](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpc3x0c.md)** (interpretive) is the assistant's answer. Transient
support can be withdrawn safely when three conditions hold:
- it starts a self-sustaining process on a flux that stays available;
- the threat it stood in for has gone;
- the supported party did the work throughout.

Support that does the work wears capacity down by disuse, by slack effort
and, fastest, by selection for the supported conditions. Which kind of
support it was shows only in persistence through a full turnover after
withdrawal. The claim also records what the readings do not support:
- thresholds and hysteresis are rare in the field;
- longer support is not always worse;
- the evidence is not strong.

The owner wrote: "probably wouldn't hurt to counterpoint some of this
"dependency" material with literature discussing e.g. the myth of the
lazy poor". They then suggested urban development and city planning. Ten

- **Interruption.** The container restarted during the coproduction

**What changed.** The household evidence contradicts slack effort as a
general route: cash barely changes work, and graduation packages raise
it. CLAIM-tmpc3x0c is revised.
- Its title adds a condition on size, after Balboni et al.'s asset
  threshold. Support too small decays without dependence.
- The supported party must be able to do the work, after Michaels et al.
- Inputs the party cannot produce are a standing part of the
  arrangement, after Evans and Watson.
- Persistence after withdrawal is necessary but not sufficient, since
  slow reversion can mimic it, after Kline and Moretti.
- The unit whose persistence counts must be the one the support was for,
  after Collins and Shester and LaVoice.

CLAIM-tmpqa0qu, CLAIM-tmpoh07b and CLAIM-tmpw4ai2 gain sections on the
new readings. Dated notes go on QUESTION-398 and CLAIM-597.
