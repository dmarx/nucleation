# WORK-004: The Self as a Recursive Ecology of Meaning and Influence

## About

The owner's manuscript idea, proposed 2026-10-13 from an exchange on
meaning operators: a self as a metastable, recursively composed
organization of processes that transform shared meaning and allocate
authority to one another by endorsement, with trust, specialization and
higher-level agency emerging rather than imposed; r/place as the
observable case, and a constitutive question kept apart from the
explanatory one

Until [ADR-067](../decisions.d/ADR-067.md) this work was the `works` vocabulary term `self-as-recursive-ecology`.
