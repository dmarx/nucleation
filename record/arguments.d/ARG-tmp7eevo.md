# ARG-tmp7eevo: The selection mechanism shown for loot-box economies transfers to news in its structure, demand read through an economic weight shapes supply, and does not transfer in what it weights or in what it predicts, since news is weighted by attention and an investigation's value is largely external and cannot be shown before it is found

## The inference

The loot-box case: a producer reading demand through revenue lets the most
responsive spenders set a shared design (CLAIM-tmpmz271). By analogy, a
newsroom reading demand through attention lets the most attention-getting
content set its allocation of reporting, and investigations lose
(CLAIM-tmpifp69).

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is it the respect the conclusion
  needs?** In both, an organization adapts its supply to an economically
  weighted reading of demand, and the adaptation feeds back into what it
  next observes (A33 §6: "In both, the product environment changes the
  behavior subsequently used to evaluate the product"). That is the
  respect the conclusion needs.
- **What relevant difference is there?** Three, all A33's. The weight
  falls on content and behaviour rather than on a minority of spenders.
  Much of an investigation's value goes to nonreaders, which the game
  case lacks. And demand for an undiscovered finding cannot be observed at
  all, so the newsroom's data are silent on it rather than biased.
- **What transfers, and what does not?** The structure transfers.
  CLAIM-tmpmz271's specific result, the marginal-response condition, does
  not: there is no design parameter whose revenue-optimal value a group
  sets. What replaces it is the case CASE-tmpd5eqf, in which the
  capability that would generate counter-evidence decays. That is a
  different mechanism, CLAIM-342's lock-in, not CLAIM-tmpmz271's
  weighting.

The evidence that the mechanism operates in news is observational and
unread (Bisiani and colleagues, LIT-tmp1bfvb; Hayes, LIT-tmpk0xvf), and
Christin (LIT-tmpw11wj) reports newsrooms that resist it.

## Verdict

Proposed. The analogy carries the owner's claim's structure and not its
causal strength; the claim's own defeater, the advertising-collapse rival,
is untouched by it.
