# LIT-191: AI and Consciousness

Eric Schwitzgebel (2025), *Cambridge University Press, Elements series (monograph); preprint on arXiv (cs.AI)* — [ARXIV-2510.09858](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09858)

## Key takeaways

- We will soon build AI systems that are conscious by some mainstream theories and not by others, and none of the standard arguments for or against AI consciousness will let us know which — so we face deep, possibly lasting uncertainty.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The book is a skeptical overview of the literature on AI consciousness. Schwitzgebel predicts we will soon create AI systems that some influential mainstream theories count as conscious and others do not. We will not be in a position to know which theories are right, so we cannot tell whether we are surrounded by richly conscious systems or by systems as experientially blank as toasters. He argues that none of the standard arguments on either side takes us far.
