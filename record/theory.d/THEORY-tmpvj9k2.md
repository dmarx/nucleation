# THEORY-tmpvj9k2: Finn (2023) argues that when an organism of the gestating organism's kind begins and whether the foster is a proper part of or merely contained by the gestating organism are independent questions, connected only by a Maximality principle that no proper part of an F is an F, which needs independent justification; without it, parthood and containment, together with overlap and an underlap view on which the foster is both part and contained, each combine with any answer about timing.

## The account

Finn (2023) argues that when an organism of the gestating organism's kind begins and whether the foster is a proper part of or merely contained by the gestating organism are independent questions, connected only by a Maximality principle that no proper part of an F is an F, which needs independent justification; without it, parthood and containment, together with overlap and an underlap view on which the foster is both part and contained, each combine with any answer about timing.
