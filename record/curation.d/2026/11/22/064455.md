## What the readings changed

- **CLAIM-795** now rests on readings for items 4, 5 and 6. Item 4 is narrowed: a mixture gives a power law only for spreads with weight at zero rate, so slow members, not heterogeneity as such, make the tail. Item 6 holds as Reed and Hughes's noise-free limit; with noise the law is a double Pareto.
- **CLAIM-817's** kernel regimes rest on Krapivsky et al.; the boundary exponent is 3 only for A_k = k, and asymptotically linear kernels give any exponent above 2. Its naming clause fails: Merton's Matthew effect is credit allotted by rank for a given contribution, not linear attachment.
- **CLAIM-824** rests on the theorem's own conditions, at second hand: log-contraction is required, the additive and reflecting floors share one exponent equation (Goldie), and a Pareto tail can come from the additive term alone (Grey).
- **QUESTION-505** gains a general form: with noise, the exponent is the moment whose growth balances the loss rate (Reed and Hughes; Kesten), and "ratio of rates" holds only without noise.
- **QUESTION-508**: "slowly driven", time-scale separation and conservation are later framing; the founding letter's answer is marginal stability in d ≥ 2.
- **CLAIM-841**: the founding letter infers criticality from a straight log-log line, the inference the claim doubts; the record's simulation supplies the size check the letter asserted without data.
