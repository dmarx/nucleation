# ARG-251: Chubby and two-term norm → reliance on restraint

## The inference

1. A provider whose realized reliability exceeds its declared guarantee
   can induce dependents to drop fallbacks ([CLAIM-1256](../claims.d/CLAIM-1256.md), from
   [CASE-424](../cases.d/CASE-424.md)).
2. An institution's restraint under a convention is a realized regularity
   that exceeds what law guarantees, "The legally permissible behavior of
   an institution can become substantially broader than the behavior other
   institutions ordinarily expect it to exhibit" (A36 §3).
3. Other institutions "can then develop their own capacities around the
   expectation of restraint" (A36 §3).
4. So institutions can come to depend on the restraint beyond what law
   guarantees, and a lawful departure defeats what was built on it.

## Critical questions

**In what respect are they alike, and is it the respect the conclusion
needs?** In the gap between declared and realized regularity, and in
dependents organizing around the realized one. That is the respect
[CLAIM-1343](../claims.d/CLAIM-1343.md) needs.

**What relevant difference is there?** Three. A service's dependents are
designed artefacts whose fallbacks can be audited; an institution's
"alternatives" to another's restraint are not named in A36, and may be
nothing more definite than unused legal powers or vigilance. A service's
provider declares its guarantee; a convention's "declared" level is the
law, which no one declared as a reliability target. And the provider
chooses to exceed its guarantee or not; an institution's restraint is
chosen, case by case, by actors whose choices are what is at issue.

**What transfers, and what does not?** The dependence transfers. The
remedy does not: SRE keeps reliance honest by synthesizing outages, and
no one can synthesize breaches of a convention, nor should. The polity's
remedy in [CASE-425](../cases.d/CASE-425.md) ran the other way, raising the guarantee to the
realized level by amendment. So the analogy supports the vulnerability
and leaves the response open; it supports nothing about which response
is right.

## Verdict

Proposed. It carries the conclusion's vulnerability half, at the
strength of the premise (one specimen), and with the first difference
unresolved: until an institution's lost alternative is identified in a
real case, the step rests on a resemblance of structure.
