# LEAD-tmpu1jj5: Test formation and maintenance provenance for a maintained object richer than a scalar capacity, first in artificial agents, with interventions on the maintaining mechanism inside a declared boundary and dispositional models as the rival

## What it would do

Three arms, with the object a perspective or another maintained
organization richer than a scalar, in artificial agents first:

1. **Boundary declared in advance**, with the maintaining mechanism named.
2. **Maintenance provenance**: knock out the maintaining mechanism after
   formation and see whether the organization decays (CLAIM-522).
3. **Formation provenance**: intervene during formation, or keep a record
   then (TERM-100, CASE-191), since after formation the two provenances
   cannot be told apart.

Interventions on state alone are the control. Withdrawal is observed over
a horizon fixed in advance.

## Expectations, and the rival

If the organization is maintained by the declared mechanism, the knockout
in arm 2 degrades it while state interventions do not. The rival is a
dispositional model, in the habitus sense, in which the organization
persists without any mechanism inside the boundary maintaining it. The
rival predicts no difference between arm 2 and the control.

## Relation to other leads

CASE-148 transplants memes; LEAD-tmpiktom intervenes on mechanisms for
memes (its step 4). Neither declares the boundary for a perspective.
