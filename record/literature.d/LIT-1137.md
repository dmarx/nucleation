Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1400](../notes.d/NOTE-1400.md)). One account was drawn from it: [THEORY-1163](../theory.d/THEORY-1163.md).

## Why this is here

A10 and A11 of the exchange of 2026-10-16 put it first for the individuation problem. The record held Bourrat's critique of its measures ([LIT-005](LIT-005.md), [NOTE-027](../notes.d/NOTE-027.md)) without the paper itself. Bears on [QUESTION-115](../questions.d/QUESTION-115.md) and [CASE-101](../cases.d/CASE-101.md). A reading should check A11's claim that A* and A do not decrease as the candidate is enlarged, and the size cost the paper says is needed.
