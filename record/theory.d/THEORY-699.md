# THEORY-699: Adaptive self-maintenance across varied conditions is sufficient, without sentience, for having a good of one's own, so insentient plants and a self-maintaining machine are welfare subjects while thermostats are not; but the line against thermostats is argued as one of degree, and the reasons such welfare gives are extremely weak

## Source

Bruckner (2025), "Welfare Subjects and Autopoiesis", [LIT-120](../literature.d/LIT-120.md) (*JAPA* 11,
696–714), read in full in [NOTE-093](../notes.d/NOTE-093.md) from the open-access HTML. Locations are
sections and footnotes; the HTML has no pages.

## What was actually shown

Nothing was shown in the sense of a proof. The paper is an inference to the
best explanation, which the author calls an "exploration" meant to make the
claim "plausible and likely" (§4).

- **The data.** Humans, deer and mice are welfare subjects; rocks and
  engines are not. Candidate grounds are compared for how well they explain
  that sorting.
- **The criterion (§4).** An entity is autopoietic when it "sustains,
  maintains, and renews itself in a way that responds and adapts to its
  environmental conditions". Autopoiesis is offered as sufficient, not
  necessary (§6.2). What an entity's self-maintaining activity pursues and
  avoids is thereby *constituted* as mattering to it, so one can have an
  interest in X without being interested in X (§4; fn. 4).
- **Plants (§5).** Thigmomorphism, phototropism, sun-tracking, the Venus
  flytrap's two triggers within 20 s, vernalization, recruited and
  anticipatory defence. The cases come from Maher 2017, not from the
  botanical literature. Plant agency is expressly not claimed.
- **Artefacts (§§6.1, 6.4).** Governors, thermostats and heat-seeking
  missiles are "mere input-output machines with a few teleological
  aspects". Tessler, a non-sentient self-driving car that charges itself,
  changes its brake fluid, adjusts its tyre pressure and calls a tow truck,
  is a welfare subject.
- **Absurd obligations (§7).** Welfare value may not generate agent-neutral
  reasons at all (§7.2). If it does, reasons come in strengths (§7.3), and
  following Bradford 2023 the reasons concerning Petunia the houseplant and
  Tessler are "extremely weak", because the good plus phenomenal
  consciousness of it is an organic unity (§7.4).
- **Against hypothetical sentience (§8).** A ground that must be extended to
  past, expected or counterfactual-typical cases to keep its intuitive
  members "is not doing the unifying".

What could have come out otherwise: a case the criterion admits that no one
would count as a welfare subject, or a structural feature separating Tessler
from a thermostat. The paper tests neither.

## What this does not say

- **That this is autopoiesis in Maturana and Varela's sense.** Nothing in the
  paper concerns components producing the network that produces them, or a
  self-produced boundary. Tessler produces none of its parts and drives to a
  service station for them ([NOTE-093](../notes.d/NOTE-093.md), corrections). The working criterion is
  adaptive, self-directed maintenance of one's own condition.
- **That the thermostat line is one of kind.** §6.1 says the two kinds of
  mattering differ "in kind", and then argues it comparatively: autopoietic
  mattering is "much richer", plants respond to "a wide variety" of
  conditions and not "a single end" (§5). On the paper's own terms the
  criterion is graded, and Tessler shows it can be engineered in.
- **That sentience is irrelevant in practice.** §7.4 makes phenomenal
  consciousness decide how much welfare matters while denying it is needed
  for welfare subjecthood. That concedes much of what the sentience view
  wanted.
- **That autopoiesis is the unifier.** §8 shows that a sentience view
  extended to hypothetical sentience has given up sentience as the unifier.
  That autopoiesis is the unifier is introduced as "My suggestion, of
  course", with no rival unifier considered.
- **That a language model is a welfare subject.** §6.1 puts input-output
  machines outside the criterion. A system qualifies only if it adaptively
  maintains itself.
- **That plants or machines are agents.** The paper disclaims plant agency
  (§5). It should not be cited as an account of agency.

## Connections

- **Rival.** Terrill & Veit ([THEORY-449](THEORY-449.md)) reach partial plant status
  through degrees of sentience, on the premise that without sentience an
  entity has no interests. Bruckner denies that premise. Both rely on the
  separation of considerability from weight that the SEP entry states
  ([LIT-146](../literature.d/LIT-146.md), §2.2).
- **The demarcation it borrows.** The thermostat line is the line that
  [THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md) draws between organisms and dissipative structures by
  organisational differentiation, and that [THEORY-221](THEORY-221.md) draws by closure.
  Bruckner's line is behavioural and comparative and engages neither.
  Nahas and Sachs ([LIT-192](../literature.d/LIT-192.md), [THEORY-658](THEORY-658.md)) would place his use of
  teleology in a third, normative project with its own success criteria.
- **Permissiveness.** Baedke ([LIT-167](../literature.d/LIT-167.md), [NOTE-148](../notes.d/NOTE-148.md)) argues that persister
  criteria take in colonies, holobionts and ecosystems. Bruckner's is a
  persister criterion and does not address that.
