# CLAIM-tmpmgb7l: The crossed intervention on a small conventionally trained network does not meet the condition that would defeat the claim that a condition of training is not thereby a condition of the network, and corroborates it: the optimizer arm moved the response in every population, and what the learned state adds the claim already allows

## The claim

[CLAIM-367](CLAIM-367.md)'s defeat condition is a conjunction: the optimizer arm
null *and* the learned-state arm not. The run ([CLAIM-tmp60fme](CLAIM-tmp60fme.md)) found
the optimizer arm not null in either trained population, at matched pace
and with the network's parameters exactly fixed. The conjunction fails
at its first limb, whatever the second does.

It corroborates rather than merely failing to defeat, because the
optimizer's effect is of the kind [CLAIM-367](CLAIM-367.md) predicts: how much of
task A a network gives up for task B is not settled by the network, since
the same parameters give up different amounts under different update
rules, and under the same rule with and without its carried state. The
outcome of a conflict in training belongs at least to the
network–optimizer pair.

The learned-state result is the claim's own allowance: "It does not say a
network cannot come to hold it". A learned task separation that changes
how conflict spreads is structure the network holds, within the system
the claim names.

## What it does not say

- That the optimizer holds *most* of the governance in this network. The
  shares varied with the training optimizer, and for one population the
  learned state's share was the larger. The claim's "most" is not tested
  by a share of variance in one small grid, and this reading does not
  lean on it.
- That a network with an internal adaptive controller would come out the
  same way; [CLAIM-367](CLAIM-367.md) excludes those from its defeat condition.

## Alternative

[CLAIM-tmpyf8s0](CLAIM-tmpyf8s0.md) reads the same run as showing that the regulation of
conflict is shared between network and optimizer in proportions the
training fixes, against [CLAIM-367](CLAIM-367.md)'s "most". The owner chooses.
