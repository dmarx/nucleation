# LIT-tmpqbo3w: The relationship between addiction and reward bundling: an experiment comparing smokers and non-smokers

Andre Hofmeyr, George W. Ainslie, Richard Charlton and Don Ross (2011). *Addiction* 106(2): 402-409. DOI-10.1111/j.1360-0443.2010.03166.x

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* By the abstract as the UCC research portal gives it: a
sequence of four choices two weeks apart; three conditions (independent,
suggested series, forced series); smokers increased their preference for
larger later rewards when decisions were bundled into a sequence, and
with repeated experience; non-smokers showed neither pattern. Whether the
suggested condition alone moved smokers, and by how much, is unread.
