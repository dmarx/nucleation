**A fix that did not hold.** After Part II, the devlog note of 03:30
put the step-3a miss down to searching by the article's words, and Part
III's digest brief told each digester to search by mechanism, with its
search terms listed. The digests did so, and the review still found held
work missed in four places, two of them central to the drafts' "not done"
verdicts. The hidden-subsidy loop's budget half was run ([CASE-412](../../record/cases.d/CASE-412.md),
[CASE-343](../../record/cases.d/CASE-343.md) with [CLAIM-921](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-921.md), all Active). The seed's withdrawal is covered in
assembly models ([CASE-308](../../record/cases.d/CASE-308.md), [CASE-365](../../record/cases.d/CASE-365.md), [CASE-389](../../record/cases.d/CASE-389.md), [CASE-374](../../record/cases.d/CASE-374.md)). A study of real
organizations ([CASE-411](../../record/cases.d/CASE-411.md) with [CLAIM-1158](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1158.md)) contradicts Article 7's "no study
of real people". The points-of-contact search found more: [CLAIM-1064](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1064.md) for
Article 8's repair section, and the rival literatures Article 9 says the
record has "read almost none" of ([THEORY-1979](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1979.md), 1985, 1960, 152, 1918).
So the wrong theory was that the search terms were the problem. A
digester searches from what it already expects the article to need. The
review's searchers, given a region and no draft to defend, found what the
digesters did not. A search of held results is better run as its own
region-by-region job before drafting, by readers who are not drafting,
than as a section of each digest.

**A process error, found by the reviewer.** The drafts' joins were
filed as dated notes (commit 39a0a7dc) before the [ADR-075](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-075.md) reader had
tested them. The reviewer pointed out that this makes the join test's
question "is it stated by an entry?" circular, since the entry stating it
is the drafter's own note. The test then found two of those notes wrong
(on [CLAIM-tmphq6hp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmphq6hp.md) and [CLAIM-741](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-741.md)). Skill step 6 sends joins to the
accounts, after the test, not to notes on components before it. Gaps can
be filed at drafting; joins should wait for their verdicts.

**Going right.** The joins reader rechecked the one computation that
passed ([CLAIM-tmp6nzs1](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6nzs1.md)) from the claim's stated model rather than from
the script, and matched every figure to three decimals. That is the
second-session check a demonstrated claim needs, and it came from
the review at no extra cost.
