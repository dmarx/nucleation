summary: >-
  Gelfand & Neumark (1943), DOI-10.1090/conm/167/16. Theorem 1 is the
  first abstract characterisation of C*-algebras. Every normed *-ring (a
  unital Banach *-algebra with ‖x*x‖ = ‖x*‖·‖x‖, ‖x*‖ = ‖x‖, and e + x*x
  invertible) is isometrically *-isomorphic to a norm-closed *-subalgebra
  of the bounded operators on some, not necessarily separable, Hilbert
  space. The proof builds, for each maximal left ideal M, a positive
  functional f vanishing on M. The inner product is (ξ, η) = f(y*x) on
  R/M, and the construction takes the direct sum over all M. This is the
  GNS construction. Lemma 1 is the commutative case: such an algebra is
  isometrically *-isomorphic to C(𝔐) for its compact maximal-ideal space.
  §4 adds that the simple weakly closed rings on separable Hilbert space
  are exactly the factors of types I_n, II₁ and III (Theorem 3).
