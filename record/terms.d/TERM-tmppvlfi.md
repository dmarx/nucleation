# TERM-tmppvlfi: harmonious integration, of a plurality of constituents over a horizon: viability, accommodation and correctability together, as distinct from coexistence, stability or agreement

## Definition

For a plurality S of declared constituents (perspectives, members,
species, firms) over a horizon H, A24 §4:

- **Viability_H(S)**: the relevant constituents can persist. In
  CLAIM-554's form, the histories the organization can produce over H meet
  every constituent's persistence requirement at once.
- **Accommodation_H(S)**: their interactions "sustain compatible activity
  without requiring their differences to disappear".
- **Correctability_H(S)**: "a constituent can still make a consequential
  intervention when the organization needs to change".

Harmony is the conjunction. A24: "Harmony, on this account, is not the
elimination of opposition. It is the organization of conditions under
which differences can coexist productively without losing their capacity
to matter."

## What it is not

- **Coexistence.** Negative frequency dependence can keep two types
  together without making their interaction cooperative (CLAIM-546).
- **Agreement.** Accommodation holds among constituents that disagree,
  as practical synchronization does among regimes whose valuations differ
  (TERM-061).
- **Stability.** "A team might look well integrated because one dominant
  perspective has made the others quiet" (A24). That organization can be
  viable and accommodating and fail correctability.
- **Relief of frustration.** Relief enlarges what can be jointly
  realized, under fixed demands (CLAIM-554, CLAIM-361); harmony is a state
  of the plurality, which relief may bring about.

## What would settle it

Adoption needs each conjunct operationalized for a declared plurality and
horizon: viability by CLAIM-347's compatibility complex, correctability
by an intervention test such as recovery when rare (QUESTION-132) or
CLAIM-362's access to consequential roles, accommodation by a measure
not reducible to either.
