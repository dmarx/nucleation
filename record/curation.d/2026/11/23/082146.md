The owner asked on 2026-11-23 to "get started on that membership pass".
It is the pass that [ADR-143](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-143.md) and the write-manuscript skill require
once a draft takes over a work. The work governance-of-possibility
([ADR-144](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-144.md)) was defined on A48, ChatGPT's draft. The revised
manuscript in `scratch/manuscripts/governance-of-possibility/` fleshes
A48 out and cites 170 claims and arguments, against A48's 37 members.
The pass reads that manuscript as the work's successor draft. Whether a
successor draft is the same work is the owner's ruling (the skill, and
[ADR-135](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-135.md)'s precedent), so this pull request waits on the owner's merge.

## Method

Five readers each took a group of files: 00–01, 02, 03–05, 06 with 08,
and 07 with 09. Each read every passage citing a CLAIM or ARG and the
entry itself, and gave one verdict per code under [ADR-104](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-104.md)'s rule:

- **member**: the text states the proposition, in any wording, or uses
  it as a step;
- **cite**: the text only points to it, lists it or places it in a
  table;
- **proviso**: the text uses only a proviso or a reason the entry gives;
- **stated-rejected**: the text states it only to set it aside.

The brief stated the rule and the verdicts neutrally. A code took its
strongest verdict across the files.

## Result

Of the 170 codes:

- **158 are members.** 35 of A48's 37 are among them. 116 are added
  here, each with `works` and a history note giving its sections. Seven
  more are listed below and not added.
- **5 are stated only to be set aside.** Two of them are A48 members.
- **2 use only a proviso.**
- **5 are cited only.**

No member of A48 drops to cite.
