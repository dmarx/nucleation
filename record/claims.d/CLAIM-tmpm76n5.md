# CLAIM-tmpm76n5: An ambiguity becomes consequential only when a case first gives its readings different consequences, so a misunderstanding dates the distinction's relevance rather than its existence, and clarifying it can coarsen as well as refine: readings found to agree on every case that matters are merged

## When an ambiguity becomes consequential

Two parties use an expression with readings R₁ and R₂. On every case they
have met, R₁ and R₂ prescribe the same response, so no test they have run
tells the readings apart (CLAIM-302: equivalence under feasible tests can
be much coarser than identity). The ambiguity is latent. It becomes
consequential on the first case where R₁ and R₂ prescribe different
responses, and the misunderstanding is the parties' discovering that case.

So the owner's "novel" has a precise sense here. The readings may have
existed all along, even in one party's head. What is new is a case that
makes their difference matter. Detection is dated by that case, not by the
expression.

Three origins should be kept apart, because they call for different
reorganizations:

1. one party already drew the distinction and the other did not: the
   misunderstanding transfers it, as translation does;
2. neither party drew it, and the separating case creates it for both:
   the strict sense of a novel distinction;
3. the readings differ only verbally, and no case will ever separate them.

## Clarification can coarsen

Clarifying an ambiguity is recalibrating the parties' partition to the
consequences that cases have shown. Where readings diverge in
consequence, the partition is refined: the owner's case. Where a
difference the parties disputed turns out to change nothing they care
about, the readings are merged. That is CLAIM-762's mechanism, in which
collapsing differences that do not matter is how the remaining classes
become units.

A misunderstanding that ends in "we meant the same thing after all" is
then productive too. It removes a distinction the parties were paying to
maintain.

## What follows for the owner's thesis

The thesis holds for refinement, and needs a companion clause for
coarsening. Read together they say that clarifying an ambiguity aligns the
parties' joint partition with the distinctions that cases have made
consequential, in either direction. "Productive" then has a test borrowed
from CLAIM-762: the clarified partition is reused, and is closed under the
parties' later dealings.
