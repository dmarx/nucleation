# LIT-tmptlpr8: Five Things We Need to Know About Technological Change

Neil Postman (1998). "Five Things We Need to Know About Technological Change". Talk, Denver, Colorado, 28 March 1998. Copy on a UC Davis course page.

## Key takeaways

- "The consequences of technological change are always vast, often unpredictable and largely irreversible."
- Mythic technologies are "not easily susceptible to modification or control".
