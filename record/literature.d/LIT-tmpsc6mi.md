# LIT-tmpsc6mi: Split-Brain: What We Know Now and Why This is Important for Understanding Consciousness

de Haan et al. (2020), Neuropsychology Review 30(2): 224-233. DOI 10.1007/s11065-020-09439-3.

## Key takeaways

- "callosotomy leads to a broad breakdown of functional integration
  ranging from perception to attention", but "several processes, such as
  action control, seem to remain unified".
- Split-brain patients detect and localise stimuli anywhere in the field,
  with either hand and verbally; identity and shape are mostly not
  compared across the midline.
- Whether consciousness is split is open: "The current consensus is that
  the body of evidence is insufficient to answer this question".
