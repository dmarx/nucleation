## Why this is here

A25: it "extends the information-design viewpoint beyond one sender and receiver to strategic interaction among multiple agents. Particularly relevant to reflexive control and institutionally mediated persuasion." A26 §4 used it for control over what is known. The general frame for the record's held information-design work, [LIT-1021](LIT-1021.md) (Kamenica & Gentzkow) and [THEORY-404](../theory.d/THEORY-404.md).
