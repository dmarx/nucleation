# LIT-tmp3h572: Keep Your Options Open: An Information-Based Driving Principle for Sensorimotor Systems

Klyubin, A. S., Polani, D., Nehaniv, C. L. (2008). Keep Your Options Open: An Information-Based Driving Principle for Sensorimotor Systems. *PLoS ONE, 3(12), e4018*. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0004018.

Read in full on 2026-11-03 (NOTE-tmpgoetm; THEORY-tmpuw92f, THEORY-tmpoy5a7).

## Key takeaways

- Contextual empowerment ≥ context-free (eq. 3): state ≥ own history ≥
  bounded automaton ≥ none.
- Cart-pole: the empowerment maxima are off-centre and unreachable; greedy
  maximization still balances the pole (pp. 4–7).
- The horizon is "the main free variable" (p. 12).

## Standing in the record

Empowerment as channel capacity from actions to later sensor states, which A21 reads as controllability and observability together. Bears on [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md).
