# TERM-tmp6c38c: concept, as realized by an organization: a reusable organization of distinctions together with their inferential or operational consequences, applicable across a stated range of contexts and open to correction by what it is applied to; distinct from the feature or representation that carries it, from the organization that realizes it, and from what it is about

## Definition

A concept is a pattern of distinctions an organization draws, together with
what follows from drawing them: the inferences, actions or further
distinctions it licenses. Three marks separate it from a discriminator that
merely sorts:

- **Reuse.** The same distinctions serve across a stated range of
  contexts and tasks, not one.
- **Counterfactual reach.** They apply to cases the organization has not
  met, in the way they apply to those it has.
- **Correctability.** Their application can be found wrong by what they
  are applied to, and revised.

"Concept" names a role an organization's distinctions play, not a kind of
substance. A5: "a reusable, corrigible organization of distinctions", and
"Being an effective constraint is not sufficient for being conceptual."

## What it is not

- **Not the feature or representation that carries it.** A feature, in the
  sense of [THEORY-965](../theory.d/THEORY-965.md), is a unit a model computes with. A concept may be
  carried by many features, or by none in particular. "A change in the
  local learning coefficient is not itself concept formation" (A4).
- **Not the organization that realizes it.** Two organizations can realize
  one concept, as a communicative kind has many tokens ([TERM-040](TERM-040.md)).
- **Not its object.** A concept is about something it is not. Where it
  partly constitutes its object, as the perceiver's concepts group emotion
  categories ([THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md)) or status functions make a legal person
  ([THEORY-625](../theory.d/THEORY-625.md)), that is the constitutive sense of individuation
  ([TERM-088](TERM-088.md)), and it must be shown case by case.
- **Not a thing or entity.** "Thing" is an individuated organization;
  "entity" (A6) is anything treated as a distinct bearer of properties in a
  domain. A concept can be an entity in a theory without being a thing.

## What would settle it

Whether reuse, counterfactual reach and correctability arrive together or
apart in a learning system ([QUESTION-tmp181oz](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp181oz.md)). If they come apart, the term may need to
split, and a definition fixed now would hide that.
