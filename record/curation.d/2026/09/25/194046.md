The anthology's reading-time triage (its curation entry of 2026-09-25)
set aside 34 works, about 14.4 hours of reading, that no anthology topic could
hold. The owner kept that record's topic decision and made this repository
the catchall for what it leaves out. This entry records how the vocabulary
was seeded, so the first filings can test it.

## The seed

Each of the nine topics is one of the triage's clusters, renamed to say what
the cluster is about rather than how it was found:

| topic | the cluster it came from | works | reading time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `quantum-foundations` | quantum foundations | 7 | ~2 h |
| `mathematics` | the maths neighbours of that cluster (spectral sets, Betti Lagrangians, topoi) | 3 | ~40 min |
| `network-science` | network science | 3 | 2.4 h |
| `complex-systems` | emergence and information decomposition | 5 | 1.35 h |
| `probabilistic-modeling` | Bayesian nonparametrics | 2 | 1.0 h |
| `neuroscience` | the cortical-representations paper | 1 | 15 min |
| `natural-sciences` | planetary, toxicology, concussion | 3 | ~4 h |
| `operations-research` | vehicle routing and facility location | 1 | 31 min |
| `society-and-governance` | law, politics, security | 8 | 2.0 h |

## What the seed does not settle

- **Filing depth.** Nothing is filed yet. A NOTE's `Read` and `Skimmed`
  statuses already say how deeply a work was read. Each filing batch should
  say which it did, here.
- **Non-papers.** Eight of the 34 are court filings, doctrine, reports or
  opinion pieces. A LIT can carry a `url:`. Whether this record wants them is
  the owner's call, and it should be made once, in writing.
- **Boundary cases.** Four works may fit anthology topics after all:
  *Language Design as Information Renormalization* (`signal-structure`), the
  cortical-representations paper (`concept-geometry`), *Topos and Stacks of
  Deep Neural Networks* (`analysis-and-evaluation`) and AgentSociety
  (`deployment-and-society`). Each should be placed in one record or the
  other, never in both.
