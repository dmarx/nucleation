# QUESTION-264: Under what conditions do the organization that determines what counts as meaningful, the organization that determines what becomes effective in action, and the organization that reproduces either one through time coincide, diverge, and reorganize one another?

## Why it is a question

[CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md) says perspectival, agential and institutional boundaries need
not coincide. It does not say when they do. A3 proposed that question as
the manuscript's research problem, because it "offer[s] a way to make the
mismatch among interpretive, agential, and historical boundaries the
explanandum rather than merely acknowledging that such boundaries
differ." It names the three processes A3 said the framework's distinctive
opportunity lies in coupling: the organization of significance, of
authority and of persistence.

A3's example: an organization that has learned to dismiss certain safety
objections has a problem of interpretation (the objections stop
registering), of authority (they cannot alter decisions) and of
persistence (the machinery keeps reproducing both). [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) is the
record's documented case, with [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md)'s separation of a corrective act,
a procedure and a maintained capacity.

It refines [QUESTION-259](QUESTION-259.md), which asks how perspectives are generated and
reproduced and when one becomes the object of its own governance, by
naming the three organizations whose alignment both halves depend on.

## What would count as an answer

Conditions, in a stated class of systems, under which the three coincide,
come apart, or one reorganizes another, with a case of each. By
[CLAIM-427](../claims.d/CLAIM-427.md), the answer should predict something under intervention
that an account treating the three processes separately does not.
[CASE-144](../cases.d/CASE-144.md) is a proposed test.

## What would not answer it

A redescription of a case in which the boundaries differ. A3: "bringing
these dimensions together does not yet establish that the integration is
explanatorily necessary."
