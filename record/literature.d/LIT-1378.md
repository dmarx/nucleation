# LIT-1378: NASA: Better Mechanisms Needed for Sharing Lessons Learned

U.S. General Accounting Office (2002). *NASA: Better Mechanisms Needed for Sharing Lessons Learned*. GAO-02-195, 30 January 2002. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-02-195.

Read in full on 2026-10-22 in [NOTE-1088](../notes.d/NOTE-1088.md).

## Why this is here

A25: "A correction is recorded ⇏ The corrected capacity is maintained." This is the report it leaned on. Bears on [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) and [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md).

**Corrected 2026-10-22, from the reading.** The report does not concern
Challenger or the corrections made after it. It was prompted by the
losses of the Mars Climate Orbiter and Mars Polar Lander in 1999, and it
audits the Lessons Learned Information System, created in 1995;
Challenger appears only as one column of a RAND table. What it shows is
a written requirement to apply lessons, practice that did not follow,
and an agency that measured searches of its database and not the
application of lessons. That supports [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md)'s separation of
procedure from capacity, as a recorded lesson not applied, rather than
A25's reading of a correction that held and then decayed. In [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md) it
is evidence of the same gap at the same agency before Columbia, not of
the post-Challenger reforms eroding.
