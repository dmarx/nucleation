# CLAIM-762: Collapsing differences that do not matter into equivalence classes is how those classes come to be reified as new distinctions: where a process identifies its microstates by what they do, the classes it keeps, and not the microstates, become the units of further computation and organization; the claim is substantive only where the classes are closed under the system's own operations and are reused

## The claim

The owner, at U10 of the exchange "Topological protection explained"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afe6dd0-9d78-83ea-9782-f55680665dcf), quoting
A's sentence that "the emergence of structure can involve both the
creation of new distinctions and the collapse of irrelevant distinctions
into equivalence classes":

> My understanding (perhaps I should call this a hypothesis) is that the collapse of irrelevant distinctions into equivalence classes is the mechanism that underlies reification of those equivalence classes into useful new distinctions.

A's sentence treats the two as separate routes. The owner's hypothesis
makes the first the mechanism of the second: a new distinction becomes
available as a unit because the differences within it stopped mattering.
In the owner's example, films that draw the same audiences become
interchangeable for the purposes of the engagement process, and the class
they form, a genre, becomes something the platform, its users and its
recommenders can act on ([CASE-296](../cases.d/CASE-296.md)).

## What makes it more than a partition

A24 §6 put the objection: "any equivalence relation partitions a set, so
of course collapsing distinctions creates classes distinguishable from
other classes." The claim is filed with the condition that answers it.
The classes that become units are those the system's own operations
respect, a congruence in which the quotient map commutes with the
dynamics, q∘F = F̄∘q ([THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md)'s closure, A26 §3), and that further
operations reuse. [CLAIM-154](CLAIM-154.md) states when such a unit is worth naming, and
[TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s concept is a reusable, consequential pattern of distinctions.
A26 §2's textbook case is the minimal automaton: histories identified by
their futures (Myhill–Nerode) become its states, and the transitions are
well defined on the classes.

## Its completion (A's)

A29 §5: "Selective contraction alone explains how distinctions disappear.
Bifurcation and other differentiation mechanisms explain why several
distinct organizational outcomes remain available." In a pitchfork the
basins are told apart by the bifurcation and their members are identified
by contraction within each ([CLAIM-408](CLAIM-408.md), [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md)). The owner's mechanism
is the second half. The record already had both halves in [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md)'s
last clause, where modes slowing near an instability become order
parameters.

## Rests on

- [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md) (the owner's): a relation settled at one scale works as a
  constraint at another. This claim says the settled relation can be an
  identification, and the constraint a unit.
- [CLAIM-154](CLAIM-154.md): when a candidate unit is worth keeping.

## The stronger reading it invites and does not support

That identification is the only route to a new unit. Differentiation
without prior identification may also produce units, which is what
defeated_if names. That the classes are real kinds: whether a category
used by the process that confirms it is a real kind is [CLAIM-402](CLAIM-402.md)'s
question, and the claim does not answer it.

## A note on a rate condition

A29 restated the mechanism as "selective contraction": within-class
variation decays faster than between-class variation. That condition may
be neither necessary nor matched to A29's own example. [CLAIM-388](CLAIM-388.md) holds an
exactly closed collective variable with no weakly coupled block, and
A29 §3's own coordinate z + (a/λ)ξ is conserved without any grouping of
coordinates into fast and slow classes. The claim is filed without the
rate condition.

## Note of 2026-11-19: readings for the exchange "Topological protection explained"

Belkin and Niyogi ([THEORY-2047](../theory.d/THEORY-2047.md)), read, put the owner's thesis as a passing speculation in their introduction: if recovering low-dimensional structure is local, "a natural clustering will emerge" and might serve as a basis for categories in perception. They give no argument and no condition under which the clusters become units of further operation. It leaves the claim open.

Lohmiller and Slotine ([THEORY-2043](../theory.d/THEORY-2043.md)), read: full contraction identifies all states and leaves a single class in any convex contraction region. That is why the claim needs its completion by differentiation (A29 §5): identification by contraction yields several classes only where something else has first separated basins or confined contraction to some directions. It qualifies the mechanism and agrees with the claim's completion.

Wang and Isola ([THEORY-2033](../theory.d/THEORY-2033.md)), read, note (§4.2) that on a finite augmented dataset perfect alignment and uniformity cannot both hold, "because perfect alignment implies that all augmentations from a single element have the same feature vector". The identification the loss enforces is the augmentation's, and uniformity resists any coarser one. Whether coarser classes form, and whether they are closed under further operations, is not addressed, so it leaves the claim open.

No status change.

## Note of 2026-11-19: Wang and Slotine, read
<!-- inactive-ok-file: THEORY-2044 — Proposed; cited from the readings of 2026-11-19 -->

Wang and Slotine ([THEORY-2044](../theory.d/THEORY-2044.md); [NOTE-2096](../notes.d/NOTE-2096.md)), read. An analogy, not evidence, but it answers the objection the reading of Lohmiller and Slotine raised, that contraction "leaves a single class" unless confined to some directions. Partial contraction is that confinement: differences across the synchronous manifold are erased exponentially while a continuum of collective states along it survives, set by initial conditions, and the surviving variable keeps well-defined dynamics ("both oscillators tend to their original limit cycle behavior, but with a common phase", p. 7). Two qualifications. What survives is a continuum, not discrete classes, so classes need more than contraction. And partial contraction needs no condition that within-class variation decay faster than between-class variation, which bears on the claim's rate condition. Nothing here bears on reuse or on classes becoming units. The claim is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-11-19: Krohn and Weninger, read

Krohn and Weninger ([THEORY-tmpj5upc](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpj5upc.md); [NOTE-tmp39hi8](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp39hi8.md)), by analogy: on Reddit a name can come before its community, "links are made to subreddits that do not exist" "(often as a joke), and occasionally these links spur the creation of a new community", a route to a named unit that starts from a symbol rather than from collapsing differences. Correlational, and nothing on closure or reuse. Neither is evidence for the claim; the note above on the Reddit case should be read as illustration.
