The owner sent 44 readings on agency, control, the self and
self-governance, with two distinctions to build into the vocabulary.
Agent, individual, self and person should not be synonyms. Behavioural
integration, phenomenal unity, narrative unity and normative
self-governance should be represented separately. [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md) did both, and
retagged the record before any reading was filed. Three readings were
already held: Gallagher 2000 ([LIT-453](../../record/literature.d/LIT-453.md)), Ryan & Deci 2000 ([LIT-559](../../record/literature.d/LIT-559.md)), and
*Being No One* ([LIT-467](../../record/literature.d/LIT-467.md), now read through its précis).

The owner asked for the work to be committed as it came in. Five agents
filed in parallel, and each group was linted and committed on its own.

## What was filed

The batch is 44 LITs and 28 NOTEs. Twenty-seven of the LITs are read.
Seventeen are Deferred seeds: the books, and articles with no lawful
copy. Most seeds have a shorter same-author reading beside them.

- **Biological autonomy, enaction and the free-energy principle.**
  - Barandiaran et al. separate individuality from agency, the owner's
    agent and individual distinction argued from inside one theory.
  - Mossio et al. 2009 grant a candle flame closure and draw the line at
    organisational differentiation. That bears on [NOTE-094](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-094.md)'s demarcation
    question.
  - Two critiques of the Markov blanket now `correct` Friston's "Life as
    we know it" ([LIT-526](../../record/literature.d/LIT-526.md)): Bruineberg et al. on reification, Raja et al.
    on autopoiesis.
  - Kelso's *Dynamic Patterns* is read through his 2021 retrospective on
    the HKB model.
  - *Sophisticated Inference* carries the `anthology-candidate` flag.
- **Control and self-control.**
  - Ainslie's précis, as the owner suggested.
  - Schelling's 1980 *Public Interest* essay, standing in for the 1984
    lecture.
  - Daw et al., Dayan & Berridge, Shenhav et al., Botvinick et al.
  - Carver & Scheier, and Norman & Shallice, are Deferred.
- **Evidence accumulation.**
  - Usher & McClelland, Ratcliff & McKoon, Bogacz et al. and HDDM.
  - Schurger et al., whose readiness-potential reinterpretation sits
    beside Dennett & Kinsbourne's treatment of Libet ([LIT-431](../../record/literature.d/LIT-431.md)).
- **Self, person and practical reason.**
  - Metzinger's précis.
  - McAdams 2019, standing in for McAdams & McLean, and declared `rivals`
    with Strawson's "Against Narrativity".
  - Bratman's 2017 essay on self-governance over time, which `rivals`
    Ainslie's bargaining account of willpower.
  - Korsgaard's Tanner Lectures, and her 2014 "Normative Constitution of
    Agency".
  - IFS, through Schwartz's own account of the model.
  - *Reasons and Persons* was already seeded ([LIT-469](../../record/literature.d/LIT-469.md)). Parfit 1971
    ([LIT-447](../../record/literature.d/LIT-447.md)) stays the record's reading of it.
- **Authenticity and self-governance.**
  - Sheldon & Elliot, and Schlegel et al., are read.
  - Frankfurt's "Identification and Wholeheartedness", Bratman's "Three
    Theories", Taylor, Kernis & Goldman, and Wood et al. are Deferred,
    with no lawful copy found.
