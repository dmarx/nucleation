# CLAIM-tmpvu536: Brooks's architecture, Kelso's coordination dynamics and Levin's bioelectric selves are three different ways of understanding what an organized whole is

## The claim

"These are not merely three pieces of evidence for distributed agency. They
suggest three different ways of understanding what the whole is" (A8):
observer-recognized organization (Brooks, [LIT-436](../literature.d/LIT-436.md)), goal-directed organization
(Levin, [LIT-439](../literature.d/LIT-439.md)), and coordination dynamics (Kelso, [THEORY-069](../theory.d/THEORY-069.md)).

## Why it was rejected

[CLAIM-tmpce4jz](CLAIM-tmpce4jz.md) objects to it. A10: "I treated differences in explanatory
emphasis as though they implied competing ontologies. They don't." Earlier, A1
had called these accounts complementary; the reversal at A8 and its reversal
at A10 is the oscillation a recorded relation prevents.
