# CLAIM-tmp0ogo4: If members absorb what an arrangement leaves unmet, resources are set by measured performance, and complaints count only past an intervention threshold, then the compensation hides the shortfall from the rule that sets resources, apparent spare capacity is removed, correction grows harder and the burden returns to the members, a loop that can lock the arrangement in without anyone's misconduct; and where revealing capacity invites its removal, the members' best responses feed the loop

## The claim

A33 §5 of the exchange
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b) takes "a
simplified institution with these properties":

- "It has a fixed set of obligations."
- "Its frontline staff compensate for inadequate resources."
- "Leadership periodically adjusts budgets according to observed
  performance."
- "Complaints influence decisions only when they cross an intervention
  threshold."
- "Revising the institution's operating rules takes substantially longer
  than routine service delivery."

"None of these properties necessarily involves misconduct. But together,
they can create a self-reinforcing structure", the loop in the summary.

**The strategic layer.** With [TERM-110](../terms.d/TERM-110.md)'s ratchet, a principal that cannot
commit not to revise terms on revealed capacity: "If they compensate
successfully, they may reinforce the conditions that make future
compensation necessary. If they refuse, someone dependent on their care
may suffer immediately. If they reveal unused capacity, management may
remove it. If they conceal capacity, they may act dishonestly or
inefficiently." "Those responses may then become evidence for further
intervention by management."

**What is new.** Each link but one is held. The budget that cannot rise is
[CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md)'s, and the loss of transitions as slack goes is [CLAIM-679](CLAIM-679.md)'s. The
fall in complaints that is not a fall in unmet demand is [CLAIM-549](CLAIM-549.md)'s. The
link that is not held is that compensation hides the shortfall from the
rule that sets resources, so the rule cuts what it cannot see is in use.
That is [CLAIM-tmpe5u7i](CLAIM-tmpe5u7i.md)'s subsidy entering a budget rule. A toy model
adding an unobserved compensation term to [CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md)'s rule would make the
claim demonstrable; A gives none, and the filer has not built one.

**A correction.** A says [CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md) "studies a budget rule in which next
period's resources equal the resources consumed in the previous period."
[CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md)'s rule sets the budget to the previous period's **peak**
spending, not to what was consumed, and that is what makes it unable to
rise.

## Admission

Found at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17 ([SYNTHESIS-tmphd6ep](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-tmphd6ep.md), Arm A),
under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s test for a claim whose premises are claims.

**What it adds.** Unpaid extra work that keeps measured performance
acceptable is invisible to a budget rule set on that performance, so the
rule removes capacity that is in use. None of its premises states this.
[CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md)'s rule is peak spending, which never rises and cuts nothing by
itself; [CLAIM-549](CLAIM-549.md) is about intervention thresholds; [CLAIM-tmpe5u7i](CLAIM-tmpe5u7i.md) writes
no budget rule. The body above says the same ("The link that is not
held").

**The separating case.** A hospital whose unpaid extra hours are logged
and read by the budget rule as demand. All three premises hold: the
subsidy is unfunded, members carry the shortfall, and complaints stay
below the threshold. But the shortfall is not hidden from the rule, so no
capacity in use is cut, and the loop does not close. The case is
realistic.

**The reading it fixes.** Whether that case defeats the claim turns on
"measured performance". If the phrase covers logged inputs, the case
meets the claim's own defeater ("the budget rule responds to the
compensation"). Read as output measured against obligations, which is
how the claim is to be taken, the hospital's rule is not set on measured
performance, so the case falls outside the claim's properties. The claim
is then separate from its premises and not shown false by the case.

## What it does not say

- Not that institutions run this way. A33 §5: "A possible reinforcing loop,
  not a universal prediction about institutions."
- Not that the loop is the same as interpersonal coercion. A33 §5: it is
  "structurally similar to our earlier discussion of coercive control,
  without being morally or legally equivalent to interpersonal coercion."
