# LIT-tmpe25cj: The laws of emotion

Nico H. Frijda (1988), *American Psychologist* 43(5):349–358 — DOI-10.1037/0003-066X.43.5.349

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read. The following is what read works in the record say
of it, attributed to them.*

- **Mood as information about the group.** Barsade ([LIT-tmpojrfm](LIT-tmpojrfm.md), read)
  cites it for mood contagion being "a direct source of information in its
  own right for providing information about the how the group is doing".
- **Flexible modulation.** Gross ([LIT-554](LIT-554.md), read) cites it on the flexible
  modulation of emotion (p. 288, as [NOTE-429](../notes.d/NOTE-429.md) records).
- **What the laws are** is not stated by any read work in the record, so it
  is not stated here. Search-engine summaries name several, but none was
  read from a source the record can use.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-04 at the owner's request, as the shorter substitute for
Frijda's 2007 book *The Laws of Emotion*, and as the "efficient first step"
that the entry for *The Emotions* ([LIT-546](LIT-546.md)) names. Frijda, Ridderinkhof
and Rietveld ([LIT-540](LIT-540.md)), the record's read statement of action readiness,
already stands in for *The Emotions*. On 2026-10-03 the filing of [LIT-540](LIT-540.md)
tried this article and could not reach it lawfully. Neither could this
one.

**The 2007 book is not filed.** The owner's rule files a book as a Deferred
seed only if the record would lose something without it. Here it would
not. This article carries the laws in their short form, [LIT-546](LIT-546.md) carries the
canonical theory, and [LIT-540](LIT-540.md) the read late statement. A book entry would
be a third unread Frijda with nothing to say that these do not. [NOTE-433](../notes.d/NOTE-433.md)
records that [LIT-540](LIT-540.md) cites the 2007 book alongside *The Emotions*; that
citation has no entry, and a reader following it can start here.

Where it bears, once read:

- **On collective emotion.** Von Scheve & Ismer ([LIT-tmp0fwb0](LIT-tmp0fwb0.md)) explain
  convergence through shared appraisal structures, that is, through
  concerns members share. Concern is the condition of emotion in Frijda's
  theory as [LIT-540](LIT-540.md) states it: "In the absence of concern pertinence, …
  no emotion". Von Scheve & Ismer also use Frijda's later (1994) account
  of sentiments as appraisal dispositions for their group-based
  sentiments. The laws would say whether a shared concern is all that
  shared emotion needs.
- **On regulation.** Gross cites it on the flexible modulation of
  emotion. A reading would say whether it already holds the view [LIT-540](LIT-540.md)
  defends, that regulation is part of emotion, on which [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) rests.

No relation is declared. A relation to [LIT-546](LIT-546.md) or [LIT-540](LIT-540.md) needs the text.

**Priority for a reading: medium**, and higher than the book's. Ten pages,
and they would let the record say what the laws are.
