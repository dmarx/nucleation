# LEAD-093: Construct a nucleus that, once formed, takes part in producing or repairing the constraints of its own formation, and measure when the loop closes

## What it would do

Extend [CASE-190](../cases.d/CASE-190.md) so its student becomes a teacher, or use the Life eater
constructions, and measure the turnover the loop survives and the outside
supply it still needs.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-399](../questions.d/QUESTION-399.md): "A system, molecular or organizational, in which a nucleus
  that speeds formation also, once formed, takes part in producing or
  repairing the constraints of its own formation. Then a measure of when
  that loop closes"
- [QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md): "Its student never becomes a teacher"

## What would count

A loop that closes answers [QUESTION-399](../questions.d/QUESTION-399.md) by construction and supplies
[QUESTION-361](../questions.d/QUESTION-361.md)'s missing succession.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out by a delegated lead worker

Done, on the branch claude/lead-093-closing-nucleus. Code, tests and
results are in scratch/studies/lead-093/.

- **The construction**, CASE-tmpy4orj (executed-study). It is a new
  kinetic model, not CASE-190's equations, which are not all on the
  record, and not the Life eaters, which have no turnover to vary. Nuclei
  form fast only on a teacher, and each nucleus makes teachers from
  material. It closes CASE-190's three named gaps.
- **When the loop closes**, CLAIM-tmpocjfb. It closes at the crossing of a
  separatrix in the joint state, not at any level of nuclei or of the
  self-made share.
- **The turnover it survives.** Closure is lost at a fold, k_T* = 0.19 at
  the base point, rising with the teaching rate.
- **The supply it still needs.** Material always. Teacher supply never,
  once closed.
- **A partial loop can cost more supply than none**, CLAIM-tmpk6abl.
- **Repair is not production**, CLAIM-tmpnyf9e. Repair without production
  divides the supply needed by 1 + ρN/λ and never closes.
- **Succession**, CLAIM-tmppgdad. Students become teachers, and closure
  turns on the aggregate teaching gain.
- **Finite numbers.** In finite systems the closed loop is lost after a
  time that grows roughly exponentially with size.
- **Closure or autocatalysis** is left to the owner, as two alternative
  readings: CLAIM-tmp1qqso (closure by TERM-070's definition, the
  assistant's lean) and CLAIM-tmps1ll3 (autocatalysis, not the closure
  QUESTION-399 distinguishes).

Remaining:

- the owner's choice between the two readings;
- teaching quality inherited with error down the generations, which
  QUESTION-361's "degraded teacher" asks about and this model lacks;
- a physical or documented system in place of an invented one.

The status and stage are the owner's.
