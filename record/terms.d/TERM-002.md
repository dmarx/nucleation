# TERM-002: communicative object, as a compatible family of local sections

## Definition

A78: measurement contexts form a cover M of the observables X; the event
presheaf assigns E(U) = ∏_(x∈U) O_x with restriction maps; local sections are
compatible when they agree on overlaps. "The sheaf approach is more general: we
can begin with locally defined observations and their restriction relations
**without assuming that a global assignment satisfying all empirical constraints
exists**." Manuscript §3: an observational scenario (X, M, O), the event
presheaf, restriction, compatibility, and "the nontrivial question arises when
empirical supports or distributions rule out the resulting global assignment".

## What it is not

Not a point in a semantic space, and not necessarily a region in one ([TERM-022](TERM-022.md)).
It may have no global realization at all ([CLAIM-038](../claims.d/CLAIM-038.md)).
