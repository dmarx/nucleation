# LIT-tmpyiplw: Human leukocyte antigens and fetal microchimerism: Relating fetal-origin cells in maternal circulation to maternal and fetal HLA genetics

Daniel P. Jacobsen, Maria B. Olsen, Heidi E. Fjeldstad, Meryam Sugulle,
Anne Cathrine Staff and others (2026). "Human leukocyte antigens and fetal
microchimerism: Relating fetal-origin cells in maternal circulation to
maternal and fetal HLA genetics." *Journal of Reproductive Immunology* 173:
104826. DOI-10.1016/j.jri.2025.104826.

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* From the abstract: whether fetal cells persist in the mother
after pregnancy may depend on how the mother's killer-cell receptors read
the fetal HLA-C. If so, the persistence of foreign cells is governed by the
host's immune relation to them, not by their presence alone.

## Standing in the record
