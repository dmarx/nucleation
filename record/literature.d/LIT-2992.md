# LIT-2992: Self-Organized Criticality: An Explanation of 1/f Noise

Bak, Tang & Wiesenfeld (1987). Physical Review Letters. DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.381.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A names it at A3, A5, A24 for the sandpile model of self-organized criticality. It bears on the exchange's third route to heavy tails, criticality reached without tuning, set against preferential attachment. Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-2269](../notes.d/NOTE-2269.md), [THEORY-2179](../theory.d/THEORY-2179.md)); see the note of that date below.

## Note of 2026-11-22: Newman, read

Newman's review ([LIT-2976](LIT-2976.md), read 2026-11-22) cites this paper as the origin of self-organized criticality: "As first proposed by Bak et al. [57], however, it is possible that some dynamical systems actually arrange themselves so that they always sit at the critical point, no matter what state we start off in." He illustrates the idea with the forest-fire model of Drossel and Schwabl, not with the sandpile, and lists avalanches among SOC's proposed applications. So the record still holds no reading of the sandpile model itself; this work stays Deferred.

## Note of 2026-11-22: Bak, Tang and Wiesenfeld, read

Read in full ([NOTE-2269](../notes.d/NOTE-2269.md); the reading is [THEORY-2179](../theory.d/THEORY-2179.md)). The
exchange's characterization holds except in two places. "Slowly driven"
is the later framing: the letter sets the pile up "with random initial
conditions z ≫ K", lets it relax, and probes the result with "small local
random perturbations" (p. 382), and never names slow drive, time-scale
separation or conservation. And "1/f" is loose in the paper too: its
spectra are ω^(−1.58) in 2D and ω^(−1.1) in 3D, with "1/f" in quotation
marks in Fig. 3's caption. Newman's attribution (above) checks against
"an attractor reached by starting far from equilibrium" (p. 381). The
finite-size cutoff the letter asserts without data is reproduced in
[CLAIM-858](../claims.d/CLAIM-858.md). Status Deferred to Active.
