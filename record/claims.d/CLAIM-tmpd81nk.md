# CLAIM-tmpd81nk: Different hidden-state models can produce identical observations, so pragmatic identity and fidelity should be defined on observable responses before any equivalence of internal states is assumed

## The claim

A39 §4.4: "different hidden-state models may produce identical observations";
"Distinguish predictive or operational equivalence from equivalence of internal
representations." A39's preamble: the revision "prevents us from prematurely
defining faithful translation as preservation of internal cognitive states we
cannot observe or uniquely identify". A39 §3.3 accordingly demoted the
interpretive state: its dimensions "do not define a unique natural coordinate
system", and states are "model-dependent and potentially latent".

Manuscript §2: "We use 'observable' in an operational rather than narrowly
physical sense ... Neither a high-level latent coordinate nor a fixed hierarchy
of levels is assumed primitive. Latent variables may be learned as compressed
summaries of recurring observational patterns, but their significance must be
validated by predictive consequences." §3: "An observational equivalence class
identifies realizations indistinguishable by the chosen procedures."

## What it does not say

That meaning is nothing but behaviour ([CLAIM-tmpwcpf8](CLAIM-tmpwcpf8.md)). Nor that states are useless: the
manuscript's §8–9 still transports state representations, which [CLAIM-tmp2rve9](CLAIM-tmp2rve9.md) bears on.
