# NOTE-tmpdyoej: Associated Press — Nationals win first World Series title, beat Astros in Game 7

## Contribution

The wire report of the Nationals' 2019 championship, which counts it twice over: by franchise and by city.

## Key results

- **By franchise.** "to claim the first title in franchise history"; "This franchise started out as the Montreal Expos in 1969".
- **By city.** "brought the first World Series championship to the nation's capital" "since ol' Walter Johnson delivered the crown for the Senators in 1924."

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. Statements by an interested party are taken as
their stated position, not their motive.

## Concepts

Continuation of a club by franchise (legal and statistical succession)
against heritage by place (a city's baseball history).

## Bearing on the record

The press counts two units in one story: the franchise, which runs through Montreal and has no title before 2019, and the city, whose last title was the original Senators'. Coded C-EX for the franchise and H-SEN for the city (statement 10).

## Limitations

One wire story; it is the press's framing, not the league's or the club's.
