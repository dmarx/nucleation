# TERM-tmp3t5jq: consolidation

## Definition

A27 §5 separates three operations:

| Operation | What changes | What is preserved |
|---|---|---|
| Cooling | Default accessibility | Original knowledge and identity |
| Consolidation | Representational granularity | Selected distinctions and dependencies |
| Erasure | Retained information | Only whatever the disposal policy explicitly preserves |

"Cooling is comparatively reversible. Consolidation involves semantic
judgment. Erasure may extinguish a developmental possibility entirely.
They should therefore have progressively stronger review requirements."
Cooling is [TERM-tmp96b3g](TERM-tmp96b3g.md)'s move between tiers; this term is the second.

## Against supersession

A27 §10. The record already has supersession, which says that a successor
holds a different judgment. Consolidation keeps the judgment and changes
its representation. "A reference to a particular historical argument must
not be silently redirected to a synthesis that makes a subtly different
argument." A consolidated entry keeps a resolvable stub: its identity,
title, standing and scope; its "irreducible conclusion, qualification or
defeater"; its dependency relations; the representation it was
consolidated into; the grounds; and the conditions for reactivation.

## Its test

A27 §6 states the criterion as a loss over a declared family of tasks Q:

  Loss_Q(R, R′) = sup over q in Q of d(q(R), q(R′)),

acceptable when within a tolerance and the tasks stay affordable. The
family must include "withdrawing evidence, reopening arguments, tracing
provenance and detecting conflicts", because "A summary can preserve all
the conclusions of a record while destroying its capacity for targeted
correction." [CLAIM-tmpnf6q4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnf6q4.md) says exactly when a consolidation passes for a
declared family of corrections.

A29 §9 makes the family explicit as slices: explanatory (understand a
result), justificatory (assess its grounds), correction (revise it when
premises change), developmental (continue its line) and historical
(reconstruct its formation). One entry can be kept at different fidelity
for different slices, and different lines can have different working sets
("root-indexed working sets").

## Where it would apply

Two places A names. Epistemic fragmentation (A29 §13): a result spread
across a claim, its corrections, a case, an argument, a synthesis and
several status decisions, each needed to reconstruct the current position;
consolidation would let a mature representation replace the trail in
ordinary use, which the record's SYNTHESIS scheme does not do, since a
synthesis integrates its components and leaves them in place. And accreted
dated notes on held entries, which [CLAIM-tmpu1fef](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu1fef.md)'s count puts at about a
quarter of the bytes of claims. [CLAIM-767](../claims.d/CLAIM-767.md)'s restatement chains are the
same structure seen as drift. [CLAIM-tmp8gkmi](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8gkmi.md) is A's hypothesis that
consolidation does more than save effort.
