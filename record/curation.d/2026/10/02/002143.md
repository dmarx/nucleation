**The bridge, as the readings support it.** Agency is granted in both
directions, by Block, List and Levin. The dispute is over experience. Every
way to accept Minsky's downward move while refusing Schwitzgebel's upward
one takes one of two routes:

- an anti-nesting principle, stipulated by Putnam, postulated by IIT, or
  weakly justified by Kammerer;
- an architectural criterion, which would have to spare a loosely coupled
  society of mind while excluding a nation.

Nobody here computes that difference. [THEORY-tmp31lxe](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp31lxe.md) is `Proposed`, and its
`promote_when` names the criterion that would settle it.

**Corrections to [NOTE-131](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-131.md) the readings found**, held in the readings and
not yet applied to [NOTE-131](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-131.md) itself:

- Block's counterexample to Putnam is elementary-particle people, not tiny
  people as neurons.
- Schwitzgebel's Putnam "quotation" is a paraphrase, and the first
  appearance is 1967, from a 1965 colloquium.
- The "Brooks (1986)" of the group-mind tradition is D. H. M. Brooks, not
  Rodney Brooks.

Kammerer's reader also found that [NOTE-188](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-188.md)'s gloss of his principle omits
its second condition.
