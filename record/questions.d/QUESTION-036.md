# QUESTION-036: Can the admissible symmetries of a communicative kind be reconstructed from a sufficiently rich family of its observable realizations and transformations?

## Why it is a question

At U55 the owner asked the assistant to "develop this further", and A198
built a ten-section construction on character-like signatures ([TERM-041](../terms.d/TERM-041.md))
for communicative kinds ([TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)). Its §V brought in Tannaka
reconstruction as a model, which the workbench says it "correctly
labelled 'an inspiration rather than an applicable theorem'", and it asked
the question above (workbench 2026/10/10/040457).

**The model.** Tannaka (1939), and later Krein, showed that a compact group
can be recovered from its representations: from the category of its
representations together with the functor that forgets each one down to
its underlying vector space. Grothendieck and Deligne generalized this to
Tannakian categories. This summary is the assistant's, given from memory.
The record holds no reading of Tannaka or Krein.

**Read across.** A communicative kind would play the group's part. Its
observable realizations (translations, retellings, renderings in other
media) would play the representations, and the transformations between
them the morphisms. The question is whether the kind's admissible
symmetries, what it can undergo and remain itself, could be recovered from
enough of its realizations alone. That would make [QUESTION-029](QUESTION-029.md)'s "how can
it be recognized apart from any one representation" exact, which is why
this question refines it.

**What an answer needs.** A precise sense of "sufficiently rich". Something
that plays the role of the forgetful functor. And the structure on the
realizations that Tannaka reconstruction depends on, such as a tensor
product, which communicative realizations may simply lack. The label A198
gave it, inspiration rather than theorem, says that none of this is in
hand. An answer could also be negative: a showing that communicative
realizations lack the structure any such reconstruction needs.

**Neighbours.** [QUESTION-001](QUESTION-001.md) asks whether pragmatic identity can be
reconstructed from context-relative judgements, which is reconstruction
from a different kind of data. [QUESTION-027](QUESTION-027.md) asks for a relational signature
that is stable and discriminating, which is the invariant side of the same
problem.

**How it was dropped.** A198 posed it. One turn later the second
crystallized argument (A203, [TERM-052](../terms.d/TERM-052.md)) left out Tannaka with no
turn deciding to. Whether the question dissolved as the argument matured
or was simply not carried forward, the exchange does not say. Filing it
gives it a status, which is how the record can tell the two apart.

## Note of 2026-10-12: an assessed result filed on 2026-10-10

The note of 2026-10-11 says none of the listed material has been assessed.
PR [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/121)'s round of 2026-10-10 filed one assessed result on the
construction this question comes from. [CLAIM-tmpt76qw](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpt76qw.md), granted and Active,
says "The transformations the line studies are not group elements", and
that the group "has to be a symmetry group of the situation that the
analyst chooses". It also shows that "the same character" across
realizations is relative to a chosen correspondence. That leans toward the
negative answer this question allows for, beside [CLAIM-132](../claims.d/CLAIM-132.md) and [THEORY-019](../theory.d/THEORY-019.md).
[CASE-tmp4iy8h](../cases.d/CASE-tmp4iy8h.md) is a constructed case of such a chosen symmetry: swapping
the two persons of a dyad changes nonreciprocal T/V address and leaves
reciprocal address fixed.
