# CLAIM-459: An input to a bistable balance of authority can be sincere and causally efficacious without changing which regime operates: below a threshold the structure of the competition sets, it moves the balance within the current basin, so a higher-order volition that changes nothing visible need not be idle

## The claim

[CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md)'s normal form: ẇ = μw − w³ + h, with w the balance of authority
between two regimes and h an input favouring one. For μ > 0 there are two
basins, and the operative regime changes only when |h| exceeds
h_c = 2μ^{3/2}/(3√3), where the occupied equilibrium meets the saddle and
disappears. For 0 < |h| < h_c the equilibrium within the current basin
shifts with h: the input has an effect, on w, and none on which regime
operates. [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md), from A8 of the exchange of 2026-10-17: "An endorsement
can be sincere and causally efficacious while remaining too weak to shift
the system out of its current attractor basin. It may change the local
balance without producing a behavioral transition."

Two things follow that the record's claims on efficacy do not say.
[CLAIM-047](CLAIM-047.md) separates possessing a regime from its being effective, and
[CLAIM-122](CLAIM-122.md) shows behaviour changing with the conditions while the attitudes
stay fixed; this is the converse case, the attitude changing and the
behaviour staying fixed, with the attitude still doing work. And, as this
filing reads the normal form, because an input against the current regime shifts the equilibrium
toward the saddle, a later change in μ or a further input can
tip the regime sooner than it would have without the earlier one, so the
earlier input is not idle even counterfactually. Which regime operates
then depends on the path of h, which is the hysteresis [CLAIM-463](CLAIM-463.md)
states for a capacity's maintenance.

## What it does not say

- **That any agent's volitions behave so.** [CASE-112](../cases.d/CASE-112.md) is fictional by
  construction; reading h as a higher-order volition is the exchange's
  interpretation.
- **That a volition below threshold is as good as one above.** It makes a
  difference to the balance, not to what is done.
- **How to detect it.** Behaviour alone cannot; an input's subthreshold
  effect shows only under a further perturbation, as [CLAIM-414](CLAIM-414.md)'s method
  requires.
