# LEAD-129: Compare institutions with and without effective means to revise their governing standards, matched on resources and independence of evidence

## What it would do

Choose matched institutions and compare.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md): "the record holds no comparison of institutions with and
  without effective means to revise their governing standards"

## What would count

The comparison is what [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) lacks.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out, as an illustrative matched comparison

Carried out by a delegated worker. [CASE-377](../cases.d/CASE-377.md) (executed-study,
documented) files a small within-domain matched comparison of public standards
bodies: WHATWG against the W3C HTML/XHTML track; the W3C before against after
its 2019 living-standard adoption; the Unicode Consortium against the frozen
code-page regime; with the Julian calendar as an unmatched extreme. In every
matched pair the arm with an effective living-revision mechanism corrected
recognized inadequacies within days to a few years, the fixed outcome-monitoring
arm left them for years to decades, and the extreme for centuries — the
direction [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) predicts, with the resource-reversed and same-institution
pairs cutting against its defeater. It is illustrative, not powered (N = 3 pairs,
chosen not sampled), so it illustrates the claim without meeting its defeater.
Data and scripts are under scratch/studies/lead-129/.

What remains: a powered test the claim's defeater could turn on would need a
pre-registered sample of institutions rather than a chosen set, and a defect
severity measure independent of the standards under test. The owner's call left
open in the case: whether to keep `illustrates: CLAIM-685` or bind it only to
`QUESTION-128`. The lead's status and stage are left for the owner.
