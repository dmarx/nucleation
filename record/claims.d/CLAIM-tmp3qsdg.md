# CLAIM-tmp3qsdg: Each human design found for isolating a germline route of inherited trauma removes some of the other routes and leaves at least one: donor gametes remove the womb and rearing but cannot divide the gamete's marks from its genes, a father's exposure before conception removes the womb but leaves his rearing and the group, and a pregnant grandmother's exposure removes none; no study found combines designs so as to remove all of them

## The claim

[QUESTION-297](../questions.d/QUESTION-297.md) asks for "a human study that separates germline transmission
from uterine, parenting and cultural routes", or evidence that none can.
Genetic inheritance is a fifth thing any such study must also hold apart.
The designs found, and what each removes, are these:

| design | removes | leaves | read here |
|---|---|---|---|
| donor gametes: egg, sperm or embryo donation, with the exposure on the donor's or the recipient's side | the womb and rearing, from what the gamete carries | genes, which travel with the gamete's marks ([CLAIM-tmpv8nto](CLAIM-tmpv8nto.md)) | Rice et al. 2010 ([THEORY-tmpo8don](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpo8don.md)), prenatal stress; Lewis et al. 2011 ([THEORY-tmp4fo7b](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4fo7b.md)), depression. No study of trauma-exposed donors found |
| adoption at birth | rearing and, across groups, culture | the womb (the birth mother carried the child) and genes | Named in [QUESTION-297](../questions.d/QUESTION-297.md); Horsthemke ([THEORY-1618](../theory.d/THEORY-1618.md)) says it weakens the confounds. No adoption study of inherited trauma found |
| father exposed before conception, who raises the child | the womb | rearing by the exposed father, the group's story, genes | Costa et al. ([THEORY-1594](../theory.d/THEORY-1594.md)); Yehuda et al. 2014's paternal PTSD, at second hand ([NOTE-tmp05o0u](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp05o0u.md)); the sperm marks of Tuulari et al. ([THEORY-tmpseccf](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpseccf.md)) |
| conception before against after the exposure, in one family | fixed family traits and, in expectation, genes | rearing after the exposure, which the later child alone, or more, receives | Costa et al.'s within-family estimate ([NOTE-1255](../notes.d/NOTE-1255.md)), P = .056 |
| grandmother exposed while pregnant, grandchild measured ("germline exposure") | none of the three: the middle generation was exposed in utero, raised by the exposed grandmother, in the exposed community | the womb, rearing, the group, cohort | Mulligan et al. 2025 ([THEORY-tmppcqht](../theory.d/THEORY-tmppcqht.md)) |

No study found combines a paternal or donor exposure with rearing by
someone unexposed and a within-person contrast. That combination is what
removing all of them would take. The field's own survey to mid-2023
(Zhou and Ryan, [THEORY-tmplq0um](../theory.d/THEORY-tmplq0um.md)) lists two paternal-only studies, one in
sperm, and no donor-gamete or adoption study of trauma.

## Why it is put as a thesis

It goes further than [CLAIM-644](CLAIM-644.md), which grants that the designs "weaken
the confounds without removing them" without saying which design leaves
which route. Here each design's residue is named, and the residues
differ. So the answer to the routes question is not one missing study.
It is a combination no single natural experiment supplies.

## The alternative

The reading this claim does not take is [CLAIM-tmpgv8bs](CLAIM-tmpgv8bs.md). It counts the
Syrian design as a partial separation, from the care of an exposed parent,
because the germline grandchildren's mothers did not themselves live
through the violence as persons. [ARG-tmpmenxz](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpmenxz.md) sets out that inference. The
lean here is against it: the mothers were exposed in utero, so their own
development is a path to the next pregnancy, and they were raised by the
exposed grandmothers. The owner chooses.

## What it does not say

- Not that a germline route is absent. Sperm marks of childhood
  maltreatment exist in men ([THEORY-tmpseccf](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpseccf.md)). The step from them to
  children is untested.
- Not that the parenting route is unproven. Donor-gamete designs show it
  for parental depression ([THEORY-tmp4fo7b](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4fo7b.md), [NOTE-tmp18gag](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp18gag.md)).
- Not about the identity route. A search for studies of descendants'
  distress against group identification, measured apart from family,
  found none ([LEAD-156](../leads.d/LEAD-156.md)'s dated note).
