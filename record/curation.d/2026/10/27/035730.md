## What was read of each

Every copy was lawful: arXiv, the work's own venue (distill.pub, PMLR, the
NeurIPS proceedings, the Alignment Forum), or the authors' copies on the
Cambridge MLG and Oxford servers. Figures in charts were read from
captions and text, and no number was taken from a chart. Each NOTE says
so.

- **Early Vision**: the Distill page's HTML in full, with every section,
  group and circuit caption, the footnotes and the references. The neuron
  grids were parsed to count each group's units. The counts agree with
  each "Show all N" line and, unit for unit, with the five layer_data
  taxonomy files the article offers. The images were not looked at.
- **Curve Circuits**: the article's text in full. The text (about 20,600
  characters) and every figure caption are compiled into the page's
  index.js and were extracted by a static parse of the bundle, without
  running it. Every section, footnote and caption, the contributions and
  the acknowledgements were read. The figures were not rendered, and the
  Colab notebook was not opened.
- **Scherlis et al.**: arXiv v4 (23 pp.) in full, §§1–5 and Appendices
  A–H with their lemmas and theorems. v1–v3 were compared where it
  mattered, and the Alignment Forum post was read in full.
- **Griffiths and Ghahramani 2005**: the NeurIPS proceedings PDF, 8 pp., in
  full, with §§1–4, the captions and the references. The Gatsby technical
  report it defers to was not read.
- **Teh, Görür and Ghahramani 2007**: PMLR's PDF, 8 pp., in full, with
  §§1–8, the appendix, the captions and the references.
- **The beam sampler**: the authors' PDF, which carries the ICML 2008
  footer, in full, with §§1–6, the captions, both footnotes and the
  references. The ACM copy was not compared.
- **Factorial HMMs**: the journal version in full (§§1–6, Appendices A–D,
  the ten notes, Tables 1–2 and the references), from the authors' copy.
  Its numbers, notes and received dates were checked against the
  publisher's PDF. The NIPS 1995 version was downloaded and not read.
- **Knowles and Ghahramani 2007**: the authors' PDF in full, with §§1–5,
  the captions and the 8 references. The Springer copy was not compared.

## Errors found in the works

Numbers and claims were checked where cheap. The scripts are the readers'
own and are not part of the record.

- **Early Vision.**
  - mixed3b's "Color Contrast/Gradient" caption repeats the
    center-surround caption word for word, which contradicts its own
    first sentence.
  - A parenthesis is misplaced: "(Color Center-Surround mixed3a)".
  - The downloadable taxonomy files are not valid JSON (trailing commas).
  - There are typos.
  - The printed DOI does not resolve.
  - Checked and correct: 1,056 = 64 + 64 + 192 + 256 + 480, and conv2d2's
    "about 25%" line detectors are 49 of 192.
- **Curve Circuits.**
  - The downstream-use sentence says the curve units build "sophisticated
    curves, circles, S-shapes, spirals, divots and evolutes" in mixed4a.
    Each word links to a mixed3b group of Early Vision, in the curve
    units' own layer, and the sentence is nearly Early Vision's mixed3b
    text. Either the links or the layer are wrong.
  - The "~50x" reduction and "more than 50,000 parameters" are stated, not
    derived.
  - There are caption typos, and the printed DOI does not resolve.
  - Not an error, but the two articles sort mixed3a's line-like units
    differently.
- **Scherlis et al.**
  - The model is "one-layer" in §§1–2 and "two-layer" in §§3 and 5, in
    all four versions.
  - λ means three different things. Eq. 15's λ is √(λ₄/2) of eq. 4's
    multiplier.
  - §4.3 states n_i = λ_k C_i where it needs √λ_k R_k. v4 corrected §4.4
    but not §4.3.
  - §3.6 calls the same dense-input curves "convex" and "concave" within
    one paragraph.
  - Figure 4's caption calls ∂L/∂C_i the "marginal loss reduction", which
    is its negative.
  - Appendix E prints "(k − 1)(X, Y)" where the covariance is meant. The
    result holds with the covariance.
  - §3.2 claims no assumption beyond mean and covariance, but eq. 12 needs
    independence and a common fourth moment.
  - The contributions credit "JS", which matches no listed author. The
    Alignment Forum post names Jacob Steinhardt as a co-author, and all
    four arXiv versions thank him instead of listing him.
- **Griffiths and Ghahramani 2005.** Nothing of substance. There is one
  garbled sentence, the image size is given as 240 × 320 in the text and
  320 × 240 in the caption, and the NeurIPS site reverses the author
  order.
- **Teh, Görür and Ghahramani 2007.** The derivations re-derive. The
  truncation error is never computed, and the sampler comparison exists
  only in a chart. Ref. [6] dates the NIPS 18 paper 2006. There are typos.
  PMLR misspells the second author as "Grür".
- **The beam sampler.**
  - §4 writes [0, π_{s_t,s_{t−1}}] for [0, π_{s_{t−1},s_t}].
  - Neal's *Slice sampling* is cited as pp. 705–741. Teh et al. cite it as
    705–767, and neither was checked.
  - Daumé is garbled to "III, H. D.".
  - One hyperparameter setting was "chosen using the values the beam and
    Gibbs samplers converged to", which is circular for a comparison of
    the two.
- **Factorial HMMs.**
  - Note 3 is wrong as stated. Orthogonal columns of W^(m) and W^(n) with
    a diagonal C do not decouple the chains given an observation. A
    counterexample gives a posterior odds ratio of e^{−1} whatever Y is.
    The note needs C ∝ I, or orthogonality in the C^{−1} inner product.
  - Note 7's factor "3.8" is √15 = 3.87.
  - The Bach result's "an order of magnitude larger" leaves its unit open.
- **Knowles and Ghahramani 2007.**
  - §2.1 calls Nα "the total number of features". Nα is the expected
    number of non-zero entries. The same paragraph gives the total as
    Poisson(αH_N).
  - Figure 2's caption gives "D = 7, N = 6, N = 100" where the text has
    D = 7, K = 6, N = 200.
  - The prior on β is G(1, 2) in the table of variants and G(1, 1) in the
    MH step.
  - "Significantly better" is used with no test reported.
