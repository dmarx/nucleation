# Article 1. The Identity of Organization

## Individuation, maintenance provenance, and the limits of behavioral equivalence

**Abstract.** Organizations are usually told apart by their behaviour, their boundaries, their coherence or their persistence. These need not pick out the same thing. This article sets out what the Nucleation record holds about the difference, and with what standing. It opens with the record's executed result: in one simulated population, three ways of keeping two governing rules give identical ordinary trajectories and come apart under interventions on the processes that produce the rules. It then argues for five points.

1. "Individuation" has several senses, and a claim should say which one it means. Being an individual in the dynamical sense does not require maintaining oneself.
2. The achievements that lead from interconnection to individuation, and from there to autonomy and inquiry, form a partial order and not a ladder. A coupling statistic does not register who made the coupling, and a description closed under ordinary dynamics need not keep an organization's responses to intervention.
3. Passive observation does not show what maintains an organization. Where an outside controller supplies the internal production law as state feedback, interventions on state do not show it either, though a controller that reproduces less of the law can be found out by them. Maintenance is then located by interventions on the maintaining mechanism, relative to a boundary declared in advance. In the record's one construction of formation, no knockout after formation tells a self-formed organization from one installed from outside and then self-maintained; a knockout during formation does.
4. What persists through a change of every part is a lineage, carried by causal transmission. Resemblance does not carry it, so a character that two unrelated organizations share is a kind, not the persistence of either.
5. The record's separations are also limits. Self-production is not robustness, identification works within a known catalogue, and one closure measure does not fix an organization's identity.

The article also reports what the record holds about symbiosis, where dependence and individuality come apart, about character in the sense of representation theory, and about resource-bounded profiles. It ends by asking why individuation does not establish agency, autonomy, experience or standing. Its formal results and constructions are mostly Active and hold in models. Its executed studies hold in simulated populations whose laws the modeller stipulates, and two of them are Proposed. Its character half rests on Proposed entries.

### 1.1 Matched behaviour, different maintenance

A51 opens here: "Matched ordinary behavior does not identify the organization that maintains it." The record has run this, and it says how far the run reaches.

**The run.** CASE-161 (Active, an executed study, stipulated) keeps two governing rules in a 32-member population in three ways: by external reset, by reciprocal production between two producer teams, or by feedback without production. The ordinary trajectories are identical. The arrangements come apart under interventions:

- reciprocal production "repairs the loss of one rule and not of both";
- a producer outage of 80 updates ends both rules in 48 of 48 reciprocal runs and leaves the other arrangements unaffected; the audit team's outage is survived at 32 updates and not at 36, the admission team's at 36 and not at 40, and "These are grid points under these laws, not a critical duration";
- after the same audit pulse, an intervention on state, the next audit integrity is "1.00 under external reset, 0.18 under reciprocal production and 0.00 under feedback only".

The general claim behind it is Active: "Matched ordinary trajectories can conceal differences in an interpretation's efficacy, in the allocation of that efficacy, and in the maintenance of its enabling constraints", and in two finite constructions "a specified intervention exposes the difference that passive observation of the matched trajectories does not" (CLAIM-414, demonstrated). The record asks the open form of the question: whether two systems with matching behaviour and persistence can be told apart by whether their own processes generate and revise the constraints that keep them going, "and by what intervention?" (QUESTION-086, Open). Its account of the answers so far is SYNTHESIS-079 (Proposed): "Agreement in ordinary operation does not fix how a system responds to an intervention on its mechanism".

**Identification.** CASE-163 (Proposed, an executed study, stipulated) asks which of four known mechanisms maintains the rules. With histories matched, a model fitted under reciprocal production predicts 0.97055 for both reciprocal (actual 0.97093) and self-dependent production (actual 0.00000). Without probes the class is identified in 25.0 per cent of cases; with a complete audit pulse and a short producer outage, in 100.0 per cent, and withheld interventions are predicted. It holds only "within a known catalog and with every probe run on a counterfactual reset": "A posterior over a known catalog is not identification of an unknown law." CASE-163 is Proposed because the re-read of its stored results that CASE-161 had before its promotion has not been made; "with that repository attached the re-read is the whole of the fix" (its note of 2026-11-24).

**What the run says against itself.** Internal maintenance can be the more fragile arrangement:

> Maintaining a governing rule internally, by the organization's own reciprocal production, can be more fragile than having it reset from outside: arrangements identical in ordinary operation can differ in which losses are repairable, and the self-produced rule can be lost to a double loss or an interruption of the work that produces it which outside reset survives (CLAIM-456, Active, demonstrated, on CASE-161).

"It says nothing against self-governance, only that self-production is not robustness" (CLAIM-456). Both cases also say where their boundary comes from: "The producer teams, schedules and production law are assigned by the modeller, so 'internal' means inside the simulated population by stipulation" (CASE-161). That is the boundary §1.5 says the inference needs, and it is why the cases do not show a population drawing its own.

**Neighbours.** The same concealment can be kept in place by the questions asked: of two ecosystems "equivalent under the routine queries", the one "that optimized away capacities only an unasked query needs is rewarded" (CLAIM-1054, Proposed, interpretive). And matched output can hide a difference in readiness rather than in maintenance: in a matched-performance model of regulation, "every regime produces the same output until a contingency arrives, while readiness at a deadline of six periods ranges from 0.147 to 0.894" (CASE-412, Active, an executed study). A51 gives CASE-412 its principal home in Article 2, and this article cites it only as the same shape in another quantity.

### 1.2 Which individuation?

A claim that something is an individual can mean different things. The record's term for this lists four senses (TERM-088, Active):

- "descriptive (an inquiry can distinguish and track it)";
- "dynamical (its organization persists under specified dynamics and perturbation, with robust identity and boundaries)";
- "constitutive (its relational organization helps fix its identity and boundary)";
- "organizational autonomy, the further achievement of contributing to the maintenance of its own constitutive constraints". This is closure in TERM-070's sense (Active): "a set of constraints each of which depends directly on another in the set and has another depending on it".

The senses "are not stages, and not degrees of existence. A pattern can be dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside, like a convection roll fed by an imposed gradient. That is individuation without autonomy" (TERM-088). This is the owner's point, made at their second review of the exchange of 2026-10-27: "A persistent externally sustained pattern may still be a dynamically individuated entity" (TERM-088, summary; CLAIM-474, version 2). An organization's being an individual is one question. Whose processes keep it going is another.

Two further cautions come with the term.

- **TERM-088 names what is claimed, "not how it is measured".** Boundaries, coupling strength, coherence and persistence are criteria, and none of them is a sense. A criterion is also relative: "An individuation is well defined only once its criterion is fixed (the target it serves, the transformations its unity must survive, the scale), and that dependence does not make it arbitrary: once the criterion is fixed a verdict can be false" (CLAIM-405, Active, interpretive). It rests on "Any individuation built on sufficiency is relative to the target it is sufficient for" (CLAIM-152, Active, demonstrated). The record's proposed criterion for when a combined form is one system is a block of nearly decomposable dynamics whose gap beats an Occam factor (CLAIM-157, Proposed, interpretive), its answer to QUESTION-035 (Open). It is one measure of the dynamical sense.
- **TERM-088 excludes a sense.** It sets aside "the genetic, or operational, sense of Simondon's readers, individuation as the operation by which a metastable system resolves a disparity and yields a provisional individual". That sense names "the process that would produce a candidate, not a claim about one", and "a claim that means it says so" (TERM-088). The record holds one reading of it in organization studies: Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy, after Simondon, for whom research into individuation follows "the being in its genesis" (THEORY-1628, Active). This article uses that sense in §1.6, and says so there.

A51's abstract names "the descriptive, dynamical and constitutive achievements through which organizations are individuated". Those are three of TERM-088's four senses, which the term says are not stages. A51's thesis, that the account "treats organizational identity as a plurality of related achievements rather than a single equivalence relation", is A's integration, and §1.13 tests it against the record (J1.9).

### 1.3 Achievements in a partial order

At the owner's request of 2026-10-27, the record set out the relations between interconnection and individuation "as a research program with distinct testable transitions, rather than a cluster of philosophically suggestive analogies". The result is CLAIM-474 (Active, interpretive), in the record's voice:

> From interconnection to individuation, the relationships are distinct achievements that form a partial order, not a nested sequence: none implies the next, and a later one need not require every earlier one.

Interconnection fixes what joint behaviour is possible. Compatibility fixes which local descriptions admit a coherent whole. Dynamics fixes which organizations persist, individuation which have "sufficiently robust identity and boundaries", organizational autonomy which "help maintain their own constitutive constraints", and inquiry which can revise their own models under resource bounds, "inquiry and reconstruction occurring without autonomy" (CLAIM-474). Two of the claim's listed failures of implication bear directly on this article:

- "**Persistence without individuality.** Synchrony can come from a common drive";
- "**Persistence without autonomy.** A pattern can be maintained entirely by external supply."

The claim was first stated as a nested sequence. It became a partial order at the owner's decision of 2026-10-31, with the owner's rule of method: "derive the dependency structure of the achievements separately from the order in which an inquiry met them" (CLAIM-474, version 3 and body). The order of this article's sections is an order of exposition, not a claim about which achievement comes first in any organization.

Three cautions on using the claim:

- **It has one run behind it, and the run is Proposed.** Its central experiment is CASE-179 (Active, a proposed study), which manipulates coupling, correspondence maps and their revision independently. The first run of that design is CASE-333 (Proposed, an executed study). Under a shared drive, uncoupled and coupled networks lock alike and their offsets glue alike. Displacing one member's drive moves the others by 0.2 per cent of its shift without coupling and by 30 to 58 per cent with it. After the drive is withdrawn, order is 0.47 without coupling and 0.996 with it (CLAIM-903, Proposed, demonstrated, on CASE-333).
- **An extension proposed by A is only noted.** A32 §22 inserts contracts into the order as one more achievement. The record holds this as a note on CLAIM-474, "by A, not argued for beyond its statement". Article 3 (file 02) takes contracts up.
- **The claim orders achievements of organization, not of memes.** The record rejected A4's use of CLAIM-474 for the memetic nucleation hypothesis: "CLAIM-474 orders other things, and the hypothesis needs no such order" (ARG-tmpeukxb, Rejected; the assistant's).

### 1.4 Coupling, closure and reciprocal maintenance

A51 asks next for "the distinctions among coupling, closure, reciprocal maintenance, individuation and autonomy". The record holds three, each in its own entry.

**Coupling does not register who made it.**

> A gap or coupling criterion cannot tell a boundary a system produces from one imposed on it: a membrane the cell makes and a beam joining two pendulums pass alike, so the block test cannot by itself supply the individuality minimal agency needs (CLAIM-194, Active, interpretive, a counter to CLAIM-157 and CLAIM-174).

"The criterion has no term for who made the coupling" (CLAIM-194). The reading that places the line at production, THEORY-072, is Proposed.

**A persisting capacity needs a productive cycle, and a cycle is not closure.** Where every constraint capacity turns over and is produced only by others, "capacities whose production graph is acyclic all decay to zero: a capacity that persists needs a productive cycle among its ancestors". The cycle "is necessary and not sufficient, and a one-member cycle is not closure" (CLAIM-516, Active, demonstrated). Closure in TERM-070's sense needs two or more constraints. Entrenchment is no substitute: an entrenched core "can sit at the root of an acyclic dependency order, and where interactions turn over such an order persists only while something outside supplies its core" (CLAIM-531, Active, interpretive). CASE-161's self-dependent production graph "does not regenerate a zeroed rule" (its title), which is the same point in the population.

**Informational closure is not closure of constraints, and it does not keep intervention responses.** The record separates the senses of "closure" so that a claim says which (TERM-068, Active, adopted by the owner). Informational closure, a description whose own past predicts its future as well as the full state does, is a fact about prediction, and it can hold while a description loses what an intervention would reveal:

> A coarse-graining exactly informationally closed under a process's ordinary dynamics need not preserve its responses to an intervention on the mechanism (CLAIM-407, Active, demonstrated; A's, from A16 and A17 of the exchange of 2026-10-20).

The claim's measure is the deficiency Γ = I(S; Y^(I) | T(S), I) for a declared distribution of interventions, and "Γ = 0 exactly when, for every intervention ρ charges, the response kernels are constant on T's fibres over the states of positive probability" (CLAIM-407). It is "an application of ordinary causal sufficiency, not new information theory". A51's CASE-164 (Proposed, a construction, from the owner's PerspectiveLab) is its second exact witness. On one 192-state organization with the three maintenance modes of CASE-161, "closure on a support, global lumpability and sufficiency for a declared intervention mixture give different verdicts": the description by agents and phase is informationally closed on the intact support, its strong-lumpability defect is 0.42 globally and 0 on that support ("Closure on a support is not lumpability of the chain"), and its deficiency is 0.55·H₂(1/3) ≈ 0.5051 bits for every description that omits the maintenance mode and 0 for the full state. Every verdict is relative to a declared stationary law, since the chain has none of its own. In finite samples, "a finite-sample sign test of closure fails in both directions at the sample sizes tested" (CASE-164). Refinement does not order closure either: a deficiency of sufficiency "cannot increase under refinement", while informational closure "is monotone in neither direction" (CLAIM-409, Active, demonstrated). CASE-164 is Proposed for the same reason as CASE-163 (its note of 2026-11-24).

The record has declined one join here. That CLAIM-522's pair (§1.5) is CLAIM-407's shape, with the producer's location as the hidden bit, is "already stated by CLAIM-414" and is a substitution, not a new claim (SYNTHESIS-079, "Not stated on the record"). This article does not make it.

**Two constructions of reciprocal maintenance** (CASE-189, Active, a construction from A9 to A12 of "Analyze nucleation concepts", recomputed independently):

- In the first, two constraints that produce each other hold a three-unit coupling. The coupling survives damage up to 0.4 and meets a saddle-node at damage 0.5.
- In the second, "a transient encounter … forms a connector enabling that reciprocal production, after which the connector is maintained by what it enables, every single-pathway knockout ends it, and the regime ends in a saddle-node at damage ≈ 0.691 where the coupling's gap is still ≈ 0.989".

The second shows a further dissociation: "An organization's present coupling strength need not reveal the stability of the process maintaining it" (CLAIM-521, Active, demonstrated). The record notes that this is generic for such folds and "built in rather than surprising".

What "realizes" means here needs care. "That a mechanism realizes a capacity can mean that the capacity is produced through it over time or that the capacity consists in it at a time, and interventions show only the first" (CLAIM-528, Active, interpretive). In this article, "maintained by" means produced through, the causal sense, which interventions can test. When constraints only characterize an entity and when they partly constitute it is still open (QUESTION-284, Open).

### 1.5 What interventions on state cannot show, and where the boundary comes from

The next claim is A10 §4's of "Analyze nucleation concepts", checked by the record:

> Recovery under interventions on state cannot show where maintenance is realized: an outside controller that measures the constraint capacities and supplies the internal production law as state feedback yields the same trajectory under every perturbation of state, damage and recovery included, even with every capacity observed exactly; only interventions on the production mechanisms, relative to a declared boundary, separate the two (CLAIM-522, Active, demonstrated).

"Full observation of c changes nothing." What separates the two systems is "disabling a production pathway inside the declared boundary (A loses it, B does not), or interrupting the controller (B loses it, A does not)" (CLAIM-522).

**The claim is scoped to the ideal controller, and the record holds the rungs below it.** A controller that reproduces less of the production law can be found out by interventions on state.

- In the mutual-maintenance construction, "a constant outside controller holding r at its maintained value gives the same equilibrium but recovers from c = 0.01, where the mutual system collapses" (CASE-265, Proposed, a construction; from A5 of "Explain String Theory", numbers recomputed by the filer). A calls it "another explicit witness rather than an entirely new proposition" for CLAIM-522, and the record adds that the constant controller "is found out by perturbing the state".
- A ladder of controllers in the same model: a constant supply, the production law run on a delayed reading, and the law on the present reading. "A brief knock to the structure separates the first two from internal production, the second by less as its delay shrinks, and no intervention on state separates the third until the internal pathway is cut" (CASE-268, Proposed, the assistant's construction). The largest gap under the knock falls from 0.146 for the constant controller to 0.010 for a delay of 0.03 and to 0 for the exact law.
- The general reading is Proposed: "The interventions needed to find that a structure's maintenance comes from outside grow with how much of the internal production law the outside maintainer reproduces" (CLAIM-693, Proposed, demonstrated for the delay family; the assistant's). "That the ordering holds beyond that family is the claim's general reading and stays open" (CLAIM-693).
- In CASE-332 (Proposed, an executed study), a constant-rate outside service was told apart by zeroing the state (0 of 64 second corrections against 64 of 64), which CLAIM-522's note calls "outside this claim's ideal case" and no counterexample. CASE-161's audit pulse also parts its external reset (§1.1).

**The boundary is not found by the experiments.** CLAIM-522 says the boundary "is not given": which mechanism is inside is fixed by a declared boundary, "and a gap criterion cannot supply it (CLAIM-194)". The mechanist account of constitution reaches the same place. Under mutual manipulability, an internal producer and an outside controller of this kind come out alike, "so what puts one inside and the other outside is parthood, a boundary the experiments presuppose and do not find" (CLAIM-530, Active, interpretive). The record has declined two joins here. That passive observation, interventions on state, interventions on mechanism and the boundary form "one ladder of what separates" is "a juxtaposition" of stated steps; and that CLAIM-516's productive cycle supplies the boundary CLAIM-530 says the experiments presuppose is, "with no basal production", "close to a definition" (SYNTHESIS-079, "Not stated on the record"). This article states the steps one by one, and makes neither join. CLAIM-693's ladder is another order, of outside maintainers by how much of the law they copy, and it is filed.

### 1.6 Productive nucleation, transient support, persistence and regeneration

This section uses "individuation" in the genetic sense, which TERM-088 excludes, and says so. It asks how a form comes to be and stays, not whether a candidate is an individual. The owner asked the record's question at U14 of 2026-10-27 ("bifurcation/catastrophe theory? far from equilibrium dynamics? pattern formation?"), and it is open:

> By which dynamical transitions do the distinctions individuation needs come into being and pass away (instability that amplifies small differences, nucleation across a finite barrier from a locally stable state, bifurcation of the set of attractors, noise- or rate-induced tipping), and are a form's creation, its stabilization and its coming to have a constitutive role three separate achievements? (QUESTION-281, Open)

**Assembling, persisting and regenerating are three things.** "That a form assembles, that it persists over a specified horizon and that the system regenerates it after turnover are three properties with separate probabilities" (CLAIM-566, Active, interpretive; A's). SYNTHESIS-043 (Proposed) says the realizability line's four parts "meet at the separation of assembling, persisting and regenerating a form". Persistence is not productivity: "Persistence and nucleating productivity in the committor sense vary independently" (CLAIM-595, Active, demonstrated; SYNTHESIS-078, Proposed, is its account). In the owner's Life constructions, CASE-260 (Proposed) has a block created and destroyed two generations later, a lone block stabilized with no role, and the blocks of Gosper's gun in a constitutive role (CASE-259, Proposed, is its companion). "Taking on a constitutive role can change which of these holds", so stabilization and role "though they can come apart, are not independent" (CLAIM-672, Proposed, demonstrated).

**Founding and feeding differ.** Where every replacement part is supplied from outside, an organization "can become independent of what founded it but never of what feeds it" (CLAIM-597, Active, demonstrated). In the mutual-maintenance construction, "a founding pulse can be withdrawn and the supply cannot", and the survival threshold "is a separatrix in the joint state and not a level of c" (CASE-265). Where repair draws on a flux at a rate proportional to the intact share, as a flame's upkeep does, there is "an ignition threshold below which it goes out with the supply still on", a supply level below which it collapses, and an outage after which restoring supply does not relight it. Supplied repair "has none of the three" (CLAIM-604, Active, demonstrated).

**Dependence comes in kinds.** A51's sketch names CASE-225 (Proposed, a construction, A's). A self-sustained and a damped oscillator "give the same drive-locked output"; an inhibitory control holds the self-sustained one at rest while it still answers the drive; withdrawing the drive alone lets the output die, and withdrawing drive and control "restores the limit cycle", "so dependence for timing, dependence for performance and suppression of a latent capacity are three things" (CASE-225). Its filer reran the numbers. It is CLAIM-414's and CLAIM-522's point in an oscillator: "The output under drive does not tell the three apart; only the withdrawals do."

**Withdrawing a transient support is safe only under conditions.** "Transient support can be withdrawn safely only if it started a process that keeps itself going on a flux that will stay available, was large enough to carry the supported party past any threshold that process has, outlasted the threat it stood in for, and left the work to the party throughout". The claim continues, "meeting them all is not enough", and "persistence after withdrawal is needed to tell which, though slow reversion can mimic it" (CLAIM-600, Active, interpretive; the assistant's, at the owner's request of 2026-11-05). A's withdrawal test, "establish a nucleus with external support, withdraw the support at matched intermediate states", is open (QUESTION-398, Open; A's wording). The record has run one kinetic version of the moment of withdrawal. In CASE-365 (Proposed, an executed study, the assistant's), nuclei form fast only on a teacher and, once formed, make teachers. The outside teacher can be withdrawn once the joint state crosses a separatrix, which happened "with nuclei at 0.02 % to 30 % of their maintained number and a self-made share of 0.2 % to 65 %", so neither count marks the moment (CLAIM-953, Proposed, demonstrated). Material supply is still needed.

**A seed works only in a window.** In the seeded fold model, "a transient seed leaves a maintained organization only inside the bistable window and only by carrying the state past the unstable threshold". Because the seed enters as a scalar forcing, "the model expresses the environment's receptivity but not which organization a seed nucleates" (CLAIM-tmpq1lyz, Active since 2026-11-24 at the assistant's discretion, demonstrated; the assistant's gap fill, not a member of the work).

**CASE-189 is narrower than A51's grouping makes it.** In its own words it is "a bistable system pushed across a separatrix", in which "Which terms are internal is stipulated, the substrates are assumed available" (CASE-189). It is not of CLAIM-604's kind: CLAIM-604's model draws repair from a supply at a rate set by the intact share, and CASE-189 has no supply variable. Of CLAIM-604's three signatures it shows the threshold; collapse appears in the damage parameter, not in a supply; and an outage of supply is undefined (the joins reader, J1.4). CASE-265, with a standing supply, is the record's two-variable extension of CLAIM-604's model, and it shows the threshold as a separatrix. Even where all three signatures are found, they do not certify that upkeep is self-sustaining. An outside crew delivering repair at the same state-dependent rate gives the same equation, so the inference is an instance of CLAIM-522 and needs a declared boundary (SYNTHESIS-048, Proposed, "Not stated on the record").

### 1.7 Formation provenance and the design

#### 1.7.1 Formation provenance is a fact about history

A51's subtitle names "maintenance provenance". The record keeps it apart from two other provenances. At the owner's review of 2026-11-03, it filed three senses (TERM-100, Active):

| sense | when | what can test it (TERM-100) |
|---|---|---|
| formation: "whose process produced it" | the history of production | "an intervention on the producing process while it runs, or a record kept then (CASE-191)" |
| maintenance: "whose process keeps it in being now" | the present | "knockouts of the processes that produce and uphold it (CLAIM-522)" |
| source: what it was made from | the history of a transport | a fidelity profile against the source (TERM-084's sense) |

"The first two come apart" (TERM-100). The case that shows it was built at the owner's request (CASE-191, Active, a construction, fictional, `scratch/formal/self_formation.py`). It compares three agents: one whose own frustration at two demands produces a capacity; one whose capacity was installed by an outside pulse and is then maintained by the agent's own cycle; and one whose capacity is upheld from outside. "A capacity upheld from outside is told apart by knockouts afterwards, but one installed by an outside pulse and then self-maintained is told apart only by a knockout during formation". "After the fact the two are the same organization" (CASE-191).

So a design run on matched organizations that have already formed can reach maintenance provenance by mechanism knockouts. In CASE-191's construction, no knockout after formation reaches formation provenance; a knockout during formation does, and TERM-100 adds a record kept then. That is shown for one fictional construction with a scalar capacity. No entry says that formation leaves no other trace after the fact. CASE-191 says where a trace might be in a richer object: if self-determination theory is right, an outside-installed and a self-formed capacity "must differ somewhere this model lacks", "not in the capacity, which is identical, but in its relations to the rest" (citing THEORY-047 and THEORY-054, both Proposed). This paragraph restates TERM-100's table and CASE-191 (J1.2). One route by which an outside party could act on formation is held as a claim: reflexive control can "seed interpretive, evidence-selecting and authority-allocating dispositions that the target then maintains itself", which "leaves formation provenance outside while maintenance is the target's own" (CLAIM-tmpp0ufw, Proposed, interpretive). Article 6 takes it up (§6.2).

#### 1.7.2 Intervention or history

An earlier objection in the record has the same shape. A45 of the exchange of 2026-10-17, against the owner's non-domination criterion:

> Processes conditioned never to object coordinate without override at the same behaviour and supervisory cost as processes that negotiate, so the two part only under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or in their history (CLAIM-364, Proposed, interpretive, a counter).

"Every discriminating test is an intervention or a history" (CLAIM-364). A45 also flagged a test that does not discriminate: "Remove the higher-level supervisor temporarily", since compliance may persist too.

The first draft read CLAIM-364's two discriminators as TERM-100's two provenances. An independent reader found that this misstates the maintenance sense: removing the supervisor is a knockout of an outside upholder, and both kinds of coordination survive it, so they share maintenance provenance. The corrected statement passed ADR-075's test by derivation and is filed:

> Conditioned and negotiated coordination share their maintenance provenance, since both persist when the supervisor is removed, so the interventions that part them test how the constituents' own processes answer dissent, not whose process keeps the arrangement, and removing the supervisor fails to discriminate because it is the one maintenance-provenance test among them (CLAIM-tmp9ia3w, Proposed, demonstrated; the reader's wording, filed by the assistant).

The history half holds as the draft had it: history reads formation provenance (TERM-100; CLAIM-tmp9ia3w, "What it does not say"). The claim's defeater needs a boundary "declared in advance (CLAIM-522, CLAIM-194)". SYNTHESIS-023 (Proposed) declines a different link, between CLAIM-364's interventions and a non-domination mark of integration, so it does not bear on this one.

#### 1.7.3 The design, restated, and what the record has run of it

A49's formal centre, rebuilt from these entries, has three arms matched on ordinary behaviour:

- **(a) self-formed:** the organization's own processes produced the maintained object and maintain it;
- **(b) installed, then self-maintained:** an outside process produced it, and the organization's own processes maintain it;
- **(c) upheld:** an outside controller supplies the internal production law as state feedback, CLAIM-522's ideal case.

For a scalar capacity this design has already been run. CASE-191 *is* it: its arms *own*, *pulse* and *upheld* are (a), (b) and (c), and its upheld agent's outside controller supplies "P(G) and P(S) as feedback", CLAIM-522's ideal case (the joins reader, J1.3). For governing rules in a stipulated population, CASE-161 and CASE-163 run its maintenance arm (§1.1). A51's §3 says the same of CASE-161: it "already implements much of the experimental methodology I proposed for the individuation paper". The steps, with what holds each:

1. **Declare the boundary before any intervention** (CLAIM-522, CLAIM-194, CLAIM-530). CASE-161 and CASE-163 declare it by construction.
2. **Intervene on the maintaining mechanisms after formation.** Disable a production pathway inside the boundary, and separately interrupt the outside controller. In CASE-191, internal knockout leaves own and pulse at 0 and upheld at 2.618; outside knockout the reverse. *This step holds by the definition of arm (c)*, which has no internal maintenance and is defined so that no state probe parts it from (a). A separating state probe would only show that the controller was not ideal (§1.5).
3. **Intervene on the producing process during formation, or keep a record then.** Expectation: only this separates (a) from (b) (TERM-100; CASE-191). Falsifier: a knockout after formation that separates (a) from (b). In CASE-191, knocking out frustration-driven production from t = 0 leaves own at 0 and pulse at 2.618, and no later knockout separates them. For an object richer than a scalar the falsifier is live (§1.7.1).
4. **Withdraw the initiating support,** and read persistence after withdrawal over a horizon fixed in advance, since "slow reversion can mimic it" (CLAIM-600). In CASE-191 the pulse arm's supply ends at t = 2 and the capacity persists. CASE-333 withdraws a shared drive (§1.3), and CASE-365 times a withdrawal (§1.6). For memes, the record names the same test: whether a hyperstition's product is maintained by its own processes "is a fact about maintenance provenance, told apart by withdrawing the circulation once the realization has formed" (CLAIM-tmpyp5bu, Proposed, interpretive).
5. **Run state interventions too.** *This step also holds by the definition of arm (c)*: under the ideal controller they cannot separate (a) from (c) (CLAIM-522). They are a control, and they do part non-ideal controllers (CASE-265, CASE-268, CASE-332; CASE-161's audit pulse).

So steps 3 and 4 carry the design's test, together with a question the design cannot answer from inside: whether any real system realizes arm (c).

**What remains.** No entry holds a case in which the maintained object is a perspective, or anything richer than a scalar capacity or a governing rule. That extension is LEAD-tmpu1jj5 (Open, specified): "Test formation and maintenance provenance for a maintained object richer than a scalar capacity, first in artificial agents, with interventions on the maintaining mechanism inside a declared boundary and dispositional models as the rival". Its note of 2026-11-24 narrows it: "What stays open is this lead's own addition: an object richer than a scalar rule, such as a perspective, and the dispositional rival". The note carries a warning from CASE-346 (Active, an executed study): "a habit of matched memory reproduces every intervention it can receive", so the design "needs an intervention inside the boundary that a habit cannot receive". The lead's expectation, stated before any run: the knockout "degrades it while state interventions do not", and the dispositional rival "predicts no difference between arm 2 and the control" (LEAD-tmpu1jj5). The perspective-transplantation study is not run (CASE-148, Active, a proposed study), and its source warns: "No transfer experiment can settle numerical identity merely by demonstrating causal continuity" (A4, in CASE-148). The joins reader noted one prediction that would pass ADR-075's test if anyone put it forward: that for an object richer than a scalar, (b) can be told from (a) after formation, "by interventions on how the formed part is integrated with the rest". This article does not assert it.

### 1.8 Symbiosis: dependence without a new individual

A51 adds "Biological symbiosis" as "a complementary evidential domain", on SYNTHESIS-053 (Proposed), whose title states what the record holds:

> When formerly autonomous partners come to depend on one another, which properties each route to dependence brings about, and which make the partnership a new individual, is open: the documented symbioses show dependence, reproductive cohesion, the distribution of functions across genomes and host control coming apart, dependence directional, and integration undone as well as deepened, and the record holds that gene ancestries and stem lengths alone cannot tell a partner from a donor or time its acquisition (SYNTHESIS-053).

Its question asks, for each route by which partners lose autonomy, which of "mutual dependence, reproductive cohesion, the distribution of essential functions across genomes, the host's regulatory control and the alignment of selection" it brings about, "and which of them, alone or together, make the partnership a new evolutionary individual" (QUESTION-277, Open). The exchange's thesis, in its words: "A system can become more interdependent without becoming more cooperative, more unified, more autonomous, or even more adaptive" (in SYNTHESIS-053). The record "takes no position on its answer until the works are read".

- **The specimens** are six, each Proposed: aphids and *Buchnera* (CASE-173), the mealybug's nested symbionts (CASE-171), *Hodgkinia* in cicadas, where "dependence and the number of parts grow with no new collective function, and the host pays" (CASE-177), *Paulinella* (CASE-174), the nitroplast (CASE-167), and *Monocercomonoides* (CASE-168), "the negative control: loss of function dissolving an integrated compartment, not deepening dependence on it".
- **What the phylogenomic evidence cannot show** is held in three Active interpretive claims, made Active at delegated status reviews. Among them: "Gene ancestries alone cannot tell a symbiotic partner from a source of transferred genes" (CLAIM-508).

This is the record's one domain in which the dimensions of this article are documented in organisms rather than constructed. It bears on §1.3: dependence is one achievement and individuality another, and here they come apart in the record's cases. The account declines to say more: "What would answer the question, a criterion tested on the worked systems, is not on the record" (SYNTHESIS-053, "Not stated on the record"). This article does not map QUESTION-277's five dimensions onto TERM-088's senses; no entry does (G1.8).

### 1.9 Persistence: product, producer and lineage

A49 says that "organizational persistence is better understood through the maintenance and regeneration of characteristic relationships than through the preservation of fixed components", and A51 that organizations "can preserve characteristic relations through changes of components and modes of realization". The second half of A49's sentence has support on the record. The first half needs a correction from it. The record's term for A49's phrase is normative: "constitutional regeneration, of an organization: its capacity to maintain valuable constitutive relationships through changes in the processes that realize them, while remaining answerable to justified claims concerning those relationships" (TERM-tmpx4zke, Proposed). Article 14 takes it up. This section is about persistence alone.

**Persistence does not need fixed components.** The record's case is the owner's, from r/place. An artwork defended for days has its pixels repeatedly replaced, its defenders come and go, and its attackers keep working, "yet the image stays recognizable". The claim, in A8's wording:

> A pattern can persist while every constituent is replaced and some constituents work against it, as a high-turnover regime of damage and repair, so the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts (CLAIM-280, Active, interpretive).

The claim does not say "that the pattern, or the coalition, is a self".

**What persists through turnover is carried by transmission, not resemblance.**

> A collective that replaces all its members persists, when it does, by causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by resemblance: an organization of the same kind that arises independently is not its continuation (CLAIM-329, Active, interpretive).

The formal form is Active: "Isomorphism is not persistence, in either direction", so "which morphisms count as continuations is structure added by assumptions about the domain" (CLAIM-593, Active, demonstrated). The record's Proposed thesis on individuals carries this further. Identity over time is "carried by the causal transmission of the relations that keep its repertoire realizable, not by its constituents or by any structure invariant across its realizations", so that "two structurally identical copies can be two individuals" (CLAIM-615, Proposed, interpretive). Much of it is the owner's (U14 and U15 of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"), and the integration is A's. Its account is SYNTHESIS-046 (Proposed). What must stay continuous, and which of several successors continues the original, is still open (QUESTION-118, Open).

So "characteristic relationships" cannot carry persistence by being characteristic. A character, in the record's sense, classifies "kinds, not tokens or positions" (CLAIM-132, Active, demonstrated). Two unrelated organizations can share one (CLAIM-417, item 2). If a character were what persisted, persistence would be resemblance, which CLAIM-329 excludes. What persists is the lineage, a causal chain of realizations. A51's "causal transmission supplies a separate criterion of historical continuity" says the same, and is CLAIM-329. This section restates CLAIM-329, CLAIM-615 and CLAIM-417 together (J1.5, a restatement).

Two neighbouring integrations have been declined by the record's accounts, and this article does not make them:

- that a collective's product, carrier and perspective "each persist by their own criterion": "no case on file separates a product from a perspective" (SYNTHESIS-047, Proposed, "Not stated on the record");
- that a block's lasting (CLAIM-157) "is what persistence by causal transmission (CLAIM-615) measures": "a free parameter, what makes a later block 'that block'" (SYNTHESIS-049, Proposed, "Not stated on the record").

### 1.10 Character, realization, lineage and representation

#### 1.10.1 Four objects kept apart, and a fifth

The owner proposed at U31 of 2026-10-22 that the record's earlier "selves" are "better described as 'organizational perspectives'" (CLAIM-417, Proposed, interpretive). The claim's sharp edge is what the replacement must keep apart:

1. "A perspective as realized", in one carrier or distributed across several (TERM-072, Active, sense 1);
2. "Its character, a pattern by which realizations are recognized as of one kind. Two unrelated traditions can share one";
3. "Its lineage, a chain of causal transmission from one realization to the next";
4. "Its representations: self-descriptions, narratives, reputations, which can misdescribe the other three".

A40, in the claim: "Resemblance ≠ Historical descent ≠ Numerical organizational identity". TERM-072 records that it was "Adopted 2026-10-29, with character and lineage read as relations among realizations." The claim does not say "that perspectives are agents" (§1.12).

The claim's note of 2026-11-07 records a fifth object: A50 §5 "adds a 'dispositional basis' (CLAIM-421) beside realization, character and lineage; A52 §7 restores representation, which makes five objects". CLAIM-421 (Active, interpretive) says why: "The cessation of an organizational perspective's enactment need not imply the loss of the dispositions, capacities or reproductive relationships from which it can be reinstated". The claim is held back by a live competitor: "habitus and transposable schemas may explain the same transitions, and no model in the record out-predicts them at matched complexity" (CLAIM-417, notes of 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-20). Habitus is a theory of a dispositional basis, so the competitor is pitched at the fifth object, and LEAD-tmpu1jj5 names it as the rival (§1.7.3). Until a model out-predicts it, the vocabulary of perspectives is a proposal, not a result.

#### 1.10.2 What character theory gives, and on what condition

The owner's U54 put the priority of characters: "Character's are 'more essential' than the representations that are comprised of them". The record keeps it as classificatory priority (CLAIM-137, Proposed, interpretive):

> A communicative kind is identified by an invariant that does not depend on any one realization, as a group character identifies a representation up to isomorphism, once one symmetry group is identified across the realizations by its action on the situation and not by labels of a medium, so that invariant has classificatory priority over any particular realization.

The record corrects the owner's wording: "The owner's 'comprised of' inverts the mathematics". A representation decomposes into irreducible representations, and its character is computed from it, so "The owner's priority survives the correction" (CLAIM-137). The claim does not say "That classificatory priority is constitution", and "No such invariant has yet been built for any communicative case".

Three accepted limits bound the analogy.

- **Characters classify kinds up to isomorphism, and only in a narrow setting.** They do so for finite-dimensional representations of a finite or compact group "only over a field of characteristic zero". They "forget the isomorphisms, fix neither the group nor a permutation action, and classify kinds, not tokens or positions" (CLAIM-132, Active, demonstrated, granted). SYNTHESIS-077 (Proposed) is the part account of what an invariant keeps and forgets, and puts "identifiability before automorphisms and characters".
- **"The same character" depends on a chosen correspondence.** Comparing characters across realizations "needs one group identified on both, which is fixed only up to automorphism, and automorphisms permute the irreducible characters, so 'the same character' is relative to a chosen correspondence" (CLAIM-249, Active, demonstrated, granted).
- **The word "character" is reserved.** It is used literally only "when χ is a trace character of a specified representation". Otherwise the object is a "character-like structural signature" (TERM-041, Active, after A203 §36). This article follows that rule. The record uses the word outside TERM-041's sense once, and says so: "agnotological character, of a perspective: a candidate invariant profile of its organized epistemic limitations … not a group character" (TERM-tmp9mag8, Proposed).

The step from groups to communicative kinds is made on a condition that is "still unmet" (CLAIM-137, note of 2026-11-24), and the step from communicative kinds to perspectives is "new territory, with CLAIM-249 standing against any easy identification" (CLAIM-417). The first thing the record needs, for any perspective, is a declared set of transformations that count as the same in both realizations: "Say which changes of situation count as the same transformation in both populations, the correspondence CLAIM-249 says comparison needs, before comparing profiles" (LEAD-tmpqucxc, Open, idea, step 4).

#### 1.10.3 What a character can see

One result here is Active, and it limits what a character can be used for. It is the assistant's gap fill for A6 §3 of the exchange behind this series, made Active on 2026-11-24 at the assistant's discretion after an independent recomputation:

> The character of a perspective's representation detects the transformations it is blind to, not the states it merges: its kernel is the observational invariance subgroup when the projection is equivariant and its image spans, but a projection can merge states no transformation relates while its character shows no blindness at all (CLAIM-tmpqbaic, Active, demonstrated; not a member of the work).

The claim is about one representation, the permutation representation of G on Y_P "as A6 §3 sets them up", and is "Not defeated by another choice of representation". Where a perspective's representation is of that kind, its character profiles its invariances under the chosen transformations. It does not record every distinction the perspective fails to draw.

#### 1.10.4 Resource-bounded profiles

A51's abstract offers "Representation-theoretic characterizations and resource-bounded profiles" as "complementary means of investigating these distinctions". The record holds what such profiles do, in the realizability line's part on budgeted individuation (SYNTHESIS-040, Proposed):

- "Distinctions drawn by restricted Yoneda profiles only grow as nested probe families grow", so "an equivalence a richer regime breaks was relative to its regime, not false", and "the monotonicity needs an every-probe comparison, which an averaged distortion is not" (CLAIM-574, Active, demonstrated; A's).
- "Which category is chosen changes which configurations count as the same": in one configuration space every two configurations are uniquely isomorphic in the groupoid, while in the category enriched in peak footprint two folded states are 1.866 apart, "so equivalence of possibilities is not equivalence of resource-bounded capabilities" (CLAIM-590, Active, demonstrated).
- Observing and acting budgets run in opposite directions, "so a system's budgeted individuation runs in opposite directions from outside and from inside and is indexed by a pair of budgets, not one" (CLAIM-557, Active, demonstrated; the assistant's).

The account's title ends: "neither isomorphism nor a profile settles persistence" (SYNTHESIS-040). So profiles index the descriptive sense of individuation by a budget, and the record holds that they do not settle the persistence §1.9 asks about. No entry builds a profile of an organization and uses it to tell two organizations apart (G1.6). This article reports the profiles' limit, not their use (departure 4).

#### 1.10.5 Memes and contracts, for Articles 7 and 3

At U4 the owner proposed "memes as characteristics (qua representation theory) of perspectives". The record filed it in two readings. On 2026-11-24 the owner ruled: "I think both readings are valid. I mainly meant 'meme as value'". ### 1.12 Agency as a further achievement

A49's last section asks why individuation alone does not establish autonomy, consciousness, moral standing or legitimate authority. A51's Article 1 drops it (departure 1). The record holds each separation in its own entry.

- **Not autonomy.** "Persistence without autonomy. A pattern can be maintained entirely by external supply" (CLAIM-474). Individuation without autonomy is TERM-088's convection roll.
- **Not agency.** "Possessing, enacting or being shaped by an organizational perspective does not establish an independent agent, and perspectival, agential and institutional boundaries need not coincide" (CLAIM-418, Active, interpretive). A31, in CLAIM-417: "Recognizing an organizational perspective does not license attributing desires, intentions, authority, ownership, or experience to that perspective without independent justification".
- **Not reflexive governance, and not governing well.** Reflexive governance is "a capacity some organizations of perspective have and others lack, not what makes something a perspective; and the capacity to revise those standards is not the capacity to revise them well" (CLAIM-424, Active, interpretive). It is not TERM-088's autonomy, which is a relation among constraints.
- **Not experience.** Phenomenal perspective is TERM-072's third sense, "A lived point of view", kept apart from the organizational sense, and the subject sense of "self" "stays a separate question" (CLAIM-417).
- **Not standing or legitimacy.** "Viability does not establish what ought to persist": "An unjust institution may be highly viable" (CLAIM-075, Active, interpretive, disclaimed). For moral standing, "The grounds and extent of standing remain questions for ethical argument, not conclusions of the definition" (TERM-083, Proposed, the owner's).

Set side by side, these separations are A49's list. *Putting them in one list adds nothing to them* (J1.7, a conjunction of held entries). Individuation, in any of TERM-088's senses, is compatible with every one of these further achievements failing. Later articles in the series must establish each one they need on its own evidence; A51's Article 4 takes up will and authority.
