  estimates the metric to accuracy ε with probability 1−δ from

# LIT-948: Distances for Markov chains from sample streams

Sergio Calo, Anders Jonsson, Gergely Neu, Ludovic Schwartz and Javier Segovia-Aguas (2025), *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38* (NeurIPS 2025).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-763](../notes.d/NOTE-763.md).*

- **A kernel-free LP (Proposition 1).** The coherence constraints of
  [LIT-962](LIT-962.md)'s LP, which multiply by P_X(x'|x), are equivalent to
  Σ_{y'} µ(x,y,x',y') = ν_X(x,x') λ_X(y|x) for some conditional λ_X, and
  likewise for Y. Here ν_X is the discounted occupancy of consecutive
  state pairs, which can be sampled.
- **A guarantee from samples (Theorem 1).** SOMCOT reaches
  |⟨µ̄_K − µ*, c⟩| ≤ ε with probability 1−δ once
  K = O((|X||Y|(|X|+|Y|) + (|X|+|Y|)log(1/δ))/((1−γ)²ε²)). It uses one
  sampled transition per chain per step and Θ(|X|²|Y|²) computation per
  step, with no problem-dependent constants. The authors call it "the
  first result of its kind". The claim that the ε⁻² rate is optimal is
  argued, not proved.
- **The sampling model is narrower than the title.** The theorem assumes
  independent draws from ν_X and ν_Y. Exact draws need a run of
  geometric length from the fixed initial state, so restarts are needed.
  Correlated pairs from one long trajectory are "abuse" the authors
  "expect" the method to survive. Every experiment uses independent
  draws.
- **What must be known.** The finite state spaces (the primal variable is
  a full table over pairs of state pairs), the states themselves in each
  sample, and the ground cost on every state pair. Only the kernels may
  be unknown.
- **Uses shown.** The averaged conditionals λ_X and λ_Y act as an
  encoder–decoder that recovers latent block structure. In a parametric
  family, the distance is smallest at the true model, including in a
  discretised pendulum that is not Markov. Tuning is "a grueling task".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-008](../questions.d/QUESTION-008.md), and read
on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-763](../notes.d/NOTE-763.md). The filing said the paper "would make
approximate bisimulation measurable for systems whose update structure is
not known". The reading supports that only where the states are
observed: the kernel can be unknown, but the states cannot be latent.
The record's fidelity notion ([TERM-033](../terms.d/TERM-033.md)) compares observed responses with
no shared latent state, which is the case the estimator does not cover.
The guarantee also assumes sampling with restarts, and the chains have no
actions. The estimation result is filed as [THEORY-280](../theory.d/THEORY-280.md), with no
`answers:` relation, since the question is Deferred. It rests on [LIT-962](LIT-962.md)
([THEORY-247](../theory.d/THEORY-247.md)) for the identification of the transport distance with
the bisimulation metric. The `anthology-candidate` flag stays: the
representation-learning and model-selection uses in §§5–6 are
machine-learning practice, and belong in the anthology if anywhere.
