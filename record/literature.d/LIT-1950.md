# LIT-1950: Irruption Theory: A Novel Conceptualization of the Enactive Account of Motivated Activity

Froese, T. (2023). Irruption Theory: A Novel Conceptualization of the Enactive Account of Motivated Activity. *Entropy, 25(5), 748*. doi:10.3390/e25050748.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-1521](../notes.d/NOTE-1521.md)).

- Irruption Theory's axioms, its three theses and its commitment that
  irruptions are undirected ([THEORY-1348](../theory.d/THEORY-1348.md)).
- Its proposed answer to the randomness horn of the libertarian dilemma,
  attunement by self-optimization, in which the resets are arbitrary
  ([THEORY-1321](../theory.d/THEORY-1321.md)).
- A libertarian source that concedes the point of [CLAIM-533](../claims.d/CLAIM-533.md): amplified
  indeterminacy "cannot directly control".

## Standing in the record

The source of Irruption Theory, which [LIT-1132](LIT-1132.md) and Sánchez-Cañizares 2026 apply.
