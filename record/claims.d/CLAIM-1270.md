# CLAIM-1270: Process contracts hide in ordinary behaviour

## The claim

A16 §19, the candidate thesis: "Interdependent processes can establish
maintained relationships that specify conditional requirements, reciprocal
commitments, allocations of precedence, and mechanisms of breach
detection, arbitration and revision. These relationships can operationalize
standing without presupposing moral agency, phenomenal experience or
explicit agreement." "The characteristic structure of an organizational
individual may consequently include a maintained ecology of interprocess
contracts. Its capacity for reflexive governance consists partly in its
ability to transform those contracts and the distribution of authority
through which they are constituted."

A16 §15: "An organization may formally preserve all existing contracts
while changing the conditions through which they can effectively be
fulfilled. Conversely, it may preserve all current behavioral outcomes
while radically changing who possesses authority over future
contingencies." "Two systems with identical ordinary behavior may diverge
when a contract is breached, an assumption fails, or competing processes
invoke their standing."

## Admission

Filed from the synthesis by a separating case ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s first route).
The premises: contracts can form between processes ([CLAIM-1293](CLAIM-1293.md)); a
contract-like relation is marked by what it does on deviation, conflict
and breach ([CLAIM-1239](CLAIM-1239.md)); and processes that never object coordinate
like negotiating ones until intervention ([CLAIM-364](CLAIM-364.md)). [CASE-427](../cases.d/CASE-427.md) has
two arrangements, fixed priorities and priority inheritance, that give the
same schedule whenever the low-priority process holds no lock the
high-priority one needs, and part when it does. Every premise holds there,
and a characterization of the organization by its ordinary behaviour
cannot tell them apart. What the premises leave open, and the claim
asserts, is that this difference belongs to the organization's character,
its characteristic structure, rather than to its circumstances.

## What it does not say

- It does not say that an organization's contracts are its whole
  character, or that every regularity among its processes is a contract
  ([CLAIM-1239](CLAIM-1239.md)).
- The remainder of A16 §19 ("Operational normativity provides a formal
  substrate for analyzing requirements, standing, violations and
  correction, while their moral significance remains a distinct question
  requiring independent justification") is the owner's U14 with [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md)
  and [CLAIM-725](CLAIM-725.md), and is not restated here.

## Note of 2026-11-24: the mechanisms behind the same compliance

Also taken in from [CLAIM-1219](CLAIM-1219.md), which this claim supersedes: compliance
underdetermines the mechanism that produces it. A22 §16 lists seven that can
produce the same compliance: internalization, mutual accommodation, strategic
compliance, dependency, enforcement, constitutive stabilization and
suppression, and "These mechanisms can coexist". That is why the ordinary
course does not reveal the contract: the probes above are what tell the
mechanisms apart. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session).


## Note of 2026-11-25: which "character" is meant, from the manuscript governance-of-becoming, Article 1
<!-- inactive-ok-file: TERM-171 TERM-172 — not in force; cited in the note of 2026-11-25 with their standing -->

The manuscript's Article 1 (G1.3) and its README's open item 17 note that
no term separates this claim's "characteristic structure" from [TERM-041](../terms.d/TERM-041.md)'s
reserved "character" (the audit of what was left out (2026-11-25), B14). This claim's body also says the
difference "belongs to the organization's character, its characteristic
structure".

Which is meant: the plain sense, not [TERM-041](../terms.d/TERM-041.md)'s. [TERM-041](../terms.d/TERM-041.md) (Active) is a
realization-independent invariant of a communicative kind, "called a
character only when it is the trace of a specified representation", and
none has been constructed. This claim names no representation, no group
and no trace. What it asserts is that an organization's maintained
contracts are part of what distinguishes it, read from how it answers a
breach, a failed assumption or an invocation of standing. The held term
nearest that reading is [TERM-067](../terms.d/TERM-067.md)'s governance-response profile (Active),
the tuple of response kernels "for a declared family of interventions on
the mechanisms that govern efficacy", with the probes in this claim's
notes as the family. On that reading the claim says the profile over a
family that includes contract probes can part two arrangements the
ordinary course does not. [TERM-171](../terms.d/TERM-171.md)'s "agnotological character" and
the third sense of [TERM-172](../terms.d/TERM-172.md) use the word in the same plain sense.

The alternative is an extension of [TERM-041](../terms.d/TERM-041.md) to organizations, under which
"character" would need a specified representation of the organization's
transformations. That is a term revision and the owner's, if taken. Until
then, this claim's "character" is read as "characteristic structure", in
the sense above. The claim is unchanged; no change of status. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
