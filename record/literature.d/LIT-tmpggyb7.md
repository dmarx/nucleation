# LIT-tmpggyb7: The looping effects of human kinds

Hacking, I. (1995). The looping effects of human kinds. In D. Sperber, D. Premack, & A. J. Premack (Eds.), *Causal Cognition: A Multidisciplinary Debate* (pp. 351–383). Oxford University Press. doi:10.1093/acprof:oso/9780198524021.003.0012.

Not read: no lawful full text was reached on 2026-10-27 (see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for and from the exchange's description of it. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A9 §9 used it to split two effects of an apparatus: constitutive (a
classification establishes what counts as an entity of an institutional
kind) and looping causal (the classification changes the system
classified, which changes the classification). [CLAIM-tmpl228b](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl228b.md) claims the
first for luria's grammar and leaves the second to be tested. The record
holds Hacking's *The Social Construction of What?* ([LIT-475](LIT-475.md)),
unavailable.
