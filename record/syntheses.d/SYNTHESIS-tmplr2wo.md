# SYNTHESIS-tmplr2wo: Frustration among an organization's demands is an ordinal fact about which sets of them can be jointly met, and relief is the enlargement of that complex for the same demands, judged against a fixed reference set; the residual, governance regret and budgets refine it without identifying thwarting, quiet, thresholds and tolerances are kept apart from it, niche differentiation matters as possible relief that the open questions ask whether operators can reach by themselves, and smaller families on cycle obstructions, coexistence without cooperation, affect and roles hang from it

*A part account of the distributed-agency line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)): the part the community map of the line calls frustration, 52 entries. The line's account is built over it and the accounts of its six sibling parts (conferral, normative-institutional, unit-boundary, individuation, persistence, collective-stance). Most of its core came from the exchange of 2026-10-17 (U33 to U38), the reviews the owner relayed on 2026-10-18 and the synthesis relayed the same day; the rest from "Organizational Nucleation and SLT" (2026-11-04), "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07) and readings. Twenty-five of its entries carry another line, and eleven have another line first. Several are integrated by other lines' accounts as well, listed in the section on where else they are integrated; this account integrates every member again.*

## The account

**The primitive.** [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) (the owner's U33 of the exchange of
2026-10-17, granted by A33; interpretive, Active) makes frustration, not
distress, the primitive for an organization's regulatory difficulty: the
owner, "maybe we should focus less on distress and more on frustration";
A33, "Frustration is the most promising theoretical primitive of the
three." It rests on [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), and states the link: "A demand
that is not jointly realizable is a precedence claimed and not granted".
[CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) (A4 §2, reading Frijda; interpretive, Active) is that
link's premise: "An operator can urgently demand control without receiving
it." Its note of 2026-10-18 records A34's p_i − w_i as "a candidate
indicator of frustrated precedence", and says "The frustration account rests
on this claim ([CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md))". [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md) (A33's term at U33, made ordinal at
U37; Active) defines frustration as "the failure of the demands it maintains
to be jointly realizable under its admissible transformations and its
governance", structural or by governance, apart from strain and distress,
and "not [CLAIM-153](../claims.d/CLAIM-153.md)'s physics sense, though it shares that sense's
three-demand minimal obstruction". Its note of 2026-10-18 records an error
in the definition and three corrections the claims now carry, and leaves the
relation to operative thwarting to [QUESTION-131](../questions.d/QUESTION-131.md). [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md)'s version 2 made
strain an independent dimension.

**Ordinal first: the complex.** [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) (the owner's correction at U37
with A37's construction, corrected twice in review; interpretive, Active)
rests on [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) and uses [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md). The owner: "We can do quite a lot with
access to an ordering, even if it doesn't give us a true measure." The
jointly satisfiable sets of demands form a downward-closed complex whose
minimal non-faces are the minimal conflicts; three gradients at 120° are
pairwise compatible and jointly infeasible; relief is strict enlargement for
the same declared demands; and its title ends "dropping or suppressing a
demand is not relief". Its notes index the complex to a horizon and a
budget, read it as a nerve, and set it beside the readings on constraint
architectures, [CLAIM-586](../claims.d/CLAIM-586.md) and [CLAIM-564](../claims.d/CLAIM-564.md). [CASE-119](../cases.d/CASE-119.md) (a construction from the
synthesis of 2026-10-18; Active) is its three-member minimal frustrated
collection, relieved when a composite policy enlarges the attainable set:
"relief in [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s sense, by changing what is attainable, not by
weakening a demand". [CASE-196](../cases.d/CASE-196.md) (A8's Proposal A, the reader's thresholds;
Proposed), a variant of [CASE-119](../cases.d/CASE-119.md) and [CASE-118](../cases.d/CASE-118.md) that illustrates [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and
[CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md), derives such a missing triangle from ecological dynamics and
finds a band in which the face depends on what compatibility is declared to
be: "It shows that the face criterion is part of what is declared."

**Quantities that refine it.** [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) (A34 §4 and A35 §3, checked;
demonstrated, Active) proves that the local residual Φ_r equals a maximum
over the simplex, so infeasibility has a certificate and any admissible
update exceeds Φ_r by a nonnegative regret; since its version 2 it
complements [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) rather than resting on it, "a quantitative refinement
of a more basic order-theoretic feasibility problem, not the definition of
frustration itself" (A37). [CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md) (A's, A34 and A35 restated at A39 and
A40; interpretive, Active) rests on [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) and [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) and separates
structural infeasibility (Φ > 0), realized violation (Γ > −Φ when Φ ≤ 0)
and minimax suboptimality (Γ > 0), and "causal governance failure, which
neither quantity identifies". Governance-induced shortfall needs "an
intervention on the allocation with search and information held fixed", and
it quotes A35: "Importantly, these dual multipliers are not endorsement weights." Its
note of 2026-11-04 recomputes A22's toy as realized violation with no
structural infeasibility, "and [CASE-119](../cases.d/CASE-119.md) is the same three-demand complex".
[CLAIM-351](../claims.d/CLAIM-351.md) (A33 §5; demonstrated, Active) shows that "A zero aggregate
gradient does not show that the demands it aggregates are reconciled", on
[CASE-115](../cases.d/CASE-115.md). [CASE-115](../cases.d/CASE-115.md) (A33, A35 and A38's construction; Active) is the shared
instance: two quadratic demands frustrated at every budget, relieved by
splitting the parameter. [CLAIM-351](../claims.d/CLAIM-351.md), [CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md), [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) and [CLAIM-554](../claims.d/CLAIM-554.md)
ground on it, its front matter illustrates only [CLAIM-360](../claims.d/CLAIM-360.md) (learned-structure),
and its body lists what it shows for [CLAIM-351](../claims.d/CLAIM-351.md), [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md), [CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md),
[CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and [CLAIM-360](../claims.d/CLAIM-360.md). [QUESTION-126](../questions.d/QUESTION-126.md) (A35's question; Open) asks whether
the weights governance grants track the multipliers that bind: "That is
[CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md)'s governance-induced frustration seen from the weights."
[CLAIM-586](../claims.d/CLAIM-586.md) (A's proposal, shown in the claim; demonstrated, Active; realizability first)
rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and bears on [QUESTION-131](../questions.d/QUESTION-131.md): the complex at a budget is
monotone in it and bounded by the unbudgeted complex, "So a missing face is
either short of resources at b or structurally frustrated, and the
