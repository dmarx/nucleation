# THEORY-tmpl4czv: Lu et al. (2026) report an LLM pipeline that generates ML research ideas, runs the experiments and writes the paper

## The account

Lu et al. (2026) report an LLM pipeline that generates ML research ideas, runs the experiments and writes the paper. Of three such papers, hand-filtered at each stage and submitted blind to an ICLR 2025 workshop with a 70% acceptance rate, one scored above the average acceptance threshold. The paper quality the system's own LLM reviewer assigns rises with base-model capability and with search compute.
