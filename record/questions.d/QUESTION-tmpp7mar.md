# QUESTION-tmpp7mar: Does the QQ equality of survey question-order data hold in question pairs chosen in advance by investigators other than its proposers, and does a classical model with memory, constrained to satisfy it, fit the per-pair tables as well as the projection model?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-166](../theory.d/THEORY-166.md) holds the regularity for one archive and shows that the
equality alone does not favour quantum over classical probability: a
classical model with order events can satisfy it. Whether the regularity
survives question pairs chosen before the data are seen, by other
investigators, and whether a classical model with memory constrained to
the same equality fits the per-pair tables as well as the Lüders
projection model, the evidence does not say. Until 2026-10-20 this was
[THEORY-166](../theory.d/THEORY-166.md)'s promote_when.

## What would answer it

- A test of the equality on question pairs chosen before the data were
  seen, by investigators other than its proposers, with the per-pair
  tables published.
- A comparison, on the same tables, of the projection model against a
  classical model with memory that is constrained to satisfy the same
  equality.

What would not answer it: re-analyses of the same 72 studies, or a fit of
the quantum model alone. That is what [THEORY-166](../theory.d/THEORY-166.md) already holds.
