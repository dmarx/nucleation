# CLAIM-419: The record's grammar is fixed at any moment but amendable by decision, and in practice a move that fit no scheme or relation has repeatedly been treated as a defect of the grammar and answered by amending it, so a move the grammar cannot hold is not only forced or lost; whether every such move is caught is not shown

## The claim

[CLAIM-181](CLAIM-181.md) says the record "fixes the grammar an argument is expressed in",
and that "a move that fits no CLAIM, TERM, ARG or declared relation is
either forced into one or lost". It asked to be answered by "a move that
mattered in the exchange and that the record could not hold", or by
showing that every case of drift the record caught was drift by the
exchange's own standards.

The owner, at U18, quoting A17's "epistemic accountability requires not
only traceability within the current conceptual scheme but also some means
of contesting the scheme used to make inquiry traceable": "this definitely
aligns with how we've been using luria: when we've encountered
representational challenges, we've generally interpreted those as a
symptom of a represntational weakness of the record and used that
opportunity to mature the luria configuration, featureset, or both."

The record's decisions bear it out. Each was a move the grammar could not
hold, recognized as such and answered by amending the grammar:

- **[ADR-044](../decisions.d/ADR-044.md)** (2026-10-15): a claim that gave evidence for another without
  the other depending on it could only be written as `supports`, and the
  link fixer then wrote a false `rests_on`. The grammar asserted an
  inference nobody made, and `corroborates` was added ([CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md)).
- **[ADR-043](../decisions.d/ADR-043.md)** (2026-10-15): claims of different kinds of support were
  indistinguishable, and `warrant` was added.
- **[ADR-042](../decisions.d/ADR-042.md)** (2026-10-14): a thought experiment, a construction and a real
  specimen were told apart only by prose, and CASE gained `kind`.
- **[ADR-046](../decisions.d/ADR-046.md)** (2026-10-18): a term that rivalled or narrowed another could
  not be filed as Proposed, and could be.
- **[ADR-049](../decisions.d/ADR-049.md)** (2026-10-21): a question partly answered had no status, and
  partly answered questions split.

So the grammar is fixed at any moment and amendable over time, which is
[CLAIM-195](CLAIM-195.md)'s point that the constraint set is "fixed by an amendable
decision". A move that fits nothing is not only forced or lost: a third
outcome is that the failure to fit becomes evidence against the grammar.
A18 named the condition: "A record becomes second-order corrigible when
failures of its representational distinctions can themselves become
objects of inquiry, argument, and justified revision", and its test of
openness, whether failures of the categories can become "recognizable,
contestable, and actionable *without requiring the failed categories to
validate the challenge in advance*". A18 found the bootstrap in the
record's practice: "ordinary prose, source files and observed
discrepancies can provide that provisional external language."

## What it does not say

- **Not that the grammar catches everything.** The evidence is the
  amendments made. A move the grammar forced into a wrong shape without
  anyone noticing leaves no decision behind, so the record of decisions
  cannot show its absence. [CLAIM-190](CLAIM-190.md)'s point stands: measures computed in
  the grammar cannot certify it alone.
- **Not that every misfit was amended.** [ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) kept the literature
  roles (foil, method, analogue, boundary marker) in prose though they
  "recurred", which is [CLAIM-181](CLAIM-181.md)'s own evidence. That was a decision not to
  amend, and it is recorded, but the move is still held only in prose.
- **Not that amendment is free of the risk A18 named.** Its anti-ad-hoc
  constraint: an amendment should answer a recurring misfit and not be
  made to save a particular claim.
- **Warrant.** Empirical: it rests on the record's own decision history,
  observed, not on an argument about grammars in general.

## Note of 2026-10-27 (evening): the readings of the U15–U23 works

CEGAR, read today ([NOTE-1231](../notes.d/NOTE-1231.md)), was offered by the exchange as luria's
[LU-DP-016](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md#dp-16) in formal dress. The pattern matches: a failure under a fixed
specification is located and repaired by refining the abstraction. Two
disanalogies bear on this claim's limit.

- **CEGAR's misfit is certified.** Lemma 1 decides whether a counterexample
  is spurious against the concrete model, so every wrong merge that matters
  eventually surfaces. A record has no concrete model to check against.
  That is why this claim's limit, that a silently forced move leaves no
  trace, has no counterpart there.
- **CEGAR only splits.** The amendments this claim lists ([ADR-043](../decisions.d/ADR-043.md)'s field,
  [ADR-044](../decisions.d/ADR-044.md)'s relation) are additions, not splits.

And even with a certified misfit, the least amendment can be hard to find: the
coarsest split that removes a counterexample is NP-hard (Thm 3).

## Note of 2026-10-31: the misfit as a nonprototypical case, and as disindividuation

<!-- inactive-ok-file: THEORY-1279 THEORY-1280 — Proposed; the readings set beside this claim -->

Two of the readings filed for Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy bear on this
claim's third outcome.

- **Tsoukas and Chia** ([LIT-1913](../literature.d/LIT-1913.md), pp. 573–575; [THEORY-1279](../theory.d/THEORY-1279.md)).
  Categories are radially structured. Applying one to a nonprototypical
  case is "an imaginative extension" that widens "the radius of
  application of the concept, thus transforming it" (p. 574). That act
  is normative, and "The indeterminacy of extension does not indicate
  arbitrariness". The amendments listed above are such cases: support
  that was not dependence ([ADR-044](../decisions.d/ADR-044.md)), and a thought experiment among
  specimens ([ADR-042](../decisions.d/ADR-042.md)). What the record adds is where the extension
  happens. For Tsoukas and Chia it happens in use and may never be
  institutionalized (p. 579). Here the closed vocabularies rule out the
  silent form: a missing word "is added by decision ... never replaced by
  the nearest wrong one" (CLAUDE.md). So the extension is made at the
  case that needed it, as a decision, and institutionalized at once
  ([CASE-178](../cases.d/CASE-178.md)). Their open question, how a local extension becomes
  organizational, has a designed answer in this record. This is the
  record's own case. It does not test [THEORY-1279](../theory.d/THEORY-1279.md), because nobody
  classified the cases as prototypical before seeing what changed.
- **Disindividuation** ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p. 1356): "the experience of needing
  to reorganize in order to deal with a preindividual element that
  resists incorporation", read at p. 1365 as an "inflection point" that
  calls "for creativity". This has the structure of the third outcome: a
  move that does not fit becomes the occasion for reorganizing the
  grammar. It is analogy only. The paper identifies disindividuation by
  felt hesitation ([THEORY-1280](../theory.d/THEORY-1280.md)), and the record identifies a misfit
  by a filer's judgement that a structure is awkward ([LU-DP-016](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md#dp-16)). Neither
  is a check, so this claim's caveat stands: a misfit absorbed without
  being noticed leaves no decision behind.

No status, title or warrant changes.

that fits from one whose misfits go unnoticed. The 2026-11-12 filing
itself chose tags under the closed vocabulary for 33 works, and whether
any was a nearest-wrong choice was not checked. The audit A25 proposed,
rereading the sources against the grammar of their day, remains the test;
it was not run. The claim is unchanged. The wording is the assistant's
(Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.

## Note of 2026-11-12: 8 readings of the works of "luria experiment design"

Read on 2026-11-12 among the works of the exchange "luria experiment design"; each reading's NOTE and THEORY hold the detail.

- **Chong, Zhang, Fan and Du** (THEORY-tmp4d13i): **contrast case**. Here misfits found in trajectories are answered by schema patches accepted on measured gain, with no decision record beyond the log.
- **Hegde et al.** (THEORY-tmpe8wmn): **contrast**. In the OBO workflow, content change is reviewed by PR and, as the paper describes it, the change language itself is extended by "community-driven use cases"; no decision record is described.
- **Fang, Jian, Li and Ma** (THEORY-tmp9ispo): **analogy only**, not claimed: AI-Newton extends a law's form when a case fails, but its DSL (the grammar) is fixed.
- **Jackson et al.** (THEORY-tmpm8l7x): **corroborates by analogy**, not support: OBO's principles are revised when found imprecise, and the checks themselves were revised after false positives and negatives a standard and its checker both under amendment. It is about a different object (member ontologies' conformance, not a record's own grammar).
- **Orzechowski, Tawfiq and Kosciolek** (THEORY-tmpxbva2): CLAIM-419 and QUESTION-tmps5iou: the finding that a curated schema "disagrees with its own data" is the schema-misfit situation CLAIM-419 describes from the record's side; the paper's remedy (validate the ontology against a data-derived summary before each release) is a governance step.
- **Flouris et al.** (THEORY-tmplwnpv): **corroborates by analogy**: the survey lists discovering "a design flaw in the original conceptualization" as a standard reason for changing an ontology and gives a process for it; it says nothing on how often misfits are caught.
- **Halilaj, Grangel-González, Coskun and Auer** (THEORY-tmp706lw): Comparison class for "grammar amended by decision": Git4Voc has amendment by commit and discussion, not by decision record.
- **Tudorache, Nyulas, Noy and Musen** (THEORY-tmphxh86): WebProtégé records schema changes but not decisions about them; the contrast is exactly CLAIM-419's "amendable by decision".

The claim is unchanged.
