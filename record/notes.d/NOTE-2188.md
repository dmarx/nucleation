# NOTE-2188: Moll and Meltzoff — Perspective-Taking and its Foundation in Joint Attention

## Contribution

A stage model of perspective-taking from birth to about five, revising
Flavell's levels and grounding them in joint attention.

## Key results

1. **Foundation.** "joint attention is a necessary condition for
   appreciating perspectives" (p. 286); infants are at "level 0
   perspective-taking" (p. 287).
2. **Learned in cooperation.** "just as role-taking is learned within the
   context of simple cooperative activities, so is perspective-taking
   acquired within joint attentional sequences." (§1.1, p. 289).
3. **Level 1.** "The research suggests that young children begin to
   appreciate that others may not see what they see at around 2 to 2.5
   years of age." (§2.1, p. 292). Experiential perspective-taking comes
   first: "Counterintuitively, the latter seems to develop significantly
   earlier than level 1 visual perspective-taking." (p. 287).
4. **Over-attribution.** "the two-year-olds over-attributed experience to
   the adult in all these three cases" (pp. 294–295); "what is primary is
   the sharedness of the situation" (p. 296).
5. **Piaget.** The "mountain problem designed by Piaget and Inhelder
   (1956)" has "led to significant underestimations of children's
   capacities" (§3, p. 296).
6. **Level 2.** "level 2 research has consistently shown that
   4.5-year-olds are mostly successful in judging how an object looks from
   perspectives other than their own, whereas 3-year-olds are not." (p.
   297); "While 2A is well in place by 3 years of age, 2B emerges at
   around 4.5 years" (§3.1, p. 299).

## Assumptions

Interactive measures show competence; sharing perspectives is knowing-how,
not knowing-that.

## Concepts

Levels 0, 1 experiential, 1 visual, 2A and 2B; triangulation; the sharing
hypothesis; over-attribution of shared experience.

## Bearing on the record

- **[THEORY-495](../theory.d/THEORY-495.md)**, support: joint engagement comes before
  perspective-taking, as the shared-intentionality account holds.
- **The owner's question on the recursive reading**, a qualification: by 3
  children take another's view but do not yet confront two, so a
  three-year-old's equal split need not rest on "I expect you to expect".
  The chapter says nothing of coordination or focal points.
- **[LIT-837](../literature.d/LIT-837.md)** (Piaget), a contrast: the three-mountains task
  underestimated children.

## Limitations

Mostly the authors' own studies; the 2A/2B split rests largely on one
colour-filter study; OCR text.
