# NOTE-tmp1hpuh: Dræbel, Sørensen and Meyrowitsch (2025) — Meaning and value attributed to non-therapeutic circumcision of boys among Danish parents of Jewish background

## Contribution

Twenty semi-structured interviews (2017–2021) with Danish parents of Jewish background (12 fathers, 8 mothers; 18 chose circumcision, 2 did not), analysed with Antonovsky's Sense of Coherence and Davies and Harré's positioning theory, against the background of a Danish public debate (c. 2010–2022) on banning non-therapeutic circumcision of boys (NTCB), including a 2018 citizen proposal to criminalize it. Parents describe NTCB as "inevitable", a taken-for-granted ritual and a rite of passage through which they become Danish-Jewish parents and a family and enrol the son in the family biography, whether or not they are religious. Under the debate they position themselves as making a well-informed choice, as passing on heritage, and as visibly Danish-Jewish; the debate is experienced as incriminating and reactivates fears of antisemitism.

## Key results

1. Discussion: "Interviewees explained NTCB as “inevitable”."
2. Discussion: "As a rite of passage, NTCB confirms the belonging to the Jewish community of the boy, his parents and family."
3. Discussion: NTCB "is both religiously and not religiously emphasized".
4. Discussion: becoming parents of a boy "raises immediate, universal, and eternal questions about how one, as a new parent, enrols the child in the family biography".
5. Conclusion: "NTCB thereby becomes a political manifestation and a way by which the interviewees can redefine what it means to be Danish-Jewish in the context of the public debate on a ban of NTCB."
6. Limitations: the authors cannot tell whether the "incriminating tone of the debate" sharpened the practices and reflections they found.

## Assumptions

Interviews reveal meaning-making; a small, highly educated, mostly Copenhagen sample (snowball sampling) reflects Danish-Jewish parents' perspectives; theoretical lenses from medical sociology.

## Concepts

Non-therapeutic circumcision of boys (NTCB); rite of passage; family biography; Sense of Coherence; positioning, subjectivation, becoming, belonging; minority identity under public debate.

## Bearing on the record

- **[QUESTION-tmp2lvhi](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp2lvhi.md)**: **bears on "the relation between them" as a candidate ground, descriptively.** Parents here justify their authority not by the child's welfare or projected wishes but by the relation (enrolling the son in a family and community, continuity of identity). That is evidence of how one class of surrogate deciders understands their authority, not an argument that the relation legitimates it; the paper does not discuss the child's later view, consent or assent (one passing mention of consent, which concerns interviewees).
- **[TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)** (formation and maintenance provenance): the parents' account is of an authorization formed by others (parents, community) in order to form the child's membership, the case the question's note calls formation by another.

## Limitations

Twenty interviewees, 18 of whom chose circumcision; one country and minority; conducted during a heated debate the authors think may have shaped the answers; no ethical analysis.
