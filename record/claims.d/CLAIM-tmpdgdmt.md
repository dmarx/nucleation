# CLAIM-tmpdgdmt: Communication transports structures of observational constraints rather than invariant semantic objects, and translation, language-model reconstruction and conditional image generation are realizations of one process

## The claim

A100, proposal v6: "**Central thesis:** Communication transports structures of
observational constraints rather than invariant semantic objects. Translation,
language-model reconstruction, and conditional image generation are different
realizations of this process. Fidelity depends on which distinctions survive,
how their relationships are preserved, and whether locally accessible
observations remain jointly compatible." "The most important architectural
change is that **conditional generation becomes part of the central theory, not
merely an application at the end**." The closing formulation: "Communication does
not preserve meaning by transporting a fixed object unchanged. It selectively
preserves and reconstructs constraints on what can be observed, inferred, and
jointly realized." And Chapter 17: "What survives is a selected family of
distinctions and relationships, under a particular transformation, for
particular observers and communicative purposes", which the manuscript's
conclusion keeps.

## Why it was replaced

U36 brought in structuralism ([CLAIM-tmpartp6](CLAIM-tmpartp6.md)). The claim that generation *realizes* the
process was later weakened to analogy ([CLAIM-tmpzaee6](CLAIM-tmpzaee6.md) → [CLAIM-tmpjlq36](CLAIM-tmpjlq36.md)).
