# QUESTION-tmptcuch: Under which computational, observational and architectural conditions does a relational structure suffice to individuate an object, and which transformations, of budget, of probes, of time and of the category itself, preserve that individuation?

## Why it is a question

A (§9): "Instead of asking only: Is an object determined by its relations?
we can ask: Under which computational, observational, and architectural
conditions does a relational structure suffice to individuate an object,
and which transformations preserve that individuation?" "The first
question invites a direct move from mathematics to metaphysics, which
Nucleation correctly resists. The second gives us a series of mathematical
and empirical problems."

The record holds the guard and several parts. [THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md): Yoneda
determines an object up to isomorphism among objects the category already
has; [ARG-023](../arguments.d/ARG-023.md), that Yoneda shows relations constitute an object, is
Rejected; [CLAIM-150](../claims.d/CLAIM-150.md): identification underdetermines constitution.
[TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md) separates descriptive, dynamical and constitutive individuation
and organizational autonomy, and [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) makes them a partial order of
achievements. What it lacks is the question of the conditions, in each
of A's three respects:

- **Computational and observational.** At a budget, the distinctions
  available are those of the affordable probes, and along nested families
  they only grow ([CLAIM-tmpg7w69](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpg7w69.md)). Which category, and which enrichment,
  decides what counts as the same: a groupoid identifies what an
  enrichment in cost separates ([CLAIM-tmpxcbet](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpxcbet.md)). [QUESTION-031](QUESTION-031.md) asks which
  probe families suffice for a task, for communicative realizations.
- **Over time.** A (§4): "A purely instantaneous Yoneda profile doesn't
  settle that persistence question. We need relations between profiles at
  different times", a family t ⟼ Y_b(A_t) with transformations
  η_{t,t'}: Y_b(A_t) ⟶ Y_b(A_{t'}); and "persistence need not mean that
  the profiles remain isomorphic. A growing, learning, or reorganizing
  system can persist while changing its capabilities." A's table, which
  "mirrors the distinctions in [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md), rather than collapsing them":
  descriptive individuation as "Distinguishability under a specified
  family of resource-bounded probes", dynamical as "Persistence of
  selected relational structures under admissible evolution and
  perturbation", constitutive as requiring "an additional account of how
  relations help determine identity, not merely characterize it", and
  autonomy as requiring "processes that contribute to maintaining the
  constraints supporting the organization". And: "two systems can be
  descriptively equivalent at a particular resource scale while having
  different dynamical persistence properties", as [CLAIM-tmp99w2g](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp99w2g.md)
  separates persisting from regenerating.
- **Architectural.** A (§5): with z ⟼ 𝒞_z the category an architecture
  admits, a reconfiguration may come with a functor F: 𝒞_z ⟶ 𝒞_{z'}, "But
  such a functor is not automatic. An architectural change may create
  objects or distinctions that cannot be faithfully represented in the
  previous vocabulary" ([TERM-091](../terms.d/TERM-091.md); [QUESTION-283](QUESTION-283.md)). "Individuation is not
  only resource-relative because observers have limited access to
  relations. It can also be architecture-relative because the repertoire
  of admissible relations is itself produced and modified by computational
  processes." The record's operational instance is [CLAIM-469](../claims.d/CLAIM-469.md): a split
  scheme makes one object two and a promoted vocabulary makes a value an
  entity. [CLAIM-tmpisvzb](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpisvzb.md) keeps the same two apart for a system's
  repertoire: observation makes an existing possibility discoverable,
  architecture alters what is possible. [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) is the frame in which
  the constraints are themselves realized.

**A's formulation.** 𝔍 = ({𝒞_z}_{z∈Z}, {𝒫_{𝓔,b}}, 𝒜), the categories
indexed by architecture, the probes available under regime 𝓔 ([TERM-065](../terms.d/TERM-065.md))
and budget b, and the admissible architectural transformations, with
profiles Y_{𝓔,b,z}(A), "a research placeholder" for a graded or enriched
construction. Its five questions, mapped:

**A's conjecture**, recorded with its disclaimers:

> Resource-relative categorical individuation conjecture: For suitably
> defined classes of constrained computational systems, there exist
> resource-graded relational profiles that preserve the distinctions
> relevant to feasible interaction, support comparisons of persistence and
> architectural change, and admit nontrivial equivalences across different
> physical implementations.

"This deliberately does not assert that such profiles uniquely determine
metaphysical individuals. Nor does it assert that all resource-bounded
constrained systems inhabit one universality class." Its last clause is
[CLAIM-tmpe18y0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpe18y0.md)'s hypothesis stated for profiles. It is recorded here
rather than as a claim because, as A says, "It proposes a mathematical
structure within which those stronger questions can be investigated";
the profiles it posits are not yet defined.

## What would answer it

For a stated class of systems, with a declared category, enrichment and
probe regime: conditions under which the profile at a budget separates
the objects the regime needs separated; a notion of transformation of
profiles over time under which a stated kind of persistence is preserved,
with an example of persistence without isomorphism of profiles; and, for a
stated class of reconfigurations, a condition under which a transport
functor exists and is faithful, with an example where none is. Each part
can be answered separately; the question is answered when the three are
stated in one setting, as A proposes.

## What would not answer it

- **A Yoneda argument alone.** It presupposes the category and its
  objects ([THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md)), which is what the architectural part puts in
  question.
- **Agreement under probes chosen to agree.** [CLAIM-322](../claims.d/CLAIM-322.md): such probes show
  only that they can be selected.
- **A metaphysical verdict.** A: "That's not a theorem of Yoneda."
