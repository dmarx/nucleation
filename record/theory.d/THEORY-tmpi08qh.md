# THEORY-tmpi08qh: Wilkins and Bourrat report Griesemer's reproducer as an entity that develops and overlaps materially with its progeny, reproduction being development plus progeneration, inheritance a system property acquired in development, and replication an evolved terminal form of reproduction, with no level of organization fixed in advance

## Source

Wilkins and Bourrat, "Replication and Reproduction" (SEP, 2022
revision), LIT-tmp460ih, read in NOTE-tmp7ylue.

## What was actually shown

Nothing is argued in §5.4; it reports Griesemer (2000a, b, 2002, 2005,
2014; Wimsatt and Griesemer 2007) and the objections to him. What could
be checked against Griesemer's texts is the attribution.

## What this does not say

- That material overlap is necessary for evolution by natural
  selection; the entry reports Godfrey-Smith's and Charbonneau's
  objections and Griesemer's reply that causation of progeny by parents
  is the point.
- Anything about the three-stage scenario of LIT-1937.
