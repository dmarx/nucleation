# CLAIM-1252: Standing constrains what an agent may optimize

## The claim

A48 closes: "**To possess legitimate standing is not merely to appear in
another agent's objective function. It is to possess claims that can,
under appropriate circumstances, constrain what that agent is entitled to
optimize.**"

**Instrumental and normative recognition (§1).** Employees as "Labor,
expertise and retention" or as "Persons with justified claims concerning
working conditions"; customers as "Revenue and lifetime customer value" or
as "Persons owed appropriate treatment". "Shareholder primacy becomes
normatively restrictive when it recognizes the first column for
non-shareholders while denying the independent significance of the
second." "The discriminating test concerns what happens when respecting
their justified claims ceases to be profitable." Donaldson and Preston
([LIT-3220](../literature.d/LIT-3220.md), Deferred) are its precedent.

**An objective as a rule of eligibility (§2).** Adopted as the final
criterion, shareholder value "becomes a **meta-principle governing which
concerns are eligible to acquire practical authority**"; "if those
perspectives can acquire authority only by demonstrating their
contribution to shareholder value, their normative efficacy is
subordinated to a particular concern. The corporation has not necessarily
failed to recognize them epistemically. It has restricted their
jurisdiction." That is [CLAIM-283](CLAIM-283.md)'s claimed precedence and granted authority.

**Normative ignorance without factual ignorance (§3).** A48 separates
factual ignorance, representational ignorance, normative ignorance ("Harm
is recognized but its independent moral significance is not admitted"),
volitional exclusion and reflexive exclusion, and names **normative
agnotology**: "the organized prevention of relevant facts from acquiring
the normative significance they warrant."

**Constrained maximization (§4).** Unrestricted maximization picks from
argmax over X of V_S(x), and selects x over x′ when V_S(x) > V_S(x′) though x
violates an independently justified requirement; the alternative is argmax
over X_N = {x : Perm_N(x) = 1}. "The decisive question is consequently not
whether corporations maximize something. It is **who establishes the
constraints upon maximization, whether those constraints are adequate, and
how their inadequacies can become consequential**." The first half applies
[CLAIM-348](CLAIM-348.md) and [CLAIM-350](CLAIM-350.md), as A48 says.

**The division of normative labour (§5).** Friedman ([LIT-3253](../literature.d/LIT-3253.md),
Deferred) "explicitly qualifies profit-seeking by reference to law and
ethical custom", which "presupposes that other institutions can adequately
discover, formulate, authorize, enforce and revise the constraints required
to govern corporate activity. That is an extraordinarily demanding
service-level guarantee", and "the corporation itself may possess
considerable influence over the institutions upon which its normative
regulation depends" ([CLAIM-1287](CLAIM-1287.md)). Hence §14's last sentence: "The
legitimacy of a specialized organizational objective therefore depends
partly upon the adequacy of the surrounding governance relationships
through which independently justified constraints remain effective."

**Specialization is not restriction (§§7, 9, 12, 15).** "**Constitutive
contribution does not automatically confer unrestricted governing
authority, just as financial ownership does not automatically exhaust the
grounds of legitimate standing**" (after Blair and Stout, [LIT-3217](../literature.d/LIT-3217.md);
the firm-specific investments are [CLAIM-728](CLAIM-728.md)'s territory, noted there).
Jensen's enlightened value maximization ([LIT-3245](../literature.d/LIT-3245.md)) answers temporal
but not jurisdictional greed ([TERM-179](../terms.d/TERM-179.md)): "The issue is not merely the
length of the optimization horizon. It is the authority structure
governing what gets optimized." The distinction is "between
**specialization of purpose and restriction of moral standing**". And an
organization can come to recognize "morality as a resource for achieving
its objectives rather than as a source of reasons capable of constraining
those objectives": "Trust becomes brand value. Responsibility becomes risk
management."

## Admission

A48 §14 is a proposed synthesis of the owner's U30 with held entries. What
it adds beyond them passes by a **discriminating prediction**: an
institution that recognizes a party only instrumentally drops a practice
that honours the party's claim when the practice stops paying, and one
constrained by the claim does not. [ARG-243](../arguments.d/ARG-243.md) narrows the prediction:
by [CLAIM-1169](CLAIM-1169.md)'s analysis it separates the two only with reputation and
other benefits held fixed, and [CLAIM-431](CLAIM-431.md) doubts that any corporate concern
is more than prudence.

"The proposed claim does not establish that shareholder-oriented
corporations necessarily exhibit this pathology, or that
stakeholder-oriented corporations necessarily avoid it" (A48 §14).

## Note of 2026-11-24: normative agnotology and the exchange's earlier agnotology, the closing pass

<!-- inactive-ok-file: CLAIM-1286 CLAIM-1249 TERM-171 TERM-174 CLAIM-1293 TERM-083 — Proposed; filed from the same exchange -->

A48's "normative agnotology" is not the same idea as A6's transmitted
agnotological character ([CLAIM-1286](CLAIM-1286.md), [TERM-171](../terms.d/TERM-171.md)), though both say
an organization can be ignorant without believing anything false. A6's is
a limit on what becomes legible or credible; A48's is a limit on what a
fact already known is allowed to count for. The nearer neighbour is A6's
manufactured doubt working on the threshold for action ([CLAIM-1249](CLAIM-1249.md)),
where the evidence is held and action is deferred. The three complement
one another. Standing here is [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md)'s normative sense, which
constrains what may be optimized; it is not the operational standing of
the owner's U14 ([CLAIM-1293](CLAIM-1293.md), [TERM-174](../terms.d/TERM-174.md)), which is eligibility to
have a claim become consequential under an arrangement. The wording is
the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's
call.
