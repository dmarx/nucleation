# LIT-tmpp546j: The 10 Principles of Burning Man

Harvey, L. (2004). The 10 Principles of Burning Man. Burning Man Project. https://burningman.org/about/10-principles/. Earlier addresses: http://www.burningman.com/whatisburningman/about_burningman/principles.html (archived 2005-12-02) and http://burningman.org/culture/philosophical-center/10-principles/ (archived 2014-12-29). Author and year per St John (2017).

## Key takeaways

*Not read. Everything here is second-hand, from St John (2017), LIT-tmptwxqv, read in NOTE-tmpdk5dq; page numbers are St John's.*

- **The list (p. 3).** "Formulated by Larry Harvey in 2004", the ten are
  Radical Inclusion, Gifting, Decommodification, Radical Self-Reliance,
  Radical Self-Expression, Communal Effort, Civic Responsibility, Leave No
  Trace, Participation and Immediacy.
- **Gifting, as St John quotes it (p. 4).** "Burning Man is devoted to acts
  of gift giving. The value of a gift is unconditional. Gifting does not
  contemplate a return or an exchange for something of equal value."
- **Not verified.** The wording of the other nine principles, Radical
  Self-Reliance among them. St John describes Decommodification, Leave No
  Trace and Participation in his own words; he quotes none of them.
