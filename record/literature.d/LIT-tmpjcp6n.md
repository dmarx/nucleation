## Key takeaways

*Not read. Nothing below is a claim about what the paper argues.*

- How the record's readings place it:
  - [NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md) (Schwitzgebel, fn 21) lists Lycan 1981 with Brooks 1986,
    Wilson 2004 and Huebner as "notable exceptions" to the neglect of group
    consciousness. It says Huebner, Brooks and Lycan "endorse hypothetical
    group consciousness under certain counterfactual conditions … while
    refraining from stating that their arguments … extend to any group
    entities that actually exist".
  - In §7, after Block's China brain, Schwitzgebel adds "(though see Lycan
    1981)". In fn 20 he says the US case "is importantly different from
    more artificial cases discussed in Lycan 1981 and Brooks 1986".
- Block thanks "Bill Lycan" for comments on earlier drafts of "Troubles with
  Functionalism" ([LIT-tmpc6np5](LIT-tmpc6np5.md), n. 25), and answers Lycan's 1974 paper on
  Putnam's functionalist hypothesis there (p. 282).
