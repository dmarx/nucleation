The owner asked whether "there are any results at the intersection of
database theory and semantic search that might be useful to the
nucleation record", and on the answer, to "register and read them all",
then to add HNSW. Twenty-three works were registered; twenty-one were read
and two are Unavailable, Cho and Garcia-Molina 2003 ([LIT-tmp580ot](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp580ot.md): the
author's copy timed out on every try) and Fagin 1996 ([LIT-tmpem2ov](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpem2ov.md): the
open copies returned 403). Neither the anthology nor this record held any
of them.

**What was asked of them, and what came back.**

- **Staleness priced** ([QUESTION-114](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-114.md)). Cho and Garcia-Molina 2000 prices
  it as freshness or age under a refresh budget; Olston, Loo and Widom
  2001 trade it against upkeep in one objective. The work named as the
  nearest joint model, Labrinidis and Roussopoulos 2003, is not one: its
  staleness is a threshold. The question stays Open.
- **The cost of recognizing reuse** ([CLAIM-303](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-303.md), [CLAIM-308](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-308.md)). Certifying
  that stored views answer a new query exactly is NP-complete for
  conjunctive queries; partial reuse is cheap. This is a concrete source
  of the drifting per-use cost [CLAIM-308](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-308.md) already excludes. Filing it as a
  new objection to [CLAIM-308](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-308.md) would be the record's own move, not the
  papers', so it was left to the owner.
- **Organizing for similarity** ([QUESTION-114](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-114.md), [CLAIM-310](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-310.md)). An exact
  space-time curve for hashing, cell-probe floors, the dimensions past
  which partitioning stops paying, and graph indexes whose guarantees hold
  only for a slow build. Fagin, Lotem and Naor reproduce Treisman's
  feature-versus-conjunction contrast in access costs.
- **Revision** ([QUESTION-105](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-105.md)). Derivation counts, delete-and-rederive,
  witness bases and provenance polynomials answer it for changes to base
  facts; none covers defeat or proves minimality.

**Readers' corrections to the registrations.** The "Why this is here"
written at registration, from memory, overstated several works; every
one was rewritten from its reading. The larger ones: Chandra and Merlin state
equivalence, not containment; Beyer et al. bear on [CLAIM-310](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-310.md), not
[CLAIM-309](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-309.md), and their condition is sufficient only; the "about 10
dimensions" of Weber et al. is a measured crossover, not their analysis;
Indyk and Xu prove nothing about HNSW, and show its bad cases only by
experiment; the lower bounds of Andoni et al. hold on random instances in
one model; HNSW's final version drops deletion; provenance semirings never
mention deletion; and Labrinidis and Roussopoulos 2003 constrain staleness
rather than trade it.

**Copies.** Several were read from versions other than the one
registered, each said in its NOTE: Fagin, Lotem and Naor and Indyk and
Motwani from the authors' fuller versions, Beyer et al. from the authors'
technical report, HNSW from arXiv v4. Course-hosted publisher copies were
used for Chandra and Merlin, Halevy, and Gupta, Mumick and Subrahmanian,
under the owner's earlier rule that course copies are acceptable.

**Statuses.** Each reading drafted its own THEORYs, twenty-nine in all.
Where a reader marked a result Active on one synthetic experiment (the
HNSW neighbour-selection rule), it was filed Proposed, as the record has
treated a single unreplicated study. [CLAIM-310](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-310.md) now grounds five of the
accounts and [CLAIM-303](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-303.md) two.
