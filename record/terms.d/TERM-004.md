# TERM-004: equivariance

## Definition

A105: invariance is F(g·s) = F(s); equivariance is F(g·s) = ρ_g F(s), where ρ_g
acts on the representation. "equivariance may be the more general principle for
translation, with invariance as a special case." Manuscript §4: "For
translation, equivariance is often more apt than literal invariance: a verbal
joke can become a visual relation conveyed by expressions and gestures. One
does not expect equality of coordinates, only corresponding structure."

## What it is not

Not a group action in general: A105 and the manuscript both say translation
"is not necessarily an invertible group action", so the general setting is a
semigroup of stochastic maps ([CLAIM-098](../claims.d/CLAIM-098.md)). Equivariance under a semigroup is the
manuscript's "representation-changing" case (ii).
