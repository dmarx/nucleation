# CLAIM-1133: A team is a pseudo-self whose perspectives are necessarily separable

## The claim

The owner's turn, U10 of the exchange "Explain First Contact RL"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6b037165-3a1c-83ea-9d7c-311c7807acd3):

> a team as a pseudo-self where the perspectives are necessarily separable

It follows the owner's U6, which proposed a self as "a literal sloppy mess
of connected perspectives" whose separation into perspectives is relative
to the tests one can afford, and A's account of Luria as a stabilizer of
collaborators' perspectival evolution (U8, U9). A team has what a person
lacks for this purpose: its constituents come with boundaries no one has to
infer.

## Two readings

- **Separable because their carriers are.** Each member can be questioned,
  isolated, given evidence or replaced on their own, so the team's
  perspectives, taken as its members', can be addressed one by one. A21
  agrees: "its constituent perspectives have independently addressable
  human carriers", which makes a team "an unusually useful experimental
  model".
- **Separable in every sense.** The team's perspectives are separable
  observationally, causally and organizationally, as its members are.
  A21 objects to this reading ([CLAIM-1039](CLAIM-1039.md)), from [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md).

The owner did not say which was meant. Both are kept in the condition; the
owner chooses.

The name is the owner's. A21 defined it ([TERM-166](../terms.d/TERM-166.md)): a
multi-perspectival organization that reproduces some of the integration,
persistence and adaptive governance associated with selves, while leaving
open whether it has one subject of experience.
