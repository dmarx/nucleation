## What A got wrong, across the works

- **Quotations.** A's quoted sentences for Oskar, Ratliff, Liebman,
  Siemroth and Bogetoft are in none of the texts. They are A's paraphrases.
- **The USFA case.**
  - The page cancels one tanker and one rescue vehicle over a false alarm,
    not "rescue and support vehicles".
  - There is no heavy rescue squad on it.
  - Calls a department could not answer for lack of resources fall outside
    NFIRS altogether, and a separate record of them is recommended, not
    required. That gap is the readiness case A wanted, and A missed it.
- **Overstatements.**
  - McLay & Mayorga's headline is that 7- and 8-minute standards also
    maximize survival.
  - Siemroth finds full rollover optimal for most parameter values.
  - Halfin & Whitt's capacity is busy, not idle reserve.
  - Jordan & Graves is a simulation study, not "a mathematical study".
  - ReVelle & Hogan's comparison is with other coverage models, not with
    "utilization maximization".
  - Larson's model computes workloads, not "a unit's value".
- **Missed mechanisms.** Liebman & Mahoney's precautionary buffer, and the
  hypercube model's units of last resort, are the papers' own ties to
  readiness.
