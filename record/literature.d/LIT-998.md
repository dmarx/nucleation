# LIT-998: A measure for brain complexity: relating functional segregation and integration in the nervous system.

Giulio Tononi, Olaf Sporns and Gerald M. Edelman (1994), *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 91(11), 5033–5037.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-767](../notes.d/NOTE-767.md).*

- **The measure.** Integration is I(X) = Σᵢ H(xᵢ) − H(X). Neural
  complexity is C_N(X) = Σ_{k=1}^{n} [(k/n) I(X) − ⟨I(X_j^k)⟩], which
  equals Σ_{k=1}^{n/2} ⟨MI(X_j^k; X − X_j^k)⟩. For Gaussian activity,
  I(X) = −ln|CORR(X)|/2.
- **Both extremes are low.** For Toeplitz covariance matrices (n = 64),
  C_N is very low at independence and at near-total correlation, and
  maximal when correlations span the range from 0 to 1. It is a worked
  example, not a proof.
- **Patchy beats uniform.** In a simulated visual area, the cortex-like
  patchy pattern gives C_N = 2244.7. The same connections spread
  uniformly give a higher I(X), global synchrony and C_N = 1240.2. In
  small linear systems, concentrating a fixed amount of connection on
  fewer partners raises C_N/I.
- **Reciprocity and grouping.** At constant I, C_N tends to rise with the
  number of reciprocal pairs. Dissolving neuronal groups lowers it, but
  that result is "data not shown".
- **Scope.** The system is isolated, stationary and Gaussian, with
  homogeneous components fixed in advance. Validation on recorded brains
  is left to later work.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-042](../questions.d/QUESTION-042.md), and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-767](../notes.d/NOTE-767.md). It was registered as "the rival measure the Occam-priced
block test must be compared with". The reading shows it is not a rival of
that kind. C_N scores a whole system and draws no boundary. It has no
threshold and no Occam price, and its stationarity rules out short-lived
blocks. Its point of contact with [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) is its oneness condition: a
system is an individual only if no bipartition has zero mutual
information. That is the gap test with the threshold set to zero.
[THEORY-262](../theory.d/THEORY-262.md) holds the measure's two-extremes property and its link to
patchy connectivity, at the strength the simulations give. No THEORY or
CLAIM cited the paper before this reading.
