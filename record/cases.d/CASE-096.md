# CASE-096: The smoothie: milk makes it better for a while and then worse, unless there is more of everything else

## The case

The owner, quoted at the head of `III. Nominal Compatibilism/misc/Smoothies.md`
in the LEGO monograph (Mathematics of Meaning, commit d987e2a):

> if I put milk in my smoothie, it gets More Good for a while until it gets
> Less Good, unless there's more of all the other stuff.

## What it can show

That a quantity can help and then hurt at fixed amounts of everything
else, and help without limit along a ray where everything scales together.
The note's first model has goodness depend on the milk fraction
f = m/(m + S) through a single-peaked Q(f), times a weakly increasing
function of volume. It says this exactly: "If you increase both milk and
the other stuff so as to hold f near f*, you stay at the top of the hill".
The preference is over a composition, not an amount. That is why "unless
there's more of all the other stuff" is part of the observation and not a
hedge.

## What it cannot show

What the owner's peak is, or that it is the same across smoothies. And
nothing about discriminability: the note's second reading, which makes
"Less Good" a loss of d′, is the subject of [CLAIM-309](../claims.d/CLAIM-309.md).
