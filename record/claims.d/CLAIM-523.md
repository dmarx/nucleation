# CLAIM-523: For a normatively relevant discrepancy to change what an organization does, three distinct achievements are needed and any can fail while the others hold: legibility, by which the discrepancy becomes recognizable; volitional efficacy, by which a corresponding demand can move action; and correctability, by which a fitting revision can be carried out within deadline and resources; none establishes that the demand or the revision is justified, and whether the norm-satisfying histories the agent can still realize are empty depends partly on what its earlier actions destroyed

## The claim

Faced with a normatively relevant discrepancy, an agent or organization
needs three things for it to make a difference:

- **Legibility.** The discrepancy becomes recognizable through the
  distinctions the organization draws ([TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)).
- **Volitional efficacy.** A corresponding demand can influence action
  through the organization's will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)).
- **Correctability.** A fitting revision can be carried out, found,
  authorized, coordinated and executed within deadline and resources
  ([TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)).

Call **normative efficacy** the realized ability of a normatively relevant
distinction to change an organization's behaviour or governing standards.
It can fail along any of the three paths. So an agent can be sincere, well
informed and able to act, and still not act on a commitment:
- the reason never becomes legible (epistemic blockage);
- it becomes legible and is not given authority (volitional blockage);
- it is given authority and no revision can be executed in time.

This is [CLAIM-349](CLAIM-349.md)'s split of access, authority and action, carried from an
organization's own signals to normative demands.

## Histories available and histories required

Separate:
- 𝓗_exec(O, W, Λ; B, T): the histories the agent can realize through its
  actual organization O, will-organization W and legibility apparatus Λ,
  under budget B and horizon T;
- 𝓗_N: the histories that satisfy an independently specified standard N.

𝓗_exec ∩ 𝓗_N is the set of norm-satisfying histories open to the agent as
it actually is. Two cautions:

- **A nonempty intersection does not show the agent will choose such a
  history.** Availability is not choice. Resolve ([CLAIM-344](CLAIM-344.md)) and the
  volitional path are what make an available history the chosen one.
- **An empty intersection does not show the agent is beyond criticism.**
  Its earlier actions may have destroyed the capacities required. An
  intervention can make its own correction costlier or unreachable
  ([CLAIM-464](CLAIM-464.md)).

The second caution ties historical responsibility to the record's
resource-bounded account of what futures remain reachable ([CLAIM-475](CLAIM-475.md)).

## What it does not say

- **That any of the three justifies the demand or the revision.** A legible,
  effective, executable revision can be wrong. Viability does not establish
  what ought to persist ([CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md)), and endorsing a motive does not
  justify it.
- **That the three come in a fixed order.** Like the achievements of
  [CLAIM-474](CLAIM-474.md), they form no necessary sequence. An organization can have
  executable corrective machinery for discrepancies it cannot see.
- **Who has the authority to decide that a change is warranted.** That is
  [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md)'s question: endogenous governance, historically
  continuous authorization and legitimate authority are three things.

## Note of 2026-10-31: a legibility category that is also the refusal, and responsibility as incorporation

Two of the readings filed on this date bear on this claim.

- **One label does the work of legibility and of refusal.** In Bencherki
  (2017; [LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md), read in [NOTE-1482](../notes.d/NOTE-1482.md), pp. 787–788) military
  spouses ask for a newsletter on daycare, dental care and pensions. The
  base commander reads every item as "political activity", which is
  forbidden on the base. The demands are legible, but only under a
  category that denies them authority. Bencherki: "such a label seems to
  be the only organ available to the army's body to capture these
  actions". In this claim's terms that is volitional blockage achieved
  through the distinctions that make a demand legible. It is not the
  epistemic blockage of a demand never seen. This claim says the three
  achievements can fail separately. The case shows a way they can fail
  together: the classification that makes a demand recognizable can also
  be the reason it is refused.
- **The parent's responsibility lacks the step this claim says is
  missing.** Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy say "people become responsible
  as they incorporate/integrate (or deny the incorporation/integration
  of) the actions of others into their own becoming, which enables those
  others to continue their individuation" ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p. 1366). That
  places responsibility in granting another's demand efficacy in one's
  own organization, this claim's volitional path. The paper gives no
  standard by which a demand ought to be incorporated, and this claim
  says none of the three achievements supplies one. Bencherki (2017)
  needs such a standard to call the army's taking of the women's daycare
  work "usurpation" (pp. 791–792). Its theory disclaims one
  ([THEORY-1285](../theory.d/THEORY-1285.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-01: the three achievements in one mind

<!-- inactive-ok: THEORY-tmptbvmq QUESTION-tmp7czq0 — Proposed or Open; the reading and the question this note reports, cited as open -->

Metzinger's work on mind-wandering (LIT-tmp23d7b, LIT-tmp0w298) has
instances of two of this claim's separations within one person. Same
structure, not shown same mechanism: the discrepancy is being off task,
not a normative demand on an organization.

- **Legible, and no volitional efficacy.** Noticing that one has been
  mind-wandering can itself start a second episode: "Oops, I have just
  been daydreaming again!" leads to "another train of thought about having
  regained meta-awareness", with "a new phenomenal UI ... but the
  functional properties characterizing M-autonomy are not realized"
  (LIT-tmp23d7b, p. 14).
- **Correction without legibility.** "Successful inhibition can be an
  entirely subpersonal process" (LIT-tmp23d7b, p. 13).
- **An order at the personal level.** Meta-awareness is "a necessary
  precondition for second-order mental action" (LIT-tmp0w298, p. 277).
  This claim says the three form no necessary sequence. Metzinger asserts
  one for deliberate control in one mind, and allows correction without it
  below the personal level, which is consistent with the claim if the
  claim's "volitional efficacy" includes subpersonal correction.

Metzinger's condition for volitional efficacy is not legibility of the
discrepancy but the agent's representation of its own capacity to act on
it. That is a different variable, filed as QUESTION-tmp7czq0.

No status, title or warrant changes.
