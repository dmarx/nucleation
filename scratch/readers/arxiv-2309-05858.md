## Step 2: main text read (pp. 1-14: intro, Results, Discussion, Methods incl. proofs of Props 1-2, partially-observed construction, CompressedAlg, probing, few-shot)

- Mesa-optimization defined after Hubinger et al. as the "hypothetical acquired process" vs base-optimization of Eq. 1 (p. 2).
- Prop 1 (p. 3): tokens [Φ0 s_t, s_t, s_{t-1}]; one causal LSA head with given PW_v, W_k^T W_q gives e_t <- ((Φ0 - η∇L_t(Φ0)) s_t, s_t, s_{t-1}). Proof (p. 12) checked: value η(s_t' - Φ0 s_{t'-1}), key-query s_{t'-1}^T s_t; exact. Needs a residual or second head to add.
- Prop 2 (p. 3; full statement p. 12-13): truncated Neumann / Richardson / Chebyshev iteration for (S S^T + I/λ)^{-1} s_t, one LSA layer per iteration; then one GD step (Prop 1) on preconditioned inputs gives ridge LSQ. Approximation, error decreasing with depth k; existence construction.
- Mesa-layer (p. 4, Eqs. 4-8): Δe = Σ_h P_h Φ̂_h q_h, Φ̂ = argmin ½Σ||v - Φk||² + ||Φ||²/(2λ) = V K^T (K K^T + I/λ)^{-1}; Sherman-Morrison / recursive least squares, R_0 = λI. "As λ_h → 0, we recover a standard linear self-attention layer" - reader's arithmetic: R→λI, so recovered only up to the vanishing scale λ.
- p. 4: Delta-Net "hardwired to do one gradient descent step per time point"; p. 11 delta rule "requires multiple steps to converge".
- Experiments: probes for token binding (Fig. 2); trained 1-layer LSA matches Prop 1 (Fig. 3-4), deep LSA matched by k steps of Prop 2 by performance, CompressedAlg-d (16 of 3200 params per head), target and preconditioning probes (Fig. 4E-F); softmax layers 2+ linearizable by distillation at large n_s, first layer not (Fig. 5A); LSQ vs softmax kernel regression scaling (Fig. 5B); softmax Transformers: probes and k-step Prop 2 performance match (Fig. 6-7); hybrid softmax+mesa best; few-shot regression with early ascent from spurious associations (Fig. 8).
