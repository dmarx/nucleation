## 3. Claims, in A51's order

### 3a. Rights, demands and commitments; a right as a constraint on policy (A51: "begin with a sharper distinction")

- **CLAIM-738** (Active, v4, interpretive, thesis; member). "Rights, demands and commitments are distinct and none establishes another … each passage between them … needs its own warrant". **The contrast is the owner's, U41 of "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" ("rights vs demands vs commitments"); the definitions are A's (A95, A97).** "A does not credit the contrast to the owner." It went Active at the review of 2026-11-18, "at the status review's ruling (the owner delegated these rulings)". CLAIM-tmp0i6c0 rests on it.
- **CLAIM-725** (Active, interpretive; A's, A106 §4; a member of governance-of-possibility only). "the joint satisfiability of demands cannot certify that any deserves fulfilment, and an institution's maintained requirements cannot certify their own authority". This is the record's form of A51's "successful cooperation cannot establish the legitimacy of its own terms", with CLAIM-075 (Active, disclaimed: "Viability does not establish what ought to persist") and CLAIM-060 (Active; the owner's thread at U37 and U39: success "does not establish the well-being, viability or autonomy of its constituents").
- **CLAIM-741** (**Proposed, demonstrated**, thesis; A's, A74 to A106; not a member). Respecting a choice-protecting right "is a property of its policy over the protected choices, not of any realized history … so an evaluation over endpoints or realized histories cannot represent such rights, which constrain policies and transition rules". It is grounded in **CASE-283** (Proposed, construction, stipulated): "on every occasion the patient consents their histories are identical, and only A respects the right". CASE-283's "Checked" section is the filer's. "What it cannot show: which rights protect choices rather than outcomes … consent observed, however often, cannot show A's."
- **CLAIM-743** (Proposed; A's): a demand "can cease to apply legitimately only by the exercise of a valid normative power". **CLAIM-720** (Proposed, v5; A's at the owner's U37): no delegation "confers authority to determine whether that person keeps the standing from which claims against the arrangement arise". ARG-117 narrows it: Locke's source "supports the claim's form and not its scope". **CLAIM-735** (Proposed; A's): "rights can be prior in organization … even where principles are prior to them in justification". **CLAIM-729** (Proposed; A's): the duty "to help establish a needed one" is "bounded by whether the needed arrangement can be reached … by permissible, resource-bounded transitions".

### 3b. Defeated standing and the instrumentalist objection (A51's "Actual remaining work")

- **CLAIM-723** (Proposed, v4, interpretive, thesis; member; also capability-restraint-relational-freedom). "where a party has standing to authorize, suspend or contest an act, an actor who without adequate justification makes the act's consequences irreversible before that standing can be exercised defeats it, even where the objection would have failed". **The criterion is the owner's (U27 of "Assess Depp Heard Abuse": "acting before a recognized opportunity for intervention is able to take effect"; "wrongful unilateral action"). A59 gave it four conditions.** It is grounded in THEORY-1972 (Proposed; Fuentes v. Shevin), which its note reads as "a legal analogue, not a moral principle", whose "later doctrinal standing was not checked". Its causal half is CLAIM-464 (Active).
- **CLAIM-1155** (Proposed, interpretive, thesis; **the assistant's**, for LEAD-097; member; rests on CLAIM-348, CLAIM-523 and CLAIM-403). Where those harmed "have standing to contest the harm with it, reproducing the projections that keep their claims from registering wrongs them beyond the harm … so it holds where contesting would not have changed the harm's course". It keeps H, C and D apart (the harm, its continuation, the defeat of standing). It takes standing as "a condition" and not as derived, "since [CLAIM-738] says a right does not by itself generate a warranted demand". Its alternative is "the owner's choice": "If standing and duty are taken as one relation seen from two ends, this claim restates [CLAIM-624]". The assistant leans to keeping it.
- **ARG-228** (Proposed, form existence; rests on CLAIM-348, CLAIM-523 and CLAIM-723; grounds THEORY-1972; concludes CLAIM-1155 and CLAIM-614). It is a pair of water utilities "with the harm, its continuation and its expected continuation matched, differing only in a reproduced complaint taxonomy" (LEAD-097's note). It carries CLAIM-723's wrong "from timing to representation". Its precedent is **CASE-229** (Proposed, construction; A's), whose ARG-055 left open that "B's missing procedure would raise the risk of later harm".
- **CLAIM-1184** (Proposed, interpretive, **counter**; the assistant's; objects to CLAIM-1155 and CLAIM-614; not a member). "Standing to contest a harm matters only as a means of preventing or remedying harm … where the harm's course is fixed by causes contest could not touch, reproducing the projections that defeat it adds no wrong beyond the harm". Its best support is harmless-error practice. The record's answer, ARG-228, "concedes premise 3's case and denies premise 1". What would revive the objection: "a showing that the record's judgements in [CLAIM-723] and [THEORY-1972] are themselves only judgements of expected harm under uncertainty". Its note of 2026-11-23: "the objection is neither answered by a held argument nor shown sound … whether this objection goes Rejected, is the owner's call."
- **CLAIM-614** (Proposed; **the owner's**, U27 of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition") is the counter-normativity thesis CLAIM-1155 argues for. **CLAIM-624** (Proposed, v12; A's; member) is the duty-side form, "a verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible alternative". It has 34 dependants, and CLAIM-876 and CLAIM-877 rest on it.
