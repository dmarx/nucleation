# CLAIM-tmpsx7jd: Physics is the subset of mathematics relevant to phenomena it is possible to observe

## The claim

The owner: "physics as the subset of mathematics that's relevant to
phenomena for which it is possible to be observed". It follows the
regress of anchors (CLAIM-tmpw11e6). The physical witness ends that
regress (CLAIM-tmpo7kx7), but only through a defined standard
(CLAIM-tmpnl4xk).

## The assistant's refinement

CLAIM-tmppmx4w, with ARG-tmpwamhy: not a subset but a quotient, together
with a coordination. Physics is mathematics taken up to the equivalences
observation cannot break, tied to phenomena by definitions mathematics
does not contain.
