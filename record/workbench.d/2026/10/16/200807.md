*A Claude Code session on this repository. Owner turns are U1–U2; A1–A2 are
the replies.*

- **U1: the owner's formulation.** "Friston's 'Free Energy' approach is
  generally correct, but the cybernetic model is wrong. Rather than
  cycling between `Action <--> Perception`, we can cut 'Action' out of the
  loop completely and constrain attention to the internal feedback loop,
  which decomposes nicely into `Judgement (qua measurement that generates
  interpretations) <--> Observation`, which connects us back to our model
  and also makes our complaint with Friston's formulation precise."
  Filed as [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md).
- **A1: a restatement and three objections.** The restatement: active
  inference conditions on observations; the owner holds that the
  measurement context intervenes in them ([CLAIM-127](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-127.md), [TERM-039](../../record/terms.d/TERM-039.md)). The
  objections:
  - active inference already has mental action on precision, and deep
    models make a lower level the environment of the one above, so the
    loop may be active inference's one blanket in. Filed as
    [CLAIM-tmp9cjyo](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9cjyo.md);
  - after [ARG-002](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-002.md) and [CLAIM-041](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-041.md), "measurement that generates" has classical
    readings, and the complaint is distinct only on the strong one, which
    is [CLAIM-009](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-009.md)'s contextuality. Kept as [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md)'s `rests_on` and
    its defeater (a), not as a separate claim;
  - without action the loop has a self-confirming minimum, which the record
    holds as pathology ([THEORY-285](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-285.md), [TERM-020](../../record/terms.d/TERM-020.md)). Filed as [CLAIM-tmpki5gp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpki5gp.md).

  A1 also proposed an answer to the third: the world fixes each
  measurement context's outcome distribution, and the judgement chooses
  the context. It noted that U1 wavers between cutting action out and
  bracketing it. The owner has not yet answered either. Both are recorded
  in [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md) as A1's proposal.
- **U2: one model, and a case.** Asked which model "our model" meant, the
  pragmatic-transport or the will-organization one: "those are both
  manifestations of the same underlying model." The claim is filed on
  both lines and on distributed agency, where [CLAIM-275](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-275.md)'s operators act on
  a meaning canvas before they have the interpretation the act makes
  available. That claim is the record's nearest statement of the U1 idea,
  and [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md) rests on it. The statement is not filed as a claim:
  doing so needs the underlying model stated, which no entry yet does.
  The case: "a lucid human who struggles with locked-in syndrome." Filed
  as [CASE-tmpxgcq2](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpxgcq2.md).
- **A2: what the case does.** It cuts both ways. Classic LIS spares
  vertical eye movement and blinking, and eye movement is the action
  active inference models first ([LIT-tmp670rc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp670rc.md)'s oculomotor simulations), so
  classic LIS cannot decide the claim against active inference. The
  completely locked-in state is the test. Kübler and Birbaumer predict that
  goal-directed thinking is extinguished there ([LIT-tmpxcw33](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpxcw33.md)). Chaudhary et
  al. report a counterexample under a cloud ([LIT-tmpzuemc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpzuemc.md)). And any
  evidence of lucidity in CLIS crosses a channel that is itself an action,
  so the case can show the loop running down but not running with no
  action. Six works were registered unread for it (curation entry of
  20:08).
