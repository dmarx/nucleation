# SYNTHESIS-060: A symmetry principle can be borrowed for reconstruction kernels but a conservation law has to be derived: under Markov kernels a harmonic observable conserves only its mean and an approximately harmonic one drifts by at most the summed defects, mean conservation does not make an observable a symmetry, on a finite irreducible chain only constants are harmonic, and which pragmatic observables real kernels conserve is open

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line ("Contextual pragmatic
information transport"): the inquiry into which observables reconstruction
kernels conserve, begun by the owner's appeal to Noether at U37. The line's
account is built over it and the accounts of the line's other parts. The
mechanical map put these five entries in one part with seventeen on
reconstruction chains, drift bounds and attraction ([SYNTHESIS-067](SYNTHESIS-067.md)); they
share 9 pairs among themselves and 2 with those seventeen, so each group
has its own account. All four claims here also carry formal-apparatus.
[CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md) is the claim filed at this review (Arm B).*

## The account

- **Symmetry is invariance.** [CLAIM-040](../claims.d/CLAIM-040.md) (the owner's, at U37, conceded at
  A105; counter, demonstrated, Active) objects to [CLAIM-015](../claims.d/CLAIM-015.md): "Symmetry is a
  kind of invariance, and Noether's theorem is the point: invariance under
  transformation yields conservation". Its body: "The Noether half carries
  over only in a limited form ([CLAIM-093](../claims.d/CLAIM-093.md)): it holds for Markov processes in
  Baez and Fong's sense ([CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md), [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md)) and not in general."
- **The principle borrowed, the law derived.** [CLAIM-093](../claims.d/CLAIM-093.md) (A105's;
  disclaimed, demonstrated, Active), complementing [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md): "Not every
  invariance of a stochastic or communicative process implies a Noether
  conservation law: the symmetry principle can be borrowed, a conservation
  analogue has to be derived". The derivation it found is Baez and Fong's,
  "([CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md), qualified by [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md))". Its note of 2026-10-13 adds an
  instance from learning dynamics ([THEORY-211](../theory.d/THEORY-211.md)) that "shows the derivation
  can come out negative".
- **The criterion.** [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md) (the manuscript's §4, citing Baez and Fong;
  thesis, demonstrated, Active): "Under Markov reconstruction kernels, an
  observable's expectation is invariant when the observable is harmonic
  (K f = f), and drifts by at most Σ ε_i when ‖K_i f − f‖∞ ≤ ε_i; on a finite
  irreducible chain only constants are harmonic". Its note of 2026-10-10:
  "a non-trivial exactly conserved observable needs reducible dynamics with
  several closed classes, a restricted state space, or time-dependent
  kernels".
- **Only the mean.** [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md) (from the reading of Baez and Fong, [NOTE-598](../notes.d/NOTE-598.md);
  granted, demonstrated, Active) objects to [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md): "A harmonic
  observable conserves only its mean, and Baez and Fong show that mean
  conservation for every initial state does not make an observable a
  symmetry". [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md)'s version 2 narrowed its title to mean conservation,
  and its history says [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md) "holds both points against this claim,
  and they are now in its statement". [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md) also argues, as "the
  record's connection; neither paper draws it", that a Markov kernel's
  setting is the Baez–Fong case and not the Hamiltonian one.
- **The question.** [QUESTION-006](../questions.d/QUESTION-006.md) (A110 §IX.3; Open), refining [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md):
  "Which pragmatic observables are conserved, exactly or within a bounded
  defect, by actual reconstruction kernels, and how does that depend on
  frame, genre and adaptation?" Its note of 2026-10-11 lists [CLAIM-094](../claims.d/CLAIM-094.md) and
  [CLAIM-093](../claims.d/CLAIM-093.md) as bearing on it, unassessed; its body names [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md) as the
  manuscript's criterion.
- **Exact conservation keeps the start (filed at this review).**
  [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md) (the assistant's; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed), resting
  on [CLAIM-107](../claims.d/CLAIM-107.md) and [CLAIM-270](../claims.d/CLAIM-270.md): under Markov kernels, an observable
  conserved exactly at every step and not constant on the support of the
  starting law keeps information about the start at every generation,
  bounded below independently of the generation, so such a chain cannot
  converge to a start-independent law; a bounded defect does not prevent
  forgetting.
