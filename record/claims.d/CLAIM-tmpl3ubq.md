# CLAIM-tmpl3ubq: The 2005 Washington Nationals are not a case of a collective counted as continuing another with no causal path between their organizations: no participant counts them the original Senators continued, and the continuity the club and its supporters claim is a city's baseball, carried through the gap by the city and its supporters

## The claim

CLAIM-329's defeater asks for "cases in which competent participants and
the record's criteria count a later collective as the earlier one's
continuation with no causal path between their organizations, so that
resemblance plus a shared name does the work." CASE-105 offered the 2005
Nationals as the test, and the note of 2026-11-20 on CLAIM-329 held that
they do not meet it, pending LEAD-099.

The verdict CLAIM-tmp7z7si answers the pending part. The Nationals share
the original club's official name and its city, and still no party read
counts them that club continued: the franchise is counted through
Montreal, the original club's seasons stay with the Twins, and the club
at its founding called itself "a new team". So resemblance plus a shared
name did not do the work here, which is what the claim predicts.

What the club and supporters do claim is continuity of something else:
baseball in Washington, "the proud Major League tradition that has existed
in Washington since 1901", honoured in a Ring of Honor that takes in the
Homestead Grays, who were never in either Senators lineage. That is a
place's history claimed by its present club. If it is a persisting
collective at all, its carriers are the city's supporters and institutions
that kept it through 1972 to 2004, a causal path, and CLAIM-329 already
allows that "a lapse in its coherence need not end it".

## Why this reading is preferred

The statements themselves separate the units: the AP counts "the first
title in franchise history" and the first "to the nation's capital" since
1924 in one story, and the club displays the Senators' records on their
own page rather than combining them. Reading the heritage claim as a claim
of continuation would count as one what the participants keep as two.
