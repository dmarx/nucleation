# TERM-tmp8nuak: potential consequentiality

## The sense

The owner's phrase names a turn from asking what an organization can
reach to asking which differences can come to matter to it. A23 §1
grounds it in a case: "The presence of lithium is a physical distinction
regardless of the technological organization of society. But its
practical, economic, political and ecological significance depends on"
the capabilities and institutions that develop around it. "consequentiality
is neither an intrinsic property of a difference nor a fixed relationship
between that difference and an objective. It is a relation mediated by an
evolving organization."

A23 §2 separates four geometries: configuration ("How do two states
differ?"), accessibility ("Can one state be transformed into another?"),
consequentiality ("Does the distinction between two states produce a
difference elsewhere?") and potential consequentiality ("Under what
attainable conditions could that distinction become consequential?"). Its
examples of present equivalence without potential equivalence are genetic
variants that diverge under stress, constitutions that diverge when
interests conflict, and theories that become distinguishable after "the
construction of a new instrument".

## The measures

A23 §3: Δ_{g,p}(x, x′) = d_Y(F_g^p(x), F_g^p(x′)) for a probe p in
context g, and D_{B,T}^g(x, x′) = sup over the witness family
W_{B,T}(g) of Δ_{h,p}(x, x′), with x ∼_{B,T}^g x′ when D is zero. A23 §4:
κ_ε(x, x′) = inf_w {c(w) : Δ_w(x, x′) ≥ ε}. CLAIM-tmpik3z0 shows κ_ε is
the least budget at which D reaches ε, the record's separating budget,
and not a distance.

## Conditions the measures need

- **A common witness.** D is a pseudometric only if one witness family
  serves both configurations, as A23 says ("For fixed common witness
  families"). The development in question is then the party's, the one
  to whom the difference matters, and x and x′ are configurations of
  something else, as the lithium is of the land. If the developing
  organization is itself the system whose configurations differ, the
  witnesses reachable from x and from x′ need not be the same, and the
  comparison is of two developments, not of one difference under one
  development.
- **Outcome discrimination is not causation.** A23: "this is initially a
  geometry of outcome discrimination. Establishing that the focal
  difference *causes* the outcome difference requires appropriately
  controlled interventions".
- **Statistical distinguishability is not efficacy.** A23 §6 extends the
  Fisher metric to a family G_g(θ), with a direction in the kernel under
  g becoming distinguishable under g′, and adds: "A difference might be
  statistically obvious yet unable to influence institutional action."
  That is CLAIM-450's three standards.

## What the term is used for

- **Kinds of consequentiality.** A23 §9's table: physical, epistemic,
  practical, affective, axiological, volitional and constitutional, "not
  necessarily stages in a fixed developmental sequence". CLAIM-tmpk8knm
  uses the axiological kind for the emergence of value.
- **Modal reading.** A23 §10 writes C_i(d) for "distinction d is
  consequential to process or party i", latent consequentiality as
  ¬C_i(d) ∧ ◇C_i(d), and entrenchment as ◇□C_i(d). CLAIM-tmpi0rdz shows the
  two cannot be combined at one reversible peak budget.
- **Complementary capacities.** A23 §7: a distinction may become
  consequential only through several capacities acquired together
  (CLAIM-tmp6u4yn).
- **Abstraction.** A23 §8: "abstraction is a redistribution of
  consequentiality across organizational scales", the owner's CLAIM-762
  read through this term.
- **Richness of plurality.** A23 §14: "Ten perspectives that respond
  identically under every attainable circumstance provide little
  additional consequential coverage", with the partial order
  D_{B,T}(P₁) ⊆ D_{B,T}(P₂), CLAIM-1038 and CLAIM-1002 made comparable.
- **Capture.** A23 §13: under a narrow optimizer, distinctions "can remain
  intelligible while becoming practically ineffective", QUESTION-270's
  evaluation that is right and not consequential.

## What the term does not settle

A23 §15: "I would avoid using the total number of potentially
consequential distinctions as a universal measure of developmental
value." Which distinctions count depends on the granularity of the
language, and generation of new distinctions, as against activation of
existing ones, is not captured by a supremum over a fixed family
(CLAIM-tmp0k78x). LEAD-tmp18reb holds A23's separating cases.
