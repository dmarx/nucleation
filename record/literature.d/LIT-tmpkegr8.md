summary: >-
  Wang, Isola (2020), ICML 2020 (PMLR 119), arXiv 2005.10242. As the
  number of negatives grows, the normalized InfoNCE loss on the unit
  hypersphere converges to an alignment term, minimized exactly when both
  members of every positive pair share a feature, plus a uniformity term,
  minimized by a uniform distribution of features on the sphere. On a
  finite augmented dataset the two optima cannot both be met, and
  weighting alignment far above uniformity maps every input to one vector.
  Across several hundred encoders, low alignment and uniformity losses go
  with good downstream accuracy, and optimizing them directly matches or
  beats the contrastive loss. Here because A27 used it for how self-
