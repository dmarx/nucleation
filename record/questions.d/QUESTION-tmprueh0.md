# QUESTION-tmprueh0: When an agent's own processes revise its will-organization, what makes the result the continuation of that agent's will rather than a replacement authored by something else, and how can a history of authorization count when the standards for reading authorization are themselves revised?

## Why it is a question

The record separates endorsing a motive from making it effective
([CLAIM-047](../claims.d/CLAIM-047.md), [CLAIM-122](../claims.d/CLAIM-122.md)), explaining efficacy from settling whose the will
is ([CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md)), and endogenous self-legislation from legitimate
self-legislation ([CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md)). It holds that history matters to authorship:
[CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)'s voluntary restriction differs from an imposed one only in how it
came about.

What it does not settle is the case the exchange's constructions made
concrete: an organization that revises the machinery by which it revises
itself. If the standards by which an earlier self authorized a change are
themselves among what changes, the later self cannot simply check the
history against them. A28: "Endogenous self-legislation and legitimate
self-legislation remain different achievements."

## What would count as an answer

A criterion of continuation for will-organization under revision of its
own authorizing standards, with a case it classifies as continuation and
one it classifies as replacement, and the reason the history of
authorization decides between them. [QUESTION-118](QUESTION-118.md)'s successor relations are
the collective analogue; [CLAIM-344](../claims.d/CLAIM-344.md)'s resolve, which keeps the capacity to
revise a commitment, is the nearest held account of what must persist.

## Note of 2026-10-31: legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability

The owner's synthesis from review of the filing is filed as a claim,
CLAIM-tmpvr0mx: legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability are distinct
achievements, none of which justifies a demand. It bears on this question
because a revision can be legible, effective and executable while who has
the authority to make it stays open.

## Note of 2026-10-31: historically situated corrective authority

Also from the owner's review. Two organizations can have the same present
decision procedures and make the same decisions while differing in how
those procedures were authorized. [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md) is the basic case: voluntary
precommitment against imposed restriction.

This question asks how authorization persists, or fails to persist,
through transformations of the very standards used to judge authorization.
Identifying the process now effective cannot settle that, and neither can
showing that the process is maintained internally ([CLAIM-tmpr8p2x](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpr8p2x.md)).
Three things are distinct:

  endogenous governance ≠ historically continuous authorization ≠
  legitimate authority.

The owner judged this possibly the most important remaining connection
between the record's account of will and its account of normativity.
