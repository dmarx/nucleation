# LEAD-tmpuoufv: Run repeated reconstruction chains, cross-modal (text, image, caption, image) against text-only of the same length, scored with an overlapping-context design at each stage

## What it would do

Fix CLAIM-087's margin and rate, then run CASE-009's chains under its four
conditions against text-only chains of equal length, scoring each stage
with overlapping contexts.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-004: "Repeated reconstruction chains"
- QUESTION-014: "Independently sampled chains under stable and shifting
  frames, scored for footing by raters blind to condition"
- CLAIM-087: "The defeat condition's margin and rate are still not fixed"

## What would count

Faster drift in cross-modal chains than in text-only ones beyond the fixed
margin supports CLAIM-076 and CLAIM-087; equal drift defeats them.
