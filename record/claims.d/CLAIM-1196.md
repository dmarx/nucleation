# CLAIM-1196: A self may be considered as a sloppy mess of connected perspectives: overlapping, partly coordinated and interfering perspectives with no clean boundaries between them and no single executive over them, and since what can separate one perspective from another is relative to the tests that can be afforded, so is the self's apparent division into them

## The claim

The owner, U6: "therefore, so is our ability to form distinctions relevant
to distinguishing (perhaps 'separating' is Nucleation's sense?)
perspectives. Consider a 'self' as a literal sloppy mess of connected
perspectives."

Read colloquially: a self is many perspectives, connected, overlapping and
interfering, with no clean boundaries and no executive above them; and the
lines one draws between them are drawn by the tests one can afford, so they
are as budget-relative as the perspectives' addresses ([TERM-165](../terms.d/TERM-165.md)).

## What the record holds of it

- **The inference.** "Separating" is the record's word, in [TERM-138](../terms.d/TERM-138.md)'s
  sense of classes: an observer or readout under its budget telling two
  states or populations apart. That this is budget-relative and grows with
  nested probe families is [CLAIM-574](CLAIM-574.md) and [SYNTHESIS-109](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-109.md).
- **Perspectives without an executive.** [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md) (several perspectives in
  one agent, one across agents), [CLAIM-355](CLAIM-355.md) (binding real while the single
  owner is attributed), [CLAIM-454](CLAIM-454.md) (a recovered mode is at most a
  constituent of a perspective), [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md) (identifying overlapping
  perspectives without fixing their number).
- **A self as an organization of processes**, not a further perspective
  over them: [CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md), [CLAIM-417](CLAIM-417.md).

What the proposal adds is to take the mess as the self rather than as an
obstacle to finding it: no privileged decomposition need exist. A13 §6 and
A15 developed it ([CLAIM-1044](CLAIM-1044.md), [CLAIM-1175](CLAIM-1175.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-23: what holds it back

Filed as one of two readings of the owner's U6, beside [CLAIM-1174](CLAIM-1174.md), and
the owner has not chosen between them. [CLAIM-574](CLAIM-574.md) carries only its last
clause, the test-relativity of the division; 'no clean boundaries' and 'no
single executive' have no case in the record.
