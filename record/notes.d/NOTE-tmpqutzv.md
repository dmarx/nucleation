# NOTE-tmpqutzv: Benjamin (2019) — Race After Technology, Introduction "The New Jim Code"

## Contribution

A framework for discriminatory design.

## Key results

1. p. 1: "so few people have a genuine say in the world in which they are forced to live".
2. p. 4: "the road to inequity is paved with technical fixes".
3. p. 6: "tech designers encode judgments into technical systems" and claim the results are exterior to the encoding.
4. p. 24: the four forms, "engineered inequity, default discrimination, coded exposure, and technological benevolence".

## Assumptions

Social norms are constitutive of technical design.

## Concepts

New Jim Code; default discrimination; coded exposure; technological benevolence.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmpkjv7u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkjv7u.md)**: contests version 1's last clause. "Default discrimination" is subordination produced by defaults and inherited data with no discretionary act now; if it is domination, a dependence lacking a present discretion-holder can be domination, unless discretion exercised once, at design, counts. See [ARG-tmpsh7he](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpsh7he.md).
- **[CLAIM-tmpu2cwc](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu2cwc.md)**: consistent with its premise that inherited standards (defaults) cause persistent failures.

## Limitations

The Introduction only.
