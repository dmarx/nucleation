# LIT-tmpgv3da: Unhappy or unsatisfied: distinguishing the role of negative affect and need frustration in depressive symptoms over the academic year and during the COVID-19 pandemic

Levine, S. L., Brabander, C. J., Moore, A. M., Holding, A. C., Koestner, R. (2021). Unhappy or unsatisfied: distinguishing the role of negative affect and need frustration in depressive symptoms over the academic year and during the COVID-19 pandemic. *Motivation and Emotion, 46(1), 126-136*. doi:10.1007/s11031-021-09920-3.

## Key takeaways

Read in full on 2026-11-03 ([NOTE-tmpe4mmg](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe4mmg.md)). THEORY: [THEORY-tmpymwd8](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpymwd8.md)
(Active).

- Need frustration, not negative affect, predicted later depressive symptoms
  in both samples.
- Order is not established: no random intercepts in the reported models, and
  depression predicted later frustration at one lag.
- One symptom domain and all self-report, so it meets only part of
  [THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md)'s promote_when.

## Standing in the record

Whether need frustration predicts depressive symptoms beyond negative affect, the distinction [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) draws between frustration and distress.
