# LEAD-029: Derive a prediction that follows from the shared organizational variables and not from phenomenon-specific or central-executive models, and a joint prediction of two lines through the mapping

## What it would do

Derive the prediction with flexibility penalized, then run A55 §7's
comparison of one model against separate models.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-286](../claims.d/CLAIM-286.md): "a prediction that follows from the shared organizational
  variables and not from phenomenon-specific or central-executive models
  with flexibility penalized"
- [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md): "no joint prediction of the pragmatic-transport and
  will-organization lines has been derived through the mapping"
- [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md): "the test that would tell them apart, which A55 §7 states, has
  not been run"

## What would count

A joint prediction that holds where separate models miss supports
[CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md); none defeats the unification.
