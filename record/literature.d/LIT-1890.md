# LIT-1890: Stochastic dynamics and non-equilibrium thermodynamics of a bistable chemical system: the Schlögl model revisited

Vellela, M., & Qian, H. (2009). Stochastic dynamics and non-equilibrium thermodynamics of a bistable chemical system: the Schlögl model revisited. *Journal of the Royal Society Interface*, 6, 925–940.

Read in full in [NOTE-1474](../notes.d/NOTE-1474.md).

## Why this is here

A13's worked example of a bistable dissipative system whose maintained state has a finite stochastic lifetime. It is the chemical counterpart of the saddle-node constructions in [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md), and of [CASE-111](../cases.d/CASE-111.md). The reading bears that out and says what the deterministic cases leave out: at finite size a maintained regime is metastable, it is lost by fluctuation on a time exponential in size, and the barrier setting that time closes at the fold, as (distance)^{3/2} in this model ([THEORY-1273](../theory.d/THEORY-1273.md)). It is also a clean counterexample to ranking steady states by entropy production ([THEORY-1270](../theory.d/THEORY-1270.md)), against [THEORY-671](../theory.d/THEORY-671.md).
