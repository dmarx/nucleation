# LIT-tmp9n5sf: Maximum entropy production principle in physics, chemistry and biology

L. M. Martyushev, V. D. Seleznev (2006), *Physics Reports 426(1), 1–45* — DOI-10.1016/j.physrep.2005.12.001

## Standing in the record

Seeded on 2026-10-02 with the critics of Prigogine's extremum principles and
the maximum-entropy-production (MEP) literature, at the owner's request to
fill out the record's coverage of dissipative structures. No anthology topic
holds it, and the anthology does not hold it.

`Deferred` because neither the text nor the publisher's abstract could be
reached, so no NOTE is filed and nothing here describes its argument. What
the record can say comes from the works filed beside it.

- **Kleidon's review** ([LIT-tmp8wfl8](LIT-tmp8wfl8.md), read) names it, with Martyushev 2007,
  as the existing review of MEP's "physical foundation". It defers to it
  instead of covering that ground. For "technical discussion" of how MEP
  differs from Prigogine's minimum principle, it points to a different
  paper, Martyushev, Nazarova and Seleznev 2007 (J. Phys. A 40:371), which
  the record does not hold.
- **Dewar 2003** ([LIT-tmpbf3wy](LIT-tmpbf3wy.md), read) cites the same authors' earlier
  crystal-growth work (Doklady Physics, 2000) as empirical support for
  MEP.
- **Semantic Scholar's machine-generated summary** (not the authors'
  abstract, and not checked against the text) says the review's objectives
  are "summation and analysis of studies dealing with MEPP and the
  thermodynamic and statistical basis of the principle (including the
  relationship of MEPP with the second law of thermodynamics and
  Prigogine's principle)".

It is the reading that would settle two questions this batch leaves open:

- how the MEP literature itself relates MEP to Prigogine's minimum
  principle, beyond Kleidon's relaxation-versus-selection distinction;
- whether it answers Landauer's 1975 objection ([LIT-tmpohska](LIT-tmpohska.md)) that
  steady-state selection is kinetic.

It predates Grinstein and Linsker's critique of Dewar ([LIT-tmpflluj](LIT-tmpflluj.md)), so it
cannot reflect it.
