The owner asked to "update the record to account for this discussion, including attempting to fill in gaps where you identify them", for the ChatGPT exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (27 owner turns, 43 visible assistant turns, 2026-11-20 to 21). It follows the `read-and-file` skill. The dialectic regime was on.

## The source

The owner asked why assistant turns fell from 96 to 43, suspecting the decoder dropped content. It did not drop visible turns, but it did drop code and code output stored in `content.text`, and it left out reasoning summaries and hidden drafts without a count. The decoder was changed to keep all assistant content (pull request 608, merged before this filing). On this share, all 292 assistant and 95 tool messages are in the file or its closing count. The 53 hidden assistant messages are copies: every line of 15 or more characters, and every paragraph, also appears in a visible turn. The filing worked from the full output, reasoning blocks included.

## How it was filed

1. **Ideas.** Seven readers each built an idea ledger for a run of turns (about 350 items), mapping each to the record as held, partly held or not held, and listing works and gaps.
2. **Works.** 51 cited works were registered as Deferred LITs in a commit of their own. 25 more were already held.
3. **Themes.** Five drafters filed the argument by theme from a shared brief (voices, fields, gaps, checks). Each drafter checked every number it filed with a script, and the entry says how:
   - mechanisms and distributions;
   - scale invariance and criticality;
   - complexity, information and sloppiness;
   - high-dimensional representations;
   - singularities and small eigenvalues.
4. **Coverage.** A separate reader reread the whole exchange against what was filed (step 10). A fix-up drafter then corrected what that reread found.
5. **Syntheses.** A separate auditor did step 9b.
