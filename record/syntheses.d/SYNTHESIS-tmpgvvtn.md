# SYNTHESIS-tmpgvvtn: Forming, keeping and moving into a capacity are separate resource requirements in the record, reaching a configuration can cost more than holding either end, feasible, reachable and reliably reached come apart, and a budget pegged to last period's peak loses transitions that need slack

Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own (ADR-075, step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which ADR-083's one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**Three requirements.** CLAIM-569 (Active, demonstrated, thesis): a form has at least three resource requirements, "to construct it, to maintain it, and to move into or out of it, and none fixes another". So "having enough to sustain it is not having enough to acquire it".

**Reaching has its own threshold.** CLAIM-571 (Active, demonstrated, thesis): for a fixed architecture and one resource held at each moment, the threshold for reaching a configuration is "the least, over admissible transition paths, of the peak requirement along the path". It is infinite when no admissible path joins. TERM-106 (Active) names the difference: reconfiguration slack.

**Three properties.** CLAIM-567 (Active, demonstrated, thesis): a configuration's being feasible, reachable and reliably reached are three properties, "each implied by the next and none implying it".

**The ratchet.** CLAIM-577 (Active, demonstrated, thesis): "A budget set each period to the peak resource spent the period before never rises". The reachable configurations shrink, and a transition whose threshold exceeds the holding cost is lost after one period of holding, "unless the budget is raised from outside". The claim covers only that rule. It excludes rules "with a floor, growth term, carry-over or discretion".

## Not stated on the record

- **Budgets set by recent activity lose that ability silently,** as a general law. The manuscript's P6 stated it. CLAIM-577 holds it only for the pure spend-matching rule, with no raise from outside.
