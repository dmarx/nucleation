**What the pass took.** [ADR-065](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-065.md) was filed this morning, in the same pull
request, from a survey of every Proposed THEORY's promote_when. The survey
flagged about 58 whose conditions ask for more than their titles assert.
This pass reviewed those 58 in six clusters: two of one-study titles, one of
attribution titles, the endosymbiosis batch of 2026-10-27, one of titles to
narrow first, and one of proofs waiting on a second checker. Six reviewers
read, the rulings were made here, and the same reviewers applied them.

**53 promoted, 5 kept.**

- **The 53 are now Active.** For each, the reading the title states is
  read in full, and nothing in the record contradicts it.
- **What a promote_when asked beyond the title was kept.** It went to one
  of three places:
  - a Proposed THEORY that `extends` the reading and keeps the old
    condition word for word, where the source asserts the wider thesis
    ([ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md) kind 1);
  - a QUESTION, where it does not;
  - an interpretive CLAIM, where the old title carried the record's own
    critique (kind 2).

  That made 46 new entries: 32 THEORYs, 10 QUESTIONs and 4 CLAIMs.
- **Kept Proposed:**
  - **[THEORY-989](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-989.md)** and **[THEORY-613](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-613.md)**: [THEORY-989](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-989.md)'s condition disputes the
    study's own estimator (§7); [THEORY-613](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-613.md)'s dimension count is
    inconsistent in the copy read, so the version of record is asked for
    (§4).
  - **[THEORY-1192](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1192.md)**, **[THEORY-564](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-564.md)** and **[THEORY-351](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-351.md)**: their titles are
    general (§6). [THEORY-1192](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1192.md)'s condition asked for less than its title
    claims, and now asks for the per-domain bias-corrected estimates.

**The review found errors, not just missing hedges.** The survey's premise
was that these titles were fine and their conditions too strict. For most
that held. But a careful reading for promotion found titles that misstate
their sources:

- **[THEORY-375](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-375.md)**: MRU beat LRU by up to 9.6 times, not 2.3. The 2.3 was
  its margin over the distance value.
- **[THEORY-383](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-383.md)**: "whenever updates arrived" contradicts the authors' own
  conclusion that the stored result won below five updates a second.
- **[THEORY-1158](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1158.md)**: "without beating spectral synchronization" is false
  against plain spectral synchronization, on three of four rows of Table 1.
- **[THEORY-889](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-889.md)**: the thermometer effect is about a third of a standard
  deviation, not a tenth (p. 626).
- **[THEORY-1104](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1104.md)**: "no pathway from one donor" missed the Myxococcota
  lead in two pathways.
- **[THEORY-924](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-924.md)**: Elo ratings are the row means of the log-odds only when
  those have no cyclic component. In the entry's own copy example the
  batch fixed point is ±71.9 Elo points, against row means of ±95.4.
- **[THEORY-943](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-943.md)**: uninformative equilibria exist in the authors'
  two-signal example, not in general. A four-signal model under the
  quadratic rule has none; it is written out in the entry. The recorded
  misreport score of −0.754 is −0.760, and −0.754 comes from rounded
  posteriors.

Each wrong clause is dropped with a dated note (kind 3), or corrected in
the wide form filed beside the reading. A reading's title had gone
unchecked against its source as long as nothing tried to promote it; the
same is likely true of the Proposed readings [ADR-065](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-065.md) did not flag.

**The second checker re-derived, and the bodies now carry the work.**
[ADR-065](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-065.md) §5 counts a re-derivation recorded in the file once a second
checker has followed it. The original scripts were not on the machine, so
the checker wrote its own, numpy only, each with a control that had to
fail. Scripts in a session's scratchpad are not in the record. So each of
the ten proof entries now writes its derivation out, and says in a
sentence what was run, on how many cases, and what the control did.
Someone can redo it from the file alone.

**Two rules came out of applying the decision.**

- **An `answers` edge is not an attribution.** All twelve endosymbiosis
  readings `answered` [QUESTION-277](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-277.md). Promoting them would have left an Open
  question with Active answers. None answers it as posed, so each is now
  `bears_on` ([ADR-060](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-060.md)).
- **The README and [ADR-065](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-065.md) disagreed on the version of record.** §4 drops a
  demand for it where no discrepancy is named. The README sent it to a
  QUESTION. The README is corrected. Five entries dropped the demand:
  [THEORY-899](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-899.md), [THEORY-926](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-926.md) and [THEORY-943](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-943.md), and the wide forms beside
  [THEORY-927](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-927.md) and [THEORY-911](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-911.md). The last says that only the abstract was
  compared, so a discrepancy in its counts would restore the demand.
