The owner asked for a pass over `metaphysics`, after `mereology` and
`agency`. The tag held 45 works: 16 read, 27 skimmed seeds and 2 seeded
from the abstract. The 29 unread went to seven close readers, grouped by
cluster. The coverage audit added four works to the tag, and the three of
them that were unread went to an eighth reader.

## Outcome

- **Read in full: 30.**
  - **Active: 27.**
  - **Proposed: 1.** Allori, *Wave-functionalism* ([LIT-200](../../record/literature.d/LIT-200.md)). Its claim to
    bypass PBR is asserted in one footnote. It is also inconsistent about
    whether ψ is nomological.
  - **Rejected: 2.**
    - Karpenko ([LIT-215](../../record/literature.d/LIT-215.md)). It misreads Floridi three ways. It says an
      AdS/CFT boundary theory is two-dimensional, and that a quantum field
      operator obeys the Schrödinger equation. Its abstract's "it is proven"
      has no argument behind it.
    - The pregeometry paper by Arsiwalla, Elshatlawy & Rickles ([LIT-102](../../record/literature.d/LIT-102.md)).
      Nothing in it is constructed; the "link" it draws is a summary of
      other programmes. The same group's observers paper ([LIT-133](../../record/literature.d/LIT-133.md)) was
      Rejected in the agency pass.
- **Not obtainable: 2.** Pernu 2015 ([LIT-144](../../record/literature.d/LIT-144.md)) and Carson's Element
  ([LIT-104](../../record/literature.d/LIT-104.md)). Both stay `Deferred`, with the attempts in their status notes.
  Pernu's open 2013 dissertation (HELDA) covers the same argument at an
  earlier stage.

**The skims again overstated in the same direction:** a programme or proposal
was reported as a result. The most consequential cases:

- **Chakravartty ([LIT-196](../../record/literature.d/LIT-196.md))** says outright that nothing in the chapter tells
  against *eliminative* OSR. The skim had it collapsing the view into
  eliminativism.
- **Gao's 194-page monograph ([LIT-121](../../record/literature.d/LIT-121.md))** gives three routes to ψ-reality. All
  three rest on one premise that his own fn. 10 undercuts.
- **Holik et al. ([LIT-107](../../record/literature.d/LIT-107.md))** do not derive the Indistinguishability Principle.
  They move it into a postulate, and two printed proofs contain errors.
- **Barrett et al. on IIT ([LIT-185](../../record/literature.d/LIT-185.md))** concede that IIT 4.0 does say "high Φ =
  more consciousness". Their multi-dimensional measure is a proposed
  revision of the theory.
- **The SEP *Personal Identity and Ethics* ([LIT-130](../../record/literature.d/LIT-130.md))** shows the metaphysics
  constraining the ethics but rarely settling it. The current entry, Dufner
  2025, also replaced Shoemaker's at the same URL.
- **De Haro & Butterfield ([LIT-199](../../record/literature.d/LIT-199.md))** say AdS/CFT is an exception only to
  duality being compatible *solely* with epistemic emergence. It is not an
  exception to "dualities are not indicative of emergence".
- **Hazelwood ([LIT-156](../../record/literature.d/LIT-156.md))** targets Laland and colleagues, not [LIT-110](../../record/literature.d/LIT-110.md). [LIT-110](../../record/literature.d/LIT-110.md)'s
  own standing note, which said otherwise, is corrected.
- **Rizi ([LIT-150](../../record/literature.d/LIT-150.md)) and McKenzie ([LIT-141](../../record/literature.d/LIT-141.md))** both lean on LLM "emergent
  abilities".
  - Rizi does so in one unhedged sentence. Its example is arithmetic, the
    case Schaeffer et al. ([ANTH-LIT-471](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-471.md)) show to be a metric artefact.
  - McKenzie examines the question (§21.2), and still calls Wei et al.
    ([ANTH-LIT-470](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-470.md)) "concrete and rigorous".
