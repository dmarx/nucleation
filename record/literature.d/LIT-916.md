# LIT-916: Brief Online Training in Mindfulness Meditation Momentarily Reduces Some Impulsive Behaviors: A Randomized Controlled Trial

Chunlei Ge, Yuhong Xie, Meijuan Sun, Fanfan Fan, Yiemin Zhang and Lei Liu (2024), "Brief Online Training in Mindfulness Meditation Momentarily Reduces Some Impulsive Behaviors: A Randomized Controlled Trial", *Mindfulness* 16: 448–464 — [doi:10.1007/s12671-024-02479-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12671-024-02479-3)

## Key takeaways

*From the title and a search-result summary of the abstract; the paper is unread, and which impulsive behaviours improved is unchecked.*

- **Design.** Brief online mindfulness training against a monitor group and a control group; impulsivity measured by Stroop, Go/No-Go and delay-discounting tasks, at post-test and follow-up.
- **Result, as summarised.** Relative to the monitor and control groups, the mindfulness group did not show significantly improved impulsive decision-making (delay discounting) at post-test. The title reports that some impulsive behaviours were reduced momentarily. No effect was sustained at follow-up.
- **Why the record wants it.** If the reduction was in response inhibition while discounting stayed put, that is the dissociation [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md) predicts: which regime acts changes without the valuations changing. Unread, it cannot carry that weight; it is filed so that the check is one reading away.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-11 beside [LIT-906](LIT-906.md), the earlier and smaller study it partly disagrees with. Tags as for that one, with `cognition` for the Stroop and Go/No-Go tasks. Not held in the anthology.

## Note of 2026-11-22: read

Read in full for [LEAD-078](../leads.d/LEAD-078.md) ([NOTE-tmp1yxz6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp1yxz6.md), [THEORY-tmpbidvo](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpbidvo.md)). The
takeaways above, written before reading, hold as far as they go: the
"reduced impulsive behaviours" are shorter Stroop and Go/No-go reaction
times with accuracy unchanged, and delay discounting did not differ
between arms. Whether faster responses are better self-control the
paper does not show, so it does not carry the weight [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md) hoped
for ([ARG-tmpc8jqe](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpc8jqe.md), Rejected).
