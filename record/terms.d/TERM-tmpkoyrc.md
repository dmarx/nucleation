# TERM-tmpkoyrc: framing intervention

## Definition

A33 §2 first separated "an interpretive *frame* from the *operation of
framing*": A and B "prompt someone to interpret an utterance through
affectionate complicity" or "moral judgment". A39 §3.2 made the split explicit:
a framing intervention is "an action that changes the conditions under which an
utterance is interpreted". "Avoid conflating these concepts:

- A friend speaking is a property of a communicative situation.
- Telling a reader that the speaker is a friend is an intervention.
- Asking a reader to consider an utterance as affectionate is another
  intervention."

## What it is not

Not the frame ([TERM-tmpu9tab](TERM-tmpu9tab.md)). The split exposes an ambiguity in A30's operators A and B
([TERM-tmppital](TERM-tmppital.md)), which were at once a frame adopted and a question asked. A35 §6 had seen
the consequence for experiments: "forcing a participant to make an explicit
judgment is itself an intervention." The manuscript's "stochastic framing
operations" (§9) are interventions in this sense, and its §10 measurement
protocol separates "context-free descriptions from elicitation-context
variables".
