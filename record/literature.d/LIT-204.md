# LIT-204: Realism about the Wave Function

Eddy Keming Chen (2018), *Philosophy Compass 14(7), e12611 (2019); preprint arXiv* — [ARXIV-1810.07010](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.07010)

## Key takeaways

- A realist about quantum mechanics has three families of options for what the wave function is — a physical object (ontological), something law-like (nomological), or an entity of its own kind (sui generis) — and which ones are available depends on the chosen solution to the measurement problem.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Chen surveys how a realist should understand the wave function, the puzzling central object of quantum mechanics. Setting aside instrumentalist and epistemic readings, he sorts realist proposals into ontological interpretations, nomological interpretations, and a sui generis interpretation. The survey is confined to non-relativistic quantum mechanics and ties each option to the main solutions of the measurement problem.
