The owner pointed out that the record held hundreds of read works and very few
theories. The count was 17, not nine, but the point stood. [THEORY-001](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-001.md) to 009 are
on contrastive-learning kernels, 010 to 016 on contextuality, and 017 on
Hilbert-space intrinsic structure. Twenty-one topics had no theory at all,
among them philosophy of science (about 40 read works), ethics (about 20),
society and governance (about 22), agency, consciousness, metaphysics,
learning theory and probabilistic modelling.

**How it was done.** Five agents drafted theories under one brief, each in its
own domain:

- information and learning theory, with thermodynamics;
- representation learning and symmetry;
- logic, quantum logic and probability;
- agency, ethics and mind;
- philosophy of science, metaphysics and society.

The brief had four rules:

- draw only from readings the record already holds, with no new reading;
- every `source:` must be a read work, never a `Deferred` one;
- default to `Proposed` unless the readings settle the claim;
- a `Rejected` account is welcome when a reading refutes it.

The drafts were reviewed before filing.

- **Titles.** Titles over about 280 characters were shortened to the claim.
  The qualifications stay in the body.
- **Dropped.** One draft was dropped: the Arrow/Paretian-liberal contrast
  duplicated [THEORY-027](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-027.md).
- **Rejected accounts.** The two Rejected drafts are restated so that the
  title is the account being rejected, matching how the record's other
  statuses read.

**What the four phase papers showed.**

- **Saxe et al.** refutes all three IB claims as general statements:
  - the information-plane phases depend on the nonlinearity and on the
    estimator;
  - compression and generalisation dissociate in all four combinations;
  - full-batch gradient descent compresses as SGD does.

  The drift-to-diffusion transition in gradient signal-to-noise is real, but
  it is generic.
- **Zucchet et al.** show three phases in fact learning: marginals, then a
  plateau while the recall circuit forms, then storage. That is the reverse of
  Nanda et al.'s memorisation, then circuit, then cleanup ([LIT-345](../../record/literature.d/LIT-345.md)).
- **Prakash & Martin** report a late collapse in generalisation, tracked by a
  spectral weight metric. Their headline claim that α < 2 heralds the
  collapse is contradicted by their own weight-decay control.
- **Zhou et al.** show a chaos-to-confinement transition: a parameter
  perturbation stops changing the run's basin, and the tangent kernel settles
  into a cone.

None of the four measures a network-intrinsic fall in I(X;T). A circuit
forming during a flat loss is shown by intervention only in Nanda and Zucchet.
