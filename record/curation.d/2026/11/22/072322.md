## What the readings changed

- **CLAIM-841**: the KPZ row holds untuned for d ≤ 2 only, and in experiment untuned is not generic; the flocking row cites a diverging structure factor, not ΔN ∝ ⟨N⟩^φ; the XY row rests on Kosterlitz and Thouless, not on Mermin and Wagner.
- **CLAIM-842**: the graph version it supposes is a theorem for spin models in equilibrium (Cassi), with conditions (a symmetry generated by local generators, finite second moments) and exceptions; Toner–Tu bounds it out of equilibrium.
- **QUESTION-508**: four partial answers, and one shape across them (CLAIM-tmpvq33w).
- **CLAIM-809**: three cases with the optimum in three places (toward, below, and on the transition).

- **The owner's CLAIM-tmp2r6ur** stays as worded; Wang et al. find the top outliers to be the signal in their settings, against its stop-word half, and Benigni and Péché, now registered, may test that half directly.
