# LIT-1393: Report of the Presidential Commission on the Space Shuttle Challenger Accident

Presidential Commission on the Space Shuttle Challenger Accident (1986). *Report to the President*, vol. 1, 6 June 1986. https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/genindex.htm. Chapter 5, "The Contributing Cause of The Accident" (the launch decision), chapter 6, "An Accident Rooted in History", and chapter 7, "The Silent Safety Program".

Skimmed on 2026-10-22 in [NOTE-1090](../notes.d/NOTE-1090.md): volume 1's chapters V–VIII and
the Recommendations in full, the rest of volume 1 in part, volumes 2–5
not read.

## Why this is here

The owner proposed the events before and after Challenger as a case of an institution documenting and learning from mistakes (U25). The record reads the Columbia board's account as [THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md). The case is [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md).

**From the reading, 2026-10-22.** The report supports the case's account
of the warning and the reversal, and is the primary source for the
Challenger half of [THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md), which could take it as a second source.
It does not contain the post-1986 reforms the case lists (it recommends
the independent safety office; it does not record its creation), and
the case's account of the launch decision is in chapter V, which the
case does not cite. See [NOTE-1090](../notes.d/NOTE-1090.md) for its bearing on [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md),
[CLAIM-416](../claims.d/CLAIM-416.md) and [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md).
