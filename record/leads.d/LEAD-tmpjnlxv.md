# LEAD-tmpjnlxv: Learn a non-abelian task with no matrix representation imposed, and recover its irreducibles from the weights without supplying the group, or show them unrecoverable

## What it would do

Train a network on a small non-abelian group operation and search the
weights for irreducible representations by a method that is not given the
group.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-058: "A non-abelian task learned without a matrix
  representation imposed, analysed without the group being supplied in
  advance."

## What would count

Irreducibles recovered blind answer QUESTION-058; failure with the group
supplied as a control says the method, not the network, is the limit.
