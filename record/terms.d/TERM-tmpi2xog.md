# TERM-tmpi2xog: precedence, jurisdiction and suspension

## Definition

- **Precedence:** among applicable constraints, which one controls?
- **Jurisdiction:** which constraints count as applicable to this case?
- **Suspension:** when may an otherwise applicable constraint be disabled?

## What it is not

Not a ranking of rules. A formally absolute rule can have little practical
authority if another process controls entry into its domain. The distinction
came out of the Fraenkel discussion (U63) and is what [CLAIM-tmpgnnj0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgnnj0.md)
and [CLAIM-tmpo4t0w](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpo4t0w.md) are stated in.
