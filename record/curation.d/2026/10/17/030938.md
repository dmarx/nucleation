**Not registered.** Lefebvre and Lefebvre (1984), *Reflexive Control: The
Soviet Concept of Influencing an Adversary's Decision Making Process*,
Science Applications report SAI-84-024-FSRC-E. DTIC blocked this client,
and the Internet Archive has no copy. Chotikul quotes its definition.

**What the reading changed.** [CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md)'s model covers only one of the
methods Chotikul lists, "transferring false information about the real
situation", as the steering term b. The others act on the target's goal,
doctrine, decision, or decision procedure. "Creating the doctrine for the
opponent" makes commitment a target as well as a defence.

**A slip.** One reading agent's first Unpaywall query sent the owner's email
address as the API's contact parameter. It was not repeated, and later
briefs forbid sending any personal identifier to a web service.
