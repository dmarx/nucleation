## How each is done

- **With a script**: run it unchanged with python3 -I in a fresh
  session, compare every figure the entry quotes, and record the result
  in a dated note on the entry ("rerun by a session other than the
  filer's", with the commit and any mismatch).
- **Without a script** ([CLAIM-tmp7rl3d](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7rl3d.md), [CLAIM-tmp9ia3w](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9ia3w.md), constructions
  stated in prose): re-derive from the entry's own derivation, step by
  step, and note any step that does not follow.
- **No status change in the pass itself.** Promotions under the
  delegation follow, one by one, where the recomputation matches and the
  premises are Active; the rest go to the owner. [CLAIM-889](../claims.d/CLAIM-889.md) already meets
  the rule (its ground [CASE-325](../cases.d/CASE-325.md) was rerun, its edges are Active) and was
  "Not promoted here" in the standing review; [CLAIM-tmp7rl3d](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7rl3d.md) is held back
  by its Proposed premise [CLAIM-tmpm76n5](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpm76n5.md) whatever the rerun finds.

## What would count

A mismatch is a correction filed on the entry, in the assistant's voice,
and in the manuscript's text where it quotes the figure. A match is
evidence of reproduction, which [LEAD-tmp0j28l](LEAD-tmp0j28l.md)'s first signal (an executed
study that reproduces) reads for its promotion review.
