**How they were read.** Thirteen readers each read one paper in full, in
parallel. Each filed a NOTE, one to three THEORYs and the LIT update. The
quotations the dated notes rest on were rechecked against the extracted texts.
Two needed the PDF itself, because of a hyphenated line break and a figure
caption. Several readers checked results by computation:
- Torres-Hugas et al.'s spectra and instability threshold;
- the conserved quantity of Hansen and Ghrist's Thm 9.3, which holds only
  with its weights;
- Spivak's wave and heat steps.

They found errors in the papers, recorded in the NOTEs:
- a sign in Di Biagio and Rovelli 2025's (B8);
- the proof of Hansen and Ghrist 2021's Thm 9.3 holds only for α = β;
- the uniqueness claim of Robinson's Prop. 25 is false under the max norm.

Sources were the arXiv preprints, except:
- Simon: a scan of the published article that Unpaywall lists;
- Clarke et al.: the authors' manuscript in Carnegie Mellon's repository;
- Stewart et al.: the accepted version in Warwick's repository.

**Lookups and corrections.**

- **Dates under [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md).**
  - Torres-Hugas et al. now take their arXiv v1 date, 21 April 2026; the
    journal version is of 13 October.
  - Zurek takes the journal's online date, 2 March 2009, which is earlier
    than the arXiv posting ([ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md) v2).
  - Stewart et al. take the electronic publication date the article prints,
    22 December 2003; Crossref's January 2003 print month is impossible for
    a paper accepted in August.
- **Summaries rewritten from the texts.** The exchange's descriptions were
  mostly right in outline and wrong in emphasis:
  - Hansen and Ghrist prove no Cheeger inequality.
  - Their "synchronization" is an estimation problem.
  - Spivak's paper never speaks of time scales; the difference between its
    heat and wave dynamics is the integrator, not the coupling.
  - Robinson's "canonical" rests on axioms, not a theorem, and his networks
    do not recalibrate.
  - Di Biagio and Rovelli's stable facts are stable for a third system,
    through inaccessibility. They are not records that persist.
- **Corrected in place:**
  - [QUESTION-285](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-285.md)'s attributions of persistence to Di Biagio and Rovelli
    and of recalibration to Robinson;
  - [CLAIM-471](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-471.md)'s "prove" for Torres-Hugas et al.;
  - [CLAIM-471](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-471.md)'s "which the dynamics selects" for Stewart et al.
- **What was not used.** CiteSeerX caches were not used, being neither the
  authors' nor the publisher's copies. Neither were the Internet Archive's
  controlled-lending copies or a Digital Library of India scan of uncertain
  status. No headless browser was used.

**What the readings found against the record.**

- **[CLAIM-471](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-471.md)'s thesis half is untested.** Almost every paper takes its
  correspondences as given, measured or designed. Interaction changes a
  correspondence only in Hansen and Ghrist's discourse sheaves, a
  constructed model, where the outcome is "learning to lie".
- **[CLAIM-480](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-480.md)'s computation is Baez and Fong's Example 2.9.** Composition
  can make a constraint of a new kind: eliminating an interior of a sheaf can
  leave a boundary constraint no pairwise sheaf expresses (Hansen and Ghrist
  §4.2).
- **[CLAIM-475](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) is supported by Simon.** Singer adds a third regime, where
  the evidence does not determine the gluing whatever the resources.
- **[CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md).** CEGAR is a worked case of refinement within a fixed
  answer space, the shape of its defeater (a), and so no case for its last
  clause.
- **[THEORY-621](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-621.md)'s price ("values are not held at all times") is dated.** Di
  Biagio and Rovelli 2025 give relative facts between interactions.
- **[QUESTION-285](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-285.md).** It gains an observer-free criterion for correlation,
  a repeatability criterion for records, a negative case for the top rung
  (learning to lie) and a positive one (a truth withheld from the
  apparatus).
