# SYNTHESIS-tmpj2fl0: A system's budgeted individuation is indexed by two budgets that run in opposite directions, observing splitting what can be told apart and acting under peak costs merging what can be turned into one another, so the two partitions agree only on levels common to both chains, which the order of probe costs decides; which category and enrichment is chosen fixes what counts as the same, and neither isomorphism nor a profile settles persistence

*A part account of the realizability line: the part filed from A's pastes
on Yoneda, individuation and resource-bounded objects (2026-11-05) and the
assistant's propositions offered with them. The line's account is built
over it and its four sibling parts.*

## The account

- **From outside.** CLAIM-574 (A's, demonstrated, Active): restricted
  Yoneda profiles over nested probe families only split objects as the
  budget grows, and "an equivalence a richer regime breaks was relative to
  its regime, not false"; the filer's guard is that the monotonicity needs
  "an every-probe comparison, which an averaged distortion is not".
- **From inside.** CLAIM-581 (the assistant's, demonstrated, Active): under
  peak costs on reversible moves the transition threshold is an extended
  pseudo-ultrametric, so identification within a budget is an equivalence
  whose classes nest into one tree, and under additive costs it is not.
  "The resource semantics is an individuation choice."
- **The pair.** CLAIM-557 (the assistant's, demonstrated, Active), resting
  on both: "A system's budgeted individuation is then indexed by a pair, an
  observing budget and an acting budget, and the two can be traded or
  decoupled." CLAIM-570 (A's construction, made exact by the assistant,
  demonstrated, Active) gives the two boundaries: the budget pairs at which
  everything indistinguishable is mutually reachable form an up-set, those
  at which everything mutually reachable is indistinguishable a down-set,
  with closed forms. Its body: "CLAIM-557 says the outside and inside
  partitions move in opposite directions; this gives the two boundaries at
  which one contains the other, and the region where they coincide, as
  numbers to compare across systems." CLAIM-592 (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active): the partitions agree exactly on partitions common
  to the observer's refinement chain and the merge tree, and "the cost
  order decides which of those levels actually appear"; its body:
  "CLAIM-557 said individuation is indexed by two budgets; this says what
  decides whether those budgets ever see the same thing."
- **One budget for both.** CLAIM-572 (the assistant's, demonstrated,
  Active): when observing and acting share one budget, the cheapest
  readiness buys a whole prefix of the probes, the affordable splits can be
  disconnected, and stopping when the next probe does not pay can miss the
  optimum. Its note of 2026-11-07 adds, "noted, not filed", that on
  CASE-206's vertex the non-trivial agreement of CLAIM-592 needs a shared
  budget of 4.125, more than the cheapest readiness and more than full
  observation.
- **Which category.** CLAIM-590 (the filer's construction from A's,
  demonstrated, Active): in the path groupoid of the degree-4 vertex every
  two configurations are uniquely isomorphic, while in the category
  enriched in peak footprint its folded states are 1.866 apart, a distance
  the enriched Yoneda embedding keeps; so "equivalence of possibilities is
  not equivalence of resource-bounded capabilities". CASE-199 (A's toy,
  with the filer's additions, Active) gives one move graph a directed,
  graded geometry that the groupoid forgets, and under peak and additive
  costs different distances. TERM-116 (A's, Proposed) separates incoming,
  outgoing, observational and maintenance profiles, the last of which
  "Yoneda does not supply"; its minimum distinguishing budget "is the
  observing counterpart of the transition threshold".
- **Isomorphism and persistence.** CLAIM-593 (the filer's construction
  from A's, demonstrated, Active): "Isomorphism is not persistence, in
  either direction": two separately made copies are isomorphic and two, and
  one mechanism with a hinge replaced can persist while its costed
  structure becomes non-isomorphic, so continuations are "structure added
  by assumptions about the domain, not read off the category".

What stays open is the structure and its tests: the bifiltration's
invariants and where one budget serves both (QUESTION-374, the
assistant's); the conditions under which a relational structure suffices
to individuate an object, and which transformations preserve it
(QUESTION-393, A's), refined by which interface individuates a component
(QUESTION-382, A's) and by whether restricted enriched profiles agree with
bisimulation (QUESTION-384, A's); and A's proposed probing study
(CASE-202, Proposed).
