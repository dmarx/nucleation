# LIT-2176: The fitness consequences of wildlife conservation translocations: a meta-analysis

Gross, Wilson & Wolak (2023). The fitness consequences of wildlife conservation translocations: a meta-analysis. *Biological Reviews* 99(2): 348–371. doi:10.1111/brv.13025. Authors' given names not checked.

## Key takeaways

*Not read. From the Crossref abstract, unverified until read.*

- **Translocated animals do worse than wild residents**: 64% lower odds
  of out-performing them, over 821 comparisons from 171 studies of 101
  species.
- **Pre-release enrichment narrowed the gap**, significantly.
- **No overall effect of captive generation time.**

## Standing in the record

Unfetched. It agrees in direction with Morris et al. (2021),
[LIT-2163](LIT-2163.md), read as its preprint, where captive-bred stock showed lower
population growth after release than wild stock, and it adds what Morris
et al. do not test: a pre-release support, enrichment, that reduced the
deficit. Nothing should be cited from it as read until its text is
obtained. It bears on [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) (capacity-building support against
support that does the work) and [QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md).
