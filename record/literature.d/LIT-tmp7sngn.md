# LIT-tmp7sngn: Ecological Niches and Diversity Maintenance

Chesson, P. (2011). Ecological niches and diversity maintenance. In
*Research in Biodiversity – Models and Applications* (pp. 43–62). InTech.
doi:10.5772/24534.

## Key takeaways

- In MacArthur's consumer-resource model the overlap is
  ρ = Σ_l c_jl v_l K_l c_kl / r_l, divided by s_j s_k, with
  s_j = √(Σ_l c_jl² v_l K_l / r_l): a cosine of the consumption vectors
  under weights v_l K_l / r_l. Heights of the consumption curves do not
  affect it (Fig. 2).
- κ_j ρ / κ_i = α_ij / α_jj, and two species coexist when
  ρ < κ₁/κ₂ < 1/ρ. "Stable coexistence" means each species recovers from
  low density, not a stable equilibrium.
- Feedback through predators enters ρ and κ in the same way as feedback
  through resources.

## Standing in the record

Registered as a stand-in for Chesson (1990), LIT-1826, whose author's
reprint could not be fetched. It restates that paper's model and its
overlap measure for the two-species Lotka–Volterra reduction. By the
1990 paper's abstract, as LIT-1826 summarizes it, that paper develops
statistical measures of association between utilization functions,
and how far those go beyond Table 1's ρ cannot be told without it. So the chapter stands in only for the overlap
measure, the fitness measure and the coexistence condition it states.
