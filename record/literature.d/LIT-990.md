# LIT-990: Agency, Goal-Directed Behavior, and Part-Whole Relationships in Biological Systems

Richard Watson (2023), *Biological Theory* 19(1), 22–36.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpo6vec](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpo6vec.md).*

- **Comparative, not absolute.** A system is "agential (with respect to
  its parts)" if its organization makes the parts "do what they 'normally
  do' but more, as if the complex constraints between them … were
  alleviated". No line between agents and non-agents is drawn.
- **Relative to a decomposition.** The whole's norm is borrowed from the
  parts' goals. Agency "can be different or absent if we define the
  agency of the parts differently".
- **The measure.** Compare "the total frustration or energy of the system
  at local equilibria" under the original and the new organization. No
  formula is given beyond this.
- **Goal-directedness is delayed gratification.** The system's dynamics
  must differ from the objective set by the parts' constraints, so that
  it goes uphill "in a specific direction that leads to lower points in
  the long term". A ball with momentum is just above zero.
- **Organization belongs to the whole's history.** In a two-unit XOR
  network, correct behaviour leaves each hidden unit free to compute any
  of 8 functions. The roles settle by mutual symmetry-breaking, and that
  organization fixes how the network generalizes (Boxes 1, 2).
- **It can be learned bottom-up (reported).** Hebbian change in a
  Hopfield-type network, and relationship changes among self-interested
  agents or species, yield equilibria that better satisfy the original
  constraints. No system-level teacher or group selection is needed
  (Watson et al. 2011a, c; Davies et al. 2011; Power et al. 2015).

## Standing in the record

Held for [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md) and read for it. It offers a criterion for when
coordination makes a whole *more agential than its parts*: when the
coordination resolves more of the parts' frustrations than they resolve
alone. It refuses to say when a further agent exists outright. So it
answers a comparative version of [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md) rather than the question as
asked. [NOTE-tmpo6vec](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpo6vec.md) sets out how it bears on [QUESTION-032](../questions.d/QUESTION-032.md):
- it agrees with [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md)'s level-relativity;
- it moves the circularity of [CLAIM-168](../claims.d/CLAIM-168.md) down a level by presupposing the
  parts' goals;
- it gives [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md)'s goal-directedness test a behavioural mark;
- it accepts a minimal agency in a ball with momentum, so meets [CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)
  by biting the bullet;
- its reported timing result (Tissot et al.) is close to [CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md)'s
  collective variable.
