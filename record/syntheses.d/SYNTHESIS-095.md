# SYNTHESIS-095: The dependence-and-domination line is six accounts: what wholes and parts owe and claim; what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe; responsibility for another's conditions, and moral exploitation; restricting capabilities, and who controls the terms of waiting and contracting; remedies foreclosed before review; and answerability and contestability; the parts meet mostly where the later four use the first part's term for domination and point at its claims on control of terms, and the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the dependence-and-domination line, "Wholes, parts and dependence", split from organizational-normativity (ADR-100) at the owner's approval of 2026-11-15 ("yes on the two line split"). It is a line account over the accounts of its parts (ADR-083, ADR-084). Version 1 had two parts. Version 2 (2026-11-17) adds the four filed at the interim synthesis review of that day, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46). "Organization" is TERM-074's sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default, and the bearer of an obligation is kept apart from its ground; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none. Every link below is attributed to the part account or component that states it.*

- SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm is mostly closed: 23 of its 30 outgoing relations

relation it has outward goes into SYNTHESIS-092. Whether that makes two
mostly closed parts is a judgment the counts do not settle. The review
reports it to the owner; a split is a decision about the lines
vocabulary (ADR-082), not this account's.

## Not stated on the record

- How CLAIM-687's rule, that accountability rises with how indispensable
  an arrangement has become, relates to CLAIM-690's, that the magnitude
  of a dependence sets only how much rides on it and control over the
  terms sorts it. Neither cites the other.
- How CLAIM-690 and CLAIM-682 relate (SYNTHESIS-092).
- Whether CLAIM-624's duty of answerability, in organizational-normativity,
  falls on an arrangement's controllers, and so whether CLAIM-673's
  erosion of governing capacity breaches it (SYNTHESIS-093).
- Whether activating another's obligations is control over the terms in
  TERM-124's sense (QUESTION-tmpbzq36): SYNTHESIS-tmphd6ep tested it and
  found the word "terms" a free parameter.
- Whether the remedies part and the answerability part state one thing
  from two sides, a right to contest kept effective against the party
  contested. CLAIM-tmpcpijj and CLAIM-tmpi68s2 complement
  CLAIM-tmpbqunx, and no entry says more.
- Whether CLAIM-tmpmeabu's power to restrict, CLAIM-tmpdic1g's residual
  control and CLAIM-tmpcpijj's authority over the conditions of challenge
  are one kind of power, control over another's terms in TERM-124's
  sense. Each uses or complements TERM-124 or the others pairwise, as
  above; no entry states the three together.
- That the six parts are one inquiry beyond the term for domination most
  of them use and the controllers they name.
- Each part account lists the links its components leave unstated.
