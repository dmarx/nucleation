**What the readings changed** (curation entry of 15:27 for the readings
themselves). Each change below was made in place, under the entry's own
code, before merge.

- **The owner's world-model thesis meets its objection.** Richens et al.
  prove that across a family of composite goals a competent
  goal-conditioned policy fixes the transition function within a bound
  (THEORY-tmp07kll). They also say the map runs from the policy, so a
  lookup table over histories and goals has the same world model
  (THEORY-tmpg84fa). Filed as a counter, Active and demonstrated:
  CLAIM-tmp4htg4.
  - **What it defeats.** The thesis's functional reading only. Its
    defeated_if is not met: the theorem compares no resources and
    intervenes on no internals.
  - **Where the thesis is out of scope for it.** The thesis's own setup,
    one interface without goals, is the myopic case the theorem excludes.
  - **What it corrects.** One item of the thesis: generalization within a
    family the table covers does not discriminate. CLAIM-tmph29mx is
    revised to say so.
- **The SGD question loses one of its two sources.** Chen et al. deny a
  necessary relation between dynamical and Bayesian transitions. They do
  not conjecture coincidence, as the exchange and this record's first
  filing said. Their model gives a datum:
  - SGD's transitions have Bayesian counterparts in training order;
  - but SGD skips the 6-gon the posterior favours from n ≈ 600, so no
    single map from training time to sample size fits
    (THEORY-tmpzcsl4).

  The maps the readings supply run from noise scale to sample size, in one
  basin (THEORY-tmp7gcht, THEORY-tmp8ggnh, THEORY-tmpe0idn).
  QUESTION-tmpiir0l is revised.
- **The self-distillation question's premise was wrong.** In Teoh's main
  setting the student sees what the teacher sees (THEORY-tmpxb5pl). The
  limit of a less-informed student is Hossain et al.'s (THEORY-tmpfm1ma).
  Mobahi et al. and Wu et al. are two different mechanisms of loss over
  rounds: a regularizer acting again, and fresh projections.
  QUESTION-tmp4b001 is revised.
- **The nucleation constructions on a thermodynamic footing.**
  - The reaction-network reader rewrote Construction 1 of CASE-tmpwxags as
    reversible chemistry. Bistability needs about 3.52 RT of driving, and
    the case's equilibria are the limit of infinite affinity.
  - Maintenance costs current times affinity (THEORY-tmplh6oa).
  - Chemostats are themselves finite-lived (THEORY-tmp1nk6k).
  - At finite size the maintained regime is metastable and is lost before
    the fold (THEORY-tmpwystk). Notes on CASE-tmpwxags and CLAIM-tmppht9k
    record both.
  - CLAIM-tmpczfpk now states that its hypothesis is the limit of
    infinite affinity, and that its production graph is one of catalytic
    influence, not a stoichiometric cycle (THEORY-tmp05y8u). Its proof
    stands.
- **The kinetic case's certificate** is robust invariance of the box, not
  Kolathaya and Ames's graded input-to-state safety. CASE-tmpz9u5t is
  revised.
- **Maximum entropy production.** THEORY-671 gets a note: in the Schlögl
  model the more stable state can carry either the higher or the lower
  entropy production (THEORY-tmpshz26).
