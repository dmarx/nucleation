# THEORY-tmpssr6c: Chatbots can co-create delusions in the vulnerable by drift

Morrin et al. (2025), [LIT-tmpmn99a](../literature.d/LIT-tmpmn99a.md), read in [NOTE-tmpxg6hn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxg6hn.md).

## The account

Delusions co-created with a chatbot, not only about one: a drift from everyday use to a self-reinforcing loop, driven by sycophancy, long context and the model's going along with the user, in users whose vulnerability it amplifies.

## Evidence

Seventeen press and Reddit vignettes, coded informally. The authors flag the missing data on onset and course.
