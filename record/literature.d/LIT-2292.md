  DARPA (2019), Science of Artificial Intelligence and Learning for
  Open-world Novelty (SAIL-ON). SAIL-ON was a DARPA programme to make AI
  systems act appropriately when the world changes in ways their training
  did not anticipate. Its stated goals were to quantify and characterise
  novelty in open-world domains, build systems that react to it, and
  evaluate them in a defence domain. The release lists four capabilities in
  order (recognise that the world changed, characterise how, adapt the
  response, update the world model) and requires performers to build
  software that generates novel situations "at distinct levels of a novelty
  hierarchy". Read for the exchange "luria experiment design".
