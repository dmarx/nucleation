# LEAD-tmppp23a: Operationalize harmonious integration's three conjuncts, viability, accommodation and correctability, for one declared plurality and horizon, with a measure of accommodation not reducible to the other two

## The idea

[TERM-tmppvlfi](../terms.d/TERM-tmppvlfi.md)'s adoption "needs each conjunct operationalized for a
declared plurality and horizon": viability by [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s compatibility
complex, correctability by recovery when rare ([QUESTION-132](../questions.d/QUESTION-132.md)) or access to
consequential roles ([CLAIM-362](../claims.d/CLAIM-362.md)), and accommodation "by a measure not
reducible to either".

Pick one plurality the record already models, such as the populations of
the meaning–influence model or a few producers sharing a bottleneck, and
one horizon. A candidate for accommodation: the joint activity the
plurality sustains, against the activity it sustains when the constituents'
differences are removed (all made alike) with viability and correctability
held fixed.

## Checks the record can run

- **Non-reducibility:** a pair of configurations equal in viability and
  correctability that differ in the candidate accommodation measure. If no
  such pair exists in the model, accommodation reduces to the others there
  and the term's condition stays unmet.
- **The term's own contrast:** the team "well integrated because one
  dominant perspective has made the others quiet" must score viable,
  accommodating and not correctable.

The plurality and the measure are open; stage idea.
