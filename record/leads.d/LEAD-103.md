# LEAD-103: State the operational equivalences among renderings a pragmatic experiment assumes and derive the noncontextual inequality they imply

## What it would do

Write the equivalences, derive the inequality, and settle the related
points the curation entry of 2026-11-12 left unclaimed (sheaf against
generalized noncontextuality; linear probes on one representation),
reading Kunjwal and Spekkens (2015).

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-067](../questions.d/QUESTION-067.md): "A statement of the operational equivalences among
  renderings that a pragmatic experiment would assume. With it, a derivation
  of the noncontextual inequality those equivalences imply, separate from
  the sheaf criterion."

## What would count

An inequality an experiment could violate answers [QUESTION-067](../questions.d/QUESTION-067.md) and gives
the factorial study a contextuality test.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out

Done under this lead, by a delegated worker.

- The operational equivalences a pragmatic rendering experiment assumes are
  stated (OE1, equal probe predictabilities in the four sign patterns; OE2,
  one mixture equivalence among renderings), and the generalized- (preparation-)
  noncontextuality inequality |⟨X⟩| + |⟨Z⟩| ≤ 1 they imply is derived, in
  [CLAIM-tmp59ejz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp59ejz.md) with its study
  [CASE-tmp7nwhq](../cases.d/CASE-tmp7nwhq.md). It specializes
  [THEORY-315](../theory.d/THEORY-315.md) to the pragmatic case and verifies it
  numerically: the bound is returned exactly by linear program on all four
  diamond faces, corroborated for non-deterministic responses, violated by a
  qubit orbit up to √2, and the mixture equivalence is shown load-bearing.
- This is the first route of [QUESTION-067](../questions.d/QUESTION-067.md)'s
  "What would answer it". It bears on, and does not answer, that question:
  whether a pragmatic probe family can realize OE1 and OE2 (tomographic
  completeness, exact equivalence) is left open, as is its second route.

What remains, and was not attempted here:

- The other points the curation entry of 2026-11-12 left unclaimed (the sheaf
  criterion against generalized noncontextuality beyond what QUESTION-246 and
  THEORY-315 settle; linear probes on one representation).
- A reading of Kunjwal and Spekkens (2015), the general method for
  noncontextuality inequalities without assuming determinism — not held, not
  read (see THEORY-015); it is unregistered. The derivation here leans instead
  on the held THEORY-315 / NOTE-825, which gives a complete worked proof.
- A noise-robust version (OE1, OE2 are imposed exactly).
