# ADR-tmp7kh0t: An owner's earmark for the retrospective is persisted: kept as a devlog note, and open until a retrospective answers it, saying what came of it; how the note is written is a separate decision

## Context

The owner, 2026-11-21, after an earmark of theirs was filed as a devlog note:
"oh nice, let's persist and standardize that as a new convention with an ADR. I feel like those are separable." "Separable" has at least three readings, which would be filed
differently. Under the dialectic regime's rule for several readings, each is
filed as an alternative decision, and the owner chooses:

- **[ADR-tmpyzxw8](ADR-tmpyzxw8.md)**: separable from [ADR-115](ADR-115.md). One decision on earmarks, apart from the
  one on the assistant's own devlog notes.
- **ADR-tmp7kh0t** and **[ADR-tmpuu4y4](ADR-tmpuu4y4.md)**: separable as *persist* and *standardize*. Two
  decisions: that an earmark is kept until a retrospective answers it, and
  what form the note takes.
- **[ADR-tmps83ka](ADR-tmps83ka.md)**: separable as *convention* and *ADR*. The convention stated as
  steps in a procedure of its own, apart from the decision that records why,
  as [ADR-068](ADR-068.md)'s invariants stand apart from the `read-and-file` skill's
  steps.

When one is chosen the others go Rejected, and the readings stay on the
record. The assistant's lean is in [ADR-tmpyzxw8](ADR-tmpyzxw8.md)'s Readings section.

## Decision

When the owner earmarks something for the retrospective, the assistant
files a devlog note for it at once, regardless of the dialectic regime, and
the next retrospective answers it by name: a practice kept, a decision
prompted, a change called for, or set aside and why. An earmark stays open
until answered. The note's form is [ADR-tmpuu4y4](ADR-tmpuu4y4.md)'s.

## Alternatives considered

[ADR-tmpyzxw8](ADR-tmpyzxw8.md), which decides both halves together, and [ADR-tmps83ka](ADR-tmps83ka.md).

## Consequences

The obligation can be adopted without settling the form, and the form
revised without reopening the obligation.

## First application

As for [ADR-tmpyzxw8](ADR-tmpyzxw8.md): the owner's earmark on pull request 570.
