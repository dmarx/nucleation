# NOTE-tmp07kxd: Eclipse Foundation — Jenkins (Eclipse wiki)

## Contribution

The Eclipse Foundation's infrastructure page on its move from Hudson instances to Jenkins instances, dated by the document itself.

## Key results

- **The concession.** "Since Hudson is not maintained anymore, Jenkins is the replacement for it. They share a common ancestry, but have diverged in the past. Eventually all Hudson Instances Per Project (HIPP) will be completely replaced by Jenkins Instances Per Project (JIPP)."

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. It is a statement by an interested party: what it
says of its own reasons is its stated reason, not its motive.

## Concepts

The continuation of a project apart from its name and steward.

## Bearing on the record

The study's end of the original line for Hudson: the original line's later steward (Eclipse, to which Oracle handed Hudson in 2011) says it is not maintained and names Jenkins its replacement. On or before 2017-05-11, 2,294 days after the Jenkins vote.

## Limitations

One document, by one party, on one day. It records a stated position, and
says nothing of how many people shared it.
