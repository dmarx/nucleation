# THEORY-tmpnm1gx: Dijkema reads Crawford as holding that distraction dissolves individuality and freedom because modern epistemology relocated truth inside the head, leaving attention open to those who monopolize it, and that the remedy is an ecology of attention found in communities of skilled practice, where competence rests on an apprehension of real features of the world

## The account

As Dijkema reports it, with the book's words: "The standard for truth is relocated: it is no longer found out in the world, but inside our own head"; the remedy is "an ecology of attention" in communities of skilled practice, where "competence rests on an apprehension of real features of the world".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmppa0w2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmppa0w2.md), results 1–6.

## What it does not say

That Crawford wants authority as a check on relativism; that is Dijkema's argument (result 7). It is a reading of a reading, attributed to Crawford through the reviewer.
