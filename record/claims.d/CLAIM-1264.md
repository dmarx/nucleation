# CLAIM-1264: Memes can carry portable protocols

## The claim

A16 §10: "A meme can potentially communicate not merely a proposition or
concern, but an implicit governance protocol." Examples: "Safety first."
("Protective concerns receive precedence under specified circumstances"),
"Innocent until proven guilty." ("Punitive authority is conditioned on an
evidential threshold"), "Trust, but verify." ("Delegated authority remains
subject to monitoring"), "Measure twice, cut once." ("Verification precedes
irreversible execution"). "The words do not constitute a complete
contract. They are portable specifications around which contractual
relationships can become organized." "Two organizations might therefore
share a characteristic governance profile despite differences in their
constituent processes."

A18 §12, for inquiry: "Consider the opposite." ("Maintain resources for
competing hypotheses"), "Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence."
("Distinguish a failed observation from an adequate test"), "Extraordinary
claims require extraordinary evidence." ("Allocate evidential burdens
relative to the claim"). "A culture of inquiry is therefore not necessarily
a collection of shared beliefs. It may consist partly in a shared
repertoire of protocols determining which questions are appropriate, how
they acquire resources, what counts as an answer, and how answers can
subsequently govern activity."

Both turns propose to study invariants of such protocols "under
replacement, relabeling or transformation of their constituent processes"
(A16) or "under transformations of language, personnel, implementation or
institutional context" (A18).

## Where the record stands

The exchange's earlier claims about memes as characteristics of
perspectives and as nucleation sites (U1–U5) are filed with their own
codes; the closing pass relates this claim to them. In the record before
this exchange, [CLAIM-068](CLAIM-068.md) holds that pragmatic stances, not only lexicon
and grammar, can be stabilized by transmission; this claim names one kind
of stance so stabilized, an allocation of precedence or burden, and says it
is completed differently in each host.

## What it does not say

It does not say that the expression carries the contract; the contract is
completed, differently, in each organization that takes it up.
