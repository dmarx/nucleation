# THEORY-1493: Banerjee, Duflo and Sharma find, in a randomized trial in West Bengal, that the treatment effects of a one-time Targeting the Ultra Poor package on household consumption, income and health grew for seven years after the asset transfer and persisted to year ten, although the implementer had no contact with households after eighteen months; they attribute the persistence mainly to treated households taking better advantage of wage employment and migration, and hold it consistent with, but not proof of, escape from a poverty trap

## Source

Banerjee, Duflo & Sharma (2021), [LIT-2170](../literature.d/LIT-2170.md); read in [NOTE-1667](../notes.d/NOTE-1667.md) as NBER
Working Paper 28074.

## What was actually shown

- **No contact after 18 months** (p. 5): "Bandhan had no contact with
  beneficiary households starting 18 months after the asset transfer".
- **Growth, then persistence** (Table 1; abstract): consumption +0.31, +0.29,
  +0.72 and +0.58 SD at 18 months, 3, 7 and 10 years; income +0.15, +0.17,
  +0.33, +0.26 SD; physical and mental health higher at years 7 and 10. What
  could have come out otherwise: fade-out once contact ended, or controls
  catching up as they did in the Uganda study the authors cite.
- **Work** (p. 9): productive time higher at every wave, 60 more minutes at
  18 months and about 30 at years 7 and 10.
- **The channel** (pp. 3, 8, 10–13): by year 10 the asset effect has fallen,
  "largely attributable to treated households diversifying out of livestock
  and non-farm micro-enterprises and into labor income"; wage income and
  remittances are higher from year 7, and treated migrants stay longer. A
  decomposition of the income gain, not a separately identified cause.
- **The hedge** (pp. 2, 14): "consistent with the alleviation of a poverty
  trap", "also consistent with there being persistent effects without
  actually getting out of a trap", and "It would take an even longer follow
  up".

## What this does not say

- That the households escaped a poverty trap: the authors decline to
  conclude it.
- That the treated will stay ahead: controls rose from $1.35 to $2.9 a day
  and "may eventually catch up".
- That it generalizes: one site, the best-performing one in 2015.
- That migration or confidence is the mechanism: these are the authors'
  conjectures.
- That the version of record's numbers match the working paper's exactly.
- Anything about aid to governments, or about help the recipient can count
  on indefinitely.
