# SYNTHESIS-tmp3crnp: When a learned distinction becomes a concept is open, and a candidate term holds the record's working answer: a concept is a reusable, consequential pattern of distinctions, distinct from what carries it, what realizes it, what it is about and its corrigibility; the owner's question whether concepts individuate runs against the term's clause that a concept is not a thing; and Proposed claims that rival uses can keep an expression circulating without one concept, and that concepts are individuated by transmission, bear on it, with a proposed study to tell agreement in a concept from agreement in how it is revised

*A part account of the learned-structure line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)); the line's account is built over it and its sibling parts. Three of its six components have pragmatic-transport as their first line.*

## The account

- **The term and its question.** [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) (filed at the owner's decision
  of 2026-10-31; Proposed) names conceptual organization by two marks,
  reuse and counterfactual reach. Its version 2, at the owner's review,
  took correctability out: "An incorrigible concept is still a concept."
  It says a concept is not its carrier, its realizer, its object, or "a
  thing or entity". [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md) (the owner's questions at U4 to U6 of
  "Analyze nucleation concepts"; Open) asks when a learned distinction
  becomes a concept rather than a discriminator or a habit, and is what
  would settle [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) ("this question asks what would settle it", its
  summary). Its note of 2026-10-31 asks a study to include a frozen,
  reused component as a negative control.
- **Rival uses of one expression.** [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) (the owner's U19 of
  "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition", with A41's three
  options; Open) refines [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md). It "runs against [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s clause
  that a concept is "Not a thing or entity"" (its summary), and [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s
  note of 2026-11-07 leaves that clause open as [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md). It holds,
  from [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) and A54, that a difference in conditions of revision
  alone is no evidence of several concepts. Its third option, the
  relations among the competing uses as the most persistent structure, is
  [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md).
- **Rival use as what reproduces an expression.** [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md) (A41 §5, A's
  wording; Proposed, "Weak: it may be [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) restated") uses [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md):
  an expression can be kept in circulation by communities using it
  against one another, so "symbolic stability can persist while
  conceptual stability fails" (its title). It bears on [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md) and
  [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md), and leaves to [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) whether the rival uses are one
  concept or several.
- **Concepts individuated by transmission.** [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) (the exchange's
  thesis, the owner's course with A's wording corrected by the filer;
  Proposed, admitted as an independent hypothesis under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)) applies
  [CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md)'s transmission criterion to concepts, with [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) as its
  layer 1 and [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md) in its layer 5. It bears on [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) and
  [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md), and its corrections note that U19's individuating
  concepts "runs against [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s "Not a thing or entity"".
- **The study.** [CASE-226](../cases.d/CASE-226.md) (A's design, A50 §7, with A55 §4's third arm;
  Proposed, stipulated) would test [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s split of a concept from its
  corrigibility: two communities that agree on a category and differ in
  revising it. It bears on [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md) (its body), [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) places
  A55's arm there, [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s note cites it, and [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) names it "the
  two-community study".

Outside this part, [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md) proposes [QUESTION-275](../questions.d/QUESTION-275.md)'s setting ([SYNTHESIS-tmptiwlg](SYNTHESIS-tmptiwlg.md))
for tracking its criteria over training. No other relation or mention
joins this part to the line.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed.

- **[CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md)'s individuated concepts against [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s "not a thing".**
  Stated as open: [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s note of 2026-11-07, [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md)'s correction
  and [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md)'s last clause ("in which sense of individuation, if
  any") all hold it. A sentence settling it would fix "individual" either
  as an organization, which [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md) says a concept is not, or as
  anything with a transmission lineage, which [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md)'s own defeater
  leaves to [CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md); that is a free parameter until [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) is
  answered.
