# SYNTHESIS-061: Conditional generation is a case for the pragmatic-transport theory and not a realization of it: adding scores composes two conditions exactly when a likelihood ratio does not vary with the noisy state, benchmarks show conjunctions and spatial and attribute bindings failing where single conditions are met, no benchmark the record has read measures the stance or uptake an image conveys, and what explains a given relational failure, and whether the path of a reconstruction is informative, are open

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part on conditional
generation, mostly text-to-image diffusion, as a case for the theory. The
line's account is built over it and over the accounts of the line's other
parts. The mechanical map put these nine entries in one part with the
line's tests and studies and with language models as a test bed. They are
joined by 14 linked pairs among themselves and by two pairs to the other
two, so each has its own account; the links are given below.*

## The account

- **Tools, not a mechanism.** [CLAIM-001](../claims.d/CLAIM-001.md) (the owner at U34; counter,
  interpretive, Proposed): "I think conditional diffusion literature has us
  covered here." Answered by A97 as "right about the tools, unshown for the
  question": diffusion supplies compositional conditioning and transport
  between distributions, and "Nothing in A97 or later addresses changing
  covers". [CLAIM-071](../claims.d/CLAIM-071.md) (A97's own two limits, which the manuscript §7 keeps;
  disclaimed, Active) objects to it: "Our claim is a shared mathematical
  vocabulary of constrained distributions and stochastic transformations,
  not an identification of their physical or computational mechanisms",
  and "a generative failure is not itself a proof of sheaf contextuality".
- **When composing by addition is exact.** [CLAIM-123](../claims.d/CLAIM-123.md) (the manuscript's §7,
  corrected at the owner's direction on 2026-10-19; thesis, demonstrated,
  Active; also formal-apparatus): adding scores composes two conditions
  "exactly when the ratio p(y₁,y₂|x)/(p(y₁|x)p(y₂|x)) does not vary with
  the noisy state x", which conditional independence "is sufficient for but
  does not require". [CLAIM-321](../claims.d/CLAIM-321.md) (split from it under [ADR-043](../decisions.d/ADR-043.md); thesis,
  empirical, Active) is the observed half: "each condition of a prompt can
  be met while their conjunction or relational binding fails", with single
  objects "rendered almost always", spatial relations and attribute binding
  "failing most", and composing by adding scores doing "worst on
  relations". It "does not say what causes the failures". [QUESTION-018](../questions.d/QUESTION-018.md)
  (A110 §IX.4; Open) asks what does: "Which of these explains a given
  relational failure is the open part."
- **What benchmarks measure.** [CLAIM-013](../claims.d/CLAIM-013.md) (the manuscript's §10, Case III,
  narrowed at the seventh status review; thesis, interpretive, Proposed):
  existing benchmarks evaluate "object presence, attributes and whether
  spatial or interactive relations are depicted, not the stance, social
  alignment or uptake an image conveys". [CLAIM-097](../claims.d/CLAIM-097.md) (from the reading of
  T2I-CompBench; granted, empirical, Active) objects to the older form:
  T2I-CompBench's non-spatial category "already probes interactions", but
  it is "an interaction benchmark ... that cannot separate a complicit
  exchange from a reprimand". [CASE-004](../cases.d/CASE-004.md) (A97's prompt conditions;
  stipulated, Active) is the stimulus: two people and an empty wallet, as
  shared complicity, normative authority and an underspecified relation,
  so "object-level evaluation cannot tell a faithful image from one that
  reverses the relation"; "Without condition C there is no measure of which
  relation a generator supplies when none is specified."
- **The path.** [CLAIM-007](../claims.d/CLAIM-007.md) (A97 §3 on Schrödinger bridges, recovered;
  thesis, interpretive, Proposed): "The path itself becomes observable."
  Its note of 2026-10-30 holds it back: its defeat condition "is met as
  written", and what it means is "that the order in which relations are
  fixed along the path is informative, and no result in the record
  measures that for a reconstruction chain".

What stays open is [QUESTION-018](../questions.d/QUESTION-018.md), which of estimator error, model
misspecification, sampling or a failure of the ratio condition explains a
given relational failure; a benchmark of the stance or uptake an image
conveys; and an observation of a reconstruction path.

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **Composition on both sides has the same form.** That [CLAIM-123](../claims.d/CLAIM-123.md)'s
  ratio condition on the generation side and the composition of
  approximate transports under [CLAIM-260](../claims.d/CLAIM-260.md)'s conditions (chains) both make
  composition predictable from parts only under a stated condition, so
  that a failed prediction outside it counts against neither. It is a
  parallel of form across two vocabularies; [CLAIM-071](../claims.d/CLAIM-071.md) says the shared
  vocabulary is "not an identification of their physical or computational
  mechanisms", and the parallel settles no outcome either side leaves open.
- **Generators may not fail on [CASE-004](../cases.d/CASE-004.md)'s contrast.** That the relational
  failures the record documents are spatial and attribute bindings, and
  interactions are rated near ceiling, so nothing on the record shows that
  generators fail to render complicity against reprimand. [CLAIM-097](../claims.d/CLAIM-097.md) states
  it ("an interaction benchmark exists, but one that cannot separate a
  complicit exchange from a reprimand"), with [CLAIM-321](../claims.d/CLAIM-321.md)'s narrowing and
  [CLAIM-013](../claims.d/CLAIM-013.md)'s gap.

## What it does not say

- That generation is translation, or that denoising time is historical or
  telephone-game time ([CLAIM-071](../claims.d/CLAIM-071.md)).
- That a generative failure is evidence of sheaf contextuality, or of the
  ratio condition failing ([CLAIM-321](../claims.d/CLAIM-321.md)).
- That every kind of relation fails ([CLAIM-321](../claims.d/CLAIM-321.md)).
