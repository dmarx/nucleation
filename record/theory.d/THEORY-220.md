# THEORY-220: A far-from-equilibrium system that contributes to its own maintenance has functions relative to itself, with no history or observer needed, and one that switches among self-maintaining processes has indications of success that can be false by its own lights

## Source

Bickhard (2000), [LIT-1029](../literature.d/LIT-1029.md), read in [NOTE-729](../notes.d/NOTE-729.md): the sections
Autonomy (the ladder from minimal to recursive self-maintenance),
Self-Maintenance and Function (serving and having a function, dynamic
presupposition, against etiology) and Recursive Self-Maintenance and
Representation (interactive content, system-detectable error).

## What was actually shown

Nothing is measured. The paper argues from cases and from two premises:
emergence must be causally efficacious, and only a system's current state
is efficacious.

**Function.** A far-from-equilibrium process ceases if it reaches
equilibrium, so it needs exchanges with its environment. Some such
processes contribute to their own continuation. A candle flame keeps
itself above combustion temperature, vaporises wax and drives the
convection that brings oxygen. Those contributions are functional "for
that system". Function is relative to the system served, which is why a
parasite's gut is functional for the parasite and dysfunctional for the
host. Having a function, as distinct from serving one, is being of a type
that the whole's processes presuppose to meet some condition; the paper
sketches this and defers it to Christensen & Bickhard.

**Against etiology.** On a history-based account a molecule-for-molecule
duplicate of a lion has no functions, so function is not a property of
current state and is causally epiphenomenal. History explains why a
kidney exists, not what its having a function is.

**Representation.** A system that maintains its self-maintenance across
changing conditions must switch among processes, and so must differentiate
environments. Differentiations alone have no content. Content belongs to
the indication that a further interaction will succeed after a given
differentiation, and is the implicit set of conditions under which it
would. The indication can be false, and the system can register that from
an internal outcome. Information semantics, Fodor's asymmetric dependence
and Dretske's account are argued to make error, or error the system can
detect, impossible.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say a flame is alive, or an agent.** It gives the flame
  functions and nothing more. The organism line is not drawn.
- **It does not say what ought to persist.** The norm is persisting
  versus ceasing for a given system. "Normative" here is not value, which
  agrees with [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md).
- **It does not define having a function or malfunction rigorously.** The
  typing that carries malfunction is a sketch, deferred to another paper.
- **It does not say anything about designed or artificial systems**
  beyond one closing sentence that their design will have to meet these
  conditions. The application to simulations below is the record's.
- **It does not give an individuality condition.** The system whose
  persistence fixes the functions is taken as given.

## Connections

- **[CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) (modeller-fixed objectives cannot show
  self-constitution).** Supported. On this account a function is relative
  to the system whose far-from-equilibrium persistence it serves, and a
  function that exists only in an ascription or explanation is not a
  natural function at all. A reward the modeller writes is not any
  operator's persistence condition, so it confers no function on anything
  in the simulation. This is a second route to the claim's premise,
  beside [THEORY-071](THEORY-071.md) and [LIT-1017](../literature.d/LIT-1017.md). If this account were rejected the
  premise would still stand on those.
- **[QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md) (an intrinsic standard for malfunction or
  capture).** A partial answer, yes. A self-reproducing authority structure
  serves functions for itself, and whether it is dysfunctional for the
  organization is fixed by the organization's own persistence conditions,
  as the parasite's gut is fixed by the host's. A part the whole's
  processes presuppose to meet a condition, and that fails to, malfunctions.
  The limit: persistence is the only standard. Captured authority that
  keeps the organization alive is not dysfunctional here, so the account
  cannot tell entrenched authority from merited trust when both keep the
  organization going. That is the gap [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) marks.
- **[CLAIM-284](../claims.d/CLAIM-284.md) (self as endogenous governance).** Not supported.
  Recursive self-maintenance is governing among regimes, the second of
  [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)'s layers. Transforming the organization that governs does not
  appear, and selves and authority are not discussed. No relation is
  declared.
- **[THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md) (organisms versus flames).** A disagreement about
  functions, not about the demarcation. One of [THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md)'s sources holds
  that a flame's components have no functions; this account gives the
  flame functions it serves, though no typed parts that have them. Its
  infrastructure tiers, structure stable on a slower time scale that
  directs energy and switches processes, sit near [THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md)'s
  differentiation, but the paper gives no closure condition. [THEORY-072](THEORY-072.md)'s
  title is not contradicted, since this account draws no organism line. No
  `rivals` is declared, because the two accounts answer different
  questions and could both be right about their own.
- **[THEORY-071](THEORY-071.md) (minimal agency) and [LIT-1017](../literature.d/LIT-1017.md) (Di Paolo).** The same
  bacterium, and a near match between self-maintenance and recursive
  self-maintenance here and robustness and adaptivity in Di Paolo. The
  difference is where norms begin. Di Paolo denies a merely conserving
  system any norm it can use; this account gives it functions but puts
  error the system can detect only at the recursive level, which is about
  where Di Paolo puts norms for the system. [THEORY-071](THEORY-071.md)'s individuality and
  asymmetry conditions have no counterpart here.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a contribution measure of the kind the promote_when asks for, not yet passing its test

A16 of the exchange of 2026-10-17, under the owner's question about
normativity from first principles ([QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md)), proposed the model
this entry's promote_when asks for, contribution to self-maintenance
defined from the dynamics alone:

  Δ_p(T) = Pr(τ_O > T | do(p intact)) − Pr(τ_O > T | do(p disabled)),

with τ_O the time at which the specified organization stops being
maintained. A16 called it "a **functional contribution measure**, not yet a
moral value or a proof of normativity", dependent on "how \(\mathcal O\), the
intervention, and organizational failure are specified".

It does not yet meet the promote_when's own test. Any component whose
removal shortens persistence scores positive, so Δ_p as stated cannot tell
functions "from mere causal contributions to persistence that no one counts
as functional". It was not applied to the flame, the protocell or the
designed controller. [THEORY-628](THEORY-628.md) (Di Paolo) is a further objection to the
step A16 takes from self-maintenance to "endogenous conditions of success
and failure": persistence against cessation is an all-or-nothing norm
"available to an observer, not to the system", and a graded norm needs
adaptivity. Δ_p measures the all-or-nothing norm. The status is unchanged.
