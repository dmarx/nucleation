# CLAIM-080: The agent is independently warranted: the account asks how a given agent's practical efficacy is organized, not what makes something an agent

## The claim

"The present paper therefore assumes an independently defensible candidate
agent and asks how its practical efficacy is organized." It "presupposes
rather than derives the relevant agent" (Section 7.4).

## What it does not say

That any particular institution is an agent. If Northstar is not, "the same
relations can still be described as institutional coordination without
attributing a collective will". Whether it is, is [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md).
