**Corrections the lookup made to the exchange.**

- The classic doorway paper's subtitle is "Situation models and
  experienced space", not "mental space".
- Su's study of Shaolin, cited as 2019, is a 2016 article in *The
  International Journal of the History of Sport*, reprinted as a chapter
  in 2019. Its abstract dates the restriction of Shaolin martial arts to
  the whole Mao era, 1949–1978, not to the Cultural Revolution alone.

  forgetting across four experiments" is McFadyen et al., "Doorways do not
  always cause forgetting".
- A37's "2016 investigation" is Pettijohn and Radvansky, and the
  return-to-the-room finding is Radvansky, Krawietz and Tamplin (2011).
