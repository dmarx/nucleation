**What changed and why.** [LIT-373](../../record/literature.d/LIT-373.md) and [LIT-374](../../record/literature.d/LIT-374.md) were first read one at a time, and each was filed `Rejected` largely on
mathematical slips. Those slips included an "if and only if" that should be "if", a pseudo-dimension that should be
fat-shattering, a bouquet built with open covers where partitions were wanted, and a word used in two senses. The owner
objected on two counts. The papers are pieces of one research programme and must be read together, and the slips were
minutiae: what matters is whether the major takeaways are argued well.

One reader then read both papers together. It also read the author's two companion papers, which are filed here:
- [LIT-tmp11m3i](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp11m3i.md), arXiv 2602.07974 v2, the fullest formal statement;
- [LIT-tmp8xsqx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8xsqx.md), arXiv 2603.15412 v1, "Local Urysohn Width".

It read charitably: a technical error counts only if a major takeaway depends on it and its sensible repair does not
save it.

**The programme.** Structure is discovered by costly search, condensed into frozen context tokens, and then reused
cheaply. Discovering structure and using it are separate problems, with separate complexities (width against VC
dimension) and separate mechanisms (scaffold against flow, router against experts). [LIT-373](../../record/literature.d/LIT-373.md) is the cognitive and
neural statement of it. [LIT-tmp11m3i](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp11m3i.md) and [LIT-tmp8xsqx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8xsqx.md) are the formal statements, and [LIT-374](../../record/literature.d/LIT-374.md) is the alignment and
architecture statement.

**Verdicts on the takeaways.**
- **Width is incomparable with VC dimension.** Established in repaired form. [LIT-tmp8xsqx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8xsqx.md)'s bouquet is the consistent
  construction the others lacked. The moral drawn from it, that "scaling cannot repair structure", is undermined: the
  comparison is with a per-cell smooth predictor class, not with what a model can represent. Width is in effect a
  covering number at scale, and [LIT-tmp8xsqx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8xsqx.md)'s own Remark 8.11 concedes that the obstruction is metric, not topological.
- **An error floor below width (the "phase transition").** Established in repaired form, but close to definitional.
- **Width is estimable from data.** The Ω(w log w) discovery cost is established. The spectral estimator is plausible
  but under-argued: it converges to a count relative to a given predictor, not to width.
- **Decouple router training from task loss, and freeze settled contexts.** Plausible but under-argued. It is a
  testable design hypothesis with continual-learning precedent, but the additive bound it is said to follow from holds
  for joint training too.
- **Amortisation: stored search makes inference cheap.** Argued well enough to take seriously, once "Savitch" is read
  as memoisation. The idea is borrowed. The exponential-reach scaling law ([LIT-373](../../record/literature.d/LIT-373.md) Theorem 2) is not argued.
- **Collapse defeats the curse of dimensionality, and equals free-energy minimisation.** Plausible but under-argued,
  because it moves the curse into the cost of learning the collapse. The equivalence with free-energy minimisation is
  asserted.
- **The neural instantiation.** Plausible but under-argued: the predictions do not discriminate it from standard
  consolidation accounts.
- **Alignment is a scaffold property.** Not argued.

**Standings.**
- **[LIT-373](../../record/literature.d/LIT-373.md)** stays `Rejected`, on new grounds. After repair its core restates amortised inference and complementary
  learning systems, its predictions do not discriminate, and its one original quantitative claim is unargued.
- **[LIT-374](../../record/literature.d/LIT-374.md)** goes from `Rejected` to `Proposed`. It is the only place the decoupling and safety material appears, so it
  is not superseded, though its learning theory is better read in [LIT-tmp11m3i](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp11m3i.md).
- **[LIT-tmp11m3i](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp11m3i.md)** and **[LIT-tmp8xsqx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp8xsqx.md)** are `Proposed`.
- [NOTE-321](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-321.md) and [NOTE-322](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-322.md) carry new summaries and a superseding "Joint re-reading" section. Their first readings stay in
  place below, as the record of what was said.

What would settle the programme is one set of synthetic benchmarks of known width:
- a sweep of the number of contexts, comparing routed learners with capacity-matched monolithic models;
- recovery of width from a cold start;
- decoupled against joint router training, compared on forgetting and on boundary error.

A THEORY is not yet warranted. The proved results are a covering number and a coupon-collector bound, and the
explanatory claim (that forgetting is router drift, and that decoupling prevents it) has no evidence in the record. A
draft is held for when an empirical source is filed.

**Versions.** Both companion identifiers now serve different papers from the ones read.
- arXiv 2602.07974 v1 is "Beyond Optimization", so the entry is dated by v2.
- arXiv 2603.15412 v2 (2026-09-14) is "The Metric Slingshot", a grid-cell paper. The entry is v1, the paper the owner
  asked for, with a `source-ok` directive. Its NOTE judges v2 separately, and v2 would be `Rejected`: its grid-ratio
  result depends on a free capacity parameter, and its anaesthesia "confirmation" is post hoc.

**Lesson.** Read a programme's papers together, and judge a hypothesis paper by whether its takeaways are argued, not by
whether every statement survives a referee's pen. Both first readings were right about the slips and wrong about what
they implied.
