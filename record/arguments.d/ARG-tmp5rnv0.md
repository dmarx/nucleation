# ARG-tmp5rnv0: The charge sectors of topological solitons carry the topological route to persistence as the protection of a kind and its net count, not as the identity of a particular soliton: a translated kink and a winding-two vortex sector keep the charge while the token changes

## The inference

[THEORY-1507](../theory.d/THEORY-1507.md), from Tong ([NOTE-1694](../notes.d/NOTE-1694.md)), shows the mechanism. A
finite-energy configuration must approach a vacuum at infinity. The map
from the boundary to the vacua falls into homotopy classes, so
configurations split into sectors labelled by a charge, and continuous
evolution that keeps the boundary data cannot change the sector. Applied
to [CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md), this gives the topological route: a structure that
continuous change cannot remove. It gives that route for the sector.

The step the row makes beyond that is from "the charge is conserved" to
"the charge carries this structure's identity". It holds for the kind and
fails for the token, as [CASE-tmp5mact](../cases.d/CASE-tmp5mact.md) shows:

- two kinks of equal charge and energy sit anywhere;
- a perturbed kink can return to the kink family with its centre moved;
- a winding-two vortex sector holds one core or two, and above critical
  coupling the symmetric single core is unstable (Czubak & Jerrard,
  [NOTE-tmpplgz6](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpplgz6.md)).

## Critical questions

- **Do the premises hold?** For [THEORY-1507](../theory.d/THEORY-1507.md), on Tong's stated conditions.
  It is Proposed and holds [CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md) back for that reason already. For the
  case, the kink statements are exact and were rechecked on filing. The
  vortex instability is read. That two unit vortices can sit apart or
  together at critical coupling is A's statement, not read.
- **Does the formal result apply to what the conclusion is about?** To
  persistence, yes: a nonzero net charge guarantees a defect somewhere.
  To "what carries its identity", only if identity means kind. [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s
  dynamical sense asks for "robust identity and boundaries" of a
  particular pattern. The sector does not supply that, so the application
  carries the column only in the weaker sense.
- **Is anything else needed?** To track a particular topological soliton
  the record needs a further, declared criterion: a centre followed modulo
  translation, a pinning term, a core count, or a history. A's warning
  applies: the criterion must be declared before the run, "Otherwise one
  can always rescue a disappearing structure by widening the identity
  criterion after the fact."

## What follows for [CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md)

The topological route stands. Its identity column is read as "the charge,
for the kind and net count; the dynamics, for the token". This is
recorded in [CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md)'s note of 2026-11-15. No status changes.
