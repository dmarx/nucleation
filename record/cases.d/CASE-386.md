# CASE-386: An employee with an idea who must justify it to a manager before time goes to it: an opportunity open for ten days, formulated in one, justified in two, approved in two and implemented in six, is lost under that workflow and kept by preauthorized discretion that lets justification run beside a first experiment

## The case

The owner's analogy, at U28: "reminds me of an underling having an idea but
needing to justify it to their manager to allow them to allocate time and
energy to the idea".

A54 §1 sets out the stages, conceive, investigate, justify, authorize and
execute, and an opportunity open for ten days:

| Stage | Time |
|---|---:|
| Initial formulation | 1 day |
| Preparing justification | 2 days |
| Waiting for managerial approval | 2 days |
| Implementing the idea | 6 days |
| **Total** | **11 days** |

"The idea cannot be implemented before the deadline under this workflow.
But if the employee has preauthorized discretion to explore and act within
an appropriate resource limit, some of the justification work can occur
after a small initial experiment, or alongside development." A54's reading:
"**changing the allocation of authority can change the set of achievable
outcomes even when productive capabilities remain fixed.**" And its
counterweight: "Managerial review may prevent costly, unsafe, or poorly
justified projects."

## What it shows

- **The depth trade-off, with authority as a stage.** Justifying the idea
  is inquiry spent before execution can start, as in [CLAIM-1008](../claims.d/CLAIM-1008.md), and
  waiting for approval is time no one on the project controls. The total,
  1 + 2 + 2 + 6 = 11 days, misses a 10-day window by one.
- **Fixed capability, changed outcome.** The record holds the pieces:
  [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s correctability counts authorizing among what must finish
  before the deadline, and [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md) decomposes a capability into
  competence, exercise, authority and governance, which vary independently.
  [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md) separates access to a signal from its authority over action.
- **The trap A54 §6 draws from it**, no evidence, so no authorization, so no
  exploration, so no evidence, is [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md)'s form (indicators of competence
  that only being sought produces) and, where competence decays with
  disuse, [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)'s.

## Variants

- **Parallel justification.** Preauthorized discretion moves the
  justification off the critical path, as in [CLAIM-1114](../claims.d/CLAIM-1114.md): if the
  justification and the wait for approval run beside the six days of
  implementation, the route takes 1 + 6 = 7 days, inside the window.
- **A maintained nucleus.** If the idea had been kept alive in a maintained
  perspective ([CLAIM-1148](../claims.d/CLAIM-1148.md)), formulation and part of the justification
  would be done before the window opened.
