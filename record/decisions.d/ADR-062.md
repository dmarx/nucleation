# ADR-062: social-identity and altruism join the topics

## Context

On 2026-10-28 the owner asked how the record stood on self-categorization
theory. It held nothing, and the readings that followed had nowhere to go
but `social-science`:

- Turner et al. 1994 (LIT-tmpzgybs) and Hornsey 2008 (LIT-tmpi8o30);
- Skorich (LIT-tmpmzum3), Reimer et al. (LIT-tmpxgtr8) and León
  (LIT-tmpe36at), read in place of the 1987 book (LIT-tmper0q2);
- the fandom works registered on 2026-10-16: Biel (LIT-1133), Berendt and
  Uhrich (LIT-1147), Giulianotti (LIT-1150), and the four not yet read.

`social-science` is the vocabulary's widest word ("economics, psychology,
anthropology and sociology"). `social-ontology` asks what a group is.
Neither picks out the psychology of membership: when a person sees
themselves as one of us, and what follows. By 2026-10-29 that cluster held
nine readings, six Proposed THEORYs and some twenty works filed unread.

The same day the owner gave the context of the original question. It came
from their position that there is no selflessness: people with collectivist
attitudes have a larger self-identity, and collectivism is a self-interest
that respects the good of a group identity. The works that bear on it are:

- the empathy–altruism debate (Cialdini et al. 1997 against Batson et al.
  1997);
- self-expansion and identity fusion;
- self-construal;
- team reasoning and identity in utility;
- Sober and Wilson on psychological egoism;
- Johnston on the self's extent.

The record already held part of this under other words: Hamilton's kin
selection (LIT-497, LIT-506, THEORY-655) under `natural-sciences`, Boehm
(LIT-514) under `moral-psychology`, and Parfit (LIT-469) under
`personhood`. Nothing joined them.

The owner said: "don't hesitate to expand the tagging vocabulary if you
think it's merited."

## Decision

Add two topics:

- **`social-identity` (Social identity).** The psychology of group
  membership:
  - social identity and self-categorization theory;
  - categorization and salience;
  - identification and its measurement;
  - in-group bias and intergroup relations;
  - rivalry and recategorization;
  - group-based emotion, crowds and fandom.

  What a group *is* stays `social-ontology`.
- **`altruism` (Altruism and self-interest).** Whether and how action can
  aim at another's good rather than one's own:
  - psychological egoism and the empathy–altruism debate;
  - self–other merging, self-expansion and identity fusion;
  - team reasoning and identity in utility;
  - the evolution of unselfish behaviour;
  - the extent of the self as it bears on what counts as self-interest.

How they are applied:

- **First** on the works and THEORYs filed in the contribution that adds
  them, where they are the subject:
  - social-identity: LIT-tmpzgybs, LIT-tmpi8o30, LIT-tmper0q2,
    LIT-tmpmzum3, LIT-tmpxgtr8; THEORY-tmpo85ke, THEORY-tmpk1fow,
    THEORY-tmpubn0r; and most of the works filed unread on 2026-10-29;
  - altruism: Cialdini et al., Batson et al., Aron et al., Sober and
    Wilson, Sugden, Akerlof and Kranton; the owner's thesis
    (CLAIM-tmpjylq8) and its question and counters.
- **Beside the existing tags** on works and THEORYs already held whose
  subject it is. Nothing is removed and primaries are unchanged, as in
  ADR-035:
  - social-identity: LIT-tmpe36at, THEORY-tmpdftev, THEORY-tmpsm03n,
    THEORY-tmp800bi; the fandom LITs 1125, 1131, 1133, 1138, 1140, 1147
    and 1150; Pettigrew (LIT-1408); von Scheve and Ismer (LIT-696), for
    group-based emotion;
  - altruism: Hamilton (LIT-497, LIT-506, THEORY-655), Boehm (LIT-514)
    and Parfit (LIT-469).
- **Not added** where the subject is cooperation or fairness without the
  question of whose good is aimed at: Peters on the cooperation puzzle
  (LIT-035), and the mutualistic account of fairness (LIT-508).

## Alternatives

- **Leave both under existing words.** `social-science` would hold the
  social-identity cluster beside economics and sociology, where nobody
  could find it. The altruism works would scatter across `moral-psychology`,
  `motivation`, `natural-sciences` and `personhood`, with no single place
  for the owner's question.
- **Fold social-identity into `social-ontology`.** That word's blurb is
  philosophy's question: what groups and institutions are. The readings
  are psychology's: when and why people act as members. They cite each
  other (Salice and Miyazono; León), and the two words can sit side by
  side.
- **Name the second word `egoism`, or `selflessness`.** Either would
  prejudge the owner's question. `altruism` names the phenomenon in
  dispute, and the label "Altruism and self-interest" names both sides.
- **One word, `group-psychology`, for both.** The altruism question
  arises equally for a single helper and a single beneficiary (Batson's
  experiments). It is not a group question.

## Consequences

- The vocabulary gains two words that the owner can rename. A rename is
  a decision that supersedes this one.
- Seven merged works and one THEORY take a version bump for the added
  tag. The fandom LITs were already bumped in this contribution and
  record the tag in that entry.
- Future readings in either area start with a word. The owner's thesis
  has a topic page that gathers its evidence and its objections.
