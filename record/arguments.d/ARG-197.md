# ARG-197: Toner–Tu flocking → power law is not criticality

## The inference

[CLAIM-841](../claims.d/CLAIM-841.md)'s row holds that an ordered flock is scale free with nothing
tuned. [THEORY-2199](../theory.d/THEORY-2199.md), read as a source, would carry it.

## Critical questions (source)

**Does the work say this, read in context?** The substance, yes: the
density fluctuations "become infinitely bigger than those in a fluid or
an ideal gas" (p. 7), Cρ(q) diverging as q → 0, unlike an equilibrium
system's, "which goes to a finite constant (the compressibility)" (p. 8);
and the scaling is the ordered phase's: "the low temperature fixed point
we have studied here", not "the fixed point that controls the
order-disorder transition" (p. 64). The form, no: ΔN ∝ ⟨N⟩^φ, an exponent
φ and the words "giant number fluctuations" are not in the paper
(searched). The two are related, since a structure factor diverging at
small q makes the count in a large region fluctuate faster than √⟨N⟩,
but the exponent φ would have to be computed from it, and the record has
not read the paper that states it.

**Is it said within the scope the claim needs?** Yes, and more sharply:
the paper separates ordered-phase scaling from the transition's.

**Is the source in a position to know?** It is the theory's own statement.
Its equations are posited from symmetry, its d = 2 exponents rest on a
"highly probable" flow ([THEORY-1095](../theory.d/THEORY-1095.md)'s question), and it reports "good
agreement" with simulations it does not show (p. 70); the disagreement A
reported is later work, unread and unnamed.

## Verdict

[THEORY-2199](../theory.d/THEORY-2199.md) joins [CLAIM-841](../claims.d/CLAIM-841.md)'s `grounds` for the row, which now reads
"a density structure factor diverging as q → 0 through the ordered
phase"; the ΔN form is dropped from the row as unread.
