# THEORY-tmpfnn68: The High Court found, on the balance of probabilities and incident by incident, that 12 of the 14 assaults pleaded against Mr Depp were proved, all but Incidents 6 and 11, and held The Sun's words substantially true in the meaning that he had committed physical violence against Ms Heard, causing significant injury and on occasion leading her to fear for her life

Depp II v News Group Newspapers Ltd & Anor [2020] EWHC 2911 (QB), Nicol J,
[LIT-tmpqju5l](../literature.d/LIT-tmpqju5l.md).

## The findings

The court asked of each pleaded incident whether it was "more likely than
not" (para 41), requiring stronger evidence for more serious allegations
(para 42), and then stood back to view the evidence as a whole (para 107).
It found "the great majority of alleged assaults ... have been proved to
the civil standard", the exceptions being "Incidents 6, 11 and the
additional confidential allegation regarding Hicksville" (para 575). It
rejected the Claimant's "hoax" case (para 578), held the words
"substantially true in the meanings I have held them to bear" (para 583)
and dismissed the claim (para 584).

## What it does not show

- **Anything beyond this action.** Ms Heard was not a party (para 576); the
  findings bind within these pleadings.
- **Who held power in the relationship.** The meaning tried was about acts
  and injury, not control of finances, isolation or terms.
- **Later proceedings.** It says nothing of any appeal or of the 2022 US
  trial.
