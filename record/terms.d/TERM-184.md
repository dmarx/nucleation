# TERM-184: shortcut (in a learning system)

## Definition

A solution that is right on the training family for the wrong reason: it
reads a correlate of the truthmaker ([CASE-416](../cases.d/CASE-416.md)'s watermark) rather than the
truthmaker or its effects. Its training objective still rewards the truth
off the family. So by that objective's own lights it fails there. Its job is
to name the failure of a solution, as against the failure of an objective.

## Distinctions

From a proxy objective ([TERM-108](TERM-108.md)), by [DISTINCTION-002](../distinctions.d/DISTINCTION-002.md).
