# CLAIM-1054: Repeated questioning from a routine family can set a query-induced capability trap: of two ecosystems equivalent under the routine queries, the one that optimized away capacities only an unasked query needs is rewarded, its success confirms the questioner's belief that the routine family suffices, and the two strategies co-adapt to exclude the distinction that would reveal the lost capacity

## The claim

A50 §7: systems A and B have R_A(q; H, B) = R_B(q; H, B) for every routine
query q ∈ Q₀, but "A maintains alternative suppliers, transition reserves,
and revision mechanisms, whereas B has eliminated them". On a new query q⋆,
reorganizing after a major supplier failure within a deadline,
R_A(q⋆) > R_B(q⋆) "is possible". That much is [TERM-069](../terms.d/TERM-069.md): equivalence is
relative to a declared probe family, and [CASE-412](../cases.d/CASE-412.md) constructs it.

The addition: "Suppose the regulator repeatedly samples only Q₀. Then
industry B may be rewarded for optimizing away capacities relevant only to
q⋆. Its success under repeated questioning reinforces the regulator's
belief that its tests are adequate. The target's response strategy and the
questioner's query strategy co-adapt, progressively excluding the very
distinction that would reveal declining reorganizational capacity. This is
a query-induced capability trap."

The safeguard "is not necessarily randomizing over every imaginable
question"; a regulator "could maintain independently generated stress
tests, periodically revise its query family, and evaluate whether its
ordinary tests remain sufficient".

## The gap

A50 asserts the co-adaptation without a model. Its §4 dynamics, ρ_{t+1} =
G(ρ_t, a_t, S_{t+1}), leave G unspecified, so nothing yet shows that the
query family narrows rather than merely stays fixed. Under a fixed family
the trap is [TERM-164](../terms.d/TERM-164.md)'s destructive lock-in and [CLAIM-577](CLAIM-577.md)'s
utilization-driven loss; the claim's own content is the narrowing.
[LEAD-218](../leads.d/LEAD-218.md) is the model that would show it or not.
