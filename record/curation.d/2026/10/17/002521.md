After the Granger-causality filing (the entry of 2026-10-16 23:59), the
owner asked how the record stood on transfer entropy, and then for the gaps
named to be filled. The record used the measure in a dozen documents and
held one source for it, Barnett and Bossomaier ([LIT-048](../../record/literature.d/LIT-048.md)). Five gaps were
named: Schreiber's paper, which defines it, was not held. There was no
TERM, and the documents' definitions differed. Whether it measures flow at
all had no source. Its estimators were cited but not held. The neighbouring
measures, directed information, interventional flow and local transfer
entropy, were absent.

## What entered

The assistant registered the eight works and read seven in parallel,
four readers taking two each. Each reader wrote only its own LITs, NOTEs
and THEORYs, and the assistant wrote the TERM, the claim and every edit to
an existing document.

- **Schreiber (2000)**, [LIT-1177](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1177.md), arXiv, read in [NOTE-940](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-940.md), filed as
  [THEORY-701](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-701.md). The Letter's tent-map coefficient is twice what its Eq. 4
  gives at lowest order. The reader found it and the assistant rederived
  it; α is fitted, so only the fit is rescaled.
- **Kaiser and Schreiber (2002)**, [LIT-1167](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1167.md), `Unavailable`: ScienceDirect
  only. Read through Barnett, Barrett and Seth.
- **Kraskov, Stögbauer and Grassberger (2004)**, [LIT-1163](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1163.md), arXiv, read in
  [NOTE-938](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-938.md), §§III–IV from prose and captions; filed as [THEORY-709](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-709.md). The
  estimator was checked against −½log(1 − r²) on correlated Gaussians. The
  anthology uses it as a tool in its information-bottleneck readings but
  does not hold it, and it has no topic for an estimator of mutual
  information.
- **Lizier, Prokopenko and Zomaya (2008)**, [LIT-1175](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1175.md), arXiv, read in
  [NOTE-943](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-943.md), filed as [THEORY-702](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-702.md). `published:` is the journal's 15 February
  2008, earlier than the arXiv posting.
- **Williams and Beer (2011)**, [LIT-1176](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1176.md), an arXiv preprint, read in
  [NOTE-942](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-942.md), filed as [THEORY-712](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-712.md). One corner of its three-process
  example is labelled with c and d transposed.
- **James, Barnett and Crutchfield (2016)**, [LIT-1165](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1165.md), arXiv, read in
  [NOTE-945](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-945.md), filed as [THEORY-707](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-707.md). Both its examples were recomputed
  exactly.
- **Massey (1990)**, [LIT-1166](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1166.md), from his ETH institute's archive, read in
  [NOTE-939](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-939.md), filed as [THEORY-711](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-711.md). The reader showed directed
  information is full-history transfer entropy plus instantaneous terms,
  and checked it numerically.
- **Ay and Polani (2008)**, [LIT-1174](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1174.md), the 2006 MPI preprint in the
  Hertfordshire repository, read in [NOTE-946](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-946.md), filed as [THEORY-710](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-710.md).
  `published:` is the preprint's date: Crossref gives only a month for
  print and a later online date. Examples 3–5 reproduce. Example 6 holds
  only at k = 1 or for an intervention on the last step alone.

## The record's own argument

- [TERM-057](../../record/terms.d/TERM-057.md) fixes the word with k, l and the conditioning set explicit.
  [NOTE-053](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-053.md) and [NOTE-788](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-788.md) turned out not to disagree. Both choose within
  Schreiber's free k and l. Only [NOTE-788](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-788.md)'s lag-τ form for τ > 1 falls
  outside his definition.
- [CLAIM-335](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-335.md) answers the flow question. Transfer entropy is unique plus
  synergistic information, so it is directed predictive improvement, not
  flow. This is a second failure, beside [CLAIM-333](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-333.md)'s prediction against
  intervention: in the first worked example an intervention does move the
  target, and what fails is localizing the bit.

## What remains

- Williams and Beer's partial information decomposition (arXiv 1004.2515),
  the source of I_min, is not held, and neither are the critiques of I_min.
  The claim needs only the two-term split, but every number past it rests
  on that measure.
- [NOTE-320](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-320.md) says "the Kraskov k-NN estimator … estimates H(T)". An entropy
  estimate from that paper is the Kozachenko–Leonenko estimator it
  restates, not its mutual-information estimators. Which one Saxe et al.'s
  code uses was not checked, so the note is left alone.

## Boundary

None of the eight is held in the anthology. A grep of its `record/`
(d1573d2) for the authors and identifiers found only Kraskov's estimator
used as a tool. None carries an instruction for machine-learning practice.
