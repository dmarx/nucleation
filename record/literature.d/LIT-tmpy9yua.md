# LIT-tmpy9yua: State of Indiana v. Richard Sackler et al., Complaint

State of Indiana (2019), Marion Superior Court, cause 49D13-1905-PL-020498 — https://content.govdelivery.com/attachments/INAG/2019/05/21/file_attachments/1215129/Sackler%20Complaint%20-%20REDACTED%20VERSION.PDF

## Key takeaways

- **A harm signal reported as revenue.** Staff gave the board "a list of
  the specific problem prescribers by name, along with the exact number of
  prescriptions and dollars of revenue each provided to Purdue" (Region
  Zero, 2010), and told it "that if Region Zero doctors stopped prescribing
  opioids, Purdue would lose almost 10% of its sales" (2011).
- **After the agreement's term.** Key initiatives reported for Q3 2013
  included promoting OxyContin "to elderly patients on Medicare" and "for
  patients who had never taken opioids before".
- **The paper trail, not the conduct.** After a 2013 subpoena, "Purdue
  discontinued the detailed Quarterly Reports that had created a paper
  trail of targets for sales visits", and for 2014 limited board reports
  "to numbers and graphs, and relay other information orally".

## Standing in the record

A primary document, not an account, and a party's allegations. No THEORY
is filed from it.
