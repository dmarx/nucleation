# CASE-tmp8ayjc: Solidarity, condemnation and humour judged in pairs: the probabilistic version

## The case

A24: "Imagine assessing Henley's poem along three dimensions: S: solidarity
with the audience; M: moral condemnation of the behavior; H: humorous treatment
of the subject. Different experimental contexts might jointly assess {S,M},
{M,H}, and {H,S}." The question is whether the pairwise laws are marginals of
a joint law on (S, M, H). A24 warned that "human judgments rarely satisfy the
no-disturbance assumptions automatically".

## What it can show

The sheaf-theoretic question put to pragmatic judgements ([CLAIM-tmpaviuz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpaviuz.md), [LIT-016](../literature.d/LIT-016.md)). The
manuscript §3 keeps the three observables and the cover but makes the example
a logical parity obstruction ([CASE-tmpzeh33](CASE-tmpzeh33.md)), with no note of the change. Where the
change was made is not in owner turns U1–U13.
