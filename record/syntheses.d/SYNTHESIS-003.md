# SYNTHESIS-003: When an organized individual persists through changes of state and mode, in the dynamical and historical senses of persistence, what persists is the particular organization that keeps its repertoire realizable, not the repertoire it keeps, nor any state, mode or boundary; that organization may lie inside the individual or outside it

*Moved from [CLAIM-611](../claims.d/CLAIM-611.md), where its development up to version 2 is recorded. Admitted as an independent hypothesis ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)).*

## The claim

A29 §11: "**an individual may persist by maintaining the organization that
makes its changing behavioral repertoire realizable**". A29's division of
labour: "**Synergetics explains how collective constraints become
dynamically effective. Coordination dynamics explains the structured
repertoire of modes and transitions those constraints support. Sheaf theory
explains how locally possible behaviors compose into histories. Viability,
stochastic dynamics, and control theory determine which histories can
continue and at what cost. Productive and transformative organization
determines how a system preserves or reconstructs those possibilities.**"

The architecture, in layers:

1. **Present coordination.** Constraints become effective where some
   degrees of freedom settle fast relative to others: the owner's [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md)
   (Proposed, U12 of 2026-10-27, returned to at U6 here), with [CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md)
   and [CLAIM-481](../claims.d/CLAIM-481.md) (Active, demonstrated). A14 §11's limit is kept:
   time-scale separation is "neither a universal prerequisite nor a
   sufficient criterion for their individuality or autonomy".
2. **The repertoire.** Which modes, histories and transitions are
   realizable. The structured family of modes is coordination dynamics
   ([THEORY-1227](../theory.d/THEORY-1227.md), Active). That a constrained system is characterized by a
   repertoire of realizable processes and not by its states is [CLAIM-576](../claims.d/CLAIM-576.md)
   (Proposed, A's, 2026-11-05), which the exchange never cites. Feasible,
   reachable and reliably reached part ([CLAIM-567](../claims.d/CLAIM-567.md), Active, demonstrated),
   and so do stability and viability ([CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md), Active). Gluing and
   continuation are different properties of histories ([CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md),
   Proposed, A's, on the owner's U11).
3. **Maintenance.** What keeps the repertoire realizable. Assembly,
   persistence and regeneration part ([CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md), Active). Recovery under
   interventions on state cannot show where maintenance lies ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md),
   Active, demonstrated).
4. **Transformation.** What changes the repertoire. The operative regime,
   the organization among regimes and the capacity to transform it fail
   separately ([CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md), Active).

The integrating claim is A's. The locus of an individual's persistence
through change of mode is layer 3 acting on layer 2. It is not a state, a
mode or a boundary. The senses of persistence are [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md)'s (Active), and
the claim is about the dynamical and historical ones. The senses of
individuation are [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s (Active), and the claim is not about the
constitutive one.

## Corrections to A's version

- **Correction (attribution).** A22 §10: "That connects directly to the
  user's original intuition about maintaining a structured space of
  possibilities". The sentence is A's own, from A20 §10: "It is
  **maintaining a structured space of possible states and the means of
  moving among them**." The owner's U10 quoted it and added "has me
  thinking about metastability, ergodicity, stationarity, absorbing
  states...". The owner's contributions are U4, U6, U10's four concepts
  and U11's conjecture. A29 §11 credits none of them.
- **Correction (coverage): the owner's laboratory.** No later synthesis
  mentions an Arnold tongue, entrainment or coercion. The owner's U4 is
  [CLAIM-616](../claims.d/CLAIM-616.md) (Proposed), which this claim complements: forcing and
  suppression as a minimal model of autonomy against coercion, and
  entrainment as an individuation transition. The locking region inside
  the compatibility region is the owner's conjecture, [CLAIM-618](../claims.d/CLAIM-618.md)
  (Proposed), and the forced oscillator is [CASE-225](../cases.d/CASE-225.md). They are where
  layers 1 to 3 are separated in a system small enough to compute.
- **Correction (fidelity): statistical persistence.** A22 §11: "stationary
  and quasi-stationary measures characterize statistical persistence".
  Statistics conditioned on survival can be stationary while absorption is
  certain ([CLAIM-612](../claims.d/CLAIM-612.md), Proposed, demonstrated in [CASE-231](../cases.d/CASE-231.md)). They
  are no evidence that the repertoire persists.
- **Correction (fidelity): maintenance may be external.** A22 §11 has
  identity "realized through the historically continuous production and
  preservation of relationships", and A29 §11 has the individual persist
  "by maintaining" the organization. Both read as self-maintenance. A32
  §12 corrected it ("organizational persistence and organizational
  autonomy must remain separate"), as [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) and [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) require. The
  title says "inside the individual or outside it".
- **Correction (standing).** A29 says each tradition "explains" its layer.
  Layers 2 and 3 rest on Proposed claims ([CLAIM-576](../claims.d/CLAIM-576.md), [CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md),
  [CLAIM-612](../claims.d/CLAIM-612.md)) and layer 1 on the owner's Proposed [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md). Whether a
  failure of gluing diagnoses an omitted capacity is [QUESTION-437](../questions.d/QUESTION-437.md),
  Open ([CLAIM-623](../claims.d/CLAIM-623.md), [CASE-224](../cases.d/CASE-224.md)).

## What it does not say

- **That the repertoire constitutes the individual.** A29 §11: "this
  remains a candidate criterion of *organizational* identity". [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)
  and [CLAIM-150](../claims.d/CLAIM-150.md)'s underdetermination stand.
- **That any one combination of layers defines an individual.** A29: "The
  proposed theory should account for all these cases without declaring one
  particular combination the universal definition of an individual."
- **That the four traditions form one theory.** The division of labour is
  a map of which formalism treats which layer. That they compose is
  [QUESTION-379](../questions.d/QUESTION-379.md) (Open).

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test, the integration must be able to fail while every component holds. As first titled, "can be" made it existential, and read as a role ("whatever keeps the repertoire realizable") no case could defeat it. Read as a particular, it can fail: replace the system's internal production by [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)'s ideal outside controller, supplying the same production law, so that the same repertoire stays realizable and every trajectory is unchanged, and let every criterion count it one individual through the swap. [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) and [CLAIM-576](../claims.d/CLAIM-576.md) hold, and what persisted is the repertoire, not its maintainer. The claim is admitted in the particular reading, and the title now states it.
