# LEAD-tmplrmhl: Tell the structural and the regime-relative answers apart on an industrial ecosystem that adopts a shared standard: compute the pseudo-self integration diagnostic at two horizons, before and after adoption, and look for a threshold at a fixed horizon

## The design, at stage idea

Take a model, or a documented case, of an industrial ecosystem in which a
shared standard is adopted part way (a technical or quality standard members
must read, condition on and keep up). Under a declared probe family, compute
[TERM-tmpxwa7g](../terms.d/TERM-tmpxwa7g.md)'s integration diagnostic at a short and a long horizon, before
and after adoption.

- [CLAIM-tmpcljke](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcljke.md) predicts a threshold: at a fixed horizon the diagnostic
  moves from coupled to integrated when the standard is regenerated as the
  sector's own, and back when it lapses.
- [CLAIM-tmpptr3w](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpptr3w.md) predicts no threshold at a fixed horizon, only a smooth
  change with the horizon.

The diagnostic itself is only necessary for integration ([TERM-tmpxwa7g](../terms.d/TERM-tmpxwa7g.md)'s
limits), so a pass is weaker evidence than a fail. Stage idea until the
diagnostic and the model are fixed.
