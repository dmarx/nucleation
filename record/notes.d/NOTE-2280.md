# NOTE-2280: Yang, Feature Lottery? A Bifurcation Theory of Concept Emergence, read for where a probe's collapsed state loses stability and what the crossing says of training

## Contribution

The paper makes Rose, Gurewitz and Fox's static critical temperature for
soft clustering ([LIT-3048](../literature.d/LIT-3048.md)) dynamic. A K-prototype isotropic Gaussian
mixture with one learned precision β is fitted to an encoder's output; its
collapsed state S0 (all means at the data mean) becomes a saddle when
β crosses βc(t) = 1/λmax(Cov z(t)), and βc moves as the encoder learns.
It separates that crossing from the macroscopic split, uses log(β/βc) as a
label-free training coordinate, and draws a per-atom prediction ("feature
lottery") from the degeneracy of the unstable subspace. The reading is
[THEORY-2193](../theory.d/THEORY-2193.md).

## Key results

- **Critical precision** (§3.2, Eq. 4; App. A.3). The anti-symmetric
  eigenvalues are λ⊥ᵢ(β) = (β/K)(1 − βσᵢ²); "The lowest such eigenvalue"
  "crosses zero exactly at the critical precision". "The unstable
  direction is the principal eigenvector of Σ in spatial space combined
  with any anti-symmetric w in component space." The paper's parametrization
  note: Rose's Tc = 2λmax becomes βc = 1/λmax under β = 2/T (App. A).
  Checked numerically by the reader (above).
- **Normal form** (§3.5, Eq. 7): ε̇ = (β − βc)ε − αε³ + η(t), with growth
  from the noise scale "on a characteristic timescale that scales as
  1/(β − βc)". α is not computed.
- **An operational definition** (§1): "We adopt this Hessian-pitchfork
  event as our operational definition of concept emergence".
- **Endogenous crossing** (Prop. 1, §3.4; App. B). The proof "is
  essentially a continuity argument"; "The substantive content is in the
  hypotheses" (β non-decreasing, βc non-increasing).
- **Metastability** (Remark 1, §3.5): "The crossing event of Prop. 1 marks
  when S0 becomes unstable, not when the system reaches the
  broken-symmetry state."
- **Grokking in three acts** (§4.2, Tables 2–3). Crossing at steps 34–60
  in all 15 runs, weight decay 0 included; grokking at 8,900 ± 864 steps
  (weight decay 1.0) to 147,167 (0.1); "the γ = 0 control fails to escape
  in any of 3 seeds within 50k steps"; a fit τesc ∝ WD^−1.23 on six points,
  "specific to the modular-arithmetic grokking setup". "the model becomes
  eligible to acquire it within the first ∼ 40 steps and then spends
  thousands of steps trying to escape a saddle."
- **Escape needs a tilt added by hand** (App. C). The post-critical normal
  form gets "an additional drift −γU′(ε) from the encoder's upstream loss
  component that favors the memorization basin"; "The output is empirical,
  not theoretical; we do not claim a closed-form prediction for τesc from
  the bifurcation framework alone".
- **Feature lottery** (§5.1). Early-to-late Spearman ρ of per-atom POS
  purity: +0.03 at step 46 ("noise floor"), +0.16 at 261 ("bifurcation
  onset"), +0.41 at 1,000 (5% of training), +0.88 at 10,000; stable
  across K from 256 to 8,192 (§5.4). The direction each atom takes is
  fixed by "initialization noise and the cubic terms" on the
  (K − 1)-fold degenerate unstable subspace (App. A.4), not by the linear
  analysis.
- **Prop. 2** (§5.5; App. D): directional persistence E[dk(0)ᵀdk(T)] > 0
  in a finite window, for weakly coupled modes and an initial perturbation
  above the noise floor; the magnitude +0.41 "is not predicted by the
  theory."
- **Hierarchy and reversal** (§3.3; App. F): a secondary threshold
  βc⁽²⁾ = 1/λmax(Σwithin) inside each supercluster; "decreasing β
  continuously merges the broken-symmetry branches back into S0".

## Assumptions

- The probe is joint-detached: "the encoder's gradients never see the
  probe" (App. K). Every bifurcation derived is in the probe's parameters
  on a given representation, not in the network's weights.
- Isotropic components with one shared β; analysis at the exactly
  collapsed state; β(t) non-decreasing and βc(t) non-increasing (Prop. 1).
- A one-dimensional Langevin reduction, quasi-static geometry, SGD noise
  as Langevin noise (App. C).
- Prop. 2: decoupled or weakly coupled modes, finite horizon.

## Concepts

Critical precision βc; the collapsed state S0; the anti-symmetric channel,
(K − 1)-fold degenerate; endogenous criticality; post-critical
metastability; drift- against activation-dominated escape; the bifurcation
arc in (log β/βc, log NC1) and its four shapes; feature lottery; identity
lock; secondary bifurcation; reverse traversal as merge. Terms of art:
deterministic annealing (Rose et al. 1990), NC1 (Papyan et al., [LIT-618](../literature.d/LIT-618.md)),
Kramers' law (Berglund, [LIT-2037](../literature.d/LIT-2037.md)), lottery ticket.

## Limitations

- **The pitchfork is the probe's, and the probe is detached.** The theory
  is annealing analysis applied to a mixture fitted to z(t). What it shows
  of the network is λmax(Cov z(t)) against the probe's β, whose growth is
  set by the probe's own optimizer (lrβ = 10⁻², log β0 = −2.5). The
  crossing time is in part a probe hyperparameter; App. K shows Act 1
  invariant in the probe's K but sweeps neither lrβ nor β0.
- **The grokking plateau joins two order parameters by hand.** ε in
  Eq. 7 is the probe's split; the plateau measured is NC1 of the encoder's
  features. The bridge is the tilt γU(ε) of App. C, which the paper adds
  rather than derives and itself calls empirical. A rival reading: the
  crossing near step 40 says only that the residual stream became
  anisotropic, and the escape is the known weight-decay-driven circuit
  formation, independent of the probe.
- βSAE, "the SAE's own precision", is not defined anywhere the reader
  found.
- Prop. 1 is an intermediate-value step whose content is its hypotheses;
  Prop. 3's four shapes are three binary axes, not a derived count.
- The feature lottery measures one kind of interpretability (POS purity,
  15 classes from spaCy), on three seeds; the §6 diagnostics are
  single-seed. The WD^−1.23 fit has six points.
