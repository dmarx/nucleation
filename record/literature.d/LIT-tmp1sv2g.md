# LIT-tmp1sv2g: Social Acceleration: Ethical and Political Consequences of a Desynchronized High-Speed Society

Hartmut Rosa (2003). Social Acceleration: Ethical and Political Consequences of a Desynchronized High-Speed Society. *Constellations* 10(1), 3–33. DOI-10.1111/1467-8675.00309. Read in the authorized Spanish translation, "Aceleración social: consecuencias éticas y políticas de una sociedad de alta velocidad desincronizada", *Persona y Sociedad* XXV(1) (2011), 9–49.

## Key takeaways

- "permanecer detenido es equivalente a quedarse atrás" (p. 21): standing still means falling behind.
- People keep pace "para evitar la pérdida de opciones y conexiones" (p. 21).
