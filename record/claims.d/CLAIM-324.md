# CLAIM-324: In the What Survives Translation? exchange, the manuscript drafted straight from the crystallized argument changed the thesis with no turn arguing the move and dropped 24 of that argument's items, none on request, and the second crystallized argument announced its change of question but left items out without saying which had dissolved and which were lost

## The claim

This is the case [CLAIM-178](CLAIM-178.md) argues from, stated on its own so that its
warrant can be. "At U38 the owner asked for 'the whole (crystallized)
argument', and three minutes later for a manuscript. The draft:

- changed the thesis from [CLAIM-017](CLAIM-017.md) to [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), with no turn arguing the
  move;
- added 'necessary' to 'not sufficient', supported by an example that shows
  only insufficiency ([CLAIM-053](CLAIM-053.md));
- dropped 24 items of the crystallized argument, none of them on request."

"The second crystallized argument (A203), the terminal state at U56
([TERM-052](../terms.d/TERM-052.md)), announced its change of question. It also left out Tannaka
reconstruction, which A198 had offered as a model for rebuilding a
communicative kind's symmetries from its realizations ([QUESTION-036](../questions.d/QUESTION-036.md)),
[CLAIM-125](CLAIM-125.md)'s two open problems, and more. Maturing can legitimately drop
things, so some of these may have dissolved rather than been lost, but
nothing in the exchange says which. Each draft read as finished."

The count and the turns are from the workbench reconstructions of the
exchange (2026/10/09/025539, 2026/10/10/040457).

## What it does not say

- That drafts written straight from an exchange generally drop
  commitments or change their thesis silently. That generalization, and
  the remedy of keeping the record as the reference a draft is checked
  against, are [CLAIM-178](CLAIM-178.md)'s.
- That every omission was a loss. Some may have dissolved, and the
  exchange does not say which.
- That the change of thesis was wrong. It was not argued, and not
  announced.
