  "concepts are directions" are made precise with counterfactual pairs,

  separable concepts orthogonal, the Riesz map sends the first to the

  ↦ Aγ + β, λ ↦ A⁻ᵀλ, so no inner product is identified, and the paper

  3.4). Evidence: 27 concepts on LLaMA-2 7B (26 show a common direction;
