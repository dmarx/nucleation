# TERM-130: protected contestability, of an arrangement, for those with standing under a declared standard: the maintained, resource-bounded capability of those parties to formulate and present objections and obtain an appropriately consequential assessment of them, robust to a declared class of opposition; not a guarantee of success, a veto or costless adjudication

## Definition

A81 §14 of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b):

> The maintained, resource-bounded capability of actors with legitimate standing to formulate, present, and obtain appropriately consequential assessment of objections under the governing normative framework.

"Distinguish it from guaranteed success, unrestricted veto, or costless
adjudication."

A81 §11 makes it a set of states. With 𝒥_N(x) the corrective objectives
the institution is obligated to support at state x, C_J^N(x) the robust,
resource-bounded corrective capability for J ([CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md)) and δ_J a
tolerance "part of a justified institutional specification, not universal
constants", the adequacy region is

    𝒜_N = { x : ∀ J ∈ 𝒥_N(x),  C_J^N(x) ≥ 1 − δ_J },

and the "protected contestability viability kernel" is

    𝒱_N = { x₀ : ∃ π_G ∀ σ ∈ Σ_N,  Pr_{x₀}^{π_G, σ}[ x_t ∈ 𝒜_N for all t ≤ H ] ≥ 1 − ε }.

An arrangement has protected contestability at x₀ when x₀ ∈ 𝒱_N: some
permissible governance policy keeps it adequate for every corrective
objective it owes, against every opposition in Σ_N, over the horizon.
Both sets are definitions; nothing is derived from them in the exchange.

The term adds "maintained" to correctability. A81 §11: "**an
institution's present performance and its capacity to remain
appropriately answerable are distinct objects of evaluation**". A change
can improve ordinary performance while moving the arrangement toward the
kernel's boundary: automation that erodes the competence needed to detect
mistakes, thinner oversight staffing, heavier litigation requirements.

## What it is not

- **Not correctability.** [TERM-085](TERM-085.md) (Active) is the chance a plan reaches
  a corrective objective from the current state, with no opponent and no
  horizon of maintenance. This term is built from its robust form and
  asks that it hold over time; the two are in force side by side.
- **Not the six-stage objection.** [CLAIM-732](../claims.d/CLAIM-732.md) says what an effective
  objection needs at one time. This term says when an arrangement keeps
  providing it.
- **Not answerability.** [TERM-126](TERM-126.md) is the relation of owing an
  answer; this is one form of answer, contestation, as a maintained
  capability of the arrangement.
- **Not viability of a system's own persistence** ([CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md), Active;
  [CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md), Proposed, on viable histories). The set kept invariant here
  is an adequacy region defined by obligations to others, not the
  system's survival set.
- **Not a measure of whether wrongs occur.** A81 §11: "A system may
  experience violations while preserving the means to recognize and
  remedy them."

## What would settle it

Adoption is the owner's. A use in a claim would test it: a matched pair
of arrangements with the same present corrective capability, one inside
𝒱_N and one outside, differing only in what they maintain (a reserve,
practised procedures, independent expertise). [CLAIM-686](../claims.d/CLAIM-686.md) (Proposed), on
where a dependent's claims fail of legibility, efficacy or
correctability, is the nearest claim that could be restated in it.
