# CLAIM-1430: Revealed demand fails as alignment evidence once shaped

## The claim

A8 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c) on the tobacco case ([CASE-428](../cases.d/CASE-428.md)): "consumer demand was not
an independent input to the optimization process. It was something the
industry could influence", so

> **Alignment implication:** Revealed preferences become unreliable
> evidence of alignment when the system being evaluated has substantial
> influence over those preferences.

Its proposed comparative study then orders three cases:

- "Wells Fargo demonstrates optimization corrupting a measure of human
  demand" (gaming demand measurement; [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md));
- "Tobacco demonstrates optimization changing the motivational conditions
  underlying human demand" (engineering demand);
- "Social media introduces the possibility of continuous optimization of
  the broader cultural environment in which human preferences develop"
  (governing the conditions of demand formation; [CASE-429](../cases.d/CASE-429.md)).

"These are progressively more expansive forms of optimization
jurisdiction, not necessarily a historical sequence or a ranking of harm."

## How each rung defeats the evidence

- **Gaming the measurement** leaves preferences alone and corrupts the
  reading: Wells Fargo's accounts recorded demand nobody had.
- **Engineering demand** changes the preferences the reading reads: the
  demand is real, and partly the seller's product.
- **Governing the conditions of formation** changes what the next
  generation of preferences will be, so even a correct reading of present
  demand is a reading of the optimizer's environment.

Below the ladder, [CLAIM-658](CLAIM-658.md) shows a design set against majority
preference with no change in anyone's taste, by the weighting of activity
alone; and [CLAIM-653](CLAIM-653.md) the drift that follows if the heaviest users
habituate. [CLAIM-954](CLAIM-954.md)'s laboratory is what separates the third rung from
the first: only randomized exposure tells classes that reshape
preferences from classes merely used. [CLAIM-482](CLAIM-482.md) is the organizational
counterpart: an organization's own revealed preference is fitted to one
regime of authority.

## Note of 2026-11-26: the condition the claim leaves out (the assistant)

A gap. As A8 states it, any influence defeats the evidence, and that
proves too much: education, advice and a good product all shape
preferences, and what people choose after them is not thereby poor
evidence of being served. What the tobacco case has and good teaching
lacks is influence that those shaped would not endorse if they saw it, or
could not contest: dependence designed in, and the information that
would let them judge it suppressed. The `defeated_if` above is written
with that condition, and the claim should be read with it: revealed
demand fails as evidence of service to the extent the optimizer's
influence over it is neither endorsed nor contestable by those whose
demand it is. Which influence is endorsable is the record's authenticity
question, not settled here. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
