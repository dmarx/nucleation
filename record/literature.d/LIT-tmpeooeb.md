# LIT-tmpeooeb: The Mothership Vortex: An Investigation Into the Firm at the Heart of the Democratic Spam Machine

Adam Bonica (2025), *On Data and Democracy (Substack newsletter)* — <https://data4democracy.substack.com/p/the-mothership-vortex-…>

## Key takeaways

- From his analysis of FEC filings, Bonica argues that a network of PACs linked to the consulting firm Mothership Strategies raised about $678M from individuals since 2018, but passed at most about $11M (about 1.6%) to candidates and party committees. Mothership itself received $159M from the network, part of $282M from all clients.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The author traces the high-pressure Democratic text and email fundraising ecosystem to one firm, Mothership Strategies, founded in 2014 by former DCCC digital staff. He describes it as sitting at the centre of an interlocking set of PACs (Progressive Turnout Project, Stop Republicans, End Citizens United, and others), some co-founded by its principals. Following the disbursements in FEC data, he argues that most of the money goes to consulting, texting vendors, payroll and further fundraising, and very little reaches campaigns. He calls on the party to cut ties with such vendors and to set efficiency standards.
