# THEORY-tmps3qun: A protective measurement yields the expectation value ⟨A⟩ on a single system in the ideal limit, and with enough observables ψ up to a global phase; this shows ψ to be ontic only if the protection procedure carries no information about ψ, a premise that Zeno and adiabatic protection, which select ψ from a discrete eigenbasis the experimenter chose, do not meet

## Source

Gao (2016; Cambridge University Press 2017), [LIT-121](../literature.d/LIT-121.md), chs. 1 and 4, read in
full in [NOTE-140](../notes.d/NOTE-140.md) (arXiv draft).

## What was actually shown

**Protective measurement (§1.3).** With Zeno protection over [0, τ] at
N → ∞ times, |t=τ⟩ = |ψ⟩|φ(x₀ + ⟨A⟩)⟩ (eq. 1.10): the pointer shifts by
⟨A⟩ and the system is not entangled with it. By Ehrenfest, any
non-disturbing measurement shifts the pointer by ⟨ψ|A|ψ⟩ (eqs. 1.12–1.13).
Taking A as normalised projectors on regions, and B as a current operator,
gives ρ and j and hence ψ up to a global phase (eqs. 1.14–1.16). This is the
standard Aharonov–Anandan–Vaidman result ([NOTE-140](../notes.d/NOTE-140.md), C1, strong).

**From there to ψ-ontology (ch. 4).** Three routes:
- inside an extended ontological-models framework, with a preparation
  non-contextuality assumption: |0⟩ and |+⟩, each protected along its own
  spin direction, give 1 and ½ for a protective measurement of |0⟩⟨0|, so
  no λ can be compatible with both (pp. 33–35; C2, moderate);
- two versions said to need "no auxiliary assumptions" (pp. 35–37; C3,
  weak);
- a "weaker criterion of reality" outside the framework: a definite pointer
  value whose shift rate is also fixed by that value marks a real property
  (§4.4; C4, weak).

Gao presents the argument as free of PBR's preparation independence and of
Hardy's ontic indifference (ch. 4).

**The load-bearing premise.** All three routes assume the protection
procedure contains "no information about the measured wave function"
(pp. 36–37). Gao's fn. 10 (p. 37) undercuts it: Zeno protection needs an
observable of which ψ is a nondegenerate eigenstate, and adiabatic
protection needs ψ to be a nondegenerate eigenstate of H, so the procedure
selects ψ from a discrete set. This is Combes, Ferrie, Leifer and Pusey's
objection, answered in one footnote (fn. 13, p. 40) by assertion.

**What could have come out otherwise.** The pointer could have shifted by a
random eigenvalue, as in a projective measurement, or the shift could have
needed an ensemble. In the ideal limit it does neither. Whether that bears
on ψ's reality is where the dispute sits.

## What this does not say

- **That ψ is ontic without assumptions.** The claim to need none rests on
  the contested premise above ([NOTE-140](../notes.d/NOTE-140.md), C3, weak).
- **That the charge distribution |ψ|²Q is a real field.** Gao's own next
  step denies it, on a premise about self-interaction that he calls "a
  fundamental assumption in physics" (p. 168) and does not argue.
- **That particles move randomly and discontinuously.** The random
  discontinuous motion ontology rests on that self-interaction premise and,
  for randomness, on an assumption Gao leaves for future work (p. 67; C7–C9).
- **That a collapse model is required or confirmed.** Collapse follows only
  from a further assumption that the Born probabilities originate in that
  motion (§8.3). The model's predictions, a ¹⁸⁰Ta isomer superposition
  collapsing in about 20 minutes and a seasonal variation of collapse
  times, are untested (C16–C17).
