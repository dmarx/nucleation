# ARG-tmpo7ijm: Post-abolition race power → endurance not adequacy

## The argument

1. A norm of race-based power outlasted the chattel-slavery theories that
   first justified it, by close to a century in law ([CASE-tmp3euq0](../cases.d/CASE-tmp3euq0.md); the
   owner's [CLAIM-tmp8l5s9](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8l5s9.md)).
2. A norm can outlast its justification because its support comes apart
   from its justification: replacement rationales, the interests the norm
   serves and the sanctions of those it favours keep it up ([CLAIM-tmps8ou2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmps8ou2.md)).
3. So the norm's endurance tells us how much maintenance it received and
   from whom. It tells us nothing about whether the norm was adequate. Here
   it endured because those it advantaged had the power to maintain it.
4. So endurance cannot serve as a general presumption of adequacy, which is
   the clause of [CLAIM-tmplc0vv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmplc0vv.md) this argument concludes.

## Critical questions

- **Is the evidence as stated?** The case's dates are the standard ones,
  unchecked here ([LEAD-tmpuuj6e](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpuuj6e.md) would check them).
- **Does one case defeat a general presumption?** A presumption is not
  defeated by one counterexample. The case shows something narrower: a
  presumption from endurance is weakest exactly where a norm's maintainers
  are its beneficiaries, since then endurance is what their power
  predicts whatever the norm's merit.
- **Does it meet the conservative's own statement?** [CLAIM-tmpdzcj6](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdzcj6.md)
  steelmans conservatism as a presumption under uncertainty that puts the
  burden of proof on change and concedes that conservation needs
  correction. Burke and Oakeshott would agree that segregation was unjust.
  So the case does not refute that presumption. It shows who bears its
  costs: those a norm subordinates must carry the burden of proof against
  it, and they are the least able to discharge it. Whether a presumption
  that allocates its burden that way can be justified is the question the
  case leaves open.
- **Is the reliance loop doing the work?** No. [ARG-tmpqkfwb](ARG-tmpqkfwb.md): the case does
  not separate maintenance by reliance from maintenance by sanction. This
  argument needs only that support and justification came apart, not which
  mechanism kept up the support.

## Verdict

Supports [CLAIM-tmplc0vv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmplc0vv.md)'s clause on endurance as a presumption of
adequacy. Narrows [CLAIM-tmpdzcj6](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdzcj6.md) without defeating it: the presumption
survives, but its costs are shown to fall on those the persisting norm
subordinates.
