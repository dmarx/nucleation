## The account

Flouris et al. (2008) define ontology change as deciding the modifications to make to an ontology
in response to a need, implementing them and managing their effects on dependent elements, divide it
into ten subfields (mapping, morphism, matching, articulation, translation, evolution, debugging,
versioning, integration and merging), and report ontology evolution as Stojanovic et al.'s cyclic
process of change capture, representation, semantics of change, implementation, propagation and
validation, with the semantics-of-change phase, which determines a change's side-effects, as the most
crucial.
