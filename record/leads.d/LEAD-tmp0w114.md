# LEAD-tmp0w114: Vary the direction of a social attribution while holding its stated grounds fixed, and estimate the part of each person's response that reverses with the attribution, to tell conformity and defiance, which share it, from a response governed by the grounds

## The plan

[CLAIM-425](../claims.d/CLAIM-425.md) says neither absorbing nor resisting every judgement is
self-governance, "since both let the judgements set the terms of
response". [CLAIM-tmpj4pw3](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj4pw3.md) turns that into a quantity: under reversal of an
attribution with its grounds held fixed, the size of the response's
reversing part.

A design in [CASE-140](../cases.d/CASE-140.md)'s form, which fixes the words of a characterization
and varies only its attributed breadth: present the same evidence about a
person's conduct (the grounds), paired with a characterization of one
direction or the other ("hostile", "conciliatory"), and record a response
on a scale running between the two. For each person, half the difference
between their responses under the two directions estimates the reversing
part; half the sum, the part the grounds and everything else fix.

**What would count.** Conformity and defiance should show reversing parts
of opposite sign and similar size; [CLAIM-425](../claims.d/CLAIM-425.md)'s self-governing response a
small one together with responsiveness to changes in the grounds (vary the
evidence too). If defiant respondents show no larger reversing part than
indifferent ones, [CLAIM-415](../claims.d/CLAIM-415.md)'s "resisting it makes it consequential" loses
its measurable form.

## Questions to settle first

- A within-person design leaks: the second presentation is read against
  the first. A between-person design needs matched groups.
- The response scale must allow defiance to show as reversal, not as
  refusal to answer.
- A8's other two separations, formation provenance and corrective
  capacity, are not measured by this design.
