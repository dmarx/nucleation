# THEORY-tmpqb5z0: The TRACE-OU-Bench card (2026) describes 620 LLM-generated ontology-update proposals from SEC 10-Q filings of 2016–2026

## The account

The TRACE-OU-Bench card (2026) describes 620 LLM-generated ontology-update proposals from SEC 10-Q filings of 2016–2026. One annotator, following a written governance protocol, judged each as accept, modify, reject or insufficient evidence against the ontology snapshot then in force. Snapshots are rebuilt from the accepted updates of each earlier period, so later proposals are judged against an ontology the earlier decisions produced.
