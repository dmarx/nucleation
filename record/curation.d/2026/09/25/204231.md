The seed entry of this afternoon left three questions open, and the owner
answered all three: **file all of it**, including the works that are not
papers; **seed from abstracts and skims**, then triage the result, because
the deeper readings still have to happen; and **when in doubt, the
anthology**. This entry records what the seeding did and the order the deeper
readings should take.

**29 works filed here, 5 to the anthology. 26 carry a `Skimmed` NOTE, 3 carry
none. Every LIT is `Deferred` ("Unreviewed"), with a status note saying it was
seeded and not read in full.**

## How it was done, and how far to trust it

Four agents each fetched one cluster's sources and wrote a dossier per work:
the bibliography, a paraphrased abstract, skim bullets each naming the
section or page it came from, what a deeper reading should check, a priority,
and what could and could not be reached. A script turned each dossier into a
LIT and, where the work was actually skimmed, a NOTE. The LIT's "Standing in
the record" keeps the priority, the checks and the access record, so the
triage below can be audited from the documents rather than from this entry.

**Depth was downgraded where the skim was not a skim.** The concussion review
(`LIT-029`) and Bourrat on individuality (`LIT-005`) were reached
only through a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary; the
publishers served a bot check. A summary of the text is not a skim of it, so
both are filed at abstract depth, with no NOTE. So is the wasp-venom
proteomics paper (`LIT-026`), where only the abstract was reachable.

**Identities corrected on the way.** The network-dimension paper the triage
called *Unveiling the Dimensionality of Networks of Networks* is filed under
the title it took in v2 and in *Phys. Rev. E* 114, L022301. The arXiv hash
`quant-ph/9601020` is Fuchs's 1995 PhD thesis. The two unnamed PDFs are
Bottke et al. on the Eulalia impact shower and Rungsa et al. 2016 in
*Toxicon*. The hearing transcript (`LIT-012`) matches the CourtListener
docket for D. Minn. 0:26-cv-00603 by case number and judge; the Drive copy
has not been checked against a docket entry, a transcript holds no ruling,
and whether a written order followed is unverified.

**The seven that are not papers.** The ISW report, JP 3-12, Drezner's essay
and Gelman's post carry `url:` sources. The three court documents carry
descriptive titles marked `[descriptive title]`, because a filing has a
caption, not a title.

## The five that went to the anthology

The seed named four boundary cases, and the owner's rule sent all four there.
A fifth joined them: *Rigor with Machine Learning from Field Theory to the
Poincaré Conjecture*, triaged under "other sciences", whose transferable
claim is methodological — let a learned model search and an exact procedure
verify what it finds. That is an `analysis-and-evaluation` claim, so it is in
doubt, so it is the anthology's. *Topos and Stacks* went under
`model-architecture` and `concept-geometry` rather than the
`analysis-and-evaluation` the seed guessed: it is about what an architecture
lets information and semantics do, not about how to measure anything.

That leaves `neuroscience` with no work under it as the primary topic, only
as the concussion review's second tag. The word stays; it was blurbed for a
cluster the reading feed keeps producing.

## Reading time is not the priority here, and why

The two largest totals in the whole feed are the Eulalia paper (11,500 s) and
the network-dimension letter (6,080 s), and their dossiers rank them low and
medium. That is not a contradiction: time measures the owner's attention, and
the priority below measures what a close reading would add to this record.

*Corrected the same evening.* This section first said the long totals meant
those works had "probably been read", and that a deeper-reading pass should
begin by asking the owner which NOTEs to mark `Read`. That misreads the
status. A NOTE records a reading *this record* made — `Read` is "the full
text, closely enough to state its assumptions and results exactly" — and it
is earned by doing that reading, not by the owner's attention in a browser
tab. The owner's reply: the status is an invitation to read the thing. So the
deeper readings below are this record's to perform, in this order, and a
`Skimmed` NOTE is replaced by a `Read` one when the reading is done.

## The order for deeper readings

**First — read in full.** Short, foundational, and each unlocks others.

1. **Spekkens, *Quasi-quantization*** (`LIT-007`, 1,615 s). The framing
   for the two later Spekkens-group papers. Read it before *Why interference
   phenomena…* (`LIT-019`) and the structure theorem (`LIT-003`),
   whose skims are enough once this is in hand. §II–III, the technical core,
   is what the skim missed.
2. **Abramsky & Brandenburger, the sheaf-theoretic structure of non-locality**
   (`LIT-016`). The core theorems are short. *Corrected by the reading
   ([NOTE-016](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-016.md)):* this entry first called it "this record's one bridge to the
   anthology", with *Topos and Stacks* building on it and sheaf neural
   networks descending from it. Neither holds. Belfiore & Bennequin cite it
   once, in a related-work list, and use nothing from it; the sheaf-network
   papers checked do not cite it. It is first on its own merits, as the
   founding and fully proved paper of its programme.
3. **The *U.S. v. Google* remedies opinion** (`LIT-006`, 1,040 s over
   four sessions). The skim did not reach the findings of fact on generative
   AI (pp. 17–36): how a federal court understood LLMs, grounding and the GenAI
   market. That is the part worth reading.
4. **Mediano et al. and Varley & Hoel, side by side** (`LIT-025`,
   `LIT-027`, ~2,500 s combined). Two definitions of emergence from the
   same theme issue. A deeper reading is a comparison, and it should take in
   Rosas et al. 2020, the primary behind the first. Neither is the anthology's
   `capability-thresholds` sense of "emergence", and whoever reads them should
   write that down.

**Second — targeted sections, not the whole work.**

- Forman–Ricci curvature (`LIT-020`): the curvature formula and flow
  definitions, §2.3 and §4.
- Fuchs's thesis (`LIT-018`): chapters 2–3.
- Nested HDP (`LIT-002`): the SVI derivation in §4.
- The network-dimension letter (`LIT-028`): the Supplementary Material
  derivation, after asking whether it was already read.
- JP 3-12 (`LIT-015`): the definitions and the authorities chapter.
- The Rhode Island Hospital subpoena order (`LIT-024`): whether it was
  appealed, and how it interacts with the Texas enforcement order.
- Lindgren & Liukkonen (`LIT-001`): the linearization assumptions,
  against Nelson's stochastic mechanics.

**Third — the skim is enough unless an interest makes it otherwise.** The
remaining sixteen: Eulalia, wasp venom, vehicle routing, the emergence
theme-issue introduction, Betti Lagrangians, spectral sets, Ricci-flow
community detection, quantum thermodynamics, Pitman–Yor topic models, the two
Spekkens-group follow-ups named above, the concussion review, Bourrat, the ISW
report, the hearing transcript, Drezner and Gelman. Several of their
dossiers say the same thing: the value is in the primary sources the piece
cites, not in a second pass over the piece.

None of this promotes anything. A work leaves `Deferred` when somebody has
read it closely, and here nobody has yet.
