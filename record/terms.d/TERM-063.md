# TERM-063: agent, as an individual that is the active source of modulations of its coupling with its environment and regulates them against norms its own organization sets (Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde)

## Definition

[THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md), from Barandiaran, Di Paolo and Rohde ([LIT-566](../literature.d/LIT-566.md)). A system is an
**agent** on this definition when:

1. **Individuality.** It distinguishes itself from its environment, with no
   observer doing it. Here that means organizational closure
   ([TERM-070](TERM-070.md)).
2. **Interactional asymmetry.** It is the active source of modulations of
   its coupling with the environment.
3. **Normativity.** It regulates that coupling against norms its own
   organization sets.

A11's form: "A system qualifies as an agent under this criterion when its
own maintained organization establishes conditions of continued
functioning and actively modulates its coupling with the environment in
accordance with those conditions."

**Why it rivals [TERM-044](TERM-044.md), and does not complement it.** The two give
different verdicts on cases the record already holds:

- **The thermostat with its designers** passes [TERM-044](TERM-044.md) ([CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md): it would
  be redesigned, so its policy would adapt). Here it fails: the norm is the
  designers', not set by its own organization, which is [CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md)'s point
  about norms a modeller fixes.
- **A trained policy's training process** is an agent on Kenton et al.'s
  account, which is [TERM-044](TERM-044.md)'s third test ([THEORY-527](../theory.d/THEORY-527.md)). Here it is doubtful,
  with no organization of its own that sets norms ([CLAIM-367](../claims.d/CLAIM-367.md), [CASE-113](../cases.d/CASE-113.md)).
- **A cell in passive osmosis** is an individual and not an agent here,
  because it is not the source of the modulation ([THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md)'s own case).
  [TERM-044](TERM-044.md) likely agrees for another reason, its description and adaptation
  tests, so the two part on who is an agent, not only on why.

So the two cannot both be the unqualified "agent" of the distributed-agency
line. [ADR-046](../decisions.d/ADR-046.md)'s lifecycle fits: a Proposed candidate `rivals` the Active
term it would replace for the same use. A12 put the choice: "If proposed as
replacements for [TERM-044](TERM-044.md), then `rivals: [TERM-044]` is appropriate. If
instead they are separate dimensions that can coexist, `complements` is the
eventual relationship." If the owner wants both in force as dimensions,
the relation becomes `complements`, and this title must stop saying
"agent" unqualified.

**What would settle it.** [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md)'s own promotion test: measures of
closure and of normativity, and an asymmetry criterion that does not
depend on how the modeller splits parameters from variables, applied
"almost mechanically" to a protocell model, an evolved robot controller and
a homeostat, with verdicts that match [LIT-566](../literature.d/LIT-566.md)'s cases without being tuned
to them. And [QUESTION-032](../questions.d/QUESTION-032.md) and [QUESTION-093](../questions.d/QUESTION-093.md), which ask what makes a factor
an agent and which autonomy it needs.

## What it is not

- **Not a homeostat.** [THEORY-583](../theory.d/THEORY-583.md)'s cybernetic Self counts any homeostat.
  This definition does not.
- **Not [TERM-044](TERM-044.md)'s third test,** policy adaptation ([THEORY-527](../theory.d/THEORY-527.md), [THEORY-801](../theory.d/THEORY-801.md)).
  "Agent, as an adaptive policy mechanism", which A11 and A12 also
  proposed as a rival, is that test taken alone: [TERM-044](TERM-044.md) narrows it rather
  than rivalling it, and it is not filed.
- **Not a subject.** [THEORY-071](../theory.d/THEORY-071.md)'s "autonomy" and "self" mean
  organizational closure, not a subject or self-governance.
- **Not a change to the claims stated in [TERM-044](TERM-044.md)** ([CLAIM-155](../claims.d/CLAIM-155.md), [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md),
  [CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md), [CLAIM-168](../claims.d/CLAIM-168.md), [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md) and [CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md)). They are unchanged.
