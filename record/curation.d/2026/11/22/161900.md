refusal was met. The scan has no text layer, so I read it as page

## What was left, and why

- **[CLAIM-783](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-783.md)**: no note. Its sixth part argues by witness, and
  Kolmogorov's contentless problem resembles an empty intersection, but
  the paper says nothing about adding predicates or about their sets
  meeting. The bearing is too loose to state.
- **[THEORY-1142](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1142.md)**: no note. Kolmogorov's coincidence "nach der Form" with
  Heyting is an identity of solved formulas under a reinterpretation. It
  is not an institution morphism, and the paper touches nothing in the
  Goguen–Burstall account.
- **Works the paper cites, not registered**:
  - Heyting 1930, the formalization the calculus is said to coincide
    with;
  - Heyting's 1931 paper in *Erkenntnis* 2, named in n. 17;
  - Glivenko 1929, for p. 64;
  - Weyl 1921, "Über die neue Grundlagenkrise der Mathematik", the
    source of §2's principle.

  None is quoted or relied on beyond Kolmogorov's own report. Heyting
  1930 would be the natural next reading if the claim of coincidence came
  to matter.
- **The worked examples** (Fermat, the circle through three points, the
  quadratic, π and e) are in the NOTE only. They illustrate the concept,
  and the record needs no case for them.

## Gaps

These are gaps in the work's own argument. They sit in the NOTE's
Limitations and are not filled, because the record does not use the
steps:

- the coincidence with Heyting's calculus is asserted and its derivations
  are omitted (p. 63);
- "problem" is never defined (p. 59), and "general method" is informal
  (p. 60);
- §2's own question, which laws hold for statements whose negation has no
  sense, is left open (p. 65, n. 16).

## Offered, not filed

These are for the owner, ranked by how far I would stand behind each:

1. **Revise [CLAIM-949](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-949.md)'s question-as-problem bullet** to "a question
   is a problem whose answer space is a disjunction of sub-problems; the
   calculus has no counterpart of a question's revision". This is
   demonstrated from the reading, and it is a text change to a Proposed
   claim, so it is the owner's.
2. **A claim that a failed presupposition is handled oppositely by the
   two accounts.** Kolmogorov counts the proof that a problem is
   contentless as its solution (p. 59). [CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md) treats the matching
   case, a question with no adequate answer, as a call to revise the
   question. It would hold that whether a failed presupposition closes an
   inquiry or reopens it is the line between solving and inquiring. This
   is a hypothesis. Defeated if it only restates [CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md)'s last clause.
   Cost: one claim and a check against [CLAIM-470](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-470.md)'s defeater (a).
