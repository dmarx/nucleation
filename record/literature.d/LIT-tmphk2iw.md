# LIT-tmphk2iw: The Border Between Seeing and Thinking

Ned Block, *The Border Between Seeing and Thinking*, New York: Oxford
University Press, 2023 — DOI-10.1093/oso/9780197622223.001.0001.

## Key takeaways

*Not read. What follows is what Crossref's chapter records and Block's
précis say.*

- **Chapter records Crossref returned to a search** (titles, and pages
  where given; the list may be incomplete): Introduction
  (1–60); "Two kinds of seeing-as and singular content"; "Perception is
  constitutively nonpropositional and nonconceptual"; "Perception is iconic;
  cognition is discursive"; "Nonconceptual color perception"; "Neural
  evidence that perception is nonconceptual"; "Evidence that is wrongly
  taken to show that perception is conceptual"; "Cognitive penetration is
  common but does not challenge the joint"; "Top-down effects that are
  probably not cases of cognitive penetration"; "Modularity"; "Core
  cognition and perceptual analogs of concepts"; Consciousness (417–467);
  Conclusions (468–474). Crossref lists the chapter records without a
  consistent chapter numbering, so none is given here.
- **The thesis**, in the précis's words: "perception is constitutively
  iconic, non-conceptual and non-propositional while cognition is
  paradigmatically (but not constitutively) discursive (non-iconic),
  conceptual and propositional".
