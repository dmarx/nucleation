summary: >-
  A short NDPR review of Scheffler's *Death and the Afterlife*. It summarizes the afterlife conjecture and the conclusion that "our own survival is less important to us than the survival of the human race" in one specific sense. It reports the third lecture: life must be temporally bounded, so personal immortality is no remedy, yet confidence in our values depends on others living on. It reports Frankfurt's Darwinian doubt that the concern is altruistic, and Wolf's argument that people facing extinction could and should "snap out of it". It faults all parties for ignoring religious thought on finitude.
