The second half of the philosophy-of-science group in the THEORY backfill
the owner asked for on 2026-10-16. Fourteen LITs, each with a `Read` or
`Skimmed` NOTE and cited as no THEORY's `source`, were audited against
their readings. Nothing was fetched; every THEORY rests on the record's
NOTE, and its numbers and page references are the NOTE's.

## What entered

Eleven THEORYs, two `Active` and nine `Proposed`, from ten readings. The
codes are the temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

- **Two findings, `Active`.**
  - [THEORY-569](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-569.md), from [LIT-165](../../record/literature.d/LIT-165.md) (Hartmann & Trpin, [NOTE-168](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-168.md)): the
    set-valued CohOG+ measure of explanatory power satisfies TLI for
    single hypotheses, fails OLI, and in one chain and one collider ranks
    the richer explanans set higher over a stated range of priors. A
    theorem, a counterexample and two existence examples, all three

    persistence and selection are logically independent, NCT's
    evolutionary niche and the realized population ecological niche differ

    that populate the gap are weaker and are said to be.

## What did not enter, and why

- **Wigner, Symmetry and Conservation Laws ([LIT-180](../../record/literature.d/LIT-180.md), [NOTE-147](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-147.md)).** The LIT
  is `Unavailable`, and the NOTE is a skim that says it is "not enough to
  state its assumptions or results exactly". A THEORY on it would have
  nothing to promote it against but the skim.
- **O'Malley & Parke, Philosophy of Microbiology ([LIT-210](../../record/literature.d/LIT-210.md), [NOTE-159](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-159.md)).** An
  SEP survey whose positions are reported and attributed. Its reading calls
  it "an orientation, not an argument for a thesis". Its own commitments
  (the contingency of zoocentrism, the deflation of microbiome-and-self
  claims) are graded weak or are disambiguations, not accounts. Its report
  that eukaryogenesis may not have been a transition in individuality is
  named in the Connections of [THEORY-418](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-418.md).
- **Piaget, Structuralism ([LIT-837](../../record/literature.d/LIT-837.md), [NOTE-618](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-618.md)).** A skim of 17 pages, one
  in four, of chs. I–IV. The NOTE files no THEORY and says why: the pages
  seen are definitional, and a quarter of four chapters cannot ground a
  claim about what is true.
- **Floridi, The Method of Levels of Abstraction ([LIT-900](../../record/literature.d/LIT-900.md), [NOTE-700](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-700.md)).** A
  method stated in definitions, not a result. Its one formal claim fails
  under its own definitions, and its guard against relativism ranks levels
  only within a fixed purpose. The NOTE files no THEORY, and [NOTE-099](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-099.md) filed
  none for the ISR paper that uses the same machinery.

So ten of fourteen readings became sources: ten of the twelve `Read`
NOTEs, and neither of the two skims. Of the two `Read` readings that
entered nothing, one is a survey and one a method. The owner's hypothesis
held for readings that argue a thesis, and not for surveys, methods or
skims. Nine of the eleven THEORYs are `Proposed`, because most of these
works argue rather than test.

## Choices made

- **Two THEORYs from one book.** De Haro & Butterfield's underdetermination
  and structural-realism verdict, and their emergence result, rest on
  different parts of the book and could fail separately, so each has its
  own document.
- **No `rivals`, `extends` or `presupposes`.** Worrall 2007 and Cartwright
  both deflate the evidence hierarchy, from different sides, and can both
  be right. French and De Haro & Butterfield concern different phenomena
  (inequivalent representations of one theory; dualities between models).
  Worrall 1989 is the epistemic position the ontic accounts start from, not
  a premise they take. These are stated in prose.
- **No `answers`.** Griesemer & Shavit's entrenchment-plus-closure
  criterion is a candidate for [QUESTION-035](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-035.md), but that question asks for a
  criterion that returns a verdict that can be wrong, and the sketch has no
  measurement; three readings of 2026-10-14 bearing on it were likewise not
  linked. Its closure is a mark of individuality, not agency, so it does
  not answer [QUESTION-093](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-093.md) either. Both are named in its Connections.
- **No existing THEORY duplicated these.** None of the record's 391
  THEORYs cites any of the fourteen LITs. [THEORY-034](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-034.md) and [THEORY-038](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-038.md) cover
  structural realism from Chakravartty's dilemma and category theory and do
  not state Worrall's, French's or De Haro & Butterfield's accounts.
