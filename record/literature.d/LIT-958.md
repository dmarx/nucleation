# LIT-958: How manipulable are prediction markets?

Itzhak Rasooly and Roberto Rozzi (2025), *arXiv:2503.03312*.

Filed for [QUESTION-047](../questions.d/QUESTION-047.md): random price shocks, which no trader reasoned to, persist for up to 60
days. How long they persist depends on trader count, volume and outside information. Those are
community-level mechanisms.
