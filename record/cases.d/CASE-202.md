# CASE-202: A proposed study: two small constrained mechanisms that agree under a restricted family of probes and differ internally, probed at rising budgets, to find when they become distinguishable, what the distinguishing operations cost and whether the distinctions survive a change of architecture, with controls that separate finding a difference from making one

## The case

**What it alters in [CASE-179](CASE-179.md).** [CASE-179](CASE-179.md) varies physical coupling, the
correspondence maps and the apparatus that revises them, and reads off
compatibility, attraction, boundary laws, survival under intervention and
the cost of recovering the correspondences. This variant holds a pair of
mechanisms fixed and varies what the observer can do to them.

A (§8): "Construct two small constrained mechanisms that agree under a
restricted set of observable interactions but differ internally. Origami
is a possible substrate, though a finite transition-system model would
make the initial mathematics easier to verify. Then vary the available
computational budget and repertoire of probes. Measure when previously
indistinguishable systems become distinguishable, what the distinguishing
operations cost, and whether those distinctions survive changes to their
constraint architecture."

**Controls.** "The important controls would separate an increase in
observational power from a physical modification of the mechanism.
Otherwise, discovering a difference and creating a difference could become
conflated." Concretely: every probe is run on a reset copy as well as on
the mechanism that earlier probes have acted on, so that a difference that
appears only on the acted-on copy is attributed to the probing. And, from
[CLAIM-322](../claims.d/CLAIM-322.md), "probe families should be justified independently and evaluated
on held-out examples, not selected merely to produce the desired
classification" (A's gloss): the families are fixed before the pair is
seen, and checked on pairs they were not chosen on.

**A seed pair (the filer's).** [CLAIM-583](../claims.d/CLAIM-583.md)'s two three-bit systems have
the same compatibility complex, the same single-flip move rule and the
same costs: {000, 111}, where the configuration meeting one demand cannot
reach the one meeting the other, and {000, 001}, where it can in one move.
A static probe family, which asks of a configuration only which demands it
meets, gives the two the same answers. A probe that may attempt one move
tells them apart. That is A's "especially revealing result" in its
smallest form: "two architectures with identical static compatibility
structures and identical low-budget relational profiles, but different
higher-budget reachability or maintenance structures." The study's work is
to find such pairs where the distinguishing probe is costly, so that the
budget at which they part is a measurement and not a design choice. On a
vertex like [CASE-206](CASE-206.md)'s, that budget would be a bottleneck
([CLAIM-571](../claims.d/CLAIM-571.md); [CLAIM-590](../claims.d/CLAIM-590.md)).

**Readouts.**

- the least budget, and the cheapest probe, at which the pair is told
  apart; along nested probe families the distinctions can only grow
  ([CLAIM-574](../claims.d/CLAIM-574.md)), so this is a threshold;
- the distinctions found on reset copies against those found only on
  acted-on ones;
- whether each distinction survives a declared change of architecture,
  or has to be re-established in the new one;
- for maintenance, whether the pair differs in regenerating after
  turnover where it agrees in assembling and persisting
  ([CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md)).

## What it can show

That the process layer, reachability and maintenance under resources,
carries distinctions that static compatibility does not, with the budget
at which they appear measured; and, through the controls, whether a probe
found a difference or made one.

Given its standing, it shows nothing yet: it is a design. The seed pair
is a construction, and shows only that such pairs exist.

## What it cannot show

- **That the distinctions are of individuals.** A profile that separates
  two mechanisms is descriptive individuation in [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s sense; the
  other senses need more (A, §2).
- **Anything about organizations**, unless an organizational model with
  declared probes and costs is put in place of the mechanisms.
