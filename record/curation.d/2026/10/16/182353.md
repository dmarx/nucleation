Part of the THEORY backfill of 2026-10-16. This group held 26 LITs with a
`Read` or `Skimmed` NOTE that no THEORY cited as `source`: eleven under
`representation-learning`, eight under `learning-theory` and seven under
`loss-landscapes`. Each NOTE and LIT was read in full; no paper was
fetched. Codes are the temporary ones `luria new` assigned.

## What entered

Twenty THEORYs, eleven `Active` and nine `Proposed`, drawing on 23 of
the 26 readings.

## What did not enter, and why

- **[LIT-978](../../record/literature.d/LIT-978.md) (Kothapalli's review of neural collapse, [NOTE-772](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-772.md), Skimmed).**
  A survey: every result in it is another paper's, unchecked here, and
  the LIT says no THEORY rests on it. [THEORY-083](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-083.md) already carries the
  point the review would add, that collapse is derived only in models
  that remove the data.
- **[LIT-954](../../record/literature.d/LIT-954.md) (Gokhale's semantic landscape, [NOTE-807](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-807.md)).** A vocabulary, with
  no experiment and no proof. Its one derivation shows only that chosen
  assumptions can yield the observed exponents.
- **[LIT-677](../../record/literature.d/LIT-677.md) (Zhang & Blei on the ELBO landscape, [NOTE-522](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-522.md)).** The workshop
  paper reports none of the results it describes. It is named in
  [THEORY-557](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-557.md)'s "does not say" as a claim without evidence.

So 23 of 26 readings became sources, which fits the owner's hypothesis for
this group. Two caveats. Fifteen of the 23 NOTEs had declined or held back a THEORY,
for reasons that were judgements rather than "no finding": a measurement
"better cited from the LIT" ([LIT-833](../../record/literature.d/LIT-833.md)), an aggregate with exceptions
([LIT-814](../../record/literature.d/LIT-814.md)), evidence in one figure ([LIT-662](../../record/literature.d/LIT-662.md)), a result "about training
transformers" ([LIT-859](../../record/literature.d/LIT-859.md)), material the anthology already reads ([LIT-856](../../record/literature.d/LIT-856.md)).
Following the owner's ruling, those were filed, with the NOTE's reason
stated in the THEORY where it bears on status. And the three that entered
nothing are a survey, a vocabulary and a paper without results.

## Choices made

- **Status.** Active for a finding the record believes as scoped in its
  title, or a proved result: the G-CNN and MoE symmetry results, the
  lattice theorem's triviality, the Li programme's width, which the NOTEs
  derive from the papers' own remarks. Proposed where the NOTE rates the
  evidence weak, where the result is reported second-hand ([LIT-672](../../record/literature.d/LIT-672.md)'s
  perceptron), or where the interesting part is this record's inference
  ([LIT-879](../../record/literature.d/LIT-879.md)).
- **Boundary.** Several of these works carry practice (Gemma Scope's
  engineering, test-time training, order selection, path repair, per-class
  tuning). Each THEORY states the finding only and names the practice as
  the anthology's. THEORYs on ML-system behaviour carry
  `anthology-candidate` last, as their LITs do.
- **One account from three papers.** Li's three papers were read jointly as
  one programme, so they source one THEORY, [LIT-376](../../record/literature.d/LIT-376.md) first as the paper whose
  proofs are cleanest.
- **[LIT-676](../../record/literature.d/LIT-676.md) sources two accounts**, the curved-path connectivity and the
  data-dependence of the linear barrier, which are different claims.
