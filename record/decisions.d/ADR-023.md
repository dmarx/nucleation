# ADR-023: motivation joins the topics

## Context

The owner asked how the record covered self-determination theory, then for
it to be filed alongside the theories drawn from the psychopathology batch
([ADR-021](ADR-021.md)). The record held none of it. It does hold the philosophy of
autonomy: Frankfurt's lectures ([LIT-293](../literature.d/LIT-293.md)), the regress about what makes a
motive one's own ([THEORY-029](../theory.d/THEORY-029.md)), and the manipulation argument against
present-structure conditions on free agency ([THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md)). All of that sits
under `agency`.

Self-determination theory is the empirical programme on the same question.
It distinguishes autonomous from controlled regulation, grades
internalization from introjected to integrated, and tests what each does
to persistence and well-being. Filed under `agency`, a reader could not
tell a theory of what an agent is from a theory of why people act and
when that acting is their own.

## Decision

Add `motivation` to `topics`, outside the philosophy group: why organisms
act and persist. It covers intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, needs,
goals and values, the internalization of regulation, and the difference
between autonomous and controlled motives.

- **The SDT filing:** all seven of its notes take the word first,
  including both sides of the dispute over rewards and intrinsic
  motivation.
- **Earlier filings:** two take it second, after `agency`. They are
  Frijda, Ridderinkhof and Rietveld's action readiness ([LIT-540](../literature.d/LIT-540.md)), whose
  states are motives, and Bandura's self-efficacy ([LIT-545](../literature.d/LIT-545.md)), a theory of
  why people persist.

## Alternatives considered

- **`agency` and `social-science` alone.** `agency` is in the philosophy
  group and is about what it is to be an agent. The reward and
  internalization experiments are not about that, and `social-science`
  is too large to browse them.
- **`self-determination`.** That names one theory, where the subject is
  motivation. The behavioural account it argues with would need a second
  word.

## Consequences

The SDT agent named two gaps this does not close:

- **Operant learning and reinforcement.** Behaviour analysis has no word;
  `learning-theory` is the machine-learning sense. Relational frame
  theory met the same gap ([ADR-021](ADR-021.md)).
- **Well-being and flourishing.** SDT defines its needs by their effect on
  well-being, and well-being has no word.

Each should get its word when a second work needs it.
