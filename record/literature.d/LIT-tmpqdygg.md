# LIT-tmpqdygg: Global Institutions and Human Rights: A Critical Essay on Thomas Pogge, and the Attribution of Remedial Responsibility in Cases of Human Rights Violations

Shaw-Young, J. (2008). *Global Institutions and Human Rights: A Critical Essay on Thomas Pogge, and the Attribution of Remedial Responsibility in Cases of Human Rights Violations.* MA thesis, Department of Philosophy, Queen's University, Kingston, Ontario.

## Key takeaways

- Miller's six connections (thesis p. 20, citing Miller 2007 pp.
  100–104): "moral responsibility, outcome responsibility, causal
  responsibility, benefit, capacity, and community".
- Capacity without fault (p. 29, Miller 2007 p. 103): the river bank.
- Coercion (p. 25, Miller 2007 p. 102): "If the coercer himself disappears
  from the scene, causal responsibility falls on to the person who has been
  made to inflict the injury, and with it the responsibility to care for
  the injured party."
