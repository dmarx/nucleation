Giovanni B. Moneta and Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi (1996). *Journal of Personality* 64(2): 275-310. DOI-10.1111/j.1467-6494.1996.tb00512.x

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* By the abstract: experience sampling of 208 talented adolescents; concentration, wish to do the activity, involvement and happiness regressed on challenge, skill and their imbalance; balance had a positive, independent effect on the quality of experience, with heterogeneity across dimensions and contexts.
