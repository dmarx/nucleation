# LIT-tmpc1xee: Illich as Revolutionary

David Cayley (2021). "Illich as Revolutionary". *Ill Will*, 2021-03-07: an excerpt from Cayley, *Ivan Illich: An Intellectual Journey* (Penn State University Press, 2021), published at the book's release.

## Key takeaways

- "A radical monopoly exists where all alternatives have been rendered either inaccessible or unimaginable."
- The test: "whether one can still find an alternative or decently do without the monopolized commodity".
