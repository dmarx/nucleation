# QUESTION-tmp2s3h6: With V1/V2 and category-selective cortex densely sampled in the same subjects, during clearly seen images held for over a second, is content-specific gamma-band phase or amplitude coupling between them, with the evoked response removed, again brief or absent at a sensitivity that detects it elsewhere, or is it sustained for the duration of the image?

## Why it is a question

THEORY-296 holds what COGITATE measured: no sustained content-specific
synchronization between category-selective cortex and early visual cortex. The
preregistered phase coupling was brief, low-frequency and mostly evoked, and
exploratory amplitude coupling was also brief. With only 12 V1/V2 electrodes in
the iEEG, whether the null reflects the brain or the sampling is open.

## What would answer it

A recording with dense sampling of V1/V2 and of category-selective cortex in the
same subjects, during clearly seen images held for over a second, that tests
content-specific phase or amplitude coupling in the gamma band over the whole
stimulus with the evoked response removed. The null is borne out if such
coupling is again brief or absent at a sensitivity that detects it elsewhere,
and overturned if it is sustained for the duration of the image. Results from
other metrics or bands, or from coupling with prefrontal cortex, cannot settle
it.
