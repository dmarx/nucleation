  Shalizi & Moore (2003; Foundations of Physics 55, 2, 2025), What Is a Macrostate? Subjective Observations and Objective Dynamics, arXiv cond-mat/0303625. Macrostates are both subjective and objective: the observer picks the observables, and the macrostates are then the causal states of the observed process, the coarsest partition of observation histories that predicts the observables optimally and is Markov in its own past, which the dynamics fix. "A different set of initial variables will, generally, lead to a different set of causal states"; "Nature has no preferred questions." One causal description is emergent from another when it is a coarse-graining of it and is predicted more efficiently, E/Cµ (argon's thermodynamic variables 0.87 against the microstates' 0.5). Argument and examples, no new theorems. Here for the exchange's question of which coarse-grained descriptions are natural (A17). # LIT-2996: What Is a Macrostate? Subjective Observations and Objective Dynamics

Shalizi & Moore (2003). Foundations of Physics 55, 2 (2025). arXiv cond-mat/0303625.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A invokes it without naming it at A17 for macrostates as the coarse-grainings under which dynamics close. It bears on the exchange's question of which coarse-grained descriptions are natural. Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-tmpunt6q](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpunt6q.md)) and filed as [THEORY-tmpttfgp](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpttfgp.md). A17's account of causal states, grouping histories "according to whether they imply the same probability distribution over possible futures", is accurate; its gloss "group together microscopic states" is looser, since the paper groups observation histories relative to chosen observables, and the microstate is its own causal state. The coarse-grainings "under which dynamics close" are closed in the sense that the macro process is Markov in its own past, not relative to the micro past (contrast [THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md), [THEORY-257](../theory.d/THEORY-257.md)). Its definition of emergence by predictive efficiency, which [NOTE-935](../notes.d/NOTE-935.md) refers to and the record did not hold, is filed as [TERM-tmpaztqn](../terms.d/TERM-tmpaztqn.md); with Quinn and colleagues it grounds [CLAIM-tmp3ima1](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3ima1.md).
