# LIT-2989: Learning Along the Arrow of Time: Hyperbolic Geometry for Backward-Compatible Representation Learning

Bui et al. (2025). ICML 2025 (PMLR 267); arXiv 2506.05826.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A33 for hyperbolic embeddings for backward-compatible representation updates. It bears on the exchange's use of hyperbolic geometry for hierarchy and refinement in representations.

Read on 2026-11-22 in [NOTE-tmpvu3vp](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpvu3vp.md); the reading is [THEORY-tmpe9367](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpe9367.md). The
first summary is broadly right: "further out" along the time-like axis is
entailed by the cone construction ([CLAIM-tmpxcy5s](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpxcy5s.md)) and visible in the
raised clipping and falling uncertainty, though the paper's own words are
"constraining updated embeddings to lie within the entailment cone of the
old ones". [CLAIM-839](../claims.d/CLAIM-839.md)'s "already constrains updated embeddings to earlier
cones" holds with four qualifications: a soft penalty, with cone
satisfaction never reported; per-item cones; two separately trained
models, not checkpoints; an order imposed by design ([ARG-tmpf206e](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpf206e.md)).
