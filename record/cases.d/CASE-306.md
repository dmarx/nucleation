# CASE-306: Coding the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" move by move under a codebook fixed in advance finds identity failures outnumbering structure failures by about 43 to 5, mostly re-derivations, with one silent redefinition; a second exchange of the same owner and assistant family, coded once by an AI coder

## What was done

[LEAD-079](../leads.d/LEAD-079.md) asks for the reading behind [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) and [CLAIM-483](../claims.d/CLAIM-483.md), which coded every argumentative move of the will exchange for identity failures (concepts redefined silently, a worked case changing identity, objections re-derived as though new) against structure failures (premises not stated, inferences not laid out, objections not answered), to be repeated "on a second exchange, and on one with another owner or another assistant family". The exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-20 to 21; the verbatim transcript the filing of curation 2026/11/21/180128 used) is a second exchange of the same owner and, like the will and translation exchanges, of the same assistant family.

The record fixes the original categories and the will exchange's counts per item, but not a codebook text, a segmentation rule, totals per category or an agreement figure, so no numeric ratio is held for the will exchange and the comparison is qualitative. The coder fixed the rest in a codebook written before coding (scratch/studies/lead-079/codebook.md, timestamped 07:16Z): visible turns only; a move as the unit, counted per occurrence and grouped into items; a re-derivation is a point restated as new with no back-reference (a pointer back or an explicit recap exempts it); structure codes apply to each reply's headline conclusions; "rare" for the defeater is fewer than one redefinition or re-derivation per five substantive replies. Every coded failure, with its turn, a verbatim quote and its earlier occurrence, is in scratch/studies/lead-079/coding.md.

Sensitivity: without the weak codes, 36 against 4; treating every expository re-derivation as harmless tutorial repetition and dropping frame drift, 10 against 5, with redefinitions and re-derivations at 0.26 per reply, still above the rarity bar.

Instances: at A30 the assistant calls the owner's point "a crucial correction to the model we've been developing" and re-derives three results A29 had already given; at A41 "hyperribbon" silently changes from sensitivity along parameter directions (A19, A39) to a variance hierarchy; "scale-free does not mean critical" returns as new at A10, A23, A24 and A26; A42 re-derives A39's two singular-learning results with no back-reference.

## What it shows

[CLAIM-483](../claims.d/CLAIM-483.md)'s defeated_if asks for a comparable exchange in which most defects are missing structure and redefinition and re-derivation are rare. This exchange is not one: identity failures dominate under every reading, and structure is good (premises stated, caveats and tests given, the owner's challenges answered). The [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) failures recur in a different mix: re-derivation is common, silent redefinition rare (once), and in the argument phase every change of hypothesis is announced and argued, unlike the will and translation exchanges.

## Limits

- Only [LEAD-079](../leads.d/LEAD-079.md)'s first half: the same owner and assistant family. Another owner or family is still undone.
- One coder, an AI coding another AI's exchange, with no second coder and no agreement figure; the coder had read [CLAIM-483](../claims.d/CLAIM-483.md) and [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) before writing the codebook, though the codebook preceded the coding.
- Structure codes were confined to headline conclusions, identity codes to any restated move, which may favour identity.
- The exchange is mostly exposition driven by "continue", where re-explaining is arguably what was asked; the will exchange was joint essay-writing with the record open to the assistant, this one had no record access.
- The cheapest improvement is a second coder, a person or a model of another family, recoding about 30% of turns under the same codebook ([LEAD-206](../leads.d/LEAD-206.md)'s remedy, applied here).
