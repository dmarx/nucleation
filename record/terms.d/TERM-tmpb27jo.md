# TERM-tmpb27jo: fidelity, as matching pragmatic signatures

## Definition

A18: a pragmatic signature I_f(u) is a vector of illocutionary function, speaker
alignment, audience alignment, evaluative stance and genre, evaluated in frame
f. A transformation T_(g←f) is faithful when I_g(T_(g←f)(u_f)) ≈ I_f(u_f).
"fidelity is sometimes achieved *through* lexical deviation, not despite it."

## Why it was replaced

It needs the frame-independent act of [TERM-tmpdb2i3](TERM-tmpdb2i3.md), and it compares single points.
A24 compared distributions of judgements across contexts instead ([TERM-tmpmvvf2](TERM-tmpmvvf2.md)).
