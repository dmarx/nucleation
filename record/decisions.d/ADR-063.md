# ADR-063: future-generations joins the topics

## Context

On 2026-10-29 the owner noted that Johnston's *Surviving Death* opens the
angle of legacy, and the record filed ten works on it with
[QUESTION-tmpruhu4](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpruhu4.md). The owner then asked for that line to be extended "to
broader questions about obligations towards the future, e.g.
longtermism". Sixteen further works were filed with [QUESTION-tmpmx9ha](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpmx9ha.md):
the case for longtermism and its critics, existential risk,
intergenerational justice, the non-identity problem, person-affecting
views, discounting, and the psychology of judgements about extinction and
future generations.

The vocabulary had nowhere to put them together. `ethics` is all of moral
philosophy. `altruism` asks whose good an action aims at, which is one
question about the future but not the question of what is owed to people
who do not yet exist. `motivation` and `self` hold the psychology of legacy
but not the ethics. The record's only earlier work in the area, Parfit's
*Reasons and Persons* ([LIT-469](../literature.d/LIT-469.md)), sits under `personhood` and `altruism`.

[ADR-062](ADR-062.md) records the owner's word that the vocabulary may be expanded where
merited.

## Decision

Add one topic, **`future-generations` (Future generations)**: what the
living owe, and why they care about, people who will live after them.

- **Normative:** longtermism, existential risk, intergenerational justice,
  the non-identity problem and person-affecting views, population ethics,
  discounting of future welfare.
- **Descriptive:** legacy motivation, generativity, symbolic immortality,
  continuity with one's future self, moral judgements about extinction and
  about future people.

How it is applied:

- **First**, on the works filed with [QUESTION-tmpmx9ha](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpmx9ha.md), where it is the
  subject.
- **Beside the existing tags** on the legacy works filed earlier in this
  contribution whose subject it is: Scheffler 2013, Wade-Benzoni 2006 and
  2012, Zaval et al. 2015, McAdams and de St. Aubin 1992 and Hershfield
  2011. Nothing is removed and primaries are unchanged.
- **Beside the existing tags** on Parfit's *Reasons and Persons* ([LIT-469](../literature.d/LIT-469.md)),
  whose Part IV is the source of the non-identity problem. It takes a
  version bump.
- **Not added** to the terror management works (Burke et al., Castano et
  al., Lifton) or to Pitcher. Their subject is one's own death and its
  aftermath, not people who come after.

## Alternatives

- **Leave the works under `ethics`, `altruism` and `motivation`.** The
  normative and the psychological halves of one question would not meet,
  and `ethics` would hide them among all of moral philosophy.
- **Name the word `longtermism`.** That names one position, the one in
  dispute, and leaves out person-affecting views, intergenerational
  justice and the psychology of legacy. "Future generations" names the
  subject and not a side.
- **Fold it into `altruism`.** Concern for people in the far future is a
  hard case for the owner's thesis, but the obligations question arises
  whatever motivates concern, and much of it (non-identity, discounting,
  population ethics) is not about motive.

## Consequences

- The vocabulary gains a word the owner can rename. A rename is a decision
  that supersedes this one.
- One merged work, [LIT-469](../literature.d/LIT-469.md), takes a version bump for the added tag.
- The legacy question ([QUESTION-tmpruhu4](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpruhu4.md)) and the obligations question
  ([QUESTION-tmpmx9ha](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpmx9ha.md)) share a topic page.
