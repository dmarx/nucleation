# SYNTHESIS-tmplxxfq: Along a chain of reconstructions, per-step discrepancies weighted by later sensitivity bound the departure from a reference transport but give no testable prediction and do not say whether the act survives, which a reorganized constraint or a phase change can decide; approximate transports compose additively only under a triangle inequality and nonexpansive kernels; a chain can forget its start while staying far from the original; and attraction toward conventional stances, filtered by the transmitters' biases, is proposed, with chain evidence for evaluative stance only

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line ("Contextual pragmatic
information transport"): the part on repeated reconstruction chains, drift
bounds and attraction toward conventional regions. The line's account is
built over it and the accounts of the line's other parts. The mechanical
map put 22 entries in this part; five of them (CLAIM-040, CLAIM-093,
CLAIM-094, CLAIM-107 and QUESTION-006) are a second inquiry, conservation
of observables under reconstruction kernels, that barely relates to the
other seventeen, and have their own account (chains-conservation-account).
The counts are under "Whether the part holds together". CLAIM-260 also
carries formal-apparatus; CLAIM-267 and CLAIM-270, in formal-apparatus
only, hold the general results CLAIM-056 and CLAIM-081 apply.*

## The account

- **The bound.** CLAIM-056 (A50 §2–3, kept in the manuscript §8; thesis,
  interpretive, Active): with local discrepancy ε_i and later sensitivity
  L_i, e_n ≤ (∏ L_j) e_0 + Σ ε_i ∏_(j>i) L_j, so "the impact of local
  translation errors depends on the dynamics of subsequent
  interpretation", with three regimes, contractive, neutral and
  expansive. The manuscript adds: "Crucially, the bound quantifies
  departure relative to a reference transport, not loss of every
  communicative virtue." The general recursion is CLAIM-267's (note of
  2026-10-13); what CLAIM-056 adds "is that a serial reconstruction chain
  is a composition of transports measured against a reference transport
  (CLAIM-106)" (note of 2026-10-29).
- **What the bound does not say, conceded.** CLAIM-031 (A75's
  self-critique; granted, interpretive, Active) objects to it: a chain "is
  not simply the accumulation of metric error", and "changing one crucial
  constraint—such as the speaker's alignment with the audience—may change
  the identity of the communicative act even if most other observables
  remain stable." CLAIM-208 (found in the record's audit of the line of
  2026-10-10, empirical lens, "no turn of the exchange raises it"; counter,
  demonstrated, Active) objects to CLAIM-056, CLAIM-059 and CLAIM-078: with
  true constants the recursion "holds for every chain: it is a theorem,
  and no data can contradict it", a sensitivity "is a property of the
  reteller's kernel", which a serial-reproduction chain, giving "one
  retelling of one input at each node", cannot estimate, and no design
  fixes the metric. CLAIM-056's version 3 (2026-10-29) concedes both in its
  title and defeat condition, which now names CLAIM-208's design.
- **A phase change.** CLAIM-114 (A73, recovered; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed), resting on CLAIM-056: drift "can be marked by a pragmatic
  phase change, the first generation at which a different communicative
  configuration dominates, which per-step error in surface content does
  not show". Its body: "CLAIM-031 is the same point made about
  constraints." Its note of 2026-10-29: no chain the record holds has
  per-generation judgements of configuration.
- **Regimes in real chains.** CLAIM-059 (A110 §19, from A50 §3 and A52
  §5.3; thesis, interpretive, Proposed), resting on CLAIM-056, CLAIM-267
  and CLAIM-068: real chains "fall into different regimes under
  conditions assigned in advance", and "amplification needs a metric
  other than total variation, an enlarged state or state-dependent
  dynamics". Its body: "The contracting regime is CLAIM-068; the
  amplifying one needs the enlarged state of CLAIM-049." Version 2
  restated its condition after CLAIM-208, fixing the metric in advance;
  its note of 2026-10-29 says that "no metric is fixed and no condition is
  assigned, so the test it names has nothing yet to test".
- **Composition of approximate transports.** CLAIM-260 (found in the
  audit of 2026-10-10, formal lens; granted, demonstrated, Active; also
  formal-apparatus) objects to CLAIM-098's "category or semigroup": the
  composite of an ε₁-transport and an ε₂-transport is within Lε₁ + ε₂,
  additive when the distortion obeys a triangle inequality and the later
  kernel is nonexpansive, with counterexamples under squared Hellinger
  distance and an expansive kernel. "In total variation both conditions
  hold", and "So the gap is for the other distortions, and CLAIM-059 says
  amplification needs one". It answers QUESTION-293 (split off by the
  status review of 2026-10-27; Answered), which refines QUESTION-017
  (from the tension in A50 between a metric bound and directed
  distortion; Open). QUESTION-293 ends: "What weaker bound holds where the
  additive one fails, and what holds for directed distortion, remain
  QUESTION-017's." CLAIM-056 does not say the bound holds "for directed,
  non-metric distortion (QUESTION-017)", and CLAIM-208 sets its own point
  apart: QUESTION-017 "is a question about the theorem. This objection is
  about measuring its constants."
- **Forgetting the start is not preserving the original.** CLAIM-081 (C1
  Appendix D and A59; thesis, interpretive, Active), resting on CLAIM-056
  and CLAIM-270: retention and drift are different profiles, "so
  convergence is not preservation and transmission is not degradation".
  CASE-017 (E1, run at U23 on hand-chosen matrices; stipulated, executed
  study, Active) is its instance: "The contractive row is the two
  forgettings of CLAIM-081." CASE-017 adds that no run "exercised the
  amplifying regime of CLAIM-056".
- **Attraction toward conventions.** CLAIM-068 (A50 §3 and §6, A52 §5.5
  and §7.4–7.5, recovered; thesis, interpretive, Proposed), resting on
  CLAIM-090 and stated in TERM-027: "Repeated reconstruction is attraction
  toward conventional regions of communicative possibility: pragmatic
  stances, not only lexicon and grammar, can be stabilized by
  transmission." TERM-027 (A110 §20; Active) reads the attractor as "a
  shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object",
  and says "reaching it is evidence of forgetting the start (CLAIM-081)".
  CLAIM-068's body: "E1's contractive chain (CASE-017) is the stipulated
  instance of convergence to a convention (CLAIM-081)". CLAIM-056 does not
  say that drift is random: "A50 went on to attraction (CLAIM-068)", and
  CLAIM-081 leaves which profile matters to "whether the question is about
  the original or about the convention (CLAIM-068)".
- **Filtering by the transmitters' biases.** CLAIM-090 (the manuscript's
  §8; thesis, empirical, Proposed): "Serial transmission is reconstructive
  and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases, so a stable endpoint
  can be far from the ancestral utterance while remaining interpretable."
  Its evidence is Kalish, Griffiths and Lewandowsky's function-learning
  chains, with "The prior was taken from earlier studies rather than
  measured on these learners." ARG-035 (filed by the ADR-061 migration
  from CLAIM-066; source, Proposed) concludes it from Griffiths and Kalish
  and Kirby, Cornish and Smith, who "show different things": "the stable
  endpoint far from the ancestor survives for sampling learners, and
  whether people learn that way is QUESTION-137". QUESTION-137 (split from
  THEORY-155's promotion condition at the owner's direction; Open) asks
  whether human chains converge to an independently measured prior.
  CLAIM-090's and CLAIM-068's notes of 2026-10-29 both say they wait on
  it.
- **The stance extension and its evidence.** ARG-027 (filed by the
  ADR-061 migration from CLAIM-234; evidence, Rejected) concluded CLAIM-068
  from the iterated-learning readings: "None measures an interpersonal
  stance, a footing or a speech-act function along a chain." It is "minor
  for the argument: CLAIM-068 feeds CLAIM-059's regimes". CASE-068
  (two reported diffusion-chain studies found by a search the owner asked
  for; documented, Active) illustrates CLAIM-068 and CLAIM-090: the
  evaluative signal "survived and drifted negative", and the transmitters'
  measured risk perception "predicts the direction of the change". Its
  summary: "Documented chain evidence for evaluative stance; none for
  footing." For CLAIM-090 it says "A clean prior. CLAIM-090 is met only
  partly", and for CLAIM-068 that "Every Jagiello and Hills chain started
  balanced", so convergence from different starts is untested. CLAIM-068's
  note of 2026-10-29 reads CASE-068 so: "an evaluative signal kept and
  amplified, not chains from different stances converging."

What stays open is whether real chains fall into regimes under conditions
fixed in advance (CLAIM-059), whether a configuration flips at a marked
generation (CLAIM-114), what bound holds for directed distortion
(QUESTION-017), whether chains reach a prior measured on their own
transmitters (QUESTION-137), and whether chains started from different
stances converge (CLAIM-068's defeat condition).

## Whether the part holds together

Counting pairs linked by a front-matter relation or a mention in either's
body or summary, the 22 entries the map put here share 46 pairs. The
seventeen here share 35. The conservation five share 9 among themselves
and 2 with the seventeen (CLAIM-107 with CLAIM-059 and with TERM-027), and 7
with other parts (contextuality 3, fidelity 3, kind 1). Within the
seventeen, the bound-and-regimes group (CLAIM-056, CLAIM-031, CLAIM-208,
CLAIM-114, CLAIM-059, CLAIM-260, QUESTION-293, QUESTION-017, CLAIM-081,
CASE-017) shares 16 pairs, the attraction group (CLAIM-068, CLAIM-090,
ARG-027, ARG-035, CASE-068, QUESTION-137, TERM-027) 13, and the two share 6,
so they are kept together. The seventeen share 44 pairs with other parts:
fidelity 14, study 10, act 8, contextuality 4, kind 4, kernels 2, frames 2.
By stored relations only, directed, the 22 have 20 inside, 9 out to other
parts and 4 in. No member plainly belongs to another part.

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **Attraction as the contracting regime in a metric.** That CLAIM-068's
  attraction is CLAIM-059's contracting regime in the metric CLAIM-208 asks
  to be fixed: CLAIM-059 states the identification, and "in the metric"
  is a free parameter, since no metric is fixed.
- **The phase change as the testable remainder.** That, the bound being
  untestable (CLAIM-208) and the act turning on one constraint (CLAIM-031),
  the chain claim left to test is CLAIM-114's phase change: CLAIM-114's
  body and note already state the relation to CLAIM-031 and CLAIM-056,
  and "left to test" absorbs CLAIM-059's and CLAIM-068's own tests.

The one link that passed, that exact conservation of a non-constant
observable keeps information about the start at every generation, joins
this account to the conservation account and is filed at this review as CLAIM-tmp008ey, which that account integrates.

## What it does not say

- That real serial reconstruction is Markov; CLAIM-056's defeat condition
  names the chain whose retellers see earlier generations.
- That any chain the record holds converges toward conventional stances
  from different starts, or ends on a prior measured on its transmitters.
- That footing or any interpersonal stance is stabilized by transmission.
- Anything with standing of its own.
