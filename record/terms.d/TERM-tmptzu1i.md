# TERM-tmptzu1i: frame, as the position a rendering is delivered from

## Definition

U6, the owner: "the main thing that fundamentally distinguishes variations of a
translation is the frame from which it is being delivered" ([CLAIM-tmp1bwsn](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1bwsn.md)). The frame
is where the rendering speaks from: its implied speaker and their stance toward
the audience.

## How it changed

A11 agreed and replaced "delivered" with "reconstructed" ([TERM-tmp36e3d](TERM-tmp36e3d.md)). The owner
put delivery back at U7, and A12 widened the term to hold both ([TERM-tmpx0l4b](TERM-tmpx0l4b.md)).
