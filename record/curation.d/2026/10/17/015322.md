entry of 2026-10-17 00:25) to be filled. Two were named. The PID paper
behind I_min was not held, and neither were its critiques. Every
transfer-entropy number past the two-term split rested on that measure,
and so did the ΦID and emergence readings ([NOTE-010](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-010.md), [NOTE-020](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-020.md), [THEORY-497](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-497.md),

three works were read by two parallel readers. The assistant made every

## What the readings changed

- The record's transfer-entropy examples have the same values under all
  three measures. Bertschinger et al.'s Remark 14 proves the two-term split
  for any bivariate decomposition. The reader's explanation is that in each
  example the target's own past says nothing about its next value.
  [CLAIM-tmpu9ivq](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu9ivq.md) now says so, and is grounded in [THEORY-tmp8ox3d](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp8ox3d.md). Away from
  those examples the split depends on the measure: in Harder, Salge and
  Polani's bystander example it reverses ([THEORY-tmpt4nh7](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpt4nh7.md)).
- [NOTE-010](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-010.md)'s permutation example is checked under I_red: no synergy.
  [THEORY-581](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-581.md) and [NOTE-020](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-020.md), which rest on I_min, point to the critiques.
  Their systems were not recomputed.
- [NOTE-320](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-320.md): Saxe et al.'s JSTAT 2019 Appendix B.3 uses the
  Kozachenko–Leonenko entropy estimator in Kraskov et al.'s Eq. 20 form.
  The public code (ibsgd, iclr2018) holds only KDE and binning, so the k-NN
  result cannot be checked against it.
- [NOTE-828](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-828.md) names the held Kraskov et al. Whether MINE's d = 20 is within
  Kraskov et al.'s tested range is open. Granger (1969) was searched again
  and stays `Unavailable`.

## What remains

  Salge and Polani cite, is not held.
- Every measure here is bivariate. No held work gives ΦID a redundancy
  function with the identity property for more than two sources.
