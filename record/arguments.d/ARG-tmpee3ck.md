# ARG-tmpee3ck: The record's capacity model, extended to two capacities, supports the Luddite objection for capacity and not for dependency

## The inference

Premises: CLAIM-602's model (capacity learned by doing, lost with disuse,
work capped by capacity) and its two-capacity extension (CLAIM-tmp87zug),
in which the substituted capacity's path does not depend on what the
freed effort builds. Conclusion: CLAIM-tmpz33db, that a retained-skill
model cannot carry conclusions about net empowerment.

## Critical questions

1. **Does the formalism apply to the claim's subject?** To the part A4
   states as a model, yes: A4 §9 asks for "a vector" and "a finite
   activity budget", which is the extension. To the trajectories it does
   not model, externalization through infrastructure and emergence in the
   joint organization, no; the extension speaks only to formation.
2. **Does the derivation show what the claim says?** It shows the claim's
   negative half: with reinvestment the party holds a capacity the
   one-capacity model does not represent (0.625 on the second task at a
   supply share of 0.5), so a fall in the modelled capacity does not imply
   a fall in what the party can do.
3. **Does it show more than the claim, or less?** It shows a limit A4 does
   not state. Reinvestment leaves the recovery after withdrawal at 6.38
   units whatever is reinvested, and under a finite budget the new skill
   falls below 0.001 within 30 units of the supply stopping. So the
   extension does not answer the owner's objection where the objection
   bites hardest, on what is lost if the supply goes.

Verdict: Proposed as support for CLAIM-tmpz33db as a claim about the
accounting of capacity. As an answer to QUESTION-tmpsgbj7 about
dependency, the extension adds nothing, and the question's standing on
that half lives in CLAIM-tmp5vitv.
