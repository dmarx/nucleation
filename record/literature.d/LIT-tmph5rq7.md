# LIT-tmph5rq7: Molecular insights into transgenerational non-genetic inheritance of acquired behaviours

Bohacek, J. & Mansuy, I. M. (2015). *Nature Reviews Genetics*, 16(11), 641–652.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract or from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

The proponents' review of the mechanisms. For [QUESTION-tmp8bttd](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp8bttd.md).

## Read in its place

The work cannot be read here, so the record holds it at second hand.

Jawaid and Mansuy 2019 ([LIT-tmpro9qi](LIT-tmpro9qi.md), NOTE-xxxx), by the same senior
author four years later. It cites this review (its ref. 3) as the account
of germline-dependent transmission, of the tests by artificial
insemination, IVF and sperm-RNA injection, and of the MSUS mouse model. It
gives the laboratory's case in short form, updated with 2016–2018 results
on enrichment, sperm RNA and human sperm. The LIT summary agrees with what
the later review shows. Missing still: the 2015 text itself; its treatment
of germline reprogramming and of marks that escape it; its account of DNA
methylation and chromatin as carriers, beside RNA; any criteria it set for
inter- versus transgenerational effects; whether it answered the critics;
and what it said of humans.
