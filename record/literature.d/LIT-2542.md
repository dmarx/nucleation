# LIT-2542: The Vulnerable Subject and the Responsive State

Martha Albertson Fineman (2010). The Vulnerable Subject and the Responsive State. *Emory Law Journal* 60(2), 251–275. A symposium Essay.

## Key takeaways

Read in [NOTE-1899](../notes.d/NOTE-1899.md). Vulnerability is universal and constant; resilience comes from assets institutions confer; the state that constitutes those institutions must monitor them, secure equal access and opportunity, and justify the privilege it confers or tolerates. The state and market owe a share of dependency's burdens because they benefit from care work.
