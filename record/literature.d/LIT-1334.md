# LIT-1334: Causal Consistency of Structural Equation Models

Rubenstein, P. K., Weichwald, S., Bongers, S., Mooij, J. M., Janzing, D., Grosse-Wentrup, M. & Schölkopf, B. (2017). Causal Consistency of Structural Equation Models. *Proceedings of the 33rd Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI 2017)*, reissued as PMLR R15: 151–160. Preprint: arXiv:1707.00819 (4 July 2017).

Read in full ([NOTE-1037](../notes.d/NOTE-1037.md)).

## Why this is here

A25: it "formalizes transformations between models that agree on intervention effects", the first of the three steps (exact transformation, then abstraction, then approximation) A25 gave before saying governance-response sufficiency is not a new general theory of causal abstraction. Bears on [CLAIM-407](../claims.d/CLAIM-407.md) and [CLAIM-409](../claims.d/CLAIM-409.md).
