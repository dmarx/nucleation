## The assistant's contributions

- **[CLAIM-594](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-594.md),** demonstrated. When the resource that pays for crossing
  barriers also sets the costs it pays, as heat does, reaching a target
  within a time budget is a window in the resource, more time widens it,
  and a schedule can reach what no constant level reaches. The record's
  fixed-cost budgeted reachability is the special case. It is corroborated
  by the dissertation's narrow folding window and hysteresis, by
  Wagenbauer et al.'s stepped schedule and by Dannenberg et al.'s
  rate-dependent hysteresis ([THEORY-1440](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1440.md), [THEORY-1443](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1443.md),
  [THEORY-1447](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1447.md)).
- **[CASE-216](../../record/cases.d/CASE-216.md),** its construction: a four-state annealing toy with
  free energies linear in temperature, computed with python3 -I. Within
  one hour the best constant temperature reaches 90.4 % folded and none
  reaches 95 %. Five minutes at 327 K then 318 K reaches 98.5 %.
- **Notes read across the readings,** on [QUESTION-374](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-374.md), [CLAIM-570](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-570.md),
  [CLAIM-572](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-572.md), [CLAIM-577](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-577.md) and [THEORY-1398](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1398.md):
  - probes are not ordered by cost (Wagenbauer et al.);
  - observing can act (Rothemund's AFM);
  - observers coarser than the system are the common case (Dunn et al.'s
    routings, DeLuca et al.'s hybridization states);
  - staple excess is standing slack against spend-matching;
  - a second account of traps (Sobczak's dissertation) beside DeLuca et
    al.'s.
