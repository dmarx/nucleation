# QUESTION-259: How do populations of heterogeneous processes generate, reproduce and transform organizational perspectives, and what further conditions let such a perspective become the object of its own effective governance?

## Why it is a question

A31, after the owner's U31: "How do populations of heterogeneous processes
generate, reproduce, and transform organizational perspectives, and what
additional conditions allow such a perspective to become the object of its
own effective governance?" It proposed this as a successor to
[QUESTION-090](QUESTION-090.md) "without overwriting its historical wording".

[QUESTION-090](QUESTION-090.md) asks one thing: when does a population develop
"a self-maintaining, internally differentiated governance structure that
acts coherently without suppressing the plurality of processes it emerges
from?" This question separates two:

1. **Generation and reproduction.** How a perspective forms and persists,
   which can happen with no governance of itself at all. A31: a
   perspective can be "historically reproductive" without being
   "reflexively self-governing". The exchange's cases are traditions
   reproduced through training ([CASE-130](../cases.d/CASE-130.md)) and orientations that
   change carriers ([CASE-133](../cases.d/CASE-133.md)). Its answer has to say which of
   [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md)'s objects persists: the realized perspective, its
   character, its lineage or its representation.
2. **Reflexive governance.** What more makes the perspective an object of
   its own effective governance. That is [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)'s capacity, and
   [QUESTION-090](QUESTION-090.md)'s "self-maintaining" governance is one form of it.

It refines [QUESTION-090](QUESTION-090.md) by separating the parts, and keeps its condition
that plurality not be suppressed: A40 asks the organization to tell
"appropriate constraint from entrenched exclusion" and treats low overt
conflict as no evidence of either ([CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)).

An answer to part 1 would have to meet A40's test, that the organization
be "characterized independently of the outcome being explained", or the
perspective is "just a new name for whatever happened" ([CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md)). An
answer to part 2 would give conditions under which governing standards
become revisable from within, and show where revision becomes either
capture or regress.

## Note of 2026-10-26: the owner's employees as sub-perspectives

<!-- inactive-ok-file: CLAIM-418 TERM-072 CASE-tmprl1px — Proposed; the claim and term the note sets the owner's wording against, and the reported study, cited as open -->

The owner, at T18 of the exchange "Experimental Infrastructure Review":
"maybe employees are "pre-perspectives" or sub-perspectives, which can
reorganize amongst themselves to form the perspectives that influence the
higher level organizational behavior". That is this question in a firm,
with employees and teams as the heterogeneous population. The reply
narrowed the owner's word: employees "can be full individual interpreters.
They're constituents of candidate higher-level organizational
perspectives". It also added teams as a middle level that is not itself a
perspective: "a relatively persistent arrangement of communication,
shared memory, responsibilities, and decision-making procedures".

The two readings differ on whether a constituent may itself be a
perspective. [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) allows it ("several overlapping, nested, or
partially integrated regimes may exist"), and [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md)'s "one agent can
act through several" is consistent with the owner's wording. The exchange
built a model of it ([CASE-tmprl1px](../cases.d/CASE-tmprl1px.md)), whose analyzer found "candidate
organizational modes" only. How one would know that such a population had
formed a perspective is [QUESTION-tmpixgs8](QUESTION-tmpixgs8.md), which refines this question.
The question is unchanged.
