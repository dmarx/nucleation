# THEORY-tmpee6xr: Amano and Simonov estimate, from the full transaction log of one free-to-play mobile game, that regular players value loot boxes mainly for their help in play while the 1.5% of players who give 90% of revenue get almost all their loot-box value from opening the boxes themselves, and their simulations find that the game's difficulty is set to balance this minority's revenue against the majority's engagement rather than at the revenue-maximizing level the minority's spending would favour.

## The account

Amano and Simonov estimate, from the full transaction log of one free-to-play mobile game, that regular players value loot boxes mainly for their help in play while the 1.5% of players who give 90% of revenue get almost all their loot-box value from opening the boxes themselves, and their simulations find that the game's difficulty is set to balance this minority's revenue against the majority's engagement rather than at the revenue-maximizing level the minority's spending would favour.
