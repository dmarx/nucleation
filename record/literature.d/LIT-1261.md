# LIT-1261: On the Spectral Bias of Neural Networks

Rahaman, N., Baratin, A., Arpit, D., Draxler, F., Lin, M., Hamprecht, F. A., Bengio, Y. & Courville, A. (2019). On the Spectral Bias of Neural Networks. *Proceedings of the 36th International Conference on Machine Learning*, PMLR 97: 5301–5310. Preprint: arXiv:1806.08734 (22 June 2018).

Read in full on 2026-10-20 in [NOTE-983](../notes.d/NOTE-983.md). The proved Fourier bound is
[THEORY-761](../theory.d/THEORY-761.md), and the experimental order of fitting is [THEORY-759](../theory.d/THEORY-759.md).

## Why this is here

A2 listed it as a contrastive reading: an order of acquisition by frequency, which need not be an order by semantic generality. Bears on [QUESTION-217](../questions.d/QUESTION-217.md), beside [THEORY-086](../theory.d/THEORY-086.md). [NOTE-483](../notes.d/NOTE-483.md) and [NOTE-777](../notes.d/NOTE-777.md) cite it without a LIT. The anthology does not hold it (only its [ANTH-NOTE-296](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/notes.d/NOTE-296.md) mentions it), and it is held here for this record's question about orders of acquisition ([ADR-036](../decisions.d/ADR-036.md)), flagged `anthology-candidate`.
