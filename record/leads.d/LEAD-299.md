# LEAD-299: Read the work on heightened meaningfulness under psychedelics, against apophany: meaning attribution, the noetic quality and false insights, relaxed beliefs, and psilocybin as a model of early psychosis

## What was found

All entries are from search results on 2026-11-27 and are unverified.

1. **Meaning attribution.**
   - Preller et al. (2017), "The fabric of meaning and subjective effects in LSD-induced states depend on serotonin 2A receptor activation", Current Biology, doi 10.1016/j.cub.2016.12.030.
   - Songs rated meaningless became personally meaningful under LSD. Ketanserin reduced the effect and fully blocked LSD's general subjective effects. The changes were tied to cortical midline structures.
2. **The noetic quality and false insights.**
   - McGovern et al. (2024), "An integrated theory of false insights and beliefs under psychedelics" (FIBUS), Communications Psychology, doi 10.1038/s44271-024-00120-6, PMC11332244.
   - The felt sense of knowing is James's noetic quality. The paper traces false insights to altered precision weighting, dopaminergic salience and shifts in memory.
3. **Relaxed beliefs.**
   - Carhart-Harris and Friston (2019), "REBUS and the anarchic brain", Pharmacological Reviews 71(3):316-344, doi 10.1124/pr.118.017160. The record holds it only through [LIT-080](../literature.d/LIT-080.md) and [THEORY-571](../theory.d/THEORY-571.md).
   - The 2025 extension, REBAS (revised beliefs after psychedelics), in Scientific Reports, PMC11779827.
4. **Psilocybin as a model of early psychosis.**
   - Vollenweider et al. (1998): psilocybin produces a syndrome resembling first-episode schizophrenia, and ketanserin blocks it.
   - Carhart-Harris et al. (2012), proposing psilocybin as a model of early psychosis (PMC3280661).
   - "LSD, madness and healing", Psychological Medicine: LSD-induced aberrant salience correlated with mystical experience and ego dissolution.
   - A review suggesting the 5-HT2A state models paranoid-type psychoses better than others.

## Note

The psychedelic literature does not usually use Conrad's "apophany". Its
words are "noetic quality" (from the Mystical Experience Questionnaire),
"insightfulness", "meaning attribution" and "aberrant salience". Whether the
altered-states questionnaires have a scale for changed meaning of percepts is
to be checked; the assistant recalls one and could not confirm it.
