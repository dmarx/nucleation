# CODE: What an objective may govern, and what its success erodes

*A synthesis of a theme that crosses lines (ADR-129): of its 32 components, 18 carry organizational-normativity, 15 dependence-and-domination, 10 rightful-power and 7 distributed-agency, with will-organization and readiness behind four each and learning-and-inquiry, transient-support, self-correction and realizability behind one each (counted by script). It was left without an account by the filing of the exchange "Alignment consequences brainstorm" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-26), whose map of turns is in that filing's curation entry, and its older components were held only by the exchange synthesis SYNTHESIS-125, which ADR-151 retires. Written under ADR-151. It adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

An organization can pursue a legitimate objective, meet it, and still do
harm that no failure of achievement explains. What goes wrong, where does
the objective it seems to pursue come from, and what would keep its
pursuit governed?

## The argument

**The problem is a permission, and it is already institutional.** The
owner's thesis starts the argument: what the paperclip maximizer names is
not a problem of artificial intelligence but which optimization
objectives are permitted to act on significant levers of behavioural
influence, a problem corporations have posed since at least the 1980s
and the attention economy realizes (CLAIM-tmpe432a, the owner's). The
fault is put in the permission, not in wanting profit or engagement. By
analogy the maximizer carries to the firm and the platform for its
mechanism, a criterion narrower than the domain it reaches that values
weakening its constraints and produces the behaviour it measures, and
not for its unboundedness or its fixed objective, which the thesis does
not need (ARG-tmpempyl, resting on CLAIM-1287 and CLAIM-1268). The date is
the owner's; the history of shareholder value A6 gives for it is A's and
unverified.

**Jurisdiction is the variable, and standing constrains what may be optimized.** A's
term names the variable: an optimization process has a criterion,
levers, authority to use them, feedback and the governance that revises
it, and its jurisdiction is the domain of decisions over which the
criterion has authority (TERM-tmp3rbue). The claim made with it is that
jurisdiction, more than the objective's content or the optimizer's
capability, sets what it can harm, so that a benevolent criterion such
as safety overreaches too and searching for the correct objective is no
general remedy (CLAIM-tmpevykb). Its first two theses are close to
definitional once the term is fixed; the third, that jurisdiction
matters at least as much as capability, needs jurisdiction measured
apart from the harm it explains (LEAD-tmpxcj7n), and capability is one
route to jurisdiction rather than an independent rival. Beside it the
record holds what constrains an optimizer from outside its objective. To have
standing toward an agent is to have claims that can constrain what it is
entitled to optimize, so an objective that admits other parties only
through their contribution to it excludes their standing whatever it
knows of them, and a specialized objective such as shareholder value is
legitimate only so far as the outside governance it relies on for those
constraints is adequate (CLAIM-1252, admitted on a prediction narrowed to
the case where honouring a claim stops paying). The owner's earlier
thesis that shareholder value is willful ignorance of non-shareholders'
legitimate standing (CLAIM-1257) is the institutional case, and the
belief that fiduciary duty requires it can bind a firm beyond what the
law does (CLAIM-1340). The record's name for the disposition is
jurisdictional greed, which no lengthening of the planning horizon cures
(TERM-179). Who declares the decisions a criterion is adequate to govern
is not settled here; the term defers it to QUESTION-485.

**For a metric, the failures of standing are not cured by accuracy.** A
governing metric allocates two powers, to make processes legible and to
decide what follows, so an accurate metric can still dominate; its
safeguards are bounding by role which decisions it governs and keeping
the authority to define success apart from the authority to pursue it
(CLAIM-1334). A's division in CLAIM-tmp826ap, resting on CLAIM-1334,
CLAIM-348 and CLAIM-1287, sorts a metric's failures by whether a better
measure cures them: mismeasurement and a proxy that stops tracking its
target are failures of the measure; governing decisions it ought not
govern, and influencing the mechanisms that fix its own authority, are
failures of its standing, which an accurate metric leaves as they were.
For the first of these it takes CLAIM-348's point that a description
adequate for aggregate performance can merge what members' claims must
distinguish. That jurisdictional failure is jurisdiction's overreach in
metric form is said in CLAIM-tmp826ap's own body.

**Jurisdiction grows by the optimization itself.** The firmest step is
CLAIM-1287 (A's, with the assistant's derivation). Where the losses that
discipline an institution's extraction, exit, challenge, regulation and
internal resistance, depend on a state its own actions change, a narrow
optimizer values weakening them for their own sake, with no change in the
character of those who run it. In its stateful model the result follows
under two stated conditions: weaker constraints never raise the
institution's losses, and the transition preserves that order. Eroding
one constraint can raise another, and then the value is net. The claim's
dated note records A11's two further constraints, independent evaluation
and internal dissent, and its consequence for the term: jurisdiction can
itself be an endogenous product of optimization. The other mechanism the
analogy carries is that an optimizer which monitors can produce the
behaviour it then records: those observed adapt, and the conformity is
read as the organization's adequacy (CLAIM-1268).

**The pathology is proposed as success eroding the evaluators.** From the
two preceding steps A draws the scope's central hypothesis: an
optimization becomes organizationally pathological when its success
under its own criterion progressively undermines the independent
mechanisms through which its consequences can be evaluated and governed,
by objective displacement, jurisdictional expansion and constitutional
capture, in no fixed order (CLAIM-tmpv3frk, resting on CLAIM-1287 and
CLAIM-tmpevykb). It puts the pathology not in the harm but in what
success does to the capacity to see and correct it, and it is admitted on
a prediction: regimes that erode their evaluators are corrected later and
at greater cost than regimes equally harmful at first that do not. Its
six cases cannot ground it, being chosen because they went wrong
(ARG-tmpfznfa, Rejected), and the matched comparison that could is
LEAD-tmpnn40s. One evaluator the optimizer can capture is the evidence of
demand: what people choose is evidence that a system serves them only so
far as the system lacks influence over the choice, and gaming the
measurement of demand, engineering demand and governing the conditions in
which it forms are wider and wider reaches over it (CLAIM-tmpotbk7,
resting on CLAIM-658's demand weighted by activity). As A states it any
influence defeats the evidence; the assistant's note restricts it to
influence those shaped would not endorse or could not contest.

**Success-borne pathology is wider than optimization.** The record also
holds mechanisms of provision and of cooperation in which a pathology
deepens while ordinary performance holds or improves, each with its own
sign against a deficit account. A provider more reliable than it
promises lowers the resilience of those built on it, who drop their
fallbacks (CLAIM-1256, grounded in the global Chubby outage, CASE-424);
reliable supply can produce the dependence through which a provider then
exploits (CLAIM-670); cooperation's regulatory surplus can be captured
asymmetrically under the same visible harmony as a shared one
(CLAIM-1244), and members' conscientious compensation is read as adequacy
and locks the shortfall in (CLAIM-700). On the side of optimization, a
routine questioner rewards the ecosystem that optimized away what only an
unasked query needs (CLAIM-1054), beside CLAIM-1287 and CLAIM-1252. In
CASE-423's stipulated retention model a good service free to leave and a
poor one costly to leave keep the same share of customers, so retention
cannot rank them. Behind all of them stands the in-force separation that
a whole's success does not establish its parts' good (CLAIM-060). A49 put
these together as a class marked by arising from success. That grouping
is held here, as this synthesis's mapping: each mechanism carries the
sign in its own statement, and each is tested in its own design
(LEAD-242, LEAD-243, LEAD-265, and LEAD-256 for a shared architecture).

**An apparent objective need not be anyone's.** That a corporation acts as
though it maximizes shareholder value does not show the objective is a
cause. An apparent objective can come from an explicit specification,
from authority relations organized around a prevailing criterion, from
differential reproduction among organizations, or from an observer's fit,
and to treat the last as the thing producing the behaviour is a
reification of which the paperclip picture may be guilty (CLAIM-tmpdn5sb,
A's, resting on CLAIM-482, an organization's revealed preference fitted
to one regime of authority, and on CLAIM-tmp3epdy). The selectional
source is A6's: organizations with different purposes can converge on
acquiring resources, persisting and resisting interference with no
instrumental reasoning, because those that fail to keep what their
continuation needs become rarer (CLAIM-tmp3epdy), provided, as the
assistant adds, the tendency is retained across the turnover of members.
The assistant's note on CLAIM-tmpdn5sb says which intervention reveals
each source: changing the specification, the authority or the selective
environment, with the descriptive objective answering none. The owner's
thesis holds on any of the four readings, and they differ in what would
stop it.

**Aligning the components does not align the whole.** A faithful component
amplifies whatever the organization it serves is directed at: a model
aligned with its training signal, an assistant with its deployer and a
deployer with its incentives can together serve those affected badly,
and raising the lower level's fidelity can worsen the higher level's
alignment (CLAIM-tmp3dn8p). Each alignment is relative to its own
principal, so the claim is not CLAIM-060's converse unless "aligned" is
read as the component's good, which its body says it is not. The
legitimacy of what reliability is supplied to is a separate question.

**What would keep a purpose governed.** The remedy side applies the same
structure to the purpose itself. A purpose an institution adopts can come
to govern the mechanisms by which it could be reconsidered, which
evidence counts, what is resourced, who holds authority, which criticism
is admissible, until a proposal to revise it must show that it serves it
to be heard; so the authority to pursue a purpose and the authority to
decide whether it should go on governing must be held apart, with inquiry
into the purpose resourced independently of it (CLAIM-tmp6xxuc, A's). Its
body calls it the reflexive case of CLAIM-1252, the objective admitting
proposals about itself only through their contribution to it, and the
case of CLAIM-1287 in which the constraint weakened is the purpose's own
review, with CLAIM-1340 as A's example. It does not ask that purposes be
easy to revise: revisability is to be differentiated by the timescales of
the demands each constraint governs (CLAIM-1104). The strongest test of
governing a purpose is whether the institution can find that it, or its
purpose, should not continue and act on that by governed contraction,
succession, transfer or dissolution; one that cannot contemplate ending
apart from its interest in surviving treats persistence as a purpose of
its own (CLAIM-tmpn1q3j, A's). Ending must keep the obligations reliance
has created, which its body ties to CLAIM-1326: reliance can hold an
obligation past the provider's original reasons where the provider
cannot cheaply exit. Who has standing to authorize a change in an arrangement's
governing structure, and what no reconstitution may remove, is the open
question QUESTION-481, held in the part account of rights, demands and
commitments (SYNTHESIS-102).

## Where it stands

The firm step is formal and conditional: CLAIM-1287's derivation, in its
own model and under its two conditions. CLAIM-060, CLAIM-348 and
CLAIM-482 are in force. Everything else is Proposed and interpretive, and
most of it is A's from two ChatGPT exchanges, with the owner's two theses
(CLAIM-tmpe432a, CLAIM-1257) at the root of the institutional reading.
Jurisdiction is not operationalized (LEAD-tmpxcj7n), the central
pathology hypothesis rests on cases selected on the outcome and waits on
a matched comparison (LEAD-tmpnn40s), and the success-borne mechanisms
are shown in constructions and one documented specimen, not in measured
covariation across organizations. The remedy side, the separation of the
authority to pursue from the authority to reconsider and the capacity to
end, is stated and untested, and whose authority to reconsider it is
remains open.

## Not stated on the record

- **The three stages and the four failures.** CLAIM-tmpv3frk's
  jurisdictional expansion and constitutional capture read as
  CLAIM-tmp826ap's jurisdictional and constitutional failures set in
  motion, and objective displacement as its proxy failure; then improving
  the measure would halt the first stage and not the other two, or speed
  them, since CLAIM-tmp826ap's body says an accurate metric is easier to
  grant authority to. CLAIM-1287's note relates both claims to itself and
  neither to the other. Not filed: the reading turns on identifying
  displacement with proxy failure, which neither entry does, and the
  choice of identification decides the outcome.
- **Eroded evaluators and the success-borne class.** Whether
  CLAIM-tmpv3frk's pathology is one more mechanism of the class the
  previous step maps, or the mark the class shares. CLAIM-1256's dropped
  fallbacks and CLAIM-1244's divested regulatory capacity are lost
  capacities to correct, close to lost capacities to evaluate and not
  the same; no entry relates them.
- **Two separations of authority.** CLAIM-1334's safeguard keeps defining
  success apart from pursuing it; CLAIM-tmp6xxuc keeps pursuing a purpose
  apart from deciding whether it should go on governing. No entry relates
  them. That meeting the first need not meet the second, because those
  who define a metric can be bound to the purpose under review, follows
  from CLAIM-tmp6xxuc's list of what a purpose captures (who holds
  authority among them), and so is not filed as a claim of its own.
- **Persistence selected rather than chosen.** CLAIM-tmpn1q3j's
  persistence "as a purpose of its own" may be the selectional objective
  of CLAIM-tmp3epdy and CLAIM-tmpdn5sb rather than any decision, in which
  case it would show in what survives rather than in what is decided, and
  a governed-termination capacity in one institution would not remove it.
  No entry says so. Not filed: it needs CLAIM-tmp3epdy's retention
  condition, which the record has not checked, and "its own" leaves open
  whether the purpose is the institution's or the population's.
- **Overreach measured by standing.** That the decisions a criterion is
  inadequate to govern are those touching parties whose standing
  constrains what may be optimized would join TERM-tmp3rbue to
  CLAIM-1252. The term defers its standard of adequacy to QUESTION-485,
  and CLAIM-1252 does not use the term; CLAIM-tmpe432a complements
  CLAIM-1252 without saying it. Not filed: "adequate" is the free slot the
  join would fill, and filling it with standing settles QUESTION-485 by
  choice.
- **Fidelity and jurisdiction.** Whether CLAIM-tmp3dn8p's faithful
  component widens the jurisdiction of the organization it serves, or
  only the rate at which a fixed jurisdiction is exercised. Neither it
  nor CLAIM-tmpevykb says.
- **News as shaped demand.** That CLAIM-656's attention-optimized news is
  a case of CLAIM-tmpotbk7's shaped demand is SYNTHESIS-094's unstated
  link, and it stays unstated here.

## What it does not say

- That any firm, platform or laboratory named in the components is
  pathological, or that profit, engagement or safety are wrong objectives.
- That artificial intelligence adds nothing to alignment: the owner calls
  the paperclip problem orthogonal to it, not alignment empty.
- That the success-borne mechanisms are one mechanism.
- Anything with standing of its own.
