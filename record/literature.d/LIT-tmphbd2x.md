# LIT-tmphbd2x: Vortex-line models for dual strings

Nielsen, H. B. & Olesen, P. (1973). Vortex-line models for dual strings. *Nuclear Physics B* 61: 45–61. doi:10.1016/0550-3213(73)90350-7.

## Key takeaways

*From the abstract only; the body is unread.*

- "We call attention to the possibility of constructing field theories for
  the dual string."
- "a Higgs type of Lagrangian allows for vortex-line solutions, in analogy
  with the vortex lines in a type II superconductor. These vortex lines can
  approximately be identified with the Nambu string."
- "In the strong coupling limit we speculate that the vortex lines make up
  all low energy phenomena", a limit that is "'super quantum mechanical'"
  because the typical action is small next to Planck's constant. The
  exchange leaves this point out.

## Standing in the record

Filed from the exchange of 2026-11-15 on solitons in a continuous medium.
The abstract confirms the exchange's main point: vortex lines of a Higgs
field, on the superconductor analogy, approximately identified with the
string. The rest of the exchange's account is not confirmed by anything
read, or is wrong in its attribution:

- the Lagrangian's exact form, the flux quantum Φ = 2πn/e and the radial
  equations are unverified for this paper;
- the claim that the paper treats Nambu–Goto as the vortex's "leading
  effective action" is later effective-string language. Czubak & Jerrard
  say Nielsen and Olesen "conjectured a relationship" and "argued" it;
- the Bogomolny bound is Bogomol'nyi's (1976), as the neighbour attributes
  it.

How far the neighbour stands in:

- **Czubak & Jerrard (2015)**, [LIT-tmp0jm6f](LIT-tmp0jm6f.md), read in part. It reports the
  conjecture: vortex lines "should sweep out worldsheets that are
  approximately governed by the Nambu-Goto action". It gives the abelian
  Higgs setting, the quantized vorticity, the sectors and the Bogomol'nyi
  identity in its own normalization. It proves the scenario wherever the
  Jaffe–Taubes conjecture holds.
- **Not carried by anything read**: the paper's own equations, its
  superconductor argument and its strong-coupling argument.
