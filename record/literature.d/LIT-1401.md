## Why this is here

The exchange cited it as the reverse of the favour: interdependence as a lever of coercion when the network is asymmetric. The abstract agrees. It bears most on [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md), which holds that an organization can be governed from outside through what its procedures receive: the panopticon and chokepoint effects are that, between states.
