**What went wrong.** The new step 3a ("what the record has already
done") ran on Part II through the digests, and the digest
`affect-and-authority.md` still missed held work. The review of
2026-11-25 had to name it: [CASE-382](../../record/cases.d/CASE-382.md)'s pricing of accommodation, [LEAD-141](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-141.md)'s
reading of the person-level test, [CLAIM-758](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-758.md)'s six readings and [CLAIM-552](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-552.md)'s
note. A digest searched by the article's terms finds entries filed under
those terms. It does not find a result filed under its mechanism's name.
Step 3a needs its own search of executed studies and readings by
mechanism, not one that rides on the digest.

**A wrong count that propagated.** The review's Part B said 19 joins.
The account [SYNTHESIS-125](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-125.md) and the README took that number over, but
the review's own table has 17 rows. Only the fact-check counted the
labels. A tally copied from a summary should be recounted against the
table it summarizes.

**Going right.** Four revisers worked in parallel, one per file, and each
re-checked its changes against the entries. That left one inconsistency
between files: Articles 5 and 6 each took the other's reading of the
owner's "handle the fixes for the twelve". The lead settled it by the
reading the entries' histories already use. The independent fact-check
then found 17 problems in roughly 940 quotations and 1,800 standings.
None of them changed an argument.
