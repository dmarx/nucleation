**One account at a time, seven of eight are bridges.** Each principle's question is held by a line its components already sit in, and the account carries it across to the others. Only P5's question, how a system comes to learn, is held by no line.

**Taken together, they indicate a candidate line.** The question the eight answer jointly, with P1 and P3 (CLAIM-535, CLAIM-402) and the one narrowed part of the predicted cycle (CLAIM-tmpzrl0a), is how any system draws distinctions, learns and inquires. No line's blurb names it: realizability, readiness, will-organization and record-as-apparatus each hold one part. It recurs: the manuscript's Part I is built on it, and the eight accounts were filed together for it. And it is found today only through the manuscript, not through the record's own lines. All three questions are answered yes for the set.

**The candidate, for the owner.**

- **Name:** `learning-and-inquiry`.
- **Blurb (draft):** how a system that draws distinctions comes to learn and to inquire. Distinctions are relative to a target and a budget. Learning is the formation of a maintained capacity, a distinction or a channel, occasioned and not guaranteed by a discrepancy it cannot resolve. Inquiry is specification that can revise its own language, answers and criteria. Kept apart from what a record of inquiry secures (records-of-inquiry) and from the record as its own apparatus (record-as-apparatus).
- **It would gather:** the eight accounts above, as a line account over them; CLAIM-535, CLAIM-402, CLAIM-tmpzrl0a; and the claims behind P5 and P8 that no other line holds as its own question.
- **It would take from existing lines:** "inquiry as specification", now in record-as-apparatus's blurb. The claims would keep their present lines as well, since `line` is many-valued.

Adding the line is a decision about the `lines` vocabulary, for the owner (ADR-082). Nothing is changed here.
