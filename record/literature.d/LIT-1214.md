# LIT-1214: Some constraints and tradeoffs in the design of network communications

Akkoyunlu, E. A., Ekanadham, K. & Huber, R. V. (1975). Some constraints and tradeoffs in the design of network communications. In *Proceedings of the Fifth ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles (SOSP '75)*, pp. 67–74. doi:10.1145/800213.806523.

Not read: the lawful copy located on 2026-10-29 could not be fetched
(see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is
standardly cited for. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

The owner raised the problem at U36 of the exchange ("Maybe also cryptography? Reminds me of the two generals problem."), and A36 used it without a source: "Under two generals, agents may agree on what they want but cannot obtain the knowledge necessary to guarantee coordination." Bears on [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md), as the canonical case of the epistemic obstruction that claim separates from feasibility and aggregation.
