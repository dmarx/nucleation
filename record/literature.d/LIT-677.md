# LIT-677: Unveiling mode-connectivity of the ELBO landscape

Edith Zhang and David Blei (2021), Bayesian Deep Learning workshop, NeurIPS 2021 — https://bayesiandeeplearning.org/2021/papers/3.pdf

## Key takeaways

- **The claim.** Local optima of the ELBO for latent Dirichlet allocation,
  fitted with stochastic variational inference, are not isolated: two optima
  from different random initialisations are connected by a path along which
  the ELBO stays essentially at its optimal value. The optima "lie on a
  connected subset in parameter space" (abstract, §1.1).
- **The method.** The simplified string method of E, Ren and Vanden-Eijnden,
  built to find transition paths in chemistry: 15 beads on the line between
  two optima, each taking a natural-gradient (batch VI) step, then
  re-spaced evenly along the string, repeated to convergence (§2, App. C).
- **The explanation.** Over-parameterisation, with more topics K than the
  corpus has, lets a reassignment of one word be absorbed by small shifts in
  the topics, as with neurons in an over-fitted network (§3). Beyond that,
  "continuous statistical degeneracy", a connected set of optimal
  parameters, would produce near-optimal paths even in an under-parameterised
  model (§1).

## Standing in the record

Filed with the owner's batch on mode connectivity and model merging, under
`loss-landscapes` ([ADR-027](../decisions.d/ADR-027.md)), with `probabilistic-modeling` for its subject:
variational inference on a topic model, outside deep learning. The
anthology flag follows [ADR-027](../decisions.d/ADR-027.md)'s batch rule; of the nine works in this
filing, it is the one least likely to belong there.

`Proposed`, not `Active`. The paper says its experiments show near-optimal
paths, flattening with data size and K, and paths in an under-parameterised
model. None of these results appears in it. The only figure is a schematic of
two topic sets, §1 refers to an "experimental section" that the paper does
not have, and App. C's loop says "repeat steps 3 and 4" where steps 2 and 3
are meant.
Its value now is the conjecture and the method, transplanted from chemistry
by way of Draxler et al. ([LIT-653](LIT-653.md)), whose method it says it resembles.
It sets its theoretical discussion closest to Kuditipudi et al.
([LIT-671](LIT-671.md)), on over-parameterisation and resilience. It builds on
neither and measures neither, so no relation is declared.

"Statistical degeneracy" is the record's singular-model question in
another vocabulary: Watanabe's singular learning theory ([LIT-354](LIT-354.md), unread)
is the frame in which non-identifiable models have connected sets of
optima, and the batch's paper on generative and contrastive models
([LIT-660](LIT-660.md)) cites it for the same intuition. Neither paper connects the
two.
