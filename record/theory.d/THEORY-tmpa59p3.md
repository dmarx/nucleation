# THEORY-tmpa59p3: Feenberg argues that technology is neither neutral nor determined, that its design embodies a contingent technical code reflecting the values of dominant groups, that technological development is a field of social struggle with many feasible outcomes, and that an economic code that rewards deskilling could be replaced by one that values skill

## The account

Feenberg argues that "Technological development is a scene of social struggle" over "the variety of possible technologies and paths of progress among which we must choose"; that "The idea that industrial technology is irredeemable is essentially determinist"; and that the capitalist economic code "rewards deskilling".
