# SYNTHESIS-062: A frame acts on an interpreter, and an interpreter's judgements act back on what it observes: order dependence among framing operations shows that the operations do not commute, not that the probability is nonclassical; conditioning on a situation, changing it, telling an interpreter about it and eliciting a judgement are different operations, and only changing the situation intervenes on what would constitute an act; a judgement reshapes the observer whose later observations it judges, objected to as action relocated and as a loop that confirms itself, and what keeps that loop honest is evidence that does not pass through the interpretation it tests, a condition the record carries over to when a category's success warrants a kind, beside an account of how a contested label can be reproduced by rival use while whether it carries one concept stays open

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line. The line's account is
built over it and its sibling parts. The part holds three groups, joined
mostly through the judgement–observation loop: the frame and its
operations, from the exchange that led to the manuscript and its do()
refinement (2026-10-08 to 10); the loop and its objections, from the
exchange of 2026-10-16 and the claims it fed; and the contested label
"anti-vax", from the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and
Disposition" (2026-11-06 to 07). [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md), [CLAIM-337](../claims.d/CLAIM-337.md), [CLAIM-338](../claims.d/CLAIM-338.md),
[CLAIM-339](../claims.d/CLAIM-339.md), [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) and [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) are first in distributed-agency, whose
accounts are pending; [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md) and [CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md) are first in
will-organization, and [CASE-226](../cases.d/CASE-226.md) in learned-structure. [SYNTHESIS-025](SYNTHESIS-025.md)
(learned-structure) integrates [CASE-226](../cases.d/CASE-226.md), [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md), [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md) and
[QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md); [SYNTHESIS-036](SYNTHESIS-036.md) (will-organization) integrates [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md),
[CASE-230](../cases.d/CASE-230.md), [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) and [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md); [SYNTHESIS-041](SYNTHESIS-041.md) (will-organization)
integrates [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) and [CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md). This account integrates them again
because their `line` includes pragmatic-transport.*

## The account

- **A frame as an operation, and what its order shows.** [TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md) (A30's,
  Active): "Applying one frame can transform the state on which
  subsequent frames operate." [CLAIM-052](../claims.d/CLAIM-052.md) (A30's H1, what the owner's U13
  claim became, interpretive, Proposed): "Interpretive framing operations
  generally do not commute", and "This can be investigated without
  committing to quantum probability"; its note of 2026-10-29: "The record
  holds no observation of framing-order effects on pragmatic judgements".
  The owner's [CLAIM-073](../claims.d/CLAIM-073.md) (Rejected) took noncommutativity to rule out one
  ordinary joint distribution: "I think we get that via demonstrating
  non-commutativity." A30's [CASE-001](../cases.d/CASE-001.md) (stipulated construction, Active)
  refutes it: "AB = A and BA = B, so AB ≠ BA, and every state is an
  ordinary probability vector." [TERM-024](../terms.d/TERM-024.md) (A30's disambiguation, Active)
  keeps the senses apart: "observing order dependence is sufficient to
  establish noncommutativity of the effective operations, but not
  sufficient to establish nonclassical probability", and records A40's
  third sense, order dependence in observed responses, which "may arise
  from framing effects, memory, learning, task demands, or measurement
  disturbance". [CLAIM-041](../claims.d/CLAIM-041.md) (built over A21 to A30, granted, demonstrated,
  Active): context-sensitive interpretation, order effects and
  noncommuting framing operations "do not by themselves establish
  nonclassical probability or quantum contextuality", with the
  quantum-cognition readings pointing the same way. [ARG-002](../arguments.d/ARG-002.md) (A21's
  analogy, Rejected) read framings as non-commuting measurements that
  make a meaning actual; [CLAIM-041](../claims.d/CLAIM-041.md) undercuts it. [CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md) (A21 and A24's
  proposed factorial study, Active, not run) would cross wording,
  attributed speaker and judgement order.
- **Five things "frame" was split into.** [TERM-031](../terms.d/TERM-031.md) (A24's, Active): a
  frame as a measurement context, the situation tuple indexing judgement
  distributions; "a context indexes a distribution, an operator changes a
  state". [TERM-030](../terms.d/TERM-030.md) (A38 and A39's, Active) extends it to "a configuration
  of relevant social and interpretive conditions", and says it is "Not an
  act on a reader". [TERM-018](../terms.d/TERM-018.md) (A33 and A39's, Active): a framing
  intervention is "an action that changes the conditions under which an
  utterance is interpreted"; "Telling a reader that the speaker is a
  friend is an intervention." [TERM-011](../terms.d/TERM-011.md) (A44 to A48's, Active) adds the
  middle term, a frame the interpreter infers from evidence, P(z | c):
  "Being told that a speaker is a friend supplies evidence about the
  situation", and it is "Not the frame that obtains ([TERM-030](../terms.d/TERM-030.md)), nor an
  intervention ([TERM-018](../terms.d/TERM-018.md))". [TERM-039](../terms.d/TERM-039.md) (A125 and A129's, Active): the
  elicitation protocol, which in A129's model "enters the equation for the
  judgement and not the update of the interpreter's state"; whether
  eliciting is also an intervention "is open, and the exchange answers it
  both ways", and "Sequential designs, where one judgement is elicited
  before another, separate the two readings." [TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md) and [TERM-031](../terms.d/TERM-031.md) carry
  `superseded_by` ([TERM-018](../terms.d/TERM-018.md), [TERM-030](../terms.d/TERM-030.md)) and stay Active, each saying why in
  "Why it is still Active".
- **Three operations, and what a cue can show.** [CLAIM-127](../claims.d/CLAIM-127.md) (the owner's
  do() move at U41, formalized at A129, interpretive, Proposed):
  conditioning on a situation, intervening on it and intervening on what
  the interpreter is told are three operations, so a framing intervention
  indexes the empirical model, e_C^a = P(Y_C | do(a)), rather than
  entering its cover; A129 "derives the framing kernel instead of positing
  it", which "gives [TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md)'s operators and [CLAIM-052](../claims.d/CLAIM-052.md)'s noncommutativity
  ([TERM-024](../terms.d/TERM-024.md)) a causal reading". Its note of 2026-10-30: "The defeat
  condition's second branch appears met for the manuscript's
  experiments", and "What the do-index still separates is the frame
  entered as separate contexts of one cover, which turns a framing effect
  into signalling." [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md) (the owner's U41 and A129 §1's distinction,
  interpretive, Active): do(I = a) and do(S = s) act on different
  variables, "only a do(S) intervenes on the relations that would
  constitute an act", and "In a vignette or rating study every
  manipulation is a change in what the reader is told." It answers
  [QUESTION-296](../questions.d/QUESTION-296.md) (split from [QUESTION-030](../questions.d/QUESTION-030.md) by the status review of
  2026-10-28, Answered): "No: telling an interpreter about the situation,
  do(I), acts on a different variable from changing the situation, do(S)."
  [ARG-012](../arguments.d/ARG-012.md) (filed by the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration from [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md), evidence,
  Rejected) read [CASE-021](../cases.d/CASE-021.md)'s speaker factor as evidence that an act is
  partly constituted by its relation to the speaker; [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md) undercuts
  it: "Telling readers who spoke is a do(I), not a do(S), so such an
  effect is evidence about interpretation under instruction."
- **The loop.** [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) (the owner's, U1 of 2026-10-16, stated in
  [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)'s terms in A4's diachronic form, interpretive, Active): the
  loop that keeps a lucid agent's model in touch runs between judgement
  and observation. "A judgement need not produce the observation it
  judges. It changes the observer, which produces the later
  observations." It reads the query as "the judgement's measurement
  context" and the judgement as acting on "the canvas and the retrieval
  policy ([TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md), [CLAIM-275](../claims.d/CLAIM-275.md))". It holds "the agent's main influence on
  what it observes is an intervention on itself, the observer, made by
  judging", and does not claim the synchronic form: "the record demoted it
  once before" ([CLAIM-109](../claims.d/CLAIM-109.md), through [ARG-002](../arguments.d/ARG-002.md) and [CLAIM-041](../claims.d/CLAIM-041.md)).
- **Two objections and the answer.** [CLAIM-337](../claims.d/CLAIM-337.md) (the assistant's, A1 and
  A4, counter, Proposed): the loop is active inference's action and
  perception one Markov blanket inward; "Cutting action out" "draws the
  blanket around the upper level and calls what crosses it judgement".
  Its own "What it does not say": the redescription "concedes [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md)'s
  point that action on the world is not part of it". [CLAIM-339](../claims.d/CLAIM-339.md) (the
  assistant's, A1 narrowed at A4, counter, Active): a loop cut off from
  action loses one route for evidence it did not select; conceded, "A
  loop that selects its own evidence can confirm itself with or without
  action ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md))", and "On its own terms, action is one more way a
  loop selects its own evidence." [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) (the assistant's synthesis of
  A3 and A4, filed at the owner's request, Active) answers it: what keeps
  the loop from confirming itself is evidence "whose arrival or scoring
  does not pass through the interpretation it tests"; "Action on the
  world is one source of such evidence: it brings in what the agent did
  not ask for." It limits itself: "the independent channel anchors it
  only as far as it outweighs the selected one".
- **Selective sampling as authority.** [CLAIM-338](../claims.d/CLAIM-338.md) (the owner's, U10,
  worked out by A10, Proposed): Friston's "sampling selectively what one
  expects to experience" is played in the coupled model by the authority
  space, "the β arrow of [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)". "It is [CLAIM-337](../claims.d/CLAIM-337.md)'s move run in the
  other direction." "Both say the two are formally alike. They disagree
  about which description is primary." It holds only if the agent's store
  can surprise it: "The mapping needs the store to play the world's
  part".
- **One model of two lines.** [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) (the owner's thesis, U2; the
  mapping the assistant's, A6, endorsed at U8; Proposed): the
  pragmatic-transport and will-organization lines are "the outside and
  inside views of one coupled meaning–influence operator model", meeting
  at [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md)'s loop; its table puts the frame ([TERM-031](../terms.d/TERM-031.md), [TERM-039](../terms.d/TERM-039.md)) at
  the query and the interpretive state ([TERM-026](../terms.d/TERM-026.md)) at the meaning state.
  It "says the lines share one model, not that either line's results
  transfer to the other unchanged." The lab's v2.9, the one version with
  frames, operators and a governor, "reduces to a feature selector".
  [ARG-065](../arguments.d/ARG-065.md) (A55 §7's abduction, judged by the reader, Proposed) would
  conclude [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) and "carries no weight for [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) before" its
  test is run; A: "that final may is the unresolved point".
- **Three standards, and what only intervention separates.** [CLAIM-450](../claims.d/CLAIM-450.md)
  (the owner's, 2026-10-24, interpretive, Active): "So statistical
  distinguishability ≠ usability by a receiver ≠ pragmatic efficacy", and
  where response models are singular a missing response can come from an
  absent attempt or an unresponsive receiver. [CASE-152](../cases.d/CASE-152.md) (the construction
  from the exchange of 2026-10-24, stipulated, Active): Y ∼ N(ab, 1), and
  "An intervention on the receiver separates what no number of ordinary
  observations does." [CLAIM-336](../claims.d/CLAIM-336.md)'s note of 2026-10-24: "The connection is
  filed as [CLAIM-450](../claims.d/CLAIM-450.md)". [CLAIM-450](../claims.d/CLAIM-450.md)'s note of 2026-10-25 records A31's test
  "with actual and told role crossed ([CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md))".
- **From the loop to the warrant for a kind.** [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) (A27 §1's, on
  the owner's U27, Active), resting on [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md): predictive success
  across contexts warrants a real effective kind only when the evidence
  does not pass through the category's own use, or that use is modelled.
  Its summary: "[CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s condition on evidence, moved from a
  judgement–observation loop to the warrant for a kind", and "it defeats
  one inference and is not a criterion of kindhood".
- **A contested label.** [CASE-222](../cases.d/CASE-222.md) (the owner's case, A41's analysis, a
  documented specimen on unread sources, Proposed): "The label's
  stability is not evidence that the extension or inferential role of the
  concept is stable." [CLAIM-621](../claims.d/CLAIM-621.md) (A41 §5's, Proposed; "Weak: it may be
  [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) restated"): "The symbol's reproduction does not require
  semantic consensus", so a durable dispute shows a durable relation
  among uses, not one concept. [QUESTION-439](../questions.d/QUESTION-439.md) (the owner's U19, A41's
  options, Open) asks whether rival uses are one concept, several, or an
  organization of the rivalry, and holds that "A difference in U alone
  does not decide for option 2 ([TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md))". [CASE-226](../cases.d/CASE-226.md) (A50 §7's design,
  third arm A55 §4's, Proposed): two communities agreeing in present use,
  one revising and one not; "the experiment could distinguish conceptual
  agreement from similarity of the organization that maintains and
  revises concepts", and its third arm "tells a difference in what a
  community can see from a difference in who may act on it". [CASE-230](../cases.d/CASE-230.md)
  (A41 §12's and A55 §5's design, Proposed) crosses the label's presence,
  the classifier's authority and a chance to reconsider; under "What it
  cannot show": "telling participants about cases is a do(I) intervention
  in [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md)'s sense". [CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md) (A58 §3's, demonstrated, Proposed):
  two organizations can each be minimally adequate while drawing
