# ADR-065: A reading's promote_when asks only for what its title asserts

## Context

An Active THEORY asserts its title: the THEORY statuses say whether the
record believes the account, and [ADR-059](ADR-059.md) makes every status credence in
what the entry itself asserts. So a promote_when should ask for whatever
would warrant that assertion, and no more.

[ADR-047](ADR-047.md)'s section "What one study counts for" (2026-10-19) said so for one
case. An empirical THEORY may be promoted on one study read in full, when
its title keeps that study's hedges and nothing contradicts it. A demand in
its promote_when for replication moves to a QUESTION. [ADR-047](ADR-047.md) is
Superseded and [ADR-057](ADR-057.md) carries its rulings forward, so the rule now lives
in a superseded document, worded as a step of a promotion audit. Intake
did not apply it. Readings were filed Proposed with conditions copied from
the general case. On 2026-10-30, 462 of the 597 Proposed THEORYs were still
at version 1, and the worklist surfaces only entries something relies on.

A survey on 2026-10-30 classified every Proposed THEORY's promote_when.
Its scripts and data are in the session's scratchpad (pw/), and its counts
are estimates from keyword flags, with the cases below read by hand.

- **Most conditions are right.** Roughly 85–90% of titles assert a general
  claim. For those, a study nobody has run, a replication, or a specific
  further work is what would warrant it, and a reading cannot supply it.
  That is the difference between reading a source and believing it.
- **About 58 ask for more than their titles assert.**
  - **21 one-study titles keep the study's own hedges** and still ask for
    replication, or for the very thing the title concedes. [THEORY-329](../theory.d/THEORY-329.md)'s
    title says "that anger causes the spread is not shown". Its condition
    asks for a randomized study.
  - **19 attribution titles ("X proposes", "On X's analysis") ask that X be
    right.** One example is [THEORY-1084](../theory.d/THEORY-1084.md). The endosymbiosis batch filed on
    2026-10-27 is another. [THEORY-1110](../theory.d/THEORY-1110.md) already shows the right shape: "the
    authors propose" is Active once read, and whether they are right stays
    with [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md).
  - **About 10 proofs say the condition is a checked re-derivation already
    recorded in the file.** [THEORY-924](../theory.d/THEORY-924.md) is one. Three of them also ask for
    the version of record, with no discrepancy named ([THEORY-943](../theory.d/THEORY-943.md)).
  - **8 need their titles narrowed first.**
- **Demands for the version of record split.** Where a discrepancy between
  versions is named, the demand is justified ([THEORY-931](../theory.d/THEORY-931.md)'s inconsistent
  table). Where none is, and the copy read is the author's version of the
  same work, it asks for nothing the title needs.

## Decision

1. **A promote_when asks for exactly what the title asserts.** This holds
   at filing and at every review.
2. **A reported title goes Active on its reading.** That is a title that
   says what a source proposes, argues or finds, or what one study
   measured with the study's own hedges kept. It goes Active once a NOTE
   reads the source in full and nothing in the record contradicts the
   report. Its promote_when says so.
3. **What a condition asked beyond the title is kept, not deleted.** It
   moves by [ADR-047](ADR-047.md)'s routing:
   - A request to replicate or test the same claim independently moves to
     a QUESTION.
   - A wider thesis the source asserts moves to a Proposed THEORY that
     `extends` the reported entry. That THEORY keeps the old condition word
     for word ([ADR-047](ADR-047.md) kind 1).
   - The record's own extrapolation moves to an interpretive CLAIM (kind 2).
4. **The version of record is asked for only where a discrepancy is
   named.** That means a table, a sign or an omitted proof that the copy
   read may get wrong. Otherwise the NOTE records which copy was read, and
   that is enough.
5. **A proof condition is met by a re-derivation recorded in the file, once
   a second checker has gone through it.** The second checker follows each
   step and re-runs any script. A summary saying it was checked is not
   enough ([ADR-047](ADR-047.md), "A summary that says it was checked is not enough").
6. **A general title keeps its strict condition.** A title that asserts an
   empirical generalization, a causal claim or a theory's truth keeps its
   demand for evidence beyond the source. Nothing here lets one
   unreplicated study promote a general claim.
7. **A condition that disputes a study's internal validity stays.** It is
   not a demand for generality. Such an entry is narrowed to "X report…"
   only when that frees something (the status-review README, §2).

The status review applies this to the entries the survey found, in the
same pull request that files this decision. Intake applies it from now on.

## Alternatives considered

- **Loosen promote_when across the board, so that a reading promotes its
  entry.** Rejected. For a general title that would make Active mean
  "read", which is what Proposed already means for a reading. The record
  would lose the difference between holding a source and believing it, and
  it would promote empirical generalizations on one study, which [ADR-047](ADR-047.md)'s
  one-study rule forbids.
- **Rewrite only the conditions, without filing the wider theses beside the
  reported entries.** Rejected. [ADR-047](ADR-047.md) v4 was written because narrowing
  had been losing the wider claims. An attribution promoted without its
  "P" beside it would read as if the record held P.
- **Status quo: leave the rule in [ADR-047](ADR-047.md) and apply it at review.**
  Rejected. Intake did not apply it, and review reaches only entries
  something relies on, so most such entries would stay misstated
  indefinitely.
- **Put each case to the owner.** Rejected, as [ADR-047](ADR-047.md) rejected it. The
  owner delegated these rulings to the status review (2026-10-29), and the
  rule makes the cases mechanical.

## Consequences

- **About 50 readings become promotable under this rule.** Each still needs
  a reviewer to confirm that its hedges are kept and that nothing
  contradicts it. Each attribution promoted gets its wider thesis filed
  beside it, Proposed. Almost nothing relies on these entries; the
  worklist's largest reach is 1. So the gain is statuses that say what the
  record believes, not freed dependents.
- **The risk is reading "On X's analysis, P" as P.** The paired Proposed
  entry carries P. Where a reported title also carries the record's own
  critique, an Active status asserts that critique too, and the reviewer
  checks it.
- **Filing gets stricter about wording.** A reading whose title reports a
  source must say so in the title, and its promote_when must match. A
  reading whose title is general keeps a condition the reading cannot meet.
