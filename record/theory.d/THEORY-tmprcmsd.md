# THEORY-tmprcmsd: Bragg et al. (2025) report that across 57 agents on AstaBench's 2,400+ problems, scored with standardized date-restricted literature tools and cost accounting, literature understanding is fairly well handled

## The account

Bragg et al. (2025) report that across 57 agents on AstaBench's 2,400+ problems, scored with standardized date-restricted literature tools and cost accounting, literature understanding is fairly well handled. Coding, data analysis and end-to-end discovery are not: the best data-analysis score is 34%, and no agent completes all steps of an end-to-end experiment more than about 5% of the time. The authors hold that agent comparisons must control for compute cost and tool access.
