# NOTE-tmpuvcpx: Risk management in a dynamic society: a modelling problem

## Contribution

It moved safety analysis from errors and task sequences to the mechanisms
that shape behaviour: constraints, boundaries and the gradients that move
activity within them. It also framed risk management as control
distributed across government, regulators, companies, managers and
operators.

## Key insight

Nobody has to make a mistake for an organization to drift into an
accident. Everyone adapting sensibly to the pressures they face will move
the system toward its boundary, and redundancy hides how close it is.

## Assumptions

- Work leaves many degrees of freedom, closed by adaptive search under
  local criteria (effort, cost, risk, interest).
- Boundaries of acceptable performance exist and can in principle be
  identified for a given hazard source.

## Key results

Conceptual, drawn from accident analyses (Zeebrügge, Bhopal, Flixborough,
Chernobyl, and ferry and tanker cases):

- Migration toward the boundary under cost and effort gradients (Figure 3).
- Systematic degeneration of defence in depth under cost pressure.
- The AcciMap: mapping the decision makers whose normal decisions
  together prepared an accident (Figures 5–6).
- Control requirements per hazard source: objectives, information on the
  actual state, competence, commitment.
- A taxonomy of hazards by frequency and magnitude, with different
  management strategies for each (Figure 7).

## Concepts

- **boundary of acceptable performance** — the edge of the work space set by
  functional, administrative and safety constraints.
- **gradient** — the steady pressure (cost from management, effort from the
  work) that moves activity within the space.
- **defence in depth** — redundant independent protections, whose
  redundancy hides local breaches.

## Connections

Draws on Gibson's ecological psychology, naturalistic decision making,
Wilde's risk homeostasis and the closed-loop control view of management,
and cites Leveson (1995) on predictive risk analysis.

## Bearing on the record

Source of [THEORY-tmpj5hlr](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpj5hlr.md). Rasmussen's work space bounded by constraints
is an informal viability set, and his migration is a trajectory heading
for the edge of it: [THEORY-tmp4t87t](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp4t87t.md) in organizational dress. For the
owner's essay, it supplies an empirical mechanism for §6's precariousness
that the essay currently lacks. It also offers a contrast for Northstar.
Drift erodes the margins of a routine regime, while protective lock-in
reproduces an emergency regime. Both degrade viability, by different
routes.

## Limitations

Qualitative and built from accident histories, so it is fitted to
failures. It does not show how often drift occurs without an accident. Its
remedies are argued, not evaluated.

## Open questions

How to locate a boundary before it is crossed in systems that are not
tightly coupled, which the paper calls "still a research issue".
