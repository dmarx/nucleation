# CLAIM-tmpp0ufw: Reflexive control can act on how a target's characteristic organization forms, not only on the evidence, criteria or circumstances of its decisions: a transmissible expression the target takes up can seed interpretive, evidence-selecting and authority-allocating dispositions that the target then maintains itself, so the influence outlasts its source, reaches circumstances the intervention never presented, and leaves formation provenance outside while maintenance is the target's own

## The claim

A7 §1, at the owner's U6: "**Reflexive control can operate through the
nucleation of perspectival character, rather than merely through the
manipulation of particular decisions or beliefs.** The target may
subsequently reproduce the organization through which it is influenced,
using its own capacities for reasoning, evaluation and self-maintenance."

A7 §2 separates the two forms. With decisions a_t = π_P(x_t), an
intervention on the circumstances changes the decision and leaves the
perspective P as it was; an intervention on formation changes P to P′, and
with it the whole response profile: "The second mechanism potentially
changes responses to future circumstances, including those never
encountered during the original intervention." "The distinction is between
producing an outcome and participating in the formation of an organization
that produces a class of outcomes."

A7 §11's candidate thesis: "Transmissible cultural structures may
participate in this process by expressing or nucleating patterns of
interpretation, epistemic selection and authority allocation. Where these
patterns become endogenously maintained, external formation provenance can
coexist with present maintenance autonomy."

**What it adds to the routes held.** [CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)'s evidence route moves the
decision with the criteria fixed, its criteria route moves the criteria,
and [CLAIM-703](CLAIM-703.md)'s route moves the situation the target's sound values meet.
Each is an influence on a decision or on the standards a procedure applies
while the influence lasts. The claim's route acts on how the organization
that produces decisions is formed, and it predicts two things the others do
not: the effect persists after the source stops, because the target
maintains it ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s maintenance provenance is the target's), and it
reaches circumstances of the same class that the intervention never
presented, the transfer by which [CLAIM-532](CLAIM-532.md) tells a formed capacity from a
temporary state.

**Its parts on the record.** [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) builds a capacity installed by an
outside pulse and then maintained by the agent's own cycle, told apart from
a self-formed one only by a knockout during formation; its "Reflexive
control" bullet already reads this as [CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)'s informational route
with no continued control. [CASE-123](../cases.d/CASE-123.md)'s one-time-perturbation variant is the
same for an institution's interpretation–authority loop ([CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md)).
[THEORY-855](../theory.d/THEORY-855.md)'s propagandist, who forwards only real results, is the
evidence route without fabrication; A7 §5 asks whether the selection can
itself be passed on, so that "externally maintained epistemic distortion"
becomes "an internally reproduced epistemic limitation" ([LEAD-tmpuxizu](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpuxizu.md)).

**The carrier.** That the seed can be a transmissible cultural expression,
a meme in the exchange's sense, is the exchange's earlier thesis (memes as
nucleation sites and as expressions of a perspective's character, filed
from U1–U5). This claim applies it to outside governance.

## What it does not say

- **Not that external formation is manipulation.** A7 §8: "external
  formation is not sufficient to establish manipulation, and endogenous
  maintenance is not sufficient to establish freedom." Whether influence
  on formation is domination is [CLAIM-545](CLAIM-545.md)'s question (another's capacity
  to govern one's governing relations) and needs control channels,
  accountability and the target's capacity for revision (A9 §V).
- **Not that the character can be read off.** A7 §6: two perspectives with
  the same character need not share formation provenance; "Character
  identifies kind; provenance identifies aspects of genesis." That is
  [CLAIM-132](CLAIM-132.md) (characters classify kinds, not tokens) with [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md)'s
  result, and it is [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)'s problem.
- **Not that any actual influence campaign works this way.** It names a
  route and a test, as [CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md) does.

## Note of 2026-11-25: the summary's "procedure that stays what it was"

The revision of the manuscript governance-of-becoming, Part II (Article 6,
G6.5), found that the summary's phrase that the three held routes act on a
decision "by a procedure that stays what it was" fits the evidence route
([CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)) and the circumstance route ([CLAIM-703](CLAIM-703.md)), and sits uneasily with
the criteria route ([CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)), which changes the weights the procedure
applies. The body's wording, an influence "on the standards a procedure
applies while the influence lasts", is the more careful one, and is the one
to read the summary by. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this
session); adopting it is the owner's call.
