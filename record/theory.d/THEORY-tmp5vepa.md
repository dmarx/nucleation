## The account

Hu, Wang and McAuley (2025) find that LLM memory agents fed information
incrementally do well at retrieving stored snippets but all fail to act on
the latest of conflicting facts when the answer needs several hops, reaching
at most 28% accuracy, while long-context models do best at learning and
understanding across the whole input.
