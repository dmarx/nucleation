*At the owner's request: "file the claim with a connection to [CASE-111](../../record/cases.d/CASE-111.md),
then investigate if other CASEs are potentially hiding CLAIMs we should
expose more explicitly like this." The first filing of the exchange "About
the Chagossians" (workbench entry of 01:01:26) had declined A4's claim
candidate, "Resource restoration does not imply organizational
restoration", because [CASE-111](../../record/cases.d/CASE-111.md) already derives it as a consequence
("Restoration can cost more than preservation"). The owner's point is that
a proposition held only in a case cannot be cited, objected to or given a
status. It is now [CLAIM-tmpcwl5o](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcwl5o.md), Active and demonstrated, grounded in
[CASE-111](../../record/cases.d/CASE-111.md) and illustrated by the federal science, USIP and Chagos cases.*

**Method.** All 171 cases were read in three passes by kind:
constructions (35), specimens (56), and thought experiments with
reported, executed and proposed studies (80). Superseded and Deferred
cases were skipped. For each case, every general proposition it asserts or
derives (usually a bold-lead bullet under "What it shows") was checked
against the titles and claim sections of the claims it links to, and the
titles of all claims were grepped for its key terms. A proposition counted
as held only if some claim's title or claim section says it; a dated note
on a claim does not hold it, which is the second pattern below. Three
read-only agents made the passes; every candidate filed here was then
checked against the case text and the linked claims before filing.

**Two patterns.**

- **The lab's findings went into notes.** The strongest candidates from
  studies ([CASE-158](../../record/cases.d/CASE-158.md), [CASE-161](../../record/cases.d/CASE-161.md), [CASE-162](../../record/cases.d/CASE-162.md), [CASE-163](../../record/cases.d/CASE-163.md)) are PerspectiveLab runs
  whose results were filed as dated notes on objection or method claims
  ([CLAIM-278](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-278.md), [CLAIM-414](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)), so the findings themselves had no codes.
- **Objections waiting for codes.** Five propositions sit in a claim's
  dated note rather than in a claim of their own. On a second reading,
  requested by the owner, two are objections: CASE-063's against CLAIM-212
  (a narrowing "proposed, not made") and CASE-067's against CLAIM-240 ("a
  limit of the model"). A sweep of all claims' notes for the same wording
  found a third, the difficulty CLAIM-440's clause 3 records against itself
  from Hesp et al. and Carver. The three are filed as counters,
  CLAIM-tmpb4dok, CLAIM-tmp4pmcv and CLAIM-tmpra2cf, all Proposed. The
  other three notes are not objections, and the first version of this
  entry was wrong to list them as such: CLAIM-364's note lists CASE-128
  among cases that "add to the claim"; CLAIM-327's says arm B "does not cut
  against it"; CLAIM-401's says "The claim is unchanged". They stay
  candidates for complementary claims, listed below. CLAIM-096's note
  declines a counter on purpose ("It is not filed as a counter: the owner
  did not take the fourth paper up") and is left as it is.

**A tension, not a gap.** [CASE-054](../../record/cases.d/CASE-054.md) (Crow and Hidatsa joking relationships)
says that kind-sharing by descent of an institution "is not a transport of
any token, so it shows that descent and transport are also different
facts". [CLAIM-210](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-210.md)'s body says "Descent is supplied by the transport
clause". One of the two needs revising.

**Threshold.** Rejected near-misses included propositions a linked claim
states (most cases), facts of the case rather than general lessons,
predictions the case hedges ("might", "suggests"), and results from works
held only as abstracts. Most cases are fully held: of 171, about 32 had a
candidate, and the eight filed are those that were stated firmly in the
case, checked, and held nowhere else in the record.
