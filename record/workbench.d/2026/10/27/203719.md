**Two hubs that were definitions and an easy half.**

- **[THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) (Blackwell).** It had 20 transitive dependents and held
  three Active claims over a Proposed account. Ten of its twelve direct
  dependents used only Blackwell's definitions, or the easy direction that
  a garbling never helps an unrestricted decision-maker. [THEORY-1096](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1096.md)
  states both on any outcome spaces, with the two-line proof (composition
  of kernels, then Fubini) written out under [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md) v2's ruling. Only
  [CLAIM-259](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-259.md) and [CLAIM-326](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-326.md) use the converse, and they stay on [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md).
- **[THEORY-174](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-174.md).** Its monotonicity half was already Active as [THEORY-720](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-720.md).
  All five dependents used that half or the definition of a classical
  simulation, and none used the unproved characterization.

**The inversions.** Three readers reviewed the 27 dependents behind the
remaining 38 Active-over-Proposed edges, read-only. Their finding drove a
config change. Nearly every edge was a reading linked to a claim as
evidence: a note headed "evidence now linked", an instance, an outside
echo, an attribution. None was a premise. `corroborates` ([ADR-044](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-044.md)) ran
only from claim to claim, so readings had no place to go but `grounds`.
[ADR-058](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-058.md) lets a THEORY corroborate a CLAIM.

**Questions with an Active answer.** [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md) says a partly answered
question is split, and the answered part becomes an Answered child.
[QUESTION-127](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-127.md) was split by hand as the template. Three agents took the
other 36, with questions grouped so that no answering entry was edited by
two of them. Eight were split:

- [QUESTION-017](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-017.md), -045, -060, -105, -112, -118, -220 and -222, with
  [QUESTION-288](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-288.md) the only one kept to its old title, since it already
  asked only the remainder.

Twenty-six were left, each with the reason that its Active answerer settles
no delimited part, for example a sufficient case for a which-question, or
one model for a general one. The count stays at 34 because `answers`
also links entries that bear on a question without settling any part of
it. A question such as [QUESTION-025](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-025.md), with five Active answerers none of
which treats correlated attributes, is not [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md) debt. It is a relation
used in two senses, which is the next thing to decide.

**[ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md)'s size clause** followed [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md)'s limit of three splits per pull
request. [ADR-047](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-047.md) is Superseded, so [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md) version 2 defers to [ADR-057](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-057.md)'s
pass size. This pull request is past what one sitting reads, and the next
pass should open its own.

**Owner rulings this pass leaves:**

- **What an ARG's status means.** Either the inference is accepted, or its
  premises are held. [ARG-008](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-008.md)'s body reads it the first way. On that
  reading, neither [ARG-006](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-006.md) nor [ARG-008](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-008.md) was an inversion.
- **[CLAIM-069](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-069.md).** It is granted, and its second clause uses exactly what
  [THEORY-168](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-168.md) keeps Proposed. Either the title is narrowed to the authors'
  assertion, or the claim goes Proposed.
- **[CLAIM-438](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-438.md).** It is Active because the owner conceded it on
  2026-10-23. [ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md) says a conceded counter goes Active. Its evidence,
  [THEORY-893](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-893.md), is Proposed until it is replicated. The two conventions
  disagree here.
