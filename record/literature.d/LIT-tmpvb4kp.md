# LIT-tmpvb4kp: T2I-CompBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Open-world Compositional Text-to-image Generation

Kaiyi Huang, Kaiyue Sun, Enze Xie, Zhenguo Li and Xihui Liu (2023), *NeurIPS
2023 Datasets and Benchmarks* —
[ARXIV-2307.06350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06350)

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the current arXiv abstract only, which is now
that of the journal version T2I-CompBench++: text-to-image models often fail
to compose scenes with several objects, attributes and relations. The
benchmark groups compositional prompts into attribute binding, object
relationships and complex compositions (the ++ version adds numeracy and
3D-spatial relations, for 8,000 prompts in eight sub-categories) and proposes
detection-based and multimodal-LLM metrics. What the conference version itself
contains was not checked.
