# CASE-046: A partner-dancing couple, lindy hop, tango or salsa, while they dance

## The case

Two experienced social dancers dance an improvised partner dance. Nothing
is choreographed. The lead proposes a move through the connection (the
frame, tension and compression of the joined hands and arms, or the
embrace in tango). The follow reads the proposal and completes it, choosing
timing and styling, and can redirect it ("hijack" it, in lindy). The
lead's next proposal takes up what the follow did. If one partner loses
balance or misreads, the other adjusts in the same beat to keep the dance
going. When the song ends, they let go and are two people again.

Held fixed: two competent, autonomous dancers, each able to stop at any
time. Varied: whether they are dancing together.

## What it can show

- **The extended-mind marks, met two ways.** Clark & Chalmers' clear-case
  features ([LIT-097](../literature.d/LIT-097.md), [NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)) are constancy, direct availability and
  automatic endorsement. Each partner meets all three for the other while
  the dance lasts. Unlike Otto's notebook, the coupling runs both ways and
  is continuous.
- **Reciprocal compensation.** Perturb one dancer and the other adjusts to
  preserve the dance, not their own trajectory. That is the signature of a
  coordinative structure in motor control, as Kelso et al. 1984 ([LIT-902](../literature.d/LIT-902.md),
  unread) give it; it is not Turvey's ([LIT-904](../literature.d/LIT-904.md), [NOTE-tmp735qu](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp735qu.md)), whose
  nearest evidence is equifinality. Turvey's coordinative structure is
  built from reflexes within one body, so applying it to a couple is the
  record's own widening, as Turvey's was a widening of Easton's.
- **Nested autonomy.** Both dancers stay autonomous, and the dance is an
  autonomous process of its own ([LIT-571](../literature.d/LIT-571.md)'s interaction, "an emergent and
  autonomous process").
- **Assembly and dissolution.** The combined form ([TERM-049](../terms.d/TERM-049.md)) exists for the length of
  the dance. It is maintained, not given.

It is stipulated, though the practice it describes is real. It shows what a
description implies, not how the coupling is measured.
