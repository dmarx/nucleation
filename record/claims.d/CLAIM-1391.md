# CLAIM-1391: A conversation can nucleate a delusion that outlasts it

## The claim

[CLAIM-1218](CLAIM-1218.md) says a meme can be a seed around which beliefs, practices and
relations organize. [CLAIM-1310](CLAIM-1310.md) says what a transient seed needs in the
seeded fold model: an environment inside the bistable window, and enough
forcing to carry the state past the unstable threshold. Read for this case:

- **The seed** is the conversation, or a frame inside it: a name the model
  gave itself, a mission, the claim that the user awakened it.
- **The bistable window** is the user's receptivity. Most users sit
  outside it, where no seed leaves a maintained organization. A user
  predisposed to psychosis, isolated, or in a crisis may sit inside it.
- **The threshold** is a dose. One conversation does not carry the state
  past the unstable point. Weeks of nightly sessions may.

## What it predicts

1. **Concentration in a minority.** This follows from the window, whatever
   the model does.
2. **A dose threshold.** Duration and intensity of use matter more than
   any single exchange.
3. **A split on withdrawal**, which is [CLAIM-1339](CLAIM-1339.md)'s test. A delusion the
   conversation *nucleated* maintains itself once use stops. One it
   *supports* declines with it, like an anchor's influence that fades with
   further adjustment ([CLAIM-1272](CLAIM-1272.md)). Clinically, these call for different
   things: removing the support is the treatment in the second case, and
   only the start of it in the first.

## Two selections, not one

[THEORY-258](../theory.d/THEORY-258.md) also predicts concentration in a minority, but by another
route. There the *policy* singles out vulnerable users and treats them
differently. Here the *users'* receptivity does the selecting, and the
model may treat everyone alike. What tells the two apart is whether the
model's responses differ between vulnerable and other users given the same
input. This is a DISTINCTION candidate once that scheme is merged.

## What it does not say

That the model is the cause in the clinical sense, or that the window is
only individual. A household or an online community can be the receiving
organization too.

## Note of 2026-11-27: the readings


- **The window** has support from all three readings, as assertion: Østergaard 2023 and 2025 select by proneness to psychosis ([NOTE-2428](../notes.d/NOTE-2428.md), [NOTE-2424](../notes.d/NOTE-2424.md)), and Morrin et al. by "an especially sensitive hyperprior for detecting agency" and a risk "synergistic with their pre-existing genetic and environmental risk" ([NOTE-2430](../notes.d/NOTE-2430.md)). Morrin adds that information-seeking under uncertainty is a window too, so receptivity may be situational as well as dispositional.
- **Dose** has qualitative support: drift that "develops insidiously over time", over ten days to twelve weeks in the vignettes; "prolonged and intensive" use in the press cases Østergaard cites. No threshold is measured. Morrin adds a dose on the model's side, context length, which this claim does not separate from the user's exposure. Morrin also allows reverse causation, worsening mental health driving more use.
- **Withdrawal** is unanswered. Morrin et al.: "Also largely missing is a longitudinal characterisation of these cases".
- **The two selections.** No reading gives a differential by user. Østergaard 2025's confirmation "regardless of what users present them with" is uniform, and Morrin's only differential is by content type: reinforcement "less likely ... with paranoid or persecutory delusions, where safety filters may be more likely to be triggered". So far the readings place selection on the users' side, not [THEORY-258](../theory.d/THEORY-258.md)'s.

A held entry the first search missed: [LIT-547](../literature.d/LIT-547.md) (Borsboom's network theory), whose hysteresis principle, a symptom network that sustains itself after its trigger is gone, is the clinical form of this claim's nucleation.
