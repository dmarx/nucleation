# NOTE-tmphjyfw: Han (2014) — Why revolution is no longer possible

## Contribution

The author's statement of the thesis of Psychopolitik.

## Key results

1. The power maintaining the system is "no longer repressive, and instead seductive".
2. "There is no person opposite, no enemy who represses liberties".
3. "we are all self-exploiting workers in our own enterprises".
4. Power "tries to make them dependent".
5. "the subjugated individual is not even aware of being subjugated".

## Assumptions

Neoliberal power operates through freedom.

## Concepts

Seductive power; self-exploitation; dependence without an opposite.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpkjv7u**: results 2 and 4 are dependence with no identifiable discretion-holder, which Han calls a system of rule. Version 1 of the claim called that not domination. See ARG-tmpsh7he.
- **QUESTION-071**: result 5 is the question's premise.
- **CLAIM-670**: result 4 is an arrangement producing dependence, the claim's mechanism, asserted.
- **CLAIM-tmpbypt7**: freely chosen self-exposure normalizing surveillance fits the cultural mechanism; asserted, not shown.

## Limitations

A newspaper essay; Big Data and "smart power" are not developed here.
