# QUESTION-tmpipa8k: If an agent is a population of interests, what makes it act as one: bargaining among the interests, a change in which of them may act, or agreement among them?

## Why it is a question

The record reads the self as many in several places. Ainslie's interests
bargain across delays ([THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md)). Minsky's and Selfridge's societies are
compositions of mindless agents ([THEORY-043](../theory.d/THEORY-043.md)). Internal Family Systems has
simultaneous parts led by a Self ([LIT-591](../literature.d/LIT-591.md)). Emotion regulation is one motive
state checking another ([THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md)). Each says how the many are composed. Of
how they come to act as one, the record holds one mechanism, bargaining by
precedent, and one rival, Bratman's intrapersonal shared intention
([THEORY-076](../theory.d/THEORY-076.md)).

The owner's prompt names three further candidates: temporal discounting as
the source of the problem, and mindfulness and flow as two ways it is
solved. They are not obviously the same kind of solution. Bargaining leaves
the conflict in place and outweighs it. Mindfulness, on ACT's reading
([THEORY-051](../theory.d/THEORY-051.md)), leaves the impulse in place and changes its standing. Flow, as
it is described, has no conflict to resolve.

[QUESTION-015](QUESTION-015.md) asks what maintained relations govern which regime becomes
effective. This question takes the population case and asks which of three
things does the governing:

- **enforcement among the interests**, as in bargaining or precommitment;
- **a change in which interest may act**, with their valuations unchanged:
  suspension and accessibility in [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md)'s and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s sense;
- **agreement among them**, so that there is nothing to govern: the
  interests are synchronized on the course of action ([TERM-tmpu28lt](../terms.d/TERM-tmpu28lt.md)).

## What would count as an answer

A way to tell the three apart in a given episode of self-control, and a
showing that the episodes the literature calls flow and mindfulness fall
on the sides the record claims. The record's proposed answers are
[CLAIM-tmpmf12z](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmf12z.md), which says where the population comes from;
[CLAIM-tmpf301t](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpf301t.md), which says flow is agreement; and [CLAIM-tmp7lzto](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7lzto.md), which
says mindfulness is a change in which interest may act. The standing
objection to calling any of this synchronization is [CLAIM-tmpigdl0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpigdl0.md).

A list of further techniques of self-control is not an answer. Ainslie
already lists external constraint, attention control and emotion control
beside will. Nor is a demonstration that mindfulness or flow improves
self-control: every route does that.

## What is already known

- Hyperbolic discounting, and the preference reversal it predicts, are
  well supported. That the reversal is what makes the interests many is
  [THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md)'s reading, and its mechanism is untested.
- The record holds no reading on flow and no study of mindfulness and
  discounting. The works it would need are registered unread: [LIT-tmpwvter](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwvter.md),
  [LIT-tmpqpq9d](../literature.d/LIT-tmpqpq9d.md), [LIT-tmpjoaq1](../literature.d/LIT-tmpjoaq1.md), [LIT-tmp8dmxy](../literature.d/LIT-tmp8dmxy.md) and [LIT-tmpz3v2y](../literature.d/LIT-tmpz3v2y.md).
