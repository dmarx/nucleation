# CLAIM-913: Alternative reading (a) of the discriminating variants: where what a power's holder would do is fixed and known, an arrangement in which the subjects hold no share of the power to review or revise is no less legitimate than its match, the sense that it is defective is a residue of protective reasons that no longer apply, and reasonable rejection is right to find nothing to reject

## The reading

**This is one of three alternatives.** The others are [CLAIM-911](CLAIM-911.md),
reading (b), and [CLAIM-912](CLAIM-912.md), reading (c). The choice is the owner's;
the assistant leans to (b).

In C2* and C4* of [CASE-336](../cases.d/CASE-336.md), nothing a courier cares about happens
differently in W2′ than in W2, or in W4′ than in W4, under any
contingency. Every reason a courier had in C2 and C4 to object to the
platform's control of review and revision was a reason about what the
platform might do, and the stipulation removes it. Reasonable rejection
judges principles by individuals' reasons ([THEORY-1971](../theory.d/THEORY-1971.md)); with no reason
left to press, there is nothing to reject. The intuition that W2′ and W4′
are still worse is explained as the protective reasons persisting in
judgment after the stipulation has removed them, the way an intuition
trained on ordinary cases outruns an extraordinary one.

## What it gives the record

The supplement answer to [QUESTION-483](../questions.d/QUESTION-483.md) stands for review and revision as
for the stated constructions, and the record's account of rightful power
adds to contractualism only an account of how its verdicts are realized
and kept effective (A102 §10). For [QUESTION-484](../questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) the pair C2, C2* is a
dependence: the same parties and interests get different verdicts at two
levels of the decider's reliability.

## What it must answer

The record's own test of domination, [CLAIM-682](CLAIM-682.md), counts a dependence as
presumptively dominating where the controllers "hold consequential
discretion over the dependent" and the dependent lacks "effective means
of protection, influence, challenge"; it does not ask how the discretion
will be used. W4′'s couriers meet it. On this reading [CLAIM-682](CLAIM-682.md) is either
wrong about benevolent discretion or picks out something other than a
wrong; the reading has to say which.

## Note of 2026-11-25: a parallel instrumentalism, from the manuscript governance-of-becoming, Article 8
<!-- inactive-ok-file: ARG-228 CLAIM-1184 CLAIM-723 — Proposed; the two constructions, their instrumentalist readings and the alternatives the owner has not ruled on, cited with their standing -->

The manuscript's Article 8 (§8.5, join J8.2) sets two constructions side
by side that the record has not linked. [ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md)'s matched utilities,
which answer [CLAIM-1184](CLAIM-1184.md), fix expected harm and vary only whether those
affected can contest it. [CASE-336](../cases.d/CASE-336.md)'s variants C2* and C4* ([CLAIM-909](CLAIM-909.md)),
which CLAIM-913 reads, fix what a power's holder would do and vary only
who holds a share of the power to review or revise. Both fix the
instrumental difference and vary only standing or the allocation of a
power, and [CLAIM-1184](CLAIM-1184.md) and CLAIM-913 are the instrumentalist reading of
each: what is left once the instrumental difference is matched is a
residue, "no wrong beyond the harm" in one and "a residue of protective
reasons that no longer apply" in the other.

The parallel routes; it does not bind. Standing to be heard on a harm is
not a share of a power to revise rules, so one can reject [CLAIM-1184](CLAIM-1184.md) and
accept CLAIM-913, or the reverse, without inconsistency; the stronger
form, that the two stand or fall together, fails on that reading. What
carries over is a candidate, not an entailment: a non-instrumental reason
the owner accepts in one ([CLAIM-911](CLAIM-911.md)'s "personal reason of status", or
[ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md)'s carried-over judgement from [CLAIM-723](CLAIM-723.md)) is a reason to test in
the other. The join has not yet been put to an independent reader under
[ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md). No change of status; the rulings on [CLAIM-1184](CLAIM-1184.md) and on [CLAIM-911](CLAIM-911.md)
to CLAIM-913 stay the owner's. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in
this session).

**Correction of 2026-11-25 (the review of Part III, Part B).** The
review's independent reader of the joins tested J8.2 under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md). The
parallel is accurate, and this note is right as a note, but the join
fails as a claim: "a non-instrumental reason accepted in one is a
candidate in the other" admits no separating case, since "candidate"
absorbs every outcome. Its general form is held already, by [CLAIM-909](CLAIM-909.md):
matched constructions discriminate "only when they hold fixed what a
power's holder would do with it", and there "the verdict turns on
whether a reason to hold or share a power, apart from its use, is an
input" reasonable rejection admits. [ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md) is a construction of that
form for standing to contest, so the shared form is [CLAIM-909](CLAIM-909.md) applied to
[ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md), a restatement. One detail: of [CASE-336](../cases.d/CASE-336.md)'s two variants, C2*
(who controls review) is nearer to [ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md)'s standing than C4* (who
holds the power to revise), and this note's "standing to be heard is not
a share of a power to revise" separates [ARG-228](../arguments.d/ARG-228.md) from C4* only. No change
of status. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session);
adopting it is the owner's call.
