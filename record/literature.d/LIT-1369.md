# LIT-1369: Dense amnesia in a professional musician following herpes simplex virus encephalitis

Wilson, B. A., Baddeley, A. D., & Kapur, N. (1995). Dense amnesia in a professional musician following herpes simplex virus encephalitis. *Journal of Clinical and Experimental Neuropsychology*, 17(5), 668–681. doi:10.1080/01688639508405157.

Not read: no lawful copy was reached on 2026-10-22 (see the status note).
The summary is drawn from what the paper is standardly cited for and from
its PubMed abstract. The abstract confirms the amnesia, semantic as well as
episodic, and the persistent belief that he has just woken, which it calls
a delusion. It does not mention the preserved musical skill, which a
reading should check first.

The exchange (A26) took up the owner's suggestion of Clive Wearing as a case for the person as an organization of partly independent processes. This is the primary case study it cited. The case is [CASE-137](../cases.d/CASE-137.md).
