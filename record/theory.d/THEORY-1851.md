# THEORY-1851: New tech deepens racial hierarchies

## The account

Benjamin argues that "the road to inequity is paved with technical fixes": "tech designers encode judgments into technical systems" and treat the results as exterior; the forms include "default discrimination".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1940](../notes.d/NOTE-1940.md). It bounds [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md): subordination by default with no present discretion-holder, outside the claim's test by its version 2.

## What it does not say

That every default is discriminatory.
