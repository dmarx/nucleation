# LIT-215: Defending Informational Structural Realism: The View of Alternatives

Ivan A. Karpenko (2025), *Epistemology & Philosophy of Science (Эпистемология и философия науки) 62(2), 190–207* — DOI-10.5840/eps202562230

## Key takeaways

- Informational structural realism, extended with the claim that empirically equivalent but structurally different descriptions (dualities with different spacetime metrics or dimensions) have equal ontological standing, makes information (the descriptions themselves) fundamental and so vindicates theoretical objects against non-eliminative OSR.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Karpenko surveys versions of structural realism and proposes an expanded informational structural realism (ISR). He criticises a non-eliminative OSR that takes entities and structures as primitives but denies theoretical objects. He draws on scientific realism, the information interpretation of quantum mechanics, digital ontology and string-theory dualities. He argues that theories built on different spacetime metrics can yield the same description and the same predictions while implying different fundamental pictures. On his view, what is fundamental is the family of equivalent descriptions (information) rather than substantival spacetime, and theoretical objects therefore exist as ontological primitives.
