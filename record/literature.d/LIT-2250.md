# LIT-2250: Generalised Langevin Dynamics: Significance and Limitations of the Projection Operator Formalism

Christoph Widder and Tanja Schilling (2026). "Generalised Langevin Dynamics: Significance and Limitations of the Projection Operator Formalism." arXiv preprint. [ARXIV-2604.20453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20453).

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-tmp2mtul](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2mtul.md)). The projection operator formalism is rigorous only for bounded PL; Mori's equation is structureless and adds nothing to the fitted autocorrelation; the memory term is a coupling term, not necessarily memory.
