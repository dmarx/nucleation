The owner asked for the second pass of the promotion audit of 2026-10-19
(the first pass is the entry of 02:41 today). It covered four Proposed
THEORYs whose proved parts were held back by promotion conditions that
also asked for something else. Each was checked against the NOTE that read
its source before anything changed. None of the four parents was promoted
whole. In three, the proved part is filed apart, and the fourth is
narrowed to it.

- **[THEORY-008](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-008.md) → narrowed and promoted.** [NOTE-834](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-834.md) met the promote_when's
  first clause and found the population clause unmeetable from [LIT-257](../../record/literature.d/LIT-257.md).
  It named the alternative, "a rewording that confines the claim to finite
  M". The body was already about M stimuli, so only the title changed. The
  population clause is now [QUESTION-tmp4uhs6](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp4uhs6.md).
- **[THEORY-005](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-005.md) → [CLAIM-tmpu2dsm](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu2dsm.md).** The operator identity with the finite-χ²
  condition was, in the THEORY's own words, "the record's own". The
  THEORY's promotion condition asks for a published statement, which a
  reading must meet but a proof does not. A result the record proves is a
  CLAIM ([ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)), so it is filed as one with a written proof, demonstrated
  and Active. Writing the proof added the converse, which the THEORY had
  stated without argument: if T is Hilbert–Schmidt, its kernel is the
  density of P⁺ on rectangles, so P⁺ ≪ p⊗p and χ² is finite. [THEORY-005](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-005.md)
  stays Proposed for what the sources show and for trained features.
- **[THEORY-001](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-001.md) → [THEORY-tmpsuto2](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpsuto2.md).** The promote_when recorded the spectral
  and NT-Logistic derivations as done. The new THEORY holds them, sourced
  to [LIT-258](../../record/literature.d/LIT-258.md) App. B.2–B.3. [THEORY-001](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-001.md) extends it and waits, as before, on
  the InfoNCE/NT-Xent minimizer.
- **[THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) → [THEORY-tmp2m4ke](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp2m4ke.md).** The promote_when concerns the
  arbitrary-outcome case, which rests on Theorems 1–2 quoted from 1951. In
  the finite case Blackwell (1953) proves the converse himself (Theorems
  4–6), and [NOTE-595](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-595.md) followed that proof step by step. The direct
  implication is written in matrix form, Q = PM ⇒ PMD = QD, so the finite
  THEORY does not lean on the quoted theorems. [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) extends it.

**Not done.** [THEORY-087](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-087.md) was in the audit's table but not in its second
pass, so its regular-model half is not split here. The thirteen claims that
cite [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) are not re-pointed at the finite THEORY. Whether each one
needs only finite outcomes is a reading of each claim, and [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md)'s
note says they can be re-pointed.
