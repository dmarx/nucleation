# LEAD-140: Compare an earlier and a later improver of a record-keeping collective from the same fresh state at the same budget, then damage its diagnostic machinery

## What it would do

Run [CASE-255](../cases.d/CASE-255.md), which adapts [CASE-186](../cases.d/CASE-186.md): let a collective improve its
procedures over generations, transplant an earlier and a later improver
onto the same fresh state at equal budget, then damage the diagnostic
machinery.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md): "A study in which a collective improves its procedures
  over generations, an earlier and a later improvement procedure are each
  applied to the same fresh starting state at the same budget, and the
  collective's diagnostic machinery is then damaged"
- [QUESTION-388](../questions.d/QUESTION-388.md): "a governing rule's change must alter behaviour under
  controlled later tasks"

## What would count

The later improver doing better from the same start, and losing that
advantage when diagnosis is damaged, answers [QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md) for the
collective; [QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md) itself warns that it does not establish
independent self-governance.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out in reduced form

Done by the assistant as a delegated lead worker, on
[CASE-tmpl0ccq](../cases.d/CASE-tmpl0ccq.md)'s simulation (scratch/studies/lead-140/), about 64 minutes of
CPU. What was run:
- 6 arms × 12 lineages × 60 generations;
- the earlier and the later improver, and knowledge-only and
  organization-only successors, applied to the same fresh artifact at
  equal budget;
- undisclosed lesions of the improver's suite, with a sham and with the
  suite's self-test removed.

Filed as [CASE-tmp6btcq](../cases.d/CASE-tmp6btcq.md) and [CLAIM-tmp7ggg0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7ggg0.md). By the lead's "What would
count":
- the later improver did better from the same start;
- the advantage did not vanish when diagnosis was damaged where the
  improver kept a self-test or read its record. It degraded where the
  improver had shed its self-test.

What remains:
- [CASE-255](../cases.d/CASE-255.md) with agents that keep a prose record;
- the plain-Markdown arm;
- schema adequacy;
- the specialist with a poor record;
- a second correction of the improver;
- an outside suite that tests the improver's own checks rather than its
  products;
- longer horizons.
