The owner asked whether the QUESTIONs, like the THEORYs and CLAIMs in the
promotion audit, had outgrown their status. Of 240 questions, 229 were
Open, 9 Deferred and 1 Superseded. None had ever been Answered or
Dissolved. All 228 Open questions were read: the 44 that list an Active
answerer in full, and the other 184 for signs of being dissolved,
superseded, parked or stale. No status was changed. The owner's ruling on
what counts as an answer, and the split pattern, are [ADR-049](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-049.md).

**Answered as posed: none.** In every one of the 44, the Active entries
answer one half, one model class, or a sufficient condition where a
characterization is asked.

**Split candidates (16).** The answered part can be stated exactly. The
parent stays Open, reworded to the rest.
