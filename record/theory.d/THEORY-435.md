# THEORY-435: In Moral Foundations Questionnaire responses, Care and Fairness are endorsed across the political spectrum, while Loyalty, Authority and Sanctity rise toward the conservative end, in every world region sampled

## Source

Graham, Haidt, Koleva, Motyl, Iyer, Wojcik & Ditto (2013), [LIT-499](../literature.d/LIT-499.md), read in
[NOTE-396](../notes.d/NOTE-396.md), section 3.2, Figure 2.1 and Table 2.3. Atari et al. (2023),
[LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md), read in [NOTE-398](../notes.d/NOTE-398.md), for the MFQ-2.

## What was actually shown

**MFQ-1 ([LIT-499](../literature.d/LIT-499.md)).** In large internet samples, Care and Fairness are valued
across the spectrum, slightly more by liberals. Loyalty, Authority and
Sanctity rise linearly toward conservatives (Figure 2.1). Table 2.3 reports
the pattern in 12 world regions, with Authority and Sanctity correlating
most strongly with ideology. Across 20 contested issues (N = 24,739),
Sanctity was the strongest predictor for most, after ideology and
demographics were partialled out (p. 88).

**MFQ-2 ([LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md)).** In 19 nations, Care and Equality go with the left;
Proportionality, Loyalty, Authority and Purity go with the right. Splitting
Fairness moves Proportionality to the right-hand side of the profile.

**What could have come out otherwise.** Conservatives could have scored
higher on all five, or the gradient could have appeared in English-speaking
samples only. Neither happened in the regions sampled.

## What this does not say

- **That conservatives have more moral foundations.** The finding is about
  questionnaire endorsement, not about which intuitions people possess or
  use when judging.
- **That the pattern holds outside online, literate samples.** The chapter
  calls its samples likely WEIRD (p. 92). The MFQ-2's are online, literate
  and mostly urban, about 200 per nation, with no small-scale society ([LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md),
  p. 65).
- **That Purity scores can be compared across nations.** In the MFQ-2,
  Purity is the one foundation whose intercepts fail the alignment
  invariance threshold (39.5% non-invariant against a 25% cut-off).
- **That the binding foundations form one cluster everywhere.** In network
  analyses, the US split between Care–Fairness and the binding three appears
  in only three of 19 nations ([THEORY-613](THEORY-613.md)).
- **That Sanctity's predictive power shows its evolutionary origin.** It is
  evidence that Sanctity scores predict attitudes, which every rival account
  of purity can accept.
