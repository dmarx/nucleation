# QUESTION-085: Does a maintained r/place artwork feed back into the flow of repair that keeps it, so that it is self-maintaining like a candle flame, or is its defence supplied from outside like the heat under a convection cell, and does the answer change when its coalition starts switching strategies?

## Why it is a question

[CLAIM-273](../claims.d/CLAIM-273.md) puts contested artworks among dissipative structures for
their maintenance. The record's readings already separate the rungs above
that:

- **Dissipative, externally held.** A Bénard cell is held by a temperature
  gradient it does nothing to keep up ([THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md), from [LIT-570](../literature.d/LIT-570.md)).
- **Self-maintaining.** A candle flame keeps itself above the combustion
  threshold, vaporizes wax and draws in oxygen. Its macroscopic pattern
  exerts "at least one 'top-down' constraint" on the flow that sustains it
  ([THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md)). Bickhard ([THEORY-220](../theory.d/THEORY-220.md)) counts its contributions as
  functions for it. It "cannot seek more fuel when the candle is almost
  gone".
- **Recursively self-maintaining.** A system that switches among
  self-maintaining processes according to its situation ([THEORY-220](../theory.d/THEORY-220.md)).

For an artwork, the flame's top-down constraint would be the image itself
recruiting or directing its defenders. A recognizable, growing image would
draw more placers, and visible damage would draw repair. That is testable.
The conditional repair hazard after an overwrite ([CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md)), and
whether recruitment rises with an image's size or recognizability, would
show feedback from pattern to flow. If the defenders arrive at a rate fixed
by the subreddit's size, whatever the image does, the artwork is a
convection cell. Falmagne et al. find the defenders' own conduct among the
precursors of overthrow ([THEORY-214](../theory.d/THEORY-214.md)), which suggests the flow is not
fixed.

The recursive rung is the coalition's, not the image's. Coalitions merge
over the event ([THEORY-216](../theory.d/THEORY-216.md)), and their planners admit or refuse
additions and move or redesign images ([THEORY-218](../theory.d/THEORY-218.md)). Whether that
switching is in the service of the coalition's persistence, and not just
its members' tastes, is the same problem one level up.

The answer bears on [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md), whose producer would then be
self-maintaining or not, and on [QUESTION-086](QUESTION-086.md). A self-maintaining
artwork and a twin held up by an outside crew would behave alike until the
crew is withdrawn. [CLAIM-194](../claims.d/CLAIM-194.md)'s warning applies to drawing the artwork's
boundary: the coupling between image and placers does not by itself say
whose boundary it is.

## Note of 2026-10-13: two routes of feedback, one observed

The owner proposed selective retention: participants stay while their art
survives, so surviving art keeps its maintainers ([CLAIM-274](../claims.d/CLAIM-274.md), which
answers this question in part, untested). The readings already show the
other route, recruitment. The Mona Lisa group wrote its forum's URL on the
canvas, and within two hours newcomers said they had come because of it
([THEORY-218](../theory.d/THEORY-218.md)). Both are the image acting on the flow that keeps it,
which is the flame's rung, not the convection cell's. The test above now has
two parts: recruitment after visible growth or damage, and retention as a
function of the survival of one's own pixels.
