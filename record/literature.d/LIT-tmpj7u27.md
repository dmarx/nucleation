# LIT-tmpj7u27: The transaction test: An experimental method for assessing online interfaces

Francesco Bogliacino, Leonardo Pejsachowicz, Giovanni Liva and Francisco Lupiáñez-Villanueva (2026). "The transaction test: An experimental method for assessing online interfaces." *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 244: 107469*. DOI-10.1016/j.jebo.2026.107469.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-tmprz4xm](../notes.d/NOTE-tmprz4xm.md)). A preference-relative, outcome-based
criterion of manipulative design that distinguishes harmful from beneficial
steering even when the technique is the same, with large-sample evidence
that common dark patterns raise preference-inconsistent choice and that a
confirmation-prompt remedy does not reliably undo it.
