**What this is.** A preview, filed with ADR-061 while it is Proposed, of
the migration its acceptance would trigger. Nothing here is applied. On
2026-10-28 four reviewers read every claim the decision's classification
names (65 of them), on main after the fifth status review, and said for
each what the migration would do. Each call names the ARG it would file:
premises, conclusion, form and status, and the critical question that
carries the verdict. The calls were checked against each other, and where
two moves interact the interaction is said below.

**The headline: the classifier overcounted by half.** Of 65 claims, 35
stay claims on a close reading, 18 split, 10 become ARGs outright and 2
fold into a THEORY's reading plus a Rejected ARG. The low-confidence calls
shrank as the decision expected, and so did some high ones. Most claims
that stay are `disclaimed` boundary claims of the form "X does not
establish Y" where X is not an entry in the record, so there is no
inference between entries to file; ADR-032 already called them claims the
record disowns. The rest that stay are about the subject matter after all,
or are about whether a defeat condition can be met, which the decision
already keeps.

The forms used: `evidence` most often, then `analogy`, `formal-application`,
`existence`, `abduction` and `source`. Both new forms are used, `source` by
three ARGs.

**Three worked examples.**

*An objection to an inference becomes the inference, Proposed: CLAIM-163.*
Now a counter that `objects_to` CLAIM-162 and grounds on THEORY-069.
After, one file:

    rests_on: [CLAIM-169]
    grounds: [THEORY-069, THEORY-070]
    concludes: [CLAIM-162]
    # body: "what relevant difference is there?" is answered: "Interests
    # whose curves do not cross are not coupled, have no phase, and do not
    # 'lock'". CLAIM-198, which supplies the missing coupling, enters a
    # later version as a premise.

CLAIM-163 goes Superseded by it. Its one inbound objection, CLAIM-198,
cannot `objects_to` an ARG, so it becomes a premise of the ARG's next
version, as the decision's "one ARG per inference" says.

*A source claim becomes a THEORY's reading plus a Rejected ARG:
CLAIM-072.* Now a granted claim that Saussure says "purely differential"
only of signifier and signified taken apart. THEORY-159 already holds all
of that, so it gains nothing. What CLAIM-072 adds is a verdict on
support, and it holds two inferences, not one:

    grounds: [THEORY-159]
    concludes: [CLAIM-082]      # "drop the referent contrast, which is not Saussure's"
    # ARG B: form source, status Proposed
    rests_on: [CLAIM-082]
    grounds: [THEORY-159]
    concludes: [CLAIM-115]      # CLAIM-254's objection is a revision of this one

CLAIM-072 goes Superseded by both, and CLAIM-254 too.

*A claim others rest on is split, and the verdict goes to an ARG that
exists: CLAIM-085.* Now granted and Active, and CLAIM-243, CLAIM-248 and
CLAIM-252 rest on it. Its circularity limb, "That would primarily
characterize the model's own preferences", is about ARG-008's inference,
which already names it. So ARG-008 gains that answer in version 2 and stays
Active, and CLAIM-085 is retitled to its subject-matter part: "A framing
effect in a language model is a property of that model under those
prompts, and what a translation ought to preserve is a normative question
that model response distributions, being descriptive, cannot settle." Its
three dependents keep `rests_on`; nothing is repointed.

**What the preview found wrong in the decision, now corrected in it.**

- *CLAIM-163's example named the wrong premise.* The analogy's source is
  THEORY-069, phase synchrony, which CLAIM-163 grounds on. THEORY-070 is
  CLAIM-162's own premise.
- *The split rule must be applied after the moves, not before.* CLAIM-053
  is forced to split only because Active CLAIM-229 rests on it, and
  CLAIM-229 itself becomes ARGs; the same holds for CLAIM-066 and
  CLAIM-234. A move that removes a claim's last Active dependent frees the
  claim to move too, and two moves that file the same inference file it
  once.
- *CLAIM-066 belongs on the forced-split list*, and CLAIM-011 does not: its
  title holds no support clause, and the verdict is already ARG-006's.
- *CLAIM-097 has a THEORY behind it*, THEORY-522, which reads
  T2I-CompBench. It stays a claim and gains that ground.
- *CLAIM-169 is not a SOURCE case.* The decision uses it as a claim
  premise, and it stays one.
- *Five "what a test could show" claims are about defeat conditions*
  (CLAIM-206, -208, -222, -226, -244), the group the decision keeps.
- *CLAIM-073*, a Rejected support claim, is missing from the
  classification.
- *The ARG scheme lacks slots a moved claim uses.* ARG has `rests_on`,
  `grounds`, `concludes`, `undercut_by` and `superseded_by`. A claim moved
  to an ARG loses `works` (CLAIM-427 is in a manuscript), `defeated_if`,
  `uses`, `complements` and `illustrated_by`. Either the scheme gains
  `uses` and `works`, or those go to the body. And `objects_to` cannot
  target an ARG, so a reply to a moved objection becomes a premise of the
  ARG's next version.
- *Two moves need a CASE that does not exist yet*: the anchoring measures
  behind CLAIM-190, and the central defeat test behind CLAIM-232 and
  CLAIM-233.

**What the record would notice.** The 30 moving claims are cited in 442
files, and the 12 that would be Superseded in 175, mostly in prose and
acknowledgement directives. The repair rewrites links mechanically; what
a citation should now say is read entry by entry. The lint's checks would see
three things change.
- Superseded claims cited as open lose their `inactive-ok` reason, which
  the lint reports for review.
- No Active claim would rest on a retired one, because of the split rule.
- The dialectic page would gain each new ARG once the pin moves to luria
  1.5.0, with its undercuts nested beneath.
