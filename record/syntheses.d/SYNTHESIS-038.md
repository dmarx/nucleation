# SYNTHESIS-038: The organizational-normativity line is four accounts that meet at its root questions and the older parts' questions: what a normative judgment needs; the wrong beyond harm and becoming normatively less informed; rights, demands and commitments; and accountability without blame and normative governability; what wholes and parts owe and claim, and what those who govern, restrict or answer for an arrangement owe, are the line dependence-and-domination, whose entries that carry this line second are integrated here; the line makes no claim beyond its parts

*The account of the organizational-normativity line, "Normative judgment about organizations", split from will-organization ([ADR-089](../decisions.d/ADR-089.md)), where it was a part account. Since version 4 (2026-11-15) it is the line's account over the accounts of its parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)); versions 1 to 3 mapped what is now the first two parts directly. At version 5 the line's third and fourth parts, [SYNTHESIS-092](SYNTHESIS-092.md) and [SYNTHESIS-093](SYNTHESIS-093.md), became the line dependence-and-domination, at the split of this line ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)), and that line's account is [SYNTHESIS-095](SYNTHESIS-095.md). Version 6 (2026-11-17) adds the two parts filed at the interim synthesis review of that day, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and records that [SYNTHESIS-091](SYNTHESIS-091.md) and [SYNTHESIS-094](SYNTHESIS-094.md) were revised then (each to version 3). "Organization" is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none. Every link below is attributed to the part account or component that states it.*
