**The owner's prompts**, verbatim:
1. Quoting the open objection, "> a metronome driving a musician, or a nerve
   driving a muscle, passes", the owner asked: "and why shouldn't they?"
2. After the assistant's answer in chat: "yes, file them on the PR branch".

**What moved.** The assistant agreed in chat that they should pass, for
three reasons, which the owner accepted for filing as [CLAIM-tmpl4ha3](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl4ha3.md):
- **The objection repeated a worry already set aside.** [CLAIM-tmptsdgt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md) had
  set aside the coupling-constitution worry by individuating a new system,
  not parthood. Nerve and muscle form the neuromuscular system, not one
  part of the other.
- **Its intuitions were about agency, not system-hood.** [QUESTION-tmpm56ps](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpm56ps.md)
  keeps those apart.
- **The study design already treated a one-way driver as an exogenous
  input.** [CASE-tmpazmsq](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpazmsq.md) does this with the music's beat. Inside or input
  is a choice relative to the target.

Consequences:
- [CLAIM-tmpw0mrc](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw0mrc.md) went Rejected.
- [CLAIM-tmptsdgt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptsdgt.md) went to version 3. Mutual entrainment is now a sufficient
  mark of a combined form whose members are *peers*. It is not a condition
  of membership.
- The QUESTION's adequacy condition on one-way drive was reversed.
- The study's direction measurement became an architecture measurement.

**What was learned.** The direction of coupling had been doing two jobs, and
only one of them was right. It cannot decide membership, and the attempt to
make it do so would have excluded the dancing couple with its lead and
follow. It does decide the *architecture*: peers or hierarchy, driver and
driven. That is what the agency question will need next. "Who drives?" is
part of whether, and where, there is an agent. The assistant's own
objection had smuggled an agency intuition into a question that was
written to exclude it. That is the same drift [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md) was made to stop.
