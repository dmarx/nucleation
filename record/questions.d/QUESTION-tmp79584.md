# QUESTION-tmp79584: Does a systematic review or meta-analysis of experimental and quasi-experimental evaluations in low- and middle-income countries find, for transfers without an employment focus, pooled effects on prime-age adults' employment and hours near zero, with confidence intervals that exclude a reduction of more than a few percent; reduced work among pension-age recipients; and positive effects on work or earnings for transfers tied to job search or business start-up?

## Why it is a question

Baird, McKenzie and Özler (THEORY-1466) review studies they judge well-
identified, with no pooling, quality scoring or publication-bias check,
so "little changed" is a reading of significance, not a bounded effect.
A meta-analysis with intervals excluding more than a small reduction
would answer it; pooled reductions for prime-age adults would count
against.
