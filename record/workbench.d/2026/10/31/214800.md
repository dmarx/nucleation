The owner, on the first filing of Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy 2024 and
its sources: "surely these readings connect more richly to the
preexisting material on the Nucleation record? these works seem extremely
likely to be relevant to nucleation's accounts of pragmatic transport,
distributed agency, will organization, individuation..." The owner was
right. The first filing had linked the readings mostly to [CLAIM-355](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) and
[CLAIM-418](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-418.md).

Four readers each took a set of lines:
- pragmatic transport;
- distributed agency, which holds the record's individuation material;
- will-organization, organizational affect and authenticity;
- nucleating argument and formal apparatus.

Each read the new readings against the record's own entries on its
lines. Each wrote dated notes only on entries whose first line was its
own, so no two readers touched one file. Each filed new entries only
where a reading added something the record lacked. The text each
proposed for the readings' own files was added under a section headed
"Bearing on the record's own argument" (NOTEs) or "Connections to the
record's own argument" (THEORYs), with relations to the questions they
bear on.

**New entries.**
- **[CLAIM-525](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-525.md)** (pragmatic transport, Proposed). Information in the
  Simondonian sense, a disparity whose resolution reorders the system
  that takes it up, is in the line's terms a change of interpreter or of
  the questions a receiver can decide. So Blackwell's order leaves it out
  by construction, and the two accounts are not rivals.
- **[CLAIM-526](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-526.md)** (distributed agency, Proposed). Describing persons and
  organizations as coexisting levels removes the question of how agents
  add up, but not the question of levels. Whether a collective variable
  is closed against the activity realizing it stays decidable without
  agents ([CLAIM-388](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-388.md), [CLAIM-386](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-386.md)).
- **[ARG-047](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-047.md)** (Rejected). The paper's "agency is a matter of
  perspective" does not support [CLAIM-418](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-418.md), since it means the analyst's
  cut, not an organizational perspective. This corrects the first filing.
- **[CLAIM-527](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-527.md)** (organizational affect and will, Proposed). Viable
  reach, valence and emotion are three quantities in an organization, not
  one. Massumi's capacitation and the paper's "augmented ability to act"
  are the first.
- **[QUESTION-365](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-365.md)** (will-organization). Can an internal conflict that
  neither local accommodation nor escalation settles be settled outward,
  by another organization taking up the ungranted demand, and how would
  that be told apart from suppression?
- **[QUESTION-366](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-366.md)** (nucleating argument). Does an inquiry need the
  record of its own development to know its object, as Simondon's method
  holds, or only to revise its conclusions, which is what the record has
  shown?

**What the pass found, in brief.**
- **The paper's agency is not will-organization.** It is a change in what
  can be reached ([CLAIM-352](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-352.md)). Its "augmented" needs a comparison that
  [CLAIM-354](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-354.md) makes partial.
- **Its ethics.** The duty "to foster each being-in-becoming" is the
  inference [CLAIM-075](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) disclaims. Its prescription falls on [CLAIM-512](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-512.md)'s
  broad horn.
- **The readings' metastability is not the nucleation sense.** It is
  heterogeneity, with no barrier or seed ([QUESTION-281](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-281.md)). Simondon's own
  crystal, growing in a metastable solution, is the line's image of
  anchoring ([CLAIM-185](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-185.md)). The paper's dense crystal is entrenchment
  ([CLAIM-184](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-184.md)).
- **Coexisting meanings show less than claimed.** They jointly refine and
  cost nothing ([CLAIM-310](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-310.md)). They are incoherent only under a stated
  correspondence ([TERM-097](../../record/terms.d/TERM-097.md)), so their coexistence alone shows neither
  several individuations nor failure ([CLAIM-496](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-496.md)).
- **Massumi's view of transmission.** Affect is not transmitted, and
  bodies respond differentially. That is common drive, not coupling
  ([QUESTION-063](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-063.md)), and a further option for collective affect
  ([QUESTION-053](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-053.md)). The paper's "spillover" is coupling.
- **Bencherki 2017's usurpation and co-individuation.** They add
  attribution as an axis [CLAIM-403](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s ladder lacks. They support
  history-dependent judgment ([CLAIM-518](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-518.md)).
- **Tsoukas & Chia's radial categories.** They describe how the record's
  closed vocabularies turn silent extension into decided amendment
  ([CLAIM-419](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-419.md), [CASE-178](../../record/cases.d/CASE-178.md)). [CASE-178](../../record/cases.d/CASE-178.md)'s note sets out a blind self-test.

**Judged not to be connections.** Each reader listed what it rejected,
for example Wilhoit on footing, and [CASE-112](../../record/cases.d/CASE-112.md)'s bistability read as
Robert's reversal. Those judgments are in the readers' notes and are not
filed.

**Two disagreements between readers, settled.**
- **Tsoukas & Chia's page numbers.** They are corrected to p. 579 (see the
  curation entry).
- **[CLAIM-181](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-181.md).** The nucleating reader called its "No crystal-growth work
  is held" an uncorrected error. It was already corrected by the dated
  note of 2026-10-27, so nothing was changed.
