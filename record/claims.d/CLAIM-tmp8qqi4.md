# CLAIM-tmp8qqi4: The organization that breaches the second-order duty of answerability is a collective institution that is an agent under a stated criterion, and the duty is its own: its obligations, capabilities and reasonable alternatives are the ones the verdict weighs, and those who control its arrangements bear the duty only derivatively, as its officers, so that an arrangement no such institution governs falls outside the claim

## The reading

[CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) declares no sense of [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md). Senses 2 and 4 are set aside on
its own text, as [CLAIM-tmp7mors](CLAIM-tmp7mors.md) argues: sense 2 is the reproducing the
verdict is about, and [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) covers reproduction maintained from outside,
so closure is not required. This claim takes sense 3, the collective
institution ("A corporation, a university, an agency, a federation"), and
restricts it to institutions that are agents under a stated criterion
([TERM-044](../terms.d/TERM-044.md), or [TERM-063](../terms.d/TERM-063.md) if adopted), since [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) says an organization in
any sense is a candidate for an agent only so.

## Why

- **The wording.** [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s title has the organization commit the
  wrong, and A62 §3's fifth condition weighs "the organization's
  obligations, capabilities, and reasonable alternatives". On this reading
  both are literally true, and the claim needs no paraphrase.
- **The readings that bear on it.** Fineman ([THEORY-1818](../theory.d/THEORY-1818.md)) corroborates
  [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) "for a sense-3 whole", placing "an affirmative obligation on
  the state and its institutions". [CLAIM-685](CLAIM-685.md), A's from the same line,
  declares sense 3. The owner's review in [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md): "For a corporation
  and its employees, the corporation may be a plausible duty-bearer."
- **Something is left over that is the institution's.** An institution's
  duty can outlast its officers and be owed across changes of control:
  a successor board inherits the duty to restore a review its predecessor
  dismantled. [ARG-114](../arguments.d/ARG-114.md)'s first conjunct, "O_N(A, Honor(r))", is the
  institution's first-order obligation, and a duty to maintain the means
  of honouring it is most naturally the same bearer's.

## What it costs

- **Scope.** An arrangement that several institutions control together
  without forming one agent ([QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md)), or a platform and its
  dependents with no common agent, falls outside the claim, though its
  controllers can reproduce occluding representations with feasible
  alternatives. [CLAIM-tmp7mors](CLAIM-tmp7mors.md) covers it.
- **A criterion of agency.** The claim applies only once one is stated,
  and the record has adopted none for institutions.
- **The owner's abstract sense.** The owner's correction on [CLAIM-614](CLAIM-614.md),
  that "a 'system' is just a kind of 'organization'", and the standing
  concern that "organization" is sense 1 unless a component says
  otherwise, point the other way. This reading is a component saying
  otherwise, and the owner may not mean it.
- **The waiting integration.** [CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md)'s erosion of governing capacity
  is a breach of the duty, on this reading, only where an agent-institution
  governs the arrangement and the eroded capacities are ones the standard's
  violations need. [CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md)'s own case, a civilization's technological
  systems, mostly has no such governor.

The assistant leans to [CLAIM-tmp7mors](CLAIM-tmp7mors.md), for the scope and because it needs
no criterion of agency. The owner chooses.
