# LIT-2986: Phase transition of the largest eigenvalue for nonnull complex sample covariance matrices

Baik, Ben Arous & Péché (2005). Annals of Probability 33(5) (doi 10.1214/009117905000000233); arXiv math/0403022.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A names it at A31, A32, A33, A37, A42 for the BBP transition, where a spike in a covariance separates from the bulk. It bears on the exchange's model of when learned structure emerges from noise in a spectrum.

Read on 2026-11-21 in [NOTE-2241](../notes.d/NOTE-2241.md); the reading is [THEORY-2148](../theory.d/THEORY-2148.md). The
exchange's formulas and threshold are right after a change of notation,
and its attribution is loose: this paper proves the threshold for complex
Gaussian samples and the largest eigenvalue only. The real case, more
variables than samples and several outliers are Baik and Silverstein's
([LIT-2988](LIT-2988.md)); the eigenvector overlap is Paul's ([LIT-3006](LIT-3006.md)) and
Benaych-Georges and Nadakuditi's ([LIT-2964](LIT-2964.md)), whose additive model is
also the one the exchange's mixture and mean-shift examples use.
