# THEORY-tmp1w39g: Dranseika, granting Devanesan that a mereology of pregnancy shapes which analogies an argument about abortion can use without fixing its conclusion, argues that the dependence also runs the other way: normative commitments may legitimately guide the choice among mereological views still in contention, but a mereology chosen partly to fit a commitment gives that commitment no independent support

## The account

Dranseika, granting Devanesan that a mereology of pregnancy shapes which analogies an argument about abortion can use without fixing its conclusion, argues that the dependence also runs the other way: normative commitments may legitimately guide the choice among mereological views still in contention, but a mereology chosen partly to fit a commitment gives that commitment no independent support.
