# TERM-100: provenance, of a capacity or an organization, and which sense: formation provenance (whose process produced it), maintenance provenance (whose process keeps it), or source provenance (what it was made from, the sense the descent term holds)

## Definition

A claim about where a capacity or an organization comes from says which of
these it means.

1. **Formation provenance.** Whose process produced it: the agent's own
   activity, as in self-formation ([TERM-099](TERM-099.md)), or another's, as in
   installation or reflexive control. A fact about the history of its
   production.
2. **Maintenance provenance.** Whose process keeps it in being now: the
   agent's own productive cycle ([CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md)) or an outside supply. A fact
   about the present organization.
3. **Source provenance.** What it was made from, a single transport from a
   given source with a fidelity to it. [TERM-084](TERM-084.md)'s sense 1, kept there.

| sense | when | what can test it |
|---|---|---|
| formation | the history of production | an intervention on the producing process while it runs, or a record kept then ([CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md)) |
| maintenance | the present | knockouts of the processes that produce and uphold it ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)) |
| source | the history of a transport | a fidelity profile against the source ([TERM-043](TERM-043.md)) |

The first two come apart. [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) builds a capacity installed by an
outside pulse and then maintained by the agent's own cycle: its maintenance
provenance is the agent's, its formation provenance is not, and after
formation no knockout tells it from a self-formed one.

## Why formation provenance matters

These entries turn on formation provenance, not only on maintenance:

- **Responsibility and free agency.** A condition stated in present
  structure is met by a manipulated agent ([THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md), [THEORY-1118](../theory.d/THEORY-1118.md)), so it
  needs a historical condition; historical ownership by taking responsibility
  ([THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md)); Kane's ultimate responsibility ([CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md)'s note of
  2026-11-02); and the warning that a historical condition may not separate
  the manipulated agent from an ordinarily caused one ([THEORY-328](../theory.d/THEORY-328.md)).
- **Kane's ultimate responsibility is formation provenance.** Whether "the
  sources of our wills" can "be completely traced backwards to something over
  which we had no control, such as … social conditioning or hidden controllers"
  ([LIT-1984](../literature.d/LIT-1984.md), p. 37).
- **Whose the will is.** [CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md), [QUESTION-011](../questions.d/QUESTION-011.md), [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) and
  [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md).
- **Capture and domination.** Reflexive control by a one-time perturbation
  ([CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md), [CASE-123](../cases.d/CASE-123.md), [TERM-066](TERM-066.md)), and another's standing capacity to govern
  one's governing relations ([CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)'s last clause).
- **Autonomy as psychology measures it.** The history of internalization
  ([THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md)), which would have to show in a regulation's relations to the
  rest ([THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md)).
- **Self-governance by history.** [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md)'s voluntary delegation, which
  differs from an imposed restriction only in how it came about.
- **Persistence and continuation.** [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) and
  [CLAIM-544](../claims.d/CLAIM-544.md).
- **Self-formation itself.** [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md).

And one entry limits it: [CLAIM-356](../claims.d/CLAIM-356.md), on which exogenous origin does not by
itself disqualify a goal.

## What it is not

- **Lineage or causal descent** ([TERM-084](TERM-084.md)'s senses 2 and 4), which say how a
  later realization stands to an earlier one, not whose process made a
  capacity.
- **Historical persistence** ([TERM-071](TERM-071.md)), which says what kept an organization
  going through time, the maintenance sense extended backwards.
- **Authorship of an act.** Formation provenance is of a capacity. Whose a
  particular decision was is a further question.

## What it leaves open

What a record of formation provenance would have to contain, which is
[QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)'s part 2. And whether formation provenance matters in itself, or
only as evidence about something present the record has not yet located,
which is the dispute between [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) and [THEORY-328](../theory.d/THEORY-328.md).
