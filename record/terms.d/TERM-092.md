# TERM-092: inquiry frontier, of an agent at a budget: the questions it can formulate, making them determinate enough to pursue, with the concepts, records, tools, time and computation it actually has; distinct from the questions its language can express, from those it can pursue to a stated standard, and from those that have answers

## Definition

For an agent with concepts K, budget B and horizon H, the **inquiry frontier**
is the set of questions q for which some procedure available to the agent
formulates q, making it determinate enough to pursue, within B and H
(A17 §§2, 7). Formulating is neither answering nor pursuing: A17 §2's askable
set is "distinct from the investigable and the answerable questions". Four
sets differ:

- **expressible:** statable in some language, in principle;
- **askable:** on the frontier;
- **investigable:** on the frontier, with budget left to pursue it to a stated
  standard;
- **answerable:** having an answer that some process could find.

A question can be expressible and off the frontier. The owner's U17: "I can't
interrogate a question that takes me more time than I have to even ask it." A
question can also be askable and unanswerable from the evidence available, as
below Singer's information bound ([NOTE-1224](../notes.d/NOTE-1224.md)).

## The distinctions it draws

- **From legibility** ([TERM-064](TERM-064.md)). Legibility is whether a distinction can be
  recovered for a task already set. The frontier is whether the task can be
  set at all. A17 §4: "whether the agent can **construct the task that
  would make that distinction relevant**."
- **From the regime** ([TERM-065](TERM-065.md)). The regime fixes a query family for a
  comparison. The frontier is where a query family could come from (the
  note on [TERM-065](TERM-065.md) of 2026-10-27).
- **Movement, not only extent.** Reusable specifications, such as a notation,
  a theory or a schema, move the frontier without enlarging the budget.
  They can also make other questions harder. A17 §7: "The change could be
  in the shape of the frontier rather than its total size."

## Why it is proposed

[CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) is stated in it, and its defeater (b) is a test of frontier
movement. [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) lists "askable without answerable" among the failures.
What would settle it: adoption once the claims stated in it use
one of its sets each; rejection if [TERM-065](TERM-065.md) extended to generated query
families serves, an extension [TERM-065](TERM-065.md)'s note of 2026-10-27 declined.
