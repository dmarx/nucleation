# CLAIM-tmp4fc0k: Reliance and revision arrive together here

## The claim

[CASE-307](../cases.d/CASE-307.md) counted, from 2026-10-07 to 2026-11-22, the substantive
revisions of 859 claims against their inbound `rests_on` and `grounds`
edges. At matched age, claims with an inbound edge were revised about 1.47
times as often as claims without one (Mantel-Haenszel by age, interval
1.22 to 1.77), and "148 of the 303 revisions after a first edge fall
within a day of it: edges and revisions are made in the same sessions and
reviews, so an edge marks attention as well as constraint".

A33's stability-generativity plane assumes the opposite coupling, or none.
Its established anchor has a "Stable formulation" and "Supports
development"; its unresolved churn is "Frequently revised" with "Little
downstream use". If reliance draws revision, the claims most built on are
pushed out of the anchor quadrant toward the developing nucleus, and the
quadrants record the record's habits of review as much as its entries'
development. So any reading of the plane has to match on age, as [CASE-307](../cases.d/CASE-307.md)
did, and has to count the revisions within a day of a new edge separately
from the rest. A revision that answers a new dependant is the recheck
[CLAIM-863](CLAIM-863.md) describes, not instability.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the regimes of [CLAIM-tmpk015q](CLAIM-tmpk015q.md) do not exist; it says
  where the measurement has to be careful for them to show.
- It does not choose between [CLAIM-864](CLAIM-864.md) and [CLAIM-863](CLAIM-863.md), the two readings of
  [CASE-307](../cases.d/CASE-307.md). On either, the axes are coupled.
- It does not extend past [CASE-307](../cases.d/CASE-307.md)'s window. The record was about 46 days
  old then; the coupling may weaken as entries age, which the defeater
  allows for.
