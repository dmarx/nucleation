# THEORY-tmptbea0: Kuhn, as Kindi reads his 1959 essay, holds that innovation in a mature science depends on convergent thinking as much as on divergent, because training that commits a scientist firmly to a tradition is what lets research recognize where the tradition fails and break with it

## The account

The productive scientist is "a traditionalist and an iconoclast": only research rooted in the tradition is likely to break with it, since a precise paradigm is a sensitive indicator of anomaly. Training that inculcates commitment is therefore a precondition of revolution, not its obstacle.

## Evidence

Interpretive history of the physical sciences; the essay itself not read.
