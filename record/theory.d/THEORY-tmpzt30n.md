# THEORY-tmpzt30n: Davidson argues that a creature has beliefs, and so any propositional attitude and any rationality, only if it has the concept of belief, which liability to surprise shows every believer must have, and that this concept, being the concept of objective truth, comes only from triangulating with another creature in communication, so that only communicators are rational animals

## The account

Rationality is having propositional attitudes, which come as a holistic
set. Having a belief requires the concept of belief, since surprise
requires awareness of a contrast between what one believed and what is so.
That concept is the concept of objective truth, which comes only from
triangulation: two creatures responding to each other and to a shared
world through communication. So rationality is a social trait.
