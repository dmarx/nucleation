**What the readings gave the record's own argument:**

- *Aggregation is not decomposition, exactly.* [THEORY-tmpj32n2](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpj32n2.md): a
  partition's block sums close for every state iff the partition is
  column-equitable, which is a symmetry condition, not weak coupling.
  [CLAIM-tmpkoi6k](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkoi6k.md)'s uniform-coupling system turns out to be aggregable
  along every partition. [QUESTION-tmplru2n](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplru2n.md) gains a third kind of unit.
- *Near-decomposability against dynamical dependence.* [CLAIM-tmpymy0a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpymy0a.md): for
  a block's full state, dependence is of second order in the coupling into
  the block. [CLAIM-tmp614wx](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp614wx.md): the Simon–Ando aggregate's closure defect is
  exactly QA(I − VQ), and its intra-block part vanishes iff column sums are
  equal. Together they answer the forward half of [QUESTION-tmpt64jh](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpt64jh.md) in
  part.
- *Memory against dynamical independence.* [CLAIM-tmpmonrv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmonrv.md): in VAR(1),
  independence implies zero memory, not conversely.
- *Augmentation, in practice.* [THEORY-tmpswb4h](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpswb4h.md): the finite-memory LES
  closure is a finite Markovian augmentation whose extra coordinate is the
  subgrid force. Its error is not controlled.
- *Scale locality is banded, not gapped.* [THEORY-tmpfcxzq](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpfcxzq.md), conditional on
  an unproved scaling hypothesis.

Four demonstrated claims ([CLAIM-tmpymy0a](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpymy0a.md), [CLAIM-tmp614wx](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp614wx.md), [CLAIM-tmpmonrv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmonrv.md)
and the earlier [CLAIM-tmpkoi6k](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkoi6k.md)'s note) were re-checked numerically from
scratch before filing. The readers' scripts are not kept in the record.

What it gave the argument:
- *Nesting of optimal abstractions.*
  - Optimal deterministic IB partitions at two budgets can be
    incomparable ([CLAIM-tmphan8l](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmphan8l.md), re-run here).
  - Every IB optimum nests under the causal partition, its β → ∞ limit
    ([THEORY-tmp35hmc](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp35hmc.md)).
  - Rate–distortion refinability is a Markov condition on some optimal
    pair ([THEORY-tmpe422n](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpe422n.md)).
  - [QUESTION-tmpproc1](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpproc1.md)'s deterministic half is answered; the stochastic
    half is open.
- *The three orders.* Diffusion maps are the case where they agree
  exactly, because they share an eigenbasis ([THEORY-tmp3xg7a](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp3xg7a.md)). Refinement
  and relevance to parameters share a chain under coarse-graining
  ([THEORY-tmp1bqec](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp1bqec.md)).
- *Emergence criteria.* Rosas et al.'s Ψ and dynamical independence
  diverge both ways ([THEORY-tmpyjeab](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpyjeab.md)). The paper's Proposition 1 claims
  more than its proof shows ([NOTE-tmpvy64o](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpvy64o.md)).

Errors found in the papers are listed in each NOTE. Among those that bear
on the record:
- Coifman and Lafon's truncation bound is absolute, not relative.
- Crutchfield and Shalizi's logistic-map dive figure is the single-symbol
  entropy, not the entropy rate.
- Raju et al.'s relevant directions lose information when 0 < λ < d/2.

What it gave the argument:
- Two more commuting cases for [QUESTION-tmp8qovh](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp8qovh.md) (diffusion wavelets,

  ReLU networks".
- Constantin–E–Titi's final estimate is misprinted, without effect on the
  result.
