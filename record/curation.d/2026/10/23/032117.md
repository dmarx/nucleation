**What the abstracts corrected in the exchanges.**
- Paluck, Green and Green: studies of adults' racial or ethnic prejudice
  are absent, not "limited"; the paper first appeared in 2017, not 2019.
- Pettigrew 1998: the abstract says "ingroup reappraisal";
  "deprovincialization" is unchecked.
- Kleinman, Liu and Redding: first appeared in 2020; the abstract names no
  outcome measure, so the exchange's UN voting and rivalries are unchecked.
- Kalla and Broockman: "durable" is at least four months, the effects are
  small (d = 0.08 and 0.04), and only Experiment 1 had an argument-only arm.
- Pettigrew and Tropp 2008: knowledge still mediates, less strongly than
  anxiety and empathy.
