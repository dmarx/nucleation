# SYNTHESIS-tmp3urdo: Reading a language model's response to its context as a parameterized frame makes context-conditioned models a controlled test bed for the theory only with generator and evaluator separated, stimuli held out from training and agreement required across model families, never as evidence about human pragmatics or about what a translation ought to keep, and the frame reading itself is held back because its defeat condition is met as written

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part on whether
context-conditioned language models can test the theory. The line's
account is built over it and over the accounts of the line's other parts.
The mechanical map put seven of these entries in one part with the line's
tests and studies and with composition in conditional generation; the
eighth, QUESTION-024, was moved here from the contextuality part, since its
body calls it the U17 claim "at its most testable". The eight are joined by
13 linked pairs among themselves and by three pairs to the other two
inquiries, so each has its own account; the links are given below.*

## The account

- **The frame reading.** CLAIM-029 (the owner at U17, made "the primary
  formal example" by A43; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): "A language
  model's response to a context is functionally a parameterized pragmatic
  frame". TERM-025 (A49's; Active) is the claim in notation: "Define the
  pragmatic frame as a parameterized family of admissible contexts: c_f =
  Γ(f, c_0)". It "was dropped silently at A52". CLAIM-029 is held back:
  "Its defeat condition is met as written by an Active reading", Lu et al.'s
  order effects (THEORY-683), and its note of 2026-10-15 holds that this
  defeats "a stronger hypothesis, that the model's representation is
  invariant to surface form, not the reading of a model's response as a
  parameterized frame"; "restating it is the owner's call".
- **From the frame to a test bed.** ARG-008 (the owner's U17 analogy, which
  A43 enlarged; analogy, Active) infers from CLAIM-029 to CLAIM-058: models
  "separate changes in communicative context from changes in the
  underlying interpretive system". Its verdict since version 2: "a testing
  ground with separate generator and evaluator, stimuli held out from
  training, and agreement required across model families", and "It does
  not extend to evidence about human pragmatics." CLAIM-058 (A45 §8;
  thesis, interpretive, Proposed): models "give a controlled computational
  setting in which the theory's frame, order and transport effects can be
  tested before human studies". Its version 2 defeat condition asks for the
  pattern of effects across conditions, across model families and sizes, on
  stimuli written after the training cut-offs. It is held back because
  ARG-008's premise CLAIM-029 is Proposed, "so the argument is not sound",
  and its title "is also wider than ARG-008 version 2 carries".
- **What a model result is.** CLAIM-085 (A45 §8–9 and A48 §8.5, recovered;
  granted, Active): "A framing effect in a language model is a property of
  that model under those prompts", and "model response distributions,
  being descriptive, cannot settle" what a translation ought to preserve.
  It undercuts ARG-008. CLAIM-248 (the record's audit of 2026-10-10,
  empirical lens; counter, Active) undercuts it too: the founding stimuli
  "are likely in language models' training data", so "a model's reading of
  their footing may be recall", and "what a context does to one model need
  not be what it does to another".
- **Inducing a frame.** QUESTION-024 (A44 §3C, from Min et al.; kept in
  outlines v3 and v4, absent from the manuscript; Open) asks whether "a
  pragmatic frame be induced through examples of communicative behavior,
  and can that induced frame be transferred across different linguistic
  realizations". An answer would be "A design in which demonstrations with
  a stance (casual, affectionate) but no stated proposition shift the
  interpretation of a new utterance, and the shift survives paraphrase."
  Two readings filed since compare demonstrations with instructions and do
  not answer it (its note of 2026-10-14).
- **Equivalence of interventions.** QUESTION-106 (A3 of the exchange of
  2026-10-14; Open) asks whether a textual instruction, a retrieved record
  and an activation intervention preserve the same pragmatic obligations
  across context shifts, judged on decisions. It asks directly the third of
  the three observations A3 separated in CLAIM-029's defeater, with A3's
  counter: "Behavioral equivalence cannot establish mechanistic
  equivalence."

What stays open is whether the frame reading survives a defeat condition
restated at the level it means (the owner's call), whether a frame can be
induced from demonstrations and survive paraphrase (QUESTION-024), and
whether any model experiment meets CLAIM-058's version 2 conditions. None has been run.

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **A model named as the receiver population.** That CLAIM-220's thesis,
  instantiated with a model family as its named receivers, is tested
  directly in CLAIM-058's test bed, so that CLAIM-248's and ARG-008's
  limits bear only on carrying the result to people. As a derivation it is
  the instantiation of a thesis indexed to "a named population", which is
  trivial, and CLAIM-085 already says a model's framing effect is "a
  property of that model under those prompts". The receiver population is
  also a free slot: choosing it absorbs the objections rather than
  answering them.
- **Lu et al.'s nontransfer would meet CLAIM-058's condition.** That the
  failure of good orders to transfer across model sizes, if it held of
  pragmatic framing effects, would meet CLAIM-058's version 2 defeat
  condition. CLAIM-248 states the evidence and its limit ("That is order
  sensitivity, not a reversal of a pragmatic effect"); the conditional adds
  nothing a study could settle that CLAIM-058's own condition does not.

## What it does not say

- That a model's judgements measure human meaning (CLAIM-058, CLAIM-085).
- That language models are useless as a test bed (CLAIM-248: "Control of
  frames and orders is real").
- That the frame reading is false: it is held back on a defeat condition
  the record holds may be too strong.
- Anything with standing of its own.
