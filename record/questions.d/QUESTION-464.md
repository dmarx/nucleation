# QUESTION-464: When an organized whole's trajectory is said to diverge from the purposes its constituents would endorse, which procedure of evaluation fixes those purposes, so that a divergence can be found and not only asserted?

## Why it is a question

A20 §I of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6af6b47c-8b0c-83ea-ae04-3e3eb2649597) proposed:

> An organized system exhibits emergent purpose divergence when the
> trajectory reproduced by its governing mechanisms is persistently
> misaligned with the purposes its constituents would endorse under an
> appropriate procedure of informed evaluation.

The descriptive half is held: [CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md) (Active) says an organized whole's
operative intention "is produced by systemic mechanisms that can evolve
apart from what the members intend". What A adds is a comparison with what
the members "would endorse under an appropriate procedure", and A does not
say which procedure. A lists only what the concept does not assume: no
consciousness, no unanimous objection, no "hidden controller".

Without the procedure the concept cannot be applied. Different procedures
give different purposes: what members say now, what they would say
informed, what they would agree to deliberating together, what an
independently justified standard says they have reason to want. A
divergence under one need not be a divergence under another, and any
trajectory diverges from some idealized endorsement.

## What would answer it

- **A procedure, with a case.** A stated procedure and an organization in
  which it finds a divergence that a weaker or stronger procedure would
  not, so the choice matters and is made.
- **Relativization.** An argument that the procedure is a declared
  parameter, as [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md) makes the parties, claims and horizon of
  concordance, with what then counts as finding a divergence.
- **Or a dissolution.** An argument that "would endorse" adds nothing to
  [CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md) that a declared evaluative standard does not, so that the
  question is [QUESTION-465](QUESTION-465.md)'s in another form.

## Bears on

[CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md), which it would extend from intention to evaluation; [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md) and
[TERM-121](../terms.d/TERM-121.md), since the procedure may be the reflexive capacity those
describe; and A's use of it to give "Ellul's account of autonomous
technique a more precise foundation".
