# LIT-721: Multilevel Selection and the Major Transitions in Evolution

Samir Okasha (2005), *Philosophy of Science* 72(5):1013–1025 —
DOI-10.1086/508102

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What the record holds of its argument comes from
Okasha's 2022 overview ([LIT-749](LIT-749.md)), which cites it twice: for the
potential, in a transition, for selection to act at two levels, and, with
Griesemer, for the observation that the traditional levels of selection
debate "largely set aside the question of the origin of the biological
hierarchy itself". The 2022 text does not use the MLS1/MLS2 terms, so
nothing is recorded here about them.
