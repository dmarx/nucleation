**The tiers.** Control system, then agent, then willed agent, then self.
Each inclusion is strict. [CLAIM-tmp4g4je](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4g4je.md) states the tiers. [CLAIM-tmptd6s2](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptd6s2.md)
(version 2) narrows the owner's individuation thesis to willed agents.
[CLAIM-tmpwv313](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwv313.md) (version 2) splits the admission test into an agent test and
a will test. "Closer to a self" is made exact: closer in [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md)'s
self-governing sense, not in the subject sense. That is why the firm is the
hard case. On the tiers, the will paper's permissiveness objection
([CLAIM-010](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)) is false on its merits, and is not only set aside by supposing
the agent ([CLAIM-080](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-080.md)).

**What the pass found.** δ/ε had been used in two senses without notice: a
spectral gap against off-block coupling in the threshold's search step, and
a posterior effect against its uncertainty in its pricing step. The
criterion joins them on [MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md)'s identification. That holds only once
the gap is expressed as an estimated parameter with a posterior. It is now
an explicit assumption in [TERM-tmpgthc0](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpgthc0.md), not an implicit one in
[CLAIM-tmp8tsw5](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8tsw5.md). A concept pass that finds nothing is not evidence it was
needed. This one found a real equivocation.
