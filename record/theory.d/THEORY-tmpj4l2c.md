# THEORY-tmpj4l2c: Rakoczy reviews theory-of-mind development as a perception–goal psychology from about nine months, which supports joint attention and joint intention, followed by explicit belief–desire meta-representation from about age four and a gradual later development of recursive mindreading, and reports that infant implicit false-belief findings have repeatedly failed direct replication

## The account

Theory of mind changes at about 9 months, when a perception–goal
psychology supports joint attention and joint intention, and at about 4,
when explicit meta-representation of belief arrives. Recursive mindreading
develops gradually after that. Claims of earlier implicit false-belief
understanding have largely failed to replicate.

## Evidence

Review of developmental and replication studies.
