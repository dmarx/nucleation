# LIT-246: On Variational Bounds of Mutual Information

Ben Poole, Sherjil Ozair, Aäron van den Oord, Alexander A. Alemi, George Tucker (2019), *Proceedings of the 36th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2019), PMLR 97:5171-5180* — [ARXIV-1905.06922](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.06922)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-739](../notes.d/NOTE-739.md).*

- **One family.** With q(x|y) = p(x)e^{f(x,y)}/Z(y) in the Barber–Agakov
  bound, the entropy term cancels and only log Z(y) is left to bound.
  Donsker–Varadhan, TUBA, NWJ (a(y) = e), MINE's bias-corrected gradient
  (a(y) an EMA) and InfoNCE are different ways of bounding it (§2.2–2.3).
- **InfoNCE is a bound, exactly.** NWJ applied to I(X_1, X_2:K; Y) with the
  batch mean (1/K)Σ e^{f(x_i,y)} as partition estimate gives I(X;Y) ≥ I_NCE
  for every critic, when the K pairs are i.i.d. from the joint (Eqs. 8–10).
  CPC's derivation "relied on an approximation, which we show is
  unnecessary" (footnote 2).
- **Capped at log K.** I_NCE ≤ log K, so its bias grows linearly with MI
  once MI exceeds log K (Fig. 3). Its optimal critic, log p(y|x) + c(y),
  does not depend on K; that form is cited to Ma & Collins (2018), not
  proved here.
- **Eq. 10's indices are transposed.** As printed it normalizes over y_j for
  fixed x_i; the derivation, Table 2 and Eq. 12 normalize over x_j for fixed
  y_i. Both are valid bounds; the stated optimal critic fits the derived one.
- **Bias against variance.** NWJ is unbiased but high-variance; InfoNCE is
  low-variance but biased. The interpolated bound I_α (α ∈ [0,1],
  I_α ≤ log(K/α)) gives the best estimates after a full hyperparameter sweep
  (Table 3), and no bound gives low bias and low variance at large MI and
  small batch (§4).
- **Known encoders.** When p(y|x) is tractable, Eqs. 12–13 sandwich MI with
  no learned parameters, and a reparameterized NWJ critic (Eq. 14) only has
  to learn the marginal q(y).
- **Density ratios to bounds.** The optimal NWJ and InfoNCE critics are
  functions of log p(y|x)/p(y); a log-ratio estimator trained by a
  Jensen–Shannon objective and evaluated with NWJ (I_JS) gives a bound, not
  a plug-in estimate (§2.6, App. D).

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 while pursuing, at the owner's request, the connection
*Radon–Nikodym, density ratios and contrastive objectives* (see the curation
entry of that date), and read in full on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-739](../notes.d/NOTE-739.md). It is
held as the record's exact proof that InfoNCE lower-bounds mutual
information and as its statement of the log K ceiling and of the optimal
critic as a log density ratio.

It is the source of [THEORY-006](../theory.d/THEORY-006.md) and a supporting source of [THEORY-001](../theory.d/THEORY-001.md).
Its derivation of the critic-based bounds from one unnormalized
Barber–Agakov bound is filed as [THEORY-237](../theory.d/THEORY-237.md). The
reading confirms [THEORY-006](../theory.d/THEORY-006.md)'s proof, with the index caveat above, and finds
that the proof does cover in-batch negatives drawn from i.i.d. pairs. For
[THEORY-001](../theory.d/THEORY-001.md) it supports the density ratio as the InfoNCE and NWJ optimum for
general densities, but states the InfoNCE case on cited authority, and says
nothing of the spectral or logistic losses. Its §2.5 structured bounds are
the ones CEB ([LIT-226](LIT-226.md)) relies on; that link is not checked from the CEB side.
Tschannen et al. ([LIT-247](LIT-247.md)) restate its proof to show where the i.i.d.
assumption enters.

Tagged `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../decisions.d/ADR-005.md)): it carries methodological
advice for MI estimation in machine learning. It is kept here by the owner's
decision of 2026-09-26 that new work stays in nucleation until a transfer is
judged appropriate ([ADR-010](../decisions.d/ADR-010.md)).
