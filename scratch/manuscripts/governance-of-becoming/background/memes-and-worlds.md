- **CLAIM-270** (Active, demonstrated, thesis; grounds CASE-017; no work). ## 7. The digester's own reading (not the record's)

**What the article can rest on.** The transport floor is Active and formal: CLAIM-270 and CLAIM-635, with CLAIM-081 and CLAIM-056, and TERM-027 for the attractor as convention. The hyperstition models (CLAIM-tmpvic6m, CLAIM-tmpnws9d), the phrase's split (CLAIM-tmpj4pw3) and the seed window (CLAIM-tmpq1lyz) are Active and demonstrated, each in a stated toy. CLAIM-402, CLAIM-342, CLAIM-280 and CASE-141/142 are Active and interpretive. Of the article's own members only CLAIM-402 is Active, so every memetic thesis (seed, generativity, protocol, second-order meme, invitation, hyperstitional success, operative norm) is Proposed, and almost all are A's.

**A51 is right that the transport theory need not be recreated, and wrong by omission about what else is held.** The README's row that memetic nucleation is "not done" with "formal precedents only" misses: CLAIM-tmpnws9d for seed dispensability; CASE-347 for transmission under turnover; CASE-410, which has run LEAD-tmpiktom's named first site; and above all CASE-367, an executed study of exactly the distinction A51's last section asks for, a representation that reshapes its referent against one that only wins on the evaluation regime's measures. The "empirical study" stays not done, and the README row should read done in part.

**Traps, from the Part I–II reviews.**
- *Models told as findings.* CASE-367 says "The mechanisms were written into the simulator"; CASE-362 "Not real groups"; CLAIM-tmpvic6m is "not a way to measure" gains. Say "in the model".
- *Necessary read as sufficient.* CLAIM-402 "defeats one inference and is not a criterion of kindhood"; QUESTION-028's note makes the randomized-exposure test "necessary, not sufficient". CLAIM-tmpyp5bu's withdrawal test tells outlasting from dependence; it does not say what realized the referent.
- *"Is" for "can".* Nearly every meme claim is a possibility ("can act", "can carry"). CLAIM-tmp4uj40's "come apart" is not "are opposed".
- *Standing.* CLAIM-954, CLAIM-955 and CLAIM-946 are Proposed and filer-run, and their leads still read Open.

**Tensions between entries.**
- **CLAIM-tmphq6hp against CLAIM-946.** An operative norm is marked by meeting a discrepancy by restoring behaviour; CASE-362 finds restoration "passed by plain conformity". On answers in a shared world, the marker does not separate a norm enforced from a majority copied. The article's ladder from regularity to operative norm needs another marker, or must say the marker is one of disposition, not of observed restoration.
- **CLAIM-tmpyp5bu against CLAIM-tmpnws9d.** A's "outlast" is a possibility; the assistant's model says it needs a second stable state of the realized organization's own. The same shape as CLAIM-tmpjg5gx's bistable norm and CASE-189's connector: a cue for the "careful integration" A51 wants, which no entry states (SYNTHESIS-tmp4tvbr: no entry derives the applications from the account of memes).
