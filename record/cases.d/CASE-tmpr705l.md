# CASE-tmpr705l: Modern slang that makes the insider sound like an outsider imitating the subculture

## The case

A35 §7: "Consider a bawdy poem delivered by someone who appears to belong to the
criminal subculture it depicts. Replacing the vocabulary with contemporary slang
may preserve intelligibility but inadvertently make the speaker sound like an
outsider imitating that subculture. The audience may understand the observation
perfectly while losing the sense of complicity that made the original work."

## What it can show

That understanding and social effect come apart ([CLAIM-tmpfbpte](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfbpte.md)). Stipulated, so it
shows the possibility; whether actual modernizations of [CASE-tmpagzmj](CASE-tmpagzmj.md) do this is
untested. It was dropped with [CLAIM-tmpfbpte](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfbpte.md).
