# THEORY-tmptax6l: King holds that responsibility as attributability carries desert as fittingness and responsibility as accountability carries desert as fairness, which depends also on the blamer's standing and the agent's opportunity to avoid sanction, and that neither notion of desert extends to the other face

King (2013), LIT-tmpcg293, after Watson (1996), LIT-tmpmsh0d.

## The account

Attributability-blame is fitting when the conduct discloses the agent's
ends. Accountability-blame is sanction-like, so whether it is deserved
turns on fairness: Watson's "unfair to impose sanctions upon people unless
they had a reasonable opportunity to avoid incurring them" (237, as quoted)
and the blamer's standing ("if I lack the standing to hold you to a
legitimate demand, then you cannot be accountable to me", p. 8). So
something can be attributable to an agent without the agent being
accountable for it (p. 13).

## What it does not show

- **Watson's own argument.** Only what King quotes is Watson's.
- **When opportunity to avoid is reasonable.** The condition is stated, not
  measured.
- **Anything about a third party who removed the opportunity.** The account
  concerns the agent and the blamer.
