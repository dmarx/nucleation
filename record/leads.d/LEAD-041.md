# LEAD-041: Specify a procedure that reads from a record of conduct which ordering prevails, how strongly and whether it resumes, separating ambivalence from neutrality; and argue that the model's two channels are perspectives

## What it would do

Write the argument, then the procedure, and apply it to one record of
conduct.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md): "A procedure that, applied to a record of conduct, returns
  which ranking prevails and how strongly"
- [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md): "Two things are owed: an argument that the model's two
  channels are perspectives in this sense"

## What would count

A procedure that separates ambivalence from neutrality on a case answers
[QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md) and supplies what [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) owes.

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out, in part

The argument, the procedure and one application are filed.

- **The argument.** CLAIM-tmp0uism: the evaluative space model's channels
  are perspectives in TERM-076's thin sense (a ranking each, contest on
  approach–avoidance pairs, authority in gain, offset and override), so the
  model's neutral point is no contest and its "middling expression" splits
  into a prevailing compromise and ambivalence. The alternative, that the
  channels are inputs to one evaluator, is CLAIM-tmpaibxx; the choice is
  the owner's.
- **The procedure.** CLAIM-tmpkcsp7, answering QUESTION-268, as code with
  tests (scratch/studies/lead-041/).
- **The application.** CASE-tmpjw1l1, the FOMC's policy-rate record
  1966–2026 with the dual mandate's channels as perspectives. The record
  separates no contest, indeterminacy, a prevailing compromise and
  ambivalence; it shows ambivalence only weakly (Burns, 1980–81), no
  persistent potential and so no resumption, because two-year windows hold
  too few contests at the committee's own noise.

What remains: a record with enough contests per window to show a lead or
resumption (the FOMC's meetings and members' dissents are the nearest);
any record of an organism with both channels measured apart from the
choices; and the interventions that would say whether actual and
dispositional potential agree. Status and stage are unchanged.
