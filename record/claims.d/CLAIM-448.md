# CLAIM-448: An ordinal potential describes only the winning-move relation it is computed on; where an organization's legibility regime selects which contests are admitted or recorded, an acyclic record, even a persistent potential, can be the product of selective contestation, so observed coherence is not evidence that the organization's perspectives are integrated, and an intervention that improves its self-governance may at first increase the disagreement observed

## The objection

[CLAIM-440](CLAIM-440.md) reads an organization's goal-conduciveness valence off the
ordering that prevails in its negotiation, and [TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md) says when such an
ordering exists: when the record of wins in a window is acyclic. Both are
statements about a specified relation. Neither says which contests reach
it.

[CASE-124](../cases.d/CASE-124.md) adds a maintained legibility regime, the categories and channels
through which evidence and objections become readable, which authority
moves. If that regime excludes one perspective's objections, the recorded
negotiation can become perfectly consistent while the organization's
evaluations remain opposed. The potential is then mathematically valid and
describes only the admitted negotiation.

**Three relations, not one.** The relation of the constituents'
preferences; the relation of contests possible under a specified decision
rule; and the relation of wins actually admitted and recorded. [TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md)'s
test answers whether the third admits an ordering. Whether that ordering
reflects the evaluations available in the organization, and whether the
conditions producing it can be revised, are separate questions.

**Accommodation and suppression look the same.** A learned arrangement
can remove a cycle by accommodation or by entrenched precedence
([CLAIM-451](CLAIM-451.md)), and [CLAIM-364](CLAIM-364.md) already holds that low supervisory cost
cannot show non-domination. The same holds of coherence: an acyclic,
persistent record cannot show that the dissent it lacks was satisfied
rather than excluded ([CLAIM-361](CLAIM-361.md): judge relief against the demands, not
against the accounting).

**A prediction.** Raising the legibility of a marginalized perspective,
for example by a protected challenge channel, can reveal conflicts that
were previously invisible. An intervention that improves self-governance
may then increase observed disagreement for a time. Low conflict,
consensus or an easily found potential is not a measure of good
governance.

## What would settle it

A three-perspective agenda model with a latent Condorcet cycle, where a
chair or legibility regime controls which pairwise contests enter the
record. Compare the graph of all possible contests with the graph of
admitted ones, then add a protected challenge channel. [CLAIM-440](CLAIM-440.md) survives
where its valence readings are made only on records shown to admit the
contests that bear on them, as [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md)'s ladder (access, recognition,
credibility, uptake) would require.


Self-reinforcing authority is not itself new, and the DeGroot–Friedkin model of social power evolving by reflected appraisal is the baseline. Jia et al. 2015 ([NOTE-1135](../notes.d/NOTE-1135.md), [THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md)) prove a special case: every issue ends in consensus, so the record is always coherent. Its content is weighted toward the most central members, and members whom no path of influence reaches end with zero power. Friedkin, Jia and Bullo's laboratory test ([NOTE-1126](../notes.d/NOTE-1126.md), [THEORY-902](../theory.d/THEORY-902.md)) supports the individual step: influence on one issue predicted closure on the next, and networks thinned. The predicted end state was not reached, and the authors leave open whether the gain in efficiency goes with more or less collective intelligence. Both exclude by uptake through a fixed network, with no maintained regime deciding who is heard; that regime is what this claim adds. One complication for the prediction above: the supplement argues, untested, that a reserved authority enforcing a full airing of every opinion still concentrates power. A protected challenge channel may therefore reveal disagreement without stopping concentration.

## What it does not say

- That a potential is uninformative. It is informative about the relation
  it was computed on.
- That every coherent record conceals dissent.
