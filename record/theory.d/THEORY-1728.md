# THEORY-1728: Thorne et al. (2018) built a dataset of 185,445 claims derived by altering Wikipedia sentences, with independent annotators labelling each claim against Wikipedia as supported, refuted or not verifiable and recording the evidence sentences (Fleiss κ 0.68)

## The account

Thorne et al. (2018) built a dataset of 185,445 claims derived by altering Wikipedia sentences, with independent annotators labelling each claim against Wikipedia as supported, refuted or not verifiable and recording the evidence sentences (Fleiss κ 0.68). Their retrieve-then-entail baseline is correct 31.87% of the time when correct evidence is required and 50.91% when it is not. Selecting the evidence sentences is the hardest step.
