**Substitute (2026-10-03).** The record reads the book's core results in
Watanabe's *A Widely Applicable Bayesian Information Criterion*
([LIT-616](LIT-616.md)). It defines a model as regular when the map from parameters
to distributions is one-to-one and the Fisher information is positive
definite, and singular otherwise, which is the owner's "degeneracy =
singular Fisher". It states the standard representation theorem (the
book's Theorem 6.1), the real log canonical threshold and its
multiplicity, and the Bayes free-energy asymptotics, and cites the book for
their proofs. The book would add those proofs, the theory of the
generalization error, and WAIC.
