# LEAD-268: Let directed tolerances and the conditions of a grant be learned from the history of interaction, extending the forager that learns its own warning signs, and test whether learned tolerances do anything a fixed threshold does not

## The gap

[CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md) (Active) makes a tolerance directed and the perceiving
perspective's own: Q_{i→j}(y, c) = 1[d_i(y, c) ≤ τ_{i→j}(c)]. [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md)
(Active, demonstrated) derives τ from an activation threshold, with b(c)
and α(c) given. [CLAIM-538](../claims.d/CLAIM-538.md) and [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md) give compatibility for symmetric
bounds on an edge. In every one the tolerance is supplied by the modeller.
[QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) asks whether norms "can be defined from an organization's
own dynamics", and [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md)'s burden is the standard "learned rather than
supplied". [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md)'s forager was built and run as [CASE-421](../cases.d/CASE-421.md) (Active), for
criteria of failure: which features predict a loss of viability. It has
no directed tolerances, no second perspective and no grant.

## What it would take

1. **Start from [CASE-421](../cases.d/CASE-421.md)'s forager** (scratch/studies/lead-177/), and add
   a second process j whose contributions y the forager i receives in
   context c. i's discrepancy measure d_i is its own predicted loss of
   viability given y, learned as [CASE-421](../cases.d/CASE-421.md) learns warning signs.
2. **Learn τ_{i→j}(c) from deviation histories**: i raises or lowers its
   tolerance of j's contributions in c by the realized effect on its
   viability of having accepted past contributions near the bound.
3. **A grant rule**, under which i's conferral ([TERM-103](../terms.d/TERM-103.md)) raises j's share
   of future influence when y is inside τ, as [CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md) states; and,
   in a second arm, grant conditions learned in the same way.
4. **Compare against fixed thresholds**: the same system with τ set by
   [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md)'s formula from a fixed θ, b(c) and α(c), matched on the
   ordinary course.
5. **Probe with an unforeseen change**: change how j's contributions bear
   on i's viability in one context only, and read whether the learned
   tolerance moves there and not elsewhere.

## What would count

Learned tolerances that track the change in the context where it occurs,
and only there, while the fixed threshold does not, would be a standard
learned rather than supplied, at the level of one directed edge (the
burden [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md) state). **Defeater:** learned tolerances
add nothing over a fixed threshold, either because [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md)'s formula
with the forager's learned features as b(c) already reproduces them, or
because the unforeseen change is tracked as well by the fixed arm. A
second check: if j's influence rises with i's conferral only through the
grant rule the modeller wrote, the terms are learned and the authority is
not, and the co-evolution of tolerances with authority that Article 4 §4.4
names stays unbuilt.

## Relation to other leads

[LEAD-243](LEAD-243.md) is the broader multi-agent environment in which implicit
contracts form; this lead is one directed edge of it, and its note of
2026-11-25 points here. [CLAIM-1293](../claims.d/CLAIM-1293.md) and [CLAIM-1239](../claims.d/CLAIM-1239.md) read a tolerance
as negotiated between perspectives ([CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md)'s note of 2026-11-24 on
A16 §8); a third arm, with τ fixed jointly by i and j, would test that
extension.
