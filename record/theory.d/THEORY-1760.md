# THEORY-1760: Jansen, Clark, Downey and Weld (2026) report that LLM-generated scientific laws grounded in up to 100 retrieved papers per query predict results in later-published papers better than laws from model knowledge alone

## The account

Jansen, Clark, Downey and Weld (2026) report that LLM-generated scientific laws grounded in up to 100 retrieved papers per query predict results in later-published papers better than laws from model knowledge alone. The gain is small when generation aims at accuracy (precision 0.90 vs 0.88) and large when it aims at novelty (61% vs 34% of tested predictions supported). Under novelty-focused generation the generating model overestimates how often its predictions will hold.
