# LIT-tmp4vtkl: Transcranial direct current stimulation to lateral prefrontal cortex could increase meta-awareness of mind wandering

Fox, K. C. R. & Christoff, K. (2015). Transcranial direct current
stimulation to lateral prefrontal cortex could increase meta-awareness of
mind wandering. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 112(19):
E2414. DOI 10.1073/pnas.1504686112.

Read in full in NOTE-tmp58cil.

## Key takeaways

- **The alternative reading.** "In addition to, or instead of, increasing
  the frequency of thought, this stimulation may also have altered
  participants' meta-awareness of those thoughts."
- **Why reports may track awareness.** "The mind wandering that
  participants report in an experimental setting might merely represent
  that portion of thought that they become meta-aware of."

## Standing in the record

A supplement to LIT-tmpquab4 for LEAD-141. Its direction is the reverse of
what Metzinger's H1 would need: it supposes that stimulation raised the
agent's awareness of its own thought, where H1 needs it blocked.
