### Added

- ADR-tmppj46c: `bounds`, a reading that marks the limit of a claim's scope, with `bounded_by` derived on the claim; the readings view counts it as use, and the reading-review and read-and-file skills say when to write it. First application: of 124 claim-reading pairs where a claim cited a reading only as a limit, 91 now carry `bounds` and 2 `corroborates`.
