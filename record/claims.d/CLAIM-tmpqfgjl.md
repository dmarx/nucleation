# CLAIM-tmpqfgjl: The defeat tests take one holistic, task-free judgement of fidelity as ground truth, though the line's own theses say fidelity is task-relative, interpreter-relative and not a single ranking

## The objection

The criterion in each central defeat condition is a human judgement with no
task and no receiver named:

- [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md): "Human judgements of communicative fidelity";
- [CLAIM-005](CLAIM-005.md): "held-out human communicative-fidelity judgements";
- [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md): "Human judgements of shared communicative kind";
- [CLAIM-078](CLAIM-078.md): "communicative-identity changes", which A203 §37, quoted there,
  makes "whether humans recognize two realizations as instances of the same
  communicative kind".

The line's own theses say there is no such single quantity:

- [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md): "Transports whose distortion profiles trade off are incomparable
  until a task fixes weights, so no single ranking of translations holds
  across tasks";
- [CLAIM-106](CLAIM-106.md): fidelity is "a graded, potentially asymmetric property of
  transport, not an equivalence relation";
- [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md): "fidelity under a fixed interpreter, across interpreters and
  across states of adaptation are different quantities";
- [TERM-034](../terms.d/TERM-034.md): fidelity is "relative to an interpreter".

A small case shows the difficulty. Rendering A keeps the source's footing and
loses its stance; rendering B keeps the stance and loses the footing. By
[CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md) they are incomparable until a task fixes the weights. A judge asked
which is more faithful ranks one above the other anyway, and so supplies a
weighting nobody stated. The theory can then go one of two ways.

- It fits its weights to the judges. The weights [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md) leaves "to be
  chosen per task" are then chosen to match the criterion, and the
  prediction is circular.
- It keeps its own weights, and a mismatch is put down to the judges having
  weighed for a different task, which [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md) allows. Then no result
  defeats the thesis.

The same holds for the interpreter. The judges are some interpreters, at
some state of adaptation. By [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md) their fidelity is one quantity among
several, and the defeat conditions do not say which.

The "same kind" question has a further gap: there is no wording to give lay
judges. [TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md): "What makes two such systems the same kind is the open
part."

So the line faces a choice the record does not state. If [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md), [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md)
and [CLAIM-106](CLAIM-106.md) are right, the criterion in the defeat conditions is not well
defined. If the criterion is well defined, those theses are in trouble.

## What it does not say

- It does not say holistic judgements carry no information about fidelity.
- It does not say which horn is right. If the relativity theses are wrong,
  the defeat conditions are well posed and those theses fall instead.

## What would answer it

- A defeat condition indexed to a stated task and a stated receiver
  population, with the weights of [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md)'s profile fixed before the data
  are seen.
- Or an argument that holistic judgements fix the weighting non-circularly:
  for instance, weights estimated from one set of renderings and judges and
  tested on another, so that the weighting is a finding and not a fit.
- A QUESTION refining [QUESTION-002](../questions.d/QUESTION-002.md) could hold the open part: which task and
  which receivers fix the human criterion against which fidelity measures are
  validated?

## Where it stands

On 2026-10-10 the record replied. [CLAIM-tmpnttsk](CLAIM-tmpnttsk.md) answers the dilemma: a
holistic judgement is fidelity under the judges' default task, a weighting of
the profile that can be estimated on some renderings and tested on others, so
it is well defined and consistent with [CLAIM-128](CLAIM-128.md), and a weighting fixed before
the test leaves no room to blame the task. That the defeat conditions name no
task or receivers is met by the revised theses [CLAIM-tmpc1o4m](CLAIM-tmpc1o4m.md) and
[CLAIM-tmp3wo5j](CLAIM-tmp3wo5j.md), and is held open in [QUESTION-tmppstva](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmppstva.md). The objection stays
open until the owner decides on those two, since [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)'s and [CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)'s
own conditions are unchanged.
