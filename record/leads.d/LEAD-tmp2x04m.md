# LEAD-tmp2x04m: Compare across interpretive methodologies the actual distribution of interpretive authority, evidential burdens and correction costs, to test whether recovering historical meaning relocates discretion rather than eliminating it

## The idea

A40 §2.4 prices originalist interpretation in the record's cost model:
retrieval, verification, disambiguation, translation, authorization,
revision and residual loss. "This exposes a possible tension between the
originalist objective of constraining discretion and the discretion
involved in reconstructing the governing historical specification. A
methodology can relocate discretion rather than eliminate it."

## What it would settle

Whether the Constraint Principle's rationale, constraining discretion,
holds up when the discretion in reconstructing meaning is counted: the part
of the originalism critique that survives CLAIM-tmpmp2wh. A40 cites
CLAIM-760 for "Work and discretion can be transferred without
disappearing"; CLAIM-760 holds it for work, and this would test it for
discretion.

## What it needs first

A design: which decisions, which methodologies, and a measure of
discretion that does not presuppose one methodology's answer, for example
the spread of outcomes among competent practitioners of each method on the
same questions. Not specified in the exchange.
