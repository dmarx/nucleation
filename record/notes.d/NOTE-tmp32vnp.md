# NOTE-tmp32vnp: Arnstein — A Ladder of Citizen Participation

## Contribution

A typology that sorts forms of participation by the power they confer
over outcomes, with a middle band, tokenism, where voice is granted and
power withheld. It also states the conditions that made power-sharing
real in the programs she saw.

## Key insight

Being heard is not having one's view heeded: participation counts by the
power over the outcome it carries.

## Assumptions

- Power over outcomes is the measure of participation; information and
  voice count only as they lead to it.
- "Have-nots" and "powerholders" are treated as blocs, as each perceives
  the other (p. 217).
- The rungs are ordered; she grants there may be "150 rungs" in reality.

## Key results

- **Thesis** (p. 216): "There is a critical difference between going
  through the empty ritual of participation and having the real power
  needed to affect the outcome of the process."
- **Tokenism** (p. 217): citizens may hear and be heard, "But under these
  conditions they lack the power to insure that their views will be
  heeded by the powerful."
- **Manipulation** (p. 218): "people are placed on rubberstamp advisory
  committees or advisory boards for the express purpose of "educating"
  them or engineering their support."
- **Consultation** (p. 219): "this rung of the ladder is still a sham
  since it offers no assurance that citizen concerns and ideas will be
  taken into account."
- **Placation** (p. 220): "If they are not accountable to a constituency in
  the community and if the traditional power elite hold the majority of
  seats, the have-nots can be easily outvoted and outfoxed." How far they
  are placated depends on "the quality of technical assistance they have
  in articulating their priorities".
- **Partnership** (p. 221): "After the groundrules have been established
  through some form of give-and-take, they are not subject to unilateral
  change." Where power was shared (p. 222), "it has had to be wrested by
  the powerless rather than proffered by the powerful."
- **Delegated power** (p. 222): "city councils have final veto powers even
  when citizens have the majority of seats on the CDA Board."
- **Community control** (p. 224) can be "a new Mickey Mouse game for the
  have-nots by allowing them to gain control but not allowing them"
  sufficient resources (the OCR garbles the rest).
- **Self-limitation** (p. 217): "the typology does not include an analysis
  of the most significant roadblocks to achieving genuine levels of
  participation."

## Concepts

Ladder of citizen participation; non-participation (manipulation,
therapy); tokenism (informing, consultation, placation); citizen power
(partnership, delegated power, citizen control); empty ritual;
redistribution of power; powerholders and have-nots; Model Cities;
ground rules not subject to unilateral change; technical assistance under
the citizens' control.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-523** (legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability can
  fail separately). Corroborates. Informing and consultation make
  citizens' views legible while giving them no efficacy ("no assurance
  that citizen concerns and ideas will be taken into account");
  placation shows efficacy failing through the powerholders' majority.
- **CLAIM-403** (admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and a part in
  revising the standards of uptake are separable). Corroborates.
  Informing and consultation are admission without uptake, and
  partnership's ground rules "not subject to unilateral change" are the
  last stage, a part in setting the terms of uptake.
- **CLAIM-tmpm95mo** (an independent opposition whose capacity to change
  the arrangement is contained by another's control). Corroborates, with
  three instances: citizen majorities under the city council's "final
  veto powers" (p. 222); community control without sufficient resources
  (p. 224); and placation's dependence on technical assistance the
  citizens do not control (p. 220). Her conditions for real partnership
  (own funds, own technicians, fixed ground rules) are the converse.
- **CLAIM-tmpi68s2** (an objection is effective only if formulable,
  legible, taken up, sustainable, corrected and maintained). Related, not
  filed as a relation: technical assistance (formulable), one-way
  informing (not taken up) and paid staff (sustainable) map onto its
  stages.

## Verdict on the exchange's characterization

Faithful but narrower than the paper. A37: "institutions can permit people
to speak without giving them meaningful influence" is the tokenism band
and the "empty ritual" thesis. Her concern is broader: the redistribution
of power, with manipulation and therapy ranked below voice, and real
influence made concrete as seats, vetoes, funds, staff and ground rules.

## Limitations

1969 US federal programs, illustrated by anecdote and agency reports. The
author calls the ladder a simplification and says it omits the
roadblocks. The OCR copy is noisy.
