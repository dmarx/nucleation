The owner asked for the two items the second pass left (entry of 03:41
today).

**[THEORY-087](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-087.md).** The audit said the regular-model Laplace/Occam half "is
established in the sources", and [THEORY-087](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-087.md)'s own promote_when said "the
regular half is derived in the sources". The check found that this holds
for one model class only. MacKay's Occam-factor formula is exact for
linear-in-parameters models with Gaussian noise and prior, and a Gaussian
approximation otherwise ([NOTE-471](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-471.md)). Grünwald & Roos's eq. 16 is cited to
Rissanen and to Suzuki & Yamanishi ([NOTE-760](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-760.md)). Watanabe's Theorem 5 is
about WBIC. The free-energy expansion that would give the regular penalty
is his Theorem 2, also cited ([NOTE-473](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-473.md)). So only the exact case is
promoted: [THEORY-718](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-718.md), with the (k/2) log n step written out as the
record's algebra. [THEORY-087](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-087.md) extends it. Its promote_when now asks for a
held proof of the general regular expansion (Schwarz, or a Laplace or
Bernstein–von Mises argument) as well as the singular one. The promotion
was smaller than the audit proposed, because the premise was overstated.

**The thirteen claims citing [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md).** Each was read for what it takes
from Blackwell's theorem, and whether its experiments have finitely many
outcomes.

- **Re-grounded: [CLAIM-323](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-323.md).** Three situations and two-valued reports. It
  uses the definition and the direct implication.
- **Both grounds: [CLAIM-237](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-237.md) and [CLAIM-326](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-326.md).** [CLAIM-237](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-237.md)'s measurement
  scenarios are finite, but its situations need not be. [CLAIM-326](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-326.md)'s Z is
  finite, but its renderings' outcome spaces are not restricted. Each gets
  a note saying when the finite THEORY suffices.
- **Unchanged: [CLAIM-050](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-050.md), 125, 126, 199, 212, 218, 225, 228, 258 and
  259.** They cite [THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md) for its definitions (the n-tuple of measures
  on a common space, the risk-set order), or apply the order to texts and
  images with unrestricted outcome spaces. Most are interpretive. [CLAIM-225](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-225.md)
  uses only the direct implication, with sums over texts; that implication
  holds on any outcome space, but the record's Active statement of it is
  for finite ones, so it is not re-grounded.
