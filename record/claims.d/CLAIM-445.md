# CLAIM-445: A protective regime, in a person, an institution or a system of states, becomes self-maintaining when the interventions it authorizes shift the evidence used to judge its necessity, the authority and resources of those who judge it, the capacities of its constituents or the threat environment itself in ways that favour its continuation; this can happen when the original threat was real and the first protection apt, and it is a failure only where the regime has become less responsive to evidence that it is no longer needed

## The claim

The owner, 2026-10-23: "this seems like it would also be supported
political and social scientific observations about state/societal
protectionism".

Institutions make authority, threat classification, information access
and persistence easier to observe separately than a mind does, so they are
a good test of the mechanism, not just a metaphor for it.

**Three feedbacks.**

1. *Epistemic.* Protection restricts which evidence is encountered or
   admitted, as when the judgement that an emergency has ended rests on
   information the emergency apparatus controls.
2. *Political-institutional.* Protection distributes authority, resources
   and constituencies that then defend it. This is Pierson's policy
   feedback, which the record reads through increasing returns
   ([THEORY-885](../theory.d/THEORY-885.md)): policies supply resources and incentives, and information
   and interpretive cues, and so make the politics that decides their
   future. Emergency-powers scholarship (Scheuerman) adds executives'
   incentives to declare, prolong and use emergencies, with no fabrication
   of threat required.
3. *Threat-producing.* Protection changes others' responses. Jervis's
   security dilemma: one state's defensive measures lower another's
   security and provoke the responses that confirm the first state's fear.
   The threat becomes real as a consequence of the response, with neither
   side malicious or irrational.

Securitization (Buzan and Wæver) supplies the authorizing move: calling an
issue an existential threat, where an audience accepts it, changes the
permissible decision procedure and who decides. Tilly's account of war
making and state making treats protection as a process that reproduces the
organizations that provide it.

**Three kinds of persistence.** Evidence-tracking (the threat persists);
institutional inertia (the threat has declined and reversal is costly);
endogenous protective lock-in (the regime sustains the evidence,
interests, authority or conditions of its continuation). Only the third is
the analogue of governance-maintained anxiety, and the three can coexist.

**Economic protection is the same shape through material interest.** A
temporary tariff creates investments and a constituency that make its
removal costlier. It need not be self-defeating. Juhász (read from the
2015 working draft, [NOTE-1122](../notes.d/NOTE-1122.md), [THEORY-919](../theory.d/THEORY-919.md)) found that French
regions more shielded from British competition by the Napoleonic blockade,
a war measure, not a tariff, built more mechanized cotton-spinning
capacity that stayed after 1815. What persisted was the industry's
location and productivity, not the protection, and the long-run estimates
rest on a weak instrument. The contrast is between
capability-building protection, which can make itself obsolete, and
dependence-building protection, which makes its own continuation more
necessary: the individual contrast between skill acquisition and
regulatory lock-in.

**Schematic dynamics.** With b the authoritative threat assessment, g the
authority of protective institutions, r their resources and
constituencies, u the measures taken and T the actual threat:
g′ = G(b, g, r, oversight), r′ = R(r, u), b′ = B(b, evidence selected under
u), T′ = F(T, u, u_other). Protection changes the observations used to
judge its necessity and the standing of those who judge it, and through
others' responses can change T. Genuine protection lowers T; the
interesting regime is the one in which g stays high partly because the
policies made under g make its own reduction less likely.

## The test

When external threat declines, does protective authority decline? If not,
is the residue explained by continuing genuine risk, by switching costs,
or by the institution's control of evidence and revision? Independent
oversight, changes of jurisdiction and exogenous changes of threat
separate them.

## What it does not say

- That a state is a person with clinical anxiety. The claim is that both
  may realize one abstract causal organization ([CLAIM-427](CLAIM-427.md)'s evidence
  grades apply).
- That positive feedback or path dependence is pathological. The last
  clause of the title carries the weight: what fails is governance over
  the protective regime itself, the capacity to discover that protection is
  no longer needed and to make that discovery consequential for who
  governs ([CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md), [CLAIM-359](CLAIM-359.md)).
- That publics resist the threat framing they are offered. Ku and Finch's
  South Korean case ([NOTE-1133](../notes.d/NOTE-1133.md)) finds publics keeping ordinary
  routines against the anxiety, while the security narrative is ignored
  and held latently, which they argue "may allow for the reproduction of
  securitising practices": accepting securitization delegates the
  management of anxiety to elites, the epistemic feedback from the
  audience's side. It cuts the other way once: when the military itself
  disrupted routines, the public blamed the military and the
  government's secrecy.

## Note of 2026-10-30: what holds it back

Its premise [CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md) is Proposed: no result in the record measures
defensive authority apart from threat belief. Its own test, whether
protective authority declines when an independently measured threat
declines, with the residue divided among genuine risk, switching costs and
the regime's control of evidence, has not been run on any case the record
holds; Bjørnskov, Voigt and Khesali ([THEORY-911](../theory.d/THEORY-911.md)) state the prolongation
mechanism as a hypothesis and do not test it.
