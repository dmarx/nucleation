# CASE-031: Marot's "A une Damoyselle malade" and Hofstadter's translations of it

## The case

U1 asked for the Hofstadter podcast; A2 found Radiolab's "100 Flowers" (2014)
and *Le Ton beau de Marot* ([LIT-766](../literature.d/LIT-766.md)). The poem's constraints, as A2 gives
them: "28 lines, three syllables per line, rhyming couplets, a light and
playful tone, the poet's name embedded in the middle, and the opening line
echoed at the end." Its semantic content is "almost trivial". Hofstadter
"sent the poem to dozens of people and collected radically different
translations".

Two of its renderings are cases of their own: [CASE-019](CASE-019.md) and [CASE-011](CASE-011.md).

## What it can show

That several preservation targets compete, so that no single rendering is
*the* translation. The manuscript §1: "meter, sound, playfulness,
intelligibility, and context are different preservation targets". The details
come from the assistant's reading of the episode transcript (A2, A4), and
[LIT-766](../literature.d/LIT-766.md) is unread here, so the case stands on a report.

## Note of 2026-10-10: the replies

A candidate for the necessity half of [CLAIM-061](../claims.d/CLAIM-061.md) ([CLAIM-tmpfe37n](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfe37n.md)): renderings
that keep the address and tone while changing what is said. It stands on a
report until [LIT-766](../literature.d/LIT-766.md) is read.

## Note of 2026-10-10: a documented case

The search of 2026-10-10 that the owner asked for checked this case as the
candidate for [CLAIM-061](../claims.d/CLAIM-061.md)'s necessity half, and finds that it does not fill
that role. The renderings it could reach, French's "Fairest Friend", "Hi
Toots!" and Hofstadter's prose versions, keep almost all of Marot's
propositions: the greeting, the room as a prison, the order to recover and
go out, the warning about sweets, the blessing. What varies is form,
register, persona and tone. These are changes of words, not of propositions.
That is this case's existing use for [CLAIM-115](../claims.d/CLAIM-115.md): competing preservation
targets. It is not A without P ([CLAIM-tmpfe37n](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfe37n.md)). Reading [LIT-766](../literature.d/LIT-766.md) could change
this only if some rendering there changes what the poem says and Hofstadter
judges that the get-well address survives. The search found no report that
one does.

The necessity role passes to [CASE-tmp25bmx](CASE-tmp25bmx.md), Phillips's handshake for the
"holy kiss", which changes what is directed and was judged by Nida to keep
the greeting. That case supplies documentation. It does not supply reader
evidence, which this case lacks too.
