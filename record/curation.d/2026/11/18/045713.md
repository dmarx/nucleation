The owner asked on 2026-11-18 to "revisit the ADR-100 change to make sure
there weren't any broken links we want to repair", noting that an entry
can carry several lines, so a line can be added without taking one away.

## The split

ADR-100 moved 29 entries from organizational-normativity to
dependence-and-domination, in place. It kept organizational-normativity
second on the three that take part in that line's argument under ADR-085
(QUESTION-460, QUESTION-468, CLAIM-685). A script over every moved entry
checked each relation to an entry whose first line is
organizational-normativity, in both directions.

- **No participation was severed.** No moved entry answers, refines,
  objects to or bears on an organizational-normativity entry without
  carrying the line.
- **Two dependencies were severed, as ADR-085 provides.** These are links
  the rule does not count as taking part in a line's argument:
  - CLAIM-687 `complements` CLAIM-624. ADR-100 named this one and dropped
    the line ("`complements` alone does not add a line").
  - CLAIM-707, filed later in organizational-normativity, `complements`
    CLAIM-678.

  Under the rule as it stands, neither should carry the other line. If
  the owner wants complementary claims to share a line additively, that is
  a change to ADR-085, not a repair. It is left to the owner.

## Drift since

The same check over the whole record found 26 entries that answer, refine
(questions only), object to or bear on an entry of another line and do not
carry that line. Most were filed after ADR-085, many from the exchange
"Assess Depp Heard Abuse" and its readings. Each now carries the missing
line after its own, with a version and a history note naming the relation:

- CASE-238, CASE-239, CASE-240, CASE-242, CASE-243, CASE-248, CASE-249,
  CASE-269, CASE-274, CASE-275, CASE-282, CASE-286, CASE-290 and CASE-292;
- CLAIM-649, CLAIM-651, CLAIM-653, CLAIM-654, CLAIM-655, CLAIM-658,
  CLAIM-663, CLAIM-674, CLAIM-703, CLAIM-727, CLAIM-733 and CLAIM-742.

The repairs follow ADR-085's own terms:
- No entry's first line changes, and nothing cascades.
- CLAIM-534 remains ADR-085's listed exception.
- Accounts (SYNTHESIS) are not changed: their line is their scope
  (ADR-083), and a `bears_on` from an account maps a question rather than
  arguing it.
- CLAIM-742 gains the set-aside authenticity line, because it bears on
  QUESTION-068. That line's status is unchanged, so it sets nothing aside.

## Note for the lint

The lint's "share no line" warnings for `rests_on` and `complements` are
dependencies by ADR-085's terms and are not repaired here.
