# LIT-1534: A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits

Elhage, N., Nanda, N., Olsson, C., Henighan, T., Joseph, N., Mann, B., et
al. (2021). A mathematical framework for transformer circuits.
*Transformer Circuits Thread*, 22 December 2021.
https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html

Read in full on 2026-10-26 ([NOTE-1189](../notes.d/NOTE-1189.md)). Two accounts were drawn from
it: [THEORY-988](../theory.d/THEORY-988.md) and [THEORY-977](../theory.d/THEORY-977.md).

## Why this is here

Registered and read at the owner's request of 2026-10-26, after an answer to
"how's the record on neural circuit theory?" found that the record held
almost none of the core circuits literature. The record's transformer
circuits line rested on this paper without holding it. The induction-head
paper ([LIT-1538](LIT-1538.md)) takes its induction head and its K-composition
mechanism from here, and [THEORY-973](../theory.d/THEORY-973.md) and the readings of Wang et al.'s
refined learning coefficients ([THEORY-975](../theory.d/THEORY-975.md), [THEORY-967](../theory.d/THEORY-967.md)) and of
Musat et al. ([THEORY-981](../theory.d/THEORY-981.md), [THEORY-1009](../theory.d/THEORY-1009.md)) use its two-layer
attention-only setting and its vocabulary of QK and OV circuits. Its
observation that the residual stream has no privileged basis is the setting
of [THEORY-802](../theory.d/THEORY-802.md) and [THEORY-018](../theory.d/THEORY-018.md).
