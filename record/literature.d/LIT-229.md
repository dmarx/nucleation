# LIT-229: Meaningful Information

Paul M. B. Vitányi (2001), *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 52(10):4617–4626 (Oct 2006); earlier version in Proc. ISAAC 2002 (LNCS); first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [arXiv:cs/0111053](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0111053)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpjp0xd](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpjp0xd.md).*

- **The two-part split.** K(x) =⁺ min_T {K(T) + K(x | T)}. A total
  recursive f is a sufficient statistic for x when K(f) + l_x(f) =⁺ K(x),
  and sophistication is the least such K(f).
- **A universal model trivialises it.** With partial recursive models,
  soph(x) =⁺ 0 for every x, because the universal machine absorbs every
  model into the data part (Lemma 6.2). Totality is what keeps the split
  non-trivial.
- **Both extremes occur.** Random strings have sophistication about 0
  (Theorem 6.5(ii)). For every n some string has sophistication above
  n − log n − 2 log log n (Theorem 6.5(iv)): nearly incompressible, yet
  almost all structure.
- **The model classes agree, asymmetrically.** Finite-set → probabilistic
  → total-function sufficient statistics correspond within a constant
  (Lemma 7.1). The way back costs O(log n), in sufficiency too
  (Lemma 7.2).
- **Uncomputable.** soph is not recursive (Theorem 8.1). A
  sufficient-statistic oracle computes K and the halting sequence
  (Theorem 8.3). The witness program is as hard to get from x as K(x) is
  (Theorem 8.5).
- **Stated, not proved here.** Sophistication is where Kolmogorov's
  structure function first reaches K(x) (§IX, deferred to
  Vereshchagin–Vitányi).

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped under
the heading *Relating K-complexity to minimum sufficient statistics*. Read
in full on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-tmpjp0xd](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpjp0xd.md). It is the record's statement of
the minimal sufficient statistic for total-function models, and of why
that class, not the universal one, is where the split into structure and
noise makes sense. Its main results are filed as [THEORY-tmpevdge](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpevdge.md).

It is the predecessor of Vereshchagin and Vitányi's structure-function
paper ([LIT-239](LIT-239.md)), which holds the full finite-set theory this paper
transfers. Read the two together. For [THEORY-036](../theory.d/THEORY-036.md) (Dennett's real
patterns) the reading adds a contrast: strings of near-maximal
sophistication are nearly incompressible, so compressibility and
meaningful structure come apart for an individual object. The Sefidgaran
papers filed under the same heading ([LIT-233](LIT-233.md), [LIT-236](LIT-236.md)) share none of its
machinery. No instruction for machine-learning practice.
