# LIT-tmpdvx5j: Biological Embedding of Early-Life Adversity and a Scoping Review of the Evidence for Intergenerational Epigenetic Transmission of Stress and Trauma in Humans

Zhou, A. & Ryan, J. (2023). *Genes*, 14(8), 1639.

## Key takeaways

- Up to mid-2023, the human literature is small, observational, mostly
  retrospective and mostly maternal, and it measures offspring methylation
  in tissues other than the germline.
- Paternal-only designs are two, both epigenome-wide: one in sperm
  (Vietnam veterans with PTSD) and one in blood.
- The authors judge the separation of epigenetic transmission from
  genetic, environmental and behavioural transmission unresolved, and not
  easily resolved.
