# NOTE-1352: Marsland, Cui and Mehta — The minimum environmental perturbation principle

## Contribution

MacArthur (1969–70) showed that equilibria of his consumer–resource model
minimize a quadratic in the consumer abundances, and could not interpret
the objective outside one special case. This paper moves the optimization
to the space of environmental states, states exactly when an optimization
principle exists in contemporary niche theory (interaction symmetry, up to
a state-dependent rescaling of abundances), and gives the objective one
reading for all such models: how far the community pushes the environment
from where it would rest without consumers.

## Key insight

Species are constraints. Each species' zero-net-growth isocline bounds the
region of environments in which nobody can grow; the community settles
where the environment is as close as possible to its unperturbed supply
point inside that region, and the species that persist are the
constraints that bind, at abundances equal to their Lagrange multipliers.

## Assumptions

- **Niche-theory form**: dN_i/dt = N_i g_i(R), dR_α/dt = h_α(R) + Σ_i N_i
  q_iα(R); all interactions mediated by the environment R.
- **Symmetry**: dg_i/d(a_j N_j) = dg_j/d(a_i N_i) for some positive,
  possibly state-dependent a_i. Appendix B shows this holds iff q_iα =
  −a_i(R) b_α(R) ∂g_i/∂R_α with a diagonal b (generically), and then
  h_α/b_α = −∂d/∂R_α for some d.
- A stable supply point R0 of the intrinsic environmental dynamics, b_α >
  0, and invertibility conditions used in Appendix B.
- **Monostability** for the assembly result.

## Key results

- **MEPP.** Equilibria satisfy 0 = a_i N_i g_i, 0 = h_α/b_α − Σ a_i N_i
  ∂g_i/∂R_α, g_i ≤ 0, a_i N_i ≥ 0: the KKT conditions for minimizing d(R0,
  R) subject to g_i(R) ≤ 0, with a_i N_i the multipliers.
- **The measures.** Logistic resources: d = ½ Σ w_α r_α K_α⁻¹ (R_α −
  K_α)², with R ≥ 0 imposed explicitly (eq. 14). Interacting plants, when
  r_α = r and w_α ∝ a_α: d ∝ (1 − Σ a_α R_α)² (eq. 17). Predators: an added
  Σ u_a P_a, symmetric only if e_i = e0/η_i, which corrects MacArthur's
  claim that the model is generically symmetric (eq. 21). Chemostat: a
  weighted Kullback–Leibler divergence, τ⁻¹ Σ w_α[R0_α ln(R0_α/R_α) −
  (R0_α − R_α)], which needs no explicit feasibility constraint (eq. 24).
- **Asymmetric models.** Byproduct secretion and Liebig's law break
  symmetry; an equilibrium still minimizes the chemostat d about a
  shifted, self-consistent supply point R̃0, found by iteration (eqs. 27,
  A26).
- **Rotifers.** For two Brachionus species on two algae, the symmetry
  condition (maximal carbon uptake independent of food) holds for B.
  calyciflorus within error and only at low food for B. rubens; three
  fitted parameters per species give good fits (Table 2).
- **Assembly.** Every successful invasion adds a constraint, so the
  minimized d cannot fall: "community assembly and evolution are
  unidirectional", and limit cycles or chaos in composition are ruled out
  in this class.
- **Duality with MacArthur** (Appendix A): with resource extinction
  handled through R̄(N) = max(0, K(1 − r⁻¹ Σ N c)), MacArthur's Q(N) is the
  Lagrange dual of MEPP. MacArthur's sum over all resources is wrong when
  any resource goes extinct; the correct Q is piecewise.

## Concepts

- **Supply point R0**: the environmental state the intrinsic dynamics
  rest at without consumers.
- **ZNGI**: the surface g_i(R) = 0; the outer envelope of the pool's ZNGIs
  bounds the uninvadable region Ω.
- **Environmental perturbation d(R0, R)**: the function whose gradient,
  rescaled by b, is the supply vector; zero at R0.
- **Interaction symmetry**: equal effects of a rescaled abundance change
  of i on j and of j on i, mediated through the environment.

## Connections

Gatto (1982, 1990) saw the rescaling freedom and used the KKT conditions
for MacArthur's model; this paper generalizes his. The companion
[LIT-1231](../literature.d/LIT-1231.md) runs the same duality from the optimization side, for
quadratic programming. Chizat and Bach's optimality condition for
measures ([LIT-1232](../literature.d/LIT-1232.md)) is the continuum form of the same KKT structure.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-369](../claims.d/CLAIM-369.md)** is the population-space side of this paper's result. Its
  objective ½‖b − Un‖² + cᵀn is MacArthur's Q with resources eliminated,
  and its KKT points are the uninvadable equilibria. Two things this
  reading adds. First, read as the fast-resource reduction of a
  consumer–resource model, the claim's A = UᵀU and r = Uᵀb − c hold only
  while every resource stays positive. Once a resource is driven
  extinct, its row drops out and the effective objective is MacArthur's
  piecewise Q (Appendix A), a point MacArthur himself missed. The claim
  states the Lotka–Volterra form directly, so it is not wrong, but a
  third boundary belongs beside its two: the reduction from resources. Second, the dual (environment-space)
  reading gives the claim's objective a physical meaning: the
  perturbation the active components impose on the supply.
- **[QUESTION-083](../questions.d/QUESTION-083.md)**, **[QUESTION-132](../questions.d/QUESTION-132.md)**: in this class, assembly is
  monotone in d. Differentiated specialists that each recover from rarity
  are exactly the binding constraints, and no assembly sequence can undo
  an invasion except by killing species. That is a structural limit on
  "revisable" authority in any analogue built on symmetric resource
  competition: revision needs an external removal or an asymmetry.
- No instruction for machine-learning practice; nothing for the
  anthology.

## Limitations

- **Symmetry is the whole hinge.** The asymmetric extension needs the
  equilibrium to construct d, so it is a reformulation, not a prediction.
- The monotone-assembly result also needs monostability, and needs every
  species once introduced to remain in the pool as a constraint; it says
  nothing about systems with alternative stable states, which Liebig's
  law "generically" has.
- The rotifer test checks the symmetry condition on growth curves; the
  zooplankton assembly experiment of Figure 5 is hypothetical.
- Many displayed equations in Appendices B and C did not extract
  legibly from the arXiv PDF and were followed from the prose.
- No errors of substance found. Small slips: "a optimization
  principle", "resource contribute".

## Open questions

- For asymmetric niche models with alternative stable states, is there
  any monotone quantity of assembly?
- Does d, measured in a real community, rise with richness as C5 says?
