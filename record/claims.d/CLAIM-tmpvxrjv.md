# CLAIM-tmpvxrjv: Identification is not constitution: neither the Yoneda lemma, nor the invariance of a character, nor predictive or interventional success shows that relations constitute what a communicative act is, because an ontology of intrinsically individuated objects with relational properties can reproduce the same predictions

## The claim

Three things can be said of a communicative act's relations, and the first
two do not give the third.

- **Categorical identification.** In a specified category, an object's full
  hom-functor fixes it up to isomorphism. That is the Yoneda lemma.
- **Operational identification.** A chosen family of probes, interventions
  or predictive models tells some realizations apart from others.
- **Constitution.** Certain relations partly make the act the kind of act it
  is ([CLAIM-tmpw83rc](CLAIM-tmpw83rc.md)).

A191 §4 states the split: "Yoneda establishes the first. Our experiments
investigate the second. Moderate communicative structural realism asserts the
third. Neither of the first two logically entails the third." A187 had made
the first half of the point at U52: "Yoneda establishes the first. It does not
independently establish the second." It pairs with the distinction between
descriptive, causal and constitutive relations ([TERM-tmp4ztih](../terms.d/TERM-tmp4ztih.md)), which A191
§11 draws and A203 §16 repeats: "Causal intervention results can support a
constitutive account, but they do not by themselves prove metaphysical
constitution."

**Characters.** A198 §VI separates three senses of "essential":
"**Representational independence** … **Classificatory sufficiency** …
**Ontological constitution**". "Character theory can establish the first, and
under suitable mathematical assumptions the second. Your non-eliminative OSR
makes a claim about the third. It would be a mistake to infer the third
automatically from the first two." So the owner's character analogy, at its
strongest, gives classificatory priority ([CLAIM-tmpaedgb](CLAIM-tmpaedgb.md)) and not constitution.

**Predictive success.** A203 §35 separates an ontological, a mathematical and
an empirical thesis: "These claims support one another, but they are not
logically equivalent. A successful predictive model does not prove OSR. A
mathematical character does not establish metaphysical constitution." A214 §13
gives the reason: "A traditional ontology of intrinsically individuated
objects plus relational properties might reproduce the same statistical
predictions." So the data underdetermine the ontology. A214 then splits the
argument into two parts. The "**Conceptual argument**" is that some acts
"cannot be adequately specified without reference to constitutive social and
normative relationships". The "**Empirical argument**" is that relational
models capture stable, discriminating, predictive properties of those kinds.
Success in the second would support a real-patterns reading of communicative
kinds and make a non-eliminative structuralism attractive. Only the first
bears on constitution. A218 §13: "The central ontological thesis must be
defended through argument concerning social acts and supported by robust
explanatory evidence, not derived automatically from algebraic or statistical
models."

**The stronger form.** The record's reading of the lemma goes further than the
exchange's ([THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md), from [LIT-221](../literature.d/LIT-221.md) read in [NOTE-195](../notes.d/NOTE-195.md)). The Yoneda lemma
fixes an object only up to isomorphism, among objects the category already
has. y(c) is indexed by every object of C, and the conclusion is an
isomorphism between two of them. So as a formal version of "an object is
determined by its relations" it presupposes the relata. It cannot establish
constitution, and it gives elimination of the relata no support at all. Lam
and Wüthrich reach the matching conclusion for category theory generally
([LIT-222](../literature.d/LIT-222.md); [THEORY-038](../theory.d/THEORY-038.md)): morphisms relate objects the category presupposes.
That fits the non-eliminative choice the owner made at U54.

## What it does not say

- That the ontological thesis is false or idle. It says only that the
  mathematics and the data underdetermine it.
- That the three senses are unrelated. A198 §VI: "we can formulate a coherent
  metaphysical position in which they reinforce one another."
- What *would* establish constitution. A214's conceptual argument is named,
  not given. The question of when relational structure warrants treating a
  category as a real kind is [QUESTION-tmplp4fu](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmplp4fu.md).

A187 §8 proposed a narrower disclaimer for filing, "Observational relational
identity does not entail radical OSR". It is merged here rather than filed
separately: the manuscript's relational account does not commit it to ontic
structural realism, and Yoneda does not supply the commitment.
