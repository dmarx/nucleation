# SYNTHESIS-116: Unresolved frustration occasions self-formation

*A thematic account, across lines, of principle P5 of the owner's manuscript (scratch/manuscripts/from-obstruction-to-reorganization/, §7.1, on its own branch). Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which [ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**The occasion.** [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis): an organization's regulatory difficulty is frustration, "a relation between maintained, consequential demands and the incompatibilities, constraints or countervailing processes that limit their realization". [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis): frustration is ordinal. Relief is "strict enlargement" of the jointly satisfiable sets, and "dropping or suppressing a demand is not relief".

**The sufficient condition.** [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis, version 5) holds that an unresolved discrepancy is an occasion for self-formation, "and not a guarantee of it". It suffices that the agent's own activity on the discrepancy produce a maintained capacity, either a new distinction or a new channel, "that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later ones". This is tested by transfer, and by intervention on the producing processes while the capacity forms. The claim states a sufficient condition, not a definition (its `defeated_if`). [TERM-099](../terms.d/TERM-099.md) (Active) names the product, self-formation.

**What maintenance needs.** [CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md) (Active, demonstrated, thesis): where capacities turn over and are produced only by others, with no basal production, a persisting capacity "needs a productive cycle among its ancestors". The cycle is necessary, not sufficient.

**What does not count.** [CLAIM-540](../claims.d/CLAIM-540.md) (Active, demonstrated, disclaimed): an observer's updated estimate is not a change in what selects. The figures are 2/3 against 1/2.

**A construction.** In [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) (Active, fictional, construction), below a threshold of discrepancy nothing forms, and above it a capacity forms and serves later conflicts of its class.

**A case left open.** [QUESTION-488](../questions.d/QUESTION-488.md) (Open): a uniform change of priority, which neither refines a distinction nor enlarges a reachable set.

**Learning, defined more broadly.** [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md) (Proposed): "an experience-dependent change in the system's retained organization that alters what it can later do", judged against a counterfactual. No discrepancy is required.

## Not stated on the record

- **Learning is the formation of a maintained capacity occasioned by a discrepancy.** The manuscript's P5 stated this. It turns [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s sufficient condition into a definition and relabels self-formation as learning. Latent learning separates the two: exploration without reward forms a map later used, so learning by [TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md) occurs with no discrepancy.
- **That a discrepancy below threshold is never learned from, in general.** The threshold belongs to [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md)'s construction.

## Note of 2026-11-21: how a learned representation draws a new distinction

Filed since, from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", with its part account [SYNTHESIS-120](SYNTHESIS-120.md) (`learned-structure`). These entries bear on this account's first product, a new distinction. For its next version to weigh:

- **A threshold below which nothing forms.** A factored feature under weight decay switches on only when its signal s exceeds the decay λ. At s = λ a pitchfork in the parameters, algebraic relaxation and a dip in the local coefficient from 1 to 3/4 coincide ([CLAIM-807](../claims.d/CLAIM-807.md), A's, checked). Below threshold nothing forms. This parallels [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md)'s threshold, under which discrepancies "are tolerated … and form nothing". The parallel is the assistant's: no entry reads the model as self-formation, and nothing in it is a conflict among demands in [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md)'s sense.
- **Formed distinctions gate later ones.** With a parent gate the child switches on only when gs > λ, so acquisition follows "a partial order of dependencies rather than a fixed coarse-to-fine chronology" ([CLAIM-828](../claims.d/CLAIM-828.md), A's). A distinction once formed lowers the threshold of those depending on it. The assistant reads this as one way a capacity "serves a class of later ones", which [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) requires.
- **Active before a passive observer sees it.** Onset and the singular point coincide, but sample PCA detects the feature only after a window of order √(D/N) ([CLAIM-837](../claims.d/CLAIM-837.md), the assistant's). The feature has more than one date: encoding, stability and expression ([CLAIM-828](../claims.d/CLAIM-828.md)). [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s two tests, transfer and intervention while the capacity forms, would need a declared date and a probe tied to the task.
- **What it does not reach.** A gradient learner's threshold is set by its objective and regularizer, so these entries do not bear on whether the formation is the agent's *own* ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)). None runs [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s tests; [LEAD-207](../leads.d/LEAD-207.md) (Open) proposes a study that would date them. That a stage of learning is a transition among singular strata is held only as a question ([QUESTION-514](../questions.d/QUESTION-514.md)).

This account's "Not stated" link is unaffected: none of these makes [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) a definition of learning. No component, status or link of this account is changed. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session).
