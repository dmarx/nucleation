# QUESTION-265: Is "psychopathic" a category error when said of a corporation, or can the construct, its affective features included, be assigned to a corporation functionally, and if so, what in a corporation would remorse, or its absence, be?

## Why it is a question

The owner, 2026-10-23: "how's the nucleation record on topics like
corporate psychopathy? qua treating the corporation as a sociopathic
entity." It was not, before this filing. Every "psychopath" in the record
was a person's disorder or Wolf's stipulated case ([THEORY-147](../theory.d/THEORY-147.md)).

The thesis needs three premises, and the record holds unequal parts of
them.

1. **The corporation is an agent.** French ([THEORY-131](../theory.d/THEORY-131.md)) and Pettit
   ([THEORY-139](../theory.d/THEORY-139.md)) say so; List's result on aggregation ([THEORY-127](../theory.d/THEORY-127.md), Active)
   and [CLAIM-271](../claims.d/CLAIM-271.md) say a group's attitudes can be no member's. Velasquez ([LIT-1429](../literature.d/LIT-1429.md),
   [LIT-1432](../literature.d/LIT-1432.md)) denies it: responsibility needs a mind that forms intentions and a
   body it controls, and a corporation has neither.
2. **Its wrongs are its own, not distributed to its members.** Isaacs
   ([LIT-698](../literature.d/LIT-698.md)) on collective guilt, and Floridi ([LIT-899](../literature.d/LIT-899.md)) on harm no node
   intends.
3. **It lacks the moral emotions.** Huebner ([THEORY-125](../theory.d/THEORY-125.md)) allows a group
   functional, unfelt emotions, and has found no actual case; List
   ([NOTE-340](../notes.d/NOTE-340.md)) doubts a group agent is phenomenally conscious. Nothing in
   the record addresses remorse, guilt or empathy in a corporation.

The third premise is where "psychopathic" gets its force. The record's
own framework bears on it: [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md), reopened by the owner on
2026-10-23, characterizes affect functionally, after Frijda ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)),
as an appraisal of an event's pertinence to a concern eliciting action
readiness, and so lets affect be assigned to a corporation. The owner:
"one of the specific things our framework equips us to do is to assign
affect to corporation-like things." The question is then not whether a
corporation can lack remorse but what its remorse would be, and how it
would differ from its fear of sanction.

It also has a rival that shares its name. Boddy ([LIT-1431](../literature.d/LIT-1431.md)) says
"corporate psychopaths" are people, individuals with psychopathic traits
who rise in corporations. An answer has to say whether a corporation's
harms need its members' traits at all.

## What would count as an answer

A claim that says, of the clinical construct's features (Hare and
Neumann's four factors, [THEORY-892](../theory.d/THEORY-892.md)), which can be said of a corporation and in what sense. It should say whether the absence it names is a deficit or a design. It should say
what does the work in a corporation that a moral sense does in a person,
and tested on a documented case, it should separate the corporation's
contribution from its members'.

## What would not answer it

A list of corporate wrongs, however long, since a person can do wrong
without psychopathy. Nor would the thesis restated as a metaphor without
saying which features transfer, or a verdict on whether corporations are
bad.
