# LIT-981: Geometric diffusions as a tool for harmonic analysis and structure definition of data: Diffusion maps

R. R. Coifman, S. Lafon, A. B. Lee, M. Maggioni, B. Nadler, F. Warner, S. W. Zucker (2005), *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 102(21), 7426–7431*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-784](../notes.d/NOTE-784.md) (Skimmed).*

- **Symmetry is the starting point.** The kernel is required to be
  symmetric, positivity preserving and positive semi-definite. The Markov
  normalization is not symmetric, but its conjugate is, and that conjugate
  carries the spectral decomposition.
- **Diffusion coordinates.** The top eigenfunctions, scaled by λ^m, give an
  embedding in which Euclidean distance equals diffusion distance at time
  m. A truncation to the significant eigenfunctions is approximately
  isometric.
- **Geometry apart from density.** A family of normalizations recovers the
  graph Laplacian (α = 0), the backward Fokker–Planck operator (α = 1/2) or
  the Laplace–Beltrami operator (α = 1). The heat-kernel convergence
  theorem is stated without proof, which is deferred to a companion paper.
- **"Directed diffusion"** means diffusion along a function's level sets
  through an anisotropic distance. It is not a non-reversible walk.
