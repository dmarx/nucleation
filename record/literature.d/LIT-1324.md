# LIT-1324: The Theory of Economic Regulation

Stigler, G. J. (1971). The Theory of Economic Regulation. *The Bell Journal of Economics and Management Science* 2(1): 3–21. doi:10.2307/3003160.

Not read: no lawful copy was reached on 2026-10-21 (see status_note). The
summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for; the OECD's
report ([LIT-1325](LIT-1325.md), read) and Schram and Ule ([LIT-1340](LIT-1340.md), read) cite it
for the same thesis.

## Why this is here

A20: "George Stigler's classic account explicitly treats government regulation as something industries may demand for their own benefit." The classical source for the conventional sense of capture that [TERM-066](../terms.d/TERM-066.md) has to distinguish from capture of the conditions of decision ([CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md)). Stigler appears elsewhere in the record only in unrelated works ([LIT-044](LIT-044.md), [LIT-1054](LIT-1054.md)).
