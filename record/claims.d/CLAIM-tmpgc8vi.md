# CLAIM-tmpgc8vi: A meme's agnotological effect is set by its position in an organization of interpretation and authority, not by its propositional content: perspectives that share a meme can differ in characteristic ignorance by applying it selectively, and perspectives with different memes can share one

## The claim

A6 §2: "The meme's agnotological significance is therefore not intrinsic
to its propositional content. It depends on its **position within the
organization of interpretation and authority**." Its example: "two
perspectives might both endorse the importance of scientific skepticism.
One applies skepticism symmetrically; the other selectively applies it to
conclusions that threaten its commitments. They share a meme but
potentially possess different perspectival characters. Conversely, two
perspectives might employ entirely different memes while realizing the
same characteristic structure of selective skepticism."

The maxims A6 lists ("Correlation is not causation.", "More research is
needed.", "The market knows best.", "That's just anecdotal.", "Absence of
evidence is not evidence of absence.", "What gets measured gets
managed.", "Consider the source.") are offered as "illustrative
expressions, not inherently pathological ones".

## What it rests on

Reading (b) of the owner's U4 ([CLAIM-tmp4mk0t](CLAIM-tmp4mk0t.md)): if a meme were a whole
character (reading (a), [CLAIM-tmphi3mx](CLAIM-tmphi3mx.md)), perspectives sharing one could
not differ in character. The claim needs memes to be values that
perspectives can share while their characters differ.
