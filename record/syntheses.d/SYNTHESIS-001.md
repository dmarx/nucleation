# SYNTHESIS-001: An organized individual's identity over time is carried by the causal transmission of the relations that keep its repertoire realizable, not by its constituents or by any structure invariant across its realizations, so a structuralism about individuals has to be historical: the structure can change under maps that are not isomorphisms while the individual continues, two structurally identical copies can be two individuals, the transmitting organization may lie outside the individual, and when those relations part at a succession, different successors can continue different respects of it

*Moved from [CLAIM-615](../claims.d/CLAIM-615.md), where its development up to version 3 is recorded. Admitted as an independent hypothesis ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)).*

## The claim

A37: "**An individual is a historically connected organization of
dynamically realized relationships through which a distinguishable
repertoire of activity, capacities, and possible transformations is
sustained.**" "Instead, persistence may depend on the causal transmission
and regeneration of relationships that make successive realizations
continuous with one another." A34 §10 named it: "I would call the emerging
view **historical organizational structuralism**, while treating it as a
working proposal rather than a settled philosophical position."

The architecture, in layers:

1. **What is transmitted.** The organization that keeps a repertoire
   realizable ([SYNTHESIS-003](SYNTHESIS-003.md), Proposed, A's), and not a state space fixed
   in advance. A34 §10: "The space of possibilities is itself constructed
   and reconstructed." The record's form of that is [CLAIM-576](../claims.d/CLAIM-576.md) (Proposed,
   A's), on a change of architecture altering which transformations are
   possible.
2. **The criterion.** Transmission, not resemblance ([CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), Proposed,
   A's): "an organization of the same kind that arises independently is
   not its continuation". The fork fits it (A34 §4). Two repositories that
   are identical at the fork become two projects.
3. **Structure may change.** A34 §3: "The maps \(f_i\) are not generally
   isomorphisms." This is the step beyond [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md), whose case is member
   replacement. It is the owner's U15: identity as "a maintained set of
   commitments" is "a form of Structuralism". That carries [QUESTION-393](../questions.d/QUESTION-393.md)'s
   transformations "of time" (Open, A's, from the owner's paste of
   2026-11-05) from kinds to
   individuals. Both of this layer's directions, two structurally
   identical copies that are two and one individual through a change
   that is not an isomorphism, are [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md)'s for mechanisms (Active),
   which the exchange's first share does not cite; A55 §1 of the second
   share does. The first carries over whole; the second only as
   consistency, since for mechanisms it rests on a stipulation and here
   on [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md)'s criterion ([ARG-075](../arguments.d/ARG-075.md)). It also extends the owner's [CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md) (Proposed), which
   is synchronic and scoped to communicative acts.
4. **Transmission may be external.** A32 §12: "A product can be a coherent
   historical individual even though almost all the processes sustaining
   and transforming it lie outside its boundary." That is [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) (Active)
   on the owner's U14. Whether PyTorch maintains itself depends on the
   boundary: "The boundary of the individual is not incidental to the
   self-maintenance claim. It determines what that claim means." (A34 §10;
   [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md)).
5. **Succession can be plural.** Different successors can continue
   different respects ([CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md), Active), and descent need not be an
   equivalence relation ([CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md), Active). The Senators are the record's
   case ([CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md)). The owner's U16 study is the test ([CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md),
   Proposed), and participants' verdicts can move the producers
   ([CLAIM-617](../claims.d/CLAIM-617.md), Proposed, A's).

The three questions A37 ends on are already held: "Identity asks what makes
this a distinguishable individual. Continuity asks what makes its successive
realizations belong to one historical organization. Autonomy asks how much
of the production and governance of that organization is realized through
its own processes." They are [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md)'s senses and [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md)'s (Active),
neither cited in the exchange.

## Corrections to A's version

- **Correction (fidelity): plural succession, not fission.** A37 §12C,
  "Organizational identity can fission", and its "organizational
  continuity may branch". [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md) holds "plural successor relations, not
  branching identity". Which successor relation, if any, is the
  persistence is [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) (Open); that succession can be plural
  without identity branching is [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md), answering [QUESTION-290](../questions.d/QUESTION-290.md). The title says different successors continue different respects.
- **Correction (fidelity): the transmission is causal.** A37's "historically
  connected" is weaker than its own next clause and than [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md). The
  title keeps "causal transmission".
- **Correction (attribution).** A37 §9 asks "What sort of structuralism
  have we arrived at?". A45 §XI: "Our original structuralist insight". The
  structuralism is the owner's U15. A34 had credited it ("I think you're
  onto something important"). A37 §7, "Software made the abstract theory
  materially explicit", is the owner's U14.
- **Correction (coverage).** A37 keeps A's instances (Freenode/Libera,
  Redis/Valkey, GIMP/SourceForge, RethinkDB, all unverified) and drops the
  owner's U16 design. That design is a project "previously popular" that
  "died when its maintainer stopped attending to it", was revived by a
  new owner, and drew "it's not the same thing that it was" from the
  community, "measurably", with acquisition and decline as a variant.
  The design is [CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md)'s. The exchange never cites [CLAIM-328](../claims.d/CLAIM-328.md),
  [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md), [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md), [QUESTION-393](../questions.d/QUESTION-393.md) or [CLAIM-136](../claims.d/CLAIM-136.md). A36 §8 closes on
  [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md)'s question without citing it.
- **Correction (fidelity): target-relativity.** A37 §8 cites [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) for
  identity judgements being target-dependent. [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) is about
  achievements forming a partial order. Target-relativity is [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) and
  [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md).
- **Correction (standing).** A cites [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) "never as Proposed" (its
  note of 2026-11-07), and the succession instances are A's reports,
  unverified.

## What it does not say

- **That relations constitute the individual.** A37 §9: "these commitments
  do not yet prove strong ontic structural realism", per [CLAIM-150](../claims.d/CLAIM-150.md). Layer 3
  says what individuates it over time, not what it is made of
  ([QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)).
- **That identity judgements fix identity.** They are causes of what is
  transmitted ([CLAIM-617](../claims.d/CLAIM-617.md)), not the criterion ([CASE-105](../cases.d/CASE-105.md)).

## Versions

A32 §12, "**An individual is a historically connected organization whose
changing realizations preserve a sufficiently coherent structure of
capacities, commitments, and possibilities relative to specified
criteria.**", first separates persistence from autonomy, against the
earlier thesis. A34's opening ("a **historical, dynamical structuralism**")
and §10 add constructed possibility and the boundary point. A36 §8 adds
succession: "**The historical identity of an organized individual depends
on the causal continuity of relationships that realize and reproduce its
defining capacities.**" A37 is the version filed. A45 §XI and §XVI and A52
§11 restate it inside the theses on meaning and will ([SYNTHESIS-005](SYNTHESIS-005.md)) and
on disposition (the dispositional reframing, since withdrawn), which rest on it.

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test, the integration must be able to fail while every component holds. It can. A mechanism maintained by one workshop loses a part and passes to an unrelated workshop that maintains it from then on, with nothing of the first workshop's maintaining organization transmitted; let the domain's continuation morphisms, which [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md) says must be stipulated, count it the same mechanism by continuity of its remaining parts. [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) (about collectives), [CLAIM-593](../claims.d/CLAIM-593.md), [CLAIM-280](../claims.d/CLAIM-280.md) and [SYNTHESIS-003](SYNTHESIS-003.md) all hold, and identity runs through continuity of constituents, not transmission. Generalizing [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) beyond collectives is a step that can fail, so the claim is admitted. Its last three title clauses are components, marked as such in the body.
