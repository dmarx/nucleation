# CLAIM-tmp3e6nd: Influence that depends on the target's load bypasses evaluation, and restoring capacity tells it apart

## Two kinds of persuasion

The record's persuasion so far is the ideal receiver's. [THEORY-404](../theory.d/THEORY-404.md) (Kamenica
and Gentzkow) has a sender move a fully Bayesian receiver with an openly
designed experiment. That influence needs no shortage in the receiver. It
works at [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s upper bound, what the source makes possible for an ideal
receiver.

The psychology of persuasion, as the assistant recalls it, is about the other
kind. In the elaboration likelihood and heuristic-systematic models, a target
with the motivation and capacity to process a message is moved by the
quality of its arguments. A target without them is moved by cues: who says
it, how many agree, how confident it sounds, how scarce the offer is. Petty,
Wells and Brock (1976) report that distraction weakened counter-arguing, so
weak arguments became more persuasive, as the assistant recalls it.

On [CLAIM-tmplbobh](CLAIM-tmplbobh.md), that is a statement about legibility. Under load the
argument is illegible to the target and the cue is not. Influence that works
through the cue succeeds because evaluation was unaffordable.

## Load raised on purpose

Many of the familiar pressure and trickery tactics raise the target's load
themselves:
- **time pressure,** "today only";
- **complexity,** terms too long to read;
- **distraction and fatigue,** the long session, the noisy room;
- **emotional arousal,** which occupies the capacity evaluation would need.

That is extraneous load in [CLAIM-tmpgu8no](CLAIM-tmpgu8no.md)'s sense, imposed by the other
party. It does not serve the target's task and does serve the influencer's.

## The test

If the effect shrinks when the target gets time, quiet and a simpler
presentation, it depended on the shortage. That sorts a large class of
cases. It is not a definition of manipulation:
- **[THEORY-225](../theory.d/THEORY-225.md)** says no single mechanism fits all clear cases;
- **[CLAIM-703](CLAIM-703.md)** (Active) has a manipulator who works through sound reasoning,
  by changing the circumstances it responds to. That passes this test.

The test does fit [THEORY-235](../theory.d/THEORY-235.md)'s harm, covert influence that exploits a
person's decision-making vulnerabilities. Working memory is one.
