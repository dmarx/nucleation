# NOTE-tmpnqe9r: Susen (2020) — The Resonance of Resonance

## Contribution

A critical restatement of Rosa's theory, with page references to the German original.

## Key results

1. p. 311: resonance as "a form of world-relation, in which subject and world meet and transform each other".
2. p. 311: it "cannot be brought about at will".
3. p. 318: "access to resources is a necessary but not sufficient condition for human fulfilment".
4. p. 327: the good life is one "rich in experiences of resonance" with stable axes of resonance.

## Assumptions

Rosa's account as Susen reads it.

## Concepts

Resonance; alienation; world-relation; unavailability.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmp6wuql](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6wuql.md)**: qualifies its evaluative measure from outside. On Rosa's criterion a capability set, even assessed as a trajectory, describes options and resources, not the quality of the relation (result 3); the derivation is untouched.
- **The exchange's** A11 §7 account of resonance is accurate to Susen's restatement.

## Limitations

A review essay; the book's spheres and axes of resonance not covered in detail.
