# CASE-tmpx4tix: Hodgkinia in cicadas: one endosymbiont splitting into two to dozens of interdependent lineages, with near-complementary gene sets, idiosyncratic fragmentation in Magicicada, and hosts transmitting up to six times as many cells to keep every lineage

## The case

Cicadas carry two nutritional endosymbionts, *Sulcia* and *Hodgkinia*.

- **Splitting ([LIT-1563](../literature.d/LIT-1563.md)).** In some *Tettigades*, *Hodgkinia* has split
  into two cytologically distinct species. Their gene loss and retention
  are almost perfectly complementary, so a three-member symbiosis became
  four. The authors suggest nonadaptive forces can build organismal
  complexity.
- **Repeated ([LIT-tmp190pe](../literature.d/LIT-tmp190pe.md)).** A single ancestral lineage split at least
  six independent times in *Tettigades* over four million years, giving two
  to six lineages per host. Together the complexes encode nearly the
  ancestral gene set.
- **Fragmentation ([LIT-tmpnzwyn](../literature.d/LIT-tmpnzwyn.md), [LIT-tmpdkj4h](../literature.d/LIT-tmpdkj4h.md)).** In *Magicicada tredecim*
  the genome fragmented into many chromosomes, together almost ten times
  the ancestral size. Every *Magicicada* species holds at least twenty
  circles, with little synteny between species and very different gene
  dosages.
- **The host's cost ([LIT-tmp4u7hy](../literature.d/LIT-tmp4u7hy.md)).** Rare but essential lineages risk
  being lost at transmission. Eggs still receive the full set, because
  hosts with more complex *Hodgkinia* transmit up to six times as many
  cells. The authors propose this as a cost of the fragmentation.

## What it can show

- **More parts, more dependence, no new function.** The complexes do what
  one lineage did. Integration measured by dependence rose with no evident
  gain, which is A2's "partly nonadaptive" dependence ([QUESTION-277](../questions.d/QUESTION-277.md)).
- **Host compensation at transmission.** The host's response raises
  reproductive cohesion (R) to rescue a collective made fragile by
  constituent changes.
- **Constituent change that costs the whole.** This is the converse
  direction of [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md). What the constituents became is not what the
  collective needed.
