# SYNTHESIS-tmprhzl8: Transient support can be withdrawn safely only under conditions the record states jointly in one claim: supply that does the upkeep never becomes optional, supply that does the party's work lowers the capacity it keeps and, where capacity is needed to grow capacity, has a deadline after which withdrawal does not restore it, a savings buffer is a stock of time, and a capability lost to reliable supply is dependency or division of labour by who supplies it; most of the part's questions are replications of the readings it rests on

*A part account of the readiness line: the part filed from the DNA
origami, aid and development, ecology, urban, medicine and windfall
readings of 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06, on A's QUESTION-398. Every entry
in it that links to another component also carries realizability, where
CLAIM-600, CLAIM-603, CASE-225 and QUESTION-398 are first. The line's
account is built over it and its two sibling parts.*

## The account

- **Supplied upkeep.** CLAIM-597 (the assistant's, demonstrated,
  Active): where every replacement part comes from outside, "an
  organization can become independent of what founded it but never of
  what feeds it"; the intact share holds at ρs/(ρs + d) and decays as
  e^(−dt) once the supply stops. Its body adds, for readiness, that such a
  cost is "easy to read as waste while it is working".
- **Supply that does the work.** CLAIM-602 (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active): where capacity is learned by doing and decays
  with disuse, supply used first lowers the capacity kept and lengthens
  recovery, while supply used after capacity leaves learning alone, so
  "what decides dependence is the order in which supply and capacity are
  used, not the amount of supply". CLAIM-605 (the assistant's,
  demonstrated, Active), resting on CLAIM-602 and CLAIM-604, adds the
  threshold: full substitution has a deadline (3.10 time units in the
  example), and "a slow taper of such support is worse than an abrupt
  cut". CASE-225 (A's, Proposed) separates dependence for timing,
  dependence for performance and suppression of a latent capacity, and
  says suppression becomes atrophy only through a slow variable, which is
  CLAIM-602's and CLAIM-605's disuse decay.
- **A buffer as time.** CLAIM-601 (the assistant's, after the owner's
  reading that deprivation adds time pressure; demonstrated, Active): "A
  savings buffer is a stock of time"; below a buffer that rises with a
  payoff's delay, declining a profitable investment is the policy that
  meets the deadline, and covering needs through the delay buys the time.
- **Who supplies.** CLAIM-603 (the assistant's, on the owner's remark
  linking assured help to specialization; interpretive, Proposed): a
  capability lost to reliable supply is the Black Queen's loss, and
  whether it is dependency or division of labour is decided by the
  supply, so "the aim framed as self-sufficiency is better framed as
  reciprocity". Its note of 2026-11-06 records two holds: the title states
  the favoured loss generally against counterpoint readings that find no
  such response in poor households, and Moore's thesis (THEORY-1459) is
  not held.
- **When withdrawal is safe.** CLAIM-600 (the assistant's, interpretive,
  Active), resting on CLAIM-597, CLAIM-604, CLAIM-602 and CLAIM-566,
  states the conditions jointly: a self-sustaining process on a lasting
  flux, support large enough to pass its threshold, the threat gone, and
  the party doing each subtask itself at a bearable effort; standing
  inputs stay supplied, and "persistence after withdrawal is needed to
  tell which, though slow reversion can mimic it". Its body: "No premise
  states the joint sufficiency." Its "Admission" section gives a
  separating case (a village water scheme) and closes a free parameter by
  judging the conditions before withdrawal.

What stays open is QUESTION-398 (A's, Open): under what conditions a
partial organization becomes independent of the intervention that
established it, with A's measure D(s), and A's separation "The nucleus
can become unnecessary while the organization it helped establish
persists". QUESTION-432 refines it for aid; QUESTION-401, QUESTION-413 and
QUESTION-427 bear on it.

## Links the components state, and where

- **From upkeep to work.** CLAIM-602's body: "There the supply repairs
  the structure and leaves its own capacity alone. Here the supply is not
  neutral", and "Under full substitution CLAIM-597's dependence is the
  best case." CLAIM-597's note of 2026-11-05 points to CLAIM-602 for the
  case in which supply lowers the organization's own capacity.
- **From no threshold to a threshold.** CLAIM-602 names the threshold
  case under "That a threshold is absent", and its note of 2026-11-05
  says CLAIM-605 models it.
- **The conditions.** CLAIM-600 takes CLAIM-597 as its first condition,
  CLAIM-602 and CLAIM-558 as routes for its third, CLAIM-603 for slack
  effort, CLAIM-605 for the deadline, and qualifies CLAIM-602's "not the
  amount of supply" by Balboni et al.'s threshold. CLAIM-601's body:
  "The condition on size is also a condition on timing." CLAIM-603
  corroborates CLAIM-600, and CLAIM-605 says it reconciles Azam et al.'s
  up-front cut with the Kenya exit study's gradual exits.
- **To the question.** CLAIM-597, CLAIM-600, CLAIM-602, CLAIM-603 and
  CLAIM-605 bear on QUESTION-398; QUESTION-398's notes record CLAIM-602's
  and CLAIM-604's models and CLAIM-600's three conditions as answers in
  part.

## Not stated on the record

- **Practice outside operation as capacity-first support.** Reading
  CLAIM-558's remedy as CLAIM-602's capacity-first rule applied to a
  rare capability relabels one with the other and predicts nothing that
  either leaves open. Not filed.
- **A funding loop with a deadline.** That QUESTION-386's loop, run on
  CLAIM-605's dynamics, makes a quiet spell beyond a deadline
  unrecoverable by restoring funding. It needs a premise no component
  holds, that learning from drills needs capacity already present;
  CLAIM-605's learning is by doing the served work.
- **CLAIM-600's admission case as evidence against it.** Its village
  water scheme is offered as a stipulated separating case. It could
  plainly occur, and as written it meets the first clause of CLAIM-600's
  own `defeated_if` (the conditions met, judged before withdrawal, and
  decline within a turnover). Whether it shows the claim false or only
  separate from its premises is not stated on the record.

  "It is not shown for support in general."
- That the models' numbers fit any organization, household or state; each
  claim disclaims that.
- That aid recipients are like microbes in mechanism (CLAIM-603).
- Anything with standing of its own.
