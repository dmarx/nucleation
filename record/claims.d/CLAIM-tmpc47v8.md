# CLAIM-tmpc47v8: In the public records of contested open-source successions, a verdict by an independent party on which line continues the project appears only as the stated reason for that party's own move, made in the same act, so the records cannot time verdicts apart from migration

## The claim

[ARG-067](../arguments.d/ARG-067.md) held that [CLAIM-617](CLAIM-617.md) could be told from [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s criterion alone
only by "Verdicts expressed by people other than the movers, or before the
move, that predict who moves". [CASE-tmpntt8e](../cases.d/CASE-tmpntt8e.md) looked for them in the public
records of eight contested successions and found three kinds of verdict,
none of that sort:

- **arranged** with the founders: endorsements printed in the successor's
  own announcement (LibreOffice, Valkey), and a foundation's taking it in
  (OpenTofu, Valkey, Forgejo);
- **by the movers**, before their move: the Hudson community's vote, 214
  to 14, after which the project's lists and accounts moved;
- **independent, and the speaker's own move**: SourceHut's "We entirely
  reject the illegitimate new leaders" in the post moving its channels to
  Libera Chat "effective today"; Fedora's replacement of OpenOffice.org and
  MySQL, whose stated reasons are the decision to replace.

The one independent statement made before its speaker's move, Fedora's
proposal to replace Redis with Valkey, gave the licence as its only reason.

So in these records the verdict a non-mover holds is visible only when it
moves, and the timing [CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md)'s design needs cannot be read from them.

## What it does not say

- That no such verdicts exist. Surveys, chat logs and closed mailing lists
  were not read; Fedora's devel archive was behind a bot check.
- That the verdicts did no causal work. That is [CLAIM-617](CLAIM-617.md)'s question,
  which this leaves open.
- That the alternative reading ([CLAIM-tmppui4z](CLAIM-tmppui4z.md)) is wrong. The two read the
  same records.
