# SYNTHESIS-101: Restricting capabilities, and who controls the terms of waiting and contracting: the grounds of a restriction are kept apart from the authority to impose it, a capability is decomposed into what a restriction can reach, a restriction justified by incompetence can produce the incompetence, an opposition can be free as an actor while another holds its efficacy, and control over a deferred reward or over what a contract left open is control over the other party's terms

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line. Version 1 was filed at the interim synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day and before the readings. Version 2 is the follow-up review of 2026-11-18: it integrates the argument the readings filed and cites the readings as the support each component has, by the THEORY that carries the relation; a reading is not a component. Every component comes from the continuation's filing (pull request 512); none was folded at the reconciliation. The question is the owner's and Open; the claims and the term are A's and Proposed; the case is a reported study, now read, with A's analysis. Where a component cites another, the account says which entry does.*

## The account

**The question.** [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md) (the owner's, U20; Open; "and who may
restrict it" is A45's refinement, added by the filer): under what
conditions should a capability be restricted, and who may restrict it?
Its body lists what the record held before: [CLAIM-684](../claims.d/CLAIM-684.md), [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md),
[CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) with [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), [TERM-120](../terms.d/TERM-120.md) with [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md), and [CLAIM-688](../claims.d/CLAIM-688.md), as
conditions for particular cases and no general answer. It sorts the
exchange's parts of the question: the grounds and the authority
([CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), "which answers this question in part"), what is restricted
([TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)), what a restriction does over time ([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)), the
capability to change the conditions of one's own challenge
([QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), with [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) as two such capabilities),
and who sets the terms (A53, at the owner's U24). It holds A49 §13's
five candidate conditions as "a list to test, not yet an answer". No
reading bears on it.

**Grounds and authority.** [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md) (A's, A45 §6, A41 §5 and A49 §12;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also organizational-normativity): that a
capability's exercise should be limited and that a given party may limit
it are separate conclusions, neither entailing the other; the power to
restrict is itself a capability, whose legitimacy turns on how it is
bounded and answerable. It answers [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md), bears on [QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md),
uses [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md), and complements [CLAIM-680](../claims.d/CLAIM-680.md) (its summary: "the special case
in which the ground is a judgment of worth"), [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), [CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md) and
[CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md). Its body adds A49 §12's third, relational justification,
under which "A protective restriction may become dominating if its
continuation depends entirely on unchecked discretion". No reading
carries a relation to it.

**What a restriction reaches.** [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md) (A's, A41 §1, A45 §4 and A49 §3;
Proposed): capability decomposed for restriction into competence,
exercise, authority and governance, which vary independently, so a
restriction on one is not a restriction on another. Its body says a
restriction on exercise can erode competence where competence is kept by
use ([CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)), and that it is not [CLAIM-678](../claims.d/CLAIM-678.md)'s dimensions of power,
[CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s resource requirements, Sen's capability or [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s
correctability. No reading bears on it.

**A restriction that produces its own warrant.** [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md) (A's, A41 §8,
A45 §9 and A49 §8.1; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
transient-support): where competence is kept by use and lost with
disuse, and a restriction is lifted when competence is shown, a
restriction justified by a lack of competence lowers the competence it
waits for, so that without a route to practice it produces the evidence
for its own continuation. It bears on [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md), uses [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md), and
complements [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) and [CLAIM-675](../claims.d/CLAIM-675.md); its body says it needs
only [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md)'s and [CLAIM-605](../claims.d/CLAIM-605.md)'s premise of decay with disuse, that A45
§9's instance is [CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md) reached by restriction rather than
substitution, and that [CLAIM-714](../claims.d/CLAIM-714.md) asks whether the party that keeps the
trap closed is responsible. Its body states the obligation A41 §8 draws:
"*Who is responsible for making condition X feasible?*" No reading
carries a relation to it.

**Controlled incapacity.** [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md) (A's, A37 §10 to §11, at the owner's
U18; thesis, interpretive, Proposed): an opposition can be independent
as an actor while its capacity to change the arrangement it opposes
depends on another's control of its resources, standing, corrective
authority or time; that controlled incapacity, not incapacity or failure
as such, makes contained opposition a mark of domination. It bears on
[QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), and complements [CLAIM-686](../claims.d/CLAIM-686.md) (its summary:
"this adds that such failure can be held by another party") and
[CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md) (its body: "the converse arrangement"). Its body corrects A's
gloss of [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md).

*Readings.* Two THEORYs corroborate it, independently. [THEORY-1968](../theory.d/THEORY-1968.md)
(Arnstein 1969) gives three instances: citizen majorities on Model
Cities boards under a city council's "final veto powers", community
control without sufficient resources, and placation that depends on the
technical assistance granted; her conditions for partnership, own funds
and ground rules "not subject to unilateral change", are the converse.
Her participants are citizens' groups, not an opposition party.
[THEORY-1953](../theory.d/THEORY-1953.md) (Szakonyi 2025), which A had offered as a counterexample,
supports the claim's warning that inclusion is not itself containment
(opposition councils changed local governance measurably) and its
mechanism one level up (the municipal filter, criminal charges, blocked
candidacies). The author does not frame it so.

**Control over a deferred reward.** [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) (A's, A51 §4 and A53 §2,
from the owner's U23 and U24; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also
will-organization): where one party gives up something now for a benefit
another controls later, whether waiting is good judgment depends on the
controller's reliability, so a controller not answerable for delivery
can make the other's patience a means of its control. It grounds on
[CASE-281](../cases.d/CASE-281.md) and on [THEORY-1963](../theory.d/THEORY-1963.md), uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md), and complements [CLAIM-601](../claims.d/CLAIM-601.md)
(its body: "the same misreading from the other side") and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md). Its
body checks A53 §2's inequality, wait only if 2pδ > 1, and calls
[TERM-110](../terms.d/TERM-110.md)'s ratchet "a mechanism by which a controller changes delivery
after restraint is shown".

*Readings.* [THEORY-1963](../theory.d/THEORY-1963.md) (Kidd, Palmeri and Aslin 2013), now among its
grounds, carries the first half: children who had just seen the
experimenter break promises waited a mean 181.57 s against 722.43 s, and
"Waiting is only the rational choice if you believe that a second
marshmallow is likely to actually appear". It says nothing about
control, exploitation or answerability, so the second half stays A's.

[CASE-281](../cases.d/CASE-281.md) (the owner's association at U23, A's analysis in A51 and A53
§2; documented reported study, Proposed; first line will-organization):
children who had seen an experimenter break a promise waited about a
quarter as long. It illustrates [CLAIM-344](../claims.d/CLAIM-344.md); its body says [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md)
grounds on it, that waiting behaviour alike over different organizations
is [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s form, and that the case applies the owner's method
([CLAIM-718](../claims.d/CLAIM-718.md)). Its dated note records the four studies read ([THEORY-1963](../theory.d/THEORY-1963.md);
[THEORY-1983](../theory.d/THEORY-1983.md), Mischel, Ebbesen and Zeiss 1972; [THEORY-1980](../theory.d/THEORY-1980.md), Watts, Duncan
and Quan 2018; [THEORY-1989](../theory.d/THEORY-1989.md), Sperber et al. 2024): A's figures check (the
ratio is 0.25), the attentional arm was redirected by the experimenters
and not by the children, and the follow-ups weaken the prediction of
later life.

**Residual control.** [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md) (the connection to contract theory the
owner's, U24; the residual-control reading A's, A53 §2; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed): in an incomplete contract the holder of residual
rights of control decides what the contract left open, so a relationship
fair in every specified term can leave one party's terms at the other's
discretion, exercised and seen only when an uncovered contingency arises.
It uses [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) and complements [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) (its summary: "this names a
form that control takes in a contract"), [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) and [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), which
its body calls "control acquired by violation" against this, "the
authorized case". Its body also reads [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md)'s parting under
intervention as two contracts that part "at the first uncovered
contingency".

*Readings.* [THEORY-1985](../theory.d/THEORY-1985.md) (Grossman and Hart 1986, the claim's named
source) and [THEORY-1979](../theory.d/THEORY-1979.md) (Hart's Nobel lecture, standing in for Hart and
Moore) corroborate its first clause, the definition. [ARG-116](../arguments.d/ARG-116.md) (the
assistant's, at the readings of 2026-11-18; source, Proposed) grounds on
both and concludes that they carry no more: in their model the holder
does not decide the contingency alone, since the parties renegotiate and
ownership sets the split, and the allocation acts before any contingency,
through investment, "in spite of the fact that the noncooperative outcome
never occurs!". Hart adds that power over a worker runs through the
assets she needs and her replaceability. The claim's later clauses,
unilateral decision and control seen only at the contingency, are A's.

## Arm B

Run again with the readings. No candidate passes.

- **[QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md)'s answers as one answer** ([CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md),
  [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) and [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), across four lines). The candidate: a
  restriction is justified only if its ground holds, its imposer has
  authority, and, where lifting waits on shown competence, the restrictor
  supplies the route back. Most of it is stated already: the route back
  is in [CLAIM-721](../claims.d/CLAIM-721.md)'s body (A41 §8, A49 §8.1), and the question's body holds
  A49 §13's five conditions as a list to test. The residue, that the trap
  fails relational justification even when an independent examiner
  decides the lifting, has a separating case (that examiner) but turns on
  [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md)'s "acceptable arrangement", which absorbs every case. No
  reading bears on any of the four. Fails for a free parameter.
- **One form across waiting, contracting and opposing** ([CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md),
  [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md), [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md)): the act is the party's own and another controls
  the conditions that decide its effect. Already stated: each claim uses
  [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) for exactly that control, and [CLAIM-744](../claims.d/CLAIM-744.md) and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md)
  complement each other. The readings now pull the first two apart in
  timing (above), and the pairing with [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md) adds nothing beyond
  [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md). Nothing filed.
- **Controlled incapacity is a restriction on governance**
  ([CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md), [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md), with [THEORY-1953](../theory.d/THEORY-1953.md)). The sentence tried: an
  opposition contained in [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md)'s sense is restricted in [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)'s
  governance dimension and not in its exercise. Separating case, from
  [CLAIM-736](../claims.d/CLAIM-736.md)'s own list: an opposition held by another's control of its
  resources, as in Arnstein's community control without funds
  ([THEORY-1968](../theory.d/THEORY-1968.md)), is restricted in the means of exercise, not in
  governance. As an identity it fails on a case the claim allows; as "it
  can be" it adds nothing. Fails.
