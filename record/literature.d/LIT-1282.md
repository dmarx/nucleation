# LIT-1282: Random Hyperbolic Graphs: Degree Sequence and Clustering

Luca Gugelmann, Konstantinos Panagiotou and Ueli Peter (2012), ICALP 2012,
Lecture Notes in Computer Science — [ARXIV-1205.1470](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.1470)

## Key takeaways

- **Model.** n points in a hyperbolic disk of radius R = 2 log n + C,
  angles uniform, radii with density α sinh(αr)/(cosh(αR) − 1), α > 1/2;
  two points joined if their hyperbolic distance is at most R. This is
  Krioukov et al.'s model at zero temperature with curvature fixed at −1,
  so α here is the ratio α/ζ there.
- **Clustering (Theorem 2.1).** The average of the local clustering
  coefficients is Θ(1) in expectation and with high probability
  (1 + o(1)) times its expectation. A lower bound, not a limit.
- **Degrees (Theorems 2.2–2.4).** The fraction of vertices of degree k is
  (1 + o(1)) times an explicit Gamma-function expression for k up to
  n^δ′, and the fraction of degree at least k is (1 + o(1)) times a
  constant times k^(−2α) up to n^(1/(2α)) up to logarithms: a power law
  with exponent 2α + 1 up to the maximum degree. The average degree
  tends to an explicit constant and the maximum degree is n^(1/(2α)+o(1)).
- **Scope.** Only the threshold model. The soft model with a
  distance-dependent connection probability is named and not treated.
