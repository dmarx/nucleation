## What it would do

Read Grice and Strawson (Bach and Harnish, [LIT-1040](../literature.d/LIT-1040.md), is Unavailable), then
run the elicited test of [CASE-069](../cases.d/CASE-069.md): the speaker's intention fixed, the
audience's standing varied.

Where the record names it:

[CLAIM-151](../claims.d/CLAIM-151.md); judgements fixed by intention give the residue to the rival.
