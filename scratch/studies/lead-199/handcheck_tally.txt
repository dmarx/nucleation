Hand-check of the classifier (LEAD-199)

Round 1: 20 changes drawn at random (seed 7) from the classifier's first version,
non-mechanical, non-creation changes. 16 non-substantive: 16 correct.
4 substantive: 0 correct by intent (3 were the 289-file sweep that back-filled
the then-new required `warrant` field; 1 was a sweep adding "(TERM-008)" cites
to bodies). Fixes: absent->present role/warrant is a schema backfill, not a
revision; codes are masked and parentheses holding only citations dropped
before comparing text. Effect: substantive changes 1192 -> 835.

Round 2 (final classifier), stratified: 10 substantive (seed 2026), 10 non-substantive (seed 2027).
 handcheck_substantive.txt / handcheck_nonsubstantive.txt hold the diffs.
 Non-substantive: 10/10 correct (inactive-ok comments, dated notes, warrant
   backfill, relation adds/removes, a status change with history entry).
 Substantive: 7/10 clearly correct (title narrowed, defeater restated,
   stale text corrected, WIP review changes).
   2/10 are the ADR-070 vocabulary sweep ("endorsement" -> "conferral"):
   correct by the letter (title/body wording changed) but the history says
   "the content is unchanged" -- a convention rewrite, i.e. contraction.
   1/10 borderline: body gained a sentence naming the proposed study case
   (a citation in prose; not a change to what the claim says).
 Estimated precision of "substantive" ~0.7 by intent (0.9 by the letter);
 non-substantive error not observed in 10 (upper 95% ~0.26 by rule of three).
