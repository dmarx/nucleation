### Added

- ADR-tmptwlsg (Proposed): an ARG may conclude a TERM, in a new form,
  `explication`, whose critical questions are Carnap's adequacy
  conditions and the rivals a definition is judged against; the TERM's
  `argued_by` is derived. Its first application, ARG-tmp23khr, argues
  that the definition of learning (TERM-tmp1ix99) does its job better
  than a definition by improved performance, with two questions answered
  only in part.
