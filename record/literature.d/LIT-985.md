# LIT-985: Can Kernel Methods Explain How the Data Affects Neural Collapse?

Vignesh Kothapalli, Tom Tirer (2024), *Transactions on Machine Learning Research (2025); arXiv:2406.02105*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-774](../notes.d/NOTE-774.md).*

- **NC1 from a kernel (Theorem 4.1).** tr Σ_W and tr Σ_B of
  kernel-defined features are sums over the class blocks of the kernel
  matrix. So NC1 = tr Σ_W / tr Σ_B can be computed for the NNGP, the NTK or
  any other kernel, with the data in.
- **The lazy regime does not collapse (Theorem 5.1).** The setting is
  one-dimensional data, two classes N(μ_c, σ_c²) on opposite sides of 0,
  ReLU, and σ_b → 0. There the NNGP kernel is (σ_w²/2)xx′ within a class
  and 0 across, and the NTK is (σ_w⁴/2 + σ_w²/2)xx′ within a class and 0
  across. So their expected NC1 is equal.
- **Beyond the theorem (Figures 2–3).** On Gaussian mixtures up to
  d₀ = 128 the NTK tracks the NNGP with Erf, and is less collapsed with
  ReLU. Erf gives about ten times lower NC1 than ReLU at d₀ = 1.
- **A data-aware kernel (§6, Appendix G).** Seroussi et al.'s equations of
  state lower NC1 below the NNGP at width 500, toward finite networks, for
  separable data. With overlapping classes in d₀ ≥ 64, finite networks'
  NC1 is about twice the kernel's, and under imbalance the two move in
  opposite directions.
- **Raw NC1 can mislead (Appendix F).** Relative to the inputs' own NC1,
  the ReLU kernel features are about half as collapsed as the data when
  the classes are symmetric. With well-separated inputs the data are more
  collapsed than the features.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-051](../questions.d/QUESTION-051.md), and read in [NOTE-774](../notes.d/NOTE-774.md). Its
lazy-regime result is filed as [THEORY-294](../theory.d/THEORY-294.md), at the strength of its

computed. It sits beside [THEORY-212](../theory.d/THEORY-212.md)'s account of static-kernel learning.

from [LIT-974](LIT-974.md) and [LIT-994](LIT-994.md) ([THEORY-278](../theory.d/THEORY-278.md), [THEORY-286](../theory.d/THEORY-286.md)), which cannot
