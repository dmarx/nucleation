# LIT-tmpfjc6u: Alabama's LePage decision, the future of ex vivo embryos, and more

Henry T. Greely (2026). "Alabama's LePage decision, the future of ex vivo
embryos, and more" *Journal of Law and the Biosciences 13(1): lsag013*.
DOI-10.1093/jlb/lsag013.

## Key takeaways

LePage's holding was narrow; the legislative response immunized IVF
providers too broadly; broad political support keeps IVF largely safe in the
United States.
