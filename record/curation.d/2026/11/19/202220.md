## After the readings: in-context learning is learning

The owner answered the note on [TERM-131](../../record/terms.d/TERM-131.md) (2026-11-19): "learning from
context in activation space is still a form of learning, and so the
assertion that pretraining makes certain forms of later learning (like
ICL) easier would hold". The term never required weights; what it lacked
was the system and horizon over which organization is retained. [TERM-131](../../record/terms.d/TERM-131.md)
version 2 makes it relative to a declared system and horizon: in-context
learning is episodic learning of the model running in an episode, and the
same clause settles [ARG-119](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-119.md)'s question about selection. [THEORY-2062](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-2062.md)
now corroborates [CLAIM-756](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-756.md), and the notes that said the in-context
"acquisition" retains nothing were rewritten. The owner also suggested the
data-scheduling literature, where ordering experiments have the shape
[CLAIM-756](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-756.md) needs; filed as [LEAD-002](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-002.md), stage idea.
