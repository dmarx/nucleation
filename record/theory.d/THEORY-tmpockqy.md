# THEORY-tmpockqy: Sen argues that development should be evaluated as the expansion of the substantive freedoms people enjoy, not as growth of income, industrialization or technological advance, and that freedoms are both its primary ends and among its principal means because freedoms of different kinds strengthen one another

## The account

Sen argues that development is "a process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy" [OCR-normalized], not GNP growth, industrialization or technological advance. Freedoms are its primary ends (the constitutive role) and among its principal means (the instrumental role), since freedoms of different kinds strengthen one another.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmplmlej](../notes.d/NOTE-tmplmlej.md), results 1–2 and 4–6. Corroborates [CLAIM-tmp6wuql](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6wuql.md): result 6, unemployment's "debilitating effects on individual freedom, initiative, and skills" [OCR-normalized], which income support does not make up, is a support that replaces an outcome but not the activity that keeps a capacity. The claim does not rest on it.

## What it does not say

Not the fasting/starving example or the agency/well-being distinction, which are not in the chapters read. Not that the capability set includes later sets: the dynamics Sen describes are instrumental, at the level of society.
