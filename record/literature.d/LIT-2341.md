# LIT-2341: InstantID: Zero-shot Identity-Preserving Generation in Seconds

Qixun Wang, Xu Bai, Haofan Wang, Zekui Qin, Anthony Chen, Huaxia Li, Xu Tang and Yao Hu (2024). "InstantID: Zero-shot Identity-Preserving Generation in Seconds." arXiv 2401.07519; technical report (arXiv comment: "Technical Report").

## Key takeaways

Read in [NOTE-1723](../notes.d/NOTE-1723.md); the account is [THEORY-1643](../theory.d/THEORY-1643.md). Single-image, tuning-free identity preservation for a frozen SDXL: a face-recognition embedding injected by decoupled cross-attention, plus IdentityNet, a ControlNet conditioned on facial keypoints and the embedding.
