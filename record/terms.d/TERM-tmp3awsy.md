# TERM-tmp3awsy: productive nucleus: a partial organization whose establishment, relative to a declared target, context, horizon and comparator, causally improves the probability, timeliness, robustness or resource-feasible accessibility of forming the target by changing later interaction opportunities, constraints or transition rates; distinct from a structural seed and a kinetic nucleus

## Definition

A's candidate term: "An identifiable partial organization whose
establishment, relative to a declared target, environment, horizon, and
comparator, causally improves the probability, timeliness, robustness, or
resource-feasible accessibility of forming the target by altering
subsequent interaction opportunities, constraints, or transition dynamics."
In A's words, "The definition does not require a particular nucleus size,
uniqueness, persistence after its contribution, autocatalysis, or
organizational closure."

Four things must be declared, by A's four questions:

- **relative to what target:** a nucleus "may promote one organizational
  form while obstructing another";
- **against what comparator:** the nucleus absent, disrupted or replaced,
  with other conditions controlled;
- **over what horizon:** speeding the first hour can lower final yield;
- **by what mechanism:** favourable contacts, suppressed competitors, a
  lowered barrier, coordination, or a template.

## Three notions kept apart

| Notion | Criterion (A) | Establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Structural seed | a recognizable substructure appears early | that an intermediate exists |
| Kinetic nucleus | crossing a state or region changes later progression probabilities | a transition in the dynamics |
| Productive nucleus | establishing it causally improves formation of a declared target against a comparator | a target-relative contribution |

## Measures

- **Committor.** q(s) = Pr_s(τ_F < τ_K), with F the target and K a declared
  failure set. On a continuous-time Markov chain it solves
  Σ_j Q_ij q(j) = 0 off F ∪ K, with q = 1 on F and 0 on K. It ranks
  intermediates by their futures, but "a high committor is not sufficient
  to prove causal productivity".
- **Interventional effect.** Δ_N(F, T, c) is the difference in the chance
  of reaching F by T between establishing the candidate and the
  comparator. A observed association Pr(F | N) > Pr(F | ¬N) does not
  identify it.
- **Kinds of productivity,** declared separately: probability, expected
  time, robustness to disturbance, access to capabilities.
- **Non-additivity.** V(S) = Pr(τ_F ≤ T | do(S)) for a set S of established
  relations. A's example has an interaction contrast of 0.58
  (0.80 − 0.21 − 0.21 + 0.20). A minimal productive nucleating set is one
  reaching a threshold η with no proper subset that does, and there may be
  several incomparable ones. The assistant's qualification is
  [CLAIM-tmpuv9yj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpuv9yj.md): where V is not monotone, the minimal sets do not
  determine the productive ones.

## Related distinctions A draws

- **Fragile, productively persistent, kinetically trapping.** "neither
  maximum stability nor minimum flexibility is generally the appropriate
  objective". Dunn et al. ([NOTE-tmpvgzvb](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpvgzvb.md)): "reversible bond-formation is
  important in recovering from transient misfoldings".
- **Not autocatalysis, not closure, not regeneration.** Each can occur
  without the others ([TERM-070](TERM-070.md), [CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md)).
- **Persistence, productivity, integration and closure** "can vary
  independently" ([CLAIM-tmpiyvk1](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpiyvk1.md) for the first two).

## The molecular case

Schneider, Möritz and Dietz ([NOTE-tmpqi66a](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpqi66a.md)) removed the earliest-binding
segments. The compacted intermediate did not form, and folding proceeded "via
another, slower pathway". That is the term's comparator in
experiment, with the time to fold as the measure of productivity.

## Note of 2026-11-05: A's predictions on productive nucleation

From the fourth synthesis on DNA origami by another assistant (A), "Predicting which organizations will successfully nucleate", pasted by the owner on 2026-11-05.

A's fourth synthesis adds two refinements. Productivity is a vector over targets, not a scalar: a nucleus may raise one target's chance and lower another's, so "a theory of organizational agency cannot conclude that the nucleus is globally desirable without specifying how the competing objectives are evaluated". A also separates two ways a nucleus can help, a "State advantage", moving the system to a better starting state under the same transition rules, and a "Generative architectural advantage", changing the rules themselves, as when early binding brings distant sites together. A: "Merely starting closer to the finished structure is not the same as constructing a partial organization that makes later assembly more favorable."

No status changes.
