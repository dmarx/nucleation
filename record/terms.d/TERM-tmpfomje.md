# TERM-tmpfomje: coherence, of a family of descriptions, transports or judgements: their agreement with one another under a stated correspondence structure (on overlaps, around cycles, between routes), distinct from fidelity to a source, from integration by dynamics, and from knowledge of the world

## Definition

A family is coherent, under a correspondence structure, to the degree that
its members agree with one another where the structure compares them. The
comparisons can be:

- on overlaps, as local sections that glue ([THEORY-012](../theory.d/THEORY-012.md), [CLAIM-131](../claims.d/CLAIM-131.md));
- around cycles, as trivial holonomy ([THEORY-1082](../theory.d/THEORY-1082.md), [THEORY-tmpf3jtf](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpf3jtf.md));
- between routes, as zero path discrepancy ([CLAIM-tmpgwud2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgwud2.md)).

Coherence is internal to the family. It can be exact or graded: by a
smallest Laplacian eigenvalue, by a holonomy's distance from the identity,
or by direction ([CLAIM-tmp3lml8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3lml8.md)). It is always relative to the
correspondence structure, so a claim of coherence should name the maps
([TERM-093](TERM-093.md)).

## What it is not

- **Fidelity.** Fidelity is a relation between a rendering and a source,
  relative to a receiver and a task. The record's senses of it are the
  Active fidelity TERMs, [TERM-034](TERM-034.md) among them. A constant rendering is
  perfectly coherent and has no fidelity at all, and two faithful routes
  can disagree ([CLAIM-tmpgwud2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpgwud2.md)).
- **Integration.** Compatible descriptions can belong to parts that do not
  interact. [CASE-179](../cases.d/CASE-179.md)'s "coherence without integration" arm, and [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md)'s
  separation of compatibility from dynamics, keep the two apart.
- **Knowledge or correctness.** A family can become coherent by changing
  how its members are expressed ([THEORY-1088](../theory.d/THEORY-1088.md)), or by a record admitting
  only the disagreements that fit ([CLAIM-448](../claims.d/CLAIM-448.md)). Coherence counts as evidence
  of correctness only where the truth is known to be coherent, as in a
  synchronization benchmark built flat ([THEORY-tmpy1s8c](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpy1s8c.md)).

## What would settle it

Adoption by the owner, after which claims that use "coherent" or
"consistent" in this sense can name it in `uses`. A rival would be a
narrower term that restricts coherence to gluing. That would leave
agreement between routes and around loops without a word.
