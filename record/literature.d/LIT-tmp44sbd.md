## Why this is here

The full statement of [LIT-tmp626gh](LIT-tmp626gh.md)'s theory, for [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md)'s rivals. Registered so that a reading of perceptual control theory has its source. The book is the expected place to read PCT's "imagination connection" at length (its jacket names "the nature and significance of memory"; the connection is in Powers's 1960 paper and 1988 outline): a reference signal routed back as the system's own perception without passing through the environment, the one kind of PCT loop that does not bottom out in action. The Science article ([NOTE-tmpxdsyb](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpxdsyb.md)) does not mention it, so whether PCT shares [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md)'s internal loop, or treats it only as imagination in which every goal is met at once, waits on this book. It is also where reorganization, which the article gives one paragraph, is set out, which bears on [CLAIM-tmpt568z](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpt568z.md) and [QUESTION-tmpr5tck](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpr5tck.md).
