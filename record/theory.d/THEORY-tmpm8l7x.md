## The account

Jackson et al. (2021) report that the OBO Foundry turned its originally imprecise, subjectively
interpreted principles into operational rules with automated checks shown on a public dashboard,
kept both principles and checks under continuing community revision, and saw reported errors fall
after the dashboard was announced, while some principles (collaboration, scope, plurality of
users) still need human review.
