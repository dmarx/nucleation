# TERM-tmpo4pxh: shared perspective, of a population, as against correlated responses and socially learned norms, restorative reading: members whose interpretive structures are alike, whose interpretive states depend on one another in both directions, and who restore a member's deviation to the shared structure rather than average it into the population

## Definition

Conditions 1 and 2 are [TERM-tmpsu4df](TERM-tmpsu4df.md)'s, word for word in their
measurement: structural concordance beyond members trained on different
schemas, and cross-member dependence both ways under a perturbation of a
subset S of members.

3. **Restoration.** A deviation carried by S is corrected back toward
   the shared structure, and little of it passes to the unperturbed
   members U: the population keeps its organization rather than settling
   at its members' average. Unconditional averaging of n members, a
   share f of them deviant, moves U by f and S by 1 − f of the deviation,
   a dilution ratio of f/(1 − f).

## How it is measured

In the measures of [TERM-tmpsu4df](TERM-tmpsu4df.md), for the unwarranted perturbation:
recovery g of S must exceed the same population's g with interaction
removed by ε = 0.05, and the dilution ratio ρ = s_u / g must stay below
half the averaging benchmark, f/(2(1 − f)), which is 0.125 for f = 0.2.
"Below" means the upper end of a 95% interval over seeds is below it.

## How it differs from its rival

[TERM-tmpsu4df](TERM-tmpsu4df.md) asks that uptake follow the population's standard of
evidence: a warranted deviation spreads, an unwarranted one does not.
This term asks only that the shared structure be what is defended. A
population that enforces its norms against all deviation, warranted or
not, passes this term and fails its rival; that is the case on which
they differ. A11's "possible revision" is the argument for the rival: a
regime that can be revised only by numbers revises by majority, not by
evaluation. Its "organization within which disagreement has
consequences" (A8) is the argument for this one: enforcement gives
disagreement consequences. The choice is the owner's.

## What would settle it

As for [TERM-tmpsu4df](TERM-tmpsu4df.md): if unconditional conformity passes condition 3
here, or common training or socially learned norms pass condition 2, the
term fails A4's test, and that counts against the category.
