# QUESTION-398: Under what conditions does a partial organization become independent of the intervention that originally established it?

## Why it is a question

A productive nucleus ([TERM-117](../terms.d/TERM-117.md)) may need support to form and keep
growing. A's question is when the growing structure's own relations take
over. A's measure:

  D(s) = q_supported(s) − q_withdrawn(s),

the committor with support kept minus the committor with support removed,
from the same intermediate state s. A expects D(s) to be large at first and to approach zero once enough
downstream organization has formed. A's analogy:
"temporary scaffolding used to construct a building", whose removal tests
construction without making the building "self-repairing".

The record separates assembling, persisting and regenerating ([CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md))
and asks when persistence makes an anchor ([CLAIM-185](../claims.d/CLAIM-185.md), [QUESTION-038](QUESTION-038.md)). It
does not ask when what was built ceases to need what built it.

## What the record has on it

- **DNA origami's supports.** Rothemund's and Douglas et al.'s staple
  excess and annealing programs, and Ni et al.'s thermal cycling and UV
  crosslink. Each is withdrawn after formation, and the objects persist:
  support-independence after completion, not a measured D(s) along the
  way.
- **Marras et al.'s closing strands** hold the compact state. Their
  displacement returns it to about 10 % compact, so there the support is
  never dispensed with.

## What would answer it

A system where support can be withdrawn at chosen intermediate states,
with the committor measured afterwards from each, in molecules or in a
model. A's rival to exclude: "The system merely completed before
withdrawal".

## Note of 2026-11-05: the readings on aid dependency and self-sufficiency

Read at the owner's request to look to aid and development for theory on how aid-dependent organizations become self-sufficient; the works were chosen by the assistant.

- **Answers in part, from practice.** The Kenya summary ([NOTE-tmp46avq](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp46avq.md)) names resources, capacity and motivation, "sustainability will not be achieved if any one factor is missing", and linkages formed early, and finds that "gradual exit, with the opportunity for project participants to operate independently prior to project closure, made it more likely that activities would be continued". Fees charged "from the outset" held. In India ([NOTE-tmp9wlcj](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp9wlcj.md)) continuation followed a receiving programme with its own resources and a legal duty, linked before exit. Ostrom et al. ([NOTE-tmp22pjn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp22pjn.md)) make beneficiary ownership, including their own contribution and a say over continuing, "necessary, though not sufficient".
- **The route, from the state literature.** A state leaves dependency as it "learns how to tax" (Kaldor, in Moss et al., [NOTE-tmpd45i4](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpd45i4.md)). Moore ([NOTE-tmpfngj0](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpfngj0.md)) separates the origins of a fiscal bargain from its persistence.
- **A tension.** Azam, Devarajan and O'Connell's ([NOTE-tmpvgfdp](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpvgfdp.md)) optimal exit cuts aid up front, while the Kenya summary favours gradual exit. In Azam et al.'s model the recipient "is actually better off" dependent, so graduating is the donor's gain.
- **Two models.** In [CLAIM-tmpoh07b](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpoh07b.md), under aid used after the organization's own capacity, the support withdraws itself and A's measure D falls to zero with it; under substitution D grows with the support's duration. In [CLAIM-tmpw4ai2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw4ai2.md), where upkeep is self-sustaining, D depends on how long the withdrawal lasts as well as on the state.

No status changes.

## Note of 2026-11-05: the readings on reintroduction and ecological recovery

Read at the owner's suggestion to look to reintroduction and ecological recovery for what makes support safe or hazardous to remove; the works were chosen by the assistant. The assistant's synthesis is [CLAIM-tmpc3x0c](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpc3x0c.md).

- **A test.** Schröder, Persson and De Roos ([NOTE-tmph4p4q](../notes.d/NOTE-tmph4p4q.md)): a state is independent of an intervention once it lasts a full turnover after the intervention ends. The field's own standard for translocations is persistence "without further augmentation" (Morris et al., [NOTE-tmpbfqjw](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpbfqjw.md)).
- **A partial answer.** Tree islands weeded for 2.5 years kept their advantage to 16–18 years ([NOTE-tmpcw7tn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpcw7tn.md)). They became independent of the planter and stayed dependent on the landscape.
- **A counter-case to A's expectation.** Under captive rearing the cost of withdrawal grows with the support's length rather than falling ([NOTE-tmpkfeb0](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpkfeb0.md)). Once hatchery fish dominate, the unsupported comparison is gone and D cannot be measured.
- **The synthesis.** [CLAIM-tmpc3x0c](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpc3x0c.md) answers the question in three conditions.

No status changes.

## Note of 2026-11-05: the counterpoint and urban-development readings

Read at the owner's request for a counterpoint to the record's dependency material and suggestion of urban development and city planning; the works were chosen by the assistant. CLAIM-tmpc3x0c was revised in light of them.

- **Independence after support ended, in households.** Graduation packages kept their gains one year after all support ended in six countries, and in India they grew for years after contact stopped at 18 months (NOTE-tmpl3nvb, NOTE-tmpicnhp). The control group also grew, and the treated "barely escaped poverty".
- **A threshold for it.** Balboni et al. (NOTE-tmp6tzyt) find that the transfer had to carry a household past an asset threshold. Those left just below it fell back.
- **A caution on A's measure.** The Tennessee Valley Authority's gains persisted forty years after its subsidy ended, yet on Kline and Moretti's model (NOTE-tmp0lzl8) the region is not independent and is slowly reverting. D measured over a fixed horizon cannot tell the two apart.
- **Independence from a founder, not an agency.** In coproduced sewers the service became independent of its founding team and not of the agency's standing part (NOTE-tmpckznv).

No status changes.
