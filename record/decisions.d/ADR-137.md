# ADR-137: Filing a source fills the gaps it leaves in its own argument, in the assistant's voice, without offering first

## Context

The `read-and-file` skill ([ADR-068](ADR-068.md)) maps a source's ideas against the record and files what is not held, in the voice of whoever said it. It had no step for what the source leaves out. A source states a result and skips its derivation. It leaves a condition unstated, gets a number or an attribution wrong, or proposes a test with no design. It asserts a link between two ideas that neither idea's statement carries. The ledger noticed such gaps, but nothing said what to do with them. Step 12 covered only the assistant's own ideas across the whole filing, and asked that they be offered before anything was filed.

On 2026-11-21 the owner asked for the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" to be filed "including attempting to fill in gaps where you identify them". The idea ledgers listed the gaps item by item. Five drafters filled the real ones in the assistant's voice, all Proposed, each with any number checked by script:

- missing derivations, such as the hitting-time origin of the shared 3/2 exponent and the participation ratio under power-law spectra;
- corrections to eight of the other assistant's figures and attributions, each in the entry that uses the turn;
- questions and leads where a gap could not be closed.

Each gap left was given a reason. The owner then asked: "update the skill instructions (via ADR) to include the gap filling instruction."

## Decision

- **A gap** is a step the source's own argument needs and does not supply:
  - an unstated premise or condition;
  - a result asserted without its derivation;
  - a number, formula or attribution that does not check;
  - a test proposed without a design;
  - a link the source asserts between ideas that no component states.

  A gap is internal to the source. What only the whole filing shows, across sources or readings, is step 12's, not this.
- **Identify.** The idea ledger (step 2) gives each item a gaps column. Readers and drafters report the gaps they find, and drafters' briefs carry this decision.
- **Fill.** For each gap, decide first whether it is real. A real gap is filled in the assistant's voice, as a Proposed entry or a dated note, in one of these ways:
  - **the missing step**, as a claim with its derivation, checked with `python3 -I` where it computes;
  - **a correction**, in the entry that uses the mistaken step, quoting it and giving the right value with the check;
  - **a question**, when the gap is open and the record should hold it as open;
  - **a lead** ([ADR-109](ADR-109.md)), for a test or reading that would settle it.

  Each says which gap it fills and which turn or passage it answers. Its summary ends with the assistant's attribution sentence (step 12).
- **Left.** A gap that cannot be filled, or that is not real, is listed in the curation entry under "Gaps", with the reason. That section lists every gap the ledger found, as filled (with codes) or left.
- **Without offering first.** Gap fills are filed in the same pull request as the filing. Their standing is Proposed, and their voice line names the assistant, so adopting them stays the owner's call. Step 12's offer-first rule is unchanged for its own scope.
- **Always.** It is part of every filing of an exchange, a pasted synthesis or an upload. A filing of a single work reads the work; gaps in the work's own argument go in its NOTE's Limitations, and are filled the same way only when the record uses the step.

## Alternatives considered

- **Fill gaps only when the owner asks.** This is the status quo for this filing. It makes the most useful part of a filing depend on remembering to ask, and a filing that maps a source's gaps without filling them hands the owner a list of known holes.
- **Offer each gap fill first, as step 12 does.** The filing of 2026-11-21 had some fifty (the curation entry lists 54 filled). Offering them one by one turns a filing into a long negotiation. The protection the offer gives is already given by Proposed standing and the voice line. Step 12's ideas are different: they are new theses drawn across sources, and they move the record's own argument.
- **Fold gap filling into step 12.** This merges two scopes that differ in what they need: a gap is checked against the source it is in, and a cross-source idea against the whole record. It would also put step 12's offer-first rule on both.

## Consequences

- The `read-and-file` skill changes, in the same pull request:
  - step 2 adds the ledger's gaps column;
  - a new step 9a, "Fill the gaps";
  - step 10's curation entry adds a "Gaps" section;
  - step 12 says how its scope differs;
  - the reader's brief asks readers to report gaps in the work's argument.
- A filing takes longer, by the time to check each fill. It also catches errors a plain filing would carry into the record as the source's word: eight on 2026-11-21.
- The first application is the filing of "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (curation entry of 2026-11-21, 18:01), whose "Gaps" section cites this decision.
