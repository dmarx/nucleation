# CLAIM-1140: Failing to persuade does not show worthlessness

## The claim

A56 §5. An employee needs evidence to obtain authorization; the evidence
needs resources; the resources need authorization. "This is a circular
dependency in the query–response architecture." The proposal may be
undeveloped "not because the idea is fundamentally unpromising, but because
the institution denies it the developmental opportunities needed to become
evaluable." So "Importantly, the absence of a persuasive proposal does not
necessarily imply the absence of a worthwhile underlying possibility."

## Where it sits

[CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md): durable authority can be the regularity of grants earned by being
right, and the same loop entrenches what gained influence early. [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md)
reads it as structural testimonial injustice: an arrangement that decides
whose word is sought keeps an exclusion going through indicators of
competence that only being sought produces. This claim moves the loop from
persons to proposals, and from authority in general to authority to
inquire. [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md)'s caution carries over: the loop alone cannot tell a
neglected possibility from a worthless one.

## A gap, filled

A56 names the remedies (exploratory budgets, staged approval) without saying
why they work. [CLAIM-1016](CLAIM-1016.md): they change the unit of authorization from a
question to a domain, so evidence can be produced before any one question
has to be justified.

## Standing

Proposed, in A's voice.

## Note of 2026-11-23: what it adds to [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md) and [CLAIM-721](CLAIM-721.md)

A54 §6's loop, filed in the same batch as a note on [CLAIM-721](CLAIM-721.md), is "held by
[CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md)". So is this claim a restatement? It shares their form, a
restriction that withholds what would justify lifting it, and adds two
things neither states.

- **The unit and what is lost.** [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md)'s loop runs on persons and their
  indicators of competence; [CLAIM-721](CLAIM-721.md)'s on a competence that decays with
  disuse. Here it runs on a proposal, with nothing decaying: the evidence is
  never produced rather than lost, so [CLAIM-721](CLAIM-721.md)'s first condition, decay with
  disuse, is not needed. What the circle removes is a possibility from the
  organization's inquiry, not a person from its consultation.
- **An evidential conclusion, not a normative one.** [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md) needs a
  prejudice condition to call the loop an injustice, and holds that the loop
  alone cannot tell merited from unmerited standing. This claim needs no
  prejudice: its conclusion is about evidence, that a failure to persuade is
  weak evidence against the possibility behind it where the circle closes.
  [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md)'s caution is its other half: the same failure is no evidence
  for the possibility either.

So it stays a claim. The remedy it calls for is [CLAIM-721](CLAIM-721.md)'s "route to
practice" in its own unit, the change of authorization unit in
[CLAIM-1016](CLAIM-1016.md). The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session);
adopting it is the owner's call.

## Note of 2026-11-24: the exchange 'Memes, Contracts, and Systemic Normativity', A18 §5

A18 §5 ("Epistemic deadlock") reads this claim, cited by its code on main, in
the terms of distributed computing: "Both processes may behave exactly as
their contracts require. Neither needs to be incompetent, irrational or
malicious. Nevertheless, their contractual composition produces an
epistemic pathology. If there is no seed evidence or alternative route,
neither process can discharge the condition on which the other depends.
This is structurally analogous to deadlock." The remedy is [CLAIM-1016](CLAIM-1016.md)'s
change of unit: "A standing exploratory allocation can break the circular
dependency", which "does not guarantee that the inquiry will prove
worthwhile. It makes discovering whether it is worthwhile organizationally
possible." It names [CASE-388](../cases.d/CASE-388.md), by its code on main, as the design that
would test it. Bearing: an analogy ([CLAIM-tmpzuzeh](CLAIM-tmpzuzeh.md)'s catalogue), no edge.
