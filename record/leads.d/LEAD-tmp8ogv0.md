# LEAD-tmp8ogv0: Run the study of research organizations matched on familiar inquiry and differing in how inquiry is authorized and perspectives maintained, in simulation first, with an arm on slack that holds authorization fixed

## The design, as CASE-395 holds it

Organizations "possessing matched initial expertise and technical
resources but different arrangements of inquiry authorization and
perspective maintenance" meet "research opportunities varying in their
novelty, conceptual distance from established methods, and temporal
sensitivity". The outcomes are "the cost of formulating questions, the cost
of making them legible to evaluators, the probability of authorization,
time to adequate answer, and the ability to resume initially inactive
research programs". The discriminating result: matched performance on
familiar inquiries and "different rates of success on novel inquiries
because their authorization and perspective-maintenance arrangements
differ".

## What this lead adds, fixed before any run

1. **In simulation first**, so that each opportunity carries a hidden true
   value and success on the novel ones is scored independently of every
   arrangement (CASE-388's note of 2026-11-23, point 2, which CASE-395 says
   carries over).
2. **The slack arm**: an organization that keeps a reserve and one that
   does not, with authorization held fixed. Without it, a difference on
   novel opportunities could be CASE-412's and CASE-397's mechanism, a
   budget rule and a reserve, and not authorization or perspective
   maintenance.
3. **The unit of authorization as its own factor**: per-question approval
   against a standing grant for a domain, at matched total cost, since by
   CLAIM-1016 staged and protected arrangements change the unit and not
   only the cost per question.
4. **A control in which novel inquiry is genuinely unhelpful**, so that an
   arrangement that authorizes everything does not win by construction
   (A27's Study C condition, in CASE-388's note of 2026-11-24).

## What would count

- **For CASE-395's reading**: with slack and the unit of authorization
  held fixed, the arrangements still part on novel opportunities and not
  on familiar ones. That bears on CLAIM-1190 and CLAIM-1025 (joint
  inquiry capacity not fixed by present competence).
- **Against it**: the whole difference is carried by the slack arm, or by
  the unit of authorization, so authorization as such and perspective
  maintenance add nothing once those are matched.
- **The stronger test** (CASE-395): separate interventions on conceptual
  resources, authorization procedures and answer production, read only
  once the first comparison parts the arrangements.
