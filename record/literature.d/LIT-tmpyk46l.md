  2023). A 100-unit RNN trained trial by trial, without a meta-learning
  objective, on successive delayed sensorimotor association problems needs
  a few thousand trials for the first, a few hundred for the second, and
  an exponentially falling number after, plateauing near 20 (30 seeds). It
  reuses a low-dimensional decision subspace (the schema). Rotating the
  frozen readout off that subspace after the first problem makes the
  second as slow to learn as the first; control rotations do not. The
  monkey comparison is cited, unpublished. Read in NOTE-tmpk8fli; account
