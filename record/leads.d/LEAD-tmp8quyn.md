# LEAD-tmp8quyn: Study community formation on Reddit as it happens: monthly user and link graphs around the creation of new subreddits, tracking where founders come from, whether the community individuates and lasts, and whether it makes further communities cheaper to form

## What it would do

Follow candidate new subreddits through their formation, from monthly
graphs rather than a flattened map, and measure the case's four outcomes
(formation, individuation, complementarity, recursive productivity).
Hessel, Tan and Lee's early-participant fraction and exploration effect,
and Krohn and Weninger's inventive links, give measures for some of them.

## What is not yet settled

Which data are lawfully available now; what counts as a formation event
and as a parent; and a control for platform growth, which both readings
name as the confound for anything that looks like formation getting
cheaper. Specifying these is the next step.
