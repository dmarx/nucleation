# ADR-067: A manuscript is a view over OUTLINE and SECTION documents

## Context

[ADR-032](ADR-032.md) gave the record's own argument its schemes and a `works`
vocabulary, so a claim can say which manuscript it is marshalled into.
The manuscripts themselves stayed outside the record. CLAIM-178 says
what that costs: a manuscript drafted straight from an exchange drops
commitments and changes its thesis without saying so, and CLAIM-324
counted it happening.

In the exchange "Explain Nucleation Website" (2026-11-01) the owner
drafted a philosophy article, The Individual and Its Conditions, and
asked for it to be chunked into record objects that luria assembles as a
view. At U15 the owner said: "we can create our own local scheme in the
luria config, just add whatever you think would be useful. Maybe OUTLINE,
SECTION etc." luria 1.6.0 renders Jinja templates over the record
(`views:`; luria's ADR-130), reading documents with `doc` and their
sections with `part`.

## Decision

- **OUTLINE** (`record/outlines.d`): one per manuscript. Its frontmatter
  holds the title, an optional `subtitle`, its `works` value and
  `sections`, the ordered SECTIONs. Its body holds the abstract under
  "## Abstract" and anything else the editors want kept beside it.
- **SECTION** (`record/sections.d`): one per section. Its prose is under
  "## Manuscript text", and only that part is transcluded. Other parts
  (revision notes) stay in the record. `draws_on` names the CLAIMs, CASEs,
  TERMs, QUESTIONs and ARGs the prose uses. It is not a premise list, and
  being drawn on is not endorsement: each entry keeps its own status and
  warrant.
- **The view** is a template under `record/views.d/` that holds layout
  only, and an entry under `views:` that renders it into
  `docs/manuscripts/`. Moving a section is an edit to the outline's
  `sections`; revising one is an edit to the SECTION.
- **Status is the manuscript's own.** A Proposed outline or section is a
  draft. Its status says nothing about the claims it discusses, and
  marshalling a claim into a work (`works:`) changes no claim's status.

## Alternatives

- **A hand-written manuscript under docs/.** Lowest ceremony, and the
  thing CLAIM-178 warns about: it is a copy of the argument that drifts
  from it.
- **A template holding the prose, reading only claims from the record.**
  luria's own suggestion of a manuscript built from its claims. But a
  claim's title is not prose a reader can follow, and the article's
  connective text would still live outside the record.
- **One scheme (SECTION only), ordered by number.** Order by number makes
  reordering a renumbering, and leaves the abstract nowhere.

## Consequences

- The first manuscript is The Individual and Its Conditions: OUTLINE-001
  and SECTION-001 to SECTION-009, rendered to
  `docs/manuscripts/the-individual-and-its-conditions.md`.
- A new manuscript needs an OUTLINE, its SECTIONs, a `works` term, a
  template and a `views:` entry. The template is short enough to copy.
- The schemes are local: the anthology has nothing like them, and a work
  moving between the records does not carry them.
