# THEORY-tmpqgklr: The Moral Foundations Questionnaire-2's six factors, Care, Equality, Proportionality, Loyalty, Authority and Purity, fit across 19 nations, Equality and Proportionality are nearly uncorrelated in WEIRD nations, and the US split between individualizing and binding foundations appears in only three national networks

## Source

Atari, Haidt, Graham, Koleva, Stevens & Dehghani (2023), [LIT-500](../literature.d/LIT-500.md) (*Journal of
Personality and Social Psychology* 125(5)), read in [NOTE-398](../notes.d/NOTE-398.md).

## What was actually shown

**Development (Studies 1a–1c, N = 3,360).** 116 candidate items, written and
tested in the US, India, Iran, Ecuador and China, were cut to 36, six per
foundation, in a "describes me" format.

**Structure (Study 2, N = 3,902, 19 nations).** ESEM: CFI .979, RMSEA .024.
Multi-group CFA: CFI .896, SRMR .070, taken as configural invariance.
Reliabilities ω .73–.95, Purity lowest.

**Invariance (Table 6).** Intercept non-invariance by alignment: Care 5.3%,
Equality 21.9%, Proportionality 11.4%, Loyalty 24.6%, Authority 16.7%,
Purity 39.5%. Only Purity exceeds the 25% threshold.

**Equality and Proportionality.** Mean r = .21 (SD .13) across nations, from
.47 (UAE) to −.04 (Belgium), more nearly orthogonal in WEIRDer nations. They
predict different criteria: redistribution and merit.

**Networks.** In the US, Care–Fairness and the binding three form separate
clusters. Exploratory graph analysis finds one dimension in most of the 19
nations and two in three (Ireland, New Zealand, Peru). Authority is the most
central node in 14.

**Cultural distance.** Distance from the US correlates with all six, most
with Purity (r = .43), then Loyalty (.25).

**Validity (Study 3, N = 1,410).** 17 of 18 preregistered criterion
correlations are supported. Mean adjusted R² over 30 outcomes: MFQ-2 .37,
MFQ-1 .30.

## What this does not say

- **That Purity is the least WEIRD foundation.** That correlation uses the one
  subscale that fails invariance, over 19 nations.
- **That the six fit in small-scale societies.** The samples are online,
  literate and mostly urban (p. 65).
- **That every item measures its own foundation.** Four items load more
  strongly on Authority than on their own factor ([NOTE-398](../notes.d/NOTE-398.md), Corrections).
- **That the MFQ-2 predicts better everywhere.** Study 3 is 82% US.
- **That six is the count.** Liberty, honour, honesty, ownership and
  efficiency remain unmeasured (p. 64).
