# SYNTHESIS-tmp8lbdb: The distributed-agency line is ten part accounts, of conferral and authority, of availability before a deadline, of partners coming to depend on one another, of perspectives, legibility and correction, of the unit of a combined form, of individuation, of frustration among demands, of persistence, of world-modeling and of collective stance, with twenty-one entries integrated directly; conferral, frustration and perspectives are joined by complements over self-confirming authority and what a legibility regime admits, unit and individuation by the boundary test and its objection, and persistence by the slow variable and the senses of persistence; no link between the parts that no part states passes the claim test

*The account of the distributed-agency line ("Distributed agency": what an organized whole is, and how its operative direction relates to its members'), over the accounts of its parts (ADR-083). It
integrates every entry whose `line` includes distributed-agency on
2026-11-11: 474 entries through ten part accounts, and 21 directly. A
mechanical community map of the line (stored relations and mentions)
gave seven parts; reading them, three fell into two or three inquiries
each, so there are ten accounts. CASE-028 carries the line and is
Superseded by CASE-006, which SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj integrates; it is not
integrated again. Many members carry another line, often first, and are
integrated by that line's accounts too; the part accounts say where.*

## The parts

- **Conferral and authority (SYNTHESIS-tmpg8tir), 99 entries.** Conferral
  is any effect on another process's share of future influence, and a
  grant is conferral through a maintained relation. How grants are
  counted and scored decides whether authority tracks reliability or
  entrenches: scored against the interpretation it helped make dominant,
  the loop can amplify an initial advantage without fidelity (CLAIM-297),
  and what keeps a judgement–observation loop from confirming itself is
  evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the
  interpretation it tests (CLAIM-342). Capture runs through the evidence
  an evaluation receives or the criteria it weighs by (CLAIM-404).
- **Availability (SYNTHESIS-tmpzmzgz), 21 entries.** What a system can
  deploy before a deadline is not what it has or knows it has (TERM-055);
  a failed search shows absence only with a certificate about the
  unsearched remainder; retrieval and evidence pull apart when a category
  is carved.
- **Partners coming to depend on one another (SYNTHESIS-tmpqnl6z), 11
  entries.** Which properties each route to dependence brings about, and
  which make a partnership a new individual, is open (QUESTION-277); the
  documented symbioses show those properties coming apart.
- **Perspectives, legibility and correction (SYNTHESIS-tmpvvtbk), 63
  entries.** An organizational perspective is not thereby an agent
  (CLAIM-418); an encompassing organization's success does not establish
  its constituents' well-being, and the converse holds (CLAIM-060); which
  distinctions must be legible is relative to a task (TERM-064) and to a
  policy (CLAIM-535); an arrangement can keep its effectiveness while its
  correctability degrades; and the wrong of reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining an organization is proposed as a breach of a bounded
  duty of answerability.
- **The unit of a combined form (SYNTHESIS-tmprxn12), 81 entries.**
  Relative to a stated target, a combined form is proposed to be one
  system while it is a gap-isolated block whose gap beats the cost of
  naming it (CLAIM-157, Proposed); any individuation built on sufficiency
  is relative to its target (CLAIM-152); the test cannot tell a produced
  boundary from an imposed one (CLAIM-194). A self in the self-governing
  sense takes part in governing the conditions of its own persistence
  (CLAIM-284).
- **Individuation (SYNTHESIS-tmp4vrjc), 71 entries.** From
  interconnection to individuation the record holds distinct achievements
  in a partial order. An individuation is well defined once its
  criterion is fixed (CLAIM-405), its senses need different evidence
  (TERM-088), and whether relations constitute an entity stays open
  (QUESTION-284).
- **Frustration (SYNTHESIS-tmplr2wo), 53 entries.** Frustration among an
  organization's demands is an ordinal fact about which sets of them can
  be jointly met (CLAIM-347, TERM-060); relief enlarges that complex for
  the same demands; the residual and governance regret refine it without
  identifying thwarting (CLAIM-357). For demands on offsets between
  contexts, the minimal conflicts are the cycles whose holonomy exceeds
  their tolerance (CLAIM-tmp9hy40, filed with this review, Proposed).
- **Persistence (SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj), 40 entries.** A collective that
  replaces all its members persists by causal transmission of the
  organization that produces it, not by resemblance (CLAIM-329,
  CLAIM-615); persistence has senses that come apart (TERM-071); a slow
  variable belongs to a collective's dynamics when its members read and
  regenerate it (CLAIM-327), and a level has a temporal facet (CLAIM-331).
- **World-modeling (SYNTHESIS-tmptxoa2), 6 entries.** Competent behaviour
  across a family of goals fixes the world model an observer must ascribe
  without identifying a mechanism; producing, maintaining and revising it
  are further achievements (TERM-086).
- **Collective stance (SYNTHESIS-tmpnu96v), 29 entries.** What a
  collective is credited with need not be what its members hold: an
  organized whole's operative intention is not the expectation of its
  members' intentions, a diffuse population can show a public stance
  without a collective intention, and a person can be organized by a
  judgement attributed to a collective that does not hold it.

## Integrated directly

- **Selflessness and the extended self.** CLAIM-491 (the owner's thesis,
  Proposed) answers QUESTION-300: what is called selflessness is concern
  for the good of a self extended to include others. CLAIM-492 (Active)
  objects that with no criterion of the self's extent the thesis is true
  by definition; CLAIM-494 (Proposed, empirical) objects that empathic
  concern produces motivation aimed at the other's welfare when perceived
  merging is controlled. QUESTION-301 extends the thesis across time to
  legacy, QUESTION-297 runs it backward to inherited trauma, QUESTION-299
  asks what the living owe the far future, and QUESTION-302 asks what
  carries a memory across the rebuilding of a nervous system. QUESTION-297
  and QUESTION-302 cite CASE-098, and QUESTION-301 cites CLAIM-420, both
  in SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj; these are its only links to the rest of the line.
- **Complementary accounts.** CLAIM-045 (Active) holds architectural,
  dynamical and goal-directed accounts of distributed agency to be
  complementary components of one account, and objects to CLAIM-110
  (Rejected), which held them three different ways of understanding an
  organized whole.
- **QUESTION-365** (first line will-organization): whether a conflict
  among an agent's processes can be settled by another organization
  taking up the ungranted demand. Its links (CLAIM-080, CLAIM-358,
  CLAIM-484, QUESTION-364, CASE-128) are to entries outside this line.
- **Ten questions that link to no entry of the line.** QUESTION-152,
  QUESTION-170, QUESTION-187, QUESTION-337, QUESTION-338, QUESTION-340,
  QUESTION-341, QUESTION-347, QUESTION-348 and QUESTION-352. Nine say they hold a promotion condition, or part of a claim, moved from a THEORY entry; QUESTION-170 was filed by a promotion audit. Four are near the
  line's subjects (corporate attitudes, a regulator's channel, payoff-driven
  synchronisation, influence networks); six are not (an index's search
  cost, compute trade-offs in game search, cache eviction, a Lorenz 96
  closure, materialized views, a court's remedy).

## Not stated on the record

Links between the parts, tested at this review by ADR-075's step 5 and
not filed:

- **Entrenchment as relief.** That authority entrenched by
  self-confirmation relieves frustration by removing demands from
  contention (SYNTHESIS-tmpg8tir with SYNTHESIS-tmplr2wo): a free parameter, whether a demand no process presses is removed or only quiet, which the part keeps apart from frustration (CLAIM-549: the quiescent complex is not the requirement complex).
- **One legibility for both loops.** That the evidence CLAIM-342 requires
  and the contests CLAIM-448 admits are one legibility regime
  (SYNTHESIS-tmpg8tir with SYNTHESIS-tmpvvtbk): CLAIM-448 and CLAIM-444
  already complement each other, and the conjunction adds nothing.
- **The block's lasting as persistence by transmission.** That the unit's
  lasting (CLAIM-157) is what persistence by causal transmission
  (CLAIM-615) measures (SYNTHESIS-tmprxn12 with SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj): a
  free parameter, what makes a later block "that block"; with an exact
  copy put in without transmission, the two can be read either to agree
  or to conflict.
- **Individuation's criterion as the unit's target.** That CLAIM-405's
  criterion is CLAIM-152's target: CLAIM-405 rests on CLAIM-152 and says
  so.

## What it does not say

- That the ten parts are one inquiry. They are kept in one line because
  they are densely joined and the line's heading, what an organized whole is, covers them, not because a component says they are.
- That the ten questions without links belong in this line. Whether they
  should carry another line is the owner's call.
