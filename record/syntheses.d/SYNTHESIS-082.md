# SYNTHESIS-082: Whether local descriptions make one global one is settled only once the failure is located: the event sheaf always glues, and contextuality is a failure of extension over supports or distributions, which Contextuality-by-Default and the sheaf criterion judge alike until marginals shift; order effects, a sense that differs over disjoint contexts, frames that differ in coordinates and sortings of determinate objects are no failure of gluing, and ambiguity is proposed as several global readings where contextuality has none; passing to distributions creates solutions of a causal loop and never a global section the supports lack; over time intervals viable histories glue and may have no future, while observations that forget a hidden mechanism can fail to glue themselves; and when a coherent family has a realization stays open

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line ("results that hold whatever
any substantive line concludes"): the part on sheaves, presheaves, gluing
and global extension, and contextuality, as a mechanical community map of
the line's stored relations and mentions found it. The line's account is
built over it and its sibling parts ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)). Most of its entries are
first in another line. [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md), [CLAIM-044](../claims.d/CLAIM-044.md), [CLAIM-131](../claims.d/CLAIM-131.md), [CLAIM-141](../claims.d/CLAIM-141.md),
[CLAIM-144](../claims.d/CLAIM-144.md) and [CLAIM-197](../claims.d/CLAIM-197.md) are integrated by the pragmatic-transport account
[SYNTHESIS-069](SYNTHESIS-069.md), and [CLAIM-041](../claims.d/CLAIM-041.md) by [SYNTHESIS-062](SYNTHESIS-062.md); [CLAIM-310](../claims.d/CLAIM-310.md) and [CLAIM-313](../claims.d/CLAIM-313.md) by
the distributed-agency account [SYNTHESIS-057](SYNTHESIS-057.md); and [CLAIM-623](../claims.d/CLAIM-623.md), [CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md),
[ARG-074](../arguments.d/ARG-074.md), [QUESTION-379](../questions.d/QUESTION-379.md) and [QUESTION-437](../questions.d/QUESTION-437.md) by the realizability part account
[SYNTHESIS-046](SYNTHESIS-046.md). [CLAIM-374](../claims.d/CLAIM-374.md), [CASE-095](../cases.d/CASE-095.md) and [CASE-224](../cases.d/CASE-224.md) are integrated by no
account before this one. This account integrates every member again,
because each one's `line` includes formal-apparatus.*

## The account

- **Where an obstruction can lie.** [CLAIM-131](../claims.d/CLAIM-131.md) (A's, at A125 §2 and A129 §2
  after the owner's U41; granted, demonstrated, Active): "In the ordinary
  event sheaf, a compatible family of local sections already glues to a
  global section." What may fail is "the empirically constrained
  structure", and the claim sets two extension questions, over supports
  and over distributions, of which "Failure of the first is a stronger
  obstruction than failure of the second." Its "What it does not say"
  keeps out causal measurement scenarios, where "the strategy presheaf is
  not a sheaf", and the grading of obstructions.
- **Contextuality is a failure to extend, and the criterion depends on the
  marginals.** [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md) (the manuscript's §3 and §9; thesis,
  interpretive, Proposed) separates "three things: context dependence,
  stochastic noncommutativity, and contextuality as an obstruction to a
  global section", and holds that it "needs Contextuality-by-Default when
  marginals shift with context". [CLAIM-374](../claims.d/CLAIM-374.md) (the record's own derivation,
  split from [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md); demonstrated, Active) proves that "On a
  consistently connected system, the two agree", and leaves which to use
  where marginals shift to [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md): "Which to use there is [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md)'s
  question." [CLAIM-037](../claims.d/CLAIM-037.md)'s note of 2026-10-29 says what holds it back: "It
  goes Active on a comparison of a sheaf route with CbD 2.0 on one
  signalling data set."
- **What is no failure of gluing.**
  - *Order effects.* [CLAIM-041](../claims.d/CLAIM-041.md) (built over A21 to A30, stated in the
    manuscript's §9; granted, demonstrated, Active): order dependence "is
    sufficient to establish noncommutativity of the effective operations,
    but not sufficient to establish nonclassical probability".
  - *Disjoint contexts.* [CLAIM-197](../claims.d/CLAIM-197.md) (dual-filed from the Mathematics of Meaning record; thesis,
    demonstrated, Active): where two contexts do not overlap, "agreement
    is vacuous and they glue", so "tall" for people and for trees is one
    global section; "A gluing failure needs an overlap", and "which
    families count as covers decides whether anything glues".
  - *Frames.* [CLAIM-144](../claims.d/CLAIM-144.md) (A164's correction of the owner's U47, with the
    record's two repairs; granted, demonstrated, Active): inertial frames
    "differ in coordinates, not in a failure to glue", since "There is no
    cover whose sections could fail to glue."
  - *Sortings of determinate objects.* [CLAIM-310](../claims.d/CLAIM-310.md) (what is directionally
    right in the owner's LEGO monograph, as Mathematics of Meaning graded
    it; thesis, demonstrated, Active): sortings "never conflict as
    descriptions, since the product of any sortings refines them all",
    and conflict as arrangements, where the cheapest partition turns on
    the bin budget, the query mix and "what the wanted
    item must still be told apart by". [CLAIM-313](../claims.d/CLAIM-313.md) (filed at the owner's request; counter,
    demonstrated, Active) narrows the arrangement clause: "sortings
    conflict as arrangements only under a one-copy constraint, which
    binds bricks but not index entries", and [CLAIM-310](../claims.d/CLAIM-310.md)'s version 2 takes
    the condition. [CASE-095](../cases.d/CASE-095.md) (the monograph's example rebuilt; fictional
    construction, Active): "what the builders cannot share is an
    arrangement, not a description".
- **Other local-to-global problems.** [CLAIM-141](../claims.d/CLAIM-141.md) (A161 after the owner's
  U46, sharpened by the record; granted, demonstrated, Active): a causal
  loop and a contextual model are both local-to-global problems, but "A
  distributional fixed point exists even though no deterministic fixed
  point exists", while "a model with no global assignment consistent with
  its supports has no global distribution either". Its methodological
  half, from A161: "Always specify the domain of global realizability."
  [CLAIM-044](../claims.d/CLAIM-044.md) (A81 §3.4 and A82 §8.7, recovered; thesis, interpretive,
  Proposed): an ambiguous utterance "admits several compatible global
  assignments, a contextual one admits none". Its note of 2026-10-30 finds
  the title fails under both readings of "admits none" and leaves the
  restatement to the owner: "a merely ambiguous model is a mixture of
  several global assignments, a contextual one a mixture of none, and one
  utterance can be both".
- **Over time: gluing and continuation.** [CLAIM-625](../claims.d/CLAIM-625.md) (A's, at A24 and A25
  §2 of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition", with the reader's
  qualification; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed): "gluing is about
  agreement on overlaps; continuation is about whether a history has an
  admissible future"; under local dynamics and a pointwise constraint the
  viable histories form a sheaf, and for time-invariant dynamics a viable
  history extends exactly when its endpoint lies in the finite-horizon
  viability kernel ([THEORY-134](../theory.d/THEORY-134.md)), so "a
  coherent history can be terminal".
- **Over time: a presheaf that fails to glue.** [CLAIM-623](../claims.d/CLAIM-623.md) (A's, at A29 §4
  of the same exchange, with the reader's qualification; thesis,
  demonstrated, Proposed): forgetting a hidden mechanism can leave a
  presheaf of realizable observations that is not a sheaf, where "each
  local observation may have a valid hidden realization, while no single
  hidden realization supports the combined observed history". [CASE-224](../cases.d/CASE-224.md)
  (A's construction, checked by the reader's enumeration; stipulated,
  Proposed) is the instance: realizable (1, 0) and (0, 1) agree on period
  2, and "no run realizes their pasting (1, 0, 1)". [ARG-074](../arguments.d/ARG-074.md) (the
  assistant's wording on A's construction, filed at the owner's review;
  existence, Proposed) concludes [CLAIM-623](../claims.d/CLAIM-623.md) from it, and finds on the same
  enumeration that "a gluing failure need not show a disagreement on an
  overlap, and need not have an overlap", the second only because the
  periods are discrete.

What stays open is when a coherent family has a realization at all.
[QUESTION-379](../questions.d/QUESTION-379.md) (A's, Open): "when is such a presheaf representable in a
category of realizable systems", and is the object constructible within
the available resources; its "What would not answer it" excludes "A
failure of gluing". [QUESTION-437](../questions.d/QUESTION-437.md) (A's, Open) refines it: when locally realizable histories of a reduced
variable have no common realization, "does the failure identify a
capacity or constraint the reduction omitted", and whether it can be told apart from
modelling error.
