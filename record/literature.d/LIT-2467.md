# LIT-2467: Gacha Game: When Prospect Theory Meets Optimal Pricing

Tan Gan (2022). "Gacha Game: When Prospect Theory Meets Optimal Pricing"
*arXiv preprint, v3 2023-08-26*. [ARXIV-2208.03602](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.03602).

## Key takeaways

Randomized selling, loot boxes and pity guarantees included, can be
revenue-optimal against a single buyer, with no screening of buyers' values
and no minority of heavy spenders in the model.

## Standing in the record

Registered as a stand-in for [LIT-2381](LIT-2381.md). It carries the conclusion that
loot boxes can be optimal for the seller, not that paper's algorithms or
hardness results.
