# CLAIM-tmphsmbq: The regress of anchors, in which a protected anchor's own error needs a further anchor to correct it, resembles Gödel's incompleteness

## The claim

The owner: "the bottomless regress issue reminds me of Godel
Incompleteness". The regress is [CLAIM-tmpw11e6](CLAIM-tmpw11e6.md)'s. A protected anchor's
own error passes into the system it anchors. Only a further protected
channel corrects it, and that channel's own error would need another.

## The assistant's reading

[ARG-tmpqy0xr](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpqy0xr.md) assesses the analogy. [CLAIM-tmpklggb](CLAIM-tmpklggb.md) gives the mechanism:
the anchor's error is unidentifiable from inside by a symmetry. The
resemblance holds in its shape, and the mechanism differs.
