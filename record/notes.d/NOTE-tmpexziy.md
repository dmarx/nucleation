# NOTE-tmpexziy: Sridhar, Biagi and Wake, read for whether an instruction on the link from threat to action moves avoidance with threat expectancy unchanged

## Contribution

The first test of an instruction to drop avoidance against individual
differences in social anxiety, with behaviour, US expectancy and skin
conductance all measured. It reports no reliable effect of the
instruction on any of the three. The reading is THEORY-tmpwvuzp.

## Key results

1. **Design** (§§2.2, 2.4). Four phases: threat acquisition (CS+ paired
   with an aversive vocal comment and shock on 50% of trials), US-avoidance
   acquisition (an "avoid" button cancels the US), US-avoidance extinction
   (avoidance still available, US never delivered), extinction test (no
   avoidance, no US). The groups differed only in the instruction given
   before avoidance extinction. CBT group: "Please press the non-avoidance
   button to test your expectancy of whether the noise and electrical
   stimulus will occur"; control: "you may take a short break and
   continue when you are ready". "No information was given regarding
   CS-US contingencies."
2. **Avoidance, main effect of instruction** (§3.2.3): "There was not a
   significant main effect of instruction type (CBT-instruction vs
   control-instruction) or a significant Stimulus x Instruction Type
   interaction", F(1, 88) = 1.46, p = .230, and F(1, 88) = 0.21,
   p = .646.
3. **Avoidance, by social anxiety** (§3.4.3). With SPIN alone, no
   interaction with instruction (Instruction Type x SPIN F(1, 88) = 1.14,
   p = .288). Estimated at +1 SD of SPIN, "there was no significant
   difference in avoidance between instruction conditions (p = .066),
   although estimated means suggests lower avoidance in the
   CBT-instruction compared to the control-instruction condition";
   "Given the absence of significant interactions involving SPIN, this
   follow-up analyses are interpreted as exploratory."
4. **US expectancy** (§3.3): after the instruction, "This model did not
   demonstrate any significant main effects of or interactions with
   Instruction Type on US expectancy ratings [max F = 0.48, min
   p = .617]." Means across both groups, CS+: 7.31 after avoidance
   acquisition, 5.10 after avoidance extinction, 3.28 after the test
   (Table 2).
5. **The authors' reading** (§4, Conclusion): "a brief instruction derived
   from cognitive behavioural principles reduced avoidance behaviour
   among individuals with higher social anxiety, it did not attenuate
   conditioned threat expectancies", and "These findings underscore a
   potential dissociation between behavioural responses and cognitive and
   physiological components of fear".

## Assumptions

US expectancy, rated on a 9-point scale at six block boundaries, is taken
as the represented threat. Avoidance is the proportion of "avoid" presses
over 8 CS+ and 8 CS− trials per phase. Social anxiety is a continuous
SPIN score; 68% of the sample exceeded the clinical cut-off.

## Concepts

- **Instructed extinction of avoidance**: telling participants to
  withhold avoidance in order to test their expectancy, after Pittig and
  Wong (2021), LIT-tmpi1fyv.
- **Dissociation of fear components**: behaviour, expectancy and
  physiology responding differently to one intervention.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-446, near miss.** The design is close to the claim's
  authority-correction arm: the instruction targets what governs the
  avoid choice, not the CS–US contingency, and threat expectancy is
  measured. It does not meet the claim's test. Threat belief is measured
  and not different between groups, not held fixed by design; the
  instruction is a request the participant may simply obey, so it does
  not separate a change in the defensive process's authority from
  compliance; and the effect on avoidance is not significant. It neither
  supports nor counts against the claim. No relation written.
- **QUESTION-325, background.** Neither the uncertainty estimate nor the
  belief about one's own future avoidance is measured, so the reading does
  not discriminate the rivals.

## Limitations

- The avoidance result the abstract states is a non-significant
  exploratory contrast (p = .066); the main test is p = .230.
- Single session, 8 trials per stimulus in the phase the instruction
  targets, power planned for f = 0.25 on an ANOVA approximation.
- Allocation was by a second assistant balancing SPIN scores, not stated
  as random.
- No manipulation check that the instruction was followed, beyond the
  avoidance rate itself.
