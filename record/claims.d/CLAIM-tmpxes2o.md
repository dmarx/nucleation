# CLAIM-tmpxes2o: If a willed agent is the jurisdiction of its will-organization, then governing one's own governability is in part governing one's own individuation, in the constitutive sense of individuation at that level: a revision of the will-organization that changes which regimes it governs changes which willed agent there is, so a self-forming revision can be a re-individuation and not only a change in an agent's states, and whether the revised jurisdiction continues the earlier agent is a question of continuation, not of resemblance

## The claim

[CLAIM-170](CLAIM-170.md), the owner's: willed agents are individuated by their
will-organization; a willed agent is the jurisdiction of a
will-organization. [CLAIM-tmpxpdqi](CLAIM-tmpxpdqi.md): freedom is an agent's governance of its
own governability, including revising the relations that govern it.

Put together:

- **Revision can move the jurisdiction.** When the agent revises its
  will-organization so that it governs regimes it did not, or no longer
  governs some it did, the jurisdiction changes. By [CLAIM-170](CLAIM-170.md) so does which
  willed agent there is.
- **So reflexive governance reaches individuation.** A self-forming revision
  ([CLAIM-tmpbeh2e](CLAIM-tmpbeh2e.md)) that adds a channel or a distinction under the
  organization's governance is, at this level, a re-individuation, not only a
  change of state in a fixed agent.
- **Continuation, not resemblance.** Whether the revised jurisdiction is the
  same agent is then [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md)'s question, asked of jurisdictions: a matter
  of how the later organization came from the earlier ([CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md),
  [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md)), not of how much they resemble each other.

This is the positive side of [CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md), a self as "a historically continuous
organization of processes that takes part in governing the conditions of its
own persistence, differentiation and transformation". It says what the
taking part does: it can change which willed agent there is.

## What it does not say

- **That every revision re-individuates.** One that leaves the jurisdiction
  unchanged does not.
- **Anything about the other senses of individuation.** A pattern can be
  dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside ([TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md);
  [CLAIM-167](CLAIM-167.md)). A31 conflated these, as the owner corrected on 2026-10-27.
- **That the re-individuated agent is the agent's own.** Whose revision it was
  is [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md)'s, and manipulation is [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)'s.
