# LIT-tmpwfksd: Communication dynamics in the human connectome shape the cortex-wide propagation of direct electrical stimulation

Seguin, C., Jedynak, M., David, O., Mansour L, S., Sporns, O. & Zalesky, A. (2023). Communication dynamics in the human connectome shape the cortex-wide propagation of direct electrical stimulation. *Neuron* 111(9): 1391–1401.e5. doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2023.01.027. Read as the bioRxiv preprint, doi:10.1101/2022.07.05.498875.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of the preprint, [NOTE-tmpgs5vt](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpgs5vt.md).*

- **Data.** "550 participants, 29,055 stimulations and 2.77 million pairs
  of recording electrodes".
- **Diffusion beats routing.** "search information consistently ranked as
  the most explanatory network measure of response probability and
  amplitude". Between unconnected regions, "search information and
  communicability markedly outperformed alternative network measures".
- **Distance first.** Euclidean distance has the strongest single
  correlation. In the multivariate model, "Euclidean distance (40.2% average
  contribution), search information (32.5%) and FC (24.6%)" give
  out-of-sample R² = 54% for response probability.

## Standing in the record

A's counts are exact; A said nothing of the results. This is graph
communication, not wave dynamics. Its measured pairwise responses, which
include indirect paths, are the macroscale analogue of Randi & Leifer's
connected response functions ([LIT-tmp0mf8t](LIT-tmp0mf8t.md)). [LIT-998](LIT-998.md) shares Sporns as an
author, not the result.
