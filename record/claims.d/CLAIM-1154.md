# CLAIM-1154: In contested open-source successions an outside party's verdict on which line continues the project is given by moving to it: the statement of legitimacy and the move are one act, so the verdict is a participation that makes the succession and not a prior judgement that causes it

## The claim

The same records as [CLAIM-1067](CLAIM-1067.md), read the other way. SourceHut did not
first judge freenode's new leaders illegitimate and then move; its post
does both, and its reason for the destination is that "the staff — have
left for Libera Chat", and SourceHut is "proud to be following them". Fedora
did not first hold that MariaDB continued MySQL and then replace it; the
replacement, with its reasons, is the verdict. On this reading the verdict
of a party outside the founders is performed by its move, and
[CLAIM-617](CLAIM-617.md)'s A36 gloss, "Through coordinated participation, it can help
constitute a new one", fits these cases better than its causal title.

## What it does not say

- That every move is a verdict. Fedora's replacement of Redis gave only
  the licence, a move with no verdict on identity in it.
- Which reading is right. This is filed as the alternative to
  [CLAIM-1067](CLAIM-1067.md), which the assistant leans to because the records'
  silence has a plain cause (the kinds of document read) that needs no
  claim about verdicts.
