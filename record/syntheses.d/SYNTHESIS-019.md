# SYNTHESIS-019: The owner's thesis that experience is constituted by interpretive organization stands with its sufficiency condition unstated: interpretation as such does not suffice, current grounding and a represented owner are kept apart from what would make an organization experiential, the line's one candidate condition makes valence felt only in the access sense, and each test of the rest meets the limit that the record's evidence about experience reaches it through access

## The account

**The thesis and what it concedes.** The owner's thesis, at U1 and U2 of
the exchange of 2026-10-21, is that experience is constituted by
interpretive organization, not produced by it: an appropriately organized
process in which signals acquire significance for a system is the
experience ([CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md), Proposed). Interpretation is defined without
experience ([TERM-073](../terms.d/TERM-073.md)), so that the identity is not circular, and the
definition's cost is [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md) (Active): under it, processing not shown
to be experiential, apparently unconscious blindsight among it, counts as
interpretation, so "interpretation alone does not establish experience".
[CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md) says what kind of objection it is: "The objection is that the
thesis is incomplete, not that it is false." [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) concedes it (note
of 2026-10-30: "The objection is conceded: interpretation alone does not
establish experience"), names the further condition as perspectival
organization (its part 3), and has not stated it. [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md) asks for
that condition; it "narrows [QUESTION-046](../questions.d/QUESTION-046.md) to the constitutive thesis",
[QUESTION-046](../questions.d/QUESTION-046.md) being the criterion that would tell a subject from an agent,
applied one way to a modular mind and to a group. In [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s words the
thesis is a bridge: "The claim's bridge is from sense 1 to sense 3"
([CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s note of 2026-10-22), from an organizational perspective to a
phenomenal one, and [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) says the bridge "can't be built into the
operational definition" (A11).

**What is kept apart from the condition.** Two things are separated from
what would make an organization experiential, the first as a constraint
on any answer, the second as a different question.

- *Current grounding.* [CLAIM-413](../claims.d/CLAIM-413.md) (Active) makes grounding a relation and
  separates its current, developmental and epistemic senses; in the
  current sense it comes apart from whether the interpretation occurs: "What
  makes an interpretive organization experiential is not necessarily
  what makes its contents veridical" (A7). [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) does not rest on it:
  "Ungrounded experience is a case the thesis must cover, not a premise it
  needs." [CLAIM-413](../claims.d/CLAIM-413.md) says the same from its side: "Neither rests on the
  other." [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md) takes [CLAIM-413](../claims.d/CLAIM-413.md)'s narrowed condition into its
  title: an answer "must not require an episode's current contents to be
  veridically constrained by their referents", and may still require
  developmental grounding, bodily regulation or counterfactual
  sensorimotor organization.
- *A represented owner.* [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) (Proposed) holds that in a distributed
  agent the binding of appraisal, readiness and governance can be real
  while the single owner a self-model attributes it to is a
  representational construction, and "this deflates the self without
  settling whether experience is real". [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) places it: "If the claim
  holds, a constructed owner does not mean there is no experience", in
  A5's words "there might be real experience without an additional entity
  that owns it". [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) (version 2) fixes which representation is
  meant: "[CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)'s constructed owner is a represented owner, not a
  represented perspective", and [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)'s second contrast, a
  perspective against a representation of one, records that "[CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)
  puts the illusion, where there is one, in the second". [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md), the
  design that would test [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)'s misattribution, "tests attribution
  of control, not phenomenal properties"; its note of 2026-10-21 records
  A5's proposal of [CLAIM-413](../claims.d/CLAIM-413.md)'s grounding as a third factor. The
  separation is not made a requirement on the condition: [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) only
  declines to say "that a self is required", and [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)'s note of
  2026-10-22 records that higher-order theories contest its second
  contrast, since on them representing one's own state is what makes it
  experiential.

**Organizational facts that matched behaviour hides.** [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) (Active,
demonstrated, on [CASE-126](../cases.d/CASE-126.md)) shows that matched ordinary trajectories can
conceal an interpretation's efficacy, the allocation of that efficacy and
the maintenance of its enabling constraints, and that a specified
intervention exposes them. Its defeater says that "it does not say any of
them bears on experience". [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)'s note of 2026-10-22 uses it on
one candidate condition, self-maintenance: "[CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md) shows that this is
a separate, interventional fact", and A11 withdrew it as necessary, so it
is a subclass in [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md). [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s note of 2026-10-22 draws the
consequence for proposed criteria of consciousness (Whyte et al.,
Laukkonen et al.): "behavioural tests for them ... need an intervention".

**The one candidate condition on the line.** [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) (Proposed, filed
at the owner's direction) holds that a regime of valence is felt "in the
sense of attended and available to the system's other processes" when a
deeper process in the system models it and the model is used elsewhere.
It offers itself as "a candidate for [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s open sufficiency
condition, for affect only", and adopts the access sense of "felt"; if
"felt" is given a phenomenal sense beyond access, "the claim's third
defeater applies". [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s promotion (history, version 5) records the
record's ruling on it: "none of the candidates tried in the exchange or
in the readings of 2026-10-22 ([QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md), [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)) states a
qualifying organization independent of report". [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md) (Open),
from the synthesis review of 2026-11-07, asks whether [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s
self-model and [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md)'s are one: "The record holds two self-models
and does not say whether they are one."

**The evidential limit.** [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md) (Open) states the line's standing
problem: "Every kind of evidence the record holds about experience
reaches it through access". The thesis's own defeater meets it: a
defeating case "needs evidence of experience that does not run through
the organization, which is [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)'s difficulty" ([CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)).
[QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md): "Its evidential side is [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)'s." [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) keeps
[QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)'s distinction between access and experience, and
[QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md) meets "[QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)'s difficulty" wherever its outcome can
be read only through report. [CLAIM-460](../claims.d/CLAIM-460.md) (Active) applies the same limit
to judgement, "applied to judgement rather than experience": in the
completely locked-in state, evidence of lucid judgement must cross a
channel the patient drives, and "a null result cannot tell lost
judgement from a lost channel". [TERM-082](../terms.d/TERM-082.md) (Proposed) keeps the senses of
"consciousness" apart as a boundary: "a claim about organization, access
or self-modelling does not become a claim about experience by sharing a
word with it".

**Why the evidence offered does not decide it.** The owner's examples,
the rubber hand illusion and CBT, with interoceptive inference added,
are what a production account predicts too. [ARG-018](../arguments.d/ARG-018.md), the argument from
them to [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md), is Rejected, on A7's verdict, which [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) adopts:
"These motivate interpretive construction. They do not, individually or
collectively, prove interpretive constitution."

**What stays open.** The condition ([QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)) and the criterion it
narrows ([QUESTION-046](../questions.d/QUESTION-046.md)); what any kind of evidence can establish about
experience rather than access ([QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)), with COGITATE's two
promotion conditions on synchronization ([QUESTION-350](../questions.d/QUESTION-350.md)) and prefrontal
content ([QUESTION-351](../questions.d/QUESTION-351.md)); what unity is under the thesis, "What unity is
under the claim is [QUESTION-255](../questions.d/QUESTION-255.md)" ([CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)); whether the two self-models
are one ([QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md)); and whether phenomenality without a self or
valence grounds moral standing ([QUESTION-368](../questions.d/QUESTION-368.md)). [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md) has not been run.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each route of the test ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5) was
tried against the components as written.

- **The complements cluster** ([CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md), 411, 412, 413, 439). Taken whole
  it conjoins its components, and a conjunction cannot fail while every
  conjunct holds. Its integrating sentence, that what would make an
  organization experiential is neither interpretation as such, nor
  current grounding, nor a represented owner, is stated: [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)'s
  title holds the first two conditions and its second contrast the third,
  and [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s "Where it sits in the record" places [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) and
  [CLAIM-413](../claims.d/CLAIM-413.md). The narrower link between [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) and [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) is
  [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md), asked and not claimed. That [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) is a candidate for
  [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s condition is [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s own summary, and [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s
  history rules that it does not meet the defeater.
- **Derived.** That the line's experiential claims can be tested only
  through evidence that reaches experience by access is stated piecemeal:
  [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s defeater, [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md) and [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md) each name
  [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md)'s difficulty, and [CLAIM-460](../claims.d/CLAIM-460.md) calls it the line's standing
  point. As a single derived claim it needs a premise no claim holds:
  [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md) is an open question, and the deficit limit it cites is a
  reading ([THEORY-091](../theory.d/THEORY-091.md)). That [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s facts satisfy [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s demand
  for an organization independent of report is trivial for report and
  leaves the classification [CLAIM-411](../claims.d/CLAIM-411.md)'s defeater asks for, and
  [QUESTION-254](../questions.d/QUESTION-254.md)'s perspectival-target problem, where they were.
- **Integration.** The discriminating comparisons the line names are
  [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md) (owner content varied, control held), [QUESTION-442](../questions.d/QUESTION-442.md)'s crossed
  intervention, and [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s case against Grandjean et al. (strong,
  lasting synchronization that no further regulation uses). Each is
  stated in its entry and none has been run, so a sentence settling one
  would have nothing to stand on.

## What it does not say

- That experience is constituted by interpretive organization; [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)
  is Proposed, and the condition it owes is unstated.
- That any system, group or machine has, or lacks, experience.
- That a constructed owner makes experience unreal ([CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) refuses the
  inference), or that "felt" in [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md)'s sense is phenomenal.
- That any of [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md)'s organizational facts bears on experience.
- Anything with standing of its own.
