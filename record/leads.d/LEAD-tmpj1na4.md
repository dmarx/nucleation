# LEAD-tmpj1na4: Find cases where access and experience come apart and each kind of evidence follows one of them: superblindsight, inconspicuous rivalry recorded intracranially, a marker accepted in advance

## What it would do

Search the literature for each kind of evidence (report, no-report,
deficit, architectural) for a case where access and experience dissociate,
and register what is found.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-048: "Superblindsight, inconspicuous rivalry recorded
  intracranially, or a marker both sides accept in advance would each do
  this."
- QUESTION-061: "A recording in which one content is available to one
  consumer and demonstrably absent from another at the same time"

## What would count

A case for each kind answers QUESTION-048 and settles what CLAIM-039 and
CLAIM-003 need from it. Finding none for some kind says that kind cannot
tell access from experience.
