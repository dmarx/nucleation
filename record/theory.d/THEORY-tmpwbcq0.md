# THEORY-tmpwbcq0: Agential realism has no answer to the measurement problem as it stands: holding that measurement never disentangles apparatus and object, it cannot borrow relational quantum mechanics' treatment of the wave function as bookkeeping without giving up the ontological holism, and its appeal to an improper mixture does not explain why one value is found

## Source

Faye & Jaksland (2021), LIT-tmpstodb, §5; read in NOTE-tmpdn71t.

## What was actually shown

Barad (2007, 348): "we should not conclude from the fact that the
entanglement is not made explicit by this measurement that the
entanglement has become ontologically 'disentangled'". Relational quantum
mechanics has no collapse because it treats the wave function as a
bookkeeping device; agential realism has intra-actions that produce
determinate values while the systems "become (ontologically) entangled".
That the system "appears as a mixture if the degrees of freedom of the
instruments are bracketed" (2007, 346) "does not explain why a measurement
finds one rather than another value associated with the eigenstates of the
(improper) mixture". Taking Rovelli's solution "would compromise the
ontological relational holism that drives many of the other alleged
consequences of agential realism".

## What this does not say

- That agential realism is incoherent. The authors allow that subtler
  answers may exist, and conclude that saying it is consistent with
  quantum mechanics "involves a leap of faith".
- That relational quantum mechanics' answer is satisfactory; a note cites
  Laudisa (2019) that treating the evolution as brute is not explanatory.
