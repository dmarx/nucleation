# QUESTION-003: For a family of communicative decisions, what is the minimum information rate at which a translation preserves decision performance within a tolerance?

## Why it is a question

A110 §IX.2: "For a family Q of communicative decisions, what is the minimum
information rate required to preserve decision performance within a tolerance?
This is a context-indexed pragmatic rate–distortion problem." A86 §10 had named
it first: "compression preserving a chosen family of decision problems".

## Where it stands

The manuscript §5 has the ingredients: the rate–distortion function, Blackwell
comparison restricted to a family Q, and decoder side information ([CLAIM-050](../claims.d/CLAIM-050.md)). It
does not ask for the rate. The audience-dependent version is [QUESTION-020](QUESTION-020.md).
