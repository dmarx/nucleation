# SYNTHESIS-tmp4vrjc: From interconnection to individuation the record holds distinct achievements in a partial order: interconnection makes effective constraints that gluing only expresses, coupling separates time scales only where it dissipates differences, an individuation is well defined once its criterion is fixed and its senses need different evidence, where maintenance is realized is shown only by interventions on production relative to a declared boundary, and assembling, persisting, regenerating and being supplied come apart, while how distinctions are made and unmade, and when relations constitute an entity, stay open

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the part a mechanical
community map of the line's stored relations and mentions calls
individuation, 71 entries. The line's account is built over it and the
accounts of its sibling parts. Fourteen of its entries carry another line
first (realizability, transient-support, will-organization,
formal-apparatus, pragmatic-transport), and some are integrated again by
those lines' accounts: SYNTHESIS-032 (CASE-225, CLAIM-597, CLAIM-604,
CLAIM-606, QUESTION-399), SYNTHESIS-041 (CLAIM-532, CLAIM-610),
SYNTHESIS-042 (QUESTION-373, QUESTION-388, QUESTION-390), SYNTHESIS-043
(CLAIM-566), SYNTHESIS-046 (ARG-072, CASE-225, CLAIM-611, CLAIM-616),
SYNTHESIS-037 (CLAIM-616) and SYNTHESIS-029 (CLAIM-394). This account
integrates them again because their `line` includes distributed-agency.
It adds nothing of its own.*

## The account

- **The partial order.** CLAIM-474 (the record's, at the owner's request
  of 2026-10-27, interpretive, Active) holds that from interconnection to
  individuation the relationships are distinct achievements: "none implies
  the next, and a later one need not require every earlier one". Its table
  sets interconnection, compatibility, dynamics, individuation (with
  organizational autonomy as the further question) and inquiry against the
  claims that hold each, and its list of failed implications gives each
  step holding while the next fails. Its note of 2026-10-31 records the
  owner's rule of method: "derive the dependency structure of the
  achievements separately from the order in which an inquiry met them".
  CASE-179 (a proposed study from A22 §10, A23 §XIII and the owner's
  review, Active) is its central experiment: coupling, correspondence maps
  and apparatus revision varied independently, so that "The transitions
  CLAIM-474 says are distinct can be made to come apart on purpose".
  TERM-088 (A18 and A23 with the owner's review, Active) names the senses
  of individuation, descriptive, dynamical, constitutive and organizational
  autonomy: "The senses are not stages, and not degrees of existence", and
  a pattern sustained wholly from outside is "individuation without
  autonomy". CLAIM-515 (the owner's principle, interpretive, Active) asks
  of any cognitive property four questions, recoverable, exercisable,
  realized and maintained, that "can come apart", and lists CLAIM-474 among
  its instances.
- **Interconnection, compatibility and correspondence.** CLAIM-471 (the
  owner's U20, restated at the owner's review, interpretive, Active)
  separates physical constraint formation, correspondence discovery and
  correspondence adaptation: "Gluing, in every case, is the statement that
  local descriptions are compatible under specified relations. It neither
  generates the relations nor certifies them." TERM-093 (the record's,
  Active) names the correspondence structure and the three ways it is held,
  and "Only formation bears on the thesis that interaction generates
  constraints." CLAIM-480 (A22 and A23, demonstrated, Active): eliminating
  an equilibrated interior leaves a coupling no part has, a slow interior
  leaves a memory kernel, and "separation of time scales decides only
  whether that constraint is approximately memoryless"; for cellular
  sheaves the reduced constraint can be three-way, so "composition followed
  by reduction can force a change in the type of relational structure an
  adequate description needs". TERM-090 (the record's, Active) names that
  effective boundary constraint on two axes, memory and kind, and says it
  marks an interface, not an individual. CLAIM-495 (from A2 and A3 of
  "Paper assessment", proof the record's, demonstrated, Active):
  "compatibility among local frames can be partial and graded by
  direction", and "non-commutativity is not holonomy". CLAIM-510 (the
  record's, Proposed) keeps the stronger form CLAIM-471 dropped, that
  measurement can establish a correspondence, with no held case.
  QUESTION-282 (the owner's review, Open) asks when interaction changes the
  operative correspondence structure itself; QUESTION-283 (the owner's
  review, Open) when composition forces a richer relational vocabulary,
  and QUESTION-390 (A's, Open, first line realizability) asks it of
  origami. QUESTION-319 (A2 §5 of "Paper assessment", Open) asks whether
  holonomy among an organization's parts is an error or a maintained
  feature.
- **Coupling and time scales.** CLAIM-473 (the owner's U20, Rejected) said
  coupling necessarily separates time scales. CLAIM-481 (A20, A21 and A23,
  a counter, demonstrated, Active) refutes it: conservatively coupled
  oscillators split into normal modes and neither decays, "So coupling
  does not necessarily induce scale separation, and CLAIM-473 is false as
  stated", while dissipative coupling and a Laplacian's spectrum give the
  separation. CLAIM-478 (the owner's U12 with A12, interpretive, Proposed)
  holds that separation makes structure, "what is a realization at one
  scale works as a constraint at another", and that organization makes new
  separations near an instability. CLAIM-394 (the record's derivation,
  demonstrated, Active) gives an exact case, the reversible linear Gaussian
  VAR(1), where "any span of slow features is an exactly dynamically
  independent coarse-graining". CLAIM-395 (the record's proof,
  demonstrated, Active): a closed set of constraints has at least two
  members and, under two readings, a slow and a fast dependence, "so a
  system with a single constraint of its own cannot realise closure".
- **Slowing, and present strength against maintenance.** CLAIM-521 (A12
  §6, demonstrated, Active): in CASE-189's second construction the
  maintenance's recovery rate goes to zero at its fold while the coupling's
  gap stays near 0.989; "The coupled units are as tightly integrated at the
  edge of collapse as well inside the regime." CLAIM-626 (A's, A20 §4,
  demonstrated, Proposed) sorts critical slowing three ways, collective,
  transverse and maintenance, and "So an observed slow mode does not say,
  by itself, whether organization is forming, a reduction is failing, or a
  maintained capacity is failing." QUESTION-125 (A6 §2, taken up by the
  owner, Open) asks whether a block's identifiable boundary and a
  bifurcation coincide; its note of 2026-11-07 places CLAIM-626's locking
  arm and CLAIM-521 in two of its three regions, and "The third region, a
  gap closing with the regime and its behaviour intact, still has no
  case." CASE-227 (A's A29, Proposed) couples the Haken–Kelso–Bunz phase
  to a decaying capacity, and ARG-068 (the assistant's wording, Rejected)
  rejects A's inference that it instances CLAIM-521: "The case fits the
  threshold and not the fold."
- **Relations, relata and the criterion.** CLAIM-477 (the owner's U8,
  sharpened by A8, interpretive, Proposed): what an entity is, is partly
  fixed by the constraints on the configurations it can enter, and those
  constraints are realized and maintained by something, so "neither relata
  nor relations, nor specifications nor realizations, are prior without
  qualification"; it adds that "Maintained by something" is not
  "maintained by itself". CLAIM-405 (A8 and A9 on the owner's U8,
  interpretive, Active): an individuation is well defined once its
  criterion is fixed, and then "a verdict can be false", and units picked
  out by different criteria can cross. CLAIM-526 (the record's,
  interpretive, Active) answers the Simondonian readers: coexisting levels
  remove the question of agents adding up, not the question of levels, and
  "some of them are no". CLAIM-529 (the record's, interpretive, Active)
  sorts Griesemer and Shavit's criteria into those on interactions, which
  test a declared candidate, and those on members' attitudes, which
  individuate by a different relation: "So endogenization cannot draw the
  boundary first. It can test one that has been declared." QUESTION-284
  (A5, A6 and A10, Open) asks when constraints only characterize an entity
  and when they partly constitute it, and whether a detected, a maintained
  and a constituted boundary differ; its note of 2026-11-07 finds three
  relations under the word and asks that "An answer should say which of the
  three it is about." QUESTION-030 (A191, Open, first line
  pragmatic-transport) is its question for communicative acts.
  QUESTION-280 (Jaksland's, as A6 gave it, Open) asks whether separability
  is approximate independence: "Neither the paper nor the claim says whether
  separable enough is approximately independent."
- **Where maintenance is realized.** CLAIM-522 (A10 §4, demonstrated,
  Active): an outside controller supplying the production law as state
  feedback gives the same trajectory under every intervention on state,
  "Full observation of c changes nothing", and only interventions on the
  production mechanisms, relative to a declared boundary, separate the two.
  ARG-050 (the record's, contrast, Active) answers the fat-handedness doubt:
  the pathway causes the capacities with a delay, so the knockout is
  surgical, and "So intervention data locate a producer relative to a
  boundary and do not fix the boundary." CASE-189 (A9 to A12's
  constructions, Active) is the pair's model: reciprocal production holding
  a coupling, and a transient encounter forming a maintained connector.
  CLAIM-528 (the record's, interpretive, Active) splits "realized" into
  produced through and consisting in, of which interventions show only the
  first, and places the line between them at the description's time
  resolution: "This is CLAIM-405's criterion-relativity applied to the
  causation–constitution line." CLAIM-530 (the record's, interpretive,
  Active): producer and controller meet mutual manipulability's two
  manipulability conditions alike, so the boundary "is the input
  constitutive relevance itself needs". CLAIM-194 (the record's, a counter,
  interpretive, Active): a gap or coupling criterion passes a cell's
  membrane and a beam alike, and "The criterion has no term for who made
  the coupling." QUESTION-086 (A11's Q4, Open) asks by what intervention
  matched systems are told apart; its note of 2026-11-01: "If the question
  has an answer that does not presuppose a boundary, it will not come from
  interlevel experiments alone." CLAIM-456 (the record's, from CASE-161,
  demonstrated, Active): internal maintenance can be the more fragile, so
  "self-production is not robustness". CLAIM-532 (A33 §7 sharpened by the
  reader, interpretive, Active, first line will-organization) defines
  self-formation and its two tests, the second resting on CLAIM-522, and
  finds that after the fact "whether a self-maintained capacity was
  self-formed is a question about its history".
- **Cycles and closure.** TERM-070 (Montévil and Mossio's, adopted by the
  owner, Active) is closure of constraints. CLAIM-516 (A10, demonstrated,
  Active): under turnover with no basal production an acyclic production
  graph goes extinct, so a persisting capacity needs a productive cycle
  among its ancestors, and "The theorem's necessary condition is weaker
  than closure." CLAIM-531 (the record's, interpretive, Active): generative
  entrenchment can be acyclic, "So entrenchment plus a knockout effect does
  not give closure", and by CLAIM-516 read for interactions an acyclic
  order persists only on outside supply.
- **Persistence, regeneration and supply.** CLAIM-566 (A's, interpretive,
  Active, first line realizability): assembling, persisting over a
  specified horizon and regenerating after turnover have separate
  probabilities, and "A specified horizon, as A writes it, is what keeps
  them distinct." CLAIM-612 (A's wording on A22 §5, the reader's framing,
  demonstrated, Proposed): statistics conditioned on survival can be
  stationary and fast while absorption is certain, so "a surviving
  organization's stable statistics are no evidence that it persists";
  CASE-231 (A's, Proposed) is its chain, and ARG-056 (the assistant's
  wording, existence, Proposed) finds that "The last clause holds of
  stationarity and speed, and only nearly of the values." CLAIM-597 (the
  assistant's, demonstrated, Active, first line transient-support): with
  every part supplied from outside, an organization "can become independent
  of what founded it but never of what feeds it". CLAIM-604 (the
  assistant's, demonstrated, Active, first line transient-support): repair
  drawn in proportion to the intact share gives an ignition threshold,
  collapse and an unrecoverable outage, which supplied repair lacks;
  "Self-sustaining upkeep needs a nucleus. Supplied upkeep does not."
  CLAIM-606 (the assistant's, interpretive, Proposed, first line
  transient-support) gives three routes by which a localized structure
  persists and qualifies CLAIM-604: "The signatures are consistent with
  state-dependent upkeep. They do not identify it." QUESTION-399 (A's,
  Open, first line transient-support) asks when a productive nucleus
  becomes self-maintaining. QUESTION-373 (the assistant's, Open, first line
  realizability) asks who supplies the resource across a bottleneck, and
  QUESTION-388 (A's, Open, first line realizability) what makes a system's
  reconfiguring its own.
- **The persisting individual, and agency over it.** CLAIM-611 (A's
  integration corrected by the filer, interpretive, Proposed): when an
  organized individual persists through change of mode, "what persists is
  the particular organization that keeps its repertoire realizable", and
  that organization may lie inside or outside it; it was admitted by
  ADR-075's hypothesis route on CLAIM-522's controller swapped in.
  CLAIM-610 (A7, supposed, interpretive, Proposed) supposes the record's
  hypotheses demonstrated and makes agency and autonomy further
  organizations of the same individuated unit; it was admitted on one link,
  "The claim is admitted on that link alone, and the title says so."
- **Synchronization as a laboratory.** CLAIM-616 (the owner's thesis with
  A's qualification, interpretive, Proposed) offers oscillators as model
  systems for the record's distinctions, "a drive fixes where control lies,
  not whether it is legitimate". CASE-225 (A's, Proposed, first line
  realizability) is its forced Stuart–Landau oscillator, in which
  "dependence for timing, dependence for performance and suppression of a
  latent capacity are three things". ARG-072 (the assistant's wording,
  analogy, Proposed) concludes CLAIM-616: "The respect holds, and the
  differences limit what transfers without defeating the claim."
- **A maintained artwork.** CLAIM-273 (on the owner's suggestion,
  interpretive, Active): a maintained r/place artwork copied from a design
  is dissipative in its maintenance, but "its order is imported from the
  design and not born of an instability". CLAIM-274 (the owner's
  correction, interpretive, Proposed): participation thins selectively,
  "a cooling schedule the population produces for itself". TERM-054 (the
  owner's, Active) is canvas temperature. QUESTION-085 (Open) asks whether
  an artwork is self-maintaining like a flame or supplied like a convection
  cell, and holds that "A self-maintaining artwork and a twin held up by an
  outside crew would behave alike until the crew is withdrawn." CASE-078
  (the owner's proposed survival analysis, Active) is the test CLAIM-274
  names; "Nothing has been run." QUESTION-169 and QUESTION-320 (promotion
  conditions moved from THEORY-216 and THEORY-214, Open) ask how lasting
  artworks persist as a population and whether overthrow precursors hold
  in a second system.
- **Higher-level agents and causal emergence.** QUESTION-093 (A11's Q3,
  Open) asks which of predictive autonomy, interventional autonomy,
  closure and self-individuation a higher-level agent needs, and which come
  apart. QUESTION-250 (split from it, Answered by THEORY-222): an
  effective-information advantage can be had without closure or
  self-individuation. QUESTION-204 (Open) asks whether that advantage
  survives one intervention distribution; its first half is answered no.
  CLAIM-497 (the record's, at the owner's direction, interpretive, Active)
  holds that causes are found at the coarsest description whose
  interventions are unambiguous: "A macro can be the proportionate
  description. It is never a richer one." QUESTION-335 (Open, first line
  formal-apparatus) keeps the temporal macros.
- **How distinctions are made and unmade.** QUESTION-281 (the owner's U14
  with A14, Open) asks by which transitions the distinctions individuation
  needs come into being and pass away, and whether a form's creation, its
  stabilization and its constitutive role are three achievements. Its
  notes add Toner and Tu's creation and stabilization coming apart,
  frustration and composition as further routes, Griesemer and Shavit's
  stabilization before entrenchment, Dong's nucleation, and synchronization
  onset as the owner's proposed transition.

  from CLAIM-194.

  maintained organization's present strength or stationary statistics
  never show whether it will persist (CLAIM-521, CLAIM-612). As "can" it is
  a conjunction QUESTION-281 already states for its cases ("a way for a
  form's persistence to come apart from what an instantaneous criterion
  reads"); as a law it is against CLAIM-521's own note on the SCN, where
  coupling carries maintenance and the dissociation fails, and against
  ARG-056's trace of the hazard in the survivors' law.
- **One dependence as constituent, constraint or cause by resolution.**
  That a fast interior is at once a boundary constraint and a synchronic
  constituent, and a slow one a history-dependent constraint and a cause
  (CLAIM-478, CLAIM-480, CLAIM-528). CLAIM-480's note of 2026-11-01 states
  it for the chain.
- **Closure as the source of the boundary.** That under CLAIM-516's
  hypotheses the cycle a persisting capacity needs settles which producer
  is inside, answering CLAIM-522's declared boundary. Which capacities are
  in the production graph is itself the boundary, a free parameter;
  CLAIM-531 says counting members' own motives as outside supply "is a
  choice of boundary, not a finding", and CLAIM-530 leaves closure as a
  candidate that one-variable models cannot test.
- **Endogenized upkeep as a trade of robustness for independence.** That
  making upkeep an organization's own always adds failures it cannot
  recover from (CLAIM-456's double loss and producer outage, CLAIM-604's
  thresholds). As "can" it is a conjunction; as a law it fails on
  CLAIM-604's own linear uptake, which "gives a single transcritical
  threshold in supply, with no ignition barrier at fixed supply".
- **Coupling criteria blind twice.** That a gap criterion misses both who
  made a boundary (CLAIM-194) and how stable its maintenance is
  (CLAIM-521): a conjunction of the two, which cannot fail while they hold.
