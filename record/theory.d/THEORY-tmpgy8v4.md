# THEORY-tmpgy8v4: In data generated by a random hierarchical grammar, the Bayes-optimal denoiser recovers the top-level class only below a threshold noise, which becomes sharp as the tree deepens and exists only when the grammar uses a small enough fraction of possible tuples (branching times that fraction below one), while low-level features degrade smoothly with noise and so survive past the threshold

## Source

Sclocchi, Favero and Wyart (2024; PNAS 2025), [LIT-tmpxr3zk](../literature.d/LIT-tmpxr3zk.md), §§3–4, Eq. 8,
Figs. 5–7, SI 2–3 (Eqs. 19–59, Figs. S2–S7), as read in [NOTE-tmpe9j5q](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe9j5q.md).

## What was actually shown

**The setting.** The Random Hierarchy Model of [LIT-877](../literature.d/LIT-877.md): v classes and v
symbols per level, branching s, depth L, m rules per symbol drawn without
replacement from the v^s tuples, no tuple with two parents. f, about
m/v^(s−1), is the fraction of tuples a level uses. Each leaf is corrupted
to belief 1 − ε in its true symbol, ε spread uniformly; with the rules
known, the exact posterior is belief propagation on the tree.

**The calculation.** Averaging one upward BP step over draws of the rules
leaves messages of the same two-valued form, so the belief p in the true
symbol obeys p′ = F(p) = (p^s + f (m−1)/(mv−1)(1 − p^s)) /
(p^s + f(1 − p^s)). Since F′(1) = s f m(v−1)/(mv−1), the fully informed
point p = 1 is stable exactly when s f < 1 + O(1/v). Then a third fixed
point between 1/v and 1 divides initial noises that flow to recovery from
those that flow to chance, a transition at ε*(s f) as L → ∞. When
s f > 1, every ε > 0 flows to chance. Leaves have no such flow, so their
reconstruction falls smoothly in ε, and above ε* the levels' "unchanged"
curves cross: the class has changed while many leaves have not.

**What could have come out otherwise.** Exact BP on single draws of the
rules (v = 32, s = 2, L = 10) could have departed from the annealed map;
it matches the map for the class and the leaves (Figs. 6, 7a, S4, S6),
and the phase diagram's boundary agrees with ε*(s f). Gaussian diffusion
of one-hot leaves, mapped to an effective ε(t), gives the same shape
(Fig. S7).

## What this does not say

- **Not proved.** The threshold is the fixed-point structure of an annealed
  approximation that drops the fluctuations of the random rules; its
  fidelity to quenched BP is numerical, for one or two parameter sets and
  s = 2 only.
- **Not about trained diffusion models.** The denoiser knows the grammar.
  That a diffusion model trained on images computes something like this is
  an analogy from the shapes of classifier-activation curves (Fig. 2);
  nothing here shows a trained network performs belief propagation.
- **Not for every hierarchical grammar.** The sparsity condition is
  essential: with f = 1, the maximal case of [LIT-877](../literature.d/LIT-877.md) where every string is
  grammatical, the class is lost at any noise on deep trees. Results
  about the model in that regime, such as [THEORY-195](THEORY-195.md)'s, and results in
  this one should not be pooled without stating f.
- **Not that images are generated by such a grammar.** The ImageNet
  observations are consistent with it; the Gaussian-mixture contrast
  rules out one alternative only.
- **Not about hierarchies of attributes.** The hierarchy is part–whole, as
  in [THEORY-195](THEORY-195.md); it leaves [QUESTION-025](../questions.d/QUESTION-025.md) where it was.
- **Not an instruction.** It describes what an ideal denoiser can recover,
  not how to build or train one.

## Connections

It is an inference-side companion to [THEORY-195](THEORY-195.md) on the same grammar (that
one counts the examples needed to learn the grammar; this one assumes it
learned). Unlike [THEORY-186](THEORY-186.md), where hierarchical levels separate in
training time from coarse to fine, here they separate in noise, and the
coarsest level is the one that changes abruptly. The threshold is a
problem of reconstruction on trees, of the kind Mossel studied; the
criterion here is the stability of the informed fixed point, not a proved
reconstruction bound.
