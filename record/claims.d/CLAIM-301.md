# CLAIM-301: Iterative retrieval can turn a provisional interpretation into a premise: a framing selects what is encountered, what is encountered reinforces the framing, and the question being investigated drifts as well as the queries

## The claim

A21: "Iterative retrieval can make interpretation path-dependent: a
provisional framing changes what evidence is encountered, and the
encountered evidence can reinforce the framing that selected it." Its
example: asked why a treatment failed, an agent guesses dosage, searches
for dosage failures, finds dose-response reports, and ends by explaining
why the dosage was insufficient. "The agent has promoted one possible
answer into a premise of subsequent inquiry."

A21 separated two drifts. Query drift is the IR literature's: expansions
move a search off the information need ([LIT-1079](../literature.d/LIT-1079.md)). Question drift ([TERM-056](../terms.d/TERM-056.md)) is a
change in the need itself.

## The exchange as a case

A2 opened: "Nucleation is not a theory of prompting or neural-state
nucleation. It's an epistemic record." From there each turn searched the
record's machinery: memory architectures, filing economics, cache
invalidation, then complexity bounds for incremental maintenance. At U14
the owner: "I think you've lost the plot a bit here. luria is the
epistemic record, nucleation is an instance of it being used for a
particular purpose". A14 agreed it had turned "a discussion about the
scientific questions represented in nucleation into a research program
about improving the software that represents them". That is question
drift, corrected from outside, and it is the drift [CLAIM-191](CLAIM-191.md) predicts
toward the collaborator's framing.

## What it bears on

[CLAIM-184](CLAIM-184.md) says anchors entrench. This gives one route, and [CLAIM-191](CLAIM-191.md)
another reading of drift. A21 placed the remedy in the older
architecture: keeping "the user's information need distinct from the
agent's evolving working hypothesis may reduce the risk that retrieval
converts a conjecture into a hidden constraint". In the record that
separation is a QUESTION kept apart from the CLAIMs that answer it.

## What it does not say

That adaptive retrieval is biased. [CLAIM-292](CLAIM-292.md) is granted against that
reading.

## Note of 2026-10-29: filed with its concessions

CLAIM-292 is granted against reading this claim as saying adaptive retrieval
is biased, and "What it does not say" has said so since the claim was filed.
The title's pathology is turning a provisional interpretation into a premise,
which is where CLAIM-292 puts it too.
