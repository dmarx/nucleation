# QUESTION-tmpvpsv1: What must stay invariant across a developing inquiry's representations, its views, presentations and revisions of its grammar, for its intellectual objects to keep their identity?

## Why it is a question

The record keeps an object's identity by decision: a code, carried through
concretization by `formerly:`, with its status and title revised under
it ([SYNTHESIS-013](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-013.md): identity criteria the record stipulates, addressability
rather than storage). That fixes which object a view is about. It does not
say when a view, a manuscript or a revised grammar still describes the
same object rather than a different one under the old code: whether a
claim's revised title is the same claim, whether a manuscript's
restatement is faithful, or which relations survive a change of grammar
([CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md), where a relation had to be removed: [CLAIM-666](../claims.d/CLAIM-666.md)).

An answer would name what a correspondence across representations must
preserve: the proposition, its standing, its relations, the
distinctions it depends on, or some of these. A49 offers the atlas
([CLAIM-tmplz7ng](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmplz7ng.md)) as a proposed answer, and [CLAIM-tmp6xzjy](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6xzjy.md) says what its
compatibility condition requires.

## Where the record holds part of an answer

For communicative kinds the line pragmatic-transport asks the same
question of utterances ([QUESTION-029](QUESTION-029.md)) and proposes an invariant
independent of any one realization ([CLAIM-137](../claims.d/CLAIM-137.md)). For the record's own
arguments, [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) records how identity is lost across restatements.
