# THEORY-1438: Marras, Zhou, Su and Castro show that DNA origami joints made of stiff double-stranded bundles connected only by single-stranded scaffold restrict thermal motion to one designed degree of freedom whose range and stiffness are set by connection length, that a crank–slider and a Bennett linkage assembled from such joints follow their rigid-body motion paths, and that the Bennett linkage, rarely found unaided in its higher-energy compacted state, is held there by added bridging strands and released by toehold-mediated strand displacement, each move taking about a minute

## Source

Marras, Zhou, Su and Castro (2015), [LIT-2118](../literature.d/LIT-2118.md); read in [NOTE-1635](../notes.d/NOTE-1635.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Range set by connection length** (Simple Motion; Fig. 2): slider 1
  "revealed a range of thermal motion of 14.0 nm", slider 2, with longer
  connections, "resulted in a range of motion of 29.2 nm"; stiffnesses
  read off Boltzmann-inverted TEM distributions (0.42 pN/nm for slider 1;
  25 to 45 pN-nm/rad for hinge 1).
- **Rigid-body paths** (Figs. 4–5): the crank–slider (n = 56) follows its
  prediction "with some fluctuation about the ideally constrained motion
  path"; the Bennett linkage (n = 52) "closely follows the expected motion
  path for its rigid-body counterpart".
- **Holding and switching** (Figs. 5D–E, 6): 9.9% of free linkages
  bundled; locking staples hold 93% compacted; closing strands give
  93.1% bundled and opening strands return it to 10.0%; half-times 55 s
  closing and 49 s expanding.

## What this does not say

- That the grid fractions are the solution fractions: "These results
  represent upper bounds because surface deposition may force nearly
  expanded or nearly compacted mechanisms into their respective extreme
  configuration."
- Anything about folding yield: "Misfolded structures were omitted from
  the motion analysis", and no yield is given.
- That switching is repeatable: one closing and one opening are reported.
- Why expansion is fast: the electrostatic account is the authors' guess.
- Anything about how origami folds; it does not bear on [THEORY-1398](THEORY-1398.md).
