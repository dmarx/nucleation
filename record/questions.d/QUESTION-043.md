# QUESTION-043: For a person, what coherence measure is conserved along the trajectory when the person persists as the same self, and is a life story, whose content is known to change, a candidate for it?

## The question

[CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md) makes persistence approximate conservation of a coherence measure γ along the system's own trajectory. It stays Proposed because γ is unspecified. [QUESTION-035](QUESTION-035.md) asks how long a combined form lasts. For a person, the record holds one measurable candidate for γ: narrative identity, which the psychology literature codes and relates to a sense of sameness over time. The question is whether that candidate meets [CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md)'s form. Content that changes, as therapy stories do, might still conserve the coherence the measure needs, or the conservation might fail exactly where identity is said to hold.

## Why the record needs it

[CLAIM-161](../claims.d/CLAIM-161.md) offers the person across time ([CASE-013](../cases.d/CASE-013.md), [CASE-048](../cases.d/CASE-048.md)) "a test that can fail", but not until γ is fixed. [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) warns that every γ is target-relative. A candidate with its own measures, and its own dissenters, makes that relativity concrete. If narrative identity is the form diachronic identity takes for some people only, γ differs across persons, and the criterion must say so.

## What the record holds

- [THEORY-060](../theory.d/THEORY-060.md): a life story is "the form a person's diachronic identity takes", in Erikson's sense of "sameness and continuity across different social roles and over time". It is coded, and its content changes first, then "the storyteller gets better". That offers a measurable γ and shows the content is not conserved. Limits: a review essay; universality is its weakest point, and Strawson's Episodics deny it. It makes no claim about numerical identity.

## What would answer it

A specified γ computed on repeated narrative codings of one person. It should be level through role changes the person reports as continuous and drop where they report discontinuity. People low in narrative orientation should show the same identity under some other γ, or show that they lack it.
