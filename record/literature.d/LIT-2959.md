# LIT-2959: Concepts Whisper: Spectral Anti-Concentration and the Dual Geometry of Transformer Representations

Pratyush Acharya, Nuraj Rimal & Habish Dhakal (2026). EMNLP 2026 (main); arXiv 2605.01609.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A cites it at A41 for concept information in low-variance spectral directions. Read the same day ([NOTE-2251](../notes.d/NOTE-2251.md)) and filed as [THEORY-2159](../theory.d/THEORY-2159.md). Its spectrum is the unembedding's, the logit metric Xu uses ([LIT-2967](LIT-2967.md)), not representation variance ([ARG-139](../arguments.d/ARG-139.md)). It supports [CLAIM-835](../claims.d/CLAIM-835.md) weakly at most. Its sign flip between activation-side and vocabulary-side vectors is predicted by Park, Choe and Veitch's map ([LIT-304](LIT-304.md); [CLAIM-852](../claims.d/CLAIM-852.md), [ARG-155](../arguments.d/ARG-155.md)).
