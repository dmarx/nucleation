# TERM-003: will, as a second- or higher-order attractor

## Definition

A second- (or higher-) order attractor: a stable regime of a system's dynamics
that governs which lower-order regimes become operative. The owner, U34:
"I'm not convinced that 'second (or higher) order attractor' isn't a perfectly
viable operational definition of 'will'."

## Why it was replaced

It made the permissiveness objection ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)) bite: every control
system has such an attractor. The owner's answer was to accept that
([CLAIM-062](../claims.d/CLAIM-062.md)); the paper's eventual answer was to index the concept to an
independently warranted agent, and to speak of the *organization* of efficacy
rather than of an attractor ([TERM-023](TERM-023.md)). By A225 the attractor reading had
become "one possible mathematical realization", not the definition.
