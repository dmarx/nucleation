**What the readings corrected in the filings of a few hours earlier.**

- **Kamenica & Gentzkow do not give [QUESTION-071](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) an invisible influence.**
  The filing said nothing in the persuaded receiver's present reasoning
  shows the steering, only its history. The paper says the receiver knows
  the signal and why it was chosen. The optimum also shows in her present
  beliefs. What the paper gives [QUESTION-071](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) is influence that is overt and
  still effective. The paper never cites Blackwell, and the filing's phrase
  "preferring a garbling of full information" was empty. Under the
  commitment assumption every signal is such a garbling.
- **Di Paolo is not [CLAIM-075](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)'s rival.** The filing called his paper the
  route from viability to norms that [CLAIM-075](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) must face. He argues that
  viability alone yields no norm the system can use, which is [CLAIM-075](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)'s
  own point made more strongly. His norms are for the system only. So Enoch's
  objection to constitutivism ([LIT-117](../../record/literature.d/LIT-117.md)) does not apply as the filing
  implied. His adaptivity is closer to an operative regime than to
  [CLAIM-025](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)'s transformation capacity.
- **Gallego & Orús does not bear on [QUESTION-026](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-026.md) or [CLAIM-098](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-098.md).** It computes
  syntactic probability only, with meaning set aside. Its many-to-one step
  stays inside one sentence. It keeps the word "renormalization" without
  rescaling, fixed points or flow. Its reader recommends setting it Rejected
  here, with the anthology's holding ([ANTH-LIT-695](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-695.md)) as the reason. That is
  for the owner to decide. The LIT stays Active, and its standing says why it
  barely bears.

**What the readings confirmed or sharpened.**

- **Fiat/bona fide crosses produced/imposed ([CLAIM-194](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-194.md)).** The membrane is
  bona fide and produced, the beam bona fide and imposed. On Smith & Varzi's
  terms every combined form of separate bodies is fiat. So the distinction
  cannot pick between answers to [QUESTION-035](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-035.md). A fiat boundary is still not
  arbitrary: the material "enables" and "constrains" where it can be drawn.
- **Self-production has a definition but no test ([CLAIM-194](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-194.md)).** Weber &
  Varela define the boundary a system makes for itself. They give no test for
  it. They restrict autopoiesis to the cell, which makes the counter stronger
  than it states.
- **Lewis's "almost one" fits [CLAIM-164](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-164.md) only in part.** It fits perturbed
  copies of one gap-isolated subspace, where the gap gives the threshold
  Lewis leaves open. It does not fit vectors inside a degenerate cluster.
  For those, his supervaluation suggests "indeterminate reference" rather
  than [CLAIM-164](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-164.md)'s "names nothing".
