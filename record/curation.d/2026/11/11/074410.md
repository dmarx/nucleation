## What the case adds, and what it does not

The case separates two readings of the question's "whose process now
maintains it": a host's routine that carries a mechanism, and an installed
component that maintains itself by running inside that routine, hidden
from the host. The story is of the second kind, so it does not reach an
organization that reproduces a mechanism it can see. The reading is the
assistant's, offered in the notes and not filed as a claim.
