# QUESTION-tmpds4rq: Does the potential account of valence need the will-organization's other relations, accessibility, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation, beside the priority, persistence and resumption it reads off which side wins, and does a system's valence change when one of them changes and the record of wins does not?

## Why it is a question

Will-organization ([TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)) is defined by six relations: accessibility,
precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation.
[CLAIM-057](../claims.d/CLAIM-057.md) shows precedence, jurisdiction and suspension vary
independently, and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) holds that which regime becomes effective is
not reducible to the contents of the competing regimes. The potential
account of valence ([TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md), [TERM-078](../terms.d/TERM-078.md)) uses three marks, priority,
persistence and resumption, and reads all three off the record of which
side won each contested pair. Each of the other relations bears on valence
in a way that record may not show.

- **Jurisdiction** decides which conflicts are contested at all: which of
  a perspective's demands count as applicable to the case. The
  nonvacuity condition (valence undefined where no pair is contested) is
  therefore relative to jurisdiction, and so is every share of contested
  wins. Two organizations with the same wins and different jurisdictions
  have different contested sets.
- **Suspension** disables an applicable perspective without its losing a
  contest. Mindfulness works this way on [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md)'s account, and
  [CLAIM-tmp0jfuz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0jfuz.md) reads it as changing the system's valence while the
  perspectives' claims stay fixed. A suspended perspective's demand may
  never appear in the record as a lost contest.
- **Accessibility** fixes the options X_t over which the ordering is read.
  Narrowing it can make a record acyclic that would cycle on the full set,
  or leave nothing contested.
- **Transformation** changes the perspectives themselves. A reorganization
  that merges two perspectives removes their conflicts, which on the
  account changes intensity and can make valence undefined, without any
  ordering having prevailed.

## What would count as an answer

For each relation, either an argument that its effect on valence shows up
in the record of wins after all, or a case in which it changes the
system's valence (or the definedness of valence) with the wins fixed, and
a statement of how the measurement procedure of [QUESTION-268](QUESTION-268.md) must record
it.

## What would not answer it

A redefinition of valence that folds the relation into "who won" by
stipulation, which is the reduction [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) denies.
