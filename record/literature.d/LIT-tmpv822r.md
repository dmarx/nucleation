summary: >-
  Jacobs, Budzinski, Muller, Ba and Keller (2025), arXiv 2502.06034.
  Recurrent networks with only local encoders and local recurrence learn
  to propagate travelling waves that bring whole-shape information to each
  pixel; reading each neuron's activity over time, rather than its last
  state, lets them segment images that need global context, beating feed-
  forward CNNs of matched receptive field and, on Multi-MNIST, U-Nets of
  similar size. The motivation is Kac's "hearing the shape of a drum", and
  a wave RNN reproduces square drums' fundamental frequencies; even an
  unbiased convolutional LSTM learns waves. Here because A15 of the
  exchange "Topological protection explained" listed it beside Keller et

  LIT-647 mentions it. The role of the waves rests on comparisons and a
