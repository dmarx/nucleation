# CLAIM-534: A plural will's motivational integration, a dimension of freedom of will the record names and leaves unanalysed, is the extent to which the demands it maintains are jointly met, within tolerances it can recognize, across the contexts where they interact, and kept so under its own dynamics; it is neither agreement among the demands nor the dominance of one, and its parts come apart: realizability (the tolerance frontier), realization (governance frustration), maintenance (viability) and the allocation of the tolerance each demand gives up

## The claim

[CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md) lists motivational integration among the dimensions of freedom of
will and gives it no content. This claim gives it one. A will made of several
demands is integrated to the extent that those demands are co-satisfied:

- **within tolerances**, because demands are threshold demands ([TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)), met
  within a bound and not at a point;
- **that it can recognize**, because a tolerance it cannot detect it cannot
  keep ([NOTE-1222](../notes.d/NOTE-1222.md), Thms 6.1–6.2; [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md));
- **across the contexts where they interact**, because compatibility is a
  gluing question, and pairwise compatibility does not give joint
  compatibility ([CASE-119](../cases.d/CASE-119.md); [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md)'s cycle criterion);
- **kept so under its own dynamics**, because a jointly satisfiable point that
  the dynamics leave is not maintained integration (A27's ẋ = 1 + u, |u| ≤ ½;
  [CLAIM-079](CLAIM-079.md), stability does not entail viability).

Four things then come apart, any of which can hold while another fails:

- **realizability**, whether the frontier of [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md) admits an
  allocation, [CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)'s complex;
- **realization**, whether the organization actually meets it, the
  governance frustration [CLAIM-357](CLAIM-357.md) separates from structural frustration;
- **maintenance**, whether it stays met under the dynamics, viability
  ([THEORY-134](../theory.d/THEORY-134.md));
- **allocation**, where on the frontier the mismatch falls, which overlap
  gives up how much, a choice [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md) shows is real and [CLAIM-361](CLAIM-361.md)
  asks who may make.

Integration is then neither agreement, all demands the same, nor dominance,
one demand silencing the rest. Suppression that removes a demand from what is
evaluated produces apparent integration and is excluded, by [CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)'s relief
as enlargement for the same demands and by [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md)'s omission clause.

## What it does not say

- **That integration is good.** Neither the demands nor the allocation are
  shown warranted ([CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md)).
- **That integration is the whole of freedom.** It is one of [CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md)'s
  dimensions. [CLAIM-545](CLAIM-545.md) places it among the relations an agent governs.
- **That experience tracks it.** [QUESTION-255](../questions.d/QUESTION-255.md) asks whether unified experience
  survives conflicting demands; this account is about co-satisfaction, not
  experience.

## Note of 2026-11-04: refined legibility as relief, the transition graph, and an uncited restatement, from "Organizational Nucleation and SLT"

**Refined legibility as relief.** A12 §5, after the owner's U12: a safety
process that cannot tell dangerous from harmless exploration vetoes both.
Learning the distinction makes it "more capable of expressing its
commitment discriminately. The new legibility makes a new endorsement
relation possible." A13 §5: "It is not simply increasing tolerance. It is
learning *what should be tolerated and why*." In this claim's terms, a
finer distinction the organization can recognize (TERM-064) can enlarge
the frontier at unchanged demands. By CLAIM-347 that is relief, not a
widened tolerance. It is TERM-099's self-formation, a new distinction made
legible, applied to integration.

**A transition graph on satisfaction signatures (A23 §4).** Each
effective-will state g gets the signature S(g) = {i : g ∈ T_i}. An edge
S → T exists when the dynamics permit the move "under a specified context,
time bound, and resource budget". "This third structure can be
dramatically different for organizations with identical compatibility
complexes." "The complex describes possible accommodation. The transition
structure describes **reconfigurability of accommodation**." A: "I think
the real payoff is the transition structure between feasible
combinations." It is a candidate way to operationalize a part this claim
does not list. CLAIM-354's "governance reconfigurability", CLAIM-025,
CLAIM-099 and QUESTION-054 hold the idea. Not filed.

**An uncited restatement (A26).** A26 boxed compatibility ≠ reachability ≠
viability ≠ reconfigurability, and wrote: "**Third, compatibility is not
realization.** The existence of a mutually acceptable arrangement does not
mean the organization can discover it, choose it, reach it from its present
state, or maintain it." That is this claim's realizability, realization and
maintenance, with CLAIM-354's reconfigurability added. A cited neither
claim. A26 leaves out allocation.

No status changes.
