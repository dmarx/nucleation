The owner asked how the record covers stability theory: generalized,
variational, dynamic and thermodynamic. A survey of titles, keywords and
summaries found:

- **Ecological and resilience stability:** good and mostly read (Holling,
  [LIT-741](../../record/literature.d/LIT-741.md); Scheffer; [THEORY-132](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-132.md), [THEORY-136](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-136.md)).
- **Viability:** held (Aubin's notes, [LIT-714](../../record/literature.d/LIT-714.md), read; the monograph,
  [LIT-738](../../record/literature.d/LIT-738.md), unread; [THEORY-134](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-134.md)).
- **Variational principles:** held as two debates read critically, the free
  energy principle and maximum or minimum entropy production.
- **Thermodynamic stability:** held but largely unread. The Brussels books,
  Glansdorff & Prigogine ([LIT-537](../../record/literature.d/LIT-537.md)) and Nicolis & Prigogine ([LIT-524](../../record/literature.d/LIT-524.md)), are
  Deferred.
- **Generalized stability theory**, in Farrell and Ioannou's sense: nothing.
  The foundations of dynamical stability were thin too.

The owner asked for four works to fill the gaps that matter most to the
line, and for a reading of [THEORY-134](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-134.md) against the persistence claim.

## What entered

- **Farrell & Ioannou 1996, "Generalized Stability Theory", Parts I and
  II** ([LIT-tmpnkkua](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpnkkua.md), [LIT-tmp0yq7w](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0yq7w.md)). The founding statement of the
  non-modal view, for autonomous and then time-dependent operators. Part I
  is free from the publisher.
- **Trefethen, Trefethen, Reddy & Driscoll 1993, "Hydrodynamic Stability
  Without Eigenvalues"** ([LIT-tmpv21m0](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpv21m0.md)). The short, widely read case: shear
  flows judged stable by their eigenvalues turn turbulent through transient
  amplification.
- **May 1972, "Will a Large Complex System be Stable?"** ([LIT-tmp2aj0y](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp2aj0y.md)). A
  size threshold for the stability of large random systems.

All four were checked against Crossref and are Deferred and unread. The
anthology holds none of them.

## What they bear on

- **Growth rate ([CLAIM-186](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-186.md)).** The GST works are grounds, and a note draws
  the consequence. An argument that settles in the end can still show a
  burst of drift after a disturbance, and directed dependencies are a
  standard source of non-normality.
- **Capture ([CLAIM-179](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-179.md)).** May is a ground, and a note adds a possible size
  limit on settling, beyond unused anchors.
- **Persistence ([CLAIM-185](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-185.md)).** [THEORY-134](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-134.md) was added as a ground, with a
  note. Persistence is relative to a constraint set fixed independently of
  the dynamics, and for an argument the record's grammar is that set. A
  matching note on [CLAIM-181](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-181.md) records that the counter and the persistence
  claim meet there.

## Gaps noted and not filled

- Lyapunov's 1892 memoir, structural stability (Andronov–Pontryagin,
  Smale), Thom's catastrophe theory, and a standard dynamics text
  (Strogatz; Guckenheimer & Holmes).
- Classical extremum principles.
- Gibbs's stability conditions, Callen's text, and Keizer & Fox's critique
  of the Glansdorff–Prigogine criterion. The record holds Landauer's
  objection ([LIT-532](../../record/literature.d/LIT-532.md)) but not that one.
