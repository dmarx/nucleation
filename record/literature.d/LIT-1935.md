# LIT-1935: Can models of evolutionary transition clarify the debates over the Neolithic Revolution?

Ayelet Shavit, Gonen Sharon (2023). "Can models of evolutionary transition clarify the debates over the Neolithic Revolution?" *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B* 378(1872): 20210413. DOI-10.1098/rstb.2021.0413.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading (NOTE-tmpots55).* The 3Cs, Michod's fitness decoupling and ecological scaffolding are used together as a heuristic (§1(a)). The coordination stage is assigned to the Early and Middle Epipalaeolithic, cooperation to the Natufian, whose varied artefacts show low group-level inheritance, and collaboration to the PPNA and PPNB, with community "signatures" and group fitness decoupled "at least for some crucial traits" (§1(c)). Once group traits "can no longer" exist separately, farming is entrenched and the "point of no return" has passed. The authors "will not examine to what degree and in what respects the Neolithic Revolution fits the ETI criteria" (§1).

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-01 from the re-reading of [LIT-188](LIT-188.md) ([NOTE-173](../notes.d/NOTE-173.md)). [LIT-188](LIT-188.md)'s status note names it as the kind of worked case that could settle that paper: one that tells collection, conexus and community apart by measurable criteria. Its abstract promises a lens, not such criteria, so it may not. A reading should check whether it scores any Natufian or Neolithic site against the 3Cs. The reading of 2026-11-22 found that it does not: the stages are assigned to periods by fit with a published synthesis, with no measure (NOTE-tmpots55).
