# LEAD-220: Build one model that hosts the four mechanisms of the governance leads together, evidence from outside the proposals, protection of that evidence from revision and refusal, a return channel from evaluated to evaluator, and scoring apart from agreement, and test whether each is a separate requirement for self-correction or whether one entails the others; add a miscalibrated protected anchor and a slower survey of it

## The design

Start from [LEAD-190](LEAD-190.md)'s population on the coupled meaning–influence model,
which already has grants, critics and factions. Add four switches:

1. a block of tasks on which some process holds evidence that does not
   come from the proposals ([LEAD-180](LEAD-180.md)'s condition);
2. whether that evidence can be rewritten or refused by the revision
   ([LEAD-177](LEAD-177.md)'s);
3. a return channel from the evaluated party's standing to the
   evaluator's resources, with a strength ([LEAD-015](LEAD-015.md)'s);
4. whether critics are scored by observed relevance or by agreement
   ([LEAD-190](LEAD-190.md)'s).

Score correction on ground truth the population never sees, and on the
honest minority's standing, as [LEAD-180](LEAD-180.md) does.

## What would settle it

- If each switch, turned off alone, defeats correction while the others
  hold, they are separate requirements, as [CLAIM-1024](../claims.d/CLAIM-1024.md) read strictly
  predicts.
- If turning one off is harmless whenever another holds, the principle
  narrows to fewer conditions.
- Also, a miscalibrated protected anchor with and without a slower
  survey, to test [CLAIM-1185](../claims.d/CLAIM-1185.md) in a second model.

## Note of 2026-11-25: from the review of the manuscript governance-of-becoming, Part II

Article 6's join J6.3 read [CASE-346](../cases.d/CASE-346.md) as showing two of [CLAIM-1024](../claims.d/CLAIM-1024.md)'s
conditions coming apart: protected evidence held while authority stayed
coupled to apparent success. An independent reader found it misstated the
case. [CASE-346](../cases.d/CASE-346.md) ran the independent-reinforcement intervention in the
success-and-restricted run, which has no inspection floor, and two of its
results point the other way: with independent evaluation every run leaves
protection, restricted runs included, which suggests [CLAIM-1024](../claims.d/CLAIM-1024.md)'s
conditions narrowing rather than separating; and at the lower hazard rate
(h = 0.002) the inspection floor alone ends protection in 99.5% of runs.
Which part of the model counts as which condition is a free choice. This
lead, which would test the conditions separately, should fix that mapping
in advance. The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session).
