# THEORY-830: In human visual cortex the content of a sustained seen image is carried by a stable population code that lasts as long as the image, not by a sustained level of activity, and frontoparietal cortex marks only its onset; so a sustained experience rests on sensory representation and its discrete updates on frontoparietal representation

## Source

Vishne, Gerber, Knight and Deouell (2023), [LIT-644](../literature.d/LIT-644.md), "Distinct ventral
stream and prefrontal cortex representational dynamics during sustained
conscious visual perception", Cell Reports 42: 112752, read in full in
[NOTE-499](../notes.d/NOTE-499.md). The general reading is the authors' Discussion and Conclusion
([NOTE-499](../notes.d/NOTE-499.md) claims C4, a conditional interpretation, and C5, a proposal).

## What is claimed, and by whom

The authors read their result as a general division of labour: the
content of what is seen while an image stays on lives in a stable
pattern across ventral visual cortex, whose position in an "experience
subspace" fixes the content while its overall amplitude falls, and
frontoparietal cortex registers the content only when it changes. [NOTE-499](../notes.d/NOTE-499.md)
names the same claim as a candidate THEORY and notes that one source is
not enough.

## What the narrowed entry establishes toward it

[THEORY-102](THEORY-102.md) holds what was measured: in subdural high-frequency activity
from ten epilepsy patients viewing clearly visible images for 300 to
1,500 ms, ventral temporal category and exemplar information stayed
decodable by a time-invariant code for as long as the image was on while
amplitude fell about 80%, and prefrontal and parietal decoding was a
burst about 150 to 600 ms after onset that did not track duration. That
is one method, one band, sparse and mostly right-hemisphere frontal
coverage, and only seen images.

## Evidence for and against so far

COGITATE ([LIT-973](../literature.d/LIT-973.md), read in [NOTE-812](../notes.d/NOTE-812.md)), an independent intracranial cohort
with 576 prefrontal electrodes, finds a sustained posterior category
representation and prefrontal category content confined to onset, as
claimed. It does not hold for every dimension: face identity was not
sustained in posterior cortex, and face orientation decayed after about
0.5 s while the image stayed on. So "the content" is so far supported for
category, mixed for identity, and untested where percept and input come
apart.

## What would confirm or reject it

The promote_when, carried over from [THEORY-102](THEORY-102.md) unchanged: a second
recording of the same dissociation by another method or cohort, and a
paradigm where percept and input come apart (binocular rivalry, or a
sustained image that is present but unseen) with the ventral code
following the percept. It is refuted if that code follows the input when
the percept changes, if an unseen sustained image is coded the same way,
or if a sustained content-specific prefrontal code turns up in bands or
sites the study did not sample.

## What this does not say

- **Anything about the theories' formal content.** The study tested the
  COGITATE paradigm predictions attributed to IIT and the global
  workspace, not integrated information or a workspace mechanism.
- **That "representation" is causal.** The authors define it as a pattern
  correlating with stimulus descriptors.
