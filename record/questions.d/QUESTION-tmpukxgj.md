# QUESTION-tmpukxgj: Does an independent study using randomly sized windfalls, in another lottery, state or period, find that large winners' lower rate of bankruptcy in the first two years or so after winning is offset by a higher rate later, leaving no detectable difference over about five years, and that large winners who file have net assets and unsecured debt like small winners who file?

## Why it is a question

THEORY-1490 reports Hankins, Hoekstra and Skiba's Florida lottery study,
read in the working draft. The later rise is significant only for large
winners and only at the margin, and the balance-sheet comparison rests on
47 against 103 filers.

## What would answer it

A study of randomly sized windfalls elsewhere finding the same
postponement and the same balance sheets at filing would answer yes; a
lasting fall in bankruptcy would answer no.
