# ARG-194: Ghadimi Atigh and colleagues' cone-and-prototype supervision at any granularity, read as an existing instance, supports the semantic half of progressive learning as two partial orders, and nothing of its informational half

## The inference

[CLAIM-839](../claims.d/CLAIM-839.md) holds that progressive learning can be described by two partial
orders, semantic entailment among the regions a representation assigns
and informational refinement among its checkpoints, and that a narrower
region counts as progress only while it stays correct. Ground:
[THEORY-2192](../theory.d/THEORY-2192.md).

## Critical questions (existence)

**Is the instance real, and is it of the kind claimed?** Yes, for the
semantic order. The paper's rule is "the more specific a sample's label,
the more narrowly defined the region in the embedding space should be for
optimization" (§1): a coarse label confines the embedding to its node's
entailment cone and spreads the target over its leaves (§3.2), and the
cones nest down the tree. Its framing, that "images are optimized to the
correct region", fits the claim's rule that narrowing counts only while
correct: accuracy is measured at held-out leaves, siblings and cousins,
not by cone occupancy.

**Does it reach the other half?** No. There are no checkpoints compared,
no garbling, no branching order, no U_i(t + Δt) ⊆ U_i(t). The prototypes
are fixed before training. §3.2's "Coarse-to-fine" names the projection
of a label to a boundary direction, not an order of acquisition.

**Does an instance show the description is useful?** Weakly: it shows the
semantic order can be imposed as a training target and pays off when most
labels are coarse (56.13% against 20.22% leaf accuracy at ρ = 0.95 on
CIFAR-100). It does not show learning discovers such an order.

## Verdict

Supports the semantic half weakly; nothing on the informational half or
on the thesis that learning branches. The reading moves from [CLAIM-839](../claims.d/CLAIM-839.md)'s
`grounds` to `corroborates` on [THEORY-2192](../theory.d/THEORY-2192.md).
