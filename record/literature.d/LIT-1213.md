# LIT-1213: Knowledge and common knowledge in a distributed environment

Halpern, J. Y. & Moses, Y. (1990). Knowledge and common knowledge in a distributed environment. *Journal of the ACM* 37(3): 549–587. doi:10.1145/79147.79161. First in *Proceedings of the Third Annual ACM Symposium on Principles of Distributed Computing (PODC '84)*, pp. 50–61, doi:10.1145/800222.806735.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmpi6s63), from the authors' arXiv
deposit (arXiv:cs/0006009).

## Why this is here

A36 of the exchange used the coordinated-attack result, without a source, for the epistemic obstruction it separated from feasibility and aggregation. This is the paper that proves common knowledge unattainable over unreliable channels. Bears on [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md).
