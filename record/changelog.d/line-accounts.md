### Added

- ADR-tmpyihxy (Proposed): a line whose account the record holds only in parts gets an unadmitted SYNTHESIS that restates it, giving the account an address. A line account adds nothing, has no admission route and cannot be set Current. `docs/current-account.md` lists these accounts last, under "Accounts held in parts".
- SYNTHESIS-tmpllsy7, the demonstration, is the nucleating-argument line's account. It maps all 74 of the line's in-force entries, plus four cross-line entries its components cite. It covers five parts: identity drift; what an argument settles on; the costs of anchoring; what a record keeps and what that buys; and the record as its own apparatus. It also lists seven integrations the record leaves open.

### Changed

- The `synthesis-review` skill files or revises a line account when an opportunity fails admission only because the record already holds it. Readers' briefs now state the tests neutrally.
- The current-account template cites ADR-075 by number.
