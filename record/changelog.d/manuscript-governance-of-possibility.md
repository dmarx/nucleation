### Added

- A protocol for writing a manuscript from the record ([ADR-tmpbdfmk](record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpbdfmk.md), Proposed) and the `write-manuscript` skill, with eight invariants: a manuscript is a view outside the record; every step cites its entry with standing; nothing in it is new to the record, its joins filed as accounts (the owner's ruling); voices stay separate; a motivating draft is audited first; results are executed; gaps go back to the record; every substantive version is checked, and relevant uncited readings are cited. The steps were revised on the record of how the first manuscript was made (pull requests 555 to 623, [RETRO-006](record/retrospectives.d/RETRO-006.md) to [RETRO-008](record/retrospectives.d/RETRO-008.md)), at the owner's question. CLAUDE.md names the skill.
