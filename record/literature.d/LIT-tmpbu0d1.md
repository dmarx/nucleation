# LIT-tmpbu0d1: The Unabomber's Ethics

Ole Martin Moen (2019). The Unabomber's Ethics. *Bioethics* 33(2), 223–229. DOI-10.1111/bioe.12494. Online 2018-08-23.

## Key takeaways

- Kaczynski "assumes an ethical theory according to which the benefits of technology have little or no real value" (§3).
- Control of technological progress faces "both an epistemic problem and a coordination problem" (§3).
