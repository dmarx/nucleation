## What "the skim is enough" turned out to mean

The low tier was rated low because its seeds said the skim captured the work.
**In most cases the skim was not enough.** Full readings corrected a seed claim
in nearly every one:

- **Local separators ([LIT-068](../../record/literature.d/LIT-068.md)) is `Rejected`.** Its critique of modularity
  is factually wrong by its own formula, and its method is never defined.
- **Rosenblatt mean-field games ([LIT-050](../../record/literature.d/LIT-050.md)) is `Rejected`.** Its headline, that
  fBM surrogates give suboptimal control, is false for the linear-quadratic
  problems it solves. Its "super-diffusion transformer" has no transformer.
- **The Broadview Six transcript ([LIT-065](../../record/literature.d/LIT-065.md)).** The 42 pages the seed thought
  were lost were only redacted in one filing and filed whole in another. The
  reading covers all 77 pages, and the caption is *United States v. Rabbitt
  et al.*
- **The Mothership Vortex ([LIT-060](../../record/literature.d/LIT-060.md)).** Its headline numbers cannot be
  reproduced from its own replication repository.
- **Causal claims in economics ([LIT-079](../../record/literature.d/LIT-079.md)).** Between versions, its validation
  F1 against the same benchmark fell from 0.99 to about 0.12.
- **The Airbnb paper ([LIT-094](../../record/literature.d/LIT-094.md)).** Its abstract's "more than 2% misused" is
  0.16% in its body.
