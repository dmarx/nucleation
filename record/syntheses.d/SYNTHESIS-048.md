# SYNTHESIS-048: From interconnection to individuation the record holds distinct achievements in a partial order: interconnection makes effective constraints that gluing only expresses, coupling separates time scales only where it dissipates differences, an individuation is well defined once its criterion is fixed and its senses need different evidence, where maintenance is realized is shown only by interventions on production relative to a declared boundary, and assembling, persisting, regenerating and being supplied come apart, while how distinctions are made and unmade, and when relations constitute an entity, stay open

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the part a mechanical
community map of the line's stored relations and mentions calls
individuation, 71 entries. The line's account is built over it and the
accounts of its sibling parts. Fourteen of its entries carry another line
first (realizability, transient-support, will-organization,
formal-apparatus, pragmatic-transport), and some are integrated again by
those lines' accounts: [SYNTHESIS-032](SYNTHESIS-032.md) ([CASE-225](../cases.d/CASE-225.md), [CLAIM-597](../claims.d/CLAIM-597.md), [CLAIM-604](../claims.d/CLAIM-604.md),
[CLAIM-606](../claims.d/CLAIM-606.md), [QUESTION-399](../questions.d/QUESTION-399.md)), [SYNTHESIS-041](SYNTHESIS-041.md) ([CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md), [CLAIM-610](../claims.d/CLAIM-610.md)),

## The account

- **The partial order.** [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) (the record's, at the owner's request
  of 2026-10-27, interpretive, Active) holds that from interconnection to
  individuation the relationships are distinct achievements: "none implies
  the next, and a later one need not require every earlier one". Its table
  sets interconnection, compatibility, dynamics, individuation (with
  organizational autonomy as the further question) and inquiry against the
  claims that hold each, and its list of failed implications gives each
  step holding while the next fails. Its note of 2026-10-31 records the
  owner's rule of method: "derive the dependency structure of the
  achievements separately from the order in which an inquiry met them".
  [CASE-179](../cases.d/CASE-179.md) (a proposed study from A22 §10, A23 §XIII and the owner's
  review, Active) is its central experiment: coupling, correspondence maps
  and apparatus revision varied independently, so that "The transitions
  [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) says are distinct can be made to come apart on purpose".
  [TERM-088](../terms.d/TERM-088.md) (A18 and A23 with the owner's review, Active) names the senses
  of individuation, descriptive, dynamical, constitutive and organizational
  autonomy: "The senses are not stages, and not degrees of existence", and
  a pattern sustained wholly from outside is "individuation without
  autonomy". [CLAIM-515](../claims.d/CLAIM-515.md) (the owner's principle, interpretive, Active) asks
  of any cognitive property four questions, recoverable, exercisable,
  realized and maintained, that "can come apart", and lists [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) among
  its instances.
- **Interconnection, compatibility and correspondence.** [CLAIM-471](../claims.d/CLAIM-471.md) (the
  owner's U20, restated at the owner's review, interpretive, Active)
  separates physical constraint formation, correspondence discovery and
  correspondence adaptation: "Gluing, in every case, is the statement that
  local descriptions are compatible under specified relations. It neither
  generates the relations nor certifies them." [TERM-093](../terms.d/TERM-093.md) (the record's,
  Active) names the correspondence structure and the three ways it is held,
  and "Only formation bears on the thesis that interaction generates
  constraints." [CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md) (A22 and A23, demonstrated, Active): eliminating
  an equilibrated interior leaves a coupling no part has, a slow interior
  leaves a memory kernel, and "separation of time scales decides only
  whether that constraint is approximately memoryless"; for cellular
  sheaves the reduced constraint can be three-way, so "composition followed
  by reduction can force a change in the type of relational structure an
  adequate description needs". [TERM-090](../terms.d/TERM-090.md) (the record's, Active) names that
  effective boundary constraint on two axes, memory and kind, and says it
  marks an interface, not an individual. [CLAIM-495](../claims.d/CLAIM-495.md) (from A2 and A3 of
  "Paper assessment", proof the record's, demonstrated, Active):
  "compatibility among local frames can be partial and graded by
  direction", and "non-commutativity is not holonomy". [CLAIM-510](../claims.d/CLAIM-510.md) (the
  record's, Proposed) keeps the stronger form [CLAIM-471](../claims.d/CLAIM-471.md) dropped, that
  measurement can establish a correspondence, with no held case.
  [QUESTION-282](../questions.d/QUESTION-282.md) (the owner's review, Open) asks when interaction changes the
  operative correspondence structure itself; [QUESTION-283](../questions.d/QUESTION-283.md) (the owner's
  review, Open) when composition forces a richer relational vocabulary,
  and [QUESTION-390](../questions.d/QUESTION-390.md) (A's, Open, first line realizability) asks it of
  origami. [QUESTION-319](../questions.d/QUESTION-319.md) (A2 §5 of "Paper assessment", Open) asks whether
  holonomy among an organization's parts is an error or a maintained
  feature.
- **Coupling and time scales.** [CLAIM-473](../claims.d/CLAIM-473.md) (the owner's U20, Rejected) said
  coupling necessarily separates time scales. [CLAIM-481](../claims.d/CLAIM-481.md) (A20, A21 and A23,
  a counter, demonstrated, Active) refutes it: conservatively coupled
  oscillators split into normal modes and neither decays, "So coupling
  does not necessarily induce scale separation, and [CLAIM-473](../claims.d/CLAIM-473.md) is false as
  stated", while dissipative coupling and a Laplacian's spectrum give the
  separation. [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md) (the owner's U12 with A12, interpretive, Proposed)
  holds that separation makes structure, "what is a realization at one
  scale works as a constraint at another", and that organization makes new
  separations near an instability. [CLAIM-394](../claims.d/CLAIM-394.md) (the record's derivation,
  demonstrated, Active) gives an exact case, the reversible linear Gaussian
  VAR(1), where "any span of slow features is an exactly dynamically
  independent coarse-graining". [CLAIM-395](../claims.d/CLAIM-395.md) (the record's proof,
  demonstrated, Active): a closed set of constraints has at least two
  members and, under two readings, a slow and a fast dependence, "so a
  system with a single constraint of its own cannot realise closure".
- **Slowing, and present strength against maintenance.** [CLAIM-521](../claims.d/CLAIM-521.md) (A12
  §6, demonstrated, Active): in [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md)'s second construction the
  maintenance's recovery rate goes to zero at its fold while the coupling's
  gap stays near 0.989; "The coupled units are as tightly integrated at the
  edge of collapse as well inside the regime." [CLAIM-626](../claims.d/CLAIM-626.md) (A's, A20 §4,
  demonstrated, Proposed) sorts critical slowing three ways, collective,
  transverse and maintenance, and "So an observed slow mode does not say,
  by itself, whether organization is forming, a reduction is failing, or a
  maintained capacity is failing." [QUESTION-125](../questions.d/QUESTION-125.md) (A6 §2, taken up by the
  owner, Open) asks whether a block's identifiable boundary and a
  bifurcation coincide; its note of 2026-11-07 places [CLAIM-626](../claims.d/CLAIM-626.md)'s locking
  arm and [CLAIM-521](../claims.d/CLAIM-521.md) in two of its three regions, and "The third region, a
  gap closing with the regime and its behaviour intact, still has no
  case." [CASE-227](../cases.d/CASE-227.md) (A's A29, Proposed) couples the Haken–Kelso–Bunz phase
  to a decaying capacity, and [ARG-068](../arguments.d/ARG-068.md) (the assistant's wording, Rejected)
  rejects A's inference that it instances [CLAIM-521](../claims.d/CLAIM-521.md): "The case fits the
  threshold and not the fold."
- **Relations, relata and the criterion.** [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) (the owner's U8,
  sharpened by A8, interpretive, Proposed): what an entity is, is partly
  fixed by the constraints on the configurations it can enter, and those
  constraints are realized and maintained by something, so "neither relata
  nor relations, nor specifications nor realizations, are prior without
  qualification"; it adds that "Maintained by something" is not
  "maintained by itself". [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md) (A8 and A9 on the owner's U8,
  interpretive, Active): an individuation is well defined once its
  criterion is fixed, and then "a verdict can be false", and units picked
  out by different criteria can cross. [CLAIM-526](../claims.d/CLAIM-526.md) (the record's,
  interpretive, Active) answers the Simondonian readers: coexisting levels
  remove the question of agents adding up, not the question of levels, and
  "some of them are no". [CLAIM-529](../claims.d/CLAIM-529.md) (the record's, interpretive, Active)
  sorts Griesemer and Shavit's criteria into those on interactions, which
  test a declared candidate, and those on members' attitudes, which
  individuate by a different relation: "So endogenization cannot draw the
  boundary first. It can test one that has been declared." [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)
  (A5, A6 and A10, Open) asks when constraints only characterize an entity
  and when they partly constitute it, and whether a detected, a maintained
  and a constituted boundary differ; its note of 2026-11-07 finds three
  relations under the word and asks that "An answer should say which of the
  three it is about." [QUESTION-030](../questions.d/QUESTION-030.md) (A191, Open, first line
  pragmatic-transport) is its question for communicative acts.
  [QUESTION-280](../questions.d/QUESTION-280.md) (Jaksland's, as A6 gave it, Open) asks whether separability
  is approximate independence: "Neither the paper nor the claim says whether
  separable enough is approximately independent."
- **Where maintenance is realized.** [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) (A10 §4, demonstrated,
  Active): an outside controller supplying the production law as state
  feedback gives the same trajectory under every intervention on state,
  "Full observation of c changes nothing", and only interventions on the
  production mechanisms, relative to a declared boundary, separate the two.
  [ARG-050](../arguments.d/ARG-050.md) (the record's, contrast, Active) answers the fat-handedness doubt:
  the pathway causes the capacities with a delay, so the knockout is
  surgical, and "So intervention data locate a producer relative to a
  boundary and do not fix the boundary." [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md) (A9 to A12's
  constructions, Active) is the pair's model: reciprocal production holding
  a coupling, and a transient encounter forming a maintained connector.
  [CLAIM-528](../claims.d/CLAIM-528.md) (the record's, interpretive, Active) splits "realized" into
  produced through and consisting in, of which interventions show only the
  first, and places the line between them at the description's time
  resolution: "This is [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md)'s criterion-relativity applied to the
  causation–constitution line." [CLAIM-530](../claims.d/CLAIM-530.md) (the record's, interpretive,
  Active): producer and controller meet mutual manipulability's two
  manipulability conditions alike, so the boundary "is the input
  constitutive relevance itself needs". [CLAIM-194](../claims.d/CLAIM-194.md) (the record's, a counter,
  interpretive, Active): a gap or coupling criterion passes a cell's
  membrane and a beam alike, and "The criterion has no term for who made
  the coupling." [QUESTION-086](../questions.d/QUESTION-086.md) (A11's Q4, Open) asks by what intervention
  matched systems are told apart; its note of 2026-11-01: "If the question
  has an answer that does not presuppose a boundary, it will not come from
  interlevel experiments alone." [CLAIM-456](../claims.d/CLAIM-456.md) (the record's, from [CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md),
  demonstrated, Active): internal maintenance can be the more fragile, so
  "self-production is not robustness". [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) (A33 §7 sharpened by the
  reader, interpretive, Active, first line will-organization) defines
  self-formation and its two tests, the second resting on [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md), and
  finds that after the fact "whether a self-maintained capacity was
  self-formed is a question about its history".
- **Cycles and closure.** [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md) (Montévil and Mossio's, adopted by the
  owner, Active) is closure of constraints. [CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md) (A10, demonstrated,
  Active): under turnover with no basal production an acyclic production
  graph goes extinct, so a persisting capacity needs a productive cycle
  among its ancestors, and "The theorem's necessary condition is weaker
  than closure." [CLAIM-531](../claims.d/CLAIM-531.md) (the record's, interpretive, Active): generative
  entrenchment can be acyclic, "So entrenchment plus a knockout effect does
  not give closure", and by [CLAIM-516](../claims.d/CLAIM-516.md) read for interactions an acyclic
  order persists only on outside supply.
- **Persistence, regeneration and supply.** [CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md) (A's, interpretive,
  Active, first line realizability): assembling, persisting over a
  specified horizon and regenerating after turnover have separate
