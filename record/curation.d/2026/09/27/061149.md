The owner asked for a reading of arXiv 2103.09780. It is Carroll, *Reality as
a Vector in Hilbert Space*, which the record already held as [LIT-123](../../record/literature.d/LIT-123.md), read
in full the day before ([NOTE-134](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-134.md), `Active`). A second reading would have
added nothing. What had changed was the record around it: van Rijsbergen,
the two DisCoCat papers and the Riesz/GNS kernel thread were all filed after
[NOTE-134](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-134.md) was written. On the owner's go-ahead, this contribution does two
things.

**[NOTE-134](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-134.md) gains a connections paragraph (version 3).** The reading itself is
unchanged. Its "Bearing on the record" line saying no THEORY depended on the
paper is updated.

**[THEORY-tmpscxh5](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpscxh5.md) is new, `Proposed`.** It says that in a Hilbert-space model
only unitarily invariant structure is intrinsic, and that the record's
Hilbert-space works differ in where they get a basis or factorisation. There
are four answers:

- **Carroll** takes it from the dynamics.
- **Van Rijsbergen** takes it from the observable.
- **DisCoCat 2010** needs none; its composition is orthogonally covariant.
- **Kartsaklis et al. 2014** stipulates a corpus basis.

[THEORY-004](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-004.md) is the same point for learned representations, so the new document
`extends` it. Two inferences are marked as the document's own, not the
sources':

- **Contextuality needs more than one basis**, so a single-stipulated-basis
  model such as [LIT-272](../../record/literature.d/LIT-272.md)'s cannot be contextual.
- **Distributive, Boolean concept structure is evidence that a basis was
  chosen.**

**Why `Proposed`.** The piece that would turn "basis", "Frobenius algebra"
and "classical data" from analogies into one notion is Coecke, Pavlovic &
Vicary's theorem. That theorem is cited by [LIT-272](../../record/literature.d/LIT-272.md) and not held here. So the
`promote_when` asks for that paper to be filed and read, and for a check that
each placement in the table rests on something measured or proved, not
inferred. It also names what would refute the account.

**Checked while writing.** A first draft said [LIT-272](../../record/literature.d/LIT-272.md)'s basis-dependent model
"scores as well as its invariant rivals". It doesn't have invariant rivals in
that sense. Kron and Multp are elementwise too, so basis-dependent, and the
only rotation-invariant baseline, additive, scores lowest (ρ = 0.050 against
0.163–0.172). The caveat now says so. The pattern is worth noting: on that one
task, the basis-dependent models lead.

**Candidate follow-up.** Filing Coecke, Pavlovic & Vicary (arXiv 0810.0812)
would let this THEORY be promoted. It would also give [LIT-272](../../record/literature.d/LIT-272.md)'s
Frobenius-algebra claims ([NOTE-246](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-246.md), C3) a source in the record rather than a
citation.
