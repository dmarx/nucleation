# QUESTION-tmpeiurf: Do entrusted authority, or control over the essential conditions of another's participation, ground obligations to those affected in their own right, beyond those their vulnerability, interests and rights ground?

## Why it is a question

[QUESTION-460](QUESTION-460.md) asks on what ground an organized whole, or those who
maintain or control it, owes its constituents anything: "that the parts
constitute it, contribute to it, are vulnerable to it or are governed by
it, its commitments to them, or its history with them". The manuscript's
Article 8 sorts A51's six candidate grounds against the record. Two have
no claim of their own:

- **Entrusted authority.** [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md) lists "Assumed or entrusted
  authority", "The actor exercises a role whose powers exist for
  particular purposes", as a ground of *answerability*: an answer owed to
  "Those to whom the role's duties are owed". Whether the role's purposes
  also ground obligations of conduct, beyond owing an account of it, is
  not held.
- **Control over essential conditions.** [CLAIM-739](../claims.d/CLAIM-739.md)'s "Consequential
  dependency" ("The actor controls essential conditions of another's
  participation and alternatives") is again a ground of answerability.
  [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) makes such control a condition of presumptive domination, and
  [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) holds that control over exit, renegotiation and the
  conditions of supply sorts on whom a strengthened responsibility falls.
  None holds the control itself as what grounds the obligation.

The other grounds are held otherwise: membership in the alternatives
[CLAIM-959](../claims.d/CLAIM-959.md) and [CLAIM-960](../claims.d/CLAIM-960.md), vulnerability in [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md) (supposed, "under an
independently justified principle"), induced reliance in Article 3's
entries.

## What would count as an answer

- **Yes, for either**: an argument that holding entrusted authority, or
  controlling essential conditions, grounds an obligation that a party
  equally vulnerable to an agent without the role or the control is not
  owed. A matched pair in the manner of [CASE-256](../cases.d/CASE-256.md), holding vulnerability,
  interests and rights fixed and varying only the role or the control,
  would test it.
- **No**: a showing that whatever the role or the control adds is
  answerability or a locating of the bearer, and that the obligation
  itself is grounded in the party's vulnerability, interests and rights,
  as [CLAIM-960](../claims.d/CLAIM-960.md) holds for bare constitution.
- **Dissolved**: a showing that the distinction between grounding an
  obligation and grounding answerability for it does no work here.

[LEAD-tmphsmng](../leads.d/LEAD-tmphsmng.md)'s readings (fair play, associative obligation) bear on it:
fair play grounds obligation in a scheme's benefits, not in a role or in
control.
