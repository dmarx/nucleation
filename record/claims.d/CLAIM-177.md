# CLAIM-177: An argument developed with collaborators who keep no memory fails at identity, not at structure: concepts are redefined silently, objections and readings come back as though new, and the thesis changes without anyone arguing for the change

## The claim

[ADR-032](../decisions.d/ADR-032.md) drew the lesson from the will exchange: "What went wrong was
identity, not missing structure." In that exchange, "will" was redefined
five or more times. One worked case changed identity six times. The
permissiveness objection was re-derived six or more times, and readings
the record already held were recommended three times.

The translation exchange shows the same pattern, and it was found without
looking for it.

- The passive/active distinction ([CLAIM-004](CLAIM-004.md)) "kept being re-derived after the
  manuscript dropped it". The ordinary-control objection ([CLAIM-024](CLAIM-024.md)) was
  re-derived three times, with different criteria each time.
- The thesis changed four times, and the first two changes were never
  announced. [CLAIM-017](CLAIM-017.md) became [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), and then [CLAIM-133](CLAIM-133.md), without a turn
  arguing either move. Only at A203 was the change of question stated
  ([CLAIM-139](CLAIM-139.md)).
- When the owner asked what to file (U48), the assistant offered "three
  genuinely new connections". Only one of them, TV Tropes, was new to the
  record. The proposed Yoneda THEORY duplicated [THEORY-032](../theory.d/THEORY-032.md), and the
  relativity analogy had been rejected as [ARG-004](../arguments.d/ARG-004.md) (workbench
  2026/10/10/040456).

None of these is a missing premise. In each case something already said
lost its identity between turns.

## What it does not say

- It does not say structure is never missing. In the final will manuscript,
  7 of about 30 claims had more than one independent line of support.
- It does not blame the assistant. A collaborator with no memory between
  sessions, or whose context has been compacted, can only re-derive. The
  owner reintroduced the relativity analogy after it had been rejected
  ([ARG-004](../arguments.d/ARG-004.md)).
- Two exchanges, both owner with chat assistant, are a pattern, not a
  measurement.
