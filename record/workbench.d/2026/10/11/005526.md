**What moved.**

- **Extended self and entrainment** (distributed-agency, [CLAIM-172](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-172.md)).
  Mutual entrainment individuates a coupled system by its collective
  variable. That sidesteps the coupling-constitution objection, which
  forbids inferring parthood in the organism. It excludes one-way drive
  (the nerve and the muscle, the metronome). The objection [CLAIM-165](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-165.md),
  two dancers in phase who stay two selves, is open: the claim bounds a
  system and has no criterion for when that system is a self.
- **Two routes to extension.** Entrainment is synchronic. Otto's notebook
  does not keep time with Otto. It is coupled by a write and a later read.
  So the owner's two prompts meet: the storage variant ([CASE-043](../../record/cases.d/CASE-043.md)),
  with the writer as their own future reader, is the diachronic route.
- **Storage** (pragmatic-transport). The tree ([CASE-049](../../record/cases.d/CASE-049.md)) is the
  baseline with no sender. The variant adds a sender and a projected
  receiver and removes only the actual one. The owner's claim then
  separates two times: intended meaning is fixed at the write, relative to
  the writer's frame ([TERM-030](../../record/terms.d/TERM-030.md)), and fidelity is measured at each read
  ([CLAIM-115](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-115.md), [CLAIM-074](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-074.md)). This fills a gap the line had: production versus
  reception was never decided. The anticipated objection, [CLAIM-171](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-171.md),
  is that intention is just one more reading. It is open.
- **The joke** ([CASE-048](../../record/cases.d/CASE-048.md)) is filed as a test that cuts both ways. The
  dual-address joke was designed to be missed, which supports a meaning
  fixed at the write. The adult's experience that the scene became a
  different joke supports the objection. [CLAIM-074](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-074.md)'s states of adaptation
  of one interpreter is where the line already had room for it.

**Bearing on the morning's claims.** Entrainment supplies, for a person and
an external process, the coupling and order parameter that [CLAIM-163](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-163.md)
said intrapersonal "synchronization" lacks. It does not supply them for
interests over time. That objection stands.
