## Why this is here

A8 put it beside the historical cases "as an important competing explanation of strategic institutional behavior": a self-reinforcing loop is not the same explanation as the deliberate use of legal procedure to weaken accountability. [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) is stated so that it does not choose between them.
