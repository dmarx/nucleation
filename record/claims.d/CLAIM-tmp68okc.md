# CLAIM-tmp68okc: In the transmission criterion of identity over time, an outside maintainer's organization is part of what is transmitted just insofar as it holds organization the repertoire depends on that the individual does not carry and that a competent unrelated replacement, working from the individual, would not supply; a garage holds none of a car's, so a change of garage continues the car whole, while an unrelated new maintainer can continue a project only in the respects its code carries

**Reading (b), an alternative to [CLAIM-tmp5koor](CLAIM-tmp5koor.md).** Filed for [LEAD-163](../leads.d/LEAD-163.md).
The assistant leans to this one.

## The slot

As [CLAIM-tmp5koor](CLAIM-tmp5koor.md) sets it out. [CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md)'s admission case moves from
"nothing of the first workshop's maintaining organization transmitted" to
its `defeated_if`'s "nothing of that organization was transmitted". The
synthesis review of 2026-11-11 found the case realistic, a car that
changes garages. Left open, the car defeats the claim.

## The claim

Two steps.

**Shared with reading (a): restoration from the thing is transmission
through it.** A new garage repairs the car to the arrangement it finds in
the car. The restored relations are caused by the earlier ones, through
the car, so they are not "an organization of the same kind that arises
independently" ([CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)). [ARG-tmplptzc](../arguments.d/ARG-tmplptzc.md) carries this, by analogy from
[CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s newcomers, who take a collective's organization from the
collective they join.

**This reading's own step: which outside organization is transmitted.**
[CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) holds that the organization which persists "may lie inside the
individual or outside it", and was admitted in the reading that it is a
particular organization, not a role. An outside maintainer's organization
is part of that particular organization just insofar as three things hold
of it:

1. the repertoire depends on it;
2. the individual does not carry it, so it cannot be read off the
   individual;
3. a competent replacement of the same kind, with no tie to the
   maintainer and working from the individual alone, would not supply it.

What fails the test is a role, filled by whoever fills it. What passes is
the maintainer's particular contribution.

Consequences:

- **The admission case is not a defeater.** Everything a garage needs to
  restore a car is in the car or in the type's manual, and any competent
  garage supplies the rest. The garage fills a role. Nothing of the car's
  particular organization lay in it, so its replacement breaks nothing.
- **Some replacements part continuities.** A software project's
  maintainers hold conventions, plans, review judgement and knowledge of
  why the code is as it is. Much of it is not in the code, and an
  unrelated competent team would not supply it. When such a team takes the
  code, the project continues in what the code carries and not in what the
  maintainers carried. That is [CLAIM-328](CLAIM-328.md)'s plural succession, with one
  successor, partial. It is what the owner's study ([CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md)) would
  measure where participants say "it's not the same thing".
- **Participants' verdicts can track a parting.** [CLAIM-617](CLAIM-617.md) holds them a
  cause of succession, and [CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md) holds them not the criterion. Both
  stand. On this reading they can also be evidence that something
  the individual did not carry was lost.
- **A failure case for admission remains,** as in reading (a): a replica
  built from the type with no tie to the token, counted the same by a
  stipulated continuation morphism.
- **[CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md)'s own admission case is read the same way.** An outside
  controller that supplies the internal production law ([CLAIM-522](CLAIM-522.md))
  breaks continuity only if that law was not taken from the system.

## What it does not say

- **That substitutability is all or nothing.** A maintainer can hold some
  of what the repertoire depends on. Then the succession parts in that
  respect, not wholly.
- **That a maintainer is part of the individual's boundary.** What is
  transmitted can lie outside the boundary ([CLAIM-152](CLAIM-152.md), [CLAIM-280](CLAIM-280.md)). The
  question here is only what continuation requires.

## Where it differs from reading (a)

On a project whose code passes whole to an unrelated team. Reading (a)
says it continues, changed, and that participants' verdicts are causes.
Reading (b) says it continues only in the code's respects, and that the
verdicts can track that. [CASE-223](../cases.d/CASE-223.md), if run, would bear on which reading
fits the cases. Reading (a) keeps the criterion simpler and reads
[CLAIM-615](CLAIM-615.md)'s layer 4 by its words, "the transmitting organization".
Reading (b) keeps [CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md)'s words, "may lie inside the individual or
outside it", and pays for it with a counterfactual test. The assistant
leans to (b), because it revises no premise.

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), the claim can fail while its premises hold. [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md),
[CLAIM-611](CLAIM-611.md) and [CLAIM-328](CLAIM-328.md) hold if non-substitutable organization held by a
maintainer is lost and the project is still counted the same in every
respect, this claim's first defeater.
