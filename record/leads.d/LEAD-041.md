# LEAD-041: Specify a procedure that reads from a record of conduct which ordering prevails, how strongly and whether it resumes, separating ambivalence from neutrality; and argue that the model's two channels are perspectives

## What it would do

Write the argument, then the procedure, and apply it to one record of
conduct.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md): "A procedure that, applied to a record of conduct, returns
  which ranking prevails and how strongly"
- [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md): "Two things are owed: an argument that the model's two
  channels are perspectives in this sense"

## What would count

A procedure that separates ambivalence from neutrality on a case answers
[QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md) and supplies what [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) owes.
