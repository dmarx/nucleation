# SYNTHESIS-002: The wrong beyond violation that an organization commits by reproducing representations and allocations of authority that keep a justified standard from constraining it is a breach of a second-order duty of answerability: to maintain feasible means for relevant violations to become knowable, contestable and correctable; it is a verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible alternative

*Moved from [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), where its development up to version 4 is recorded. Admitted as an independent hypothesis ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)).*

## The claim

A answered the owner's U27 ([CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md), [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md)) in the
second share of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c). The
version filed is A62 §13's "revised central formulation":

> **Reflexive counter-normativity is the reproduction by an organization of relationships that obstruct the effective application of justified normative constraints to its own activity.**

> Its pathology is therefore not exhausted by the harms committed, the errors made, or the objections disregarded. It includes the maintenance of a disposition to prevent relevant harms from becoming effective reasons for organizational transformation.

> The normative verdict requires independently justified obligations and attention to feasibility, responsibility, and competing claims. Self-maintenance, representational selectivity, and procedural stability alone do not establish wrongdoing.

The layers, each with its codes and voice:

1. **Occlusion** (the record's). The organization's projection merges
   circumstances the standard distinguishes: ∃w₀, w₁ with h_r(w₀) = h_r(w₁)
   and N(w₀) ≠ N(w₁) (A62 §3). This is [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) and [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md)'s fibre
   criterion, with [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md) (6/6 against 10/2) as the case, and [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s
   directed legibility ("invisibility must be specified *to whom*", A60
   §4). That the records "need not be fraudulent" (A60 §1) is [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md)'s
   own point.
2. **Self-immunization**, the reproduction (the record's form). A60 §3's
   dynamics, with the regime moved by its own outputs, are [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md)'s
   schematic dynamics and [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md)'s protective lock-in, with [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md)'s
   maintained legibility state. A62 §3B's "Self-maintenance requires more
   than stability in observed measurements" is [CLAIM-566](../claims.d/CLAIM-566.md) and [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md).
   The whole loop is documented in [THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md) and [CASE-138](../cases.d/CASE-138.md), Challenger to
   Columbia, reforms undone included.
3. **Ineffectiveness** (the owner's and the record's). Claims under the
   standard are not recognized, or cannot move action, or cannot be acted
   on: [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s three achievements and [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s rungs. That an
   organization can grow in effective action while its correctability by
   others degrades is [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md); A62 §9 restates it.
4. **The second-order duty** (A's). A60 §6: "**Second-order norm:**
   Maintain adequate, reasonably feasible means for relevant violations to
   become knowable, contestable, and correctable." It is conditional: "That
   duty is not an unconditional command to maximize transparency or
   investigate every conceivable harm." Answerability is meant in
   [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md)'s sense, to "remain answerable for those actions through other
   institutions", not [CLAIM-016](../claims.d/CLAIM-016.md)'s ownership of a will.
5. **The criterion** (A's). A62 §3: an organization is "normatively
   self-immunizing relative to a justified standard N" when:
   1. Its activity violates or materially threatens \(N\).
   2. Its operative representation or governance systematically impairs the recognition or corrective efficacy of relevant claims under \(N\).
   3. The organization reproduces the mechanisms responsible for that impairment.
   4. The impairment materially contributes to continued nonanswerability or to the persistence or risk of violation.
   5. The omission or obstruction is itself unjustified given the organization's obligations, capabilities, and reasonable alternatives.

   "The fifth condition is what turns a causal diagnosis into a normative
   verdict."
6. **Non-reduction** (A's). A62 §3 defines Δ_occlusion as the difference
   in expected normative loss under do(r) for the current and an
   alternative regime, then: "a second-order wrong need not always be
   reducible to this loss difference". This is what keeps the wrong
   distinct from the harm.
7. **The relational wrong** (A's, after the record). A62 §7: "a
   deprivation of normative standing within a relationship of governance".
   Its home on the record is [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)'s domination clause, another's
   capacity to govern the relations of one's governability. "Normative
   standing" here is standing to place claims on an organization that
   governs one, not [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md)'s moral standing.
8. **Grades** (A's). A60 §8 orders cases from unavoidable limitation
   through negligent, self-maintaining and self-exculpating occlusion to
   "Strategic normative self-insulation". The mechanism "does not require
   deliberate deception", which [CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md) ("no continuing prejudiced act")
   and [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md) ("no fabrication of threat required") already hold.

The second-order illegibility of A60 §9 and A62 §6, an organization that
corrects within a representation and not the representation itself, is
held twice: [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)'s note "Organized ignorance" and [THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md)
(Argyris's single-loop learning). The response of an organization to
challenge as such is A's companion claim [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md).

## Three failures, kept apart (A's review, item 4)

A's review of the pull request dmarx/nucleation#422 asks that A60's
"self-certifying ignorance" be "an explicit part of the mechanism". The
layers above run three failures together, and they can come apart:

1. **Normative occlusion.** A: "The representation excludes a relevant
   distinction." This is layer 1: h_r(w₀) = h_r(w₁) while N(w₀) ≠ N(w₁),
   so "The projection cannot preserve a distinction required by the
   normative standard."
2. **Self-certifying occlusion.** A: "The organization treats the
   resulting absence of recognized violations as evidence of its own
   adequacy." The failure is inferential, not representational: from no
   recorded violations to no violations. "That implication is invalid
   when the recording mechanism cannot reliably identify the relevant
   violations." A60 had put it so: "The absence of recorded harm is now
   being treated as evidence of the absence of harm, even though the
   reporting process was incapable of registering it."
3. **Reproductive self-immunization.** A: "That apparent adequacy helps
   sustain the representation and governance relations responsible for
   the failure." This is layer 2, [CLAIM-445](../claims.d/CLAIM-445.md)'s form, now with its
   input named: what the regime is rewarded on is the absence that tier 2
   misreads.

**The connection to [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md).** Tier 2 is the case [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md) names. What
keeps a judgement–observation loop from confirming itself is "evidence
whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the interpretation it
tests", and the records of an occluding projection are scored by the very
projection whose adequacy is in question. Their silence is therefore no
evidence of adequacy, and an organization that reads it as evidence is the
loop that "selects its own evidence". A60 drew the link itself: [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)
"explains why self-confirming judgment–observation loops need evidential
constraints whose admission or scoring does not pass exclusively through
the interpretation being tested". [CLAIM-446](../claims.d/CLAIM-446.md) is held nearby.

Each tier can hold without the next (the reader's qualification). An organization can occlude without
certifying itself, if it does not read its records' silence as evidence;
and it can certify itself without reproduction, if the certification
moves nothing in its governance. A: "The most interesting organization is
one that uses the apparent absence of violations to reward and reproduce
the reporting mechanism responsible for that absence." That organization
has all three, and it is the one this claim is about. The wording of the
tiers is A's; adopting it is the owner's call.

## What it does not say

- That the standard is established ([QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md), [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md)).
- That the organization is an agent, or each member culpable ([CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md),
  [QUESTION-068](../questions.d/QUESTION-068.md)). A62 §8 keeps reproduction, control, agency and
  responsibility apart; external maintainers are [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)'s outside
  controller, whose maintenance provenance ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)) lies outside the
  declared boundary.
- That corrigibility is owed unconditionally ([CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md), disclaimed);
  condition 5 bounds the duty, and [CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md) makes keeping a corrective
  capacity conditional on cost.
- That self-maintenance establishes wrongdoing ([CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md), [CLAIM-472](../claims.d/CLAIM-472.md)):
  A60's "self-maintenance does not itself establish wrongdoing".

## What would establish it

A62 §11 gives seven levels of evidence, from a demonstrated representational
defect to an intervention on the reproductive mechanism, with "the decisive
experiment" intervening on representation, authority and reproduction
separately. The intervention that separates internal from external
maintenance of the blindness is on the production mechanism, not the state
([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)); A60 §11's "Change the reproductive feedback" is that.

**The matched-harm construction** (A's review, item 3). [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md) holds
the first-order harm and its remedy fixed between two institutions under a
stipulated duty to maintain independent review, and varies whether the
duty was kept and whether its breach is reproduced. A: "If the claim can
survive a case in which the first-order harms are held constant, its
proposed non-reducibility becomes much easier to evaluate." The argument
from it to layer 6 is [ARG-055](../arguments.d/ARG-055.md). It is a construction on a stipulated
duty: "The construction cannot establish that every organization has such
a duty." The wording is A's; adopting it is the owner's call.

## Versions

A60 §12, the "record-grade formulation", in four paragraphs: "That wrong
occurs when the organization reproduces a legibility regime that
systematically merges, excludes, or discredits the distinctions needed to
identify the violations". A60 §13: "**normative self-immunization** when the
organization uses its own restricted measures to certify its continued
acceptability". A62's opening sets out three concepts, occlusion,
self-immunization and reflexive counter-normativity; A62 §3 gives the
criterion; A62 §13 is the version filed. A62 §12 positions the contribution
against the literature and is descriptive, not filed. Distinct from the
five synthesis claims filed from the first share ([SYNTHESIS-004](SYNTHESIS-004.md),
[SYNTHESIS-003](SYNTHESIS-003.md), [SYNTHESIS-001](SYNTHESIS-001.md), [SYNTHESIS-005](SYNTHESIS-005.md), and the since-withdrawn dispositional reframing), none of which
is normative; [SYNTHESIS-005](SYNTHESIS-005.md)'s last clause is this claim's descriptive base.

## Admission

Under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test, the integration must be able to fail while every component holds. Two cases show it can.

- **Condition 5 idle.** Take a projection that is unavoidable but reproduced. If the record must count its reproduction a wrong, the feasibility bound fails, though every component holds. Whether it must is open (below).
- **Not answerability.** Take a case meeting all five conditions in which the wrong beyond the harm lies wholly in another's capacity to govern those affected, [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md)'s domination clause, and persists unchanged once means of knowledge, contest and correction are restored.

The claim is admitted on the narrower title. Its non-reduction to the harm is [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md)'s thesis and [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md)'s construction, not this claim's integration.

**Open: unavoidable reproduction.** The owner's formulation does not explicitly settle whether unavoidable reproduction can constitute a further wrong. The proposed feasibility-bounded answerability account excludes such cases; whether that limitation captures the owner's intended counter-normativity remains open. (A's second review of the pull request, which corrected an earlier version of this section that had attributed the opposite position to the owner. That attribution rested on a clause a filer had written into [CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md)'s `defeated_if`.)
