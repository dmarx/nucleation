# CLAIM-007: In a bridge or chain of progressive reconstruction the path itself is observable: which constraints survive early, which are introduced late, and which relations emerge only through refinement

## The claim

A97 §3: a Schrödinger bridge Q* = argmin KL(Q ‖ P) with fixed endpoints treats
translation as "a path through a space of progressively reconstructed
representations. **The path itself becomes observable.** We can investigate
which constraints survive early transformations, which are introduced later, and
whether particular communicative relations emerge only through successive
refinements."

## Where it went

Manuscript §7: "Schrödinger bridges offer a complementary path-space viewpoint
... Such constructions can model guided stochastic transport and conditional
simulation (Shi et al., 2022)." Nothing is observed along the path. It is the
diffusion-side counterpart of the pragmatic phase change ([CLAIM-114](CLAIM-114.md)). It
is not the same as historical drift ([CLAIM-071](CLAIM-071.md)).

The crystallized argument's §17 gave the rationale its last form: "the bridge
perspective is useful because intermediate states may matter. A generative
process can gradually establish scene geometry, objects, attributes, and
relationships. We can investigate the trajectory by which a relational
configuration emerges or fails." The manuscript keeps the bridge and the
disanalogy of times, and drops the reason, so bridges enter §7 with no stated
purpose.
