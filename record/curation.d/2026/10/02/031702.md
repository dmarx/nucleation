The owner asked for three things in a row: file the seven texts the
converted-texts collection had left "unidentified"; say how the record
stood on identity, personhood and selfhood; then file the literature that
answer exposed and add a word for it. Twenty-four works came in, and the
topic vocabulary gained two words and renamed a third.

## How the record stood on the self

`identity` carried 39 works about three different things: the identity and
individuation of things (structural realism, quasi-sets, the Yoneda lemma),
biological individuality, and personal identity and the self. A reader
looking for the self met quasi-set theory first. No THEORY touched the
self, and the personal-identity literature the record's cognitive-science
and AI readings answer to was almost entirely missing: no Locke, Hume,
Parfit, Williams, Lewis, Olson, Schechtman, Strawson, Gallagher, Zahavi or
Metzinger.

## The vocabulary

- **`self-and-personhood`** ([ADR-015](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-015.md)) holds what a self or a person is and
  what makes one persist. Fifteen existing works took it, and every self
  filing above leads with it.
- **`identity` became `individuation`** ([ADR-015](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-015.md), at the owner's
  suggestion). With the self moved out, what was left was the
  individuation of things, and "identity" read alone invites the personal
  sense. The 41 bearers changed in place.
- **`phenomenology`** ([ADR-016](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-016.md)) is for the first-person description of
  experience as a method. A filing agent proposed it for Zahavi, and the
  owner accepted. Six works carry it, none first.

## For the next person

Three agents filed the self literature into one checkout at once, each
running only `luria new`. A `luria link --fix` run by the integrator spells
targets into their in-progress files too; that is harmless, since it only
spells links. Integrating each batch in a separate worktree at HEAD, with
`repair`, `link --fix`, `index` and `lint` run there, kept every commit
linted without waiting for the slowest agent.
