# TERM-030: frame, as a configuration of the communicative situation

## Definition

A38 §2.3 restored the tuple f = (S, A, K, N, G): speaker position, audience,
common ground, operative norms, genre. A frame "must be more than an analyst's
interpretive label", and changing "the *description* of an utterance" differs
from changing "the *communicative situation*". A39 §3.1 folded the utterance and
the interaction history in, c = (u, s, a, k, n, g, h), and §3.2 defined "a
pragmatic frame as a configuration of relevant social and interpretive
conditions". It extends A24's measurement context ([TERM-031](TERM-031.md)) with norms and
history, and is no longer an index on judgements only.

## What it is not

Not an act on a reader: that is a framing intervention ([TERM-018](TERM-018.md)). A39: "A friend
speaking is a property of a communicative situation."
