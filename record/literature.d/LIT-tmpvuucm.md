# LIT-tmpvuucm: Brain of the Firm

Stafford Beer (1972), *Brain of the Firm*, London: Allen Lane; 2nd edn
(1981), Chichester: John Wiley — <https://openlibrary.org/books/OL5330074M>

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What the record holds of it comes from Beer's 1984
retrospective ([LIT-tmpzk76q](LIT-tmpzk76q.md)). There Beer says the book was a streamlined
version of his set-theoretic model, written in neurophysiological terms
instead of mathematics, and that some readers wrongly took it as an
analogy. Five chapters on the 1971–73 application in Allende's Chile were
added to the second edition. He refers readers to it, with *The Heart of
Enterprise*, for the functions of the five subsystems that the
retrospective does not expound.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-05 at the owner's request. The 1984 paper extends it by
adding the methodology and the pathology to the model it restates. The
