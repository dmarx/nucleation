# CLAIM-tmpbucep: Shareholder value maximization is a kind of willful ignorance with respect to the legitimate standing of a corporation's non-shareholder relationships

## The claim

The owner, at U30: "shareholder value maximization as a kind of willful
ignorance wrt legitimate standing of non-shareholder relationships".

The owner's word is "ignorance", and its object is standing ([TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md)),
not information. A corporation run for shareholder value may know a great
deal about its workers, customers, suppliers and communities; what the
thesis says it does not admit is that their claims count for their own
sake.

## A48's reply

A48 agreed and sharpened: "non-shareholders can become extensively legible
to the corporation without their legitimate claims acquiring independent
normative authority over it", "not necessarily ignorance of relevant
facts, but the systematic exclusion of their independent normative
significance" ([CLAIM-tmpa61rf](CLAIM-tmpa61rf.md)). It qualified the owner's adjective: "Calling
this *willful ignorance* is strongest when there is evidence that the
institution knowingly maintains this exclusion or avoids inquiry that
could challenge it. Mere ignorance, genuine normative disagreement and
deliberate avoidance should remain distinguishable." And it kept
shareholder value as "itself a legitimate corporate concern. The problem
under investigation is the elevation of that concern into an exhaustive
criterion of corporate purpose and practical authority."

## How it stands to the record

- **The wrong in standing.** [CLAIM-1155](CLAIM-1155.md): reproducing the projections that
  keep claims from registering wrongs those with standing to contest, "and
  it needs neither an intending actor nor their ignorance of the harm".
  The owner's thesis is a case of it if the exclusion is reproduced.
- **Wilful.** [CLAIM-714](CLAIM-714.md): an incapacity the party dismantled in breach of a
  duty does not excuse it. [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md): the duty to keep violations knowable,
  contestable and correctable.
- **The counterweight.** [CLAIM-749](CLAIM-749.md) (Active): "Influence over others does not
  by itself ground a duty to answer to them: a legitimate right can assign
  a decision to its holder". The thesis needs non-shareholders' claims to be
  of the kind [CLAIM-739](CLAIM-739.md) lists (interference with a protected interest,
  consequential dependence, control over recourse), not mere effects.
- **Corporate character.** Bakan ([LIT-1430](../literature.d/LIT-1430.md), Unavailable) and [QUESTION-265](../questions.d/QUESTION-265.md);
  A48 §10 links the thesis to [CLAIM-tmptrn75](CLAIM-tmptrn75.md).
- **Agnotology.** A48 calls the exclusion "a particularly consequential form
  of agnotology"; the exchange's account of agnotology is filed by another
  group and the link is left to the closing pass.
