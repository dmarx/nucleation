# LIT-tmphjhp1: Agency Is Frame-Dependent

David Abel, André Barreto, Michael Bowling, Will Dabney, Shi Dong, Steven Hansen, Anna Harutyunyan, Khimya Khetarpal, Clare Lyle, Razvan Pascanu, Georgios Piliouras, Doina Precup, Jonathan Richens, Mark Rowland, Tom Schaul and Satinder Singh (2025), *arXiv:2502.04403*.

Filed for [QUESTION-032](../questions.d/QUESTION-032.md): every essential property of agency in Barandiaran's and Moreno's accounts can be measured only relative to a reference frame. That is a direct position on what makes a factor an agent relative to a level and a target, and on the thermostat requirement ([CASE-044](../cases.d/CASE-044.md)).
