# CLAIM-001: The conditional-diffusion literature already covers directed transport between observational scenarios, including changing covers

## The claim

U34, quoting [CLAIM-100](CLAIM-100.md): "I think conditional diffusion literature has us covered
here. we could even use text-to-image generations as another case study in
operationalizing this."

## How it was answered

A97 accepted most of it. Diffusion supplies "*compositional conditioning*", with
composable diffusion's operators "resembling conjunction and negation of
conditions" ([LIT-770](../literature.d/LIT-770.md)), and "diffusion bridges provide a way to study stochastic
transport between distributions" ([LIT-776](../literature.d/LIT-776.md)). It also limited the point: "It isn't
automatically formal sheaf contextuality ... The sheaf formalism lets us state
the stronger compatibility question separately" ([CLAIM-071](CLAIM-071.md)). Nothing in A97 or
later addresses changing covers, which was the part of [CLAIM-100](CLAIM-100.md) the owner quoted.
Proposed: right about the tools, unshown for the question.
