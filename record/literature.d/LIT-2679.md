# LIT-2679: Freedom and Resentment

P. F. Strawson (1962). Freedom and Resentment. *Proceedings of the British Academy* 48: 187–211. Collected in *Freedom and Resentment and Other Essays* (Routledge, 2008), DOI 10.4324/9780203882566.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of 2026-11-17.*

- The reactive attitudes "rest on, and reflect, an expectation of, and
  demand for, the manifestation of a certain degree of goodwill or regard"
  (p. 200).
- Pleas like "He was pushed" "do not invite us to see the agent as other
  than a fully responsible agent"; pleas like "His mind has been
  systematically perverted" "invite us to suspend our ordinary reactive
  attitudes towards the agent" (p. 193).
- Blame's "partial and temporary withdrawal of goodwill" is "the
  consequence of continuing to view him as a member of the moral
  community" (p. 207).
- The framework "neither calls for, nor permits, an external 'rational'
  justification" (p. 208).

## Reading of 2026-11-16

Not read. A7 §4 says "Strawson emphasized ... the reactive attitudes ...
These include resentment, gratitude, indignation, guilt, and forgiveness",
and gives an example in which an accidental and a deliberately humiliating
injury are physically equal but differ morally. Both are faithful to
Strawson as [LIT-691](LIT-691.md) reports him: the contrast between an accidental injury
and one done from "contemptuous disregard" or "a malevolent wish to injure
[you]" is Strawson's own ([1962]1993, 49). A's next step is A's, not
Strawson's: coercive control as "an ongoing denial of the other person's
standing as an independent moral agent". So is the closing's
"Strawson-style" inclination. One thing in Strawson, as reported, bears on
A9 §5's abused-then-cruel adult and is not drawn on by A: the exempting
condition of "peculiarly unfortunate … formative circumstances", which for
Strawson moves us to the objective attitude (an exemption), not to reduced
blame (an excuse). [LIT-691](LIT-691.md)'s distinction between excuse and exemption (due
to Watson 1987) is the record's place for it.
