# NOTE-1693: Cui and Wang — Eigenvalues of the Zakharov–Shabat system

## Contribution

A numerical method for the Zakharov–Shabat eigenvalue problem: Chebyshev
polynomials on nodes placed by a tanh(ax) mapping, turning the problem into
a matrix eigenvalue problem solved by the QR algorithm, with spectral
accuracy and faster convergence than Fourier collocation. Its bearing here
is only the exact result it tests against.

## Key insight

For the focusing NLS equation the number of solitons an initial pulse forms
is read off its scattering spectrum; for a sech pulse that spectrum is known
exactly, and it is discrete only above a minimum amplitude.

## Assumptions

The focusing NLS equation iq_t + q_xx + 2|q|²q = 0 (eq. 1 with λ = 1);
potentials decaying at infinity; for the exact case, q = A sech x with A
real.

## Key results

- **Counting rule** (§1): "The number of solitons emerged in the initial
  profile for the NLS equation is determined by the discrete eigenvalues of
  the Zakharov-Shabat system."
- **Satsuma–Yajima** (§3.1, eq. 12): "Satsuma and Yajima found the discrete
  eigenvalue in upper half complex plane" C+ to be κn = i(A + 1/2 − n),
  "where n is a positive number satisfying n" ≤ A + 1/2 (fraction restored
  from the layout).
- **Worked case** (§3.1): q = 1.8 sech x has κ1 = 1.3i and κ2 = 0.3i in C+,
  so two solitons; the method finds κ1 to an absolute error of
  1.85 × 10⁻¹⁵.
- **Beyond sech** (§3.2): a chirped sech(0.2x) potential with two pure
  imaginary and four complex eigenvalues evolves, in an NLS simulation,
  into four solitons and a second-order breather.

## Concepts

Zakharov–Shabat scattering problem; discrete spectrum as the count of
solitons; Satsuma–Yajima potential; formation threshold in initial
amplitude.

## The formation threshold: the reader's derivation, not the paper's

The paper does not state a threshold. From its quoted formula: an
eigenvalue in the open upper half-plane needs A + 1/2 − n > 0 for some
n ≥ 1, so no soliton forms for A ≤ 1/2, one for 1/2 < A ≤ 3/2, and N for
N − 1/2 < A ≤ N + 1/2. Since ∫ sech x dx = π, the pulse's area is πA and the
threshold area is π/2. The quoted "n ≤ A + 1/2" admits the boundary case
κ = 0, which is not in the open upper half-plane; how Satsuma and Yajima
treat it has not been seen. The arithmetic is the reader's, rechecked by
the filer; it holds for the sech profile only.

## Bearing on the record

- **[QUESTION-398](../questions.d/QUESTION-398.md) (independence from the founding intervention)**: bears. In
  a lossless medium a soliton, once formed, is wholly independent of the
  pulse that launched it, provided the pulse passed the formation
  threshold, which by derivation is A > 1/2 for a sech pulse. Independence
  of the founder is complete only in the lossless limit.
- **[CLAIM-604](../claims.d/CLAIM-604.md)**: a contrast. The only threshold here is in the initial
  data, not in an ongoing supply.

## Limitations

A numerical-methods paper; its physics is quoted, not derived. The threshold
is a derivation from a quoted formula, for one profile. Nothing on
radiation.
