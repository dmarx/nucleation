# NOTE-tmpe9epc: Tomasello — Response to Commentators

## Contribution

Where the philosophers pressed hardest on the book, and how its author
answers.

## Key results

1. **The charge.** The commentators find his account of joint
   intentionality "too generous and too individualistic (neo-Cartesian)"
   (p. 118).
2. **Objectivity.** The objective view is from "anyone who would be one
   of us", so the objective "perspective is in fact a perspectiveless view
   from nowhere" (p. 119).
3. **Seeds.** Joint intentionality is not propositional "but it has the
   seeds of this by joining" individual intentionalities (p. 120).
4. **Admitted gap.** On the assumption of cooperation, "I had no clear
   idea of its cognitive status" (p. 120); enactive alternatives are
   rejected because "they are vague at all the key places" (p. 120).
5. **Minimal shared goals.** Butterfill's proposal: "this is just way
   too thin for joint attention" (p. 122).
6. **With Moll.** "shared intentionality is no ordinary adaptation" (p.
   122).
