# CLAIM-1233: Several conventionally distinct cognitive biases can be observable marks of one organizational constraint, such as a protective regime that governs attention, recall, evidence thresholds and avoidance together, so the catalogue of biases is not a catalogue of mechanisms; the thesis earns standing only if one intervention on such a constraint, measured independently and with the information held fixed, moves several bias measures together

## The claim

A11 §2: "The same observed bias can arise through different mechanisms.
Conversely, one organizational constraint can produce multiple
conventionally distinguished biases. This is an important move away from
treating the familiar catalogue of cognitive biases as a catalogue of
independently individuated psychological mechanisms." Its §3 taxonomy lists
45 named biases in six families (access and sampling, representation,
inference, evaluation and authority, social organization, memory and
reflexive correction), and concludes: "The taxonomy contains over forty
familiar phenomena, but its explanatory units are considerably fewer."

A12 §2 supplies the mechanism that couples them: "affect supplies one
mechanism through which otherwise distinct cognitive processes become
coordinated. A protective regime, for example, might simultaneously exhibit
heightened threat attention, selective recall, asymmetric evidence
thresholds, pessimistic forecasting and avoidance. What psychology describes
as several biases might in this instance be **multiple observable
characteristics of a single affectively organized regime**." It keeps the
claim a candidate: "These are candidate explanations, not reductions of all
these phenomena to emotion. Some instances of the same biases may instead
arise from memory noise, computational shortcuts or environmental
sampling."

Both turns say what would earn it standing. A11 §15: "The primary danger is
constructing an organizational vocabulary capable of redescribing every
known cognitive bias without explaining anything new", and "The strongest
result would be evidence that several independently named biases exhibit a
common, transferable organizational signature, and that interventions on
this signature produce predictable changes across tasks." A12 §15: "Alter
concern-relative significance while holding information fixed | Several
judgment biases change together."

## Admission ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md))

By a discriminating prediction. The components the syntheses integrate say
that a protective regime governs attention and evidence acquisition
together ([CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md)), that authority over a choice is learned ([CLAIM-443](CLAIM-443.md))
and distinct from the precedence claimed ([CLAIM-283](CLAIM-283.md)), and that
significance is relative to a motivating perspective's concerns
([TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md)). None of them says that the named biases of the psychology
literature co-vary, or that they co-vary under an intervention on
significance with the information held fixed. That outcome is left open by
the components and is what the rival unifications predict differently:
Hilbert's model ([LIT-3244](../literature.d/LIT-3244.md)) predicts co-variation of its eight biases
from noise in processing, and resource-rational analysis ([THEORY-359](../theory.d/THEORY-359.md)) from
the cost of computation. The claim goes against its own prediction if the
co-variation appears only under those manipulations.

## Where the record stands

- [CLAIM-446](CLAIM-446.md) is the record's worked instance: governance-maintained anxiety,
  whose avoidance "can make competing hypotheses empirically
  indistinguishable" (A12 §6). [CLAIM-924](CLAIM-924.md) simulates it.
- [CLAIM-427](CLAIM-427.md) (Superseded) grades the evidence for the organizational
  framework: same ordinary behaviour, different response to independently
  specified interventions, and a better model. This claim's test is of the
  second grade.
- [CLAIM-278](CLAIM-278.md) (Superseded by [ARG-036](../arguments.d/ARG-036.md)) says an operator framework "explains
  none of them until it forbids some outcome". The shared constraint has to
  be measured apart from the biases, or the thesis is that redescription.
- [CASE-148](../cases.d/CASE-148.md), the proposed perspective-transplantation study, is the design
  A11 §15 and A12 §15 would extend.

## What it does not say

That every bias, or most, comes from an organizational constraint; that
affect is the only coupling; or that the six families of A11 §3 are
mechanisms. A11 §3 presents its taxonomy as "candidate mechanisms rather
than asserting that the empirical literature has established a single
explanation for each effect".
