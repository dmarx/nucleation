# CLAIM-tmpcd1e0: The atlas as a manuscript of manuscripts

The atlas is a plan of publications. Each scope is a future manuscript with
an audience, a boundary and an outline, and the atlas says how they connect
(its section V). That is what a series is, and the record already holds one
series as a work.

What the form would be:

- a work `atlas`, labelled with A65's title, defined on
  scratch/atlas/atlas-v0.3.md;
- its articles: the existing works its scopes name, and the three of
  [ADR-tmp85hdc](../decisions.d/ADR-tmp85hdc.md), each kept a work of its own, as governance-of-becoming
  keeps governance-of-possibility;
- its members by [ADR-104](../decisions.d/ADR-104.md)'s rule, read against the outlines. "Define inquiry
  in terms of language, answer space, interventions, observations,
  criteria, and resources ([TERM-095](../terms.d/TERM-095.md), [CLAIM-470](CLAIM-470.md))" states [CLAIM-470](CLAIM-470.md)'s
  proposition, so [CLAIM-470](CLAIM-470.md) is a member; a fiber list is a citation.

What it does not settle: whether the atlas's argument is faithful to the
record. That is an account's job ([CLAIM-tmpqhazs](CLAIM-tmpqhazs.md)), and the two forms answer different
questions.
