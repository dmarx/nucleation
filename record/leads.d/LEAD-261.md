# LEAD-261: Classify luria's decisions since Nucleation began by what occasioned each and whether its answer is configuration any record can use or a change only Nucleation needs

## The idea

Read luria's decisions dated on or after 2026-09-25 (22 at luria's checkout
of 2026-11-25, 17 naming Nucleation). For each, code from its Context the
record whose case occasioned it, and from its Decision whether the answer is
a mechanism every record configures or a behaviour one record needs. A
codebook fixed before coding, and a second coder for the second column,
which takes judgement.

## What it would settle

Whether the Nucleation transfer was answered by generalizing luria
([CLAIM-1234](../claims.d/CLAIM-1234.md)) or by fitting it to one record, and so whether the owner's
inference in [CLAIM-1247](../claims.d/CLAIM-1247.md) holds.

## What it needs first

Nothing: luria's record is local. The anthology of the SOTA is a third luria
record; its decisions could be coded the same way.
