# CLAIM-tmpexvc6: A plural will's motivational integration, the dimension of freedom CLAIM-354 names and leaves unanalysed, is the extent to which the demands it maintains are jointly met, within tolerances it can recognize, across the contexts where they interact, and kept so under its own dynamics; it is neither agreement among the demands nor the dominance of one, and its parts come apart: realizability (the tolerance frontier), realization (governance frustration), maintenance (viability) and the allocation of the tolerance each demand gives up

## The claim

CLAIM-354 lists motivational integration among the dimensions of freedom of
will and gives it no content. This claim gives it one. A will made of several
demands is integrated to the extent that those demands are co-satisfied:

- **within tolerances**, because demands are threshold demands (TERM-060), met
  within a bound and not at a point;
- **that it can recognize**, because a tolerance it cannot detect it cannot
  keep (NOTE-1222, Thms 6.1–6.2; TERM-064);
- **across the contexts where they interact**, because compatibility is a
  gluing question, and pairwise compatibility does not give joint
  compatibility (CASE-119; CLAIM-tmplvc8b's cycle criterion);
- **kept so under its own dynamics**, because a jointly satisfiable point that
  the dynamics leave is not maintained integration (A27's ẋ = 1 + u, |u| ≤ ½;
  CLAIM-079, stability does not entail viability).

Four things then come apart, any of which can hold while another fails:

- **realizability**, whether the frontier of CLAIM-tmplvc8b admits an
  allocation, CLAIM-347's complex;
- **realization**, whether the organization actually meets it, the
  governance frustration CLAIM-357 separates from structural frustration;
- **maintenance**, whether it stays met under the dynamics, viability
  (THEORY-134);
- **allocation**, where on the frontier the mismatch falls, which overlap
  gives up how much, a choice CLAIM-tmplvc8b shows is real and CLAIM-361
  asks who may make.

Integration is then neither agreement, all demands the same, nor dominance,
one demand silencing the rest. Suppression that removes a demand from what is
evaluated produces apparent integration and is excluded, by CLAIM-347's relief
as enlargement for the same demands and by CLAIM-tmplvc8b's omission clause.

## What it does not say

- **That integration is good.** Neither the demands nor the allocation are
  shown warranted (CLAIM-523, CLAIM-075).
- **That integration is the whole of freedom.** It is one of CLAIM-354's
  dimensions. CLAIM-tmpxpdqi places it among the relations an agent governs.
- **That experience tracks it.** QUESTION-255 asks whether unified experience
  survives conflicting demands; this account is about co-satisfaction, not
  experience.
