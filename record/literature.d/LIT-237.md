# LIT-237: Bra–ket notation (Wikipedia)

Wikipedia contributors (2026), *Wikipedia* — <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bra%E2%80%93ket_notation>

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp5cyxp](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp5cyxp.md).*

- **Kets and bras.** A ket |v⟩ is a vector of a complex vector space; a
  bra ⟨f| is a linear form on it. With an inner product antilinear in its
  first slot, each ket gives a bra, ⟨φ| = (φ, ·), and on a Hilbert space
  the article cites the Riesz theorem for every bra coming from a ket.
- **Only on Hilbert space.** On a Banach space or a space without
  topology bras and kets can still be written, but "the bracket does not
  have the meaning of an inner product, because the Riesz representation
  theorem does not apply".
- **Toolkit.** Outer products |φ⟩⟨ψ| are rank-one operators and |ψ⟩⟨ψ|
  the projector onto a unit ψ; Hermitian conjugation reverses order and
  conjugates each factor; Σ_i |e_i⟩⟨e_i| = 𝕀 over a complete orthonormal
  system, with the continuous forms ∫|x⟩⟨x|dx = ∫|p⟩⟨p|dp = 𝕀.
- **Improper kets.** Position and momentum eigenkets are not in the
  Hilbert space; the article points to rigged Hilbert spaces and, less
  aptly, to the GNS construction.
- **Date of origin.** The reference the article gives is Dirac (1939), "A
  new notation for quantum mechanics". The lead's "monograph … 1930" is an
  unsourced edit of 2026-09-30.
