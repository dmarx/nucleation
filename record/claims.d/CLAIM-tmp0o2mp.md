# CLAIM-tmp0o2mp: Senses of purpose come apart

## The claim

A17 §1:

| Kind of purpose | What constitutes it |
|---|---|
| Attributed | An observer interprets behaviour as serving an end. |
| Functional | A process contributes to maintaining some organization. |
| Operative | An end effectively governs the organization of activity. |
| Articulated | The organization can represent the end it pursues. |
| Constituted | The organization establishes and maintains commitments concerning that end. |
| Legitimate | There are adequate normative grounds for pursuing that end. |

"A forest can exhibit self-maintaining dynamics without articulating a
purpose. A corporation can exhibit an operative purpose that contradicts
its stated mission. An institution can deliberately constitute a purpose
that is morally objectionable."

A16 §2 adds declared purpose, "What the institution says it exists to
accomplish", and developmental purpose, "What it might come to pursue
through inquiry and reorganization", and puts the weight on the gap
between declared and operative: "An institution may sincerely maintain
its original mission statement while its effective governance
increasingly serves another purpose." So "a platform for governing
purpose cannot simply be a platform for editing mission statements."

## How it stands to the record

- **Operative purpose** is [CLAIM-030](CLAIM-030.md)'s operative intention, "produced by
  systemic mechanisms that can evolve apart from what the members
  intend", and A16 reads it through [CLAIM-482](CLAIM-482.md): an aggregate revealed
  preference, where it exists, "is fitted to one regime of authority", so
  operative purpose "may be better understood through the authority
  relations producing institutional decisions than through a declared or
  inferred objective function alone". A16's citation is apt.
- **Legitimate against viable:** [SYNTHESIS-091](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-091.md), which A17 cites for "the
  viability of an organization does not establish what ought to persist".
- **Functional purpose** without articulation is [THEORY-666](../theory.d/THEORY-666.md)'s intrinsic
  purpose of autopoietic systems.
- **Constituted purpose** is the subject of [CLAIM-tmpvuiy4](CLAIM-tmpvuiy4.md).

## What it does not say

That the list is complete, or that the senses are ordered. A17 §6 offers a
developmental sequence through some of them as "my proposed
developmental decomposition, not an established sequence in the record";
[CLAIM-474](CLAIM-474.md) holds that such achievements form a partial order.
