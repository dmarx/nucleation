The owner asked for arXiv 2512.12249 v2: Ghose, *Measurement as
Sheafification: Context, Logic, and Truth after Quantum Mechanics*. It is
filed as [LIT-276](../../record/literature.d/LIT-276.md), read in full as [NOTE-249](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-249.md), `Rejected`. The
reader read v2 and compared it word by word with v1. No journal version was
found.

**Verdict.** It is an interpretive essay with no theorem, model or
computation of its own. Its correct formal core is Abramsky–Brandenburger's
global-section criterion, which the record holds first-hand as [LIT-016](../../record/literature.d/LIT-016.md) and
[THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md). Each of its three additions is asserted without a construction,
or is wrong as stated:

- **"Measurement is sheafification."** It gives no site, no topology and no
  sheafified presheaf. Sheaf toposes are not Boolean in general (Sh(ℝ) is
  not). And classical probability distributions do not form a sheaf, since
  the marginal problem breaks gluing.
- **The seven-valued Ghose–Patra logic as "a finite Heyting algebra".** It
  gives no order and no operations. The structure its Appendix B implies is
  not a lattice, and completing it gives the Boolean algebra 2³.
- **The σ–λ dynamics.** λ(σ) is never specified, nothing is computed, and
  there is no prediction.

**v2's added Čech obstruction is always zero.** I checked this against the
text when filing. The appendix defines the class as [δs] for a 0-cochain s.
That is the class of a coboundary, so it vanishes for every s, and "[δs] ≠ 0"
can never hold. The paragraph that follows describes Abramsky, Mansfield &
Barbosa's actual construction correctly, but only by citation.

**Beyond its comment, v2 also:**

- deletes v1's introduction, leaving refs [1]–[8] uncited;
- deletes v1's only empirical claim, the micellar-surfactant one;
- adds a quantum-gravity paragraph.

**Bearing.** It supports no THEORY. Its slogan "Boolean needs a sheaf"
resembles [THEORY-017](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-017.md)'s inference "Boolean structure needs a basis", and the
NOTE warns against reading it as support. The slogan is false as stated,
and [THEORY-017](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-017.md)'s inference rests on different grounds.

**Candidate follow-up, not filed.** Abramsky, Mansfield & Barbosa's
cohomology of contextuality is the correct version of what v2's appendix
attempts, and neither record holds it. Filing it would give [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md) and
[LIT-265](../../record/literature.d/LIT-265.md) their cohomological companion.
