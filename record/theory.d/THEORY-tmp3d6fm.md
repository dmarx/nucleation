# THEORY-tmp3d6fm: A member of a discipline who has been trained into its paradigms yet remains rooted in experience those paradigms omit or distort is better placed than its insiders to perceive those omissions and distortions as anomalies, and better placed than outsiders who share the experience to have them taken up, because insiders share the assumptions that produced the anomalies and outsiders lack the professional authority to press them

## Source

Collins (1986), "Learning from the Outsider Within", *Social Problems*
33(6): S14–S32, LIT-tmp2ojrd, read in full in NOTE-tmpj0rwa in the
authorized Portuguese translation (*Sociedade e Estado* 31(1), 2016). The
quotations below are the translation's, with the record's English gloss.

## What was actually shown

The argument has three steps.

1. *Paradigms make facts.* Following Kuhn, facts become significant in the
   context of theories, and a paradigm has two elements, its thought and
   its practitioners. Insiders acquire a shared worldview by long
   immersion (Merton, Schutz).
2. *Insiders cannot see what they made.* Traditional insiders "certamente
   não estão em posição de perceber as anomalias específicas ... pois
   esses mesmos insiders sociológicos produziram essas anomalias" (are not
   in a position to perceive the specific anomalies, since they produced
   them).
3. *Outsiders cannot press what they see.* Black women outside the
   academy may know the omissions, but "falta-lhes autoridade profissional
   legítima para desafiar as anomalias sociológicas" (they lack legitimate
   professional authority to challenge them).

The outsider within has both the sight and the standing. The illustrations
are two kinds of anomaly Black feminist sociologists reported, omission of
Black women from the facts and distortion of them (the matriarchy thesis),
and the revisions they prompted in theories of work and the family.

What could have come out otherwise: the argument predicts that reports of
such anomalies come disproportionately from outsiders within and not from
insiders of similar training. The paper shows that outsiders within made
such reports. It does not show that insiders did not, which is what the
promote_when asks for.

## What this does not say

- **That the outsider within is right.** The position makes anomalies
  visible. Whether a reported anomaly is real is a further question.
- **That every outsider within sees them, or that no insider can.** The
  claim is about position, and Collins notes that some outsiders within
  resolve the tension by assimilating and "pensam como de costume" (think
  as usual).
- **That position is enough for uptake.** The standing is professional
  membership. Whether the discipline takes up what is reported is
  Longino's question (THEORY-151) and CLAIM-tmpj163u's later rungs.
- **That the position is special to Black women.** Collins treats them as
  an extreme case of any less powerful group of outsiders entering a more
  powerful community of insiders.
