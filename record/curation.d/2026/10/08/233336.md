The owner asked for two works to be filed here: a *Radiolab* segment in
which Douglas Hofstadter describes translating Clément Marot's
"A une Damoyselle malade" ([LIT-tmplggfb](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmplggfb.md)), and the Stanford Encyclopedia of
Philosophy entry on dynamic semantics ([LIT-tmpx7zhl](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpx7zhl.md)). Both were read in
full, and each has a NOTE at `Read` ([NOTE-tmpjn2wc](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpjn2wc.md), [NOTE-tmpefif9](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpefif9.md)).

## Why here

Neither is anthology material. A grep of the anthology's record for
Hofstadter, Radiolab, dynamic semantics, dynamic predicate logic,
Groenendijk and discourse representation found nothing. Neither work
carries an instruction for machine-learning practice, and no anthology
topic is about translation or formal semantics.

## How they were tagged

- **The interview**: `philosophy-of-language` first, because its question
  is what a translation preserves of a text's meaning. Then `cognition`,
  for the analogy thesis it applies, and `individuation`, for the Chopin
  remark that a person is many things "united by analogy" into one.
- **The entry**: `philosophy-of-language` first, because it is a theory of
  meaning. Then `linguistics`, for anaphora and presupposition as linguists
  pose them, and `logic`, for DPL and dynamic epistemic logic.

## What was not done

- The interview describes a 700-page book of translations without naming
  it. It is *Le Ton beau de Marot* (1997), which the record does not
  hold. It was not filed, because the owner asked for the interview. It
  is the reading that would say whether Hofstadter's considered view is
  the interview's "no rules".
- No relation was declared. The interview builds on Hofstadter's analogy
  books ([LIT-420](../../record/literature.d/LIT-420.md), [LIT-417](../../record/literature.d/LIT-417.md)) but does not extend them in the `extends` sense,
  and the SEP entry's nearest neighbour, [LIT-208](../../record/literature.d/LIT-208.md), answers a different
  question. Both connections are stated in prose.
- No THEORY was filed. The record has none on translation, analogy-based
  categorization or formal semantics, and an interview is too thin to
  source one.
