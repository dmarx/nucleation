# TERM-tmpu28lt: intrapersonal synchronization

## Definition

An agent's available action-generating regimes ([TERM-019](TERM-019.md)) are
**synchronized** on a course of action over a horizon when each of them
ranks that course first from every delay at which it is evaluated within the
horizon. The course then becomes effective without any regime being
overridden, bargained with or suspended.

They are **desynchronized** over a horizon where their valuations of the
course cross within it. The paradigm is hyperbolic preference reversal: a
smaller, sooner reward ranked second from a distance and first just before
it is due ([THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md)).

Synchronization is a matter of degree in two ways. It can hold over a
shorter or longer horizon. And it can hold among more or fewer of the
regimes in play.

## What it is not

- **Not neural phase synchrony.** No oscillation, coupling or order
  parameter is part of the definition. Whether the word earns more than a
  name is [CLAIM-tmpigdl0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpigdl0.md)'s objection.
- **Not agreement at one moment.** All the interests can rank the larger,
  later reward first at the time of choosing and still be desynchronized,
  if their curves cross before the sooner reward is due. That is
  Ainslie's case exactly.
- **Not integration by arbitration.** When one regime wins against
  others, as in reliability arbitration ([THEORY-062](../theory.d/THEORY-062.md)), a bargain
  ([THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md)) or a costly override ([THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md)), the agent acts as one
  but its regimes are not synchronized. Synchronization is the case in
  which there is no contest to win.
- **Not will-organization** ([TERM-023](TERM-023.md)). Will-organization is the maintained
  organization of relations that governs which regime is effective.
  Synchronization is a condition in which that organization has nothing to
  decide.
