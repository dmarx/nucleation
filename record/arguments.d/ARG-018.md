# ARG-018: Illusions, reappraisal and interoceptive inference show that experience is constituted by interpretive organization

## The inference

At U2 the owner chose the constitutive reading and named the brain in a
vat, the rubber hand illusion and CBT. A2 set them in a table, with what
each varies. Its reading: "The result of interpretation can be the
experience itself", and "changing the interpretive organization changes
the experience itself". The record's readings of the three that it holds
are the premises: the rubber hand illusion ([THEORY-766](../theory.d/THEORY-766.md)), reappraisal
read as part of generation ([THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md)) and as separate from it
([THEORY-680](../theory.d/THEORY-680.md)), and interoceptive inference ([THEORY-767](../theory.d/THEORY-767.md)). The conclusion
is [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md): an appropriately organized process "is the experience". The
brain in a vat is a thought experiment, and the record holds no case for
it.

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes.
  A2 gave the caveat for CBT: it "allows a causal reading in which
  cognition modifies an independently constituted emotional experience",
  so its success "is consistent with your constitutive thesis but doesn't
  uniquely establish it". The same holds for the illusion. If
  interpretation produces experience, changing it changes the experience
  just as well.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No, and this is
  the question that fails. [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s own title: "the illusions and
  reappraisals that motivate it show that experience is constructed, not
  that construction is experience". A3 separated three theses: content is
  interpretively constructed, which is well supported; experience is
  constituted by interpretive organization, which is [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md); and the
  organization can be realized without a body, which is not claimed. The
  evidence supports the first. The record holds both readings of
  reappraisal, [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) and [THEORY-680](../theory.d/THEORY-680.md), as rival accounts of the same
  finding.
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** Not yet. [THEORY-766](../theory.d/THEORY-766.md)
  separates reports of referred touch, reports of ownership and
  proprioceptive drift, and its standard controls do not equalize
  expectation ([LIT-1276](../literature.d/LIT-1276.md)). So the illusion "might change the constituted
  experience of bodily ownership, the interpretation of an unchanged
  experience, the reporting of an experience, or some combination". The
  constitutive reading needs the first.

## The readings since

[CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s notes record two later readings of the works the exchange
cited. Both are the same inference, and they need no second argument.

- **2026-10-22.** Eighteen of the twenty-two works were read. "None
  supports the identity." Bo et al. ([THEORY-878](../theory.d/THEORY-878.md), [THEORY-871](../theory.d/THEORY-871.md)) found
  that reported affect fell as cortical appraisal was reorganized, which
  "fits the claim" and does not tell it apart from production. Lyre
  ([THEORY-880](../theory.d/THEORY-880.md)) holds that structure fixes which experience a state is,
  and leaves whether there is one to a separate mechanism.
- **2026-10-25.** Isaac ([LIT-1361](../literature.d/LIT-1361.md), read in [NOTE-1163](../notes.d/NOTE-1163.md)) addresses the
  character of experience only. His three layers are a structural
  analogue of interpretive organization. "He does not argue
  constitution."

## Verdict

Rejected. The record already holds this verdict. [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md)'s version 3
took the four readings out of its grounds, because "they motivate the
question and do not support the identity". [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) itself stays open,
and this argument is one route to it that fails. [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) does not
undercut it. What defeats the inference is the rival readings, which are
THEORYs, not the identity thesis.
