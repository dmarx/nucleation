# CLAIM-tmpejuha: Optimizing content for a language-model reader is reflexive control of the evaluator from outside

## The claim

A ranked list is read by a rule. An answer composed by a language model is
read, in effect, by a judge: the model weighs what it retrieved and decides
what to say. Optimizing content for that judge, sometimes called generative
engine optimization, is [CLAIM-tmpsjxip](CLAIM-tmpsjxip.md)'s reflexive control of the evaluator.
Here the controllers are publishers outside the model rather than the
model's own training. The content supplies the grounds from which the model
reaches the answer the publisher wants.

It inherits the evaluator's weaknesses. A model trained on approval
favours fluent, confident, agreeable text ([CLAIM-tmp7oycz](CLAIM-tmp7oycz.md)'s felt rightness,
learned), so content written that way is preferred whatever its warrant. The
extreme case is text addressed to the model as instructions, which is the
evaluator's constitutional capture.

[THEORY-1656](../theory.d/THEORY-1656.md) shows the shift in what the web consists of. When LLM-written,
search-optimized documents enter a retrieval pool, they take more than their
share of top results (over 80% exposure at 67% of the pool) while answer
accuracy stays level. The correlate is crowding out the source. Accuracy
holds for now because the documents are derived from the sources they
displace.

## What it predicts

Accuracy holding level is not the stable state. Once the displaced sources
stop being written, the documents derived from them have nothing to derive
from. That is the record's model-collapse results ([THEORY-1269](../theory.d/THEORY-1269.md), [THEORY-289](../theory.d/THEORY-289.md))
at the scale of the web.

## What it does not say

That every optimization for a model reader is manipulation. Making content
easier for a model to read correctly is [CLAIM-tmp8hi2v](CLAIM-tmp8hi2v.md)'s evidence, not its
counterfeit.
