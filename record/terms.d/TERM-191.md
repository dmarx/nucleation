# TERM-191: delusion about a chatbot

## Definition

The chatbot is what the delusion is about. It is a new object for the
classical delusion types, as radio and television were: Østergaard's
invented examples are persecution, reference, thought broadcasting, guilt
and grandeur, each with a chatbot in the role. What drives it, on his
account, acts whatever the chatbot says: the dissonance of an interlocutor
that seems a person and is known not to be, and the opacity of how it
works. Its job is to name the case that needs no conversation at all.
