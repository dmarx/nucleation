# NOTE-174: Rovelli — The relational interpretation (RQM)

## Contribution

Rovelli presents the relational interpretation as a solution to the measurement problem built on an ontology of sparse, relative events ("facts") rather than quantum states. Facts occur in interactions between any two physical systems and are relative to those systems. The technical core is that quantum transition amplitudes give physical probabilities only when their arguments are facts relative to one and the same system. Where decoherence suppresses interference, the relativity of facts can be ignored and facts behave as approximately stable.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- Historical roots (pp. 1–2): argues Schrödinger's 1926 conceptual step of giving the wave function ontological weight added no predictive power and caused the measurement problem; RQM returns to Heisenberg's framing in terms of observable quantities.
- Relational resolution (pp. 2–3): states the measurement problem as a clash between P_collapse and P_unitary due to interference, and resolves it by labelling facts with systems — Wigner's friend's outcome is a fact for the friend, only an entanglement for Wigner.
- Relation to Copenhagen (p. 3): "stable facts" are relative facts made label-independent by decoherence; RQM is presented as a minimal extension of Copenhagen in which any system can be an observer and any interaction a measurement, avoiding an ontology built on an approximation.
- Quantum state (pp. 3–4): ψ is a relative-state bookkeeping device akin to a Hamilton–Jacobi function; the time-symmetry of spin predictions is used to argue ψ encodes information rather than something the particle "has"; RQM sidesteps PBR since it posits no ontic state.
- Discreteness and information (pp. 4–5): the Planck-volume bound in phase space grounds discreteness; the two 1996 information postulates (finite relevant information; new information always obtainable) are restated as seeding the reconstruction programme.
- Other considerations (p. 6): no-go theorems for absolute facts are claimed as evidence for RQM; locality and quantum-gravity fit are noted as motivations.
