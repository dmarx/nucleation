# THEORY-tmpcpzrq: Brown (2025) argues that recommender systems bear on authenticity in two opposed ways: by ranking content on behavioural signals that cannot reveal users' second-order desires they foster volitional misalignment by design, while by showing users whom they behave like they can prompt self-understanding, a benefit that opacity threatens, so that recommenders serve authenticity only if they are controllable and explain which similarity relation drove each recommendation.

## The account

Brown (2025) argues that recommender systems bear on authenticity in two opposed ways: by ranking content on behavioural signals that cannot reveal users' second-order desires they foster volitional misalignment by design, while by showing users whom they behave like they can prompt self-understanding, a benefit that opacity threatens, so that recommenders serve authenticity only if they are controllable and explain which similarity relation drove each recommendation.
