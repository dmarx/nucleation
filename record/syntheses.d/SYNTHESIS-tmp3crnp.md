# SYNTHESIS-tmp3crnp: When a learned distinction becomes a concept is open, and a candidate term holds the record's working answer: a concept is a reusable, consequential pattern of distinctions, distinct from what carries it, what realizes it, what it is about and its corrigibility; the owner's question whether concepts individuate runs against the term's clause that a concept is not a thing; and Proposed claims that rival uses can keep an expression circulating without one concept, and that concepts are individuated by transmission, bear on it, with a proposed study to tell agreement in a concept from agreement in how it is revised

*A part account of the learned-structure line (ADR-083); the line's account is built over it and its sibling parts. Three of its six components have pragmatic-transport as their first line.*

## The account

- **The term and its question.** TERM-098 (filed at the owner's decision
  of 2026-10-31; Proposed) names conceptual organization by two marks,
  reuse and counterfactual reach. Its version 2, at the owner's review,
  took correctability out: "An incorrigible concept is still a concept."
  It says a concept is not its carrier, its realizer, its object, or "a
  thing or entity". QUESTION-360 (the owner's questions at U4 to U6 of
  "Analyze nucleation concepts"; Open) asks when a learned distinction
  becomes a concept rather than a discriminator or a habit, and is what
  would settle TERM-098 ("this question asks what would settle it", its
  summary). Its note of 2026-10-31 asks a study to include a frozen,
  reused component as a negative control.
- **Rival uses of one expression.** QUESTION-439 (the owner's U19 of
  "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition", with A41's three
  options; Open) refines QUESTION-360. It "runs against TERM-098's clause
  that a concept is "Not a thing or entity"" (its summary), and TERM-098's
  note of 2026-11-07 leaves that clause open as QUESTION-439. It holds,
  from TERM-098 and A54, that a difference in conditions of revision
  alone is no evidence of several concepts. Its third option, the
  relations among the competing uses as the most persistent structure, is
  CLAIM-621.
- **Rival use as what reproduces an expression.** CLAIM-621 (A41 §5, A's
  wording; Proposed, "Weak: it may be CLAIM-280 restated") uses TERM-098:
  an expression can be kept in circulation by communities using it
  against one another, so "symbolic stability can persist while
  conceptual stability fails" (its title). It bears on QUESTION-360 and
  QUESTION-439, and leaves to QUESTION-439 whether the rival uses are one
  concept or several.
- **Concepts individuated by transmission.** CLAIM-620 (the exchange's
  thesis, the owner's course with A's wording corrected by the filer;
  Proposed, admitted as an independent hypothesis under ADR-075) applies
  CLAIM-615's transmission criterion to concepts, with TERM-098 as its
  layer 1 and CLAIM-621 in its layer 5. It bears on QUESTION-439 and
  QUESTION-360, and its corrections note that U19's individuating
  concepts "runs against TERM-098's "Not a thing or entity"".
- **The study.** CASE-226 (A's design, A50 §7, with A55 §4's third arm;
  Proposed, stipulated) would test TERM-098's split of a concept from its
  corrigibility: two communities that agree on a category and differ in
  revising it. It bears on QUESTION-360 (its body), QUESTION-439 places
  A55's arm there, TERM-098's note cites it, and CLAIM-620 names it "the
  two-community study".

Outside this part, QUESTION-360 proposes QUESTION-275's setting (SYNTHESIS-tmptiwlg)
for tracking its criteria over training. No other relation or mention
joins this part to the line.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed.

- **CLAIM-620's individuated concepts against TERM-098's "not a thing".**
  Stated as open: TERM-098's note of 2026-11-07, CLAIM-620's correction
  and QUESTION-439's last clause ("in which sense of individuation, if
  any") all hold it. A sentence settling it would fix "individual" either
  as an organization, which TERM-098 says a concept is not, or as
  anything with a transmission lineage, which CLAIM-620's own defeater
  leaves to CLAIM-615; that is a free parameter until QUESTION-439 is
  answered.
- **CLAIM-621 as QUESTION-439's third option.** Stated by QUESTION-439.
- **Arm A.** CLAIM-620 rests on six claims and has an "Admission" section
  of 2026-11-07; its premises have changed since only in their lines
  (the sweep of 2026-11-10), so it is not re-tested here.

## What it does not say

- That any learned distinction in a network is a concept, or that a
  feature is one: TERM-098 keeps the concept apart from the feature that
  carries it.
- That "anti-vax" is one concept or several (QUESTION-439).
- That the reciprocal arrows of CLAIM-620 are shown: its own "What it
  does not say" disclaims it.
- Anything with standing of its own.
