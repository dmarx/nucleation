  Bogdan Georgiev, Javier Gómez-Serrano, Terence Tao and Adam Zsolt Wagner
  (2025), arXiv 2511.02864. AlphaEvolve is an evolutionary coding agent: an
  LLM mutates programs, a user-supplied evaluator scores them, and better
  programs seed the next generation. The authors apply it to 67 problems in
  analysis, combinatorics, geometry and number theory, mostly evolving
  search heuristics rather than constructions directly ("search mode"). It
  rediscovers the best known constructions in most cases and improves
  several; some findings generalize to formulas, and one (finite-field
  Kakeya) was proved by Deep Think and formalized in Lean by AlphaProof. It
  works best where a smooth score can be hill-climbed and at scale across
  families of related problems; it is not the tool where deep new insight is
