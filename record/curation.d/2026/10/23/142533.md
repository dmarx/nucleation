**What the reading corrected, before anything merged.**
- **The sign clause is Carver and Scheier's**, and its neutral point was
  wrong. Their reference is a rate of progress, not rest; the draft
  called a system standing still neutral where they call it behind. The
  claim now reads sign as progress against a reference course.
- **The intensity clause has a known difficulty.** Carver 2003 is reported
  to hold that positive affect lowers a goal's priority. The clause is
  held for negative valence until it is read.
- **The ambivalence–neutrality distinction is the evaluative space
  model's**, and the model's ambivalent and neutral outputs coincide, so
  telling them apart needs a record of which ranking each decision
  followed. The model's heterarchy is named in CLAIM-tmp83ge2's defeater.
- **The Veit–ALARM test was wrong as drafted.** It said a threshold
  override has no currency. It has a discontinuous one; only a graded
  override has none. CLAIM-tmp85p0g gained part 3a and CLAIM-tmp83ge2
  now separates continuous from discontinuous dominance. The
  representation theorem is credited to Eilenberg and Debreu.
