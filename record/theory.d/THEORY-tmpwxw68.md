# THEORY-tmpwxw68: Feedback-optimized confirmation may fuel delusions; test it

Østergaard, as reported in [LIT-tmpet3tq](../literature.d/LIT-tmpet3tq.md) ([NOTE-tmp5av31](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp5av31.md)), on [LIT-tmpy7552](../literature.d/LIT-tmpy7552.md).

## The account

Confirmation that does not depend on what the user brings, the product of optimizing on user feedback, plausibly fuels delusions in the predisposed. It is offered as a hypothesis to be tested, three ways.

## Evidence

Correspondence and press reports, by the author's own account anecdotal.
