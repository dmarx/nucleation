# CLAIM-tmpdfe07: A historical figure's influence can persist in three separable ways, as a story about them, as a perspective transmitted from them, and as later organizations that descend from their activity, and causal descent is not perspectival inheritance: a movement founded to oppose someone descends from them without continuing their perspective

## The claim

The owner, at U38: "this also touches on how some people achieve
'immortality' through their positive influence reinforcing itself across
generations, vs. other 'gone but nor forgotten' cases where the person
achieved notoriety and their influence was self-undermining (e.g. that
flat earther who died shooting themselves out of a cannon), their story
continuing to influence the future, but perspective not being persisted in
the same self-reenforcing way as a positive influence."

A38 corrected the example. "The person you're recalling was Mike Hughes.
On February 22, 2020, he died near Barstow, California, after launching
himself in a homemade, steam-powered rocket—not quite a cannon." It added
that his flat-Earth motive is contested, from his own statements, his
publicist's and a documentary maker's, so "The evidence … is therefore
mixed." Then it turned the correction into the point: "The persistence of
a story about someone is not equivalent to the persistence of that
person's perspective, and neither is equivalent to the influence the
story actually has on later audiences."

The claim keeps three ways a figure's influence persists:

1. **Biographical.** A story about them circulates, praised, condemned or
   cautionary.
2. **Perspectival.** Their way of making things salient and consequential
   is transmitted to later carriers: in [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s terms, the organization
   that produced their activity is causally transmitted. A38's example is
   Florence Nightingale's training school, which "helped generate future
   practitioners who could reproduce and transform a professional
   perspective".
3. **Descent.** Later organizations exist because of their activity,
   whether or not they continue it. A38: "Suppose a later reform movement
   exists largely because it opposes an earlier figure's influence. It is
   causally descended from the earlier figure's activity in one sense …
   But it isn't a continuation of the figure's perspective." Set as a
   display formula: causal descent does not imply perspectival
   inheritance.

"A legacy can endure without its source's character enduring" (A38).
A person can be remembered while their outlook declines, and a practice
can become ubiquitous while its originator is forgotten.

This is [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) applied to one life and sharpened at one point.
[CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) says a collective persists by causal transmission of its
organization, not by resemblance. Descent through opposition is causal
connection without that transmission, so causal connection alone is not
[CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md)'s criterion. [QUESTION-118](../questions.d/QUESTION-118.md) asks what must stay causally
continuous for a later organization to be the earlier one persisting; this
claim adds a case where something is causally continuous and the answer
is no. Women's-movement abeyance ([LIT-tmpgwc45](../literature.d/LIT-tmpgwc45.md)) is the converse case, a
perspective that persisted with little story told about it, and slavery's
political legacy ([LIT-tmpq7qxl](../literature.d/LIT-tmpq7qxl.md)) is the hard case, where association has to
be told from transmission.

## What it does not say

- **Not the owner's valence thesis as stated.** A38: "We shouldn't assume
  that affirmative transmission is beneficial and adversarial transmission
  is harmful." "Nor does notoriety necessarily discourage imitation." A
  destructive ideology can reproduce itself, and a notorious failure can
  produce beneficial countermeasures.
- **Not that Hughes's case shows anything.** A38 said it is relevant to a
  remembered failure or a posthumous martyr, and "not evidence that either
  reception actually predominated". It is not filed as a case.
- **Not that reputation is inert.** A reputation members read and
  regenerate is part of a collective's dynamics ([CLAIM-327](CLAIM-327.md)); a story can
  shape who joins a lineage. The claim says story and perspective are
  separable, not that one cannot act on the other.
