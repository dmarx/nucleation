# CLAIM-tmpyqjn0: The belief that fiduciary duty requires maximizing shareholder value can bind a corporation more tightly than the applicable law does, gaining authority through management education, pay and investor expectation, and so help produce the organizational character later presented as a legal necessity

## The claim

A48 §11: "We should also distinguish shareholder primacy as a legal
doctrine from shareholder primacy as an organizational meme." On the law:
"American corporate law does not impose a universal duty to maximize
immediate share prices in every business decision. Delaware jurisprudence
distinguishes ordinary business judgment and long-term corporate strategy
from particular sale-of-control circumstances governed by *Revlon* duties"
([LIT-tmps71a9](../literature.d/LIT-tmps71a9.md)), and the public benefit corporation's directors "must
balance shareholder financial interests, the interests of materially
affected parties and specified public benefits. This does not, by itself,
give every affected party a directly enforceable fiduciary claim"
([LIT-tmprbi96](../literature.d/LIT-tmprbi96.md)).

On the meme: "The expression *fiduciary duty requires shareholder value
maximization* may circulate within an organizational culture with
implications stronger than those warranted by the applicable legal
doctrine. It can acquire practical authority through management
education, compensation practices, investor expectations and institutional
routines." "A cultural interpretation of corporate law can become a
constraint upon organizational behavior even where the law leaves greater
discretion. The meme can help produce the organizational character
subsequently presented as a legal necessity."

## How it stands to the record

It is [CLAIM-402](CLAIM-402.md)'s pattern, a category institutions act on producing the
regularities that confirm it, with the category a reading of a legal rule.
A48 calls it "another instance of the distinction between a represented
specification and an operative contractual arrangement"; that distinction,
and memes as contracts, belong to the exchange's earlier turns, filed by
other groups.

## Note of 2026-11-24: the legal premise is narrower than A48 says (the assistant)

The claim needs a gap between what Delaware law requires and what the
belief says it requires. A48 gives the business judgment rule and Revlon's
limit to sales of control, which support a gap in *enforcement*: outside a
sale, courts rarely second-guess how directors pursue value. It omits
eBay Domestic Holdings, Inc. v. Newmark ([LIT-tmpn8fqh](../literature.d/LIT-tmpn8fqh.md), Del. Ch. 2010,
unread), which secondary summaries, found by a web search on 2026-11-24,
report as listing among a for-profit corporation's fiduciary standards
acting "to promote the value of the corporation for the benefit of its
stockholders", and which commentators read either as making stockholder
value the mandatory objective or only as forbidding its sacrifice to
protect other interests. On the first reading the gap is smaller than A48
says: the law states the objective and leaves the means to the directors,
and the meme's surplus is the step from promoting stockholder value to
maximizing it in every decision and over the short run. The claim survives on either reading
but should be stated for that surplus; which reading is right is a question
for reading the opinion. The wording of this note is the assistant's
(Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
