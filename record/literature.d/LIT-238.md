# LIT-238: Minimum Description Length Revisited

Peter Grünwald, Teemu Roos (2019), *International Journal of Mathematics for Industry 11(1):1930001 (2019)* — [ARXIV-1908.08484](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08484)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-760](../notes.d/NOTE-760.md).*

- **One distribution per model.** MDL scores a model by a single universal
  distribution p̄. Because it sums to one, p̄ cannot fit every sample well,
  and that is the guard against overfitting. The kinds are Bayes, NML,
  two-part, prequential plug-in, switch and RIPr.
- **NML is the minimax choice.** p̄_nml,v(zⁿ) ∝ max_θ p_θ(zⁿ)v(θ) uniquely
  maximises the worst-case fitness ratio. With v ≡ 1 it is the
  minimax-regret predictor, and comp(M; v) is the minimax regret. NML is
  undefined with v ≡ 1 for most models on unbounded outcome spaces.
- **The penalty.** comp(M; v) = (k/2) log(n/2π) + log ∫ v(θ)√|I(θ)| dθ +
  o(1) for exponential families (Suzuki & Yamanishi 2018). Bayes with the
  generalised Jeffreys prior matches it asymptotically. Every standard
  universal code pays (k/2) log n + O(1) on regular data.
- **Estimation is penalised ML.** θ̂_v = argmax p_θ(zⁿ)v(θ). Joint MDL over
  model and parameter selects the same model as meta-NML. Two-part codes
  have no special status (Grünwald & Mehta 2019).
- **Recent universal codes.**
  - The switch distribution is strongly consistent and prediction-optimal
    within O(log log n). It "almost" resolves the AIC–BIC dilemma, which
    Yang (2005) shows cannot be resolved exactly.
  - The RIPr makes likelihood-ratio tests of composite nulls safe under
    optional continuation.
- **Frequentist guarantees and their limit.** General MDL estimators are
  consistent when a generalised complexity comp_η, η < 1, grows as o(n).
  Under misspecification, MDL and Bayes can be inconsistent, or choose
  arbitrarily bad models for fairly large n. Tempering the likelihood
  (η < 1) repairs this.
- **Deep learning (§6.4).** Flat minima need fewer bits, and PAC-Bayes
  bounds give that a precise frequentist form. The review cites this and
  calls it one of several explanations.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped under
the heading *Relating K-complexity to minimum sufficient statistics*. Read
on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-760](../notes.d/NOTE-760.md). It is the record's reference for
statistical MDL. [LIT-623](LIT-623.md) named it as such while it was unread. It does not
serve the heading it was filed under. It sets Kolmogorov-complexity MDL
aside, and it mentions the structure function only in pointing to
Rissanen's later programme.

No THEORY is filed. The review cites its results and proves none. Its
(k/2) log n penalty with the Fisher-volume constant adds a second,
description-length source for the regular half of [THEORY-087](../theory.d/THEORY-087.md). Its
singular-model formula (31) quotes the same λ log n leading term from
Watanabe ([LIT-616](LIT-616.md)). Its §6.3, on inconsistency under misspecification,
is a ground for caution about Bayes-factor thresholds that [CLAIM-188](../claims.d/CLAIM-188.md) does
not yet name ([NOTE-760](../notes.d/NOTE-760.md), Bearing on the record). The companion
readings on Kolmogorov-complexity MDL are [LIT-225](LIT-225.md) and [LIT-239](LIT-239.md).
