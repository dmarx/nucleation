# THEORY-tmp5rm74: Park et al. (2023) give LLM agents a memory stream of timestamped natural-language observations, retrieved by a sum of recency, importance and relevance scores, plus periodic reflections that cite the memories they are inferred from and are stored back into the stream, and find in an ablation rated by human judges that memory, reflection and planning each add to the believability of agent behaviour, with errors coming from failed retrieval and embellishment rather than from memories being absent

## The account

Park et al. (2023) give LLM agents a memory stream of timestamped natural-language observations,
retrieved by a sum of recency, importance and relevance scores, plus periodic reflections that cite
the memories they are inferred from and are stored back into the stream, and find in an ablation
rated by human judges that memory, reflection and planning each add to the believability of agent
behaviour, with errors coming from failed retrieval and embellishment rather than from memories
being absent.
