# QUESTION-tmpbvc9e: What makes a will-organization the agent's own, rather than merely effective within it?

## Why it is set aside

Velleman's objection — why should a higher-order attitude count as the agent's
own merely because it is higher-order — applies equally to a higher-order
organization. The authenticity account ([CLAIM-tmp4o38w](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4o38w.md)) tried to meet it
and the audit judged that its "ownership criteria remain insufficiently
defended". Future work, Section 7.9.
