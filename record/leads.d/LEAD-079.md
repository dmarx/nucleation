# LEAD-079: Do the move-by-move reading of identity failures against structure failures on a second exchange, and on one with another owner or another assistant family

## What it would do

another owner or assistant family.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-483](../claims.d/CLAIM-483.md): "the population is one owner and one assistant family."

through it [CLAIM-176](../claims.d/CLAIM-176.md); a different one bounds it to the first exchange.
