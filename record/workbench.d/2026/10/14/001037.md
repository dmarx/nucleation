*Same contribution as the meaning-operators filing (dmarx/nucleation#140).*

Filing the r/place survival analysis as a stipulated case prompted the
owner to ask for a typology of cases: "proposed empirical experiments vs
thought experiments vs. steel-man vs. etc".

**Survey first.** All 81 cases were listed with their standing, relations
and summaries before any category was named. Six kinds covered every case,
with four judgement calls, which [ADR-042](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-042.md) lists for the owner. The decisive
observation was the cross-tabulation. `stipulated` holds five of the six
kinds, and thought experiments sit under all three standings. So the
typology is a second axis, not a refinement of standing.

**What was declined.** Steel-man was not made a kind. It is a voice, and the
record holds it as a counter claim. Analogy and counterexample were not made
kinds either, since they are relations. A separate STUDY scheme was set
aside, because studies serve claims through the same relations as other
cases.

**Not yet applied.** [ADR-042](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-042.md) is Proposed. luria.yaml and the 81 cases are
unchanged until the owner approves, as with [ADR-040](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-040.md).
