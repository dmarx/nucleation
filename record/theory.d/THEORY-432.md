# THEORY-432: In Lévi-Straussian analysis of myth the group of transformations is not given by the material: which variants count as transforms of which, and by what operation, is cut by the analyst from a continuum, unlike transformations that develop an initial form by a rule, and the computational encodings of the method confirm this by taking the analyst's table of oppositions as input

## Source

Descola (2016), [LIT-822](../literature.d/LIT-822.md) (*HAU* 6(3):33–44, a keynote lecture), read in
full in [NOTE-641](../notes.d/NOTE-641.md). Santucci, Doja and Capocchi (2020), [LIT-807](../literature.d/LIT-807.md) (*Symmetry*
12(10):1706), read in full in [NOTE-632](../notes.d/NOTE-632.md).

## What was actually shown

**A structure is a set of transformations ([LIT-822](../literature.d/LIT-822.md)).** Elements are
characterized "not by intrinsic properties but by their position vis-à-vis
one another"; the model "provides the syntax of the transformations which
allows one to move from one variant to another". "A structure is not a
system": it requires invariant relations that allow moving from one set to
another by a transformation.

**Two regimes ([LIT-822](../literature.d/LIT-822.md); C2, C3 moderate).**
- *Goethean.* Variants develop an initial form. In *Les structures
  élémentaires*, reciprocity develops from restricted exchange between two
  moieties to generalized exchange, to its combination with bride purchase
  among the Kachin. The variants are generated by a rule.
- *Thompsonian.* Continuous deformation between given forms, after D'Arcy
  Thompson, which Lévi-Strauss cites for myth. But unlike organisms, whose
  differences rest on the discontinuities of the genetic code, the group
  of a myth's transformations is "a virtual continuum upon which the
  analyst performs arbitrary cuts" (citing *L'homme nu*, 603–4), so "each
  analyst will trace the path of transformation which his or her fancy
  will suggest". D'Arcy Thompson needs stable forms at both ends; myth
  analysis has none.

**The encoding ([LIT-807](../literature.d/LIT-807.md)).** A myth is a list of (term, function)
mythemes. Homology, inversion, opposition and symmetry are each "replace
term a by term t in every mytheme", with t supplied by the user; adding
and removing a mytheme complete the set (§2.2). The canonical formula is
explained by the Jealous Potter, where the key step (the ovenbird inverts
the nightjar) is ethnographic knowledge, and no rule computes it. The
Discussion concedes that the inverse of the ogre, and every such value,
"is not obtained from the software system", and that without it "it is
impossible to define the transformation". The validation generates 70
*Mythologiques* myths and 28 Corsican tales "by selecting each time a set
of transformations to be applied on a given myth".

**What could have come out otherwise.** An implementation could have
derived the oppositions from the variants, or produced Lévi-Strauss's
orderings from the reference myth alone. [LIT-807](../literature.d/LIT-807.md)'s does neither, and says
so.

## What this does not say

- **That Lévi-Strauss's analyses are wrong or arbitrary in content.**
  The claim is about where the path between variants comes from, not
  about whether a given analysis is apt. Descola names no criterion for
  preferring one ordering to another, and the record holds none.
- **That every structural analysis is constructed.** In the Goethean
  regime the variants follow from a rule, and Descola puts his own four
  ontologies there (C4, stated, not defended here).
- **That the software tests anything about myth.** Reproducing given
  analyses with operations chosen to reproduce them cannot fail ([NOTE-632](../notes.d/NOTE-632.md),
  C4). It is evidence here only through its concession about the table.
- **That the method cannot be made non-arbitrary.** Fixing stable end
  forms, as Thompson's method needs, or inferring the table from a corpus,
  are open ([NOTE-641](../notes.d/NOTE-641.md) and [NOTE-632](../notes.d/NOTE-632.md), Open questions).
- **That the distinction is Lévi-Strauss's.** The Goethe/Thompson names
  are Descola's. Lévi-Strauss credited Thompson, and Goethe only "in
  passing".

  Descola reads it and as computationally modelled, is prior art for
  structural transport. This account fixes what that prior art contains:
  an encoding of the analyst's transformations, not a procedure that
  finds them. **ARG-014** draws the same conclusion for trope
  annotations.

  [NOTE-641](../notes.d/NOTE-641.md) adds, as the reader's own connection, that Propp's Goethe
  epigraphs announce the Goethean regime.
