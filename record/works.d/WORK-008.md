# WORK-008: The Character That Changed Parties

## About

The owner's essay, proposed 2026-10-22 as one of four 6,000-word essays
from the exchange "Graph Learning Applications" (A33), later folded into
organization-of-perspective: political realignment and the persistence of
perspectives across institutions. The continuity of a political
organization, the persistence of a perspective and the descent of a
tradition are distinct, and a perspective can move between carriers
without an immutable essence or one continuing collective agent
