# LIT-2520: Groups as intergenerational agents: Responsibility through time and change

Janna Thompson (2022). Groups as intergenerational agents: Responsibility through time and change. *Journal of Social Philosophy* 53(1), 8–20. DOI-10.1111/josp.12459.

## Key takeaways

Read in [NOTE-1895](../notes.d/NOTE-1895.md). The natural person analogy fails for want of identity criteria; temporal trustworthiness is what intergenerational responsibility needs, and obligations persist and pass to successors. §10, which A12 missed: essentially intergenerational groups owe members' children entitlements to resources, respect and protection, grounded in needs intrinsic to human life.
