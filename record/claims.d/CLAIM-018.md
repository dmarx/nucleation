# CLAIM-018: Canguilhem's distinction between functioning under a norm and capacity for norm-changing adaptation corresponds to transformation capacity

## The claim

"An agent can be highly competent relative to one established governing regime
while possessing little capacity to reorganize when that regime ceases to
support viable activity."

## What it does not say

"Institutions are not organisms, and regulatory failure is not literally
disease." The audit's verdict on Canguilhem: an "illuminating philosophical
analogue, not a co-foundation". It rests on Canguilhem as cited
([LIT-817](../literature.d/LIT-817.md)); the record holds no reading of the book.
