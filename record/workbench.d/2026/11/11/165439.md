  what carries a memory through the rebuilding of the nervous system in
  the animal that learned; sensitivity in offspring does not meet it. The
  question's body citation stays.

  utility is a self-image of conformity, with no motive aimed at another's
  good and no self that includes the group's good.

  influence; it holds no result on helping. Its corroboration of CLAIM-493
  stays, since the claim's own words need the step from prototypicality to
