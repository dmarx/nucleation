# TERM-tmp8j1kl: levels, as levels at which observables exist

## Definition

U28: "it's more like a hierarchy of levels at which observables exist". A75:
O = ∪_ℓ O_ℓ, indexed by levels: linguistic (word choice, syntax, meter,
register), conceptual (referents, causal relations), pragmatic (force, irony,
presupposition), interactional (footing, alignment, commitments), dynamical
(responses to framing, trajectories, adaptation). "These aren't necessarily
disjoint or linearly ordered. An observable can participate in several levels,
and the levels themselves might form a partial order." A75: "**The hierarchy
belongs to how we can observe and distinguish communicative phenomena, not
necessarily to the phenomena themselves.**"

## What it is not

Not a measurement context. The chunk-5 reader notes that C6 §2 orders sheaf
contexts (sets of jointly measured observables) by inclusion and calls that the
partial order, which is a different order from the owner's levels. The
manuscript §2 keeps the levels without the order: "These observations are not
ordered into one strict ontological ladder." The dynamical level is not in the
manuscript's list.
