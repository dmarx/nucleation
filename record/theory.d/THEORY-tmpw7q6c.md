# THEORY-tmpw7q6c: Greely argues that the Alabama Supreme Court's LePage decision only extended a punitive-damages wrongful-death action to negligently destroyed frozen embryos and would not have ended IVF, but that its language provoked an overreaction ending in a statute that immunizes IVF providers far more than negligence warrants, and that broad political support keeps IVF largely safe in the United States.

## The account

Greely argues that the Alabama Supreme Court's LePage decision only extended a punitive-damages wrongful-death action to negligently destroyed frozen embryos and would not have ended IVF, but that its language provoked an overreaction ending in a statute that immunizes IVF providers far more than negligence warrants, and that broad political support keeps IVF largely safe in the United States.
