# THEORY-tmprpct4: Montero-Porras, Smets and Lenaerts show that in a two-population replicator model of a platform that rewards costly quality content and creators who invest in it, quality and its absence are both stable, the platform needs far more creators investing than creators need the platform to reward them, and a temporary rise in the payoff to promoting cheap content that lasts long enough moves the system into decline that restoring the original payoff does not undo.

## The account

Montero-Porras, Smets and Lenaerts show that in a two-population replicator model of a platform that rewards costly quality content and creators who invest in it, quality and its absence are both stable, the platform needs far more creators investing than creators need the platform to reward them, and a temporary rise in the payoff to promoting cheap content that lasts long enough moves the system into decline that restoring the original payoff does not undo.
