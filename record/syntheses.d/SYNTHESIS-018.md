# SYNTHESIS-018: A corporation can bear functional affect in Frijda's sense, without its implying experience, and so can be read as lacking remorse when others' harms reach its decisions only as threats, a reading Wells Fargo's sales practices document and an unanswered objection says cannot be told from prudence; the functional reading is held back by the identity of valence with a felt state, whether such affect needs a group subject or is fixed by the attributor's concept is open, and the line's first framing, what it is like to be a corporation, is retired

*A part account of the organizational-affect line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)): corporate affect and responsibility. The line's account is built over it and its two sibling parts, [SYNTHESIS-020](SYNTHESIS-020.md) (the potential account of valence) and [SYNTHESIS-022](SYNTHESIS-022.md) (feeling and access).*

## The account

**The thesis.** [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) (the owner's move at U19–U20, 2026-10-04;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed): affective states can be properties of
organized systems, characterized functionally through Frijda's action
readiness with control precedence ([TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md)), "without implying
subjective experience". It was set aside from the will paper on
2026-10-04 and reopened by the owner on 2026-10-23: "one of the specific
things our framework equips us to do is to assign affect to
corporation-like things". What holds it back is one objection, named in
its notes of 2026-10-12, 2026-10-23, 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-06: Veit's
identity of hedonic valence with a felt regulatory state ([THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)).
The note of 2026-10-29: "The record holds no claim showing either that
action readiness with control precedence ([TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md)) can be had without
hedonic valence, or that Veit's identity, argued for animal valence and
rated a sketch in [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md), does not extend to it." Massumi's affect,
its note of 2026-10-31 says, neither grounds nor defeats it.

**Corporate remorse.** [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) (thesis, interpretive, Proposed; rests
on [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) and [CLAIM-271](../claims.d/CLAIM-271.md); first line distributed-agency, then this one), the
record's reading of Bakan's "institutional psychopath", filed at the
owner's request and revised the same day at the owner's correction. A
corporation can be an agent (French), fit to be held responsible
(Pettit), and a bearer of functional affect. A harm it does to others
reaches its decisions either as one of its own concerns, through
procedures and role duties, which is functional remorse, or only as a
threat from a regulator or a market, which is functional fear. A
corporation with only the second has "threat without remorse", across
the construct and to a degree. The profile is changed by redesigning the
corporation's concerns, and the claim predicts "that a corporation
changed only by sanction reverts when the sanction lifts, and one whose
concerns were redesigned does not". It joins [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s and [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)'s
distinctions: "a corporation can hold a signal of its harm, and the
signal can claim precedence, without being granted it". Its defeater
names the Veit route only joined to an accepted argument that a
corporation is not phenomenally conscious (version 2).

**The documented case.** [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md) (documented, Active; primary line distributed-agency), from the independent directors' report:
the Community Bank's sales model, enforced by performance management
and a decentralized structure, with the signals of harm held by people
without authority to act or filtered before the board. [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) reads
it through the claim: concerns narrowed to fees, signals without
precedence, misconduct appraised as employees' doing, change only after
the regulators. The case says of itself: "The case is about routing and
authority, not absence." It does not show intent, the absence of
individual culpability, or "Anything about corporations in general".

**The objection.** [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md) (counter, interpretive, Proposed; rests on
[CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md); primary line distributed-agency) objects that a
corporation's functional remorse cannot be told from prudence: any
procedure that makes others' harms its concern was installed for its own
ends, so "The two routes differ in how far back the threat sits, not in
kind". It grants [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md). It names three answers, and its note of
2026-10-30 says none is met: the test whether harms keep being weighed
once sanction lifts is unrun, and "nothing in the record says what about
a corporation, unlike a person, keeps an installed concern answerable to
the end it was installed for". [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)'s note of 2026-10-23 adds
Trivers ([THEORY-663](../theory.d/THEORY-663.md)) as sharpening the objection, and states the
discriminating design: hold reputation, sanctions and commercial
benefit roughly fixed and ask whether others' harms keep independent
authority.

**What fixes the affect.** [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md) (Open) asks whether an
organization's emotion is fixed by a mode of action readiness it has, or
by the attributor's concept grouping variable states ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md) against
[THEORY-057](../theory.d/THEORY-057.md)). Its note of 2026-10-18 says a regulation-defined condition
sidesteps part of the split, but only "if the demands are the
organization's own", since a choice made by whoever measures "brings the
attributor back in". [QUESTION-053](../questions.d/QUESTION-053.md) (Open) asks whether organizational
affect needs a group subject or only members' convergence ([THEORY-125](../theory.d/THEORY-125.md)
against [THEORY-126](../theory.d/THEORY-126.md)). Its notes add a third option from Nowak et al., a
coordinated configuration; record A27's separation of a state of the
group from a subject that owns it, on which [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) answers in part
([SYNTHESIS-022](SYNTHESIS-022.md)); and, from Massumi, differential attunement, whose nearest held
account is [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) ([SYNTHESIS-020](SYNTHESIS-020.md)), "an analogy and not a shared mechanism".

**The first framing, retired.** [QUESTION-019](../questions.d/QUESTION-019.md) (Superseded by [QUESTION-015](../questions.d/QUESTION-015.md);
the owner's U9), "What is it like to be Microsoft?", and [CASE-014](../cases.d/CASE-014.md)
(stipulated, Deferred; primary line will-organization), the
corporation that framing asked about, committed to as a touchstone and
then dropped; its note of 2026-10-22 records the question asked again
and redirected "from phenomenology to perspective". [CASE-024](../cases.d/CASE-024.md) (documented,
Active; the owner's U30), a coral reef as a coordinated ecological
whole, whose role "drifted" four times; its current use is a boundary
case for [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md), of distributed-agency. No component of the line
cites it or is cited by it; it is listed because its `line` field places
it here.

- **Derived: [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s attribution inherits [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md)'s split.**
  [QUESTION-044](../questions.d/QUESTION-044.md) states it for [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md): "[CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md) inherits a
  perceiver-relativity it does not state". [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md) rests on [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md),
  so the inference is one step and trivial.
- **Hypothesis: origin is not current function, for corporations.** This
  is [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)'s own second answer, which its note of 2026-10-30 says is
  unmet. Asserting it would be a claim against [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md) with nothing to
  stand on that the record holds.

## Not stated on the record

- **What keeps an installed concern answerable to its end** in a
  corporation and not in a person ([CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)'s note of 2026-10-30).
- **Whether any corporation reverts when sanction lifts.** [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s
  part 5 test and [CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)'s design are unrun.
- **A case where convergence holds and integration fails, or the
  reverse** ([QUESTION-053](../questions.d/QUESTION-053.md)). A29's Test B and Massumi's prediction are
  designs, not cases.
- **Whether [CASE-024](../cases.d/CASE-024.md) bears on anything in the line.**

## What it does not say

- That a corporation feels anything. The affect is functional ([CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md),
  [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)), and whether functional affect must be felt is [QUESTION-059](../questions.d/QUESTION-059.md)
  ([SYNTHESIS-022](SYNTHESIS-022.md)).
- That the remorse–threat line is drawable for a corporation: [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md)
  is unanswered.
- That Wells Fargo shows more than "one firm, one business line, one
  decade" ([CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)), or that its later change was remorse or fear
  ([CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)).
- Anything with standing of its own.
