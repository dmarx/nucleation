The owner read the first filing and pushed back. "even just looking at the
last message in the exchange, I expected at least three more questions. is

**What went wrong.** The overlap the first pass found was real, but it was
counted as if it closed questions:

- Three of A11's four questions were folded into material the record holds.
  - Q1 was turned straight into a thesis.
  - Q2 was "protective lock-in".
  - Q3 was "[CLAIM-170](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-170.md) and [CLAIM-154](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-154.md)".
- The owner's U5 question was called [QUESTION-056](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-056.md).

In each case the record held something near the question, a phenomenon, a
disclaimer, a partial answer, or the same question about a different
account, and not the question itself. A claim with no question to answer,
and a reading near a question nobody asked, are what [ADR-032](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-032.md)'s questions
exist to prevent. Filing questions costs nothing. Leaving them out means
the readings registered for them have nothing to attach to.
