# CLAIM-tmpki5gp: Without action, free energy minimized over observations that the judgements themselves produce has a self-confirming minimum, and the free-energy principle's reason for minimizing surprise, that a persisting system must keep its states in bounds, needs action on the world

## The claim

The free-energy principle explains why surprise matters. A system that
persists must keep its sensory states within bounds, and Friston et al.
([LIT-tmp670rc](../literature.d/LIT-tmp670rc.md)) say the active sampling that does so is "mandated
by ergodic arguments based on the very existence of adaptive agents".
Take action out, and let judgements produce the observations the model
updates on. Then free energy has a cheap minimum: judge so that what is
observed confirms what is expected.

The record already holds that minimum as a failure.
[THEORY-285](../theory.d/THEORY-285.md): an over-precise prior makes exploratory sampling look
uninformative, so the agent never receives the evidence that would
correct it. [TERM-020](../terms.d/TERM-020.md), protective lock-in: a regime kept stable by
behaviour that blocks its own disconfirmation. A judgement–observation
loop without an outside constraint is the general form of both.

Kübler and Birbaumer ([LIT-tmpxcw33](../literature.d/LIT-tmpxcw33.md)) give the empirical form. In
the completely locked-in state no patient of theirs regained yes/no
communication, and they proposed that "the complete lack of motor control
and feedback ... might be responsible for the cessation of voluntary
cognitive activity". The judgement loop, on this view, runs down when
action is gone ([CASE-tmpxgcq2](../cases.d/CASE-tmpxgcq2.md)).

## What it does not say

- It does not show that the world can enter the loop only through action.
  [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md) proposes that it enters as the outcome distribution each
  measurement context allows. If that holds, a judgement cannot return
  whatever confirms the model, and the cheap minimum is not available.
- The extinction hypothesis is a hypothesis. Chaudhary et al. report a
  CLIS patient who communicated ([LIT-tmpzuemc](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzuemc.md)), with cautions the
  case states.
