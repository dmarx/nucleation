# LIT-1765: Reconceptualizing Individuals, Categories, and the Social Group: Self-Categorization Theory 2.0

Skorich, D. P. (2025; version 2, 2026). Reconceptualizing individuals, categories, and the social group: Self-categorization theory 2.0. PsyArXiv preprint. doi:10.31234/osf.io/h7s9p_v2.

Read in full in [NOTE-1295](../notes.d/NOTE-1295.md).

## Why this is here

Turner et al. 1987 ([LIT-1723](LIT-1723.md)) cannot be read. This paper takes "the plain language reading" of that book's chapter 3, and of Oakes's chapter 6, as its definition of the theory, and quotes the book's assumptions and hypotheses with page numbers (pp. 42–56). It is the record's closest lawful access to the book's own words. It is also the strongest critique the record holds of meta-contrast, the mechanism behind [THEORY-1114](../theory.d/THEORY-1114.md).
