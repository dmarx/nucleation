# CLAIM-tmpeli2w: Information in the sense the Simondonian readings give it, a disparity whose resolution reorders the system that takes it up, is in the line's terms a change in the interpreter or in the questions receivers can decide, not evidence about a fixed situation, so Blackwell's order leaves it out by construction and the two accounts are not rivals

## The claim

**The readings' information.** [THEORY-tmp1g3ve](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp1g3ve.md) reads Simondon through
Iliadis ([LIT-tmpj9jyk](../literature.d/LIT-tmpj9jyk.md), pp. 10–13) and Bencherki and Iliadis ([LIT-tmpwspny](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwspny.md),
propositions 1 and 3). Information is "never a given thing" but a disparity
between two differently ordered domains, and its meaning is the new
structure that transduction produces in resolving it. Bencherki, Brummans
and Vézy ([LIT-tmp3mudg](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3mudg.md), p. 1357, [THEORY-tmp5ux44](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp5ux44.md)) give it two senses:

- a fact's meaning "depends on that fact's contribution to the
  constitution of a new field of relation";
- information "in-forms" that field, as lower enrolment leaves "the
  university in its new form".

How far a field is informed depends on the field. Densely assembled ones,
"crystals or well-oiled bureaucracies", "remain mostly unaffected" (pp.
1356–1357).

**The line's information.** [CLAIM-050](CLAIM-050.md) and [CLAIM-259](CLAIM-259.md) compare a source and a
rendering as experiments about one situation, for receivers whose decision
problems are fixed in advance. A rendering is charged when it raises a
fixed receiver's risk ([THEORY-1096](../theory.d/THEORY-1096.md) for the definitions). Neither the
receiver nor the set of questions changes.

**Where the first falls in the line.** The line keeps two things apart
from evidence, and the disparity account's information falls in them:

- **A change in the interpreter.** [CLAIM-118](CLAIM-118.md) separates what the current
  context supplies, the signal, from what is left in the interpreter and
  carried into new contexts. Reordering a field so that it continues
  differently is the second. The "dense" field that is not reorganized is,
  in these terms, an interpreter whose response function the message does
  not move. [CLAIM-074](CLAIM-074.md) already treats fidelity across states of adaptation
  as a separate quantity.
- **A change in which questions can be decided.** A new structure gives
  the receiving system questions it could not decide before. In the line
  that is the change of cover. [CLAIM-256](CLAIM-256.md) found source-only distortion
  blind to it, and [CLAIM-257](CLAIM-257.md) reports it separately. Operationally it may
  be the change of empirical model that [CLAIM-251](CLAIM-251.md) distinguishes from a
  change of cover.

**So they are not rivals.** Blackwell's order is silent on
disparity-information by construction, because it holds the receiver and
the problem fixed. The disparity account is silent on the ceiling a
rendering sets for a fixed receiver, which report fidelity needs. The
line covers the disparity account's ground through [CLAIM-118](CLAIM-118.md)'s interpreter
channel and [CLAIM-257](CLAIM-257.md)'s change of cover, not through [CLAIM-259](CLAIM-259.md)'s decision
term.

**What this gives the readings.** [NOTE-tmphu9ok](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphu9ok.md) leaves open whether
"information as resolution of disparity" can be stated so that something
counts as a failure to inform. Here is an operational candidate. An action
fails to inform a population in this sense if, after exposure, neither
its response function on held-out items nor the set of questions it can
decide has changed. That can be measured without reading the outcome back
into the action, which the readings' retrospective meaning ("action
contributes to informing a system that, retrospectively, gives that
action its meaning", [LIT-tmp3mudg](../literature.d/LIT-tmp3mudg.md) p. 1357) cannot.

## What it does not say

- **That Simondon rejects the quantitative theory.** Iliadis argues he
  extends it ([NOTE-tmphu9ok](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphu9ok.md), his C2, weak). This claim says only that the
  readings' information is not what the order compares.
- **That the readings are right.** They are Proposed and conceptual.
- **That the mapping is Simondon's.** It places two of his commentators'
  formulations. His text is unread here ([LIT-1648](../literature.d/LIT-1648.md), Unavailable), and the
  defeat condition names it as one place a counter could come from.
- **That the interpreter channel and the change of cover exhaust
  "reordering".** They are the line's nearest slots, not a reduction.
- **That act identity reduces to effects on receivers.** That is the
  question [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md) and [CLAIM-171](CLAIM-171.md) dispute, and this claim does not take a
  side in it.
