# ADR-059: A status is credence in the entry's own content: an ARG's is in its inference, within the limits its undercutters grant, and a conceded objection is recorded by revising its target, not by holding the objection

## Context

The worklist of [ADR-057](ADR-057.md) counted an Active ARG resting on a Proposed claim
as a credence inversion, which treats an ARG's status as a CLAIM's. Two
cases did not fit.

- **[ARG-008](../arguments.d/ARG-008.md)** is Active and rests on [CLAIM-029](../claims.d/CLAIM-029.md), Proposed. Its body says
  "Active: the inference holds as stated, to a testing ground".
- **[CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md)** was Active only because it was conceded: [ADR-032](ADR-032.md)'s
  convention, kept in the claims template, says "a conceded one went
  Active". Its evidence, [THEORY-893](../theory.d/THEORY-893.md), is Proposed. The owner ruled it
  Proposed.

The ten ARGs show how the status is already used:

- **[ARG-009](../arguments.d/ARG-009.md) is Proposed with every premise Active.** Its status cannot
  mean that the premises are held.
- **[ARG-008](../arguments.d/ARG-008.md) is Active over a Proposed premise.** So Active cannot mean
  that the premises are held either.
- **[ARG-003](../arguments.d/ARG-003.md), [ARG-006](../arguments.d/ARG-006.md) and [ARG-008](../arguments.d/ARG-008.md) are Active and have Active undercutters,**
  and every one of those undercutters is a granted limit on what the
  inference carries. [CLAIM-032](../claims.d/CLAIM-032.md), for example, says "the Dual State supplies
  one structural point and nothing more". An undercutter that is held, and
  that the ARG survives, narrows the inference. It does not defeat it.
- **[ARG-002](../arguments.d/ARG-002.md), [ARG-004](../arguments.d/ARG-004.md) and [ARG-010](../arguments.d/ARG-010.md) are Rejected,** each undercut outright.

## Decision

**Status is credence in the entry's own content, in every scheme.**

- **A CLAIM** asserts a proposition.
- **An ARG** asserts an inference: its premises, if held, carry its
  conclusion as stated. `undercut_by` claims that are held narrow the
  inference, and the ARG is Rejected only when one defeats it outright.
  - Active: the inference is accepted.
  - Proposed: the inference is offered and not yet accepted.
- **Soundness is derived.** An argument is sound when its inference is
  Active and every premise is Active. That is a fact about several
  entries, so it is computed and not filed ([DP-3](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-3)).

**What follows for the checks.**

- **An Active ARG on a Proposed premise is not an inversion.** It is an
  accepted inference waiting on a premise.
- **The check that does apply is a sound ARG whose conclusion is not
  held.** Either the conclusion should be promoted, or the inference
  carries less than the conclusion says, and an undercutter should say
  so. [ARG-006](../arguments.d/ARG-006.md) is such a case today. Its inference is Active, its one
  premise [CLAIM-373](../claims.d/CLAIM-373.md) is Active, and its conclusion [CLAIM-063](../claims.d/CLAIM-063.md) is Proposed.

**A conceded counter is recorded by revising its target.**

- Conceding an objection accepts its force against the target, and the
  target changes. [CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md) is [CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md)'s revision.
- The counter's status stays credence in the counter. Where its evidence
  is Proposed, so is the counter.
- The claims template's "a conceded one went Active" becomes "a conceded
  one is recorded by its target's revision, and keeps the status its
  evidence gives it".

## Alternatives considered

- **An ARG's status means its premises are held.** That duplicates the
  premises' own statuses, which is a projection that drifts ([DP-3](../../docs/design-principles.md#dp-3)), and it
  contradicts [ARG-009](../arguments.d/ARG-009.md) and [ARG-008](../arguments.d/ARG-008.md) as filed.
- **An ARG's status means its conclusion is believed.** That duplicates
  the conclusion's status, and it cannot express an accepted inference from
  premises the record does not yet hold. That is what an ARG is for.
- **Keep "a conceded counter goes Active".** A concession is made in one
  exchange, about one target. Holding the counter Active lets it ground,
  corroborate or undercut anything that cites it, with credence its
  evidence does not give. That is the spill-over the owner named.
- **A separate field for concession.** The target's revision is already
  the record of what was conceded. A field on the counter would be a second
  copy of it.

## Consequences

- **Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-28** ("accept ADR-059").
- **The worklist** no longer counts an ARG's premises as inversions. It
  lists sound ARGs whose conclusion is not held. An Active counter whose own
  evidence is not held is an ordinary credence inversion on a CLAIM, and
  the existing check already lists it.
- **The claims template's convention line** now reads: a conceded counter
  is recorded by its target's revision, and keeps the status its evidence
  gives it.
- **No ARG's status changes.** ARG-008 is an accepted inference waiting on
  a premise, and no longer an inversion. ARG-006 is the one sound argument
  whose conclusion is not held, and it goes to the next status review.
