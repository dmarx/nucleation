# CLAIM-1045: Migration is an endogenous slow collective variable that presses harder than a predator–prey cycle: a route is followed by members as the group's route and regenerated by being followed, and where it is learned socially it is held as the group's own, so it may meet even the narrowed criterion of a variable members address as the whole's, leaving no difference in kind between an integrated plurality and an ecosystem of migrants

## The claim

A predator–prey cycle meets [CLAIM-327](CLAIM-327.md)'s three conditions, but no member
carries it: it exists only in the coupled populations ([CLAIM-1168](CLAIM-1168.md)). A
migration route is held differently. Members follow it as the group's route;
following it regenerates it (the trail, the stopovers, the timing); and where
it is learned from conspecifics rather than inherited, it is passed on as
the group's, and can be lost with the group's knowledge. That is close to
what [CLAIM-1126](CLAIM-1126.md) required of an integrated plurality: a whole-level
variable its members address as the whole's own.

If so, the narrowed structural answer does not separate a plurality of
pseudo-selves from an ecosystem of migrants either, and the question
([QUESTION-521](../questions.d/QUESTION-521.md)) leans to [CLAIM-1153](CLAIM-1153.md): the difference is in the regime
of analysis, or there is none in kind.

Migration also reshapes what drives it: grazing, nutrient transport and
trampling change the landscape the next migration reads, a case of the
reciprocal causation niche construction describes ([THEORY-469](../theory.d/THEORY-469.md), [THEORY-446](../theory.d/THEORY-446.md)),
so the line between driver and regenerated variable is itself crossed over
generations.

## Its premise, not held

That migration routes are socially learned and regenerated by use is the
assistant's general knowledge, not a reading the record holds. [LEAD-233](../leads.d/LEAD-233.md)
would read for it. Until then the claim is conditional on it.

## What it does not say

- Not that every migration is held as the group's: one fixed by inheritance,
  or by gradients alone, is a driver or an individual trait.
- Not that following is addressing: if [CLAIM-1126](CLAIM-1126.md)'s condition requires
  representing the route as the group's, following may fall short; that is
  the defeater's second clause.
