# NOTE-tmpr5h7q: Schlager and Ostrom — Property-rights regimes and natural resources

## Contribution

Unbundles "common property" into five rights at two levels and four
positions, separating the exercise of a right from authority over the rules
that define rights, and adds de facto rights as an empirical category.

## Key results

- **Rules and rights** (§II, p. 250): "'rights' are the product of 'rules'
  and thus not equivalent to rules"; "A property right is the authority to
  undertake particular actions related to a specific domain"; "all rights
  have complementary duties."
- **Levels** (p. 250, n. 2): "Constitutional-choice actions entail devising
  collective-choice rules."
- **The five rights** (pp. 250–51): access, "The right to enter a defined
  physical property"; withdrawal, "The right to obtain the 'products' of a
  resource"; management, "The right to regulate internal use patterns and
  transform the resource by making improvements"; exclusion, "The right to
  determine who will have an access right, and how that right may be
  transferred"; alienation, "The right to sell or lease either or both of
  the above collective-choice rights."
- **Exercise against definition** (p. 251): "It is the difference between
  exercising a right and participating in the definition of future rights
  to be exercised." "The authority to devise future operational-level
  rights is what makes collective-choice rights so powerful."
- **Independence** (p. 252): "The five property rights are independent of
  one another but, in relation to fisheries, are frequently held in the
  cumulative manner arrayed in Table 1"; "It is possible ... to have
  management rights without exclusion rights". Authorized users "lack the
  authority to devise their own harvesting rules or to exclude others".
- **De facto rights** (§III, n. 13): "Only if de facto rights are challenged
  do the differences between the two classes of rights become apparent."
- **Legitimacy** (§IV, p. 257): "Since authorized users do not design the
  rules they are expected to follow, they are less likely to agree to the
  necessity and legitimacy of the rules."
- **Comparison** (§VI, p. 260): "No real-world institution can win in a
  contest against idealized institutions."

## Assumptions

Common-pool resources, mainly inshore fisheries (groundwater basins named
as a further domain, n. 18). Rights are rule-backed authorizations enforced
and accepted as legitimate. Alienation covers sale or lease of
collective-choice rights only.

## Concepts

Operational, collective-choice and constitutional levels; rules and
rights; access, withdrawal, management, exclusion, alienation; authorized
user, claimant, proprietor, owner; de jure and de facto rights.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmpcpijj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcpijj.md).** The structural premise: authority at one level does
  not carry authority at another (the rights are "independent of one
  another"; management without exclusion), and exercising a right is
  distinct from defining it. The paper does not discuss contesting
  decisions. [CLAIM-086](../claims.d/CLAIM-086.md) is the record's nearest own statement of the level
  separation.
- **[QUESTION-tmpgeqju](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpgeqju.md).** The constitutional level (n. 2) is where the
  question sits; the paper names positions but not which protections lie
  beyond revision.
- **[CASE-tmp9vd9x](../cases.d/CASE-tmp9vd9x.md).** In this vocabulary the recycler's owner holds
  exclusion and alienation over a complementary facility and bargains for
  management-level authority over the aquifer; the paper treats one
  resource's bundle, not leverage across resources.
- **[CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md).** Its use of the operational/collective-choice split
  (through A93 §2) is supported by the text.
- The exchange's A93 ("five kinds of rights") is faithful. A92's mapping
  onto "having a capability and governing the conditions of its exercise"
  is partly faithful: "governing the conditions" here means devising
  operational rules, not setting the terms of challenge; the paper has a
  third, constitutional level; and its rights are rule-made
  authorizations, not capabilities.
- The same three levels are [THEORY-1809](../theory.d/THEORY-1809.md)'s IAD framework (Polski and
  Ostrom); this paper is its property-rights application.

## Limitations

Fisheries evidence, mostly from secondary ethnographies; the Maine income
comparison is tentative (N = 27). The four positions assume the cumulative
pattern. The constitutional level gets one footnote.
