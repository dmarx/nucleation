# ADR-016: phenomenology joins the topics

## Context

Filing the self literature ([ADR-015](ADR-015.md)) brought in Zahavi's *Subjectivity and
Selfhood* and Gallagher's "Philosophical conceptions of the self". Their
subject, the self, has a word. Their method has none. The vocabulary had
words for subjects (consciousness, the self, cognition) but none for the
phenomenological way of studying them. Zahavi's reply to the no-self views
the record holds, Dennett's and Metzinger's, is a phenomenologist's reply,
and the record could not say so.

The record already held other works of this kind with nowhere to put them:

- a reading of Kaepernick through Merleau-Pontian phenomenology;
- a mixed-methods study coding the phenomenology of meditation experience;
- Dennett's *Consciousness Explained*, whose "method for phenomenology" is
  heterophenomenology;
- IIT 3.0, which derives its postulates from axioms about experience.

The filing agent proposed the word, and the owner accepted it.

## Decision

Add `phenomenology` to `topics`, in the `philosophy` group, after
`self-and-personhood`. Its blurb: "the first-person description of
experience as a method — the philosophical tradition (Husserl, Heidegger,
Merleau-Ponty), its use in the sciences of mind, and the theories that take
their data from it".

The word names a method, not a subject, so it is never expected to lead.
A work carries it when a reader browsing phenomenology would be right to
expect it there: because the work practises the method, or argues about it,
or builds a theory on its data.

**Tagged.** No tag was removed, and no first tag changed.

- **Second tag:** [LIT-tmppe4q2](../literature.d/LIT-tmppe4q2.md) (Zahavi, *Subjectivity and Selfhood*).
- **Last tag:**
  - [LIT-tmpeqp99](../literature.d/LIT-tmpeqp99.md) (Gallagher 2000, the minimal self);
  - [LIT-209](../literature.d/LIT-209.md) (Kaepernick, read through Merleau-Ponty);
  - [LIT-066](../literature.d/LIT-066.md) (the varieties of contemplative experience);
  - [LIT-443](../literature.d/LIT-443.md) (*Consciousness Explained*, heterophenomenology);
  - [LIT-445](../literature.d/LIT-445.md) (IIT 3.0, from the phenomenology to the mechanisms).

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave method unsaid.** Each of these works already has its subject
  tagged. But a reader who wants the phenomenological literature has no
  page to find it on, and a reply such as Zahavi's to Dennett is a reply
  *from* a method.
- **Stretch `consciousness` or `self-and-personhood`.** That is the
  nearest-wrong-word move the vocabulary rule forbids. Kaepernick and the
  meditation study are not about either subject in the sense those words
  hold.
- **A narrower word, `minimal-self`.** It names only Zahavi's and
  Gallagher's thesis, which `self-and-personhood` already holds.

## Consequences

Metzinger's self-model theory engages phenomenology closely but is not a
work of the method, and was left untagged; a reading of *Being No One*
may change that. Future filings of Husserl, Merleau-Ponty, Varela's
neurophenomenology or Thompson's *Mind in Life* have a word waiting.
