# THEORY-tmpcjdte: In Niemiec, Ryan and Deci's post-college sample, attaining intrinsic aspirations went with more well-being and less ill-being, and attaining extrinsic aspirations, entered alongside it, added nothing to well-being and went with more ill-being; the extrinsic null is a unique-variance result, since alone extrinsic attainment correlated positively with well-being and need satisfaction

## Source

Niemiec, Ryan and Deci (2009), [LIT-tmp99221](../literature.d/LIT-tmp99221.md), read in [NOTE-tmpmgw5i](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpmgw5i.md) from the
PubMed Central author manuscript PMC2736104.

## What was actually shown

**The sample and design.** 147 graduates of two US colleges answered at one
and two years after graduation. Aspiration importance (Time 1), attainment
(both times), basic need satisfaction, and well-being (life satisfaction,
self-esteem, positive affect) and ill-being (anxiety, physical symptoms,
negative affect) were all self-reported.

**At Time 2**, with intrinsic and extrinsic attainment as joint predictors,
intrinsic attainment predicted well-being β = .77 and ill-being β = −.66,
and extrinsic attainment predicted well-being β = .00 and ill-being β = .38
(p < .01). Extrinsic attainment could have predicted well-being uniquely;
it did not.

**Over the year**, residualized change in intrinsic attainment predicted
change in well-being (β = .63) and ill-being (β = −.59), and change in need
satisfaction carried most of it: the well-being path fell to .16 (ns), the
ill-being path to −.42. Extrinsic attainment change predicted neither need
satisfaction (β = .03) nor well-being change (β = −.02), and ill-being
change only marginally (β = .27, p < .07).

**The zero-order picture differs.** Extrinsic attainment correlated with
well-being at mean r = .19, with need satisfaction at mean r = .18 and with
ill-being at mean r = .03. The authors name competition for variance with
the more strongly related intrinsic attainment as one explanation.

## What this does not say

- **That getting rich or famous does not make people happier.** Alone,
  extrinsic attainment went with slightly more well-being. The result is
  that it adds nothing once intrinsic attainment is known.
- **That need satisfaction causes the well-being.** Mediator and outcome
  are self-reports over the same interval, and need satisfaction had no
  rank-order stability across the year (β = −.09).
- **That intrinsic aspirations are identified independently of the needs.**
  They are labelled intrinsic because they are taken to satisfy the needs,
  so the mediation tests a link the classification presumes
  ([QUESTION-tmpee04a](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpee04a.md)).
- **Anything about need frustration or psychopathology.**
