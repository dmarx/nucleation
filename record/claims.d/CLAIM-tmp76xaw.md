# CLAIM-tmp76xaw: What an organization's filtering treats as noise is itself governed: if objections to its established categories are classed as high-frequency disturbance, stabilization that succeeds against its current classification of noise can fail against the demands it exists to answer, so governing the distinction between demand and disturbance is a higher-order task, and the capacity to recognize a demand as demand-worthy is a slow capability that must stay open to correction

## The claim

A44 §3: "Suppose a regulatory institution treats objections to its
established industrial categories as high-frequency disturbances. ...
some objections are evidence that the consensus is wrong. Then
successful stabilization against the institution's *current
classification* of noise can amount to failure against the underlying
public demands." And: "the process deciding what merits a response has
itself become an entrenched organizational mode."

A44 §7: the filtering architecture "determines which signals can enter
those categories in the first place", and "The ability to recognize a
demand as demand-worthy is itself a slow organizational capability that
must remain open to correction."

## Against the record

- Suppressing a demand is not satisfying it: [CLAIM-361](CLAIM-361.md).
- Admission, recognition, credibility, uptake and revision are distinct:
  [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md).
- Lock-in from selectively admitted evidence: [CLAIM-466](CLAIM-466.md).
- A coarse-graining adequate under ordinary dynamics can lose what an
  intervention would reveal: [CLAIM-407](CLAIM-407.md), which A44 cites. That is why the
  architecture must be tested by interventions, not ordinary operation.

## Admission ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md))

*A separating case.* Take an institution whose admission of evidence is
unbiased ([CLAIM-466](CLAIM-466.md)'s remedy applied) and whose stages of uptake are all
in place ([CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md) satisfied). Suppose its filter treats a whole class of
signal, objections to a category, as transient because of the rate at
which they arrive and not because of their content. The premises hold,
and yet the demands behind the objections are never answered. What the
claim adds is that the classification of signal against noise is a
variable of the institution's temporal organization, and is not fixed by
its admission rules.
