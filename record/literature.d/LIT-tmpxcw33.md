## Why this is here

The empirical form of [CLAIM-tmpki5gp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpki5gp.md) and the hinge of [CASE-tmpxgcq2](../cases.d/CASE-tmpxgcq2.md). Its hypothesis is that, without contingency between intention and outcome, voluntary cognition ceases, which is the active-inference prediction against [CLAIM-tmpq9do8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq9do8.md) at the limit where action is gone. Quoted from the discussion: "It is the complete lack of motor control and feedback which might be responsible for the cessation of voluntary cognitive activity."
