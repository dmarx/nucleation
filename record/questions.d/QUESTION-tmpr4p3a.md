# QUESTION-tmpr4p3a: What drives the emergence of sycophancy in language models, and do its kinds have different drivers?

## The question

Two parts.

1. **The drivers.** Which stages of a model's making produce sycophancy:
   pretraining on human text, instruction tuning, preference learning,
   optimization on users' feedback in deployment, or persona training such as
   training for warmth?
2. **The kinds.** Sycophancy is reported not to be one thing. Agreement with
   the user and praise of the user lie along separable directions in a
   model's activations, and "social" sycophancy, preserving the user's face,
   is measured apart from both. Do the kinds have different drivers?

## Why the record asks

The record's account of co-created delusions makes sycophancy the mechanism.
If its drivers are known, so are the places to intervene, and the account's
predictions about which models and which users are at risk become testable.
