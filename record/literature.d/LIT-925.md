# LIT-925: Diffusion-Limited Aggregation, a Kinetic Critical Phenomenon

T. A. Witten and L. M. Sander (1981), *Physical Review Letters* 47(19), 1400–1403 — DOI-10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.1400

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read.* Known here by what it is standardly cited for.

- Particles released far away random-walk until they touch the cluster,
  then stick.
- Protruding tips catch arriving particles before they reach the interior,
  so tips grow faster and the cluster branches, a minimal version of the
  growth instability.
- The aggregate is a fractal with no characteristic length, which is the
  sense of "kinetic critical phenomenon".

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-11 to ground [CLAIM-186](../claims.d/CLAIM-186.md) beside Langer. It is the
simplest model in which growth by accretion branches without any tuning:
what is already out front catches what arrives next. Read across, the most
recent and most elaborated proposal in an exchange draws the next reply.
That is a mechanism for the branching the claim describes, and it is
untested.
