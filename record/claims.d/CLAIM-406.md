# CLAIM-406: Structural, pre-emptive testimonial injustice has the form of the record's trust loop: an arrangement that decides whose word is sought can keep an early exclusion going through the indicators of competence that only being sought produces, with no continuing prejudiced act; but the loop alone cannot tell perpetuated privilege from merited expertise, because that needs the prejudice condition the dynamics do not contain

## The claim

The owner, at U24 of the exchange of 2026-10-20, quoting A23: "> The
deeper thesis is that the organization of recognition and the
organization of practical authority can become coupled, self-maintaining
processes. / this seems to me reminiscent of themes in feminist
philosophy, e.g. perpetuation of privilege". A24: "feminist epistemology
supplies one of the clearest existing articulations of the phenomenon
we've been reconstructing", and "Our contribution would be to connect
those accounts ... not to rediscover epistemic injustice under new
terminology."

**The loop is held.** [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md): one chain is good policy, then
endorsement, then greater influence, then more chances to show competence;
the other is early advantage, then greater influence, then more
visibility, then greater endorsement. "This is how a system might develop
both well-calibrated expertise and undeserved authority." Its note of
2026-10-18 runs it from the side that loses: "Low exposure → Poor
competence estimates → Low endorsement → Even lower exposure". [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md)
(Active, demonstrated) is its linear core: interpretation shaping
authority and authority shaping interpretation destabilize symmetry
exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ).

**The case is held.** [THEORY-327](../theory.d/THEORY-327.md), from Fricker 2017 following Anderson:
"a list of expert witnesses that no one has refreshed for a good while,
so that (through no one's fault, let us add) the same old white male
witnesses tend to be drawn on". The others "are effectively silenced".
"Their views are not sought, and this is owing to a structural prejudice
(the un-refreshed list)."

**The claim.** The list is the loop's state. Who is on it decides who is
consulted, and being consulted produces the credentials, citations and
visibility by which the list would be refreshed. A24 §3 gave the chain,
"Prior institutional standing → Opportunities to contribute and be
recognized → Visible indicators of expertise → Future institutional
standing", and the point that makes it self-concealing: "**a measure of
demonstrated competence can partly reflect the history of access to
opportunities for demonstrating competence**." So the arrangement can
keep an exclusion going "without requiring a continuously acting
discriminator" (A24), and the institution's own evidence appears to
justify it. A26 §3: "**Institutional recognition can depend on evidence of
prior recognition.**"

**What the loop cannot do.** The same dynamics entrench an earned lead and
an unearned one. A24: "Our mathematical instability condition in [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md)
doesn't determine whether an emergent authority difference represents
deserved expertise, legitimate specialization, or the perpetuation of
privilege." [THEORY-327](../theory.d/THEORY-327.md)'s condition is the missing premise: "An omission
from a list for reasons that track no misjudgement of the omitted is not
covered." That is why the claim is interpretive, and why A24 called its
own version "a **proposed general organizational mechanism**, not a newly
demonstrated theorem". [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md)'s demonstration is not inherited.

## Where it bears

- **[QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md)**, whether a self-reproducing authority structure can be
  pathological by a standard internal to the organization. A26: "The
  mechanism itself is not inherently unjust. A normative conclusion
  requires establishing relevant prejudice, wrongful exclusion, or another
  justified standard of unfairness."
- **[CLAIM-276](CLAIM-276.md)**: a collective interpretation's stability "is no evidence
  of its truth".
- **[THEORY-145](../theory.d/THEORY-145.md)** (Argyris): A24 §4's "the mechanisms determining what it
  learns are themselves insulated from certain kinds of correction" is
  single-loop learning, with the insulation sourced in social position
  rather than defensive routine.
- **[THEORY-151](../theory.d/THEORY-151.md)** (Longino): the correction is uptake and tempered equality
  of intellectual authority, which [CLAIM-403](CLAIM-403.md) separates from access.
- **[QUESTION-072](../questions.d/QUESTION-072.md)**, which asks the same of the record's own contributors.
  A24: "our terminology registry and filing procedures are themselves
  legibility and authority-allocation mechanisms." Until this claim,
  [THEORY-317](../theory.d/THEORY-317.md), [THEORY-326](../theory.d/THEORY-326.md) and [THEORY-327](../theory.d/THEORY-327.md) were cited only from that
  question, and [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md)'s pointer to it was the one bridge to the
  distributed-agency line.
- **Collins 1986** ([LIT-1312](../literature.d/LIT-1312.md), [NOTE-1042](../notes.d/NOTE-1042.md), read in an authorized
  Portuguese translation): the mechanism, with the prejudice condition
  built in, predates this claim's sources in that tradition. Denying a
  group literacy and then citing its lack of facts as proof of poor
  judgement is, in the translation, "atribuir a um grupo um status
  inferior e depois usar esse status inferior como prova da inferioridade
  do grupo" (assign a group an inferior status and then use it as proof of
  the group's inferiority; the gloss is the record's). The status is
  assigned by domination, so the prejudice the loop lacks is there from
  the start.
- **Espeland and Sauder** ([NOTE-1050](../notes.d/NOTE-1050.md)): standing that brings resources
  and reputation is produced by having had them, with no continuing
  prejudiced act ("It's a self-fulfilling nightmare", of the third tier;
  [THEORY-854](../theory.d/THEORY-854.md)). They call the initial differences "often largely a
  product of measurement noise", on cited evidence: the loop entrenching
  what was neither earned nor prejudiced, which is the claim's point that
  the dynamics cannot tell the cases apart.
- **Kay, Kasirzadeh and Mohamed** ([NOTE-1049](../notes.d/NOTE-1049.md)) assert the loop for
  generative models, "a feedback loop where dominant narratives are
  amplified and marginalized voices are further silenced", with no
  condition ([THEORY-864](../theory.d/THEORY-864.md), Proposed). New in it: the loop can run
  through credibility given to a system, with no hearer judging the
  silenced speaker. The prejudice is carried by training data, so the
  claim's limit stands.

## What it does not say

- **That any actual roster, field or institution perpetuates privilege.**
  It names a possible mechanism. A24: "This is a possible structural
  mechanism, not proof that any particular merit-based selection process
  is unfair", and "not every inherited expert roster is unjust."
- **That every self-reproducing authority is privilege.** That is
  [QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md), open.
- **That the dynamics supply the normative criterion.** A24: "Feminist
  social epistemology provides normative and historical distinctions that
  the dynamical model alone lacks."
- **That the record's own filing is such a loop.** [QUESTION-072](../questions.d/QUESTION-072.md) is open,
  and needs a prejudice, not only a grammar ([NOTE-853](../notes.d/NOTE-853.md)).
- **Anything about hermeneutical injustice beyond [THEORY-326](../theory.d/THEORY-326.md)**, or about
  Pohlhaus's willful hermeneutical ignorance and Dotson's epistemic
  oppression, which A24 drew from them. Both works were sought on
  2026-10-21 and are held Unavailable ([LIT-1311](../literature.d/LIT-1311.md), [LIT-1329](../literature.d/LIT-1329.md)). Kay et
  al. gloss Pohlhaus's term secondhand ("the continued misunderstanding or
  misinterpretation of marginalized experiences despite their
  articulation"), which is not a reading of Pohlhaus.
