## Why this is here

A42 of the exchange cited it for the owner's U42 idea of carrying ACT and DBT to the organizational scale, and said "I would add both works to the record before claiming novelty for an organizational-flexibility interpretation of ACT" (the other is Bond's Organizational Flexibility Model, not identified). Its thesis that flexibility is propagated through leverage over consequences is close to [CLAIM-tmp6ap9y](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6ap9y.md) (access to a signal, its authority over action and action on it are separable; overcontrol is a maladaptive regime). A reading should come before that claim is asserted as new. The record holds the individual-scale account in [LIT-553](LIT-553.md) (Kashdan & Rottenberg, read).
