# CLAIM-tmpqofon: Authenticity is an organizational achievement marked by non-domination of an agent's subcomponents: higher-level processes need not impose the outcome because the lower-level ones already support it or settle their disagreement among themselves, and a process that rarely decides keeps access to the roles in which it is consequential

## The claim

A44 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 corrected "the simplistic assumption
that authenticity reduces effort": "**An authentically maintained goal may
demand more effort, not less.**" The owner, at U45:

> the record may not characterize it this way precisely, but I think what
> I was trying to elucidate was that 'authenticity' should be treated as
> an organizational achievement characterized by non-domination of
> subcomponents.

And:

> I've been using 'authenticity' and 'mindfulness' to try to describe
> states where higher level processes don't need to impose themselves on
> lower level processes because the lower level processes are already
> aligned with the outcomes the higher level processes would otherwise
> enforce at the cost of additional effort (i.e. because the lower level
> processes would resist)

A45 granted the correction: "I think this is an important correction to
our developing theory. You're using **authenticity** to describe an
organizational achievement in which constituent processes participate in
producing a coherent course of action, rather than having that course
repeatedly imposed on them by a higher-order controller." It is a
correction of the framing, not of A44's point about effort: authenticity
was never meant as low effort, but as a way the organization achieves its
course. A45: "authenticity isn't simply how closely behavior matches an
endorsed ideal. It concerns **how the organization achieves that
correspondence**."

A45's formulation:

> **Organizational authenticity is a form of integration in which the
> effective commitments of the whole arise through maintained,
> non-dominating relations among its constituent processes, such that
> their contributions to collective action are accommodated, negotiated,
> and revisable rather than ordinarily overridden by unilateral
> higher-level control.**

## What non-domination is

- **Not equal influence.** A45: "Non-domination concerns whether one
  process or level possesses *unaccountable, effectively arbitrary
  control* over the others, not whether every process contributes equally
  to every decision."
- **Not a ban on override.** A45: "It also shouldn't prohibit warranted
  override. Genuine emergencies, irreconcilable demands, and binding
  constraints may require supervisory intervention." What matters is
  whether the intervention "is bounded, accountable, and capable of giving
  way to stable cooperation when the conditions for cooperation exist."
- **Continued access to consequential roles.** A46, at the owner's U46 on
  niche specialization: "Your proposed non-domination criterion may be
  better understood as requiring **continued access to consequential
  roles**, not uniform access to every decision." A rarely decisive
  critic can remain "an indispensable member of the governing
  organization because its influence increases when anomalous evidence
  appears". Its test is counterfactual: "When the conditions under which
  a specialist is useful arise, can that specialist recover effective
  influence, even after a long period in which its contributions were
  unnecessary?" The loop that defeats it, a rare specialist losing
  exposure and so influence, is filed on [CLAIM-277](CLAIM-277.md).

## Where it sits in the record

The authenticity line ([TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md), [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md), [QUESTION-011](../questions.d/QUESTION-011.md)) was set aside at
the audit of 2026-10-05: "ownership criteria remain insufficiently
defended". [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md) says a will is the agent's own to the degree its
commitments "arise through, remain integrated with, and are answerable
to" its constitutive organization. It never said what "integrated"
requires. This claim is a candidate answer: integration is
non-domination of the processes that carry the commitment. It is an
alternative reading of [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md)'s middle condition, not a premise of
it, and it does not rest on [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md). [THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md)'s graded integration,
already among [CLAIM-016](CLAIM-016.md)'s grounds, is the nearest reading.

The owner tied the criterion to an economy: where the lower processes
already support the outcome, the higher ones need not spend effort
imposing it. That economy is [CLAIM-tmpmr1t8](CLAIM-tmpmr1t8.md), filed on the
will-organization line on its own, because it holds or fails apart from
any account of authenticity.

## What it does not say

- **That low effort marks authenticity.** A45: "That doesn't imply
  authentic action is always effortless." The owner's resistance is the
  cost of imposition, not the cost of the work.
- **That non-domination can be read from low supervisory cost.** A45: "A
  quiet subordinate process isn't necessarily a satisfied one." That
  objection is [CLAIM-tmptg7io](CLAIM-tmptg7io.md), and it is open.
- **That it settles the manipulation objection.** A45: "Your
  non-domination proposal gives us a better mechanism to investigate, but
  it doesn't yet settle the manipulation objection." It proposed
  "**non-dominating integration as a candidate constituent of
  authenticity**", not a definition. [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) and [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) stand.
- **That an agent who overrides is not the author of the act.** [CLAIM-261](CLAIM-261.md)
  holds that a divided agent who keeps to the diet acts, "and the act is
  theirs". This claim grades authenticity; it does not take the act away.
- **That subprocesses have standing.** A45: "We are proposing an
  organizational criterion, not asserting rights for neural modules."
- **That it is republican non-domination.** The word recalls political
  theory's, which the record holds no reading of; neither the owner nor
  A45 cited it.
