It is the programme's fullest and most careful formal statement, and it carries the learning theory of [LIT-374](LIT-374.md) §3 with the assumptions in the body. Its routine results hold, and its bouquet slip is repairable. What would settle it: - a theorem that the CS count for a learned predictor equals w(P; γ, δ), not w_G(P); - a K-sweep on benchmarks of known width, showing the floor at K < w for routed learners, and testing whether a capacity-matched monolithic model shows it; - the decoupled-versus-joint training comparison it motivates but does not run.
