# ADR-027: loss-landscapes joins the topics

## Context

The owner asked for 33 readings to be filed here. Most are about the
geometry of trained solutions in weight space:

- mode connectivity, both linear and along curves;
- the permutation symmetries behind barriers between minima, and Git
  Re-Basin;
- star domains and connecting simplexes;
- mode connectivity in generative models, GNNs, transformers and
  mixtures of experts;
- model merging (TIES, ZipIt!, and weight-space regions of fine-tuned
  models).

The rest are on statistical-mechanics and random-matrix accounts of
generalization and double descent, and on where in a network
generalization is carried.

The anthology holds two of the set: Git Re-Basin and Entezari et al. on
permutation invariance. This record held none of it.

No topic word fits. `information-geometry` is the Fisher metric and the
curvature of statistical models, not the arrangement of minima.
`learning-theory` is why learning generalizes, which only some of the
batch addresses.

## Decision

Add **`loss-landscapes`**: the geometry of trained solutions in weight
space. It covers:

- mode connectivity, linear and along curves;
- barriers and the permutation symmetries that hide or remove them;
- re-basin alignment;
- star domains and connecting simplexes;
- local versus global landscape structure;
- model merging, as the practical use of the above.

The works on double descent and statistical-mechanics generalization take
`learning-theory`, and also `loss-landscapes` where they are about the
landscape.

Every work in the batch is a machine-learning work that an anthology topic
could hold, so each carries `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](ADR-005.md)).
The two the anthology holds are filed here as [ADR-013](ADR-013.md) second readings, and
each pair names the other.

## Alternatives considered

- **`information-geometry` or `learning-theory`.** Either would then hold
  two subjects, and a 33-paper line would be invisible as one.
- **A separate `model-merging` word.** Merging in this batch is argued
  from connectivity: weights can be averaged because the minima lie in one
  basin. It is a use of the landscape, not a separate subject, so it stays
  under the one word until a merging work arrives that does not rest on
  connectivity.
