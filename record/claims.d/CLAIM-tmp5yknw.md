# CLAIM-tmp5yknw: A translation's quality is the number of formal and semantic constraints it preserves

## The claim

A4: "He begins with something close to: quality ≈ number of preserved
constraints, and discovers that this doesn't work either." Its weighted form,
also A4: translating "is selecting a point on a Pareto frontier", with
T* = argmax [w_s S + w_f F + w_r R + w_t Tn + w_p P].

## How it was answered

By [CASE-tmpbg7r1](../cases.d/CASE-tmpbg7r1.md): a rendering that broke most of the constraints and was judged
perhaps the best. A4: "So even Hofstadter's own carefully articulated notion of
fidelity gets revised through the experiment."

## What survives

A distant descendant: the manuscript's weighted functional L = L_obs + λL_str +
γL_dec, with "no universal choice of weights or observational tasks" (§6). That
keeps the weighting and drops the count. The record holds Hofstadter's book
([LIT-766](../literature.d/LIT-766.md)) unread, so this stands on A4's report.
