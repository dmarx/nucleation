# QUESTION-tmpzj3tb: Can a pragmatic frame be induced from examples of communicative behaviour, and does the induced frame transfer across different wordings?

## Why it is a question

A44 §3C: "A prompt can establish a communicative convention without
necessarily conveying accurate propositional examples of the task. That
resembles what we've been calling a delivery frame." Then: "Can a pragmatic
frame be induced through examples of communicative behavior, and can that
induced frame be transferred across different linguistic realizations?" A48
§4.4–4.5 and A52 §8.4 kept it.

## What would count as an answer

A design in which demonstrations with a stance (casual, affectionate) but no
stated proposition shift the interpretation of a new utterance, and the shift
survives paraphrase. It would show a frame ([TERM-tmpu9tab](../terms.d/TERM-tmpu9tab.md)) being inferred ([TERM-tmpa0fi1](../terms.d/TERM-tmpa0fi1.md))
rather than told. It is the U17 claim ([CLAIM-tmp98mw2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp98mw2.md)) at its most testable.
