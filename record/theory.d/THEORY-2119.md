# THEORY-2119: Minimal theory of mind can't track identity

## The account

An agent can predict others' actions from the objects they have
encountered and the locations at which they last encountered them, without
representing beliefs with content. This suffices for location false-belief
tasks and fails, as a signature, on identity and quantity, since
registrations cannot embed.

## Evidence

Philosophical construction with predicted signature limits.
