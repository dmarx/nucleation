## 1. Core concepts (TERMs)

| Code | Status | Definition (exact or close to the entry's words) |
|---|---|---|
| TERM-023 | Active | **will-organization**: "the maintained organization of relations governing the conditions under which an agent's available action-generating regimes acquire, retain, lose, or alter practical efficacy". It is agent-indexed and "Functional, not additive: not another desire, an inner executive, or a causal substance". Its dimensions are the efficacy relations (accessibility, precedence, persistence) and the meta-regulatory ones (TERM-017, "plus transformation"). |
| TERM-099 | Active | **self-formation, of an agent**: "the production, by the agent's own activity on a discrepancy it cannot yet resolve, of a maintained capacity, a new distinction it can make legible or a new channel by which it can act, that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later ones". It refines TERM-023. |
| TERM-100 | Active | **provenance**, in three senses: *formation* ("whose process produced it"), *maintenance* ("whose process keeps it in being now") and *source* ("what it was made from"). "The first two come apart" (CASE-191). |
| TERM-017 | Active | **precedence, jurisdiction and suspension**, the meta-regulatory relations among regimes. |
| TERM-020 | Active | **protective lock-in**: a disturbance activates a protective regime, the regime reorganizes ordinary activity, that "generates secondary disturbances or suppresses restorative processes", those effects support continued activation, and "continued activation further reduces the practical capacity to leave or revise it". It is not "a mistake in the initial response", and not "inertia or habit". |
| TERM-064 | Active | **legibility**, of a distinction to an observer for a task: whether the observer, "with the rules, categories and resources it actually has, can recover the distinction" and bring it to bear. |
| TERM-085 | Active | **correctability**, of an arrangement for a corrective objective: "the chance that the arrangement ... finds, authorizes, coordinates and carries out a plan achieving the objective before the deadline and within its resources". |
| TERM-070 | Active | **organizational closure**, as closure of constraints (Montévil and Mossio): each constraint depends on another in the set and has another depending on it. |
| TERM-089 | Active | **apparatus**, operationally: "a realized organization that selectively couples differences in one system to distinguishable outcomes in another"; it may "differentiate, retain, enforce and revise, and the four come apart". |
| TERM-115 | Active | **scenario-conditioned response readiness**: "the probability that an organization operating under a specified policy can deliver an effective response to a specified contingency within its deadline". "It is defined under intervention, so a record of what was done does not measure it" (SYNTHESIS-027). |
| TERM-105 | Active | **resource constraint, by type**: "a fixed stock, an instantaneous capacity, a rate limit, a cumulative expenditure, a replenishable reserve and a deadline, which bound different functions of a path and are not one budget". |
| TERM-106 | Active | **reconfiguration slack**: "the temporary resources a system needs to move between two configurations it can each hold, beyond what holding either one requires". In SYNTHESIS-024's quotation of A: "Slack isn't merely unused capacity held in reserve against disturbances." |
| TERM-109 | Active | **provisional commitment**: "the costly allocation of a capability to a possible future need before that need is established, under a policy that permits subsequent release or redirection". |
| TERM-107 | Proposed | **organizational slack**: resources beyond what current output requires, "some useful and some wasteful, which the output alone does not tell apart". |

### 2.1 Self-formation and the transformation capacity

- **CLAIM-025**: Active; interpretive; thesis. "An operative regime, the
  governing organization among regimes, and the capacity to transform that
  organization can persist or fail separately." It quotes the will paper: an
  organization "may become increasingly competent at operating inside the
  emergency regime while becoming less capable of changing the organization
  that keeps the regime in place". *Grounds:* CASE-036, CASE-111, CASE-112.
  Rests on no claim. It was promoted on 2026-10-27 as an existence claim
  shown in two constructions: CASE-111's fold and CASE-112's tilted
  bistability (its note of 2026-10-27). Its note of 2026-11-05 finds that in
  the physical reconfigurable-matter readings "the capacity to transform
  comes from outside, a robot, rig or supervisor".
- **CLAIM-532**: Active; interpretive; thesis; version 5. Title: "An
  unresolved discrepancy is an occasion for self-formation, the
  transformation dimension of a will-organization, and not a guarantee of
  it; it suffices for self-formation that the agent's own activity on such
  a discrepancy produce a maintained capacity, either a new distinction,
  its policy coming to depend on a feature of its situation it did not
  depend on, or a new channel, the outcomes it can reach under its own
  policy growing, that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later
  ones", shown by transfer and by "interventions on the processes that
  produce the capacity while it forms". *Rests on:* CLAIM-025, CLAIM-347,
  CLAIM-522, CLAIM-540, CLAIM-535. *Uses:* TERM-099, TERM-100, TERM-023,
  TERM-064. Main points:
  - *Occasion, not guarantee.* "An agent may stay stuck on a discrepancy, or
    settle it another way" (CLAIM-532).
  - *The products are defined by behaviour.* A new distinction refines the
    agent's partition of situations, which "by CLAIM-535 is what a policy
    needs where acceptable actions differ". A new channel enlarges the
    outcomes reachable under its own policy. Either "enlarges what is
    co-satisfiable, which is relief in CLAIM-347's sense, not suppression"
    (CLAIM-532).
  - *Why behaviour, not mechanism.* "If the components of a frozen random
    projection are counted, every change a learned readout makes is a
    reweighting of them, yet the readout can come to separate inputs it did
    not" (CLAIM-532). This was the owner's observation that led to the
    restatement of 2026-11-18 (CLAIM-532 history, version 5).
  - *Maintained.* Persistence "needs a productive cycle that keeps it"
    (CLAIM-516, CASE-189, cited in CLAIM-532).
  - *Two tests.* The first is transfer: remove the inducing conditions and
    present a new discrepancy of the same class. The second is intervention
    on the producing processes, which by CLAIM-522 alone separates own
    formation from outside upkeep. By CLAIM-540, "the change must be in
    what selects, not in an observer's estimate of it" (CLAIM-532). After
    CASE-191 the second test means intervention *while the capacity forms*
    (CLAIM-532).
  - *Admission.* The separating case is a uniform change of precedence that
    refines no distinction and enlarges no reachable set. Every premise
    holds and the claim's condition is not met. As a sufficient condition
    the claim "does not cover it either way", and the case is QUESTION-488
    (CLAIM-532).
  - *Defeater.* A system that meets every condition and that the record
    "would nonetheless not count as self-formation", for instance a
    capacity "in substance supplied from outside by a route that
    intervention does not reach" (CLAIM-532).
  - *Disclaimers.* It is non-libertarian and sets Kane's ultimate
    responsibility aside (CLAIM-022). The steps from discrepancy to revised
    governance "are not a sequence" (CLAIM-474, cited in CLAIM-532).
  - *Note of 2026-11-07.* An organization "can overcome a limitation by
    changing what it can do, not only by improving what it can see" (A58,
    quoted in CLAIM-532), which is the new channel. CASE-194 and ARG-060
    support the two routes as separate repairs.
- **CLAIM-540**: Active; demonstrated; *disclaimed*. "The persistence a
  Dirichlet–categorical hierarchy gives a run of selections is an observer's
  learning about a fixed mixture, not a change in what selects." With
  θ ~ Beta(1,1), P(Z₂=1 | Z₁=1) = 2/3, while P(Z₂=1 | do(Z₁=1)) = 1/2.
  Self-formation "needs the mixture itself to change because of the choice",

  A13, quoted in the summary: "Self-formation requires a causal update, not
  merely a posterior update in the observer."
- **CLAIM-522**: Active; demonstrated; thesis (lines distributed-agency and
  formal-apparatus). "Recovery under interventions on state cannot show where
  maintenance is realized." System A has ċ = P(c) − D(c). System B has
  ċ = −D(c) + u_ext with u_ext = P(c) supplied from outside as state
  feedback. They have "the same trajectory under every perturbation of

- **CLAIM-516**: Active; demonstrated. Where every constraint capacity turns

### 2.2 Maintaining the capacity to govern

- **CLAIM-079**: Active; demonstrated. "Stability does not entail viability:
  a system can occupy a stable regime while losing access to trajectories
  that keep satisfying its constraints" (CLAIM-079). "A narrow path can be
  viable. A large option set can be nonviable" (CLAIM-079).
- **CLAIM-099**: Active; interpretive. "Maintaining a regime of effective
  action and maintaining the capacity to govern among regimes are not the
  same achievement" (CLAIM-099). ARG-007 (Active) concludes it from
  CLAIM-079 and CLAIM-025 on CASE-036.
- **CLAIM-018**: Active; interpretive. Canguilhem's capacity for
  norm-changing adaptation "corresponds to transformation capacity"
  (CLAIM-018). It is held as an interpretation, not as a premise of ARG-007
  (SYNTHESIS-039).
- **CLAIM-027**: Active; interpretive; the will paper's thesis. Which regime
  becomes effective "depends on maintained relations — accessibility,
  precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation —
  that are not reducible to the contents of the competing regimes"
  (CLAIM-027). *Rests on:* CLAIM-047, CLAIM-080.
- **CLAIM-422**: Active; interpretive; thesis. "A successful corrective act,
  an executable procedure and a maintained organizational capacity are three
  different things, so an organization can record a correction, even rewrite
  its rules, without the corrected capacity being maintained" (CLAIM-422).
  *Rests on:* CLAIM-025. *Grounds:* CASE-138, CASE-135. The third object is
  "the organization's continuing disposition to recognize the condition the
  procedure is for, and to act on it with the authority the procedure
  assigns" (CLAIM-422). The claim "adds that the record of a transformation
  is a fourth thing, and the one most easily mistaken for the third"
  (CLAIM-422). Its method of telling them apart is CLAIM-414's: organizations
  alike in ordinary operation "part under the intervention the capacity is
  for" (CLAIM-422). It does not say procedures are idle: "A procedure is
  evidence of a capacity and often a cause of it" (CLAIM-422). Its defeater
  asks for "a showing that an organization's maintained capacity is fixed by
  its adopted procedures", and "a case in which procedure and capacity
  coincide does not defeat it" (CLAIM-422). Notes add measured decay of
  procedural skill, about 0.06 to 0.08 SD a month (Tatel, LIT-2079;
  THEORY-1424), and Uber ATG's policies "sporadically implemented" (NTSB,
  note of 2026-11-16).
- **CLAIM-414**: Active; demonstrated. "Matched ordinary trajectories can
  conceal differences in an interpretation's efficacy, in the allocation of
  that efficacy, and in the maintenance of its enabling constraints", and "a
  specified intervention exposes the difference" (CLAIM-414).
- **CLAIM-349**: Active; interpretive. Access to a signal, its authority over
  action and action on it "are separable", and overcontrol "is a maladaptive
  regime and not more control" (CLAIM-349). *Rests on:* CLAIM-025, CLAIM-027.
- **CLAIM-344**: Active; interpretive. "Resolve is a maintained organization
  of precedence, commitment and revision that keeps a temporally extended
  policy effective ... while keeping the capacity to revise it; mere slowness
  or persistence is not resolve" (CLAIM-344). *Rests on:* CLAIM-277,
  CLAIM-331, CLAIM-099. It lists two ways to fail, "**Fragility**" and
  "**Rigidity**", against "**Adaptive robustness**" (A41, quoted in
  CLAIM-344).
- **CLAIM-465**: Proposed; interpretive. "Escalation without a release
  condition leaves authority with a regime after the demand that justified
  it has passed" (CLAIM-465). Its note of 2026-10-30 records what holds it
  back. Its one case, CASE-135, shows a release that policy provided for
  and that was not carried out. The record has not settled whether release
  is a form of TERM-017's suspension (CLAIM-465; SYNTHESIS-035).
- **CLAIM-443**: Active; interpretive. "A learned prior over which processes
  govern" is a candidate mechanism for durable authority, "but persistence is
  not amortization" (CLAIM-443).
- **CLAIM-446**: Proposed. Some persistent anxiety may be
  governance-maintained, since "under the avoidance that authority selects,
  the hypotheses that the situation is dangerous and that it is safe predict
  the same observation" (CLAIM-446). **CLAIM-445**, also Proposed, extends
  this to institutions: a protective regime "becomes self-maintaining when

- **CLAIM-359**: Active; interpretive. Robustness to compromised constituents
  "is a property of the processes that allocate, audit and revise
  authority", and security and self-governance are "distinct achievements and

### 2.3 What a form costs, and readiness for the rare case

- **CLAIM-571**: Active; demonstrated. "For a fixed architecture and one
  resource that must be held at each moment, the resource threshold for

### 2.4 Support that keeps or erodes capacity

- **CLAIM-597**: Active; demonstrated. "An organization can become independent
  of what founded it but never of what feeds it". The intact share holds at
  ρs/(ρs + d) and decays as e^(−dt) once supply stops (CLAIM-597). Its body
  says such a cost is "easy to read as waste while it is working"
  (SYNTHESIS-032).
- **CLAIM-604**: Active; demonstrated. Repair drawn "at a rate proportional to
  the structure's intact share, as a flame's upkeep is" gives "an ignition
  threshold below which it goes out with the supply still on", collapse
  rather than decline, and an outage after which restoring supply "does not
  relight it". Supplied repair "has none of the three" (CLAIM-604).
- **CLAIM-602**: Active; demonstrated; thesis. "Where an organization's
  capacity is learned by doing the work it serves, and decays with disuse,
  outside supply that does the work first lowers the capacity kept ... while
  supply that covers only what the organization cannot yet do leaves its
  learning untouched, so what decides dependence is the order in which supply
  and capacity are used, not the amount of supply" (CLAIM-602). Its lines are
  transient-support and readiness. The disuse term is "an assumption, stated
  in the claim's condition", and CLAIM-558 holds it for individual skill
  (CLAIM-602). The model is in section 3.
- **CLAIM-605**: Active; demonstrated; thesis. "Where capacity is learned by
  doing, decays with disuse and needs capacity already present to grow,
  support that does the work has a deadline after which withdrawal no longer
  restores capacity, and a slow taper of such support is worse than an abrupt
  cut ...; support that covers only what the party cannot yet do needs no
  taper but cannot lift a party already below the threshold" (CLAIM-605).
  *Rests on:* CLAIM-602, CLAIM-604.
- **CLAIM-675**: **Active** since the status review of 2026-11-18; demonstrated;
  thesis; lines transient-support, will-organization, flourishing-and-meaning.
  "Two forms of support that give a party the same capabilities now can leave
  it different capabilities later ... so support, or a technology, cannot be
  ranked by present capability sets and has to be assessed by the capability
  trajectory it produces" (CLAIM-675). *Rests on:* CLAIM-605, CLAIM-352. It
  does not say any technology deskills, and "Not that substitution is wrong"
  (CLAIM-675). SYNTHESIS-032 (version 7) left it out of every account, "for
  this line's next review".
- **CLAIM-673**: **Active**; interpretive. "Where the capacities a collective
  needs to govern an arrangement are learned by exercise and lost with
  disuse, and the arrangement does the work through which they are
  exercised, the arrangement can erode the capacity to govern it"
  (CLAIM-673). *Rests on:* CLAIM-605. "The condition is the claim's
  interpretive part" (CLAIM-673). SYNTHESIS-032 calls it Proposed; the entry
  is now Active.
- **CLAIM-600**: Active; interpretive. Transient support "can be withdrawn
  safely only if" five things hold. It must have started a self-sustaining
  process on a lasting flux, been large enough to pass any threshold, and
  outlasted the threat. It must have "left the work to the party throughout,
  in each subtask", at bearable effort, and been kept up where the field
  moves fast. Inputs the party cannot produce "must stay supplied", and
  "meeting them all is not enough, since support can carry a function nobody
  names before withdrawal" (CLAIM-600). *Rests on:* CLAIM-597, CLAIM-604,
  CLAIM-602, CLAIM-566. Since its version 8 it claims necessity only, on its
  own village water scheme (SYNTHESIS-032).
- **CLAIM-601**: Active; demonstrated. "A savings buffer is a stock of time"
  (CLAIM-601).
- **CLAIM-603**: Proposed; interpretive. A capability lost to reliable supply
  is "the Black Queen's loss", and "whether it is dependency or division of
  labour is decided by the supply, not the loss", so "the aim framed as
  self-sufficiency is better framed as reciprocity" (CLAIM-603).
- **CLAIM-758**: Proposed; interpretive. "Overconsolidation, a generalization
  of overfitting": continued stabilization can improve a learner's present
  objective "while raising the budget it needs to acquire" a declared family
  of later acquisitions (CLAIM-758). *Rests on:* CLAIM-675, CLAIM-569.
  **CLAIM-756** (Proposed; demonstrated) holds that "an acquisition can be
  worth making for what it does to the cost of other acquisitions".

## 3. Formal results and constructions

**CASE-191, a construction of self-formation.** Active; fictional,
construction; it illustrates CLAIM-532, CLAIM-516 and CLAIM-522; code in
`scratch/formal/self_formation.py`.
- *Setup.* An agent in contexts A and B, with targets a and b and
  D = |a − b|, has output y_k = x + s_k·u·z. Differentiation capacity is
  u = σ(S)σ(G), where S is a sensor and G a channel, and residual
  frustration is F(u, D) = max(0, D/2 − u)². The dynamics are
  Ṡ = αF + βP(G) + e_S(t) − (1+d)S and
  Ġ = αF + βP(S) + e_G(t) − (1+d)G, with P(c) = 3c²/(1+c²). αF is
  "production driven by the agent's own frustration", βP the mutual
  maintenance, e outside supply and d damage (CASE-191).
- *Maintained state.* With no frustration, S = G = (3+√5)/2 ≈ 2.618 is stable,
  with a saddle at 0.382, and the formed capacity "resolves any D ≤ 1.0472"
  (CASE-191).
- *Formation and transfer.* D = 1 on t < 20, nothing on 20 to 60, then D = 0.8.
  The self-forming agent has ∫F = 0.794 during formation and 0.000 on the

  out internal maintenance or outside supply after formation separates

- *Damage.* The capacity settles at 1.730 (d = 0.3) and 1.299 (d = 0.45), and
  is lost at d = 0.55 (CASE-191).

## 6. Digester's notes

*Everything in this section is the digester's reading, not the record's.
Codes are cited for what is used. The connections drawn here are not filed

### 6(a) Bearing on a general theory of learning and inquiry for arbitrary systems

1. **Learning as the formation of a maintained capacity.** The record offers
   a substrate-neutral criterion for when a system has learned something more
   than a response. The occasion is an unresolved discrepancy, read as a
   missing face in an ordinal compatibility complex (CLAIM-347, CLAIM-368).
   The product is a new distinction, a refinement of the policy's partition,
   or a new channel, a growth of reachable outcomes. Both are defined at the
   level of the agent's policy, "whatever implements them" (CLAIM-532). This
   lets a general theory avoid fixing a mechanism. The frozen-projection
   argument (CLAIM-532, QUESTION-488) shows why a mechanistic test such as "a
   new unit" or "a new weight" is ill-posed. The two tests, transfer within a
   class and intervention on production during formation, are operational
   and apply to any system with a declared boundary (CLAIM-532, CLAIM-522).
2. **Three things to keep apart in any account of learning.** The first is an
   observer's posterior about a fixed selector, which is not learning by the
   system (CLAIM-540). The second is a learned action that does not transfer
   (TERM-099). The third is a capacity that transfers and is maintained
   (CLAIM-532). A theory of inquiry that scores learning by behavioural
   persistence alone would confuse the first with the third. CLAIM-540's 2/3
   against 1/2 is a compact demonstration of this.
3. **Learning is a relief of frustration, not a loss of demands.** On
   CLAIM-347, dropping or suppressing a demand is not relief. CLAIM-532's
   products are relief. QUESTION-488's uniform priority is the case where a
   system "settles" a conflict by giving one demand up. For a general theory
   this marks the line between learning, which enlarges what is jointly
   satisfiable, and capitulation, which shrinks the demand set. The record
   keeps it open whether capitulation by the agent's own activity is still
   self-formation (QUESTION-488).
4. **Learning has a threshold and a class.** In CASE-191 small discrepancies
   are "tolerated, at a standing frustration, and form nothing", and the
   formed capacity serves only a bounded class. A general theory would say
   that a discrepancy below an activation threshold is borne, not learned
   from, and that what is learned has a domain (CASE-191; compare CLAIM-550 on
   tolerance derived from thresholds).
5. **History is not legible in the present.** A self-maintained capacity
   carries no trace of whether it was self-formed or installed (CASE-191,
   TERM-100). For a theory of learning across arbitrary systems, this means
   attribution of learning (did *this* system learn it?) needs a record kept
   at formation time, or an intervention made then. It cannot come from
   inspecting the learned state. This is as true of a trained model or an
   institution as of a person (CLAIM-532's list of where origin matters).
6. **Keeping is a separate achievement from acquiring.** CLAIM-569 separates
   construction, maintenance and transition costs. CLAIM-025 and CLAIM-099
   separate operating, governing and transforming. CLAIM-422 separates the
   act, the procedure and the maintained capacity. Read together, a learning
   theory should have at least three ledgers: the cost to form a capacity,
   the cost to keep it, and the cost to move between capacities. Success at
   one says little about the others. "Having enough to sustain it is not
   having enough to acquire it" (CLAIM-569). CLAIM-758's overconsolidation
   applies this to learners: present objective up, cost of later acquisition
   up (CLAIM-758, Proposed).
7. **How capacities are lost.** The region gives four distinct routes:
   - Disuse decay, which a written procedure does not prevent (CLAIM-558,
     CLAIM-422).
   - Supply that does the work the capacity is learned from, which lowers
     the kept level in proportion and, past a deadline, traps (CLAIM-602,
     CLAIM-605).
   - Protective lock-in, where a regime shapes the evidence that would end it
     (TERM-020, CLAIM-445, CLAIM-446; the last two Proposed).
   - Budget rules that ratchet away the slack transitions need, with "no
     trace in output" (CLAIM-577).

   The common thread is that each loss is invisible under ordinary operation
   and shows only under the intervention or contingency the capacity is for
   (CLAIM-414, TERM-115, CLAIM-632). A general theory of inquiry might state
   this as a principle: *maintained capacities are known only by
   intervention.* The record holds the instances, not that principle.
8. **Support should be assessed by trajectory.** CLAIM-675 is the most
   portable result here. Two forms of help that are equal now can differ
   later, so any evaluation of tutoring, tools, or AI assistance by present
   capability is underdetermined (CLAIM-675, CASE-262). CLAIM-605's note adds
   asymmetric guidance in its model: gradualism is safe on entry and risky on
   exit, and reversibility "runs down while a pilot runs" (CLAIM-605). These
   are model results with invented parameters, and the record says so
   (CLAIM-605, "What it does not say").
9. **Resource bounds shape what is learnable.** Reachability has its own
   threshold, the least peak over paths, which can exceed holding either end
   (CLAIM-571). Several resources give a non-convex frontier (CLAIM-585).
   Observing and acting compete on one budget (CLAIM-572). For learning, this
   suggests that a capacity can be feasible to hold and still unreachable from
   where the learner is. In the record's terms, a new channel (CLAIM-532) may
   need reconfiguration slack (TERM-106) that the learner's budget rule has
   removed (CLAIM-577). The record does not join these; SYNTHESIS-031 tested
   related joins and filed none.
10. **Inquiry includes revising the standards.** CLAIM-485 places what
    endogenous governance adds in revising "its own governing standards when
    they break down". QUESTION-388 adds that reconfiguration under a criterion
    supplied from outside is programmability, not self-governance. A general
    theory of *inquiry*, as distinct from learning, would then need a level at
    which the criterion g is itself the system's product (QUESTION-388,
    TERM-070). The record leaves this open.

### 6(b) Operationalizing in a governed research record worked by AI assistants

1. **The record as a case of its own theory.** QUESTION-457 asks whether a
   collective that keeps its inquiry in a governed record can improve its
   improver without losing correctability. Its line is record-as-apparatus.
   The schema revised by decisions (ADRs) is the record's z. The question is
   whether g, the criterion by which ADRs are proposed and accepted, is the
   record's own (QUESTION-388). Several features observable in these entries
   bear on that:
   - Review procedures generate their own corrections. CLAIM-532's restatement
     followed a synthesis review's separating case (CLAIM-532 history, versions
     4 and 5), and TERM-099 was coded once the second-time rule was passed
     (TERM-099 summary).
   - The owner still rules on adoption ("adopting it is the owner's call",
     CLAIM-602, CLAIM-605, CASE-262). On QUESTION-388's terms that places the
     final criterion outside the assistant collective.
2. **Self-formation in the record.** By CLAIM-532's terms, a schema change
   would count as the record's self-formation if four things held. Its own
   activity on an unresolved discrepancy, such as a lint failure, a
   mis-scoped definition or an account found stale, produces a new distinction
   (a field, a vocabulary entry, a relation type) or a new channel (a new
   review procedure). The change is then maintained by the record's own
   cycles (CI, reviews). It serves a class of later discrepancies. And it
   would pass a transfer test: later, unrelated filings are handled without
   the original friction. TERM-099's note of 2026-11-12 already reports such
   an instance in a formal system: an added concept "registered in the theory
   base and reused across later experiments" (THEORY-1746). An operational
   version for the record would log, per ADR, the discrepancy that occasioned
   it and later uses of what it introduced. That gives the transfer test.
3. **Formation provenance needs a record kept at formation time.** CASE-191
   shows that after the fact, own formation and outside installation are
   indistinguishable. Applied to a record worked by assistants, whether a
   distinction was formed by the record's own processes or installed by one
   session's say-so can be read only from the history kept at the time. PR
   discussions, changelog fragments and `history` notes are that history. The
   digester reads this as an argument that those provenance trails are
   constitutive of the record's self-governance, not overhead.
4. **Procedure versus maintained capacity in the record.** CLAIM-422 warns
   that "a correction is recorded ⇏ the corrected capacity is maintained". An
   ADR or a skill file is a procedure. Whether assistants actually apply it is
   the maintained capacity, and it can decay without trace (CLAIM-558, by
   analogy). Two observations from this digest:
   - SYNTHESIS-041's phrasing lags CLAIM-532's restatement.
   - TERM-099's "What it leaves open" lags CLAIM-532's version 5.

   These are small instances of a recorded change not yet propagated (section
   4). An operational test in CLAIM-414's spirit would be a held-out audit:
   present a fresh session with a case the procedure is for and check
   uptake. This is close to CASE-255's design (QUESTION-457).
5. **Capacity-first assistance as a design rule.** CLAIM-602 and CLAIM-605,
   with CLAIM-675, give a model-based reason to prefer that AI assistants do
   only what the owner or the record's own processes cannot yet do, where the
   capacity at stake is learned by doing. Examples are the owner's judgment of
   adoption, or the record's review practices. The record's own scope note
   applies: whether the relevant capacity decays with disuse and has a
   threshold is empirical (CASE-262, CLAIM-675). CLAIM-673 names the specific
   risk: an arrangement that does the work through which its governors'
   capacity is exercised "can erode the capacity to govern it". For a record
   whose schema assistants largely revise, that capacity is the owner's and
   the review process's ability to judge those revisions.
6. **Resource ledgers for the record.** CLAIM-569's three costs map onto a
   schema change. Constructing it is drafting the ADR and its first
   application. Maintaining it is the lint, CI and reviewer attention it
   needs. Transition is migrating existing entries. CLAIM-577 warns that a
   budget rule tied to last period's activity removes the slack that
   transitions need. A record whose review effort is set by recent volume
   might lose the capacity for large migrations "with no sign in output".
   This is the digester's analogy; the record states it only for its models
   (SYNTHESIS-024).
7. **Measuring improver gain, not product gain.** QUESTION-457's distinction
   is directly usable. A process change to the record should be evaluated by
   whether a *later* procedure, applied to a fresh starting state at a matched
   budget, produces better further improvements. Counting entries filed or
   lint passes is a product measure, and it may be read off the record's own
   evaluator, which CLAIM-266 says "cannot certify" what it preserves
   (QUESTION-457). The stronger achievement is preserved recursive
   correctability, the ability to detect damage to its own checks, which
   QUESTION-457 found tested by none of the studies read.
8. **What stays open for the manuscript.** The record does not settle the
   following:
   - Whether reweighting or reprioritization counts as self-formation
     (QUESTION-488).
   - What makes a revision a continuation of the same will (QUESTION-364).
   - The criterion that makes reconfiguration self-governed (QUESTION-388).
   - Whether a record-keeping collective can improve its improver
     (QUESTION-457).

   A general theory built from this region should present these as open
   joints, not as results.
