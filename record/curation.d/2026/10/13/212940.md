**What the readings corrected.**

- **Litherland and Mørch.** "Instruction" in their title means Reddit's own
  rules, and the planners' designs fall under "emergence". The case, the
  equal-budget claim and the identifiability question had read it the
  other way. All three are corrected, and the paper now answers
  [QUESTION-084](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-084.md) in part.
- **Hawkes processes.** The branching ratio is a control parameter, and
  outside the linear excitatory case only a sufficient bound. It is not
  the order parameter that the question, both workbench entries and the
  Hawkes LIT said it supplies to [CLAIM-163](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-163.md). Corrected where it was said.
- **Equal budgets.** Use of r/place's equal cooldown was itself heavy-tailed
  (Wu and Silva), so coordination is one source of unequal influence, not
  the only one. [CLAIM-272](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-272.md) now says so.
- **Lahiri.** The filing called it the closest case of constitutive
  influence. Its top level of autonomy is mostly not constitutive
  influence, and none of its vignettes shows an agent changing a control on
  itself.
- **Segura.** The filing's summary, "assay sets conservative for candidate
  models", is not in the SSRN abstract, which describes a topos-theoretic
  formalization. The summary and [QUESTION-086](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-086.md) now mark it unconfirmed.
- **Bickhard against [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md).** Bickhard holds that a candle flame's
  self-maintaining processes are functional for it. Mossio, Saborido and
  Moreno ([LIT-570](../../record/literature.d/LIT-570.md)), whom [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md) relies on, deny that. It is a
  disagreement about function and does not touch [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md)'s title.
- **Adams et al. and Wu and Silva.** Adams et al. model no participants, so
  they do not estimate reach as the exchange expected. Wu and Silva
  clustered artworks for 2017 only.
- **Hoel et al.** The macro advantage depends on imposing a uniform
  intervention distribution at each level separately. "Causally
  privileged" overstated it.
