# QUESTION-408: Is the positive interaction of R&D intensity with distance to the productivity frontier on productivity growth replicated in an independent industry or firm panel, or under an external instrument for R&D, with absorption still near or above half of R&D's return at about half the frontier's productivity?

## Why it is a question

Griffith, Redding and Van Reenen's key interaction is significant at 10% in
the base specification and stronger in the adjusted ones, and its causal
reading rests on lagged R&D being predetermined ([THEORY-1523](../theory.d/THEORY-1523.md), [NOTE-1709](../notes.d/NOTE-1709.md)).
Whether it holds in another panel or under an external instrument is open.

## What would answer it

An independent test of the same result, as the condition asked. The
condition as it stood on [THEORY-1523](../theory.d/THEORY-1523.md): The positive interaction of R&D
intensity with distance to the productivity frontier on productivity growth
is replicated in an independent cross-country industry or firm panel, or
holds under an external instrument for R&D, and the share of R&D's return it
implies for units at about half the frontier's productivity stays near or
above half.
