Findings worth carrying:
- The owner's recollection is still unfound. The two attribution
  studies are the nearest; by construction they credit only immediate
  alignment, so they could not show an early step easing later ones.
- Code first is contested. Aryabumi and colleagues' advantage is gone
  after cooldown, and the authors recommend mixing. Stern and colleagues
  find no compute-matched benefit, and find that a first phase is
  overwritten at the switch.
- No promote_when was met. Stern and colleagues qualify [THEORY-2071](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-2071.md)
  but do not reproduce its setup. The procedural paper is a second
  instance without the natural-language control that [THEORY-2071](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-2071.md)'s
  promote_when needs.
- Corrections:
  - Feng, Prabhumoye and colleagues are a different work from Feng,
    Goodman and Frank ([THEORY-2079](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-2079.md)), so they are cited with a
    second author.
  - Petty and colleagues' arXiv listing abstract swaps the definitions
    of its two settings; the PDF's definitions are used.
