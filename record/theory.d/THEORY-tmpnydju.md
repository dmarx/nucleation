# THEORY-tmpnydju: Farr reads Marcuse as holding that advanced industrial society sustains a democratic unfreedom in which domination takes the form of administration and needs neither force nor an authority figure, because the society makes people think themselves freer than they are and integrates them through needs it creates

## The account

Farr reads Marcuse as holding that a "democratic unfreedom prevails in advanced industrial civilization", that "all domination assumes the form of administration", and that "domination no longer requires force or the presence of an authority figure"; Kellner adds that the society "created false needs" that integrate individuals.

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmpi8t8i](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpi8t8i.md), results 1–5. It bounds [CLAIM-tmpkjv7u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkjv7u.md): the claim's version 2 names impersonal domination of this kind as outside its test ([ARG-tmpsh7he](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpsh7he.md)).

## What it does not say

A test for when such domination exists.
