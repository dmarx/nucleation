# LIT-2456: Pregnancy, Parthood and Proper Overlap: A Critique of Kingma

Alexander Geddes (2022). "Pregnancy, Parthood and Proper Overlap: A Critique of Kingma." *The Philosophical Quarterly 73(2): 476-491*. DOI-10.1093/pq/pqac044.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1732](../notes.d/NOTE-1732.md)). A mereological correction: four
possibilities (parthood either way, proper overlap, disjointness) must all
be addressed; criteria for organismality or co-parthood are not criteria for
one organism's being part of another.
