## Corrections to the exchange

- **Bogomolny.** The bound at λ = e² is Bogomol'nyi's (1976), not Nielsen
  and Olesen's. Czubak & Jerrard attribute the identity "due to
  Bogomol'nyi".
- **Nambu–Goto.** Nielsen and Olesen "conjectured" and "argued" that vortex
  lines follow Nambu–Goto worldsheets. A's "leading effective action" is
  later language. The rigorous form holds where Jaffe and Taubes's
  conjecture does (λ = 1 or large λ).
- **Unverified for Nielsen & Olesen.** A's flux quantum, radial equations
  and Lagrangian normalization are not confirmed by anything read.
- **Hasimoto.** "Preserving its characteristic shape" is not in the
  abstract. The two-level hierarchy (field, filament, soliton) is A's
  framing: the filament is assumed, and only the soliton on it is
  derived.
- **Vortex counts.** A's statement that winding-two configurations at
  critical coupling include two separated unit vortices was not checked
  against a read source. Above critical coupling, the symmetric
  winding-two vortex is unstable (read).

As first offered (the assistant, Claude, in this session):

1. **A ladder of outside maintainers.** A constant controller is found out
   by perturbing the state. [CLAIM-522](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-522.md)'s state-feedback controller is found
   out only by intervening on the mechanism. The class of interventions
   needed to find an outside maintainer grows with how much of the
   production law it reproduces. Both ends are demonstrated, the gradation
   is a hypothesis, and a middle rung (feedback with delay or saturation)
   is cheap to construct.
2. **Run the pinned kink** for [QUESTION-438](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-438.md). It would be an executed study
   of feasibility without attraction for a localized structure: a small
   numerical job.
3. **Carriers assumed, patterns derived.** In each read model the upper
   level is derived and the lower one is assumed or constructed: a
   soliton on an assumed filament, a vortex line from constructed data for
   a finite time. That suggests a hypothesis about nested individuals in
   the record's cases. It would be defeated by any read model that derives
   both levels.
4. **The coupling, not the charge, decides how many bodies carry a
   conserved quantity.** Higher-charge symmetric vortices split above
   critical coupling. As an organizational analogue this is only an
   analogy, with no test proposed.
