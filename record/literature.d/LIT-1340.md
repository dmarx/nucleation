# LIT-1340: Regulatory independence may limit electoral holdup but entrench capture

Schram, A. & Ule, A. (2024). Regulatory independence may limit electoral holdup but entrench capture. *Public Choice* 198(3–4): 403–425. doi:10.1007/s11127-023-01136-y.

Read in full on 2026-10-21 ([NOTE-1053](../notes.d/NOTE-1053.md)), from the open-access article.
Its capture is an alignment of policymakers' payoffs, not influence over
evidence or criteria, so it bears on the last clause of [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md) (an
independent procedure need not be independent) more than on the claim's
two routes.

## Why this is here

A25: "independence from one source of authority does not imply independence from every other source." Bears on [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md), whose two routes of independence (informational and evaluative, R7) it complicates.
