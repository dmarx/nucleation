# CLAIM-tmpzaee6: Conditional generative systems realize stochastic transports between constrained distributions, and their compositional fidelity depends on preserving the compatibility and relational structure of constraints across media

## The claim

A97: "The strongest new proposition is: **Conditional generative systems realize
stochastic transports between constrained distributions of possible
representations. Their compositional fidelity depends not only on satisfying
individual observational constraints, but also on preserving the compatibility
and relational structure of those constraints across changes of medium.**" A100
put generation "part of the central theory" ([CLAIM-tmpdgdmt](CLAIM-tmpdgdmt.md)).

## Why it was replaced

The manuscript says "analogues" (Abstract) and "a shared mathematical vocabulary
... not an identification of their physical or computational mechanisms" (§7)
([CLAIM-tmpjlq36](CLAIM-tmpjlq36.md)). The chunk-6 reader finds no argument for the change in U33–U37. It is
later.
