# CLAIM-tmpersgx: An organization's characteristic structure can include the contracts maintained among its processes, which its ordinary behaviour does not reveal: two arrangements alike on the ordinary course can part when a contract is breached, an assumption fails or a process invokes its standing

## The claim

A16 §19, the candidate thesis: "Interdependent processes can establish
maintained relationships that specify conditional requirements, reciprocal
commitments, allocations of precedence, and mechanisms of breach
detection, arbitration and revision. These relationships can operationalize
standing without presupposing moral agency, phenomenal experience or
explicit agreement." "The characteristic structure of an organizational
individual may consequently include a maintained ecology of interprocess
contracts. Its capacity for reflexive governance consists partly in its
ability to transform those contracts and the distribution of authority
through which they are constituted."

A16 §15: "An organization may formally preserve all existing contracts
while changing the conditions through which they can effectively be
fulfilled. Conversely, it may preserve all current behavioral outcomes
while radically changing who possesses authority over future
contingencies." "Two systems with identical ordinary behavior may diverge
when a contract is breached, an assumption fails, or competing processes
invoke their standing."

## Admission

Filed from the synthesis by a separating case ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s first route).
The premises: contracts can form between processes ([CLAIM-tmpkvzcn](CLAIM-tmpkvzcn.md)); a
contract-like relation is marked by what it does on deviation, conflict
and breach ([CLAIM-tmp7hqtl](CLAIM-tmp7hqtl.md)); and processes that never object coordinate
like negotiating ones until intervention ([CLAIM-364](CLAIM-364.md)). [CASE-tmptpna1](../cases.d/CASE-tmptpna1.md) has
two arrangements, fixed priorities and priority inheritance, that give the
same schedule whenever the low-priority process holds no lock the
high-priority one needs, and part when it does. Every premise holds there,
and a characterization of the organization by its ordinary behaviour
cannot tell them apart. What the premises leave open, and the claim
asserts, is that this difference belongs to the organization's character,
its characteristic structure, rather than to its circumstances.

## What it does not say

- It does not say that an organization's contracts are its whole
  character, or that every regularity among its processes is a contract
  ([CLAIM-tmp7hqtl](CLAIM-tmp7hqtl.md)).
- The remainder of A16 §19 ("Operational normativity provides a formal
  substrate for analyzing requirements, standing, violations and
  correction, while their moral significance remains a distinct question
  requiring independent justification") is the owner's U14 with [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md)
  and [CLAIM-725](CLAIM-725.md), and is not restated here.

- a substitute provider;
- competing demands on the provider;
- removal of an enforcer;
