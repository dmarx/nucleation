# THEORY-tmpj5upc: Krohn and Weninger find over all Reddit posts and comments from 2005 to 2019 that links to subreddits that did not yet exist preceded the creation of a sixth of the subreddits later created, that a linked subreddit was created sooner than a randomly drawn later one, and that the chance of creation rose with repeated links to about a third at some twenty, and report all of it as correlation, not cause; links to existing subreddits come with a small, short-lived rise in activity, about as often after a rise as before

## The account

On Reddit, writing "r/name" for a subreddit that does not exist is
sometimes followed by someone creating it: a sixth of created subreddits
were linked before they existed, a linked subreddit was created sooner
than a random later one in a survival comparison, and repeated links
raised the chance of creation to about a third. Most such links are never
followed by creation. Links to existing subreddits come with a small,
short-lived rise in the target's activity, as often after a rise as
before one. The authors disclaim any causal reading.

## Evidence

Observational, over 110 million links from Pushshift data, with a random
baseline and a Cox regression. No assignment, matching or tracking of the
created communities' fate.
