# THEORY-1992: Rights are complexes of Hohfeldian incidents

Wenar & Cruft, "Rights" (SEP, 2025 revision), [LIT-2765](../literature.d/LIT-2765.md).

## The account

"the incidents fit together in characteristic ways to create complex
"molecular" rights" (§2.1). "A has a power if and only if A has the ability
to alter her own or another's Hohfeldian incidents", and "Power-rights to
alter the authority of others are ... definitive of all developed legal and
political systems" (§2.1.3). On function, will theory and interest theory
remain the main rivals (§2.2.2).

## What it does not show

- That rights once justified structure later justification.
- Whether inalienable rights are possible: the entry reports the dispute.
