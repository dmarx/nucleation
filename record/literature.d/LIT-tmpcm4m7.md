# LIT-tmpcm4m7: The Future of Military Virtue: Autonomous Systems and the Moral Deskilling of the Military

Shannon Vallor (2013). "The Future of Military Virtue: Autonomous Systems and the Moral Deskilling of the Military". In K. Podins, J. Stinissen and M. Maybaum (eds.), *2013 5th International Conference on Cyber Conflict*. Tallinn: NATO CCD COE Publications.

## Key takeaways

- "The virtue of honesty, for example, can only be acquired through repeated practice of truth-telling."
- The loss of moral skill "would not be rendered insignificant by the emergence of machines that produce equivalent, or arguably even better outcomes".
- "An expert supervisor of another’s decision, in order to be worthy of the authority to override it, must have acquired expertise in making decisions of the very same or a similar kind."
