# THEORY-tmpeqxmj: In Belgian adolescents, need frustration measured by the twelve BPNSFS frustration items statistically absorbs most of the association between depressive and eating disorder symptoms: cross-sectionally their latent correlation falls from .50 to a non-significant .18, and in a two-wave latent change model their correlated change falls from .43 to .24; its mediating role is shown in correlated change over the same six months, not in temporal order

## Source

Campbell, Boone, Vansteenkiste and Soenens (2018), LIT-tmpazv5c, Journal of
Clinical Psychology 74(10); read in NOTE-tmpxovm7.

## What was actually shown

**Study 1** (cross-sectional, N = 284, mean age 14.1). Latent variables
from parcels, adjusted for age, gender and BMI. Depression (CDI) and eating
pathology (EDI-II composite) correlate .50. Self-critical perfectionism
(Frost CM and DA) predicts both (β .45, .34) and lowers the correlation to
.42. With need frustration as full mediator (partial mediation no better,
Δχ²(2) = 3.08), indirect effects are .55 [.41, .69] on depression and .28
[.18, .39] on eating pathology, and the residual symptom correlation is .18,
not significant (pp. 6–7).

**Study 2** (two waves six months apart, N = 608, 477 at both, mean age
16.2). Latent change models. Correlated change of the symptoms .43;
with perfectionism change .39; with frustration change as partial mediator
(better than full, Δχ²(2) = 17.34) .24, p < .01. Indirect effects of
perfectionism change via frustration change: .26 [.17, .36] on depression
change, .12 [.06, .19] on eating change (pp. 10–11).

The test could have failed: had the symptom association stayed near .50
with frustration in the model, frustration would not explain the
comorbidity. In Study 1 it did not stay.

## What this does not say

- **That frustration precedes or causes the symptoms.** Study 2's mediator
  and outcomes are changes over one interval. The authors: "Due to the
  correlational nature of these findings, future experimental research is
  needed" (p. 12). LIT-561's "predicted" is statistical.
- **That it holds beyond internalizing symptoms**, in clinical samples or
  in adults; the authors list these as untested.
- **That it holds for need frustration as SDT defines it.** The measure is
  the BPNSFS frustration items, which on THEORY-tmp62iwr may be keyed low
  satisfaction, and whose content (failure, pressure) is close to the
  perfectionism and depression measures.
