# QUESTION-tmp6d8jq: Does pragmatic drift under repeated reconstruction systematically increase or reduce measured contextual dependence?

## Why it is a question

A52 §12.4, "Contextuality under transmission": "Investigate how contextual
judgment structure evolves as an utterance undergoes repeated reconstruction.
Ask whether certain forms of pragmatic drift systematically reduce or increase
measured contextual dependencies."

It is the one point where the two halves of the manuscript, serial
reconstruction (§8) and contextuality (§3, §9), would bear on each other. The
manuscript's Proposition 2 says when a transport preserves a global extension,
so it gives a sufficient condition for contextuality not to appear. It says
nothing about drift that removes it.

## What would count as an answer

Repeated reconstruction chains (§10 Case II) scored with an
overlapping-context design at each stage. Or a theorem saying which classes of
reconstruction kernel can create or destroy global-extension failure.
