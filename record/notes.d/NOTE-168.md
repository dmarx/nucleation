# NOTE-168: Hartmann & Trpin — Coherence-based explanatory power

## Contribution

Recent critiques have questioned whether probabilistic measures of explanatory power can work at all. The authors respond with a family of coherence-based measures that avoids those problems. Instead of scoring only how well one hypothesis accounts for the evidence, the measures compare how well the whole explanatory package, which may contain several explanantia, coheres with the explanandum against how well it coheres with the explanandum's negation. They show that this accommodates key scientific judgments while keeping the advantages of a probabilistic framework.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- §1 names the two targets. Lange (2022): scientists distinguish the explanatory power of hypotheses that all entail the explanandum. Roche & Sober (2023): every extant measure satisfies the "Two Likelihood Inequalities" (TLI) and so misranks temporal chains and negatively interacting causes.
- §1 lists three contributions. The entailment problem arises only when auxiliary assumptions are counted in the explanans. A coherence-based measure built on CohOG+ satisfies TLI without the shallowness and negative-interaction failures. And such measures rank explanatory fit for a fixed explanandum; they do not decide what counts as an explanation.
- §3 argues that standard likelihood-based measures reduce to "reverse confirmation". Fn. 7 shows that the Good, Schupbach–Sprenger and Crupi–Tentori measures can be restated with Shogenji coherence. Proposition 1: using Shogenji coherence in the set-based definition recovers Schupbach–Sprenger.
- §5 treats explanatory power as a relation between sets, the explanans H = {H1..Hn} and the explanandum E. The worked example: good study conditions plus careful homework explain high grades better jointly than good conditions plus cheating, because the explanantia in the second pair are in tension. CohOG+ is Olsson/Glass relative overlap normalized by the overlap under an independence distribution with the same marginals.
- §6–§7: conjunctions of explanantia remain legitimate when they individuate a single explanatory variable. The authors do not claim CohOG+ is uniquely correct or give an axiomatization, and they present coherentism as a refinement of probabilism, not a rejection of it.

## Open questions

- A formal, set-valued measure of explanatory power is relevant wherever the anthology weighs competing explanations of a training phenomenon that involve several interacting factors.
- Check §5.1's handling of Roche & Sober's temporal-chain and negative-interaction cases; that is where the measure earns its claims.
- Check the companion paper (Hartmann & Trpin, forthcoming) that introduces and defends CohOG+, since the result depends on that choice of coherence measure.
