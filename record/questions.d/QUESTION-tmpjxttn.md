# QUESTION-tmpjxttn: Does an independent study of randomly assigned lottery prizes or other unconditional wealth shocks, on the Swedish registers or on another country's administrative data, find a reduction in the recipient's labour earnings of the same order (about 1% of the windfall a year), appearing in the year of the shock and lasting at least five years, coming mostly through hours, and smaller for the spouse than for the recipient?

## Why it is a question

Cesarini, Lindqvist, Notowidigdo and Östling (THEORY-1479) estimate the
response from three Swedish lotteries. An independent study of random
windfalls finding a response of the same order, timing and margin would
answer it; US lottery winners in Golosov and colleagues (THEORY-1498),
measured per dollar of annuitized income, bear on it without settling
it.
