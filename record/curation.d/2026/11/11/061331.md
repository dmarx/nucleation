The ninth synthesis review, at the owner's request ("proceed"), the last
of the large lines with no account: formal-apparatus, 234 in-force
entries when it began.

## How it ran

The line is unlike the others. Most of its entries are first in another
line, and its relations are sparse: a Louvain map (stored relations
weighted 2, mentions 1, resolution 0.7) found seven parts of 9 to 38
entries, eleven small clusters of two to six with no link to the rest,
and 46 entries linked to no other entry of the line. Nine read-only
readers took the seven parts, the clusters together and the unlinked
entries together, under one brief that added a note on the line's shape.
The filer then checked coverage (every in-force entry integrated once),
replaced the readers' working labels ("part-1", "cluster 2") with the
accounts' codes, checked the three drafted claims' derivations, dropped
one drafted account (below) and wrote the line account.

## Claims filed (Arm B, derivation)

The filer checked each derivation.

- **[CLAIM-tmpopi1b](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpopi1b.md)** (reachability reader): knowing each requirement to
  within ε fixes every threshold to within ε ([CLAIM-580](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-580.md)), but two merges
  of [CLAIM-592](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-592.md)'s merge tree within 2ε of each other can be reordered, so
  which levels an observer's partitions agree with is not fixed. It
  qualifies [CLAIM-592](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-592.md)'s design lever.
- **[CLAIM-tmpsh6jv](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpsh6jv.md)** (stability reader): with symmetric exchange among
  viable regimes, the restricted generator is L + E with 0 ≤ E ≤ ε, so by
  Weyl's inequality the survivors' relaxation rate is within ε of the
  exchange Laplacian's gap, and the separation between settling and dying
  out goes as 4k/ε for two regimes, π²k/(nε) for a line and n²k/ε for a
  complete graph. Textbook mathematics; it joins the stability part's two
  strands.
- **[CLAIM-tmpz1gnk](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpz1gnk.md)** (unlinked reader): the kernel w(u,v)/√(w(u)w(v)) is
  [CLAIM-370](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-370.md)'s conditional-expectation operator carried by the unitary
  multiplication by √w, so the two have one spectrum and the kernel's
  squared norm is 1 + χ².

Each is short; whether that is enough to keep it is the owner's call. No
other Arm B sentence passed; the readers tested about fifty.
