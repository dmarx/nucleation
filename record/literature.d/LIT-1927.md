# LIT-1927: Evolutionary Transitions in Individuality by Endogenization of Scaffolded Properties

Pierrick Bourrat (2022 online; 2025 in print). "Evolutionary Transitions in Individuality by Endogenization of Scaffolded Properties." *The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science* 76(2): 269–294. DOI-10.1086/719118.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-2361](../notes.d/NOTE-2361.md)).* Lewontin's three conditions are met by arbitrary groupings, so Bourrat adds a condition on their source (§2, Table 1): pseudo-Darwinian properties come from an observer's choice, Darwinian-like ones from "external (that is, ecological) individuating mechanisms", Darwinian ones from "internal individuating mechanisms that provide the entity some resilience to perturbations". Against Clarke, the mechanisms "have to be internalized or endogenized for an ETI to be genuine". Black et al.'s patch model ([LIT-1940](LIT-1940.md)) gives Darwinian-like collectives, "a way of initiating the ETI". The new model couples low growth to stickiness; after the migration ban is lifted, collectives keep low growth for small propagule timescale T and patch capacity S and revert otherwise (§6.3). Seeded earlier from [LIT-188](LIT-188.md)'s report, which the reading bears out.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-01 from the re-reading of [LIT-188](LIT-188.md) ([NOTE-173](../notes.d/NOTE-173.md)). Griesemer and Shavit endorse its scaffolding and endogenization account and relocate endogenization "into the community rather than into a lower-level individual" (§2). The record's question about it is whether endogenization can serve as a criterion of individuality without a boundary declared in advance ([CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md), [CLAIM-194](../claims.d/CLAIM-194.md), [CLAIM-529](../claims.d/CLAIM-529.md)). A reading should check how the paper states the release test, since that test needs a declared inside and outside. The reading of 2026-11-22 found that it does: the candidate is the patch's cells, the inside is their genotype, and the release lifts only the migration ban, leaving patches, capacity and the periodic propagule generations in place ([NOTE-2361](../notes.d/NOTE-2361.md), Bearing on the record). Bourrat's later paper on reproducers is held as [LIT-157](LIT-157.md).
