# THEORY-tmp66aty: Pettit proposes that a state is legitimate insofar as it imposes its order under popular control, and that the people control the state insofar as they have an individualized, unconditioned and efficacious influence over it that pushes it in a direction they find acceptable, realized by individual contestation, a resistive citizenry and channels of influence insulated from electoral pressures and private lobbies

Pettit, *On the People's Terms* (2012), LIT-tmpueqz7.

## The account, as far as read

"people will control the state just insofar as they have an
individualized, unconditioned and efficacious influence that pushes it in
a direction that they find acceptable" ("Democratic control", p. 239). "The
republican theory of legitimacy gives the state a democratic job
specification, requiring it to operate under equally shared, popular
control" (Conclusion, p. 293).

## What it does not show

- What "unconditioned" requires, or whether it means independence from
  officials' willingness to cooperate, as A83 says: unread.
- The argument for any of it: only the openings were read.
