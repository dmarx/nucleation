# TERM-tmpxr1ox: greedy agency: agency that pursues the improvements its own evaluation rewards without due account of what they do beyond it; temporally greedy when it discounts the developmental consequences of immediate gains, jurisdictionally greedy when it gives no authority to the demands and standing of other affected processes, which no length of planning horizon cures

## Definition

The owner, at U29: "relate measurement naivety, greedy agency,
enshitification, and institutional sociopathy". A46 §2 gave the phrase two
senses:

- "**Temporal greediness** describes agency that privileges immediately
  available improvements without adequately considering their
  developmental consequences."
- "**Jurisdictional greediness** describes agency that privileges
  improvements according to its own evaluative perspective without
  appropriately accounting for the demands and standing of other affected
  processes."

"The second is more fundamental to our moral inquiry. An organization can
be extraordinarily sophisticated at long-term planning while remaining
jurisdictionally greedy." "The problem is not necessarily insufficient
intelligence, planning or self-control. It is the scope of the concerns
permitted to govern that intelligence." A46 states it as ΔK_O(a) > 0
establishing neither ΔV_ecology(a) > 0 nor Perm_N(a) = 1.

Its examples cross scales: "a department minimizing its expenditure by
transferring work to another department, a platform increasing
advertising revenue by degrading users' experience, or a protective
psychological process repeatedly recruiting resources at the expense of
recovery and exploration."

## Use

A48 §9, on Jensen ([LIT-tmpo2igd](../literature.d/LIT-tmpo2igd.md)): enlightened value maximization "addresses
an important dimension of greedy agency: temporal short-sightedness. But
it does not necessarily resolve jurisdictional greediness." Used by
[CLAIM-tmpa61rf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa61rf.md) and [CLAIM-tmpjnly0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjnly0.md) ("This is where greedy agency becomes
reflexive control").

## Not to be confused with

- **A greedy algorithm**, which takes the locally best step. The temporal
  sense is that; the jurisdictional sense is not, since a jurisdictionally
  greedy agent may plan far ahead. A46: "Greedy algorithms and locally
  optimizing processes are not inherently pathological. Their adequacy
  depends on the architecture of constraints within which their activity
  occurs."
- **A proxy objective** ([TERM-108](TERM-108.md)): greed is about whose demands govern, a
  proxy about whether the measure tracks the outcome. An agent can be
  greedy on the true outcome of its own.
- **Failure of cross-scale concordance** ([TERM-122](TERM-122.md)), which is the
  encompassing organization's property; jurisdictional greed is a
  constituent's way of producing that failure.

The owner's companion phrase, "measurement naivety", is not given a term:
A46 §1's six forms (representational, statistical, causal, performative,
organizational and normative naivety) are each held, at [TERM-108](TERM-108.md),
[CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md), [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md) and [CLAIM-060](../claims.d/CLAIM-060.md).
