# LIT-tmpnno4c: The Ethics of Attention in an Age of Distraction

Brian Dijkema (2015). "The Ethics of Attention in an Age of Distraction". *Comment* (Cardus), 25 June 2015. Review of Matthew B. Crawford, *The World Beyond Your Head*.

## Key takeaways

- The book quoted: "The standard for truth is relocated: it is no longer found out in the world, but inside our own head."
- The remedy, in the reviewer's words: "a type of rationality he calls “an ecology of attention.”", found in communities of skilled practice.
- The reviewer's own argument, not Crawford's: without authority "we’re likely to end up with relativism again."
