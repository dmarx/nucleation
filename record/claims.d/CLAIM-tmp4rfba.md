# CLAIM-tmp4rfba: What scholarly evaluation should recognize is contribution to a community's maintained capacity for inquiry and not the maintenance of a body of knowledge as such: a record can grow while the community's capacities stay the same, so rewarding maintenance without that test rewards accumulation

## The claim

A24 of the exchange "Hyper article interpretation"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6b064f1b-9ea8-83e8-951e-634ed41675b6), §5: "The
record has grown in both cases. But the community's capabilities may have
changed only in the second." A community that meets a result it cannot
explain may add the result to its database. Or it may develop "a new
conceptual distinction, experimental procedure, mathematical method, or
organizational capacity that makes the discrepancy tractable and transfers
to future problems". Only the second is learning in CLAIM-532's sense.

The questions an evaluator would ask, in A24's words: "Can researchers now
formulate questions they couldn't formulate before? Can they identify an
error they previously wouldn't have noticed? ... Can they correct their own
methods of inquiry?" The first is CLAIM-475's: a stabilized specification
is past inquiry stored so that later questions become cheaper to ask.

This revises the wording of QUESTION-tmpz0154. A24, under "What this does
to the academic-incentives thesis": "The objective shouldn't merely be to
reward researchers for maintaining a valuable body of knowledge. That still
leaves open what *valuable* means".

## What it does not say

- Not that a capacity is easy to measure. A24's capacities have no measure
  here, and a count of them would meet CLAIM-tmp2timl's objection.
- A24 adds: "A large record that an assistant can retrieve from is not
  proof that the assistant or researcher has acquired the ability to reason
  correctly with it." That sits beside CLAIM-668: access to a record is not
  evidence of coordinating on it.
- CLAIM-532 gives a sufficient condition for self-formation, not a
  definition of learning, and this claim uses it only as that.
