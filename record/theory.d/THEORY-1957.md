# THEORY-1957: Schlager and Ostrom propose that property rights in a common-pool resource unbundle into rights of access and withdrawal at the operational level and of management, exclusion and alienation at the collective-choice level, the latter being authority to define future operational rights, that the five are independent but usually held cumulatively as the positions of authorized user, claimant, proprietor and owner, and that a constitutional level devises the collective-choice rules

Schlager & Ostrom (1992), [LIT-2761](../literature.d/LIT-2761.md).

## The account

"A property right is the authority to undertake particular actions related
to a specific domain" (p. 250). Collective-choice rights are "the
difference between exercising a right and participating in the definition
of future rights to be exercised" (p. 251). "The five property rights are
independent of one another but, in relation to fisheries, are frequently
held in the cumulative manner arrayed in Table 1" (p. 252).
"Constitutional-choice actions entail devising collective-choice rules"
(n. 2).

## What it does not show

- Anything about challenge or contestation of decisions.
- Which protections lie beyond revision at the constitutional level.
