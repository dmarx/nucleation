# NOTE-tmp1ip5i: Torres and Rosa (2021) — Acceleration theory, temporal regimes, and politics today

## Contribution

The author's own account of the book's thesis, and of the later origin of the term "dynamic stabilization".

## Key results

1. p. 519: acceleration "is a structural requirement of modern, capitalist societies", not a normative demand.
2. p. 520: "I call this in the books published after Social Acceleration a mode of dynamic stabilization."
3. p. 520: we must run faster "just to stay in place".
4. p. 521: "I am NOT a proponent of slow-down or deceleration."
5. p. 522: a modern society can be slowed "but only at the cost of its institutions getting in crises".

## Assumptions

Modernity is defined by its mode of stabilization.

## Concepts

Dynamic stabilization; post-growth; desynchronization.
