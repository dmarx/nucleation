# TERM-tmp8cp5q: self-formation, of an agent: the production, by the agent's own activity on a discrepancy it cannot yet resolve, of a maintained capacity, a new distinction it can make legible or a new channel by which it can act, that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later ones

## Definition

Self-formation is the production of a capacity, with four parts:

- **by the agent's own activity**, so its formation provenance is the
  agent's (TERM-tmp8spbn, sense 1);
- **on a discrepancy it cannot yet resolve**, a demand or conflict its
  present regimes do not meet;
- **of a maintained capacity**, a new distinction or a new channel, kept by
  the agent's own processes (maintenance provenance, sense 2);
- **that outlasts the discrepancy and serves a class of later ones.**

## What it is not

- **An observer's learning.** The persistence a hierarchy gives a run of
  choices is the observer's estimate, not a change in what selects
  (CLAIM-tmplz9zf).
- **Learning an action.** A response that does not transfer to a new
  discrepancy of the same class after the inducing one is gone.
- **Installation.** A capacity put in place from outside, even one the agent
  then maintains. CASE-tmph9iwi shows such a capacity is indistinguishable
  afterwards, so whether a capacity was self-formed is a question about
  formation provenance.
- **Kane's self-forming action** (LIT-tmpnqv86), which is an undetermined
  choice. CLAIM-tmpex9rg sets the two side by side.

## What it leaves open

Whether a reweighting of existing regimes with no new distinction or
channel, TERM-023's precedence, should count. CLAIM-tmpbeh2e's defeater
names this as the case that would make the definition too narrow.
