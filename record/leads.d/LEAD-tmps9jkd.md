# LEAD-tmps9jkd: Compare six documented episodes of American governance, the Twenty-Second Amendment, the Electoral Count Reform Act, continuing resolutions, debt-limit impasses, federal disaster assistance and Medicaid redetermination, reconstructing for each the legal specification, the operative arrangements, the dependencies formed, the distribution of authority and costs, how the deficiency became legible and what the interventions did

## The idea

A36 §25's cases and mechanisms:

| Case | Principal mechanism to investigate (A36) |
|---|---|
| Twenty-Second Amendment ([CASE-tmpfh2in](../cases.d/CASE-tmpfh2in.md)) | Cultural norm formation and formalization |
| Electoral Count Reform Act | Clarification of relational jurisdiction |
| Continuing resolutions | Recurrent workaround costs and inhibited development |
| Debt-limit impasses | Compositional incompatibility and distributed costs |
| Federal disaster assistance | Fragmented contractual interfaces |
| Medicaid eligibility redetermination | Effective standing and administrative burden |

For each, A36 would reconstruct "The declared legal specification", "The
operative implicit arrangements", "The expectations and dependencies
formed around them", "The distribution of authority and regulatory
costs", "The mechanisms through which deficiencies became legible" and
"The attempted interventions and their demonstrated consequences". Its
standard: "The framework should earn its explanatory value through
predictions or distinctions those existing accounts do not already
adequately supply."
