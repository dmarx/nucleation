# CLAIM-1227: Memes are characteristic values of perspectives: each records how a perspective responds to one kind of transformation of a situation, so a family of memes samples a perspective's character without being it

## The claim

The owner's words, at U4: "memes as characteristics  (qua
representation theory) of perspectives". A character χ is a function on
the group, constant on conjugacy classes; its value χ(g) at one class is
one "characteristic" of the representation.

**Reading (b), this entry.** A meme records one such value: how a
perspective transforms one kind of situation into an interpretation. A5
§2's examples fit it: "Follow the money" as "Interpret behavior through
material incentives", "Extraordinary claims require extraordinary
evidence" as "Scale evidential requirements with prior plausibility".
Several memes sample the character; none is it. Perspectives can share
some memes and differ in others.

**Reading (a), the alternative,** is [CLAIM-1278](CLAIM-1278.md): the meme is the
whole character.

## Where the record stands

- **Sampling a character by probes is held, with its limit.** A5 §4's
  "family of probes", Φ_M(P) = (R_P(m₁), …, R_P(m_n)), is the record's
  relational identification by probes ([CLAIM-142](CLAIM-142.md), Proposed): "Two
  communicative realizations can agree on every probe in a restricted
  family without being structurally identical, so observational
  equivalence is relative to the probes". A finite set of memes fixes a
  character only if the probes are independently justified and the
  values sampled determine it; for a finite group, the values on every
  conjugacy class do.
- **The group is still owed** ([CLAIM-249](CLAIM-249.md)), as on reading (a).
- **A5's development** is [CLAIM-1285](CLAIM-1285.md), and A6 uses this reading when it
  has two perspectives share a meme and differ in character
  ([CLAIM-1271](CLAIM-1271.md)).
