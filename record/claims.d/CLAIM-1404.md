# CLAIM-1404: Cognitive load theory prices peaks and resource-rational analysis prices totals; bounded inquiry needs both

## The claim

The record holds a reading of resource-rational analysis ([THEORY-359](../theory.d/THEORY-359.md)). A
mechanism is derived as the one that best trades the expected utility of its
result against "the opportunity cost of the time and resources it uses". The
price it puts on a mechanism is a *total*. Cognitive load theory prices a
*peak*: how much must be held at once ([CLAIM-1360](CLAIM-1360.md)). [SYNTHESIS-024](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-024.md) says the
two come apart. A transition needs a peak that budgets on totals miss, and a
system can afford the total and not the peak, or the reverse.

So neither account is the whole of bounded cognition. Each explains choices
that trade one budget against the other:
- **[THEORY-330](../theory.d/THEORY-330.md).** People choose the memory load that gave them the best
  reward rate. That trades peak (items held) against total (time to finish).
- **[THEORY-343](../theory.d/THEORY-343.md).** People offload cognition as felt demand rises, even where
  offloading gains nothing. Offloading lowers the peak, and it can raise the
  total, by the cost of writing down and looking up.
- **[QUESTION-095](../questions.d/QUESTION-095.md)'s amortized retrieval.** Computing once and reading back
  later lowers later peaks at the cost of an earlier total.

## Bounded inquiry

The record's claims on inquiry are total-budget claims: [CLAIM-1157](CLAIM-1157.md) on
deadlines and shared pools, [CLAIM-1065](CLAIM-1065.md) on the cost of asking, [CLAIM-697](CLAIM-697.md) on
success as a nondecreasing function of budget. The readiness line's peak
results ([CLAIM-571](CLAIM-571.md), [SYNTHESIS-024](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-024.md)) were filed for configurations and
transitions, not for inquiry. This claim says inquiry has both. An
institution can have the person-hours for an investigation, its total,
and still not be able to hold its interacting parts in one place at once,
its peak. A record is one way to lower that peak.

## What it does not say

That peak and total are the only budgets. Time to a deadline ([CLAIM-1157](CLAIM-1157.md)) is a
third kind, a bound on elapsed time rather than on what is spent.
