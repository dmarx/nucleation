# LIT-3027: Optimal dynamical range of excitable networks at criticality

Kinouchi & Copelli (2006). Nature Physics 2, 348–351; arXiv q-bio/0601037.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-22 from the reading of Azizpour, Priesemann, Zierenberg and Levina ([LIT-2980](LIT-2980.md), [NOTE-2259](../notes.d/NOTE-2259.md)), who take its dynamic-range maximum at criticality as the gain in their trade-off and show that with a finite readout window the maximum moves to a subcritical coupling. [NOTE-054](../notes.d/NOTE-054.md) records the same maximum as a cited assumption of a whole-brain review, and [CLAIM-809](../claims.d/CLAIM-809.md) cites the finite-window qualification. Not read.
