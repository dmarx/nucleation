# QUESTION-402: Does an experiment or an independent simulation show DeLuca et al.'s folding pathway in DNA origami: in an accessible design, a collapse into a locally ordered, globally disordered precursor before global ordering, and, in a large interior-heavy design, trapped products with defective interiors whose early folding progress does not predict the final outcome?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1398](../theory.d/THEORY-1398.md) reports what DeLuca et al.'s mesoscopic simulation found
([NOTE-1561](../notes.d/NOTE-1561.md)): ten runs per design, in a model without misbinding,
helical topology, base stacking or annealing. The authors propose
time-resolved SAXS, fluorescence and AFM tests and run none. Whether real
origami folds through the precursor, and whether large interior-heavy
designs are trapped in ways early progress does not foretell, is open.
Schneider, Möritz and Dietz's compacted intermediate ([NOTE-1641](../notes.d/NOTE-1641.md)),
seen by gel only, is consistent with the first part and does not settle it.

An answer is an experiment or an independent simulation that shows, or
fails to show, both parts in designs of the kinds named.
