On 2026-11-14 the owner asked to "read and file"
https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.2618656123. The source is a
single work, a research article; there is no exchange, and no voice but
the authors'.

## Access

Crossref gave the citation and a CC BY 4.0 licence. The publisher's PDF
returned a Cloudflare challenge (HTTP 403), and it was not retried. PubMed
Central did not yet hold the article, Europe PMC answered 503, and
OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar hit their free quotas after listing only the
publisher's copy. OpenAlex's quota message suggested requesting a key by
email. That was not done ([ADR-008](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-008.md)). The work was registered Unfetched, and
two arXiv neighbours were sent to readers as stand-ins: Martinez-Garcia et
al. (2023, arXiv:2101.07049) and Zelnik, Meron & Bel (2015,
arXiv:1703.00732). The owner then uploaded the PDF. The work was read in
full, and the readers were stopped before they reported. Nothing was
filed from them, and neither neighbour is registered. Martinez-Garcia et
al. is the paper's own ref. 34, and both would be natural next readings
on dryland pattern formation.

## Support judgements

Three judgements are made, and none needs an ARG. Each concerns the
paper's support for its own conclusions, inside one THEORY, and no CLAIM
concludes anything from it.

- The field data do not show a first-order transition. This is recorded
  in the NOTE and the THEORY. [THEORY-1315](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1315.md)'s scope supplies the reason,
  and its dated note says so.
- The trait regressions do not measure the kernels.
- The fairy-circle simulation imposes the contraction it explains.

If a claim of the record's later rests on this reading, these become ARGs.

## What was left, and why

- **The SI** (the Ising comparison, the hysteresis runs, Fig. S3),
  **Dataset S1** and **the Zenodo code** are not in the PDF and were not
  fetched. The code is the THEORY's promotion route.
- **The conservative-traits-as-by-product argument** (p. 5) is kept in
  the NOTE as interpretive. It is a passing argument with no test, so it
  gets no code.
- **The "evade tipping" reading of fairy circles** (Rietkerk et al.
  2021) and **the aridity-index-0.8 alignment with global thresholds**
  (Berdugo et al. 2020) are kept in the NOTE. The cited works are not
  registered.
- **The management and "universal paradigm" claims** in the conclusion
  are programmatic, and nothing was filed for them.
