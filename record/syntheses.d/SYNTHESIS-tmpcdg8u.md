# SYNTHESIS-tmpcdg8u: Directed transport between context-indexed judgement models keeps overlap consistency and global extendability under two sufficient conditions, but for one source–target pair a constant kernel meets them at zero distortion, so it constrains a translation only as a property of a rendering procedure with one kernel family fixed across items, where exact fit is a garbling and Le Cam deficiency a finite linear programme; fidelity as intertwining of framing operations holds as algebra, but its defects are not identified from data and the order effect is bounded on observed responses instead; what stays open is decision-relevant information across a change of cover, signalling data, and whether the restated measures are informative, with tropes and adaptation as the place where structure and function come apart

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part a mechanical
community map of the line's stored relations and mentions calls kernels,
38 entries on directed transport, outcome-level kernels, D_obs,
intertwining and the decision-theoretic order. The line's account is built
over it and over the accounts of the line's other parts. It integrates
every member the map gave it; none plainly belongs to another part (see
"Whether the part holds together"). Several members also carry
formal-apparatus (CLAIM-011, CLAIM-070, CLAIM-121, CLAIM-245, CLAIM-381;
CLAIM-503 first), and no account of that line integrates them yet.*

## The account

- **Transport.** TERM-008 (the owner's word at U20, Active): a transport
  from a source to a target "consists of a correspondence τ between
  appropriate source and target contexts and local stochastic kernels",
  and "It is intentionally directed: compression and interpretation need
  not be invertible." TERM-008 also records that the utterance-level
  channel T(v | u) and the outcome-level kernels are "never formally
  related, and the manuscript uses both". TERM-021 (A24, Active) is
  fidelity as context-indexed distribution matching, D(τ), "a candidate
  objective function, not an established translation metric"; TERM-007
  (A30, Active) adds the dynamics of interpretation, T_τ(f) Φ ≈ Φ T_f, as
  "a further requirement" and not a replacement.
- **What the kernels preserve.** CLAIM-121 (the manuscript's Proposition
  1, demonstrated, Active): local kernels that commute with restriction
  carry an overlap-consistent source to an overlap-consistent target; it
  "concerns marginal consistency, not global noncontextuality". CLAIM-070
  (Proposition 2, demonstrated, Active): restrictions of one global
  kernel carry a global extension to a global extension. Both answer
  QUESTION-240 (Answered), split from QUESTION-022 (Open), which keeps
  "whether either condition is necessary, whether other classes
  preserve, and transports that change the cover". Both claims say
  they are special cases of Karvonen's natural transformations (LIT-846).
- **For one pair the kernels are unconstrained.** CLAIM-207 (the record's
  audit, granted, demonstrated, Active): "Every overlap-consistent target
  is naturally transported from every source at zero observational
  distortion, whatever the translation did", by a constant kernel, and
  "Until that is done, Propositions 1 and 2 are theorems about a map that
  translation is never shown to induce." CLAIM-077 (A49's answer to the
  collapse objection, interpretive, Active) would exclude the constant
  kernel by an admissibility condition: "If some pair has d_O(s_1, s_2) >
  δ, a constant Φ sends both to one point, at distance 0 ≤ δ′, so it is
  not admissible." CLAIM-207 records that "Separation narrows the freedom
  and does not remove it." CLAIM-011 (A34 and A40, granted, demonstrated,
  Active) is the principle under both: an uninformative correspondence
  makes intertwining vacuous or lossy, and its remedy is to "Require state
  correspondences to be estimated on one collection of tasks and evaluated
  on another".
- **The measure, restated over a procedure.** CLAIM-005 (A24's D(τ) and
  the manuscript's L_obs, interpretive, Proposed) narrows its own title to
  say that "the match constrains a translation only once one outcome
  correspondence is fixed across many items". CLAIM-381 (the record's
  derivations, demonstrated, Active): over a finite class of items, a
  constant kernel fits every item "only when the target readers' averaged
  responses do not vary with the item", an exact fit "is a garbling, with
  the item as the common parameter", and Le Cam's deficiency between the
  two finite tables "is one linear programme". CLAIM-228 (the record's
  repair, empirical, Proposed) keeps the proposal that the restated D_obs
  separates rendering procedures that receivers' task-indexed recovery
  separates, and waits on CASE-055 (in the study part). ARG-017 (formal
  application, Proposed) derives from CLAIM-207, CLAIM-011 and CLAIM-381
  that one pair does not identify the procedure-level score; it "does not
  carry that the restated measure is informative". CLAIM-503 (the status
  review's re-derivation, demonstrated, Active): over finite states and
  outcomes, ε-deficiency in every [0, 1]-loss problem holds "exactly when
  a single Markov kernel brings it within ε in total variation at every
  state". CLAIM-488 (interpretive, Active), resting on CLAIM-381 and
  CLAIM-503: Le Cam deficiency "would quantify the approximate case of
  the Blackwell order for translation", and decoder-only audience
  knowledge "would make the coding problem a Wyner-Ziv problem".
- **What is open across covers.** CLAIM-125 (interpretive, Active),
  resting on CLAIM-121 and CLAIM-070: simulations between empirical models
  settle global compatibility, and two parts stay open, decision-relevant
  information and signalling data. Its three objections are written in.
  CLAIM-237 (granted, demonstrated, Active): under the order on menus of
  experiments "any state-independent classical simulation is dominated
  automatically, so what is open is only the converse or a deficiency
  bound". CLAIM-245 (granted, demonstrated, Active): under
  Contextuality-by-Default a context-wise recoding can create or remove
  contextuality, so per-content signalling is not monotone and "the
  quantity to bound is joint overlap discrepancy". CLAIM-235 (counter,
  demonstrated, Active): the line's quantities "have no finite-data
  estimators"; it undercuts ARG-037 (Rejected), the reading of Wang et
  al.'s counts as showing that signalling is typical of language data.
  CLAIM-126 (interpretive, Active), complementing CLAIM-125: model
  stitching tests decision-relevant transport "only in its reconstruction
  form". QUESTION-026 (Open) asks whether categorical compositional
  semantics constrains transport: "does the constraint reach past
  propositional content?"
- **Dynamics and intertwining.** CLAIM-117 (A30 and the manuscript's §9,
  interpretive, Proposed): fidelity "requires preserving the dynamics of
  interpretation, not only final judgements", under an anchored
  correspondence; CASE-022 (stipulated, Active) is its concrete form, two
  renderings with "similar static profiles" whose responses to a later
  framing differ. CLAIM-063 (interpretive, Proposed), concluded by ARG-006
  (formal application, Active): approximate intertwining of each framing
  approximately preserves their commutator, and "on observed responses
  that degree is the order effect". CLAIM-011 objects to CLAIM-117 "as
  stated, not to its truth". CLAIM-253 (counter, demonstrated, Active):
  the defects "are not identified from judgement data", by a hidden-bit
  construction, and "The readout defect E_O is a third defect. No draft
  names it." CLAIM-231 (the record's repair, demonstrated, Active): on
  observed responses, under one outcome correspondence, "the target's order
  effect differs from the transported source order effect by at most 2ε";
  CASE-065 (a reported study, Active) illustrates it in kind and gives it a
  scale, "2ε is about as large as the effect it would certify" at poll
  sizes.
- **Equivalence and its weaker forms.** QUESTION-008 (Deferred) asked
  whether fidelity is approximate bisimulation, set aside at A39–A40; "The
  one-way case survives as CLAIM-034." QUESTION-190 (Open) refines it to
  the sample complexity of estimating the bisimulation metric between
  finite chains.
- **Fidelity as one goal, and tropes.** CLAIM-034 (A38 and A40,
  interpretive, Proposed): "Distinguish fidelity as one evaluative goal
  from adaptation, critique, parody, and transformation", with one-way
  simulation for an adaptive rendering. CASE-122 (documented, Active)
  illustrates it: in the Warhol case "fidelity of purpose is a cost, not a
  goal, the reverse of a translator's". CLAIM-147 (A155, interpretive,
  Proposed): a trope is "a family of observational profiles connected by
  admissible transformations", so its instances "can separate structural
  preservation from pragmatic preservation"; CASE-038 (documented, Active)
  is TV Tropes. CLAIM-145 (counter, interpretive, Active): the analysis is
  Propp's and Lévi-Strauss's. ARG-014 (Rejected): agreement with the
  catalogue's labels does not validate the structural account, since
  "Editors write a trope's description from its examples".

What stays open: necessary conditions and cover-changing transports
(QUESTION-022); the converse and a deficiency bound, and signalling data
(CLAIM-125's two parts); whether the restated D_obs is informative
(CLAIM-228, CASE-055); whether dynamics add to the prediction of fidelity
verdicts (CLAIM-117's and CLAIM-063's defeat conditions, no study run);
compositional constraint (QUESTION-026); bisimulation's estimation
(QUESTION-190); and the trope test with held-out families (CLAIM-147).

## Whether the part holds together

Within the part, 90 pairs of members are joined by a relation or a
mention (57 by a front-matter relation). Read by inquiry it has three
strands: kernels and their measures (20 members, 40 pairs inside),
intertwining and dynamics (10 members, 22 pairs inside) and fidelity's
goals with tropes (8 members, 11 pairs inside). The first two share 13
pairs; the third reaches them by 4 pairs (CLAIM-147 rests on CLAIM-077 and
uses TERM-008; ARG-014 and CLAIM-145 cite CLAIM-011) and the first by 1.
The third strand is the loosest, but its premise is the second's
admissibility condition, so the part is given one account. CLAIM-005,
CLAIM-011 and CLAIM-077 are hubs whose links run as much to other parts as
here; none runs mainly to one other part, so none is moved.

## Not stated on the record

Links across the components that no entry states, tested by ADR-075's
step 5 at this review:

- **The restated measure runs in the garbling direction.** That the
  kernel fit of the restated D_obs, and the deficiency CLAIM-381 computes
  and CLAIM-488 reads as the approximate Blackwell order for translation,
  vanish whenever the rendering's item tables are a garbling of the
  source's, a procedure that renders every item alike included, so they
  bound what a rendering adds about the item and not what it loses. Filed at this review as CLAIM-tmpol63b (Proposed).
- **Order effects cannot grow under one kernel.** That under one outcome
  kernel for both orders a rendering's order effect on an item exceeds the
  source's by at most the sum of its two order-context distortions, so a
  growth in the order effect bounds the distortion from below whatever the
  kernel, while kernels fitted to the two orders separately carry no bound. Filed at this review as CLAIM-tmptimdb (Proposed).
- **The dynamics thesis as an instance of the signalling problem.** That
  order effects are signalling (CLAIM-235's "order effects are signalling
  in the CbD sense"), so CLAIM-117's dynamics wait on CLAIM-125's second
  part. Not stated, and it fails: CLAIM-231 bounds order effects on
  observed responses without any extension of transport to signalling
  data.

## What it does not say

- That translation induces the outcome kernels; CLAIM-207 says nothing
  on the record derives them from T(v | u).
- That any restated measure is informative (CLAIM-228, Proposed), or that
  dynamics add to fidelity judgements (CLAIM-117, CLAIM-063, Proposed).
- That the two open parts of CLAIM-125 are hard; only that the literature
  read leaves them open.
- That a trope has a definition, or that the catalogue's labels validate
  the structural account (ARG-014, Rejected).
- Anything with standing of its own.
