# ADR-100: The organizational-normativity line is split in two: organizational-normativity keeps what a normative judgment needs and the wrong beyond harm; what wholes and parts owe each other, and what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe, become the line dependence-and-domination, because those parts relate to the first two almost only through its root questions

## Context

A line is "a named line of the record's own argument, so that a line can
be set aside as a whole and what depends on it can be found" (the `lines`
vocabulary, [ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). organizational-normativity was split from
will-organization by [ADR-089](ADR-089.md) with 23 entries, for "whether an
organization's own dynamics ground norms of function and failure, and what
judging an organization needs beyond them". By 2026-11-15 it held 71,
filed from "About Plantation Paradise", "Analyze technological
civilization trap" and the exchanges around them.

The synthesis review of 2026-11-15, pull request [#505](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/505), gave the line an
account over four part accounts, with [SYNTHESIS-038](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-038.md) (version 4) as the
line's account over them:

1. **what a normative judgment needs** ([SYNTHESIS-091](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-091.md), 19 components): viability does not
   establish what ought to persist, a judgment is well-defined once its
   relata are fixed and can then be false ([CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md)), some judgments need
   histories, and a self-maintaining system's failure norm is set by its
   own upkeep; with the root questions [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md);
2. **the wrong beyond harm, and becoming normatively less informed**
   ([SYNTHESIS-094](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-094.md), 24):
   a bounded duty of answerability ([CLAIM-614](../claims.d/CLAIM-614.md), [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)) and the routes
   by which an organization loses sight of what it owes;
3. **what wholes and parts owe and claim** ([SYNTHESIS-092](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-092.md), 20): what a whole owes its
   parts, with ground and bearer kept apart ([QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)), when a whole's
   need of a part becomes the part's authority ([QUESTION-461](../questions.d/QUESTION-461.md)), the routes
   into exploitation ([CLAIM-669](../claims.d/CLAIM-669.md), [CLAIM-670](../claims.d/CLAIM-670.md)), control over the terms as
   what sorts a dependence ([CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md)), exploitation and domination
   relative to a declared standard ([TERM-123](../terms.d/TERM-123.md), [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)), and the
   domination threshold ([CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), [CLAIM-686](../claims.d/CLAIM-686.md));
4. **what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe**
   ([SYNTHESIS-093](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-093.md), 9): A's
   claims from "Analyze technological civilization trap" on separable
   dimensions of power, the burden of justification, accountability that
   rises with indispensability ([CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)), the erosion of governing
   capacity ([CLAIM-673](../claims.d/CLAIM-673.md)), declining a technology, and reflexive institutions
   ([CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md)), with the concordance term [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md).

Counting stored front-matter relations as `scratch/formal/line_parts.py`
does, by the part each starts in, 93 of 102 fall within parts:

| part | inside |
|---|---|
| what a normative judgment needs | 24 |
| the wrong beyond harm and becoming less informed | 38 |
| what wholes and parts owe and claim | 29 |
| what those who govern an arrangement owe | 2 |

Of the nine that cross, seven point into the first part, and all seven are
`refines` or `bears_on` onto [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) or [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md). The other two
are [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s `complements` onto [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) (second part) and [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)
(third). The second and third parts are mostly closed, so by [ADR-083](ADR-083.md)'s
signal the line is a candidate to split. The review left the split to the
owner, with [ADR-082](ADR-082.md) as the precedent.

The assistant proposed a split in two, and the owner approved it on
2026-11-15: "yes on the two line split". This is the owner's decision,
made on the assistant's proposal.

## Decision

- **One line is added** to the closed `lines` vocabulary:
  `dependence-and-domination`, "Wholes, parts and dependence". Its blurb
  names what wholes and parts owe each other; when dependence becomes
  domination or exploitation; that control over the terms, not the size
  of the dependence, decides which way a dependence runs; and what those
  who control an arrangement others depend on owe. It says that
  "organization" here is [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s sense 1, a relational arrangement, by
  default, and that the bearers of obligations, agents or roles, are kept
  apart from the ground of the obligation. Both are the separations the
  owner asked the line's accounts to keep, and the components keep them:
  every bearer a component of the two parts names is an agent or a role.
  It says it was split from organizational-normativity.
- **organizational-normativity keeps the first two parts** and its root
  questions, [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md). Its blurb is narrowed to them
  and points to the new line.
- **The 29 entries of the third and fourth parts move.** In each, `line`
  changes organizational-normativity to dependence-and-domination in place.
  Other lines are kept. Each entry gets a version and a history note. No
  status, title or relation changes.
  - Every entry whose first line was organizational-normativity now has
    dependence-and-domination first. [QUESTION-464](../questions.d/QUESTION-464.md), whose first line is
    distributed-agency, keeps it first and has dependence-and-domination
    second.
- **An entry that argues within organizational-normativity keeps it**
  ([ADR-082](ADR-082.md), version 2; [ADR-085](ADR-085.md)). It does when it refines a question, or
  answers, objects to or bears on an entry, whose first line is
  organizational-normativity. Three do, and carry it second:
  - [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) refines [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md);
  - [QUESTION-468](../questions.d/QUESTION-468.md) refines [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md);
  - [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md).

  These are three of the seven crossings into the first part; the other
  four come from the second part, which stays. No moved entry rests on an
  entry of the first two parts. [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s `complements` onto [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) is
  not participation ([ADR-085](ADR-085.md)), so [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) drops organizational-normativity.
  No entry outside the two parts answers, refines, objects to or bears on a
  moved entry, so no entry gains the new line. The readings that bear on
  [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) and [QUESTION-461](../questions.d/QUESTION-461.md) take their lines from the entries that use
  them, derived, not stored ([ADR-090](ADR-090.md)).
- **The fourth part is not a line of its own.** It has only two relations
  inside it, and it hangs together by its source, one exchange, more than
  by its relations. Its relations to the rest of the line are [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md)'s
  bearing on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md), a root question, and [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s complements
  onto [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) and onto [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), which is in the third part. Its
  subject, what the controllers of an arrangement others depend on owe, is
  the third part's question asked of the controllers: [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s body
  says who owes is "those who control" the arrangement, "the agents
  [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md)'s second condition names".
- **The accounts follow the split** ([ADR-083](ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](ADR-084.md)). [#505](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/505) merged
  while this decision was drafted, and the restructuring is part of its
  first application:
  - [SYNTHESIS-092](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-092.md) and [SYNTHESIS-093](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-093.md) take the new line (version 2), and stay
    its two part accounts;
  - [SYNTHESIS-095](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-095.md) is filed as the new line's account over them. It
    states where they meet, [CLAIM-687](../claims.d/CLAIM-687.md)'s complement onto [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), and
    what crosses to organizational-normativity;
  - [SYNTHESIS-038](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-038.md) is revised over the first two parts (version 5). It
    integrates directly the three entries that carry organizational-normativity
    second, [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md), [QUESTION-468](../questions.d/QUESTION-468.md) and [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md), as [ADR-087](ADR-087.md)'s
    revision of readiness's account integrated [CLAIM-602](../claims.d/CLAIM-602.md), and keeps its
    version 4 counts as the record of why the line was split;
  - [SYNTHESIS-091](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-091.md) and [SYNTHESIS-094](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-094.md) say their line's account is now built
    over two parts (version 2);
  - the accounts of other lines that state a moved entry's line say the
    new one: [SYNTHESIS-032](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-032.md), [SYNTHESIS-035](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-035.md), [SYNTHESIS-041](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-041.md), [SYNTHESIS-050](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-050.md),
    [SYNTHESIS-052](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-052.md) and [SYNTHESIS-056](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-056.md).

  The two parts are not merged into one account. The synthesis-review
  skill gives a line that falls into parts an account for each part and a
  line account over them, and both part accounts were filed and mapped
  hours earlier. Merging would supersede an account no reviewer has found
  unfaithful, and would hide that the fourth part hangs together by its
  source.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep one line.** What argues for it: [TERM-122](../terms.d/TERM-122.md), [TERM-123](../terms.d/TERM-123.md) and [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)
  apply [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md)'s relata in their bodies, and part 3's questions refine
  the old root questions ([QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md) refines [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md), [QUESTION-468](../questions.d/QUESTION-468.md)
  refines [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md)). But both are a few relations against 29 inside
  the third part, and setting organizational-normativity aside would take
  the inquiry into domination and exploitation with it, which is the
  opposite of what a line is for. The split leaves those relations
  crossing lines, as [ADR-082](ADR-082.md)'s split did: the two refinements are kept as
  participation by the second line the questions carry, and the terms'
  use of [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) is a dependency the argument chain reports.
- **A line of its own for the fourth part.** It has two relations inside,
  and it would be a line held together by one exchange. Besides a root
  question, its relations within the line go to [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md), in the third
  part, and [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md). As a line of its own it would make the relation to
  the third part a crossing too, and gain nothing.
- **Split in four.** The first two parts share the root questions: four
  of the seven crossings into the first part come from the second. Apart,
  they would leave the wrong beyond harm without the question it bears on.
- **Move the third and fourth parts into distributed-agency.** Five of
  their entries already carry it, and they keep it. But distributed-agency
  asks "what an organized whole is, and how its operative direction
  relates to its members'". Whether a dependence is domination, and what
  its controllers owe, is a normative question about the terms of an
  arrangement, and distributed-agency is already one of the record's
  largest lines.

## Consequences

- The current-account view shows two lines where there was one, each with
  a line account over two part accounts. None is set Current.
- `luria lint` may report more `rests_on` steps across lines, for example
  from [ARG-110](../arguments.d/ARG-110.md) (dependence-and-domination) into [CLAIM-428](../claims.d/CLAIM-428.md)
  (distributed-agency), as before. They are dependencies, and they are
  reported, not failures.
- [ADR-099](ADR-099.md) placed the claims on domination and exploitation through
  dependence ([CLAIM-669](../claims.d/CLAIM-669.md), [CLAIM-670](../claims.d/CLAIM-670.md), [CLAIM-671](../claims.d/CLAIM-671.md)) in organizational-normativity.
  They are now in dependence-and-domination first. [ADR-099](ADR-099.md) takes a
  history note saying so; its body is unchanged.
- The name and blurb of the new line are the assistant's proposal. The
  owner may rename it before merging. A rename is a vocabulary edit plus
  the same 29 `line` values.
