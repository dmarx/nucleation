# CLAIM-tmp83ge2: Veit's common currency and ALARM's override are two regimes of ascendancy of one ordering, compensatory and overriding, so their rivalry over what the first felt valence did becomes the question whether the earliest ascendant orderings are continuous: in motivational trade-off experiments, a continuous indifference curve between a noxious cost and a resource for Veit, a discontinuity at a threshold beyond which no resource buys the cost for ALARM; only a graded override, deciding at every level of its own intensity, lacks a currency altogether

## The claim

[THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md) and [THEORY-090](../theory.d/THEORY-090.md) are declared rivals on what the first
consciousness did. For Veit it weighs options in a common currency. For
ALARM it gives an extreme signal "100% weight" through behavioural
programmes whose cost avoidance "does not involve a choice". [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s
body names the condition under which the rivalry would dissolve: "if
ALARM's "100% weight" were recast as a weight in a common currency rather
than an override of it."

On the owner's reframing ([CLAIM-tmpz8lli](CLAIM-tmpz8lli.md)) both are ascendancy of an
ordering of the animal's own states.
- **Veit's currency is compensatory ascendancy.** The ascendant ordering
  trades off against the others in proportion: a larger resource buys a
  larger cost. As an ordering of cost–resource pairs it is continuous,
  and has a continuous numerical representation ([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md),
  part 3).
- **ALARM's override is overriding ascendancy.** Past a threshold the
  alarm decides, and other concerns decide at most its ties. ALARM's "no
  choice" is the limiting case in which the ascendant ordering leaves one
  option at the top.

Whether the override has a currency depends on its form
([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md), part 3a).
- **A threshold override**, alarm on or off, ranks everything above the
  threshold below everything under it. It has a currency, a
  discontinuous one: in that form it *is* an extreme weight, and
  [THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s dissolution goes through.
- **A graded override**, the alarm deciding at every level of its own
  intensity with other concerns breaking only exact ties, has no
  currency at all. In that form the rivalry does not dissolve.

Either way the two accounts agree that the earliest valence is ascendancy
by an ordering of the animal's own states. They disagree on whether that
ordering is continuous, and, if it is not, ALARM's form decides whether
any currency remains.

## The test

[THEORY-101](../theory.d/THEORY-101.md)'s promote_when names the experiment: "an animal accepting a
noxious cost for a resource, in proportion to both". Fit the animal's
choices between cost–resource pairs.
- **Veit predicts** a continuous indifference curve: for every cost some
  resource buys it, and the price rises smoothly.
- **ALARM predicts** a discontinuity at a threshold: above it no resource
  buys the cost. Whether resource still breaks ties among options above
  the threshold that differ in cost tells the threshold form from the
  graded one.
- **A realistic override has a third signature.** If the alarm decides
  only past a discrimination threshold, the rule is a lexicographic
  semiorder ([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md), part 3b), and a chain of options each a
  little costlier and a little richer than the last produces cyclic
  choices: each step taken, the endpoints reversed. Tversky's
  intransitivity experiments are the design. Cycles are evidence for an
  override with a threshold and against a currency of either kind, which
  cannot cycle.

- **Sweep the cost both ways.** Raising and then lowering the cost tells
  a fixed threshold from a hysteretic one, which the reframing does not
  place.

**What the data so far show.** Gibbons et al. ([LIT-tmpskuar](../literature.d/LIT-tmpskuar.md), [NOTE-tmpt0lr2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpt0lr2.md))
found bumblebees' feeding from a noxious (55 °C) feeder graded with the
sucrose offered elsewhere: "Bees avoided noxious feeders when the unheated
feeders contained high sucrose concentrations, but progressively
increased feeding from noxious feeders when the sucrose concentration at
unheated feeders decreased" (p. 1). The companion hermit-crab study
([LIT-tmpwwll5](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwwll5.md), from its abstract) found crabs in preferred shells
evacuating "at a higher shock level than those in poor quality shells".
Both look compensatory at the costs tested. Neither reaches the question:
the bee study used one cost, ran the resource levels in separate groups
and fitted a smooth model that assumes gradedness, and the crab studies
used two shell qualities, too few to show a ceiling. Within-subject
cycles of Tversky's kind are also hard to establish; the replication
record is contested (Birnbaum and Gutierrez 2007; Regenwetter et al.
2011; not read).

Both regimes can occur in one animal, a currency below the threshold and
an override above. On this reframing that is expected, one ordering
whose ascendancy changes regime past a point, unless the two are shown to
be separate mechanisms, which is the first defeater.

## What it does not say

- That either account is right about the origin of consciousness, or
  that either regime is felt. The recasting is functional.
- That the mathematical contrast settles the empirical one. A finite set
  of tested pairs can always be fitted by a currency ([CLAIM-tmp85p0g](CLAIM-tmp85p0g.md),
  part 1), so the test needs enough pairs to tell a steep curve from a
  discontinuity, and a design showing that the discontinuity does not
  move with the size of the resource.
