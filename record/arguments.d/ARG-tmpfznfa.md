# ARG-tmpfznfa: Six failure cases, by generalization, show that optimization's pathology is eroded evaluation

## The inference

Premises: six documented episodes in each of which an organization's
success under a narrow criterion went with harm and with damage to what
could detect or correct it (A8, https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c). Conclusion: CLAIM-tmpv3frk,
that this erosion is what makes optimization pathological.

## Critical questions

1. **Is the sample representative?** No. A8 chose the cases "that
   illustrate different mechanisms of organizational misalignment", and
   says "They are predominantly failure cases, and a serious comparative
   study should include organizations where similarly powerful
   optimization processes remained effectively constrained." Selected on
   the outcome, they cannot show what distinguishes the regimes that
   failed.
2. **Does the property hold in each case?** Not uniformly, on the record's
   own readings. ARG-071 (Rejected) already finds that the Wells Fargo
   report shows "occlusion and blocked uptake, not a performance regime
   reproducing its representation through its success"; that is erosion of
   evaluation by structure, not by the optimization's success. In the
   Purdue sub-case of CASE-405 the outcomes coded after the sanction are
   allegations, not findings. The cod and Facebook cases are A's accounts
   of unread sources.
3. **Is there a rival generalization the same cases fit?** Yes: that
   regimes doing great harm attract both concealment and capture, so that
   erosion of evaluators follows harm rather than marking a distinct
   pathology. The six cases fit both; only a comparison holding harm fixed
   separates them (CLAIM-tmpv3frk's `defeated_if`).

Verdict: Rejected as grounds for CLAIM-tmpv3frk. The cases `illustrate`
it, which costs the claim examples, not premises, if any is lost.
