# CASE-tmp32e66: Northstar-A and Northstar-B: same policy, same evidence, different escalation authority

## The case

In Northstar-A, an authorized reliability specialist's objection automatically
suspends deployment pending review. In Northstar-B, the same objection lets
deployment continue unless a severity threshold is met or senior management
orders suspension. The concern, the information and the formal priority are
the same in both.

## What it can show

That the difference in collective action can lie in the organization of
authority, *if* everything said to be fixed is fixed. The first draft said
plainly that the contrast does not establish "that real organizations would
behave so cleanly"; the rewrite dropped that sentence. It is restored here
as an open critical question on [ARG-tmpuhkwv](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpuhkwv.md).
