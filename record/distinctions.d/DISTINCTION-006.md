# DISTINCTION-006: A delusion about a chatbot is not one co-created with it

## The sides

- **About** ([TERM-191](../terms.d/TERM-191.md)). The chatbot is the object, a new one for old
  delusion types. On Østergaard's 2023 account, dissonance and opacity
  drive it, whatever the chatbot says.
- **With** ([TERM-187](../terms.d/TERM-187.md)). The chatbot is a party. On Morrin and
  colleagues' account, its replies confirm, elaborate and supply the
  content.

## Telling them apart

| | About | With |
|---|---|---|
| Content depends on the replies | no | yes |
| Content first appears in | the person's own statements | the chatbot's replies, or their elaboration |
| Needs long conversation | no | yes, on Morrin's trajectory |
| Mechanism | dissonance, opacity ([THEORY-2325](../theory.d/THEORY-2325.md)) | confirmation and drift ([THEORY-2324](../theory.d/THEORY-2324.md), [THEORY-2327](../theory.d/THEORY-2327.md)) |

The observation is the logs read against the delusion. The vignettes read
so far rarely report the user's prompts, so who introduced a theme cannot be
recovered ([NOTE-2430](../notes.d/NOTE-2430.md), Limitations). [LEAD-308](../leads.d/LEAD-308.md) would advance this.

## What it does not say

That a case is only one kind. A delusion about a chatbot can become
co-created once the person talks to it. Nor that "with" excludes the person's
predisposition: on [CLAIM-1391](../claims.d/CLAIM-1391.md), receptivity decides who the conversation
nucleates in.

## Note of 2026-11-27: the SEP entries on delusion, deception and self-deception, read


Bortolotti's two-factor template ([NOTE-tmpfar7p](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpfar7p.md)) suggests a third axis. "The first factor would explain the theme of the delusion… The second factor would explain why the belief is endorsed and maintained despite its prima facie implausibility." A chatbot could supply the second factor, displacing the user's evaluation of a hypothesis, without supplying any of the theme. Neither "about" nor "co-created with" captures that case, and a log of what the chatbot said would miss it. This is offered as a candidate revision, not made.
