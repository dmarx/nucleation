# LIT-3067: Lead yourself to the zone and be happy: the effect of self-leadership development on flow and happiness

Ricardo J. Vargas et al. (2025). PsyArXiv preprint. DOI-10.31234/osf.io/wfa4h_v1

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* By the abstract: 64 middle managers in an eight-week self-leadership programme gave 4,383 daily measures, situational flow and happiness by experience sampling during working hours; self-leadership rose with both, and flow did not mediate between self-leadership and happiness. Unverified until read.
