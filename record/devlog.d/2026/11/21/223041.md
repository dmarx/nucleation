**The owner's earmark**, 2026-11-21, after the retrospective [RETRO-008](../../record/retrospectives.d/RETRO-008.md) was filed: "one other thing to add for the retro: one of the reasons I chose the name "dialectic" for this mode of engagement was to evoke exchanging ideas. it would be helpful if you were more transparent within this space about what you are filing so I have an opportunity to monitor your activity. maybe more "thinking out loud" in addition to something like a periodic checkin to provide an executive summary could be helpful. I do still have the staging PR as an intervention opportunity, but the github interface isn't super convenient and I'd like to maintain the more natural discoursive flow for exchanging ideas that we have in the chat here."

**What it answers.** During the stretch [RETRO-008](../../record/retrospectives.d/RETRO-008.md) covers, the assistant's turns mostly reported filings after the fact, each in a closing "Filed" block. The reasoning that decided what to file happened in delegated drafters, in commit messages and in pull-request descriptions. The regime's rule, answer first and then end with a Filed block ([ADR-127](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md)), was met to the letter. But the exchange of ideas the regime is named for happened mostly off the page. The owner could follow it only through GitHub.

**Answered in** [RETRO-008](../../record/retrospectives.d/RETRO-008.md), which takes it as recommendation 5, in the same pull request.
