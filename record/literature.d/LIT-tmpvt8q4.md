# LIT-tmpvt8q4: Dynamic scaling of growing interfaces

Le Doussal (2025). arXiv 2507.08341.

## Key takeaways

- For d ≤ 2 the KPZ coupling flows to strong coupling; for d > 2 a
  tuned intermediate fixed point separates a weak-noise phase (the
  linear, Edwards–Wilkinson interface) from the strong-noise KPZ phase
  (p. 4, eq. 10).
- "Galilean invariance (i.e. the STS symmetry) ensures the relation
  χ = 2 − z" (p. 4).
- The 1986 hope that χ = ½, z = 3/2 hold in every dimension failed: in
  d = 2, "χ = 0.3869(4)" for stationary RSOS interfaces (p. 4).
