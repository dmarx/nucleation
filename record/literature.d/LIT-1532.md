# LIT-1532: Sparse Feature Circuits: Discovering and Editing Interpretable Causal Graphs in Language Models

Marks, S., Rager, C., Michaud, E. J., Belinkov, Y., Bau, D., and Mueller,
A. (2025). Sparse feature circuits: Discovering and editing interpretable
causal graphs in language models. *ICLR 2025*. arXiv:2403.19647.

Read in full on 2026-10-26 ([NOTE-1187](../notes.d/NOTE-1187.md)). Two accounts were drawn from
it: [THEORY-1007](../theory.d/THEORY-1007.md) and [THEORY-1014](../theory.d/THEORY-1014.md).

## Why this is here

Registered on 2026-10-26 at the owner's request, when the record was found
to hold almost none of the core circuits literature. It is the bridge from
features to circuits: the record held sparse-autoencoder features as units
([LIT-1545](LIT-1545.md), [LIT-1533](LIT-1533.md)) and circuits over heads ([LIT-1538](LIT-1538.md)), but
not a circuit built of features. Its indirect effects with the
integrated-gradient approximation are what the record's crosscoder reading
builds on ([LIT-1555](LIT-1555.md), [THEORY-1004](../theory.d/THEORY-1004.md)),
and its last author leads MIB ([LIT-1550](LIT-1550.md)). It bears on [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md),
[THEORY-968](../theory.d/THEORY-968.md) and [QUESTION-275](../questions.d/QUESTION-275.md).
