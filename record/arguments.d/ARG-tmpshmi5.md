# ARG-tmpshmi5: The survey and audit evidence A reports supports the structural half of the heavy-user mechanism in pornography distribution only as possible, and does not support the escalation half: the associations are small, observational and self-reported, and the one longitudinal content study runs against it

## The inference

From the reported findings (A27 §2, §4) to [CLAIM-tmpcv1pg](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcv1pg.md)'s explanation of
why mainstream channels carry formerly niche or extreme content.

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes,
  for the escalation findings. A27's rival "Heavy-user selection", in which
  people already interested in a category consume much more, predicts
  that heavy users report more intense content with no habituation. A
  retrospective self-report of a shift does not separate the two. For the
  platform audits (A27 §4), yes again: that a platform sorts and tags
  content is expected under every rival in A27's table.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No. A27 §5 lists
  six mechanisms, among them preference progression, heavy-user selection,
  algorithmic amplification, producer adaptation, catalogue expansion and
  classification change, each with a distinguishing prediction. None of
  the reported studies tests those predictions. The test A27 §8 proposes,
  comparing production against person-weighted and economically weighted
  demand, D_person against D_economic, would.
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** Partly. "Extreme" is
  measured as self-reported intensity, tags or coded aggression, which A27
  warns are not one scale. Shor and Seida's measure is aggression in
  popular videos, so their null bears on one reading of "extreme" only,
  and only to 2016.

## Verdict

Proposed. The structural half stands as a demonstrated possibility,
through [CLAIM-tmpmz271](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmz271.md), and is untested as fact. The escalation half is not
supported by the evidence reported and is contradicted on one measure. The
owner's own move at U14, to a measurable economy, is the right response,
and [CASE-tmpmw3hc](../cases.d/CASE-tmpmw3hc.md) carries it.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Shor and Seida unavailable; Shor and Liu registered

Shor and Seida 2018 ([LIT-tmp9goww](../literature.d/LIT-tmp9goww.md)), one of this argument's grounds and the
study it called contrary, is Unavailable after the reading pass of
2026-11-12. Its abstract matches A's report. A later longitudinal study by
the same first author, Shor and Liu 2025 ([LIT-tmpagy32](../literature.d/LIT-tmpagy32.md), unread), reports by
its abstract a rise in mostly consensual aggression over 2000-2024. The
verdict that the escalation half is "contradicted on one measure" therefore
rests on an unread 2018 study whose own successor may reverse it on that
measure; read it as "unsettled on one measure" until both are read.
