# SYNTHESIS-tmp4csi2: The record's grammar is an apparatus in the operational sense: fixed at any moment, amended by decision when a misfit is noticed, and made of typed relations more rigid than their relata without being sound for it; that it constitutes the record's objects is Proposed; and its revision is the record's own case of inquiry as specification, an inquiry that can revise what an answer could be and specifies under resource bounds

## The account

The record fixes the grammar an argument is expressed in, though not its
shape: a move that fits no scheme or declared relation is forced into one
or lost until the misfit is noticed (CLAIM-181, Proposed). In practice a
misfit that was noticed has repeatedly been treated as a defect of the
grammar and answered by amending it (CLAIM-419, from five decisions and
CASE-131); whether every such move is caught is not shown, and ADR-032's
literature roles are the misfit noticed and left in prose. A typed
relation is more constrained than either of its relata, and that rigidity
"is a fact about the grammar, not evidence that the dependency it records
is sound" (CLAIM-479); CASE-180 shows the same asymmetry in the record's
rates, where no relation type has been redeclared. CASE-131 is the
instance where the rigidity outran the relata: a fixed converse asserted
an inference nobody made, and the grammar gained a relation.

Put together under TERM-089's four functions, this is CLAIM-469's thesis:
luria is an apparatus in the operational sense, differentiating,
retaining, enforcing and revising the distinctions that fix what counts
as a referable and governable object in the record. CLAIM-469 rests on
CLAIM-479 and CLAIM-419 and is grounded in CASE-131 and CASE-178, so the
line's operational integration is already a claim of its own. What goes
beyond it, that the record's objects are constituted in Barad's sense
(TERM-087's agential cut), is CLAIM-476, Proposed: its first defeater,
that "constituted" adds nothing to the operational facts, is unanswered,
and QUESTION-366 asks for one consequence that would answer it. If an
apparatus makes some distinctions determinate and leaves others
unstatable, its choice is open to evaluation, though no exclusion fixes an
obligation by itself (CLAIM-472, Proposed, resting on CLAIM-476); the
record's own instance of that question is QUESTION-072, whether its
grammar and addressability distribute credibility among contributors.
That generated views fed back become instruments of the argument is
CLAIM-183, Proposed, on one instance.

CASE-178 measured how the grammar is revised: on demand, in the pull
request whose documents needed the refinement, nine times in ten with an
ADR, and at falling rates while filing rose. CLAIM-470, the owner's
"inquiry is a form of specification", states the general form and names
the record as its case: "CLAIM-476 says luria is an apparatus for a
record's objects whose grammar the record's own inquiry revises. This
claim is the general form". Its history note of 2026-10-29 reads CASE-178
as "this record's own answer space revised by decision". TERM-091 says
what such an amendment enlarges, the relational vocabulary. CLAIM-475
extends CLAIM-470: specifying is itself a resource-bounded realization,
and a stabilized specification is stored inquiry that makes later
questions cheaper and can entrench, stated in TERM-092's inquiry frontier
and TERM-095's tuple. CLAIM-476's note of 2026-10-27 (U15–U23) states the
same hinge from the other side: CLAIM-470 "complements this claim", and
"This claim is the case where the inquiry is a record's and the
specification is luria's grammar."

## Admission

Unadmitted. Each integration the account states is stated by a
component:

- the operational apparatus out of CLAIM-419, CLAIM-479, CASE-131 and
  CASE-178, by CLAIM-469;
- the constitutive reading on top of it, by CLAIM-476;
- the grammar's revision as the record's case of inquiry as
  specification, by CLAIM-470's "Why it bears on the record", its history
  note of 2026-10-29 and CLAIM-476's note of 2026-10-27 (U15–U23);
- the resource bound on specifying, by CLAIM-475;
- the bearing of QUESTION-366 on CLAIM-476's first defeater, by
  QUESTION-366 and CLAIM-476's note of 2026-11-06.

No route passes for the account as a whole. Hypothesis: its clauses are
the components' own, so a case in which every component holds leaves
every clause standing. Derived: joining them is juxtaposition, and the
non-trivial joins (CLAIM-469 from CLAIM-479 and CLAIM-419) are already
held as claims. Integration: the comparisons the line leaves open, the
two-apparatus experiment of CLAIM-476 and QUESTION-366 and CLAIM-470's
three-regime study, are unbuilt, and the account cannot settle them.

Three narrower candidates were checked so that a real integration is not
lost to loose wording:

- **The grammar's falling rates as settling, not entrenchment.**
  CLAIM-475 with CASE-178: revision cost one ADR in a unit of work already
  open, so CLAIM-475's cost-entrenchment has not operated on the grammar.
  CASE-178's note of 2026-10-27 (later) leaves this open ("not separated
  by this case"). It turns on a free parameter, the measure of a
  revision's cost. Counted in ADRs per unit of work, it is cheap and
  level. Counted in the documents that carry a type, the dependence
  CLAIM-479's note of 2026-10-27 (later) reads, after A11, as CLAIM-184's
  entrenchment, it grows with the record. Choosing the measure chooses the
  verdict, so it is not filed, and it is not an account.
- **The relation's rigidity as what makes a misfit visible.** CASE-131
  says its misfit was noticed because the false converse "was written
  somewhere visible", and that a forcing which produces nothing false
  leaves no trace. The general form, that the record catches only
  misfits whose forcing writes a false statement, goes against CLAIM-419's
  amendments for ADR-042 and ADR-043: as CLAIM-419 describes them, their
  misfits were distinctions the grammar could not draw (kinds of case
  "told apart only by prose"), not false statements it wrote. The narrow
  form is CASE-131's own.
- **Amendment by addition as revision of the answer space.** CASE-180 and
  CLAIM-479's note find types added beside old ones, never redeclared;
  CLAIM-419's note of 2026-10-27 contrasts this with CEGAR, which only
  splits; CLAIM-470's history reads CASE-178 as the answer space revised;
  TERM-091 names what grows. Held.

The candidate of the first review, views as what catches the grammar's
misfits, is not repeated here (curation entry of 2026-11-07, 070342).

## Not stated on the record

Integrations the components leave open. Each would need admission by a
route to be filed.

- **Settling or entrenchment.** Whether the grammar's rates fell
  (CASE-178) because the grammar fits or because revising it now costs
  more (CLAIM-475). No component separates them, and the candidate above
  needs its cost measure fixed first.
- **CLAIM-184's count carried to CLAIM-475.** CLAIM-475 reads its
  entrenchment clause as CLAIM-184's, now in the nucleating-argument line;
  no entry says whether CLAIM-184's note of 2026-11-06, that superseded
  anchors stop attracting new structure, bears on it.
- **CLAIM-183 and CLAIM-419.** CLAIM-476 complements CLAIM-183, but no
  entry says whether a fed-back view has ever prompted one of the
  grammar's amendments; the first review found the general link against
  CASE-178.
- **QUESTION-072 and CLAIM-419's limit.** CLAIM-472's note of 2026-10-27
  (later still) reads QUESTION-072 as CLAIM-419's limit made a question of
  who can act; no entry says whether any of the five amendments, or
  ADR-032's refusal, changed whose moves the grammar can hold.

## What it does not say

- That the record's objects are constituted in Barad's sense (CLAIM-476 is
  Proposed), or that any exclusion its grammar makes is wrong (CLAIM-472,
  QUESTION-072).
- That every misfit is caught, or that the grammar's distinctions are the
  right ones (CLAIM-419, CLAIM-469, TERM-089).
- That luria or the record maintains itself (CLAIM-469, CLAIM-476).
- That inquiry makes what it investigates (CLAIM-470), or that its
  askable questions bound what exists (CLAIM-475).
- That the line's layers are a dynamical hierarchy of time scales; CASE-178
  and CASE-180 call that "An interpretation, not shown."
- Anything with standing of its own.
