# THEORY-tmp9psoh: The host lineage elaborated its cytoskeleton, endomembrane system and nuclear machinery before mitochondrial endosymbiosis began, about 2.2 billion years ago: most pre-LECA duplications in eukaryotic gene families of archaeal origin predate it

## Source

Kay et al. (2026), [LIT-1566](../literature.d/LIT-1566.md), read in full in [NOTE-1234](../notes.d/NOTE-1234.md). The authors conclude that the host elaborated these systems before mitochondrial endosymbiosis, which they align to the onset of duplication in genes of alphaproteobacterial origin.
