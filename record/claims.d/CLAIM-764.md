# CLAIM-764: Experiment is not the only way to advance

## The claim

The owner, U38: "we should probably prioritize experimentation based on
which open questions we hope to answer", and "I don't think we should
feel like experimentation is the only opportunity to progress science
here. we have a lot of value here from a philosophy perspective already."

ChatGPT's agreement (A84) named the kinds of advance: "establishing a
distinction that previous theories conflate, finding a counterexample to
an apparently general claim, demonstrating that two accounts are
equivalent only under specific assumptions, or clarifying what would
count as an explanation." It singled out the critical result, one that
"shows that an apparently promising account cannot support its claimed
conclusion", with the record's own example: that a hard constraint added
to a fixed dynamical system cannot enlarge what it can reach existentially
is why the claim that constraints expand capabilities needs costs
([CLAIM-761](CLAIM-761.md)). "No new experiment was necessary to establish the
distinction."

## What it does not say

That experiments are not needed: where a question turns on what happens
in the world, only observation answers it. Nor which questions come
first; that is [QUESTION-496](../questions.d/QUESTION-496.md).
