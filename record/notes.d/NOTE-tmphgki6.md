summary: >-
  Levin argues that biomedicine's bottom-up, molecular ("hardware") paradigm
  cannot achieve anatomical control (birth defects, regeneration, cancer,
  aging) and should be complemented by a top-down program that treats cells
  and tissues as an "agential material" with memories, goals and
  problem-solving competencies, i.e. a collective intelligence navigating
  anatomical, transcriptional and physiological spaces. Systems sit on a
  "spectrum of persuadability" (hardware rewiring → setpoint rewriting →
  training → reasoning with), and where a system sits is "not a
  philosophical question but an empirical one". Bioelectric networks (ion
  channels, gap junctions) are the "cognitive glue" that binds cells into
  larger collectives with larger goals, and the most tractable interface;
  existing ion-channel drugs can be repurposed as "electroceuticals". Cancer
  is read as cells' "cognitive light cone" shrinking: "cancer cells are not
  more selfish; they just have smaller selves", with gap-junction closure
  starting a feedback of dissociation. A roadmap follows: rewrite setpoints
  rather than force states (getting the system's "buy-in"),
  first/second/third-order interventions, patient-derived biobots, disease
  as learned attractors, and "eavesdropping" on tissues as sensors. The
  end-game is an "Anatomical Compiler".

# NOTE-tmphgki6: Levin 2024/2025 — The Multiscale Wisdom of the Body: Collective Intelligence as a Tractable Interface for Next‐Generation Biomedicine

## Contribution

A programmatic synthesis of Levin's lab's framework (basal cognition, bioelectric control, cognitive light cone) for a biomedical audience, with a classification of interventions by the agency they presuppose. It is a position paper: claims rest on cited experiments, mostly from the author's lab; no new data.

## Key results

- Abstract: "we can study development, regenerative repair, and cancer suppression as behaviors of a collective intelligence of cells navigating the spaces of possible morphologies and transcriptional and physiological states."
- Abstract: "The bioelectric networks that bind individual cells toward large‐scale anatomical goals are an especially tractable interface to organ‐level plasticity, and tools to modulate them already exist."
- §1 / Box 1: the position is "pragmatic and naturalist", with "empirical success in biomedicine and bioengineering should be the only arbiters of conceptual frameworks."
- Fig. 1 caption: "the position of any given system (e.g., single cells) on this spectrum is not a philosophical question but an empirical one".
- §5: "the cognitive light cone model suggests that cancer cells are not more selfish; they just have smaller selves."
- §5, cited evidence: "(a) cancer induced by human oncogenes in vivo can be detected early by bioelectrical imaging , (b) transient perturbation of bioelectrical and serotonergic signaling among cells can induce a melanoma‐like phenotype in the absence of carcinogens, oncogenes, or DNA damage , and (c) tumors induced by powerful human oncogenes can be prevented and normalized by managing their bioelectric state" (stray spaces are where citation numbers were stripped).
- §6.1: rewriting setpoints "gets the system's “buy‐in” as to the correct state, avoiding triggering resistive responses because it makes the intervention appear as if it originated within the system itself."
- Fig. 6 caption: a gall is a leaf "prompted by a non‐human bioengineer: an insect parasite that hacks the competencies of the leaf cells"; gall sophistication "matches the overall complexity level of the parasite".
- §6.2: "Packets of stress, setpoints, self‐models, and estimates of safety may be agents in our body".

## Assumptions

- Goal-directedness is cybernetic (return to an attractor after perturbation); "intelligence", "memory", "self" are used in that basal sense, defended pragmatically as metaphors judged by control afforded (Box 1).
- Bioelectric state is causally upstream of anatomy in the cited systems (frog, planaria, tadpole), and those results generalize to human biomedicine.
- Cancer results (a)–(c) are from cited studies, largely animal models from the author's lab.

## Concepts

Diverse intelligence; basal cognition; agential material; spectrum of persuadability; cognitive glue; cognitive light cone; setpoint rewriting; electroceuticals, morphoceuticals, dynamiceuticals; first/second/third-order interventions; biobots/Anthrobots; hyperembryo; disease as learned attractor; anatomical compiler.

## Bearing on the record

**On the LIT's and the exchange's characterization.** A's account (development, regeneration and cancer suppression as collective problem-solving by cells, with bioelectric networks coordinating anatomical outcomes) is accurate. It is a programmatic review, not a report of findings, and the cancer claims are cited, not new.

- **THEORY-583** (Levin 2019 via LIT-439: agents as goal-sharing sets bounded by a cognitive light cone; bioelectric coupling forms larger agents; cancer as the larger agent shrinking to the cell): **supports and restates**, five years on, in a biomedical frame; adds the persuadability spectrum and cites three cancer results (early bioelectric detection, induction without genetic damage, normalization of oncogene-driven tumours) that bear on THEORY-583's `promote_when` ("experiments showing that changing gap-junctional coupling changes which goal is pursued"), though only as citations here: the filer should not count them as read. It does not supply a measurement of the light cone.
- Other held entries (not named in the LIT):
  - **THEORY-135** (Aktipis et al.: cancer as cheating, multicellularity as cheater suppression): **in explicit tension**: Levin's "not more selfish; they just have smaller selves" rejects the cheater framing for a boundary-shrinkage one. A useful rival pairing.
  - **CLAIM-tmpu69tn** (provenance, control and alignment are separate): Levin's setpoint intervention that "appear[s] as if it originated within the system itself" is an exogenous influence engineered to be taken as endogenous: a clean case for the CLAIM's separation of provenance from governance.
  - **CLAIM-tmpoxhqq** (manipulating parasites control a host through its own machinery): the gall example is exactly that, at the tissue level ("hacks the competencies of the leaf cells").
  - **CLAIM-060** (an encompassing organization's success does not establish its constituents' well-being, and conversely): cancer as cells reverting to treating the body as environment is one direction of it.
  - **CLAIM-110** (Brooks, Kelso and Levin's bioelectric selves as three ways of understanding an organized whole).
