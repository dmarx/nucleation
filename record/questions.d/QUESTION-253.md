# QUESTION-253: In the coupled meaning–authority model, is authority's effect on what becomes legible as evidence a third channel beside β and γ, a slower variable with an instability condition of its own, or is it already the commitment regime's κ′, how far the prevailing interpretation bends the reading of the evidence?

## Why it is a question

A22 §4 revised the loop of [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md) and [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md), interpretive advantage
→ authority → further interpretive advantage, by inserting a "**Legibility
regime**" ("Attention, categories, representation, available evidence,
interpretive resources") between the world and the problems an
institution recognizes, with "Feedback: authority can alter the future
legibility regime". It tied the layer to the existing mathematics: "the
coefficient γ, representing interpretation's effect on future authority,
implicitly depends on how evidence is registered and scored", and
"Legibility helps us see that the reading of evidence is itself an
organized activity."

What the record holds:

- **[CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)** (Active, demonstrated): two arrows, β (authority shaping
  interpretation) and γ (interpretation shaping endorsement). The
  symmetric state is unstable when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ).
- **[CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md)** (Active, demonstrated): with committed predictions,
  γ = 4ηs(κ′ − κ̄), where κ′ is how far the shared meaning bends the reading
  of the evidence.
- **[CLAIM-338](../claims.d/CLAIM-338.md)**: influence "decides which operators retrieve and so what
  the next judgement observes", which is β by retrieval.
- **[CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)**: "a loop that selects its own evidence can lock in".

So there are two readings of A22's layer.

- **Renamed.** Legibility is κ′, or β's retrieval, already in the model,
  and the categories and reporting requirements an institution keeps are
  how κ′ is set. Then nothing new needs deriving.
- **A third, slower variable.** If authority changes the categories (call
  their lean r) on a slower time-scale than it changes the reading, and r
  sets κ′, the model gains a state and a longer loop: authority → r → κ′ →
  γ → authority. Its linearization is 3×3, and its instability condition
  need not be [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s. Even where it is stable, a slow r relaxes
  slowly, so a lean could outlast the return of m and a, which the
  two-variable model cannot show.

## What would count as an answer

A three-variable linearization of [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md) with a legibility state r, its
instability condition, and a showing either that the condition reduces to
[CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s with κ′ replaced by a function of r (the layer is κ′ renamed)
or that it allows an instability, or a slow mode, that no choice of the
two-variable parameters gives. [CASE-106](../cases.d/CASE-106.md) is the construction it would
extend.

## What it does not ask

Whether any institution's categories follow such dynamics. That needs
cases the record does not hold. The readings of 2026-10-21 do not supply
one. Agenda-setting ([THEORY-842](../theory.d/THEORY-842.md); [THEORY-838](../theory.d/THEORY-838.md), Proposed) is an
empirical candidate for which facts become legible enough to count as
evidence, but it is an aggregate cross-section with no dynamics. Szeidl and
Szucs's endogenous-ownership counterfactual has the shape of the slower
variable, authority setting owners' ideology and ideology setting slant
([THEORY-852](../theory.d/THEORY-852.md)), but only across episodes in a static model,
not as a dynamical one. Nor whether legibility should be adopted
as a term ([TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) is Proposed).

## Answer, 2026-10-21

[CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md) (demonstrated, Active), on the construction [CASE-124](../cases.d/CASE-124.md).
Both readings above hold, for different questions about the model. For
whether symmetry breaks, the regime is renamed: det(I − J₃) is (1 − λ)
times [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s expression with β + νε/(1 − λ) for β and δ + με/(1 − λ)
for δ, and the slower the regime the more it adds. For how long a lean
lasts and how the system returns, it is not: the regime is a third mode,
and in the second witness a lean in r holds m and a off symmetry long
after the two-variable system would have returned. The construction was
proposed in the owner's review of 2026-10-21 with the route r → m; the
route r → a, the reduction and the stable witness were added when it was
checked. The route r → a is a direct effect of the regime on scoring.
This question's own reading, r setting κ′, is different: because
γ = 4ηs(κ′ − κ̄), a regime that only varies κ′ enters the authority update
as r·m, second order at symmetry, and changes no linear threshold (noted
at the owner's second review of 2026-10-21). What this question does not ask, whether any institution's
categories have such dynamics, stays open.
