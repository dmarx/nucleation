# THEORY-tmpvst9f: Choifer argues that Piaget's egocentrism is an undifferentiation of self and other rather than a preference for one's own view, that children under about four switch between vantage points without assigning them to anyone, and that only passing false belief shows the other grasped as an epistemically separate subject

## The account

Piaget's childish egocentrism is an inability to self-refer, not an excess
of self-reference, so it can only decrease with age. Before about 4.5
children switch vantage points within joint attention without attributing
them to subjects; passing false belief marks the differentiation of self
and other.

## Evidence

Conceptual argument reinterpreting published developmental findings.
