# LEAD-128: Find or make a fit of the expected-value-of-control model in which control intensity falls with its cost

## What it would do

Search for published fits; failing one, fit the model to an open
effort-discounting data set.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md): "Proposed because no fit in the record shows control
  intensity falling with its cost"

## What would count

Intensity falling with cost promotes [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md).

## Note of 2026-11-22: carried out — a fit was made; no cost-side published fit found

Carried out by the assistant in this session. A search of open literature
(Europe PMC, arXiv) found no published fit that plainly demonstrates control
intensity *falling with its cost* as its model-compared result; the closest
open EVC accumulator fits demonstrate the complementary *payoff* direction
(Leng, Yee, Ritz & Shenhav 2021, PMC8746743, CC BY; Masís Obando, Musslick &
Cohen 2025, PMC12595486). So the fit was made, as this lead provides for.

[CASE-tmprt0hl](../cases.d/CASE-tmprt0hl.md) fits the expected-value-of-control effort-cost model to
122 participants' cognitive-effort-discounting choices (OSF vnj8x, CC-BY).
The fitted cost of control rises with task load, the cost term is required
(likelihood-ratio χ²(3) = 10.5, p = 0.015; AIC favours it), group-mean
subjective value falls monotonically with intensity, and three-quarters of
agents have a positive marginal cost of control. [CLAIM-tmpwma3v](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwma3v.md) states the
finding and corroborates [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md) and [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md).

What remains: the fit measures intensity by revealed choice, not by the
accumulator parameter or conflict adaptation [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md)'s `promote_when`
(1) names, and touches none of `promote_when` (2)'s dissociation. Whether the
evidence suffices to promote [THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) (and with it [CLAIM-358](../claims.d/CLAIM-358.md),
[CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md)) is the owner's call. The status and stage of this lead are
left for the owner to mark.
