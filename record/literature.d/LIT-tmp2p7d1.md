  (I3E 2005), IFIP, pp. 141–155, DOI 10.1007/0-387-29773-1_10. The paper

  and reconfiguring e-government services consistently when law or
  organization changes. The **Ontology Management System** has four parts: -
  **Service Modeller** - **KAON2 OI-Modeller** - **Service Registry** -
  **Service API**, which detects inconsistencies automatically and assists
  in resolving them. The **Service Configuration System** turns the
  resulting service model into deployed web-service orchestrations. A fixed
  **meta-ontology cluster** is made up of seven ontologies: - Legal -
  Organisational - Lifecycle - Domain - Service - LifeEvent - Profile The
  **Lifecycle Ontology** records design decisions and their reasons, and
