# CLAIM-015: Classical structuralism studies relations within a system, whereas this theory makes the transport of relational systems its object and does not require structure to remain invariant

## The claim

A102: "classical structuralism tends to emphasize relations within systems,
whereas our proposal makes **the transport, reconstruction, and transformation
of those relational systems** the central object of study." A102 §3: "We do not
require the structure to remain invariant. Instead, we investigate the extent to
which particular relations survive, change, or cease to be jointly realizable."

## Why it was replaced

The owner quoted those two sentences at U37 ([CLAIM-040](CLAIM-040.md)). A105: "My earlier
contrast between structural invariance and structural transport was too sharp."
Its replacement is [CLAIM-113](CLAIM-113.md).
