# LIT-986: Transformers learn factored representations

Adam Shai, Loren Amdahl-Culleton, Casper L. Christensen et al. (2026), *arXiv:2602.02385*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-758](../notes.d/NOTE-758.md).*

- **The theory is elementary and exact.** Tensor-product operators keep a
  product belief a product (Prop. 2.2). Product beliefs embed linearly and
  invertibly in a direct sum of per-factor subspaces (Thm 2.3). Off the
  product manifold the embedding loses the total correlation among factors
  (App. A.5). Dependency chains between factors are covered.
- **What trained transformers do.** With five factors they use about 10–11
  effective dimensions, not 242, even at d_model = 480. Each factor's belief
  is linearly decodable. The factor subspaces become nearly orthogonal in
  their first two components. The structure is visible already in the
  token embedding.
- **The noise result.** With tokens randomized at rate ε, every run
  collapses to about 10 dimensions within hundreds of steps and then
  expands. For ε ≤ 0.05 it barely departs from the noiseless case.
- **Its limits.** The paper calls the effect an inductive bias of the
  architecture, but its appendix leaves open whether training dynamics are
  the source. RNNs and LSTMs factor less sharply. All data are synthetic.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-060](../questions.d/QUESTION-060.md), and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-758](../notes.d/NOTE-758.md). [THEORY-256](../theory.d/THEORY-256.md) is filed from it as a partial answer: a
family with known relevant variables in which the learned variables match
them when factoring is lossless. When factoring is lossy, the cheap
representation is the one training reaches first. It varies the data, not
the objective or the architecture, so it does not apportion the two that
[QUESTION-060](../questions.d/QUESTION-060.md) names. The seeded reading that cheapness "fixes the learned
variables" was stronger than the paper: under heavy noise the objective
pulls the representation away from the factored form. The
anthology-candidate flag stays. An anthology topic on interpretability
could hold it, though no instruction for practice was found.
