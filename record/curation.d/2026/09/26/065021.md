The earlier pass over the tag (the previous entry) added eight works whose
subject is parts and wholes. Two of them, [LIT-025](../../record/literature.d/LIT-025.md) and [LIT-027](../../record/literature.d/LIT-027.md), had already been
read. The other six are now read in full:

| work | status | what the reading found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEP, *Biological Individuals* ([LIT-168](../../record/literature.d/LIT-168.md)) | Active | Wilson & Barker's own nine-region framework: organism / some parts / some groups, crossed with living agent / Darwinian individual. It never defines "part" and gives no rule for which parts or groups count. |
| Bourrat, *Reproducees, reproducers, and Darwinian individuals* ([LIT-157](../../record/literature.d/LIT-157.md)) | Active | nested statuses, with material overlap necessary for reproducers but not reproducees. Its measures are recipes, never computed. |
| Baedke, *The Organism* ([LIT-167](../../record/literature.d/LIT-167.md)) | Active | the clearest statement of persisters vs overcomers. Separation from the environment is [LIT-110](../../record/literature.d/LIT-110.md)'s model, re-presented. |
| Griesemer & Shavit, *Scaffolding individuality* ([LIT-188](../../record/literature.d/LIT-188.md)) | Proposed | a sketch. Its non-circularity is one sentence in the abstract, and its third stage presupposes the group. |
| Schwitzgebel, *If materialism is true…* ([LIT-159](../../record/literature.d/LIT-159.md)) | Active | a whole of conscious parts can be conscious; it rejects anti-nesting, including IIT's exclusion postulate |
| Clark & Chalmers, *The Extended Mind* ([LIT-097](../../record/literature.d/LIT-097.md)) | Active | the step from coupling to parthood rests on assumed functionalism and a proposal about the word "belief". "Part" is never given a mereological sense. |

## What the readings corrected

The skims overstated in the same direction as the first pass found: a proposal
was reported as a result, or a sufficient condition as a list of paradigm
features.

- **Clark & Chalmers' conditions** on a coupled resource were summarised as
  sufficient ("provided…"). The paper says it gives no "categorical answers".
- **Schwitzgebel's §6** does not test the Dretske, Dennett, Humphrey and Tononi
  theories. It rebuts four objections, Chalmers's the most seriously.
- **Bourrat's dossier** had reproductive causal control evolving through
  scaffolding. The paper (p. 21) says scaffolded collectives get control for
  free, and that what must evolve is autonomy.
- **The SEP entry's framework** was credited to Pepper & Herron, who only pose
  the question.

## Where the works disagree

- **Genes.** Bourrat ([LIT-157](../../record/literature.d/LIT-157.md)) makes genes, like hearts, mere reproducees. The
  SEP entry ([LIT-168](../../record/literature.d/LIT-168.md)) places genes as Darwinian individuals. The record holds
  both. Neither is retired.
- **Bourrat against his own earlier papers** ([LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md), [LIT-049](../../record/literature.d/LIT-049.md)):
  - *Inherited:* nothing is computed; the environment is handled by
    conditioning; and the control measure is biased toward fine grains.
  - *Fixed:* autonomy (associational) and control (interventional) are kept
    apart.
  - *Scale rule:* "smallest object at maximal MI" runs opposite to [LIT-049](../../record/literature.d/LIT-049.md)'s
    coarsest grain.

## A defect found and fixed along the way

The script that writes a Read NOTE rewrites the LIT's summary. It kept a
citation head only when the head ended in ")." or ">.". For "Clark et al.
(1998), DOI-10.1093/analys/58.1.7." it fell back to splitting at the first
". ", and kept only "Clark et al.". That dropped the year and the identifier.

- **Two of this pass's summaries** ([LIT-097](../../record/literature.d/LIT-097.md), [LIT-188](../../record/literature.d/LIT-188.md)) were hit.
- **Eight summaries from earlier readings** were hit too: [LIT-001](../../record/literature.d/LIT-001.md), [LIT-031](../../record/literature.d/LIT-031.md),
  [LIT-035](../../record/literature.d/LIT-035.md), [LIT-053](../../record/literature.d/LIT-053.md), [LIT-069](../../record/literature.d/LIT-069.md), [LIT-080](../../record/literature.d/LIT-080.md), [LIT-087](../../record/literature.d/LIT-087.md) and [LIT-094](../../record/literature.d/LIT-094.md).

All ten were restored from each document's own fields. The script now matches
"Author (YEAR), identifier." first.

- **Check that finds it:** any summary whose first lines carry no "(YYYY"
  lost its head.
- **Left alone:** [LIT-074](../../record/literature.d/LIT-074.md), whose unverified head is deliberate.
