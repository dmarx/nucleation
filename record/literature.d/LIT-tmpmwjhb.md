# LIT-tmpmwjhb: Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition

Umpleby, Stuart A. (2016). Vladimir Lefebvre's Theory of Two Systems of Ethical Cognition. *Journal of Systemics, Cybernetics and Informatics* 14(5): 65-67. https://www.iiisci.org/Journal/PDV/sci/pdfs/IP020LL16.pdf

## Key takeaways

Two ethical systems from one formal model; first (West): the end does not justify the means, compromise is saintly; second (USSR): the end justifies the means, confrontation is saintly. Anecdotal, uncritical.
