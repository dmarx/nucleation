# CLAIM-545: Freedom of will, in the operational sense, is an agent's governance of its own governability: the extent to which its own organization makes the relevant distinctions legible, gives the demands they raise practical efficacy, directs its transitions, and revises the relations that govern those three, including under another's interference; openness, sensitivity and controllability of outcomes each fall short of it, and domination is another's capacity to govern those same relations, used or not

*Returned to CLAIM from [SYNTHESIS-007](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-007.md) on 2026-11-08 ([ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)). The notes below that speak of a synthesis are the synthesis's own.*

*Moved from CLAIM-545, where its development up to version 2 is recorded, at the synthesis review of 2026-11-07 ([ADR-078](../decisions.d/ADR-078.md)). Admitted as an independent hypothesis ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)).*

## The integration

A26, headed "Freedom is the governance of one's own governability", put it
as: "Freedom is not independence from influence. It is the organized
capacity to determine which influences become authoritative."

The record already holds the parts:

- **The dimensions of freedom of will**, as a partial order and not a
  scalar ([CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md), the owner's of 2026-10-17), with the cost of control
  ([CLAIM-345](CLAIM-345.md)).
- **Normative efficacy's three achievements**, legibility, volitional
  efficacy and correctability ([CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md), the owner's of 2026-10-31), on
  [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md) and [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md).
- **Reflexive revision** of one's governing standards as what endogenous
  governance adds ([CLAIM-485](CLAIM-485.md), [CLAIM-424](CLAIM-424.md)), with the regime, its governing
  organization and the capacity to transform it separable ([CLAIM-025](CLAIM-025.md),
  [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md)).
- **Outside governance without decisions**, through evidence or criteria
  ([CLAIM-404](CLAIM-404.md)).
- **Sensitivity is not control** ([CLAIM-533](CLAIM-533.md), the owner's U16 and U20).

The claim identifies freedom, operationally, with the agent's own governance
of these relations, its governability. The architecture in which routing and
transport come apart, a QK circuit choosing what is read and an OV circuit
what it carries ([THEORY-988](../theory.d/THEORY-988.md)), is the owner's U23 analogy for the
separation of legibility from efficacy ([TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s note of 2026-11-02).
Pettit's non-domination ([LIT-1614](../literature.d/LIT-1614.md)) supplies the last clause: an agent can be
unfree because another holds an unaccountable channel to those relations,
whether or not it is ever used. *(Correction (a) below: [LIT-1614](../literature.d/LIT-1614.md) no longer grounds
this clause.)*

"Freedom is an organizational achievement", A26's other line, is [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md),
the owner's term, which A26 did not cite.

**Its components, with voice and standing** (all premises Active):

## Admission

Admitted under [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s hypothesis route: the integration can fail while every component holds.

- **Matched agents.** Ann and Bea are matched in present organization on every clause of the title, with "relevant" fixed as in correction (d). Bea's organization was installed by covert conditioning and the manipulator is dead, so no channel to her relations remains and the domination clause is idle ([THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md)'s case, with [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s formation provenance). Stipulate a historical condition on freedom, so that Bea is unfree. No premise speaks to formation history and no premise's `defeated_if` is met, yet the identification fails.
- **The last clause.** An unused, unaccountable channel that leaves freedom intact, the liberal reading against the republican one. No component settles it.

**Free parameter.** "Relevant", left open; correction (d) closes it.

The derived and integration routes were not needed.

## What it does not say

- **That the agent's governance is justified.** A26 wrote "appropriate
  practical authority" and "warranted changes". [CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md), which A26 cites,
  says none of its three achievements "establishes that the demand or the
  revision is justified", and [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md) says viability does not establish
  what ought to persist. The claim is descriptive.
- **That it settles ultimate responsibility or metaphysical free will**
  ([CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md); [CLAIM-022](CLAIM-022.md)).
- **Whose governance it is when the governing standards are themselves
  revised** ([QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md)).
- **That capture is self-maintaining.** A26 called the [CLAIM-400](CLAIM-400.md) dynamics
  "self-maintaining capture". [CLAIM-400](CLAIM-400.md)'s model is linear with λ < 1: below
  threshold a lean decays, slowly. Self-maintenance needs the system past
  threshold ([CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md), [CLAIM-340](CLAIM-340.md)) or hysteresis ([CLAIM-463](CLAIM-463.md)). A25 had this
  right; A26 dropped it.
- **That the present profile is enough.** The claim is stated in present
  organization, and [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) and [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) are the objection to any
  such condition. It is the claim's defeater, not something it answers.

## Note of 2026-11-03: tolerance bounds, the owner's next step

The owner's U27 followed this synthesis with "compatibility of tolerance
bounds", and U28 with "nucleation has some sheaf theory tools that might be
useful here". That gives the clause "revises the relations that govern those
three" a formal object. An agent can move the mismatch along the tolerance
frontier of [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md), change which overlaps are evaluated, or enlarge
its repertoire so that the frontier itself moves ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)). Only the last is
relief; the first is a choice of who bears the mismatch, and the second can
fake compatibility. Motivational integration so understood is
[CLAIM-534](CLAIM-534.md). A29's closing "domination occurs when the power to define,
conceal, or revise compatibility is exercised without effective
accountability" restates this claim's last clause and is not filed.

## Note of 2026-11-04: normative axes, from "Organizational Nucleation and SLT"

The owner's U10: "i wonder if this framework alludes to some useful axes for normative evaluation?"

**A10's axes.**
- justificatory standing;
- legibility and recognition;
- effective voice and non-domination;
- fidelity of accommodation;
- distribution of burdens;
- sustainable capability;
- reflexive accountability.

A10 also proposed an "accommodation-profile set" of tolerances, cost bearers, legibility and voice, ordered by inclusion. Its synthesis: "**The normative quality of an organizational arrangement depends not only on what it realizes, but also on which demands it recognizes, how it determines permissible accommodations, how it distributes their burdens, whose contributions remain consequential, and whether those arrangements can be justifiably contested and revised.**"

**What the record already holds.** Most of this is already here.
- **Legibility, efficacy and revision:** this claim's own clauses. Directed legibility is [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md).
- **Non-domination:** this claim's last clause, after Pettit.
- **Burdens:** the note of 2026-11-03 says that moving the mismatch along [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md)'s frontier "is a choice of who bears the mismatch". [CLAIM-534](CLAIM-534.md)'s allocation part says the same.
- **Fidelity:** suppression faking compatibility is [CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)'s relief condition and [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md)'s omission clause.
- **Justification:** [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md), [CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md) and [CLAIM-060](CLAIM-060.md) disclaim it.
- **Just tolerances:** [CLAIM-416](CLAIM-416.md)'s "a fair test need not be neutral across competences" is the record's form of A10's "the smallest possible tolerance is not necessarily the most just tolerance".

**What A10 adds.** It turns this claim's descriptive clauses into evaluative questions. Two of A's phrasings are new to the record: "accommodation equity", which A flags as "a new proposal, not an established Nucleation term", and "epistemic accessibility", from [CLAIM-475](CLAIM-475.md), for objections that are too costly even to formulate. A10 itself: "This doesn't yet constitute a moral theory."

**The second appearance of a line.** A10 §2C's "Efficiency can conceal domination" is the second appearance of A29's line, recorded in the note of 2026-11-03 and not filed: "domination occurs when the power to define, conceal, or revise compatibility is exercised without effective accountability". It restates this claim's last clause. If it gets a code, it belongs as a revision of that clause.

The wording of the axes is A's, sharpened by the reader; adopting them is the owner's call. No status changes.

## Note of 2026-11-07: dynamical subordination, from "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"

A12 §4.3 of the exchange (2026-11-06 to 07) names "**dynamical subordination**: the degree to which one process controls another process's accessible rhythms, amplitudes, or responses", and says the oscillator setting "separates the *location of control* from its *legitimacy* and from the *capacity being controlled*". That is a dynamical reading of this claim's domination clause, with one difference: A's measure is control exercised, while this claim's domination is a capacity, used or not. A's caveat, "The dynamics alone do not establish the normative relationship", is kept in [CLAIM-616](CLAIM-616.md) and [CASE-225](../cases.d/CASE-225.md). Leaves the claim as it was; no status change.

## Note of 2026-11-12: three restatements, from "Medical Ethics and Media Enshitification"

The exchange (2026-11-11) restates this claim three times without citing it
as such. A17's thesis: "Autonomy is not independence from the processes that

regulate, integrate, and revise the conditions under which those processes

2026-11-12 as one thesis in three versions; each adds only its examples, so
nothing was filed. No status change.

## Note of 2026-11-12: Naudts, Helberger, Veale and Sax (2025), read
