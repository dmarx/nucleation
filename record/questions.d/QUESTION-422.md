# QUESTION-422: Does an independent re-estimate on US portage and fall-line sites find that the portage effect on density is larger in the late 20th century than in the 19th relative to 1850, that the portage coefficient conditional on lagged density falls to insignificance by the mid-20th century, and that conditional on density portage sites show no differences in the age, price or quantity of housing and other sunk assets?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1505](../theory.d/THEORY-1505.md) reports Bleakley and Lin's within-watershed comparison, read
in the post-referee working paper. The identification is cross-sectional,
the rise relative to 1850 may partly be late adjustment, and the authors
do not rule out a persistent sunk factor.

## What would answer it

A re-estimate finding no decay, convergence to comparably dense places by
the mid-20th century and no sunk-asset signature would answer yes; decay
toward the comparison sites, or a durable sunk factor oversupplied at
portages, would answer no.
