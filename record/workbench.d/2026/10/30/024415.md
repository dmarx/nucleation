**Why now.** The owner asked, after the sixteenth pass, to revisit the triage
procedure before the next pass and to build a fresh worklist from the
record's current state. ADR-057 set the procedure on 2026-10-27. Sixteen
passes and the ADR-047 kind-1 sweep have run since then.

**What the procedure got wrong, measured on main after pass 16.**

- **The ranking had stopped ranking.** ADR-057 ordered work by reach: how
  many entries rest, transitively, on a Proposed one. When it was adopted,
  the top entries had 19, 13 and 13 dependents. Now the most any Proposed
  entry has is 1, and only 13 entries have any. From pass 12 on, clusters
  were chosen by hand.
- **The unblocked list read no blocker notes.** 124 claims were listed as
  unblocked, and 45 of them already carried a recent note saying what they
  wait on. A pass had to read each one to find that out.
- **The move that freed most claims had no section.** In passes 13 to 16
  most promotions came from showing that a Proposed ground was corroboration
  (ADR-058). The worklist showed only the premise, not that it was the one
  thing holding the claim back.
- **The procedure was not in the record.** The reviewer brief, the
  conventions for applying rulings, and a local check for inverted grounds
  lived in a session's scratchpad. That is how the wrong precedent for a
  narrowing's dropped clauses travelled for seven passes before a reviewer
  checked it against ADR-047. The brief still carried a variant of it
  ("move the rest to a QUESTION") until today.

**What changed.**

- **The worklist's order** (ADR-057 version 6). The sections that must be
  empty come first. Stale converses joins them: a Proposed reading still
  listing an Active claim under `supports` is an inverted ground waiting for
  the repair bot. Then the work, in the order a pass takes it:
  1. claims ready for a ruling, grouped by line;
  2. sole blockers, the unheld premises ranked by how many claims each alone
     holds back;
  3. blocker notes that a premise has changed since;
  4. unblocked claims held by a recent note;
  5. Proposed readings no source of which is read in full, which need
     reading, not review.

  Reach is printed last.
- **The tests.** Each new section is fired on the fixture record in
  `test_worklist.py`. The suite went from 13 tests to 18.
- **The procedure.** `record/status-review/README.md` now holds it, with the
  narrowing rule as ADR-047 states it and the conventions every pass applied.
  It also records the owner's delegation of rulings of 2026-10-29.

**What did not change.** The criteria for promotion, the evidence rulings,
and the rule that reasoning lives on the entries.

`written()` still counts any history note that names an objection, even one
that only adds the `objected_by` edge. This is a known limit, left because
tightening it would need the note to say what was conceded, and no field
holds that.

Stale `inactive-ok` acknowledgements are left to the lint, which already
reports them (DP-004).

3. **Pass 19: the rest.**

pass re-runs the worklist first, so this plan is a starting order, not a
ledger.
