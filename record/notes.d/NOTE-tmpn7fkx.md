# NOTE-tmpn7fkx: Froese — Irruption Theory

## Contribution

It names a problem and a research programme rather than a result. The
problem: enactive and "embodied dynamicist" accounts give motivation a role
only by identifying its efficacy with that of a system-level property (a
global value constraint, an order parameter), "while assuming operational
equivalency", which "push[es] the problem of reification to a higher level
of description" (abstract; §4). The programme: treat behaviour's
underdetermination by its material basis as where motivation shows up,
and measure it as unpredictability, by information-theoretic entropy of
neural, physiological and behavioural signals (§5.2.1).

## Key insight

The difference a motivation makes cannot be seen as a motivated difference.
It can only show up indirectly, as "a special subset of material changes
that are not attributable to the activity's material basis" (§5.2). Hence
the working hypothesis: "The more an agent's embodied activity is
motivated, the less that activity is determined by its material basis"
(§5.2). The effect is undirected, so direction has to come from somewhere
else. Froese places it in the body's attunement, built by evolution,
development and learning: "the unfolding of future behavior can then be
largely offloaded into the affordances and constraints of the
agent–environment system" (§5.2.3).

## Assumptions

- **Axiom 1, motivational efficacy**: "An agent's motivations, as such,
  make a difference to the material basis of the agent's behavior" (§5.1).
  Its support is "our first-person perspective".
- **Axiom 2, incomplete materiality**: "It is impossible to measure how
  motivations, as such, make a difference to the material basis of
  behavior" (§5.1).
- **Axiom 3, underdetermined materiality**: "An agent's behavior is
  underdetermined by its material basis" (§5.1). Adopted "in line with
  libertarian positions", with quantum physics cited against "a clockwork
  universe". Its extent "remains to be established".
- **Motivations are taken as given.** The paper is about efficacy, "while
  taking the presence of motivations, such as value, for granted" (§2).
- **Irruptions are small.** They "would have to be exceptionally
  small-scale; otherwise, their consequences at the material level would
  have presumably already been noticed" (§5.2.2).

## Key results

There are no theorems, models or data of the author's own. What the paper
establishes is a set of definitions and an argument.

- **The diagnosis (§§3–4).** Di Paolo, Rohde and De Jaegher's enactive
  value moves value from a local appraisal to "a global constraint on all
  processes", but keeps "identifying value with an aspect of the material
  living system". Thompson and Varela's consciousness-as-order-parameter
  fares no better: "it ultimately does not seem to matter whether we assign
  the subject's efficacy to a collective property (order parameter) or to
  the efficacy of a material property (neural substrate)". "Embodied
  dynamicism", which appeals to "emergent top-down constraints in nonlinear
  dynamical systems", shares the problem; Juarrero 1999 (ref. 56) and Kelso
  1995 (ref. 57) are among its examples (§4).
- **The libertarian double challenge (§4).** Reject "the complete material
  determination of agent behavior" while not "falling into the opposite
  extreme of completely random agent behavior". Irruption theory is
  offered as "a novel systematic framework that solves this double
  challenge".
- **Three questions, three theses (§5.2).** How is an irruption quantified
  (irruption thesis: as material underdetermination, by entropy)? How does
  it make a difference (scalability thesis: "a poised system such that it
  amplifies microscopic irruptions to macroscopic fluctuations")? How does
  it lead to appropriate behaviour (attunement thesis: "an attuned system
  such that it responds to scaled up irruptions in a context-sensitive and
  adaptive manner")?
- **Scalability (§5.2.2).** Jonas's cone balanced on its tip; chaos,
  defined as sensitivity "to microscopic, nearly infinitely small,
  differences in initial conditions" (ref. 86, which is Langton's "edge of
  chaos" paper); 1/f noise; self-organized criticality; global-workspace
  "ignition". "Future work will need to work out a more detailed
  neurophysiological account of this scalability."
- **Attunement (§5.2.3), the load-bearing passage.** "Irruptions cannot
  directly control a motivated activity's material basis; they can only
  serve to counteract existing material constraints by increasing their
  underdetermination. Essentially, the changes induced by irruptions are
  arbitrary with respect to those material constraints … they can loosen
  the organization of the current behavior but cannot force the formation
  of the behavior that is to come."
- **The self-optimization model as the answer (§5.2.3).** A Hopfield
  network is reset at intervals to "an arbitrary configuration" and allowed
  to relax, with Hebbian learning; it "will undergo a process of
  generalization over the set of visited equilibria" and comes to reach
  "otherwise unvisited, deeper equilibria" (Watson, Buckley and Mills 2011,
  ref. 102; Fig. 4). Froese proposes that motivations, expressed as
  irruptions, "could serve the role of the 'resets'", which "holds
  potential to provide a novel systems theoretic answer to the main concern
  raised against libertarian interpretations … how an agent's behavior can
  be effective even when it is underdetermined".
- **Evidence read as support (§5.2.1).** Levels of consciousness track
  neural entropy (the "entropic brain"; dreamless sleep low, psychedelics
  high). Sergent et al. 2021's increased variability in near-threshold
  auditory classification. Greater neural entropy with more task degrees of
  freedom. Schurger et al.'s finding that movement onset follows increased
  stochastic fluctuation, read as answering their challenge of making such
  fluctuations attributable to the subject.

## Concepts

- **Motivated activity** — "any embodied activity that is directed at an
  end", including regulation; all cognitive activity counts (§1).
- **Motivation-involving account** — one on which motivation "as such"
  participates in generating behaviour, not via a material surrogate (§4).
- **Irruption** — the increase in material underdetermination associated
  with motivational involvement; from the Latin for a sudden bursting in,
  recalling Ashby's "breaks" (§5.2).
- **Embodied dynamicism** — Thompson's label for accounts that explain
  motivated activity by emergent top-down constraints in nonlinear dynamics
  (§4).
- **Relaxed naturalism** — nature counted as including an agent's
  motivations as well as its materiality (§6).

## Connections

- **Fuchs, *In Defense of the Human Being*** (ref. 39) supplies the
  starting position: freedom needs only that "actions are physically
  underdetermined" (§5).
- **Ashby's homeostat** (ref. 69): adaptation by instability-triggered
  random "breaks". Irruptions are modelled on these.
- **Watson, Buckley and Mills 2011** (ref. 102) and Froese's own
  self-optimization papers (refs. 11, 103–106).
- **Juarrero 1999** (ref. 56; LIT-tmpqbxw7) is cited among the
  embodied-dynamicist accounts that the diagnosis targets. **Kane is not
  cited.** Neither is any work on control of chaos.
- Later work: Froese 2024's absorption (*Entropy* 26:288, not held), and
  Sánchez-Cañizares 2024 and 2026 (LIT-1132; LIT-tmpqt7ea, NOTE-tmpbmoj1).

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpex9rg: a held source that says sensitivity is not control, in
  its own terms.** The scalability thesis is the Kane-style move the claim
  addresses: microscopic indeterminacy amplified by chaos or criticality
  into behaviour. The attunement thesis then concedes the claim's central
  point: amplified irruptions "cannot directly control", are "arbitrary
  with respect to those material constraints", and "cannot force the
  formation of the behavior that is to come" (§5.2.3). Froese's answer to
  where direction comes from is not a channel. It is attunement: a body
  shaped so that perturbations relax into good states. That answer has a
  consequence the claim predicts. In the self-optimization model the resets
  are "an arbitrary configuration", so on the model offered nothing
  distinguishes a motivated irruption from noise: replace the irruptions
  with random resets and the model does the same. The agent's share
  against noise, which the claim says amplification cannot supply, is not
  supplied here either. This is a second independent statement of the
  claim's gap, after Bishop's SEP entry, and from a libertarian.
- **CLAIM-478: a rival reading of constraints.** Froese's §4 targets the
  family of views CLAIM-478 belongs to: top-down constraints and order
  parameters as what organizes behaviour. His objection is not that they
  fail to constrain. It is that crediting motivation to them makes
  motivation's efficacy a material surrogate's. That bears on whether
  CLAIM-478's constraints can carry agency, not on whether they make
  structure, which is what CLAIM-478 claims. Noted, not a defeater.
- **CLAIM-354 and QUESTION-071.** On this theory an agent's motivations
  act by widening a system's options, so a manipulator's injected noise and
  the agent's irruption are, by Axiom 2, indistinguishable in any
  measurement. That is QUESTION-071's present-structure problem in an
  extreme form, at the level of a single act. The paper does not raise it.
- **THEORY-328 and THEORY-040 (the luck objection).** The paper claims to
  answer "the main concern raised against libertarian interpretations",
  the randomness horn. What it offers locates the effectiveness in
  attunement, which is the agent's history and not the indeterminacy. That
  is the compatibilist's resource, not a reply to luck. THEORY-tmped3yc
  files this as Proposed.
- **No instruction for machine-learning practice.**

## Limitations

- **No model or measurement of its own.** Every empirical point is a
  reinterpretation of others' findings.
- **Entropy does not separate irruption from noise.** The paper offers no
  way to tell motivated unpredictability from any other. By Axiom 2 it
  cannot, except through correlation with first- and second-person reports
  (§5.2.1).
- **The chaos citation** is to Langton's "Computation at the edge of
  chaos", not to a source on sensitive dependence.
- **The attunement model's resets are arbitrary**, so the model shows that
  random perturbation plus learning can improve an attractor landscape. It
  does not show that motivation adds anything to that.
- Froese says himself that the mechanism "may ultimately turn out to be
  conceptually impossible even, in principle" (§6).

## Open questions

- What measurement would distinguish a motivated irruption from noise
  entering the same variables? The paper's own axioms seem to rule one out.
- In the self-optimization model, does it matter when the resets come or
  where they land? If it does, the timing of resets is a channel, and the
  question becomes whose it is.
