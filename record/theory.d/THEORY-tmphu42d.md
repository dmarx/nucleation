# THEORY-tmphu42d: In Chirkov et al.'s student samples from South Korea, Russia, Turkey and the USA, the more autonomous people's reasons for engaging in practices of any cultural orientation, collectivist and vertical included, the higher their well-being, with no detectable difference across countries or genders; and vertical practices were much less internalized than horizontal ones

## Source

Chirkov, Ryan, Kim and Kaplan (2003), [LIT-tmporplv](../literature.d/LIT-tmporplv.md), pp. 100–106; read in
[NOTE-tmpgd5c5](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpgd5c5.md).

## What was actually shown

**The measure.** For 24 practices, six for each of horizontal and vertical
individualism and collectivism, participants rated external, introjected,
identified and integrated reasons, combined into a relative autonomy index
(pp. 100–103).

**The relation.** In one regression per orientation, the index predicted a
composite of life satisfaction, self-actualization, self-esteem and
(reversed) depressive symptoms, β = .24, .24, .27 and .29 (Table 6), with
no significant country × index interaction among twelve. Within each
country, all sixteen betas were positive and significant (Table 7: Korea
.23–.28, Russia .17–.24, Turkey .34–.42, USA .15–.22). Gender did not
moderate. The relation could have been absent or reversed for collectivist
or vertical practices, or in Korea; it was not.

**Internalization differs by practice.** Horizontal practices averaged 4.68
on the index and vertical 1.60, t(557) = 27.20 (p. 104).

## What this does not say

- **That autonomy causes well-being.** The authors note the reverse is
  "conceivable" (p. 107).
- **That the relation is equally strong everywhere.** Turkey's betas are
  roughly twice the USA's; the interactions are non-significant in small
  samples, which does not bound them.
- **That autonomy is distinct from individualism as a matter of fact.**
  That is SDT's definition. What is shown is that autonomy so measured
  predicts well-being for collectivist practices too.
- **That autonomy is a need** rather than a universal correlate of
  well-being ([QUESTION-tmpee04a](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpee04a.md)).
- **That vertical arrangements frustrate needs.** The lower internalization
  is a mean difference on the authors' item set, which they caution does
  not represent all vertical practices (p. 107).
