# THEORY-2242: Meckes, Cole, Gee and Lancaster report that, in one session of exposure for spider fear, an experimenter-supplied safety behaviour faded, kept or never offered made no detectable difference to outcome at post-treatment or one month, while it raised approach during exposure and acceptability after it

## Source

Meckes, Cole, Gee & Lancaster (2025), [LIT-3123](../literature.d/LIT-3123.md), read in
[NOTE-2332](../notes.d/NOTE-2332.md).

## The account

Fifty-six spider-fearful adults had one 30-minute exposure, randomized to
experimenter-supplied rubber boots faded after 20 minutes, kept for all
30, or not worn. "Repeated measures ANOVAs yielded no significant
time × SB condition interactions across phobia-related outcomes (ps >
.05)", at post-treatment and at about one month. The boots raised
approach in the first trial, and the arm without them rated the
treatment less acceptable afterwards. The authors conclude that "using
(vs. not using) SBs during a single session of exposure generally did not
hinder exposure therapy outcomes" and that "fading SBs did not improve
long-term therapy outcomes". They place it beside Blakey et al. 2019
([LIT-3122](../literature.d/LIT-3122.md)), a four-session trial with the same null.

## What it does not show

That cutting a person's own, established safety behaviour makes no
difference. The boots were new to every participant, and the authors say
an established safety behaviour "selected by the participant" may act
differently. Salkovskis et al. 1999 ([LIT-3121](../literature.d/LIT-3121.md)) cut patients' own
safety behaviours and reported a difference two days later. Nor does it
show equivalence: the null is a non-significant interaction in a sample
powered for a medium effect.

## Bearing

- [QUESTION-045](../questions.d/QUESTION-045.md): evidence on its experiment, null at one month, for a
  supplied safety behaviour.
- [THEORY-058](THEORY-058.md): does not meet route 2 of its promote_when, which asks for
  lasting remission produced by the cut; for the reason above it does not
  refute it either.
