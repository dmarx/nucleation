# CLAIM-405: An individuation is well defined only once its criterion is fixed (the target it serves, the transformations its unity must survive, the scale), and that dependence does not make it arbitrary: once the criterion is fixed a verdict can be false, and differently fixed criteria can pick out units of one system whose boundaries cross rather than nest, none of them less real for it

## The claim

The owner asked at U8 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 about agency,
individuation, boundaries, orders of explanation and
"contextuality/relativity/subjectivity". A8 §9 called the resulting
position "constrained relational realism": "the underlying system is
objectively real, while some scientifically meaningful properties of its
descriptions are objectively relational." A9 named it "objective
relational individuation": "A boundary or individual is not automatically
intrinsic in the sense of being definable independently of every
explanatory or intervention regime. But neither is it constituted solely by
subjective choice. Instead, an individuation claim can be objectively
assessed once its relevant organizational criteria are specified."

Three parts:

1. **Relative.** Which partition wins depends on the target it is
   sufficient for ([CLAIM-152](CLAIM-152.md)). Closure, persistence and adaptation are
   different criteria and need not agree ([QUESTION-093](../questions.d/QUESTION-093.md), [QUESTION-115](../questions.d/QUESTION-115.md)).
2. **Not arbitrary.** With the criterion fixed, the verdict is a fact
   about the system: [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md)'s test can be failed, a partition can fail
   to be sufficient, a coarse-graining can fail to be closed. A8's table:
   "observer-relative need not mean subjective". That is the structure of
   the owner's [CLAIM-363](CLAIM-363.md) for normative judgment ("once they are fixed, the
   judgment can be false"), carried from judgment to description.
3. **Crossing, not nesting.** A9 against two familiar positions, "a
   system has one true, observer-independent boundary" and "its boundary
   is chosen pragmatically by investigators": "A system may exhibit
   several objectively grounded forms of unity that need not share the
   same boundary. ... This is not mere semantic pluralism. It is a claim
   about the possibility of cross-cutting objective organizations." The
   record's cases: [CLAIM-174](CLAIM-174.md)'s dancing couple, an agent at the level of
   the room while lead and follow are agents at the level of the pair,
   and Kenton et al.'s actor-critic, two agents or one on accurate
   descriptions of one system; [CASE-110](../cases.d/CASE-110.md), one system with two adequate
   descriptions for two targets; and [THEORY-820](../theory.d/THEORY-820.md), where closure does not
   survive common refinement, so plurality is not removed by refining
   ([CLAIM-409](CLAIM-409.md)).

A8 §5 kept apart context-indexed objectivity, formal contextuality and
subjectivity, and A9 three kinds of disagreement about a boundary:
empirical (the criterion is fixed and the facts are disputed), conceptual
(which criterion is meant) and normative (which criterion should be used).
The claim makes the first a real disagreement, and the others are what
fixing a criterion has to settle.

**How it stands to [CLAIM-195](CLAIM-195.md).** [CLAIM-195](CLAIM-195.md) says the record's own
persistence criterion for an argument is explicit "without making it
non-arbitrary". That is about choosing the criterion. This claim is about
verdicts once it is chosen, so they do not conflict.

## What it does not say

- **Not which criterion is right.** That is [QUESTION-065](../questions.d/QUESTION-065.md)'s question,
  whether the system can supply its own target, and this claim leaves it
  open.
- **Not that every criterion is as good as every other.** [CLAIM-348](CLAIM-348.md) warns
  against reading authority off the description that preserves a target.
- **Not that standards of individuation are themselves objectively
  ranked.** [CLAIM-363](CLAIM-363.md) has the same limit.
- **Not contextuality.** Units that cross are not local descriptions that
  fail to glue ([CLAIM-219](CLAIM-219.md)'s warning that context dependence is not
  contextuality).

## Note of 2026-10-27: discovered or enacted, and approximate independence

Two questions from the exchange of 2026-10-27 press on this claim. A5 §2,
from Barad: "**Does the interaction structure reveal the preexisting
boundaries of a system, or does the process of interaction also
participate in producing those boundaries?**" This claim makes a verdict
objective once its criterion is fixed; it is silent on whether the
arrangement through which a system is probed is part of what makes the
unit, which is [QUESTION-284](../questions.d/QUESTION-284.md)'s split of detected, maintained and
constituted boundaries. And from Jaksland ([LIT-1673](../literature.d/LIT-1673.md), read later the same day in [NOTE-1230](../notes.d/NOTE-1230.md)): whether
a unit the criterion passes is approximately independent or only
approximately separable for its target, [QUESTION-280](../questions.d/QUESTION-280.md). A10 §2 restated
the claim's point as "**Relativity to conditions does not entail
subjectivity.**" Nothing here changes.

## Note of 2026-10-29: a court's criterion, fixed in advance

[CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md) illustrates this claim with three boundaries for one detention
arrangement: who held the prisoners, who selected and sent them, and who
could bring about their release. *El-Masri*, [LIT-1619](../literature.d/LIT-1619.md), read in
[NOTE-1391](../notes.d/NOTE-1391.md), separates custody, control and responsibility in much the
same way, and on a criterion stated in advance: responsibility follows
the State's having "assumed control over an individual" (§ 233) and its
facilitation, wherever the custody then lay. Under that criterion the
sending State is responsible for the detention abroad (Article 5) and for
the exposure to risk (Article 3), not for the receiving state's acts
(§ 212). It is a worked case of this claim's point: once the criterion is
fixed, the verdict is settled, and the boundary it draws need not be the
geographical one. The claim is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-10-31: Simondon's organization-studies readers, and two senses of "relative"

<!-- inactive-ok-block: THEORY-1280 THEORY-1277 THEORY-1285 ARG-047 — Proposed or Rejected; the readings filed 2026-10-31 and the verdict on one of them, cited as context -->
The readings filed today on Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy 2024 and its
sources say that individuation is relative, in words close to this
claim's. They mean two things by it, and only one of them is this claim's.

- **Relative to other processes.** "individuation is always relative and
  is constituted through/in communication between preindividual elements"
  ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p. 1355), and "The individual is thus 'only individual in
  relation to the multiplicity of preindividual potentialities that exist
  within it'" (the same page, quoting Bluemink). This is an ontological
  relationality: what an individual is depends on what it is in process
  with. [CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md) is the record's nearest claim. It is not relativity of a
  verdict to a criterion.
- **Relative to a cut.** "Describing an action by starting from a human
  being is but an analytical choice ... it corresponds to an 'agential cut'
  ... Hence, agency is a matter of perspective" (p. 1357; [THEORY-1280](../theory.d/THEORY-1280.md)).
  Bencherki and Iliadis individuate domains by "an internal ordering whose
  logic distinguishes different domains for practical purposes"
  ([LIT-1922](../literature.d/LIT-1922.md), ms. p. 20; [THEORY-1277](../theory.d/THEORY-1277.md)). Bencherki 2017: which being
  is focal and which is its associated milieu "is a matter of perspective"
  ([LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md), p. 781). This is this claim's part 1, with the criterion
  left unfixed: no target, no transformations the unit must survive, no
  scale. As stated it is A9's second rejected position, a boundary "chosen
  pragmatically by investigators". This claim supplies what the readings
  lack. Once the cut's criterion is stated, the verdict can be wrong, and
  the readings' agent-free descriptions become assessable rather than only
  available. [ARG-047](../arguments.d/ARG-047.md) records that this sentence bears here and not on
  [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md).

One thing in the readings is a case of part 3. In Bencherki 2017
([THEORY-1285](../theory.d/THEORY-1285.md)), one action, the spouses' volunteer daycare, counts
toward the army's individuation and is prevented from counting toward
the women's (p. 792). Whose action it is gets different, crossing
answers depending on which individuation is the criterion. The paper
calls the army's answer usurpation, so it treats one answer as wrong.
That needs a criterion fixed in advance, which the paper's theory
disclaims ([NOTE-1482](../notes.d/NOTE-1482.md), "Open questions").

No status, title or warrant changes.

## Note of 2026-11-01: constitution in the mechanists' sense is set-relative and not arbitrary

<!-- inactive-ok-block: THEORY-1294 CLAIM-528 — Proposed; filed 2026-11-01, cited as context -->
Two readings filed today give this claim's form in another literature.

- **Relative to a phenomenon, and still capable of being false.**
  Baumgartner and Casini's No De-Coupling account ([THEORY-1294](../theory.d/THEORY-1294.md)) makes
  constitutive relevance depend on whether a part belongs to some set
  coupled with the phenomenon, which they call "extrinsic", and yet "does
  not turn it into a relativized notion": once the phenomenon is fixed the
  verdict holds across every expansion of the variables or not at all
  ([NOTE-1490](../notes.d/NOTE-1490.md), pp. 16–17). The mechanists' levels are likewise local,
  defined by direct parthood relative to a phenomenon ([NOTE-1494](../notes.d/NOTE-1494.md),
  footnote 6). So the criterion is fixed by the phenomenon, which is the
  claim's "target it serves".
- **The causation–constitution line is itself criterion-relative.** The
  same debate draws that line at simultaneity, and whether a part's
  response counts as simultaneous depends on the time resolution of the
  description ([CLAIM-528](CLAIM-528.md) part 3, from [CLAIM-480](CLAIM-480.md)). That is this claim's
  "scale" entering a verdict the debate treats as absolute.

Same structure, not shown to be the same relation: the mechanists'
constituents are parts of a behaviour, not units of a system.

No status, title or warrant changes.
