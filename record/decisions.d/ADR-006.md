# ADR-006: epistemology and logic join the philosophy group

## Context

The 2026-09-26 sweep of the papers-feed tracker for philosophy seeded dossiers
for 119 works. The dossiers were limited to the closed topic list, and two
kinds of work did not fit it:

- **Epistemology.** *Knowledge is not always more valuable than mere true
  belief* had no topic at all; its dossier said `none — wants: epistemology`.
  Others were tagged with the nearest wrong word, and their dossiers said so:
  - Pettigrew's *What is purely epistemic normativity, and why?* was tagged
    `ethics`, because the normativity is epistemic, not moral.
  - The epistemic-injustice paper and Keeley's two conspiracy-theory papers
    had approximate tags.
  - Pettigrew's *The epistemology of accurate credences*, Douglas on inductive
    risk, Reiss's pragmatist theory of evidence, and the work on evidence-based
    medicine and explanatory power sat under `philosophy-of-science`. That
    covers what science says about the world, not what it is to know or to
    have evidence.
- **Logic.** Turner's *Ultrafilters as Propositional Theories* is a logic
  tutorial, and its dossier said `none — wants: logic`. Marabini on Brandom's
  inferentialism is about meaning as inferential role.

The record's rule is that a missing word is added, not approximated.

## Decision

Add `epistemology` and `logic` to `topics`, each with a label and a blurb, and
to the `philosophy` tag group ([ADR-003](ADR-003.md), [ADR-004](ADR-004.md)).
The group stays a label (`require: any`).

## Alternatives

- **Keep `philosophy-of-science` for the evidence papers.** That is the nearest
  wrong tag. The rule this record adopted is meant to prevent exactly that.
- **Leave `logic` out, and drop or approximate the one or two works that want
  it.** A word used by one work is still a word the record needs to file that
  work honestly. Nothing about the vocabulary is finished.
- **Add `philosophy-of-mind` as well.** Three works on mental causation and
  physicalism (Pernu twice, and Poltorak) reached for it. They are honestly
  described by `metaphysics`, with `agency` or `philosophy-of-science`, and
  the group already holds `consciousness` and `cognition`. Not added, but open
  to revisit if a work cannot be placed without it.

## Consequences

The works from the sweep are filed with these words where they apply:
`epistemology` on fourteen and `logic` on two. The already-held works were not
retagged in this change.
