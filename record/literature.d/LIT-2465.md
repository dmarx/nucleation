# LIT-2465: Ccl2/Ccr2 signalling recruits a distinct fetal microchimeric population that rescues delayed maternal wound healing

Mathieu Castela, Dany Nassar, Maria Sbeih, Marie Jachiet, Zhe Wang and Sélim
Aractingi (2017). "Ccl2/Ccr2 signalling recruits a distinct fetal
microchimeric population that rescues delayed maternal wound healing"
*Nature Communications 8: 15463*. DOI-10.1038/ncomms15463.

## Key takeaways

Fetal cells persisting in the mother are recruited by a chemokine signal to
repair her tissue; the effect needs the fetal cells and CCR2.

## Standing in the record

Registered as a stand-in for [LIT-2439](LIT-2439.md). It carries the CCL2/CCR2
recruitment mechanism, not that paper's results on maternal brain injury.
