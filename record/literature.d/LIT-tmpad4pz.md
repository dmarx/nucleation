## Key takeaways

*Unread. From general knowledge, to be checked against the text.*

- **The degrees-of-freedom problem.** A limb has far more joints and muscles
  than any movement needs, so control cannot be a matter of setting each.
- **Synergies.** Coordination is "the mastering of redundant degrees of
  freedom", achieved by constraining components to act as units.
