# NOTE-tmp60nf7: Crawford and Joler (2018) — Anatomy of an AI System

## Contribution

A map and essay of AI's material and social infrastructure.

## Key results

1. Convenience "requires a vast planetary network, fueled by the extraction of non-renewable materials, labor, and data".
2. Wealth and power are "concentrated in a very thin social layer".
3. Without real choice and accountability, "mere transparency won't shift" the asymmetries.
4. Cugurullo's review (p. 158): the book presents AI as an extractive technology.

## Assumptions

Extraction of labour, resources and data is to be thought together.

## Concepts

Extraction; planetary computation; power asymmetry.

## Bearing on the record

- **CLAIM-tmpkjv7u**: supports the weight of its third condition: real choice and accountability, not information alone, are what limit the asymmetry (result 3).
- **CLAIM-tmpbypt7**: "real choice" gestures at the non-adopter's options without developing it; leaves open.
- The anthology-candidate flag stands.

## Limitations

An essay and map; the book's chapters on classification, affect and the state are not covered.
