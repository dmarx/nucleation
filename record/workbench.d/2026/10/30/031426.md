**CLAIM-004 was not really a premise.** CLAIM-143 and CLAIM-151 both rested
on CLAIM-004, but each cites it only as where its own distinction came from.
That is an attribution, and an attribution takes no edge (ADR-058). Neither
needs the criterion that CLAIM-004 still lacks. Dropping the two edges freed
CLAIM-143. CLAIM-151 stays held for a different reason, CLAIM-221's residue
for reproach, so CLAIM-137 and CLAIM-217, which really do rest on it, stay
held too. CASE-056 now gives CLAIM-004 the documented first pair the fifth
review asked for. What is still missing is the criterion itself.
