# THEORY-tmpyuoss: Hui argues that technology is not an anthropological universal but is enabled and constrained by particular cosmologies, so that there are many cosmotechnics, unifications of the cosmic and moral order through technical activity, and that a politics adequate to the Anthropocene needs technological futures to bifurcate instead of following one planetary trajectory

## The account

Hui argues that "there is no one single technology, but rather multiple cosmotechnics", cosmotechnics being "the unification of the cosmos and the moral through technical activities", and that the Anthropocene calls for "the bifurcation of technological futures by conceiving different cosmotechnics".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmp2ky8f](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2ky8f.md), results 1–5. Hui offers it as a possibility, "not sufficient to be formulated into a program".

## What it does not say

A mechanism by which an alternative technological trajectory is kept open; the account is cultural, not institutional.
