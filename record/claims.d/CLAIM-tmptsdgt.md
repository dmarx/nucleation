# CLAIM-tmptsdgt: Mutual entrainment is a sufficient mark of a combined form, and marks it as one of peers: when each side adjusts the other's timing the pair carries a collective variable neither has alone; one-way drive also makes a combined form, with a hierarchical architecture

## The claim

Clark & Chalmers ([LIT-097](../literature.d/LIT-097.md), [NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)) mark a clear case of extension by
availability. The resource is constantly consulted, directly available, and
automatically endorsed. The step from that coupling to parthood is, in
[NOTE-180](../notes.d/NOTE-180.md)'s judgement, "stipulated given functionalism, not argued". Adams &
Aizawa ([LIT-455](../literature.d/LIT-455.md)) name the step a fallacy, and their counter-case is the
nerve and the muscle: coupled, and not part of each other.

Coordination dynamics offers a different kind of criterion. In HKB
([THEORY-069](../theory.d/THEORY-069.md)), two effectors coupled strongly enough stop being described
well by their separate states. The pair has a collective variable, relative
phase, with its own attractors, its own critical fluctuations and its own
switch. Kelso extends this to person–person and person–machine coordination
([LIT-601](../literature.d/LIT-601.md)'s C4, rated weak to moderate). Levin's Self is bounded the same
way: "cut the coupling and the larger agent dissolves into its parts"
([LIT-439](../literature.d/LIT-439.md)). In participatory sense-making ([LIT-571](../literature.d/LIT-571.md)), a social interaction
gains "its own precarious operational closure" through coordinated
coupling.

On this criterion an external process belongs to the extended system when
the entrainment is mutual (each side's timing shifts with the other's) and
the pair's best description has a collective variable. That does not infer
the process's parthood in the organism, which is what the
coupling-constitution objection forbids. It individuates a new system by a
variable that only the pair has.

**Version 2 said the nerve and the muscle fail it**, because the drive runs
one way. It said the same of a metronome, which entrains a musician without
being entrained back: the fastest pacemaker "entrains the rest"
([NOTE-414](../notes.d/NOTE-414.md)). Version 3 withdraws that ([CLAIM-tmpl4ha3](CLAIM-tmpl4ha3.md)). By the same move that
makes a new system and infers no parthood, a one-way-driven pair is also a
combined form, relative to a target that needs both. What mutuality marks
is the *architecture*. Mutually entrained members are peers. A driven pair
is a hierarchy, as a lead and follow are. Mutual entrainment is sufficient
for a combined form, not necessary, and it says which kind.

## What it does not say

- **That entrainment is the only route to extension.** Otto's notebook does
  not keep time with Otto. A passive store is coupled across time by a write
  and a later read, and its writer can be its own future receiver. That is
  the storage route, [CASE-tmp3lyh3](../cases.d/CASE-tmp3lyh3.md). This claim covers the synchronic route
  only.
- **That an entrained system is a self in the subject sense**
  ([TERM-tmpg0hhr](../terms.d/TERM-tmpg0hhr.md), sense 1). The objection [CLAIM-tmpk62ey](CLAIM-tmpk62ey.md)
  presses this, and it is open. A coupled system is at most the extended
  agent's body in the sense the four unities keep apart ([ADR-024](../decisions.d/ADR-024.md)). Which
  unity entrainment supplies, if any, is not argued here.
- **That the analogy objection to intrapersonal "synchronization"
  ([CLAIM-tmpigdl0](CLAIM-tmpigdl0.md)) is answered.** Entrainment between a person and a process
  has the coupling and the order parameter that objection asks for.
  Interests over time, on Ainslie's reading, still have neither.
