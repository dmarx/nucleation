# LIT-2579: One Half a Manifesto

Jaron Lanier (2000). "One Half a Manifesto". *Edge*, with a Reality Club discussion.

## Key takeaways

- Lanier names the dogma "cybernetic totalism" and disclaims "glum rejection of digital technology".
- Real computers resist improvement "in large part due to legacy and lock-in".
