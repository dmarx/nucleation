# LIT-2249: The lattice of balanced equivalence relations of a coupled cell network

Ian Stewart (2007). "The lattice of balanced equivalence relations of a coupled cell network." *Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society* 143(1): 165–183. DOI-10.1017/S0305004107000345.

## Key takeaways

Read on 2026-11-22 ([NOTE-tmp14xdi](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp14xdi.md)). The balanced partitions of a network form a complete lattice with a computable coarsest element; meet is not intersection; nothing is said about which patterns attract.
