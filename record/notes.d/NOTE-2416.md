# NOTE-2416: HHS Office of Inspector General — Corporate Integrity Agreement with Purdue Pharma L.P.

## Contribution

The terms of the sanction that ended in 2012: a five-year agreement
requiring a compliance officer and council, a code of conduct, written
policies, training, an independent review organization and reporting.

## Key results

- **Term.** "five years from the effective date of this CIA, unless
  otherwise specified" (Section II.A).
- **The firm's own programme came first.** "Prior to the Effective Date
  (as defined below), Purdue established a voluntary compliance program
  (Corporate Compliance Program)" (Preamble).
- **Incentives.** Policies on compensation "designed to ensure that
  financial incentives do not inappropriately motivate such individuals to
  engage in the improper promotion or sales of Purdue's products" (Section
  III.B.2).
- **Abuse and addiction** appear as subjects of accurate promotion and of
  responses to information requests, not as a gate on sales.

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind.

## Concepts

Corporate integrity agreement; effective date; reporting period.

## Bearing on the record

Dates the end of the sanction coded for Purdue in [CASE-405](../cases.d/CASE-405.md), and
shows that what the agreement required was compliance machinery, not a
procedure that could stop sales to a prescriber on harm grounds, which is
why Purdue is coded "sanction only".

## Limitations

A scan with OCR errors; the effective date's day is not legible. Read for
its terms, not for how they were carried out.
