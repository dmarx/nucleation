# LIT-239: Kolmogorov's Structure Functions and Model Selection

Nikolai K. Vereshchagin, Paul M. B. Vitányi (2002), *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 50(12):3265–3290 (Dec 2004); in part in Proc. 43rd IEEE FOCS 2002; first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [arXiv:cs/0204037](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0204037)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-787](../notes.d/NOTE-787.md).*

- **Code length finds fit (Theorem IV.8).** For every string x of length
  n and every α, β_x(α) + K(x) ≅ λ_x(α) ≅ h_x(α) + α to within O(log n) in
  argument and value. Every set of complexity at most α + O(log n) that
  minimises K(S) + log |S| has deficiency within O(log n) of the best
  (Remark IV.10). The proof turns on δ(x | A) = Λ(A) − K(A | x*) − K(x) +
  O(1) and a counting lemma (Lemma C.4).
- **Not conversely.** A best-fitting set need not have a short two-part
  code, except at the "critical" complexities where λ_x is still falling
  (Remark IV.10, Theorem IV.11).
- **All shapes occur (Theorem IV.4, Corollary IV.9).** Any non-increasing
  λ with λ(0) ≤ n and λ(k) = k is λ_x for some x, to logarithmic
  precision. So non-stochastic strings exist at every length and
  complexity. For α₀ + β₀ < n − O(log n), some string is not
  (α₀, β₀)-stochastic, which strengthens Shen's result.
- **The worst-case data code matters.** The guarantee is for K(S) +
  log |S|. Minimising K(A) + K(x | A), "ultimate compression", can pick a
  model of deficiency n/2 when n/4 is achievable (§V-B(iii)).
- **Computability (§VII, App. D).**
  - h_x and λ_x are upper semicomputable but not approximable within
    |x|/(10 log |x|), even given K(x).
  - β_x is neither lower nor upper semicomputable within large margins.
  - No algorithm finds the minimal sufficient statistic from x and K(x).
  - Given that statistic's complexity, λ_x is computable (Muchnik,
    Theorem D.5).
- **Scope.** Finite-set models, with computable distributions and total
  recursive functions equivalent to within O(log n) (Appendix B). The
  authors say parametric statistical classes are "partially poorer and
  partially richer" and that the results "cannot be directly transplanted"
  to them.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 at the owner's request, from a list they grouped under
the heading *Relating K-complexity to minimum sufficient statistics*. Read
on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-787](../notes.d/NOTE-787.md). It is the record's technical core for that
heading. Its main result is filed as [THEORY-277](../theory.d/THEORY-277.md): within a
complexity-bounded class, the shortest two-part code finds the
best-fitting model for the individual datum, to O(log n), while the fit
itself is uncomputable.

Grünwald & Vitányi ([LIT-224](LIT-224.md)) restate it and relate its expectation to
Shannon's distortion-rate function. Vitányi & Li ([LIT-225](LIT-225.md)) reach ideal
two-part MDL from Bayes's rule, and this paper's §V-B(iii) shows why the
data part should be log |S| rather than K(x | S). The reading bears on
[CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md) by giving a sufficiency with no target variable, which is still
not a unique boundary, and on [QUESTION-040](../questions.d/QUESTION-040.md), as a description-length Occam
term ([NOTE-787](../notes.d/NOTE-787.md), Bearing on the record). Its O(log n) precision is the
same order as the parametric penalties of [THEORY-087](../theory.d/THEORY-087.md), so it cannot speak
to them.
