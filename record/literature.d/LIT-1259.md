# LIT-1259: Non-Markovian closure models for large eddy simulations using the Mori-Zwanzig formalism

Parish, E. J. & Duraisamy, K. (2017). Non-Markovian closure models for large eddy simulations using the Mori-Zwanzig formalism. *Physical Review Fluids* 2(1): 014604. doi:10.1103/PhysRevFluids.2.014604. Preprint: arXiv:1611.03311 (10 November 2016).

Read in full on 2026-10-20 ([NOTE-973](../notes.d/NOTE-973.md)). The reading's findings for
the record are [THEORY-760](../theory.d/THEORY-760.md), on what the closure is (a finite Markovian
augmentation with uncontrolled error), and [THEORY-747](../theory.d/THEORY-747.md), on how well
it did against DNS.

## Why this is here

A3 cited it as the turbulence literature's own use of Mori–Zwanzig for unresolved scales. Bears on [QUESTION-222](../questions.d/QUESTION-222.md).
