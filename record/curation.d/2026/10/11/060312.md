The owner proposed that agents are a factorization of a system relative to a
level of description, individuated by their will-organization
([CLAIM-tmptd6s2](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptd6s2.md)). The assistant suggested two formal definitions of agency
as the test's two halves, and the owner asked for everything to be filed.

## What entered

Both were read in full from arXiv:
- **Orseau, McGill & Legg (2018), "Agents and Devices: A Relative Definition
  of Agency"** ([LIT-tmpq7txx](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpq7txx.md), [NOTE-tmpijpu3](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpijpu3.md), arXiv:1805.12387). Read by the
  assistant. Agency is a Bayesian agent-versus-device comparison, relative
  to the observer.
- **Kenton et al. (2022), "Discovering Agents"** ([LIT-tmpbu984](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbu984.md), [NOTE-tmpoybi6](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpoybi6.md),
  arXiv:2208.08345). Read by a reader working for the assistant, which drafted
  a digest. Agency is counterfactual adaptation in mechanised causal models.
  Its finding that the thermostat with its creation process is an agent is
  the outside support for the jurisdiction thesis.

Quotations were checked against the extracted texts by script. Ten of ten
matched for Orseau et al., and thirteen of fourteen for Kenton et al.; the
one that did not match is not used.

## Boundary

Both carry the `anthology-candidate` flag last ([ADR-005](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-005.md)). The anthology's
`agents-and-environments` topic ("systems that perceive, act and are changed
by the consequences") could hold them for their RL and AI-safety framing.
They are filed here for this record's own question, which is what makes a
factor of a person, a couple or a firm an agent. Neither is held in the
anthology: a grep of its record for both titles and arXiv identifiers found
nothing. If the anthology files them, [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md)'s dual holding applies.
