# LIT-971: Is Model Collapse Inevitable? Breaking the Curse of Recursion by Accumulating Real and Synthetic Data

Matthias Gerstgrasser, Rylan Schaeffer, Apratim Dey, Rafael Rafailov, Henry Sleight, John Hughes, Tomasz Korbak, Rajashree Agrawal, Dhruv Pai, Andrey Gromov, Daniel A. Roberts, Diyi Yang, David L. Donoho and Sanmi Koyejo (2024), *arXiv:2404.01413*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-815](../notes.d/NOTE-815.md).*

- **The closed form.** Real covariates X reused every round, labels
  regenerated from the previous fit with noise σ², ridgeless OLS,
  isotropic features, T ≥ d + 2. Accumulating all rounds' data gives
  ŵ_n = w* + (XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀ·Σ E_i/i and test error
  σ²d/(T−d−1)·Σ_{i≤n} 1/i² < σ²d/(T−d−1)·π²/6 (Theorems 1–2).
- **Against replacement.** Replacing each round's data gives
  σ²d/(T−d−1)·n (Dohmatob et al.); a newest-only set as large as the
  accumulated one gives about σ²d/(T−d−1)·log n. Keeping the earlier
  data, not the amount of data, is what bounds the error.
- **Bounded, not stopped.** The sum rises every round; no model beats the
  first. Each is the first estimate plus independent noise.
- **Neural models, briefly.** Language models on TinyStories: round-4
  loss −0.06 to −0.28 nats under accumulation, +0.53 to +0.66 under
  replacement. GeoDiff holds under accumulation. A VAE on CelebA still
  degrades under accumulation, more slowly. Short, apparently single
  runs.

## Standing in the record

Filed 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-016](../questions.d/QUESTION-016.md), and read
in full on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-815](../notes.d/NOTE-815.md). The seed body called it "a case
of retained side information stopping the loss of sufficiency". The
reading supports a narrower claim, filed as [THEORY-289](../theory.d/THEORY-289.md): an exact
reconstruction chain in which degradation for one squared-error task is
bounded because the source sample stays in the pool. The error still
grows every round, every model is a garbling of the real sample, and
side information at an interpreter, the case [QUESTION-016](../questions.d/QUESTION-016.md) holds open, is
not what is retained. It is also the record's first closed-form instance
of the bounded regime of [CLAIM-267](../claims.d/CLAIM-267.md)'s recursion, and a contrast to
[THEORY-155](../theory.d/THEORY-155.md)'s chain, which replaces its data and forgets its start. It
keeps the `anthology-candidate` flag: its practical message, accumulate
rather than replace, is advice for training, which an anthology topic
could hold.
