The owner asked for the record to be updated for the exchange of
2026-10-19, "Assessing Coarse To Fine Theory" (shared at
https://chatgpt.com/share/6ad69b23-7a98-83e9-aefa-2c80ffa10982; workbench
entry of 2026-10-19). Its replies recommended about twenty works. Each one
filed here was looked up first, against Crossref, OpenAlex and the arXiv
API.

**Corrections the lookup made to the exchange.**

- Shalizi and Crutchfield's information-bottleneck paper was dated 2002, the
  journal year. It was on arXiv in June 2000.
- Eyink and Aluie's title is "Localness of energy cascade in hydrodynamic
  turbulence. I. Smooth coarse graining", not "..., I".
- Parish and Duraisamy was cited by DOI only. Its title is "Non-Markovian
  closure models for large eddy simulations using the Mori-Zwanzig
  formalism".
- "Aggregation of Variables and System Decomposition" was cited without
  authors. They are Shpak, Stadler, Wagner and Hermisson. Crossref lists
  only the first, and the full list comes from the Leipzig repository and
  the erratum.
- Crutchfield and Shalizi's thermodynamic-depth paper was posted to arXiv
  under a different title.
- Nothing else was wrong: titles, journals and years match.

**Identifications that are the record's, not the exchange's.**

- A2 named "successive refinement in rate–distortion theory" and no paper.
  The record took Equitz and Cover (1991) as its classical source.
- A3 named "the classic Constantin–E–Titi work". The record took their
  1994 note on Onsager's conjecture.
