The owner's second review of the pull request judged it "substantively
close to merge-ready". It found the more interesting opportunity in the
world-model disagreement: "The current world-model debate is attempting to
adjudicate whether something exists before establishing which kind of
thing the term names."

**What was filed.**
- **[QUESTION-362](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-362.md), a question.** It separates five notions of world model
  (explanatory, behaviourally encoded, world-modeling competence, realized
  mechanism, self-maintaining modeling organization), with what would
  establish each. It also carries the owner's four counterexamples: a
  reactive controller, a full lookup policy, an unused simulator, and a
  distributed predictive controller. It extends [NOTE-136](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-136.md)'s three senses
  from Safron et al.
- **[CLAIM-514](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-514.md), the owner's preferred formulation, as a Proposed claim.**
  World-modeling is a family of resource-bounded, query-relative
  capabilities, defined by a single implementable procedure meeting a
  loss bound within budget and deadline. A representational world model
  is one realization of them. It rests on both [CLAIM-519](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-519.md) and its
  counter [CLAIM-513](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-513.md), reading each as about its own notion:
  recoverability from a policy is not the agent's own access.
- **[CLAIM-515](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-515.md), the general principle, as a Proposed claim.** Whether an
  organization has a cognitive property is four questions: what is
  recoverable from its behaviour, what it can exercise, what realizes it,
  and whether it can maintain or transform that. Following the owner's
  word at the previous round, that a synthesis belongs in a claim and not
  in prose, both are filed as claims, with the record's instances listed.
- **[CASE-187](../../record/cases.d/CASE-187.md), the owner's proposed study.** Three architectures matched on goal
  competence are compared on goals, on-demand queries, internal
  interventions, and damage, under matched resources.

**What was revised.**
- **[CLAIM-519](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-519.md).** "Not evidence" becomes "not sufficient to establish".
  The equivalence shows that competence does not establish or identify a
  model, not that it gives no evidence for one.
- **[ARG-042](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-042.md).** Its premises are now sorted into executed studies
  ([CASE-161](../../record/cases.d/CASE-161.md), [CASE-162](../../record/cases.d/CASE-162.md)), a construction ([CASE-190](../../record/cases.d/CASE-190.md)) and a proposed
  experiment not run ([CASE-186](../../record/cases.d/CASE-186.md)). It had said the record "has built
  and run" all of them.

**Not done.** The owner advised against replacing the world-model thesis
and its counter with one broad definition: "Their disagreement is
productive, provided it remains clear which notion each concerns." Both
stand; [CLAIM-514](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-514.md) places them.
