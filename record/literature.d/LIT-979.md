# LIT-979: Dynamical independence: Discovering emergent macroscopic processes in complex dynamical systems

Lionel Barnett and Anil K. Seth (2023), *Physical Review E* 108, 014304.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-791](../notes.d/NOTE-791.md).*

- **The criterion.** Y = f(X) is dynamically independent of X given E iff
  I(Y_t : X_t⁻ | Y_t⁻, E_t⁻) ≡ 0. The micro history adds nothing to Y's
  self-prediction. The same transfer entropy measures the departure from
  independence.
- **Properties.**
  - Invariant under reparametrization.
  - Transitive across nested scales (proved in App. A).
  - Equivalent, in suitable coordinates, to a sub-graph of the causal
    graph with no incoming transfer. Such a variable may drive the rest of
    the system, but must not be driven by it.
- **Linear systems.** F(X → Y) = log|LVLᵀ| from a reduced Riccati
  equation, or as a spectral integral. Exact independence holds iff
  LCA^kKMᵀ ≡ 0, or LA_kMᵀ ≡ 0 for a VAR(p). Generically it exists at every
  scale for VAR(1) and at none for VAR(p > 1).
- **Discovery.** Minimize over the Grassmannian at each scale. The cost
  is multimodal, and every local optimum is kept as part of the portrait.
  A 9-variable VAR(7) with planted modules gave its exact optima at
  scales 2 and 6, nested.
- **Inputs, not outputs.** The system/environment boundary is "given",
  the scale is chosen, and the candidates in the linear theory are
  subspaces mixing units, not partitions of units.
- **Not autonomy.** An independent variable need not predict itself. A
  white-noise coordinate can qualify.
- **Flows.** For deterministic flows, the independent coarse-grainings
  are built from the flow's invariants and one time-like function.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 for [QUESTION-065](../questions.d/QUESTION-065.md) and read on 2026-10-14 in
[NOTE-791](../notes.d/NOTE-791.md). Its finding is filed as [THEORY-257](../theory.d/THEORY-257.md), which answers
[QUESTION-065](../questions.d/QUESTION-065.md) in part on both its questions.

- **On the first,** it supplies a system-fixed target, the coarse
  variable's own future, so no query is chosen. That is not the
  locality-based target the question names. Boundary, scale and the
  class of maps remain the analyst's.
- **On the second,** the winner at a scale is not unique in general.

The registration's phrase "a coarse-graining fixed by the system rather
than by an analyst's target" is right about the target and too strong
about the coarse-graining.

It also bears on [CLAIM-157](../claims.d/CLAIM-157.md) and [CLAIM-167](../claims.d/CLAIM-167.md). Dynamical independence needs no
incoming coupling but allows outgoing coupling, so a driver is emergent at
its own scale, and a driven module never is. That is a different block
from near-decomposability's.
