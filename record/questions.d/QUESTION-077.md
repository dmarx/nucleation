# QUESTION-077: Is agreement among an agent's interests produced by coupling, as when each sees and follows the current majority, a different kind of integration from agreement because their valuations coincide?

## The question

In the democracy-mode variant of Twitch Plays Pokémon with the tally
visible ([CASE-074](../cases.d/CASE-074.md)), the voters can come to agree quickly. That is
not because they want the same thing. Each one sees where the majority
is going and follows it. The agreement is in what the crowd does, and it
is produced by the coupling. The voters' valuations need not move at all.

The record has two accounts of how an agent's interests come to act
together without a fight:

- **They agree in value.** In flow the immediate reward and the
  longer-run value coincide, so no interest has to be overridden
  ([CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md), [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md)).
- **One is kept from acting.** Mindfulness suspends the step from an
  interest's momentary dominance to its control of action, and leaves
  its valuation alone ([CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md)).

Agreement by coupling fits neither. The parts do not agree in value, and
none of them is suspended. Each one changes what it does because of what
the others are doing.

## Why the record needs it

- If coupled agreement is a distinct route, [CLAIM-162](../claims.d/CLAIM-162.md)'s "no interest has
  to be overridden" no longer marks flow off from conformity. Both are
  effortless in that sense, and the account of flow needs a further mark.
- It bears on [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md). Coupled agreement is the one route that has the
  parts [CLAIM-163](../claims.d/CLAIM-163.md) asks for: a coupling, a collective variable and
  transitions. If it turns out to be what intrapersonal "synchronization"
  actually is, the word may earn its mechanism, though not in the sense
  [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md) gives it.
- It bears on [CLAIM-198](../claims.d/CLAIM-198.md). There, the coupling through self-prediction is
  where interests contend. In the case, a coupling makes them converge.

## What would answer it

- **Within one person:** a case where interests come to agree in action
  because each tracks the agent's current overall leaning, with their
  valuations measured as unchanged. Momentum in a choice, a "decided"
  feeling that recruits the reluctant interest, or the escalation of
  commitment are candidates, none of them in the record.
- **Against the distinction:** an argument that coupled agreement
  reduces to one of the two existing routes. For example, the coupling
  might change the valuations after all, which would make it the flow
  route, or it might work by suspending the minority, which would make
  it [CLAIM-156](../claims.d/CLAIM-156.md)'s route.
