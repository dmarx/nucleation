# THEORY-tmpuucqv: Bardsley, Mehta, Starmer and Sugden find in one-shot coordination experiments that focal points are sometimes reached by team reasoning, choosing the rule that is best for the pair, and sometimes by predicting others' pre-reflective picks as cognitive hierarchy theory holds, depending on the decision context

## The account

Coordinators who team-reason choose the rule that is best for the pair,
expecting the partner to do likewise; coordinators who use cognitive
hierarchy reasoning guess what a naive picker would choose. Both modes
occur, in different contexts.

## Evidence

Two experiments with adult students (N = 161 and N = 134).
