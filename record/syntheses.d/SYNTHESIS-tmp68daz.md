# SYNTHESIS-tmp68daz: Whether demands or local descriptions can be met or agree together is decided only against a declared structure of comparison, and where it fails the failure is certified by a dual witness, a cycle or a set of multipliers, that re-centring and a change of chart leave in place, while a budgeted magnitude keeps its value only with the metric carried along; whether a compatible state can be selected depends on what the agent can read; quiet is not met, so raising a threshold relieves nothing and a third complex decides when frustration forces an unmet demand that is acted on; an input below a fold's threshold, or conditions restored after a capacity was lost, can leave the visible regime as it was; and agreement among routes or descriptions is coherence, not fidelity

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line ("Formal apparatus": results
that hold whatever any substantive line concludes, held as a second line by
the general results other lines borrow, ADR-040): the part on the formal side
of frustration, its complexes, residuals, offsets and cycles. The line's
account is built over it and its sibling parts. Every claim among the
members is a thesis with a demonstrated warrant. CLAIM-265, CLAIM-305,
CLAIM-495, CLAIM-628 and TERM-097 are first in this line; the rest are
first in distributed-agency,
will-organization, learned-structure, pragmatic-transport or readiness, and
most are already integrated by those lines' accounts: CLAIM-365, CLAIM-538,
CLAIM-549, CLAIM-631 and CLAIM-634 by SYNTHESIS-051 (distributed-agency,
frustration); CLAIM-463, CLAIM-535 and CLAIM-537 by SYNTHESIS-056
(distributed-agency); CLAIM-495 by SYNTHESIS-048 (distributed-agency);
CLAIM-537, CLAIM-549, CLAIM-550, CLAIM-628 and CLAIM-631 by SYNTHESIS-044

## The account

- **The cycle certificate.** CLAIM-538 (the owner's question at U27, worked
  out by A; the criterion classical, "the result is granted, not the
  record's discovery"; Active): on a graph of contexts with preferred real
  offsets and tolerances, an assignment within every tolerance exists
  "exactly when no cycle's holonomy exceeds the sum of its tolerances", the
  least uniform enlargement is the largest simple-cycle ratio, "so the
  obstruction is certified by a cycle"; the feasible allocations depend on
  the offsets only through their class in H¹, so "Local re-centring
  redistributes an obstruction and never removes it"; and omission can fake
  compatibility: "Feasibility on the whole graph implies it on any evaluated
  subgraph, not conversely, and on a forest it always holds." CLAIM-634 (the
  assistant's, at the synthesis review of 2026-11-11; Proposed) applies the
  criterion to every set of overlaps: "The minimal non-faces of 𝒦 are
  exactly the violating simple cycles", so without parallel overlaps every
  pair of demands is compatible, a minimal conflict can have any length from
  three to the number of contexts, "Helly's bound is attained", and "in this
  class a missing face of the compatibility complex is a failed global
  assignment". CLAIM-538 itself disclaims "That a minimal non-face of
  CLAIM-347's complex is a cycle obstruction"; CLAIM-634 holds the link for
  CLAIM-538's class only.
- **The multiplier certificate and its invariance.** CLAIM-365 (A34 and A35
  of the exchange of 2026-10-17, checked; Active): on a norm-ball update set
  the frustration residual Φ_r equals a maximum over the simplex, so
  infeasibility "has a certificate", and any admissible actual update exceeds
  Φ_r by a nonnegative governance regret. In its worked case "No budget
  helps: this is structural frustration (CLAIM-357)." The multipliers "certify
  difficulty; the weights governance actually assigns are another matter",
  and the residual's size depends on units. CLAIM-366 (A35, proved by the
  record; Active): whether several objectives share a strict first-order
  descent direction, 0 ∉ conv{g_i}, is invariant under smooth invertible
  reparameterization, while the sign of a Euclidean inner product is not:
  "The objectives are the same; the conflict label flips." "A budgeted test
  needs the transported metric." CLAIM-629 (the assistant's, at the second
  synthesis review of 2026-11-07; Proposed) carries the unbudgeted verdict
  from two charts to two points of one chart: it is constant on the orbits
  of any function-preserving symmetry that is a diffeomorphism, while a
  residual in a fixed Euclidean norm changes along rescalings, "So
  quotienting before diagnosing matters only for the budgeted test."
- **What a reparametrization cannot do.** CLAIM-305 (the record's
  correction of the owner's monograph; Active): resolving a singularity by
  blow-up "reparametrizes the states and adds no observation", so "States
  that no data could tell apart before are still not told apart, and there
  are now more of them"; what it buys is a normal form for the divergence,
  and so the learning coefficient. It is the case CLAIM-366 and CLAIM-629
  leave out, a symmetry that is not a diffeomorphism.
- **Compatibility graded by direction.** CLAIM-495 (from A2 and A3 of
  "Paper assessment"; the proof and example the record's; Active): for an
  O(d) connection on a connected graph the global sections are the vectors
  fixed by every holonomy, the kernel of the connection Laplacian; for
  d = 1 that is all or nothing, but for d > 1 "compatibility among local
  frames can be partial and graded by direction", and "non-commutativity is
  not holonomy".
- **Existence is not selection.** CLAIM-537 (A30, the second and third
  conditions the reader's; Active) disturbs CLAIM-538's offsets: a
  compatible configuration for every disturbance (I) is weaker than one
  configuration for all (II), and an agent choosing from what it observes
  needs II within each class it cannot tell apart (III); "Perfect legibility
  gives I; none gives II." In its triangle "An agent that cannot tell the two
  worlds apart cannot select a compatible state, though one exists in
  each." CLAIM-628 (the assistant's, at the synthesis review of 2026-11-07;
  Proposed) states III for a given class: each overlap's half-range within
  tolerance, and the cycle condition at the class's midpoint offsets with
  tolerances reduced by it; since existence depends only on the H¹ class
  and selection on the edgewise spread, "an agent that reads the cohomology
  class exactly need not be able to select one". CLAIM-535 (A33 of "Assess
  SDT Coverage", the policy-relativity the reader's; Active) states the
  criterion in general: a policy acceptable everywhere exists exactly when
  the circumstances an observation cannot tell apart share an acceptable
  action; "Possibility is not enough", a finer observation never removes a
  policy, and "which distinctions must be made legible depends on which
  acceptable action the organization commits to". CLAIM-559 (A's proposal
  from the exchange on emergency readiness; Active) is the sequential case:
  provisional activation and later cancellation "can both be optimal under
  one unchanged objective", so "a reversal is not by itself evidence of an
  error in either decision".
- **Quiet is not met.** CLAIM-549 (A's, sharpened by the reader; Active):
  the quiescent complex is not the requirement complex, "either can hold a
  face the other lacks", raising thresholds can only enlarge the quiescent
  complex, "so it is not relief of frustration": "A larger quiescent
  complex with the same requirement complex is a quieter organization, not a
  less frustrated one." CLAIM-550 (the owner's chain at U19, A's derivation
  generalized by the reader; Active) derives a perspective's tolerance from
  its activation threshold, so one fixed threshold gives different tolerances
  across contexts, and "raising a threshold enlarges tolerance without
  relieving any demand". CLAIM-631 (the assistant's, returned from
  SYNTHESIS-008 at ADR-084; Proposed): structural frustration forces an
  unmet member that acts toward its demand, the precondition of operative
  thwarting, exactly when no attainable arrangement leaves each member met
  or quiet; "lying outside both the requirement complex and the quiescent
  complex does not suffice"; in a minimal such conflict each member is the
  unmet, acting one under some arrangement and none under all; and the band
  where a demand is unmet and quiet "can hide a structural frustration
  completely".
- **Thresholds and folds.** CLAIM-459 (A8 of the exchange of 2026-10-17,
  stated in CASE-112; Active): an input to a bistable balance of authority
  can be causally efficacious below the tilt threshold without changing
  which regime operates, "so a higher-order volition that changes nothing
  visible need not be idle". CLAIM-463 (A4's candidate from "About the
  Chagossians", filed at the owner's request, demonstrated by CASE-111;
  Active): where maintenance reinforces itself, restoring the conditions
  under which a capacity was lost need not restore it; "Return is not
  reversal", and "Restoration can cost more than preservation."
- **Coherence.** CLAIM-265 (the assistant's restatement, split from
  CLAIM-143 at the owner's request; Active): "Local descriptions that
  disagree are not thereby incompatible"; the verdict is relative to a
  specified class of admissible correspondences, and "a correspondence
  admitted only because it removes the disagreement decides nothing".
  CLAIM-496 (from A4 and A5 of "Paper assessment", the constructions the
  record's; Active): "Agreement between routes of translation is a measure
  of coherence, not of fidelity, and the two are independent"; "So neither
  measure bounds the other." TERM-097 (filed on the owner's question,
  adopted by the owner on 2026-10-29; Active) defines coherence as agreement
  under a stated correspondence structure, on overlaps, around cycles or
  between routes, which "can be exact or graded", and "is always relative to
  the correspondence structure, so a claim of coherence should name the
  maps".

## Not stated on the record

Integrating sentences across the members, tested by ADR-075's step 5 at
this review and not filed:

- **The two certificates are one.** That CLAIM-538's least uniform
  enlargement, less one, is CLAIM-365's residual for the normalized overlap
  demands with an unbounded update set, so the cycle certificate is the
  multiplier certificate with the multipliers on a cycle. Derivation route:
  it does not follow from the members as stated, since CLAIM-365 requires
  ε_i > 0 and a compact norm ball, and here half the ε_i are negative and
  the update set is unbounded; what holds is that both are linear
  programming duality, which CLAIM-538 ("the ratio is LP duality") and
  CLAIM-365 each state for themselves. Not filed.
- **Conflicts that block selection.** That, for a class of disturbances an
  agent cannot tell apart, the minimal sets of overlaps that block selection
  are the single overlaps whose spread exceeds their tolerance and the simple
  cycles that violate at the midpoint offsets with tolerances reduced by the
  spread. Derivation route: valid, but it is CLAIM-634 applied to
  CLAIM-628's reduced problem, which CLAIM-628 already writes as an instance
  of CLAIM-538's, and the singletons are CLAIM-537's "each overlap must
  absorb its own disturbance". A substitution, not filed.
- **One invariance principle.** That every verdict in the part is invariant
  under the transformations its comparison structure admits and every
  magnitude needs an added metric. CLAIM-538's ρ_δ is a magnitude invariant
  under re-centring, so the sentence is false as worded, and the narrower
  form turns on "admits", a free parameter that absorbs every case.
- **The tolerance results are an instance of the order of tests.** That
  CLAIM-538, CLAIM-537, CLAIM-628 and CLAIM-634 fix the class of
  correspondences before the verdict, as CLAIM-265 requires. A label: no
  case, derivation or prediction separates it from the components, and
  CLAIM-538's use of TERM-093 already names its correspondence structure.
- **Existence is not what an agent can do or tell.** A theme across
  CLAIM-537, CLAIM-535, CLAIM-549, CLAIM-463, CLAIM-496 and CLAIM-305. As
  "can", a conjunction of held instances; nothing is added.

Stated already, so nothing is filed: that raising thresholds hides
frustration without relieving it (CLAIM-631's band and "What it does not
say", with CLAIM-549 and CLAIM-361); that disagreement is not
incompatibility and agreement is not fidelity (TERM-097, with CLAIM-265 and
CLAIM-496); that the fold of CLAIM-459 and the hysteresis of CLAIM-463 are
one shape (CLAIM-459's body); and that CLAIM-535's criterion is CLAIM-537's
condition III in general (CLAIM-537).
