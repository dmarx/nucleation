# LIT-755: A Process-Oriented Approach to Design Rationale

E. Jeffrey Conklin and K. C. Burgess Yakemovic (1991), *Human–Computer Interaction* 6(3–4):357–391 — DOI-10.1207/s15327051hci0603&4_6

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the abstract only: the approach supports the
design process so that a trace of the rationale is captured with little
disruption of it. It describes IBIS and two implementations, the graphical
gIBIS of [LIT-762](LIT-762.md) and a simple indented-text notation. In an industrial
field trial the indented-text form was used to capture more than 2,300
requirements and design decisions. The paper draws implications for tools
that, like gIBIS, try to capture rationale nonintrusively.
