Fenichel, N. (1979). Geometric singular perturbation theory for ordinary differential equations. *Journal of Differential Equations*, 31(1), 53–98. doi:10.1016/0022-0396(79)90152-9.

Not read: a lawful open copy exists but could not be fetched on 2026-10-27 (see the status note). The summary is drawn from what the work is standardly cited for and from the exchange's description of it. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A12 and A13 gave Tikhonov and Fenichel as "the precise conditions for an
effective lower-dimensional dynamics to exist", the mathematical core of
[CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md): under them the relation x ≈ h(y) becomes a constraint on
the fast variables. The record holds Bousquet et al.'s singular-
perturbation account of near-decomposability ([LIT-1292](LIT-1292.md)), which neither
reply cited. Tikhonov (1952) is named, not registered.
