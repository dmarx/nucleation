# LIT-3111: Where New Words Are Born: Distributional Semantic Analysis of Neologisms and Their Semantic Neighborhoods

Maria Ryskina, Ella Rabinovich, Taylor Berg-Kirkpatrick, David R. Mortensen
and Yulia Tsvetkov (2020) — arXiv:2001.07740

## Key takeaways

- Unread. Reported: new words appear where the semantic space is sparse
  (supply-driven) and where neighbours' frequency grows (demand-driven).

## Standing in the record

Registered by [LEAD-056](../leads.d/LEAD-056.md) ([THEORY-159](../theory.d/THEORY-159.md)'s promote_when). It concerns where an
addition happens, not what it does to the neighbours afterwards, so it is
background to the lead's design, with a matched-control method the design
could borrow. Not read in this batch.
