# SYNTHESIS-tmp8ru2q: Which of an agent's available regimes becomes effective is decided by maintained relations of efficacy that are not further preferences and are indexed to an agent rather than to any regulator; precedence, jurisdiction and suspension among them vary separately; and whether describing them as one organization explains anything that the fields studying each realizer do not is open

*A part account of the will-organization line (ADR-083, ADR-084). The line's account is built over it and four sibling parts. A sixth, on normative judgment about organizations, became the line organizational-normativity at the split of will-organization (ADR-tmpczmfi). "The will paper" is the owner's essay, The Organization of Will (the `works` vocabulary), cited in components by section.*

## The account

**The thesis and what it rests on.** The paper asks its question in its own
words (QUESTION-015, Answered): given an independently warranted agent with
several available regimes, what maintained relations govern which becomes
effective. CLAIM-027 (thesis, interpretive, Active since the status review
of 2026-10-28) answers it: accessibility, precedence, persistence,
jurisdiction, suspension and transformation, "not reducible to the
contents of the competing regimes, whatever mechanisms realize them". It
rests on CLAIM-047, that possessing an action-generating regime (TERM-019)
is not its being effective, which grounds on Frankfurt's unwilling addict
(CASE-181), and on CLAIM-080, the supposition that scopes the paper: the
agent is given, and the account asks how its efficacy is organized.
TERM-023 states the organization those relations make up. QUESTION-062 and
QUESTION-066 refine QUESTION-015 with readings no other question placed.

Three lines of support conclude CLAIM-027, all Proposed: the reluctant
writer's precommitment device (ARG-005, from CASE-013), which "does not
rest on the supposition that a firm is an agent"; Northstar-A against
Northstar-B (ARG-009, from CASE-003, a variant of CASE-023), conditional on
CLAIM-080; and mind-wandering read through Metzinger (ARG-051), which would
give suspension in one mind and is held back by two unanswered questions.
QUESTION-367, filed from the same readings of Metzinger, asks whether an
agent's representation of its own capacity to control a process is a
condition of that capacity's efficacy; QUESTION-407 is a share-of-time
clause moved off one of them (THEORY-1297), and no component cites it.

**Agent-indexing answers permissiveness.** The account first stated will as a
higher-order attractor (CLAIM-012, TERM-003, both Superseded by CLAIM-027
and TERM-023). CLAIM-010, the permissiveness objection (Rejected), objects
to CLAIM-012 and CLAIM-027; its summary says it is "Answered by
agent-indexing (CLAIM-080, CLAIM-116)". CLAIM-116 (disclaimed, Active)
objects to it: regulation, even higher-order, does not establish a will,
with the thermostat and adaptive controller (CASE-034) as illustration. The
owner's reply that every such regulator may count (CLAIM-062) is
Superseded by CLAIM-080, and CASE-034 is filed as its counterexample.

**Not one more preference.** CLAIM-089 (Rejected), that Northstar "simply
prefers speed", objects to CLAIM-027. CLAIM-122 (Active) objects to it:
changing the conditions of efficacy changes behaviour with the competing
preferences fixed, grounded on CASE-013 and CASE-003. CLAIM-482 (counter,
Active) answers the aggregate version, resting on CLAIM-434 of
distributed-agency: a revealed preference exists exactly when the record of
winning moves never cycles, and where it exists it is fitted to one regime
of authority. CLAIM-089's history (version 2) records it as answered by
CLAIM-482. ARG-046, a Total Quality instance concluding CLAIM-122, is
Rejected because the proposals were not held fixed. CASE-112 (A's
bistable balance of authority) illustrates CLAIM-122, and CLAIM-459
(demonstrated, Active) grounds on it: a sincere, efficacious higher-order
volition (TERM-102) can move the balance without moving the regime.
CASE-125, the hidden governance bit, illustrates CLAIM-122 and CLAIM-407;
CLAIM-407 (demonstrated, Active, first line formal-apparatus)
corroborates CLAIM-122, showing that a description closed under ordinary
dynamics can lose the response to an intervention, measured by TERM-067's
governance-response profile. CLAIM-399 rests on CLAIM-407: causal
abstraction in three published senses accepts the coarse-graining that
loses it. CLAIM-539 rests on CLAIM-122 and CLAIM-022: Jennings's attention
is Frankfurt's architecture with a process for an attitude, so it bears on
manipulation (QUESTION-071).

**Precedence, jurisdiction and suspension.** TERM-017 names them. CLAIM-057
(demonstrated, Active) says they vary independently, and ARG-001 concludes
it, and CLAIM-086, from the Northstar classification variant (CASE-002).
CLAIM-086 (Active), a constraint governing conduct without governing its
own authority, has a second line, ARG-003 from Fraenkel's Dual State
(CASE-016), which CLAIM-032 undercuts to its structural respect. Three
claims carry these relations further: CLAIM-410 (distributed-agency first)
rests on CLAIM-086 and CLAIM-057 and makes the removal of a constraint a
change in a profile of governing positions; CLAIM-353 rests on CLAIM-027
and grounds on Sen's liberal paradox (THEORY-027), so that what distributed
jurisdiction gives up is jurisdiction, unanimity or the domain; CLAIM-627
(Proposed, A's) rests on CLAIM-057 and CLAIM-197 and carries jurisdiction
to the compatibility of a concept's uses. ARG-057, from CASE-002 to
CLAIM-627, is Rejected: the case "measures outcomes and not
compatibility". CASE-230 (Proposed, A's design) illustrates CLAIM-627.
QUESTION-440 (A's) asks whether dynamical slaving can realize precedence,
jurisdiction or suspension.

**Possession, efficacy and forking.** CLAIM-613 (Proposed, A's) rests on
CLAIM-047 and CLAIM-287: a fork can be a new organization through which a
disposition the parent kept ineffective gains efficacy. ARG-069 concludes
it from Hirschman's exit and voice. Both use TERM-118, disposition, which
complements TERM-019 and keeps disposition apart from an action-generating
regime, "the sense the record retired".

**Whether the description earns its keep.** CLAIM-021 (granted) concedes the
mechanisms are not new. CLAIM-024 (counter, Proposed) objects to CLAIM-487,
the explanatory-gain claim the status review of 2026-10-28 split from
CLAIM-027, which rests on CLAIM-027 and bears on QUESTION-056. CLAIM-159
(Active) objects to CLAIM-024: beside the expected value of control and
Ainslie's interests, will-organization governs how the drive profile
changes; CLAIM-024's history (version 4) concedes this only for executive
control and one account of resolve, not control theory's regulation.
QUESTION-056 asks whether the mechanisms' description implements the
account or replaces it, and QUESTION-088 (distributed-agency first) refines
it into a test of prediction. CLAIM-336 (Proposed, the owner's thesis with
the assistant's mapping) reads this line and pragmatic-transport as one
model of operators and bears on QUESTION-088; CLAIM-341 complements it,
and ARG-065 (Proposed) states what would make CLAIM-336's premises favour
one model: predictions for combined interventions that separate models
miss.

**The worked case.** Northstar (CASE-023) illustrates CLAIM-027; CASE-002,
CASE-003 and CASE-134 are its variants here, and CASE-036 is a variant in
the part on maintaining the capacity to govern. CASE-134 illustrates
CLAIM-355 and CLAIM-417 of other lines. CASE-014, Microsoft as the first
framing's target, is Deferred.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim.

- **Sen's paradox and the redescription objection.** That a will-organization
  meeting CLAIM-353's conditions has no aggregate revealed preference, so
  CLAIM-089 is unavailable for it. Held in substance: CLAIM-482 says the
  objection is "unavailable for one whose record cycles", and CLAIM-440 of
  organizational-affect says Sen's theorem and CLAIM-353 show a negotiation
  "need not produce an ordering". The remainder is a composition of the two.
- **Distribution and the paradox.** That of CLAIM-410's eliminated,
  distributed and transferred authority only distribution carries
  CLAIM-353's impossibility. Trivial once "distributed" is closed to
  pairwise decisiveness, which CLAIM-353's own edge leaves open; Sen's
  theorem already needs two decisive parties.
