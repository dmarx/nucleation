# LIT-2711: Higher Education for Citizens of Caring Democracies

Tronto, J. C. (2018). Higher Education for Citizens of Caring Democracies. *South African Journal of Higher Education* 32(6): 6–18. doi:10.20853/32-6-2710.

## Key takeaways

- **Privileged irresponsibility** (p. 8): "a major source of injustice in
  the world comes from the capability of the relatively privileged to
  refuse to accept unpleasant or burdensome responsibilities (privileged
  irresponsibility)."
- **Diffused responsibility excuses** (p. 11): if every nation is
  responsible for educational development everywhere, a former colonizer
  "bears no special responsibility for the underdevelopment that occurred
  in its own colonies."
- **Caring Democracy's first steps** (p. 14): "to realign a system of
  privileges that make it impossible to discuss how some people take on an
  unfair load of care responsibilities", and "everyone's voice must be
  heard in making these adjustments".

## Standing in the record

Registered as a read neighbour of Moral Boundaries ([LIT-2708](LIT-2708.md)) and
Caring Democracy ([LIT-2669](LIT-2669.md)). It stands in for the term and its sense,
the capability of the privileged to refuse burdensome responsibilities,
and for Tronto's own outline of Caring Democracy's programme. It does not
stand in for either book's argument, mechanism or examples, nor for the
exchange's "because other people routinely perform that work on their
behalf" or "socially organized inattention".
