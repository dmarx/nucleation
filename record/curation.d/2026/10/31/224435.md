ADR-056 proposed a `kind` field for TERM, with four kinds:
- `disambiguation`: separates senses that must not be conflated;
- `construct`: introduces a defined theoretical object;
- `criterion`: states when a concept applies, with a way to assess it;
- `position`: names an articulated position without adopting it.

It made adding the field wait on a pilot. The pilot classifies a mixed
sample: the nine terms A12 named, the six filed with the decision, and
TERM-098, filed since. It reports where the kinds force an arbitrary
choice. The field was to be added only if the kinds "sort the record
without arbitrary choices and give a view or a review decision the record
lacks". The owner asked on 2026-10-31 for the pilot to be run. Each
term's definition was read, not only its title.

| term | kind | clean? | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| TERM-045 self | disambiguation | yes | six senses, "a claim should say which" |
| TERM-071 persistence | disambiguation | yes | five senses in the title |
| TERM-074 organization | disambiguation | yes | four senses in the title |
| TERM-082 consciousness | disambiguation | yes | four senses |
| TERM-080 collective intention | disambiguation | yes | three senses |
| TERM-081 moral worth | disambiguation | yes | three senses |
| TERM-023 will-organization | construct | yes | a defined theoretical object |
| TERM-098 concept | construct | yes | defines conceptual organization; its separations are the usual "what it is not" |
| TERM-044 agent | criterion | yes | three tests at a level of description |
| TERM-069 observational equivalence | criterion | yes | when two objects count as equivalent, assessed formally |
| TERM-059 relational normative realism | position | yes | named, explicitly not held in full |
| TERM-072 perspective | disambiguation and construct | **no** | a map of three senses and a substantive definition of the first; the stress test ADR-056 itself predicted |
| TERM-086 organizational achievement | criterion, construct or disambiguation | **no** | a criterion only while its comparison requirement stands (ADR-056's own caveat), and also three modes (episode, capacity, reproduction) |
| TERM-055 operational availability | construct or criterion | **no** | it defines a probability; read as a quantity it is a construct, read as the condition for "available" a criterion |
| TERM-085 correctability | construct or criterion | **no** | the same: a defined chance, which A12 called a criterion |
| TERM-083 moral standing | criterion with no criterion | **no** | it names what a claim of standing must specify, and leaves the ground "for ethical argument", so the application contract has nothing in its criterion slot |

**Sorting.** Eleven of sixteen sort cleanly. The five that do not fall in
two places:
- **A sense map that also defines one sense** (TERM-072, TERM-086).
- **The boundary between construct and criterion** (TERM-055, TERM-085,
  TERM-083). That boundary is where the field was meant to work: the
  application contract applies to criterion terms. A field that cannot
  say which terms owe the contract does not give the review decision it
  was for.

**Benefit.** The six clean disambiguations already announce themselves:
four carry ", and which sense" in their titles, and the other two say so
in their first line. A stored field would duplicate what the titles
derive (DP-2). Of the other kinds, the sample has one position and two
constructs, too few to show a view the record lacks.

**Verdict, on ADR-056's own test.** The kinds do not sort the record
without arbitrary choices. Where they would matter, at criterion against
construct, they are least reliable. The field is not added, and ADR-056
is set Rejected.

Two parts of the decision are independent of the field and were judged
sound:
- the application contract for a TERM that states when a concept applies
  (bearer, target, conditions, criterion, discrimination, evidence,
  non-entailments);
- the distinction between a criterion and an indicator.

They stand in ADR-056's text as advice. They bind nothing unless a later
decision adopts them. TERM-083's empty criterion slot, found here, is the
kind of gap the contract would catch.
