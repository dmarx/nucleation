# LIT-059: The Strongest El Niño Ever

Zeke Hausfather (2026), *The Climate Brink (Substack newsletter of Andrew Dessler and Zeke Hausfather)* — <https://www.theclimatebrink.com/p/the-strongest-el-nino-ever>

## Key takeaways

- In the post, Hausfather reports that the July 2026 initializations of 14 seasonal models (667 ensemble members) put the multi-model median peak Niño 3.4 anomaly for 2026-27 at about 3.6 °C, against a prior record of about 2.75 °C (2015-16). That gives about a 91% chance of a record by Niño 3.4 and about 77% by relative ONI. He cautions that no model has ever been verified in this range.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Hausfather argues that, with the July model runs in, the developing 2026 El Niño is very likely to be the strongest on record, possibly by a wide margin. He compares the multi-model forecast with every event since 1877, looks at the per-model spread and at the relative index that removes tropical-mean warming, and traces how forecasts rose from March to July. He also notes that observed daily Niño 3.4 anomalies are already at "super El Niño" levels in mid-July. He expects most of the global-temperature effect in 2027, and ends by warning that the models are untested at this intensity.
