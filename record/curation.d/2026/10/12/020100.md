## How it was checked

- **The argument** is the sheaf condition applied to a cover whose
  members do not meet. It was re-derived, not taken from MOM.
- **The quotations** were checked against MoM's source chapters in a local
  clone at d987e2a: v1 "Sheaf Semantics of Context" §10.4 and §10.6, and
  "Epistemic Drift and Semantic Simulation" §11.3.3. All are verbatim.
- **Not dual-filed:** v1 §11.5 of the same chapter, "Identity as Global
  Section". It treats a person's roles as sections over family,
  professional and social contexts, and is the same move applied to the
  self. MOM has no claim on that section. [CLAIM-tmpk4fd7](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk4fd7.md) cites it by
  section and does not depend on it.
