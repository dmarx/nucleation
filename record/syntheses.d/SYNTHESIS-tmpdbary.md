# SYNTHESIS-tmpdbary: What an invariant keeps and what it forgets: equal entropy does not give equal relational structure, characters classify representations only up to isomorphism and only in characteristic zero and forget the isomorphisms, the group and the action, and a factorization is fixed only up to a gauge that is no symmetry of the data, so identifiability comes before automorphisms and characters; that a signature sufficient for decisions beyond the probes is identified where the factorization is not is proposed, and none is named

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line ("Formal apparatus",
[ADR-040](../decisions.d/ADR-040.md)), the part on what an invariant keeps and what it forgets. The
line's account is built over it and the accounts of the line's other
parts. The mechanical community map put these four entries with the part
on when a structure is worth naming; read through, they are a separate
inquiry, joined to that part by one pair ([CASE-097](../cases.d/CASE-097.md) illustrates [CLAIM-148](../claims.d/CLAIM-148.md)),
and that part has its own account, drafted with this one. All four are
first in the pragmatic-transport line, and that line's part account of
communicative kinds ([SYNTHESIS-068](SYNTHESIS-068.md)) integrates them; this account
integrates them again because their `line` includes formal-apparatus.*

## The account

- **Entropy is not structure.** [CLAIM-043](../claims.d/CLAIM-043.md) (A86 §4 and the manuscript §5,
  granted, demonstrated, Active): "a collection of local entropies need not
  characterize the global compatibility structure", and of sheaf structure
  and information, "Neither structure is reducible to the other." Its
  example: perfect correlation and perfect anticorrelation of two fair bits
  have equal entropy.
- **What characters classify.** [CLAIM-132](../claims.d/CLAIM-132.md) (standard results checked by the
  record, with the exchange's statements of them, granted, demonstrated,
  Active): characters classify finite-dimensional representations of a
  finite or compact group up to isomorphism only over a field of
  characteristic zero; they fail in characteristic p, in non-semisimple
  settings and for infinite groups. A character forgets the isomorphisms
  ("It is a decategorification."), the group (D₄ and Q₈) and the
  permutation action. Its summary: "a character is a counts-only relational
  profile, complete because the category is semisimple." "So a character
  can classify a kind. It cannot individuate a token or a position."
- **The gauge of a factorization.** [CLAIM-148](../claims.d/CLAIM-148.md) (the assistant's at A207,
  A214 and A218, with the record's additions, granted, demonstrated,
  Active): R ≈ PQᵀ is unchanged under P ↦ PA, Q ↦ QA⁻ᵀ for every
  invertible A; weight decay narrows this to O(k), and for nonnegative
  factorization the freedom "depends on the data, is not a group in
  general". "Nonnegativity reduces the freedom. It does not simplify it."
  What is invariant is the predicted matrix and what is computed from it,
  so similarity from response profiles is gauge-invariant and similarity
  from latent vectors is not. "A gauge change alters the description and
  leaves the relata where they are. An automorphism moves relata among
  structurally equivalent positions." Without identifiability "no
  representation on the factor space is determined": "The order of A214's
  arrows is a dependency, not only a sequence." Its dated notes find the
  same gauge in [CASE-165](../cases.d/CASE-165.md)'s latent-feature model and in three works read
  for that exchange (2026-10-26), and find the gauge freedom of
  Sengupta et al. to be reparameterization (2026-10-29).
- **What can be identified without the factorization.** [CLAIM-209](../claims.d/CLAIM-209.md) (A240's
  T2, the assistant's, thesis, interpretive, Proposed) answers
  [QUESTION-027](../questions.d/QUESTION-027.md): "a signature sufficient for a stated family of
  communicative decisions outside the observed probes can be identified
  when the full latent factorization is not". Its own reading: "The first
  sentence restates held claims", "The unrestricted second sentence is
  already true", and "So the thesis is the restricted clause." Its note of
  2026-10-30: "No signature, family of decisions or class of
  transformations is stated."
