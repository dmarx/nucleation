# QUESTION-304: Does acting as one require that an agent's interests synchronize?

## Why it was a question

[QUESTION-078](QUESTION-078.md) asked it together with its alternative, whether
differentiated interests can keep distinct roles within a stable pattern of
coordination. The first half has an answer the record holds; the second
does not, so they are filed apart ([ADR-049](../decisions.d/ADR-049.md)).

## The answer

No ([CLAIM-261](../claims.d/CLAIM-261.md)). [TERM-050](../terms.d/TERM-050.md)'s integration by arbitration is an agent whose
regimes are overridden and which acts as one unsynchronized. Synchronization
is desirable where it can be had, not necessary.
