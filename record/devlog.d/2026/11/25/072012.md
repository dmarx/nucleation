**What went wrong.** On 2026-11-25 the owner asked whether the hyperarticle atlas should be filed as a synthesis or as a manuscript composed of manuscripts. The assistant answered with a recommendation and filed nothing, holding the work's registration because CLAUDE.md's dialectic paragraph lists `luria.yaml` among the steps that still wait for the owner. The owner asked whether the regime was on, then: "registering the work doesn't need to wait on me either. the whole point of dialectic mode is to be a largely 'hands free' mode."

**Why.** Two things. First, the answer argued two readings and ended on a request for a go-ahead, which the regime already forbids: a turn that would end by asking files each reading instead. Second, the list of held steps in CLAUDE.md (status changes on held entries, ADRs and DPs, `luria.yaml`, CI, delegated readings, outside services, merges into main) is broader than the owner holds it to be; the earlier works registrations of this session (ADR-tmp85hdc, ADR-tmpd3ckn) went ahead only because the owner had asked for them.

**What to change.** Read the held list as the owner's: the steps whose harm is hard to undo or whose blast radius is wide (merges into main, outside services, status changes on the owner's own entries), not every `luria.yaml` edit. A vocabulary entry that registers a work or a line under an existing rule is reversible by status and in scope; file it, and say so in the Filed block. Whether ADR-127's wording should be narrowed to match is the next retro's question.
