# CASE-377: Standards bodies with and without revision

## What the lead asked

[LEAD-129](../leads.d/LEAD-129.md) asks for a comparison of institutions with and without effective
means to revise their governing standards, matched on resources and on the
independence of evidence ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)), to advance [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md): that institutions
able to recognize, authorize and carry out revisions of their own governing
standards avoid persistent normatively significant failures better than
institutions that only monitor outcomes against fixed standards, where the
inherited standards are inadequate and not on routine work. The comparison is
what [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) was filed lacking.

## Selection rule

Public standards bodies, from open lawful sources — their own published
revision procedures and change histories — each governing a technical standard
that (a) was found inadequate to problems it had to represent, and (b) whose
body either has or lacks a *standing, resourced procedure* to recognize,
authorize and execute revisions. Pairs are matched *within a domain* so that
resources and the arrival of defect evidence ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s independence) are as
alike as the public record allows. This is a small, purposively chosen set, not
a sample. Choices, obstacles and how to rerun are in
scratch/studies/lead-129/README.md; the dates are in
scratch/studies/lead-129/pairs.json.

## The measure

Persistence: the years a recognized, normatively significant inadequacy of the
governing standard stood uncorrected, from the earliest dated recognition to its
correction, or to supersession of the regime.

## The record

- **P1, web markup, same era.** Revising: the WHATWG HTML Living Standard
  (founded 2004 after a W3C workshop, by Apple, Mozilla and Opera, over the
  W3C's lack of interest in evolving HTML; continuous editor-driven revision).
  Fixed: the W3C HTML/XHTML track — HTML 4.01 frozen December 1999, effort
  redirected to XHTML, conformance checked by the W3C Markup Validator (outcome
  monitoring against a fixed grammar). The same recognized inadequacy (HTML
  could not express rich web applications) was addressed continuously from 2004
  by the WHATWG and left standing until the W3C rechartered an HTML Working
  Group in 2007. Persistence 2 years against 8. The *better-resourced, more
  formal* body is the fixed arm.
- **P2, the same institution, before against after.** The W3C under
  version-and-freeze (1999–2011) against the W3C after the MOU of 28 May 2019,
  under which HTML and DOM are a single Living Standard developed at the WHATWG
  that the W3C endorses. The organization, its domain and its evidence sources
  are held constant; only the revision regime varies. Persistence 12 years
  against about zero (a defect becomes a GitHub issue addressed in due course).
- **P3, character representation.** Revising: the Unicode Consortium — versioned
  releases, a public "Report Error in Publication/Data" ticket with posted
  errata, a script-and-character proposal pipeline, and a committee empowered to
  authorize and execute additions; new scripts absorbed version by version (the
  single-default-skin-tone inadequacy corrected by modifiers in Unicode 8.0,
  2015, after public criticism). Fixed: the pre-Unicode code-page regime (ASCII
  and the ISO/IEC 8859 parts), each part effectively frozen, which answered
  new-script demand by minting new incompatible code pages rather than revising
  one standard; the multilingual-interchange inadequacy persisted for roughly
  two decades and was resolved only by Unicode superseding the regime.
  Persistence 2 years against about 20.
- **X1, unmatched extreme.** The Julian calendar had no standing organ empowered
  to revise the civil or ecclesiastical calendar. Drift from the Council of
  Nicaea's (325) reference equinox was recognized by Bede (8th century, about
  three days) and Roger Bacon (c. 1200–1267, seven or eight days) and corrected
  only by the bull *Inter gravissimas* (24 February 1582), which dropped ten
  days. Persistence of order 850 years (or about 1250 from Nicaea). Included
  only to bound the relationship at the no-revision-organ extreme; it has no
  matched revising partner.

## What it found

All three matched gaps run the same way: the arm with an effective
living-revision mechanism corrected recognized inadequacies in the order of days
to a few years; the fixed, outcome-monitoring arm left them standing for years
(web, ~8) to decades (code pages, ~20), and the unmatched extreme for centuries.
The direction is [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md)'s. Two features bear on its defeater, which would
have the difference come only from better evidence or more resources once those
are matched: in P1 the slower arm is the better-resourced one, and P2 holds the
institution and its evidence constant, varying only the regime. Both cut against
that rival. Unicode's stability policy — encoded characters, names and
normalization never revised — is *selective* non-revision, which matches
[CLAIM-665](../claims.d/CLAIM-665.md)'s routine-work limit rather than telling against the hypothesis: an
effective means to revise is not a disposition to revise everything.

## What it can show, and what it cannot

Being documented, it can show that the pattern [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md) predicts is visible in
real institutions whose procedures and histories are public, and that the two
nearest confounds its defeater names can be blunted by choosing a
resource-reversed pair and a within-institution pair. It cannot establish the
claim. The set is N = 3 pairs plus one extreme, chosen not sampled; "fixed" was
sometimes a deliberate governance bet (the W3C's XHTML2) rather than an absence
of capacity, so the operative variable is the operative standard's revision
regime, not the body's latent capacity; the persistence figures move with the
judgement of when recognition is dated; independence of evidence is matched only
roughly, best in P1 and P2; and famous standards are over-represented. It
therefore does not meet [CLAIM-685](../claims.d/CLAIM-685.md)'s defeater — it finds a difference, not none,
and not one explained away by evidence — so it *illustrates* the claim without
bearing its weight.
