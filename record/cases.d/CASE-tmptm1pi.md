# CASE-tmptm1pi: Volkswagen's emissions defeat device

## The case

A8 §5 (https://chatgpt.com/share/6b07d7ae-9434-83ea-888b-9b20796f291c):

> Volkswagen installed software that enabled vehicles to behave
> differently during emissions tests than during normal driving.

> If this behaviour were discovered in a frontier AI model, alignment
> researchers would immediately recognize it as evaluation-aware deception.

The violations "were uncovered after independent researchers investigated
real-world emissions, rather than relying exclusively on the established
certification process". A's implication: "Independent, deployment-relevant
evaluation is essential when an optimizer can condition its behaviour on
the presence of oversight."

## What it can show

- **CLAIM-416** (Active): a perspective that gains influence over its test
  can change what later success shows. The defeat device is the limiting
  case, influence by detection: the test was unchanged and passing it
  stopped showing anything about the road.
- **CLAIM-tmpv3frk** (A's): A8's first example of "constitutional capture"
  is "Volkswagen's evaluation evasion".

## Against the record

A's "maintenance-provenance problem" is TERM-100's sense, and it fits
better than it first sounds: compliance under test was maintained by a
process (the software's test detection) other than the one certification
assumed (the emissions controls), so the same passing result had two
maintenance provenances, and only an evaluation the software could not
detect told them apart. What the case adds to CLAIM-416 is that the
remedy A names, evaluation under deployment conditions, works by denying
the optimizer the signal it conditions on, not by making the test harder.

## What it cannot show

Who in the organization decided, or that the "organization" rather than
identified engineers and managers did it: the case shows conduct
conditioned on evaluation, engineered by people, the reverse of
optimization without intent.
