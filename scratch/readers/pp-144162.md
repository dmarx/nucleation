## Step 2: front matter, introduction, material, method (pp. 121-128)

- Intro p. 122: role vs identity taken from Ortmann & Sprott [8]: identity
  "relatively permanent in comparison with the role which may become
  fluctuant, related to the context, specific situation and/or activity".
  Switch defined p. 122. p. 123: "identity-related terms are approached
  descriptively and there is no reference to a specific theoretical concept."
- Sample p. 123: 12 self-selected (6 F, 6 M by designated gender; one,
  G4, genderfluid), aged 20-49, recruited "through thematic forums and
  websites"; interviews 15 min to 1.5 h; anonymous, unpaid; ethics approval
  (no number). Table 1 pp. 124-126: orientation, role, relationship status.
- Method pp. 126-128: constructivist grounded theory (Charmaz, Glaser &
  Strauss, Konecki); unstructured interviews on 8 listed topics; word-by-word,
  line, in vivo, focused coding; two coders coded separately then compared;
  saturation claimed. p. 128: the terms "identification (as a process of
  developing an identity) and a role (assumed in a given situation)" were
  introduced during analysis.
- Noted inconsistency: p. 129 "Three out of four examined females described
  themselves as heteroflexible", but Table 1 has six females, four of them
  heteroflexible (F1, F2, F3, F6).
