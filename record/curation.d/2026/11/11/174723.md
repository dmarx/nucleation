The fourth run of the reading review (ADR-091), and its first in the
by-topic mode, at the owner's word to proceed with the next pass. After
the read-and-file pass and its status review, the view of readings by
line counted 929 readings in force, 366 of them used by no line. The
by-question passes were done; this run takes the largest primary topics
of the unused list and asks of each reading whether the argument should
use it.

## The list

Five readers, each in its own worktree, by primary topic:
representation-learning (49); mathematics (45); quantum-foundations with
contextuality, logic, compositionality and philosophy-of-mathematics
(37); probabilistic-modeling with information-theory, information-geometry
and mathematical-statistics (48); learning-theory with loss-landscapes,
causality and game-theory (43). 222 readings, of which about 150 were read
in full after triage. The filer combined the parts, resolving one
conflict: two parts each changed CLAIM-543's grounds, and both changes
stand as successive versions.

## Raised

- **QUESTION-tmpiib1m** (Open, distributed-agency): does a law that
  constrains without being produced by what it constrains, such as a
  symmetry, a conservation law or constructor theory's laws of
  possibility, count as a constraint realized and maintained by
  something, or does it individuate kinds while realized by nothing? It
  is CLAIM-477's defeater (b), which its notes of 2026-10-27 to 11-06
  leave unruled. THEORY-1078 and THEORY-1066 bear on it. Two parts
  reached it from different topics; the second withdrew its duplicate.
- **CLAIM-tmpsx3zh** (Proposed, demonstrated, pragmatic-transport): a
  correspondence fitted from two structures' internal similarities alone
  is identified only up to their symmetries, and anchors break only the
  ties that move them. It joins three readings (THEORY-1133, 1155, 1129)
  that CASE-185 names only as a design threat, and corroborates
  CLAIM-143's proviso that correspondences be "independently anchored".

## What the skill should say

The readers found the same gaps: whether a claim's "supporting cases" and
a question's named instruments count as use (they read them as
`corroborates` and `bears_on`); how to count a reading tied to several
entries (once); that two parts of a split run should check each other's
raises; and that a case's or term's citation of a reading is use the
schemes cannot record. These are for a revision of the skill.
