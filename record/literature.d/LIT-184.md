# LIT-184: Effective Ontic Structural Realism

James Ladyman, Lorenzo Lorenzetti (2023), *The British Journal for the Philosophy of Science* — DOI-10.1086/729061

## Key takeaways

- The three current versions of "effective realism" (about non-relativistic quantum mechanics, about quantum field theory, and about superseded theories) can be unified, and made explicitly realist, under ontic structural realism, using realism about modal structure and the scale-relativity of ontology (real patterns).

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

Three versions of effective realism (ER) have been proposed in the BJPS for three problems facing scientific realism. Fraser and Vickers apply ER to non-relativistic quantum mechanics to avoid underdetermination among its interpretations. Williams and Fraser apply it to quantum field theory. Robertson and Wilson use it for the ontological status of entities in superseded theories. Ladyman and Lorenzetti argue that ontic structural realism (OSR), with its realism about modal structure and its scale-relative ontology, unifies these proposals and makes clear in what sense they are realist. They also answer a recent critique of the OSR treatment of quantum mechanics that they rely on.
