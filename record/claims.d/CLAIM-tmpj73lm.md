# CLAIM-tmpj73lm: A perspective's continued existence is not its operational availability: an interruption can be of activation (the disposition is held but not engaged or retrievable), of enactment (held, but the material, institutional or social conditions for exercising it are gone) or of reproduction (held by some bearers, but the processes that pass it on are damaged), and temporary unavailability is not extinction, though prolonged interruption of reproduction can end a tradition

## The claim

The owner, at U37 of the branch: "I think we also may have landed on a
likely (at least partial) simple, high-level explanation (or at the very
least intuition) for The Doorway Effect (i.e. connect perspective and
grounded experience). Also, another potentially interesting case to
examine in the neighborhood of the martial arts material is the (various)
perspectival interruption(s) caused by the Chinese Cultural Revolution."

A37 found in both "an important missing dimension in our theory of
organizational perspectives: **the conditions under which a perspective
remains available, not merely the conditions under which it is acquired
or reproduced**". It proposed three kinds of interruption, "not mutually
exclusive":

1. **Activation interruption.** "The relevant disposition remains
   available in principle but is no longer currently engaged or easily
   retrievable." The doorway effect is the near case ([CASE-tmprzyow](../cases.d/CASE-tmprzyow.md)): "an
   inaccessible intention is not necessarily an erased intention".
2. **Enactment interruption.** "The disposition remains possessed, but the
   material, institutional, or social conditions needed to exercise it are
   unavailable." A master whose skill is intact but who can no longer
   teach openly ([CASE-tmpvnchr](../cases.d/CASE-tmpvnchr.md)).
3. **Reproductive interruption.** "The disposition may continue to exist
   in some current bearers, but the processes needed to transmit it
   reliably to future bearers are damaged or destroyed." The loss of
   teachers and teaching institutions ([CASE-tmpvnchr](../cases.d/CASE-tmpvnchr.md)).

And the temporal point: temporary unavailability does not imply historical
extinction, but "Persistent interruption of reproduction" can contribute
to "eventual loss of the tradition". What returns afterwards may be
reinstatement of what was interrupted, or reconstruction through a
different organization of teaching, evaluation and authority; A37 called
the second a fifth possibility the martial case adds.

The record has the vocabulary on each side. [TERM-055](../terms.d/TERM-055.md) separates possessing
a resource, knowing it is there, retrieving it and deploying it in time;
activation interruption is a loss at the third step with the first
intact. [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md) and [CLAIM-287](CLAIM-287.md) hold persistence through inactivity as a
reinstatable capacity. Minsky's K-lines ([NOTE-352](../notes.d/NOTE-352.md)), memory as partial
reinstatement of an earlier organization of processes, is the record's
nearest reading of why an intention can be held and not retrievable. And
the record already holds a doorway in a learner: options learned for an
earlier layout of rooms kept an agent from finding a new shortcut
([THEORY-688](../theory.d/THEORY-688.md), from [LIT-581](../literature.d/LIT-581.md)), which A37 read as "restrictive segmentation".

The owner's connection to grounding is the claim's second point. A37: "A
perspective can be grounded not only in how it was acquired, but also in
the **ongoing environmental arrangements that make its enactment
possible**." That extends [CLAIM-413](CLAIM-413.md), which keeps grounding apart from
whether an experience occurs: here grounding bears on whether a held
perspective can be exercised at all.

## What it does not say

- **Not that a doorway and a revolution are alike.** A37: "They differ
  profoundly in mechanism, timescale, severity, and moral significance."
  The comparison "concerns specific structural relations, not equivalence
  of experience or consequence."
- **Not a new explanation of the doorway effect.** A37: "That isn't yet a
  new scientific explanation. It's a way of interpreting established
  event-cognition findings through our broader organizational-perspective
  framework." The owner offered it as "at least partial" and "at the very
  least intuition", and the claim keeps that standing.
- **Not that the effect is robust.** It has failed to replicate under
  some conditions ([CASE-tmprzyow](../cases.d/CASE-tmprzyow.md)).
