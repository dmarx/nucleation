## The search for tests of H1 and H3

Europe PMC REST search, 2026-11-22, no email sent:

- Titles with mind-wandering and tDCS, stimulation, theta burst, TMS, tACS
  or stimulating: 47 hits. They divide into the frontal anodal tDCS line
  (Axelrod 2015; Axelrod, Zhu and Qiu 2018; Boayue et al. 2019; Filmer,
  Griffin and Dux 2019; Coulborn and Fernández-Espejo 2022, null), reverse
  polarity and parietal or medial montages (Kajimura and Nomura 2015;
  Bertossi et al. 2017; Coulborn et al. 2020; Filmer, Marcus and Dux 2021),
  theta-burst TMS (Aasen et al. 2024; Drevland et al. 2024; Alexandersen et
  al. 2025, angular gyrus, pre-registered), a meta-analysis (Nawani,
  Mittner and Csifcsák 2023: "no reliable evidence for tDCS influencing MW
  in the healthy", abstract only) and a review (Kam, Mittner and Knight
  2022).
- Titles with mind-wandering and meta-awareness, neurofeedback, closed-loop,
  self-caught, lucid or metacognition: 21 hits, among them Zedelius,
  Broadway and Schooler 2015 (incentives for catching one's own
  mind-wandering) and Kawashima et al. 2023 (neurofeedback that raises
  meta-awareness of mind-wandering), both closest in kind to H3, neither
  read.
- "epistemic agent model", or Metzinger with mental autonomy and
  mind-wandering: 13 hits, all theoretical (commentaries on Metzinger 2013
  and 2015, active-inference papers), none a test.

No study found manipulates the agent's awareness of its own control, checks
that the manipulation reached it, and records the onset (H1) or end (H3)
of mind-wandering episodes apart from the probe that reports them. The
stimulation studies change how much mind-wandering is reported; the
meta-awareness studies measure or train noticing. The best two of the
first line were read: the replication that added a meta-awareness probe
(nearest to H1) and the pre-registered replication (the strongest test of
the effect).
