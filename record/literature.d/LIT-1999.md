# LIT-1999: Methodological considerations for studying neural oscillations

Donoghue, T., Schaworonkow, N., Voytek, B. (2021). Methodological considerations for studying neural oscillations. *European Journal of Neuroscience, 55(11-12), 3502-3527*. doi:10.1111/ejn.15361.

## Key takeaways

- Non-sinusoidal waveforms produce significant phase-amplitude coupling with no second rhythm; coupling confined to exact harmonic multiples is the warning sign ([THEORY-1312](../theory.d/THEORY-1312.md)).
- Aperiodic 1/f activity has power at all frequencies and varies with state, so band power and 1/f structure are not by themselves evidence of oscillations or of any particular organization ([THEORY-1337](../theory.d/THEORY-1337.md)).
- Read in [NOTE-1529](../notes.d/NOTE-1529.md).

## Standing in the record

The methodological caution A5 raises against phase-amplitude coupling: non-sinusoidal waveforms produce apparent coupling. A5's "Park and colleagues (2017)" could not be identified.
