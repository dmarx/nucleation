# LIT-2967: The Smallest Singular Directions of the Unembedding Matter Disproportionately for Next-Token Prediction

Weilun Xu (2026). arXiv 2605.17084 (v2, November 2026), preprint.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws", where A cites it at A41 for the weight of the unembedding's smallest singular directions. Read the same day ([NOTE-2249](../notes.d/NOTE-2249.md)) and filed as [THEORY-2162](../theory.d/THEORY-2162.md). It is the record's best evidence that the logit metric and the pulled-back predictive Fisher disagree in trained models ([CLAIM-835](../claims.d/CLAIM-835.md), [ARG-139](../arguments.d/ARG-139.md)), as a disagreement per unit of logit distance, not a reversal. Its directions are quiet in the logit metric and loud in representation variance ([QUESTION-511](../questions.d/QUESTION-511.md)).
