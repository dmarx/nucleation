summary: >-
  Driscoll, Shenoy and Sussillo (Nature Neuroscience 2024). Recurrent
  networks trained on 15 cognitive tasks reuse dynamical motifs
  (attractors, decision boundaries, rotations around fixed points) across
  tasks that share subtasks; with non-negative activations motifs sit in
  unit clusters whose lesion impairs only the tasks using them. With all
  recurrent weights frozen, a held-out task is learned by training only
  its rule input: fast after pretraining that contained its motifs, slow,
  variable or failed without, and behind full-weight training for tasks
  needing motifs no other task used. Three to five networks per condition.
  Read in NOTE-tmp7fq38; account THEORY-tmpmir63.
