# LIT-1800: CycleGAN, a Master of Steganography

Chu, C., Zhmoginov, A., & Sandler, M. (2017). CycleGAN, a master of steganography. NIPS 2017 Workshop on Machine Deception.

## Why this is here

A5 called it "the most revealing conceptual warning": a round trip can be perfect while the intended correspondence is not learned, because the information travels in a channel the audience cannot see. It is the converse failure to [CLAIM-496](../claims.d/CLAIM-496.md)'s collapse, where routes agree because nothing travels, and a learned instance of [THEORY-1088](../theory.d/THEORY-1088.md)'s consensus by changed maps.

The reading ([NOTE-1346](../notes.d/NOTE-1346.md)) bears the description out. One addition: the
loop is not wholly unchecked, since discriminators score the map; but they
score whether it looks like a map, not whether it says what the photograph
shows.
