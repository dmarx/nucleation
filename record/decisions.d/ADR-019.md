# ADR-019: game-theory joins the topics

## Context

Filing the morality-as-cooperation literature ([ADR-018](ADR-018.md)) brought in its
evolutionary sources: Maynard Smith & Price on animal conflict, Axelrod &
Hamilton on the evolution of cooperation, Nowak's five rules, and Curry's
founding chapter, which builds each of its seven domains on a game. Two
agents filing different parts of the batch each found the same gap and
proposed the same word. Neither existing candidate fits:

- `probabilistic-modeling` is Bayesian inference and statistical models;
- `mathematics` is "mathematics read for itself", with frameworks applied
  to other fields as a clause, which is how the evolutionary papers were
  tagged as a stand-in.

The record already held game-theoretic works with nowhere to put the fact:
Lewis's *Convention*, Hindriks & Guala's rules in equilibrium, Bicchieri's
norms, and Debreu's social equilibrium existence theorem.

## Decision

Add `game-theory` to `topics`, beside `mathematics`, in no group. Its
blurb: "strategic interaction and its equilibria — evolutionary stability,
repeated games, bargaining, coordination, signalling and conventions — in
biology, economics and philosophy".

It is a method and a body of results, so like `phenomenology` ([ADR-016](ADR-016.md)) it
is not expected to lead. A work carries it when its argument runs through
a game, not merely when it mentions cooperation.

**Tagged.** No tag was removed.

- **After the subject:** [LIT-505](../literature.d/LIT-505.md) (Maynard Smith & Price 1973),
  [LIT-498](../literature.d/LIT-498.md) (Axelrod & Hamilton 1981), [LIT-501](../literature.d/LIT-501.md) (Nowak 2006),
  [LIT-473](../literature.d/LIT-473.md) (Lewis, *Convention*), [LIT-481](../literature.d/LIT-481.md) (Hindriks & Guala), [LIT-493](../literature.d/LIT-493.md)
  (Bicchieri) and [LIT-067](../literature.d/LIT-067.md) (Debreu 1952).
- **Last:** [LIT-503](../literature.d/LIT-503.md) (Curry 2016).

Hamilton 1964 is population genetics, not a game, and stays without it.
Peters & Adamou's ergodicity solution of cooperation ([LIT-035](../literature.d/LIT-035.md)) pools
resources under multiplicative growth with no strategic choice, and
stays without it too.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep `mathematics` as the stand-in.** It says the papers are
  mathematical, not what the mathematics is, and Lewis and Bicchieri are
  not mathematics papers at all.
- **Widen `probabilistic-modeling`.** Its blurb is samplers, priors and
  topic models; a reader browsing it for Bayesian methods would meet
  hawks and retaliators.
- **A narrower word, `evolutionary-game-theory`.** It would leave out
  Lewis's conventions and Debreu, which are classical, and the record holds
  both kinds.
