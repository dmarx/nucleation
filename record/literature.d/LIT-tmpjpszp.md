# LIT-tmpjpszp: Within-person variation in security of attachment: A self-determination theory perspective on attachment, need fulfillment, and well-being.

La Guardia, J. G., Ryan, R. M., Couchman, C. E., Deci, E. L. (2000). Within-person variation in security of attachment: A self-determination theory perspective on attachment, need fulfillment, and well-being.. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 79(3), 367-384*. doi:10.1037/0022-3514.79.3.367.

## Key takeaways

- Attachment security is mostly relational, not a trait: within-person
  variance across partners was 56%, 79% and about two thirds in the three
  studies, with error included.
- Need satisfaction with a partner predicts security with that partner, in
  every relationship tested (Table 3, betas .59–.79 for overall security in
  Study 2).
- The relatedness items overlap conceptually with security, which the authors
  handle by repeating every analysis without them; autonomy then predicts most.

## Standing in the record

Registered as part of Relationships Motivation Theory's evidence, which the
record held only through Patrick et al. 2007. It bears on [QUESTION-tmpee04a](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpee04a.md)'s
interdefinition worry at instrument level: the need most predictive of the
outcome is the one closest to it in content ([NOTE-tmpo9h31](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpo9h31.md)).
