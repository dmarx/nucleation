# SYNTHESIS-090: How the record as an apparatus would be put to the test: whether governing schema change does work, whether a shared record moves distributed evidence into judgement, and whether a collective keeping one can improve its improver without losing correctability

## The account

The record describes its grammar as fixed at any moment and amended by
decision when a misfit is noticed ([CLAIM-419](../claims.d/CLAIM-419.md)), and luria as an apparatus
that differentiates, retains, enforces and revises ([CLAIM-469](../claims.d/CLAIM-469.md)). Three
questions ask whether that does any work, and each is the owner's.

**Governed schema change.** [QUESTION-458](../questions.d/QUESTION-458.md) (U8): is the coupling of
schema change to decision, migration and checks what distinguishes luria
from ontology and metamodel evolution, and does it do work that
argumentation-governed or performance-gated evolution does not?
[CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md) (A) proposes the answer to the second half, that
governance raises the value of modifiability on tasks whose categories
are inadequate; [CLAIM-665](../claims.d/CLAIM-665.md) (A) is a secondary prediction, separate
and not following from it, that on routine tasks governance adds cost and
no benefit. [CASE-253](../cases.d/CASE-253.md) (A) is the study that would measure both,
seeded with [CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md). [CLAIM-666](../claims.d/CLAIM-666.md) (A) says what a migration in such a
study must preserve; [CLAIM-664](../claims.d/CLAIM-664.md) (A) says what the record leaves to
judgement when an entry is retired.

**Distributed evidence.** [QUESTION-459](../questions.d/QUESTION-459.md) (U12, U13): does a shared,
typed, governed record move distributed evidence into judgement better
than a structured protocol, and at which of [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s stages? It is
stated in [TERM-119](../terms.d/TERM-119.md) (A, at the owner's request), and [CLAIM-668](../claims.d/CLAIM-668.md)
(A) bounds what such a record can represent of its collaborators, resting
on [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md)'s third obstruction. [CASE-252](../cases.d/CASE-252.md) (A) is the study,
adapting an existing benchmark, with the benchmark's Secretary arm, a
shared summary of what has been disclosed, as the baseline a record must
beat; [CASE-254](../cases.d/CASE-254.md) (A), from [CASE-194](../cases.d/CASE-194.md), tests
whether a team can find which stage or level failed before it repairs.

**Recursive improvement.** [QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md) (U17): can a collective
keeping such a record become a better improver, compared from the same
start at the same budget, without losing the capacity to detect failures
in its own apparatus? [QUESTION-388](../questions.d/QUESTION-388.md) holds the distinction it probes.
[CASE-255](../cases.d/CASE-255.md) (A) is the study, a variant of [CASE-186](../cases.d/CASE-186.md).

**Across the three.** [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md)'s correctability is the outcome each study
reports (its note of 2026-11-12). [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)'s frontier says how a change of
grammar would be scored (its note of 2026-11-12), and [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md)'s
legibility is the explanation [CASE-254](../cases.d/CASE-254.md)'s budget arms would test.
[QUESTION-108](../questions.d/QUESTION-108.md)'s three mechanisms are the ablations. [CLAIM-266](../claims.d/CLAIM-266.md) states the
limit every one of them shares: a study scored by the record's own past
decisions, or by an evaluator its subjects can edit, certifies nothing.

## What is left open

Each question is Open and every design unrun. No component says the
record would do better than its comparators; [CLAIM-667](../claims.d/CLAIM-667.md), the central
prediction, and [CLAIM-665](../claims.d/CLAIM-665.md), a secondary one, are the only claims about
outcomes, and neither has a result behind it.
