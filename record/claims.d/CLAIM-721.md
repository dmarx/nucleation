# CLAIM-721: Competence restrictions confirm themselves

## The claim

A41 §8 of the exchange (https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b),
"Restrictions can become ratchets":

> An actor's capability is temporarily restricted.
>
> The restriction prevents them from exercising and maintaining the relevant competence.
>
> Their competence deteriorates.
>
> The deterioration becomes evidence that they remain unqualified.
>
> The original restriction is extended.

"This is a possible **restriction-induced capability trap**." The
restriction's justification "may have been sound", but "the institution
has organized it in a way that produces the conditions for its own
indefinite continuation." A49 §8.1: "The original limitation has begun
producing the conditions for its own persistence."

In [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)'s terms: a restriction on **exercise**, through disuse,
becomes a restriction on **competence**, and the party who holds the
**authority** to lift it reads the decay as the reason not to.

**Three conditions** make the loop close, and the title carries them:
competence decays with disuse; lifting depends on competence shown; and
the restricting party supplies no route to practice. Drop the first and
the restriction is only a pause. Drop the second and the restriction ends
on a date, whatever the decay. Drop the third and A41 §8's contrast case
holds: an institution that "temporarily limits the same activity while
providing training, evaluation, and a clear route to restoring
authority". "The two restrictions may look identical at the moment
they're imposed." What differs is "**the organization of restoration**".

**The obligation it implies.** A41 §8: "It may not be enough to say: *You
cannot exercise this capability until condition X is met.* We may also
need to ask: *Who is responsible for making condition X feasible?*" A49
§8.1: where restoration is part of a restriction's justification, "the
organization must not treat an inaccessible restoration pathway as if it
were a genuine opportunity."

**A second instance**, A45 §9: a company restricts overrides of an AI
system for consistency, independent expertise declines, and "That decline
subsequently becomes the reason management gives for extending automation
and further restricting human discretion." Here the decaying competence
is a workforce's, and the restriction's beneficiary gains discretion as
it lasts.

## How it sits with the record

- **[CLAIM-602](CLAIM-602.md) and [CLAIM-605](CLAIM-605.md)** (Active) demonstrate the disuse dynamics in
  a model: where capacity is learned by doing and decays with disuse,
  outside supply that does the work lowers the capacity kept, and support
  has a deadline after which withdrawal no longer restores it. This claim
  needs only their premise, decay with disuse, and not their supply; the
  restriction removes the doing directly. A model of the loop would be
  [CLAIM-605](CLAIM-605.md)'s with reinstatement made a threshold on capacity, which would
  make the trap demonstrable; no one has written it.
- **[CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md)** (Proposed): an arrangement that does the work of governing
  erodes the capacity to govern it. A45 §9's second instance is that
  claim reached by restriction rather than substitution.
- **[CLAIM-675](CLAIM-675.md)** (Proposed): supports that look alike now leave different
  capabilities later. Restrictions do too, A45 §9: "The same immediate
  restriction may lead to very different relational futures."
- **[CLAIM-714](CLAIM-714.md)** (F5, same exchange): a present incapacity does not
  show that the incapacitated party is responsible for it. This claim is a
  route by which an incapacity can be produced by the very party that
  cites it.

## What it does not say

- Not that restrictions on the incompetent are wrong. A49 §8.1: "Not every
  restriction must contain such a pathway. Certain conduct should remain
  prohibited regardless of the actor's competence."
- Not that the trap is intended. The loop closes without anyone aiming at
  it; whether a party that keeps it closed is responsible is
  [CLAIM-714](CLAIM-714.md)'s question.
- Not that restriction always shrinks future capability. A41 §8: "A
  temporary suspension of a risky automated system may preserve evidence,
  prevent irreversible harm, and provide time to investigate", so
  "present restraint protects future governability."

## Note of 2026-11-22: the model has been written ([LEAD-104](../leads.d/LEAD-104.md))

The model this claim named and said "no one has written" — [CLAIM-605](CLAIM-605.md)'s
"with reinstatement made a threshold on capacity" — was written and run
carrying out [LEAD-104](../leads.d/LEAD-104.md). It is [CASE-364](../cases.d/CASE-364.md), an executed study, and its
finding is [CLAIM-950](CLAIM-950.md). Making reinstatement a threshold C_r on capacity
closes this claim's loop in the model: with no route a party restricted
below C_r decays monotonically, never reaches C_r, and is never
reinstated, so the restriction manufactures the evidence that extends it;
the trap has a recoverability deadline past which release no longer helps;
and it is escaped only by a practice route above a critical fraction (which
cannot ignite a party below threshold) or a training route that adds
capacity directly and ignites from zero, which is this claim's "route to
practice **or training**" told apart. Each of the three conditions in the
title is shown necessary. [CLAIM-950](CLAIM-950.md) corroborates this claim with a
formal demonstration; it does not rest on the model, which reads no real
institution. Whether the demonstration moves this claim's warrant from
interpretive to demonstrated is the owner's call. No status change.

## Note of 2026-11-23: the trap without decay, from "Explain First Contact RL"

A54 §6 of the exchange "Explain First Contact RL" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-23), on the owner's employee who must justify an idea to a manager ([CASE-386](../cases.d/CASE-386.md)): "A manager who requires extensive justification before allowing any exploration may demand evidence that cannot exist until exploration occurs", a loop of no evidence, no authorization, no exploration, no evidence. That form is held by [CLAIM-406](CLAIM-406.md) (Active), where an arrangement keeps an exclusion going "through the indicators of competence that only being sought produces". A54's remedy is this claim's "route to practice": "A small preauthorized exploration budget", "A staged process in which early experiments generate evidence before larger authorization is required", and maintained perspectives in which questions "can be developed and provisionally tested without requiring immediate endorsement by the whole organization" ([CLAIM-1177](CLAIM-1177.md)). No status change.
