# LIT-1374: Post-Incident Analysis: Independent Review of the Brookhollow Apartment Fire

Jensen Hughes, Inc. (2024). *Post-Incident Analysis: Independent Review of the Brookhollow Apartment Fire*. Prepared for Sedgwick County and the City of Wichita, project 1JHM24002, Rev. 0, 16 December 2024. https://sedgwickcounty.org/media/67974/12162024_brookhollow_pia_report_r0.pdf.

Read in full on 2026-10-22 in [NOTE-1073](../notes.d/NOTE-1073.md).

## Why this is here

The owner proposed escalating responses under the National Incident Management System, and a fireground mayday that changed a safety culture, as multi-regime cases (U23). A23 and A24 took Brookhollow as the documented instance. The case is [CASE-135](../cases.d/CASE-135.md).

**From the reading, 2026-10-22.** The report confirms the case's quoted
findings and the exchange's unverified details, with one exception. It
calls a mayday an "incident within an incident"; it finds that search of
the burning building was not resumed for over eleven minutes after the
mayday although the mayday was under control within minutes; and it
finds that command did not announce the mayday's end, as WFD policy
required. The "four firefighters" is not in it. The later mayday
procedure postdates it; the county's implementation dashboard records a
"COMMAND PROCEDURES - MAYDAY" chapter adopted into WFD policy by 24
October 2025. See [NOTE-1073](../notes.d/NOTE-1073.md) for the bearing on [CLAIM-422](../claims.d/CLAIM-422.md),
[CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) and [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md).
