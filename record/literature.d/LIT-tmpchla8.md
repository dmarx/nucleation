# LIT-tmpchla8: Topological Mechanics of Origami and Kirigami

Chen, B. G., Liu, B., Evans, A. A., Paulose, J., Cohen, I., Vitelli, V. & Santangelo, C. D. (2016). Topological Mechanics of Origami and Kirigami. *Physical Review Letters* 116(13): 135501. doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.135501. arXiv:1508.00795 (v1 2015-08-04).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, NOTE-tmpztu5h.*

- **Topology, not counting.** "These folding motions exist due to an
  underlying topological invariant rather than a local imbalance between
  constraints and degrees of freedom" (abstract).
- **Linear counting fails.** "Coplanar hinges in the flat state are
  redundant constraints, and this results in extra zero modes at linear
  order which do not extend to higher order."
- **Flexible walls.** "a domain wall between two topologically distinct,
  mechanically rigid structures is deformable even when constraints
  locally match the degrees of freedom" (abstract); but "a domain wall
  between distinct patterns with equal polarization has no such localized
  modes".
- **Origami unpolarized, conjecturally.** "Though a proof of this
  statement for all triangulated origami eludes us, extensive numerical
  tests on a large number of distinct fold patterns bear out this
  conjecture."
- **Walls soften or stress.** Walls between different polarizations
  create either "soft" lines or "stressed" lines "which first bear the
  loads under applied strains".

## Standing in the record

The pasted synthesis on constraint architectures cites it for a localized
flexible mode at the interface between two rigid regions, predicted by a
topological distinction. Right, but narrower than the synthesis suggests:
the interface result is for kirigami, not origami proper; it needs
different polarizations on the two sides; and such walls can equally be
stressed lines that carry load. It is also the clearest support in this
batch for the synthesis's caveat that a constraint count does not
guarantee finite motion. It applies the topological polarization of Kane
and Lubensky (LIT-tmpldjyt). Filed from it: THEORY-tmp2e4h8. No
instruction for machine-learning practice.
