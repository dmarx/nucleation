### Added

- **Works are documents, and a manuscript is anchored to the argument**
  (ADR-067). The `works` vocabulary becomes the WORK scheme (WORK-001 to
  WORK-010, the four folded essays Superseded by WORK-005). A manuscript's
  prose is SECTION documents whose paragraphs say in `ref::asserts` and
  `ref::discusses` directives what they stake on the record's claims. Each
  manuscript renders a reading copy and a ledger, and four patterns warn
  when a section drops a member, asserts outside its work, or asserts a
  Rejected or Superseded claim.
- The Individual and Its Conditions (WORK-010) with its nine sections, on
  ten existing claims, with one new claim (an organized product and the
  processes maintaining it need not share a boundary) and one new question
  (when provenance justifies constitution).

### Changed

- `works:` on claims, arguments, cases and questions now holds WORK codes,
  and the per-work facet pages under `docs/claims/works/` are replaced by
  the WORK pages.
