# THEORY-621: Indexing each quantum fact to the system it is a fact for, and computing probabilities only between facts relative to the same system, removes the contradiction between the collapse and unitary probability rules in Wigner's-friend cases; the price is that values are not held at all times or by all systems alike, there is no global view, and no quantum state of the universe

## Source

Rovelli (2021), [LIT-177](../literature.d/LIT-177.md), a handbook chapter, read in full in [NOTE-174](../notes.d/NOTE-174.md).

## What was actually shown

**The contradiction (pp. 2–3).** For a fact a and mutually exclusive facts
bᵢ, P_collapse(c|a) = Σᵢ |W(c,bᵢ)|²|W(bᵢ,a)|² (eq. 3), while
P_unitary(c|a) = |Σᵢ W(c,bᵢ)W(bᵢ,a)|² (eq. 4). These differ by interference.

**The rule.** Facts are sparse, occurring only in interactions, and
relative, labelled by the interacting systems. Amplitudes W(b,a) have
physical meaning only if a and b are relative to the same system. Eq. (3)
"does not hold if the bᵢ have different labels than c". What Wigner's friend
sees is a fact for the friend; for Wigner it is a correlation, which can
still interfere. Any physical system can be an observer (p. 7).

**Stable facts (p. 3).** When decoherence makes P_collapse ≈ P_unitary,
labels can be ignored. On this view, ontologies built on stable facts alone
(Copenhagen, QBism) rest on an approximation: "we are all actually Wigner's
friends". The decoherence result is cited, not shown ([NOTE-174](../notes.d/NOTE-174.md), C3).

**ψ (pp. 3–4).** ψ is a relative state with no ontological weight, compared
to a Hamilton–Jacobi function. RQM posits no ontic state and so
"circumvents" PBR, whose hidden premise is said to be strong realism.

**The costs (pp. 6–7).** Strong realism is given up in three respects:
variables do not take values at all times, and take them at different times
for different systems; values held relative to different systems can be
compared only by a further physical interaction, so "there is no coherent
global view available"; and "there is no meaning in 'the quantum state of
the full universe'".

**What could have come out otherwise.** A relabelled rule could have left
some Wigner's-friend prediction inconsistent. Inside the chapter none is
derived, because eq. (3) is never applied across labels.

## What this does not say

- **That the relative-labelling rule is derived.** It is postulated as RQM's
  "core idea" ([NOTE-174](../notes.d/NOTE-174.md), C2). It removes the contradiction by restricting
  when eq. (3) applies.
- **That perspectives cohere.** Inter-observer consistency, the chief
  objection in the literature, gets one sentence. Whether what one system
  records about another agrees with what it later learns is not addressed.
- **That no-go theorems for absolute facts favour RQM.** Calling them
  "direct evidence in favour of RQM" (p. 6) outruns them: they rule out
  observer-independent facts under their assumptions, a premise QBism and
  Everett also deny (C8).
- **That all of quantum phenomenology follows from qp − pq = iħ.** The
  chapter also needs a representation, a composition rule and the Born rule
  (C7).
- **That ψ-realism is answered.** The chapter argues against the 1926
  "waves in space" reading and by analogy; Wallace- and Carroll-style
  realism is not engaged.

## Connections

- **Rivals** [THEORY-630](THEORY-630.md). Gao's protective-measurement argument makes ψ
  ontic and adds a preferred frame; here ψ has no ontological weight and
  there is no global view. Both cannot be right about ψ.
- PBR ([THEORY-605](THEORY-605.md)) is sidestepped by positing no ontic state. Whether
  that is a third option or falls outside the framework is the question
  [THEORY-586](THEORY-586.md) raises.
- Dorato ([LIT-176](../literature.d/LIT-176.md)) classes RQM as a ψ-antirealist ontology of events
  ([THEORY-656](THEORY-656.md)). Cuffaro & Hartmann's open-systems view shares the
  denial of a closed universal system ([THEORY-631](THEORY-631.md)). Carroll's
  Hilbert-space fundamentalism ([LIT-123](../literature.d/LIT-123.md)) is the opposite pole.

## Note of 2026-10-27 (evening): the readings of the U15–U23 works

Two readings bear on this reading's open points.

- **The decoherence step, "cited, not shown", is shown.** Di Biagio and
  Rovelli 2021 (NOTE-tmppgt2i, eqs. 5–8, p. 4) give it, with an O(ε) error
  that is stated without a bound. The same-context rule is still asserted
  ("it is always the case", p. 3), and agreement between observers still
  gets one sentence (p. 10), so the promote_when is not met.
- **A correction to the title's price.** "Values are not held at all times"
  belongs to the 2021 sparse-facts formulation. Di Biagio and Rovelli 2025
  (NOTE-tmp2eky0, p. 7) say their reformulation "allows talk of relative
  facts also in-between interactions". A relative fact is now a property
  of the joint distribution at any time.

The title is left as it stands, as a reading of the 2021 text. Whether it
should be superseded by one of the 2025 formulation is open.
