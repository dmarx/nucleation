The THEORY backfill reached the consciousness readings: eighteen LITs with
a `Read` NOTE that no THEORY cited as `source`. Each NOTE was read in full
with its LIT, and existing THEORYs were checked for the same account
before anything was filed. Codes are the temporary ones `luria new`
assigned.

## What entered

Thirteen THEORYs, two `Active` and eleven `Proposed`.

- **Two findings, `Active`.**
  - [THEORY-tmpo9c4c](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpo9c4c.md), from Lindahl et al. ([LIT-066](../../record/literature.d/LIT-066.md)): challenging
    meditation-related experiences span 59 categories in seven domains and
    occur in daily practice, at low dose and in the first days, not only
    on retreat. Filed as a description of a sample chosen for difficulty,
    with self-attributed causation.
  - [THEORY-tmpd2ifs](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpd2ifs.md), from Kleiner & Ludwig ([LIT-143](../../record/literature.d/LIT-143.md)): satisfying a
    structure's axioms on experiential aspects does not make it a
    structure of experience. The negative half is proved by construction,
    which is why it is `Active`; the two positive cases are carried with
    their assumed premises.
- **The hard problem and its rival.** [THEORY-tmpz08ce](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpz08ce.md) (Chalmers 1995,
  [LIT-406](../../record/literature.d/LIT-406.md), with Nagel, [LIT-096](../../record/literature.d/LIT-096.md)) and illusionism, [THEORY-tmpspj51](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpspj51.md) (Frankish
  2016, [LIT-458](../../record/literature.d/LIT-458.md), with Dennett's "Facing Backwards", [LIT-397](../../record/literature.d/LIT-397.md), and Frankish
  2025, [LIT-103](../../record/literature.d/LIT-103.md)), filed as `rivals`.
- **Chalmers's other papers.** Organisational invariance ([THEORY-tmpdz76a](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpdz76a.md),
  [LIT-392](../../record/literature.d/LIT-392.md) with [LIT-406](../../record/literature.d/LIT-406.md)), the combination problem ([THEORY-tmpuo0qe](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpuo0qe.md), [LIT-395](../../record/literature.d/LIT-395.md)),
  the meta-problem challenge ([THEORY-tmpltl13](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpltl13.md), [LIT-425](../../record/literature.d/LIT-425.md)) and the
  temporary-objections argument about language models ([THEORY-tmp7xef7](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp7xef7.md),
  [LIT-411](../../record/literature.d/LIT-411.md)).
- **Dennett and his readers.** "Quining Qualia" ([THEORY-tmp26rh2](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp26rh2.md), [LIT-433](../../record/literature.d/LIT-433.md))
  and Taylor's real-pattern reading ([THEORY-tmpk5k3c](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpk5k3c.md), [LIT-461](../../record/literature.d/LIT-461.md)).
- **The rest.** IIT's ill-definedness and the proxy point ([THEORY-tmpfj3d3](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpfj3d3.md),
  [LIT-185](../../record/literature.d/LIT-185.md)); Shanahan's candidature by encounter ([THEORY-tmppj0ht](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmppj0ht.md), [LIT-465](../../record/literature.d/LIT-465.md));
  pooled processing feelings as a contributor to cross-modal unity
  ([THEORY-tmplcsdb](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmplcsdb.md), [LIT-190](../../record/literature.d/LIT-190.md), itself a `Proposed` LIT).
- **`answers`.** Invariance answers [QUESTION-046](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-046.md) in part: it is a constraint
  on any criterion (a population isomorph is a subject), not a criterion.
  The meta-problem challenge answers [QUESTION-048](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-048.md) in part: a basis that
  does not explain the reports cannot be confirmed by them, and puzzled
  reports can be mimicked.

## What did not enter, and why

- **Schwitzgebel's reply to Kammerer ([LIT-459](../../record/literature.d/LIT-459.md), [NOTE-381](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-381.md)).** No new THEORY.
  [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) already states and argues from its three points (the
  condition-A gap, the Leo case, the simplicity dilemma) in its Kammerer
  and invariance bullets and in `promote_when`. The reply was added to
  that account's `source` instead.
- **Mashour & Alkire ([LIT-965](../../record/literature.d/LIT-965.md), [NOTE-797](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-797.md)).** No THEORY, as the NOTE decided.
  It is a perspective with no data; its one finding is second-hand from a
  study the record does not hold; its marker of returning consciousness
  is an access marker, which the authors concede. What it bears on is held
  by [THEORY-091](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-091.md), [THEORY-090](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-090.md), [THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md) and [THEORY-100](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-100.md).

So seventeen of eighteen readings became sources, sixteen of them of new
accounts. That fits the owner's hypothesis for this topic, with three
caveats. Most of these readings are philosophical arguments, so most
accounts are `Proposed` and say what kind of result would move them;
several of those results do not yet exist anywhere. Two NOTEs said "No new
THEORY" ([NOTE-346](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-346.md), [NOTE-357](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-357.md)) because the record lacks the replies, not
because the works lack a claim; under the owner's ruling those were filed
`Proposed`, and [THEORY-tmp26rh2](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp26rh2.md) says what its NOTE said. [NOTE-089](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-089.md) said Nagel
supports no theory "in the anthology's sense"; Nagel entered as the second
source of the hard-problem account rather than as an account of his own,
since a separate Nagel THEORY would have restated the same gap.

## Choices made

- **Rivals.** Only the hard problem and illusionism are declared rivals:
  they disagree about whether the further question has an answer to find.
  Shanahan's account denies [THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md)'s presupposition rather than giving
  another answer to it, so it is named in Connections, not as a rival.
  Invariance and IIT cannot both be true for isomorphs, but the record has
  no THEORY stating IIT's exclusion as an account, so that conflict is
  stated in prose.
- **Frankish's commentary ([LIT-103](../../record/literature.d/LIT-103.md))** entered as a source of the illusionism
  account, for the bureau-de-change application, rather than as a THEORY
  of its own. Its charge against Veit is conditional, and [THEORY-101](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-101.md)
  already records Veit's concession.
- **Edits to existing documents.** [LIT-185](../../record/literature.d/LIT-185.md) v3, above. [THEORY-043](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-043.md) v6 gains [LIT-459](../../record/literature.d/LIT-459.md) in its
  `source` and a Connections line naming the new accounts. [THEORY-023](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-023.md) v4
  gains [LIT-411](../../record/literature.d/LIT-411.md) in its `source`, as [NOTE-345](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-345.md) recommended, with a short section
  on what it adds and Connections lines for Shanahan and the meta-problem.
  Both claims are unchanged.

  takeaway is corrected ([LIT-185](../../record/literature.d/LIT-185.md) v3), and the THEORY follows the NOTE.
