# CLAIM-tmps1h34: Emotion communicates affect to the grantor

*Alternative D to [QUESTION-tmppt23x](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmppt23x.md), the owner's proposal of 2026-11-26. Alternatives A, B and C are [CLAIM-tmpy367s](CLAIM-tmpy367s.md), [CLAIM-tmp8ikja](CLAIM-tmp8ikja.md) and [CLAIM-tmp23xa5](CLAIM-tmp23xa5.md).*

## The claim

Three terms, kept apart:

- **Affect** is a perspective's state: concern-relative significance
  ([TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md)) and the action readiness it conditions ([TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md)'s state of
  preparation for action).
- **Valence** is the sign of that state, the perspective's own ([CLAIM-437](CLAIM-437.md)),
  and, at the level of the system, the sign along the potential the
  negotiation's grants climb ([TERM-076](../terms.d/TERM-076.md)).
- **Emotion** is the act: affect communicated as a claim to precedence to
  the processes that can grant it authority. Frijda's marks of control
  precedence ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)), persisting, resuming after interruption,
  capturing attention, are how the claim insists.

On this reading [CLAIM-283](CLAIM-283.md)'s gap is a communicative structure. The claim is
the message; the grant ([TERM-101](../terms.d/TERM-101.md)) is its uptake; and a claim can be heard
without being granted, which is [CLAIM-349](CLAIM-349.md)'s separation of access from
authority.

## What it buys

- **Misreport becomes possible.** A claim can communicate affect the
  perspective does not have, or more or less of it. That gives a place to
  Trivers's strategically simulated emotion ([THEORY-663](../theory.d/THEORY-663.md)) and to [CLAIM-431](CLAIM-431.md)'s
  objection, that a corporation's expressed remorse may not be remorse:
  the question becomes whether the communication is honest.
- **[CLAIM-439](CLAIM-439.md)'s report to governors is emotion at the system level.** The
  organization's reporting of what wins its conflicts to those who govern
  it is a claim addressed to a grantor, and the Wells Fargo board's
  "mis-felt valence" is a misreport in this sense.
- **Engineered cases fit directly.** A burn-rate page ([CLAIM-1262](CLAIM-1262.md)) is a
  communicated claim to precedence routed to whoever can grant it
  authority, the on-call responder.

## What it costs

- **[TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md) splits.** Frijda's action readiness is a state; this reading
  takes the state as affect and the claim it makes as emotion. Whether the
  readiness and the claim can come apart in Frijda's own account is for
  the reading [LEAD-tmptupsj](../leads.d/LEAD-tmptupsj.md).
- **[CLAIM-442](CLAIM-442.md) needs a choice.** It says "a perspective's valence is read on
  its claims". Here that must be epistemic: claims are evidence of a
  valence that is prior to them. Read constitutively, the reading would be
  circular, with claims communicating a valence the claims themselves
  constitute.
- **"Communicating" needs a thin sense.** It means a signal whose function
  is to obtain authority from processes able to grant it. It does not mean
  an intention to inform. Without that thin sense the claim imports
  intentionality the line has avoided; and if the sense is made too thin,
  the defeater applies.

## What it does not say

That every emotion is expressed to another agent; the addressee may be
internal. Nor that the communication is honest. Nor which of A to D the
record should adopt.
