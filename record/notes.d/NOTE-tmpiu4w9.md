# NOTE-tmpiu4w9: Kleiner & Ludwig — Mathematical structure of experience

## Contribution

Consciousness science has several promising ways of representing experience with mathematical spaces and structures (quality spaces, qualia spaces, phenomenal spaces), but no explicit definition of what a "mathematical structure of conscious experience" is. Kleiner and Ludwig propose one. It connects abstract mathematical entities with the concrete facts of experience, complements existing quality-space approaches, gives a general method for finding and studying experiential structures, and may unify approaches across fields. They hope it will ground a common formal language for studying consciousness.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- Introduction (p. 1): the tradition runs from 1860 psychophysics through quality spaces, Q-spaces, Φ-structures and phenomenal spaces, and it remains fragmented without a definition of the key term.
- §1 "The Status Quo" (pp. 2–5): the implicit condition (MDC) says a structure pertains to experience iff it describes it. Taken as sufficient, it faces Problem 1, incompatible structures (e.g. discrete vs other structures both qualify); Problem 2, arbitrary re-definitions (from one metric, uncountably many others); and Problem 3, indifference to consciousness.
- §2 (pp. 5–10): the definition (MSC) rests on *variations*, changes of one experience into another induced by stimuli, occurring naturally, or imagined in Husserl's sense. An S-aspect is changed by a variation iff the variation does not preserve S. The construction is axiomatic over a set E of experiences, their aspects and variations.
- §§3–5 (pp. 10–19): relative similarity qualifies. Metric structure does not, partly because the real numbers, built from equivalence classes of Cauchy sequences, correspond to no aspect of experience. Topology qualifies through phenomenal unity.
- §6–7 (pp. 19–21): automorphism reasoning shows (MSC) resolves the three problems. The definition is conceptually neutral (qualia, qualities, phenomenal distinctions) and methodologically neutral (introspective, laboratory and theory-based variations). There are prospects for the measurement of consciousness via the representational theory of measurement.

## Open questions

- It gives a principled criterion for when a formal model is *of* experience rather than a convenient description. That matters for IIT's Φ-structures ([LIT-tmprityc](../literature.d/LIT-tmprityc.md)) and for any claim that an ML representation space mirrors phenomenal structure.
- The rejection of metric structure is striking and bears on psychophysical quality-space work that assumes metrics. A deeper reading should check the second reason for rejecting metrics, which this skim did not reach.
