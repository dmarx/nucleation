# LIT-1856: 24.120 Moral Psychology, XIX: Identification

Holton, R. (2009). *24.120 Moral Psychology*, lecture handout XIX,
"Identification". MIT OpenCourseWare, Spring 2009.

## Key takeaways

- In "Identification and Wholeheartedness" identification is a decision,
  and is "acceptance, not ... endorsement" of the desire.
- The decision is unanalysed: Frankfurt "hasn't done it in terms of
  desires", so the Hobbesian model of the will is left behind.
- A decision need not make one wholehearted: intransitive pairwise
  decisions, or outlawing desires one by one, give no guarantee of a
  consistent remainder.
