# SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa: Answerability and contestability: answerability is proposed as a directed relation, owed on several grounds to a party with a claim and not to everyone affected, with forms and dimensions that come apart; authority over a decision is proposed not to carry authority over the conditions of its challenge; explanation, contestation and interruption, and the six achievements of an effective objection, are proposed as separable; and contestability is proposed as a maintained capability robust to opposition

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination line, on answerability and contestability: when an answer is owed and to whom, who may set the terms of challenge, and what an effective challenge needs. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and every component is new to any account. The held claims these relate to are CLAIM-624 (A's second-order duty of answerability; Proposed) and CLAIM-687 (A's rule that accountability rises with indispensability; Proposed). The account cites them and does not integrate them. The part's sibling, on remedies foreclosed before review, is SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm.*

## The account

**The question.** QUESTION-tmpea19q (the owner's, U36, verbatim; Open;
lines dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity) asks when
answerability is required of an authority or governing capability, and
keeps three parts apart: when, to whom, and why. Its body says CLAIM-624,
CLAIM-687 and TERM-124 hold pieces and "None asks when any actor owes an
answer at all", names CLAIM-tmpnhesz and TERM-tmp4zjar as A83's answer and
CLAIM-tmpcpijj as its principle, and carries A83's closing problem as a
sub-question: the minimum conditions under which a claim of answerability
stays effective without giving the claimant unlimited authority. It calls
the second side of that problem "the limit CLAIM-tmpyqfrf files", and
leaves the why open.

**The term.** TERM-tmp4zjar (A's, A83 §1 and A106 §17; Proposed):
answerability, of an actor to a party concerning an action or governing
power under a declared standard, is the relation in which the actor owes
the party some form of answer (an account, reasons, responsiveness, an
effective opportunity to contest, or judgment and redress), in one of four
dimensions (normative, institutional, effective, maintained), and not the
accountability mechanisms that realize it. Its body says it gives one sense
to the word as CLAIM-624, TERM-124 and CLAIM-462 use it; that "when it is
owed, and to whom, is CLAIM-tmpnhesz"; that CLAIM-tmpyqfrf bounds it; that
A74's "dynamic answerability" (CLAIM-tmpx1g4r) is its effective and
maintained dimensions without the normative one; and that protected
contestability (TERM-tmpsit04) is "one form of answer, contestation, made a
maintained capability".

**When an answer is owed, and to whom.** CLAIM-tmpnhesz (A's, A83 §§3–5
and §10, A90 IV; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
dependence-and-domination and organizational-normativity) answers
QUESTION-tmpea19q, uses TERM-tmp4zjar and TERM-083, and complements
CLAIM-687, CLAIM-624, CLAIM-tmpcpijj and CLAIM-tmpyqfrf: answerability is
owed to a party with a claim concerning the matter, on one of several
grounds that need not presuppose the actor's legitimate authority
(interference with a protected interest, entrusted authority, collective
rule, consequential dependence, control over recourse); the party owed, the
recipient of reasons, the forum and the remedier can differ; and what is
owed is indexed to the claim, not to how much the party is affected. Its
body calls CLAIM-687 the dependence ground "with an intensity", CLAIM-624
downstream of it, CLAIM-tmpcpijj what the fifth ground "generates", and
says its fourth part "depends on" CLAIM-tmpyqfrf. It leaves open "why
anyone is owed reasons at all".

**The limit.** CLAIM-tmpyqfrf (A's, A106 §10 and §17; counter,
interpretive, Proposed) objects to CLAIM-687: influence over others does
not by itself ground a duty to answer to them, since a legitimate right can
assign a decision to its holder. Its body says CLAIM-687 survives if read
as stated, about arrangements others depend on, and "the counter then marks
where its slope starts"; that against CLAIM-624 it "has less purchase",
since a protected sphere has no standard to answer to; and that it and
CLAIM-tmpnhesz's limit "are the same point in different settings".

**Who sets the terms of challenge.** CLAIM-tmpcpijj (A's, A83 §3E, §7 and
close; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements CLAIM-624, CLAIM-619,
CLAIM-tmpnhesz and CLAIM-tmpbqunx, bears on QUESTION-tmpcz1ui,
QUESTION-tmphoumu and QUESTION-441, and uses TERM-124: authority over a
decision does not carry authority over the cost, timing, evidence, forum or
remedy of its challenge, and where an actor's authority is meant to be
constrained by independently justified claims, its own judgment that it has
met them cannot always establish that it has. Its body says CLAIM-624's
duty "presupposes that someone may set the terms of contest" and this
claim says "the decider is not that someone by default"; that CLAIM-619 is
"one way of exercising the second authority without justification"; and
that how independent the evaluator must be is QUESTION-tmphoumu.
QUESTION-tmphoumu (A's, A79 §3, with A81 §5's authority-provenance graph;
Open; lines dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency) asks when the
allocation of authority to evaluate and constrain an actor is sufficiently
independent of that actor's capacity to influence it. Its body says the
record holds the mechanisms (CLAIM-277, CLAIM-282, CLAIM-330, CLAIM-406,
CLAIM-359) "and not the criterion", and calls itself "the institutional
side of CLAIM-tmpcpijj".

**Three entitlements.** CLAIM-tmpohafo (A's, A23 §6 and A19 §7, developing
the owner's U11; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-523 and
CLAIM-624: explanation, contestation and interruption are separate
entitlements of an affected party, and each without the next fails in its
own way. Its body says CLAIM-523's achievements are the organization's and
these the affected party's; that its split between giving an account and
answering "is what" CLAIM-624's duty "requires of contestation"; and that
interruption is the owner's U11 (CLAIM-tmp11xvc, in SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm) "in the form of an entitlement". It names
TERM-tmpsit04, TERM-tmp4zjar and CLAIM-tmpnhesz as from the same exchange.

**Six achievements.** CLAIM-tmpi68s2 (A's, A79 §10, A81 §2 and §9; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; lines dependence-and-domination and
will-organization) rests on CLAIM-523, complements CLAIM-403, CLAIM-632 and
CLAIM-tmpbqunx, uses TERM-092 and TERM-085, and bears on
QUESTION-tmpea19q: an objection is effective only if it becomes
formulable, legible, taken up by an authorized channel, sustainable by the
objector, corrected and maintained, so its chance is no greater than its
weakest stage, and a reform that raises one stage can lower the whole (A81
§9's numbers, checked). Its body says it extends CLAIM-523's three
achievements upstream and downstream, that its bound is CLAIM-632's upper
bound, that A's "Adversarial control over contestability" is TERM-124
restricted to contest, measured by CLAIM-tmpbqunx, and that the
institutional property built on it is TERM-tmpsit04.

**Protected contestability.** TERM-tmpsit04 (A's, A81 §11 and §14;
Proposed) complements TERM-085: the maintained, resource-bounded capability
of those with standing to formulate and present objections and obtain an
appropriately consequential assessment of them, robust to a declared class
of opposition; not a guarantee of success, a veto or costless
adjudication. Its body defines it as a viability kernel over an adequacy
region built from CLAIM-tmpbqunx's robust capability, and says it is not
correctability, not CLAIM-tmpi68s2's objection at one time, and not
TERM-tmp4zjar's relation of owing an answer.

## Not stated on the record

- Whether interruption, in CLAIM-tmpohafo's sense, is a form of answer
  under TERM-tmp4zjar. The term's five forms do not include suspending
  execution, and CLAIM-tmpohafo cites the term only as from the same
  exchange.
- How CLAIM-tmpohafo's three entitlements map onto CLAIM-tmpi68s2's six
  achievements. Neither cites the other.
- Whether an arrangement can show it has TERM-tmpsit04's protected
  contestability by its own assessment, or by a class of opposition it
  declares itself. TERM-tmpsit04 does not cite CLAIM-tmpcpijj or
  QUESTION-tmphoumu.
- Why anyone is owed an answer at all: QUESTION-tmpea19q and CLAIM-tmpnhesz
  leave it open.
- Whether CLAIM-tmpnhesz's grounds settle CLAIM-687's scope as
  CLAIM-tmpyqfrf reads it. CLAIM-tmpnhesz calls CLAIM-687 the dependence
  ground with an intensity, and CLAIM-tmpyqfrf says where its slope starts;
  neither says whether the grounds other than dependence also scale.

## Arm B

- **QUESTION-tmpea19q's answers and bearings** (CLAIM-tmpnhesz,
  CLAIM-tmpi68s2, with TERM-tmp4zjar and CLAIM-tmpyqfrf). The sentence
  tried: an answer is owed when one of CLAIM-tmpnhesz's grounds holds, to
  the claim-holder, in the forms and dimensions of TERM-tmp4zjar, and not
  within a protected sphere. Route: a derivation. Verdict: already stated,
  in CLAIM-tmpnhesz's body (its fourth part "depends on" CLAIM-tmpyqfrf)
  and in QUESTION-tmpea19q's. The question's why stays open. Nothing filed.
- **Protected contestability is not self-certifying** (TERM-tmpsit04 with
  CLAIM-tmpcpijj and QUESTION-tmphoumu). The sentence tried: an arrangement
  whose challenged actor declares the class of opposition, or assesses its
  own adequacy, cannot thereby establish protected contestability. Route: a
  derivation. Verdict: it fails as trivial. It instantiates
  CLAIM-tmpcpijj's second half for one property, and CLAIM-tmpbqunx's body
  already says the permitted class is a normative question. The missing
  cross-reference is listed above.
- **Interruption is the stage at which a late contest fails**
  (CLAIM-tmpohafo with CLAIM-tmpi68s2). The sentence tried: contestation
  without interruption fails at CLAIM-tmpi68s2's correction stage. Route: a
  discriminating prediction. Verdict: it fails. It relabels
  CLAIM-tmpohafo's "a right that arrives too late" in CLAIM-tmpi68s2's
  stages and predicts nothing either leaves open; which stage a late
  contest fails at is a free choice of mapping.

No candidate passes ADR-075's test.
