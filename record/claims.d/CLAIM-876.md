# CLAIM-876: The organization whose reproduction of occluding representations and allocations of authority breaches the second-order duty of answerability is a relational arrangement, the ground and object of the verdict and not its bearer; the duty falls on the agents or roles that control that reproduction, inside the arrangement or outside it, each as far as its control reaches, so that where no one controls it the duty is breached by no one

## The problem

[CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s title has "an organization" commit a wrong "by reproducing
representations and allocations of authority", and calls the wrong "a
breach of a second-order duty of answerability". Its fifth condition makes
the verdict depend on "the organization's obligations, capabilities, and
reasonable alternatives". Its section "What it does not say" denies "That
the organization is an agent". And it declares no sense of [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md). The
curation entry of 2026-11-15 (044148) found the gap: whether the duty
"falls on an arrangement's controllers is not on the record", and the
integration of [CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md) with [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) waits on it. The owner's review of
[#488](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/488) asked that the ground of an obligation be kept apart from its bearer
([QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)). [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) uses one word for both.

## Which sense of "organization"

Of [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s four senses, two can be set aside on the claim's own text;
the choice between the other two is this claim's and its rival's.

- **Not sense 2, organizational maintenance.** Sense 2 is the activity
  that reproduces an arrangement. In [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) that activity is named by
  the verb, "reproducing"; the organization is what does it, or what it is
  done to. Sense 2 is the act the verdict is about, not its subject.
- **Not sense 4, organizational closure.** [CLAIM-614](CLAIM-614.md), on which [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)
  rests, has a "self-maintaining" bearer whose maintenance regenerates its
  own constraints ([TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)). [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md) drops "self-maintaining" from its
  title and, under "What it does not say", places external maintainers as
  [CLAIM-522](CLAIM-522.md)'s outside controller, "whose maintenance provenance ([TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md))
  lies outside the declared boundary". A reproduction maintained from
  outside is covered, so closure is not required.
- **Sense 1, the relational arrangement (this claim).** The arrangement of
  representations and allocations of authority (who records what, whose
  report counts, who can stop or revise) is what is reproduced. This is
  [TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)'s default, the sense the owner's standing concern assigns where
  none is declared (curation entry of 2026-11-15), and the sense of the
  owner's correction on [CLAIM-614](CLAIM-614.md) that "a 'system' is just a kind of
  'organization'". It keeps [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s own disclaimer: a sense-1
  arrangement need not be an agent.
- **Sense 3, the collective institution**, is [CLAIM-877](CLAIM-877.md).

On sense 1, an institution in sense 3 is one kind of arrangement and,
where it is an agent, one possible controller; it is not excluded.

## Why the bearer is the controllers

1. **The arrangement cannot be the bearer.** A sense-1 arrangement "can
   contain conflicting processes, have no controller, and be maintained
   from outside", and is a candidate for an agent "only under a stated
   criterion of agency" ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md); [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md), Active). A duty whose
   breach turns on "obligations, capabilities, and reasonable
   alternatives" needs a bearer that can take an alternative. So on sense
   1 the arrangement is the ground and object of the verdict, and the
   bearer is a further relatum, as [TERM-123](../terms.d/TERM-123.md) says of exploitation ("Not the
   bearer of the remedy") and [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md) of domination (it "names a likely
   bearer, the holder").
2. **What the duty asks is in the controllers' hands.** The duty is "to
   maintain feasible means for relevant violations to become knowable,
   contestable and correctable". Changing a representation or an
   allocation of authority is [TERM-129](../terms.d/TERM-129.md)'s governance, "who can set and
   revise the relations fixing the other three". The parties who can
   discharge the duty are those who hold that capacity, used or not:
   [TERM-124](../terms.d/TERM-124.md)'s holder. [CLAIM-727](CLAIM-727.md) (Active) says why their authority over the
   decisions does not cover them here: authority over a decision "does not
   carry authority over the conditions on which it may be challenged", and
   control of those conditions "needs its own justification".
3. **The derivation the record has puts the bearer there.** [ARG-114](../arguments.d/ARG-114.md)
   derives [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty for an actor that owes a duty, needs a
   capability to honour it, is "ResponsibleFor" that capability, and can
   maintain it at justified cost. Its condition C is control: "a governing
   body with authority over staffing, reporting requirements, funding, and
   review procedures is differently situated", and where authority is
   fragmented "the duty may be distributed across several offices or
   organizations". This claim states C as the bearer of [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty.
4. **Condition 5 grades the bearers by control.** Who else could bear it?
   [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)'s candidates are those who "maintain, control, or benefit
   from the arrangement". A maintainer who carries out the reproduction
   without the capacity to change it (the clerk who files the coarse
   report) has, for that reproduction, no reasonable alternative, and
   condition 5 excuses the omission; to the extent the maintainer has some
   control, the maintainer is a controller to that extent. A beneficiary
   who controls nothing cannot maintain the means the duty names, though it
   may owe a remedy for the harm, which is another duty ([TERM-123](../terms.d/TERM-123.md)). So the
   duty falls on control, and on maintenance or benefit only through the
   control they carry. This is the sense of "each as far as its control
   reaches".
5. **The record's other bearers are the same.** [CLAIM-687](CLAIM-687.md) (Active) puts
   accountability on "those who control an arrangement", with the
   arrangement in sense 1; [CLAIM-682](CLAIM-682.md) is "a test for domination by an
   arrangement's controllers"; [SYNTHESIS-093](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-093.md) and [SYNTHESIS-092](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-092.md) map every
   bearer the line names as an agent or a role. With this claim [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s
   duty and [CLAIM-687](CLAIM-687.md)'s accountability fall on one set of parties, and
   [CLAIM-687](CLAIM-687.md)'s rule reads as how strongly [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty binds them as
   dependence rises.

## Consequences

- **No controller, no breach.** Where the occluding reproduction is
  impersonal, held by no one ([ARG-109](../arguments.d/ARG-109.md)'s Han and Benjamin, [CLAIM-682](CLAIM-682.md)'s
  "impersonal constraint that no one holds"), the arrangement can still be
  judged occluding and self-immunizing in [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s descriptive layers,
  and the duty is breached by no one. That is the price of the reading,
  and it is the one [CLAIM-682](CLAIM-682.md) already pays.
- **Outside controllers bear it.** A regulator, owner or parent body that
  resets the arrangement from outside the declared boundary ([CLAIM-522](CLAIM-522.md))
  is a bearer though not a member. Membership does not fix the bearer,
  which is [CASE-256](../cases.d/CASE-256.md)'s second and third results rather than its first.
- **The wrong is not the arrangement's "own" as an agent's.** [QUESTION-441](../questions.d/QUESTION-441.md)
  asks when the counter-normativity is the organization's own "rather than
  its participants'". On this reading it is the arrangement's as its
  ground and the controllers' as its bearers, and neither is every
  participant's.
- **What it does not settle.** Whether a whole that is not an agent can
  bear any obligation at all is [QUESTION-460](../questions.d/QUESTION-460.md)'s prior part, which the owner
  left open. This claim does not need it: it says where [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty
  falls, and is defeated only if the record must count the duty breached
  with no controller.

## The waiting integration, tested

The curation entry of 2026-11-15 tested "An arrangement that erodes the
capacities that govern it breaches [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty of answerability" and
found it failing on a free parameter, the bearer. With this claim it reads:
where the controllers of an arrangement let it erode, by doing the work
through which they are exercised ([CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md)), capacities that those it
governs need for relevant violations to become knowable, contestable or
correctable, and maintaining those capacities was feasible at justified
cost, the controllers breach [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty.

[ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s admission test asks whether it can fail while every component
holds. As first worded it can: [CLAIM-673](CLAIM-673.md)'s "capacities a collective needs
to govern an arrangement" include capacities no violation of the standard
turns on (to redesign it for efficiency, say), whose erosion is no breach
of a duty about violations. Narrowed to the capacities the standard's
violations need, as above, it passes, and adds something no component
states: the duty acquires a deadline. Under [CLAIM-605](CLAIM-605.md)'s model the erosion
is past recovery after a point, so the breach can be complete, and
irremediable by withdrawal, before any violation is recorded, which is the
temporal point A110 §6 drew for [ARG-114](../arguments.d/ARG-114.md) and [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md) builds. Whether to
file the narrowed integration is for the synthesis review of the line or
the owner; it is not filed here. On [CLAIM-877](CLAIM-877.md)'s reading it holds only
where an agent-institution governs the arrangement.

## Against the rival reading

[CLAIM-877](CLAIM-877.md) reads "organization" as an institution that is an agent and
makes it the bearer. It fits A's wording of condition 5 best, and Fineman's
reading ([THEORY-1818](../theory.d/THEORY-1818.md)) is of that kind. It costs the claim the cases the
owner's abstract sense was meant to keep (an arrangement several
institutions control, [QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md); a platform and its dependents with no
one collective agent), and it needs a criterion of agency the record has
not adopted ([TERM-063](../terms.d/TERM-063.md) is Proposed). This claim keeps those cases and needs
no criterion, at the price above. The owner chooses; a revision of
[CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s wording or a declared sense in it waits on that.
