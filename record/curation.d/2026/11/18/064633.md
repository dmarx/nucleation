The line rightful-power ([ADR-103](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-103.md)), the work for the owner's manuscript
([ADR-104](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-104.md)), the line audit after [ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md) and the follow-up synthesis
review left six calls to the owner. On 2026-11-18 the lead proposed a
resolution for each, and the owner approved them all ("i approve your
suggestions"). This entry applies them.

## The decisions

1. **[QUESTION-484](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) refines [QUESTION-483](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-483.md): kept.** [QUESTION-484](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) asks about one
   input to reasonable rejection, feasibility, and [QUESTION-483](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-483.md) already
   calls it the narrower question. The sentence in [QUESTION-484](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) that said it
   "sits beside" [QUESTION-483](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-483.md), which contradicted the refinement, is
   reworded (v3).
2. **[QUESTION-481](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-481.md) refines [QUESTION-486](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-486.md): written.** Its boundary clause, which
   protections survive a legitimate reconstitution (A85 §8's limit on a
   reforming majority, A90 XII's "bounded constituent authority"), asks
   [QUESTION-486](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-486.md)'s question where it binds hardest; [CLAIM-720](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) already bears on
   both. It carries rightful-power after its own lines under [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md)
   (v2), with the reason in its body.
3. **[CASE-282](../../record/cases.d/CASE-282.md) stays out of the line.** Its tie to [QUESTION-485](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-485.md)'s candidate E
   is the recycler's ownership, a shared topic; the case was built for
   feasibility and bears on [QUESTION-484](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-484.md). Nothing changes.
4. **The work's judgement calls.** [CLAIM-736](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-736.md) stays a member: §14.5
   states its proposition. [CLAIM-720](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) leaves the work (v5): §4 uses only its
   proviso and never states that inalienability limits normative powers,
   and the work's curation entry already counted it as holding §4 in part.
   [ADR-104](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-104.md) (v2) now counts 27 members, 6 in rightful-power; its rule is
   unchanged.
5. **`complements` gives no line; [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md) is unchanged.** The two
   dependencies [ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md)'s split severed were checked one at a time:
   - [CLAIM-707](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-707.md) onto [CLAIM-678](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-678.md): moot, since [CLAIM-707](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-707.md) already carries
     dependence-and-domination.
   - [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) onto [CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md): [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) argues no organizational-normativity
     question ([CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s are QUESTION-441, 129 and 041), so it does not
     take that line. Its note of 2026-11-17 does argue the root question
     [QUESTION-480](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-480.md), relaying A83's answer to the owner's U36, which the line's
     reviewer missed. `bears_on: QUESTION-480` is written with a reason,
     and [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) carries rightful-power after its own line (v4).
6. **Placements.** [QUESTION-473](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-473.md) moves from dependence-and-domination's
   [SYNTHESIS-099](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-099.md) to its own line's part on accountability without blame,
   [SYNTHESIS-104](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-104.md). [CLAIM-701](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-701.md) stays in [SYNTHESIS-098](../../record/syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-098.md), which covers its
   distributed-agency line and its subject, the fait accompli.
