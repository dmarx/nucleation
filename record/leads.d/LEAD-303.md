# LEAD-303: Read the social-choice accounts of domain decisiveness and of top cycles against the record's indecision

## What it would do

Read, by the read-and-file procedure, the following works.

- **Sen, "The Impossibility of a Paretian Liberal"** (1970) is already
  held: [LIT-285](../literature.d/LIT-285.md), read in full in [NOTE-255](../notes.d/NOTE-255.md), with [THEORY-027](../theory.d/THEORY-027.md). Its question
  here is answered from [NOTE-255](../notes.d/NOTE-255.md) in [CLAIM-1459](../claims.d/CLAIM-1459.md)'s note of 2026-11-26.
  Sen's cycle needs Pareto edges, and the potential account has none, so
  [CLAIM-1459](../claims.d/CLAIM-1459.md) uses decisiveness alone, which is the weaker condition.
- **Top-cycle solution concepts.** Good's note on Condorcet sets (1971);
  Smith on aggregation with a variable electorate (1973), the source of
  the Smith set; Schwartz, "Rationality and the Myth of the Maximum"
  (1972), and *The Logic of Collective Choice* (1986), for the Schwartz
  set. For a survey, Laslier, *Tournament Solutions and Majority Voting*
  (1997). Registered 2026-11-26, Deferred: [LIT-3267](../literature.d/LIT-3267.md) (Good),
  [LIT-3298](../literature.d/LIT-3298.md) (Smith), [LIT-3276](../literature.d/LIT-3276.md) (Schwartz 1972), [LIT-3318](../literature.d/LIT-3318.md)
  (Schwartz 1986) and [LIT-3297](../literature.d/LIT-3297.md) (Laslier). These are the prior art for [CLAIM-1366](../claims.d/CLAIM-1366.md)'s terminal
  component.
- **What the literature calls the dynamics.** Whether social choice has a
  name and results for one-move-at-a-time dynamics on a majority relation,
  and for how likely each terminal set is from a given starting
  distribution. That is the measure [CLAIM-1421](../claims.d/CLAIM-1421.md) and [CLAIM-1353](../claims.d/CLAIM-1353.md)
  share.

## What it would settle

- Whether [CLAIM-1459](../claims.d/CLAIM-1459.md) adds anything beyond Sen's reading for
  organizational valence, or is Sen restated.
- Whether [CLAIM-1366](../claims.d/CLAIM-1366.md)'s demonstrated part should cite the Smith and
  Schwartz sets rather than restate them. The Schwartz set is defined for
  relations that need not be complete, which is the sparse case the
  record's relation of winning moves is.
- Whether the literature already distinguishes a cycle the dynamics passes
  through from one it never leaves, and whether it has a measure of how
  likely the latter is.

Each work is one reading. Delegating the reading waits for the owner.
