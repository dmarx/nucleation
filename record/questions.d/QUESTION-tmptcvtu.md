# QUESTION-tmptcvtu: Should the hyperarticle atlas be filed as an account of the record's argument, or as a manuscript composed of manuscripts

The atlas (ChatGPT's A65 in "Hyper article interpretation", kept verbatim in
scratch/atlas/atlas-v0.3.md) divides the record into fifteen scopes, each
with a microabstract, an outline and a "core fiber" of entries. The record
has two kinds of entry an object like that could be: an account
(SYNTHESIS), which maps a scope of the argument and adds nothing
([ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)), and a work, a manuscript in the `works` vocabulary whose members
are found by reading its text ([ADR-104](../decisions.d/ADR-104.md)).

The owner's words: "the atlas should probably be filed as a synthesis? or
maybe a manuscript comprised of manuscripts?"

Answered when the owner adopts one of [CLAIM-tmpcd1e0](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpcd1e0.md) and [CLAIM-tmpqhazs](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqhazs.md), or both in sequence, or
rules the atlas something else.
