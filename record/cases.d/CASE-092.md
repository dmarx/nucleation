# CASE-092: A minimal coupled meaning–influence model: two hypotheses, two retrieval specialists, a fixed balanced corpus, queries set by the dominant interpretation and endorsement updated from apparent retrieval success

## The case

Two hypotheses with comparable evidence in a fixed corpus; two operators,
one better at finding evidence bearing on each; queries conditioned on the
currently dominant hypothesis; endorsement updated from apparent retrieval
success, with an adjustable memory timescale.

Regime A scores operators against the current dominant interpretation.
Regime B scores them by independently assessed relevance and role
([CLAIM-281](../claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)). Controls (A23 XI.B): initial framing asymmetry, coupling
strength, exploration rate, endorsement timescale, independence of
evaluation, role specificity.

Measures (A22 §7): meaning concentration, influence concentration,
evidential fidelity, hysteresis. Analysis targets: fixed points, stability
of the symmetric state, multiple attractors, transitions between adaptive
and captured regimes.

## What each outcome would show

A23 XI lists five candidate predictions, among them amplification of small
framing asymmetries relative to a fixed-influence model, and influence
concentrating while independently scored fidelity stays flat. Its
failure criteria: "If the second influence field yields no distinctive
predictions beyond a standard adaptive-retrieval process, the additional
organizational structure is not explanatory."

## Main threat

[CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md): the operators' objectives and the score of successful inquiry
are the modeller's, so the model cannot show self-constitution, only
dynamics. A23 XIV.F names the objection. Ablations against fixed weights
and against a matched single policy are what separate the coupling from
ordinary adaptive retrieval.

## Note of 2026-10-16: the linearization, and the analyses proposed for it

A14 of the exchange of 2026-10-16 on sports fandom (workbench entry of
2026-10-16) read this case correctly and linearized it at the symmetric
state. The checked result is [CASE-099](CASE-099.md) and [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md): with each
field stable alone, only the two-way loop can destabilize symmetry, at
βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ), and the growing direction mixes meaning and
authority. Regime B, which sets γ near zero, removes that instability.

A14 §9 proposed six analyses beside the four measures above: the
eigenmodes of the joint dynamics against ablations of each arrow;
effective information of macrovariables spanning both fields, against
either field alone and a matched full-state model ([THEORY-222](../theory.d/THEORY-222.md)); predictive
closure ([THEORY-257](../theory.d/THEORY-257.md)); persistence under periodic replacement of operators;
role-indexed against scalar endorsement ([CLAIM-281](../claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)); and ablation against
simpler models ([CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md)). A15 named the simpler models: reflected
appraisal (DeGroot–Friedkin), adaptive networks and persuasion bias, as
the baseline before any claim of novelty ([QUESTION-117](../questions.d/QUESTION-117.md)). They are
recorded here as proposals; the case is unchanged.
