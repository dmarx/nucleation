- **U12: "please".** A12 did all three:
  - **[MOM-CLM-132](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-132.md) read**, after the Mathematics of Meaning repository was
    added to the session. It holds that the mechanism is correct and the
    conclusion about selfhood empty, that "Depth is an arms race, not a
    defence", that the genuine defences are structural, and that
    "adaptivity for manipulability" is the theorem left unstated.
  - **Five works registered**: Thomas (2004), and Lefebvre's 1977 book,
    *Algebra of Conscience*, his 1987 paper and his 2009 chapter. Two
    readers are on them (curation entry to follow).
  - **[CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md), granted.** Evidence a loop did not select can have
    been selected by someone who models it, so the channel that anchors a
    loop is the one through which it can be controlled. In [CASE-106](../../record/cases.d/CASE-106.md)'s
    linearization, an adversary tracking the target's lean with gain ρ
    adds ρ to κ′: γ = 4ηs(κ′ + ρ − κ̄), checked with sympy. A constant push
    b gives a steady drive 4ηs·b on authority, which steers without
    destabilizing. [CLAIM-342](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s condition was strengthened to match (version
    2): independence of selection by anyone. [QUESTION-121](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-121.md)'s candidates 1 and
    5 carry the caveat.
