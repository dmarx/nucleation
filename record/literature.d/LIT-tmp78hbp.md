# LIT-tmp78hbp: FDA Approval of the Congenital OTOF Gene Therapy Otarmeni Reshapes the Future of Hearing Loss Treatment

Ellen Reisinger and Christopher R. Cederroth (2026). "FDA Approval of the
Congenital OTOF Gene Therapy Otarmeni Reshapes the Future of Hearing Loss
Treatment" *Journal of the Association for Research in Otolaryngology 27(4):
491-493, editorial*. DOI-10.1007/s10162-026-01053-z.

## Key takeaways

A time-sensitive gene therapy changes the clinical sequence: diagnose
genetically before implanting.
