# THEORY-2254: Snapshots don't determine a temporal narrative

## Source

Bumpus, Fairbanks, Karvonen, Leal and Simard (2026; arXiv 2024),
[LIT-2238](../literature.d/LIT-2238.md), skimmed in [NOTE-2345](../notes.d/NOTE-2345.md).

## What was actually shown

Definitions; a proposition on what determines a discrete narrative; an
adjunction proved for value categories with limits and colimits;
temporal-graph examples.

## What this does not say

Which narratives are records of some dynamical system, or how to tell.
