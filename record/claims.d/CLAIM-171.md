# CLAIM-171: A stored text means only what its readings make of it: the writer's projected receiver is one more reading, so an unread text has possible meanings and no fixed one

## The claim

The writer's intention is not in the stored bits. Every attribution of an
intended meaning is itself made by some reader, the writer included, at some
later time, from the text and whatever context survives. So "the meaning
fixed by the write" is a reading privileged by convention. An unread text
supports a range of meanings, which [CLAIM-111](CLAIM-111.md) already models as a
distribution over communicative situations. It has no one meaning until it
is read, and then it has as many as it has readings.

The joke over a life ([CASE-048](../cases.d/CASE-048.md)) is this objection's best case. The
adult who finally gets the double entendre does not experience recovering a
fixed meaning. They experience the scene becoming a different joke.

## Why it is still open

The reply in [CLAIM-160](CLAIM-160.md) separates intended meaning from what a reader
can reconstruct, and lets the second vary. It holds against the joke only
if the writer's frame is a fact independent of every reading. The film's
dual address is evidence that it is, since the adult layer was written to be
missed. But the line's own account of identity runs through probes and
receivers ([CLAIM-140](CLAIM-140.md), [QUESTION-031](../questions.d/QUESTION-031.md)). Nothing filed says how a projected
receiver is identified other than by a reading. No reader-response or
reception theory is held, so the objection is stated here from the
record's materials only.

## Note of 2026-10-12: a non-reading identification of the receiver

This claim says "Nothing filed says how a projected receiver is identified
other than by a reading." PR [#121](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/121)'s round of 2026-10-10 restated [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md)
at version 2, where the constituents are relations to participant roles,
[TERM-040](../terms.d/TERM-040.md)'s positions, so that a new audience in the same role keeps them.
On version 2 the constituent is fixed by a role, not by any audience's
reading. That is one filed candidate for identifying a projected receiver
otherwise than by a reading; whether it identifies the writer's projected
receiver, or only the addressee position, no entry argues. [CLAIM-226](CLAIM-226.md)
bears in this claim's direction on the measurement side: "for historical
sources there is no source audience at all". [QUESTION-073](../questions.d/QUESTION-073.md) holds the
reception-side readings this claim says the record lacks, Austin on uptake
and Goffman on keying, both unread.

## Note of 2026-10-30: what holds it back

Unanswered: whether a writer's projected receiver can be identified
other than by a reading. Two filed candidates say it can, and neither is
held: [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md) version 2's role-based constituents, which no entry argues
fix the projected receiver rather than the addressee position
([QUESTION-031](../questions.d/QUESTION-031.md)); and Grice's analysis ([THEORY-444](../theory.d/THEORY-444.md), read in full in
[NOTE-735](../notes.d/NOTE-735.md)), on which what an utterer means is fixed by the utterer's
intention toward an audience and read by public criteria, so that being
identified through a reading would not make it constituted by one. The
reception-side readings this objection needs ([QUESTION-073](../questions.d/QUESTION-073.md): Austin on
uptake, Goffman on keying) are unread.

## Note of 2026-10-31: held readings on this side, and what they cost

<!-- inactive-ok-file: THEORY-1278 THEORY-1276 THEORY-1285 — Proposed; readings filed on 2026-10-31 that take a systemic form of this claim's position -->

This claim says "No reader-response or reception theory is held". Since
2026-10-31 the record holds three readings, all Proposed, that take a
systemic form of its position.

- **Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy** ([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), [THEORY-1278](../theory.d/THEORY-1278.md)):
  "action contributes to informing a system that, retrospectively, gives
  that action its meaning" (p. 1357). And: "What communication (and
  information) means is not so much what people think about it, but rather
  how it affects them" (p. 1358).
- **Bencherki and Iliadis** ([LIT-1922](../literature.d/LIT-1922.md), [THEORY-1276](../theory.d/THEORY-1276.md)): the meaning
  of an action "corresponds to its participation in the resolution of a
  disparity of ordering" (proposition 3).
- **Bencherki 2017** ([LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md), [THEORY-1285](../theory.d/THEORY-1285.md)): "Concurrent
  significations, then, are not mere misunderstandings, but rather the
  outcomes of simultaneous individuation processes" (p. 783).

They go further than this claim. It says an unread text has possible
meanings and takes on one with each reading. They say meaning is the
effect on the system that takes an action up. They also set the writer's
intention aside as a matter of method: research "grounded in people's
understandings is unable to attune to the processual nature of the world"
([LIT-1902](../literature.d/LIT-1902.md), p. 1358). So they give [CLAIM-160](CLAIM-160.md)'s intended meaning no slot
at all, which is stronger than this claim's "a reading privileged by
convention".

They do not answer what holds this claim open, whether a projected
receiver can be identified other than by a reading. They do not ask it.
They also show what the position costs at full strength. If meaning is
the reordering an action produces, it is known only after the reordering
([THEORY-1276](../theory.d/THEORY-1276.md), "What this does not say"). Every reordering qualifies,
so nothing counts as failing to mean. Bencherki and Iliadis's evidence
that an ordering propagated is that the event took place ([NOTE-1483](../notes.d/NOTE-1483.md),
Limitations). The systemic view cannot draw [CLAIM-160](CLAIM-160.md)'s reply to [CASE-048](../cases.d/CASE-048.md),
that the child's reading is the layer addressed to the child. On it, the
child's and the adult's readings are two concurrent significations, with
nothing to say which was addressed to whom.

The readings show that the position is held in organization studies.
They are conceptual and are not evidence for it. The open point is
unchanged.

No status, title or warrant changes.
