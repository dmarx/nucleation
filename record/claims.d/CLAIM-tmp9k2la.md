# CLAIM-tmp9k2la: Manufactured doubt can work on the threshold of evidence for action rather than on belief: an organization of interpretation in which residual uncertainty outweighs accumulated evidence defers action while the evidence is still recognized, and can outlast the campaign that seeded it

## The claim

A6 §4: "A conventional account emphasizes the production and circulation
of misleading information. An organizational account adds another
explanatory possibility: the production of a perspective within which
uncertainty acquires a particular practical significance." The
transformation that matters "may not be", in its displayed formulas, true
belief → false belief, "but rather" adequate evidence → evidence deemed
insufficient for action. "The distinction matters because the second
transformation can occur without eliminating recognition of the evidence
itself." And: "Memes can serve as
nucleation sites for such an organization. ... The original campaign
could disappear while aspects of the perspectival character it helped
establish survive."

A6's figure contrasts "Uncertainty evaluated against risks of delay"
with "Residual uncertainty privileged over evidence", and marks itself
"Illustrative ... not a reconstruction of the tobacco industry's specific
procedures."

## Where the record stands

[CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md) separates legibility (the discrepancy becomes recognizable) from
volitional efficacy (a demand can move action). The claim places
manufactured doubt's effect on the second with the first intact. It is
an application of the memetic account ([CLAIM-tmpjlxfy](CLAIM-tmpjlxfy.md)) and of the seed
figure ([CLAIM-tmpgvsgg](CLAIM-tmpgvsgg.md)) to a documented kind of campaign.
