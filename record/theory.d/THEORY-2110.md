# THEORY-2110: Killen argues against Tomasello that cooperation and interdependence are a basis for morality but not sufficient for it, since from early childhood independent judgments of fairness coexist with group-minded cooperation and let children oppose group norms that treat others unequally, and peer conflict, not collaboration alone, drives moral change

## The account

Cooperation can serve unjust ends, so it is not morality. Interdependence
develops alongside autonomous thinking from infancy, and objective
morality appears when children challenge groups that treat others
unequally, not when they conform. Change comes from resolving peer
conflicts, as Piaget held, with ages earlier than his.

## Evidence

Review of developmental studies, mostly the author's, set against
Tomasello's book.

others unequally, and "authority and institutional practices" is A's

morally right, as developmental evidence that the capacity to oppose a
cooperating group's norm is part of morality from early on. That claim
