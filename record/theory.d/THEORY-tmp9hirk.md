# THEORY-tmp9hirk: Tomasello proposes that great apes add a metacognitive tier of control to the executive tier common to mammals, monitoring their own executive decisions and uncertainty, and that they understand others as agents by attributing to them the goals, perceptions and knowledge they monitor in themselves

## The account

Mammals monitor their own behaviour executively. Great apes add a tier
that monitors those executive processes, which shows in opting out under
uncertainty and seeking missing information. They understand others'
goals, perception and knowledge by attributing the states they monitor in
themselves; beliefs remain unclear.

## Evidence

Review of 25 years of experiments with great apes; five testable
predictions.
