# THEORY-232: A whole can be compared in agency with its parts, relative to a chosen decomposition and the parts' goals: it is more agential when its organization lets the parts resolve more of their mutual constraints than they do at the equilibrium of their former organization, which shows as delayed gratification

## Source

Watson (2023), [LIT-990](../literature.d/LIT-990.md), *Biological Theory* 19:22–36, read in full in
[NOTE-747](../notes.d/NOTE-747.md): the criterion and measure (main text), the
delayed-gratification argument, Boxes 1 and 2, and the Discussion.

## What was actually shown

An argued definition with illustrations. The essay proves nothing new.

**The criterion.** "If a system at a higher level of organization causes
the parts it contains to do what they 'normally do' but more, as if the
complex constraints between them (that would have otherwise prevented
this) were alleviated, then that system is agential (with respect to its
parts)."

**The measure.** Agency is evaluated "by comparing the total frustration
or energy of the system at local equilibria given some original
organization of the parts to that obtained with some new organization of
the parts." No formula or scale is given beyond this comparison.

**The behavioural mark.** To beat a local optimum, "the energy function
(describing the dynamics of the system) must be different from the
objective function (describing the quality of solutions to the problem)".
The system "goes uphill in a specific direction that leads to lower
points in the long term". Stochastic escape does not count. A ball with
momentum, which finds better optima than one without in some landscapes,
is just above zero.

**The relativity, stated by the author.** The whole's norm is borrowed
from the parts' goals ("robbing Peter to pay Paul"). The result is
"sensitive to different ways of defining its subagents", and the
organism's agency "can be different or absent if we define the agency of
the parts differently". Given the parts' goals, though, "goal-directed
behavior can be quantified objectively at the system level".

**One worked example (Box 1).** In a two-hidden-unit XOR network either
hidden unit can compute any of 8 functions while the network still
classifies all four samples, so correct behaviour does not fix component
organization, and the organization fixes generalization. This is the
essay's only result shown in full, and it supports the claim that the
whole's organization is a further fact about it, not the criterion
itself.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say when there is an additional agent.** The criterion is
  comparative. It declines any line "regardless of whether the parts, or
  the system as a whole, are considered to pass some absolute threshold".
  As an answer to [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md) it changes the question.
- **It is not observer-free.** The comparison needs a chosen decomposition
  and chosen goals for the parts, which the author concedes can be
  "defined arbitrarily". No way of choosing among decompositions is
  offered, and different choices may rank systems differently.
- **It does not say that agency requires learned organization.** Learned
  origin is offered as an optional restriction ("Perhaps we might want to
  restrict a definition to systems that are able to acquire an internal
  organization"). Whether designed organization counts is left open.
- **It does not show that bottom-up learning produces agency.** The
  Hebbian self-modelling network (Watson et al. 2011a, c), the
  self-interested agents (Davies et al. 2011) and the ecological networks
  (Power et al. 2015) are reported from earlier papers, not re-derived.
  The Hebbian improvement depends on low-order constraints and on a
  correlation between basin size and solution quality, which need not
  hold in general.
- **It does not compute the measure for any real organism.** The
  baseline "disorganized" equilibrium is not observed in a real
  multicellular or social system, and the biological examples in the
  Discussion are not connected to the measure.

- **[CLAIM-174](../claims.d/CLAIM-174.md).** Delayed gratification gives the goal-directedness test a
  behavioural mark: excursions uphill in the objective, in a specific
  direction, that reach lower minima.
- **[CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md) (permissiveness).** The essay bites the bullet at the low end
  (a ball with momentum is barely, not zero, agential), and because the
  account is comparative the bullet costs little.
- **[CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md).** The reported timing result (Tissot et al., then under
  submission, now held as [LIT-989](../literature.d/LIT-989.md) and read in [NOTE-773](../notes.d/NOTE-773.md)) has learned
  relationships pick out groups that "work together", close to a
  collective variable carried by mutual entrainment. The learned
  relationship there is phase timing, not coupling weight, and the groups
  it finds are modules the couplings already define ([THEORY-265](THEORY-265.md)).
- **[THEORY-071](THEORY-071.md) (minimal agency).** A different kind of account. Barandiaran
  et al. set absolute conditions, including individuality with no
  observer doing the distinguishing and norms the system's own
  organization sets. Watson's account sets no threshold and borrows its
  norm from a stipulated decomposition. They answer different questions
  and are not declared rivals.
- **[THEORY-249](THEORY-249.md) (Abel et al., [LIT-969](../literature.d/LIT-969.md), read in [NOTE-793](../notes.d/NOTE-793.md)).**
  It generalizes this account's frame-dependence. Watson's comparison is
  relative to a chosen decomposition and stipulated goals for the parts,
  which are two of Abel et al.'s frame components; Watson concedes that
  relativity for one measure, and Abel et al. argue that every account of
  agency has it. Watson's measure is comparative and stays meaningful
  within a frame, which Abel et al. do not dispute. They predict no
  ranking stable across decompositions in general, but show no instance
  for this measure, so they do not settle its `promote_when`.

## Note of 2026-10-18: frustration as the record's primitive, and what this reading's measure aggregates

The exchange of 2026-10-17 made frustration, not distress, the primitive of
the record's account of regulatory difficulty (CLAIM-tmpyk3it,
TERM-tmp9lmlt), and A34 called this reading "the most important additional
connection". Three things bear on it.

- **The measure is an aggregate.** Watson compares "the total frustration
  or energy of the system". A34 §2 concluded: "A higher-level organization
  should not be credited with resolving constituent frustration merely
  because its aggregate objective improves", and offered it as following
  "directly from combining Watson's proposal with Nucleation's existing
  distinction between the good of a whole and the good of its
  constituents". It does not follow from Watson. It is a criticism of his
  measure as read here: a lower total can come from sacrificing one part's
  goal, which is the "robbing Peter to pay Paul" the essay itself names.
  CLAIM-tmp55mls gives the record's refinement: compare which sets of the
  parts' demands are jointly satisfiable, by inclusion, and leave two
  organizations unranked when each relieves a conflict the other does not.
- **The decomposition-relativity is half of A38's principle.** A38 §2:
  "The attribution of frustration relief must be indexed to an
  individuation and an intertemporal correspondence of demands", which
  A38 called "a defensible new formal principle". The first half is this
  reading's, already in its title ("relative to a chosen decomposition and
  the parts' goals"). The second, a correspondence of demands across time
  when the parts change, is new, and is CLAIM-tmpq80iq's.
- **Delayed gratification is the precedent for resolve.** The behavioural
  mark here, an energy function different from the objective that "goes
  uphill in a specific direction that leads to lower points in the long
  term", is what A35 §3 meant by a system that may "deliberately worsen
  some objective now", and what A37 and A39 developed as resolve. None of
  A34–A40 cited this reading for it.
