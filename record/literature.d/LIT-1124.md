# LIT-1124: Reconceptualizing Organizational Routines as a Source of Flexibility and Change

Feldman, M. S. & Pentland, B. T. (2003). *Administrative Science Quarterly* 48(1):94–118.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1386](../notes.d/NOTE-1386.md)). To A6's question, no: the
paper analyses one routine in one organization and only doubts, as an
empirical matter, that routines pass unchanged between organizations.

## Why this is here

A6 of the exchange of 2026-10-16 put it first: the organizational-studies form of persistence through changing enactments, and the test of whether [CLAIM-329](../claims.d/CLAIM-329.md) is already known there. A6's question for it: does it separate a particular organization's continuity from reproduction of an organizational kind?
