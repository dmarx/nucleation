# SYNTHESIS-098: Remedies foreclosed before review, by faits accomplis, speed, friction and siege: acting before an opportunity to intervene can take effect is proposed as a wrong of its own, an irreversible act is proposed to convert a holder's consent into a claim to compensation, to give a reviewer genuine reasons to accommodate it, and to give the breacher leverage over its own settlement but no power over what is owed, an opponent can put a remedy out of reach by raising what pursuing it costs, the cost of imposing a decision and of contesting it can move apart, and the baseline against which terms are judged can be the actor's own product

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency lines, on remedies foreclosed before they can be reviewed: by acting first, by outlasting, by moving friction, and by setting the baseline. Version 1 was filed at the interim synthesis review of 2026-11-17, before the readings and the reconciliation. Version 2 is the follow-up review of 2026-11-18. It adds the first filing's [CLAIM-701](../claims.d/CLAIM-701.md), describes [CLAIM-710](../claims.d/CLAIM-710.md) as it stands after the reconciliation (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46, its reconciliation section), and cites the readings as the support each component has, by the THEORY that carries the relation; a reading is not a component. Most components complement or rest on [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) (Active, the owner's earlier observation that an intervention can make a corrective outcome costlier or unreachable before review while the standards stay the same). The account cites [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) and does not integrate it. The line's account is [SYNTHESIS-095](SYNTHESIS-095.md); the sibling part, on answerability and contestability, is [SYNTHESIS-103](SYNTHESIS-103.md).*

## The account

**The wrong of acting first.** [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) (the owner's, U27, with A59's
four conditions, three kinds of standing and the narrowing to one
mechanism of bullying; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) grounds on
[THEORY-1972](../theory.d/THEORY-1972.md) and complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md)
and [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md): where a party has standing to authorize, suspend or
contest an act, an actor who without adequate justification makes its
consequences irreversible before that standing can be exercised defeats
it, "even where the objection would have failed". Its body says
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) "holds the timing" and "is causal and says nothing about
standing"; it keeps the single act apart from "the wrong of
constructing a relationship in which such acts can routinely prevail",
which it calls [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s. It names the family "wrongful unilateral
action", with remedial hold-up ([CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md)) and counter-corrective
domination ([CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md)) among its kinds (A59 §9).

*Readings.* Its ground, [THEORY-1972](../theory.d/THEORY-1972.md) (Fuentes v. Shevin, 1972, which A59
cited and the record had not read), locates the wrong in the timing: the
hearing "must be granted at a time when the deprivation can still be
prevented"; the right "does not depend upon an advance showing that one
will surely prevail", which is the claim's "even where the objection
would have failed"; and its "extraordinary situations" match "without
adequate justification". Its scope is a constitutional rule on
state-assisted seizures of property.

**The rule converted.** [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) (the connection the owner's, U26; the
form A's, A57, after Calabresi and Melamed; thesis, interpretive,
Proposed) grounds on [THEORY-1962](../theory.d/THEORY-1962.md), bears on [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md), and complements
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md): an irreversible act
against a consent-protected entitlement leaves the holder in practice
only a claim to compensation, so the actor has changed the rule of
allocation "from consent to liability". Its body sets the pairs: it is
"the rule changing" and [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) "the terms reached once it has";
[CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) "names the wrong in the order of moves; this one names what the
wrong converts"; and with [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) "the two agree".

*Readings.* Its ground, [THEORY-1962](../theory.d/THEORY-1962.md) (Calabresi and Melamed 1972, now
read), states the conversion mechanism in its §V. [THEORY-1972](../theory.d/THEORY-1972.md) (Fuentes)
corroborates it by analogy: for hearings, the Court refuses to equate a
prior opportunity with compensation after.

**The reasons a reviewer is given.** [CLAIM-701](../claims.d/CLAIM-701.md) (the assistant's, offered
at step 12 of the first filing and accepted by the owner on 2026-11-16;
thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines distributed-agency and
organizational-normativity) rests on [CLAIM-703](../claims.d/CLAIM-703.md) ([SYNTHESIS-099](SYNTHESIS-099.md)) and
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), complements [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md), [CLAIM-708](../claims.d/CLAIM-708.md) and [CLAIM-726](../claims.d/CLAIM-726.md), and bears on
[QUESTION-278](../questions.d/QUESTION-278.md): an actor that wrongfully creates circumstances in which
reversing its act would harm innocent third parties can give a reviewing
institution genuine reasons to accommodate those harms, as moral
blackmail gives its target a genuine obligation; the reviewer's sound
weighing does not discharge the actor's responsibility, and the reasons
are no larger than the circumstances support. Its body says it answers
in part the question [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) leaves aside (what weight risks created by
the disputed conduct should have), keeps three propositions apart and
asserts the third only for wrongful acts with foreseeable costs, and
cites [CASE-170](../cases.d/CASE-170.md) as an instance of the first two. Its Admission is a
separating case: harms that fall only on the actor give no genuine
reason. Its body calls [CLAIM-726](../claims.d/CLAIM-726.md) distinct and complementary, a reviewer's
genuine reasons there and an audience's true accusation here. No reading
carries a relation to it.

**The leverage gained.** [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) (A's, A55 §3 and §9, at the owner's
U25; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency) complements
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md), and bears on
[QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md): a breach can lower the injured party's position if it
refuses to settle, so a settlement it rationally accepts can leave the
breacher ahead, "the leverage coming from the violation". Its body says
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) holds "the reachability half exactly" and this adds the
bargaining channel, and it quotes A55 §8 against inferring from B's
choice that "The circumstances making that settlement B's best available
option were legitimate." No reading carries a relation to it.

**The power not gained.** [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) (A's, A85 §5, A87 §2 and §10, A90 V
and VII, A97; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
organizational-normativity and distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md),
grounds on [THEORY-1984](../theory.d/THEORY-1984.md), complements [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) and
[CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md), and uses [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): making a remedy unreachable gains a
wrongdoer no normative power over what is owed; the claim persists while
its remedy's correctability falls to zero, and a settlement reached
under the conditions the violation created does not authorize the
violation. Its body calls its third part "the normative complement" of
[CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), the conversion of [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) "practical, not normative", and
A87 §10's "Litigation by attrition" the owner's siege, [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md).
*Readings.* Its ground, [THEORY-1984](../theory.d/THEORY-1984.md) (Hohfeld 1913), is the distinction
A87 §2 uses, legal power against "the physical power to do the things
necessary"; Hohfeld analyses positive legal relations and says nothing
of moral claims or correctability.

[QUESTION-475](../questions.d/QUESTION-475.md) (A's, A68 §4, from Fiss as the record read him; Open; line
distributed-agency) asks whether the capacity to make remedies costly can
bend, over time, the standard they are measured against. Its body says
that if it can, [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md)'s premise holds at a time and can fail over
time, and [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) "would have a route by which it gains that power
after all"; it names Levinson ([LIT-1592](../literature.d/LIT-1592.md), Unfetched) as the reading to do
first. *Readings.* [THEORY-1981](../theory.d/THEORY-1981.md) (Galanter 1974) bears on it: evidence for
a yes by another route, repeat players who "play for rules" and skew
precedent, and overload that "shields existing rules from challenge".
Its note says Galanter changes the stock of rules, not the standard.

**Siege.** [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md) (the owner's, U28; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed;
also distributed-agency) grounds in [CASE-287](../cases.d/CASE-287.md), bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), uses
[TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md) and [TERM-105](../terms.d/TERM-105.md), and complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md), [CLAIM-601](../claims.d/CLAIM-601.md),
[CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md), [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md): an actor can make a
legally recognized corrective objective unreachable for another, with
the merits, the law and that party's preferences unchanged, by raising
what pursuing it costs, in spending or in time, above what that party can
sustain. Its body records that A64 told the point as its own "key
insight"; sets "Temporal preemption" ([CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md)) and "Attritional
exhaustion" beside each other as sharing one result (A64's close); calls
A61 §5's resource-mediated hold-up [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md); and calls A61 §10's split
of adjudicative from attritional friction "related" to [CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md).

*Readings.* Three THEORYs corroborate its mechanism, none its
threshold, the comparison of what the target can sustain with the time
to relief, which stays the exchange's construction: [THEORY-1981](../theory.d/THEORY-1981.md)
(Galanter: overload presses claimants to settle "by causing delay" and
"by raising costs (of keeping the case alive)"), [THEORY-1923](../theory.d/THEORY-1923.md) (Cuéllar
2026, read in part: attacks that "levy a high cost on those targeted in
the meantime", costly rather than unreachable) and [THEORY-1966](../theory.d/THEORY-1966.md) (Federal
Rules of Civil Procedure 11, 26 and 37: the law forbids papers and
discovery "to harass, cause unnecessary delay, or needlessly increase
the cost of litigation", a partial guard).

[CASE-287](../cases.d/CASE-287.md) (A's constructions, A61 and A64, every number rechecked and
five of A's statements corrected by the filer; construction, fictional,
Proposed; also distributed-agency) bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md): the four- and
seven-month game, the exponential race and the threshold. Its correction
of A64 §9's vulnerability gap requires the baseline course to be among
the opponent's admissible strategies, and it points to [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md) for
"the adversarial quantity itself".

**The measure.** [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md) (A's, A76 VIII.4 and A81 §3; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; lines realizability and dependence-and-domination)
complements [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md), [CLAIM-568](../claims.d/CLAIM-568.md) and [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md), bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md) and
uses [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): against a challenged party who can act on the means of
correction, correctability is robust to a declared class of opposition,
depends on who moves first, and splits permitted from merely available
opposition, failure against the first showing the institution inadequate
and against the second a wrong by the opponent as well. Its body says it
gives "the measure on which a siege succeeds and the split that says
whether the siege was a wrong", and that its third part "says where the
answer enters" to [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md): "in what goes into Σ_permitted". No
reading carries a relation to it.

**Whether siege is wrong.** [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md) (A's, asked at the close of
A61, A64 and A68; Open) refines [QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md): may a party under a
justified constraint set or raise the cost another must bear to make it
effective, and within which limits? Its body names A64's Candidate C as
"a normative proposal requiring independent justification", says A64
§10's definition moves the question into a counterfactual course "which
must itself be defended", and lists [CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md) (in [SYNTHESIS-103](SYNTHESIS-103.md)) as what
an answer denying the party such authority "would rest on". *Readings.*
[THEORY-1966](../theory.d/THEORY-1966.md) (the Federal Rules) bears on it with a partial answer in
positive law, confined to US federal civil procedure: not "for any
improper purpose", within proportionality, binding both sides.
[THEORY-1981](../theory.d/THEORY-1981.md) (Galanter) bears on it too.

**Friction.** [CLAIM-710](../claims.d/CLAIM-710.md) (the owner's, U11; thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; line distributed-agency) complements [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) and
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md): automation reduces procedural friction, yet friction is where

[CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md), [CLAIM-740](../claims.d/CLAIM-740.md), [CLAIM-715](../claims.d/CLAIM-715.md), [CLAIM-730](../claims.d/CLAIM-730.md) and [CASE-285](../cases.d/CASE-285.md) as filed from it
and says it does not rest on them, and names [CLAIM-711](../claims.d/CLAIM-711.md) (the first
filing's, distributed-agency, outside this account) as the objection to
its "characterizes" reading. *Readings.* [THEORY-1938](../theory.d/THEORY-1938.md) (Bovens and
Zouridis 2002: automating a step left one point of intervention, a
formal objection the affected person must open) and [THEORY-1947](../theory.d/THEORY-1947.md) (Citron
2008) corroborate it.

[CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md) (A's, A23 §2, at the owner's U12; thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; first line distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md) and
[CLAIM-710](../claims.d/CLAIM-710.md), complements [CLAIM-706](../claims.d/CLAIM-706.md) (the first filing's, outside this

of contesting it are separate, so an institution can lower the first and

deciding while challenge comes to need costly decoding of the system.

[CLAIM-730](../claims.d/CLAIM-730.md) (A's, A33 §4 and §8; supposed, interpretive, Proposed; first

can dominate those it harms with no one acting, and, where the rule is
