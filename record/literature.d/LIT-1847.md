# LIT-1847: Lecture 4: Statistical decision theory: model distance and equivalence

Han, Y. (2021). *Lecture 4: Statistical decision theory: model distance
and equivalence*. Stanford EE378C, lecture slides with annotations, April
7, 2021.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1426](../notes.d/NOTE-1426.md).*

- **Deficiency by decisions (slide 11).** For every finite Θ₀ ⊆ Θ, finite
  action set, loss L : Θ × A → [0, 1] and rule δ_N there is a rule δ_M
  with R_θ(δ_M) ≤ R_θ(δ_N) + ε for all θ ∈ Θ₀.
- **The randomization theorem (slide 13).** M is ε-deficient relative to
  N if and only if some kernel K : 𝒳 → 𝒴 has sup_θ ‖Q_θ − K P_θ‖_TV ≤ ε.
- **Easy direction, proved (slide 14).** δ_M = δ_N ∘ K, and for a loss in
  [0, 1] the risk gap is at most ‖Q_θ − K P_θ‖_TV, at every θ.
- **Hard direction, sketched (slide 15).** For finite Θ only: write the
  condition as sup over losses and priors of inf over δ_M, swap the inf
  and the sups, read the inner term as a total variation distance, and
  take the action set to be 𝒴 with δ_N the identity.
- **Le Cam's distance (slide 16)** is the larger of the two directed
  deficiencies, equivalently the larger of two inf–sup TV programmes.
