## What the readings change

- **A correction to this filing.** [QUESTION-399](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-399.md) said that nothing in the
  origami read here repairs itself, and [QUESTION-090](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-090.md)'s note said no reading
  reached replacement of damaged parts. Scheckenbach et al. show both are
  wrong. Folded origami replaces labels and missing staples from a strand
  pool, and in serum keeps 76 % intact over 11 days against 12 %. Both
  entries are corrected in place, saying what they said before.
- **Correction is internal, its supply external.** Proofreading tiles turn a
  mismatch into a stall, so the error falls off before it is locked in. At
  a target error rate of 10⁻⁴ this buys about 10⁴ in speed, and it fails on
  facet errors, which, in the authors' words, need "Some other error-correcting strategy". The value
  of correction depends on which errors dominate ([CLAIM-520](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-520.md)).
- **Barriers can be designed in or out.** Cumberworth et al. find no barrier
  in typical designs, and one of about 10 kBT at the melting temperature
  where stacking between staples is strong. It vanishes a few degrees
  lower. This is consistent with their 2025 review.
- **Dated notes** go on 13 entries, under "the readings on correction,
  repair, replication and nucleation barriers".

## The assistant's contributions

- **[CLAIM-596](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-596.md)** (demonstrated): yields cannot tell a generative nucleus
  from a head start. Two systems give 80.0 % with the nucleus and 56.7 %
  without it, one with the downstream rate quadrupled and one with it
  unchanged. Only the per-step rate decides.
- **[CLAIM-597](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-597.md)** (demonstrated): where every replacement part comes from
  outside, maintenance never becomes independent of the supply. The intact
  share is ρs/(ρs + d), holding θ costs d·θ/(1 − θ), and withdrawal returns
  decay at the damage rate. It is grounded in Scheckenbach et al. and in
  Zhou et al.'s replicators, whose outcome forgets the founding seed while
  every cycle needs fresh inputs.
- **A note on [QUESTION-397](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-397.md).** Equilibrium evidence such as Cumberworth
  et al.'s fixes only the ratio of forward and backward rates, so it cannot
  show a generative effect on forward hazards either.
- **Reproduction.** Both claims' numbers are added to
  scratch/formal/dna_origami_toys.py and asserted.
