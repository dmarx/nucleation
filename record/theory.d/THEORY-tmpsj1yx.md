# THEORY-tmpsj1yx: Wardrope reads Leopold's Land Ethic as giving biotic and human communities intrinsic value that extends, rather than replaces, the value of their individual members, so that the ethic requires respect both for fellow members and for the community as such, and escapes the charge of environmental fascism

## The account

Wardrope reads Leopold's Land Ethic as giving biotic and human communities intrinsic value that extends, rather than replaces, the value of their individual members, so that the ethic requires respect both for fellow members and for the community as such, and escapes the charge of environmental fascism

## Evidence

The skimmed reading in NOTE-tmpitkr7.

## What it does not say

That a community owes its members anything: the respect runs from members. Nor anything of the 2020 paper's argument about health justice.
