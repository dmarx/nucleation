# LIT-1996: Testing a continuum structure of self-determined motivation: A meta-analysis.

Howard, J. L., Gagné, M., Bureau, J. S. (2017). Testing a continuum structure of self-determined motivation: A meta-analysis.. *Psychological Bulletin, 143(12), 1346-1377*. doi:10.1037/bul0000125.

## Key takeaways

- "Results largely supported the presence of a continuum ordering of
  motivation" (manuscript p. 23).
- Integrated regulation "correlated with regulations in much the same
  direction and strength as identified regulation" (p. 19), and "may not
  have a place on the continuum" (p. 28).
- Nothing provides "evidence for the weights used in the RAI" (p. 31).

## Standing in the record

Bears on THEORY-054, whose promote_when asks for a measure of integrated
regulation separable from identified regulation. Read on 2026-11-22
(NOTE-tmp3k6if; THEORY-tmp0vgh7): across the validated scales to 2016
there is none. Ryan et al. 2022 (LIT-1967) reported it accurately.

## Note of 2026-11-22: Howard, Gagné and Bureau, read

A correction to version 2 of this entry's history, which said the
meta-analysis did not support the differentiation "partly because too few
studies measured integration". That is not the paper's reason. Integrated
regulation has 84 to 98 samples behind its pooled correlations (Tables
1–2), and the authors put the problem in the construct and its measures:
"new ways of conceptualizing and operationalizing integrated regulation
need to be developed" (p. 29). Version 2 is left as filed, since this
entry was filed before 2026-11-21. The gloss is Ryan et al.'s own ("due in
part to both too few studies measuring integration and its high correlation
with identified regulation", as NOTE-1500 quotes them), not this paper's.
