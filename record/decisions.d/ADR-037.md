# ADR-037: Mathematics of Meaning is a remote, MOM, and what this record needs from it is dual-filed as its own reading

## Context

On 2026-10-11, working on what makes a combined form one system (the dancing
couple, [CASE-tmpbv3ts](../cases.d/CASE-tmpbv3ts.md); the owner's [CLAIM-tmpygda8](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpygda8.md)), the owner pointed to
their Mathematics of Meaning project ("I think there might be some useful
ideas regarding system boundary identification in there"). It is a Luria
record of its own (dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning). Its prefixes are CLM,
ARG, POS, CON, LIT, ADR and DP. Its status means *standing* (does the record
still assert it), and its tags grade the footing (`derived`, `borrowed`,
`asserted`, `programmatic`, `stipulated`).

A survey found what this record lacks: a priced boundary criterion
([MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md) with [MOM-CLM-144](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-144.md) and [MOM-LIT-018](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-018.md)), a persistence criterion
([MOM-CLM-153](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-153.md)), a gluing test for unity ([MOM-CLM-165](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-165.md)), and the caution that
sufficiency-based individuation is relative to a target ([MOM-POS-001](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/positions.d/POS-001.md)). The
owner then asked: "register MoM as a remote and then dual file anything we
need from there on nucleation directly".

## Decision

- **The remote.** `MOM` in `luria.yaml`, schemes CLM, ARG, POS, CON, LIT,
  ADR and DP at their `record/` directories, `ref: main`. A citation is
  written `MOM-CLM-160`. Codes are not shared between the records.
- **Dual filing, extended to claims.** [ADR-013](ADR-013.md) lets a *work* be held in both
  records when each reads it for its own question. This decision applies the
  same rule to MOM's own claims and positions:
  - **A needed MOM claim or position gets an entry here** in the scheme that
    fits this record's use. That is usually a CLAIM, in the line that needs
    it. MOM is the owner's own reasoning, so it is the record's own
    proposition, not a reading of someone else's work, which would be a
    THEORY.
  - **The entries name each other one way.** The entry here cites the MOM
    code in its summary and body, and its first history note says it is
    dual-filed. MOM is not edited from here.
  - **Status is this record's own.** MOM's `derived` grade is not inherited.
    An entry is `Active` here only if this record re-ran the check or reads
    the result as standard. Otherwise it is `Proposed`, with the reason in
    the entry.
  - **Disagreements are reported, not fixed across the boundary.** The same
    rule as [ADR-013](ADR-013.md).
- **Works held in both.** Where MOM's LIT and an entry here are the same
  work, [ADR-013](ADR-013.md) applies as written. Each names the other. So far that is
  Simon (1962): [MOM-LIT-018](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-018.md) and [LIT-tmp6qp14](../literature.d/LIT-tmp6qp14.md). And Bruineberg et al. (2022):
  [MOM-LIT-023](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-023.md) and [LIT-603](../literature.d/LIT-603.md).
- **Only what is needed.** An entry is dual-filed when a question here uses
  it, and the curation entry that files it says which question. MOM is not
  mirrored.

## Alternatives

- **Cite across only.** With the remote alone, the citation would resolve.
  But a CLAIM here cannot `rests_on` a MOM code, so the argument chains this
  record draws ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)) would stop at the border. MOM's status would also
  stand in for this record's credence.
- **File MOM's claims as THEORY.** THEORY is a reading of a work's account,
  sourced from a LIT. MOM's claims are the owner's reasoning, often repairs
  of the owner's own drafts, and the record's own propositions are CLAIMs.
- **Copy MOM's status and grade.** That would let a `derived` tag there stand
  in for a check here. MOM's own design principles ([MOM-DP-010](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/principles.d/DP-010.md)) ask that a
  check be run before a claim is called derived, and this record does the
  same.

## Consequences

- **The first batch.** Five MOM entries are dual-filed as CLAIMs in
  `distributed-agency`, from [MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md), [MOM-CLM-144](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-144.md), [MOM-CLM-165](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-165.md),
  [MOM-CLM-153](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-153.md) and [MOM-POS-001](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/positions.d/POS-001.md). Three were re-checked by script before being
  made Active (curation entry of the same date).
- **The lint does not check MOM citations.** This was fired on
  2026-10-11 (curation entry of that date). A planted [MOM-CLM-999](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-999.md) passed
  `luria lint`, because remote codes resolve by filename convention without
  checking that the file exists. `luria remotes --check` cannot verify a
  private repository anonymously. Until something better exists, a MOM
  citation is checked against a local clone of the repository when it is
  filed. All eight cited so far exist at MOM commit d987e2a.
- **Drift is possible.** A MOM claim may be retired after its twin is filed
  here. `luria remotes --pin` can endorse the cited MOM documents by hash, so
  that a change upstream is reported. That is not done yet, and it waits for
  a second need.
