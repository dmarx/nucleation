# LIT-258: Contrastive Learning Can Find An Optimal Basis For Approximately View-Invariant Functions

Daniel D. Johnson, Ayoub El Hanchi, Chris J. Maddison (2022), *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2023); first appeared as arXiv preprint* — [ARXIV-2210.01883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01883)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpu7m7r](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpu7m7r.md).*

- **One kernel behind three losses.** The exponential and the temperature
  are counted as part of the model. Over all kernel functions on a finite
  augmentation space, the NT-Logistic and spectral losses are then
  minimized by K⁺(a₁,a₂) = p⁺(a₁,a₂)/(p(a₁)p(a₂)). NT-Xent/InfoNCE is
  minimized by K⁺·b(a₁), with b constant on each communicating class of
  the positive-pair chain (App. B). K⁺ is a Mercer kernel with an explicit
  feature map φ⁺(a)_z = p(a|z)√p(z)/p(a) (Def. 2.1).
- **Invariance in the eigenbasis.** In the L²(p)-orthonormal
  eigenfunctions of the chain p⁺(a′|a),
  E_p⁺[(g(a₁) − g(a₂))²] = Σ(2 − 2λ_i)c_i² (Prop. 3.1).
- **Kernel PCA is the chain's eigendecomposition.** Population kernel PCA
  under K⁺ gives σ_i² = λ_i and h_i = λ_i^(1/2) f_i (Thm 3.2). The proof is
  one SVD: the feature covariance and the symmetrized adjacency
  D_A^(−1/2)P_(A,A)D_A^(−1/2) are BBᵀ and BᵀB for B = Φ⁺D_A^(1/2).
- **Minimax optimality.** The span of the top-d eigenfunctions minimizes
  both the worst-case discrepancy of a unit-norm predictor and the
  worst-case squared approximation error over targets with discrepancy at
  most ε (Thm 4.1). The worst case is ε/(2(1 − λ_(d+1))). Below
  d* (the number of eigenvalues equal to 1), no d-dimensional space has
  finite worst-case error (App. D.1).
- **In practice, partly.** On two synthetic tasks with computable K⁺,
  learned kernels plus kernel PCA approximately recover the eigenfunctions.
  Recovery degrades with head constraints and with weak augmentation. The
  authors could not obtain reliable eigenfunctions from real-data models
  (App. E.1, F.5).

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-09-26 while pursuing, at the owner's request, the connection
*Riesz, reproducing kernels and spectral representation learning* (see the
curation entry of that date). Read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-tmpu7m7r](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpu7m7r.md).

It is held as the most explicit single statement that InfoNCE-family
losses target one kernel, and that the downstream-optimal representation
is that kernel's top eigenfunctions. It gives the spectral reading of
self-supervised learning an optimality claim, not only a correspondence.
It is the principal source of these theories:

- **[THEORY-001](../theory.d/THEORY-001.md)** is confirmed. For InfoNCE the minimum is over symmetric
  functions and its form is cited from Van den Oord et al.
- **[THEORY-007](../theory.d/THEORY-007.md)** is confirmed on every cited result. Theorem 4.1 needs
  d ≥ d*.
- **[THEORY-005](../theory.d/THEORY-005.md).** The operator identity it calls its own appears in App. E.2,
  without an integrability condition.
- **[THEORY-009](../theory.d/THEORY-009.md)** is borne out. Self-adjointness comes from P_(A,A) being a
  Gram matrix, so both views must be drawn from the same p(A|Z), and Riesz
  is not used.

It reads HaoChen et al. ([LIT-249](LIT-249.md)) as the same decomposition up to a
p(a)^(1/2) scaling.

Tagged `anthology-candidate` ([ADR-005](../decisions.d/ADR-005.md)). The paper recommends a training
pipeline (a contrastive loss, then kernel PCA, with unconstrained heads),
which is machine-learning practice. It is kept here by the owner's decision
of 2026-09-26 that new work stays in nucleation until a transfer is judged
appropriate ([ADR-010](../decisions.d/ADR-010.md)). What this record reads it for is the kernel and
eigenfunction statement.
