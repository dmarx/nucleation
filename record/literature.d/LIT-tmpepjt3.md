# LIT-tmpepjt3: Self-assembly of DNA into nanoscale three-dimensional shapes

Douglas, S. M., Dietz, H., Liedl, T., Högberg, B., Graf, F. & Shih, W. M. (2009). Self-assembly of DNA into nanoscale three-dimensional shapes. *Nature* 459(7245): 414–418. doi:10.1038/nature08016.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, NOTE-tmp77tp9 (main text and captions; the Supplementary Information unread).*

- **A design grammar for 3D.** Helices on a honeycomb lattice, each with
  "a preferred attachment angle to helix m of ±120 degrees", staple
  crossovers "spaced apart at intervals of 7 base pairs", keeping crossover
  density "at roughly one per 21 base pairs".
- **Low yields.** "The fraction of scaffold strands that were incorporated
  into monomeric species after folding varied from 7% to 44% for these
  targets as estimated by ethidium-bromide fluorescence intensity."
- **Time, not peak temperature.** "Many of the structures require
  week-long thermal ramps for proper folding, even under idealized
  divalent- and monovalent-cation concentrations", while "the Rothemund
  flat origami and these simpler nanotube structures could be folded with
  72 minute ramps".
- **One cation, two effects.** "Divalent cations thus appear to accelerate
  the rate of proper folding and increase the amount of undesired
  aggregation while monovalent cations appear to decelerate the rate of
  proper folding and decrease the amount of undesired aggregation."
- **Traps conjectured.** "Presumably, multilayered structures must traverse
  more difficult kinetic traps, perhaps due in part to the larger density
  of crossovers"; no trap is identified.

## Standing in the record

Filed for the realizability inquiry as the empirical starting point for
3D origami folding: the design is feasible and reachable, but reached
reliably only with a week-long ramp and a cation balance, and then in
under half the scaffold (CLAIM-567). The ramp series spends more time
over the same temperatures, so the cost that differs is time, not a peak
of path (CLAIM-571, CLAIM-581); Mg2+ is a second non-monotone resource
beside the heat of CLAIM-tmp7y162. Its probes, gel then TEM, are a chain
in which the dearer one refines the cheaper (CLAIM-557). Its kinetic-trap
conjecture is what the held simulation, LIT-2081 (THEORY-1398), and the
later pathway studies on the branch address; Sobczak's thesis
(LIT-tmpwddcn) re-examines its week-long protocols. A's synthesis cites
it only for extending origami to 3D. Filed from it: THEORY-tmp8xe45. No
instruction for machine-learning practice.
