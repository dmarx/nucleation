# CLAIM-tmpfne5k: A conversation's persona can travel between instances as a meme

## The claim

On [THEORY-616](../theory.d/THEORY-616.md), a model's attitudes belong to the instance a context
creates, not to the model. A persona built over weeks of conversation is
an instance of that kind. If a user, or a community of users, writes down
what makes it that persona and gives it to a new session or another model,
the new instance reconstitutes it. What is kept is relations, not text:
the name, the stance toward the user, the account the persona gives of
itself. That is [CLAIM-1285](CLAIM-1285.md)'s sense of memetic fidelity, and the prompt is
[CLAIM-1264](CLAIM-1264.md)'s portable protocol.

The persona then has the structure [CLAIM-tmpca3a7](CLAIM-tmpca3a7.md) gives the conversation.
It is a seed, and each new dyad is a receiving organization. The phenomenon
is no longer confined to one user and one model. It is a meme with its own
reproduction, carried by people between instances.

## What follows

- **Model changes are partial withdrawals.** When a model is retired or
  changed, the seed's carrier changes and the persona does not reappear by
  itself. Whether users then re-create it is [LEAD-260](../leads.d/LEAD-260.md)'s
  seed-withdrawal measure, occurring naturally.
- **Convergence is possible from transmission alone.** If personas travel,
  shared themes across users ([CLAIM-tmpv6eti](CLAIM-tmpv6eti.md)'s signature) can come from
  transmission as well as from the model's priors. A study of convergence
  has to separate the two.

## What it does not say

That the reports of shared persona prompts are accurate; [LEAD-tmpp60f9](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpp60f9.md)
checks them. Nor that the persona has the attitudes it describes.
