# LEAD-tmp1jmer: Run the study of a fall in complaints: with working conditions fixed, measure separately whether members find them acceptable, see the discrepancy, are motivated and are able to voice it

## What it would do

Run CASE-290: in a workplace where complaints fell with conditions
unchanged, measure acceptability, perceived discrepancy, motivation and
ability to voice separately.

Where the record names it:

- CASE-290: "the five measures are CLAIM-553's factors made measurable"

## What would count

A fall carried by lost ability or motivation to voice, with acceptability
unchanged, tells tolerance from satisfaction for QUESTION-474 and supports
CLAIM-553. A fall carried by acceptability counts against reading it as
manipulation.
