# LIT-1619: El-Masri v. the former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia

European Court of Human Rights, Grand Chamber (2012). *El-Masri v. the former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia*, no. 39630/09, judgment of 13 December 2012.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmps22mh), from the HUDOC copy of the
judgment (item 001-115621) rather than the Information Note the `url:`
names.

## Why this is here

A8 offered it as the comparator for [CASE-175](../cases.d/CASE-175.md): responsibility found across a transfer between states, which would separate what is new in CECOT from long-standing problems of rendition and outsourced detention.
