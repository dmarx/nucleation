---
title: 'A prior-art search by the wrong key reported a held, read work as absent'
created: '2026-11-26T18:13:08'
tags: [retro]
---

**What went wrong.** During the dialectic session of 2026-11-26, the
assistant twice told the owner that Sen's "The Impossibility of a Paretian
Liberal" was not on the record. It wrote the same thing into CLAIM-tmpzzeqo
("Sen is not on the record") and into LEAD-tmpl2s2z ("Neither is on the
record"). The work is LIT-285, read in full in NOTE-255, with THEORY-027
drawn from it.

**Why.** The check was a single grep of `record/literature.d` for the
strings "paretian liberal|liberal paradox|Sen, A|Amartya Sen". The hits
were five other Sen works (capabilities, the Tanner Lectures), and none
was LIT-285, whose title uses "Paretian Liberal" in title case. The grep
was case-sensitive, and the assistant read five non-matches as
confirmation that the paper was absent. Step 2 of the read-and-file skill
says to search "by DOI, arXiv id, exact title and first author". The
first check skipped that, as an aside in the middle of an argument and not
as a filing step. A case-insensitive search for the title, run later when
LITs were being registered, found it at once.

**What went right.** The registration step's own check, a
case-insensitive search for every work before `luria new lit`, caught the
error before a duplicate LIT was filed. The correction then improved the
claim: NOTE-255's statement of Sen's proof showed that his route to a
cycle runs through Pareto edges, which the potential account does not
have.

**For the retro.** A claim that "X is not on the record", made in
passing during the dialectic, should get the same search as a
registration: case-insensitive, by exact title and first author, and by
DOI where one is known.
