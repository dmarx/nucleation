# THEORY-tmprxeh7: Privileged irresponsibility, in Tronto's own later gloss, is a kind of bad care, whose danger is that it cuts off one's reaction to others

Parra Jounou and Tronto (2024), [LIT-2709](../literature.d/LIT-2709.md), read in [NOTE-tmpko5k2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpko5k2.md).

## The account

"The danger of privileged irresponsibility is to cut off one's reaction to
others." "privileged irresponsibility is a kind of bad care." Of the coach
image in Caring Democracy: "Once you climb into it, you pretend that you
don't know all those people pushing the carriage."

## What it does not show

An interview answer and an image, not an account of the mechanism.
