**Going wrong.** The search before the SEP readings followed [ADR-154](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-154.md): open pull requests, case-insensitive, by mechanism words. But the words chosen were the topic's: deception, self-deception, delusion, Mele. The mechanism [CLAIM-tmpu6c7h](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu6c7h.md) turns on is reward selecting a belief, and "reward" was not searched. The self-deception reader found [NOTE-443](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-443.md) (Ainslie), which holds that premise directly.

**Going right.** The readers' rechecks, which search again with what the reading surfaced, caught it, along with three more: [NOTE-998](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-998.md), [NOTE-1000](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1000.md) and [NOTE-1450](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1450.md). The rule's second search is doing its job.

**For the next retro.** The mechanism words for a claim are its premises' words, not its topic's. Before briefing, list the premises of each claim the reading bears on, and search for those.
