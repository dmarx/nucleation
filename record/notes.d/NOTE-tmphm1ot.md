# NOTE-tmphm1ot: Schmietendorf 2026, read for how far the slaving principle licenses causal talk of order parameters acting on their constituents

## Contribution

A map of how enslavement and circular causality can be read
metaphysically, for a scientific audience. The reading is [THEORY-tmpkt3xf](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpkt3xf.md).

## Key results

1. **Synergetics in brief** (§2, p. 2): "When the quantity playing the role
   of the control parameter changes such that the system reaches a
   critical point, a separation of time scales occurs. This allows the
   emerging macro-dynamics to be described by only a small number of
   slowly varying unstable modes, so-called order parameters." The stable
   modes "adjust to values dictated by the order parameters almost
   instantaneously"; then "circular causality sets in". Synchronization is
   the example: the phases "collectively constitute an order parameter
   that, in turn, feeds back onto the individual phase dynamics".
2. **Two readings** (§2.1, after Stephan 2005): the descriptive reading
   "amounts to nothing more than the thesis that the macroscopic behavior
   of the system can be represented by only a few variables"; the causal
   reading says the constituents' behaviour "is determined by the
   macroscopic entity represented by the order parameter". "Without
   further argument, such a shift amounts to a post hoc ergo propter hoc
   fallacy".
3. **Objections to downward causation** (§2.3): circularity, from the
   transitivity of causation (Sperry's vortex, Kim), and exclusion, from
   the micro-level's causal closure.
4. **Weak emergence** (§2.4.1): on Bedau's account enslavement is
   reducible downward causation, diachronic and so not circular, with
   macro-causes as "aggregated micro-causes rather than competing with
   them"; Synergetics "is readily situated within this conceptual
   framework".
5. **Constraints** (§2.4.2): on Bishop's downward causation through
   constraints, Bénard cells, once formed, "come to play the role of new
   internal constraints that both restrict and enable the dynamics of the
   constituent fluid elements".
6. **Verdict** (§3, p. 5): "Synergetics does not, by itself, establish
   ontological anti-reductionism, and current debate tends to favor
   reductionist interpretations."

## Assumptions

A broadly naturalistic metaphysics, with science guiding but not settling
metaphysical debate (note 1).

## Concepts

Order parameter, enslavement, circular causality (Haken); descriptive and
causal readings of slaving (Stephan); weak emergence and explanatory
incompressibility (Bedau); downward causation through constraints and
contextual emergence (Bishop, Ellis).

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md)** (note only). Key result 1 restates the claim's second
  half as Haken's: a critical point makes the separation, and the slow
  modes become order parameters. It is a secondary account, so not
  independent evidence. Key result 2 marks a limit the claim should keep
  in view: it calls the settled relation a constraint in Montévil and
  Mossio's sense, which is the descriptive reading plus a time-scale
  condition, not the causal reading Stephan warns against.
- **[CLAIM-526](../claims.d/CLAIM-526.md)** (note only). The paper's verdict, that whether the order
  parameter has causal efficacy of its own is not settled by the
  dynamics, fits the claim's point that a closed collective variable can
  be an aggregate coexisting with its realization.
- **[CLAIM-327](../claims.d/CLAIM-327.md)** and **[CLAIM-334](../claims.d/CLAIM-334.md)** (note only). The record's own
  conditions for a slow collective variable being part of the dynamics
  (read and acted on) and its caution about macro-to-micro Granger
  causation are finer instruments than the descriptive and causal readings
  the paper contrasts.
- **Haken 1975** ([LIT-2255](../literature.d/LIT-2255.md), unavailable). This paper restates the slaving
  account in two sentences; it stands in for the review only that far.

## Limitations

A perspective; it reports positions rather than arguing for one, and the
Kuramoto example is stated, not analysed.
