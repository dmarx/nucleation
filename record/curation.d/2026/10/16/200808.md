The owner's thesis that judgement and observation, not action and
perception, make up the loop active inference describes, and the owner's case of
locked-in syndrome (workbench entry of 20:08), needed works the record did
not hold. Each was checked on 2026-10-16 against Crossref and, for
abstracts, OpenAlex. Bauby's memoir was checked against Open Library, since
it has no DOI. None is held in the Anthology of the SOTA: a grep of its
record/ (clone of 2026-10-16, commit d1573d2) for "locked-in", the authors
and the titles found nothing. None is about machine learning.

**Depth.** For five of the works the abstract was read. For Kübler and
Birbaumer, the abstract and the discussion's statement of the hypothesis
were read from PMC2644824. Parr and Friston and Laureys et al. have no
abstract in either index, so their summaries are what the works are
standardly cited for, and they say so.

**Dates.** Sandved-Smith et al. and Chaudhary et al. are dated by their
preprints (PsyArXiv 2020-06-10, medRxiv 2020-06-12), under [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md)'s
earlier-date rule. Bruno et al. and Laureys et al. carry year-only dates.

**Cautions carried into the entries.** Chaudhary et al.'s senior authors had
earlier CLIS papers retracted after a DFG misconduct finding (STAT,
2022-03-22). Crossref gives Laureys et al.'s pages as 495–611, which looks
wrong for one chapter, and the entry says so. Limanowski and Friston (2018),
named from memory at A1, was not found under the DOI tried and is not
registered.
