# QUESTION-038: What turns a structure that persists into an anchor that later structure can be built on?

## Why it is a question

The owner's statement has two halves: "anything that triggers persistence",
and "persistent structures that are available to serve as anchors". The
second half is the one the first does not supply. A chat transcript
persists, and so does a manuscript draft, but neither has served as an
anchor in this record until something was done to it.

Three candidates for what availability takes:

- **Addressability.** The thing has a code, or a label, that later entries
  can point at ([CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md) argues that this is the decisive one).
- **Stable identity.** It is revised by versioning or supersession rather
  than edited away, so that what points at it still points at the same
  thing.
- **Discoverability.** A later writer can find it. In luria this is the
  generated views and indexes.

What would count as an answer: a case where a structure had one or two of
these and was still not built on, which would show what the missing one
contributes. A measure would also help. Inbound references per code tell
an anchor from a structure that only persists.

## Note of 2026-10-14: Ashby's answer

Ashby ([LIT-787](../literature.d/LIT-787.md), read in [NOTE-851](../notes.d/NOTE-851.md); [THEORY-307](../theory.d/THEORY-307.md), [THEORY-324](../theory.d/THEORY-324.md))
is a direct ancestor of the owner's statement, down to the crystal
(Ch. 11). His condition for a kept structure to stay kept is isolation: a
retained part must be cut off by constancies from what is still changing
(Ss. 11/10, 12/17). His condition for it to be usable again is access: a
store closed to new trials and re-opened when its own situation recurs
(Ss. 10/8–10/9). In record terms, an entry that new filing cannot
overwrite, and a way to reach it by its situation. That is a fourth
candidate beside the three above, or a sharper form of stable identity
and discoverability together. It is analogy, and does not answer the
question.
