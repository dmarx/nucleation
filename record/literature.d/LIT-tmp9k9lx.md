## Why this is here

The owner's exchange of 2026-10-21, "Experience and interpretation", asked whether experience is constituted by interpretation or produced by it. The exchange cited this paper for a representation space in a language model whose contents are available for verbalization, deliberate manipulation and flexible reasoning, a functional analogue of the global workspace. It took the paper to make the overgeneration problem concrete: if interpretive organization of this kind sufficed for experience, the thesis would have to say whether this system has it. Bears on [QUESTION-048](../questions.d/QUESTION-048.md), [QUESTION-046](../questions.d/QUESTION-046.md) and [QUESTION-tmpc4j7p](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpc4j7p.md). The anthology may want it as interpretability work.
