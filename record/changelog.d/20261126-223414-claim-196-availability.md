### Changed

- [CLAIM-196](record/claims.d/CLAIM-196.md), v2, at the owner's word: "Roles only respectively available
  in disjoint contexts cannot conflict". The condition is now that each
  role is available only in its own context, not merely that the audiences
  are disjoint. A role carried beyond its context, by memory, anticipation
  or identification, can conflict even while the audiences are kept apart.

### Added

- [CLAIM-tmprejdn](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmprejdn.md): a self can be contextual. Its roles can be consistent on
  every pairwise meeting occasion with no global presentation around a
  cycle. Opening any one overlap cures it, and collapse can create it
  without any pairwise clash. It follows the owner's point that [CLAIM-196](record/claims.d/CLAIM-196.md)
  is near-tautological and that its importance lies in the contextuality
  program. A parity toy check is in scratch/studies/contextual-self/.
- [CLAIM-tmpw7swg](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw7swg.md): a presented self's coherence is relative to a cover the
  person partly controls. It is the structural half of the audience
  management that [CLAIM-317](record/claims.d/CLAIM-317.md) describes, formerly nested in [CLAIM-196](record/claims.d/CLAIM-196.md), and
  [CLAIM-tmprejdn](record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmprejdn.md) now rests on it.
