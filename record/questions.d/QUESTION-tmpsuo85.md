# QUESTION-tmpsuo85: Outside NASA, does an organization's first acceptance of a deviation, traced prospectively, predict widening acceptance and later failure more often than in comparable organizations that eliminate deviations, and does an independent safety function interrupt the pattern?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md) holds the Board's reconstruction of two NASA accidents, made
after the fact. The general theory is Vaughan's ([LIT-715](../literature.d/LIT-715.md)), which the
record has not read, and retrospective reconstructions select the cases
that ended badly.

## What would answer it

[THEORY-140](../theory.d/THEORY-140.md)'s former promote_when, unchanged:

Evidence beyond the two Shuttle accidents that the pattern is general
and causal: organizations in which accepted anomalies can be traced
prospectively, showing that the first acceptance of a deviation predicts
widening acceptance and later failure more often than in comparable
organizations that eliminate deviations. It would also need evidence that
an independent safety function, as the Board recommends, interrupts the
pattern. More retrospective accident reconstructions cannot settle it,
since they select the cases that ended badly.
