# LEAD-317: Test whether losing control of audience separation shifts repair onto the roles

## What it would do

Compare people facing the same collision of presentations with and
without control over audience separation. Examples: posts to a public
versus a segmented audience, or a scripted versus an unscripted meeting of
audiences. Measure how often each repairs by changing the cover
(segregating, scripting, withdrawing) and how often by changing the
presentation itself.

- **For [CLAIM-1447](../claims.d/CLAIM-1447.md):** more role revision where the cover cannot be
  changed.
- **Against it:** equal rates either way.
