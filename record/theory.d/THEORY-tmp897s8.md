# THEORY-tmp897s8: Leach and colleagues report that in-group identification among Dutch students has five components on two correlated higher-order dimensions, group-level self-definition and self-investment, and that faced with their group's misdeeds, self-definition predicted group-based guilt while self-investment predicted legitimizing the misdeed

## The account

Identification has a structure: five narrow components under two
higher-order dimensions, taken from Durkheim's and Tönnies's
distinctions between likeness and chosen commitment. Defining oneself
as a member and being invested in the group are different, and under
threat they pull apart: "When confronted with an in-group's misdeeds, it
is the individuals' self-definition at the group level that seems
necessary to the self-critical feeling of group-based guilt", while
"those who most invested themselves in their in-group identity most
strongly defended this identity by legitimizing the in-group's misdeed"
(p. 163). The investment dimension is not plain affect, since
centrality loads on it as highly as satisfaction (p. 162).

## Evidence

From [NOTE-1262](../notes.d/NOTE-1262.md): confirmatory models fit three groups and two cohorts
(CFI .966 to .983), better than one-factor or cognitive-versus-affective
models. In Study 7 (N = 49) self-stereotyping as European was the only
predictor of guilt (.33), and satisfaction (.39) and centrality (.29)
predicted legitimizing European asylum policy. The overlap circles
correlated .20 with self-stereotyping and .31 with satisfaction for
the Dutch. Self-report throughout; no helping is measured.

## What it bears on

[QUESTION-300](../questions.d/QUESTION-300.md) asks for "a criterion of the self's extent that can
come apart from caring". Self-stereotyping, seeing oneself as like the
average member, is a criterion fixed by perceived likeness, and here it
separates from the investment components and predicts differently. The
overlap circles, which the question lists as a candidate, fell on the
investment side. [CLAIM-492](../claims.d/CLAIM-492.md)'s note of 2026-10-29 records the same.
