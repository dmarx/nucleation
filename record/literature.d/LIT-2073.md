# LIT-2073: Connectivity of spaces of directed paths in geometric models for concurrent computation

Raussen, M. (2023). Connectivity of spaces of directed paths in geometric models for concurrent computation. *Computational Geometry: Theory and Applications* 109: 101942. doi:10.1016/j.comgeo.2022.101942. arXiv:2106.11703 (v1 2021).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1581](../notes.d/NOTE-1581.md). Section and result numbers are arXiv
v3's.*

- **The model** (§1.1). Executions are directed maps, where "directed
  means continuous and (not necessarily strictly) increasing", and they
  "are not allowed to enter the forbidden region F" in which some resource
  is over capacity.
- **Connected, not traversable** (Example 2.4): "The red vertex represents
  a deadlock: no non-trivial directed path in the state space starts from
  that vertex", though the state space is connected.
- **Spare capacity** (§1.2.2, Def. 3.9): "Roughly speaking, the spare
  capacity at a vertex v expresses the number of processors that can
  proceed from v concurrently"; a vertex is a deadlock "if and only if
  κ(X; v) = 0" (Proposition 3.12).
- **Local to global** (Theorem 4.3, Corollary 4.4): "If κ(X) ≥ 1, then
  P~ (X)tv0 is non-empty. If κ(X) ≥ 2, then P~ (X)tv0 is path-connected."
- **Doomed regions** (Remark 4.2): "the set of all elements that cannot be
  connected to t by a directed path".

## Standing in the record

The neighbour standing in for Fajstrup, Goubault & Raussen 2006
([LIT-2065](LIT-2065.md)), which the pasted synthesis on resource-bounded
realizability probably cites. It stands in for the PV model, directed
paths, d-homotopy, deadlocks and doomed regions, not for the 2006 paper's
own results. It supports A's point that connectivity is not reachability,
precisely; and it shows that in this model the directed reachability and
the resource capacities A puts on separate levels are one structure, the
capacities defining the forbidden region. It also bears on [CLAIM-568](../claims.d/CLAIM-568.md),
whose "no path" should say whether it means directed paths. Filed from it:
