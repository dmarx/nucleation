# LIT-tmpq5p0l: Moral Failure — Response to Critics

Tessman, Lisa (2016). Moral Failure — Response to Critics. *Feminist Philosophy Quarterly* 2(1), Article 6. DOI 10.5206/fpq/2016.1.6.

## Key takeaways

Moral failure can be unavoidable: non-negotiable requirements survive being overridden or made impossible. Another's wrongdoing can create a duty that is unfair to its bearer and still binding, provided the person owed is innocent; a wrongdoer-dependent may forfeit the care.
