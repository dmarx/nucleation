# CLAIM-1105: Fundamental symmetries defeat relata–realization

## The objection

[CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md)'s defeater (b) is "A constraint that does individuating work
while realized by nothing, a specification prior to every realization,
which would make constraints prior without qualification for the kinds
it individuates." A spacetime symmetry of the current fundamental
theories is one.

1. **It does individuating work.** In Wigner's classification an
   elementary particle kind is an irreducible representation of the
   Poincaré group, fixed by mass and spin ([CLAIM-134](CLAIM-134.md)'s partial reply).
   That fixes kinds, which is what (b) counts.
2. **It is realized by nothing.** The particles realize the kinds. They do
   not realize the symmetry. Deutsch and Marletto take conservation of
   energy–momentum as the model of a principle: "a law of physics that
   constrains other laws rather than the behaviour of physical objects
   directly" ([THEORY-1078](../theory.d/THEORY-1078.md)). A constraint on laws is not produced by the
   processes those laws govern.
3. **It is maintained by nothing.** It is conserved at every time scale and
   produced at none, so [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s second half fails. Nothing would have
   to keep doing anything for the symmetry to go on holding.
4. **A law can fix a kind before any member is found.** [THEORY-1078](../theory.d/THEORY-1078.md) gives
   the pattern from the paper's footnote 1: beta decay "kept energy
   conservation by way of the neutrino". The conservation law called for a
   particle with stated properties before any had been detected, so the
   law fixed a kind ahead of its members. That is a specification prior to its
   realizations in the plainest sense.

## Against the leaned-to reading

[CLAIM-1094](CLAIM-1094.md) grants points 1 to 3 for a fundamental symmetry and
answers that no result shows a symmetry fundamental. But the record does
not hold its other claims to the standard of being shown final. It takes
its physics from the best current theories. Requiring a proof that a law
is fundamental makes (b) impossible to meet for any law, which is the
same fault [CLAIM-1094](CLAIM-1094.md) finds in the thin reading: a defeater that
cannot fire. Parity does not change this. Its refutation showed that
parity was not a principle. It did not find an organization below parity
that realizes it.

## What follows for [CLAIM-477](CLAIM-477.md)

Not rejection. Its domain shrinks. The frame holds for every kind whose
constraints are effective, which covers all its own cases ([CLAIM-480](CLAIM-480.md)'s
boundary coupling, closure of constraints, the record's own schemes). At
the level of elementary particle kinds, the constraint is prior to its
realizations, and "neither ... prior without qualification" fails there.
The claim's defeater (b) says what happens then: the claim is restricted
"to the kinds its constraints are effective for".

## What it does not say

- That a symmetry fixes which member of a kind is which. Characters do
  not individuate ([CLAIM-134](CLAIM-134.md)). The objection is about kinds, which (b)
  counts.
- That every conservation law is fundamental. Effective ones meet the
  definition, as [CLAIM-1094](CLAIM-1094.md) shows. The objection concerns only the
  symmetries current theory treats as fundamental.
- That current theory is final. It says the record should rule on the
  theory it has.

## Why it is not leaned to

Whether to take current fundamental theory at face value is the crux.
[CLAIM-1094](CLAIM-1094.md)'s reason against it is that the frame's last clause is a
claim about kinds of constraint, not about the current state of physics,
so it should be ruled on conditionally rather than by today's theory. A
second reason is that "realized by nothing" for a fundamental law depends
on a metaphysics of laws the record has not chosen, and this objection
assumes one. Which reading of (b) the claim intends is the owner's.
