# THEORY-tmpwf9vz: Griesemer and Shavit propose, as a first and rough sketch, that a collective becomes a new level of individuality only when collaboration toward a shared goal, rather than coordination or cooperation, organizes it, some core interactions have become generatively entrenched, and it has organizational closure, and that such a community can be reproduced only by exporting an organized sample of its roles and interactions, not of its members alone

## Source

Griesemer & Shavit (2023), [LIT-188](../literature.d/LIT-188.md) (*Philosophical Transactions of the
Royal Society B* 378, 20210398), read in full in [NOTE-173](../notes.d/NOTE-173.md) from the Europe
PMC XML, with Table 1 fetched as an image. Section numbers stand in for
pages.

## The proposal

Three grades of interaction, each with its own grade of group (§1, Table
1). Coordination orders co-located members of a *collection*. Cooperation
makes a *conexus*, a web of binary interactions with no shared goal
required, whose benefit can still be booked per member. Collaboration makes
a *community*: heterogeneous multi-way interactions for a shared goal,
whose benefit "cannot even be recognized as group benefits without
reference to the special interactions engendered by the group". Table 1
draws "Transition to a new level-of-individuality perspective" between the
second and third stages only.

Generative entrenchment drives the step from conexus to community: core
interactions come to be ones "later-evolved interactions will depend on
for their existence and character, so that perturbations of it will tend
to be catastrophic" (§2). Community needs organizational closure, taken
from Moreno and Mossio, and "Hence, community constitutes a new level of
individuality" (§3). Following Caporael, a community "must be exported as
an organized generative package larger than a single individual member"
(§3).

## What this does not say

- **That the proposal is right.** The authors call it "not a fully-fledged
  model but only a first and rough sketch". It gives no criteria by which a
  real group could fail to be a community, and "No data were reported".
  That is [THEORY-418](THEORY-418.md)'s promote_when.
- **That real transitions follow the sequence.** It is "one possible
  pathway", and reverse paths are allowed.
- **The abstract-only claims.** It does not say that transitions are
  described without circularity, or that individuality is a special case of
  collectivity ([NOTE-173](../notes.d/NOTE-173.md), corrections).
- **That the Neolithic case shows anything.** It is conditional: a migrant
  group "might require" one of each occupation.
