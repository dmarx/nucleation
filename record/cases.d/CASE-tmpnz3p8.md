# CASE-tmpnz3p8: The compensating evidence problem: two records with the same support graph in which discounting one support leaves odds of 20:1 or of 1.1:1

## The case

Two pieces of evidence support H, conditionally independent, prior odds
1:1, Bayes factor 10 for E₁ in both worlds. E₂ has Bayes factor 20 in
world A and 1.1 in world B. E₁ is found unreliable and discounted. Posterior
odds are 20:1 in A and 1.1:1 in B. "The unweighted graph has the same
topology in both cases."

## What it can show

That which supports exist does not fix how a conclusion should move when
one fails. A12's "independent support survives invalidation" is the same
structure seen from the review side: losing a ground need not defeat the
conclusion, and how much it matters depends on what the other ground
carries.

## What it cannot show

A6: "That graph-based memory is intrinsically insufficient. An attributed
graph could include likelihoods, dependence models, assumptions, and
other relevant information."
