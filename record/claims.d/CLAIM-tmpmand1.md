# CLAIM-tmpmand1: Relata stand to relations as realizations stand to specifications: what an entity is, is partly fixed by the constraints on the configurations of entities and relations it can enter, and those constraints are themselves realized and maintained by something, so neither relata nor relations, nor specifications nor realizations, are prior without qualification

## The claim

The owner, at U8: "maybe relata:relations::realization:specifications/constraints?"
It came after U6, which set the scope ("the project's ambitions are
extremely abstract and unrestricted"), and U7, which found the analogy in
luria ([CLAIM-tmpl228b](CLAIM-tmpl228b.md)). The claim is filed without a domain.

**Two axes, not one opposition.** A8 §1 and A9 §1 corrected the analogy
before using it: relations are not themselves specifications. Relation
*types* are specified and particular relations realized, and entity kinds
are specified and particular entities realized.

| | Specification | Realization |
|---|---|---|
| Entity | the conditions under which something counts as an entity of a kind | a particular entity meeting them |
| Relation | the conditions under which a relation is admissible, and what it commits to | a particular relation holding between particular entities |

In the record, `CLAIM` specifies an entity kind and each claim realizes it;
`rests_on` specifies a relation type and each dependency realizes it. A8:
"The constraints don't necessarily specify the individual entities in
advance. They specify which configurations of entities and relations are
admissible." So the claim's first half: what an entity is, is partly
fixed by its place in a constrained configuration, and "Its identity as a
claim is established within a structure of constraints." The owner's U6
observation that a typed relation is less flexible than its relata
([CLAIM-tmpoe0v5](CLAIM-tmpoe0v5.md)) is the record's own instance of the asymmetry the
analogy rests on.

**The recursion.** The second half is what keeps the frame from being
structure-first. A8 §6: "Those constraints may themselves be historically
constituted, maintained, and transformed through the very processes they
organize", and so "constraints are themselves realizations within larger
relational structures." A9 §2's example is a membrane, which constrains
transport and is itself a structure the transport-dependent chemistry
must produce. The read prior art for this half is Montévil and Mossio
([LIT-582](../literature.d/LIT-582.md), read in [NOTE-468](../notes.d/NOTE-468.md)): a constraint
is an entity that acts on a process while its relevant aspects are
conserved at that process's time scale, and closure is a set of
constraints each depending on and generating another ([TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)). So a
constraint at one time scale is a realization maintained at another, and
A10 §7 states the role-relativity: "Constraint at level n ≈ realized
organization at level n−1". Pattee ([LIT-1190](../literature.d/LIT-1190.md), read in
[NOTE-952](../notes.d/NOTE-952.md)) supplies the case where a specification
is itself material: symbolic constraints are realized in rate-independent
structures and constrain rate-dependent dynamics, and in semantic closure
the reader of the symbols is built from the symbols.

So neither side is prior without qualification. A10 §1: "What something
is depends partly on the constraints governing its possible
relationships, while those constraints depend on something realizing
them." That is how this differs from the two frames A10 set it against: a
substance-first ontology, and "a pure structure-first ontology" that
"risks treating relations as fundamental while leaving unexplained how
structure is physically instantiated."

**Its neighbours in the record.** It is the frame of which two filed
theses are domain cases. [CLAIM-151](CLAIM-151.md) holds that a communicative act is
partly constituted by its relations to roles, norms and uptake, and
[CLAIM-136](CLAIM-136.md) that communicative relata have no identity beyond what their
relations differentiate. [CLAIM-405](CLAIM-405.md) supplies the point that relativity to a
fixed criterion is not arbitrariness, which this claim needs for its
"partly fixed". [CLAIM-195](CLAIM-195.md) is its case inside the record: the constraint
set that a claim's persistence is relative to is "partly constitutive,
since it defines what the objects are", and is fixed by an amendable
decision. Ladyman and Lorenzetti's effective OSR ([LIT-184](../literature.d/LIT-184.md),
read in [NOTE-129](../notes.d/NOTE-129.md)) is the read precedent for
scale-relative structure that is real without one privileged
decomposition, which A9 §10 set beside it; A9's own distinction is that
effective OSR says what to be realist about and this claim says how some
structures come to persist.

## What it does not say

- **Not that a specification produces its realizations.** A9 §1:
  "model theory alone doesn't establish that the specification *causes* its
  models to exist. Mathematical satisfaction is not physical production."
  Goguen and Burstall's theory–model connection ([LIT-tmpoqxdo](../literature.d/LIT-tmpoqxdo.md), unread)
  relates the two "without either being ontologically prior".
- **Not that the relata are eliminated.** It is non-eliminative, as the
  record's structural realism is ([CLAIM-136](CLAIM-136.md); [CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md)'s stronger form).
- **Not that a specification individuates.** A8 §5: two models of one
  specification "need not be numerically identical" even if isomorphic, so
  "relational specifications alone may establish **kinds and admissibility
  conditions** without uniquely determining particular individuals." What
  else individuates (history, continuity, symmetry breaking, primitive
  numerical diversity) is left open, as [CLAIM-136](CLAIM-136.md) leaves it.
- **Not that every constraint is one kind of thing.** A9 §12 listed
  logical, physical, biological, computational, institutional and
  normative constraints, each with its own source of force, and warned that
  "treating every constraint as physically causal in the same way would
  probably erase precisely the distinctions worth preserving." The claim
  is stated over all of them and asserts no reduction among them.
- **Not that every organization maintains its own constraints.** A9 §6
  separated externally imposed, adaptive and organizationally closed
  constraints; only the third is closure, and a thermostat's feedback is
  not it. "Maintained by something" is not "maintained by itself".
- **Not that it is new.** A9 §15 found close precedent for each
  ingredient (Simondon, Montévil and Mossio, Maturana and Varela, Pattee,
  Brandom, Hacking, Searle, Barad, Ladyman and Lorenzetti, Goguen and
  Burstall) and named only the combination, with ontology revision treated
  as re-individuation, as a possible contribution.
- **Not that identification shows it.** That a specification picks out its
  models, or a probe its realizations, does not show that the
  specification constitutes them ([CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md)).

The exchange's own name for the developed position, "relational
constraint realism" (A9 §16, A10 §8), is not adopted here: the owner has
not taken it up.

## Note of 2026-10-27 (later): an unrestricted template, and Bohr as the precedent

Two readings bear on how this claim is unrestricted. Jaksland 2024
([LIT-tmpvh3kt](../literature.d/LIT-tmpvh3kt.md), [NOTE-tmpwiza2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpwiza2.md)) distinguishes a metaphysics applied once to
the one reality and extended in scope, which is Barad's ([THEORY-tmposaze](../theory.d/THEORY-tmposaze.md)),
from a template that each domain instantiates. This claim is the second:
it is stated over every domain and "settled domain by domain". Jaksland
shows what each route costs. Barad's needs ontological features that admit
no approximation ([THEORY-tmprm5rv](../theory.d/THEORY-tmprm5rv.md)); a template needs, in each domain, a
warrant of that domain's own. The record's route is the second, and the
owner's "extremely abstract and unrestricted" (U6 of 2026-10-27) is an
unrestricted template, not a claim that a fundamental ontology settles every
domain.

Faye and Jaksland ([LIT-tmpstodb](../literature.d/LIT-tmpstodb.md), [NOTE-tmpdn71t](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpdn71t.md)) supply the physical
precedent closest to the claim's non-eliminative "partly fixed", and it is
Bohr, not Barad. Bohr's relational holism is epistemic: properties are had
only relative to an experimental arrangement, and "both atomic objects and
instruments are real, and as such they figure as relata in a sentence like
'… is recorded to have position p in relation to …'" ([THEORY-tmpixkwj](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpixkwj.md)).
Barad's phenomena are "relations without preexisting relata" (2003, 815, as
both readings quote it), which is eliminative at the fundamental level.
Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics is a second non-eliminative case:
values relational, systems prior to interactions ([THEORY-tmpggk6i](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpggk6i.md)).
