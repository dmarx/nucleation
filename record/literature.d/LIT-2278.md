summary: >-
  Xue et al. (2026), PAST-Bench: Benchmarking the Foundations of Recursive
  Self-Improvement in Persistent Agents. PAST-Bench tests whether personal
  AI agents (Hermes, OpenClaw and others) get better at later tasks by
  reusing experience retained from earlier ones, without retraining. Each of
  26 task families is a sequence of fresh-session episodes (context wiped
  between them) in which earlier episodes give the agent a chance to save a
  fact, a procedure, a correction or a pre-seeded reference, and later ones
  test reuse; every evaluation episode is run with persistence on and off
  under the same prompt, grader, tools and seed, and controls check
  shortcuts, distractors, stale state and wrong-mechanism reuse. Alongside
  the score gap Δ it reports mechanism evidence (memory writes and reads,
  skill creation and patching, update correctness). Across seven models and
  four frameworks, improvement from retained experience is real but uneven
  by capability, and equal Δs can hide different pathways. Read for the
  exchange "luria experiment design".
