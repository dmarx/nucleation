# THEORY-tmpwjlal: An agent launders its agency, on Rubel, Castro and Pham's account, when, being morally responsible in a domain, it gives a process causal responsibility there, ascribes the process qualities such as neutrality or reliability, and thereby obscures its own causal role and fails to give the account it owes; humans in the loop can be such a process as well as automation

Rubel, Castro and Pham (2021), chapter 7, LIT-tmpnl1ls, read in
NOTE-tmpe43ee; it reproduces their 2019 article, LIT-tmpqui4c, "in large
part".

## The account

The five conditions of p. 143; the wrong, that it undermines "the ability
of others to ask the agent to provide an account" (p. 144). Honest
delegation is not laundering (Democratic Chair). "invoking the idea of a
responsibility gap is a mechanism by which people may launder their
agency" (p. 157), and "humans in the loop can be a means of laundering just
as well as automation itself" (p. 160).

## What it does not show

- **Whether laundering can be inadvertent**: left open (p. 161).
- **That every interposed process launders**: two of the four cases fail
  the conditions.
