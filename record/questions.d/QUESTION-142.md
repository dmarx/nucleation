# QUESTION-142: For unital JB-algebras, is the existence of a dynamical correspondence exactly what makes observables generate symmetries of what they conserve, as Alfsen and Shultz's theorem, quoted by Baez, states?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-160](../theory.d/THEORY-160.md) holds the symmetry–conservation equivalence for a bracket with
unique flows. If the bracket is antisymmetric, "a generates symmetries of b"
holds exactly when "b generates symmetries of a" does. The record
re-derived that from Baez's Theorems 3, 4, 8 and 10. Where observables and
generators are different kinds of thing, the equivalence needs a map
ψ : O → L. Baez quotes Alfsen and Shultz (1998): for unital JB-algebras, a
map with ψ_a(a) = 0 and a second condition exists exactly when O is the
self-adjoint part of a C*-algebra. Until 2026-10-19 [THEORY-160](../theory.d/THEORY-160.md)'s
promote_when offered a checked reading of that theorem as one way to
promote it. At the owner's direction, from the promotion audit's batch 008
([ADR-047](../decisions.d/ADR-047.md)), the THEORY was narrowed to the bracket equivalence, and this
clause moved here.

It is open because the record holds the theorem only as Baez quotes it.

## What would answer it

A checked reading of Alfsen and Shultz (1998), showing that a dynamical
correspondence's condition (A) is exactly what gives the equivalence for
JB-algebras.
