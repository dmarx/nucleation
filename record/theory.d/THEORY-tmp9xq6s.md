# THEORY-tmp9xq6s: Żuradzki argues that where biological evidence and standard theoretical virtues underdetermine competing ontologies of pregnancy and early human life, considered normative judgments may count as evidence for choosing between them, either by weighting which biological features are metaphysically salient or by breaking ties among otherwise defensible frameworks

## The account

Żuradzki argues that where biological evidence and standard theoretical virtues underdetermine competing ontologies of pregnancy and early human life, considered normative judgments may count as evidence for choosing between them, either by weighting which biological features are metaphysically salient or by breaking ties among otherwise defensible frameworks.
