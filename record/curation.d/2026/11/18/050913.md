The owner decided on 2026-11-18 to add a line for the argument filed from
the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse", with the first-line option: "go
ahead with the line, first-line option". Before that, the owner had
corrected the lead's reasoning. A line can be added without moving any
entry out of the lines it carries, so the lead's objection that a line
would cut real links held only against a split. [ADR-tmpkc7xl](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-tmpkc7xl.md) records the
decision. This entry records its first application.

  luria.yaml.

## What the line holds

26 entries:
- the six root questions;
- three questions that refine them;
- fifteen claims;
- two cases.

The line is narrower than the exchange. A member is an entry that argues
one of the line's questions, not every entry the exchange produced. The
work proposed for the exchange's manuscript is filed separately and is
expected to be mostly a subset of the line.
