## Why this is here

A20 put reflexive control at the head of its comparison with regulatory capture: "the target makes a decision through its own decision procedure that nevertheless advances the influencing actor's objective." That is the mechanism [CLAIM-tmpjo2zr](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjo2zr.md) generalizes, control exercised through the conditions of a decision rather than over the decision. The record frames reflexive control as information design through [LIT-1021](LIT-1021.md) and [THEORY-404](../theory.d/THEORY-404.md); [LIT-023](LIT-023.md) (Clark) is the nearest held work on state information operations.
