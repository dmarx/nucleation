# TERM-083: moral standing: being owed moral consideration directly, for one's own sake, and not only for one's effects on others

## Definition

A12: "The status of being owed direct moral consideration, rather than
being considered only because of effects on others." A claim of standing
specifies:

- the **bearer**: a person, an animal, a group, a system;
- the **interests or claims** at issue;
- the **ground** of standing: sentience, agency, having a good of one's
  own, a relation, a status;
- **what follows**: what is owed, and how it weighs against others.

A12: "The grounds and extent of standing remain questions for ethical
argument, not conclusions of the definition." The record's readings show
why the ground must be named: standing graded by degree of sentience
([THEORY-449](../theory.d/THEORY-449.md)); grounds now commonly accepted that, with not unreasonable
premises, would let insects or near-future AI outweigh humanity
([THEORY-554](../theory.d/THEORY-554.md)); adaptive self-maintenance as enough, without sentience, for a
good of one's own ([THEORY-699](../theory.d/THEORY-699.md)).

A12's further distinction: **moral agents**, who can be responsible for
conduct, and **moral patients**, whose interests matter directly. One can
be either without the other.

## What it is not

From A12: not **consciousness** as its sole possible ground ([TERM-082](TERM-082.md));
not **collective agency**; not **legal personhood**; not **equal strength
of every interest**; and not **moral responsibility**, which the record
reads in [LIT-691](../literature.d/LIT-691.md) ([THEORY-118](../theory.d/THEORY-118.md) to [THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md)) and, for corporations,
in [THEORY-139](../theory.d/THEORY-139.md). A12: "the rights of displaced people do not depend on
whether the Chagossian population satisfies a demanding criterion of
unified collective agency" ([CASE-169](../cases.d/CASE-169.md)); A3: "It would be perverse to infer
that the absence of a unified political agent undermines its members'
rights to restitution or participation."
