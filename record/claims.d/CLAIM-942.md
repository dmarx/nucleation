# CLAIM-942: Inquiry and engineering differ by the direction of fit of their predicates, not in kind: inquiry adds predicates its object already passes and engineering imposes predicates its object must be made to pass, so the one difference the record draws between inquiry and specification, that inquiry can revise what an answer could be, recurs inside engineering whenever building shows an imposed requirement cannot be met and it is rewritten to describe what can

## The claim

[CLAIM-470](CLAIM-470.md) holds that inquiry stands to its findings as a specification to
its realizations, with one difference: an inquiry can revise what an answer
could be. [CLAIM-784](CLAIM-784.md) holds that engineering is inquiry whose interventions
make the object they judge, and that it becomes reflexive when building
revises the specification.

Read through [CLAIM-943](CLAIM-943.md), the two are one operation with two directions of fit.
A finding is a predicate its object already passes, added to what is known:
a descriptor, an informative update. A requirement is a predicate its
object does not yet pass, which building must make it pass: an imposition,
a performative update. Inquiry accrues descriptors; engineering enforces
impositions.

The "one difference" then recurs inside engineering. When building shows an
imposed requirement cannot be met, or should not be, the requirement is
rewritten to say what the object can do: an imposition turned into a
descriptor. That is the revision of what an answer could be, met from the
engineering side; the record's own case is its staging branch, whose
specification was rewritten by what its first use showed ([CASE-299](../cases.d/CASE-299.md)). And inquiry has its engineering side: an experiment
imposes conditions on its object before it describes it.

## What it does not say

- Not that the motive does not matter. Inquiry aims at knowing and
  engineering at use; the claim is that the operations are the same two,
  in different proportions.
- Not that the maker's knowledge question ([QUESTION-503](../questions.d/QUESTION-503.md)) is settled. If a
  maker knows which of an artifact's predicates were impositions and which
  descriptors, and an observer sees only that it passes them, this claim
  says what the maker knows that the observer does not: the direction of
  fit, not a further property.

## Note of 2026-11-23: the synthesis review's audit

Audited at the synthesis review of 2026-11-23 ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5) by a reader
briefed neutrally. **Verdict: it adds nothing to its components.** The
distinction by direction of fit is [TERM-151](../terms.d/TERM-151.md)'s definition, applied; that
building can revise a specification, and that inquiry into a made thing
differs from inquiry into a given one by its interventions making the object,
is [CLAIM-784](CLAIM-784.md); that a build's failures are findings is [CLAIM-786](CLAIM-786.md). No sentence
was found that the claim adds and that passes a separating case, a
derivation or a discriminating prediction.

Its example does not fit it. [CASE-299](../cases.d/CASE-299.md) is building finding a requirement no
one had stated, which adds a requirement; it is not an imposed requirement
that could not be met rewritten into a description of what can be. It is an
instance of [CLAIM-784](CLAIM-784.md) and [CLAIM-786](CLAIM-786.md).

**Recommendation, for the owner:** fold the mapping into the record-as-apparatus
account ([SYNTHESIS-114](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-114.md)) and set the claim Superseded, repointing whatever
rests on it to its premises ([CLAIM-943](CLAIM-943.md) names it under `complements`). The
skill would do this itself for a Proposed claim, but this one was filed the
day before under the dialectic regime with adopting it left to the owner, so
the reversal is put to the owner rather than made.
