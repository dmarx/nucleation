# CLAIM-071: Conditional generation shares a mathematical vocabulary with translation, not a mechanism: denoising time is not telephone-game time, and a generative failure is not a proof of sheaf contextuality

## The claim

A97 §2: "It isn't automatically formal sheaf contextuality: diffusion
conditioning failures can arise from imperfect score estimation, compositional
generalization, or sampling." A97 §3: "The diffusion process's internal
denoising time should not be identified with historical telephone-game time.
They are different dynamics."

Manuscript §7: "This provides a concrete compositional mechanism; it does not
guarantee correct joint bindings, and a generative failure is not itself a proof
of sheaf contextuality." "Denoising time, however, is not the same as historical
translation or telephone-game time. Our claim is a shared mathematical
vocabulary of constrained distributions and stochastic transformations, not an
identification of their physical or computational mechanisms."

## What it protects

[CLAIM-123](CLAIM-123.md) and [CLAIM-013](CLAIM-013.md), which use generation as a case, from being read as claims that
generation is translation.
