primary tag is social-science, network-science, compositionality,
neuroscience or philosophy-of-biology, each with a `Read` or `Skimmed`
NOTE and no THEORY citing it as `source`. Each NOTE was read in full; no
paper was fetched.

## What did not enter, and why

- **What Lives? ([LIT-211](../../record/literature.d/LIT-211.md), [NOTE-109](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-109.md)).** Its reading calls it a dataset: 68
  hand-picked definitions of life. Its computed "landscape" is one run of
  an unvalidated LLM pipeline, with silhouette 0.079 at the chosen k. The
  one sound count, that 17 of 68 definitions invoke autonomy or agency,
  describes a curated, unrepresentative sample and comes from a single
  coder. No account of life or of expert opinion can rest on it. It is
  named in the Baedke THEORY's Connections, and it was already named in
  [THEORY-072](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-072.md)'s.

So 24 of 25 readings became sources, which fits the owner's hypothesis
for this group. Three caveats:
- Several of the eleven `Active` THEORYs are findings of narrow scope: a
  dataset, a benchmark, a proved special case.
- Two readings, [LIT-091](../../record/literature.d/LIT-091.md) and [LIT-028](../../record/literature.d/LIT-028.md), had already named their THEORY.
- Two notes said "no THEORY" for reasons this batch did not follow, given
  below.
