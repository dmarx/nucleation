# LIT-tmpnlsfl: Meaning in Life and Why It Matters (The Tanner Lectures on Human Values, Lectures I and II)

Susan Wolf (2007). "Meaning in Life and Why It Matters". The Tanner Lectures on Human Values, Lectures I and II, delivered at Princeton University, 7–8 November 2007. Text on the Tanner Lectures site.

## Key takeaways

- Meaning "arises when subjective attraction meets objective attractiveness", with active engagement (Lecture I).
- Meaningfulness is a third dimension of a good life beside happiness and morality.
- Lecture II admits no positive theory of nonsubjective value and names "a paradox of meaningfulness".
