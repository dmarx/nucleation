# LIT-tmpldtwo: Herbert Marcuse

Arnold Farr (2013, substantive revision 2025). "Herbert Marcuse". *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. With Douglas Kellner's essay "Herbert Marcuse" on his UCLA faculty page, read in part.

## Key takeaways

- "domination no longer requires force or the presence of an authority figure" (§5).
- Kellner: advanced industrial society "created false needs which integrated individuals into the existing system of production and consumption".
