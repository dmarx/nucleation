# LIT-tmpgnf38: Zur Deutung der intuitionistischen Logik

A. Kolmogoroff (1932). "Zur Deutung der intuitionistischen Logik."
*Mathematische Zeitschrift* 35, 58–65. doi:10.1007/BF01186549. English
translation, "On the interpretation of intuitionistic logic", in *Selected
Works of A. N. Kolmogorov*, Vol. I (1991), not consulted here.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-tmpmkrpn](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpmkrpn.md)).*

- Problems are not defined but explained by examples (p. 59); a problem is
  solved, not true, and "für die Aufgaben hat man keinen dieser Richtigkeit
  entsprechenden Begriff" (p. 61, n. 8).
- a ⊃ b is the problem of reducing the solution of b to that of a; ¬a, of
  deriving a contradiction from a supposed solution of a; (x)a(x), of
  giving a general method for every x (pp. 59–60).
- The calculus of problems coincides in form with Heyting's intuitionistic
  propositional logic (pp. 58, 63); a ∨ ¬a is not among its solved
  problems, ¬¬(a ∨ ¬a) is.
- An existence statement splits into an objective element, the problem
  of finding an element, and a subjective one, its solution (p. 65).
