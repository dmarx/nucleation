# LIT-2406: Mechanisms Underlying Influence of Bioelectricity in Development

Laura Faith George and Emily Anne Bates (2022). "Mechanisms Underlying
Influence of Bioelectricity in Development" *Frontiers in Cell and
Developmental Biology 10: 772230, review*. DOI-10.3389/fcell.2022.772230.

## Key takeaways

Bioelectric signals affect development through calcium and conserved
signalling pathways; how they coordinate tissue-level pattern is still open.

## Standing in the record

Registered as a partial stand-in for [LIT-2396](LIT-2396.md), for developmental
bioelectricity and its open mechanisms, not for repair, disease or electric
fields.
