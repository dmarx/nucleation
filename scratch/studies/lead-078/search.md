Two targets:

- **(A) Bundling by self-prediction**: THEORY-067 `promote_when` (1).
  Only whether a single choice is perceived as a precedent for later ones
  changes; the rewards and the choice set stay the same.
- **(B) Discounting as a mediator**: CLAIM-156. Discounting and choice
  under temptation (or response inhibition) are measured before and after
  mindfulness practice, and the analysis asks whether the change in
  discounting carries the change in self-control.
