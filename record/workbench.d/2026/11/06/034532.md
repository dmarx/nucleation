- **Clusters J and K, held entries whose blockers moved.** Of 40, one wait was over: CASE-165, narrowed to the confounds that hold exactly and promoted. The others got notes naming the move checked. Most moves were readings the entry had already taken up, the ADR-070 vocabulary sweep, or the status review's own edits.
