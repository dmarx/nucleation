## Note of 2026-11-06: what holds it back

Beyond the trials the condition asks for, the source's own discrepancy is
open: its abstract says SIMV is the most effective method of weaning after
failed trials and its body, conclusion and trial figures the least. The 2006
correction to Alía and Esteban (2000), doi:10.1186/cc4996, unread, should
settle which the authors hold; reading Esteban et al. (1995) and Brochard et
al. (1994) would settle which the trials show.
