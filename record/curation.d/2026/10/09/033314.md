The owner asked for two sets of works to be registered: the 46 works the
bibliography of What Survives Translation? proposed and the final reference
list dropped, and the prior art the exchange conceded. Registering means
filing a LIT, reading the work and filing a NOTE, with a THEORY where the
reading warrants one. Each work was read on its own merits. Nine readers
worked in parallel by subject, and each subject was committed as it landed.

## How the readings bear on the argument

The coordinator added grounds and notes to the claims of the manuscript's
argument (line `pragmatic-transport`). Several readings change those claims.

- **The conceded prior art is stronger than the exchange knew.** Zhao et
  al. already formalise preserving the posterior over interpretations,
  which is the information bottleneck under KL divergence.
- **The quantum-cognition evidence counts against quantum contextuality.**
  Its strongest result, the QQ equality, counts against reading order
  effects that way, which is what the manuscript claims.
- **The Δ_CF claim needs restating.** It is undefined under signalling and
  cannot rise along free operations. Its premise that the contextual
  fraction is discontinuous is wrong within a scenario.
- **The Noether-type claim is weaker than it reads.** Baez's account puts
  the symmetry–conservation equivalence in an antisymmetric bracket, which a
  Markov kernel lacks.
- **The CbD 2.0 qualification holds, but only for binary observables.**
  The canonical-systems paper shows that the verdict depends on the
  representation.

## Points for the owner

- **Brandom was skimmed from the author's own posted course scans** of the
  whole book. The history note says so. Revert it to Deferred if that
  source is not acceptable.
- **"Frank, Goodman & Tenenbaum 2009" is ambiguous.** The paper registered
  is the word-learning paper. The informative-speaker precursor of rational
  speech acts is Frank, Goodman, Lai and Tenenbaum 2009 (CogSci), which is
  not held.
- **Gogioso and Pinzani's successor** (arXiv 2206.08911) is not held. If it
  is filed, the withdrawn paper becomes Superseded by it.
- **One reader sent the owner's email address to Unpaywall** in a single API
  query. It was not repeated, and the other readers were told not to send it.
