# LIT-2729: Analysing and Assessing Accountability: A Conceptual Framework

Mark Bovens (2007). "Analysing and Assessing Accountability: A Conceptual Framework". *European Law Journal* 13(4): 447–468. DOI-10.1111/j.1468-0386.2007.00378.x.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-2069](../notes.d/NOTE-2069.md)).*

- The definition (p. 450): "a relationship between an actor and a forum, in
  which the actor has an obligation to explain and to justify his or her
  conduct, the forum can pose questions and pass judgement, and the actor
  may face consequences."
- "The possibility of sanctions—not the actual imposition of sanctions—"
  makes the difference (p. 451); answerability is the interrogation stage,
  "the close semantic connection between 'accountability' and
  'answerability'" (p. 451).
- "the forums are not principals of the actors" in many relations, and "the
  consequences are not necessarily brought upon the actor by the forum
  itself" (pp. 451–52).
- Mapping accountability "is a dichotomous exercise that follows the logic
  of either/or" (p. 460); assessing it rests on "a theory, often implicit"
  (p. 467).
