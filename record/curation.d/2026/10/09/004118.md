## The boundary

None of the 22 is held in the Anthology of the SOTA. I grepped a clone of
its record/ taken 2026-10-09 (commit 1cffe8f) for every author, arXiv id and
title. GenEval and T2I-CompBench are named there only in prose inside other
entries, and the rest are not named at all.

The four text-to-image works are machine-learning papers. The anthology's
`generative-modeling` and `analysis-and-evaluation` topics could hold them,
so by default they belong there. They are filed here because the owner asked
for the manuscript's references to be filed in this record. Each carries
`anthology-candidate` last ([ADR-005](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-005.md)), which leaves the move to a later
decision.

They were first tagged with the nearest words this vocabulary had, and the
readings found those words did not fit. `compositionality` now holds GenEval,
T2I-CompBench and Composable Diffusion ([ADR-031](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-031.md)). The conditional
Schrödinger bridge stays under `probabilistic-modeling` and `mathematics`,
and its reader judged those tags defensible. No word for generative
modelling was added. That is the anthology's subject.

The other works fit existing words. Blackwell and Torgersen are the
exception, and they moved to `mathematical-statistics` ([ADR-031](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-031.md)).
`linguistics` is a loose fit for the two translation books, Gutt and
Hofstadter. A `translation` word waits until one of them has been read.

Points the readers raised:

- **Sources worth noting.**
  - Saussure was read in French from Wikisource. That text follows the 1931
    third edition, because no scan of the 1916 edition was reachable.
  - Blackwell was read from page images on Project Euclid.
  - Du Bois was read from his own upload of the chapter, through a Wayback
    Machine capture, because ResearchGate refused the request.
  - Shannon was read from the Harvard posting of the corrected reprint.
- **Corrections to what was first filed from abstracts.**
  - Blackwell 1953 does not contain the whole "more informative iff garbling"
    equivalence. Part is Blackwell 1951 and part is Sherman and Stein. The
    1953 paper extends it to arbitrary outcome spaces and adds the
    two-state result.
  - Saussure says a sign as a whole is "un fait positif". Only signifier and
    signified, taken separately, are purely differential.
  - In Kirby, Cornish and Smith, compositional structure appeared only under
    the experimenter's homonym filter. Without it, the languages collapsed.
  - Composable Diffusion loses to its baseline at composing relations.
- **Claims the papers' own tables contradict.** The NOTEs record these.
  - CDSB: "lowest error consistently" in filtering.
  - T2I-CompBench: GORS's drop on unseen pairs, which the paper calls
    "slightly lower".
- **A finding against an older entry.** [LIT-317](../../record/literature.d/LIT-317.md) credits Shannon's 1949
  paper with the sampling theorem and the Gaussian capacity formula. Both
  are already in the 1948 paper. [NOTE-591](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-591.md) reports this, and [LIT-317](../../record/literature.d/LIT-317.md) is
  left for its own reading to correct.
- **Options for the owner.** Each of these is a separate work that could be
  filed on its own if wanted:
  - Goffman's "Footing" (Semiotica 1979), once a copy is supplied;
  - Lévi-Strauss's "The Structural Study of Myth" (Journal of American
    Folklore 1955);
  - Gutt's 1989 thesis.

## What a reading should check

These entries deliberately say nothing about the manuscript. The owner wants
each work read first on its own merits, so that the reading is not tilted by
the use the owner expects to make of it. So a reading of any of these works
answers what the work itself establishes, and leaves the manuscript out. How
a work serves the manuscript is a later and separate question.

The four text-to-image works have been read, and the move to the anthology
can now be decided on what they are. Each NOTE marks the ML-practice content
in it as anthology material. The CDSB filtering experiments are probabilistic
modelling outside deep learning, and they belong here whichever way the
move goes.
