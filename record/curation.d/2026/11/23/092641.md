## Gaps filled (the assistant's, Proposed)

- **Whether the query–answer relation is a game (A50 §3).** It is
  strategic only when one prepared policy serves several queries through
  shared resources, or when query distributions are restricted
  ([CLAIM-tmp62ldm](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp62ldm.md), [ARG-tmpo1l8y](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpo1l8y.md); checked on a three-query LP).
  [QUESTION-tmpn4we1](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpn4we1.md) holds the non-adversarial forms.
- **What A50's anchor buys.** Measured compliance understates inadequacy
  unless κ is comparable to α+β ([CLAIM-tmp8nzk0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8nzk0.md)).
- **A correction to A50 §6.** With meanings fixed, a new question cannot
  change implications among demands, and a new answer can only remove
  them ([CLAIM-tmpt8pvt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpt8pvt.md)).
- **A50's capability trap** has no model; [LEAD-tmp6gz07](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmp6gz07.md) proposes one.
- **A52's depth optimum.** If F is log-concave, the best depth never
  falls as the deadline lengthens. A bimodal F reverses this
  ([CLAIM-tmpzbytm](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpzbytm.md)).
- **"Cannot be recovered from separate frontiers."**
  - The frontiers give an outer bound and, along one way of posing, a
    sequential inner bound ([CLAIM-tmp4u0mj](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4u0mj.md)).
  - The real reason is the shared budget, not A58's projection argument
    ([CLAIM-tmpj15c6](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpj15c6.md), [ARG-tmp7xi5t](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmp7xi5t.md)). Its construction 4 qualifies the
    inner bound.
- **A54's nuclei model.** Upkeep is paid whether or not a nucleus is
  used. A sigmoidal growth rule gives a threshold and hysteresis
  ([CLAIM-tmpy25jo](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpy25jo.md)).
- **A52's "slow modes amortize"** is narrowed to the economic sense
  ([ARG-tmpzaupx](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpzaupx.md)).
- **Authorization by domain** breaks A56's evidence–permission circle by
  changing the unit of authorization ([CLAIM-tmp3l7is](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3l7is.md)).
- **A60's coverage model.**
  - It presupposes a selector that can tell questions apart, so it
    reverses under parallel attempts and a shared budget, and it reduces
    demand uncertainty to the mean ([CLAIM-tmp7iie0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp7iie0.md), [ARG-tmpbchnj](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpbchnj.md)).
  - A60's "transient variety" is not Zollman's transient diversity
    ([ARG-tmpdnptq](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-tmpdnptq.md)).
  - When diversity tracks richness is open ([QUESTION-tmpzblpf](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpzblpf.md)), with
    readings for it in [LEAD-tmptd829](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmptd829.md).
- **A62b's illustration.** Under its own metric, consistently wrong
  responses cannot be highly diverse ([CLAIM-tmpjk3nm](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjk3nm.md)).
- **A64b's response kernel**, compared directly, is not observational.
  The bisimulation metric repairs it ([CLAIM-tmpfc364](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpfc364.md), objecting to
  [CLAIM-tmpq1usu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq1usu.md)).
- **A62b's test** has its design in [LEAD-tmpps25j](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpps25j.md).
