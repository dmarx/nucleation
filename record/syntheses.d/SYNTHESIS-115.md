# SYNTHESIS-115: In the record, iterated transmission can forget its origin while staying far from it, serial reconstruction is filtered by the transmitters' biases with convergence to the prior proved for sampling learners, arguments restated across memoryless sessions lose identity, an anchor is held to secure what its reference is tied to, re-reading a kept source to settle interpretation on an offset rather than a drift, and exact copies of a revised source to go stale, and what a record can secure is held to be traceability

*A thematic account, across lines, of principle P9 of the owner's manuscript (scratch/manuscripts/from-obstruction-to-reorganization/, §7.1, on its own branch). Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which [ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**Convergence is not preservation.** [CLAIM-270](../claims.d/CLAIM-270.md) (Active, demonstrated): "a chain can forget its initial state while its distribution stays far from where it began". [CLAIM-081](../claims.d/CLAIM-081.md) (Active, interpretive) says the same of transmission, which "is not degradation". [TERM-027](../terms.d/TERM-027.md) (Active): a cultural attractor may be "a shared decoding convention rather than a preserved historical object".

**The transmitters' biases.** [CLAIM-090](../claims.d/CLAIM-090.md) (Proposed, empirical): serial transmission is reconstructive and filtered by the transmitters' inductive biases. [ARG-035](../arguments.d/ARG-035.md) (Proposed) qualifies the support: Griffiths and Kalish's theorem and Kirby, Cornish and Smith's chains "show different things", and convergence to the prior is proved only for sampling learners.

**Local error, global drift.** [CLAIM-056](../claims.d/CLAIM-056.md) (Active, interpretive): the same local error can be damped, accumulated or amplified, and local similarity can coexist with large global drift.

**Arguments in this record.** [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) (Active, empirical): restated across memoryless sessions, the argument repeatedly loses identity. [CLAIM-191](../claims.d/CLAIM-191.md) (Proposed, interpretive) reads that as iterated transport with a weak channel back, drifting toward the collaborators' framings.

**Candidate anchors,** stated separately in the record:

- **exact conservation:** an exactly conserved observable not constant on the start's support keeps information about the start ([CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md), Active, demonstrated, for Markov reconstruction);
- **independent evidence:** evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the interpretation it tests ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md), Active, interpretive);
- **anchored correspondence:** a correspondence anchored to independently measured distinctions and evaluated on other tasks ([CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md), Active, demonstrated, granted);
- **address:** "storage is not anchoring, addressability is" ([CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md), Proposed, empirical).

**Anchoring as one contraction.** [CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md) (Proposed, interpretive), with [ARG-124](../arguments.d/ARG-124.md) (Proposed) as its application: forgetting a source and anchoring to it are one contraction, told apart by what each restatement step conserves exactly. The application "holds for the text and not for its interpretation".

**What an anchor secures.** [CLAIM-790](../claims.d/CLAIM-790.md) (Proposed, interpretive): in any inquiry anchoring is one mechanism, a reconstruction pulled toward a fixed reference, and what it secures is set by what the reference is tied to. Tied to the target by a channel independent of the inquirer, it gives content and lets a discrepancy register. Tied to an earlier state of the inquiry, it gives continuity, not correctness. Tied to nothing, it is bias: [CLAIM-315](../claims.d/CLAIM-315.md) (Proposed) holds that an anchor shifts an estimate "without adding evidence about the target". Of the four candidates above, independent evidence and anchored correspondence are ties to the target; exact conservation and address are anchors to the inquiry's own earlier states. [CLAIM-184](../claims.d/CLAIM-184.md) (Proposed): "anchors entrench".

**Two refinements, stated generally** (both Proposed, objections to [CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md)):

- [CLAIM-793](../claims.d/CLAIM-793.md) (interpretive): where an inquiry keeps its sources and each step reads the stored source, re-reading is conservation on the state enlarged by the source. On that structure an interpretation settles on a bounded offset toward the reader's prior, the same at every reading, which readers who share the prior cannot see. Drift is confined to chains of derived texts.
- [CLAIM-789](../claims.d/CLAIM-789.md) (empirical): where sources are revised, an exact copy is anchored to the version it was taken from and keeps what a revision removed. What anchors a derived text to a revisable source is a tie a revision must pass through, not exactness. [RETRO-004](../retrospectives.d/RETRO-004.md)'s summary is the case.

**What a record can secure.** [CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md) (Proposed, interpretive, a counter to [CLAIM-191](../claims.d/CLAIM-191.md)): "traceability, change that is announced and located, not preservation". [CLAIM-299](../claims.d/CLAIM-299.md) (Active, demonstrated): sufficiency, accountability and correctness come apart. [CLAIM-664](../claims.d/CLAIM-664.md) (Active, demonstrated): the record computes no withdrawals.

## Not stated on the record

- **A stable code is [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s exactly conserved observable, so an address anchors.** The manuscript's P9 offered this reading, and it does not follow. One argument restated is a point mass, which [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md) excludes. A conserved code keeps which item the chain started from, not its content. Address-based anchoring consults stored content, which takes the chain outside [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s Markov hypothesis. The record now holds a narrower form, under which what is conserved is the text a verbatim read copies ([CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md), [ARG-124](../arguments.d/ARG-124.md)); see the notes below.
- **That the four anchors are one requirement.** [SYNTHESIS-066](SYNTHESIS-066.md) declined that join, and [CLAIM-790](../claims.d/CLAIM-790.md) does not make it: it sorts the four by what each ties an inquiry to, and leaves what counts as independent a free parameter of the first kind.

## Note of 2026-11-21: the "Not stated" link, now held in a narrower form

This account put under "Not stated on the record" the reading of a stable code as [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s exactly conserved observable. It gave three reasons: one argument restated is a point mass; a code conserves which item, not its content; and anchoring by address consults stored content, outside [CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)'s Markov hypothesis. Filed since, the record holds a narrower form of the link, Proposed:

- **[CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md):** forgetting a source and anchoring to it are one contraction, told apart by what each restatement step conserves exactly. The record copies content exactly by code *and verbatim reading*, and leaves form to contract onto its schema.
- **[ARG-124](../arguments.d/ARG-124.md):** the conservation results carry over if a session's restatement is a Markov step and reading a coded entry verbatim copies its text exactly. The mapping "holds for the text and not for its interpretation".

So the three reasons still hold against the code alone. The narrower link, that what anchors is the exact copying of an entry's text which the code makes possible, is now an entry of its own. The same night filed a formal account of drift that bears on this theme:

- [CLAIM-773](../claims.d/CLAIM-773.md) and [CLAIM-779](../claims.d/CLAIM-779.md) (drift and retention by the part of a step each feature lies in);
- [CLAIM-777](../claims.d/CLAIM-777.md) (drift before coding as a queue);
- [CLAIM-776](../claims.d/CLAIM-776.md) (the ideas furthest from the collaborators' framings are lost first);
- [CLAIM-782](../claims.d/CLAIM-782.md) (slowing an exchange, against anchoring);
- [CLAIM-768](../claims.d/CLAIM-768.md) (settling does not show what stopped an entry).

All are candidates for this account's components at its next synthesis review, which settles them; none is added here.

## Note of 2026-11-21: anchoring, generalized


Later the same day, in the owner's exchange on anchoring ("we're being
myopic about the literal luria record rather than the broader behavior of
anchoring during a general inquiry"), the record filed three entries
that bear on this account's second "Not stated" item, that the four
anchors are one mechanism. All are Proposed.

- **[CLAIM-790](../claims.d/CLAIM-790.md)** (thesis, interpretive): in any inquiry,
  anchoring is one mechanism, a reconstruction pulled toward a fixed
  reference, and what it secures is set by what the reference is tied to.
  Tied to the target by an independent channel, it secures content and lets
  a discrepancy register. Tied to an earlier state of the inquiry, it
  secures continuity, not correctness. Tied to nothing, it is bias
  ([CLAIM-315](../claims.d/CLAIM-315.md)).
  - It sorts this account's four mechanisms into two of those kinds rather
    than joining them. Independent evidence ([CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)) and anchored
    correspondence ([CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md)) are ties to the target. Exact conservation
    ([CLAIM-635](../claims.d/CLAIM-635.md)) and address ([CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md)) are anchors to the inquiry's
    own earlier states.
  - It takes up [SYNTHESIS-066](SYNTHESIS-066.md)'s refusal and leaves what counts as
    independent as a free parameter, as that review found it.
- **[CLAIM-793](../claims.d/CLAIM-793.md)** (counter to [CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md)): the record anchors
  mainly by having each session read the stored entry, a star around the
  source and not a chain. On that structure an interpretation settles on a
  bounded offset toward the reader's prior. It does not random-walk.
- **[CLAIM-789](../claims.d/CLAIM-789.md)** (counter to [CLAIM-772](../claims.d/CLAIM-772.md)): exact copying anchors a copy
  to the version copied, so it keeps what a revision of its source removed.

So this account's second "Not stated" item stands: the four mechanisms are
not shown to be one. A sorting of them by what each ties an inquiry to is
now a candidate claim. If [CLAIM-790](../claims.d/CLAIM-790.md) is adopted, the account's P9
clause on anchors would read "an anchor secures what it is tied to". That
is the form the manuscript now states. As with the note above, these are
candidates for this account's components at its next synthesis review, and
none is added here.
