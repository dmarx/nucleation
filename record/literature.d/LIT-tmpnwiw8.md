# LIT-tmpnwiw8: The Soviet Theory of Reflexive Control in Historical and Psychocultural Perspective: A Preliminary Study

Chotikul, D. (1986). *The Soviet Theory of Reflexive Control in Historical and Psychocultural Perspective: A Preliminary Study*. Naval Postgraduate School report NPS55-86-013, Monterey, CA. DTIC ADA170613.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmppxn3n](../notes.d/NOTE-tmppxn3n.md). Page numbers are the report's own.*

- **Definitions are Lefebvre's.** The first is "conveying to a partner or
  an opponent specially prepared information to incline him to voluntarily
  make the predetermined decision" (1984 report p. 4, quoted p. 5). The
  second: advantage comes from "an accurate image of the opponent's image of
  the situation", and "above all" from influencing "the opponent's
  perception of the situation or his goals or his doctrine and at the same
  time conceal from him the fact that one "is" influencing him" (Algebra of
  Conflict p. 45, quoted p. 78).
- **The target derives the decision itself.** Control works "by means of
  providing him with the grounds by which he is able to logically derive his
  own decision, but one that is predetermined by the other side" (Lefebvre,
  pp. 83–84). It needs an accurate imitation of the target's reasoning, not
  good reasoning by the target (Chotikul, p. 79).
- **Methods (p. 82, Chotikul's outline of the 1984 report).** Transferring
  false information about the situation, creating a goal, creating the
  doctrine, transferring a decision, and deeper "transformation chains" on
  the target's image of the controller's image, "When the rank of reflection
  ... is raised". The last is "neutralization of an opponent's deductions".
  The modes are cognitive (transforming the target's information processing)
  and informational ("selecting the messages"). The types are constructive
  and destructive, the latter to "destroy, paralyze, or neutralize" the
  target's decision procedures (p. 81).
- **Claims without evidence.** Control "may be conducted effectively
  without a chain of feedback" (p. 81, uncited; the report's Figure 15
  shows a loop with feedback). The "advantage ... lies on the side having
  the highest rank of reflection", with the US at rank 0 and the USSR at 1
  (Lefebvre interview, p. 80).
- **Defences.** Only vigilance and raising one's own rank of reflection.
  The controller guards itself with "non-repeating techniques" and
  randomness (pp. 80, 83).
- **The psychocultural thesis.** Swaddling, projection, the idealized
  leader, *vranyo*, *maskirovka* and risk aversion (pp. 39–77). "it cannot
  be said that they have been proved as presented here" (p. 93).

## Why this is here

For [CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md). Thomas (2004), Lefebvre's books and the 1984 report
could not be reached lawfully (curation entry of 2026-10-17). This
public-release report is the record's access to Lefebvre's definitions and
to an outline of the 1984 report's methods, at second hand. The reading
found three things.

- **It supports the core of [CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md).** Reflexive control works
  through the evidence channel: "selecting the messages", and "purposefully
  influencing the inputs he receives from his environment" (p. 45).
  Concealment of the influence is part of the definition, which separates it
  from Bayesian persuasion ([THEORY-404](../theory.d/THEORY-404.md)).
- **It does not match the claim's ρ/b split.** The methods are indexed by
  which component of the target's decision they replace and at what depth.
  They are not indexed by whether the signal tracks the target's lean. Only
  false situation information is an evidence term, and it is a steering
  push b. Supplying goals and doctrine, the cognitive mode, and destructive
  control act on the interpretation or break the loop. Tracking the target's
  lean appears only as a condition for a deception to be accepted, that it
  "conform to ... his strategic assumptions" (p. 70). The no-feedback claim,
  if it holds, makes the source's control open-loop: selection fixed in
  advance by a model of the target, which is [CLAIM-342](../claims.d/CLAIM-342.md)'s "anyone's model of
  it".
- **For [MOM-CLM-132](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-132.md) and [QUESTION-121](../questions.d/QUESTION-121.md).** The source treats depth (rank of
  reflection) as both weapon and defence and never considers escalation,
  which is the arms race [MOM-CLM-132](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-132.md) names. It offers no structural defence
  except, on the controller's side, unpredictability. It shows [QUESTION-121](../questions.d/QUESTION-121.md)'s
  candidates 1 and 5 as working channels of control, and a fixed doctrine
  (candidate 3) as a target.

It carries no instruction for machine-learning practice.
