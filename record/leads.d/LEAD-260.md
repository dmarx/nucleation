# LEAD-260: Design a memetic nucleation study that varies the seed and the receiving organization separately, withdraws the seed once organization forms, interrupts its endogenous reinforcement, and measures new persistent relations among participants against a threshold-contagion baseline

## The gap

A4 §8: "A possible experimental design would compare populations exposed
to different seeds while controlling for exposure frequency, network
structure and resources", with six interventions:

| Intervention | What it tests (A4) |
|---|---|
| Hold exposure fixed, vary prior organization | Environmental receptivity |
| Hold the environment fixed, vary the seed | Nucleation specificity |
| Remove the seed after formation | Seed dispensability |
| Interrupt endogenous reinforcement | Maintenance provenance |
| Replace participants | Organizational continuity |
| Preserve the symbol but alter its interpretation | Carrier versus concept persistence |

It names no population, no measure of "organization", and no baseline.

## The plan

1. **Baseline.** Fit threshold (complex-contagion) adoption models as the
   transmission rival named in [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md)'s defeater. They already
   produce critical masses and hysteresis, so a threshold or a lasting
   regime after withdrawal is not by itself evidence of nucleation.
2. **Measure.** Take A4's own discriminating variable: "whether exposure
   to a meme produces new, persistent relations among participants,
   rather than merely transmitting content through their existing
   relations". Measure tie formation and persistence, fixed before
   exposure data are seen, beside adoption.
3. **More than one regime.** Nucleation specificity needs seeds that can
   nucleate different organizations; [CLAIM-1310](../claims.d/CLAIM-1310.md) shows that a model
   with one maintained state and a scalar seed cannot express it, so
   the model fitted must have several.
4. **Maintenance provenance.** Intervene on the reinforcing mechanisms,
   not only on state: [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md) holds that recovery after perturbations
   of state "cannot show where maintenance is realized".
5. **A first site.** The record's Juggernaut study ([CASE-100](../cases.d/CASE-100.md), and
   [CASE-410](../cases.d/CASE-410.md), which A4 cites by its code on main) asks whether distributed
   circulation earns explanatory standing ([CLAIM-332](../claims.d/CLAIM-332.md)). A4: "A nucleation
   study would ask an additional question". Check whether its data
   record relations among circulators at all.
6. **Read** the nearest published model, Schweitzer and Andres on group
   formation as a phase transition ([LIT-3224](../literature.d/LIT-3224.md)), for whether its
   nucleation is around a seed.

## What each outcome would show

New persistent relations formed after exposure, beyond what the fitted
threshold model predicts, would favour [CLAIM-1275](../claims.d/CLAIM-1275.md). Adoption patterns
the threshold model fits, with no relational excess, would meet its
defeater.
