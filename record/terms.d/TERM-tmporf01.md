# TERM-tmporf01: frame, as a parameterized family of contexts

## Definition

A49: "Define the pragmatic frame as a parameterized family of admissible
contexts: c_f = Γ(f, c_0), where Γ realizes a pragmatic frame f relative to a
background context c_0." The induced response distribution is
Q_(θ,f,u)(y) = P_θ(y | Γ(f, c_0), u). A pragmatic realization is r = (u, f, θ).

## What it is not

Not the prompt itself (A43), and not the tuple (S, A, K, N, G, H) of A48 §3.1
([TERM-tmpu9tab](TERM-tmpu9tab.md)). The frame is an abstract index, and Γ says how it is realized in a
given context. It is the owner's U17 phrase, "functionally a parameterized
frame" ([CLAIM-tmp98mw2](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp98mw2.md)), made into a definition. It was dropped silently at A52,
which went back to the tuple and "experimentally manipulable contexts".
