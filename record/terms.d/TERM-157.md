# TERM-157: epistemic ecological intelligence: the maintained capacity of an organization of differentiated perspectives to recognize, investigate and answer consequential questions while preserving and developing the diversity of dispositions through which new questions and answers become possible; distinct from epistemic diversity, epistemic richness and adaptive epistemic viability

## Definition

A62b §8: "Epistemic ecological intelligence is the maintained capacity of an organization of differentiated perspectives to recognize, investigate, and answer consequential questions, while preserving and developing the diversity of dispositions through which new questions and answers become possible." "Its key features are differentiation, integration, generativity, and viability." None of them, A62b adds, "requires a singular perspective, a central executive, or a unified phenomenal subject", and the intelligence "remains conditional on resources, histories, environments, and demands".

## What it is not

A62b's closing formulation sets four concepts apart:

- **Epistemic diversity** "concerns the differentiation of interpretive, investigative, and response-producing dispositions".
- **Epistemic richness** "concerns the breadth and developmental potential of the inquiry–answer relations sustained by that differentiation": [TERM-158](TERM-158.md)'s richness, in its epistemic part.
- **Epistemic intelligence** "concerns the capacity to mobilize, coordinate, evaluate, and expand those relations under resource-bounded demands": this term.
- **Adaptive epistemic viability** "concerns the continued maintenance of those capacities, including the capacity to reorganize the epistemic ecology itself". Its formal neighbour is the viability kernel of reorganization readiness, [TERM-167](TERM-167.md), which A62b §8 reuses as V_ep = Viab(M_ep).

The definition and the list differ in one respect. The definition folds maintenance and development into intelligence ("maintained capacity", "preserving and developing"); the list gives maintenance to viability. Read together, intelligence is mobilization and expansion, and the definition's "maintained" says that it lasts only while viability holds.

## Where it sits

It is a candidate. The record holds no adopted definition of intelligence: [THEORY-092](../theory.d/THEORY-092.md) says only what behaviour does not fix, and [THEORY-402](../theory.d/THEORY-402.md) reads collective intelligence as resource-bounded computation. A62b's inquiry relation, F_S(H,B) ⊆ Q × A, is the joint inquiry frontier proposed in the same exchange ([TERM-160](TERM-160.md), [TERM-159](TERM-159.md)), and its "developmental epistemic richness", the family of such relations reachable "through affordable learning", is [CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md)'s developmental frontier carried to that relation. [LEAD-229](../leads.d/LEAD-229.md) would set the term against the definitions of intelligence the record does not yet hold.
