# THEORY-1953: Opposition control cuts rent-seeking

Szakonyi (2025), [LIT-2737](../literature.d/LIT-2737.md).

## The evidence

Difference-in-differences comparisons of 29 opposition-held and other
Moscow councils over 2018–2022, with deputy fixed effects: "roughly 22%
less income" for ruling-party deputies on opposition-held councils (p. 2),
"Roughly 10% more contracts" by auction (p. 13), lower expenditure with
slightly faster revenue growth (p. 14). "holding a majority was critical
to execute oversight" (p. 17).

## What it does not show

- **Hidden rents.** "We see no real change in the incidence of hidden
  assets" (p. 11); reported income mixes legal and illegal earnings.
- **That the opposition could change the regime's rules.** It did not win
  enough seats to pass the municipal filter, and in 2022 it was blocked
  and defrauded.
- **Durability.** The window closed in 2022.
