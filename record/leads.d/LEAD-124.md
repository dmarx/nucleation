## Note of 2026-11-22: what was done

The article could not be fetched: no PMC copy (Europe PMC REST); the APA DOI answered with an Imperva WAF challenge (HTTP 403), and no preprint was found. It stays Unfetched (LIT-2088, dated note). OpenAlex led to the article's OSF project, which holds its coded data and R script and is filed as LIT-tmp6fb6y (NOTE-tmps1muw). It was refitted by task type with the authors' specification (CASE-tmp4f2jr; scratch/studies/lead-124/). The refit reproduces the abstract's slopes and the dissertation's task-type interactions. The dissertation's task-type sections were re-read beside it.

The answer to "decay estimates by skill type": for accuracy, laboratory tasks decline by 0.16 SD a month, real-world tasks by about 0.05, medical and dental by 0.04, and sports and military or transport not measurably. Speed shows the same trend, not significant. Every type declines faster over short intervals than over long ones. Filed: THEORY-tmppzw1a (Proposed), and ARG-tmpbjn5v (Proposed), which finds CLAIM-558 qualified, not defeated.

What remains: QUESTION-433 is not answered, since the data are not independent. The article's own text and moderator tables remain unread; a human reader can likely open the APA copy. Not done: a non-linear model by type, half-loss times by type, and the practice moderator by type. The lead's status and stage are the owner's.
