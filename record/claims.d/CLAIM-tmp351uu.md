# CLAIM-tmp351uu: What the line adds to functional and relevance-theoretic accounts of translation is measured structure beyond a static pragmatic profile: the context-indexed profile, its response to later framings and its decision relevance predict receivers' recovery beyond the similarity of static stance and footing ratings

## The claim

This is the record's reply of 2026-10-10 to [CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md). It is a
proposal, Proposed until the study it names is run.

[CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md) is right that a pass on the central defeat conditions is what
a functional-equivalence account, Gutt's relevance theory ([LIT-784](../literature.d/LIT-784.md), unread)
and plain speech-act pragmatics all predict. So the line's distinctive claim
needs its own thesis and its own test. [CLAIM-023](CLAIM-023.md) says what the claim is: the
contribution is "not that translation is pragmatic or that speech acts change
contexts, but a specified, testable account of which communicative
distinctions a translation preserves". This entry states that account as
something a study could find false.

Three predictors are compared, each for the same renderings and the same
named receivers.

1. **Semantic similarity**: human-rated propositional sameness, and machine
   embedding similarity.
2. **The static pragmatic profile**: human ratings of the rendering's stance
   and footing, each taken in one context, compared with the same ratings of
   the source.
3. **The transport structure**: how the rendering's judged profile varies
   across contexts, how it responds to later framings, and what difference it
   makes to the receivers' decisions. These are the measures of
   [CLAIM-tmpeqvy4](CLAIM-tmpeqvy4.md), [CLAIM-tmpfzcik](CLAIM-tmpfzcik.md) and [CLAIM-tmpz239h](CLAIM-tmpz239h.md).

The thesis is that the third adds to the second, by more than a margin fixed
in advance, in predicting what the receivers recover on held-out renderings.

Why the second is the rival. A functional-equivalence account asks for the
same receptor response in one context; a relevance-theoretic one asks for
interpretive resemblance, the same contextual effects in the context of
reading. Each fixes a profile of the rendering as read once, and the static
stance and footing ratings are that profile made measurable. If the transport
structure adds nothing to them, the line has said in more machinery what
those accounts already say.

The design that tests this is old in the record. [CASE-005](../cases.d/CASE-005.md)'s Experiment 2
chose translations "by maximum semantic similarity, by maximum static
pragmatic similarity and by maximum trajectory similarity", and [CASE-005](../cases.d/CASE-005.md)
records that "the three-way selection design is gone by A52". [CLAIM-083](CLAIM-083.md)
(Superseded) noticed the consequence: the manuscript's criterion "is weighed
against embedding similarity, not against static pragmatic similarity". The
discriminating study ([CASE-tmp1uzfn](../cases.d/CASE-tmp1uzfn.md)) restores the three-way comparison, with
human receivers in place of a model judge, and its arm (c) supplies the
renderings this condition needs: matched to the source on static stance and
footing, different in response to later framings.

## What it answers

[CLAIM-tmphn6za](CLAIM-tmphn6za.md)'s main point: the stated tests cannot tell the line from the
accounts [CLAIM-023](CLAIM-023.md) says it must be told apart from. This entry gives the line
a test that can. [CLAIM-tmpc1o4m](CLAIM-tmpc1o4m.md) keeps the central thesis's own test, which a
functionalist would also pass.

## What it does not say

- It does not say Nida, House, Reiss and Vermeer, Nord or Gutt are wrong.
  None of them is read here: the first four are absent from the record, and
  Gutt is held unread ([CLAIM-023](CLAIM-023.md)). It says only what result would show the
  line adds nothing to them, on the reading of them given above, which is the
  record's and not theirs.
- It does not say the static profile is useless. If the thesis fails, the
  static profile is what predicts recovery, and that is a finding for the
  functional accounts.
- It does not fix the margin. That is the owner's, in the preregistration.
