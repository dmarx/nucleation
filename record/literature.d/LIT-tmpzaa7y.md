# LIT-tmpzaa7y: How to Do Things with Words

J. L. Austin (1962), Oxford: Clarendon Press, 166 pp. — the William James
Lectures at Harvard, 1955 — https://lccn.loc.gov/63000359

## Key takeaways

*Filed, not read*: no lawful full text was reachable (see the history
note). Known here only by its standard description: Austin
begins from performatives ("I name this ship…"), which are not true or false
but happy or unhappy under felicity conditions, and ends by dissolving the
performative/constative distinction into a general theory of the locutionary,
illocutionary and perlocutionary acts every utterance performs.
