Three further readiness links were found already stated, in CASE-200,
CLAIM-572 and TERM-112.

**CLAIM-tmpmrnnq's derivation.** CLAIM-524's network computes u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ with
resource ½ Σ(uᵢ + vᵢ). By AM–GM the resource is at least √(1 − p) + √p,
where p = u₂v₂, and that bound peaks at √2 when p = ½. A budget below √2
therefore splits the zero-loss set into a piece near p = 0 and a piece
near p = 1. Swapping the paths sends p to 1 − p and keeps the resource.
Each piece retracts to its endpoint without the resource rising: first
along a hyperbola to its balanced point, then along balanced points. No
entry names the swap, the orbit or the exchange. CLAIM-263's step 1
quotients the full symmetry group. The claim is Proposed, and its
wording is the assistant's.

**Arm A.** No claim on either line only maps its components. CLAIM-600,
CLAIM-632 and CLAIM-620 have "Admission" sections, and their premises have
not changed in content. CLAIM-556 adds an ordering and two repairs. Its
regeneration repair is close to a free parameter, which is flagged for
the owner.

**CLAIM-600's admission case meets its own defeater.** The village water
scheme in its "Admission" section was offered as a stipulated case
separating the claim from its premises. It could plainly occur. As
written, it meets the first clause of CLAIM-600's `defeated_if`: every
condition is judged met before withdrawal, and the scheme declines within
one turnover. When CLAIM-600 was an admitted synthesis, the case showed only
separation. Since ADR-084 it is a claim again, and the skill's step for a
realistic separating case applies: the case is possible evidence against
the claim. SYNTHESIS-tmprhzl8 lists it under "Not stated on the record".
The claim is unchanged, and its status is for the owner.
