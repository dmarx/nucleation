Batch C of the reading queue that the sweep of the Unfetched works produced
(curation 2026/11/22/104609, PR #644): LIT-2447, LIT-2328 and LIT-536. One
reader wrote the reports (`scratch/readers/` on `claude/readers/sweep-c`),
and the filer checked each against the downloaded copy before filing, as a
claim to verify, not a finding. Batches A and B are curation
2026/11/22/113444.

Readers read three works each in sequence, not one reader per work in parallel, to stay under the account's usage limit.

## How each work was read

- **LIT-2447** (Ivern and Carbonell 2026): read in full from the Springer
  open-access PDF. Springer's first answer was a 303 redirect to its
  ordinary cookie handshake, not a challenge; following it with a cookie
  jar returned the PDF. Table 1 skimmed; the references and Supplementary
  File 1 not read.
- **LIT-2328** (Georgiev, Gómez-Serrano, Tao and Wagner, PNAS 2026): **not
  read**. The sweep's "open copy" at arXiv, 2511.02864, is the authors'
  preprint, held as LIT-2474; the PNAS copy had challenged the client and
  was not retried, and PMC's is embargoed to 2027-03-30. The reader read
  §§1–5 of the preprint again and §6, which NOTE-1825 had skipped, by a
  script over the outcome sentences of all problems plus spot reads of
  seven write-ups; proofs, tables and figures were skipped.
- **LIT-536** (Moreno and Mossio, Biological Autonomy): **unchanged**. The
  HAL PDF is the Introduction deposit NOTE-1437 already read, with no
  chapter. **Two neighbours read in its place**, both by the authors, both
  on HAL, neither held before (no hit for the HAL ids, the DOI or the
  titles): Mossio and Moreno 2010 (halshs-00792436, read in full) and Bich,
  Mossio, Ruiz-Mirazo and Moreno 2016 (hal-01185296, read in full, §§4 and
  6 at their conclusions).

Every quote that a title, a status note or a dated note leans on was
rechecked against the copy's text, and all matched. The reader's page counts
for the HAL deposits were corrected (16 and 28 pages).
