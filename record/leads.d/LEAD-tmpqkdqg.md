# LEAD-tmpqkdqg: Read the psychology of persuasion, and of influence under load

## Read

*Dual-process persuasion*
- **Petty and Cacioppo (1986)**, *Communication and Persuasion: Central and
  Peripheral Routes to Attitude Change*. The elaboration likelihood model.
- **Chaiken (1980)**, "Heuristic versus systematic information processing and
  the use of source versus message cues in persuasion", JPSP.
- **Petty, Wells and Brock (1976)**, on distraction and counter-arguing, JPSP.

*The cues*
- **Cialdini**, *Influence*. The cue families: reciprocity, commitment,
  social proof, authority, liking, scarcity.

*Belief and choice under load*
- **Gilbert, Tafarodi and Malone (1993)**, "You can't not believe everything
  you read", JPSP. Load makes people accept statements they would otherwise
  reject.
- **Shiv and Fedorikhin (1999)**, on load and impulsive choice, JCR.

*Scarcity as load*
- **Mani, Mullainathan, Shafir and Zhao (2013)**, "Poverty impedes cognitive
  function", Science. Read it with its replication record.

*Malign influence*
- **Paul and Matthews (2016)**, "The Russian 'Firehose of Falsehood'
  Propaganda Model", RAND.

## What to look for

- Whether load moves people from argument to cue with argument quality held
  fixed. This is CLAIM-tmp3e6nd's test.
- Whether a defence that restores capacity reduces the effect.
- What the scarcity results look like after replication. CLAIM-tmpu99qr leans
  on them.
