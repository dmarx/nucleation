# TERM-tmp8spbn: provenance, of a capacity or an organization, and which sense: formation provenance (whose process produced it), maintenance provenance (whose process keeps it), or source provenance (TERM-084's sense 1: what it was made from)

## Definition

A claim about where a capacity or an organization comes from says which of
these it means.

1. **Formation provenance.** Whose process produced it: the agent's own
   activity, as in self-formation (TERM-tmp8cp5q), or another's, as in
   installation or reflexive control. A fact about the history of its
   production.
2. **Maintenance provenance.** Whose process keeps it in being now: the
   agent's own productive cycle (CLAIM-516) or an outside supply. A fact
   about the present organization.
3. **Source provenance.** What it was made from, a single transport from a
   given source with a fidelity to it. TERM-084's sense 1, kept there.

| sense | when | what can test it |
|---|---|---|
| formation | the history of production | an intervention on the producing process while it runs, or a record kept then (CASE-tmph9iwi) |
| maintenance | the present | knockouts of the processes that produce and uphold it (CLAIM-522) |
| source | the history of a transport | a fidelity profile against the source (TERM-043) |

The first two come apart. CASE-tmph9iwi builds a capacity installed by an
outside pulse and then maintained by the agent's own cycle: its maintenance
provenance is the agent's, its formation provenance is not, and after
formation no knockout tells it from a self-formed one.

## Why formation provenance matters

These entries turn on formation provenance, not only on maintenance:

- **Responsibility and free agency.** A condition stated in present
  structure is met by a manipulated agent (THEORY-040, THEORY-1118), so it
  needs a historical condition; historical ownership by taking responsibility
  (THEORY-121); Kane's ultimate responsibility (CLAIM-022's note of
  2026-11-02); and the warning that a historical condition may not separate
  the manipulated agent from an ordinarily caused one (THEORY-328).
- **Whose the will is.** CLAIM-022, QUESTION-011, QUESTION-071 and
  QUESTION-364.
- **Capture and domination.** Reflexive control by a one-time perturbation
  (CLAIM-404, CASE-123, TERM-066), and another's standing capacity to govern
  one's governing relations (CLAIM-tmpxpdqi's last clause).
- **Autonomy as psychology measures it.** The history of internalization
  (THEORY-047), which would have to show in a regulation's relations to the
  rest (THEORY-054).
- **Self-governance by history.** CASE-128's voluntary delegation, which
  differs from an imposed restriction only in how it came about.
- **Persistence and continuation.** CLAIM-329, QUESTION-118 and
  CLAIM-tmpxes2o.
- **Self-formation itself.** CLAIM-tmpbeh2e.

And one entry limits it: CLAIM-356, on which exogenous origin does not by
itself disqualify a goal.

## What it is not

- **Lineage or causal descent** (TERM-084's senses 2 and 4), which say how a
  later realization stands to an earlier one, not whose process made a
  capacity.
- **Historical persistence** (TERM-071), which says what kept an organization
  going through time, the maintenance sense extended backwards.
- **Authorship of an act.** Formation provenance is of a capacity. Whose a
  particular decision was is a further question.

## What it leaves open

What a record of formation provenance would have to contain, which is
QUESTION-071's part 2. And whether formation provenance matters in itself, or
only as evidence about something present the record has not yet located,
which is the dispute between THEORY-040 and THEORY-328.
