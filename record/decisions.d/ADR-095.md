# ADR-095: A case or a term that uses a reading other than as its source carries `draws_on` to it, and the reading's `drawn_on_by` is derived; how a case uses it is its kind's to say, and a term draws on the readings its sense is fixed by

## Context

A case's relations reach claims (`illustrates`, `counterexample_to`),
questions (`bears_on`, [ADR-092](ADR-092.md)), other cases, and its source
(`reported_in`, provenance, [ADR-051](ADR-051.md)). A term's reach other terms
(`refines`, `rivals`, `complements`) and the claims that use it. Neither
reaches a reading for any use but provenance.

The reading reviews of 2026-11-11 met the gap. [CASE-165](../cases.d/CASE-165.md) checks itself
against nine readings; [CASE-185](../cases.d/CASE-185.md) takes the symmetry results of three as
the threat to its identification arm; [CASE-189](../cases.d/CASE-189.md) uses several
thermodynamic readings in its reading of its result; [TERM-057](../terms.d/TERM-057.md) and
[TERM-058](../terms.d/TERM-058.md) fix their senses against five information-theory readings.
[ADR-093](ADR-093.md) (point 5) had a review hold such readings as used in fact and list
them by name, and said `reported_in` is not to be stretched to cover the
use. On 2026-11-11 [ADR-094](ADR-094.md) added `bounds` for a claim's limits; the
owner asked for cases and terms to follow as a decision of their own.

A count on 2026-11-12 found 309 citations of a reading in force in the
body of a case (156) or a term (153), not its `reported_in`, across 119
cases and terms and 227 readings, 21 of them among the readings the view
counted unused. Some are attributions or passing mentions, which take no
edge ([ADR-058](ADR-058.md)).

## Decision

1. **CASE carries `draws_on`, to THEORY.** A case draws on a reading it
   uses other than as where it comes from: a proposed study's method,
   measure, design constraint or threat to inference; a check its result
   is set against; a result a construction is built from or applies. The
   case's `kind` ([ADR-042](ADR-042.md)) says which, so one relation serves, as one
   `bears_on` serves every kind of case ([ADR-092](ADR-092.md)).
2. **TERM carries `draws_on`, to THEORY.** A term draws on a reading its
   sense is fixed by: the reading's usage it adopts, or the usage its
   definition is set against.
3. **`drawn_on_by` on THEORY is derived** from both, never stored.
4. **Not provenance, not support, not attribution.** `reported_in` stays
   provenance, and a case may carry both to one reading only where it does
   both. A reading cited only as where a word or case was first named, or
   in passing, takes no edge ([ADR-058](ADR-058.md)). A case that is evidence for a
   claim reaches the claim (`illustrates`, `counterexample_to`), not the
   reading. The first application settled the near cases:
   - **A reading of a work the case already reports** is provenance where
     the case cites it for what the work reports about this case, and
     `draws_on` where the case uses the work's general result (a
     classification, a threshold, a model).
   - **A contrast draws on the reading when it does work**: it sets the
     case's design ("the null to beat", a feedback the case's protocol
     differs from) or corrects the case's reading of its result. A
     contrast that does nothing in the case takes no edge.
   - **A reading whose conditions limit what the case can infer** is a
     threat to inference, `draws_on`. A reading whose stronger thesis the
     case only says it cannot show takes no edge.
   - **A check the case plans** counts as much as one it has run.
   - **For a term, a "what it is not" line that sets the definition
     against a reading's usage** draws on it; a homonym, a placement of
     the reading on the term's scale, or an instance of what the term
     names takes no edge unless the term's account is built from it.
5. **The readings view counts it as use** ([ADR-090](ADR-090.md)): a reading a case or
   term draws on serves that entry's first line.
6. **Syntheses are not covered.** An account that names a reading still
   has no relation to it; that waits until a case for one recurs.

The reading-review and read-and-file skills carry 1 to 4 as instructions.

## Alternatives considered

- **A relation per use** (`measures_with`, `constrained_by`,
  `checked_against`, `built_from`). Each restates a kind, a case whose
  kind and standing cross would need two relations for one fact, and the
  view and reviews would have to merge them back. [ADR-092](ADR-092.md) rejected the
  same split for a case's bearing on a question.
- **Stretching `reported_in`.** [ADR-051](ADR-051.md) and [ADR-093](ADR-093.md) keep it provenance: a
  proposed study is reported in no reading it takes a constraint from.
- **Leaving it to the reviews' accounts**, as [ADR-093](ADR-093.md) did. The use stays
  invisible on the case, the term and the reading, and each review
  re-finds it.
- **Covering syntheses now.** An account maps the record's own argument
  ([ADR-084](ADR-084.md)); the readings it names reach it through the claims it maps.
  No synthesis was found that uses a reading no claim it maps uses.

## Consequences

- A reading's page lists the cases and terms that draw on it; a case's and
  a term's pages list what they use.
- The view's unused list loses the readings used only by cases and terms.
- [ADR-093](ADR-093.md)'s point 5 now holds only for syntheses; the reading-review skill
  says so.
- The demonstration ([ADR-071](ADR-071.md)) writes `draws_on` where a case's or term's
  own text uses a reading, each with the entry's words quoted.
