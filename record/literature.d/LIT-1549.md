# LIT-1549: Causal Scrubbing: a method for rigorously testing interpretability hypotheses

Chan, L., Garriga-Alonso, A., Goldowsky-Dill, N., Greenblatt, R.,
Nitishinskaya, J., Radhakrishnan, A., Shlegeris, B. & Thomas, N. (2022).
Causal scrubbing: a method for rigorously testing interpretability
hypotheses. *AI Alignment Forum*, 3 December 2022.

Read in full on 2026-10-26 ([NOTE-1168](../notes.d/NOTE-1168.md)); the companion posts were not
read. One account was drawn from it: [THEORY-978](../theory.d/THEORY-978.md).

## Why this is here

The owner asked on 2026-10-26 how the record stood on neural circuit
theory; the answer found it held almost none of the core circuits
literature, and the owner said "go ahead and register and read those".
This is the method by which a circuit hypothesis is tested rather than
illustrated, and the source of the resampling-ablation convention that
ACDC ([LIT-1547](LIT-1547.md)) and AtP* ([LIT-1531](LIT-1531.md)) use. The record's circuits
line held induction heads ([LIT-1538](LIT-1538.md)), grokking ([LIT-345](LIT-345.md)) and the
causal-abstraction readings ([LIT-961](LIT-961.md), [LIT-968](LIT-968.md), [LIT-1310](LIT-1310.md)), but no account of
how a stated circuit is checked. It bears on [QUESTION-234](../questions.d/QUESTION-234.md) and [THEORY-802](../theory.d/THEORY-802.md):
its score depends only on the partitions a hypothesis induces, so
basis-related hypotheses can tie by construction.

## Note of 2026-10-27: the companion posts, now held

The status note's "detailed in companion posts the record has not read"
and the body's "the companion posts were not read" were true when
written. The three posts were registered and read in full on 2026-10-27,
at the owner's request, each as its own work extending this one: the paren
balance checker results ([LIT-1580](LIT-1580.md), [NOTE-1195](../notes.d/NOTE-1195.md)), the induction
results ([LIT-1579](LIT-1579.md), [NOTE-1196](../notes.d/NOTE-1196.md)) and the appendix ([LIT-1583](LIT-1583.md),
[NOTE-1202](../notes.d/NOTE-1202.md)). They are separate posts with their own addresses, and the
two results posts add an author (Tao Lin).

Two corrections to how the summary reads, from those posts:

- **The two sets of figures are on different scales.** The appendix says
  both results posts use the randomized-loss baseline, but they do not do
  so alike. The paren figures (93% down to 72%) are fractions of a
  4.30-nat gap to a label-shuffled baseline; the induction figures (35% and
  86%) are fractions of the 0.053 nats the two induction heads contribute
  on induction-favouring tokens. Set side by side, as in the summary, they
  are not comparable.
- **"86% refined" is hypothesis 4.** The induction post's own headline,
  89%, is a later bonus hypothesis with a different rule for the
  previous-token head.

The measured results are [THEORY-1029](../theory.d/THEORY-1029.md) (paren) and [THEORY-1020](../theory.d/THEORY-1020.md)
(induction), and the appendix's maximum-entropy theorem is [THEORY-1025](../theory.d/THEORY-1025.md).
