# LIT-2462: Tolerance to non-inherited maternal antigen is sustained by LysM+ CD11c+ maternal microchimeric cells

Yanyan Peng, Giang Pham, Jiahui Sun, Ujjwal Adhikari, Sudeep Kumar, Tzu-Yu
Shao, Jeremy M. Kinder, Lucien H. Turner and Sing Sing Way (2025).
"Tolerance to non-inherited maternal antigen is sustained by LysM+ CD11c+
maternal microchimeric cells." *Immunity* 58(9): 2176–2189.e3.
DOI-10.1016/j.immuni.2025.08.005.

## Key takeaways

Read (NOTE-tmpeyhkz). A conditional, cell-lineage-specific depletion platform
lets defined maternal microchimeric cell types be removed in offspring on
demand. NIMA-specific tolerance (FOXP3+ Treg expansion; cross-generational
resiliency against fetal wastage) is carried by LysM+ CD11c+ Vav1+ maternal
leukocytes, and removing that subset erases the tolerance while leaving overall
maternal microchimerism intact. Presence and tolerogenic function of
foreign-origin cells thus come apart by intervention.
