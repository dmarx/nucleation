Evgeny Morozov (2013). "To Save Everything, Click Here: The Folly of Technological Solutionism". Talk and transcript, Carnegie Council for Ethics in International Affairs, 12 April 2013.

## Key takeaways

- Declining self-tracking is "no longer an autonomous decision" once institutions penalize it.
- Reform should aim "to reform the system itself".
