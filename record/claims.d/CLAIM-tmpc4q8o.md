# CLAIM-tmpc4q8o: A series of blog posts can be laid out as a route through the space of hyperarticles over the record, chosen as an orbit or as a geodesic

## The claim

The owner, U28: "what are some interesting orbits or geodesics through
hyperarticle space we might consider for e.g. series of blog posts". The
request presupposes that the hyperarticles over the record (TERM-tmpjn06u)
form a space with an atlas's geometry (CLAIM-tmpxkeu9), so that a series
of posts is a path through it, and that some paths, orbits and geodesics,
are worth choosing.

A61's reading: "The geodesic is the reader's journey; the hyperarticles
are the durable intellectual terrain it crosses. That distinction lets us
publish a continuing stream of interesting stories without turning each
blog post into another independently maintained fragment of the theory."
A series is then a view of views, maintained by its anchors and not as a
further copy of the argument (CLAIM-178).

## A61's routes, and their anchors

A61 proposes four geodesics ("The Knowledge Graph That Lied" from
CASE-131 through CLAIM-469, CLAIM-419 and CLAIM-470 to CASE-084; "One
Complaint, Two Correct Classifications" through CASE-132, TERM-064 and
CLAIM-523; "What Makes Something the Same Thing?" from the proposed study
CASE-223; "More Resources Don't Always Mean Finer Categories" from
CLAIM-387 to CLAIM-470) and four orbits. The codes exist and say what the
routes use them for; A61 marks CASE-223 as "a proposed study, not an
established empirical finding", correctly. The record's nearest existing
series is the work governance-of-becoming, a series of interrelated
articles in an order the owner took on 2026-11-25.
