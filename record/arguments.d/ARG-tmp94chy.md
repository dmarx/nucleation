# ARG-tmp94chy: SURD's definitions, as evidence, show an information decomposition that its authors call causality measuring predictive information about a target's future and not a flow, and its "energy cascade of causality" reading a prediction pattern as transport, the slide the claim on transfer entropy warns against

## The inference

CLAIM-335: transfer entropy "measures directed predictive improvement, not
information flow", being unique information plus synergy with the
target's past. SURD (eq. 1, p. 3) splits H(Q⁺ⱼ), the entropy of a target's
future, into redundant, unique and synergistic parts of I(Q⁺ⱼ; Q) plus a
leak; its assumptions (p. 12) are that "causal relationships emerge as a
result of transitional probabilities between states" under stationarity.
On the turbulence fluxes, its unique terms "vividly capture the forward
energy cascade of causality", read as "no new information is conveyed from
the smaller scales to the larger scales".

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the claim were false?** If such
  decompositions measured a flow, one would expect a conservation or a
  transport identity among the terms. There is none: the terms are
  defined from conditional distributions of states, and a measurement of
  the physical transfer is the flux itself, Σ̃ = τᵢⱼS̄ᵢⱼ, which the paper
  takes as given and computes separately.
- **Does it tell the claim apart from its rivals?** Only for the "not
  flow" half. SURD is not transfer entropy, so it says nothing of the
  claim's composition of transfer entropy as unique information plus
  synergy with the target's past; the paper notes only that multivariate
  conditional transfer entropy "may yield negative values".
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** That is the point: the
  paper's own words read the prediction pattern as transport. Its success
  criterion on benchmarks is agreement "with the functional dependencies of
  the system", which is a check of dependence, not of flow.

## Verdict

Supports CLAIM-335's first half by a sibling measure, and shows the slide
from prediction to flow it warns against; nothing on the composition of
transfer entropy. Proposed.
