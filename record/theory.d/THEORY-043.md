# THEORY-043: A mind of mindless agents and a mind of minded agents are one functionalist move run in two directions, and what separates them is an anti-nesting principle or an architectural criterion, neither yet principled

## Source

- Schwitzgebel (2014), [LIT-159](../literature.d/LIT-159.md), read in [NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md): the composition argument.
- Minsky (1977), [LIT-399](../literature.d/LIT-399.md), read in [NOTE-344](../notes.d/NOTE-344.md): the earliest reading
  here of the decomposition, with K-lines ([LIT-402](../literature.d/LIT-402.md)) and Singh's
  exposition ([LIT-444](../literature.d/LIT-444.md)). *The Society of Mind* itself ([LIT-429](../literature.d/LIT-429.md)) is
  unread, so this account does not lean on it.
- Block (1978), [LIT-407](../literature.d/LIT-407.md), read in [NOTE-366](../notes.d/NOTE-366.md): the China-brain and the
  first critique of anti-nesting.
- List (2016), [LIT-401](../literature.d/LIT-401.md), read in [NOTE-340](../notes.d/NOTE-340.md): group agency granted,
  group experience denied.
- Kammerer (2015), [LIT-409](../literature.d/LIT-409.md), read in [NOTE-362](../notes.d/NOTE-362.md): the functional
  anti-nesting principle.
- Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi (2014), [LIT-445](../literature.d/LIT-445.md), read in [NOTE-365](../notes.d/NOTE-365.md):
  the exclusion postulate in formal statement.

## The two directions

**Downward.** Minsky's 1977 memo, [LIT-399](../literature.d/LIT-399.md), takes as background (its C1,
asserted rather than argued) that a mind is a society of simple agents whose
interactions produce intellect and affect alike. The move is older than
Minsky. Selfridge's Pandemonium ([LIT-427](../literature.d/LIT-427.md)) assembles a competence the
whole has, recognising a pattern, from demons none of which has it. Brooks
([LIT-436](../literature.d/LIT-436.md)) builds and runs it: a robot with no central representation,
control or goals, whose coherence, he says, is imputed by an observer (his
C4). He cites Minsky as giving "a similar account". The parts are never
minded, so nothing nests.

**Upward.** Schwitzgebel, [LIT-159](../literature.d/LIT-159.md), runs the same functionalist reasoning from
the other end. If what makes a brain conscious is its organisation, then
contiguity and the mindedness of parts are not disqualifying ([NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md),
C2–C3), and the United States has the materialist marks in some degree
(C7). Here the parts are minds, so the whole nests minds inside a candidate
mind.

The functionalist premise is the same both ways: the whole's mental
properties come from how parts are organised, not from what the parts are.
Hofstadter's "Ant Fugue" in GEB ([LIT-437](../literature.d/LIT-437.md), unread here) is the meeting
point, a colony whose ants are mindless and whose organisation is a mind.

## What the readings agree on: agency survives both directions

- **Block** grants that a homunculi-headed system could have beliefs and
  other non-qualitative states ([NOTE-366](../notes.d/NOTE-366.md), C6). His doubt is about
  qualia only.
- **List** grants that organised groups are agents in a thin functionalist
  sense and are aware in the access sense ([NOTE-340](../notes.d/NOTE-340.md), C1–C2). He says the
  question of group experience then depends wholly on which bridge
  principle is true (C3).
- **Levin** ([LIT-439](../literature.d/LIT-439.md), [NOTE-341](../notes.d/NOTE-341.md)) finds goal-pursuing Selves nested at
  every biological scale (C2), and proposes that human groups may have
  their own cognition (C6). Both claims are about agency; his extension to
  experience is labelled speculation (C8).

So the decomposition and the composition are not in dispute as accounts of
*agency*. The dispute is over experience, and it is there that someone who
accepts Minsky's downward move must find a reason to refuse Schwitzgebel's
upward one.

## The two ways to refuse the upward move

**1. Anti-nesting: a conscious whole cannot have conscious parts.**

- *Putnam* ([LIT-396](../literature.d/LIT-396.md)) introduces it by stipulation, with one stated
  motive: to keep a swarm of bees from counting as a single pain-feeler.
  Block shows it ad hoc. Each of three readings he tries fails on a case,
  including people the size of elementary particles who take over a body's
  microphysics ([NOTE-366](../notes.d/NOTE-366.md), C4).
- *IIT* states it formally. Of all overlapping sets of elements, only the
  local maximum of Φ is conscious ([NOTE-365](../notes.d/NOTE-365.md), C2). That is a postulate,
  derived from an axiom that experience has definite borders, with no
  argument specific to the system level. Schwitzgebel's reductio, that a
  single ballot could switch a voter's consciousness off ([NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md), C5), is
  not answered in the paper read.
- *Kammerer* moves the line from "are the parts conscious?" to "does the
  whole depend on parts consciously representing the whole, in a role
  nothing else could fill?" That keeps Minsky's agents, workspace modules
  and Block's tiny people on the permitted side ([NOTE-362](../notes.d/NOTE-362.md), C1). But the
  verdict that excludes the United States rests on an asserted
  counterfactual about what it would lose without its citizens'
  representations (C2), and the principle's own justification is weak by
  its author's account (C6).

**2. Architecture: the whole is organised the wrong way.**

- *Block's* intuition is not about nesting at all. His doubt survives
  replacing every homunculus with a mindless gate ([NOTE-366](../notes.d/NOTE-366.md), C5), and
  rests on the system being unlike us and built to mimic us (C3), which he
  calls "not an overwhelmingly powerful argument".
- *IIT* gives an architectural verdict too: a feed-forward system can match
  a conscious one's behaviour without being conscious ([NOTE-365](../notes.d/NOTE-365.md), C7).
  Modular networks split into small complexes (C5).
- *List* argues that a typical group agent has low Φ because it is made of
  self-contained units ([NOTE-340](../notes.d/NOTE-340.md), C5). That is asserted, not computed.

The architectural route has a cost the anti-nesting route does not. A
criterion that marks a group down for being built from loosely coupled,
self-contained units marks a society of mind down for the same reason.
Minsky's agents communicate mainly without shared symbols, through K-lines
and learned codes ([NOTE-339](../notes.d/NOTE-339.md), C3), and Selfridge's design argument is
that quasi-independent modules are easier to modify ([NOTE-360](../notes.d/NOTE-360.md), C2).
IIT's modular-network result ([NOTE-365](../notes.d/NOTE-365.md), C5) is the measured version of
the worry. The criterion would have to separate a modular mind from a
nation by degree, and nobody here computes the degree for either.
Schwitzgebel's own claim is that no architectural criterion excludes the
United States without excluding admissible aliens ([NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md), C8); this
account adds that it would also have to spare a mind built as Minsky
describes.

## What the Chalmers and Dennett readings add

Read after this account was drafted, they leave its verdict standing and
sharpen it in five places.

- **Why agency is cheap to agree on.** Dennett's intentional stance is
  blind to composition and to consciousness by design ([LIT-440](../literature.d/LIT-440.md),
  [NOTE-350](../notes.d/NOTE-350.md)): a system has beliefs if treating it as a believer pays.
  On the criterion of "True Believers" ([LIT-398](../literature.d/LIT-398.md), [NOTE-353](../notes.d/NOTE-353.md)), a
  group whose group-level stance pays would count, though Dennett never
  applies it to one. That is why every reading here grants agency in both
  directions.
- **Why the downward move was licensed and the upward one was not.**
  Dennett's discharge of homunculi ("Artificial Intelligence as Philosophy
  and as Psychology", [LIT-393](../literature.d/LIT-393.md)) is harmless only when the parts are
  stupider than the whole and are replaced step by step by machines. That
  work is filed unread and known through Stich's summary, which Dennett
  accepted. Minsky's and Selfridge's agents are on the licensed side.
  Schwitzgebel's citizens, who are as smart as the whole or smarter, are
  exactly what discharge never licensed. That is a reason the two moves are
  not symmetric for *intelligence*. Block's n. 19 shows it settles nothing
  about experience.
- **Organisational invariance narrows the dispute to architecture.**
  Chalmers's fading-qualia argument ([LIT-392](../literature.d/LIT-392.md), [NOTE-347](../notes.d/NOTE-347.md)), if sound,
  makes any fine-grained functional isomorph of a conscious being
  conscious, including a population of people implementing a person's
  neurons. That closes Putnam's stipulation for isomorphs and conflicts
  with IIT, which lets a functional equivalent lack experience. It leaves
  Kammerer's principle standing. The United States is not an isomorph of
  anything, so invariance does not reach it. What remains is the
  architectural route, which invariance cannot decide.
- **Functionalism itself forbids no nesting.** Chalmers's implementation
  conditions ([LIT-404](../literature.d/LIT-404.md), [NOTE-342](../notes.d/NOTE-342.md)) do not care whether a part is a
  neuron or a person, and they let one system host two minds. The
  combination-problem paper ([LIT-395](../literature.d/LIT-395.md), [NOTE-358](../notes.d/NOTE-358.md)) reaches this
  account's two routes independently, from panpsychism. Its candidate for
  what bounds a subject is integration, which it finds unprincipled. It adds
  an IIT-independent objection to exclusion: exclusion makes consciousness
  extrinsic. The meta-problem paper ([LIT-425](../literature.d/LIT-425.md), [NOTE-354](../notes.d/NOTE-354.md)) adds
  another: a zero-Φ isomorph reports alike.
- **Two new candidates for a principled line, neither yet enough.**
  - Dennett & Kinsbourne's argument against a conscious central part of a
    brain ([LIT-431](../literature.d/LIT-431.md), [NOTE-367](../notes.d/NOTE-367.md)) has Kammerer's shape and is
    motivated independently of groups, but does not reach the United
    States.
  - Chalmers's 2019 condition, that consciousness needs access "not
    mediated by other mental states" ([LIT-415](../literature.d/LIT-415.md), [NOTE-356](../notes.d/NOTE-356.md)), would
    plausibly exclude a nation and spare a society of mind. That
    application is the reader's, not Chalmers's, and its motivation has the
    weakness the anti-nesting route already has.

  Both are the right kind of thing for `promote_when`. Neither has been
  applied by one procedure to a group model and a modular-mind model.

**Two positions that refuse the framing.** Searle ([LIT-400](../literature.d/LIT-400.md),
[NOTE-349](../notes.d/NOTE-349.md)) rejects both directions at once: it is causal powers, not
organisation, that matter. His key step against the systems reply ("the
system is just a part of him") is asserted. Dennett's "Quining Qualia"
([LIT-433](../literature.d/LIT-433.md), [NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md)) denies there is a further property to
dispute, so on his view the dispute never narrows to experience. Neither
supplies the criterion this account asks for; each denies that one is
needed.

## What this does not say

- **It does not say the United States is conscious.** Schwitzgebel's own
  conclusion is conditional and "gappy" ([NOTE-131](../notes.d/NOTE-131.md), C1 and C11), and nothing
  here strengthens it.
- **It does not say Minsky's society of mind is conscious.** The account
  draws no conclusion about it either way. The 1977 memo speaks of intellect
  and affect as products of the society ([NOTE-344](../notes.d/NOTE-344.md), C1), and *The Emotion
  Machine* ([LIT-432](../literature.d/LIT-432.md)) treats feelings as reflective self-descriptions.
  Whether experience comes with them is the question this account leaves
  open.
- **It does not say no principled line exists.** Only that none of the
  readings supplies one. Kammerer's is the best candidate on offer, and its
  weakness is its justification, not its shape.
- **It does not rest on the unread works.** Lycan ([LIT-419](../literature.d/LIT-419.md)), Huebner
  ([LIT-426](../literature.d/LIT-426.md)) and List & Pettit ([LIT-391](../literature.d/LIT-391.md)) are filed unread. Each is
  reported to bear on this seam, and Huebner's test for a collective mind
  (integrated specialised subroutines) is the one most likely to change it.
