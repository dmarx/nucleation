# ARG-231: Devezer and colleagues' model of discovery and Li and colleagues' study of theory building, as evidence, support epistemic diversity as a contributor to intelligence and not as sufficient for it, since in each the diverse population is protective or gains from aggregation without being best on every measure

## The inference

A62b §7 cites two readings. Of Devezer et al. ([NOTE-792](../notes.d/NOTE-792.md)): "Epistemically diverse populations protect against some ineffective search strategies. But the diverse population was not always the best-performing one." Of Li et al. ([NOTE-1884](../notes.d/NOTE-1884.md)): "human theories were more semantically diverse and gained more from aggregation, while individual AI-generated theories were often highly competitive." Its conclusion: "diversity may protect against particular kinds of epistemic lock-in without maximizing every measure of immediate success", and "the relationship is mediated by the structure of inquiry and aggregation, not merely the number of contributors". That is [CLAIM-1106](../claims.d/CLAIM-1106.md)'s step 3.

## The readings checked

- **[NOTE-792](../notes.d/NOTE-792.md).** The record's reading agrees: an equal mix of the four strategies "always performs better than the worst homogeneous population with respect to all properties" and has low variance (its C4, moderate), while Tess and Mave beat it on time at the true model. The record rates the paper's stronger "optimizes" claim weak (C5). A62b used the weaker claim, which is the right one.
- **[NOTE-1884](../notes.d/NOTE-1884.md) and [THEORY-1777](../theory.d/THEORY-1777.md).** The LLMs individually matched or beat most humans; human theories were more diverse and human crowds gained more from aggregation. A62b's summary is accurate.

## Critical questions

Answered by the assistant (Claude, in this session).

- **Is the evidence about the conclusion's terms?** In part. Devezer's diversity is a fixed mix of model-proposal strategies, not perspectives with differentiated response profiles, and its measures are reproducibility and time at the truth, not intelligence. Li et al.'s diversity is semantic dispersion of theories, and its gain is predictive accuracy from aggregation. Both are diversity in A62b's §3 sense only loosely.
- **Does it support the whole conclusion?** No. It bears on the claim that diversity contributes without being sufficient: in both, diversity helps under one rule of aggregation and is beaten on some measure. It says nothing about the developmental half, that equally competent systems differ in intelligence for tomorrow's demands, which neither study tests.
- **Is there contrary evidence?** [THEORY-148](../theory.d/THEORY-148.md), Zollman's transient diversity, read with [ARG-226](ARG-226.md): diversity of pursuit kept indefinitely can stop a group from settling. That bounds "maintained diversity" and agrees with A62b's warning.

So the support is real and weak, and scoped to the half of the conclusion that concerns diversity.
