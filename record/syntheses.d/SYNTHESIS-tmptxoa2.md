# SYNTHESIS-tmptxoa2: Competent behaviour across a family of goals fixes the world model an observer must ascribe without identifying any mechanism inside the agent, so world-modeling is a family of query-relative, resource-bounded capabilities, of which a representational model is one realization, and producing, maintaining and revising them are further organizational achievements that a proposed study would separate

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the entries filed from the
second review of the exchange "Analyze nucleation concepts" (2026-10-31),
with the term for an organizational achievement they use. The mechanical
map put them in the part on persistence and transmission, where they barely
relate to the rest: six entries with twelve linked pairs among them and two
to the other forty-one, both through [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md). So this part has its own
account, beside that part's, [SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj](SYNTHESIS-tmp2stjj.md). The line's account is built over both
and the line's other parts.*

## The account

- **Behaviour does not establish a model.** [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md) (the owner's thesis at
  U20, with A20's construction; interpretive, Proposed): over a fixed
  interface and horizon, an agent "whose actions are looked up from the
  history so far, reproduces the interaction distribution of an agent that
  has one exactly". Compression and generalization support comparing
  architectures, and "establishing an internal mechanism needs evidence that
  discriminates candidate implementations, such as targeted interventions".
- **Behaviour across goals fixes the model ascribed.** [CLAIM-513](../claims.d/CLAIM-513.md) (from the
  reading of Richens, Abel, Bellot and Everitt; counter, demonstrated,
  Active) objects to [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md): a goal-conditioned policy competent on every
  composite goal "fixes every transition probability" to within a bound, "so
  observers reading the same policy must ascribe the same world model". It
  "defeats that thesis's functional reading only"; what it leaves open is
  "whether a predictive component is realized inside the agent". [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md),
  at its owner's review: "On that functional reading their theorem holds and
  is filed as the counter [CLAIM-513](../claims.d/CLAIM-513.md). On the explicit-component reading, the
  claim stands."
- **The join.** [CLAIM-514](../claims.d/CLAIM-514.md) (the owner's preferred formulation at the second
  review; interpretive, Proposed), resting on [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md) and [CLAIM-513](../claims.d/CLAIM-513.md):
  world-modeling "is a family of resource-bounded capabilities for producing
  and using predictions", each relative to a declared family of questions,
  with a single-procedure condition so that "An outside oracle must not be
  allowed to pick a different best answerer for each query". The two claims
  it rests on "do not conflict once each is read as being about its own
  notion", and producing, maintaining and revising world-modeling
  capabilities are "further achievements, not prerequisites of agency in
  general".
- **Five notions.** [QUESTION-362](../questions.d/QUESTION-362.md) (the owner's, at the same review; Open)
  separates an explanatory, a behaviourally encoded and a realized world
  model, world-modeling competence and a self-maintaining world-modeling
  organization: "behavioural recoverability ≠ internally accessible
  predictive capability ≠ mechanistically realized representation ≠
  self-maintained and revisable modeling capacity." "What an investigator
  can recover from an agent is not what the agent can recover from itself."
  Read through [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md), world-modeling has an episodic, a dispositional and
  a reproductive form, and revising the repertoire is "a fourth thing,
  distinct from reproduction".
- **The achievement.** [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md) (the owner's request at U11 of 2026-10-26,
  A11's definition; Proposed): an organizational achievement is the
  attainment or maintenance of a target to which "an identifiable
  organization of component activities makes an explanatory causal
  contribution", as an episode, a maintained capacity or the reproduction of
  what sustains it. Its note of 2026-11-06 records that [CLAIM-514](../claims.d/CLAIM-514.md) names no
  mode for its "further achievements", and that the term does not say
  whether revising a capacity is its reproductive mode or, as [QUESTION-362](../questions.d/QUESTION-362.md)
  says, a fourth thing. Its note of 2026-11-07 records A's proposal of a
  fourth, "Transformative" row.
- **The test.** [CASE-187](../cases.d/CASE-187.md) (the owner's proposal at the second review;
  proposed study, Active) matches three architectures on competence and
  compares them on queries, internal interventions, and change and damage,
  to find "which world-modeling achievements are linked and which come
  apart under matched resources". It illustrates [CLAIM-514](../claims.d/CLAIM-514.md), [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md) and
  [CLAIM-513](../claims.d/CLAIM-513.md).

What stays open is [QUESTION-362](../questions.d/QUESTION-362.md): which query capabilities competence
implies, which the agent can exercise, which need a distinct mechanism, and
which it can maintain, revise or rebuild.
