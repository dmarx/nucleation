# QUESTION-tmpkzu6g: Does an organization's actual allocation of authority track the demands that bind its feasible adaptation, or does it systematically privilege something else?

## Why it is a question

A35 §3.2, after deriving the dual of the local residual: "The optimizing
λ identifies a particularly difficult combination of requirements.
Importantly, these dual multipliers are not endorsement weights. They are
mathematical certificates of difficulty or infeasibility. Nucleation's
w_i, by contrast, represent influence granted by the actual governance
organization." Then the question, and A35's gloss: "That is where the
geometry of optimization meets the geometry of volition."

Two quantities, one organization:

- **What binds.** The maximizing λ of Φ_r = max_{λ∈Δ}[Σλ_iε_i −
  r‖Σλ_ig_i‖_*] ([CLAIM-tmpue67e](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpue67e.md)). It is a property of the demands and the
  admissible set, computable by an outside solver.
- **What is granted.** The influence w_i a demand's process holds, in the
  sense of [TERM-053](../terms.d/TERM-053.md): any effect of other processes on its share of future
  influence. [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) holds the gap between precedence claimed and
  authority granted; A34 restated it as p_i − w_i, "a candidate indicator
  of frustrated precedence".

A40 §4: "The weights assigned by actual governance need not coincide with
the multipliers identifying the most difficult combination of
constraints. That divergence is a candidate source of avoidable
frustration." That is [CLAIM-tmpl8vsy](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpl8vsy.md)'s governance-induced frustration seen
from the weights.

It is open because nothing says what w should follow. Tracking λ is not
obviously right: governance may weight a demand because it matters, not
because it binds, and [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s own caution applies, that "a good
organization may correctly prevent an unreliable process from taking
control" (A34). And w can be captured: on [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md), authority updated on
the interpretation it helped make dominant amplifies without fidelity, so
the "something else" may be the dominant interpretation itself.

## What would count as an answer

In a model or a system where both are measurable (A39's case study 2,
[CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md) with demands and an oracle, is one), the relation between w_t and
the binding λ_t over training: whether w follows λ, lags it, or follows
something identifiable instead (incumbency, agreement with the dominant
interpretation, loss scale), and whether divergence predicts governance
regret Γ. A negative finding counts: that w and λ are unrelated and Γ is
explained without reference to either.
