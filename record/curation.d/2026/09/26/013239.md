## What the readings changed

**Framing this record got wrong.**

- The reading order called Bourrat's coarse-graining paper his "next move"
  after [LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md). It is the *earlier* paper: [LIT-005](../../record/literature.d/LIT-005.md) cites it. The reading
  order carries the correction.
- The seed's "tokenization" bearing for Haspelmath is extrapolation. The
  full text says nothing about subword segmentation, so it is not filed.

**Theorems stated beyond their proofs.**

- Talagrand's Theorem 3 constant: its proof gives only cσ/√2.
- The GPT review defers its headline tensor-product theorems to other papers.
- Riehl & Shulman's semantics is explicitly a sketch.
- Baudot & Bennequin's quantum case is the *opposite* of the seed's claim:
  there, entropy is a coboundary.
- The Work Capacity paper prints its capacity formula with the sign flipped
  from its own proof, and never exhibits the prediction–forgetting
  trade-off its title names.

**Empirical headlines the methods do not carry.**

- Morality-as-cooperation: the test is weakly diagnostic. The blind coder's
  κ is .58, and the codebook defines behaviours in virtue terms.
- The inequality paper: its governance index codes architecture, the
  outcome it predicts.
- Surface optimisation: the SI's trifurcation merge partly makes the
  trifurcations it counts.
- The ant–human puzzle: no significance test anywhere.
