Part of the THEORY backfill the owner asked for on 2026-10-16. The working
hypothesis is that a work read closely enough to carry a NOTE will usually
support at least one THEORY. This entry covers four topic groups:
linguistics, pragmatics, philosophy-of-language and myth-and-folklore. Each
group had LITs with a `Read` or `Skimmed` NOTE that no THEORY cites as
`source`, seventeen in all.

Ten THEORYs, one `Active` and nine `Proposed`. Codes are the temporary ones
`luria new` assigned.

    ([LIT-063](../../record/literature.d/LIT-063.md)). Its NOTE named C1 and C4 as the defensible content, and the

## What did not enter, and why

- **Language Design as Information Renormalization ([LIT-1023](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1023.md), [NOTE-718](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-718.md)).**
  Its reading ends "No THEORY should be produced from this reading". The
  power law restates Lin and Tegmark less rigorously with an asserted
  averaging step, nothing is tested on data, and the architecture claim is
  ML practice, which is the anthology's ([ANTH-LIT-695](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-695.md)).
- **Semantic Unification ([LIT-841](../../record/literature.d/LIT-841.md), [NOTE-644](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-644.md)).** Its one structural point is
  Proposition 1, a fact about the construction, not a finding about
  language. The NOTE says so and also warns against citing it for
  contextuality in [THEORY-012](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-012.md)'s sense.
- **Logic and Conversation ([LIT-1041](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1041.md), [NOTE-734](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-734.md)).** Unavailable, and only
  pp. 45–47 read. The pages state the Cooperative Principle and the maxims
  but not the account of implicature, so there is no account to file.
- **Marabini on Brandom ([LIT-106](../../record/literature.d/LIT-106.md), [NOTE-179](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-179.md)).** Unavailable, and a seeded
  skim that says it is "not enough to state its assumptions or results
  exactly".
- **Doja, Capocchi and Santucci 2021 ([LIT-804](../../record/literature.d/LIT-804.md), [NOTE-609](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-609.md)).** Software and a
  map display, with no claim about myth tested. It summarises [LIT-807](../../record/literature.d/LIT-807.md)'s
  engine faithfully, so it is named in [THEORY-tmp48ml1](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp48ml1.md)'s Connections and
  is not a source.
- **Morphology of the Folktale ([LIT-830](../../record/literature.d/LIT-830.md), [NOTE-642](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-642.md)).** A skim of the English
  translation of [LIT-828](../../record/literature.d/LIT-828.md). [THEORY-167](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-167.md) already holds the content from the
  Russian reading, and the NOTE judged its translation observation too
  thin for a THEORY.

So eleven of seventeen readings became sources. Two of the six that did
not are Unavailable partial skims. Two are software or programme papers
whose readings judged them to carry no finding. One is a translation whose
original already sources a THEORY. One ([LIT-1023](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1023.md)) has its substance in the
anthology. That fits the owner's hypothesis for these topics, with one
caveat: four of the eleven ([LIT-779](../../record/literature.d/LIT-779.md), [LIT-822](../../record/literature.d/LIT-822.md), [LIT-807](../../record/literature.d/LIT-807.md), [LIT-031](../../record/literature.d/LIT-031.md)) carry
NOTEs or LIT notes that said no THEORY should be filed. In each case the
THEORY filed here avoids the part the reading rejected:

- Du Bois's NOTE offered the too/either rule as a candidate. It is filed
  inside the triangle account as that account's one prediction.
- Krauska and Lau's NOTE ruled out the unimplemented model as a source.
  The THEORY rests on the critique (C1–C5) and lists the model under
  "does not say".
- Descola's and Santucci's notes said each reports no finding about myth.
  Together they support a claim about the method, which is what is filed.
- Brandom's NOTE said "No THEORY is filed from a skim". The brief for this
  backfill allows a `Proposed` THEORY on a skim if it says so, and
  [THEORY-tmpx81wa](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpx81wa.md) says so in its summary, Source and `promote_when`.

## Choices made

- **One `Active`.** Haspelmath's criterion-by-criterion result is a
  counterexample argument the record believes as stated, with the
  orthographic target named in "does not say". Every other account is
  argued from chosen examples, a skim, or a parameter-free fit that cannot
  separate it from a simpler rival.
- **`answers`.** Only [QUESTION-073](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-073.md), from Strawson. The question names the
  intention-based line as its rival, and Strawson's scheme makes the
  prediction the question needs: no non-institutional case with the overt
  intention fixed and the act changed. Grice individuates no act types, so
  his THEORY carries no edge. Du Bois offers stance categories but no
  reliability test, so [QUESTION-076](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-076.md) gets no edge.
- **No `rivals`.** Brandom's normative account of content and Grice's
  intention-based account run in opposite orders of explanation. As each
  is stated here they do not exclude each other, so the contrast is in
  prose in both.
- **No edge to [THEORY-172](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-172.md).** The informative-speaker THEORY is the
  precursor of the rational-speech-act account. A relation would belong on
  [THEORY-172](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-172.md), which this task does not edit.
