# CASE-084: The perfectly accountable false theory: a community whose record traces every commitment and whose vocabulary leaves out the right explanation

## The case

A6: "A research community maintains an impeccable argument record. Every
conclusion is linked to sources. Objections are logged. Changes are
versioned. No commitment disappears without a trace. Unfortunately, the
community systematically excludes a class of alternative explanations
from its conceptual vocabulary." A second group keeps a worse record,
runs better interventions, and finds the right explanation.

## What it can show

That accountability and correctness come apart, and that a record judged
only in its own grammar can miss what the grammar excludes. That is
[CLAIM-181](../claims.d/CLAIM-181.md)'s worry about a fixed grammar and [CLAIM-190](../claims.d/CLAIM-190.md)'s about measures
computed in it.

## What it cannot show

A6: "That accountability lacks instrumental value. An accountable record
may help identify errors once the excluded possibility is made
available."
