# ADR-060: A claim or account that bears on a question without answering it says bears_on, and answers is for an answer, held or offered

## Context

[ADR-049](ADR-049.md) says a question is Answered when Active entries answer it in full,
and that one answered in part is split, so that a settled part becomes its
own Answered question. The third status review found 37 Open questions with
an Active entry in `answered_by`. It split 9 of them.

Readers then went through all 102 Active edges on the remaining 34. None
settled a delimited part of its question. The reasons recur:

- **A sufficient case for a which-question.** [THEORY-185](../theory.d/THEORY-185.md)'s independent
  attributes, against [QUESTION-025](../questions.d/QUESTION-025.md)'s correlated ones.
- **One model where the question asks about all.** [THEORY-215](../theory.d/THEORY-215.md)'s Hawkes
  stability conditions, against [QUESTION-091](../questions.d/QUESTION-091.md)'s allocation-dependent
  kernels.
- **A premise of the question.** [THEORY-758](../theory.d/THEORY-758.md) and [THEORY-745](../theory.d/THEORY-745.md) on nested
  spectral subspaces, for [QUESTION-217](../questions.d/QUESTION-217.md).

Several of the entries already said so in their own directives. [THEORY-185](../theory.d/THEORY-185.md)
has "cited as what this result bears on".

## Decision

- **`bears_on` (CLAIM or THEORY to QUESTION), with the converse
  `bearing`:** evidence for or against a question, or a premise of it,
  that neither answers it nor settles a part of it exactly.
- **`answers`:** an answer to the question as posed, alone or with others.
  An Active entry holds the answer, and a Proposed one offers it. A part
  that is settled exactly is split off under [ADR-049](ADR-049.md), and the answer links
  to the child.
- **The 102 Active edges on Open questions are moved** from `answers` to
  `bears_on`, each on both sides. The review that read them is the third
  status review's workbench entry.

**An offered part-answer.** A Proposed entry that would, if held, settle a
delimited part of a question keeps `answers`. It goes Active and is split
off under [ADR-049](ADR-049.md) then, and not before, because [ADR-049](ADR-049.md) splits only what is
settled. The fourth status review found 15 such edges among the 240 it read.

## Alternatives considered

- **A qualifier on `answers`,** such as `answers: {code, partial: true}`.
  Every consumer would have to read two cases out of one relation, and the
  question page would still list the evidence under "Answered by".
- **Leave `answers` overloaded and acknowledge the rows.** The check "Open
  with an Active answer" would then stay noise, which is how it came to
  list 37 questions with no debt behind most of them.

## Consequences

- An Open question with an Active `answers` link is now real [ADR-049](ADR-049.md) debt.
  The worklist's section, and luria's `answered_but_open` pattern once it
  is adopted, report only that.
- **240 edges from Proposed entries on 109 Open questions were not
  reviewed when this was decided.** The fourth status review read them
  all and moved 168 to `bears_on`. Some are offered answers. Others, such as [THEORY-201](../theory.d/THEORY-201.md) on
  [QUESTION-025](../questions.d/QUESTION-025.md) ("cited as partial evidence, not as settled"), are bearing
  and should move. A status review that meets one moves it.
- Question pages show evidence under "Bearing on it", apart from the
  answers.
