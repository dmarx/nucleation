# THEORY-628: Self-production alone gives a system only an all-or-nothing norm, to go on or to die, and that distinction is available to an observer, not to the system; a graded norm the system itself has, by which viable states are better or worse for it, needs adaptivity, a further capacity to detect tendencies toward the boundary of its viability and to act so as to reverse them, and sense-making and agency need adaptivity

## Source

Ezequiel A. Di Paolo (2005), "Autopoiesis, Adaptivity, Teleology, Agency",
*Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 4(4), 429–452, [LIT-1017](../literature.d/LIT-1017.md), read
in full in [NOTE-719](../notes.d/NOTE-719.md).

## What was actually shown

**Conservation, not homeostasis.** Autopoiesis, "a concept allowing no
degrees, can only entail the more general case of conservation (thus
admitting the possibility of fortuitous self-maintenance)". Its only norm
is that "organisms live as long as they don't die". That lets an observer
tell lethal from non-lethal encounters, Weber and Varela's step A, but not
the system, which their step B needs.

**Two cases.** "Balancing at the edge of a cliff is a perfectly viable
behaviour, so is falling over the edge – both are interactions that
conserve autopoiesis." A bacterium whose nutrient intake is below its
consumption, "leading to certain loss of autopoiesis in the near future",
will not "seek higher concentrations just because [it is] autopoietic".

**The definition.** Adaptivity is "a system's capacity, in some
circumstances, to regulate its states and its relation to the environment
with the result that, if the states are sufficiently close to the
boundary of viability, 1. Tendencies are distinguished and acted upon
depending on whether the states will approach or recede from the boundary
and, as a consequence, 2. Tendencies of the first kind are moved closer to
or transformed into tendencies of the second". It is "not a direct
consequence of the definition of autopoiesis". Adaptivity without
self-production is cybernetics, whose purpose is "external to it".

**What adaptivity gives.** "An inner norm, a distinction between good,
neutral, and bad ways of realizing autopoiesis", without any optimum. It
is graded, and recursive: regulation can be regulated, and "plastically
adjusted or transformed depending on their effectiveness". Pathology
becomes definable as exhaustion, malfunction or conflict of adaptive
mechanisms in a system that is still autopoietic, and a cure may
"re-define rather than simply restore" the organism's norms.

**Agency.** Behaviour is the regulation of structural coupling, not the
coupling. An agent is "a self-constructed unity that engages the world by
actively regulating its exchanges with it for adaptive purposes that are
meant to serve its continued viability". Agency comes in degrees and is
not implied by autopoiesis and adaptivity together (note 8).

**What could have come out otherwise.** If the original definition of
autopoiesis already implied active compensation, the gap would not
exist. Di Paolo's reading of the primary texts says it does not; he grants
(note 1) that later formulations may contain adaptivity and asks that it
then be made explicit.

## What this does not say

- **That the organism, or anything, ought to persist.** The norm is for the
  system. Viability alone yields only "the rather useless a posteriori
  realization by the external observer". [NOTE-719](../notes.d/NOTE-719.md) reads this as a stronger
  version of [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)'s disclaimer, not a rival to it.
- **That plasticity can fail separately from regulation.** Di Paolo treats
  the layers of adaptivity as aspects of one graded property; [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md)'s
  thesis is that they come apart, and this paper does not show it.
- **What the viability set is.** It is "finite measure, bounded, and
  possibly time-varying", fixed by the organisation and current state.
  Whether it is a constraint set or a viability kernel is not said
  ([NOTE-719](../notes.d/NOTE-719.md) against [THEORY-134](THEORY-134.md)).
- **That non-metabolic forms generate their own values.** The last
  section's habits, goggles and addiction are declared speculative.
- **That adaptivity is time-asymmetric** beyond a thought experiment.

## Connections

- **Rival: Weber and Varela** ([THEORY-666](THEORY-666.md), from [LIT-1018](../literature.d/LIT-1018.md)). They take
  self-production to give a point of view, valence and a world at once.
  Di Paolo grants their intrinsic teleology and denies that autopoiesis
  alone yields sense-making. Both cannot be right about what
  self-production suffices for, and neither paper settles it.
- **Minimal agency** ([THEORY-071](THEORY-071.md), from [LIT-566](../literature.d/LIT-566.md)). Barandiaran, Di Paolo and
  Rohde formalise this paper's definition: adaptive regulation of coupling
  is their asymmetry condition, and agency comes in degrees.
- **Malfunction from viability** ([THEORY-217](THEORY-217.md), from [LIT-1026](../literature.d/LIT-1026.md)). Barandiaran's
  normative field gives the precarious region a measure, where this paper
  speaks of tendencies and an "outward velocity".
- **Function from self-maintenance** ([THEORY-220](THEORY-220.md), from [LIT-1029](../literature.d/LIT-1029.md)). Bickhard
  gives a candle flame functions; Di Paolo's norm requires regulation, which
  a flame lacks.
- **Viability** ([THEORY-134](THEORY-134.md)). The bacterium on its way to starving is inside
  the tolerated set with no viable future, [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md)'s case; the mapping is
  [NOTE-719](../notes.d/NOTE-719.md)'s.
- **[QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md)** asks whether a self-reproducing structure can be
  pathological by a standard grounded in its own organisation. For
  organisms this account says yes: failure of adaptivity while
  self-production goes on. It gives no criterion for an authority
  structure, so it answers in part.
- [CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) grounds its demand that norms be set by the organisation itself
  partly in this paper.
