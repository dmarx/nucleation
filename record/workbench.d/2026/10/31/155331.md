The owner reviewed the pull request that files the exchange "Analyze
nucleation concepts", its readings and the decisions taken on it.

**What the review kept.** Three changes, singled out as particular
improvements:
- **[CLAIM-474](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) as a partial order.** It corrects a tendency to present
  maintenance, reconstruction, inquiry and self-governance as a necessary
  developmental sequence.
- **The counter to the world-model thesis, [CLAIM-tmp4htg4](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp4htg4.md).** It separates
  whether a policy functionally determines a world model from whether a
  model is implemented as a component.
- **Finite driving and stochastic persistence** for the nucleation
  constructions. They strengthen the physical reading while limiting
  claims of indefinite maintenance.

The review praised the filing's "epistemic discipline", and in particular
the account of the adaptive reconstruction model's limits. Those limits
are the reserved allocation floors, the regularizer behind the copy rule's
return to fidelity, and the one-way coupling. They "identify the
mechanisms that a stronger future construction must endogenize."

**What was revised.**
- **The normative thesis, [CLAIM-tmpg69cm](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpg69cm.md), version 2.** "A judgment about
  what is wrong need not specify what should be done about it."
  Evaluating a history, J_N(γ), is separated from fixing its admissible
  continuations, 𝒜_N(γ). The claim now holds only that judgments about
  continuity, reform and obligation can need diachronic relations that
  state descriptions miss. Prescription is a further question. The
  objection [CLAIM-tmp1l3am](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1l3am.md), version 2, adds that distinction, conceded,
  and keeps its dilemma open.
- **The corrigibility study, [CASE-tmp061to](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmp061to.md), version 2.**
  - Conditioning on a successful first correction selects different
    organizations across arms. So P(S₁ ∧ S₂) and P(S₂ | S₁) are both
    preregistered.
  - A branch starts every arm from a matched, corrected first-stage state.
  - Detecting the failure of the diagnostic apparatus itself is separated
    from detecting that an environmental hypothesis has failed.
- **The concept term, [TERM-tmp6c38c](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmp6c38c.md), version 2.** Correctability comes out
  of the definition. An incorrigible concept is still a concept: a frozen
  classifier, an entrenched misconception, a rigid category. Mathematical
  concepts are corrected by proof, inconsistency or changed commitments,
  not only by what they are applied to. Conceptual corrigibility is a
  named further achievement, consistent with [CLAIM-474](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-474.md). [QUESTION-tmp181oz](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmp181oz.md)
  gets the owner's negative control.
- **The world-model thesis, [CLAIM-tmph29mx](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmph29mx.md), version 2.**
  - Compression and generalization support comparing architectures. They
    do not identify one, since many compact controllers are not explicit
    generative world models.
  - Establishing a mechanism needs evidence that discriminates candidate
    implementations, chiefly targeted interventions.
  - Richens et al.'s result is on the side of what a policy identifies.
- **Thermodynamic qualifications**, in [CASE-tmpwxags](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpwxags.md) and [CLAIM-tmppht9k](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmppht9k.md).
  - The 3.52 RT threshold belongs to one reversible network with chosen
    parameters.
  - At finite affinity the lower branch is low-activity, not extinct,
    since the reverse of decay is a basal production.
  - Pre-fold escape is not inevitable on every protocol. The hazard rises
    as the fold nears, and where the transition is observed depends on
    size, noise, horizon and sweep rate.
  - Three claims are kept apart: a deterministic saddle-node in the
    limiting model; possible escape before it at finite size; and an
    observed transition point that depends on the protocol.
  - The workbench entry of 15:27 says the regime "is lost before the fold".
    It is not rewritten; read it with this qualification.
- **Housekeeping.** The curation entry of 15:27 said twenty works were
  read in full and one skimmed. Two NOTEs are Skimmed, Chen et al. and
  Falasco and Esposito, so the count is nineteen and two; it is corrected.
  The changelog's `record/...` links were left as they are. Fragments are
  collected into the root CHANGELOG.md, where those paths resolve, as the
  review of 2026-10-27 established.

**What was added.**
- **Two notes on [QUESTION-tmprueh0](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmprueh0.md)**, as synthesis and not as claims.
  - The first separates legibility ([TERM-064](../../record/terms.d/TERM-064.md)), volitional efficacy
    ([TERM-023](../../record/terms.d/TERM-023.md)) and correctability ([TERM-085](../../record/terms.d/TERM-085.md)), none of which justifies a
    demand. It sets the histories executable by an agent as it actually is
    against those that satisfy a standard. A nonempty intersection does
    not show the agent will choose well. An empty one does not exempt it,
    if its own earlier actions destroyed the capacities required.
  - The second records the owner's demand for a theory of historically
    situated corrective authority: endogenous governance ≠ historically
    continuous authorization ≠ legitimate authority.
- **[CASE-tmpm4ah0](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpm4ah0.md), a separate proposed study** of corrigible resolve against
  entrenchment. Two agents decide alike in ordinary conditions; one
  protects its commitment from irrelevant fluctuation and lets specified
  defeaters revise it, and the other grows better at enforcing it while
  evidence loses its way in. It is kept apart from [CASE-tmp061to](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmp061to.md), which
  the review judged would otherwise become too complex to interpret.

The review's summary, kept as the larger lesson: "An organization can
maintain a commitment, recognize a reason to revise it, give that reason
practical authority, and execute a revision. These are different
achievements. Whether the commitment should persist, whether the reason
is justified, and whether the revision is legitimately authorized are
further questions."
