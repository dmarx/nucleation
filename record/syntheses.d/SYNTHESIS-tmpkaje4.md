# SYNTHESIS-tmpkaje4: Inquiry in the record is a form of specification that can revise what an answer could be, reflexive when its language, answer space, interventions, observations, criteria or resources become its object; specifying is resource-bounded and stored specification cheapens some questions and hardens others; and what makes reconfiguration self-governed is an open question

*A thematic account, across lines, of principle P8 of the owner's manuscript (scratch/manuscripts/from-obstruction-to-reorganization/, §7.1, on its own branch). Filed at the owner's direction on 2026-11-20 after an independent reader's audit found the principle to be a conjunction of entries the record holds rather than a claim with content of its own ([ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md), step 5; workbench entry of this date). It adds nothing; its standing is its components'. Its scope is a theme, not a line or a part of one, which [ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md)'s one account per scope does not anticipate.*

## The account

**Inquiry as specification.** [CLAIM-470](../claims.d/CLAIM-470.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis, the owner's): "Inquiry is a form of specification … with one difference, that an inquiry can revise what an answer could be". Its body draws "the line between refining within a framework and revising it". [TERM-095](../terms.d/TERM-095.md) (Active, from A15 and A16) writes an inquiry as a tuple of language, answer space, interventions, observations, criteria and resources, "reflexive when any of these becomes what it is pursuing".

**Resource bounds.** [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md) (Active, interpretive, thesis, the owner's): "Specifying is itself a resource-bounded realization", and "a stabilized specification is past inquiry stored so that later questions become cheaper to ask, and it can make other questions harder". [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md) (Active) is the inquiry frontier.

**What a vocabulary cannot say.** [TERM-091](../terms.d/TERM-091.md) (Active) defines a relational vocabulary, in which a constraint can be inexpressible without being false or unknown.

**Inquiry among other achievements.** [CLAIM-474](../claims.d/CLAIM-474.md) (Active, interpretive) places inquiry in a partial order of distinct achievements, "not a nested sequence", and says inquiry can occur without autonomy.

**Self-governance.**

- [QUESTION-388](../questions.d/QUESTION-388.md) (Open) asks what distinguishes a programmable system from one that governs its own reconfiguration.
- [CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md) (Active, interpretive) holds that endogenous governance adds "that it can revise its own governing standards when they break down".
- [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md) (Active, interpretive) separates the capacity to revise one's standards from revising them well.
- [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) (Active, interpretive) holds that an operative regime, its governing organization and the capacity to transform it "can persist or fail separately".

**Stored work, Proposed.** [CLAIM-761](../claims.d/CLAIM-761.md) (Proposed, interpretive): a stabilized constraint can be stored work that changes which activities are realizable within a fixed budget, and "the persistence that buys it can entrench".

**Improving the improver.** [QUESTION-457](../questions.d/QUESTION-457.md) (Open) asks whether a collective keeping its inquiry in a governed record can become a better improver without losing the capacity to detect failures in its apparatus.

## Not stated on the record

- **Reflexive inquiry is self-governed only where the criteria of revision are the system's own and remain revisable.** The manuscript's P8 stated this. Whether criteria are the system's own is exactly what [QUESTION-388](../questions.d/QUESTION-388.md) asks. Requiring the criteria of revision, and not only the governing standards, to be one's own goes beyond [CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md), and opens the regress [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md)'s `defeated_if` names.
