# Article 1. Becoming Through Relations

## Organizational individuation, character, and the emergence of agency

**Abstract.** Organizations are usually told apart by their boundaries, their coherence, their persistence or their coordinated behaviour. These need not pick out the same thing. This article sets out what the Nucleation record holds about the difference, and argues for four points.

1. "Individuation" has several senses, and a claim should say which one it means. Being an individual in the dynamical sense does not require maintaining oneself.
2. The achievements that lead from interconnection to individuation, and from there to autonomy and inquiry, form a partial order and not a ladder.
3. Organizations matched in what they ordinarily do can differ in what keeps them in being and in what produced them. Passive observation does not show either difference. Where an outside controller supplies the internal production law as state feedback, interventions on state do not show it either, though a less ideal controller can leave a trace that a state probe finds. Maintenance is then located by interventions on the maintaining mechanism, relative to a boundary declared in advance, and the record has run such interventions in simulated populations. In the record's one construction of formation, no knockout after formation tells a self-formed organization from one installed from outside and then self-maintained; a knockout during formation does.
4. What persists through a change of every part is a lineage, carried by causal transmission. Resemblance does not carry it, so a character that two unrelated organizations share is a kind, not the persistence of either.

The article then sets out what the record holds, and what it does not yet hold, about character in the sense of representation theory. It ends by asking why individuation does not establish agency, autonomy, experience or standing. The individuation half of the argument rests mostly on Active entries, many of them made Active at status reviews whose rulings the owner delegated, and two on the day of drafting at the assistant's discretion (see "Standing, voices and limits"). The character half rests on Proposed ones.

### 1.1 Which individuation?

A claim that something is an individual can mean different things. The record's term for this lists four senses (TERM-088, Active):

- "descriptive (an inquiry can distinguish and track it)";
- "dynamical (its organization persists under specified dynamics and perturbation, with robust identity and boundaries)";
- "constitutive (its relational organization helps fix its identity and boundary)";
- "organizational autonomy, the further achievement of contributing to the maintenance of its own constitutive constraints". This is closure in TERM-070's sense (Active): "a set of constraints each of which depends directly on another in the set and has another depending on it".

The senses "are not stages, and not degrees of existence. A pattern can be dynamically individuated while sustained wholly from outside, like a convection roll fed by an imposed gradient. That is individuation without autonomy" (TERM-088). This is the owner's point, made at their second review of the exchange of 2026-10-27: "A persistent externally sustained pattern may still be a dynamically individuated entity" (TERM-088, summary; CLAIM-474, version 2). The point matters for everything that follows. An organization's being an individual is one question. Whose processes keep it going is another.

Two further cautions come with the term.

- **TERM-088 names what is claimed, "not how it is measured".** Boundaries, coupling strength, coherence and persistence are criteria. Each can be used to support one of the senses, and none of them is a sense. A criterion is also relative: "An individuation is well defined only once its criterion is fixed (the target it serves, the transformations its unity must survive, the scale), and that dependence does not make it arbitrary: once the criterion is fixed a verdict can be false" (CLAIM-405, Active, interpretive). It rests on "Any individuation built on sufficiency is relative to the target it is sufficient for" (CLAIM-152, Active, demonstrated). The record's proposed criterion for when a combined form is one system is a block of nearly decomposable dynamics whose gap beats an Occam factor (CLAIM-157, Proposed, interpretive), its answer to QUESTION-035 (Open). It is one measure of the dynamical sense (TERM-088).
- **TERM-088 excludes a sense.** It sets aside "the genetic, or operational, sense of Simondon's readers, individuation as the operation by which a metastable system resolves a disparity and yields a provisional individual". That sense names "the process that would produce a candidate, not a claim about one", and "a claim that means it says so" (TERM-088). The record holds one reading of it in organization studies: Bencherki, Brummans and Vézy, after Simondon, for whom research into individuation follows "the being in its genesis" (THEORY-1628, Active). A49's abstract for this article says that "individuals emerge through the formation and maintenance of constraints". Read as a claim about a process of production, that is the genetic sense. This article declares it where it uses it, in §1.4. Elsewhere "individuation" means one of the four senses, and the article says which.

A49's abstract also speaks of "reflexive autonomy". That is not TERM-088's autonomy. Closure is a relation among constraints. Reflexive governance, "making the conditions that govern its own interpretation, evaluation and action available for effective examination and revision", is a further capacity "some organizations of perspective have and others lack" (CLAIM-424, Active, interpretive). Section 1.8 keeps the two apart.

### 1.2 Achievements in a partial order

At the owner's request of 2026-10-27, the record set out the relations between interconnection and individuation "as a research program with distinct testable transitions, rather than a cluster of philosophically suggestive analogies". The result is CLAIM-474 (Active, interpretive), in the record's voice:

> From interconnection to individuation, the relationships are distinct achievements that form a partial order, not a nested sequence: none implies the next, and a later one need not require every earlier one.

Interconnection fixes what joint behaviour is possible. Compatibility fixes which local descriptions admit a coherent whole. Dynamics fixes which organizations persist, individuation which have "sufficiently robust identity and boundaries", organizational autonomy which "help maintain their own constitutive constraints", and inquiry which can revise their own models under resource bounds, "inquiry and reconstruction occurring without autonomy" (CLAIM-474). The claim lists, with sources, the places where an implication fails. Two of them bear directly on this article:

- "**Persistence without individuality.** Synchrony can come from a common drive";
- "**Persistence without autonomy.** A pattern can be maintained entirely by external supply."

The claim was first stated as a nested sequence. It became a partial order at the owner's decision of 2026-10-31, with the owner's rule of method: "derive the dependency structure of the achievements separately from the order in which an inquiry met them" (CLAIM-474, version 3 and body). The article follows that rule. The order of its sections is an order of exposition. It is not a claim about which achievement comes first in any organization.

Three cautions on using the claim:

- **It has one run behind it, and the run is Proposed.** Its central experiment is CASE-179 (Active, a proposed study), which manipulates coupling, correspondence maps and their revision independently. The first run of that design is CASE-333 (Proposed, an executed study). It "made the achievements this claim keeps apart come apart on purpose in one oscillator network" (CLAIM-474, note of 2026-11-22). Under a shared drive, uncoupled and coupled networks lock alike and their offsets glue alike. Displacing one member's drive moves the others by 0.2 per cent of its shift without coupling and by 30 to 58 per cent with it. After the drive is withdrawn, order is 0.47 without coupling and 0.996 with it (CLAIM-903, Proposed, demonstrated, on CASE-333).
- **An extension proposed by A is only noted.** A32 §22 inserts contracts into the order as one more achievement, "A possible formation history. The achievements remain distinct and need not occur in this order". The record holds this as a note on CLAIM-474, "by A, not argued for beyond its statement". Article 2 takes contracts up.
- **The claim orders achievements of organization, not of memes.** A4 of the exchange behind this series cited CLAIM-474 in support of the memetic nucleation hypothesis. The record rejected that use: "CLAIM-474 orders other things, and the hypothesis needs no such order" (ARG-tmpeukxb, Rejected; the assistant's). This article does not cite CLAIM-474 for anything about memes, and nor should Article 7.

### 1.3 Produced and imposed: constraints and constitutive relations

A49's second section asks how processes come to produce the conditions of one another's activity, and how organization maintained from inside differs from organization imposed from outside. The record's first point is negative. A coupling statistic does not register who made the coupling:

> A gap or coupling criterion cannot tell a boundary a system produces from one imposed on it: a membrane the cell makes and a beam joining two pendulums pass alike, so the block test cannot by itself supply the individuality minimal agency needs (CLAIM-194, Active, interpretive, a counter to CLAIM-157 and CLAIM-174).

"The criterion has no term for who made the coupling" (CLAIM-194). The claim does not say that production is measurable wherever coupling is. The reading that places the line at production, THEORY-072, is Proposed (CLAIM-194).

The record's second point is positive and formal. Where every constraint capacity turns over and is produced only by others, "capacities whose production graph is acyclic all decay to zero: a capacity that persists needs a productive cycle among its ancestors". The cycle "is necessary and not sufficient, and a one-member cycle is not closure" (CLAIM-516, Active, demonstrated). So a productive cycle is the minimal structure a persisting capacity needs, under that claim's hypotheses, and having one does not make an organization internally maintained. Closure in TERM-070's sense needs two or more constraints. Entrenchment is no substitute: an entrenched core "can sit at the root of an acyclic dependency order, and where interactions turn over such an order persists only while something outside supplies its core" (CLAIM-531, Active, interpretive).

The record holds two constructions of such an organization (CASE-189, Active, a construction from A9 to A12 of "Analyze nucleation concepts", recomputed independently):

- In the first, two constraints that produce each other hold a three-unit coupling. The coupling survives damage up to 0.4 and meets a saddle-node at damage 0.5.
- In the second, "a transient encounter … forms a connector enabling that reciprocal production, after which the connector is maintained by what it enables, every single-pathway knockout ends it, and the regime ends in a saddle-node at damage ≈ 0.691 where the coupling's gap is still ≈ 0.989".

The second construction shows a further dissociation. The coupling is "as tightly integrated at the edge of collapse as well inside the regime": "An organization's present coupling strength need not reveal the stability of the process maintaining it" (CLAIM-521, Active, demonstrated). The record notes that this is generic for such folds and "built in rather than surprising".

What "realizes" means here needs care. "That a mechanism realizes a capacity can mean that the capacity is produced through it over time or that the capacity consists in it at a time, and interventions show only the first" (CLAIM-528, Active, interpretive). In this article, "maintained by" means produced through, the causal sense, which interventions can test. When constraints only characterize an entity and when they partly constitute it is still open (QUESTION-284, Open).

### 1.4 Nucleation and stabilization

This section uses "individuation" in the genetic sense, which TERM-088 excludes, and says so. It asks how a form comes to be, not whether a candidate is an individual. The owner asked the record's question at U14 of 2026-10-27 ("bifurcation/catastrophe theory? far from equilibrium dynamics? pattern formation?"), and it is open:

> By which dynamical transitions do the distinctions individuation needs come into being and pass away (instability that amplifies small differences, nucleation across a finite barrier from a locally stable state, bifurcation of the set of attractors, noise- or rate-induced tipping), and are a form's creation, its stabilization and its coming to have a constitutive role three separate achievements? (QUESTION-281, Open)

The record holds partial answers.

- **In Life constructions, the achievements come apart.** Built at the owner's suggestion, CASE-260 (Proposed) has a block created and destroyed two generations later, a lone block stabilized with no role, and the blocks of Gosper's gun in a constitutive role. That makes "two of the three achievements come apart, each way" for a formal system (QUESTION-281, note of 2026-11-14; CASE-259, Proposed, is its companion). "Taking on a constitutive role can change which of these holds", so stabilization and role "though they can come apart, are not independent" (CLAIM-672, Proposed, demonstrated; the owner proposed the Life case and asked for the general claim). A neighbouring triple is Active: "That a form assembles, that it persists over a specified horizon and that the system regenerates it after turnover are three properties with separate probabilities" (CLAIM-566, Active, interpretive). And persistence is not productivity: "Persistence and nucleating productivity in the committor sense vary independently" (CLAIM-595, Active, demonstrated; SYNTHESIS-078, Proposed, is its account).
- **Supplied upkeep and self-sustaining upkeep differ.** Where every replacement part is supplied from outside, an organization "can become independent of what founded it but never of what feeds it" (CLAIM-597, Active, demonstrated). Where repair draws on a flux at a rate proportional to the intact share, as a flame's upkeep does, there is "an ignition threshold below which it goes out with the supply still on", a supply level below which it collapses, and an outage after which restoring supply does not relight it. Supplied repair "has none of the three" (CLAIM-604, Active, demonstrated).
- **Withdrawing a transient support is safe only under conditions.** "Transient support can be withdrawn safely only if it started a process that keeps itself going on a flux that will stay available, was large enough to carry the supported party past any threshold that process has, outlasted the threat it stood in for, and left the work to the party throughout". The claim continues, "meeting them all is not enough", and "persistence after withdrawal is needed to tell which, though slow reversion can mimic it" (CLAIM-600, Active, interpretive; the assistant's, at the owner's request of 2026-11-05). These are necessary conditions. The claim says they are not sufficient. Its empirical counterpart is open: A's withdrawal test, "establish a nucleus with external support, withdraw the support at matched intermediate states", measuring the drop in the chance of completing (QUESTION-398, Open; A's wording).
- **A seed works only in a window.** In the seeded fold model, "a transient seed leaves a maintained organization only inside the bistable window and only by carrying the state past the unstable threshold". Because the seed enters as a scalar forcing, "the model expresses the environment's receptivity but not which organization a seed nucleates" (CLAIM-tmpq1lyz, Active since 2026-11-24 at the assistant's discretion, demonstrated; the assistant's gap fill, not a member of the work).

How CASE-189's second construction fits these entries is narrower than this article's draft said. In its own words it is "a bistable system pushed across a separatrix", in which "Which terms are internal is stipulated, the substrates are assumed available" (CASE-189). It is not of CLAIM-604's kind. CLAIM-604's model draws repair from a supply at a rate set by the intact share, and CASE-189 has no supply variable. Of CLAIM-604's three signatures it shows the threshold; collapse appears in the damage parameter, not in a supply; and an outage of supply is undefined (the joins reader, review Part B, J1.4). Even where all three signatures are found, they do not certify that upkeep is self-sustaining. An outside crew delivering repair at the same state-dependent rate gives the same equation, so the inference is an instance of CLAIM-522 and needs a declared boundary (SYNTHESIS-048, Proposed, "Not stated on the record"). Whether adding a supply parameter to the construction yields all three signatures is a computation not yet done.

Three of the owner's hypotheses belong here. All are Proposed and interpretive.

- **Perspectives as nucleation sites.** "maybe persisted alternative perspectives serve as conceptual nucleation sites for parallel depth pursuit?" (CLAIM-1004, the owner's U28 of "Explain First Contact RL").
- **Memes as nucleation sites.** In the exchange behind this series: "memes as nucleation sites", "a seed around which beliefs, practices and relations organize, and not only a unit that copies itself" (CLAIM-tmp1ng5k, the owner's U2). CLAIM-tmpq1lyz bounds what the one-dimensional model can say about it. The record keeps three persistences apart here: "the persistence of the seed, of the organization it nucleates and of the perspective it carries are distinct phenomena" (CLAIM-tmpgvsgg, Proposed, interpretive). And how much a meme is copied does not fix how much it organizes: "a widely copied meme can organize little and a rarely copied one much" (CLAIM-tmp4uj40, Proposed, interpretive). Whether nucleation can be told from threshold contagion is LEAD-tmpiktom's design (Open, idea), which Article 7 takes up. Its note of 2026-11-24 finds formal precedents for three of its interventions (CASE-189, CASE-191, CASE-148) and no executed memetic study: "The empirical study stays open".
- **Reification by equivalence classes.** "the collapse of irrelevant distinctions into equivalence classes is the mechanism that underlies reification of those equivalence classes into useful new distinctions". The owner offered it as a hypothesis, and the claim makes it "substantive only where the classes are closed under the system's own operations and are reused" (CLAIM-762, the owner's U10 of "Topological protection explained").

A49 also speaks of "durable organizational regimes". The record holds no entry for that beyond CLAIM-597, CLAIM-604, CLAIM-600 and CASE-189's folds. No answer to QUESTION-281's first clause has been given for a form the record individuates. Whether the record individuates the Life block at all is filed in two readings (CLAIM-871 and CLAIM-870), and the owner has not chosen between them.

### 1.5 Matched behaviour, different provenance

A49 puts the article's formal centre here: "Construct organizations with matched external behavior but different maintenance provenance". Its primary contribution is "distinguishing what an organization is from how it originated, how it is implemented and how it persists". The record has already done much of this work, and it constrains how the design may be built. The record asks whether two systems with matching behaviour and persistence can be told apart by whether their own processes generate and revise the constraints that keep them going, "and by what intervention?" (QUESTION-086, Open). Its account of the answers so far is SYNTHESIS-079 (Proposed): "Agreement in ordinary operation does not fix how a system responds to an intervention on its mechanism".

#### 1.5.1 What passive observation cannot show

"Matched ordinary trajectories can conceal differences in an interpretation's efficacy, in the allocation of that efficacy, and in the maintenance of its enabling constraints". In two finite constructions, "a specified intervention exposes the difference that passive observation of the matched trajectories does not" (CLAIM-414, Active, demonstrated). The record has run this in a simulated population: three ways of keeping two governing rules, with "ordinary trajectories identical", come apart under loss, repair and interruption of their producers (CASE-161, Active, an executed study, stipulated). A neighbouring claim shows the same concealment kept in place by the questions asked: of two ecosystems "equivalent under the routine queries", the one "that optimized away capacities only an unasked query needs is rewarded" (CLAIM-1054, Proposed, interpretive).

#### 1.5.2 What interventions on state cannot show

The record goes further. The next claim is A10 §4's of "Analyze nucleation concepts", checked by the record:

> Recovery under interventions on state cannot show where maintenance is realized: an outside controller that measures the constraint capacities and supplies the internal production law as state feedback yields the same trajectory under every perturbation of state, damage and recovery included, even with every capacity observed exactly; only interventions on the production mechanisms, relative to a declared boundary, separate the two (CLAIM-522, Active, demonstrated).

"Full observation of c changes nothing." What separates the two systems is "disabling a production pathway inside the declared boundary (A loses it, B does not), or interrupting the controller (B loses it, A does not)" (CLAIM-522). The claim is scoped to the ideal controller. Controllers that are not ideal may leave signatures under state probes. In CASE-332 (Proposed, an executed study), a constant-rate outside service was told apart by zeroing the state (0 of 64 second corrections against 64 of 64), and the claim calls this "outside this claim's ideal case" and no counterexample (CLAIM-522, note). The claim also says the boundary "is not given": which mechanism is inside is fixed by a declared boundary, "and a gap criterion cannot supply it (CLAIM-194)". The mechanist account of constitution reaches the same place. Under mutual manipulability, an internal producer and an outside controller of this kind come out alike, "so what puts one inside and the other outside is parthood, a boundary the experiments presuppose and do not find" (CLAIM-530, Active, interpretive).

This is where A49's design needed correcting, as the audit found. A49's centre lists "Remove initiating supports, exchange components, perturb relational constraints". Exchanging components and perturbing relational constraints are interventions on state. By CLAIM-522 they cannot locate maintenance against an ideal controller. Removing initiating supports is an intervention on a mechanism, but on the mechanism that *initiated* the organization. CLAIM-522's separating move is on the mechanism that *maintains* it.

**The record has already run that move.** A51 §3 says CASE-161 "already implements much of the experimental methodology I proposed for the individuation paper", and the audit of A51 confirms that CASE-161 and CASE-163 intervene "on the maintaining mechanism, which the audit of A49 found its design lacked" (curation entry of 2026-11-24, "A51 audited").

- **CASE-161** (Active, an executed study, stipulated) keeps two rules by external reset, by reciprocal production between two producer teams, or by feedback only. Disabling a producer team is the intervention on mechanism. A producer outage of 80 updates ends both rules in 48 of 48 reciprocal runs and leaves the other arrangements unaffected; the audit team's outage is survived at 32 updates and not at 36, the admission team's at 36 and not at 40. "These are grid points under these laws, not a critical duration." Its outside arrangement is a reset, not CLAIM-522's ideal controller, and a state intervention also parts it: after the same audit pulse the next audit integrity is "1.00 under external reset, 0.18 under reciprocal production and 0.00 under feedback only".
- **CASE-163** (Proposed, an executed study, stipulated) identifies which of four known mechanisms maintains the rules. With histories matched, a model fitted under reciprocal production predicts 0.97055 for both reciprocal (actual 0.97093) and self-dependent production (actual 0.00000). Without probes the class is identified in 25.0 per cent of cases; with a complete audit pulse and a short producer outage, in 100.0 per cent, and withheld interventions are predicted. It holds only "within a known catalog and with every probe run on a counterfactual reset": "A posterior over a known catalog is not identification of an unknown law."

Both cases say what limits them. "The producer teams, schedules and production law are assigned by the modeller, so 'internal' means inside the simulated population by stipulation" (CASE-161). The boundary is declared by construction, which is what CLAIM-522 and CLAIM-530 require and also why the cases do not show a population drawing its own.

#### 1.5.3 Formation provenance is a fact about history

A49's contribution also names "how it originated". That is a different provenance, and the record keeps it apart. At the owner's review of 2026-11-03, the record filed three senses of provenance (TERM-100, Active):

| sense | when | what can test it (TERM-100) |
|---|---|---|
| formation: "whose process produced it" | the history of production | "an intervention on the producing process while it runs, or a record kept then (CASE-191)" |
| maintenance: "whose process keeps it in being now" | the present | "knockouts of the processes that produce and uphold it (CLAIM-522)" |
| source: what it was made from | the history of a transport | a fidelity profile against the source (TERM-084's sense) |

"The first two come apart" (TERM-100). The case that shows it was built at the owner's request (CASE-191, Active, a construction, fictional, `scratch/formal/self_formation.py`). It compares three agents:

- one whose own frustration at two demands produces a capacity;
- one whose capacity was installed by an outside pulse and is then maintained by the agent's own cycle;
- one whose capacity is upheld from outside.

The result: "a capacity upheld from outside is told apart by knockouts afterwards, but one installed by an outside pulse and then self-maintained is told apart only by a knockout during formation". "After the fact the two are the same organization" (CASE-191). TERM-100 states it as: "after formation no knockout tells it from a self-formed one".

So A49's design, run on matched organizations that have already formed, can reach maintenance provenance by mechanism knockouts. In CASE-191's construction, no knockout after formation reaches formation provenance; a knockout during formation does, and TERM-100 adds a record kept then. That is shown for one fictional construction with a scalar capacity. No entry says that formation leaves no other trace after the fact, and CASE-191 itself says where a trace might be in a richer object: if self-determination theory is right, an outside-installed and a self-formed capacity "must differ somewhere this model lacks", "not in the capacity, which is identical, but in its relations to the rest" (CASE-191, citing THEORY-047 and THEORY-054, both Proposed). This paragraph restates TERM-100's table and CASE-191, applied to A49's design (J1.2). One route by which an outside party could act on formation is held as a claim: reflexive control can "seed interpretive, evidence-selecting and authority-allocating dispositions that the target then maintains itself", which "leaves formation provenance outside while maintenance is the target's own" (CLAIM-tmpp0ufw, Proposed, interpretive). That is the installed arm, described as an influence.

#### 1.5.4 Intervention or history

An earlier objection in the record has the same shape. A45 of the exchange of 2026-10-17, against the owner's non-domination criterion, put it this way:

> Processes conditioned never to object coordinate without override at the same behaviour and supervisory cost as processes that negotiate, so the two part only under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or in their history (CLAIM-364, Proposed, interpretive, a counter).

"Every discriminating test is an intervention or a history" (CLAIM-364). A45 also flagged a test that does not discriminate: "Remove the higher-level supervisor temporarily", since compliance may persist too.

This article's draft read CLAIM-364's two discriminators as TERM-100's two provenances, intervention as maintenance and history as formation. An independent reader found that this misstates the maintenance sense. Maintenance provenance is *whose* process keeps the arrangement in being, and removing the supervisor is a knockout of an outside upholder, the very test CASE-191 uses afterwards. Both kinds of coordination survive it, so they share maintenance provenance. The corrected statement passed ADR-075's test by derivation and is filed:

> Conditioned and negotiated coordination share their maintenance provenance, since both persist when the supervisor is removed, so the interventions that part them test how the constituents' own processes answer dissent, not whose process keeps the arrangement, and removing the supervisor fails to discriminate because it is the one maintenance-provenance test among them (CLAIM-tmp9ia3w, Proposed, demonstrated; the reader's wording, filed by the assistant).

The history half holds as the draft had it: history reads formation provenance (TERM-100; CLAIM-tmp9ia3w, "What it does not say"). The claim's defeater needs a boundary "declared in advance (CLAIM-522, CLAIM-194)". The draft also called the supervisor test "the same mistake" as §1.5.2's removal of an initiating support. That is an analogy at most: the supervisor upholds the arrangement and did not initiate it. SYNTHESIS-023 (Proposed) declines a different link, between CLAIM-364's interventions and a non-domination mark of integration, so it does not bear on this one.

#### 1.5.5 The design, restated, and what the record has run of it

Here is A49's formal centre rebuilt from these entries, with three arms matched on ordinary behaviour:

- **(a) self-formed:** the organization's own processes produced the maintained object and maintain it;
- **(b) installed, then self-maintained:** an outside process produced it, and the organization's own processes maintain it;
- **(c) upheld:** an outside controller supplies the internal production law as state feedback, CLAIM-522's ideal case.

For a scalar capacity this design has already been run. CASE-191 *is* it: its arms *own*, *pulse* and *upheld* are (a), (b) and (c), and its upheld agent's outside controller supplies "P(G) and P(S) as feedback", CLAIM-522's ideal case (the joins reader, J1.3). For governing rules in a stipulated population, CASE-161 and CASE-163 run its maintenance arm (§1.5.2). The steps, with what holds each:

1. **Declare the boundary before any intervention** (CLAIM-522, CLAIM-194, CLAIM-530). CASE-161 and CASE-163 declare it by construction.
2. **Intervene on the maintaining mechanisms after formation.** Disable a production pathway inside the boundary, and separately interrupt the outside controller. CASE-191's table gives the result: internal knockout leaves own and pulse at 0 and upheld at 2.618; outside knockout the reverse. *This step holds by the definition of arm (c)*, which has no internal maintenance and is defined so that no state probe parts it from (a). A separating state probe would only show that the controller was not ideal, so the step tests nothing about any system.
3. **Intervene on the producing process during formation, or keep a record then.** Expectation: only this separates (a) from (b) (TERM-100; CASE-191). Falsifier: a knockout after formation that separates (a) from (b). In CASE-191, knocking out frustration-driven production from t = 0 leaves own at 0 and pulse at 2.618, and no later knockout separates them. For an object richer than a scalar the falsifier is live (§1.5.3).
4. **Withdraw the initiating support,** and read persistence after withdrawal over a horizon fixed in advance, since "slow reversion can mimic it" (CLAIM-600). In CASE-191 the pulse arm's supply ends at t = 2 and the capacity persists. CASE-333 withdraws a shared drive (§1.2). For memes, the record names the same test: whether a hyperstition's product is maintained by its own processes "is a fact about maintenance provenance, told apart by withdrawing the circulation once the realization has formed" (CLAIM-tmpyp5bu, Proposed, interpretive). QUESTION-398 (Open) asks the general form.
5. **Run state interventions too:** exchange components, perturb relational constraints. *This step also holds by the definition of arm (c)*: under the ideal controller they cannot separate (a) from (c) (CLAIM-522). They are a control, and they do part non-ideal controllers (CASE-332; CASE-161's audit pulse).

So steps 3 and 4 carry the design's test, together with a question the design cannot answer from inside: whether any real system realizes arm (c).

**What remains.** No entry holds a case in which the maintained object is a perspective, or anything richer than a scalar capacity or a governing rule. That extension is LEAD-tmpu1jj5 (Open, specified): "Test formation and maintenance provenance for a maintained object richer than a scalar capacity, first in artificial agents, with interventions on the maintaining mechanism inside a declared boundary and dispositional models as the rival". Its note of 2026-11-24 narrows it after the A51 audit: "What stays open is this lead's own addition: an object richer than a scalar rule, such as a perspective, and the dispositional rival". The note also carries a warning from CASE-346 (Active, an executed study): "a habit of matched memory reproduces every intervention it can receive", so the design "needs an intervention inside the boundary that a habit cannot receive". The lead's expectation, stated before any run: the knockout "degrades it while state interventions do not", and the dispositional rival "predicts no difference between arm 2 and the control" (LEAD-tmpu1jj5). The perspective-transplantation study, which would exchange "learned interpretive rules, specialist parameters, training histories, institutional rules and shared memory" between behaviourally matched populations, is not run (CASE-148, Active, a proposed study). Its source warns: "No transfer experiment can settle numerical identity merely by demonstrating causal continuity" (A4, in CASE-148). The joins reader noted one prediction that would pass ADR-075's test if anyone put it forward: that for an object richer than a scalar, (b) can be told from (a) after formation, "by interventions on how the formed part is integrated with the rest". This article does not assert it.

### 1.6 Persistence: product, producer and lineage

A49's abstract says that "organizational persistence is better understood through the maintenance and regeneration of characteristic relationships than through the preservation of fixed components". The second half has support on the record. The first half needs a correction from it. The record has a term for A49's phrase: "constitutional regeneration, of an organization: its capacity to maintain valuable constitutive relationships through changes in the processes that realize them, while remaining answerable to justified claims concerning those relationships" (TERM-tmpx4zke, Proposed). The term is normative ("valuable", "answerable"), and Article 14 takes it up. This section is about persistence alone.

**Persistence does not need fixed components.** The record's case is the owner's, from r/place. An artwork defended for days has its pixels repeatedly replaced, its defenders come and go, and its attackers keep working, "yet the image stays recognizable". The claim, in A8's wording:

> A pattern can persist while every constituent is replaced and some constituents work against it, as a high-turnover regime of damage and repair, so the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts (CLAIM-280, Active, interpretive).

"An image can endure without an enduring organization behind it, while an organization can endure despite radically changing the image it produces" (A8, in CLAIM-280). The claim does not say "that the pattern, or the coalition, is a self".

**What persists through turnover is carried by transmission, not resemblance.**

> A collective that replaces all its members persists, when it does, by causal transmission of the organization that produces it, not by resemblance: an organization of the same kind that arises independently is not its continuation (CLAIM-329, Active, interpretive).

The formal form is Active: "Isomorphism is not persistence, in either direction", so "which morphisms count as continuations is structure added by assumptions about the domain" (CLAIM-593, Active, demonstrated). The record's Proposed thesis on individuals carries this further. Identity over time is "carried by the causal transmission of the relations that keep its repertoire realizable, not by its constituents or by any structure invariant across its realizations", so that "two structurally identical copies can be two individuals" (CLAIM-615, Proposed, interpretive). Much of it is the owner's: the software product as a model of an individual, PyTorch, and "a form of Structuralism" (U14 and U15 of "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"). The integration is A's. Its account is SYNTHESIS-046 (Proposed). What must stay continuous, and which of several successors continues the original, is still open (QUESTION-118, Open).

So "characteristic relationships" cannot carry persistence by being characteristic. A character, in the record's sense, classifies "kinds, not tokens or positions" (CLAIM-132, Active, demonstrated). Two unrelated organizations can share one (CLAIM-417, item 2). If a character were what persisted, persistence would be resemblance, which CLAIM-329 excludes. What persists is the lineage, a causal chain of realizations. Regeneration of characteristic relations counts toward persistence only when it is carried by transmission from what came before. A49's own clause, "without identifying character with … developmental lineage", points the right way. This section restates CLAIM-329, CLAIM-615 and CLAIM-417 together (J1.5, a restatement).

Two neighbouring integrations have been declined by the record's accounts, and this article does not make them:

- that a collective's product, carrier and perspective "each persist by their own criterion". Two of the pairs are held, but "no case on file separates a product from a perspective" (SYNTHESIS-047, Proposed, "Not stated on the record");
- that a block's lasting (CLAIM-157) "is what persistence by causal transmission (CLAIM-615) measures": "a free parameter, what makes a later block 'that block'" (SYNTHESIS-049, Proposed, "Not stated on the record").

### 1.7 Character, realization, lineage and representation

#### 1.7.1 Four objects kept apart, and a fifth

The owner proposed at U31 of 2026-10-22 that the record's earlier "selves" are "better described as 'organizational perspectives'" (CLAIM-417, Proposed, interpretive). The claim's sharp edge is what the replacement must keep apart:

1. "A perspective as realized", in one carrier or distributed across several (TERM-072, Active, sense 1);
2. "Its character, a pattern by which realizations are recognized as of one kind. Two unrelated traditions can share one";
3. "Its lineage, a chain of causal transmission from one realization to the next";
4. "Its representations: self-descriptions, narratives, reputations, which can misdescribe the other three".

A40, in the claim: "Resemblance ≠ Historical descent ≠ Numerical organizational identity". TERM-072 records that it was "Adopted 2026-10-29, with character and lineage read as relations among realizations." The claim does not say "that perspectives are agents" (§1.8).

The claim's note of 2026-11-07 records a fifth object. A50 §5 "adds a 'dispositional basis' (CLAIM-421) beside realization, character and lineage; A52 §7 restores representation, which makes five objects". CLAIM-421 (Active, interpretive) says why the dispositional basis is its own object: "The cessation of an organizational perspective's enactment need not imply the loss of the dispositions, capacities or reproductive relationships from which it can be reinstated". The claim's title keeps four objects, and this article cites the fifth because it matters for what holds the claim back. The claim is held back by a live competitor: "habitus and transposable schemas may explain the same transitions, and no model in the record out-predicts them at matched complexity" (CLAIM-417, notes of 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-20). Habitus is a theory of a dispositional basis (the review of 2026-11-24, its point 1.7). So the competitor is pitched at the fifth object, and LEAD-tmpu1jj5 names it as the rival (§1.5.5). Until a model out-predicts it, the vocabulary of perspectives is a proposal, not a result.

#### 1.7.2 What character theory gives, and on what condition

The owner's U54 put the priority of characters: "Character's are 'more essential' than the representations that are comprised of them". The record keeps it as classificatory priority (CLAIM-137, Proposed, interpretive):

> A communicative kind is identified by an invariant that does not depend on any one realization, as a group character identifies a representation up to isomorphism, once one symmetry group is identified across the realizations by its action on the situation and not by labels of a medium, so that invariant has classificatory priority over any particular realization.

The record corrects the owner's wording: "The owner's 'comprised of' inverts the mathematics". A representation decomposes into irreducible representations, and its character is computed from it, so "The owner's priority survives the correction" (CLAIM-137). What the claim does not say matters as much:

- not "That classificatory priority is constitution";
- not that a communicative kind is the trace of a group representation; "No such invariant has yet been built for any communicative case".

Three accepted limits bound the analogy.

- **Characters classify kinds up to isomorphism, and only in a narrow setting.** They do so for finite-dimensional representations of a finite or compact group "only over a field of characteristic zero". They "forget the isomorphisms, fix neither the group nor a permutation action, and classify kinds, not tokens or positions" (CLAIM-132, Active, demonstrated, granted).
- **"The same character" depends on a chosen correspondence.** Comparing characters across realizations "needs one group identified on both, which is fixed only up to automorphism, and automorphisms permute the irreducible characters, so 'the same character' is relative to a chosen correspondence" (CLAIM-249, Active, demonstrated, granted).
- **The word "character" is reserved.** It is used literally only "when χ is a trace character of a specified representation". Otherwise the object is a "character-like structural signature" (TERM-041, Active, after A203 §36). This article follows that rule. Where it speaks of an organization's character without a specified representation, it means a character-like signature, a pattern by which realizations are recognized as of one kind (CLAIM-417, item 2). The record already uses the word outside TERM-041's literal sense once, and says so: "agnotological character, of a perspective: a candidate invariant profile of its organized epistemic limitations … not a group character" (TERM-tmp9mag8, Proposed).

The step A49 takes in its §4 is twice unearned on the record. First, character theory is moved from groups to communicative kinds, on a condition that is "still unmet" (CLAIM-137, note of 2026-11-24). Then it is moved from communicative kinds to perspectives, which CLAIM-417 calls "new territory, with CLAIM-249 standing against any easy identification". A49's "without assuming every organizational transformation possesses group structure" concedes the difficulty without meeting it. The first thing the record needs, for any perspective, is a declared set of transformations that count as the same in both realizations. The meme transplantation lead puts it first: "Say which changes of situation count as the same transformation in both populations, the correspondence CLAIM-249 says comparison needs, before comparing profiles" (LEAD-tmpqucxc, Open, idea, step 4).

#### 1.7.3 What a character can see

One result here is Active, and it limits what a character can be used for. It is the assistant's gap fill for A6 §3 of the exchange behind this series, made Active on 2026-11-24 at the assistant's discretion after an independent recomputation (see "Standing, voices and limits"):

> The character of a perspective's representation detects the transformations it is blind to, not the states it merges: its kernel is the observational invariance subgroup when the projection is equivariant and its image spans, but a projection can merge states no transformation relates while its character shows no blindness at all (CLAIM-tmpqbaic, Active, demonstrated; not a member of the work).

The claim is about one representation, the permutation representation of G on Y_P "as A6 §3 sets them up", and is "Not defeated by another choice of representation" (CLAIM-tmpqbaic). So where a perspective's representation is of that kind, its character profiles its invariances under the chosen transformations. It does not record every distinction the perspective fails to draw.

#### 1.7.4 Memes as characteristics: the owner's U4, and the ruling

At U4 the owner proposed "memes as characteristics (qua representation theory) of perspectives". The record filed it in two readings. On 2026-11-24 the owner ruled: "I think both readings are valid. I mainly meant 'meme as value'". The two are no longer alternatives. Both are Proposed and interpretive, and neither is a member of the work.

- **Primary (CLAIM-tmp4mk0t).** "Memes are characteristic values of perspectives: each records how a perspective responds to one kind of transformation of a situation, so a family of memes samples a perspective's character without being it".
- **Beside it (CLAIM-tmphi3mx).** "what a meme carries between carriers is a realization-independent invariant by which a perspective is recognized, not a particular content".

Both owe CLAIM-249's group before they can be tested. A49 Art. 7 §3 declines "identifying memes literally with mathematical characters". It states A's looser thesis instead, which is a member: "memetic fidelity can be the preservation of characteristic relations rather than the copying of content" (CLAIM-tmpjitli, Proposed, interpretive; in the claim's wording, after A5 §8). That thesis names CLAIM-249 as what it "Must answer".

The owner's ruling added a further point. "persistent reproduction of a meme can nucleate related but distinct perspectives (e.g. 'death of the author' type phenomena)": circulation "can diversify the perspectives it touches rather than copy one" (CLAIM-tmp590sw, Proposed, interpretive). The claim rests on the owner's U2 and on the primary reading. Barthes's essay is not yet registered in the record. The point bears on §1.4: a seed can nucleate more than one organization. It bears on §1.6 too, since related perspectives that descend from one meme are linked by lineage through the meme, not by sharing a character.

Whether a family of memes inherits CLAIM-tmpqbaic's limit is not a further result, and this article does not offer it as one. The record already says it, for memes that express characteristic limitation: "If it means a group character, CLAIM-tmpqbaic shows the character sees only the transformations a perspective is blind to, not the states it merges" (CLAIM-tmpjlxfy, Proposed, interpretive, "Gaps"). A sample of a character's values carries no more than the character, and less where the probes are few (CLAIM-tmp4mk0t, "Where the record stands"). What is open is the representation. CLAIM-tmpqbaic holds for A6's permutation representation, and CLAIM-tmp4mk0t's "character" is not identified with it.

#### 1.7.5 Contracts in the characteristic structure

A16 §19 of the same exchange proposed that "The characteristic structure of an organizational individual may consequently include a maintained ecology of interprocess contracts". The record filed it narrowed, in A's wording:

> An organization's characteristic structure can include the contracts maintained among its processes, which its ordinary behaviour does not reveal: two arrangements alike on the ordinary course can part when a contract is breached, an assumption fails or a process invokes its standing (CLAIM-tmpersgx, Proposed, interpretive; on CASE-tmptpna1, Proposed).

Its separating case is priority inversion. Fixed priorities and priority inheritance give the same schedule until a lock is contended. The probes that part such arrangements are all interventions: "interruption of the relied-upon behaviour; a substitute provider; competing demands on the provider; removal of an enforcer; withdrawal of a discretionary contribution; a request to renegotiate" (CLAIM-tmpersgx, note of 2026-11-24, carrying over from the Superseded CLAIM-tmp1nubb). That fits CLAIM-364 and CLAIM-522. Two cautions apply:

- "Characteristic structure" in this claim is not TERM-041's character. It means what belongs to the organization rather than to its circumstances. CLAIM-417's objects do not list contracts.
- What the claim's premises leave open, and the claim asserts, is exactly that the difference "belongs to the organization's character … rather than to its circumstances".

Article 2 develops contracts. Here the claim is one more instance of §1.5: matched ordinary behaviour, parted only by intervention.

### 1.8 Agency as a further achievement

A49's last section asks why individuation alone does not establish autonomy, consciousness, moral standing or legitimate authority. The record holds each of these separations in its own entry.

- **Not autonomy.** "Persistence without autonomy. A pattern can be maintained entirely by external supply" (CLAIM-474). Individuation without autonomy is TERM-088's convection roll.
- **Not agency.** "Possessing, enacting or being shaped by an organizational perspective does not establish an independent agent, and perspectival, agential and institutional boundaries need not coincide" (CLAIM-418, Active, interpretive). Whether every rich exercise of agency involves a perspective stays open. A31, in CLAIM-417: "Recognizing an organizational perspective does not license attributing desires, intentions, authority, ownership, or experience to that perspective without independent justification".
- **Not reflexive governance, and not governing well.** Reflexive governance is "a capacity some organizations of perspective have and others lack, not what makes something a perspective; and the capacity to revise those standards is not the capacity to revise them well" (CLAIM-424, Active, interpretive).
- **Not experience.** Phenomenal perspective is TERM-072's third sense, "A lived point of view", kept apart from the organizational sense, and the subject sense of "self" "stays a separate question" (CLAIM-417).
- **Not standing or legitimacy.** "Viability does not establish what ought to persist": "An unjust institution may be highly viable" (CLAIM-075, Active, interpretive, disclaimed). For moral standing, "The grounds and extent of standing remain questions for ethical argument, not conclusions of the definition" (TERM-083, Proposed, the owner's).

Set side by side, these separations are A49's list. *Putting them in one list adds nothing to them* (J1.7, a conjunction of held entries). The section's point is the order they keep. Individuation, in any of TERM-088's senses, is compatible with every one of these further achievements failing. Later articles in the series must establish each one they need on its own evidence.
