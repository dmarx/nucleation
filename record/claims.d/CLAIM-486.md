# CLAIM-486: A rendering can keep the communicative act while changing the proposition, so preserving the proposition is not necessary for preserving the communicative event

## The claim

A12: "you can alter many of the propositions while preserving the
speaker's attitude, social position, and communicative intention—and the
result may feel more faithful." The manuscript states the half in §1:
preserving the proposition is not necessary for preserving the
communicative event.

[CASE-056](../cases.d/CASE-056.md) is the documented case: Phillips renders Paul's "holy kiss" as
"a hearty handshake all round", which changes what the verse directs, and
Nida judged that it keeps the greeting. CASE-031 was checked as a candidate and
does not fill the role: the renderings it reaches change form, register
and tone and keep Marot's propositions (its note of 2026-10-10).

## Why it is Proposed

- **The manuscript does not argue it** ([CLAIM-053](CLAIM-053.md), granted): it argues
  insufficiency only.
- **The invented case does not show it** ([CLAIM-229](CLAIM-229.md), granted): [CASE-018](../cases.d/CASE-018.md)
  holds the words fixed and bears on sufficiency only.
- **The documented case is contested** ([CASE-056](../cases.d/CASE-056.md)'s own limits). The
  verdicts are expert judgements, divided on the residue, with no reader
  evidence. And the defender of necessity has a reply: the proposition
  worth keeping is the coarser "greet one another warmly", which the
  handshake keeps. That reply is this claim's defeated_if.

It goes Active if a documented case changes the proposition at every
grain a defender could choose, with independent evidence the act was kept.

## Note of 2026-10-29: what holds it back

The one documented case, CASE-056, is answered by the defender's reply that
its defeat condition states: the handshake keeps the coarser proposition
"greet one another warmly". No case in the record changes the proposition at
every grain a defender could choose, with independent evidence that the act
was kept.
