# THEORY-tmp8mi75: Postman holds that technological change is a trade-off whose costs and benefits fall unevenly, that each technology carries an ideological bias that reshapes a culture's terms, that its effects are ecological, vast, often unpredictable and largely irreversible, and that technologies become mythic, accepted as natural and hard to control

## The account

Postman holds that "Every technology is both a burden and a blessing"; that its benefits and costs fall unevenly; that "Technological change is neither additive nor subtractive. It is ecological."; that its consequences are "always vast, often unpredictable and largely irreversible"; and that mythic technologies are "not easily susceptible to modification or control".
