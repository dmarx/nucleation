# LIT-1208: Binding and the Phenomenal Unity of Consciousness

Revonsuo, A. (1999). Binding and the Phenomenal Unity of Consciousness. *Consciousness and Cognition* 8(2): 173–185. doi:10.1006/ccog.1999.0384. Not to be confused with Revonsuo, A. & Newman, J. (1999), "Binding and Consciousness", the issue's introduction, doi:10.1006/ccog.1999.0393.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmpishg4), from the publisher's PDF on
hedweb.com. It is the paper that draws the distinction, in its own terms
(stimulus-related against consciousness-related binding) and crediting
Smythies; the issue introduction was not read.

## Why this is here

A27 of the exchange gave it as the longer history of the distinction between functional and phenomenal binding. Bears on [CLAIM-355](../claims.d/CLAIM-355.md) and [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md). A reading should first confirm that this paper, not the issue introduction, is the one that draws the distinction.
