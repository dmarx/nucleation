# THEORY-2212: Simić, Sacchi and Perugini report that expecting one's national group to stay the same in the future lowers fear for its future and, through that, prejudice and anxiety toward immigrants, among those strongly attached to the group and not among the weakly attached

## Source

Simić, Sacchi and Perugini (2025), [LIT-3081](../literature.d/LIT-3081.md), read in
[NOTE-2302](../notes.d/NOTE-2302.md).

## The account

Future collective continuity (FCC) is the group-level counterpart of
future-self continuity: how similar the group will be, how vividly its
future can be imagined, and how positively it is judged. Its similarity
component reassures members that what they value in the group will last.
That lowers collective angst, fear for the group's future, and lower
angst goes with less hostility to outgroups and more willingness to act
for them. Because the threat matters to those attached to the group, the
reassurance works for high identifiers and not for low ones.

## What was actually shown

- Two correlational studies (N = 202, 200): FCC similarity and positivity
  went with prosocial beliefs about Italians toward foreigners and with
  less prejudice toward immigrants, through less collective angst.
- A preregistered experiment (N = 252): videos and a writing task moved
  FCC similarity (d = 0.90) and lowered collective angst (d = −0.38) and
  outgroup anxiety (d = −0.46). Prejudice and collective action
  intentions moved only with political orientation added as a
  covariate. Indirect effects through angst were significant at +1 SD of
  national attachment and not at −1 SD.

Each could have come out otherwise: the manipulation could have left
angst alone, and the indirect effect could have held at both levels of
attachment.

## What this does not say

- **Not concern for future people.** The outcome is attitudes toward
  present immigrants; the future in question is the group's.
- **Not that continuity raises care for what continues.** It lowered fear
  for the group, which is the opposite direction from a larger self
  caring more.
- **Not behaviour.** Beliefs, attitudes and intentions only.
- **Not a robust moderation.** The moderated mediation departed from the
  preregistration, and attachment here merges love of the nation with its
  glorification, which is closer to caring than to the self's extent.
