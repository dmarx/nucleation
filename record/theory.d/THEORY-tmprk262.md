# THEORY-tmprk262: Botnan and Lesnick report that multiparameter persistence modules are of wild representation type, so that no barcode-like complete invariant is practical and good barcodes need not exist, while the interleaving distance extends the bottleneck distance with stability and universality but is NP-hard to compute for two or more parameters

## Source

Botnan & Lesnick (2022; 2023), [LIT-tmp1045q](../literature.d/LIT-tmp1045q.md); read in [NOTE-tmp6e0rl](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp6e0rl.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Wild type** (§1.1, §8.2): stated, with Drozd's trichotomy (§8.1);
  "the representation type of a commutative grid is wild even for small
  grid sizes".
- **No good barcode** (Example 4.6): proved by a short contradiction on a
  3 × 3 grid.
- **Incomplete rank invariant** for N² (§4.2).
- **Stability and universality** (§6.1; Theorem 6.7, prime fields).
- **NP-hardness** (§1.2): reported from the literature.

## What this does not say

- That multiparameter modules cannot be compared: the metrics are well
  behaved, only hard to compute.
- That useful incomplete invariants are lacking: several are surveyed.
- Anything about direction or reachability.
