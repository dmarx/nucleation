# NOTE-tmpfpn7t: Tronto — Against Good Caring

## Contribution

Tronto's own compressed statement of Caring Democracy's political
definition, and of exemption, the means by which the privileged absent
themselves from care.

## Key results

- **"Neoliberal care"**: "Good care = personal responsibility + market";
  "If you don't have enough resources to care well, it is your own fault".
- **"Political account"**: "Democracy as the allocation of caring
  responsibilities in a political community and the method of giving
  equal voice to all members of the community in making these
  allocations".
- **"Inclusion and Exemption"**: "Exemption: absent oneself from
  responsibilities because one has more important duties: protection,
  production, taking care of one's own"; and "Exclusion v. inclusion: as a
  way to allocate care responsibilities is to exclude those you want to
  saddle with the most difficult tasks".
- **"Ethical"**: "Responsibilities v. obligations".
- **Care defined** (slide "Caring Democracy", Fisher and Tronto 1990,
  cited as Tronto 1993, 103): "everything that we do to maintain,
  continue, and repair our 'world' so that we can live in it as well as
  possible."

## Assumptions

Care is relational, and all humans give and receive it. Neoliberal market
society is "utopian" in Polanyi's sense.

## Concepts

- **Democratic caring** against **neoliberal care**.
- **Allocation of caring responsibilities**, with equal voice.
- **Exclusion** and **exemption** as misallocations.
- **Responsibilities** against **obligations**.

## Bearing on the record

Filed as [THEORY-tmpxw0zo](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpxw0zo.md).

- **Which of the originals' results it restates.** For Caring Democracy
  ([LIT-2669](../literature.d/LIT-2669.md)): the definition of democracy as the allocation of caring
  responsibilities with equal voice, in Tronto's words, which bears out
  A17's "democratic justice" in substance; and exemption, which is the
  book's privileged irresponsibility in outline. For Moral Boundaries
  ([LIT-2708](../literature.d/LIT-2708.md)): only the definition of care it quotes.
- **[CLAIM-707](../claims.d/CLAIM-707.md)**, the upstream half: exemption by appeal to "more
  important duties", and exclusion to "saddle" others "with the most
  difficult tasks", are Tronto's own version of upstream parties shedding
  obligations onto those downstream. Corroborates; blame and control are
  not addressed.
- **[QUESTION-tmpvoufs](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpvoufs.md)**: "giving equal voice to all members ... in making
  these allocations" is a democratic route to the accountability the
  question asks about, not a principal–agent mechanism.

## Limitations

Slides: claims are telegraphic and unargued, and many slides quote
others.
