# CLAIM-417: What the record explained by "self" in its dispositional, persisting, self-governing and integrated senses is better explained by organizational perspectives, with a perspective, its carriers, its lineage and its representations kept apart; the subject sense stays a separate question, and narrative, presented and modeled selves involve representations that may describe a perspective, attribute it to a bearer or construct an owner, none of them the perspective itself

## The claim

The owner, at U31: "tie this back to nucleation's earlier treatment of
'selves', which I think we've essentially since determined are better
described as 'organizational perspectives'". The term the owner meant is
[TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md), filed that morning from U8–U11 of the exchange on experience and
interpretation, whose sense 1 is the organizational perspective and whose
"What it is not" begins "Not a self". The replies of 2026-10-22 did not
cite it.

A31 agreed, with a narrowing: "the phenomenon we were originally trying to
explain by invoking 'selves' is better understood as the emergence,
persistence, and transformation of organizational perspectives. That
doesn't mean the concept of a self has become useless. It means *selfhood
was carrying several explanatory burdens that we can now separate*." Its
table translated each burden and left one standing: "A self is one
subject", a separate question. Set against [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md)'s senses, the claim
is:

- **Sense 2, dispositional or extended**, becomes a perspective's
  dispositional repertoire and the resources coupled to it (habitus, in
  the sociology the exchange leaned on: [LIT-1377](../literature.d/LIT-1377.md), [LIT-1367](../literature.d/LIT-1367.md)).
- **Sense 3, persisting**, becomes the persistence of a perspective or of
  its carrier, in one of [TERM-071](../terms.d/TERM-071.md)'s senses, and these come apart (below).
- **Sense 5, self-governing**, becomes reflexive governance of a
  perspective, a special achievement and not part of what a perspective
  is ([CLAIM-424](CLAIM-424.md), re-scoping [CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md)).
- **Sense 6, integrated**, becomes the organization of a perspective:
  [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) sense 1, in which interpretations are mutually answerable
  without having to agree.
- **Senses 4 and 7, narrative and presented, and the "modeled" sense**
  [TERM-045](../terms.d/TERM-045.md)'s note of 2026-10-18 flagged, involve representations, and
  representations of different things. One may describe an organizational
  perspective ([TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) sense 2); one may attribute it to a continuing
  bearer; one may construct an owner of the activity represented, which is
  [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s represented owner and [CLAIM-355](CLAIM-355.md)'s construction. These are
  distinct achievements with distinct errors, and none is the perspective
  itself. A narrative self can represent the history of a person, with
  relationships, actions and obligations that are not aspects of one
  perspective; a presented self may concern a role and impressions, not an
  interpretive standpoint. A narrative can also be causally constitutive
  of a person's practical organization, not only an account of something
  complete without it. A31: "A self-model is a representation that
  attributes some portion of an organized perspective and its activity to
  a bounded, continuing owner." (Narrowed the same day after a review the
  owner relayed; the first version said these selves "become
  representations of a perspective".)
- **Sense 1, subject**, is untouched. Nothing here says whether an
  organizational perspective is a phenomenal one; that is [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s
  sense 3 and [CLAIM-412](CLAIM-412.md)'s bridge.

What persuaded the exchange was that the burdens separate in cases. Clive
Wearing keeps skilled and short-horizon organization while
autobiographical continuity fails ([CASE-137](../cases.d/CASE-137.md)). A party keeps its name
and organization while its coalition and character change
([CASE-133](../cases.d/CASE-133.md)). A martial art's technique, lineage, institution and story
can each continue without the others ([CASE-130](../cases.d/CASE-130.md)). A commander hands
off an incident and the organization continues with no one continuous
([CASE-134](../cases.d/CASE-134.md)). In each the self vocabulary asks "is it the same self?"
and gets no answer; the perspective vocabulary asks which of four things
continued.

## What the replacement has to keep apart

The exchange's own perspective was mostly a kind or a lineage: "A
perspective may have many bearers", it can "migrate between parties", be
"realized across multiple persons and institutions" (A31, A32). It was
also a token: a person "may enact several perspectives". [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) sense 1
is a token, "a dynamically realized regime". So the claim needs four
objects, which A37 and A40 tabulated in different forms and the claim
fixes as:

1. **A perspective as realized** ([TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) sense 1). Its realization need
   not lie inside one pre-individuated carrier. Several people or
   institutions can share a kind, or a history, without forming one
   operative perspective; but a continuously interacting school, or an
   emergency response across agencies, may realize one perspective through
   the relations among its participants, where integration of
   interpretation and practical consequence across them can be shown
   independently. The claim does not stipulate that such distributed
   realizations exist, and does not rule them out by requiring one
   carrier. Whether one is an agent is a further question
   ([QUESTION-260](../questions.d/QUESTION-260.md)).
2. **Its character**, a pattern by which realizations are recognized as of
   one kind. Two unrelated traditions can share one. The record's
   character theory ([CLAIM-137](CLAIM-137.md)) is scoped to communicative kinds and is
   Proposed; extending it to perspectives is new territory, with [CLAIM-249](CLAIM-249.md)
   standing against any easy identification, as A36 noted.
3. **Its lineage**, a chain of causal transmission from one realization to
   the next. [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) holds that a collective persists by this and not by
   resemblance.
4. **Its representations**: self-descriptions, narratives, reputations,
   which can misdescribe the other three ([CLAIM-355](CLAIM-355.md), [CLAIM-415](CLAIM-415.md)).

A40: "Resemblance ≠ Historical descent ≠ Numerical organizational
identity." [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md), adopted on 2026-10-29, takes them as relations among
realizations, not as further senses.

## What it does not say

- **Not that perspectives are agents.** A31: "Recognizing an
  organizational perspective does not license attributing desires,
  intentions, authority, ownership, or experience to that perspective
  without independent justification." It kept the objection that "We could
  simply move the reification one level down." A40: "A perspective can be
  real as a causally important organization without itself wanting
  anything." This also leaves behind the will-organization account's
  supposition of an independently warranted agent ([CLAIM-080](CLAIM-080.md)), so the
  perspective account is a sibling of that account, not its extension, as
  [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md)'s "Not a will-organization" already says.
- **Not that persons, subjects or self-models are unreal.** A40,
  Objection 6: "Individuals remain distinct organisms, persons, and
  possible subjects of experience."
- **Not a new sociology.** After the owner's U30 ("we basically have all
  of sociology to lean on here"), every reply conceded the ground. A40,
  Objection 2: "That's largely correct. The proposed contribution should
  not be novelty by redescription." What the claim adds is the record's
  distinctions, and it is defeated if they buy nothing.
- **Not that the gluing account transfers.** [CLAIM-153](CLAIM-153.md)'s global section is
  "one coherent self" in senses 3 and 6. Under this claim its object
  becomes a carrier's several perspectives failing to glue, which is
  [CLAIM-196](CLAIM-196.md)'s segregated contexts. [TERM-072](../terms.d/TERM-072.md) says a perspective is "Not a
  global section", and [QUESTION-255](../questions.d/QUESTION-255.md) asks whether one can contain models
  that do not glue. The replies never mentioned gluing.

## Note of 2026-10-22: the social theory, read

- **Swidler and Wacquant disagree about sense 2.** The claim cites both
  for "a perspective's dispositional repertoire". Swidler's repertoire is
  not dispositional: culture does not work "via enduring psychological
  proclivities implanted in individuals by their socialization" ([LIT-1367](../literature.d/LIT-1367.md),
  p. 283). Wacquant's habitus is exactly such dispositions, "deposited
  inside persons" ([LIT-1377](../literature.d/LIT-1377.md), p. 65). The claim should say which it means.
  On Swidler, the organized thing is the strategy of action built from the
  kit, not the kit.
- **The defeater is live.** Habitus as Wacquant states it is already
  layered and can be split, shared as a kind and unique as a token, and
  distinct from the positions that call it out; Sewell's transposition of
  schemas is a theory of how one structure changes into another. Neither
  has the claim's lineage, its distinction between a lineage and a
  character, or a perspective realized across several people; that is
  what the claim must show it adds.
- **Collective agency without collective agents.** Sewell holds that
  collective agency is real without collectivities being agents in the
  same sense as persons (p. 21), which is [CLAIM-418](CLAIM-418.md)'s point reached from
  structure. And for him one person enacting several incompatible
  structures is the normal case: persons hold "a wide range of different
  and even incompatible schemas" (p. 17).
- **Abbott.** Read only through a 2003 precursor ([NOTE-1077](../notes.d/NOTE-1077.md)): it is
  about jurisdictions and alliances, not perspectives.

## Note of 2026-10-29: deprovincialization, read

The contact works the exchange cited for a perspective revised by being
made one of several are now read. Pettigrew's 1998 review, [LIT-1408](../literature.d/LIT-1408.md)
([NOTE-1408](../notes.d/NOTE-1408.md)), names the nearest process. The fourth of its four
processes of contact is ingroup reappraisal: optimal contact shows that
"ingroup norms and customs turn out not to be the only ways to manage the
social world", which "can reshape your view of your ingroup and lead to a
less provincial view of outgroups in general ('deprovincialization')"
(p. 72). The word occurs once. Its evidence is thin: one unpublished
analysis relating outgroup friendship to less national pride, and one
laboratory study. Pettigrew and Tropp's 2008 meta-analysis of mediators,
[LIT-1405](../literature.d/LIT-1405.md) ([NOTE-1405](../notes.d/NOTE-1405.md)), tests knowledge, anxiety and empathy with
perspective taking, and lists "broadened views of the ingroup" only among
the mediators not tested. Its perspective-taking leg, the nearest it
comes, could not be separated from empathy, and nothing in it tests a
change in how one's own view is held. The claim is unchanged.
