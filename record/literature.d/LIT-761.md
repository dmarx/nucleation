# LIT-761: Argumentation-based design rationale: what use at what cost?

Simon Buckingham Shum and Nick Hammond (1994), *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies* 40(4):603–652 — DOI-10.1006/ijhc.1994.1029

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* As secondary descriptions report it: design
rationale is a representation of the reasoning behind the design of an
artifact. The article reviews why rationale has been expressed as design
argumentation, and surveys design studies for when and how building explicit
argumentation during design is productive. It then discusses the cognitive,
organizational and technological factors that could ease the uptake of
rationale systems. Its findings on cost are not known here.
