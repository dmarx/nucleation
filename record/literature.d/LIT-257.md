# LIT-257: What Representational Similarity Measures Imply about Decodable Information

Sarah E. Harvey, David Lipshutz, Alex H. Williams (2024), *Proceedings of the II edition of the Workshop on Unifying Representations in Neural Models (UniReps 2024), as printed on the arXiv PDF; first appeared as arXiv preprint (proceedings volume and pages unverified)* — [ARXIV-2411.08197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08197)

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpcc47a](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpcc47a.md).*

- **One matrix.** The readout maximizing zᵀXw/M − ½wᵀG(X)w outputs
  Xw* = K_X z with K_X = XG(X)⁻¹Xᵀ/M (eqs. 2, 7–8). The best and worst
  task alignments are the extreme eigenvalues of ½(K_X K_Y + K_Y K_X)
  (Prop. 1). The average is Tr(K_X K_z K_Y) (Prop. 2).
- **The family.** With K_z = I (Cor. 1, Table 1, eqs. 17–21, re-derived):
  - G = bI gives linear CKA, a ratio of average similarities;
  - G = C_X gives mean squared CCA, with K_X the projection onto col(X);
  - G = C_X + λI gives the plug-in GULP distance, an average distance;
  - a scaled identity gives ENSD.
- **Invariance.** K_X is invariant under orthogonal maps when
  G = aC_X + bI (eqs. 22–23). Only for CCA is it invariant under every
  invertible linear map.
- **Procrustes against decoding (Prop. 3, App. A.1).**
  P⁴/R_Δ ≤ E‖Xw* − Yv*‖² ≤ (2(α + β)P² − P⁴)/R_Δ. So a small Procrustes
  distance forces a small decoding distance, but not conversely. The
  Procrustes–Bures equivalence is imported from [LIT-250](LIT-250.md).
- **The population limit is a sketch (App. A.3).** It needs b > 0, so it
  excludes CCA. It asserts concentration, and eq. 77 has a stray factor.
  It treats a trace-class task covariance, and at K_z = I its limit is
  zero.
