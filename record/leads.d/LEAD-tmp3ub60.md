# LEAD-tmp3ub60: Measure the dancing couple: is the pair a gap-isolated block during the dance and not after it, with direction, compensation and the time-lagged form of the block criterion

## What it would do

State the time-lagged block criterion, then apply it to motion-capture
data of dancing pairs (a reanalysis of Bigand and colleagues is the
candidate), with direction, a perturbation and compensation test, and the
block gap measured during and after the dance.

Where the record names it:

- QUESTION-035: "the couple after the song must come out two."
- CLAIM-157: "CASE-045 is the study it would need."
- CLAIM-157: "the notebook's coupling needs the time-lagged test
  QUESTION-116 asks for"
- CASE-045: "a reanalysis candidate"

## What would count

One block during the dance and two after supports CLAIM-157 and gives
QUESTION-035 a criterion that returned a verdict that could have been
wrong. A block that persists after the song, or none during it, defeats
CLAIM-157 as stated.
