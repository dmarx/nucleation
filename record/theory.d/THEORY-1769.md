# THEORY-1769: Gottweis et al. (2026) report that in Co-Scientist, Gemini agents that generate, critique, rank by simulated-debate Elo tournaments and evolve research hypotheses produce top hypotheses whose Elo rises with computation across 203 research goals

## The account

Gottweis et al. (2026) report that in Co-Scientist, Gemini agents that generate, critique, rank by simulated-debate Elo tournaments and evolve research hypotheses produce top hypotheses whose Elo rises with computation across 203 research goals. On 11 goals experts preferred these hypotheses and rated their novelty and impact above other models'. Some of its candidates were confirmed in vitro with experts in the loop. The authors state that the Elo is self-evaluated, not grounded in independent truth, and that validation is preliminary.
