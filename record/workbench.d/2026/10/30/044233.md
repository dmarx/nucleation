**CLAIM-282 narrowed, and a construction filed.**

- **The construction.** The new claim CLAIM-tmphnxf0 is Active and
  demonstrated. It uses leave-one-out delegation: each operator's authority
  is computed by the damped recursive rule, with that operator's own
  endorsements removed.
- **What it shows.** Authority still reaches each operator along paths of
  any length, but it is exactly independent of the operator's own
  endorsements.
- **CLAIM-282.** That falsified CLAIM-282's "only one-hop allocation" and
  its general "recycle". CLAIM-282 stays Active, narrowed to its body
  (ADR-047 kind 3), and it records the objection.
- **The checks.** The reviewer checked each step with controls
  (p19/claim282_loo.py). Step 3's derivative was re-derived by hand here:
  ∂g_k/∂E_ij = (1 − η)η M_kj (Mb)_i ≥ 0.
- **CLAIM-285.** It now rests on the construction, and stays Proposed.
  Beyond one hop, the incentive to delegate to a nearer ally holds in 1,772
  of 2,141 trials, not in all of them, and no population model shows
  operators delegating.
