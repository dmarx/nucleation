# LIT-1079: Improving automatic query expansion

Mitra, M., Singhal, A. and Buckley, C. (1998). Improving automatic query expansion. In *Proceedings of the 21st Annual International ACM SIGIR Conference on Research and Development in Information Retrieval*, 206–214. DOI 10.1145/290941.290995

Read in full on 2026-10-30 ([NOTE-tmpzgzg8](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzgzg8.md)), from the ACM proceedings text
re-hosted by SciSpace.

## Why this is here

The owner suspected at U21 that agent harnesses rediscover older information retrieval. A21 cited relevance feedback and the query-drift problem. This is a standard source for query drift, which [TERM-056](../terms.d/TERM-056.md) distinguishes from question drift and [CLAIM-301](../claims.d/CLAIM-301.md) builds on. Rocchio's relevance feedback (1971) is the older source and is not registered, having no DOI. The reading confirms that query drift, in this paper, is the query moving off a topic that stays fixed ([NOTE-tmpzgzg8](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpzgzg8.md)).
