# LIT-1743: Preregistered Replication and Extension of “Moral Hypocrisy: Social Groups and the Flexibility of Virtue”

Robertson, C. E., Akles, M. & Van Bavel, J. J. (2024). *Psychological Science*, 35(7), 798–813.

Read in full in [NOTE-1245](../notes.d/NOTE-1245.md), from the authors' accepted manuscript.

## Why this is here

The nearest preregistered test found of minimal-group favouritism, asked for after Hornsey ([NOTE-1253](../notes.d/NOTE-1253.md)) and Reimer et al. ([NOTE-1251](../notes.d/NOTE-1251.md)) leaned on 1970s studies. It is not a direct replication of Tajfel et al. 1971 ([LIT-1697](LIT-1697.md)); a search on 2026-10-29 found none at scale.
