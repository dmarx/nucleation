## The verdict, in brief

Ott and Antonsen's reduction is exact on an invariant manifold, needs no
small parameter, and (by the 2009 sequel) attracts the order parameter for
any frequency spread with no rate condition. CLAIM-478's defeater (a) asks
for a constraint in place "although nothing they consist of is slower or
more persistent than that process", and counts its candidates only if
typical. CLAIM-tmpa694z holds that an exact invariance is persistence in
the limit, as CLAIM-478's own note of 2026-11-02 ruled for conservation
laws, and that the class is narrow (CLAIM-tmpo6ehq; and Caprioglio and
Berthouze needed a large gap to use the reduction on a network).
CLAIM-tmpervl4 holds that the off-manifold parts are averaged out by
dephasing, not held by anything slower, and that the class is standard for
synchronization, so (a) is met within it. The assistant leans to the
first. Which reading of "more persistent" (a) intends is the owner's.
