# THEORY-tmps3a0q: Morozov argues that solutionism, recasting political and moral problems as technical problems to be optimized with sensors, data and incentives, leaves structural causes untouched, displaces moral motivation, and can make a nominally voluntary technology such as self-tracking a penalty on those who decline it

## The account

Morozov argues that once most people self-track for advantage, those who refuse "will be treated with suspicion", so that it is "no longer an autonomous decision" when institutions punish not self-tracking; reform should aim "to reform the system itself", not optimize behaviour within it.
