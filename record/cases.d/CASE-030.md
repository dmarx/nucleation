# CASE-030: E2: assistant-authored reconstruction chains with content-word overlap (C4)

## The case

A62: "The runtime check found no accessible language-model inference endpoint or
locally installed model weights. I can generate reconstruction chains ... in
this conversation, but that is not equivalent to running independent, blinded
model calls." C4's recorded provenance: "Assistant-authored sequential
demonstration, one conversation; not independent model invocations or a blinded
evaluation." Seed: "You can hustle all week, win a bet, or work an honest job.
Come Friday, you spend your earnings on drinking, flirting, and celebrating. By
Monday the money is gone, and you are ready to do it all again. The storyteller
sounds like a knowing friend, amused rather than outraged."

Content-word Jaccard with the seed, as A63's chart states it:

| Generation | stable_complicit | shifting_frames | comic_to_moral |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.1667 | 0.1081 | 0 |
| 2 | 0.0714 | 0.0238 | 0.0476 |
| 3 | 0.1 | 0 | 0.0556 |
| 4 | 0.0789 | 0 | 0 |

Final texts: "The paycheck lands, the party takes it, and by Monday you're broke.
Your friend grins: same time next week?"; "Poor financial discipline can
undermine long-term stability. A responsible person should limit discretionary
spending and save consistently."; "Hard-earned income should be saved instead
of wasted on short-lived pleasures. Financial responsibility matters more than
partying."

## What it can show

A63: "the stable-frame chain loses much of the original wording while retaining
the implied friend's affectionate complicity ... Because I authored these chains
with their intended trajectories in mind, their differences demonstrate the
proposed *measurement approach*, not a discovered effect." The metric is
lexical; the retention of complicity is the author's reading, not a
measurement. It illustrates [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md) and can test nothing.
