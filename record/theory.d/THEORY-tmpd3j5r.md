# THEORY-tmpd3j5r: Doyle's truth maintenance system records, for each belief, the justifications offered for it and holds a belief exactly when at least one justification is valid, a justification being valid when the beliefs it lists as required are held and those it lists as absent are not; contradictions are resolved by tracing support to the latest assumptions, recording the inconsistent set, and retracting one of them chosen by the backtracker, which may blame the wrong one. `

## The account

Doyle's truth maintenance system records, for each belief, the justifications offered for it and
holds a belief exactly when at least one justification is valid, a justification being valid when
the beliefs it lists as required are held and those it lists as absent are not; contradictions are
resolved by tracing support to the latest assumptions, recording the inconsistent set, and
retracting one of them chosen by the backtracker, which may blame the wrong one.
