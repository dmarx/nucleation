# NOTE-022: Spectral Networks and Betti Lagrangians

## Contribution

The authors develop spectral networks within real contact and symplectic topology. They prove that spectral networks exist for Lagrangian fillings in the cotangent bundle of a smooth surface and characterise them pseudo-holomorphically, via adiabatic degeneration of Floer trajectories and explicit continuation strips. They build a Family Floer functor for fillings with a spectral network and show it is equivalent to the non-abelianization functor. As a result, framed 2d-4d BPS states and GMN non-abelianized parallel transport appear inside the A∞-operations of 4d partially wrapped Fukaya categories. They also relate spectral networks to Lagrangian fillings through Demazure weaves and to augmentations of the Legendrian contact dg-algebra.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- Motivation: GMN (2012-14) spectral networks computed BPS degeneracies in 4d N=2 class S theories; the paper aims to give rigorous symplectic foundations to physics claims (§1, §1.1).
- Key generalisation: the real (not holomorphic) cotangent bundle and only an almost complex structure, which allows non-meromorphic asymptotics set by arbitrary positive braids and so covers wild Betti moduli beyond WKB networks (§1.1, p. 3-4).
- Extends the authors' earlier rank-2 work (Casals-Murphy 2018; Nho 2024) to arbitrary rank SL_n using Legendrian weaves (§1.1, p. 4).
- The body proceeds through Betti Lagrangians and flowlines (§2), Morse spectral networks and 2d-4d BPS indices (§3), adiabatic convergence (§4), Family Floer vs non-abelianization (§5), Fukaya categories (§6) and weaves/Demazure computations (§7) (Contents).
- Claims to prove physics conjectures, including a pseudo-holomorphic-disk characterisation and higher-order Stokes phenomena such as the Berk-Nevins-Roberts example (§1).
