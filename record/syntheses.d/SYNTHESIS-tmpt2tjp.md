# SYNTHESIS-tmpt2tjp: The dependence-and-domination line is two accounts that meet where the controllers of an arrangement are named as those who owe: what wholes and parts owe and claim, and what those who govern an arrangement others depend on owe; the line makes no claim beyond its parts, and its parts relate through one complement

*The account of the dependence-and-domination line, "Wholes, parts and dependence", split from organizational-normativity (ADR-tmpbthzh) at the owner's approval of 2026-11-15 ("yes on the two line split"). It is a line account over the accounts of its two parts (ADR-083, ADR-084), which were filed as parts of organizational-normativity at that line's synthesis review the same day. "Organization" is TERM-074's sense 1, a relational arrangement, by default, and the bearer of an obligation is kept apart from its ground; each part account says which sense each component declares, and assigns none where a component declares none.*

## The parts

- **SYNTHESIS-092, what a whole and its parts can owe and claim** (20
  components). The owner's QUESTION-460, ground and bearer kept apart,
  with the owner's CASE-256 and CLAIM-671 (vulnerability to a responsible
  agent, not integration); A's QUESTION-461 with CASE-257 and
  QUESTION-463; A's CLAIM-669 and CLAIM-670, two routes into exploitation
  kept apart; the assistant's TERM-123, TERM-124, QUESTION-468, CLAIM-690
  and CASE-264 (control over the terms sorts a dependence); A's CLAIM-682,
  the domination threshold, with the assistant's CLAIM-686 and A's
  CASE-261.
- **SYNTHESIS-093, what those who govern an arrangement others depend on
  owe** (9). A's CLAIM-678, CLAIM-684, CLAIM-687, CLAIM-673, CLAIM-688 and
  CLAIM-685, TERM-122, QUESTION-464 and ARG-103, all from "Analyze
  technological civilization trap" (2026-11-13).

Every one of the line's 29 entries is integrated by exactly one part
account. Several also carry another line and are integrated again by that
line's accounts, which the part accounts name: distributed-agency
(QUESTION-461, CASE-257, CLAIM-690, CASE-264, and QUESTION-464, whose first
line it is), transient-support (CLAIM-669, CLAIM-670, CLAIM-671,
CLAIM-673), will-organization (CLAIM-686), and organizational-normativity
(QUESTION-460, QUESTION-468, CLAIM-685, integrated directly by
SYNTHESIS-038).

## Where they meet

Each link below is a front-matter relation of the entry named first,
unless a body is cited.

- **The threshold and what is owed before it.** CLAIM-687 (fourth part)
  complements CLAIM-682 (third). Its body: who owes is "those who
  control" the arrangement, "the agents CLAIM-682's second condition
  names", and its summary calls it a scaling rule for what is owed before
  CLAIM-682's threshold.
- **Ground and bearer, in bodies.** TERM-122's summary keeps "the parties
  who are owed, and the bearers of what is owed" apart "as QUESTION-460
  keeps them".
- **Who owes.** Every component of either part that names a bearer names
  an agent or a role: CLAIM-671 "a responsible agent", CLAIM-690 the
  agent with discretion over the terms, TERM-124 "an agent or role",
  CLAIM-682 and CLAIM-687 "those who control" the arrangement, CASE-256's
  third result "agents or roles maintaining the arrangement". This is a
  map of the components as filed, as SYNTHESIS-092 says, not a thesis.

Across to organizational-normativity (SYNTHESIS-038): QUESTION-460 refines
QUESTION-128, QUESTION-468 refines QUESTION-129, and CLAIM-685 bears on
QUESTION-128, so those three carry that line second (ADR-085); CLAIM-687
complements CLAIM-624; and TERM-122, TERM-123 and TERM-124 make their
standards relata declared with the judgment, as CLAIM-363 requires.

Across to other lines, by dependency: the third part rests on CLAIM-428
(CLAIM-690, ARG-110), CLAIM-603 (CLAIM-670) and CLAIM-523 (CLAIM-686); the
fourth on CLAIM-605 (CLAIM-673) and CLAIM-457 (CLAIM-688).

**Whether the parts hang together.** Counted at organizational-normativity's
account, version 4, storing front-matter relations as `line_parts.py`
does: 29 within the third part, 2 within the fourth, and one between
them, CLAIM-687's complement onto CLAIM-682. The fourth part hangs
together by its source, one exchange, more than by its relations, and is
a part of this line, not a line of its own (ADR-tmpbthzh).

- How CLAIM-690 and CLAIM-682 relate (SYNTHESIS-092).
- Whether CLAIM-624's duty of answerability, in organizational-normativity,
  falls on an arrangement's controllers, and so whether CLAIM-673's erosion
  of governing capacity breaches it (SYNTHESIS-093).
- That the two parts are one inquiry beyond the bearer they name in common.
- Each part account lists the links its components leave unstated.
