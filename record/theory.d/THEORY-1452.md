# THEORY-1452: Scheckenbach, Schubert, Forthmann, Glembockyte and Tinnefeld show that folded DNA origami can be repaired from strands supplied in solution, transiently bound labels regenerating indiscriminately after photobleaching and added staples being taken up only where shorter staples left a scaffold toehold of 4 or 8 nt, which they take to be how damaged sites are repaired, so that a matching staple pool keeps 76 % of nanorulers intact over 11 days in 0.2 % serum against 12 % without; such self-healing fails to outpace damage at higher nuclease levels, and damage to the scaffold cannot be repaired

## Source

Scheckenbach, Schubert, Forthmann, Glembockyte and Tinnefeld (2021),
[LIT-2139](../literature.d/LIT-2139.md); read in [NOTE-1636](../notes.d/NOTE-1636.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Regeneration** (Fig. 1): 13-nt labels exchanging with a 5 nM pool
  recover over about three hours after bleaching, to 20–60% with oxygen
  damage and to 100% with oxygen removal and ROXS.
- **Selectivity** (Figs. S8, S9, as the main text reports them): added
  staples were incorporated into intact nanorulers only where shorter
  staples left a 4 or 8 nt toehold.
- **Healing at a low damage rate** (Fig. 3): 0.2% serum, 11 days, intact
  nanorulers 85% → 76% with matching staples, 87% → 12% with none, 83% → 56%
  with non-matching DNA.
- **Failure at a high one** (Figs. S11, S12, as reported): in 10% serum the
  matching and non-matching DNA stabilize alike.
- **Limit**: only staples can be repaired, not the scaffold.
- **Could have come out otherwise**: the matching pool could have done no
  better than sacrificial DNA at the low damage rate, and added staples could
  have entered intact structures; neither happened.

## What this does not say

- That repair continues once the supply is withdrawn: it was never
  withdrawn.
- That nuclease damage exposes toeholds: inferred, not observed.
- That repair is complete: the 12HB's defective fraction fell from 63% to
  32%, and only with an anneal from 50 °C.
- Anything about structures in solution, rather than on a surface, or about
  the cost of the strand pool.
