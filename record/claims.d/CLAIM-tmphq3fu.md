# CLAIM-tmphq3fu: A transport that approximately intertwines each framing operation approximately preserves their commutator, so the degree to which interpretive perspectives interfere can itself be part of what a translation preserves

## The claim

A34 §4: "the degree to which interpretive perspectives interfere with each other
could itself be part of what a faithful translation preserves." Manuscript §9:
"If ΦA≈A'Φ and ΦB≈B'Φ, compare Φ[A,B] with [A',B']Φ ... This identity bounds
commutator distortion by local intertwining defects and operator norms."

Its argument is [ARG-tmpjbue6](../arguments.d/ARG-tmpjbue6.md).

## What it does not say

That the commutator *structure* is preserved: only its image under Φ ([CLAIM-tmp2rve9](CLAIM-tmp2rve9.md)).
