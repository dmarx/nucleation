# TERM-tmp9mag8: agnotological character, of a perspective: a candidate invariant profile of its organized epistemic limitations, read from its responses to probes as the legibility, epistemic standing, practical efficacy and revisability it gives a distinction; not a group character

## Definition

A6 §9: for a probe q, 𝒜_P(q) = (L_P(q), E_P(q), R_P(q), C_P(q)), with L_P
the "Legibility of the relevant distinction", E_P the "Epistemic standing
allocated to it", R_P the "Practical efficacy of its recognition" and C_P
the "Capacity to revise the organization in light of it". "A suitable
profile would need to exhibit stability under changes of representation
while distinguishing genuinely different epistemic organizations."

The four components are achievements the record already separates:
legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability are [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s three
(A6 cites it); epistemic standing is [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s credibility and uptake.
The term's content is that the profile, taken over a family of probes, is
a property of a perspective that can persist across its realizations
([TERM-072](TERM-072.md)'s organizational sense) and be transmitted ([CLAIM-tmpjlxfy](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjlxfy.md)).

## The forms of ignorance it profiles

A6 §1 distinguishes six, each mapped to where the record holds it:

| A6 §1 | A6's characterization | Held as |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | "Relevant information is absent." | not an organizational achievement; none |
| Representational | "Available distinctions cannot express the relevant phenomenon." | the fibres of a projection, [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md); [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md) |
| Interpretive | "Information is available but systematically assigned inadequate significance." | [CLAIM-403](../claims.d/CLAIM-403.md)'s credibility and uptake |
| Volitional | "Information is recognized but cannot influence organizational action." | [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s volitional efficacy |
| Investigative | "Questions that would produce knowledge are not pursued." | [CLAIM-657](../claims.d/CLAIM-657.md)'s costly discovery; [CLAIM-1140](../claims.d/CLAIM-1140.md)'s circle |
| Reflexive | "The organization cannot adequately recognize or revise its own epistemic limitations." | [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md)'s correctability; [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md) |

## What it is not

- **Not a group character.** A6: "not necessarily a literal group
  character". What a representation's character does detect of a
  perspective's blindness is limited: [CLAIM-tmpqbaic](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqbaic.md).
- **Not a verdict.** A6 §3: "neither a kernel nor an invariance is
  intrinsically a defect. Abstraction itself depends on ignoring
  distinctions." A limitation is ignorance only relative to "an
  independently specified epistemic or normative task" ([CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md),
  [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)).

## What would settle it

Whether such a profile is stable across realizations and transmitted with
memes is the test [LEAD-tmpqucxc](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpqucxc.md) proposes.
