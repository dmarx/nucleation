# LIT-tmps6sc3: An Abductive Theory of Constitution

Baumgartner, M., & Casini, L. (2017). An abductive theory of constitution. *Philosophy of Science*, 84(2), 214–233.

Read in full in NOTE-tmp5hk5o.

## Key takeaways

- Mutual manipulability fails "in all cases", not some: the required
  surgical interventions are impossible in principle (pp. 6–8).
- Constitutive underdetermination is of a different kind from causal
  underdetermination: the confounding is introduced by the manipulation
  itself, so idealizing the experiment does not remove it (pp. 10–11).
- No De-Coupling (NDC, p. 15): parthood, redundancy-free common-cause
  coupling, and coupling unbroken under every expansion of the variable
  set. Constitution is defined by the impossibility of top-down and
  bottom-up interventions, not their possibility.
- Constitutive relevance is extrinsic to a part–whole pair, set-relative,
  but not relativized (pp. 16–17).
