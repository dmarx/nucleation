  families who left before 1980. They found 14 sites linked to germline

# LIT-tmpglael: Epigenetic signatures of intergenerational exposure to violence in three generations of Syrian refugees

Mulligan, C. J., Quinn, E. B., Hamadmad, D., Dutton, C. L., Nevell, L., Binder, A. M., Panter-Brick, C. & Dajani, R. (2025). *Scientific Reports*, 15, 5945.

## Key takeaways

- The design: when a grandmother is pregnant with a daughter, the
  daughter's oocytes are already forming, so the grandchild was "germline
  exposed". In this study that group is 18 children from 10 families.
- 14 differentially methylated positions (DMPs) are linked to germline
  exposure and 21 to direct exposure. None is linked to prenatal exposure,
  and none appears in both lists. 32 of the 35 shift in the same direction
  across all three exposures.
- The authors call it "the first report of a germline DNAm signature of
  violence trauma in humans using a cohort study with an unexposed
  comparison group". The design does not hold the other routes fixed: see
  NOTE-tmpxwx4b.
