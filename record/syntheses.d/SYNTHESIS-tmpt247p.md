# SYNTHESIS-tmpt247p: The authenticity line holds, set aside, that a will is the agent's own to the degree its commitments arise through, remain integrated with and are answerable to its constitutive organization, and that this grounds normativity from a whole to its constituents but not between peers; it was deferred because its ownership criteria do not survive manipulation, and what it has gained since is a candidate content for "integrated", non-domination of the processes that carry a commitment, with the objection that non-domination cannot be read from low supervisory cost, or from override alone, but only under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or from history

## The account

The line was built to answer the ownership question, what makes a
will-organization the agent's own rather than merely effective within it
(QUESTION-011). Its answer is CLAIM-016, stated in TERM-006: a will is the
agent's own "to the degree that its operative commitments arise through,
remain integrated with, and are answerable to its constitutive
organization". TERM-006's thick form absorbed TERM-012, cross-scale
concordance, "which is where its circularity worries came from" (TERM-006).
On that account the owner drew a limit: authenticity "gives us an account
for normativity at least from the downward direction of
superagent-->constituents", but not "across agents that share an
environment and level of organizational complexity" (CLAIM-104, quoting
U75). The cross-agent question is QUESTION-007, and the assistant's answer
to it, reciprocal agency, is CLAIM-096. Both CLAIM-104 and CLAIM-096 rest
on CLAIM-016. CLAIM-104's body records that the exchange did not keep the
directions fixed: A271 "re-mapped authenticity to the 'inward' direction
and left 'downward' to concordance, without noting the change", and
CLAIM-096 says it "presupposes authenticity as the inward term of a
three-direction taxonomy".

The whole line was set aside at the audit of 2026-10-05: "ownership
criteria remain insufficiently defended" (CLAIM-016's summary). The reason
was manipulation: "A sophisticated manipulator could engineer all four"
of A263's ownership conditions (CLAIM-016's body). CLAIM-096 was deferred
as needing "independent moral argument" (its summary), and TERM-012
because "Evaluative standards remain underspecified". Every component
filed before the audit is Deferred.

Later filings kept the line's questions in view without reopening it.

- **Grounds.** CLAIM-016's note of 2026-10-12 adds THEORY-054, THEORY-047,
  THEORY-029 and THEORY-040 as grounds, which "support this claim's 'arise
  through, remain integrated with' against the hierarchical account", and
  notes that THEORY-040 finds no historical condition shown to escape its
  own objection. QUESTION-057 asks whether integration has any mark other
  than the agent's avowal: "If it has none, the ownership criterion cannot
  be told apart from endorsement." QUESTION-068 asks what settling
  ownership would buy, and whether accountability needs conditions
  ownership does not supply.
- **A candidate content for "integrated".** The owner, at U45 of the
  exchange of 2026-10-17, proposed that "'authenticity' should be treated
  as an organizational achievement characterized by non-domination of
  subcomponents" (CLAIM-362). CLAIM-362 calls itself "an alternative
  reading of CLAIM-016's middle condition, not a premise of it", and "a
  candidate constituent of authenticity ... not a sufficient criterion of
  ownership". A46 refined non-domination to "continued access to
  consequential roles". TERM-006's note of 2026-10-18 says CLAIM-362 gives
  the term's "remain integrated with" a content.
- **The objection.** CLAIM-364, A45's: processes conditioned never to
  object "coordinate without override at the same behaviour and
  supervisory cost as processes that negotiate", so the two part "only
  under intervention, when a constituent dissents, or in their history".
  It is "the manipulation objection (THEORY-1118) ... applied to the new
  criterion", and "does not say the criterion fails, only that cost cannot
  certify it" (its summary). The inference it defeats is ARG-030, from
  CLAIM-358's economy to CLAIM-362's non-domination, which CLAIM-364
  undercuts "while leaving CLAIM-362 standing" (ARG-030's verdict).
  On CLAIM-016's manipulation defeater, its note of 2026-10-18 says it "is
  not met by either" CLAIM-362 or CLAIM-364, and CLAIM-364's body that it
  "is still outstanding".
- **The cases.** CASE-129, the alarm that learned and the alarm that was
  disabled, makes CLAIM-364's point a pair: "a quiet signal may represent
  either successful learning or a loss of the capacity to learn", and the
  test is "**response to changed evidence**". CASE-128, the voluntary
  delegation, marks the converse limit: "whether a constituent is
  dominated cannot be read from whether it is overridden, but from who
  authorized the override and whether it stays answerable". CLAIM-364's
  note of 2026-10-22 joins the two: "So whether a constituent is
  dominated cannot be read from whether it is overridden."

Whether the line reopens is the owner's call: CLAIM-362, CLAIM-364 and
ARG-030 each say so, and the Deferred components' later notes leave their
status unchanged.

## Links tested as claims

None is filed as a claim. Each route of the test (ADR-075, step 5) was
tried against the components as written.

- **Override in neither direction shows domination.** That domination is
  read neither from the absence of override (CLAIM-364) nor from its
  presence (CASE-128), but from authorization, answerability and response
  under intervention. It is stated: CLAIM-364's note of 2026-10-22 ("So
  whether a constituent is dominated cannot be read from whether it is
  overridden") and CASE-128's body. A18's summary, quoted in that note:
  "The common mistake is to infer something about the **distribution of
  practical authority** from the **absence of overt struggle**."
- **Authenticity gives at most a downward ground, and the cross-agent one
  needs independent argument.** Stated: CLAIM-104, QUESTION-007's body,
  and CLAIM-096's note of 2026-10-18 ("interdependence ⇏ obligation").
- **Non-domination does not repair CLAIM-016 against manipulation.**
  Stated: CLAIM-362 ("it doesn't yet settle the manipulation objection",
  A45), CLAIM-364's body, CLAIM-016's note of 2026-10-18.
- **Non-domination as a non-avowal mark of integration (QUESTION-057).**
  Not stated; see below. As a derivation it is trivial once its parameter
  is closed, and as a prediction no component bears on it.
- **Non-domination as the content of the downward norm (CLAIM-104).** Not
  stated; see below. On the reading that the whole owes its constituents
  non-domination it runs against CLAIM-362's "We are proposing an
  organizational criterion, not asserting rights for neural modules"; on
  the other reading it needs a premise no component holds.

**Whether the line falls into parts.** Not into separate ones. Counting
each pair of components joined by a front-matter relation or a mention in
either's body or summary, and dividing the line into ownership (CLAIM-016,
TERM-006, QUESTION-011, QUESTION-057, QUESTION-068, CLAIM-362, CLAIM-364,
ARG-030, CASE-128, CASE-129) and cross-agent normativity (CLAIM-096,
CLAIM-104, QUESTION-007, TERM-012): 18 pairs within the first, 2 within
the second, and 5 across. All 5 run through CLAIM-016 or TERM-006: the two
`rests_on` from CLAIM-096 and CLAIM-104, CLAIM-016's `uses` of TERM-012,
QUESTION-007's mentions of CLAIM-016, and TERM-006's of TERM-012. The
normativity part hangs from the ownership claim and has no account of its
own to give.

## Not stated on the record

Integrations the components leave open. Each would need to pass a route to
be filed.

- **Non-domination as a mark of integration other than avowal.**
  QUESTION-057 asks whether integration has a mark other than avowal;
  CLAIM-362 offers non-domination as a candidate constituent of
  integration; CLAIM-364 and CASE-129 give interventions that part
  negotiated from conditioned coordination. No entry joins them. Read as
  "a partial mark", it follows trivially once constituency is granted; read
  as "a mark of integration", CLAIM-362 concedes it is not sufficient.
  QUESTION-057's answer condition, a measure that "predicts something
  avowal does not and survives a multitrait-multimethod design", is met by
  no component.
- **Non-domination as what the downward ground requires.** CLAIM-104's
  downward direction and CLAIM-362's non-domination are not linked by any
  entry, and CLAIM-362 disclaims standing for subprocesses.
- **Whether CASE-128's authorization condition escapes manipulation.**
  CASE-128 makes "who authorized the arrangement" the question; CLAIM-016
  was deferred because a manipulator could engineer its conditions, and
  QUESTION-011's note of 2026-10-31 says a recognition-based account is
  "more open to the same objection, not less". No entry asks whether an
  authorization condition is open to it too.
- **Whether settling ownership by non-domination would settle
  accountability.** QUESTION-068 asks it of ownership in general; no entry
  asks it of CLAIM-362's candidate.

## What it does not say

- That the line is reopened. Every pre-audit component is Deferred, and
  CLAIM-362, CLAIM-364 and ARG-030 were filed without reopening it.
- That non-domination is ownership. CLAIM-362 is "a candidate constituent
  ... not a sufficient criterion of ownership", and its defeater excludes
  cases that show insufficiency.
- That the non-domination criterion fails. CLAIM-364: "Non-domination
  stated counterfactually, as A45 and A46 state it, survives the
  objection."
- That history settles manipulation. CLAIM-364: THEORY-040 "notes that no
  historical condition in the record is shown to escape its own
  objection".
- That an agent who overrides is not the author of the act (CLAIM-362,
  after CLAIM-261).
- Anything with standing of its own.
