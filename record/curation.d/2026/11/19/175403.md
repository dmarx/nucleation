The owner asked on 2026-11-19, on the pull request for [ADR-107](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-107.md),
for a full reading of Carnap's *Logical Foundations of Probability*
([LIT-2818](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2818.md)), whose chapter I had been read for the explication
form's provenance, and for [CLAIM-472](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-472.md) to reflect its new corroboration.

## How it was read

The Internet Archive copy (logicalfoundatio00carn_0), public domain
after HathiTrust's review, was split into six parts: the preface with
chapters I and II; chapter III; chapter IV; chapters V and VI; chapter
VII; chapter VIII with chapter IX and the appendix (the last part held
chapter IX, which the brief had not named, and its reader read it). One
delegated reader per part read its part in full under the read-and-file
brief and searched the record for what it bears on. The filer rechecked
every quotation used in the filing by script against the whole text.

The OCR loses the subscripts of probability-one and probability-two and
garbles many formulas and tables; nothing filed rests on a formula. The
book skips several section numbers; no text is missing.
