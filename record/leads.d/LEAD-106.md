Carried out by a delegated lead worker on the branch
claude/lead-106-ptsd-context-sampling, following the run-lead and
read-and-file skills. Europe PMC's REST search was used (no email
parameter), for eye tracking or gaze with PTSD and recall, script,
context, ambiguity, scene or periphery, and for exposure-matched
intervention contrasts; OpenAlex shows no work citing the account's
source (LIT-983).

**No study meets the design.** None measures gaze or context sampling
after cued traumatic recall in PTSD against trauma-exposed controls, and
none runs the same exposure with and without restored context sampling.

**Read** (lawful open copies): Zidda and colleagues 2025 (LIT-tmpmkmjq,
NOTE-tmpoybvl, THEORY-tmp1ov9x), the nearest study: people with PTSD
first looked at the neutral background of their own trauma cue later than
trauma-exposed controls, with no difference for the cue; and Diotaiuti and
colleagues' 2026 scoping review (LIT-tmpogxg7, NOTE-tmp9jy7p,
THEORY-tmp8n4s7), whose 17 eye-tracking studies include none after
recall.

**Registered, Deferred** (the closest, with what each lacks): Blekic and
colleagues 2025 (LIT-tmp5bqfy, shorter scanpaths in PTSD than
trauma-exposed controls, no recall; open in PMC, next to read); Kimble
and colleagues 2014 (LIT-tmpocdt2) and Kimble, Cappello and Fleming 2023
(LIT-tmpizs88), more scanning of ambiguous scenes with hypervigilance;
Milanak and colleagues 2018 (LIT-tmpgjodp), more gaze to emotional
backgrounds with symptoms; Andrews and colleagues 2023 (LIT-tmpozodu),
recall then a gaze task, but brain imaging of being looked at; for the
second half, Sack and colleagues 2016 (LIT-tmp4oubb), EMDR with and
without an external visual focus, and Tyson, Ehlers and Wild 2025
(LIT-tmpg13ib), stimulus-discrimination training, analogue.

**The verdict** is filed as alternatives: CLAIM-tmpcug8h (leaned to: weak,
indirect support for the first half's direction) and CLAIM-tmpc7tmg (no
support: the background disambiguated nothing), with ARG-tmpc0892.

What remains: the evidence the lead looks for does not yet exist. Reading
Blekic and colleagues, and getting copies of the three Deferred works with
no open copy found, would complete the near literature. The lead stays
Open; whether to mark it Done is the owner's.
