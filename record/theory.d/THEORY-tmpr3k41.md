# THEORY-tmpr3k41: Spatial boundaries are bona fide where a spatial discontinuity or a heterogeneity of material is present and fiat where they exist only by demarcation; the two obey different topologies, distinct bodies are never in bona fide contact, so any whole of separate bodies is a fiat object, and real properties of the material still enable and constrain where fiat boundaries can be drawn

## Source

Barry Smith and Achille C. Varzi (2000), "Fiat and Bona Fide Boundaries",
[LIT-1024](../literature.d/LIT-1024.md), *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 60(2): 401–420, read in
full in [NOTE-721](../notes.d/NOTE-721.md). The OCR garbled the logical symbols, and the formulas were
restored by eye; axioms A≤1–A≤3 and A≈2–A≈3 were given from the prose.

## What was actually shown

**The typology (§1).** "Inner boundaries involving spatial discontinuity (holes,
fissures, slits) or qualitative heterogeneity (of material constitution,
texture, electric charge) we shall call bona fide boundaries." The rest are
fiat: the hemispheres of a homogeneous sphere, national borders "at least in
those cases where they lie skew to any qualitative differentiations or spatial
discontinuities", the equator. Fiat boundaries may be social, individual,
perceptual, linguistic or mathematical. Any object with a fiat portion of
boundary is a fiat object: the North Sea, and also "A mountain, a bay, the
branches of a tree, or the stem of a champagne glass".

**Not arbitrary.** "Real properties of the underlying factual material ... both
enable the drawing of fiat borders and constrain the types and location of
fiat borders which can be drawn."

**No contact between bodies (§§1.2, 2).** "As far as bona fide outer boundaries
are concerned, however, no genuine contact or coincidence of boundaries
between John and Mary is possible at all." Bona fide objects are connected;
fiat objects may be scattered (Polynesia, Orion).

**The distinction is absolute (§2.1).** Cutting, or building a fence along a
border, makes a new bona fide boundary; "this is not a process of
transformation".

**Two theories (§§3.3–3.4).** Bona fide boundaries take a primitive B(x, y), a
mereologised closure c(x) := x + b(x), and the Kuratowski axioms. Contact is
external connection, and two closed things cannot touch (TB4). Fiat boundaries
take B*(x, y) with B*(x, y) → x ≤ y: they "are necessarily parts of the
entities they bound", so there is no closure operator and no open/closed
distinction. Each side owns its own, and "each hemisphere has its own equator,
and the two equators coincide".

**One formal surprise (§3.5).** A boundary needs a self-connected host, and
self-connection has to be defined by fiat connection: "A self-connected entity
is one all of whose parts are separated at most by fiat". So "the theory of
bona fide boundaries presupposes the theory of fiat boundaries".

**What could have come out otherwise.** A single topology could have handled
both kinds, or the puzzles of contact and division could have survived the
split. The paper argues case by case that they do not (sphere, disk, equator,
Mason–Dixon line).

## What this does not say

- **Who or what made a boundary.** A cell membrane and a beam joining two
  pendulums are both bona fide. The equator is fiat and imposed. The paper has
  no category for a boundary the bounded thing draws and keeps for itself.
- **Anything about time or process.** The axioms are read synchronically (fn.
  15). Boundaries that come and go, or are maintained by a process, are outside
  the theory.
- **That the theory is consistent.** It is "illustrative": no models, no
  consistency or completeness result.
- **That bona fide boundaries exist at the physical level.** The authors grant
  that physics shows macroscopic objects "do not have boundaries of the sort
  countenanced by common sense at all". The theory is of the common-sense world.
- **That reality is vague.** Vagueness "pertains ontologically only to the fiat
  world", and every candidate aggregate is "precisely determinate". [NOTE-721](../notes.d/NOTE-721.md)
  rates this claim weak.
- **That an interaction gap is a bona fide boundary.** The heterogeneity is of
  "material constitution, texture, electric charge", not of coupling strength.
  Reading a near-decomposability gap as bona fide would extend the paper.
