# THEORY-2105: Human communication is cooperative at base

## The account

Communication has a cooperative pragmatic structure before and beneath
language. A communicator makes a communicative intention overt within
common ground, for helping (requesting, informing) or sharing; recipients
reason cooperatively. This rests on shared intentionality, evolved in
obligate collaborative foraging. Conventions arise naturally when imitated
iconic gestures become opaque to learners outside the original common
ground.

## Evidence

Restates experiments with infants and apes, mostly from the author's lab.
