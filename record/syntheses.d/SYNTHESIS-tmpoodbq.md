# SYNTHESIS-tmpoodbq: Rights, demands and commitments: the three are distinct and none establishes another, a demand's satisfaction condition carries no warrant so that an institution's maintained requirements cannot certify their own authority, a right that protects a choice constrains a policy and not a history, a demand ceases legitimately only by a valid normative power, inalienability limits what a holder can confer, rights once justified organize later justification, and a duty to help establish a needed institution is bounded by reachability

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, on rights, demands and commitments. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" the same day, from which every component comes; none was in any account before. Several components also carry dependence-and-domination, and two cases carry it and not this line (below). Its sibling on accountability without blame and normative governability, filed at the same review, is [SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9](SYNTHESIS-tmpzqep9.md).*

## The account

Each link is stated by the entry named first, in its front matter unless
its body is cited. Every component is Proposed, or Open for a question.

**The contrast.** [CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md) (the owner's contrast, U41, "rights vs
demands vs commitments"; the definitions and non-implications are A's,
A95 and A97; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also will-organization):
rights, demands and commitments are distinct, none establishes another,
and each passage between them "needs its own warrant". It uses [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md)
and complements [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md), [CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md) and [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md). Its body
says [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md) files the demand side "in its sharpest form" and
[CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md) "the one passage A97 X singles out", and that the aquifer,
[CASE-282](../cases.d/CASE-282.md), "puts all three to work in one arrangement".

**A demand carries no warrant.** [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md) (A's, A106 §4; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) stores no relation. Its body: a demand's
acceptance set fixes "what meets it, not its source, who bears it, to
whom it is addressed or why it binds", so the compatibility problem
"cannot establish that each deserves fulfillment"; it calls itself the
formal reason for [CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md)'s point, says [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md) holds the
other half of A's two kinds of compatibility, and says [CLAIM-745](../claims.d/CLAIM-745.md)
applies it. [CLAIM-745](../claims.d/CLAIM-745.md) (A's mechanism and wording, A108 §1 and §4, at
the owner's U46, whose specimen it is; thesis, interpretive, Proposed;
also dependence-and-domination) rests on [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md) and grounds in
[CASE-289](../cases.d/CASE-289.md): a categorical administrative gate can make evidence the
governing standard requires to count unable to change a decision, and
that the criterion is imposed and complied with "establishes neither its
legal authority nor its justification". It makes no finding on motive.
[CASE-289](../cases.d/CASE-289.md) (the owner's specimen; documented, Proposed; also
dependence-and-domination) illustrates [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md): the 75-country
immigrant-visa pause of January 2026 and its vacatur on 2026-08-21. Its
body records the scope of closure and the motive as the owner's
characterization, with A108 §1's judgement that "all" avenues is too
broad.

**Rights that protect a choice.** [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md) (A's, A74, A85, A90,
A97 III, A102 §4; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also will-organization)
grounds in [CASE-283](../cases.d/CASE-283.md), bears on [QUESTION-486](../questions.d/QUESTION-486.md), and complements
[CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), [CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md) and [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md): whether an
arrangement respects a right that protects a choice "is a property of its
policy over the protected choices, not of any realized history".
[CASE-283](../cases.d/CASE-283.md) (A's construction, checked on filing; stipulated, Proposed;
also will-organization) bears on [QUESTION-454](../questions.d/QUESTION-454.md): under a right of refusal,
two arrangements produce identical histories whenever the patient
consents, and only one respects the right.

**Release, not suppression.** [CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md) (A's, A97 X; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; first line distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md),
bears on [QUESTION-456](../questions.d/QUESTION-456.md), and complements [CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and
[CLAIM-662](../claims.d/CLAIM-662.md): a demand owed to a party ceases legitimately only by a valid
normative power, which changes the reference set; an institution's
ceasing to count it changes only the set it recognizes. Its body says
some rights "may not be releasable at all", citing [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md).

**Inalienability.** [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) (A's, A85 §2 and §4 as corrected by
A87 §4, at the owner's U37; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; first line
dependence-and-domination) uses [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md), bears on [QUESTION-486](../questions.d/QUESTION-486.md),
[QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md) and [QUESTION-454](../questions.d/QUESTION-454.md), and complements [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and
[CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md): no delegation, contract, collective decision or success
in imposing an arrangement confers authority over whether a person keeps
the standing from which claims against it arise, "because the holder
lacks the power to confer it". [CASE-286](../cases.d/CASE-286.md) (A's, A85 §11 Case A;
stipulated, Proposed; in dependence-and-domination only) illustrates
[CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) and bears on [QUESTION-486](../questions.d/QUESTION-486.md) and [QUESTION-454](../questions.d/QUESTION-454.md): two
informed, voluntary delegations, one limited and one purporting to be
unlimited. [QUESTION-486](../questions.d/QUESTION-486.md) (the owner's, U37; Open; also
dependence-and-domination) refines [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md): can rights no
institution creates or can extinguish serve as the institution-independent
criteria the record's judgments need? [QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md) (A's, A85 §8 and
A90 XII; Open; first line dependence-and-domination) stores no relation:
who has standing to change an arrangement's governing structure, and
which protections lie beyond revision. Its body says [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md)
bears on the limit half ("no reconstitution by those who delegated can
either"), and that [QUESTION-486](../questions.d/QUESTION-486.md) asks whether rights of that kind can
be had.

**Rights in later justification, and the reply to Scanlon.**
[QUESTION-483](../questions.d/QUESTION-483.md) (the owner's, U44; the alternatives and the test are
A's; Open) stores no relation: does the record's account only supplement
contractualism, or show an allocation of normative powers reasonable
rejection cannot explain without importing principles of standing or
authority? Its body names [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md), [CLAIM-735](../claims.d/CLAIM-735.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md),
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md) and [CLAIM-747](../claims.d/CLAIM-747.md) as pieces a reply would use, and
[QUESTION-484](../questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) as narrower. [CLAIM-735](../claims.d/CLAIM-735.md) (A's, A102 §3 and A106 §19,
at the owner's U44; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) stores no relation:
once justified, rights and powers become "constitutive elements of the
structure within which further justification occurs", so they can be
prior in organization where principles are prior in justification. Its
body relates it to [CLAIM-086](../claims.d/CLAIM-086.md), to [CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md) ("This claim says what a
right does once the passage is made") and to [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md), and says it
is compatible with [QUESTION-483](../questions.d/QUESTION-483.md)'s supplement answer.

**Duties to establish, and feasibility.** [CLAIM-729](../claims.d/CLAIM-729.md) (A's, A102 §5,
after A's report of Scanlon's *Contractualism and Intuitionism*; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-560](../claims.d/CLAIM-560.md): the duties to comply with a
justified rule, to support a justified institution and to help establish
a needed one are distinct, and the third is bounded by reachability by
permissible, resource-bounded transitions and by whose part it is. Its
body says [CASE-282](../cases.d/CASE-282.md) illustrates the gap the bound names, that
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s duty is A's third illustration, and that it leaves
[QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md) open. [CASE-282](../cases.d/CASE-282.md) (A's construction, every number
rechecked; stipulated, Proposed; lines dependence-and-domination and
realizability) illustrates [CLAIM-586](../claims.d/CLAIM-586.md), [CLAIM-560](../claims.d/CLAIM-560.md), [CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md) and
[CLAIM-738](../claims.d/CLAIM-738.md) and bears on [QUESTION-484](../questions.d/QUESTION-484.md): two households, an
aquifer, and a recycler that makes all three demands compatible and that
one household owns and uses as leverage for voting authority.
[QUESTION-484](../questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) (A's, A100 §8B and §13, at the owner's U43; Open; also
realizability) stores no relation: can expanding what an arrangement can
realize change which principles governing it can reasonably be rejected?
Its body puts it to the aquifer and sets it beside [QUESTION-483](../questions.d/QUESTION-483.md).

## Arm B

so nothing is filed.

- *[CLAIM-745](../claims.d/CLAIM-745.md) with [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md)*: a categorical gate fails a right
  to individualized consideration at the level of policy, so no audit of
  realized refusals of otherwise ineligible applicants can show it.
  Route: derivation. Fails: it is a substitution instance of
  [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md)'s second separation in [CLAIM-745](../claims.d/CLAIM-745.md)'s model, an
  inference [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md) already holds. Left under "Not stated".
- *[CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md) with [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md)*: a demand grounded in inalienable
  standing cannot cease to apply by release. Already stated, in
  [CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md)'s body.
- *[CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md) with [CLAIM-735](../claims.d/CLAIM-735.md)*: whether a demand still binds is
  fixed by the history of valid normative acts, not by the recognized
  set. Route: derivation. Fails as trivial: it restates [CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md)'s
  N_t against N̂_t, and [CLAIM-735](../claims.d/CLAIM-735.md)'s promise example states it ("The
  promisor's later preferences do not automatically extinguish the
  obligation"). Its classification under [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md) is left under "Not
  stated".
- *[CLAIM-729](../claims.d/CLAIM-729.md) with [QUESTION-484](../questions.d/QUESTION-484.md) and [CASE-282](../cases.d/CASE-282.md)*: the
  reachability bound filters duties, not principles, and so is an
  instance of the question's independence answer. Route: discriminating
  prediction. Fails: it predicts nothing [CLAIM-729](../claims.d/CLAIM-729.md) does not, and
  [CLAIM-729](../claims.d/CLAIM-729.md) says nothing about principles, which is the question's
  open part. Left under "Not stated".
- *[CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) with [QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md)*: no reconstitution can
  extinguish standing no delegation could confer. Already stated, in
  [QUESTION-481](../questions.d/QUESTION-481.md)'s body.
- *[CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md) with [CLAIM-741](../claims.d/CLAIM-741.md)*: requirement compatibility and
  normative compatibility come apart for two reasons, unwarranted demands
  and rights over choices. Already stated, in [CLAIM-725](../claims.d/CLAIM-725.md)'s body.
