# LIT-tmp5vc66: Linear Representations of Finite Groups

Jean-Pierre Serre (1977), *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*, Graduate
Texts in Mathematics, New York: Springer — DOI-10.1007/978-1-4684-9458-7

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* What follows is the standard content of the book as
the record's claim uses it, not checked against the text.

- **Characters are complete in characteristic zero.** For a finite group and
  finite-dimensional complex representations, every representation is a
  direct sum of irreducibles (Maschke), the irreducible characters are
  orthonormal for the usual inner product on class functions, and so a
  representation is fixed up to isomorphism by its character.
- **The irreducible characters are a basis of the class functions**, as many
  as there are conjugacy classes.
- **Beyond characteristic zero the classification changes.** Modular
  representations, where the characteristic divides the group order, are
  treated separately, and there characters alone do not classify.
