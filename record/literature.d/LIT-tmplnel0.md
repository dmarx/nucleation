# LIT-tmplnel0: Conditional Simulation Using Diffusion Schrödinger Bridges

Yuyang Shi, Valentin De Bortoli, George Deligiannidis and Arnaud Doucet
(2022), *UAI 2022*, PMLR 180 —
[ARXIV-2202.13460](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.13460)

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* From the abstract only: denoising diffusion models
solve conditional simulation problems such as inverse problems, but are slow
at generation. A Schrödinger-bridge formulation shortens generation for
unconditional simulation, and the paper extends that framework to conditional
simulation, demonstrated on image super-resolution, optimal filtering for
state-space models and the refinement of pre-trained networks.
