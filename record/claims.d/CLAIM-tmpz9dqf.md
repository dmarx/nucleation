# CLAIM-tmpz9dqf: A manipulation that installs a will-organization within the agent's own reach and then withdraws its channel leaves no feature of will-organization that differs from its unmanipulated twin, because every relation the account names, maintenance and the capacity to transform included, is fixed by the agent's present state, dynamics and couplings; so the more an agent can reach by its own revision, the more manipulation its present can hide, and only a record kept while the organization formed, outside the state a manipulator could set, of which channel carried each governing relation across its threshold, can tell the two apart

## The claim

QUESTION-071 asks whether the will line's notion of organization, with
its governance among regimes and its capacity to transform, escapes
THEORY-040's argument, that any condition stated in present psychological
structure is met by a manipulated agent. It does not, for manipulation
that installs and withdraws. The argument has three premises.

1. **Supervenience.** Every relation the account names is fixed by three
   things: the agent's present state, its dynamics (how its state changes
   under each input, its own maintaining and revising processes among
   them), and its present couplings (which channels reach which of its
   variables). The efficacy relations of CLAIM-027 are states.
   Maintenance is a present process (TERM-100, sense 2): TERM-023 defines
   will-organization as the *maintained* organization, and what maintains
   it is the dynamics running now. Transformation, revisability, the
   response to any intervention or to a constituent's dissent (CLAIM-364),
   knockout results (CLAIM-522) and the frustration and integration
   profile (CLAIM-347, CLAIM-534) are dispositions: properties of the
   dynamics at the present state. This is the premise that makes the claim
   interpretive. It reads TERM-023 as naming nothing beyond these three.
2. **What the manipulation fixes.** In the withdrawn case the manipulator
   acts on the state, during formation, and on nothing else. The dynamics
   are those of the agent's kind, and after the channel is removed the
   couplings are those of an agent with no manipulator.
3. **Reachability.** A twin exists if and only if the installed state lies
   in the agent's own reachable set: what its own channels and ordinary
   inputs could bring about from the same start, or, for original design,
   from where agents of its kind ordinarily start. CASE-tmp06m60 is such a
   twin for CASE-tmpj29sq.

By 1 and 2, the manipulated agent after formation and its twin share
state, dynamics and couplings; by 1, then, every relation of
will-organization. What is stipulated is one state at one time and
membership of one kind. The match in maintenance, transformation and
response to intervention is not stipulated with it; it follows. So the
account's additions over a mesh account give a manipulator nothing more to
fake than the mesh did. CASE-191 found this for one capacity; the pair
finds it for a governing relation.

## Where present structure does show something

Each of these is a failure of the match, not a feature that separates a
matched pair (CASE-tmpj29sq's variants):

- **The installation is upheld from outside**, not kept by the agent's own
  cycle. A knockout of the outside supply separates the two (CASE-191).
- **The agent's own revision works against it.** That shows, as
  introjection (THEORY-047), and it does not show manipulation, since
  ordinarily controlling formation, Pereboom's Case 3, leaves the same.
- **The installed state is out of the agent's own reach.** There is no
  twin. That the agent's processes could not have produced its state is
  read from the present and the dynamics, but it counts as installation
  only against where agents of its kind start, a fact about histories.
- **The manipulator keeps its channel.** CLAIM-tmp9jo1z and
  CLAIM-tmpner6t divide on whether that is a feature of will-organization.
- **The match is only asymptotic.** In an exact deterministic model a
  decaying transient is a present trace; noise erases it.

## A corollary: freedom and hiding

The installations with twins are those inside the agent's own reachable
set, and that set grows with the dimensions CLAIM-354 counts as degrees
of freedom of will: viable reachability, governance reconfigurability,
revisability. So the agents the record would call freer in its
operational sense (CLAIM-545) are those whose present structure can hide
the most manipulation, and an agent whose own revision could reach almost
any organization has a present that can certify almost none. Present
structure reads installation best in agents least able to remake
themselves.

## What a record of formation must contain

Part 2 of QUESTION-071. A record that tells a manipulated will from one
that is merely effective must hold six things.

1. **Which channel carried each governing relation across its threshold**:
   the agent's own, another agent's, or no agent's (noise, a natural
   force, the Natalie case of THEORY-328). Three values, since the
   natural-force case has no manipulator and is still not self-formed.
   Where several channels moved it together, each one's share, which
   their Gramians can measure for one transition (CLAIM-542).
2. **Changes in the governing relations, not only in the regimes.**
   Precedence, jurisdiction, suspension, accessibility, persistence, and
   the transformation relation itself, since a revision process that was
   installed will endorse what it was installed to endorse.
3. **Kept while it ran.** After formation no knockout separates the two
   (CASE-191), so the record is either made during formation or is a test
   made then, such as a knockout of the producing process. The path a
   transition took can tell a directed transition from a noise-driven one
   in small-noise gradient systems (QUESTION-071's notes of 2026-11-02 and
   2026-11-03), but only if it was observed while it ran: it is evidence
   about history, not a feature of the present organization.
4. **Kept outside the state a manipulator could set.** The agent's memory
   of how it came to be as it is, and its avowals, are part of its state
   (QUESTION-057's point, that avowal is one more attitude), and
   CASE-tmpj29sq's team installs them. The agent's own record counts only
   if it was kept where the channel that might have manipulated did not
   reach.
5. **Back to the start, for original design.** Where the organization was
   designed at origin (Pereboom's Case 2, Mele's Ernie), there is no
   bypassed capacity to record (THEORY-328), so the record must include
   the formation of the initial state.
6. **For another agent's channel, what makes its input manipulation and
   not mere effect.** The four conditions QUESTION-071's note of
   2026-11-17 took from the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse": what the
   influencer intended, what it anticipated, whether it could produce the
   conditions, and whether it did; and whether the agent was, or could
   easily have become, aware of the influence (THEORY-235).

## What it does not say

- **That formation provenance matters for responsibility.** The record
  separates Pereboom's Case 2 Plum from his Case 4 twin: one was
  designed, the other formed by his own processes on ordinary inputs.
  Whether that difference bears on responsibility is Pereboom's no-
  difference premise (THEORY-328), which this claim does not touch.
- **That present structure is idle.** It detects outside upholding,
  non-integration and installations out of reach.
- **That the manipulated agent is unfree** or that its will is not its
  own. With CLAIM-022 it says only that explaining efficacy cannot decide
  it. That is how the claim makes CLAIM-022 testable in the historical
  way LEAD-038 names: what would show the disclaimer wrong is a
  present-structure feature separating such a pair, which the defeater
  states.
