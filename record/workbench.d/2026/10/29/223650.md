**Checks run before anything was applied.** The formal cluster's three
scripts were re-run here.

- **CLAIM-326's decision term.** D⁺ is directed, and it is zero from a
  garbling back to its source to 10⁻¹⁶. A strict garbling has a witness of
  0.312, and a permuted-outcome control gives zero both ways. The rescaling
  identity is exact, and so is the symmetrization to D_Q over 300 families.
- **CLAIM-199's XOR example.** The example is exact: a one-coordinate
  receiver has risk ½ from the source and 0 from the rendering. The ideal
  receiver's ceiling held in 300 random garblings, and a control that is not
  a garbling breaks it.
- **CLAIM-269's readouts.** Ridge readouts on X and XQ agree to 6×10⁻¹⁶,
  while a non-orthogonal map moves them (4×10⁻²). A representation rebuilt
  from its kernel matches up to Procrustes to 3×10⁻¹⁵. A single-coordinate
  probe separates X from XQ (0.997 against 0.770). So the claim's old last
  clause, "supplied by the analyst", was more than the result showed. What
  is shown is "not by the readouts". The analyst clause already lives in
  CLAIM-193, which is interpretive.

CLAIM-489's rejection was checked against its sources. Its defeat condition
is "a case where contrast fixes a sign's interpretation only through an
alternative tied to its object by resemblance or reference". NOTE-613 says of
Bergen, Goodman and Levy's Experiment 1 (THEORY-172, Active) that "the
contrast works only because one alternative is iconically tied to an
object".
