# THEORY-tmp26rh2: Nothing has all four marks that qualia are said to have, being ineffable, intrinsic, private and directly apprehensible, because neither the subject nor an outside tester can tell a change in a quale from a change in the subject's reactions to it; what is real in their place is the practical ineffability and idiosyncrasy of information-rich property detection

## Source

Dennett (1988), "Quining Qualia", in Marcel & Bisiach (eds),
*Consciousness in Contemporary Science*, OUP, pp. 42–77, [LIT-433](../literature.d/LIT-433.md), read in
full in [NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md) from the author's web text. [NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md) cites sections and
the paper's own numbering of its intuition pumps, never pages.

[NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md) said "No new THEORY": a THEORY that "the absent-qualia intuition
has no subject-accessible content" would need the replies, which the
record does not hold. That reason bars an `Active` account, not a stated
one. This THEORY is filed `Proposed`, on the reading's own claims, and
the missing replies are named in what would settle it.

## What was actually shown

**The target (§2).** Qualia as the tradition has them are (1) ineffable,
(2) intrinsic, (3) private and (4) directly or immediately apprehensible.
The claim is that experience has no properties special in all these ways.
It does not deny that experience is real or has properties.

**No privileged access (pumps #5–#6).** An "evil neurosurgeon" can invert
an early visual channel, so that qualia are inverted, or the memory links
used to compare today's hues with yesterday's, so that only reactions are.
On waking the subject should say: "either my qualia have been inverted or
my memory-linked qualia-reactions have been inverted. I wonder which!"

**Chase and Sanborn (pump [#7](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/7)).** Two coffee tasters no longer like the
coffee. Chase says his standards changed and the taste did not; Sanborn
says his tasters changed. Third-person tests, blind retasting or
physiology, can favour one pole in extreme cases. They cannot separate the
components when the effects are small, and the taster's own judgment is
no better evidence than a naive observer's. What he can report with
authority is that the relation between sipping and judging changed.

**The dilemma (§4).** Either reactions are partly constitutive of how a
thing tastes, as the experienced beer drinker insists (pump [#9](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/9)), and
qualia are relational, not intrinsic; or they are not, and the subject
cannot get at them. The ordinary concept does not choose.

**The replacement (§6).** Practical ineffability is the information
richness of a property detector's deliverance, like the osprey cry heard
once (pump [#13](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/13)) or the torn edge of a Jello box, which cannot be described
without becoming a recipe for forging its mate (pump [#14](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/14)). Privacy is
idiosyncrasy of discrimination profiles, which is testable. Felt
homogeneity is functional invariability, which training can break (pump
[#15](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/15), the guitar harmonic).

**What could have come out otherwise.** The two surgeries could have
differed from the inside, or physiology could have placed a small
compensation before or after "the qualia phase" (pump [#8](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/8)). The paper
stipulates that they do not, and gives four clinical achromatopsia cases
that the qualia vocabulary describes in competing ways.

## What this does not say

- **That there is no experience, or that experience lacks properties.**
  The denial is of the four marks together. Frankish's "access
  consciousness is the whole story" ([LIT-103](../literature.d/LIT-103.md), note 2) is stronger than
  anything this paper asserts ([NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md), Connections).
- **That the indistinguishability is shown.** Pump #6 needs the two
  surgeries to have identical introspective effects. That is stipulated
  and not defended, and a defender of qualia can deny it ([NOTE-357](../notes.d/NOTE-357.md), C2).
- **That every definition of qualia is answered.** The paper argues against
  Shoemaker's milder "qualitative or phenomenal features" only by asking
  what "phenomenal" means. Definitions by example, such as Schwitzgebel's
  ([LIT-191](../literature.d/LIT-191.md)), are not addressed.
- **A metaphysical thesis stronger than the wording.** Endnote 2 calls the
  choice between eliminative and reductive materialism here "not so much …
  a doctrinal issue as a tactical issue". Read with it, "there simply are
  no qualia at all" is a recommendation about vocabulary.
- **That the absent-qualia intuition about groups or machines is false.**
  The paper's point is that such an intuition is not about anything the
  subject could detect; it offers no criterion that separates a modular
  mind from a nation ([THEORY-043](THEORY-043.md)).

## Connections

- **Illusionism.** Frankish ([THEORY-tmpspj51](THEORY-tmpspj51.md)) takes the four marks from this
  paper as what weak illusionists deflate, and generalises the denial from
  qualia to all phenomenal properties. Taylor ([THEORY-tmpk5k3c](THEORY-tmpk5k3c.md)) reads the
  qualia attack as peripheral to Dennett's positive view.
- **Organisational invariance** ([THEORY-tmpdz76a](THEORY-tmpdz76a.md)) turns on the same premise,
  that a change in experience the subject could not notice is a reductio,
  and concludes the opposite: qualia are fixed by organisation.
- **Block.** "Troubles with Functionalism" ([LIT-407](../literature.d/LIT-407.md)) is quoted for the jazz
  reply ("if you got to ask, you ain't never gonna get to know"), and the
  wine-tasting machine (pump #2) is an absent-qualia case in miniature.
  Block's Inverted Earth ([THEORY-095](THEORY-095.md)) answers the memory-malfunction
  diagnosis by building a case with no internal disturbance for it to
  attach to. [THEORY-103](THEORY-103.md) records that Block rejects by name the move from
  the subject's inability to tell two hypotheses apart to the emptiness of
  the notion.
- **The hard problem** ([THEORY-tmpz08ce](THEORY-tmpz08ce.md)): "Facing Up" lists Dennett under
  the strategy "deny the phenomenon"; for qualia, this is the paper it
  points to.
