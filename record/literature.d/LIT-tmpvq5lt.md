# LIT-tmpvq5lt: The Structure of Awareness: Toward a Symbolic Language of Human Reflexion

Lefebvre, V. A. (1977). *The Structure of Awareness: Toward a Symbolic Language of Human Reflexion*. Translated from the Russian and with a foreword by Anatol Rapoport. Beverly Hills: Sage. 199 pp. LCCN 76051843.

Not read: no lawful copy was reached (see the status note). The summary is
drawn from what the work is standardly cited for. A reading should check it
first. Rapoport's foreword to *Algebra of Conscience* ([LIT-tmpvp0b1](LIT-tmpvp0b1.md), 1982,
p. xi, read in the publisher's free front matter) says this book built "a
similar apparatus, based on a different formalism", and that the 1982
formalism "is a significant step forward in that it permits the calculation
of 'ethical statuses'". So the later books are not a restatement of this one.

## Why this is here

Reflexive control is the influence the record's anchor question did not anticipate: conveying to a target prepared information so that it reaches, by its own reasoning, the decision the controller wants. In the record's terms it acts on the judgement–observation loop of [CLAIM-341](../claims.d/CLAIM-341.md) by shaping the observer, and it makes [QUESTION-121](../questions.d/QUESTION-121.md)'s 'unselected' evidence selected by someone else ([CLAIM-tmpa2zsl](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpa2zsl.md)). This is the formal source: the nested images of self and other that [MOM-CLM-132](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-132.md) calls level-k recursive modelling.
