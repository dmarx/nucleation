Exactly in a linear rank-1 teacher–student model under full-batch gradient descent, and heuristically beyond it, training shrinks the weight covariance's random bulk while growing a spike, so an aligned outlier detaches by a BBP transition at a critical step size or a critical time; the student converges for every step size below one whether or not the outlier has appeared.
