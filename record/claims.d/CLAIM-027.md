# CLAIM-027: Which of an agent's available regimes becomes effective depends on maintained relations — accessibility, precedence, persistence, jurisdiction, suspension and transformation — that are not reducible to the contents of the competing regimes

## The claim

Section 1: "Agents may possess multiple action-generating regimes whose
practical efficacy depends upon maintained relations governing their
accessibility, precedence, jurisdiction, persistence, suspension, and
transformation."

It has two independent lines of support, [ARG-009](../arguments.d/ARG-009.md) (Northstar-A/B) and
[ARG-005](../arguments.d/ARG-005.md) (the reluctant writer), which is what lets the paper claim
the role spans scales. Its standing objections are [CLAIM-010](CLAIM-010.md) (answered)
and [CLAIM-089](CLAIM-089.md) and [CLAIM-024](CLAIM-024.md) (both still open).

## What it does not say

That the dimensions are a complete taxonomy (Section 7.5), or that the
mechanisms are new ([CLAIM-021](CLAIM-021.md)).
