# THEORY-tmp0dvpy: Rosa holds that social acceleration has three dimensions, technological acceleration, acceleration of social change and of the pace of life, forming a closed, self-propelling cycle in which standing still means falling behind, so that modern societies must accelerate and grow merely to keep their institutional status quo, a mode he later calls dynamic stabilization

## The account

Rosa holds that acceleration in three dimensions forms a cycle that is "cerrado y autoimpulsado", in which "permanecer detenido es equivalente a quedarse atrás". In 2021 he names the resulting mode "dynamic stabilization", a society that must grow and accelerate "just to stay in place", and adds that slowing it is possible "but only at the cost of its institutions getting in crises".

## Evidence

The readings in [NOTE-tmp6llzw](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp6llzw.md) and [NOTE-tmp1ip5i](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp1ip5i.md). Corroborates [CLAIM-tmpbypt7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbypt7.md): keeping pace "para evitar la pérdida de opciones y conexiones".

## What it does not say

That deceleration is the remedy: "I am NOT a proponent of slow-down or deceleration." Nor that "dynamic stabilization" is the book's own term: Rosa says it is the name of later books.
