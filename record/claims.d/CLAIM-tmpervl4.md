# CLAIM-tmpervl4: Within large populations of phase oscillators coupled through the first harmonic with spread frequencies, Ott and Antonsen's reduction meets the rate-difference claim's first defeater: the order parameter's closed law is in place over the time scale of the dynamics it governs, the approach to it is by phase mixing with no condition that it be faster than that dynamics, and the off-manifold parts are not slower or more persistent than the process but simply averaged out; so the claim should name invariant-manifold closure reached by phase mixing as a route to effective constraint on which rate differences do no work

## The objection

[CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md)'s mechanism is that degrees of freedom which "settle faster than
others by more than the rates vary among them" settle into a relation that
then constrains. Its defeater (a) is a constraint in place "although
nothing they consist of is slower or more persistent than that process, so
that relative invariance does no work in making them constraints".

Ott and Antonsen's populations give such a constraint:

1. **It is in place over the process's time scale.** For Δ > 0 every
   long-time behaviour of the order parameter obeys the reduced law
   ([THEORY-tmp0d58z](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp0d58z.md)), whatever the mean field does in time.
2. **Nothing settles faster by a margin.** The proof's only condition is
   Δ > 0. The guaranteed approach rate is Δ, while the order parameter's own
   relaxation on the manifold runs at K/2 − Δ in the locked regime, faster
   than Δ once K > 4Δ. The constraint is reached without the fast-relative-
   to-slow ordering the claim's mechanism needs.
3. **What it consists of is not more persistent than the process.** The
   off-manifold harmonics of the distribution do not decay and do not
   persist as a slow variable either; they keep their size and dephase
   ([NOTE-tmp5oph4](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp5oph4.md), key result 4). The order parameter stops seeing them
   because a frequency-weighted average of rotations at different rates
   cancels, not because something slower holds them. The manifold's
   invariance is a property of the coupling's form, which is not a
   degree of freedom of the system at all.
4. **It is typical where it applies.** The class is not an exotic
   fine-tuning within synchronization: Ott and Antonsen 2009 say the phase
   velocity has the required form for all the problems of their
   references 2 to 14 (p. 2), from Josephson junctions and footbridges to
   circadian drives, delays, communities and chimeras, and the
   Kuramoto model is the standard model the record's own synchronization
   claims use ([CLAIM-481](CLAIM-481.md), [CLAIM-626](CLAIM-626.md)).

## What follows for [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md)

Not rejection. [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md) already says separation is not necessary. What
this adds is that the route without separation is not only exact
lumpability and reversible linear cases at measure zero, but the standard
nonlinear model of collective synchronization, so the claim's "Not one
mechanism" list should carry invariant-manifold closure by phase mixing as
a route to effective constraint on which rate differences do no work. Near
threshold the same model does show the claim's second half (the collective
mode slows), so the claim holds there.

## Why it is not leaned to

[CLAIM-tmpa694z](CLAIM-tmpa694z.md) answers points 3 and 4: an exact invariance is persistence
in the limit, as [CLAIM-478](CLAIM-478.md)'s note on Yang and Abanin ruled for
conservation laws, and a class defined by first-harmonic coupling, infinite
N and spread frequencies is narrow ([CLAIM-tmpo6ehq](CLAIM-tmpo6ehq.md)). Which reading of
"more persistent" the defeater intends is the owner's.
