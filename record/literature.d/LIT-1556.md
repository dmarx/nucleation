# LIT-1556: The Indian Buffet Process: An Introduction and Review

Griffiths, T. L. & Ghahramani, Z. (2011). The Indian buffet process: An
introduction and review. *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 12:
1185–1224. https://jmlr.org/papers/v12/griffiths11a.html.

Read 2026-10-26 in full, in [NOTE-1172](../notes.d/NOTE-1172.md). The summary was checked
against the reading and holds. What the reading adds is in
[THEORY-999](../theory.d/THEORY-999.md): the αH_N count T23 quotes is the limit's, and at T23's
K = 16 the finite model expects 6.83 occupied features.

## Why this is here

Cited at T22, where the owner proposed the Indian buffet process for
inferring employees' overlapping latent features without fixing their
number, and at T23, whose dynamic beta–Bernoulli model starts from an IBP
prior truncated at a finite K. The record held the IBP only as the
alternative Friston et al. name in [NOTE-485](../notes.d/NOTE-485.md) ([LIT-615](LIT-615.md)). The reply's marginal
E[K_N] = αH_N is checked in [CASE-165](../cases.d/CASE-165.md), with where its finite and dynamic
versions depart from it. It is the candidate method of [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md), and the
IBP is the latent-feature prior on which [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md)'s distinction turns.

## Note of 2026-10-27: the Markov IBP, now held

The Markov IBP that this review's §7.4 describes, and that the status note
calls the closest variant to a dynamic feature model, was registered and
read on 2026-10-27 as [LIT-1577](LIT-1577.md) ([NOTE-1199](../notes.d/NOTE-1199.md)). The review cites it as
Van Gael et al., NIPS volume 21, 2009, the year of the printed volume; the
conference was December 2008, and the record dates it 2008-12-01. §7.4's
description of its two per-column parameters is confirmed against it.
