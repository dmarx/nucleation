# ARG-119: Two-step acquisition → definition of learning

## The inference

[TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md) defines learning as an experience-dependent change in a
system's retained organization that alters what it can later do, at what
cost and with what reliability, judged against a counterfactual in which
the experience did not occur. Successful learning, relative to a declared
criterion, is kept apart. Its job is to let claims about learning in any
of the record's lines say what changed.

Version 1 argued that it does this better than the definition A46 says it
extends: a system learns when its performance at a class of tasks, by a
measure, improves with experience (Mitchell's). Carnap's first question
of rivals is whether they explicate the same thing (§9; [NOTE-2110](../notes.d/NOTE-2110.md),
result 8). They do not. Mitchell's definition fixes a task class and a
measure in advance and asks whether performance rose; that is what the
term calls successful learning relative to a declared criterion.
[TERM-131](../terms.d/TERM-131.md) explicates something else: what the experience changed in
what the system can later do, and at what cost, whatever criterion is
later applied. So, as with Carnap's two probabilities, the two are
compatible, and both stand. The argument is that the record's claims
about learning need the second explicandum, and that the term explicates
it adequately.

## Critical questions

Asked as [ADR-107](../decisions.d/ADR-107.md) gives them, from Carnap's requirements and his
practice ([THEORY-2017](../theory.d/THEORY-2017.md), [NOTE-2110](../notes.d/NOTE-2110.md)).

- **Is the explicandum clear, and is it the rivals'?** Mostly clear, by
  the term's own examples. Meant: training, will-organization,
  institutions and self-formation, where the record already used the word
  undefined. Not meant: a cooling material relaxing, a population changing
  by differential survival, a fixed controller responding (A46 §7). The
  performance definition explicates another explicandum, as above. Whether
  a population is meant as the system was never said, which is the next
  question.
- **Is it similar enough: clearly too wide, or clearly too narrow?**
  - *Selection.* If a population may be the declared system, differential
    survival is an experience-dependent change in its retained
    organization, and the definition counts it as learning. Whether
    evolution "learns" is not a case of practical agreement either way, so
    this does not show the definition clearly too wide. It is a contested
    case the term has to decide and say it has decided. Version 2 of the
    term decides it: relative to the declared system. [CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) supports
    the exclusion for members only: what a population appears to maintain
    is set by what selection keeps, not produced by any member.
  - *Memorization.* Counted as learning, as ordinary use does. [CLAIM-519](../claims.d/CLAIM-519.md)
    shows why competence cannot test anything more: over a finite horizon
    a lookup table reproduces a model's behaviour exactly.
  - *A step that loses on its own tasks.* In [CASE-295](../cases.d/CASE-295.md) a procedure
    loses on the one question it serves but cuts the cost of a second
    acquisition from 10 to 1. The term counts it as learning: retained
    organization changed and later costs fell. That departs from ordinary
    use, which ties learning to improvement, and is paid for by
    [CLAIM-756](../claims.d/CLAIM-756.md), which turns on it.
  - *Overconsolidation.* Stabilization that improves the present objective
    while raising the cost of later acquisition ([CLAIM-758](../claims.d/CLAIM-758.md)) is
    learning on both definitions; only this one can state what it costs.
  - No case of practical agreement was found that the term gets wrong.
- **Does it meet the necessary conditions?** The one any explicatum of
  learning plausibly needs is that the experience make a difference to the
  system, judged against what it would otherwise have been. Carnap draws
  the same line for confirming evidence, an increase over the prior and
  not a level reached (§87). The counterfactual clause meets it. A fixed
  controller and relaxation fail it, as they should.
- **Is it exact enough to apply, and interpreted?** Only once a comparator
  and a budget are declared. The counterfactual is [TERM-117](../terms.d/TERM-117.md)'s comparator;
  "what it can later do, at what cost" needs the operating and acquisition
  budgets of [CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md). "Retained organization" is not
  operationalized; it is whatever state persists between the experience
  and the later test.
- **Is it fruitful: what becomes statable that was not?** The three
  frontiers moving separately ([CLAIM-754](../claims.d/CLAIM-754.md)), overconsolidation
  ([CLAIM-758](../claims.d/CLAIM-758.md)), and an acquisition valued by what it does to other
  acquisitions ([CLAIM-756](../claims.d/CLAIM-756.md)). Four claims and one argument are stated
  in it.
- **Is it as simple as the rest allow?** Four elements: experience, a
  change in retained organization, a later consequence and, kept apart,
  an evaluation. Cost and reliability are each used by a claim above.
- **Against which rivals, by what consequences, with what evidence?** No
  earlier TERM for learning is on record, and the performance definition
  is not a rival. Learning as compression, or as Bayesian updating, were
  not weighed. The evidence of fruitfulness is internal in Carnap's sense
  (§103): the claims that show it were written in the term's own words, so
  they show it can be used, not that it is adequate. An outside check is
  whether those claims survive test; the Pythia study ([CASE-294](../cases.d/CASE-294.md))
  would be one.

## Verdict

Proposed. The term explicates what the record's claims about learning
need, and it stands beside the performance definition rather than against
it. Two things would strengthen it: the term saying how it decides the
population case, and an outside test of a claim stated in it. A rival that
explicates the same thing and does as well on these questions would
weaken it.
