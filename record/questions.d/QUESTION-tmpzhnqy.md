# QUESTION-tmpzhnqy: Is work deferred to a later time a movement of work of its own, beside moving it earlier, into upkeep or to other parties, and does it differ from a transfer in that a deferred cost can grow before it is paid?

## The gap

[CLAIM-760](../claims.d/CLAIM-760.md) holds that structure moves work rather than removing it: "to an
earlier time, to upkeep, or to other parties". Article 3 of the manuscript
needed a fourth case: work put off to later, which may be larger when it
falls due. [CLAIM-569](../claims.d/CLAIM-569.md)'s note of 2026-11-07 on technical debt is the nearest
entry, and [CLAIM-760](../claims.d/CLAIM-760.md)'s note on A40 §4.4 touches it.

## What would answer it

A construction in the stored-work model ([CASE-326](../cases.d/CASE-326.md)) where deferring a
maintenance task raises its later cost, against one where the same task is
handed to another party at unchanged cost. If the two can always be
written as one movement, the question is answered no.
