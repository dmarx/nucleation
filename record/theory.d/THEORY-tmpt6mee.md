# THEORY-tmpt6mee: Adewuyi reports Vallor as holding that AI systems mirror past human data and can change human character, that reliance on algorithmic decision produces moral deskilling, a loss of the capacity for moral reasoning, and that the remedy is collective self-renewal guided by practical wisdom

## The account

As the review reports it, Vallor holds that AI mirrors humanity "as we already are and have long been", that it can change human character, that dependence on algorithmic decision produces moral deskilling (the reviewer's paraphrase: humans "lose the capacity for moral reasonings"), and that the remedy is democratic self-renewal "guided by practical wisdom".
