The owner asked for a pass over `identity` after the metaphysics pass. Its
blurb: "what makes something the same thing over time or across descriptions
— individuality, persistence, personal identity". The tag held 18 works. 13
were already read, eleven of them by the mereology, agency and metaphysics
passes. The coverage audit added five, one of them unread.

## Tags

Five works newly tagged `identity`:

- **[LIT-188](../../record/literature.d/LIT-188.md)** (*Scaffolding individuality*), on individuality.
- **[LIT-045](../../record/literature.d/LIT-045.md)** (SEP *Structural Realism*), because quantum non-individuality
  motivates ontic structural realism.
- **The three duality works** ([LIT-115](../../record/literature.d/LIT-115.md), [LIT-199](../../record/literature.d/LIT-199.md), [LIT-113](../../record/literature.d/LIT-113.md)). Each asks when two
  formulations describe the same world, which is identity across descriptions.

Two readers flagged a question about the tag's reach:

- **Archer & Robb** concerns *narrative or practical* personal identity. Fans
  who freeze a celebrity's persona are the subject.
- **Browning & Veit** concerns *individuality* as a boundary with others.

The blurb's "personal identity" covers both. Neither concerns numerical
identity. The tags stay, and this entry records the reading so that a later
narrowing of the word has the cases in front of it.

## A defect found and repaired

The audit found [LIT-045](../../record/literature.d/LIT-045.md)'s summary saying its first sentence twice. The cause
was the first version of the script that applies readings. It took a summary's
citation to end at the first ")." in the text, so part of the seed summary could
be kept in front of the reading's summary.

- **Checked:** all 142 summaries written from full readings, against their
  original seed text in git, following each file back through its renaming
  from a temporary code.
- **Affected:** five.
  - [LIT-045](../../record/literature.d/LIT-045.md);
  - four from earlier readings: [LIT-047](../../record/literature.d/LIT-047.md), [LIT-059](../../record/literature.d/LIT-059.md), [LIT-066](../../record/literature.d/LIT-066.md) and [LIT-075](../../record/literature.d/LIT-075.md).

  Six others share wording with their seed deliberately, and were left alone.
- **Repair:** in each of the five, exactly the text before the reading's own
  summary (as its NOTE gives it) is removed. Each LIT's history records the
  repair.
- **Script:** fixed earlier, in the mereology pass, when the related "et al."
  defect was found.
