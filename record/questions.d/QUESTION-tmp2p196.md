# QUESTION-tmp2p196: When locally realizable histories of a reduced variable have no common realization in the full system, does the failure identify a capacity or constraint the reduction omitted, and can it be told apart from modelling error or from observations of different systems?

## Why it is a question

A29 §4 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e): "Can the
failure of locally realizable macro-histories to admit a common
microscopic realization diagnose organizational capacities that a
proposed coarse-graining has omitted?" A's own guard: "The answer is not
always yes; inconsistency can also come from modeling errors or an
incorrect assumption that two observations belong to the same system."

[CLAIM-tmprmy7a](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmprmy7a.md) holds that such failures occur, and [CASE-tmpdwka1](../cases.d/CASE-tmpdwka1.md) is one in
which the failure is witnessed by an omitted variable, the hidden mode.
[QUESTION-379](QUESTION-379.md) asks when a coherent family of local observations has a
realizable object at all. This asks what a failure tells about the
reduction that produced it.

A29 §10 proposes the test on an adaptive HKB system ([CASE-tmpn5x96](../cases.d/CASE-tmpn5x96.md)): check
"whether locally realizable histories of the observed relative phase can
be assembled into a globally realizable history of the full system", and
if not, "investigate which omitted variables explain the obstruction".

## What would answer it

- A class of reductions in which every such failure is witnessed by an
  omitted variable, or a counterexample: a failure no added variable
  explains.
- A criterion that separates an omitted capacity from a modelling error
  or from observations of two systems.
- A negative answer A29 §10 names: "If every claim reduces immediately to
  a conventional reachability calculation without providing new
  compositional methods, invariants, or cross-scale predictions, the sheaf
  framework may not earn its added complexity."
