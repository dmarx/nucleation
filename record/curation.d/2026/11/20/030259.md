The owner asked on 2026-11-20, on pull request 546, that question
dependencies be backfilled in a new pull request: recommendation 5 of the
postmortem of the session's luria.yaml changes, which found QUESTION
`presupposes` unused. Four read-only sweepers each took a quarter of the
questions, 457 open ones in all, and proposed edges by the field's own
test: a question whose concepts or result any answer to this one must use;
not one whose answer would only make it easier, not a sharpening (which is
`refines`), and not a dependence on a claim or term. Each edge came with a
sentence and a quote from each side; the filer checked every quote against
the files.

## Findings

Dependencies in the strict sense are rare: 15 proposed among 457 open
questions, 4 filed. Most questions are either sharpenings of another,
already held as `refines`, or narrow checks of single results that stand
alone. The field is worth keeping for the few it fits; it will not carry
much of the structure among questions, which `refines` already does.
