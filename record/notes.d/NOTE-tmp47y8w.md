# NOTE-tmp47y8w: Floridi — A defence of informational structural realism

## Contribution

This is the revised text of a 2003 keynote at the first Australian Computing and Philosophy Conference. The first part defends an informational version of structural realism in three steps. First, epistemic and ontic structural realism are compatible. Second, not every relatum is logically prior to every relation. Third, structural objects can be spelled out as informational objects, which applies to observables and unobservables alike. The upshot is a world made up of interacting informational objects. The second part answers ten objections raised by colleagues.

## Skim

*Abstract, figures and selected sections, read when the work was seeded. Not enough to state its assumptions or results exactly; a `Read` note replaces this one.*

- §2 (pp. 5–21): the method of levels of abstraction (LoAs) sets the ontological commitments, and ESR and OSR are said to be compatible once each is indexed to an LoA: first-order commitment to structures, and a reflective, minimal commitment to structural objects.
- §3 (pp. 22–24): answers the "relations need relata" regress objection (Psillos) by pointing to the internal relation of *difference*, which constitutes its relata. This is his "Ur-relation", supported by a "modified Leibnizian principle of the necessary inexistence of the logically undifferentiable".
- §4 (pp. 25–30): structural objects are informational objects, meaning data as "differences de re" (a mind-independent lack of uniformity), not alphanumeric data.
- §5 (pp. 31–33): the formal definition of ISR, and the claim that it reconciles Wiener's "information is information, not matter or energy" with Wheeler's "it from bit".
- §6 (pp. 34–43): ten objections and replies. The first argues that the Psillos/Papineau "collapse" argument against ESR is valid but unsound.
- §7 (pp. 43–45): a "double box" analogy (Sextus' dark room plus Turing's black boxes). Knowledge is constructionist (designing and modelling), not mimetic.

## Open questions

- This is the source position that [LIT-tmpzstkk](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzstkk.md) (Karpenko) extends. A deeper reading should compare what Karpenko adds, such as digital ontology and quantum-information interpretations.
- Check whether the difference-as-Ur-relation argument really escapes the regress, or only moves it into the LoA machinery.
- The "information" at issue is Floridi's philosophy-of-information notion, not Shannon's measure. The information-theory tag is justified only in that broad sense, so a deeper reading should confirm it.
