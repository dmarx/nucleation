The owner asked for arXiv 2107.09133 to be read and filed here "if we
don't already have it". This record did not hold it. The anthology does:
[ANTH-LIT-454](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-454.md), with [ANTH-NOTE-202](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/notes.d/NOTE-202.md), [ANTH-THEORY-031](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/theory.d/THEORY-031.md) and [ANTH-SOTA-270](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/practices.d/SOTA-270.md).

**Held in both, under [ADR-013](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-013.md).** Each record reads the paper for its own
question:

- **The anthology** reads it for practice, as one of three measured ways
  the aggregate loss curve hides what training does ([ANTH-THEORY-031](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/theory.d/THEORY-031.md)). It
  gives the instruction not to read a smooth curve as smooth training
  ([ANTH-SOTA-270](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/practices.d/SOTA-270.md)).
- **This record** reads it for the learned-structure line: what a
  converged run is. On the paper's account it is a nonequilibrium
  stationary distribution on a modified loss, with spread and correlations
  set by the optimizer. That bears on [QUESTION-069](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-069.md)'s "regime" alternative.

The entries here are [LIT-tmp0kjhc](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp0kjhc.md), [NOTE-tmpq7q6s](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpq7q6s.md) and [THEORY-tmp4lwrf](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmp4lwrf.md). The
LIT names [ANTH-LIT-454](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-454.md) in its status note and history. It carries the
`anthology-candidate` flag last, as [ADR-005](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-005.md) requires of a work an
anthology topic can hold, and as this record's other dual holdings do.

**What the reading added beyond the anthology's.** Two limits the account
needs for this record's question, recorded in the NOTE and the THEORY:

- The model is silent on the Hessian's null space, where flat and
  symmetric directions live, so it cannot speak to connectivity along
  them.
- Its deep-network evidence is one ResNet-18, in one two-dimensional
  projection.
