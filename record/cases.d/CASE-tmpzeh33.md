# CASE-tmpzeh33: Solidarity, condemnation and humour: the parity obstruction

## The case

Manuscript §3: "take three binary observables solidarity S, condemnation M, and
humor H, available in pairs {S,M}, {M,H}, and {H,S}. Constraints S=M, M=H, and
H≠S are individually pairwise satisfiable but admit no common global
assignment. This parity example demonstrates an obstruction, not a claim about
actual judgments."

## What it can show

That local consistency need not glue ([CLAIM-tmpaviuz](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpaviuz.md)). It changes [CASE-tmp8ayjc](CASE-tmp8ayjc.md) from a
probabilistic question about data into a logical example. It cannot show that
pragmatic judgements behave so ([CLAIM-tmp1ycte](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1ycte.md)).
