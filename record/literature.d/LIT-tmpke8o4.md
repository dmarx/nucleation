# LIT-tmpke8o4: Philosophy of Probability: Foundations, Epistemology, and Computation

Sylvia Wenmackers (2011), *PhD dissertation, University of Groningen (supervisor I. E. J. Douven)* — <https://philpapers.org/rec/WENPOP>

## Key takeaways

- A fair lottery on the natural numbers, impossible under Kolmogorov's axioms, can be modelled by a non-Archimedean probability ("normalised numerosities") giving each ticket a non-zero infinitesimal chance, and the same non-standard strategy yields "Stratified Belief", a contextualist resolution of the Lottery Paradox.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

The dissertation is a contribution to formal and computational philosophy. Its first part exploits parallels between very large finite lotteries and countably infinite ones to gain insight into both the foundations of probability and epistemology, solving their respective "adding problems" with non-standard analysis. Its second part uses computer simulation to study communities of agents who update beliefs by averaging, and computes the probability that an agent ends in an inconsistent belief state.
