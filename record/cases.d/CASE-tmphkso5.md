# CASE-tmphkso5: A proposed study: two communities that agree in their present use of a category are shown evidence that it conflates two phenomena, and one revises the distinction while the other reinterprets the evidence as irrelevant, with their understanding of the evidence and their incentives controlled, to tell agreement in a concept from similarity in the organization that maintains and revises it

## The case

A50 §7 of the exchange "Individuation, Possibility, and Disposition"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e), after
its *anti-vax* example: "Suppose two communities currently agree about a
category." "Present both with evidence showing that the category conflates
two genuinely different phenomena." "One community revises the
distinction." "The other retains the old application rule and instead
reinterprets the evidence so that it appears irrelevant." A: "Their
initial consensus concealed different **epistemic dispositions**."

**What is measured.** Each community's application of the category before
and after the evidence, on ordinary and borderline cases: whether it
splits the category, keeps it, or keeps it while discounting the
evidence.

**The main threat to inference.** A: "We would need controls to establish
that the communities understood the evidence similarly and faced
comparable incentives. Otherwise, differences in response could have
simpler explanations." A difference in comprehension, or in what revising
would cost each community, would explain the split without any difference
in their organization.

**What each outcome would show.** If both revise, or neither does, the
agreement gives no evidence either way. If, with those controls met, one
revises and the other does not, "the experiment could distinguish
conceptual agreement from similarity of the organization that maintains
and revises concepts."

## What it can show

- **A test of [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)'s split.** The term keeps a concept apart from
  conceptual corrigibility, "the further capacity to find and revise its
  inadequacies". Matched present use with different revision is that split
  observed. In [TERM-tmp6ersv](../terms.d/TERM-tmp6ersv.md)'s terms, the communities share a present
  concept and differ in a disposition (sense 1 or 2, the study does not
  say which) to revise it.
- **The concept-level counterpart of [CASE-188](CASE-188.md)**, which separates
  corrigible resolve from entrenchment in two agents who decide alike under
  ordinary conditions.
- **Bears on [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md)**, when a learned distinction is a concept.

As a stipulated design it has no results. It says which outcome would
count, not which is common, and whose disposition it is (the members', or
the community's procedures) is a boundary the design must declare.
