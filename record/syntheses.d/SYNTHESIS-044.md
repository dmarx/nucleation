# SYNTHESIS-044: An organization's regulatory difficulty is frustration among its maintained demands, an ordinal fact about which sets of them can be jointly met; its perspectives' activation, tolerance, claims and grants are quantities separate from that fact, so a quiet perspective need not be satisfied and raising a threshold is not relief; and motivational integration is the co-satisfaction of the demands within tolerances the will can recognize

*A part account of the will-organization line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)); the line's account is built over it. Five of its claims have distributed-agency as their first line, and four organizational-affect or formal-apparatus; [CASE-129](../cases.d/CASE-129.md)'s is authenticity.*

## The account

**Frustration, ordinal.** [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) (the owner's U33 of the exchange of
2026-10-17, Active; distributed-agency first) rests on [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) and
[CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md): the primitive for regulatory difficulty is frustration, a
relation between maintained demands and what limits their realization,
distinct from strain and from distress. [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) (the owner's correction
at U37, Active) rests on it: jointly satisfiable sets form a complex, relief
is its strict enlargement for the same demands, and "dropping or
suppressing a demand is not relief". [CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md) (A's, Active) rests on
[CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md) and [CLAIM-365](../claims.d/CLAIM-365.md) and separates structural infeasibility, realized
violation and minimax suboptimality, none of them thwarting. [CLAIM-500](../claims.d/CLAIM-500.md)
(Proposed), split from [CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md) by the eighteenth status review, rests on
it: governance-induced frustration is not idle. [CLAIM-631](../claims.d/CLAIM-631.md) (Proposed,
demonstrated; the derivation that was [SYNTHESIS-008](SYNTHESIS-008.md)) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and
[CLAIM-549](../claims.d/CLAIM-549.md) and gives a third complex, met or quiet, that decides when
frustration forces a member that acts while unmet.

**Relief by niche differentiation.** [CLAIM-554](../claims.d/CLAIM-554.md) (A7, Active) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md) and
[CLAIM-369](../claims.d/CLAIM-369.md): niche differentiation matters as possible relief, not as reduced
competition.

**Integration and tolerance.** [CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md) (A29's wording at the owner's U33,
Active) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-538](../claims.d/CLAIM-538.md), [CLAIM-357](../claims.d/CLAIM-357.md) and [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md): [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md)'s
motivational integration is the extent to which maintained demands are
jointly met within tolerances the will can recognize, across contexts and
under its own dynamics, with realizability, realization, maintenance and
allocation coming apart. [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md) (demonstrated, Active) rests on
[CLAIM-538](../claims.d/CLAIM-538.md) and gives the cycle conditions under disturbance; [CLAIM-628](../claims.d/CLAIM-628.md)
(demonstrated, Proposed; formal-apparatus first) rests on [CLAIM-537](../claims.d/CLAIM-537.md) and
[CLAIM-538](../claims.d/CLAIM-538.md) for a class of disturbances the agent cannot tell apart.

**Activation is not satisfaction.** [CLAIM-548](../claims.d/CLAIM-548.md) (the owner's U18, Active)
rests on [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) and uses [TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md): a process can become active and act
without expecting success, so activation, authority granted and efficacy
are separate. [CLAIM-549](../claims.d/CLAIM-549.md) (A's construction at the owner's U17,
demonstrated, Active) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and [CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md), grounding on [CASE-109](../cases.d/CASE-109.md)
and [CASE-129](../cases.d/CASE-129.md): the quiescent complex is not the requirement complex, and
"raising thresholds can only enlarge the quiescent complex" without relief.
[CASE-129](../cases.d/CASE-129.md) (A18's, at the owner's U18; Active; authenticity first) is two
alarms quiet in ordinary operation, only one of which responds to a new
hazard: "A quiet signal may be calibration or suppression". It also grounds
[CLAIM-553](../claims.d/CLAIM-553.md), and illustrates [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md) and [CLAIM-364](../claims.d/CLAIM-364.md) of other parts.
[CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md) (demonstrated, Active) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md) and derives a
perspective's tolerance from its activation threshold. [CLAIM-551](../claims.d/CLAIM-551.md) (Active),
the DBT reading of the owner's U29, rests on [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md): cognitive
dysregulation as a consequence of emotion dysregulation, which makes the
effective will mood-dependent. [CASE-192](../cases.d/CASE-192.md) (Proposed) illustrates it against
an attentional reading.

**Tolerance as conferral.** [CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md) (the owner's U12, Active) rests on
[CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md) and [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md): a perspective's tolerance is its criterion for
conferring influence, directed and set by its own priorities. [CLAIM-553](../claims.d/CLAIM-553.md) (the
owner's U15 as corrected at U17, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-417](../claims.d/CLAIM-417.md) and
[CLAIM-534](../claims.d/CLAIM-534.md): observed intervention is a propensity times legibility times a
channel, "so a perspective that does not intervene has not thereby
endorsed", and wholeheartedness read through Frankfurt lies within every
perspective's tolerance. It uses [TERM-102](../terms.d/TERM-102.md). [QUESTION-372](../questions.d/QUESTION-372.md) (the owner's U19,
verbatim) asks whether threshold induces tolerance, tolerance compatibility,
and compatibility flexibility or effectiveness; [CLAIM-548](../claims.d/CLAIM-548.md) and [CLAIM-550](../claims.d/CLAIM-550.md) bear
on it, and it refines [QUESTION-090](../questions.d/QUESTION-090.md). [CASE-195](../cases.d/CASE-195.md) (Proposed) is a construction
for its last step, flexibility spread by leverage and culture.

**Claims and grants.** [CLAIM-484](../claims.d/CLAIM-484.md) (Proposed), split from [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) by the
status review of 2026-10-27, rests on it: internal conflict is the gap
between the precedence a motive claims and the authority it is granted.
[ARG-049](../arguments.d/ARG-049.md), from the MSF transcript, is Rejected. [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md) (the assistant's,
Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md) and [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) and
puts Frijda's emotion ([TERM-094](../terms.d/TERM-094.md)) on the claim side and system valence on
the grant side; since its version 5 (2026-11-11) its body cites [CLAIM-484](../claims.d/CLAIM-484.md),
not [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md), for internal conflict as the gap, and says its title uses
only [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s distinction. [CLAIM-527](../claims.d/CLAIM-527.md) (Proposed; organizational-affect first) rests on
[CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) and [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md): viable reach, valence and emotion are three
quantities. [CLAIM-447](../claims.d/CLAIM-447.md) (the owner's, Proposed; organizational-affect first)
rests on [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md): model-based wanting ranks predicted futures under
interventions. [TERM-079](../terms.d/TERM-079.md) (Proposed) proposes belief of a process on the same
footing, and [QUESTION-270](../questions.d/QUESTION-270.md) asks when an accurate evaluation is registered and
still denied authority; [CLAIM-552](../claims.d/CLAIM-552.md) and [CLAIM-553](../claims.d/CLAIM-553.md) bear on it.
