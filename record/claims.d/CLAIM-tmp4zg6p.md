# CLAIM-tmp4zg6p: A contested r/place artwork is a dissipative structure in Prigogine's structural sense, order held only by a sustained flow of repair, but its dissipation is supplied by rival coalitions and its order by an imported design, so it is dissipative only while contested and is not order born of an instability

## The claim

The owner, on [THEORY-tmp7w5tq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7w5tq.md)'s "lasting artworks are those whose coalitions
keep working under continual overwriting": "sounds like there's likely a
connection to dissipative structure here".

Prigogine's criterion, as the record reads his Nobel lecture ([LIT-516](../literature.d/LIT-516.md),
[NOTE-424](../notes.d/NOTE-424.md)), is an open system held far from equilibrium, maintained by flows,
whose order is born from an instability: dissipative structures are "giant
fluctuations" amplified past an instability and stabilized by exchange with
the outside ([NOTE-424](../notes.d/NOTE-424.md), C6). The r/place readings meet the maintenance half:

- Under 7% of 2017 updates survive to the end, and the lasting artworks are
  the ones whose coalitions keep placing under overwriting
  ([THEORY-tmp7w5tq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp7w5tq.md)).
- Compositions persist as a balance of attack and repair, and are
  overthrown when attack outruns defence or defence lapses ([THEORY-tmp07t7w](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp07t7w.md)).
- The pattern is a property of the flow, not of the pixels, which turn
  over. That is [CLAIM-tmpjhd7t](CLAIM-tmpjhd7t.md)'s persistent product and persistent producer,
  and it is how a convection roll relates to its fluid.

Two features break the analogy, and they are what makes it informative:

1. **The dissipation is supplied by rivals.** A pixel nobody touches stays
   put. The canvas has no thermal decay. Falmagne et al. find low past
   activity protective: some compositions last because nobody attacks them
   ([THEORY-tmp07t7w](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp07t7w.md)). So an artwork is dissipative only while contested. In
   quiet regions it is closer to inert storage. Each coalition's "outside"
   is mostly other coalitions, whose own artworks are maintained the same
   way. That is mutual dissipation, nearer to ecological competition than to
   a convection cell held by a fixed temperature gradient.
2. **The order is imported, not born of an instability.** Adams et al.
   ([THEORY-tmpy8l36](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpy8l36.md)) separate two kinds of object. Some spread by a local
   recolouring rule, such as the Blue Corner and the German flags, and are
   predictable from the canvas. Others are copied from designs coordinated
   off the canvas, such as the EU dove and the US flag, and local dynamics
   do not predict them. Only the first kind is near order arising from local
   instability, Turing's or Prigogine's sense of self-organization. The
   second is a pattern specified elsewhere and held in place by flow,
   dissipative in its maintenance and not in its origin.

## What it does not say

- It does not define a thermodynamic quantity. "Dissipation" here is
  structural. Entropy production is undefined for pixels, and neither
  minimum entropy production, which holds only in the linear regime near
  equilibrium ([NOTE-424](../notes.d/NOTE-424.md), C2), nor the maximum entropy production principle
  transfers. [CLAIM-163](CLAIM-163.md)'s caution applies: the analogy earns its place only
  by what it lets one measure. Here that is the flow needed to hold a
  pattern against a given rate of attack.
- It does not say an artwork is self-maintaining. Whether the pattern feeds
  back into the flow that keeps it is the next rung of the ladder
  ([THEORY-072](../theory.d/THEORY-072.md), [THEORY-tmpidvlt](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpidvlt.md)), and it is open: [QUESTION-tmpgjjah](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpgjjah.md).
- Kugler, Kelso and Turvey made the same move for coordinative structures in
  action ([LIT-913](../literature.d/LIT-913.md), unread), and the coordination-dynamics line that followed
  is [THEORY-069](../theory.d/THEORY-069.md). Their reception is the precedent to read before this claim
  is pressed further.
