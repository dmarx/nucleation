# LIT-1445: Pleasure as a sign you can attend to something else: Placing positive feelings within a general model of affect

Charles S. Carver (2003), *Cognition and Emotion* 17(2):241–261 — DOI-10.1080/02699930302294

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-10-23, unread, with the readings of the control-process
account of affect ([LIT-1441](LIT-1441.md), read through [LIT-1449](LIT-1449.md)). The reading
of Carver (2018), [NOTE-1102](../notes.d/NOTE-1102.md), names it as where positive affect is
said to lower a goal's priority. If so, it bears against [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md)'s
reading of intensity as the strength of an ordering's potential, at least
for positive valence, and should be read before that clause is promoted.
