# THEORY-tmpbzy1i: Moerman and colleagues report that in the failed Fyra V250 rolling-stock introduction most of the patterns predicted by Woods's ten proto-theorems of graceful extensibility were found in the documentary record, while the theory's central quantity, capacity for maneuver, and the distinction between base and extended adaptive capacity were not recognised by the actors.

## The account

Moerman and colleagues report that in the failed Fyra V250 rolling-stock introduction most of the
patterns predicted by Woods's ten proto-theorems of graceful extensibility were found in the
documentary record, while the theory's central quantity, capacity for maneuver, and the
distinction between base and extended adaptive capacity were not recognised by the actors.
