# LEAD-101: Test whether corporations changed only by sanction keep weighing others' harms once the sanction is lifted, with reputation and benefit held fixed

## What it would do

Find cases where a sanction ended and harm-weighing can be traced after.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-431](../claims.d/CLAIM-431.md): "is a test nobody has run."
- [SYNTHESIS-018](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-018.md): "the test whether harms keep being weighed once sanction
  lifts is unrun"

## What would count

Harm-weighing that persists supports [CLAIM-432](../claims.d/CLAIM-432.md)'s part 5.

## Note of 2026-11-23: the run on open sources

Run by a delegated worker on 2026-11-23 (CASE-tmphvbov; rules, data,
script and tests under scratch/studies/lead-101/). Coding rules were written
before the table. One quantitative study and three firm cases were coded.

- **The result: no case meets the standard.** None holds reputation and
  benefit fixed, and no post-lift outcome is a finding against the firm.
  The test CLAIM-431 and CLAIM-432 call unrun is still unrun in the form
  this lead sets.
- **What was found.** A monitor's effect on penalised violations lasts only
  while it is on site (Gallo, Lynch and Tomy 2023; THEORY-tmpvtotj,
  CLAIM-tmpm9a3z). Purdue Pharma, sanction only, widened promotion after its
  integrity agreement ended in 2012, by Indiana's allegations. Siemens,
  coded redesigned on an outside body's assessment, stopped a bribe at a
  cost during its monitorship and is reported to have gone on with the harm
  after it. Wells Fargo's growth restriction was lifted on 2025-06-03: too
  recent to trace.
- **What the test can show** (CLAIM-tmpsuj5n): reversion by a sanction-only
  firm is predicted by both claims; only a redesigned firm's conduct after
  the lift, when keeping the procedure no longer pays, separates them. The
  "held fixed" clause carries the test.
- **Alternatives for the owner:** CLAIM-tmpuvgxw and CLAIM-tmpioexk read the
  board-change result two ways; the run leans to CLAIM-tmpioexk.
- **What remains.** A redesigned firm's private, costly post-lift decision,
  from documents released later; the Purdue plea of 2020 (the Justice
  Department's site showed a bot check); Wells Fargo from mid-2027. The
  status and stage are unchanged.
