# LIT-976: Emergence of Quantised Representations Isolated to Anisotropic Functions

George Bird (2025), *arXiv:2507.12070*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-759](../notes.d/NOTE-759.md).*

- **The ablation.** Autoencoders of width 12–32 with up to three hidden
  blocks are trained by momentum SGD on MSE. Everything but the
  activation commutes with rotations. Elementwise tanh,
  Σᵢ tanh(x·êᵢ)êᵢ, is set against tanh(‖x‖)x̂, and elementwise Leaky-ReLU
  against a radial one. Two signed-permutation Leaky-ReLUs are added.
- **The result.** Elementwise activations give narrow beams.
  - Tanh's lie along and against the axes.
  - Permutation-only Leaky-ReLU's lie on the ±1 diagonal.
  - Signed-permutation Leaky-ReLU's lie on the axes, like tanh's.
  - About 2,000 samples pass the plotting threshold early, against about
    200,000 late in training.
  - Radial activations leave the representations spread.
  - The effect is seen at the first latent layer, which no activation
    precedes.
- **The method.** Representations are projected onto all planes spanned
  by pairs of basis vectors, within an angular threshold. App. A shows and
  partly controls its artefacts along the measured basis.
- **The mechanism offered.** A first-order step through an elementwise
  activation rescales each gradient component by ψ′, orthant by orthant.
  Through a radial one it is a multiple of the gradient plus a radial term
  (App. C.2). This is description, not derivation.
- **What is conjecture.** That this causes grandmother neurons,
  discrete superposition or neural collapse. That isotropic networks
  reconstruct better is preliminary (App. D) and mixed without input
  standardisation.

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-058](../questions.d/QUESTION-058.md), and read

counterpart in the task. It complements [THEORY-184](../theory.d/THEORY-184.md), on what a
permutation-equivariant update preserves. Its prescriptions for choosing
