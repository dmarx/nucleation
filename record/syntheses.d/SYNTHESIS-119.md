# SYNTHESIS-119: In the record, a power law does not identify its mechanism: many routes produce one, each read from its dynamics, criticality among them; chaos, criticality and a cascade through scales come apart, as do scale invariance, dependence across scales and a heavy-tailed marginal; the owner's bulk-and-tail conjecture is held in two readings, of which only a separation of scales fits a continuous transition approached from below; and what survives a change of scale is counted by several effective dimensions whose agreement is open

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line: what the record holds on power laws, scale-free behaviour and criticality. Filed from the synthesis audit of the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21; [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md) step 5, [ADR-131](../decisions.d/ADR-131.md)). It maps A's syntheses at A1 §7, A3 §6–7, A5's close, A7 §5, A8 §8, A10 §8–9, A12 §9, A13 §8–9, A15 §10, A17 §10–11, A19 §8–10, A21 §9–10, A23 §9–10, A24 §8 and close, A26 §8–9 and A27 §8–10 onto the entries filed from that exchange and the held entries they bear on. No held account covered this scope. The line's account, [SYNTHESIS-088](SYNTHESIS-088.md), predates these entries; integrating this part is for its next review. The neighbouring parts on effective descriptions ([SYNTHESIS-075](SYNTHESIS-075.md)) and on coarse-graining criteria against renormalization ([SYNTHESIS-084](SYNTHESIS-084.md)) integrate none of these components. It adds nothing; its standing is its components'.*

## The account

### What produces a power law, and what one shows

- **The distribution does not name the mechanism.** [CLAIM-803](../claims.d/CLAIM-803.md) (A's, A1 §7 and A3 §7, interpretive, Proposed): a power-law distribution does not identify its mechanism. Nor does a shared power law show a shared mechanism or universality class, and the mechanism has to be read from the dynamics. [LEAD-209](../leads.d/LEAD-209.md) (the assistant's, Open) proposes a protocol for reading it: from the cutoff, rescaled distributions, dynamics and response to a symmetry-breaking field.
- **Cumulative advantage, in a particular form.** [CLAIM-817](../claims.d/CLAIM-817.md) (A's, A1 and A3, demonstrated, Proposed): attachment gives a power law only when it is linear in current size and new units keep entering. "Unequal outcomes need no unequal ability." [CLAIM-830](../claims.d/CLAIM-830.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): the Barabási–Albert law is the age mechanism in logarithmic time, so continual entry is what separates it from Gibrat's lognormal growth. [THEORY-1499](../theory.d/THEORY-1499.md) (Active) carries the exchange's note on cities, where a shock to relative size reverts.
- **Multiplicative growth.** [CLAIM-824](../claims.d/CLAIM-824.md) (A's, A3, demonstrated, Proposed): with i.i.d. returns it gives a lognormal; with an additive floor it gives a power-law tail with exponent κ solving E[A^κ] = 1.
- **Routes without positive feedback.** [CLAIM-795](../claims.d/CLAIM-795.md) (A's, A5, demonstrated, Proposed) lists them: critical branching, a tuned critical point, first passage, mixtures of exponential rates, sample-space reduction, growth with a spread of ages, and geometry. Its summary keeps A's "absence of a characteristic scale" as a gloss, not a claim. [CLAIM-815](../claims.d/CLAIM-815.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): the shared 3/2 of critical cascades and first passage is one identity, the hitting-time theorem. [CLAIM-769](../claims.d/CLAIM-769.md) (Proposed, interpretive) holds the mixture route, and its note of 2026-11-21 records A5 §4's gamma case.
- **Rich-get-richer against criticality.** [CLAIM-796](../claims.d/CLAIM-796.md) (A's, A8 §8, interpretive, Proposed) holds that the two reach power laws "by almost opposite routes". [CLAIM-806](../claims.d/CLAIM-806.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed) objects that linear attachment is itself balanced at a boundary, between stretched-exponential and condensing regimes.
- **A power law does not indicate criticality.** [CLAIM-841](../claims.d/CLAIM-841.md) (A's from A10 §6 to A27 §8, with the assistant's checks; demonstrated, Proposed) lists routes that remove a characteristic scale with no tuned transition: symmetry, flux, growth, coarsening (as dynamic scaling), geometry and broad relaxation spectra. [THEORY-1085](../theory.d/THEORY-1085.md) (Active, Toner and Tu) carries the exchange's note using a flock's ordered phase as one such route. [CLAIM-692](../claims.d/CLAIM-692.md) (Active) holds that 1/f scaling does not discriminate a macro-level constraint from micro-level mechanism. [CLAIM-626](../claims.d/CLAIM-626.md) (Proposed) holds that critical slowing has no single organizational meaning; its note adds slow modes from a conserved density, with no transition.
- **Which scale-free property.** [TERM-142](../terms.d/TERM-142.md) (the assistant's term, Proposed) separates three meanings of "scale-free": a heavy-tailed marginal, a scale-covariant law of correlation or dynamics, and a power-law spectrum. [CLAIM-846](../claims.d/CLAIM-846.md) (A's, A27 §9, demonstrated, Proposed): scale-free correlations need no heavy-tailed marginals. [CLAIM-810](../claims.d/CLAIM-810.md) (A's, A23, demonstrated, Proposed): scale invariance, dependence across scales and transfer across scales come apart, so "a hierarchy of structure is not a hierarchy of causation".
- **Chaos, criticality and self-similarity.** The owner asked at U4 whether scale-free behaviour at transitions relates to chaos and is self-similarity ([QUESTION-516](../questions.d/QUESTION-516.md), Open). [CLAIM-850](../claims.d/CLAIM-850.md) (the assistant's, answering U4, demonstrated, Proposed): chaos, criticality and a cascade through scales are distinct, each occurring without the others. They meet only through particular mechanisms, such as the period-doubling accumulation point. [ARG-129](../arguments.d/ARG-129.md) (Proposed) holds that this meeting is formal, a renormalization fixed point with one relevant direction, though not every row of A6's table pairs like with like. [TERM-139](../terms.d/TERM-139.md) (A's, Proposed) names relevant, irrelevant and marginal perturbations. [THEORY-2041](../theory.d/THEORY-2041.md) (Proposed) is the deep-network instance of an order-to-chaos transition.
- **Left open.** [QUESTION-505](../questions.d/QUESTION-505.md) (A5, Open): what fixes a mechanism's exponent. [QUESTION-508](../questions.d/QUESTION-508.md) (A's, Open): why some systems reach scale invariance untuned while others need tuning.

### The owner's bulk-and-tail conjecture

- **The conjecture.** [CLAIM-840](../claims.d/CLAIM-840.md) (the owner's, U5, empirical, Proposed): "the combination of criticality and powerlaws has me suspecting this pair often manifests as a bulk mass separated by a low mass long tail below the critical transition". It admits two readings, filed as alternatives; the owner chooses.
- **Reading (a), a gap in support.** [CLAIM-845](../claims.d/CLAIM-845.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): a continuous transition does not show this from below, where the size distribution falls without a break into an exponential cutoff. [CASE-305](../cases.d/CASE-305.md) (the assistant's simulations: percolation and a Reed–Frost epidemic) checks it.
- **Reading (b), a separation of scales.** [CLAIM-829](../claims.d/CLAIM-829.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): a continuous transition does show this from below. The bulk keeps a fixed small scale while the tail's cutoff grows without bound, and at the transition no size marks off the extraordinary. This is A7's closing line, "the scale separating ordinary from extraordinary behavior ceases to be fixed". Its "What 'closes' means" holds A10 §8's point, that the tail is "the bulk's own law continued". [ARG-136](../arguments.d/ARG-136.md) (Proposed) holds that A's branching example supports reading (b) and not (a).
- **Count against aggregate.** [CLAIM-832](../claims.d/CLAIM-832.md) (A's, A7 §3 and A8, demonstrated, Proposed): for τ < 2 small events dominate the count and rare large ones the total. [CASE-303](../cases.d/CASE-303.md) (A's worked Poisson cascade, checked) is the example.
- **The test A proposed.** [LEAD-211](../leads.d/LEAD-211.md) (A's, Open): track the cutoff, and its scaling with system size, rather than the exponent, and set it beside the variance and autocorrelation warnings.

### What survives a change of scale

- **Three properties.** [CLAIM-805](../claims.d/CLAIM-805.md) (A's, A13 §8 and A21 §8, interpretive, Proposed): entropy, correlation and scaling are distinct. A critical point is not the maximum of complexity; what is special there is the absence of a finite correlation scale. [TERM-141](../terms.d/TERM-141.md) (A's, Proposed) defines predictive information.
- **Many dimensions.** [QUESTION-517](../questions.d/QUESTION-517.md) (A's, A19's close and A21 §1, Open) asks whether the effective dimensions agree: stiff Fisher directions, predictive-state dimension, relevant renormalization directions and fractal dimension. [LEAD-208](../leads.d/LEAD-208.md) (the assistant's, Open) would compute them on the 2D Ising model. [CLAIM-825](../claims.d/CLAIM-825.md) (A's, A21 §5 and §7, demonstrated, Proposed): a model has no single practical dimension, since the count of resolved directions grows with the data. [CLAIM-849](../claims.d/CLAIM-849.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): the Fisher spectrum's shape fixes how predictive information grows.
- **Simplicity in response, not in mechanism.** [CLAIM-820](../claims.d/CLAIM-820.md) (A's, A19, interpretive, Proposed): parameter complexity and behavioural complexity come apart. [TERM-135](../terms.d/TERM-135.md) (A's, A19 §4, Proposed) names the hyperribbon. [THEORY-777](../theory.d/THEORY-777.md) (Proposed) is the record's worked case of compression of parameter space under decimation. [CLAIM-836](../claims.d/CLAIM-836.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): at criticality the Fisher information about a relevant coupling grows as L^(2y), so the few relevant directions become more distinguishable, not less.
- **Grouping by future consequence.** A17 §11's emergent variables, "the distinctions that matter for predicting its future", are the record's causal states. [THEORY-756](../theory.d/THEORY-756.md) (Active) holds the partition of a process's past that preserves prediction.
- **Levels and time scales.** [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md) (Proposed, interpretive) holds that rate differences help make structure. Its note of 2026-11-21 records A13 §9's spatial counterpart: levels become approximately autonomous when smaller-scale detail is irrelevant, and less so at criticality.
- **Computation near a transition.** [CLAIM-809](../claims.d/CLAIM-809.md) (A's, A15, interpretive, Proposed): nearness to a transition redistributes a bounded capacity rather than raising every ability. [CLAIM-814](../claims.d/CLAIM-814.md) (the assistant's, demonstrated, Proposed): in the scalar linear reservoir, the gain does not trade memory against noise. [ARG-148](../arguments.d/ARG-148.md) (Proposed) holds that the reservoir is an exact instance of the redistribution, but not by A's tradeoff. [QUESTION-507](../questions.d/QUESTION-507.md) (A's, Open) keeps the general question.

### Components filed after the coverage check

- [TERM-138](../terms.d/TERM-138.md) (the assistant's, Proposed): "separation" in three senses, of scales or levels, of classes, and spectral. In this part, sense 1 is the bulk-and-tail separation of scales and the separation of levels at criticality.
- [CLAIM-826](../claims.d/CLAIM-826.md) (A's, A17 §5, Proposed): critical systems are "harder to separate but easier to classify": fluctuations stay consequential across scales, so levels do not separate, while microscopic detail becomes irrelevant to the exponents. ## Not stated on the record

Links A's syntheses draw across these components that no entry states, tested at the audit and not filed as claims:

- **"The absence of a characteristic scale" as what unifies the mechanisms** (A5's close; A8 §8's "common mathematical result"). For a pure power law this is a definition, so as a unifier it is a label. It passes no route: a pure power law with a characteristic scale is a contradiction, and nothing is derived from it. A26 §9 withdraws it in A's own words: "The absence of a characteristic scale is only one way that a system can exhibit universal scaling". [CLAIM-795](../claims.d/CLAIM-795.md) keeps it as a gloss.
- **A relationship that survives a change of scale as "the most general conceptual thread connecting power laws, criticality, self-similarity, chaos, information, and emergence"** (A13 §9; again at A15 §10, A17 §10 and A19's close). As to chaos, it contradicts a component. [CLAIM-850](../claims.d/CLAIM-850.md) holds that chaos occurs without criticality or a cascade through scales, and A6 §5's logistic map at r = 4 is chaotic with no power law. The rest restates the definition of scale invariance.
- **"A critical system can be simple in the space of laws and complicated in the space of realizations"** (A21 §9–10, "the central insight connecting the ideas we've explored"). As a general sentence it holds of any parametric stochastic model, so it is trivial. Of criticality it is the conjunction of few relevant directions ([TERM-139](../terms.d/TERM-139.md), [THEORY-777](../theory.d/THEORY-777.md)) with correlations that are scale-free ([CLAIM-805](../claims.d/CLAIM-805.md)). It has no case in which both hold and it fails. "Complicated" is unmeasured, a free parameter.
- **A family resemblance of concentrations** (A19's close): resources under cumulative advantage, the aggregate in rare events, sensitivity at criticality, predictive importance in sloppy models. A disclaims it as a mechanism ("These shouldn't be mistaken for one universal mechanism"). It is a label over [CLAIM-817](../claims.d/CLAIM-817.md), [CLAIM-832](../claims.d/CLAIM-832.md), [CLAIM-836](../claims.d/CLAIM-836.md) and [CLAIM-820](../claims.d/CLAIM-820.md).
- **The trajectory distribution as the universal object** (A24 §10, "is the universal object a state or a process?"). No entry holds it. Its examples are textbook (equal stationary laws with different currents; path-space relative entropy and entropy production), and the exchange gives no case that turns on it.
