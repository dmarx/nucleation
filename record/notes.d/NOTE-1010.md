# NOTE-1010: Random Hyperbolic Graphs: Degree Sequence and Clustering

## Contribution

The first rigorous treatment of Krioukov et al.'s hyperbolic random graph.
Before it, the degree law and the clustering were shown by a large-distance
approximation and simulations ([LIT-865](../literature.d/LIT-865.md)); this paper proves both, for the
threshold model.

## Key results

- **Theorem 2.1.** E[c] = Θ(1) and c = (1 + o(1))E[c] with high
  probability, c the average of the local clustering coefficients
  (a vertex of degree below 2 counts as 0).
- **Theorem 2.2.** With high probability, for 0 ≤ k ≤ n^δ′ the fraction
  of vertices of degree exactly k is (1 + o(1)) times
  (2αe^(−αC)/k!) (2α/(π(α − 1/2)))^(2α) (Γ(k − 2α) − ∫₀^ξ t^(k−2α−1)e^(−t) dt);
  for larger k up to about n^(1/(2α)), the fraction of degree at least k is
  (1 + o(1)) (2α/(π(α − 1/2)))^(2α) e^(−αC) k^(−2α). Since
  Γ(k − 2α)/k! = Θ(k^(−2α−1)), the number of vertices of degree k is
  (1 + o(1)) c_{α,C} k^(−2α−1) n: a power law with exponent 2α + 1.
- **Theorems 2.3–2.4.** Average degree (1 + o(1)) 2α²e^(−C/2)/(π(α − 1/2)²);
  maximum degree n^(1/(2α)+o(1)).

## Method

The ball of radius R around a point at radius r subtends an angle
θ_r(y) = 2e^((R−r−y)/2)(1 + Θ(e^(R−r−y))) at radius y (Lemma 3.1), from
which the measure of the neighbourhood of a vertex at radius r is
(2α/(π(α − 1/2))) e^(−r/2)(1 ± O(e^(−(α−1/2)r) + e^(−r))) (Lemma 3.2): the
expected degree falls as e^(−r/2), the large-distance relation of [LIT-865](../literature.d/LIT-865.md)
now with its error bounded. Concentration comes from an Azuma–Hoeffding
inequality with a bad event (Theorem 4.1), applied only to vertices far
from the centre, whose degrees are at most c n^(1−β) except with
probability e^(−Ω(n^(1−β))) (Lemma 4.2). The clustering lower bound counts
only triangles among outer vertices.

## Limitations

- **Temperature zero only.** The soft model with a distance-dependent
  connection probability is mentioned and not treated, so nothing here
  bears on clustering as a function of temperature.
- **Curvature fixed at −1.** α here plays the role of α/ζ in [LIT-865](../literature.d/LIT-865.md).
- **A lower bound for clustering, not a limit.** Convergence to a constant
  was left open (later settled by other authors, not held).
