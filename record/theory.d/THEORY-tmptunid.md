# THEORY-tmptunid: In Jennings's hybrid account, attention is prioritization of processing by a subject that is the whole collection of an organism's fixed, formed and fluid interests, separated from filtering by whether selection answers to that collection, marked by feedback from dorsolateral prefrontal cortex; when interests are equally weighted, attention breaks the stalemate and updates the chosen option's value, so that a fluid interest becomes a formed one

## Source

Jennings (2026), [LIT-tmpasemk](../literature.d/LIT-tmpasemk.md), pp. 21–32 of the manuscript of 2026-10-29;
read in [NOTE-tmpby7i2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpby7i2.md).

## What was actually shown

Nothing new was measured. The paper assembles:

- **A definition.** "A subject is a collection of interests with the power
  to constrain those interests" (p. 21), and attention is "prioritization
  by the subject, or by this collection of interests, which is separable
  from local filtering and automatic selection" (p. 32).
- **A criterion.** "One way of determining this is through the presence or
  absence of feedback from dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC), since
  such feedback is essential for the expression of fluid interests" (p.
  32). The support is that prefrontal feedback is "widely considered to be
  necessary for interests that require ongoing maintenance" (p. 31), while
  reward history can drive selection without it (Awh, Belopolsky and
  Theeuwes 2012).
- **A mechanism**, from Voigt 2022 (LIT-tmphwtm4): with equally valued options, dlPFC and
  precuneus raise attention on one, "allowing for both a decision and an
  updating of values in the memory system" (p. 25).

Each could have been otherwise: selection between equal options could be
noise-driven with no later preference change, or prefrontal feedback could
accompany formed-interest selection as well.

## What this does not say

- **That the subject is non-reducible.** That is [THEORY-tmp6xjgq](THEORY-tmp6xjgq.md), a
  separate and weaker part of the paper.
- **That attention so defined is free will.** The paper's step from here to
  sourcehood is asserted ([NOTE-tmpby7i2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpby7i2.md), C6).
- **That the stalemate mechanism is established.** It is one model, cited.
- **That the subject is the agent's own.** A collection whose interests were
  installed meets the definition ([QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md)).
