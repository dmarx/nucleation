# CLAIM-tmp4o38w: An organized agent's will is its own to the degree that its operative commitments arise through, remain integrated with, and are answerable to its constitutive organization

## The claim

Built from [TERM-tmp5izuc](../terms.d/TERM-tmp5izuc.md) and [TERM-tmpaqil3](../terms.d/TERM-tmpaqil3.md), and defended against Velleman
with four ownership conditions and a manipulation test (A263).

## Why it is deferred

A274 found the ownership conditions insufficient: "A sophisticated
manipulator could engineer all four." The owner agreed at U77 and tried to
fork the conversation upstream of where authenticity entered; the fork did
not hold, which is why the retraction had to happen in-band. In the record it
is a status change on the members of the line, and [CLAIM-tmpuaqoi](CLAIM-tmpuaqoi.md) and
[CLAIM-tmps8d1r](CLAIM-tmps8d1r.md), which rest on this, are deferred with it.
