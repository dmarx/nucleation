### Added

- A periodic synthesis review, ADR-tmpw6bwa (Proposed), carried by the new `synthesis-review` skill. Its first stage is mechanical: `scratch/formal/synthesis_candidates.py` ranks CLAIMs that may be syntheses filed as claims, and integrations the record holds only as parts (`complements` clusters, open questions with several answers, deep `rests_on` chains), and decides nothing. A reviewer then shortlists, audits each candidate with ADR-075's checks and routes, and files only what is admitted. No synthesis is set Current.
- The first review, its demonstration. It audited twelve candidates:
  - CLAIM-545, A's freedom-of-will synthesis, is moved into the scheme as SYNTHESIS-tmpsw5k6 (admitted by hypothesis, four corrections marked), and SYNTHESIS-002 and SYNTHESIS-004 are repointed to it.
  - SYNTHESIS-tmpxwffr, in the assistant's voice, answers part of QUESTION-131 by derivation: a met-or-quiet complex, which is neither the requirement complex nor the quiescent complex, decides when structural frustration forces a member that is unmet and acting.
  - CLAIM-474 is admitted, but stays an Active CLAIM until the owner decides whether to move it.
  - Nine candidates were not admitted, each with its reason.

  The curation entry "Synthesis review of 2026-11-07" holds the ranking, the verdicts and the lines still without a synthesis.
