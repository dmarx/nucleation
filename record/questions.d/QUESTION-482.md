# QUESTION-482: Under what conditions is the allocation of authority to evaluate and constrain an actor sufficiently independent of that actor's capacity to influence the allocation?

## Why it is a question

A79 §3 of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b), after
relaying the record's recognition loops:

> **Under what conditions is the allocation of authority to evaluate and constrain an actor sufficiently independent of the actor's capacity to influence that allocation?**

"This question is different from whether the final evaluator is
personally impartial. It concerns the organizational provenance of the
evaluator's authority." A81 §5 gives examples: a tribunal "formally
distinct from the corporation whose conduct it evaluates while relying
almost entirely on information generated by that corporation", a
licensing body drawing its standards from "a narrow, self-reproducing
professional network", a compliance function that depends on management
"for staffing, promotion, evidence, or permission to investigate".

The record has the mechanisms and not the criterion:

- **[CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md)**: forbidding self-granting does not stop an operator from
  amplifying its own authority through reciprocal and cyclic grants.
- **[CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)**: meaning and influence coupled can destabilize symmetry
  exactly when βγ > (1 − α)(1 − δ). A81 §5 adds an exogenous evidence
  term e_t to the recognition equation; that does not change the
  instability condition, which concerns the homogeneous part.
- **[CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md)** (trust from repeated endorsement) and **[CLAIM-406](../claims.d/CLAIM-406.md)** (an
  early exclusion kept going by the indicators that only being sought
  produces; the owner's, from 2026-10-20). Both show that the same loop
  can track competence or entrench privilege, and cannot tell which.
- **[CLAIM-359](../claims.d/CLAIM-359.md)**: a self-governing organization's robustness to compromised
  constituents is a property of how it allocates, audits and revises
  authority.

Each says how dependence of the evaluator on the evaluated can arise. None
says how much is too much, or by which paths.

"Provenance" here is [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s formation and maintenance provenance,
applied to an evaluator's authority: whose process produced it and whose
keeps it.

## A's proposed method

A81 §5: "Define an *authority-provenance graph* whose nodes include
decision-makers, evidence sources, appointing bodies, and enforcement
agents. Its directed edges represent relations such as appointment,
funding, information supply, and revocation. Then ask whether the
challenged actor can influence the relevant corrective decision through
paths other than the merits of its case." A adds the caution: "The mere
presence of such a path does not prove capture."

## What would count as an answer

- **A criterion on paths.** Which paths from the evaluated actor to the
  evaluator's appointment, funding, evidence or removal defeat
  independence, and which are compatible with it (a regulated firm
  supplying data under audit, say), with the difference stated in terms of
  the graph: path type, weight, or whether a path passes through a party
  the actor cannot influence.
- **A link to the dynamics.** A condition, in [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s or [CLAIM-282](../claims.d/CLAIM-282.md)'s
  terms, under which a provenance path lets the evaluated actor's
  recognition feed back into the evaluator's authority, so that
  independence at one time decays.
- **A case pair.** Two review bodies with the same formal separation from
  the actor, one funded and informed through the actor and one not, that
  differ in what a challenge can achieve.

How independent the evaluator must be is the institutional side of
[CLAIM-727](../claims.d/CLAIM-727.md), that an actor's own judgment that it has met the claims
constraining it cannot always establish that it has.

Refines [QUESTION-485](QUESTION-485.md): it asks one condition on the legitimacy of an
authority to evaluate and constrain an actor, its independence from that
actor, which that question places on its first half, the justification
of authority.
