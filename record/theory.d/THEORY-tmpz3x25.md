# THEORY-tmpz3x25: Schepanski and colleagues (2022) show, in mice, that maternal microchimeric cells seeding the fetal brain maintain microglial homeostasis, restrain synaptic pruning, support prefrontal-hippocampal circuit maturation and early behavior, and that restoring them rescues these functions, so the cells are a functional mechanism of neurodevelopment rather than an inert by-product

## The account

Schepanski and colleagues (2022) show, in mice, that maternal microchimeric cells seeding the fetal brain maintain microglial homeostasis, restrain synaptic pruning, support prefrontal-hippocampal circuit maturation and early behavior, and that restoring them rescues these functions, so the cells are a functional mechanism of neurodevelopment rather than an inert by-product.
