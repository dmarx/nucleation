# QUESTION-513: Does a pragmatic frame take part in determining which meaning becomes actual, or does it only select among descriptions of a meaning fixed without it?

## Why it is a question

The record had two forms of the thesis:
- **[CLAIM-109](../claims.d/CLAIM-109.md).** The strong, general form: the frame "participates in determining which meaning becomes actual". It was reached by the quantum-cognition analogy ([ARG-002](../arguments.d/ARG-002.md), Rejected), and superseded.
- **[CLAIM-009](../claims.d/CLAIM-009.md).** A narrow, testable form: pragmatic judgements may be formally contextual, admitting no global extension once direct context effects are accounted for.

Between them lies the question itself, which neither the rejection nor the supersession answered.

## What would answer it

- **Yes, by [CLAIM-009](../claims.d/CLAIM-009.md).** If the narrow form holds, meaning is not fixed independently of the frame in the sense that no frame-free joint distribution of the judgements exists.
- **No.** A frame-free model of meanings, with frames acting only as selections or noisy readouts, that fits pragmatic judgements across frames once direct context effects are accounted for.
- **Other routes, not yet held.** Accounts on which interpretation is constitutive without contextuality, such as the parallel [CLAIM-412](../claims.d/CLAIM-412.md) in the domain of experience.

The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
