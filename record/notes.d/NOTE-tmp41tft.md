## Contribution

## Key results

- **The franchise.** Dennis Martinez's 1991 game was "the first perfect game in the history of the Expos/Nationals franchise"; Pete Rose's 1984 milestone came "over two decades before the Expos moved to Washington, D.C. to become the Nationals".
- **The Senators, on the Nationals' page.** Walter Johnson appears there as "star of the old Washington Senators (now the Minnesota Twins)".

## Assumptions

None of the formal kind. Statements by an interested party are taken as

## Concepts

Continuation of a club by franchise (legal and statistical succession)
against heritage by place (a city's baseball history).

## Bearing on the record

The game's museum counts the Nationals the Expos' franchise and the original Senators the Twins', while placing the Senators' greatest player on the Nationals' page: continuation by franchise through Montreal, Washington's baseball by place. Coded C-EX (statement 7) and D-SEN (statement 8).
