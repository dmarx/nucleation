# SYNTHESIS-tmp4fiqm: Remedies foreclosed before review, by faits accomplis, speed, friction and siege: acting before an opportunity to intervene can take effect is proposed as a wrong of its own, an irreversible act is proposed to convert a holder's consent into a claim to compensation and to give the breacher leverage over its own settlement but no power over what is owed, an opponent can put a remedy out of reach by raising what pursuing it costs, the cost of imposing a decision and of contesting it can move apart, and the baseline against which terms are judged can be the actor's own product

*A part account of the dependence-and-domination and distributed-agency lines, on remedies foreclosed before they can be reviewed: by acting first, by outlasting, by moving friction, and by setting the baseline. It was filed at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, after the filing of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (the curation entry of 2026-11-17, 20:57:46), and every component is new to any account. Most components complement or rest on [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) (Active, the owner's earlier observation that an intervention can make a corrective outcome costlier or unreachable before review while the standards stay the same). The account cites [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) and does not integrate it. The dependence-and-domination line's account is [SYNTHESIS-095](SYNTHESIS-095.md); this part is not yet among its parts. Its sibling, on answerability and contestability, is [SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa](SYNTHESIS-tmpsodpa.md).*

## The account

**The wrong of acting first.** [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) (the owner's, U27, with A59's
four conditions, three kinds of standing and the narrowing to one mechanism
of bullying; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md),
[CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md), [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) and [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md): where a party
has standing to authorize, suspend or contest an act, an actor who without
adequate justification makes its consequences irreversible before that
standing can be exercised defeats it, "even where the objection would have
failed". Its body says [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) "holds the timing" and "is causal and says
nothing about standing"; it keeps the single act apart from "the wrong of
constructing a relationship in which such acts can routinely prevail",
which it calls [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md)'s. It names the family "wrongful unilateral
action", with remedial hold-up ([CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md)) and counter-corrective
domination ([CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md)) among its kinds (A59 §9).

**The rule converted.** [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) (the connection the owner's, U26;
the form A's, A57, after Calabresi and Melamed, unread here; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md),
[CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md): an irreversible act against a
consent-protected entitlement leaves the holder in practice only a claim to
compensation, so the actor has changed the rule of allocation "from consent
to liability". Its body sets the pairs: it is "the rule changing" and
[CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) "the terms reached once it has"; [CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md) "names the
wrong in the order of moves; this one names what the wrong converts"; and
with [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) "the two agree".

**The leverage gained.** [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md) (A's, A55 §3 and §9, at the
owner's U25; thesis, interpretive, Proposed) complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md),
[CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-728](../claims.d/CLAIM-728.md), and bears on
[QUESTION-478](../questions.d/QUESTION-478.md): a breach can lower the injured party's position if it
refuses to settle, so a settlement it rationally accepts can leave the
breacher ahead, "the leverage coming from the violation". Its body says
[CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md) holds "the reachability half exactly" and this adds the
bargaining channel, and it quotes A55 §8 against inferring from B's choice
that "The circumstances making that settlement B's best available option
were legitimate."

**The power not gained.** [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) (A's, A85 §5, A87 §2 and §10, A90
V and VII, A97; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; lines
organizational-normativity and distributed-agency) rests on [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md),
complements [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md), [CLAIM-720](../claims.d/CLAIM-720.md) and
[CLAIM-743](../claims.d/CLAIM-743.md), and uses [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): making a remedy unreachable gains a
wrongdoer no normative power over what is owed; the claim persists while
its remedy's correctability falls to zero, and a settlement reached under
the conditions the violation created does not authorize the violation. Its
body calls its third part "the normative complement" of [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), the
conversion of [CLAIM-734](../claims.d/CLAIM-734.md) "practical, not normative", and A87 §10's
"Litigation by attrition" the owner's siege, [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md).
[QUESTION-475](../questions.d/QUESTION-475.md) (A's, A68 §4, from Fiss as the record read him; Open;
line distributed-agency) asks whether the capacity to make remedies costly
can bend, over time, the standard they are measured against. Its body says
that if it can, [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md)'s premise holds at a time and can fail over time,
and [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) "would have a route by which it gains that power after
all"; it names Levinson ([LIT-1592](../literature.d/LIT-1592.md), Unfetched) as the reading to do first.
It has no front-matter relations.

**Siege.** [CLAIM-748](../claims.d/CLAIM-748.md) (the owner's, U28; thesis, demonstrated,
Proposed) grounds in [CASE-287](../cases.d/CASE-287.md), bears on [QUESTION-479](../questions.d/QUESTION-479.md), uses
[TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md) and [TERM-105](../terms.d/TERM-105.md), and complements [CLAIM-464](../claims.d/CLAIM-464.md), [CLAIM-619](../claims.d/CLAIM-619.md), [CLAIM-601](../claims.d/CLAIM-601.md),
[CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md), [CLAIM-716](../claims.d/CLAIM-716.md), [CLAIM-722](../claims.d/CLAIM-722.md) and [CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md): an actor
can make a legally recognized corrective objective unreachable for another,
with the merits, the law and that party's preferences unchanged, by raising
what pursuing it costs, in spending or in time, above what that party can
sustain. Its body records that A64 told the point as its own "key
insight"; sets "Temporal preemption" ([CLAIM-723](../claims.d/CLAIM-723.md)) and "Attritional

split of adjudicative from attritional friction "related" to
[CLAIM-731](../claims.d/CLAIM-731.md). [CASE-287](../cases.d/CASE-287.md) (A's constructions, A61 and A64, every number
rechecked and five of A's statements corrected by the filer; construction,

game, the exponential race and the threshold. Its correction of A64 §9's

**The measure.** [CLAIM-724](../claims.d/CLAIM-724.md) (A's, A76 VIII.4 and A81 §3; thesis,

**The baseline.** [CLAIM-747](../claims.d/CLAIM-747.md) (A's, A102 §6 and A106 §9, at the owner's
