# CLAIM-tmpqlt2o: The neural signature Jennings offers for an emergent subject's constraint on its parts, 1/f scaling and phase-amplitude coupling tied to attention, does not discriminate a macro-level constraint from micro-level mechanism: 1/f structure is present in every field recording, a coupling measure can be met by one non-sinusoidal rhythm, and the attention coupling it cites is a correlation with no control of waveform shape

## The claim

Jennings's case that an emergent subject constrains its parts rests, on
the neural side, on two signatures ([NOTE-1508](../notes.d/NOTE-1508.md), pp. 27–29): 1/f scaling,
and phase-amplitude coupling (PAC) in which "low frequency waves
constrain high frequency waves", tied to attention by Szczepanski et
al. 2014. Neither discriminates a macro-level constraint from a
mechanism that is wholly micro-level:

- **1/f structure is background.** Aperiodic 1/f-like activity is "a
  ubiquitous and sometimes dominant feature of neural field data"
  ([THEORY-1337](../theory.d/THEORY-1337.md)). Since every field recording shows it, its presence
  cannot favour one account of the subject over another; that reading
  draws exactly this consequence for [THEORY-1314](../theory.d/THEORY-1314.md).
- **A coupling measure can be met by one rhythm.** A single
  non-sinusoidal rhythm yields significant PAC, because its harmonics
  carry amplitude that depends on its own phase ([THEORY-1312](../theory.d/THEORY-1312.md)). A
  measured coupling is evidence of one rhythm modulating another only
  after waveform shape is controlled.
- **The attention coupling is a correlation.** In Szczepanski et al.,
  2–5 Hz phase is coupled to 80–250 Hz amplitude during held covert
  attention, more for the attended field, and coupling strength
  correlates with faster detection at 28 of 123 coupled electrodes;
  nothing was manipulated, and no shape control is reported
  ([THEORY-1317](../theory.d/THEORY-1317.md)). Its broadband amplitude lies far above the slow
  rhythm's harmonics, the less suspect kind by [THEORY-1312](../theory.d/THEORY-1312.md)'s own
  diagnostic, so the coupling is probably real. Its mechanism, as the
  paper gives it, is excitability modulation through membrane
  potentials, entirely micro-level.

So the signature is compatible with Jennings's account and with any
account on which all causal work is local. What would discriminate is
an intervention on the slow rhythm with local inputs held fixed, which
[CLAIM-334](CLAIM-334.md) says is not available under supervenience and which
contextual emergence, by denying supervenience, owes.

## What it does not say

- That PAC or 1/f activity is meaningless. The aperiodic exponent is
  "itself a physiologically informative feature" ([THEORY-1337](../theory.d/THEORY-1337.md)), and
  genuine nesting of rhythms is possible ([THEORY-1312](../theory.d/THEORY-1312.md)).
- That Jennings's account is false. The claim is about what this
  evidence can show; her account may have other support, and the
  boundary argument ([THEORY-1314](../theory.d/THEORY-1314.md)) is untouched here.
- That the coupling in Szczepanski et al. is an artefact. It is
  uncontrolled for shape, not shown to be shape.
