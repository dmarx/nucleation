# THEORY-tmp60tmj: Normalized stem length is not a clock of gene acquisition time: it is set mainly by factors other than when a gene was acquired, such as adaptation to the new cell after acquisition, so relative timings of eukaryogenesis drawn from stem lengths alone are not supported

## Source

Tobiasson, Luo, Wolf & Koonin (2026), LIT-1558, read in full in NOTE-1242. The authors conclude that "even normalized stem lengths are determined primarily by factors other than the gene acquisition time", and suggest post-acquisition adaptation as one such factor.

## Where the evidence stands

What the paper shows, and what it does not, is THEORY-1101, Active, which this entry extends: that entry is the reading, this one the thesis.
