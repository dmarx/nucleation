# LIT-2717: An Updated and Revised Dyadic Model of Partner Violence

Bahare Mazinani and Brian P. O’Connor (2026). An Updated and Revised Dyadic Model of Partner Violence. *Behavioral Sciences* 16(9): 1468. DOI 10.3390/bs16091468. Preprint DOI 10.20944/preprints202607.0312.v1.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading of 2026-11-17; the reading of 2026-11-16 is below.*

- Eight meta-analyses; the strongest, most consistent markers at the
  relationship-context level (Abstract).
- "bidirectional violence refers only to relationships in which both
  partners were reported to have used violent acts. The term does not imply
  equivalence in motive, coercive control, severity, consequences, or
  responsibility" (§2.3).
- "Effect sizes based primarily on the occurrence of physical acts may not
  capture motive, coercive control, fear, chronicity, severity, injury,
  sexual violence, or constraints on safety and leaving" (§7).
- "the present synthesis does not constitute empirical validation of the
  model’s integrated temporal or causal pathways" (§7).

## Reading of 2026-11-16

Read from the Europe PMC full text. A selective, unregistered review: eight meta-analyses, physical IPV, adult heterosexual couples, risk markers mapped by one author onto three levels of the dyadic model. The relationship context holds the most consistent associations; "the present synthesis does not constitute empirical validation of the model’s integrated temporal or causal pathways". The authors say: "This relational framing does not imply equivalent agency, mutuality, or shared responsibility; responsibility for a violent or coercive act lies with the person who commits it". They also say "a victim’s resistance or constrained responses should not be treated as equivalent to the perpetrator’s controlling violence". A5 §6's summary is accurate. A's caution, that a systemic perspective can obscure asymmetry, is one the paper itself states and guards against. The paper corroborates [CLAIM-709](../claims.d/CLAIM-709.md), as framing rather than finding. See [NOTE-2000](../notes.d/NOTE-2000.md).
