# TERM-104: motivating perspective: an organizational perspective whose interpretations give circumstances a significance relative to concerns it maintains, and whose significance conditions action readiness, a claim to control that the will-organization may or may not grant; motivating as a disposition, whether or not it is activated, granted or operative

## Definition

A **motivating perspective** is a perspective in [TERM-072](TERM-072.md)'s sense 1
(organizational) that has two further features.

1. **Concern-relative significance.** Its interpretations rank
   circumstances by how they bear on concerns it maintains. That is
   [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md)'s salience relative to a process, and Frijda's concern
   pertinence ([THEORY-055](../theory.d/THEORY-055.md)) read as one perspective's.
2. **Motivational coupling.** That significance conditions action
   readiness ([TERM-094](TERM-094.md)). The readiness claims control precedence. Whether
   the claim is granted is a further fact ([CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md), [CLAIM-442](../claims.d/CLAIM-442.md)).

A28's wording: "An organizational perspective whose context-dependent
interpretations systematically shape dispositions toward action or
intervention, by making possibilities, circumstances, or outcomes
motivationally salient relative to concerns maintained within that
perspective."

**The test.** A28's counterfactual criterion: "Holding relevant
opportunities and constraints fixed, changing how a circumstance is
interpreted within the perspective changes the disposition to mobilize or
redirect some available response." A28 adds that the change "need not
alter actual behavior", and that the counterfactual "isn't guaranteed to be
experimentally isolatable".

**A28's example.** Two agents meet an obstruction. Both show arousal and an
urge to strike. One reads a threat to safety, the other an affront. "If
Agent A's action-readiness changes when the obstruction is shown to be
harmless, while Agent B's changes when the obstruction is shown to be
unintentional, we've found different counterfactual signatures of
practical significance." Intervening next on their authority relations
separates the perspective from the will-organization ([TERM-023](TERM-023.md)) that sets
its efficacy.

**Three states kept apart.** A perspective can be motivating while it is:

- **dormant.** A28: "Its motivation can persist as a disposition without
  becoming activated." That is [CLAIM-421](../claims.d/CLAIM-421.md)'s distinction, and [CLAIM-287](../claims.d/CLAIM-287.md)'s
  persistence as a capacity to reinstate.
- **activated but not granted.** A28: "its activation can remain
  ineffective without ceasing to be motivating." That is [CLAIM-283](../claims.d/CLAIM-283.md)'s claim
  without grant, and [CLAIM-459](../claims.d/CLAIM-459.md)'s input below threshold.
- **granted.** It then governs, and is operative in [CLAIM-430](../claims.d/CLAIM-430.md)'s sense.

## What it is not

- **Not an activated drive.** A28: "A fixed reflex may recruit action
  without exhibiting the organized, revisable interpretation required by
  [TERM-072](TERM-072.md)."
- **Not an agent or subagent.** A motivating perspective may have "no
  self-model, explicit goal representation, or independent decision-making
  capacity" (A28). That is [CLAIM-418](../claims.d/CLAIM-418.md).
- **Not necessarily the agent's own.** A socially transmitted perspective
  can move an agent without being part of its practical organization.
  That is [QUESTION-260](../questions.d/QUESTION-260.md).
- **Not a ranking alone.** A28: "A preference ranking is not necessarily a
  motivational process." A ranking with no effect on readiness is an
  evaluation.
- **Not a mode count.** The number of perspectives is not the number of
  activation modes measured. That is [CLAIM-454](../claims.d/CLAIM-454.md).
- **Not committed to a separate governing layer.** A28 writes governance
  as a map G apart from the perspectives. [THEORY-680](../theory.d/THEORY-680.md) treats regulation as
  distinct from generation. [THEORY-056](../theory.d/THEORY-056.md) holds instead that it is "one motive
  state checking another, with no distinct regulating system above". This
  term takes no side.
- **Not every perspective, nor every motivation.** A28: "This does not
  assert that every motivation is perspectival, that every perspective is
  motivating, or that every motivating perspective belongs to an agent."

## A word to watch: "effective will"

A28 and A29 write w for the "effective will", a policy or an allocation of
authority. [TERM-023](TERM-023.md) says a will-organization is "Not the operative will,
which is whichever regime currently acts." A's w is nearer the state of the
will-organization than the operative will. A claim using this term should
say which it means.

## What it leaves open

- **Individuation.** A28: "What makes two different motivational responses
  enactments of the same motivating perspective, rather than distinct
  motivations that merely share a cue or a goal?" [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md) and [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md)
  say any answer is relative to a stated criterion. [QUESTION-276](../questions.d/QUESTION-276.md)'s note of
  2026-11-04 carries the question.
- **Whether it earns its place.** A28: "its value depends on whether it
  earns a distinction that “motivational process” and “organizational
  perspective” separately cannot capture." The term would be shown idle if
  every use of it could be rewritten as [CLAIM-437](../claims.d/CLAIM-437.md) plus [TERM-094](TERM-094.md) with
  nothing lost.
