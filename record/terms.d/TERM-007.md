# TERM-007: fidelity, as preserving the dynamics of interpretation

## Definition

A30: "pragmatic equivalence between translations may need to preserve not just
distributions of final judgments but the *dynamics of interpretation*", a
"commuting diagram for pragmatic fidelity": T_τ(f) Φ ≈ Φ T_f, where Φ maps
source interpretive states to target ones. "*interpretive operations may fail to
commute with each other, while a good translation should preserve the structure
of those operations across contexts.*"

## What it is not

Not a replacement for [TERM-021](TERM-021.md): A30 calls it "a considerably richer notion of
fidelity", a further requirement. It uses the operator sense of frame ([TERM-026](TERM-026.md)).
