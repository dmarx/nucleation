## What was filed

- A NOTE (Read) and a Proposed THEORY for each: NOTE-tmpwmsxg and
  THEORY-tmps2gur; NOTE-tmp39hi8 and THEORY-tmpj5upc; NOTE-tmp074zx and
  THEORY-tmpkjzqg (corroborates CLAIM-350 and CLAIM-152); NOTE-tmpr453b
  and THEORY-tmppm9la (corroborates CLAIM-754 and CLAIM-675). LITs
  Active at version 2.
- **The Reddit case corrected.** CASE-tmp6idy5's account of the two
  Reddit works was A82's; read, they are correlational. Hessel and
  colleagues measure short-term individual activity, not fragmentation,
  and the sign reverses for some kinds of spinoff; Krohn and Weninger
  disclaim causation and run no natural experiment. Corrected in place
  (the case is unmerged).
- **The owner's point at U37.** That the pretraining literature ought to
  show acquired organization making later learning cheaper is borne out
  only weakly by these two: Anguita and colleagues give an analytical
  instance in sample cost; McDermott and colleagues measure final
  performance. Noted on CLAIM-756, with what a reading that would bear it
  out needs to measure.
- **Dated notes on twenty-nine entries**, each saying which kind of
  bearing it is, among them CLAIM-754 and CLAIM-675 (same present
  capability, different later learning), CLAIM-758 (neither work bears on
  its time course; a bound form of its overcompression mechanism),
  CASE-294 (design points), THEORY-tmp5zxkk (nesting is not descent), and
  CLAIM-762 (a name-first route to a unit).
