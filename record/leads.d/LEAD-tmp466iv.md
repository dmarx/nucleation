# LEAD-tmp466iv: Test whether an institution's answerability to those it affects survives perturbations that make it unprofitable or unobserved, such as credible alternatives appearing, scrutiny ending, a hidden harm coming to light, costly internal objection and leadership turnover

## What it would do

A46 §13: "Rather than attempt to construct a sociopathy score—which would
risk reproducing the very pathology under investigation—we could
characterize institutions through response profiles." Its perturbations:

| Perturbation | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|
| Profitability and justified claims conflict | Which consideration acquires authority? |
| External scrutiny disappears | Does corrective activity persist? |
| Users acquire credible alternatives | Do the institution's contractual terms improve? |
| Previously hidden harm becomes visible | Is discovery supported or discouraged? |
| Employees raise costly objections | Are they empowered, ignored or penalized? |
| A KPI becomes inadequate | Can its jurisdiction be challenged? |
| A beneficiary becomes unable to reciprocate | Does the institution retain appropriate commitments? |
| Leadership changes | Which normative dispositions survive turnover? |

"Such tests would still require independent grounds for deciding which
claims are justified. Responsiveness to every asserted demand would not
establish moral competence."

## What is held

[LEAD-101](LEAD-101.md) and [CASE-405](../cases.d/CASE-405.md) hold the second row, for sanctions. The others
are open.

## A first step (the assistant)

The third row has natural experiments: rules that lower switching costs by
order (number portability, data portability or interoperability mandates).
Whether the incumbent's terms improved after them, against comparable
services the rule did not reach, reads answerability to users from conduct
once leaving became credible. [ARG-tmp4er8c](../arguments.d/ARG-tmp4er8c.md)'s condition applies: improvement
predicted by competition alone shows prudence, so the informative arm is a
failure to improve where competition would have rewarded it, or
persistence of better terms after the competitive pressure fades.
