# LEAD-001: Construct cases that separate reliable performance from non-accidental epistemic competence in a learning system

## What it would do

A short set of cases, each with what it separates (stored information,
belief, correct output, knowledge, understanding), checked against the
candidate answers the question already lists.

## Note of 2026-11-23: the case set filed

The assistant carried this lead out on 2026-11-23. Nine cases, each
fictional or a construction and Proposed, with what each separates:

- [CASE-408](../cases.d/CASE-408.md): a fact a probe decodes and the model does not use;
  stored, not believed.
- [CASE-400](../cases.d/CASE-400.md): right once in two hundred samples; correct output, not
  reliable.
- [CASE-416](../cases.d/CASE-416.md): a classifier reliable on its family by reading a
  watermark; reliable output, not knowledge.
- [CASE-399](../cases.d/CASE-399.md): a stale source made true again by a reappointment; true,
  reliably sourced belief, not knowledge.
- [CASE-413](../cases.d/CASE-413.md): a fact the model uses under one cue and not under its
  default; knowledge not accessible under the default.
- [CASE-409](../cases.d/CASE-409.md): A73's table and rule for x²; knowledge without
  understanding.
- [CASE-394](../cases.d/CASE-394.md): the rule with a noisy multiplier; understanding without
  reliably correct output.
- [CASE-414](../cases.d/CASE-414.md): a generator and an exact verifier; knowledge in the
  composite and in neither part.
- [CASE-403](../cases.d/CASE-403.md): a frozen exact rule, on which the readings of
  understanding part.

Verdicts, Proposed: [CLAIM-1142](../claims.d/CLAIM-1142.md) (the separation table, demonstrated by
construction), [CLAIM-1100](../claims.d/CLAIM-1100.md) (knowing is relative to the declared
procedures and budget), [CLAIM-1109](../claims.d/CLAIM-1109.md) (knowledge in a generator-verifier
composite). Two pairs of alternatives for the owner to choose between:
on grounding, [CLAIM-1086](../claims.d/CLAIM-1086.md) (lean: grounding is reliability over a
family closed under interventions on the truthmaker) against
[CLAIM-1028](../claims.d/CLAIM-1028.md) (grounding a further condition); on understanding,
[CLAIM-1092](../claims.d/CLAIM-1092.md) (lean: stable tracking, so a frozen rule understands)
against [CLAIM-1059](../claims.d/CLAIM-1059.md) (developmental value, so it does not).

Every case was checked against the candidate answers [QUESTION-493](../questions.d/QUESTION-493.md) lists.
They hold on every case but one: reliability over a family as declared
does not suffice (the watermark and stale-source cases), which A73's
grounding criterion and its second and third tests catch. What remains:
the owner's choice between the alternatives, and any empirical version
of the cases (none was run). Stage and status unchanged.
