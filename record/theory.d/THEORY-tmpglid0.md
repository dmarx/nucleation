## The account

de Kleer and Williams (1989) extend their General Diagnostic Engine into Sherlock, in which each component has good, known-fault and unknown behavioural modes with prior probabilities, diagnoses are the mode assignments containing no minimal conflict, and the next measurement is chosen to minimize expected entropy over the distinctions the diagnostic objective requires, so that an unforeseen fault is localized as a component in its unknown mode.
