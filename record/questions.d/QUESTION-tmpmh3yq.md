# QUESTION-tmpmh3yq: Do the semantic rate–distortion frameworks beyond Zhao et al. and Chai et al., Liu, Zhang and Poor's among them, also treat meaning as a latent variable with a known joint law with the observation, or does any limit depend on a property of meaning beyond that law?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-161](../theory.d/THEORY-161.md) now holds for Zhao et al. and Chai et al.: in each, a source
emits pairs (S, X) with a joint law known to the designer, and the
theorems are indirect source-coding results that use nothing about S
beyond that law and the distortion. The record knows Liu, Zhang and
Poor's framework (ISIT 2021; IEEE Trans. Commun. 2022) only through Xin,
Fan and Letaief's survey, and has read no framework by other groups. So
whether the field as a whole has this form is unsettled.

## What would answer it

A reading of Liu, Zhang and Poor's papers, and of one further semantic
rate–distortion framework by other authors, each found to treat meaning as
a latent variable with a known joint law with the observation, coded
through the observation, would answer it yes. A framework whose limit
changes when S is given logical, compositional or contextual structure,
with the joint law and the distortion held fixed, would answer it no.
Agreement among papers by the same groups does not count.
