**Finding.** No work runs the lead's full design. Its three parts are
covered separately, and [LEAD-006](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-006.md)'s note maps the coverage:
- (a) later cost: Zhang, Goudar, Ito, Driscoll and Furuta;
- (b) organizations compared: He et al. only, not matched;
- (c) intervention: Zhang by transplant, Goudar by readout, and Maennel
  by covariance resampling.

Two readings cut against the lead's hope:
- Thomm et al.: mixing sub-tasks in gives little.
- Furuta et al.: transplanting g's own grokked module often raises the
  cost of h∘g.

Maennel et al. show that a memorization arm needs input-statistics
controls. The lead stays at stage idea: the combined experiment is not
in the literature found.
