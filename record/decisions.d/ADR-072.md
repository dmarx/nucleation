# ADR-072: The second-time rule governs the record's process, not its insights: a decision or principle gets written down when its occasion recurs, while an idea a source surfaces is filed when it has content of its own and the record does not already hold it

## Context

[ADR-032](ADR-032.md), under Consequences, made the rule the record's own: "a thing gets
a code the second time it turns up. The drift the exchange shows is exactly
the set of things that recurred. When an agent does the filing, the risk is
no longer that capture stops but that it floods, and this rule is the
throttle." CLAUDE.md and step 9 of the `read-and-file` skill repeat it.

The rule comes from process. Luria's working agreement treats a hazard that
catches you twice as a bug report about the workflow (Luria's ADR-104), and
its design-principle template writes a principle from "the specific thing
that happened twice". For a decision or a principle that is the right bar:
one occurrence may be noise, and a rule made from one case binds every
later case.

For insights it is the wrong bar. On 2026-11-04, filing the pasted
bibliography on emergency readiness (dmarx/nucleation#401), the filer
briefed an agent to file only ideas that "turn up at least twice across the
readings". The owner: "this should reference a particular ADR, which
explains that this is specifically for the luria meta record, i.e. ADRs and
DPs (decisions and design principles) that govern our process for engaging
with the project. we don't need to be so strict against insights."

## Decision

- **Process documents.** An ADR or a DP is written when its occasion
  recurs: the second time a hazard, a judgement or a convention comes up.
  That is the second-time rule, and it governs these documents only.
- **Insights.** A TERM, CASE, QUESTION, CLAIM or ARG drawn from a source is
  filed when it has content of its own and the record does not already
  hold it. That holds even if it turns up only once. Searching the record
  first ([ADR-068](ADR-068.md)) is what prevents duplicates. It is not recurrence.
- **Passing mentions** with no content of their own stay in the reading's
  NOTE. The curation entry says which were left and why, so nothing is
  dropped silently ([ADR-068](ADR-068.md)).
- [ADR-032](ADR-032.md)'s filing rule is narrowed accordingly; the rest of [ADR-032](ADR-032.md)
  stands. CLAUDE.md's map line and the skill's step 9 now point here.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the rule for insights.** It throttled flooding, but at the cost
  of ideas that are worth holding and that one reading surfaces, which is
  most of what a single source offers.
- **Drop the throttle altogether.** The flooding risk [ADR-032](ADR-032.md) named is
  real. The check against the record and the bar of content of its own
  are the throttle now.
- **A different count.** No count separates an idea worth filing from a
  passing remark; content does.

## Consequences

- The PR that prompted this files the readings' single-source ideas as
  entries. Larson's units of last resort and the metric-as-proxy point
  from McLay and Mayorga are examples.
- A filer still writes ADRs and DPs only on the second occurrence of
  their occasion.
