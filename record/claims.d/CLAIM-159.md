# CLAIM-159: The record's rival accounts of will are claims about drive profiles in different cells: Ainslie's interests are aggregate or emergent, the expected value of control is top-down, and will-organization is the maintained organization that governs how the drive profile changes

## The claim

Read through [TERM-051](../terms.d/TERM-051.md), with the person as parts (interests or
regimes) and a higher level (resolve, control, the will):

- **[THEORY-067](../theory.d/THEORY-067.md) (Ainslie).** "Interests ... are not options chosen by an
  overarching ego", and the person is "just the resultant". That is the
  aggregate kind. If resolve, the recursive staking of future choices, is
  a property only the bargaining process has, it is the emergent kind,
  with decoupling. The précis does not say which.
- **[THEORY-070](../theory.d/THEORY-070.md) (expected value of control).** A dedicated system computes
  how much control to exert and specifies it to implementing structures.
  That is the top-down kind, with a designated driver.
- **[CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md) and [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) (will-organization).** "The maintained
  organization of relations governing the conditions under which an
  agent's available action-generating regimes acquire, retain, lose, or
  alter practical efficacy." These are the conditions under which the
  driver changes. Will-organization is a claim about the system's
  *drive history*: what governs which regime drives, and when. That is the
  owner's point that "who drives" is not constant ([CLAIM-316](CLAIM-316.md)), given
  a home in the will line.

If this holds, part of the dispute among the three is a dispute about
which cell an agent resisting temptation occupies. That is measurable in
principle, with the decomposition over windows around a temptation.

## What it does not say

- **That the three exclude each other.** A person could be aggregate over
  some choices and top-down over others, which is the time-varying point
  again.
- **That the placement is the authors'.** None of them uses these terms.

## Note of 2026-10-27: a reply to [CLAIM-024](CLAIM-024.md)

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md)) added `objects_to: CLAIM-024`.
[CLAIM-024](CLAIM-024.md) says that control and institutional theory already study every
mechanism the account names. This claim's placement is a partial reply on
two of its four fronts. The expected value of control is top-down and
Ainslie's interests are aggregate or emergent, and will-organization is a
claim about what governs how the profile changes. Planning theory and
institutional theory are not placed.
