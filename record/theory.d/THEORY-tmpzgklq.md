## The account

Muñoz and colleagues (2020) report that researchers running adaptive deep brain stimulation trials most often raise data privacy, safety and consent, and that over half raise patient autonomy and control because stimulation changes automatically and may go unnoticed, while some hold that a patient override or clinician-managed settings largely meet the concern.
