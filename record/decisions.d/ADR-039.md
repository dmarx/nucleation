# ADR-039: Two lines of inquiry, evidence-for-experience and learned-structure, so that readings no existing line can hold get questions of their own

## Context

A line names a strand of the record's own argument, so that it can be set
aside as a whole ([ADR-032](ADR-032.md)). The vocabulary is closed, which means a line is
one somebody named and blurbed. In the third synthesis pass (workbench of
2026-10-12), 102 Active or Proposed THEORYs still had no edge to any CLAIM or
QUESTION. Most of them sat in two clusters that no line asked about:

- About eighteen readings in consciousness science and neuroscience: report
  confounds, deficit arguments, crows reporting without laminar cortex,
  overflow, and the case of AI. They ask what could show that a system has
  experience. The nearest line, `organizational-affect`, is set aside, and it
  is about a functional characterization ([CLAIM-039](../claims.d/CLAIM-039.md)) that explicitly brackets
  experience.
- About twenty-five readings in learning dynamics, loss landscapes and
  representation learning. They ask why trained networks have the structure
  they have. `pragmatic-transport` uses a few of them for identifiability
  ([CLAIM-193](../claims.d/CLAIM-193.md), [CLAIM-187](../claims.d/CLAIM-187.md)). It is about what survives translation, not
  about training.

The assistant had at first treated the closed vocabulary as a reason not to
propose lines. The owner pointed out that closedness only requires a blurb.

## Decision

Two lines are added to `luria.yaml`:

- **`evidence-for-experience`**: what could show that a system, organized,
  non-biological or without a cortex, has experience and not only access or
  function, and what each kind of evidence (report, deficit, architecture,
  behaviour) can and cannot establish.
- **`learned-structure`**: why trained networks come to have the structure
  they have, which features, symmetries and connected solution sets training
  reaches and why. The line covers understanding, not instruction for
  practice, which belongs to the anthology.

Their first members are the questions filed to place the unlinked THEORYs.

## Alternatives considered

- **Force the clusters onto existing lines.** Rejected. Questions on
  experience filed under `organizational-affect` would contradict that line's
  bracketing of experience. Questions on training dynamics filed under
  `pragmatic-transport` would stretch "what survives translation" until it
  named nothing.
- **Leave the readings unplaced.** This was the assistant's first
  recommendation for some of them. It costs nothing to file. But a reading no
  question asks for is one no later session will find by following the
  argument.
- **One broader line for both clusters.** Rejected. The clusters share no
  question. A line that holds both could be set aside only as a block of
  unrelated material.

## Consequences

- The record now has eight lines. Neither new line has a claim yet. Their
  questions are open, and the readings answer them in part.
- `learned-structure` sits near the anthology's subject matter. A question
  or claim on it that would tell a practitioner what to do belongs in the
  anthology instead, by the boundary rule in CLAUDE.md.
- A cluster that fits no line is now a prompt to propose one, with a blurb,
  for the owner to approve. It is not a reason to force a fit.
