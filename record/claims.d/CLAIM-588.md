# CLAIM-588: Relieving an incompatibility, redistributing an unavoidable mismatch and exploiting a mismatch dynamically are three design targets, which change respectively the compatibility complex, where residuals or loads concentrate, and stability, barriers or transition paths, and the last two can be achieved without the first

## The claim

A, Part 3, as a table: "Relieve incompatibility: Change the feasible set or
compatibility complex; Redistribute unavoidable mismatch: Change where
constraint residuals or loads are concentrated; Exploit mismatch
dynamically: Alter stability, energy barriers, or transition paths." And:
"The last two could occur without achieving the first."

**Relief.** The record has it exactly: relief is strict enlargement of the
compatibility complex for the same declared demands, and dropping or
suppressing a demand is not relief ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md)).

**Redistribution without relief.** Demonstrated in [CLAIM-538](CLAIM-538.md). On a graph of
contexts with offsets and tolerances, the feasible allocations depend on
the offsets only through their class in H¹, so local adjustment can move a
mismatch among the overlaps of a cycle and never remove it. Rocks and Mehta
([NOTE-1557](../notes.d/NOTE-1557.md); [THEORY-1414](../theory.d/THEORY-1414.md)) give a mechanical parallel, weaker than a witness.
Their prestress sits on states of self-stress that the geometry fixes. Placed
on different bonds of that same space, it changes the response while the zero
modes and the self-stresses stay as they were: the force that couples to a
mode "no longer points in the same direction, but instead localizes to the
line of prestressed bonds". What moves is that force; the load is where the
designer put it, and their examples add a mismatch rather than move a given
one ([ARG-054](../arguments.d/ARG-054.md)).

**Exploitation without full relief.** Zang et al. ([NOTE-1569](../notes.d/NOTE-1569.md);
[THEORY-1417](../theory.d/THEORY-1417.md)): prestress in Kresling cells induces frustration that
"allows changing the energy barrier", and "by controlling the level of
prestress in the negative mode, we can switch the instability behavior
from bistable to monostable". It also gives a "programmable
(frustration-dependent) noncommutative state transition". On the record's
reading of the 56 ordered transitions as demands, prestress relieves too:
it opens 8 blocked paths ([CLAIM-347](CLAIM-347.md), note of 2026-11-05). The 16 paths
that need intermediate states stay unachievable while the stability and
the order of transitions are retuned. So exploitation went further than
relief. Rocks and Mehta add a cleaner instance on the mechanical side: in
their examples prestress stabilizes the network while "the numbers of LZMs
and LZEFs do not change" ([NOTE-1557](../notes.d/NOTE-1557.md)), so stability is retuned with the count
of freedoms and of self-stresses unchanged.

**What it adds to [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md).** The term is ordinal: frustration is an empty
intersection of acceptance sets, and it is "Not Watson's total
frustration", an energy. On that reading only relief acts on frustration
itself. Redistribution acts on where its residual or load is carried
([CLAIM-365](CLAIM-365.md)'s residual is the record's quantity). Exploitation acts on what
the load does to dynamics. A design that does either has not made the
demands jointly realizable, and should not be reported as if it had.

## What it does not say

- **That the targets exclude one another.** One intervention can do two or
  three; Zang et al.'s prestress does two.
- **That redistributing or exploiting a mismatch is good.** A: "There is no
  general reason to assume such tensions are beneficial." Whether it can
  be is [QUESTION-387](../questions.d/QUESTION-387.md).
- **That mechanical prestress is organizational frustration.** The reading
  of Zang et al. calls their frustration energetic and applied from
  outside, which [TERM-060](../terms.d/TERM-060.md) is not. The table is a classification of
  design targets that carries across the two senses, not an identification
  of them.

## Note of 2026-11-17: who carries an unavoidable mismatch, as a mark of coercion, from "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"

A39 §4 of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" (https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b, 2026-11-16), answering the owner's U19, takes a case this claim's second target covers: two parties with "legitimate but incompatible needs", where nothing relieves the incompatibility. In a constructive relationship "the incompatibility becomes a shared problem" and the costs are divided, imperfectly. In a coercive one "One person's needs become nonnegotiable constraints. The other's needs become problems they must personally solve", and "The organization resolves the conflict by assigning the burden of incompatibility to the less powerful participant." A's summary: "**A productive relationship treats legitimate conflicts as problems of coordination. A coercive relationship can transform those conflicts into obligations of unilateral accommodation.**"

This is the claim's redistribution without relief, with a normative test attached: where the residual is made to sit, and whether the party carrying it had any say in that. The claim keeps the design target apart from its worth ("That redistributing or exploiting a mismatch is good" is not said; [QUESTION-387](../questions.d/QUESTION-387.md) asks whether it can be), and A's reading does not touch that; it says which redistributions mark a relationship as coercive, relative to a standard on who should bear an unavoidable cost. A also ties it to the record's tolerance work: an organization "may remain quiet because unmet demands have been absorbed", which is [CLAIM-549](CLAIM-549.md)'s quiescence without satisfaction. The step from design target to verdict is A's and interpretive.

No status changes.

## Note of 2026-11-22: a regulatory illustration, from the exchange "Explain First Contact RL"

A33 §5 of the exchange "Explain First Contact RL" (ChatGPT, 2026-11-22)
reads the three design targets for an industrial process with a
hazardous byproduct. "A regulation that limits exposure may redistribute
where the hazard is borne. A requirement to adopt a genuinely cleaner
process might eliminate a technical incompatibility. A monitoring and
shutdown regime might instead make the hazardous system safer under
disturbances." Its discipline: "Don't confuse making an undesirable
arrangement more stable with changing the conditions that made it
undesirable." The relief case needs the regulation to change the
dynamics, which [CLAIM-tmpk4bou](CLAIM-tmpk4bou.md) derives. The illustration is A's. No status
change.
