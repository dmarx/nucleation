# LIT-tmp6ahq0: Some Consequences of Having Too Little

Shah, A. K., Mullainathan, S. & Shafir, E. (2012). Some Consequences of Having Too Little. *Science* 338(6107): 682–685. doi:10.1126/science.1222426.

## Key takeaways

*Unread. What follows is carried by the stand-ins, not by the article.*

- **Five experiments, as the authors restate them** ([LIT-tmpb0bn3](LIT-tmpb0bn3.md), Table 1):
  Wheel of Fortune with budgets of guesses, then an attention task
  (Study 1); Angry Blueberries with budgets of shots (Study 2); Family
  Feud with budgets of time, Family Feud with Debt, and Family Feud with
  Previews (Studies 3–5).
- **The borrowing result replicated.** In the authors' own pre-registered
  replications, poor players borrowed more and did worse with
  interest-bearing borrowing, for shots and for time, with smaller effects
  ([THEORY-tmplj3t9](../theory.d/THEORY-tmplj3t9.md)).
- **Study 1 did not.** The Social Sciences Replication Project's
  replication of it failed (Camerer et al. 2018, [LIT-tmpess4c](LIT-tmpess4c.md);
  [THEORY-tmp6qwmt](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp6qwmt.md)), the authors' own replication ran the other way, and
  the authors now call it a false positive.

- **The working paper** ([LIT-tmpkabn6](LIT-tmpkabn6.md), read in [NOTE-tmp5jtai](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp5jtai.md); [THEORY-tmp008j1](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp008j1.md))
