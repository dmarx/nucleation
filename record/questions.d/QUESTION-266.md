# QUESTION-266: Can felt valence be operationalized, for systems that report and systems that cannot, by three layers measured independently, an ordering serving as the negotiation's potential in the system's conflicts, a hedonic marker that does not depend on choice, and the system's own model of its potential, and do the layers dissociate where the potential account and its rivals say they should?

## Why it is a question

[CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) is functional: valence is potential of a negotiation by an
ordering of the system's own states. [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md) adds a candidate for
when that regime is felt: when the system also models its potential.
Neither is testable until each layer can be measured without the others.

**The three layers, with measures already in use elsewhere.**
1. **Potential.** Which ranking wins the system's conflicts, for how long,
   and whether it returns ([QUESTION-268](QUESTION-268.md)): a persistent potential
   ([TERM-078](../terms.d/TERM-078.md)), reported as the class of orderings the record fits
   until an intervention narrows it. For animals and people,
   arbitration weights estimated from choice, in the tradition of Daw,
   Niv and Dayan's uncertainty-based competition between controllers
   ([LIT-580](../literature.d/LIT-580.md)). For organizations, a record of decisions on conflict pairs.
2. **A hedonic marker that does not depend on choice.** Orofacial taste
   reactivity, which Berridge's programme uses to measure "liking" across
   species; judgement bias, the reading of ambiguous cues, used for animal
   affect; and moment-by-moment reports of experienced utility, which
   Kahneman, Wakker and Sarin separate from the decision utility that
   choice reveals.
3. **The system's model of its own potential.** Confidence and
   metacognitive report, or, for systems that cannot report, an internal
   variable that tracks layer 1 and is available to other processes, as
   Brouillet and Friston's felt fluency is an inference about a change in
   precision ([THEORY-662](../theory.d/THEORY-662.md)) and Hesp et al.'s affective precision is a
   belief about the reliability of one's own policies.

**Two constraints from the readings.**
- Layer 3 cannot be validated by retrospective report. Remembered utility
  is a biased model of experienced utility, by peak-end weighting and
  duration neglect ([LIT-1448](../literature.d/LIT-1448.md), [NOTE-1105](../notes.d/NOTE-1105.md)).
- Layer 3 has a test that needs no report. Hesp et al. ([LIT-1468](../literature.d/LIT-1468.md),
  [NOTE-1111](../notes.d/NOTE-1111.md)) compare an agent with and without the level that models
  its own action-model precision: behaviour is the same while
  contingencies are stable and diverges after a reversal. A self-model of
  potential should show itself in how potential is re-set after a change,
  not in steady-state choice.

**A human candidate for separating layers 1 and 2.** Mohammadi, Van De
Ville and Vuilleumier ([LIT-1444](../literature.d/LIT-1444.md), [NOTE-1107](../notes.d/NOTE-1107.md)) find two networks
correlating with valence: one that "may relate to the theoretical
construct of "wanting"" (p. 8005) and one "reminiscent of the "liking"
rather than "wanting" aspect of valence" (p. 8006). That would let the
two layers be measured apart in people. It does not yet show them
dissociating: the latent variables are orthogonal by construction and
both come from one retrospective report.

**Where the layers should come apart.**
- On [CLAIM-440](../claims.d/CLAIM-440.md) as revised, layers 1 and 2 need not coincide at
  all. Layer 1 is goal conduciveness and layer 2 intrinsic pleasantness,
  two levels of valence with their own orderings ([CLAIM-433](../claims.d/CLAIM-433.md)), and
  the claim implies nothing about how they relate. An unpleasant treatment
  that serves the system's maintenance is negative on layer 2 and, if it
  prevails, positive on layer 1. So their covariation is a conditional
  hypothesis: they covary where the state the system pursues is the state
  it consumes, where what serves its maintenance is also what is
  pleasant, and come apart where pursuit and consummation part, as in the
  treatment and in wanting without liking ([THEORY-893](../theory.d/THEORY-893.md), [CLAIM-438](../claims.d/CLAIM-438.md)).
  The test is whether divergence is confined to such cases. An earlier
  draft of this question predicted outright that the two layers coincide
  when a self-maintenance ordering prevails; a review of the pull request
  that filed it found the revised claim did not imply that, and the
  prediction was made conditional. Decision utility diverging from
  experienced utility is no objection either way, provided layer 1 is
  read within the episode ([QUESTION-268](QUESTION-268.md)).
- On [CLAIM-439](../claims.d/CLAIM-439.md), felt valence is present where layer 3 tracks layer 1
  and absent where layer 1 runs without it, as unfelt fluency does.
- For organizations, layer 3 is the organization's reporting of what wins
  its conflicts to those who govern it. [CASE-151](../cases.d/CASE-151.md)'s board was told
  terminations were "in the range of 200-300", which, if the claim holds,
  is a mis-modelled potential, not an absent one.

## What would count as an answer

A protocol that measures each layer without using the others, applied to
at least one system, with a verdict on whether the predicted coincidences
and dissociations hold.

## What would not answer it

A single measure presented as felt valence, or any measure of layer 3
validated only by reports that are themselves layer 3.
