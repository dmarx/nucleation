# CASE-019: "Hug from Doug"

## The case

A4: "Replacing 'Clément' with 'Doug' would be a glaring error in a historical
translation. But structurally, the translator's name occupying the
corresponding slot captures something about what Marot himself was doing. So
Hofstadter is implicitly asking whether **relational invariance** can sometimes
matter more than token identity."

## What it can show

That what a rendering preserves can be a position in a structure rather than a
token: a sign's significance set by its relations ([CLAIM-082](../claims.d/CLAIM-082.md)). It is the seed of
the invariance programme, and it is absent from the manuscript.
