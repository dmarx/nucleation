# NOTE-2343: Dörfler and Bullo — Synchronization in complex networks of phase oscillators: A survey

## Contribution

A survey for control theorists of results on the coupled oscillator model
θ̇ᵢ = ωᵢ − Σⱼ aᵢⱼ sin(θᵢ − θⱼ) on weighted undirected graphs, organized by
synchronization notion and analysis method, with a closing list of open
problems.

## Key results (parts read)

1. **Landscape (§4).** "Of particular interest are so-called S1
   -synchronizing graphs for which all critical points of (25) are
   hyperbolic, the phase-synchronized state is the global minimum of U (θ),
   and all other critical points are local maxima or saddle points. The
   class of S1 -synchronizing graphs includes, among others, complete graphs
   and acyclic graphs".
2. **Identical oscillators (§5).** Phase synchronization is locally
   exponentially stable exactly when frequencies are equal (Theorem 5.1);
   oscillators within an open semicircle synchronize (Theorem 5.2); on
   circulant graphs, phase-balanced states and symmetric (m, n) cluster
   patterns are locally stable under suitable coupling (Theorems 5.3–5.4).
   "Theorem 5.4 establishes only the local stability of (m, n) patterns;
   the dynamics (35) may feature also other stable equilibria."
3. **Sparse graphs (§7).** Sufficient conditions on algebraic connectivity
   (Theorems 7.1–7.2); the fixed points written through auxiliary variables
   ψ = Bᵀ L†ω + ψ_hom with the homogeneous part in the cycle space (Lemma
   7.3); on acyclic graphs the condition ‖L†ω‖_E,∞ < 1 is exact (Corollary
   7.5), and it is tight also for "small cycles of length strictly less than
   five".
4. **What fails (§8).** With phase-shifted coupling sin(θᵢ − θⱼ − ϕᵢⱼ),
   "many of the presented analysis schemes either fail or lead to overly
   conservative results"; directed coupling has "only a few theoretical
   investigations"; and below the critical coupling "partial
   synchronization or clustering" is "vastly unexplored".

## Assumptions

Symmetric, diffusive, sinusoidal coupling on undirected graphs, mostly
without phase shifts; phase-reduced oscillators; deterministic dynamics.

## Concepts

Phase and frequency synchronization; phase cohesiveness (practical phase
synchronization); phase balancing; (m, n) cluster patterns; S¹-synchronizing
graph; algebraic connectivity; cutset and cycle space; critical coupling.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-935](../claims.d/CLAIM-935.md).** That every local minimum is global is the survey's
  S¹-synchronizing property, held by complete and acyclic graphs; a cycle
  of five or more is not, and the "strictly less than five" boundary for
  tightness on small cycles is the same threshold. The claim restates a
  known fact of this literature for [CLAIM-153](../claims.d/CLAIM-153.md)'s energy.
- **[CLAIM-153](../claims.d/CLAIM-153.md).** Its energy has phase offsets on the couplings, the
  frustrated case the survey lists among those where its methods "fail or
  lead to overly conservative results". The record's frustrated results
  ([THEORY-1082](../theory.d/THEORY-1082.md), [LIT-1668](../literature.d/LIT-1668.md)) sit outside the survey's main theorems.
- **[QUESTION-147](../questions.d/QUESTION-147.md).** Symmetric cluster patterns are locally stable on
  circulant graphs (Theorem 5.4), a symmetry-based case of the stability
  prediction the question asks to test, with the warning that other stable
  states may coexist.
- **[CLAIM-172](../claims.d/CLAIM-172.md)** (background). Synchrony here is coupling-made and
  peer-to-peer; the survey does not treat external drive.
