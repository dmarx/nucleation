# LEAD-tmpxcj7n: Operationalize optimization jurisdiction and test whether it explains outcomes beyond incentive and regulatory-capture accounts

## The gap

TERM-tmp3rbue defines a jurisdiction as the domain of decisions over
which a criterion has authority. A6's table of reasonable and overreaching
jurisdictions is a judgement, and nothing measures how far a criterion
reaches. The claims built on it compare jurisdiction with the content of
the objective and the optimizer's capability (CLAIM-tmpevykb); that
comparison needs each measured on its own.

## A first design (the assistant)

1. **Measure.** For an organization and a criterion (a platform and
   engagement), list the decision types the organization takes (ranking,
   notification, design, moderation, lobbying positions) and score each
   by whether the criterion is the operative standard for it: whether
   decisions of that type are revised when the criterion's measured value
   would rise. Jurisdiction is the share, or the set, of decision types so
   governed. This uses the organization's own records of decision
   criteria, not outcomes, so it is measured apart from the harm.
2. **Variation.** Find organizations, or periods of one organization,
   with the same criterion and different jurisdictions: a platform before
   and after a criterion is extended to a new decision type (from ranking
   to notification timing), or two firms in one industry whose governance
   confines the same metric differently.
3. **Rivals.** Incentive accounts predict harm from the strength of the
   reward attached to the criterion; capture accounts from the
   organization's influence over its regulators. The test is whether
   jurisdiction predicts the harm with incentive strength and regulatory
   influence held fixed.
4. **What would go against it.** Harm tracking incentive strength or
   capture and not jurisdiction, at matched jurisdiction.

## What it needs before it is specified

A source of decision-level criteria (internal documents, published
ranking policies, litigation records), and a declared standard for the
harm, which is where QUESTION-485's question of who declares it enters.
