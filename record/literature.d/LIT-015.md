# LIT-015: Joint Publication 3-12, Cyberspace Operations

Joint Chiefs of Staff (U.S.), prepared under the direction of the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff. Hosted by the National Security Archive (Cyber Vault). (2018), *Joint Chiefs of Staff joint doctrine publication (U.S. DoD). Archived copy at the National Security Archive, George Washington University.* — <https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/16681-joint-chiefs-staff-joint-publication-3-12>

## Key takeaways

- The publication sets unclassified US joint doctrine for cyberspace operations. It organizes all military cyberspace action into three missions (DODIN operations, defensive cyberspace operations and offensive cyberspace operations), places the preponderance of cyber forces under USCYBERCOM, and describes cyberspace in three layers: physical network, logical network and cyber-persona.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

JP 3-12 gives authoritative joint doctrine for planning, executing and assessing cyberspace operations (CO) by US armed forces. It describes cyberspace as part of the information environment and dependent on the physical domains. It sets out the challenges of attribution, geography, technology exposure and reliance on private infrastructure and foreign supply chains. It covers authorities, roles and responsibilities and the command-and-control arrangements. This 2018 revision replaces the classified 2013 edition with an unclassified publication plus a classified appendix.
