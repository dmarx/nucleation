## Two findings worth keeping

- **[CLAIM-604](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-604.md)'s "lacks" clause.** The soliton readings report Kerr solitons with an existence window and path dependence under a pump that does not depend on the soliton. Read with Herr et al. ([NOTE-1692](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1692.md)), the soliton's uptake of the pump is parametric and drawn through its own field, so the Kerr case is [CLAIM-604](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-604.md)'s state-dependent uptake from a fixed flux, not supplied repair. The clause was kept, scoped to repair supplied at a rate set by the supply alone. [CLAIM-606](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-606.md) has a note saying its qualification of [CLAIM-604](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-604.md) now rests on the oscillon.
- **[THEORY-1483](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1483.md)'s stability.** A reconstruction of Azam, Devarajan and O'Connell's model from the equations the reading quotes (scratch/formal/azam_stability_check.py) reproduces the steady states, the saddle at the low-aid point and the myopic threshold, but finds the high-aid point an unstable focus with no stable manifold, so not saddle-path stable. The paper's system may differ from the reconstruction. The reading stays Proposed; [CLAIM-602](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-602.md) and [CLAIM-603](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-603.md) cite that stability in prose and should be revisited if the doubt holds.
