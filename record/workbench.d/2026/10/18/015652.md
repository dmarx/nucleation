*Written at the owner's request in review of the filing of the 2026-10-17
exchange (the review entry of 2026-10-18). It is a position note, not a
claim. It says how entries filed separately fit into one account, so that
a reader can tell the synthesis from the neighbouring hypotheses the
exchange explored. It asserts nothing the entries it cites do not assert.
Most of those entries are Proposed. The note is the shape the latest
manuscript proposals were reaching for: A43's "The Geometry and
Governance of Frustration" and A47's conditional synthesis "The
Organization of Agency". No `works` term has been added for either.*

**The position, in the owner's words from the review.** "Higher-order
governance can achieve maturity by cultivating relationships among
lower-order processes that reduce the need for its continual
intervention. The resulting coordination may preserve divergent
evaluations, unequal but contextually appropriate authority, and the
capacity for legitimate renegotiation." A47 §8 had put the same turn in
one line: "The achievement of governance may be to make its repeated
exercise unnecessary."

**How they fit.** Read in this order, the entries make one argument.
Its weak steps are marked.

1. An organization maintains several demands. When they cannot all be met
   under its admissible transformations, it is frustrated, and that is
   an ordinal fact before it is a quantity ([CLAIM-tmp55mls](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp55mls.md)). Relief means
   the complex of jointly satisfiable demands grows, for the same demands
   ([CLAIM-tmpq80iq](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpq80iq.md)). Dropping or suppressing a demand is not relief.
2. One way to relieve frustration is specialization: differentiating a
   shared resource so that demands which competed for it stop competing
   ([CASE-tmpuc54q](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpuc54q.md)). This is the move from compromise to differentiation in
   A46: "That can enlarge the feasible satisfaction set rather than merely
   choose a point within the old one."
3. Differentiated processes still conflict. They can settle that among
   themselves, or the conflict can be escalated to a higher level that
   mediates or overrides. Where a recurring conflict admits a stable
   accommodation, settling it locally can cost less over repeated
   encounters ([CLAIM-tmpmr1t8](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmr1t8.md)). Local settlement is not free, and the
   claim is conditional.
4. Practical synchronization is that local settlement sustained over time
   ([TERM-tmpnfj83](../../record/terms.d/TERM-tmpnfj83.md)). It needs no agreement in rankings. Divergent
   evaluations can stay divergent.
5. Governance is what assigns influence among the processes. Its mature
   form does not stop the higher level from acting. It makes the higher
   level's routine intervention unnecessary, while keeping it available
   for exceptions and for revision. This is the owner's position above,
   and the step with the least direct support. No construction or study
   in the record yet shows a governance process cultivating the
   relations that make it idle ([CLAIM-162](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-162.md) records only that "a supervisor
   with nothing to arbitrate may idle").
6. Resolve is that organization keeping a temporally extended commitment
   effective across local fluctuations ([CLAIM-tmp0wvwc](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0wvwc.md)). Mere persistence
   does not count, and resolve must stay revisable.
7. Non-domination says the arrangement is the organization's and not
   merely imposed on it. Constituents' contributions stay consequential
   without routine override ([CLAIM-tmpqofon](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqofon.md)). It is a candidate
   constituent of authenticity, not a test of ownership. Low supervisory
   cost does not show it, because conditioned conformity is also quiet
   ([CLAIM-tmptg7io](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptg7io.md)).
8. Adaptive flexibility keeps all of this revisable. Signals stay
   accessible while their authority over action is made conditional on
   evidence ([CLAIM-tmp6ap9y](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6ap9y.md)). Safeguards protect the processes that
   revise governance as well as the governance itself ([CLAIM-tmpp9ox0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpp9ox0.md)).

**Recoverability of a specialist's influence.** The owner asked that this
be explicit, as "our most useful ecological connection": "non-domination
does not require equal influence, but a competent constituent must not
become permanently inconsequential merely because the circumstances in
which its contribution matters are rare." The pieces are already filed:

- **The condition.** A46 turned non-domination into "continued access to
  consequential roles", tested counterfactually: "When the conditions
  under which a specialist is useful arise, can that specialist recover
  effective influence, even after a long period in which its contributions
  were unnecessary?" That is the last clause of [CLAIM-tmpqofon](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpqofon.md). It is the
  analogue of recovery from rarity, the invasion criterion of modern
  coexistence theory (Chesson 2000, registered unread). A46 warned that it
  "is not directly a measure of authority".
- **The threat.** Allocating influence by demonstrated competence starves
  a rare specialist. Low exposure gives poor competence estimates, which
  give low endorsement and lower exposure still. So the organization can
  lose the capacity "not by explicitly suppressing constituent processes,
  but by allocating authority in a way that prevents rare specialists from
  reproducing their value" (A46; note of 2026-10-18 on [CLAIM-277](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-277.md)). March's
  exploitation crowding out exploration is the antecedent ([THEORY-142](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-142.md)).
- **What follows for the account.** Unequal influence is compatible with
  non-domination only if it is context-sensitive and recoverable: low in
  the contexts where a process is not competent, and able to rise again
  when its contexts return. Equalizing influence is not the remedy. In
  Chesson's terms, as A46 used them, coexistence comes from stabilizing
  niche differences, not equalizing mechanisms: "Equality of authority ≠
  coexistence through differentiated competence".

**What is held, and what is not.** The position is the record's working
synthesis, assembled from Proposed claims, and nothing here promotes any of
them. Three things would turn the note into a claim:

- a construction in which a governance process lowers its own future
  intervention rate by changing the relations among the processes it
  governs (step 5);
- a test that tells negotiated coordination from conditioned conformity
  ([CLAIM-tmptg7io](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptg7io.md));
- evidence that recoverable, context-sensitive influence preserves a rare
  specialist's capacity where competence-weighted endorsement loses it.
  [CASE-tmp4nzjz](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmp4nzjz.md)'s evaluator variant and A46's proposed experiment are
  candidates.

The organizational-affect and authenticity lines, which this position
touches at steps 1 and 7, remain set aside unless the owner reopens
them.
