# CASE-tmpo7zlu: Member and non-member under one arrangement: a person's vulnerability, the controlling actors' capacities and the institutional consequences held fixed, with the person a constituent of the organization in one case and affected by the same arrangement without being a constituent in the other, to test whether membership adds an obligation

## The case

The owner's review of [#488](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/488) on 2026-11-13 asked: "Does being a constituent
of a particular whole generate obligations beyond those already arising
from the constituent's vulnerability, interests, rights, and exposure to
another's power?" Its construction:

> Imagine two cases in which a person's vulnerability, the controlling
> actors' capacities, and the relevant institutional consequences are
> held fixed. In one case, the person is a constituent of the
> organization. In the other, the person is affected by precisely the
> same arrangement but is not considered a constituent. Would the
> organization owe something additional in the first case?

Only membership, or constitution, varies.

## The three results

| Result | What it would show |
|---|---|
| Obligations arise specifically from constitution or membership | "a genuinely whole–part ground of obligation"; what gives membership that significance must then be explained |
| Obligations arise from vulnerability or control, regardless of membership | "a relational responsibility theory that applies across boundaries"; the mereological relation would "help identify where the obligations apply without independently grounding them" |
| The arrangement creates normative constraints but is not itself a duty-bearer | "obligations must be attributed to agents or roles maintaining the arrangement" |

The review: "The third possibility is especially relevant to Nucleation's
broad ontology of organizations."

## What it is for

It separates the two inquiries [QUESTION-tmp9bbpk](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp9bbpk.md) keeps apart since
2026-11-13, the ground and the bearer. The first two results answer the
ground; the third answers the bearer, for an arrangement that is not an
agent ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md) sense 1). [CLAIM-tmpwn3wr](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpwn3wr.md), as retitled the same day, is a
version of the second result for one ground: vulnerability to a
responsible agent's discretion strengthens that agent's responsibilities,
whatever the constituent's membership.

## What it does not settle

- **Which result holds.** The case is a test, not an answer.
- **That constituency can be varied while everything else is held.** In
  some arrangements being a constituent may change the person's
  vulnerability or the consequences; a construction that holds them fixed
  has to be built for each kind of whole.
- **Wholes whose parts are not persons.** As stated, the pair is about a
  person; for an organism and its cells the "non-member" case may not be
  coherent.
