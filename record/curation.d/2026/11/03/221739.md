**Status of the THEORYs** follows the readers' checks. A THEORY is Active
where the paper establishes it and the reader checked it, by re-derivation,
recomputation from the paper's tables, or an independent simulation (Slemp et
al.'s Table 2 from Table 1; Donoghue et al.'s waveform artefact; the
Sánchez-Cañizares model; LDA's agreement formula). It is Proposed where the
evidence is correlational in a way the theory's causal reading needs, or
argument without a model.

**What the readings corrected.**

- **In the registrations of 2026-11-02.**
  - Ryan et al. 2022 is not "the nearest thing to an audit": it is a narrative
    map by the theory's co-founder, not preregistered, grading no
    meta-analysis.
  - Ntoumanis et al. do not supply the intervention evidence THEORY-052 asks
    for: they measure no need frustration and test no mediation.
  - Murphy et al.'s objection is to the satisfaction–frustration *split* and
    to items with no thwarting content, broader than "the frustration factor".
  - Persona Vectors fits no persona distribution.
  - Slemp et al.'s evidence is associational; its authors take direction "on
    theoretical grounds".
  - Patrick et al. 2007 does not use the name "relationships motivation
    theory"; it is a study the mini-theory was built on.
  - Voigt 2022 is an Opinion article, not a review.
  - Ben-Tal et al.'s in-between case restricts the rule's form, not what it
    reads; and their online date, 2003-08-08, is now `published:`.
  The LITs' histories record each.
- **In the exchange's replies and in this branch's entries.**
  - Jennings's uses of her sources: Szczepanski et al. are correlational, so
    "improve" is not supported; Voigt proposes the value update and its
    attention is bottom-up, against THEORY-tmptunid's dlPFC criterion;
    Anastassiou et al. explain field effects by circuit physics, against her
    inference from them; and the constraint without downward causation is
    Bishop's own (2024 SEP), so what is hers is the boundary condition and the
    causal-power step. NOTE-tmpby7i2 and THEORY-tmp6xjgq are corrected.
  - CLAIM-tmphbnqm: smoothed LDA's topic priors are draws from one shared
    Dirichlet, and the Assistant Axis's input-driven switching fits neither
    limit. TERM-064's note: "QK/OV circuit" is Elhage et al.'s vocabulary.
  - CLAIM-tmpex9rg: Froese, a libertarian, concedes its core; the irruption
    model has no chaos. Kane himself is still unread.
- **In the record.**
  - THEORY-049 said Ryan et al. 1983 found its pattern "not significantly"; the
    primary paper reports a significant contrast.
  - THEORY-052: Campbell et al. give no temporal order; Levine et al. are the
    first prospective evidence; the frustration scale's split is organized by
    keying.
  - NOTE-439's C4, NOTE-441's C8 and NOTE-442 are qualified against their
    primary sources; NOTE-440's "derivative" reply answers SDT's own
    researchers.
  - LIT-1132's note of 2026-11-02 overstated what the 2026 paper covers, and its
    body still called LIT-588 Unavailable.

**Errors found in the papers** are in the NOTEs: Murphy et al.'s Discussion
against their Table 3, Levine et al.'s repeated descriptive table, Campbell et
