The owner asked of the filing of the exchange "Analyze nucleation
concepts": "no ARGs?" The owner also said the synthesis on legibility,
volition and correctability "should probably be a new CLAIM, not hidden
inside the prose of a QUESTION".

**The ARG question.** [ADR-061](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-061.md) puts in an ARG what the record says about
support between its entries; "X does not show Y" is the ARG from X to Y.
Every claim filed from the exchange was checked against that rule.

- **[CLAIM-511](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-511.md)** was a verdict on support. It said the record's
  constructions of corrigibility do not establish that it ought to be
  kept, and it had a subject-matter half beside that. It is split, as
  [ADR-061](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-061.md) says a mixed claim is:
  - **[ARG-042](../../record/arguments.d/ARG-042.md)**, Rejected, form `evidence`, holds the verdict. Its premises
    are [CASE-161](../../record/cases.d/CASE-161.md), [CASE-162](../../record/cases.d/CASE-162.md), [CASE-190](../../record/cases.d/CASE-190.md), [CASE-186](../../record/cases.d/CASE-186.md) and [CLAIM-342](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-342.md).
    The question that fails is whether the measure measures what it is
    said to: every measure is of capacity, none of value.
  - **[CLAIM-520](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-520.md)**, demonstrated and Active, is the subject-matter half:
    corrigibility's value is conditional, from A24's model. It
    `undercuts` the ARG.
  - **[CLAIM-511](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-511.md)**, version 2, is now the bare proposition, disclaimed
    and Proposed, as the ARG's conclusion.
- **The other claims stay claims.** They are about their subject matter:
  the fold, the provenance result, acyclic production, viable
  connectivity, distillation and viability, the normative thesis and its
  objection.
- **One borderline case.** The world-model thesis, [CLAIM-519](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-519.md), says
  competent behaviour "does not establish" an internal world model, which
  reads like a verdict on support. Its content is a demonstrated
  observational equivalence, a fact about agents and their interfaces,
  and its premises are not record entries. It stays a claim.
- **Not filed as ARGs.**
  - [CASE-186](../../record/cases.d/CASE-186.md)'s "what would tell the accounts apart" is prospective,
    so there is no verdict to file until the study is run.
  - The owner's U20 point, that the order an inquiry met the achievements
    is no dependency order, has premises that are not record entries. It
    stays a note on [CLAIM-474](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-474.md).

**The synthesis.** It is filed as [CLAIM-523](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-523.md), a Proposed interpretive thesis
that uses [TERM-064](../../record/terms.d/TERM-064.md), [TERM-023](../../record/terms.d/TERM-023.md) and [TERM-085](../../record/terms.d/TERM-085.md). It extends [CLAIM-349](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-349.md)'s split of
access, authority and action from an organization's own signals to
normative demands. The dated note on [QUESTION-364](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-364.md) now points to it.
The note on historically situated authority stays on the question, since
it is a question.
