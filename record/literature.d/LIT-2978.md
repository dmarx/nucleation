# LIT-2978: Dynamic Scaling of Growing Interfaces

Kardar, Parisi & Zhang (1986). Physical Review Letters 56, 889–892. DOI
10.1103/PhysRevLett.56.889.

## Key takeaways

- Eq. (1) is "the lowest-order nonlinear term that can appear in the
  interface growth equation"; higher-order terms "are irrelevant" (p. 889).
- It is invariant under h → h + const and under the tilt h → h + ε·x,
  x → x + λεt (p. 889); the mean velocity is removed by a moving frame.
- d = 1: χ = ½ and z = 3/2 "are actually exact", by a
  fluctuation-dissipation theorem "that holds in d = 1 only" (p. 891);
  the static exponent equals the linear interface's, so only the dynamics
  tell the classes apart.
- d = 2: λ "is marginally relevant, and grows under rescaling"; the
  fixed point "is not accessible by perturbation theory" (pp. 891–892).
- d ≥ 3: λ "is irrelevant, and an asymptotically ideal smooth surface is
  expected with z = 2"; a strong-coupling transition is possible (p. 892).
- The conclusion misprints z as ⅔ (p. 892).
