# CLAIM-047: Possessing an action-generating regime is not the same as its being practically effective

## The claim

The first item of the paper's positive contribution (Section 7.6). Frankfurt's
unwilling addict has the desire not to take the drug, and the higher-order
preference that it prevail; another desire moves him.

## What it does not say

What makes the difference — that is [CLAIM-027](CLAIM-027.md).

## Note of 2026-10-27: grounded in the case, not the account

The status review of this date ([ADR-057](../decisions.d/ADR-057.md)) found this Active claim grounded
in [THEORY-120](../theory.d/THEORY-120.md), Frankfurt's account of ownership, which is Proposed. The
claim uses Frankfurt's unwilling addict and none of the account's thesis
about identification or responsibility. The case is now [CASE-181](../cases.d/CASE-181.md),
reported in [LIT-692](../literature.d/LIT-692.md) and read in [NOTE-545](../notes.d/NOTE-545.md), and the claim is grounded in it.

## Note of 2026-10-31: three surpluses the readings run together

The readings filed on this date each name something available that does
not act. The record keeps three such things apart.

- **An available regime that is not effective.** This claim.
- **A claim to precedence that is not granted.** CLAIM-484's gap. The
  parent paper's "excess action", "the feelings you cannot deal with,
  unchanneled energy, skills in search of an outlet" (LIT-tmp3mudg, p.
  1351), is closest to this.
- **Potential not yet captured into any regime.** Massumi's "excess of
  activity over action" (LIT-tmpi0u4n, p. 29) and the "surplus of
  unacted-out potential that is collectively felt" (LIT-tmp8hcfz, p. 7).
  NOTE-tmpn58uh observes that the parent's excess action is closer to
  Massumi's "capture" than to his "excess".

This claim covers only the first. Keeping the three apart lets the
record separate what the parent's "excess action" joins: a regime that
is available and idle, a claim that is refused, and a potential that no
regime yet carries.

No status, title or warrant changes.
