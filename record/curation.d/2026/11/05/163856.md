## What was done

1. **Occupancy against first passage (high).**
   - [CASE-216](../../record/cases.d/CASE-216.md)'s figures are the fraction folded at the observation
     time, Pr(X_T = F), not the chance of having reached the folded state,
     Pr(τ_F ≤ T). The title, summary and [CLAIM-594](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-594.md) had blurred the two.
   - Both are now computed. With F made absorbing for first passage, they
     agree to better than 10⁻⁷ at every temperature from 290 to 345 K and
     for the schedule, because F barely melts on these timescales. So the
     results stand under either reading.
   - The labels now say which is meant. [CLAIM-594](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-594.md)'s point that more
     time widens the window is qualified: it is automatic for first
     passage, and holds for occupancy only because F holds.
2. **The committor is eventual (high).** [CLAIM-595](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-595.md) gains a section,
   checked in the script. Scaling a nucleus's exits tenfold slower keeps
   its eventual success at 80 % and cuts success by T = 10 from 50.6 % to
   7.6 %. So stabilizing a nucleus can keep its eventual chance and spoil
   its readiness.
3. **Exposure is not stock (medium).**
   - [CASE-215](../../record/cases.d/CASE-215.md) and [CLAIM-598](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-598.md) now say "equal integrated exposure",
     ∫c dt, where they said "equal total supply". The curation entry and
     changelog are changed to match.
   - The case adds a paragraph on the stock model,
     ḃ = s − Σ ν_i k_i(x, b) with b ≥ 0, in which equal expenditure is equal
     ∫s dt. It notes that the link to use-it-or-lose-it budgets runs
     through that version, which is not computed.
4. **Evidence against demonstration (medium).** [CLAIM-599](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-599.md) now separates
   the two:
   - the numerical example refutes upward closure;
   - Dunn et al. and Majikes et al. 2026 are evidence for competing early
     commitments, not for the factorial proposition that two productive
     nuclei together make a trap.
5. **Generative nucleation (medium).**
   - [QUESTION-397](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-397.md), A's question: does a nucleus help by changing the
     transition dynamics, or only by being a more advanced state?
   - Checked against [QUESTION-380](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-380.md) as A asked. It is filed separately because
     [QUESTION-380](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-380.md) asks when a subsystem helps, and this asks by which route.
     [QUESTION-380](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-380.md) has a pointer.
   - The assistant adds the observable: under a pure state advantage,
     later steps' hazards from matched configurations do not depend on
     whether the nucleus formed.
6. **Reproduction (optional).** scratch/formal/dna_origami_toys.py
   recomputes and asserts every number of the toy models:
   - the annealing toy, both measures;
   - A's branching model and its uniform scaling;
   - placement and timing;
   - A's stabilization toy;
   - the deadline table.
