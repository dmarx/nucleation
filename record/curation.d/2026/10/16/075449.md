071404). This group took the sixteen LITs tagged `self`, `personhood` or
`individuation` that had a `Read` NOTE and no THEORY citing them as
`source`. [ADR-024](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-024.md) keeps agent, individual, self and person apart, and the
tags follow it: persistence and what matters went to `personhood`, what a
self is to `self`, and counting organisms and their parts to

## What did not enter, and why

- **Dennett, "Where Am I?" ([LIT-405](../../record/literature.d/LIT-405.md), [NOTE-338](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-338.md)).** Its reading calls it
  intuition pumps with no thesis defended to the end. The official answer,
  that a person is where his point of view is, is "tentative, offered with
  stated doubts". The duplicate is stipulated, and the ending, two persons
  once the brains drift apart, is "illustrated", the punch line and not an
  argument. There is no finding to state. Its cases (Yorick, Hamlet,
  Hubert) would serve better as a CASE, if the record's own argument needs
  them a second time.

So fifteen of sixteen readings became sources, which fits the owner's
hypothesis for this group. The caveats: thirteen of the fourteen are
`Proposed`. Most are philosophical accounts whose `promote_when` names a
kind of argument that would settle them; for Gallagher, Strawson and
Shanahan it names a kind of study. One NOTE said no THEORY was indicated:
[NOTE-370](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-370.md), for the role-play paper, unless joined with a reading that
answers its question. That paper entered only as the second source of
Shanahan's 2025 account, which is the pairing [NOTE-370](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-370.md) names.

## Choices made

- **Surveys.** The three SEP entries were read as surveys, and the account
  filed from each is the reading's own synthesis or the entry's own
  argument, not a position it reports. For Olson that is the map of
  questions, not his animalism. The too-many-thinkers objection is set out
  in the continuity criterion's "does not say", since the animalist book
  ([LIT-448](../../record/literature.d/LIT-448.md)) is unread.
- **No relations to existing THEORYs.** This filing touches no existing
  THEORY. Where an existing one is a rival or a neighbour, the body says so
  in prose: [THEORY-060](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-060.md) against Strawson, [THEORY-322](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-322.md) beside Gallagher, and
  [THEORY-064](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-064.md) beside Hume.
- **Back-references.** `luria link --fix` wrote `answered_by` into
  [QUESTION-035](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-035.md) and [QUESTION-043](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-043.md), and `presupposed_by` into the Parfit THEORY.
- **A dropped rivalry.** Dennett and Strawson were not made `rivals`.
  Strawson's grid credits Dennett with falsifying revision, which [NOTE-364](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-364.md)
  and [NOTE-383](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-383.md) both doubt, and the two disagree about how widely people
  narrate, not about the same claim.
