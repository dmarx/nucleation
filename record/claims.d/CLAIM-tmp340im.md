# CLAIM-tmp340im: The organized possibility in which the record's account locates an individual is dispositional: what a declared bearer would manifest across declared conditions, grounded in its actual organization and persisting while unmanifested; so disposition is a dimension of the capacities, perspectives, concepts and will-organizations the record already names, not a further layer or object, and an individual's dispositional profile is its character, not its identity; and its dispositional equivalence to another, its historical continuity, its numerical identity and its autonomy are four properties, none following from another

## The claim

A52 §17: "These relationships ground a structured repertoire of
dispositions: possible manifestations across relevant circumstances,
including capacities not currently exercised." "The individual is not
simply its present state, its current collective mode, or its
dispositional profile." A47 §9: "**My strongest recommendation is to treat
disposition as the modal dimension of organizational capacity, not as a new
ontological primitive or a replacement for will.**" A50 §11: "**organized
possibility that has a real causal basis, even when it is not presently
manifest.**"

The architecture, in layers:

1. **What is dispositional.** The repertoire [CLAIM-tmp6xhn3](CLAIM-tmp6xhn3.md) (Proposed, A's)
   says persists. In [TERM-tmp6ersv](../terms.d/TERM-tmp6ersv.md)'s terms (Proposed, the owner's U22, A's
   wording) it is an ability across declared conditions, or a tendency to
   manifest. Vetter's split between "fragile" and "breakable" is the
   record's source for the two ([THEORY-670](../theory.d/THEORY-670.md), Proposed; [LIT-109](../literature.d/LIT-109.md) and [NOTE-125](../notes.d/NOTE-125.md),
   Read).

   The repertoire has three dimensions (A58 §10, second share, answering
   the owner's U26): the **discriminative**, "What circumstances can the
   organization distinguish?", which is TERM-064's legibility; the
   **practical**, "What actions can it undertake in those circumstances?";
   and the **transformative**, "How can it revise its distinctions,
   actions, and the relationships governing them?", which is TERM-023's
   transformation, with CLAIM-025 and CLAIM-532. "The first two jointly
   determine which tasks it can perform", as CLAIM-535 and CLAIM-572 do:
   a hedge can stand in for a distinction. *The reader's correction:* A
   drops CASE-194's third repair, authority, which lies between the
   practical and the transformative.
2. **It persists while unmanifested.** A47 §9: "a disposition provides a way
   of describing continuity through nonmanifestation". The record holds it.
   A perspective's enactment can stop while its dispositions remain
   ([CLAIM-421](CLAIM-421.md), Active). What persists through inactivity is the capacity to
   reinstate ([CLAIM-287](CLAIM-287.md), Active). Possessing a regime is not its being
   effective ([CLAIM-047](CLAIM-047.md), Active).
3. **A dimension of the existing terms.** The motivating perspective is
   "motivating as a disposition" ([TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md), Proposed, the owner's U28 of
   2026-11-04). The dispositional mode of an organizational achievement is
   [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md)'s (Proposed, the owner's term). A disposition is read through a
   governance-response profile ([TERM-067](../terms.d/TERM-067.md), Active).
4. **Character, not identity.** A52 §11 and A47 §7: an identical profile in
   a fork does not give one individual. Identity over time stays with
   [CLAIM-tmpcdshj](CLAIM-tmpcdshj.md) (Proposed).

What the integration adds is A52's "And now we can see how these are
connected through dispositions": the claim that one modal notion, borne by
a declared bearer, is the common dimension of the record's capacity,
perspective, concept and will terms.

## Corrections to A's version

- **Correction (fidelity): a dimension, not a layer.** A52 §2 lists
  "**Dispositional organization**" among "five fundamental layers". A52
  calls them aspects, "not necessarily separate physical components". Even
  so, this goes against A47 §9 ("not as a new ontological primitive") and
  against A50 §11: "This seems preferable to introducing *disposition* as
  one more object alongside all the others." The title takes A47's form.
- **Correction (fidelity): the declared bearer.** [TERM-019](../terms.d/TERM-019.md) (Active) retired
  "disposition" because it "had been carrying both the person-level and the
  institution-level reading". A47 §8 respected that, and A50 and A52 do
  not mention it. A50 §6 then gives a community a "disposition to reject
  unilateral governance changes". The title says "a declared bearer", as
  [TERM-tmp6ersv](../terms.d/TERM-tmp6ersv.md) does.
- **Correction (fidelity): ability and tendency.** A47 §2 separates them
  ("A person may know how to perform a task and be disposed to avoid
  performing it"). A47 §9's "modal dimension of organizational capacity"
  folds the tendency sense back into capacity. Layer 1 keeps both.
- **Correction (fidelity): Vetter.** A50 §2: "She challenges the assumption
  that each disposition must be defined by one specified stimulus followed
  by one specified response". It then models dispositions as
  stimulus-conditioned kernels, the conditional conception Vetter argues
  against ([NOTE-125](../notes.d/NOTE-125.md)). A's profile is [TERM-067](../terms.d/TERM-067.md)'s kind of characterization,
  and Vetter is its rival, not its support.
- **Correction (fidelity): numerical identity.** A52 §16 says [CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md)
  "already establishes this caution" that indistinguishable organizations
  need not be numerically identical. [CLAIM-150](CLAIM-150.md) is about constitution.
  Numerical identity is [CLAIM-329](CLAIM-329.md) and [CLAIM-287](CLAIM-287.md).
- **Correction (attribution and coverage).** A52 §1's "The recent turn to
  dispositions" is the owner's U22, and the owner guessed right that the
  word reframes an adopted term. A52 drops A47's [TERM-019](../terms.d/TERM-019.md) warning, A50's
  mimicking case, the fork as a new channel for a suppressed disposition
  ([CLAIM-tmp7fzb9](CLAIM-tmp7fzb9.md), Proposed), Swidler and habitus ([LIT-1367](../literature.d/LIT-1367.md), [LIT-1377](../literature.d/LIT-1377.md);
  [ARG-025](../arguments.d/ARG-025.md)'s competitor), and A50 §3's caution that an intervention can
  change the disposition it probes.
- **Correction (standing).** A52 §1: "we have reached a more precise and
  more ambitious formulation". The terms it rests on are Proposed
  ([TERM-tmp6ersv](../terms.d/TERM-tmp6ersv.md), [TERM-086](../terms.d/TERM-086.md), [TERM-104](../terms.d/TERM-104.md), [THEORY-670](../theory.d/THEORY-670.md)).

- **Correction (attribution), version 2.** A55 §11 tells the inquiry's
  course as what the work "taught us", crediting no turn. "The synergetic
  and coordination-dynamics work" is the owner's U4 laboratory
  ("synchronization phenomena might be a useful laboratory") and U6's
  timescale question. "The software-project examples taught us" is the
  owner's U14 (software product), U15 (PyTorch, and "a form of
  Structuralism") and U16 (the contested succession, CASE-tmpcr92d). "The
  meaning inquiry" is joined at the owner's U18 ("connect this to
  Nuckeation's inquiry examining how meaning works"). "the dispositional
  turn" is the owner's U22, uncredited again, as in A52 §1. Its first
  clause, that productive organization "can survive implementation
  replacement", is CLAIM-280 (Active), held before the exchange.
- **Correction (standing), version 2.** The four-way clause is held
  piecewise: isomorphism is not persistence (CLAIM-593, Active), persistence
  is by transmission (CLAIM-329, Proposed), and autonomy is a sense of its
  own (TERM-088). It generalizes layer 4, "character, not identity", and
  is a guard rather than a new integration.

## What it does not say

- **That dispositions are a new ontological primitive**, per A47 §9.
- **That a concept bears dispositions.** A47 §8: "an abstract concept need
  not itself be a causal bearer of dispositions. The dispositions are
  realized in its interpreters and the organizations sustaining its use".
- **That a profile measured on conflicts actually won reads the
  disposition.** That is [QUESTION-268](../questions.d/QUESTION-268.md) (Open).

## Versions

doing, or by the structures it presently realizes, but by what it is
disposed to do, recognize, sustain, resist, or transform under different
circumstances.**", and §9: "**An individual is a historically continuous
organization of realized relationships and maintained dispositions through
which a distinguishable repertoire of possible activities and
transformations is sustained.**" A50 §11: "**An individual is a historically
continuous organization whose causal relationships realize, regulate, and
transform a structured repertoire of dispositions.**" A52 §1 and §17 are
the version filed for version 1. Its clauses on meaning and will restate [CLAIM-tmpnjz70](CLAIM-tmpnjz70.md),
which this claim reframes and does not replace.
