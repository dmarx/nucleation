# LIT-tmp5bf7n: Saving the mutual manipulability account of constitutive relevance

Krickel, B. (2018). Saving the mutual manipulability account of constitutive relevance. *Studies in History and Philosophy of Science Part A*, 68, 58–67.

## Key takeaways

*Not read.* From catalogue descriptions and from the work read beside it:

- Baumgartner, Casini and Krickel, footnote 3: "One author (BK) of this
  paper argues that MM may be saved if the phenomenon is represented by
  multiple variables standing for different temporal phases of the
  phenomenon (Krickel, 2018)."
- Catalogue descriptions of the abstract: the mutual manipulability
  approach looks inconsistent because it is spelled out in interventionist
  (causal) terms while constitutive relevance is said to be non-causal;
  the proposal is a causal interpretation of mutual manipulability, as a
  relation between components and temporal parts of the phenomenon.

## Standing in the record

If the move works, it puts a time scale into the line between causation
and constitution, which is [CLAIM-tmp1qybv](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp1qybv.md)'s third part. That claim does
not rest on this reading, since the paper was not read.
