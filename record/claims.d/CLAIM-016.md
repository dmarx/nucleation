# CLAIM-016: An organized agent's will is its own to the degree that its operative commitments arise through, remain integrated with, and are answerable to its constitutive organization

## The claim

Built from [TERM-006](../terms.d/TERM-006.md) and [TERM-012](../terms.d/TERM-012.md), and defended against Velleman
with four ownership conditions and a manipulation test (A263).

## Why it is deferred

A274 found the ownership conditions insufficient: "A sophisticated
manipulator could engineer all four." The owner agreed at U77 and tried to
fork the conversation upstream of where authenticity entered; the fork did
not hold, which is why the retraction had to happen in-band. In the record it
is a status change on the members of the line, and [CLAIM-104](CLAIM-104.md) and
[CLAIM-096](CLAIM-096.md), which rest on this, are deferred with it.

## Note of 2026-10-12: grounds now held (status unchanged)

The authenticity line was set aside at the audit of 2026-10-05 because
"ownership criteria remain insufficiently defended". The record has since
read four works that bear on exactly this claim, and they are added as
grounds:

- [THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md): a regulation is autonomous "to the degree it is integrated
  with one's other values and needs", not by the order of the attitude
  that endorses it.
- [THEORY-047](../theory.d/THEORY-047.md): a value taken in under controlling conditions is introjected,
  not integrated, so the history of how it was acquired fixes how
  autonomous acting on it is.
- [THEORY-029](../theory.d/THEORY-029.md): a higher-order attitude cannot make a motive one's own by its
  order alone (Velleman's regress).
- [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md): a condition stated only in present structure is met by a
  manipulated agent, so a historical condition is needed.

Together they support this claim's "arise through, remain integrated with"
against the hierarchical account ([THEORY-120](../theory.d/THEORY-120.md)) that [CLAIM-022](CLAIM-022.md) and [CLAIM-047](CLAIM-047.md)
rest on. All four are Proposed, and [THEORY-040](../theory.d/THEORY-040.md) notes that no historical
condition in the record is shown to escape its own objection. The claim
stays Deferred. Reopening the line is the owner's decision; the synthesis
review only found that the grounds it lacked are now held.

## Note of 2026-10-18: graded integration returns, and a candidate repair

The exchange of 2026-10-17 came back to this claim's ground several
times, and cited the claim only at the end.

- **Second appearance: [THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md) at the center of a proposed paper.** A14:
  "**The biggest discovery from the second audit is [THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md).**" A15:
  "**[THEORY-054](../theory.d/THEORY-054.md) now anchors the graded-autonomy argument**". This claim had
  already made graded integration against endorsement order its own, and
  deferred it. Neither turn cited it.
- **Third appearance: graded ownership from the control side.** The owner,
  at U9: "controllability of a system isn't binary, and neither is
  sensitivity to perturbation. I'm wondering if our account equips us to
  make concrete statements about gradations (perhaps even
  gradients/spectra?) of freedom of will/autonomy". Filed as
  [CLAIM-tmpilfhr](CLAIM-tmpilfhr.md). A9's measures are of influence, not ownership: "It
  measures influence, not the normative validity of endorsement." So the
  ownership criteria this claim lacks are not supplied.
- **A35's split is close to this claim's three conditions.** A35, on the
  owner's U35 thesis that an external objective can become the training
  organization's goal ([CLAIM-tmpkb2i9](CLAIM-tmpkb2i9.md)): "the key is to distinguish **goal
  provenance, goal realization, and goal governance**". Provenance,
  realization and governance sit near "arise through, remain integrated
  with, and are answerable to".
- **A candidate repair of "integrated".** The owner, at U45: "'authenticity'
  should be treated as an organizational achievement characterized by
  non-domination of subcomponents." Filed as [CLAIM-tmpqofon](CLAIM-tmpqofon.md), an
  alternative reading of this claim's middle condition, with A45's
  objection that low supervisory cost cannot show non-domination as
  [CLAIM-tmptg7io](CLAIM-tmptg7io.md). The manipulation defeater this claim states is not met
  by either.

The status stays Deferred. Reopening the line is the owner's call.
