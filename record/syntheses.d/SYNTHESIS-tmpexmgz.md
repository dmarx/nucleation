# SYNTHESIS-tmpexmgz: Whether the solutions training reaches are connected is asked of the solutions, the path family, the shared history and the regime: one construction shows two realizations of one function joined by a curve and separated by the straight line and by any budget below √2 times their cost; the record's steps from pairs to many-model averages and to depth after alignment are Proposed; and whether the measured onsets, alignments and feature interpolation hold beyond the settings measured is open

*A part account of the learned-structure line (ADR-083); the line's account is built over it and its sibling parts.*

## The account

- **The root.** QUESTION-069 (filed in the fourth synthesis pass, the
  record's; Open) asks whether connectedness belongs to the solutions or
  to "the path family searched, the shared history that produced them
  and the regime they were trained in" (its title). Its body lists what
  each reading measured and with which instrument: curves (THEORY-109),
  onset in training (THEORY-114), a centre (THEORY-110), the convex hull
  from one start (THEORY-105), sign conflicts (THEORY-107), phase III
  (THEORY-108) and SGD's stationary distribution (THEORY-209). What would
  answer it: phase III runs aligned by permutation and measured on
  several path families, and a pair known to share a mechanism.
- **The path family.** CLAIM-524 (from the owner's remark at U17 of
  "Analyze nucleation concepts", A17's curve and A18's budget, the general
  bound the reader's; demonstrated, Active) bears on QUESTION-069. For
  f = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ the realizations (1, 1, 0, 0) and (0, 0, 1, 1) are
  joined by a curve at f = 1, the straight line between them falls to ½,
  and under a budget below √2 no path at f = 1 joins them. Its reading of
  the question: "This construction answers one part: for a fixed function
  and fixed endpoints, the answer changes with the path family admitted,
  and a resource bound is one way to restrict it."
- **The regime.** QUESTION-164 (the promotion condition of THEORY-211;
  Open) refines QUESTION-069 as its "regime" alternative: whether the
  law of balance holds in a deep network on real data. Its body says it
  bears on QUESTION-080 (the collapse family), "since motion along a
  symmetry changes no function".
- **Onset, alignment, merging and feature interpolation, beyond the
  settings measured.** Four questions hold the promotion conditions of
  the readings narrowed at the tenth and eleventh status reviews:
  QUESTION-312 (THEORY-114, linear connectivity emerging in training
  outside image classification) and QUESTION-317 (THEORY-112, alignment
  at standard width, what the residual barrier is, and depth) refine
  QUESTION-069; QUESTION-316 (THEORY-113, whether task difference makes
  merging fail) and QUESTION-318 (THEORY-111, layerwise feature
  connectivity beyond image classification and for many-model averages)
  name QUESTION-312 only as the pattern their move followed.
- **Two steps the record took.** CLAIM-498 (Proposed) is the averaging
  clause of THEORY-111's old title, the record's step from pairs to
  many-model averages, and bears on QUESTION-318, whose averaging half is
  its defeater. CLAIM-499 (Proposed) is the depth clause of THEORY-112's
  old title, composed from Entezari et al.'s unaligned curves and
  Ferbach et al.'s width bound, and bears on QUESTION-317. Each says that
  no reading measures it.

## Links tested as claims

- **CLAIM-524's two realizations as one symmetry orbit.** Not stated by
  any entry. Swapping the two paths maps each of CLAIM-524's endpoints to
  the other and each budgeted piece of its zero-loss set onto the other,
  so in the quotient CLAIM-263 prescribes the budgeted set is connected.
  It passes ADR-075's derivation route and is drafted as a claim
  (the line's account; separate draft).
- **The four promotion-condition questions as one question.** A
  classification, not a claim: their common pattern is their filing
  history, which their summaries state.

## Not stated on the record

- **CLAIM-263 (SYNTHESIS-tmp4xa7p) and this part.** CLAIM-263's protocol is assembled
  from THEORY-111, THEORY-112 and THEORY-113, whose open conditions are
  QUESTION-318, QUESTION-317 and QUESTION-316, and its body names
  THEORY-108's phase III as "a ready case for the protocol", which is
  what QUESTION-069 says would answer it. No relation or mention joins
  CLAIM-263 to any entry of this part. They meet only outside the line,
  through CLAIM-187 (pragmatic-transport), which rests on CLAIM-263 and
  which QUESTION-069 bears on.
- **QUESTION-164 and QUESTION-081 (SYNTHESIS-tmptiwlg)**: one law of balance, no
  relation (SYNTHESIS-tmptiwlg).

## What it does not say

- That trained networks are disconnected under realistic budgets
  (CLAIM-524).
- That averaging weights averages features, or that the aligned barrier
  rises with depth: CLAIM-498 and CLAIM-499 are Proposed and unmeasured.
- That a barrier surviving alignment marks a different mechanism; that is
  CLAIM-263's step 2, in SYNTHESIS-tmp4xa7p.
- Anything with standing of its own.
