# LIT-2194: B. Initial Value Problems of One-Dimensional Self-Modulation of Nonlinear Waves in Dispersive Media

Satsuma, J. & Yajima, N. (1974). B. Initial Value Problems of One-Dimensional Self-Modulation of Nonlinear Waves in Dispersive Media. *Progress of Theoretical Physics Supplement* 55: 284–306. doi:10.1143/PTPS.55.284.

## Key takeaways

*Not read. What follows is from Cui & Wang (2023), not from the paper.*

- For the focusing NLS equation iq_t + q_xx + 2|q|²q = 0 and q = A sech x,
  "Satsuma and Yajima found the discrete eigenvalue in upper half complex
  plane" to be κn = i(A + 1/2 − n), "where n is a positive number
  satisfying n" ≤ A + 1/2 (Cui & Wang, §3.1, eq. 12; the fraction is
  garbled in extraction and restored from the layout).
- The number of solitons is the number of these eigenvalues (Cui & Wang,
  §1). **The threshold is the reader's derivation, not a sentence in any
  source:** an eigenvalue in the open upper half-plane needs
  A + 1/2 − n > 0, so no soliton forms for A ≤ 1/2, one for
  1/2 < A ≤ 3/2, and N for N − 1/2 < A ≤ N + 1/2; since the integral of
  A sech x is πA, the threshold area is π/2. The quoted "n ≤ A + 1/2"
  admits the boundary case κ = 0, which is not in the open upper
  half-plane; how Satsuma and Yajima treat it has not been seen.

## Standing in the record

Filed with the soliton batch of 2026-11-06, beside Kivshar & Malomed
(1989). Cui & Wang (2023), [LIT-2202](LIT-2202.md), stand in for the eigenvalue formula
only, which they quote and check numerically; nothing else of the paper is
carried, including what fraction of an initial pulse is shed as radiation.
A reading should check the formula, the boundary case A = N + 1/2, and
whether the paper says how much of the pulse goes into radiation.
