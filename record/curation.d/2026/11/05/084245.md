## The assistant's contributions

- **CLAIM-tmpt0fnx,** demonstrated. Uniform abundance leaves a branch
  choice unchanged when the competing steps consume supply alike, and
  shifts it when they differ, so more of everything hurts where the wrong
  step consumes and the right one does not. It extends A's model
  (CASE-tmpj3cmd): 59.3 % falls to 19.0 % at twentyfold supply. It matches
  Majikes et al. 2021's blocked single fold, with yield j/(j + S₀).
- **Corrections to its own entries** from the readings:
  - CLAIM-tmp7y162: staple excess and magnesium also set the costs they
    pay. Excess does so by equilibrium, so time does not widen its window.
  - CLAIM-577's note: excess slack is a window for a single fold.
- **A refinement of CLAIM-566** (note): replication from supplied parts,
  as in Ni et al., is not regeneration after turnover.

## What the readings correct in A

- **Majikes et al. 2021:** A's "kinetic blocking" and its use of the paper
  as a precedent for kinetic trapping. The authors' blocked state is an
  equilibrium share, unchanged by a slower anneal.
- **Wah et al. 2016:**
  - A's "relative concentration of staples" omits the cut in edge staples
    and the slower ramp;
  - "final object remained broadly the same" is unmeasured;
  - A's kinetic competition model conflicts with the authors' equilibrium
    account.
- **Aksel et al. 2024:** first credited to "Cumberworth and colleagues", a
  slip A corrected. The yield gains are 8- to 30-fold by the paper's own
  numbers, against its abstract's "6- to 30-fold". One of the four blocks
  also changed its scaffold route. "Optimizing a developmental mechanism"
  goes beyond an equilibrium model.
- **Majikes et al. 2026** is in Nucleic Acids Research, not an RSC journal.
  A's "binding-site proximity" and "loop-mediated cooperation" are A's
  paraphrases.
- **Douglas et al. 2009:** "multilayer helix bundles" is A's paraphrase.
  A leaves out the week-long anneals and the 7–44 % yields.
- **Ni et al. 2026:** what is supplied is more than a "chemical
  environment" (a thermal program, UV, fresh tiles and sample splitting),
  and "molecular machinery" is not in the paper.
