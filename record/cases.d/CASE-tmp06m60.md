# CASE-tmp06m60: The manipulated will's unmanipulated twin: the same agent, from the same state, reaches the very same organization by its own revision under a discrepancy its considerate precedence cannot meet, and from then on receives the same inputs; the two differ only in which channel carried the precedence across its threshold while it moved

## The case

The agent is [CASE-tmpj29sq](CASE-tmpj29sq.md)'s Plum, of the same kind (the same regimes,
relations, maintaining cycle and revision process) and in the same state
at t0, with considerate precedence p_c. No one has a channel into him.

Between t0 and t1, a demand Plum maintains, a project that the considerate
regime keeps setting aside whenever the two conflict, goes unmet on
occasion after occasion. The discrepancy persists, his revision process
pushes his precedence toward the egoistic regime that would meet it, and
the push carries it across the threshold θ of his maintaining cycle. From
then on his own cycle maintains p_e and his own revision, consulted on
later occasions, finds nothing it leaves unmet. At t1 his state is
x_U(t1), memory of having worked it through included. After t1 he
receives exactly the inputs his manipulated counterpart receives, and on
the next occasion of conflict the egoistic regime prevails.

What his revision formed is a change of precedence among regimes he
already had, not a new distinction or channel, so whether it is
self-formation in [TERM-099](../terms.d/TERM-099.md)'s sense is the question that term leaves open;
it meets [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md)'s occasion (an unresolved discrepancy acted on by the
agent's own activity), and that is all the pair needs.

## Why this twin and not another

The twin keeps everything [CASE-tmpj29sq](CASE-tmpj29sq.md) holds fixed and changes only the
input between t0 and t1, from the team's to Plum's own. Other twins would
change more:

- **Pereboom's Case 4**, ordinary determination, is the twin of the
  original-design variant ([CASE-tmpj29sq](CASE-tmpj29sq.md)'s variant 5), where t0 is the
  start of life.
- **An egoistic upbringing**, Pereboom's Case 3, is formation by others
  without a manipulator. It is not this case's twin, since it changes the
  input's channel to another party's; it belongs with what a record of
  formation must tell apart ([CLAIM-tmpz9dqf](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpz9dqf.md)).

## What it can show

That a will of this kind can reach [CASE-tmpj29sq](CASE-tmpj29sq.md)'s installed state by its
own revision, which is the condition for the pair to exist at all: the
installed state lies in the agent's own reachable set. Given its standing,
nothing about real agents.
