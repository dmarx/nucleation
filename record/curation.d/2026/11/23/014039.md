## Alternatives for the owner

- **[CLAIM-1083](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-1083.md) against [CLAIM-164](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-164.md): `corroborates` (filed) or
  `complements`.** The lean is to corroborates, as the claim's body says.
- **Which bound counts as "the" bound.** Theorem 3 (residual plus drift
  over the smallest kept eigenvalue; any T with ‖T‖₂ ≤ 1) and Theorem 3′
  (the Davis–Kahan form; symmetric T, tighter near the cut) are both
  proved in one claim. Read as an a priori bound in ε alone, the result
  carries the factor 2^{j+1} − 1 from the drift recursion, which the
  check never sees attained. Both readings are in the one entry, since
  they are one proof; splitting them is the owner's call.

## What was left, and why

- The 2005 report's own content beyond its abstract, unread.
- Sevi et al.'s §§2–7 and 8.1, which do not bear on the bound.
- **Gaps** in the neighbour's argument (step 9a): the unquantified
  "[T²]_{Φ₁} ≈ ([T]_{Φ₁})²" is filled by the claim's Lemma 2, which shows a
  row residual enters for directed T; ‖T‖₂ ≤ 1, unstated there, is noted
  in [NOTE-2414](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-2414.md)'s Limitations and checked numerically.
- Whether the drift's doubling per level is ever attained: left open,
  stated in the claim and in the note on [LEAD-192](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-192.md). A further lead could
  take it; none was filed, since the lead asked for the bound only.
