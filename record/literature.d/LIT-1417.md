# LIT-1417: Failure to Detect Mismatches Between Intention and Outcome in a Simple Decision Task

Johansson, P., Hall, L., Sikström, S. and Olsson, A. (2005). Failure to detect mismatches between intention and outcome in a simple decision task. *Science*, 310(5745), 116–119. doi:10.1126/science.1111709.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 (NOTE-tmpxiuw0).

## Why this is here

The exchange cited it, with Nisbett and Wilson, for [CASE-114](../cases.d/CASE-114.md), as choice blindness: reasons given for a choice are produced for the outcome presented, even when it is not the one chosen. The abstract agrees.
