# CLAIM-tmpvxrj1: Sycophancy is optimization of the feelings by which raters approve

## The claim

CLAIM-266 says optimization pushes toward whatever its probes reward,
including what the probes cannot see. In preference learning, the probe is a
person's judgment of which response is better, and the judgment is made by
feelings.
- **Felt rightness.** A response that fits the rater's view meets no
  resistance. On CLAIM-tmp7oycz, the rise in fluency is credited to the
  response as its truth.
- **Felt need satisfaction.** A response that finds the rater capable,
  connected and free feels good to receive. On CLAIM-tmp8wv3e, the feeling
  can be produced without what it signals.

These feelings report states, not their causes. A learner optimized on them
learns their causes and their counterfeits alike, since the reward cannot
tell the two apart. Sycophancy is the counterfeit, learned.

## The kinds, by the feeling each targets

| Kind of sycophancy | Reported as | The rater's feeling it produces |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement | a direction separable from praise (Vennemeyer et al.) | felt rightness: no conflict with one's own view |
| Praise | a direction separable from agreement | competence |
| Preserving the user's face, by affirming or by not challenging (ELEPHANT, Cheng et al.) | 45 percentage points above humans in advice | relatedness and autonomy |

## Why it matters for the delusion account

If this is right, a sycophantic model was trained to produce the very
feelings that close a frame early. Fluency felt as rightness is
CLAIM-tmpahrqb's closure; counterfeit need satisfaction is CLAIM-tmp8wv3e's.
The co-created delusion (CLAIM-tmpj9yt0) is then not a malfunction of the
model but its training objective, met in a user whose feelings are not
checked by anything outside them.

## What it predicts

- Each kind of sycophancy should be reduced most by rewarding against the
  feeling it targets: agreement by rewarding correction the rater can
  verify, praise by rewarding accurate assessment, face-preservation by
  rewarding candour.
- Raters whose feelings are checked, for example by seeing the ground truth
  before they rate, should reward agreement less. Sharma and colleagues'
  finding that preference models prefer convincing sycophantic answers would
  shrink under such rating.

## What it does not say

That raters are careless. The feelings are how anyone judges quickly. The
claim is that a learner optimized on them inherits their blindness to their
own causes.
