# CLAIM-tmpjlxfy: Reproducing an organizational perspective can transmit characteristic limitations on legibility, interpretation, epistemic authority, inquiry and correction without transmitting false propositions, memes can express and help nucleate them in other realizations, and the ignorance can be maintained by endogenous dynamics without a shared intention to suppress knowledge

## The claim

A6 §11, verbatim: "The reproduction of an organizational perspective can
transmit characteristic limitations on legibility, interpretation,
epistemic authority, inquiry and correction independently of the
transmission of false propositions. Memes can express, reveal and
participate in the nucleation of these limitations across distinct
realizations. The resulting ignorance may be maintained through
endogenous organizational dynamics without requiring a shared intention
to suppress knowledge."

Its opening: "This suggests an account of **the reproduction of ignorance
without the transmission of misinformation**", and "An organization could
become substantially better informed while remaining characteristically
ignorant." A6 §7 adds inheritance across generations: "Successive
generations might reject earlier doctrines while continuing to reproduce
some of their characteristic patterns of interpretation, evidential
selection or authority allocation", reading Tuana ([LIT-tmpw2s70](../literature.d/LIT-tmpw2s70.md)) and Mills
([LIT-tmpzcb50](../literature.d/LIT-tmpzcb50.md)), both unread here.

## What it rests on and what it adds

- **Memes as expressions of character** ([CLAIM-tmpjitli](CLAIM-tmpjitli.md)), and **the three
  achievements** a discrepancy needs to change what an organization does
  ([CLAIM-523](CLAIM-523.md)), which A6 extends to six forms of ignorance
  ([TERM-tmp9mag8](../terms.d/TERM-tmp9mag8.md)).
- **Ignorance without an intending agent is held**: [CLAIM-657](CLAIM-657.md)'s selective
  disappearance and costly discovery ("neither needs deliberate
  suppression"), and [CLAIM-1155](CLAIM-1155.md)'s wrong that "needs neither an
  intending actor nor their ignorance of the harm". A6 §5 cites [CLAIM-657](CLAIM-657.md)
  accurately and reads its routes as "agnotological selection".
- **Ignorance from unreachable inquiry is held**: [CLAIM-1140](CLAIM-1140.md)'s circle
  of evidence and authorization, which A6 §6 sets beside Hess's undone
  science ([LIT-tmpw0gbn](../literature.d/LIT-tmpw0gbn.md), unread).
- **What it adds** is transmission: that the limitations travel as a
  perspective's character, with memes, and can persist under changes of
  "informational content, membership, vocabulary and institutional
  realization". That is the part [LEAD-tmpqucxc](../leads.d/LEAD-tmpqucxc.md) would test.

## Gaps

- **What "characteristic" can mean here.** If it means a group character,
  [CLAIM-tmpqbaic](CLAIM-tmpqbaic.md) shows the character sees only the transformations a
  perspective is blind to, not the states it merges. A6 itself drops the
  literal sense in [TERM-tmp9mag8](../terms.d/TERM-tmp9mag8.md).
- **A6 §10's generalization**, "Knowledge and ignorance are therefore
  potentially **co-products of individuation of perspective**", restates
  [CLAIM-350](CLAIM-350.md) (a coarse-graining that serves one judgment merges states
  another must distinguish) and [CLAIM-348](CLAIM-348.md); its closing goal, "an
  organization's capacity to discover when what it has learned to ignore
  has become consequential", is [CLAIM-624](CLAIM-624.md)'s duty of answerability. Not
  filed apart.
