# SYNTHESIS-tmpvdeqh: The wrong beyond harm and how an organization becomes normatively less informed: reproducing what keeps a justified standard from constraining it is proposed as a breach of a bounded duty of answerability, and an organization can lose sight of unmet obligations by the exit of the harmed, by discovery it is penalized for, or by weakening the channels challenge succeeds through, routes the record keeps apart

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line, filed at the synthesis review of the line on 2026-11-15. The line's account, SYNTHESIS-038, is built over it and three sibling parts. Its first twelve components were mapped in SYNTHESIS-038 versions 1 to 3, and that mapping is kept here unchanged; the rest were filed from 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12 and are new to any account. Most members are also in distributed-agency, and SYNTHESIS-056, that line's account of perspectives, legibility and correction, integrates them again. Where a component declares which sense of "organization" it means (TERM-074), the account says so; none of this part's claims declares one, and several carry readings that are about sense 3.*

## The account

**The question.** QUESTION-441 (the owner's U27, verbatim; Open; also
distributed-agency) refines QUESTION-129: what is the counter-normativity
committed by a self-maintaining organization that harms and reproduces
what makes the harm non-legible, what makes it reproductive, and when is
it the organization's own. Its notes of 2026-11-12 file readings that are
sense-3 cases.

**The wrong beyond harm.** CLAIM-614 (the owner's presupposition;
Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-348 and CLAIM-523, uses
TERM-064 and TERM-070, and bears on QUESTION-441 and QUESTION-129: such an
organization commits a counter-normativity distinct from the harm. CLAIM-624
(A's, A60 to A62; Proposed; also distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-614,
CLAIM-348, CLAIM-523, CLAIM-445 and CLAIM-462: the wrong is a breach of a
second-order duty of answerability, "to maintain feasible means for
relevant violations to become knowable, contestable and correctable", "a
verdict only given an independently justified standard and a feasible
alternative". It was admitted on that narrower title (its history,
version 3); its body states where it diverges from the owner's thesis on
unavoidable projections. A note of 2026-11-12 says a sense-3 reading, the
state and its institutions, corroborates it. Six arguments conclude it:

- ARG-055 (Proposed), from CASE-229 (A's matched-harm construction): the
  second-order wrong comes apart from the harm and its continuation;
- ARG-058 (Proposed), resting on CLAIM-520: an unconditional duty would
  entail CLAIM-511, which the record disclaims, so the duty is bounded;
- ARG-062 (Proposed), from THEORY-140 and CASE-138: an organization can
  reproduce the conditions that keep a known hazard from constraining it,
  "though it says nothing of a second-order wrong";
- ARG-063 (Proposed; in distributed-agency only), from CASE-228: a harmful
  outcome and the regime that keeps it unrecorded can sustain each other
  without intent, and it "shows nothing normative";
- ARG-064 (Proposed), resting on CLAIM-445: "the reproductive mechanism
  transfers and the verdict does not";
- ARG-071 (Rejected), from Wells Fargo (CASE-151): occlusion and blocked
  uptake, not reproduction.

**The counter-corrective route.** CLAIM-619 (A's, A62; Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-402 and CLAIM-406, uses TERM-118 and
bears on QUESTION-441: an organization can be disposed to answer a
credible challenge by reducing the capacity for future challenge, and the
test alone does not say whether the response "finks the disposition to be
corrected, masks it, or changes only the challengers' access and
incentives". ARG-059 (Proposed) concludes it from the self-confirming
label (CASE-141); ARG-070 (Deferred) offers Pohlhaus, unread. A note of
2026-11-12 says a sense-3 reading, about universities, corroborates the
phenomenon, not its test.

**Who installed it.** QUESTION-449 (the assistant's, raised by the reading
review of 2026-11-11; Open) refines QUESTION-441: when an organization
reproduces a mechanism that keeps its harm non-legible, does it matter
that another installed it, or does only whose process now maintains it
decide? CASE-232 (Stute's story, THEORY-1561; specimen, stipulated,
Active; also distributed-agency) bears on it and illustrates CLAIM-522: a
compiler Trojan formed by one party, reproduced by the host's own
compiling, hidden from the host.

**Selective disappearance.** CLAIM-652 (the assistant's, accepted by the
owner on 2026-11-12; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-655, grounds in CASE-246, bears on
QUESTION-441 and complements CLAIM-662 and CLAIM-619: an organization that
weighs a harmful practice by the failures its present users report "can
expand the practice with no act against complaint", as the harmed leave.
CASE-246 (the assistant's toy; stipulated, Proposed) illustrates CLAIM-655
and bears on QUESTION-441; ARG-092 (Proposed) concludes CLAIM-652 from it.

**Costly discovery.** CLAIM-662 (A's, A44 §3, narrowed by the owner's
review of #485 on 2026-11-12; thesis, demonstrated, Proposed; also
distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-347, bears on QUESTION-441 and
QUESTION-456, and complements CLAIM-619 and CLAIM-475: expanding the
recognized demand set cannot improve conjunctive compliance or decrease
recognized violations, and "where governance penalizes newly recognized
violations without rewarding their discovery", an organization "may
acquire an incentive not to improve its identification of its
obligations". Its summary says the monotonicity facts are checked by
enumeration and the incentive is conditional. QUESTION-456 (A's, A42 §8;
Open; also distributed-agency) refines QUESTION-129: how the demand set is
to be established defensibly when the organization's own governance shapes
who counts as a claimant. CASE-249 (A's construction, not run;
stipulated, Proposed; first line realizability) illustrates CLAIM-662 and
CLAIM-663 and bears on QUESTION-456 and QUESTION-279.

**Two routes, kept apart.** CLAIM-657 (the owner's, from the review of #485
on 2026-11-12; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also distributed-agency)
rests on CLAIM-652 and CLAIM-662, bears on QUESTION-441, and complements
CLAIM-619 and CLAIM-624: an organization can become normatively less
informed without censoring anyone by selective disappearance and by costly
discovery; "neither needs deliberate suppression, and both differ from the
counter-corrective weakening of the channels through which challenge
succeeds, so that they should not be conflated in attributing
responsibility". QUESTION-441's note of 2026-11-12 records the two routes.

**News.** CLAIM-656 (the owner's, U16 of the same exchange; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; first line distributed-agency) rests on CLAIM-658,
grounds in CASE-247 and bears on QUESTION-441: where attention is the
revenue, optimizing for it works against investigative journalism, whose
findings "cannot be shown to be in demand before they exist". CASE-247
(A's thought experiment; stipulated, Proposed) illustrates CLAIM-656,
CLAIM-342 and CLAIM-463 and bears on QUESTION-441: a newsroom whose
inability to observe the need for investigation "becomes part of the
justification for not investigating".
