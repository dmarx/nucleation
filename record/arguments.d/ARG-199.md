# ARG-199: Calvet's Boolean reservoirs → criticality reshapes

## The inference

[CLAIM-809](../claims.d/CLAIM-809.md)'s last clause: "the best distance from the transition depends on
the task, the integration window and the correlations of the
environment". [LIT-3056](../literature.d/LIT-3056.md), Discussion: "this alignment is contingent upon
the sign of excitatory-inhibitory balance b. For b > 0 ... the critical
region coincides with the highest performance. However, for b < 0, the
region of optimal performance does not coincide with the critical region";
"Refining initial literature (Bertschinger und Natschläger, 2004; ...),
the highest-performing region is characterized by a preponderance of
irregular attractors within the disordered region."

## Critical questions

- **Is the evidence what the clause needs?** Partly. It shows the best
  distance moving with a parameter of the system (the balance), within one
  model class and the paper's tasks; the clause names task, window and
  environment, and the balance is none of these. It supports "depends",
  against "only near", not the clause's particular list.
- **Is the reading adequate?** Only the Discussion was read; how the
  critical region is located and how large the gap is are not known here.
- **Could it be the same result?** No: the system differs (Boolean
  functions rather than threshold gates with Gaussian weights) and the
  conclusion contradicts the 2004 headline for one sign of b.

## Verdict

Supports [CLAIM-809](../claims.d/CLAIM-809.md)'s dependence clause, weakly, from a passage. The LIT
joins the claim's grounds. Proposed.
