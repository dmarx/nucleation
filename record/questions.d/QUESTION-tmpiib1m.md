# QUESTION-tmpiib1m: Does a law that constrains without being produced by what it constrains, such as a symmetry, a conservation law or constructor theory's laws of possibility, count as a constraint realized and maintained by something, or does it individuate kinds while realized by nothing?

## Why it is a question

[CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) holds that what an entity is, is partly fixed by the
constraints on the configurations it can enter, and that "those
constraints are themselves realized and maintained by something". Its
defeater (b) is "A constraint that does individuating work while realized
by nothing, a specification prior to every realization". The claim's own
notes name a candidate four times and do not rule on it:

- 2026-10-27, on constructor theory: "The possibility facts doing the work
  are realized by no particular thing". "The claim should say whether a
  law of possibility counts as something that realizes a constraint."
- 2026-10-27, from A23 §XII: "**Not all constraints are generated by
  interactions in the same sense.** Conservation laws, symmetries, or
  background structures can constrain physical interactions without being
  produced by the particular interactions under investigation." The claim
  "does not say they are realized by the interactions they constrain".
  "Whether a conservation law is 'realized' in the claim's sense is open."
- 2026-10-29: "Defeater (b) has a candidate the record holds and has not
  ruled on. A symmetry or conservation law individuates kinds (Wigner's
  classification, discussed in [CLAIM-134](../claims.d/CLAIM-134.md)), and Deutsch and Marletto's
  possibility facts do the same ([THEORY-1066](../theory.d/THEORY-1066.md)), with nothing obviously
  realizing them."
- 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-06: the candidate is "still unruled".

[THEORY-1078](../theory.d/THEORY-1078.md) gives the sharpest form of the candidate. Constructor
theory puts constraint in two places. A principle is "a law of physics
that constrains other laws rather than the behaviour of physical objects
directly", and a task is possible when the laws "impose no limit, short of
perfection" on how well it could be performed, so possibility needs no
actual constructor. A constructor is realized, approximate and
deteriorating: "No perfect constructors exist in nature." The reading
leaves the question to the record: the paper "treats possibilities as
facts about the laws and says nothing about what realizes a law".

## Why it is open here

Three answers are on offer, and the record holds no argument for any of
them.

- **Realized by the substrate's dynamics.** A law is realized by
  everything that obeys it, as a regularity is realized by its instances.
  Then defeater (b) never fires. But "maintained by something" would no
  longer mean what it means in [TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s closure, where a constraint is
  conserved at the time scale of the process it acts on and is produced
  by processes at another, and the
  claim's recursion would hold for laws only verbally.
- **Effective, at some level.** A10 §7's role-relativity, "Constraint at
  level n ≈ realized organization at level n−1", applied to laws: a
  conservation law that is exact at one level is the effective form of an
  organization below it. That works for effective constraints, which are
  all the exchange's examples ([CLAIM-480](../claims.d/CLAIM-480.md), [CLAIM-478](../claims.d/CLAIM-478.md)), and needs an
  argument for a fundamental symmetry.
- **Realized by nothing.** A fundamental law individuates kinds and is
  realized by no particular thing. Then constraints are prior without
  qualification for the kinds it individuates, and [CLAIM-477](../claims.d/CLAIM-477.md) is
  restricted to the kinds its constraints are effective for, as its
  defeater (b) says.

## What would count as an answer

An account of what it is for a law, as against a constraint in
[TERM-070](../terms.d/TERM-070.md)'s sense, to be realized, that either holds of a symmetry or
conservation law and of a constructor-theoretic possibility, with what
realizes each, or shows of one of them that nothing does while it does
individuating work. A worked case would be Wigner's classification:
whether the representation of the symmetry group that fixes a particle
kind is realized by anything the kind's members do.

It differs from [QUESTION-284](QUESTION-284.md), which asks when constraints constitute
rather than characterize. This question asks, of the constraints that
plainly individuate kinds, whether they are realized at all.
