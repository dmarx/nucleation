# LIT-072: Iranian Hackers Targeted US Officials in Elaborate Social Media Attack Operation

Mike Lennon (2014), *SecurityWeek (news)* — <https://www.securityweek.com/iranian-hackers-targeted-us-officials-ela…>

## Key takeaways

- The article reports iSIGHT Partners' account of NEWSCASTER, a multi-year (from 2011) campaign that it believes was run from Iran. Fake personas, including falsified journalist identities and a fictitious news site, befriended more than 2,000 people, including US officials, and then used the social links for credential phishing and reconnaissance.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

SecurityWeek reports a campaign that the intelligence firm iSIGHT Partners called NEWSCASTER. More than a dozen fabricated personas on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Google+, YouTube and Blogger, backed by a fake outlet (NewsOnAir.org), connected with targets such as US military, diplomatic and congressional staff, journalists, think tanks and defence contractors. The connections were then used for spear-phishing and credential harvesting, with some unsophisticated malware. iSIGHT attributed the operators to Iran, but said there was no direct evidence of government sponsorship.
