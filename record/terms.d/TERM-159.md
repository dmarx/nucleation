# TERM-159: inquiry frontier, as the boundary of the question–answer processes an agent or organization can jointly realize within a declared budget, horizon and inquiry regime, with a query frontier and an answer frontier as components that do not determine it

## Definition

A58 §3's wording, as A58 gave it:

The inquiry frontier "of an agent or organization, relative to a declared
resource budget, horizon, and inquiry regime, is the boundary of the
question–answer processes it can jointly realize." It has two components:

- **Query frontier:** "The questions the organization can formulate, making
  them determinate enough to pursue with its available concepts, records,
  tools, time, and computation."
- **Answer frontier:** "The answers it can discover, construct, verify, or
  realize for specified questions, using its available evidence,
  interventions, productive capabilities, and resources."

"The inquiry frontier is not generally determined by these components
independently." Formally, for state S, horizon H and budget B, 𝓕_S(H, B)
holds the admissible pairs (q, a) jointly realizable by a process that
formulates q and produces and evaluates a within the constraints, and the
inquiry frontier is "the resource-, time-, and quality-indexed boundary of
this feasible relation". The askable questions can be formulated within the
bounds; the resolvable ones also have an adequate answer jointly attainable,
and need not include every askable question. The answer frontier for a
question already supplied may differ from the one when formulating it uses
the same resources. "Neither query expansion nor answer expansion alone
guarantees an expansion of jointly feasible inquiry."

## What it is not

Not the operational frontier: by A58 §4, epistemically answering q is not
practically realizing what q specifies, and the answer frontier is indexed by the kind
of inquiry and its acceptance criteria ([TERM-095](TERM-095.md)'s 𝓚).

## Note of 2026-11-23: what this wording drops

Three things, if (a) is chosen. (1) [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s four sets. A58's wording
keeps askable and adds resolvable, and drops the expressible and the
investigable. The investigable ("on the frontier, with budget left to pursue
it to a stated standard") is the resolvable set already, if pursuing to a
standard means attaining an answer that meets it; A58's "it left the broader
inquiry boundary unnamed" misses that [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s version 2 named its
question side. A revision should keep the four and say that the investigable
is the joint relation's question projection. (2) [TERM-092](TERM-092.md)'s distinctions from
legibility ([TERM-064](TERM-064.md)) and from the regime ([TERM-065](TERM-065.md)), which the wording
drops. (3) The composition: "not generally determined by these components"
is true at one budget and false for the frontiers over all budgets under a
declared rule ([CLAIM-1115](../claims.d/CLAIM-1115.md)); the definition should say the coupling is
the rule, the schedule and what formulating does to answering. The note is
the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.

## Note of 2026-11-23: the bounds the components do give

[CLAIM-1025](../claims.d/CLAIM-1025.md), filed from A52 §7 and A54 §7 in the same batch, bounds
its resolvable set, the questions with some jointly attainable answer, from the
components: inside the questions each of whose processes fits alone (the
askable set and the conditional answer frontier), and containing those whose
posing and answering, along one way of posing, fit run one after the other.
Read off the components, the inner bound holds only where posing a question
does not change what answering it costs ([CLAIM-1115](../claims.d/CLAIM-1115.md)'s construction 4).
Where it holds, "not determined by" its components is true between the
bounds and only there. The note is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is
the owner's call.
