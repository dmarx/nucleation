On 2026-10-29 the owner said: "we have a ton of deferred LITs. file
readings for any you can find." The record held 99 LITs at `Deferred`.
Each was searched the same day for a lawful full text. The search
covered arXiv, the publisher's own free copy, OpenAlex's open-access
locations, PubMed Central and Europe PMC, institutional repositories,
authors' pages, SSRN, PhilArchive and HAL, Project Gutenberg, and
official government, court and UN sites. Shadow libraries were not used,
and neither were uploads of unknown provenance. No email address was
sent to any service, so Unpaywall was not asked; OpenAlex reports the
same locations. The work was split into ten batches of about ten works,
read in parallel. Each work then got one of four outcomes:

- **Read in full.** A NOTE (Read) was filed, and the LIT moved to Active.
  Its status note names the copy, and its summary was checked against the
  reading. 49 of the 51 summaries were rewritten against it, five of
  them because they were wrong in substance.
- **Read in part.** A NOTE (Skimmed) was filed, and the LIT stays
  Deferred, as that status's blurb allows ("reading stopped at … a
  skim").
- **A free copy was located, and this client was blocked from it.** The
  LIT moved to Unfetched under ADR-053. Its status note gives the address
  and the obstacle.
- **No lawful free copy was found, or only a paywalled one.** The LIT
  moved to Unavailable under ADR-041. Its status note says where the
  search looked.

Every LIT changed got a version bump and a dated history item. The
readings filed three THEORYs:

- THEORY-tmpx3lyj (Active, re-derived). Krakauer et al.'s individuality
  measures cannot decrease as variables move from the environment into
  the candidate, so a boundary chosen by maximizing them needs a size
  cost. That cost is not in the measures.
- THEORY-tmpncu1p (Active, re-derived from Blumenthal et al.). Where the
  consumer–resource cavity fixed point loses stability.
- THEORY-tmpbnint (Proposed). Under reflected appraisal across issues,
  power ends up in one leader with Friedkin–Johnsen anchoring and stays
  spread under DeGroot consensus. This was re-run on the same random
  networks, and the scripts are not in the record.

## Judgement calls on copies

- **Course pages.** Readers were told to refuse uploads of unknown
  provenance. Two readings used copies of published versions that sit on
  university course or group pages:
  - LIT-1594, Jasanoff, from a University of Colorado science-policy
    course page;
  - LIT-1217, PBFT, from MIT PDOS's 6.824 page. Liskov's group is at MIT,
    and the authors' own copy returned a 403.

  Other readers refused similar copies: an MIT course-readings copy of
  Edmondson (LIT-1220), and course-folder copies of Pettigrew & Tropp,
  Merton, Nisbett & Wilson and Akkoyunlu et al. So the standard was not
  applied uniformly. The owner may want to rule on whether such copies
  count. If they do not, LIT-1594 and LIT-1217 return to Unfetched, with
  the publishers' addresses.
- **A song.** LIT-1394, "The Way I Am", is a recording, and this client
  cannot hear audio. It is Unfetched at the official video
  (youtube.com/watch?v=mQvteoFiMlg), which is a minute's work for the
  owner. Lyrics sites were not used.
- **Versions.** Several readings are of an accepted manuscript or an
  earlier version, each named in its NOTE:
  - Kalla & Broockman: the SocArXiv accepted manuscript;
  - DeMarzo et al.: the LSE copy of April 2003;
  - Kleinman et al.: the NBER revision of March 2023;
  - Cooper & West: the Oxford ORA accepted manuscript;
  - Soros: the author's own reproduction;
  - Halpern & Moses: the authors' arXiv deposit, which LIT-1213 now
    cites as `arxiv:`.

## Errors found in the works

Numbers and derivations were checked where cheap. The checks' scripts
belong to the readers and are not part of the record. The substantive
errors, one line each (typos are in the NOTEs):

- Zhang et al. (LIT-1129): Proposition 5 is false. The SVD lumping does
  not commute with the dynamics for every grouping, and a counter-example
  confirms it.
- Switch Transformers (LIT-1412): the auxiliary loss is not minimized by
  uniform routing (a two-expert counter-example gives 0.92 against 1).
  §5.6's "by over 0.061" is 0.051 for Switch-C in Table 9.
- Hofbauer & Schreiber (LIT-1233): in §4.2 the invasion conditions for
  {1,2} and {2,3} are swapped.
- Sengupta et al. (LIT-1215): it claims every statistical manifold is
  negatively curved. The multinomial is positively curved.
- Krakauer et al. (LIT-1137): in the 2020 example, A = A* at γ = 0 holds
  only when self-coupling dominates.
- Kleinman et al. (LIT-1416): footnote 4 applies Perron–Frobenius to a
  matrix with negative entries. Table 2's 0.26 SD is about 0.22 by its
  own R².
- RefraMED (LIT-1223): its "throughout" is false at 7 months for both
  secondary outcomes. Its Fisher P = 0.004 does not reproduce (it is at
  most 0.029).
- Kalla & Broockman (LIT-1407): two p-values are too small for their
  t-statistics.
- Flack (LIT-1122): the "165×" ratio does not follow from the counts
  given (about 128 to 150).
- Hazen et al. (LIT-1148): −log₂(1000/26¹⁰) is 37 bits, not "≈36".
- Engineering Emergence (LIT-1123): Figure 2's values give CP = 0.15, not
  0.13. CE 2.0 (LIT-1136) prints the degeneracy with H(E|C) for H(E).
- Mehta et al. (LIT-1231), Power et al. (LIT-1235), Blumenthal et al.
  (LIT-1236), Désidéri (LIT-1212) and Kolchinsky & Wolpert (LIT-1149):
  equation slips, none of which changes a result. Each is listed in its
  NOTE.
- Johansson et al. (LIT-1417): 120 participants in the paper and 118 in
  the supplement.
- HRW 2023 (LIT-1593): internally inconsistent figures (the plantation
  buy-out, the cost of return, and the dates of the negotiations). It
  says the Human Rights Committee "recognized Chagossians as a people",
  and the source it cites does not say so.
- Herd & Moynihan (LIT-1609): "$1.2 billion, or 1.2 percent of GDP".
  1.2% of GDP is about $230 billion.
- SEI guide (LIT-1216): it calls the GDPR a directive and counts the UK
  as an EU member. Its "3,000 cases" does not match its charts' n.
- SP 800-53 (LIT-1206): AC-3(2) and AU-9(5) disagree on the safety
  exception to dual authorization.
- Loss-Free Balancing (LIT-1414): 300B tokens in the conclusion and 200B
  everywhere else.
- Friedkin (LIT-1404), PBFT (LIT-1217), Halpern & Moses (LIT-1213),
  Jasanoff (LIT-1594) and Scheppele (LIT-1604): wrong volumes, years or
  order numbers in references.
- Gross & Blasius (LIT-1139): it inverts Holme–Newman's φ.
- OpenAlex lists arXiv 1705.06614 as a copy of LIT-1215. It is a
  different paper.
