# LIT-tmplagrk: The Search for Ethics in a Technicist Society (introductory paragraphs, as posted by the International Jacques Ellul Society)

Jacques Ellul (1983). "Recherche pour une éthique dans une société technicienne", *Morale et Enseignement*, pp. 7–20; English "The Search for Ethics in a Technicist Society", trans. D. Gillot and C. Mitcham. Introductory paragraphs posted by the International Jacques Ellul Society, with a Fowler excerpt quoting the preface of *The Technological Society*.

## Key takeaways

- "Technique is the complex and complete milieu in which human beings must live".
- The technical phenomenon's characteristics: "Autonomy, Unity, Universality, Totalization".
