# LIT-2968: Network Science, Chapter 5: The Barabási-Albert Model

Barabási (2016). Network Science (Cambridge University Press), chapter 5, web
edition. https://networksciencebook.com/translations/en/partials/ch-5.html.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A cites it at A1 for a textbook treatment of the Barabási-Albert model, giving this URL. It bears on A's cited exposition of preferential attachment, the exchange's base case. Not read when registered.

Read on 2026-11-22 in [NOTE-2270](../notes.d/NOTE-2270.md); the reading is [THEORY-2184](../theory.d/THEORY-2184.md). The
first summary holds, and the chapter carries more than A used: the kernel
regimes (credited to Krapivsky, Redner and Leyvraz, [LIT-2974](LIT-2974.md)), measured
attachment exponents, and a rival framing of [CLAIM-817](../claims.d/CLAIM-817.md)'s aside on
hyperbolic geometry, in which geometric and optimisation models generate
linear attachment. It grounds [CLAIM-817](../claims.d/CLAIM-817.md) with [LIT-3004](LIT-3004.md) ([ARG-179](../arguments.d/ARG-179.md)) and
corroborates [CLAIM-806](../claims.d/CLAIM-806.md) and [CLAIM-830](../claims.d/CLAIM-830.md).
