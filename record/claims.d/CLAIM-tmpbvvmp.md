# CLAIM-tmpbvvmp: The form in which knowledge becomes institutionally legible is separated from the form in which it is productively developed, so a researcher who can sustain an inquiry and not its conversion into manuscripts meets a design choice of the publishing apparatus and not only a personal obstacle, and a record from which manuscripts are drawn as views reduces the separation

## The claim

The owner's account of their motivation, at U9 of the exchange "Hyper
article interpretation"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6b064f1b-9ea8-83e8-951e-634ed41675b6): "I
actually started with luria, and was using Nucleation as a demonstration",
and they found themselves "persistently motivated to continue moving
forward the theory and struggling to put pen to paper to publish my ideas."

A18 drew a thesis from it: "rather than forcing researchers to stop doing
research long enough to write up what they've done, why not make publishing
a natural consequence of maintaining the research?" A24 put it as a claim
about the apparatus:

> Your difficulty getting from sustained theoretical inquiry to a
> conventional manuscript is not merely a personal productivity obstacle
> that software might compensate for. It exposes a design choice in the
> publishing apparatus: the form in which knowledge becomes institutionally
> legible is separated from the form in which you're productively
> developing it.

It rests on [CLAIM-178](CLAIM-178.md), that a manuscript is a view over the argument and
not its only copy. If the argument is kept as it develops, a manuscript is
drawn from it and is not rebuilt from scattered material, which A18 calls
"forcing myself to do the intellectual work twice". It rests on the
owner's [CLAIM-tmpkvi10](CLAIM-tmpkvi10.md) for the many-manuscript case.

## What it does not say

- Not that writing is only transcription. A18: "Writing can be a powerful
  tool for thinking." [CLAIM-178](CLAIM-178.md): a view's prose "is written, not
  assembled", and [ADR-143](../decisions.d/ADR-143.md) makes drawing a manuscript from the record a
  protocol with reviews and fact-checks.
- Not luria's origin. A18 inferred that luria was built for the publishing
  bottleneck ("You were developing infrastructure for a problem you were
  experiencing directly"). U9 says only that luria came first and
  Nucleation is its demonstration. At U14 of the same exchange the owner
  says: "luria evolved from the process I was using to steer a pair
  programming collaboration with an LLM to develop a webapp." That origin
  is [CLAIM-tmp97pg4](CLAIM-tmp97pg4.md).
- [CLAIM-1200](CLAIM-1200.md)'s condition applies here too: where filing is tedious or the
  grammar distorts what is filed, the record adds a cost and does not
  remove one.
