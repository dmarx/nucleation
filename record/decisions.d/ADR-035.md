# ADR-035: causality joins the topics

## Context

Registering the successors to the withdrawn Gogioso and Pinzani 2021 brought
in a programme on causal order:

- The Combinatorics of Causality;
- The Topology of Causality;
- The Geometry of Causality;
- [THEORY-171](../theory.d/THEORY-171.md), on when causal data fails to glue.

No word fitted, so the reader tagged them by their sheaf and contextuality
content. The owner asked for a topic.

The record already held two other clusters about causation:

- **Interventionism and causal models.** Woodward ([LIT-713](../literature.d/LIT-713.md), [LIT-723](../literature.d/LIT-723.md)), the
  interventionist [THEORY-138](../theory.d/THEORY-138.md), causal knowledge ([LIT-038](../literature.d/LIT-038.md)) and causal claims
  in economics ([LIT-079](../literature.d/LIT-079.md)).
- **The metaphysics of causation.**
  - causal exclusion ([LIT-144](../literature.d/LIT-144.md));
  - mental causation ([LIT-147](../literature.d/LIT-147.md));
  - causal emergence ([LIT-005](../literature.d/LIT-005.md), [LIT-025](../literature.d/LIT-025.md), [THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md));
  - causal closure ([LIT-164](../literature.d/LIT-164.md));
  - reciprocal causation in biology ([LIT-110](../literature.d/LIT-110.md), [LIT-156](../literature.d/LIT-156.md));
  - a causal account of intentional action ([LIT-132](../literature.d/LIT-132.md));
  - causal loops in psychopathology ([THEORY-058](../theory.d/THEORY-058.md)).

A first draft proposed `causal-structure`, which took the order programme
and interventionism and left the metaphysics under `metaphysics`. The owner
asked whether one `causality` word would join the clusters better. It does:
the clusters ask what a causal relation is and how causes are structured,
and they cite each other.

## Decision

Add `causality` (Causality). It covers:

- causal order, definite and indefinite, and its combinatorics, topology
  and geometry;
- sheaf- and process-theoretic accounts of causality;
- causal models, causal inference and interventionism;
- the metaphysics of causation.

How it is applied:

- **First** on the four Gogioso–Pinzani works and [THEORY-171](../theory.d/THEORY-171.md), whose
  subject it is.
- **Added beside the existing tags** on every other work and THEORY whose
  subject is causation. Nothing is removed and primaries are unchanged, as
  the owner asked:
  - [LIT-005](../literature.d/LIT-005.md), [LIT-025](../literature.d/LIT-025.md), [LIT-038](../literature.d/LIT-038.md), [LIT-079](../literature.d/LIT-079.md), [LIT-110](../literature.d/LIT-110.md), [LIT-132](../literature.d/LIT-132.md), [LIT-144](../literature.d/LIT-144.md), [LIT-147](../literature.d/LIT-147.md),
    [LIT-156](../literature.d/LIT-156.md), [LIT-164](../literature.d/LIT-164.md), [LIT-713](../literature.d/LIT-713.md) and [LIT-723](../literature.d/LIT-723.md);
  - [THEORY-058](../theory.d/THEORY-058.md), [THEORY-133](../theory.d/THEORY-133.md) and [THEORY-138](../theory.d/THEORY-138.md).
- **Not added** to works that only mention causes in passing: [THEORY-018](../theory.d/THEORY-018.md) (a
  "causal inner product" in language models), [THEORY-065](../theory.d/THEORY-065.md) and [THEORY-121](../theory.d/THEORY-121.md).

## Alternatives

- **`causal-structure`, narrower.** It would keep the metaphysics under
  `metaphysics` and split works that answer one question between two pages.
- **Leaving the order programme under `quantum-foundations`.** Its spaces of
  input histories apply to classical and quantum data alike.
