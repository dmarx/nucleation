# QUESTION-tmp8v6pp: Does an independent experiment or simulation on prestressed multistable origami cells reproduce continuous tuning of the energy barrier and of bistability by prestress, and show that reordering the barriers of the cells in an assembly makes feasible folding sequences the unprestressed assembly cannot follow?

## Why it is a question

THEORY-1417 reports what Zang, Zhao, Misseroni and Paulino showed in one group's model and prototypes (NOTE-1569): three kinds of spring prestress tune a Kresling cell's energy barrier continuously and can switch it from bistable to monostable, and in a three-cell stack prestress opens the 8 of 56 transitions blocked by the ordering of the barriers, four of them shown by experiment. The assemblies have three cells, the springs are linear in the main analysis, and the theory misses the panel buckling seen on loading. No second group's result is in the record.

## What would answer it

The condition as it stood on THEORY-1417: An independent experiment or simulation on prestressed multistable origami cells reproduces continuous tuning of the energy barrier, and of bistability, by prestress, and shows that reordering the barriers of the cells in an assembly makes feasible folding sequences that the unprestressed assembly cannot follow.
