# LIT-tmpnuvqj: Double Loop Learning in Organizations

Chris Argyris (1977), "Double Loop Learning in Organizations", *Harvard
Business Review*, September–October 1977.

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* The distinction it is cited for is single-loop
learning, which corrects an error within the norms that govern action,
versus double-loop learning, which questions and changes those norms. That
much is common knowledge of the literature, not a reading. Nothing beyond
it is claimed here.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-05 at the owner's request. The owner's essay (§8.3)
distinguishes procedures for changing ordinary policies from procedures for
reconsidering "the standards through which policies acquire authority".
That is the single-loop and double-loop distinction, and the essay should
cite it or say how it differs. No theory is filed: the record has not read
the work.
