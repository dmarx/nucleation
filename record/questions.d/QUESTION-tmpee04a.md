# QUESTION-tmpee04a: What would show that an experience is a basic psychological need, something an organism's functioning requires, rather than something that reliably raises reported well-being, when self-determination theory identifies needs by their effects on well-being and treats well-being as the presence of need satisfaction?

## Why it is a question

Self-determination theory names three basic needs, autonomy, competence and
relatedness, and defends them by criteria. Vansteenkiste, Ryan and Soenens
2020 list nine ([NOTE-440](../notes.d/NOTE-440.md), Table 2). The "essential" and "distinct"
criteria are the theory's attempt at this question. If well-being is
measured by the satisfaction of the same needs, a need claim cannot be
tested without assuming the measure. [NOTE-439](../notes.d/NOTE-439.md)'s "Interdefinition" limitation
says so.

The record's evidence bears on it in four places:

- **Candidates that fail.** Martela and Ryan ([LIT-tmpkzr6r](../literature.d/LIT-tmpkzr6r.md), held
  second-hand in [NOTE-440](../notes.d/NOTE-440.md)) class beneficence a wellness enhancer, not a
  need, because its frustration adds no variance. Lubics et al. 2026
  ([LIT-tmpk1ssy](../literature.d/LIT-tmpk1ssy.md)) test it again.
- **Rival candidates.** Sheldon et al. 2001 ([LIT-tmpd3l9z](../literature.d/LIT-tmpd3l9z.md)) found self-esteem
  strong among ten candidates. [NOTE-440](../notes.d/NOTE-440.md) holds SDT's reply, that it is
  derivative.
- **Frustration as a separate test.** [THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md) holds that need frustration
  predicts ill-being apart from low satisfaction. Murphy et al. 2023
  ([LIT-tmpjcuzb](../literature.d/LIT-tmpjcuzb.md)) argue the frustration scale may largely measure item keying.
- **The measures themselves** (Kermavnar et al. 2024, [LIT-tmpohjdb](../literature.d/LIT-tmpohjdb.md)).

## What would answer it

A criterion for need-hood that does not pass through the same well-being
measure. For example: a functional deficit, not a report, that follows
thwarting of the candidate need and not of a matched enhancer, under
intervention rather than correlation. Or a showing that the interdefinition
is harmless because the nine criteria fix the needs independently.

## Bears on

[THEORY-052](../theory.d/THEORY-052.md) and [CLAIM-368](../claims.d/CLAIM-368.md), the record's own use of frustration as a
primitive, which inherits whatever this question finds about need
frustration's measurement.
