## Gaps filled (the assistant's, Proposed)

- **Publisher functions.** Hosting gives access only. The owner's split
  leaves registration with nobody ([CLAIM-tmpzz0eu](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpzz0eu.md); [LEAD-tmpxhtlf](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpxhtlf.md) for the
  readings).
- **When a feed is a journal.** For a deterministic query, only the
  judgements need storing. A journal is a feed whose data are attributable
  judgements under a declared rule ([CLAIM-tmptn64c](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmptn64c.md), demonstrated).
- **Version-resolvable citation.** A code reaches the current version of
  an entry. Content pins detect a change but do not reach the version
  that was vouched for, and the record pins nothing ([CLAIM-tmpk6kcf](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpk6kcf.md)).
- **A13's migration test, applied.**
  - The sources and their git history pass.
  - Choices kept in pull-request descriptions do not, and 297 files cite
    pull requests by number.
  - 86.2% of commits are unsigned ([CLAIM-tmp80nuw](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp80nuw.md)).
- **What the record's use of luria shows.** 22 of luria's 143 decisions
  date from after the record's first decision, and 17 of them name it.
  The misfits were met by generalizing luria ([CLAIM-tmp5fv9h](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp5fv9h.md);
  [LEAD-tmpkhcmm](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpkhcmm.md)).
- **Trusting provenance.** Attribution is reliable only against a
  transcript the contributor did not write ([CLAIM-tmpmacjk](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpmacjk.md); [LEAD-tmphmakt](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmphmakt.md)
  for a grading study).
- **Incomparable views.** A24's goal leaves out half of [CLAIM-535](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-535.md). Two
  evaluators facing one decision can need coarsest sufficient views that
  neither refines ([CLAIM-tmpu6e4u](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpu6e4u.md)).
- **Charts and cocycles.** Views whose correspondences all pass through
  one source meet the cocycle condition. Views related pair by pair can
  be inconsistent around a cycle, which corrects A59's transition map
  ([CLAIM-tmp6xzjy](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6xzjy.md), demonstrated).
- **"Geodesic" has no metric.** Six of nine steps in A61's routes are
  joins no entry states ([LEAD-tmpx9p54](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpx9p54.md)). A47's corrections were sorted
  after the fact ([LEAD-tmpf2lhq](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpf2lhq.md)). A53's recommendation is [LEAD-tmpsgkxe](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpsgkxe.md),
  and the prior art A21 names is [LEAD-tmpni7tu](../../record/leads.d/LEAD-tmpni7tu.md).
