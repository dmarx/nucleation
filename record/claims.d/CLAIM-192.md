# CLAIM-192: A multi-session argument's thesis can move without any participant arguing for the move, which is a case of an organized whole's course coming apart from its members' intentions

## The claim

[CLAIM-030](CLAIM-030.md): "An organized whole's operative intention is not the expectation
of its members' intentions; it is produced by systemic mechanisms that can
evolve apart from what the members intend." [CLAIM-177](CLAIM-177.md) records that in the
translation exchange "The thesis changed four times, and the first two
changes were never announced": [CLAIM-017](CLAIM-017.md) became [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), and then
[CLAIM-133](CLAIM-133.md), "without a turn arguing either move".

Read together, the exchange is an organized whole: an owner, a series of
assistant sessions with no memory of one another, and, later, a record. Its
operative thesis moved by a mechanism no member intended. Each draft
restated the argument, and the restatement carried the move. That is the
pattern [CLAIM-030](CLAIM-030.md) names, in a second domain.

List and Pettit ([THEORY-725](../theory.d/THEORY-725.md)) give the formal version of the possibility.
A group's judgments can depart from what its members judge on the same
proposition while being wholly fixed by the members' whole sets of
judgments.

The test that would bear on both claims is already stated. [CLAIM-186](CLAIM-186.md)
proposes changing the anchoring rate while the participants and pace stay
the same, and seeing whether drift changes. If it does, structure, not the
members alone, carries part of the argument's course. That is [CLAIM-030](CLAIM-030.md)'s
own defeater turned into an experiment, and it is the kind of intervention
[CLAIM-122](CLAIM-122.md) asks for: change the conditions while the competing preferences
stay fixed.

## What it does not say

- It does not say the thesis moved against the owner's will. The owner
  holds memory and veto, and an unannounced move may be the owner's own
  unrecorded change of mind. [CLAIM-177](CLAIM-177.md) records that the owner, not the
  assistant, reintroduced a rejected analogy. That is the defeater.
- It does not say the authoring collective is an agent. Whether it is one
  is [QUESTION-009](../questions.d/QUESTION-009.md), which is set aside. The members are unequal: one holds
  memory and veto, and the sessions couple only through the record and
  the owner.

## Note of 2026-10-19: the formal ground is the court-agenda result

The possibility cited above is [THEORY-725](../theory.d/THEORY-725.md)'s weak discontinuity. On the
court agenda under the premise-based procedure, two profiles give the
members the same verdicts on the conclusion, (yes, no, no), and the group
accepts the conclusion in one and rejects it in the other. In both, the
group's judgment set is a function of the members' whole sets. The claim
needs nothing more. [THEORY-127](../theory.d/THEORY-127.md), from which [THEORY-725](../theory.d/THEORY-725.md) was split, keeps the
general impossibility for proposition-wise rules, Propositions 2–3, which
the source cites without proof. This claim does not use it.

## Note of 2026-10-30: what holds it back

Its first clause is [CLAIM-177](CLAIM-177.md)'s finding, which is Active. Its second
clause is not shown. Nothing in the record separates an unannounced move
carried by restatement from the owner's own unrecorded change of mind,
which is its defeat condition's first clause and the doubt its summary
names. The intervention that would separate them, changing the anchoring
rate with the participants fixed ([CLAIM-186](CLAIM-186.md)), has not been run.
