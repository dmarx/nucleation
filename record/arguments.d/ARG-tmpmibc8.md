# ARG-tmpmibc8: Mind-wandering, read as Metzinger reads it, is an individual-scale case in which a capacity the agent keeps, veto over its own mental processes, loses and regains efficacy through a change in a condition of efficacy and not in the competing processes, so it is a further instance of the conditions of efficacy governing which regime acts, the first in the record for suspension in one mind

## The inference

[CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md): which of an agent's available regimes becomes effective depends
on maintained relations, not on the contents of the competing regimes.

Metzinger's case ([THEORY-tmpcq8bx](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpcq8bx.md), [THEORY-tmptbvmq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmptbvmq.md)). A person has the
capacity to suspend or end an ongoing train of thought, M-autonomy's veto.
Many hundreds of times a day, without noticing, they lose its use: an
episode of mind-wandering runs and "cannot be terminated at will"
([LIT-tmp23d7b](../literature.d/LIT-tmp23d7b.md), p. 6). The capacity is kept: "what we have lost is a
specific form of knowledge, and not the ability itself" ([LIT-tmp0w298](../literature.d/LIT-tmp0w298.md), p.
282). What changes is whether the capacity is represented in the
self-model, and "one can only deliberately and autonomously exert an
ability if that ability is explicitly represented" (p. 280). Control
returns when the representation returns, "caused by an unconscious event,
perhaps by chance" (p. 283). The 2017 chapter adds that deliberate thought
selects from the candidates mind-wandering supplies ([THEORY-tmp92dsq](../theory.d/THEORY-tmp92dsq.md)).

So a held regime, second-order mental control, gains and loses efficacy by
a change in the condition under which it can act, and the competing
regime, the stream of thought, is the same before and after. If that is
right, the case is [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s at the scale of one mind's thinking, and
its relation is [TERM-017](../terms.d/TERM-017.md)'s suspension, "when an applicable one may be
disabled", together with accessibility.

## Critical questions

- **In what respect are they alike, and is it the respect the conclusion
  needs?** Alike in that a capacity is possessed and not effective
  ([CLAIM-047](../claims.d/CLAIM-047.md)), and its efficacy turns on something other than the contents
  in competition. That is the respect [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) needs. The relation is
  suspension: veto is "the capacity to voluntarily suspend or inhibit an
  action" ([LIT-tmp23d7b](../literature.d/LIT-tmp23d7b.md), p. 4). [ARG-005](ARG-005.md) says jurisdiction and suspension
  "have only institutional examples"; this would be the first
  individual-scale one.
- **What relevant difference is there?** Three.
  1. **The relation is a representation.** [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s six relations hold
     among regimes; Metzinger's condition is the agent's model of having the
     capacity. Whether that is a relation of the will-organization or only
     tracks one is [QUESTION-tmp7czq0](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp7czq0.md), and Metzinger himself says in 2017
     that the data cannot decide ([LIT-tmppbt0s](../literature.d/LIT-tmppbt0s.md), pp. 13–14).
  2. **The onset may be content-driven.** Metzinger traces the start of an
     episode to "introspectively inaccessible goal representations",
     "postponed goal-states ... environmentally cued by goal-related
     stimuli" ([LIT-tmp23d7b](../literature.d/LIT-tmp23d7b.md), p. 13; [LIT-tmp0w298](../literature.d/LIT-tmp0w298.md), p. 275). That is [CLAIM-122](../claims.d/CLAIM-122.md)'s
     defeater: a change in a goal state predicting which process acts. Only
     the return is put down to something other than content.
  3. **Scale and object.** M-autonomy is control of mental processes, not
     of action-generating regimes in [TERM-019](../terms.d/TERM-019.md)'s sense. A train of thought is
     a regime only if mental behaviour counts as action-generating.
- **What transfers, and what does not?** The structure transfers: a kept
  capacity, cyclical loss and return of efficacy, and a named condition.
  The mechanism does not: the representation's causal role is open, and no
  intervention holding the competing thoughts fixed is reported.
- **Does changing R change anything else?** ([ARG-009](ARG-009.md)'s question, and
  [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md)'s defeated_if.) Unanswered. The test is Metzinger's H1 and H3
  ([LIT-tmppbt0s](../literature.d/LIT-tmppbt0s.md), p. 14): block or restore the epistemic agent model and see
  whether control ends or returns, with the task, the competing thoughts and
  an externally cued measure of the ability held fixed. Axelrod et al.
  (2015), which the chapter cites for H1, is not in the record.

## Verdict

Proposed. The case is the right shape for [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md) and would extend it to
suspension in one mind. It does not yet support it, because the
representation's role is undecided by its own author and the onset of an
episode has a content-driven explanation in the same papers.
