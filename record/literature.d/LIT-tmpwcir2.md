# LIT-tmpwcir2: Validation of Morality as Cooperation Questionnaire in Turkey, and Its Relation to Prosociality, Ideology, and Resource Scarcity

Onurcan Yilmaz, Mehmet Harma, Burak Doğruyol (2021), *European Journal of
Psychological Assessment* 37(2):149–160 — DOI-10.1027/1015-5759/a000627

## Key takeaways

- An independent Turkish team reproduces the MAC-Q's pattern: seven factors
  fit the Relevance items and not the Judgement items, so the authors
  recommend the Relevance subscale alone.
- The seven are highly intercorrelated, and a single higher-order
  "cooperation" factor also fits acceptably.

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, as one independent
cross-cultural validation of the MAC-Q ([LIT-tmp0moou](LIT-tmp0moou.md)), with the rest of the
morality-as-cooperation programme ([ADR-018](../decisions.d/ADR-018.md)). The owner's brief listed Curry
among its authors. He is not one: the authors are Yilmaz, Harma and
Doğruyol, all at Istanbul universities, and that independence is why this
validation was chosen.

[NOTE-tmp3alj2](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp3alj2.md) is the close reading of 2026-10-02, and it placed the work
**Active**. It is a careful, modest replication. It does not move [LIT-047](LIT-047.md).
Like the MAC-Q it has no non-cooperative item, so it says nothing about
whether purity, sex or harm are moralized. Its MFQ data show a
Turkish-sample latent correlation of .97 between Authority and Sanctity,
which is the only purity evidence in it. The correlation shows that
sanctity concerns in this sample are hard to separate from deference. It
does not show that they are cooperative.
