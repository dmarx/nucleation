# NOTE-tmp2ky8f: Hui (2017) — Cosmotechnics as Cosmopolitics

## Contribution

A programmatic thesis of technological pluralism, offered as the condition of a post-hegemonic cosmopolitics.

## Key results

1. The antinomy's antithesis: "there is no one single technology, but rather multiple cosmotechnics."
2. Cosmotechnics "is the unification of the cosmos and the moral through technical activities".
3. Confronting the Anthropocene needs "the bifurcation of technological futures by conceiving different cosmotechnics."
4. "an ontological pluralism can only be realized by reflecting on the question of technology".
5. His sketch "is not sufficient to be formulated into a program".

## Assumptions

Heidegger's account of modern technology as the standard to overcome; that cultures can systematize distinct cosmotechnics.

## Concepts

Cosmopolitics; technological antinomy; cosmotechnics; Qi and Dao.

## Bearing on the record

- **[CLAIM-tmpbypt7](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpbypt7.md)**: qualifies in direction, not mechanism. If technological futures can bifurcate, lock-in to one trajectory is not necessary; Hui gives no mechanism that keeps a non-adopter's options open.
- **The exchange's** use (A5, A6 §V, A11 §14: modernity need not follow one trajectory) is accurate as Hui's thesis, which he offers as a possibility.

## Limitations

Programmatic, by its own account; the book's historical case is unread.
