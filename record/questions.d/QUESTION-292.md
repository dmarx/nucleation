# QUESTION-292: What evidence would show that a maintaining link, and not a regime's content, keeps a disorder in place: does the efficacy of a treatment that targets the link count, or only change in the link that is specific and comes before the outcome?

## The question

[QUESTION-045](QUESTION-045.md) asks whether a disorder that outlasts its trigger is a
protective lock-in, and what would show that the maintaining link, such as
safety behaviour, and not the content of a fearful belief, keeps it in
place. A maintaining link is a theory of change. This question asks what
evidence bears on such a theory.

## The answer

[THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md), Active, states the standard, from both wings of CBT
(Salkovskis et al. and Hofmann and Hayes):

- **Efficacy does not count.** Every theory of change predicts that the
  treatment it informs works, so a treatment's working cannot tell a
  link account from a content account.
- **What counts** is change in the theorised process that is specific to
  it, comes before the outcome, and moves the outcome more than the
  rival's process does.
- **The disconfirming case** is a treatment that moves the process and
  leaves the outcome alone. A treatment that fails to move the process
  has not tested it.

Mediation evidence of this kind is necessary, not sufficient. Whether any
disorder meets the standard, and so is a protective lock-in, remains
[QUESTION-045](QUESTION-045.md)'s.

## Note of 2026-10-29: a trial that looked for the link

The RefraMED monograph, [LIT-1838](../literature.d/LIT-1838.md) ([NOTE-1421](../notes.d/NOTE-1421.md)), is a worked case of
the answer's two legs. Its causal mechanism analyses were meant as the
evidence [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md) asks for, and they meet neither leg. On specificity,
the mediators are sessions attended, alliance and DBT skills, of which
alliance and exposure are common factors and not RO DBT's theorised
process; the processes it names are reported only as differences between
arms. On precedence, mediator and outcome are both at 7 months, and the
planned analyses of temporal order are not reported. It does not count
against the theory either: the processes moved, and whether the outcome
moved through them was not estimated. [THEORY-053](../theory.d/THEORY-053.md) is noted the same day.
The answer is unchanged.
