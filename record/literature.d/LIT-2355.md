  bodily integrity, developing sexual boundaries and future genital

# LIT-2355: Genital Modifications in Prepubescent Minors: When May Clinicians Ethically Proceed?

The Brussels Collaboration on Bodily Integrity (2024). "Genital Modifications in Prepubescent Minors: When May Clinicians Ethically Proceed?" *The American Journal of Bioethics 25(7): 53-102*, Target Article. DOI-10.1080/15265161.2024.2353823.

## Key takeaways

Skimmed on 2026-11-12 ([NOTE-1799](../notes.d/NOTE-1799.md)). A sex-neutral, medical-necessity
standard for nonvoluntary genital procedures, grounded in the special social
meaning of "intimate" anatomy and a right held in trust, used to expose
inconsistency in hospital policies that protect intersex children but
continue routine circumcision and cosmetic hypospadias surgery.
