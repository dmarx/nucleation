# THEORY-1207: Russian military writers of 2015–2020 use 'hybrid war' for a category of war marked off by its objective, reshaping a target state's governance, geostrategic orientation and worldview, in which every action up to conventional military force is subordinate to an information campaign, and not, as in Western usage, for a set of means below the threshold of armed conflict

## Source

[LIT-023](../literature.d/LIT-023.md), read in full in [NOTE-012](../notes.d/NOTE-012.md). What the source measured is [THEORY-488](THEORY-488.md), which this entry extends.

## The claim

Clark argues that Russian military writing of the period, as an institution
and not a handful of authors, treats hybrid war as a type of war defined by
its objective, with conventional force inside it and subordinate to an
information campaign.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried word for word from [THEORY-488](THEORY-488.md) when that entry was narrowed. More readings of the same source cannot settle it.
