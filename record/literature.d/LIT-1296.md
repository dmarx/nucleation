# LIT-1296: Slow Feature Analysis as Variational Inference Objective

Schüler, M. & Wiskott, L. (2025). Slow Feature Analysis as Variational
Inference Objective. arXiv:2506.00580 [cs.LG]. Institute for Neural
Computation, Ruhr University Bochum. Preprint, one version.

Read in full in [NOTE-1015](../notes.d/NOTE-1015.md).

## Key takeaways

- **An identity, not a limit.** Under the paper's assumptions (prior
  N(z_{t−1}, I), encoder N(g(x_t), I) factorized per time step), the KL
  term of the ELBO equals ½Σ_t‖g(x_{t−1}) − g(x_t)‖² + (T − 1)d/2. The
  paper writes it without the constant, through a slip in Eq. 35 that is
  harmless for optimization.
- **The constraints become a reconstruction term.** SFA's unit-variance
  and decorrelation constraints are replaced by E_q log p_θ(x_t | z_t). The
  argument for the substitution is informal (§7): the fixed encoder noise
  forbids shrinking the features, and a bottleneck discourages redundancy.
- **Linear case.** With q(z | x) = N(Wᵀx + b, I) and p(x | z) = N(Vz + o, I)
  the paper gives the objective and the necessary condition VᵀV = WᵀĊW
  (Ċ the second moment of the step differences). It does not solve for
  the optimum. [THEORY-775](../theory.d/THEORY-775.md) does: on whitened input the optimum spans
  the slowest SFA directions with lag-one autocorrelation above ½; on raw
  input it is not SFA.
- **Turner and Sahani, as reported here only.** §2 says PSFA derives SFA
  from a linear Gaussian state-space model with linear emissions, that
  linear SFA is "the maximum-likelihood estimate of the emission model",
  with exact equivalence "only in the limit of zero observation noise and
  annealing of the internal dynamics towards white noise", the latter
  "mainly for scale correction", and (§4) that its prior is
  z_t ~ N(Λz_{t−1}, ·) with Λ diagonal and ordered. That is this paper's
  summary, not a reading of [LIT-1287](LIT-1287.md).

## Standing in the record

Filed as the open substitute for [LIT-1287](LIT-1287.md), for [QUESTION-217](../questions.d/QUESTION-217.md)'s
generative-assumptions item, [CLAIM-331](../claims.d/CLAIM-331.md) and [THEORY-733](../theory.d/THEORY-733.md). It answers the first
only partly: the paper's generative model puts slowness into the prior as
independent random walks, so slow latents are closed by assumption, and
the paper says nothing on predictive sufficiency. The record's own
derivations for the linear, noise-free PSFA-type model are in
[NOTE-1015](../notes.d/NOTE-1015.md).
