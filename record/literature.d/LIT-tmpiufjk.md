  Park et al. (2023), Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human
  Behavior. The paper builds LLM-driven agents that simulate believable
  everyday human behaviour in a Sims-like town of 25 agents. Its
  architecture has three parts: a memory stream (a timestamped list of
  natural-language observations), retrieval scored by recency, importance
  (LLM-rated poignancy) and relevance (embedding similarity), and
  reflection, in which the agent periodically asks itself high-level
  questions, retrieves memories and writes "insights" that cite the memories
  they came from; reflections go back into the stream and can be reflected
  on, forming trees. Planning sits on top. An interview-based ablation,
  ranked by human raters, finds each component adds to believability. Read
