# CODE: A perspective is held apart from its agents, its carriers and its governance

*A synthesis of the line organization-of-perspective, atlas scope 09 (ADR-146), which had none. Its components are the line's in-force entries (CLAIM-418, CLAIM-329, CLAIM-424, ARG-025, CASE-133, CASE-137, CASE-138, TERM-071, TERM-121) and six entries of neighbouring lines that the argument cannot be stated without: TERM-072, the term the line is stated in; CLAIM-417, which ARG-025 concludes and CASE-133 and CASE-137 illustrate; CLAIM-025, which CLAIM-424 rests on; and the three questions the line's claims leave open, QUESTION-260, QUESTION-264 and QUESTION-118. Most of the components carry distributed-agency first, and several are integrated again by that line's part syntheses (SYNTHESIS-047, SYNTHESIS-056). It adds nothing. Its standing is its components'.*

## The question

How is a perspective organized, held and changed, by an individual or a
collective, and how does an organization of perspectives keep or lose its
identity? The line takes "perspective" in TERM-072's first sense: an
organizational perspective, a dynamically realized regime of
interpretation, kept apart from a represented perspective and from a
phenomenal one (TERM-072). Whether such a perspective is anyone's
experience is not this line's question.

## The argument

**What the record explained by "self", it now proposes to explain by
perspectives.** The owner's recasting at U31 of the exchange of
2026-10-22 is that selves are "better described as 'organizational
perspectives'". It is filed as CLAIM-417: the dispositional, persisting,
self-governing and integrated senses of "self" are better explained by
organizational perspectives, provided a perspective, its character, its
lineage and its representations are kept apart, and the subject sense is
left as a separate question (CLAIM-417). The voice matters here, since
the line's thesis is the owner's and the rest of its argument was built
to make that thesis safe. What persuaded the exchange is that the burdens
of "self" separate in cases. Clive Wearing keeps skill and short-horizon
organization while autobiographical continuity and experienced ownership
of his past fail, so a perspective's realization continues while its
representation fails (CASE-137). The offered evidence is that
organizations matched in ordinary behaviour part under intervention
(ARG-025, from CLAIM-414 and the lab's runs).

**A perspective is not thereby an agent, and the boundaries need not
coincide.** Possessing, enacting or being shaped by a perspective does
not establish an agent. Several agents can share one perspective, one
agent can act through several, and a perspective can be reproduced with
no agent governing it. Where a perspective contributes to an agent's
activity, it enables and constrains the exercise of agency, and that
exercise can reproduce or transform it (CLAIM-418). This holds under both
of the record's criteria of agency (CLAIM-418, "Which agent"), and it is
what keeps CLAIM-417's distributed realization from being read as a
distributed agent. Whether agency always requires a perspective, and
whether a perspective can be agentic in its own right, are left open
(QUESTION-260). That the boundaries can come apart is held; when they
coincide, diverge and reorganize one another is QUESTION-264. Its
documented case is Challenger to Columbia, where a warning's standing
changed as it passed through the institution and a correction made in
the rules was not maintained (CASE-138; QUESTION-264, "Why it is a
question").

**A carrier persists by transmission, and what it carries may not.**
"Persist" has five senses that dissociate: state, dynamical, historical,
viable and reinstatable, and a claim should say which (TERM-071). For a
collective that replaces all its members, persistence is the historical
sense: the organization that produces it is causally transmitted, and an
organization of the same kind arising independently is not its
continuation (CLAIM-329). What must be transmitted is open (QUESTION-118),
and where every candidate is causally continuous the criterion gives no
verdict (CLAIM-329, note of 2026-11-20). The criterion is for a carrier.
American party realignment is the case that shows it: each party's name,
institutions and organization were transmitted while its coalition and
its stance on civil rights changed, some orientations migrating between
carriers (CASE-133). So organizational and perspectival persistence need
separate criteria (CASE-133; CLAIM-329, "What it does not say"), and
whether a perspective persists is CLAIM-417's question, which assigns it
to one of TERM-071's senses (CLAIM-417, sense 3).

**Governing a perspective is an achievement, not what a perspective is.**
Making the conditions that govern one's own interpretation, evaluation
and action available for effective examination and revision is a capacity
some perspective-bearing organizations have and others lack (CLAIM-424).
It is the third of CLAIM-025's separable objects, the capacity to
transform, turned on the standards of evaluation themselves (CLAIM-424,
resting on CLAIM-025). Two limits come with it. The capacity to revise
standards is not the capacity to revise them well, and reflexive
governance needs no single topmost reviser: mutually checking routes of
correction can carry it (CLAIM-424). NASA after Challenger made a
technical and a constitutional correction, and the second did not hold
through Columbia (CASE-138, read through CLAIM-424). Where a perspective
is shared by many organizations and governed by none, which CLAIM-418
allows, the same capacity can be held jointly by the organizations that
share it as cultural reflexivity, set apart from correcting violations of
standards the perspective leaves unquestioned (TERM-121).

## Where it stands

The distinctions are firm. CLAIM-418, CLAIM-329 and CLAIM-424 are in
force, as is TERM-071, and the three cases are documented, though some of
their sources were not reached (CASE-133, CASE-137). The thesis the
distinctions serve is not. CLAIM-417's defeater, that established
accounts such as habitus and transposable schemas explain the same
transitions as well with fewer assumptions, is live. ARG-025's evidence
reaches the first two of its grades, non-identifiability and a difference
under intervention, within synthetic constructions, and not the third, out-predicting those accounts at
matched complexity (ARG-025; CLAIM-417, note of 2026-11-20). TERM-121 is
Proposed and A's. So the record can say what a perspective is not, and
how its carrier persists. It cannot yet say that perspectives explain
selves, when the three boundaries coincide, or what a perspective's own
persistence is.

## Not stated on the record

- **A persistence criterion for a perspective, as against its carrier.**
  CASE-133 shows the two need separate criteria. CLAIM-329 gives the
  carrier's, and CLAIM-417 assigns a perspective's persistence to one of
  TERM-071's senses without saying which or by what test. No entry states
  the perspective's criterion.
- **Whether CLAIM-329's criterion reaches a perspective no one governs.**
  CLAIM-329 is stated for a collective that replaces its members.
  CLAIM-615 carries it to organized individuals in general, through
  changes of structure. No entry says whether a perspective shared across
  organizations, none of which governs it (CLAIM-418, TERM-121), persists
  by transmission in that sense, or what its members would be.
- **Whether a perspective survives the reflexive revision of its own
  standards.** CLAIM-424 says some bearers can revise the standards that
  govern their interpretation, and the line's question asks how an
  organization of perspectives keeps or loses its identity. No entry
  says whether such a revision continues the perspective or replaces it,
  or that CASE-138's constitutional correction was a change of
  perspective. Stated as a claim, the link would turn on what must be
  transmitted (QUESTION-118), which is open, so it cannot yet be tested
  without fixing that slot.
- **That CASE-137 bears on the carrier's persistence.** CASE-137 is read
  through CLAIM-417, as realization continuing while representation
  fails. No entry reads it against CLAIM-329 or TERM-071, that is, says
  which of the five senses Wearing's preserved organization has.

## What it does not say

That perspectives are agents, subjects or selves; that persons or
self-models are unreal (CLAIM-417, "What it does not say"); that
transmission is enough for being a self, an agent or one system
(CLAIM-329); that reflexive governance is good governance or legitimate
(CLAIM-424); or anything about phenomenal perspective (TERM-072, sense 3).
Two of the line's entries are carried further by claims of other lines:
CLAIM-329's criterion is generalized past collectives by CLAIM-615, and
CLAIM-424 is a premise of CLAIM-545's account of freedom, which
will-organization's SYNTHESIS-041 integrates. CLAIM-610 rests on both.
These are those lines' arguments, not this one's.
