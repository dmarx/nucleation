# NOTE-087: Kerr County Commissioners' Court transcript, 27 June 2016

## Contribution

This is a verbatim record of what the Kerr County court said about a proposed flood early-warning system in June 2016. It records the cost figures, the funding partners contemplated, the framing of the question, and the members' attitudes. It also records the day's other county business.

## Key insight

The court did not decide anything about flood warning at this meeting. The subject arose only as a commissioner's report and as a side question about a reimbursement fund. The committee's framing, as Moser described it, began from whether any change was needed. He also invoked risk mitigation and drownings. Any link between this meeting and the July 2025 Guadalupe River flood is inference, and it is not made here. The document does not mention it and could not.

## Assumptions

Authorities and premises of the proceeding:
- The Texas county commissioners' court acts by recorded vote on posted agenda items.
- Reports under item 5.1 are informational; no action was taken.
- The cost figures are Moser's own ballparks. He sourced them only loosely: "We've got a ballpark", and "Comal County spent about $320,000, I think".

## Key results

What the record shows, by agenda item. All votes were 4–0.

- **Visitors and comments (tr. 5–10).**
  - An Animal Services "clear the shelter" event.
  - Chief Deputy Barton named chief deputy of the year.
  - The Wounded Warrior event.
  - Reeves urged fireworks caution because heavy rain had grown "a lot of good grass" that "can turn dry real quick".
- **Jail population (tr. 12–13).** 185 inmates, with a possible need to "ship some to Fredericksburg".
- **Items 1.2 and 1.3 (tr. 13–28).**
  - Road & Bridge and Engineering reorganisations and job descriptions were passed to a workshop after Baldwin objected that duties were being delegated.
  - Letz proposed funding an engineering assistant from the East Kerr project grant.
- **Item 1.4 (tr. 28–35).** Right-of-way deeds for widening School Lane in Hunt were approved, not to exceed $5,000 from line 15-611-580. This was subject to the County Attorney's review of Letz's concern about work on private property.
- **Item 1.5 (tr. 35–43).** The TAC Risk Control Reimbursement application was approved. The funds were said to be about $50,000, earmarked for a fire alarm system, a restraint chair, three defibrillators and blood-borne-pathogen testing.
  - Moser asked whether the flood warning system could qualify.
  - Lantz: "No, Sir. That this is strictly to do with workers comp" and public liability (tr. 37).
- **Item 1.6 (tr. 43–46).** TAC liability coverage renewal.
- **Items 1.7 and 1.9 (tr. 46–50).** Wounded Warrior resolutions ratified; Bridgit Giedeman appointed to the Child Services Board.
- **Item 1.8 (tr. 50–53).** GrantWorks was selected for procurement services on the Hazard Mitigation Application and Plan as the only respondent. The agreement itself was to be executed on 11 July (Mrs. Grinstead).
- **Items 1.10–1.12 (tr. 53–61).**
  - A postage-meter lease.
  - Recycling trailer repairs, not to exceed $500.
  - Moser's motion for department liaisons, withdrawn after Letz objected.
- **Item 1.13 (tr. 61–66), Castlecomb.**
  - Status only; no resolution was proposed.
  - Weekly monitoring still showed contamination "potentially into Silver Creek and into the Guadalupe River".
  - A letter to Rep. Murr led to a letter to the TCEQ executive director and the Attorney General, and TCEQ acted quickly.
  - The operator has "been charged with a felony".
  - The long-term fix is likely connection to City sewer.
- **Item 1.14 (tr. 66–92), LED retrofit.**
  - A vendor proposal: $311,457 for 12 buildings, with claimed annual savings of about $123,000 and a positive cash flow of $53,358 per year on 5-year financing.
  - The auditor questioned the hours-of-use assumptions.
  - Reeves and Letz wanted it bid; Moser was named lead on an RFP.
- **Items 4.1–4.5 (tr. 92–94).** Bills, 15 budget amendments and the Treasurer's May report were approved.
- **Item 5.1 (tr. 95–98), flood warning.** The figures and exchanges are as given in the summary and corrections above.
  - Baldwin mentioned "a baby flood a couple weeks ago" and the phone chain ("the Jones call the Smiths, and the Smiths call Camp Rio Vista").
  - Moser named the audiences: "the general public ... and then the camps".
  - The County Attorney asked that the item go on the next agenda after Thursday's meeting; Moser agreed.
- **Remaining reports and adjournment (tr. 98–101).** A CSU transport update; Letz to speak at UGRA's water symposium; adjourned.

## Corrections to the seeded skim

- **Moser's "going-in position" was the committee's opening question, not his conclusion.** The dossier quotes him as "why change anything... maybe we don't need to do a thing", which, unqualified, reads as his settled view. The full passage (tr. 97) continues:
  > "I think the going in position is that we don't need to change anything, and is there a need to improve what we have. And if there's a need to improve how much is improved. And what the options for doing that and what it would cost. And I think the first thing to do is say why change anything. It worked this long and maybe we don't need to do a thing. And then it gets into the thing we talk about earlier today, and that's risk mitigation. And you know there's still people drowned and you know --"

  He closes with "the question is do we need to do anything. And what do we want to do and what can we afford" (tr. 98).
- **Baldwin's siren remark carries a joking tail.** The full line is "The thought of our beautiful Kerr County having these damn sirens going off in the middle of night, I'm going to have to start drinking again to put up with y'all" (tr. 97). He also asked whom a warning is for: "these crazy people from Houston that build homes right down on the water?"
- **Moser said the item "It's in for this next budget year"** (tr. 95), and that the first step, a grant administrator, "would cost nothing" (tr. 95–96). The dossier omitted both.
- **The TAC reimbursement exchange is at tr. 37, under item 1.5.** Staff (Mrs. Lantz) said the approximately $50,000 was "strictly to do with workers comp" and public liability. The dossier has this right.
- **New: the certificate is dated before the meeting.** It is dated "this the 21st day of June, A.D. 2016" (tr. 102), six days before the meeting it certifies. It is apparently a clerical error in the record.
