# THEORY-1800: DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus (2010), meta-analysing 23 studies of shared team mental models, find that the similarity of members' mental models predicts team process only when it is measured in a way that represents the structure of knowledge (for example similarity ratings analysed with Pathfinder), not when measured with rating scales or within-group agreement, while it predicts team performance under every measurement approach

## The account

DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus (2010), meta-analysing 23 studies of shared team mental models, find
that the similarity of members' mental models predicts team process only when it is measured in a
way that represents the structure of knowledge (for example similarity ratings analysed with
Pathfinder), not when measured with rating scales or within-group agreement, while it predicts team
performance under every measurement approach.
