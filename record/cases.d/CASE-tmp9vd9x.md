# CASE-tmp9vd9x: The aquifer with privately owned recycling: two households each need four units of water service from an aquifer that sustains six units of extraction, so every pair of the three demands can be met and the three cannot; recycling that adds one unit of service each meets all three at three units of extraction apiece; with the recycler owned by one household, which can cut the other off and threatens to unless it yields voting authority, the physical conflict is solved and a dependence is turned into a claim to govern

## The case

From A93 §6 and A97 VI of the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse"
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6afcb46d-83a8-83e9-9c85-4a0c3604405b).
Households A and B share an aquifer. q_A, q_B are extraction; y_A, y_B
usable water service. Each household's stipulated basic requirement is four
units of service; the aquifer sustains six units of extraction a period.

**Stage 1, no recycling** (y = q). Demands d_A: y_A ≥ 4, d_B: y_B ≥ 4,
d_s: q_A + q_B ≤ 6.

| Set | Witness | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| {d_A, d_B} | q = (4, 4) | yes |
| {d_A, d_s} | q = (4, 2) | yes |
| {d_B, d_s} | q = (2, 4) | yes |
| {d_A, d_B, d_s} | needs q_A + q_B ≥ 8 > 6 | no |

The compatibility complex is the hollow triangle: every pair a face, the
triple the one minimal conflict.

**Stage 2, recycling.** Each household gets one unit of service without
further extraction, y_i = q_i + 1. With q = (3, 3), y = (4, 4) and
q_A + q_B = 6: all three demands are met. A93 §6: "**The technology
expands the set of jointly realizable legitimate demands.**" The demands
are unchanged: "We do not change what the households are owed."

**Stage 3, reachability** (A93 §6). The technology "costs more than either
household can individually finance", while the community could: "affordable
collectively yet unreachable through the available financing and
decision-making procedures."

**Stage 4, ownership and leverage** (A97 VI). A owns the recycler and can
terminate B's access on short notice; the council governs extraction; a
contract gives B access (d_5), and members have management-participation
rights (d_4). A then threatens to revoke access unless B accepts a
different allocation of voting authority. "A's commitment to operating the
technology remains. The technology continues functioning. The groundwater
remains sustainable." What has changed is "**the transformation of
dependency into claimed authority**".

A97 VI keeps four judgments apart: rights legitimacy (may A impose the
terms?), demand compatibility (can all five demands be met?), commitment
fidelity (are undertakings kept and revised appropriately?) and
organizational adequacy (can the arrangement deliver under disturbance and
strategy?). A100 §10 adds three candidate principles for the recycler: P1
unrestricted exclusion, P2 compulsory uncompensated access, P3 protected
access with compensation and governance; "We cannot establish from the
description alone that P3 is the only reasonable principle."

## What it can show

- **[CLAIM-586](../claims.d/CLAIM-586.md)** (Active): a set outside the complex under one
  architecture can enter under another. Stage 1 to 2 is structural
  frustration removed by changing the architecture, with the reference set
  fixed ([CLAIM-361](../claims.d/CLAIM-361.md)). [CASE-212](CASE-212.md) is the contrast: there more budget never
  helps.
- **[CLAIM-560](../claims.d/CLAIM-560.md)** (Active): stage 3, a configuration feasible to hold and
  not reachable by the procedures available.
- **[CLAIM-690](../claims.d/CLAIM-690.md)** (Proposed): stage 4. The magnitude of B's dependence is
  the same whoever owns the recycler; who controls the terms of supply
  decides whether it is A's hold on B. [CLAIM-682](../claims.d/CLAIM-682.md) and [CLAIM-669](../claims.d/CLAIM-669.md) (Proposed)
  are the neighbouring entries on presumptive domination and exploitation
  through access.
- **[CLAIM-tmpnb4v4](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpnb4v4.md)**: stage 4 separates rights, demands and commitments in
  one arrangement. The demands stay jointly satisfiable and A's
  commitment holds, while A lacks the authority it claims.
- **[QUESTION-tmpkqvxv](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpkqvxv.md)**: whether the recycler's existence changes which of
  P1–P3 could reasonably be rejected, and not only which demands can be
  met.

The four judgments of A97 VI are not those of A74 §9 ([CLAIM-tmpx1g4r](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpx1g4r.md)):
they replace jurisdiction with rights legitimacy and add commitment
fidelity.

Being stipulated with illustrative numbers, it shows possibility only:
that capability expansion and a new domination can come in one step. It
does not show how often they do.
