# CLAIM-870: Taken as one system by the gap-isolated block test, the record does not individuate the Game of Life block, and the eater only at a one-generation horizon below a threshold Life does not set, so the Life constructions do not meet the first half of what would answer the achievement question and need another substrate or another criterion

**Reading (b), an alternative to [CLAIM-871](CLAIM-871.md).**

## The claim

[QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md)'s "Why it is open here" names the record's individuation
claims as those that "say when a form is one system and how long it
lasts", citing [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) and [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md). On this reading "a form the
record individuates" is a form that passes [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md). [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) is the
record's proposed answer to [QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md), and its duration clause is the
record's account of how long a form lasts.

Applied in Life ([CASE-311](../cases.d/CASE-311.md); [CLAIM-869](CLAIM-869.md)):

- **The block fails.** Read as sensitivity to single flips, its inside
  coupling is zero at every horizon tested, and its border's is 0.42 to
  0.75. In Gosper's gun the figures are 0.06 against 0.38. No threshold
  passes it. Read on realized interaction in an empty surround, nothing
  interacts at all, so every region passes and the test picks out nothing.
- **The eater is undecided.** Its inside coupling beats its border's by
  about 3.5 times at one generation and not at five generations or more.
  [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) is relative to a target, and the horizon is part of the
  target, so the verdict needs a horizon and a threshold τ. Life sets
  neither.

So by this criterion the block of [CASE-260](../cases.d/CASE-260.md) is not a form the record
individuates, and the eater of [CASE-259](../cases.d/CASE-259.md) is one only at one generation,
under an assumption about τ. The Life constructions show creation,
stabilization and constitutive role coming apart, but for a form the
record does not individuate. [LEAD-069](../leads.d/LEAD-069.md)'s "if not, the constructions need a
different substrate" then applies. Two ways out:

- **Another substrate**, one with an interaction matrix and a computable
  Occam factor, where a localized form can pass the gap test, as in
  [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md)'s own linear setting. The localized structures of
  [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md)'s notes (Knobloch's pinned fronts, Zelnik et al.'s fairy
  circles) are candidates. Neither has been tested against [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md).
- **Another criterion**, which is reading (a), [CLAIM-871](CLAIM-871.md).

**What would favour it.** [QUESTION-281](../questions.d/QUESTION-281.md)'s explicit pointer to [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md) and
[QUESTION-035](../questions.d/QUESTION-035.md). On this reading, too, the result bears back on [CLAIM-157](CLAIM-157.md).
The block is a form whose cells compensate for one another and that fails
the test, a case of the kind its `defeated_if` names.

the owner's call.
