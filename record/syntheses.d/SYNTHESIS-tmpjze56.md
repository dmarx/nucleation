# SYNTHESIS-tmpjze56: Freedom of will comes in degrees along separable dimensions, compared by inclusion of what an agent can viably reach, and in the operational sense the record proposes it is an agent's governance of its own governability; sensitivity and controllability fall short of it, self-formation is the production of a new distinction or channel that outlasts its occasion, and explaining efficacy does not settle whose the will is

*A part account of the will-organization line ([ADR-083](../decisions.d/ADR-083.md), [ADR-084](../decisions.d/ADR-084.md)); the line's account is built over it.*

## The account

**Degrees, not a scalar.** [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md) (the owner's U9 of 2026-10-17, worked
out by A9, A10 and A15; Active) rests on [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md), [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) and [CLAIM-345](../claims.d/CLAIM-345.md):
freedom of will and autonomy come in degrees along several dimensions,
compared "by inclusion of what they can viably reach under matched
conditions", a partial order. [CLAIM-345](../claims.d/CLAIM-345.md) (A9 and A12; demonstrated, Active):
equal controllability rank does not fix the cost of control. [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md) (the
owner's U11, worked out by A11; Active) rests on [CLAIM-079](../claims.d/CLAIM-079.md) and [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md):
Sen's opportunity aspect is what viable reachability can stand for only
through a map to valued functionings, and will-organization gives a causal
account of part of the process aspect.

**Sensitivity is not control.** [CLAIM-542](../claims.d/CLAIM-542.md) (demonstrated, Active) rests on
[CLAIM-345](../claims.d/CLAIM-345.md): sensitivity does not fix the allocation or authorship of
control. [CLAIM-533](../claims.d/CLAIM-533.md) (the owner's U16 and U20; Active) rests on [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md),
[CLAIM-345](../claims.d/CLAIM-345.md) and [CLAIM-542](../claims.d/CLAIM-542.md) and bears on [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): the sensitivity formal
chaos gives Kane's self-forming actions magnifies the agent's efforts,
fluctuations and outside inputs alike.

**What normative efficacy needs.** [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) (the owner's synthesis at review
of 2026-10-31; Active) uses [TERM-064](../terms.d/TERM-064.md), [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) and [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md) and bears on
[QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md): legibility, volitional efficacy and correctability, any of
which can fail while the others hold. [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) (A33; demonstrated,
Active) rests on [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md): a policy acceptable in every circumstance exists
exactly when the circumstances an observation cannot tell apart share an
acceptable action, so which distinctions must be legible depends on the
policy. [CLAIM-622](../claims.d/CLAIM-622.md) (A58; demonstrated, Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md): two
organizations can each be minimally adequate while drawing incomparable
distinctions.

**Governance of governability.** [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md) (A26's wording at the owner's
request for the strong form; Proposed) rests on [CLAIM-354](../claims.d/CLAIM-354.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md),
[CLAIM-485](../claims.d/CLAIM-485.md), [CLAIM-424](../claims.d/CLAIM-424.md), [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md), [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), [CLAIM-404](../claims.d/CLAIM-404.md) and [CLAIM-533](../claims.d/CLAIM-533.md) and bears
on [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md) and [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md): freedom of will in the operational sense
is "an agent's governance of its own governability". Its "Admission"
section gives the separating case (two agents matched in present
organization, one manipulated) and closes "relevant" by [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md).
[CLAIM-544](../claims.d/CLAIM-544.md) (the reader's draft at the owner's U31; Proposed) rests on
[CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md) and [CLAIM-170](../claims.d/CLAIM-170.md): governing one's governability is in part governing
one's own individuation. [CLAIM-610](../claims.d/CLAIM-610.md) (A7, in the supposed voice; Proposed;
distributed-agency first) rests on [CLAIM-545](../claims.d/CLAIM-545.md), [CLAIM-027](../claims.d/CLAIM-027.md), [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md) and three
claims of other lines: if the record's hypotheses held, agency and autonomy
would be further organizations of one individuated unit. [CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md)
(Proposed; pragmatic-transport first) rests on [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) among six premises:
concepts are individuated over time by the transmission criterion, so in
reflexive social systems meaning and will remake each other. [CLAIM-610](../claims.d/CLAIM-610.md) and
[CLAIM-620](../claims.d/CLAIM-620.md) each carry an "Admission" section.

**Self-formation.** [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) (A33's account, sharpened by the reader;
Active) rests on [CLAIM-025](../claims.d/CLAIM-025.md), [CLAIM-347](../claims.d/CLAIM-347.md), [CLAIM-522](../claims.d/CLAIM-522.md), [CLAIM-540](../claims.d/CLAIM-540.md) and [CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) and
uses [TERM-099](../terms.d/TERM-099.md): self-formation is the production, by the agent's own
activity on a discrepancy, of a maintained new distinction or channel that
outlasts it and serves a class of later ones; it is told apart from a
learned action by transfer and from outside installation by interventions
on its production. [TERM-099](../terms.d/TERM-099.md) (Active) refines [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md) and fixes the sense;
its summary says [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) "is the thesis that it is what [TERM-023](../terms.d/TERM-023.md)'s
transformation dimension produces". [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) (Active) illustrates [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md),
a construction in which the first test passes and the second is narrowed
to interventions made during formation. [ARG-060](../arguments.d/ARG-060.md) (Proposed) rests on
[CLAIM-535](../claims.d/CLAIM-535.md) and concludes [CLAIM-532](../claims.d/CLAIM-532.md) from [CASE-194](../cases.d/CASE-194.md): a new distinction and a new
channel are separate repairs. [CLAIM-540](../claims.d/CLAIM-540.md) (A13; disclaimed, demonstrated,
Active) rests on [CLAIM-414](../claims.d/CLAIM-414.md): the persistence a Dirichlet–categorical
hierarchy gives is an observer's learning, "not a change in what selects".

**Whose the will is.** [CLAIM-022](../claims.d/CLAIM-022.md) (the paper's, Section 7.2; disclaimed,
Active) uses [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md): explaining practical efficacy does not settle whose
the will is. [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md) (Active) separates formation, maintenance and source
provenance, after [CASE-191](../cases.d/CASE-191.md) found that only maintenance provenance can be
read off a present organization. [QUESTION-071](../questions.d/QUESTION-071.md) (Open) asks whether a
will-organization can be manipulated with nothing in its present
structure showing it; [QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md) (A29's; Open) asks what makes a revision
the continuation of the agent's will. [CLAIM-356](../claims.d/CLAIM-356.md) (the owner's U35;
Active; learned-structure first) uses [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md): an objective that
originates outside a learning system can become a goal of the training
organization.
