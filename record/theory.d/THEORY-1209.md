# THEORY-1209: In genetically identical E. coli, a cell's swarming potential is a transient inherited state: its descendants share it for about four generations and lose it by about seven, and it tracks the cell's iron status rather than a bistable switch

## Source

[LIT-853](../literature.d/LIT-853.md), read in full in [NOTE-657](../notes.d/NOTE-657.md). What the source measured is [THEORY-179](THEORY-179.md), which this entry extends.

## The claim

The authors argue that a single E. coli cell's swarming potential is a
transient physiological state set by its iron status, passed to its
descendants for a few generations and then relaxed, and not a bistable
switch.

## What would settle it

The promote_when, carried word for word from [THEORY-179](THEORY-179.md) when that entry was narrowed. More readings of the same source cannot settle it.
