# LIT-tmpqx6sc: The Danger Of Superhuman AI Is Not What You Think

Shannon Vallor (2024). "The Danger Of Superhuman AI Is Not What You Think". *Noema* (Berggruen Institute), 23 May 2024.

## Key takeaways

- The ideology "collapses the distinction between our conscious minds and the mechanical tools we’ve built to mirror them."
- "OpenAI’s AGI bait-and-switch wipes anything that does not count as economically valuable work from the definition of intelligence."
- After Borgmann: "it is precisely when a technology has nearly supplanted a vital domain of human meaning that we are able to feel and mourn what has been taken from us."
