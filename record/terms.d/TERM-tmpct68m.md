# TERM-tmpct68m: fidelity, as graded, directed transport distortion

## Definition

The owner, U20, quoting the exact intertwining ΦA_i = B_iΦ: "I think presenting
this as an equivalence might be too constraining. I agree that there should be
some transport map that at least gets to within some eps, but that's just a
similarity constraint, not an equivalence. This is why the game 'telephone'
works." A50: local discrepancy δ_i(s) = d_t(Φ(A_i(s)), B_i(Φ(s))) with
E_μ[δ_i] ≤ ε_i, per operation; directed distortion D(u⇝v) ≠ D(v⇝u); fidelity
of a stochastic translation process F(T; u, c) = E_(v∼T)[Sim_O(u, v)]. "define
fidelity as a graded quantity, not an equivalence relation."

## What it is not

Not an equivalence, approximate or otherwise ([TERM-tmpvpzy0](TERM-tmpvpzy0.md)): "Even approximate
equivalence may be the wrong organizing concept if the phenomenon we're
interested in is *graded preservation under successive transformations*."
Equivalence is recovered "as a special case—for example, zero distortion under a
suitably separating observation family". Manuscript §12: "stochastic transport
generalizes exact equivalence to graded fidelity."
