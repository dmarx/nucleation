# LIT-tmpliv2g: Temporary Protection and Technology Adoption: Evidence from the Napoleonic Blockade

Juhász, R. (2018). Temporary protection and technology adoption: Evidence from the Napoleonic blockade. *American Economic Review* 108(11): 3339–3376. doi:10.1257/aer.20151730.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmp4zjzy](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp4zjzy.md). Page numbers are the December 2015 draft's.*

- **Short run.** The interquartile difference in the blockade's trade-cost
  shock predicts extra spinning capacity about equal to mean capacity in
  1812, with no pre-trend and no effect on wool or leather (pp. 27–32).
- **Long run.** One more spindle in 1812 meant 2–3 more in 1840 and 5–6 in
  1887; firms were more productive in 1840; industrial value added was
  higher in 1860 only (pp. 36–38).
- **Weak instrument.** "The Kleibergen-Papp F-statistic for the first stage
  is generally below 10" (p. 35).
- **Not a policy.** Napoleon "remained highly ambivalent of developments in
  the cotton industry" (p. 24), and after the wars "tariff and non-tariff
  trade barriers were put in place between the two countries" (p. 33).

## Why this is here

The exchange cited it at turn U16 as evidence that a temporary, crisis-born measure can leave a lasting structure behind it. The abstract supports that, with the limit it states: the advantage faded by the later nineteenth century.

**Corrected 2026-10-24, from the reading.** The lasting structure is the
industry's location and productivity, not the protection, and the claim's
"temporary tariff" is not what happened: protection came from a war
blockade aimed at Britain, and post-war tariffs kept France protected, so
the within-France results compare regions under continued national
protection. The reading supports [CLAIM-tmp28sti](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp28sti.md)'s capability-building
contrast and says nothing about whether protection maintained itself,
though a petition by large spinners for a complete ban on English cloth
(p. 13, fn. 7) shows the constituency forming. Filed from it:
[THEORY-tmpkjber](../theory.d/THEORY-tmpkjber.md).
