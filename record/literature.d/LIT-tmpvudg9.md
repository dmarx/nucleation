# LIT-tmpvudg9: Interference traps waves in an open system: bound states in the continuum

Sadreev, A. F. (2021). Interference traps waves in an open system: bound states in the continuum. *Reports on Progress in Physics* 84: 055901. doi:10.1088/1361-6633/abefb9. [ARXIV-2011.01221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01221).

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-tmpqaps1](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpqaps1.md), Skimmed.*

- **Definition** (§1): a BIC "lies inside the continuum and coexists with
  extended waves, but it remains perfectly confined without any radiation".
- **Criterion** (§3): decoupling from all channels of the continuum "is a
  necessary and sufficient condition for a resonance state to be the BIC".
- **Friedrich–Wintgen** (§3): one state is "decoupled from the continuum
  owing to exact destructive interference of leaking eigenmodes", and its
  partner "acquires maximal coupling with the continuum".
- **Signature** (§3): "maximal transmittance coalesces with the maximal
  reflectance (collapse of Fano resonance)".
- **Idealization** (§1): practical BICs are "quasi-BICs".

## Standing in the record

Filed as the read neighbour of Hsu et al. (2016), [LIT-tmp83mpa](LIT-tmp83mpa.md), for the
definition and mechanisms only. A's description holds for two of its four
mechanisms. Its bearing, from the reader's reading of the scattering
formula rather than any sentence of the review: a mode with zero coupling
W is invisible in the scattering matrix and cannot be excited from
outside. So two interiors can present the same boundary relation
([TERM-090](../terms.d/TERM-090.md)). The state is inaccessible rather than masked ([TERM-118](../terms.d/TERM-118.md)).
