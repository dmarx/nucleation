# THEORY-tmp7qfc2: Lee Tin Wah and colleagues show, from AFM snapshots of Rothemund's tall rectangle adsorbed out of solution along a slow cooling ramp, that with 52 nM of each staple folding nucleates at the top and bottom ends between about 65 and 55 °C and grows toward the centre, and that raising the central staples to 670 nM while lowering the outermost edge-row staples to 1.2 nM makes folding nucleate at the centre instead, which they attribute to the concentration dependence of staple melting temperatures under thermodynamic rather than kinetic control

## Source

Lee Tin Wah, David, Rudiuk, Baigl and Estévez-Torres (2016), [LIT-tmps9gx3](../literature.d/LIT-tmps9gx3.md);
read in [NOTE-tmp059hz](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp059hz.md).

## What was actually shown

- **Edges first, under standard conditions** (abstract; Fig. 2): "In
  standard conditions folding was initiated at the edges of the rectangle
  and progressed towards the center"; folding "starts at well-defined
  positions". This could have come out random, and the images say it did
  not.
- **Centre first, under the altered concentrations** (Fig. 5): with the
  central and border staples "changed from 52 nM to, respectively, 670 and
  1.2 nM ... the folding pathway was dramatically changed".
- **The attribution** (Conclusion): "the pathway is primarily defined by
  the distribution of the melting temperature of staples bound to the
  scaffold". Supporting it: a quench from 95 °C to 57 °C still gives
  well-formed rectangles, and order-of-magnitude melting-temperature
  estimates match the direction of the measured shifts.

## What this does not say

- That the final object is unchanged under the altered allocation: the
  paper reports only "normal rectangles" on a rugged surface and a reduced
  bulk signal, with no yield.
- That a fixed stock was reallocated: the total rose and the ramp halved
  in rate.
- Which of the two changes, centre up or edges down, moved the nucleus.
- Anything about 3D designs or global traps, the subject of [THEORY-1398](THEORY-1398.md).
- That the mechanism is settled: the model is single-staple mass action
  without cooperativity, and the estimates are order-of-magnitude.
