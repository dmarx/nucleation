# CASE-029: The dynamical-fidelity research programme of the outlines (A35, A38, A40)

## The case

- **A35 §6.** Three translations and the original; manipulate wording, footing
  (the attributed speaker: "a fellow reveler, a concerned friend, or a
  disapproving outsider"), interpretive order and initial state. Compare "a
  classical state-space model with stochastic updates, a constrained
  quantum-instrument model, and a general contextual measurement model". The
  caveat: "forcing a participant to make an explicit judgment is itself an
  intervention."
- **A38 §IX.** Stimuli to "Include semantically matched but pragmatically
  divergent versions and pragmatically matched but lexically divergent
  versions." Falsifiers include "No reproducible order dependence" and
  "Dynamical metrics add no predictive value."
- **A40 §XI–XII.** A worked case study whose annotations are hypotheses, not
  facts about the texts; pilot validation, measurement invariance, classical
  baselines primary. The order-effect statistic
  Δ_(A,B) = d(P(Y | A→B), P(Y | B→A)), with the confounds "framing effects,
  memory, learning, task demands, or measurement disturbance". Falsification
  criteria (§12.7): no added predictive value over static equivalence; order
  effects not reproducible; "Correspondence maps fail to generalize beyond their
  estimation tasks"; process models explain no extra variance; quantum models
  no better than constrained classical ones.

## What it can show

If run, it tests [CLAIM-052](../claims.d/CLAIM-052.md), [CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md), [CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) and [CLAIM-009](../claims.d/CLAIM-009.md). None of it has been run. The
manuscript's §10 keeps the classical-first stance and the separation of context
effects from contextuality. The confound list and the held-out evaluation of
correspondence maps are not in it.

Outline v3 (A48 §15.5) restated the failure conditions: no stable framing
effects across paraphrases; order effects explained by prompt artifacts; no
added prediction from trajectories; Φ failing on held-out tasks; TTT giving no
transferable behaviour; complex models no better than simple baselines. U19's
instruction to withhold hedging removed the section ([CLAIM-095](../claims.d/CLAIM-095.md)). A49 said
the conditions belonged in the methods "as **predefined decision rules**", but
outline v4 (A52) lists only outcomes.
