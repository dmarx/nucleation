# LIT-2759: The Declaration of Independence

Second Continental Congress (1776). *The Declaration of Independence*, adopted 4 July 1776. Transcript at the U.S. National Archives.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading ([NOTE-2057](../notes.d/NOTE-2057.md)).*

- "endowed by their Creator with certain unalienable Rights, that among
  these are Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness."
- "Governments are instituted among Men, deriving their just powers from
  the consent of the governed".
- "it is the Right of the People to alter or to abolish it", but
  "Governments long established should not be changed for light and
  transient causes".
