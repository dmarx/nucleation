# THEORY-tmpgs5bb: Rupe and Crutchfield define local causal states as classes of past lightcones with the same distribution over future lightcones, domains as regions where the resulting state field is invariant under a group of space and time shifts, and coherent structures as localized, persistent deviations from domains, and show on elementary cellular automata that the domains match the domain-particle decomposition exactly and its particles largely

## The account

At each spacetime point of a field produced by a system with finite propagation speed, take the past lightcone and the future lightcone. Past lightcones with the same conditional distribution over future lightcones are equivalent. The classes are local causal states, minimal sufficient statistics for local prediction. Labelling each point by its class gives a state field. A domain is a region where shifts in space and time generate a symmetry group over the state field, even when they do not over the raw field (hidden symmetry, as in ECA 18). A coherent structure is a contiguous nondomain region, spatially bounded by domain states and persisting within the future lightcone. On ECAs 54, 110 and 18 the local-causal-state domains have the same periods and recurrence times as the domain-particle decomposition's, and the structures largely coincide with its particles.

## What it does not show

- That local causal states identify causal (interventional) structure. The authors say they "do not detect direct causal relationships".
- Anything for continuous-valued, noisy or learned fields, or in more than one spatial dimension in practice.
- That the unsupervised filter isolates structures in stochastic domains without extra hand-applied steps.
- How structures defined this way relate to topological charge or to a structure's functional role.
