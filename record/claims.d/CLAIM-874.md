# CLAIM-874: Share-dependent advantage counts as independent

## The claim

**Reading (b), an alternative to [CLAIM-873](CLAIM-873.md).** On this reading [CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md) means the first sense of [TERM-147](../terms.d/TERM-147.md): scoring that does not read the interpretation under test.

Three reasons.

1. **The wording.** The title, the thesis and the defeater all say "the interpretation it tests". None names authority, share or the party.
2. **The anchor.** The claim's bullet "Independent scoring prevents it" cites one result, [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md): "Scoring operators independently of the dominant interpretation (Regime B, γ ≈ 0) removes the coupling-driven instability". γ is the effect of interpretation on conferral ([CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)). That is the first sense exactly. [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md) gets its result only with authority's own persistence below one (δ < 1, "each field stable alone"), and [CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md) does not carry that premise into its thesis or its defeater.
3. **The disclaimer does not reach it.** The first "What it does not say" concerns an independent channel pooled with one the loop selects, "as the audience of Weatherall, O'Connor and Bruner does". Share-dependent advantage selects no evidence. It is a term in the score that reads the share. And the disclaimer that [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md) does not show "that it prevents every form of lock-in" is about what the anchor shows, not a limit on the thesis, whose defeater is explicit.

**The separating case.** [CASE-312](../cases.d/CASE-312.md) keeps interpretation and authority apart, as [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md) does. Its authority score never reads the interpretation (γ = 0), and the interpretation has its own independent evidence. The score rises with the incumbent's share. Above π* = (1 − h/c)/2 the process the evidence disfavours keeps its authority, and the interpretation settles on its side, at +0.5 against evidence at −0.5. That is "a loop whose evidence is scored independently of the interpretation it tests ... shown to lock in all the same".

**What follows.** As worded, [CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md) meets its first defeater. It holds narrowed to loops in which authority does not reinforce itself apart from the interpretation, [CLAIM-330](CLAIM-330.md)'s δ < 1 made explicit, or with share-dependent advantage added beside the interpretation as something independent scoring must not read, which is the revision [CLAIM-873](CLAIM-873.md) calls a reading. Either change is to a held claim, and it is the owner's. The claim's status is not changed here.

## What it does not say

- That [CLAIM-342](CLAIM-342.md)'s substance is wrong. On either reading the same facts hold: share-dependent advantage locks a loop in when c > |h|, and c = 0 or |h| ≥ c removes it ([CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md)). The readings differ on whether the claim already says so.
- That [CLAIM-408](CLAIM-408.md) or Arthur's model ([THEORY-863](../theory.d/THEORY-863.md)) meets the defeater by itself. Neither has an interpretation or an evidence channel, so neither is a judgement–observation loop. The defeater is met only by a loop that has both, as [CASE-312](../cases.d/CASE-312.md) does.

The wording is the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's call.
