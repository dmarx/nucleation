# CLAIM-1277: Stable classification lets conflations split

## The claim

A47: "Distinctions can advance an inquiry by stabilizing the
classification of its objects across changes of context, representation,
or use. Such stabilization allows previously conflated operations to be
evaluated independently and permits conclusions or maintenance
obligations to transfer across contexts without silently changing their
referents." And its qualification: "Stabilizing a class does not
establish that the class is correct, that its boundaries are adequate, or
that maintaining the distinction is worth its cost. Those questions
remain subject to inquiry."

A47 gives the pattern as a sequence: "An ambiguity becomes
consequential", "A distinction is introduced", "The relevant classes
become more stable", "New inferences and interventions become available",
"The inquiry frontier changes". And it names three routes: "splitting a
class we previously treated as homogeneous", "recognizing that apparently
different instances belong to the same class", and "separating an
object's identity from properties that are allowed to change".

## A47's examples, and where they are held

A47 draws seven examples from the exchange's own corrections. Each is
the owner's, and each is filed from its own turn by this exchange's
filing:

1. where an abstraction originated against the contexts it applies to
   (U14, luria's origin; [CLAIM-1247](CLAIM-1247.md));
2. the research object against its presentation (U10; [CLAIM-1292](CLAIM-1292.md));
3. a feed against a view with a memory (U5; [TERM-177](../terms.d/TERM-177.md));
4. editorial judgment against publication infrastructure (U4, U6;
   [CLAIM-1344](CLAIM-1344.md), [CLAIM-1330](CLAIM-1330.md));
5. who generated an idea against its standing and against the user's
   competence (U17, [CLAIM-1213](CLAIM-1213.md); [CLAIM-668](CLAIM-668.md) holds that a record can say who
   contributed a proposition and its standing, not what anyone
   understands);
6. theory, demonstration and proposal as three classes of contribution
   with different warrants (U21);
7. a persisted CASE against an illustrative example (U19).

A47 also offers the exchange as "a candidate persisted CASE, with the
successive corrections preserved as the observations", and itself says
"we haven't independently tested whether the resulting distinctions
improve performance on future tasks". The test is [LEAD-255](../leads.d/LEAD-255.md).

## What it does not say

The qualification is held: [CASE-084](../cases.d/CASE-084.md) (a perfectly accountable record of a
false theory) shows a stable vocabulary can omit the right explanation,
and [CLAIM-472](CLAIM-472.md) that choosing and keeping a distinction is open to
evaluation, not only verification. A49's point that a faithful migration
may have to remove relations the old grammar imposed is [CLAIM-666](CLAIM-666.md).
