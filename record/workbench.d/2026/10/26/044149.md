*The owner supplied the lab's v2.7 to v2.9, now in dmarx/PerspectiveLab at
@c54617e: trained affect (v2.7, experiments/trained_affect/), the
perspective engine (v2.8, experiments/perspective_engine/) and endogenous
frames (v2.9, experiments/endogenous_frames/), with reports, handoffs,
architecture specs and three record proposals. The proposals named
eleven entries. They were treated as claims to check, not as
instructions.*

**How it was checked.** Every number kept was read against the stored
results (results/*.csv and *.json) and the code that made them. The test
suites passed: 11 tests for v2.7, 14 for v2.8 and 17 for v2.9. All three
studies were regenerated into a scratch directory, with matplotlib
installed for the run. v2.7 and v2.9 reproduced their stored results byte
for byte. v2.8 reproduced every CSV byte for byte, and its DDM-control
JSON differs only in the last digit of some floats. The notes of
2026-10-26 from PR [#332](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/332) were read first, so as not to repeat them. Where
an entry already had a note of that date, the new one is titled "(later)",
the record's precedent for a second note on one day.

**The notes.** Six were applied, each with a version bump and a history
item.

- [QUESTION-270](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-270.md) (later): v2.7's fitted predictors. Its numbers agree. Two
  facts the proposal left out were added. All eight worlds are one
  logistic law of pursuit with different coefficients, the uptake term
  among them in every world (−0.20 to −1.9). Registration and authority
  are set by the experimenter and given to every predictor as inputs, so
  the question's second requirement, registration measured separately,
  is not met.
- [CLAIM-427](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-427.md) (later): v2.7 to v2.9. The v2.9 proposal called the 15-feature
  ridge regression "an additional stronger conventional comparator". It
  is weaker. It is worse than the selected frame in the five worlds whose
  law does not change, and better only before new feedback in the two
  whose law changes, where both are worse than a constant forecast. Both
  same-feature ties are identities: v2.7's parity row is a copy of the
  fitted uptake predictor, and v2.9's conventional row runs the same
  selection code. On the v2.7 source data a BIC-like score already favours
  the main-effects model before the report's 12-bit surcharge, which the
  proposal did not say.
- [CLAIM-278](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-278.md) (later): v2.7 to v2.9. The v2.7 proposal said a conventional
  predictor learns the effect of authority "without representing that
  effect". Its 15-coefficient predictor contains the same three-way
  product of liking, authority and registration, without the sign
  restriction. The sharper fact, used in the note, is that a
  7-coefficient model with main effects and no interaction also beats the
  uptake predictor in the governance world (0.01378 against 0.01586).
- [CLAIM-336](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-336.md) (later): frames in the lab. The proposal's statement holds:
  the frame changes what the retained evidence means while the raw
  evidence and the first four updates stay the same, as a lab test checks.
  Two facts were added. Every v2.8 arm except all three changes in the
  85% world scores worse than a constant forecast of one half. And the
  study has no arm with the frame and the raised grant alone, so how
  meaning and influence interact is not isolated. In the 85% world the
  revised operators alone (−0.082 against baseline) do better than the
  frame alone (−0.066).
- [QUESTION-088](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-088.md) (later): v2.7's fitted habit and cue predictors win where
  their phenomenon dominates, within one phenomenon. v2.8 and v2.9 build
  reframing and predict no phenomenon. The proposals' lists of what the
  lab does not yet explain were not copied.
- [QUESTION-273](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-273.md): v2.8 builds one mechanism, a convex share of one step,
  and a sequential mode that only the tests run. Its influence measure
  was corrected in two ways. The report's "matched positive-cue initial
  state" is 16 positive and 8 negative cues, each run started with its arm
  already applied. And the rise of the reframer's exclusion effect from
  0.059 to 0.748 is mostly the new concept, which only the reframer
  writes: excluding it removes that coordinate. A rerun of the lab's code
  on the decision coordinate alone gave 0.247 and 0.069 for the positive
  and negative cue. The note says that the apps its body calls not held
  are now held.

Two descriptions in the v2.9 results were left out of every note and are
recorded here. The critic changes the outcome only in the null world.
There the guarded and unguarded policies differ, and the guarded one
still does worse than admitting nothing (0.25030 against 0.25). Its null
admission rate was 9.375% against a 5% target, as the report says.

**Declined.**

- [CLAIM-437](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-437.md). Its note of 2026-10-26 already says the lab's wanting and
  liking are terms in one logit, with no pursuit or consummatory process.
  v2.7's wanting proxy is anticipated value plus cue drive, so that note
  still holds as written. The proposal's "multiple specified dynamics"
  restates v2.6.
- [CLAIM-286](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-286.md). Its test is [QUESTION-088](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-088.md)'s, as the claim itself says. The
  note on [QUESTION-088](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-088.md) holds v2.7's comparison, and the rest of the
  proposals' points would record only what the lab has not done.
- [CLAIM-453](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-453.md) and [CASE-160](../../record/cases.d/CASE-160.md). v2.8's ddm.py re-checks the linear closure that
  both entries record as checked when filed. The proposal said nonlinear
  workspace transformations "do not inherit it". The lab tests only net
  linear reinforcement (coupling 0.85 or 0.8 against leak 0.6 or 0.5),
  and no nonlinear case is run. Its "exact decomposition" of a changing
  readout into accumulation and reallocation is a three-term algebraic
  identity, the discrete form of [CASE-160](../../record/cases.d/CASE-160.md)'s (dℓ)ᵀx bullet.
- [QUESTION-031](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-031.md). In v2.9 the raw fields and labels are the same for every
  learner. What a unary-only learner fails to separate, a product rule
  against a null world, is a limit of its hypothesis class, not of a
  probe family, and that unary features cannot fit a parity rule is
  textbook. The rest, spurious admission under null cues and grammar
  options outside the search, is in kind what the question's note of
  2026-10-26 already says of v2.5's alarms, or else an absence.
- The v2.9 part of [QUESTION-273](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-273.md)'s proposal. In v2.9 the grant is 0 or 1
  by the critic's verdict, so the separation of capability, evidence
  access and governance is architectural and not measured. The proposal
  itself says it is not the comparison the question asks for.
- The proposals' calls to keep statuses. A note changes none.
