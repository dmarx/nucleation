# SYNTHESIS-086: In the linearized meaning–authority model, committing predictions before the evidence removes capture only when the interpretation bends the reading of the evidence no more than it bends the predictions, a maintained legibility state shifts the instability threshold as stronger couplings would and adds a slow mode they cannot, and feasibility, aggregation and epistemic coordination are separate obstructions of which a commitment protocol fixes only timing

*A part account of the formal-apparatus line: one of the line's eleven
small clusters, three entries first in distributed-agency, where
[SYNTHESIS-050](SYNTHESIS-050.md) integrates [CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md) and [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md) and [SYNTHESIS-051](SYNTHESIS-051.md)
integrates [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md). This account integrates them again because their
`line` includes formal-apparatus. The line's account is built over it and
its sibling parts.*

## The account

- **Commitment is not Regime B.** [CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md) (the record's derivation in
  [CASE-099](../cases.d/CASE-099.md)'s linearization, [CASE-106](../cases.d/CASE-106.md); thesis, demonstrated, Active,
  answering [QUESTION-239](../questions.d/QUESTION-239.md)): scored by squared error on predictions
  committed before the evidence, the coupling of interpretation to
  conferral is γ = 4ηs(κ′ − κ̄), corrective, null or capturing as κ′ is
  below, at or above κ̄. "The order in time is necessary for learning. It
  does not make the scoring independent."
- **A third, maintained state.** [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md) (from the owner's review of
  2026-10-21, with the record's added route, reduction and witness;
  thesis, demonstrated, Active, answering [QUESTION-253](../questions.d/QUESTION-253.md)): with a legibility
  state r of persistence λ, the threshold is the two-variable one with
  β + νε/(1 − λ) and δ + με/(1 − λ), and away from it r is a third mode.
  So whether legibility is "renamed" depends on what is asked: "for
  whether symmetry breaks, it is; for how long a lean lasts and how the
  system returns, it is not."
- **Three obstructions.** [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md) (owner-seeded at U36, A36's table,
  with three constructions; thesis, demonstrated, Active): feasibility,
  aggregation and epistemic coordination are independent obstructions to
  an organization's realizing its demands. "Each can occur without the
  other two." The independence holds when the demands are the
  constituents' own acceptance conditions. That no remedy for one removes
  another is A36's further reading, "argued from the independence, not
  shown by it".

## Links the components state, and where

Two pairs are linked, both by mention:

- **Commitments and the obstructions.** [CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md)'s note of 2026-10-18,
  on A36 §4's commitment protocols: a commitment "secures the order in
  time, which keeps learning, and removes capture only when κ′ ≤ κ̄, which
  no protocol guarantees", and "[CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md) holds the three obstructions A36
  set this in". [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md) names [CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md) in return: a cryptographic
  commitment solves "the timing and integrity of the prediction", not the
  scoring.
- **κ′ and the legibility state.** [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md)'s "What it answers": since
  γ = 4ηs(κ′ − κ̄) ([CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md)), a regime that only varies κ′ adds a
  second-order term, "so it moves no linear threshold at all".

[CLAIM-340](../claims.d/CLAIM-340.md) and [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md) both rest on [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md), outside formal-apparatus.

## Not stated on the record

Tested by [ADR-075](../decisions.d/ADR-075.md)'s step 5 at this review and not filed:

- **Capture is one of [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md)'s three obstructions.** Which one depends
  on how the demands are fixed: [CLAIM-346](../claims.d/CLAIM-346.md)'s own "What it does not say"
  shows an aggregation failure recoded as a feasibility failure by adding
  a demand on the procedure. The slot is free, so the sentence absorbs
  every case. Fails on a free parameter.
- **A legibility regime that raises κ′ undoes commitment.** [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md)
  says a regime that only varies κ′ moves no linear threshold. Already
  stated, and against the sentence at linear order.

- That organizations have legibility regimes with dynamics of their own;
  [CLAIM-400](../claims.d/CLAIM-400.md) says the record holds no case.
