# SYNTHESIS-091: What a normative judgment about an organization needs: viability does not establish what ought to persist, a judgment is well-defined only once its relata are fixed and can then be false, some judgments of continuity, reform and obligation need histories, and a self-maintaining system's failure norm is proposed to be set by its own upkeep, while what fixes a signal's content is left open

*A part account of the organizational-normativity line ("Normative judgment about organizations"), filed at the synthesis review of the line on 2026-11-15. The line's account, [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md), is built over it and one sibling part, [SYNTHESIS-094](SYNTHESIS-094.md); the other two parts it was first built over became the line dependence-and-domination ([ADR-100](../decisions.d/ADR-100.md)). Its first nine components were mapped in [SYNTHESIS-038](SYNTHESIS-038.md) versions 1 to 3, and that mapping is kept here unchanged; the rest were filed from 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-12 and are new to any account. Version 3, at the synthesis review of 2026-11-17, integrates three more, filed from the exchange "Assess Depp Heard Abuse" that day. Where a component declares which sense of "organization" it means ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md)), the account says so; where it declares none, the account says that, and assigns none.*

## The account

**The limit.** [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) (the paper's, Sections 6.10 and 7.3; disclaimed,
interpretive, Active): viability does not establish what ought to
persist; "An unjust institution may be highly viable." It complements
[CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md), [CLAIM-352](../claims.d/CLAIM-352.md), [CLAIM-523](../claims.d/CLAIM-523.md) and [CLAIM-547](../claims.d/CLAIM-547.md). Its note of 2026-11-12 says
two readings corroborate it "for sense-3 wholes ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md))"; the claim
itself declares no sense. [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) (the owner's U16 of 2026-10-17;
Open) asks whether the organizational account gives a path to normativity
from first principles: "can norms of function and failure be defined from
an organization's own dynamics, and what more do practical and moral
authority need?"

**Relata, then truth.** [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) (the owner's U17 and U18; thesis,
interpretive, Active) rests on [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) and [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md), uses [TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) and
complements [CLAIM-405](../claims.d/CLAIM-405.md): a normative judgment is well-defined only once its
relata are fixed ("the organization or standpoint it concerns, the
standard, the scale and the context"), "and that dependence does not by
itself make it relative: once they are fixed, the judgment can be false".
[TERM-059](../terms.d/TERM-059.md) (the owner's name; Active) states relational normative realism
and says its third condition, constraints on admissible perspectives, is
not established. [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md) (Open) refines [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and asks whether
such judgments can be objective about the standards and perspectives
themselves.

**Histories.** [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md) (the owner's U26 of 2026-10-31, narrowed the same
day and again on 2026-11-06; thesis, interpretive, Active) rests on
[CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) and [CLAIM-350](../claims.d/CLAIM-350.md), bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and [QUESTION-256](../questions.d/QUESTION-256.md), and
complements [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md) and [CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md): judgments about an organization's
continuity, reform or obligations can depend on diachronic relations that
present states and terminal outcomes do not show. Its note of 2026-11-12
says Thompson corroborates it "for sense-3 wholes ([TERM-074](../terms.d/TERM-074.md))". [ARG-043](../arguments.d/ARG-043.md)
(Proposed) concludes it from Bencherki's checklist and daycare ([LIT-1901](../literature.d/LIT-1901.md),
[THEORY-1285](../theory.d/THEORY-1285.md)). [CLAIM-512](../claims.d/CLAIM-512.md) (the assistant's, A26 and A27; counter,
interpretive, Active) objects to [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md) and [CLAIM-608](../claims.d/CLAIM-608.md) with a dilemma and
a distinction; [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md)'s version 3 concedes the broad horn, and the
clause it dropped is [CLAIM-608](../claims.d/CLAIM-608.md) (the record's gloss; thesis, Proposed),
which rests on [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md) and [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) and holds that histories add a
relatum only if counterfactual discrimination, transfer or causal
specificity is shown. [QUESTION-256](../questions.d/QUESTION-256.md) (A26's, from the owner's case of Clive
Wearing; Open) asks which continuities self-governance needs.

**Corrigibility, not claimed.** [CLAIM-511](../claims.d/CLAIM-511.md) (the owner's question; disclaimed,
interpretive, Proposed; first line distributed-agency) is the bare
proposition that a self-maintaining organization ought to preserve its
corrigibility. It bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md), [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md) and [QUESTION-087](../questions.d/QUESTION-087.md) and
complements [CLAIM-462](../claims.d/CLAIM-462.md); its summary says the record's constructions do not
establish it and its value is conditional ([CLAIM-520](../claims.d/CLAIM-520.md)).

**A failure norm set by the system's own upkeep.** [CLAIM-648](../claims.d/CLAIM-648.md) (the
assistant's, a reading across the filing of "Meaning Transmission
Explained"; thesis, interpretive, Proposed; also record-as-apparatus)
rests on the owner's [CLAIM-649](../claims.d/CLAIM-649.md), grounds in Bickhard ([THEORY-220](../theory.d/THEORY-220.md), Proposed)
and [CASE-241](../cases.d/CASE-241.md), and bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md): a self-maintaining system that
anticipates an interaction will sustain it "holds an expected response
profile set by its own upkeep", the shortfall means by its own lights
that the interaction failed, and that norm and a designer's profile "come
apart where the system's persistence is at stake". [ARG-083](../arguments.d/ARG-083.md) (Proposed)
concludes it, and says the construction shows the two norms differ, not
that a simulated self-set norm is the system's own. [CASE-241](../cases.d/CASE-241.md) (the
assistant's construction; fictional, Proposed; a variant of [CASE-240](../cases.d/CASE-240.md), the
debugger's test) builds both foragers: they agree on a silent patch and
part when the yield falls but still pays and when the upkeep rises past
an unchanged yield. [CASE-239](../cases.d/CASE-239.md) (a proposed study, stipulated, Proposed;
variant of [CASE-241](../cases.d/CASE-241.md); bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md) and [QUESTION-285](../questions.d/QUESTION-285.md)) is the forager
the review of [#483](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/483) asked for, one that learns which distinctions predict a
loss of viability; its summary names the threat that "the designer still
chooses the learning rule and the viability variable". None of the four
declares a sense of "organization"; their subject is a self-maintaining
system.

**What a signal's content is answerable to.** [CLAIM-650](../claims.d/CLAIM-650.md) (the assistant's,
revised at the review of [#483](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/483) relayed on 2026-11-12; thesis, interpretive,
Proposed; also pragmatic-transport) grounds in Grice ([THEORY-444](../theory.d/THEORY-444.md)) and the
owner's smoke detector ([CASE-243](../cases.d/CASE-243.md)) and bears on [QUESTION-128](../questions.d/QUESTION-128.md): a designed
alarm's content can be false, its error lies in a non-factive content,
and what makes "fire" the content it is supposed to carry is "answered
for this designed alarm by its designer's intention and left open,
between selection history and organizational maintenance, for other
systems". It says [CLAIM-648](../claims.d/CLAIM-648.md) takes up the second. [ARG-082](../arguments.d/ARG-082.md) (Proposed)
concludes the factivity half; [ARG-081](../arguments.d/ARG-081.md) (Rejected) is the step from
non-factivity to intentional origin, which "does not follow from
factivity alone".

**Surrogate authority.** [QUESTION-454](../questions.d/QUESTION-454.md) (A's, A9 §5 of "Medical Ethics and
Media Enshitification", 2026-11-11; Open; also will-organization) asks
what makes one agent's authority to decide about another's body
legitimate when the other has standing but cannot decide: welfare,
earlier wishes, projected future wishes, or the relation. It cites
[QUESTION-364](../questions.d/QUESTION-364.md), [TERM-083](../terms.d/TERM-083.md), [THEORY-464](../theory.d/THEORY-464.md) and [CASE-128](../cases.d/CASE-128.md), none of them in this
line, and stores no relation. Its note of 2026-11-12 (the assistant's,
accepted by the owner) sorts its cases by [TERM-100](../terms.d/TERM-100.md)'s formation and
maintenance provenance, and says that if only maintenance provenance can
be read off a present organization, "a surrogate's authority cannot be
checked from the present arrangement alone, which is [CLAIM-518](../claims.d/CLAIM-518.md)'s point
about histories", calling it "An interpretive mapping, not an answer".

**Taking the verdict as a premise.** [CLAIM-718](../claims.d/CLAIM-718.md) (the owner's U19 of
"Assess Depp Heard Abuse", 2026-11-16; the name "normative morphology"
and the three-step test against circularity are A39's; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also dependence-and-domination) rests on
[CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) and bears on [QUESTION-468](../questions.d/QUESTION-468.md): a normative verdict on a
relationship can be taken as a premise to find what in its organization
makes it true, by holding behaviour and outcome fixed across cases judged
differently, looking for the organizational difference, then trying to
break it; the method "is circular without the last step". Its body says
it rests on [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md) because the verdict must be well-defined, that the
record's stipulated pairs ([CASE-264](../cases.d/CASE-264.md), [CASE-229](../cases.d/CASE-229.md) with [ARG-055](../arguments.d/ARG-055.md), [CASE-263](../cases.d/CASE-263.md))
already use the method without its third step, and that whether the
standard behind a verdict is justified is [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md)'s question.

**Legitimacy and adequacy.** [QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md) (the owner's U31,
2026-11-16; A71's survey is carried in its body as A's; Open; also
dependence-and-domination) stores no relation: "From where does a
relationship, a capability or a restriction derive its legitimacy, and
from where its adequacy?" Its body names [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md), [CLAIM-363](../claims.d/CLAIM-363.md),
[CLAIM-348](../claims.d/CLAIM-348.md), [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md) (narrower in object, wider in asking for
adequacy), [QUESTION-454](../questions.d/QUESTION-454.md) ("One case of this question") and [QUESTION-464](../questions.d/QUESTION-464.md),
and says [CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md) and [QUESTION-482](../questions.d/QUESTION-482.md) are on its first half.
[CLAIM-746](../claims.d/CLAIM-746.md) (A's, A74 §9 and A76 X, corrected by the filer; thesis,
interpretive, Proposed; also realizability) rests on [CLAIM-560](../claims.d/CLAIM-560.md), [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)
and [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), bears on [QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md) and [QUESTION-129](../questions.d/QUESTION-129.md), and uses
[TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md): the warrant of an authority's obligations, its jurisdiction,
its arrangement's capacity to realize them together, and its capacity to
reach compliant arrangements and correct failures are distinct; "of the
last two only the dynamic one implies the static one, for what it
reaches". Its separation 3 is [CLAIM-075](../claims.d/CLAIM-075.md)'s. Its "Admission" section says
what it adds is the ordering, five separations and one implication; its
body says it answers none of [QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md)'s grounds, and that A74
§9's adjacent thesis is a dated note on [CLAIM-624](../claims.d/CLAIM-624.md). [CLAIM-737](../claims.d/CLAIM-737.md), outside
this part, complements it and bears on [QUESTION-485](../questions.d/QUESTION-485.md).
