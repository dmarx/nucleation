# LEAD-107: Measure in the reconstructed exchanges each restatement's distance from its original against whether it was re-derived as new, and coded against uncoded items, with raters blind to direction

## What it would do

Code restatements in the workbench reconstructions; count re-derivation of
the same content before and after its parts got addresses.

Where the record names it:

- [CLAIM-180](../claims.d/CLAIM-180.md): "restatement distance set against re-derivation and a count
  of coded against uncoded items, has not been run."
- [CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md): "The before-and-after count is still not made."

## What would count

Less re-derivation after coding supports [CLAIM-182](../claims.d/CLAIM-182.md) and [CLAIM-180](../claims.d/CLAIM-180.md).

## Note of 2026-11-23: run, and what remains

The lead was run on 2026-11-23, and [CASE-tmp4niuz](../cases.d/CASE-tmp4niuz.md) records the study. A
script coded, by fixed rules, every restatement event that 48
reconstructions of exchanges report. The materials are in
`scratch/studies/lead-107/`.

- **Coded against uncoded.** The comparison is degenerate. Of 265 events,
  76 are re-derived as new and one is retrieved by citation. The
  reconstructions record a restatement almost only when it fails
  ([CLAIM-tmp9w3p9](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp9w3p9.md)).
- **Before and after a code.** Of 21 items re-derived before they had
  codes, four were re-derived again after. None is reported cited.
- **Distance.** The test could not be run. Only 16 events quote the
  restatement beside one code, three re-derived and none retrieved.
- **Direction.** [CLAIM-191](../claims.d/CLAIM-191.md)'s direction was not rated.

The counts show content held under codes re-derived without them in 20
of the 48 reconstructions. Two readings of that are filed as
alternatives. [CLAIM-tmpc5mmp](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpc5mmp.md) objects to [CLAIM-180](../claims.d/CLAIM-180.md)'s bypass clause.
[CLAIM-tmp49ilj](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmp49ilj.md) reads the counts as failures to find the code, a question
of discoverability; the lead worker leans to this one. There was one
coder, working by rule, and no agreement measure.

What remains is for a separate lead and the owner:

- a verbatim transcript coded for every restatement, recognised or not,
  each paired with its earlier statement;
- a human second coder;
- a blind direction rating for [CLAIM-191](../claims.d/CLAIM-191.md).

The status and stage of this lead are unchanged.
