# THEORY-tmpqmcmz: Damavandi, Hagh, Santangelo and Manning show that, for elastic energies quadratic in their constraints, constraint counting predicts energetic rigidity only when the system has no states of self-stress, and that when it has them the spectrum of the prestress matrix decides whether second-order rigidity implies energetic rigidity, with no general result when that matrix has negative eigenvalues

## Source

Damavandi, Hagh, Santangelo and Manning (2022; arXiv v1 2021-02-22), LIT-tmp925so; read in NOTE-tmp8m06t.

## What was actually shown

- **Counting** (abstract; Sec. II, Case 1): "We find that constraint
  counting robustly predicts energetic rigidity only when the system has no
  states of self stress."
- **With self-stress** (Sec. II; App. A): with Hessian H = RᵀR + P, P = 0
  makes second-order rigidity imply energetic rigidity at quartic order,
  even with zero shear modulus; P positive semidefinite makes it imply
  energetic rigidity. A first-order rigid system can fail to be
  energetically rigid when prestressed, as when the contact forces of a
  jammed packing are scaled up.
- **The open case** (Sec. II, Case 2C): with negative eigenvalues of P, "we
  have been unable to derive analytic results for whether first-order or
  second-order rigidity implies energetic rigidity."

## What this does not say

- That systems in Case 2C are not energetically rigid: only that no
  general result is known. The abstract's "there may be systems for which
  neither first nor second-order rigidity imply energetic rigidity" is a
  possibility, not an exhibited example.
- That the prestress a system adopts is the one that would stabilize it;
  the authors warn it may not be.
- Anything for energies not quadratic in the constraints.
