# LEAD-188 coding rules for testimony

Written 2026-11-23, before any testimony was read in this run. They are
not revised after reading; a rule found wanting gets an amendment below,
dated, with the item that showed it.

## Unit

One finding = one statement, in one source, about how the line "I'm the
Juggernaut, bitch!" came to be in *X-Men: The Last Stand* (2006), or about
who on the production knew of the redub before the line was shot.

Each finding records: source URL, outlet, date published, date read,
speaker, a short verbatim quote (under ~40 words), and the codes below.

## Codes

**V — voice (who is speaking)**

- V1 production, first-hand: someone on the production speaking of their
  own knowledge (director, writer, actor, producer, editor, studio).
- V2 production, reported: a production member's words quoted second-hand
  by another outlet, original not located or not read.
- V3 creator: the redub's makers (Nazario, Hayes).
- V4 journalist or commentator: the writer's own statement or inference.

**R — route asserted (how the redub reached the decision)**

- R0 none: the finding dates or describes the line but gives no route.
- R1 direct individual: a named decision-maker saw the redub (or was shown
  it by a named person) and added the line.
- R2 circulation: the line reached the production through its general
  spread (crew or cast quoting it, it being everywhere, audiences or press
  expecting it), with no single named conveyor.
- R3 test audience / marketing: the line's inclusion responded to test
  screenings, fan reaction or studio marketing.
- R4 independent origin: the line did not come from the redub.

**E — epistemic status of the route**

- E1 asserted as known by the speaker.
- E2 hedged or inferred ("likely", "probably", "I think").
- E3 hearsay ("word got around", "I heard").

**T — timing**

- T1 places the addition at reshoots / additional photography.
- T2 places it in the original shoot or script.
- T0 no timing.

## Decision rules

- A finding counts as **production testimony on the route** only if it is
  V1 and R1–R4. V2 with R1–R4 is reported testimony, counted separately.
- V3 and V4 never count as production testimony, whatever route they
  assert.
- A finding that only repeats an earlier source (same quote, attributed)
  is coded once, at the earliest source read; later repeats are listed
  as repeats.
- Where the wording supports two route codes, both are recorded and the
  finding is flagged ambiguous; it is not resolved by the coder's view.
- A source not reached (bot check, paywall, refusal) is listed with the
  obstacle and the date and is not coded.
- Nothing is coded from memory: only text read in this run.
