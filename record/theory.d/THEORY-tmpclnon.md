# THEORY-tmpclnon: Han argues that the power maintaining the neoliberal system is seductive rather than repressive, exploiting freedom instead of suppressing it, making people dependent and each a self-exploiting entrepreneur, so that there is no one to resist and the subjugated do not experience themselves as subjugated

## The account

Han argues that the power maintaining the system is "no longer repressive, and instead seductive"; that "There is no person opposite, no enemy who represses liberties"; that it "tries to make them dependent"; and that "the subjugated individual is not even aware of being subjugated".

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-tmphjyfw](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphjyfw.md). It bounds [CLAIM-tmpkjv7u](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpkjv7u.md): dependence without a discretion-holder, outside the claim's test by its version 2.

## What it does not say

Any account of how such power is identified or resisted.
