The owner asked on 2026-11-18 to "revisit the [ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md) change to make sure
there weren't any broken links we want to repair", noting that an entry
can carry several lines, so a line can be added without taking one away.

## The split

[ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md) moved 29 entries from organizational-normativity to
dependence-and-domination, in place. It kept organizational-normativity
second on the three that take part in that line's argument under [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md)
([QUESTION-460](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-460.md), [QUESTION-468](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-468.md), [CLAIM-685](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-685.md)). A script over every moved entry
checked each relation to an entry whose first line is
organizational-normativity, in both directions.

- **No participation was severed.** No moved entry answers, refines,
  objects to or bears on an organizational-normativity entry without
  carrying the line.
- **Two dependencies were severed, as [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md) provides.** These are links
  the rule does not count as taking part in a line's argument:
  - [CLAIM-687](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-687.md) `complements` [CLAIM-624](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-624.md). [ADR-100](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-100.md) named this one and dropped
    the line ("`complements` alone does not add a line").
  - [CLAIM-707](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-707.md), filed later in organizational-normativity, `complements`
    [CLAIM-678](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-678.md).

  Under the rule as it stands, neither should carry the other line. If
  the owner wants complementary claims to share a line additively, that is
  a change to [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md), not a repair. It is left to the owner.

The repairs follow [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md)'s own terms:
- No entry's first line changes, and nothing cascades.
- [CLAIM-534](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-534.md) remains [ADR-085](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-085.md)'s listed exception.
- Accounts (SYNTHESIS) are not changed: their line is their scope
  ([ADR-083](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-083.md)), and a `bears_on` from an account maps a question rather than
  arguing it.
- [CLAIM-742](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-742.md) gains the set-aside authenticity line, because it bears on
  [QUESTION-068](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-068.md). That line's status is unchanged, so it sets nothing aside.
