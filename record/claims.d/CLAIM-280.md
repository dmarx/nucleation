# CLAIM-280: A pattern can persist while every constituent is replaced and some constituents work against it, as a high-turnover regime of damage and repair, so the persistence of a product and the persistence of the organization producing it are separate facts

## The claim

A8 §4 separated a construction regime, in which the local structure keeps
changing, from a maintenance regime with "Low net visual change despite
continued editing and repair". "The maintenance regime is conceptually
interesting because apparent stability may require continuous activity. An
artwork can persist not because nothing happens to it, but because
destructive interventions are continually counteracted by restorative
interventions." Its proposed measure is a conditional repair hazard: the
rate of restoring edits at a site just after an overwrite, compared with the
baseline rate there.

A8 §9: "**A collectively maintained pattern can possess coherence,
persistence, and recognizable identity even though none of its constituents
continuously controls the pattern, all of its constituents are replaceable,
and some of their activities oppose one another.**" And: "the distinction
between a persistent product and a persistent producer. An image can endure
without an enduring organization behind it, while an organization can
endure despite radically changing the image it produces."

The record already holds persistence through turnover, at the level of
organizations. Open Horizon ([CASE-006](../cases.d/CASE-006.md)) persists through turnover while
losing its founding intentions. What this claim adds is the split between
the thing maintained and the maintainer, with a case that shows both
([CASE-079](../cases.d/CASE-079.md)). The pixel record makes the product observable directly.
The producer has to be inferred.

A8 drew the moral for selves: "**a stable self may be maintained by
continuous internal contestation and repair, rather than by an absence of
conflict.**" And: "this distinguishes persistence of self-representation
from persistence of the processes that construct and govern that
representation."

## What it does not say

- It does not say an artwork or its coalition is a self or an agent. A8:
  "an artwork is not thereby a self, and an artwork-maintaining coalition
  does not automatically satisfy the conditions for agency."
- It does not supply a persistence criterion for selves. [CLAIM-161](CLAIM-161.md) asks
  for a coherence measure conserved along a system's own trajectory, and
  [QUESTION-043](../questions.d/QUESTION-043.md) asks which one. A10 §4.3's "causal-organizational
  continuity" is a candidate of the same shape, and it is not filed.
- No repair-hazard analysis of the released 2022 data has been done here.
  The case's "documented" standing covers the event and the data, not the
  regime.
