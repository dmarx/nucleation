# LIT-tmpbv38s: The New Presidential Adjudication

Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar (2026). The New Presidential Adjudication. *The Yale Law Journal Forum* 135: 597–653 (Essay, 9 February 2026). https://yalelawjournal.org/collections/the-new-presidential-adjudication.

## Key takeaways

- Presidents "make fact-specific, high-stakes decisions about sanctions,
  investigations, contracts, legal interpretations, and enforcement, with
  limited or minimal judicial oversight" (abstract, p. 597).
- "Executive coercion" is "the intentional use of presidential power
  against a specific target with the goal of extracting concessions ...
  unrelated to either public policy or the purpose behind the grant of
  power itself" (p. 626).
- Attacks may be "thwarted by a court eventually but levy a high cost on
  those targeted in the meantime" (p. 647); the harder look comes only
  after final agency action (p. 645).
