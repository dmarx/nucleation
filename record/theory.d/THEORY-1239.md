# THEORY-1239: No logistic system, one that separates propositions from general laws of inference, can carry commonsense knowledge, whose defaults later evidence displaces: attempts to repair its monotonicity will continue to fail

## Source

Minsky (1974), MIT AI Memo 306, [LIT-410](../literature.d/LIT-410.md), §6, read in full in [NOTE-337](../notes.d/NOTE-337.md) from
an OCR of the scan. [THEORY-462](THEORY-462.md) holds the definitional half and reports
that this further claim is asserted.

## Why it is Proposed

The memo gives five counts against "logistic" systems. Monotonicity is one
of them: "each added axiom means more theorems, none can disappear". That
much holds of classical logic. That no repair can succeed is a forecast.
Default logic and circumscription were built to make that repair, and the
record has not read them. The memo's one engaged target is McCarthy's
Airport problem.
