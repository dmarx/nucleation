summary: >-
  Kochen & Specker (1967), DOI-10.1512/iumj.1968.17.17004. Theorem 1
  exhibits a finite partial Boolean algebra D, generated by 117 directions
  in E³, that admits no homomorphism onto Z₂. §4 embeds D in the
  projections of the spin-1 space of orthohelium's 2³S₁ state, via α ↦ P
  J_α². So no assignment of 0/1 values to those quantum propositions
  respects all the Boolean relations *within* each commeasurable set.
  Theorem 0 makes the target precise: a partial Boolean algebra embeds in
  a Boolean algebra iff its Z₂-homomorphisms separate points. That is,
  hidden variables in the paper's sense need enough "global valuations",
  and quantum mechanics in dimension 3 has none. The same paper builds a
  classical hidden-variable model for spin ½ (§6). It argues that von
  Neumann's no-go needed additivity on non-commuting observables (Theorem
  3).
