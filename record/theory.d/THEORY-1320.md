# THEORY-1320: In Ntoumanis et al.'s meta-analysis of 73 SDT-informed health interventions, mostly randomized, the interventions raised perceived need support (g = 0.64), autonomy and competence satisfaction (0.37, 0.31), autonomous motivation (0.30) and health behaviour (0.45) by the end of the intervention, but not relatedness, controlled motivation or amotivation, and their small effect on psychological health (0.29) was gone at follow-up (0.14, ns); need frustration was not an outcome and mediation was not tested

## Source

Ntoumanis, Ng, Prestwich, Quested, Hancox, Thøgersen-Ntoumani, Deci, Ryan,
Lonsdale and Williams (2020), [LIT-1962](../literature.d/LIT-1962.md), pp. 222–237; read in
[NOTE-1526](../notes.d/NOTE-1526.md).

## What was actually shown

**The studies**: 73 experiments (58 RCTs) with 30,088 participants,
searched to November 2018. Each compared a more with a less SDT-supportive
arm; effects are Hedges' g, random effects (pp. 219–222).

**End of intervention** (Table 1): need support 0.64 [0.35, 0.93], k = 21;
autonomy 0.37 [0.15, 0.60], k = 17; competence 0.31 [0.12, 0.49], k = 22;
relatedness 0.20 [−0.04, 0.45], k = 14; autonomous motivation 0.30
[0.17, 0.42], k = 37; controlled motivation 0.07, amotivation −0.07 (both
ns); health behaviour 0.45 [0.33, 0.57], k = 49; physical health 0.04 (ns);
psychological health 0.29 [0.14, 0.45], k = 22.

**Follow-up**: behaviour 0.28 [0.17, 0.38], k = 28; physical health 0.28
[0.03, 0.53], k = 14; psychological health 0.14 [−0.09, 0.36], k = 10.

Each of these could have been null; the proximal constructs and behaviour
were not, and relatedness and the controlled side were.

**What the design cannot show.** The link between constructs and outcomes
is a meta-regression of one study-level effect size on another (Tables
7–8), significant for need support and autonomous motivation on behaviour
only after one outlying study is removed. No within-study mediation was
tested (p. 237). No study's outcome was need frustration.

## What this does not say

- **That the benefits come through need satisfaction.** Need satisfaction
  effect sizes did not predict behaviour effect sizes (Table 7).
- **That SDT interventions treat psychopathology.** "Psychological health"
  is a pooled category (quality of life, depression and others) in mostly
  non-clinical, physical-activity trials, and its effect did not last.
- **That more need support gives more benefit.** The number of SDT
  techniques moderated nothing (p. 236).
- **That the estimates are unbiased.** Blinding was rare, allocation
  concealment shrank effects, and Egger's test flags small-study bias for
  behaviour and psychological health.
