# CLAIM-tmp48sm1: Reading A of the Juggernaut testimony: the line reached the film through the redub's circulation, which carried it to the one person who decided, so the case now has the route its third rung needed, though one case supports that rung only weakly against a list that names the distributors

## The reading

CLAIM-tmp24fw0's testimony fixes the last link: one director's request,
for a line he met online. Reading A takes "online" at its 2006 value. The
redub was not sent to a studio; its makers posted it and many people
passed it on (CASE-100, NOTE-1272). A director meets such a video
because it is everywhere in his part of the web, and the testimony names
no one who brought it. The route is maker, circulation, director: the
organized pattern of passing-on is the middle of the explanation, which
is CLAIM-332's third rung, collective causal efficacy, with no collective
intention.

## How much it carries

Weakly. CLAIM-tmp4ybsw: one adopted video favours circulation over a
list that names the distributors by 2.3 to 4.7 at most, and by under two
once the case's fame is allowed for. The reading gives CLAIM-332 the
route it lacked, not a test it has passed.

## Against reading B

CLAIM-tmp9av3u counts the director as one identifiable contributor and
stops there. Reading A answers that the defeater asks whether the
influence disappears once identifiable contributions are modelled, and
the director's own viewing is not an influence on him: what put the
video in front of him is the question, and the only answer on the
record is its spread.
