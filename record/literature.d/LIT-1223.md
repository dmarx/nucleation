# LIT-1223: Refractory depression – mechanisms and efficacy of radically open dialectical behaviour therapy (RefraMED): findings of a randomised trial on benefits and harms

Lynch, T. R., Hempel, R. J., Whalley, B., Byford, S., Chamba, R., Clarke, P., Clarke, S., Kingdon, D. G., O'Mahen, H., Remington, B., Rushbrook, S. C., Shearer, J., Stanton, M., Swales, M., Watkins, A. & Russell, I. T. (2020). Refractory depression – mechanisms and efficacy of radically open dialectical behaviour therapy (RefraMED): findings of a randomised trial on benefits and harms. *British Journal of Psychiatry* 216(4): 204–212. doi:10.1192/bjp.2019.53.

Read in full on 2026-10-29 ([NOTE-1358](../notes.d/NOTE-1358.md)).

## Why this is here

A42 of the exchange cited the RefraMED trial as the main controlled evidence for RO DBT. Bears on [CLAIM-349](../claims.d/CLAIM-349.md): the trial's gain in flexibility without a lasting gain on the primary outcome is a caution on reading overcontrol as the whole of the problem.

## Note of 2026-10-29: the trial's monograph, read

The NIHR Efficacy and Mechanism Evaluation monograph of the same trial,
[LIT-1838](LIT-1838.md), is read ([NOTE-1421](../notes.d/NOTE-1421.md)). Three points bear on this paper.

- **"Throughout."** The monograph's Table 8 has the same 7-month
  differences in flexibility and emotional coping, with intervals about
  1.8 times narrower, and finds them significant (AAQ-II p = 0.010, EAC p
  = 0.036), where this paper's Table 1 has P = 0.096 and P = 0.132. So
  this paper's abstract matches the monograph's analysis and not its own
  table. The conflict is between two analyses of one data set; neither
  document says why the intervals differ. The status note's caution stands
  for this paper.
- **The adverse reactions.** The monograph gives "p < 0.01" where this
  paper gives one-tailed P = 0.004. Neither reproduces from the counts:
  with eight reactions in eight participants, P = C(162,8)/C(250,8) =
  0.029, and larger if fewer participants were affected. The imbalance
  stays under 0.05.
- **The title's "mechanisms".** The monograph reports the mediation
  analyses this paper omits. The mediators are sessions attended,
  therapeutic alliance and DBT skills; flexibility and emotional coping
  are not entered as mediators; mediator and outcome are both at 7 months;
  and the instrumented analysis failed on weak instruments (Cragg–Donald
  statistics no larger than 1). So the mechanism the title names is still
  not estimated.
