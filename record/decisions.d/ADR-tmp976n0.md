# ADR-tmp976n0: A copy its host says is published with the rights holders' permission, and a reply posted by the scholar it answers beside that scholar's own paper, count as lawful copies to read, beside the kinds already admitted

## Context

[ADR-074](ADR-074.md) lists the copies that count as lawful to read: copies of a
published article or an author's manuscript on a course or reading-list
page, a faculty or research-group page, a scholarly third-party site, or an
author's own upload. It excludes shadow libraries and uploads of whole
in-copyright books. A lead's brief may narrow the list, never widen it.

On 2026-11-17 the readers of the works registered from the exchange "Assess
Depp Heard Abuse" located two copies that fit none of [ADR-074](ADR-074.md)'s kinds:

- **Strawson, "Freedom and Resentment" (1962).** The only full copy reached
  is a scan of the British Academy offprint on the site of the Moscow
  Center for Consciousness Studies. The page states: "It is published with
  permission of the British Academy and Galen Strawson." The British
  Academy is the publisher, and Galen Strawson is the author's son and
  literary heir.
- **Scanlon, "Reply to Leif Wenar" (2013).** It was read from Leif Wenar's
  personal site, where Wenar posts his article "Rights and What We Owe to
  Each Other" beside the reply it drew. The site is the author's, but the
  author is Wenar, not Scanlon.

The lead judged both lawful and asked the owner to confirm. The owner ruled
on 2026-11-18: "yes, both permission copies count as lawful. update the
skill to reflect this with an adr."

## Decision

[ADR-074](ADR-074.md)'s kinds are extended by two:

1. **A copy published with the rights holders' permission.** The host
   states that the copy is published with the permission of the rights
   holders: the publisher, the author, or the author's estate or heir. The
   NOTE quotes the statement and gives the address it appears at. A
   statement that names no rights holder ("used with permission") does not
   count. Nor does a host's general claim of fair use.
2. **A reply posted by the scholar it answers.** A reply, comment or
   response, posted on the personal or faculty site of the scholar it
   answers, beside that scholar's own contribution to the same exchange,
   counts. The NOTE says whose site it is and what it sits beside. The kind
   covers the pieces of a published exchange (a symposium, a reply and
   rejoinder) as a participant keeps them together. It does not cover a
   scholar's collection of others' papers in general.

Everything else stands:

- [ADR-074](ADR-074.md)'s exclusions (shadow libraries, ebook-piracy sites, uploads of
  whole in-copyright books) still apply, whatever a host says.
- A bot check, paywall or refusal still stops the fetch.
- [ADR-053](ADR-053.md)'s statuses still sort what was not read.

The read-and-file skill's step 5 and its reader's brief carry the two kinds.

## Alternatives

- **Leave both Unfetched.** The copies were located and read. Filing them
  as unread would misstate what was done, and would keep two readings the
  owner judged lawful out of the record.
- **Decide each case in the curation entry.** The cases recur: permission
  statements on society and centre sites, and replies kept with the paper
  they answer, are common for older philosophy. A rule that only a
  curation entry records would be re-derived by each lead, the drift
  [ADR-074](ADR-074.md) was written to stop.
- **Admit any copy a host says is lawful.** Too wide. A statement must name
  the rights holders, and the second kind needs the poster to be a party
  to the exchange.

## Consequences

- The readings of 2026-11-18 cite this decision in the history of
  [LIT-2679](../literature.d/LIT-2679.md) and [NOTE-tmpnfib1](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpnfib1.md) (Strawson), and of [LIT-tmplag34](../literature.d/LIT-tmplag34.md) and its
  NOTE (Scanlon's reply). Both drop "to be confirmed by the owner".
- Proposed until adopted. The owner ruled on its substance on 2026-11-18.
