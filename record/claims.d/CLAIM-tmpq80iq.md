# CLAIM-tmpq80iq: Relief of an organization's frustration must be judged for an identified organization against a fixed reference set of its original demands, under an explicit correspondence between earlier and later demands: a demand excised or suppressed has changed the comparison and has not been satisfied, an endogenous revision of what the organization maintains is a transformation of it and not relief, and learning can change which processes bear the demands

## The claim

A37: "If the system simply deletes a difficult demand, it has changed the
comparison problem rather than shown an expansion of its ability to realize
the original demands." The owner, at U38: "it might be helpful to connect
back to our notions of individuation".

A38's central distinction: "**Resolving a conflict, reorganizing the
individual that bears it, and eliminating one of the parties to the conflict
are different transformations—even when all three reduce an aggregate
loss.**" Its table of responses to demands that cannot all be met:

| Transformation | A38: did it resolve the original conflict? |
|---|---|
| New coordination | "Yes" |
| Functional differentiation | "Yes, if the original requirements are preserved" |
| Priority revision | "Not as originally posed; it resolves a choice problem" |
| Constitutive revision | "It may be legitimate organizational transformation, but not satisfaction of the original requirements" |
| Excision or suppression | "No—not without further argument" |

A43 reduced it to four: "**Achievement:** More of the original demands
become jointly attainable. **Reprioritization:** The organization changes
which demands receive precedence. **Constitutive transformation:** The
organization changes which demands it maintains and which processes bear
them. **Suppression:** A demand or its evidence is excluded without
demonstrating that the original requirement has been satisfied."

Only achievement is relief. The claim says what the comparison needs for
that to be decidable:

1. **A fixed reference set D₀ of the original demands.** A38 indexes the
   compatibility complex to it and to the organization:
   K(O_t; D₀, H, B) = {S ⊆ D₀ : R(O_t; H, B) ∩ ⋂_{i∈S} A_i ≠ ∅}, where
   R(O_t; H, B) is what the organization at t can attain within horizon H
   and budget B. Relief is K(O_t; D₀, H, B) ⊊ K(O_t′; D₀, H, B). The
   family stays downward closed with R in it, so it is still the complex
   of [CLAIM-tmp55mls](CLAIM-tmp55mls.md). With D₀ fixed, deleting a demand from evaluation
   leaves K unchanged; it can look like relief only if D₀ is allowed to
   shrink.
2. **An identified organization at both times.** "So the question is not
   simply whether a demand disappeared. It is **what organization existed
   before and after the change, and how the transformation occurred**."
3. **An explicit correspondence between earlier and later demands.** A38
   keeps two records, "**The current constitutive profile**" and "**The
   reference profile**": which demands existed earlier, "whether they were
   fulfilled, transferred, revised, suppressed, or abandoned, and through
   what causal process". "The first helps individuate the present
   organization. The second lets us assess transformation without confusing
   it with satisfaction."

## Why causal history matters

A38's case: a learning system maintains capabilities A and B, learns that
keeping B blocks adaptation, and "gradually reorganizes its internal
governance to relinquish B". By contrast "an external optimizer might simply
erase B's representation". "The observable loss of B could be identical. Yet
the causal histories differ." The first is constitutive revision, which
[CLAIM-284](CLAIM-284.md) counts as part of a self's governing "the conditions of its own
persistence, differentiation and transformation". The second is excision.
[CLAIM-194](CLAIM-194.md) is the reason the outcome cannot settle which: a coherent boundary
does not show whether the organization made it or had it imposed.

A38 also kept the guard [CLAIM-075](CLAIM-075.md) states: "a demand's causal contribution to
persistence does not establish its moral authority." That a demand was the
organization's own does not make its loss a harm.

## Learning can change the bearer

A38 §7: "if learning creates differentiated processes, changes their
relative authority, or reorganizes the boundaries between them, the original
decomposition may cease to be the most explanatory one." Two analyses are
then needed: "The first holds the original demands fixed and asks whether
the later organization satisfies them more effectively. The second allows
the internal decomposition to change and asks whether a new, persistent
organization has emerged."

[CASE-tmpuc54q](../cases.d/CASE-tmpuc54q.md) is the simple case: L_A(θ) = (θ − 1)² and L_B(θ) = (θ + 1)²
on one shared θ, then θ_A and θ_B with routing by context. If the demands
are stated on outcomes (A-context outputs near 1, B-context outputs near −1),
they are the same demands after the split, and only R(O_t) grew: achievement.
The correspondence is trivial there because the demands, not their bearers,
are what D₀ indexes. It stops being trivial when the bearer itself splits or
merges, so that one earlier demand has two later heirs or none. [CLAIM-287](CLAIM-287.md)
says continuity of the organization can branch and merge and "is not an
identity relation"; demands borne by it can do the same.
[QUESTION-tmp4yz9o](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp4yz9o.md) asks how to track them then.

## Against [THEORY-232](../theory.d/THEORY-232.md)

A38 called "**The attribution of frustration relief must be indexed to an
individuation and an intertemporal correspondence of demands**" "a
defensible new formal principle". Half of it is Watson's, as the record
reads him: the comparison of a whole with its parts is "relative to a chosen
decomposition and the parts' goals" ([THEORY-232](../theory.d/THEORY-232.md)), and the organism's agency
"can be different or absent if we define the agency of the parts
differently". Watson compares one set of parts under two organizations,
"given some original organization of the parts to that obtained with some
new organization of the parts". His parts stay fixed. What this claim adds
is the case where they do not: when a part is excised, split or made to
bear a different demand, the comparison needs a correspondence as well as a
decomposition. Watson's measure is also a total ("the total frustration or
energy"), and a total cannot tell achievement from excision, since removing
a demand also lowers it.

## The converse error

The suppression row warns that a measure can score suppression as relief.
The anthology's reading of First Contact ([ANTH-LIT-503](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/literature.d/LIT-503.md), [ANTH-NOTE-250](https://github.com/dmarx/anthology-of-the-sota/blob/main/record/notes.d/NOTE-250.md)) has
the converse. In shared-autonomy Lunar Lander, an assistant that prevents
crashes, "which necessarily involves ignoring a large fraction of the user's
commands", drove the one-step mutual-information score down while increasing
the user's influence over later states. The proxy scored genuine relief as
suppression. The repair was a horizon Δ, which "requires prior knowledge of
the timescale of the user's desired influence". In this claim's terms the
horizon H is part of the comparison, fixed before scoring, and the wrong H
misclassifies in either direction.

## What it does not say

- It does not say constitutive transformation is failure. A38: "I would not
  require an individual to preserve every historical commitment. Doing so
  would turn our account of selfhood into an implausible essentialism."
  Achievement and transformation answer different questions.
- It does not say how to choose D₀, H or B. Different choices give
  different verdicts, as Watson's decomposition does.
- It does not supply the correspondence when processes branch or merge. That
  is [QUESTION-tmp4yz9o](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp4yz9o.md).
- It does not say that differentiation makes new individuals. A38: it "does
  **not** automatically mean that two new autonomous individuals have
  formed."
