# LIT-tmpr01z3: What is nonclassical about uncertainty relations?

Lorenzo Catani, Matthew Leifer, Giovanni Scala, David Schmid and Robert W. Spekkens (2022), *Physical Review Letters* 129, 240401.

Filed for [QUESTION-tmpsbq58](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpsbq58.md): the result that uncertainty-type trade-offs as such are compatible with generalized (Spekkens) noncontextuality, while their functional form can violate it. That marks which observed trade-offs in pragmatic judgements would not refute that notion.
