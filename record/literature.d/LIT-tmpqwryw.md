# LIT-tmpqwryw: The stance triangle

John W. Du Bois (2007), in Robert Englebretson (ed.), *Stancetaking in
Discourse: Subjectivity, evaluation, interaction*, Pragmatics & Beyond New
Series 164, John Benjamins, pp. 139–182 — DOI-10.1075/pbns.164.07du

## Key takeaways

*Registered, not read.* Crossref deposits no abstract. Known here by what it
is standardly cited for: the stance triangle, in which a single act of
stancetaking by one subject both evaluates a shared object and, through that,
positions the subject and aligns or disaligns them with another subject who
takes a stance on the same object.
