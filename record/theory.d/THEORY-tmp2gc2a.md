# THEORY-tmp2gc2a: Wang and Tang show that cells coupled through a shared dissipative signal begin a collective oscillation when loop gain and phase match, Nα₁R̃ₐ(ω)R̃ₛ(ω) = 1, and that because the medium lags the cells must lead in phase, which cells that adapt to a constant stimulus do in an active frequency window where energy flows from cell to signal, so quiescent adaptive cells oscillate together past a density where none oscillates alone

## Source

Wang and Tang (2019), [LIT-2256](../literature.d/LIT-2256.md), read in [NOTE-tmpe2mdk](../notes.d/NOTE-tmpe2mdk.md).

## What was actually shown

A linear-instability condition for the quiescent state of N cells and a
shared signal; a proof that adaptation yields a phase lead in a frequency
window; simulations of three model classes in which onset density and
frequency match the prediction; agreement with two published experiments.

## What this does not say

That every collective oscillation of cells is of this kind (glycolytic
cells can also oscillate alone in part of their phase diagram), or
anything about the amplitude far from onset.
