# CLAIM-tmpyaro8: Noticing a distinction that keeps a class stable, so that instances of one kind are still recognized as that kind across changes of context, presentation or application and are not confused with a neighbouring kind, is one way an inquiry progresses

## The claim

The owner, U22: "highlight examples from this conversation that
demonstrate how noticing a distinction that maintains class stability
contributes to the progression of an inquiry". The request presupposes
the thesis: a distinction can advance an inquiry not by adding a
category but by keeping one stable, so that what the inquiry calls one
kind is still recognized as that kind when the context, the presentation
or the application changes, and is no longer confused with a kind beside
it.

## Where it stands on the record

- [CLAIM-764](CLAIM-764.md) lists "a distinction previous accounts conflate" among the
  ways the inquiry advances without an experiment. This claim says how
  one kind of distinction does it: by stabilizing a class.
- Luria's own principle that an awkward structure reports a distinction
  the model has stopped expressing ([LU-DP-016](https://github.com/dmarx/luria/blob/main/docs/design-principles.md#dp-16)) is the noticing half, for
  the record's grammar: the friction is the sign, and the remedy is to
  represent the distinction again. [CLAIM-419](CLAIM-419.md) holds that the record treats
  such a misfit as a defect of the grammar and amends it, with [CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md)
  as its instance, and [CLAIM-794](CLAIM-794.md) that refining a category makes further
  misfits more noticeable. This claim generalizes from the grammar to any
  object of an inquiry.
- The identity failures the record finds in its exchanges ([CLAIM-177](CLAIM-177.md),
  [CLAIM-483](CLAIM-483.md): concepts redefined silently, objections returning as new)
  are failures of class stability, and its codes are its device for
  keeping identities fixed by decision ([SYNTHESIS-013](../syntheses.d/SYNTHESIS-013.md)).
- [CLAIM-762](CLAIM-762.md) is the converse movement: collapsing differences that do not
  matter is how classes are made in the first place.

## What it does not say

That every new distinction advances an inquiry, or that a stable class
is a correct one: an accountable record can keep a vocabulary stable that
leaves out the right explanation ([CASE-084](../cases.d/CASE-084.md)). A's qualification at A47
says the same ([CLAIM-tmph11zb](CLAIM-tmph11zb.md)).
