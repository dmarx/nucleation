# LEAD-156: Search for human studies that separate the germline route of intergenerational trauma from the uterine, parenting and cultural routes, and for studies of descendants' distress against group identification

## What it would do

Search for paternal-line and adoption designs, and read the registered
works.

Where the record names it:

- [QUESTION-297](../questions.d/QUESTION-297.md): "children of trauma-exposed fathers conceived after the
  trauma and raised apart from them, or adoptees."
- [NOTE-1291](../notes.d/NOTE-1291.md): "A study measuring descendants' identification with the
  group, separately from family exposure, would answer the question's second
  part"

## The works

- [LIT-1692](../literature.d/LIT-1692.md) (Unfetched): Yehuda 2016, "Holocaust Exposure Induced
  Intergenerational Effects on FKBP5 Methylation"
- [LIT-1715](../literature.d/LIT-1715.md) (Unfetched): Yehuda 2018, "Intergenerational transmission of
  trauma effects: putative role of epigenetic mechanisms"
- [LIT-1764](../literature.d/LIT-1764.md) (Unfetched): Pembrey 2005, "Sex-specific, male-line
  transgenerational responses in humans"
- [LIT-1678](../literature.d/LIT-1678.md) (Unfetched): Ord 2020, "The Precipice: Existential Risk and the
  Future of Humanity"

## What would count

A study that separates the routes answers [QUESTION-297](../questions.d/QUESTION-297.md), or shows that no
separation has been achieved.
