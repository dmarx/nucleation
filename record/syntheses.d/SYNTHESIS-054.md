# SYNTHESIS-054: Relative to a stated target, a combined form is proposed to be one system while it is a gap-isolated block whose gap beats the cost of naming it, a test that passes one-way drive, reads peers or hierarchy from the direction of coupling, cannot tell a produced boundary from an imposed one, and has an unanswered necessary half because a closed collective mode can exist with no block; drive between levels is apportioned by a profile that changes over time; a factor is proposed to be an agent when it is one system, is better described as pursuing goals than as a device and would adapt its policy, and a willed agent when a will-organization over several regimes describes it better still; and in the self-governing sense what endogenous governance adds is the capacity to revise its own standards, which a population optimizing objectives the modeller fixed does not show

*A part account of the distributed-agency line: the part on when a
combined form is one system, where its boundary lies, when a structure is
worth naming as a unit, how drive is apportioned in it, and what makes a
factor an agent, with the self-governing sense of "self" that the agent
tiers end in. The line's account is built over it and its six sibling
parts (conferral, normative-institutional, individuation, frustration,
persistence, collective-stance). Five components are first in another
line and are integrated here because their `line` includes
distributed-agency: [CLAIM-159](../claims.d/CLAIM-159.md) (will-organization, where [SYNTHESIS-036](SYNTHESIS-036.md) sets
it out), [CLAIM-356](../claims.d/CLAIM-356.md) (learned-structure, also [SYNTHESIS-026](SYNTHESIS-026.md) and
[SYNTHESIS-041](SYNTHESIS-041.md)), [CLAIM-367](../claims.d/CLAIM-367.md) (learned-structure, [SYNTHESIS-026](SYNTHESIS-026.md)), [CLAIM-409](../claims.d/CLAIM-409.md)
(formal-apparatus) and [CASE-024](../cases.d/CASE-024.md) (organizational-affect, [SYNTHESIS-018](SYNTHESIS-018.md)).
Eleven more also carry formal-apparatus. The part was found by a mechanical
community map; its 81 components are linked by 260 pairs of relations or
mentions among themselves.*
