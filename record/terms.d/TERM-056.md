# TERM-056: question drift: a provisional answer becoming part of the question an inquiry is pursuing, as distinct from query drift, in which the queries move away from a question that stays fixed

## Definition

A21 §3: "Query drift: retrieval terms progressively depart from the
original information need. Question drift: a provisional explanation
becomes embedded in the definition of the problem being investigated."
Its example: "Why did it fail?" becomes "Why was the dosage
insufficient?" A23 VI.C: "The latter can persist even when individual
searches appear locally relevant."

## What it must not be confused with

- **Query drift**, the IR term ([LIT-1079](../literature.d/LIT-1079.md)), where the need stays fixed and the
  search leaves it.
- **Legitimate refinement.** QUESTION entries refine one another on
  purpose. Refinement is announced and keeps the broader question; drift
  replaces it unannounced. [CLAIM-189](../claims.d/CLAIM-189.md)'s line between announced and silent
  change is the line here.
- **Concept drift** as [CLAIM-177](../claims.d/CLAIM-177.md) uses it, where a term's meaning moves.
  Here the term can stay put and the question move.
