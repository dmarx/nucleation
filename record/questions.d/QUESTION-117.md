# QUESTION-117: Does coupling the construction of meaning to the allocation of interpretive authority produce modes, attractors or transitions that established models of opinion dynamics with evolving influence (reflected appraisal, adaptive networks, persuasion bias) cannot reproduce as economically?

## Why it is a question

A15: "**Does coupling the construction of meaning to the allocation of
interpretive authority produce causal modes, attractors, or transitions
that cannot be reproduced as economically by established models of
opinion formation with evolving influence networks?**" It is the
explanatory-novelty half of A15's question; the other half, whether such
modes persist through turnover and individuate an organization, is
[QUESTION-115](QUESTION-115.md) and [QUESTION-118](QUESTION-118.md). A15: "We can establish the first
without establishing the second."

The candidate predecessors, as A15 described them (registered, unread):

- **Reflected appraisal** (Jia, MirTabatabaei, Friedkin and Bullo, [LIT-1146](../literature.d/LIT-1146.md)): opinions
  converge on each issue, and each member's social power on the next
  issue is set by its influence on the last. A15's mapping: the record's
  momentary endorsement, trust and authority ([CLAIM-277](../claims.d/CLAIM-277.md)). The difference
  A15 claims: the record's operators decide what is retrieved, what counts
  as evidence and how the next query is framed, and their authority is
  indexed by role ([CLAIM-281](../claims.d/CLAIM-281.md)).
- **Adaptive networks** (Holme and Newman, [LIT-1128](../literature.d/LIT-1128.md); Gross and Blasius, [LIT-1139](../literature.d/LIT-1139.md)): opinions and
  ties co-evolve, with a phase transition between regimes.
- **Persuasion bias** (DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel, [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)): agents who do not
  discount repeated information give social position influence apart
  from accuracy. A15: "Endorsement-mediated reinforcement could sometimes
  amount to repeated counting of correlated evidence."

The record already holds two that A15 named as new: Golub and Jackson's
naïve learning ([THEORY-321](../theory.d/THEORY-321.md), [LIT-999](../literature.d/LIT-999.md), read), on which A15 rested
[CLAIM-276](../claims.d/CLAIM-276.md)'s fidelity, and Kirby, Cornish and Smith's cumulative cultural
evolution ([LIT-771](../literature.d/LIT-771.md), read), on which it rested selection for
transmissibility over accuracy. Its readings of Zollman ([THEORY-143](../theory.d/THEORY-143.md)) and
Holman and Bruner ([THEORY-146](../theory.d/THEORY-146.md)) are right.

This is what [CLAIM-278](../claims.d/CLAIM-278.md) demands of the operator framework: an outcome it
forbids. If a reduced [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) is a DeGroot–Friedkin system, A15: "it
would let us adopt an established stability analysis and reserve the new
work for the mechanisms that actually differ."

## What would count as an answer

A derivation placing a reduced [CASE-092](../cases.d/CASE-092.md) inside one of these models, or a
phenomenon from A15's suite (capture without fidelity, hysteresis,
role-sensitive recovery, persistence through operator turnover) that the
coupled meaning–authority model produces and they cannot at matched
complexity. [CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s instability condition is the linear part
any of them would have to reproduce.

## Note of 2026-10-18: a crossed ablation, and the condition it must meet

A32 of the exchange of 2026-10-17 proposed a design for this question while
applying the coupled model to learning: crossed ablations in a learner with
specialized operators, an influence-allocation mechanism and an internal
estimate of strain, in four conditions (strain represented or not, able to
change endorsement or not). Its prediction is not lower loss in the fully
coupled condition but "distinctive **persistent regulatory modes**:
coordinated shifts in which operators are recruited, which evidence is
inspected, and which adaptation strategies are maintained". And it named this
question's condition without citing it: "the model must make a prediction
that its constituent frameworks do not make separately."

The design answers this question only if a persistent mode appears in the
coupled condition that a model of evolving influence with fixed operators
does not produce at matched complexity. The comparison classes are the ones
above, and [CLAIM-297](../claims.d/CLAIM-297.md)'s defeater, reproduction by a single adaptive-retrieval
process, is the same test.

## Note of 2026-10-21: a fifth candidate predecessor

A25 §2 of the exchange of 2026-10-20 asked this question again without
citing it: "whether our second authority variable changes the dynamics in
ways that cannot already be represented as endogenous network weights,
strategic signaling, or standard adaptive learning", and "[CLAIM-330](../claims.d/CLAIM-330.md)'s
instability condition is valid mathematics. Its novelty cannot rest on the
eigenvalue calculation itself." It adds a candidate: Mostagir, Ozdaglar
and Siderius, "When Is Society Susceptible to Manipulation?"
(Management Science 2022, doi:10.1287/mnsc.2021.4265), now registered
unread as [LIT-1318](../literature.d/LIT-1318.md). A25 says it "concerns strategic interference with
social learning, rather than a dynamically evolving allocation of
authority over interpretation". That is the third exchange to pose the
question, after A15 of 2026-10-16 and A32 of 2026-10-17. The predecessors
registered here ([LIT-1146](../literature.d/LIT-1146.md), [LIT-1128](../literature.d/LIT-1128.md), [LIT-1139](../literature.d/LIT-1139.md), [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)) are still unread.
A25's other first-ranked work, Weatherall, O'Connor and Bruner, is
registered as [LIT-1341](../literature.d/LIT-1341.md).

Both were sought on 2026-10-21. Mostagir, Ozdaglar and Siderius is
Unavailable: copies exist (Deep Blue, CORE, an author's site) but none
could be fetched here, so the check against it stays open. Weatherall,
O'Connor and Bruner was read in full ([NOTE-1038](../notes.d/NOTE-1038.md)): its dynamics are
open-loop, with no coupling from the audience back to the evidence or the
scientists, so it is not a predecessor of the second authority variable;
A25's "alongside" fits. The question stays Open.

## Note of 2026-10-25: endogenous influence and repeated messages, again

From A36, A38 and A43 of the exchange of 2026-10-24, continued.

- **Endogenous influence.** A36 proposed an influence matrix that evolves,
  under which dL = ℓᵀdx + (dℓ)ᵀx, and a loop from access through influence,
  observed performance and endorsement back to access, as "a microscopic
  realization of the meaning–authority–legibility loop". The nearest exact model is
  reflected appraisal ([THEORY-916](../theory.d/THEORY-916.md)), in which ℓ is fed back as
  self-weight. Not cited.
- **Repeated messages.** A38 reported that a population counting forwarded
  messages as new evidence was more confident and less accurate than one
  that tracked sources: 98.4% against 95.6% confidence, 95.0% against 97.0%
  accuracy, with about 16 repeated observations counted per episode, over
  eight seeds. That is persuasion bias ([LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)), from a simulation the
  record does not hold. A43 added that distinct source identities need not
  be independent (its `correlation_group`), which is also what changes the
  collective noise in [CASE-160](../cases.d/CASE-160.md) from Σℓᵢ²σᵢ² to ℓᵀΣℓ.

The question is unchanged.

## Note of 2026-10-26: phenomena shown, no comparison made

Of A15's suite, the Perspective Population Lab now shows capture without
fidelity and history dependence in stipulated models ([CASE-162](../cases.d/CASE-162.md)), and
responses to intervention that ordinary trajectories conceal
([CASE-161](../cases.d/CASE-161.md)). None was set against DeGroot–Friedkin, an adaptive
network or persuasion bias, so none bears on "as economically".

A comparator pilot (dmarx/PerspectiveLab@dce302b
experiments/comparator_pilot/) adds a constraint on any such comparison.
A generic model with the same transition kernels makes the same
predictions, by induction in time. So the comparison has to be of
restrictions, compression or transport, with knowledge of the candidate
mechanisms priced. The pilot's report says it does not settle this
question. The question is unchanged.

The lab's v2.3 (dmarx/PerspectiveLab@e9efcf4
experiments/matched_comparator/) is the first comparison on equal terms
of data and fitted parameters, though not yet against the models this
question names. An 8-coefficient structural predictor and generic system
identifiers are fitted on the same source transitions and tested on
withheld production laws and a withheld dependency graph. A same-basis
state-space model ties exactly. A generic 20-coefficient polynomial does
better on the unseen bottleneck law with the mixed graph, MSE 0.001865
against 0.004514 over 24 seeds. An 8-coefficient generic polynomial
leads in one familiar regime, within seed noise. The structural basis is not priced in
description length, and DeGroot–Friedkin, adaptive networks and
persuasion bias are still not among the rivals.

## Note of 2026-10-29: three of the predecessors, read

Holme and Newman ([LIT-1128](../literature.d/LIT-1128.md)), Gross and Blasius ([LIT-1139](../literature.d/LIT-1139.md)) and DeMarzo,
Vayanos and Zwiebel ([LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)) are now read, so the summary's "registered
unread" and the note of 2026-10-21's "still unread" no longer hold for
them. Each sets a different floor.

- **Holme and Newman** ([NOTE-1385](../notes.d/NOTE-1385.md)). It has no influence weights, no
  authority, no evidence and no turnover: opinions are labels, every tie
  is equal, and the dynamics is absorbing. What it reproduces
  economically is fragmentation into like-minded camps against one
  dominant camp, controlled by the ratio of selecting one's contacts to
  being influenced by them, with a critical point between. It cannot
  express an outcome that depends on who has authority over whom. An
  answer here that rests on fragmentation alone would be matched by it.
- **Gross and Blasius** ([NOTE-1394](../notes.d/NOTE-1394.md)). The review supplies vocabulary
  and a list of mechanisms, not tools: no model in it has authority or
  role, and its only weighted, directed cases are coupled oscillators.
  Two items bear. Like-and-like reinforcement producing persistent leader
  and follower classes is a predecessor to beat if endorsement
  concentration is offered as new. Moment closure, a few variables for
  state plus topology, is the kind of economical reduction "as
  economically" invites. Its summary of Holme and Newman is wrong, so it
  should not be used as a pointer to that model.
- **DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel** ([NOTE-1392](../notes.d/NOTE-1392.md)). The fixed-weight model
  already gives one of A15's suite, capture without fidelity: by its
  Theorem 2 an unbalanced network's consensus departs from the
  precision-weighted optimum with no change in anyone's accuracy. Its
  weights never move, so it has no hysteresis and no feedback from
  outcome to authority, and operator turnover would only change the
  listening matrix. Whatever the coupled model claims beyond
  position-weighted consensus has to come from moving weights.

Guida, Minutola and Presti, [LIT-1218](../literature.d/LIT-1218.md) ([NOTE-1353](../notes.d/NOTE-1353.md)), demand that an
agent's leverage be measured independently of how far its output
spreads. That is the circularity guard an answer here needs.

The question is unchanged and stays Open.

## Note of 2026-10-29: the like-and-like predecessor, read, and the moving-weights companion, not reached

- **Ito and Kaneko**, [LIT-tmpwxstp](../literature.d/LIT-tmpwxstp.md) ([NOTE-tmp2xl62](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp2xl62.md)), the like-and-like
  reinforcement the earlier note of this date takes from Gross and
  Blasius, is now read. It is closer to this question's endorsement
  circuit than the review suggests. Each unit allocates a fixed budget of
  attention (its incoming weights sum to 1), moves attention toward those
  whose state resembles its own, and is driven by those it attends to.
  From identical units with no role primitive, it gives a persistent
  asymmetry of influence, a core of about 15 attended by most and
  attending almost only to itself, with links turning over, and an
  ablation shows that the coupling of state to allocation is needed. So an
  answer that offers persistent authority classes, or their need for the
  coupling of state and authority, as new is matched by this model. What
  it cannot express is what the question's circuit adds: states with
  content that can be right or wrong, any evaluation of outcomes, and
  authority indexed by role or issue. The outcome to beat is concentration
  without regard to accuracy. That is also persuasion bias's outcome
  ([LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md)), and this model gets it with moving weights and hysteresis,
  which persuasion bias lacks.
- **DeMarzo, Vayanos and Zwiebel 2001**, [LIT-tmpapuas](../literature.d/LIT-tmpapuas.md), the companion to
  [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md) in which whom an agent listens to is chosen by what listening
  earns, is registered and Unavailable: no copy of the working paper was
  found. As [LIT-1134](../literature.d/LIT-1134.md) describes it, it would be a predecessor with evolving
  influence that already gives capture without fidelity. Whether listening
  there is scored against the interpretation it made dominant, or by
  trading profits that depend on what the asset pays, can only be read
  from the paper.

The question is unchanged and stays Open.
