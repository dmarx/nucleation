# QUESTION-tmpilsf5: Is the presheaf of causal functions on a space of input histories, with the lowerset topology, a sheaf exactly when the space has no solipsistic contextuality witness, as Theorem 2.34 of The Topology of Causality states?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-171](../theory.d/THEORY-171.md) holds the existence statement: when causal constraints depend on
context, deterministic assignments that are causal in each context can fail
to glue. One checked example settles that, and [LIT-808](../literature.d/LIT-808.md)'s Θ₃ is one, with
[LIT-843](../literature.d/LIT-843.md)'s Example 7.2 a second ([NOTE-612](../notes.d/NOTE-612.md)). Until 2026-10-19 [THEORY-171](../theory.d/THEORY-171.md)'s
promote_when also asked for the characterisation. At the owner's direction,
from the promotion audit's batch 008 (ADR-047), the THEORY was narrowed to
what is checked, and the characterisation moved here.

It is open because the proof of Theorem 2.34 has not been read ([NOTE-612](../notes.d/NOTE-612.md),
claim C2: "proof, not checked"), and the paper is an unrefereed preprint.
The count in its introduction, 1767 of the 2644 causally complete spaces on
3 events with binary inputs, is also unchecked.

## What would answer it

The condition formerly on [THEORY-171](../theory.d/THEORY-171.md), unchanged: a published, refereed
version of Theorem 2.34 of [LIT-808](../literature.d/LIT-808.md), or an independent proof of it read and
checked, showing that the presheaf of causal functions over the lowerset
topology of a space of input histories is a sheaf exactly when the space has
no solipsistic contextuality witness. One more worked non-gluing example
does not count, since an example shows only that some spaces fail, which is
the half already checked.
