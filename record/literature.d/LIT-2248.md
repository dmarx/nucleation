# LIT-2248: Low Dimensional Behavior of Large Systems of Globally Coupled Oscillators

Edward Ott and Thomas M. Antonsen (2008). "Low Dimensional Behavior of Large Systems of Globally Coupled Oscillators." *Chaos* 18(3): 037113. [ARXIV-0806.0004](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0004) DOI-10.1063/1.2930766.

## Key takeaways

- An exact finite-dimensional law for the order parameter on an invariant
  manifold, with no small parameter: the Kuramoto problem with Lorentzian
  frequencies reduces to dρ/dt + (1 − K/2)ρ + (K/2)ρ³ = 0, solved in
  closed form; drives, communities and delays reduce likewise.
- On the manifold the distribution keeps "a continuous spectrum of neutral
  modes" (p. 9): the law is closed without the microscopic degrees of
  freedom relaxing.
- The manifold "need not be attracting for the microscopic state" (note
  24); the sequel (LIT-tmpd7koy) shows it attracts the order parameter
  when frequencies are spread.
