- **A correction.** [THEORY-tmprpscg](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmprpscg.md) (the reading of Lohmiller and
  Slotine) said partial contraction keeps the distinctions transverse to
  the manifold. It erases them and keeps those along it. Corrected in
  place, version 2.
- **A citation that overreaches.** Velarde and colleagues cite Chen and
  colleagues for "Any parameter set that is invariant under GD becomes a
  stable attractor under stochastic GD for sufficiently large learning
  rates". The paper gives a sufficient condition for sets invariant under
  every minibatch, for a continuous model, and shows collapse stopping
  above a critical learning rate in a toy. The note on [THEORY-tmpz825j](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpz825j.md)
  says so. No ARG: the overreach is in a reading's account of its source,
  not in a support relation the record argues.
- **Dated notes on fifty-two entries**, each saying which kind of bearing
  it is (step 8): sixteen from Wang and Slotine, twenty-five from Chen and
  colleagues, eleven from Poole and colleagues. Among them: [CLAIM-481](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-481.md)'s
  open point on selective contraction now has its source;
  [CLAIM-tmpw8xkt](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpw8xkt.md)'s objection that contraction leaves a single class is
  answered by partial contraction, which keeps a continuum, not classes;
  Chen and colleagues' collapse is a candidate mechanism, by analogy, for
  overconsolidation ([CLAIM-tmp8ljvp](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp8ljvp.md)) and plasticity loss
  ([THEORY-tmpgjb4y](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpgjb4y.md)), with a measure for [CASE-tmphsr99](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmphsr99.md); and Poole and
  colleagues never speak of an edge of chaos, an emphasis that is
  Schoenholz and colleagues' ([THEORY-tmpqtb3q](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpqtb3q.md), [THEORY-tmpvtafk](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-tmpvtafk.md)).
