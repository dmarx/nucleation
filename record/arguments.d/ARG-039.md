# ARG-039: Reconstruction chains → frame retention

## The inference

[CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md) and [CASE-032](../cases.d/CASE-032.md) are chains of reconstructions written by the
assistant. In the stable chain the final text drops the hustle and the bet
and is said to keep the friend's complicity, while content-word overlap
falls. So lexical similarity cannot tell kept footing from a changed act,
and a reconstruction study needs a pragmatic outcome ([CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md)).

## Critical questions

- **Would the evidence be expected if the hypothesis were false?** Yes. The
  retention was asserted by the author of the chains, who held the
  hypothesis.
- **Does it tell the hypothesis apart from its rivals?** No. Without an
  independent judgement of footing there is no measured retention for
  overlap to fail to predict.
- **Does the measure measure what it is said to?** No. This is the question
  that fails. [CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md): "the retention of complicity is the author's
  reading, not a measurement. It illustrates [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md) and can test
  nothing." [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md) says the same of itself: "They illustrate it, and
  their pragmatic retention was asserted, not measured."

Rejected as evidence for [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md). The chains now stand as [CLAIM-084](../claims.d/CLAIM-084.md)'s
`illustrated_by`, which is what the verdict asks. [CLAIM-229](../claims.d/CLAIM-229.md) also noted that
[CASE-030](../cases.d/CASE-030.md)'s chain is the record's nearest case of the act kept while the
proposition changes, and that it fails for the same reason
([ARG-021](ARG-021.md)).

Filed 2026-10-28 by the [ADR-061](../decisions.d/ADR-061.md) migration, from [CLAIM-229](../claims.d/CLAIM-229.md).
