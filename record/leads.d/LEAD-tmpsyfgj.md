# LEAD-tmpsyfgj: Read Slingerland's openly available work on wu-wei, as a neighbour for his unavailable book, to check the record's wu-wei entries: whether the paradox of wu-wei is about acquiring and keeping effortless action, and whether the cultivation and unlearning routes differ in what they conserve

## What to do

Find a lawful copy of an openly available work of Slingerland's on
wu-wei, such as an article or an author's manuscript (step 5 of the
read-and-file skill, ADR-074). File it as a neighbour of LIT-1994 with a
LIT, a NOTE and a THEORY. Read it for three things:

1. **The paradox.** How it is stated, and whether it concerns how
   effortless action is acquired and kept (CLAIM-tmpaj28t).
2. **The routes.** Whether the Confucian and Daoist routes are
   distinguished by what they conserve and what they shed (CLAIM-tmpaj28t).
3. **Effortlessness.** Whether wu-wei is described as action timed and
   directed by the situation's own tendencies, which CLAIM-541 and
   CLAIM-tmp0doyo read it as, or as the absence of agency.

## What would close it

A NOTE on a neighbour that bears on each of the three, and dated notes on
the three entries saying whether it supports, qualifies or leaves them
open.
