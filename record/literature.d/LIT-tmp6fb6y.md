# LIT-tmp6fb6y: Procedural Skill Retention and Decay: A Meta-Analytic Review, data and analysis script

Tatel, C. E. & Ackerman, P. L. (2024–2025). *Procedural Skill Retention & Decay: A Meta-Analytic Review* [data and R script]. OSF project. doi:10.17605/OSF.IO/BGFYP. https://osf.io/bgfyp/

## Key takeaways

*From the analysis, NOTE-tmps1muw and CASE-tmp4f2jr.*

- **The article's data.** 1,273 independent-samples effect sizes (827
  accuracy, 268 speed, 178 mixed), coded by task domain: medical/dental
  504, laboratory/artificial 382, sports 222, military and transport 91,
  miscellaneous 74.
- **Reproduces the abstract.** The authors' specification gives slopes of
  −0.076, −0.063 and −0.061 SD a month for accuracy, speed and mixed
  measures.
- **By task type (accuracy).** Laboratory tasks decline by 0.16 SD a
  month, medical and dental by 0.04, and sports and military and transport
  tasks not measurably.
- **Not linear.** Every type's slope is steeper when only short intervals
  are fitted. Within six months laboratory accuracy declines by 0.54 SD a
  month and medical and dental by 0.12.
