# LIT-tmprst8w: Order Out of Chaos: Man's New Dialogue with Nature

Ilya Prigogine, Isabelle Stengers (1984), *Toronto and New York: Bantam Books (xxxi, 349 pp.; foreword by Alvin Toffler; LCCN 83021403; OCLC 10146336); also London: Heinemann and Boulder: New Science Library, 1984* — https://www.worldcat.org/oclc/10146336

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-02 at the owner's request, as one of the works filling out
the record's coverage of Prigogine's dissipative-structure programme. It is
the programme's statement for general readers, where the Nobel lecture
([LIT-tmp452jw](LIT-tmp452jw.md)) is its technical statement.

`Deferred` because no lawful full text was reachable, so it is not read and
no NOTE is filed. What follows is only what the catalogue records show.

Two contents headings bear directly on what the record holds. "Linear
Thermodynamics" and "Far from Equilibrium" are the distinction the Nobel
lecture draws around minimum entropy production. "The Encounter with
Molecular Biology" is where the book presumably takes up the
life-as-dissipation question that [LIT-211](LIT-211.md)'s "Dissipative Self-Organizing
Systems" cluster rests on. Whether it does, and how far it goes, is for a
reading to say.

**Priority for a deeper reading: low.** The technical claims are in the
Nobel lecture, which was read. The book's distinctive content is
philosophical and historical (time, the "new alliance" of science and
nature), and the reviews found would be the place to start on its reception.
