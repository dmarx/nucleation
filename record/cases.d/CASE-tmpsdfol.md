# CASE-tmpsdfol: A proposed study of the label "anti-vax": matched descriptions of vaccination-related positions, crossed with the label's presence, the classifier's authority and a chance to reconsider, measuring where the label is applied, what is inferred, how the labelled are treated, what evidence or authority leads to reclassification, and whether those labelled adopt it, to tell descriptive classification, external labelling effects, self-identification and practical resistance apart

## The case

**The design (A41 §12).** From the exchange "Individuation, Possibility,
and Disposition" (first share
https://chatgpt.com/share/6aee7a9b-f13c-83e9-a865-6a29c2c3ff7e; second
share https://chatgpt.com/share/6aeea2bc-d280-83e9-ac1f-f6772adf263c).
Give participants "descriptions of vaccination-related positions that
independently vary the type of vaccine, evidence concerns, acceptance
behavior, policy preferences, and access conditions"; ask which they
classify as *anti-vax* and what they infer; "ask which additional evidence
would lead them to change the attribution". For otherwise matched
descriptions, "vary whether an evaluative label is used or whether the
relevant behavior is described without a label". Then follow those
classified: "The third component would investigate social feedback
longitudinally."

**What A55 §5 adds.** Two factors and a measure. "The design could cross
descriptions of vaccination-related attitudes with the presence or absence
of the label, the authority of the classifier, and the opportunity to
reconsider the attribution." "The study should also measure whether
individuals adopt, reject, or remain indifferent to the label." Its four
variables are category application, inferential consequences, practical
consequences and revision governance ("What evidence or authority can lead
to reclassification?"). Its purpose is "to discriminate among descriptive
classification, external labeling effects, internal identification, and
practical resistance".

**Kept apart.** The positions [CASE-tmpb16ru](CASE-tmpb16ru.md) records: "It would be important
to distinguish generalized opposition to vaccination, uncertainty
concerning a particular vaccine, opposition to a mandate, and barriers to
access. They should not be stipulated to constitute one attitude."

## What it can show

- **The classifier's authority bears on [CLAIM-tmpxe1ih](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpxe1ih.md).** The reader's
  reading: if the same description is reclassified when the classifier
  lacks authority and not when it has it, jurisdiction is acting on the
  conditions of interpretation, not only on its outputs.
- **The adoption measure is [CASE-141](CASE-141.md)'s point at population scale.** A55:
  [CASE-141](CASE-141.md) "establishes why adoption of a label is not necessary for its
  use to affect someone's circumstances". If treatment of the labelled
  changes whether or not they adopt the label, the label acts as [CLAIM-402](../claims.d/CLAIM-402.md)
  says a classification can, through others' use of it.

## What it cannot show

- **The vignettes reach interpreters, not the classified.** The reader's
  qualification: telling participants about cases is a do(I) intervention
  in [CLAIM-130](../claims.d/CLAIM-130.md)'s sense (as [QUESTION-360](../questions.d/QUESTION-360.md)'s note of 2026-11-07 says of A41
  §12). The vignette arms measure interpreters' profiles. The effects of the
  classification on those classified need the longitudinal arm or field
  data.
- **No causal claim about vaccination.** A55 §5: it "should not assume that
  the *anti-vax* label itself causes people to oppose vaccination", and "Nor
  should a theory of the label be used to relativize established empirical
  evidence about vaccines."
- **Not several concepts from revision alone.** The reader's
  qualification: a difference in what leads to reclassification is not by
  itself evidence that the communities hold two concepts, since [TERM-098](../terms.d/TERM-098.md)
  keeps a concept apart from its corrigibility ([QUESTION-tmpdalhx](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmpdalhx.md)).

As a stipulated design it has no results.
