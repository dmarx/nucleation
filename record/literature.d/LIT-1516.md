# LIT-1516: α-Rank: Multi-Agent Evaluation by Evolution

Omidshafiei, Papadimitriou, Piliouras, Tuyls, Rowland, Lespiau, Czarnecki,
Lanctot, Pérolat & Munos (2019), *Scientific Reports* 9: 9937 —
arXiv:1903.01373, doi:10.1038/s41598-019-45619-9

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-1156](../notes.d/NOTE-1156.md). Page numbers are arXiv v4's.*

- **The chain.** One finite population per player slot. Imitation is
  Fermi with intensity α and mutation is vanishingly rare, so the chain
  runs over pure strategy profiles, with transitions the fixation
  probabilities of a single mutant. Its stationary distribution is unique
  at every finite α (Theorem 2.1.2), and the ranking is the order of its
  masses.
- **The limit.** As α → ∞ the transitions become those of Markov-Conley
  chains on the sink strongly connected components of the weakly
  better-response graph (Theorem 2.5.1). For the replicator flow, every
  asymptotically stable sink chain component contains one, and each lies
  in one chain component (Theorem 2.4.24; [THEORY-942](../theory.d/THEORY-942.md)). "every game is
  a “potential” game, if only we change our solution concept from
  equilibria to chain recurrent sets" (p. 13).
- **What it leaves open.** The infinite-α chain is reducible when there
  are several sinks, as the authors say (p. 15). α-Rank uses a large
  finite α found by a sweep, and no result says what its stationary
  distribution tends to, or how it divides mass among sinks.
- **What it is not.** Not invariant to copies. In rock-paper-scissors
  with scissors duplicated, the single-population ranking moves from a
  three-way tie to rock first (0.4, 0.2, 0.2, 0.2), at each α tried from
  0.1 to 50 ([NOTE-1156](../notes.d/NOTE-1156.md), checked by script).
- **Applications.** AlphaGo's seven variants: all mass on AG(rvp).
  AlphaZero: 56 snapshots, the last on top with 0.39, with a mid-training
  snapshot ranked fifth. MuJoCo soccer: 6 of 10 agents survive, among many
  cycles. Kuhn poker with 3 and 4 players. Leduc poker's PSRO meta-game.

## Why this is here

Filed 2026-10-24 for the record's cycles and governance lines. Its
Conley reading, a potential always exists once each recurrent class is
collapsed to a point, is the dynamical form of a question [TERM-077](../terms.d/TERM-077.md) and
[CLAIM-451](../claims.d/CLAIM-451.md) leave open: what order there is when wins cycle. Its chain
is the finite-population replicator that [CASE-155](../cases.d/CASE-155.md) and [CLAIM-444](../claims.d/CLAIM-444.md)
read as authority ([NOTE-1156](../notes.d/NOTE-1156.md)). It carries `anthology-candidate`
because it is a method for evaluating ML agents. It is filed for
understanding, not as an instruction for practice.
