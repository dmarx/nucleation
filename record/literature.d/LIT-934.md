# LIT-934: Quantum structure in cognition

Diederik Aerts (2009), *Journal of Mathematical Psychology 53(5), 314–348*.

## Key takeaways

*From the reading, [NOTE-808](../notes.d/NOTE-808.md).*

- **Exact classical criteria.** An item's weights µ(A), µ(B),
  µ(A and B) come from one Kolmogorovian space, with "A and B" the
  intersection event, iff µ(A and B) ≤ min(µ(A), µ(B)) and
  1 − µ(A) − µ(B) + µ(A and B) ≥ 0 (Theorems 1–3); dually for "or"
  (Theorems 4–6).
- **Two kinds of failure.** Over- or underextension (∆-type, the guppy
  effect: Cuckoo is 0.575 a Pet and 0.842 a "Bird and Pet"), and a
  negative Kolmogorovian factor (k-type: Almond is 0.2 a Fruit, 0.1 a
  Vegetable and 0.425 a "Fruit or Vegetable").
- **The lattice fits only the k-type.** Tilted subspaces, whose lattice
  is not Boolean, fit k-type items (§2.2). Theorem 9 proves that no
  subspace model with conjunction as meet and disjunction as join can
  fit a ∆-type item, because projection weights are monotone.
- **An emergent concept carries the guppy effect.** The model adds a
  second sector with a fixed 2-dimensional subspace per pair, read as
  "A or B" (or "A and B") formed as a new concept. With one free
  parameter per item it fits all of Hampton's items (Tables 3–4).
- **Negation is a device.** The conjunction model is obtained from the
  disjunction model of hypothetical complements A′, B′ with weight
  1 − µ, through the orthocomplement (§3.3). No negated concept is
  measured.
- **Interpretation, not test.** Two superposed layers of thought,
  classical logical and quantum conceptual, and a link to the
  disjunction effect (one ℂ³ fit of the Hawaii problem).

## Standing in the record

Filed on 2026-10-12 from the owner's papers-feed for [QUESTION-055](../questions.d/QUESTION-055.md), and
read on 2026-10-14 in [NOTE-808](../notes.d/NOTE-808.md). It does not supply the
orthocomplemented negation the filing expected: the subspace lattice it
uses cannot hold its main data, and its orthocomplement only derives one
model from another. What it gives [QUESTION-055](../questions.d/QUESTION-055.md) is a constraint, filed as
[THEORY-255](../theory.d/THEORY-255.md): guppy-type blends cannot be the lattice meet, whatever
the negation. It is the later Aerts paper that [NOTE-294](../notes.d/NOTE-294.md) names as where a
non-distributive concept lattice is put to the test, and it is the first
reading in the record that uses non-commuting subspaces on concept data.
