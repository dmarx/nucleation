# LIT-tmp460ih: Replication and Reproduction

John S. Wilkins, Pierrick Bourrat (2022 revision; first published 2001 by David Hull and John Wilkins). "Replication and Reproduction." In E. N. Zalta and U. Nodelman (eds.), *The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/replication/.

## Key takeaways

From §5.4 ([NOTE-tmp7ylue](../notes.d/NOTE-tmp7ylue.md)): "A reproducer is an entity that develops and has a material overlap between the “parent” and “progeny”"; progeneration is the "increase in numerically distinct objects of a given kind"; "inheritance is a system property, not a property of parts, unless they are also reproducing systems, and is developmentally acquired". Replication is "the terminal form of reproduction, once coding mechanisms have been evolved, which is itself an evolved form of multiplication". Godfrey-Smith finds material overlap too narrow; Griesemer replies that his concern is that parents cause progeny.
