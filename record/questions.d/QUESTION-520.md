# QUESTION-520: Since asking a question and answering it are each resource-bounded, do the two compete for related resources, or is the coupling between them, through time as a shared resource, already shown?

The owner's words, at U27: "so because both queries and responses are
resource bounded, they'd be in competition for related resources? or is
that resource coupling something we've already shown? I think we've
demonstrated time as an influential shared resource in the dynamics here."

## What the record held

- **Both are bounded.** [CLAIM-475](../claims.d/CLAIM-475.md): "Specifying is itself a resource-bounded
  realization". The questions an agent can formulate within its budget are
  [TERM-092](../terms.d/TERM-092.md)'s inquiry frontier.
- **Time is shared.** [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md) decomposes the time to correct into
  discovery, verification, coordination and execution, which "overlap and
  compete", so "A sufficiently long search can make an otherwise available
  remedy unusable". The owner's sense that time has been shown to be a
  shared resource is right on the record's own terms.
- **How stages combine when they do not compete.** [CLAIM-1114](../claims.d/CLAIM-1114.md): in
  sequence their times add, overlapping they contribute the longest, and a
  stage done in advance contributes nothing.

## What makes it open

Whether boundedness implies competition. A52's answer ([CLAIM-1157](../claims.d/CLAIM-1157.md)) is
that it does not: a common budget, a common bottleneck or a sequential
dependence supplies the coupling. What the coupling does to how deep an
inquiry should go is [CLAIM-1008](../claims.d/CLAIM-1008.md), and what it does to the set of
questions an organization can both pose and answer is [CLAIM-1025](../claims.d/CLAIM-1025.md). The
owner's example at U28, a proponent who must justify an idea before time
goes to it, is [CASE-386](../cases.d/CASE-386.md).

## Note of 2026-11-25: answered

[CLAIM-1157](../claims.d/CLAIM-1157.md), A52's answer, went Active on 2026-11-25 under the owner's
delegation of 2026-11-24, after a rerun by a second session reproduced it
and its premise [CLAIM-1114](../claims.d/CLAIM-1114.md) was promoted (dmarx/nucleation#813, [#814](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/issues/814)). It
settles the first part: boundedness does not make asking and answering
compete; a common budget, a common bottleneck or a sequential dependence
supplies the coupling. [TERM-085](../terms.d/TERM-085.md), Active, settles the second: time is a
shared resource, and [CLAIM-1157](../claims.d/CLAIM-1157.md) adds that a shared deadline is not a
shared pool of worker-hours, since independent processes can spend the
same elapsed time at once. Both parts having Active answers, the question
is Answered ([ADR-049](../decisions.d/ADR-049.md)), at the owner's word of 2026-11-25 that whether a
question is answered "shouldn't be a matter of opinion". The wording is
the assistant's (Claude, in this session); adopting it is the owner's
call.
