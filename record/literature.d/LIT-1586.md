Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A2's claim that in *Tettigades* "separate cellular lineages collectively preserve much of the ancestral functionality" rests on [LIT-1563](LIT-1563.md), which reports one split. This paper shows the splitting is repeated, so the pattern is not a one-off. Its search results include it. Registered for [CASE-177](../cases.d/CASE-177.md).
