# LIT-3122: A randomized controlled trial of the judicious use of safety behaviors during exposure therapy

Blakey, Abramowitz, Buchholz, Jessup, Jacoby, Reuman & Pentel (2019),
Behaviour Research and Therapy 112, 28–35. DOI 10.1016/j.brat.2018.11.010.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-22 by [LEAD-032](../leads.d/LEAD-032.md)'s search. With Meckes et al.
([LIT-3123](LIT-3123.md)), the two trials in the search that compare the same
exposure with and without a safety behaviour over follow-up. Both found
no difference in outcome. In both, Meckes et al. report, the safety
behaviour was the experimenter's ("participants were asked to select from
a list of experimenter-generated SB options" here), not the person's own.
Not read.
