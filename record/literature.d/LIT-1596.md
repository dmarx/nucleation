# LIT-1596: Genome sequence of the endocellular bacterial symbiont of aphids Buchnera sp. APS

Shigenobu, S., Watanabe, H., Hattori, M., Sakaki, Y. & Ishikawa, H. (2000). Genome sequence of the endocellular bacterial symbiont of aphids *Buchnera* sp. APS. *Nature* 407(6800): 81–86. doi:10.1038/35024074.

Not read. The summary is drawn from the abstract. A reading should check it first.

## Why this is here

A1 proposed a CASE for aphid–*Buchnera*, the obligate insect–bacterial association, and the record held *Buchnera* only through [NOTE-114](../notes.d/NOTE-114.md). This is the founding genome paper, registered as the primary source for [CASE-173](../cases.d/CASE-173.md). The abstract says the mutualism "is so obligate that neither can reproduce independently". That is mutual dependence in the sense of [LIT-1570](LIT-1570.md).
