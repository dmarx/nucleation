# ARG-tmpmj7av: The graph that asserted an inference nobody made, read as two views of one argument that its grammar made incompatible until a distinction was added, shows by existence that an inquiry can progress by a distinction that restores compatibility between its representations

## The inference

A49: "The old representational scheme could not preserve an important
difference between two relationships. It treated them as the same kind of
edge, even though translating between the corresponding descriptions
produced different consequences." In [CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md) one claim was filed as
supporting another; the link fixer wrote the converse, that the other
rested on it, which was false. Two views of one argument, the supporting
claim's page and the supported claim's, said incompatible things about
the same relation. Adding `corroborates` let both describe it
consistently.

## Critical questions

- **Is the case coherent?** Yes. It is documented (standing documented,
  kind specimen), and its account is checked: the relation was removed
  before merge, and [ADR-044](../decisions.d/ADR-044.md) added `corroborates`.
- **Does it separate the very things the conclusion says can come
  apart?** For one part of [CLAIM-tmplz7ng](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmplz7ng.md), yes: it shows progress made not
  by adding information within a representation but by a distinction that
  made two descriptions agree. The case is about the grammar, the record's
  coordinates in A49's terms; it does not show progress of that kind in the
  inquiry's substantive objects, and one case cannot show that an inquiry is
  best understood as an atlas. That stronger part rests on the analogy.
- **Does the reading add to what the case is already held to show?**
  [CASE-131](../cases.d/CASE-131.md) grounds [CLAIM-419](../claims.d/CLAIM-419.md) and [CLAIM-469](../claims.d/CLAIM-469.md) and illustrates [CLAIM-181](../claims.d/CLAIM-181.md). The
  atlas reading redescribes the same event; it adds that the misfit
  appeared as a disagreement between two views, which is what made it
  noticeable (the case's own "What it does not show").
