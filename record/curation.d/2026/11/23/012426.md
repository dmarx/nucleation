Under the dialectic regime, on 2026-11-23, the owner proposed a principle
the governance experiments may be converging on: "A system's ability to
correct itself depends not simply on having critics or feedback, but on
how evidence, evaluation, and authority are coupled—and what the system
is prevented from rewriting or rejecting." The owner gave four instances,
from LEAD-177, LEAD-015, LEAD-190 and LEAD-180. The owner's words are
quoted from the message.

## Where the record already held it

- **CLAIM-342 (Active)** holds the evidence half: a loop is kept honest by
  evidence whose arrival or scoring does not pass through the
  interpretation it tests.
- **CLAIM-466 and CLAIM-404** hold selection and scoring as separate
  routes. CLAIM-462 and CLAIM-099 hold correctability as its own
  achievement.
- **SYNTHESIS-111** is the account of this theme.
- **The four instances** are Proposed claims on staging, each demonstrated
  in its own model, and each characterized faithfully by the owner.

## What was filed

  resting on the four instances and CLAIM-342. Its admission: a derivation
  for its negative half, a discriminating prediction for its positive
  half.
- **CLAIM-tmpw11e6,** the assistant's qualification (counter,
