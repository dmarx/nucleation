# LIT-tmp6hh1r: The Kosterlitz-Thouless phase transition: an introduction for the intrepid student

Drouin-Touchette (2022). arXiv 2207.13748.

## Key takeaways

- Spin waves alone: g(r) ≈ (πr/a)^(−η(T)), η = k_BT/2πJ, "at all
  low-temperatures, the system is critical" (§2.2, eq. 18).
- The flows "terminate on a line of fixed points" where "the fugacity
  becomes irrelevant" (§3.3).
- At T_KT the correlation length diverges as an essential singularity,
  "not in the same way as usual second-order phase transitions" (§3.4).
