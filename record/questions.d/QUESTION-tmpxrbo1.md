# QUESTION-tmpxrbo1: When a diffusely organized population with no procedure for collective judgment is credited with a stance, or with an effect on an outside institution, when is the credit due to the population as organized, and when is it a biased sample of what some members said or did?

## Why it is a question

A5 §3: "Under what conditions does a diffusely organized population
acquire a collective-level public stance without possessing a procedure
for collective judgment, and what distinguishes that stance from a biased
observation of individual expressions?" Its case is [CASE-tmpq90dm](../cases.d/CASE-tmpq90dm.md). A5
judged that a general claim that collective attitudes diverge from
members' would duplicate [CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md) and [CLAIM-271](../claims.d/CLAIM-271.md), and that the case is
a possible boundary for [CLAIM-030](../claims.d/CLAIM-030.md): "**publicly attributable collective
orientation is weaker than collective intention**".

A7 added effect without intention, from the owner's Juggernaut case
([CASE-tmpj323r](../cases.d/CASE-tmpj323r.md)): a fan-made parody, circulated by many people for their
own reasons, entered a studio film. A7's ladder: aggregate behaviour,
collective amplification, collective causal efficacy, collective
intention, corporate agency. The case reaches the third and not the
fourth. A7's candidate claim, "distributed cultural uptake", is not
filed: A7 said its defeater "needs refinement", since a micro description
can always list every share, and the record already holds a thesis that
moved with no member arguing for the move ([CLAIM-192](../claims.d/CLAIM-192.md)) and a crowd steering
one avatar ([CASE-075](../cases.d/CASE-075.md)).

A7 §4 also asked which collective acted: "The fandom's identity boundary
and the causal boundary of a particular collective effect may differ."
The effective system may be a short-lived coalition of creators,
sharers, commentators and production staff, across the fandom's edge.
That is [CLAIM-152](../claims.d/CLAIM-152.md)'s target-relativity applied to credit.

It refines [QUESTION-049](QUESTION-049.md), whose attributions are to corporations with
procedures. Reputation ([CASE-tmpothd4](../cases.d/CASE-tmpothd4.md)) is the third member of the family:
an attribution with no procedure behind it that still shapes what it
describes.

## What would count as an answer

Measures that separate the four readings of [CASE-tmpq90dm](../cases.d/CASE-tmpq90dm.md) (aggregate,
shared orientation, norm, corporate judgment) in a real fanbase, with a
test of whether a visible minority can hold a stance the private majority
rejects. For effects, A7: whether modelling the propagation network adds
"a causally meaningful and explanatorily economical structure" over a
list of the identifiable creators, distributors and deciders.
