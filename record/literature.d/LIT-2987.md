# LIT-2987: Power-law distributions in empirical data

Clauset, Shalizi & Newman (2009). SIAM Review 51(4), 661–703. arXiv
0706.1062.

## Standing in the record

Registered on 2026-11-21 from the exchange "Rich Get Richer Power Laws" (2026-11-20 to 21), where A invokes it without naming it at A3 for testing whether data really follow a power law rather than a lognormal or other heavy tail. It bears on the exchange's point that a power-law-looking distribution is not by itself evidence for a mechanism.

Read on 2026-11-22 in NOTE-tmphbwdu; the reading is THEORY-tmpw4qgu. The
first summary's "many claimed power laws do not survive it" undersells
the sharper finding: seven of 24 rejected, seventeen plausible, one
convincing, and the lognormal excluded for one. The paper's lesson is
indistinguishability more than refutation. It supplies CLAIM-803's
fitting clause and none of its dynamical tests (ARG-tmps95tv).
