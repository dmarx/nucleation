# THEORY-034: Chakravartty's dilemma reaches only structural realisms that keep relata but deny them every intrinsic identity-fixing feature, and among the record's readings only Floridi's informational structural realism is of that kind

## Source

- Chakravartty, [LIT-196](../literature.d/LIT-196.md), §§10.2–10.6, read in [NOTE-149](../notes.d/NOTE-149.md).
- Placed against it:
  - Ladyman 2001, [LIT-217](../literature.d/LIT-217.md) ([NOTE-192](../notes.d/NOTE-192.md))
  - Morganti, [LIT-173](../literature.d/LIT-173.md) ([NOTE-095](../notes.d/NOTE-095.md))
  - Floridi, [LIT-152](../literature.d/LIT-152.md) ([NOTE-099](../notes.d/NOTE-099.md))
  - Ross 2008, [LIT-218](../literature.d/LIT-218.md) ([NOTE-189](../notes.d/NOTE-189.md))
  - Wallace, [LIT-151](../literature.d/LIT-151.md) ([NOTE-142](../notes.d/NOTE-142.md))
  - Ladyman 2024, [LIT-219](../literature.d/LIT-219.md) ([NOTE-191](../notes.d/NOTE-191.md))

## What was actually shown

**The dilemma.** Chakravartty reads non-eliminative OSR as a priority
thesis: relata depend on their relations for the determination of their
identity, and not the reverse (p. 193). Every theory of objects he surveys
leaves intrinsic features to fix identity: substance, bundles, and bundles
of dispositionally individuated properties. An object with no intrinsic
features "is not an object at all" (p. 197). The no-priority variant, with
relations and relata on a par, has the same two options (pp. 200–201). His
conclusion (p. 200): one cannot deny both eliminativism and "a role for the
intrinsic in the analysis of identity". He says in so many words that
nothing in the chapter tells against eliminative OSR.

**Where the record's texts fall.** This is the record's synthesis.

| Text | Intrinsic features of relata? | Position relative to the dilemma |
|---|---|---|
| Ladyman 2001, [LIT-217](../literature.d/LIT-217.md) | Granted. Entangled particles keep "charge, mass and so on" (p. 72). | Intrinsic horn. The claim is only that entangled relations do not supervene on those features, so the priority thesis is never stated ([NOTE-192](../notes.d/NOTE-192.md)). |
| Morganti, [LIT-173](../literature.d/LIT-173.md) | Insisted on. State-independent kind properties are not structurally reducible. | Intrinsic horn, with symmetric *partial* dependence among objects ([NOTE-095](../notes.d/NOTE-095.md)). |
| Floridi, [LIT-152](../literature.d/LIT-152.md) | Denied. Structural objects without intrinsic natures, and a "package" of relata and relations. | Target. This is the no-priority variant with no intrinsic features, the case pp. 200–201 argue is eliminativism. Floridi cites Chakravartty as an ally ([NOTE-099](../notes.d/NOTE-099.md)). |
| Ross 2008, [LIT-218](../literature.d/LIT-218.md); Ladyman 2024, [LIT-219](../literature.d/LIT-219.md) | Beside the point. Individuals are "book-keeping devices" at the fundamental level, real at their own scales. | Eliminative only about fundamental individuals, scale-relative elsewhere. Neither horn as framed ([NOTE-189](../notes.d/NOTE-189.md), [NOTE-191](../notes.d/NOTE-191.md)). |
| Wallace, [LIT-151](../literature.d/LIT-151.md) | Not asked. Objects are what the best precisification quantifies over. | Outside the framing. Math-first OSR makes no priority claim. On Wallace's terms the dilemma belongs to language-first metaphysics ([NOTE-142](../notes.d/NOTE-142.md)). |

So the dilemma is well aimed at one held position. The founding
non-eliminative text escapes it at a price: it concedes the premise, and
what remains distinctively structural is a non-supervenience claim about
relations.

## What this does not say

- That the dilemma is sound. Its key step, that purely extrinsic
  characterisation regresses, is one the author admits may look
  question-begging (p. 201).
- That Floridi's position is refuted. The package hypothesis is a reply to
  a regress. Whether it answers the dilemma is open ([NOTE-099](../notes.d/NOTE-099.md)).
- That Every Thing Must Go is outside the dilemma's reach. Chakravartty
  quotes it for objects "purged of their intrinsic natures", and it is
  unread ([LIT-301](../literature.d/LIT-301.md), Deferred).
- That the category-theoretic or graph-theoretic routes rescue the target
  position. Yoneda fixes identity only up to isomorphism among presupposed
  objects ([THEORY-032](THEORY-032.md)). Leitgeb & Ladyman ([LIT-154](../literature.d/LIT-154.md)) defend primitive structural
  identity for mathematics only, and Chakravartty's point about graph
  labels (pp. 197–200) addresses exactly that.

## Note of 2026-10-10: a second position of the kind

The title says only Floridi's view, among the record's readings, keeps relata
while denying them every intrinsic identity-fixing feature. Esfeld and Lam's
moderate structural realism ([LIT-895](../literature.d/LIT-895.md), read in [NOTE-695](../notes.d/NOTE-695.md)) is a second.
It holds that relata have no fundamental intrinsic properties. Where nothing
discerns them, it accepts "a numerical distinction (diversity)" as primitive,
which it says is "not a primitive thisness" nor any other sort of identity
(p. 33). That meets the first condition of this entry's `promote_when`.
Whether primitive diversity meets the dilemma is left open here. The title is
not changed by this note.
