**What the readings gave the record's own argument:**

- *Aggregation is not decomposition, exactly.* [THEORY-755](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-755.md): a
  partition's block sums close for every state iff the partition is
  column-equitable, which is a symmetry condition, not weak coupling.
  [CLAIM-388](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-388.md)'s uniform-coupling system turns out to be aggregable
  along every partition. [QUESTION-218](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-218.md) gains a third kind of unit.
- *Near-decomposability against dynamical dependence.* [CLAIM-390](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-390.md): for
  a block's full state, dependence is of second order in the coupling into
  the block. [CLAIM-386](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-386.md): the Simon–Ando aggregate's closure defect is
  exactly QA(I − VQ), and its intra-block part vanishes iff column sums are
  equal. Together they answer the forward half of [QUESTION-221](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-221.md) in
  part.
- *Memory against dynamical independence.* [CLAIM-389](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-389.md): in VAR(1),
  independence implies zero memory, not conversely.
- *Augmentation, in practice.* [THEORY-760](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-760.md): the finite-memory LES
  closure is a finite Markovian augmentation whose extra coordinate is the
  subgrid force. Its error is not controlled.
- *Scale locality is banded, not gapped.* [THEORY-754](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-754.md), conditional on
  an unproved scaling hypothesis.

Four demonstrated claims ([CLAIM-390](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-390.md), [CLAIM-386](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-386.md), [CLAIM-389](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-389.md)
and the earlier [CLAIM-388](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-388.md)'s note) were re-checked numerically from
scratch before filing. The readers' scripts are not kept in the record.

What it gave the argument:
- *Nesting of optimal abstractions.*
  - Optimal deterministic IB partitions at two budgets can be
    incomparable ([CLAIM-387](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-387.md), re-run here).
  - Every IB optimum nests under the causal partition, its β → ∞ limit
    ([THEORY-743](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-743.md)).
  - Rate–distortion refinability is a Markov condition on some optimal
    pair ([THEORY-751](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-751.md)).
  - [QUESTION-220](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-220.md)'s deterministic half is answered; the stochastic
    half is open.
- *The three orders.* Diffusion maps are the case where they agree
  exactly, because they share an eigenbasis ([THEORY-745](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-745.md)). Refinement
  and relevance to parameters share a chain under coarse-graining
  ([THEORY-742](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-742.md)).
- *Emergence criteria.* Rosas et al.'s Ψ and dynamical independence
  diverge both ways ([THEORY-764](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-764.md)). The paper's Proposition 1 claims
  more than its proof shows ([NOTE-987](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-987.md)).

Errors found in the papers are listed in each NOTE. Among those that bear
on the record:
- Coifman and Lafon's truncation bound is absolute, not relative.
- Crutchfield and Shalizi's logistic-map dive figure is the single-symbol
  entropy, not the entropy rate.
- Raju et al.'s relevant directions lose information when 0 < λ < d/2.

- *No, Ingber & Weissman* is about second-order refinability, not
  log-loss. The log-loss result is No & Weissman 2015, and it concerns
  direct log-loss, where refinability is trivial. The information
  bottleneck is remote log-loss. So [QUESTION-220](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-220.md)'s stochastic half
  stays open. The reader names Charvin, Catenacci Volpi & Polani 2023
  (Entropy 25:1355, open in PMC) as the direct lead: a test by linear
  programming for exact IB refinability.
- *Isett.* Skimmed, about 40 pages of estimates unread, so its THEORY is
  Proposed. It gives the negative half of Onsager. Read through the
  record's [THEORY-749](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-749.md), the flux survives the limit, but in both
  directions: the solutions are not admissible, so this is not a
  dissipative cascade.
- *Nadler et al.* The record's [THEORY-762](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-762.md): a generator eigenfunction
  of a reversible diffusion has zero Mori–Zwanzig memory and noise in
  mean. It is Markov in law only if |∇ψ|² is a function of ψ. The paper's
  printed generators are off by a factor of two (checked numerically).
- The [ADR-002](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-002.md) date for No, Ingber & Weissman is arXiv v1. Partial
  precursors appeared at Allerton 2013 and ISIT 2014 under other titles,
  following [LIT-1238](../../record/literature.d/LIT-1238.md)'s precedent. The owner may overrule.
