Code, tests and outputs under scratch/studies/lead-116/.

Grounds in [CASE-tmpndpz3](record/cases.d/CASE-tmpndpz3.md); corroborates [CLAIM-567](record/claims.d/CLAIM-567.md) and [CLAIM-599](record/claims.d/CLAIM-599.md).
