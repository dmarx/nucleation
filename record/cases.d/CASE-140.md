# CASE-140: The attributed self: two people with similar dispositions hear the same negative characterization, one told it comes from a few critics and the other believing it speaks for nearly everyone who matters to them, so that the words are fixed and only their attributed breadth differs

## The case

A39: "Two individuals possess similar initial dispositions. Both encounter
a negative characterization. One is told it comes from a few critics; the
other believes it represents nearly everyone important to them. Both hear
the same words, but the second experiences them as a collective judgment.
Does the attributed breadth of opinion change their self-interpretation,
conduct, or willingness to reconsider the characterization?"

Held fixed: the words, the dispositions, and the actual critics. Varied:
only [TERM-075](../terms.d/TERM-075.md)'s imagined audience, who the hearer takes "them" to be.

## What it can show

- **Whether the audience does work of its own.** [CLAIM-415](../claims.d/CLAIM-415.md)'s defeater asks
  for evidence that people respond to attributed collective judgement only
  through what its actual holders do. Here the actual holders are the
  same in both arms, so any difference in the two people's later
  self-interpretation or conduct is the attributed audience's.
- **Which outcome would matter.** A39 listed three: self-interpretation,
  conduct, and willingness to reconsider. They can come apart. A
  difference only in conduct would be conformity of expression, which
  [CLAIM-415](../claims.d/CLAIM-415.md)'s defeater counts against it; a difference in
  self-interpretation or in willingness to reconsider would be the
  reorganization the claim asserts.
- **A connection the record already holds.** It is [QUESTION-119](../questions.d/QUESTION-119.md) asked from
  inside the person credited: the collective whose judgement the second
  hearer attributes need not be organized to hold it, and need not exist.

## What it does not show

- Anything, as it stands. It is a design, not a result, and its standing
  is fictional.
- What the actual distribution of opinion is. Pluralistic ignorance
  ([LIT-1370](../literature.d/LIT-1370.md), not reached) and the spiral of silence ([THEORY-242](../theory.d/THEORY-242.md)) concern
  that; this case concerns only what the hearer takes it to be.
