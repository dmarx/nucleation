# THEORY-tmpl9xun: Meta-learned questions can build a representation alone

Veeriah et al., "Discovery of Useful Questions as Auxiliary Tasks" (2019), LIT-tmpl1kcf.

## The account

Questions, in the form of general value functions, are generated by a
network whose parameters are trained by meta-gradient. A meta-loss "may be
evaluated after one update (myopic) or k > 1 updates (non-myopic)", and
"the representation learned with k = 1 and k = 2 was insufficient for the
final policy to do anything meaningful". With the encoder trained only on
the answers, "in the two gridworld domains the resulting policies were
optimal".

## What it does not show

- **That the representation is built by the questions alone.** The
  questions are themselves trained on the main-task loss, so main-task
  signal reaches the encoder through them.
- **That the discovered questions are interpretable.** They are never
  inspected.
- **A general data-efficiency gain in joint training.** Over all 57 games
  the median is not above IMPALA's.
