On 2026-11-15 the owner asked to "read and file"
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41604328/, Yuhai Tu's review in the
Annual Review of Biophysics. A single work, in the author's voice only.

## Access

PubMed gave the citation and abstract. Crossref gave a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
licence and the publisher's PDF address. **This session's permission
system refused the fetch from annualreviews.org**, and it was not retried
or worked around. The work is therefore Unfetched ([ADR-053](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-053.md), [ADR-074](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-074.md)), not
Unavailable: a lawful copy is located at an exact address. PubMed Central
and Europe PMC hold no copy. Allowing annualreviews.org, or supplying
the PDF, returns the work to reading.

No neighbour was read. The two open primary papers by the author that
would stand in are named in the LIT. The lead asked the owner first,
since a copy the owner supplied overtook the neighbour readings in the
last filing.

## Later the same day: the owner's copy, read

The owner uploaded the publisher's PDF. It was read in full by the lead,
and every quotation filed was string-matched against its text.

- **Filed**: [NOTE-1904](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-1904.md) (Read); [THEORY-1830](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1830.md) (Active, see below);
  [LIT-2547](../../record/literature.d/LIT-2547.md) to version 2, Active; dated notes on [QUESTION-452](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-452.md) and
  [THEORY-1268](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-1268.md).
- **Checked by computation.** The four-state adaptation model was
  reimplemented and solved exactly. It confirmed three things: that an
  equilibrium network's activity tracks its input, that strong driving
  makes it adapt, and that the ESA relation holds to leading order (ratio
  0.72–0.94 for γ = 10²–10⁴). A first run broke the model's own timescale
  condition and was discarded. A second showed accuracy saturating at a
  residual fixed by r and c, which the review does not mention; widening
  the timescale gap did not remove it, and the review's formula for R∞
  predicts it. Because the title's assertions beyond the author's report
  were checked, the THEORY was filed Active, and its history says so.
- **Support judgements.** The NOTE judges the proposed law to be a schema
  with no content until F is given. That concerns the author's own
  support for a proposal, inside one THEORY, and no claim concludes from
  it, so no ARG is filed. The bearing on [QUESTION-452](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-452.md) is written as the
  THEORY's `bears_on` with a dated note ([ADR-093](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-093.md)).
- **Left**: the review's sources for §§3.3 and 4 (Lan et al. 2012, Cao et
  al. 2015, Zhang et al. 2020), named in the LIT as next readings, and the
  synchronization results, which were not rederived. No note was filed on
  [CLAIM-616](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-616.md) or [CLAIM-172](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-172.md), since neither speaks of cost; the NOTE records
  the bearing.
