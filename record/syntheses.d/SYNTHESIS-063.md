# SYNTHESIS-063: Directed transport between context-indexed judgement models keeps overlap consistency and global extendability under two sufficient conditions, but for one source–target pair a constant kernel meets them at zero distortion, so it constrains a translation only as a property of a rendering procedure with one kernel family fixed across items, where exact fit is a garbling and Le Cam deficiency a finite linear programme; fidelity as intertwining of framing operations holds as algebra, but its defects are not identified from data and the order effect is bounded on observed responses instead; what stays open is decision-relevant information across a change of cover, signalling data, and whether the restated measures are informative, with tropes and adaptation as the place where structure and function come apart

*A part account of the pragmatic-transport line: the part a mechanical
community map of the line's stored relations and mentions calls kernels,
38 entries on directed transport, outcome-level kernels, D_obs,
intertwining and the decision-theoretic order. The line's account is built
over it and over the accounts of the line's other parts. It integrates
every member the map gave it; none plainly belongs to another part (see
"Whether the part holds together"). Several members also carry
formal-apparatus ([CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md), [CLAIM-070](../claims.d/CLAIM-070.md), [CLAIM-121](../claims.d/CLAIM-121.md), [CLAIM-245](../claims.d/CLAIM-245.md), [CLAIM-381](../claims.d/CLAIM-381.md);
[CLAIM-503](../claims.d/CLAIM-503.md) first), and no account of that line integrates them yet.*

## The account

- **Transport.** [TERM-008](../terms.d/TERM-008.md) (the owner's word at U20, Active): a transport
  from a source to a target "consists of a correspondence τ between
  appropriate source and target contexts and local stochastic kernels",
  and "It is intentionally directed: compression and interpretation need
  not be invertible." [TERM-008](../terms.d/TERM-008.md) also records that the utterance-level
  channel T(v | u) and the outcome-level kernels are "never formally
  related, and the manuscript uses both". [TERM-021](../terms.d/TERM-021.md) (A24, Active) is
  fidelity as context-indexed distribution matching, D(τ), "a candidate
  objective function, not an established translation metric"; [TERM-007](../terms.d/TERM-007.md)
  (A30, Active) adds the dynamics of interpretation, T_τ(f) Φ ≈ Φ T_f, as
  "a further requirement" and not a replacement.
- **What the kernels preserve.** [CLAIM-121](../claims.d/CLAIM-121.md) (the manuscript's Proposition
  1, demonstrated, Active): local kernels that commute with restriction
  carry an overlap-consistent source to an overlap-consistent target; it
  "concerns marginal consistency, not global noncontextuality". [CLAIM-070](../claims.d/CLAIM-070.md)
  (Proposition 2, demonstrated, Active): restrictions of one global
  kernel carry a global extension to a global extension. Both answer
  [QUESTION-240](../questions.d/QUESTION-240.md) (Answered), split from [QUESTION-022](../questions.d/QUESTION-022.md) (Open), which keeps
  "whether either condition is necessary, whether other classes
  preserve, and transports that change the cover". Both claims say
  they are special cases of Karvonen's natural transformations ([LIT-846](../literature.d/LIT-846.md)).
- **For one pair the kernels are unconstrained.** [CLAIM-207](../claims.d/CLAIM-207.md) (the record's
  audit, granted, demonstrated, Active): "Every overlap-consistent target
  is naturally transported from every source at zero observational
  distortion, whatever the translation did", by a constant kernel, and
  "Until that is done, Propositions 1 and 2 are theorems about a map that
  translation is never shown to induce." [CLAIM-077](../claims.d/CLAIM-077.md) (A49's answer to the
  collapse objection, interpretive, Active) would exclude the constant
  kernel by an admissibility condition: "If some pair has d_O(s_1, s_2) >
  δ, a constant Φ sends both to one point, at distance 0 ≤ δ′, so it is
  not admissible." [CLAIM-207](../claims.d/CLAIM-207.md) records that "Separation narrows the freedom
  and does not remove it." [CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) (A34 and A40, granted, demonstrated,
  Active) is the principle under both: an uninformative correspondence
  makes intertwining vacuous or lossy, and its remedy is to "Require state
  correspondences to be estimated on one collection of tasks and evaluated
  on another".
- **The measure, restated over a procedure.** [CLAIM-005](../claims.d/CLAIM-005.md) (A24's D(τ) and
  the manuscript's L_obs, interpretive, Proposed) narrows its own title to
  say that "the match constrains a translation only once one outcome
  correspondence is fixed across many items". [CLAIM-381](../claims.d/CLAIM-381.md) (the record's
  derivations, demonstrated, Active): over a finite class of items, a
  constant kernel fits every item "only when the target readers' averaged
  responses do not vary with the item", an exact fit "is a garbling, with
  the item as the common parameter", and Le Cam's deficiency between the
  two finite tables "is one linear programme". [CLAIM-228](../claims.d/CLAIM-228.md) (the record's
  repair, empirical, Proposed) keeps the proposal that the restated D_obs
  separates rendering procedures that receivers' task-indexed recovery
  separates, and waits on [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md) (in the study part). [ARG-017](../arguments.d/ARG-017.md) (formal
  application, Proposed) derives from [CLAIM-207](../claims.d/CLAIM-207.md), [CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) and [CLAIM-381](../claims.d/CLAIM-381.md)
  that one pair does not identify the procedure-level score; it "does not
  carry that the restated measure is informative". [CLAIM-503](../claims.d/CLAIM-503.md) (the status
  review's re-derivation, demonstrated, Active): over finite states and
  outcomes, ε-deficiency in every [0, 1]-loss problem holds "exactly when
  a single Markov kernel brings it within ε in total variation at every
  state". [CLAIM-488](../claims.d/CLAIM-488.md) (interpretive, Active), resting on [CLAIM-381](../claims.d/CLAIM-381.md) and
  [CLAIM-503](../claims.d/CLAIM-503.md): Le Cam deficiency "would quantify the approximate case of
  the Blackwell order for translation", and decoder-only audience
  knowledge "would make the coding problem a Wyner-Ziv problem".
- **What is open across covers.** [CLAIM-125](../claims.d/CLAIM-125.md) (interpretive, Active),
  resting on [CLAIM-121](../claims.d/CLAIM-121.md) and [CLAIM-070](../claims.d/CLAIM-070.md): simulations between empirical models
  settle global compatibility, and two parts stay open, decision-relevant
  information and signalling data. Its three objections are written in.
  [CLAIM-237](../claims.d/CLAIM-237.md) (granted, demonstrated, Active): under the order on menus of
  experiments "any state-independent classical simulation is dominated
  automatically, so what is open is only the converse or a deficiency
  bound". [CLAIM-245](../claims.d/CLAIM-245.md) (granted, demonstrated, Active): under
  Contextuality-by-Default a context-wise recoding can create or remove
  contextuality, so per-content signalling is not monotone and "the
  quantity to bound is joint overlap discrepancy". [CLAIM-235](../claims.d/CLAIM-235.md) (counter,
  demonstrated, Active): the line's quantities "have no finite-data
  estimators"; it undercuts [ARG-037](../arguments.d/ARG-037.md) (Rejected), the reading of Wang et
  al.'s counts as showing that signalling is typical of language data.
  [CLAIM-126](../claims.d/CLAIM-126.md) (interpretive, Active), complementing [CLAIM-125](../claims.d/CLAIM-125.md): model
  stitching tests decision-relevant transport "only in its reconstruction
  form". [QUESTION-026](../questions.d/QUESTION-026.md) (Open) asks whether categorical compositional
  semantics constrains transport: "does the constraint reach past
  propositional content?"
- **Dynamics and intertwining.** [CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md) (A30 and the manuscript's §9,
  interpretive, Proposed): fidelity "requires preserving the dynamics of
  interpretation, not only final judgements", under an anchored
  correspondence; [CASE-022](../cases.d/CASE-022.md) (stipulated, Active) is its concrete form, two
  renderings with "similar static profiles" whose responses to a later
  framing differ. [CLAIM-063](../claims.d/CLAIM-063.md) (interpretive, Proposed), concluded by [ARG-006](../arguments.d/ARG-006.md)
  (formal application, Active): approximate intertwining of each framing
  approximately preserves their commutator, and "on observed responses
  that degree is the order effect". [CLAIM-011](../claims.d/CLAIM-011.md) objects to [CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md) "as
  stated, not to its truth". [CLAIM-253](../claims.d/CLAIM-253.md) (counter, demonstrated, Active):
  the defects "are not identified from judgement data", by a hidden-bit
  construction, and "The readout defect E_O is a third defect. No draft
  names it." [CLAIM-231](../claims.d/CLAIM-231.md) (the record's repair, demonstrated, Active): on
  observed responses, under one outcome correspondence, "the target's order
  effect differs from the transported source order effect by at most 2ε";
  [CASE-065](../cases.d/CASE-065.md) (a reported study, Active) illustrates it in kind and gives it a
  scale, "2ε is about as large as the effect it would certify" at poll
  sizes.
- **Equivalence and its weaker forms.** [QUESTION-008](../questions.d/QUESTION-008.md) (Deferred) asked
  whether fidelity is approximate bisimulation, set aside at A39–A40; "The
  one-way case survives as [CLAIM-034](../claims.d/CLAIM-034.md)." [QUESTION-190](../questions.d/QUESTION-190.md) (Open) refines it to
  the sample complexity of estimating the bisimulation metric between
  finite chains.
- **Fidelity as one goal, and tropes.** [CLAIM-034](../claims.d/CLAIM-034.md) (A38 and A40,
  interpretive, Proposed): "Distinguish fidelity as one evaluative goal
  from adaptation, critique, parody, and transformation", with one-way
  simulation for an adaptive rendering. [CASE-122](../cases.d/CASE-122.md) (documented, Active)
  illustrates it: in the Warhol case "fidelity of purpose is a cost, not a
  goal, the reverse of a translator's". [CLAIM-147](../claims.d/CLAIM-147.md) (A155, interpretive,
  Proposed): a trope is "a family of observational profiles connected by
  admissible transformations", so its instances "can separate structural
  preservation from pragmatic preservation"; [CASE-038](../cases.d/CASE-038.md) (documented, Active)
  is TV Tropes. [CLAIM-145](../claims.d/CLAIM-145.md) (counter, interpretive, Active): the analysis is
  Propp's and Lévi-Strauss's. [ARG-014](../arguments.d/ARG-014.md) (Rejected): agreement with the
  catalogue's labels does not validate the structural account, since
  "Editors write a trope's description from its examples".

What stays open: necessary conditions and cover-changing transports
([QUESTION-022](../questions.d/QUESTION-022.md)); the converse and a deficiency bound, and signalling data
([CLAIM-125](../claims.d/CLAIM-125.md)'s two parts); whether the restated D_obs is informative
([CLAIM-228](../claims.d/CLAIM-228.md), [CASE-055](../cases.d/CASE-055.md)); whether dynamics add to the prediction of fidelity
verdicts ([CLAIM-117](../claims.d/CLAIM-117.md)'s and [CLAIM-063](../claims.d/CLAIM-063.md)'s defeat conditions, no study run);
compositional constraint ([QUESTION-026](../questions.d/QUESTION-026.md)); bisimulation's estimation
([QUESTION-190](../questions.d/QUESTION-190.md)); and the trope test with held-out families ([CLAIM-147](../claims.d/CLAIM-147.md)).
