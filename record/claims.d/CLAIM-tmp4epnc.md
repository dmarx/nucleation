# CLAIM-tmp4epnc: Communicative identity is relational continuity under admissible transformation: translation transports relationally organized observational structure, correspondence fixes what can be compared, admissibility which transformations count, and fidelity which communicatively consequential distinctions remain recoverable

## The claim

A151 opens the assistant's "current state of our argument" (U43, 2026-10-10
00:37) with its central claim: "**Communicative identity is characterized
through systems of observable distinctions and relations. Communication
transforms those systems. Fidelity is the graded, directed preservation of the
observational, structural, causal, and decision-relevant properties that
matter for a specified communicative purpose.**" Its crystallized paragraph
keeps the receiver: "Blackwell-style information comparison characterizing
which distinctions a receiver can still recover and use".

Outline v7 (A178, U49, 02:02) restates it in two places. §2, "Central thesis":
"Communicative identity is characterized through the organized family of
distinctions and relationships that appropriately situated observers can
recover, test, and act upon. … It is **relational continuity under admissible
transformation**, evaluated relative to specified probes and communicative
purposes." And A178's revised one-sentence thesis: "**Translation is the
directed transport of relationally organized observational structure;
correspondence determines what can be compared, admissibility determines which
transformations count, and fidelity measures which communicatively
consequential distinctions remain recoverable under that transformation.**"

The record files the two as one thesis, because A178 builds on A151 and adds
two clauses to it. Its premises are correspondence before comparison
([CLAIM-tmpe2csg](CLAIM-tmpe2csg.md)), the gap between structural and observational identity
([CLAIM-tmpd1aee](CLAIM-tmpd1aee.md)), admissibility ([CLAIM-077](CLAIM-077.md)) with its anchoring remedy
([CLAIM-011](CLAIM-011.md)), and Blackwell's order for the decision clause ([CLAIM-050](CLAIM-050.md)). Its
terms are the communicative object as a system of empirical constraints
([TERM-tmpgok7x](../terms.d/TERM-tmpgok7x.md)) and fidelity as a profile of distortions ([TERM-tmpz8i47](../terms.d/TERM-tmpz8i47.md)).

## How it differs from [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md)

- [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) says what survives: what "a receiving system can still
  reconstruct and act upon". This says what identity is, and makes fidelity
  relative to a stated purpose.
- It brings back [CLAIM-108](CLAIM-108.md)'s identity clause ("Communicative identity is
  characterized by the observational structures and relations …"), which the
  record had found superseded on the way to the manuscript.
- It adds causal properties, which [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) lacks.
- A178 adds correspondence and admissibility. Neither is in [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md), and
  both are older than it: admissibility was A49's ([CLAIM-077](CLAIM-077.md)), and the
  manuscript dropped [CLAIM-011](CLAIM-011.md)'s remedy.
- It drops "not the wording or the proposition" from the headline. A151 keeps
  it as P1: "Communicative fidelity cannot be determined from preservation of
  propositional or lexical content alone."
- It keeps the receiver, as "a receiver" (A151) and as "appropriately situated
  observers" (A178). The October thesis does not ([CLAIM-tmpbaexb](CLAIM-tmpbaexb.md)).

No turn argues any of these changes. U43 was a bare request, and U49 asked for
an update and made no cut.

## What it does not say

A178 §9, "Scope and validation": "The theory does not assume that every
communicative object has a unique global semantic state, that contextual
dependence implies contextuality, or that exact conservation laws govern
language." It does not say every relation must survive. A151 §5: "Not every
source difference must be preserved." Its defeat condition is empirical, and
no study has been run ([CLAIM-028](CLAIM-028.md)).

It is not the manuscript's thesis. Outline v7 is a proposal, not a draft, so
this entry complements [CLAIM-115](CLAIM-115.md) rather than superseding it. If the owner
treats a later outline as the work's plan, that changes.
