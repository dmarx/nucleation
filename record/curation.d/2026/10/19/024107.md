The owner brought an audit of Proposed entries that might be promoted.
It covered the pending-decisions report, the most-cited CLAIMs and
THEORYs, the formal-apparatus line, and the promotion conditions those
entries record. The owner chose its first pass. The second pass, which
would split the proved parts of [THEORY-008](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-008.md), [THEORY-005](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-005.md), [THEORY-001](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-001.md) and
[THEORY-156](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-156.md), is not done here.

**Checked before acting.** Each entry in the first pass was checked against
the record before its status changed.

- [CLAIM-264](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-264.md). The audit asked for a final check of the density and
  strong-generator statements. One statement failed. A strong generator,
  in a category with finite limits, makes the restricted functor
  conservative, but objects with isomorphic profiles need not be
  isomorphic. (ℤ/p)³ and the Heisenberg group mod p, for p odd, have the
  same profile through ℤ: a set of p³ elements with the same power maps.
  The body's "any other family identifies objects only up to a coarser
  equivalence" was wrong in the other direction as well, because a
  non-dense family can still identify objects: in finite-dimensional
  vector spaces over a finite field, the field alone fixes the dimension.
  The claim was corrected and then promoted. [CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md) has the same
  sentence and gets a dated note pointing to the correction; its
  application is unchanged.
- [CLAIM-123](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-123.md). The audit pointed out that conditional independence is
  sufficient, not necessary, for s₁₂ = s₁ + s₂ − s₀. The record checked
  this and built an explicit example: three-valued conditions coupled by
  a factor 1 + ε v(y₁)v(y₂), where v is orthogonal to every conditional
  p(·|x). This met the claim's own defeat condition. The derivation
  survives, so the claim was corrected in place rather than rejected. It
  stays Proposed, because the audit did not propose promoting it.
- [THEORY-155](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-155.md). The title was already the conditional theorem. Only
  the promotion condition, a human-chain experiment, held it back, and the
  body already said that the THEORY does not claim human chains converge.
  The record checked the stationarity of the prior again. The condition
  moved unchanged to [QUESTION-tmpxheie](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpxheie.md). The title gained the
  ergodicity condition the body already stated.
- [CLAIM-128](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-128.md). The claim was filed as demonstrated, but its defeat
  condition is about which tasks show trade-offs, and its thesis is
  argued. The part that is proved is now [CLAIM-tmpzk12h](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpzk12h.md), with a
  proof, filed Active. [CLAIM-128](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-128.md) rests on it and is interpretive.

**Left alone, as the audit advised.** The audit named [CLAIM-025](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-025.md),
[CLAIM-284](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-284.md) and [THEORY-232](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-232.md) as highly cited and still interpretive or
untested. They stay Proposed. [CLAIM-269](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-269.md) is not narrowed here either:
it depends on [THEORY-008](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-008.md), [THEORY-002](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-002.md) and [THEORY-019](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-019.md), which are Proposed.

**The pattern the audit named.** Some promotion conditions combine two
jobs: checking a proof and showing that the result explains something
beyond itself. Separating them, as [QUESTION-tmpxheie](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpxheie.md) does for [THEORY-155](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-155.md),
is the move the second pass would make for the other THEORYs.
