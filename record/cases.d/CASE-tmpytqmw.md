# CASE-tmpytqmw: The thermostat and the adaptive controller

## The case

"Thermostats regulate variables; adaptive controllers alter their own
parameters; institutions can contain elaborate decision procedures. None of
these facts, by itself, warrants attribution of an agent." (Section 1)

## What it can show

That higher-order regulation is not sufficient for will — against
