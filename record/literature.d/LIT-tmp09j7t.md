# LIT-tmp09j7t: Delay of gratification and adult outcomes: The Marshmallow Test does not reliably predict adult functioning

Jessica F. Sperber, Deborah Lowe Vandell, Greg J. Duncan and Tyler W. Watts (2024). Delay of gratification and adult outcomes: The Marshmallow Test does not reliably predict adult functioning. *Child Development* 95(6): 2015–2029. DOI 10.1111/cdev.14129.

## Key takeaways

- "Using a preregistered analysis, Marshmallow Test performance was not
  strongly predictive of adult achievement, health, or behavior"
  (abstract).
- Educational attainment fell to non-significance once child
  demographics were controlled (β = .04, p = .23).
- Exploratory and not preregistered: waiting the full period predicted
  lower BMI with full covariates (β = −.17, p = .03); multiple comparisons
  were not corrected.
