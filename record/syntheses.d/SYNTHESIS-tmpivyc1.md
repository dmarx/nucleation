# SYNTHESIS-tmpivyc1: The learned-structure line is several inquiries into what training reaches that barely relate: whether a learner's demands are frustrated and whose they are, when structure is acquired, whether optimal representations nest, whether trained solutions are connected, when a learned distinction is a concept, and what classification leaves in the last layers, with smaller families on personas, group structure and learned variables and twenty-three entries linked to no other; the parts meet almost only through two questions on acquisition, and the line is a candidate to split

*The account of the learned-structure line ("How training finds structure"), over the accounts of six parts (ADR-083). It integrates directly the three families too small for an account of their own, and the entries linked to no other component. Of the line's 86 entries, 57 are Open questions, 4 Answered questions, 20 claims, 4 cases and one term. Five carry the line after another, and four of those gained it at the sweep of 2026-11-10 (ADR-085): CLAIM-394, CLAIM-620, CLAIM-621 and QUESTION-439.*

## Links tested as claims

- **CLAIM-tmpmrnnq (SYNTHESIS-tmpexmgz with SYNTHESIS-tmp4xa7p), drafted.** CLAIM-524's two
  realizations, which a budget below √2 separates, lie on one orbit of
  the swap of the two paths, and the swap exchanges the two budgeted
  pieces, so up to CLAIM-263's quotient the budgeted set is connected.
  Passes by derivation; no entry states it. If filed, the claim is
  integrated here.
- **CLAIM-382 with CLAIM-629.** CLAIM-382's maps are orthogonal linear
  maps of the parameters that preserve the loss, so by CLAIM-629 both
  common descent and the Euclidean residual are constant on their
  orbits. A substitution into CLAIM-629's item 2: trivial, not filed.
- **QUESTION-363 with CASE-197: the relief the posterior's 5 → 6
  transition gives is not reached by SGD.** A conjunction of CASE-197's
  computed relief and THEORY-1275's datum, and "not reached" is open:
  QUESTION-363 says that "Inaccessible" and "not reached in 4,500 epochs
  at one setting" are not yet told apart. Not filed.
- **CLAIM-367 with QUESTION-193: the order of acquisition belongs to the
  training organization.** Stated: CLAIM-367 cites THEORY-253 as the
  documented ground of its step 3, and CASE-113 calls the order "the
  optimizer's".
- **CLAIM-290 with CLAIM-263.** One fixes a representation up to an
  orthogonal map; the other quotients parameters by a symmetry group. The
  objects differ, and no sentence joining them has a case or a
  derivation of its own. Not filed.
