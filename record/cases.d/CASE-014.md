# CASE-014: Microsoft, as the philosophical target

## The case

The owner's frame at U9 ([QUESTION-019](../questions.d/QUESTION-019.md)), and the owner's example at U34 of a
corporation that can "act freely" or have "its hand forced". At A205 the
assistant committed to "return to Microsoft at the end of every major
movement". At A222 it became a motivating case; by the A227 draft and the A243
outline it was gone, and the commitment was never retired.

## What it can show

Stipulated: nothing in the exchange is a claim about Microsoft's actual
history. Its job was to make the question vivid. Deferred, because the
manuscript no longer uses it; a future paper that asks [QUESTION-019](../questions.d/QUESTION-019.md) again
would reopen it.

## Note of 2026-10-22: the question asked again

At U20 of the exchange "Graph Learning Applications" the owner asked: "an
early motivating exercise involved the topical prompt: 'What is it like to
be Microsoft?' Is this still a potentially interesting angle upon which to
build on nucleation as a substrate?" A20 found this case and [QUESTION-019](../questions.d/QUESTION-019.md)
through the connector, called the case "Deferred, not rejected", and said
"I would reopen CASE-014". It warned that a Nagel-style essay is "a
difficult originality bet" (List's "What is it like to be a group agent?"
is [LIT-401](../literature.d/LIT-401.md), read), and redirected: "What could it mean for Microsoft to have
its own perspective on the world, even if there is no phenomenal
experience of being Microsoft?"

The exchange then left Microsoft for documented organizations: incident
response under NIMS ([CASE-135](CASE-135.md)), NASA ([CASE-138](CASE-138.md)), Crew Resource
Management ([CASE-127](CASE-127.md)). A37 reframed the question once more: "What
organized ways of encountering and acting upon the world does Microsoft
participate in, how are they reproduced across changing people and
institutions, and which mechanisms can make their governing assumptions
genuinely revisable?" That is a second reframing. [QUESTION-015](../questions.d/QUESTION-015.md) was the
first, and it presupposes "an independently warranted agent", which A37's
does not.

No manuscript uses the case yet, so it stays Deferred. A reopening would
be the owner's call, with a paper that asks the question again.
