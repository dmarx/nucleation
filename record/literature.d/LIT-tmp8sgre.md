  Physical Review Letters, DOI 10.1103/jw9p-s3kk; read as arXiv 2509.01706
  v1 (2025). In a chain of buckled polymer beams, bits stored as buckling
  domains are moved one unit cell per cycle of an applied compression
  pattern. The wall advances when a mode localized at it, softened by the
  opposite domain's pressure, goes unstable. The authors call this a
  topological ratchet and distinguish it from Chern-number pumping, which
  is ill-defined on their path. Logic gates are simulated only. It is here
  as information transport by boundaries without learning, against the
