# THEORY-tmp9pem2: During held covert spatial attention in human fronto-parietal and visual cortex, the amplitude of broadband high gamma (80–250 Hz) is coupled to the phase of an endogenous 2–5 Hz rhythm, more strongly for the contralateral field, and the trial-by-trial strength of that coupling correlates with faster detection; the evidence is correlational, so it shows that the coupling tracks attention, not that it improves it

## Source

Szczepanski, Crone, Kuperman, Auguste, Parvizi and Knight (2014),
LIT-tmpkq6x5, pp. 2–12; read in NOTE-tmpryuio.

## What was actually shown

Eight patients with subdural grids held covert attention on a cued
hemifield for 1000–2000 ms. In 123 of 273 high-gamma-responsive electrodes,
80–250 Hz amplitude was locked to the 2–5 Hz phase (surrogate test, FDR
q < 0.01), and locked to its trough in 117 (pp. 3–4). In 55 the coupling
was stronger for the contralateral field, with no matching power
difference in 53 of them (p. 4). Dropping the first 500 ms, where the
evoked potential lies, left 107 electrodes significant (p. 4). In 28
electrodes, stronger coupling in the 1000 ms before the target went with
faster reaction times, contralateral trials only; neither band's amplitude
alone predicted RT (pp. 4–5).

Each of these could have failed: coupling could have been absent, equal
across hemifields, confined to the evoked window, or unrelated to RT.

## What this does not say

- **That the coupling improves attention or causes faster responses.**
  Nothing was manipulated. The title's "facilitate" and the Discussion's
  "serves as a mechanism" (p. 9) are interpretation; the closing reading is
  that it indexes "the overall level of engagement" (p. 10).
- **That slow waves constrain fast ones in any sense beyond a statistical
  dependence.** The paper's mechanism is excitability modulation through
  membrane potentials (p. 2), entirely micro-level; it does not bear on
  whether a macro pattern has a power its parts lack (THEORY-tmp6xjgq).
- **That the coupling is free of waveform-shape artefact.** No shape
  control is reported (see THEORY-tmp66yzx).
- **Anything about choice, value or stalemates.** The task is instructed
  spatial attention.
