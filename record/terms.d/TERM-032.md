# TERM-032: levels, as a hierarchy of latent representations

## Definition

U27: "use higher order latents to characterize the more abstract similarity we
need to invoke here". A73 gave four levels, surface realization, conceptual
structure, pragmatic operation and communicative configuration, factored as
z(u, c) = (z_lex, z_concept, z_act, z_social). The highest is a relational
configuration Z(u, c) = (V, E, α): "who stands in what relation to whom,
concerning what, and through which communicative action". Its own caveat:
"conceptual rather than a claim about a literal hierarchy of neural
representations".

## Why it was replaced

U28: "of course, this isn't a strict hierarchy. it's more like a hierarchy of
levels at which observables exist" ([TERM-009](TERM-009.md)). Levels stopped being layers of an
object.
