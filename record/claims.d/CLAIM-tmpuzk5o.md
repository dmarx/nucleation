# CLAIM-tmpuzk5o: Collective identity may be a sloppy mess of governed perspectives, that is, a pseudo-self, playing a question–answer game

## The claim

The owner's words, at U33b: "collective identity as a (sloppy?) mess of (governed) perspectives (i.e. a pseudo-self?) playing a question-answer game".

What is new beside the owner's earlier turns is the join. U6 made a self "a literal sloppy mess of connected perspectives" ([CLAIM-tmpy4mba](CLAIM-tmpy4mba.md)). U10 made a team a pseudo-self ([TERM-tmpxwa7g](../terms.d/TERM-tmpxwa7g.md)). U26 proposed a game between queries and targets ([CLAIM-tmp61i14](CLAIM-tmp61i14.md)). U33b carries the first to a collective, names it with the second, says its perspectives are governed, and says what holds the mess together: the question–answer game it plays.

## The question marks

- **"(sloppy?)"** asks whether the word fits. The record holds two readings of it from U6, filed as alternatives: colloquial ([CLAIM-tmpy4mba](CLAIM-tmpy4mba.md)) and technical ([CLAIM-tmptsbhv](CLAIM-tmptsbhv.md)). The owner's choice there carries here. A64b §1 gives it a third, precise meaning of its own: "The decomposition into perspectives is not necessarily unique, stable, or equally resolvable under every observational regime." That is the regime-relative decomposition the record holds as [CLAIM-tmp7pe33](CLAIM-tmp7pe33.md) and, for teams, [CLAIM-tmp7580l](CLAIM-tmp7580l.md).
- **"(i.e. a pseudo-self?)"** asks whether a collective identity is a pseudo-self. A64b §6 answers with five criteria for a stronger candidate ([CLAIM-tmp812ex](CLAIM-tmp812ex.md)), and wonders whether the name "undersells the possibility".

## Standing

Proposed, the owner's. A64b develops it without objecting ([CLAIM-tmprkpq5](CLAIM-tmprkpq5.md)), and so the claim stays open on the record's own pseudo-self question, [QUESTION-tmp4fkkx](../questions.d/QUESTION-tmp4fkkx.md): a collective that plays such a game may still be an ecosystem of pseudo-selves and not one.
