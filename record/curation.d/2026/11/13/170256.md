**The idea's word "who controls the terms decides" is narrowed in the
claim.** As the idea put it, the magnitude of dependence would be idle.
It is not: in the toy model of [CASE-tmpzh595](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpzh595.md), P's share in the bargained
workshop rises by 1/2 for each unit of the workshop's need. The claim says
instead that magnitude sets how much rides on the relation and control
sets which way it runs. The ARG's verdict says the contrast supports that
and not the stronger reading.

**The obligation clause is conditional.** It falls on the controlling
agent only under a principle grounding obligations in vulnerability to
another's discretion. That is [CLAIM-671](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-671.md)'s supposition, Goodin's, unread.
The claim says where a strengthened responsibility falls (the bearer),
not whether it is grounded.
