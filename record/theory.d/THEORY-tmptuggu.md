# THEORY-tmptuggu: Instructed reappraisal of aversive pictures engages anterior prefrontal, temporo-parietal and cerebellar regions that show evidence of no response to negative emotion generation, beside a larger set of regions engaged both by generation and by reappraisal: in this task part of regulation runs in regions apart from generation, and most of it in regions shared with it

## Source

Bo, Kraynak, Kwon, Sun, Gianaros & Wager, [LIT-tmptaneb](../literature.d/LIT-tmptaneb.md), read in full in
[NOTE-tmphhgp9](../notes.d/NOTE-tmphhgp9.md) from the bioRxiv preprint, version 2 (10 May 2023), pp. 5–12
and 17–20, with Figures 1, 2 and 5. The version of record, Nature
Neuroscience 27(5), 975–987 (2024), is closed and was not read; its abstract
matches the preprint's.

## What was actually shown

Participants in two samples looked at neutral pictures, looked at aversive
pictures, or reappraised aversive pictures to feel less negative. Each voxel
was tested on two contrasts, emotion generation [Look Negative − Look
Neutral] and reappraisal [Reappraise Negative − Look Negative], with Bayes
factors that can support a null (BF > 10 for an effect, BF < 1/10 for none).

- **A selective set.** Sixteen regions met the axioms for "Reappraisal
  only" in both samples: an effect of reappraisal and evidence of no effect
  of negative pictures (p. 8). They are anterior DLPFC and VLPFC, TPJ, IPL,
  posterior middle and anterior inferior temporal gyri and superior
  cerebellum, mostly in default-mode and frontoparietal networks.
- **A shared set.** Fourteen regions met the axioms for "Common appraisal":
  engaged by negative pictures and further by reappraisal (p. 9). They are
  the larger set: DLPFC, VLPFC, SPL, pSTS, anterior insula, dMPFC/aMCC/SMA,
  precuneus and cerebellum. The authors: "the majority of the regions
  activated by reappraisal were also activated during negative
  emotion-generation without explicit reappraisal instructions" (p. 18).
- **Which predicts success.** Reappraisal-related activity in the selective
  set correlated with the drop in reported negative affect (r = 0.2), more
  than the shared set (r = 0.14), and the best model joined it with the
  regions reappraisal reduces (r = 0.26, adjusted R² = 0.063) (pp. 11–12).

What could have come out otherwise: every region engaged by reappraisal
could also have responded to negative pictures, which is what a view in
which regulation is generation re-run would expect. A set did not.

## What this does not say

- **That the selective regions regulate.** They are engaged during
  regulation and predict its success weakly. No interference was tried, and
  the authors' description of them as "in a position to regulate" sensory
  hierarchies (p. 17) rests on anatomy and on the literature.
- **That they are an emotion regulator in general.** One deliberate
  strategy, instructed downregulation, on IAPS pictures. Effortless
  regulation, which is where [THEORY-056](THEORY-056.md) makes its claim, was not studied.
  Their best Neurosynth match is "response inhibition", so they may be
  task control rather than anything particular to emotion.
- **That passive viewing is unregulated.** The authors allow that the
  shared regions "may be involved in spontaneous self-regulation in the
  absence of explicit regulatory instructions" (p. 18). If so, some
  regulation is in the baseline, and the shared set mixes generation with
  regulation.
- **That the null is a true zero.** BF < 1/10 at the voxel and region level
  is evidence against a mean difference of the size the prior expects. It
  does not exclude small or patterned responses.
- **The version of record.** Only the preprint and the published abstract
  were read.

## Connections

It answers [QUESTION-054](../questions.d/QUESTION-054.md) in part. For one deliberate strategy there is a set
of regions engaged only when regulating, high in the cortical hierarchy and
predictive of success: a candidate governing level in the anatomical sense.
It does not reach effortless regulation, where [QUESTION-054](../questions.d/QUESTION-054.md)'s accounts
differ.

- **[THEORY-056](THEORY-056.md) (regulation is not separate from generation).** The shared
  set agrees with it; the selective set is what a distinct regulating
  system would look like in imaging. [THEORY-056](THEORY-056.md)'s own `promote_when` says
  that a difference in network activation "cannot settle it". So this
  result does not refute it, but it does show that this exclusion keeps
  [THEORY-056](THEORY-056.md) out of reach of imaging data.
- **[THEORY-680](THEORY-680.md) (regulation is a set of processes distinct from
  generation).** The selective set agrees with its first clause. Its stage
  claims are not tested, since only one strategy was used, and its demand
  for an independently estimated unregulated response is not met.
- **[THEORY-070](THEORY-070.md) (a distinct system that specifies control).** The selective
  regions' annotation (response inhibition, executive function) fits a
  control system that is not particular to emotion. That is the record's
  reading; the paper does not discuss it.
- **[THEORY-tmpak7ax](THEORY-tmpak7ax.md).** The same study's other half: what reappraisal does
  and does not change.
- **[CLAIM-tmpdga80](../claims.d/CLAIM-tmpdga80.md).** Its grounds include [THEORY-056](THEORY-056.md) and [THEORY-680](THEORY-680.md) as the
  two readings of reappraisal. This result divides between them and does
  not choose.
