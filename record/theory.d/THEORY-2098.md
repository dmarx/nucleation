# THEORY-2098: Solms argues that consciousness is generated in the upper brainstem and is fundamentally affective, feeling being the precision-weighting by which an organism that minimises free energy prioritises among its homeostatic needs in unpredicted contexts, so that rising uncertainty about a need is felt as unpleasure and falling uncertainty as pleasure

## The account

Mind and brain are two aspects of one function. Consciousness is generated
in the upper brainstem, not the cortex, and is affective. Self-organising
systems minimise free energy; with many homeostatic needs to prioritise in
novel contexts, the organism must weight the precision of its predictions,
and that weighting, felt, is consciousness: felt uncertainty.

## Evidence

Clinical, lesion and stimulation findings, with a free-energy formalism.
