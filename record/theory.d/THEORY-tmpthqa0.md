# THEORY-tmpthqa0: Hu, Jagadeesan, Jordan and Steinhardt prove that, in a model where non-myopic, discounting producers choose content every round and a platform recommends by a standard no-regret online-learning algorithm (LinHedge or LinEXP3), equilibrium producer effort and user welfare vanish over time under typical learning-rate schedules, because a slow learner makes today's effort move future recommendations too little to repay its cost; algorithms that permanently stop recommending producers whose content falls below a threshold sustain constant effort, and individualized criteria that make producers specialize recover near-optimal welfare in limiting cases

## The account

Hu, Jagadeesan, Jordan and Steinhardt prove that, in a model where non-myopic, discounting producers choose content every round and a platform recommends by a standard no-regret online-learning algorithm (LinHedge or LinEXP3), equilibrium producer effort and user welfare vanish over time under typical learning-rate schedules, because a slow learner makes today's effort move future recommendations too little to repay its cost; algorithms that permanently stop recommending producers whose content falls below a threshold sustain constant effort, and individualized criteria that make producers specialize recover near-optimal welfare in limiting cases.
