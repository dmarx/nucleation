# QUESTION-009: When does a pattern of coordination constitute an additional agent?

## Why it is set aside

Every attempt to answer it in the exchange produced the permissiveness
objection ([CLAIM-010](../claims.d/CLAIM-010.md)). The paper's move was to stop deriving agency and
assume it: "It presupposes rather than derives the relevant agent" (Section
7.4). The question stays real, and three adequacy standards the paper keeps
apart (constitutive, explanatory, agentive) say what an answer would need.
