# THEORY-1166: Di Paolo argues that autopoiesis as originally defined entails conservation and not active homeostasis, so it gives a system only an all-or-nothing norm, to go on or to die, which an observer and not the system can use; that a graded norm the system itself has needs adaptivity, a capacity to detect tendencies toward the boundary of its viability and to act so as to reverse them; and that sense-making and agency need adaptivity

## Source

Ezequiel A. Di Paolo (2005), "Autopoiesis, Adaptivity, Teleology, Agency",
*Phenomenology and the Cognitive Sciences* 4(4), 429–452, [LIT-1017](../literature.d/LIT-1017.md), read
in full in [NOTE-719](../notes.d/NOTE-719.md).

## The argument

**Conservation, not homeostasis.** Autopoiesis, "a concept allowing no
degrees, can only entail the more general case of conservation (thus
admitting the possibility of fortuitous self-maintenance)". Its only norm
is that "organisms live as long as they don't die". That lets an observer
tell lethal from non-lethal encounters, but not the system.

**Two cases.** "Balancing at the edge of a cliff is a perfectly viable
behaviour, so is falling over the edge – both are interactions that
conserve autopoiesis." A bacterium whose nutrient intake is below its
consumption will not "seek higher concentrations just because [it is]
autopoietic".

**The definition.** Adaptivity is "a system's capacity, in some
circumstances, to regulate its states and its relation to the environment
with the result that, if the states are sufficiently close to the
boundary of viability, 1. Tendencies are distinguished and acted upon
depending on whether the states will approach or recede from the boundary
and, as a consequence, 2. Tendencies of the first kind are moved closer to
or transformed into tendencies of the second". It is "not a direct
consequence of the definition of autopoiesis".

**What adaptivity gives.** "An inner norm, a distinction between good,
neutral, and bad ways of realizing autopoiesis", graded and recursive.
Norms are then norms for the system, which it can use.

**Agency.** An agent is "a self-constructed unity that engages the world by
actively regulating its exchanges with it for adaptive purposes that are
meant to serve its continued viability". Agency comes in degrees.

This is the norm-for-the-system [CLAIM-288](../claims.d/CLAIM-288.md) cites and the enactive account
of norms [CLAIM-339](../claims.d/CLAIM-339.md) sets beside the free-energy principle.

## What this does not say

It does not say the argument is right. Di Paolo argues from the bacterium
and the cliff, chosen to illustrate it, and grants (note 1) that later
formulations of autopoiesis may contain adaptivity. Weber and Varela
([THEORY-666](THEORY-666.md)) hold that self-production suffices for sense-making. A worked
case with the viability set and state space stated formally is
[THEORY-628](THEORY-628.md)'s `promote_when`, and [THEORY-628](THEORY-628.md) stays Proposed until the
record holds it.
