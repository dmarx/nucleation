# THEORY-tmpqxwyk: Schelling holds that people who must coordinate without communicating can often do far better than chance by converging on a focal point that their shared situation makes prominent and unique, and that the same convergence of mutual expectations on conspicuous terms such as equal splits, round numbers, precedent and natural boundaries settles where explicit bargains and tacit limits in war come to rest, even against one party's interest

## The account

Coordination without communication succeeds by mutual expectation: each
seeks the option the other will expect him to expect, and the shared
situation supplies a clue, prominent and unique for these parties.
Explicit bargaining needs the same convergence, so conspicuous terms
settle outcomes, and part of fairness's force is its power to focus
expectations. Tacit limits in war must be qualitative to be recognized.

## Evidence

Informal questionnaires and historical examples.
