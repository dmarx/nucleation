# CLAIM-541: Wu-wei is better read as control placed and timed where a system's own dynamics do most of the work than as the absence of control: Ott–Grebogi–Yorke control of chaos is a formal instance, in which small perturbations timed by the system's own wandering hold it on an unstable orbit, and the control, though small, continues for as long as the orbit is held; so reported effortlessness has at least three distinct sources, no conflict to override, conflict settled cheaply by learned accommodation, and conflict met as it arises by small well-timed feedback, which may feel alike and differ in mechanism

## The claim

A16: "The classic Ott–Grebogi–Yorke method demonstrates that small,
carefully timed control interventions can stabilize selected trajectories of
a chaotic system." The owner: "that's very wu-wei" (U17).

The method ([LIT-1997](../literature.d/LIT-1997.md)) waits for the state's own wandering to bring it
near an unstable periodic orbit embedded in the attractor. It then makes
small, bounded parameter perturbations that put the state on the orbit's
stable manifold. Little force is spent because the dynamics do the moving.
Shinbrot et al. ([LIT-1972](../literature.d/LIT-1972.md)) add targeting, one well-placed nudge that uses
sensitivity to reach a region quickly.

Read this way, wu-wei is not the absence of control. A17: control "that no
longer needs to fight what it governs". A18 §2: restraint is part of an
effective policy. Effortlessness then has three sources (A18 §4):

1. **No conflict to override.** [CLAIM-162](CLAIM-162.md)'s synchronized valuations, with no
   dynamical mechanism claimed.
2. **Conflict amortized.** [CLAIM-358](CLAIM-358.md)'s learned local accommodation.
3. **Conflict met as it arises** by feedback small because it is well timed
   and well coupled, the OGY case.

They may be reported alike and differ in mechanism.

## What it does not say

- **That OGY's effects persist.** A18 §7 claimed "large and persistent
  self-formative effects". OGY holds an unstable orbit, so when the control
  stops the trajectory leaves it at the orbit's own rate. Effects that
  outlast the intervention come from switching basins or from drift of a
  slow mode. OGY-style wu-wei is small control that never reaches zero.
- **That this is what wu-wei means in early Chinese thought.** The record
  holds no Daoist text, and its one work on effortless action, Bruya's
  *Effortless Attention* ([LIT-910](../literature.d/LIT-910.md)), is Unavailable. Slingerland
  ([LIT-1994](../literature.d/LIT-1994.md)) is the reading to check it against.
- **That small control is autonomous.** Low effort is not ownership: a
  remote operator can steer cheaply too ([CLAIM-354](CLAIM-354.md); [CLAIM-533](CLAIM-533.md)).

## Note of 2026-11-03: its sources are unread

Ott, Grebogi and Yorke, Shinbrot et al. and Slingerland were all looked for and
are Unavailable, so both the claim's formal instance and the source named in
its defeater rest on unread works. OGY is one of PRL's milestone letters; if
the APS makes those free it should be Unfetched.

## Note of 2026-11-06: what holds it back

The record has read no account of wu-wei: Slingerland ([LIT-1994](../literature.d/LIT-1994.md)) and Bruya ([LIT-910](../literature.d/LIT-910.md)) are Unavailable, so the sinological test its defeater names has not been run. Its formal instance, Ott, Grebogi and Yorke's method, is known here only second-hand ([LIT-1997](../literature.d/LIT-1997.md), [LIT-1972](../literature.d/LIT-1972.md), Unavailable). A reading of either would let the ruling be made.
