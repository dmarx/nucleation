# QUESTION-421: Does an independent re-estimate on Davis and Weinstein's Japanese city data find 2SLS coefficients on instrumented 1940–47 growth indistinguishable from −1 for 1947–60 without the prewar-trend control and for 1947–65 with it?

## Why it is a question

[THEORY-1499](../theory.d/THEORY-1499.md) reports the working paper's coefficients (−1.048 for 1947–60;
−0.759 and −1.027 with the prewar trend for 1960 and 1965). They rest on
instruments that assume the bombing did not target growth, and no one
else's estimate on the same data has been read.

## What would answer it

A re-estimate on the same data finding coefficients indistinguishable from
−1 would answer yes; one significantly above −1 by 1965 with the trend
control would answer no.
