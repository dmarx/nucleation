# LIT-089: Software engineering job openings hit five-year low?

Gergely Orosz (2025), *The Pragmatic Engineer (blog / newsletter excerpt)* — <https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/software-engineer-jobs-five-year-lo…>

## Key takeaways

- The author reports that software-developer job postings on Indeed are about 35% below January 2020 and about 3.5× below the mid-2022 peak, the largest boom-and-bust of any sector tracked, while all postings are up about 10%. He attributes most of the drop to the end of zero interest rates, with GenAI productivity expectations, over-hiring in 2021–22, and leaner teams as secondary and uncertain factors.

*Seeded from the abstract and a skim, not a reading. What follows is what the work says about itself.*

(Blog post; no abstract.) Using Indeed's aggregated postings index (January 2020 = 100) via FRED, the author shows software development vacancies at about 65% of the 2020 baseline in the US, with similar drops in the UK, France and Germany and an exception in Australia. He compares other sectors and weighs explanations: interest rates, US Section 174 amortization, over-hiring during the pandemic, GenAI tooling, and smaller teams. He also questions how representative Indeed's data is.
