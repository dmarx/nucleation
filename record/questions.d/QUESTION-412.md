# QUESTION-412: Does a re-analysis of the Social Sciences Replication Project's data for Study 1 of Shah, Mullainathan and Shafir (2012), or a further independent replication of that study, also find no poor-versus-rich difference in the original direction on the Dots-Mixed task?

## Why it is a question

The Social Sciences Replication Project ([THEORY-1469](../theory.d/THEORY-1469.md)) ran one two-stage
replication of this study, and the original authors' own replication
also failed ([THEORY-1484](../theory.d/THEORY-1484.md)). A re-analysis of the project's data, or a
third independent replication, finding no difference in the original
direction would answer it; one finding the original effect would count
against.
