# THEORY-1819: Lovett and Riedener argue that organizations, being group agents without welfare, have no moral status, and that what we seem to owe them, such as promise-keeping and gratitude, we owe to their members, whose interest in willing participation in successful group agency grounds claims that mimic the organization's

## The account

Lovett and Riedener argue that organizations, being group agents without welfare, have no moral status, and that what we seem to owe them, such as promise-keeping and gratitude, we owe to their members, whose interest in willing participation in successful group agency grounds claims that mimic the organization's

## Evidence

The reading in [NOTE-1891](../notes.d/NOTE-1891.md); its key results are quoted there with pages.

## What it does not say

What an organization owes its members. The paper treats only what outsiders owe organizations. Its denial of standing rests on an assumption (no welfare without phenomenal consciousness) it does not argue.
