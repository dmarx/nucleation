# THEORY-tmp0lc8z: Bencherki and Iliadis propose that knowledge of an entity should focus on its individuation process and reflect on the way that knowledge itself has individuated, and the first author, the ethnographer of their illustrating case, describes his own individuation through the fieldwork

## The account

Proposition 4: "Knowledge of an entity ... should focus on its
individuation process and should reflect on the way that knowledge
itself has individuated" (ms. pp. 21–22). In the paper's illustration, a
committee meeting of community organizations in a Montréal district, the
first author was the ethnographer, and he describes his own
individuation through the fieldwork.

## Evidence

- A proposition among four, offered as a consequence of Simondon's
  informational individuation rather than argued for.
- The illustration is one meeting excerpt, read by a participant-author
  (ms. pp. 25–30).

## What it bears on

QUESTION-366 quotes proposition 4 as the second of the two
methodological demands it tests: knowledge of an entity "should reflect
on the way that knowledge itself has individuated", and notes that "The
first author describes his own individuation through the fieldwork
(NOTE-1483)." The reading is that demand, filed so the question can cite
it.
