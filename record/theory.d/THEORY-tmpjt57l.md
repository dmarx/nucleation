# THEORY-tmpjt57l: Huang et al. (2026) report that on DISCERN, a 203-task life-science benchmark scored separately for data integrity, analysis verification and hypothesis revision, eight LLM agents earned perfect scores in 60.8%, 34.2% and 0.6% of evaluations at the three levels, and that a recognised analytical problem was often not carried into the agent's calibrated conclusion (126 of 425 fully recognised challenged L2 evaluations).

## The account

Huang et al. (2026) report that on DISCERN, a 203-task life-science benchmark scored separately for data integrity, analysis verification and hypothesis revision, eight LLM agents earned perfect scores in 60.8%, 34.2% and 0.6% of evaluations at the three levels, and that a recognised analytical problem was often not carried into the agent's calibrated conclusion (126 of 425 fully recognised challenged L2 evaluations).
