The owner asked: "I think there are some (albeit half baked, maybe
quackish but at the very least directionally correct) ideas built around
LEGO case studies that would be useful for us to port to (or modify for)
the nucleation record: see `Main Project/Geometric Epistemology/The Lego
Parable`". Mathematics of Meaning was read at commit d987e2a. Every MOM
code cited below was checked against that clone, since [ADR-037](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-037.md) says a bad
MOM code is caught no other way.

## What was read

- **The monograph, in full.** About 57,000 words in three parts,
  LLM-drafted from the owner's prompts. A first pass read the Preface,
  the outlines, all of Part II, Part I's chapters 2, 6 and 10 and
  "Definitions and Theorems", and Part III's LEGO/LDA chapters and
  worked example. At the owner's request ("read the rest of that stuff,
  don't make assumptions about whether or not MoM touches on it the same
  way"), the rest followed the same day. That covered Part I's chapters
  0, 1, 3–5, 7, 8c and 9, the Overview and Outline, Part II's Preface and
  Appendices, and all of Part III, including the attic and misc folders
  and the Smoothies note.
- **MOM's own grading of it.** [MOM-CLM-135](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-135.md), [MOM-CLM-136](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-136.md), [MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md),

## What was filed

First pass:

- **[CASE-tmpd5exw](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpd5exw.md)**, the owner's LEGO parable, a stipulated thought experiment. It
  is the second time [CASE-085](../../record/cases.d/CASE-085.md)'s pattern has turned up, so it gets a code.
- **[CLAIM-tmp6u639](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6u639.md)** (Active), from [MOM-CLM-166](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-166.md). Retrieval and evidence are
  different criteria for a category, and they move oppositely with copy
  count. It complements [CLAIM-154](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-154.md), the detection criterion dual-filed from
  [MOM-CLM-160](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-160.md) on 2026-10-11. MOM's table was re-run and reproduced, and a
  simulation of linear search agreed with m/(c+1) to within about 1%. One
  correction: that the criteria disagree on every row holds for MOM's
  deadline, not in general.
- **[CASE-tmpjgubn](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpjgubn.md)** (fictional construction) and **[CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md)**. This is the directionally
  correct part of the monograph's contextuality, modified. As
  descriptions, sortings of bricks always share a joint refinement
  ([MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)) and their events stay Boolean ([MOM-ARG-047](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/arguments.d/ARG-047.md)), so the
  monograph's central example is the non-contextual case of [CLAIM-037](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-037.md) and
  [CLAIM-131](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-131.md). As arrangements under a bin budget, they really compete. The
  monograph's "Sheafification as Descriptive Compromise" survives as the
  choice between the meet and the join of two partitions.
- **[QUESTION-tmplg07t](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplg07t.md)**, refining [QUESTION-095](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-095.md): is sorting amortized search, and is
  database index selection the cost model [QUESTION-095](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-095.md) lacks?
- **[QUESTION-tmpjnf86](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpjnf86.md)**: evidence, retrieval and persistence across refinement as three
  criteria for naming a category.

Second pass, after reading the rest:

- **[CLAIM-tmp0pdrg](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp0pdrg.md)** (Active). Blow-up adds no observation. The monograph's Part I
  says resolution of singularities "restores identifiability", adds "new
  observables" and makes Fisher information non-degenerate ("Definitions
  and Theorems", Theorem 3.2), and maps this onto splitting a LEGO bin.
  The pulled-back model gives one distribution over the whole exceptional
  divisor. In x ~ N(ab, 1) the Fisher matrix is zero all along it,
  checked numerically. Splitting a bin is refining the observation, a
  Blackwell matter. [MOM-CLM-135](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-135.md) graded the definitions correct, and
  [MOM-CLM-099](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-099.md) the three senses of the word. Neither reached this step.
- **[CASE-tmpliavq](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpliavq.md)** (construction). Appendix H's castle topic persists by
  construction but as no eigenmode: the nearest eigenvector changes rank
  and turns about 51°, computed. It illustrates [CLAIM-148](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-148.md) and narrows
  [QUESTION-tmpjnf86](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpjnf86.md), whose persistence criterion has to be stated for factors.
- **[CASE-tmpjuth9](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpjuth9.md)** and **[CLAIM-tmpd7v8w](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpd7v8w.md)** (Active). The owner's smoothie line, and the
  finding that the note's signal-detection reading reverses its rising
  half. Discriminability from milk only falls as milk is added; the line
  is a preference over the ratio. This is the same kind of reversal
  [MOM-CLM-166](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-166.md) found in the LEGO parable's formal reading.
- **[CLAIM-tmp3ejtf](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp3ejtf.md)** (Proposed). "If I can't find it, it isn't there" is negation
  as failure, a closed-world assumption taken under a deadline. It is
  safe when the arrangement makes retrieval complete in time, given by
  P(present | not found) = π(1 − h)/(π(1 − h) + 1 − π). This is where the
  owner's question about database theory meets the parable. Reiter 1978
  ([LIT-tmpj4tnh](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpj4tnh.md)) and Clark 1978 ([LIT-tmpix3rp](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpix3rp.md)) are registered unread.
- **[QUESTION-tmplg07t](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplg07t.md) extended** with a survey of the database literature for the
  owner ("work at the intersection of database theory and information
  retrieval"). It covers physical design and index selection, Yao's
  "Should tables be sorted?", database cracking, move-to-front and
  rent-or-buy. All citations were Crossref-checked; none is registered
  yet. The survey found no single cost model covering bugs, filing and
  bins.
- **[QUESTION-tmpjnf86](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpjnf86.md) extended** with the owner's objections in the source: a criterion
  for naming must not be trivial, and must apply to objects as well as
  descriptors.

## The two visual-search works

Read at the owner's request.

- **Treisman and Gelade 1980** ([LIT-tmp9g55n](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp9g55n.md)): Read, [NOTE-tmpe7gfj](../../record/notes.d/NOTE-tmpe7gfj.md), from a
  Princeton course copy. It corrected the first pass. The paper puts
  serial search down to conjunctions of features, not similarity. It
  argues against similarity as the cause, and similarity on one dimension
  made search slow but not item by item. [CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md) part 3 was restated:
  sorting on one attribute turns a conjunction search into a feature
  search within each bin. [CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md) moved to Active on that narrower form.
  [CASE-tmpjgubn](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpjgubn.md), [CASE-tmpd5exw](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpd5exw.md) and [CLAIM-tmp6u639](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6u639.md) were corrected to match.
- **Duncan and Humphreys 1989** ([LIT-tmp95pcw](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmp95pcw.md)): Unavailable. The one course copy
  now returns 404, the Internet Archive's capture could not be reached,
  and OpenAlex reports it closed. Its abstract, checked by script against
  the MRC CBU bibliography page, states the similarity account. The
  record holds the disagreement with one side read.

## Four works on database physical design, and [ADR-044](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-044.md)

At the owner's request ("register and read those, and draft the ADR"):
Finkelstein, Schkolnick and Tiberio 1988 ([LIT-tmpbs1gh](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpbs1gh.md)), Yao 1981
([LIT-tmpxy7er](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpxy7er.md)), Idreos, Kersten and Manegold 2007 ([LIT-tmpmqtpm](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpmqtpm.md)) and
Sleator and Tarjan 1985 ([LIT-tmpt85t0](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmpt85t0.md)). All four were Read, from a
University of Crete course copy, a University of Maryland course copy
(compared with Yao's own Stanford report), the CIDR proceedings and
Sleator's CMU page. ACM's open copies refused every request.

Three of the survey's descriptions were corrected by the readings.
Finkelstein's model is steady-state and has no one-time build cost. Yao
shows sorted order optimal only in an extreme regime, and counts lookup
only. Sleator and Tarjan compare move-to-front with the best dynamic
offline rule, not with an arrangement. Their Theorem 3, that rearranging
ahead of need never beats moving the found item, is the finding that most
changes [QUESTION-tmplg07t](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplg07t.md). It supports [CASE-tmpd5exw](../../record/cases.d/CASE-tmpd5exw.md)'s practice and puts
"sorting is amortized search" in question. [QUESTION-tmplg07t](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmplg07t.md) records all
four and stays Open: none gives the joint cost model, and the
precomputed-answer case (the cockpit's bugs) is in none of them.

These works had no topic of their own and were first tagged
`information-retrieval`, the nearest term. The owner: "don't hesitate to
extend the vocabulary as needed". [ADR-045](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-045.md) adds `databases` (Databases and
data structures) and retags them. Harinarayan, Rajaraman and Ullman 1996
([LIT-tmptn63k](../../record/literature.d/LIT-tmptn63k.md)), the survey's pointer for precomputed answers, was registered
and read at the owner's request for the case the four leave out.

[ADR-044](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-044.md) proposes a `corroborates` relation between claims, after a
`supports` filed in the first pass wrote a false `rests_on` onto [CLAIM-296](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-296.md).
The owner accepted it ("accept [ADR-044](../../record/decisions.d/ADR-044.md) and apply it"). The relation is
declared in `luria.yaml` and applied first to [CLAIM-tmpjmtw0](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmpjmtw0.md), which
corroborates [CLAIM-296](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-296.md).

## Not ported, and why

- **The Epistemic Geometry Theorem** (Part II chapter 9), its extensions
  in Part III and Appendix G, and Part I's Theorem 4.3. [MOM-CLM-139](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-139.md)
  rejects the theorem. Its commutativity condition holds for every pair
  of sortings ([MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)), condition 2 is about the site, not the
  presheaf, and the Rademacher, VC, rank and "archetype" clauses added
  later are not conditions of the same kind.
- **The topos chapter, the Čech cocycle definition, meta-contexts, stacks
  and the "compatibilist subsheaf".** These are standard definitions with
  no instance that is not empty in this framework. The cocycle definition
  is garbled, and the subsheaf theorem takes as hypothesis the vanishing obstruction that, by [MOM-CLM-139](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-139.md), does not even imply gluing.
  [CLAIM-153](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-153.md) holds the record's worked monodromy case.
- **SDT as non-neutral measurement** (Part I chapter 2) and **Part I
  chapters 0, 1 and 3.** These are surveys. Chapter 3's "event as an
  equivalence class of indistinguishable outcomes" confuses
  distinguishable in distribution with decidable from one observation:
  with any noise the relation is the identity, yet no single observation
  decides an event. The record's [THEORY-031](../../record/theory.d/THEORY-031.md) and [QUESTION-103](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-103.md) already hold
  the graded version.
- **State–observable duality** (Part I chapter 5). A real Galois
  connection between sets of observables and the partitions they induce,
  stated loosely. [CLAIM-264](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-264.md) and [CLAIM-142](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-142.md) hold what the record needs.
- **Nominal compatibilism, archetypes and PRH** (Part III). [MOM-CLM-138](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-138.md)
  rejects the position. The observation it kept is in [QUESTION-tmpjnf86](../../record/questions.d/QUESTION-tmpjnf86.md).
- **LEGO sets as LDA documents, and hierarchical probability** (Part III,
  attic). Sound analogies for mixture models, with no claim of their own
  to test.
- **Two collectors with different collections** (Part I chapter 10). Its
  contextuality claim is [MOM-CLM-137](https://github.com/dmarx/mathematics-of-meaning/blob/main/record/claims.d/CLM-137.md)'s defect again. What it gestures at,
  that a builder's bins are fitted to their own collection, follows from
  [CLAIM-tmp6u639](../../record/claims.d/CLAIM-tmp6u639.md): bins depend on copy counts and use.
