## The account

Khatua, Zhu et al. (2026) report that coding agents given compatible but
overlapping features and a real-time messaging channel succeed markedly less often than one
agent doing both features, that the channel reduces merge conflicts without raising
success, and that the failures arise from gaps in communication, commitment and modelling
the partner's plans.
